# Day 83: I can use feedback on my math.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private; generated answer key holds new practice steps. Prepare student pages, practice-83.md, counters, frames, mats, record.md and one assigned transfer-83.md page per child. Have day 79 originals ready. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

#### 10 min. Finish and revise

Return day 82's exit, s04-W82 and conference feedback. Five minutes apply exit/paper cues; five apply conference cues, originals retained. Within these allocations, replace up to two minutes of finished children's work with at most two missing 45-second asks and transitions; no secure interview repeated. First-made/first-heard evidence stays pending for later feedback/revision.

#### 10 min. Interpret existing records

Review day 79 originals, written feedback and dated revisions: F1 four teacher minutes, F2 six. Retain separate quantity/action/question/equality judgments: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Selections alone cannot establish unheard reasons. Children independently correct [[s04-P83]]; unscored paper stays in folders. Keep day 64 diagnostic separate.

#### 20 min. Independent feedback application

Assign one transfer-83.md page from earlier written feedback. Two self-directed paper groups work simultaneously for two ten-minute rounds. Read only assigned single stems: [[s04-T83-A]] [[s04-T83-B]] [[s04-T83-C]] [[s04-T83-D]] [[s04-T83-E]] [[s04-T83-F]] [[s04-T83-G]] [[s04-T83-H]]. Private key supplies exact scenes, look-fors and samples. No worked example, hints, reteaching or new score. Circulate for access; collect the assigned transfer pages at this block's close and retain support notes. Unheard reasons stay pending.

#### 20 min. Counting and closing

Review counting, day 1: five minutes, **Ask.** [[s04-K-count-83]] Then eight minutes familiar supplied correction: **Ask.** [[s04-P83]] Then [[steps:s04-P83]]. Five-minute synthesis: **Ask.** [[s04-K-close-83]] Two volunteers at most, 90 seconds. Two-minute tidy. Carry records to days 84/85 without recollection/rescoring. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class to finish feedback/component records.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy original masters. Large paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printing, folded paper replaces mats/cards. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the assigned picture; paper squares replace counters.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **10 min. Assessment record.** Review earlier F1/F2 originals, feedback and revisions. Hold judgments until after the revision below.
- **5 min. Finish and revise.** Return day 82 written feedback. Child applies its picture cue; retain original.
- **10 min. Feedback application.** Read one assigned transfer-83.md stem. Child marks independently; save unscored paper.
- **5 min. Close.** Review revised eligible evidence, revisit familiar work and tidy.
  **Say.** "What change did your feedback help you make?" Expect: a specific changed mark or group, including pointing. Carry pending components to day 84.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar closing repetitions, retain feedback/revision and existing-record interpretation. Longer: complete familiar unscored paper; no new check or reteaching.

### Words and supports

Review familiar count, keep track, group and mark words. Read every word aloud. Pointing, signs and child-directed scribing count. Separate access from mathematical help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 84 reuses day 83's assessment records: existing F1/F2 originals, feedback, revisions and pending notes, plus unscored transfers. No secure interview repeated or record rescored. New day 83 evidence waits for later feedback/revision before judgment. Unresolved action, question and equality components remain pending. Course-level check samples earlier counting, comparing and turned shapes.
