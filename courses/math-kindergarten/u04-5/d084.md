# Day 84: I can check a puppet's idea.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare course-check cards, record and paper squares. Keep teacher-key-84-86.md and generated answer key private. Assign five cohorts of six and copy assigned pictures into student-page spaces as record-84-86.md directs. Retain day 83 originals/feedback/revisions. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out; replace unsafe pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point. No independent reading.

### The day

#### 5 min. Carried records

Return day 83's assessment record with its existing written feedback in this slot; retain pending components and supported revisions separately.

Carry day 83 s04-A83-F1/F2, underlying originals, practice observations and transfers under [[sC-E84-R]]. Preserve existing judgments, pending action/question/equality notes and unscored work. No new story, interview or rescore. Children revisit retained paper silently.

#### 5 min. Collection launch

Explain listening/paper routine without demonstrating check cases. Say "These pictures are invented for practice." Children use assigned picture spaces to mark answers at their conference. Counting centers review unit 1 day 1; retained familiar paper occupies waiting children. No peer checking.

#### 50 min. Domain conferences and centers

Five cohorts of six, ten minutes each: two separate 45-second asks per child = nine minutes, one transition minute. A: **Ask.** [[sC-E84-1]] then [[sC-E84-2]]. B: [[sC-E84-3]] then [[sC-E84-4]]. C: [[sC-E84-5]] then [[sC-E84-6]]. D: [[sC-E84-7]] then [[sC-E84-8]]. E: [[sC-E84-9]] then [[sC-E84-10]]. Private key supplies setups/look-fors. Record heard names, words/actions, mathematical help separately. Each domain samples six, never all thirty. Paper records do not establish unheard speech. Preserve first responses; no reteaching. Collect at transitions; unfinished asks remain pending.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy original cards; large paper replaces counters. One shared screen may enlarge pictures; paper suffices.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw assigned original cards; large paper pieces replace counters.

**Skip.** Class cohorts and waiting centers.

- **5 min. Assessment record.** Preserve day 83 records and their existing judgments.
- **10 min. Check.** Use child's assigned cohort pair from private key, one prompt at a time. The puppet-count repair and how-many prompts are the counting pair; use assigned pair instead if different. Keep first response/help notes.
  **Say.** "Fix the puppet counting this mat." Expect: one word per tile, seven tiles each once, for cohort A.
- **5 min. Paper and close.** Child marks assigned spaces. Give written picture feedback for tomorrow's revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep unfinished components pending. Longer: familiar paper, never extra secure interviews.

### Words and supports

Review count, altogether, more, fewer and shape names from written units 1 to 3. Allow usual communication, pointing and directed adult movement; record mathematical hints. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 85 returns today's descriptive feedback in Return and carry, without secure-domain repetition. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class for feedback. Day 86 groups use observed errors; unsampled components remain pending.
