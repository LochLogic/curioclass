# Day 86: I can use a better way.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare course-check cards, record, paper squares and one assigned course-check transfer slip per child from exit-86.md. Keep teacher-key-84-86.md and generated answer key private. Select counting, comparison or numeral group from days 84/85; record six to hear, two per group. Copy supplied practice into student-page spaces. Return written picture feedback. Adult cuts large pieces; drawings replace unsafe pieces. No mouthing; read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 85's assessment originals and observed-item records with written picture feedback. Children revise one mark; preserve first work. Carry day 84 revisions/unit 4 records intact. Judge observed components after feedback/later revision: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing evidence remains pending. This is the combined day 85 hand-back, no extra slot per letter.

#### 8 min. Response launch

Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: one handles, one watches, then swap. Say "These pictures are invented for practice." Explain working spaces and supplied partial records from cards-84-86.md; no transfer art. Each child has one priority group. Others correct their own familiar retained paper while waiting. No public ability labels.

#### 36 min. Focused groups

Three twelve-minute groups, one teacher, ten children at most per group. Each: demonstrate three minutes, simultaneous paired practice three, two selected 45-second turns, feedback/transition 1.5, paper correction three. Hear six total, never every group member. If more than ten share a gap, corresponding slot becomes whole-class work, counted once; others keep familiar paper. Queue further needs, including turned-shape naming, without claiming repair.

Counting: **Ask.** [[sC-P86-count]] then [[steps:sC-P86-count]]. Use chosen observed action's repair in cards-84-86.md: boundary/cover for omissions/repeats, paced ordered words, enclosed total, unchanged-members moves, one arrival/next word or requested stop. One priority, not all. Demonstrate objects, connect picture dots, then numeral. Fade supplied route for independent work.

Comparison: **Ask.** [[sC-P86-compare]] then [[steps:sC-P86-compare]]. Model unique partners, count totals and indicate extras. For print-size confusion, connect equal-print cards to practice quantities; words only, no inequality signs.

Numeral connection: **Ask.** [[sC-P86-symbol]] then [[steps:sC-P86-symbol]]. Count pieces, connect picture to numeral, trace/cover/write needed form. Connect empty to zero. This group serves numeral gaps; unit 4 operation evidence stays carried, without another unit 4 response.

#### 3 min. Independent practice

Finish assigned supplied correction in student-page spaces; collect with group feedback, no new score or extra spoken ask.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Give one assigned half slip per child from exit-86.md, never all forms together. Print/cut the assigned master only; it is the child ticket. Read its single stem to relevant group: **Ask.** [[sC-X86-count]] OR [[sC-X86-compare]] OR [[sC-X86-symbol]] OR [[sC-X86-move]] OR [[sC-X86-next]] OR [[sC-X86-stop]] OR [[sC-X86-fewer]]. Every child marks paper in Exit box. Three minutes group directions/setup, four concurrent work, one collection. Match repaired component: count for tracking/total, move for invariance, next for one more, stop for requested count-out, compare for matching, fewer for counted comparison, symbol for numeral connection. Correct selection never supplies an unheard explanation. No second oral conference; six heard in groups. No hints/peer checking. Private key has samples/look-fors. Retain first slips; unseen reasoning stays pending.

### Other rooms

Large paper replaces counters; hand-copy supplied practice/assigned slip. No device needed. Shared screen enlarges teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper replaces counters; hand-copy one assigned practice record/fresh slip.

**Skip.** Rotations/waiting centers.

- **5 min. Return.** Read earlier picture feedback; child revises one mark.
- **10 min. Respond.** Use selected practice and private key: counting, comparison or numeral connection. Demonstrate with pieces, connect picture/number, child corrects supplied record.
  **Say.** For counting, "Finish my record of this collection." Expect: five dots, 5, five altogether. Other groups use their own printed prompt/key.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read one assigned fresh half slip: [[sC-X86-count]] OR [[sC-X86-compare]] OR [[sC-X86-symbol]] OR [[sC-X86-move]] OR [[sC-X86-next]] OR [[sC-X86-stop]] OR [[sC-X86-fewer]], without hints; child marks independently. Keep original for descriptive feedback and later revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one priority group; keep fresh exit. Longer: supplied practice correction, never rehearsed transfer.

### Words and supports

Review count, altogether, partner, more, fewer, same and zero from written units 1/2. No new symbols. Large/tactile outlines, nonslip mats, pointing, usual communication and adult movement exactly as directed are available. Separate mathematical cues from access. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 87 retrieves unit 4 equation model and begins breaking numbers to nine into two parts. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class for feedback on practice/transfer; retain unresolved components with record. Day 87 receives written feedback and slip revision in five existing diagnostic minutes of Centers and repair, replacing one diagnostic action; judge eligible revisions afterward. Secure records stay intact.
