# Teacher-held original picture masters

Invented for practice. Hand-copy large pieces/layouts exactly. Teacher reads each stem from teacher-key-84-86.md; never hand children the full master. Adult cuts intact large pieces; count in/out. One folded-paper puppet is the only actor; it speaks one claim at a time, then falls silent.

## Day 84

A: seven separate blank tile pieces in a staggered path. Puppet touches first six once, one through six, leaves last untouched. No corrective model between repair ask and total ask.

B: eight pieces. Count once in compact cluster, spread same members in zigzag. Puppet: "Wider means more." Ask arrangement prompt. Keep pieces; indicate opposite start. Puppet: "New start means new amount." Ask order prompt. No addition or removal.

C: six paper wheel disks, one spare. Adult counts six and slides only spare in. Puppet: "Still six."

D: equal cards with 7 at 42 point and 10 at 24 point, both black. No comparison signs.

E: six wide-spaced tray outlines, eight close tag pieces; all movable. Puppet: "The wide row has more." No supplied links.

F: nine small disks close, four large disks spread, all fully visible. Puppet: "The big disks have more."

G: five unlabelled boundaries below. Name prompt first, then square alone. Puppet: "Tilted means not square." Rotate only, never stretch. Cut cards or hand-copy; square corners must be exact. The sloping rectangle has unequal adjacent sides and exact square corners.

## Day 86 supplied practice records

Before class hand-copy the assigned supplied record into each child's student-page space. No improvising or transfer art. Model only the chosen practice case.

Counting: five squares scattered; record with two filled dots, three empty places, supplied 5. Pick one observed need: boundary/cover for omissions or repeats (A/B), pause after each touch for two words (N), model ordered words for sequence (M), enclose/name whole (C), move same members/change start (D/E), model one arrival then next word (F), or spoken request five from a larger supply and stop (J). Keep models in this practice case; its first five-square arrangement is not an assessment.

Comparison: three squares, six disks, two supplied correct links. Complete third pair, count both groups, connect extras to more. Supplied caption "fewer" is on disk group for correction. For numeral print error H, place equal-print 3 and 6 under these practice quantities, then connect value to amount. No inequality symbols.

Numerals: three trays empty/two/five with wrong labels 2/5/0 respectively. Count actual pieces, show quantity picture, match/trace/cover/write needed numeral. Explicitly connect none with 0. Remove the model for independent correction. Practice values/formats never appear on the reserved transfer slips.
