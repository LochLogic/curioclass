# Private teacher key, days 84 to 86

Read each single stem aloud after showing only its assigned card. Keep this key away from child copies. Cases are invented for practice. Mark observed components shown, shown with help, not yet seen; missing evidence stays pending in Next need. Record actions before assigning letters. A wrong final number alone cannot identify its cause.

## sC-E84-1

**Show.** Card A: seven staggered tiles. Puppet indicates first six once, saying one through six, stops with last untouched.

**Say.** Fix the puppet counting this mat.

**Answer.** One through seven, one touch per tile.

**Look for.** Observe ordered words and each member once, one word per action; retain actual route, not just total.

Shown: All seven indicated once with ordered words. | Not yet seen: Leaves last tile untouched, stops at six.

**Error A.** Observed omission, unit 1 error A.

## sC-E84-2

**Answer.** Seven altogether.

**Look for.** Last word names whole collection; faulty first count remains separate.

Shown: Seven, indicating the whole mat. | Not yet seen: Repeats sequence without naming how many altogether.

**Error C.** Last-number meaning, unit 2 error A.

## sC-E84-3

Shown: Still eight; only moved. | Not yet seen: More now because they take more room.

**Error D.** Spread replaces quantity, unit 2 error B.
