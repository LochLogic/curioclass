# Day 85: I can make missing labels.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print course-check labels sheet, two sides, at 18 point; prepare H1 to H4 and J from course-check cards. Keep teacher-key-84-86.md and generated answer key private. Course-check record names six needing fresh counting evidence. Prepare day 84 written picture feedback. Large paper squares replace counters; no small pieces or mouthing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and carry

Return day 84's assessment papers and observed-item records with descriptive feedback for revision; retain originals. Carry its named-domain evidence and existing unit 4 story/equation record without new interview or rescore. Correct paper never fills an unheard component.

#### 20 min. Missing labels

Say "These collections are invented for practice." Point to each tray and read its single prompt, giving time before next: **Ask.** [[sC-E85-L1]] [[sC-E85-L2]] [[sC-E85-L3]] [[sC-E85-L4]] [[sC-E85-L5]] [[sC-E85-L6]] [[sC-E85-L7]] [[sC-E85-L8]] [[sC-E85-L9]] [[sC-E85-L10]] [[sC-E85-L11]]. Everyone labels independently, no numeral models or counting hints. One extended missing-label collection task. Private key holds quantities/look-fors; recognizable reversal receives formation feedback separately.

#### 25 min. Counting conferences and centers

Assigned six only: five separate 45-second asks each = 22.5 minutes, plus 2.5 transition. **Ask.** [[sC-E85-C1]] [[sC-E85-C2]] [[sC-E85-C3]] [[sC-E85-C4]] [[sC-E85-C5]]. Observe route, sequence and requested stop separately; retain heard names. Others finish labels/familiar retained paper. Do not repeat secure day 84 domains. Unfinished turns pending; no reteaching or diagnosis from totals alone.

#### 10 min. Record and collect

Collect labels and descriptive observations; children continue familiar paper and tidy. First performances wait for feedback and later revision before judgments. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class for picture feedback/day 86 routing. This samples within ten; no completed twenty-object standard claimed.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy both sides without answers or models. No device needed; read every stem aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy original trays/blank number boxes; large paper replaces counters.

**Skip.** Waiting centers.

- **5 min. Assessment return.** Read day 84 feedback; allow revision.
- **10 min. Labels.** Read tray prompts one at a time; child writes numbers independently. [[sC-E85-L1]] [[sC-E85-L2]]
  **Say.** "Label tray A." Expect: 0 for no objects. Continue other trays while comfortable.
- **10 min. Counting.** Use assigned missing counting cards from private key; keep first responses/help notes.
- **5 min. Collect.** Retain originals; give picture feedback for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: retain incomplete labels for later completion. Longer: familiar paper, no duplicate interviews.

### Words and supports

Review numeral forms, unit 2 day 30, and tracking, unit 1 day 12. Read letters as tray locators; no independent reading. Allow usual communication or directed adult movement. Record mathematical help separately.

### Tomorrow

Day 86 returns day 85 feedback, groups one priority counting/comparison/numeral gap per child, and collects one fresh assigned transfer. Other gaps stay recorded.
