# Day 43: Finish the picture orders

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use embedded practice-43.md cases on paper pizzas. Hide exit-43.md and food-pieces.md until exit. Keep teacher-key.md and d043-key.html private. Bring day 42 exit/practice/reason feedback, initial F4 and written feedback. Set out trays for revised mats and outlines. Choose only flagged gaps for conferences. Exit roster: 25 to 30. Inspect large adult-cut pieces for damage; keep pieces out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported oral count toward twenty: [[s02-R40-oral]]. Review day 32 stopping at a requested total; point to familiar numeral cards. Read all print aloud, 18 point. Say: "These play orders are invented for practice." No meal preferences or home reports.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 42 E42 and practice feedback. For error F, point to the rule exemplar and correct one misplaced picture on a fresh copy of the partial border record, [[s02-P42-1]]. One five-minute action, whole class above ten sharing the letter, otherwise group while others revise their feedback. Queue other letters; keep originals separate.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

One invented actor, Jo, fills one order at a time. Show three rings already on a paper pizza and request five: [[s02-P43-1]]. Ask before demonstrating two added rings, counting from the existing three and stopping at five. Separately show five strips and request eight: [[s02-P43-2]]. Three additional strips make eight. Connect objects, picture and supplied numeral; no addition symbols or new content. Keep surplus pieces outside each picture. Children repair their own partly filled orders on paper; no blank complete-order practice.

#### 7 min. Connected work

First three minutes return day 42 final F2/F3 reason feedback; revise the flagged component on retained paper. Others continue familiar partial-order corrections. Then four minutes compare the supplied packed five/eight pictures: [[s02-P43-compare]], followed separately by [[s02-P43-reason]]. Pairs alternate 45-second explaining turns concurrently; 2.5 minutes remain for matching and marking. Teacher confirms matched pairs and extras, then connects supplied 5/8 labels. No bare-number selection grid.

#### 5 min. F4 revision

Return day 42 F4 with written feedback; give five full minutes. Revise only flagged membership, count or order. Keep initial card outlines/counts/order and supported revision separately with dates. Correct numeral reversals with formation feedback when quantity is recognizable. Do not reveal the whole key or replace independent work with a copied answer. Retain for day 44 judgment.

#### 20 min. Flagged gaps and centers

Only absences or missing components, no repeated complete interview. Choose at most five four-minute counting conferences OR ten two-minute reason turns. Counting-conference.md uses separate [[s02-M1a-34]], [[s02-M1b-34]], [[s02-M1c-34]], [[s02-M1d-34]], [[s02-M2a-34]] prompts: five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes. Use seven first-tray tabs and ten spare second-tray tabs; rearrange only the first seven. Reuse the unseen component's original prompt for an absentee, never as practice. A reason turn uses the child's retained F2/F3 paper, two separate 45-second prompts plus thirty seconds notes. Others finish familiar partial-order corrections. Record names, help and missing components on record.md.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Put paper food in table trays concurrently. Collect practice separately from revised F4. Thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes after class; return day 45 in its existing response slot. Missing-evidence feedback takes ten teacher minutes after class, return day 45.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal blank pizza, order icons and ten spare pictures of each food only now. Read the order labels aloud, then [[s02-X43]] exactly. One-minute launch; two simultaneous paper minutes, attaching and outlining selected pictures. Hear six with [[s02-O43]], 45 seconds each including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect in half a minute. Record completed quantities separately from heard stop reasons; observed continued additions show error D. Thirty papers at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes after class. Return feedback day 44; revision waits for day 45.

### Other rooms

Draw the exact supplied cases on scrap paper; use large paper food or child-directed adult drawing. No device or real food needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the partial pizzas and packed pictures; use large scrap food pieces.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **3 min. Return.** Give day 42 feedback; correct one rule action.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P43-1]] Expect two added rings for five. Separately [[s02-P43-2]]: three added strips for eight.
- **4 min. Compare.** [[s02-P43-compare]] **Say.** "Explain the comparison." Expect five pairs, three extras in eight; five has fewer.
- **5 min. Revise.** Give written sorting feedback. Child revises; keep the original and changed work.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice food.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal blank pizza and new food pictures. Read icons three/six and [[s02-X43]]. Child fills alone. Ask how they stopped; expect the requested three mushrooms and six pepper pieces. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated shop rounds; keep F4 revision, missing-evidence capacity and exit. Longer: rearrange packed orders without changing their totals.

### Words and supports

Review count-out stopping, day 32; numeral, day 30; more/fewer, day 21; same, day 20; rule/category, day 41. Review Partner turns and Counting centers, day 1, Return and repair, day 2. Use large/tactile pictures, pointing, signs and exact child-directed movement. Record support; scribe reasons, never checked numerals. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 closes earlier records and gathers only missing evidence. Return day 43 exit feedback during Close records, two minutes, without reteaching. Keep first-made work and unresolved components for day 45's five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer; day 46 handles one remaining priority. Unheard reasons stay not yet seen.
