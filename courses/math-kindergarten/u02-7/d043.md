# Day 43: Finish the picture orders

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use embedded practice-43.md cases on paper pizzas. Hide exit-43.md and food-pieces.md until exit. Keep teacher-key.md and d043-key.html private. Bring day 42 exit/practice/reason feedback, initial F4 and written feedback. Set out trays for revised mats and outlines. Choose only flagged gaps for conferences. Exit and heard-check roster: 19 to 24. Inspect large adult-cut pieces for damage; keep pieces out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported oral count toward twenty: [[s02-R43-oral]]. Review day 32 stopping at a requested total; point to familiar numeral cards. Read all print aloud, 18 point. Say: "These play orders are invented for practice." No meal preferences or home reports.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 42 s02-X42, s02-O42, s02-P42a, s02-P42b and s02-P42c, s02-R42-oral notes and s02-P42-1 and s02-P42-talk feedback. If many day 42 tickets show error F, correct [[s02-F43-rule]]: two single-border squares left; five double-border and one single-border square right. Supplied counts are 3/5. Point to the exemplars, then mark the misplaced card's route. Return 1, model 1, concurrent fresh correction 2, collect 1 minute. Work together above ten sharing the letter; otherwise group while others revise. Keep originals; queue other letters.

#### 9 min. Rehearsal

First four minutes: the separate Exit box paper check, [[s02-P43]]. Read each stem separately; launch/read 1, independent work 2.5, collect 0.5. No coaching or new teaching.

Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O43]] from heard-43.md, one ask only. Twenty-four children mark a relevant feature on paper while the six keep their sheets covered. Reveal each of the six sheets at that child's turn; scribe the heard words beside their mark. Hear roster 19 to 24: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 6 min. Connected work

First three minutes return day 42 final F2/F3 reason feedback; revise the flagged component on retained paper. Others continue familiar partial-order corrections. Then three minutes compare the supplied packed five/eight pictures, reviewing Complete one-to-one matching with visible extras from day 21: [[s02-P43-compare]], followed separately by [[s02-P43-reason]]. Pairs alternate 45-second explaining turns concurrently; 1.5 minutes remain for matching and marking. Teacher confirms matched pairs and extras, then connects supplied 5/8 labels. No bare-number selection grid.

#### 5 min. F4 revision

Return day 42 F4 with written feedback; give five full minutes. Revise only flagged membership, count or order. Keep initial card outlines/counts/order and supported revision separately with dates. Correct numeral reversals with formation feedback when quantity is recognizable. Do not reveal the whole key or replace independent work with a copied answer. Retain for day 44 judgment.

#### 20 min. Flagged gaps and centers

Only absences or missing components, no repeated complete interview. Choose at most five four-minute counting conferences OR ten two-minute reason turns. Counting-conference.md uses its five separately printed prompts: five 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes. Use seven first-tray tabs and ten spare second-tray tabs; rearrange only the first seven. Reuse the unseen component's original prompt for an absentee, never as practice. A reason turn uses the child's retained F2/F3 paper, two separate 45-second prompts plus thirty seconds notes. Others finish the familiar partial orders [[s02-P43-1]] and [[s02-P43-2]] with supplied labels. No new teacher prompt or blank-order rehearsal. Record names, help and missing components on record.md.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Put paper food in table trays concurrently. Collect practice separately from revised F4. Thirty practice sets, including P43 and O43, at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes after class; return day 45 in its existing response slot. Missing-evidence feedback takes ten teacher minutes after class, return day 45.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-43.md only now and read [[s02-X43]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children mark or direct marks on paper for two minutes; roster 19 to 24 waits with covered cards. Observe those six first responses, one 45-second turn including notes each, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return E43, O43 and P43 feedback in day 44 Close records, two minutes without reteaching; revise one priority in day 45 Unit 2 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Draw the exact supplied cases on scrap paper; use large paper food or child-directed adult drawing. No device or real food needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the partial pizzas and packed pictures; use large scrap food pieces.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **3 min. Return.** Give day 42 feedback; correct one rule action.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P43-1]] Expect two added rings for five. Separately [[s02-P43-2]]: three added strips for eight.
- **4 min. Compare.** [[s02-P43-compare]] **Say.** "Explain the comparison." Expect five pairs, three extras in eight; five has fewer.
- **5 min. Revise.** Give written sorting feedback. Child revises; keep the original and changed work.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice food.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X43]] on the unseen exit-43.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated shop rounds; keep F4 revision, missing-evidence capacity and exit. Longer: rearrange packed orders without changing their totals.

### Words and supports

Review Count out additions to a requested final total and count-out stopping, day 32; numeral, day 30; more/fewer, day 21; same, day 20; rule/category, day 41. Review Partner turns and Counting centers, day 1, Return and repair, day 2. Use large/tactile pictures, pointing, signs and exact child-directed movement. Record support; scribe reasons, never checked numerals. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 closes earlier records and gathers only missing evidence. Return day 43 exit feedback during Close records, two minutes, without reteaching. Keep first-made work and unresolved components for day 45's five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer; day 46 handles one remaining priority. Unheard reasons stay not yet seen.
