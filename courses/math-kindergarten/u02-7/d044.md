# Day 44: Close the number record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Gather M1/M2/M3 and F1/F2/F3/F4 originals, feedback and revisions, including revised day 42 F4 from day 43. Keep teacher-key.md and daily keys private. List missing components by child. Prepare only their unseen original papers. Read all print aloud, 18 point. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 10 min. Familiar independent centers

Review Counting centers, day 1. Revisit [[s02-P44-familiar]], the known partial color diagram, without a new teaching demonstration. All pictures are invented for practice. Familiar practice stays in the folder, not collected or judged as final evidence.

#### 40 min. Missing evidence

Reuse complete records; never repeat a complete interview. Capacity: ten four-minute counting conferences OR twenty two-minute reason turns, not both. Each counting conference uses five separate prompts from counting-conference.md, 45 seconds each plus fifteen seconds notes; seven first-tray tabs remain seven when moved, and seven are requested from ten spare tabs. Reasons use retained F2/F3 papers, two 45-second prompts plus thirty seconds notes.

For missing paper, reserve fifteen minutes including separately read directions and ten independent minutes; then at most six four-minute conferences or twelve two-minute turns. Reveal original F1, F2/F3, numeral-record-1.md/numeral-record-2.md or fresh F4 only to a child without that component's first response. Use original stems, no coaching; never center practice. Collect missing papers when that paper block ends. Keep membership outlines, labels and physical mat order. First-made work receives feedback after class and day 45 revision before judgment. No reteaching today. Record names, help and uncollected components honestly.

#### 10 min. Close records

Eight record minutes, then two for returning day 43 s02-X43, s02-O43 and s02-P43 feedback and concurrent cleanup. Judge earlier revised records using [[s02-A44-M1]] [[s02-A44-M2]] [[s02-A44-M3]] [[s02-A44-F1]] [[s02-A44-F2]] [[s02-A44-F3]] [[s02-A44-F4]]. These are retained-work judgments, never new asks. Never judge day 41 sorting practice as F4. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component; missing or unheard evidence stays not yet seen. Keep first-made work separate. Thirty records at one minute each require thirty further teacher minutes after class; first-made feedback takes fifteen more. Date original, feedback, revision and judgment. No extra exit.

### Other rooms

Use the same retained papers; hand-copy missing original art privately. No device turns or replacement full test.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw an exact missing sheet; large scrap tabs replace counters.

**Skip.** Class rotations and any already completed check.

- **25 min. Assessment: missing evidence.** Examine earlier counting, numeral, one-more and sorting papers. Read only a missing component's original prompt aloud, one at a time. Your child responds alone; keep paper and any help noted. For a missing counting check, set seven tabs on one tray and ten on another; use counting-conference.md, changing only the first seven's arrangement. Expect seven still, and seven counted out from ten. Stop when missing work is collected; take breaks freely.
- **5 min. Familiar work.** Revisit [[s02-P44-familiar]]. **Say.** "What needs fixing?" Expect the misplaced blue square; counts three, five, eight.
- **10 min. Close.** Return day 43 feedback without reteaching. Keep first attempts, corrections and notes. Give new-work feedback afterward and revise next day before deciding what is shown. [[s02-A44-M1]] [[s02-A44-M2]] [[s02-A44-M3]] [[s02-A44-F1]] [[s02-A44-F2]] [[s02-A44-F3]] [[s02-A44-F4]]


### Shorter or longer

Shorter: carry unresolved components to response. Longer: familiar centers only; no repeated assessment.

### Words and supports

Review one more, day 36; more/fewer, day 21; numeral, day 30; sort/category/rule, day 41. Keep established access supports, recording help. Public talk may be passed; scribe reasons, never checked numerals.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 teaches classroom shapes and position words. Its Unit 2 response holds one five-minute repair and five-minute fresh transfer, including day 43 feedback and first-made day 44 revision before judgment. Choose one action: error D, show the requested stop; error E, connect empty to zero; error G, align counts; error H, advance after one; error I, name extras; error J, build quantities; error K, track each object. Prioritize one; day 46 holds the remaining priority in another ten-minute response. Preserve unresolved records.
