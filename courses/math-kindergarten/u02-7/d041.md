# Day 41: Sort by a rule, order by count

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use embedded practice-41.md and one-more-record.md. Read all print aloud; pictures are invented for practice. Inspect adult-cut pieces; keep them out of mouths. Hide Side 2 until F1; hide exit art until collection. Adult-cut mitten-sock-pieces.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d041-key.html private; use the daily key for F1 observation letters. Bring day 40 feedback and retained F2/F3 papers. Final reason roster: 11 to 20; exit roster: 7 to 12. Prepare short/long exemplar strips, three color mats with matching border cues, and partial equal-six groups.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Return and repair

Return day 40 exits, practice, heard notes and F2/F3 papers with written feedback, including s02-O40, s02-R40-oral, s02-P40-1, s02-R40, s02-K40-more, s02-K40-less and s02-X40. Two numeral minutes: choose error J, [[s02-F41-J]], or error M, [[s02-F41-M]]. Three comparison minutes: error I uses [[s02-F41-I]]. These share one five-minute packet action: return 1, model 1, concurrent correction 2, collect 1. Above ten sharing the priority letter, work together; otherwise group while others revise. Keep originals; queue other letters.

#### 10 min. Model sorting and counts

Teach sort: put together what matches a rule. A rule tells what belongs together. A category is one matching group. Show one short strip beside its exemplar, then one long strip; name short as less length and long as more length, without measuring. Set these aside.

Start red/single-border, blue/double-border and green/triple-border groups. Border cues repeat color for black-and-white access. Explain membership, count every group once, connect totals to supplied 3/5/8. Read [[s02-P41-1]] before demonstrating. Move the blue square to its matching mat; touch each square once, crossing the counted-uncounted boundary. Review that day 12 model. For error K or error L, repeat the single visits. Count five blue squares; a label of 2 would show error M, so match 5 to the count. Review day 36 fewest/most and order; align equal-sized squares to order three, five, eight. Show partial equal-six groups; complete both and explain that tied counts may go either way. No independent full sort here.

Use [[s02-K41-order]] with the partial color record during this demonstration; do not add another paper check.

#### 5 min. Rule check

Read [[s02-O41]] from heard-41.md, one ask only. Twenty-four children mark a relevant feature on paper while the six keep their sheets covered. Reveal each of the six sheets at that child's turn; scribe the heard words beside their mark. Hear roster 7 to 12: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Connected work and F1

First four minutes: [[s02-P41a]] [[s02-P41b]] on the Exit box. Read each ask separately; launch/read 1, independent paper 2.5, collect 0.5.

Six independent F1 minutes: reveal Side 2 now. Read [[s02-F1a]], pause; [[s02-F1b]], pause; [[s02-F1c]]. Children mark paper independently; no modeled answer. Keep this evidence separate from unscored sorting practice. After class, thirty F1 papers at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 42.

#### 20 min. Final reasons and centers

Hear children 11 to 20, using retained day 40 papers: [[s02-F2reason-41]], then separately [[s02-F3reason-41]]. Two 45-second asks plus thirty seconds notes/transition total two minutes each. No second paper collection. Others repeat familiar supplied corrections in Counting centers. Record names heard, help and components on record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper alone cannot prove unheard counting or reasoning. After class, five teacher minutes per reason domain prepare day 42 feedback.

#### 2 min. Tidy

During concurrent tray cleanup, join [[s02-R41-oral]] once as a class, up to thirty seconds. This familiar count is rehearsal, not individual sequence evidence. 

Collect practice separately from evidence; return pieces concurrently to table trays. After class, thirty practice sets, including O41 and P41a/P41b, at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; return in day 42 Connected repair. Unheard language stays not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-41.md only now and read [[s02-X41]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children mark or direct marks on paper for two minutes; roster 7 to 12 waits with covered cards. Observe those six first responses, one 45-second turn including notes each, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return E41 with O41 and the practice packet in day 42 Connected repair, five minutes for one priority action.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the same art; large scrap pictures replace printing. Every task has paper. No device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw mats, strips and supplied diagrams; use large scrap pieces.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar count words toward twenty.
- **5 min. Model.** Explain sort, category and rule with started color groups. Name short/long strips.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P41-1]] **Say.** "What needs fixing?" Expect: move blue to blue; totals three, five, eight.
- **7 min. Paper.** [[s02-K41-order]] Expect three, five, eight. Reveal the frame; read [[s02-F1a]], [[s02-F1b]], [[s02-F1c]] separately. Child marks alone; expect five circled, tenth cell, 10. Keep the paper.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put pieces away.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X41]] on the unseen exit-41.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce model repeats, keeping repairs, F1 and exit. Longer: revisit partial tied groups within ten.

### Words and supports

Review count and number word, day 1; more/fewer, day 21; same, day 20; numeral, day 30; one more and fewest/most, day 36. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; counted-uncounted tracking, day 12. Use enlarged/tactile pictures, signs, pointing or directed adult movement; record help. Scribe reasons, never a checked numeral. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 changes the category rule. Return day 41 F1 for two revision minutes and reason feedback for three in Return and repair. Connected repair returns E41 and practice: one five-minute correction, error F matching exemplars or error G aligning counts; queue other letters. Hear final children 21 to 30. Judge only the fresh F4 transfer later, never today's sorting practice.
