# Day 41: Sort by a rule, order by count

## Teacher page

### Before class

Embed practice-41.md and one-more-record.md as shown below. Hide Side 2 until F1; hide exit art until collection. Adult-cut mitten-sock-pieces.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d041-key.html private; use the daily key for F1 observation letters. Bring day 40 feedback and retained F2/F3 papers. Final reason roster: 11 to 20; exit roster: 13 to 18. Prepare short/long exemplar strips, three color mats with matching border cues, and partial equal-six groups.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported oral count toward twenty: [[s02-R41-oral]]. Review day 37 numeral value with represented five/eight, equal-print cards. Read all print aloud, 18 point. Say: "These pictures are invented for practice." Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 40 exits, practice and F2/F3 papers with feedback. Two numeral minutes: error J uses [[s02-F41-J]], represented five/eight then labels; error M uses [[s02-F41-M]]. Three comparison minutes: error I uses [[s02-F41-I]]. Choose the flagged action in each domain; queue other letters. Above ten sharing a letter, correct together; otherwise small group while others revise feedback. Preserve originals.

#### 10 min. Model sorting and counts

Teach sort: put together what matches a rule. A category is one matching group. Show one short strip beside its exemplar, then one long strip; name short/long without measuring. Set these aside.

Start red/single-border, blue/double-border and green/triple-border groups. Border cues repeat color for black-and-white access. Explain membership, count every group once, connect totals to supplied 3/5/8. Use [[s02-P41-1]]: ask before demonstrating the misplaced blue-square correction. Review day 36 fewest/most; align equal-sized squares to order three, five, eight. Show partial equal-six groups; complete both and explain that tied counts may go either way. No independent full sort here.

#### 10 min. Connected work and F1

Four practice minutes: partners correct the partial diagram on paper, then [[s02-K41-order]]. Three is supplied first; position the remaining labels. Two concurrent 45-second turns leave 2.5 minutes for drawing and feedback.

Six independent F1 minutes: reveal Side 2 now. Read [[s02-F1a]], pause; [[s02-F1b]], pause; [[s02-F1c]]. Children mark paper independently; no modeled answer. Keep this evidence separate from unscored sorting practice. After class, thirty F1 papers at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 42.

#### 20 min. Final reasons and centers

Hear children 11 to 20, using retained day 40 papers: [[s02-F2reason-41]], then separately [[s02-F3reason-41]]. Two 45-second asks plus thirty seconds notes/transition total two minutes each. No second paper collection. Others repeat familiar supplied corrections in Counting centers. Record names heard, help and components on record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper alone cannot prove unheard counting or reasoning. After class, five teacher minutes per reason domain prepare day 42 feedback.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice separately from evidence; return pieces concurrently to table trays. After class, thirty practice sets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 42.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-41.md now. Launch one minute, read [[s02-X41]] exactly. Children sort, outline membership and ring on paper for two minutes. Hear six with [[s02-O41]], 45 seconds each including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect in half a minute. Record paper membership/comparison separately from heard reasons. After class, thirty papers at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 42.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the same art; large scrap pictures replace printing. Every task has paper. No device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw mats, strips and supplied diagrams; use large scrap pieces.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar count words toward twenty.
- **5 min. Model.** Explain sort, category and rule with started color groups. Name short/long strips.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P41-1]] **Say.** "What needs fixing?" Expect: move blue to blue; totals three, five, eight.
- **7 min. Paper.** [[s02-K41-order]] Expect three, five, eight. Reveal the frame; read [[s02-F1a]], [[s02-F1b]], [[s02-F1c]] separately. Child marks alone; expect five circled, tenth cell, 10. Keep the paper.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put pieces away.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X41]] on unseen paper; child sorts and rings alone. Ask how their picture shows more. Expect socks with extras. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce model repeats, keeping repairs, F1 and exit. Longer: revisit partial tied groups within ten.

### Words and supports

Review more/fewer, day 21; same, day 20; numeral, day 30; one more and fewest/most, day 36. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; counted-uncounted tracking, day 12. Use enlarged/tactile pictures, signs, pointing or directed adult movement; record help. Scribe reasons, never a checked numeral. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 changes the category rule. Return day 41 F1 for two revision minutes and reason feedback for three in Return and repair. Connected repair returns E41 and practice: one five-minute correction, error F matching exemplars or error G aligning counts; queue other letters. Hear final children 21 to 30. Judge only the fresh F4 transfer later, never today's sorting practice.

Ours, all of it.
