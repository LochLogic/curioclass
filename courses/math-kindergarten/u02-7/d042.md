# Day 42: A different sorting rule

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut off the lower mat panel of the student page, then separate its three mats; keep practice strip intact. Adult-cut shape-cards.md on square borders. Hide mats/cards and exit art until their own tasks. Keep teacher-key.md and d042-key.html private. Bring day 41 F1/reason/exit/practice feedback and retained F2/F3 papers. Final reason roster: 21 to 30; exit roster: 19 to 24. Have plain paper for tracing revisions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported count: [[s02-R40-oral]]. Review day 41 sort, category and rule by pointing to the corrected color diagram. Read every word aloud, 18 point. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 41 F1 with feedback for two revision minutes. Then three minutes correct F2/F3 reason feedback from children 11 to 20, using retained paper. A heard reason is not a second paper task. Preserve original and supported revision. Other children revisit familiar corrections; queue unresolved components.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review day 41 category rules. Use the corrected color-square record from day 41, then show the supplied partly changed border record: [[s02-P42-1]]. Name the new one-border/two-border examples; the picture is already partly sorted. Children mark the misplaced double-border square's route and correct counts on paper. Ask before showing the correction. Both completed groups have eight; review day 36 fewest/most and tied positions. Two concurrent partner turns, 45 seconds each, leave 6.5 minutes for counting, recording and feedback. No independent full re-sort practice.

#### 5 min. Connected repair

Return day 41 E41 and practice feedback. One correction action: error F matches each card to its exemplar using today's partial border diagram; error G aligns counted equal-square rows, then corrects the partial order. Choose one, whole class above ten sharing it, otherwise group while others revise. Keep original and fresh correction; queue other letters.

#### 25 min. Final reasons and F4 centers

First five minutes: reveal fresh cards and shuffled example mats only now. Read [[s02-F4a]]. Separately read [[s02-F4-circle]], pointing only to its blank count box; repeat the exact shared stem for [[s02-F4-square]] and [[s02-F4-triangle]]. Then read [[s02-F4order]]. No model, correct placement or key. Children get ten independent work minutes within the remaining twenty, then familiar Counting centers.

During those twenty minutes hear children 21 to 30 from retained day 40 papers: [[s02-F2reason-42]], then separately [[s02-F3reason-42]]. Each child gets two 45-second asks plus thirty seconds notes/transition, two minutes total. No concurrent teacher-led repair. Record support and unheard components honestly.

#### 4 min. Compare and tidy

Two minutes: partners explain the familiar corrected border diagram, [[s02-P42-talk]], 45 seconds each concurrently. No sharing F4 answers. Two minutes: leave F4 mats in child-chosen order on trays; record each order before collection, thirty children at four seconds each. Keep outlined membership, count labels and adult-recorded order together. Collect practice separately.

After class: thirty F4 records at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; give written component feedback for day 43's full revision. Reason feedback takes five teacher minutes per domain. Practice takes fifteen teacher minutes; return day 43. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; numeral reversal alone gets formation feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-42.md and its initially striped/plain groups. Read [[s02-X42]] exactly, one-minute launch. Two simultaneous paper minutes: move cards to length mats, outline membership and ring. Hear six with [[s02-O42]], 45 seconds each including notes, 4.5 minutes; collect in half a minute. Only observed sorting actions establish error F; unheard rule explanations stay unclassified. Thirty papers at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes after class; return day 43.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact supplied pictures and cut large scrap cards. Children direct adult movement if needed; retain their paper traces. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw supplied art on scrap paper; cut large cards and three mats.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting toward twenty.
- **4 min. Return.** Give day 41 feedback; keep the first paper and its correction.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P42-1]] **Say.** "Explain the corrected count order." Expect eight in each group, either first.
- **10 min. Sort.** Reveal fresh shapes and shuffled mats. Read [[s02-F4a]], then the shared count stem separately for each mat, then [[s02-F4order]]. Your child works alone; keep grouping, numbers and mat order. Expect two circles, four squares, six triangles, ascending.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put pieces away.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal length cards; read [[s02-X42]]. Child re-sorts and rings alone. Ask their rule afterward; expect short/long, six short and four long. Keep paper for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce rehearsal repeats; protect F4, reason turns, repair and exit. Longer: revisit the partial tied groups.

### Words and supports

Review sort/category/rule/short/long, day 41; fewest/most, day 36; more/fewer, day 21. Review Partner turns and Counting centers, day 1, and Return and repair, day 2. Enlarge or use tactile pictures; accept pointing, signs and directed adult movement. Record help. Scribe reasons, never checked numerals. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 applies counting to partial paper pizza orders. Return day 42 exit/practice feedback in Return and repair; one five-minute category-rule correction, fresh partial border diagram, error F. Three Connected minutes return final reason feedback. F4 has five separate revision minutes; preserve original membership, counts and order for day 44 judgment.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
