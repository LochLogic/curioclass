# Day 42: A different sorting rule

## Teacher page

### Before class

Adult-cut the three mats from a separate category-record.md copy; keep its directions as your prompt master. Cut shape-cards.md on square borders. Cover Side 2; seal the Exit box and F4 directions separately. Hide cards/mats and exit art. Keep teacher-key.md and d042-key.html private. Bring day 41 F1/reason/exit/practice feedback and retained F2/F3 papers. Reason roster: 21 to 30; exit roster: 13 to 18.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported count: [[s02-R42-oral]]. Review day 41 sort, category and rule with [[s02-P42-1]]. The colors remain; the new supplied rule uses borders. Children correct the misplaced double-border card; confirm eight in each group. Either tied group may come first. Count together for thirty seconds; use two minutes for the correction and one concurrent pair round of two 45-second turns for [[s02-P42-talk]], with one minute handling. This is application of day 41 teaching. Read every word aloud, 18 point. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 41 F1 with feedback for two revision minutes. Then three minutes correct F2/F3 reason feedback from children 11 to 20, using retained paper. A heard reason is not a second paper task. Preserve original and supported revision. Other children revisit familiar corrections; queue unresolved components.

#### 9 min. Rehearsal

First four minutes: the separate Exit box paper check, [[s02-P42a]] [[s02-P42b]] [[s02-P42c]]. Read each stem separately over the shared picture; point to the matching blank number box. For P42c, children mark routes to the two order spaces. Launch/read 1, independent work 2.5, collect 0.5. No coaching or new teaching.

Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O42]] from heard-42.md, one ask only. Twenty-four children mark a relevant feature on paper while the six keep their sheets covered. Reveal each of the six sheets at that child's turn; scribe the heard words beside their mark. Hear roster 13 to 18: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Connected repair

Return day 41 s02-X41, s02-O41, s02-P41a and s02-P41b, s02-R41-oral notes and practice feedback, including s02-P41-1, s02-K41-order and s02-F41-J, s02-F41-M and s02-F41-I. If many day 41 tickets show error F, match pictures to exemplars on the partial border diagram. For error G, align equal-square rows before correcting order. Choose one priority: return 1, model 1, concurrent correction 2, collect 1 minute. Work together above ten sharing it; otherwise group while others revise. Keep originals and revisions; queue other letters.

#### 25 min. Final reasons and F4 centers

First five minutes: reveal fresh cards and shuffled example mats only now. Read [[s02-F4a]]. Read [[s02-F4-circle]], [[s02-F4-square]] and [[s02-F4-triangle]] separately, pointing only to each blank count box. Then read [[s02-F4order]]. No model, correct placement or key. Children get ten independent work minutes within the remaining twenty, then familiar Counting centers, repeating the supplied border correction. This familiar partial correction stays separate from independent F4.

During those twenty minutes hear children 21 to 30 from retained day 40 papers: [[s02-F2reason-42]], then separately [[s02-F3reason-42]]. Each child gets two 45-second asks plus thirty seconds notes/transition, two minutes total. No concurrent teacher-led repair. Record support and unheard components honestly.

#### 3 min. Compare and tidy

One minute: one partner repeats the familiar border-diagram explanation, 45 seconds, concurrently across pairs. No sharing F4 answers. Two minutes: leave F4 mats in child-chosen order on trays; record each order before collection, thirty children at four seconds each. Keep outlined membership, count labels and adult-recorded order together. Collect practice separately.

After class: thirty F4 records at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; give written component feedback for day 43's full revision. Reason feedback takes five teacher minutes per domain. Practice, P42a/P42b/P42c and O42 take fifteen teacher minutes; return in day 43 Return and repair. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; numeral reversal alone gets formation feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-42.md only now and read [[s02-X42]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children mark or direct marks on paper for two minutes; roster 13 to 18 waits with covered cards. Observe those six first responses, one 45-second turn including notes each, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return E42 with O42 and the paper-check/practice packet in day 43 Return and repair, one five-minute action.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact supplied pictures and cut large scrap cards and separate mats. Children direct adult movement if needed; retain their paper traces. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw supplied art on scrap paper; cut large cards and three mats.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **1 min. Count.** Join familiar counting toward twenty.
- **4 min. Return.** Give day 41 feedback; keep the first paper and its correction.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P42-1]] **Say.** "Explain the corrected count order." Expect eight in each group, either first.
- **10 min. Sort.** Reveal fresh shapes and shuffled mats. Read [[s02-F4a]], read each mat's own number prompt separately, then [[s02-F4order]]. Your child works alone; keep grouping, numbers and mat order. Expect two circles, four squares, six triangles, ascending.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put pieces away.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X42]] on the unseen exit-42.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce rehearsal repeats; protect F4, reason turns, repair and exit. Longer: revisit the partial tied groups.

### Words and supports

Review sort/category/rule/short/long, day 41; fewest/most, day 36; more/fewer, day 21. Review Partner turns and Counting centers, day 1, and Return and repair, day 2. Enlarge or use tactile pictures; accept pointing, signs and directed adult movement. Record help. Scribe reasons, never checked numerals. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 applies counting to partial paper pizza orders. Return day 42 exit/practice feedback in Return and repair; one five-minute category-rule correction, fresh partial border diagram, error F. Three Connected minutes return final reason feedback. F4 has five separate revision minutes; preserve original membership, counts and order for day 44 judgment.
