# Day 3: Explore counters and mats

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and private key-s01.md. Prepare practice-3.md, five-frame.md and covered exit-3.md. Cut counter-pieces.md; trim the duplicate counter supply from the response master. Bring day 2 crayon cards and practice with feedback. Use record.md, cards-centers.md, counting-mat.md and paper-tools.md. Hear roster 13 to 18 in Guided pairing and the exit. Use large, blunt pieces and non-toxic pencils; replace damaged tools.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review: day 2 word-and-pass pairing with [[s01-W3]]. Partners take turns simultaneously, 45 seconds each. Echo one through five slowly. Touch one loose piece during each word; do not use a pictured group.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Say: "These pictures and quantities are invented for practice." Introduce a counter as a piece we can move while counting. Name the 5-frame, a mat with five spaces. Show it empty, without filling or counting its spaces. [[s01-H3]] Children explore; overlap, gaps and pieces outside the frame are welcome. Partners share one arrangement each, 45 seconds per turn. Teach no frame-filling rule.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 2's crayon cards and written task s01-P2-1: distribute and read feedback aloud, one minute. If day 2's tickets showed error A, review the puppet's omission and correction with fresh squares; error C, review ordered words before distinct passes. Show right before wrong, one minute. Choose the first obstruction only; more than ten sharing it means whole class, otherwise one small group. Retry [[s01-P3-1]] with fresh loose counters simultaneously, two minutes; collect, one minute. Queue other errors. Do not infer unheard words from dots.

#### 20 min. Explore counters and mats

Children arrange five counters freely on, across or beside a frame for ten minutes. No one-object-per-cell demonstration. For ten minutes, set the frame aside and count by passing loose counters to a partner. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously; two 45-second turns each take three minutes. Reuse the pieces in new free designs. Watch for a word racing ahead of a hand; slow to one pass, one word, then pause.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Review: moving each object once from days 1 and 2 with [[s01-K3-1]].

[[steps:s01-K3-1]]

Show the correct ordered words with distinct passes before the puppet's wrong sequence; wait for children to try the correction.

**Ask.** [[s01-F3]] Expect: one through five, matched to five distinct passes.

Children complete [[s01-P3-1]]. Hear initially unheard roster 13 to 18, one 45-second counting turn each, while other pairs continue. Reserve 4.5 minutes plus half a minute recording on record.md. Later words are new evidence, not day 1 results; absences go to day 6 Familiar centers.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Keep dotted practice counters in the student-page box. Collect practice with the exit for day 4 Return and repair; file dated work after its retry. Give immediate spoken feedback. Two volunteers at most show a pass, 45 seconds each. Keep pieces out of mouths. Tidy at table trays; use the room's usual hand-cleaning routine. Put away the exploratory frames.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Allow directions one minute, independent paper work two, six individual turns 4.5, and collection half a minute. Uncover exit-3.md only for each child's response; the six wait with covered cards until their turns and count once. Read [[s01-X3]] exactly. Use the familiar Paper count-dot record as each counter moves. No partner help or demonstration of destination filling.

Record actual words, actions and help on record.md, using shown, shown with help, not yet seen separately for pairing and sequence. Unheard words remain not yet seen. Return every practice and exit paper on day 4, Return and repair. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty sets, thirty seconds each, including retry checks.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without a printer, copy the exact stem and draw four boxes in an irregular column, with four cut-paper counters alongside. Enlarge for access, keeping the same layout. Hand-drawn five-space frames support free exploration.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the exploratory frame; use paper counters. Draw and cover the separate exit card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Pass three strips, one through three.
- **5 min. Explore.** Your child arranges five counters freely on the frame.
- **13 min. Practice.** Put the frame aside. Read [[s01-P3-1]]. Your child passes and dots the paper counters.
  **Say.** "Pass each piece with its word." Expect: one through five, once each.
- **4 min. Repair.** Rehearse ordered words, then pass the pieces again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X3]] and read it without modeling. Keep the marked pieces and note heard words. Return feedback before tomorrow's practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated free designs, keeping the model, record and exit. Longer: make another free arrangement with the same five counters, then pass them to count.

### Words and supports

Counter and 5-frame are new tool names. Review: count, object, number word, touch, move, keep track, tool and partner from day 1. Review: Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Offer tactile outlines, enlarged spaces or adult movement directed by the child; record help. Frame structure and numeral writing are not required.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 explores solid building pieces. If day 3's exit showed error A, include a missed piece in a fresh loose-square count. Error D calls for a pause between actions. Use one five-minute repair, then count building pieces during dismantling; continue the six-child listening rotation.
