# Day 3: Explore counters and mats

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hide the counter exit cards and bag each set of pieces separately. Teacher-only answer key: d003-key.html, from items.csv. Draw five adjoining empty spaces on each exploratory frame. Prepare day 2 crayon cards with feedback. Select the next six exit children and six different practice listeners on the class list. Inspect materials; use large paper replacements for small or damaged tools. Lay out a folded-paper puppet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 2 word-and-pass pairing with [[s01-W3]]. Partners take turns simultaneously, 45 seconds each. Echo one through five slowly. Touch one loose piece during each word; do not use a pictured group.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Introduce a counter as a piece we can move while counting. Name the 5-frame, a mat with five spaces. Show it empty, without filling or counting its spaces. [[s01-H3]] Children explore; overlap, gaps and pieces outside the frame are welcome. Partners share one arrangement each, 45 seconds per turn. Teach no frame-filling rule.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 2's exit cards with feedback. If day 2's tickets showed error A, pass five fresh paper squares, restarting to include a missed square. If error C, use [[s01-F3]]. Choose one action. More than ten affected: whole class; otherwise one small group while others explore freely. Continue descriptive listening for children not yet heard; never infer spoken words from dots.

#### 20 min. Explore counters and mats

Children arrange five counters freely on, across or beside a frame for ten minutes. No one-object-per-cell demonstration. For ten minutes, set the frame aside and count by passing loose counters to a partner. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously; two 45-second turns each take three minutes. Reuse the pieces in new free designs. Watch for a word racing ahead of a hand; slow to one pass, one word, then pause.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Review moving each object once from days 1 and 2 with [[s01-K3-1]].

[[steps:s01-K3-1]]

Use the puppet sequence only if not already used in repair.

**Ask.** [[s01-F3]] Expect: one through five, matched to five distinct passes.

Children complete [[s01-P3-1]]. Hear six practice children, 45 seconds each, while others alternate partners. Record names, words and any help on the class list. The paper count-dot record remains review from day 1.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Keep dotted practice counters in the student-page box, in the folder. Practice stays in the folder; give immediate feedback. Two volunteers at most show a pass, 45 seconds each. Tidy at table trays and wash hands. Put away the exploratory frames.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover the new counter card only at each child's own turn. Read [[s01-X3]] exactly. Each child moves and dots the pieces independently. This counted destination placement is new; do not demonstrate it.

Hear today's six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total. Directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking take 3.5 minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen, with actual words only when heard. Return the paper and feedback on day 4, Return and repair. After-class feedback takes 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without a printer, copy the exact stem and draw four boxes in an irregular column, with four cut-paper counters alongside. Enlarge for access, keeping the same layout. Hand-drawn five-space frames support free exploration.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the exploratory frame; use paper counters. Draw and cover the separate exit card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Pass three strips, one through three.
- **5 min. Explore.** Your child arranges five counters freely on the frame.
- **13 min. Practice.** Put the frame aside. Read [[s01-P3-1]]. Your child passes and dots the paper counters.
  **Say.** "Pass each piece with its word." Expect: one through five, once each.
- **4 min. Repair.** Rehearse ordered words, then pass the pieces again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X3]] and read it without modeling. Keep the marked pieces and note heard words. Return feedback before tomorrow's practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice freely arranges a frame and passes loose counters away from it; the new exit fills four boxes.

Shorter: reduce repeated free designs, keeping the model, record and exit. Longer: make another free arrangement with the same five counters, then pass them to count.

### Words and supports

Counter and 5-frame are new tool names. Review count, object, word, touch, move and partner from earlier days. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Offer tactile outlines, enlarged spaces or adult movement directed by the child; record help. Frame structure and numeral writing are not required.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 explores solid building pieces. If day 3's exit showed error A, include a missed piece in a fresh loose-square count. Error D calls for a pause between actions. Use one five-minute repair, then count building pieces during dismantling; continue the six-child listening rotation.
