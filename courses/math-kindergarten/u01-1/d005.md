# Day 5: Choose a way to keep track

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the leaf exit cards. Teacher-only answer key: d005-key.html, from items.csv. Prepare day 4 car cards with feedback. Select the final six exit children and six different practice listeners on the class list. Set out familiar tools, plain mats and the day 4 build card. Inspect age labels and pieces; replace small or damaged tools with large paper. No pictured tray choices are used in practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 4 dismantling and the earlier touch-and-move methods with [[s01-W5]]. All pairs take 45-second turns simultaneously. Rehearse one through five slowly. These are familiar words and actions; no new method today.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

[[s01-H5]] Children choose a familiar tool and handle it quietly, then show their partner a possible build. Allow one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Two public responses at most; children may pass. Reuse the original five-piece build card from day 4 as a choice, without counting its picture.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 4's exit with feedback. If day 4's tickets showed error A, use [[s01-F5]]. If error B, use day 2's repeat-pass demonstration with five fresh squares. Choose only the first needed action. More than ten affected: whole class; otherwise one small group while others use familiar centers. Do not practice the returned car row.

#### 20 min. Try and compare supports

Pairs use five loose blocks to try familiar touching, passing and a plain mat for ten minutes. Review: all were introduced on days 1 to 4. Keep pieces visible and reach each once. Then choose a support and complete [[s01-P5-1]] with paper pieces for ten minutes.

**Ask.** [[s01-P5-2]] Expect: a reason tied to a demonstrated action, such as seeing each piece separately.

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Each child gets two 45-second turns, one action and one explanation; three minutes per pair, within this part. Give immediate feedback, not a ranking. If many repeat or skip, revisit one familiar method with fewer pieces before returning to five. Never offer tray pictures or leaves here.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Review [[s01-K5-1]] using loose blocks, not exit artwork.

[[steps:s01-K5-1]]

Watch partners test whether the chosen action reaches every object once. Hear six practice children, 45 seconds each, recording actual words and help. Others repeat the familiar chosen action. Review the day 1 count-dot record; let an adult mark exactly as directed if needed.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Keep dotted practice pieces in the student-page box, in the folder. Practice stays in the folder. Two volunteers at most show their chosen action, 45 seconds each. Give one next-step comment; tidy at table trays and wash hands. Keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

At each child's own turn, uncover the new leaf card. Read [[s01-X5]] exactly. Children mark independently; do not request a second answer or an explanation. The middle tray keeps all four leaves fully visible in separate compartments; keep that answer on the teacher page only.

Observe today's six, at most 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for the selection. Wrong selection is error x; it cannot diagnose an omission or repeat. No oral count is claimed. Return cards on day 6, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and all three tray pictures, preserving leaf overlap and the clear organized tray. Cover until the exit. Paper strips, shapes and folded solids replace every handled tool.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces and a plain mat replace the tools. Copy and cover the leaf card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Count five loose pieces using a familiar action.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a familiar low build.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P5-1]]. Your child tries familiar supports, chooses one, then counts and dots.
  **Say.** "Which action helped you reach each piece once?" Expect: pointing to distinct pieces or showing one pass per piece.
- **4 min. Compare.** Repeat using another familiar support.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X5]]; read it without clues. Keep the marked card and return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice tries actual supports with loose blocks; the new exit recognizes an organized leaf tray in pictures.

Shorter: cut repeated builds, keeping comparison, the chosen action and exit. Longer: rearrange the same five pieces and test whether the chosen support still helps.

### Words and supports

Review tool, count, object, touch, move and partner from earlier days. Review keep track from day 1: reach each piece once while counting, as the familiar actions show. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Enlarge without changing overlap; offer tactile outlines. A child may direct an adult's mark; record help.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 recognizes small groups to four and checks by touching. Return day 5's selection and names heard. Error x: compare familiar loose-piece supports in the five-minute repair slot, using a fresh practice layout. Keep any unheard ordered-word sequence not yet seen; continue descriptive listening during guided pairing. Retain chosen supports.
