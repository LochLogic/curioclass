# Day 5: Choose a way to keep track

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and private key-s01.md. Prepare practice-5.md and covered exit-5.md; use its equal-compartment master. Reorder whole panels on alternate copies; record the organized panel's position privately. Bring day 4 car cards and practice with feedback. Set out build-card.md, record.md, cards-centers.md, counting-mat.md and paper-tools.md. Hear roster 25 to 30 in Guided pairing and the exit. Inspect large, blunt tools and non-toxic pencils.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review: day 4 dismantling and the earlier touch-and-move methods with [[s01-W5]]. All pairs take 45-second turns simultaneously. Rehearse one through five slowly. These are familiar words and actions; no new method today.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Say: "These pictures and quantities are invented for practice." [[s01-H5]] Children choose a familiar tool and handle it quietly, then show their partner a possible build. Allow one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Two public responses at most; children may pass. Reuse the original five-piece build card from day 4 as a choice, without counting its picture.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 4's car cards and written task s01-P4-1: distribute and read feedback aloud, one minute. If day 4's tickets showed error A, use [[s01-F5]] with fresh squares; error B, review day 2's repeated pass and correction. Show right before wrong, one minute. Choose the first obstruction only; more than ten sharing it means whole class, otherwise one small group. Retry [[s01-P5-1]] with fresh loose pieces simultaneously, two minutes; collect, one minute. Queue other errors. No returned car-row practice.

#### 20 min. Try and compare supports

Pairs use five loose blocks to try familiar touching, passing and a plain mat for ten minutes. Review: all were introduced on days 1 to 4. Keep pieces visible and reach each once. Then choose a support and complete [[s01-P5-1]] with paper pieces for ten minutes.

Show a correct five-block count. Then the puppet passes only four, leaving one block untouched.

**Ask.** [[s01-P5-2]] Expect: a familiar touch or pass reaching every block once, one through five. Children correct the supplied action; give feedback after the attempt.

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Each child gets two 45-second turns, one chosen action and one puppet correction; three minutes per pair, within this part. Give immediate feedback, not a ranking. If many repeat or skip, revisit one familiar method with fewer pieces before returning to five. Never offer tray pictures or leaves here.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Review [[s01-K5-1]] using loose blocks, not exit artwork.

[[steps:s01-K5-1]]

Watch partners test whether the chosen action reaches every object once. Hear initially unheard roster 25 to 30, one 45-second counting turn each, while other pairs continue. Reserve 4.5 minutes plus half a minute recording on record.md. Later words are new evidence, not day 1 results; absences go to day 6 Familiar centers. An adult may mark exactly as directed; record help.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Keep dotted practice pieces in the student-page box. Collect practice with the exit for day 6 Return and repair; file dated work after its retry. Two volunteers at most show their chosen action, 45 seconds each. Give one next-step comment; keep pieces out of mouths and tidy at table trays. Use the room's usual hand-cleaning routine. Keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Allow directions one minute, independent paper work two, six observed turns 4.5, and collection half a minute. Uncover exit-5.md for each child's own response; the six wait with covered cards until their turns and respond once. Read [[s01-X5]] exactly. Children mark alone; no extra count or explanation. Use key-s01.md and the privately recorded panel positions.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for the selection. An unexplained wrong selection is error x, not evidence of an omission or repeat. Claim no oral count. Return every practice and exit paper on day 6, Return and repair. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty sets, thirty seconds each, including retry checks.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the exact stem and all three tray pictures, preserving leaf overlap and the clear organized tray. Cover until the exit. Paper strips, shapes and folded solids replace every handled tool.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper pieces and a plain mat replace the tools. Copy and cover the leaf card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Count five loose pieces using a familiar action.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a familiar low build.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P5-1]]. Your child tries familiar supports, chooses one, then counts and dots.
  **Say.** "Use your chosen way to count these pieces." Expect: one through five, one indication per piece.
- **4 min. Compare.** Repeat using another familiar support.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X5]]; read it without clues. Keep the marked card and return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut repeated builds, keeping comparison, the chosen action and exit. Longer: rearrange the same five pieces and test whether the chosen support still helps.

### Words and supports

Review: tool, count, object, number word, touch, move, cube and partner from day 1; pattern block from day 2; solid from day 4. Review: Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2. Review: keep track from day 1, reach each piece once while counting, as the familiar actions show. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Enlarge without changing overlap; offer tactile outlines. A child may direct an adult's mark; record help.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 recognizes small groups to four and checks by touching. Return day 5's selection and names heard. Error x: compare familiar loose-piece supports in the five-minute repair slot, using a fresh practice layout. Keep any unheard ordered-word sequence not yet seen; hear up to six absentees in day 6 Familiar centers. Retain chosen supports.
