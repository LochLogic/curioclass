# Unit 1 teacher key

Teacher only. Keep this file off student pages, cards and displays. Read each stem aloud as printed. One scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Judge the observed action, not just a final total. Blank or unheard evidence is not yet seen; x means an unexplained response, with no diagnosed repair group. No item requires independent reading. K.CC.B.4a counts spoken words paired with distinct object actions; no numeral notation is tested.

## s01-X1

Read: "Move each button into the tray with one counting word for each move."

Answer: Four distinct button moves: one, two, three, four; one dot per moved button.

Look-fors: Observe one move per button and one ordered word per move. | Four different dotted pieces in the tray support correspondence only. | Record actual heard sequence separately; unheard sequence is not yet seen. | Scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen.

Samples: Shown: four separate moves, saying one, two, three, four. | Not yet seen: one button remains above the tray, error A.

Wrong responses: One button remains above the tray. (A): Observed omission; three of four were moved.; One button returns and is counted again. (B): Observed repeat; a fifth word does not mean five buttons.

## s01-X2

Read: "Count your crayons by touching each picture."

Answer: On either chosen form: one, two, three, four; one dot on each crayon.

Look-fors: Choice of card is unscored. | Observe one touch per distinct picture and ordered words. | One pencil dot records each touch; paper cannot prove unheard words. | Scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen.

Samples: Shown: all four pictures touched once with one through four heard. | Not yet seen: says one, two, five, seven on four distinct touches, error C.

Wrong responses: One crayon untouched. (A): Observed omission, one pictured object lacks a word.; One, two, five, seven on four distinct touches. (C): The words are not the standard sequence; the touches are one per picture.

## s01-X5

Look-fors: Mark only the organized tray, identified by its four fully visible separate leaves. | Reordering whole panels does not change the correct picture; record its position on the teacher class list. | Selection shows recognition of a tracking support, not an oral count. | Scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen.

## s01-X6

## s01-X7

## s01-X9

## s01-X10
