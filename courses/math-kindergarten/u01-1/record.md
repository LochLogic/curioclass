# Unit 1 counting record

Teacher record. Use one scale only: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Write the words actually heard, not an inferred count. A paper dot records an indicated object; it cannot establish unheard number-word order. A correct total cannot reveal whether an object was omitted or revisited. Record observed action before assigning A, B, C, D or E; x means the action is unknown, with no repair group inferred.

The roster assigns six exit listeners daily. All children return paper. Children not heard on an earlier exit remain not yet seen for that spoken component; later hearing is dated new evidence. Initial unheard children 7 to 12, 13 to 18, 19 to 24 and 25 to 30 are heard during Guided pairing on days 2, 3, 4 and 5; each group takes 4.5 of ten minutes. Day 6 Familiar centers reserves another 4.5 minutes for up to six absentees.

Unit conferences use three separate 45-second asks, at most one 45-second retry, and 30 seconds for feedback/reset, 3.5 minutes total. Eight children take 28 of 30 center minutes on days 15 to 17. Day 18 hears the remaining six in 21 minutes, then at most five absentees, eleven total in 38.5 minutes. Keep other centers independent; never run a second teacher-led repair during conferences. Cover the assessment pieces from other children. Missing evidence beyond that capacity stays not yet seen and goes to day 19's missing-name list.

Give a brief private note during the conference, offer one retry on the first obstruction and preserve both performances. Record any cue. Classify the three earlier batches during day 18 Close records, three minutes a batch, then one minute filing. Classify first-made day 18 evidence before day 19, ten teacher minutes outside class. Return records on day 19 with feedback inside the one ten-minute Counting centers response reservation, shared with day 17 papers. Day 20 has the unit plan's ten-minute fresh-transfer reservation; keep initial evidence, feedback and later revision dated separately.
