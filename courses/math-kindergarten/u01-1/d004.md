# Day 4: Build with solid pieces

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and private key-s01.md. Prepare practice-4.md, build-card.md and covered exit-4.md. Bring day 3 counter cards, pieces and practice with feedback. Use record.md, cards-centers.md, counting-mat.md and paper-tools.md. Hear roster 19 to 24 in Guided pairing and the exit. Gather five large solid models per pair, or clean boxes, tubes and folded paper solids. Check age labels, edges and non-toxic pencils.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review: day 3 touch-and-word pairing using [[s01-W4]]. All partners take a 45-second turn. Echo one through five slowly; no numeral reading. Stop after each word until a finger reaches the next loose piece.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Say: "These pictures and quantities are invented for practice." Introduce solid pieces as tools we can hold and build upward with. [[s01-H4]] Children explore safely at table height. Partners show an idea in two 45-second turns, simultaneously. Hear two public responses at most; children may pass. Do not require formal shape names or properties.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 3's counter cards and written task s01-P3-1: distribute and read feedback aloud, one minute. If day 3's tickets showed error A, review the puppet's omission and correction with fresh squares; error D, model pausing until the next square before saying another word. Show right before wrong, one minute. Choose the first obstruction only; more than ten sharing it means whole class, otherwise one small group. Retry [[s01-P4-1]] using a fresh low build simultaneously, two minutes; accept rough or scribed outlines. Collect, one minute. Queue other errors; keep box-filling cards out of practice.

#### 20 min. Build with solids

For ten minutes pairs build freely with five pieces. In the next ten minutes, build from build-card.md: three pieces along the bottom, two above. Copy its low arrangement approximately, using the solid models; discuss no formal geometry. Then dismantle while passing pieces with counting words. This combines exploration, building to a model and counting actions.

All fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes, leaving handling and rebuilding time. Watch for a piece counted while still in the build and counted again after passing. Pause and restart if many repeat. Keep builds low and materials out of mouths.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Show [[s01-K4-1]].

[[steps:s01-K4-1]]

Show a correct five-square touch count, then the puppet's two words on one touch. Let children correct it before giving feedback.

**Ask.** [[s01-F4]] Expect: one distinct touch for each ordered word.

Children complete [[s01-P4-1]]. Model a rough drawing of the practice build, not a fixed picture row. An adult may scribe outlines. Hear initially unheard roster 19 to 24, one 45-second counting turn each, while other pairs continue. Reserve 4.5 minutes plus half a minute recording on record.md. Later words are new evidence, not day 1 results; absences go to day 6 Familiar centers.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Collect practice drawings with the exit for day 5 Return and repair; file dated work after its retry. Give immediate spoken feedback. Two volunteers at most show one counted piece, 45 seconds each. Return tools to table trays; use the room's usual hand-cleaning routine. Keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Allow directions one minute, independent paper work two, six individual turns 4.5, and collection half a minute. Uncover exit-4.md for each child's own response; the six wait with covered cards until their turns and count once. Read [[s01-X4]] exactly. Use the familiar Paper count-dot record during each indication; no partner help or worked car count.

Record actual words, actions and help on record.md. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen separately for pairing and sequence. Dots cannot establish unheard words. Return every practice and exit paper on day 5, Return and repair. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty sets, thirty seconds each, including retry checks.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the build card and student frame; draw four separate cars in a line under the exact exit stem. Cover until the exit. Folded paper solids keep building three-dimensional; flat drawings alone do not replace that exploration.

### At home

**Swap.** Use clean boxes or folded paper solids. Draw and cover the car exit card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Touch five loose squares, one word each.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a low five-piece build like the card.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P4-1]]. Help with outlines if needed; your child passes and dots each piece.
  **Say.** "Pass each piece with its word." Expect: one through five, each once.
- **4 min. Repair.** Rehearse one touch, one word, then pause.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X4]]. Read it without a demonstration; retain the paper and note heard words. Return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated building, keeping the model, drawing, dismantling and exit. Longer: change the five-piece build and count by dismantling again.

### Words and supports

Solid: a piece that takes up space and can be built with. Review: count, object, number word, touch, move, keep track, tool and partner from day 1. Review: Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Enlarge pictures or use tactile outlines. An adult may mark as directed; record help. Judge pairing separately from drawing skill.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 chooses among familiar touching, moving and plain-mat supports. If day 4's exit showed error A, include the missed piece in a new loose-square count; error B, stop a repeat pass. Use one five-minute repair. Keep chosen supports available and continue the listening rotation.
