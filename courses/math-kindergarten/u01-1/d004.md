# Day 4: Build with solid pieces

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare five safe solid models per pair; use clean boxes, tubes or folded paper solids with no sharp edges. Set out the original build card on Side 1. Hide car exit cards. Teacher-only answer key: d004-key.html, from items.csv. Bring day 3 counter cards with feedback and the class list. Choose the next six exit children and six different practice listeners. Replace small or damaged tools with large paper pieces.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 3 touch-and-word pairing using [[s01-W4]]. All partners take a 45-second turn. Echo one through five slowly; no numeral reading. Stop after each word until a finger reaches the next loose piece.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Introduce solid pieces as tools we can hold and build upward with. [[s01-H4]] Children explore safely at table height. Partners show an idea in two 45-second turns, simultaneously. Hear two public responses at most; children may pass. Do not require formal shape names or properties.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 3's exit cards and feedback. If day 3's tickets showed error A, restart with five new paper squares to include the missed piece. If error D, use [[s01-F4]]. Choose one action: whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one small group while others explore solids. Keep counted box-filling cards out of practice.

#### 20 min. Build with solids

For ten minutes pairs build freely with five pieces. Then use the original five-piece build card: three pieces along the bottom, two above. Copy its low arrangement approximately, using the solid models; discuss no formal geometry. Dismantle while passing pieces with counting words for ten minutes. This combines exploration, building to a model and counting actions.

All fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes, leaving handling and rebuilding time. Watch for a piece counted while still in the build and counted again after passing. Pause and restart if many repeat. Keep builds low and materials out of mouths.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Show [[s01-K4-1]].

[[steps:s01-K4-1]]

Use the puppet case only if not already used in repair.

**Ask.** [[s01-F4]] Expect: one distinct touch for each ordered word.

Children complete [[s01-P4-1]]. Model a rough drawing of the practice build, not a fixed picture row. An adult may scribe outlines. Hear six practice children, 45 seconds each; record actual words and help. Others work with partners. Review the plain counting mat and count-dot record from day 1.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Practice drawings stay in the folder with immediate spoken feedback. Two volunteers at most show one counted piece, 45 seconds each. Return tools to table trays and wash hands. Keep pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

At each child's own turn, uncover the new car row. Read [[s01-X4]] exactly; each child dots each indicated car using the familiar mark routine. Give no worked count of the row.

Hear today's six, 45 seconds each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking take 3.5 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper indicates correspondence; mark unheard sequence not yet seen. Return every card with feedback on day 5, Return and repair. Finish feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without printing, copy the build card and student frame; draw four separate cars in a line under the exact exit stem. Cover until the exit. Folded paper solids keep building three-dimensional; flat drawings alone do not replace that exploration.

### At home

**Swap.** Use clean boxes or folded paper solids. Draw and cover the car exit card.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Touch five loose squares, one word each.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a low five-piece build like the card.
- **8 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P4-1]]. Help with outlines if needed; your child passes and dots each piece.
  **Say.** "Pass each piece with its word." Expect: one through five, each once.
- **4 min. Repair.** Rehearse one touch, one word, then pause.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X4]]. Read it without a demonstration; retain the paper and note heard words. Return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated building, keeping the model, drawing, dismantling and exit. Longer: change the five-piece build and count by dismantling again.

### Words and supports

Solid: a piece that takes up space and can be built with. Review count, touch, move and partner from earlier days. Read all printed lines aloud at 18 point. Enlarge pictures or use tactile outlines. An adult may mark as directed; record help. Judge pairing separately from drawing skill.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 chooses among familiar touching, moving and plain-mat supports. If day 4's exit showed error A, include the missed piece in a new loose-square count; error B, stop a repeat pass. Use one five-minute repair. Keep chosen supports available and continue the listening rotation.
