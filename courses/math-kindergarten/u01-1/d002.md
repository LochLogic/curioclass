# Day 2: Explore pattern blocks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut apart the two crayon masters; cover both before the child chooses one. Teacher-only answer key: d002-key.html, from items.csv. Bring day 1 button cards with feedback and the class list. Select the next six children for the exit and a different six for practice listening. Use large, sound pieces, kept out of mouths.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 1 partner turns and word-and-pass pairing with [[s01-W2]]. All pairs act simultaneously, then exchange. Echo one through five slowly while touching five loose paper shapes. A child may listen before joining; no timer or numeral reading.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Show pattern blocks, flat pieces for building designs. Children handle them quietly, then [[s01-H2]]. All fifteen pairs work simultaneously: one 45-second turn per partner takes 90 seconds per pair. Invite two public responses at most; pointing or passing is welcome. Record a child's design idea with a quick teacher drawing, without formal shape names.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 1's exit with feedback. If day 1's tickets showed error A, demonstrate the omission repair from day 1 with five fresh paper squares. If error B, use [[s01-F2]]. Choose only the earliest needed action. More than ten children: whole class; otherwise one small group while others explore. Never work the button exit again. An unheard sequence remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Explore and build

Pairs explore designs for ten minutes. Then build with five loose blocks; dismantle by passing one per counting word for ten minutes. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously; two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes. Repeat with a changed low design. Check that a child can wait for the next object before saying the next word. If many rush, reduce to one piece, then rebuild toward five. No fixed-picture counting occurs.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Show [[s01-K2-1]].

[[steps:s01-K2-1]]

Review the plain mat as a work surface. Children try touching loose pieces, then passing them; these are different actions for the same pairing. Use [[s01-F2]] only if that repair was not already used today.

**Ask.** [[s01-F2]] Expect: each square once, then stop.

Complete [[s01-P2-1]]. Hear six practice children, 45 seconds each, while others exchange roles. Note words beside the names actually heard; continue this rotation on days 3 to 6.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Place dotted shapes in the student-page box; practice stays in the folder. Give immediate feedback on one pass per word. Two volunteers at most, 45 seconds each, show what helped. Tidy at table trays and wash hands.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

At each child's own turn, offer two covered cards; let the child choose before revealing one. Read [[s01-X2]] exactly. Tell children to leave their familiar pencil dot during each touch. All work independently; hear today's six individually, 45 seconds each, totaling 4.5 minutes. Directions, paper work, collection and brief marking use 3.5 minutes.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record number-word order only when heard. A correct dotted card supports touches, not unheard words. Return every card on day 3, Return and repair. Finish written feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes.

### Other rooms

Paper shapes do the same work without blocks. Without printing, copy the page frame and exact exit stem; draw four crayons in a curve on one card and a slanting column on another. Cover both. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut-paper shapes replace blocks. Draw and cover the two crayon cards.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Pass one strip with one word.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a low paper design.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P2-1]]. Your child passes and dots the five shapes; watch each action.
  **Say.** "Pass each shape with its word." Expect: one through five, each once.
- **4 min. Repair.** Repeat a piece deliberately; restart without the repeat.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Offer the covered choice for [[s01-X2]]. Read it; record the words heard without supplying them. Return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice builds and passes loose shapes; the new exit touch-counts a chosen fixed crayon picture.

Shorter: cut repeated designs, keeping the model, paper record and exit. Longer: remake a design with the same five pieces and count while dismantling.

### Words and supports

Pattern block: a flat piece for making designs. Review count, touch, move, object and partner from day 1. Read all stems aloud at 18 point. Enlarge pictures; a child may direct an adult's dot, touch or move. Record the help. A home-language count or familiar communication aid can show the sequence.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 explores counters and a 5-frame. If day 2's exit showed error A, include the missed piece in a new loose-square count. Error C calls for ordered-word rehearsal before pairing. Use one five-minute repair only; continue hearing children whose words remain not yet seen.
