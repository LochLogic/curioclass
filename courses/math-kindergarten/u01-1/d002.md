# Day 2: Explore pattern blocks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and teacher-only key-s01.md; keep the answer key private. Prepare practice-2.md and cut apart exit-2.md's two covered forms. Bring day 1 button cards and practice with private feedback. Use record.md, cards-centers.md, counting-mat.md and paper-tools.md for large stand-ins. Hear roster 7 to 12 in Guided pairing and at the exit. Use large, blunt tools and non-toxic pencils; replace damaged pieces.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review: day 1 Partner turns and word-and-pass pairing with [[s01-W2]]. All pairs act simultaneously, then exchange. Echo one through five slowly while touching five loose paper shapes. A child may listen before joining; no timer or numeral reading.

#### 8 min. Explore and notice

Say: "These pictures and quantities are invented for practice." Show pattern blocks, flat pieces for building designs. Children handle them quietly, then [[s01-H2]]. All fifteen pairs work simultaneously: one 45-second turn per partner takes 90 seconds per pair. Invite two public responses at most; pointing or passing is welcome. Record a child's design idea with a quick teacher drawing, without formal shape names.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 1's button cards and written task s01-P1-1: distribute and read feedback aloud, one minute. If day 1's tickets showed error A, review the puppet's omission and correction with fresh squares; error B, show a repeat and correction. Model right before wrong, one minute. Choose only the first obstruction; more than ten sharing it means whole class, otherwise one small group. Retry [[s01-P2-1]] with fresh loose paper shapes simultaneously, two minutes; collect, one minute. Queue other errors. Record later words as new evidence, never as day 1's unheard sequence.

#### 20 min. Explore and build

Pairs explore designs for ten minutes. Then build with five loose blocks; dismantle by passing one per counting word for ten minutes. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously; two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes. Repeat with a changed low design. Check that a child can wait for the next object before saying the next word. If many rush, reduce to one piece, then rebuild toward five. No fixed-picture counting occurs.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Show [[s01-K2-1]].

[[steps:s01-K2-1]]

Review the plain mat as a work surface. Children try touching loose pieces, then passing them; these are different actions for the same pairing. Show a correct five-square pass before the puppet repeats a square. Let children try the correction before giving feedback.

**Ask.** [[s01-F2]] Expect: each square once, then stop.

Complete [[s01-P2-1]]. Hear initially unheard roster 7 to 12, one 45-second counting turn each, while other pairs continue. Reserve 4.5 minutes plus half a minute recording within this part. Use fresh pieces; record today's words and help. Absences go to day 6 Familiar centers.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Place dotted shapes in the student-page box; collect it with the exit for day 3 Return and repair. Give immediate feedback on one pass per word; file dated work after its retry. Two volunteers at most, 45 seconds each, show what helped. Keep pieces out of mouths. Tidy at table trays; use the room's usual hand-cleaning routine.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Allow directions one minute, independent paper work two, six individual turns 4.5, and collection half a minute. Offer two covered forms from exit-2.md; each child chooses before seeing one. Read [[s01-X2]] exactly. Use the familiar Paper count-dot record during each touch. The six wait with covered cards until their turns and count only once. No partner help.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record actual words and actions on record.md; dots cannot prove unheard words. Return every practice and exit paper on day 3, Return and repair. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for thirty paper sets, thirty seconds each, including retry checks.

### Other rooms

Paper shapes do the same work without blocks. Without printing, copy the page frame and exact exit stem; draw four crayons in a curve on one card and a slanting column on another. Cover both. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut-paper shapes replace blocks. Draw and cover the two crayon cards.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Pass one strip with one word.
- **5 min. Explore.** Make a low paper design.
- **13 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P2-1]]. Your child passes and dots the five shapes; watch each action.
  **Say.** "Pass each shape with its word." Expect: one through five, each once.
- **4 min. Repair.** Repeat a piece deliberately; restart without the repeat.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Offer the covered choice for [[s01-X2]]. Read it; record the words heard without supplying them. Return feedback before the next practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut repeated designs, keeping the model, paper record and exit. Longer: remake a design with the same five pieces and count while dismantling.

### Words and supports

Pattern block: a flat piece for making designs. Review: count, touch, move, object, number word, keep track, tool and partner from day 1. Review: Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record; use the same cue and actions. Return and repair means reading feedback and trying fresh work. Read all stems aloud at 18 point. Enlarge pictures; a child may direct an adult's dot, touch or move. Record the help. A home-language count or familiar communication aid can show the sequence.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 explores counters and a 5-frame. If day 2's exit showed error A, include the missed piece in a new loose-square count. Error C calls for ordered-word rehearsal before pairing. Use one five-minute repair only; continue hearing children whose words remain not yet seen.
