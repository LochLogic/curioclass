# Day 1: Explore cubes and count together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the button circles from Side 2, keeping its tray and printed stem. Bag each child's pieces separately, face down. Teacher-only answer key: d001-key.html, from items.csv; keep it away from children. Rule a class list: day, name heard, actual words, actions, support, next step. Select six different children today; assign the other groups to days 2 to 5. Check age labels; replace small, sharp or damaged pieces with large paper.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Pairs explore three cubes without a counting prerequisite. Introduce a tool as something that helps us do something. [[s01-H1]] Accept a build or gesture. Invite quiet thinking, then one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Children may pass. Observe spontaneous words and actions; do not score them. Count objects, never children or their features.

#### 5 min. Counting words

Whole class watches [[s01-K1-1]].

[[steps:s01-K1-1]]

Say the words slowly; children echo or gesture. Begin with one before five. Touch means put a finger on an object; move means change its place. Do not print number symbols.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

There is no earlier day or paper to return. Introduce partner turns: one handles, one watches, then exchange. Model offering a piece and waiting for the partner's hand. Practice passing one cube back and forth with its single word. Introduce the plain counting mat as a work surface, not a set of destinations.

#### 20 min. Explore and share

Pairs make low constructions, first with one, then five cubes. Give ten minutes to building and ten to dismantling with the word-and-pass action of [[s01-P1-1]], using loose pieces before paper. All fifteen pairs work together: two 45-second turns each take three minutes. Repeat slowly with rearranged loose pieces. Watch for a piece passed twice, error B. Pause and restart together if many lose their place. Keep tools on the table and out of mouths.

#### 10 min. Guided pairing

Demonstrate the paper puppet's omission in [[s01-F1]].

**Ask.** [[s01-F1]] Expect: restart, including every square once. Show the repair only after children try.

Children complete [[s01-P1-1]] with their own strips. Partners watch without supplying words. Model the pencil dot again if needed: it remembers one indicated piece. Hear short practice turns; these observations are descriptive. A mark cannot prove an unheard word.

#### 4 min. Record and tidy

Put the marked strips in the student-page box. Practice stays in the folder, with immediate spoken feedback. Two volunteers at most show a pass, 45 seconds each. Everyone returns tools to table trays together; wash hands after handling. Leave pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Give each child the covered button card and pieces for their own turn. Read its exact stem aloud: [[s01-X1]]. Children move and dot each button during its word, without partners helping. The dot routine is familiar; this destination task is new.

Hear today's six individually, 45 seconds each: 4.5 minutes. The remaining 3.5 minutes cover directions, simultaneous paper work, collection and brief marking. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record words only when heard. Unheard sequence: not yet seen. Return all cards with one feedback note on day 2, Return and repair. Finish paper feedback after class, 30 children times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. Without a printer, copy the two page frames and the exact exit stem; draw four button circles above a tray, then cut the circles. Keep the card hidden until the exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper strips replace cubes; folded paper replaces the puppet. Draw the button exit card if needed.

**Skip.** Class sharing and the observation list.

- **3 min. Explore.** Let your child join or arrange strips.
- **5 min. Model.** Pass one strip saying one; then five, one through five.
- **8 min. Practice.** Read [[s01-P1-1]]. Take turns passing; your child makes the dots.
  **Say.** "Pass each strip with its counting word." Expect: one through five, each strip once.
- **4 min. Repair.** Omit a strip; restart together, including it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s01-X1]]. Read without demonstrating. Keep the pieces and note the words heard. Return feedback before tomorrow's practice.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated building, keeping pairing, the paper record and exit. Longer: rearrange the same five loose strips; partners watch for one pass per word.

### Words and supports

Count: say number words with objects. Object: one thing. Number word: a spoken counting word. Cube: today's joining piece. Partner: the person taking turns with you. Point while naming each. Read every printed line aloud at 18 point. Accept signs or aided directions; an adult may move and dot exactly as directed, recording help.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 explores pattern blocks. If day 1's exit showed error A, include the omitted piece in a fresh square demonstration; error B, stop a repeat pass. Use the first needed repair only. Hear the next six and continue descriptive sequence observations for those not yet heard.
