# Day 131: I can build with solids and show flat faces.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print both prompts and one picture on the half slip; no duplicate. Prepare practice-131, partial rectangular face traces and low adult-started oblong box structures. Seal exit-131; give every child their own cube/cone models only at exit. Select eight unheard conference children. Keep remaining twenty-four F3/F4 masters shielded until paper time. Use large models without staples, residue, sharp edges, glass or metal cans. No mouthing/throwing; spheres loose on mats, builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W120-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 130's written tasks with annotated feedback in this existing slot. Keep first marks beside later revisions; no extra interview or minutes.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 130 tickets, Exit boxes and written tasks in the practice/game packet with written feedback. If many day 130 tickets showed error E, reversed depth: [[s07-K131-depth]] [[steps:s07-K131-depth]]. Fresh retry and original-record revision share five minutes. Error F and other needs stay recorded for days 135/136; never repeat reserved cone scene.

#### 14 min. Connected work

Five minutes revise earlier final-window written feedback. Eight minutes complete F4a matching and retained F3 target-mark revision for the remaining twenty-four children; the first six revise their day 130 work. One minute synthesize. [[s07-P131]] No additional interview is added here.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

Keep day 127's unheard exit name and day 130's unheard exit language on the record. The new naming evidence belongs to the funded F4 component, never retroactive exit credit; the early F3 position roster is already complete. 

Three minutes cover setup and tidy. Use 20 conference minutes for children 7 to 14, 2.5 minutes each. Follow conference-card and naming-cards-130: F2f whole/face description, cylinder name, cone name, three separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds reset. There is no new position interview. Five minutes familiar games for all children; waiting children continue independently, then resume their interrupted paper work. Missing words stay not yet seen; matching never establishes names. [[s07-K131-face]] [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal both exit-master questions together; collect both in this slot.

Reveal the exit-131 model picture, sealed second exit question and individual cube/cone sets now. Read [[s07-X131a]], wait for independent builds and drawings, then [[s07-X131b]]. Children sketch their build on the half slip, then mark both contact surfaces on the Exit box picture or trace them in its blank area; they may separate their build to inspect hidden contact. Read exact stems, one at a time. No construction demonstration. Observe three children per ask, six at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes; others work simultaneously. Remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, record completion and collection. Retain builds if drawings leave evidence unclear.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Collect the half slip and Exit box together. Return both with the practice/game packet day 132 Return and repair. Return window feedback to day 132 Connected work, five minutes. Build errors alone never establish error B without evidence that a whole cube was called a flat square.

### Other rooms

Paper and real models are the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only. Without a printer, hand-draw named masters with blank response spaces. Clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones, adult-cut shapes and paper counters replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; record access separately from mathematical help. Per-child lightweight exit models prevent a shared-equipment queue.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean boxes and adult-made paper solids replace kits; paper squares replace counters, crayons replace pencils. Hand-draw masters; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Public turns and rotations. Complete reserved F3/F4 once in another sitting if needed.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens/ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Move a named ball behind a box; revise yesterday's feedback.
- **5 min. Build.** [[s07-P131]] **Say.** "Repair this started structure." Expect the oblong box resting on a flat supporting face.
- **9 min. Paper.** Finish one partial face trace; play the familiar game. Preserve five minutes to revise earlier written feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the half slip and sealed second exit question. [[s07-X131a]] [[s07-X131b]] Child builds and draws alone, then marks square top/circular base. Adult steadies only as directed. Record support; return written feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated box repairs, preserving paper preparation, later revision, conferences, games and exit. Longer: finish another partial trace on a known oblong face, without contact comparisons.

### Words and supports

Review model, day 72; above/below/beside/next to/in front of/behind, day 45. Read every word aloud at 18 point; independent reading zero. Tactile models, home language, signs, directed adult marks and dictated labels are welcome. Public talk may be passed; note mathematical help separately from usual access.

### Tomorrow

Day 132 counts solid pieces and compares features. If many day 131 tickets call the whole cube a square, error B, its five-minute Return and repair wraps a different known cube and repairs a supplied label. Other needs remain for 135/136. Return tickets, practice/game packet and window feedback to their named parts. Continue assigned unheard name/place domains through F3/F4, without crediting earlier unheard exits.
