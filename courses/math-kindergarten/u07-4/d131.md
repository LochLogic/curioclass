# Day 131: I can build with solids and show flat faces.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Prepare practice-131, partial rectangular face traces and low adult-started oblong box structures. Seal exit-131; give every child their own cube/cone models only at exit. Select eight unheard conference children. Keep remaining twenty-four F3/F4 masters shielded until paper time. Use large models without staples, residue, sharp edges, glass or metal cans. No mouthing/throwing; spheres loose on mats, builds low.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W131-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W131-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 130 tickets, Exit boxes and practice/game packet with written feedback. If many day 130 tickets showed error E, reversed depth: [[s07-K131-depth]] [[steps:s07-K131-depth]]. Fresh retry and original-record revision share five minutes. Error F and other needs stay recorded for days 135/136; never repeat reserved cone scene.

#### 14 min. Connected work

Eight minutes: remaining twenty-four independently complete locations-131, four minutes, and solid-pairs-131, four. Read each exact stem on its named master separately: s07-F31 s07-F32 s07-F33 s07-F34 s07-F35 s07-F36, then s07-F4a. Pause after each. Yesterday's six quietly revise earlier written feedback, no new sitting. No peer coaching.

Next five minutes: everyone revises day 130's classification/comparison papers and recorded conference words from written feedback. Keep original marks and dated corrections. Use only actual words, never backfill unheard descriptions. Later teacher judgment uses this revision.

One minute: connect yesterday's classroom descriptions to low stable building. Review face/cylinder/cone/sphere, day 127; cube, day 1; flat/square/rectangle, day 45; curved, day 46; whole, day 87. Read [[s07-P131]], then show one adult-started oblong box repair. A flat support can touch another flat part. Never model exit's cone-on-cube construction.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

Three minutes setup/tidy, twenty conference minutes, five whole-class game minutes. Others repair supplied low oblong box structures and record the change on practice-131. Read [[s07-K131-face]] and finish a supplied isolated face trace on construction-record. Partners alternate builder/checker; sphere stays loose. If a child treats the whole as flat, steady it while they feel its depth; no whole/face question pair.

Eight assigned conferences, no repeated secure domains.

Use conference-card with assigned children only: s07-F2f, s07-F3s, s07-F4b, 45 seconds each, plus 15 seconds transition, 2.5 minutes per child. Actual cubic box/square panel, retained marked locations and solid pairs appear only at that child's turn. Record names heard and words. F1 reason uses F2 evidence only if actually supplied. Unfinished components remain not yet seen. Fresh domain evidence never fills an unheard day 127 or 130 exit retrospectively.

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Read separately: [[s07-G131-1]] [[s07-G131-2]] [[s07-G131-3]] [[s07-G131-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Expect 2, 3, 4, 3 objects/marks on game-record. Immediate feedback: error I counts arrivals only; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-131 and fresh individual cube/cone sets now. Read [[s07-X131a]], wait for independent builds and drawings, then [[s07-X131b]]. Children mark both touching surfaces on their own paper record; they may separate their build to inspect hidden contact. Read exact stems, one at a time. No construction demonstration. Observe three children per ask, six at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes; others work simultaneously. Remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, record completion and collection. Retain builds if drawings leave evidence unclear.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Return exit and practice/game packet day 132 Return and repair. Return window feedback to day 132 Connected work, five minutes. Build errors alone never establish error B without evidence that a whole cube was called a flat square.

### Other rooms

Paper and real models are the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only. Without a printer, hand-draw named masters with blank response spaces. Clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones, adult-cut shapes and paper counters replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; record access separately from mathematical help. Per-child lightweight exit models prevent a shared-equipment queue.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean boxes and adult-made paper solids replace kits; paper squares replace counters, crayons replace pencils. Hand-draw masters; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Public turns and rotations. Complete reserved F3/F4 once in another sitting if needed.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens/ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Move a named ball behind a box; revise yesterday's feedback.
- **5 min. Build.** [[s07-P131]] **Say.** "Repair this started structure." Expect the oblong box resting on a flat supporting face.
- **9 min. Paper.** Finish one partial face trace; play the familiar game. Preserve five minutes to revise earlier written feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-131. [[s07-X131a]] [[s07-X131b]] Child builds and draws alone, then marks square top/circular base. Adult steadies only as directed. Record support; return written feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated box repairs, preserving paper preparation, later revision, conferences, games and exit. Longer: finish another partial trace on a known oblong face, without contact comparisons.

### Words and supports

Review model, day 72; above/below/beside/next to/in front of/behind, day 45. Read every word aloud at 18 point; independent reading zero. Tactile models, home language, signs, directed adult marks and dictated labels are welcome. Public talk may be passed; note mathematical help separately from usual access.

### Tomorrow

Day 132 counts solid pieces and compares features. If many day 131 tickets call the whole cube a square, error B, its five-minute Return and repair wraps a different known cube and repairs a supplied label. Other needs remain for 135/136. Return tickets, practice/game packet and window feedback to their named parts. Continue assigned unheard name/place domains through F3/F4, without crediting earlier unheard exits.
