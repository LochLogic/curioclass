# Day 134: I can show a change on my shape paper.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Arrange retained papers, dated feedback, revisions and recorded words by child. Keep unused masters sealed. Bring game-record, construction-record and large safe models: no staples, sharp edges, glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Low builds, spheres on mats. No new ticket or interview.

### The day

#### 10 min. Feedback revision

First five minutes: return day 133 exit and practice/game packet. Next five: return final-window feedback. Children revise earlier papers from written notes, preserving originals. Error D, color-only fit, uses the written feature note, not a new lesson. Other unresolved letters remain recorded. Read all print aloud; no independent reading or new teaching.

#### 10 min. Close retained records

Children make familiar independent paper choices. Teacher reviews thirty records, twenty seconds each: F1 two total minutes, F2 four, F3 two, F4 two. Judge only earlier-made work with written feedback and later revision; use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

Retained references only, never readminister: [[s07-F1]]; [[s07-F2a]] [[s07-F2b]] [[s07-F2c]] [[s07-F2d]] [[s07-F2e]] [[s07-F2f]]; [[s07-F31]] [[s07-F32]] [[s07-F33]] [[s07-F34]] [[s07-F35]] [[s07-F36]] [[s07-F3s]]; [[s07-F4a]] [[s07-F4b]]. Carry earlier flat names/arithmetic only from actual records. Missing or unheard remains not yet seen; marks never establish unspoken words.

#### 20 min. Familiar centers

Fifteen minutes finish chosen earlier products; five minutes familiar within-five game, immediate feedback.

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Read separately: [[s07-G134-1]] [[s07-G134-2]] [[s07-G134-3]] [[s07-G134-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Expect 2, 3, 4, 3 objects/marks on game-record. Immediate feedback: error I counts arrivals only; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 15 min. Paper choices and reflection

Finish earlier products. [[s07-P134]] Mark one correction on retained paper. At most two voluntary 45-second reflections, included here; no peer inference or new judgment. Date new work for later feedback.

#### 5 min. Synthesis and tidy

Retain originals, feedback and revisions. Select each child's first unmet component for ten-minute response centers on days 135/136. Newly made work receives written feedback afterward; day 135 revision, day 136 judgment. After class allow fifteen seconds per child, 7.5 minutes, for new-work feedback. No extra interview or exit.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw retained layouts if needed. Paper solids, clean boxes/tubes, paper counters and crayons replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; note access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper solids, clean boxes/tubes, paper counters and crayons replace kits. Hand-draw retained layouts; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated questions. No new assessment.

- **10 min. Choose.** Child finishes a familiar earlier paper; include five minutes of the familiar game.
- **5 min. Record.** Privately review earlier papers and words with the answer key. Missing words remain unheard.
- **10 min. Revise.** Return written feedback on earlier work. [[s07-P134]] **Say.** "Mark one correction on your earlier paper." Expect a change responding to the earlier note, such as marking a curved side.
- **5 min. Tidy.** Keep originals and revisions. Write feedback on new work afterward; revise day 135 and judge day 136. Choose one remaining need, not another battery of questions.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer familiar choices; preserve revision/record review. Longer: finish an earlier drawing, no new check.

### Words and supports

Review familiar feature words in retained feedback. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept tactile models, home language, signs, dictated or directed marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not neatness.

### Tomorrow

Day 135 sorts, counts and orders classroom categories. Its ten-minute Centers response receives this record and first unmet component; day 136 Centers receives revised paper for judgment. Keep gaps explicit; no new teaching today.

### Credit

Curio Class original, using retained records.
