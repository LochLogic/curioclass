# Day 130: I can describe solids and where they are.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Seal reserved masters separately. Set upright practice tube, cubic block, oblong box and viewing arrow. Assign six conference names and six different exit samples, three per spoken ask. Prepare F1/F2 for everyone and F3/F4 for today's six only. Inspect large models: no staples, residue, sharp edges, glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low; spheres loose on mats.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W120-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return s07-X129 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 129's build, trace, practice and game packet. Honor its Tomorrow: if many day 129 or day 128 tickets show error O, share one action: [[s07-K130-whole]] [[steps:s07-K130-whole]]. Include fresh retry and revision in these five minutes. Extra needs remain recorded for days 135/136.

#### 14 min. Teaching and connected work

Five minutes: describe classroom solids as promised. Review shape/flat/square/triangle/rectangle/hexagon and all six position words, day 45; solid, day 4; cube, day 1; circle, day 25; side/corner, day 48; equal sides, day 51; curved, day 46; sphere/cylinder/cone/face, day 127.

**Ask.** [[s07-K130-name]] [[steps:s07-K130-name]] Read [[s07-P130]]: cross out supplied above label and dictate below; no spelling needed. Review Position picture key with fixed viewing arrow, day 45. Demonstrate six separate partial-arrow corrections with named cube/oblong box: [[s07-K130-place-1]] [[s07-K130-place-2]] [[s07-K130-place-3]] [[s07-K130-place-4]] [[s07-K130-place-5]] [[s07-K130-place-6]]. Beside and next to interchange; viewpoint stays fixed. Guided corrections only, no reserved target grid.

Nine independent paper minutes: reveal classification-130 for s07-F1, three minutes; matching real solids beside drawings. Reveal comparison-130, six minutes. Read each exact stem on its named master separately and wait: s07-F2a s07-F2b s07-F2c s07-F2d s07-F2e. Record geometric marks, not drawing skill. Never display answers or infer an unheard reason.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

First eight minutes: today's six independently complete locations-131, four minutes, then solid-pairs-131, four. Read exact stems on the masters: s07-F31 s07-F32 s07-F33 s07-F34 s07-F35 s07-F36 separately, then s07-F4a. Shield papers from peers. Others repeat partial-label corrections on practice paper.

Next fifteen minutes: six conferences at 2.5 minutes.

Use conference-card with assigned children only: s07-F2f, s07-F3s, s07-F4b, 45 seconds each, plus 15 seconds transition, 2.5 minutes per child. Actual cubic box/square panel, retained marked locations and solid pairs appear only at that child's turn. Record names heard and words. F1 reason uses F2 evidence only if actually supplied. Unfinished components remain not yet seen. Fresh domain evidence never fills an unheard day 127 or day 130 exit retrospectively.

Last five minutes: everyone plays; setup/tidy uses conference transitions.

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Expect 2, 3, 4, 3 objects/marks on game-record. Immediate feedback: error I counts arrivals only; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-130 and student-page Exit box only now. Read [[s07-P130-exitbox]] exactly; all thirty mark independently. Six different speakers: three hear [[s07-X130a]], three hear [[s07-X130b]], exactly one 45-second ask each, 4.5 minutes total. Others' names/position words stay unheard. Remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, marking and collection. A marked target never proves a spoken word.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Return exit/Exit box/practice/game packet day 131 Return and repair; window papers/recorded words day 131 Connected work, five minutes.

### Other rooms

Paper and real models are the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only. Without a printer, hand-draw named masters with blank response spaces. Clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones, adult-cut shapes and paper counters replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; record access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean boxes/tubes and paper models replace kits; paper counters and crayons replace counters/pencils. Hand-draw masters without answers; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Class rotations/public turns. Split reserved work across sittings; each master appears once.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's tube record.
- **5 min. Describe.** [[s07-P130]] **Say.** "Correct this label." Expect below the shelf; point and scribe.
- **9 min. Paper.** F1/F2; keep F3/F4 for another sitting before the single conference. Return written feedback tomorrow.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-130 and Exit box. [[s07-X130a]] [[s07-X130b]] [[s07-P130-exitbox]] Read one stem, wait. Expect cone, behind, cone marked. Record words separately from marks; no choice hints.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated label corrections, preserving reserved paper/conferences, games and exit. Longer: repeat a partial-arrow correction with another oblong box; no reserved scene practice.

### Words and supports

Read all print aloud at 18 point, independent reading zero. Review model, day 72. Accept home language, signs, tactile models, directed marks and dictated labels. Public talk may be passed. Shape hints count as help; usual access does not.

### Tomorrow

Day 131 composes low models and identifies flat parts. If many day 130 tickets reversed depth, error E, its five-minute Return and repair moves a different named model behind/in front of a box. Error F, triangle for cone, and other needs remain for next available slot or 135/136. Unheard names/positions continue through assigned F3/F4 domain turns, never inferred from paper. Return every marked packet and written feedback as allocated above.
