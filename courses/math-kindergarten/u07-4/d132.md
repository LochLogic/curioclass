# Day 132: I can count solid pieces and compare their parts.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately; keep reserved exit-132 hidden. Cover stimulus-master prompts; print child prompts only on the slip and Exit box. Set six-piece supplied records, adult-started low cube arrangements and single-face markers. Select next eight unheard conference children. Provide each child an exit picture and actual cube/cone to inspect without a queue. Inspect large models for residue, staples and sharp edges; no glass, metal cans, mouthing or throwing. Keep builds low and spheres loose on mats.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W120-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 131 exit drawings/build records, Exit boxes and written tasks in the practice/game packet with written feedback. If many day 131 tickets showed error B, whole cube called square: [[s07-K132-whole]] [[steps:s07-K132-whole]]. Fresh retry and original revision share five minutes. Other needs remain recorded for days 135/136. No repeated independent whole/face interview.

#### 14 min. Connected work

First five minutes: revise earlier window papers and recorded explanations from day 131 feedback. Keep original evidence and date corrections. No secure-domain repeat.

Next nine: apply yesterday's solid parts to tracking pieces. Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; cube, day 1; flat/square, day 45; curved, day 46; face/cylinder/cone/sphere, day 127. Each whole object counts once even when several faces show.

**Ask.** [[s07-P132]] Use practice-132's six visible cubes and supplied 7. Start at one end, tick each object once and correct to 6, not seven visible parts. Marks, six tallies, dictation or numeral 6 count. Never count the same face as another piece. Keep the drawing partial: correct the supplied count, no blank independent build count.

**Ask.** [[s07-K132-face]] [[steps:s07-K132-face]] Verify one indicated flat region on a known tube, never a more-face pair. Flat faces can be tracked one by one just like objects; turning does not make a curved part flat. Review more, day 21, and match, day 8: matching leaves extras in the greater amount. No memorized face totals. At most two voluntary 45-second contributions, included in these nine minutes.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

Three minutes setup/tidy, twenty minutes for eight conferences, five whole-class game minutes. Other children repair supplied six-piece records and low partial cube arrangements, each keeping a marked construction-record. Partners alternate builder/checker. If many revisit a cube, cover each counted one on the partial record, then remove covers after checking.

Use conference-card with the rotating group of eight children only: s07-F2f, s07-F3s, s07-F4b, 45 seconds each, plus 15 seconds transition, 2.5 minutes per child. Actual cubic box/square panel, retained marked locations and solid pairs appear only at that child's turn. Record names heard and words. F1 reason uses F2 evidence only if actually supplied. Unfinished components remain not yet seen. Fresh domain evidence never fills an unheard day 127 or day 130 exit retrospectively.

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Expect 2, 3, 4, 3 objects/marks on game-record. Immediate feedback: error I counts arrivals only; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

The separate exit-132.md master now includes both independent questions. Reveal and collect both together within this slot.

Reveal the exit-132 picture and sealed Side 2 Exit box only now. Read [[s07-X132a]], then [[s07-X132b]] separately. Everyone records the count on the half slip and independently marks the chosen arrowed solid in the Exit box. Actual models support face inspection; do not supply totals or choice hints. Three children observed per ask, six at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes total. Remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, paper completion and collection. Three cubes plus two cones are five whole pieces. Cube has more flat faces than cone, six versus one; totals need not be recited. A wrong total without a visible counting method gets descriptive notes.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Collect both papers. Return the half slip, Exit box and practice/game packet day 133 Return and repair; window feedback day 133 Connected work, five minutes. Paper marks never prove an unheard reason.

### Other rooms

Paper and real models are the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only. Without a printer, hand-draw named masters with blank response spaces. Clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones, adult-cut shapes and paper counters replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; record access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean plain boxes/tubes or paper solids replace kits; crayons and paper counters replace pencils/counters. Hand-draw masters and point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Public turns and rotations; do not repeat a completed conference.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens and ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's whole-cube label.
- **5 min. Check.** [[s07-P132]] **Say.** "Correct this supplied count." Expect 6, each whole cube once.
- **9 min. Paper.** Correct the single tube-face marker, play the familiar game and revise earlier written feedback for five minutes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the half slip and sealed Side 2 Exit box. [[s07-X132a]] [[s07-X132b]] Read one stem, wait. Child records five pieces and marks cube for more flat faces. Allow handling without naming totals. Record help and give written feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated count corrections, preserving revision, conferences, games and exit. Longer: repair another tick on the supplied six-piece record; no independent whole-build count practice.

### Words and supports

Read all print aloud at 18 point; independent reading zero. Review whole, day 87; model, day 72. Offer tactile models, large nonslip paper bases, home language, signs, directed marks and dictation. Public talk may be passed. Separate access from mathematical help; neatness is not judged.

### Tomorrow

Day 133 models classroom objects using solid features. If many day 132 tickets show error L, repeated piece during counting, its five-minute Return and repair corrects a fresh supplied partial record using a counted boundary. Error D, color-only feature choice, and further needs stay for days 135/136. Return exit/practice/game packet and window feedback to their allocated parts; continue last unheard domain conferences without backfilling earlier exits.
