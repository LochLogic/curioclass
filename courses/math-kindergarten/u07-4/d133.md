# Day 133: I can model objects with solid shapes.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Print both prompts and one picture on the half slip; no duplicate. Prepare practice-133 and an adult-started partial room model: cylindrical paper holder on oblong desk model. Supply unfinished drawing, cylinder side labeled all flat. Seal exit-133 and individual loose solids; no ball/block corner in practice. Select final eight unheard conference children. Inspect large models: no staples, residue, sharp edges, glass or metal cans. Builds low, spheres loose on mats; no mouthing or throwing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W120-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 132's written tasks with annotated feedback in this existing slot. Keep first marks beside later revisions; no extra interview or minutes.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 132 tickets, Exit boxes and written tasks in the practice/game packet with written feedback. If many day 132 tickets showed error L, repeated piece: [[s07-K133-track]] [[steps:s07-K133-track]]. Fresh partial retry and original-record revision share five minutes. Error D or other feature needs stay recorded for days 135/136; final conferences keep their time.

#### 14 min. Connected work

Five minutes revise earlier final-window written feedback. Nine minutes correct the familiar supplied partial mathematics; keep all complete new builds and model choices for the exit. [[s07-P133]] No additional interview is added here.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

Keep day 127's unheard exit name and day 130's unheard exit language on the record. The new naming evidence belongs to the funded F4 component, never retroactive exit credit; the early F3 position roster is already complete. 

Three minutes cover setup and tidy. Use 20 conference minutes for children 23 to 30, 2.5 minutes each. Follow conference-card and naming-cards-130: F2f whole/face description, cylinder name, cone name, three separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds reset. There is no new position interview. Five minutes familiar games for all children; waiting children continue independently, then resume their interrupted paper work. Missing words stay not yet seen; matching never establishes names. [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]

#### 8 min. Exit ticket


Reveal the exit-133 corner picture, sealed second exit question and loose models only now. Read [[s07-X133a]], wait, then [[s07-X133b]], exactly as printed. All independently build and draw on the half slip. In the Exit box, trace one chosen model surface and link it to the pictured object; no second whole-model drawing. Sphere-like model beside cube-like model fits the scene. Curved surface fits ball; square flat face fits storage block. Color alone does not establish a geometric fit, error D only if observed.

Three observations per ask, six at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes. Others work simultaneously; remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, paper marking and collection. An adult may steady models or mark as directed, without suggesting a choice. Keep new application unscored at collection.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Collect both papers. Return the half slip, Exit box and practice/game packet day 134 Feedback revision, first five minutes; window feedback uses its other five minutes. Day 134 closes only earlier work with written feedback and later revision. Do not infer an unheard reason from an unconnected sketch.

### Other rooms

Paper models and drawings are the complete path. Hand-draw masters without a printer. Adults move or mark as directed; record access separately from shape hints.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean tube/box and paper solids replace kits, paper counters replace counters, crayons replace pencils. Hand-draw masters; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Public turns, rotations and completed conferences.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens/ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct a duplicated tick, then revise yesterday's record.
- **5 min. Model.** Show the started paper-holder/desk model. [[s07-P133]] **Say.** "Correct this label." Expect curved side, not all flat.
- **9 min. Paper.** Finish its partial drawing; play familiar game and revise earlier feedback for five minutes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the half slip and sealed second exit question. [[s07-X133a]] [[s07-X133b]] Child builds/draws alone, then marks a feature connection. Expect sphere beside cube and curved ball surface or flat square block face. Record help; give written feedback tomorrow before judging later revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated partial-model repairs; preserve feedback, conferences, games and unseen exit. Longer: improve another supplied cylinder-holder outline; never rehearse a whole reserved corner.

### Words and supports

Review sphere/cone, day 127; cube, day 1; square/beside, day 45; count, day 1. Read all print aloud at 18 point; independent reading zero. Tactile models, home language, signs, directed marks and dictation are accepted. Public talk may be passed. Separate access from shape hints; judge features, not craftsmanship.

### Tomorrow

Day 134 reviews retained records and revises feedback without new teaching or interviews. Error D, color-only fit, receives written feature feedback in the returned packet. Other unresolved letters stay recorded. First-made day 134 work receives feedback afterward, revision day 135 and judgment day 136. Unit 8 response centers take ten minutes on each of days 135/136, one first-unmet component per child.
