# Day 133: I can model objects with solid shapes.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Prepare practice-133 and an adult-started partial room model: cylindrical paper holder on oblong desk model. Supply unfinished drawing, cylinder side labeled all flat. Seal exit-133 and individual loose solids; no ball/block corner in practice. Select final eight unheard conference children. Inspect large models: no staples, residue, sharp edges, glass or metal cans. Builds low, spheres loose on mats; no mouthing or throwing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred, day 104: two simultaneous echo minutes, [[s07-W120-tens]]. Three minutes: [[s07-W120-ones]], one through twenty. Say the sequence first if needed. No individual judgment from chorus; written numerals stop at twenty.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 132 tickets and written tasks in the practice/game packet with written feedback. If many day 132 tickets showed error L, repeated piece: [[s07-K133-track]] [[steps:s07-K133-track]]. Fresh partial retry and original-record revision share five minutes. Error D or other feature needs stay recorded for days 135/136; final conferences keep their time.

#### 14 min. Connected work

First five minutes: revise earlier window papers and recorded explanations using day 132 written feedback. Date corrections without replacing original evidence. No new conference on a secure component.

Next nine: apply solid features to a supplied partial classroom model, as promised. Review model, day 72: something represents another object. Review cylinder/face, day 127; curved, day 46; rectangle/flat, day 45; whole, day 87. Show invented paper holder on desk and adult-started model; say "Invented for practice." The holder has a curved side and flat circular ends; model cylinder preserves those features, although size differs.

**Ask.** [[s07-P133]] Use practice-133 beside real cylinder. Children cross out all flat and mark its curved side; adult scribes curved when directed. Leave the started desk model in place. No blank whole-corner model, no independent feature justification in practice. Hear at most two voluntary 45-second contributions inside nine minutes. If many retain all flat, feel around the cylinder side and across an end, then retry its supplied label. New model work receives feedback, not same-day judgment.

#### 28 min. Observation centers

Three minutes setup/tidy, twenty for final eight conferences, five whole-class game minutes. Others revise adult-started cylinder-holder/desk models and partial records by features. Pairs exchange two 45-second builder/checker roles simultaneously; no coaching. 
Use conference-card with the rotating group of eight children only: s07-F2f, s07-F3s, s07-F4b, 45 seconds each, plus 15 seconds transition, 2.5 minutes per child. Actual cubic box/square panel, retained marked locations and solid pairs appear only at that child's turn. Record names heard and words. F1 reason uses F2 evidence only if actually supplied. Unfinished components remain not yet seen. Fresh domain evidence never fills an unheard day 127 or day 130 exit retrospectively. Check the roster: 6/8/8/8 across days 130 to 133 reaches thirty only if each attended once. Missing/unheard components remain not yet seen for days 135/136; no extra interviews today.

Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second roles simultaneously. Read separately: [[s07-G120-1]] [[s07-G120-2]] [[s07-G120-3]] [[s07-G120-4]]. Rin alone acts; say "Invented for practice." Expect 2, 3, 4, 3 objects/marks on game-record. Immediate feedback: error I counts arrivals only; error H adds during removal. No timer.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-133 and loose models only now. Read [[s07-X133a]], wait, then [[s07-X133b]], exactly as printed. All children independently model the new corner and draw it; each marks one shape-to-object feature connection on the half slip. Sphere-like model beside cube-like model fits the scene. Curved surface fits ball; square flat face fits storage block. Color alone does not establish a geometric fit, error D only if observed.

Three observations per ask, six at 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes. Others work simultaneously; remaining 3.5 minutes: directions, paper marking and collection. An adult may steady models or mark as directed, without suggesting a choice. Keep new application unscored at collection.

After class: four minutes inspect thirty paper records at eight seconds each; 7.5 minutes exit feedback at fifteen seconds each; thirty separate teacher minutes for window feedback/notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Written feedback comes before later revision and judgment. Return exit/practice/game packet day 134 Feedback revision, first five minutes; window feedback uses its other five minutes. Day 134 closes only earlier work with written feedback and later revision. Do not infer an unheard reason from an unconnected sketch.

### Other rooms

Paper and real models are the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges pictures only. Without a printer, hand-draw named masters with blank response spaces. Clean plain boxes/tubes, paper balls/cones, adult-cut shapes and paper counters replace kits. Adults steady or mark at child direction; record access separately from mathematical help. Every child has loose lightweight exit solids; no shared-device or model queue.

### At home

**Swap.** Clean tube/box and paper solids replace kits, paper counters replace counters, crayons replace pencils. Hand-draw masters; point instead of projecting.

**Skip.** Public turns, rotations and completed conferences.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo tens/ones.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct a duplicated tick, then revise yesterday's record.
- **5 min. Model.** Show the started paper-holder/desk model. [[s07-P133]] **Say.** "Correct this label." Expect curved side, not all flat.
- **9 min. Paper.** Finish its partial drawing; play familiar game and revise earlier feedback for five minutes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal exit-133. [[s07-X133a]] [[s07-X133b]] Child builds/draws alone, then marks a feature connection. Expect sphere beside cube and curved ball surface or flat square block face. Record help; give written feedback tomorrow before judging later revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated partial-model repairs; preserve feedback, conferences, games and unseen exit. Longer: improve another supplied cylinder-holder outline; never rehearse a whole reserved corner.

### Words and supports

Review sphere/cone, day 127; cube, day 1; square/beside, day 45; count, day 1. Read all print aloud at 18 point; independent reading zero. Tactile models, home language, signs, directed marks and dictation are accepted. Public talk may be passed. Separate access from shape hints; judge features, not craftsmanship.

### Tomorrow

Day 134 reviews retained records and revises feedback without new teaching or interviews. Error D, color-only fit, receives written feature feedback in the returned packet. Other unresolved letters stay recorded. First-made day 134 work receives feedback afterward, revision day 135 and judgment day 136. Unit 8 response centers take ten minutes on each of days 135/136, one first-unmet component per child.
