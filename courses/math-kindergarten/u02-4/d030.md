# Day 30: Connect quantities and numerals

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-30.md through practice-records.md; prepare loose numeral tracing strips and prefilled blank-frames.md, one quantity at a time. Hide exit-30.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-4/teacher-key.md and d030-key.html private; use today's item samples for reverse matching. Choose roster 1 to 6. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R30-oral]] Review day 29's full-five structure. [[s02-R30]] Show eight squares, five above and three below. Review familiar counting words through ten, with adult-led words to twenty; everyone joins. All cases are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 29 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O29. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return s02-P29-1, s02-P29-2, s02-P29-3, s02-F29 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 29 s02-X29, s02-O29 and practice with feedback. If many day 29 papers or heard reasons show error I, use [[s02-F30]]: match five partners, then add an extra for more. For heard error B, spread the same five before matching again. Choose one action; collect fresh correction beside original and queue other needs.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

A numeral is a written number form. Say each word, reveal its card, then connect matching occupants: zero through ten, one at a time. Zero means none, an intentionally empty frame. Model strokes; children finger-trace concurrently, about forty seconds per form. For ten, both marks together name ten. Read each stem before its steps.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Partners use the same single-card tasks: trace, cover, form, uncover, compare. One form at a time on loose strips; no eleven-box sheet. Allow about fifty seconds per form with teacher guidance; retain zero, four and eight. **Ask.** [[s02-P30-1]] Ring the matching picture after hearing five. Then compare counts; a wrong mark alone does not establish error M. For difficulty, put one piece per occupied cell and reconnect the supplied numeral. Correct recognizable reversals with stroke guidance.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O30]] from heard-30.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 1 to 6: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover and two collection. First correct [[s02-P30-2]], [[s02-P30-3]], [[s02-P30-4]], [[s02-P30-5]] and [[s02-P30-6]], separately read in that order. Model zero's correction: cross out the square, leaving none. Guide one mark per object and familiar Finish tick outside the group. Then repeat single-card formation with matching frames. One 45-second turn per partner takes 1.5 minutes per pair concurrently; feedback follows. Everyone returns pages and strips. After class mark thirty sets at thirty seconds, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 31. No buttons or group-to-many-card practice.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently at table trays; keep exit pictures hidden.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-30.md only now and read [[s02-X30]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 1 to 6, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact diagrams and exit. Handwritten cards, equal-cell frames and large scrap squares provide the full paper path; no device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares, hand-drawn frames, handwritten cards and crayons instead of printed pieces and broad pencils.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Model.** [[s02-P30-2]] Model crossing out the square for none. Show card 0; trace its closed loop. [[s02-P30-5]] Model adding one to seven for eight; trace card 8 beside the repaired group.
- **7 min. Together.** Cover each card; your child forms it, then uncovers to check.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P30-1]] **Say.** "Which picture goes with five?" Expect five-square picture. [[s02-P30-2]] Cross out the square for none. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X30]] on the unseen exit-30.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repeats, retain all eleven numeral connections and exit. Longer: repeat cover-and-form with the same cards and check strokes.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; 10-frame and five-and-some, day 26; Supported oral joining through twenty, day 25. Read all print aloud; 18 point. Enlarge or use tactile squares; record motor help separately from quantity prompts. Public talk may be passed. New: numeral, zero, none. Zero names an empty group.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 keeps a numeral when its objects move. Return day 30 paper and strips in Return and repair. For heard error A, enclose a counted practice group and name its total; for error M, reconnect supplied five to its group. For error E or error O, clear a frame for zero. Choose one five-minute repair; queue others. Recognition alone does not prove numeral writing or unheard counting.
