# Day 33: Rehearse requested drawings and zero

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-33.md through practice-records.md; the student page keeps each supplied sketch and correction together. Lay out blank-frames.md and numeral-cards.md; prepare fresh copies of familiar sketches. Hide exit-33.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-4/teacher-key.md and d033-key.html private. Choose roster 25 to 30. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R33-oral]] Review day 32's requested stop: [[s02-R33]]. Review zero/none from day 30. Join familiar oral counting words through ten and adult-led words toward twenty. All cases are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 32 s02-X32, s02-O32, s02-F32, s02-P32-1 and s02-P32-2 with feedback. If many day 32 papers or observed moves show error D, use [[s02-F33]] with request visible. For error M, count completed five, match its supplied label, cover, form, uncover and check. Choose one action; collect fresh correction beside original, queue others. Do not rehearse the exit order format.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review only day 30's single numeral traces and sketch repairs. [[s02-P33-1]] Read zero as none. Children trace 0 on a loose strip, cross out supplied square, and mark Finish outside group. One dot kept for zero shows error O when the action is observed. Review one, three, eight and ten with their existing correction cases, one at a time. No new formation or quantity meaning.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs correct [[s02-P33-2]], [[s02-P33-3]], [[s02-P33-4]] and [[s02-P33-5]], separately read in that order. Trace supplied numeral, then add or cross out one square to repair sketch. **Ask.** Read each row's single stem before feedback. Crossed-out marks do not count. Each child has four 45-second turns, six minutes concurrently; remaining six minutes allow handling and immediate feedback. For error M, count actual occupants against supplied numeral rather than guessing from picture size.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover and two collection. Repeat individual sketch repairs or familiar single-card matching; keep positive and zero requests separate. No paired blank order slip or independent eleven-box sheet. Everyone returns corrected practice with finish marks outside groups. Preserve first response and help. After class mark thirty sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 34. No timer or new assessment window.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently at table trays; store numeral cards and reveal each child's own exit only now.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: give each child the covered exit-33.md half slip, then reveal it; read [[s02-X33]] exactly. Independent paper two minutes. Read labels five and zero, none. Finish ticks sit outside groups, never counted as objects. Hear roster 25 to 30: [[s02-O33]], six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Collect in half a minute. Record names, help and shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Unheard explanations remain not yet seen. After class mark thirty papers at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 34.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied sketches and exact exit. Handwritten cards, equal-cell frames and large paper squares provide every action without a device.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy diagrams, use scrap squares, drawn frames, handwritten cards and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P33-1]] Trace zero, cross out square for none.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P33-3]] Fix two squares for numeral 3 by adding one. **Say.** "What does this numeral request?" Expect three squares.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P33-4]] Add one to seven for eight. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X33]] over fresh slip, reading labels five and zero aloud. Your child draws alone: five marks and an intentionally empty group, with finish ticks. "What does your empty group show?" **Say.** "What does your empty group show?" Expect zero, no pieces requested. Keep for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repeats, retain positive and zero repairs and exit. Longer: repeat same sketches with partner feedback. Fluency rehearses accuracy on taught material.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; 10-frame and five-and-some, day 26; Supported oral joining through twenty, day 25. Read all print aloud; 18 point. Enlarge or use tactile squares; record motor help separately from quantity prompts. Public talk may be passed. Review numeral, zero, none and drawing repairs, day 30; requested stop, day 32. A directed adult may record marks; note motor help separately from quantity prompts.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 labels counted collections, including empty spaces. Return day 33 paper in Return and repair. For observed error O, clear a fresh practice frame and connect none to supplied 0; for error D, retain requested stop while finishing five; for error M, reconnect completed amount and label. Choose one five-minute repair; queue rest. Keep unseen oral components not yet seen.
