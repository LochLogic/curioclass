# Quantities and numerals: teacher answer key

Teacher only. Keep separate from student copies. These cold-solved keys supply look-fors and two samples. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Diagnose letters from observed actions or heard reasons.

## s02-X30

Prompt: Ring the numeral for this group.

Answer: Only 6 ringed.

Look for: Six scattered buttons; eleven equal-size numeral choices. Recognition, not writing evidence. | Shape: Recognizes the symbol for an unseen collection among all eleven forms. No buttons, six-button case or group-to-many-numerals sheet in practice; work the reverse direction, numeral to quantity picture or constructed group.

Shown: Only 6 ringed. | Not yet seen: Counts six correctly but rings 3. (M).
