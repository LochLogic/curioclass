# Day 31: Keep the numeral when pieces move

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-31.md through practice-records.md. Prepare five and eight squares, blank-frames.md and their numeral-cards.md labels. Hide exit-31.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-4/teacher-key.md and d031-key.html private. Choose roster 13 to 18. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R31-oral]] Review day 30's quantity-label connection, then day 20's unchanged group: [[s02-R31]]. Count familiar words through ten and join the adult-led extension to twenty. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. All cases are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 30 s02-X30, s02-O30, s02-F30, s02-P30-1, s02-P30-2, s02-P30-3, s02-P30-4, s02-P30-5, s02-P30-6 and numeral strips with feedback. If many day 30 marks and observed counting establish error M, use [[s02-F31]]. For heard error A, enclose counted five and supply its total. For error E or error O, review zero beside a cleared frame. Choose one action, collect fresh correction beside original, queue others. No error is inferred from a reversal alone.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Review moving without adding or losing, day 20. Now connect that action to a numeral retained across arrangements. [[s02-K31-1]] [[steps:s02-K31-1]] Show each arrangement after its direction. Partners watch actual members; five stays five and eight stays eight. If spread seems to change the label, move the same pieces back and track each once across the counted-uncounted boundary. The frame supports counting; only its occupants are counted.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs repeat physical five/eight moves, keeping their supplied labels. **Ask.** [[s02-P31-1]] Read the adult diagram's label aloud. Children repair its label without redrawing the group. Correct eight-square count with label 2 shows error M; spacing alone does not change the number. Partners each have two 45-second turns, three minutes concurrently; remaining time permits verification, formation and feedback. Use the frame first, then move those same squares onto plain paper. No four-disk redraw-and-label sheet.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover and two collection. Repeat physical moves, single numeral traces from day 30, and supplied-label corrections. Every child returns a corrected diagram with a Finish tick in its margin. Keep initial work and corrections. A corrected label does not show that an unheard child explained the unchanged quantity. After class mark thirty practice pages at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 32.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently at table trays. Hide numeral cards before revealing the independent exit.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: give each child the covered exit-31.md half slip, then reveal it; read [[s02-X31]] exactly. Independent paper two minutes. Assess changed arrangement, unchanged quantity and written label separately. Hear roster 13 to 18: [[s02-O31]], six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Collect in half a minute. Record names, help and shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Unheard explanations remain not yet seen. After class mark thirty papers at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 32.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy diagrams and exact exit. Large scrap squares, equal-cell frames and handwritten cards supply the complete paper path; no device.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-copy diagrams, use scrap squares, drawn frames, handwritten cards and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words.
- **5 min. Model.** [[s02-P31-1]] Count eight pictured squares and correct label 2 to 8. Place eight paper squares over the picture, then move the same pieces while keeping supplied card 8.
- **7 min. Together.** Alternate handling and watching. **Say.** "Did moving change how many?" Expect no: nothing entered or left.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P31-1]] Fix the adult's label; expect 8. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X31]] over exit-31.md. Your child draws alone. Expect four disk marks arranged differently and label 4. [[s02-O31]] **Say.** "What explains your number label?" Expect still four, none came or went. Keep for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repeats; keep physical movement, label correction and exit. Longer: move the same eight squares into another compact grouping and verify unchanged membership.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; 10-frame and five-and-some, day 26; Supported oral joining through twenty, day 25. Read all print aloud; 18 point. Enlarge or use tactile squares; record motor help separately from quantity prompts. Public talk may be passed. Review numeral, zero and none from day 30. Keep quantity understanding separate from numeral formation.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 completes requested groups and stops at the named number. Return day 31 paper in Return and repair. If many show heard error B, track the same eight squares through a move; for error M, retain supplied 8 while forming its copy. Choose one five-minute repair. Paper shows amount, arrangement and written label; the six heard reasons supply explanation evidence only for those children.
