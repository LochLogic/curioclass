# Day 32: Remember the requested stopping number

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-32.md through practice-records.md; the student page encloses existing squares and additions together. Lay out blank-frames.md and numeral-cards.md; draw fresh single-sketch strips from today's item look-fors for the five familiar repairs. Hide exit-32.md and button-pieces.md until exit. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-4/teacher-key.md and d032-key.html private. Choose roster 19 to 24. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

[[s02-R32-oral]] Review day 31's stable label: [[s02-R32]]. Count familiar words through ten, joining adult-led extension to twenty. All cases are invented for practice. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 31 s02-X31, s02-O31, s02-F31 and written task s02-P31-1 with feedback. If many day 31 papers or heard reasons show error B, move the same eight squares and track unchanged membership. For observed error M use [[s02-F32]]. Choose one action; collect fresh correction beside original, queue others. A numeral reversal receives formation feedback, not an automatic letter.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Remember the requested final total while moving one piece per counting word. [[s02-K32-1]] [[steps:s02-K32-1]] Keep the card visible; unused supply is allowed. Model paper recording by adding one drawing mark for each new piece inside the existing group's outline. Then [[s02-P32-2]]: five already placed, add three, stop at eight. Guide matching, covering and writing the label, then uncover to check. Keep optional frame support.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs finish adult-started groups: [[s02-P32-1]] and [[s02-P32-2]]. Supply six loose squares beside each partial group. Children count existing members before adding, then mark only additions on paper. Briefly cover the request card after naming it; uncover to check. Children needing it keep the card visible. **Ask.** [[s02-P32-3]] Each partner has a 45-second turn, 1.5 minutes concurrently. Expect the card keeps the stopping number in mind. If many show error D, touch the supplied numeral at the stop and leave unused pieces aside.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover and two collection. Repeat supplied completions, formation and physical rearrangements; no independent full order mat. Then repair day 30's familiar cases on fresh single-sketch strips: [[s02-P32-4]], [[s02-P32-5]], [[s02-P32-6]], [[s02-P32-7]], [[s02-P32-8]], separately with feedback. These familiar strips stay in folders. Everyone returns today's corrected page with Finish ticks outside groups. After class mark thirty sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 33. No buttons or seven-from-ten tab requests in practice.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return squares concurrently at table trays. Store numeral cards. Give each child covered exit-32.md and adult-cut button-pieces.md only now.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: give each child the covered exit-32.md half slip, then reveal it; read [[s02-X32]] exactly. Independent paper two minutes. Children mark selected supply outlines, outline moved paper buttons on the mat and label it. Slow finishers continue paper during conferences. Classify stopping only if observed. Hear roster 19 to 24: [[s02-O32]], six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Collect in half a minute. Record names, help and shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Unheard explanations remain not yet seen. After class mark thirty papers at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 33.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact partial groups and exit. Large scrap squares and hand-drawn frames replace counters and printed frames; large paper circles with two small drawn holes replace button-pieces.md. No real buttons or device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares, hand-drawn frames and cards, crayons, and adult-cut paper button pictures.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words.
- **5 min. Model.** [[s02-P32-1]] Start with three squares; stop at five after adding two.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P32-2]] Add three to supplied five for eight. **Say.** "What keeps the request in mind?" Expect the numeral card.
- **5 min. Repair.** Count again; cover and form supplied numeral, then check. Put squares away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X32]] over exit-32.md; your child works alone using paper buttons. Expect eight selected and outlined, label 8. "Where did you stop?" **Say.** "Where did you stop?" Expect eight. Keep for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeats; retain both completion cases, stop cue and exit. Longer: partners verify unused pieces remain when the requested total is reached.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; 10-frame and five-and-some, day 26; Supported oral joining through twenty, day 25. Read all print aloud; 18 point. Enlarge or use tactile squares; record motor help separately from quantity prompts. Public talk may be passed. Review numeral, zero and none, day 30. New routine: remember the requested stop while completing a group. Directed adult movement records motor help; supplying the stopping number records mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 rehearses drawing requests and zero. Return day 32 paper in Return and repair. If many show observed error D, finish a fresh five-square partial group with its request visible; for error M, count-match-cover-form-check its label. Give one five-minute action and queue others. Finished paper shows quantity and label; stopping and counting actions require observation.
