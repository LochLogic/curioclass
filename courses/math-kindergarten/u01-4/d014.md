# Day 14: The last counting word

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-14.md and covered exit-14.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 13 chosen tracking with [[s01-W14]], then [[s01-N14]] together. Partners take one 45-second moving turn each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 13's exit and practice s01-P13 with feedback. If day 13's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square; error B: cover each counted square before moving on. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise a small group while others use known supports. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review chosen supports. Use [[s01-K14]].

[[steps:s01-K14]]

**Ask.** After step 2, before revealing step 3: [[s01-Q14]] Expect five, accepting a recount. Then name last word as the final word in the completed count. Model saying, Five was my last word, while pointing to the whole group; partners use the phrase in their reply. Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F14]], one puppet, invented for practice. Counting again is allowed; last-word meaning is not assessed here.

#### 14 min. Last word in conversation

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Move five, then ten loose squares across a mat boundary, one word per move. After each count, the listener uses [[s01-Q14]] for five squares and [[s01-Q14-10]] for ten; expect five or ten respectively. Two 45-second counts and two 15-second replies per child take four minutes per pair; ten remain for resetting and [[s01-P14]]. Collect paper. If ordered words break, slow the sequence before moving again.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers using u01-1/cards-centers.md: movable loose-object work, linked adjacent ticks or low constructions with five or ten pieces.  Support one group and hear six practice children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; record only evidence actually heard.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a move and its word, 45 seconds each. Public talk may be passed. Give feedback; return trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-14.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X14]] exactly. Children dot each cork as they count. Record whether the final word refers to the whole group, accepting a whole-group gesture. Keep this descriptive note separate from correspondence; add no total question. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 15, Return and repair. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-14.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-14.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Move five loose squares with one word per move. Pause before discussing how many, then trace and tick the pieces on Side 1.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P14]].
  **Say.** "How many squares did you count?" Expect five; a recount is welcome.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X14]] exactly on exit-14.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and line from day 12; scattered from day 13. New: last word, the final word said in a count. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 corrects a puppet count and begins reserved observations. Return day 14 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square. Error B: cover each counted square. Choose one repair; last-word meaning remains descriptive.
