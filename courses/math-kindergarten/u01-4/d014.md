# Day 14: The last counting word

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover cork exit card. Prepare five and ten loose squares and boundary mats. Bring day 13 papers with feedback. Select six rotating listeners. Keep large pieces on trays. Teacher-only answer key: d014-key.html. Until the section opener exists, use this page's setup and directions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 13 chosen tracking with [[s01-W14]], then [[s01-N14]] together.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 13's exit and practice with feedback. If day 13's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square; error B: cover each counted square before moving on. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise a small group while others use known supports.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review chosen supports. Use [[s01-K14]], then [[steps:s01-K14]]. Introduce last word by naming the final word after a completed count. **Ask.** [[s01-Q14]] Expect five, accepting a recount. Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F14]], one puppet, invented for practice. Counting again is allowed; last-word meaning is not assessed here.

#### 14 min. Last word in conversation

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Move five, then ten loose squares across a mat boundary, one word per move. After each count, the listener uses [[s01-Q14]] for five squares and [[s01-Q14-10]] for ten; expect five or ten respectively. Two 45-second counts and two 15-second replies per child take four minutes per pair; ten remain for resetting and [[s01-P14]]. Collect paper. If ordered words break, slow the sequence before moving again.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Use movable loose-object work, linked adjacent ticks or low constructions with five or ten pieces. No corks or independent fixed-picture scatter counts. Support one group and hear six practice children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; record only evidence actually heard.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a move and its word, 45 seconds each. Public talk may be passed. Give feedback; return trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X14]] exactly. Children dot each cork as they count. Echo the last word afterward if helpful, with no added scored question. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 15. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Hand-copy exact master stems and pictures if printing is unavailable; cover reserved papers. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters, strips replace cubes, paper shapes replace blocks, drawn boxes replace mats. Use the covered exit master.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Revisit yesterday's paper with one practice action.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Repeat today's loose-piece demonstration.
- **13 min. Practice.** Move five squares, then talk about the last word. Complete [[s01-P14]].
  **Say.** "How many squares did you count?" Expect five; recounting is welcome.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X14]] exactly with the fresh master. Your child marks paper independently. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice moves loose pieces and discusses the last word; the exit independently counts a fixed cork scatter.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and line from day 12; scattered from day 13. New: last word, the final word said in a count. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 corrects a puppet count and begins reserved observations. Return day 14 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square. Error B: cover each counted square. Choose one repair; last-word meaning remains descriptive.
