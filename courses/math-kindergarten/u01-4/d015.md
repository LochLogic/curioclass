# Day 15: Correct the counting action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep acorn exit card and check pieces covered separately. Cut six kites and nine boats per assigned child. Copy the three check stems onto separate papers. On the class list add initial/revised sequence, actions, support, explanation and heard-name columns. Assign ten children today, different rosters tomorrow and day 17. Teacher-only answer key: d015-key.html. Until the section opener exists, use this page's setup and directions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 14 last-word conversation with [[s01-W15]], without scoring cardinality. Rehearse [[s01-N15]] together.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 14's exit and practice with feedback. If day 14's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square; error B: cover each counted square. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one group. Ordered-word needs, error C, use slow words before paired moves.

#### 8 min. Correct a puppet count

Review touch and move. Use [[s01-K15]], showing right before wrong with one paper puppet, invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s01-Q15]] Expect a changed action that reaches the missed square. Fifteen pairs correct simultaneously, one 45-second turn each, 1.5 minutes; the remaining 6.5 cover demonstration and handling. No acorns or blank self-explanation panels.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use the reserved kite and boat masters from day 15, items s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c, with ten assigned children not heard earlier. Three asks at 45 seconds each plus 45 seconds for a brief written note and one missed-count retry total three minutes each, thirty minutes. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Dot cut-paper pieces; scribe the explanation. Record actual sequence, object actions, chosen support and help on the class list. Others repeat known five- or ten-piece centers and complete today's practice paper. Give written feedback now; classify records on day 18, after this revision opportunity. A prompted revision is shown with help. No reserved case appears at other centers. Use [[s01-P15]] for the supplied-route center. Teacher-only key for these three items: d018-key.html.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most demonstrate a corrected action for 45 seconds each. Give brief feedback and return table trays together. Public talk may be passed.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X15]] exactly. Let children count for themselves first, unscored. They draw the tracking explanation on the acorn paper; hear six reasons, one ask each. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 16. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Hand-copy exact master stems and pictures if printing is unavailable; cover reserved papers. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters, strips replace cubes, paper shapes replace blocks, drawn boxes replace mats. Use the covered exit master.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Revisit yesterday's paper with one practice action.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Repeat today's loose-piece demonstration.
- **13 min. Practice.** Correct the supplied puppet route with five loose squares. Complete [[s01-P15]].
  **Say.** "Show one object for each counting word." Expect one distinct action per object, with ordered words when heard.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X15]] exactly with the fresh master. Your child marks paper independently. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice corrects a supplied puppet route; the exit produces a tracking explanation for a new acorn case.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; last word from day 14. Explain a corrected count names how the changed action reaches each object once. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 revisits adjacent linked marks. Return day 15 exit and practice in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square; error B: cover counted pieces. Continue observations with ten different children. Retain check records for day 18.
