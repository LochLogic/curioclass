# Day 15: Correct the counting action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the boxed acorn exit card; keep it covered. Cut separate check pieces from the masters below: six kites and nine boats per assigned child. Prepare initial/revised sequence, actions, support, explanation and heard-name columns on the class list. Assign ten children today and different rosters tomorrow and day 17. Teacher-only keys: d015-key.html and d018-key.html. Until the opener exists, use this page.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Partners move five loose squares, one word per move, in simultaneous 45-second turns. Review day 14 last-word conversation with [[s01-W15]], without scoring cardinality. Rehearse [[s01-N15]] together.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 14's exit and practice s01-P14 with feedback. If day 14's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square; error B: cover each counted square. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one group. Ordered-word needs, error C, use slow words before paired moves.

#### 8 min. Correct a puppet count

Review touch and move. Use [[s01-K15]] with one paper puppet, invented for practice.

[[steps:s01-K15]]

**Ask.** After step 2, before the corrected action in step 3: [[s01-Q15]] Expect reaching the missed square and separating counted pieces to prevent another omission or repeat. Fifteen pairs correct simultaneously, one 45-second turn each, 1.5 minutes; the remaining 6.5 cover demonstration and handling. No acorns or blank self-explanation panels.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use the reserved kite and boat masters from day 15, items s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c, with ten assigned children not heard earlier. Three asks at 45 seconds each plus 45 seconds for a brief written note and one missed-count retry total three minutes each, thirty minutes. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Dot cut-paper pieces; scribe the explanation. Record actual sequence, object actions, chosen support and help on the class list. Others repeat known five- or ten-piece centers and complete today's practice paper. Give written feedback now; classify records on day 18, after this revision opportunity. A prompted revision is shown with help. Face check pieces away from centers and speak quietly. No reserved case appears at other centers. Use [[s01-P15]] for the supplied-route center. The practice arrow stops below the fourth square; extend it to the fifth. A reason names what the correction changes, rather than only giving a total. Teacher-only key for these three items: d018-key.html.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most demonstrate a corrected action for 45 seconds each. Give brief feedback and return table trays together. Public talk may be passed.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X15]] exactly. Let children count for themselves first, unscored. They draw the tracking explanation on the acorn paper; hear six reasons, one ask each. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 16. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use the boxed picture master as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Print Side 1 and the boxed master from the teacher page. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Show the puppet reaching only four of five squares. Your child finishes the supplied route on Side 1 and demonstrates the corrected action.
- **10 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P15]].
  **Say.** "What did the puppet need to change?" Expect reach the missed square and keep counted pieces apart.
- **3 min. Observation.** If no record exists, use day 18's two check sides once. Ask its three prompts separately; note initial work, feedback and a retry. Keep the record.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X15]] exactly on the uncovered picture master. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice corrects a supplied puppet route; the exit produces a tracking explanation for a new acorn case.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; last word from day 14. Explain a corrected count names how the changed action reaches each object once. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 revisits adjacent linked marks. Return day 15 exit and practice in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square; error B: cover counted pieces. Continue observations with ten different children. Retain check records for day 18.
