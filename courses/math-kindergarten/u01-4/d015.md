# Day 15: Correct the counting action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-15.md and covered exit-15.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools. Cover assessment.md, kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md for today's eight conferences.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Partners move five loose squares, one word per move, in simultaneous 45-second turns. Review day 14 last-word conversation with [[s01-W15]], without scoring cardinality. Rehearse [[s01-N15]] together.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 14's exit and practice s01-P14 with feedback. If day 14's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square; error B: cover each counted square. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one group. Ordered-word needs, error C, use slow words before paired moves. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Correct a puppet count

Review touch and move. Use [[s01-K15]] with one paper puppet, invented for practice.

[[steps:s01-K15]]

**Ask.** After step 2, before the corrected action in step 3: [[s01-Q15]] Expect reaching the missed square and separating counted pieces to prevent another omission or repeat. Fifteen pairs correct simultaneously, one 45-second turn each, 1.5 minutes; the remaining 6.5 cover demonstration and handling.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use u01-1/assessment.md, u01-4/kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md with roster children 1 to 8. Read s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c separately, exactly as printed. Six kites start in a line; nine boats start two high, four middle, three low. Each 3.5-minute conference holds three 45-second asks, one possible 45-second retry and 30 seconds for private feedback/reset. Eight conferences use 28 minutes; two remain for materials. Children dot the indicated pieces; scribe their explanation. Retain initial/revised words, actions, chosen support, help and heard names in record.md. A strategy cue means shown with help. Retry only the first obstruction and queue another. Classify on day 18 after this feedback/revision. Keep check pieces covered from other centers; run no simultaneous teacher group. Others review Counting centers with u01-1/cards-centers.md and return today's practice paper. At the supplied-route center use [[s01-P15]]. The arrow stops below the fourth square; extend it to the fifth, then demonstrate with loose pieces. A reason names what the correction changes.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most demonstrate a corrected action for 45 seconds each. Give brief feedback and return table trays together. Public talk may be passed.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-15.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X15]] exactly. Let children count for themselves first, unscored. They draw the tracking explanation on the acorn paper; hear six reasons, one ask each. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 16, Return and repair. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-15.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-15.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations. If unheard, use assessment.md once in 3.5 practice minutes: three asks, one retry and feedback. Keep the record.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Show the puppet reaching only four of five squares. Your child finishes the supplied route on Side 1 and demonstrates the corrected action.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P15]].
  **Say.** "What did the puppet need to change?" Expect reach the missed square and keep counted pieces apart.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X15]] exactly on exit-15.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; last word from day 14. Explain a corrected count names how the changed action reaches each object once. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 revisits adjacent linked marks. Return day 15 exit and practice in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square; error B: cover counted pieces. Continue observations with the next eight children. Retain check records for day 18.
