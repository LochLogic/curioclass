# Day 13: Choose a way to keep track

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep crayon exit mat and cut-paper supply covered. Prepare five and ten loose squares, boundary mats and covers. Select six rotating exit listeners and six practice listeners. Bring day 12 papers with feedback. Keep large pieces on trays. Teacher-only answer key: d013-key.html. Until the section opener exists, use this page's setup and directions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 12 partner-guided rows with [[s01-W13]], then [[s01-N13]] together.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 12's exit and practice with feedback. If day 12's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square once; error D: pause after one word until the next move. Choose one action for five minutes, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group while others use familiar rows.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review the Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12. Introduce scattered as pieces spread around. Model [[s01-K13]], then [[steps:s01-K13]]. Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F13]], one puppet, invented for practice. A done pile means a place for finished pieces; name it in conversation without counting into a pile.

#### 14 min. Choose a boundary or cover

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously with five, then ten loose squares. Children choose a boundary or covers before counting, without a teacher-selected route. Two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes per pair; eleven remain for choices, resetting and [[s01-P13]]. Collect the traced record. If many revisit, leave counted pieces covered. Do not rehearse collecting into a pile.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Repeat familiar moving, covering or low constructions with five or ten pieces. Hear six practice children at 45 seconds each while others continue. Optional final five minutes: children already secure may explore twenty loose pieces with covers, unassessed, with adult-supported words as needed. No other child needs this choice.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a support, 45 seconds each. Children may pass. Return materials in table trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X13]] exactly. Give the blank exit mat and eight separately cut crayons; children create their own done pile, dot and trace its pieces. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 14. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Hand-copy exact master stems and pictures if printing is unavailable; cover reserved papers. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters, strips replace cubes, paper shapes replace blocks, drawn boxes replace mats. Use the covered exit master.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Revisit yesterday's paper with one practice action.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Repeat today's loose-piece demonstration.
- **13 min. Practice.** Choose a boundary or covers for five loose squares; exchange roles. Complete [[s01-P13]].
  **Say.** "Show one object for each counting word." Expect one distinct action per object, with ordered words when heard.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X13]] exactly with the fresh master. Your child marks paper independently. Keep it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice crosses a boundary or covers pieces in place; the exit creates a done pile from a fresh crayon scatter.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12. New: scattered and done pile. A pile is named without practicing its reserved counting task. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 counts movable collections and connects the last word with how many. Return day 13 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh piece. Error B: cover each piece after its word. Choose one repair; unheard words remain not yet seen.
