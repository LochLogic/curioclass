# Day 13: Choose a way to keep track

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-13.md and covered exit-13.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools. Cut crayon-pieces.md and prepare covers.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Review day 12 partner-guided rows with [[s01-W13]], then [[s01-N13]] together. Partners take one 45-second moving turn each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 12's exit and practice s01-P12 with feedback. If day 12's tickets showed error A, reach every fresh square once; error D: pause after one word until the next move. Choose one action for five minutes, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group while others use familiar rows. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review the Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12. Introduce scattered as pieces spread around. Model [[s01-K13]] with loose pieces.

[[steps:s01-K13]] Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F13]], one puppet, invented for practice. Gather unused covers in a done pile: a place for finished pieces. Children name it; keep counted squares covered in place or across a boundary.

#### 14 min. Choose a boundary or cover

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously with five, then ten loose squares. Children choose a boundary or covers before counting, without a teacher-selected route. Two 45-second turns per partner take three minutes per pair; eleven remain for choices, resetting and [[s01-P13]]. Collect the traced record. If many revisit, leave counted pieces covered. Do not rehearse collecting into a pile.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers using u01-1/cards-centers.md: moving, covering or low constructions with five or ten pieces. Hear six practice children at 45 seconds each while others continue. Optional final five minutes: children already secure may explore twenty loose pieces with covers, unassessed, with adult-supported words as needed. No other child needs this choice.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a support, 45 seconds each. Children may pass. Return materials in table trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-13.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X13]] exactly. Use crayon-pieces.md: eight large cut crayons scattered as on its master, beside the exit mat; children move them into their own done pile, keep every piece visible, and dot and trace the pieces. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 14, Return and repair. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-13.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-13.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Let your child choose covers or a boundary for five scattered squares. Trace the layout and its support on Side 1.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P13]].
  **Say.** "Show a way to keep track of these scattered squares." Expect covers or one boundary crossing per square.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X13]] exactly on exit-13.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12. New: scattered and done pile. A pile is named without practicing its reserved counting task. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 counts movable collections and connects the last word with how many. Return day 13 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh piece. Error B: cover each piece after its word. Choose one repair; unheard words remain not yet seen.
