# Day 12: One word for each object

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the boxed spoon exit card; keep it covered. Lay out five and ten large loose pieces and boundary mats. Bring day 11 papers with feedback. Select six rotating exit listeners on the class list. Use intact pieces on trays; reject sharp or swallowable materials. Teacher-only answer key: d012-key.html. Until the opener exists, this page supplies setup and directions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 11's complete place partners with [[s01-W12]]. Partners take one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Review [[s01-N12]] slowly together; support missing words. Oral counting through ten is review from day 6.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 11's exit and s01-P11 with feedback. If day 11's tickets showed error E, check five fresh square-place pairs before judging enough. Error B: cover a supplied place before choosing another. Choose one five-minute repair, whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one small group while others revisit familiar pairs.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Name a line as a row and its boundary as the dividing line. Name counted and uncounted by showing the two sides of a mat. Model [[s01-K12]] while saying one, two, three, four, five on five different moves.

[[steps:s01-K12]] Show right before wrong in [[s01-F12]]. Use one paper puppet; say the scene is invented for practice.

#### 14 min. Partner-guided lines

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. One partner points along five loose squares; the counter moves each across the boundary with one word. Repeat with ten, then exchange roles. Two 45-second counts per child take three minutes per pair, leaving eleven for setup, checking and [[s01-P12]]. Collect practice paper. If many omit a piece, touch every remaining piece before stopping. No independent fixed-picture dot count.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers: loose-piece moves, square-place matching or low constructions with five or ten pieces. Keep exit and check materials covered. Support one group; hear six practice children at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record observed actions on the class list.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most demonstrate a move, 45 seconds each; children may pass. Give feedback, then table groups return trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X12]] exactly. Children dot each spoon once while counting; no supplied route or partner pointer. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 13. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use the boxed picture master as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Print Side 1 and the boxed master from the teacher page. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Point along five loose squares while your child moves each across the line; count one through five. Trace the moved squares on Side 1.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P12]].
  **Say.** "Move each square across the line as I point." Expect five moves with one through five.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X12]] exactly on the uncovered picture master. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice moves loose rows with a partner pointer; the exit independently dots a stationary spoon line.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: place, enough and Object-place pairs from day 10. New: line, counted and uncounted, named through mat actions. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 chooses a boundary or covers for scattered objects. Return day 12 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach each fresh square once. Error D: pause after one word until the next move. Keep unheard words not yet seen.
