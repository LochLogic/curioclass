# Day 12: One word for each object

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-12.md and covered exit-12.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools. Prepare boundary-mat.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 11's complete place partners with [[s01-W12]]. Partners take one 45-second turn each, simultaneously. Review [[s01-N12]] slowly together; support missing words. Oral counting through ten is review from day 6.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 11's exit and s01-P11 with feedback. If day 11's tickets showed error E, check five fresh square-place pairs before judging enough. Error B: cover a supplied place before choosing another. Choose one five-minute repair, whole class if more than ten need it; otherwise one small group while others revisit familiar pairs. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Name a line as a row and its boundary as the dividing line. Counted means we have said a word for that object; uncounted means we have not. Show the two sides of boundary-mat.md. Model [[s01-K12]] while saying one, two, three, four, five on five different moves.

[[steps:s01-K12]] Show right before wrong in [[s01-F12]]. Use one paper puppet; say the scene is invented for practice.

#### 14 min. Partner-guided lines

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. One partner points along five loose squares; the counter moves each across the boundary with one word. Repeat with ten, then exchange roles. Two 45-second counts per child take three minutes per pair, leaving eleven for setup, checking and [[s01-P12]]. Collect practice paper. If many omit a piece, touch every remaining piece before stopping.

#### 16 min. Familiar centers

Review Counting centers using u01-1/cards-centers.md: loose-piece moves, square-place matching or low constructions with five or ten pieces. Keep exit and check materials covered. Support one group; hear six practice children at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes. Record observed actions on the class list.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most demonstrate a move, 45 seconds each; children may pass. Give feedback, then table groups return trays together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-12.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X12]] exactly. Children dot each spoon once while counting; no supplied route or partner pointer. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 13, Return and repair. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-12.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-12.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Point along five loose squares while your child moves each across the line; count one through five. Trace the moved squares on Side 1.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P12]].
  **Say.** "Move each square across the line as I point." Expect five moves with one through five.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X12]] exactly on exit-12.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: place, enough and Object-place pairs from day 10. New: line, a row; boundary, the dividing line; counted and uncounted, shown on the mat. Children use these words while moving pieces. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 chooses a boundary or covers for scattered objects. Return day 12 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach each fresh square once. Error D: pause after one word until the next move. Keep unheard words not yet seen.
