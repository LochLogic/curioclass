# Day 137: I can show adding and taking one.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately. Use the student page for today's partial practice; set out mixed game cards, optional frames, large paper counters, feedback pictures and unit record. Print exit-137.md as the sealed half slip. Keep unused evidence masters sealed. Read all print aloud. Select today's six observation names. Pre-draw the named fresh records from their item steps. Prepare the named fresh repair on scrap paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review the familiar within-twenty sequence and tens from day 104: [[s08-Q137]] [[steps:s08-Q137]] Echo each supplied word and mark the paper route; no score.

#### 10 min. Counting instruction

Read [[s08-Q137-teach]] and demonstrate its successive spoken names: [[steps:s08-Q137-teach]] Keep numeral writing and object counts within twenty; the longer sequence is oral only. This is first teaching, not a fluency game.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return all of day 136's written tasks, including game results, practice pictures, count routes and repair records, with their picture feedback. Return day 136's exit, practice/game and transfer papers with picture feedback; transfer revision waits until day 142. Review Return and repair. If many tickets show error B, repair supplied links: [[s08-R137]]. [[steps:s08-R137]] Otherwise use the slot with the affected group while others repeat partial practice. Other needs remain pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review one more, day 36, and Original whole with separate moved region, day 68. Say "Invented for practice." Rin is the only actor. Read [[s08-P137-1]], act with paper pieces, then reveal correction: [[steps:s08-P137-1]] Read [[s08-P137-2]] before correcting its added piece: [[steps:s08-P137-2]] Error C joins during removal; keep removed pieces visible outside the mat. Introduce the phrase one less: taking away one leaves one less. It names the familiar action; show it first. Introduce neighbors as the count words one step before or after a count. Demonstrate each direction on a separate change, never a paired neighbors sheet.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs repair supplied records, not blank complete stories. [[s08-P-join137]]: [[steps:s08-P-join137]] Then [[s08-P-take137]]: [[steps:s08-P-take137]] Each child marks both corrections. **Ask.** [[s08-P-take137]] again, pointing to the partial record; hear at most two voluntary 45-second contributors within these minutes. If many join during removal, act the arrow once into the moved region and retry the same partial record. Never use the exit story in teaching.

#### 10 min. Counting centers

Three minutes: children quietly correct one supplied fact picture using the original cards: [[s08-G137-1]] [[s08-G137-2]] [[s08-G137-3]] [[s08-G137-4]] [[s08-G137-5]] [[s08-G137-6]]. Each chooses one known addition and one removal; immediate checking uses the supplied picture. No maker/checker game today.

Five minutes: use the unit 7 priority transfer only for actual pending E/F names carried from day 136. Hear up to six 45-second responses and allow thirty seconds reset. Other children continue familiar independent paper. Write feedback afterward for day 142 revision.

Two minutes tidy and collect. Return the practice packet on day 138.

Check: Compare the corrected claim with its supplied quantity picture.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out day 137 sealed exit only now. Read [[s08-X137]] exactly, including the invented-for-practice label. Every child marks the paper picture; an adult may mark at the child's direction. Three minutes independent work, six optional 45-second observations and half-minute collection total eight. Today's six are roster 13 to 18; record names actually heard. No extra question or supplied strategy. Never infer an unheard reason.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class, allow thirty seconds per paper, fifteen minutes for thirty: use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately from mathematical help and give a specific picture note. Return every exit and today's practice on day 138; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw the supplied pictures and dot routes. Paper and objects give the full path; one optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. No device turns. An adult moves or marks at child direction, recording access separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters; hand-drawn cards/frames replace kits or printing. A broad crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback and revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's short stretch and tens with your child; touch a paper dot route.
- **5 min. Show.** Use today's partial practice picture and demonstrate its correction with paper pieces.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s08-P137-2]] **Say.** "Repair the partial taking picture." Expect six with one removed leaves five.
- **9 min. Count and paper.** First demonstrate the new spoken names with [[s08-Q137-teach]], then repeat one supplied partial correction. No independent full recital or game today.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X137]] Read exactly; child marks today's unseen picture on paper. Keep it for day 138 picture feedback. Expected response: eight.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving first teaching, response and exit. Longer: use a different tracking route on the same partial picture, within the day's number limits.

### Words and supports

Review object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track through the partial records. Review zero/none with the empty game result, order with supplied labels, add/take away in the game. Review + and -, day 78, by acting their changes. All directions read aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signing, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review optional 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; each piece means one.

### Tomorrow

Day 138 returns all papers. Error D leaves the old total after an arrival; error C joins during removal. Choose one priority repair in its five-minute Return and repair. Next, rehearse neighboring counts, using one single change at a time.
