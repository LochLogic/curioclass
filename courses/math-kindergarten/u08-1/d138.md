# Day 138: I can explain one more and one less.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately. Use the student page for today's partial practice; set out mixed game cards, optional frames, large paper counters, feedback pictures and unit record. Print exit-138.md as the sealed half slip. Keep unused evidence masters sealed. Read all print aloud. Select today's six observation names. Pre-draw the named fresh records from their item steps. Prepare the named fresh repair on scrap paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. Read [[s08-Q138]]; demonstrate its short stretch, then children echo and touch the paper dot route. [[steps:s08-Q138]] No individual full recital or written numerals above twenty. If words are missed, echo that stretch again; no score.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return all of day 137's written tasks, including game results, practice pictures, count routes and repair records, with their picture feedback. Return day 137's exit and practice/game papers. Review Return and repair. For error C, use [[s08-R138]], [[steps:s08-R138]] For error D instead, [[s08-R-D138]], [[steps:s08-R-D138]] Choose one priority per child, not two sequential groups. If more than a third need it, use the full-class slot. Others repeat their partial practice; further needs stay pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review one more, day 36, and one less/neighbors, day 137. Read [[s08-P138-1]] before revealing correction: [[steps:s08-P138-1]] Move one large paper counter, give it one new counting word, then label. Error D leaves total unchanged. **Ask.** [[s08-Q-why138]]. [[steps:s08-Q-why138]] At most two voluntary 45-second explanations; all mark the arrival. A correct numeral alone never establishes an unheard explanation.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First nine minutes: Work alone, then compare corrections. First [[s08-P138-2]] on partial practice: [[steps:s08-P138-2]] Remove it before [[s08-P-less138]]: [[steps:s08-P-less138]] Use separate single-change records; never a paired neighbors sheet around a middle collection. Review Original whole with separate moved region for removal. If many keep the old count, move the piece visibly and recount, then label again.

Final three minutes: uncover the one-less record and read [[s08-A138-less]]. Every child independently makes the changed collection on paper. No additional heard ask. Write feedback afterward; revise in day 139 centers before day 151 judgment.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Three 90-second rounds, switching maker/checker after 45 seconds, plus 30-second tidy fit five minutes. Read separate cards: [[s08-G138-1]] [[s08-G138-2]] [[s08-G138-3]] [[s08-G138-4]] [[s08-G138-5]] [[s08-G138-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; objects remain optional. Teacher gives immediate feedback from the private answer key. Partner checks the supplied picture by counting; no speed contest. Use the shared nonzero-addend cards `s08-G135-7` `s08-G135-8` `s08-G135-9` `s08-G135-10` from game-addition-cards.md in the same three rounds: two additions and one removal per child. Open section 8.1's private packet for their supplied-picture keys. These fixed cards return for rehearsal, with no extra rounds.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: simultaneous partners repeat these supplied partial changes with optional frames/counters and individual paper records. Accept recounting; no new shortcut. Teacher notices whether a changed collection gets a changed count and gives immediate feedback. Two minutes tidy. Return papers day 139.

Within the existing thirteen practice minutes, reserve five for actual pending unit 7 E/F language names from day 136: up to six 45-second turns plus thirty seconds reset. Others continue independent known paper; feedback returns for day 142 revision. 

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Read the minus-one and plus-one icons as access directions, without supplying counts. Children complete the two picture spaces as one neighboring-count relation.

Hand out day 138 sealed exit only now. Read [[s08-X138]] exactly, including the invented-for-practice label. Every child marks the paper picture; an adult may mark at the child's direction. Three minutes independent work, six optional 45-second observations and half-minute collection total eight. Today's six are roster 19 to 24; record names actually heard. No extra question or supplied strategy. Never infer an unheard reason.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class, allow thirty seconds per paper, fifteen minutes for thirty: use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately from mathematical help and give a specific picture note. Return every exit and today's practice on day 139; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw the supplied pictures and dot routes. Paper and objects give the full path; one optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. No device turns. An adult moves or marks at child direction, recording access separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters; hand-drawn cards/frames replace kits or printing. A broad crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback and revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's short stretch and tens with your child; touch a paper dot route.
- **5 min. Show.** Use today's partial practice picture and demonstrate its correction with paper pieces.
- **5 min. Change.** [[s08-P138-1]] **Say.** "Correct this supplied next count." Expect four gains one and becomes five.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Alternate maker/checker with the same mixed game cards for five minutes; child marks a result each turn. Then repeat a partial correction. Give immediate feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X138]] Read exactly; child marks today's unseen picture on paper. Keep it for day 139 picture feedback. Expected response: ten before, twelve after.

Use one-less-138.md in the last three Paper and game minutes, replacing repeated supplied practice. Read its one prompt and keep the independent paper for day 139 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving response, game and exit. Longer: use a different tracking route on the same partial picture, within the day's number limits.

### Words and supports

Review object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track through the partial records. Review zero/none with the empty game result, order with supplied labels, add/take away in the game. Review + and -, day 78, by acting their changes. All directions read aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signing, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review optional 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; each piece means one.

### Tomorrow

Day 139 returns all papers and repairs error D, keeping the old count after a change, with a new supplied partial arrival. It then repairs numeral order and supplied starts; no extra independent neighbors test.
