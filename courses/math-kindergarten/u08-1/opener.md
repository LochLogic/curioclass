# Counting and comparing

Review count, sort, category, fewest, most, more, fewer, equal and one more with gestures and fresh partial records. First demonstrate one less and neighbors on day 137: take away one, then connect the result to the count just before. These are not assumed written prerequisites. Counted-uncounted boundary separates visited objects. Complete one-to-one matching with visible extras makes a comparison independent of spacing. Given-category mats with counted and ordered groups connects categories, counts and order. Original whole with separate moved region preserves what was present before taking. Echo supplied start then continue begins at the named word.

Keep comparisons within ten, arranged counts/numerals to twenty, scattered counts to ten, stories within ten. Retrieval day 135 is diagnostic only. Day 135 centers receive actual unit 7 feedback; day 136 assigns one fresh geometry transfer per child, then day 142 revises written feedback before judgment. Recognition does not establish an unheard naming component.

Supplies: large paper counters replace loose counters; hand-drawn frames/cards replace kits. Plain box, paper ball and folded cone replace solid models; pencil/patterns replace crayons. Use frames.md, game-cards.md, game-addition-cards.md, retrieval-135.md, daily practice/exit masters, feedback.md and record.md. Keep end records in this section's folder. No readiness check is added.

Read every printed word aloud. No independent reading is required. Print pictures at 18 point with thick boundaries. Offer large tactile marks, directed adult marks, usual communication aids and home-language reasoning. Record access support separately from mathematical help. A child may pass in public talk.

Before class, open key-teacher.md privately, print only today's partial practice and sealed evidence master, and put unused reserved masters away. The key is never a student handout. All pictures and quantities are invented for practice; say so aloud. Real objects are clean, large and handled low on the table. Adult cuts paper. No small loose pieces, sharp joins, unstable stacks or required device.

Use Counting centers, Partner turns, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair with the ledger's wording. Demonstrate intended review again when earlier teaching is absent. A planned ledger entry is not evidence of learning. Ten means ten actual individual ones, never one counter representing ten. Optional 5-frame and 10-frame connect objects and pictures; supplied frames withdraw day 149, child choice remains with support recorded.

Every child marks each exit paper. On days 135 to 145, three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and half a minute collection fit eight minutes. Rotate six names across five days. On days 146 to 151, at most two 45-second observations are embedded in five paper minutes; these samples never require every child to speak. Paper evidence suffices only for its named look-fors. Never infer a matching strategy, sequence or explanation from a mark.

Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep separate descriptive strategy notes, names heard, access and mathematical help. Counting all can be accurate and is not an error; it does not by itself establish fluent arithmetic. Missing components stay not yet seen with a pending note. Return specific written picture feedback; revise on the named later day before judging a product. No repeated secure check.

The daily untimed mixed game uses game-cards.md and game-addition-cards.md from section 8.1: three parallel partner rounds, 45 seconds for each child per round, plus thirty seconds tidy fit five minutes. Every child marks paper and receives immediate feedback. Rehearse the supplied fact images, not the reserved expressions. The oral count is teacher-started/echoed short stretches and tens, not individual full recitals. Written numerals stop at twenty. No hundred chart or number line.

Paper and objects provide the full path. Draw these original masters by hand if there is no printer. One optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. At home, adult is partner, with the section plan's thirty-minute schedule and the same sealed exit. Use only supplied partial practice and the permitted game. Never rehearse a later evidence case.


Expected errors: A revisits object; N missorts category; B uses spread as amount; C joins during removal; D leaves total unchanged; E restarts at one. Anticipated examples and fresh repair routes are in the private key/data, not claims about prevalence.

Use sort-label-135.md only for the separate Model/Connected work paper checks, then the already-sorted exit. Day 136 has nine priority geometry routes: transfer-136a.md, transfer-136b.md, transfer-136c.md and the flat, behind, cone, picture, parts and body transfer masters. Each child receives just one. For missing relation/name evidence, hear six per day on days 136 to 140; use the funded five Counting centers minutes on 137 to 140. Keep other children on familiar independent paper.

Day 137 Counting lasts fifteen minutes, including ten minutes first teaching the oral ones names through one hundred; omit that day's optional game and use ten Counting centers minutes. Never infer a full sequence from an echoed stretch.
