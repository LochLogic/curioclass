# Day 139: I can order counts and start inside the count.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Use the student page for today's partial practice; set out mixed game cards, optional frames, large paper counters, feedback pictures and unit record. Print exit-139.md as the sealed half slip. Keep unused evidence masters sealed. Read all print aloud. Select today's six observation names. Pre-draw the named fresh records from their item steps. Prepare the named fresh repair on scrap paper.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. Read [[s08-Q139]]; demonstrate its short stretch, then children echo and touch the paper dot route. [[steps:s08-Q139]] No individual full recital or written numerals above twenty. If words are missed, echo that stretch again; no score.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return all of day 138's written tasks, including game results, practice pictures, count routes and repair records, with their picture feedback. Return day 138's exit and practice/game papers. Review Return and repair. If many left the total unchanged, error D, [[s08-R139]]: [[steps:s08-R139]] Otherwise meet affected children while others repeat single-change records. One five-minute priority; remaining needs pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. Read [[s08-P139-1]] before showing correction: [[steps:s08-P139-1]] Error E restarts at one. Point at the supplied start, echo that word, continue; this is counting from a number, not an addition strategy. Everyone marks the corrected route on partial practice.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Teacher supplies the sequence; children repair one gap: [[s08-P139-2]], [[steps:s08-P139-2]] Then [[s08-P-order139]], [[steps:s08-P-order139]] No independent four-array sort. **Ask.** [[s08-P139-2]] while pointing to the gap; hear at most two 45-second volunteers, all mark. Review numeral/count; preserve neighboring labels. If many restart, teacher supplies the preceding word again before a fresh echo.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Three 90-second rounds, switching maker/checker after 45 seconds, plus 30-second tidy fit five minutes. Read separate cards: [[s08-G139-1]] [[s08-G139-2]] [[s08-G139-3]] [[s08-G139-4]] [[s08-G139-5]] [[s08-G139-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; objects remain optional. Teacher gives immediate feedback from the private answer key. Partner checks the supplied picture by counting; no speed contest.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: pairs work on supplied sequences, never mixed quantity-card sorts. [[s08-P-start139]]: [[steps:s08-P-start139]] Then [[s08-P-label139]]: [[steps:s08-P-label139]] Each child marks a paper count route and label; optional frames remain available. Teacher records who actually starts at the supplied word, gives immediate feedback, and returns all practice day 140. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Adults pre-cut the four array cards. Children arrange them and record the order on the half slip; quantities stay attached to their numerals. Show no answer strip beforehand. Order alone does not establish an unheard oral start.

Hand out day 139 sealed exit only now. Read [[s08-X139]] exactly, including the invented-for-practice label. Every child marks the paper picture; an adult may mark at the child's direction. Three minutes independent work, six optional 45-second observations and half-minute collection total eight. Today's six are roster 25 to 30; record names actually heard. No extra question or supplied strategy. Never infer an unheard reason.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class, allow thirty seconds per paper, fifteen minutes for thirty: use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately from mathematical help and give a specific picture note. Return every exit and today's practice on day 140; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw the supplied pictures and dot routes. Paper and objects give the full path; one optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. No device turns. An adult moves or marks at child direction, recording access separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters; hand-drawn cards/frames replace kits or printing. A broad crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback and revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's short stretch and tens with your child; touch a paper dot route.
- **5 min. Show.** Use today's partial practice picture and demonstrate its correction with paper pieces.
- **5 min. Start.** [[s08-P139-1]] **Say.** "Repair this supplied start." Expect eight, nine, ten.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Alternate maker/checker with the same mixed game cards for five minutes; child marks a result each turn. Then repeat a partial correction. Give immediate feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X139]] Read exactly; child marks today's unseen picture on paper. Keep it for day 140 picture feedback. Expected response: thirteen, fourteen, fifteen, sixteen.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving response, game and exit. Longer: use a different tracking route on the same partial picture, within the day's number limits.

### Words and supports

Review object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track through the partial records. Review zero/none with the empty game result, order with supplied labels, add/take away in the game. Review + and -, day 78, by acting their changes. All directions read aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signing, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review optional 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; each piece means one.

### Tomorrow

Day 140 returns all papers in Return and repair. A descending order gets a descriptive note, error x. Error E is used only if restarting at one was actually heard in practice. Echo a supplied six, then continue seven/eight in the existing five-minute slot. Keep other needs pending. Next, plan classroom number-book pages.
