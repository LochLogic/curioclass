# Day 136: I can count and compare groups.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately. Use the student page for today's partial practice; set out mixed game cards, optional frames, large paper counters, feedback pictures and unit record. Print exit-136.md as the sealed half slip. Keep unused evidence masters sealed. Read all print aloud. Select today's six observation names. Pre-draw the named fresh records from their item steps. Assign one unit 7 priority transfer per child from the actual retained record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. Read [[s08-Q136]]; demonstrate its short stretch, then children echo and touch the paper dot route. [[steps:s08-Q136]] No individual full recital or written numerals above twenty. If words are missed, echo that stretch again; no score.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return all of day 135's written tasks, including game results, practice pictures, count routes and repair records, with their picture feedback. Return day 135's exit, game, practice and diagnostic notes. Review Return and repair. First five minutes, error A: [[s08-R-A136]], [[steps:s08-R-A136]] Next five, error N: [[s08-R-N136]], [[steps:s08-R-N136]] If more than a third need a repair, all follow; otherwise teacher meets that group while others revisit partial practice. Keep other needs pending.

Return day 135's sort and label record as well. Children independently revise its written feedback within these same ten minutes while the teacher runs the already allocated A/N priority groups. Keep originals for eligible day 151 judgment.

#### 7 min. Model

Review more/fewer, day 21, equal, day 22, and Complete one-to-one matching with visible extras, day 21. Read [[s08-P136-1]] before correcting supplied links: [[steps:s08-P136-1]] Error B uses spread as amount. Four has fewer partners; six has extras. Then [[s08-K-equal136]]: [[steps:s08-K-equal136]] Count partners, not line length. Comparisons stay within ten; larger collections are counted singly.

#### 5 min. Connected work

All repair the supplied single line: [[s08-P136-2]], [[steps:s08-P136-2]] Review numeral, day 30. Children mark visits and label the count. A missing mark is not proof of error A; use that letter only when a repeated visit is seen. If many struggle, move pieces across a boundary before marking.

#### 25 min. Response centers
First ten: retain the earlier day 134 judgment; give each child only their first unmet unit 7 route: [[s08-T136a]] [[s08-T136b]] [[s08-T136c]] [[s08-T136-flat]] [[s08-T136-behind]] [[s08-T136-cone]] [[s08-T136-picture]] [[s08-T136-parts]] [[s08-T136-body]]. Read up to nine route stems in 1.5 minutes, then three concurrent paper minutes. Hear six actual E/F language responses, 45 seconds each, with one minute collection/reset. Others work independently. Record names and words actually heard. Continue pending E/F names in the funded five-minute center slots on days 137 to 140. Give written feedback, then independent day 142 geometry revision before judgment. Paper marks do not establish unheard names or positions.

Next five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Three 90-second rounds, switching maker/checker after 45 seconds, plus 30-second tidy fit five minutes. Read separate cards: [[s08-G136-1]] [[s08-G136-2]] [[s08-G136-3]] [[s08-G136-4]] [[s08-G136-5]] [[s08-G136-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; objects remain optional. Teacher gives immediate feedback from the private answer key. Partner checks the supplied picture by counting; no speed contest. Use the shared nonzero-addend cards `s08-G135-7` `s08-G135-8` `s08-G135-9` `s08-G135-10` from game-addition-cards.md in the same three rounds: two additions and one removal per child. Open section 8.1's private packet for their supplied-picture keys. These fixed cards return for rehearsal, with no extra rounds.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Eight minutes: distribute four single-collection repairs, two minutes each, no comparison pairs: [[s08-P-arr136]] [[s08-P-circle136]] [[s08-P-scatter136]] [[s08-P-out136]]. [[steps:s08-P-arr136]] [[steps:s08-P-circle136]] [[steps:s08-P-scatter136]] [[steps:s08-P-out136]] Two minutes tidy. Give immediate feedback; return papers day 137 without a new geometry judgment. Allow thirty seconds per transfer for written feedback after class, fifteen minutes total.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out day 136 sealed exit only now. Read [[s08-X136]] exactly, including the invented-for-practice label. Every child marks the paper picture; an adult may mark at the child's direction. Three minutes independent work, six optional 45-second observations and half-minute collection total eight. Today's six are roster 7 to 12; record names actually heard. No extra question or supplied strategy. Never infer an unheard reason.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class, allow thirty seconds per paper, fifteen minutes for thirty: use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately from mathematical help and give a specific picture note. Return every exit and today's practice on day 137; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw the supplied pictures and dot routes. Paper and objects give the full path; one optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. No device turns. An adult moves or marks at child direction, recording access separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters; hand-drawn cards/frames replace kits or printing. A broad crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback and revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's short stretch and tens with your child; touch a paper dot route.
- **5 min. Show.** Use today's partial practice picture and demonstrate its correction with paper pieces.
- **5 min. Match.** [[s08-P136-1]] **Say.** "Repair these supplied links." Expect four unique pairs and two extras in six.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Alternate maker/checker with the same mixed game cards for five minutes; child marks a result each turn. Then repeat a partial correction. Give immediate feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X136]] Read exactly; child marks today's unseen picture on paper. Keep it for day 137 picture feedback. Expected response: nine circles.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving response, game and exit. Longer: use a different tracking route on the same partial picture, within the day's number limits.

### Words and supports

Review object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track through the partial records. Review zero/none with the empty game result, order with supplied labels, add/take away in the game. Review + and -, day 78, by acting their changes. All directions read aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signing, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review optional 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; each piece means one.

### Tomorrow

Day 137 returns all papers and repairs error B, choosing the wider group as more, with fresh supplied links. Unit 7 priority transfers receive written feedback now, revise day 142 before judgment. Unheard shape names stay pending. Next, adding-one and taking-one stories.
