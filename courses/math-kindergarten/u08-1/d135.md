# Day 135: I can organize groups.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately. Use the student page for today's partial practice; set out mixed game cards, optional frames, large paper counters, feedback pictures and unit record. Print exit-135.md as the sealed half slip. Keep unused evidence masters sealed. Read all print aloud. Select today's six observation names. Pre-draw the named fresh records from their item steps. Bring day 134 feedback and retained papers plus diagnostic retrieval cards.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Whole class, picking up day 134's promise to organize classroom categories. Review sort/category, day 41, and fewest/most, day 36. Supplied partial cap/eraser picture: [[s08-H135]]. [[steps:s08-H135]] Everyone marks one proposed move; hear at most two 45-second suggestions. Keep the rest supplied. Say the unit question aloud; no complete independent sort.

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. Read [[s08-Q135]]; demonstrate its short stretch, then children echo and touch the paper dot route. [[steps:s08-Q135]] No individual full recital or written numerals above twenty. If words are missed, echo that stretch again; no score.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Two minutes: [[s08-Q135-flat]] on diagnostic retrieval cards beside the actual cube. Review flat/triangle, day 45, solid, day 4, cube, day 1. Three minutes: [[s08-Q135-count]]. All mark; scan without scoring. [[steps:s08-Q135-count]] These diagnostics select support, never replace retained evidence.

#### 7 min. Model

Review Given-category mats with counted and ordered groups, day 41, and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Read [[s08-P135-1]] before revealing correction: [[steps:s08-P135-1]] Error N leaves a stripe on dot mat. Then [[s08-F135]]: [[steps:s08-F135]] Error A revisits an object. Move once, mark once; the last count names the total. Relate the supplied order to counts without constructing a whole new ordered sort.

#### 2 min. Connected work

Alone: [[s08-P135-2]] on partial practice. [[steps:s08-P135-2]] All restore the omitted mark; teacher scans, no interview. If many miss it, repeat one-to-one touching during centers.

#### 25 min. Response centers

First ten: receive day 134's first unmet geometry component. Review Return and repair, day 2. Five-minute feature demonstration from the actual picture note, then five-minute later revision: [[s08-R135]]. Preserve originals; new day 134 work revises now and is judged day 136. Missing components remain pending; no repeated battery or invented earlier success.

Next five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. Three 90-second rounds, switching maker/checker after 45 seconds, plus 30-second tidy fit five minutes. Read separate cards: [[s08-G135-1]] [[s08-G135-2]] [[s08-G135-3]] [[s08-G135-4]] [[s08-G135-5]] [[s08-G135-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; objects remain optional. Teacher gives immediate feedback from the private answer key. Partner checks the supplied picture by counting; no speed contest.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Eight minutes partial practice repeats, with individual paper records; two tidy. Retain diagnostic notes for day 136. Give immediate feedback; practice returns tomorrow.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

For the paper sort, children mark paths from the pictured objects to category mats, then connect the mats in their chosen order. The mat icons are labels, not objects to count. Give these response directions once; do not demonstrate a sort.

Hand out day 135 sealed exit only now. Read [[s08-X135]] exactly, including the invented-for-practice label. Every child marks the paper picture; an adult may mark at the child's direction. Three minutes independent work, six optional 45-second observations and half-minute collection total eight. Today's six are roster 1 to 6; record names actually heard. No extra question or supplied strategy. Never infer an unheard reason.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class, allow thirty seconds per paper, fifteen minutes for thirty: use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately from mathematical help and give a specific picture note. Return every exit and today's practice on day 136; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw the supplied pictures and dot routes. Paper and objects give the full path; one optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. No device turns. An adult moves or marks at child direction, recording access separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters; hand-drawn cards/frames replace kits or printing. A broad crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback and revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's short stretch and tens with your child; touch a paper dot route.
- **5 min. Show.** Use today's partial practice picture and demonstrate its correction with paper pieces.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s08-P135-1]] **Say.** "Repair this partial category sort." Expect move stripe to stripe mat.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Alternate maker/checker with the same mixed game cards for five minutes; child marks a result each turn. Then repeat a partial correction. Give immediate feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X135]] Read exactly; child marks today's unseen picture on paper. Keep it for day 136 picture feedback. Expected response: empty balls, three books, five pencils.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving response, game and exit. Longer: use a different tracking route on the same partial picture, within the day's number limits.

### Words and supports

Review object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track through the partial records. Review zero/none with the empty game result, order with supplied labels, add/take away in the game. Review + and -, day 78, by acting their changes. All directions read aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signing, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review optional 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; each piece means one.

### Tomorrow

Day 136 returns all papers: five minutes for error A, a repeated visit, and five for error N, a misplaced category card. It compares groups within ten and counts larger single collections. Day 134's new work, revised here, is judged there; missing geometry evidence stays pending.
