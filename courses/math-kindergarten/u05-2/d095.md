# Day 95: I can change parts and keep the total.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-95.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-95.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 94 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 19-24. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount95]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount95]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 94 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 94 tickets show error A, use first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-A95]] then [[steps:s05-F-A95]]. If they show error F, use second five: **Ask.** [[s05-F-F95]] then [[steps:s05-F-F95]]. Each includes a fresh supplied retry. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small groups while others correct familiar starters. With neither need, correct familiar records. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Story rehearsal

Day 94 assigned every object once; today change a labelled solution without changing the whole. Review One-up one-down transfer, day 87, and labelled counts, day 93. **Ask.** [[s05-Ktiles95]] before [[steps:s05-Ktiles95]]. Wait during the missing-count choice; connect the answer to the actual tiles. No new method. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; others handle pieces. Practice changes supplied components, never generates two complete solutions.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P95]] using practice-95.md. Identify the first region as patterned and the second as plain. The supplied second record already has two patterned; complete only its plain part. Watch for unchanged counts called a new solution, error B. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G95-1]], [[s05-G95-2]], [[s05-G95-3]], [[s05-G95-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-95.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X95]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 19-24 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares replace counters/tiles. Draw mats/frames, fold paper covers, and copy the supplied page and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount95]] Child touches six squares and leaves six dots.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Supply one patterned/five plain. Change one plain to patterned and supply the next patterned count two.
  **Say.** "Complete the changed part count." Expect: four plain; six altogether.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P95]] Child completes the supplied plain part; keep the labels fixed.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 94 feedback. Repair one missed component now; use another sitting for a second need.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X95]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone; keep both records for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: continue a supplied two/four labelled tile record to a started three/blank record and explain what stayed the same.

### Words and supports

Review patterned/plain, day 91, and labelled counts, day 93. Gesture the changed square and unchanged whole; no label reading required. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 96 applies representations to mixed story actions. Error B, identical labelled counts redrawn as different, gets five minutes comparing counts and changing one supplied tile record, including a fresh retry. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 96 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
