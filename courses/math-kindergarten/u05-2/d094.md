# Day 94: I can show story parts with pictures.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-94.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-94.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 93 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 13-18. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount94]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount94]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 93 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 93 tickets show error B, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-B94]] then [[steps:s05-F-B94]], fresh retry included. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters. Second five: older error A [[s05-F-A94]] with its retry, or familiar supplied corrections if no unmet need. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Story rehearsal

Day 93 showed that labeled parts can vary; today apply that idea to a block record. Review One counter stands for one story object, unit 4 day 71. **Ask.** [[s05-Kblocks94]] before [[steps:s05-Kblocks94]]. Show error A and error F separately so omission and double assignment remain distinct. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; others match objects. No new method and no blank whole-story drawing in practice.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P94]] using practice-94.md. Use one paper square per block picture. Point to long and short labels; judge membership, never the amount of paper. Watch for unused blocks, error A, or a block assigned twice, error F. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G94-1]], [[s05-G94-2]], [[s05-G94-3]], [[s05-G94-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-94.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X94]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 13-18 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares stand for counters or blocks; use drawn block outlines, mats/frames and folded covers. Copy the supplied page and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount94]] Child touches six squares and leaves six dots.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Supply two long/three short block marks and one loose short square.
  **Say.** "Repair the missing block assignment." Expect: two long/four short, each once.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P94]] Child repairs the supplied record with a line or moved square and paper mark.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 93 feedback; revise one supplied labeled record.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X94]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone. Keep it for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: explain how one supplied square corresponds to one block even when the pictured lengths differ.

### Words and supports

Review long/short, unit 2 day 41, and the labeled-count idea, day 93. A square can stand for a block of either length; its size does not change the count. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 95 changes labeled solutions while keeping the whole. Error A, an unused hat, and error F, one hat assigned twice, each receive five minutes in Return and repair with a separate supplied-record retry. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 95 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
