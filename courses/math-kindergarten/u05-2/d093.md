# Day 93: I can show possible story parts.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-93.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-93.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 92 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 7-12. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount93]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount93]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 92 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 92 tickets show error A, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-A93]], then [[steps:s05-F-A93]], fresh retry included. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters. Second five: older error C [[s05-F-C93]] with its retry, or familiar partial-record corrections if no unmet need. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Day 92 combined given parts; today the whole is given and the labeled parts can vary. Review story and retell from unit 4, and One-up one-down transfer from day 87. **Ask.** [[s05-Kparts93]] before [[steps:s05-Kparts93]]. Define the picture labels with gestures before children use them. Show one supported allocation at a time. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; others handle squares. Two independent complete solutions remain an exit format.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P93]] using practice-93.md. Match each supplied striped picture to a square; fill only the missing plain count. Watch for an omitted picture, error A. Review One counter stands for one story object, unit 4 day 71. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G93-1]], [[s05-G93-2]], [[s05-G93-3]], [[s05-G93-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-93.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X93]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 7-12 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper squares replace counters; draw apple circles, stripe marks, mats/frames and folded covers. Copy the supplied page and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount93]] Child touches six squares and makes six marks.
- **5 min. Model.** Show six apple circles. Start one striped/five plain; change one plain to striped. Supply the next striped count two.
  **Say.** "Complete the changed label count." Expect: four plain, still six total.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P93]] Child finishes the supplied plain count.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 92 feedback; assign one unused square in a supplied retry.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X93]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone; keep both labeled solutions for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: continue a supplied labeled six-apple record, changing one picture category while keeping all six.

### Words and supports

Striped: lines across a picture. Plain: no lines. Review story and retell, unit 4 days 68/70. Read the labels while pointing; a pattern key replaces color. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 94 applies the known-whole story to objects and drawings. Error B, unchanged labeled counts called new, gets five minutes comparing counts and changing one supplied record with a fresh retry. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 94 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
