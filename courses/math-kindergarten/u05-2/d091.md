# Day 91: I can draw parts and put the total first.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-91.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-91.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 90 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 25-30. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount91]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount91]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 90 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 90 tickets show error B, use five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-B91]], then [[steps:s05-F-B91]], fresh retry included. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters. Second five: one older unmet component, error A [[s05-F-A91]] OR error C [[s05-F-C91]], with its steps and retry. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Review and model

Day 90 organized pairs; today connect the same split to a drawing and equation. Review Two labeled part mats and One-up one-down transfer, day 87. **Ask.** [[s05-Kequal91]] before revealing [[steps:s05-Kequal91]]. Contrast the supplied wrong/right only after matching equal quantities. Review Equal quantities linked to both equation orders, unit 4 day 78. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; others use pieces. Keep equation starters supplied, never two blank drawing/equation panels.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P91]] using practice-91.md. Circulate for rejection of total-left order, error C. Match six on each side before completing the supplied link. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G91-1]], [[s05-G91-2]], [[s05-G91-3]], [[s05-G91-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-91.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X91]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 25-30 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut large paper squares instead of counters; draw mats/frames and fold paper covers. Copy the supplied practice and fresh slip; no screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount91]] Touch six squares; child marks each.
- **5 min. Model.** Build six beside one/five, then two/four.
  **Say.** "How do both sides show six?" Expect: each has six squares. Read both equation orders.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P91]] Supply the picture and incomplete equation; child fills the link.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 90 feedback; use one supplied-record retry.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X91]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone; accept dictated equations with help recorded. Keep it for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: continue a supplied three/three six-square record and explain why its two sides match.

### Words and supports

Review equation and equals, unit 4 day 78: an equation names equal quantities; equals says same quantity. Read symbols aloud. Name squares with marks patterned; squares without marks plain. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 92 applies parts to a total-unknown story. Error C, rejecting a total-first record for order alone, gets five minutes with matching quantities and a fresh supplied link. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 92 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
