# Day 92: I can show two parts together.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-92.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-92.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 91 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 1-6. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount92]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount92]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 91 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 91 tickets show error C, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-C92]] then [[steps:s05-F-C92]], including the fresh retry. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise a small group while others correct familiar starters. Second five: older error B [[s05-F-B92]] and its retry, or familiar partial-record corrections if no unmet need. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Story rehearsal

Day 91 linked a split to equations; today use those quantities in a story. Review story, retell and One counter stands for one story object, unit 4. **Ask.** [[s05-Kstory92]] before [[steps:s05-Kstory92]]. Contrast the incomplete and complete record, error A. Point between labels, squares and marks. No new story method. Hear at most three 45-second contributions; everyone else handles pieces. Never give a blank total-unknown scene during practice.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P92]] using practice-92.md. Restore the missing whole in the adult-started equation, keeping both pictured parts. Watch for unused squares, error A; do not reward a total that loses a story object. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G92-1]], [[s05-G92-2]], [[s05-G92-3]], [[s05-G92-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-92.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X92]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 1-6 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares for counters, drawn mats/frames and folded covers; copy the supplied record and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated game rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount92]] Touch six squares; child marks each.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Set two patterned and four unmarked squares, with one plain mark missing in your record.
  **Say.** "Complete the missing part of this story record." Expect: restore the fourth plain mark, six altogether.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P92]] Give the supplied picture/equation; child fills the whole.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 91 feedback; match equal quantities in one supplied link.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X92]] Read the fresh slip exactly. Child marks alone. Save it for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: explain which supplied marks stand for each kind of square, still using six altogether.

### Words and supports

Review equation/equals, day 91, and story/retell, unit 4 days 68/70. Use pattern and empty surface to identify the two kinds; read every label aloud. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 93 considers possible labelled parts for a known total. Error A, an object omitted from both parts, gets five minutes assigning the unused square and a fresh supplied-record retry. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 93 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
