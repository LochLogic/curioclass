C: 6 = 2 + 4 rejected versus six built on both sides. Repair: Build both equal quantities, connect supplied equation.

D: Four/four makes eight; four/six makes ten. Repair: Preserve given part, fill only empty places.

E: Five spaces numbered 0-4 reported four versus count 1-5. Repair: Touch each place while counting from one.
