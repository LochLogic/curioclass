# Day 96: I can choose a model for a story.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the supplied practice-96.md picture and six-square starters. Keep exit-96.md covered and key-teacher.md/generated key private. Bring annotated day 95 packets, older unresolved work, record and feedback. Set squares, part mats, frames/covers, crayons and game cards. Select roster 25-30. Adult cuts large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Read all print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, total and altogether, days 87/88. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount96]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount96]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: everyone marks together, no thirty-child oral round. Optional frames review Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, unit 2 day 26. A folded cover separates counted pieces; words stay within ten.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 95 exit and all written tasks, including counting, practice and game papers, with picture feedback. If day 95 tickets show error B, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-F-B96]] then [[steps:s05-F-B96]], including a fresh retry. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group while others correct familiar starters. Second five: older error C [[s05-F-C96]] with its retry, or familiar partial corrections if no unmet need. Further needs wait.

#### 8 min. Mixed-story rehearsal

Day 95 kept the whole while parts changed; today match models to familiar actions. Review story/retell, unit 4, and equation/equals, day 91. Use a single supplied picture at a time. **Ask.** [[s05-Kjoin96]] then [[steps:s05-Kjoin96]]; [[s05-Kremove96]] then [[steps:s05-Kremove96]]; [[s05-Kstatic96]] then [[steps:s05-Kstatic96]]; [[s05-Ksplit96]] then [[steps:s05-Ksplit96]]. Review One counter stands for one story object, unit 4 day 71. Children fix the missing event/quantity with objects or marks. Do not offer a choice array in practice. Hear at most three 45-second contributions total; no new method.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-P96]] using practice-96.md. Keep the starting six visible and repair the second removal mark. Describe an action mismatch rather than forcing a partition letter. Review that an equation can have its total on either side; count alone does not tell which event a drawing shows. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. Each child then finishes a supplied paper record. Collect all papers. If many miss, use the objects to restore the missing component before a fresh supplied retry.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and the Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, units 1/4. Five simultaneous game minutes: game-cards.md, [[s05-G96-1]], [[s05-G96-2]], [[s05-G96-3]], [[s05-G96-4]]. Read each invented Rin story aloud; partners alternate maker/checker and touch-check immediately. Everyone leaves quantity marks in the four numbered spaces. No speed demand.

Thirteen minutes: repeat today's supplied partial-record correction with loose squares beside the picture; keep original marks and retry distinct. Children hold paper/pieces, teacher retains records. Two minutes tidy. Independent evidence cases and layouts stay covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-96.md only now. Read its shared line and labels neutrally, then the exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X96]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 25-30 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. Every child marks paper; no hints, extra asks or public key. Record help and heard names; unheard reasons remain pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use key-teacher.md privately; a wrong numeral alone establishes no error letter.

### Other rooms

Copy the same supplied starters and fresh exit by hand; no device needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Every child has paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn mats/frames and folded covers; copy the supplied page and fresh slip. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount96]] Touch six squares; child leaves six dots.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Supply six marks with one crossed out for moving two aside.
  **Say.** "Repair the removal marks." Expect: one more cross-out, four remain.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P96]] Child repairs the supplied paper drawing. Connect each crossed mark to a moved square.
- **5 min. Repair.** Read day 95 feedback; revise one supplied labelled count.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X96]] Read the fresh story and slip exactly. Child circles alone. Keep it for feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, repairs and exit. Longer: compare the event marks in two supplied square records shown serially, completing one missing component in each.

### Words and supports

Review story/retell, unit 4 days 68/70, and equation/equals, day 91. Read arrows as movement and cross-outs as removal, pointing neutrally. Use objects beside practice drawings. Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87. Tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve the ask. Public talk may be passed; no required fingers or child cutting.

### Tomorrow

Day 97 uses a full frame to find places missing from ten. Error C, refusing the chosen model solely for total-left order, gets five minutes in Return and repair with matching quantities and a fresh supplied retry. Other action mismatches get descriptive feedback in that slot; a choice alone does not prove the reason. Reserve thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty practice/game/counting packets at 30 seconds each, including picture annotations. Return all in day 97 Return and repair. Other needs remain pending.
