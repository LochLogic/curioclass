# Section 5.2 opener

Equals relates equal quantities; connect pieces, supplied drawings and both equation orders. Use six-square partial models, 6 = 2 + 4 alongside 2 + 4 = 6. In labeled stories, swapping category counts can change the allocation. Rin is the sole actor. Every practice story is invented for practice. Supported correction of a partial record is distinct from independent generation.

Read every word and equation aloud; no independent reading. Before class print practice and exit masters at 18 point or larger, the local record and key-teacher.md. Keep the teacher key private. Never show held exit/end layouts, pictures or independent response formats in practice. Use the supplied partial records for corrections. The separate loose-piece task lets children choose and sketch parts on plain mats. Finite number pairs recur in full-range records, but independent cases/layouts are held.

Keep ledger wording: Partner turns, Return and repair, Paper count-dot record, Counting centers, Close retained observation records, and Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames. Demonstrate intended reviews afresh before use; never assume planned teaching has happened. There is no readiness assessment; day 87 retrieval is unscored.

Paper squares replace counters; adult-cut paper shapes replace blocks; marked strips replace cubes; folded paper replaces cups; draw frames as two rows of five. Patterns distinguish categories without color. Supervise large pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawn marks. Pictures only, no real food, required fingers, fine cutting or hidden children. Use high contrast, tactile outlines, nonslip mats, broad crayons, signing or directed adult marks. One shared screen may enlarge a master; every task works on paper. Without a printer, draw the same full-size stimulus.

Each eight-minute half-slip exit uses launch 1, six concurrent paper minutes including six same-ask 45-second observations, and collection 1. Children keep working while the teacher hears or watches the six. A child may pass in public talk. All thirty return paper; unheard reasons stay pending. Rotate exit groups 1-6, 7-12, 13-18, 19-24, 25-30 over each five exits. Later speech on another case is not proof of an earlier unheard explanation. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Help, names heard, scheduling and dates have separate cells. A wrong numeral alone cannot establish a register letter.

More than ten of thirty with the same observed letter triggers one five-minute class repair. Otherwise use the same slot with a small group while others correct familiar records. Ten minutes holds at most two sequential actions including fresh retries. Additional needs stay pending. [The private teacher answer key](key-teacher.md) gives wrong beside right. Predictions and timings are untested.

Connected work gives two one-minute Partner turns, then everyone returns marked paper. The teacher circulates without promising thirty oral turns. Five daily game minutes use the four fixed rounds in u05-1/game-cards.md with immediate collection/partner feedback; demonstrate day 87 before rehearsal. No timer. Day 100 puts games in connected rehearsal; conferences/centers hold them on days 101-103.

One child: counting 3, demonstration/rehearsal 5, paper work 9, repair 5, exit/conversation 8 minutes. Adult alternates roles. Split sittings as needed. Independent evidence replaces repetition; preserve written feedback, later revision and then judgment.

Child-led work replaces four repeated-correction minutes, never adds time. Both partners have a 45-second directing turn; two minutes cover handling/checking and thirty seconds a small sketch. Return every sketch with the next named exit-feedback packet. Large paper key shapes or square stand-ins are enough. On day 100 these four minutes replace the four partner-record correction minutes; the five within-five game minutes remain. The practice sheets contain the reusable row for each whole, not a separate new prompt for each repeated use.
