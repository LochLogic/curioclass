# Day 47: Same shape after a turn

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Draw a disk above a shelf, wrongly labelled below, for the queued repair. Select practice-47.md through u03-1/practice-records.md and repair cards, repair-47-D.md. Cut a matching triangle from its first panel and trace a separate square before turning the paper. Draw one larger circle outline. Keep exit-47.md covered; no choice cards in centers. Set flat shape cards and large paper squares. Adult cuts intact pieces; keep out of mouths. Read all child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review counted, uncounted and keep track, day 12. Model moving each of six squares across a boundary once, [[s03-W47]]. Pairs count, then join oral counting to ten, supported toward twenty. No timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 46's tickets and practice feedback. If many treated open as closed, use [[s03-F47-D]]. Review open/closed, day 46: trace the fresh four-sided doorway frame, stop at the gap, join it and mark the correction. If day 45's queued above/below error J is more urgent, use [[s03-F47-J]] on a fresh disk/shelf diagram instead. One five-minute action, never both. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise familiar traces. Keep everybody's corrected paper; no concealed-outline rehearsal.

#### 8 min. Model matching

Review shape names, day 45. One piece and one outline at a time. **Ask.** [[s03-P47-overlay]] Wait, then turn the triangle and slide it onto its tracing; sides and corners meet. Turning moves a shape without changing its boundary. **Ask.** [[s03-K47-square]] Wait, then turn one square over its same-size tracing. **Ask.** [[s03-K47-circle]] Trace the larger circle: the small piece cannot cover it, yet both are circles. Size can change while shape stays the same. Never stretch a piece to claim exact fit. All pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Connected overlay correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs: three rounds, two 45-second turns plus handling each, six minutes; six remain for corrections and records. Adult starts the triangle away from its outline; child turns/slides it into place. Then [[s03-P47-corners]]: find the omitted corner dot on the single large triangle. Children mark matching corners in their tracing, without choice displays. If a turned shape seems different, turn it back and retrace; retain its name. Practice stays in the folder.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes repeat the single-piece triangle fit and corner correction; ten minutes make a familiar six-square dot record using [[s03-W47]]. One partner moves, one records, then trade. Teacher watches that corners, not direction alone, guide a match. One outline at a time; no three-choice cards or target display. Review visible tracing from day 46 for motor rehearsal.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return flat shape cards and pieces to table trays concurrently. Put practice in folders.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-47.md now. **Ask.** [[s03-X47]] Use its exact stem. Explain format without pointing to a choice: join target to match and mark corresponding sides/corners. No example given. Directions/paper: 2.5 minutes; roster 13 to 18, six 45-second turns; collection: one minute. Everyone returns paper. Record names/help; a choice alone does not establish matching method. After class, thirty papers at 20 seconds take ten teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; return day 48.

### Other rooms

All work has a paper path. Hand-copy these same panels before class if printing is unavailable. No device turns; one shared screen may enlarge teaching drawings only. Keep reserved exits covered until the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut a large paper triangle, trace it and turn the tracing. Draw a separate larger circle outline.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Move six squares once each while counting.
- **5 min. Model.** Turn the triangle onto its tracing. Trace the larger circle: a small circle shares its shape but cannot cover its whole outline.
  **Say.** "Slide this piece onto its outline." Expect the triangle to fit after turning.
- **9 min. Practice.** Your child repeats the fit and adds the missing corner dot on the practice triangle.
- **5 min. Repair.** Join the missing doorway edge if yesterday's paper treated an open shape as closed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X47]] First show exit-47.md. Read exactly. Child joins and marks independently. Expect the larger square, with corresponding sides/corners. Note help, keep paper and return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, priority repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat the same practice with a partner describing a feature; no reserved cards.

### Words and supports

Turn and size; review shape names, straight parts and pointy corners from days 45/46. Keep large tactile outlines and nonslip paper available. A child can direct adult movement; record whether a mathematical choice was supplied. Say the name across a turn without demanding a definition.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 counts sides/corners and checks equal side lengths. If today's tickets show error B, rejecting a turned square, Return and repair rotates a new practice tile over its tracing. Return all paper with feedback; do not infer names or methods from a choice alone.
