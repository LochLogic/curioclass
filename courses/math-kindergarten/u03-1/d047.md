# Day 47: Same shape after a turn

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-47.md, repair-47-D.md or a drawn disk-above-shelf card labeled below, and covered exit slips. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review counted, uncounted and keep track, day 12. Model moving each of six squares across a boundary once, [[s03-W47]]. Pairs count, then join oral counting to ten, supported toward twenty. No timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 46's exit ticket, written task records and heard notes with feedback: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. If many treated open as closed, use [[s03-F47-D]]. Review open/closed, day 46: trace the fresh four-sided doorway frame, stop at the gap, join it and mark the correction. If day 45's queued above/below error J is more urgent, draw a disk above a shelf with the wrong word below; use [[s03-F47-J]] instead. Read its stem aloud, then replace the wrong word while the child indicates up from the shelf. One five-minute action, never both. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise familiar traces. Keep everybody's corrected paper; no concealed-outline rehearsal.

#### 8 min. Model matching

Review shape names, days 45/46. Turn means change direction; size means how large. One piece and one outline at a time. **Ask.** [[s03-P47-overlay]] Wait, then turn the triangle and slide it onto its tracing; sides and corners meet. Turning moves a shape without changing its boundary. **Ask.** [[s03-K47-square]] Wait, then turn one square over its same-size tracing. **Ask.** [[s03-K47-circle]] Trace the larger circle: the small piece cannot cover it, yet both are circles. Size can change while shape stays the same. Never stretch a piece to claim exact fit. All pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Connected overlay correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs: three rounds, two 45-second turns plus handling each, six minutes; six remain for corrections and records. Adult starts the triangle away from its outline; child turns/slides it into place. Then [[s03-P47-corners]]: find the omitted corner dot on the single large triangle. Children mark matching corners in their tracing, without choice displays. If a turned shape seems different, turn it back and retrace; retain its name. Practice stays in the folder.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Others repeat the single triangle overlay, missing-dot correction and six-square dot record independently.

Use assessment-display.md and viewing arrow for F1 cells 1 to 18 for children 1 to 3, child first then above, below, beside, next to, in front of, behind. Indicate one target and read its one master prompt. Let the child mark that target and respond; scribe words without supplying them. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 minutes setup take fifteen minutes. The six component rows are s03-F1-talk-58, s03-F1-talk-59, s03-F1-talk-60, s03-F1-talk-61, s03-F1-talk-62, s03-F1-behind. Keep other children on familiar paper; never show this display in practice.

Five remaining teacher minutes launch familiar work and collect records. Keep other targets covered during each ask. Record each first response once; written feedback returns for independent picture revision in day 48 Centers. No secure oral turn is repeated.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return flat shape cards and pieces to table trays concurrently. Put practice in folders.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-47.md now. **Ask.** [[s03-X47]] Use its exact stem. Explain format without pointing to a choice: join target to match and mark corresponding sides/corners. No example given. One minute launch, two concurrent paper minutes, six 45-second turns for children 13 to 18, half a minute collection. Others keep finished papers face down. Everyone returns paper. Record names/help; a choice alone does not establish matching method. After class, allow fifteen teacher minutes: thirty packets at thirty seconds each for paper, heard notes and F1 feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work in day 48 Return and repair; F1 revisions use its first five independent center minutes.

### Other rooms

All work has a paper path. Hand-copy these same panels before class if printing is unavailable. No device turns; one shared screen may enlarge teaching drawings only. Keep reserved exits covered until the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Cut a large paper triangle, trace it and turn the tracing. Draw a separate larger circle outline.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Move six squares once each while counting.
- **5 min. Model.** Turn the triangle onto its tracing. Trace the larger circle: a small circle shares its shape but cannot cover its whole outline.
  **Say.** "Slide this piece onto its outline." Expect the triangle to fit after turning.
- **9 min. Practice.** Repeat the fit and missing-dot repair. Then use assessment-display.md for six covered targets, one at a time. Read "Where is this object?" once per target; child marks and answers. Keep both responses. Allow five minutes; never repeat a completed cell.
- **5 min. Repair.** Join the missing doorway edge if yesterday's paper treated an open shape as closed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X47]] First show exit-47.md. Read exactly. Child joins and marks independently. Expect the larger square, with corresponding sides/corners. Note help, keep paper and return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, priority repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat the same practice with a partner describing a feature; no reserved cards.

### Words and supports

Turn and size; review shape names, straight parts and pointy corners from days 45/46. Keep large tactile outlines and nonslip paper available. A child can direct adult movement; record whether a mathematical choice was supplied. Say the name across a turn without demanding a definition.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 counts sides/corners and checks equal side lengths. If today's tickets show error B, rejecting a turned square, Return and repair rotates a new practice tile over its tracing. Return all paper with feedback; do not infer names or methods from a choice alone.
