# Day 49: Groups with a shape rule

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-49.md through u03-1/practice-records.md and repair cards, repair-49-B.md. Set flat shape cards, viewing arrow and cup; make no group headings. Keep exit-49.md covered. Set large paper squares and paper and broad pencils. Adult cuts large intact pieces, no small joins or mouth contact. Read all child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review category counts, day 41, and keep track, day 12. Use [[s03-W49]] with eight squares. Touch each once; the last word gives the group's count. Count aloud to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 48's tickets and practice feedback. If many rejected a turned square, use [[s03-F49-B]]: rotate its separate square onto its tracing, then correct the claim. Review turn and square, days 45/47. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise known marks. Prioritize one queued letter if needed, with a familiar correction; never two repairs. Everybody returns corrected paper. No reserved pair rehearsed.

#### 8 min. Rehearse sorting

Review sort, category and rule, day 41. Show four familiar practice cards: circle, broad triangle, square, hexagon. **Ask.** [[s03-P49-sort]] Children choose groups before any labels are supplied. Then **Ask.** [[s03-P49-rule]] Hear two volunteers at most; passing allowed. Accept any consistent geometric feature. If a child chooses color alone, model tracing a boundary feature and invite regrouping. This applies days 46/48 features; no new geometry. Pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Connected sort correction

Review position words, day 45. Begin [[s03-P49-position-beside]], then [[s03-P49-position-above]]. Fifteen pairs each take one 45-second turn, plus thirty seconds handling: two minutes. Keep the viewing arrow and cup fixed; record routes. Next [[s03-P49-repair]]: the supplied group claims straight sides but includes a circle. Cross out the intruder, then trace a feature on a remaining member. Ten minutes allow modeling, a two-turn partner correction and revision. No fixed group headings.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes: self-chosen boundary-feature grouping with the same cards, [[s03-P49-sort]] and [[s03-P49-rule]]; adult scribes the child's rule without supplying one. Ten minutes: [[s03-P49-count]] on the corrected three-card group, then its dot record. Every group stays within ten. Partners trade moving/recording roles. Teacher checks that the rule includes every member. Practice stays in the folder, unscored.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect cards. Put practice in folders; keep exits covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-49.md only now. **Ask.** [[s03-X49]] Read exactly. Explain format: draw or trace the target in one blank group box and mark its sides. Do not name a heading or target. Directions/paper: 2.5 minutes; roster 25 to 30 get six 45-second turns; one minute collection. Everyone returns paper. Record names/help; placement alone does not establish a spoken rule. After class, thirty papers at 20 seconds take ten teacher minutes. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; return day 50.

### Other rooms

All work has a paper path. Hand-copy these same panels before class if printing is unavailable. No device turns; one shared screen may enlarge teaching drawings only. Keep reserved exits covered until the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw/cut the four practice cards; a drawn cup and arrow replace objects.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight paper squares once each.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Your child chooses shape-feature groups from familiar cards, with no supplied headings.
  **Say.** "Tell your grouping rule." Expect a feature fitting every member, such as straight sides or curved boundary.
- **9 min. Practice.** Give beside/above movement commands separately. Child moves the card, records routes, then corrects the straight-side group containing a circle. Count three remaining cards.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct one returned boundary or turned-square idea using its practice card.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X49]] First reveal exit-49.md. Read exactly; child draws independently under one picture heading. Expect the three-sided group and three side marks. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, priority repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat the same practice with a partner describing a feature; no reserved cards.

### Words and supports

Review sort, category and rule, day 41; side, corner, straight/curved, days 46/48; above/beside, day 45. Accept gestures indicating a boundary feature. Adult scribes a spoken practice rule; do not supply one during the exit. Record access and mathematical help separately.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 names circles and varied triangles and explains what stays the same. Return today's paper with feedback. Error C, rejecting a thin triangle, calls for tracing a different closed three-sided boundary in its five-minute Return and repair, never replaying this heading card. Preserve unheard explanations as not yet seen.
