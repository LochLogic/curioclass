# Day 46: Follow a boundary

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-46.md through u03-1/practice-records.md. Prepare one of the repair cards, repair-46-A.md or repair-46-J.md. Make thirty concealed tactile packet originals from packet-46.md in folded screens, separate from practice. Keep exit-46.md covered. Set viewing arrow and Counting mat; gather remaining day 44 feedback. Adult cuts broad intact strips, no small joins. Read all child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 12; numeral and unchanged quantity, days 30/32. Use eight squares, as [[s03-W46]] specifies. Move each once, spread the unchanged group, retain its numeral. Oral count to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 45's tickets and practice feedback. If many showed error A, reversing front/behind, use [[s03-F46-A]]: move a card across a cup from the fixed viewer, then correct the separate cup diagram. If error J, reversing above/below, is the priority, use [[s03-F46-J]] instead with disk/shelf. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise a group while others revise known paper. One letter only, queue the other for day 47. Everybody returns a correction; no badge/folder replay.

#### 8 min. Model features

Review boundary from day 12 as the edge of a counting area; now trace a shape's boundary. Draw a circular lid and an open doorway frame, visibly, one at a time. Straight parts do not bend; curved parts bend; joins can be pointy. An open boundary has a gap; closed joins all the way around. A flat paper face lies on the table.

**Ask.** [[s03-K46-curve]] then [[s03-P46-features]], then [[s03-K46-point]]. Wait before modeling each trace/dot. Use objects and gestures, not definitions to recite. These pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Connected features

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs run three rounds: visible curve trace, straight-part trace, open-frame correction [[s03-P46-closure]]. Two 45-second turns plus handling per round uses six minutes; six remain for modeling the missing join and revising. Each child marks paper. Distinguish a gap from an enclosed space; no concealed practice. If many call open closed, follow the boundary and stop at its gap.

#### 10 min. Unit 2 Return and repair

Continue day 44's flagged gaps and day 45's response. Review Return and repair. Five minutes: revise one priority counting/numeral paper using its feedback. Review Counting mat, day 1, and Hand-drawn paired 5-frames as a 10-frame, day 26; use one support, then withdraw it. Five minutes: [[s03-R46-retry-count]] with six fresh squares, then [[s03-R46-retry-numeral]]. Others correct retained number work. Preserve unresolved needs by name; never repeat a completed interview. Original, revision and helped response stay distinct.

#### 10 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Repeat the visible straight trace and doorway correction; optional known eight-square dot record. Teacher checks access to the tactile packet without exposing its outline. Practice stays in the folder, unscored.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return teaching cards in table trays. Retain folded screens; avoid a queue for supplies.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Only now distribute concealed tactile packet and exit-46.md. **Ask.** [[s03-X46]] Read exactly. Explain format: feel through the screen, sketch the boundary before uncovering. No shape name supplied. Separate packets avoid equipment queues. Use 2.5 minutes for launch/paper; hear roster 7 to 12 for six 45-second descriptions; one minute collection. Record names, access and mathematical help separately. After class, thirty papers at 20 seconds take ten teacher minutes. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard descriptions remain unclassified. Return day 47.

### Other rooms

All work has a paper path. Hand-copy these same panels before class if printing is unavailable. No device turns; one shared screen may enlarge teaching drawings only. Keep reserved exits covered until the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the doorway and lid; wide folded paper makes raised strips and a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight squares; spread them without changing the count.
- **5 min. Model.** Trace a visible lid's curve and the doorway's straight parts. Show an open gap, then a joined closed frame.
  **Say.** "Trace the straight part." Expect a doorway edge.
- **9 min. Practice.** Your child traces visible parts and closes the practice doorway. Correct one earlier number paper; count six new squares and write 6.
- **5 min. Repair.** Move a card across a cup from one viewing place; correct the returned position idea.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X46]] First give the concealed packet and exit-46.md. Child feels, then sketches before uncovering. Expect straight parts, points and a gap. Note help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, priority repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat the same practice with a partner describing a feature; no reserved cards.

### Words and supports

Straight, curved, pointy, open, closed, flat, boundary. Review day 45 position words. A child can trace or sign; use large raised boundaries and a nonslip surface. Guided tactile movement follows the child, without naming the concealed shape or closing its gap. Record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 matches shapes after turns and size changes. Return and repair uses error D, open treated as closed, to join a fresh doorway frame. Any queued error J uses that slot instead, never additional minutes. Paper shows marked features; unheard descriptions remain not yet seen. Keep first responses, feedback and corrections.
