# Unit 3 record sheet

Teacher only. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A blank heard date means no oral evidence; record that component not yet seen and put pending in the scheduling column. Pending is not a fourth score. Record access support separately from mathematical help. Keep original, written feedback and revised work.

Exit rotation: children 1-6 on days 45/50/55/60; 7-12 on 46/51/56/61; 13-18 on 47/52/57/62; 19-24 on 48/53/58 then fresh transfer day 64; 25-30 on 49/54/59 then fresh transfer day 65. Later evidence never proves an earlier unheard reason. Everyone returns paper on all exit days.

Final conferences: five separate 45-second asks and 30 seconds setup/reset, 4.25 minutes per child. Six children use 25.5 of 28 minutes. F1 all six relations, F2 ten names, F5 several tub attributes, F6 weight and capacity are recorded separately. Use 2.5-minute daily reserve for pauses or unclear methods; unresolved evidence uses day 63 missing-evidence ten minutes, then remains pending. Never rush.
