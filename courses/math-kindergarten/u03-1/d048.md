# Day 48: Sides, corners and lengths

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-48.md through u03-1/practice-records.md and repair cards, repair-48-B.md. Set individual flat shape cards, a side-length paper strip and folded paper corner. Use one card at a time; keep exit-48.md covered. Adult cuts large intact pieces, no small joins or mouth contact. Read all child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review counted/uncounted and keep track, day 12. Five squares, [[s03-W48]]: move each across the counting boundary once, then make matching dots. Oral count to ten, supported toward twenty. No timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 47's tickets and practice feedback. If many rejected the turned square, use [[s03-F48-B]] on the separate repair card. Review turn, day 47: rotate the square onto its tracing, tracking each corner. Correct the wrong claim on paper. More than ten of thirty: class repair; otherwise group while others revise familiar traces. Everybody returns a correction. Other letters stay queued. No paired square/rectangle comparison.

#### 8 min. Model sides and corners

Review straight/curved and boundary, day 46. A side is one straight boundary part; a corner is where sides join. Show one broad triangle, then one regular hexagon. **Ask.** [[s03-K48-sides]] then [[s03-K48-corners]]. Mark a start so the first part is not counted twice. Name hexagon across regular and irregular examples: six sides, not necessarily six equal sides. Review equal, day 22; here it describes side lengths. **Ask.** [[s03-K48-equal]] Move the strip along each side without stretching. Show the wide rectangle alone: [[s03-K48-rectangle]]. Opposite sides fit, adjacent ones do not. A folded paper corner fits each corner. A square is a rectangle with all four sides equal. Pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Connected feature correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen concurrent pairs run three rounds: omitted side mark on one irregular hexagon [[s03-P48-boundary]], corner dots on that card, curved trace [[s03-P48-circle]]. Two 45-second turns plus handling per round takes six minutes; six remain for demonstration/revision. Place a start dot, mark each segment once, stop on returning. The irregular hexagon has six sides and six corners. If many miscount, track corner to corner slowly; length/tilt do not create extra sides. No paired comparison sheet.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes repeat single-card corrections; ten minutes use the known five-square record, [[s03-W48]]. Teacher checks start marks and separate side/corner marks. Use the regular hexagon strip on its own card for equal-length rehearsal. Keep the reserved square/rectangle pair out of centers. Practice stays in the folder, unscored.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect strips/cards. Keep corrections with each child's practice.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-48.md for the first time. **Ask.** [[s03-X48]] Read exactly; one comparison requested. Explain format: marks on both pictures can show shared/different features. Supply no feature or name. Directions/paper: 2.5 minutes; roster 19 to 24 give six 45-second comparisons; collect for one minute. Record names/help, not unheard reasons. After class, thirty papers at 20 seconds take ten teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; return day 49.

### Other rooms

All work has a paper path. Hand-copy these same panels before class if printing is unavailable. No device turns; one shared screen may enlarge teaching drawings only. Keep reserved exits covered until the independent turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw practice shapes; cut a broad side-length strip and fold a paper corner.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch five squares once each; make five dots.
- **5 min. Model.** Trace one triangle, then one hexagon; mark sides/corners from a start dot. Check equal hexagon sides with the strip. Show the rectangle alone with its two long sides.
  **Say.** "Fix the side marks." Expect six separate marks on the irregular hexagon.
- **9 min. Practice.** Your child repairs that single card and traces the circle's curve.
- **5 min. Repair.** Turn the practice square over its tracing; correct yesterday's claim if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X48]] First reveal exit-48.md. Read exactly; child marks independently. Expect four sides/corners on both, contrasting equal side lengths. Note help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keeping the model, priority repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat the same practice with a partner describing a feature; no reserved cards.

### Words and supports

Side, corner; review boundary, straight, curved and turn. Review equal as lengths that fit the same strip. Use large side marks and corner dots, not color. Folded paper supports corner comparison without angle measurement. A scribe records words but does not choose features.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 applies known boundary features to self-chosen sorts. Its Return and repair uses error B, turned square rejected, on a separate tile; record feature-count gaps descriptively and queue one if more urgent. Return every comparison with feedback, without supplying an unheard explanation.
