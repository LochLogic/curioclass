# Day 114: I can record teen parts with drawings and equations.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare supplied practice-114 records and movable squares. Select exit roster 1-6; cover reserved copies. Bring day 113 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K114]], then [[steps:s06-K114]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, and tens aloud together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats in the counting space. Marks never prove unheard words. No individual round or written numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 113's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/copy/repair paper with written feedback, including s06-P113 and s06-K113. Review Return and repair, day 2. Honor yesterday's error F handoff: if many, more than ten, show it, **Ask.** [[s06-F114]], then [[steps:s06-F114]]. Include its fresh partial retry within five minutes. Use the other five for one first remaining need or familiar review. Remaining needs stay pending.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Day 113 linked visible parts to an equation. Review expression, equation, plus, equals and same quantity, day 78; review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-M114]], then [[steps:s06-M114]]. Connect objects, a supplied partial drawing and both equation orders. Equals relates equal quantities; the whole may appear on either side.

**Ask.** [[s06-P114]], then [[steps:s06-P114]]. Correct one further-part numeral beside fourteen already drawn ones. Teach 19 formation: [[s06-N114]], then [[steps:s06-N114]]. No whole blank drawing-equation practice. Preview the Four-ask counting and teen conference with [[s06-V114-1]], [[s06-V114-2]], [[s06-V114-3]], [[s06-V114-4]]: one volunteer, four separate 45-second asks, three minutes included here. Others mark supplied practice. These supported teaching cases never become final evidence.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Read [[s06-G114-1]] [[s06-G114-2]] [[s06-G114-3]] [[s06-G114-4]] separately. Rin alone adds/removes squares; children mark four game-record boxes. Partners touch-check the resulting sets with immediate feedback.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat supplied partial records, movable frames and supported numeral copying. Fifteen concurrent pairs exchange two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes, recording names/help. No complete independent check formats. Collect all marks for day 115 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-114 only at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X114]] exactly. Everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Children draw every one. Hear six matching equations; others may write theirs or use usual accessible communication. Unheard equations remain pending through day 115 feedback and days 115 to 118 assigned follow-up, never inferred from drawings. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for exit marking/written feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards; copy supported practice and sealed exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** Restore one further mark in supplied twelve; read both supplied equation orders.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P114]] Use the fourteen picture and supplied equation.
  **Say.** "Correct the further part in this supplied record." Expect: replace 3 with 4. Model 19 beside counted squares; child copies.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's work; restore all ten individual ones if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X114]] Read the exact stem on the blank reserved slip. Child draws; hear or scribe a matching equation without suggesting it. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, teaching and exit. Longer: another supplied partial record with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87; ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read everything aloud. Large tactile marks, broad pencils, signs, home-language reasoning, pointing and directed adult marks preserve access. No required fingers; public talk may be passed. Separate ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 115 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. Error F gets all ten ones restored and a fresh partial retry, at most five minutes. Day 115 rehearses taught numerals, collects numeral and teen-composition products and starts assigned final conferences. Unheard equations remain pending through feedback and scheduled follow-up.
