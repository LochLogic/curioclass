# Day 113: I can connect teen parts to expressions and equations.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-113 with a supplied 14-to-whole link and movable squares. Select exit roster 25-30; cover reserved copies. Bring day 112 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K113]], then [[steps:s06-K113]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, and tens aloud together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats in the counting space. Marks never prove unheard words. No individual round or written numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 112's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/copy/repair paper with written feedback, including s06-P112 and s06-K112. Review Return and repair, day 2. Honor yesterday's error E handoff: if many, more than ten, show it, **Ask.** [[s06-F113]], then [[steps:s06-F113]]. Include its fresh partial retry within five minutes. Use the other five for one first remaining need or familiar review. Remaining needs stay pending.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Day 112 connected teen parts to whole numerals. Review expression, equation, plus, equals and same quantity, day 78, with Equal quantities linked to both equation orders. Extend those connections to teens. **Ask.** [[s06-M113]], then [[steps:s06-M113]]. Read every symbol aloud; 10 names all ten individual squares.

**Ask.** [[s06-P113]], then [[steps:s06-P113]]. Trace the supplied whole link; repair missing links to all ten and the further four. Use one supplied equation, never expression choices. Teach 17/18 formation: [[s06-N113]], then [[steps:s06-N113]]. Copy beside counted squares. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point in simultaneous pairs.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Read [[s06-G113-1]] [[s06-G113-2]] [[s06-G113-3]] [[s06-G113-4]] separately. Rin alone adds/removes squares; children mark four game-record boxes. Partners touch-check the resulting sets with immediate feedback.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat supplied partial records, movable frames and supported numeral copying. Fifteen concurrent pairs exchange two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes, recording names/help. No complete independent check formats. Collect all marks for day 114 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-113 only at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X113]] exactly. Everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Read all expression choices neutrally. Require positive links to all ten squares and seven further ones; elimination alone does not show connection. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for exit marking/written feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards; copy supported practice and sealed exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** Build fourteen; connect its parts to the supplied expression and equation, reading symbols aloud.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P113]] Use the supplied equation and picture.
  **Say.** "Repair the links to this supplied equation." Expect: ten linked to all ten squares, four to further four, fourteen to whole. Model 17/18; child copies beside counted squares.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's work; correct a supplied reversed label if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X113]] Reveal the reserved square card. Read exact stem and choices. Child makes their own match and links. Record help; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, teaching and exit. Longer: another supplied partial record with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87; ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read everything aloud. Large tactile marks, broad pencils, signs, home-language reasoning, pointing and directed adult marks preserve access. No required fingers; public talk may be passed. Separate ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 114 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. Error F, one object replacing ten, gets ten separate squares restored and a fresh supplied partial retry, at most five minutes. Day 114 connects supplied drawings to both equation orders and teaches 19 formation.
