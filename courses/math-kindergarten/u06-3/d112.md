# Day 112: I can split teens and connect parts to a numeral.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare supplied practice-112 records and movable squares. Select exit roster 19-24; cover reserved copies. Bring day 111 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K112]], then [[steps:s06-K112]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, and tens aloud together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats in the counting space. Marks never prove unheard words. No individual round or written numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 111's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/copy/repair paper with written feedback, including s06-P111 and s06-K111. Review Return and repair, day 2. Honor yesterday's error F handoff: if many, more than ten, show it, **Ask.** [[s06-F112]], then [[steps:s06-F112]]. Include its fresh partial retry within five minutes. Use the other five for one first remaining need or familiar review. Remaining needs stay pending.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review decompose, part and whole, day 87: split the whole into parts. Day 111 made teens from ten and more; today separate those visible ones. Review Two labeled part mats, day 87, and Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-M112]], then [[steps:s06-M112]]. Count all if needed. Reconnect the supplied numeral to both parts; no complete unframed split record.

**Ask.** [[s06-P112]], then [[steps:s06-P112]]. Correct the supplied whole label. Teach 15/16 beside counted squares: [[s06-N112]], then [[steps:s06-N112]]. Copy in two boxes. Record formation separately from quantity understanding. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point in pairs.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Read [[s06-G112-1]] [[s06-G112-2]] [[s06-G112-3]] [[s06-G112-4]] separately. Rin alone adds/removes squares; children mark four game-record boxes. Partners touch-check the resulting sets with immediate feedback.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat supplied partial records, movable frames and supported numeral copying. Fifteen concurrent pairs exchange two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes, recording names/help. No complete independent check formats. Collect all marks for day 113 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-112 only at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X112]] exactly. Everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use reserved leaves; keep all individual ones visible. Accept dictated whole label; 19 formation is taught day 114, so record it separately. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for exit marking/written feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards; copy supported practice and sealed exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** Move the further two from supplied twelve; repeat with fourteen. Retain all ten ones.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P112]] Use the supplied picture.
  **Say.** "Correct the supplied whole label for twelve." Expect: 12. Model 15/16 beside counted squares; child copies.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper; restore a missing further mark if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X112]] Read the exact stem on the fresh leaf slip. Child records independently. A dictated whole label preserves quantity evidence; note formation separately.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, teaching and exit. Longer: another supplied partial record with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87; ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read everything aloud. Large tactile marks, broad pencils, signs, home-language reasoning, pointing and directed adult marks preserve access. No required fingers; public talk may be passed. Separate ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 113 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. Error E, confirmed reversed digit order, gets a supplied twelve-label correction and a fresh supplied fourteen retry, at most five minutes. Day 113 connects teen collections to expressions and equations.
