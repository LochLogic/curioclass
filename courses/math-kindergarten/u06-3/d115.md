# Day 115: I can match numerals to collections and write them.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-115 and reserved numeral-115, teen-compose-115, conference-115, hidden until each child receives them. Conference roster 1-8; exit sample 7-12. Bring day 114 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point; say "Invented for practice." Store references during independent products.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K115]], then [[steps:s06-K115]]. Echo six/seven/eight, eight/nine/ten, and tens aloud together. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: children mark beats in the counting space. Marks never prove unheard words. No individual round or written numbers above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-X110 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 114's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/copy/repair paper with written feedback, including s06-P114 and s06-K114. Review Return and repair, day 2. Honor yesterday's error F handoff: if many, more than ten, show it, **Ask.** [[s06-F115]], then [[steps:s06-F115]]. Include its fresh partial retry within five minutes. Use the other five for one first remaining need or familiar review. Remaining needs stay pending. Unheard day 114 equations remain pending through feedback and assigned fresh-domain follow-up days 115 to 118; paper cannot prove unheard speech.

#### 9 min. Connected work

Day 114 recorded teen parts. Rehearse only taught numeral forms: 0 to 10, day 30; 20, day 107; 11 to 14, days 108 to 111; 15/16, day 112; 17/18, day 113; 19, day 114. **Ask.** [[s06-M115]], then [[steps:s06-M115]]. Review zero for none; contrast error D without new content.

**Ask.** [[s06-P115]], then [[steps:s06-P115]]. Match supplied 12 to its collection and trace the form; use familiar cards for supported copying. In the last two minutes read each numeral-115 row and teen-compose-115 target separately, without answering. Each child first sees a reserved panel when receiving their own paper. Store references before independent products.

#### 28 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Children work independently: numeral-115 for eight minutes, teen-compose-115 for eight. Prompts/targets were read separately beforehand. No peer keys, coaching or teacher-led repair. Unfinished panels stay pending. Stagger paper blocks around assigned conferences so every child has sixteen uninterrupted paper minutes.

Teacher hears roster 1-8 privately: [[s06-C115-F2-count]], [[s06-C115-F2-out]], [[s06-C115-F3-name]], [[s06-C115-F4-start]]. Read each exact stem separately; reveal one conference-115 panel at its own turn. Four 45-second asks are three minutes each, 24 teacher minutes; four minutes cover transitions/collection. Children mark or direct paper traces. Record initial/help/access separately. This provides fresh evidence for unheard day 106 supplied starts and day 110 teen names, never retroactive proof.

After their paper/conference, children have five independent untimed game minutes: [[s06-G115-1]] [[s06-G115-2]] [[s06-G115-3]] [[s06-G115-4]] Read familiar asks before rotations; use immediate object feedback, never final-product help. Rin alone acts; children mark four boxes. Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Each child has 16 paper + 3 conference + 5 game = 24 minutes, leaving four for choice/tidy. Collect all for day 116 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-115 only at each child's own turn; sampled slips stay covered until heard. Read [[s06-X115]] exactly. Everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Use the fresh shell circle. Scribing preserves quantity evidence but does not establish independent formation. Keep products for later revision before judgment. After class: sixty teacher minutes for component feedback/records, including thirty exits. Day 116 revision precedes later judgment.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards; copy supported practice and sealed exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Rehearse.** Match supplied 0/1 to empty/single groups; copy familiar forms.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P115]] Use its supplied label and collection.
  **Say.** "Match this supplied numeral to its collection." Expect: 12 matched to twelve squares.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's work; restore missing individual ones if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X115]] Read its exact stem. Child writes independently; note if a scribe records quantity instead.

In separate sittings, give eight independent minutes per final sheet and one three-minute four-ask conference, using the reserved masters. Return feedback next sitting; revise before judgment. Unfinished evidence stays pending.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, teaching and exit. Longer: another supplied partial record with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87; ten ones and further ones, day 108. Read everything aloud. Large tactile marks, broad pencils, signs, home-language reasoning, pointing and directed adult marks preserve access. No required fingers; public talk may be passed. Separate ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 116 returns day 115's exit tickets and practice with written feedback in Return and repair. Error E gets a supplied twelve-label correction and fresh partial retry. Its first five Connected work minutes revise day 115's assessment products and conference annotations before judgment. Day 116 rehearses ten-and-more counts and supplied starts; frames/references become optional. Roster 9-16 receives its fresh conference; unresolved needs remain pending.
