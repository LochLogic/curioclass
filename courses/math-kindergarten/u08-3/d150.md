# Day 150: I can add and take within five.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for evidence and today's teacher-only answer key for practice. Print the student page as corrected partial practice and today's sealed exit master. Lay out listed materials. Read every printed word aloud; say "Invented for practice." Fill actual names beside assigned roster numbers. Put other reserved cards away. Read paper cues before centers; private answers stay out of peers' hearing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q150]] [[steps:s08-Q150]] Echoed partial stretches are practice; no independent full recital. Repeat a missed word with its object action.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return all day 149 papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 149's tickets ignore removal or add instead, error C only when observed, [[s08-R150]]: [[steps:s08-R150]] Whole class above a third, otherwise group. Fresh triangle correction, never replay exit placement. Fact feedback gets independent center revision.

#### 5 min. Fact model

Review familiar within-five relationships, objects available. [[s08-P150-1]] [[steps:s08-P150-1]] Then [[s08-P150-2]]: [[steps:s08-P150-2]] Compare supplied claims: removal changes four; adding nothing keeps four. Describe just knows, quick strategy or counts all separately from correctness. Counting all is not error. Recheck object action when claim accepted without verification.

#### 5 min. Connected work

Pairs verify supplied claims, one 45-second strategy turn each, then draw corrections and check. Both keep own papers. Point to objects while reading complete equations. No blank two-panel result sheet. Correct part-as-whole by enclosing both regions, error L only when observed. Familiar facts, no new strategy lesson.

#### 35 min. Fluency observations

Assigned roster 11 to 20, after practice: privately read [[s08-F1a]], [[s08-F1b]], [[s08-F1c]], [[s08-F1d]], waiting after each. Use fact-record.md's four quantity boxes, counters optional. Four 45-second asks plus 15 transition = 3.25 minutes; ten need 32.5, leaving 2.5 setup/tidy. No supplied solutions, peer coaching or child-facing timer. Record accuracy/help by item; separate just knows, quick strategy, counts all notes. Four facts cannot establish every fact's fluency. Unfinished stays pending.

Others play five minutes: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs use [[s08-G150-1]] [[s08-G150-2]] [[s08-G150-3]] [[s08-G150-4]] [[s08-G150-5]] [[s08-G150-6]]. Teacher reads cards beforehand. Three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, thirty seconds tidy. Each records three quantities; immediate feedback compares supplied pictures. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to supplied picture.

Unassigned time: three independent minutes revising day 149 fact feedback, then quiet supplied partial-practice repetitions. Preserve original strategy/help and actual changes; never recollect secure component. Teacher remains with private observations.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Show exit-150.md only now; read two minus one. Child marks blank result panel, no supplied answer or extra strategy ask. Read [[s08-X150]] exactly. All thirty mark independently or direct adult mark. At most two 45-second observations embedded in three paper minutes; thirty seconds collection fits five. Record actual names heard; no added oral requirement.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each; fifteen for practice packets; ten for window picture feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; access and mathematical help separate. Return every paper day 151; retain original evidence and later revisions. 

### Other rooms

Paper and objects carry every task. Hand-draw masters without printer. Shared screen may enlarge instruction; remove for observations. Adult marks follow child direction; access help noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters; use large paper counters and crayon. Frames optional.

**Skip.** Rotations; never repeat completed private fact prompts.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo short stretches.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P150-1]] **Say.** "Verify this four-minus-three result claim." Expect one after three leave four. Child corrects supplied four; counters optional.
- **5 min. Game and repair.** Revise yesterday's feedback, then supplied game pictures.
- **12 min. Paper and facts.** [[s08-P150-2]] **Say.** "Check this four-plus-zero joining claim." Expect four; adding nothing leaves four. Finish unheard fact components privately, no answers in view. Child records quantities; preserve original help/strategy notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X150]] Show unseen two-panel card; read two minus one, then exact stem. Child marks alone; expect one. Give written feedback for tomorrow's revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed paper-repeat minutes, preserving game, revision, observations, model and exit. Longer: rearrange same permitted practice quantity. No reserved case or larger arithmetic.

### Words and supports

Review count, number word, keep track, day 1; counter, day 3; group, day 6; zero, day 30; add/take away, days 64/68; expression, equation, equals, day 78; part/whole, day 87; 5-frame/10-frame, days 3/26. Use objects/gestures. Read all print aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signs, tactile dots, directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting.

### Tomorrow

Day 151 returns every paper in Connected work, three independent feedback revision minutes including fact records. If day 150 tickets add for removal, error C, route needs to day 154 Groups; tomorrow feedback, no reteaching. Next hidden-part claims, complete joining/taking equations, final ten private facts.
