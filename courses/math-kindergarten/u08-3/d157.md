# Day 157: I can show more than one way.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open today's teacher-only answer key (d157-key, generated from items.csv) privately, plus teacher-key.md for carried evidence. Read all print aloud. Say "Invented for practice." Record actual names heard, original response, access support and mathematical help separately. Reserve transfer materials until their named part. Draw round and square practice trays. Keep the Len alternative-plan picture covered until exit. Print the separate half-sheet exit ticket; do not display its given pair during practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P157-count]]. Rehearse oral ones and tens using day 146 loose-one groups. A coached stretch updates practice needs, not full-sequence independence.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P157-gamea]] [[sC-P157-gameb]] Fifteen pairs verify supplied action pictures. Three 90-second rounds, 45-second maker/checker swaps, thirty seconds tidy; each child marks quantities on scrap paper. Immediate feedback, no speed score.
Check: Match supplied objects and result.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 156 papers with written picture feedback, three independent minutes and two collection. Preserve originals. Judge eligible work after class, never infer fluency from a corrected answer.

#### 10 min. One story

Review day 156 parts. [[sC-K157-1]] [[steps:sC-K157-1]] Only Rin acts, with paper pieces in two pictured trays. Read invented story aloud. Model joining the parts into ten; child draws whole. Then hold ten fixed while the same actor changes the tray arrangement, not the total. Optional squares/frame remain available, not required.

#### 10 min. More than one way

[[sC-P157-1]] [[steps:sC-P157-1]] Each child records two distinct pairs. Partners may move one piece from one tray to the other, then draw changed parts. [[sC-F157-1]] [[steps:sC-F157-1]] Compare faulty five/six against actual five/five; one gains what the other loses. All children mark paper. An optional ordered sweep includes empty trays and has a finite end: from zero/ten to ten/zero. Finding every pair is not required.

#### 20 min. Targeted centers

Two eight-minute centers, four setup/tidy. Repeat the supplied part/whole correction if day 156 tickets use ten as a part (error R); fill unoccupied places if partner copying persists (error Q). Other children record two tray arrangements or use the outstanding day 154/155 practice priority. Keep today's alternative-plan ticket unseen. Hear six rotating children, one 45-second practice ask each, 4.5 minutes within centers. All others mark paper concurrently. Update only actually observed components.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[sC-X157]] Use the fresh Len tray story and supplied three/seven plan. Child draws a different ordered plan on the half-sheet, conserving ten; read one stem, no extra why question. One minute directions, three paper minutes, one collection. Retain original and new plans together. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for written picture feedback and fifteen for eligible earlier judgments. New work revises day 158 before judgment; missing evidence stays pending.

### Other rooms

Objects and paper carry every task. A shared screen only enlarges pictures. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; unheard explanations stay pending.

### At home

**Swap.** Ten scrap-paper squares, two drawn trays, divider and crayon replace class materials. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Center rotations and optional search for every pair.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P157-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Revise day 156 feedback independently.
- **8 min. Story.** [[sC-P157-1]] **Say.** "Show two ways Rin can put ten pieces in the trays." Expect distinct ten-total pairs, such as one/nine and five/five.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[sC-F157-1]] Move one piece; expect five/five instead of five/six.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P157-gamea]] [[sC-P157-gameb]] Verify supplied object pictures and mark quantities.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sC-X157]] Show fresh Len plan. Expect different pair totaling ten, such as six/four. Give picture feedback afterward, independent revision day 158 before judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut optional repetitions, preserving revision and fresh evidence. Unfinished components remain pending. Longer: repeat corrected practice, keeping transfers unseen.

### Words and supports

Review count/numeral (days 1 and 115), part/whole (day 87), ten ones (day 112), flat/solid (day 126), longer/heavier (days 54 and 55), as used. Optional frames remain a choice; record help. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Strategy notes are unscored.

### Tomorrow

Day 158 receives day 157 written feedback for three independent Revision minutes before first judgment. Error X copies the supplied pair; error R changes the whole: use a supplied whole-preserving correction in targeted practice. Next, find and record partners of ten for numbers one to nine, as its days.csv row says. Preserve unresolved needs and actual strategy notes for the later grade handoff.
