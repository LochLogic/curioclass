# Day 155: I can repair partners and comparisons.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open today's teacher-only answer key (d155-key, generated from items.csv) privately, plus teacher-key.md for carried evidence. Read all print aloud. Say "Invented for practice." Record actual names heard, original response, access support and mathematical help separately. Reserve transfer materials until their named part. Prepare three priority groups from actual records. Draw each assigned fresh stimulus from its setup above the half-sheet Exit box; cover until Exit ticket. Verify actual weight/length answers privately before use.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P155-count]]. Keep day 154 unresolved number needs on the record; today does not replace their later practice.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P155-gamea]] [[sC-P155-gameb]] Fifteen pairs, three 90-second rounds with 45-second swaps, thirty seconds tidy. Mark quantities on scrap paper; compare supplied pictures for immediate feedback. Untimed facts, no rankings.
Check: Match objects to the supplied result.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 154 transfers with written picture feedback for three independent minutes, two for collection. Preserve first attempts, strategy and help. Judge eligible work after class, not before revision.

#### 40 min. Groups

Take remaining day 153 needs and day 151 s08-X151 hidden-part needs. Three 13-minute slots plus one minute transition, one priority per child. Ten children per slot. If many share one need, model once to the whole class within one slot; other slots cover further needs, never extra minutes per letter.

Partners: [[sC-K155-1]] [[steps:sC-K155-1]] Keep given part fixed. For hidden-part need, [[sC-K155-5]] [[steps:sC-K155-5]] This practice is not the old five-total exit. Comparisons: [[sC-K155-2]] [[steps:sC-K155-2]] Align before comparing. For weight, heft a different practice carton/book pair low over table; for attributes [[sC-K155-4]]. Shapes: [[sC-K155-3]] [[steps:sC-K155-3]] Trace one face while feeling depth. Side 1 retains supported corrections.

Each slot has four model, six practice and three independent rehearsal minutes. Hear two named children per slot, one 45-second practice ask each, within practice time; six overall. Others mark paper. Queue other needs for days 156 to 160.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Give ONE matched unseen route: [[sC-X155-1]] split, [[sC-X155-2]] partner, [[sC-X155-3]] length, [[sC-X155-4]] face, [[sC-X155-5]] attribute, [[sC-X155-6]] weight or [[sC-X155-7]] flat/solid or [[sC-X155-8]] hidden part. Select at most three active priorities today so directions fit: one minute read-aloud, three paper minutes, one collection. Use the half-sheet Exit box; child-directed marks count as access. Missing components stay pending; no unsupported whole-standard claim. Allow fifteen teacher minutes afterward for written picture feedback and fifteen for earlier judgments. Day 156 Revision precedes first judgment of new transfers.

### Other rooms

Objects and paper carry every task. A shared screen only enlarges pictures. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; unheard explanations stay pending.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper pieces, strips, safe book/carton, paper tube/cone, drawn shapes and crayon. Keep hefting over the table; no device needed.

**Skip.** Rotations and branches outside the child's priority.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P155-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Change day 154 paper from feedback independently.
- **13 min. Repair.** [[sC-K155-1]] **Say.** "Correct this supplied partner record." Expect four joins six for ten. Choose a comparison/shape example instead if needed.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P155-gamea]] [[sC-P155-gameb]] Verify supplied pictures; mark results on scrap paper.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Choose only the route matching the selected group: [[sC-X155-1]] [[sC-X155-2]] [[sC-X155-3]] [[sC-X155-4]] [[sC-X155-5]] [[sC-X155-6]] [[sC-X155-7]] [[sC-X155-8]]. Read that one stem aloud; child marks the assigned half-sheet alone. Use its teacher answer afterward. Keep original response/help; written picture feedback afterward, independent revision tomorrow before judgment.
