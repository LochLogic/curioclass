## sC-X158

Draw the pieces needed for the empty pockets.

**Answer.** Six additions, one per empty pocket; four existing and six new make ten.

**Look-fors and setup.** Invented for practice. Sol fills ten pockets, one piece per pocket; four already hold pieces.
