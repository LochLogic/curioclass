# Day 147: I can use dot parts for adding and taking.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for evidence and today's teacher-only answer key for practice. Print the student page as corrected partial practice and today's sealed exit master. Lay out listed materials. Read every printed word aloud; say "Invented for practice." Fill actual names beside assigned roster numbers. Put other reserved cards away. Read paper cues before centers; private answers stay out of peers' hearing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q147]] [[steps:s08-Q147]] Echoed partial stretches are practice; no independent full recital. Repeat a missed word with its object action.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return all day 146 papers; pattern notes/count feedback wait for center revision. Review Return and repair. If many of day 146's tickets or heard counts skip a tens name, error J, first five minutes: [[s08-R147]] [[steps:s08-R147]] Next five address observed unit switches, error K: [[s08-RK147]] [[steps:s08-RK147]] Physical groups only, never full recitals or exit route. More than a third means whole class; otherwise groups.

#### 5 min. Dot model

Review Two labeled part mats, day 87, and Equal quantities linked to both equation orders, day 78. Touch objects, match frame dots, then read supplied claim. [[s08-P147-1]] [[steps:s08-P147-1]] Rehearse cover checking: [[s08-P147-2]] [[steps:s08-P147-2]] Hidden means under cover; visible means in view. Correct before revealing check.

#### 5 min. Connected work

Pairs repair supplied dot/cover claims; pointer/recorder swap after 45 seconds, both keep own corrections. Use supplied four-plus-zero game card for another dot image. No independently invented joining facts. If many name part as whole, error L, enclose both regions; for visible called hidden, error M, uncover and separate.

#### 30 min. Counting observations

Assigned roster 11 to 20: [[s08-F2-ones]] then, after finishing or recording pending, [[s08-F2-tens]]. Two separate private asks with count-record.md; remove group cues. Ten times three minutes: two ones, 45 seconds tens, 15 transition. Child marks completion pictures; marks never prove unheard sequence. Keep separate components; unfinished stays pending, never rushed or replaced by choral work.

Others play five minutes: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs use [[s08-G147-1]] [[s08-G147-2]] [[s08-G147-3]] [[s08-G147-4]] [[s08-G147-5]] [[s08-G147-6]]. Teacher reads cards beforehand. Three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, thirty seconds tidy. Each records three quantities; immediate feedback compares supplied pictures. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to supplied picture.

Unassigned time: three independent minutes revising day 146 counting feedback, then quiet repeated supplied practice with demonstrated picture cues. Collect every paper. Return unscored pattern note without another interview. Teacher stays with assigned counts. Partners choose next supplied card when one is called; tidy during unassigned time.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Show exit-147.md only now: one striped dot and two plain dots. Accept joined picture or complete equation; equation not required. No strategy cue. Read [[s08-X147]] exactly. All thirty mark independently or direct adult mark. At most two 45-second observations embedded in three paper minutes; thirty seconds collection fits five. Record actual names heard; no added oral requirement.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each; fifteen for practice packets; ten for window picture feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; access and mathematical help separate. Return every paper day 148; retain original evidence and later revisions. 

### Other rooms

Paper and objects carry every task. Hand-draw masters without printer. Shared screen may enlarge instruction; remove for observations. Adult marks follow child direction; access help noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw frames/masters; use large paper squares, folded cover and crayon.

**Skip.** Rotations; continue two-count observation only if not already heard.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo short stretches together.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P147-1]] **Say.** "Check this supplied dot fact." Expect three, including empty part. Touch objects, then dots.
- **5 min. Game and repair.** Revisit yesterday's picture feedback; play supplied mixed game.
- **12 min. Paper and count.** [[s08-P147-2]] **Say.** "Repair this supplied cover claim." Expect three hidden, one visible from four total. Uncover after correction. Finish unheard count components privately, group cues removed. Keep originals.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X147]] Show unseen dot parts. Child marks alone; expect both parts joined as three. Read exactly, no hints. Keep for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed paper-repeat minutes, preserving game, revision, observations, model and exit. Longer: rearrange same permitted practice quantity. No reserved case or larger arithmetic.

### Words and supports

Review count, number word, keep track, day 1; counter, day 3; group, day 6; zero, day 30; add/take away, days 64/68; expression, equation, equals, day 78; part/whole, day 87; 5-frame/10-frame, days 3/26. Use objects/gestures. Read all print aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signs, tactile dots, directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting.

### Tomorrow

Day 148 returns every paper. If day 147 joining tickets use part as whole, error L, five Connected work minutes enclose fresh whole. Observed cover error M gets five Return and repair minutes on different within-four cover. Count feedback gets three independent center revision minutes. Next unhidden domino parts and supplied corrections.
