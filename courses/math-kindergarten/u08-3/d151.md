# Day 151: I can find a hidden part.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for evidence and today's teacher-only answer key for practice. Print the student page as corrected partial practice and today's sealed exit master. Lay out listed materials. Read every printed word aloud; say "Invented for practice." Fill actual names beside assigned roster numbers. Put other reserved cards away. Read paper cues before centers; private answers stay out of peers' hearing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q151]] [[steps:s08-Q151]] Echoed partial stretches are practice; no independent full recital. Repeat a missed word with its object action.

#### 10 min. Hidden-part model

Review hidden-part skill from day 99, using smaller familiar quantities. [[s08-P151-1]] [[steps:s08-P151-1]] Partners correct supplied claim, then uncover. Point to same four objects in both complete equations; joining parts and removing visible part describe that whole. [[s08-P151-2]] [[steps:s08-P151-2]] Only within-four covers or zero/all five. Rehearsal with feedback, no new reteaching or unknown-position equation.

#### 5 min. Connected work

Return all day 150 practice, game, exit and fact-feedback papers. Three independent minutes for day 150 feedback revision; preserve original strategy/help. Two minutes: children point to kept number book pages, story records, number clue record and count meaning record. Review actual revisions and MP.1 notes, no new conference. If day 150's tickets add for removal, error C, retain need for day 154; no reteaching today.

#### 35 min. Fluency observations

Assigned roster 21 to 30, after practice: privately read [[s08-F1a]], [[s08-F1b]], [[s08-F1c]], [[s08-F1d]], waiting after each. Use fact-record.md's four quantity boxes, counters optional. Four 45-second asks plus 15 transition = 3.25 minutes; ten need 32.5, leaving 2.5 setup/tidy. No supplied solutions, peer coaching or child-facing timer. Record accuracy/help by item; separate just knows, quick strategy, counts all notes. Four facts cannot establish every fact's fluency. Unfinished stays pending.

Others play five minutes: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs use [[s08-G151-1]] [[s08-G151-2]] [[s08-G151-3]] [[s08-G151-4]] [[s08-G151-5]] [[s08-G151-6]]. Teacher reads cards beforehand. Three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, thirty seconds tidy. Each records three quantities; immediate feedback compares supplied pictures. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to supplied picture.

Unassigned time: three independent minutes revising earlier window feedback not already revised, then quiet supplied partial-practice repetitions. Preserve original strategy/help and actual changes; never recollect secure component. Teacher remains with private observations.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Show exit-151.md only now. Display five actual squares, then cover three leaving two visible, matching master. Child draws hidden quantity before uncovering. Full five must be seen first; printed cover alone insufficient. Read [[s08-X151]] exactly. All thirty mark independently or direct adult mark. At most two 45-second observations embedded in three paper minutes; thirty seconds collection fits five. Record actual names heard; no added oral requirement.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each; fifteen for practice packets; ten for window picture feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; access and mathematical help separate. Return every paper day 152; retain original evidence and later revisions. Close only eligible earlier components afterward using actual dated papers and eleven retained-component rows in private key. Missing evidence stays pending. New day 151 work gets feedback now, day 152 revision, then first judgment. Carry F1/F2 once to days 152/153, no rescore or recollection.

### Other rooms

Paper and objects carry every task. Hand-draw masters without printer. Shared screen may enlarge instruction; remove for observations. Adult marks follow child direction; access help noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters; use large paper squares, folded cover and crayon.

**Skip.** Rotations and kept-work interviews; finish unheard fact components only.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo short stretches.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P151-1]] **Say.** "Correct this supplied hidden-part claim." Expect three hidden from four, one visible. Uncover; point to complete joining/taking equations.
- **5 min. Game and repair.** Revise day 150 feedback independently. Look at genuine book/story changes; play supplied game pictures.
- **12 min. Paper and facts.** [[s08-P151-2]] **Say.** "Check this supplied all-hidden picture." Expect all five hidden. Finish unheard private facts without supplied answers. Keep actual records; no second collection.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X151]] Show five, cover three, leave two visible on unseen card. Read exactly. Child draws alone; expect three hidden. Feedback afterward, revision day 152 before judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed paper-repeat minutes, preserving game, revision, observations, model and exit. Longer: rearrange same permitted practice quantity. No reserved case or larger arithmetic.

### Words and supports

Review count, number word, keep track, day 1; counter, day 3; group, day 6; zero, day 30; add/take away, days 64/68; expression, equation, equals, day 78; part/whole, day 87; 5-frame/10-frame, days 3/26. Use objects/gestures. Read all print aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signs, tactile dots, directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting.

### Tomorrow

Day 152 returns day 151 exit, practice, game and fact papers for three independent Revision minutes before first judgment. If day 151 tickets draw visible two as hidden, error M, route need to day 155 Groups. Carry eligible counting/fact records once to days 152/153. Next earlier collection/teen reasoning; never repeat counting/fluency samples.
