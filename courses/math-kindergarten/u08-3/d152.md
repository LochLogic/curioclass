# Day 152: I can repair a number display.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open today's teacher-only answer key (d152-key, generated from items.csv) privately, plus teacher-key.md for carried evidence. Read all print aloud. Say "Invented for practice." Record actual names heard, original response, access support and mathematical help separately. Reserve transfer materials until their named part. Prepare twenty large squares per child; use only adult-cut large safe pieces. The Side 2 first blank is the nineteen repair; the second is the sixteen record.

### The day

#### 3 min. Revision

Return day 151 practice, game, s08-Q151, s08-P151-3, exit and final fact-feedback papers. Three independent minutes use written picture feedback. Preserve original strategy/help; supported revision is not independent fluency. First judgment follows revision, after class.

#### 4 min. Record

Carry days 146 to 148 counting and days 149 to 151 fact records once, without recollection or rescore. Missing components stay pending. Retain earlier story and pattern notes. Children organize retained papers; no oral count today.

#### 25 min. Numeral display

Side 1: read sC-E152-N01 through N21 in picture order, pointing at each panel. Each child writes independent labels, including the empty collection. About one minute each leaves four for directions/access. Remove numeral strips. Consult the panel answer key privately; keep the collection pictures unlabeled.

#### 23 min. Collection and teen display

Side 2: [[sC-E152-L]] [[sC-E152-A]] [[sC-E152-C]] [[sC-E152-S]] in picture order. Then [[sC-E152-O]] uses real squares and an envelope; retain placed set. Read [[sC-E152-T]] with damaged teen picture, then [[sC-E152-D]] in blank box. Seven single asks take about two minutes each. Hear six assigned children on ONE item each, 45 seconds each, embedded in the remaining nine minutes; record actual names and component heard. Paper labels do not establish an unheard tracking route.

#### 5 min. Collect

Retain both sides and count-out envelope, without public answers. No exit: today's fresh items are end items. Allow twenty teacher minutes afterward for written picture feedback and fifteen for eligible day 151 judgments. Return day 152 work for day 153 Revision before first judgment. Number and teen gaps go to day 154 Groups.

### Other rooms

Objects and paper carry every task. A shared screen only enlarges pictures. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; unheard explanations stay pending.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw collections and boxes; use large paper squares, envelopes and crayon. Point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotation and finalized counting/fact interviews.

- **3 min. Assessment revision.** Revise day 151 feedback independently.
- **12 min. Labels.** Use the Side 1 panels. **Say.** "Write the number for this collection." Expect actual count, including zero for none.
- **12 min. Display.** [[sC-E152-O]] [[sC-E152-T]] **Say.** "Repair this nineteen drawing." Expect ten individual ones and nine further ones.
- **3 min. Keep.** Retain work, give picture feedback afterward, revise day 153 before judgment. Finish other components later; never infer them from this subset.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut optional repetitions, preserving revision and fresh evidence. Unfinished components remain pending. Longer: repeat corrected practice, keeping transfers unseen.

### Words and supports

Review count/numeral (days 1 and 115), part/whole (day 87), ten ones (day 112), flat/solid (day 126), longer/heavier (days 54 and 55), as used. Optional frames remain a choice; record help. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Strategy notes are unscored.

### Tomorrow

Day 153 Revision returns day 152 written feedback. Error L mismatches numeral/amount, error P replaces ten ones by one mark, error J counts past request: reserve day 154 Groups. Day 153 adds partner/attribute/shape evidence and carries unit 8 records once.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
