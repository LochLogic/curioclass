# Day 160: Ten ones and further ones

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open the teacher-only d160-key.md privately. Print the picture ticket d160-ticket.md, keeping it covered until exit. Read every direction and label aloud. Choose six children for today's single practice observation, with actual names on the private record. Prepare complete practice cards from the item steps. Retain earlier originals alongside written picture feedback. Say "Invented for practice" before each invented scene.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P160-count]]. Rehearse ones and tens to one hundred together. Rebuild a missed transition using ten groups of ten loose ones, never a bundle counted as one or written numerals above twenty. Each child marks a practice participation circle on paper; independent sequence evidence remains the earlier record.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P160-gamea]] [[sC-P160-gameb]] Fifteen pairs check supplied action pictures. Three ninety-second rounds, forty-five seconds per partner, then thirty seconds tidy. Each child marks the resulting quantities. Immediate feedback; no speed score. Objects are optional for known facts.
Check: Match the supplied action and quantity.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 159 practice and its exit with written picture feedback. Allow three independent revision minutes and two for return/collection. Keep originals; judge eligible earlier components afterward. Do not score supplied corrections as independent fluency.

#### 10 min. Connected work

Day 159 checked guesses by organizing and counting. If many tickets revisited bars (error B) or mismatched the numeral (error L), demonstrate one pass through the supplied twelve-square array, then connect its numeral. Review ten ones and further ones from written day 108. [[sC-K160-1]] [[steps:sC-K160-1]] Ada alone acts; children draw along. Keep all ten ones visible, then connect the complete equation. No missing-addend notation is required.

#### 10 min. Make a teen record

[[sC-P160-1]] [[steps:sC-P160-1]] Fifteen pairs organize fourteen, then each child makes a separate drawing of both parts. Choose optional frame or plain mat; withdraw supplied structure when unnecessary, keeping access supports. A drawing is sufficient; an optional equation must match it. Switch handler/checker after four minutes, keeping two for recording and tidy.

#### 20 min. Targeted centers

[[sC-F160-1]] [[steps:sC-F160-1]] Error P replaces ten ones with one mark: unpack and draw every one. Error R uses whole as part: keep the supplied fourteen fixed while separating its two parts. If many, spend five minutes on this supplied correction within two eight-minute centers and four setup/tidy minutes. Hear six rotating children for one forty-five-second practice ask each, 4.5 minutes within centers; others draw concurrently. Reserve the torn-record case. Retain the year-end records once, with actual help and remaining needs; no replacement full counting or fluency battery.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[sC-X160]] Give the fresh torn-record half-sheet; read the invented context and one stem. Child restores missing part on paper, keeping the printed further ones: one minute directions, three work, one collect. Use its Exit box for the restored part. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for written feedback and fifteen for the handoff record. Today's fresh work stays awaiting independent revision before any judgment; pass original and feedback together to the next teacher. This does not schedule another course day. Preserve eligible earlier evidence and unresolved needs without inferring whole-standard mastery.

### Other rooms

All tasks use paper and objects. No shared device is needed. Draw the masters on scrap paper if printing is unavailable. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Record mathematical help separately from access support; public talk may be passed.

### At home

**Swap.** Scrap-paper squares and hand-drawn mats replace counters and frames. Copy the torn-record ticket if no printer; no device needed.

**Skip.** Rotations and extra record sorting.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P160-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Independently revise day 159 feedback.
- **8 min. Teen record.** [[sC-P160-1]] **Say.** "Record this collection in two parts." Expect ten individual marks and four further marks; an equation is optional.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[sC-F160-1]] Replace one claimed-ten mark with ten separate ones.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P160-gamea]] [[sC-P160-gameb]] Verify supplied pictures.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sC-X160]] Read the fresh torn-record context; let your child draw alone. Expect ten restored marks beside eight printed ones. Keep original and feedback for later independent revision before judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut five minutes of repeated center records, preserving revision, model and transfer. Longer: use the same collection to verify a complete equation with the total on either side; never turn ten ones into one mark.

### Words and supports

Review count, numeral, partner of ten, part, whole and ten ones only where used, from written days 30, 97, 108 and 146. Ten ones remain ten separate objects. Judge mathematics, not neatness. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Note strategy and names actually heard separately. A choral response or supplied answer never establishes individual counting or fluency.

### Tomorrow

This is the final course day. Carry the existing counting, fluency and component records forward once, naming actual help and unfinished components. Error P uses one mark for ten; error R adds the whole beside the further part. Attach original exit and written feedback for later independent revision before judgment. Do not invent a completed mark or a new meeting.
