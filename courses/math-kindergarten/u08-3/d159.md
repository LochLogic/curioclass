# Day 159: A guess and a checked count

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open the teacher-only d159-key.md privately. Print the picture ticket d159-ticket.md, keeping it covered until exit. Read every direction and label aloud. Choose six children for today's single practice observation, with actual names on the private record. Prepare complete practice cards from the item steps. Retain earlier originals alongside written picture feedback. Say "Invented for practice" before each invented scene.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P159-count]]. Rehearse ones and tens to one hundred together. Rebuild a missed transition using ten groups of ten loose ones, never a bundle counted as one or written numerals above twenty. Each child marks a practice participation circle on paper; independent sequence evidence remains the earlier record.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P159-gamea]] [[sC-P159-gameb]] Fifteen pairs check supplied action pictures. Three ninety-second rounds, forty-five seconds per partner, then thirty seconds tidy. Each child marks the resulting quantities. Immediate feedback; no speed score. Objects are optional for known facts.
Check: Match the supplied action and quantity.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 158 practice and exit sC-X158 with written picture feedback. Allow three independent revision minutes and two for return/collection. Keep originals; judge eligible earlier components afterward. Do not score supplied corrections as independent fluency.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Day 158 used a complete ten to find its partners. Reuse its completed-frame correction if tickets copied the given part (error Q) or revisited a place (error B). Teach estimate as new: a sensible first guess before counting, allowed to change. [[sC-K159-guess]] [[steps:sC-K159-guess]] Wait for a guess before [[sC-K159-count]] [[steps:sC-K159-count]]. Keep first guess visible; a changed guess is useful, not a failure. Model paper eye-row/hand-row cues and writing the checked numeral.

#### 10 min. Guess then check

[[sC-P159-guess]] [[steps:sC-P159-guess]] Wait for every first mark, then [[sC-P159-check]] [[steps:sC-P159-check]]. Partners organize objects and mark the checked count. Exact count corrects the guess. Match eight to ten for formal group comparison, staying within ten. Larger arranged counts support magnitude reasoning; they extend no comparison-standard range. Keep sets above ten in rows or arrays.

#### 20 min. Targeted centers

[[sC-F159-1]] [[steps:sC-F159-1]] Error B revisits a member; error D uses spread as quantity. If many, spend at most five minutes matching the supplied eight/ten picture once per object. Two eight-minute partner centers revisit the supplied guess/count record or complete-ten cards; four minutes setup/tidy. Hear six rotating children for one forty-five-second practice ask each, 4.5 minutes inside centers. All others mark paper. Observe numeral formation separately from quantity knowledge.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[sC-X159]] Use the fresh two-row bar ticket and its picture record. Explain the eye/hand fields, then read one stem. Pause for the first guess mark before children organize and count; no second spoken ask. One minute directions, three independent work, one collect. Each child marks the picture, organizes in the Exit box and records the checked numeral. After class reserve fifteen teacher minutes for written picture feedback and fifteen for eligible earlier judgments; new work revises day 160 before judgment. A wrong guess alone earns no error letter.

### Other rooms

All tasks use paper and objects. No shared device is needed. Draw the masters on scrap paper if printing is unavailable. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Record mathematical help separately from access support; public talk may be passed.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares, drawn frames and numeral cards. Copy the eye/hand ticket fields if no printer. No device needed.

**Skip.** Center rotations and extra completed-ten cards.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P159-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Independently revise day 158 feedback.
- **8 min. Guess then check.** [[sC-P159-guess]] Wait for a first mark, then [[sC-P159-check]]. **Say.** "Finish the count record." Expect eight, written 8, fewer than ten; a changed guess is welcome.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[sC-F159-1]] Match each object once.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P159-gamea]] [[sC-P159-gameb]] Verify supplied pictures.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sC-X159]] First guess, then independent picture/count record. Expect fourteen, 14, more than ten. Give feedback afterward; revision day 160.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut five center-repetition minutes, keeping first teaching, revision and fresh evidence. Longer: describe how organizing made the supplied count easier; a better guess is not a speed contest.

### Words and supports

Review count, numeral, partner of ten, part, whole and ten ones only where used, from written days 30, 97, 108 and 146. Ten ones remain ten separate objects. Judge mathematics, not neatness. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Note strategy and names actually heard separately. A choral response or supplied answer never establishes individual counting or fluency.

Teach estimate as new on this day: a sensible first guess before counting.

### Tomorrow

Day 160 receives day 159 written feedback for independent Revision. Error B counts one bar twice; error L mismatches marks and numeral: track each supplied object once and connect the checked quantity to its numeral. Then record teen collections as ten individual ones and further ones. Keep incorrect initial guesses as evidence of checking, not errors.
