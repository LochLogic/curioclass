# Day 153: I can show partners and compare features.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open today's teacher-only answer key (d153-key, generated from items.csv) privately, plus teacher-key.md for carried evidence. Read all print aloud. Say "Invented for practice." Record actual names heard, original response, access support and mathematical help separately. Reserve transfer materials until their named part. Verify each weight pair is clearly B heavier and each strip pair B longer. Match models to the printed drawings. The two hand arcs record higher/lighter or lower/heavier; demonstrate this recording gesture on empty hands only.

### The day

#### 3 min. Revision

Return day 152's assessment with written picture feedback. Independent revision preserves first responses and help; eligible judgment follows class. Day 151 records arrive in Record without another performance.

#### 7 min. Product directions and Record

Carry finalized unit 8 evidence once. Read [[sC-E153-A]] [[sC-E153-L]] [[sC-E153-W]] [[sC-E153-F]] [[sC-E153-C]] [[sC-E153-B]] one at a time. Point to each Side 2 panel; explain mark conventions, not answers. Each pair shares verified real objects with separate papers and covered answers. Every child handles each comparison pair. Heft low over the table; never infer weight from drawings.

#### 48 min. Groups and independent products

Six groups of five, eight minutes each: two 45-second asks per child use 7.5 minutes, leaving half a minute for transition. Read [[sC-E153-P]], then [[sC-E153-Q]] individually; each child records before explaining. No coaching or peer answer reveal. Hear all thirty on these two components once; record names, actual help and any unfinished turns. Unheard components stay pending.

Other children complete six independent products: attributes of ONE real carton; aligned strips with exposed length difference; real hefting with heavier/lighter marked; represented flat/solid sort; square sides/corners/equal-side marks across turn; matched square faces of two differently sized cubes. Pictures record direct comparisons. Use the printed models and blanks, with actual objects alongside. Children waiting repeat corrected folder practice; no secure display practice.

#### 2 min. Collect

Retain products without public answers. No exit: fresh rows are end items. Allow twenty teacher minutes afterward for written picture feedback and eligible day 152 judgments. Return day 153 work day 154 for independent revision before judgment. Partners, comparisons and shapes reach day 155 Groups. Missing evidence remains pending.

### Other rooms

Objects and paper carry every task. A shared screen only enlarges pictures. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; unheard explanations stay pending.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the same panels. Use paper strips, carton, closed book, paper solids and large squares. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotation and completed counting/fact interviews.

- **3 min. Assessment revision.** Revise day 152 feedback independently.
- **10 min. Partners.** [[sC-E153-P]] [[sC-E153-Q]] **Say.** "Show two different splits of these eight squares." Expect distinct pairs totaling eight, such as three/five and two/six; three joins seven for ten.
- **12 min. Compare.** [[sC-E153-L]] [[sC-E153-W]] Child handles real pairs and marks. Expect B longer after alignment and verified B heavier. Do not coach.
- **5 min. Keep.** Save marks; feedback afterward, revision day 154 before judgment. Other components stay pending until completed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut optional repetitions, preserving revision and fresh evidence. Unfinished components remain pending. Longer: repeat corrected practice, keeping transfers unseen.

### Words and supports

Review count/numeral (days 1 and 115), part/whole (day 87), ten ones (day 112), flat/solid (day 126), longer/heavier (days 54 and 55), as used. Optional frames remain a choice; record help. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Strategy notes are unscored.

### Tomorrow

Day 154 Revision returns day 153 feedback before first judgment. Day 154 Groups takes number/teen needs from day 152 and unit 8 gaps from days 150 and 151. Day 155 Groups takes error Q/R partners, S/T comparisons, U/W shapes and V attributes.
