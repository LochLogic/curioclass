# Day 158: Find the partner of ten

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open the teacher-only d158-key.md privately. Print the picture ticket d158-ticket.md, keeping it covered until exit. Read every direction and label aloud. Choose six children for today's single practice observation, with actual names on the private record. Prepare complete practice cards from the item steps. Retain earlier originals alongside written picture feedback. Say "Invented for practice" before each invented scene.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P158-count]]. Rehearse ones and tens to one hundred together. Rebuild a missed transition using ten groups of ten loose ones, never a bundle counted as one or written numerals above twenty. Each child marks a practice participation circle on paper; independent sequence evidence remains the earlier record.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P158-gamea]] [[sC-P158-gameb]] Fifteen pairs check supplied action pictures. Three ninety-second rounds, forty-five seconds per partner, then thirty seconds tidy. Each child marks the resulting quantities. Immediate feedback; no speed score. Objects are optional for known facts.
Check: Match the supplied action and quantity.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 157 practice and exit sC-X157 with written picture feedback. Allow three independent revision minutes and two for return/collection. Keep originals; judge eligible earlier components afterward. Do not score supplied corrections as independent fluency.

#### 10 min. Connected work

Day 157 kept ten fixed while changing tray parts. Review [[sC-K158-1]] [[steps:sC-K158-1]]. Point from each part to its place in the complete equation, then to the whole. Children copy a picture record. The question comes before revealing the key.

#### 10 min. Partner records

[[sC-P158-1]] [[steps:sC-P158-1]] All nine starting numbers have complete picture cards. Work in fifteen pairs, one card at a time: thirty seconds each to verify and mark, nine minutes altogether, one minute tidy. Choose objects, drawings or known partners; no missing-addend notation required. Keep this supplied verification distinct from producing the exit additions.

#### 20 min. Targeted centers

If many day 157 tickets repeated the same tray plan (error X) or changed the whole (error R), model a single moved square while conserving ten, within five minutes. Reuse that supplied correction, never its reserved exit case. [[sC-F158-1]] [[steps:sC-F158-1]] Error Q copies the given part: connect only the other places to its partner. Fifteen pairs repeat completed records in two eight-minute centers, four minutes setup/tidy. Hear six children for one forty-five-second practice ask each, 4.5 minutes inside centers; others mark paper concurrently. Queue needs that do not fit.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

[[sC-X158]] Give the fresh pocket ticket; read context and single stem, without a second explanation ask. Every child draws additions on paper: one minute directions, three work, one collect. Use the ticket's Exit box for further marks. After class reserve fifteen teacher minutes for picture feedback and fifteen for eligible earlier judgments; new work receives independent revision on day 159 first. Record only sampled partner evidence, never mastery of all nine from one ticket.

### Other rooms

All tasks use paper and objects. No shared device is needed. Draw the masters on scrap paper if printing is unavailable. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Record mathematical help separately from access support; public talk may be passed.

### At home

**Swap.** Scrap-paper squares, drawn frames, handwritten number cards and the copied pocket ticket replace class supplies. No device needed.

**Skip.** Rotations and the nine-card round; keep one completed card.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P158-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Independently revise day 157 feedback.
- **8 min. Partners.** [[sC-P158-1]] **Say.** "Check each completed partner card." Expect complementary parts totaling ten, such as eight and two.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[sC-F158-1]] Replace the copied three with seven.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P158-gamea]] [[sC-P158-gameb]] Verify supplied action pictures; mark quantities.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sC-X158]] Read the fresh pocket context, then let your child draw alone. Expect six additions to empty pockets. Give feedback afterward; independent revision comes day 159.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five minutes of repeated center cards, preserving revision, both daily routines and exit. Longer: verify another listed partner with a different chosen support; keep the pocket case reserved.

### Words and supports

Review count, numeral, partner of ten, part, whole and ten ones only where used, from written days 30, 97, 108 and 146. Ten ones remain ten separate objects. Judge mathematics, not neatness. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Note strategy and names actually heard separately. A choral response or supplied answer never establishes individual counting or fluency.

### Tomorrow

Day 159 returns day 158 feedback for independent Revision before judgment. Error Q copies the given four; error B revisits an occupied pocket: verify the supplied completed-ten card and track empty places. Then teach the first guess-and-check routine with arranged collections; a guess may change after counting.
