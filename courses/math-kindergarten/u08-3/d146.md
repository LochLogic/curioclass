# Day 146: I can count two ways and show what repeats.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for evidence and today's teacher-only answer key for practice. Print the student page as corrected partial practice and today's sealed exit master. Lay out listed materials. Read every printed word aloud; say "Invented for practice." Fill actual names beside assigned roster numbers. Put other reserved cards away. Read paper cues before centers; private answers stay out of peers' hearing.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q146]] [[steps:s08-Q146]] Echoed partial stretches are practice; no independent full recital. Repeat a missed word with its object action.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return the full practice packet: `s08-G145-1`, `s08-G145-2`, `s08-G145-3`, `s08-G145-4`, `s08-G145-5`, `s08-G145-6`, `s08-P145-1`, `s08-P145-2`, `s08-R145-L`, `s08-R145-C`, `s08-Q145`. Preserve original marks with feedback.

Return day 145's exit, count, game and partial-practice papers with picture feedback. Review Return and repair. If many of day 145's tickets join stored flags, error C, [[s08-R146]]: [[steps:s08-R146]] Keep story revision for centers. More than a third means whole class; otherwise this slot serves the group.

#### 10 min. Counting model

Teach the pattern with ten groups of ten separate squares. Review count and keep track: one square remains one. [[s08-P146-1]] [[steps:s08-P146-1]] Compare repeated ones words. Then [[s08-P146-2]]: [[steps:s08-P146-2]] Move ten groups while supplying all tens names through one hundred. No numeral above twenty, chart or paper route. If many miss a transition, finish that group and echo boundary words again. Reserve the last two minutes to teach quiet centers: point to picture, correct supplied mark, keep paper, choose next card. Demonstrate before independent work.

#### 5 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s08-P146-3]] Six optional 45-second responses plus thirty seconds setup; others sketch physical groups. Expect repeated one, two words after next tens name. Retain actual MP.8 explanations as unscored notes, never infer speech from drawings.

#### 30 min. Counting observations

Assigned roster 1 to 10: [[s08-F2-ones]] then, after finishing or recording pending, [[s08-F2-tens]]. Two separate private asks with count-record.md; remove group cues. Ten times three minutes: two ones, 45 seconds tens, 15 transition. Child marks completion pictures; marks never prove unheard sequence. Keep separate components; unfinished stays pending, never rushed or replaced by choral work.

Others play five minutes: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs use [[s08-G146-1]] [[s08-G146-2]] [[s08-G146-3]] [[s08-G146-4]] [[s08-G146-5]] [[s08-G146-6]]. Teacher reads cards beforehand. Three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, thirty seconds tidy. Each records three quantities; immediate feedback compares supplied pictures. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to supplied picture.

Unassigned time: three independent minutes revising day 145 story feedback, then quiet repeated supplied practice with demonstrated picture cues. Collect every paper. Teacher stays with assigned counts. Partners choose next supplied card when one is called; tidy during unassigned time.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Show exit-146.md only now: ten clusters, each ten dots. Child traces once per group while whisper-counting. Paper shows tracing; only heard words support sequence notes. Never infer full count from traces. Read [[s08-X146]] exactly. All thirty mark independently or direct adult mark. At most two 45-second observations embedded in three paper minutes; thirty seconds collection fits five. Record actual names heard; no added oral requirement.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class: fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each; fifteen for practice packets; ten for window picture feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; access and mathematical help separate. Return every paper day 147; retain original evidence and later revisions. 

### Other rooms

Paper and objects carry every task. Hand-draw masters without printer. Shared screen may enlarge instruction; remove for observations. Adult marks follow child direction; access help noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters; use large paper squares in ten loose groups, crayon and scrap work mats.

**Skip.** Rotations; observe each complete count once across days 146 to 148.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo class short stretches.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P146-1]] **Say.** "Continue the teacher-started sixty-seven stretch." Expect sixty-eight, sixty-nine, seventy, seventy-one. Move separate squares as each word sounds.
- **5 min. Game and repair.** Play supplied game; revise yesterday's story from picture feedback.
- **12 min. Paper and count.** [[s08-P146-3]] **Say.** "What repeats after the next tens word?" Expect ones words repeat. Child sketches groups. Remove cues for private counts, each once; keep incomplete components pending. Mark blank completion pictures.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X146]] Show unseen group route. Child traces alone; expect ten traces, spoken tens only if heard. Save feedback for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed paper-repeat minutes, preserving game, revision, observations, model and exit. Longer: rearrange same permitted practice quantity. No reserved case or larger arithmetic.

### Words and supports

Review count, number word, keep track, day 1; counter, day 3; group, day 6; zero, day 30; add/take away, days 64/68; expression, equation, equals, day 78; part/whole, day 87; 5-frame/10-frame, days 3/26. Use objects/gestures. Read all print aloud at 18 point; accept home language, signs, tactile dots, directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting.

### Tomorrow

Day 147 returns every paper. If day 146 shows skipped decade, error J, five Return and repair minutes use fresh partial stretch. Observed ones during tens, error K, gets separate five. Exit route alone cannot diagnose J. Return pattern/count feedback for three independent center revision minutes. Next supplied dot parts for joining/taking.
