# Day 156: I can make and break apart ten.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open today's teacher-only answer key (d156-key, generated from items.csv) privately, plus teacher-key.md for carried evidence. Read all print aloud. Say "Invented for practice." Record actual names heard, original response, access support and mathematical help separately. Reserve transfer materials until their named part. Keep large paper pieces as the safe bead substitute. Print the separate half-sheet exit ticket; keep its parts picture covered until Exit ticket.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review [[sC-P156-count]] from day 146, with loose ones and spoken decades. Echo missed transitions without claiming a new full-sequence assessment.

#### 5 min. Game

[[sC-P156-gamea]] [[sC-P156-gameb]] Fifteen pairs, three 90-second rounds, swapping after 45 seconds; thirty seconds tidy. Mark matching quantities on scrap paper; compare supplied answers immediately, no speed score.
Check: Match the supplied drawing and result.

#### 5 min. Revision

Return day 155 transfer papers with written picture feedback. Three independent revision minutes plus two to collect. Preserve original accuracy/help. Eligible judgments follow class; unresolved components stay on the record.

#### 10 min. Parts model

Day 155's partner needs guide today's review. [[sC-K156-1]] [[steps:sC-K156-1]] Scatter, then regroup the same ten into visible striped/plain parts. Each child takes ten large paper squares and slides them on a drawn strip with a divider. This replaces small beads and wire. Keep every square separate; no bundle standing for one ten. Draw parts, then read complete equations.

#### 10 min. Connected work

[[sC-P156-1]] [[steps:sC-P156-1]] Read both total-right and total-left equations; nothing about the pieces changes when we read the equality in reverse. Then [[sC-P156-2]] distinguishes given part, missing part and whole. [[sC-F156-1]] [[steps:sC-F156-1]] Correct the supplied seven-whole claim by enclosing both parts. All children mark paper, teacher models after initial thinking.

#### 20 min. Targeted centers

Two eight-minute centers plus four setup/tidy. First slide and record permitted practice splits; then choose the recorded day 154/155 priority: loose-one counting transition, teen drawing, supplied action picture, real aligned strips/hefting or whole/face tracing. For day 154: error A includes unused places, error M switches counting units, error P misrepresents ten ones, error R omits part of the whole and error Y joins during removal. Repeat the matched corrected practice; keep today's storage exit unseen. Each child leaves one paper record. Teacher hears rotating six, one 45-second practice ask each, 4.5 minutes embedded in centers. Others work simultaneously; no device queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Use [[sC-X156]] with its new storage picture, not the divider model. Each child marks the half-sheet: both parts distinguished and ten conserved. Three paper minutes, one directions, one collection. No extra oral interview. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for written picture feedback, plus fifteen for earlier judgments. Return today's papers day 157 Revision before first judgment; keep unresolved evidence for remaining review days.

### Other rooms

Objects and paper carry every task. A shared screen only enlarges pictures. Offer tactile outlines, home language, signs or child-directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; unheard explanations stay pending.

### At home

**Swap.** Ten large scrap squares, striped with crayon, replace beads/wire; draw divider, optional frame and record. No screen or class set needed.

**Skip.** Center rotations and unneeded repairs.

- **3 min. Count.** [[sC-P156-count]] Rehearse a stretch.
- **4 min. Revision.** Independently change day 155 feedback.
- **8 min. Parts.** [[sC-P156-1]] **Say.** "Show this split of ten." Expect four/six, unchanged with total on either side.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[sC-F156-1]] Enclose both parts; expect ten, not seven.
- **5 min. Game.** [[sC-P156-gamea]] [[sC-P156-gameb]] Verify supplied object pictures and mark quantities.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sC-X156]] Show unseen storage picture; expect six/four conserving ten. Feedback afterward, revision day 157 before judgment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut optional repetitions, preserving revision and fresh evidence. Unfinished components remain pending. Longer: repeat corrected practice, keeping transfers unseen.

### Words and supports

Review count/numeral (days 1 and 115), part/whole (day 87), ten ones (day 112), flat/solid (day 126), longer/heavier (days 54 and 55), as used. Optional frames remain a choice; record help. Judge mathematics, not handwriting. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Strategy notes are unscored.

### Tomorrow

Day 157 Revision returns day 156 written feedback. If sC-X156 uses ten as an extra part, error R, repeat day 156 supplied whole correction in targeted centers. If a child copies the given part as its partner, error Q, fill only unoccupied places. Next, two different splits of ten in a one-actor tray story.
