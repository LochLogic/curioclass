# Day 20: Move the pieces, keep the amount

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-20.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Cover exit-20.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-1/teacher-key.md and d020-key.html private. Choose roster 7 to 12 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 19's totals and day 6's ordered words: [[s02-R20]] with ten moving squares. Then [[s02-L20]], a fresh physical eight-leaf transfer. Fifteen concurrent pairs take two 45-second turns, 1.5 minutes. Say these cases are invented for practice. Keep fixed picture routes covered.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return s02-P19-1 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 19's exit, spoken notes and linked practice. If many day 19 tickets showed an observed omission, review boundary moving with three fresh squares; if a heard sequence replaced the total, model enclosing the complete three-square group and naming three altogether. Choose one action, not both. Children correct supplied links on fresh paper; retain the original. Use [[s02-P20-2]] on three squares in a fresh L arrangement, with one mark and link supplied.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

[[s02-K20]] Count eight squares, then move them into compact clusters while keeping each visible. Ask before revealing the steps. [[steps:s02-K20]] Teach same number: the members moved, but none entered or left. Recounting is welcome. **Ask.** [[s02-C20]] Expect nothing. Show the puppet's false claim, [[s02-F20]], beside the checked eight. Watch for error B, arrangement treated as quantity.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs verify the adult's compact regrouping, one handler and one checker; switch. Two 45-second turns fit in 1.5 minutes, leaving time to discuss membership. Give the supplied incomplete eight-square record, [[s02-P20-1]]. Children link each square to one mark and repair the omission. Complete eight marks show the quantity without a numeral. Add the familiar finish tick. Every child keeps paper for collection.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Ten minutes continue unit 1 response from day 19: five for [[s02-S20]], five for a freshly turned five-square layout with the same repair. Return earlier feedback; choose the earliest tracking need from day 18, not a second assessment. Other pairs work on the supplied eight-square record and check displayed amounts from day 19. For the remaining ten minutes all pairs repeat compact regroupings and correct links. No bear pictures or widened five-piece arrangements. Collect practice for day 21.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return practice pieces to paper trays and screen. Set out the covered bear slips and five adult-cut pieces per child.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One minute: reveal each child's own half slip and read [[s02-X20]] exactly. Two minutes: everyone works independently on paper. Before the prompt, count the five starting bears once together. Children spread all five adult-cut pieces and outline each in its final place. Hear only roster 7 to 12, [[s02-O20]], six times 45 seconds, including notes: 4.5 minutes. Others finish quietly. Collect in half a minute. Mark in five teacher minutes after class, thirty papers at ten seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and heard names separately. Paper cannot establish unheard words. Return exit and practice with feedback on day 21; retain originals beside corrections.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large scrap squares and hand-copy the supplied record. Copy the two exit patches and adult-cut five paper bears.

**Skip.** Class rotations; keep the independent paper.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Join the ordered words.
- **5 min. Model.** Compact eight squares into clusters. **Say.** "What entered or left?" Expect nothing; still eight.
- **7 min. Repair.** [[s02-P20-1]] Link every square once and add the missing mark; expect eight marks.
- **5 min. Feedback.** Revisit one earlier missed touch using five squares.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Count the five bears once together, then read [[s02-X20]]. Keep the outlines. **Say.** "What did spreading do to the number of bears?" Expect it stayed five; none came or went. Keep the work for feedback and put pieces away.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 21 compares very different quantities by matching. Return day 20 papers: a missing piece during moving is observed error K; more because of spread is heard error B; sequence without total is error A. If many need a repair, use one fresh compact square regrouping in Return and repair, then collect its linked correction. Carry unresolved tracking notes, never invent unheard reasons.
