# Day 20: Move the pieces, keep the amount

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep u02-1/teacher-key.md and d020-key.html private. Prepare record.md and the student page; read every printed word aloud at 18 point. Choose roster 1 to 6. Cover each heard check and exit until its own turn. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Fingers are optional. Cut five bears per child from bear-pieces.md; keep eight fresh leaves covered.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count and retrieval

Review day 19's totals and day 6's ordered words: [[s02-R20]] with ten paper squares. Everyone joins twice, then a partner points as the other counts. Two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes across fifteen concurrent pairs; remaining time holds setup and feedback. Say all cases are invented for practice. Keep the leaf transfer covered until Counting centers.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return the whole prior-day paper packet and observation notes: one minute return, one model, two concurrent fresh corrections, one collection. Use one priority action; retain originals and revisions.

Return day 19's exit, s02-O19 heard notes and s02-P19-1 linked practice. If many day 19 tickets showed an observed omission, review boundary moving with three fresh squares; if a heard sequence replaced the total, model enclosing the complete three-square group and naming three altogether. Choose one action, not both. Children correct supplied links on fresh paper; retain the original. Use [[s02-P20-2]] on three squares in a fresh L arrangement, with one mark and link supplied.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

[[s02-K20]] Count eight squares, then move them into compact clusters while keeping each visible. Ask before revealing the steps. [[steps:s02-K20]] Teach same number: the members moved, but none entered or left. Recounting is welcome. **Ask.** [[s02-C20]] Expect nothing. Show the puppet's false claim, [[s02-F20]], beside the checked eight. Watch for error B, arrangement treated as quantity.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs verify the adult's compact regrouping, one handler and one checker; switch. Two 45-second turns fit in 1.5 minutes, leaving time to discuss membership. Give the supplied incomplete eight-square record, [[s02-P20-1]]. Children link each square to one mark and repair the omission. Complete eight marks show the quantity without a numeral. Add the familiar finish tick. Every child keeps paper for collection.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O20]] from heard-20.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 1 to 6: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Ten minutes continue day 18's Unit 1 response records from day 19: five return earlier feedback and repair the earliest tracking need with [[s02-S20]], then five for the fresh transfer [[s02-L20]]. Reveal eight new loose paper leaves only now. Each child chooses touching or moving and dots every counted leaf once. Hear six children still missing tracking evidence, one 45-second ask each plus thirty seconds setup, while classmates work concurrently. Retain each marked leaf set in its own folded scrap-paper pocket, labeled by the adult, with the dated observed actions and no extra explanation ask. Missing earlier observations remain not yet seen.

Remaining ten minutes: pairs repeat the supplied eight-square compact regroupings and correct adjacent links. All return paper for day 21. No bear pictures or five-piece spreading. Allow ten teacher minutes after class to mark thirty transfer papers at twenty seconds each.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return practice pieces to paper trays and screen. Set out the covered bear slips and five adult-cut pieces per child.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-20.md only now and read [[s02-X20]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children begin paper for two minutes. The six named children wait with cards covered until their own observed turns. Observe the sole requested action for roster 1 to 6, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return the exit and heard-check paper on day 21 in Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large scrap squares, eight adult-cut paper leaves, and copied practice and covered bear slips.

**Skip.** Class rotations; a session may be skipped.

- **2 min. Count.** Join familiar words through ten.
- **4 min. Model.** Compact eight squares. **Say.** "What entered or left?" Expect nothing; still eight.
- **4 min. Work.** [[s02-P20-1]] Expect eight linked marks.
- **10 min. Response.** Return earlier feedback and repair one missed move for five minutes. Then reveal eight fresh leaves. **Say.** "Show how you keep track while counting these leaves." Expect eight different marked visits. Keep initial work and revision.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put practice away; uncover five large paper bears.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X20]]. Expect all five bears retained, farther apart and outlined. Note help and give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12; Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8. Review total and Finish tick from day 19. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 21 compares very different quantities by matching. Return day 20 papers: a missing piece during moving is observed error K; more because of spread is heard error B; sequence without total is error A. If many need a repair, use one fresh compact square regrouping in Return and repair, then collect its linked correction. Carry unresolved tracking notes, never invent unheard reasons.
