# Day 19: An amount and its total

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-19.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Add one adjacent mark and link to the master before copying. Cover exit-19.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-1/teacher-key.md and d019-key.html private. Choose roster 25 to 30 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Day 18 closed counting records; today begins showing quantities and includes its promised response. Say all cases are invented for practice. Show three squares. **Ask.** [[s02-H19]] Expect fingers, pieces or marks. Model supplied fingers beside the squares; offer a plain mat. Leave the unit question open for later comparisons.

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review ordered words through ten from day 6, using the teacher's fingers or pointing to ten squares: [[s02-R19]]. Count together twice with a pause after ten, not a speed goal. Fingers optional; point to squares instead. Model slowly if words and actions separate.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review one word per object from unit 1. [[s02-L19]] Fifteen pairs work together, two 45-second turns: 1.5 minutes, plus demonstration and handling. Notice missed touches, not totals alone. This is unscored retrieval.

#### 7 min. Connect quantities

Interpret displayed amounts in order: [[s02-T19]], [[s02-U19]], [[s02-V19]], [[s02-W19]]. **Ask.** Read each single stem before showing its steps. [[steps:s02-T19]] Repeat that process for five, eight and ten. Name total as how many altogether, including every member. Partners point to the complete group; no numeral cards or structured frames.

#### 5 min. Connected work

Read [[s02-O19]] from heard-19.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 25 to 30: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Return s01-P17, s01-X17 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 17 papers and day 18's assessment records with feedback. Choose one unit 1 priority: error A, reach a missed piece; error B, cover a revisited piece; error C, model ordered words; error D, pair one word per move. Ten minutes fulfill unit 1 response: five for [[s02-F19]], five for a fresh five-square arrangement and retry. Review the boundary from day 12. Select the earliest need: reach missed pieces, cover revisited pieces, model ordered words or pair one word per move. Preserve original and corrected records; unheard assessments stay on the missing list.

Use five separate minutes to repair a retrieval miss through [[s02-L19]] with freshly turned loose leaves. Final five: pairs interpret supplied amounts from Connect quantities and check the paper links. Others continue these known tasks during the response group. No individual teacher interview is promised here. Send family.md without requesting home reports.

Use the final five familiar-practice minutes for [[s02-P19-1]]: finish adjacent links on the supplied three-square picture and review the Finish tick.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Pairs return pieces concurrently to paper trays and screen off exit supplies.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-19.md only now and read [[s02-X19]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 25 to 30, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut scrap squares replace counters; use a plain sheet instead of fingers. Copy the supplied picture and covered half slip if needed.

**Skip.** Whole-class rotations; keep feedback if available.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Count together without rushing.
- **5 min. Connect.** Display three, five, eight and ten in turn. **Say.** "How many do you see?" Expect each displayed total.
- **7 min. Record.** Start one linked mark on the supplied three-square picture. [[s02-P19-1]] Expect three unique links. Add a finish tick.
- **5 min. Repair.** Fix the missed touch. Move each of five squares once; expect five touches.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X19]] on the unseen exit-19.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 20 rearranges a counted set. Return day 19 paper in Return and repair: an omitted requested mark suggests error K only when observed; a heard sequence without a total is error A. If many need either action, model one correction on a fresh supplied square case, collect a retry and queue the other. Keep ten unit 1 response minutes in centers.
