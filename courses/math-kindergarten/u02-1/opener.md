# Total and arrangement

A count names a whole set. Moving the same members changes position, not quantity. Interpret supplied quantities first; independent seven-clip production is held for the exit. Move eight squares into compact clusters on day 20. Numerals stay stored until day 30; frames are not yet structured models.

Read every printed word aloud; no independent reading. Demonstrate familiar Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair again if needed. Fifteen concurrent pairs can each take two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes; at most two public volunteers, and a child may pass. Large adult-cut paper squares replace counters; hand drawings replace printing. Inspect for damage and keep pieces out of mouths. Fingers are optional. No digital tool or device turns are needed.

Before class, print only the needed day practice and exit pictures at 18 point. Adult-cut the paper pieces. Keep teacher-key.md private, never on a student page or display. Each eight-minute half-slip exit uses launch 1, simultaneous paper 2, six 45-second follow-ups 4.5, collection 0.5. Everyone returns paper. Reserved task pictures appear only at their collection, not at centers.

Return feedback in the next named repair part. More than ten sharing one letter triggers one five-minute class correction with a fresh practice response; otherwise group while others correct familiar work. Queue other errors, never stack repair minutes or run a teacher-led repair during conferences. Preserve initial and supported work, feedback, revision and judgment dates. A paper mark cannot prove unheard words, reasons or error letters.

Common errors: A: Repeats sequence instead of naming total. Enclose the counted group and name its total. B: Judges quantity by spread. Move the same members; check none entered or left. K: Omits an object. Move each across counted-uncounted boundary once.

The record sheet in u02-1 holds the thirty-child roster. Counting conferences: five children daily on days 34 to 39, four minutes each. Day 40: thirteen paper minutes, then twenty reason minutes. Days 41/42: ten two-minute F2/F3 turns each, no second paper collection. Keep written feedback after class and revision in the named parts. Fresh day 42 sorting, not day 41 practice, is scored from retained work on day 44. Forty minutes on day 44 handles bounded missing evidence only; unresolved components stay not yet seen. First-made day 44 work is revised on day 45 before judgment.

For one child, use count 3, model/rehearsal 5, guided work 7, repair 5, exit 8, tidy 2, thirty minutes. An adult takes both partner roles and hears this child. Split or skip a day as needed. Assessment replaces familiar work; preserve the same independent response and supports. No child-facing timer.
