# Section 3.3 teacher-only answer key

Read every student prompt aloud. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. An unheard name or explanation remains not yet seen, even if paper is correct. Record motor or communication access separately from mathematical help. Never print these answers on a child card.

## s03-X53, day 53

Show one thing about this toy we could measure.

Answer: One of length, height, weight or how much the open wagon body holds, indicated on the toy picture.

Look for: Every child marks one attribute on the reserved paper toy wagon. Hear six descriptions. Accept an end-to-end length mark, upward height mark, lifting gesture or marked inside capacity. No second attribute or choice is required. | Shape: Propose one measurable attribute of the new wagon; practice corrects one supplied attribute at a time on a different carton.

Samples: Shown: end-to-end mark with How long. | Not yet seen: only big with no identified attribute (F); color alone is L.

## s03-X54, day 54

Show a fair length comparison.

Answer: Near ends aligned, ribbons straight without stretching; wavy-edged extends beyond straight-edged.

Look for: Move actual ribbons on blank paper, trace common starts/exposed end; hear six. Edges wavy, not centerline; straighten without stretching. | Shape: Produce fair alignment of loose ribbons on blank paper; practice rigid strips with supplied baseline.

Samples: Shown: Near ends aligned, ribbons straight without stretching; wavy-edged extends beyond straight-edged. | Not yet seen: Straight-edged longer from unaligned endpoint. (G).

Samples: Shown: Small star envelope heavier, large ring envelope lighter, after hefting and swapping hands. | Not yet seen: Large ring envelope must be heavier because bigger. (H).
