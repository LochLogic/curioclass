# Day 56: Use a pour to compare

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-56.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1: touch or move each object once. **Ask.** [[s03-W56]] Count six squares and mark matching dots. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty, no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 55's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 55's tickets and practice feedback. If many assumed bigger means heavier, error H, select [[s03-F56-H]] with repair-56-H.md and fresh practice cartons: guide heft and hand swap, correct the claim. Otherwise queued error M uses [[s03-F56-M]] with repair-55-M.md and rigid strips. One five-minute action only; queue others. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise familiar paper. Everyone returns a correction. Do not use reserved envelopes.

#### 8 min. Rehearse capacity

Review capacity, day 53, and holds more/less with real transfer, day 55. Apply yesterday's procedure to the same small cup and taller, wider receiving cup. Adult fills small cup to rim, empties receiving cup, pours all the water over a tray, then lowers both. **Ask.** [[s03-P56-transfer]] Children finish the supplied diagram from the witnessed result. The source empties; the destination has space above its water. Use yesterday's explanation: receiving cup could hold more. No winner prediction or selection. Adult stops at rim and dries spills immediately; no tasting, food, glass or sand.

#### 12 min. Connected transfer record
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work concurrently. Each directs the sequence with dry hands while partner traces stages on the supplied picture; adult alone performs one shared practice pour. Two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling use two minutes; remaining ten hold drawing and correction. **Ask.** [[s03-P56-hold]] Mark the empty space in the supplied destination. Teacher checks water below the rim and the marked region above it. If many mark container height, error I, repeat this known demonstration and track where the water ends. Pictures record a real transfer; they never establish capacity by themselves.

Children continue this familiar correction independently while the teacher begins today's observation roster for all twelve minutes. Launch the paper routine before starting; no public shares or concurrent teacher-led repair.

#### 20 min. Centers

Finish F1 cells 163 to 180 on covered assessment-display.md, fixed viewing arrow: one indicated target, one master prompt, one response and retained paper mark. F1 takes eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 setup minutes, fifteen total.

Finish F2 cells 121 to 140 with one covered assessment-pairs.md panel per naming prompt, retaining marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Component rows: s03-F1-56-1, s03-F1-56-2, s03-F1-56-3, s03-F1-56-4, s03-F1-56-5, s03-F1-56-6, s03-F2-56-1, s03-F2-56-2, s03-F2-56-3, s03-F2-56-4, s03-F2-56-5.

These 31 teacher minutes use all twelve preceding Connected minutes and nineteen Center minutes. One center minute remains for collection. Other children revise written feedback for five independent minutes, then correct familiar practice claims or make count-dot records. No concurrent teacher-led repair, reserved practice preview or unsupervised water. Keep original and supported revision; never infer an unheard name.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice pieces concurrently on table trays. Adult checks dry floor and sets covered reserved apparatus on the tray.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

At the common exit turn, first reveal packet-56.md bowl/pot and exit-56.md. Adult fills the bowl, leaves pot empty, then pours into pot over tray, stopping exactly at rim; no spill. **Ask.** [[s03-X56]] Read exactly. Response format: ring the chosen container and mark where water remains. Give no winner or height hint. Shared pour/directions and concurrent marks: first 2.5 minutes. Roster 7 to 12 give six 45-second responses, 4.5 minutes total; collect for one minute. Thirty papers at twenty seconds take ten teacher minutes after class. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard dates/help. Unheard explanations stay not yet seen. Return day 57; record.md schedules fresh follow-ups. Missing real apparatus leaves capacity pending, not picture-guessed.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels before class. Household unbreakable cups, bowl and pot replace purchased vessels; tray and towel remain necessary. Paper scoops do not replace liquid evidence. Child-directed adult pouring keeps hands dry. All records use paper; one shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Record access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Use household unbreakable practice cups, reserved bowl/pot, tray, towel and room-temperature water. Draw the panels. No glass, food or tasting.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares and mark matching dots.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s03-P56-transfer]] Repeat day 55's full small-cup pour into the empty larger cup. Adult pours.
  **Say.** "Finish the transfer picture." Expect empty source and space above water in destination.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P56-hold]] Child marks that unfilled space; repeat the known pour if needed.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 55 feedback; correct one practice weight claim by hefting.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Dry and clear practice vessels.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X56]] Reveal reserved bowl/pot and slip. Pour full bowl into empty pot, stop at rim. Read exactly. Child marks independently. Expect bowl, with water remaining there. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions, preserving repair, witnessed transfer and exit. Longer: replay the known small-to-larger practice pour and check the drawing against its stages, without changing the task.

### Words and supports

Review capacity and weight, day 53; holds more/less, heavier/lighter and about the same, day 55. Frame: "There is space here." Read aloud. Offer tactile boundaries and usual communication aids; adult follows the child's movement directions. Remove practice diagrams and frames for the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 returns day 56's tickets and practice feedback before drawing shapes from builds. Error I, tall pot chosen from height, gets one five-minute fresh practice-vessel transfer and space/liquid mark in Return and repair. No reserved bowl/pot replay. Keep unheard explanations pending for the fresh-case schedule in record.md.
