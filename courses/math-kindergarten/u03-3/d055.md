# Day 55: Feel and compare weight

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md and packet-55.md instructions private. Pretest fifteen sealed ring/star envelope pairs: star noticeably heavier. Prepare fifteen guided carton sets plus matching-weight pairs, with secured paper filling. Verify full small practice cup leaves space in larger cup. Set tray, towel, broad pencils, covered exit slips and assessment panels with viewing arrow. Read all child print aloud at 18 point. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count, day 1, and equal counts, day 22. **Ask.** [[s03-W55]] Touch eight squares once and draw matching dots. Teacher aligns the squares and dots one to one: no extras. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty. No timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 54's tickets, written task records and practice feedback: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. For unaligned far ends, error G, select [[s03-F55-G]] with repair-55-G.md: align rigid starts and mark the upper strip's exposed end. For turning said to change length, error M, select [[s03-F55-M]] with repair-55-M.md instead. Queue other needs. More than ten of thirty: class; otherwise group while others revise familiar marks.

#### 4 min. Model weight

Say these scenes are invented for practice. Review weight, day 53. Demonstrate lifting near the tabletop, lowering, swapping hands and lifting again. **Ask.** [[s03-P55-weight]] Read the bigger-must-be-heavier claim. Children follow the handling cues: smaller feels heavier, larger lighter, error H. Heavier means more weight than the other; lighter means less. Then [[s03-P55-same]], with claim "These cartons feel very different." Guide the matching pair: about the same means no clear felt difference, not measured equality. Two demonstrations take one minute; two concurrent partner rounds take three. Launch the repeated correction routine.

#### 12 min. Connected guided heft
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen concurrent pairs repeat the two launched claim corrections three times. Each round gives both children 45 seconds handling/marking plus thirty seconds reset: six rounds fill twelve minutes. Follow the modeled lift/lower/swap/lift/lower sequence, using familiar cartons only. The teacher uses all twelve minutes for the roster below; no simultaneous demonstration or repair.

#### 5 min. Capacity introduction

Review capacity, day 53: how much fits when full to the rim. Adult fills small practice cup, then pours into empty larger cup over a tray. **Ask.** [[s03-P55-capacity]] Children mark space above the water. Teach holds more/holds less: the larger still has room after the full small cup empties, so larger holds more, small less. Use room-temperature water; stop at rim, dry spills/floor immediately. No glass, food or tasting. Launch independent diagram correction before returning to observations.

#### 19 min. Centers

Finish F1 cells 145 to 162, children 25 to 27, on covered assessment-display.md, fixed viewing arrow: one target, exact prompt, response and retained paper mark. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 setup minutes take fifteen minutes.

Finish F2 cells 101 to 120, children 21 to 24, with covered assessment-pairs.md panels in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order; retain marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Rows: s03-F1-55-1 through s03-F1-55-6; s03-F2-55-1 through s03-F2-55-5.

These 31 teacher minutes use all twelve Connected and nineteen Center minutes. Children collect practice papers concurrently during the final teacher reset, with no extra minute. Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Others revise day 54 observation-picture feedback for five independent minutes, then repeat launched claims/diagrams or eight-square dot records on practice-records.md. Keep originals/revisions; never infer unheard words. No unsupervised water.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect cartons. Leave paper ready; keep reserved envelopes sealed until each turn.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

**Ask.** [[s03-X55]] Read exactly. Reveal exit-55.md and reserved envelope pieces only at each child's turn; ring heavier and mark lighter after handling. Partners face away until their turn, with no coaching. One minute launch, two concurrent paper minutes including twelve pairs' two 45-second turns, six observed 45-second turns for children 1 to 6, half a minute collection. Paper alone cannot show hefting; record help/heard dates.

After class, thirty daily-work packets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; observation feedback takes fifteen more. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work day 56 Return and repair; observation pictures in its first five independent center minutes. record.md schedules fresh follow-ups.

### Other rooms

All tasks use paper, hand-copied if needed. Sealed household cartons/envelopes replace purchased objects. Pretest every pair; pictures never establish weight. An adult supports the objects at the child's direction. Real unbreakable cups remain necessary for capacity, with an adult pour. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures only.

### At home

**Swap.** Sealed cartons with secured folded-paper filling replace heft objects. Use household cups, tray, towel and room-temperature water. No glass or tasting.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count eight squares and mark matching dots.
- **5 min. Weight.** [[s03-P55-weight]] Guide both lifts and the hand swap before correcting the claim.
  **Say.** "Fix this weight claim." Expect smaller carton heavier, larger lighter after handling.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P55-same]] Guide matching-carton correction. [[s03-P55-capacity]] Fill small cup, pour fully into larger; mark spare space. Expect larger holds more, smaller less.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 54 feedback; correct either shifted starts or turned-length claim.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X55]] Reveal reserved envelopes and slip. Read exactly. Child hefts, swaps hands and marks independently. Expect star heavier, ring lighter. Record help and return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce centers, preserving matching-weight comparison, capacity introduction and exit. Longer: repeat guided hand swaps with the same practice pair to verify the unchanged relationship.

### Words and supports

Review weight and capacity, day 53; length and turn, days 51/47. Heavier/lighter name both objects. About the same reports felt similarity. Frame: "The ___ is heavier than the ___." Read aloud; accept pointing, signing or communication aids. Record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 returns day 55's tickets and practice feedback. Error H, bigger assumed heavier, gets one five-minute fresh practice-carton heft correction; queued error M gets a turned-strip correction instead. Day 56 applies today's full-small-cup transfer and space record before a new capacity exit.
