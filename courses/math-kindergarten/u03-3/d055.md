# Day 55: Feel and compare weight

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-55.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count, day 1, and equal counts, day 22. **Ask.** [[s03-W55]] Touch eight squares once and draw matching dots. Teacher aligns the squares and dots one to one: no extras. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty. No timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 54's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 54's tickets and practice feedback. If many judged from unaligned far ends, error G, select [[s03-F55-G]] with repair-55-G.md: align rigid starts and mark the upper strip's exposed end. If turning changed the judgment, error M, select [[s03-F55-M]] with repair-55-M.md instead. One five-minute action, queue the other or error L. More than ten of thirty: class repair; otherwise group while others revise familiar paper. All return corrections.

#### 4 min. Model weight

Review weight, day 53. Lift the two safe sealed practice cartons close to the tabletop, swap hands and repeat. **Ask.** [[s03-P55-weight]] Read the supplied bigger-must-be-heavier claim first. Correct from felt weight: the small carton is heavier, the larger lighter. Size alone cannot decide weight, error H. Then [[s03-P55-same]]: guide the matching practice pair and correct its supplied claim. Both feel about the same after swapping hands; this does not establish exact measured equality.

#### 12 min. Connected guided heft
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs follow the handling steps already modeled, using the supplied picture cues: lift, lower, swap hands, lift, lower. Correct the supplied claims for the familiar practice cartons on paper, then repeat with the matching practice pair. No unfamiliar independent heft comparison or reserved tubs. The teacher does not run a second demonstration during the observation turns.

Children continue this familiar correction independently while the teacher begins today's observation roster for all twelve minutes. Launch the paper routine before starting; no public shares or concurrent teacher-led repair.

#### 5 min. Capacity introduction

Review capacity, how much fits when full to the rim. Adult fills the small practice cup, then pours it into the empty larger cup over a tray. **Ask.** [[s03-P55-capacity]] Children mark space above the water on the supplied picture. The small cup was full; the larger still has room, so it holds more and the small holds less. Use room-temperature water; stop for spills and dry the floor immediately.

#### 19 min. Centers

Finish F1 cells 145 to 162 on covered assessment-display.md, fixed viewing arrow: one indicated target, one master prompt, one response and retained paper mark. F1 takes eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 setup minutes, fifteen total.

Finish F2 cells 101 to 120 with one covered assessment-pairs.md panel per naming prompt, retaining marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Component rows: s03-F1-55-1, s03-F1-55-2, s03-F1-55-3, s03-F1-55-4, s03-F1-55-5, s03-F1-55-6, s03-F2-55-1, s03-F2-55-2, s03-F2-55-3, s03-F2-55-4, s03-F2-55-5.

These 31 teacher minutes use all twelve preceding Connected minutes and nineteen Center minutes. One center minute remains for collection. Other children revise written feedback for five independent minutes, then correct familiar practice claims or make count-dot records. No concurrent teacher-led repair, reserved practice preview or unsupervised water. Keep original and supported revision; never infer an unheard name.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect cartons. Leave paper ready; keep reserved envelopes sealed until each turn.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

**Ask.** [[s03-X55]] Read exactly with exit-55.md and packet-55.md first seen at each child's independent turn. Response format: ring heavier, draw a line to lighter after handling. Twelve pairs of nonroster children use 1.5 minutes for two turns within the first 2.5 minutes, directions included; no partner coaching or answer sharing. Roster 1 to 6 then receive six 45-second first-reveal observation turns, using remaining sets. Collection one minute. Thirty papers at twenty seconds take ten teacher minutes after class. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen, with help/heard dates. Paper alone cannot show hefting. Return day 56; fresh follow-ups follow record.md.

### Other rooms

All tasks use paper, hand-copied if needed. Sealed household cartons/envelopes replace purchased objects. Pretest every pair; pictures never establish weight. An adult supports the objects at the child's direction. Real unbreakable cups remain necessary for capacity, with an adult pour. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures only.

### At home

**Swap.** Sealed cartons with secured folded-paper filling replace heft objects. Use household cups, tray, towel and room-temperature water. No glass or tasting.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count eight squares and mark matching dots.
- **5 min. Weight.** [[s03-P55-weight]] Guide both lifts and the hand swap before correcting the claim.
  **Say.** "Fix this weight claim." Expect smaller carton heavier, larger lighter after handling.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P55-same]] Guide matching-carton correction. [[s03-P55-capacity]] Fill small cup, pour fully into larger; mark spare space. Expect larger holds more, smaller less.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 54 feedback; correct either shifted starts or turned-length claim.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X55]] Reveal reserved envelopes and slip. Read exactly. Child hefts, swaps hands and marks independently. Expect star heavier, ring lighter. Record help and return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce centers, preserving matching-weight comparison, capacity introduction and exit. Longer: repeat guided hand swaps with the same practice pair to verify the unchanged relationship.

### Words and supports

Review weight and capacity, day 53; length and turn, days 51/47. Heavier/lighter name both objects. About the same reports felt similarity. Frame: "The ___ is heavier than the ___." Read aloud; accept pointing, signing or communication aids. Record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 returns day 55's tickets and practice feedback. Error H, bigger assumed heavier, gets one five-minute fresh practice-carton heft correction; queued error M gets a turned-strip correction instead. Day 56 applies today's full-small-cup transfer and space record before a new capacity exit.
