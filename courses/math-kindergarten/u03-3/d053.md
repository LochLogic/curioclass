# Day 53: Choose what to compare

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-53.md through u03-3/practice-records.md. Set clean boxes and containers, comparison objects, paper and broad pencils, large paper squares, flat shape cards and paper shapes. Prepare repair-53-D.md with four wide strips. Fold a large open toy wagon, keep it and exit-53.md covered. Use large intact pieces, no small joins or mouth contact. Read all child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and tracking, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W53]] Touch six squares once, then mark six dots. Count together to ten, with supported continuation toward twenty. Review short/long, day 41: gesture along the short and long sides of the practice carton, no question or units.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2, and closed/join, days 46 and 51. Return day 52's tickets and practice feedback. If many left open triangle joins, use [[s03-F53-D]] on repair-53-D.md, a fresh partly built four-sided frame. Join its missing edge and mark the repair. More than ten of thirty: class repair; otherwise a group while others revise familiar paper. All return corrections. Queue other letters. Unobserved construction independence remains not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Model attributes

Whole class. An attribute is something about an object. Review length, day 51: gesture along one open carton. **Ask.** [[s03-P53-length]] Then teach height, gesturing from bottom to top: [[s03-P53-attribute-height]]. Teach weight by lifting the safe carton: [[s03-P53-attribute-weight]]. Finally point inside for how much it holds: [[s03-P53-attribute-hold]]. One supplied attribute at a time, followed by its gesture. Length and height describe different directions; weight needs handling. Capacity means the most a container can hold, not what is inside now. No multiple-attribute proposal in practice.

#### 12 min. Connected attribute correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work concurrently: two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling per round. First mark this carton's length on Side 1. Then [[s03-P53-height]]: stand the actual cartons on the same bottom line before fixing the sketch. Taller top is marked, not the longest horizontal side. A lower carton has height too. Everyone moves and records. If many say only big, error F, name just the supplied height and gesture it again. Eight remaining minutes allow correction and another guided round.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Ten minutes: repeat the supplied length/height/weight/holds gestures, one prompt at a time, with this carton; draw a gesture after each. Ten minutes: count the same six squares and match one dot to each. Teacher watches for color alone, error L, and redirects to the named amount. At most two public volunteers; passing allowed. All scenes are invented for practice, said aloud. Practice stays in the folder, unscored. No storage tub or toy wagon in centers.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice pieces on table trays concurrently. Keep paper and pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's exit-53.md at their turn; show the reserved wagon then. **Ask.** [[s03-X53]] Read exactly. Response format: two attribute-gesture drawings beside the toy, one ringed. No list of attributes or demonstration. First 2.5 minutes: directions and concurrent paper for the other twenty-four. Roster 19 to 24 each get 45 seconds, 4.5 minutes total, with their first reveal. Collect for one minute. Thirty papers at twenty seconds each take ten teacher minutes after class. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names/help separately. Return day 54. Unheard words remain not yet seen; record.md schedules fresh cases through days 64/65.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the exact panels before class; household cartons and large folded paper replace purchased pieces. Every task uses paper. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Child-directed adult movement, signing or usual communication aids provide access; record mathematical help separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw panels; use a clean open carton, large paper squares and folded toy wagon. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares and draw matching dots.
- **5 min. Model.** Gesture this carton's length, then height, then lift it for weight. Point inside for how much fits.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P53-length]] [[s03-P53-height]] Move actual cartons before marking the picture.
  **Say.** "Fix the height picture." Expect bottoms on the same line, taller top marked.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 52 feedback; close one fresh partly built four-sided frame.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X53]] Reveal the toy and slip. Read exactly; child draws two gestures and rings one. Expect two measurable attributes, not just big or color. Record help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated centers, preserving every attribute, repair and exit. Longer: repeat one supplied attribute gesture from the opposite side of the same carton.

### Words and supports

Review length, longer and shorter, day 51; short/long, day 41. Height: bottom to top. Weight: what lifting lets us feel. Holds: what fits inside. Frame: "This shows ___." Read aloud; use broad pencils and tactile edges. Remove practice frames from the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 returns day 53's tickets and practice feedback. Error F, only big, or error L, only color, receives one five-minute fresh carton-attribute correction. Queue the other need. Day 54 explains why starts must meet when comparing lengths and heights.
