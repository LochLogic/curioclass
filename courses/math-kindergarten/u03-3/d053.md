# Day 53: Choose what to compare

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-53.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1, and Paper count-dot record, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W53]] Touch six squares once, then mark six dots. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty. Review short/long, day 41: gesture along the practice carton's sides.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2, and closed/join, days 46 and 51. Return day 52's tickets, written task records and practice feedback: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. If many left open triangle joins, use [[s03-F53-D]] on repair-53-D.md, a fresh partly built four-sided frame. Join its missing edge and mark the repair. More than ten of thirty: class; otherwise group while others revise familiar marks. Queue other letters. Unobserved construction independence remains not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Model attributes

Whole class. Say these scenes are invented for practice. An attribute is something about an object. Review length, day 51: gesture along one open carton. **Ask.** [[s03-P53-length]] Then teach height, bottom to top: [[s03-P53-attribute-height]]. Teach weight by lifting the safe carton near the tabletop: [[s03-P53-attribute-weight]]. Point inside for how much fits: [[s03-P53-attribute-hold]]. Capacity means the most it can hold, not its current contents. Supply one attribute at a time; children copy each gesture and picture mark. Demonstrate [[s03-P53-height]] on two cartons, bottoms aligned, before launching independent rounds.

#### 12 min. Connected attribute correction
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen concurrent pairs repeat the five launched tasks in page order: length, height, weight, what fits, then height comparison. Two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling per round use ten minutes; two minutes revisit the height correction. Move cartons before recording. Error F, only big: partners return to the supplied height gesture. The teacher uses all twelve minutes for the roster below, with no simultaneous demonstration or public share.

#### 20 min. Centers

Use assessment-display.md and viewing arrow for F1 cells 109 to 126, child first then above, below, beside, next to, in front of, behind. Indicate one target and read its one master prompt. Let the child mark that target and respond; scribe words without supplying them. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 minutes setup take fifteen minutes. The six component rows are s03-F1-53-1, s03-F1-53-2, s03-F1-53-3, s03-F1-53-4, s03-F1-53-5, s03-F1-53-6. Keep other children on familiar paper; never show this display in practice.

Also collect F2 cells 61 to 80, children 13 to 16, using covered assessment-pairs.md panels in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order. Read one prompt; retain feature marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Rows: s03-F2-53-1 through s03-F2-53-5. The combined roster uses twelve Connected and nineteen Center minutes; the last minute collects papers. No repeat interview or unheard-name inference.

Review Counting centers, day 1. Others revise day 52's observation-picture feedback for their first five independent center minutes, then repeat launched corrections or the six-square count-dot record on practice-records.md. Preserve originals and supported revisions. No unsupervised water.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice pieces on table trays concurrently. Keep paper and pencils ready.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-53.md and each reserved wagon at that child's turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X53]] Read exactly. Each child marks one measurable attribute beside or on the toy picture. No second gesture or choice. One minute launch, two concurrent paper minutes for twenty-four, six 45-second turns for children 19 to 24, half a minute collection. Record heard names/help separately; unheard words stay not yet seen.

After class, thirty daily-work packets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; observation feedback takes fifteen more. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work day 54 Return and repair; observation pictures in its first five independent center minutes. record.md schedules fresh follow-ups.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the exact panels before class; household cartons and large folded paper replace purchased pieces. Every task uses paper. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Child-directed adult movement, signing or usual communication aids provide access; record mathematical help separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw panels; use a clean open carton, large paper squares and folded toy wagon. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares and draw matching dots.
- **5 min. Model.** Gesture this carton's length, then height, then lift it for weight. Point inside for how much fits.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P53-length]] [[s03-P53-height]] Move actual cartons before marking the picture.
  **Say.** "Fix the height picture." Expect bottoms on the same line, taller top marked.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 52 feedback; close one fresh partly built four-sided frame.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X53]] Reveal toy and slip. Read exactly; child marks one measurable attribute. Expect an end-to-end length mark, height mark, lifting gesture or inside mark. Record help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated centers, preserving every attribute, repair and exit. Longer: repeat one supplied attribute gesture from the opposite side of the same carton.

### Words and supports

Review length, longer and shorter, day 51; short/long, day 41. Height: bottom to top. Weight: what lifting lets us feel. Holds: what fits inside. Frame: "This shows ___." Read aloud; use broad pencils and tactile edges. Remove practice frames from the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 returns day 53's tickets and practice feedback. Error F, only big, or error L, only color, receives one five-minute fresh carton-attribute correction. Queue the other need. Day 54 explains why starts must meet when comparing lengths and heights.
