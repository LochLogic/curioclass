# Day 54: Compare from the same start

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-54.md through u03-3/practice-records.md. Set rigid paper strips, supplied start lines, standing books or folded cards, paper and broad pencils, large paper squares, flat shape cards and paper shapes. Prepare one repair card, repair-54-F.md or repair-54-L.md. Prepare packet-54.md for each child, pretest straightened lengths, seal separately. Cover exit-54.md; no baseline on that slip. Read child print aloud at 18 point.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count, day 1, and quantity after rearrangement, day 20, with the numeral connection from day 30. **Ask.** [[s03-W54]] Count five squares, show 5, then move the same squares and recount. Nothing entered or left. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty, without a timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 53's tickets and practice feedback. If many gave only big, error F, select [[s03-F54-F]] with repair-54-F.md: mark this new carton's length instead. If only color, error L, select [[s03-F54-L]] with repair-54-L.md instead. One five-minute action; queue the other. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others correct familiar paper. All return one correction. Never reuse the toy wagon.

#### 8 min. Model fair alignment

Review length, longer, shorter and equal sides, day 51; turn, day 47; height, day 53. Explain why starts must meet: otherwise an object can look farther along just because it started farther along. **Ask.** [[s03-P54-baseline]] On the supplied line slide the lower rigid strip back; the upper extends farther. Turn the same strip without stretching, realign and compare again. Then [[s03-P54-equal]] on a different, equal rigid pair with a supplied line; both far ends meet. For height, stand books on the same table, bottoms together. Slide, do not stretch. No numbers measure the difference.

#### 12 min. Connected diagram correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work concurrently, two 45-second turns and thirty seconds handling per two-minute round. **Ask.** [[s03-P54-height]] Children place the books at the same bottom line, then draw the corrected position and mark the taller top. Next turn back to Side 1 and correct its shifted strips with actual rigid pieces on the supplied line. Repeat after turning. Every child moves and draws. Error G: point out unequal starts before discussing far ends. Error M: overlay the same strip before/after turning. Remaining eight minutes allow revision and one shared explanation, passing allowed.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers, day 1. Ten minutes: correct the same rigid-strip and height records using their printed lines. Teacher checks actual starts and the exposed end, not a guessed ring. Ten minutes: guided equal-strip correction on Side 2, then count the same five squares and match dots. No loose ribbons or blank-paper comparison, patterned strips, towers or reserved packets. At most two public volunteers. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Practice stays in the folder, unscored.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect practice pieces. Leave slips covered and clear individual comparison spaces.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-54.md and each child's packet-54.md at their own turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X54]] Read exactly. Response format: move pieces, then trace their positions and the part that extends in the blank box. No start-line hint. First 2.5 minutes: directions/concurrent paper for twenty-four; roster 25 to 30 then receive six 45-second first-reveal observation turns. Collect for one minute. Thirty papers at twenty seconds take ten teacher minutes after class. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record observed actions/help separately. Paper alone does not prove independent alignment. Return day 55; record.md carries fresh follow-up cases through day 65.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels before class. Rigid paper strips replace string in practice; flexible paper replaces exit ribbons. Folded standing cards replace books. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. All tasks use paper; adult movement follows child directions, with access and mathematical help recorded separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Use rigid scrap-card strips, folded standing cards, broad pencil and flexible exit strips. Draw the practice start lines.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count five squares, move them, count again.
- **5 min. Model.** [[s03-P54-baseline]] Slide rigid starts to the printed line, compare ends, turn and realign.
  **Say.** "Fix the length picture." Expect shared starts and exposed upper end marked.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P54-height]] Stand cards on the same bottom line before drawing the correction.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 53 feedback; replace either big or color with one fresh carton-length gesture.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X54]] Reveal sealed flexible strips and blank slip. Read exactly; let the child move and trace independently. Expect aligned starts and wavy-edged strip extending. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center rounds, preserving height, equal-length correction and exit. Longer: reorient the same rigid pair and verify the unchanged comparison using its start line.

### Words and supports

Review length, longer, shorter, equal sides, turn and height. Taller: farther from the common bottom to the top. Frame: "The ends meet here." Read aloud; tactile edges and nonslip paper support movement. Remove supplied lines and frames from the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 returns day 54's tickets and practice feedback. Error G, judging unaligned far ends, needs one five-minute fresh rigid-strip correction; error M, turning said to change length, needs a turn/realign correction instead. Queue the other. Day 55 teaches heavier/lighter by hefting, and introduces how much containers hold before day 56.
