# Day 54: Compare from the same start

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-54.md, one repair card and covered exit slips. Adult-cut thirty packet-54.md ribbon pairs; straighten and verify the wavy-edged piece is longer. Keep packet instructions private; children receive pieces only. Set rigid practice strips, standing books and broad pencils. Prepare covered assessment panels and viewing arrow. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count, day 1, and A quantity stays when its members only move, day 20. **Ask.** [[s03-W54]] Count five squares, move the same squares and recount. Nothing entered or left. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty, without a timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 53's tickets, written task records and practice feedback: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. If many gave only big, error F, select [[s03-F54-F]] with repair-54-F.md: mark a fresh carton's length. For only color, error L, select [[s03-F54-L]] with repair-54-L.md instead. Queue the other. More than ten of thirty: class; otherwise group while others revise familiar marks. Never reuse the wagon.

#### 8 min. Model fair alignment

Say these scenes are invented for practice. Review length, longer, shorter and equal sides, day 51; turn, day 47; height, day 53. An endpoint is where a strip ends. Starts must meet: starting farther along can disguise a shorter length. **Ask.** [[s03-P54-baseline]] Slide the lower rigid strip to the supplied line; the upper extends farther. Turn without stretching, realign and compare. Then [[s03-P54-height]]: stand books on the same table before correcting the picture; taller means farther up from the common bottom. Finally [[s03-P54-equal]]: realign equal rigid strips to their supplied line; both far ends meet. Launch all three corrections now.

#### 12 min. Connected diagram correction
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen concurrent pairs repeat the three launched corrections in page order, twice. Two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling per round fill twelve minutes. Each child moves pieces before marking. For error G use the start line; for error M overlay before/after turning. The teacher uses all twelve minutes for the roster below, with no public share or simultaneous repair.

#### 20 min. Centers

Use assessment-display.md and viewing arrow for F1 cells 127 to 144, child first then above, below, beside, next to, in front of, behind. Indicate one target and read its one master prompt. Let the child mark that target and respond; scribe words without supplying them. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 minutes setup take fifteen minutes. The six component rows are s03-F1-54-1, s03-F1-54-2, s03-F1-54-3, s03-F1-54-4, s03-F1-54-5, s03-F1-54-6. Keep other children on familiar paper; never show this display in practice.

Also collect F2 cells 81 to 100, children 17 to 20, using covered assessment-pairs.md panels in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order. Read one prompt; retain feature marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Rows: s03-F2-54-1 through s03-F2-54-5. The combined roster uses twelve Connected and nineteen Center minutes; the last minute collects papers. No repeat interview or unheard-name inference.

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. Others revise day 53's observation-picture feedback for five independent center minutes, then repeat launched corrections or make five-square dot records on practice-records.md. Preserve originals and supported revisions.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect practice pieces. Leave slips covered and clear individual comparison spaces.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-54.md and each child's loose ribbon pieces at their own turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X54]] Read exactly. Move pieces, then trace starts and exposed end in the blank box; no start-line hint. One minute launch, two concurrent paper minutes for twenty-four, six 45-second turns for children 25 to 30, half a minute collection. Record observed actions/help; paper alone cannot prove independent alignment.

After class, thirty daily-work packets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes; observation feedback takes fifteen more. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work day 55 Return and repair; observation pictures in its first five independent center minutes. record.md schedules fresh follow-ups.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels before class. Rigid paper strips replace string in practice; flexible paper replaces exit ribbons. Folded standing cards replace books. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. All tasks use paper; adult movement follows child directions, with access and mathematical help recorded separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Use rigid scrap-card strips, folded standing cards, broad pencil and flexible exit strips. Draw the practice start lines.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count five squares, move them, count again.
- **5 min. Model.** [[s03-P54-baseline]] Slide rigid starts to the printed line, compare ends, turn and realign.
  **Say.** "Fix the length picture." Expect shared starts and exposed upper end marked.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P54-height]] Stand cards on the same bottom line before drawing the correction.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 53 feedback; replace either big or color with one fresh carton-length gesture.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X54]] Reveal sealed flexible strips and blank slip. Read exactly; let the child move and trace independently. Expect aligned starts and wavy-edged strip extending. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center rounds, preserving height, equal-length correction and exit. Longer: reorient the same rigid pair and verify the unchanged comparison using its start line.

### Words and supports

Review length, longer, shorter, equal sides, turn and height. Taller: farther from the common bottom to the top. Frame: "The ends meet here." Read aloud; tactile edges and nonslip paper support movement. Remove supplied lines and frames from the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 returns day 54's tickets and practice feedback. Error G, judging unaligned far ends, needs one five-minute fresh rigid-strip correction; error M, turning said to change length, needs a turn/realign correction instead. Queue the other. Day 55 teaches heavier/lighter by hefting, and introduces how much containers hold before day 56.
