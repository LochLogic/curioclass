# Heft pair labels

Fifteen duplicate safe sealed envelope pairs. Larger ring lighter, smaller star noticeably heavier. Each partner hefts/swaps hands. Pretest secured safe filling, no food or loose swallowable pieces. Drawings never establish weight.
