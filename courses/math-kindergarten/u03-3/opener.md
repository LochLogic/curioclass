# Attributes and direct comparison

## The ideas

Choose an attribute before comparing. One carton can have length, height, weight and capacity. Length comparison coordinates both ends; turning without stretching preserves length. Common bottom edges make height comparison fair. Heft and swap hands before heavier/lighter judgments; about the same is not exact equality. Capacity is maximum held at rim, not current contents or height. Adult real transfer provides evidence. Teach capacity day 55 before day 56 application.

## Teach and record

Read all print aloud, 18 point on child pages. Paper, movement, drawing, pointing and usual communication methods are valid. Demonstrate earlier-unit Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, Partner turns and Return and repair if needed; do not assume earlier work was taught. Counting stays within ten, optional supported continuation to twenty, no timer.

Fifteen pairs work concurrently. One round is two 45-second turns plus handling; at most two public volunteers, passing allowed. Rin is the sole actor in invented scenes. Say invented for practice. No independent reading or home reports.

Every teaching day has an eight-minute half-slip exit: 2.5 minutes launch/concurrent paper, six 45-second turns, one minute collect. Use the exact single stem in the data and child sheet. Describe paper response format without adding a second question. Everyone marks paper. Hear only the roster six and record heard date; do not classify unheard names/reasons. Follow-up schedule is in record.md, with days 64/65 for the unfinished last rotation.

Return written feedback in the next named repair part. More than ten sharing an idea triggers one five-minute class repair; otherwise group while others correct familiar paper. Queue other letters. Use new practice cases; never replay a reserved exit. Practice stays with child, no final score.

## Common errors

F/L: gesture one measurable attribute. G/M: align ends and turn without stretching. H: heft, do not judge from size. I: actual transfer, not height guess.

## Materials and access

Adult-cut paper shapes replace blocks, wide paper strips and folded joins replace straws/clay, hand drawings replace printing. Scrap paper and broad pencils suffice for drawing. Large intact pieces only, nonslip mat if needed; no loose small joins, mouths, glass, food or body comparisons. Household sealed objects replace heft tubs; pretest safe handling. Real household cups, tray and towel remain necessary for capacity. Adult pours room-temperature water over tray, stops at rim and dries spills/floor. If apparatus missing, capacity pending, not picture-guessed.

Use raised/high-contrast boundaries and child-directed adult movement. Record access separately from mathematical help. No devices needed; at most one shared screen enlarges teacher drawings. A room without a printer draws the same original pieces before class.
