# Day 99: I can find a hidden partner of ten.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare supplied records and ten large squares per pair. Keep key-teacher.md/generated key private and exit-99.md covered. Bring day 98 annotated packets, record and feedback. Read all print, labels/equations aloud at 18 point or larger. Adult cuts pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Use assessment paper as student page; counting, game spaces and supplied correction are included.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, unit 1 day 1, and total, unit 2 day 19. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount99]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount99]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all mark together in the first response box. Numbered boxes hold game marks. A paper mark does not establish an unheard spoken sequence.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s05-P98, s05-G98-1, s05-G98-2, s05-G98-3, s05-G98-4 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 98 annotated exit, practice, counting, repair and game papers. If day 98 tickets show error C, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-FC99]] then [[steps:s05-FC99]]. Second five: error D, [[s05-FD99]] with fresh supplied retry, only if needed. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group in this slot while others correct familiar records. Further needs remain pending.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Day 98 linked parts to equations. Review Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97, and equation/equals, unit 4 day 78. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcover99]], uncover, then [[steps:s05-Kcover99]]. Rehearse day 98 role reversals with the same completed records, all squares visible before correction. No new method. Hear at most three 45-second contributions across rehearsal/connected work.

#### 9 min. Connected rehearsal

**Ask.** [[s05-P99]] using practice-99.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. All ten pieces are supplied. Each child corrects started paper; preserve initial marks. Circulate and collect every paper. If many miss, restore correspondence with loose squares before a fresh supplied retry. No independent evidence format in practice.

#### 20 min. Portfolio and centers

Ten independent minutes: portfolio-99.md, F1 zero/one once, totals two through five twice. Read each exact panel ask separately from the private key. Deliberately indicate empty parts; unexplained blanks stay pending. No coaching or concurrent conferences. Retain initial paper. Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G99-1]], [[s05-G99-2]], [[s05-G99-3]], [[s05-G99-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. Three minutes familiar supplied checks, two tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-99.md only now. Read shared line/labels neutrally, then exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X99]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 13-18 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper, including directed adult marks. No hints or extra asks. Record help/heard names; unheard reasons stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Private key only; a wrong numeral alone proves no letter.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares, drawn frames and folded covers replace counters, cubes and cups. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s05-P99]] Uncover before correction.
  **Say.** "Repair this claim after the frame is uncovered." Expect: three and seven make ten.
- **10 min. Paper.** Read each F1 panel ask separately. Child marks independently; preserve deliberate empty parts. Keep for day 100 feedback.
- **4 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's feedback.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X99]] Read the untouched slip; child draws alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87; equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 100 checks all starts. Error D gets first five Return and repair minutes; older error E gets second five. Retain initial F1 panels; never repeat or coach them. Thirty after-class teacher minutes for daily packets and F1 notes. Return daily papers day 100; formal F1 feedback follows day 100.
