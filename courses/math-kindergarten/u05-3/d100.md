# Day 100: I can check every partner of ten.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare supplied records and ten large squares per pair. Keep teacher-key.md/generated key private and exit-100.md covered. Bring day 99 annotated packets, record and feedback. Read all print, labels/equations aloud at 18 point or larger. Adult cuts pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Use assessment paper as student page; counting/game marks and supplied corrections use retained feedback paper, no extra new worksheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, unit 1 day 1, and total, unit 2 day 19. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount100]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount100]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all mark together. A paper mark does not establish an unheard spoken sequence.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 99 annotated exit, practice, counting, repair and game papers. If day 99 tickets show error D, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-FD100]] then [[steps:s05-FD100]]. Second five: older error E, [[s05-FE100]] with fresh supplied retry, only if needed. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group in this slot while others correct familiar records. Further needs remain pending.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Day 99 found a hidden partner; today rehearse all nine familiar starts. Review partner of ten and Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97. **Ask.** [[s05-Krecords100]] then [[steps:s05-Krecords100]], one completed record at a time. Children repair duplicate labels, then touch-check. No new content. Hear at most three 45-second contributions across rehearsal/connected work.

#### 9 min. Connected rehearsal

Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G100-1]], [[s05-G100-2]], [[s05-G100-3]], [[s05-G100-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. Four minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-P100]] using practice-100.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. All ten pieces are supplied. Each child corrects started paper; preserve initial marks. Circulate and collect every paper. If many miss, restore correspondence with loose squares before a fresh supplied retry. No independent evidence format in practice.

#### 20 min. Portfolio and story paper

Use assessment-100.md as the single two-sided child paper. Twelve F1 minutes: totals six through ten, two panels each; read each exact ask separately, then [[s05-F1-equation]]. Eight F3 minutes: read the invented Rin four-stamp setup from story-record.md, then [[s05-F3-1]] and [[s05-F3-2]] separately. Preserve category labels/all four stamps. No coaching or conferences. Retain earlier F1 panels and new paper.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-100.md only now. Read shared line/labels neutrally, then exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X100]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 19-24 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper, including directed adult marks. No hints or extra asks. Record help/heard names; unheard reasons stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Private key only; a wrong numeral alone proves no letter.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers and marked strips replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares, drawn frames, marked strips and folded covers replace counters, cubes and cups. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **2 min. Rehearse.** [[s05-P100]] Use completed record.
  **Say.** "Repair the partner claim on this completed ten record." Expect: two and eight make ten.
- **12 min. F1.** Read each six-through-ten panel ask separately, then [[s05-F1-equation]]. Child works independently on combined paper.
- **8 min. Story.** Read Rin's four-stamp setup, then [[s05-F3-1]] and [[s05-F3-2]] separately. Preserve originals. Give written feedback afterward, revise day 101.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X100]] Read fresh slip; child marks alone. Split sittings if needed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87; equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 101 relates towers/frames. Error E, numbering first empty place zero, gets five Return and repair minutes. Return all F1/F3 feedback in seven Feedback revision minutes; no judgment yet. Sixty after-class teacher minutes: thirty for F1/F3 written feedback, thirty for daily slips/practice/counting/game/repair packets. Return day 101; revise before judgment.
