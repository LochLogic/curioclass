# Day 98: I can connect parts to equations.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare supplied records and ten large squares per pair. Keep key-teacher.md/generated key private and exit-98.md covered. Bring day 97 annotated packets, record and feedback. Read all print, labels/equations aloud at 18 point or larger. Adult cuts pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, unit 1 day 1, and total, unit 2 day 19. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount98]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount98]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all mark together in the first response box. Numbered boxes hold game marks. A paper mark does not establish an unheard spoken sequence.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 97 annotated exit, practice, counting, repair and game papers. If day 97 tickets show error D, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-FD98]] then [[steps:s05-FD98]]. Second five: older error A, [[s05-FA98]] with fresh supplied retry, only if needed. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group in this slot while others correct familiar records. Further needs remain pending.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Day 97 found places missing; today connect parts to notation. Review equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78, and Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97. **Ask.** [[s05-Kequation98]] then [[steps:s05-Kequation98]]. **Ask.** [[s05-Kspaces98]] then [[steps:s05-Kspaces98]]. Point from each quantity to its marks. Hear at most three 45-second contributions across teaching/connected work; others mark together.

#### 9 min. Connected work

**Ask.** [[s05-Kroles98]] then [[steps:s05-Kroles98]]. **Ask.** [[s05-P98]] using practice-98.md. Review Partner turns, unit 1 day 1: two one-minute turns, handler then checker; exchange roles. All ten pieces are supplied. Each child corrects started paper; preserve initial marks. Circulate and collect every paper. If many miss, restore correspondence with loose squares before a fresh supplied retry. No independent evidence format in practice.

#### 20 min. Centers

Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G98-1]], [[s05-G98-2]], [[s05-G98-3]], [[s05-G98-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. Thirteen minutes check completed records; two tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-98.md only now. Read shared line/labels neutrally, then exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X98]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 7-12 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper, including directed adult marks. No hints or extra asks. Record help/heard names; unheard reasons stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Private key only; a wrong numeral alone proves no letter.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares, drawn frames and folded covers replace counters, cubes and cups. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** [[s05-Kcount98]] Touch six squares together.
- **5 min. Model.** Use the supplied completed record.
  **Say.** "Repair this equation for the completed four-and-six picture." Expect: change second four to six.
- **9 min. Paper.** [[s05-P98]] Child corrects the picture; preserve first marks.
- **5 min. Repair.** Revise yesterday's picture feedback using a supplied retry.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X98]] Read the fresh slip exactly; child marks independently. Keep for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87; equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 99 rehearses hidden partners. Error C, rejecting total-left order, and error D, repeating given part, each get five Return and repair minutes with fresh supplied retries. Thirty after-class teacher minutes: thirty slips and thirty daily packets at 30 seconds each. Return all day 99.
