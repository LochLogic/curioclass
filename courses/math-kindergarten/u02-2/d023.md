# Day 23: Choose and change comparison groups

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-23.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Cover exit-23.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-2/teacher-key.md and d023-key.html private. Choose roster 19 to 24 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review days 21 and 22: more, fewer, same and equal. [[s02-R23]] Everyone joins the ordered count through ten; no new counting words or notation. Say every picture case is invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 22 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O22. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 22's exit and partial-link practice s02-P22-1 with feedback. If many day 22 tickets showed observed error K, finish a missed unique partner in supplied five/eight physical groups. For heard error I, point to the extras and correct the reversed word instead. Choose one five-minute action and collect a fresh correction; others check familiar supplied records. Preserve original evidence and queue other needs.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Nothing new: review the modeled constructions. Read [[s02-M23]], [[s02-N23]], then [[s02-E23]] separately with five reference squares above the supplied group that children change. Partners change existing supplied groups, not empty panels. Use the known eight, three and five samples only after children act. Fifteen pairs take two 45-second turns concurrently each round; three rounds take 4.5 minutes plus handling and feedback.

#### 12 min. Connected work

[[s02-P23-1]] Read the supplied comparison label aloud; the lower five-square group is incorrectly called fewer than the upper five. Children change the lower group, then check partners. Accept any deliberate amount below five; crossing out all is allowed with the familiar finish tick and confirmation of intent. This is a correction, not the unseen stamp task. Each child records one correction; keep practice in the folder.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O23]] from heard-23.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 19 to 24: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First fourteen minutes: partners correct supplied square groups, returning one marked correction each. Final six minutes: comparison-checks.md is the student-page Exit box, [[s02-Gm23]] and [[s02-Ge23]], read separately. Directions 1, independent paper 4, collection 1. No coaching. Return both with the exit on day 24. Mark thirty sets after class in fifteen teacher minutes.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect Side 2 and store squares concurrently in paper trays and screen.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-23.md only now and read [[s02-X23]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 19 to 24, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares and hand-copy the supplied picture and covered checks.

**Skip.** Class rotations; keep independent work.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Join the familiar words.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Use the more, fewer and same requests on supplied groups. Expect eight, three and five as samples against five.
- **7 min. Work.** **Say.** "Fix the comparison picture." Expect: a lower group with fewer than five.  [[s02-P23-1]] Repair the false fewer picture; expect fewer than five below.
- **5 min. Check.** Read [[s02-Gm23]] and [[s02-Ge23]] on Side 2 separately. Expect six to ten marks, then eight. Keep these independent.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X23]] on the unseen exit-23.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 connects matching and counted totals. Return day 23 half slips and Side 2 in Return and repair. If many show heard error I, use supplied five/eight groups to correct one reversed word; observed error K gets one missed-partner correction. Collect a fresh response and preserve originals. A deliberate empty group needs a finish tick and confirmation; a blank unfinished page stays not yet seen.
