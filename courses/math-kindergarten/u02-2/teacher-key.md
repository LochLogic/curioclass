# Matching and comparison: teacher answer key

Teacher only. Keep separate from student copies. These cold-solved keys supply look-fors and two samples. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Diagnose letters from observed actions or heard reasons.

## s02-X21

Prompt: Show with matching which pile has more.

Answer: Three unique links, seven extra whistles; whistle pile indicated.

Look for: Three drums, ten whistles, unique partners. | Shape: Independently proves a comparison of very different picture piles with drawn correspondence. No drums/whistles, 3/10 pair or independent picture-pile proof in practice; finish and repair adult-started physical pairings.

Shown: Three unique links, seven extra whistles; whistle pile indicated. | Not yet seen: Calls drums more because the pictures are larger. (N).

## s02-O21

Prompt: How do your matches show that?

Answer: Each drum has a whistle; whistles are left.

Look for: Rotating six only, 45 seconds each; record names heard. Paper cannot prove unheard reasons.

Shown: Each drum has a whistle; whistles are left. | Not yet seen: Calls drums more because of picture size. (N).

## s02-X22

Prompt: Mark the crayon that cannot have a partner.

Answer: One of the seven crayons marked as extra.

Look for: Six unique pairings possible; any of seven may be unmatched. Links optional. | Shape: Locates the unmatched member in close crayon groups. No crayons, 6/7 pair or independent single-extra worksheet in teaching; complete adult pairings with five/eight tiles, then equal fives.

Shown: One of the seven crayons marked as extra. | Not yet seen: Skips a crayon during observed matching. (K).

## s02-O22

Prompt: Which group has fewer?

Answer: The six-crayon group.

Look for: Rotating six only, 45 seconds each; record names heard. Paper cannot prove unheard reasons.

Shown: The six-crayon group. | Not yet seen: Calls seven fewer because of its extra. (I).

## s02-X23

Prompt: Make a paper group with fewer pieces than these stamps.

Look for: Six stamps. Empty needs finish mark and confirmation of intent. | Shape: Produces one smaller comparison group for six stamps without supplied pieces or answers. No stamps, six-piece reference or independent fewer-only blank panel in practice; choose and repair existing groups against more/fewer/same requests.

Shown: Any deliberate zero to five; five dots is one sample. | Not yet seen: Makes eight and explicitly calls it fewer. (I).

## s02-O23

Shown: Some stamps have no partner. | Not yet seen: Calls the group with extras fewer. (I).

## s02-X24

Look for: Six pots and nine brushes; hear a quantity reason separately. | Shape: Creates an independent comparison explanation for unseen groups. No paint pots/brushes, 6/9 pair or independent claim-proof sheet in teaching; critique supplied reasons and correct an incomplete five/eight diagram instead.

Shown: Nine brushes indicated with six matches and three extras, or complete count marks. | Not yet seen: Calls brushes fewer despite their extras. (I).
