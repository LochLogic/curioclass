# Day 21: Matching tells more and fewer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-21.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Cover exit-21.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-2/teacher-key.md and d021-key.html private. Choose roster 13 to 18 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 20: moving the same members preserves the amount. [[s02-R21]] Count together through ten, with no numeral test. Say the paper cases are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 20's bear exit and practice papers s02-P20-1 and s02-P20-2 with feedback. If many day 20 tickets showed error K, restore a missing link in [[s02-F21]]. If heard error B blocked quantity, show the same eight moving compactly and check no member entered or left. If heard error A replaced a total with the sequence, enclose eight and name eight altogether instead. Choose one repair; all make a fresh linked correction. For a small need, meet that group while others repair familiar records. No reserved bear art is modeled.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Review match from day 8 and group from day 6. Show three large paper block pieces and five small ones. [[s02-K21]] Start one pair and finish the rest with the class. **Ask.** [[s02-C21]] Then [[s02-D21]], separately. Expect five has more and three has fewer. Teach extra: left after every piece in the other group has one partner. Correct the puppet's size claim by pointing to unique partners, error N. Never compare children's bodies.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Model [[s02-M21]], then [[s02-N21]], with five large reference squares beside an existing group of five small squares. Children change supplied groups only after seeing each process. Every pair takes two 45-second turns concurrently, leaving time for checking. Then finish adult-started five/eight physical matches. Use [[s02-P21-1]] on the supplied partial-link paper. Five unique pairs leave three in the eight-piece group. Ask only one stem at a time. Mark a familiar finish tick.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds: finish adult-started physical partners, then choose and repair an existing group against the spoken more/fewer request. Allow two minutes to change tasks and two to collect each child's corrected partial-link paper. Partners exchange handler/checker roles. If a square is missed, move each into its own partner position; if comparison words reverse, point to extras while saying more. Keep initial and repaired records together. No independent picture-pile proof is practiced. Paper records return on day 22.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return paper squares to trays, keep corrected records, and screen off exit pictures.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One minute: reveal each child's own half slip and read [[s02-X21]] exactly. Two minutes: everyone works independently on paper. Hear only roster 13 to 18, [[s02-O21]], six times 45 seconds, including notes: 4.5 minutes. Others finish quietly. Collect in half a minute. Mark in five teacher minutes after class, thirty papers at ten seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and heard names separately. Paper cannot establish unheard words. Return exit and practice with feedback on day 22; retain originals beside corrections.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large and small scrap squares for blocks. Draw the supplied partial links and copy the covered exit picture exactly.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Count together.
- **5 min. Model.** Start partners for three large and five small pieces. **Say.** "Which group has more pieces?" Expect five small pieces; two lack partners.
- **7 min. Work.** [[s02-P21-1]] Finish the supplied five/eight links; expect five pairs and three extras. Trade roles.
- **5 min. Change.** Keep five large reference squares beside five small squares. **Say.** "Change the small-square group to have more than the reference." Expect six to ten. Repeat with fewer; expect none to four.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s02-X21]]. Expect three unique links and the whistles indicated. **Say.** "How do your matches show that?" Expect every drum has one and whistles remain. Keep the work for feedback and put pieces away.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 22 matches closer groups and teaches equal. Return day 21 papers. If many show an observed missed partner, error K, repair the adult-started five/eight case; if size drove the heard comparison, error N, pair unequal-sized squares. Reverse more/fewer is error I only when heard. Use one five-minute action, collect a correction and queue other needs.
