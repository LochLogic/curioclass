# Day 21: Matching tells more and fewer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep u02-2/teacher-key.md and d021-key.html private. Prepare record.md and the student page; read every printed word aloud at 18 point. Choose roster 7 to 12. Cover each heard check and exit until its own turn. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Fingers are optional. Use the student-page triangle adaptation; do not print the original square practice master.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 20: moving the same members preserves the amount. [[s02-R21]] Count together through ten, with no numeral test. Say the paper cases are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return s02-L20 and s02-O20 with the whole prior-day paper packet and observation notes: one minute return, one model, two concurrent fresh corrections, one collection. Use one priority action; retain originals and revisions.

Return day 20's bear exit and practice papers s02-P20-1 and s02-P20-2 with feedback. If many day 20 tickets showed error K, restore a missing link in [[s02-F21]]. If heard error B blocked quantity, show the same eight moving compactly and check no member entered or left. If heard error A replaced a total with the sequence, enclose eight and name eight altogether instead. Choose one repair; all make a fresh linked correction. For a small need, meet that group while others repair familiar records. No reserved bear art is modeled.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Review match from day 8 and group from day 6. Show three large paper block pieces and five small ones. [[s02-K21]] Start one pair and finish the rest with the class. **Ask.** [[s02-C21]] Then [[s02-D21]], separately. Expect five has more and three has fewer. Teach more: the group with extras after complete matching. Fewer: the other group runs out of partners. Have partners use both words. Teach extra: left after every piece in the other group has one partner. Correct the puppet's size claim by pointing to unique partners, error N. Never compare children's bodies.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Model [[s02-M21]], then [[s02-N21]], with five large reference triangles beside an existing group of five small triangles. Children change supplied groups only after seeing each process. Every pair takes two 45-second turns concurrently, leaving time for checking. Then finish adult-started five/eight triangle matches. Use [[s02-P21-1]] on the supplied partial-link paper. Five unique pairs leave three in the eight-piece group. Ask only one stem at a time. Mark a familiar finish tick.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O21]] from heard-21.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 7 to 12: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds: finish adult-started physical partners, then choose and repair an existing group against the spoken more/fewer request. Allow two minutes to change tasks and two to collect each child's corrected partial-link paper. Partners exchange handler/checker roles. If a triangle is missed, move each into its own partner position; if comparison words reverse, point to extras while saying more. Keep initial and repaired records together. No independent picture-pile proof is practiced. Paper records return on day 22.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return paper squares to trays, keep corrected records, and screen off exit pictures.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-21.md only now and read [[s02-X21]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children begin paper for two minutes. The six named children wait with cards covered until their own observed turns. Observe the sole requested action for roster 7 to 12, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return the exit and heard-check paper on day 22 in Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large and small scrap triangles for blocks. Draw the supplied partial links and copy the covered exit picture exactly.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Count together.
- **5 min. Model.** Start partners for three large and five small pieces. **Say.** "Which group has more pieces?" Expect five small pieces; two lack partners.
- **7 min. Work.** [[s02-P21-1]] Finish the supplied five/eight links; expect five pairs and three extras. Trade roles.
- **5 min. Change.** Keep five large reference triangles beside five small triangles. **Say.** "Change the small-triangle group to have more than the reference." Expect six to ten. Repeat with fewer; expect none to four.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X21]] on the unseen exit-21.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12; Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8. Review total and Finish tick from day 19. Use the new words with pointing; partners may say "extra" while indicating a leftover. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 22 matches closer groups and teaches equal. Return day 21 papers. If many show an observed missed partner, error K, repair the adult-started five/eight case; if size drove the heard comparison, error N, pair unequal-sized squares. Reverse more/fewer is error I only when heard. Use one five-minute action, collect a correction and queue other needs.
