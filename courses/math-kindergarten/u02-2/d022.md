# Day 22: Find extras and make equal groups

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-21.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Cover exit-22.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-2/teacher-key.md and d022-key.html private. Choose roster 19 to 24 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 21's complete pairs and extras. [[s02-R22]] Count to ten together, stopping with the teacher. Review total from day 19: the last word names the complete group. All cases are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 21 papers s02-X21, s02-P21-1 and s02-F21, with feedback and spoken notes. If many day 21 tickets showed error N, pair three large squares with five small to defeat the size claim. If observed error K blocked pairing, use [[s02-K22]] with a different adult-started arrangement. Correct heard reversed words through [[s02-F22]] if that is the earliest need instead. Choose one action; all return one fresh corrected link record. Queue others.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Complete [[s02-K22]]: five/eight physical pairings, three extras. Slide extras aside only after every reference square has a unique partner. Then [[s02-E22]], equal fives. **Ask.** Read that single stem before demonstrating. [[steps:s02-E22]] Teach equal as same number, reviewing same from day 20. Model [[s02-M22]], removing three from supplied eight to make five like the reference. Check partners rather than size or row length.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs finish supplied five/eight links on today's copy of practice-21.md, [[s02-P22-1]]. Then each takes a 45-second physical turn on [[s02-M22]], with a partner checking. Fifteen concurrent pairs need 1.5 minutes per round, leaving handling and feedback time. Review [[s02-N22]] and demonstrate the deliberate empty possibility; the finish tick means completed, never correct. No numeral is shown. Children can point and direct movement.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Offer taught supplied-pair tasks: finish five/eight or equal-five physical matches, then change an existing eight group to match five. Use two eight-minute rounds, two minutes for changeover and two for collection. Children make one corrected partial-link paper each. Keep every piece visible; an extra is evidence only after complete unique pairing. If comparison words reverse, point to the group with leftovers and restate more, then its partner fewer. Collect work for day 23. No independent single-extra worksheet, crayon pictures or six/seven pair is used.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return squares concurrently to paper trays and screen; keep practice records separate from covered exits.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One minute: reveal each child's own half slip and read [[s02-X22]] exactly. Two minutes: everyone works independently on paper. Hear only roster 19 to 24, [[s02-O22]], six times 45 seconds, including notes: 4.5 minutes. Others finish quietly. Collect in half a minute. Mark in five teacher minutes after class, thirty papers at ten seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and heard names separately. Paper cannot establish unheard words. Return exit and practice with feedback on day 23; retain originals beside corrections.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Scrap squares replace tiles. Copy practice-21's partial links and the exact covered crayon exit picture.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Join ordered words to ten.
- **5 min. Model.** Finish five/eight pairs, then equal fives. **Say.** "Finish these equal partners." Expect five pairs, no extras.
- **7 min. Work.** Change this supplied group to have the same number as five. Remove three from supplied eight to match five. Check one partner per piece.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P22-1]] Finish the supplied partial paper, then add the familiar finish tick.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover [[s02-X22]]. Accept any one of the seven marked after unique matching. **Say.** "Which group has fewer?" Expect the six-crayon group. Keep the work for feedback and put pieces away.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Allow five teacher minutes after class to mark thirty practice papers at ten seconds each. Day 23 applies making more, fewer and the same, already modeled on days 21 and 22. Return day 22 paper and practice. If many show observed error K, repair a missed physical partner; if heard error I reverses fewer, point to extras and correct that one word. Collect one fresh five/eight correction in Return and repair, preserving first evidence.
