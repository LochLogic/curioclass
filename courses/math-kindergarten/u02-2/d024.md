# Day 24: Two ways to check a comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep u02-2/teacher-key.md and d024-key.html private. Prepare record.md and the student page; read every printed word aloud at 18 point. Choose roster 25 to 30. Cover each heard check and exit until its own turn. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Fingers are optional. Use the student-page triangle adaptation; do not print the original square practice master.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count and retrieval

Review day 23's changes to supplied groups and comparison words. [[s02-R24]] Count together through ten. Say all cases are invented for practice. Review complete one-to-one pairs before treating leftovers as extras.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return s02-P23-1 and s02-O23 with the whole prior-day paper packet and observation notes: one minute return, one model, two concurrent fresh corrections, one collection. Use one priority action; retain originals and revisions.

Return day 23 exit tickets s02-X23, Side 2 checks s02-Gm23 and s02-Ge23, with feedback. If many day 23 papers or heard reasons showed error I, use [[s02-F24]] to correct one reversed comparison. For observed error K, complete one missing partner instead. Choose the earliest blocking action and collect a fresh five/eight correction; queue other needs. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Intentional none requires the earlier finish tick and confirmation, not a guessed interpretation.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

[[s02-K24]] Complete the supplied five/eight matches. **Ask.** [[s02-C24]] Then [[s02-D24]], separately, with five triangles above eight. [[steps:s02-C24]] [[steps:s02-D24]] Review total: five altogether and eight altogether. Connect two methods: complete pairs reveal extras; counting names each whole amount, and matching the count-dot groups confirms which amount has more. No written numerals. [[s02-E24]] Read the supplied reasons before showing the correct one. Bigger pieces do not establish more, error N.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs correct an adult incomplete five/eight diagram, [[s02-P224]], and select the supplied explanation from [[s02-E24]]. Each partner has one 45-second turn per ask; concurrent pairs need three minutes for both asks plus handling. Then inspect [[s02-P24-1]] on the equal-five master: the wider row is incorrectly called more. Children correct its reason using complete partners; both contain five. Dot counts name the same total. Read all claims aloud; children need no reading or numeral notation.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O24]] from heard-24.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 25 to 30: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute critique-and-repair rounds repeat these supplied tasks, with two minutes for changeover and two for collection. Children hold Side 2, finish the partial five/eight links and add one adjacent count dot per picture while touching it. Each returns the corrected record plus the equal-five reason correction. If many skip a triangle, pair slowly with one touch at each end; if spacing drives a claim, check members and matches. No independent unseen claim-proof sheet or paint picture is practiced. Return all paper on day 25. Mark both practice sides in ten teacher minutes after class, thirty children times twenty seconds.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return large squares concurrently to paper trays and screen, leaving exit papers covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-24.md only now and read [[s02-X24]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children begin paper for two minutes. The six named children wait with cards covered until their own observed turns. Observe the sole requested action for roster 25 to 30, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return the exit and heard-check paper on day 25 in Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap triangles; copy both supplied practice diagrams and the covered exit picture.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Join familiar ordered words.
- **5 min. Model.** Finish adult-started five/eight partners. **Say.** "Count this group." Point to each group separately; expect five and eight altogether.
- **7 min. Repair.** [[s02-P224]] Finish links and one count dot beside each picture. Expect five pairs and three extras.
- **5 min. Critique.** [[s02-P24-1]] Read the false wider-row reason. Expect same number, five each, despite spread.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X24]] on the unseen exit-24.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary from day 12; Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8. Review total and Finish tick from day 19. Review same from day 20; more, fewer and extra from day 21. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 counts images in lines, arrays, circles and scattered groups. Return day 24 paper in Return and repair. If many show observed error K, repair a missing five/eight square link; if heard error I reverses words, identify extras and correct one word. Heard error B calls the wider window group more because of spread: keep members visible and pair them. Choose one five-minute action, collect a correction, and carry unheard reasoning as not yet seen.
