# Day 24: Two ways to check a comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the pictured paper from practice-24.md, indexed in practice-records.md. Cover exit-24.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-2/teacher-key.md and d024-key.html private. Choose roster 1 to 6 for follow-ups. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers are optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 23's changes to supplied groups and comparison words. [[s02-R24]] Count together through ten. Say all cases are invented for practice. Review complete one-to-one pairs before treating leftovers as extras.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 23 exit tickets s02-X23, Side 2 checks s02-Gm23 and s02-Ge23, with feedback. If many day 23 papers or heard reasons showed error I, use [[s02-F24]] to correct one reversed comparison. For observed error K, complete one missing partner instead. Choose the earliest blocking action and collect a fresh five/eight correction; queue other needs. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Intentional none requires the earlier finish tick and confirmation, not a guessed interpretation.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

[[s02-K24]] Complete the supplied five/eight matches. **Ask.** [[s02-C24]] Then [[s02-D24]], separately, with five squares above eight. [[steps:s02-C24]] [[steps:s02-D24]] Review total: five altogether and eight altogether. Connect two methods: complete pairs reveal extras; counting names each whole amount, and matching the count-dot groups confirms which amount has more. No written numerals. [[s02-E24]] Read the supplied reasons before showing the correct one. Bigger pieces do not establish more, error N.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs correct an adult incomplete five/eight diagram, [[s02-P224]], and select the supplied explanation from [[s02-E24]]. Each partner has one 45-second turn per ask; concurrent pairs need three minutes for both asks plus handling. Then inspect [[s02-P24-1]] on the equal-five master: the wider row is incorrectly called more. Children correct its reason using complete partners; both contain five. Dot counts name the same total. Read all claims aloud; children need no reading or numeral notation.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute critique-and-repair rounds repeat these supplied tasks, with two minutes for changeover and two for collection. Children hold Side 2, finish the partial five/eight links and add one adjacent count dot per picture while touching it. Each returns the corrected record plus the equal-five reason correction. If many skip a square, pair slowly with one touch at each end; if spacing drives a claim, check members and matches. No independent unseen claim-proof sheet or paint picture is practiced. Return all paper on day 25. Mark both practice sides in ten teacher minutes after class, thirty children times twenty seconds.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return large squares concurrently to paper trays and screen, leaving exit papers covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

One minute: reveal each child's own half slip and read [[s02-X24]] exactly. Two minutes: everyone works independently on paper. Hear only roster 1 to 6, [[s02-O24]], six times 45 seconds, including notes: 4.5 minutes. Others finish quietly. Collect in half a minute. Mark in 7.5 teacher minutes after class, thirty papers at fifteen seconds each. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and heard names separately. Paper cannot establish unheard words. Return exit and practice with feedback on day 25; retain originals beside corrections.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit stem without a printer. Large paper squares and plain mats supply the full path. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares; copy both supplied practice diagrams and the covered exit picture.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Count with me. Join familiar ordered words.
- **5 min. Model.** Finish adult-started five/eight partners. **Say.** "Count this group." Point to each group separately; expect five and eight altogether.
- **7 min. Repair.** [[s02-P224]] Finish links and one count dot beside each picture. Expect five pairs and three extras.
- **5 min. Critique.** [[s02-P24-1]] Read the false wider-row reason. Expect same number, five each, despite spread.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X24]]. Accept matching or complete count marks indicating brushes. **Say.** "What shows that in your paper?" Expect every pot has a partner, with brushes remaining, or correct counted totals. Keep the work for feedback and put pieces away.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; retain model, repair and independent exit. Longer: repeat supplied work with pieces turned, preserving quantity and task.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair from day 2; group from day 6; match from day 8; boundary from day 12. Use quantity words with pointing. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. An adult may follow directed marks; note help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral reading or writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 counts images in lines, arrays, circles and scattered groups. Return day 24 paper in Return and repair. If many show observed error K, repair a missing five/eight square link; if heard error I reverses words, identify extras and correct one word. Heard error B calls the wider pots more because of spread: keep members visible and pair them. Choose one five-minute action, collect a correction, and carry unheard reasoning as not yet seen.
