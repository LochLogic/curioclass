# Day 63: I can look back at my shape work.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Set record.md, practice-records.md, practice-63.md and F1 to F6 dated originals/feedback/revisions, days 58 to 62. Prepare unfinished original components' apparatus, sealed tubs/cups, water/tray/towel if needed. Mark roster/time limits. Read paper aloud; no new secure task/exit. Adult pours/dries spills/floor.

### The day

#### 5 min. Familiar oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W63]] Touch or move each of 5 squares once. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: mark one dot per square on scrap paper.

#### 30 min. Familiar independent centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs revisit known paper-piece puzzle: [[s03-P63-familiar]]. Familiar three-triangle partial puzzle only, no new directions/teaching. Two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds setup; remaining time repeats corrections/count-dot records. Practice stays unscored in folder. Teacher checks retained products/dates/missing flags, no repeated secure interview/reteaching.

#### 15 min. Revision and missing evidence

First five minutes return day 62 conference feedback for revision; preserve originals/helped versions. Next ten only missing observations: at most two five-minute partial conferences or twelve 45-second asks plus one minute transition. Use unfinished original component prompts separately, name item/date on record.md. No inferred unheard answer. Stop at time; excess components pending. At the end of this part, collect revised pages and any first-made records, keeping them separate from earlier originals. First-made work gets written feedback and later revision before judgment before day 64, never same-period scoring.

#### 10 min. Close records

Review Close retained observation records, day 18. Teacher alone reviews [[s03-A63-F1]], [[s03-A63-F2]], [[s03-A63-F3]], [[s03-A63-F4]], [[s03-A63-F5]], [[s03-A63-F6]], earlier papers/conference notes, respectively 2/1/2/1/1/3 minutes. Children do not answer record-review rows. Mark each component shown, shown with help, not yet seen; pending is scheduling. Names/positions require heard evidence; direct comparisons require observed method, not picture guess. Children continue familiar work; table-tray tidy last two minutes. Fifteen teacher minutes after class finish annotations/feedback, not first-made judgments. Return closed records day 64 in Centers.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels/exit before class. Paper shapes/strips replace blocks/straws; folded paper replaces clay. No device needed; one shared screen can enlarge teaching. Tactile boundaries/usual communication aids, adult follows child directions; record access separately from math help. Capacity requires real cups, water/tray/towel; otherwise pending. No glass, food, tasting, small joins or body comparisons.

### At home

**Swap.** Dated papers/earlier notes suffice; familiar paper pieces replace blocks. Real apparatus only for unfinished comparisons.

**Skip.** Class rotations/secure components.

- **5 min. Familiar work.** [[s03-P63-familiar]] Known partial puzzle, no new teaching.
  **Say.** "Fix your familiar puzzle." Expect: full-edge joins.
- **15 min. Revision and missing evidence.** First return day 62 feedback for five minutes of revision. Use remaining ten for unfinished original prompts separately. Stop; other gaps pending. Preserve original/helped versions.
- **10 min. Close records.** Grown-up compares earlier papers, feedback and notes with private key. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. New work waits for feedback/later revision before judgment. Return records day 64; no new exit.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer familiar center repetitions; preserve revision, missing-evidence time and record review. Longer: continue the same familiar puzzle, with no new teaching or judgments.

### Words and supports

Review shape names/positions, day 45; side/corner, day 48; join, day 51; turn, day 47; length, day 51; height/weight/capacity, day 53, where used. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Public talk may be passed; no child models error. Frame: "This edge meets ___." Remove frames for checks.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 begins joining two collections; ten Centers minutes hold unit 3 response, five priority repair/five fresh transfer. Return day 62 tickets/closed F1 to F6 records there. Choose observed attribute/endpoint/shape error; others queue to day 65. First-made day 63 work needs feedback/later revision before judgment; missing evidence pending.
