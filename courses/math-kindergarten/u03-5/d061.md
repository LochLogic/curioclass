# Day 61: I can make the same whole with different pieces.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare the current practice panel, one priority repair and covered exit. Set the retained paper records needed today. Pretest unequal-size sealed tubs to feel about the same after swapping hands; pretest both directions of a child-chosen transfer between the reserved vessels. Set room-temperature water, tray and towel. Adult cuts and pours; stop at the rim and dry spills. Read child print aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Use 3.5 minutes for the familiar counting routine, [[s03-W61]], and concurrent square-dot records. In the last 1.5 minutes hear children 7 and 8 on the covered fresh partial-hexagon practice case, [[s03-P61-outside]], one 45-second turn each. This is practice talk only. Other children continue their known counting paper; no exit square preview.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 60's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 60's tickets and practice feedback. Return day 60's written tasks s03-P60-mat and s03-P60-fills with recorded feedback; correction uses this same priority slot. Return day 60 conference feedback. For overlap, error K, [[s03-F61-K]] with repair-61-K.md. For an unmatched day 60 hexagon, error x, trace and turn a different six-sided practice boundary, then repair one missing side mark on a fresh outline. For observed B/N in practice instead [[s03-F61-corners]]: overlay fresh square/slanted-tile corners, rotate square to show unchanged corners. No exit choices. One action only; queue others. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise small group while others revise familiar paper. Keep original/helped versions separate.

#### 9 min. F4 and connected corrections

Six independent minutes: [[s03-F4-fill]], then separately [[s03-F4-seam]]. Keep the original nonsquare rectangle fill and seam before feedback. In the remaining three minutes, pairs correct one supplied partial hexagon with [[s03-P61-hexagon]], then count its pieces with [[s03-P61-count]]. The teacher hears children 9 to 12 on the fresh practice boundary, [[s03-P61-outside]], four 45-second turns. These are practice descriptions, not exit evidence; never show the reserved square packet.

#### 31 min. Final conferences and centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Hear children 19 to 24 only. F1/F2 naming and position records are already complete; do not ask them again. Read separately, waiting for each answer: [[s03-F5-talk-61]], [[s03-F5-talk-61-2]], [[s03-F5-talk-61-3]], [[s03-F6-weight-61]], [[s03-F6-capacity-61]].

Three attribute asks take 45 seconds each, heft 45 seconds, capacity 75 seconds, reset/record 15 seconds: 4.5 minutes per child, 27 for six. For capacity let the child choose the source and direct the adult. Narrow into wide leaves space; wide into narrow leaves water in the source. Stop at the destination rim. Child marks the real result on the retained paper; pictures alone cannot establish weight or capacity.

Other children revise yesterday's written feedback for five independent minutes, then use familiar supplied-picture corrections. No simultaneous teacher-led repair. Keep originals and supported revisions separate; write feedback in fifteen teacher minutes after class. Remaining block time is setup/collection, never extra rushed interviews.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice concurrently in table trays. Keep reserved papers separate; adult empties/dries water area and checks floor.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal packet-61.md and the equal square outlines at identical print scale. **Ask.** [[s03-X61]] One minute launch, six independent minutes fitting and tracing both different piece sets, one minute collection. No added spoken ask or six-child interview during this exit. Earlier partial-hexagon talk is separate practice and cannot establish an exit explanation. Record paper evidence and help using shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Give written feedback after class and return day 62.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels/exit before class. Paper shapes/strips replace blocks/straws; folded paper replaces clay. No device needed; one shared screen can enlarge teaching. Tactile boundaries/usual communication aids, adult follows child directions; record access separately from math help. Capacity requires real cups, water/tray/towel; otherwise pending. No glass, food, tasting, small joins or body comparisons.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw panels; paper pieces replace blocks. Real sealed tubs/cups, water/tray/towel; adult pours.

**Skip.** Class rotations/repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight squares, mark dots.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 60 feedback; fix one familiar partial join.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P61-hexagon]]
  **Say.** "Fix this partial hexagon design." Expect: six triangles complete hexagon.
- **5 min. Records.** Keep class fill/seam paper. If absent, use same outline/pieces independently once, then keep original.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X61]] Reveal reserved square outlines/packets, identical scale. Read exactly; child fills/traces alone. Expect: same square, four small squares or two triangles. Record help, return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; preserve independent work, feedback and exit. Longer: repeat same partial correction, check joins. Never practice reserved cases.

### Words and supports

Review shape names/positions, day 45; side/corner, day 48; join, day 51; turn, day 47; length, day 51; height/weight/capacity, day 53, where used. Review compose, gap, overlap, outline, same whole and different pieces, day 60. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Public talk may be passed; no child models error. Frame: "This edge meets ___." Remove frames for checks.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 returns day 61 tickets in Return and repair: E, gap, or K, overlap, gets one partial-puzzle correction. Feedback and revision holds F4/conference feedback for separate five minutes; preserve original/revision. Then own art, no partner-picture analysis before exit.
