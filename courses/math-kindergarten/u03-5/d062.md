# Day 62: I can make and describe a shape picture.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare the current practice panel, one priority repair and covered exit. Set the retained paper records needed today. Pretest unequal-size sealed tubs to feel about the same after swapping hands; pretest both directions of a child-chosen transfer between the reserved vessels. Set room-temperature water, tray and towel. Adult cuts and pours; stop at the rim and dry spills. Read child print aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W62]] Touch or move each of 6 squares once. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: mark one dot per square on scrap paper.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 61's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 61's tickets and practice feedback. Return day 61's written tasks s03-P61-hexagon and s03-P61-count with recorded feedback; correction uses this same priority slot. If many left gaps on day 61, error E, [[s03-F62-E]] with repair-62-E.md. For priority overlap, error K, instead [[s03-F62-K]] on fresh puzzle with repair-61-K.md. This is not F4 revision time. One action only; queue others. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise small group while others revise familiar paper. Keep original/helped versions separate.

#### 5 min. Feedback and revision

Return day 61 F4/conference records with written feedback. Revise flagged components five minutes; retain original, feedback, helped revision separately. No new secure interview.

#### 7 min. Own art launch

Yesterday's different-piece work becomes own design. Review compose/gap/overlap, day 60. **Ask.** [[s03-P62-art]] Familiar loose shapes, no supplied outline; full sides meet at claimed joins. Fifteen concurrent pairs: [[s03-P62-talk]], describe own pieces, two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling, two minutes within seven. **Ask.** [[s03-P62-boundary]] Draw own outside, no finished partner picture analyzed. Center directions now: continue own art/familiar corrections. Art stays unscored in folder; replacement evidence needs feedback/later revision.

#### 28 min. Final conferences and centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Hear children 25 to 30 only. F1/F2 naming and position records are already complete; do not ask them again. Read separately, waiting for each answer: [[s03-F5-talk-62]], [[s03-F5-talk-62-2]], [[s03-F5-talk-62-3]], [[s03-F6-weight-62]], [[s03-F6-capacity-62]].

Three attribute asks take 45 seconds each, heft 45 seconds, capacity 75 seconds, reset/record 15 seconds: 4.5 minutes per child, 27 for six. For capacity let the child choose the source and direct the adult. Narrow into wide leaves space; wide into narrow leaves water in the source. Stop at the destination rim. Child marks the real result on the retained paper; pictures alone cannot establish weight or capacity.

Other children revise yesterday's written feedback for five independent minutes, then use familiar supplied-picture corrections. No simultaneous teacher-led repair. Keep originals and supported revisions separate; write feedback in fifteen teacher minutes after class. Remaining block time is setup/collection, never extra rushed interviews.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice concurrently in table trays. Keep reserved papers separate; adult empties/dries water area and checks floor.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-62.md. Rin, an invented paper partner, made picture. Mark actual component boundaries; hear six names without supplied clues. **Ask.** [[s03-X62]] Exact stem aloud; response format adds no ask. Launch/concurrent paper 2.5 minutes, roster 13 to 18 six 45-second turns 4.5 minutes, collection one minute. Private teacher-key.md. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard date/help, unheard components pending. Thirty slips at twenty seconds: ten teacher minutes after class; five more for conferences. Return day 64 in Centers. No credit for unheard speech.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels/exit before class. Paper shapes/strips replace blocks/straws; folded paper replaces clay. No device needed; one shared screen can enlarge teaching. Tactile boundaries/usual communication aids, adult follows child directions; record access separately from math help. Capacity requires real cups, water/tray/towel; otherwise pending. No glass, food, tasting, small joins or body comparisons.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw panels; paper shapes replace blocks, pencil replaces crayons/glue. Real sealed tubs/cups, water/tray/towel; adult pours.

**Skip.** Class rotations/repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares, mark dots.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 61 exit feedback; fix one partial join.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P62-art]]
  **Say.** "Make your own shape picture." Expect: own full-edge composition. Describe own pieces, draw own outside.
- **5 min. Records.** Return class F4/conference feedback. Keep original and helped revision separately.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X62]] Reveal reserved Rin picture. Adult is partner. Read exactly, child marks components alone. Expect: rectangle, turned square, triangle. Record help; return ticket day 64.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; preserve independent work, feedback and exit. Longer: repeat same partial correction, check joins. Never practice reserved cases.

### Words and supports

Review shape names/positions, day 45; side/corner, day 48; join, day 51; turn, day 47; length, day 51; height/weight/capacity, day 53, where used. Review compose, gap, overlap, outline, same whole and different pieces, day 60. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Public talk may be passed; no child models error. Frame: "This edge meets ___." Remove frames for checks.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 returns conference feedback for five-minute revision and closes eligible earlier records without reteaching. Return today's exit on day 64 in Centers. Days 64 and 65 each reserve one five-minute priority repair and five-minute fresh transfer, using the recorded component and letter. Unheard language remains not yet seen; keep further needs on the bounded missing-component roster.
