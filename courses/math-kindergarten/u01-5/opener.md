# Represent and close the counting record

## Before class

Print practice-16.md through practice-17.md and each corresponding exit master at 18 point, one per child. Read the teacher-only key-s01.md in u01-1; keep it away from children and displays. Prepare the listening roster in u01-1/record.md and a folder for dated papers. Copy drawings by hand if printing is unavailable. Use the covered unit assessment in u01-1/assessment.md, with u01-4/kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md, only for assigned children. Prepare independent familiar centers for the others.

## The mathematics

Days 16 and 17 apply earlier counting. Use Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8, naming represent as a mark standing for an object. On day 17 build a low, wide five- or ten-piece sculpture before counting its completed visible parts. Paper strips provide a flat stand-in; do not model a counted vertical stack. Day 18 is familiar work and closing records, with bounded missing observations, no new teaching or repair. Children already heard are not interviewed again. Keep new day 18 observations descriptive until their before-class classification on day 19.

## Errors and the first repair

A leaves an object without a word or mark; reach each fresh practice square once. B revisits it; cover after its word. C needs ordered words. D needs one word per touch. Return day 16 papers on day 17; day 17 papers share day 19's single ten-minute response reservation with the unit records.

## Routines, evidence and materials

Read every word aloud; children do not read independently. A pencil or broad crayon makes marks; an adult may mark exactly as a child directs. Repeating a direction is allowed; demonstrating a reserved answer is not. Use large, spaced drawings and usual communication supports, record help and allow a pass in public talk. Paper works in every task; no device or outside photograph is needed.

Use Partner turns and Counting centers with the original cards-centers.md cue. Fifteen pairs work at once; two 45-second turns take 1.5 minutes, with handling and checking in the remaining part. Hear at most two public volunteers, 90 seconds total. The eight-minute exit has directions 1, simultaneous paper work 2, six observed turns 4.5 and collection 0.5 minutes. The six finish their own papers during their turns, without repeating a completed count. A selection or drawing does not add another spoken ask.

Collect every paper. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for brief private feedback. The next Return and repair part contains distribution/direction 1, one selected wrong/right correction 1, simultaneous fresh-case retry 2 and collection 1 minute. More than ten sharing an observed action triggers a whole-class correction; otherwise meet one group while others retry familiar work. Queue other errors. Day 17 papers wait for day 19 Counting centers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; a paper mark cannot prove an unheard sequence.

An invented mistake is acted by one folded-paper puppet. Show a correct action before its wrong action; never make a child the example of a mistake. All quantities and pictures are invented for practice. Say this aloud. Keep exit and assessment pieces covered until their collection, out of centers, models, warm-ups and practice. Reserved complete cases and independent task shapes stay separate even when a small quantity has appeared elsewhere.

Use adult-cut large paper squares for counters, strips for cubes, paper shapes for pattern blocks, plain paper for mats and hand-drawn cells for a 5-frame. Clean boxes, tubes or folded paper solids retain solid exploration. Check age labels and condition, use non-toxic drawing tools, keep large pieces on trays and out of mouths, and replace damaged or swallowable tools. No food, sharp pieces or requested home supplies. Cleanup and table-tray returns are inside the timed parts.

## Unit observation window

Days 15, 16 and 17 hear roster children 1 to 8, 9 to 16 and 17 to 24. Each conference is three separate 45-second asks, one possible 45-second retry and 30 seconds feedback/reset, 3.5 minutes. Eight turns use 28 of 30 minutes. Day 18 hears children 25 to 30, then up to five absentees, 38.5 of forty minutes at most. No extra teacher-led group runs alongside this. A secure conference is never repeated. Preserve initial evidence, one retry, help and private feedback. Classify earlier batches during day 18 Close records, nine minutes plus one filing. Classify first-made day 18 records before day 19, ten teacher minutes outside class. Keep missing components not yet seen.

## One child

Use 25 minutes: supported count 3, demonstrate 5, practice 8, feedback/retry 4 and the same new exit 5. One adult takes both partner roles and hears the child. Split or skip a day as needed. For day 18, use familiar work 15, earlier-record review 5 and tidy 5. If a unit observation is missing, use 3.5 minutes within familiar work; classify new evidence before day 19. Never repeat a completed conference to fill time.
