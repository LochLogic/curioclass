# Day 17: Count every visible piece

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-17.md and covered exit-17.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools. Cover assessment.md, kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md for today's eight conferences. Cut block-pieces.md separately.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 16 unique object-to-mark links: review [[s01-W17]] with one touch per completed piece, then [[s01-N17]] together. Partners touch three loose cubes, one 45-second turn each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 16's exit and practice s01-P16 with feedback. If day 16's tickets showed error A, reach each fresh square; error B: give each object only one mark. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group. Retain check records from days 15 and 16 for day 18. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Inspect a sculpture

Review day 4 low constructions and day 13 chosen supports. Name sculpture as something built to look at; a stack has pieces above one another. Name stack without building or counting one. Use [[s01-K17]] with the completed construction.

[[steps:s01-K17]] **Ask.** [[s01-Q17]] Expect behind or underneath. Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F17]], one puppet, invented for practice. Fifteen pairs take one 45-second turn per child, 1.5 minutes; 6.5 remain for showing and handling.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use u01-1/assessment.md, u01-4/kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md with roster children 17 to 24. Read s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c separately, exactly as printed. Six kites start in a line; nine boats start two high, four middle, three low. Each 3.5-minute conference holds three 45-second asks, one possible 45-second retry and 30 seconds for private feedback/reset. Eight conferences use 28 minutes; two remain for materials. Children dot the indicated pieces; scribe their explanation. Retain initial/revised words, actions, chosen support, help and heard names in record.md. A strategy cue means shown with help. Retry only the first obstruction and queue another. Classify on day 18 after this feedback/revision. Keep check pieces covered from other centers; run no simultaneous teacher group. Others review Counting centers with u01-1/cards-centers.md and return today's practice paper. Every child builds a new low, wide five-piece sculpture without counting placements, then counts its completed visible pieces. Use [[s01-P17]] to trace that actual construction and dot each piece.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a visible route, 45 seconds each. Give feedback; table groups return trays together. Children may pass.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-17.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X17]] exactly. Use block-pieces.md: eight cut-paper blocks and the exit's blank vertical area; children place all pieces in a vertical flat stack without overlap, dot and trace each. Eight short block cards fit in the building area. Actual cubes are optional; retain the dotted pieces with their traced paper record. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 19, Counting centers. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-17.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-17.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Build a low five-piece sculpture before counting. Your child traces the finished sculpture on Side 1 and dots each visible piece.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P17]].
  **Say.** "Where might a piece be hidden?" Expect behind or underneath; keep every piece visible.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X17]] exactly on exit-17.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; chosen supports from day 13. Children name their sculpture while sharing; stack names pieces above one another in the exit directions. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 closes retained observations from days 15 to 17 and hears only missing children. No reteaching or extra exit. Day 17 exit and practice wait for day 19 response: error A reaches every piece; error D pauses after one word per move. Error B or error C from observations receives a cover or slow-word response on day 19.
