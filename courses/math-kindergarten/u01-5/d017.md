# Day 17: Count every visible piece

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the boxed stack exit mat and eight paper blocks; keep both covered. Bring day 15 check masters and retained records. Assign ten children not yet heard and six rotating exit listeners. Prepare completed low, wide five-piece sculptures with every piece visible. Use paper strips instead of small cubes. Teacher-only keys: d017-key.html and d018-key.html. Until the opener exists, use this page.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 16 unique object-to-mark links: review [[s01-W17]] with one touch per completed piece, then [[s01-N17]] together. Partners take one 45-second completed-row count each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 16's exit and practice s01-P16 with feedback. If day 16's tickets showed error A, reach each fresh square; error B: give each object only one mark. Choose one five-minute action, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group. Retain check records from days 15 and 16 for day 18.

#### 8 min. Inspect a sculpture

Review day 4 low constructions and chosen routes from day 13. A sculpture is something built to look at; a stack has pieces above one another. Name stack without building or counting one. Use [[s01-K17]] with the completed construction.

[[steps:s01-K17]] **Ask.** [[s01-Q17]] Expect behind or underneath. Show correct before incorrect in [[s01-F17]], one puppet, invented for practice. Fifteen pairs take one 45-second turn per child, 1.5 minutes; 6.5 remain for showing and handling.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use the reserved kite and boat masters from day 15, items s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c, with ten assigned children not heard earlier. Three asks at 45 seconds each plus 45 seconds for a brief written note and one missed-count retry total three minutes each, thirty minutes. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Dot cut-paper pieces; scribe the explanation. Record actual sequence, object actions, chosen support and help on the class list. Others repeat known five- or ten-piece centers and complete today's practice paper. Give written feedback now; classify records on day 18, after this revision opportunity. A prompted revision is shown with help. Face check pieces away from centers and speak quietly. No reserved case appears at other centers. Use [[s01-P17]] at the completed-sculpture center, with five pieces; build without counted placement, then count. Trace the actual construction in the blank frame, keeping all parts visible. No vertical stacks or adding-count practice.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most show a visible route, 45 seconds each. Give feedback; table groups return trays together. Children may pass.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X17]] exactly. Give eight cut-paper blocks and the blank vertical area; children place all pieces in a vertical flat stack without overlap, dot and trace each. Eight short block cards fit in the building area. Actual cubes are optional; the flat paper record remains. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 19. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use the boxed picture master as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Print Side 1 and the boxed master from the teacher page. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Build a low five-piece sculpture before counting. Your child traces the finished sculpture on Side 1 and dots each visible piece.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P17]].
  **Say.** "Where might a piece be hidden?" Expect behind or underneath; keep every piece visible.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X17]] exactly on the uncovered picture master. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice counts a completed low, wide sculpture; the exit builds a vertical stack through counted placements.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; chosen supports from day 13. Sculpture and stack name arrangements, without a new counting method. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 closes retained observations from days 15 to 17 and hears only missing children. No reteaching or extra exit. Day 17 exit and practice wait for day 19 response: error A reaches every piece; error D pauses after one word per move. Error B or error C from observations receives a cover or slow-word response on day 19.
