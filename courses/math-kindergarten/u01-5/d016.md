# Day 16: Marks stand for objects

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the boxed toy-animal exit card; keep it covered. Bring day 15 check masters and records. Select ten children not yet heard for observations and six rotating exit observers. Prepare five-square adjacent records. Keep large pieces on trays. Teacher-only keys: d016-key.html and d018-key.html. Until the opener exists, this page supplies setup and directions.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 15 corrected counting actions with [[s01-W16]]. Review [[s01-N16]] together. Partners take one 45-second moving turn each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 15's exit and practice s01-P15 with feedback. If day 15's tickets showed error A, reach each fresh square once; error B: cover counted squares. Choose one five-minute repair, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group. Keep day 15 observation records for day 18, with feedback already given.

#### 8 min. Return to linked records

Review Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8. Use [[s01-K16]] with five loose squares and their traced record.

[[steps:s01-K16]] Name represent as letting a mark stand for an object, applying known marks. **Ask.** [[s01-Q16]] Expect the square at the other end of its link. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously, one 45-second turn each, 1.5 minutes, leaving 6.5 for showing and handling.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use the reserved kite and boat masters from day 15, items s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c, with ten assigned children not heard earlier. Three asks at 45 seconds each plus 45 seconds for a brief written note and one missed-count retry total three minutes each, thirty minutes. Keep initial and revised evidence separate. Dot cut-paper pieces; scribe the explanation. Record actual sequence, object actions, chosen support and help on the class list. Others repeat known five- or ten-piece centers and complete today's practice paper. Give written feedback now; classify records on day 18, after this revision opportunity. A prompted revision is shown with help. Face check pieces away from centers and speak quietly. No reserved case appears at other centers. Use [[s01-P16]] at the linked-record center. Trace five loose squares first, then put one adjacent mark by each and link the pairs. No animals or separate blank-panel records in practice.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most trace a unique link, 45 seconds each. Give feedback; groups return table trays together. Children may pass.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's fresh master at their independent turn. Read [[s01-X16]] exactly. Children draw marks in the separate blank panel. Hear or observe six individual source-to-mark routes; no extra counting ask. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions, simultaneous paper work and collection use 3.5 minutes. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return papers on day 17. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use the boxed picture master as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Print Side 1 and the boxed master from the teacher page. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Trace five loose squares on Side 1. Your child makes one adjacent tick per trace and links each pair.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P16]].
  **Say.** "Which object goes with this mark?" Expect the square at the other end of its link.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X16]] exactly on the uncovered picture master. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Practice uses adjacent marks with visible links; the exit independently represents unseen animals in a separate panel.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8 and Explain a corrected count from day 15. Represent names a familiar mark standing for its object. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 inspects low, wide sculptures with every piece visible. Return day 16 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square; error B: give each object only one mark. Continue observations with ten different children; retain earlier check records for day 18.
