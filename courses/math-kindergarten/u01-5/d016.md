# Day 16: Marks stand for objects

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Prepare practice-16.md and covered exit-16.md. Bring yesterday's papers with feedback. Use u01-1/record.md for the six exit listeners. Prepare five and ten large loose pieces on trays; reject damaged, sharp or swallowable tools. Cover assessment.md, kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md for today's eight conferences.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pick up day 15 corrected counting actions with [[s01-W16]]. Review [[s01-N16]] together. Partners take one 45-second moving turn each, simultaneously.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair from day 2. Return day 15's exit and practice s01-P15 with feedback. If day 15's tickets showed error A, reach each fresh square once; error B: cover counted squares. Choose one five-minute repair, whole class if more than ten need it, otherwise one group. Keep day 15 observation records for day 18, with feedback already given. Distribute and read feedback for one minute; model the selected correction for one, retry a fresh case simultaneously for two, and collect for one. Queue other errors.

#### 8 min. Return to linked records

Review Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8. Use [[s01-K16]] with five loose squares and the adjacent square/tick record in practice-16.md.

[[steps:s01-K16]] Name represent as letting a mark stand for an object, applying known marks. **Ask.** [[s01-Q16]] Expect the square at the other end of its link. Model, This mark represents that square, for partners to use while pointing. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously, one 45-second turn each, 1.5 minutes, leaving 6.5 for showing and handling.

#### 30 min. Observation centers

Use u01-1/assessment.md, u01-4/kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md with roster children 9 to 16. Read s01-A1a, s01-A1b and s01-A1c separately, exactly as printed. Six kites start in a line; nine boats start two high, four middle, three low. Each 3.5-minute conference holds three 45-second asks, one possible 45-second retry and 30 seconds for private feedback/reset. Eight conferences use 28 minutes; two remain for materials. Children dot the indicated pieces; scribe their explanation. Retain initial/revised words, actions, chosen support, help and heard names in record.md. A strategy cue means shown with help. Retry only the first obstruction and queue another. Classify on day 18 after this feedback/revision. Keep check pieces covered from other centers; run no simultaneous teacher group. Others review Counting centers with u01-1/cards-centers.md and return today's practice paper. At the linked-record center use [[s01-P16]]. Revisit five loose squares, then link each preprinted square to its adjacent tick on practice-16.md.

#### 4 min. Share and tidy

Two volunteers at most trace a unique link, 45 seconds each. Give feedback; groups return table trays together. Children may pass.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-16.md only at the child's own turn. Read [[s01-X16]] exactly. Children draw marks in the separate blank panel. Hear or observe six individual source-to-mark routes; no extra counting ask. Hear six rotating children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; directions use one minute, simultaneous paper work two and collection half a minute. The six complete their own papers during their turns, without recounting completed work; brief notes fit each turn. Record names heard and actual actions. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; paper alone never establishes unheard words. Return exit and practice papers on day 17, Return and repair. After class, mark thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen minutes; keep correspondence and heard words separate.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Use exit-16.md as the exit ticket. Without printing, copy its exact stem and pictures onto paper; keep it covered. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares, strips and shapes replace tools; drawn lines replace mats. Use Side 1 and covered exit-16.md. Cut any supply pieces; keep the master covered.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class rotations.

- **3 min. Review.** Rehearse words through ten together.
- **4 min. Repair.** Check yesterday's paper using fresh practice pieces.
- **5 min. Demonstrate.** Place five loose squares beside Side 1. Show its square/tick pairs; your child links each one.
- **13 min. Practice.** Complete [[s01-P16]].
  **Say.** "Which object goes with this mark?" Expect the square at the other end of its link. Model, This mark represents that square, for partners to use while pointing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s01-X16]] exactly on exit-16.md. Your child responds alone. Save it for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track and partner from days 1 to 5; mark from day 6. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1. Review: counted, uncounted and boundary from day 12; Adjacent object-to-mark links from day 8 and Explain a corrected count from day 15. Represent names a familiar mark standing for its object. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 inspects low, wide sculptures with every piece visible. Return day 16 papers in Return and repair. Error A: reach every fresh square; error B: give each object only one mark. Continue observations with the next eight children; retain earlier check records for day 18.
