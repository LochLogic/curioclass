# Day 18: Close the counting record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Bring day 15 check masters and retained records. Print both student sides only for unheard children; cut pieces from separate supply copies. Cover each check until that child's turn. Prepare the missing-name list. Teacher-only assessment key: d018-key.html. Teacher-only answer key: d018-key.html. Until the section opener exists, use this page's setup and directions.



### The day

#### 10 min. Familiar centers

Review day 17 familiar centers while checking the missing-name list from days 15 to 17. Children continue known five-square moves, adjacent linked records or low sculptures. No new teaching or repair.

#### 40 min. Missing observations

Hear only children not yet observed. Read [[s01-A1a]], [[s01-A1b]] and [[s01-A1c]] separately, using the covered cut-paper masters from day 15. Each child dots the indicated pieces; scribe the explanation. Three asks at 45 seconds plus 45 seconds for written feedback and one missed-count retry total three minutes. Thirteen children use 39 minutes; one remains for materials. Retain initial/revised evidence separately. If more remain, mark components not yet seen and send the missing-name list to day 19. Never repeat completed interviews.

#### 10 min. Close records

Collect center papers. Classify earlier records from day 15, day 16 and day 17 component by component: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A cued retry is shown with help. K.CC.B.4a evidence is ordered words paired once with each object; the explanation describes tracking. Unheard words stay not yet seen. New day 18 observations remain descriptive today: classify before day 19, allowing ten teacher minutes outside class. No extra exit or reteaching.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Hand-copy exact master stems and pictures if printing is unavailable; cover reserved papers. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Use day 15 paper kites and boats; paper squares and strips replace center tools.

**Skip.** Class rotations; never repeat completed observations.

- **3 min. Assessment record.** If unheard, ask the three separate reserved prompts using the check papers. Otherwise inspect the retained record without asking again.
- **15 min. Familiar work.** Repeat known five-square moving or adjacent-mark work.
  **Say.** "Show one object for each counting word." Expect one move per word.
- **5 min. Close records.** Keep initial work, written feedback and any retry separately. Classify earlier evidence only; classify today's new observation before day 19.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Keep materials for the next day.

### Shorter or longer

Shared shape: Familiar centers reuse taught practice; reserved interviews occur only for unheard children.

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; adjacent links from day 8; boundary from day 12. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 shows quantities to ten with fingers or a counter mat and has ten response minutes. Return day 17 papers and day 18's assessment records there. Error A: reach each piece; error B: cover counted pieces; error C: rehearse ordered words; error D: one word per move. Choose the earliest need; day 20 has ten more response minutes. Send unresolved missing observations, never invented results.
