# Day 18: Close the counting record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read u01-5/opener.md and the private u01-1/key-s01.md. Bring retained records in u01-1/record.md. Cover assessment.md and large u01-4/kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md for missing observations only. Prepare the missing-name list and familiar center papers. Print both student sides only for unheard children. Keep large intact pieces on trays and out of mouths; reject sharp or damaged tools.

### The day

#### 10 min. Familiar centers

Review day 17 Counting centers using u01-1/cards-centers.md while checking the missing-name list from days 15 to 17. Children continue known five-square moves, adjacent linked records or low sculptures on familiar center papers. These are familiar practice; drawings stay in folders. No new teaching or repair.

#### 40 min. Missing observations

Use assessment.md and the covered large kite-pieces.md and boat-pieces.md only for unheard children. Hear roster children 25 to 30 first, six conferences in 21 minutes. Read [[s01-A1a]], [[s01-A1b]] and [[s01-A1c]] separately. Start six kites in a line and nine boats two high, four middle, three low. Children dot the paper pieces; scribe the explanation. Each 3.5-minute conference holds three 45-second asks, one possible 45-second retry and 30 seconds feedback/reset. Collect assessment papers during reset. Up to five missing children fit too: eleven conferences, 38.5 minutes, plus 1.5 for materials. Preserve initial/revised evidence and help in record.md. If more remain, leave components not yet seen and send the missing-name list to day 19. Never repeat secure conferences.

#### 10 min. Close records

Classify the earlier day 15, day 16 and day 17 batches in record.md, three minutes per eight-child batch, nine total plus one minute filing. Children continue familiar table work and return materials together; drawings stay in folders. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for each component. A cued retry is shown with help. Judge ordered words paired once with each object, and an explanation tied to observed tracking. Unheard words stay not yet seen. Classify new day 18 observations before day 19, allowing ten teacher minutes outside class. No extra exit or reteaching.

### Other rooms

No device is required. Hand-copy exact master stems and pictures if printing is unavailable; cover reserved papers. Large paper pieces replace counters, cubes and blocks; drawn boxes replace mats.

### At home

**Swap.** Large paper squares and strips replace center tools. Use the two check sides and separate large cut-paper pieces.

**Skip.** Repeating a completed interview.

- **4 min. Assessment record.** If unheard, read [[s01-A1a]], [[s01-A1b]] and [[s01-A1c]] separately: three 45-second asks, one retry, 30 seconds feedback/reset. Scribe the explanation. Otherwise look through the retained record together, without repeating its prompts.
- **14 min. Familiar work.** Repeat five-square moves. Keep this practice in the folder.
- **5 min. Close records.** Keep initial work, feedback and help separate. Classify new observations before day 19.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Save the pieces and records for day 19's response.


### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated center rounds; keep evidence turns. Longer: change a practice route with the same pieces.

### Words and supports

Review: count, object, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record from day 1; adjacent links from day 8; boundary from day 12. Read every printed stem and label aloud; student type is 18 point. Offer enlarged or tactile outlines, signs or a communication aid. An adult may follow the child's directed marks; record help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral writing.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 shows quantities to ten with fingers or a counter mat and has ten response minutes. Return day 17 papers and day 18's assessment records there. Error A: reach each piece; error B: cover counted pieces; error C: rehearse ordered words; error D: one word per move. Choose the earliest need; day 20 has ten more response minutes. Send unresolved missing observations, never invented results.
