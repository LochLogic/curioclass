# Day 77: I can make a story question.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private; generated answer key holds new practice steps. Prepare student pages, practice-77.md, counters, frames, mats, record.md and thirty covered exit-77.md half slips. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

For worked and repair rows, read scene first; reveal solution steps after the ask.

Opening: Oral count, Return and repair, Finish a story question, 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 minutes.

#### 5 min. Oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1; total, day 19; Paper count-dot record, day 1. **Ask.** [[s04-K-count-77]] Count together first, then continue from the supplied word. No individual round or required counting-on strategy.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 76's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 76's tickets showed the matching action, select one priority. **Ask.** [[s04-F-77]] Then [[steps:s04-F-77]] Five guided minutes and five for a fresh supplied-cue retry. More than ten sharing it: class repair; otherwise one group while peers correct familiar paper. No extra group or check replay.

#### 10 min. Finish a story question

Review story, day 68; add and altogether, day 64. **Ask.** [[s04-K-story-77]] Then [[steps:s04-K-story-77]] Complete only missing question in adult-started object story; partner solves. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds. No whole picture-prompt story creation in practice.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap, with resets. **Ask.** [[s04-P77]] Then [[steps:s04-P77]]. Adult supplies one-and-three square story; child supplies only total question. Partner uses supplied marks to solve four. Error B excludes first group. Repeat two-and-two started story, never boxes. Keep unscored corrected paper in folders; recorded feedback returns day 78.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1; Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. **Ask.** [[s04-P-game-77]] Then [[steps:s04-P-game-77]] First five minutes use the two familiar cases with immediate partner/model feedback, no timer or ranking. Next five repeat today's partial square record. Last two tidy and count pieces in. Unscored paper stays in the folder.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-77.md at each child's own turn. Say "Invented for practice." Read exact stimulus from private key, then **Ask.** [[s04-X77]] Read exact stem. All children draw story reminders on paper. Hear roster 19 to 24 only: six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5. No public answer or practice frame.

Carry pending components for s04-X73, s04-X74, s04-X75, s04-X76 only when this fresh evidence fits. No extra ask or old check repeated; unheard explanations stay pending. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; log names heard and access separately from mathematical help.

After class: thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 78 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Unscored corrected practice stays in the folder. Recorded observations return in the next named feedback slot.

Hand-copy original masters. Large paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printing, folded paper replaces mats/cards. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut squares, folded mats and drawn frames replace class equipment. Hand-copy original masters.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count through ten, then from today's supplied word.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 76 feedback with one supplied square correction.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-P77]] Read scene; ask before revealing solution. Child directs pieces and corrects marks.
  **Say.** "What question is missing?" Expect: How many squares altogether?
- **7 min. Practice.** Swap partner roles; repeat supplied record and one familiar game round. Keep original and changed paper.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X77]] Read exact fresh stimulus from private key, then stem. Child marks alone; keep paper and words.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions; retain repair, core connections, paper evidence and exit. Longer: rearrange supplied incomplete square model, correct same missing component; stay within ten.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; counter, day 3; group and mark, day 6; total, day 19; altogether, day 64. Review 10-frame, day 26, and one counter for each story object, day 71. Read all print aloud. Offer high contrast, tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed movement/scribing. Public talk may be passed; record access separately from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 returns day 77's exit and practice feedback in Return and repair. If many treat only second group as whole, error B, enclose both on fresh one-and-three square record. Then connect actions to expressions and equations.
