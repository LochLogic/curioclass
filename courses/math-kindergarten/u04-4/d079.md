# Day 79: I can connect models and equations.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep u04-3/teacher-key.md and this section's teacher-key.md private for F2/F1; generated key holds practice steps. Prepare student pages, practice-79.md, counters, frames, mats, record.md and thirty covered exit-79.md half slips. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

For worked and repair rows, read scene first; reveal solution steps after the ask.

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1; total, day 19; Paper count-dot record, day 1. **Ask.** [[s04-K-count-79]] Count together first, then continue from the supplied word. No individual round or required counting-on strategy.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 78's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 78's tickets showed the matching action, select one priority. **Ask.** [[s04-F-79]] Then [[steps:s04-F-79]] Two guided minutes and three for a fresh supplied-cue retry. More than ten sharing it: class repair; otherwise one group while peers correct familiar paper. No extra group or check replay.

#### 10 min. Connect picture and equation

Review expression, equation, plus, minus, equals and same quantity, day 78; Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames, day 26. **Ask.** [[s04-K-frame-79]] Then [[steps:s04-K-frame-79]] Place only day 79 expression-cards.md cards beside the supplied models. Link object locations, drawing and each numeral. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds. No fans or seven-and-two partitions.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap. **Ask.** [[s04-P79]] Then [[steps:s04-P79]]. Correct partial three-and-three drawing, then six-square removal drawing beside known equations. Keep unscored corrections in folders. Use last five minutes for familiar untimed game: **Ask.** [[s04-P-game-79]] Then [[steps:s04-P-game-79]]

#### 20 min. Independent paper record

First sight of unit 4 two-story sheet, assessment-stories.md: ten concurrent paper minutes. Say "Invented for practice." Read separately **Ask.** [[s04-F2a]] and **Ask.** [[s04-F2b]]. Counters available; no required written labels. Next eight minutes: unit 4 symbol sheet, assessment-symbols.md. Reveal its joining picture first, then its removal picture at that turn. Read each picture/choice evenly from private key, then separately **Ask.** [[s04-F1a-expression]] **Ask.** [[s04-F1a-equations]] **Ask.** [[s04-F1b-expression]] **Ask.** [[s04-F1b-equations]]. No hints. Two minutes collect; no conferences. Reserve thirty teacher minutes after class for written picture-cued feedback, returned day 80 Feedback and revision. Keep originals for conferences and day 83.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-79.md at each child's own turn. Say "Invented for practice." Read exact stimulus from private key, then **Ask.** [[s04-X79]] Read exact stem. All children mark the matching mat and both groups on paper. Hear roster 1 to 6 only: six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5. No public answer or practice frame.

Carry pending components for s04-X75, s04-X76, s04-X77, s04-X78 only when this fresh evidence fits. No extra ask or old check repeated; unheard explanations stay pending. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; log names heard and access separately from mathematical help.

After class: thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 80 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Unscored corrected practice stays in the folder. Recorded observations return in the next named feedback slot.

Hand-copy original masters. Large paper squares replace counters, drawn frames replace printing, folded paper replaces mats/cards. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and copied masters replace equipment.

**Skip.** Repeated centers and class rotations.

- **4 min. Return and connect.** Return day 78 feedback; use its picture cue. [[s04-K-frame-79]] Put squares beside supplied relation.
  **Say.** "Why can six go first?" Expect: both sides are six.
- **18 min. Paper record.** Read F2a, F2b and four F1 asks separately from private keys. Use the same two masters; child works alone. Save feedback for tomorrow.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X79]] Read exact fresh stimulus and stem; child marks alone. Keep paper and words.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions; retain repair, core connections, paper evidence and exit. Longer: rearrange supplied incomplete square model, correct same missing component; stay within ten.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; counter, day 3; group and mark, day 6; total, day 19; altogether, day 64. Review 10-frame, day 26; One counter stands for one story object, day 71. Read all print aloud. Offer high contrast, tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed movement/scribing. Public talk may be passed; record access separately from mathematical help. Review expression, equation, plus, minus, equals and same quantity, day 78.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 returns day 79's exit in Return and repair. Error D: build both quantities for total-left equation. Error B: locate both parts before whole. Feedback and revision returns day 79 F1/F2 originals before roster 1 to 10 conferences. Rehearse supplied models; withdraw supplied frames, retain choice/access frames.
