# Day 28: Find the five within eight

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-28.md through practice-records.md, using the student page's picture without its printed total. Set out picture-routes.md and adult-filled blank-frames.md. Cover exit-28.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-3/teacher-key.md and d028-key.html private. Choose roster 19 to 24. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. Read all print aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 27's frame comparisons, [[s02-R28]]. Count together through ten, then join the familiar adult-led words to twenty. Review one object per occupied cell. Every case is invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 27 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O27. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 27 s02-X27, s02-O27 notes, s02-F27, s02-P27-1 and s02-P27-2. If many day 27 tickets or heard reasons show error B, trace supplied square partners despite spread, [[s02-F28]]. For observed error K, repair the missing unique partner instead. Choose one action and collect fresh paper beside the original; queue other needs. Selection alone on yesterday's ticket did not verify equality.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Whole class: [[s02-K28]] before [[steps:s02-K28]]. Review day 26's two five-cell rows; now use the full five as a known part when comparing. Move paper squares over the full top row, then circle that row on the teacher picture. Compare with a completed five frame. **Ask.** [[s02-C28]] Expect eight, because both have five and eight has three beyond. Do not count empty cells. A puppet calls eight fewer despite seeing the extras, error I; correct with partners and the word more. No equation or numeral writing.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs [[s02-P28-1]]: on the completed eight, circle the full five, leaving three below visible. Read its short stem aloud; the student picture gives no printed total. Then [[s02-P28-2]]: match eight square pictures to the supplied dots and repair the missing third lower dot. Each partner takes one 45-second turn per task, three minutes concurrently. Spend the remaining time watching correspondences and giving feedback. If many count a gap as a dot, cover only occupied cells and recount the objects.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O28]] from heard-28.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 19 to 24: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover and two collection. First repeat completed five/eight frame comparison, [[s02-C28]]. Then review day 26 drawing repair, [[s02-G28m]], on existing five-square work. Match each reference square once; add an extra lower square. Children return both sides with a Finish tick. Picture-routes.md remains available for the familiar five-square route reminder. No blank two-frame comparison sheet is used in teaching. Mark after class: thirty times twenty-five seconds, 12.5 teacher minutes. Return on day 29.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently to paper trays and screen.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-28.md only now and read [[s02-X28]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 19 to 24, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Copy the supplied diagrams and exact exit on scrap paper, keeping equal cells. Large squares replace counters; plain mats replace fingers. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large scrap squares and drawn equal cells; copy supplied pictures and covered exit.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Model.** Compare completed five/eight frames. Point to the common full five, then extras.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P28-1]] **Say.** "Check the full five." Expect: full top row of eight indicated, three remain below.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P28-2]] Expect: one added dot, full five and three. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X28]] on the unseen exit-28.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repeats; retain the full-five connection, missing-dot repair and exit. Longer: compare the same completed frames with their source squares spread apart.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; scattered, day 13; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22; row, array, circle, day 25; 10-frame, day 26. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. Directed adult marks record motor help; quantity prompts record mathematical help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 rehearses drawing repairs for more, fewer and same. If many day 28 papers or heard reasons show error B, check occupied cells against spread square pictures; for heard error I, point to extras and correct the word. Use one five-minute Return and repair action. Unheard reasons remain not yet seen.
