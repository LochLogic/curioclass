# Day 25: Keep track of fixed pictures

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use practice-25.md and picture-routes.md from practice-records.md. Cover exit-25.md until each child's turn. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-3/teacher-key.md and d025-key.html private. Choose roster 7 to 12. Adult-cut large paper pieces, inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 24's matching explanation. [[s02-R25]] Count together through ten; adult models eleven through twenty once for supported joining, not judgment. Say every picture case is invented for practice. Review one touch with one word.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 24 exit s02-X24, heard s02-O24 notes, s02-P24-1 and s02-P224 with feedback. If many day 24 tickets showed observed error K, finish a missing square partner in [[s02-F25]]. For heard error I, point to extras and correct the comparison word; for heard error B, keep members visible while spreading. Choose one action, collect a fresh correction, queue the others. Keep the original beside it.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

Whole class: [[s02-K25-1]] then [[steps:s02-K25-1]]. Review total: the last word names the whole group. A fixed picture cannot move to the counted side; mark visits instead. Demonstrate line, array rows, circle start and scattered routes on picture-routes.md. An array has rows; a circle needs a start so we stop before returning there. Show [[s02-K25-2]] before [[steps:s02-K25-2]]. Correct an omitted visit with [[s02-P25-1]]. Each picture receives one mark; empty spaces receive none.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs repair the supplied line and array routes, [[s02-PR25-1]] and [[s02-PR25-2]]. **Ask.** Read each row separately. Children point, count quietly and add the missing visit; five in the line, ten in the array. Each partner has a 45-second turn per row: three minutes across concurrent pairs, with handling and feedback in the remaining time. If many omit a square, cover completed pictures with large squares, then connect covers to marks.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds use [[s02-PR25-3]] and [[s02-PR25-4]], circle then scatter, with two minutes changeover and two collection. Adult starts; partners repair and touch-check every picture, five then ten. Review Finish tick in the corner after finishing. Everyone returns both sides. No curved path or exit pictures appear in practice. Mark practice after class: thirty children times twenty seconds, ten teacher minutes. Return on day 26.

#### 2 min. Tidy

All pairs return pieces concurrently to paper trays and screen.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: reveal each child's own half slip; read [[s02-X25]] exactly. Everyone marks paper independently for two minutes. Hear roster 7 to 12 only, [[s02-O25]], six times 45 seconds including notes, 4.5 minutes. Collect in half a minute. Paper establishes visits, not an unheard sequence. Mark after class: thirty times fifteen seconds, 7.5 teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard names and help. Return paper and practice day 26.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the same practice diagrams and exact exit picture and stem without a printer. Large paper squares replace counters; plain mats replace fingers. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Use scrap squares and crayons; copy the supplied routes and covered exit.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join the counting words through ten.
- **5 min. Model.** Mark each fixed square once; show the circle start.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-PR25-2]] Repair the supplied array. **Say.** "Fix the supplied route." Expect: ten squares visited once.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P25-1]] Expect: five single visits. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X25]] without help. After the child marks, **Say.** "Count these leaves." Expect: one through eight with one touch per leaf. Save for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one center round, retaining both layouts, model and exit. Longer: repeat the three-square reverse route without adding objects.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record from day 1; Return and repair, day 2; group, day 6; match, day 8; Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; scattered, day 13; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22. New: row, array, circle as arrangements. Enlarge or use tactile squares. Directed adult marks record motor help; quantity prompts record mathematical help. Public talk may be passed. No numeral task.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 compares despite spacing and teaches five-cell structure. If many day 25 tickets show observed error K or L, repair one missing or repeated visit on a fresh square route in Return and repair. Hear a rotating six; unheard sequences stay not yet seen.
