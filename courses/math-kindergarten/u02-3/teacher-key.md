# Picture routes and frames: teacher answer key

Teacher only. Keep separate from student copies. These cold-solved keys supply look-fors and two samples. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Diagnose letters from observed actions or heard reasons.

## s02-X25

Prompt: Mark each leaf as you count it.

Answer: Eight leaves, each marked once.

Look for: Fixed S-shaped open curve; hear sequence separately. | Shape: Counts a fixed curved leaf path, not movable objects or a taught route. No leaves, eight-picture curve, S layout or pretraced curve in teaching. Practice original square pictures in straight lines, arrays, circles and scatters, mainly five and ten.

Shown: Eight leaves, each marked once. | Not yet seen: Skips one leaf. (K).

## s02-O25

Prompt: Count these leaves.

Answer: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight.

Look for: Rotating six only, 45 seconds each; record names heard. Paper cannot prove unheard reasons.

Shown: One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight. | Not yet seen: Visits a leaf twice while counting. (L).

Shown: Five unique links, no extra bead. | Not yet seen: Calls wider row more because of spacing. (B).
