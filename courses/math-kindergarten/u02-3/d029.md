# Day 29: Repair comparison drawings

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-29.md through practice-records.md; set out picture-routes.md and adult-filled blank-frames.md for reminders. Cover exit-29.md. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-3/teacher-key.md and d029-key.html private; today's item key accepts any countable larger flower group, including above ten. Choose roster 25 to 30. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage, keep out of mouths. Read all print aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 28's full five with extras. [[s02-R29]] Join familiar words through ten and adult-led words to twenty. All cases are invented for practice. Count objects, not empty cells.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 28 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O28. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 28 s02-X28, s02-O28 notes, s02-F28, s02-P28-1, s02-P28-2 and s02-G28m with feedback. If many day 28 papers or heard reasons show error B, check occupied cells against the spread square source using [[s02-F29]]. For heard error I, compare repaired eight to completed five; point to extras and correct the word. Choose one action, collect fresh correction beside original; queue other needs.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review day 26 drawing repairs only: [[s02-P29-1]], [[s02-P29-2]], [[s02-P29-3]], one at a time. **Ask.** Read each stem aloud before feedback. Children add, cross out or complete supplied drawings for five, using one simple mark per object. Samples: six more, four fewer, five same. Each partner has three 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes concurrently; remaining time handles paper and immediate feedback. Crossed-out objects do not count. No new drawing convention or numeral work.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs repeat those repairs on existing diagrams, then verify with links. More has five partners and extras; fewer leaves extras in the reference; same has no extras. Adults read each request again, without demanding written comparison words. If many keep equal groups for more, trace all partners then point to where an extra object must be drawn. For observed error K, repair the missing partner before judging language. Preserve each child's initial paper and changed marks.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O29]] from heard-29.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 25 to 30: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds repeat the same supplied drawing tasks, two minutes changeover and two collection. Use large squares on the completed frame reminder if needed, then return to paper. Picture-routes.md supplies only the familiar five-square route reminder, never a new check. Give immediate feedback after each repair. Every child returns both sides with a Finish tick. No independent larger-only blank panel is practiced. Mark after class: thirty times thirty seconds, fifteen teacher minutes. Return on day 30.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently to paper trays and screen; keep exits covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-29.md only now and read [[s02-X29]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 25 to 30, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied diagrams and the exact exit. Equal hand-drawn cells and large squares replace printed frames and counters; plain mats replace fingers. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy supplied pictures on scrap paper, use large paper squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Add or cross out marks in an adult-started drawing.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P29-1]] **Say.** "Repair this drawing for more." Expect: more than five below, with extras after matching.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s02-P29-2]] Expect: fewer than five uncrossed squares; four is one sample. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X29]] on the unseen exit-29.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repeats, retain all three comparison requests and exit. Longer: partners verify each other's repaired drawing again with large squares.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; scattered, day 13; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22; row, array, circle, day 25; 10-frame and drawing repairs, day 26. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. Directed adult marks record motor help; quantity prompts record mathematical help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 connects quantities and spoken numbers to written numerals. Return day 29 paper in Return and repair. If many show heard error I, repair one comparison word after matching; for heard error B, check that spreading five squares leaves five. Use fresh supplied five-reference drawing work, collect a correction and queue other needs. Unheard reasons stay not yet seen.
