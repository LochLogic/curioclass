# Day 27: Rehearse more, fewer and equal

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-27.md and picture-routes.md through practice-records.md. Adult-fill blank-frames.md for five/eight, five/three and five/five. Keep exit-27.md covered; courses/math-kindergarten/u02-3/teacher-key.md and d027-key.html private. Choose roster 19 to 24. Adult-cut large pieces, inspect damage and keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 26's five-cell rows and comparisons. [[s02-R27]] Join familiar counting through ten and supported words to twenty. Every case is invented for practice. Review counting objects, never empty cells.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 26 s02-X26, s02-O26 notes, s02-F26, s02-P26-1 and s02-G26m, s02-G26f, s02-G26e. If many day 26 papers or heard reasons showed error B, trace supplied links on equal-four squares, [[s02-F27]], despite spread. Children replace the wrong second link and verify four unique pairs. Collect fresh correction, preserving originals. Queue other needs, one action only.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review yesterday's structures only. Show one completed physical frame pair at a time: [[s02-C27m]], [[s02-C27f]], [[s02-C27e]]. **Ask.** Read each stem separately, then give immediate feedback: eight more than five; three fewer than five; five equal to five. Partners alternate three 45-second turns each, 4.5 minutes concurrently, leaving setup and feedback. Review full five and extra occupied cells; no new notation or game rules. Spoken practice notes stay in the teacher's record.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs use pieces on one frame comparison at a time. Each child indicates the chosen group before the adult confirms. Fingers or large paper squares may verify. Then [[s02-P27-1]]: read the false claim aloud; children cross out the wrong word and indicate the eight group. Adult scribes their spoken or signed replacement, more; no child must read or write a word. If many reverse the comparison, trace five partners and point to extras while saying more. Correction follows each turn, without a child-facing timer.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds repeat those same frame comparisons and the supplied-picture repair [[s02-P27-2]], with two minutes changeover and two collection. The circle has five squares; repair one missing visit, then check all visits once. Adult may cover counted pictures with squares before returning to visit marks. Review Finish tick after completion. Every child returns both sides. No three-pair board appears in practice. Mark after class: thirty times twenty seconds, ten teacher minutes. Return on day 28.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return squares concurrently to paper trays and screen.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: reveal each child's own half slip; read [[s02-X27]] exactly. Independent paper, two minutes. Hear roster 19 to 24, [[s02-O27]], six times 45 seconds including notes, 4.5 minutes. Collect in half a minute. Selection alone does not establish equal numbers: look for nine unique peg links, no extras. Paper cannot establish unheard reasons. Mark after class: thirty times twenty seconds, ten teacher minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record help and heard names. Return paper and practice day 28.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied pictures and the exact exit; draw equal frame cells. Large squares replace counters; plain mats replace fingers. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the supplied pictures on scrap paper; use large paper squares on drawn frames.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** Compare filled five/eight, five/three, five/five frames, one pair at a time.
- **7 min. Correct.** [[s02-P27-1]] **Say.** "Fix the comparison word." Expect: more, because eight contains five partners and extras. Scribe the child's word after they cross out fewer.
- **5 min. Route.** [[s02-P27-2]] Expect: five square visits, one each. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X27]] independently. Expect: nine unique peg matches. **Say.** "What proves the pair is the same?" Expect: every peg has a partner, none left. Save paper for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions; keep rehearsal, correction and exit. Longer: repeat the same physical frame pairs with partners exchanging roles.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; scattered, day 13; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22; row, array, circle, day 25; 10-frame structure, day 26. Enlarge or use tactile pieces. Directed adult marks record motor help; quantity prompts record mathematical help. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 uses a full five within eight to explain comparison. If many day 27 tickets or heard reasons show error B or K, repair one spread-based claim or missing partner on supplied equal-four squares in Return and repair. Queue other needs; unheard reasons stay not yet seen.
