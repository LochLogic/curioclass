# Day 26: Space is not an object

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare practice-26.md and picture-routes.md through practice-records.md. Set out blank-frames.md, using its frames only. Keep exit-26.md covered. Keep courses/math-kindergarten/u02-3/teacher-key.md private. Choose roster 7 to 12. Adult-cut large pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. Read every printed word aloud at 18 point. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25's fixed-picture tracking. [[s02-R26]] Join counting through ten, then the adult-led words to twenty. Review total, the whole counted amount. All cases are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 25 s02-X25, s02-O25 notes, s02-F25, s02-P25-1 and s02-PR25-1, s02-PR25-2, s02-PR25-3 and s02-PR25-4. If many day 25 tickets show observed error K, repair one omitted visit in [[s02-F26]]; for error L, correct one duplicate. Choose one five-minute action; queue others. Collect fresh paper beside the original.

Use five minutes: return 1, model one correction 1, simultaneous fresh practice 2, collect 1. Retain originals and revisions.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

First two minutes: [[s02-K26-1]] before [[steps:s02-K26-1]]. Verify supplied square links despite spacing. Next three: [[s02-K26-2]] then [[steps:s02-K26-2]]. Review the material name 5-frame from day 3; teach its structure now. Last three: [[s02-K26-3]] then [[steps:s02-K26-3]]. Name the joined two-row 10-frame. Empty cells are places, not objects. Connect moved squares to one drawn dot per occupied cell; count only the dots.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Inspect the supplied eight-square picture and its seven dots, [[s02-P26-1]]. **Ask.** Read its stem. Expect one added dot in the third lower cell. Then teach repairs [[s02-G26m]], [[s02-G26f]], [[s02-G26e]], one aloud at a time. Model one square per object, adding for more, crossing out for fewer, completing missing partners for same. Expected samples: six, four, five. Children repair existing drawings and draw links. Model the three changes for two minutes. Partners try the same supplied drawings during centers; use the remaining connected-work minute to check the missing-dot correction. No blank comparison drawing is practiced.

Use the first 7 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Turn to the student-page Exit box, copied from heard-26.md. Read [[s02-O26]], one ask only; cover it again before centers. Twenty-four mark a feature on their copies. The named six keep pictures covered until their own turns; scribe their words beside their directed marks. Hear roster 7 to 12: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

Two eight-minute rounds, two minutes changeover, two collection. First compare one completed physical frame pair at a time: [[s02-C26m]], [[s02-C26f]], [[s02-C26e]], five/eight, five/three, five/five. Partners alternate a 45-second turn on each comparison, 4.5 minutes concurrently. One points while the other checks partners/extras; confirm with the private key. In the second round, give each partner three 45-second drawing-repair turns, another 4.5 minutes within eight. Then repeat supplied drawing and dot repairs; do not introduce a new sheet format. If many count spaces, touch only occupied cells with large squares. Everyone returns both sides, with the reviewed Finish tick, a corner tick meaning finished. Include practice in the after-class paper-set marking; return on day 27.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently to paper trays and screen; keep exits covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-26.md only now and read [[s02-X26]] exactly, one minute. Twenty-four children begin paper for two minutes. The six named children wait with pictures covered until their own observed turns. Observe the sole requested action for roster 7 to 12, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. After class, mark all thirty paper sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; write one useful correction per set. Return the exit, practice papers and heard-response notes on day 27 in Return and repair. Unheard words remain not yet seen.

### Other rooms

Copy equal-cell frames, supplied practice and exact exit pictures on scrap paper. Large squares replace counters; plain mats replace fingers. No device.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw equal cells and use scrap squares; copy the supplied drawings and covered exit.

**Skip.** Class rotations.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Frame.** Fill five cells, then add a second row for eight as five and three.
- **7 min. Repair.** [[s02-P26-1]] **Say.** "Fix the missing dot." Expect: one dot added, eight altogether.
- **5 min. Drawing.** [[s02-G26m]] **Say.** "Repair this drawing for more." Expect: a larger lower group, such as six; five partners and extras. Put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X26]] on the unseen exit-26.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetition, retain explicit frames, all three drawing repairs and exit. Longer: spread the same four-square partners and verify supplied links again.

### Words and supports

Review: count, touch, move, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; 5-frame, day 3; group, day 6; match, day 8; line, counted, uncounted and Counted-uncounted boundary, day 12; scattered, day 13; total and Finish tick, day 19; same, day 20; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22; row, array, circle, day 25. New: 10-frame, two five-cell rows. Use enlarged or tactile pieces; record directed adult marks as motor help, quantity prompts as mathematical help. Public talk may be passed. No numerals.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 rehearses five/eight, five/three and five/five frame comparisons. If many day 26 papers or heard explanations show error B, trace and repair a supplied equal-four square link in Return and repair, verifying that wider does not mean more. Keep unheard reasons not yet seen.
