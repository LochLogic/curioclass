# Day 74: I can read what a story drawing shows.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private for exact exit stimuli; use the generated answer key for current practice look-fors. Prepare student pages, partial square corrections from item steps, counters, frames, mats, thirty covered exit-74.md half slips and record.md. Have day 73 feedback ready. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as story objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

Opening: Oral count, Return and repair, then Read drawing actions, 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 minutes.

#### 5 min. Oral count

Review count/keep track, day 1; total, day 19; Paper count-dot record, day 1. **Ask.** [[s04-K74-count]] First count together to ten, then continue from supplied word. No individual round or required counting-on strategy.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 73's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 73's tickets showed the matching action below, choose that priority. For error G, **Ask.** [[s04-F74-G]] then [[steps:s04-F74-G]]. For error E, **Ask.** [[s04-F74-E]] then [[steps:s04-F74-E]]. One choice only: five guided minutes, five fresh supplied-cue retry. More than ten sharing it: class repair; otherwise one group while peers correct familiar supplied records. No second teacher group or check replay.

#### 10 min. Read drawing actions

Hook: cover/uncover the same drawing rearranged. Introduce change: what happens to the group. **Ask.** [[s04-K74-model]] Then [[steps:s04-K74-model]]. Teach that cross-outs record removal without erasing the start. Review model/drawing, day 72; square, day 45. Compare one stage at a time, never complete candidate choices. Next restore the missing arrival on the supplied two-and-two record, four altogether. Drawings may be untidy and faithful.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; repeat with resets. **Ask.** [[s04-P74]] Then [[steps:s04-P74]]. Read the four-square removal scene before children restore its second cross-out. Point to the original group, change, then remaining marks. Supply an arranged version, cover it, then present the same incomplete record scattered. Correct one at a time; no candidate-choice sheet. Review Original whole with separate moved region, day 68. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds within this block. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; recorded judgments return day 75 Return and repair.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. First five minutes: mixed untimed within-five game, familiar one-and-three joining and five-take-two removal. **Ask.** [[s04-P74-game]] Then [[steps:s04-P74-game]]. Adult supplies starting cues; partners check immediately by matching pieces to marks. No timer or ranking. Next five minutes repeat today's partial square correction with models available; teacher observes, no extra group. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-74.md at each child's own turn. Say "Invented for practice." Read exact stimulus from private key, then **Ask.** [[s04-X74]] Read exact stem. Each child marks the matching candidate and starting/moved groups on exit-74.md. Hear a positive action match within the same stem, not an extra question. No public answer or practice frame. Hear roster 1 to 6 only: six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5.

Return pending feedback for s04-X70, s04-X71, s04-X72, s04-X73 in this same turn if fresh evidence aligns. No additional ask or old check repeated; unheard explanations stay pending. Mark only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Log names heard, access and mathematical help separately.

After class: thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 75 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters; large paper squares replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Offer high contrast, tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed adult movement/scribing. Record mathematical help separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large adult-cut paper squares, folded mats and hand-drawn frames replace class set. Hand-copy original pictures.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together through ten, then from the class's starting word.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 73 feedback with one supplied square correction.
- **7 min. Model.** [[s04-K74-model]] Act four squares losing two. Show two cross-outs, then rearrange that drawing.
  **Say.** "What do these cross-outs show?" Expect: the two squares moved away; two still remain.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-P74]] Complete supplied cue together; keep paper. One familiar game round if time remains.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X74]] Read exact new stimulus from private key, then stem. Child marks alone; keep paper and words for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated corrections; keep repair, worked connection, five-minute game and exit. Longer: rearrange the supplied incomplete drawing, then correct the same missing component.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; counter, day 3; group and mark, day 6; altogether, day 64. Read every word aloud. Accept pointing, signs and child-directed scribing; public talk may be passed. Record access separately from mathematical help. No required written labels, art detail or operation signs.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 returns day 74's exit and practice feedback. If many chose arrivals for removal, error C, trace an outgoing path with supplied squares. For error E, point to the location asked about. Then complete missing change marks. Never replay the duck card.
