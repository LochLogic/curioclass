# Day 76: I can compare what stories do.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private for exact exit stimuli; use the generated answer key for current practice look-fors. Prepare student pages, partial square corrections from item steps, counters, frames, mats, thirty covered exit-76.md half slips and record.md. Have day 75 feedback ready. Adult cuts large pieces; count in/out, no mouthing. Replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. Never move children as story objects. Read all print aloud at 18 point; no independent reading.

### The day

For every worked or repair row, read its scene first; reveal solution steps after the ask.

Opening: Oral count, Return and repair, then Contrast action cues, 5 + 10 + 10 = 25 minutes.

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count

Review count/keep track, day 1; total, day 19; Paper count-dot record, day 1. **Ask.** [[s04-K76-count]] First count together to ten, then continue from supplied word. No individual round or required counting-on strategy.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 75's exit and recorded practice feedback. If day 75's tickets showed the matching action below, choose that priority. For error E, **Ask.** [[s04-F76-E]] then [[steps:s04-F76-E]]. One choice only: five guided minutes, five fresh supplied-cue retry. More than ten sharing it: class repair; otherwise one group while peers correct familiar supplied records. No second teacher group or check replay.

#### 10 min. Contrast action cues

Review add/put together, day 64; take away/subtract/remain, day 68; change, day 74. **Ask.** [[s04-K76-model]] Then [[steps:s04-K76-model]]. Name compare through the adult-supplied contrast. Sort single incoming/outgoing arrow cues, covering each complete story before the next. No independent complete story-pair comparison or ribbons in practice.

#### 15 min. Connected work

Review Partner turns, day 1: fifteen simultaneous pairs, 45 seconds each then swap; repeat with resets. **Ask.** [[s04-P76]] Then [[steps:s04-P76]]. Fold practice-76.md to expose one cue at a time. Adult supplies the contrast sentence; child completes only its missing arrow or word. Act with counters on supplied paths: two and three make five; five losing two leaves three. Alternate single cues, never independent whole-story comparisons. Keep corrected paper. Hear at most two volunteers, 90 seconds within this block. Corrected unscored paper stays in the folder; recorded judgments return day 77 Return and repair.

#### 12 min. Centers and tidy

Review Counting centers, day 1. First five minutes: mixed untimed within-five game, familiar one-and-three joining and five-take-two removal. **Ask.** [[s04-P76-game]] Then [[steps:s04-P76-game]]. Adult supplies starting cues; partners check immediately by matching pieces to marks. No timer or ranking. Next five minutes repeat today's partial square correction with models available; teacher observes, no extra group. Last two minutes tidy and count pieces in.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First show exit-76.md at each child's own turn. Say "Invented for practice." Read exact stimulus from private key, then **Ask.** [[s04-X76]] Read exact stem. Each child marks incoming/removing arrows in the two blank panels. The roster six independently compare actions under the exact single stem. No public answer or practice frame. Hear roster 13 to 18 only: six times 45 seconds = 4.5 minutes; reading, concurrent marking and collection get 3.5.

Return pending feedback for s04-X72, s04-X73, s04-X74, s04-X75 in this same turn if fresh evidence aligns. No additional ask or old check repeated; unheard explanations stay pending. Mark only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Log names heard, access and mathematical help separately.

After class: thirty papers times twenty seconds = ten teacher marking minutes. Return exit and recorded practice feedback day 77 Return and repair.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy masters; large paper squares replace counters, folded paper replaces mats, drawn joined frames replace printing. No device needed; one shared screen may enlarge teaching only. Offer high contrast, tactile boundaries, broad pencils, usual communication aids and child-directed adult movement/scribing. Record mathematical help separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Large adult-cut paper squares, folded mats and hand-drawn frames replace class set. Hand-copy original pictures.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Count together through ten, then from the class's starting word.
- **5 min. Return.** Use day 75 feedback with one supplied square correction.
- **7 min. Model.** Read the scene before giving the question; hold back its solution. [[s04-K76-model]] Fold practice sheet. Show two-with-three-arriving, then cover before five-losing-two.
  **Say.** "More squares come what way?" Expect: in; the supplied removal sentence ends out.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s04-P76]] Complete supplied cue together; keep paper. One familiar game round if time remains.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X76]] Read exact new stimulus from private key, then stem. Child marks alone; keep paper and words for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated corrections; keep repair, worked connection, five-minute game and exit. Longer: rearrange the supplied incomplete drawing, then correct the same missing component.

### Words and supports

Review square, day 45; counter, day 3; group and mark, day 6; altogether, day 64. Read every word aloud. Accept pointing, signs and child-directed scribing; public talk may be passed. Record access separately from mathematical help. No required written labels, art detail or operation signs.

### Tomorrow

Day 77, section 4.4, returns day 76's exit and practice feedback in Return and repair. If many treat removal as joining, error C, move original squares outward on a fresh supplied five-take-two record, never the ribbon scenes. Then children complete a story question for a partner.
