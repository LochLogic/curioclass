# Stories and drawings

## The mathematics

A drawing preserves the original whole and makes the change visible. Cross-outs record moved pieces without erasing the start. A model answers the group the question asks about; moved and remaining are different referents. Neatness, artistic detail and written labels are not the mathematics. Interpret arranged and scattered drawings by action.

All totals and initial quantities stay within ten. No comparison problems, unknown-position equations, required counting on or algorithm. Both-addends-unknown work continues in unit 5; fluent within-five recall belongs to unit 8. Read all print aloud; children use pictures, objects, gestures, usual communication methods and paper marks, with no independent reading required.

Review total and altogether from day 19, zero and none from day 30, and one more from day 36. Review the day 26 model: Five occupied cells and five-and-some on joined 5-frames. Demonstrate the familiar move again when needed. Review Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair with fresh square cases. Never use an earlier reserved check picture as a model.

## Teach, hear and hand back

Practice uses supplied movement paths, already-started records or joint corrections. Its sheet is practice-DAY.md, indexed in practice-records.md. No practice independently reproduces the whole-case shape of an exit or final record. The original pictures on exit-DAY.md are reserved for their own checks, not center cards. Never display a future sheet or teacher key. Every original scene is invented for practice; say that aloud. Rin is the sole actor, moving objects, never classmates. A child may pass in public talk.

Fifteen partner pairs work at the same time, 45 seconds each then swap. Hear at most two public volunteers inside a teaching block. Each exit uses eight minutes: 3.5 reading/concurrent marking/collection and six 45-second turns. All thirty children leave their own paper marks; only six are heard. Follow record.md for roster dates and pending components. No timer or ranking.

Return paper in the next named Return and repair block, ten minutes through day 78, five on days 79 to 82. Day 83 uses existing written feedback in Finish and revise, never reteaches. More than ten showing one matching error gets one class repair; otherwise one group while others correct supplied paper. Choose one priority letter; no simultaneous extra teacher group. Return practice and exit feedback together in that allocation; one return does not create another teacher group. Do not replay a held check case with an answer supplied.

## Error letters, in practice

A: move each square once. B: enclose both groups. C: move removed pieces off before counting what stays. D: build both sides and match one to one. E: identify the location the question asks about. F: move nothing from an empty mat. G: add one and say the next number. H: retain the starting group as incoming pieces join. Use only permitted square practice cases. A wrong number alone never establishes a letter. One marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing evidence stays pending in the schedule, not a fourth score.

## Before class and access

Print this section's practice sheet and half-slip exit for each teaching day, only on its day. Read exact stems from the data; teacher-key.md supplies stimuli, keys, samples and Shape reservations. Keep that file private. Each shared child sheet is the actual paper master, with room to mark. The unit record, optional blank frames, blank mats and bilingual family note live in u04-1. Send the family note on day 64. There is no readiness test; retrieval.md is diagnostic only.

Large adult-cut paper squares replace counters, drawn joined 5-frames replace printing, and folded scrap paper replaces mats/cards. Use broad pencils, high contrast or raised boundaries, nonslip mats and child-directed adult movement/scribing. Record access separately from mathematical help. Count pieces in and out, keep them in sight, prohibit mouthing, and stop to replace unsafe pieces with drawn marks. No real beans or small pieces. No devices are required; at most one shared screen enlarges pictures. With no printer, copy the same original master by hand, without its key.
