# Day 111: I can make teens from ten ones and more.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve/fourteen movable squares, paired frames and supplied partial records. Select roster 13-18; keep exit copies covered. Bring day 110 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for the pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K111]], then [[steps:s06-K111]]. All echo and mark beats in the counting space. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: a mark records a beat, never proves unheard words. No individual round or written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 110's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper with written feedback, including written tasks s06-P110 and s06-K110. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error G on day 110's exit, point to the correct parts and supply the usual name. **Ask.** [[s06-F111]], then [[steps:s06-F111]]. Include a fresh partial retry within five minutes; use the other five for the first remaining need or familiar review. Unheard naming stays scheduled for days 115 to 118, never inferred from paper.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review compose, day 60: put parts together. Extend to teen numbers. Review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible and ten ones/further ones, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-M111]], then [[steps:s06-M111]]. Supply the full ten before children add two; count twelve altogether. Repeat with four further ones for fourteen. Children retain all ten supplied squares; counting every square remains valid. Transfer to a supplied partial drawing: **Ask.** [[s06-P111]], then [[steps:s06-P111]]. Restore only the missing further mark. Do not practice whole compositions on blank paper.

Teach writing 14 beside fourteen counted squares: **Ask.** [[s06-N111]], then [[steps:s06-N111]]. Copy it in the blank box; quantity understanding and numeral formation get separate notes. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone adds or removes squares. Read [[s06-G111-1]], [[s06-G111-2]], [[s06-G111-3]], [[s06-G111-4]] separately; children mark four game-record boxes and partners touch-check each final set.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat today's supplied partial record, movable model and numeral copy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second turns concurrently in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes; children hold models/paper, teacher records help. No fresh independent check formats in centers. Collect all practice, counting, games, model/copy and repair marks for day 112 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-111 only at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X111]] exactly; everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. The six sampled copies remain covered until heard. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep every one visible in both regions; do not require an equation. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for written exit feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for the other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten numeral cards; copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** [[s06-M111]] Supply ten separate squares; child adds two, then replaces the two with four. Count each whole.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P111]] Use its supplied partial picture.
  **Say.** "Finish this picture of twelve." Expect: one further mark added beside the supplied ten and one. Model and copy 14 beside fourteen squares.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper; rehearse a supplied name if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X111]] Read the exact stem; child draws independently on the reserved slip. Keep all individual ones visible; record help and give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, model, numeral formation and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial model with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87. Parts together make the whole. Large tactile pictures, broad pencils, signs, home-language speech, pointing and directed adult marks preserve the ask. No required fingers; children may pass in public talk. Distinguish ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 112 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. Error F, one mark replacing ten, gets touching all ten and a fresh supplied partial retry, at most five minutes. Day 112 decomposes teen numbers and connects both parts to the numeral. Keep every individual one visible.
