# Day 110: I can name teens both ways.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve/fourteen movable squares, paired frames and supplied partial records. Select roster 7-12; keep exit copies covered. Bring day 109 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for the pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K110]], then [[steps:s06-K110]]. All echo and mark beats in the counting space. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: a mark records a beat, never proves unheard words. No individual round or written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 109's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper with written feedback, including written tasks s06-P109 and s06-K109. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error F on day 109's exit, touch every occupied place before further ones. **Ask.** [[s06-F110]], then [[steps:s06-F110]]. Include a fresh partial retry within five minutes; use the other five for the first remaining need or familiar review. Correct mathematical parts without supplying an unheard name.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review ten ones, further ones and teen, day 108. Name the parts while pointing to movable squares; then children echo. **Ask.** [[s06-P110]], then [[steps:s06-P110]]. Model the entire eleven-through-nineteen sequence as its answer specifies. Change only the further ones; keep ten individual ones visible. Partners echo supplied names, never independently name a fresh unlabelled picture in practice.

For MP.6 contrast the usual name on a correct fourteen model: **Ask.** [[s06-M110]], then [[steps:s06-M110]]. Forty is spoken only; do not write it or build forty. On practice-110 children mark the parts as they echo the teacher's supplied twelve. Teach writing 13 beside thirteen counted squares: **Ask.** [[s06-N110]], then [[steps:s06-N110]]. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point. A supported echo does not establish independent naming.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone adds or removes squares. Read [[s06-G110-1]], [[s06-G110-2]], [[s06-G110-3]], [[s06-G110-4]] separately; children mark four game-record boxes and partners touch-check each final set.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat today's supplied partial record, movable model and numeral copy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second turns concurrently in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes; children hold models/paper, teacher records help. No fresh independent check formats in centers. Collect all practice, counting, games, model/copy and repair marks for day 111 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-110 only at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X110]] exactly; everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. The six sampled copies remain covered until heard. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Paper marks identify parts; they never establish unheard names. Transcribe the six children's exact two names on their slips. Unheard names remain pending in the assigned final conference roster. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for written exit feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for the other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten numeral cards; copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** [[s06-P110]] Point to ten squares and one through nine further squares while supplying each usual name; child echoes.
- **8 min. Paper.** On practice-110 point and mark while echoing the supplied twelve. Model and copy 13 beside thirteen squares.
  **Say.** "Echo twelve, ten ones and two further ones." Expect: that supported name and pointing to both parts.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper; touch all ten ones if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X110]] Read the exact stem. Child marks the parts and says both names independently; record exact words without coaching. Give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, model, numeral formation and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial model with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87. Parts together make the whole. Large tactile pictures, broad pencils, signs, home-language speech, pointing and directed adult marks preserve the ask. No required fingers; children may pass in public talk. Distinguish ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 111 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. If many show error G, the wrong usual name for correct parts, point to a model and rehearse its supplied teen name before a fresh partial retry. Unheard naming has no score inferred from marks: assigned final conferences on days 115 to 118 provide fresh evidence, never retroactive words. Day 111 composes teens from a supplied full ten.
