# Day 108: I can find ten ones in a teen.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve/fourteen movable squares, paired frames and supplied partial records. Select roster 25-30; keep exit copies covered. Bring day 107 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for the pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K108]], then [[steps:s06-K108]]. All echo and mark beats in the counting space. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: a mark records a beat, never proves unheard words. No individual round or written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-P107, s06-K107 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Return day 107's exit tickets and practice/count/game/repair paper with written feedback. Review Return and repair, day 2. Review same, day 20, and Quantity with a retained numeral across arrangements, day 31. If many, more than ten, show error I on day 107's exit, match moved squares to former places. **Ask.** [[s06-F108]], then [[steps:s06-F108]]. If tracking still needs help, move each square onto a counted place using the same supplied twelve. At most two five-minute actions, each with a fresh partial retry. Otherwise familiar review. Keep other needs pending.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97: touch ten occupied places. Today introduce teen, ten ones and further ones: eleven through nineteen contain ten individual ones and more. Present twelve loose squares in two rows of six beside paired frames. **Ask.** [[s06-M108]], then [[steps:s06-M108]]. Move the same squares, ten onto one frame, two onto the other. Count all if needed. Repeat with fourteen and four further ones; keep every one visible.

Use the supplied partial practice-108 picture: **Ask.** [[s06-P108]], then [[steps:s06-P108]]. Children restore one missing further mark, rather than partitioning a fresh picture independently. Teach writing eleven: **Ask.** [[s06-N108]], then [[steps:s06-N108]]. Model 11 beside eleven squares and copy in the blank box. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone adds or removes squares. Read [[s06-G108-1]], [[s06-G108-2]], [[s06-G108-3]], [[s06-G108-4]] separately; children mark four game-record boxes and partners touch-check each final set.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat today's supplied partial record, movable model and numeral copy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second turns concurrently in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes; children hold models/paper, teacher records help. No fresh independent check formats in centers. Collect all practice, counting, games, model/copy and repair marks for day 109 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-108 only at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X108]] exactly; everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. The six sampled copies remain covered until heard. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Accept a dictated total recorded on the slip; do not require untaught independent 17 formation. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for written exit feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for the other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten numeral cards; copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo the familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** [[s06-M108]] Move twelve squares, ten onto one frame and two onto another. Model 11 beside eleven squares.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P108]] Show its supplied partial picture.
  **Say.** "Repair this grouping of twelve." Expect: ten visible marks and two further, restoring one missing mark. Copy 11 in the spare box.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper; match moved squares to former places if needed.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X108]] Read its exact stem; child marks the reserved picture independently. Record a dictated total without supplying it; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, model, numeral formation and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial model with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87. Parts together make the whole. Large tactile pictures, broad pencils, signs, home-language speech, pointing and directed adult marks preserve the ask. No required fingers; children may pass in public talk. Distinguish ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 109 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. If many show error F, one object replacing ten, touch the ten separate ones and repair a fresh supplied partial frame. Day 109 connects paired-frame parts to numerals. Keep dictated totals separate from numeral formation.
