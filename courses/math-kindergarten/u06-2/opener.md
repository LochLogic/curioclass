# Section 6.2: Ten visible ones inside teens

Ten ones stay ten separately visible objects. Paired frames show the full group and further ones without replacing the full group by one object. Count-all is valid. Teach 11, 12, 13 and 14 on days 108, 109, 110 and 111. Day 110 uses echo-and-point models for every usual teen name and its parts; independent usual naming and a separate parts ask remain held for the exit. Practice repairs supplied twelve/fourteen pictures.

Read every printed word aloud. Children use pictures, marks, gesture, home language, speech or directed adult movement. No independent reading, required fingers or public speech. Use 18 point type and high-contrast spaced pictures. Paper squares replace counters, hand-drawn frames replace printing, handwritten cards replace printed numeral cards, pencil replaces crayons. Adult-cut pieces; supervise, and replace mouthed pieces with drawings. Provide everything in class. One optional shared screen enlarges the same paper; no device queue.

Before class, print only that day's practice and sealed exit masters. Print u06-1/counting-mats.md and game-record.md anew as needed; keep paired-frames.md and numeral-cards.md for teaching. Open this section's teacher-key.md privately. It contains all answers and models, never a child's page or display. Print the reserved final sheets only for their collection day. Send u06-1/family.md on day 104. All pictured scenes and quantities are invented for practice; say that aloud.

Re-establish earlier Counting centers, Return and repair, Start and completed-visit marks for fixed pictures and Quantity with a retained numeral across arrangements through fresh square demonstrations. Earlier ledger entries may be planned, not evidence of completed learning. Later sections reuse the u06-1 materials and teacher record. Revisit full ten before paired frames; revisit expression, equation, plus and equals with objects before day 113. Never use reserved pictures or complete independent assessment formats as a demonstration, center choice or repair.

Each teaching day has a sixty-minute budget in days.csv. The five-minute count is simultaneous echo/pointing, not thirty individual turns. Four short mixed-game asks fill five center minutes with immediate object feedback. Fifteen concurrent pairs can alternate two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes. Hear at most two public volunteers during teaching; anyone may pass. No student timer.

Every eight-minute exit is a half slip: launch 1, simultaneous paper work and six 45-second samples within 6, collect 1. Two-panel exits hear three children on each panel. All return both panels. Drawn explanations count as mathematical evidence; a mark alone never proves an unheard sequence/name. Keep formation separate from quantity understanding. Unheard spoken components stay scheduled in record.md; final conferences provide fresh domain evidence, never retroactive proof of earlier words.

Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Pending describes scheduling, not a fourth score. Preserve first performance, mathematical help and ordinary access separately. Give written feedback; revise on a later day before final judgment. Return exits next day's Return and repair, except day 118 feedback goes to day 119 revision. More than ten sharing a letter triggers one five-minute action with fresh partial retry. Maximum two actions; further needs stay pending. Never repair with a complete reserved item.

## Common errors

- F: One object replaces ten visible ones. Touch all ten before further ones.
- G: Names teen with wrong usual name. Point to model; echo teen name and parts.

For one child use count 3, model/rehearsal 6, paper work 8, repair 5, exit 8, thirty minutes. Adult alternates partner roles. Final-window products may need separate sittings; preserve independent response and later feedback/revision. On day 119 use choices 10, earlier-record discussion 5, revision 10, tidy/reflection 5.

Unit record: ../u06-1/record.md. Shared supplies and feedback page: ../u06-1/. All masters are original; no visitor, outside photograph, unopened page or unwritten text is needed. Hand drawings are the fallback.

Day 110 has two separately read paper asks on the eleven-tab slip: usual name, then two parts. Hear roster 7 to 9 for naming and 10 to 12 for parts. Everyone marks both; do not infer unheard names from written numerals.

The first five-minute part is the warm-up, named Warm-up counting (Warm-up retrieval on day 104). The opening minutes in days.csv begin after that part; keep the warm-up separate when writing the day.
