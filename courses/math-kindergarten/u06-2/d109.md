# Day 109: I can show ten ones and more.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare twelve/fourteen movable squares, paired frames and supplied partial records. Select roster 1-6; keep exit copies covered. Bring day 108 annotated exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Say "Invented for practice" for the pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s06-K109]], then [[steps:s06-K109]]. All echo and mark beats in the counting space. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: a mark records a beat, never proves unheard words. No individual round or written numerals above twenty.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 108's exit tickets and all practice/count/game/model/repair paper with written feedback, including written tasks s06-P108 and s06-K108. Review Return and repair, day 2. If many, more than ten, show error F on day 108's exit, touch all ten ones before the further ones. **Ask.** [[s06-F109]], then [[steps:s06-F109]]. Allow one five-minute repair and a fresh partial retry; use the other five minutes for the first remaining need or familiar review. Never recopy the pennant exit as practice.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Review ten ones, further ones and teen, day 108; review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible, day 108. **Ask.** [[s06-M109]], then [[steps:s06-M109]]. Build twelve together, touching all ten cells before two further squares. Link counted twelve to supplied 12. For MP.7, the two rows each contain five separate ones; the full frame shows ten because all ten places are occupied, not because its border is one object. Move the four further squares in the fourteen model without changing its total.

Contrast the erroneous one-dot representation only after the correct model. **Ask.** [[s06-F109]], then its steps. Use the supplied filled-frame repair, never a blank requested-teen frame: **Ask.** [[s06-P109]], then [[steps:s06-P109]]. Teach writing 12 beside the counted model: **Ask.** [[s06-N109]], then [[steps:s06-N109]]. Hear at most two 45-second volunteers; others point.

#### 25 min. Counting centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Five untimed minutes: review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone adds or removes squares. Read [[s06-G109-1]], [[s06-G109-2]], [[s06-G109-3]], [[s06-G109-4]] separately; children mark four game-record boxes and partners touch-check each final set.

Twenty minutes including tidy: repeat today's supplied partial record, movable model and numeral copy. Fifteen pairs exchange two 45-second turns concurrently in 1.5 minutes. Teacher hears at most six practice turns, 4.5 minutes; children hold models/paper, teacher records help. No fresh independent check formats in centers. Collect all practice, counting, games, model/copy and repair marks for day 110 feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-109 only at each child's own turn. Read [[s06-X109]] exactly; everyone marks paper or directs adult marks. The six sampled copies remain covered until heard. Budget: launch 1 minute, simultaneous marking 2, six private 45-second same-ask samples 4.5, collect 0.5. Record names/help. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep all ten individual marks visible; do not infer error F from an unexplained wrong total. After class: 30 children times 30 seconds, fifteen teacher minutes for written exit feedback; reserve ten further teacher minutes for the other paper.

### Other rooms

One shared screen may enlarge teaching. Copy masters without keys if no printer. Adult-cut squares, drawn frames and handwritten cards replace equipment. Supervise pieces; replace mouthed pieces with drawings.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large paper squares, drawn frames and handwritten numeral cards; copy the supported practice and reserved exit masters.

**Skip.** Rotations, public turns and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo familiar starts and tens; mark beats.
- **6 min. Show.** [[s06-M109]] Count ten separate squares on one frame and two on another; connect them to 12.
- **8 min. Paper.** [[s06-P109]] Use its supplied partial record.
  **Say.** "Finish the missing mark." Expect: one additional mark makes ten and two. Model and copy 12 beside twelve squares.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's paper; touch each one if the full ten was replaced by one mark.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X109]] Read the exact stem; child fills the reserved frames independently. Record help and give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; retain repair, model, numeral formation and exit. Longer: repeat a supplied partial model with the same quantities and a different missing place.

### Words and supports

Review count, day 1; numeral, day 30; part and whole, day 87. Parts together make the whole. Large tactile pictures, broad pencils, signs, home-language speech, pointing and directed adult marks preserve the ask. No required fingers; children may pass in public talk. Distinguish ordinary access from mathematical help.

### Tomorrow

Day 110 returns all today's paper in Return and repair. Error F on day 109's exit gets touching all ten and a fresh partial repair, at most five minutes. Then day 110 links every teen's usual name to ten ones and further ones. Keep unheard words pending.
