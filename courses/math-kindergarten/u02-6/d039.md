# Day 39: Pictures explain a comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Select practice-39.md through practice-records.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d039-key.html private; hide exit-39.md. Prepare 3/5 cards, blank-frames.md, counting-conference.md and conference-tabs.md. Bring day 38 papers and counting feedback. Counting roster: 26 to 30; exit roster: 1 to 6. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported oral counting toward twenty: [[s02-R33-oral]]. Review zero and quantity labels, day 30: [[s02-R39]]. Children correct the supplied empty-tray label on scrap. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point; all pictures are invented for practice.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 38 s02-X38, s02-O38, practice and counting feedback. If many day 38 responses show error I, use three/five physical groups: [[s02-K39-more]]. Connect extras to more/fewer. For error M use [[s02-F39-M]]; for unresolved error H use [[s02-F39-H]]. Choose one blocking action, whole class above ten sharing it, otherwise group while others correct familiar diagrams. Preserve original and correction. Queue other letters for day 40; never stack repair time.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review day 31 rearrangement with retained numeral: [[s02-K39]], using three squares and its adult-started sketch. Repeat representation with supplied five squares and 5; children place pieces over the matching marks on practice-39.md. Review day 21 matching: three physical partners, two extras in five. **Ask.** [[s02-K39-more]], then separately [[s02-K39-less]]. Teacher connects each spoken comparison to quantity evidence, not spacing or ink size. No new method or symbols.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Pairs build physical three/five groups from supplied cards; then annotate existing pictures. **Ask.** [[s02-P39-1]] before revealing feedback. Point to supplied links, mark the two unmatched squares, dictate a comparison. Do not request paired blank-panel drawings. Four 45-second partner turns across two rounds take three minutes concurrently; nine remain for building, annotations, adult scribing and feedback. Teacher steps back before hearing at most two volunteers. Error I: reconnect extras with more and exhausted partners with fewer. Error M: count the represented members, then correct the label. All children retain a paper trace; no independent reading.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Review Counting centers. Hear children 26 to 30: [[s02-M1a-39]], [[s02-M1b-39]], [[s02-M1c-39]], [[s02-M1d-39]], [[s02-M2a-39]]. Spread after total; indicate the opposite start before fourth prompt. Five separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. No coaching or simultaneous teacher-led repair. Others repeat supplied three/five annotations. After class, five teacher minutes give the final cohort written feedback for day 40; do not close their records before revision.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently to table trays. Collect originals and corrections separately. After class, mark thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 40.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Launch one minute: reveal exit-39.md only now. These are invented picture orders for six and eight pieces; read labels without supplying pictures. Read [[s02-X39]] exactly. Two simultaneous paper minutes: children draw each requested amount and show which has more. Six 45-second follow-ups including notes, 4.5 minutes: [[s02-O39]], roster 1 to 6. Half-minute collection. Record quantity accuracy, label links, comparison and heard proof separately with shown, shown with help, not yet seen, names and support. Six matches leave two extras in eight; a ring alone does not establish a reason. After class, thirty papers at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 40.

### Other rooms

Copy supplied practice and exact exit art by hand; large paper squares replace counters, drawn equal cells replace frames. Paper carries every response; no device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Handwrite cards and draw supplied pictures; use large scrap squares and crayons.

**Skip.** Class conferences and repeated partner rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words; connect none to zero.
- **5 min. Represent.** Build three/five under 3/5; point to matching pictures.
- **7 min. Annotate.** [[s02-P39-1]] Your child marks extras on the supplied picture. **Say.** "What proves five has more?" Expect three partners and two extras in five. Write dictated words without adding new marks.
- **5 min. Repair and tidy.** Correct one returned day 38 component; put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X39]] on the unseen slip. Your child draws and compares alone. **Say.** "What in your drawing proves it?" Expect six matches and two extra in eight. Keep original paper; offer feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated annotations, keeping response, conferences and exit. Longer: spread the same three/five pieces differently and explain why the comparison remains true.

### Words and supports

Review numeral, zero, day 30; more/fewer/extra, day 21; equal/same, days 20/22; greater/less, one more, next, day 36. Review count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Pointing, signs, tactile/enlarged pictures and directed adult movement count. Record physical help separately; never supply a numeral during a writing check. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 rehearses numeral comparisons without supplied frames and starts final comparison papers. Return day 39 exits, practice and the last counting cohort's feedback in Return and repair. If error I appears, reconnect extras and comparison words; for error M reconnect count and numeral. Close only completed mid-unit components after revision. Unheard reasons remain not yet seen.
