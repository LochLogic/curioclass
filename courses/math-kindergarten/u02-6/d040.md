# Day 40: Compare the numbers, keep the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use embedded practice art. Use d040-key.html for current F3 look-fors. Fold Side 2 to hide numeral pairs until their slot; hide exits. Keep teacher-key.md private. Student page consolidates matching-record-u.md, matching-record-e.md and numeral-comparison-record.md, with marking directions printed once. Read joining/counting prompts from this teacher page; point to each picture and its two boxes. Bring day 39 feedback. Final reason roster: 1 to 10; exit roster: 1 to 6. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Read [[s02-O40]] from heard-40.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 1 to 6: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 39 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O39. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 39 s02-X39, s02-O39, practice and counting feedback. Return the applicable written work: s02-P39-1, s02-R39, s02-K39, s02-K39-more, s02-K39-less. If many day 39 responses show error I, use [[s02-F40-I]]; error M uses [[s02-F40-M]]. Choose one action, whole class above ten sharing it, otherwise group while others correct familiar paper. Last counting cohort revises its flagged component now. Preserve originals and revisions. After class, ten teacher minutes close completed mid-unit records only; unresolved components stay not yet seen, queued for day 41.

Use five minutes for one priority repair or feedback-led revision, then five for a fresh transfer of that component. Keep originals and revisions.

#### 2 min. Rehearsal

Withdraw supplied frames; retain optional usual supports and note their use. Compare represented five/eight, [[s02-K40-more]], then [[s02-K40-less]], separately; correct equal fives with [[s02-P40-1]]. One concurrent pair round takes two 45-second turns, with thirty seconds handling. No held bare pairs.

#### 33 min. Final evidence and centers

Nine simultaneous paper minutes, one separately read prompt at a time. Reveal the two/three picture at each child's own assessment turn. [[s02-F2um]], [[s02-F2uleft]], [[s02-F2uright]] share the top picture; [[s02-F2umore]] and [[s02-F2ufewer]] use separate ring rows. Turn to the equal-four picture: [[s02-F2em]], [[s02-F2eleft]], [[s02-F2eright]], [[s02-F2esame]]. Read joining/counting prompts from this teacher page, pointing to each picture and its boxes. Reuse those prompts for the second picture; read each separately. Children join, write or ring on paper, never give thirty serial spoken answers.

Then reveal three unseen bare numeral pairs for four independent paper minutes: [[s02-F3-1]], [[s02-F3-2]], [[s02-F3-3]]. Read their shared exact stem once, pointing to each pair. No model, hint or answer shown. Cover the final pictures until their own collection; practice stays separate.

Twenty reason minutes follow, children 1 to 10 only: [[s02-F2reason-40]], then separately [[s02-F3reason-40]]. Each 45-second ask plus thirty seconds total notes/transition makes two minutes per child. Refer to retained papers; no second paper collection. Others quietly repeat familiar supplied corrections in Counting centers; no concurrent teacher-led repair.

Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component, names heard and support. Choices alone cannot prove counted sequences, methods or reasons. More/fewer rings must be distinguishable. Keep all original papers. Children 11 to 20 give reasons day 41, 21 to 30 day 42. After class, thirty F2 papers at 45 seconds and thirty F3 at fifteen seconds take thirty teacher minutes; each reason domain receives five feedback minutes. Return day 41, before later judgment.

#### 2 min. Tidy

During concurrent tray cleanup, join [[s02-R40-oral]] once as a class, up to thirty seconds. This familiar count is rehearsal, not individual sequence evidence. Then [[s02-R40]] names one more than four, chorally; no serial individual turns. 

Return pieces concurrently to table trays. Collect practice separately from final papers. After class, thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 41.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-40.md only now and read [[s02-X40]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 1 to 6, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact reserved art and consolidate pictures as on the student page. One paper sheet, two sides, plus half exit slip; optional frames are chosen supports, never supplied representations. No device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw supplied pictures and cards; use large scrap squares.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **2 min. Count.** Join familiar words; name one more than four, five.
- **3 min. Rehearse.** [[s02-P40-1]] **Say.** "How can we fix that?" Expect equal fives, same number.
- **13 min. Papers.** Point to each matching picture. Read separately: "Join the partners." "Write how many in the left group." "Write how many in the right group." Read the more/fewer directions. For equal groups, say "Ring both groups if they have the same number." Read [[s02-F3-1]] for all three bare pairs. Your child responds alone; keep both sides.
- **2 min. Reasons.** Point to their papers. **Say.** "Use counting to explain this comparison." Expect counts two/three and three has more. Separately ask for one numeral reason; expect quantity or counting order.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Put pieces away.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X40]] on the unseen exit-40.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce rehearsal repeats; preserve repair, independent papers, reasons and exit. Longer: return to familiar represented five/eight reasoning after collection.

### Words and supports

Review more/fewer, day 21; equal/same, days 20/22; numeral, day 30; greater/less, one more, day 36; ink size and unchanged quantity, day 37. Review Partner turns, Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2. Pointing, signs, tactile/enlarged pictures and directed adult movement count; record help. Scribe reasons, never checked numeral answers. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 teaches sorting objects into given categories, counts and orders categories. Return day 40 exits, practice and F2/F3 papers in Return and repair: two minutes numeral correction, three comparison repair. For error J build quantities under equal-print cards; for error I link extras and words; for error M reconnect numeral and count. Queue additional letters to day 42. Hear children 11 to 20 using retained papers, no repeat collection. Only later supported revisions change component judgments.
