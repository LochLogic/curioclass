# Order and numeral comparison: teacher answer key

Teacher only. Keep separate from student copies. These cold-solved keys supply look-fors and two samples. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component. Diagnose letters from observed actions or heard reasons.

## s02-X37

Prompt: Make the next tower with its number label.

Answer: One square added to five; six outlined, label 6.

Look for: Add the one loose square once; retain paper action. | Shape: Extend a five-square tower once and name the next quantity. No five-tower extension in practice; predict hidden additions to four/seven loose squares, then verify. Static ordered examples never become this extension worksheet.

Shown: One square added to five; six outlined, label 6. | Not yet seen: Adds one but says total stayed five. (H).

## s02-O37

Prompt: What number comes next here?

Answer: Six, one more than five.

Look for: Rotating six only, 45 seconds each; record names heard. Paper cannot prove unheard reasons.

Shown: Six, one more than five. | Not yet seen: Adds one and says five. (H).
