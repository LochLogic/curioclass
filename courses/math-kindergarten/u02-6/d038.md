# Day 38: Written numbers and the groups they name

## Teacher page

### Before class

Select practice-38.md through practice-records.md. Keep teacher-key.md and d038-key.html private; hide exit-38.md. Set out equal-print 5/8 cards, blank-frames.md, counting-conference.md and conference-tabs.md. Bring day 37 papers and counting feedback. Counting roster: 21 to 25; exit roster: 19 to 24. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review day 25 supported oral counting toward twenty: [[s02-R38-oral]]. Review day 37 next-number prediction with [[s02-R38]]. Read all print aloud at 18 point; pictures are invented for practice.

#### 8 min. Rehearsal

Review day 36 greater/less, day 21 more/fewer and day 30 quantity labels. Build five and eight squares beneath equal-print numeral cards; count once with matching marks. **Ask.** [[s02-K38]] before finishing the supplied links. Then [[s02-K38-less]]. Five matched members belong to each group; three only belong to eight. Eight comes later in the counting words and represents more; five comes earlier and represents fewer. Use blank-frames.md as needed; no new routine. Review day 37: ink size changes no quantity.

#### 7 min. Connected work

Pairs inspect practice-38.md, supplied five/eight frames. Teacher reads its puppet reason, "Eight has fewer." **Ask.** [[s02-P38-1]] before feedback. Children mark extras and dictate a correction; adults may scribe. Then [[s02-K38-equal]], using physical fives, not a bare numeral-choice grid. Four 45-second turns across two rounds take three minutes concurrently, leaving four for handling, drawing corrections and feedback. Teacher hears at most two volunteers; passing is allowed. Error I: connect extras to more, missing partners to fewer. Do not infer error I from an unexplained mark.

Use the first 2 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O38]] from heard-38.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 19 to 24: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Mid-unit response

Return day 37 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O37. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 37 s02-X37, s02-O37, practice and counting feedback. Return the applicable written work: s02-P37-1, s02-R37, s02-T37-M, s02-T37-track, s02-T37-total, s02-T37-stop. If many day 37 responses show error H, repeat a hidden addition before checking; for error M connect counted group and numeral. One five-minute repair uses completed earlier evidence, including returned M3 or tracking records, never the unassessed counting roster. Above ten sharing a letter: whole class; otherwise group while others correct their familiar paper. Five minutes of fresh transfer on the repaired component: [[s02-T38-M]], [[s02-T38-track]], [[s02-T38-total]] or [[s02-T38-stop]]. Draw its supplied case on scrap; retain original and correction. Queue other letters for day 39.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns. Hear children 21 to 25: [[s02-M1a-38]], [[s02-M1b-38]], [[s02-M1c-38]], [[s02-M1d-38]], [[s02-M2a-38]]. Spread after total; indicate opposite start before fourth prompt. Five separate 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes each, twenty total. Others repeat supplied five/eight corrections and turn in marked diagrams. No simultaneous teacher-led repair. After class, five teacher minutes give counting feedback for day 39; children 26 to 30 remain unassessed.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces to table trays; collect originals and corrections separately. After class, thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes; return day 39.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-38.md only now and read [[s02-X38]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 19 to 24, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy supplied frames and exact exit art. Paper squares, handwritten cards and folded-paper screens replace kit materials. Every task has paper; no device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the cards and practice pictures; large scrap squares replace counters.

**Skip.** Class conferences and extra repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar words and name one more than seven: eight.
- **5 min. Represent.** Build five/eight under 5/8 cards; pair members.
- **7 min. Correct.** [[s02-P38-1]] Read the wrong reason before your child marks extras. **Say.** "How would you fix that?" Expect eight has more; five has fewer, since eight has unmatched pieces. Write dictated words.
- **5 min. Repair and tidy.** Correct one returned day 37 component and put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X38]] on the unseen exit-38.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated partner rounds, retaining response, conferences and exit. Longer: rearrange the same fives and eights while preserving counted totals and labels.

### Words and supports

Review more/fewer, day 21; equal/same, days 20/22; numeral, zero, day 30; greater/less, order, one more, next, day 36. Review count, number word, keep track, Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Pointing, signs, enlarged/tactile pictures and directed adult movement count. Record help separately; adult never supplies a checked numeral. No independent reading.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 represents three/five groups and compares in words. Return day 38 exits, practice and counting feedback in Return and repair. If error I appears, link extras to more/fewer; for error M reconnect each numeral with its count. Unheard reasons stay not yet seen; later hearing never proves an earlier unheard response.
