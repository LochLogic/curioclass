# Day 37: One added piece, the next number

## Teacher page

### Before class

Select practice-37.md through practice-records.md. Hide exit-37.md; keep teacher-key.md and d037-key.html private. Prepare counting-conference.md and conference-tabs.md, separate seven/ten trays. Bring day 36 papers with feedback. Assign counting roster 16 to 20 and exit roster 19 to 24. Adult-cut large paper pieces; inspect damage, keep out of mouths. No real food or metal clips. Fingers optional.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: Oral count and retrieval

Review supported oral counting toward twenty, day 25: [[s02-R37-oral]]. Read every printed word aloud, 18 point. Say all pictures are invented for practice. Review day 36's tower order and one more with [[s02-R37]]: static two/three towers, not a five-tower extension.

#### 8 min. Model and connect

First three minutes: [[s02-P37]] in the Exit box, with 0.5 directions, two independent paper minutes and 0.5 collection. This replaces repeated practice.

Review one more and next from day 36; teach predicting while the group is hidden. Count the starting group once. **Ask.** [[s02-K37-1]] before uncovering. [[steps:s02-K37-1]] Repeat [[s02-K37-2]], predicting before verifying. The next counting word names the quantity after exactly one joins. Model loose pieces, their drawing, then written numeral. Recounting checks a prediction; note help. Teach that numeral ink size changes no quantity: [[s02-K37-size]], a single 7 card resized beside unchanged squares. No pair selection.

#### 7 min. Connected work

Pairs take two rounds, four 45-second turns, three minutes concurrently; four minutes handle pieces, drawings and feedback. Use only four-to-five and seven-to-eight hidden loose-square cases. Each child predicts on [[s02-P37-1]], then verifies. One puppet's wrong prediction: **Ask.** [[s02-F37]] before uncovering. For error H, show the added member and connect it to the next word. Partners inspect pieces together; teacher supplies feedback, never a child-facing answer card.

Use the first 2 minutes for the work above. Last five minutes:
Read [[s02-O37]] from heard-37.md, one ask only. All children mark or direct a mark on their own copy of the supplied picture; the teacher scribes the heard words. Hear roster 13 to 18: six 45-second turns, 4.5 minutes, plus thirty seconds launch/collection. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; unheard language remains not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Mid-unit response

Return day 36 written tasks and heard-response notes with written feedback, including s02-O36. Use the already funded packet action; keep originals and revisions.

Return day 36 s02-X36, s02-O36, practice and counting feedback. If many day 36 papers show error H, the model above re-establishes the change; error M needs quantity and numeral reconnected. Spend five minutes on one completed earlier component, not the unfinished roster. Use returned M3 for error M, earlier tracking for errors K/L, invariance for errors A/B/C or stopping for error D. More than ten sharing a letter: whole class; otherwise group while others correct their retained practice. Five fresh-transfer minutes: select [[s02-T37-M]], [[s02-T37-track]], [[s02-T37-total]] or [[s02-T37-stop]] for that component. Draw its supplied case on scrap; collect the correction. Queue other letters for day 38.

#### 20 min. Counting conferences and centers

Return s02-P36-1, s02-R36, s02-P36-2 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Counting centers. Hear children 16 to 20 using [[s02-M1a-37]], [[s02-M1b-37]], [[s02-M1c-37]], [[s02-M1d-37]], [[s02-M2a-37]]. Spread after total; point to opposite start before fourth prompt. Five separately read 45-second asks plus fifteen seconds notes make four minutes per child, twenty total. Observe actions, no coaching. Others repeat familiar supplied-diagram corrections, retaining paper. No concurrent teacher-led repair. After class, five teacher minutes provide counting feedback for day 38; later cohorts remain unassessed.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Return pieces concurrently to table trays. Collect original predictions and corrections separately. After class, mark thirty practice sets at thirty seconds each, fifteen teacher minutes; return day 38.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-37.md only now and read [[s02-X37]] exactly, one minute. All children mark or direct a mark on paper for two minutes. Observe the sole requested action for roster 13 to 18, six 45-second turns including notes, 4.5 minutes. Do not add a reason, numeral or sorting demand. Collect in half a minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record names and help. Give written feedback after class, fifteen teacher minutes for thirty papers. Return in the named next feedback slot; day 43 returns day 44 and revises day 45.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact exit pictures and supplied practice; folded paper hides loose squares. Optional blank-frames.md supports counting only. No device turns.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw cards and supplied pictures; scrap squares replace counters and folded paper makes the screen.

**Skip.** Class roster conferences and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Join familiar counting words.
- **5 min. Hide.** Count four squares, cover and slide one in. **Say.** "What number comes next?" Expect five; uncover to check.
- **7 min. Together.** [[s02-P37-1]] Record the prediction before uncovering; keep both attempts. **Say.** "What changed?" Expect exactly one square joined.
- **5 min. Repair and tidy.** Correct one returned day 36 component and put pieces away.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read [[s02-X37]] on the unseen exit-37.md paper. Your child completes that one action alone. Keep the paper and any help noted; give feedback afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer repeated partner rounds; keep teaching, response, conferences and exit. Longer: explain why exactly one, rather than moving pieces, changes the total.

### Words and supports

Review tower, next, one more, order, greater/less, day 36; numeral and zero, day 30; more/fewer, day 21; equal, day 22. Review count, number word, keep track, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Return and repair, day 2; counted-uncounted boundary, day 12. Usual signs, pointing, tactile pictures and directed adult movement count. Record help; a scribe never supplies a numeral on a writing check. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 compares written numerals through quantities and counting order. Return day 37 exits, practice and counting feedback in Mid-unit response. For error H predict then uncover; for error M reconnect counted total and numeral. Queue unresolved earlier letters, one five-minute repair then fresh transfer. Unheard next-number reasoning stays not yet seen.
