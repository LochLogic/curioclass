# Order and numeral comparison

One more equal unit advances to the next counting word. Predict additions to four/seven loose squares, then verify. Correct supplied five/eight comparison pictures and reasons. Numerals are compared through quantity or counting order, never ink size. Withdraw supplied frames on day 40, retaining optional usual supports and recording use. Do not rehearse held bare pairs.

Read every printed word aloud; no independent reading. Demonstrate familiar Partner turns, Counting centers, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair again if needed. Fifteen concurrent pairs can each take two 45-second turns in 1.5 minutes; at most two public volunteers, and a child may pass. Large adult-cut paper squares replace counters; hand drawings replace printing. Inspect for damage and keep pieces out of mouths. Fingers are optional. No digital tool or device turns are needed.

Before class, print only the needed day practice and exit pictures at 18 point. Adult-cut the paper pieces. Keep teacher-key.md private, never on a student page or display. Each eight-minute half-slip exit uses launch 1, simultaneous paper 2, six 45-second observations of the single requested action 4.5, collection 0.5. Everyone returns paper. Reserved task pictures appear only at their collection, not at centers.

Return feedback in the next named repair part. More than ten sharing one letter triggers one five-minute class correction with a fresh practice response; otherwise group while others correct familiar work. Queue other errors, never stack repair minutes or run a teacher-led repair during conferences. Preserve initial and supported work, feedback, revision and judgment dates. A paper mark cannot prove unheard words, reasons or error letters.

Common errors: A: Repeats sequence instead of naming total. Enclose the counted group and name its total. B: Judges quantity by spread. Move the same members; check none entered or left. C: Changes total with starting point. Mark start and visit each once. D: Continues past requested stop. Keep request visible; stop at that number. H: Does not advance after one is added. Predict, uncover, connect new total to next word. I: Uses more/fewer opposite to extras. Point to extras while saying more/fewer/same. J: Compares numeral print size. Build quantities under equal-print cards, then withdraw supports. K: Omits an object. Move each across counted-uncounted boundary once. M: Counts correctly but chooses another numeral. Count, match numeral, cover, write, check.

Days 41/42: ten two-minute F2/F3 turns each, no second paper collection. Keep written feedback after class and revision in the named parts. Fresh day 42 sorting, not day 41 practice, is scored from retained work on day 44. Forty minutes on day 44 handles bounded missing evidence only; unresolved components stay not yet seen. First-made day 44 work is revised on day 45 before judgment.

For one child, use the section plan's thirty-minute schedule. On days 19 and 20 include ten Unit 1 response minutes within that total. An adult takes both partner roles and hears this child. Split or skip a day as needed. Assessment replaces familiar work; preserve the same independent response and supports. No child-facing timer.
