# Day 58: I can describe shapes around me.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare the current practice panel, one priority repair and covered exit. Set the retained paper records needed today. Pretest unequal-size sealed tubs to feel about the same after swapping hands; pretest both directions of a child-chosen transfer between the reserved vessels. Set room-temperature water, tray and towel. Adult cuts and pours; stop at the rim and dry spills. Read child print aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W58]] Touch or move each of 8 squares once. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer. Review Paper count-dot record, day 1: mark one dot per square on scrap paper.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 57's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 57's tickets and practice feedback. If many called open drawing complete, error D, [[s03-F58-D]] with repair-58-D.md: finish four-edge boundary, mark join. Day 57 first drawings receive descriptive feedback, no score. One action only; queue others. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise small group while others revise familiar paper. Keep original/helped versions separate.

#### 12 min. Paper records and room shapes

First four minutes, actual tub visible: **Ask.** [[s03-F5-paper]] Retain the three-panel record for the child's assigned later conference; do not supply attribute names. Next six minutes: [[s03-F1-paper]] finishes retained display marks from the earlier heard cells, with no new position or naming interview. Last two minutes: [[s03-P58-room]] corrects the supplied board boundary. Launch [[s03-P58-features]] on the familiar practice hexagon before conferences; no hallway or assessment-display practice.

#### 28 min. Final conferences and centers

Review Counting centers and Partner turns, day 1. Hear children 1 to 6 only. F1/F2 naming and position records are already complete; do not ask them again. Read separately, waiting for each answer: [[s03-F5-talk-58]], [[s03-F5-talk-58-2]], [[s03-F5-talk-58-3]], [[s03-F6-weight-58]], [[s03-F6-capacity-58]].

Three attribute asks take 45 seconds each, heft 45 seconds, capacity 75 seconds, reset/record 15 seconds: 4.5 minutes per child, 27 for six. For capacity let the child choose the source and direct the adult. Narrow into wide leaves space; wide into narrow leaves water in the source. Stop at the destination rim. Child marks the real result on the retained paper; pictures alone cannot establish weight or capacity.

Other children revise yesterday's written feedback for five independent minutes, then use familiar supplied-picture corrections. No simultaneous teacher-led repair. Keep originals and supported revisions separate; write feedback in fifteen teacher minutes after class. Remaining block time is setup/collection, never extra rushed interviews.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Collect practice concurrently in table trays. Keep reserved papers separate; adult empties/dries water area and checks floor.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-58.md original hallway pennant drawing, default without hallway access. Mark pennant boundary; hear six names/features/position. **Ask.** [[s03-X58]] Exact stem aloud; response format adds no ask. Launch/concurrent paper 2.5 minutes, roster 19 to 24 six 45-second turns 4.5 minutes, collection one minute. Private teacher-key.md. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record heard date/help, unheard components pending. Thirty slips at twenty seconds: ten teacher minutes after class; five more for conferences. Return day 59 in Return and repair. No credit for unheard speech.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy exact panels/exit before class. Paper shapes/strips replace blocks/straws; folded paper replaces clay. No device needed; one shared screen can enlarge teaching. Tactile boundaries/usual communication aids, adult follows child directions; record access separately from math help. Capacity requires real cups, water/tray/towel; otherwise pending. No glass, food, tasting, small joins or body comparisons.

### At home

**Swap.** Hand-draw panels; paper shapes replace blocks. Real sealed tubs/cups, water/tray/towel; adult pours.

**Skip.** Class rotations/repeated centers.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight squares, mark dots.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 57 descriptive feedback; close fresh practice boundary.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P58-room]]
  **Say.** "Fix the room-shape sketch." Expect: closed board boundary.
- **5 min. Records.** Keep independent class papers; if absent, mark same display/tub panels once, keeping originals. Hear their separate printed prompts without coaching.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X58]] Reveal reserved hallway slip. Read exactly; child marks alone. Expect: triangle beside doorway. Record help, return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer center repetitions; preserve independent work, feedback and exit. Longer: repeat same partial correction, check joins. Never practice reserved cases.

### Words and supports

Review shape names/positions, day 45; side/corner, day 48; join, day 51; turn, day 47; length, day 51; height/weight/capacity, day 53, where used. Read all print aloud at 18 point. Public talk may be passed; no child models error. Frame: "This edge meets ___." Remove frames for checks.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 returns day 58 tickets/conference feedback in Return and repair. Error C, rejecting narrow triangle, gets fresh tracing; A/J relationship gaps queue for fixed-view repair. Then reserved F3/F6 paper/familiar partial puzzles. Unheard descriptions stay pending.
