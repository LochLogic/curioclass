# Heft and capacity administration

Teacher only. Before class pretest two unequal-size sealed tubs that feel about the same after swapping hands. Keep them separate from practice cartons and exit envelopes. Use large light objects with secured filling; no loose swallowable parts. Paper packets may replace tubs.

Pretest two fresh unmarked unbreakable vessels: the short wide one holds more. Let the child choose which to fill. An adult follows the child's directions over a tray, stops at the destination rim and dries spills or the floor immediately. If the narrow source empties into the wide destination, empty space remains; in the reverse direction, water remains in the wide source. Accept either valid procedure. Replace a failing pair before class. Household cups replace special vessels; without any real vessels, keep capacity not yet seen until a real transfer is available.

Read only one prompt at a time:

1. How do these tubs compare in weight?
2. Show how to find which holds more.

Allow 45 seconds for heft and 75 seconds for capacity, including the child's paper result marks. Three preceding tub-attribute asks take 45 seconds each. Add 15 seconds reset/record per child. Six children use 27 minutes; do not add another interview in that block. Scribe spoken words without supplying a method. Keep each child's original marks, feedback and revised record separately.
