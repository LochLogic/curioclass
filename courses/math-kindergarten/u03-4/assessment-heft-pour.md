# Heft and pour record

Teacher administration only. Before class, pretest safely sealed unequal-size tubs to feel about the same when hands swap. Keep them distinct from heavier/lighter practice cartons and exit envelopes. Pretest a full tall narrow cup emptied into an empty short wide cup: destination still has space. Do not infer either outcome from size. Adult performs the child-directed pour over tray; dry spills immediately.

Read one prompt at a time, exactly as items.csv: "How do these tubs compare in weight?" Then "Which cup holds more?" No paper marking during these two 45-second prompts; scribe the child's gesture/words in record.md. Do not supply an explanation. If apparatus fails the pretest, replace it before use; if unavailable, leave that component pending.
