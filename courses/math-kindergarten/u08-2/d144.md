# Day 144: I can show a classroom story.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-144.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q144]] [[steps:s08-Q144]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 143's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 143's tickets included one part as whole, error L, [[s08-R144]]: [[steps:s08-R144]] Otherwise meet affected children while others repair supplied boundaries. One five-minute priority; other needs pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review story, add and take away, day 68. Read [[s08-P144-1]] before showing correction: [[steps:s08-P144-1]] Rin acts alone. Children repair the supplied missing arrival, linking action to record. If many call arrival whole, enclose start plus arrival in the supplied picture. No complete blank-story demonstration.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Uncover story records now. Assign one per child: [[s08-S144-join]] or [[s08-S144-take]]. Pair opposite assignments so tomorrow each solves the other action. Keep assigned records private until then. Read assigned prompt; children independently draw or dictate a whole representation, acting it with paper pieces if chosen. Keep original marks and genuine corrections as MP.1 evidence, never manufacture mistakes. At most two private 45-second contributions explain actual changes; record only heard explanations. Give written picture feedback afterward, ten extra teacher minutes for thirty notes; revise day 145 before day 151 judgment. Record mathematical help.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G144-1]] [[s08-G144-2]] [[s08-G144-3]] [[s08-G144-4]] [[s08-G144-5]] [[s08-G144-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: first three independently revise day 143's count meaning record from its written note; remaining ten, [[s08-P144-2]] [[steps:s08-P144-2]] Partners alternate pointing to the supplied action and recording missing remainder, each on their own paper. Revisit partial caption corrections as needed, never create another whole reserved story. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. Read action: "Rin places two tiles beside six tiles." Supply no completed model or extra explanation ask. Read [[s08-X144]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 25 to 30; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 145; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters on scrap paper; use large paper pieces and crayon.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep tomorrow's feedback revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's stretch and tens; mark route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P144-1]] **Say.** "Repair Rin's partial story caption." Expect missing arrival disk and total four.
- **5 min. Repair.** Use supplied crossed-out disk model; child records three remaining, keeping moved one separate.
- **9 min. Story and game.** Read one assigned story master now; child draws without a model. Expect six joining or four remaining. Give feedback for day 145. Play supplied game cards five minutes, recording results.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X144]] Show new tile action only now; say Rin places two beside six. Read exactly. Expect joining picture of eight, with six/two distinguishable. Keep for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review story/action; Rin acts alone. Review part and whole, day 87: arrival is one part of whole.

### Tomorrow

Day 145 returns all papers. If day 144 includes only arriving two as whole, error L, repair a supplied three-and-one whole, four. Error C requires observed joining during removal; repair supplied four-remove-one record, three. Choose one priority within five Return and repair minutes. First five Connected work minutes revise original story, then solve opposite-action partner product.
