# Day 140: I can plan a book that shows how many.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-140.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q140]] [[steps:s08-Q140]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 139's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If day 139's tickets showed descending order, give descriptive feedback, error x. Error E requires actually hearing a restart at one: [[s08-R140]] [[steps:s08-R140]] If many need it, model for all; otherwise meet affected children while others trace supplied routes. One five-minute priority; other needs pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review Make picture-book pages, day 9. Read [[s08-P140-1]] before showing correction: [[steps:s08-P140-1]] Children repair their supplied partial draft. Review numeral/count matching; a recording mark stands for one ring. No whole page, shelf choice or reserved book page is demonstrated. If many omit a mark, point along each source-to-record link again.

#### 12 min. Connected work

Turn to Side 2 only now: number book pages. Read three separate prompts: [[s08-B140-empty]] [[s08-B140-blocks]] [[s08-B140-tabs]] Children independently make three small pages with each source visible; supply no count. At most two private 45-second contributions occur within these minutes; no public book models. Retain originals, give written picture feedback afterward, revise day 141 before day 151 judgment. Budget fifteen additional teacher minutes for ninety component notes, ten seconds each. These samples cannot establish every numeral/arrangement.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G140-1]] [[s08-G140-2]] [[s08-G140-3]] [[s08-G140-4]] [[s08-G140-5]] [[s08-G140-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: turn back to Side 1 for supplied partial drafts only. [[s08-P140-2]] [[steps:s08-P140-2]] Revisit the ring omission on Side 1, linking each source once. Record three game marks and a fresh numeral label on paper. Give feedback; no reserved book/shelf model. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. The tall outlines are boxes; the low outline is an empty ball tray. Any one collection is valid. Read [[s08-X140]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 1 to 6; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 141; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw pictures on scrap paper; large paper squares replace counters, crayon replaces pencil.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback/revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's stretch and tens; touch route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** Point from each source ring to its supplied recording mark.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s08-P140-1]] **Say.** "Finish this supplied partial draft." Expect one added mark, six recorded rings.
- **9 min. Book and game.** Child independently makes Side 2 pages, sources visible; expect 0, 4 and 14. Give picture feedback for tomorrow. Play supplied game cards five minutes, recording results.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X140]] Read exactly; child marks the unseen shelf plan. Expect a matching collection sketch and 12, 2 or 0. Keep for day 141 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. 

### Tomorrow

Day 141 returns all papers. Error I is a correct teen count with reversed digits; trace correct 16 privately, then form a fresh label in Return and repair. Never display a reversed numeral. Five Connected work minutes revise all three book pages, then regroup the retained teen page with ten actual ones visible.
