# Day 143: I can plan what to count.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-143.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q143]] [[steps:s08-Q143]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 142's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 142's tickets or observations show error G, use first five: [[s08-R143-G]] [[steps:s08-R143-G]] Error H gets separate second five: [[s08-R143-H]] [[steps:s08-R143-H]] Only observed reasons earn letters; otherwise describe the trace. Others repeat isolated label corrections. Other needs pending.

#### 7 min. Model

Review part and whole, day 87. Read [[s08-P143-1]] before correcting: [[steps:s08-P143-1]] Teacher supplies the object-selection plan; children complete its boundary. Four plus four is eight. If many include only one part, trace the supplied open boundary toward the second group. This corrects a plan; it never independently chooses what objects a new scene requires.

#### 10 min. Connected work

Children choose which supplied classroom puzzle plan to repair first. [[s08-P143-2]] [[steps:s08-P143-2]] Everyone marks both already selected groups and the total. At most two 45-second contributions explain that six counts all rings. First six minutes cover these partial repairs, including contributions. Last four: uncover count meaning record, read its two shared plans aloud, then [[s08-A143-meaning]]. Children choose one and independently make its whole-count picture/label. Give written feedback afterward, ten teacher minutes; record on the retained-work sheet as Count meaning. Revise day 144 before later judgment. Paper pictures explain counted parts; never infer unheard speech.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G143-1]] [[s08-G143-2]] [[s08-G143-3]] [[s08-G143-4]] [[s08-G143-5]] [[s08-G143-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: first three independently revise day 142's number clue record from written feedback; remaining ten, parallel partner turns on supplied partial plans. One points along a supplied route, the other repairs missing link or total, then swap; each keeps their own paper. Teacher gives feedback on both parts, never shows the reserved pencil-cup case. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. Read context: "We want the number of pencils altogether." Require no extra numerical answer. Read [[s08-X143]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 19 to 24; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 144; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw pictures; clean box and paper square replace shape models, paper pieces replace counters.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's stretch and tens; mark route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** Trace a box face; feel the whole box's depth. Turn one square tracing, keeping its label.
- **5 min. Repair.** [[s08-P143-1]] **Say.** "Complete the supplied object-selection plan." Expect both groups of four included, eight squares.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Child repairs ring total, six. Uncover [[s08-A143-meaning]]; expect eight rings or ten triangles, matching the chosen whole plan. Give feedback for day 144. Play supplied game cards five minutes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X143]] Show new rack only now. Say, "We want the number of pencils altogether," then read exactly. Expect all pencils in both cups marked, no cup counted as pencil; no extra total required.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review part and whole, day 87: the whole count includes every selected part.

### Tomorrow

Day 144 returns all papers; the count meaning record gets three independent center revision minutes before later judgment. If day 143 includes only one cup as whole, error L, use one five-minute supplied three-and-three boundary repair, six altogether. Other needs stay pending. Then repair a partial story caption before independently making the assigned classroom story record.
