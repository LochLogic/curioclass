# Day 141: I can show ten ones and more in my book.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-141.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q141]] [[steps:s08-Q141]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 140's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 140's tickets showed correct counts with reversed teen digits, error I, [[s08-R141]]: [[steps:s08-R141]] Otherwise meet affected children while others trace correct supplied labels. Keep book revision for Connected work; other repairs pending.

#### 10 min. Model

Review teen, ten ones and further ones, day 108; Review Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible. Read [[s08-P141-1]] before correcting: [[steps:s08-P141-1]] Children repair on paper. If many retain one-token tens, unfold ten distinct paper pieces beside the failed model. Every one remains visible. Never model the retained fourteen-page decomposition or a blank target cover.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First five: independently revise day 140's three number book pages from written feedback; retain originals and help notes. Next seven: uncover the retained tab page, read [[s08-B141-teen]]. Children regroup their own fourteen marks into ten ones/further ones, with part/total labels or equation, without a supplied solution. Give new written feedback after class, three revision minutes day 142, judgment day 151. Allow ten extra teacher minutes for thirty page notes. Hear at most two private 45-second contributions; record only explanations actually heard.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G141-1]] [[s08-G141-2]] [[s08-G141-3]] [[s08-G141-4]] [[s08-G141-5]] [[s08-G141-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking. Use the shared nonzero-addend cards `s08-G135-7` `s08-G135-8` `s08-G135-9` `s08-G135-10` from game-addition-cards.md in the same three rounds: two additions and one removal per child. Open section 8.1's private packet for their supplied-picture keys. These fixed cards return for rehearsal, with no extra rounds.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: turn back to Side 1. [[s08-P141-2]] [[steps:s08-P141-2]] Children complete the separate total label, linking each individual piece. Revisit the supplied seventeen correction as needed. Optional frames organize pieces without replacing ten ones by one token. Give feedback on paper records. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. Say the target is nineteen paper tabs; the cover is blank, with no supplied ten. Read [[s08-X141]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 7 to 12; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 142; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw frames and pictures; use large paper squares and scrap booklet pages.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep tomorrow's revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo the short stretch and tens; touch route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P141-1]] **Say.** "Repair this supplied seventeen model." Expect ten actual ones and two more; show every piece.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return yesterday's book notes. Child revises original pages, keeping first marks.
- **9 min. Book and game.** Child groups retained tab page into ten ones/further ones; expect ten and four, fourteen. Give feedback for day 142. Play supplied game cards five minutes, recording results.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X141]] Say target nineteen paper tabs; read exactly. Child works on unseen blank cover. Expect ten individual marks and nine further, with total 19 and part labels or equation.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review teen, ten ones and further ones through supplied seventeen; part labels name actual counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 142 returns all papers. If day 141 shows one token for ten actual ones, error F, use one supplied-twelve ungrouping repair in Return and repair. New book additions get three independent revision minutes in Connected number work. Day 136 geometry feedback gets separate three-minute revision in Geometry revision centers.
