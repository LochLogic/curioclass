# Mathematics in our classroom

Review teen, ten ones, further ones, flat, solid, face, side, corner, part, whole, add, take away and zero with objects. Paired frames keep ten individual ones visible. Whole solid beside its pictured object and traced flat face distinguishes a face from the whole solid. Equal quantities linked to both equation orders connects action to a completed equation; never an unknown-position equation.

Practice repairs partial drafts, labels and captions. Book and story products are independent, retained originals, not practice models. Use book-empty.md, book-blocks.md and book-tabs.md only when collecting day 140 evidence; book-teen.md for the retained page day 141. Do not demonstrate those pages or cases. The teen practice model is twelve, distinct from the retained book. Day 141 exit is a new nineteen-tab book cover, replacing earlier seventeen-foldout reuse.

Keep actual solids beside matching pictures. A turned square remains square; a rectangular box has depth although a face is flat. Day 142 has twenty geometry minutes, including day 136 feedback revision. Each story has Rin as sole actor; day 144 one assigned action, day 145 opposite action. One ask can produce one whole representation; no extra required explanation. Retain genuine MP.1 repair, never fabricate a mismatch.

Supplies: scrap booklets, large paper pieces, pencil, clean box/paper ball/cone, matching original drawings. Use daily practice/exit masters, story masters and section 8.1 record/feedback/game/frames. Seated tabletop trays replace any school walk. No home or family survey.

Read every printed word aloud. No independent reading is required. Print pictures at 18 point with thick boundaries. Offer large tactile marks, directed adult marks, usual communication aids and home-language reasoning. Record access support separately from mathematical help. A child may pass in public talk.

Before class, open teacher-key.md privately, print only today's partial practice and sealed evidence master, and put unused reserved masters away. The key is never a student handout. All pictures and quantities are invented for practice; say so aloud. Real objects are clean, large and handled low on the table. Adult cuts paper. No small loose pieces, sharp joins, unstable stacks or required device.

Use Counting centers, Partner turns, Paper count-dot record and Return and repair with the ledger's wording. Demonstrate intended review again when earlier teaching is absent. A planned ledger entry is not evidence of learning. Ten means ten actual individual ones, never one counter representing ten. Optional 5-frame and 10-frame connect objects and pictures; supplied frames withdraw day 149, child choice remains with support recorded.

Every child marks each exit paper. On days 135 to 145, three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and half a minute collection fit eight minutes. Rotate six names across five days. On days 146 to 151, at most two 45-second observations are embedded in five paper minutes; these samples never require every child to speak. Paper evidence suffices only for its named look-fors. Never infer a matching strategy, sequence or explanation from a mark.

Use one marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep separate descriptive strategy notes, names heard, access and mathematical help. Counting all can be accurate and is not an error; it does not by itself establish fluent arithmetic. Missing components stay not yet seen with a pending note. Return specific written picture feedback; revise on the named later day before judging a product. No repeated secure check.

The daily untimed mixed game uses game-cards.md from section 8.1: three alternating 45-second maker/checker rounds plus tidy fit five minutes. Every child marks paper and receives immediate feedback. Rehearse the supplied fact images, not the reserved expressions. The oral count is teacher-started/echoed short stretches and tens, not individual full recitals. Written numerals stop at twenty. No hundred chart or number line.

Paper and objects provide the full path. Draw these original masters by hand if there is no printer. One optional shared screen only enlarges instruction. At home, adult is partner, with the section plan's thirty-minute schedule and the same sealed exit. Use only supplied partial practice and the permitted game. Never rehearse a later evidence case.


Expected errors: C joins during removal; I reverses teen digits; F treats ten as one object; G confuses whole and face; H rejects turned shape; L part treated as whole. Anticipated examples and fresh repair routes are in the private key/data, not claims about prevalence.
