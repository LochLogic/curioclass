# Day 145: I can solve a partner story.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open key-teacher.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-145.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q145]] [[steps:s08-Q145]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 144's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 144's tickets called arriving part whole, error L, [[s08-R145-L]]: [[steps:s08-R145-L]] For observed joining during removal, error C, use [[s08-R145-C]]: [[steps:s08-R145-C]] Choose one priority in five minutes; other needs pending. Save original-story revision for Connected work.

#### 10 min. Model

Review Equal quantities linked to both equation orders, day 78. Read [[s08-P145-1]] before correcting: [[steps:s08-P145-1]] Then [[s08-P145-2]]: [[steps:s08-P145-2]] Children mark corrected total. If many move the operation when total moves, touch the same four pieces before either complete equation. Partial repairs only; no whole partner-story solution model.

#### 12 min. Connected work

First five: revise day 144's assigned story records from written feedback; retain originals and actual changes. Next seven: partner points to their revised opposite-action record as teacher reads its prepared master: [[s08-S145-join]] or [[s08-S145-take]]. Child independently makes a solution. Use prepared text if partner record is unclear, never transfer wrong quantities. At most two private 45-second contributors explain actual repairs, retained as MP.1 notes; other evidence is paper. Give new products written feedback afterward, ten extra teacher minutes; day 146 gives three independent center revision minutes before later judgment.

#### 20 min. Counting centers

First five: Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G145-1]] [[s08-G145-2]] [[s08-G145-3]] [[s08-G145-4]] [[s08-G145-5]] [[s08-G145-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking. Use the shared nonzero-addend cards `s08-G135-7` `s08-G135-8` `s08-G135-9` `s08-G135-10` from game-addition-cards.md in the same three rounds: two additions and one removal per child. Open section 8.1's private packet for their supplied-picture keys. These fixed cards return for rehearsal, with no extra rounds.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.

Thirteen minutes: supplied partial partner-solution repairs on Side 1, pointer/recorder swap every 45 seconds; each retains own paper. Link the same objects to completed equations in either order. Teacher gives feedback, no new whole-story assessment. Two minutes tidy.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. The picture begins before storage; the child decides which three move. Keep displayed/stored regions distinct. Read [[s08-X145]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 1 to 6; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 146; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters; use paper strips, large paper counters and crayon.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep later feedback/revision.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's stretch and tens; mark route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P145-1]] **Say.** "Correct Rin's supplied partner solution." Expect four, starting three plus arriving one.
- **5 min. Revise.** Child follows yesterday's written feedback; preserve original story marks and genuine changes.
- **9 min. Partner and game.** Adult reads opposite-action story master; child makes a solution alone. Expect six joining or four remaining. Give feedback for day 146. Play supplied game cards five minutes, recording results.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X145]] Show unseen flags picture; read exactly. Expect five displayed after three of eight stored; optional 8 - 3 = 5. Keep for day 146.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review expression, equation and =, day 78: both sides show same amount. Review part and whole with supplied model.

### Tomorrow

Day 146 returns all papers in Return and repair. If day 145 shows stored flags joined rather than removed, error C, repair a fresh supplied four-disk/one-stored partial record, three remaining, within five minutes. New partner story gets three independent revision minutes during unassigned Counting observations center time, not teacher conference minutes. Next explicitly connect the ones/tens decade pattern; an echo cannot establish a full independent count.
