# Day 142: I can use number and shape clues.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open teacher-key.md privately for exits; use today's teacher-only answer key for other rows. Print corrected partial practice from the student page and the sealed exit from exit-142.md. Lay out listed materials. Cover evidence until each child's task. Read all print aloud; say "Invented for practice." Choose six observation names. Prepare fresh repairs from item steps.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Whole class: Review Oral tens to one hundred and Echo supplied start then continue, day 104. [[s08-Q142]] [[steps:s08-Q142]] Children mark a paper route, no independent full recital. If a word is missed, echo that short stretch again.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 141's exit and all written tasks: practice, game, count-route and repair papers. Review Return and repair. If many of day 141's tickets substituted one token for ten, error F, [[s08-R142]]: [[steps:s08-R142]] Otherwise meet affected children while others repair supplied twelve records. Other needs pending.

#### 10 min. Geometry model

Review Whole solid beside its pictured object and traced flat face, day 126. Read [[s08-P142-1]] before correcting: [[steps:s08-P142-1]] Then [[s08-P142-2]]: [[steps:s08-P142-2]] Children mark isolated labels, never select from mixed cards. If many confuse face/whole, feel depth before tracing again. Review features with actual models, not paper thickness.

#### 12 min. Connected number work

First three: independently revise day 141's number book pages from written feedback, preserving first marks. Next six: [[s08-P142-3]] [[steps:s08-P142-3]] All record on paper; at most two 45-second contributions are embedded. The numeral matches objects counted, not the number of sides. If many omit visits, redraw one link at a time. Last three: uncover the number clue record only now, read [[s08-A142-number]]; all independently mark a row. Its two rows are loops, not geometric-feature choices. Give written feedback afterward, ten teacher minutes; record on the retained-work sheet as Number clue. Revise day 143 before later judgment. No extra oral turn.

#### 20 min. Geometry revision centers

First ten geometry minutes: three independent minutes revise day 136's unit 7 priority transfer from written feedback, one assigned route only. Actual box/ball/cone stay available as its route requires. Judge that earlier transfer afterward, preserving support notes. Seven minutes repair isolated feature labels on Side 1, one tracing turned at a time. No combined selection tray. Model plus center geometry totals twenty minutes.

Other ten minutes: five game, three supplied number-link repair, two tidy. Review Counting centers, Partner turns and Paper count-dot record, day 1; Review Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, day 71. Fifteen pairs work simultaneously: three 90-second rounds, maker/checker swap after 45 seconds, then thirty seconds tidy. Read separate cards: [[s08-G142-1]] [[s08-G142-2]] [[s08-G142-3]] [[s08-G142-4]] [[s08-G142-5]] [[s08-G142-6]] Each child records three results in picture boxes; teacher gives feedback, objects optional. No speed ranking.

Check: Match each result to its supplied picture, with teacher feedback.
Keep number and geometry paper evidence separate. After class allow ten additional teacher minutes to judge thirty revised earlier transfer records, twenty seconds each.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Hand out today's sealed half slip only now. Actual cube, elongated box and paper ball sit beside matching pictures. Identify matching objects without classifying them. Trace the represented shapes, not the sheet thickness. Read [[s08-X142]] exactly. Every child marks paper or directs an adult mark. Three independent minutes, six optional 45-second observations and thirty seconds collection fit eight. Today's six are roster 13 to 18; record actual names heard, never infer unheard reasoning. No added ask or strategy cue.

Review Written component feedback before later revision and score, day 115. After class allow fifteen teacher minutes for exits, thirty seconds each, plus fifteen for practice packets. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access and mathematical help separately. Return every paper day 143; retain originals and revisions.

### Other rooms

Hand-draw masters and use large paper pieces. Paper provides the complete path. One optional shared screen enlarges instruction only; no device turns. Adult marks follow child direction, with access noted separately.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw masters; a clean folded-paper box replaces cube, large paper squares replace counters.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports; keep earlier feedback.

- **3 min. Count.** Echo today's stretch and tens; mark route boxes.
- **5 min. Show.** [[s08-P142-1]] **Say.** "Correct the supplied whole-object label." Expect solid for cube; traced face is flat.
- **5 min. Revise.** Child uses written notes to revise new teen page and retained day 136 shape record; preserve originals.
- **9 min. Paper and game.** Turn the single square tracing, retaining its label. Correct supplied twelve-square count. Uncover [[s08-A142-number]] only now; expect the fifteen-loop row marked. Give feedback for day 143. Play supplied game cards five minutes.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s08-X142]] Show only now, actual models beside matching pictures; read exactly. Expect traces around both squares only, including the turned one. Keep for day 143 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: remove five unassessed center-repeat minutes, preserving repair, game, revision and exit. Longer: show the same practice quantity/action in a different drawing, within the limits.

### Words and supports

Review count, object, number word, counter, group, match, mark and keep track with objects and records. Review numeral and zero, day 30; add, take away, +, - and = in the game, day 78. Review 5-frame and 10-frame, day 26; every counter means one. Read aloud at 18 point. Accept signs, home language, tactile outlines and directed adult marks. Public talk may be passed; judge mathematics, not handwriting. Review flat, solid, square, triangle, rectangle, cube, face, side and corner, days 45, 48 and 127. Turn/size do not change square features.

### Tomorrow

Day 143 returns all papers, including the number clue record for three independent center revision minutes. Error G, whole cube called flat because of face, gets five Return and repair minutes tracing a face and feeling a fresh box. Error H, actually rejecting square because it turned, gets separate five-minute rotation repair. Never infer reasons from wrong traces. Next complete supplied classroom counting plans.
