# Day 101: I can explain ten in a tower.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare supplied records and ten large squares per pair. Keep key-teacher.md/generated key private and exit-101.md covered. Bring day 100 annotated packets, record and feedback. Read all print, labels/equations aloud at 18 point or larger. Adult cuts pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings. Use assessment paper as student page; counting, game spaces and supplied correction are included.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, unit 1 day 1, and total, unit 2 day 19. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount101]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount101]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all mark together in the first response box. Numbered boxes hold game marks. A paper mark does not establish an unheard spoken sequence.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return s05-P100, s05-G100-1, s05-G100-2, s05-G100-3, s05-G100-4 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 100 annotated exit, practice, counting, repair and game papers. If day 100 tickets show error E, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-FE101]] then [[steps:s05-FE101]]. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group in this slot while others correct familiar records. Further needs remain pending.

#### 7 min. Rehearsal

Day 100 checked every starting partner. Review tower, unit 2 day 36; Full-ten occupied-empty model, day 97; equation/equals, unit 4 day 78. **Ask.** [[s05-Ktower101]] then [[steps:s05-Ktower101]]. Withdraw supplied practice frames; optional access remains. Review Close retained observation records, unit 1 day 18: [[s05-Kclose101]], then its steps. Practice uses familiar supplied paper, never evidence products. At most two 45-second contributions.

#### 7 min. Feedback revision

Return day 100 daily packets and F1/F3 paper, including day 99 F1 panels, with written picture feedback. Children independently revise on feedback.md; keep originals visible. Review feedback routine, no new content or score.

#### 10 min. Partner sheet

Issue partner-sheet.md once. Read [[s05-F2-1]], [[s05-F2-2]], [[s05-F2-3]], [[s05-F2-4]], [[s05-F2-5]], [[s05-F2-6]], [[s05-F2-7]], [[s05-F2-8]], [[s05-F2-9]], separately. All children mark independently. Frames here are item content, not a practice support. No hints or simultaneous conferences; collect all nine rows.

#### 18 min. Conferences and centers

Assigned roster 1-5 only: **Ask.** [[s05-C101-F1]], [[s05-C101-F2]], [[s05-C101-F3]], separately, 45 seconds each, using that child's retained products. Record initial words/help per ask; never repeat secure evidence. Children respond on retained paper with arrows or directed adult marks; usual speech or pointing may accompany the marks. Others independently revise earlier written feedback, then check familiar supplied records. Teacher retains component records; children hold paper/models. Five children take 11.25 teacher minutes; 6.75 cover transitions/access/tidy. Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G101-1]], [[s05-G101-2]], [[s05-G101-3]], [[s05-G101-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. **Ask.** [[s05-P101]] on complete supplied tower during independent work.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-101.md only now. Read shared line/labels neutrally, then exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X101]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 25-30 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper, including directed adult marks. No hints or extra asks. Record help/heard names; unheard reasons stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Private key only; a wrong numeral alone proves no letter.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers and marked strips replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Use large squares, drawn frames, marked strips and folded covers. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **2 min. Rehearse.** Use practice-101.md, all tower pieces visible.
  **Say.** "Repair the claim beside this complete tower split." Expect: four and six make ten.
- **7 min. Feedback.** Return earlier written notes. If yesterday's counting started at zero, repair that first for five minutes; revise F1/F3 with remaining time. Keep further revision pending for day 102.
- **10 min. Paper.** Read each F2 row separately; child marks alone. Keep for feedback.
- **3 min. Conversation.** If assigned today, three retained-product asks, 45 seconds each. Never repeat secure evidence.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X101]] Read fresh slip; child marks alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87; equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 102 corrects fruit fragments. Error D gets five Return and repair minutes; first unmet F2 component gets second five. Return F2 and conference feedback for independent revision during Conferences and centers. Other needs wait for days 104/105. Sixty-five after-class teacher minutes: thirty F2 feedback, five conference feedback, thirty daily-paper feedback. Return day 102; no new-work scores.
