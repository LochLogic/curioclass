# Day 102: I can change parts of ten in a story.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare supplied records and ten large squares per pair. Keep key-teacher.md/generated key private and exit-102.md covered. Bring day 101 annotated packets, record and feedback. Read all print, labels/equations aloud at 18 point or larger. Adult cuts pieces; replace mouthed/damaged pieces with drawings.

### The day

#### 5 min. Counting

Review count, unit 1 day 1, and total, unit 2 day 19. **Ask.** [[s05-Kcount102]] then [[steps:s05-Kcount102]]. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1: all mark together in the first response box. Numbered boxes hold game marks. A paper mark does not establish an unheard spoken sequence.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s05-P101, s05-G101-1, s05-G101-2, s05-G101-3, s05-G101-4 with written feedback in this existing slot. Keep initial marks beside the revision; use one priority repair within the stated minutes.

Review Return and repair, unit 1 day 2. Return day 101 annotated exit, practice, counting, repair and game papers. If day 101 tickets show error D, first five minutes: **Ask.** [[s05-FD102]] then [[steps:s05-FD102]]. Second five: older error E, [[s05-FE102]] with fresh supplied retry, only if needed. More than ten of thirty means class repair; otherwise small group in this slot while others correct familiar records. Further needs remain pending. Second action addresses only first unmet F2 component from day 101. Choose D/E by observed action; otherwise touch-check preserved given pieces and record the mismatch descriptively, with no inferred letter.

#### 7 min. Story work

Day 101 related a complete tower to ten; today apply changed parts to pictures. Review story/retell, unit 4 days 68/70. **Ask.** [[s05-P102]] then [[steps:s05-P102]]; [[s05-Kfruit102]] then [[steps:s05-Kfruit102]]. Use only Rin apple/banana fragments. For an assigned need choose at most one of [[s05-FA102]], [[s05-FC102]] or [[s05-FF102]] with their steps as supplied contrasts, not extra repair groups. At most two 45-second contributions. No whole-story invention in practice.

#### 30 min. Conferences and centers

Assigned roster 6-15 only: **Ask.** [[s05-C102-F1]], [[s05-C102-F2]], [[s05-C102-F3]], separately, 45 seconds each, using that child's retained products. Record initial words/help per ask; never repeat secure evidence. Children respond on retained paper with arrows or directed adult marks; usual speech or pointing may accompany the marks. Others independently revise earlier written feedback, then check familiar supplied records. Teacher retains component records; children hold paper/models. Ten children take 22.5 teacher minutes; 7.5 cover transitions/access/tidy. Everyone has at least five independent minutes revising day 101 F2 feedback; roster 1-5 also revise conference arrows. Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G102-1]], [[s05-G102-2]], [[s05-G102-3]], [[s05-G102-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. Then correct familiar fruit fragments and return all paper.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Issue exit-102.md only now. Read shared line/labels neutrally, then exact stem: **Ask.** [[s05-X102]]. Launch/read one minute, simultaneous paper two, six roster 1-6 same-ask turns at 45 seconds each, collection half a minute. All thirty mark paper, including directed adult marks. No hints or extra asks. Record help/heard names; unheard reasons stay pending. Scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Private key only; a wrong numeral alone proves no letter. Complete labeled picture communicates the mathematical story; never infer exact unheard wording.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares, drawn frames and folded covers replace counters, cubes and cups. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares.
- **5 min. Story.** [[s05-P102]] Read Rin's supplied fragment.
  **Say.** "Repair the total in this fruit-story fragment." Expect: ten fruit pictures.
- **9 min. Paper.** Revise F2 and earlier feedback. If assigned today, give one three-ask conversation using retained products, 45 seconds each. Never repeat secure evidence.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct first unmet supplied record.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X102]] Read fresh slip; child makes a complete labeled picture story independently. Keep for feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part, whole, pair, split, empty and repeat, day 87; equation, equals and plus, unit 4 day 78. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 103 closes retained records. Error B, repeating labeled counts, receives written picture feedback and independent Feedback revision, no teaching. Targeted response waits for days 104/105 Centers, one first unmet component per child. Forty after-class teacher minutes: ten conference feedback, thirty daily-paper feedback. Return day 103; revise before judgment.
