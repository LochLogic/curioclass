# Day 103: I can explain what my record shows.

## Teacher page

### Before class

Bring day 102 annotated packets, retained F1/F2/F3 and earlier unit 4 evidence. Keep key-teacher.md private. Select roster 16-30 only. Read all print aloud at 18 point. No new exit. Set familiar supplied centers and feedback paper.

### The day

#### 40 min. Conferences and independent centers

Assigned roster 16-30 only: **Ask.** [[s05-C103-F1]], [[s05-C103-F2]], [[s05-C103-F3]], separately, 45 seconds each, using that child's retained products. Record initial words/help per ask; never repeat secure evidence. Children respond on retained paper with arrows or directed adult marks; usual speech or pointing may accompany the marks. Others independently revise earlier written feedback, then check familiar supplied records. Teacher retains component records; children hold paper/models. Fifteen children take 33.75 teacher minutes; 6.25 cover transitions/access/tidy. Others have at least ten independent minutes revising day 102 and earlier feedback. Review Counting centers, unit 1 day 1, and Mixed untimed within-five supplied-record game, unit 4 day 71. Five simultaneous minutes: game-cards.md, **Ask.** [[s05-G103-1]], [[s05-G103-2]], [[s05-G103-3]], [[s05-G103-4]]. Say "Invented for practice." Rin alone joins/removes squares. Partners alternate maker/checker, touch-check immediately and leave quantity marks in four numbered spaces. No timer or individual teacher round. No reteaching/new scored task.

#### 10 min. Interpret existing products

Review Close retained observation records, unit 1 day 18. Use existing feedback annotations; unresolved judgments continue after class. Teacher reviews F1 four minutes, F2 three, F3 three. Judge only earlier products after written feedback and later revision. Carry secure unit 4 joining/removal evidence. Use owner look-fors; missing/first-heard-today components stay pending. Children continue familiar independent paper centers.

#### 5 min. Feedback revision

Return day 102 exit, practice, counting, repair/game packets and conference feedback. Everyone revises independently from picture notes, preserving original marks. No teaching or repeated conference.

#### 5 min. Counting and synthesis

First response box records counting; numbered boxes record games. Familiar simultaneous counting three minutes: [[s05-Kcount103]], then steps. Review Paper count-dot record, unit 1 day 1, count and total, units 1/2. Two minutes tidy; at most two 45-second volunteer reflections. No new exit.

### Other rooms

Draw identical supplied records and reserved exit pictures if no printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching; children keep paper. Large squares, drawn frames, folded covers replace equipment.

### At home

**Swap.** Large squares, drawn frames and folded covers replace counters, cubes and cups. Copy pictures if no printer; point at paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated rounds.

- **5 min. Familiar work.** Previously checked supplied records.
- **3 min. Conversation.** Only if assigned: three retained-product asks separately, 45 seconds each. Never repeat secure evidence.
- **7 min. Interpret.** Adult reads earlier products; child continues familiar work. Judge only after earlier feedback/revision.
- **10 min. Revise.** Return yesterday's picture feedback; child revises independently.
  **Say.** "Follow the picture note to revise your paper." Expect: indicated quantity or arrow changes; originals remain.
- **5 min. Synthesis.** Count/tidy. Give new explanation feedback afterward, revise day 104, judge later. No new exit.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce familiar repetitions, preserving evidence, feedback revision and repair. Longer: repeat a familiar supplied-record correction within ten; no new check or larger numbers.

### Words and supports

Review part/whole, day 87; partner of ten, day 97. Tactile outlines, pattern contrast, broad crayons, signs, pointing or directed adult marks preserve quantities/ask. Public speech may be passed; no required fingers, child cutting or real food.

### Tomorrow

Day 104 begins teen numbers with ten Centers minutes for first unmet unit 5 component: model/revise prior paper. Day 105 gives one assigned fresh unscored transfer in ten Centers minutes. Errors A omission, B repeated counts, C total-left rejection, D repeated given part, E zero start, F double assignment route by first unmet component. Other needs stay pending. After class: thirty teacher minutes finish eligible judgments, fifteen give new conference feedback. First-heard-today work revises day 104; judge day 105 or later, never today.
