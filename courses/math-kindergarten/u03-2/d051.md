# Day 51: Sides, corners and joins

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Select practice-51.md through u03-2/practice-records.md. Set flat shape cards, repair cards (repair-51-C.md), large paper squares, paper and broad pencils, strings and paper strips of varied lengths. Wide strips/paper-joins.md replace blunt straws and modeling clay. Cut matching side strips and square-corner overlays. Keep exit-51.md covered. Read child print aloud at 18 point; adult cuts, no small joins or mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W51]] Touch five squares once each; the last word gives the total. Count aloud to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 50's tickets and practice feedback. If many rejected its thin triangle, [[s03-F51-C]] on repair-51-C.md supplies one fresh labeled-boundary correction, error C. Trace three joined straight sides and correct the claim. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise known traces. One priority only; queue other needs. Everyone returns corrected paper.

#### 8 min. Model rectangles and construction

Review rectangle/square, day 45, turn, day 47, side/corner, day 48. Supply both names on the model cards below. **Ask.** [[s03-K51-names]] Pause before [[steps:s03-K51-names]]. Introduce longer, shorter, equal sides and join with strips and gestures. Teach common-start comparison; turning without stretching preserves length. Next [[s03-P51-frame]]: [[steps:s03-P51-frame]]. Model joining two already-placed squares, [[s03-P51-join]]: [[steps:s03-P51-join]]. Connect pieces to boundary marks. A square is a special rectangle, not a separate nonrectangle category. Children need no recited definition.

#### 12 min. Connected frame correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work concurrently. **Ask.** [[s03-P51-sides]] Fit movable strips to the two printed strip outlines, then align their starts and mark the exposed end on Side 1. The upper is longer. Next [[s03-P51-frame]], add the missing edge physically before drawing it. Then [[s03-P51-join]], slide the already-placed squares to full-edge contact and trace outside in the blank box on Side 2. Three rounds of two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling take six minutes; modeling and revision use six. Watch error D, open joins, and error G, displaced starts.

#### 20 min. Centers

**Ask.** [[s03-P51-corners]] Use the supplied labels. Overlay a square-paper corner at each corner before revealing [[steps:s03-P51-corners]]. Four equal sides alone are insufficient, error N. Ten minutes: corner overlays and turns; ten minutes: revisit partial frame/side comparisons and matching square side strips. Review Counting centers, day 1, and its simultaneous table roles. At most two public volunteers, passing allowed. Practice stays in the folder, unscored. Pictures are invented for practice, said aloud. No single blank classification card or triangle build.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent trays collect practice pieces; preserve repaired paper in folders. Reserved card remains covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-51.md. **Ask.** [[s03-X51]] Read exactly. Response format: a child may mark side lengths on the pictured boundary while describing it. Supply no shape label or comparison result. Directions/concurrent paper 2.5 minutes; roster 7 to 12 get six 45-second turns; collection one minute. Everyone returns paper. Heard rectangle names and paper evidence are separate; do not infer unheard names. After class thirty papers at twenty seconds need ten teacher minutes. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return day 52; naming gaps get descriptive notes without a forced error letter.

### Other rooms

Paper runs every task. Without printing, draw the same panels before class. At most one shared screen enlarges teaching pictures. Large tactile strips and directed adult movement preserve comparison; record motor access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper strips, folded joins and adult-cut squares replace straws, clay and blocks. Draw cards; fold a paper corner. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch five squares once each.
- **5 min. Model.** Supply rectangle/square names. Fit side strips, align starts, then complete the partly drawn frame.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P51-sides]] and [[s03-P51-frame]] Parent takes checking role.
  **Say.** "Show which side strip is longer." Expect: upper strip reaches farther from the same start.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 50's feedback; trace repair-51-C.md's three joined straight sides. [[s03-P51-corners]] Correct the supplied square labels if ready.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X51]] First reveal exit-51.md. Read exactly; child marks independently. Expect rectangle, not square, with unequal adjacent sides. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keep all models, repair and exit. Longer: rotate the same supplied-label cards and refit the side strips, preserving the corner checks.

### Words and supports

Longer reaches farther from a shared start; shorter stops sooner; equal sides have the same length; join means ends or edges meet. Frame: "These sides are ___." Read it aloud. Accept gestures, signing and child-directed adult movement. No frame during the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 returns day 51's tickets and practice feedback and applies joins and lengths. Prioritize descriptive rectangle-name gaps using a broad supplied-label repair. Otherwise error G from practice gets one common-start correction. Error D, an open frame, stays queued if not selected. Preserve unheard names as not yet seen on record.md.
