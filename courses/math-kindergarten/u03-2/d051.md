# Day 51: Sides, corners and joins

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-51.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1, and short/long categories, day 41. **Ask.** [[s03-W51]] Touch five squares once each. Recall the supplied short/long side strips before comparing lengths in the model. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 50's exit, C50 Exit box, written task records, practice feedback and heard notes: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. [[s03-F51-C]] on repair-51-C.md supplies a different labeled-boundary correction, error C. Trace three joined straight sides; correct the supplied claim. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise known traces. Choose one priority only; queue other needs. Everybody returns corrected paper.

#### 8 min. Model rectangles and construction

Review rectangle/square, day 45; turn, day 47; side/corner, day 48. Two minutes: supply names for [[s03-K51-names]], then [[steps:s03-K51-names]]. Length is how far from end to end. Demonstrate longer, shorter and equal sides with strips sharing a start. Equal counts, day 22, is review; matching lengths is a new use of equal.

One minute: [[s03-P51-frame]], then [[steps:s03-P51-frame]]. Two minutes: [[s03-P51-join]], then [[steps:s03-P51-join]]. Join means bring ends or edges together; overlap means one piece covers another. Model full-edge contact with no overlap. Trace outside boundary and internal seam separately. One minute: [[s03-P51-sides]], fitting and aligning movable strips, marking the exposed end. Two minutes: [[s03-P51-corners]], then [[steps:s03-P51-corners]]. Launch the rounds below. No recited definitions.

#### 12 min. Connected frame correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs independently repeat the demonstrated tasks. [[s03-P51-sides]]: fit strips, align starts, mark the exposed end. [[s03-P51-frame]]: fit the missing edge before drawing. [[s03-P51-join]]: move the supplied squares to full-edge contact, trace outside and mark the seam. [[s03-P51-corners]]: check every corner with folded paper before correcting supplied labels. Equal sides alone do not make a square. Each two-minute round gives two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling. Repeat the strip and join rounds to make six rounds. Retain marks in practice-records.md.

The teacher uses all twelve minutes for the observation roster. No concurrent modeling or public shares.

#### 20 min. Centers

Continue the roster from Connected work: fifteen minutes for F1 cells 73 to 90, children 13 to 15, using assessment-display.md and the viewing arrow. Eighteen 45-second asks plus 1.5 setup minutes. Indicate one target, read its exact prompt, retain marks and scribed words. Components: s03-F1-51-1 through s03-F1-51-6.

F2 cells 21 to 40, children 5 to 8, take sixteen minutes: twenty 45-second asks plus one reset minute. Use only the indicated assessment-pairs.md panel, in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order. Read its exact prompt; retain marks on both variants. Components: s03-F2-51-1 through s03-F2-51-5. Keep other panels covered. The combined roster uses twelve Connected and nineteen Centers teacher minutes; the last minute collects papers. Never infer unheard words.

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. During the first five independent center minutes, children revise day 50's observation-picture feedback; the teacher hears different children. No secure prompt is repeated. Then continue the launched practice rounds. Preserve originals, feedback and revisions.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent trays collect practice pieces; preserve repaired paper in folders. Reserved card remains covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-51.md only at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X51]] Read exactly. Children mark side lengths on the pictured boundary; supply no name or result. One minute launch, two simultaneous paper minutes for twenty-four, six 45-second turns for children 7 to 12, half a minute collection. The six keep slips face down until called. Everyone returns paper. Record heard names separately; no additional spoken description required. Paper alone cannot establish unheard names. Unmatched naming errors get descriptive notes.

After class, thirty packets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes for daily work and observation feedback. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work in day 52 Return and repair; observation feedback in its first five independent center minutes.

### Other rooms

Paper runs every task. Without printing, draw the same panels before class. At most one shared screen enlarges teaching pictures. Large tactile strips and directed adult movement preserve comparison; record motor access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper strips, folded joins and adult-cut squares replace straws, clay and blocks. Draw cards; fold a paper corner. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch five squares once each.
- **5 min. Model.** Supply rectangle/square names. Fit strips, align starts, complete the frame, then join the two supplied squares without overlap.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P51-sides]] and [[s03-P51-frame]] Parent takes checking role.
  **Say.** "Show which side strip is longer." Expect: upper strip reaches farther from the same start.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 50's feedback; trace repair-51-C.md's three joined straight sides. [[s03-P51-corners]] Correct the supplied square labels if ready.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear practice pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X51]] First reveal exit-51.md. Read exactly; child marks independently. Expect rectangle, not square, with unequal adjacent sides. Record help; return feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, keep all models, repair and exit. Longer: rotate the same supplied-label cards and refit the side strips, preserving the corner checks.

### Words and supports

Review boundary, day 46. Longer reaches farther from a shared start; shorter stops sooner; equal sides have the same length; join means ends or edges meet; overlap means one piece covers another. Frame: "These sides are ___." Read it aloud. Accept gestures, signing and child-directed adult movement. No frame during the exit.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 returns day 51's tickets and practice feedback and applies joins and lengths. Prioritize descriptive rectangle-name gaps using a broad supplied-label repair. Otherwise error G from practice gets one common-start correction. Error D, an open frame, stays queued if not selected. Preserve unheard names as not yet seen on record.md.
