# Day 50: Circles and triangles keep their features

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-50.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W50]] Touch each square once, then show six fingers. The last word tells how many. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty, no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 49's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 49's tickets and practice feedback. If many rejected a thin triangle, use [[s03-F50-C]] on repair-50-C.md: trace its three joined straight sides, correct the supplied claim, and return corrected paper. Review triangle, day 45. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others correct familiar traces. Select one priority only; queue other letters. Never replay day 49's heading card.

#### 8 min. Model names and features

Use the first three minutes to review the circle and triangle labels with a visible single practice shape. Trace the supplied triangular boundary, [[s03-P50-label]], closing the gap. For [[s03-P50-circle]], show the supplied label on a single practice circle and oval; correct it from the curved boundaries. No formal definition recitation.

Then reveal check-50.md, a separate reserved display. **Ask.** [[s03-C50]] Indicate its circle only. All mark the boundary in the student-page Exit box. Hear children 1 to 6: six 45-second turns plus half a minute launch/collection, five minutes. Return this record with the exit on day 51. The exit itself contains only the thin triangle.

#### 12 min. Connected label correction
Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen simultaneous pairs work on single supplied-label cards. **Ask.** [[s03-P50-label]] One child traces, the other checks; exchange roles, 45 seconds each plus thirty seconds handling, two minutes per round. Next **Ask.** [[s03-P50-open]] Each closes the broad outline and traces its three straight sides. Repeat the circle-label correction separately. Modeling, three two-minute rounds and revision fit twelve minutes. If gaps remain, trace through the join with a finger before marking.

Children continue this familiar correction independently while the teacher begins today's observation roster for all twelve minutes. Launch the paper routine before starting; no public shares or concurrent teacher-led repair.

#### 20 min. Centers

Use assessment-display.md and viewing arrow for F1 cells 55 to 72, child first then above, below, beside, next to, in front of, behind. Indicate one target and read its one master prompt. Let the child mark that target and respond; scribe words without supplying them. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 minutes setup take fifteen minutes. The six component rows are s03-F1-50-1, s03-F1-50-2, s03-F1-50-3, s03-F1-50-4, s03-F1-50-5, s03-F1-50-6. Keep other children on familiar paper; never show this display in practice.

Also collect F2 cells 1 to 20 using the five covered assessment-pairs.md panels in class order. Show one pair at a time; read its one naming prompt, retain feature marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Component rows: s03-F2-talk-58, s03-F2-talk-59, s03-F2-talk-60, s03-F2-talk-61, s03-F2-talk-62. The combined roster uses the preceding twelve Connected minutes and nineteen Center minutes; one center minute remains for collection. No repeat interview or unheard-name inference.

Others revise yesterday's written feedback during their first five independent center minutes, then continue the supplied familiar correction or count-dot record. Preserve originals and supported revisions. No unsupervised water.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect pieces. Put practice in folders; keep exits covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-50.md. **Ask.** [[s03-X50]] Read exactly. Explain response format: marks on the boundaries show the parts the child is describing. Do not supply names or features. Directions/concurrent paper: 2.5 minutes; roster 1 to 6 get six 45-second turns; collection one minute. Everybody returns paper. Record heard names/features and help separately; paper does not prove unheard names. After class, thirty papers at twenty seconds take ten teacher minutes. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen; return day 51.

### Other rooms

All tasks have a paper path. Hand-copy these exact panels before class without a printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Offer raised outlines and child-directed adult tracing; record access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the practice cards and six paper squares; broad crayons replace pencils. Point to paper instead of a screen.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares once each.
- **5 min. Model.** Supply triangle on the partly open picture and trace three straight parts. [[s03-P50-circle]] Show the circle-label panel.
  **Say.** "Fix the circle label." Expect: keep the round circle's label, cross out the oval's label.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P50-label]] and [[s03-P50-open]] Child closes and traces the supplied boundaries. Parent takes checking role.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct the returned practice claim using repair-50-C.md, never the old exit card.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X50]] Reveal exit-50.md now. Read exactly; child marks independently. Expect one triangle with three joined straight sides. Record help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetition; keep the repair, model and exit. Longer: turn the same labeled practice cards and trace again, without reserved cases.

### Words and supports

Review circle, triangle, straight, curved, closed, side and corner as above. Review boundary, day 12: here the line around a shape. Oval names the stretched curve on the practice card. Accept gesture, signing or usual communication aids. No child reads labels independently; the adult reads them. Never infer an unheard name from a boundary mark.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 returns day 50's paper and names rectangles and squares while comparing sides. Error C, rejecting the thin triangle, gets one five-minute fresh labeled-boundary repair. Preserve unheard names/features as not yet seen, with later fresh-case rotation on record.md. No exit pair is rehearsed.
