# Day 50: Circles and triangles keep their features

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-50.md, repair-50-C.md and covered exit-50.md. Set adult-cut large paper squares, broad pencils and numeral 6. Prepare covered assessment-display.md, assessment-pairs.md and retained response sheets for the assigned children. Keep Side 2's C50 Exit box covered. Use the viewing arrow. Read child print aloud at 18 point. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1, and numeral links, day 30. **Ask.** [[s03-W50]] Touch six squares once each; connect the total to the supplied numeral 6. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 49's exit, written task records and heard notes with feedback: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. Honor its triangle-feature follow-up with [[s03-F50-C]] on repair-50-C.md, a different supplied claim. Review triangle, day 45. Trace three joined straight sides; correct the claim. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise familiar traces. One priority only; queue other needs. Everybody returns corrected paper.

#### 8 min. Model names and features

First three minutes: review circle/triangle, day 45, and straight/curved/closed, day 46. Read the supplied labels. Model [[s03-P50-label]] and [[s03-P50-circle]] with separate cards; name the stretched curve oval. Turn the labeled practice triangle and trace its unchanged sides. Demonstrate [[s03-P50-open]] and launch the independent partner rounds below.

Use five minutes for C50. **Ask.** [[s03-C50]] Read exactly; indicate only the arrowed circle. Twenty-four children uncover their Side 2 Exit box for simultaneous boundary marks; children 1 to 6 keep it covered until their own turn. Hear six 45-second descriptions; half a minute launches/collects. No feature cues. Retain marks and heard words separately; return day 51.

#### 12 min. Connected label correction

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs independently repeat the launched single-card corrections: [[s03-P50-label]], then [[s03-P50-open]], then [[s03-P50-circle]]. One child traces or repairs; the other checks; exchange roles. Two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling make each two-minute round. Repeat the three-round cycle twice: six rounds, twelve minutes. Adult has already read each stem aloud. Supplied labels remain visible; never use reserved naming panels. Keep each child's marks in practice-records.md.

The teacher starts the observation roster immediately and uses all twelve minutes for it. No simultaneous modeling or public shares.

#### 20 min. Centers

Continue the roster from Connected work: fifteen minutes for F1 cells 55 to 72, children 10 to 12, using assessment-display.md and the viewing arrow. Eighteen 45-second asks plus 1.5 setup minutes. Indicate one target, read its exact prompt, retain marks and scribed words. Components: s03-F1-50-1 through s03-F1-50-6.

F2 cells 1 to 20, children 1 to 4, take sixteen minutes: twenty 45-second asks plus one reset minute. Use only the indicated assessment-pairs.md panel, in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order. Read its exact prompt; retain marks on both variants. Components: s03-F2-talk-58 through s03-F2-talk-62. Keep other panels covered. The combined roster uses twelve Connected and nineteen Centers teacher minutes; the last minute collects papers. Never infer unheard words.

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. During the first five independent center minutes, children revise day 49's observation-picture feedback; the teacher hears different children. No secure prompt is repeated. Then continue the launched practice rounds. Preserve originals, feedback and revisions.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent table trays collect pieces. Put practice in folders; keep exits covered.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-50.md only at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X50]] Read exactly. Marks show boundary parts; supply no name or feature. One minute launch, two simultaneous paper minutes for twenty-four, six 45-second turns for children 1 to 6, half a minute collection. The six keep slips face down until called. Everyone returns paper. Record heard names and help separately; no second spoken ask. Paper alone cannot establish unheard names.

After class, thirty packets at thirty seconds each take fifteen teacher minutes for daily work, C50 and observation feedback. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work in day 51 Return and repair; observation feedback in its first five independent center minutes.

### Other rooms

All tasks have a paper path. Hand-copy these exact panels before class without a printer. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures. Offer raised outlines and child-directed adult tracing; record access separately from mathematical help.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw practice cards and six paper squares; use a broad crayon and handwritten numeral 6. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch six squares and show the supplied numeral.
- **5 min. Model.** Supply triangle on the open picture; trace its straight parts. [[s03-P50-circle]]
  **Say.** "Fix the circle label." Expect: keep the circle's label, cross out the oval's.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P50-label]] and [[s03-P50-open]] Close and trace the supplied boundaries. Then uncover C50, read its prompt and retain boundary marks and the child's description. Expect circle with a curved boundary.
- **5 min. Repair.** Correct repair-50-C.md's supplied claim, never the old exit.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear pieces.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X50]] Reveal now; read exactly. Expect triangle. Retain independent marks, heard name and help; give feedback tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetition; keep the repair, model and exit. Longer: turn the same labeled practice cards and trace again, without reserved cases.

### Words and supports

Review circle, triangle, straight, curved, closed, side and corner as above. Review boundary, day 46: the line around a shape. Oval names the stretched curve on the practice card. Accept gesture, signing or usual communication aids. No child reads labels independently; the adult reads them. Never infer an unheard name from a boundary mark.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 returns day 50's paper and names rectangles and squares while comparing sides. Error C, rejecting the thin triangle, gets one five-minute fresh labeled-boundary repair. Preserve unheard names/features as not yet seen, with later fresh-case rotation on record.md. No reserved card is rehearsed.
