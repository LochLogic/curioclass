# Day 52: Repair and join flat shapes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-52.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1, and equal counts, day 22. **Ask.** [[s03-W52]] Touch eight squares once each. Recount the same unchanged group to recall equal totals. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty; no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 51's exit, written task records, practice feedback and heard notes: one minute return, one model, two concurrent corrections, one collection. Prioritize a rectangle-name gap with [[s03-F52-name]] on the supplied broad rectangle practice card, label crossed out. Otherwise use [[s03-F52-G]] on repair-52-G.md: align near ends, mark the longer exposed end, error G. More than ten of thirty: class; otherwise group while others revise familiar marks. One action only; queue other needs. Everyone returns corrected paper.

#### 8 min. Rehearse joins and lengths

Review day 51 joins, lengths and Aligned side strips and corner overlay. Two minutes: demonstrate [[s03-P52-strips]] using movable strips; align starts before marking. Two minutes: [[s03-P52-frame]], fit and connect the missing edge before drawing. Two minutes: [[s03-P52-join]], slide already-placed squares to full-edge contact; trace outside and mark the seam. Review overlap, day 51, and turning without stretching.

Last two minutes: review above/next to, day 45. Fix the cup and viewing arrow; demonstrate the two separate movement commands below. Launch independent rounds before starting conferences. Triangle packets remain sealed.

#### 12 min. Connected placements and joins

Review Partner turns, day 1. Fifteen pairs work independently. Round one: [[s03-P52-position-above]]. Round two: [[s03-P52-position-next-to]]. Each round gives both children a 45-second move-and-record turn plus thirty seconds handling, four minutes total. Keep cup and viewer fixed. Use scrap paper for each child's placement record.

Next repeat the demonstrated strip, partly built frame and square-join corrections on the student page, with a second strip round. Four two-minute rounds fill eight minutes. Retain each child's paper. Error E is a gap between the squares: move the piece to full-edge contact.

The teacher uses all twelve minutes for the observation roster; no simultaneous demonstration or repair.

#### 20 min. Centers

Continue the roster from Connected work: fifteen minutes for F1 cells 91 to 108, children 16 to 18, using assessment-display.md and the viewing arrow. Eighteen 45-second asks plus 1.5 setup minutes. Indicate one target, read its exact prompt, retain marks and scribed words. Components: s03-F1-52-1 through s03-F1-52-6.

F2 cells 41 to 60, children 9 to 12, take sixteen minutes: twenty 45-second asks plus one reset minute. Use only the indicated assessment-pairs.md panel, in circle/triangle/square/rectangle/hexagon order. Read its exact prompt; retain marks on both variants. Components: s03-F2-52-1 through s03-F2-52-5. Keep other panels covered. The combined roster uses twelve Connected and nineteen Centers teacher minutes; the last minute collects papers. Never infer unheard words.

Review Counting centers and Paper count-dot record, day 1. During the first five independent center minutes, children revise day 51's observation-picture feedback; the teacher hears different children. No secure prompt is repeated. Then continue the launched practice rounds. Preserve originals, feedback and revisions.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent trays collect practice pieces. Clear space for each child's independent construction.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-52.md and packet-52.md only at each child's own turn. **Ask.** [[s03-X52]] Read exactly. Trace the build in the blank paper box; no completed outline or angle hint. One minute launch, two simultaneous building/tracing minutes for twenty-four, six 45-second observation turns for children 13 to 18, half a minute collection. The six keep packets sealed until called. Everyone returns a trace; preserve builds on trays until recorded. Trace alone cannot prove independent joining; retain unobserved independence as not yet seen.

After class, thirty packets at thirty seconds take fifteen teacher minutes for daily work and observation feedback. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return daily work in day 53 Return and repair; observation feedback in its first five independent center minutes.

### Other rooms

Every task runs on paper. Hand-copy exact panels before class if needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures only. An adult moves large strips on the child's directions or traces around the child's build; record access separately from mathematical help. No device queue.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut wide paper strips and folded joins replace straws/clay. Paper squares replace blocks; draw cup, arrow and cards. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight squares once each.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s03-P52-frame]] Complete the partly supplied frame using day 51's join and corner checks.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P52-strips]] and [[s03-P52-join]] Parent takes checking role.
  **Say.** "Fix the length picture." Expect: both strip starts aligned to the given line before marking.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 51 feedback; choose one broad rectangle-label or shifted-strip correction.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear space.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X52]] Reveal reserved loose strips and exit-52.md. Read exactly. Child builds and traces independently. Expect three connected straight sides, no gap. Record help and give feedback tomorrow. Do not show a finished triangle beforehand.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, preserving repair, position turns and exit. Longer: turn the same repaired quadrilateral and recheck its joins and side strips.

### Words and supports

Review boundary, day 46; rectangle, square, side, corner and closed, days 45 to 48; longer, shorter, equal sides and join, day 51; above/next to, day 45. Frame: "These sides are ___." Read aloud. Accept pointing, signing or usual communication aids without supplying mathematical choices.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 returns day 52's tickets and practice feedback before naming several measurable attributes of one object. Error D, an open triangle join, calls for one five-minute fresh partly built quadrilateral closure repair. Never repeat the reserved triangle. Keep unobserved construction independence not yet seen and schedule a fresh case through record.md.
