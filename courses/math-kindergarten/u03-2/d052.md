# Day 52: Repair and join flat shapes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare practice-52.md, one queued repair card and the covered exit slip. Set large paper squares, adult-cut practice shapes and broad pencils. Prepare the covered observation master and retained paper marks for today's named children only. Use the viewing arrow for positions. Read child print aloud at 18 point; draw copies if needed. Keep all other assessment panels covered. Large intact pieces only; no mouth contact.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: oral count

Review count and keep track, day 1. **Ask.** [[s03-W52]] Touch eight squares once; the last word tells how many. Count together to ten, supported toward twenty, no timer.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 51's written task records with the existing feedback packet. Use one priority correction only.

Review Return and repair, day 2. Return day 51's tickets and practice feedback. If many could not name its rectangle, use [[s03-F52-name]] with the broad practice rectangle from d051.md, label crossed out; correct the supplied label. Otherwise select [[s03-F52-G]]: align the shifted rigid strips from repair-52-G.md and mark the upper one's exposed end, error G. Choose one action only. More than ten of thirty: whole class; otherwise group while others revise familiar paper. Everyone returns corrected paper; queue other needs.

#### 8 min. Rehearse joins and lengths

Review longer, shorter, equal sides, join and Aligned side strips and corner overlay, day 51. **Ask.** [[s03-P52-strips]] Move matching strips onto the supplied start line before correcting the picture. Then [[s03-P52-frame]], complete its partly supplied boundary and check joins and square corners. Finally [[s03-P52-join]], slide already-placed squares until whole edges meet. Teacher traces the outside rectangle and inside seam as on day 51. Apply known moves; triangle packets remain sealed. Turning preserves shape and length.

#### 12 min. Connected placements and joins
Review above and next to, day 45, and Partner turns, day 1. Keep cup and viewing arrow fixed. **Ask.** [[s03-P52-position-above]], then separately [[s03-P52-position-next-to]]. Fifteen pairs each take two 45-second turns plus thirty seconds handling, two minutes total; each child records one card position on scrap paper. Remaining ten minutes: compare practice strips, complete partly built frames, then fix square joins on Side 2. Every child handles and records. Error E, a gap at the square join: slide the piece to full-edge contact, never cover the gap by drawing over it.

Children continue this familiar correction independently while the teacher begins today's observation roster for all twelve minutes. Launch the paper routine before starting; no public shares or concurrent teacher-led repair.

#### 20 min. Centers

Use assessment-display.md and viewing arrow for F1 cells 91 to 108, child first then above, below, beside, next to, in front of, behind. Indicate one target and read its one master prompt. Let the child mark that target and respond; scribe words without supplying them. Eighteen 45-second turns plus 1.5 minutes setup take fifteen minutes. The six component rows are s03-F1-52-1, s03-F1-52-2, s03-F1-52-3, s03-F1-52-4, s03-F1-52-5, s03-F1-52-6. Keep other children on familiar paper; never show this display in practice.

Also collect F2 cells 41 to 60 using the five covered assessment-pairs.md panels in class order. Show one pair at a time; read its one naming prompt, retain feature marks on both variants. Twenty 45-second turns plus one reset minute take sixteen minutes. Component rows: s03-F2-52-1, s03-F2-52-2, s03-F2-52-3, s03-F2-52-4, s03-F2-52-5. The combined roster uses the preceding twelve Connected minutes and nineteen Center minutes; one center minute remains for collection. No repeat interview or unheard-name inference.

Others revise yesterday's written feedback during their first five independent center minutes, then continue the supplied familiar correction or count-dot record. Preserve originals and supported revisions. No unsupervised water.

#### 2 min. Tidy

Concurrent trays collect practice pieces. Clear space for each child's independent construction.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First reveal exit-52.md and packet-52.md. **Ask.** [[s03-X52]] Read exactly. Explain response format: trace the finished build in the blank paper box. No completed outline or angle hint. Directions/concurrent building and paper: 2.5 minutes; roster 13 to 18 get six 45-second observation turns; collection one minute. Everyone returns a trace; keep each build on its tray until recorded. Observe the six actual joins, not an imagined reason. Thirty papers at twenty seconds take ten teacher minutes after class. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Trace alone does not prove construction independence. Return feedback day 53.

### Other rooms

Every task runs on paper. Hand-copy exact panels before class if needed. One shared screen may enlarge teaching pictures only. An adult moves large strips on the child's directions or traces around the child's build; record access separately from mathematical help. No device queue.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult-cut wide paper strips and folded joins replace straws/clay. Paper squares replace blocks; draw cup, arrow and cards. No screen needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and repeated center rounds.

- **3 min. Count.** Touch eight squares once each.
- **5 min. Rehearse.** [[s03-P52-frame]] Complete the partly supplied frame using day 51's join and corner checks.
- **7 min. Practice.** [[s03-P52-strips]] and [[s03-P52-join]] Parent takes checking role.
  **Say.** "Fix the length picture." Expect: both strip starts aligned to the given line before marking.
- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 51 feedback; choose one broad rectangle-label or shifted-strip correction.
- **2 min. Tidy.** Clear space.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X52]] Reveal reserved loose strips and exit-52.md. Read exactly. Child builds and traces independently. Expect three connected straight sides, no gap. Record help and give feedback tomorrow. Do not show a finished triangle beforehand.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce center repetitions, preserving repair, position turns and exit. Longer: turn the same repaired quadrilateral and recheck its joins and side strips.

### Words and supports

Review boundary, day 50; rectangle, square, side, corner and closed, days 45 to 48; longer, shorter, equal sides and join, day 51; above/next to, day 45. Frame: "These sides are ___." Read aloud. Accept pointing, signing or usual communication aids without supplying mathematical choices.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 returns day 52's tickets and practice feedback before naming several measurable attributes of one object. Error D, an open triangle join, calls for one five-minute fresh partly built quadrilateral closure repair. Never repeat the reserved triangle. Keep unobserved construction independence not yet seen and schedule a fresh case through record.md.
