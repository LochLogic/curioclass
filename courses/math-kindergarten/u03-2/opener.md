# Naming and building flat shapes

## The ideas

A triangle is closed with three straight sides; circles are round. A square is a special rectangle with four equal sides and square corners. Equal sides alone do not guarantee square corners. Align side strips at a common start to compare length. Build and join quadrilaterals on day 51 before application day 52. No angle measures, written lengths or formal definitions.

## Teach and record

Read every printed prompt aloud. Child pages use pictures and 18 point text; independent reading is not needed. Review Counting centers, Paper count-dot record, Partner turns and Return and repair from earlier units, demonstrating them with fresh cases if needed. Circle as a geometric shape is a new sense, different from the earlier circular counting arrangement.

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously. A partner round has two 45-second turns plus handling. At most two public volunteers, with passing allowed. Rin is the sole actor in invented scenes; say invented for practice. Keep reserved exit and assessment cases covered during teaching and repairs.

Every child marks a paper half slip. Ordinary exits use one minute launch, two simultaneous paper minutes, six 45-second turns and half a minute collection. Hear only the roster six, with names and date recorded. Day 61 instead gives one minute launch, six independent fitting/tracing minutes and one collection minute. Its earlier practice talk never counts as an exit explanation. Unheard speech remains not yet seen.

Give written feedback in fifteen teacher minutes after class. The next named Return and repair holds one five-minute priority action, with all other papers returned in the same packet. Return one minute, model one, revise concurrently two, collect one. More than ten of thirty showing one error calls for a class repair; otherwise a group while others correct familiar paper. Queue other letters, with no second teacher-led repair in the same slot.

## Common errors

C: trace varied triangles. B: rotate a square. G: align endpoints. D: close a fresh quadrilateral. Unmatched rectangle naming gaps stay descriptive.

## Materials and access

Adult-cut paper shapes replace blocks, wide paper strips and folded joins replace straws/clay, hand drawings replace printing. Scrap paper and broad pencils suffice for drawing. Large intact pieces only, nonslip mat if needed; no loose small joins, mouths, glass, food or body comparisons. Household sealed objects replace heft tubs; pretest safe handling. Real household cups, tray and towel remain necessary for capacity. Adult pours room-temperature water over tray, stops at rim and dries spills/floor. If apparatus missing, capacity pending, not picture-guessed.

Use raised/high-contrast boundaries and child-directed adult movement. Record access separately from mathematical help. No devices needed; at most one shared screen enlarges teacher drawings. A room without a printer draws the same original pieces before class.
