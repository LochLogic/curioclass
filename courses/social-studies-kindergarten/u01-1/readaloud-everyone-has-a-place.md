# Everyone Has a Place

A picture read-aloud for day 2. Ten pages. Say first: "We made this picture to look at how people can like different things. These children are pretend." Read each page's words; the pictures do the rest. No child in the class is asked which character they are like.

Drawing style for all pages: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words. Nia has dark brown skin and hair in two puffs. Dev has light brown skin, short straight hair and round glasses. Mina has pale skin and a long braid and wears a picture-card board on a cord around her neck. The room has a rug, a reading corner with a beanbag and a low shelf of book bins, a water table (a shallow tub on a stand) with cups, one window, one door and a shelf of tidy-up bins.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A wide view of the classroom. The reading corner is at the left, the water table at the right, the window above them and the door at the back. Nobody is in the room yet.

**Read:** Here is a room. It has a place to read and a place to pour.

## Page 2

**Picture:** Nia sits in the beanbag in the reading corner holding an open picture book. Her face is calm. Book bins stand beside her.

**Read:** Nia likes the reading corner. It is soft and quiet.

## Page 3

**Picture:** Dev stands at the water table wearing an apron, pouring water from one cup into another. Small drops fly. A towel lies on the floor beside the stand.

**Read:** Dev likes the water table. Pour, pour, splash. The towel is ready.

## Page 4

**Picture:** Nia and Dev again, each inside a dotted outline that shows a little empty space around their elbows and feet.

**Read:** Nia needs room for her book. Dev needs room for his cups.

## Page 5

**Picture:** Nia with the book bin beside her. Dev with a tray of cups beside him. A short arrow from each child points to their things.

**Read:** Nia needs her books close. Dev needs his cups close.

## Page 6

**Picture:** Three children wait in a short line at the beanbag: Mina is first, Nia is behind her, and a child with tan skin and a blue shirt is last. Their faces are patient.

**Read:** Sometimes many people want the same place. Everyone needs a turn.

## Page 7

**Picture:** Mina points to a card on her board that shows a small pair of hands taking turns. Dev, who has just stepped over, nods with a smile.

**Read:** Mina points to her card to say, "I am next." Dev nods. People can say it in their own way.

## Page 8

**Picture:** The wide view again. Sunlight from the window falls on both the reading corner and the water table. A dashed line traces from the door to each place.

**Read:** One window gives light to both places. One door lets everyone in.

## Page 9

**Picture:** Nia and Dev side by side at the shelf, each putting something in a bin: Nia a book, Dev a cup. Mina waves from the rug.

**Read:** At clean-up time Nia and Dev put things away. The room is ready for tomorrow.

## Page 10

**Picture:** The wide view of the room one last time, with all three children in their places: Nia reading, Dev pouring, Mina on the rug. Over the room a thought bubble holds the shapes of a window, a door and a rug.

**Read:** Different things we like. The same room we share. What do you need? What do I need?

## For the teacher

Model the two questions on page 10 once, pointing at the picture, then your own: "Why do you think the room has a place for each?" Children echo that question once. Pairs answer by pointing, not by telling about themselves.
