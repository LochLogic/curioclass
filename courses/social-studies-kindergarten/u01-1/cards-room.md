# Room cards (day 2 supported work)

Four picture cards of the real room, for pairs to point at and sort. They show things the class actually has; draw them to match your room. Each card is a plain line drawing of one thing, nothing else on the card, with at most a one-word label under it. No people appear on these cards.

## Card 1: the rug

**Picture:** The class rug seen from above, a plain rectangle with a simple border and room for many sitting spots shown as small dotted circles.

**Label:** rug

## Card 2: the sink

**Picture:** The class sink with a tap, a soap pump and a paper towel holder beside it, seen from the front.

**Label:** sink

## Card 3: the shelves

**Picture:** A low open shelf with three rows of bins. Each bin has a simple picture of what is inside (paper, crayons, blocks).

**Label:** shelves

## Card 4: the door

**Picture:** The classroom door, open, seen from inside the room, with the hall light beyond it.

**Label:** door

## How the cards are used

Pairs point to what many people use and what one person uses at a time (the sink, at each moment). They describe the card, not themselves. The exit cards for days 1 and 2 are drawn differently and are not these.
