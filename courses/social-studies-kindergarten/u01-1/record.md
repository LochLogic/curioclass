# Unit 1 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left, and beside it the child's group day, 3 to 7: six children to each day, so
"today's six" on days 3 to 7 are that day's group, and day 8 repeats the day 3 group. Days 1 and 2 look early at the
day 4 and day 5 groups for the readiness items only. The R columns are the day 1 readiness look (no score; note the
support a child used). Each E column is one spoken exit: one tick when the child answers it, by pointing, saying or
drawing, with a puppet or a partner if that helps. The End columns are the unit assessment on day 9, one cell for
each look-for: 2 when the child does it independently, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard
and not shown even with prompts, and blank when not yet observed. Never write 0 for a part that was not heard. Put
the date in the cell for any attempt after day 9, since day 10 repeats a part. A child may pass; leave the cell blank
and try again another day.
