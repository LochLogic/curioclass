# Unit 1 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. The R columns are the day 1 readiness look (no score; note the support a child
used). Each E column is one spoken exit: one tick when the child answers it, by pointing, saying or drawing, with a
puppet or a partner if that helps. The End columns are the closing check on day 9, one cell for each look-for met.
A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
