# Day 1: Our big question and the wonder wall

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape up the wonder wall. Put the bear by the easel and the bird on the sink card. Choose today's six: the day 4 group on the record sheet, looked at early. Teacher key: key-exits-1-2.md.

### The day

#### 12 min. Hook and wonder wall

Whole class on the rug. Say: "We made this picture for our class. The puppets are pretend." Show Two Drawing Tables, reading pages 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7 and 10.

**Ask.** What does each table let Ro do? Expect: at the first, Ro waits and cannot draw; at the second, Ro reaches a crayon and draws. Pointing to a panel counts.

Post the big question: "What makes our class a good place to learn?"

#### 12 min. Start-of-year check

Not a test: pointing, signing or an aid is an answer. Begin on the rug with the bear: "I wonder why the bear is by the easel." A wonder is a question, not a statement like "That is a bear". Then a reason: "Finding this out matters to me because I like it when everyone has a turn."

Show the bird on the sink room card and ask (s01-R1): "What do you wonder?" Write each wonder on the wall beside a quick picture. No child must speak. Say a statement back once as a wonder.

The six stay with you; the rest go to tables and draw on the wonder card. Read aloud: "Draw something you wonder about our class."

With the six, (s01-R2): two puppets reach for one puzzle piece; ask what could help both play, and why. Then pairs do (s01-R3): "Choose a block. I will tell you my choice; then show or tell yours," trading twice. Note each answer and the support used.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles one spot on the room card (s01-X1); read aloud: "Circle the picture you most wonder about." Then ask the six (s01-X1b): "Why does our big question matter to you?" A child may pass or point. Any real use is a reason. "Because you told us to" is error A: thank them and go on. If a child names home, thank them in one line and go on with "and in our room ...".

### At home

**Swap.** No puppets: use two spoons. No crayon tub: hold a handful of crayons.

**Skip.** The wall and the class check.

- **5 min. The two tables.** One puppet holds every crayon and the other waits. Then the crayons sit in a cup between them.
  **Say.** "What does each way let the puppet do?" Expect: waits and cannot draw; with the cup, both can draw.
- **5 min. A wonder.** Say "I wonder why the cup is in the middle." Write what your child wonders.
  **Say.** "What do you wonder?" Expect: any question; say a statement back as "I wonder ...".
- **5 min. Draw it.** Your child draws the wonder on the wonder card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X1]] [[s01-X1b]] Draw a sink, shelf, window and rug. Your child circles one.
  **Say.** "Why does finding out matter to you?" Expect: any reason of their own.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pages 1, 2, 3, 5 and 10. Longer: all ten pages.

### Words and supports

Wonder: a question you want to find out. Frames: "I wonder ..." and "Finding this out matters to me because ...". A home language, signs, pointing or a dictated drawing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 reads the wonder wall. If many gave error A, it offers two classroom uses of an answer. Then Everyone Has a Place, room cards in pairs, and the partner-turn bridge for (s01-R3).
