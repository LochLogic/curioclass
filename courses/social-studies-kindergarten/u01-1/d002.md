# Day 2: All about our class

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the four room cards in an envelope for each pair. Lay out the ten pictures and the coat-hook and job-chart cards. Choose today's six: the day 5 group on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-1-2.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Read the wonder wall aloud. If many gave error A on day 1's exit (s01-X1, "because you told us to"), show the coat-hook card and the job-chart card: "Knowing where things go matters to me because I can hang up my coat."

**Ask.** Which one would matter to you? Expect: a point to either card.

#### 8 min. Everyone Has a Place

Say: "We made this picture to look at how people can like different things. These children are pretend." Read each page. On page 10 ask "What do you need? What do I need?", then "Why do you think the room has a place for each?" Children echo that question once; no one answers it yet.

**Ask.** What do Nia and Dev each need? What do they both need? Expect: Nia needs her book close and room; Dev needs his cups close and room; both need room and a turn.

#### 14 min. Supported work

Show Nia's book and Dev's cup from the pictures. Pairs open the room cards. Read aloud: "Point to the room card where Nia's book is used. Point to where Dev's cup is used. Now point to a card both of them use." Different cards for the two things, one shared card: we are different, and we share one room. Each child draws one thing they use and one thing we all use.

For children who needed support on (s01-R3), run the bridge: "Choose a card. I tell my choice; then show or tell yours." Trade twice, with a picture of a hand to cue the turn, then fade it. Also run (s01-R3) with the six and any child still unseen.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles on the exit card (s01-X2); read aloud: "Circle what both need." Ask the six: "What do both need in their shared room? Why does that matter to you?" A child may pass or point. The clear space is right. "They do not need the same things" is error K.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No room cards: draw a bed, a sink, a shelf and a door. No exit card: draw two children, a paintbrush and a ball of clay.

**Skip.** The pair work and the reopen.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell about Nia, who reads in a corner, and Dev, who pours water. Each needs room and a turn.
  **Say.** "What do they both need?" Expect: room, a turn.
- **5 min. Cards.** Draw a rug and a sink. Ask your child to point to where Nia's book is used, where Dev's cup is used, and a place both use.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing they use and one thing everyone uses.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2]] Your child circles what both children need.
  **Say.** "What do both need? Why does that matter to you?" Expect: space, and any reason of their own.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pages 1, 2, 3, 4, 6 and 10. Longer: a pair finds one shared thing in the picture.

### Words and supports

Need: something you must have to do a thing. Frame: "It matters to me because ...". A pointed card is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 reopens with s01-X2: for error K, one picture and one sentence, and a small group takes the rest. Then why rules exist, from rule cards and a read-aloud.
