# Unit 3 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left, and beside it the child's group day, 20 to 28: six children to each day, so "today's six" are that day's group, heard for the spoken part of the exit and for the explanations of items 2 and 3 on days 24 to 28. A child outside the day's group is recorded not yet heard for the spoken part. Each E column is one spoken exit: one tick when the child answers it, by pointing, saying or drawing, with a puppet or a partner if that helps. Day 29 has no exit ticket; its seven items below are the evidence. The End columns are the closing check on day 29, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child does it independently, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not shown even with prompts, and blank when not yet observed. Never write 0 for a part that was not heard. Put the date in the cell for any attempt after day 29, since day 30 repeats a part. Items 3 and 4 (and the revised model, graph and redrawn map) are scored from the dated records of days 26 to 28, never from first products. A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
