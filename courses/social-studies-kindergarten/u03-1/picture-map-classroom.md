# Classroom picture map (day 21)

One copy for each pair, drawn by the teacher from Our Place Record photographs of the room and printed so every pair holds it the same way up. It is the first flat picture the children read for position words. It is not a drawing of anyone's home, and no child is in it.

**Picture:** the classroom seen from above, a plain rectangle for the room with a thick border. Six to eight things from your own room as simple shapes with a one-word label under each (rug, table, shelf, sink, door, plant, bin). Place them so that at least two things are clearly left and right of each other, one thing is between two others, and one is far from the door. Keep the same way up on every copy: a small sun picture sits at the top edge of the page, so "the page this way up" means sun at the top.

**Fit to your room:** draw what is really there. The taught words on this page are near, far, left, right, behind, in front of, between and next to. Behind and in front of are read from the child's side of the page: the thing nearer the bottom edge is in front, the thing nearer the top edge is behind.

**Use:** the teacher turns the card the same way up for everyone, names left and right on the page, then says the place: "The shelf is left of the sink, with the page this way up." Each pair puts a toy person on the map and says where two things are. The teacher scribes one sentence under each child's map: "The ___ is ___ the ___, from here."

**Not here:** no key, no symbols, no drawing of a map by children (section 3.3).
