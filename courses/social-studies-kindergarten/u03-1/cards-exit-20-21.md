# Exit cards 20 and 21

Two new picture cards, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line, no brands. Never used in teaching (rule 4): the taught cases are the toy car, box, basket and toy child (day 20) and the class playground photograph and classroom picture map (day 21). If your playground really holds a gate, a slide and a sandbox in this order, redraw card 21 with other things and say so in the record.

## Card 20: two toy children

Say aloud before the card: "This is a pretend card."

**Picture:** four things in one row on a plain ground line, facing you, from the page's left: a toy child in a dotted shirt, a drum, a toy child in a striped shirt, an easel. The drum and the easel are about the same size as the children.

**Mark:** circle the child between the drum and the easel. **Spoken, from here:** "Which child is between the drum and the easel? Say where that child is."

## Card 21: the playground drawing

**Picture:** a playground seen from the front, page this way up, with a small sun picture at the top edge so the teacher and every child hold it the same way. From the page's left: a gate, a slide, a sandbox, in a row, each about the same size, with room between them.

**Mark:** circle what is between the gate and the sandbox. **Spoken, page this way up:** "Where is the gate compared with the slide?"

## Key (teacher)

Card 20: the child in the striped shirt, between the drum and the easel (both named). The dotted-shirt child is beside the drum only (error J).

Card 21: the slide. The gate is left of the slide, with the page this way up. "Right of the slide" is error K; turning the card and saying "left is the way my hand points" is error A.
