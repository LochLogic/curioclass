# Day 21: Where things are on a picture

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the playground photograph. Give each pair a picture map, sun at the top, and a toy person.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Review: the eight position words. Call "behind" or "between"; pairs place a toy. Read each pair its slip. Mark s03-X20. For error J, put a toy beside the toy child, ask what is between, then add a second thing.

#### 6 min. Model on a real place

Photograph: "The slide is next to the sandbox, from here" (your own playground). Hold a picture map the same way up for all; sweep a hand from the left edge, say "left edge", then say the place. Puppet points at the right edge: "left" (error K).

**Ask.** Which side is that, from here? Expect: left or right of a thing, "with the page this way up."

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs put the toy person on the map; each child says where two things are: "The ___ is ___ the ___, from here." Children say the viewpoint first. Hear today's six and scribe their sentences under their drawings; the rest tell a partner. Look-fors: the word fits, a second thing, the viewpoint. Left-right slips are noted only.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 21 ("a pretend card"); all circle what is between the gate and the sandbox (s03-X21). The six say where the gate is compared with the slide (s03-X21b). "Right of the slide" is error K; "left is where my hand points," error A.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture map: draw your table from above, a mark at the top edge. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The playground photograph and the class record.

- **5 min. Same way up.** Draw three things from your table in a row; keep the page one way up. Sweep a hand from the left edge, then name the places.
  **Say.** "Which side is the cup? Say it from here." Expect: left or right of the spoon, "with the page this way up."
- **5 min. Two things.** Your child puts a toy on the drawing and says where two things are.
  **Say.** "Where is the spoon?" Expect: "The spoon is next to the cup, from here."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X21]] [[s03-X21b]] Draw a gate, a slide and a sandbox in a row. Your child circles what is between the gate and the sandbox.
  **Say.** "Where is the gate compared with the slide?" Expect: left of the slide, with the page this way up.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one place. Longer: two places for one thing.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The ___ is ___ the ___, from here."

### Tomorrow

Day 22 opens with s03-X21; for errors K and A, turn the card the same way up for everyone and name left and right first.
