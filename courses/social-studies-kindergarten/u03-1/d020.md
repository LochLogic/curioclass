# Day 20: Words that say where

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 19 closed unit 2. Set out the toys and hang wonder cards 1 and 2. Marks: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook and wonder wall

Review: the wonder wall (unit 1). The puppet asks, "Where are the crayons?" Point: "Over there." Then: "Next to the paper." Read cards 1 and 2; scribe three wonders.

**Ask.** Which answer helped? Expect: "next to the paper".

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Say "from here" each time. Car next to the box; near, then far from the basket. Car left of the box; from the other side, right. Puppet: "Left is where my hand points" (error A); say "left of the box, from here." Basket behind the box, car in front. Toy child beside the basket; puppet: "between" (error J); add the box: "between the basket and the box."

**Ask.** Where is the toy? What is between them? Expect: a position word, a second thing.

#### 8 min. Supported work

Pairs with a tray take turns placing a toy and saying where. Name behind, in front of, next to. Hear today's six, 40 seconds each. Others draw the toy and a thing next to it.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 20 ("a pretend card"); all point to the child between; mark s03-X20. The six say where, two at a time. "Next to the drum" is error J. Tonight slip each pair a new position word.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No toys: use a cup, a spoon and a lid. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the class record.

- **5 min. Where is it?** Put a small thing next to a box. Ask where it is; slide it and ask again, from where your child sits.
  **Say.** "Where is it now?" Expect: next to, near, far from, left or right of the box, "from here".
- **5 min. Between.** Put a thing beside a cup, then a spoon on its other side.
  **Say.** "What is between?" Expect: the thing, between the cup and the spoon.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the thing and one other thing it is next to.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X20]] Draw three things in a row. Your child circles the middle one and says where it is.
  **Say.** "Which is between? Say where it is." Expect: the middle one, between the other two.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop near and far. Longer: two places for one toy.

### Words and supports

Near, far, left, right, behind, in front of, between, next to. Frame: "The ___ is ___ the ___, from here." Touch each toy if you cannot see.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 opens with s03-X20 and your slips; for error J, add a second thing beside the toy child.
