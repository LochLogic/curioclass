# Wonder wall cards (day 20)

The cards that start the unit's wonder wall. Large print and one picture on each card, so a child who does not read can tell them apart. The class's own wonders are added on blank cards the same size, scribed without names.

**Card 1, the unit question.** Words: "How can we show someone where things are?" Picture: two hands, one pointing and one holding a small map.

**Card 2.** Words: "Which words help us locate things?" Picture: a toy car beside a box.

**Card 3.** Words: "What can maps and globes show?" Picture: a globe beside a flat map.

**Card 4.** Words: "How can pictures stand for places?" Picture: a small tree picture beside a tree.

**Card 5.** Words: "How does an address help locate a school?" Picture: a school beside a street sign with a number. The card says "a school", never a child's own address.

Day 20 posts cards 1 and 2 and three wonders. Cards 3 to 5 are posted on days 22, 24 and 27 when the section opens. On day 29 the teacher reads the wonders and marks the ones the unit answered.

**A puppet for day 20.** A folded-paper puppet who asks where the crayons are. First it is answered with a point and "over there". Then the teacher says where they are with words: beside the paper. The puppet is pretend, and no child is the example of a mistake.
