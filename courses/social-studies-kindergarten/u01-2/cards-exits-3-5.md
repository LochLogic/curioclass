# Exit cards, days 3 to 5

Each card is marked by every child (circle or point) and a spoken question is heard from the day's group of six. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. None reuses a picture from the glue story, the day 3 matching cards, the rule-maker cards or the claim-frame cards.

## Day 3 exit card: wet painting

**Picture:** A top panel shows a wet painting with drips lying flat on a table, next to a folded shirt with clean white sleeves. Below it two rule pictures, each in a circle: (a) the painting on a drying rack with the shirt hanging on a hook across the room; (b) the painting leaning against the cubby where coats hang.

**Mark:** Circle the picture of the rule that helps. **Spoken:** "What rule could help here, and why?"

## Day 4 exit card: yard gate

**Picture:** The edge of a schoolyard with a low wooden gate across the opening to a car lane, closed. A school helper in a green cap drops the latch. On the far side of the gate a car waits in the lane. Three pictures appear below, each in a circle: the car waiting in the lane, a child on a swing and a bird on a post.

**Mark:** Circle what the gate keeps children away from. **Spoken:** "Why was this gate closed, and what does the rule do for children?"

## Day 5 exit card: choosing the song

**Picture:** Four plain paper-bag puppets, all the same, sit in a row in front of a music box, told apart by a hat, a scarf, a bow and nothing; the red hat came out of a hat bag drawn at random. Below, two rule pictures in circles: (a) only the puppet with the red hat touching the music box while three puppets look on; (b) a turn card passing from puppet to puppet, with a different puppet touching the music box each time.

**Mark:** Circle the picture of the fairer rule. **Spoken:** "Why is this not fair to the other puppets? What could we do instead?"
