# Exit cards, days 3 to 5

Each card is marked by every child (circle or point) and a spoken question is heard from the day's group of six. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. None reuses a picture from the glue story, the day 3 matching cards, the rule-maker cards or the claim-frame cards.

## Day 3 exit card: wet painting

**Picture:** A top panel shows a wet painting with drips lying flat on a table, next to a folded shirt with clean white sleeves. Below it two rule pictures, each in a circle: (a) the painting on a drying rack with the shirt hanging on a hook across the room; (b) the painting leaning against the cubby where coats hang.

**Mark:** Circle the picture of the rule that helps. The key is (a): wet paintings stay on the drying rack so clothes stay clean. **Spoken:** "What rule could help here, and why?" Accept another supported proposal.

## Day 4 exit card: pond gate

**Picture:** A path beside a pond with a low wooden gate across it, closed. A park worker in a plain vest drops the latch. On the far side of the gate, water ripples. Three people appear below, each in a circle: the park worker in a vest, a child on a bench and a person walking a dog.

**Mark:** Circle the person who made the rule. The key is the park worker. **Spoken:** "Why did they close this gate, and what does the rule do for visitors?" Key only the reason: it keeps visitors away from the water.

## Day 5 exit card: choosing the song

**Picture:** Four puppets of different colors sit in a row in front of a music box. One has a red hat. Below, two rule pictures in circles: (a) only the puppet with the red hat touching the music box while three puppets look on; (b) a turn card passing from puppet to puppet, with a different puppet touching the music box each time.

**Mark:** Circle the picture of the fairer rule. The key is (b). **Spoken:** "Why might the first rule fail? What could replace it?" Answer: the others never get a choice; take turns so all can choose.
