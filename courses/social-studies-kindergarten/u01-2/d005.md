# Day 5: Our class rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Draw three candidate-rule pictures from your room (the scissors tub, the book bin, the door line): the thing looked after, and beside it the reason. Keep a rule-maker card to hand. Choose today's six, not yet heard on a spoken exit.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Hold up a maker card from day 4; ask who made it and why. If many gave error B on day 4's exit (s01-X4, "because grown-ups said so"), set it beside "the gate keeps children out of the car lane" and point to the second.

#### 6 min. The claim frame

Show the coats-on-hooks card. Model aloud: "I think we need this rule, because the coats stay dry and easy to find." Then a child's voice: "Why do we have this rule?" and the answer, pointing to the puddle picture. Children echo both.

**Ask.** Why do we hang coats on the hooks? Expect: so they stay dry, or so we can find them. For "because it is a good rule" (error E), set it beside "because it keeps coats off the floor".

#### 10 min. Supported work

Show the three candidate pictures. The class picks two, and says "I think we need this rule, because ..." for each. Write the dictated words beside a quick picture on chart paper; finish the symbols after class. This is Our Class Charter, real unlike the invented cards. Adults keep rules about water, roads and equipment.

Show the what-if picture. Ask: "What if a rule does not work?" Agree: tell the class and the teacher, then try a change together. For "I can change it myself" (error J), set it beside "we all agree to try a change".

Each child draws one charter rule and what it helps.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles on the exit card (s01-X5); read aloud: "Circle the picture of the fairer rule." Ask the six and mark the list: "Why is this not fair to the other puppets? What could we do instead?" Right: the others never get a choice; take turns so all can choose. "It is fine, it is a rule" is error B; "I would just change it so I go first" is error J; "it is fair because it is fair" is error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart paper: use the back of a page. No exit card: draw a music box and four puppets.

**Skip.** The reopen and the class vote.

- **5 min. The frame.** Hang a coat on a hook. Say the frame.
  **Say.** "I think we need this rule, because ..." Expect: the coats stay dry or easy to find.
- **5 min. Your rules.** Pick two rules for your table. Write your child's because beside each.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one rule and what it helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X5]] Draw a music box and puppets. Your child circles taking turns.
  **Say.** "Why is it not fair? What could we do?" Expect: others never choose; take turns.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pick one rule. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Charter: the rules our class made. Frame: "I think we need this rule, because ...". A pointed card or aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 teaches talk rules for the circle: listen, wait for a turn, answer what was said. The charter stays on the wall; day 9 lays out the rule pictures again.
