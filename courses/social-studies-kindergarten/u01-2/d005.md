# Day 5: Our class rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the three candidate-rule pictures low on the wall, apart. Choose today's six: the day 5 group on the record sheet. Teacher key: key-exits-3-5.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Hold up a maker card from day 4; ask who decided and why. If many gave error B on day 4's exit (s01-X4), set "because grown-ups said so" beside "the gate keeps children out of the car lane".

#### 8 min. The claim frame

Show the coats-on-hooks card. Model: "I think we need this rule, because the coats stay dry and easy to find." Then ask in a child's voice "Why do we have this rule?" and answer, pointing to the puddle picture.

**Ask.** Why do we hang coats on the hooks? Expect: so they stay dry, or so we can find them. For "because it is a good rule" (error E), set it beside "because it keeps coats off the floor".

#### 14 min. Supported work

Each child drops a counter in a closed cup under the picture they most want, or passes; count aloud; the two with the most stay. For each say "I think we need this rule, because ..." and take a because from the children. Write the dictated words beside a quick picture. This is Our Class Charter, real unlike the invented cards.

Show the what-if picture: "If a rule does not work, we tell the class and the teacher, and try a change together." For "I can change it myself" (error J), set it beside "we all agree to try a change".

Each child draws one charter rule and what it helps. Write the six's one thing done and one next step on the record sheet; read it to each child privately by tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles on the exit card (s01-X5); read aloud: "Circle the picture of the fairer rule." Then ask the six, one at a time: "Why is this not fair to the other puppets?" (s01-X5b) and "What could we do instead?" (s01-X5c). A child may pass or point. Right: the others never choose; take turns so all can. "It is fine, it is a rule" is error B; "I change the rule myself" is J; "fair because it is fair" is E; "so I go first" is none: model the turn rule.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart paper: use the back of a page. No exit card: draw a music box and four puppets.

**Skip.** The reopen and the counters.

- **5 min. The frame.** Hang a coat on a hook. Say the frame.
  **Say.** "I think we need this rule, because ..." Expect: the coats stay dry or easy to find.
- **5 min. Your rules.** Pick two rules for your table, your child dropping a counter or a pebble in a cup beside each pick. Write your child's because beside each.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one rule and what it helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X5]] [[s01-X5b]] [[s01-X5c]] Draw a music box and puppets. Your child circles taking turns.
  **Say.** "Why is it not fair? What could we do?" Expect: others never choose; take turns.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pick one rule. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Charter: the rules our class made. Frame: "I think we need this rule, because ...".

### Tomorrow

Day 6 teaches talk rules: listen, wait for a turn, answer what was said. The charter stays on the wall; day 9 lays out its rule pictures again.
