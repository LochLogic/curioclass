# Rule-maker cards (day 4)

Three picture cards, each showing a place with a rule, who decided it, and what it is for. Say who decided and the setting before you use each card: "The town decided this. It is for the street." The people who paint or fix a sign carry out the rule; the maker is the body that decided it. Children set each card on its maker card and on a reason card (safe, fair or helps everyone learn). Plain black line drawings, no brand names, and no printed words anywhere in a picture (a sign is a picture sign). Crew and staff wear plain vests with no logos.

## Card 1: the crosswalk

**Picture:** A street with white stripes across it and a small figure of a person walking on a sign beside the road. A car has stopped before the stripes. A child and an adult cross hand in hand. In a small corner picture, two road workers in plain vests paint the stripes with a roller.

**Maker (decided):** the town. Road workers painted the stripes. **Place:** the street. **Reason:** safe.

## Card 2: the library's quiet-voices sign

**Picture:** A library with tall shelves and a low reading table. A sign on a stand shows a smiling face with one finger over its lips and a soft curved line for a whisper. Two children whisper over one open book. In a corner picture, a librarian with a cart of books smiles beside the sign.

**Maker (decided):** the library people. The librarian put the sign up. **Place:** the library. **Reason:** helps everyone learn (everyone can read and think).

## Card 3: the playground sign

**Picture:** A playground with a slide. A picture sign by the steps shows a child sliding down sitting up, feet first. One child climbs the steps while another waits at the bottom. In a corner picture, a park worker in a plain vest fixes the sign onto its post.

**Maker (decided):** the park office. Park staff fixed the sign. **Place:** the park. **Reason:** safe, and fair (each child gets a turn on the slide).

## Maker cards and reason cards (the sort)

Six small cards, one picture each, no printed words. Maker cards: the town (a small row of houses and a road), the library people (a library door with a book above it), the park office (a small hut by a park gate). Reason cards: safe (a shield), fair (two hands taking turns with one ball between them), helps everyone learn (an open book). Pairs set each rule-maker card on one maker card and one reason card.

## Places strip

Three small cards for the pointing step, each a single picture: a street corner, the library door, the park gate. Children point to one place where a rule helps. They do not tell about their own homes.
