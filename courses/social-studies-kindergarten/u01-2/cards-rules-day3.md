# Matching cards (day 3 supported work)

Three rule cards and three "what it protects" cards. Pairs match each rule to what it protects and say safe, fair or helps everyone learn. Plain black line drawings, no printed words, no people's faces shown in close-up, no brand names. None of these repeats a page of Sharing the Glue.

## Rule card 1: take turns

**Picture:** Two children stand at a drinking fountain. The first drinks; the second waits a step behind a painted line on the floor. A small arrow shows the second child's turn coming next.

## Rule card 2: wipe the table

**Picture:** A hand holds a damp cloth and wipes a table after painting. Beside it sits a clean paintbrush in a jar.

## Rule card 3: walk inside

**Picture:** Two children walk side by side down a hall, one foot forward each, arms at their sides. A dotted line shows a calm, steady path to the classroom door.

## Protects card A: a turn for each

**Picture:** Two empty open hands, side by side, each holding a small tick-mark; a drinking fountain stands between them. (Matches take turns: fair.)

## Protects card B: a clean table for the next person

**Picture:** A bare, clean table with a fresh sheet of paper and two empty chairs. (Matches wipe the table: fair, so the next person can use the table, or helps everyone learn, because the room is ready. Either reason is right; the card shows the next person, not learning alone.)

## Protects card C: people who stay upright

**Picture:** A hallway with three children walking apart from one another, none touching, none falling. (Matches walk inside: safe.)

## How the cards are used

The key is one match for each rule, but a pair that gives a different pictured reason ("take turns is also safe because nobody pushes") is correct if the picture supports it. Pairs describe the cards, not themselves. The pairs then say aloud: "This rule is safe or fair or helps us learn."
