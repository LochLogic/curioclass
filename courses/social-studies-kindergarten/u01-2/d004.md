# Day 4: Who made the rule?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Envelope the cards and places strip, one set a pair. Choose today's six: the day 4 group on the record sheet. Pick the bridge group from s01-R1.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Hold up the take-turns card; ask what it protects: a turn for each. If many gave error B on day 3's exit (s01-X3), set "the teacher says so" beside "wet paintings on the rack keep clothes clean" and point to the second.

#### 8 min. Rule-maker cards

Say who decided and the place: "The town decided this rule. It is for the street." Show the crosswalk (road workers painted it), the library sign (the library people decided; the librarian put it up) and the playground sign (the park office decided; park staff fixed it).

Model "Who decided? What is it for?" with "The town decided. It keeps people safe." Children echo both.

**Ask.** Who decided about the library sign? What is it for? Expect: the library people; so everyone can read and think.

#### 14 min. Supported work

Pairs set each card on its maker card and a reason card: a shield for safe, two hands for fair, an open book for helps everyone learn. Crosswalk is safe, library helps everyone learn, playground is safe and fair.

Children point to one place on the places strip where a rule helps. They do not tell about home. For error H, hold up two things: "Which crayon is mine to choose. The crosswalk is for everyone."

Each child draws a rule and who decided it. Meet the s01-R1 group and any child still unseen: show a toy and say "I wonder why it is here"; then "It is a toy." Which one asks? Add a new wonder to the wall.

Write the six's one thing done and one next step on the record sheet; read it to each child privately by tomorrow. These notes are practice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles on the exit card (s01-X4); read aloud: "Circle what the gate keeps children away from." (The car.) Ask the six and mark the list: "Why was this gate closed, and what does the rule do for children?" Right: it keeps children out of the car lane. "Because grown-ups said so" is error B; "children can open it if they want" is error H.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a crosswalk, a book shelf and a slide. No exit card: draw a gate and a car.

**Skip.** The reopen and the sort.

- **5 min. Cards.** Draw a crosswalk. Say who decided: the town.
  **Say.** "Who decided? What is it for?" Expect: the town; to be safe.
- **5 min. Places.** Draw the library and a slide. Ask who decided each rule: the library people, the park office.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one rule and who decided it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4]] Draw a gate across a car lane, a swing and a bird. Your child circles what the gate keeps children away from.
  **Say.** "Why is the gate closed?" Expect: so children stay out of the car lane.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two cards. Longer: a second place.

### Words and supports

Decide: choose the rule for a place. Frame: "Who decided? What is it for?"

### Tomorrow

Day 5 reopens with s01-X4: for error B, one picture and one sentence. Then the class proposes its own rules. Tape up the three candidate-rule pictures first.
