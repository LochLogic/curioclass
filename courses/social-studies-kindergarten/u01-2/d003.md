# Day 3: Why we have rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Envelope the six rule cards, one set a pair. Set the glue stick and dolls by the pages. Choose today's six: the day 3 group on the record sheet. Pick the bridge group from s01-R2.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Read the wonder wall. Hold up a frame of Two Drawing Tables: the cup between the puppets lets both reach. If many gave error K on day 2's exit (s01-X2), lay the painting and clay cards together: "Both need room." A small group takes the rest later.

#### 8 min. Sharing the Glue

Say: "We made this picture. Mimi and Bo are cloth dolls; the story is made up." Read the pages, then page 12: with the turn rule both trees get leaves; without it Bo waits and the glue dries. No one was bad.

Model "Why do we have this rule?" and an answer, "To be fair, each doll gets a turn" (pages 8 and 9). Children echo both, then ask it of a rule card (error F).

**Ask.** Why do Mimi and Bo have a rule? Expect: so both get a turn, or so the glue stays good. For "because grown-ups say so" (error B), set it beside "the turn rule means both dolls get the glue".

#### 14 min. Supported work

Pairs open the six rule cards. Read aloud: "Match each rule to what it protects. Say: this rule is safe, fair, or helps everyone learn." Take turns is fair, walk inside is safe, and wipe the table is fair (the next person can use it) or helps everyone learn; another pictured reason is right too.

Each child draws a rule and what it helps. Meet the s01-R2 group, and any child still unseen: dolls and a glue stick, once with the turn rule, once with "do it because I said". Which gives a reason?

Write the six's one thing done and one next step on the record sheet; read it to each child privately by tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Every child circles on the exit card (s01-X3); read aloud: "Circle the picture of the rule that helps." Ask the six and mark the list: "What rule could help here, and why?" A child may pass or point. The drying rack, or any rule naming what it protects, is right. "Because the teacher says so" is error B.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No dolls: use two spoons. No cards: draw a drinking fountain, a cloth and a hallway.

**Skip.** The reopen and the small group.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell about two dolls and one glue stick. First there is no rule: one doll glues and glue dries. Then a turn rule.
  **Say.** "Why do they have this rule?" Expect: so both get a turn.
- **5 min. Cards.** Draw take turns, wipe the table, walk inside. Ask what each one keeps good.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one rule and what it helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]] Draw a painting next to a shirt. Your child draws a rule that helps.
  **Say.** "What rule could help? Why?" Expect: keep the painting away from the shirt, so the shirt stays clean.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read pages 1, 2, 5, 7, 8, 11. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Rule: what we agree to do together. Fair: everyone gets a turn. Frame: "The rule is ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 4 reopens with s01-X3: for error B, one picture and one sentence. Then rules outside school: who made each, and why.
