# Job Left Unfinished

Day 11 hook. Ten pages, read aloud by the teacher. Made by Curio Class for this class to think with, so children can think about a job. It is an invented story and not a record of what our class did. Art: plain line drawings, thick outlines, no brands, no writing in the pictures. Children are of many skin tones, hair and clothes; one child uses a wheelchair and one wears glasses. Dev, who uses the wheelchair, is at the paint table mixing colors, never the one who is stopped. The puppet is a paper-bag puppet with button eyes and no clothes.

**Page 1.** Text: "Today Tuli is the plant waterer. Tuli is a puppet. Tuli is happy to have a job."
Picture: a classroom. A paper-bag puppet on a child's hand stands by a window shelf holding one potted plant. A tall jug sits on the shelf beside the plant.

**Page 2.** Text: "Tuli takes the jug. It is full of water."
Picture: Tuli, held by a smiling teacher's hand from below, grips the jug handle. Water sloshes inside.

**Page 3.** Text: "Tuli pours. Splash, splash. The plant drinks."
Picture: water pours from the jug into the pot. Small drops. The plant leans up, leaves open.

**Page 4.** Text: "Tuli says, 'I watered the plant! I am done!' Tuli walks to the rug."
Picture: Tuli is walking toward a rug at the right edge. The jug stands on the floor in the middle of a wide walkway between tables, far from the shelf.

**Page 5.** Text: "Ana comes down the walkway with a box of crayons. Ana wants to get to the paint table, where Dev is mixing colors."
Picture: Ana, a girl with a braid, walks along the walkway toward the jug. At the paint table behind it, Dev, a boy with dark curly hair in a wheelchair, stirs a jar of blue paint.

**Page 6.** Text: "Oh! The jug is in the way. Ana stops, with her box of crayons."
Picture: Ana stands still before the jug. Her face is puzzled, not cross. A question mark floats above her. Dev, at the paint table, looks up.

**Page 7.** Text: "Stop and think. What still needs doing?"
Picture: the same walkway, but empty of children. Only the jug stands in the middle, and a question mark hangs over it. (The teacher pauses here. Children point or say. Do not give the answer.)

**Page 8.** Text: "Tuli looks back from the rug. 'Oh!' says Tuli."
Picture: Tuli at the rug turns to look at the jug, mouth open round.

**Page 9.** Text: "Tuli carries the jug back to the shelf. Tuli wipes up a little drip."
Picture: Tuli at the shelf sets the jug beside the plant. A cloth in the other hand wipes a small puddle. The walkway behind is clear.

**Page 10.** Text: "Now the walkway is clear. Ana walks to the paint table. 'Thank you, Tuli,' says Ana. Dev holds up the blue paint. Now the job is finished."
Picture: Ana walking on to the paint table with her crayons, where Dev holds up the jar. Tuli beams at the shelf. Plant, jug and clear walkway all in view.

**Teacher note.** The side-by-side pictures of day 11 are page 6 (unfinished) and page 10 (finished).
