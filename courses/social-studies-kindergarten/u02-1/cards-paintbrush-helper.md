# Exit card 11: the paintbrush helper

New card, never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". One picture, a quarter page, with three small marks children can circle. Plain line drawing, no words except the label "brushes".

**Picture.** A classroom corner with an art table. A girl with tight curls and a smock stands at the sink end, smiling, her hands clean. Four paintbrushes lie on the floor in a little pool of paint-water on the walkway side of the table, bristles wet. Beside the sink is an empty brush holder, a tall cup. At the table two other children, one with glasses and one with a hearing aid, are painting and look toward the brushes.

**Circle choices (three marks on the picture).**
1. The wet brushes on the floor (the job still needs doing). This is the right mark.
2. The brush holder, empty (also meets: it shows where the brushes go).
3. The smiling girl (does not meet: appearance, not what is left to do).

Teacher says, reads aloud: "She painted already. Circle what still needs doing."
