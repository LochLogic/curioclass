# Exit card 11: the snack helper

New card, never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". One picture, a quarter page, with three small marks children can point to. Plain line drawing, no words except the label "snack".

**Picture.** A classroom snack table. Nell, a pretend child, sits at the end of the table, smiling, with her hands in her lap. Four snack cups stand in a neat row on the table. An empty napkin basket sits at the other end. Three children stand in a line near the table, waiting, looking at the basket. No other picture of a job, a walkway or a puppet.

**Points (three marks on the picture).**
1. The empty napkin basket (the job still needs doing). This is the right mark.
2. The row of snack cups (does not meet: that part is done).
3. The smiling child (does not meet: appearance, not what is left to do).

Teacher says, reads aloud: "Nell set out the snack cups and sat down. Point to what still needs doing."
