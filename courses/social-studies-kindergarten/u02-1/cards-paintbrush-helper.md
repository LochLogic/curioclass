# Exit card 11: the paintbrush helper

New card, never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". One picture, a quarter page, with three small marks children can circle. Plain line drawing, no words except the label "brushes".

**Picture.** A classroom corner with an art table. Tuli, the paper-bag puppet from day 11's story, stands at the sink end, smiling, no brush in sight. Four paintbrushes lie on the floor in a little pool of paint-water on the walkway side of the table, bristles wet. Beside the sink is an empty brush holder, a tall cup. No child is in the picture; two other paper-bag puppets are painting at the table.

**Circle choices (three marks on the picture).**
1. The wet brushes on the floor (the job still needs doing). This is the right mark.
2. The brush holder, empty (also meets: it shows where the brushes go).
3. The smiling puppet (does not meet: appearance, not what is left to do).

Teacher says, reads aloud: "Tuli painted already. Circle what still needs doing."
