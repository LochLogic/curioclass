# Day 12: Doing a job all the way

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out several sets of the job cards. Draw the day 11 corner finished. Put an envelope of sorting cards out on each table.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Show day 11's paintbrush helper card beside your finished drawing ([[s02-P12-1]]). Children point to the finished one. Model: "I think this one is finished, because the brushes are in the holder."

#### 4 min. Worked example

Model the door holder with a puppet on a stool: hold until the last child is through; ask the puppet, "May I move your stool?" and wait; leave the door as found. Say: "Helpful looks like holding until everyone is through. Respectful sounds like 'May I?'"

#### 5 min. Take a job

Each child takes a job card, or shares one. Each says to a partner, "My job is ___. ___ counts on it."

#### 6 min. Do the job through to the end

Children do their jobs. Watch the six for finishing and asking first; note it on their picture pages. Every child draws their job and who counts on it; write their words.

#### 5 min. Our Jobs Chart

On a large sheet make two columns. Children draw what helpful looks like and say what respectful sounds like; write their words.

#### 2 min. Exit

Cards start in an open tray between each pair; a child may keep theirs in an envelope. Say, "Show how to finish, and be respectful to your partner." Watch three pairs (the six) and mark s02-X12. Then show the exit pictures; every child points to the finished one or passes; the six place a token. Right: every card put away, asks first, envelope ready. Taking a card without asking is error H: show it with puppets, never in the pair; leaving cards on the table is error A.

That evening note the six's next steps on the record sheet. Read it privately on days 13 to 15; write the rest when each child is heard.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sorting cards: use paper squares or toy blocks. No chart: use one page with two columns.

**Skip.** The partner exit and the class chart.

- **5 min. Show.** Hold a door for a family member and say "May I?" before moving anything.
  **Say.** "What does helpful look like? What does respectful sound like?" Expect: holding until everyone is through; "May I?"
- **5 min. A job.** Your child picks one job: set cards in a pile. They say, "My job is ___. ___ counts on it."
- **10 min. Do it.** Your child puts every card away in a box and leaves it ready.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X12]] Your child shows how to put cards away and asks for the one in your hand.
  **Say.** "Is it finished?" Expect: cards in the box, box ready.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one round of jobs. Longer: name a second person who counts.

### Words and supports

Helpful: easy for others. Respectful: "May I?" Pointing is an answer.
