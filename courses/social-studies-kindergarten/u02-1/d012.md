# Day 12: Doing a job all the way

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out job cards with copies: paper passers, shelf tidiers, table wipers, scrap pickers, one door holder, one plant waterer. Draw the day 11 corner finished. Put an envelope of sorting cards at each table. Choose today's six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Show day 11's paintbrush helper card beside your finished drawing ([[s02-P12-1]]). Children point to the finished one. Model: "I think this one is finished, because the brushes are in the holder." If many gave error A on day 11's exit (s02-X11), set the unfinished card beside page 10 of the story.

#### 4 min. Worked example

Model the door holder at a door with a puppet on a stool: hold until the last child is through; ask the puppet, "May I move your stool?" and wait; leave the door as found. Say: "Helpful looks like holding until everyone is through. Respectful sounds like 'May I?'" Children say both back.

#### 5 min. Take a job

Each child takes a job card; two may share one. Each says to a partner, "My job is ___. ___ counts on it." No job belongs to a boy or a girl.

#### 6 min. Do the job through to the end

Children do their jobs. Watch for the finishing step and for asking before taking; note it on the picture page (practice, no score). Early finishers draw their job and who counts on it; write their words. Others finish later.

#### 5 min. Our Jobs Chart

On a large sheet make two columns. Children draw what helpful looks like and say what respectful sounds like; write their words. It shows how our charter rule looks and sounds.

#### 2 min. Exit

Pairs take an envelope; leave two cards in one partner's hand as the pair begins. Say, "Show how to finish, and be respectful to your partner." Watch three pairs (six children) and mark s02-X12; "not seen" if neither holds a card. Then show the three exit pictures; every child points to the finished one, the six place a token. Right: every card put away, asks first, envelope ready. Pulling a card from a hand is error H; leaving cards on the table is error A. For H, practise "May I?" with puppets.

That evening write one next step on each child's page, such as "Put the jug back too." Read it privately on days 13 to 15.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sorting cards: use a deck of cards or toy blocks. No chart: use one page with two columns.

**Skip.** The partner exit and the class chart.

- **5 min. Show.** Hold a door for a family member and say "May I?" before moving anything.
  **Say.** "What does helpful look like? What does respectful sound like?" Expect: holding until everyone is through; "May I?"
- **5 min. A job.** Your child picks one job: set cards in a pile. They say, "My job is ___. ___ counts on it."
- **10 min. Do it.** Your child puts every card away in a box and leaves it ready.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X12]] Your child shows how to put cards away and asks for the one in your hand.
  **Say.** "Is it finished?" Expect: cards in the box, box ready.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one round of jobs. Longer: name a second person who counts.

### Words and supports

Helpful: easy for others. Respectful: "May I?" Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 opens with a vote to name the class plant, then the class names real problems in the room. Jobs repeat in routines.
