# Day 11: Who counts on a job?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep Our Class Charter and the talk rules on the wall from day 10. Set the plant and jug out. Choose today's six, children not yet heard.

### The day

#### 3 min. Hook

Read Job Left Unfinished, pages 1 to 7.

**Ask.** What still needs doing? Expect: a point to the jug.

#### 2 min. Wonder wall

Post the unit question, "How can each of us help our class?" Add: "Who counts on a job?"

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Pairs tell, looking at the charter. Ask: "Which rule helps people share our room? Why?" Listen at five pairs; note the words (s02-R11, no score). Count thumbs up for "I can say why". A child may pass. If many have no reason, two puppets act one rule kept, then not.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Say, "Curio Class made this story for our class to think with. It is not a record of what we did."

Show the job cards, two minutes each: line leader, plant waterer, door holder. Say what the job is, who counts on it, and what finished looks like. Teach job and responsible with the pictures.

#### 5 min. Pairs

Each pair takes one job card. One partner taps who counts on the job; the other tells what finished looks like. Each child draws the finished look.

#### 3 min. Back to the hook

Read pages 8 to 10. Set page 6 beside page 10.

**Ask.** Who counts on Tuli's job? Expect: Ana, Dev, the plant. "Tuli watered it, so it is done" is error A.

#### 2 min. Exit

Show the snack card: "Nell set out the cups and sat down. Point to what still needs doing." All point ([[s02-X11]]). Ask three: "Who counts on this job?" ([[s02-X11b]]) Right: the basket; the waiting children. "Nothing, she did it" is error A.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture story: tell it with a cup and a toy. No card: draw a table, a row of cups and an empty napkin basket.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the charter.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell about Tuli, a puppet who waters a plant and leaves the jug in the walkway.
  **Say.** "What still needs doing?" Expect: put the jug back.
- **5 min. Three jobs.** Say line leader, plant waterer, door holder. Your child taps who counts on each.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws what finished looks like for one job.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Show a snack table, no napkins. Your child points to what still needs doing ([[s02-X11]]).
  **Say.** "Who counts on this job?" ([[s02-X11b]]) Expect: the waiting children.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two job cards. Longer: a second person who counts.

### Words and supports

Job: your part of our class work. Responsible: you finish it, so others can count on you. A tap or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 opens with this exit as a finished and a not finished picture. If many gave error A (s02-X11), set the two side by side first. Then children take real jobs and finish them.
