# Day 11: Who counts on a job?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep Our Class Charter and the talk rules on the wall, as day 10 left them. Set the plant and jug out. Choose today's six: children not yet heard.

### The day

#### 3 min. Hook

Read Job Left Unfinished, pages 1 to 7, showing each picture.

**Ask.** What still needs doing? Expect: a point to the jug. Do not give the answer.

#### 2 min. Wonder wall

Post the unit question, "How can each of us help our class?" Co-write one more: "Who counts on a job?"

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Pairs turn and tell, looking at the charter. Read aloud: "Which rule helps people share our room? Why?" Listen at five pairs and note the words (s02-R11, no score). Ask for a thumb up for "I can say why" and count aloud. A child may pass. If many have no reason, two puppets act one rule kept, then not kept.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Say, "Curio Class made this story for our class to think with. It is not a record of what we did."

Show the job cards, two minutes each: line leader, plant waterer, door holder. Say what the job is, who counts on it, and what finished looks like. Teach with the pictures: job and responsible. Anyone may have any job.

#### 5 min. Pairs

Each pair takes one job card. One partner taps who counts on the job; the other tells what finished looks like. Listen at three pairs. Each child draws the finished look on the page.

#### 3 min. Back to the hook

Read pages 8 to 10. Set page 6 beside page 10.

**Ask.** Who counts on Tuli's job? Expect: Dev, Ana, the plant. "Tuli watered it, so it is done" is error A.

#### 2 min. Exit

Show the paintbrush helper card. Read aloud: "She painted already. Point to what still needs doing." Every child points. Ask the six: "Who counts on this job, and what still needs doing?" Mark the class list (s02-X11). Right: the other painters; rinse the brushes. "Nothing, she painted already" is error A.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture story: tell it with a cup and a toy. No card: draw a table, wet brushes on the floor, an empty cup.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the charter.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell about Tuli, a puppet who waters a plant and leaves the jug in the doorway.
  **Say.** "What still needs doing?" Expect: put the jug back.
- **5 min. Three jobs.** Say line leader, plant waterer, door holder. Your child taps who counts on each.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws what finished looks like for one job.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X11]] Show your drawing of the brushes. Your child taps what still needs doing.
  **Say.** "Who counts on this job?" Expect: the other painters.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two job cards. Longer: a second person who counts on the job.

### Words and supports

Job: your part of our class work. Responsible: you finish it, so others can count on you. Frame: "___ counts on it." A tap or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 opens with this exit as a finished and a not finished picture. If many gave error A on day 11's exit (s02-X11), put the two side by side first. Then children take real jobs and finish them.
