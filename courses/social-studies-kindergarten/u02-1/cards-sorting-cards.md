# Exit card 12: the sorting cards

New picture pair, never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". Children place one token on the finished job. The action (a real partner job with an envelope of sorting cards) is the evidence; the pictures are for the mark.

**Picture A (not finished).** Two children, one with a black bob and one with a shaved head and freckles, at a table. Sorting cards (plain shapes: circle, square, star) lie spread across the table. The envelope is flat and empty beside them. Both are smiling and walking toward the rug.

**Picture B (finished).** The same two children and table. The table is bare. Every card is inside the open envelope, standing on its edge ready, and one child holds the envelope toward the next pair, two children at the edge of the picture reaching to take it.

**Picture C (not respectful).** One child pulls a shape card out of the partner's hand while the partner's mouth is open round.

Token goes on Picture B. Picture C is a talking picture for error H, never a trap.
