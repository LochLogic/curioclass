# Unit 2 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. R1 is the day 11 retrieval (no score): note the words or the point. Each E
column is one spoken exit: one tick when the child answers it, by pointing, saying or drawing, with a puppet or a
partner if that helps. Beside it, two dated job cells, J1 and J2: on days 13 to 17 the six chosen that day are the children watched at their job in routines; write the date and a tick when a child finishes it. The End columns are the closing check on day 18, one cell for each look-for met; End 5 has one cell each for fairness (with putting right), caring or responsibility, and in charge. A child may
pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
