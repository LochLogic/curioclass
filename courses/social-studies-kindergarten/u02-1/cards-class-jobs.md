# Three job cards (taught on day 11)

Three cards, plain line drawings, thick outlines, a one-word label under each. Each card has the same three small boxes along the bottom: a person, a finished look, a word. Children of many skin tones; gender is mixed across cards and the teacher says so when a job rotates.

**Line leader.** Label: "line". Picture: a child using a walker, in a headband, walks at the front of a short line of four children, all walking at an easy pace. Finished look: the line stops at the door and everyone is in place.

**Plant waterer.** Label: "plant". Picture: a boy with a small watering can beside a potted plant on a low shelf. Finished look: can back on the shelf, floor dry.

**Door holder.** Label: "door". Picture: a child in a wheelchair holds a door open while a child with short hair and a child with a backpack pass through. Finished look: the door is closed softly, nothing in the way.
