# Day 39: Show what you know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Collect the revised day 36 log from day 38. Add a heard column to the record sheet; pick today's ten. Give no help.

### The day

#### 2 min. Read and introduce

Say all four are made up. Read aloud "Ari dropped a mitten. Jo found it. Jo returned it to Ari." (Lost Mitten) and "Neighbors mark where a path will go. They dig a shallow trench. They lay stones. People use the finished path." (Making a Path). Show School Library and Bird Nest by picture.

#### 8 min. Individual responses

Everyone marks alone; collect each before any answer is shown. Order the four sets, first, next, last ([[s04-E1]]); point to the card just before the starred stone card ([[s04-E1b]]). On the art display log: circle today ([[s04-E2]]), token on last week ([[s04-E2c]]), second token on next month ([[s04-E2d]]). The day 38 revised log is the earlier time-word evidence.

#### 10 min. Hearing and drawing

Read each maker, place and month and year only: Canned Goods (Russell Lee, Pie Town, New Mexico, October 1940) and Going to School (John Vachon, Nebraska, October 1938), each beside today's plain photograph. Each child chooses one old photograph, points to a clue ([[s04-E3]]), circles what changed and tokens what stayed ([[s04-E3b]]), and draws the change.

Hear today's ten, one minute each, three asks: "When did the drawing session happen? Use a time word." ([[s04-E2b]]); "What can this one photograph not show us?" ([[s04-E3c]]); "Which change made the biggest difference to everyday work, and why?" ([[s04-E4]]). Scribe a few words; if missing, ask once "What stayed the same?" Others not heard stay not yet observed.

#### 2 min. Close the wonder wall

Read the wonders; mark the ones the unit answered.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No prints: use a dated photograph from a library book. No tokens: use buttons.

**Skip.** The wall and the log.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Draw three cards for a bird nest, shuffled. Your child orders them, then looks at a dated photograph.
  **Say.** "What came first? Next? Last?" Expect: eggs, hatched, left. "What can the photograph help us learn? What can it not show?" Expect: one detail; not everybody.
- **10 min. Today and yesterday.** Draw a calendar strip with today.
  **Say.** "When did you draw? Use a time word." Expect: today.
- **10 min. Claim.** Pick one change in the photograph.
  **Say.** "Which change made the biggest difference, and why?" Expect: "I choose ..., because ...". Say nothing to help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear five. Longer: the extra five wait for day 40.

### Words and supports

Photograph, clue, changed, stayed the same, because. Frames: "I see ___." "I choose ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 40 re-teaches by part: errors A, H, B, C, F, D, E and G.
