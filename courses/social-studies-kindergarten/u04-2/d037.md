# Day 37: A photograph is a clue

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the Lange print. Pick six. Teacher keys: key-sheet-drawings-and-stand-in.md, key-sheet-then-now-chart.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen and hand-back

From day 36: s04-X36a, s04-X36b (error A) and s04-X36c (error H): link each card to the one before; last at the right end.

#### 12 min. Teaching first

Three: read the Lee print's record: Russell Lee, woman washing her clothes, El Indio, Texas, March 1939. Set it far left on the timeline.

**Ask.** Who made it (review, unit 3), and when? Why? Expect: Lee, March 1939; the record does not say why.

Review "I think ..., because ..." (unit 1); start "I see ...". Describe no one by how they look.

Three: we made the drawings and stand-in today; not old objects. Photograph (review, unit 3: taken at the time), object, clue: something we see that helps us learn.

Four: the then and now chart (review of unit 2's before-and-after chart). Washing: the tool changed; both wash clothes. Slate, tablet: both write.

Two: Moss calls last week's gray drawing old (error C); read each date. Wren says one photograph shows everybody (error F).

#### 4 min. Supported work

Pairs say another "I see ..., I think ..., because ...". Hear today's six, 40 seconds each, with the Lange print: "What can this photograph help us learn about how children got to school long ago?" ([[s04-X37b]]); "How do we know it is old?" ([[s04-X37c]]).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read the Lange print's maker, place, month and year only. Each child has a small copy: "Point to a clue that shows how the children got to school" ([[s04-X37a]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No print: use a dated photograph from a library book, never your family's. No chart: fold paper in two.

**Skip.** The puppets and the gray drawing.

- **5 min. Look first.** Look at the photograph and read its date.
  **Say.** "What do you see? What do you think, because you see it?" Expect: a detail, then "I think ... because I see ...".
- **5 min. Then and now.** Draw columns, then and now: a washboard, a washing machine.
  **Say.** "What changed? What stayed the same?" Expect: the tool; clothes still get clean.
- **5 min. Old or new?**
  **Say.** "How do we know it is old?" Expect: the date, not the gray.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X37a]] [[s04-X37b]] [[s04-X37c]] Use a dated photograph your child has not seen; ask the three questions.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the slate and tablet.

### Words and supports

Photograph, object, clue, old, new, stayed the same.
