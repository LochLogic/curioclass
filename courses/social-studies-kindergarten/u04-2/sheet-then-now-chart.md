# The then and now chart (days 37 and 38)

A paper chart with two columns, **then** and **now**, one row for each pair, and a last row, **what stayed**. Large print, thick lines, picture labels, read aloud.

## Day 37: the teacher's completed examples

- **Washing:** then, the washboard drawing; now, the washing machine drawing. Changed: the tool. Stayed: clothes get clean. Shared purpose written beside it: both help wash clothes.
- **Writing:** then, the slate drawing; now, the tablet drawing. Stayed: both are for writing and drawing.

## Day 38: blank charts

The completed examples are withdrawn. Each pair gets a blank chart with three rows and the sentence frame "both help ___". Partners say "In this picture ..., but ...; both ..." and place the shared purpose in the row.
