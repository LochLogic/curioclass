# Response rows (day 39)

Fresh cards for the individual responses on day 39, items 1 and 2. These are the end items' own cards (rule 4): no other page in the unit shows any of them. The event cards show pictures only, with no time word printed on them; the teacher reads the strip's labels aloud. Every event is invented and each card is printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Large print, thick outlines, no brands. Each child answers alone by marking; no partner answers. Collect every response before showing an answer. Pointing, signs or an aid count the same as speech. Read both texts aloud once, with no help, and say they are made up.

## Row 1: four sets to order

Each set has shuffled cards, a strip with dot spaces for first, next and last, and label cards. The child orders the cards and shows first, next and last with dots or labels; speech is optional.

- **School Library**, three cards: a class arrives at the library; they choose books; they return the books.
- **Bird Nest**, three cards: eggs in a nest; young just hatched; a young bird leaving the nest.
- **Lost Mitten**, read aloud: "Ari dropped a mitten. Jo found it. Jo returned it to Ari." Three cards.
- **Making a Path**, read aloud: "Neighbors mark where a path will go. They dig a shallow trench. They lay stones. People use the finished path." Four cards; the lay-stones card is starred. Spoken or marked: "What happened just before the starred card?"

## Row 2: the art display log

A calendar strip with today's marker and four cards: drawing pictures **yesterday afternoon**, hanging the pictures **today**, a class visit **last week**, taking the pictures down **next month**. Marks: circle what happens today, put a token on last week's event, put a second token on next month's event. Spoken, one minute each for the ten heard: "When did the drawing session happen? Use a time word."

## Key (teacher)

School Library: arrives, chooses books, returns them. Bird Nest: eggs, hatched young, young bird leaving. Lost Mitten: dropped, found, returned. Making a Path: mark, dig, lay stones, people use it; just before the starred card is digging the trench. Art display: circle hanging; token on the class visit; second token on taking down; the drawing session was yesterday afternoon.
