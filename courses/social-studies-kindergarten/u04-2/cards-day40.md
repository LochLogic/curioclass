# Day 40 cards

Four groups, and pairs, named by task, never by who missed. Each group uses a move from section 4.1 or 4.2 on a case that appears nowhere else. Cards are invented, printed "invented for practice", with thick outlines. The teacher reads every label aloud. The fresh tries below are the day's own exit cards.

## Group 1: Sequence

Move: link each event to the one before it; say before and after for each pair. New cards: **a puppet lays out a mat; sits on it; folds it up.** Contrast: Wren puts "folds it up" first and says "because it is the smallest". (A, H)

## Group 2: Time words

Move: anchor on the class calendar and move the event to either side. New cards: a pretend class picnic card and today's marker. Contrast: Moss puts the picnic planned for next week behind the marker. (B)

## Group 3: Source and its limits

Move: point to the clue and name what another source would need to show. New card: the day 39 photograph the child did not choose, with its record read aloud. (C, F)

## Group 4: Claim and continuity

Move: name the changed tool and its continuing purpose, then a reason from the picture. New pair: a child carrying a pail of sand by hand, and a child pushing a wheelbarrow of sand. Contrast: Moss says nobody carries things by hand now. (D, E, G)

## Fresh tries

**Order, mark:** the text "The class takes out the blocks. They build a tower. They put the blocks back in the box." and three new cards, shuffled. "Put the cards in order."
**Time word, mark:** a dated record of a school music show next month on a calendar strip with today's marker. "Where does the music show go?"
**Claim group only, spoken:** the chair pair, a pair of pictures labeled long ago and today, examples and not every school: two children carrying chairs by hand, and a wheeled chair cart with chairs stacked on it: "Tell one thing that changed and one thing that stayed the same."
**Source recheck, mark and spoken:** the photograph not chosen on day 39: "Point to a clue. What can this photograph help us learn?"

## Key (teacher)

Sequence: mat, sit, fold. Time: the picnic is after today. Source: a visible detail, and what that one photograph cannot show. Claim group: the way the sand is carried changed, and sand still gets moved. Fresh try, chairs: hands changed to a cart, and chairs still get moved. Blocks: take out, build the tower, put back. Music show: after today's marker, in next month.
