# Day 40: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 39's record by part and day 38's exits. Set four tables by task. Pick the ten not yet heard.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Hand back day 39's record: each child sees one cell, privately. Name tables by task, never by who missed.

#### 8 min. Four groups, and pairs

Two minutes each, with day 39's items and day 38's. Sequence (s04-E1, s04-E1b; errors A and H): link each event to the one before it. Time words (s04-E2 to s04-E2d; error B): anchor on the calendar, move the event either side. Source and its limits (s04-E3, s04-E3c; errors C and F): point to the clue, name what another source would show. Claim and continuity (s04-X38a, s04-X38b, s04-X38c, s04-E4; errors D, E and G): name the changed tool and its same purpose; no ranking. Others work in pairs from a pictured move card.

#### 10 min. Fresh tries and unheard explanations

Each child tries a new card on the part missed: [[s04-X40a]] order the blocks cards; [[s04-X40b]] place the music show card on the strip. The claim table also hears [[s04-X40c]] (chairs). Hear the ten not yet heard on s04-E3 and s04-E4, a minute each, and recheck the unchosen photograph: "Point to a clue." ([[s04-X40d]]) "What can this photograph help us learn?" ([[s04-X40e]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; right after help is shown with help.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three blocks. No strip: fold paper.

**Skip.** The tables.

- **5 min. Blocks.** Draw a block tower, blocks out, blocks back.
  **Say.** "What came first? Next? Last?" Expect: out, tower, back.
- **5 min. Next month.** Draw a calendar strip with today. Put a show next month.
  **Say.** "Where does it go?" Expect: after today.
- **5 min. Chairs.** Draw chairs carried by hand, then on a cart.
  **Say.** "What changed? What stayed?" Expect: the carrying; chairs still move.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X40a]] [[s04-X40b]] [[s04-X40c]] [[s04-X40d]] [[s04-X40e]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the missed part only. Longer: the next part.

### Words and supports

Before, after, next month, changed, stayed the same. Frames: "I see ___, so ___."

### Tomorrow
