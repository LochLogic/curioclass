# Exit cards 37 and 38

Never used in teaching (rule 4): the day 37 teaching case is a different old photograph and our drawings, and the day 38 teaching cases are the four farm pairs.

## Day 37: the held-back photograph

A large print of a federal photograph, kept out of sight until the exit. Record: Dorothea Lange, *Children get into school bus on a fall morning, Dead Ox Flat, Malheur County, Oregon*, October 1939. Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration / Office of War Information Collection (reproduction number fsa.8b35057). Read aloud only the maker, the place, and the month and year. Mark: "Point to a clue that shows how the children got to school." Spoken, rotating group: "What can this photograph help us learn about how children got to school long ago?" and "How do we know this photograph is old?" The photograph shows particular children on one day; do not describe anyone by how they look, and say "the record does not say" about why it was taken.

## Day 38: the stove pair

A fresh pair of drawings, printed "invented for practice, examples and not every kitchen". Left: a cook at a wood stove with a log beside it, a pot of food on top. Right: a cook at a stove today with a pot of food on top. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no brand. Mark: "Circle what changed." Then "Put a token on what stayed the same." Spoken, rotating group: "How do you know it stayed the same?"

## Key (teacher)

Day 37: the clue is the bus with the children getting into it; the photograph helps us learn how these children got to school then, and does not show how every child went; we know it is old because the record gives October 1939, not because it is gray. Day 38: the stove, and what it burns, changed; the pot of food and the cook stayed; a person still cooks food to eat. Both are examples, not evidence of every kitchen.
