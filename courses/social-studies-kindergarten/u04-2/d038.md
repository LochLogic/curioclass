# Day 38: Then and now on one farm

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand out the logs with feedback. Pick today's six. Teacher key: key-sheet-then-now-chart.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen and hand-back

Hand-back from day 37: s04-X37c (error C): read each date aloud; s04-X37a and s04-X37b (error F): one photograph is not everybody; name what another would show.

#### 10 min. Compare

Review: the then and now chart, day 37. Nothing new is taught. Each pair gets a blank chart and the farm then and now pairs, each captioned "one farm, an example". Two minutes a pair: "In this picture ..., but ...; both ___." Write the shared purpose in the row: "both help us get water." Food and clothes are still grown and made today (error D). No then picture is bad (error G).

**Ask.** What changed, and what stayed? Expect: the tool changed; the purpose stayed.

Two minutes: each child tells a partner "I think ___ made a difference because ..."; this is practice, not scored. Wren says "it was best because it was best" (error E); the class asks for a detail.

#### 6 min. Supported work

Hand-back from day 36: read each child's feedback aloud; each moves or re-circles one relation on the dated picture log (error B). Hear today's six, one minute each: [[s04-X38c]] and one time relation.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say "this is a pretend kitchen." Each child has exit card 38: "Circle what changed" ([[s04-X38a]]); "Put a token on what stayed the same" ([[s04-X38b]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw a bucket and a tap. No chart: fold paper in two.

**Skip.** The puppets and the log.

- **5 min. Two pictures.** Draw a bucket of water, then a tap.
  **Say.** "What changed? What is the same?" Expect: the tool; both give water.
- **5 min. Another pair.** Draw a wagon and a bus.
  **Say.** "Both help ___." Expect: go places.
- **5 min. Why?**
  **Say.** "I think the tap made a difference because ..." Expect: a detail.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X38a]] [[s04-X38b]] [[s04-X38c]] Draw a cook at a wood stove and a cook at a stove today; your child circles what changed, puts a button on what stayed, and says how they know.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pairs. Longer: draw one more pair.

### Words and supports

Then, now, changed, stayed the same, both. Frames: "In this picture ..., but ...; both ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 39 is a check with new pictures. Nothing new is taught.
