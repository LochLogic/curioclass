# The pairs page (day 39)

Two old federal photographs, each beside a plain Curio Class photograph of the same task today. These are the end items' own pictures (rule 4): no other page in the unit shows any of them. Both today pictures are Curio Class originals, labeled as examples today, not as how everyone lives now. A teacher may substitute a photograph of their own school. No face is shown in the today pictures. Read aloud only each maker, place, and month and year.

## Pair 1: Canned Goods

Old: Russell Lee, *Wife of Jim Norris with canned goods, Pie Town, New Mexico*, October 1940. Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration / Office of War Information Collection. Today: a plain photograph of a refrigerator shelf with food on it, no brand visible. Say "the record does not say why it was taken" if a child asks. Describe the shelves as stored food, the way the print shows it, and do not name jars or cans unless the child can see them.

## Pair 2: Going to School

Old: John Vachon, *Farm children walking to one-room schoolhouse with lunch pails, Nebraska*, October 1938. Library of Congress, Prints and Photographs Division, Farm Security Administration / Office of War Information Collection. Today: a plain photograph of a school sidewalk and crossing, with no one in it.

## Each child's response

Choose one old photograph. Point to a clue. Circle what changed and put a token on what stayed the same. Draw the chosen change. For Going to School, "everyday work" means the everyday work of getting to school. Heard children (about ten today, ten more on day 40) also answer three questions: "What does your clue help us learn?", "Which change made the biggest difference to everyday work, and why?" and "What can this one photograph not show us?"

## Key (teacher)

Canned Goods: the clue is the stored food on the shelves; it helps us learn that food was stored on shelves there then; changed is how food is kept, stayed is that people keep food to eat later. Going to School: the clue is the children walking with lunch pails; it helps us learn that children walked to school there then; changed is the way to the door, stayed is that children go to school. One photograph shows one place and day; another source would have to show how everyone did it. Different supported choices meet; "it is different" alone does not.
