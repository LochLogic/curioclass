# Slate, tablet and washboard: drawings and a stand-in (day 37)

Four original drawings and one cardboard stand-in, all made by Curio Class today, not old objects and not photographs. Printed "drawing by Curio Class" along the bottom edge. The teacher says so aloud. Words of the day: photograph, drawing, object, clue.

## The stand-in

A ridged cardboard washboard, flat, with the ridges across it and no sharp edges. It stands for the washboard of the day 34 drawing, so children can feel the ridges.

## The drawings

1. **The washboard drawing** and 2. **the washing machine drawing**, the same two from day 34 (sheet-hook-washing.md).
3. **A slate:** a small flat board in a frame with chalk marks on it.
4. **A tablet:** a flat screen with a finger drawing a line on it. No brand, no name, no words.

## What the teacher says

"Both help wash clothes." "Both are for writing and drawing." A photograph is a picture taken at the time. A drawing is made later by someone. An object is a thing; a **clue** is something we can see that helps us learn. Say plainly that these drawings and the stand-in were made today to help us talk, and are not old objects.
