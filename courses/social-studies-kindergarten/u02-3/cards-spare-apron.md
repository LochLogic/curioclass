# Exit card 15: the spare apron

New card, never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". One picture with three marks to circle. Plain line drawing, thick outlines, no writing.

**Picture.** An art table with easels. A boy with a round face and short braids holds out a folded apron toward a girl in a light shirt who stands at the paint pots with her hands behind her back, looking at the paint. A tall child in a bright striped shirt stands at the back, painting, turned away. The boy's own apron is on; the folded one is a spare from a hook behind him.

**Circle choices.**
1. The boy handing over the spare apron (caring or fair; sharing lets the classmate join). The right mark.
2. The tall child in the bright shirt (does not meet: appearance, not an action).
3. The girl's light shirt (does not meet: clothes are not an action).

Teacher says: "Circle the caring or fair action. Point to what he did."
