# Day 16: Who did what they said?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the twelve fable pictures face down in order, with the Paper Workshop pictures beside them. Hide the Class Book pictures. Read the student page aloud later: "Draw one character doing what they said." Choose today's six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

The job routine runs. Cover the faces on page 2 of The Paper Workshop. [[s02-P16-1]] If many gave error D on day 15's exit (s02-X15), take up to 5 more minutes: cover faces and sizes, point to one action, say it.

#### 2 min. Words with pictures

Responsible: she does what she said she would do. Promise: saying you will do something. Children say each back. In charge waits for the workshop.

#### 8 min. The Lion and the Mouse

Say first: "This is a very old story, credited to Aesop." Tell it from the pictures. On page 4, read slowly and calmly, "The lion put his big paw down. He held the mouse," then pause. Or tell it: the lion held her paw, and the mouse begged. No one acts it out.

#### 5 min. Responsible

[[s02-K16-1]]

Hold up pages 5 and 10. [[steps:s02-K16-1]] Name her courage: she went to a big lion. Read the old moral once, "A kindness is never wasted." The question stays on what she did.

#### 9 min. In charge, and rules

[[s02-P16-2]]

Show page 6 of The Paper Workshop. Teach: in charge means it is someone's job to decide for the group, like a teacher. [[s02-P16-3]]

Lay page 9 beside page 10. [[s02-P16-4]] The class agreeing is how a rule is made; clearing the table is following it. For error E, ask which picture is agreeing and which is clearing, then say it together.

#### 3 min. Exit

Children draw one character doing what they said (student page) while you take the six, two at a time, through the Class Book pictures. Mark s02-X16. A child may pass. Right: Teacher Okoro is in charge; Jun did what Jun said; the card-order agreement is made, then followed. "The child with the book is in charge, she has it" is error D. "The child who passed it made the rule" is error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: tell it with two toys.

**Skip.** The class chart and the pairs.

- **8 min. Story.** Tell the fable: a mouse promises to help a lion, and does.
  **Say.** "What did she say she would do? Did she do it?" Expect: she said she would help; she gnawed the ropes.
- **7 min. In charge.** Ask who decides things at your table, and why that is a job.
  **Say.** "Is the lion in charge?" Expect: no; strong is not in charge.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one character doing what they said.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X16]] Tell it: Jun promises to bring back the class book and does; the teacher says to pass it in card order and the class agrees.
  **Say.** "Who is in charge? Who did what they said?" Expect: the teacher; Jun.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tell the fable in six pictures. Longer: find a promise in a story at home.

### Words and supports

Responsible, promise, in charge, rule, with pictures. Frames: "She said ___. She did ___." Pointing is an answer; a communication aid counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 looks back at our class decision about a week on, with a before-and-after picture chart. Results of today's exit (s02-X16) go on the record sheet and feed the day 19 groups, with errors D and E.
