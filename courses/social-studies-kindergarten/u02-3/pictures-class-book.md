# The Class Book (exit story 16)

Two scenes in five pictures, new and never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing. After the mark on who is in charge, the teacher asks: "Who did what they said they would do?" and then "Which picture shows the class agreeing? Which shows someone following it?"

**Scene 1 (page 1).** Text: "Jun borrows the class book. 'I will bring it back tomorrow,' says Jun."
Picture: Jun, a child with short curly hair and a school bag, holds a big picture book and talks to the class. The cover is plain.

**Scene 1 (page 2).** Text: "The next day Jun puts the book back on the shelf."
Picture: Jun places the book on a low shelf. A wall calendar shows a new day.

**Scene 2 (page 3).** Text: "Teacher Okoro decides who reads the book next. 'Let us pass it in card order,' says the teacher."
Picture: Teacher Okoro, a man with a short beard, holds a fan of name cards and looks at the class.

**Scene 2 (page 4).** Text: "The class agrees. They say yes. A rule is made."
Picture: raised hands across the room, children smiling.

**Scene 2 (page 5).** Text: "Now Mia gets the book, then Rafi, then Lee, the way the cards go."
Picture: three children in a line passing the book one to the next, name cards laid in order on a table.

**Circle choices (after page 5).** Who is in charge: Teacher Okoro, Jun, a child in the passing line. Then two more circles: page 4 (raised hands) and page 5 (the passing line), for "Which picture shows the class agreeing? Which shows someone following it?"

## Key (teacher)

In charge: Teacher Okoro. Did what Jun said: Jun. The card-order rule: made on page 4, followed on page 5.
