# Day 15: What did the character do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the Paper Workshop pictures face down in order. Make twelve scene cards. Hide the spare apron card. Read the student page aloud later: "Draw what one character did." Choose today's six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

The job routine runs in its usual place. Post and co-write "What do story characters show us?" Say, "Curio Class made up today's story for us to think with. It is not a record of what we did." If many gave error B, I or J on day 14's exit (s02-X14), use these 3 minutes: "More blocks chose it. That does not mean everyone wanted it." "Its reason stays on the record; we check later." "We picked it, so we are done."

#### 3 min. Words with pictures

Show a picture and a gesture for each: fair (same for each), caring (a hand held out), just (putting an unfair thing right), rule (we agree, then we all do it).

#### 10 min. Read-aloud

Tell The Paper Workshop, one picture at a time. Pause at each scene.

**Ask.** What did the character do? How do you know? Expect: Pia gave Nell paper; Nell put scraps in the bowl; Teo shared out the extra paper so Isa had the same; Teacher Ruiz said where scissors and crayons go; the class said yes to clearing the table, and everyone did. Accept a point.

#### 5 min. Teacher models pointing

[[s02-K15-1]]

Cover the faces with a hand. [[steps:s02-K15-1]] Say, "I know because she ..." For error D, cover faces and sizes again and ask only for the action.

#### 6 min. Pairs

[[s02-P15-1]]

Each pair holds one scene card. One points to an action; the other tells it. While pairs talk, show the spare apron card to six children, a pair at a time, and mark s02-X15.

#### 3 min. Closing circle

Today's six not yet marked answer the exit; every child points to the scene card. Mark the class list. A child may pass.

Right: the boy shared the spare apron so the classmate could paint. "She is caring because her apron is pretty" and "the tall one is fair" are error D; point back to the hands.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw a stick figure for each part. No cards: use paper scraps.

**Skip.** The pairs and the class chart.

- **7 min. Story.** Tell it: Pia gives Nell paper; Isa has none; Teo shares out the extra so each has the same.
  **Say.** "What did Teo do? How do you know?" Expect: he shared the paper so Isa had the same.
- **8 min. Draw.** Your child draws one character doing something and says, "The ___ did ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X15]] Tell it: a boy lends a spare apron to a classmate, who lends him a brush.
  **Say.** "What shows caring or fairness here?" Expect: he shared the apron; not how anyone looks.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three scenes. Longer: find a second character who shows a different quality.

### Words and supports

Fair, caring, just, rule, with pictures. Frames: "The ___ did ___. I know because ___." Pointing is an answer; a communication aid counts. Describe scenes aloud for a child who cannot see.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 tells The Lion and the Mouse and returns to the clear-table rule, made and followed. If many gave error D on today's exit (s02-X15), its 3-minute warm-up covers faces and points to an action.
