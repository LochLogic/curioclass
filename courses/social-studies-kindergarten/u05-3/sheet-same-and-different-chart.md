# Same-and-different chart

A blank recording sheet for days 48 to 51, made by us. Two columns headed with pictures, never with writing a child must read: a picture of two hands holding one thing (alike) and a picture of two different shapes side by side (different). A row for each pair of cards. The teacher reads the frame aloud: "Both ___, but ___."

## How it is used

- **Day 48:** the teacher models the chart with the two food pages. Pairs put tokens on a blank chart for the same two pages.
- **Day 49:** the whole class fills a chart for the koto and the singing pictures, with each maker named aloud. Pairs fill a blank chart for two new food pictures (see the day 49 pictures).
- **Day 50:** the teacher supplies no content. A blank chart and the claim frame stay available.
- **Day 51:** a blank chart is used on a changed pair of cards.

## Rules

A token for alike goes on what is the same in both; a token for different goes on what differs. At least one alike and one different is a full comparison. Naming a difference alone is the error O. No tradition is called strange or wrong. Tokens, a point, a drawing or a spoken answer all count as showing. The record notes one look-for: a supported similarity, a supported difference, and people and occasion correctly attributed.
