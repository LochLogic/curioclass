# Day 49: Music, and why people do it their way

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang pages 3 and 4. Lay a chart and two tokens at each pair. Keep the exit cards in their folder. Set Our Holiday Cards, and the sort bins with the saved symbol pictures, within reach. Nobody sings along.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Show a pretend class rug by the window (Moss: the sun is warm) and by the shelf (Wren: the books are close). Pairs tell their partner's reason before their own. Review: unit 1's "Tell your partner's reason before your own."

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 48's exit, repair the one most missed. Error F: pretend Birch Town hangs lanterns and Cliff Town paints a banner; say "some towns," never "every town." Error O: add what is the same. Error M: each has its own way; different is not wrong.

#### 5 min. Application

Read page 3, then page 4, each maker aloud. Review: "Both ___, but ___," from unit 3. Whole class on the chart: "Both are music at a celebration; but one is a koto and one is people singing."

Step back: people do things their own way; where a page gives a reason we say it, and these do not. People greet in many ways; no one greeting is the same everywhere.

#### 17 min. Supported work

Say: "These towns are pretend." Pairs fill a chart for Pond Town (neighbors share baked apples every year) and Ridge Town (neighbors make flat bread on a big stone every year): "Both ___, but ___." Three hearings, five minutes each; other pairs swap charts and say it to the next pair.

- Other half, 15 children, 20 seconds each: "Why is this symbol what it is?" Columns E5 to E10.
- Every child, 10 seconds, from Our Holiday Cards: "Who or what does this day remember?" Columns E1 and E2.
- Every child, 10 seconds, pretend Bay Town (neighbors sing rounds on the dock every year) and Glen Town (neighbors tell stories under a big tree every year): "What is alike?" then "What is different?" Columns E11 and E12.

Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit

Show the two exit cards, each maker and occasion aloud. Every child points to some families or every family ([[s05-X49a]]). Six children, 20 seconds each: "What is the same in these two cards?" ([[s05-X49b]]) then "What is different?" ([[s05-X49c]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw a person at a long stringed instrument and people singing. No exit cards: draw two foods from two celebrations.

**Skip.** The hearings and the swap.

- **5 min. Music.** Say: the two pictures come from the pages of the people who keep them.
  **Say.** "What is the same? What is different?" Expect: both are music at a celebration; a koto, people singing.
- **5 min. Two towns.** Pretend one town shares baked apples and one makes flat bread on a stone; say each way is its own, never wrong.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X49a]] [[s05-X49b]] [[s05-X49c]] Ask each; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pages only. Longer: draw a third pretend town.

### Words and supports

Koto, reason. Frame: "Both ___, but ___." A point counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 is the observation; nothing is taught. A child not yet heard on item 7 is heard then on new cards.
