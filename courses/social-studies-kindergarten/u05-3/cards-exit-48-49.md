# Exit cards 48 and 49

New cards for the exits of days 48 and 49. Plain line drawings, thick outlines. The teacher reads every label aloud and says which cards are made up. Never used in teaching (rule 4); the day 49 pair is withheld from supported comparison. The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## Day 48: River Town and Hill Town (printed "invented for practice")

Two cards standing for no real group. In pretend River Town, neighbors gather every year to make soup. In pretend Hill Town, neighbors gather every year to bake pies. Three small pictures: neighbors around a table making food, a pot of soup, a pie. Mark: put a token on the picture of what is the same in both towns. Spoken, rotating group: "Show me how the two towns are different."

## Day 49: a pair of food scenes from two community pages (each with its maker named aloud)

Two new drawings, not latkes and not rice-cake pounding. Card 1: a plate of jelly doughnuts called sufganiyot, from the Reform Judaism Hanukkah page, which names the food. Card 2: a person shaping a candy figure at a demonstration, from the Japanese American National Museum's blog about its 2023 festival, which names hand-sculpted candy. Caption each occasion and maker aloud. Neither card stands for all Jewish or all Japanese American families. Mark: circle some families or every family under the pair. Spoken, rotating group: "What is the same in these two cards?" and then "What is different?"

## Key (teacher)

48: a token on neighbors around a table making food (the shared feature), not the soup or the pie; soup in one town and pies in the other. 49: some families; both show food at a special occasion (the doughnut card shows only a plate, so not "people making or sharing food"); the foods differ, jelly doughnuts in one and sculpted candy in the other, each with its own maker and occasion. "That one is strange" is error M; naming only a difference is error O.
