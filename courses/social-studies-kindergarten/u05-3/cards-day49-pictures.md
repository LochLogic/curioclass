# Day 49 pictures

The two music pictures are pages 3 and 4 of "Two Celebrations" (the koto player and people singing at a table). For pairs, two new food pictures printed "invented for practice", standing for no real group. The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## The food pair for pairs (not the exit's)

In pretend Pond Town, neighbors gather every year to share baked apples. In pretend Ridge Town, neighbors gather every year to make flat bread on a big stone. Two cards, one for each town, with one sentence each read aloud. Pairs fill a blank same-and-different chart and say "Both ___, but ___."

## Step back (teacher)

People do things differently for reasons that matter to them, where a page states a reason; where it does not, say "the page does not say." No one greeting is universal, and no greeting custom is named without a community page that describes it.

## Key (teacher)

Alike: neighbors gather every year and share or make food. Different: baked apples in one, flat bread on a stone in the other. Neither town is wrong.
