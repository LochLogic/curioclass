# Day 50: The unit 5 observation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the withheld cards in their folder until now. Key: key-pages-day50-hearing.md. Confirm the eagle shows on the press photograph; if not, use a second federal Great Seal image. On the record sheet, mark each child's blank cells from days 48 and 49. Make three tens, privately, from what days 48 and 49 recorded.

### The day

#### 2 min. Introduce sources

Say: "I hear ten of you at a time. The rest of you work at the mat. Passing is fine." Name the sources: the wonder wall, the picture bins, Our Holiday Cards and the withheld cards. Supply no reasons; use only "Tell me more" and "Show me."

#### 15 min. Fresh-source tasks

Three tens, five minutes each, thirty seconds a child: each child is heard on one task, the one the record shows blank for that child. Mat children put a token on the clue in one holiday card that shows whom the day remembers, and tell a partner.

- **Holiday.** The pretend museum-label scene: "Who does this day remember?" ([[s05-E1]]). Then, if time, "How does remembering them honor them?" ([[s05-E2]]).
- **The eagle.** The press photograph; read the museum, the date and the use only. "Point to the bird." ([[s05-E5]]) "Why can that bird stand for the United States on this object?" ([[s05-E5b]])
- **One more.** Point to one card from the child's blank cells: the parade flag, "Tell what the stripes are for" ([[s05-E6]]), "stars" ([[s05-E7]]) or "colors" ([[s05-E8]]); the open music book, "How did these words become our country's anthem?" ([[s05-E9]]); the flag beside a speech bubble, with the Pledge read in short phrases, "What do these words mean?" ([[s05-E10]]). A child not heard on item 7 on day 49 is heard on the dreidel and calligraphy cards instead, makers said aloud: "What is alike in the two cards?" ([[s05-E11]]) and "What is different?" ([[s05-E12]]).

Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A part not reached stays not yet observed.

#### 3 min. Close the wonder wall

Each child adds a drawing; collect the pages. Ten children, fifteen seconds each: "Why do we remember some days and some people? Say, I think ___, because ___." Scribe it beside the picture the child chose ([[s05-E13]]). Review: "I think ___, because ___," from unit 1.

#### 10 min. Long route only

Ten children not yet heard on these, twenty seconds each, about four minutes: "Ask a question about a symbol you explained" ([[s05-E3]]); read the page it leads to, add nothing: "What does this page help you answer?" ([[s05-E4]]). The other twenty are heard on day 51 (list them on the record sheet). Then the twenty not scribed, ten seconds each, the claim ([[s05-E13]]), about four minutes.

### Other rooms

Hear ten at a table. The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a flag, an open song book, a glass case with two portraits.

**Skip.** The tens, the mat and the wonder wall.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Show your drawings; add no reasons.
  **Say.** "What do the stripes of the flag stand for?" Expect: thirteen, like the first states.
  **Say.** "How did this song become our anthem?" Expect: a man wrote words after seeing the flag; much later a law chose it.
  **Say.** "Why do we remember some days and some people?" Expect: I think ___, because ___.
- **10 min. Respond.** Note one part to practice: a holiday, a book question, a symbol or a tradition; show it again and ask once more.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the first two cards. Longer: hear a third symbol.

### Words and supports

Pass, point, draw or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 reads day 50's items by part: holiday (errors A, H and G); book evidence (errors B and I); symbol (errors C, D, E, J, K, L and N); tradition (errors F, M and O). It re-teaches each, then hears fresh tries.
