# Two Celebrations

Four picture pages for days 48 and 49, drawn new by us from two community pages. Each page names its maker aloud and says what the page tells, no more. No page stands for all Jewish families or all Japanese American families; say so. Nobody tastes, plays, sings along or prays; the pages are looked at and talked about. One actor in each scene. The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Say where the reason is not on the page: "the page does not say why."

## Page 1 (day 48): latkes (actor: the cook)

Our drawing of a person at a stove turning potato pancakes in a pan, a menorah on a shelf behind. Read: "This page is from Reform Judaism, a Jewish community's own page about Hanukkah. Hanukkah is celebrated with candles, food, family and friends. The page says one food is latkes, potato pancakes fried in oil. Some families make latkes for Hanukkah. Other families have other foods."

## Page 2 (day 48): rice-cake pounding (actors: the drummers)

Our drawing of two people pounding a large tub of rice with big wooden mallets while others watch. Read: "This page is from the Japanese American National Museum's blog. It tells about one festival, held on January 8, 2023, for the new year. The festival had a performance of rice-cake pounding. This is one festival at one museum, not every family's new year. The page does not say why the rice is pounded."

## Page 3 (day 49): a koto player (actor: the player)

Our drawing of a person seated on a floor mat playing a long stringed instrument called a koto. Read: "The same museum blog says the festival had a koto player. A koto is a Japanese stringed instrument. Music does not have to be a song."

## Page 4 (day 49): singing (actors: the table of people)

Our drawing of people around a table with their mouths open as if singing. Read: "The Reform Judaism page says people sing during Hanukkah. The page does not name a song here, and this is a picture of people singing, not of any one family."

## Sort and chart (teacher)

Model "Both ___, but ___" on the blank chart: Both pages show a special day with food or music and people gathering; but one shows latkes and the other shows rice-cake pounding. Add a because only where a page gives one. The perspective routine from unit 1: "Tell your partner's reason before your own."

## Local tradition slot

Before day 48, if you have found a publicly documented celebration of your own area, from its community's own page, make a fifth page the same way (maker named aloud, one or two sentences, a drawing) and swap it for one of the pages above or add it. Leave the slot blank otherwise. No family is asked to share anything.
