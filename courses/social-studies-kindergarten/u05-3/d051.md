# Day 51: Practise what we need

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 50's items by part; sort children privately into four mixed groups named by an activity. Set a fresh-try card at each table.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Say: "Today we practise what we still need." Name no child's group. Pairs begin: one says a thing about a holiday, symbol or tradition; the partner asks why.

#### 8 min. Four groups

Two minutes a table; pairs retell the move from the picture shown. Review, days 41 to 49:

- **Holiday** (errors A, H, G). A day-off picture beside a holiday card: what does the card remember? Veterans Day thanks everyone who served; Memorial Day remembers those who died serving. A praise sentence beside a card detail: which does the card support?
- **Book evidence** (errors B, I). A flower beside the eagle: ask the page. Moss guesses, then asks the page.
- **Symbol** (errors C, D, E, J, K, L, N; split in two if many). Tokens on a flag: stripes for the first states, stars for the states now. The eagle traced to the old paper. Two statements into the bins. A song beside a law. Learning the words beside saying them; fair is for everyone. Say what words mean.
- **Tradition** (errors F, M, O). "All" becomes "some families." Each tradition beside its own page, no ranking. What is alike beside what differs.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a flag with stripes and stars, and a folded flag.

**Skip.** The groups and pairs.

- **5 min. Practise.** Pick one part from the observation: a holiday, a symbol or a tradition.
  **Say.** "What do the stars of the flag stand for?" Expect: one star for each state.
- **5 min. Fresh try.** Show the part on a new drawing and ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two groups. Longer: a second try.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing counts.
