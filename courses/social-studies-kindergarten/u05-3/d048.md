# Day 48: Two celebrations, alike and different

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang pages 1 and 2; give each pair a chart and two tokens. Pick today's six. A celebration of your own area, from its community's own published page, may replace one page, made the same way. Nobody tastes, lights or prays.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Post: "How are traditions alike and different?" A tradition is what people do the same way again and again.

**Ask.** Whose reason do we tell first? Expect: our partner's. Review: unit 1's "Tell your partner's reason before your own."

#### 4 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 47's exit, repair the one most missed; otherwise a small group at the sort bins with the saved symbol pictures. Error K: a bus song beside a law. Error E: learning the words beside saying them. Error L: a block corner, everyone sharing. Error N: Moss says a phrase fast; ask what it means.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Read pages 1 and 2 as written, each maker aloud. Some families keep Hanukkah and some do not.

Review: "Both ___, but ___," from unit 3. Model: "Both are special food for a special time; but one is potato pancakes and one is rice cake pounded." The page does not say why. Moss: "Everybody eats latkes" (error F: the page names more than one food). "Fried and pounded, that is all" (error O). "They are doing it wrong" (error M).

#### 11 min. Supported work

Pairs put one token on what is the same and one on what differs, saying "Both ___, but ___."

Hear a rotating half, 15 children, 20 seconds each: read the note on the child's saved symbol picture; ask "Why is this symbol what it is?" Mark E5 to E10: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit

Read the River Town and Hill Town cards; say they are pretend. Every child puts a token on what is the same ([[s05-X48a]]). Six children, 15 seconds each: "Show me how the two towns are different" ([[s05-X48b]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw a pan of potato pancakes and a tub of rice. No tokens: use buttons.

**Skip.** The pairs and the hearing.

- **5 min. Two pages.** Say: both come from the pages of the people who keep them.
  **Say.** "What is the same? What is different?" Expect: both are special food for a special time; potato pancakes, rice cake.
- **5 min. Buttons.** One on alike, one on different.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X48a]] [[s05-X48b]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one token each. Longer: a third thing alike.

### Words and supports

Tradition, alike, different. A point counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error F, O or M, day 49 repairs the one most missed. Day 49 puts music on the chart.
