# The Rhythm Sticks (day 19 fresh try)

New picture story for the day 19 fresh try. Never used in teaching or on day 18 (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". Eight pages. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing, no brands. Children of many skin tones; one child uses a wheelchair. After the story the teacher asks the missed character or rule question and the child points to the pictured action.

**Page 1.** Text: "Teacher Dube has a box of rhythm sticks for music time."
Picture: Teacher Dube, a woman with a headscarf and a warm smile, holds a wooden box of short sticks and stands by a rug.

**Page 2.** Text: "Last week Ellis borrowed the sticks. Ellis said, 'I will bring the box back.'"
Picture: Ellis, a boy with a round face and a bandage on one knee, holds a shoebox with sticks poking out and talks to the teacher.

**Page 3.** Text: "Today Ellis returns the sticks. Every stick is in the box."
Picture: Ellis sets the box on a low shelf. The lid is next to it. The teacher smiles.

**Page 4.** Text: "Teacher Dube organizes music time. She says, 'Sit in a circle.' She decides who passes the box first."
Picture: children sit in a circle on a rug. Teacher Dube points to a girl with a braid to begin.

**Page 5.** Text: "The class talks. 'Pass the box, then each takes one stick,' says Nia. 'Yes,' says the class. They agree."
Picture: hands raised around the circle. Nia, a child in a wheelchair with a bright shirt, is speaking.

**Page 6.** Text: "Now the box goes around the circle."
Picture: the box is held out from one child to the next.

**Page 7.** Text: "Ravi waits until the box comes to him. Then he takes one stick."
Picture: Ravi, a boy with a crest of hair, holds out both hands as the box arrives; one stick in his hand.

**Page 8.** Text: "They tap the sticks all together. Tap, tap, tap."
Picture: the whole circle holding sticks up, tapping, smiling.

**Right answers.** Responsible: Ellis (said he would bring it back, did). In charge: Teacher Dube. Rule made: page 5 (the class agrees). Rule followed: page 7 (Ravi waits, then takes one).
