# Day 19: Fix it and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 18's assessment marks on the record sheet by letter. Slip the two letters most missed; if more than a third missed one, teach it to the whole class. Make a picture card for each pair. Hide the story.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Read "How can each of us help our class?" aloud.

#### 12 min. Small groups

Two groups of five minutes with you, by the error most missed. Name no group by error: call tables by activity, mixed; tell no child why. The rest are pairs who take the picture card for their own gap and do its move.

A, the unfinished job: set it beside a finished one, then finish a new shared job. B, reasons heard: puppets hear reasons, find what both accept, one block each. C, before and after: compare a detail. D, looks: point to the action; cover how the child looks. E, agreeing and doing: set them side by side. F, "because": point to a detail and say it. G, loud choosing: hear each puppet's reason first. H, taking: practice "May I?". I, the other reason: keep it beside the plan. J, step and action: match each step to an action.

#### 13 min. Fresh try

Say, "This story is invented for practice." Tell The Rhythm Sticks from its pictures. [[s02-X19]]

**Ask.** Who is in charge? Show me what they did. Who did what they said they would do? Show me. Where did the class agree on the rule? Where did someone follow it? Show me. Ask each child only the question they missed. Expect: Teacher Dube sits the circle; Ellis returns the sticks; the class agrees on page 5 and Ravi waits on page 7. "She is biggest" is error D; "the one who passed made the rule" is error E: set pages 5 and 7 side by side. Mark s02-X19; a child may pass. A child not yet taking part does a fresh shared job and one block-choice.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: tell it with two toys. No blocks: use paper scraps.

**Skip.** The groups and the class record.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell it: Ellis returns the sticks, as he said he would; the teacher organizes music time; the class agrees to pass the box, and Ravi waits for it.
- **6 min. Show me.** Ask one question at a time and have your child show the answer.
  **Say.** "Who is in charge? Who did what they said? Where did the class agree?" Expect: the teacher; Ellis; the class deciding.
- **5 min. A shared job.** Pick a job both can do, say a reason each, vote once each, do it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19]] Ask the three questions again about a new picture.
  **Say.** "Show me who is in charge." Expect: the teacher's action, not her size.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group only. Longer: find a second helper in the story.

### Words and supports

In charge, responsible, rule, agree, follow, with pictures. Frame: "The ___ did ___." Pointing is an answer; a communication aid counts.

### Tomorrow

Unit 2 ends. Day 20 opens unit 3 with position words in the room: near, far, left, right, behind, in front of, between, next to. Today's marks go on the record sheet for the year.
