# The Puppet Basket

Day 18 fresh story for items 4 and 5. Withheld from teaching and from exits: never show it before day 18. Seven scenes in eight pages; each scene is one thumbnail on the response rows. Made by Curio Class; invented and not a record of what our class did. The teacher reads each caption and describes the picture neutrally (no hints about who is fair, caring or the rest); children find the evidence. Art: plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing, no brands. A puppet-show corner with a cloth-draped stage, a curtain and a basket of paper-bag puppets. Children of many skin tones; Sam wears a hearing aid. Teacher Lena is a woman.

**Scene 1 (page 1).** Caption: "Teacher Lena says where the puppet stage goes: by the window, away from the door. She says who sets it up: Pat."
Picture: Teacher Lena, a woman with a short gray bob, points to a spot by a window. A child stands beside her.

**Scene 2 (page 2).** Caption: "Rae sees Bo standing alone. Rae says, 'Do you want to be in the show?'"
Picture: Rae, a girl with long curls, reaches a hand toward Bo, a girl with a headband, who stands off to the side near the shelves with arms at his sides.

**Scene 3 (page 3).** Caption: "The turn line moves up one place each show. Today Pat steps to the front again. Mo says, 'Pat was first yesterday. Sam should go first today.'"
Picture: a row of five children by the stage, Pat at the front. Name cards on the wall show the turn order, with Sam's card next. Mo, a boy with a wide smile, steps out and gestures toward Sam; the line begins to turn.

**Scene 4 (page 4).** Caption: "Pat sets up the stage. Bo holds the curtain steady."
Picture: Pat fastening cloth to the stage frame. Bo, a girl with a headband, holds the curtain's edge with both hands so it does not fall.

**Scene 5 (page 5).** Caption: "The show is over. Jo says, 'I will fold the stage cloth.'"
Picture: Jo, a child with short twists, near the cloth, hand raised toward it.

**Scene 5 (page 6).** Caption: "Jo folds the stage cloth, corner to corner."
Picture: Jo holding two corners of the cloth; a neat square on the floor beside her.

**Scene 6 (page 7).** Caption: "The class talks. 'We can put the puppets back in the basket,' says one. 'Yes,' says the class."
Picture: children on the rug, hands raised. The puppets lie on the table. The basket is empty beside them.

**Scene 7 (page 8).** Caption: "Now Kim puts the puppets back in the basket."
Picture: Kim lifting a paper-bag puppet into the basket; other puppets already inside; the table nearly bare.

**Answer map for after class.** Fair, Mo (scene 3). Just, Mo (asking that Sam go first, as the turns go around, puts the unfair order right). Caring, Rae or Bo. Responsible, Jo. In charge, Lena. Rule made, scene 6. Rule followed, scene 7 (Kim).
