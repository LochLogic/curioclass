# The Puppet Basket

Day 18 fresh story for items 4 and 5. Withheld from teaching and from exits: never show it before day 18. Six scenes in eight pages; each scene is one thumbnail on the response rows. Made by Curio Class; invented and not a record of what our class did. The teacher reads each caption and describes the picture neutrally (no hints about who is fair, caring or the rest); children find the evidence. Art: plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing, no brands. A puppet-show corner with a cloth-draped stage, a curtain and a basket of paper-bag puppets. Children of many skin tones; Sam wears a hearing aid. Teacher Lena is a woman.

**Scene 1 (page 1).** Caption: "Teacher Lena says where the puppet stage goes: by the window, away from the door. She says who sets it up: Bo and Pat."
Picture: Teacher Lena, a woman with a short gray bob, points to a spot by a window. Two children stand beside her.

**Scene 1 (page 2).** Caption: "Rae sees Kim standing alone. Rae says, 'Do you want to be in the show?'"
Picture: Rae, a girl with long curls, reaches a hand toward Kim, who stands off to the side near the shelves with arms at his sides.

**Scene 2 (page 3).** Caption: "Sam is always last in the line for turns. Today Mo says, 'Sam should go first.' Now the turns are equal."
Picture: a row of five children by the stage. Sam at the back of the line. Mo, a boy with a wide smile, steps out and gestures toward Sam; the line begins to turn.

**Scene 3 (page 4).** Caption: "Pat sets up the stage. Bo holds the curtain steady."
Picture: Pat fastening cloth to the stage frame. Bo, a girl with a headband, holds the curtain's edge with both hands so it does not fall.

**Scene 4 (page 5).** Caption: "The show is over. Jo says, 'I will fold the stage cloth.'"
Picture: Jo, a child with short twists, near the cloth, hand raised toward it.

**Scene 4 (page 6).** Caption: "Jo folds the stage cloth, corner to corner."
Picture: Jo holding two corners of the cloth; a neat square on the floor beside her.

**Scene 5 (page 7).** Caption: "The class talks. 'We can put the puppets back in the basket,' says one. 'Yes,' says the class. They agree."
Picture: children on the rug, hands raised. The puppets lie on the table. The basket is empty beside them.

**Scene 6 (page 8).** Caption: "Now Kim puts the puppets back in the basket."
Picture: Kim lifting a paper-bag puppet into the basket; other puppets already inside; the table nearly bare.

**Answer map for after class.** Fair, Mo. Just, Mo (asking that Sam go first puts the unfair order right). Caring, Rae or Bo. Responsible, Jo. In charge, Lena. Rule made, scene 5. Rule followed, scene 6 (Kim).
