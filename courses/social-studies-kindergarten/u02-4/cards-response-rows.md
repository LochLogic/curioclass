# The response sheet (day 18)

One sheet for each child, printed or drawn once and copied. It holds seven rows, one for each thing the teacher asks about The Puppet Basket. Every row shows the same seven small scene pictures in the story's order, left to right, each about as big as a thumb, with a thick outline and no words. At the start of each row stands one small picture that says what the row asks, with no scene in it: a pair of hands sharing (fair), a hand held out (caring), a finished job (responsible), a hand holding up a stop sign beside a group (in charge), a hand setting a crooked line straight (putting an unfair thing right), two hands raised together (a rule made), a child doing the thing agreed (a rule followed). The child puts one token or counter on a scene in the row, or points and the teacher places it.

The teacher writes the child's name in the top corner, and the sheet carries nothing else. Children sit apart or turn the sheet from their neighbor. Collect the sheets when the seven rows are done; record the marks afterward from the sheets, so each mark stays the child's own.

Art: plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing, no brands. Scene 1 shows only Teacher Lena and scene 2 only Rae and Bo, so a mark on a scene shows which actor the child means.

## Key (teacher)

Fair: scene 3. Putting an unfair thing right: scene 3. Caring: scene 2 or scene 4. Responsible: scene 5. In charge: scene 1. A rule made: scene 6. A rule followed: scene 7.
