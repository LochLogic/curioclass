# Day 18: What we know about helping

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make one response sheet for each child from cards-response-rows. Lay out the day 17 picture pages with your next steps. Choose the children not heard on day 17.

### The day

#### 3 min. Story

Say, "This story is invented for practice." Read The Puppet Basket caption by caption, describing each picture plainly.

#### 8 min. Picture responses

Each child works alone on a row for each prompt. Read each aloud; the child places a token on a scene, or points and you place it. Collect the sheets, then record the marks.

[[s02-E4]]

Ask: Who is fair? Caring? Responsible? In charge? Who puts an unfair thing right? Then [[s02-E5]]: Where is a rule made? Where is it followed? Judge the marks later. A mark for the tallest is error D; the follower marked as the maker is error E. Say nothing about right or wrong.

#### 3 min. Revise

Read each of the six their written next step from day 17 alone at their table while the others draw; the rest look at their page with a partner. Each adds or changes one mark. "We chose it, so it worked" is still error C.

#### 11 min. Conversations

Hear the children not heard on day 17, two questions each: [[s02-E1]] with Our Jobs Chart, [[s02-E1b]] [[s02-E1c]], [[s02-E2]] with Our Decision Record, or [[s02-E6]]. Read the rest from dated records: jobs from days 13 to 17, [[s02-E3]] from day 17's vote and revised card. Write the claim word for word beside its evidence. The rest draw how they help. A child may pass, or answer through a puppet.

**Ask.** How can each of us help our class? Show something in a story or on our chart. Expect: "We finish our jobs because Jo folded the stage cloth." "Help because helping is good" is error F: ask which picture shows it.

#### 2 min. Wonder wall

Read three claims aloud. Mark each part independent, content-prompted or not yet observed: unheard parts wait.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in. One projector: show one scene at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: use buttons. No pictures: draw seven small scenes.

**Skip.** The wall and the class chart.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Tell it: Teacher Lena says where the stage goes and that Pat sets it up; Rae asks Bo in; Mo asks that Sam go first; Pat and Bo set up; Jo says she will fold the cloth and does; the class agrees to put the puppets away; Kim does it.
  **Say.** "Who is responsible? Who is in charge? Where is a rule made?" Expect: Jo; Lena; the class agreeing.
- **8 min. Jobs.** Ask what job your child did in class and who counted on it; tell of a child, Jo, who promises to shelve borrowed books.
  **Say.** "What is your job? Who counts on it?" Expect: a job they finish.
- **8 min. Deciding.** Remember a choice the class made together.
  **Say.** "How can each of us help?" Expect: a claim and a because.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the wall. Longer: one more claim.

### Words and supports

Fair, caring, responsible, in charge, just (putting an unfair thing right), rule, agree, vote, with pictures. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Pointing, a token or drawing counts; never rush a communicator.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 sorts the record sheet by letter: two groups with you, the rest in pairs. Errors D and E and the parts not yet observed meet a fresh story.
