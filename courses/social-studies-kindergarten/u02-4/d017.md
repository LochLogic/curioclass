# Day 17: Did it help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Draw the reserved sketch of the changed place from the door (a clue it helped, a thing in the way); keep it face down. Choose six children for the checklist (listens, seeks agreement, votes, acts) and half the class to hear.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Read Our Decision Record from day 14 and "How can each of us help our class?" aloud. If many gave error D or E on day 16's exit (s02-X16), lay Paper Workshop pages 9 and 10 side by side and point to the agreeing and the doing, inside these 3 minutes.

#### 3 min. After sketch

Children stand on the floor mark and draw the After.

#### 4 min. Compare

Hang the before sketch beside three afters.

**Ask.** What is the same? What is different? Expect: the change the class made; there is room. Each copies one thing from the before sketch into the Before box.

#### 5 min. Confirm or revise

Puppets repeat one reason each; ask what both accept. Each child sets one block: keep or change. Leave the other reason in the perspective columns: "Their reason stays on the record while we check" (error I). Pairs do the next step, such as doing the class's first step (error J). Tick the six.

#### 10 min. Conversations

Hear half the class, two of the four questions each; note key words. The rest draw one thing that shows it.

**Ask.** How did we decide? Did it help? What shows that? What could we change? Expect: a difference they see or a problem that stays; "it did not help" with a seen reason meets. "We chose it, so it worked" is error C: ask what the picture shows now. "It helps because it helps" is error F: point to the sketch. This is practice; tonight write a next step on each card.

#### 2 min. Exit

Show the reserved sketch to six children, two at a time; each points to a clue and you circle it. Mark s02-X17. A child may pass.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No blocks: use two paper scraps. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The puppets and the class record.

- **5 min. Before.** Pick a small spot in a picture that bothers someone. Draw it. Your own space is fine if you wish, never required.
- **5 min. Change.** Each gives a reason; find a change you both accept; vote once each; do it.
- **5 min. After.** Draw it from the same place.
  **Say.** "What is different? Did it help? What shows that?" Expect: a difference they see; "no" with a seen reason is fine.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17]] Draw the spot from another corner and keep it hidden until now.
  **Say.** "Does this show it helped? What would you keep or change?" Expect: a clue they point to, and a change.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear ten children. Longer: find a second difference.

### Words and supports

Before, after, same, different, help, change, with pictures. Frame: "Before, ___. After, ___. It helped (or did not) because ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 returns each card with your written next step; each child adds or changes one mark. Today's exit (s02-X17), errors C and F included, goes on the record sheet.
