# Sort bins and the practice flag

Two bins and a practice flag for days 45 to 47, all made by us. The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No date and no statement is printed on a child's page; the teacher says them aloud.

## The two bins

1. **Old paper:** a picture of a yellowed paper with a ribbon. Say: "the record says."
2. **Speech bubble:** a picture of a round speech bubble. Say: "people say it means."

At most two statements for each symbol are sorted. Tokens go into a bin; the teacher says the statement aloud and the pair puts the token in a bin and says why.

## The practice flag (day 45)

A plain flag drawn with thirteen stripes and a block of stars, with two paper tokens. Pairs put a token on a star and say "a star stands for a state," and put a token on a stripe and say "a stripe stands for one of the first states." Stickers read aloud: *first states*, *states now*.

## Practice statements, with the bin each belongs in (teacher key)

- Flag: "long, long ago, in 1777, a law said thirteen stripes" (old paper); "people say red means being brave" (speech bubble).
- Eagle: "leaders picked it for the seal" (old paper); "people say the eagle shows strength" (speech bubble).
- The color of day 45 is red. Blue and white belong to the exits and to day 51 and are not used here.

## Question routine (days 45 to 47)

Day 45: the teacher asks the page a question and finds the answer. Day 46: each child asks the page one question about the eagle and points to the page that answers. Day 47: each child answers a question from the page.
