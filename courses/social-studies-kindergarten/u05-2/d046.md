# Day 46: The eagle, and a question for the page

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang page 2 and the day 45 flag print; set the two bins. Pick today's six.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Point to the day 45 flag print.

**Ask.** What does a star stand for? Expect: a state.

If many missed day 45's exit (error C, error J), repair four minutes: the flag with tokens on "first states" and "states now"; two new statements into the bins, the old law gave no reason for the colors.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Read page 2: a bald eagle before a round seal, and an old paper dated 1782. Its maker is us, in 2026. Review: "Who made it, and why?" from unit 3. Long, long ago, in 1782, leaders picked the bald eagle for the Great Seal. The bald eagle lives only in North America. Sources: the State Department's seal booklet and the Fish and Wildlife Service page.

Sort two statements, as on day 45. "Leaders picked it for the seal" goes in the old paper bin; "people say the eagle shows strength" goes in the speech bubble bin. Model: "Who picked this bird?" Find the answer on the page and point. Pledge continued: many children standing together in one big country outline. One nation, together: one country, not split apart. Nobody recites.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Say: "This is a pretend class." Pairs sort the two eagle statements, then each child asks the page one question about the eagle and points to its answer. Moss says "Eagles fly high, so one became our bird." Answer: the page says leaders picked it (error D). Hear the six, 60 seconds each: "Why can this bird stand for the United States?" ([[s05-X46b]]) and "What is one question about this bird that our book page could answer? Point to the page." ([[s05-X46c]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit

Every child circles the bird on the exit card's seal ([[s05-X46a]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No bins: use two bowls. No print: draw an eagle inside a circle.

**Skip.** The pairs.

- **5 min. The page.** Draw an eagle in a round seal and an old paper. Say: leaders picked it in 1782; it lives only in North America.
  **Say.** "Who picked this bird?" Expect: leaders.
- **5 min. A question.** Say: "A page can answer a question."
  **Say.** "What could we ask our book about this bird?" Expect: a question the page can answer.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X46a]] [[s05-X46b]] [[s05-X46c]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort or questions. Longer: a second question.

### Words and supports

Eagle, seal, picked, page. A point counts.

### Tomorrow

If many said "a pretty picture" (error B), "flies high" (error D) or "I just know" (error I), day 47 repairs the one most missed. Day 47: the anthem.
