# Day 22: A globe and a map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang wonder card 3. Set the globe out.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Review: the wonder wall. Read card 3, "What can maps and globes show?" Mark s03-X21. For errors K and A, turn the picture map the same way up for all and name left and right on the page first.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Look down on a tray of four things, then lay a flat drawing beside it: a map is a flat drawing seen from above and stands for the tray. Turn the globe: dots are land, plain is water; a globe is a small model of Earth. Find both on the flat map. Puppet: "Two different Earths" (error B); string round the same land on both. Puppet: "A map of one place, like our room" (error I); set the room map beside it.

**Ask.** How are the globe and the map alike? How are they different? Expect: both show land and water; one is round, one flat.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs put a token on water on the ball and on the flat outline, then say one way alike and one way different. Hear today's six, 45 seconds each. Others draw both.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 22 ("a pretend card"); all touch water on each outline; mark s03-X22. The six say one way alike and one way different. "A different Earth" is error B; "a small place" is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No ball: use an orange or a bowl. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the class record.

- **5 min. Round and flat.** Draw a few land shapes on a ball, then the same shapes on paper. Water is the empty part.
  **Say.** "Show me water on each. How are they alike?" Expect: both have land and water.
- **5 min. Different.** Turn the ball.
  **Say.** "How are they different?" Expect: one is round and one is flat; the ball shows one side at a time.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X22]] Draw a ball and a flat page with the same land. Your child touches water on each.
  **Say.** "Tell one way alike, one way different." Expect: both show land and water; round and flat.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear only today's six. Longer: one land shape on both.

### Words and supports

Globe, map, land, water, round, flat. Frame: "Both show ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 23 opens with s03-X22; for errors B and I, match one land outline on both and set a world map beside the room map.
