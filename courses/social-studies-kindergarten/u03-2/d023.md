# Day 23: Two maps of one Earth

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay Two World Maps beside the globe. Today's six are day 26's six too.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Mark day 22's s03-X22. For errors B and I, match one land outline on globe and flat map; set a world map beside the room map. Say, "The globe stands for Earth."

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Review: two views (unit 1). "This maker put the Atlantic Ocean in the middle. This maker put the Pacific Ocean in the middle." Trace the outlined land shape on the globe, then on each map: it did not move on Earth. Puppet: "The land moved!" (error B); trace it again. Say "the maker chose". No projection words.

**Ask.** What is in the middle of this map? What does it help us see together? Expect: an ocean, pointing counts; what each shows.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs trace the shape on the globe and both maps, say what is in the middle of each, and say what each helps us see. Hear today's six, 50 seconds each; scribe their "Both ___, but ___." sentences. Tonight write each a next step; read it on day 26.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 23 ("a pretend card"); all point to the island on each map; mark s03-X23. The six say whether the island moved on Earth and what changed. "It moved" is error B; "only the island's place" is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No world maps: draw the same three land shapes twice, in a different order. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The globe, the string and the class record.

- **5 min. Two pages.** Draw three land shapes in a row. Draw them again with a different shape in the middle.
  **Say.** "What is in the middle of this page?" Expect: a different shape on each.
- **5 min. Did it move?** Point to one shape on both pages.
  **Say.** "Did the land move on Earth? What changed?" Expect: no; the maker chose a different middle.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X23]] Draw one small island near the middle, then near the edge. Your child circles it each time.
  **Say.** "Did it move on Earth?" Expect: no; only the page changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one map beside the globe. Longer: a third land shape.

### Words and supports

Middle, map, globe, land, water, alike, different. Frame: "Both ___, but ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 24 opens with s03-X23; for errors B and I, set a changed page place beside an unchanged place, and a world map beside a room map.
