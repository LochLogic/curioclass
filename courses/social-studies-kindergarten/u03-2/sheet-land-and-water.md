# Land and water sheet and ball kit (day 22)

One kit for each pair of children: a ball (or a globe turned by the teacher), paper land shapes to tape on it, and one flat outline sheet of the same land.

**The flat outline sheet.** A large landscape page with a thick border. The world's land is drawn as large simple outlines redrawn from the public-domain Natural Earth land data (terms read 2026-11-19), land patterned with dots and water plain. No words and no grid. At the bottom a row of three round circles, one for each token a child places.

**The paper land shapes.** Four to six paper shapes cut from the same pattern as the flat sheet, with sticky tape rolled on the back, to tape onto the ball so that the ball shows land (patterned) and water (the ball's plain surface). A cut piece laid flat shows why the flat sheet needs a choice: wrapped paper does not lie flat without a cut.

**Use:** the pair looks down at a tray of objects, then lays the flat drawing of the tray beside it. Then the teacher turns the globe and finds land and water, finds land and water on the flat map, and says one way alike and one way different. Each pair puts a token on water on the ball and on the flat outline, then says one way alike and one way different.

**Access:** raised or string outlines for land, rough paper for land and smooth paper for water, pattern and not color alone.
