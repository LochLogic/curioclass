# Day 10: Practice what we need

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the record sheet's 0 and 1 cells from day 9's assessment (s01-E1 to s01-E8). Make at most four groups, each named by an activity, chosen privately and mixed. Set one fresh-try card at each table. List children not yet heard, and children whose item 3 outside-setting cell is blank. Teacher key: key-exits-6-10.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Say, "Today we practise what we are still working on." Name no child's group. Pairs begin: one says an idea, the partner answers it.

#### 13 min. Move groups

Spend about two minutes at each table: the move, then the fresh try on a new card. The pairs work from a picture move you show and speak.

- **The sorting table** (items 3 and 4, errors B, H, J). Puppets act a rule with and without it; name who it helps. For error H, set a crayon each child picks beside a rule for everyone. For error J, ask the people it affects first. Fresh try: the museum card (s01-X10). Read aloud: "Circle what the rule protects." Ask, "Why this rule?" Right: the objects stay whole and others can still see them. "It is a rule" is error B; "good because it is good" is error E.
- **The picture table** (items 7 and 8, errors E, F, L). Point to the result in the picture and say "because" together. Model "Why do you think that?", then swap asking and answering. Fresh try: the boot mat.
- **The circle table** (items 5 and 6, errors I, C, D). Match an answering reply to an idea card. Answer one idea sitting and one moving. Set two reasons side by side without ranking. Fresh try: the story book.
- **The wonder table** (item 1, error A). Offer two uses of an answer; the child picks one. Fresh try: the reading corner.

#### 15 min. Fresh tries

Show Garden Path to the children whose item 3 outside-setting cell is blank, six at a time: "Why is this rule needed? (s01-E3) What could happen without it? (s01-E3b)" Finish children not yet heard on day 9 and any unfinished part, with the card that fits. A child may pass or point. Record each as a new dated attempt. A part still unheard stays "not yet observed", never inferred.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: draw a glass case with a model sailboat and people looking.

**Skip.** The groups and the pairs.

- **5 min. Practice.** Choose one part from the unit assessment that was hard: a reason for a rule, or "because".
  **Say.** "Why do we keep a doorway clear?" Expect: so people can get through.
- **5 min. Fresh try.** Your child says "I think ___, because ___" about where to keep the blocks. You ask "Why do you think that?"
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a rule and who it helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X10]] Draw a glass case with a boat and people looking. Your child circles what the rule protects.
  **Say.** "Why this rule?" Expect: the boat stays whole and everyone can see it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two groups. Longer: a child gives a second reason.

### Words and supports

Frames: "I think ___, because ___." "Why do you think that?" Pointing is an answer.
