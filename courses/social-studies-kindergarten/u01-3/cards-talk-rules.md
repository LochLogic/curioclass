# Talk-rule pictures (day 6) and the class poster

Three pictures, one for each talk rule. They are made by our class's teacher for our class circle; say so, and say what they are for. They go on a chart-paper poster, one under another, with the children's own words written beside them by you. Plain black line drawings, no printed words, no brand names. Eye contact and stillness are not part of any rule, so none of the pictures shows them as required.

## Picture 1: listen to the speaker

**Picture:** A circle of five children of different heights and skin tones sit on a rug. One child in the middle has her hand raised and her mouth open; the others turn their faces or bodies toward her. One child is rocking slightly, another is holding a fidget ring. A small ear drawing floats above the circle.

**Rule:** Listen to the speaker. Listening can look different.

## Picture 2: wait for a turn

**Picture:** The same circle. A child holds a smooth stone; he is speaking. A girl waits with one finger raised, a small "me next" card on her lap (a picture of a hand and the number two). A third child holds a card showing two hands taking turns.

**Rule:** Wait for a turn.

## Picture 3: answer what was said

**Picture:** Two children side by side. The first has a speech bubble holding a small window with raindrops. The second has a speech bubble holding the same window with raindrops, plus a smile. A thick arrow joins the two bubbles.

**Rule:** Answer what was said.

## Teaching model picture pair

Use two bubble pictures under the poster: one reply that answers (the window with raindrops again) and one that changes the subject (a ball). The teacher says "I want to go to the window to see the rain" and then each reply. Children say which one answers.
