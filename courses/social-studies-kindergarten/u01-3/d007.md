# Day 7: Two views

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold two puppets: Moss, round head; Wren, tall head with a curl. Tape the two headings on the board. Choose today's six, not yet heard on a spoken exit.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Return day 6's exit (s01-X6). For error C, set sitting still beside "I answer what you said"; for error I, set "I want to talk about dinosaurs" beside "I agree, then we can reach them". A small group takes the rest. Ask what the talk rules say.

#### 6 min. Two views

Say, "We made these cards. The puppets are pretend, and neither is right." Show the question card: where to hang our class paintings, on the window wall or the door wall. Moss shows the window wall and Wren the door wall. Tape each puppet's cards under "I think" and "my friend thinks".

Model as Moss: "We both want the paintings seen, but I like the window wall because the sun makes the colors bright, and my friend likes the door wall because everyone sees it when they come in." Children echo.

**Ask.** How are Moss and Wren alike? How are they different? Expect: both want the paintings seen; they choose different walls, with different reasons. Disagreeing is normal and safe. For error D ("Wren is wrong"), set it beside "both have a reason".

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs: each child chooses a wall or a place of their own and a reason picture, and tells a partner: "I like ___ because ___; my friend likes ___ because ___." No child is assigned a view.

Today's six get practice notes toward items 2 and 5: write one thing done and one next step each; read it privately by tomorrow. The revised attempt is in their day 9 group. Read aloud: "Draw where you would hang the paintings. Draw why."

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Every child marks the exit card (s01-X7); read aloud: "Pia wants drawing first, because the paper is ready. Tov wants listening first, because the room is quiet. Draw a line from each puppet to its reason." Ask the six and mark the list: "How are their ideas alike or different?" Right: both choose a first thing; the things and reasons differ. "The listening puppet is wrong" is error D; "Drawing is best because it is best" is error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a window wall and a door wall. Two spoons are puppets. No exit card: draw two puppets, a quiet room and a table with paper.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the notes.

- **5 min. Two views.** Two spoons each choose a wall and a reason, as Moss and Wren did.
  **Say.** "How are the two spoons alike? How are they different?" Expect: both want the paintings seen; they choose different walls for different reasons.
- **5 min. Your view.** Your child picks a wall and says why. You pick the other and say why.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the wall and the reason.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]] One puppet wants drawing first, one wants listening first. Your child draws a line from each puppet to its reason.
  **Say.** "How are their ideas alike or different?" Expect: both choose a first thing; the things and reasons differ.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pair of cards. Longer: a third place.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I like ___ because ___; my friend likes ___ because ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 teaches "I think ..., because ..." and the partner's "Why do you think that?" Its reopen repairs s01-X7 error D ("the listening puppet is wrong"); error E is taught on day 8.
