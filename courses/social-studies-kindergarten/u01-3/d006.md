# Day 6: Our talk rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the talk-rule pictures in a column on chart paper, room beside each for the children's words. Choose today's six, not yet heard on a spoken exit.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Read Our Class Charter from day 5 aloud and return day 5's exit (s01-X5). Ask which rule helps if two people want the same thing. Pointing is an answer.

#### 6 min. Talk rules

Say who made the poster and why: "I drew these to help our circle talk together." Read each rule: listen to the speaker (listening can look still, moving or signing), wait for a turn (the stone), answer what was said.

Say, "I want to go to the window to see the rain." Reply "I want to see the rain too." Reply again, "I like balls."

**Ask.** Which reply answers what I said? Expect: the rain one. For error I, set the ball reply beside the rain reply and point to the second.

Sit very still: "I like balls." Then rock: "I want to see the rain too." **Ask.** Who listened? Expect: the one who answered. For error C, say moving and signing can listen.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Every two minutes a new group of six joins you in the circle. Say one idea to each: line up by the door; rain on the roof; a red crayon; read a book; a fish cloud. Children reply in turn, by word, sign or card, or pass the stone and show a reply privately. Notes today are practice.

Other children, in pairs, match each idea card to its answering reply, then draw both. Read aloud: "Draw an idea you heard. Draw a reply that answers it."

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Every child marks the exit card (s01-X6); read aloud: "Point to the picture where the second child answers the idea. Show me the picture where a child is talking about something else." Ask the six and mark the list: read "I want the scarves hung lower so we can reach them." Say, "Show how to answer that idea after your turn comes." Right: say it back, or agree or disagree with a reason; record turn and reply separately. The still picture is error C; "I want to talk about dinosaurs" is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: draw an ear, a raised hand and two speech bubbles. No exit card: draw three children and a row of scarves.

**Skip.** The circle and the pair cards.

- **5 min. The rules.** Draw the ear, the hand and the bubbles. Say what each means: listen, wait, answer.
  **Say.** "I want to go to the window to see the rain. Which reply answers me: I want to see it too, or I like balls?" Expect: I want to see it too.
- **5 min. Your turn.** Say an idea. Your child answers it. Swap.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws an idea and a reply that answers it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]] Draw one child pointing at scarves, a second child pointing too, and a third pointing at a truck. Your child shows who answers.
  **Say.** "I want the scarves hung lower so we can reach them. How could you answer?" Expect: I agree, then we can reach them.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two idea cards. Longer: add "because".

### Words and supports

Listen: answer what was said. A pointed card, a sign or an aid is an answer.
