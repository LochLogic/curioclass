# Fresh-try cards (day 10)

Four new cases, one for each group the record sheet may call on day 10. Say first: "We made this card. It is pretend." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each. Plain black line drawings, no printed words, no brand names. None repeats a card from days 6 to 9. The museum card for the sorting table is the day 10 exit card in cards-exits-6-10.

## The picture table: the boot mat

**Picture, left:** A mat inside a classroom door with a row of rain boots standing on it. The floor beside the mat is clean and dry. **Picture, right:** The same door with no mat: muddy footprints run across the floor.

**Try:** The child says "I think we need the boot mat, because ..." and a partner asks "Why do you think that?" **Key:** the floor stays clean and dry; the partner's question aims at the claim and the answer says because.

## The circle table: the story book

**Picture:** Two puppets, Bix with a round head and Dot with a tall head and a bow. Bix holds up a frog book with big pictures. Dot holds up a thin moon book. A large question mark floats above: which story first?

**Try:** The child says what each puppet likes and why, and how the two ideas are alike or different. **Key:** both want a story first; they choose different books for different reasons (big pictures, short). Neither puppet is wrong.

## The wonder table: the reading corner

**Picture:** An empty corner with a low shelf. Two small pictures beside it: a soft rug with a child resting a book on it, and a shelf of books in a row.

**Try:** The child names what would make the corner a good place to learn and why that matters to them. **Key:** any use of the corner with a personal reason, such as "I could read the frog book there." A supported private answer counts.

## Key for the sorting table

The museum card: the whole model sailboat, because the rule keeps the objects whole and others can still see them.
