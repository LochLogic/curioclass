# Day 8: I think, because

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the teaching card and the cup frame. Lay out the charter's rule pictures. Choose today's six: the day 3 group again.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Repair day 7's exit (s01-X7). For error D, set "the listening puppet is wrong" beside "both have a reason"; for error E, point to the paper ready and say "because" together. Then the class reopens one charter rule: each child points to one of the charter pictures and tells a partner, "I want ___ because ___." This is a shared choice, not a count. Hold up the cup frame: a reason you can see.

#### 7 min. Claim and why-question

Say, "We made this card to practise on. It is not about anyone in our class." Show the teaching card.

**Ask.** Why might someone think we need the paper tray? Expect: everyone can reach it; the small one cannot reach the high shelf.

Then a puppet says, "I think we need the paper tray, because everyone can reach it." The second puppet asks, "Why do you think that?" and the first answers, "Because the tray is low and the paper is right there." Children echo claim, question and answer. For error E ("it is best because it is best"), point to the card's result and say "because" together. For error F, model the question again; for error L, model the answer.

#### 14 min. Partner practice

Question: "Which rule matters most for our class?" Pairs choose a charter rule picture and say, "I think we need ___, because ___." The partner asks, "Why do you think that?" and the first answers. Swap. A puppet may ask. Read aloud: "Draw the rule you chose. Draw why it helps."

Sit with today's six and note item 5: the turn, the response and the shared choice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Every child marks the exit card (s01-X8); read aloud: "Circle the rule picture that helps." Ask the six in pairs: one answers "Which rule helps this group? Why do you think so?", the partner asks "Why do you think that?", the first answers; swap. A child may pass or point. Mark the list. Right: take turns, because both can use the bucket. "Best because it is best" is error E; asking the bucket's color, error F; repeating "Take turns" when asked why, error L.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: draw a low tub and a high box of crayons. No exit card: draw two children and one bucket.

**Skip.** The charter and the pairs.

- **5 min. The frame.** Show the low tub and the high box.
  **Say.** "I think we need the low tub. Why do you think that?" Expect: because everyone can reach it.
- **5 min. Your turn.** Your child picks a rule, such as taking turns, and says "I think we need ___, because ___." You ask "Why do you think that?" Swap.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the rule and why it helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]] Your child circles the turn rule for two children and one bucket.
  **Say.** "Which rule helps? Why do you think so?" Expect: take turns, because both can use the bucket.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pair, one swap. Longer: a second reason.

### Words and supports

Frames: "I think ___, because ___." "Why do you think that?" Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 is the unit assessment, in groups of six, on pictures the class has not seen. Nothing new is taught. Day 10 meets s01-X8 errors E, F and L.
