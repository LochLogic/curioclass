# Idea cards and reply cards (day 6)

Four idea cards. Each has two reply cards: one answers the idea and one changes the subject. Pairs match each idea to the reply that answers it. The cards are reused on day 9 for children who finish early. Plain black line drawings, no printed words, no brand names. None repeats the exit card about the scarves.

## Idea 1: the bird at the window

**Idea card:** A child points at a bird sitting on a window ledge. The child's mouth is open in wonder.
**Reply that answers:** A second child looks at the same bird and points too.
**Reply that changes the subject:** A second child holds up a block tower, looking away from the window.

## Idea 2: a turn at the easel

**Idea card:** A child stands beside the easel holding a brush, waiting, with a small number-two card in her hand.
**Reply that answers:** A second child at the easel hands over the brush and points to a waiting line.
**Reply that changes the subject:** A second child runs a toy truck along the floor, back turned.

## Idea 3: the new leaf

**Idea card:** A child points at the class plant, which has one new green leaf at its top.
**Reply that answers:** A second child bends close to the leaf with a small watering can nearby.
**Reply that changes the subject:** A second child holds a picture book open, looking at it.

## Idea 4: the tall tower

**Idea card:** A child stands beside a tower of blocks as tall as himself, arms spread wide.
**Reply that answers:** A second child holds a block, ready to add it to the top of the tower.
**Reply that changes the subject:** A second child spins a pinwheel by the door.

## Teacher note

The answering reply always shows the second child attending to the same thing as the first. It does not depend on stillness or eye contact; a child turned partly away who is still pointing at the same thing answers.
