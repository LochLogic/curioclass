# Day 9: The unit assessment

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the assessment packet in its envelope until now. Lay the charter's rule pictures from day 5 (the two chosen and the third candidate) at the drawing tables. Open the record sheet to the days 7 to 9 notes and day 8's exit (s01-X8).

### The day

#### 2 min. Introduce

Say, "I talk with six of you at a time. The rest draw one picture of why a rule from our charter helps, then sort the reply cards in pairs." Finishers draw a second rule. The drawing is not scored. Collect the pages.

#### 25 min. Fresh-source groups

Five groups of six, five minutes each, with a minute between groups for the move: each child answers by word, point, sign or aid, or passes. Use neutral prompts only ("Tell me more." "Show me in the picture."); supply no reasons. In order:

- **Two minutes (items 1 and 7).** In turn, one sentence each: "Our big question is: what makes our class a good place to learn? Why does that matter to you?" Then: "What makes our class a good place to learn? Use something from a picture we looked at."
- **One minute (items 3 and 4).** Show School Doorway and read its notice. "Why is the rule needed? (s01-E3) What could happen without it? (s01-E3b) Point to the school rule. What could the class suggest if a rule does not help?" Garden Path is heard on day 10.
- **Two minutes (items 6 and 8).** Show the playground-ball question cards. Pairs: "What do you think? What does your partner think? How are your views alike or different?" (the partner's reply to an idea gives item 5). Then the partner asks, "Why do you think that?" and the speaker answers; swap. This also gives item 2's why-question and answer.

Score items 2 and 5 only from a dated later attempt after the day 7 practice note and its feedback; with none, record "not yet observed". Mark each part 2 (independent), 1 (content-prompted), 0 (heard, not shown) or blank (not yet observed). A part not reached waits for day 10.

#### 3 min. Wonder wall look back

At the wonder wall ask, "Why did our question matter to us?" Write a few children's words, and the dictated line on the drawings of the six you heard last. Not scored.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a doorway with a cart in it, a garden bed with flat plants, and two places for balls.

**Skip.** The groups, the drawing and the wonder wall.

- **10 min. The assessment.** Show your drawings and read each notice aloud: "Keep this doorway clear." "Stay on the path." Add no reasons.
  **Say.** "Why do we have this rule? What could happen without it?" Expect: the cart blocks the door; the plants get stepped on.
  **Say.** "What makes our class a good place to learn?" Expect: a claim, a picture detail and because.
  **Say.** "Why do you think that?" Your child asks you, too.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child shows where the balls should live and what you chose. Note one part to practise.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear a missed part on day 10. Longer: a sixth minute for each group.

### Words and supports

Pass, point, sign, draw or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Mark the sheet for ten minutes after class. Day 10 forms at most four groups from the 0 and 1 cells of s01-E1 to s01-E8 and day 8's exit errors E, F and L (s01-X8), hears Garden Path (s01-E3), and finishes children not yet heard.
