# Day 9: Our check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the assessment packet in its envelope until now. Lay Our Class Charter's rule pictures from day 5 (the two chosen and the third candidate) at the drawing tables. Open the record sheet to the days 7 to 9 notes and day 8's exit (s01-X8).

### The day

#### 2 min. Introduce

Say, "I talk with six of you at a time. The rest draw one picture of why a rule from our charter helps." Finishers draw a second rule or sort the reply cards. The drawing is practice, not scored. Collect the pages at the end.

#### 15 min. Fresh-source groups

Five groups of six, three minutes each, as quick rounds: each child answers by word, point, sign or aid, or passes. Use neutral prompts only ("Tell me more." "Show me in the picture."); supply no reasons. In order:

- **Half a minute (item 1).** "Our big question is: what makes our class a good place to learn? Why does that matter to you?"
- **One minute (items 3 and 4).** Show School Doorway, then Garden Path, and read each notice. "Why is each rule needed? What could happen without it?" "Point to the school rule. How does it work here? What could the class suggest if a rule does not help?"
- **Half a minute (item 6).** Show the playground-ball question cards. Pairs: "What do you think? What does your partner think? How are your views alike or different?" The partner's reply to an idea gives item 5.
- **Half a minute (item 7).** "What makes our class a good place to learn? Use something from a picture we looked at."
- **Half a minute (item 8).** A partner asks, "Why do you think that?" and the speaker answers. This also gives item 2's why-question and answer.

Score items 2 and 5 only from a dated later attempt after the day 7 practice note and its feedback; with none, record "not yet observed". Mark each part independent, content-prompted or not yet observed. A part not reached waits for day 10.

#### 3 min. Wonder wall look back

At the wonder wall ask, "Why did our question matter to us?" Write a few children's words, and each child's dictated line on their drawing. Not scored; item 1 is scored from the group answer.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a doorway with a cart in it, a garden bed with flat plants, and two places for balls.

**Skip.** The groups, the drawing and the wonder wall.

- **10 min. The assessment.** Show your drawings and read each notice aloud: "Keep this doorway clear." "Stay on the path." Add no reasons.
  **Say.** "Why do we have this rule? What could happen without it?" Expect: the cart blocks the door; the plants get stepped on.
  **Say.** "What makes our class a good place to learn?" Expect: a claim, a picture detail and because.
  **Say.** "Why do you think that?" Your child asks you, too.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child shows where the balls should live and what you chose. Note one part to practise.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear a missed part on day 10. Longer: the thirty-minute route adds ten minutes.

### Words and supports

Pass, point, sign, draw or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Mark the sheet for ten minutes after class. Day 10 forms at most four groups from the 0 and 1 cells of day 9's assessment (s01-E1 to s01-E8) and day 8's exit errors E, F and L (s01-X8); children not yet heard finish.
