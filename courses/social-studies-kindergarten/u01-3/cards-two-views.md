# Two-view cards (day 7)

One question, two puppet views, each with a reason picture. Say first: "We made these cards. The puppets are pretend, and neither puppet is right." Plain black line drawings, no printed words, no brand names. The two puppets are folded paper with dot eyes: Moss has a round head, Wren has a tall head with a curl on top.

## Question card: where to hang our class paintings

**Picture:** A classroom with two blank walls. One wall has a window; the other has the door. A row of five paintings on a table waits to be hung. A large question mark sits above the table.

## Choice card 1: the window wall

**Picture:** Moss points at the window wall, where one painting hangs.

**Reason card:** The same painting with sunlight falling on it, its colors bright, and a sun drawing beside it. (Because the light shows the colors.)

## Choice card 2: the door wall

**Picture:** Wren points at the door wall, where one painting hangs.

**Reason card:** A line of children walking in through the open door and looking at the painting. (Because everyone sees it when they come in.)

## Column headings

Two small pictures for the columns under the board: (1) a child pointing at their own chest, for "I think"; (2) a child pointing at a friend, for "my friend thinks". Place each puppet's choice card and reason card under a heading.

## Teacher note

Model: "We both want the paintings to be seen, but I like the window wall because ... and my friend likes the door wall because ..." Pictures hold the reasons; children may choose either view, or a third. Children choose without being told which puppet they are like.
