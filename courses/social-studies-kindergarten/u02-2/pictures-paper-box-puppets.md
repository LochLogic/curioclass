# The Paper Box (puppets disagree)

Day 13 teaching story, told with two paper-bag puppets (Bibi and Oko) and these pictures. Nine pages. Made by Curio Class for this class to think with; it is invented and is not a record of what our class did. Art: plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing. Each puppet voices a reason, so no child is singled out. Two perspective columns on the board hold the reasons, drawn as a picture each.

**Page 1.** Text: "Bibi and Oko are puppets. They share a box of drawing paper."
Picture: two paper-bag puppets, one with yarn hair and one with a paper hat, behind a table. A flat cardboard box of paper sits between them.

**Page 2.** Text: "Today the box needs a home. Bibi says, 'By the door.'"
Picture: Bibi points at a low shelf by a classroom door.

**Page 3.** Text: "'Why?' asks the teacher. Bibi says, 'Because the door is where we line up. Paper is quick to grab.'"
Picture: Bibi, a small picture bubble above shows children lining up at the door.

**Page 4.** Text: "Oko says, 'By the table.'"
Picture: Oko points at a small shelf beside the art table.

**Page 5.** Text: "'Why?' asks the teacher. Oko says, 'Because we draw at the table. Paper is close to our hands.'"
Picture: Oko; bubble shows children drawing at the table.

**Page 6.** Text: "Listen. Both have a reason. Is there a place both can accept?"
Picture: the two puppets facing each other, the box between them, and two small columns on a board behind, each with a picture of its reason.

**Page 7.** Text: "Bibi thinks. 'The shelf at the end of the table is near both. Could we try that?'"
Picture: a shelf at the corner where the table meets the walkway, a short step from the door. Bibi points.

**Page 8.** Text: "Oko says, 'Yes! I can agree to that.' They agree, so they do not need to vote."
Picture: both puppets nod. The box is on the corner shelf.

**Page 9.** Text: "If they still did not agree, they could vote. Everyone would get one turn. Then they would count."
Picture: two small towers of identical blocks, one a little taller, with a hand placing a last block.

**Teacher note.** Teaches listen, agree and vote with pictures. Hold the reasons in the columns, not the puppets' names.
