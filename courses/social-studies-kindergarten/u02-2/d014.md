# Day 14: We choose and we do

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the proposal cards, record and columns. Both proposals stay safe and reachable. Hide the exit cards. Choose today's six.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Read the question and Our Decision Record aloud. Say, "We vote only when we still do not agree." If many gave error G on day 13's exit (s02-X13), set "I'm picking" beside "Tell me why" with the puppets inside these 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Two proposals

[[s02-P14-1]]

Puppets voice each proposal and its reason; tape a picture of each reason in its column. Each child says through a puppet, "I want ___ because ___." For "because I want it", ask, "What will it do for our room?"

#### 4 min. Seek a shared revision

**Ask.** What change could both ideas accept? Expect: a safe change keeping a reason from each, such as bags in a bin beside the low hooks. If every child accepts the change, say so and count hands aloud.

#### 5 min. Vote and count

If any child still wants the other, each child directs one identical block onto a tower; an adult places it if the child points. Stand the towers side by side, compare height, count each aloud. The taller tower is the choice. Say, "More blocks chose it. That does not mean everyone wanted it" (error B). On a tie, try both in turn, starting today.

#### 7 min. First action together

Ask, "What is our first step?" and draw it on the record. Adults keep safety jobs. Each child does one small part, such as carrying one bag; directing counts. Say, "We picked it, so we are done" (error J). The plan runs in routines until day 17.

#### 1 min. Record

Write the date, result and reasons, and any other wish as a reason, never a name. For error I ("so that puppet is wrong"), say, "Its reason stays on the record; we check later."

#### 6 min. Exit

Show the count, plan and do cards: "Which picture shows counting the votes, before we make the plan?" Every child points to the count card, or passes. Each draws what they did to help carry out our decision. Write the six's words; mark s02-X14 against what you saw. Right: a real action by the child, after the count, for the class's choice. "We picked it" with nothing done is error J.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No blocks: paper squares. No cards: draw a count, a plan and a step.

**Skip.** The puppets and the class record.

- **5 min. Two ideas.** Pick a small job in a picture with two ways to do it, such as where a toy goes. Your own space is fine if you wish, never required. Each of you says, "I want ___ because ___."
- **5 min. Both.** Ask what change you both could accept.
  **Say.** "What would we both like?" Expect: a way that keeps a reason from each.
- **10 min. Vote and do.** Each adds one block to a tower, count together, then do the first step.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X14]] Your child draws what they did to carry out your choice.
  **Say.** "What did you do to help?" Expect: a real thing they did.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pass through the vote. Longer: say what you would hear first in a tie.

### Words and supports

Agree, vote, choose, count. Frame: "We can try ___." Pointing is an answer; a communication aid counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 15's reopen gives its 3 minutes to errors B, I and J from today's exit (s02-X14).
