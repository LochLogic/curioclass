# Problem spot cards (taught on day 13)

Two spot cards and two blank ones for children's own ideas. A problem is a place or a thing, never a child: no person in any picture is shown as the cause. Plain line drawings, one-word label, thick outlines. The teacher swaps the picture for the class's real spot when it differs.

**Coat hooks.** Label: "hooks". Picture: a wall with a row of eight hooks, coats and bags piled on top of one another and overlapping; a few hang to the floor. Two children, one in a wheelchair, stand at the edge looking at the pile with no room to reach their hook.

**Block corner.** Label: "blocks". Picture: a carpet corner with a tall heap of wooden blocks spilling across, several children's hands reaching, and a bin at the side already overflowing.

**Blank cards.** Two empty frames for the teacher to draw a spot a child names.
