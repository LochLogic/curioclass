# Exit card 13: which is a real classroom problem

Three picture cards. Printed "invented for practice". Children circle one. Never used in teaching (rule 4): the taught cards are the coat hooks and block corner.

**Card 1 (the problem).** Label: "cushions". Picture: cushions heaped across the walkway; a child with a walker waits at the end of the heap.

**Card 2 (not a problem).** Label: "color". Picture: a child holding up a green crayon with a happy face. Nothing in the way.

**Card 3 (not a problem).** Label: "game". Picture: a child holding a ball with a happy face. Nothing in the way.

Teacher says: "A problem is something in the room that needs fixing. Circle the problem."
