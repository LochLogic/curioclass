# The Cushions (exit story 13)

Exit story for day 13, new and never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". Six pages, told with the same two puppet kinds but different puppets (Lulu and Max). The spoken question after the mark: "What should they do before choosing?" Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing.

**Page 1.** Text: "Lulu and Max are puppets. They share the reading corner."
Picture: a cozy reading corner with a rug, shelf of books, and a pile of five round cushions in the middle. Two paper-bag puppets stand at the edge.

**Page 2.** Text: "The cushions are in the way. Where should they go?"
Picture: a child with a cane cannot pass the pile on the walkway. The cushions block the path.

**Page 3.** Text: "Lulu says, 'Put them in the basket by the window. Then the walkway is clear.'"
Picture: Lulu points at a big basket under a window.

**Page 4.** Text: "Max says, 'Put them on the shelf by the books. Then we reach them to sit.'"
Picture: Max points at a low shelf next to the books.

**Page 5.** Text: "Lulu and Max are loud. Each wants to choose."
Picture: the two puppets facing one another, mouths wide open, small spiky lines at their mouths. Not mean; just loud.

**Page 6.** Text: "Stop. What should they do before choosing?"
Picture: the empty reading corner with a question mark over the pile. (Teacher pauses. Right answers: hear each reason, look for a way both can accept, vote if they still disagree.)
