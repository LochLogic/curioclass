# Day 13: What can we do before we choose?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fold two puppets: Bibi, yarn hair; Oko, paper hat. Hang the Paper Box pictures; hide the exit cards. Give each child one identical block. Choose today's six.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Post and co-write "How can we choose together?" Recall day 7's columns. If many gave error A or H on day 12's exit (s02-X12), set "I am done" beside "I asked, then finished" and practice "May I?" with puppets.

#### 5 min. Warm-up vote

[[s02-P13-1]]

Children offer two plant names. Each sets one block on a name's tower. Stand the towers side by side, compare height, count together. The taller tower names the plant; a tie puts both names on the wonder wall.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

[[s02-K13-1]]

Tell The Paper Box with the puppets and pictures. At pages 3 and 5 ask the puppet "Why?" and draw its reason in its own column. Teach listen (hand at ear), agree (nod) and vote (one block).

**Ask.** After page 5: what should Bibi and Oko do before they choose? Expect: ask why, look for a place both can accept, vote only if still apart. [[steps:s02-K13-1]] For error G, set "I'm picking" beside "Tell me why."

#### 7 min. The class names problems

[[s02-P13-2]]

Say, "A problem is a place or a thing that needs fixing, never a person." Show the hooks and block corner cards, plus a blank for a spot a child names. Children point to the problem on each. Each child drops a block in a closed cup under the card to fix first, or passes; count. On a tie the other goes on the wonder wall.

#### 3 min. Ideas

Ask, "What could we try?" Draw two ideas on proposal cards. Say, "Both are safe and each of us can reach them. We never vote on safety."

#### 4 min. Exit

Tell the six Cushions pictures, then show the three cards. Every child points or sets a token on the real problem. The six tell a partner what the puppets should do before choosing; mark s02-X13. A child may pass. Right: hear each reason, look for a way both can accept, vote if still apart. "The loudest picks" is error G; "They vote, then everybody likes it" is error B.

After class start Our Decision Record and a "before" sketch from a marked floor spot.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No puppets: two spoons. No blocks: paper squares. No pictures: draw two boxes.

**Skip.** The plant vote and the class problem.

- **8 min. Two spoons.** Tell The Paper Box with spoons: one wants the box by the door, one by the table. Ask why each wants it.
  **Say.** "What should they do before they choose?" Expect: ask why, find a place both can accept, vote only if still apart.
- **7 min. A problem.** Your child shows one thing in a shared room that is in the way, never a person.
- **5 min. Vote.** Pick two games. Each adds one block to a tower. Count together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X13]] Tell it with the spoons: two want cushions in different places, and both are loud.
  **Say.** "What should they do before choosing?" Expect: hear each reason, look for a way both can accept.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card. Longer: offer a place neither puppet said.

### Words and supports

Agree, vote, choose, count, idea, problem, with pictures. Frame: "I hear you want ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 hears both proposals, seeks a change both accept, votes and acts. If many gave error G on today's exit (s02-X13), puppets give each reason first, inside its 2 minutes.
