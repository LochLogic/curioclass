# Exit card 14: count, plan, first step

Three picture cards. Never used in teaching (rule 4). Printed "invented for practice". Children circle the one that shows counting the votes before the plan. The three steps are shown in a new setting, a puppet theater, so the cards do not repeat the day's own block towers or the class's real problem.

**Card 1 (count).** Label: "count". Picture: two rows of paper puppet-theater tickets (a green row of five, a blue row of three) and a child with a ponytail touching each ticket with a pencil and making tally marks on a slip of paper. No blocks.

**Card 2 (plan).** Label: "plan". Picture: three children gathered around one sheet of paper with a simple drawing of a curtain moved to one corner. One child points at the drawing.

**Card 3 (first step).** Label: "do". Picture: two children carrying a folded curtain together toward the corner shown on the plan.

Teacher says: "Which picture shows counting the votes, before we make the plan?"

## Key (teacher)

Circle: Card 1.
