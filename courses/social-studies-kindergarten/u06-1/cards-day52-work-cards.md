# Seven work cards and two hoops (day 52)

One set a table of five. Plain line drawings of invented workers, one card each. Two hoops (or chalk circles), one drawn as a house and labeled home, one with no house and labeled not home. Read every label aloud.

## The seven cards

1. A grown-up cooks rice on a stove in a kitchen. (home)
2. A grown-up feeds and rocks a small child in a chair at home. (home)
3. A person paints the inside of a wall with a roller in a pretend home. (home; the people who live there pay)
4. A person lays stones for a wall outside a pretend school. (not home)
5. A person hoes rows of plants on a pretend farm. (not home)
6. A person drives a bus with riders in the windows. (not home)
7. A nurse listens to a child's chest with a stethoscope at a pretend clinic. (not home)

## How they are used

Each child places a card in a hoop, says what the worker does and who it helps (the sixth and seventh cards are done together). Say: "Home or not home is only where it happens. Paid or not paid is a different question." Cards 1 and 2 are work that is usually not paid; card 3 is work at home that is paid; both are in the same hoop.

## Teacher key

All seven are work. Hoops: home for 1, 2, 3; not home for 4, 5, 6, 7. Who it helps: 1 people who eat, 2 the small child, 3 the people who live there, 4 the school, 5 people who eat the plants, 6 the riders, 7 the child at the clinic.
