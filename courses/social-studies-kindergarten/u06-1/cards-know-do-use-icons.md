# Know, do, use icon cards

Three large blank icon cards, one set a pair: a head (know: what the worker keeps in mind), a hand (do: what the worker can do because they learned it, a skill) and a tool such as a wrench shape (use: what the worker uses). One picture and the word on each, read aloud. Kept on the classroom wall after day 53 and used again on days 54, 56, 59, 60 and 61.

Raised-line copies for a child who cannot see. A room with nothing: draw the three icons on scrap paper and tape them in a row.

Frame said aloud with the icons: "I think the worker knows ___, because the page says ___."
