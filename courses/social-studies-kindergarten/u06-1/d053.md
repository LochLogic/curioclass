# Day 53: What a worker knows, does and uses

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang Davi's page. Pairs get icons and strips.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Day 52's exit, 2 minutes: cafe cook beside home cook, both work. If many missed it (error A, or error I), the four minutes are a paid and not paid grid. Then, review from unit 4, day 37: the washing chart. "What changed? What stayed? Which clue helps?" Post "What does work need?"

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Say: "Curio Class made the Jobs Book in 2026. Pine Hollow is made up." Read Davi's page; set head (the way, when to stop), hand (steer gently, a skill), tool (bus, mirror). "A picture shows what Davi does; the book tells what he knows." Words: know, skill, tool. Pip: "The driver needs a bus and that is all." Answer: the bus is his tool; he also knows and can (error B).

#### 6 min. Supported work

Read Mina's page. Pairs place the icons and say each (review, unit 1): "I think she knows ___, because ___." Hear fifteen pairs, 20 seconds each. Show the school helper drawings.

#### 2 min. Then and now

Review of unit 4: pairs tell the next pair one change and one thing that stayed on the farm strip.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say: "This is a pretend class." Read the crossing helper page. All point: row one, something she knows ([[s06-X53a]]); row two, does ([[s06-X53b]]); row three, uses ([[s06-X53c]]). Hear six, 25 seconds each: "Tell me one thing she has to know" ([[s06-X53d]]), while the rest circle what changed on the digging strip ([[s06-X53e]]). Mark the record sheet. The sign for knows is error B.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No icons: draw a head, a hand and a tool on scrap paper. No strip: draw a field twice.

**Skip.** Pairs, Pip and the then and now round.

- **5 min. Davi.** Tell: Davi drives a bus in made-up Pine Hollow. He knows the way, can stop gently, uses the bus.
  **Say.** "What does Davi know? What does he do? What does he use?" Expect: the way; steers and stops; the bus and mirror.
- **5 min. Mina.** Tell Mina's page; your child sets the three icons beside it.
- **5 min. Farm.** Show two drawings of one field, planted by hand, then by machine.
  **Say.** "What changed? What stayed?" Expect: how seeds are planted; the field and growing food.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X53a]] [[s06-X53b]] [[s06-X53c]] [[s06-X53d]] [[s06-X53e]] Read the crossing helper page; ask each, then show the digging strip.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Davi only. Longer: a helper.

### Words and supports

Frame: "She knows ___. She does ___. She uses ___."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error B, day 54 opens with a tool-only label beside a new worker's know, do, use.
