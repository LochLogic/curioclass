# Work Around Us (day 52 pictures)

Three large pictures, read aloud by the teacher. The people are invented, and you say "this is a made-up town". Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except the heading on each. Words shown with pictures: work, inside, outside, paid, not paid. Never use these pictures as an exit case.

## Picture 1: cooking at home

Mr. Tran stands at the stove in his kitchen and makes a big pot of rice. Read: "Mr. Tran cooks at home so everyone there can eat. Nobody pays him." Ask: "Who does this help?" Say: "Cooking helps people eat. It is work."

Puppet Pip says: "Cooking at home is not work. Nobody pays." Answer with the picture: "Cooking helps people eat, so it is work, paid or not." (A)

## Picture 2: painting inside a home

Ms. Abara paints the inside walls of a pretend home with a roller. Read: "Ms. Abara paints inside a home. The people who live there pay her. Work can be inside a home and be paid." Show a small second card: a grown-up setting the table for supper at home. Read: "Setting the table is work too, and nobody pays for it. One is paid and one is not paid. Both are inside."

Puppet Pip says: "Work in a home is never paid." Answer: "Ms. Abara works inside a home and is paid. Setting the table is work and is not paid." (I)

## Picture 3: building outside

Mr. Lund builds a stone wall outside a pretend school. Read: "Mr. Lund works outside. The school pays him." Say: "Outside work can be paid and it can be not paid. Work is work."

## Words

work: something a person does that helps someone. inside: in a room or a home. outside: not in a room or a home. paid: gets money for the work. not paid: no money, and still work.
