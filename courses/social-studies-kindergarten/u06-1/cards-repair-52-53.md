# Repair cards 52 to 53

Used only for the hand-back minutes, never as an exit.

## For A and I (day 53 opening)

1. A pretend cook at home beside a pretend cook in a cafe, both labeled work. Read: "Both cook food for people. Both are work. One is paid and one is not."
2. A grid of four boxes, labeled paid or not paid across the top and inside or outside down the side. Three work cards go in the boxes (read aloud, new each time): a person teaching a song at a pretend school (paid, inside), a person pulling weeds in their own yard (not paid, outside), a person who sews a button for a friend (not paid, inside). The fourth box is for a child to suggest. Say: "Paid or not, if it helps someone, it is work."
3. Three new work cards sorted by "does it help someone?".

## For B (day 54 opening, 4 minutes)

A tool-only label card (a bus picture with the word bus) beside the same worker's three icon pictures. A new worker is said aloud by the teacher: a pretend sail maker who knows the wind, can stitch strong seams and uses a needle and thread. Children place the head, hand and tool icons beside the label. Retry with another new worker, said aloud.

## Teacher key

The cook and the sail maker each have know, do and use. A tool-only label never describes the whole job.
