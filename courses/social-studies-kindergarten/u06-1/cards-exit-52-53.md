# Exit cards 52 to 53

New picture cards for the exits, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line and the headings. The teacher says "this is a pretend class" and reads every label and prompt aloud. Never used in teaching (rule 4). The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every child marks; the spoken ask is heard from the day's rotating group.

## Day 52

- s06-X52a: Invented for practice, in a pretend town; the teacher says it is pretend and tells aloud: The shop pays the window cleaner. Nobody pays the grown-up who sweeps. Three new cards: a grown-up sweeping the floor in a pretend home; a window cleaner at a pretend shop; a child on a swing. Mark: circle each picture where someone is doing work.
- s06-X52b: The same three cards. Spoken, rotating group: Is sweeping the floor work? Tell me why.

## Day 53

- s06-X53a: A fresh Jobs Book page, invented for practice, read aloud: In Pine Hollow, Ms. Okonkwo is the crossing helper at Birch Corner. She learned the safe times to cross and the rules for cars. She has a stop sign and walks beside the children as they cross. Paper: the same row of five pictures printed three times, marked with one, two and three dots: a head with a clock and a stopped car inside it; Ms. Okonkwo walking beside children; a stop sign; a hammer; a loaf of bread. Mark, row one: point to something she knows.
- s06-X53b: The same page and the same row of five pictures. Mark, row two: point to something she does.
- s06-X53c: The same page and the same row of five pictures. Mark, row three: point to something she uses.
- s06-X53d: The same page. Spoken, rotating group: Tell me one thing Ms. Okonkwo has to know to do her work.
- s06-X53e: Pretend Pine Hollow, invented for practice: a new two-picture strip, then and now, of digging a flower bed. In the first picture a person digs with a hand shovel; in the second a person digs with a small digging machine. Mark: circle what changed.

## Key (teacher)

- s06-X52a: Circle the sweeping card and the window cleaner card, not the swing. Work counts whether or not anyone pays. Look for: circles the sweeping and the window cleaning; leaves the swing; paid or not paid does not decide.
- s06-X52b: Yes. It makes the floor clean for people, whether or not anyone pays. Look for: says yes; gives a reason about helping or cleaning, not about pay.
- s06-X53a: The head picture: the safe times to cross and the rules for cars. Look for: points to the head picture.
- s06-X53b: Ms. Okonkwo walking beside the children. Look for: points to the walking picture.
- s06-X53c: The stop sign. Look for: points to the stop sign.
- s06-X53d: The safe times to cross, or the rules for cars. Look for: names something kept in mind, not a thing she holds or a job name.
- s06-X53e: The tool: a hand shovel then, a machine now. The flower bed and the digging stayed. Look for: circles the tool in each picture; not the flower bed; the digging stayed.
