# Jobs Book: Davi, Mina and two school helpers (day 53)

Large pages read aloud, invented workers in a made-up town called Pine Hollow. The teacher says so. Three icon cards sit beside each picture: a head (know), a hand (do, a skill) and a tool (use). Words with pictures: skill, tool, know. These are story facts, not claims about every worker.

## Davi, who drives a bus

Picture: a bus on a road with riders in the windows. Read: "Davi drives a bus in Pine Hollow. Davi knows the way and when to stop. Davi can steer and stop gently. That is a skill. Davi uses the bus and a mirror." The teacher models each icon: head by the way and when to stop, hand by steer and stop gently, tool by the bus and the mirror. "A picture shows what Davi does; the book tells what he knows."

Puppet Pip says: "The driver needs a bus and that is all." Answer: "The bus is his tool. Davi also knows the way and can stop gently." (B)

## Mina, who grows beans

Picture: a pretend farm with rows of bean plants. Read: "Mina grows beans on a small farm. Mina knows when to plant and when plants need water. Mina can dig a row and pull weeds. She uses a hoe and a watering can." Pairs place the three icons beside her picture and say each aloud with "I think ..., because ...".

## Two school helpers

Two drawings, shown, not modeled. The school secretary: knows who is where and which child to call; can answer the phone kindly; uses a desk phone and a list. The music teacher: knows the songs and the beat; can play a drum and lead singing; uses a drum and a song chart. A real helper's photograph or visit is optional, with their agreement, and never needed.

## Teacher key

Davi: know the way and when to stop; do steer and stop gently; use the bus and the mirror. Mina: know when to plant and water; do dig a row and pull weeds; use a hoe and a watering can. A tool alone never answers what a worker needs.
