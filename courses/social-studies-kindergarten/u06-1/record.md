# Unit 6 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left, and beside it the child's rotating day. Six children are heard each day for the spoken part of the exit (days 52 to 57); a child outside the day's six is recorded not yet heard for the spoken part. Each E column is one mark on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The picture mark every child makes is kept apart and never scored as an explanation. Columns for section 6.1: E52 mark, E52 heard, E53 know, E53 do, E53 use, E53 heard. Section 6.2 adds E54, E55 and E56, and section 6.3 adds E57, E58 and E59. Days 58 to 60 hear a roster of ten children by turns, each with a first try, one spoken next step and a revised try; only the revised try is scored on day 60. Day 60 has no exit; its items are the evidence. The End columns are the observation task on day 60, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child does it independently, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not shown even with prompts, and blank when not yet observed. Never write 0 for a part that was not heard. Record by part (Work, Choice, Exchange), never by child, when speaking aloud. Add a heard column naming who was heard on each day. Put the date in any cell tried again on day 61. A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
