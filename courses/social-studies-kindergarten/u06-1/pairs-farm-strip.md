# Farm strip (day 53, then and now)

Two drawings of one pretend farm, side by side on a strip, one set a pair. Left: a person plants bean seeds by hand in a row and covers them. Right: a person drives a small planting machine along the same row. The same field, the same field edge and the same plants in both. Invented, not a claim about when or how any real farm changed; the teacher says so.

Ask each pair for one change and one thing that stayed. The change is the tool. What stayed is the work of growing food and the plants.

If many missed the day 52 cafe-cook repair, the strip is dropped and the unit 4 washing chart is read inside this part instead.

The key is in key-pairs-farm-strip.md (teacher only).
