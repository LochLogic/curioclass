# Day 52: Work helps someone

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the Work Around Us pictures. Tables get cards and hoops. Fold Pip. Choose today's six. Ask nothing about any child's home.

### The day

#### 2 min. Hook and question

Day 51 ended unit 5. Set paper out; picture 1 hangs in view. Read one Our Jobs Chart row (review, day 12, unit 2): "Hold the door until everyone is through." Ask: "What work was that? Who did it help?" Post "Why do people work?" on the wonder wall; write three wonders.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Say: "Curio Class made these in 2026. The people are made up." Read pictures 1 to 3 (cooking, painting, building). Words: work, inside, outside, paid, not paid. Pip: "Cooking at home is not work. Nobody pays." Answer: it helps people eat, so it is work (error A). Pip: "Work in a home is never paid." Answer: Ms. Abara is paid; setting the table is not; both are work (error I).

#### 10 min. Supported work

Tables of five. 4 minutes, 40 seconds a child: place a card in a hoop; say what the worker does and who it helps. 4 minutes: draw a work done at school; hear the six, 40 seconds each. 2 minutes: drawings beside Our Jobs Chart.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say: "This is a pretend town." Tell: "The shop pays the window cleaner. Nobody pays the grown-up who sweeps." All circle each card showing work ([[s06-X52a]]). Six children, 15 seconds each: "Is sweeping the floor work? Tell me why." ([[s06-X52b]]) Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only the window cleaner circled is error A.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a cook, a painter and a wall. No hoops: use two paper loops.

**Skip.** Pip, the table sort and the class chart.

- **5 min. Pictures.** Tell: a made-up cook at home, a painter inside a home, a builder outside. Say they are pretend.
  **Say.** "Is cooking at home work if nobody pays?" Expect: yes, it helps people eat.
- **5 min. Sort.** Set four drawn cards in two piles, home and not home.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one work done at school and says who it helps; write the words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X52a]] [[s06-X52b]] Tell: "The shop pays the window cleaner. Nobody pays the grown-up who sweeps." Draw a sweeper, a window cleaner and a swing; your child circles the work.
  **Say.** "Is sweeping the floor work? Why?" Expect: yes, it makes the floor clean for people.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pictures. Longer: a second card.

### Words and supports

Frame: "___ works. It helps ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many gave error A, day 53 opens with a cafe cook beside a home cook; a grid answers error I.
