# Repair cards 57 to 59

Used only in the hand-back minutes, never as an exit.

## E (day 58 opening, 3 minutes)

A new exchange by pairs, said aloud with no picture of a store item: a pretend girl hands tokens to a boy who gives her a paper cat; ask "Who pays? Who is paid?" Children say buyer and seller. Retry with another new exchange.

## D and K (day 59 opening, 3 minutes, D first)

Two sentences side by side, read aloud: "I like the star." "There are only five tokens and two items cost three each, so I choose the star because ..." Children say which sentence names the limit. Then K: two children hold up a paper cat and a paper owl; one says yes and one says no; the swap stops. Retry with another new swap.

## Teacher key

The buyer pays; the seller is paid. A good reason names the limit and a reason. A trade needs both to say yes; if one says no, the swap does not happen.
