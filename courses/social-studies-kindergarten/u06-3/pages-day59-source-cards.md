# Day 59 source cards and the claim frame

One card for each child: three small pictures in a row, one each from Work Around Us (Mr. Tran at the stove and Mr. Lund building), the Jobs Book (Davi at the wheel and Mina at her stall) and Our Store Record (the strokes). The child picks one picture and draws it beside "Why do people work?".

## Claim frame, said aloud

"I think people work ___, because the ___ shows ___."

Teacher model: "I think people work to help others, because the Jobs Book shows Davi driving riders." Helping, caring and earning each support a claim, and a claim with a source detail is better than a claim alone.

Puppet Pip says: "People work because they work." (H) Answer: "What does a picture show? People work to help others, because Work Around Us shows Mr. Lund building a wall for a school."

## Teacher key

Any claim about helping, caring, earning or making something people need, with a detail from one card, meets. A claim that only repeats the question does not.
