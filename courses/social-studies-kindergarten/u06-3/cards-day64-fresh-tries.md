# Day 64 fresh tries

One fresh card for each part the record of days 62 and 63 shows not yet seen. Each is a new case printed "invented for practice". The teacher says "This is a pretend class" and reads each prompt aloud once. Parts unheard stay not yet seen in the year-end record. Right only after help is shown with help.

- sc2-X64a (order): Juno folds the paper; sets the paper boat on the water; watches it float. Three shuffled cards, dots one to three.
- sc2-X64b (changed): our drawings of a lace-up boot long ago and a sneaker with a strap today, examples and not every shoe. Spoken: Tell one thing that changed.
- sc2-X64c (key): a pond garden map, a sun picture at the top edge, key: diamond bench, ring birdbath, cross gate; along the middle from the left: ring, diamond, cross. Mark: token on the bench. Spoken: Where is the bench compared with the birdbath and the gate?
- sc2-X64d (I think, because): a real kitchen sponge. Spoken: What is it for? I think it is for ___, because it ___.
- sc2-X64e (needs and wants): a loaf of bread, a bright paper hat, a warm bed. Mark: token on each need.
- sc2-X64f (goods and services): a bar of soap, and a plumber fixing a sink. Mark: token on the service.
- sc2-X64g (money): a mender paid by a shoemaker; customers paying at the shoemaker's counter. Spoken: Where does the shoemaker get the money?
- sc2-X64h (holiday): a calendar card, Memorial Day, a folded flag drawn on it. Spoken: Who does Memorial Day remember? (Source 44C: people in the armed forces who died while serving.)
