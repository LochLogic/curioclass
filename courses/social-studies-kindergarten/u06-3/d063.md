# Day 63: Needs, wants and money

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 62's assessment: the record for items 1 to 9 stays as marked. Keep the funnel out of sight. Pick today's six from the children not yet heard on items 10 to 17. Keep day 62's record sheet beside you. Key: key-cards-62-63.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions

Say: "This is a pretend class. We show what we know with new cards. Mark your own paper. Pointing counts. Passing is fine." Give no example; say only "Show me."

#### 9 min. Picture rows

All children mark; read each prompt once. Needs and wants: token on each need ([[sc2-E11]]). Goods and services: token on each service ([[sc2-E13]]). Two wants: point to the one you would choose ([[sc2-E14]]). Collect the papers.

#### 12 min. Hearing

At a table, the six, 2 minutes each: show the funnel, "What is it for? I think it is for ___, because it ___" ([[sc2-E10]]); "Why is the top a want?" ([[sc2-E12]]); "Why did you pick that one?" ([[sc2-E14b]]); "Where does the sign painter's shop get money?" ([[sc2-E15]]); "Which card is the trade?" ([[sc2-E16]]); "How do you know it is a trade and not a buy?" ([[sc2-E16b]]); "Pick one day. Who or what does it remember?" ([[sc2-E17]]). The other children finish the student page, then draw one thing they need and one they want. A child may pass. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Close

Say no result aloud. Unheard parts stay not yet seen. Tomorrow reads days 62 and 63 by part.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No funnel: use a cup with a hole in a lid. No cards: draw a coat, a toy and a loaf.

**Skip.** The six and the record.

- **8 min. Assessment.** Draw a coat and a toy.
  **Say.** "Which is a need? Why?" Expect: the coat keeps a body warm.
- **7 min. Goods and services.** Draw a bucket and a person mending a fence.
  **Say.** "Which is a thing and which is a job?" Expect: bucket a thing, mending a job.
- **5 min. Buy or trade.** Draw coins and two swapped papers.
  **Say.** "What is different?" Expect: money paid; things swapped.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 10, 11 and 15. Longer: the rest of the six.

### Words and supports

Need, want, goods, services, money, trade, holiday. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 reads days 62 and 63 by part: Time, Maps, Money, Reasons. It re-teaches each and hears fresh tries.
