# Day 61 fresh tries

One fresh card for each part the record shows not yet seen. Each is a new case printed "invented for practice". The teacher reads it aloud and the child marks or speaks. The scene for error L is read aloud by an adult and printed "invented for practice"; never infer effort from possessions. Parts unheard stay not yet seen in the year-end record. Right only after help is shown with help.

- s06-X61a (whole): Invented for practice: a new card of a grown-up washing the dishes at home after supper, nobody paying. Spoken: Is washing the dishes work? Why?
- s06-X61b (knowledge): A new caption read aloud: The librarian knows where each book belongs and how to help a child find one. She can read stories aloud. She uses a cart to carry books. Spoken: What does the librarian know?
- s06-X61c (choose): Two new cards, read aloud: a rain jacket in a rainstorm, and a sparkly rain sticker. Mark: put a token on the one a person needs.
- s06-X61d (whole): A pretend class has room on its table for only one puzzle: a whale puzzle or a train puzzle. Spoken: Why must the class choose?
- s06-X61e (ask-answer): A new scene card, invented for practice: a girl gives tokens to a shop worker who hands her a toy car. Mark: put a dot on the seller.
- s06-X61f (whole): Two new cards, invented for practice: a person holding an umbrella bought at a shop, and a person painting a fence for pay. Mark: put a dot on the service.
- s06-X61g (whole): The same two cards, and a picture of the grown-up who paid the fence painter, at their own paid job. Spoken: The fence painter was paid. Where did the person who paid get that money?
- s06-X61h (whole): A new Jobs Book page, invented for practice, read aloud: Mr. Alvarez fixes the town clock so people know the time. A puppet says: People work because that is what people do. Spoken: What could we say instead, using the page?
- s06-X61i (whole): A new card, invented for practice, read aloud: a pretend electrician is paid to fix the lights inside a home. A puppet says: Work at home is never paid. Spoken: Look at the card. Is the puppet right? Why?
- s06-X61j (choose): Two new cards: rain boots on a rainy day, and a flashlight on a dark night. A puppet says: Everybody needs the same things. Spoken: Why does a person need different things on these two days?
- s06-X61k (ask-answer): The teacher holds a paper fan and the child holds a paper frog card. Spoken: Ask me to swap my fan for your frog. (s06-X61k) I say no. What do you say or do? (s06-X61m)
- s06-X61l (whole): Invented for practice, read aloud: Mr. Bello lifts heavy boxes at a pretend market all day, and he is paid a small amount. A puppet says: He did not work hard, because he has little money. Spoken: Is the puppet right? What shows how hard Mr. Bello worked?

## Key (teacher)

- s06-X61a: Yes. It cleans the dishes for people to use, whether or not it is paid. Look for: says yes; a reason about helping or cleaning, not pay.
- s06-X61b: Where each book belongs and how to help a child find one. Look for: names knowledge, not the cart or the job name.
- s06-X61c: The rain jacket. Look for: token on the rain jacket; not the sticker.
- s06-X61d: There is room for only one puzzle. Look for: names the limit, room for one.
- s06-X61e: The shop worker, who is paid. Look for: dot on the shop worker; not the girl.
- s06-X61f: The fence painting. Look for: dot on the fence painting; not the umbrella.
- s06-X61g: From the paid work the person does, as the picture shows; money also comes in other ways. Look for: says the money comes from work.
- s06-X61h: A claim with the page's detail: People work to help others, because the page shows Mr. Alvarez fixing the clock for the town. Look for: a claim; a detail from the page; a reason joined by because.
- s06-X61i: No. The electrician is paid for work inside a home; some work at home is paid and some is not. Look for: says no; names the paid work inside the home.
- s06-X61j: On the rainy day boots keep feet dry; on the dark night a flashlight lets a person see the way. What a person needs depends on the day and the place. Look for: names both needs; says the need depends on the weather or place.
- s06-X61k: Asks politely. Look for: asks; does not take the fan.
- s06-X61m: Accepts the no, and the swap does not happen. Look for: accepts a no; no trade without a yes.
- s06-X61l: No. How much money someone has does not tell how hard they worked; the picture shows him lifting boxes all day. Look for: says no; points to the work shown, not the money.
