# Day 60 assessment cards (reserved)

Withheld until day 60. Nothing here is shown in teaching. Day 58 reads item 1's two scenes aloud once before the first store turn, as examples and never as models. Cards are printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge, except the photograph, which is a federal print kept apart. The marking scale is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The teacher reads each prompt aloud once. Pointing counts.

Photograph check before day 60: the print (Library of Congress reproduction number fsa.8b23599, Russell Lee, November 1939) is checked by the teacher against the catalog record; the keys below name only making pie crusts, the worktable and the pie tins. If the print is not cleared, use another public-domain federal worker photograph or a drawing said to be a drawing.

## s06-E1 (whole)

Two withheld Work Around Us scenes, invented for practice, read aloud on day 58 and again on the child's roster day: scene one, a grown-up plants seeds in a window box at their own home and nobody pays them; scene two, a gardener tends a public garden in a pretend town and is paid by the town. Spoken: Tell me what work each person does.

## s06-E2 (whole)

The same two scenes. Spoken: How is this work like work you have seen at school?

## s06-E3 (whole)

The same two scenes. Spoken: Do both count as work? Why?

## s06-E4 (whole)

The withheld photograph, read aloud with its maker, place and date: Making pie crusts, Bakery, San Angelo, Texas, by Russell Lee, November 1939, a Library of Congress print from the Farm Security Administration and Office of War Information collection. The teacher also reads a new caption: These bakers make pies to sell. No child handles baking equipment or food. Spoken: What work is happening in the picture? Point to a clue.

## s06-E5 (knowledge)

The same photograph and caption. Spoken: What does a baker need to know to make pies?

## s06-E6 (skills)

The same photograph and caption. Spoken: What can a baker do because they learned it?

## s06-E7 (whole)

The same photograph and caption. Spoken: Point to something the bakers use.

## s06-E8 (choose)

New cards, read aloud with their contexts: mittens for freezing weather; a bowl of soup for a hungry pretend girl after a long walk; a decorative pinwheel; a costume crown. Mark: put each card that meets a basic need on the need mat.

## s06-E9 (choose)

The same four cards. Spoken: Tell me why one of the needs is a need.

## s06-E10 (choose)

The same four cards. Spoken: Tell me why one of the others is a want.

## s06-E11 (whole)

Two unseen picture choices: a paper maze and a finger puppet. The teacher says there is enough paper to make only one. Spoken: Why must you choose?

## s06-E12 (choose)

The same two cards; the child points to the one to make. Spoken: Why did you pick that one?

## s06-E13 (ask-answer)

Three new scenes, invented for practice, read aloud: scene one, a person pays a shopkeeper for a blank notebook; scene two, the shopkeeper accepts the payment and hands the notebook over; scene three, one child offers a marble for another child's toy dinosaur and the other child has not answered. Spoken: Ask me one question about one scene.

## s06-E14 (ask-answer)

The same three scenes. Spoken: What happens if the other child says no to the swap?

## s06-E15 (ask-answer)

The same scenes one and two. Spoken: In the notebook scene, who is the buyer and who is the seller?

## s06-E16 (whole)

Two new cards, invented for practice: a wooden spoon for sale at a pretend shop, and a bicycle being repaired by a repair worker who is paid by the owner. Mark: put a token on the purchase that is a good.

## s06-E17 (whole)

The same two cards. Mark: put a token on the purchase that is a service.

## s06-E18 (whole)

The same two cards, and a picture of an adult at paid work. Spoken: The owner wants to pay for the repair. Where does the owner get that money?

## s06-E19 (whole)

The wonder wall and the source cards (Work Around Us, Jobs Book, Our Store Record) are before the child. Spoken: Why do people work? Say I think ___, because ___. The teacher scribes the answer beside the card the child picks.

## s06-E20 (whole)

The same cards and the same claim. Spoken: Which card helped you decide?

## s06-E20b (whole)

The same cards and the same claim, the card named. Spoken: What does that card show?
