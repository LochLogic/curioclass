# Price tags, tokens and envelopes (days 57 to 61)

## Price tags

Five tags, one for each price. Dots and a numeral: one dot and 1, two dots and 2, up to five dots and 5. A group of dots is counted aloud by touching. Children lay a tag by counting tokens beside the item.

## Tokens and envelopes

150 paper tokens, each worth one, the same plain circle on both sides with no printed coins or bills and no number. Each child has five in an envelope with their name. The teacher holds a bank envelope. At the end of day 58 every token goes back to the bank except one saved in the envelope.

## Rules read aloud before the store

A child may say no. Return everything. Saving is fine. A trade needs both children to say yes.

## Teacher key

Counting by touching: five dots, five touches, five tokens. Any token count above five is not possible; a price of five uses the whole envelope.
