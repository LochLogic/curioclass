# Day 57: Set up our class store

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the large map. Find the day 26 map page. Fold Pip.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Day 56's exit, 3 minutes: a sweater card beside someone sewing a button on it: which is the good, which the service? Then a chain: Ms. Reed sews buttons, earns tokens, pays Davi; who worked? If many missed it (error F or error G), four minutes. Then review the unit 3 map and key: "Find a symbol. What does this picture stand for?" If it is gone, redraw a key.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

[[s06-K57-1]]

Say "pretend store." Review: buy and sell (day 56). New: token (paper, worth one) and price (what the buyer gives). Sell a drawing for two tokens; touch each as you count. **Ask.** What did the buyer give? Expect: tokens. **Ask.** What did the seller get? Expect: tokens. **Ask.** What did the buyer get? Expect: the drawing. Swap roles. Pip pays and says "I am the seller." Answer: Pip buys (error E).

#### 7 min. Supported work

[[s06-P57-1]] [[s06-P57-2]]

Six tables, one station each. 3 minutes: count the tokens beside the item; lay the price tag with that many dots. The heart table reviews trade: both say yes. 4 minutes: place your symbol on the map and key; one child says what it stands for. Visit each table, 30 seconds.

#### 3 min. Exit

All mark: circle the service ([[s06-X57a]]); dot the buyer ([[s06-X57b]]). Hear six, 20 seconds each: "What does the buyer get when her shoes are cleaned?" ([[s06-X57c]]) The bucket is error F; the man or "the tokens" is error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: tear ten paper circles. No map: draw a box with two shelves.

**Skip.** Pip, the tables and the large map.

- **5 min. Shop.** You sell a drawing for two paper circles; your child buys it. Swap.
  **Say.** "What did the buyer give? What did the seller get?" Expect: circles; circles.
- **5 min. Map.** Draw a shelf as a star in a box. Your child draws a small picture beside it in a key.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X57a]] [[s06-X57b]] [[s06-X57c]] Show a bucket, a shoe cleaner, a plant pot; tell the girl who pays the man; ask each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tags only. Longer: a third price.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I buy ___. I give ___ tokens."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error E or error F, day 58 opens with who pays and who is paid, in pairs.
