# Day 60: The unit 6 observation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the photograph print against its Library of Congress record (fsa.8b23599). If it is not cleared, use another federal worker photograph or a drawing said to be a drawing. No finished chart on the wall. Mark today's ten. Teacher key: key-day60-assessment.md, kept apart.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions

Say: "We show what we know with new cards. Mark your own paper. Pointing counts. Passing is fine." Offer no example or reason; say only "Tell me more" or "Show me."

#### 15 min. Fresh tasks

Five blocks of three minutes. In each, read the prompts once (about 40 seconds), all children mark, and hear two of the ten, about 70 seconds each: this block's short reason, then the roster item the child holds (a first try, one spoken next step, a revised try; only the revised try counts). A child may pass. Add the revised tries from day 58 and from day 59.

- **Photograph.** Read: Making pie crusts, Bakery, San Angelo, Texas, by Russell Lee, November 1939, a Library of Congress print. Say a new caption: "These bakers make pies to sell." All dot a clue to the work ([[s06-E4]]) and something the bakers use ([[s06-E7]]). Hear: "What does a baker need to know?" ([[s06-E5]]) "What can a baker do because they learned it?" ([[s06-E6]])
- **Needs and wants.** Read the four cards. All dot each need ([[s06-E8]]). Hear: why one is a need ([[s06-E9]]); why one of the others is a want ([[s06-E10]]).
- **Choosing.** Two cards, paper for one. All point to the one to make. Hear: "Why must you choose?" ([[s06-E11]]) "Why did you pick that one?" ([[s06-E12]])
- **Buying and trading.** Read the three scenes. All dot the buyer ([[s06-E15]]). Hear: "Ask me one question" ([[s06-E13]]); "What happens if the other child says no?" ([[s06-E14]])
- **Goods and services.** Two cards. All put a token on the good ([[s06-E16]]) and on the service ([[s06-E17]]). Hear: where the owner gets the money ([[s06-E18]]).

Item 1, the day 58 scenes again: "What work does each do?" ([[s06-E1]]), "How is this work like work at school?" ([[s06-E2]]), "Do both count as work? Why?" ([[s06-E3]]); or item 7, with the wonder wall and source cards: "Why do people work? I think ___, because ___" ([[s06-E19]]), scribed; "Which card helped you decide? What does it show?" ([[s06-E20]] [[s06-E20b]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Judge reasoning, not speech.

#### 3 min. Close

Collect the papers. Read the wonder wall; say no result aloud.

#### 10 min. Long route only

A child not heard on 58 or 59 is heard now, about 60 seconds each, on item 1 or item 7, with one next step and a revised try.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a mitten, a bowl of soup, a crown and a pinwheel. No photograph: draw two bakers.

**Skip.** The five blocks and the hearing table.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Show your drawings and add no reasons.
  **Say.** "Which of these do people need to stay well?" Expect: the mittens and the soup.
  **Say.** "Who pays, and who is paid, when she buys a notebook?" Expect: the buyer pays; the shopkeeper is paid.
  **Say.** "Why do people work? Say: I think ..., because ..." Expect: a claim and a reason.
- **10 min. Respond.** Pick the one part that was hard; show it again and ask once more.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: needs and wants, goods and services, the claim. Longer: the long route.

### Words and supports

Pass, point, draw or an aid is an answer. Frame: "I think ___, because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 61 reads day 60's items by part: Work (items 1, 2, 7; errors A, I, B and H); Choice (items 3 and 4; errors C, J and D); Exchange (items 5 and 6; errors E, K, F and G). It re-teaches each, then hears fresh tries.
