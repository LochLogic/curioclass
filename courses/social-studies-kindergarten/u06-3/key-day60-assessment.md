# Teacher key, day 60 assessment pages

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child page (pages-day60-assessment.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- s06-E1: One plants seeds; the other keeps the plants of the town garden growing. Look for: describes both actions; uses the read-aloud.
- s06-E2: A classroom action named, such as watering our class plant or setting out paper. Look for: links to a classroom action the child has seen; no question about home.
- s06-E3: Yes. Both help plants grow; work counts whether or not it is paid. Look for: says yes for both; the reason is the helping or making, not the pay.
- s06-E4: Bakers are making pie crusts; the child points to something that shows it, such as the pie tins or the worktable. Look for: says the work, making pie crusts or pies; points to a clue in the picture.
- s06-E5: Something kept in mind, such as how to make the dough or how a crust should look. Any defensible answer. Look for: names a thing known, not a tool; recorded apart from the skill.
- s06-E6: Shape or roll the dough into crusts, or fill and bake a pie. Look for: names something done by hand, learned by practice; recorded apart from the knowledge.
- s06-E7: A tool in the picture, such as the pie tins. Look for: points to a tool the picture shows; the sample is checked against the print.
- s06-E8: The mittens and the soup on the need mat; the pinwheel and the crown are wants. Look for: mittens and soup on the mat; pinwheel and crown left off.
- s06-E9: The mittens keep hands warm in freezing weather; the soup helps a hungry girl stay well. Accept a context-based reason. Look for: names the need and why it helps a person stay well; uses the card's context.
- s06-E10: The crown or the pinwheel is fun but not needed to stay well. Look for: says it is fun or liked and not needed to stay well.
- s06-E11: There is not enough paper to make both. Look for: names the limit, not enough paper for both.
- s06-E12: A reason about the thing chosen; either choice can meet, such as: I can tell a story with the puppet. Look for: gives a reason about the picked thing; either choice meets.
- s06-E13: A relevant question, such as: What did the person pay? or Will the other child say yes? Look for: asks a question the scene can answer; asking and answering are recorded apart.
- s06-E14: Then there is no trade; each child keeps their own thing. Look for: says no ends the swap; each keeps their own.
- s06-E15: The person who pays is the buyer; the shopkeeper is the seller. Look for: buyer is the one who pays; seller is the one who is paid and hands over the notebook.
- s06-E16: The wooden spoon. Look for: token on the wooden spoon; not on the repair.
- s06-E17: The bicycle repair, which is work someone does for the owner. Look for: token on the repair; not on the wooden spoon.
- s06-E18: From work: the adult earns money by working and uses some to pay. Money also comes in other ways. Look for: says the money comes from work; uses earn or paid.
- s06-E19: A claim with a reason, for example: People work to help others, because Work Around Us shows a cook making a meal. A claim about caring or earning also meets. Look for: a claim; a reason joined by because.
- s06-E20: Names a source card. Look for: names a source card.
- s06-E20b: One detail on the card that joins the claim. Look for: names a detail it shows.
