# Cards for course check 2 (days 62 and 63)

Withheld until days 62 and 63. Nothing here is shown in teaching. Cards are printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge, except the two photographs, which are federal prints kept apart and named with maker, place and year. The teacher says aloud: "This is a pretend class." The marking scale is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Pointing counts. The teacher reads each prompt once.

Photograph check before day 62: check each print against its Library of Congress record (Schoolchildren on circular swing, Russell Lee, San Augustine, Texas, 1938; Painting class in school, Ben Shahn, Westmoreland Homesteads, Pennsylvania, 1937). If a print is not cleared, use a drawing said to be a drawing. The keys name only what the captions say.

## Day 62

- **Room 12 end of day (sc2-E1, sc2-E2).** Four cards, same size: crayons in a box, children on the rug for the goodbye song, children in a line at the door, children waving goodbye. Read aloud first, in order.
- **Tavi the turtle (sc2-E3).** Four cards, one actor each: Tavi packs a bag, climbs a hill, eats a snack at the top, walks home.
- **Then and now (sc2-E4 to sc2-E7).** Two photographs (swing, painting class) and two of our drawings, said to be drawings: a swing set with seats in a row at a school today, children painting at a school today. Two boxes: long ago, today.
- **Pretend park map (sc2-E8, sc2-E9).** Seen from above, a sun picture at the top edge, a boxed key of four small pictures: oval fountain, square picnic table, star bike rack, arch boat dock. Down the left edge from the top: oval, square, star. The arch is at the right edge.

## Day 63

- **Funnel (sc2-E10).** A real plastic funnel.
- **Needs and wants (sc2-E11, sc2-E12).** Three cards in a long-ago style, examples and not every farm: a barrel of drinking water, a wool blanket in a cold winter, a spinning top.
- **Goods and services (sc2-E13).** Four cards: a wooden bucket on a farm long ago; a neighbor mending a fence for a farm family long ago; Room 12's job of wiping the snack table; Room 12's basket of class books.
- **Two wants (sc2-E14).** A clay turtle and a clay house; clay for only one.
- **Money (sc2-E15).** A sign painter painting a shop's sign; customers paying at the shop counter.
- **Buy and trade (sc2-E16).** Card one: tokens to a stall worker, who hands over a jar. Card two: a paper fish for a paper shell, both smiling.
- **Holidays (sc2-E17).** Two calendar cards, Veterans Day and Martin Luther King Jr. Day, names read aloud. Veterans Day honors all who served in the armed forces (source 42C); Dr. King worked with others, peacefully, to change laws that kept Black and white people apart (source 43B).
