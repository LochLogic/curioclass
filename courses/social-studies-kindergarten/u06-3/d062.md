# Day 62: Time and the pretend park

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 61 sent the class here; pick up its Tomorrow. Set the check 2 record sheet: one row per child, columns for items 1 to 9, one marking scale. Pick today's six. Check the two photograph prints against their records, else use drawings said to be drawings.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions

Say: "This is a pretend class. We show what we know with new cards. Mark your own paper. Pointing counts. Passing is fine." Give no example; say only "Show me."

#### 9 min. Picture rows

All children mark; read each prompt once. Room 12 end of day: number the cards one to four ([[sc2-E1]]); token on what comes last ([[sc2-E2]]). Tavi: number the four cards ([[sc2-E3]]). Then and now: token on the long-ago photograph and on the one that shows today ([[sc2-E4]]). Park map: token on the fountain ([[sc2-E8]]). Collect the papers.

#### 12 min. Hearing

At a table, the six, 2 minutes each: "Tell what comes first, next and last" ([[sc2-E1]]; [[sc2-E3]]); "What changed? What stayed the same?" ([[sc2-E5]]; [[sc2-E6]]); "How does a photograph help?" ([[sc2-E7]]); "Where is the fountain compared with the table? What does the star mean?" ([[sc2-E8]]; [[sc2-E9]]). A child may pass. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Close

Say no result aloud. Unheard children stay not yet seen. Tomorrow is part two.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw four steps of a day. No tokens: use buttons.

**Skip.** The six and the record.

- **8 min. Assessment.** Draw a bag, a hill and a snack.
  **Say.** "What comes first, next and last?" Expect: any order that makes a story, named with first, next and last.
- **7 min. Then and now.** Draw an old swing and a new one.
  **Say.** "What changed? What stayed?" Expect: the look changed; it is still a swing.
- **5 min. A key.** Draw a map with a star and a circle.
  **Say.** "What does the star mean?" Expect: the key says.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1 and 8 first. Longer: the rest of the six.

### Words and supports

First, next, last, long ago, today, changed, stayed, photograph, key. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 is part two of the check, on needs, wants and money. Day 64 reads days 62 and 63 by part and hears fresh tries.

### Credit

Ours, all of it. The two photographs are federal prints, named with maker, place and year on the card.
