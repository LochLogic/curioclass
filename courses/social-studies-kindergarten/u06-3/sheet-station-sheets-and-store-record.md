# Station sheets and Our Store Record (days 57 to 61)

Six large sheets, one for each station, each with its shelf symbol at the top, the item picture, the price tag, and a row of empty boxes. The record keeper at each station makes one stroke in the next empty box for each sale. A station sheet is also where the shelf checker puts a checkmark when the shelf matches the map.

## Our Store Record

After day 58 the teacher joins the six sheets into one large page, Our Store Record. The strokes are real: they show how many sales the class made. The record is read once on day 59: "Point to something Our Store Record shows. How does it support your answer?" It stays up on days 60 and 61.

## Teacher key

Each stroke is one sale. A bigger count at a station means more sales there. The record shows what happened; it never ranks a child.
