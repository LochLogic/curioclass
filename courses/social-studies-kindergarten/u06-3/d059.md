# Day 59: Why do people work?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Take down the finished charts and supplied reasons. Blank the key. Hang Our Store Record.

### The day

#### 2 min. Saved tokens

A child with a saved token buys one item; the others watch.

#### 3 min. Reopen

Day 58's exit, 3 minutes: "I like the star" beside "Two items cost three each and I have five, so I choose the star because ..." Which gives the limit? If many missed it (error D), also a swap that stops at a no (error K). Then: "Point to something Our Store Record shows. How does it support your answer?" Review: Our Store Record (day 58).

#### 11 min. Hearing

[[s06-P59-1]]

Read the ferry card once to all: Ms. Idris steers the ferry across Otter River in pretend Pine Hollow; she learned where the river is shallow and which windy days are too strong to cross. The others pick one source card, draw "Why do people work?" and tell a partner "I think ___, because the ___ shows ___" (review, day 8). Helping, caring and earning each fit. Hear today's ten, about 60 seconds each: a first try, one spoken next step, a revised try. Five get the day 58 scenes again; five get "Why do people work?" Then all ten: "What does Ms. Idris need to know to steer the ferry?" ([[s06-X59a]]) Scribe the claim. Pip says: "People work because they work." Answer: name a card and what it shows (error H). A tool alone is error B.

#### 4 min. Close the wonder wall

Review: the wonder wall. Mark the wonders the unit answered.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a stove, a wall and a bus. No wall: a sheet of paper.

**Skip.** The stations, the ten and Pip.

- **5 min. Look.** Show three drawn workers. Your child picks one.
  **Say.** "Why do people work? Say: I think ..., because ..." Expect: a reason that names what the picture shows.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws that worker and you write their sentence beside it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X59a]] Tell the ferry story and ask the question.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one claim. Longer: a second source.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I think people work ___, because the ___ shows ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 60 is the observation task: new cards, your own paper, nothing on the wall to copy.
