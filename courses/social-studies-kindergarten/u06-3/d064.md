# Day 64: Fresh tries and the year

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the check 2 record sheet from days 62 and 63 into four groups by part: Time, Maps, Money, Reasons. Mark each child's pairs. Set the day 64 cards out of sight; keep the day 62 check cards and the day 63 check cards at hand.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Say: "On day 62 and day 63 we used pretend cards. Today we try again, a new way." Look at the day 62 items 1 to 9 and the day 63 items 10 to 17 on the record. Hand back no marks and name no result.

#### 8 min. Four groups, and pairs

Time: pairs tell first, next and last about a real class job, and what changed. Maps: pairs point to a key picture and find it on a drawing. Money: pairs sort a need from a want and a thing from a job. Reasons: pairs say "I think ___, because ___" about the sponge. Send each child to the group the record names; a child with all shown helps a pair.

#### 10 min. Fresh tries

One fresh card for each part still not yet seen, read aloud once; the child points or speaks, about 2 minutes each. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Right only after help is shown with help.

#### 5 min. Record

Fill the year-end record from the marks. Unheard parts stay not yet seen. Say no result aloud.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sponge: use a spoon. No cards: draw a boat, a boot and a loaf.

**Skip.** The fresh-try cards and the record.

- **8 min. In order.** Draw a boat being folded, set down and floating.
  **Say.** "What comes first, next and last?" Expect: fold, set down, float.
- **7 min. Money.** Draw a loaf, a hat and a bed.
  **Say.** "Which are needs?" Expect: the loaf and the bed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask the one that fits: [[sc2-X64a]] [[sc2-X64b]] [[sc2-X64c]] [[sc2-X64d]] [[sc2-X64e]] [[sc2-X64f]] [[sc2-X64g]] [[sc2-X64h]]. Hold up a sponge.
  **Say.** "I think it is for ___, because it ___." Expect: wiping, because it soaks up water.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four fresh tries. Longer: all eight.

### Words and supports

First, next, last, changed, key, need, want, goods, services, money, because. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

This is the last day of the course. Days 62 and 63 close here, with the year-end record.
