# Store map, shelf symbols and key (days 57 to 61)

A large floor-plan map of the class store on chart paper, seen from above: six tables around the room's edge and a door. Six movable paper shelf symbols, one for each station: a circle, a square, a triangle, a star, a diamond and a heart. A key box at the side where each symbol sits beside a small picture of what that shelf holds. The key is read aloud and a child says what each symbol stands for.

## Stations and symbols

1. Circle: stickers, one token.
2. Square: drawings, two tokens.
3. Triangle: the paper-folding service, folding a paper hat, three tokens.
4. Star: paper stars, four tokens.
5. Diamond: paper flowers, five tokens.
6. Heart: the trade table, no price; two children swap only if both say yes.

## How it is used

Day 57: each table puts its shelf symbol on the map and in the key. On day 59 the key is blank again; children draw one symbol and say what it stands for. The day 26 map and key from earlier in the year is shown once ("Find a symbol. What does this picture stand for?"); if that map is missing, redraw a key.

## Teacher key

Symbols stand for the shelves. The map shows where a shelf is; the key says what a symbol means.
