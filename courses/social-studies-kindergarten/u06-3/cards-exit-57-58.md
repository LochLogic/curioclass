# Exit cards 57 to 59

New picture cards for the exits, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line and the headings. The teacher says "this is a pretend class" and reads every label and prompt aloud. Never used in teaching (rule 4). The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every child marks; the spoken ask is heard from the day's rotating group.

## Day 57

- s06-X57a: Invented for practice, a pretend shop: three new cards: a bucket; a girl paying a shop worker who cleans her shoes; a plant pot. Mark: circle the service.
- s06-X57b: A new scene card, invented for practice: a girl gives tokens to a man who hands her a bucket. Mark: put a dot on the buyer.
- s06-X57c: The shoe-cleaning card: a girl gives tokens to a shop worker who cleans her shoes. Spoken, rotating group: What does the buyer get when her shoes are cleaned?

## Day 58

- s06-X58a: At the store door, a new card pair that is not on any shelf: a paper windmill and a bookmark, each three tokens, and a picture of five tokens. The teacher reads: you may choose one or save in your envelope; any choice is right and the choice is not scored. Mark: put a dot on what stops you buying both.
- s06-X58b: The same cards. Spoken, the day's ten: You can have only one. Why did you choose that one?

## Day 59

- s06-X59a: A new card, invented for practice, read aloud: Ms. Idris steers the ferry across Otter River in pretend Pine Hollow. She learned where the river is shallow and which windy days are too strong to cross. A child says: I think people work to earn money, because Ms. Idris is paid for each trip. Spoken, the day's ten: What does Ms. Idris need to know to steer the ferry?

## Key (teacher)

- s06-X57a: The shoe cleaning. Look for: circles the shoe cleaning; not the bucket.
- s06-X57b: The girl, who pays. Look for: dot on the girl, the one who pays; not the man.
- s06-X57c: Clean shoes, which is work done for her. Look for: says clean shoes or the work done; not the tokens.
- s06-X58a: The five tokens: two items cost more than five. Look for: dot on the five tokens; not on an item.
- s06-X58b: Names the limit (three tokens each, five tokens, two left) and a reason; saving counts. Look for: names the limit; gives a reason for the choice or for saving.
- s06-X59a: Something kept in mind, such as where the river is shallow or which windy days are too strong to cross, with steering as what she can do. Look for: names knowledge, not only the ferry; the work named separately from the tool.
