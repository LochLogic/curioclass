# Day 61: Practice what we need

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the children into three groups from day 60's items, privately.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Hand back the record by part; each child sees one cell, privately.

#### 8 min. Three groups, and pairs

Lead each group about 2 minutes in turn; the others use a move card with a partner. Fewer than four missing a part work in pairs. Review, days 52 to 59:

- **Work** (items 1, 2, 7; errors A, I, B and H). Mr. Tran cooks at home, unpaid; Ms. Abara paints inside a home, paid: both work. Davi: the bus is the tool; the way and when to stop is what he knows. Pip says "People work because they work": name a card and what it shows.
- **Choice** (items 3 and 4; errors C, J and D). The snow card, the sun card, a scarf in winter. "I like the kite" beside "there is room for one, so I choose the kite because ..."
- **Exchange** (items 5 and 6; errors E, K, F and G). Pip pays and says he is the seller: Pip buys. A swap stops at a no. The sweater is the good, the sewing is the service. Ms. Reed earns tokens and pays Davi.

#### 10 min. Fresh tries

Each child marks one fresh card for the part missed; you hear up to eight whose explanation is unheard, about 70 seconds each. Work: [[s06-X61a]] [[s06-X61b]] [[s06-X61h]] [[s06-X61i]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Right only after help is shown with help; unheard stays not yet seen. Close: "Next time we look back at maps and time."

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a stove and a bus. No tokens: tear paper circles.

**Skip.** The groups and pairs.

- **5 min. Practice.** Pick one part from day 60: work, choice or buying.
  **Say.** "Is cooking at home work?" Expect: yes, it helps people, paid or not.
- **5 min. Fresh try.** Show the part on a new drawing and ask once.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X61a]] [[s06-X61b]] [[s06-X61c]] [[s06-X61d]] [[s06-X61e]] [[s06-X61f]] [[s06-X61g]] [[s06-X61h]] [[s06-X61i]] [[s06-X61j]] [[s06-X61k]] [[s06-X61m]] [[s06-X61l]] Ask the one that fits; wait.
