# Day 58: Store day

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill the envelopes. Set the stations. Mark today's ten.

### The day

#### 3 min. Reopen

Repair card: a pretend girl hands tokens to a boy who gives her a paper cat. "Who pays? Who is paid?" Review: buyer and seller (day 57). If many missed it (error E or error F), all three minutes, in pairs.

#### 3 min. Organize

Rules: no is fine; return everything; saving is fine; a trade needs two yeses. Read two examples, never models, from a made-up town: a grown-up plants seeds in a box on a windowsill at home, nobody paying; a gardener tends the town garden, and the town pays. Say: "Not all money comes from work." Review: work counts, paid or not (day 52).

#### 12 min. Store work

[[s06-P58-1]] [[s06-P58-2]]

Six stations: buyers give the price and get the thing; sellers are paid. A child may save a token. Shelf checkers fix the map; record keepers stroke each sale. Go to today's ten, 70 seconds each: first try, one spoken next step, a revised try. Five get "What work does each person do?" about the scenes; five get "Why do people work? I think ..., because ..." (review, day 8).

#### 2 min. Exit

At the store door show the cards. Each chooses one, or saves; any choice is right. All dot what stops you buying both ([[s06-X58a]]). The ten say: "You can have only one. Why did you choose that one?" ([[s06-X58b]]) Tokens return but a saved one. A dot on an item, or "it is best", is error D.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: tear ten paper circles. No shelf things: draw four.

**Skip.** The six stations, the record and Pip.

- **5 min. Store.** Make a small shop of three drawn things with one to three circles each. Your child has five circles and buys one thing; you swap.
  **Say.** "What do you give? What do you get?" Expect: circles, the thing.
- **5 min. Save.** Your child may keep a circle in an envelope and say why.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X58a]] [[s06-X58b]] Show a drawn windmill and a drawn bookmark, three circles each, and five circles. Ask each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three stations. Longer: trades.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I choose ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error D, day 59 opens with "I like it" beside the limit, and a swap that stops at a no (error K).
