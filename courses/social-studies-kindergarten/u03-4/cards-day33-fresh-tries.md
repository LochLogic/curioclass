# Day 33 fresh tries

One fresh card for each part the record of days 31 and 32 shows not yet seen. Each is a new case printed "invented for practice". The teacher says "This is a pretend class" and reads each prompt aloud once; the child points or speaks. Parts unheard stay not yet seen. Right only after help is shown with help.

- sc1-X33a (position): a window ledge with a plant, a shell and a stone in a row, the page this way up. Spoken: Where is the shell? Where is the stone compared with the plant?
- sc1-X33b (next to): a picture map of Room 5, a sun picture at the top edge. Along the bottom wall from the page's left: coat hooks, door, cubby shelf. The art table is on the top wall. Spoken: What is next to the door? (sc1-X33b) Which place is far from the door? (sc1-X33g)
- sc1-X33c (map or globe): the Room 5 map beside the desk globe. Spoken: A friend far away wants to see where the cubbies are. Map or globe? Why?
- sc1-X33d (class rule): two cards of the Room 5 art table, paint jars open with a sleeve over them, and every lid on. The rule: put the lid on the paint. Mark: token on the card where the rule is followed. Spoken: Why does Room 5 have this rule?
- sc1-X33e (rule outside school): a park sign, a dog on a leash beside a duckling. Spoken: Why keep dogs on a leash in the park?
- sc1-X33f (I think, because): two cards for one picture by the door, a sunflower and a sailboat. Spoken: Say I think ___, because ___.

## Key (teacher)

See each row in items.csv. sc1-X33a: the shell is between the plant and the stone; the stone is right of the plant. sc1-X33b: the coat hooks or cubby shelf are next to the door; the art table is far. sc1-X33c: the map. sc1-X33d: lids on, so the paint does not spill or dry out. sc1-X33e: the dog stays close and does not chase. sc1-X33f: a pick with a reason from the card.
