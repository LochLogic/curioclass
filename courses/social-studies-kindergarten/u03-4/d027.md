# Day 27: Our school's street and town

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set four floor rings, country largest to street. Write the school's address on five strips. Cut card 27 into rows.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Mark day 26's s03-X26. Error H: trace the symbol to the key; error G: a new detail after because. Post "How does an address help locate a school?"

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Three minutes: set the State House strips in the rings as you read: 24, Beacon Street, Boston, Massachusetts, United States. Name each part; children say it back: street, town or city, state (a part of our country), country. Puppet: "Boston is the country" (error E); children fix it. Three: the Legislative Building, 416, Sid Snyder Avenue Southwest, Olympia, Washington, United States.

**Ask.** Which strip is the street? The town? The state? The country? Expect: Sid Snyder Avenue Southwest; Olympia; Washington; United States.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs, rings on the table. Four minutes: the school's strips; each says street, town and state, with our state's outline shown as you say its name. Three: the other building. Three: each draws our school on its street; no home. Hear the six, 45 seconds each: a place near school, what is next to it, its use.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Each child has a row of card 27 and a token. Read each strip: token on the street, then the state; mark s03-X27. The six name the number, town and country. A child still learning the state's name points to its outline: shown with help. Swapping parts is error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: use string. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The rings.

- **5 min. Rings.** Write a pretend address on scraps: 8, Plum Lane, Fox Town, Iowa, United States. Set them in rings, smallest first.
  **Say.** "Which part is the street? The town? The state? The country?" Expect: Plum Lane; Fox Town; Iowa; United States. Say them with your child.
- **5 min. Draw.** Draw a school on a street. Name the street.
  **Say.** "What is the street?" Expect: the name beside the school.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X27]] Write five scraps for 30, Lark Way, Dale Town, Maine, United States, mixed up. Your child puts a finger on three.
  **Say.** "Which is the street? The town? The state?" Expect: Lark Way; Dale Town; Maine.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one building. Longer: a third.

### Words and supports

Address, street, town, state, country. Frames: "The street is ___." "The state is ___."
