# Exit cards 27, 28 and 30

New cards, printed "invented for practice, not a place to visit" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings and large print, thick outlines, no brands. Every place and address on them is invented. Never used in teaching (rule 4): the taught cases are the two capitol addresses and their maps (day 27 and 28), the nesting rings and the school's own address. Day 29's fresh cases are in the response rows; none of these cards repeats one.

## Card 27: the address strips

Five strips in a mixed order, the same for every child and not the order of the rings, large print, read aloud one at a time: **Pine Town** | **United States** | **46** | **Oregon** | **Cedar Road**. The teacher touches and reads one strip as each question is asked, rereads on request, and the child marks the strip just read.

**Mark:** circle the street, then the state. **Spoken:** "Name the number, the street, the town, the state and the country." A child still learning the state's name may point to a picture of the state's outline while the name is spoken; score shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

## Card 28: the state and the town

**Picture 1:** a diagram of an invented state, Willow State, with two dots, labeled Lake Town and Reed Town. **Picture 2:** a separate street map of Lake Town with two named streets, Birch Street and Oak Street, a school drawn on Birch Street, and a thick border. Invented, not to scale, no other streets.

**Mark:** circle the town on the state map and the school on the street map. **Spoken:** "Find the town on the state map, then the school on the street map. What street helps you?"

## Card 30: a fresh try

**Picture:** a new map beside a matching picture. Map: a school beside an orchard, a bridge over a stream, a path between them, on a page with a thick border. Picture: the same place drawn as a scene, the school beside the orchard, the bridge over the stream. A boxed key shows a crescent for the school, a cross for the bridge and a wavy line for the stream. The matching picture stays face down until the marking is done, so the key, not the picture, tells which symbol is which.

**Mark:** circle the school and the bridge on the map. **Spoken:** "Use the key to locate the school and the bridge. How do people cross the stream?"

## Card 30, other parts (asked only for the part a child missed)

- **Words (item 1):** three toys in a row, page this way up, from the page's left: a sock, a bell, a hat. "Where is the bell? Where is the hat compared with the sock, from here?"
- **Globe and map (item 2):** a small desk globe beside a flat world map with Europe in the middle, land patterned, water plain. "Show water on both. How are they alike?"
- **Graph (item 4):** a bounded patch of a table, two plates and no forks, with a blank two-row card. "Make a graph: one picture for each thing counted, and a labeled place for forks."
- **Address (item 5):** five strips in a mixed order, the same for every child: **Texas** | **12** | **Fairview** | **United States** | **Aster Court**. "Name the number, the street, the town, the state and the country."
- **Map (item 6):** a diagram of an invented state, Heron State, with two dots, labeled Mill Town and Cove Town, and a separate street map of Mill Town with two streets, Elm Street and Fir Street, and a school on Elm Street. "Show the town, then the school. Which street helps you?"

## Key (teacher)

Card 27: circle Cedar Road, then Oregon; 46 is the number, Cedar Road the street, Pine Town the town, Oregon the state, United States the country. "Oregon is the country" is error E.

Card 28: the Lake Town dot (not Reed Town), the school on the street map, Birch Street (not Oak Street). Pointing to the whole state is error F; "Birch Street is the town" is error E.

Card 30: the crescent is the school beside the orchard; the cross is the bridge over the stream; people cross by the bridge. "The crescent is the bridge, it curves like one" is error H; "people cross because they cross" is error G.

Other parts: the bell is between the sock and the hat, and the hat is right of the sock (the bell with no second thing is error J; "left of the sock" is error K). Both globe and map show Earth's land and water; the flat one is not a different Earth (error B). Two plate pictures in the plate row and a labeled fork row with no marks (error L). 12 is the number, Aster Court the street, Fairview the town, Texas the state, United States the country (error E). The Mill Town dot (not Cove Town), then the school on Elm Street; Elm Street helps (not Fir Street) (error F; "Elm Street is the town" is error E).
