# Day 32: Where things are

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hold the desk globe and the world-map pair out of sight. Pick today's six from the children not yet heard on items 4 to 8. Keep day 31's record sheet beside you.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions

Say: "This is a pretend class. We show what we know with new cards. Mark your own paper. Pointing counts. Passing is fine." Offer no example; say only "Show me." Hold every page the same way up, the sun at the top.

#### 9 min. Picture rows

All children mark; read each prompt once. Room 12 map: token on the plant shelf, then on the coat hooks ([[sc1-E4]]); token on the door's neighbor and on a far place ([[sc1-E5]]). Map and globe: point to the one that shows Room 12 ([[sc1-E6]]). World-map pair: point to the middle of each map ([[sc1-E8]]). Collect the papers.

#### 12 min. Hearing

At a table, the six, 2 minutes each: "Where is the plant shelf compared with the job chart and the sink? Where are the coat hooks compared with the door?" ([[sc1-E4]]); "What is next to the coat hooks? Which place is far from the door?" ([[sc1-E5]]); "Map or globe?" ([[sc1-E6]]); "Why?" ([[sc1-E6b]]); "How are they alike? How are they different?" ([[sc1-E7]]). Point to the middle of each world map first, then ask: "What is different? Did the land move?" ([[sc1-E8]]; scored shown with help). A child may pass. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Close

Say no result aloud. Tonight sort each cell: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 31's items are heard again on day 33 for children not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball. No map: draw a room as a rectangle with a door.

**Skip.** The six and the record.

- **8 min. Assessment.** Draw a rectangle for a room, a door, a rug and a shelf.
  **Say.** "Where is the shelf compared with the rug?" Expect: left or right, with the page this way up.
- **7 min. Map or globe.** Hold up a ball and the drawing.
  **Say.** "Which shows where things are in the room? Why?" Expect: the drawing; the ball shows all of Earth.
- **5 min. Two maps.** Draw a land shape in the middle of one page and at the edge of another.
  **Say.** "What is different?" Expect: the middle; the land did not move.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 4 and 6 first. Longer: the rest of the six.

### Words and supports

Between, left, right, next to, far, map, globe, middle. Frames: "The ___ is ___ the ___." "Both ___, but ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 reads day 31's items and day 32's items by part: Words (items 4 and 5; errors E and F); Maps (items 6 to 8; errors G and H). It re-teaches each and hears fresh tries.
