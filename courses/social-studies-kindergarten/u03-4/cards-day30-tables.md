# Day 30 table cards

Four tables, and pairs, named by task, never by who missed. Each table repairs one set of errors with a contrast taken from sections 3.1 to 3.3, using cases that appear nowhere else (rule 4): no exit, end or response case is shown here. All cards are invented, printed "invented for practice", with large print and thick outlines; the teacher reads every label aloud.

## Table 1: Words

Three toys in a row, page this way up, from the page's left: a spoon, a ball, a block. Pairs touch and name where each is, always with two things. A puppet says "the ball is between" with nothing beside it and then says "the ball is between the spoon and the block." Contrast pair: from this side the block is right of the ball; the puppet turns the page and the ball seems to move. The page is turned back. (Errors A, J, K.)

## Table 2: Key

A small map with a thick border: a house shape drawn as a square, a path, a tree drawn as a circle, with a boxed key. The puppet says "the square is a tree because it is green." Children use the key, not color. Second card: a picture of a real place beside its map, to say what the map leaves out. (Errors B, C, H, I.)

## Table 3: Map

The day 28 feedback cards and a blank map page. Children say one change that follows from the feedback, then make it. A table patch with two hats and no scarves and a blank two-row card: one picture for each thing counted and a labeled row for the thing with none. (Errors D, L.)

## Table 4: Address

Five strips for an invented address, hand-lettered for the class and read aloud one at a time: **21** | **Maple Court** | **Brook Town** | **Nevada** | **United States**, with the four nesting rings. Pairs set each strip into its ring and name it: number, street, town, state, country. The puppet says "Brook Town is the street" and "Nevada is the country"; children move each strip to its own ring and say why. Second card: a diagram of an invented state with a town dot, and a street map of that town with a school on a street; children touch the dot, then the school, then say which street helps. A state's name is checked by showing its outline while the name is said. (Errors E, F.)

## Pairs: Claim and because

No cards. A pair says one claim about maps or addresses and one because, naming a detail from a source the class has used. The puppet says "it helps because it helps." The pair adds a detail. (Error G.)

## Fresh tries

Each child's missed part is asked once more, cold, from the matching card in cards-exit-27-28-30.md. The closing claims are heard with a named source, one child at a time, at the table.

## Key (teacher)

Table 1: the ball is between the spoon and the block; from the other side the block is on the other hand. Table 2: the square is the house and the circle the tree, as the key says. Table 3: a change that answers the feedback; two plate pictures in the plate row and no marks in the labeled row. Table 4: 21 the number, Maple Court the street, Brook Town the town, Nevada the state, United States the country; the dot, then the school on the street map. Pairs: a claim with a detail from a named source.
