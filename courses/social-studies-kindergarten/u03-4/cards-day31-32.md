# Cards for course check 1 (days 31 and 32)

Withheld until days 31 and 32. Nothing here is shown in teaching. Every card is printed "invented for practice" along its bottom edge and drawn by us. The teacher says aloud: "This is a pretend class." No real child is in any picture; the two classes, Room 12 and Room 5, are made up. The marking scale is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Pointing counts. The teacher reads each prompt once.

## Day 31

- **Chair cards (sc1-E1).** Two cards of one table in Room 12. Card one: a chair pushed out into the walkway; a child with a tray of crayon boxes steps around it. Card two: every chair pushed in, the walkway clear. The rule: push in your chair.
- **Pool sign (sc1-E2).** One card: a sign with walking feet beside a puddle on tiles. The rule: walk, do not run. The teacher says, "This is a town swimming pool, not a school."
- **Song cards (sc1-E3).** Two cards: two hands for the clap song, a cloud with drops for the rain song. The teacher says Room 12 has time for one more song before lunch.
- **Record.** The teacher's notes from day 9 (item s01-E2) are read with this check; nothing new is asked for them.

## Day 32

- **Room 12 map (sc1-E4 to sc1-E7).** A room seen from above, a plain rectangle, a sun picture at the top edge so every copy is held the same way up. Along the top wall from the page's left: the job chart, the plant shelf, the sink, each a simple shape with a one-word label read aloud by the teacher. The talk circle rug, a round shape, is in the middle. The coat hooks, a row of small hooks, are at the bottom left and the door is just to the right of the hooks along the bottom wall. No key. It is not any real classroom.
- **Desk globe.** The class globe, or a ball with paper land shapes taped on.
- **World-map pair (sc1-E7 and sc1-E8).** Two flat world maps of one Earth, land dotted and water plain: one with South America in the middle and one with North America in the middle. Land shapes follow the public domain land data of source 22C. Neither was used in teaching. For sc1-E7 the teacher sets one of the two maps beside the desk globe. For sc1-E8 the teacher points to the middle of each map before asking.
