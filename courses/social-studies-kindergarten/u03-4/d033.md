# Day 33: Fresh tries

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the check 1 record sheet from days 31 and 32 into three groups by part: Words, Maps, Because. Mark each child's pairs. Set the day 33 cards out of sight; keep the day 31 check cards and the Room 12 picture map and cards at hand.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reconnect

Say: "On day 31 and day 32 we used pretend cards. Today we try again, a new way." Look at the day 31 items 1 to 3 and the day 32 items 4 to 8 on the record. Hand back no marks and name no result.

#### 8 min. Three groups, and pairs

Words: re-teach between, left, right and next to on the real room, then pairs say where two things are. Maps: the globe beside the room drawing, and pairs point to the middle of each map. Because: pairs say "I think ___, because ___" about a rule seen in the room. Send each child to the group the record names; a child with all shown helps a pair.

#### 10 min. Fresh tries

One fresh card for each part still not yet seen, read aloud once; the child points or speaks, about 2 minutes each. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Right only after help is shown with help. Unheard parts stay not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball. No cards: draw a shelf, a chair and a ball.

**Skip.** The fresh-try cards and the record.

- **8 min. Where.** Line up a cup, a spoon and a book.
  **Say.** "Where is the spoon? What is next to the book?" Expect: between the cup and the book; the spoon.
- **7 min. Which map.** Draw a room and hold a ball.
  **Say.** "Which shows where the chair is?" Expect: the drawing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask the one that fits: [[sc1-X33a]] [[sc1-X33b]] [[sc1-X33g]] [[sc1-X33c]] [[sc1-X33d]] [[sc1-X33e]] [[sc1-X33f]]. Draw a leash and a dog.
  **Say.** "Why keep a dog on a leash? I think ___, because ___." Expect: a pick and a reason.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two fresh tries. Longer: all six.

### Words and supports

Between, next to, far, map, globe, rule, because. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 begins unit 4 with first, next and last. Today's fresh tries close the record for days 31 and 32.
