# Address strips and rings (days 27 and 28)

Strips and rings for the nesting model. Large print, one part to a strip, in a fixed row; the teacher touches and reads one strip at a time. No ZIP code and no home address, ever.

## The rings

Four rings set one inside another, on the floor with hoops or string or on the table with string. Country is the largest, then state, then town or city, then street and number. A strip is set into its ring as the teacher reads it. Each ring has a small picture at its edge: a flag-like stripe for country, a state outline shape for state, a cluster of small houses for town, a row of small buildings along a line for street.

## The Massachusetts State House (taught, day 27)

Five strips: 24 | Beacon Street | Boston | Massachusetts | United States. Source row 27A: the address is on a federal government page read 2026-11-19. The teacher says only that it is a building with an address. Recheck before printing.

## The Washington State Legislative Building (taught, day 27)

Five strips: 416 | Sid Snyder Avenue Southwest | Olympia | Washington | United States. Source row 27B: the address is on the Washington Secretary of State's contact page read 2026-11-19; the page prints the street as "Sid Snyder Ave SW" and the strip spells it out. Recheck before printing.

## The school's own address (day 27, pairs)

Five blank strips with a space in each for the teacher to write the school's number, street, town, state and country, in large print, copied from the school's own printed address. The school is public and its address is not private. Never a child's address, and no child is asked for one. The state is the school's own state, worked through the same way as the two examples; where the school is in Massachusetts, the standard's words Massachusetts and United States are the model.

## Mixed-up labels (error E, day 27 and day 28 opening)

A puppet reads the State House strips and says "Boston is the country." Beside it lay the correctly nested cards: Boston the town, Massachusetts the state, the United States the country, 24 Beacon Street the number and street. Then name the parts of a new invented address using none of the exit or end cases.

## Key (teacher)

State House: 24 is the number; Beacon Street is the street; Boston is the town; Massachusetts is the state; United States is the country. Legislative Building: 416 is the number; Sid Snyder Avenue Southwest is the street; Olympia is the town; Washington is the state; United States is the country.
