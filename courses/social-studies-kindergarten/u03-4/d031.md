# Day 31: Why we have rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 30 sent the class here; pick up its Tomorrow. Set the check 1 record sheet: one row per child, columns for items 1 to 3, one marking scale. Pick today's six from the children not yet heard. Read the day 9 notes on following rules. Key: key-cards-31-32.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Directions

Say: "This is a pretend class. We show what we know with new cards. Mark your own paper. Pointing counts. Passing is fine." Give no example and no reason; say only "Tell me more" or "Show me."

#### 9 min. Picture rows

All children mark; read each prompt once. Chair cards: token on the card where the rule is followed ([[sc1-E1]]). Pool sign: token on the unsafe spot ([[sc1-E2]]). Song cards: point to the song Room 12 should sing ([[sc1-E3]]). Collect the papers.

#### 12 min. Hearing

At a table, the six, 2 minutes each, one ask on each card: "Why does Room 12 have this rule?" ([[sc1-E1]]); "Why is there a rule to walk at the pool?" ([[sc1-E2b]]); "Say I think ___, because ___" ([[sc1-E3]]). Others add a picture to the class chart. A child may pass; silence is not an error. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Unheard children stay not yet seen and are heard on day 33.

#### 2 min. Close

Read the day 9 notes on following rules with little reminding; they are read with this check and nothing new is asked. Say no result aloud.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a chair, a puddle and two pictures. No tokens: use buttons.

**Skip.** The class chart and the record.

- **8 min. Assessment.** Draw a chair pushed in and a chair pushed out.
  **Say.** "Why do we push in chairs?" Expect: so nobody bumps into them.
- **7 min. A sign.** Draw a sign with walking feet.
  **Say.** "Why does a pool say walk?" Expect: the floor is wet and slippery.
- **5 min. A pick.** Draw a clap and a rain cloud.
  **Say.** "Which song? I think ___, because ___." Expect: a pick and a reason.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the rows and one hearing item. Longer: the rest of the six.

### Words and supports

Rule, safe, fair, because, pretend. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." A point, a drawing or an aid is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 is part two of the check, on maps. Day 33 reads day 31's items by part: Because (items 1 to 3; errors A, B, C and D). It re-teaches each and hears fresh tries.
