# Day 28: Find the town, then the school

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each table our school's pair. Hang the other two pairs.

### The day

#### 2 min. Introduce

Mark day 27's s03-X27; error E: nested strips. Read each child their day 26 feedback card. Post "How can a map help us find our school?"

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Say the state and country with each map; children say them back. Three minutes: the Boston dot on the Massachusetts map, then Beacon Street on the Boston map. Three: the Olympia dot, then Sid Snyder Avenue Southwest. Puppet touches all of Washington: "it is here" (error F).

**Ask.** Which dot is the town? Where is the building? Expect: the labeled dot; on its street.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Four minutes: each child redraws the day 26 map; these are scored. Hear the six, 40 seconds each: a place near school, what is next to it, how people use it. Six: table groups of four, 90 seconds each: our town, then our school on the street map, with help; note help for the six. If time, say our state's name beside its outline; each child says it and points to it on the United States map, unscored.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Each child has card 28 ("a pretend card"). Say "The school is in Lake Town, in Willow State, in the United States." Tokens: town dot, then school; the six name the street (s03-X28 to s03-X28c). A whole state is error F; "Birch Street is the town," error E.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw them. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The redraw and the record.

- **5 min. Two sizes.** Draw a big blob for a pretend state with a dot for Bay Town. On another page draw a street, Pear Lane, with a school.
  **Say.** "Which is the town? Where is the school?" Expect: the dot; on Pear Lane.
- **5 min. Find.** Cover the labels; your child finds the dot, then the school.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X28]] [[s03-X28b]] [[s03-X28c]] Draw the state with two dots, Bay Town and Hill Town, and a street map of Bay Town. Say the school is in Bay Town.
  **Say.** "Which dot? Where is the school? Which street?" Expect: the Bay Town dot; on the street map; Pear Lane.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the United States map.

### Words and supports

State map, street map, dot. Frame: "The town is this dot. The school is on ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 29 checks fresh maps. Error F: the dot, then the street.
