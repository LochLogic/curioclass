# Day 29: What we know about where things are

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make response rows for each child. Turn School Garden's picture face down. Choose the six not yet heard on item 2.

### The day

#### 2 min. Introduce School Garden

Mark day 28's s03-X28; if many missed it, error F: the dot, then the street; error E: nested cards. Say, "The illustrator made this place up. It is not our school." Show the map; read its labels, never what a symbol means.

#### 8 min. Individual picture responses

Each child alone; no partner answers. Read each prompt aloud; a token goes on the sheet, or the child points and you place it. Row 1, [[s03-E1]]: left of the pond, then between the pond and the garden. [[s03-E3b]]: tokens on two places, from the key. Row 2, [[s03-E2]] [[s03-E2b]] [[s03-E2c]]: land and water on both, a likeness, a difference. Row 5, [[s03-E5]]: the number, street, town, state and country, by saying or pointing; a child still learning the state's name sees its outline as you say it, recorded as with help. Row 6, [[s03-E6]]: our state's outline named, our town's dot, the school, its street. Collect sheets before showing any answer. Score [[s03-E4]], [[s03-E4b]] and [[s03-E4c]] from the day 26 records and the day 28 redraw.

#### 8 min. Hearing

At a table, the six, 80 seconds each: [[s03-E3]] with the Our Place Record, the path question of [[s03-E3b]], and any [[s03-E2d]] [[s03-E2e]] explanation missing, with Two World Maps. Others add a picture to the class key card, unscored. A child may pass; silence is not an error.

#### 2 min. Close the wonder wall

Read the wonders; mark the ones the unit answered. Tonight sort each cell: independent, content-prompted (right only on a taught case), or not yet observed.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: use buttons. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The wonder wall and the class record.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Draw a pond, a bench, a swing and a tree, with a key box. Your child draws a small picture in the key for each.
  **Say.** "Where is the bench compared with the swing and the tree?" Expect: between them, with the page this way up.
- **8 min. Address.** Write five scraps for 17, Moss Lane, Apple Town, Utah, United States, mixed up.
  **Say.** "Which is the street? The town? The state? The country?" Expect: Moss Lane; Apple Town; Utah; United States.
- **7 min. Claim.**
  **Say.** "How can we show someone where things are?" Expect: "I think ..., because ..." naming a drawing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: garden, address and map rows first; unheard parts stay not yet observed. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Left, between, key, globe, street, town. Frames: "The ___ is ___ the ___." "I think ___, because ___." Pointing, symbols or an aid count; give time.
