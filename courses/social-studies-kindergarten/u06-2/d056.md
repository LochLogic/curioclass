# Day 56: Goods, services and earning

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the pictures. Fold Pip.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Day 55's exit, 2 minutes: "I like the kite" beside "There is only room for one, so I choose the kite because ...". Which gives the limit? If many missed it (error D), four minutes. Then post "How do goods, services and paying help people get things?" beside Mina and Davi (review, day 53).

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Say: "Curio Class made the Jobs Book, 2026. Pine Hollow is made up." Read the stall, ride and chain: Mina sells beans and earns; she pays Davi for a ride. The payer is the buyer; the paid is the seller. Words: goods (you can hold), services (done for you), earn, pay, buy, sell. Model: "I think the ride is a service, because the picture shows Davi driving for his rider." Some money comes other ways too. Pip: "The shirt is a service, because someone touched it." Answer: the shirt is a good; mending is the service (error F). Pip: "Money just shows up." Answer: Mina earns it (error G).

#### 7 min. Supported work

Tables of five. 4 minutes: sort a card: "I think ___, because the picture shows ___." 3 minutes: follow the chain; say who did the work. Hear six, 30 seconds each.

#### 3 min. Exit

Tell: "A pretend girl pays a piano tuner to tune her piano." All point: row one, the service ([[s06-X56a]]); row two, the good ([[s06-X56b]]). Hear six, 25 seconds each: "Where did the girl get the money?" ([[s06-X56c]]) Touching is error F; "just there" is error G.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a bag of beans, a shirt, a bus ride and a haircut. No hoops: two paper loops.

**Skip.** Pip, tables and the chain talk.

- **5 min. Story.** Tell Mina's stall and ride, made up. Pay is for work.
  **Say.** "Where did Mina get the money?" Expect: she worked and sold beans.
- **5 min. Sort.** Your child sorts four drawn cards: goods or services.
  **Say.** "Can you hold it, or does someone do it for you?" Expect: hold, a good; done for you, a service.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X56a]] [[s06-X56b]] [[s06-X56c]] Tell the piano tuner story; show a blanket, a tuner, a pencil box; then a basket, a dog walker, a singing lesson. Ask each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the stall. Longer: a shop.

### Words and supports

Frame: "It is a service because someone ___."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error F or G, day 57 opens with a mended shirt beside the sewing, and a chain.
