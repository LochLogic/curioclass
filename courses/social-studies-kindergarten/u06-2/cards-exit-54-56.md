# Exit cards 54 to 56

New picture cards for the exits, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line and the headings. The teacher says "this is a pretend class" and reads every label and prompt aloud. Never used in teaching (rule 4). The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every child marks; the spoken ask is heard from the day's rotating group.

## Day 54

- s06-X54a: Invented for practice, a pretend class: three new cards: a bed in a small room with a door and a warm cover; a ribbon bow by itself; a paper lantern. Mark: circle the one a person must have to stay well.
- s06-X54b: The same three cards. Spoken, rotating group: Why is that one a need?

## Day 55

- s06-X55a: Invented for practice, a pretend class: one empty space on the wall and two posters, a bird and a fish. Cards: the empty space, the bird poster, the fish poster. Each child first circles the poster they would put up; any choice is right and the circle is not scored. Mark: put a dot on what means you can only pick one.
- s06-X55b: The same cards. Spoken, rotating group: Why can you put up only one?

## Day 56

- s06-X56a: Paper: two rows of three pictures, marked with one and two dots. Row one: a blanket on a bed; a tuner at a piano; a pencil box. Row two: a basket; a dog on a leash held by a child; a singing lesson. Mark, row one: point to the one that is a service.
- s06-X56b: The same two rows of pictures. Mark, row two: point to the one that is a good.
- s06-X56c: The teacher reads: A pretend girl pays a piano tuner to tune her piano. No picture of a worker. Spoken, rotating group: Where could the girl get the money to pay the tuner?
