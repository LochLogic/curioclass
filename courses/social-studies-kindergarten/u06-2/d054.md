# Day 54: Needs and wants

## Teacher page

### Before class

Drawn apple and kite on the rug. Fold Pip. Ask no child what they have.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen

Day 53's exit, 3 minutes: a bus label beside Davi's icons, then the repair card's sail maker; children place the icons. If many missed it (error B), four minutes. Then: apple or kite, "Which would you pick to stay well?" Post "Why must people choose?" on the wonder wall; write three wonders.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Say: "These cards are Curio Class's, 2026. The children are made up." Show cards one to six. "I think it is a need, because ..." Review: need began in unit 1; now it is something to stay well. A want is something we like. Pip: "I want a kite, so I need a kite." Answer: I want a kite; I need water (error C). Pip: "Everybody needs a coat." Show snow, shade and ramp cards: need depends on the day and person (error J).

#### 7 min. Supported work

Tables of five. 4 minutes: sort a card or two: "I think it is a ___, because ___." 3 minutes: draw one thing to look at or play with and ask a partner "need or want?" Hear six, 30 seconds each.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say: "This is a pretend class." All circle what a person must have to stay well ([[s06-X54a]]). Hear six, 25 seconds each: "Why is that one a need?" ([[s06-X54b]]) Mark the record sheet. Ribbon, lantern or "because I want it" is error C; "everybody needs the same bed" is error J.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw an apple, a roof and a kite. No hoops: two paper loops.

**Skip.** Pip, tables and partner talk.

- **5 min. Sort.** Show six drawn cards: apple, water, roof, coat in snow, kite, boat. Your child sorts need or want.
  **Say.** "Is it a need or a want? How do you know?" Expect: a need helps us stay well; a want is nice to have.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing to look at or play with; write "I want ...".
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X54a]] [[s06-X54b]] Draw a bed in a room, a ribbon and a lantern; ask which a person must have to stay well, and why.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: six cards. Longer: the ramp card.

### Words and supports

Frame: "It is a ___, because ___."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error C or J, day 55 opens with a snow card and a sun card.
