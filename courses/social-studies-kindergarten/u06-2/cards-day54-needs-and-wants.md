# Needs and wants cards (day 54, and day 55 repair)

Eight large picture cards, invented line drawings, one set a table. Two hoops labeled need and want. The teacher says "this is a made-up class" and reads every label aloud. Words with pictures: need, want.

## The eight cards

1. An apple. (need: food)
2. A cup of water. (need: water)
3. A roof over a small house. (need: shelter)
4. A child in a coat on a snowy day. (need: clothing, in snow)
5. A kite. (want)
6. A toy boat. (want)
7. A child sitting in the shade of a tree on a hot day. (need on a hot day: shade)
8. A ramp up to a door beside a girl who rolls in a wheelchair. (need for her to get in)

Cards 1 to 6 are taught on day 54. Cards 7 and 8 are used for the repair of J and as the snow card and sun card on day 55.

## How they are used

The teacher models each: "I think it is a need, because ..." Tables sort a card or two with the frame "I think it is a ___, because ___". Each child draws one thing to look at or play with and tells a partner "need or want?" No child is asked what they have.

Puppet Pip says: "I want a kite, so I need a kite." (C) Answer: "I want a kite. I need water." Pip says: "Everybody needs a coat." (J) Answer with cards 4, 7 and 8: "A coat is a need in snow. On a hot day the need is shade. A ramp is a need for a girl who rolls."

## Teacher key

Needs: apple, water, roof, coat in snow, shade on a hot day, ramp. Wants: kite, toy boat. What a person needs depends on the day and the person; a want is never wrong.
