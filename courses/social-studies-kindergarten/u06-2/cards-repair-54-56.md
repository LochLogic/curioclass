# Repair cards 54 to 56

Used only in the hand-back minutes, never as an exit.

## C and J (day 55 opening, 3 minutes)

A snow card (a child in a coat on a snowy day) and a sun card (a child in shade on a hot day), sorted into need and want hoops with "I think ..., because ...". New cards for the retry: a woolly scarf in winter, a straw mat on warm sand. Say: "What a person needs depends on the day."

## D (day 56 opening, 2 minutes)

Two sentences side by side, read aloud: "I like the kite." "There is only room for one, so I choose the kite because ..." Children say which sentence gives the limit. New pair for the retry: two pieces of fruit and one plate.

## F and G (day 57 opening)

See the goods and services cards: an object card beside the action done for someone, and a three-picture chain on a new worker.

## Teacher key

I like it is a preference. Only enough for one is the reason the choice has to be made. A coat in snow and shade on a hot day are both needs, in different places. The shirt is a good; the mending is the service.
