# Day 55: One space, so we choose

## Teacher page

### Before class

Kite and boat on the table with one place. Fold Pip.

### The day

#### 4 min. Reopen

Day 54's exit, 3 minutes: the snow card and sun card into need or want, "I think ..., because ...". If many missed it (error C or error J), the four minutes are the repair, with the scarf and straw mat. Post "Why must people choose?" on the wonder wall.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Say: "I can only pick one. I choose the kite, because it flies." A child picks the boat for another reason; both are fine (review, unit 5). Review: choose, from unit 2. Words: not enough, trade. Pip: "I chose the kite because it is best." Answer: there is room for one, so I choose (error D). Pip grabs a star: "I traded!" Answer: a trade needs both to say yes (error K).

#### 8 min. Supported work

One space and two stickers each. 2 minutes: put one in the space; tell a partner why; hear six, 20 seconds each. 4 minutes: ask "Would you trade my moon for your star?" Yes or no; a no is fine. 2 minutes: tables tell who traded; draw your space. Stickers go back.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say: "This is a pretend class." Each circles a poster they would put up; any choice is right. All dot what means you can only pick one ([[s06-X55a]]). Hear six, 25 seconds each: "Why can you put up only one?" ([[s06-X55b]]) Mark the record sheet. A dot on a poster, or "the bird is best", is error D.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No stickers: tear two colored scraps. No kite or boat: draw them.

**Skip.** Pip, the trade round and table talk.

- **5 min. Choose.** One drawn space, a kite and a boat. "I can only pick one."
  **Say.** "Which will you put in the space? Why?" Expect: any choice, with a reason of their own.
- **5 min. Trade.** Offer your sticker for theirs; they may say yes or no. Say no once.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X55a]] [[s06-X55b]] Draw one empty space with a bird and a fish poster; your child circles one, dots what means only one fits, and says why only one.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: choose only. Longer: a third sticker.

### Words and supports

Choose, not enough, trade. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

If many gave error D, day 56 opens with "I like it" beside "only enough for one."
