# Jobs Book: the stall and the ride (day 56)

Three large pictures, read aloud. Invented people in made-up Pine Hollow, said aloud. Words with pictures: goods, services, earn, pay, buy, sell.

## Picture 1: the stall

Mina sits at a small stall with a bag of beans and a handwritten sign showing three dots. Read: "Mina sells a bag of beans at her stall. A customer gives her tokens. Mina earns money by working." Name: "The one who pays is the buyer. The one who is paid is the seller. Mina is the seller."

## Picture 2: the ride

Mina stands at a bus stop and hands a token to Davi, who drives. Read: "Mina pays Davi for a bus ride. She is the buyer. Davi is paid for his work." Davi is a seller of the ride.

## Picture 3: Mina and Davi's chain

Three small pictures in a row: Mina hoes beans, Mina hands over a bag and takes a token, Mina gives a token to Davi on the bus. Read: "Work, then earning, then paying." Say: "Some money comes in other ways too, not only from work."

## Teacher model

"I think the ride is a service, because the Jobs Book picture shows Davi driving for his rider." Goods in the pictures: the beans. Services: the ride.

Puppet Pip says: "The shirt is a service, because someone touched it." (F) Answer: "The shirt is a good. The mending is the service." Pip says: "Money just shows up." (G) Answer: "Mina sells beans and earns money; she pays Davi for a bus ride."
