# One-space cards and paper stickers (day 55)

One card each child: a heavy paper square with one empty paper space drawn on it. Two different paper stickers in a box, a moon and a star, enough for every child to have both, the same for everyone. Stickers go back to the box at the end; nothing goes home.

## Parts

- Teacher demonstration: a kite and a toy boat on the table, one place for one of them. "I can only pick one. I choose the kite, because ... " A child picks the boat with a different reason; both are right.
- Choose (2 minutes): each child chooses one sticker for the space and tells a partner why. The teacher hears the six here.
- Trade round (4 minutes): a child asks "Would you trade my moon for your star?" and the partner says yes or no. Trade only if both say yes; a no is a fine answer. Children may say no and keep their own.
- Tell (2 minutes): tables say who traded and who did not. Nobody is ranked.

Words: choose, not enough, trade. Puppet Pip says D and K (grabs a star and says "I traded"). Answer: "A trade needs both children to say yes."

## Teacher key

Any choice is right and not scored. A trade is agreed by both; a no means no trade and each keeps their own sticker.
