# Goods and services cards and the money chain (day 56)

## Eight cards, one set a table, and two hoops (goods, services)

Four goods: a bag of beans; a shirt; a hat; a picture book. Four services: a bus ride; a haircut; mowing a lawn; a teacher helping a class. Read every label aloud. A good is something you can hold. A service is something people do for you. Children sort a card and say "I think ... because the picture shows ...".

## Three chain pictures, one set a table

Work (a person hoes rows), earning (the person is handed a token for a bag of beans), paying (the person hands a token to a bus driver). The token picture is only a stand-in for money. Each table follows one chain and says who did the work. Teacher key: Mina did the work, the buyer paid her, she paid Davi.

## For the repair of F and G (day 57 opening)

An object card beside an action done for someone: a mended shirt, then a person sewing it; a clean pot, then a person scrubbing it. Children match each object (good) with the action (service). A three-picture chain on a new worker said aloud by the teacher: a pretend tailor's helper who cuts cloth, is paid, and pays a bus fare. Retry with a new worker.
