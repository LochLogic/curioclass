# Unit 5 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left, and beside it the child's group day, 41 to 49: six children to each day, so "today's six" are that day's group, heard for the spoken part of the exit. A child outside the day's group is recorded not yet heard for the spoken part. Each E column is one spoken exit, marked on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The picture mark every child makes is kept apart and is never scored as an explanation. Days 41 to 44 are holidays and feed E1, E2 and E13. Days 45 to 47 are the symbols and feed E3 to E10, which are scored later from the revised hearings on days 48 and 49 and the fresh try on day 50, never from the first hearing, which is practice. Days 48 and 49 feed E11 and E12. Day 50 has no exit; its items are the evidence, and a rotating ten children are heard each time. The End columns are the closing check on day 50, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child does it independently, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not shown even with prompts, and blank when not yet observed. Never write 0 for a part that was not heard. Add a heard column naming who was heard on each day. Put the date in any cell tried again on day 51. A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
