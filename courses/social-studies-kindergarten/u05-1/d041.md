# Day 41: A day we remember

## Teacher page

### Before class

Calendar-date lesson; day 40 does not precede it. Ring the second Monday of October on the wall calendar. Pick today's six. Keys: key-exits-41-44.md, key-cards-41.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Hook and question

Show the calendar card and read the day's name; some places use another name. Show the nation card beside it.

**Ask.** Who does this day help us remember? Expect: any guess. Chart: "Whom do holidays honor, and why?"

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Read the nation card in the present tense: the Aquinnah Wampanoag still live on Martha's Vineyard. A nation is a country or a people who govern themselves. Ask "Who made it, and why?" The tribe wrote its page; we drew this card from it. A holiday is a day set aside to remember something; to honor is to show thanks and respect.

Columbus Day remembers Columbus and his voyage in 1492. Indigenous Peoples' Day remembers Native peoples, including nations living here today. Model: "I think this day remembers Native peoples, because this card shows the Aquinnah Wampanoag, who live on Martha's Vineyard today." Review: "I think ___, because ___," from unit 1.

Moss says "It is a day off, so that is what it is for." Answer: the card tells whom (error A). Moss says "We remember them because they were the best." Answer: give a detail (error G). It is the first of Our Holiday Cards.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Say: "This is a pretend class." Pairs set three practice cards by the calendar card and say "I think ___ because ___"; a partner may speak. Hear the six, 30 seconds each. The rest draw one card.

#### 5 min. Exit

Every child circles the card the board remembers ([[s05-X41a]]), then draws on the saved card while you hear the six, 30 seconds each: "Who does this day help us remember?" ([[s05-X41b]]) Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three on scrap paper. No calendar: draw a page with one day ringed.

**Skip.** The wall and the pairs.

- **5 min. The day.** Show the ringed Monday. Read its name.
  **Say.** "Who does this day help us remember?" Expect: a guess.
- **5 min. The card.** Draw a small island on blue water. Say the Aquinnah Wampanoag live there today.
  **Say.** "I think this day remembers Native peoples, because ___." Expect: a clue.
- **5 min. Draw it.** Your child draws one picture on the student page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X41a]] [[s05-X41b]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card each. Longer: a second holiday.

### Words and supports

Holiday, honor, nation. Frame: "I think ___ because ___." A point or drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the food or fireworks, or said "a day off" (error A) or "the best" (error G), day 42 repairs it.

### Credit

Ours. Say "We drew this card from the Aquinnah Wampanoag Tribe's own page."
