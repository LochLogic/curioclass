# Exit cards 41 to 44

New picture cards for the exits of days 41 to 44, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line and the headings. The teacher says "this is a pretend class" and reads every label and prompt aloud. Never used in teaching (rule 4). The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## Day 41

A pretend school bulletin board headed with the day's local name, read aloud. For the Indigenous Peoples' Day heading, three cards: grown-ups and children at a table with a word chart in a Native nation's language class today; a pot of food at a big table; fireworks at night. For the Columbus Day heading, three cards: a ship with sails on the waves; a pot of food at a big table; fireworks at night. Each child circles the card the board is remembering. Spoken, rotating group: "Who does this day help us remember?"

## Day 42

Two cards: a grown-up in a uniform visiting a classroom while children wave; children in costumes at a front door at night. Circle the card that helps explain Veterans Day. Spoken, rotating group: "Who does Veterans Day thank?" Then, with a new record paper drawn for the exit (a ribbon, a basket and the words autumn, 1621, no people shown; not the teaching card): "Who was at the harvest meal in 1621?"

## Day 43

Three drawings in a pretend town: one grown-up putting up a new rule sign while others walk by; neighbors standing in a room around a paper rule on the wall and talking it over; children in a line following a posted rule at a fountain. Circle the picture that shows King's work. Spoken, rotating group: "Why do we remember his work?"

## Day 44

A strip mat and two cards, each read aloud with its month: Presidents' Day, February, a school door with two paper coins; Memorial Day, May, a flag standing quietly beside a stone. Put the two cards on the mat, the one that comes first in the school year on the left. Spoken, rotating group: "Pick one of your two cards. Who or what does that day remember?"

## Key (teacher)

41: Indigenous Peoples' Day, the language class of a Native nation today; Columbus Day, the ship with sails; not the pot of food or the fireworks; "I think it remembers ___ because the card shows ___". 42: circle the veteran; veterans are people who served in the armed forces; Wampanoag people and English settlers were at the meal, from the record card, and the Wampanoag nations live in Massachusetts today. 43: the neighbors around the rule paper; he worked with others to change unfair laws that kept Black and white people apart. 44: Presidents' Day on the left (February), Memorial Day on the right (May); Presidents' Day remembers Washington and Lincoln, who were presidents; Memorial Day remembers people who died while serving, not all veterans.
