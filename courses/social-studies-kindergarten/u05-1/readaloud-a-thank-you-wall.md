# A Thank-You Wall

A four-scene picture read-aloud for day 42, made up by us. Say first: "This is a pretend class. The children are made up." One actor in each scene. Large pictures, one sentence read aloud under each. No child is asked whether anyone they know served.

**Scene 1. Mira (actor: Mira).** Mira stands at a wall with a roll of paper. She says: "Today is Veterans Day. A veteran is a person who served in the armed forces. Our class will make a thank-you wall."

**Scene 2. Mr. Okafor (actor: the teacher in the pretend class, Mr. Okafor).** He holds up a blank card. He says: "A thank-you wall honors veterans. To honor is to show thanks and respect."

**Scene 3. Leo (actor: Leo).** Leo draws a flag on his card and writes a word with a helper. He says: "I made a card that says thank you."

**Scene 4. The wall (actor: none; the wall only).** The wall is full of cards under one big word, *Thank you*, and the class is looking at it. A speech bubble: "We remember people who served, and we say thank you."

## Teach by picture (after the story)

Say veteran and honor with the pictures. Ask once: "What does the wall do?" Take a spoken or pointed answer. Say the source plainly: this story was made by us, in 2026, to show what honoring can look like. Veterans Day thanks everyone who served in the armed forces. Do not ask who in a child's family served.
