# Day 43: Working together to change unfair rules

## Teacher page

### Before class

Taught in King Day week. Put the two saved cards on the rug. Pick today's six.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Show the two saved cards.

**Ask.** Whom did each card help us remember?

If many missed day 42's exit, show the record card again, four minutes: both groups were there; the Wampanoag nations live in Massachusetts today.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Say: "This is a pretend town. The real laws were like this." Page 1: a park gate with two doors under one sign. Read: long, long ago, in real places, some laws kept Black and white people apart. That was unfair. Page 2: neighbors of many kinds walking together. Read: Martin Luther King Jr. worked with many others, peacefully, to change those laws. We remember him on the third Monday in January. Look, never act out; ask nobody about being treated unfairly.

Both pages are ours, made in 2026; the King Center's page tells of his nonviolent work. Review: "Who made it, and why?" from unit 3; children ask it with you. Model: "I think we remember King's work, because this page shows people working together to change unfair laws." Review: "I think ___, because ___" from unit 1. Moss says "He was the nicest man." Answer: what the page shows (error G). Moss says "It is a day off." Answer: the card tells whom (error A).

#### 10 min. Supported work

Say: "This is a pretend class." Pairs sort three cards: a crosswalk sign everyone follows, a sign letting only some children use a slide, people signing one long paper. They say "I think King's work was ___ because ___". Hear the six, 50 seconds each, ending with "Why do we remember his work?" ([[s05-X43b]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit

Every child circles the picture that shows King's work ([[s05-X43a]]), then adds a drawing to the day 43 card for the wall.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw a gate with two doors, then neighbors walking together.

**Skip.** The sort.

- **5 min. The town.** Draw a pretend gate with two doors and a sign. Say: some real laws kept Black and white people apart. That was unfair.
  **Say.** "What did Martin Luther King Jr. work to change?" Expect: unfair laws, with others.
- **5 min. Together.** Draw neighbors walking side by side.
  **Say.** "I think we remember his work because ___." Expect: working together.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X43a]] [[s05-X43b]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pages and exit. Longer: a fourth card.

### Words and supports

Unfair, change, together. A point or drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many said "the nicest man" (error G) or "a day off" (error A), day 44 repairs it. Day 44 adds Presidents' Day and the school-year strip.
