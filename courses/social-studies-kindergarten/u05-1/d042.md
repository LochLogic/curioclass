# Day 42: Who we thank, and who was there

## Teacher page

### Before class

Taught Veterans Day week. Put day 41's card on the rug. Pick today's six. Keys: key-exits-41-44.md, key-cards-42-43.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Reopen and repair

Show the day 41 card.

**Ask.** Who did it help us remember? What helped you know?

If many missed day 41's exit (error A, error G), repair four minutes: a "day off" picture beside the card, then a praise sentence beside a detail sentence; the class picks the one a card supports.

#### 6 min. Teaching first: Veterans Day

Say: "This is a pretend class." Read A Thank-You Wall, one actor a scene. Its maker is us, in 2026. A veteran is a person who served in the armed forces. Review: to honor is to show thanks and respect, from day 41. Veterans Day, November 11, thanks veterans. Ask nobody about family.

**Ask.** What does the wall do? Expect: says thank you to veterans.

#### 6 min. Teaching first: the harvest meal

Thanksgiving is a day coming soon, the fourth Thursday in November. Show the record card: a record is words kept about what happened. Read: "Long, long ago, in 1621, English settlers at Plymouth held a harvest celebration. Wampanoag people were there too, and they brought food. The Wampanoag nations live in Massachusetts today." Its maker is a reference article, read by us on 2026-11-19; records do not agree on every part, and Wampanoag people tell their own history on their own pages. Review: "Who made it, and why?" from unit 3. No costume or acting out.

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs match two question cards to picture cards: "Who do we thank on Veterans Day?" "Who was at the 1621 meal?" They say "I think ___ because ___". Moss says "We thank them because they were the best." Answer: a detail. Hear the six, 60 seconds each: "Why do we have Veterans Day?" ([[s05-X42b]]); with the exit's record paper, "Who was at the harvest meal in 1621?" ([[s05-X42c]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Exit

Say: "This is a pretend class." Every child circles the card that helps explain Veterans Day ([[s05-X42a]]), then adds a drawing to the day 42 card for the wall.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a card, a flag and a basket on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The wall and the pairs.

- **5 min. The wall.** Draw cards on a long paper under the word Thank you.
  **Say.** "A veteran served in the armed forces. Who do we thank on Veterans Day?" Expect: veterans.
- **5 min. The record.** Draw a basket under the words autumn, 1621.
  **Say.** "Wampanoag people and English settlers were there. The Wampanoag nations live in Massachusetts today." Ask: "Who was there?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X42a]] [[s05-X42b]] [[s05-X42c]] Ask the one that fits; wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the wall only. Longer: add the day 41 nation card.

### Words and supports

Veteran, honor, record. Frame: "I think ___ because ___." A point or drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many said only settlers, or that Wampanoag people are gone, show the record card again; no register letter fits. Day 43 is Martin Luther King Jr. Day.

### Credit
