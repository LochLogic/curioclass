# Day 36: The timeline

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hide exit card 36. Pick today's six. Teacher key: key-sheet-dated-picture-log.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen and hand-back

Hand-back from day 35: s04-X35a and s04-X35b (error B): point to the marker, move the card either side. s04-X35c (error H): Wren puts next week's card behind the marker; the class moves it ahead.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Four minutes: say Ria and Tomas are made up; read Ria and the Ball, one actor a scene. Lay the four cards left to right and name the strip a timeline, a picture strip read left to right. Four minutes: read A House Is Built, an invented crew, onto a second timeline.

**Ask.** What happened just before this? Expect: the card to its left; a roof needs walls (error A: nicest card first).

Two minutes: say first, next, last, before and after for two pairs: "The walls come before the roof." Moss puts painting before the walls (error H).

#### 6 min. Supported work

Children circle each event's label card on the dated picture log, practice only. Hear today's six, one minute each, with exit card 36: "What happened first?" ([[s04-X36b]]) and "What happened last?" ([[s04-X36c]]); then "Which one is happening now?" After class write one relation to revisit on each log.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say "this is a pretend class." Each child has exit card 36. Read its bean text aloud, then: "Put the cards in order on the strip, left to right" ([[s04-X36a]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: fold a paper in thirds. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The log.

- **5 min. Ria and the Ball.** Say Ria is made up. Tell it with four drawings: kicks, rolls under a bush, Tomas finds it, rolls it back. Lay them left to right.
  **Say.** "What happened just before Tomas found it?" Expect: the ball rolled under the bush.
- **5 min. Before and after.** Point to two cards.
  **Say.** "Which comes before?" Expect: the one on the left.
- **5 min. Again.** Shuffle and rebuild the strip.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X36a]] [[s04-X36b]] [[s04-X36c]] Draw cooks washing beans, boiling them and filling a bowl; your child orders them.
  **Say.** "What happened first? Last?" Expect: washing; the bowl.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the Ria strip.

### Words and supports

Timeline, first, next, last, before, after. Frame: "___ is before ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 37 sets an old photograph on the timeline. It hands back error A (link each card to the one before) and error H (the last card put first: move it to the right end).
