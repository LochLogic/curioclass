# Teacher key, dated picture log sheet

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child page (sheet-dated-picture-log.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Morning, afternoon, night. Brushes: yesterday (washed), today (drying), tomorrow (put away). Shelf: last week (set up), next week (visitor). Plant: last month (seeds), next month (drawing). Tree: last year (planted), next year (another planned). Now is the marker's event; then is anything behind it.
