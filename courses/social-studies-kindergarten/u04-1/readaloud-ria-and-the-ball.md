# Ria and the Ball (day 36)

An original four-scene story, read aloud by the teacher, who says first that Ria and Tomas are made up. Each scene has one actor and one large card with a thick outline. No child reads. Cards are printed "invented for practice".

## Scene 1: Ria kicks the ball
Ria kicks the ball across the grass. *(Card: Ria and a ball in the air. One actor.)*

## Scene 2: The ball rolls under a bush
The ball rolls under a bush. *(Card: a ball under a low bush. One actor, the ball.)*

## Scene 3: Tomas finds it
Tomas finds the ball. *(Card: Tomas bending down to the bush. One actor.)*

## Scene 4: Tomas rolls it back
Tomas rolls the ball back. *(Card: Tomas with a ball rolling away from him. One actor.)*

## How it is used

The four cards go on a strip left to right. Name the strip a **timeline**: a picture strip read left to right. Say first, next, last, before and after for two pairs of cards ("Ria kicks the ball before it rolls under the bush"). Ask "What happened just before this?" for each card to tie it to the one before it (error A).

## Key (teacher)

Order: Ria kicks the ball, the ball rolls under the bush, Tomas finds it, Tomas rolls it back.
