# Unit 4 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left, and beside it the child's group day, 34 to 40: six children to each day, so "today's six" are that day's group, heard for the spoken part of the exit and for the explanations of items 3 and 4 on days 39 and 40. A child outside the day's group is recorded not yet heard for the spoken part. Each E column is one spoken exit: one tick when the child answers it, by pointing, saying or placing a card, with a puppet or a partner if that helps. Day 39 has no exit ticket; its items are the evidence, and about ten children are heard that day and ten more on day 40. The End columns are the closing check on day 39, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child does it independently, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not shown even with prompts, and blank when not yet observed. Never write 0 for a part that was not heard. Put the date in the cell for any attempt after day 39, since day 40 repeats a part. Item 2 is scored from the revised day 36 log (day 38), never from the first log, which is practice. Add a heard column for items 3 and 4 naming who was heard. A child may pass; leave the cell blank and try again another day.
