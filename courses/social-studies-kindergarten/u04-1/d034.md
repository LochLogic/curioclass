# Day 34: First, next, last

## Teacher page

### Before class

After day 33, hang the calendar. Set out cards and puppets, hide exit card 34, pick six. Teacher key: key-sheet-hook-washing.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Hook and wonder wall

Review: wonder wall and puppets Moss and Wren, unit 1. Show the washboard and washing machine drawings; both help wash clothes.

**Ask.** What might change about the work? Expect: any wonder; a child may pass. Write wonders on the wall; post the big question: "Which change from long ago made the biggest difference to everyday work?"

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Place cards left to right, saying each. Two minutes each: the school day, "First morning, next afternoon, last night" (night is the school under a moon); the seed, sprout and plant cards. Moss puts the plant first, "because it is the nicest" (error A); the class puts each card after the one before.

**Ask.** What happened first? Next? Last? Expect: each card after the one before.

Two minutes: move the calendar marker; place a real class event on yesterday, today, tomorrow.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs order the coat cards with "First ..., next ..., last ...", then set one real class card on a paper calendar strip. Hear today's six, 80 seconds each, with exit card 34's caterpillar set: "Say the order as you point" ([[s04-X34c]]).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say "this is a pretend class." Each child has exit card 34. Read: "Put one, two and three dots to show first, next and last" ([[s04-X34a]]); "Put the card where tomorrow's sing-along goes" ([[s04-X34b]]). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw them on scrap paper. No calendar: draw three squares.

**Skip.** The wall and the puppets.

- **5 min. Three pictures.** Draw a seed, a sprout and a plant. Lay them in a row.
  **Say.** "What happened first? Next? Last?" Expect: the seed, the sprout, the plant.
- **5 min. Three squares.** Draw squares for yesterday, today and tomorrow. Put a button on today.
  **Say.** "Which square is after today?" Expect: tomorrow's.
- **5 min. Again.** Shuffle the pictures and order them again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X34a]] [[s04-X34b]] [[s04-X34c]] Draw a caterpillar, chrysalis and butterfly; your child shows first, next and last with dots, then points as they say the order.
  **Say.** "Where does tomorrow's picnic go?" Expect: after today.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the coat cards.

### Words and supports

First, next, last, yesterday, today, tomorrow. Frame: "First ___, next ___, last ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 35 adds now, then, long ago, before and after. It hands back error A and error H.
