# Exit cards 34 to 36

New picture cards for the exits of days 34, 35 and 36, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge. Plain line drawings, thick outlines, no writing except that line, no brands. The teacher says "this is a pretend class" and reads every label and prompt aloud. Never used in teaching (rule 4): the taught cases are the school day, seeds, coat, Room 9, Ria, the house and the dated log. The marking scale on the class sheet is one: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## Day 34

**Card set 1, three cards, same size, shuffled:** a caterpillar, a chrysalis and a butterfly. Each child puts one dot, two dots and three dots to show first, next and last. Spoken, rotating group: "Say the order as you point."
**Card 2, a strip of three squares and one card:** yesterday, today (the marker on it) and tomorrow, and a card of a pretend class sing-along planned for tomorrow. "Where does the card go?"

## Day 35

**Record card:** a pretend dated record of a school gym, with a calendar strip and today's marker. Behind the marker, far back, is a card of a climbing rope hung long ago; just behind the marker is a card of a gym class last week; ahead is a card of a new floor mat coming next week. Each child circles what happened long ago and puts a token on what comes next week. Spoken, rotating group: "How do you know the mat is next week?"

## Day 36

**Text read aloud, then three cards shuffled:** "Cooks wash the beans. Cooks boil the beans. Cooks put the beans in a bowl." Cards: washing, boiling, the bowl. Each child puts the cards in order on the strip, left to right. Spoken, rotating group: "What happened first?" and "What happened last?"

## Key (teacher)

34: caterpillar one dot, chrysalis two, butterfly three; the card goes on the tomorrow square, after today. 35: circle the rope (far behind, not the gym class just behind the marker); token on the mat; because it is after today on the calendar. 36: washing, boiling, bowl, left to right; first is washing, last is the bowl.
