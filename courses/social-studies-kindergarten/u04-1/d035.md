# Day 35: Now, then, before, after

## Teacher page

### Before class

Add week, month and year rows to the calendar. Hide exit card 35. Pick today's six. Teacher key: key-sheet-room9-strip.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen and hand-back

Hand-back from day 34: s04-X34a (error A), s04-X34b and s04-X34c (error H). Moss puts the biggest seed card first; the class fixes it with a new set, earlier on the left.

#### 7 min. Teaching first

Every time word is measured from the marker. Now is the marker; then is any card behind it. Review: before and after, from unit 2, place any two events. Use real class events, one last week, one next week. Show the month and year rows, last and next month and year. Long ago is a long time before this year. Left to right is our way; some home languages run the other way.

Wren puts next week's card at "long ago", because it is far away on the page (error B).

**Ask.** Before or after today? How do you know? Expect: after; it is past the marker.

#### 9 min. Supported work

Pairs set the four Room 9 cards, a pretend class, on a strip, then say "The ___ is ___." Hear today's six, 80 seconds each, with exit card 35: "How do you know the mat is next week?" ([[s04-X35c]]).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Say "this is a pretend class." Each child has exit card 35. Read: "Circle what happened long ago" ([[s04-X35a]]); "Put a token on what comes next week" ([[s04-X35b]]).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: use buttons. No label cards: draw them.

**Skip.** The Room 9 pairs.

- **5 min. Today's marker.** Lay a strip with a button as today. Put a toy behind it and one ahead.
  **Say.** "Which is before today? Which is after?" Expect: behind, before; ahead, after.
- **5 min. Far and near.** Put a block far behind today.
  **Say.** "Is this last week or long ago?" Expect: long ago, it is far behind.
- **5 min. Again.** Move the toys; say before and after.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X35a]] [[s04-X35b]] [[s04-X35c]] Draw a rope far behind a marker, a gym class just behind it, and a mat ahead. Your child circles long ago and taps next week.
  **Say.** "How do you know the mat is next week?" Expect: it is after today.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: leave the year row.

### Words and supports

Now, then, long ago, before, after, last week, next week. Frame: "The ___ is ___."

### Tomorrow
