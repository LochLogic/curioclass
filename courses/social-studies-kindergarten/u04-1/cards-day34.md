# Day 34 cards: the school day, a plant growing, a coat, and the calendar

Large picture cards with thick outlines, plain line drawings, no writing except "invented for practice" along the bottom edge of any card that shows a made-up scene. No brands. The teacher reads every label aloud. These are the teaching cards of day 34 (rule 4): the day 34 exit uses other pictures that appear nowhere on this page.

## The school day cards

Three cards, always laid left to right: **morning** (the class hanging coats and sitting in a circle, a sun low in a window), **afternoon** (the class outside in strong light, the sun high), **night** (the school building alone under a moon, no one in it). The night card is the school under a moon and never a bedroom. Model: "First morning, next afternoon, last night." Moss puts the night card first and the class says what happens after the afternoon.

## The seed cards

Three cards: **seed** (one seed in soil), **sprout** (a small green shoot with two leaves), **plant** (a taller plant with several leaves). Model: "First the seed, next the sprout, last the plant." Moss puts the big plant card last and says "because it is the biggest" (error A). The class sets the cards in the growth order: each card comes after the one before it. A seed becomes a sprout and then a young plant; nothing else about seeds is claimed.

## The coat cards (pairs)

One set for each pair, three cards: **get the coat**, **put arms in**, **zip it up**. Pairs order them and say "First ..., next ..., last ...". A pair that finishes shuffles the cards and orders them again.

## The calendar and the paper strip

On the class calendar, move a ridged today marker one square and put one real class card on **yesterday**, **today** and **tomorrow**. Then each pair gets a paper strip with three squares (yesterday, today, tomorrow) and one real class card, such as "read a book together", to set on the strip. Real class events only, never an event from home.

## Key (teacher)

School day: morning, afternoon, night. Seeds: seed, sprout, plant. Coat: get the coat, arms in, zip it up. Calendar: yesterday is before today, tomorrow is after.
