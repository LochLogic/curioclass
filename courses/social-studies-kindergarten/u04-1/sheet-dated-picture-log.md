# The dated picture log (day 36, practice only)

A paper log for each child, with a calendar page showing today's date and a today marker. Five sets of invented events, printed "invented for practice, not what our class did", each with its own label cards. The teacher reads every label aloud. The events are far from every exit and end case. This first log is practice and is not scored: it is kept for written feedback and revised on day 38.

## The five sets

1. **A class day:** morning (the class in a circle), afternoon (outside play), night (the school under a moon). Labels: morning, afternoon, night.
2. **Brushes:** brushes washed the day before, drying that day, put away the day after. Labels: yesterday, today, tomorrow.
3. **A shelf:** a new class shelf set up a week earlier, and a visitor planned a week later. Labels: last week, next week.
4. **A plant:** seeds planted a month earlier and a drawing of the plant planned a month later. Labels: last month, next month.
5. **A tree:** a tree planted a year earlier and another tree planned a year later. Labels: last year, next year.

## How it is used

Each child circles the label card for each event, about 13 circles in all, and says or points to which one is happening now and which happened then. The teacher hears the rotating six one at a time, a minute each ("Which one is happening now?"), and notes their time words. After class the teacher writes feedback on each log: one relation to revisit, drawn as a small picture pair. It is read aloud to each child on day 38, and the child moves or re-circles that relation. Only the revised log is scored for item 2.
