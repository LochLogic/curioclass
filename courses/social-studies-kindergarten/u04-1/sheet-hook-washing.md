# The hook drawings: a washboard and a washing machine

Two original drawings, large, on separate sheets, plain line, thick outlines. Printed "drawing by Curio Class" along the bottom edge. They are drawings we made, not photographs and not old objects.

## Drawing 1: a washboard

A flat board with ridges across it, sitting in a tub, with a cloth shirt laid over the ridges and two hands rubbing it. No face is shown.

## Drawing 2: a washing machine

A box with a round door and a dial, a shirt visible through the door. No brand, no name, no control labels.

## What the teacher says

"Both help wash clothes. What might change about the work?" Ask once, write whatever children offer on the wonder wall, and let any child pass. Nothing is answered today, and nobody is asked about washing at home. The same two drawings return on day 37 as the first row of the then-and-now chart, with the shared purpose written beside them: both help wash clothes.
