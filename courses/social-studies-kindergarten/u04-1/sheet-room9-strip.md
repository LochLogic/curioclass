# Room 9: the strip with a today marker (day 35)

Room 9 is a pretend class, said aloud as made up. Four large cards, printed "invented for practice" along the bottom edge, and one blank strip for each pair with a ridged today marker.

## The four cards

1. **A puppet stage built long ago.** A small wooden stage with a curtain rail and dusty corners.
2. **A rehearsal last week.** Three paper puppets in front of the stage.
3. **A puppet show next week.** The same stage with chairs set out in rows.
4. **A new curtain next month.** A bright curtain on a hanger.

## How the pairs use it

Each pair sets the strip with the today marker in the middle and places each card against the marker: behind it for the stage and the rehearsal (the stage is far behind, the rehearsal is near behind), ahead of it for the show and the curtain (the show is near ahead, the curtain is far ahead). Each pair then says one sentence: "The ___ is ___." The teacher hears the rotating six one at a time, about 80 seconds each. Wren shows error B by putting the curtain card on "long ago" because it is far away on the page; the class points to today's marker and moves the card.
