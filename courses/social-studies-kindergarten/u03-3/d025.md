# Day 25: A model and a graph of what we saw

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the tray, the blocks and key cards, and a graph card each. Key: key-exits-24-26.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Review: count aloud (unit 2). Count the patch, trees then rocks. Mark day 24's s03-X24. Error C, hold a symbol beside the real place; for error H, trace a symbol to the key.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Three minutes: graph the tray, three shells, no feathers: one picture for each, a labeled place for none. Puppet: four marks for three shells (error L); count them. Three: your reading-corner model, a key card for each block: "this block stands for the shelf."

**Ask.** What did we see? How many? Expect: the counts.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Each child builds a model of two or three seen things and fills a graph card with the trees and rocks counted. Blue paper or clay only for a pond or hill seen. Puppet: "Add a pond, every playground has one" (error D); draw only what we saw. Both are practice. Hear today's six: "How do you know that block is the tree?" (s03-X25b)

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 25 ("a pretend card"); all circle the tree block, then mark each cup on the empty graph (s03-X25, s03-X25c); the six say why spoons get none (s03-X25d). "A real tree" is error C; "the nicest block," D; "because it is," G; the cup row, L. Tonight write feedback.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No blocks: use cups or paper squares. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The patch, the puppet and the class record.

- **5 min. Graph.** Put out three spoons and no forks. Draw two rows, a spoon and a fork, one mark for each spoon. The fork row is labeled and empty.
  **Say.** "How many spoons? What does the empty row mean?" Expect: three; none.
- **5 min. Model.** Build two things from a window or a table; a block stands for each.
  **Say.** "What does this block stand for?" Expect: the thing in that place, because it sits where it is.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X25]] [[s03-X25b]] [[s03-X25c]] [[s03-X25d]] Draw four cups and an empty graph with a cup row and a spoon row. Your child makes a mark for each cup.
  **Say.** "Why does the spoon row stay empty?" Expect: four cup marks; no spoons, so no marks.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: model or graph; hear six. Longer: a third seen thing.

### Words and supports

Model, graph, zero, hill, pond. Frame: "I counted ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 26 opens with s03-X25; for errors C, D, G and L: a block is a small version, draw what we saw, a detail after because, fix one row.
