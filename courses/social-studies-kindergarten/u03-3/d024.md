# Day 24: A small picture stands for a place

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang wonder card 4. Set the photograph beside the map.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Read card 4. Mark day 23's s03-X23. For errors B and I, set a changed page place beside an unchanged place, and a world map beside a room map.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Who took the photograph, when, why? (You did, on the date on its back.) Name two things the map leaves out. Key: heart is the playground, hexagon the library, flag the park; trace symbol to key and back. Puppet: "I can go down the slide on this heart" (error C): it stands for the playground, as the globe stands for Earth. Puppet: "The hexagon is a tree" (error H): trace to the key. Review the claim (unit 1): "The library is next to the playground because the hexagon touches the heart."

**Ask.** What does the map leave out? Expect: two things the photograph shows.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Pairs place a symbol card, trace to the key and back, and say what is next to it: "The ___ is next to the ___." Hear today's six, 50 seconds each; each adds a new position word. Others draw the playground and what is next to it.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 24 ("a pretend card"); all point to what is beside the shed; mark s03-X24. The six say what it is. "You can go in the shed" is error C; "a kite" without the key is error H.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture map: draw a table from above with a key box beside it. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The photograph and the class record.

- **5 min. A key.** Draw a cup, a plate and a spoon from above, each as a small shape. Beside it, a key box: each shape and a tiny picture of the real thing.
  **Say.** "What does this shape stand for?" Expect: the cup, because the key says so.
- **5 min. Beside.** Ask what is next to the plate.
  **Say.** "How do you know?" Expect: "The key says the circle is the cup, and it touches the plate."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X24]] Draw a new key with two shapes. Your child circles what is beside one and says how they know.
  **Say.** "Use the key. What is beside it?" Expect: goes from the shape to the key and back.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one place. Longer: a second "because".

### Words and supports

Photograph, symbol, key. Frame: "The ___ is next to the ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 25 opens with s03-X24; for errors C and H, hold a symbol beside the real place, and trace a new symbol to the key.
