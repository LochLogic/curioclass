# Day 26: Draw a map with a key

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put out each child's day 25 model, graph, card. Today's six: day 23's. Key: key-exits-24-26.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Reopen

Mark day 25's s03-X25. Hand out feedback cards; an adult reads each pair's. Error C: a block is a small version; D and G: a pretend pond beside what we saw, and a detail after because.

#### 6 min. Step back

Three minutes: your map with two alike key pictures, a dotted and a lined circle; which are unclear? Fix them. Three: review the key (day 24): "I can find the rug because the key picture matches this one."

**Ask.** How could someone find it? Expect: the key's picture, then its match on the map.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Four minutes: each child revises the model and graph from the card; these records are scored. Six: draw two features of a place from the class list on the page, each with a key picture; no home. Hear today's six: the claim, their revised "Both ___, but ___" (day 23), a place near school and what is next to it. Scribe "I can find it because ...".

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Show exit card 26 ("a pretend card"); all circle the swing in the key and point to it on the map; mark s03-X26. The six say how to find it. "The triangle" is error H; "because it is there," G. Tonight write a line on each map.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: draw a border and a key box on paper. No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The feedback cards and the class record.

- **5 min. A map.** Draw a place you both know, a park or a library, with two things in it where they are. Draw a key box with a tiny picture for each.
  **Say.** "How could someone find this one?" Expect: the key's picture, then the matching picture on the map.
- **5 min. Fix.** Make two key pictures alike. Change one.
  **Say.** "Which two are hard to tell apart?" Expect: the two alike; change one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X26]] Draw a map with a key of a swing and a fence, and a triangle that is not in the key. Your child circles the swing in the key.
  **Say.** "How could someone find the swing?" Expect: the key's swing, then the matching picture beside the fence.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one feature. Longer: a third key picture.

### Words and supports

Map, key, symbol. Frame: "I can find it because ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens with s03-X26; for errors H and G, trace a new symbol to the key, then name a different detail after because. Then the school's address: children say street and town; you name the state.
