# Key: exit cards 24 to 26

Teacher only. Keep out of the room until the cards are collected. Never print on a child's card.

## Key (teacher)

Card 24: the diamond, the flower bed beside the shed; the child goes from the symbol to the key and back. "The diamond is a kite" with no look at the key is error H; "the rectangle is a real shed, you can go in it" is error C.

Card 25: the left block is the tree, because it is in the tree's place, at the left beside the window. The wrong row is the spoon row: it has a mark but there are no spoons, and zero is a labeled row with no marks (error L). "That block is a real tree" is error C; "I picked the nicest block" is error D; "because it is" is error G.

Card 26: the key's swing picture, then the matching picture on the map beside the fence. The triangle is not in the key. "The triangle, because it looks like a swing" is error H; "you find it because it is there" is error G.
