# Section 3.3 opener

## Comparing accounts and tracing support

Keep the event fixed on day 96. Move from one model branch on 97 to a movable reason on 98 and independent evidence choice on 100. Day 101 uses the reserved print and heard texts.

A point is an idea an author wants readers to accept. A reason explains why the reader should accept it. Evidence supplies a detail that supports the reasoning. Review how a detail can be trustworthy and still be irrelevant to a particular point. Distinguish the hearing from the modern explanation. Teach introductory commas and work-title marking on released examples. Choose model material away from the held cases.

When pupils treat account dates as different events, separate the writing date from the event. When a detail goes under the wrong point, test the connection in one branch. When a heard summary gives only the topic, identify the speaker's point, then add its reason and evidence. Use one Return priority for shared repair and return other cues on paper. Keep the day 101 script on the teacher copy and offer both reads to everyone.

Print the account chart, blank argument map, source slips and the vocabulary record. Use the registered day 99 talk or released 01 lines 220 and 270. Use the C leaves only during the check. Preview the political setting and unequal treatment in transport, work and service. Keep attributions to the modern writer clear.
