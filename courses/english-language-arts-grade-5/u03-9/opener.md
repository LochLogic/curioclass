# Section 3.9 opener

## Which evidence is ready to verify?

Use the writing-process model to review how writers retain dated stages and respond to a particular note. Look for the first independent work, written feedback and the independently corrected version. Keep the pupil’s source trace available. A display feature can make a relationship clearer, and prior writing can explain what a reader needs. Verify the criterion from the relevant saved work and its recorded support.

Use the criterion checklist once the eligible work is complete. Keep the new proposed change and new display work unscored. On 122 locate the corrected opinion and caption in the folio and verify the existing feature and explanation. Mark other components from their separate records after class. Preserve the date and source of each decision. Use the class dictionary if the registered water word card is unavailable.

On 123 score groups 4 to 6 once from the protected, corrected 120 recordings. Use the group and individual coding in the rehearsal record. Leave new live work diagnostic. Use the closing evidence record and retain feedback or an unfinished date. During Response use one five-minute block for each leading priority, up to four, then provide five minutes for everyone’s independent response. Keep missing digital or speaking evidence dated for a real later opportunity.

Print the checklist, closing evidence record and written feedback slips. Preview released work that names racism, exclusion, disability or death. Give private participation options. Keep folios and recordings in restricted teacher storage and use only released passages when responding or extending.
