# Opening statement on western drought

My written testimony goes into more specifics on the actions in which we manage our 189 projects, but you will see a similar fact pattern in every major river basin--hydrologic variability, hotter temperatures leading to earlier snow melt, dry soils, all translating into earlier and low runoff. This is coupled, as the Committee has mentioned, with the lowest reservoir levels on record. There is so much to this that is unprecedented, and that is true, but unprecedented is now the reality and the normal in which Reclamation must manage our systems. A warmer, drier West is what we are seeing today. Reclamation is responding basin by basin and engaging in actions to mitigate the impacts of a drying landscape.
