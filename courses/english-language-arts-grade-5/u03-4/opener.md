# Section 3.4 opener

## From sources to an opinion

Begin with one shared source-to-reason connection. Assign the leadership opinion on day 102, hold the discussion on 103, and study one model component at a time on 104. Keep the pupil's own map and beginning in the folder on days 105 and 106. Use separate exit leaves.

Review the paragraph frame by keeping supporting information beside the idea it explains. **Consequently** introduces a result, and **moreover** adds a related point. Place a precise fact by the reason it supports. A new reader needs enough context to understand the position. Give one written cue that helps a pupil make that context clearer. Keep completed answer arrangements off all practice sheets.

Use the discussion moves chart again. Mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link, and explain what became clearer. On 103 prioritize two groups still unheard from 93. Return feedback for a fresh contribution and keep names for 120. For 105 transfer, hear a whole turn privately before dictating. With one listener, rotate five, 18 and seven pupils through Words, Choice reading and Evidence. Reserve the first reading and the spellings.

Use released print copies, the idea-to-source chart, argument map, opinion folio, second word card and numbered dictation lines. If the opinion model is unavailable, voice the planned excerpt-based example from released 01 lines 34 to 38. Preview the treatment described in the sources, allow private passes, and keep pupils' experiences private.
