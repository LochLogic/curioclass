# Comparing adventure narrators

## Reading and modeling

A perspective is a way of seeing or thinking. Review the story map and ask what each teller lets a reader know. A first-person teller can share a private reaction. A close outside teller may report a particular person's thoughts. A theme is a message supported by events and details. Compare conversation and reporting by audience and purpose. Treat both registers respectfully.

## Before class

Prepare the released parts of both stories, story maps, route sketch and plain speech and quotation records. Keep later target spans folded and the complete C companions sealed until day 58. Preview Isa's brief separation and safe reunion privately. Provide a different released reading and leave unmatched cold evidence pending. Do not reenact separation or climb.

If a reader offers only a pronoun, ask how the teller affects which detail is available. If a reader names only a topic, ask what the different choices suggest. Keep private speaking turns dated and collect an individual paper trace from everyone.
