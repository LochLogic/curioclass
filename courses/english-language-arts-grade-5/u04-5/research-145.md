# Replacing the Transcanyon Waterline

## Background

### Why is the park replacing the waterline?

The park's September 2024 FAQ described the work needed after breaks. Fixing one break in a remote spot often cost more than $25,000. Crews could reach the repair sites inside the canyon only by trail or helicopter.
