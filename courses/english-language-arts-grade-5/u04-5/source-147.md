# Rain on a paved stretch

## Sources and Solutions: Stormwater

Rain can move across hard pavement as runoff. It may reach a storm drain or a stream. Green infrastructure can slow runoff and give rain time to soak into the ground. Some examples are rain gardens, barrels, green roofs and pervious pavement.

## Types of Green Infrastructure

Pervious pavement lets rain pass through openings into layers below. From there, water can soak into the ground where conditions allow.
