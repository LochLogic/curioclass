# Two reports about protecting a supply

## Current Water Restrictions FAQ

September 3, 2024. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Background: Where does the park get its water?

The South Rim got its water from Roaring Springs through the Transcanyon Waterline.

## South Rim Water Conservation Measures

August 5, 2026. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Water conservation measures

Report and fix drips, leaks and other places where water is being lost.
