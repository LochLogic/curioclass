## Current Water Restrictions FAQ

September 3, 2024. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Background

#### What is the Transcanyon Waterline (TCWL)?

The waterline supplies drinking water to South Rim facilities and facilities inside the canyon.

## South Rim Water Conservation Measures

August 5, 2026. Grand Canyon National Park.

### News release

The park put South Rim water-saving measures in place after a break in the Transcanyon Waterline. The break stopped crews from pumping water to the South Rim.
