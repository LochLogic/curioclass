# Two dated refill reports

## Current Water Restrictions FAQ

September 3, 2024. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Camping

The water taps at Mather and Desert View campgrounds were to stay off. Visitors could get water at Mather Campground's check-in kiosk.

## South Rim Water Conservation Measures

August 5, 2026. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Locations without drinking water

The notice listed these places after the break.

- Manzanita Rest Area
- Cottonwood Campground
- Bright Angel Campground
- Delta Restrooms
- Boat Beach
