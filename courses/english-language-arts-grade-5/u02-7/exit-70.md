# Exit ticket 70

**Waiting Beneath the Leaves**

I even patted the bag, though the sleeve inside it felt nothing like a bottle. I had been so busy trying to tell what made the knock that I'd forgotten putting the bottle on the seat. Now that Dani had pointed, I could see the lid under the bench. A thin line of water crept toward it along the floor.

It was lying under the bench, where it must have rolled when I took out the sleeve. I stepped back to get it. A strap on my bag snagged on the corner of the seat. By the time I pulled it free, the last bright shirt had disappeared around the bend.
