# Independent response cards

*Invented for practice.*

## Which detail fits?

A group needs a flat surface for spreading out a map.

A. The shelf tips toward the wall.

B. The table has a wide, level top.

## Which idea is bigger?

A soft case protects the model. A stand keeps it upright.

A. The soft case is blue.

B. Two supplies keep the model safe.

## Which word signals a contrast?

A wide bench seats four. A narrow bench seats two.

A. however

B. moreover

## Which opening needs a comma?

A. At noon we tried again.

B. We tried again at noon.
