# Rewrite, record and verify the work

## What to carry

Keep revised work with its dated drafts and written cues. Use the saved word and reading records. Gather only a missing component. Use the unit’s 0, 1 and 2 marking scale. Leave an unobserved component pending and note the next opportunity.

## Timing and privacy

Return the day 38 reading feedback for independent correction. Return the program note for a separate revision. On day 40, collect the silent passage and response before reading the talk. Show only its chart. Store audio locally under school rules or use the same record for live evidence.

Offer a familiar and an unfamiliar audience. If the second audience cannot attend, use two audience-specific pieces with live questions. Give each child a new assigned speech for private preparation. A student may pass on public talk and use the private exchange. Keep discussion on fictional choices.

Collect independent editing before feedback. Put language, discussion and reading evidence in separate rows of the same record. If a passage copy is missing, restore its exact filed version. Keep a component pending when the source or needed access is unavailable. Hear pending private turns during day 42 conferences.

## Lay out

Revised folders, sealed comparison leaves, silent reading leaf, listening chart, audio or live record, preparation slips, language passage and spelling strip.
