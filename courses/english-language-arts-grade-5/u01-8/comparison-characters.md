# Sweet Corn

After a few minutes a message came from Kira. Next time she wanted a signal to use when she needed someone to cover her part, because in the corn she had to look hard to see people on the other side of two tall rows.

"We stopped in the shade when she felt sick," Jo said. "Devin covered their row until we got a look at the rest of it. I drove us back early."
