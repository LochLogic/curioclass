# Discussion role cards

Cut apart the cards. Switch roles in the next round.

| Role | What I do |
|---|---|
| Facilitator | Invite a person who has yet to speak. |
| Evidence finder | Find the line the group needs. |
| Questioner | Ask about one idea you heard. |
| Connector | Link two ideas people shared. |
| Summarizer | Name the ideas the group will carry forward. |
