# Cumulative word check forms

Teacher copy. Show only the reading word and its sentence during the private reading turn. Cover them before dictation. Run five turns at each of six stations. Give each reader up to 30 seconds and each changeover up to ten seconds. Save the first response, then use the five-minute regroup and retry. Use Form B for an absent pupil. Use another form if the class already read a listed word in this skill.

## Form A

| Word on the reading card | Sentence on the reading card |
|---|---|
| collection | The collection grew after the class found more notes. |
| revision | This revision moves the question to the top. |
| activity | The activity begins with a quiet reading. |
| partial | A partial list leaves out some names. |
| location | The location appears beside the title. |

Dictate the next two words on the numbered slip. Say each word, read its sentence, then say the word again.

1. **Selection.** We made a selection after looking at all the choices.
2. **Division.** The division separated the upper and lower shelves.

## Form B

| Word on the reading card | Sentence on the reading card |
|---|---|
| inspection | The inspection started after the room closed. |
| invention | The invention kept the flap open. |
| visibility | The fog reduced visibility near the field. |
| official | The official notice appeared on the board. |
| decision | The decision came after a second look. |

Dictate:

1. **Prevention.** The cover helps with the prevention of spills.
2. **Expansion.** The expansion added an extra shelf.

Treat word reading and spelling as separate evidence. Look for sounds, syllables and parts before meaning. Review the pattern on a different released word. File diagnostic notes without a unit component mark.
