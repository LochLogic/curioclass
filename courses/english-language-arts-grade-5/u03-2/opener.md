# Section 3.2 opener

## What different records can show

Move from listening on day 90 to identifying the maker, time and purpose of an account. Withdraw the time line before the first unaided reading on day 94. On day 95 keep B silent first and B2 for the later heard and visual task.

Review **perspective** as a way of seeing or thinking, distinguished from the form of the narration. A firsthand source describes an experience of its maker. A secondhand source draws on records or reports of others. Use the maker and purpose chart to show that a game record can establish an action and result, while a later author can explain a longer sequence. Match each number to what it measures before giving it as evidence.

Keep the first word transfer private. With one listener on day 90, hear five turns during Words, 18 during Choice reading and seven during quiet Reading. The four brief reads share one 45-second turn per pupil. Dictate after the last unaided read. Keep numbered blanks separate from print. Observe two discussion groups in two rounds on day 93. Carry the other names forward to the later listening tally. Give a private opportunity after a public pass. Return written cues and retain the first attempt.

Print only the released account leaves, the chart, discussion and transfer forms, the completion slip and the reserved B2 display. Preview abuse, exclusion and the trial without slurs or reenactment. Use local charts when earlier sourcing work is unavailable. Read B2 live if playback is unavailable.
