# Parts of a literary essay

## Observation and focus

A sign-up form can tell you what a place values.
Ed and Maren both want people to claim what they leave.

## Ed’s concern

Ed asks, “How would we tell somebody their time was up?”
He wants to reach people when their time ends.

## Maren’s change

She writes “how we can reach you.”
Her line asks for a way to contact someone.

## Link

Ed asks about time, while Maren changes how people can be reached.

## Closing fragment

A way to reach each person serves the form’s purpose.

Source: *The Next Harvest*, “Broccoli.”
