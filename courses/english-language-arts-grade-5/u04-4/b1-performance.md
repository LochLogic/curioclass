# Two Readings performance

Teacher only. Read live when needed.

**Recording score.** Read this excerpt in an even voice and give each group three steady pulses. Pause for two beats after each of the two spoken measurements. Keep the repeated measurement phrases at the same pace.

> “Two inches,” she said. She lifted the ruler, wiped it, and lowered it again.
>
> “Two inches again,” she said. Leena wrote, “The water in the pail was two inches deep.”
