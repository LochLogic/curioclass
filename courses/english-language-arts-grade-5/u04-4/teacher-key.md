## s04-A140A

Independent check. Illustration and recording score, both measurements.

Ruler placement in the pail clarifies what Leena measured. Pale dawn light makes the picture calm. Parallel marks give the image balance. In audio, two pauses clarify the repeated checks, the even voice suggests patience, and steady pulses make rhythm. Require one detail with each effect.

- Error I. There is a pail, a ruler and a tube.

- Error J. She washed the ruler and lowered it.
