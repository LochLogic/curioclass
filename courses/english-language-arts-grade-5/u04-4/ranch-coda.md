# Below the Bend

On the morning the trailer came, he stood to one side of the gate with Mara. The cattle that were leaving filed up the ramp. A red calf paused with its feet on the wood. Eli had watched it learn to walk in the upper pasture, and he had been hoping all morning for one last look at it. It took two more steps and went in.

After the trailer pulled away, Mara said, “Want to come check the other trough?”

He went with her. The cattle that stayed were lying together in the shade by the bale they'd rolled out of the way. Their ears turned at the sound of the gate. Up by the shed, the silver strip still showed below the bend. Eli could see where the strip widened now, and where the sharp little crease in the light was. He put his hand on the trough and felt the faint movement of the water as a cow stood and came over to drink.
