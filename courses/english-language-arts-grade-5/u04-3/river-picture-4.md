# Along One River

**Nia, at the gate again**

We went back to the gate.

Grandpa spread the paper on a flat stone. My line stopped where the corner had torn away. He put one of Sal's pencils beside it. I started at the tear and copied the curve Ren had shown me. There was room for the thin line that Ren's sister had drawn, if I made it right at the edge.

The gate clicked. I could see water moving below it in a narrow strip. I held the paper flat while the others drew. Every time somebody lifted a hand, the paper tried to fold shut. When we took it home, we carried it open.
