# Day 139 live performance

Teacher only. Use the following cues after the two endings are released. Read the available lines through before applying the performance cues.

In Rain Ledger, begin with a steady beat. Leave a short silence before the first drop. Tap once, then tap four times at “Then four.” Show the blank frame first and the frame with dots at the ending.

In Waiting at the Gate, hold the image still. Read evenly. On the final line, slow down and pause before “gate.” Keep that pause on a repeat.
