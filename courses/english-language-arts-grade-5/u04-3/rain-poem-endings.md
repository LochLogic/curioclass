# Two poem endings

## Rain Ledger

At dawn I open my book.  
The page for rain is white.  
In the column marked *received*  
I can write only dust.

This morning a drop strikes the tin.  
One silver coin. Then four.  
I carry the book to my brother.  
He holds the cover while I write.

## Waiting at the Gate

We wait beside the gate.  
The wheel is cold against my palm.  
A leaf comes down the empty ditch,  
sailing on its edge.

My aunt says, Give it time.  
I listen for the ripple  
that used to reach the bend  
before we could see it.

The leaf falls on its side.  
My aunt takes her hand from the wheel.  
Behind us, the pale rows curve.  
We wait beside the gate.
