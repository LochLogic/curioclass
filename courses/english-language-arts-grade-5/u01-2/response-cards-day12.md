# Discussion and source response cards

*Invented for practice.*

### Cause

A flag was too low to see. Lina moved it higher.

Draw an arrow from the problem to the change.

### Evidence

Mo pulled the shade down. She moved a bright lamp closer to the book.

Mark the detail that could support “Mo still needed light.”

### Prepare

Line A. The group moved its model into the sun.

Line B. Sam brought a pencil.

Write the letter for the line about the model.

### Role and turn

Circle your agreed role.

Reader    Questioner    Summarizer

### Reply and conclusion

A partner says the small sign fits best.

Write one question about the partner's reason.

### Conclusion

We chose to set the poster by the window. A partner said its writing was hard to see.

Write a next step that uses that idea.
