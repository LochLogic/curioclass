# Response card keys

Use after the check responses have been collected. Give a fresh retry by component and keep this key with the teacher.

## Day 9

- Scope. The studio opens to all walkers. The brushes are for members.
- Two ideas. The first pair concerns places for belongings. The second concerns opportunities for discussion.
- Nearby clue. “Two carts could not pass side by side” shows limited width.
- Word part. Repack and repaint start with re-. Unpack starts with un-.
- Check a claim. The note identifies the color. The plain sketch does not show it.
- Entry. The first syllable in STED-ee is stressed.

## Day 12

- Cause. The low flag was difficult to see, so Lina moved it higher.
- Evidence. Moving a bright lamp closer to the book supports the claim about needing light.
- Prepare. Line A gives the model detail.
- Role and turn. The reader, questioner or summarizer matches the student's agreed role.
- Reply. Accept a question tied to the partner's reason, such as “Where does the smaller sign fit?”
- Conclusion. Test the poster from the reader's position or make its writing easier to see.

## Day 22

- Exact words. Circle “once”; the source says “twice.”
- Topic and message. “Sharing a task can help solve a problem.”
- Figure. “Like a wide ribbon” helps picture the shape.
- Second sense. In the first sentence, bank is land beside water.
- Evidence. The lamp near the open book supports the possibility of reading when light is needed.
- Second detail. Lee holds the pages flat. Ava clips the loose corner.
