# Explaining sensory language

## Reading and drafting

Review quotation as source words copied exactly inside paired marks. A category is a group of things that go together. Show how an explanation connects a quotation to a category. Use the workshop model and separate released lines. Leave pupils' own introductions and paragraphs as unscored folder work until their feedback cycle.

## Before class

Set out evidence strips, category folds and lined paper. Keep the day 60 story, private word probe and language record apart. Collect the unaided story record before discussing it. If the story is unavailable, use approved independent reading and leave the assigned evidence pending. If the workshop model is unavailable, inspect a released old essay or arrange headings over notes.

If a quotation stands alone, ask what it helps the reader picture. Test figurative meaning against context. When sentences are combined, check whether both original ideas survived. Fade the sentence frame after day 61. Date actual oral turns and preserve the support history.
