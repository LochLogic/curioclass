# Reading forest relationships

## Reading and modeling

An observation tells what someone saw or heard. A possible explanation earns its place through evidence. Review evidence as the line or page that shows an idea is true. Keep the fictional team's records separate from the published experiment. Time order traces a sequence. Cause and effect links a change to what it changes.

## Before class

Send the bilingual family note on day 42. Prepare the separate packet leaves, feeding picture, forest layers page, note sheet, light and cover, and matching reference formats. Separate the daily exit leaves and keep later evidence folded. Draw the released diagram if needed. Preview animal death and hurricane damage privately. Offer the ordinary gap or quiet plot as an alternative. Keep climbing and animal handling out of the lesson.

Review partner rereading with phrasing and expression. Listen for meaning and a check from the letters. If a summary gives only a topic, sort released details beneath two ideas. If one signal word drives the structure label, sketch the paragraph's whole arrangement. If an arrow points backward, trace where the food goes. Return one written cue to every child.
