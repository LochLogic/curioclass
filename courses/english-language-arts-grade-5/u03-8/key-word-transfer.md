# Day 120 word transfer key

Confirm unfamiliarity privately before the individual turn. Record any word the pupil already knows and retain the other usable evidence. Arrange a private fresh observation if both words are already familiar. At each turn listen to both isolated words and both sentences. Use no context cue until the isolated read is collected. For dictation say the number and isolated word twice with the reading leaf closed. Allow 75 seconds for both words.

| Slip | Say | Look for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | multilingual | A complete blend and spelling; multi- means many and lingual relates to language. |
| 2 | reconstructive | A complete blend and spelling; re- means again, struct relates to building, and -ive forms an adjective here. |

Give a short written cue about the specific missed part. File the original, cue and independently corrected form separately. A learner can describe a valid sound, syllable or meaningful-part strategy in their own words.
