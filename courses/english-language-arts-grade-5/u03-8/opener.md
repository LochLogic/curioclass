# Section 3.8 opener

## Showing listeners where the evidence leads

Review reader, purpose and form using the rhetorical-situation model. Review audience as the listeners a reader reads for, then apply it to speech and a display. Offer one helpful piece of context in a released baseball claim. Use a different released position label on day 119. Later, ask pupils what a visitor needs, where to pause for a meaningful phrase, and what a display helps explain. Review how the discussion moves chart helps pupils mark a detail, listen, ask for detail, link and explain what became clearer.

On 118 issue the sealed D reading leaves and response sheet. Hold the D listening script to read twice. Keep cold reading and hearing separate. Model the audience change only in the unscored planning block. On 119 use the partner rereading feedback card and mark which features were actually heard. Keep caption, display and feedback versions separate. Make one kind of label visible away from the held exit source. Save the pupil’s caption and explanation for independent revision.

On 120 give the private word reading prompts only during the probe. Close the prompts for the numbered dictation slip. Keep the word-transfer key in Before class for the teacher. Record original responses, written cue and independent correction. Store the rehearsal recordings and student identification in restricted teacher storage. Allow six concurrent managed groups or use the single-recorder rotation with dated handovers. When audio is unavailable, keep paper and live evidence and schedule a later protected recording. Paper records the parts actually shown. Use a private option for talk. Preview racial exclusion, disability, polio, speech mockery and the fatal crash. Keep imitation and personal disclosures out of the work.

On 121 hear the corrected recordings of groups 1 to 3. Use each individual voice, visible feature and prior chart with its dated notes. Carry the remaining recordings and written cues to 123. Keep new live speaking diagnostic. Use the registered water word card or give class dictionary meanings orally.
