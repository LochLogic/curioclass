# Water Word Card

**Watershed**
An area of land where water drains to the same outlet.
Rain on both hills reaches the same stream because the hills share a watershed.

**Drought**
A stretch of unusually dry weather that causes water shortages or other water problems.
During the drought, little rain fell and the pond dropped below the dock.

**Reservoir**
A lake, pond, or basin that stores water.
The town holds water in a reservoir for use later.

**Usable**
Ready or suitable for a particular use.
The collected rainwater was usable for washing muddy tools.

**Freshwater**
Water with very little dissolved salt.
The map labels the inland lake as freshwater.

**Resource**
A supply that people or other living things can use.
The garden club uses collected rainwater as a resource for its plants.

**Distribution**
The way a supply is delivered or spread out.
The map shows water distribution through pipes to homes on three streets.

**Evidence**
A detail, record, or observation that supports an idea.
The lower marks on the gauge were evidence that the pond level had dropped.

## Teacher notes

Use the entries for word study. Read each example after identifying the word. The examples describe imagined places and observations.
