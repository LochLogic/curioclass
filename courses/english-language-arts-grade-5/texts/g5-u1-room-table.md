# Room at the Table

One piece short of finishing the river,  
I pressed a corner till it bent.  
I could almost see the water  
reach the lake.

I moved my bowl and spread the pieces out.  
A thin blue bend matched the broken bank.  
I turned the piece. It slipped into place.  
I let my soup cool a little.

## Use notes

Read aloud on day 36 and let students follow the verse. Label the speaker's first approach and the changed approach. Use details from both stanzas to compare possible themes about patience and to order summary details. On day 37, reread the familiar verse with a partner phrasing cue.
