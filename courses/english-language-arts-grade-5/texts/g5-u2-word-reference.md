# Forest and Story Word Cards

## Microclimate

**Say it** MY-kroh-KLY-mit.

**Meaning** The usual conditions in a small area, such as its light, warmth or dampness.

**Word part** Micro means small.

## Microhabitat

**Say it** MY-kroh-HAB-ih-tat.

**Meaning** A very small place where a living thing finds what it needs.

**Word part** Micro means small. A habitat is a living thing's surroundings.

## Plot

**Say it** PLOT. It rhymes with spot.

**Meaning in land study** A piece of ground set aside for a purpose.

**Meaning in a story** The connected events that make up the story.

## Reconstruct

**Say it** ree-kuhn-STRUKT.

**Meaning** To build something again.

**Word part** Re means again. Construct means build.
