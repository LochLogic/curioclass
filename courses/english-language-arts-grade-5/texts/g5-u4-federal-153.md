# A Supply by Truck

The South Rim had a water shortage in 2024, and visitors still needed water. The park had to decide whether to bring in water by truck. It weighed the cost against how much water visitors would need. Hiring enough trucks would cost more than the park could afford. Even with trucks on the road, deliveries could not keep up with demand. Together, those limits led the park to rule out trucking in water for visitors.
