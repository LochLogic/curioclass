# Waiting at the Marker and The Map Check

*Invented for practice.*

## C1. Waiting at the Marker

I had stopped beside the marked meeting place to tighten my shoe. When I looked up, the turn in the path hid my group. The leaves were a green curtain. I wanted to rush after them, but our guide had told us to stay at the marker if we became separated. I called and waited. Before I could call again, the guide came back around the turn. "You're here!" I said. At the shelter I wrote, "I remained at the designated meeting point until the guide returned." Waiting had felt like doing nothing. Now I knew it could be a choice that helped.

## C2. The Map Check

Leo held the route card while the adult survey leader checked the next marked turn. A parrot called somewhere beyond the leaves. Leo wanted to follow the sound. Instead, he asked, "Should we check the map first?" The leader nodded and pointed to the marked route. Leo stayed with the group. Later, he wrote, "The group followed the designated route during the observation." His careful question had kept a small excitement from becoming a separate journey. He still heard the bird, but he heard it beside the people he had come with.
