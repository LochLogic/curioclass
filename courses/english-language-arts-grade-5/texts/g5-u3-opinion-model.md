# Qualities in Action

## Opinion model

A biography of Jackie Robinson should make room for his mother, Mallie Robinson. Her choices helped give Jackie a place to grow up and a home there.

First, Mallie moved her family from Georgia to Pasadena, California. The biography records that she took all five of her children. The journey gave them a new place to grow up.

A move gives a family a place to start. Nevertheless, it takes steady work to make a life there. In Pasadena, Mallie earned money as a housekeeper. Her wages helped support her children after the trip was over.

The move began a new part of their lives. The work helped the family stay together there. Both choices deserve a place in the story of Jackie’s childhood.

## Writer’s notes

When I revise, I check what job each fact does. The number of children is a detail that shows who made the move with Mallie. The link between the reasons shows how getting there and staying there fit together. The word nevertheless marks the shift. The clause “Mallie earned money as a housekeeper” tells who did what. In a critique, a partner can ask how the conclusion joins the reasons.

## Teacher notes

On day 104, read with the released biography passage about the move and Mallie’s work. Compare what happens if the reasons switch places. On day 106, use individual released sentences and the writer’s notes for word study.
