# Unit 1 Glossary

## Day 4 lookup

**universal**  
yoo-nuh-VUR-suhl  
Adjective. Applies to everyone or everything in the group named.

The club held a universal vote. Every member could take part.
At the last meeting, only captains voted.

## Day 7 lookup

**arbitrarily**  
ar-buh-TRAIR-uh-lee  
Adverb. In a way that follows someone's preference without a fair reason.

The board set a meeting time arbitrarily. It picked a number without checking any schedules.

## Day 8 lookup

**entitled**  
in-TY-tuhld  
Adjective. Having the right to get or do something.

The rules say each player is entitled to one turn.

## Day 9 only

**residence**  
RES-i-dence  
Noun. A place where someone lives.

## Day 38 only

**livelihood**  
LIVE-li-hood  
Noun. Means of supporting life.
