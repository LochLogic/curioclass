# Keeping the Books Ready

*Invented for practice.*

Caring for shared books takes both protection and repair. Dry shelves and covers protect books from water and torn edges. When damage occurs, replacing a torn page or mending a loose binding can restore a usable book. Protection reduces damage; repair restores access after damage.
