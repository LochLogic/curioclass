# The Garden Storm and Two School Proposals

## C1. The Gardener

I wrote this on Tuesday, beside the school garden as a storm began. Rain struck the beds faster than I had ever seen. Water covered the seedlings. I think the storm explains the damage.

## C2. The Groundskeeper

I wrote this on Wednesday after inspecting those beds damaged in Tuesday’s storm. Leaves had blocked the drain, trapping water around the seedlings. Nearby beds with clear drains survived the same rain. I think drainage explains why the damage differed.

## C3. By the School Gate

The school should add a second bicycle rack by the school gate. At present twelve bikes share one rack, so riders lean the rest against the fence. It should also paint lines on the pavement by the same gate. Two cars stopped on the crossing last week. Lines would show drivers where children walk.

## C4. Teacher Read-Aloud: The Class Rabbit

Give the class rabbit a larger pen because it needs room to move. It bumped the walls three times in one morning. Also set a feeding schedule because meals must be steady. The water bowl sat empty on Monday afternoon.

## Teacher notes

Keep this set sealed until day 101. Give C1 to C3 in print. Keep C4 on the teacher copy and read it twice. Use the prompts and scoring anchors in section 3.3.
