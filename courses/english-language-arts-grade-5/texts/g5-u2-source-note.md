# Facts Behind My Story

## What I checked

1. El Yunque is in northeastern Puerto Rico. I used that location for the class map in my opening. (*Welcome to El Yunque*, welcome paragraph.)
2. El Yunque is a tropical rainforest. That fact led me to give the class a photograph of overlapping treetops. A forest with many kinds of life also gives a class a reason to learn about biodiversity. (*Welcome to El Yunque*, welcome paragraph.)
3. When the cover above plants opens, more sunlight can reach the plants below. I used that relationship when the narrator frames the bright leaves and the gap together. (*Forest’s Response to Hurricanes*, canopy and microclimate paragraphs, and the monitoring report, page 13.)

## What I invented

I invented Elena, the children and their exchange at the platform. The loose blue thread and the photograph on top of the pile came from my imagination. The photo shows a moment in my story. I would need more observations to say how long that patch stays bright.
