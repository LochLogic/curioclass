# Two Changes for the School Garden

*Invented for practice. The soil-tray and barrel-inspection results are invented for practice.*

The school garden should mulch bare beds because a covering slows evaporation. In paired soil trays, the mulched tray stayed damp longer. It should also cover its rain barrel because the lid blocks debris. The inspection found leaves in the open barrel but none in the covered one. Mulched soil can retain moisture: it stays damp while bare soil loses water.

## Teacher copy

For 129a, the damp tray supports mulching bare beds. The leaf inspection supports covering the barrel. For 129b, “it stays damp while bare soil loses water” supports “keep” or “hold.” Print only the label and the argument on the student copy.
