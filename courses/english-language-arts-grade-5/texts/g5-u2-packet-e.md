# Marking Study Places

*Invented for practice.*

## E1. Repeat visits

In March and again in August, a team measured how wet the soil was beside the same marked stones. Using the same stones let the team compare wet and dry months instead of mixing up a change of place with a change of weather.

## E2. Two spots

A spot under thick moss stayed damp, and a bare rock ledge dried quickly. Both lay along one trail, but the amount of water they held differed.

## E3. Covered markers

Fallen leaves sometimes covered the small markers beside the stones, so researchers could mistake one place for another. The team set short posts beside the path and drew a location map. It then checked each marker against the map before taking notes. This was a proposed answer to a record-keeping problem, not proof that every loss had stopped.
