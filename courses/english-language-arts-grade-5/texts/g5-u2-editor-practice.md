# At the Display Table

*Invented for practice.*

## At the display table

Nora lifted a stack of labels from the drying rack. She put them in order and carried them to the display table. A little space was left beneath the longest photograph. She slid the label into place. From the doorway a new reader could finally see where the picture had been taken.

## A quicker opening

After sorting the dry labels, Nora carried them to the display table.

## Teacher notes

The two openings are for comparing pace. For the device turn use the full narrative as the text to enter and reopen. Keep it separate from the scored pencil exchange and the pupil’s own revision.
