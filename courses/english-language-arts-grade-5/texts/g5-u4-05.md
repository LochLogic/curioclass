# Rain Ledger, Waiting at the Gate, and At the Pump

## Rain Ledger

At dawn I open my book.  
The page for rain is white.  
In the column marked *received*  
I can write only dust.

Yesterday the clouds carried sacks  
as dark as the bags of seed.  
I left a space for thunder.  
I left a line for rain.

This morning a drop strikes the tin.  
One silver coin. Then four.  
I carry the book to my brother.  
He holds the cover while I write.

## Waiting at the Gate

We wait beside the gate.  
The wheel is cold against my palm.  
A leaf comes down the empty ditch,  
sailing on its edge.

My aunt says, Give it time.  
I listen for the ripple  
that used to reach the bend  
before we could see it.

The leaf falls on its side.  
My aunt takes her hand from the wheel.  
Behind us, the pale rows curve.  
We wait beside the gate.

## At the Pump

*[At the gate in Along One River. Nia remains beside the map while Ren steps to a table where the water steward is taking notes.]*

**Nia:** Come on over when you're done. I'll save you a spot.

**Ren:** Good afternoon. Could you record both places on our map? This mark shows the gate. The next one marks the bridge.

**Water steward:** Certainly. Please tell me which mark shows where you measured the water.

**Ren:** The mark at the bridge. We can meet you there tomorrow.

*[Ren returns to the others and lifts one corner of the map.]*

**Ren:** Scoot over a bit, will you? I've got the rest of the story.

## Teacher performance score

Read each poem uninterrupted on the first hearing. On the second, begin *Rain Ledger* with a steady counting beat. Leave a brief silence before the single drop, then sound one quiet fingertip tap followed by four light taps at “Then four.” Show the frame of a blank ruled page first, then the same page with five small blue dots on one line. Read *Waiting at the Gate* at an even pace, then slow the last line and pause before “gate.” Keep the closed gate image still through that line. The two poems have different endings even when the same voice reads them. Save *At the Pump* for day 143.
