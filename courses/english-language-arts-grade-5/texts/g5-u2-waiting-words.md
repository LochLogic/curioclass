# Waiting Words

*Invented for practice.*

The page is blank beside my hand.  
I watch a drop become a line.  
I wait beneath the shelter roof  
and let the last wet branches shine.  
A hurried word might lose its place.  
A careful pause can keep it clear.  
I read the sentence once again  
and hear the meaning settle here.
