## Day 38 only

*Invented for practice.*

Supply this whole scene for the independent reading suite. Keep it out of advance reading, teaching and model answers. Readers infer what Mira notices and how her next choice changes. The language includes physical and figurative ways to open a door.

Mira balanced a box of donated books against her knee outside the town library. Her family had arrived only a week earlier. She wanted to prove that she could finish a job without anyone taking over. When Tomas opened the door and reached for the box, she turned away.

"I can manage," she said.

The door swung shut. With it closed, the lamp light at Mira's feet disappeared. Mira carried the box around to the side entrance, but that door was locked. Through its window she could see Tomas stacking empty shelves. He was not watching to see whether she failed. He was making space for the books.

She returned to the front. This time Tomas held the door open, but kept his hands at his sides.

"Would you like me to lend a hand, or just keep the door unlatched?"

Mira lowered the box. Her wrist ached, and the top book had slipped sideways. "Could you take this end?"

Together they carried it inside. Tomas did not choose her shelf for her. He asked where she wanted to begin. The open door had become a bridge: she could accept help and still decide where to go.
