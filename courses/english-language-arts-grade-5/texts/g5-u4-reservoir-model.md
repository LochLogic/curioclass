# A Report on Water Storage

This report concerns water storage. In 2021, Trujillo reported low levels at two reservoirs. Stored water behind the dams had fallen during the drought. The date matters when we describe those levels.

## Teacher copy

Read the four sentences as one announcement. Mark the focus, reported detail, explanation, and close. Let pairs reorder the same lines and explain the effect.
