# The Caption by the Door

*Invented for practice.*

Tomás turned the caption so a reader could see it from the doorway. It said that his class had made a collection of favorite sounds. Underneath, a visitor could write which sound they recognized first. At closing time the class found three slips by the display. Each one named the bell from the bicycle picture.

## Teacher notes

Use a neutral draft name in the managed classroom space. A practice comment may ask which caption readers are meant to look at. Leave the reply for the pupils. Keep the day 75 exit case closed.
