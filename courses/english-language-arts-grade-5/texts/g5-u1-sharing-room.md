# Sharing the Room

*Invented for practice.*

A shared reading room can support different readers in two ways. First, people need ways to reach the words: large print and a spoken version offer different routes to the same text. Second, readers need a say in the space. One group may choose quiet reading while another chooses discussion; agreeing on separate places lets both groups work. Accessible words alone do not settle how a room is shared, and a choice of places does not make small print readable.
