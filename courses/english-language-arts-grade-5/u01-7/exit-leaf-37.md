# Broccoli

There were two shelves that no one had asked about. One was too high for Leon to reach. The second was exactly high enough for him to look right into it. It held golf pencils, a folded flag and a neat pack of paper towels with its end open. Ed had things there that needed to stay out of the way while the room was used for meals. Maren told him about the paper towels. He found a roll that would fit at the low sink, where somebody could see what it was for and reach it with wet hands.
