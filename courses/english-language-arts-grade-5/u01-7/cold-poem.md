# A Place for a Voice

I thought my answer filled the room.  
I spoke before your words could grow.  
Your thought lay waiting like a seed,  
and mine was all I let us sow.

"Many hands make light work," I said,  
then heard what I had left unheard.  
I moved my chair and made a space;  
our work began with your first word.
