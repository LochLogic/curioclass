# Rights in Plain Words

Article 25 names essential needs for living, such as food, clothing, housing and medical care. It also describes security when a person loses a livelihood, including through unemployment or illness, and says mothers and children are owed special care. A livelihood is a means of supporting life. The article says all children should receive the same protection, whatever their parents' marital status.

The two ideas belong together: people need support for daily life, and losing work or becoming ill does not make those needs disappear. These are the declaration's stated protections. Readers can inspect these claims in the article.
