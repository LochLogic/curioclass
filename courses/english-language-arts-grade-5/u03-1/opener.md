# Section 3.1 opener

## Reading a life in its historical setting

Read the opening ceremony and the historical explainer on day 84. Move from a quoted request to two main ideas and then to a relationship. On day 88 distribute the reserved reading first. Day 89 distinguishes an influence from an outcome.

Review **explicit** as directly stated in the source and **inference** as an unstated conclusion supported by source details. Review how a **quotation** copies a source word sequence exactly inside paired marks. The ceremony looks forward from the earlier life story; place its action on the event line without disclosing the held dating clue. The person who tells us about an action and the person who has authority to take it can differ. For historical context use the released 06 leaves. The biography is factual life writing. Ask what each detail lets a reader understand about that life.

When a detail gets quoted inaccurately, align it word by word. For a topic-only summary, match a released detail to each of two main ideas. For a causal leap, ask what an actor could actually decide. Return the written cue before asking for an independent correction. Keep public talk optional. Preview racial exclusion and references to threats and death. Use no slurs or simulations. Refer to the source when inviting a response.

Print the family note on day 84. Use the two source cards, the quotation and main idea chart, the event line and the passage release record. Keep the special tense card with the sealed day 88 leaves. Use the day 80 folio if available and the local cards otherwise. Keep assessment anchors with the teacher.
