# Revising the literary explanation

## Teaching the close and the correction

Review the dated writing process: trace the plan, draft, feedback, revision and edit. Use a released body paragraph to ask how an explanation earns its claim. Check one spelling or punctuation feature at a time on separate released lines. The pupil’s close stays in the writing folder until its feedback cycle. Keep both day 65 exit exchanges sealed during practice and keep the convention strip with the assessment records.

## Before class

Print the convention strip, essay verification sheet, private reading leaves and Unit 2 record continuation. Keep the convention key for staff. Queue pupil codes for six stations, with headphones and private storage. On day 66, allow each child four minutes across the two genres and the context check. In a room with one recorder use the station log for continuations through day 74. A private live reading may supply the same evidence when every required take is actually heard. Record the reading conditions and keep missing turns pending.

## What to listen and look for

Review dialogue as words spoken by characters. Review conclusion as the last part that closes what a writer said. Reread released lines in pairs for meaning and expression, and confirm a word against the letters before checking context. Review how a precise term or an exact quotation supports a group of details. A generic title, a bare quotation or a surprise ending gives a different repair cue. Date the first response, cue and independent correction. Use the unit record for the component marks. Preview the storm and brief separation, and offer the alternative reading privately.
