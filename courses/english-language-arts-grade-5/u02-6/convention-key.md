# Day 65 convention key

Teacher only. Keep with Before class materials.

Yes, Lea, after waiting, we heard drips, calls, and wind, didn’t we? In *Under the Shelter* the narrator says, “The notes stayed dry.”

The opening and name take capitals. Match each of the five punctuation decisions to its own row. Accept series punctuation with or without a serial comma. Underlining is an alternative to italics for the complete title. Look for actual use of the supplied spelling reference. The exact quotation keeps “stayed” and its meaning. Credit an ending that retains both the dry notes and an outdoor sound detail. Keep the original and the independent correction for day 80.
