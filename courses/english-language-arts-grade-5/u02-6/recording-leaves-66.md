# Day 66 private reading leaves

**Waiting at the Marker**

I called and waited. Before I could call again, the guide came back around the turn. "You're here!" I said. At the shelter I wrote, "I remained at the designated meeting point until the guide returned." Waiting had felt like doing nothing. Now I knew it could be a choice that helped.

**Waiting Words**

*Invented for practice.*

The page is blank beside my hand.  
I watch a drop become a line.  
I wait beneath the shelter roof  
and let the last wet branches shine.  
A hurried word might lose its place.  
A careful pause can keep it clear.  
I read the sentence once again  
and hear the meaning settle here.
