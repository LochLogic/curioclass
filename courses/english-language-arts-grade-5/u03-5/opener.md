# Section 3.5 opener

## A reason worth keeping

On 107 draft and gather one written peer cue and one teacher cue. Save the opening and first reason before and after independent correction. On 108 the pupil adds a reason and ending independently. Score only eligible prior evidence. Return written cues on 109 and preserve that independent correction for 110. Keep 110 practice separate for 111 correction and closing verification.

Review the writing process by retaining dated stages and responding to a particular note. A reason needs a fact and an explanation of why that fact supports it. An ending draws together the reasons the writer established. In a clause, choose a link that states a real relationship. Keep the student portfolio, shared examples and check anchors separate. Judge an isolated comparison of two evidence links before collecting the pupil's own exit.

On 107 the teacher can give one 45-second written cue to each of 30 pupils in 22½ minutes within Reason draft. In partner work give each child a 45-second turn. Hear six in 4½ minutes and offer unheard pupils an accessible private turn. Return one written priority and preserve both versions. Use the dated folio, isolated evidence-link sheet and revision record.

Preview unfair treatment, threats and references to death. Keep prompts on the manuscripts and allow a private pass. Without a device keep the same dated paper versions. Return missing feedback before giving a score.
