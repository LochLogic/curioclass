# B3. Protecting observations

*A Research Route. Invented for practice.*

A gust lifted loose recording sheets from a clipboard. An observer caught them before they left the shelter. The team fastened the sheets under the clip and put completed sheets into a closed folder. These steps addressed the risk of losing notes; they did not prove that every record was accurate. The guide still checked the written entries against the original observations.
