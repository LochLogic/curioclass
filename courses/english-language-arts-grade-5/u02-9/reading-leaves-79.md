# The Closed Notebook and The Covered Page

*Invented for practice.*

## D1. The Closed Notebook

The first drops struck my notebook as the adult leader called us to the shelter. I wanted to finish the last line alone. Then I saw the dark spots spreading across the paper. I closed the notebook and joined the group. "Good call," my partner said. In the shelter I added, "Observation paused because rain threatened the written record." I had thought finishing meant never stopping. The unfinished line now reminded me that keeping the evidence readable mattered more than filling the page.

## D2. The Covered Page

Ana noticed rain on her partner's open notes. She wanted to hurry to the shelter, but first she said, "Cover your page. We'll go together." With the adult leader beside them, they carried the covered notebook under the roof. Ana's later note read, "The record remained legible because the group protected it from rain." She had left her own last sentence unfinished. Helping had delayed her ending, yet saved the observations that both partners needed.
