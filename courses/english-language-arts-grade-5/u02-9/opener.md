# Publishing with evidence

## Revised story, source note and research folio

Review the writing process through dated copies and a specific response to written feedback. Review explicit information as directly stated in the source. An inference is an unstated conclusion supported by source details. Require three separately traceable federal facts in the source note. Label imagined actions, speech and thoughts. Keep the original research folio distinct from the narrative note. Verify what each existing record can show.

## Before class

Set aside the sealed B leaves and the two complete D adventures. Prepare matching paper and accessible digital B pages. Have one functional keyboard per pupil ready for day 78’s forty-minute sitting. Use the protected class exchange and device log on day 79. Set out the closing folio inventory and Unit 2 record continuation for day 80. Give the scoring criteria and response directions at the assigned time. Use a previously released case for any demonstration.

## Making and keeping evidence

Record the earlier typing, actual online collaboration and saved publication as separate actions. If equipment fails, assign an available school slot and date the pending record. Keep the first independent reading and any support history. Finish corrections from earlier assignments in the closing response. The cover, navigation, original visual and local sharing are unscored. Use an image position and caption if the class lacks an original visual. Score after class from the dated evidence.
