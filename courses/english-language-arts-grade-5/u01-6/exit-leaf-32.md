# Eggplant

From the far side of the tree the word `stairs` was too small. She asked Tal to get the file of used paper. He ripped a sheet down the fold and handed her the bigger half.

"I was going to take a picture of it," she said.

"After you make the letters bigger?"

She laughed and picked up the marker. She made the first letter almost as tall as the sheet. He held it at arm's length while she walked down the sidewalk to check. She could see it from where the gravel began. Then she asked him to stand on the lower step so she could see it from where the strip of pavement turned.
