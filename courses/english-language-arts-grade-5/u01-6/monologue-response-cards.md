# Monologue response cards

*Invented for practice.*

## Place

Sami waits beside a stage curtain. A band begins to play.

Mark the words that tell where Sami is.

## Pacing

Jan turns a bent key until a latch clicks.

Circle the words that tell when the latch moves.

## Sequence

A flag lies folded. Lee opens it, then ties it to a fence.

Number these actions in order.

____ Lee ties the flag.

____ Lee opens the flag.
