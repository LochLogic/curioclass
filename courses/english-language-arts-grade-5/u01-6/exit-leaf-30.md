# Kale

Maren tipped the handle toward herself. It came down on her toe. The woman set her sack down and waited for Maren to pull her foot free.

"I'm Pilar. My son Tal will be home after dinner. He's always carrying something wider than he is. We'll learn where the corners are."

Maren stood the handle upright. Pilar wedged one side with a folded piece of cardboard. They both let go, a finger at a time. This time it stood.
