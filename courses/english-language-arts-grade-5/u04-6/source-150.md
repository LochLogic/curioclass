# Water-saving choices in a dated notice

## South Rim Water Conservation Measures

August 5, 2026. Grand Canyon National Park.

### Water conservation measures

- Run dishwashers and washing machines with full loads or use a water-saving setting.
- Wash vehicles only with reclaimed water. Reclaimed water has been treated so it can be used again.
