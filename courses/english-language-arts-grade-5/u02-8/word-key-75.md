# Day 75 private word probe key

Teacher only. Keep with Before class materials. Check dated word logs first. Record a familiar form as review evidence.

1. statistician. Say stat-uh-STISH-un. A statistician works with statistics.
2. regionality. Say ree-juh-NAL-ih-tee. Regionality is a regional quality or character.

Dictate first. After collecting spelling, open the isolated card, followed by the sentence card. Record the letters and every syllable the pupil uses. Listen for a word part tied to meaning, such as region with -al and -ity. Accept pronunciation differences that keep the word and its parts. Preserve the original and mark the correction separately.
