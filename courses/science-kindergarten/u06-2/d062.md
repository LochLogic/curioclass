# Day 62: Where can it live?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the cards. Lay the meadow map and the cards for the first group, and keep the rest covered. Put a mat and crayons at each seat. Day 61's impact record stays on the record sheet unchanged, and day 52 and days 53 and 54 are not collected again.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. No child is asked about their own home or what their family eats. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 4 min. Set-up and class transfer

Say "this is invented for practice." Everyone tells a partner [[c2-T62]], not scored. Seat group 1. The others draw [[c2-P62-1]] on side 2 of their mats.

#### 4 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching, and watch every child:
- 1 min 45 s, needs: [[c2-N1]], 20 s; [[c2-N2]], 20 s; [[c2-N3]], 25 s; [[c2-N4]], 25 s; [[c2-Nw]], 7 s each.
- 2 min, place: [[c2-M1]], 35 s; [[c2-M2]], 30 s; [[c2-M3]], 15 s; [[c2-Mw]], 8 s each for the reason.

Steady the cards but never give a link or a reason. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 4 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. Collect every mat.

#### 2 min. Change-overs and packing

Finish the record sheet. No exit and no repair today; day 64 repairs by letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards and the meadow by hand; fold paper into mats.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a plant, an animal and a place on paper.

**Skip.** The mat, the groups and the map.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Draw a meadow with a wet bank and a dry hill. Ask where a plant and a hare can live; do not help.
  **Say.** "What does it get there?" Expect: water and food.
- **15 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[c2-P62-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: water, light, meal, place, people. Frame: "It gets ___ here." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 63 observes change and weather for every child. Day 61's impact record and the earlier warming and shade records are carried unchanged and not scored again. Day 64 repairs by letter.

Ours, all of it.
