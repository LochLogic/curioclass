# Day 63: How does it change?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the change cards. Choose on the class year chart ten or fewer squares from a month not counted in unit 2, two months of rainy squares with different counts, and three days with morning and afternoon marks that include one cooler afternoon; write the key from the chart. If the chart has gaps, use invented dated squares. Put a mat and crayons at each seat. Day 62's record stays on the sheet unchanged.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. No child is asked about their own home. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say "this is invented for practice" for the cards. Seat group 1 at the chart. The others draw [[c2-P63-1]] on side 2 of their mats.

#### 4 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching, and watch every child:
- 1 min 30 s, change: [[c2-C1]], 15 s; [[c2-C2]], 15 s; [[c2-C3]], 25 s; [[c2-Cw]], 7 s each.
- 1 min 50 s, chart: [[c2-D1]], 25 s; [[c2-D2]], 25 s; [[c2-D3]], 25 s; [[c2-Dw]], 7 s each.
- The last 40 seconds is the change-over.

Steady the cards but never give a link or a reason. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 4 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. Collect every mat.

#### 4 min. Change-overs and packing

Spare time for a group that ran long, then packing. Finish the year-end record sheet. No exit and no repair today; day 64 repairs by letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards by hand and use a hand-drawn weather chart; fold paper into mats.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: draw ten weather squares on paper.

**Skip.** The mat and the groups.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Show a drawing of ivy climbing a brick wall, and ask how it changes its place; do not help. Count the windy squares together.
  **Say.** "What do you see change?" Expect: the ivy, the wall.
- **15 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[c2-P63-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: change, crack, hole, count, rainy, warmer. Frame: "I see ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 64 repairs by letter, and its repair groups use day 62's assessment and day 63's assessment together. Day 61's impact record and the earlier warming and shade records are carried unchanged and not scored again.
