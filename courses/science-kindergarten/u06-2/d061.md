# Day 61: Why does your change help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table: the scene, the last two groups' mats, crayons. Bring the day 60 mats and the record sheet. From the record choose at most four groups for repair: the most missed of G, H, D and E. Key: key-exits-59-61.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say once: "This schoolyard is invented for practice." The other groups draw the familiar school solution alone, as on day 60. Group 5 sits at the front table.

#### 14 min. Groups 5 and 6 observed, 7 minutes each

Exactly as day 60's assessment: same scene, same asks, same timing, no coaching, one sticker strip for each child. Mark the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Collect every mat.

#### 4 min. Record and model

The record was marked at the front table during each group, day 60's included; no second collection. The class agrees the schoolyard drawing with all four parts, the cause arrows and the evidence sentence, revised from the stickers and the counts. Check the record for blanks after class.

#### 2 min. Return and repair: feedback

Read each sticker meaning aloud once to the groups responding.

#### 5 min. Return and repair: repairs and revision

At most four groups, about 1.25 minutes each with you, from the record. G: join each footprint to a bent stem. H: draw a route that leaves every plant. D: add the action to the drawing and say it. E: put the rain and clean-up note beside the counts from day 59. A (day 55), C (day 56), B (day 57) or F (day 58) only where an exit showed them. The rest work in pairs from the pictured move and redraw one part alone. No reteaching of the scene.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say the picture is invented, and not scored. Show the open bin spilling paper toward the pond. Ask [[s06-X61a]]; all draw on their own paper. Ask [[s06-X61b]]; hear six. Paper tucked under the shrubs is error C; "a nice border shows people" is error D.

### Other rooms

Draw the scene and the bin by hand; fold mats from paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No bin picture: draw an open bin with paper blowing out toward a pond.

**Skip.** The groups, the mats and the stickers.

- **10 min. Look.** Find a path people take and ask what it changes.
  **Say.** "What would you change?" Expect: a different way for feet to go.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a change that keeps the paper in, in your bin drawing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X61a]] [[s06-X61b]] Say it is invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one group repaired. Never cut the record or the exit. Longer: draw two solutions for one place and say which spares more.

### Words and supports

Words: solution, path, protect, cannot tell yet. Frames: "If we ___, then ___." "I will ___ so ___." Pointing, home language and drawing count; help is recorded as shown with help.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 is the year-end check on living things and places, and day 63 the check on change and weather. Work from today is scored on the next school day, after revision; days 62 and 63 carry the record unchanged.
