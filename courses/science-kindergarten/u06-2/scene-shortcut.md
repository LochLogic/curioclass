# The shortcut scene (days 60 and 61)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Say aloud first that the schoolyard is invented for practice. No person is drawn, so no one is blamed. Do not reuse the corner, the bench, the stream or the puddle of any exit.

## The picture

A schoolyard with a school door on the left and a gate on the right. Between them lies a bed of small plants in rows. Across the middle of the bed runs a line of footprints, and along the footprints the stems are bent and flat. At each end of the bed three plants stand upright. A plain border strip runs along the edge of the picture. No route, path or fence is drawn for the answer.

## What the teacher asks

T1a: "Touch what people do to the bed of plants." T1b: "Touch what changes." T2a: "Draw a solution for this place." T2b: "Draw what it would change." T3: "Tell your partner why your solution helps. Use your drawing." Any feasible solution with a defensible link between the action and less harm is accepted, not only the example in the key.

## Large action pictures

Four large cards to point to, never a finished answer: a foot, a sign, a gate, a hard path.
