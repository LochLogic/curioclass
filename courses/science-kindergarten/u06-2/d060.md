# Day 60: What would you change on a place we have not seen?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table the day before: the scene, five mats, crayons, the four action cards (shown only after each child has drawn). Give the other groups drawing paper. Choose the order of groups 1 to 4. Day 59's recount stays on the class chart, unchanged. Rehearse a seven-minute turn once.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up and packing

Say once: "This schoolyard is invented for practice." The five other groups draw the familiar school solution alone. Group 1 sits at the front table.

#### 7 min. Group 1 observed

Mats in front; ask each item once, aloud, no coaching. 1.5 minutes: one child at a time touches the scene for [[s06-T1a]], then [[s06-T1b]], while the others look at their mats; about 15 seconds each. 2.5 minutes: all draw [[s06-T2a]], then [[s06-T2b]]; while they draw, choose each child's strip for T1 and T2. 2 minutes: partners tell [[s06-T3]] using the drawing; hear each child, 12 seconds, and choose that child's T3 strip; one strip a child, the look-for to work on next. 1 minute: read each sticker aloud once, about 5 seconds each; a child may redraw or retell one part alone. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child not heard is not yet seen, not wrong.

#### 7 min. Group 2 observed

Same as group 1, same scene, same wording. Do not repeat the scene's answer to a later group.

#### 7 min. Group 3 observed

Same as group 1.

#### 7 min. Group 4 observed

Same as group 1. Collect every mat. An absent child finishes on day 61 in the independent window.

### Other rooms

Draw the scene by hand: a bed of small plants, footprints across it, bent stems; no person.

### At home

**Swap.** No scene: draw a small lawn with a worn track across it. Draw no person. If your child's turn at school is on day 61, wait until after school that day.

**Skip.** The mats and the stickers.

- **10 min. The assessment.** Say the drawing is invented. Your child draws a solution for the place [[s06-T2a]]. Ask once, with no help.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws what it would change in the other box.
- **10 min. Tell.** [[s06-T3]] Ask once. Do not coach.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each group keeps its seven minutes. Longer: none; the extension waits for day 61.

### Words and supports

Words: path, protect, solution. Frame: "I will ___ so ___." Pointing, home language and a partner's gesture count; help is recorded as shown with help.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 sees the last two groups and then responds. Work from today is scored after its feedback and revision. [[s06-T1b]], [[s06-T2b]] and [[s06-T3]] go to day 61's repairs and revision: the group with error G joins each footprint to a bent stem; with H draws a route that leaves every plant; with D adds the action to the drawing and says it.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
