# Picture-caption sticker cards (days 60 and 61)

Held back. Pre-printed strips for the teacher; children do not read them. During each group's drawing the teacher picks the strip that fits each child and reads it aloud once in the group's seven minutes. On day 61 each response group hears its strip again. Each strip is two short sentences: what to look at and what to try. A child revises at most one part. Plain strips of paper, the letter large at the left, a plain picture hint at the right. The strips say what to try, never what is wrong with a child.

- **Met T1. Picture hint: footprints joined to bent stems.** "You joined what people do to what changes. Try telling your partner the join."
- **Met T2. Picture hint: a path that leaves every plant standing.** "Your route leaves the plants upright. Try adding what changes."
- **Met T3. Picture hint: a finger on a drawn action.** "You pointed to the action and said why. Try saying the plant you spare."
- **G. Picture hint: a footprint joined by a line to a bent stem.** "Feet can bend plants. Join each footprint to a bent stem."
- **H. Picture hint: a route on a hard path with the plants beside it.** "Move the route off every plant. Draw a way that leaves all of them."
- **D. Picture hint: a drawn action with an arrow to the plants.** "Your drawing tells people what to do. Add the action and say it."
- **E. Picture hint: a count card beside a rain note.** "More than one thing can change a count. Put the rain note beside the counts."
- **A, B, C, F. Picture hint: the matching 6.1 picture.** "Look at the picture that shows it. Say what you see."
