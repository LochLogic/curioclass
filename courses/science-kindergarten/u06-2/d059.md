# Day 59: Did our change help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Walk the route the day before; check gloves, grabbers and both sinks. Post the day 55 rows. Choose six children to hear.

### Safety and set-up

Same controls as day 55. Each group has a tally card and crayons; its collector, new this time, wears non-latex gloves and holds a grabber. You alone bag. Glass, sharp things, food and unknown containers stay put. Stay in sight. Wash hands after.

### The day

#### 1 min. Warm-up

Ask [[s06-P59]]: "Will we find more, fewer or about the same pieces than day 55?" Each child touches a card. Keep the guesses.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

If many gave error F in day 58's exit, point to what our bin takes.

#### 2 min. Out

Groups stand in the same zones.

#### 5 min. Tally

Same count rule, same after-lunch time. Each group points once at each piece and marks a fresh card. Write the weather, who is away and any clean-up by others beside it. You count any zone above ten.

#### 4 min. Pick-up

Two shifts of three collectors, 2 minutes each, new collectors. You alone bag. The rest sit in sight and say "I saw ___."

#### 5 min. Back and wash

Walk in; one wipe each at the door. Collectors wash at the two sinks, three at a time.

#### 3 min. Compare

Lay the day 55 and day 59 rows side by side by kind. Ask: "More, fewer or about the same?" Read the notes aloud. Check the guesses. A difference of one is about the same.

#### 5 min. Closing explanation

Scribe what the class says: how loose waste can touch land, water, air and living things here; how our action might help; what we cannot tell yet, because rain or others' clean-up could change a count. A count that did not fall is a result too: change one thing. Read the question board back and mark the answered ones.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented. Show the two corners. Ask [[s06-X59a]]; all touch more or fewer for each corner. Ask [[s06-X59b]]; six answer aloud in turn. "The reminder did it, the numbers went down" is error E; day 61 repairs it.

### Other rooms

Dated photographs of the same area, said to be photographs; no pick-up.

### At home

**Swap.** No patch: count two drawings of one table, an earlier and a later. Touch no litter.

**Skip.** The tally cards and the pick-up.

- **10 min. Count twice.** Your child touches each scrap once in each drawing.
  **Say.** "Fewer or more now?" Expect: the later drawing has fewer.
- **10 min. Other reasons.** Ask what else could change a count, such as rain or someone cleaning.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X59a]] [[s06-X59b]] Say it is invented.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one shift only. Never cut the compare or the exit. Longer: compare a second kind of litter.

### Words and supports

Words: before, after, fewer, more. Frames: "Before there were ___, after there were ___." "We cannot tell yet, because ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 is the check: each group draws and tells a solution for a shortcut across plants. The exit [[s06-X59b]] is heard again on day 61.
