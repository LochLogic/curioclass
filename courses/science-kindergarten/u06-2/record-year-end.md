# Year-end record sheet: our class list (days 62 and 63)

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. The End columns are the observations on day 62 (needs and a place, the N and M items, in order) and day 63 (change and the class chart, the C and D items, in order), three look-fors each; a child is seen once at each sitting. Only a child's first unaided try counts. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you read a card, steadied a page or placed a mark for the child, and say so. The letters on this sheet (F, G and M to V) are the year-end check letters, not the unit 6 letters. Day 64 counts each letter from this sheet. Days 61 and earlier stay on their own sheets and are not collected again.
