# Exit cards, days 59 and 61

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Mark on the one scale. Day 61's exit is a response and is not scored.

## Day 59: two corners, before and after

**Picture.** Two corners, each drawn before and after. Corner 1 (a bike rack): five wrappers before, two after; a reminder sign was added. Corner 2 (a fence): seven wrappers before, four after; no sign was added. A caption read aloud for each. The teacher checks these numbers against the class tallies and changes the card if they match.
**Spoken 59a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the corner that had fewer wrappers after the action." Touch the first or the second corner picture.
**Spoken 59b:** "Pip says: only the reminder made litter go down. Change what Pip says, using both corners."

## Day 61 (section exit, response, not scored): a spilling bin

**Picture.** An open school bin with its lid up, paper spilling out and drifting toward a pond edge. A row of shrubs stands along the pond edge. No arrows.
**Spoken 61a:** "Invented for practice. Three cards: a grown-up closing the lid, paper tucked under the shrubs, a longer path to the pond. Touch the card that keeps the paper in."
**Spoken 61b:** "Tell why it helps."
