# Day 64: Fix one part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count each letter F, G and M to V on the record sheet from days 62 and 63. Choose the three largest groups. Lay one table for each, with its repair cards. Put each child's mat at their seat. Keep the exit cards covered.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up and record read

Say once: "This is invented for practice." Read the record sheet and name the three tables.

#### 14 min. Return and repair

Teach at most three branches, the three largest letters: 4 minutes each with you, 1 minute to move. The rest work in pairs from the pictured move at their own letter's table. Repair by letter:
- F: five squares in a line; touch and slide each once.
- G: lay the day marks side by side.
- M: watered and dry plant cards side by side.
- N: the green and pale plant cards side by side.
- O: sort who takes in a meal.
- P: match the three meals to who eats them.
- Q: trace the missing line from the place to one need.
- R: follow the water line from the stream to the people.
- S: the crack before and after, side by side.
- T: two animal changes side by side.
- U: you say a liking; children find the picture that backs the claim.
- V: count the trees left by each plan.

Give no answer; the picture is the repair.

#### 6 min. Revise your mat

Read each child's caption once; this hands back day 62's assessment and day 63's assessment. Each child redraws or recounts one part, keeping the first try beside the new one, at most two parts.

#### 8 min. Section exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask once each, everyone touches, and hear six: [[c2-X64a]], [[c2-X64b]], [[c2-X64c]], [[c2-X64d]].

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures by hand; fold paper into mats.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the pictures on paper.

**Skip.** The tables.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at your child's mat from days 62 and 63. Ask which part to fix.
  **Say.** "What would you change?" Expect: one part.
- **10 min. Fix it.** Your child redraws one part beside the first try.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X64a]] [[c2-X64b]] [[c2-X64c]] [[c2-X64d]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one table repaired. Never cut the record or the exit. Longer: draw one animal's place and what changed it.

### Words and supports

Words: need, place, change, pattern. Frame: "I would change ___." Pointing and any home language count; help is recorded as shown with help.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows which letters still stand; note them on the record sheet for the grade 1 teacher. Weather watching goes on to the last day of school. Records from days 61 to 63 pass on unchanged and are not scored again.
