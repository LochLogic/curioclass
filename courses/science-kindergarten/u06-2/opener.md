# Section 6.2 opener: did our change help, and can we show it?

## What the section does

Three days, 59 to 61. Day 59 counts the same patch again, at the same after-lunch time with the same count rule, and puts the day 55 and day 59 rows side by side beside notes of rain, who was away and any clean-up by others. The class dictates how loose waste can touch land, water, air and living things here, how our action might help, and what we cannot tell yet. Day 60 and the first half of day 61 are the unit's observation: each child, in a group of five at a time, marks a picture mat, draws a solution and tells a partner why it helps, for a schoolyard picture the class has not seen. The last ten minutes of day 61 are the response.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. On days 60 and 61 one front table sees one group at a time, seven minutes each, while the other groups draw the familiar school solution alone. Five children mark at once. The teacher hears each child's brief reason, writes a picture-caption sticker for each during the drawing, and reads each once. No coaching and no reteaching during the observation. Each child may redraw or retell one part alone before leaving. Day 60 sees four groups and day 61 sees the last two. One record covers both days.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A smaller count is a result, not proof.** Rain, who was away and clean-up by others can change a count. Say "we cannot tell yet."
- **A count that did not fall is a result too.** "Did not work, change one thing."
- **The class model holds all four parts;** one child may speak to one or more.
- **A solution changes what people do.** A pretty border does not; a different way for feet to go does.
- **Moving the same harm is not a solution.** A path across another part of the same plants still bends plants.
- **Nothing is about named people.** The picture shows footprints and bent stems, never a person.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E:** "It fell, so the reminder did it." **G:** "Walking cannot change plants." **H:** "A path over other plants protects them." **D:** "A pretty border tells people what to do."
- A, B, C and F are met on days 55 to 59 and are repaired only where an exit showed them.

## Scoring

Work made on day 60 is scored in teacher planning on day 61, after the feedback and revision. Work made on day 61 is scored on the next school day. A child who is not heard in a part is marked "not yet seen", not wrong. Marks use the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## Safety

Day 59 repeats day 55's pick-up with its controls: inspect the route, gloves and grabbers the day before; teacher alone bags; wash hands after. Days 60 and 61 are paper only.

## Supplies

The tally cards, gloves, grabbers, adult bag, hand wipes, the class picture chart, the shortcut scene, thirty response mats (five in use at a time), the picture-caption sticker cards, crayons, drawing paper, the exit cards, the record sheet.
