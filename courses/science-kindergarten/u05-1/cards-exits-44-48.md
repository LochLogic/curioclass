# Exit cards, days 44 to 48

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse the slide, the benches or the pairs touched in class. Mark on the one scale. A warm mark is a small curved set of lines drawn over a surface, never a colour or a number.

## Day 44: soil by a wall

**Picture.** Two drawings of one patch of soil beside a wall. In the first the wall's shadow lies over the soil. In the second the soil is in the open with a warm mark over it. Three cards: sunlight on the soil (rays reaching it), the wall, a clock.
**Spoken 44a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the card that shows what made the soil warmer." **Key:** the sunlight on the soil. "The soil gets warm by itself" is error A. Touching the clock, "it was later", is error H.
**Spoken 44b:** "Same pictures. Say: I think, because I saw." **Key:** "I think the sun warmed it, because I saw the soil in the sun in the second picture." "Because it is later" is error H. "The sun cannot warm soil" is error A.

## Day 45: two pairs of cards, left and right

**Picture 45a.** The left card: two pots of the same soil, one in the sun, one in the shade, with the same small clock beside each at the same hour. The right card: a pot of soil in the sun and a pot of sand in the shade.
**Spoken 45a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the pair that is fair for finding out what the sun does." **Key:** the left card. The right card, "soil and sand show it", is error C.
**Picture 45b.** The left card: two bowls of sand put out together, the same small clock beside each. The right card: a bowl of sand with a clock showing a morning hour, beside a bowl with a clock showing a later hour.
**Spoken 45b:** "Invented for practice. Touch the pair that is fair." **Key:** the left card. The right card, "the long one shows it best", is error G.

## Day 46: rooftop water and a rock card

**Picture 46a.** A rooftop with two tubs of water, one in the sun and one in a shadow. Caption read aloud: "The sunny water was warmer."
**Spoken 46a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the water the card says was warmer." **Key:** the sunny tub. "Water never gets warm in the sun" is error B. The shaded tub is shown with help, no letter.
**Picture 46b.** A grid of four rocks: sunny early, shaded early, sunny later, shaded later. Caption read aloud: "Both were warmer later. At each look the sunny rock was warmer than the shaded rock."
**Spoken 46b:** "Touch the two rocks to compare to see what the sun did." **Key:** sunny early and shaded early, or sunny later and shaded later. The same rock early and later, "later makes it warm", is error H.

## Day 47: awnings over a clay path

**Picture.** A small clay path patch. Awning 1: a plain roof on posts, over the patch. Awning 2: a striped roof on posts standing beside the patch, the patch in the open.
**Spoken 47a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the cover that keeps the sun off the patch." **Key:** the plain roof over the patch. The striped roof, "it is nearer", is error D. "It is prettier" is error E.
**Spoken 47b:** "Same pictures. Point to the part that keeps the sunlight off." **Key:** the roof overhead. The stripes, "the colours do it", is error E.

## Day 48: garden path

**Picture 48a.** A garden path with two flat stones, one in the sun and one in the shade. Caption read aloud: "The sunny stone was warmer."
**Spoken 48a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the stone the card says was warmer." **Key:** the sunny stone. A wrong touch is shown with help, no letter.
**Picture 48b.** A shallow tub of water in the sun and one in the shade, clouds across the sky. Caption read aloud: "About the same." Three cards: the sunny water warmer, the shaded water warmer, about the same (two equal marks).
**Spoken 48b:** "Touch what the card says." **Key:** about the same. "Water cannot warm in the sun" is error B.
**Spoken 48c:** "Finish our sentence: sunlight ___ surfaces." **Key:** warms, taken from the stone card and not from the tie. "Does not warm, the water was the same" is error A.
