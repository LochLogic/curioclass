# Exit cards, days 44 to 48

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse the slide, the benches or the pairs touched in class. Mark on the one scale. A warm mark is a small curved set of lines drawn over a surface, never a color or a number.

## Day 44: soil by a wall

**Picture.** Two drawings of one patch of soil beside a wall. In the first the wall's shadow lies over the soil. In the second the soil is in the open with a warm mark over it. Three cards: sunlight on the soil (rays reaching it), the wall, a clock.
**Spoken 44a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the card that shows what made the soil warmer."
**Spoken 44b:** "Same pictures. If the wall shade came back over the soil, what would the soil feel like? Say it."

## Day 45: two pairs of cards, left and right

**Picture 45a.** One card: two empty bowls drawn side by side, one under a small sun and one under a shadow, with a drawn heap of wood chips beside them. No clocks, no second material.
**Spoken 45a:** "Invented for practice. Pip has two bowls of wood chips. Pip will put one in the sun and one in the shade to find out what the sun does. Say what Pip keeps the same."
**Picture 45b.** The left card: one bowl of sand in the sun and one in the shade, put out together, the same small clock beside each. The right card: a sunny bowl of sand with a clock showing a morning hour, beside a shady bowl with a clock showing a later hour.
**Spoken 45b:** "Invented for practice. Touch the pair that is fair."

## Day 46: rooftop water and a rock card

**Picture 46a.** A rooftop with two tubs of water, one in the sun and one in a shadow. Caption read aloud: "The sunny water was warmer."
**Spoken 46a:** "Invented for practice. A rooftop card shows a tub of water in the sun and a tub of water in the shade. The caption, read aloud, says the sunny water was warmer. Say the sentence: The ___ tub was warmer, so the sun warms ___ too."
**Picture 46b.** A grid of four rocks: sunny early, shaded early, sunny later, shaded later. Caption read aloud: "Both were warmer later. At each look the sunny rock was warmer than the shaded rock."
**Spoken 46b:** "Touch the two rocks to compare to see what the sun did."

## Day 47: awnings over a clay path

**Picture.** A small clay path patch. Awning 1: a plain roof on posts, over the patch. Awning 2: a striped roof on posts standing beside the patch, the patch in the open.
**Spoken 47a:** "Invented for practice. A striped awning stands beside a clay path bench, and the bench is in the sun. Tell me what to do with the awning to keep the sun off the bench."
**Spoken 47b:** "Same picture, with the awning now over the bench. Point to the part of the awning that keeps the sunlight off."

## Day 48: wood in sun and shade

**Picture 48a.** A wooden garden step in the sun and a matching step in the shade, set out together. No warm marks.
**Spoken 48a:** "Invented for practice. A wooden garden step sits in the sun, and the same kind of step sits in the shade, both put out at the same time. Touch the one you think feels warmer."
**Picture 48b.** Two tubs of water side by side, one in the sun and one in the shade, with the same warm mark drawn over each. Three cards: the sunny water warmer, the shaded water warmer, about the same (two equal marks).
**Spoken 48b:** "Invented for practice. Two tubs of water sit side by side, one in the sun and one in the shade. The same warm mark is drawn over each tub. Touch the card that says what the marks show."
**Picture 48c.** A wooden fence post in the sun with a warm mark over it, and a matching post in the shade with no mark.
**Spoken 48c:** "Invented for practice. A wooden fence post stands in the sun with a warm mark over it, and a matching post stands in the shade with no warm mark. Finish the sentence: the sun ___ the post."
