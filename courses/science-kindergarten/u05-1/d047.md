# Day 47: What must a shade do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check each cardboard piece and picture card for sharp edges and small pieces. Tape a square on the front sunny spot and put the toy animal on a rock in it. Hang the design-cycle chart and the question board.

### Safety and set-up

Cardboard and cards only. Stay in sight. Rock too big to mouth. Only you handle a lamp. Never look at the sun. A group stands back from the taped square except for its turn. Water break after.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 46's exit showed error B and error H. Two minutes each, in pairs. For error B, lay the sunny water record and the shaded water record side by side. For error H, touch the sunny and the shaded picture at one look.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

The design-cycle chart, said with a hand motion for each step: what is the problem, try an idea, test it [[s05-P47-1]].

#### 4 min. The problem

Set the toy animal on the rock in the sun. **Ask.** "What do people want to be different?" [[s05-P47-2]] Expect: less sunlight on this spot. Put up the goal card: "keep the sun off this spot". Add to the question board: if sunlight matters, what could block it? Shade need not feel cold.

#### 12 min. Umbrella, canopy, tent

Each group in turn, 1.5 minutes, holds a cardboard cover beside the animal and then over it, and watches where the shadow falls [[s05-P47-4]]. The others sort their nine cards into "over the top" and "beside" [[s05-P47-3]]. No sun: you show one lamp shadow, labelled a demonstration.

#### 2 min. Add to the chart

**Ask.** "What must a shade do?" [[s05-P47-5]] Expect: be over the spot, between the sun and the spot. The roof is the part that does the job. Spoken only; add it to the chart.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these cards are invented for practice, and not scored." [[s05-X47a]] then [[s05-X47b]]; all touch or point at once. Mark the rotating six on the scale.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cardboard and toys to the tub.

### Other rooms

No sun: the lamp shadow and the nine cards drawn on paper. No toy animal: a stuffed toy or a drawn animal on a rock.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw an umbrella over a bench and one beside it. No toy: any small toy.

**Skip.** The six turns and the sorting.

- **10 min. Shadow.** In the sun (never look at the sun), a grown-up holds a piece of cardboard beside, then over, a toy. Your child points to where the shadow falls [[s05-P47-4]].
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a cover that keeps the sun off the toy [[s05-P47-6]].
  **Say.** "What must a shade do?" Expect: be over the spot, between the sun and it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X47a]] and [[s05-X47b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort only the umbrella cards. Never cut the shadow, the Ask or the exit. Longer: draw a second cover.

### Words and supports

Words: shade, cover, roof, over the top, beside. Frame: "A shade goes ___ the spot." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 opens with 2 minutes each: error D (a roof beside the patch shades it), trace the shadow edge onto the patch; error E (the stripes do it), choose the covered-and-open comparison over the decoration. Then the class checks the pattern at a second spot.
