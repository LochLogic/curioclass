# Section 5.1 opener: sunlight warms surfaces, and a cover keeps it off

## What the section does

Five days, 44 to 48. The unit asks one question: why is the slide in the sun warmer than the one under the tree? On day 44 the class touches a sunny and a shaded surface at recess and starts a picture of the sun reaching the warm one. Day 45 plans fair pairs of sand and soil, and day 46 adds rock and water. Day 47 asks what a shade must do and tries cardboard shadows over a toy animal. Day 48 checks the pattern at a second spot. The build is in section 5.2.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. Groups of five each have their own pairs, so nobody queues. A child touches only samples the teacher has already checked, briefly, with the back of the hand. Every child marks an own sun response mat, and the teacher hears a rotating six give a reason. Every exit is spoken, with picture cards that are invented for practice; say that aloud first.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Sunlight reaching a surface warms it.** The hand can tell warmer from cooler from about the same. It cannot tell how much.
- **A fair pair is alike except for sun or shade.** Same material, same time out. Keep, change, watch.
- **A tie is a result.** "About the same" is written down as it is, never turned into a warmer one.
- **Later is not the cause.** A sunny sample and its shaded match at one look is the comparison. Afternoon is not always warmer.
- **Each material is compared with itself.** Sand with sand, never sand with water.
- **Shade is where the sunlight is blocked.** It need not feel cold. A useful shade is over the spot, between the sun and the spot.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** "The sun cannot warm things." **B:** "Water never warms." **C:** "Soil and sand show what the sun does."
- **D:** "A roof beside the patch shades it." **E:** "The stripes do it." **G:** "The long one shows it best." **H:** "Later makes it warm."
- Error F ("it is cool the moment it goes on") is met in section 5.2.

## Safety

The teacher checks every surface with the back of a hand before any child does, and replaces a surface that is hot or uncertain with the dated record. Never look at the sun. Give shade and water breaks on warm days. The teacher alone fills trays and handles soil and sand; no tasting, no sniffing, wash hands after soil. Cardboard edges and small pieces are checked on day 47.

## Supplies

Sand, soil, smooth rocks and shallow plastic water trays in matched pairs, cardboard for shade, the pair cards, the exit cards, the shade structure cards, the large toy animal, the sun response mats, crayons, hand wipes. A room with nothing uses the dated matched records and a lamp shadow over the toy animal, and says so aloud.
