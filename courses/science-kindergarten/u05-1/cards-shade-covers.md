# Shade structure picture cards (day 47)

Nine cards, three each of umbrella, canopy and tent, for each group to sort into "over the top" and "beside". Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Check the card edges for sharp corners before the lesson.

## The cards

- **Over the top.** An umbrella held over a bench seat. A canopy on four posts over a picnic table. A tent roof pitched over a sleeping mat.
- **Beside.** An umbrella leaning against a bench, closed or held to one side, the seat in the open. A canopy standing next to a table with the table in the open. A tent standing beside a mat with the mat in the open.
- **Mixed.** One card of each kind shows the shadow edge as a dashed line: over the top, the shadow covers the seat or table; beside, the shadow lies on the ground next to it.

## How they are used

Each group sorts the nine cards into "over the top" and "beside" and says which kind keeps the sun off the thing it is for. The talk: "The roof is the part that does the job. It sits between the sun and the spot." Spoken only. No roof shape is taught and none is required later.

## A room with nothing

The teacher draws one umbrella, one canopy and one tent, each over and beside.
