# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 44 to 48

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-44-48.


## Day 44: soil by a wall

- Spoken 44a: the sunlight on the soil. "The soil gets warm by itself" is error A. Touching the clock, "it was later", is error H.
- Spoken 44b: cooler. "The same because it is later" is error H. "The sun cannot change soil" is error A.

## Day 45: two pairs of cards, left and right

- Spoken 45a: the same chips and the same time; only sun or shade changes. A bowl of sand in place of chips is error C; putting one out earlier is error G.
- Spoken 45b: the left card. The right card, "the long one shows it best", is error G.

## Day 46: rooftop water and a rock card

- Spoken 46a: the sunny tub; the sun warms water too. "Water never gets warm in the sun" is error B. The shaded tub is shown with help, no letter.
- Spoken 46b: sunny early and shaded early, or sunny later and shaded later. The same rock early and later, "later makes it warm", is error H.

## Day 47: awnings over a clay path

- Spoken 47a: move the awning over the bench. "Leave it beside, it is nearer" is error D. "Paint it, it is prettier" is error E.
- Spoken 47b: the roof over the bench. The stripes, "the colors do it", is error E.

## Day 48: wood in sun and shade

- Spoken 48a: the sunny step. A touch on the shaded step is shown with help, no letter.
- Spoken 48b: about the same. The sunny or shaded card is shown with help, no letter. "Water cannot warm in the sun" is error B.
- Spoken 48c: warms. "Does not warm" is error A.
