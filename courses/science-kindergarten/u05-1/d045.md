# Day 45: What makes a pair fair?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Thirty minutes ahead, put out a sand pair and a soil pair for each group, one sunny and one shaded, set down together. Touch every sample first and clear it. You alone handle soil and sand; wet the sand lightly so it makes no dust. Key: key-exits-44-48.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on. Children touch cleared samples, back of the hand, for a count of three; one too warm for your hand at that count is not cleared. No tasting or sniffing. Stay in sight. Soap handwash after sand and soil. Outdoor check done (safety page). Never look at the sun. Water break after.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

In pairs with the day 44 pictures: error A, 2 minutes, trace the sunlight to the warmer picture; error H, 1 minute, touch the sunny and the shaded picture at one look.

#### 2 min. Warm-up

Say "I think ___, because I saw ___" about the slides [[s05-P45-1]] (review).

#### 6 min. Fair pairs

Review keep, change, watch (unit 1). New words: a **pair** is two samples alike except sun or shade; a **look** is a time we touch and record. Children point to the fair card of three [[s05-P45-2]]: card 1 (card 2 is sand beside soil; card 3 went out long before its match). Say together: "Keep the material. Keep the time. Change only sun or shade. Watch which feels warmer."

#### 3 min. Plan the afternoon look

**Ask.** "What do we keep the same?" [[s05-P45-3]] Expect: the material and the time. Rotating six answer. After class you alone photograph the afternoon pairs (a set-up).

#### 8 min. Touch the pairs

Six groups, each at its own pairs. Each child touches the four samples, a count of three each, and marks sand and soil on the mat: sunny warmer, shaded warmer, or about the same [[s05-P45-4]]. A tie is a result.

#### 2 min. Add to the model

Sand and soil join the picture model. The rotating six say what was seen [[s05-P45-5]]: "I think the sun warmed the ___, because I felt it." A tie is said as felt.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these cards are invented." [[s05-X45a]]: pairs whisper; hear the six, 15 seconds each. Then [[s05-X45b]]; all touch. Mark the six on the scale.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

You clear the samples. Hands washed by table.

### Other rooms

No sun or a hot sample: the dated photographs with the recorded comparison, said to be weaker. No printer: draw the three cards with small clocks.

### At home

**Swap.** No sand or soil: two cups of the same dirt or two matched bowls. A grown-up checks them first.

**Skip.** The cards, the groups and the afternoon look.

- **12 min. Pairs.** Stay with a grown-up and never look at the sun. Put one cup in the sun and one in the shade, set out together. After 30 minutes a grown-up checks, then your child touches both briefly with the back of a hand; no tasting or sniffing, and wash hands after dirt [[s05-P45-4]].
  **Say.** "What do we keep the same?" Expect: the cup and the time. 
- **10 min. Talk.** Say the sentence "I think ___, because I saw ___" [[s05-P45-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X45a]] and [[s05-X45b]] Say they are invented; ask once each; your child says the first answer and touches the second.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sand only. Never cut the fair pairs, touch or exit. Longer: a third pair.

### Words and supports

Words: pair, look, keep, change, watch. Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 opens with 2 minutes each: error C, swap one sample for the same material; error G, match the time.
