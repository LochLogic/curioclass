# Day 48: Does it hold at another spot?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick a second safe sunny edge with matched shade. For each group set four pairs there (sand, soil, rock, water), both samples of a pair set down at the same moment, 30 minutes ahead. Touch every sample first and clear it. You alone fill trays and handle soil and sand.

### Safety and set-up

Children touch cleared samples briefly, back of the hand. No pouring, no tasting or sniffing. Never look at the sun. Water break after. Wipes after soil.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 47's exit showed error D and error E. Two minutes each, in pairs. For error D, trace the shadow edge with a finger onto the patch: a roof beside it leaves the patch in the sun. For error E, choose the covered-and-open comparison over the stripes.

#### 2 min. Warm-up

Read the class sentence aloud from the chart. Ask for a guess: will it hold at another spot? [[s05-P48-1]]

#### 11 min. Second spot

Two minutes out to the second edge. Each group has four cleared pairs. Each child touches two pairs of their own choice, so the group does all four, and marks them on the mat [[s05-P48-2]]. Two minutes back. A child may pass.

#### 4 min. Mark and say

Each group says each pair in relative words [[s05-P48-3]]: "The sunny one is warmer," or "about the same". A tie is marked as it was felt.

#### 3 min. Revise

**Ask.** "Does our sentence hold?" [[s05-P48-4]] If every pair agrees, say so. If one differs, the class revises the sentence only as far as the records show.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these cards are invented for practice, and this is the section exit." [[s05-X48a]], [[s05-X48b]] then [[s05-X48c]]; all touch or say at once. Mark the rotating six on the scale.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Trays to you. Wipes at tables.

### Other rooms

No second spot or sun: read aloud the dated photographs from another place with their recorded comparison, said to be weaker than touching.

### At home

**Swap.** No sand or soil: any two matched cups of dirt or two bowls of water. A grown-up checks them first.

**Skip.** The groups, the trays and the walk.

- **12 min. Another place.** Put a cup of dirt in the sun and one in the shade at another spot, set down together. After 30 minutes your child touches both and marks which felt warmer [[s05-P48-2]].
  **Say.** "Does the sun still warm it here?" [[s05-P48-4]] Expect: yes, or the same.
- **8 min. Draw.** Draw the sun and an arrow to the warmer cup.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X48a]], [[s05-X48b]] and [[s05-X48c]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pairs a group. Never cut the touch, the revision or the exit. Longer: a third spot.

### Words and supports

Words: warmer, cooler, about the same, spot. Frame: "The ___ one is warmer." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 opens with 2 minutes each: error A (the sun cannot warm things), trace the sunlight to the warmer picture; error B (water never warms), put the paired water records side by side. Then the class builds a cover for a small patch.
