# Fair pair cards (day 45)

Three large cards for the teacher to show in the Fair pairs part. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the labels "pair" and "look", no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. These pictures are a taught case; the exit cards on day 45 are different pictures.

## Card 1: a sandpit pair

Two sandpit patches, each with the same amount of sand, one in the sun and one in the shade, with a small clock beside each showing the same hour. This is the fair pair. Say: "Same material. Same time. Only sun or shade is different."

## Card 2: a garden bed with unlike materials

A patch of soil in the sun beside a patch of sand in the shade, clocks showing the same hour. This is not a fair pair. Say: "Soil and sand are different things. If one feels warmer, we do not know if it was the sun or the material."

## Card 3: put out at different times

Two patches of the same garden soil, one in the sun with a clock showing morning, one in the shade with a clock showing a later hour. This is not a fair pair. Say: "One sat out long before the other. If one feels warmer, we do not know if it was the sun or the time."

## The words

A **pair** is two samples alike except for sun or shade. A **look** is a time we touch and record. The talk ends with: "Keep the material. Keep the time. Change only sun or shade. Watch which feels warmer."

## A room with nothing

The teacher draws the three cards on paper with the same small clocks.
