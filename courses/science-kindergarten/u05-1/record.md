# Unit 5 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or act it out. The End columns are the observations on day 52 (the warmth items, in order), day 53 (the cover choice and the build) and day 54 (the covered and open patch), one cell for each look-for. Only a child's first unaided try at the sitting counts. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you read a card, steadied a page or placed a mark for the child, and say so. Where a key follows a dated caption or a touch, mark against what the caption or the touch said, never against what you expected. The day 54 exit is a response and is not scored.
