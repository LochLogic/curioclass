# Day 42: Where does a living thing find what it needs?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table the day before: the map, the three cards, the resource marks, five model mats and crayons. Put one set of the water and light cards, W1 to W4 only, and a student page at each table. Keep the day 41 mats in their folders. Rehearse a four-minute turn once. Keys: key-water-light, key-assessment-model.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. Keep the walking space clear; cards are pre-cut.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Uncover the map and say once: "This place is imaginary, invented for practice." Seat group 1 at the front table. The other groups sort the four water cards at their tables, drawing a drop beside each card where something takes in water [[s04-P42-1]] and then touching each drop once to say the last number [[s04-P42-2]]. A finished child draws a plant at its place and a line to what the place gives.

#### 4 min. Group 1 observed

Mats in front, each box holding its own copy of the map. Ask each item once, aloud, no coaching; all five answer at once on their own mats. Reading the stems takes about 55 seconds and the swap of children about 30, so the asks run 2 minutes 20 seconds.
- 30 seconds, the plant: all draw the plant at its place [[s04-M1a]] (5 seconds), then draw its lines [[s04-M1b]] (25 seconds).
- 30 seconds, the animal: the same for the prairie dog card [[s04-M2a]] and its lines [[s04-M2b]].
- 80 seconds, the people: all draw lines from the people [[s04-M3a]] (50 seconds); then each child says one thing the people and the plants both use, 6 seconds each [[s04-M3b]].

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts. Labels alone earn nothing on the people lines.

#### 4 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. Collect every model mat.

#### 4 min. Change-overs and captions

Time for a group that ran long, then swap crayons. No caption is read today: day 43 reads the day 41 and model captions once. No exit and no repair today. After class write the model caption strips (E and F) on the model mats.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map on a large sheet and mark it. Cut plant, animal and people cards from paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a squirrel drinking, a robin at a bird bath, a pot being watered and a plant in rain.

**Skip.** The groups, the map and the mats.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Say the pictures are invented. Your child draws a drop beside each picture where something takes in water [[s04-P42-1]]. Ask once, with no help.
  **Say.** "What do all four take in?" Expect: water.
- **15 min. Draw.** Your child draws a plant at its place and a line to what the place gives it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the sittings are the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: place, lines, pond, bank, map. Frame: "It needs ___." Pointing, drawing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 has no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 43 is the response day: a sitting on three new change sequences, then the day 41 captions and the model captions (errors E and F) are read and each child revises at most two.
