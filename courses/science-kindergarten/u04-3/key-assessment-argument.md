# Teacher key: Change sequences and the kite card (day 43)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-assessment-argument.


## Sequence C1. A young plant and a stone

- C1a: the picture with the soil pushed aside. The cloud or the kite is shown with help, no letter. "Nothing changed, plants cannot change places" is error G.
- C1b: water, or the damp ground.

## Sequence C2. A fox and a bank

- C2a: the finished bank with the den opening. "Only people change things" is error H.
- C2b: a safe place to be. "Nothing, only people change things" is error H.

## Sequence C3. A channel for a garden

- C3a: the channel.
- C3b: route 1, across the bare strip (the C3 routes picture). The other route, or "they are the same", is error J.
- C3c: it leaves the plants standing. "I like it" is error I.
