# Day 43: How do living things change a place?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table the day before: the three sequences, the kite card among the cards, five argument mats, crayons. Put an eats card set and a student page at each table. Bring out each child's day 41 and day 42 mats with their captions. Rehearse a three-minute turn once.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. Keep the walking space clear; cards are pre-cut.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say once: "These pictures are invented for practice." Seat group 1 at the front table. The other groups match each animal on the eats cards to its food [[s04-P43-1]].

#### 18 min. Six groups observed, 3 minutes each

Each group in turn, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, no coaching. Do not repeat the needs or place sittings.
- 45 seconds, the plant sequence: all touch together [[s04-C1a]]; each child answers [[s04-C1b]], 4 seconds each.
- 45 seconds, the animal sequence: [[s04-C2a]], then each child [[s04-C2b]].
- 90 seconds, the people sequence: all touch the land the people changed [[s04-C3a]] and the route that leaves more plants standing [[s04-C3b]]; then each child answers [[s04-C3c]], 6 seconds each.

The kite card sits among the cards; touching it is shown with help. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 3 min. Read the captions

Gather children by the strip on their mat into four groups: needs (A to D), links (E and F), claims (G to I), impacts (strip J). Read each group its strip once, about 40 seconds a group. The claims and impacts strips are written on the mats after class and read at the next meeting.

#### 4 min. Return and revise

Day 41's items and day 42's assessment left captions D and E on the mats. Each child revises their own needs and model mats, independently, at most two captions, from the caption's picture only [[s04-P43-2]]. No new interview; no sitting is repeated.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X43a]], then [[s04-X43b]], once each; everyone touches or says, using the claim strip. Hear a rotating six.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each sequence in three boxes. Cut the kite from paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No eats cards: draw a squirrel, a rabbit, a goat, an acorn, clover and grass.

**Skip.** The groups, the sequences and the captions.

- **10 min. Match.** Your child draws a line from each animal to its food [[s04-P43-1]].
  **Say.** "Do they all eat the same?" Expect: no, each animal has its own food.
- **10 min. Look.** Find a worn path, a hole or a root lifting a paving stone, outdoors or in a picture. Ask what changed and what it gave.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X43a]] and [[s04-X43b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one caption each. Never cut the sittings or the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: change, roots, shelter, channel, standing. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing, drawing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 starts unit 5 and brings back the sentence "I think ___, because I saw ___". If the exit showed nothing changed (error G) or only people change things (error H), the claims strip is written on the mat after class and read at the next meeting. Impacts (error J) and a liking offered as the reason (error I) wait there too.
