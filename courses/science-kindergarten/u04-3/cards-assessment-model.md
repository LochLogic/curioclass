# Model place map and resource cards (day 42)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that this place is imaginary. One map for the front table, and a reduced copy of the same map in each of the three boxes of every child's mat, so every child answers on their own page.

## The map

An imaginary place on one page: a sunny bed with wet soil; a grassy bank with a burrow hole; a pond; wind swirls for air; a patch of trees; a garden plot; a house with a door and a stack of wood beside it. Small marks beside the pieces: a sun, a blue drop, a swirl, a brown patch. The sunny bed and the garden plot each carry all four marks (sun, drop, swirl, brown patch). The grassy bank carries a swirl, a brown patch and green grass tufts, and no drop (it is dry); the pond sits beside it. The pond carries a drop and a swirl only. The patch of trees carries a swirl and a brown patch (it is shaded, no sun mark). The house carries a swirl only. The burrow mouth carries none.

## The cards

A plant card (a small red-flowered plant), a prairie dog card (a round animal, side view, alone on a plain ground line), a people card (a grandparent, an adult and a child, no faces) and the resource marks.

## Items

**M1a:** "Invented for practice. Here is a plant card. On the map in your first box, draw the plant where it can get what it needs." (All children draw at once on their own mat.)
**M1b:** "Draw a line from the plant to each thing in its place that it uses."
**M2a:** "Here is a prairie dog card, an animal that digs and eats grass. On the map in your second box, draw the animal where it can get what it needs." (All at once.)
**M2b:** "Draw lines from the animal to what it uses in its place."
**M3a:** "Here are the people at their house. Draw lines from the people to what they use."
**M3b:** "Say one thing in this place that the people and the plants both use."
