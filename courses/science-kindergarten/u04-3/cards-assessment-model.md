# Model place map and resource cards (day 42)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that this place is imaginary. One map for the front table and a mat for each child.

## The map

An imaginary place on one page: a sunny bed with wet soil; a grassy bank with a burrow hole; a pond; wind swirls for air; a patch of trees; a garden plot; a house with a door. Small marks beside the pieces: a sun, a blue drop, a swirl, a brown patch.

## The cards

A plant card (a small red-flowered plant), a prairie dog card (a round animal at a burrow mouth), a people card (two adults and a child, no faces) and the resource marks.

## Items

**M1a:** "Invented for practice. Here is a plant card. Put the plant where it can get what it needs." **Key:** the sunny bed with wet soil. "It can live anywhere" is error E.
**M1b:** "Draw a line from the plant to each thing in its place that it uses." **Key:** lines to the sun, the water, the air and the soil. A missing line is shown with help, no letter.
**M2a:** "Here is a prairie dog card, an animal that digs and eats grass. Put the animal where it can get what it needs." **Key:** the grassy bank. Putting it where its resources are missing is error E.
**M2b:** "Draw lines from the animal to what it uses in its place." **Key:** lines to the bank (shelter), the grass (food), the pond (water) and the air. No lines and "it can live anywhere" is error E.
**M3a:** "Here are the people at their house. Draw lines from the people to what they use." **Key:** lines to the pond water, the garden food, the tree wood and the air. No lines, or "buildings mean people do not need nature", is error F. Labels alone do not earn the point.
**M3b:** "Tell how this place works together." **Key:** one connected reason, such as "the pond water goes to the plants and to the people." "The people do not need anything here" is error F.
