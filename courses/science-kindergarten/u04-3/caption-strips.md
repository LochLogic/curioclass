# Caption strips A to J (days 42 and 43)

Held back. Ten pre-printed strips for the teacher; children do not read them. After day 41 the teacher writes the strip that fits on each child's needs mat (A to D) and after day 42 on each model mat (E and F). On day 43 each error group hears its strip once, read in a quiet voice, in the three minutes of Read the captions: needs (A to D) and links (E and F). Claims and impacts (G to J) are written on the mats after day 43 and read on day 44. Each strip is two short sentences: what to look at and what to try. A child revises at most two captions. Plain strips of paper, the letter large at the left, a plain picture hint at the right.

- **A. Picture hint: a watered pot standing up.** "Plants take in water too. Put a drop on the plant."
- **B. Picture hint: a green plant in sun beside a pale plant in a box.** "Water and light. Say what the green plant had."
- **C. Picture hint: a mouse with seeds beside a watered plant.** "Animals eat. A plant is watered, not fed a meal."
- **D. Picture hint: two different meal tags.** "Different animals eat different foods. Match each tag."
- **E. Picture hint: a line that stops before the water.** "A living thing needs what its place gives. Find the missing line."
- **F. Picture hint: a line from the water source to a person.** "People use water from the land. Follow the line."
- **G. Picture hint: roots pushing soil aside.** "Plants can change a place. Point to what changed."
- **H. Picture hint: an animal's den in a bank.** "Animals change places too. Say who changed it."
- **I. Picture hint: an eye beside a cloud.** "A seeing is evidence. Say what you saw."
- **J. Picture hint: two routes, one with plants standing.** "Count the plants left on each route. Which leaves more?"

**Note.** The captions say what to try, never what is wrong with a child. Claims and impacts (G to J) are written after day 43 and read at the next meeting.
