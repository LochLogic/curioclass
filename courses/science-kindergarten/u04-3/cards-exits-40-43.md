# Exit cards, days 40 and 43

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse Pip's town. Mark on the one scale.

## Day 40

**Picture.** A town needs somewhere to park bikes. Spot 1 is a bare paved corner. Spot 2 covers a strip of wildflowers where bees visit.
**Spoken 40a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the spot that leaves more living things in place." **Key:** the paved corner. The wildflower spot, or "they are the same", is error J.
**Spoken 40b:** "Same pictures. Why that one?" **Key:** it leaves the flowers and the bees. "I like it" is error I.

## Day 43 (section exit, response, not scored)

**Pictures.** A tree over bare ground in summer, and the same tree and ground in autumn with leaves lying on the ground.
**Spoken 43a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the part of the place the tree changed." **Key:** the ground, now covered with leaves. "Nothing changed because plants cannot change things" is error G.
**Spoken 43b:** "Same pictures. Say: I think, because I saw." **Key:** a claim naming the tree and a picture, such as "I think the tree covered the ground with leaves, because I saw leaves under it." "Only people change places" is error H.
