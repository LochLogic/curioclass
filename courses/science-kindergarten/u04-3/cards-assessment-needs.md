# Needs assessment panels (day 41)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. These are other animals, plants and scenes than days 32 to 35. One copy for the front table.

## Panel N1. The caretaker's delivery log

Two rounds. Each round shows a potted plant with red flowers being watered, a mouse drinking from a dish, a turtle drinking at a tub and a child drinking from a cup, mixed with pictures of sweeping and resting. The water labels are missing.
**Spoken N1a:** "Invented for practice. Put a water drop on every picture where something is taking in water." **Key:** eight drops: the plant, the mouse, the turtle and the child in each round, no drop on the sweeping or resting pictures. No drop on the plant is error A.
**Spoken N1b:** "What did all of these need?" **Key:** water.

## Panel N2. The recovery sequence

Three pictures. First: potted plants in closed boxes with a watering can beside them. Second: the same plants unpacked into a sunny window with the same watering can. Third: days later, the plants with new green leaves. Cards: the sun in the window; the watering can.
**Spoken N2a:** "The watering stayed the same. Touch what was new for the plants after unpacking." **Key:** the sun in the window. The watering can is error B.
**Spoken N2b:** "What else does a plant need, besides water?" **Key:** light. "Water is enough" is error B.

## Panel N3. The meal records

A mouse swallowing seeds; a turtle eating a lettuce leaf; a child eating rice and fish; a potted plant being watered. A cart label with a plant pot and a plate of soil.
**Spoken N3a:** "Touch every picture where something eats a meal." **Key:** the mouse, the turtle and the child. The plant is error C.
**Spoken N3b:** "The cart label says the soil is the plant's meal. Is the label right? Touch yes or no." **Key:** no. Yes is error C.

## Panel N4. The meal tags

Three tags: seeds, a lettuce leaf, rice and fish.
**Spoken N4a:** "Match each meal tag to who ate it." **Key:** seeds to the mouse, the lettuce leaf to the turtle, rice and fish to the child.
**Spoken N4b:** "Do the mouse, the turtle and the child all eat the same? Touch yes or no." **Key:** no. Yes is error D.
