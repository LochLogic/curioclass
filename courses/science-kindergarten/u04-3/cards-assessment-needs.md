# Needs assessment panels (day 41)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. These are other animals, plants and scenes than days 32 to 35. One copy for the front table.

## Panel N1. The caretaker's delivery log

Two rounds. Each round shows a potted plant with red flowers being watered, a mouse drinking from a dish, a turtle drinking at a tub and a child drinking from a cup, mixed with pictures of sweeping and resting. The water labels are missing. A reduced copy of this log is printed in box 1 of each child's needs mat, so each child puts drops on their own.
**Spoken N1a:** "Invented for practice. Put a water drop on every picture where something is taking in water."
**Spoken N1b:** "What did all of these need?"

## Panel N2. The recovery sequence

Three pictures. First: potted plants in closed boxes with a watering can beside them. Second: the same plants unpacked into a sunny window with the same watering can. Third: days later, the plants with new green leaves. Cards: the sun in the window; the watering can; the pots.
**Spoken N2a:** "Touch what was new for the plants after unpacking."
**Spoken N2b:** "What new thing did the plants in the window get that helps them?"

## Yes and no cards

Two large cards for the front table: a big yes mark and a big no mark. They answer N3b and N4b; each child touches one.

## Panel N3. The meal records

A mouse swallowing seeds; a turtle eating a lettuce leaf; a child eating rice and fish; a potted plant being watered. A cart label with a plant pot and a plate of soil.
**Spoken N3a:** "Touch every picture where something eats a meal."
**Spoken N3b:** "The cart label says the soil is the plant's meal. Is the label right? Touch yes or no."

## Panel N4. The meal tags

Three tags: seeds, a lettuce leaf, rice and fish. A strip of three eaters (the mouse, the turtle and the child), one strip for the third box of each mat.
**Spoken N4a:** "Use the meal records. Match each tag to the one who ate it there." Ask it after N3a, with the N3 panel face up.
**Spoken N4b:** "Do the mouse, the turtle and the child all eat the same? Touch yes or no."
