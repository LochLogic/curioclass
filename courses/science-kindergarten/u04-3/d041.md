# Day 41: What do living things need?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table the day before: panels N1 to N4 face down, five mats, crayons. Put one eats card set and a student page at each table. Rehearse a three-minute turn once. Keys: key-assessment-needs, key-plans-pips-town.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors, pictures only. You alone touch the two class plants and any soil; wash hands after. Allergies are checked. Nobody tastes or sniffs a plant. No outdoor look today.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 40's exit showed errors J and I for some. Two minutes each, in pairs. First, plans 1 and 2 side by side: each child touches each tick once and says the last number, for error J. Then say "I like it" and "I saw plants left standing"; each child touches the seeing, for error I.

#### 5 min. Closing explanation

At the wall, read the dated plant photographs, the saved fall photographs and the class chart. The class dictates four lines and you scribe them: what the weed needed (water and light), what the squirrel needed (food), and what the hole gave it (a safe place). Say the weed was seen in the crack; nobody saw it form, so the class does not say it made the crack. Read the question board; dot each question still open.

#### 3 min. Stations

Seat group 1 at the front table. Say "These pictures are invented for practice, and new to you." The other groups sort the five eats cards into two boxes [[s04-P41-2]], count each box by touching each card once [[s04-P41-3]], then draw what the weed needed [[s04-P41-1]]. A finished child draws a second need on drawing paper.

#### 18 min. Six groups observed, 3 minutes each

Mats in front. Ask each item once, no coaching.
- 40 seconds: all five touch the meal records panel [[s04-N3a]]; then all five mark their own mat at once: the log in box 1 [[s04-N1a]], the recovery [[s04-N2a]], the meal tags [[s04-N4a]].
- 30 seconds, all at once: point [[s04-N1b]], [[s04-N2b]]; touch yes or no [[s04-N3b]], [[s04-N4b]].
- 70 seconds: each child's one brief reason, about 12 seconds: two meal tags that differ, and why [[s04-N4b]].
- 40 seconds to swap groups.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts. A child away is marked not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper into three boxes for the mat. No plants: use the dated photographs.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a squirrel, a rabbit, a person eating, a plant being watered and a plant in light.

**Skip.** The groups, the panels and the mat.

- **10 min. Look.** Look at a plant at home or in a picture. Ask what it needs.
- **12 min. Sort.** Your child sorts the five pictures into eats a meal and is watered [[s04-P41-2]].
  **Say.** "Does a plant eat a meal?" Expect: no, it is watered and needs light.
- **8 min. Draw.** Your child draws what a weed in a sidewalk crack needed [[s04-P41-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the sittings are the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: needs, water, light, eats, meal. Frame: "It needs ___." Pointing, drawing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. After class write the needs caption strips on the mats. Day 42 sits each child with a new place map; day 43 hands the captions back.
