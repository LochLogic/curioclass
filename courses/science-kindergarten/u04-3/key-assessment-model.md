# Teacher key: Model place map and resource cards (day 42)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-assessment-model.


## Items

- M1a: any place whose marks show the sun, the water drop, the air swirl and the soil together: the sunny bed or the garden plot. The bank, pond, trees, house or burrow mouth, or "it can live anywhere", is error E.
- M1b: lines to the sun, the water, the air and the soil marks at its place. A missing line is shown with help, no letter.
- M2a: the grassy bank. Putting it where its resources are missing is error E.
- M2b: lines to the bank (shelter), the grass (food), the pond (water) and the air. No lines and "it can live anywhere" is error E.
- M3a: lines to the pond water, the garden food, the stack of wood and the air. No lines, or "buildings mean people do not need nature", is error F. Labels alone do not earn the point.
- M3b: one shared resource, such as "water" or "air" (the pond water goes to the plants and to the people). "The people do not need anything here" is error F.
