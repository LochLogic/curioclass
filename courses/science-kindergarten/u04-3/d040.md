# Day 40: Two plans for houses

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pre-pair the class. Hang the day 37 town map, cut the person card, fill the cup, pull repair card I. Keys: key-map-pips-town, key-exits-40-43, key-plans-pips-town.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures, paper and one cup of water. You alone handle the two class plants; children stay seated. Wipe spills. Check plant allergies first. No tasting or sniffing; wash hands after soil.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

From day 39's exit, say each pair aloud; everyone touches the seeing: "I like it" or "I saw chips under the hole"; Those who touched a liking sit with you for repair card I.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Six children each touch one arrow on the class chart and say what it links [[s04-P40-8]].

#### 4 min. Hook

Say "Pip's town is invented. Its problem: the town wants more houses." Show the map. People change land for a home, as the squirrel digs a hole and the beaver builds a dam. Say it with no blame.

#### 8 min. Two plans

Pairs hold plans 1 and 2, six houses each (invented). They circle what each plan changes and tick each tree left standing. Each child draws the ticks on the page, plan 1 [[s04-P40-1]], plan 2 [[s04-P40-2]], then touches each tick once and says the last number [[s04-P40-3]], [[s04-P40-4]]. Expect eight, then one.
Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, 2 minutes: touch the plan that leaves more living things in place [[s04-P40-5]], then say "I think ___, because I saw ___" [[s04-P40-6]]. Expect plan 1: the trees and flowers stay.

#### 4 min. Add to the chart

Stand the person card by the houses; draw an arrow from the houses to shelter. Star plan 1 and say: more living things left in place. People need homes and people need trees; both plans try to meet a need. The star goes by the picture, never by who likes it.

#### 3 min. Our plants

Water one class plant with the small cup only if the soil is dry; the pot holds only so much, and the rest runs out. Children say what would waste water [[s04-P40-7]]. Mention no family or home.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented, not scored." Ask [[s04-X40a]], then [[s04-X40b]]; everyone touches or says. Hear the six, 15 seconds each, and mark the scale.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw six houses beside eight trees, then inside them with seven crossed out. No plants: show the dated photograph.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed plans: draw them as above.

**Skip.** The pairs, the chart and the plants.

- **8 min. Look.** Show the two invented plans. Ask what each changes.
- **12 min. Count.** Your child draws a tick for each tree left standing, plan 1 [[s04-P40-1]], plan 2 [[s04-P40-2]], and touches each tick once.
  **Say.** "Which plan leaves more living things in place?" Expect: plan 1, the trees and flowers stay.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Draw a bike rack on a bare corner and on a strip of flowers. [[s04-X40a]] and [[s04-X40b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: ticks for plan 1 only. Never cut the repair, counts or exit.

### Words and supports

Words: plan, problem, standing. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 opens with two minutes each: error J (the wildflower spot), count the plants left; error I (a liking as the reason), a liking beside a seeing.
