# Pip's town plans (day 40)

Said aloud to be invented. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. One copy for each pair; the same number of houses on each plan.

## Plan 1. The bare field edge

Six small houses are drawn along the edge of a bare field beside a road. Behind them stands the patch of eight trees, untouched, with small flowers at the foot of the trees.

## Plan 2. The wooded patch

The same six houses are drawn inside the patch of trees. Seven of the eight trees are crossed out as "cut"; one tree is left standing. The bare field lies empty beside it.

## What happens

Pairs mark what each plan changes (a crayon circle) and what it leaves standing (a tick). They count the trees left on each plan by touching each once, and say the last number. A rotating six says which plan leaves more living things in place, and why, from the picture.

## The small cup

After the plans the teacher waters one class plant with a small cup, only when the soil is dry, because a pot holds only so much and the rest runs out. The children say what would waste water. No family or home is mentioned.
