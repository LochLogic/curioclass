# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 40 and 43

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-40-43.


## Day 40

- Spoken 40a: the flowers and the bees stay. "Nothing is left", or "the same on both spots", is error J.
- Spoken 40b: it leaves the flowers and the bees. "I like it" is error I.

## Day 43 (section exit, response, not scored)

- Spoken 43a: the ground, now covered with leaves. "Nothing changed because plants cannot change things" is error G.
- Spoken 43b: a claim naming the tree and a picture, such as "I think the tree covered the ground with leaves, because I saw leaves under it." "Only people change places" is error H.
