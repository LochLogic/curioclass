# Teacher key: Needs assessment panels (day 41)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-assessment-needs.


## Panel N1. The caretaker's delivery log

- Spoken N1a: eight drops: the plant, the mouse, the turtle and the child in each round, no drop on the sweeping or resting pictures. No drop on the plant is error A.
- Spoken N1b: water.

## Panel N2. The recovery sequence

- Spoken N2a: the sun in the window. The watering can or the pots is error B.
- Spoken N2b: light. "Water is enough" is error B.

## Panel N3. The meal records

- Spoken N3a: the mouse, the turtle and the child. The plant is error C.
- Spoken N3b: no. Yes is error C.

## Panel N4. The meal tags

- Spoken N4a: seeds to the mouse, the lettuce leaf to the turtle, rice and fish to the child, as the meal records show. Seeds to the turtle on a first try is shown with help, no letter.
- Spoken N4b: no. Yes is error D. Its one brief reason: points to two meal tags that differ and says why, for example "different meals".
