# Section 4.3 opener: people change places too, then the unit is shown

## What the section does

Four days, 40 to 43. Day 40 adds people clearing land: Pip's invented town needs houses, and there are two plans for the same number of houses, one on a bare field edge and one on a wooded patch. The class compares what each plan changes and what it leaves standing. It is said without blame. Days 41, 42 and 43 are the end-of-unit observation days: every child, in a group of five at a time, shows what the unit taught. Day 41 is four needs patterns (water, light, eating, different foods). Day 42 is a place model with lines. Day 43 is an argument from change evidence, then a response part.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. On day 40 the class works as a whole class. On days 41 to 43 one front table sees one group of five at a time, three or four minutes each, while the other groups sort familiar needs pictures on their own. The teacher records each child, never a spokesperson, on a mat of their own. No coaching and no reteaching during the observation. Captions on the mats are read on day 42 and day 43.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **People change land to meet needs, as other living things do.** Said without blame, and without judging any family's use of resources.
- **Compare what each plan leaves standing.** Count the plants left on each plan by touching each once.
- **A liking is not a seeing.** "I saw plants left standing" is evidence.
- **A pattern is what we saw again and again.** Water for all; light for plants; animals eat and a plant is not fed a meal; different animals eat different foods.
- **A model shows relationships.** Lines from a plant, an animal or people to what the place gives.
- **An argument names the object and the picture.** "I think ___, because I saw ___."

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** "Plants do not need water." **B:** "Water is enough." **C:** "The plant eats soil." **D:** "All animals eat the same."
- **E:** "It can live anywhere." **F:** "Buildings mean people do not need nature."
- **G:** "Plants cannot change places." **H:** "Only people change things."
- **I:** "I like it." **J:** "Both routes are the same."

## Safety

Plants and soil are handled only by the teacher; wash hands after soil. Allergies are checked before a plant is chosen. Any outdoor look needs a checked route and clear ground; it is skipped if unsafe. Pictures and cards are used otherwise.

## Supplies

Pip's town plans, the bike-spot and tree panels, the delivery log, recovery and meal-tag panels, the model place map, the three change sequences, the kite card, the three response mats, the caption strips A to J, crayons and pre-cut cards. A room with nothing uses the same pictures drawn by the teacher on paper.
