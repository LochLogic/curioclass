# Assessment cards, day 19 (F1 to F3), held back

For the day 19 observation only. Never show, teach from or send home before day 19. One set for each of six table sets. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except date numerals, no brand names.

**Credit, printed on every master below (the teacher reads it):** forecast page: "Curio Class made this picture from the weather service forecast issued on 21 June 2024 for northeastern Colorado. It is a Curio Class picture, not the official forecast." Records: "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University. Denver International Airport station (KDEN) and Seattle-Tacoma Airport station (KSEA), 1 to 30 June 2023. Each square is a day when thunder was reported at the station. It counts days, not damage."

## The June 21 page

**First picture (F1).** A storm symbol in a cloud, a clock with no hands and a question mark on its face, and an outing symbol (a line of small children walking to a bus stop). Date line: 21 June 2024.
**Timing picture (F2a).** The same page with a second picture: two clock cards, a morning clock and a clock for the afternoon and evening, drawn the same size and plain.

**F1 spoken:** "Here is the first picture of a real page for one day in June: a storm symbol, a clock with no hands and an outing. Ask a question that helps the school get ready."
**F2a spoken:** "Same page. Touch when the storms could come."

## The June 2023 rows

Two long rows for June 2023. Denver: eleven squares, each a cloud with a zigzag mark and a date numeral. Seattle: an empty row.
**F2b spoken:** "Here are two rows of days with thunder reported at a station in June in one year. Denver has eleven squares and Seattle has none. Touch the place that has storm days more often."

## F3a and F3b: cards

**Before the storm.** Card one: children carrying blocks into a classroom and a signpost marked with a roof; "plan indoor play and name the shelter first". Card two: a clock for the afternoon and children still at play outside, with no plan made; "stay out until thunder".
**F3a spoken:** "Touch what the school does before the storm."
**Where we go.** Card three: a substantial school building with an open door. Card four: a single tall tree.
**F3b spoken:** "Touch where we go if thunder is heard."
