# Day 19: Ask, get ready, and revise

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay six table sets of the unfamiliar forecast picture sequence and the June 2023 rows (held back), one forecast mat for each child. Return the day 18 weather mats. Leave the class picture chart and the four-box chain on the wall. Sort the caption strips A to H by the ticks on day 18 and day 17. Have the record sheet at hand. Keys: key-assessment-forecast, key-exit-19.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. Calm pictures only. If a child speaks of a frightening storm, thank them quietly and go on.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

As day 18. Seat group 1 at its set; everyone else sorts weather squares in pairs, then draws one way people get ready for a storm.

#### 3 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, each with a mat, marking at once. Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching:
- 2 min: [[s02-F1]], each child asks aloud, 10 seconds each; then [[s02-F2a]] and [[s02-F2b]], everyone touching.
- 1 min: [[s02-F3a]] and [[s02-F3b]], everyone touching.

Hear one brief reason from each child as they touch. A question the page cannot answer goes on the not-on-this-page card and is marked shown. Mark each component on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a child's first unaided try counts.

#### 3 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. No child is observed twice.

#### 2 min. Read the chart

All together, read the live two-month chart as review: which weather has the longest row. No day 18 evidence is collected again.

#### 2 min. Read feedback captions

To each group, about 20 seconds, read aloud the caption strip for the ticks on its mats, beside the child's own marks. Say what to try, never what is wrong.

#### 3 min. Return and revise

Hand-backs: day 18's assessment, errors A and B; day 17 exit errors F and G; day 19 errors D, E, F, G and H. Each child revises their own mat, at most two captions, the two lowest components first. Unfinished revisions go to day 20.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say "this is a real page for one day in June, and this is not scored." Ask [[s02-X19a]] and [[s02-X19b]] once each; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: draw a cloud, an afternoon clock and a wind picture. No rows: draw a long row of squares and an empty row.

**Skip.** The groups, the chart and the captions.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Say "this is a real page." Ask five things once each and do not help: ask a question that helps the school get ready, touch when storms could come, touch the place that has storm days more often, touch what the school does before the storm, touch where we go if thunder is heard.
  **Say.** "Why?" Expect: a reason in any words.
- **10 min. Try again.** Your child tries one part they liked least.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19a]] and [[s02-X19b]] Ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Frames: "I want to find out ___." "We plan ___ before the storm." Pointing, drawing, symbols and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Component marks are assigned the next school day. Day 20, in unit 3, reads the chart again; unfinished revisions go to its chart retrieval.
