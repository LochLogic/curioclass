# Exit cards, days 15 to 17

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except date numerals and the caption, no brand names. Do not reuse a picture from the July page, the read-aloud, the regional July squares or the action cards.

**Credit, printed at the foot of every forecast page below (the teacher reads it):** "Curio Class made this picture from the weather service forecast issued on the date shown, for northeastern Colorado. It is a Curio Class picture, not the official forecast." **Credit for every record row:** "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University. Denver International Airport station (KDEN) and Seattle-Tacoma Airport station (KSEA)."

## Day 15 cards: June 20, 2024

**Page.** A flat plain under a tall cloud with a storm symbol, a clock hand set for the afternoon and a second set for the evening, and a curved arrow between them. Date line: 20 June 2024.
**Clock cards.** A clock for the morning (15a) and a clock for the afternoon and evening (15b), two clocks side by side.

**Spoken:** "This is a real forecast page for one day in June. Say one thing you want to find out from this page, starting with: I want to find out."

## Day 16 cards: June 22, 2024

**First page.** A tall cloud with a storm symbol and a clock set for late in the day. Date line: 22 June 2024. **Second page.** A radar-like picture with the morning's clouds fading to one side and a small sun, and a morning clock. Date line: 22 June 2024.

**Spoken:** "Pip has two real pages for one day in June. The first page shows when storms could come late in the day. The second page shows the morning clouds fading. Say a question the second page can answer and the first page cannot, starting with: I want to find out."

## Day 17 cards: August records, and June 23, 2024

**Records 17a.** Two long rows. Denver, August 2023: ten squares, each a cloud with a zigzag mark and a date numeral.
**Spoken:** "Here are two places in the month of August in one year. Denver has ten squares and Seattle has none. Each square is a day when thunder was reported at a station. Say a question you want to ask these rows, starting with: I want to find out."

**Page 17b.** A plain page with a small cloud and a storm symbol, a clock hand for late in the day, and a second small cloud with a question mark. Date line: 23 June 2024.
**Cards 17c and 17d.** 17c: a clock for the late morning and children carrying blocks into a classroom. 17d: a clock for the afternoon and a child with a hand at an ear.
**Spoken:** "Here is a real page for one day in June. It says a few storms could come late in the day, but not many. Say when we should plan indoor play." Show 17c and 17d only if a child cannot say it.
