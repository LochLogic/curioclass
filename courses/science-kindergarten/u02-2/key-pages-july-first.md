# Teacher key: Forecast pages for one day in July (days 15, 16 and 17)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece pages-july-first.


## Page 2: the discussion

- Spoken (day 16): that earlier storms were already moving away, and where. A child who says "it tells what happened" has met the idea of an observation; the same page also says what may come later (error E is not scored here: practice only).

## Model question

- The model question: "I want to find out when the storms could come." The page answers it: today and tonight. "What do we do?" the page does not say outright; the class answers it with the strip: page, plan indoor play before the afternoon, indoor recess.
