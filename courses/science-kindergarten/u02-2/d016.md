# Day 16: What does a second page tell us?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy page two of the calm forecast picture sequence six times, with a set of repair cards for each table. Keep the page, plan, recess strip on the wall. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. If a child speaks of a frightening storm, thank them quietly and go on.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Day 15's exit showed errors D and E for many. The larger error group works with you. For D, lay the might-or-might-not picture beside their answer and say "possible." For E, order three cards [[s02-P16-2]]. The rest, in pairs, use the repair cards: pick the card, point, tell a partner.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Pairs at once. Each says "I want to find out ___" with a question card; the partner points to a picture on the first page [[s02-P16-0]].

#### 5 min. Second source

Show the July 1 discussion page, a second page about the same day. **Ask.** "What can this page tell us that the first one did not?" Expect: the earlier storms are moving away; or where stronger storms may come later. Say "possible," never "will come." Practice only; nothing new is taught.

#### 8 min. Each child asks a new question

Six groups at their table copies. Each child asks a question the first page could not answer, such as "Have the storms gone?", and points to the picture on this page that answers it; about 40 seconds a child. Then each child draws one thing this page tells that the first did not [[s02-P16-1]]. Hear the six, 20 seconds each, as groups ask. A question this page cannot answer goes on the not-on-this-page card, kept and not corrected.

#### 4 min. Rug

Add a second page to the strip: two pages, plan, indoor recess. **Ask.** "Which page tells what we saw this morning? Which tells what may come later?" Expect: a point to each. Two pages tell more than one.

#### 1 min. Pack

Pages and cards into envelopes.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "these are two real pages for one day in June." Ask [[s02-X16]] once; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No prints: draw the fading clouds with an arrow, and a cloud with a late clock, on two scrap pages.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: draw page one (a cloud, a sun and a moon) and page two (fading clouds, an arrow, a small sun). No Pip: use a sock.

**Skip.** The group repair and the rug.

- **5 min. Two pages.** Show both pages. **Say.** "Which page tells what we saw this morning?" Expect: the page with the fading clouds.
- **12 min. A new question.** Your child asks one question the first page could not answer and points to the second page's answer. Then your child draws one thing the second page tells [[s02-P16-1]].
- **3 min. Order.** Lay three cards: what we saw, what the forecast says, what may come later.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X16]] Show two pages; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one question each, not two. Never cut the exit. Longer: ask the second page two questions.

### Words and supports

Words: second page, possible, saw, later. Frame: "The page says ___." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

If many touched the first page, day 17's warm-up asks which page tells what we saw, before it reads old storm records for two places.

### Credit
