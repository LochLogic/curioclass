# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 15 to 17

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-15-17.


## Day 15 cards: June 20, 2024

- Page: afternoon and evening storms.
- Spoken: a question the page can answer (when, what could happen, what we do). Asking what happened this morning is error E. A child who says "a storm will come, no doubt" is error D: put the word possible beside it. A question the page cannot answer has no register letter: record shown with help once the child points to a picture that could answer a different question.

## Day 16 cards: June 22, 2024

- First page: the first page gives late-day storms; the second says the morning storms are over.
- Spoken: a question about the morning or the earlier storms. A question the first page already answers is a wrong page: no register letter, record "shown with help".

## Day 17 cards: August records, and June 23, 2024

- Records 17a: 1, 2, 3, 5, 7, 18, 19, 24, 27 and 28 August 2023.) Seattle, August 2023: an empty row. Caption: "1 to 31 August 2023. Each square is a day when thunder was reported at the station. It counts days, not damage."
- Spoken: a question the rows can answer (which place, how many). "Both the same" is error G; a question about another month has no letter: record "shown with help".
- Page 17b: a few storms could come late in the day; the page leads with other news that is not pictured.
- Spoken: before the afternoon (17c). Saying after the first thunder (17d) is error F.
