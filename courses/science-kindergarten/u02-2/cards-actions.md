# Action cards (day 17)

Four cards for each child, used at the day 17 rug with the July page. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words, no brand names. The class chooses from them what to do and when.

**Plan before the afternoon.** A clock set for the late morning and, beside it, children carrying blocks and a ball into a school classroom. Says: plan indoor play before the afternoon.

**Wait for the first thunder.** A clock set for the afternoon, a child outside with a hand to an ear and a small zigzag mark in a cloud. Says: wait for thunder. (This is the wrong choice; the class sets it aside and says why: we get ready first.)

**Go into the school building.** A substantial school building with an open door and children walking in a line toward it. Says: if thunder is heard, go in.

**Stand under a tree.** A single tall tree with a child at its foot. Says: a tree. (This is the wrong choice; the class sets it aside: a tree is not shelter.)

**Key.** Plan before the afternoon; go into the school building. Say aloud: forecasters use records and today's page together, and the school plans before the danger and still follows the plan when thunder is heard. A child who picks wait or tree is not told off; set the card aside and say again why the other one is the plan.
