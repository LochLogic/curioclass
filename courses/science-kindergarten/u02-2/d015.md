# Day 15: I want to find out

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the calm forecast picture sequence (July 1 page) six times; a card set for each child. Lay out the read-aloud picture pages in order. Hang the three-box strip. Side table: five rain squares, a box, the live-month sheet. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. If a child speaks of a frightening storm, thank them quietly and go on.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 14's exit showed errors B and C for many. A small group puts the date square into both rows of the live-month sheet with you; say "one day, two rows." The rest, in pairs, slide five rain squares into the box while the partner counts [[s02-P15-0]].

#### 6 min. Hook: Before We Go Out

Rug. Say: "The story is invented. The weather page in it is a real one." Show and read the four pages. **Ask.** "Who asked a question? What answered it?" Expect: Noor asked; the helper's page answered.

#### 7 min. Teaching first: asking

Pip holds up the July 1 page: "I want to find out ___." Model: touch the when card and say "I want to find out when the storms could come." The sun, moon and cloud answer: today and tonight. "The page says storms are possible: they might come and they might not." Point to the cloud with the question mark. Show the other three cards. The class answers the last on the strip: page, plan, indoor recess. We plan indoor play before the afternoon; if thunder is heard, we go in. A question the page cannot answer, like last week's weather, goes on the not-on-this-page card: kept, never wrong.

#### 8 min. Each child asks

Fifteen pairs at once. Each child says "I want to find out ___" with a card; the partner points to the picture that could answer it; swap, 90 seconds a pair. Each draws that picture [[s02-P15-1]]. Hear the six, 30 seconds each.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "this is a real forecast page for one day in June." Show the June 20 page and two clocks. Ask [[s02-X15]] once; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 1 min. Pack

Pages and cards into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No prints: draw a cloud, a clock and a school door on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: draw a cloud, a sun with a moon and a school door on paper. No Pip: use a sock.

**Skip.** The story and the pairs.

- **5 min. The page.** Show the page. **Say.** "A forecast says what may happen later. Storms are possible today." Point to the cloud with the question mark: might, might not.
- **10 min. Ask the page.** Your child says "I want to find out ___" and you point to the picture that could answer. Then swap. Your child draws that picture [[s02-P15-1]].
- **5 min. Plan.** Lay three boxes: page, plan, indoor play. **Say.** "When do we plan to play inside?" Expect: before the afternoon.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X15]] Show a new page and two clocks; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: story pages 1 to 3. Never cut the exit. Longer: ask a second question.

### Words and supports

Words: forecast, possible, plan. Frame: "I want to find out ___." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

If many touched the morning clock (error E) or said a storm will come (error D), day 16 opens with a six-minute repair, then asks a second page.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
