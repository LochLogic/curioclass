# Caption strips A to H (day 19)

Eight pre-printed strips. On day 19 the teacher reads aloud, to each group, the strip for each tick on that child's mat, beside the child's own marks. Each strip is two short sentences: what to look at and what to try. Children do not read them. A child revises at most two captions in the three Return and revise minutes: the two lowest components first. Unfinished revisions go to the chart retrieval on day 20. Plain strips of paper; each strip has its letter large at the left and a plain picture hint at the right.

- **A. Picture hint: a morning and an afternoon panel with an arrow down.** "On one of the three days the afternoon was cooler. Circle that pair and say the claim again for all three days."
- **B. Picture hint: six rain squares and one arrow.** "Touch each square one time. Say the last number, and put your new number on your mat."
- **C. Picture hint: one date square with two arrows going to two rows.** "This is one day in two rows. Say it: one day, two rows." (Used when a repair from day 15 is still open.)
- **D. Picture hint: a cloud with a question mark and two arrows, a sun and a storm.** "The page says possible. It might and it might not. Say possible beside your answer."
- **E. Picture hint: three small cards in a row, an eye, a page, a clock.** "A forecast tells what may come later. Ask a question about later."
- **F. Picture hint: a plan card on the left, a storm card on the right.** "We get ready before the storm. Move the plan card to the left."
- **G. Picture hint: two long rows of squares, one full, one empty.** "Match the squares. One row has more. Say which one and replace your claim."
- **H. Picture hint: a school building with an open door.** "When thunder is heard, we go into the school building. Put the building on your mat."

**Note.** The captions say what to try, never what is wrong with a child. Read each in a quiet voice to the group. The strips are teacher-only; the mat is the child's.
