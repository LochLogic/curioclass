# Day 17: Old storm days help us get ready

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the record squares and July 1 pages six times. Hang the four-box chain, empty. Set the August records and June 23 page aside for the exit. Choose today's six. Keys: key-actions, key-exits-15-17, key-pages-july-first, key-squares-regional-july.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. If a child speaks of a frightening storm, thank them quietly and go on.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

In pairs, each child asks the July 1 page one question with a card, and the partner points to the answer [[s02-P17-0]]. If many touched the first page in day 16's exit, ask "Which page tells what we saw?" and point to the fading clouds.

#### 6 min. Model: the records

Lay Denver's and Seattle's July 2023 rows side by side. Read the caption: each square is a day when thunder was reported at the airport station. Denver has seventeen squares; you say the total. Seattle has none. Say: "This counts days, not damage, and it is not a rule for every day."

#### 7 min. Each child asks the records

Six groups. Each child asks one question (which place, how many, when) and points to the answer, about 35 seconds a child. Then each draws the place with more squares [[s02-P17-1]]. Hear the six, 20 seconds each.

#### 4 min. Rug: choose actions

Back to the July 1 page. Say: "Forecasters use records and today's page together." Show story page 4: the class went in before the afternoon. Each child holds up a card for when to plan indoor play, then where to go if thunder is heard. Wait and tree cards are set aside without blame: we get ready first; a tree is not shelter. Each child draws the plan [[s02-P17-2]].

#### 3 min. Revise the chain

Draw the four boxes with the class: records, patterns, prediction, plan. Say: "Records show a pattern. The pattern and today's page help us predict. A prediction leads to a plan, and we carry it out."

#### 3 min. Closing explanation: the coat hooks

Show day 10's coat hooks by the chart. From the morning and afternoon marks, dictate why coats came on in the morning; with no steady difference, say "it varies, so we check each day." Add: "A forecast helps us plan recess."

#### 1 min. Pack

Rows and cards away.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Show the August rows. Ask [[s02-X17a]], then show the June 23 page and ask [[s02-X17b]]. Pairs whisper; hear the same six, 20 seconds each.

### Other rooms

No prints: draw squares on scrap paper, a long row and an empty row.

### At home

**Swap.** No squares: cut paper squares and draw a cloud on each. No cards: draw a clock and a school door.

**Skip.** The groups and the chain.

- **8 min. Two rows.** Lay seventeen squares in one row and none in another. **Say.** "Which place has more days with thunder?" Expect: the long row. Your child draws it [[s02-P17-1]].
- **7 min. Plan.** **Say.** "When do we plan to play inside? Where do we go if thunder is heard?" Expect: before the afternoon; the school building. Your child draws the plan [[s02-P17-2]].
- **5 min. Four boxes.** Draw records, patterns, prediction, plan.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17a]] and [[s02-X17b]] Ask once each and your child says each answer.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the chain drawing. Never cut the exit. Longer: lay two months side by side.

### Words and supports

Words: records, plan, building. Frame: "The row with more squares is ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Errors F and G go to day 19's Return and revise.
