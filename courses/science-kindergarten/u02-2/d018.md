# Day 18: Our weather rows, shared

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay six table sets of the weather assessment cards, one mat for each child. Put the weather squares for sorting in envelopes. Leave the chart and day 17's four-box chain on the wall, unchanged. The unfamiliar forecast picture sequence stays in its folder until tomorrow. Have the record sheet at hand. Key: key-assessment-weather.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. The assessment cards stay held back: do not show or teach from them before today.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Six table sets, thirty mats, crayons. Seat group 1 at its set. Everyone else works in pairs: sort weather squares into sunny, cloudy, rainy and windy rows, then count the sunny row together and draw one weather day you remember.

#### 4 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, each with a mat, marking at once. Say "these pictures are from another observer's record." Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching:
- 1 min: [[s02-W1a]], then [[s02-W1b]].
- 1 min: [[s02-W2]], yes or no on the mat.
- 2 min: [[s02-W3a]], then [[s02-W3b]]; hear five reasons, one from each child in the group, 12 seconds each, said, pointed or drawn.

Steady the cards but never give the answer. Mark each component on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a child's first unaided try counts. Mark the tick for error A on W2 and error B on W1b.

#### 4 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1. The next group walks over on your signal.

#### 4 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. No child is observed twice.

#### 4 min. Change-overs and packing

Collect the mats and sets into the folder. A child absent today is seen at the next meeting while the others sort; if not seen, mark not yet seen. The sorters put the squares away.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No squares: draw a row of six rain squares and a row of two. No cards: draw a day with cloud, rain and wind.

**Skip.** The groups and the sorting.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say "these pictures are from another observer's record." Ask five things once each and do not help: touch every true picture for a day, touch each rainy day once and say the last number, say if the afternoon was warmer on all three days, touch the month with more rain, and show how you know.
  **Say.** "Show me how you know." Expect: the squares matched one to one, with some left over.
- **10 min. Look at the marks.** Point to a mark on the mat and ask what it shows.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Frame: "There are more ___ days than ___ days." Pointing, drawing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 19 reads feedback from your ticks, A and B, and revises them, after it observes forecasts for every child.
