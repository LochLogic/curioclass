# Before We Go Out

A picture read-aloud for day 15 (heard again on day 17's rug). Four pages, one scene each, one actor a scene. Before you show it, say who made it and what it is: "We made this picture story for our class. The story is invented. The weather page in it is a real one." Show one page at a time and read the words under it. Children do not read; the only printed word is the title on page 1. Pause after page 2 and ask: what could the helper show? Nobody has to answer out loud.

Drawing style for all pages: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words. People are drawn with different skin tones and hair. The story is calm: no frightening weather is drawn. The sky outside the door is a plain sky with a few clouds.

## Page 1: the teacher at the door

**Actor:** the teacher. **Picture:** A classroom door open on a playground with a slide and a bench. The teacher stands at the door holding a round wall clock in both hands. The title across the top in large letters: Before We Go Out.

**Read:** At the door stood the teacher. The teacher held a clock. "Soon it will be time for outdoor play," the teacher said, "but first we must find out about this afternoon."

## Page 2: the child asks

**Actor:** Noor, a child. **Picture:** Noor stands alone in the doorway, one hand up, mouth a round O. A question mark floats over Noor's head and, inside it, a small clock.

**Read:** Noor put up a hand. "When?" Noor asked. "When might the weather change?"

## Page 3: the helper answers with the page

**Actor:** the office helper. **Picture:** The office helper at a desk holds up a single page. The page shows three pictures in a row: a cloud over flat land, a clock with its hands set for the afternoon and evening, and a small child on a slide. The helper's other hand points to the clock picture.

**Read:** The office helper held up a page. "This is a forecast," said the helper. "It says storms might come this afternoon and evening. They might. They might not. Let us get ready first."

## Page 4: the class goes inside to play

**Actor:** the class, as one group. **Picture:** The whole class in a line, walking away from the playground toward the open school door, with a game of blocks and a ball visible inside. The sky behind them is plain with a few clouds. The clock on the wall inside shows a morning hour.

**Read:** So the class went inside to play before the afternoon came. They stacked the blocks. They rolled the ball. And when the afternoon came, they were ready.

## After the story

Ask: "Who asked a question? Who answered it with a page?" Say it again aloud: the story is invented, the weather page is real. Do not say a storm came. Keep one picture of the page on the wall.

**Source:** our own story; no outside tale or picture book is used.
