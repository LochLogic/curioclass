# Regional record squares, July (day 17)

Two long rows of small squares, one for Denver and one for Seattle, for the month of July in one year (2023). Six table copies. A square means a day when thunder was reported at the airport station. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words except date numerals.

**Caption printed at the foot (the teacher reads it):** "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University. Denver International Airport station (KDEN) and Seattle-Tacoma Airport station (KSEA), 1 to 31 July 2023. Each square is a day when thunder was reported at the station. It counts days, not damage."

**Denver row.** Seventeen squares, each with a small cloud and a zigzag mark and a date numeral.

**Seattle row.** No squares, only the empty row and the caption.

**Model.** Lay the two rows side by side. Say: "Denver has more squares than Seattle." Say aloud: this counts days with thunder reported at a station, not damage, and it is not a rule for every day. Take one question for each of which place, how many, when, and point to the answer.

**Held back.** The August 2023 rows (Denver ten squares, Seattle none) are for the day 17 exit and the June 2023 rows (Denver eleven, Seattle none) are for the day 19 assessment. Do not print them for teaching.
