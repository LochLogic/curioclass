# Question cards, and the not-on-this-page card

Five small cards for each child (a set in each envelope; two sets for pairs). Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words. The teacher says the question aloud and the child points to the card, or says "I want to find out ___" using the starter Pip models.

**When.** A clock with a curved arrow and a question mark.
**Where.** A plain map outline with a dot and a question mark.
**What could happen.** A cloud with a question mark and a small storm symbol.
**What do we do.** A child with a hand out and an arrow to a school building, and a question mark.
**Not on this page.** A page with a dotted outline in one corner and a question mark floating off the edge. A question the page cannot answer (what was the weather last week) goes on this card. It is kept, not corrected, and never marked an error.

**Pip's starter.** "I want to find out ___." Pip says it with the first card, a child says it with a card, and the partner points to the picture on the page that could answer it.

**Used on.** Day 15 (the first page), day 16 (the second page) and day 17 (the regional records, with which place, how many, when).
