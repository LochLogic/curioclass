# Repair cards (day 16, errors D and E)

Small cards for the children not working with the teacher in the six repair minutes. A child picks up the card that matches the error the record shows, looks, points to the answer, then tells a partner. Plain line drawings, no printed words. These reuse earlier ideas on a new page: a forecast for a school field trip to a farm, invented for practice and said aloud to be invented.

**D, possible.** A page with a cloud and a clock for the afternoon, and beside it a small card with a cloud that has a question mark and two arrows, one to a sun and one to a storm symbol. Say: "the page says storms are possible this afternoon; we get ready." Point to the might-or-might-not picture and say the word possible.

**E, three cards in order.** (1) "What we saw": a child looking up at a plain sky. (2) "What the forecast says": a forecast page. (3) "What may come later": a cloud and a clock for later. Put them in order, left to right, and say what each one is.
