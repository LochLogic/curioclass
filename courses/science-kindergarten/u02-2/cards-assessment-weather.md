# Assessment cards, day 18 (W1 to W3), held back

For the day 18 observation only. Never show, teach from or send home before day 18. One set for each of six table sets. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except date numerals, no brand names. Squares are the size of the class chart squares.

**Credit, printed on every master below (the teacher reads it):** "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University, Denver International Airport station (KDEN). Dates as marked." Teacher readings are in brackets and are never printed for children.

## W1a: one day in September

A day square for 4 September 2024 showing its afternoon record: a cloud, rain drops and wind lines. Four sky squares lie loose in a row beside it: sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy.
**Spoken:** "Here is one day in September in Denver, made from another observer's record. Touch every weather picture that is true for this day in the afternoon."

## W1b: the rainy days of September

Six rain squares in a row with date numerals: 4, 5, 15, 17, 21 and 22 September 2024.
**Spoken:** "Here are the rainy days of that September, one square each. Touch each rainy day once, and say the last number."

## W2: three days, morning and afternoon

Three day cards, 16, 17 and 18 September 2024, each with a morning panel and an afternoon panel and a warmth mark between them. 16 September: the morning sky part sun, part cloud, the afternoon sky cloudy, an arrow up. 17 September: the morning sky part sun, part cloud, the afternoon sky cloudy, an arrow down. 18 September: sunny in both, an arrow up.
**Spoken:** "Here are three days in Denver, each with a morning picture and an afternoon picture, made from another observer's record. Is the afternoon the warmer time on all three days? Touch yes or no."

## W3a and W3b: two months

Two rows of rain squares. September: six squares (4, 5, 15, 17, 21 and 22). October: two squares (18 and 21 October 2024).
**W3a spoken:** "Here is the row of rainy days for September and the row for October. Which month has more rainy days? Touch it."
**W3b spoken:** "Show me how you know."
