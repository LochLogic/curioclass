# Exit cards, day 19 (response, not scored)

Two spoken questions at the end of day 19, asked of the whole class on a new page. They are response, not scored; they show whether the revision helped. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words except date numerals and the caption, no brand names. Do not reuse a picture from the June 21 page or the caption strips.

**Credit, printed at the foot (the teacher reads it):** "Curio Class made this picture from the weather service forecast issued on 24 June 2024 in the morning, for northeastern Colorado. It is a Curio Class picture, not the official forecast."

**Page.** A tall cloud with a storm symbol and a clock set for the afternoon, and a wind picture: two long curved lines streaming sideways. Date line: 24 June 2024.

**Cards for 19a.** (1) A clock beside a picture of children on a playground with a question mark: when might outdoor play be unsafe. (2) A calendar page with a backward arrow: what was the weather last week.
**19a spoken:** "Here is a real page for one day in June. It shows an afternoon storm clock and a wind picture. Touch the question this page can answer."

**Picture for 19b.** The same page. No cards.
**19b spoken:** "Same page. Tell me one reason the school plans indoor play first."
