# Day 23: Will it reach again?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Leave each group's day 22 build in place with its release spot taped. Put one larger soft ball at each table; check that it is too big to mouth. Choose today's six. Key: key-exits-20-24.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. One child rolls at a time; nothing is thrown; retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. Check each build is as left. Lower a ramp after any bounce. With none, show photographs.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 22's exit showed errors C and E for many. The larger group works with you: roll a gentle and a harder push down two lanes and watch the start, then lay the stopping dots beside it. Then lay each result beside the want and touch yes or no [[s03-P23-0]]. The rest use the repair cards in pairs.

#### 3 min. Warm-up: guess cards

Practice only. Show the larger ball beside the ramp. Each child puts down a guess card, reach or not reach [[s03-P23-1]]. Nothing is marked right or wrong.

#### 12 min. Test the bigger ball

Three groups test while three draw; swap at 5 minutes. Each group keeps its ramp and release spot and changes only the ball. Each child rolls once, about 40 seconds, and moves a square [[s03-P23-2]]. Nothing new is taught. Hear the six: "Did it do what we wanted?"

#### 4 min. Rug: read the rows

Six children, one for each group, touch their five squares once; the class counts the reached and the missed aloud on the class chart, as in the picture graph. **Ask.** "Did every roll reach?" If all thirty reached, or none, say so and show the invented rows from the repair cards, so "sometimes" has something to point at.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the four pictures and ask [[s03-X23]] once. Everyone whispers the prediction to a partner, then hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipes from the table tub, bags, eye protection.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw five squares on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: tilt a tray on a book. No blocks: stack three cups. No second ball: use a bigger or smaller soft ball or a rolled sock. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The groups and the class chart.

- **5 min. Guess.** Show the bigger ball beside the ramp. Your child puts down reach or not reach [[s03-P23-1]].
- **12 min. Test.** Roll the bigger ball down the same ramp from the same spot, five times. After each, your child moves a square [[s03-P23-2]].
  **Say.** "Did every roll reach?" Expect: some did, some did not; or all did.
- **3 min. Count.** Touch each square once and say how many reached.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X23]] Say it is invented; draw four ramp pictures, reached, reached, stopped short, reached; ask once and your child says the prediction.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three rolls each. Never cut the rows or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: sometimes, every time, reach. Frame: "It reached sometimes." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who thinks one reach means it always will (error F). Day 24 opens with a five-minute return and repair for them, then the bumper.
