# Now, wanted, want and idea cards

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the labels named, no brand names. Children of different skin tones and hair in every picture that has a child. Do not reuse the notice, basket, wheel, bus, truck, supply box, tractor or boat of the exit cards.

## Now and wanted (day 20)

- **Now.** The card ramp on two books at the left, a floor line, a three-block tower at the right. A ball sits at a tape X well short of the tower. Label under it: now.
- **Wanted.** The same ramp and tower. A ball sits at the foot of the tower, touching it. Label under it: wanted.

The class says together: "The ball stops short. We want it to reach the tower."

## Want and idea pairs (days 20, 21, 22, 25, 26)

Three pairs, each a want card (the picture of what people want to be different) and an idea card (a picture of a thing someone could build). A pair is laid on a strip of three boxes: want, idea, test. The want card always goes first.

1. **Ramp.** Want: the ball touching the foot of the tower. Idea: one more book under the ramp.
2. **Hat.** Want: a child's hat staying on a wall peg. Idea: a small hook on the peg.
3. **Book.** Want: a book standing up on a shelf. Idea: a block at the end of the book.

## Tool cards (error B)

Three cards of things that can be named instead of a want: a big ramp, a long piece of card, a stack of books. To repair error B the teacher lays a tool card beside its want card and says: "A big ramp is a thing. The problem is what we want to be different." The child swaps the tool card for the want card.

## Strip

A strip of three boxes labeled want, idea, test, large enough for a card in each. One strip for each pair of children.
