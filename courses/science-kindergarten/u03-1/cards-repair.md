# Repair cards, days 23 to 25

Plain black line drawings on white. The cases here are for teaching the repair. They are not the exit cases, which stay in the exit cards, and they are not the end items. Say each time that a picture is invented.

## Day 23: errors C and E

- **Two lanes.** Two taped lanes on the rug from one line. A gentle push and a harder push roll side by side; the class watches which leaves ahead. Then the stopping dots are laid beside the lanes. Said aloud: "Farther is where it stopped. Faster is how it started."
- **Want and result match.** One want picture (a ball at the foot of the tower) and three result pictures: stopped short, reached, rolled past. The child lays each result beside the want and touches yes or no: did it do what we wanted. The ball stopped short is no, the ball at the foot is yes, rolled past is no.

## Day 24: error F

Invented rows. A row of four pictures for one ramp: reached, reached, reached, stopped short. A second row of four: stopped short, stopped short, reached, stopped short. The child lays them side by side and says "sometimes". If the class rows on day 23 were all reached or all missed, the teacher shows these rows instead, said to be invented, so that "sometimes" has something to point at.

## Day 25: error D

A path drawn on paper twice. First path: a ball rolls past a card wall with a gap between them, and the path stays straight. Second path: the ball touches the wall, and the path bends at the touch. The child puts a finger on the touch on the second path and says "it turned where it touched".
