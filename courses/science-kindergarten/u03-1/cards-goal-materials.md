# Goal card and materials card

Two large cards that join the question cards from unit 2 (when, where, what do we do) and the not-on-this-page card. A child asks the goal card or the materials card a question, and the teacher answers by pointing. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words except the two labels, no brand names.

- **Goal card.** The ramp at the left, the floor, and a three-block tower at the right with a small ball touching its foot. A tick mark beside the ball. Label under it: goal. The teacher says: "This is what we want. The ball reaches the tower."
- **Materials card.** Four small pictures in a row: a folded card ramp, two low books, a roll of tape, three blocks. Label under it: what we can use. The teacher says: "These are what we have. Cardboard, books, blocks and tape."

**How the cards are used.** Day 21: a child says "I want to find out ___" and the partner points to the card that could answer. A question neither card answers (what color is the ball) goes on the not-on-this-page card. Day 25: the same two cards are shown again, for the room problem the class chooses. Same pictures, new problem: the goal card is then redrawn by the teacher for that problem.
