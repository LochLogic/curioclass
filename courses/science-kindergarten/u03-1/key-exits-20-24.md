# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 20 to 24

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-20-24.


## Day 20

- Spoken: the ball needs to reach the tower. Naming a thing to build ("a bigger ramp") is error B. A child who says "just build it" and names no want is error A.

## Day 21

- Spoken: the car stops short of the garage; we want it to reach the garage. "Build a longer ramp" said instead of the problem is error A. A fact the problem cannot use, such as the car's color, is not an error: record shown with help.

## Day 22 (three questions)

- Spoken 22a: we cannot tell. Yes, or "it went farther", is error C.
- Spoken 22b: the ramp card on one or two book cards, its low end toward the cup. A tall stack or a ramp pointing away is not an error: record shown with help.
- Spoken 22c: the cup. The stickers or the ramp, "it looks good", is error E.

## Day 23

- Spoken: a prediction that does not say always, with a picture as the reason. "It reached so it always does" is error F.

## Day 24 (two questions)

- Spoken 24a: move the bumper onto the path so the truck touches it, because the truck went by without touching. "Move it closer, with a gap" is error D.
- Spoken 24b: the mark at the touch. The mark before the bumper, near it, is error D.
