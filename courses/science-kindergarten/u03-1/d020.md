# Day 20: How can we make the ball reach the tower?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, alone: tape a low card ramp on two books at one end of the rug, a three-block tower at the other, and a start line. Rehearse a gentle roll (stops short) and a harder start (goes past). Hang the design chart. Lay out the unit 1 strip and a rainy-day square.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection while a ball rolls. Only you roll, children sit outside the lanes, balls are large, soft and too big to mouth, nothing is thrown. Lower the ramp after any bounce. With no eye protection, show dated photographs, said to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook: the ramp and the tower

Roll one ball gently; tape an X where it stops short of the tower. Roll with a harder start; it goes past; tape a second X.

**Ask.** "How can we make the ball reach the tower?" First hear notices and wonders; write up to six on the question board, 20 seconds each. A child may pass. Pip says "I will build a big thing right now!" You say "First we find out what we want."

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Unscored. One child marks on the unit 1 strip where a touch changed the rug ball; one matches the rainy-day square to its weather row. Anyone with a day 19 revision unfinished finishes it. On a miss, model one and another child tries.

#### 8 min. Teaching first: the problem, then the idea

Teach three steps twice, each with a motion: "What is the problem?" (open hand out), "Try an idea" (tap the head), "Test it" (two fingers rolling forward).

**Ask.** "What do we want to be different?" Expect: the ball reaches the tower. Then lay the now and wanted cards: "The ball stops short. We want it to reach the tower." Draw the first path model: ramp, path, X, tower.

Lay the hat cards on the strip, want first. **Ask.** "Which card says what people want?" Expect: the hat staying on. Say "The want goes first. An idea comes after."

#### 8 min. Each child draws

Read aloud: "Draw the X where the ball stopped. Draw where you want the ball." [[s03-P20-1]] Pairs say "I wonder ___" at once. Hear the six, 20 seconds each, and write their wonders. A drawn tool is not wrong: ask "what do you want to be different?"

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X20]] once; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack

Crayons and cards into trays; eye protection into its bag. No ball was handled, so no wipes.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a paper into three boxes. One projector: show the stop photographs; you still roll.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: tilt a tray on a book. No blocks: stack three cups. No large soft ball: a rolled-up sock. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The question board, the weather and strip retrieval and the partner talk.

- **5 min. Roll it.** Roll gently down the ramp and put a paper X where it stops short. Roll with a harder start and mark where it goes past.
  **Say.** "How can we make the ball reach the tower?" Expect: any idea; write it down.
- **5 min. The want.** Say "First we find out what we want."
  **Say.** "What do we want to be different?" Expect: the ball reaches the tower.
- **10 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s03-P20-1]].
- **5 min. Wonder.** Say "I wonder ___." Write what your child wonders.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X20]] Say it is invented; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the retrieval. Never cut the hat cards or the exit. Longer: the child shows a grown-up the three motions.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, idea, test, reach, stop short, past. Frame: "We want it to ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who builds before saying the want (error A) and who names a tool (error B). Day 21 opens with a six-minute return and repair for them, then the question cards, one roll each, and the problem in one sentence.
