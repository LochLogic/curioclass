# Day 20: How can we make the ball reach the tower?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, alone: tape the low ramp on two low books at one end of the rug, the tower at the other, and a start line. Rehearse a gentle roll and a harder one. Hang the design chart. Key: key-exits-20-24.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection while a ball rolls. Only you roll; children sit outside the lanes. Lower the ramp after any bounce. With none, show dated photographs.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook: the ramp and the tower

Roll gently; tape an X where it stops short. Roll with a harder start; it goes past; tape a second X.

**Ask.** "How can we make the ball reach the tower?" Hear notices and wonders first; write up to six on the question board. A child may pass. Pip: "I will build a big thing right now!" You: "First we find out what we want."

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Unscored. One child marks on the unit 1 strip where a touch changed the rug ball; one matches a rainy-day square to its weather row. Day 19 revisions unfinished are finished at places. On a miss, model one.

#### 8 min. Teaching first: the problem, then the idea

Teach three steps twice, each with a motion: "What is the problem?" (open hand out), "Try an idea" (tap head), "Test it" (two fingers rolling).

**Ask.** "What do we want to be different?" Expect: the ball reaches the tower. Lay the now and wanted cards: "The ball stops short. We want it to reach the tower." Draw the path model: ramp, path, X, tower.

Lay the hat cards on the strip, want first. **Ask.** "Which card says what people want?" Expect: the hat staying on. Say "The want goes first."

#### 8 min. Each child draws

Read aloud: "Draw the X where the ball stopped. Draw where you want the ball." [[s03-P20-1]] Pairs say "I wonder ___" at once. Hear the six, 20 seconds each; write their wonders. For a drawn tool, ask what is wanted.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the picture and ask [[s03-X20]] once. Pairs whisper, then hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack

Cards into trays; eye protection into its bag. No wipes needed.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a paper into three boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: tilt a tray on a book. No blocks: stack three cups. No large soft ball: a rolled-up sock. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The question board, the weather and strip retrieval and the partner talk.

- **5 min. Roll it.** Roll gently down the ramp and put a paper X where it stops short. Roll with a harder start and mark where it goes past.
  **Say.** "How can we make the ball reach the tower?" Expect: any idea; write it down.
- **5 min. The want.** Say "First we find out what we want."
  **Say.** "What do we want to be different?" Expect: the ball reaches the tower.
- **10 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s03-P20-1]].
- **5 min. Wonder.** Say "I wonder ___." Write what your child wonders.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X20]] Say it is invented; draw a ball stopped short of a tower of cups; ask once and your child says it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the retrieval. Never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, idea, test, reach. Frame: "We want it to ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who builds before saying the want (error A) and who names a tool (error B). Day 21 opens with a six-minute return and repair for them, then the question cards and one roll each.
