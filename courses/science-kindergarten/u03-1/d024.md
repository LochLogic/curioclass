# Day 24: Where does the bumper turn the ball?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Each group's ramp, tower, bumper and two balls are ready at its table; check every ball and card. Hang the class picture chart and the design chart. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. One child rolls at a time; nothing is thrown or aimed toward people; blocks fall away from hands. Retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. Lower a ramp after any bounce. With none, show photographs.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 23's exit showed error F for many. Lay a row of four tries that reached beside a row that mostly missed [[s03-P24-0]] and say "sometimes." The class says it back.

#### 4 min. Rug: the bumper

Roll a ball past a bumper with a gap; the path stays straight. Roll it into the bumper; it turns. Draw both on the three-box strip: before, touch, after. Pip: "It was near, so it turned!" **Ask.** "Where did it touch?" Expect: a finger on the touch.

#### 14 min. Test and improve

Three groups test while three draw; swap at 6 minutes. Each child places the bumper, rolls once, and draws the path before and after the touch [[s03-P24-1]]. A group also rolls one ball into another ball, then into a two-block tower, then changes one thing and retests once: eight rolls of about 25 seconds. Check each build before it rolls, about a minute a group. Hear the six: "What touched the ball?"

#### 2 min. Revise the chart

The bumper touch joins the cause-effect arrows on the class picture chart. Tie each change on the design chart to a result picture.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these are invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X24a]] then [[s03-X24b]], once each; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Wipes from the table tub, blocks and balls into bags, eye protection.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw three boxes on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No bumper: a folded card or a book stood on its edge. No blocks: stack two cups. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The groups and the chart.

- **5 min. Past and into.** Roll a ball past the bumper with a gap, then into it.
  **Say.** "Where did it touch?" Expect: a finger on the touch.
- **12 min. Draw.** Roll once into the bumper. Your child draws the path before and after the touch [[s03-P24-1]]. Then roll the ball into a stack of two cups.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X24a]] and [[s03-X24b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the ball into the tower. Never cut the touch or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: bumper, turn, touch. Frame: "It turned where it touched." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the wall was close so it turned (error D). Day 25 opens with a five-minute return and repair for them: roll past a bumper with a gap, then into it, and put a finger on the touch.
