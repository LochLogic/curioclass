# Idea cards, guess cards and change cards

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words except the labels named, no brand names.

## Idea cards (day 22)

Three large cards. Each group picks one.
- **A book more.** The ramp with a third book under it, the new book drawn dark. Label: one more book.
- **A harder start.** A hand pushing the ball with a long arrow. Label: a harder start.
- **A bumper.** A short card wall beside the ball's path. Label: a bumper.

**Pip's three at once.** One picture of Pip's ramp with a third book, a long arrow and a bumper all drawn at the same time, and a question mark above the ball. The class says why that cannot tell us anything: we would not know which change did it.

## Guess cards (days 22 and 23)

Two cards for each child: **reach** (the ball touching the foot of the tower) and **not reach** (the ball stopped short). On day 23 the same pair is used for a larger ball, with a picture of a bigger ball above them.

## Change cards for the new target (day 26)

Three large cards, one for each child to pick from: **a book more**, **a harder start**, **a moved bumper** (the card wall drawn in a new place with an arrow showing the move). Each card has one picture only, so a child changes one thing.
