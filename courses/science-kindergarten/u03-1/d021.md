# Day 21: What is the problem?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a ramp, tower and start line in each of six lanes; a card set and a dot strip at each table. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. One child rolls at a time; the rest stand behind the line. No throwing; retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. Lower a ramp after any bounce. With none, show photographs.

### The day

#### 6 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 20's exit showed errors A and B for many. The larger group works with you: lay the ramp want, then the idea, [[s03-P21-0]] and say "first we say what we want." For error B, swap the tool card for the want card. The rest, in pairs, use the want and idea cards.

#### 4 min. Rug: ask the cards

Pip: "I want to find out ___." Show the when, where and what-do-we-do cards, then two new ones: the goal card (the ball at the tower) and the materials card (cardboard, books, blocks, tape). Pairs ask at once; the partner points. **Ask.** "Where should the ball stop?" Expect: the goal card. A question no card answers goes on the not-on-this-page card, kept.

#### 8 min. Observe the trials

Three groups roll while three draw; swap at 4 minutes. Each roller rolls once, about 30 seconds, marks a dot where the ball stopped on the group's dot strip, and draws the stop. Drawers draw where they want the ball.

#### 5 min. Say the problem

Lay the dot strips beside the tower line. **Ask.** "Are the dots nearer, farther or about the same?" Expect: different places. Pairs say "The ball stops ___ the tower. We want it to ___." Write one class sentence in the design chart's first box. Record guesses, untested: "if the ramp is higher, the ball ___."

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X21]] once; everyone touches. Hear the six, 15 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Balls and books into bags; one wipe each; eye protection into its bag.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the goal on one card and the materials on another.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: tilt a tray on a book. No blocks: stack three cups. No cards: draw a ball at a tower and four small things on scrap paper. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The six groups and the dot strips.

- **5 min. Ask.** Say "I want to find out where the ball should stop."
  **Say.** "Which card helps?" Expect: the picture of the ball at the tower.
- **10 min. Roll and draw.** Your child rolls once from a mark, puts a paper dot where it stops, and draws [[s03-P21-1]].
- **5 min. The problem.**
  **Say.** "The ball stops ___ the tower. We want it to ___." Expect: short of; reach. Write the sentence.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X21]] Say it is invented; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer cards. Never cut the sentence or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, goal. Frames: "I want to find out ___." "The ball stops ___ the tower." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many said "build a rail" (error A), day 22 opens with a five-minute return and repair that says the want first. Then each group builds one change from three idea cards and tests it.
