# Exit cards, days 20 to 24

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the caption, no brand names. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse a picture from the want and idea cards, the repair cards or the idea and guess cards.

## Day 20

**Picture.** A classroom wall with a notice hung so high that a row of children can only look up at it. **Cards.** 20a: a child looking at the notice from close up, reading it. 20b: a tall stool.
**Spoken:** "Invented for practice. A notice hangs on the wall so high that children cannot see it. Touch the picture of what the people want to be different." **Key:** 20a. A touch on 20b is error B. A child who says "just build it" and names no want is error A.

## Day 21

**Picture.** A basket sliding off the end of a bench, the basket partly over the edge. **Cards.** 21a: a question card with a basket and a floor mark, a question mark above them, heard as "where should the basket stay". 21b: a question card with a basket and a paint pot, heard as "what color is the basket".
**Spoken:** "Invented for practice. A basket keeps sliding off the end of a bench. Touch the question card that helps us find out about the problem." **Key:** 21a. A touch on 21b is not an error, record shown with help. "Build a rail" said aloud instead of choosing is error A.

## Day 22 (two questions)

**Pictures.** Two days of a toy wheel and a cone. Day one: the wheel stopped short of the cone. Day two: the wheel stopped at the cone. No picture shows the start. Cards: yes, no, we cannot tell. 22b: a ramp with a pattern of stickers on it, and a result picture of a ball stopped short of a cone.
**Spoken 22a:** "Invented for practice. Pip pushed a toy wheel on two days. On the first day it stopped short of the cone. On the second day it stopped at the cone. Nobody watched how it left the line. Did the wheel start faster on the second day? Touch yes, no, or we cannot tell." **Key:** we cannot tell. Yes, or "it went farther", is error C.
**Spoken 22b:** "Invented for practice. Pip wanted a ball to reach the cone. Pip covered the ramp in stickers, and it looks very strong. The test picture shows the ball stopped short of the cone. Did the ramp do what Pip wanted? Touch yes or no." **Key:** no. A yes, "it looks good", is error E.

## Day 23

**Pictures.** Four small pictures in a row for one ramp and a toy bus: reached, reached, stopped short, reached.
**Spoken:** "Invented for practice. Pip rolled a toy bus down one ramp four times. The four pictures show: reached, reached, stopped short, reached. Touch the picture that shows this ramp does not reach every time." **Key:** the third, stopped short. A touch on a reached picture, "it reached so it always does", is error F.

## Day 24 (two questions)

**Pictures.** A toy truck and a garage square. Picture one: a card bumper stands beside the truck's path, not touching it, and the path is a straight dotted line. Picture two: the path bends at the bumper, where the truck touches it, and ends in the square.
**Spoken 24a:** "Invented for practice. A toy truck must turn into a garage square. In picture one, a bumper stands beside the truck's path, not touching it, and the path is straight. In picture two, the path bends at the bumper. Touch the picture where the bumper made the truck turn." **Key:** picture two. Picture one, "the wall was close", is error D.
**Spoken 24b:** "Invented for practice. Same pictures of the toy truck and the bumper. Touch the place on the path where the truck began to turn." **Key:** the touch, on picture two. A spot before the touch, near the bumper, is error D.
