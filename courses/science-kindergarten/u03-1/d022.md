# Day 22: Did it do what we wanted?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape two lanes with one start line on the rug. Each group's ramp, tower and bumper are ready at its table; check every build and ball now, with no time during the test. Hang the class picture chart. Choose today's six. Key: key-exits-20-24.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. Glance at each build again before it rolls. One child rolls at a time; nothing is thrown; retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. Lower a ramp after any bounce. With none, show photographs of the stops.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 21's exit showed error A for many. Orders the book cards [[s03-P22-0]]: first what we want, then the idea.

#### 4 min. Rug: choose one change

Show the three idea cards: one more book, a harder start, a bumper. Each group picks one. Show Pip's three-at-once picture. **Ask.** "Why can't three changes tell us anything?" Expect: we would not know which did it. Pointing counts. Each child puts down a guess card, reach or not.

#### 11 min. Build and test

Three groups test while three draw; swap at 5 minutes. Each build was checked before class: a 10-second glance at each group before its first roll. Each child rolls once from the line, about 40 seconds, and moves a square [[s03-P22-1]]. Hear the six as groups roll: "Did it do what we wanted?"

#### 4 min. Rug: two pushes side by side

You and two children roll a gentle push and a harder push down the two lanes from one line. The class watches which leaves ahead, then lays the stopping dots beside it. Say: "The harder one went farther, and we watched it leave faster. Where a ball stops is where the trip ended." Add an arrow to the chart for the group changes: we did this, the ball did that.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these are invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X22a]]; all touch (20 seconds). Say [[s03-X22b]] once; each builds the ramp on a mat with cards (80 seconds) while you watch a rotating six. Ask [[s03-X22c]]; all point (40 seconds). Mark on the scale.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipes from the table tub, bags, eye protection. Each group leaves its build taped in place.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw five squares on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: tilt a tray on a book. No blocks: stack three cups. No cards: draw a ball at a tower and a ball short of it. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The groups, Pip, and the wall chart.

- **5 min. One change.** Your child picks one: one more book, or a harder start.
  **Say.** "Why one change?" Expect: so we know which did it.
- **10 min. Test.** Roll five times. After each, your child moves a square to reached or not reached [[s03-P22-1]].
- **5 min. Two pushes.** Roll gently, then harder, from one mark. Watch which leaves first.
  **Say.** "Which started faster?" Expect: the harder one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X22a]], [[s03-X22b]] and [[s03-X22c]] Say they are invented; ask once each. Your child lays a spoon on a book for the ramp and points to where the ball should be.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three rolls each. Never cut the two pushes or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: worked, did not work, reach, change. Frame: "It did not reach, so it did not work." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows errors C (farther means faster) and E (looks strong so it worked). Day 23 opens with a repair for them, then the same build with a larger ball.
