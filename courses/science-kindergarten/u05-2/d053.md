# Day 53: Build a cover for your own patch

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, fill sixty sample containers, a patch and an open match for each child, to one amount and depth; touch each pair and remake any that differ. Mark each child's patch. Set out the pile, five mats and the cover choice cards. Make the hard cuts. Rehearse a 30-second visit once.

### Safety and set-up

Children touch only cleared patches, briefly, back of the hand. No tasting or sniffing; wash hands after soil. Use rocks too large to mouth. Trays on a stable surface. One cutter at a time. Never look at the sun.

### The day

#### 4 min. Set-up

Each child has an own patch and open match, a mat and the pile. Say once: "The exhibit is invented for practice." Read the problem: "Shade this patch, and keep the side open to look." Day 52's stickers stay with the mats; errors A, B, C and H are repaired on day 54.

#### 6 min. Choose

All at once, on the mat: touch the cover that keeps the sun off the patch [[s05-D1a]], then two pieces from the pile [[s05-D1b]]. Children who finish draw the familiar picture [[s05-P53-1]].

#### 20 min. Build

Thirty builds at once, five to a group, two scissors a group, one cutter at a time. **Ask.** "Build your cover over your patch." [[s05-D2a]] Spend 15 minutes building. Visit each child for about 30 seconds (thirty visits fill the 15 minutes): the three look-fors are the roof is over the patch, the cover stands on its own, the side is open to look. Hear [[s05-D2b]] and [[s05-D2c]] at the visit, give a sticker and mark the scale. No coaching, no template. Then 3 minutes for each child to revise alone, and 2 minutes packing: each cover is set over its patch. Collect every mat.

Mark each child as shown, shown with help or not yet seen; score what you saw at the visit, never the neatness.

After class, photograph each build, then set every cover and its open match in the sun at the routine look, started alike, to sit at least 30 minutes before day 54. Note each on the set-out record.

### Other rooms

No sun or samples: the builds go ahead over a stone; day 54 uses labelled dated media for the touch.

### At home

**Skip.** The groups, the visits and the cutting.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Stay with a grown-up, use a stone too large to mouth, and never look at the sun. Set a stone or a cup of water in the sun with another beside it, open. Your child builds a roof from paper or cardboard over one, leaving the side open to look. A grown-up cuts; ask once, with no help.
  **Say.** "Will it be warmer or less warm, and why?" Expect: less warm, because the roof blocks the sunlight.
- **15 min. Draw.** Draw the slide in the sun and the slide under the tree, and mark the warmer one [[s05-P53-1]]. Ask once, with no help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the visits are the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: cover, roof, support, open, patch. Frame: "The roof keeps the sun off the ___." Pointing, building and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 has no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 54: the covers have sat out at least 30 minutes, and six groups touch their covered and open patch. Errors D, E and F from today are repaired there, with day 52's A, B, C and H, in groups of at most four.
