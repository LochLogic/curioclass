# Cover choice cards (day 53)

Held back, never taught from. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that the exhibit is invented for practice. Do not reuse the toy animal, the rock cover, the seed tray, the balcony or the picnic square.

## The exhibit

An outdoor exhibit of Earth materials has four marked patches: sand, soil, a smooth rock and a shallow tub of water. Each child has one marked patch and its open match. The exhibit keeper needs the patch shaded, and the side must stay open so visitors can see the patch.

## D1a: the cover choice

Two pictures of the same patch. Picture 1: a roof on posts directly over the patch, the side open. Picture 2: a roof on posts standing beside the patch, the patch in the open sun.
**Spoken:** "Invented for practice. Your patch needs shade, and you must still see it from the side. Touch the cover that keeps the sun off your patch."

## D1b: two pieces

The pile on the table: flat cardboard pieces, folded cardboard strips, paper, masking tape. Everything is on the table; nothing is pictured.
**Spoken:** "Touch two pieces from the pile you will use, one for a roof and one to hold it up."

## D2a and D2b: the build

**D2a:** "Build your cover over your patch." Supplies per child: cardboard pieces, paper, masking tape, blunt scissors shared with the group. The teacher makes the hard cuts the day before and pre-stages large easy-fold pieces; no shape is shown and no template is given. The teacher visits each child for about 30 seconds with the three-look-for checklist: the roof is over the patch; the cover stands on its own; the side is open to look.
**D2b:** "Show how your cover helps this patch."
**D2c:** "After both sit out in the sun, will your covered patch be warmer or less warm than the open patch?"

## After the build

The teacher photographs each revised build before it is set out, and sets each cover beside its open match in the sun at the routine look.
