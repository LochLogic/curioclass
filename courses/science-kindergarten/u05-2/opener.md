# Section 5.2 opener: build a cover and test it, then the unit is shown

## What the section does

Six days, 49 to 54. Day 49 builds a group cover over a rock and a toy animal. Day 50 compares the covered rock with its open match and improves the cover from what was felt. Day 51 checks it over a second patch. Days 52, 53 and 54 are the end-of-unit observation days. Day 52: every child, in a group of five at a time, compares sunny and shaded sand, soil, rock and water from unfamiliar dated cards. Day 53: every child builds an own cover over an own patch. Day 54: every child compares the covered patch with its open match, then the class responds to the record.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. On days 49 to 51 the class works in groups of five, each with its own cover. On days 52 to 54 one front table sees one group of five at a time, three minutes each, while the other groups draw the familiar slide-and-shade picture alone. Five children mark at once; the teacher hears one reason from each child and gives each a picture sticker, read aloud once. No coaching and no reteaching during the observation. Day 54 ends with a short response: stickers read, a revision, and an unscored exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A covered patch is read against its open match.** Same material, started alike, out the same time, the open one beyond the cover's shadow.
- **A cover keeps sunlight off; it need not make a spot cold.** Warm less is the result to look for. No clear difference is a result too.
- **The roof does the job.** Colour and stripes do not. Say what the evidence says, not what looks nice.
- **It takes time.** A cover is not cool the moment it goes on.
- **One change, then retest.** Where did the sun still reach?
- **Day 52's captions are real.** The dated cards are records from a place we did not study. They are never changed to fit an answer.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** "The sun cannot warm things." **B:** "Water never warms." **C:** "Unlike materials prove it." **D:** "A cover beside it shades it."
- **E:** "The nicest one works." **F:** "It is cool the moment it goes on." **G:** "Out longer shows it." **H:** "Later means the sun did it."

## Safety

Teacher checks every surface and every sample before a child touches it, with the back of a hand, briefly. Never look at the sun. Scissors are blunt and child-safe; the teacher makes the hard cuts the day before. Rocks stay low on a stable table. Water in shallow trays on a stable surface, spills wiped at once. Wash hands after soil and sand.

## Supplies

Cardboard, construction paper, masking tape, two blunt scissors per group, matched rocks, the large toy animal, shallow water trays, sixty sample containers (a target and an open match for each child), the cover choice cards, the unfamiliar dated cards, the response mats, the sun caption strips A to H, the set-out record, crayons. A room with nothing builds from reused cardboard and uses dated photograph sets, labelled as media.
