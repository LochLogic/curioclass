# Teacher key: Unfamiliar dated warmth cards (day 52)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-assessment-warmth.


## W1a to W3b

- W1a: card S, the two sand samples. A touch of card U is shown with help; error C only when the child says they prove it.
- W1b: the mark card S's morning caption gives, about the same included.
- W2a to W2d: as each caption says, ties included. "Water cannot warm in sunlight" against the water caption is error B, only when the child says so.
- W3a: for each material, the look or looks whose caption on card T says sunny warmer; both or neither as the captions say. Later does not always win.
- W3b and W3c: a sunny sample and its shaded match at one look, where the caption says the sunny one was warmer, so the sun warmed it. A pair captioned shaded warmer or about the same is shown with help, no letter. If every pair is a tie, the key is "we need to look again". "The sun does not warm things" is error A. "The later one is warmer, so sunlight did it" is error H.
