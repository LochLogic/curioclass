# Day 50: Did our cover work, and what do we change?

## Teacher page

### Before class

At least 30 minutes before, for each group set the covered rock under its cover with the toy on it, the open rock beyond the shadow, and the two water trays, a roof over one. You alone fill the trays, to one depth. Touch each pair first; remake any that differ. Set out one unfair pair: a rock out an hour before its partner. Key: key-exits-49-54.

### Safety and set-up

Children touch only cleared samples, briefly, back of the hand. Rocks too big to mouth. Stay in sight. Wipe spills. One cutter at a time. Never look at the sun.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 49's exit showed error D and error F. Two minutes each, in pairs. For error D, trace the shadow edge with a finger onto the patch. For error F, put the pair out and touch it after both sit the same time.

#### 2 min. Warm-up

Read the day 49 guesses aloud and ask for one more [[s05-P50-1]].

#### 8 min. Compare

Show the unfair pair first. **Ask.** "Is this a fair pair?" [[s05-P50-4]] Expect: no, different times (error G if they say the one out longer shows it). Replace the early rock with a fresh partner. Each child touches the covered and the open rock [[s05-P50-2]], then the covered and the open water [[s05-P50-3]]. Four touches of 10 seconds, groups at once: about 4 minutes. A tie is marked no clear difference.

#### 5 min. Improve

**Ask.** "Where did the sun still reach?" [[s05-P50-6]] Each group adds or moves one piece there [[s05-P50-7]], then checks the shadow edge on the patch. One change, then retest; day 51 retests.

#### 4 min. Revise the model

Say: "Which patch felt warmer?" [[s05-P50-5]] Add to the chart: blocking sunlight causes less warming, as far as the marks show [[s05-P50-8]]. Revise the evidence sentence from the touches. A tie stays a tie.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these cards are invented for practice." [[s05-X50a]] then [[s05-X50b]]; all touch or say at once. Mark the rotating six on the scale.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

You empty the trays. Covers to the shelf, hand wipes.

### Other rooms

No sun: read the dated photographs of a covered and an open rock with their recorded comparison, said to be weaker than touching.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: two cups with a little water. No rock: a flat stone. A grown-up checks each first.

**Skip.** The groups, the unfair pair and the cutting.

- **12 min. Compare.** Stay with a grown-up and never look at the sun. Put two cups of water out in the sun for 30 minutes, a paper roof over one. A grown-up checks first; your child touches both briefly with the back of a hand and marks which felt cooler [[s05-P50-3]].
  **Say.** "Which felt warmer, and why?" Expect: the open one, the sun reached it; or no clear difference.
- **8 min. Fix.** Ask "where did the sun still reach?" Move one piece of the roof there.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X50a]] and [[s05-X50b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the water pair only. Never cut the touch, the revision or the exit. Longer: retest the moved piece.

### Words and supports

Words: covered, open, cooler, no clear difference, fair. Frame: "The ___ patch felt warmer, because the sun ___." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 opens with 2 minutes each: error D (a cover beside it shades it), trace the shadow edge onto the patch; error E (the nicest one works), pick the covered-and-open comparison. Then the class tries the cover at a second patch.
