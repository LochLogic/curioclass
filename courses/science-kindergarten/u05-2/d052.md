# Day 52: Why is the slide in the sun warmer?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the front table: cards S, O, R, W, U and T, five warmth mats and crayons. On your copy write each key from the card's real caption, never the other way round. Keep the dated matched records up. Key: key-assessment-warmth.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and cards only. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Day 51's exit showed error D and error H. One and a half minutes each, in pairs. For error D, trace the shadow edge onto the patch. For error H, touch the sunny and the shaded sample at one look. Nothing else is reteached.

#### 7 min. Closing explanation

From the dated matched records the class says why the slide in the sun is warmer than the one under the tree [[s05-P52-1]]. You write it: the sunlight reaches it, the tree blocks the sunlight, shade need not feel cold. Read the question board back and mark what is answered.

#### 2 min. Set-up and packing

Say once: "The exhibit is invented for practice; the dated records are real." Seat group 1 at the front table. The other groups draw the familiar slide and tree picture alone [[s05-P52-2]]. Take down the dated matched records.

#### 3 min. Group 1 observed

Read each caption aloud once. Ask each item once, aloud, no coaching.
- 30 seconds, the fair pair: touch card S beside card U [[s05-W1a]]; all mark the sand sample that felt warmer [[s05-W1b]].
- 1 minute: for sand, soil, rock and water mark the morning card and the afternoon card [[s05-W2a]], [[s05-W2b]], [[s05-W2c]], [[s05-W2d]].
- 30 seconds: touch the earlier or later look [[s05-W3a]]. While the others mark, all touch the pair that shows the sun did it [[s05-W3b]]; hear each child say why [[s05-W3c]], 15 seconds each.
- The last minute: hear five stickers read aloud once, 12 seconds each; each may revise their own mat alone.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Marks follow the caption, ties included. Thirty children at 27 seconds is 13.5 minutes inside the 18.

#### 3 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. Collect every mat.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six cards on paper and read each caption aloud, labeled as media.

### At home

**Skip.** The groups, the cards and the mats.

- **15 min. Sun and shade.** Use stones too large to mouth, stay with a grown-up outdoors, and never look at the sun. A grown-up checks, in the sun and in the shade, two of the same thing, two stones or two patches of ground. Your child touches both with the back of a hand.
  **Say.** "Which felt warmer, and why?" Expect: the sunny one, because the sun reached it; or about the same.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the slide in the sun, the slide under the tree and an arrow to the warmer one [[s05-P52-2]]. Ask once, with no help.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the sittings are the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: sunny, shaded, earlier, later, about the same. Frame: "The ___ one is warmer." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 has no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 53 is the design check: each child builds an own cover over an own patch. The stickers stay with the mats; errors A and B, C and H wait for the repair groups on day 54.
