# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 49 to 51 and 54

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-49-54.


## Day 49: a seed tray and two crates

- Spoken 49a: the sun above the board; the shadow under the board on the seedlings. A shadow beside the box is error D.
- Spoken 49b: the crate under the roof, because the roof keeps the sun off it. "It was cool the moment the roof went on" is error F. Naming the open crate is shown with help, no letter.

## Day 50: a balcony

- Spoken 50a: stretch the roof. The flowers, "it will look better so it works", is error E. The farther cover is error D.
- Spoken 50b: "I think the open half needs cover, because I saw the card say it was warmer." "Because it is nicest" is error E.

## Day 51: a canopy and a stone seat

- Spoken 51a: no. Yes, "the canopy is close by", is error D.
- Spoken 51b: the sun reached it. "Because it was later in the day" is error H.
