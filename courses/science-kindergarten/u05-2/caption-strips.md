# Sun caption strips A to H (days 52 to 54)

Held back. Eight pre-printed strips for the teacher; children do not read them. After each sitting on days 52 and 53 the teacher writes the strip that fits on a child's mat and reads it aloud once. On day 54 each repair group hears its strip once in the five minutes of Return and repair: A and B together, C, H, D, and E or F as a separate evidence repair, at most two groups from the record (the two largest); G is read in the feedback only. Each strip is two short sentences: what to look at and what to try. A child revises at most two captions. Plain strips of paper, the letter large at the left, a plain picture hint at the right.

- **A. Picture hint: sun rays reaching a warm surface.** "Sunlight warms things. Trace the sunlight to the warmer one."
- **B. Picture hint: a tub of water in the sun beside a tub in the shade.** "Water gets warm in the sun too. Put the two water records side by side."
- **C. Picture hint: two pots of the same sand.** "Compare sand with sand. Swap one sample for the same material."
- **D. Picture hint: a roof over a patch beside a roof next to a patch.** "A cover works over the spot. Trace the shadow edge onto the patch."
- **E. Picture hint: two patches, one covered, one open.** "Look at what the touch says. Pick the covered and the open patch."
- **F. Picture hint: two clocks showing the same hour.** "A cover needs time. Compare after both sat out the same time."
- **G. Picture hint: two bowls of sand with the same clock.** "Match the time. Both samples need to sit out the same time."
- **H. Picture hint: a sunny sample beside its shaded match.** "Compare at one look. Sunny and shaded together."

**Note.** The captions say what to try, never what is wrong with a child.
