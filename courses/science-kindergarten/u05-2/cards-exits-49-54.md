# Exit cards, days 49 to 51 and 54

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse the toy animal, the rock cover or the pairs touched in class. Mark on the one scale. Days 52 and 53 have no separate exit: the end-of-unit items are their evidence.

## Day 49: a seed tray

**Picture.** A seed tray with its plants growing in a box. Cover 1: a roof on posts over the whole tray, the posts standing outside the box. Cover 2: a roof whose posts stand in the planted box among the plants. Cover 3: a roof leaning against the side of the tray, beside it, the plants in the open.
**Spoken 49a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the cover that shades the tray and keeps the plants clear." **Key:** cover 1. Cover 3, "near is enough", is error D. Cover 2 is shown with help, no letter.
**Spoken 49b:** "Touch when the covered part will feel less warm: as soon as the cover goes on, or after both sit in the sun the same time." Two cards, a clock at the start and a clock showing a later hour. **Key:** after both sit in the sun the same time. The first card, "as soon as it goes on", is error F.

## Day 50: a balcony

**Picture.** A balcony with a marked target on the floor. A cover over its left half; the right half in the sun. Caption read aloud: "The open half was warmer." Three cards: stretch the roof over the open half; paint flowers on the roof; stand the cover farther away.
**Spoken 50a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the change that fixes it." **Key:** stretch the roof. The flowers, "it will look better so it works", is error E. The farther cover is error D.
**Spoken 50b:** "Same card. Say: I think, because I saw." **Key:** "I think the open half needs cover, because I saw the card say it was warmer." "Because it is nicest" is error E.

## Day 51: a canopy and a stone seat

**Picture.** A canopy on posts with its shadow shown as a dashed edge on the ground, missing a stone seat beside it. A matching stone seat under the canopy. Caption read aloud: "The open seat was warmer than its covered match."
**Spoken 51a:** "Invented for practice. Touch yes or no: is the seat shaded?" Two cards, yes and no. **Key:** no. Yes, "the canopy is close by", is error D.
**Spoken 51b:** "Same card. Why does the card say the open seat was warmer?" **Key:** the sun reached it. "Because it was later in the day" is error H.

## Day 54 (section exit, response, not scored)

**Picture.** A miniature picnic square with a cover that misses its patch, and a matching covered patch. Caption read aloud: "The open patch was warmer than its covered match." Two cards: move or stretch the cover over the patch; add a ribbon to the cover.
**Spoken 54a:** "Invented for practice. Touch the move that fixes the cover." **Key:** move or stretch the cover over the patch. The ribbon is error E.
**Spoken 54b:** "Same card. How does the card help you choose?" **Key:** the open patch was warmer, so the sun must be kept off it. "The cover looks nice" is error E.
