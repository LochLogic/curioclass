# Day 54: Did your cover keep the sun off?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check each cover and open match has sat out at least 30 minutes; touch every sample first and clear it. Seat the front table with five mats and crayons. Sort the day 52 and 53 stickers by letter.

### Safety and set-up

Children touch only cleared patches, briefly, back of the hand. Wash hands after soil. Never look at the sun. Difficult cuts are yours.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say once: "The cards at the end are invented for practice." Seat group 1 at the front table. The other groups draw the familiar picture alone [[s05-P54-1]].

#### 18 min. Six groups observed, 3 minutes each

Each group in turn, mats in front, no coaching.
- 55 seconds: all touch their covered and open patch and mark which felt warmer [[s05-D3a]].
- 125 seconds: each child points to the part of their cover and says why [[s05-D3b]], with a sticker, 25 seconds each.

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A tie is a result. The mark follows the touch, never the cover's looks. Thirty children at 25 seconds is 12.5 minutes of the 18.

#### 2 min. Feedback

Read each strip aloud once, 8 seconds each, so every child hears their own comment. Then point to the design-cycle chart and agree it: our problem, our idea (the cover), our test (the touch), one change.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Each child revises their own day 54 mat from the sticker [[s05-P54-2]]. The record of day 52 and day 53's assessment picks groups, at most four: errors A and B together (the paired water records), error C (swap one sample for the same material), error G (match the time), error H (same look), error D (trace the shadow edge, move a piece and tape it), errors E and F (pick the covered-and-open comparison; compare after a shared time). Others use their strip's picture. No reteaching.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." [[s05-X54a]] then [[s05-X54b]]; all touch or say at once. Hear a rotating six.

### Other rooms

No sun: read the dated photographs of covered and open patches with their recorded comparison, said to be weaker than touching. The child still points to their cover.

### At home

**Skip.** The groups, the stickers and the strips.

- **10 min. Touch.** The roof from yesterday has sat in the sun 30 minutes. A grown-up checks, then your child touches the covered and the open one.
  **Say.** "Which felt warmer, and why?" Expect: the open one, because the sun reached it; or about the same.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the sun and its arrow on the slide in the sun, and the tree and its shade over the other [[s05-P54-1]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X54a]] and [[s05-X54b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the strips. Never cut the touch or the exit. Longer: plan a cover for a second material.

### Words and supports

Words: covered, open, warmer, roof, no clear difference. Frames: "The roof keeps the sun off the ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 starts unit 6 and meets the after-lunch schoolyard litter. Whatever repair is left stays on the record sheet for teacher planning; the unit is not reopened.
