# Day 49: Can we keep the sun off this spot?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, make every hard cut. For each group set a rock with the toy animal on a low table, a tub of ten cardboard pieces, construction paper, masking tape and two scissors. Hang the goal card and the design-cycle chart. Show no shape.

### Safety and set-up

One cutter at a time in a group. Rocks stay low. Never look at the sun. Wipes after.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 48's exit showed error A and error B. Two minutes each, in pairs. For error A, trace the sunlight with a finger to the warmer picture. For error B, lay the sunny water record and the shaded water record side by side.

#### 2 min. Warm-up

Say the design-cycle chart with a hand motion for each step (review). Then say the goal card together [[s05-P49-1]].

#### 3 min. The problem and the pile

Show the rock and toy in the sun: this is the patch we shade. Show the pile. **Ask.** "What will the roof keep off the spot?" [[s05-P49-2]] Expect: the sunlight.

#### 11 min. Build the group cover

Each group of five builds one cover over its rock. Each child takes one 2-minute turn adding a piece [[s05-P49-3]]: five turns are 10 minutes, all six groups at once, so you walk the room. The last minute steadies the cover. The roof must be over the animal, between the sun and it. If it stands beside it (error D), say "trace where the shadow falls". A child may pass.

#### 4 min. Guess

Each child draws the sun and the shadow of the cover on the mat [[s05-P49-4]], then marks the spot they guess will warm less [[s05-P49-5]]. Hear the rotating six, 20 seconds each [[s05-P49-6]].

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these cards are invented for practice." [[s05-X49a]] then [[s05-X49b]]; all touch at once. Mark the rotating six on the scale.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Pieces to the tub, hands wiped. Shelve the covers. As a set-up, put each group's rock and a matching open rock out at tomorrow's morning look, touched alike first.

### Other rooms

No sun: a lamp shadow over the toy, said to be a demonstration.

### At home

**Swap.** No cardboard: a paper roof on folded paper posts, with tape. No toy or rock: any small toy and a flat stone.

**Skip.** The groups, the turns and the cutting.

- **12 min. Build.** Set the toy on the stone in a sunny window. Your child builds a roof over it from paper or cardboard, a grown-up holding it steady.
  **Say.** "What will the roof keep off the toy?" Expect: the sunlight.
- **8 min. Guess.** Draw the sun and the shadow, then mark the spot that will warm less [[s05-P49-4]] and [[s05-P49-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X49a]] and [[s05-X49b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one minute a turn. Never cut the Ask, the guess or the exit. Longer: a second cover.

### Words and supports

Words: cover, roof, block, warm less, patch. Frame: "I think the ___ will warm less, because ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 opens with 2 minutes each: error D (a cover beside it shades it), trace the shadow edge; error F (it is cool at once), compare after a shared time. Then each group touches its covered rock and its open match.
