# Our science safety rules (Kindergarten, the whole year)

A teacher page: each rule once, with its days. "Outdoor check done (safety page)" and "too warm for your hand at a count of three is not cleared" point here. An adult watches every task.

## The stop rule (every day)

Anyone may say "Stop." Everyone freezes, hands down, and you look: a ball going the wrong way, a spill, a hurt or upset child, a sting, a surface too warm, thunder, anything unknown. Start again only when you say so. Days 8 and 9 practice it.

## Eye protection (days 1 to 9, 20 to 31, 45, 46, 48, 50, 53, 54)

- Everyone in the room, you too, wears it for every roll, pull, clean-up and sand, soil or water tray: rolling balls can fly.
- Child-sized safety glasses or splash goggles marked Z87.1; splash goggles also stop impacts.
- Wipe shared eyewear with a disinfectant wipe and dry it between children, strap included.
- Glasses wearers use fit-over goggles. A child who cannot wear it is the recorder, behind the line.
- None in the room: use the dated photographs, said aloud; never fake a trial.

## Hand washing (days 32, 33, 40, 41, 44 to 46, 48, 53 to 55, 57, 59)

Soap and water after soil, sand, plants, gloves, litter and samples; a wipe only with no sink. No hands to faces first.

## Allergies: plants, foods, latex (every hands-on day)

- Before the year, get each child's allergies from the nurse: plants, pollen, stings, foods, latex.
- No food is used in science, and nothing is tasted or sniffed.
- Gloves are non-latex (days 55, 59 and your soil gloves).

## Plants (days 32, 33, 40 to 42)

You alone handle pots, water and soil; children look from their seats. Store or nursery plants only, checked against the allergy list. Outdoors nobody tastes a plant or touches a fungus. Wipe spills at once.

## Soil, sand, mold (days 44 to 46, 48, 53, 54)

- Bagged potting soil, never dug from the yard. Washed play sand made for children, low dust, kept damp.
- Sand and soil count as chemicals: keep each bag's Safety Data Sheet and purchase date on file.
- You fill and clear every tray. Throw out anything sour or moldy; keep trays covered and dry between days.

## Outdoor looks and adults (days 10 to 12, 41, 44 to 46, 48, 50, 51, 53 to 55, 59)

The **outdoor check**, the day before:
- Walk the spot for wasp or bee nests, glass, needles and animal waste; move if you find any.
- Check the nurse's list for sting and plant allergies; bring what each child needs.
- Plan hats in strong sun, a water break after, and an indoor fallback.

During the look:
- One area where you see every child; children stay in sight and touch nothing unknown.
- Follow your school's rule for adults outside with a class; the litter look (55, 59) needs a second adult for the bag.
- Nobody looks at the sun. Thunder: everyone goes inside and stays in until it has stopped for a while. A tree is not shelter.

## Sun-warmed surfaces and lamps (days 12, 44 to 51, 53, 54, 64)

- Nothing is heated; children never handle a heat source or heated material.
- You touch first, back of your hand. Children touch briefly, back of a hand, for a count of three.
- **Too warm for your hand at a count of three is not cleared**: shade it five minutes or use the dated photograph.
- No child touches metal in sun; use plastic, wood or a painted wall.
- Days 53 and 54: retouch a patch just before children arrive if it sat in sun over 10 minutes since you cleared it.
- No sun: one lamp that stays cool to the touch, run by you (days 47, 49).

## Electricity (days 47, 49)

The lamp is the only plugged-in thing. You plug and unplug it; its cord is taped flat, out of lanes and paths; it goes out of reach after. Children never touch a plug, cord or outlet.

## Magnets and small parts (every hands-on day)

- No magnets this year.
- "Too big to mouth": larger than a child's fist, with no part that fits in a toilet-paper tube. Check balls, rocks and toys the day before.
- Toy cars and vans: no battery, lights or loose wheels (days 29, 30).
- Blunt scissors, paper and card only, carried closed, tips down, one cutter at a time, left-handed pairs; you make hard cuts (days 49 to 51, 53, 54).
- String is short, tied on by you, never looped around a body (days 3, 7 to 9, 29 to 31).

## Glass and sharp things (days 55, 57, 59)

No glass in science. Samples are clean, labels off, edges checked the day before. On a litter look, anything sharp, any needle, pet waste, or anything wet or sticky stays put and nobody goes near it; tell the teacher, who tells the office. Grabbers point down.

## Clean-up (every hands-on day)

- Pick up a ball only once it stops; ramps go low after a bounce.
- You empty trays and bag waste.
- Gloves come off inside out into the adult's bag; then everyone washes.
- Wipe tables and clear the floor.
