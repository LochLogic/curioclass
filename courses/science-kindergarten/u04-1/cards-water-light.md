# Water and light cards (day 33)

Pre-cut picture cards, one set for each table. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that some pictures are drawings and some are photographs.

## The water records

- **Card W1. The squirrel drinking.** A photograph. A squirrel drinks water from a bowl on a balcony. Credit printed on the back and said aloud: photograph by Bärbel Miemietz, 11 June 2023, license CC BY-SA 4.0 (Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0). The license link is on the back of the master and the card is not changed from the original.
- **Card W2. The robin at a birdbath.** Our own drawing. A round bird with a pointed beak stands at the rim of a shallow stone dish of water, one drop falling from its beak.
- **Card W3. Pip's mint pot.** Picture three of the sequence, a cup of water tipped over the soil.
- **Card W4. A plant in the rain.** Our drawing. A small flowering plant under a cloud, drops falling on the leaves.

## The light records

- **Card L1. The window plant.** A drawing of the pot at a sunny window, leaves green.
- **Card L2. The shelf plant.** A drawing of the pot on the dark shelf, leaves a paler green.
- **Dated photographs.** The teacher's real photographs (see the plant photograph record) sit beside L1 and L2.

## The marks

- **Water-drop mark.** A blue drop, cut from paper, one for each child.
- **Sun mark.** A yellow circle with short rays.

## What happens

Pairs lay a drop on every card where a living thing takes in water. The drops are counted by touching each once, and the last number says how many. The sun mark goes beside the two plant photographs.
