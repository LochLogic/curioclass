# Day 33: What do living things take in?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the two class plants in trays at the front. Lay the dated photographs side by side. Fill each table's envelope with W1 to W4, L1, L2, drop marks and a sun mark. For W1 use our own drawing of the squirrel drinking from a bowl. Keys: key-exits-32-35, key-water-light.

### Safety and set-up

You alone handle the pots. Children look from their seats. Check allergies first.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Day 32's drawings stay on the chart. Everyone touches the pattern sheet and says with a hand motion "a pattern is what shows up again and again."

#### 4 min. Guess

Pip asks, one line: "What does a plant need?" Each child draws one thing as a guess, on side 1 or drawing paper [[s04-P33-1]]. A guess is what we think before we look. Hear a rotating six and write who on the record sheet.

#### 6 min. Two plants again

Lay the window and shelf photographs side by side, both watered. **Ask.** "Which plant is greener?" Children point, then circle it [[s04-P33-2]]. Say "the shelf plant had no light for a while and is back in the light now." Say "these are these two plants, not every plant." If nothing clear shows, say so, and use the invented second record, said to be invented.

#### 6 min. Water records

Show Pip's mint pot, four pictures, one line each. **Ask.** "What did the mint take in?" Expect water. Then show the squirrel and the robin at the birdbath. Pairs put a drop on each of W1 to W4 where a living thing takes in water [[s04-P33-3]]. All four get a drop.

#### 5 min. Pattern sheet

Drop cards go in a water row; the sun mark goes by the plant photographs. Count the row, touching each once. **Ask.** "What do all of these need?" Expect water. The sheet reads: water for plants and animals; light for plants. Each of six tables says one line to you: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X33a]], then [[s04-X33b]], once each; everyone touches. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes; one wipe each.

### Other rooms

No plants: use the photographs and say aloud that no plant is in the room.

### At home

**Swap.** No plant: a picture of one. No drops: draw them.

**Skip.** The pattern sheet.

- **5 min. Look.** Look at a plant. Ask what it might need [[s04-P33-1]].
- **12 min. Water.** Look at pictures of a plant, a pet and a bird drinking. Your child marks a drop on each [[s04-P33-3]].
  **Say.** "What do they all take in?" Expect water.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X33a]] and [[s04-X33b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the squirrel and the robin. Never cut the drops or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: water, light, take in, plant, animal. Frame: "It takes in water." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many missed the first exit question or touched the boxed pot, day 34 opens with the pot drooping then watered, and the two plants side by side. Day 34 then sorts who eats.
