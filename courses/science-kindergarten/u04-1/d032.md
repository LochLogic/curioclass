# Day 32: What do the weed and the squirrel need?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today starts living things; it needs nothing from day 31's small groups (when day 31 exists, open by saying unit 3 is done). Put the two class plants in trays at the front. Hang the pattern sheet, and the dated and fall photographs low enough to point to. Tape up an empty question board. Key: key-exits-32-35.

### Safety and set-up

You alone handle the pots. Children look from their seats. Check allergies first. At home, go outside with a grown-up, look only and touch nothing unknown.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

Show the weed in the crack and the squirrel's hole beside the two plants. Ask once, before saying anything about them: **Ask.** "What do you see?" Explain nothing.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say "this is not scored." On side 1 of the page each child circles, in three weather rows kept from unit 2, the picture that shows up again and again [[s04-P32-1]] [[s04-P32-2]] [[s04-P32-3]]. Ask each row once. Then **Ask.** "What makes it a pattern?" Expect: again and again.

#### 5 min. Notice

Pairs point to what they notice: green leaves, a crack, a hole. Write each notice as a question on the board. Hear a rotating six, 30 seconds each, and write who on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Two plants

Show the dated photographs of the window plant and the shelf plant, both watered, and hold up each pot. Children say which looks different. Say only what they show; if alike, say "that is a result too."

#### 6 min. First drawing

Each child draws the weed, the squirrel or a plant, and one thing they guess it needs, on side 2 or drawing paper [[s04-P32-4]]. Children draw for 3 minutes. Then each tells it in 6 seconds while you label it: 30 children at 6 seconds is 3 minutes. Pin the drawings on the class picture chart as the first needs-and-places drawing.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Show the roof garden picture. Ask [[s04-X32a]], then [[s04-X32b]], once each. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Wonderings go on the board.

#### 3 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes; one wipe each.

### Other rooms

No plants: use the photographs and say aloud that no plant is in the room. 

### At home

**Swap.** No fall photographs: look out a window. No plant: use a picture of one.

**Skip.** The rows and the board.

- **5 min. Look.** With a grown-up, find a plant and a picture of a weed or a hole outside or out a window; look only, touch nothing unknown. Ask "What do you see?"
- **12 min. Draw.** Your child draws a plant and one thing it needs [[s04-P32-4]].
  **Say.** "What do you think it needs?" Expect any guess.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X32a]] and [[s04-X32b]] Say it is invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one weather row. Never cut the drawing or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: plant, animal, need, guess, pattern. Frame: "I saw ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 puts a water-drop mark on every picture where a living thing takes in water, and a sun mark by the two plant photographs. If a child said plants need nothing, the first drawings stay on the chart and day 33 shows the evidence.

Ours, all of it.
