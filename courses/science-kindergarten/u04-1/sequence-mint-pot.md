# Picture sequence: Pip's mint pot (day 33)

Said aloud to be invented. Four pictures for the teacher to show one at a time; the teacher tells each in the voice of Pip, one line at a time. One actor in each picture. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. One copy for each table.

## The four pictures

1. **Pip with the mint pot on a sill.** A paper puppet with big eyes beside a small pot of mint with upright leaves. Teacher: "This is my mint. It stands up straight."
2. **The pot, drooping.** The same pot, the leaves bent over, an empty watering can lying on its side beside it. Teacher: "Oh no. I forgot the water. Look at my mint."
3. **Pip pouring water.** The same pot, a small cup tipped over the soil, a few drops falling. Teacher: "I water it. Drip, drip."
4. **The pot, standing.** The same pot again with the leaves upright. Teacher: "Now it stands up. What did my mint take in?"

## What the children do

After picture four the teacher asks: "What did the mint take in?" Children say or point: water. Then the teacher puts a water-drop mark on picture three and says: "A plant takes in water." This picture is not used again in any exit or end item.

## The teacher's page

The sequence supports error A ("plants do not drink"). It shows one plant doing a thing, so the class also looks at the squirrel and the robin before the pattern sheet is touched.
