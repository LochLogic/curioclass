# Day 34: Who eats?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut and sort E1 to E5 and the six match cards for each table. For E1 to E3 use our own drawings. Keep the pattern sheet and the chart in reach. Pip's two lines are ready. Key: key-exits-32-35.

### Safety and set-up

No food, no animal and no plant soil in the room. Children look and sort only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up, return and repair

Everyone touches the water and light rows on the pattern sheet and says each aloud. If many missed day 33's exit, the larger group, error A or error B, sits with you with the repair cards; the rest sort the eats cards in pairs. For error A touch the pot that had water. For error B lay the window and shelf photographs side by side: touch the greener plant and say what it had.

#### 3 min. Hook

Pip asks one line: "Does the squirrel eat like the plant?" Children say what they think; no answer yet.

#### 8 min. Sort

Six tables sort E1 to E5 into eats and does not eat, and mark a bite on each eating picture [[s04-P34-1]]. Count each row, touching each once; the last number says how many. Say "animals eat; a plant is not fed a meal; it took in water and light." Never say a plant needs no food.

#### 6 min. Different animals

Each table matches the squirrel, the rabbit and the goat to the acorn, the clover and the grass [[s04-P34-2]]. **Ask.** "Do they all eat the same?" Expect no. Hear a rotating six say which two differ: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Chart

The eats row goes on the pattern sheet; the pictures go on the chart. The sheet now reads: water for plants and animals; light for plants; animals eat.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X34a]], then [[s04-X34b]], once each; everyone touches. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes; one wipe each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw bite marks on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw two animals and their food.

**Skip.** The pattern sheet.

- **8 min. Who eats?** Look at pictures of a pet eating and a plant being watered. Your child draws a bite on the one that eats [[s04-P34-1]].
  **Say.** "Does the plant eat?" Expect no, it takes in water.
- **12 min. Match.** Draw lines from each animal to its food [[s04-P34-2]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X34a]] and [[s04-X34b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the match without the rotating six. Never cut the sort or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: eat, food, same, different, animal. Frame: "It eats ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many touched the sunflower or said the cow and the cat eat the same, day 35 opens with the two pictures side by side and the food cards. Day 35 then checks a second place, the park pond, against the three rows.
