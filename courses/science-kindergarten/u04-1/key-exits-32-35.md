# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 32 to 35

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-32-35.


## Day 32

- Spoken 32a: any potted plant. No letter; first ideas.
- Spoken 32b: any wondering, written on the question board. A child who says plants need nothing is error A.

## Day 33

- Spoken 33a: water, or a drink, for both. Saying only the goat needs water is error A.
- Spoken 33b: the pot in the light. A touch on the boxed pot, or both because both were watered, is error B.

## Day 34

- Spoken 34a: the goat. A touch on the sunflower is error C.
- Spoken 34b: no. Yes is error D.

## Day 35

- Spoken 35a: the bush in the open sun. A miss is shown with help, no letter. This is the section exit.
- Spoken 35b: a sandwich. "Seeds, the same" is error D.
