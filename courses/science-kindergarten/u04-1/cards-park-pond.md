# The park pond set and the needs cards (day 35)

Said aloud to be invented unless the teacher has dated, credited, permitted local pictures. Pictures of local places cannot be checked from here. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. No child is pictured and no home is shown.

## The park pond set

Six cards, each with a plain caption (a date and "Curio Class drawing" on the back).

1. **P1. The pond edge.** A shallow pond with reeds standing in the water at the edge.
2. **P2. A duck on the water.** One duck at the pond edge with a water plant in its bill.
3. **P3. A sunny bank.** Grass growing on a bank in open light.
4. **P4. A shaded corner.** A tree over the corner of the bank, the grass thin below it.
5. **P5. A person with a bottle.** An adult at the path filling a water bottle at a tap beside the pond sign.
6. **P6. A dry gravel path.** A path of bare gravel with no plant.

## The needs cards

Three row cards: a water drop, a sun mark, a bite mark. They head the three rows on the pattern sheet.

## What happens

Pairs sort the cards onto the rows: water (P1, P2, P5), light for plants (P3, P4 for different light) and food for animals (P2 with the water plants near it). They tick a row when the place supplies it. P6 does not supply a need and goes to a note on the question board.
