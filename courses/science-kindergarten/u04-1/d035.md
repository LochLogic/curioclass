# Day 35: Does this place have what they need?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the park pond set for each pair. Hang the pattern sheet with its three rows. Say to yourself: no new idea today, only the three rows used on a new place.

### Safety and set-up

Children sort paper only. No food, animal or soil in the room.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 34's exit showed error C or error D for some. The larger group works with you with the repair cards. For error C, lay the goat eating beside the sunflower being watered and say "animals eat; a plant is not fed a meal." For error D, match each animal to its food card. The rest repair in pairs: pick the card, point, tell a partner.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Everyone touches the three rows on the pattern sheet and says each aloud: water, light for plants, animals eat.

#### 4 min. Look at the place

Say "this is the park pond, and it is invented for practice." Show P1 to P6 one at a time. Ask once, "What do you see?" and accept any answer. This is another observer's record, labeled by date. No child or home is pictured.

#### 9 min. Sort

Pairs lay each card by the row it shows. They draw a drop on a card that shows water [[s04-P35-1]], a sun where a plant has light [[s04-P35-2]] and a bite where an animal eats [[s04-P35-3]]. Hear a rotating six explain one card each: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes. P6 (the dry gravel path) shows no need; it goes to the question board.

#### 4 min. Check the sheet

**Ask.** "Does this place have what they need?" Expect: water, light and food. Tick each row on the pattern sheet the place supplies. The pattern holds in this second place.

#### 3 min. Exit

Section exit. Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X35a]], then [[s04-X35b]], once each; everyone touches. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes; one wipe each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw six boxes and name a pond picture in each.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a pond, or look at a puddle only with a grown-up; do not step in or touch unknown water.

**Skip.** The pattern sheet.

- **8 min. Look.** Look at a picture of a pond. Ask what the plants and animals there could use.
- **12 min. Marks.** Your child draws a drop where there is water [[s04-P35-1]] and a bite where an animal eats [[s04-P35-3]].
  **Say.** "Does the place have what they need?" Expect water and food.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X35a]] and [[s04-X35b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Never cut the sheet check or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: water, light, eat, place, same. Frame: "This place has ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many said the hiker eats the same as the chipmunk, day 36's warm-up matches each animal to its food card. Day 36 then links what a deer, grass and fish need to a forest, a meadow and a pond.
