# Repair cards for errors A to D (days 34 to 36)

Pairs of pictures, a wrong idea beside a right one. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. The teacher says a repair as a seeing, never as a blame. A child may point, say or act it out.

- **A, "plants do not drink".** Two pictures of one pot: drooping, then watered and standing. Touch the one that had water. Day 34, with B.
- **B, "water is enough".** The dated photographs of the window plant and the shelf plant, both watered, side by side. Touch the greener plant and say what it had. Day 34, with A.
- **C, "the sunflower eats the water".** The goat eating grass beside the sunflower being watered. The teacher says: "Animals eat. A plant is not fed a meal." Day 35, with D.
- **D, "they all eat the same".** The cow with hay beside the cat with fish. Match each animal to its food card. Day 35, then day 36's warm-up for a child who missed the day 35 exit.

A group is repaired in five minutes with the teacher when it is the larger one; the other children use these cards in pairs.
