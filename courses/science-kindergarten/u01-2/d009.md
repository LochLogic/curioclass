# Day 9: The last two groups, and what we saw

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the five lanes as on day 8, with a mat and the cards at each. Put the record sheet and the repair cards where you can reach them. Keys: key-same-or-different, key-day9-fresh.

### Safety and set-up

As day 8. Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll, pull and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing, fingers clear of the bump, string straight and never around a body, nothing carried to another lane. With no eye protection run no trials: say the record is of photographs, which cannot show carrying out.

### The day

#### 3 min. Set-up

Seat group 5 at the lanes. The rest go to the rug with side 2 of the page, within sight, and draw [[s01-P9-1]]. A second adult, if there is one, supervises them.

#### 7 min. Group 5 observed

Run it as group 1 on day 8: say "this is invented for practice", ask day 8's items once each, aloud, with no coaching. Steady equipment but never give the plan or the action.

#### 7 min. Group 6 observed

As group 5. Day 8's scores are never changed, and no child is observed twice.

#### 3 min. Record completion

Write each component for groups 5 and 6 on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The others finish their drawing. Collect all pages and keep them in the child folder.

#### 2 min. Read feedback

To each group in turn, about 20 seconds, say aloud one thing its marks showed, such as "You kept the same ball and changed the push." Keep to what you saw.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Count the letters on the record from day 8's items and day 7's exit: error A, D, B, F or E. The largest group works with you on its move: A, the same or different cards; B, two pulls on one box shown with hands; D, the order cards on the repair card's balls; E, keep both tries; F, one ball and two tape marks. Each other child takes the repair card for their own letter, points, then tells a partner.

#### 3 min. Fresh case

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Show the puck cards, ask [[s01-X9]] once and everyone points. Then the cloth cards, [[s01-X9b]] once.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No box and string: use a cereal box and a shoelace you tie on, with no loop. No tape: use two sheets of paper as delivery places. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair cards and the feedback round.

- **8 min. Look at the marks.** Lay out yesterday's mat. Ask your child to point to a mark and say what it shows.
  **Say.** "What did you keep the same?" Expect: the ball, the box and the start.
- **12 min. Try one part again.** Your child picks the part they liked least, and does it again with you watching.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X9]] and [[s01-X9b]] Say "this is invented." Draw two pucks, then a puck touching a cloth. Ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one sentence a group for the feedback. Never cut the observation or the repair.

### Words and supports

Frame: "It moved when ___ touched it." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Nothing from unit 1 is left to finish, and no readiness check follows. Day 10 starts unit 2: the morning coat pile and the afternoon change, then the class weather pictures for a question.
