# Exit cards, days 5 to 7

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Do not reuse a picture from the trials or the order cards.

## Day 5 cards: two toy buses

**Card 5a.** A plain toy bus (a box on four wheels) with its front bumper pressed against the back of a second bus. Two short parallel lines at the meeting place; no starburst. The second bus has a small arrow ahead of it showing it has begun to roll.
**Card 5b.** The same two buses with a clear gap between them. The second bus has the same small arrow ahead of it, showing it has begun to roll.

**Spoken:** "A rolling toy bus bumped a toy bus sitting still, and then the second bus began to roll. Touch the picture that shows where the first bus was when the second bus began to roll." **Key:** card 5a. Wrong is error D (the cause comes before the touch). Wrong is error D.

## Day 6 picture: the try

**Scene.** A toy sled on a flat floor mat with a start tape. A gentle-pull arrow is drawn and the sled has started to move. Beside the scene are two choices: a box with a tick-mark shape for keep, and a bin for throw away.

**Spoken:** "Pip's guess was that only a strong pull starts a sled. We tried it, and a gentle pull started the sled too. Do we keep that try or throw it away? Touch the box or the bin." **Key:** the box. Wrong is error E.

## Day 7 cards: two ropes

**Pair 1.** Two panels with the same toy cart and the same thin rope, on the same floor and start tape. A short pull arrow in the left panel and a long pull arrow in the right.
**Pair 2.** Two panels. The left shows the thin rope on the cart with a short pull arrow. The right shows a thick rope on the cart with a long pull arrow.

**Spoken:** "A cart started faster with a thick rope than with a thin rope. Pip says the harder pull did it. Touch the two pictures that check Pip's idea fairly." **Key:** pair 1. Wrong is error A.
