# Exit cards, days 5 to 7

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Do not reuse a picture from the trials or the order cards.

## Day 5 cards: two toy buses

**Card 5a, before.** Two plain toy buses (a box on four wheels) with a clear gap between them. The first has a small arrow ahead of it, rolling toward the second; the second has no arrow.
**Card 5b, touch.** The same two buses with the first bus's front pressed against the back of the second. Two short parallel lines at the meeting place; no starburst; no arrows.
**Card 5c, after.** An empty box of the same size, nothing drawn in it.

**Spoken:** "A rolling toy bus rolled toward a toy bus sitting still, and they touched. Say what happened to the bus that was sitting still."

## Day 6 picture: the try

**Scene.** A toy sled on a flat floor mat at a start tape. No arrows, no second sled.

**Spoken:** "Pip guessed that only a strong pull starts a sled. We tried it, and a gentle pull started the sled too, so Pip will test again. Say Pip's next test: I change one thing, I keep the same."

## Day 7 cards: two ropes

**Strip 7a, the empty cart.** A start tape at the left. Two stopping dots on two lines, a short pull arrow and a long pull arrow beside them; both dots are away from the tape, the long-arrow dot farther.
**Strip 7b, the loaded cart.** The same cart with a small bag of beans drawn in it, on the same start tape. The short-arrow dot sits on the tape (it did not move); the long-arrow dot is a short way along.

**Spoken:** "Pip pulled an empty cart, gently and then harder, and it moved both times. Pip then pulled the same cart with a bag of beans in it. Tell me what happened to the loaded cart on the gentle pull."
