# Day 5: When things bump

## Teacher page

### Before class

In each of three lanes tape a start line, a tape ring in the middle for the resting ball, and a second line a hand-width beside the ring for the pass-by. Put two balls at each lane.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing, slow rolls, wait behind the line, fingers clear of the bump. Clean-up: balls into bins, one wipe each, eye protection to you. With none, run no rolls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Tape two X marks at one lane. Children do [[s01-P5-2]] with their hands: one ball, tapped from each side. If a third or fewer made error F on day 4's exit, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the roll part.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Roll a ball and stop it with a flat hand. Pip asks: "Can touching change something that is already moving?" Then roll one ball into a resting ball. **Ask.** "What changed when they touched?" Expect: the rolling one slowed or stopped, the resting one started. "It pushed it" is enough.

#### 12 min. Bump it, pass it, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups of five, one rolling ball each. Half the class rolls slowly into the resting ball, then past it along the second line (about 35 seconds for the two tries). The waiting half draws the bump on a strip. Swap after 6 minutes.

#### 3 min. Rug

Lay out the order cards. **Ask.** "Put the cards in order." Expect: come close, touch, changed. A child who puts changed first says what the resting ball did at the touch. Add to the chart in the children's words: a bump can slow, stop or start things, and it only works when they touch.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the cushion cards, ask [[s01-X5]] once and everyone points. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, the rest drawing on the back of their strip. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into three boxes. Strips stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No tape: use a string for the lane. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the cards and the rug.

- **12 min. Bump it.** Set one ball still. Your child rolls the other slowly into it, then past it with a gap.
  **Say.** "What changed when they touched?" Expect: the rolling one slowed or stopped, the other one started.
- **8 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P5-1]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X5]] Say "this is invented." Draw two cushions touching and two with a gap. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the stop with a hand. Never cut the bump, the pass-by or the exit.

### Words and supports

Frame: "It moved when ___ touched it." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who thinks things change before they touch (error D). Day 6 opens with the order cards for them, then each group picks one change to test, how hard or which way.
