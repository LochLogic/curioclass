# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 5 to 7

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-5-7.


## Day 5 cards: two toy buses

- Spoken: it began to roll when it was touched. Wrong is error D (it moved before the touch); "nothing happened" is not yet seen.

## Day 6 picture: the try

- Spoken: one change (how hard or which way); the sled and start kept; the gentle-pull try kept. Wrong is error A (two changes) or E (the try thrown away).

## Day 7 cards: two ropes

- Spoken: it did not move; the gentle pull left it at the start tape. Wrong is error E (says it moved, passing over the try that stayed at the start) or A.
