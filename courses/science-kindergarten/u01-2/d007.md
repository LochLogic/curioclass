# Day 7: A box with a block in it

## Teacher page

### Before class

You alone tie a short thick string, no loop, to each box. Lay three straight lanes with a start line. Put one block in each box. Keep the day 3 strips to hand. Key: key-exits-5-7.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every pull and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing. The string stays straight, never around a body, and nobody walks backward. The block stays inside the box. No tasting or sniffing. Clean-up: you remove the strings, boxes into bins, one wipe each. With none, run no pulls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pull the empty box gently and then harder from the same start with a child, and keep both marks ([[s01-P7-1]]). If a third or fewer made error E on day 6's exit, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the pull part.

#### 3 min. Guess

Practice, nothing new. **Ask.** "Will the box with the block go like the empty box?" Each child draws a guess for a gentle pull and a harder pull ([[s01-P7-2]]).

#### 12 min. Loaded box pulls, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups of five, one box and block each. Half the class pulls the box gently, then harder, from the same start (about 35 seconds a try), and a partner puts a tape piece at each stop. The waiting half draws before and after for each pull ([[s01-P7-3]]) and lays it beside a day 3 strip. Swap after 6 minutes.

#### 5 min. Rug

Lay the strips side by side. Write the day 3 claim on the chart: "a harder pull moves the box farther." **Ask.** "Is it true for this box and this floor?" Expect: yes for the harder pull; the gentle pull may not start the loaded box, and we keep that mark on the chart with its harder pull. Write the claim in the children's words, with the box, the block and the floor in it.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the two cart strips, ask [[s01-X7]] once. Pairs whisper, then hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into two boxes for each pull.

### At home

**Swap.** No light box: use an empty cereal box. No block: put a rolled-up sock inside. No string: use a shoelace you tie on, with no loop. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up pulls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up and the rug.

- **5 min. Guess.** Your child draws [[s01-P7-2]].
- **10 min. Pull it.** Your child pulls the box with the sock in it gently, then harder, from the same mark. Never walk backward.
  **Say.** "Is it true for this box on our floor?" Expect: yes, or what they saw.
- **5 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P7-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]] Say "this is invented." Draw an empty cart and a loaded cart with two stopping dots each, the loaded cart's gentle dot on the tape. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the waiting half skips the day 3 strips. Never cut the pulls or the exit. Longer: pull each way twice.

### Words and supports

Frame: "For this box on our floor, ___." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who ignores the gentle try (error E). Day 9 repairs it. Day 8 is the unit observation: new objects, five lanes, each child plans, tests and gives one reason.
