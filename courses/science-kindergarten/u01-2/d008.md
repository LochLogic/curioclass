# Day 8: Our own test, watched

## Teacher page

### Before class

You alone tie a short thick string, no loop, to each box. Lay five adjacent lanes you can see at once, each with a start line, a ball, a box and two X marks beside the box. Put a mat and the cards at each lane. Hang the chart where all can see it.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll, pull and the clean-up, you too. Floor only, no throwing, slow rolls, fingers clear of the bump. The string stays straight, never around a body. Nothing is carried to another lane. Stop any lane for unsafe rolling, damaged materials or missing protection. With no eye protection run no trials: say the record is of photographs, which cannot show carrying out.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

New objects and a new layout. Seat each of groups 1 to 4 in turn at the lanes; the other children go to the rug with their mats, within sight. A second adult, if there is one, supervises them.

#### 7 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, five lanes, mats in front. Say "this is invented for practice." Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching, and watch every child:
- 1 min: [[s01-E1]], the change, keep and watch cards on the mat.
- 1.5 min: [[s01-E2]], the ball into the box, gently and then harder.
- 1 min: [[s01-E3]], two pulls on the box.
- 1.5 min: [[s01-E4]], taps from each side.
- 1 min: [[s01-E5]], the ball into the box and past it.
- 1 min: [[s01-E6]], one reason from each child, about 6 seconds each, pointing to a mark; then balls into bins, one wipe each.

Steady equipment but never give the plan or the action; mark help as shown with help. Only a child's first unaided try counts.

#### 7 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1. After each group write each component on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Leave a part blank that you did not see.

#### 7 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 7 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1. The children at the rug draw [[s01-P8-1]] on side 2 of their mats and wait. Collect the mats after the last group and keep them in the child folder. No exit and no repair today.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No box and string: use a cereal box and a shoelace you tie on, with no loop. No tape: use two sheets of paper as delivery places. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The mat, the groups and the rug.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say "this is invented." Ask the six assessment questions once each and do not help. Mark each answer on the mat.
  **Say.** "Tell me what your marks show." Expect: the ball touched the box and pushed it.
- **10 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P8-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I changed ___. I kept ___. I watched ___." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 9 observes the last two groups, completes the records, reads feedback from your marks and repairs from day 8's items by error letter: error A (item 1), D (items 2 and 5), B (item 3), F (item 4), E (item 6). Day 7's exit, error A, is repaired there too.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
