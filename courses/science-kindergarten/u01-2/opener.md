# Section 1.2 opener: when things meet

## What the section does

Five days, 5 to 9. Day 5 looks at a hand stopping a rolling ball and two large balls bumping. Children see that touching can change something already moving, and the class picture chart gets "a bump can slow, stop or start things, and it only works when they touch." Day 6 is the children's own test: each group picks one change, how hard or which way, never both, and the class keeps what the tests back. Day 7 asks whether a box with a load pulls the way the empty box did, and the claim stays small: this box, this load, this floor. Days 8 and 9 are the observation, a new set-up in which each child plans and carries out a test and gives one reason. Nothing on days 5 to 7 is scored.

## How the days run

Days 5 to 7 run like section 1.1: warm-up repair, teaching part, trials in halves, rug, clean-up, spoken exit. Days 8 and 9 are different. Five children at five lanes are watched at once for seven minutes, four groups on day 8 and two on day 9, while the others draw the rug ball before and after at the rug, within sight. Day 9 then finishes the records, reads feedback aloud, repairs with the largest error group and asks two fresh-case questions. There is no exit and no repair on day 8.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **In a bump the objects push on each other.** The moving ball slows or stops as the other starts. Children say "it pushed it." That is enough.
- **Touch comes first.** The change happens after the touch, not when the objects are close. Order cards (come close, touch, changed) make this visible.
- **A result that surprises is kept.** If a gentle pull does not start the loaded box, that is the reading. Repeat the pair once and keep both records.
- **A claim names its objects.** "This box on our floor," not "boxes."
- **Magnets and other pushes and pulls without touching are left out of this unit.** No force, friction or energy words.

## The errors, in plain words

- **D:** "They stopped before they touched because they were close." Order cards repair it.
- **E:** "Throw away the try that was not what I guessed." Repeat the pair and keep both.
- **A:** "Use the thick rope for the hard pull." Same-or-different cards; day 9 repair.
- **B and F:** Met in the end items. On day 9 repair with two pulls on one box, or the same ball and two tape marks.

On day 9 the largest error group works with you; the others use repair cards. If one letter is a third or more of the children, repair it together. Wrong answers are voiced by Pip or "someone might say."

## Scoring

Record each component on the record sheet as shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Score a child's first unaided performance at the sitting. Unsafe or absent performance is not observed, not a misconception. Accept pointing, dictated words and home-language reasons. Adults may steady equipment but never supply the plan or the action; record physical help as shown with help.

## Safety and supplies

Everyone present wears fitted impact eye protection, including for demonstrations and clean-up. Collisions: slow rolls, fingers clear, adjacent lanes watched by the teacher, nothing moved elsewhere. String is short and teacher-attached, with no loops. Day 8 and 9 need five balls, five boxes with strings, tape, five picture response mats and crayons. Clean-up on days 8 and 9 is half a minute a group inside its seven minutes. A room with nothing: paper balls and scrap boxes; with no eye protection run no trials and say the record is of photographs, which cannot show carrying out.
