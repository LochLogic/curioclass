# Day 6: Our own test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a start line and two X marks in each of three lanes. Give each of six groups a group card, a counter and the keep, change, watch cards.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing, similar slow rolls, wait behind the line. Clean-up: balls into bins, one wipe each, eye protection to you. With none, run no rolls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Children do [[s01-P6-2]]. If a third or fewer made error D on day 5's exit, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the test part.

#### 4 min. Plan

Pip says: "I will change how hard and which way, both!" Children say we change one thing. Each group of five points at one box of its group card, how hard or which way, and puts a counter on it ([[s01-P6-3]]). **Ask.** "What do we keep, and what do we watch?" Expect: the same ball and start; where the ball goes or stops. Groups may choose different tests.

#### 12 min. Our test, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups test at the three lanes while the other three draw, then swap. Each child makes the group's two settings, a gentle then a harder roll, or a side tap toward each X, about 35 seconds a try. The waiting groups draw their group's two tries on the try page. Read aloud: "Draw the first try and the second try."

#### 4 min. Rug

Each group tells one mark. **Ask.** "Which marks do we keep, and why?" Expect: the ones we saw. If two marks disagree, repeat that pair once with the group and keep both. Change an entry a test contradicted; keep the rest.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the sled picture, ask [[s01-X6]] once and everyone points. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, draw two boxes on a paper for the group card. Try pages stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No tape: use a string for the lane. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the group card and the rug.

- **5 min. Pick one.** Draw two boxes, how hard and which way. Your child points to one.
  **Say.** "What do we keep the same?" Expect: the ball and the start.
- **10 min. Try it twice.** Your child rolls the ball the two ways, a gentle and a harder roll or a tap from each side.
- **5 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P6-1]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]] Say "this is invented." Draw a sled. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the rug takes one mark. Never cut the test or the exit. Longer: repeat any pair that disagreed.

### Words and supports

Frame: "We changed ___. We kept ___." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who throws away a try that was not the guess (error E). Day 7 opens by repeating one pull with you and keeping both marks, then asks whether a box with a block in it pulls like the empty box.
