# Fresh case cards (day 9, not scored)

Two spoken questions, three minutes. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain line drawings, no printed words, no faces on objects. A puck is a flat round disc.

## Question 1: the push

**Pair 1.** Two panels: the same puck on the same floor with the same start tape and the same direction. A short push arrow in the left panel and a long push arrow in the right.
**Pair 2.** Two panels: a soft puck with a short push arrow, and a heavy-looking puck with a long push arrow.

**Spoken:** "Touch the two pictures that make a fair check of a harder push on a puck."

## Question 2: the cloth

**Card 2a.** The puck touching a folded cloth. A small starburst at the touch. The cloth has motion lines.
**Card 2b.** The puck beside the folded cloth with a clear gap between them. The cloth has motion lines.

**Spoken:** "Touch the picture where something made the cloth move."
