# Repair cards (day 9)

Five small cards, one for each error, for the children who are not working with the teacher. A child picks up the card that matches the error the record shows for them, looks and shows the answer by pointing, then tells a partner. Plain line drawings, no printed words. These reuse earlier ideas on new objects.

- **A.** Two pairs of panels. One pair has the same toy truck in both panels, pushed gently and then harder; the other has a small truck and a large truck. Point to the fair pair.
- **B.** Two panels of the same box with a string. In the first, a hand pulls gently; in the second, a hand pulls harder. Show with your hands how it started in each.
- **D.** Three order cards on a new pair of balls: come close, touch, changed. Put them in order.
- **E.** Two tries drawn side by side, one matching a guess and one not. A box and a bin. Keep both: touch the box for each.
- **F.** One ball with two tape X marks and two taps from the side, one toward each X. Point to the same ball, two ways.
