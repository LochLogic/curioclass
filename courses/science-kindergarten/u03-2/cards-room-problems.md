# Room problem cards (day 25)

Three large cards of things in the classroom, one problem each. They stand in for photographs, so no photograph is needed. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names, no pictures of homes. Six table sets, one of each card.

1. **Crayons.** A tilted tray on a desk. Crayons are rolling off the low end and one has reached the floor.
2. **Door.** A classroom door swinging shut fast, with a child's hand held back from the edge, and a small arrow showing the swing. Only the teacher shows this one live, with children back.
3. **Chair.** A chair that slides away when a child sits down, a small arrow behind it.

**How they are used.** The teacher shows the three cards and the class chooses one by hands, the teacher touching each hand once. Then the teacher shows the chosen problem for real or by demonstration: the tilted tray with crayons, the door only by the teacher, or the chair on a smooth floor. Children ask "I want to find out ___" and point to the goal card or the materials card. Then the class says "People want ___" and the teacher scribes it on the problem step of the design chart. Nothing is built.
