# Exit cards, days 25 and 26

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Do not reuse a picture from the room problem cards or the target cards.

## Day 25 (two questions)

**Picture.** An empty supply box tipping over on a narrow shelf, one corner over the edge. **Cards.** 25a: the box standing up on the shelf. 25b: a wide new shelf with no box.
**Spoken 25a:** "Invented for practice. An empty supply box tips over when it is put on a narrow shelf. Touch the picture of what the people want to be different." **Key:** 25a, the box standing. A touch on the wide shelf is error B.
**Spoken 25b:** "Same picture of the tipping box. Say one question you could ask about it." **Key:** any question about the tipping box that watching or a card could answer, such as where it tips, when, or what is in it. "Build a shelf now" is error A. A question about something else is not an error: record shown with help.

## Day 26 (two questions)

**Pictures.** A toy tractor on a path and a barn square drawn beside the path. A card guide touches the tractor on the path; the path bends at the touch. The result picture shows the tractor ended beside the barn, outside the square.
**Spoken 26a:** "Invented for practice. Pip wanted a toy tractor to stop in a barn square beside its path. A card guide touched the tractor, and the result picture shows it ended beside the barn, outside the square. Did the guide do what Pip wanted? Touch yes or no." **Key:** no. A yes, "the guide is neat and strong", is error E.
**Spoken 26b:** "Same pictures of the tractor and the guide. Touch the place on the path where the tractor began to turn." **Key:** the place where the guide touches the tractor. A spot before the touch, near the guide, is error D.
