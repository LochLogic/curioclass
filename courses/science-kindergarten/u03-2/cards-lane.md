# Lane cards (days 27 and 28)

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 27. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names, diverse children where a child appears. Every case is said aloud to be invented. Six table sets, one of each.

## Day 27 cards

- **Goal card, spoken.** The teacher says: "People want the ball to end up in the bay and not in the walking strip." The card shows the ball in the bay, large, with a tick.
- **P1c cards (two).** The ball in the bay. The ball in the walking strip.
- **P2b cards (five).** A ball with a double arrow at a small start line: starts faster. A ball turning into the bay: turns into the bay. A ball beside a low block: stays low. A tall tower: is tall. A red patch: is red. The first three are the right ones; the last two are distractors about size and colour.
- **Question card.** A single large question mark. A question no card answers goes on the not-on-this-page card.

## Day 28 cards

- **M1a cards (two).** A ball at a line with a short arrow: slow start. A ball at a line with a long arrow: faster start.
- **M1b and M3b cards (two).** A large yes mark and a large no mark.
- **Bumper.** A pre-cut card bumper for each group, cut by the teacher alone.

The teacher records the match between the child's marks and what was watched, never from the cards alone.
