# Section 3.2 opener: another problem, then a new lane

## What the section does

Four days, 25 to 28. Day 25 chooses a classroom problem from three room problem cards, finds out what people want and asks a question before anything is built. Day 26 picks a target, makes one change to the ramp, tests it and says whether it did what was wanted. Day 27 is an observation day: a pretend delivery lane the class has not seen, where each child asks, draws what was found and says why we find out what people want first. Day 28 is the response day: each child tries a change in the lane, watches the start and the path, and tells whether the design did what was wanted. Captions on the mats are read on day 28. Nothing is scored against a speed.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. Days 25 and 26 open with a short return and repair. On days 27 and 28 one supervised station sees six groups of five in turn, while the other groups draw the familiar ramp and target on their own. The teacher records each child on a mat of their own, not a spokesperson.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Define the problem before building.** Say what people want to be different, then ask, then try.
- **Farther is not faster.** Speed is judged by watching two starts side by side.
- **A guide changes the path where it touches.**
- **A miss is a result.** Did the design do what was wanted? Match the result to the want.
- **No friction, no gravity, no numbers for speed.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** "Build a wall now." Put the want before the idea.
- **B:** A favorite tool in place of the want.
- **C:** "It went farther so it was faster." Watch the start.
- **D:** "It was close so it turned." It turned where it touched.
- **E:** "It looks neat so it worked." Match the result to the want, yes or no.

## Safety

Balls are large and soft, too big to mouth. The teacher alone cuts card and tapes the lanes. The door in the room problem is shown by the teacher only, with children back. Children keep faces back, stay outside the lane when the teacher rolls, and retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. The pretend walking strip is tape only.

## Supplies

Room problem cards, target cards, the lane layout, lane cards, mats, caption strips, exit cards, balls, card ramps on low books and card bumpers. A room with nothing uses labelled pictures; children mark them, which cannot show children rolling, so say so.
