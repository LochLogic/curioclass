# Day 26: Did our change do what we wanted?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Each group's ramp, bumper, blocks and ball are ready. Hang the design chart and the class picture chart with the days 22 to 24 records. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. One child rolls at a time; nothing is thrown or aimed toward people. Retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. Lower a ramp after any bounce.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 25's exit showed errors A and B for many. The larger group works with you: lay the want picture first, then one idea [[s03-P26-0]]; for B, swap the tool card for the want card. The others use the repair cards.

#### 4 min. Rug: how it reached

Point along the wall records: the stopping marks, the two pushes, the bumper paths. The class says how the revised ramp reaches the tower; scribe three lines. Say "farther is where it stopped. Faster is what we watched." **Ask.** "Point to the push you watched leave the line faster." [[s03-P26-2]] Everyone points at once.

#### 3 min. Rug: the new target

Say the goal first. Show the two target cards: a line farther down the rug, a square to the side. The class chooses by hands; touch each hand once. The chosen card goes beside the design chart.

#### 9 min. Test one change

Three groups test while three draw; swap at 4 minutes. Check every build before it rolls. Each child picks one change card (a book more, a harder start, a moved bumper), rolls once, then draws the ball in the square, moves it to reached or not reached, and draws the path [[s03-P26-1]]. Hear the six.

#### 3 min. Rug: did it do what we wanted?

Read the rows. Each group, 25 seconds, says whether its result shows a wanted speed or a wanted direction. A miss is a result. The agreement goes on the chart.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these are invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X26a]] then [[s03-X26b]], once each; everyone touches. Hear the six. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipes from the table tub, blocks and balls into bags, eye protection.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw a square on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: a tilted book. No eye protection for both of you: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The chart and the groups.

- **5 min. Pick a target.** Tape or chalk a line far down the floor. Say it is what you want.
- **12 min. Change one thing.** Your child picks one change: a book more, or a harder start. Roll once.
  **Say.** "Did it do what we wanted?" Expect: yes or no from the result. Your child draws it [[s03-P26-1]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X26a]] and [[s03-X26b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Never cut the one change or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: target, change, reached, worked, did not work. Frame: "It did not reach, so no." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the guide looked neat so it worked (error E) or puts the turn before the touch (error D). Day 28 returns to them. Day 27 is an observation day: each group of five spends four minutes at one station while the rest draw the familiar ramp and target.
