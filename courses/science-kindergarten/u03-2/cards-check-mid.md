# Check cards, days 29 to 31 (mid-year)

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 29. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a date, no brand names, children of many skin tones and abilities. Every case is said aloud to be invented for practice. Weather pictures are calm: a cloud, a clock, a bolt kept small, never a damaged place or a frightened person. Form A is for day 29, form B for day 30. Six table sets of each.

## Motion cards (both forms)

- **Comparison cards (two).** An arrow with a thick line: how hard. A bent arrow: which way.
- **Keep cards (four).** The same car (form B: van) twice with an equals mark; the same taped lane twice with an equals mark; a different car beside it; the start line. Watch cards: a car at a line, a car stopped.
- **Lane.** A taped start line and two tape X marks, left and right. Form A: a toy car, a light box with a short thick string, a firm paper ball. Form B: a toy van, a sled box, red and blue X marks.
- **Class transfer (day 29).** A rug with an edge, a toy car stopped near the edge. Three result cards: yes, yes, no.

## Weather cards

- **Record A.** Ten dated squares in two rows of five. Cloudy: 2, 3, 5, 6, 9, 10. Windy: 3, 4, 6, 7. Sunny: 1, 4, 7, 8. Three day pairs with morning and afternoon arrows: warmer, warmer, cooler. Two month rows of windy squares: seven and four.
- **Record B.** Ten dated squares. Windy: 2, 4, 5, 8, 9. Cloudy: 1, 2, 5, 6, 8. Three day pairs: cooler, warmer, cooler. Two month rows of rainy squares: eight and five.
- **Credit line.** "Another class's record, invented for practice." If the class has a spare real month of dated squares kept by another class or school, use it and credit it.

## Forecast cards

- **Page A.** A storm cloud and a clock at late morning over a pond with a picnic blanket. Then a picture of dark clouds now. Then two town rows of small bolt squares: Hill has nine, Lake has one. Cards: plan the picnic indoors first, stay out until thunder, go into the school building, stand under a tree.
- **Page B.** A school garden and an afternoon clock; the same four cards, but the first reads plan to be indoors first.

## Day 31 exit cards (held back)

- 31a: a toy truck pushed twice; pair one, the same truck; pair two, a larger truck.
- 31b: three day pairs: cooler, cooler, warmer; a speech mark from a friend.
- 31c: a park, an afternoon clock, a small storm cloud; action cards.
- 31d: a toy bus, a line, a card bumper, and three result cards: no, no, yes.
