# Caption strips A to F (days 27 and 28)

Held back. Six pre-printed strips for the teacher; children do not read them. After day 27 the teacher writes the strip that fits on each child's mat. On day 28 each error group hears its strip once, read in a quiet voice, in the three minutes of Read the captions. Each strip is two short sentences: what to look at and what to try. A child revises at most two captions. Plain strips of paper, the letter large at the left, a plain picture hint at the right.

- **A. Picture hint: a want card with an arrow to an idea card.** "We say what people want first. Then we think of an idea."
- **B. Picture hint: a ramp card beside the ball ending in the bay.** "A tool is not the want. Draw where the ball should end up."
- **C. Picture hint: two pushes leaving a line side by side.** "Watch the start, not the stop. Say which one left faster."
- **D. Picture hint: a path with a finger on the bend.** "The ball turns where it touches. Put your finger on the touch."
- **E. Picture hint: a result picture beside a want picture.** "Look at where the ball ended. Is it what people wanted? Say yes or no."
- **F. Picture hint: a row of reached and missed squares side by side.** "Sometimes it reaches and sometimes it misses. Say sometimes."

**Note.** The captions say what to try, never what is wrong with a child.
