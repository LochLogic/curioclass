# Day 25: What do people want to be different?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the three room problem cards, the goal and materials cards and the question cards at each table. Hang the design chart. Set a ramp, a bumper and a ball for the repair roll. Lay the want and idea cards at each table. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection. You alone roll the repair ball; children stay back from the lane. If the class picks the door, you alone swing it, children behind a line, hands clear of the edge. Nothing is built today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up: return and repair

Day 24's exit showed error D for many. Roll a ball past a bumper with a gap, then into it. Each child lays a finger on the touch on the second path of the repair sheet [[s03-P25-0]] and says "it turned where it touched." Everyone points at once; look along the rows.

#### 5 min. Choose a problem

Make the hand motion for each step (problem, idea, test), then show the goal and materials cards: "Same cards, new problem." Show the three room cards: crayons rolling off a tray, a door closing fast, a chair that slides. Say "these are things in our room." The class chooses one by hands; touch each hand once.

#### 10 min. Ask, observe, gather

Show the chosen problem, for real or by demonstration. Redraw the goal card for it. In pairs each child says "I want to find out ___" [[s03-P25-1]] and the partner points to the card that could answer. Pairs go at once; hear the six, 15 seconds each. A question no card answers goes on the not-on-this-page card.

#### 5 min. Say the problem

"People want ___." Scribe it on the problem step of the design chart, before any idea; lay a want card before an idea card to show the order. Each child draws it on the student page [[s03-P25-2]]. Nothing is built.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "these are invented for practice." Ask [[s03-X25a]] then [[s03-X25b]], once each; everyone touches. Hear the six. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack

Cards into envelopes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three room things on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ramp: any tilted book. No room cards: pick something at home that bothers someone, such as a tipping cup.

**Skip.** The cards and the chart.

- **8 min. Pick a problem.** Show your child something that bothers someone, such as a cup that tips. Say it is a problem to find out about.
  **Say.** "What do you want to find out?" Expect: a question, such as where or when.
- **12 min. Say and draw.** **Say.** "What do people want to be different?" [[s03-P25-2]] Your child draws the wanted ending. Build nothing.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X25a]] and [[s03-X25b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep one pair of questions. Never cut the want before the idea or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, want, ask, idea, test. Frames: "I want to find out ___." "People want ___." Pointing and home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who offers a fix instead of the want (error B) or builds first (error A). Day 26 opens with a five-minute return and repair for them: the want picture first, then the tool card swapped. Then the class picks a new target for the revised ramp.
