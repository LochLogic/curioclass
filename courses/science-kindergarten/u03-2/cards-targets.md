# Target cards (day 26)

Two large cards, both inside the cleared area of the rug. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no printed words, no brand names.

- **Farther line.** The ramp at the left and a long taped line across the floor, a good way past the old tower place. A ball sits on the line.
- **Side square.** The ramp at the left, the old tower place, and a taped square off to the side of the path. A ball sits in the square.

The class chooses one target by hands, the teacher touching each hand once. Say the goal first: "We want the ball to reach the line", or "We want the ball to stop in the square". Keep the target inside the cleared area. The chosen card goes beside the design chart.
