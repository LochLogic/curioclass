# Day 30: New tests, new weather pages, the rest of us

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay the five lanes as on day 29, but with the van, the sled box and a red and a blue X mark. Put a mat and crayons at each lane. Form B is parallel to form A with other objects and other records, so a child told about yesterday cannot repeat an answer.

### Safety and set-up

As on day 29. Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and pull. Floor only, no throwing, string straight and never around a body. With no eye protection run no trials: the record is of pictures, which cannot show carrying out.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say "this is invented for practice." Seat group 4 at the lanes. The others sort weather pictures [[c1-P30-2]] or draw [[c1-P30-1]] on side 2. A child away yesterday is seen with group 4.

#### 8 min. Group 4 observed

Five children, five lanes, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching, in the same order and seconds as day 29:
- 3 min 40 s, motion: [[c1-K1b]], 30 s; [[c1-K2b]], 60 s; [[c1-K3b]], 45 s; [[c1-K4b]], 45 s; [[c1-K5b]], 40 s, 8 s each.
- 2 min 10 s, weather: [[c1-W1b]], 35 s; [[c1-W2b]], 35 s; [[c1-W3b]], 60 s with a reason from each child.
- 2 min 10 s, forecast: [[c1-F1b]], 8 s each; [[c1-F2b]], 55 s; [[c1-F3b]], 35 s.

Steady equipment but never give a plan or an answer. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 2 min. Change-over

Balls into the bin, one wipe each, group 5 sits.

#### 8 min. Group 5 observed

As group 4.

#### 2 min. Change-over

As before.

#### 8 min. Group 6 observed

As group 4. Collect the mats and finish the record sheet for days 29 and 30: one record for every child, no second collection. Mark a child still unseen as not yet seen. No exit and no repair today.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No toy van: a rolled sock. No box: a cereal box and a shoelace you tie on, no loop. No eye protection for both: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The mat, the groups and the rug.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Ask the motion and weather questions once each; do not help. Mark each answer.
  **Say.** "Tell me what made it go." Expect: the touch.
- **15 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[c1-P30-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: harder, gentle, same, touch, rainy, forecast. Frame: "I changed ___. I kept ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 29's assessment and today's go back on day 31, which reads the sheet, repairs by error letter A to L with the largest groups and asks four fresh questions.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
