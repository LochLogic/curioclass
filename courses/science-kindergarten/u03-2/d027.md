# Day 27: A new problem, and what we find out first

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut, tape and rehearse the day before. Lay the one lane and bay and the taped strip as the layout drawing shows; keep it covered until the first group sits. Put a mat and crayons at each seat. Check the ball. Put one card bumper per group aside for day 28.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. You alone make the baseline rolls; children stay outside the lane. Floor only, no throwing, ball too big to mouth. Retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. With none, use the layout drawing.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Uncover the lane. Say once: "This is a pretend delivery lane, invented for practice. Here is a low bay of blocks, and here is a taped strip where people walk." Seat group 1 at the lane. The rest sit with side 1 of the page and draw [[s03-P27-1]]; no one else touches a ball. A second adult, if any, supervises them.

#### 4 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, no coaching:
- 2 min, P1: roll a gentle push that stops short, then a roll that misses the bay. Each child asks [[s03-P1a]], 10 seconds each. Draw where the gentle push stopped [[s03-P1b]]. Each child asks the goal card; answer, then touch what it said [[s03-P1c]].
- 2 min, P2: draw what people want to be different [[s03-P2a]]; touch each thing a helper must do [[s03-P2b]]; each child says why we find out first [[s03-P2c]], 6 seconds each.

Steady the cards but never give an answer. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 4 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 4 min. Group 6 observed

As group 1. Collect every mat.

#### 4 min. Change-overs and packing

Finish the record sheet. Pack the lane. A child away today is seen at the next meeting while the others draw; if not seen, mark not yet seen. No exit and no repair today. After class write a caption strip (A to F) on each mat.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper into two boxes for the mat. No lane: use the layout drawing and mark it.

### At home

**Swap.** No ball: a rolled-up sock. No blocks: two books as the bay. No tape: two strings for the strip. No eye protection for both: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The groups and the record.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Lay out a bay and a strip. Roll gently so it stops short, then roll so it misses. Ask once each, with no help: what would you ask, draw where it stopped [[s03-P1b]], touch what people want.
  **Say.** "What do people want?" Expect: the ball in the bay.
- **15 min. Draw.** Your child draws [[s03-P2a]], then says why we find out what people want before we build.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: bay, strip, ask, want. Frame: "I want to find out ___." Pointing, drawing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 28 is the response day: the same lane with a fixed low ramp and a bumper for each group. It reads a caption on each mat. Day 26's errors D and E are repaired there too.
