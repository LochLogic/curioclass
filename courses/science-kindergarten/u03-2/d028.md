# Day 28: Did our design do what we wanted?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Rehearse the day before: fifteen short rolls fit in three minutes only with the ramp fixed. Lay the lane and bay as on day 27, with the fixed low ramp and a bumper at the group's seat. Put day 27's mats back with their captions. Check the ball.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll. One child rolls at a time; the others keep hands off a moving ball and stay outside the lane. Floor only, no throwing, ball too big to mouth. Retrieve a ball only when it has stopped. With none, use the layout drawing.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Say "this is the same pretend lane, invented for practice." Seat group 1 at the lane. The rest sit with side 1 of the page and draw [[s03-P28-1]]; no one else touches a ball.

#### 3 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, no coaching:
- Each child pushes gently from the line, then harder, and marks each start [[s03-M1a]]; everyone touches yes or no [[s03-M1b]].
- Each child places the bumper where it must touch the ball to send it into the bay, rolls, and draws the path [[s03-M2]]; others draw while one rolls.
- Each child says what made it turn [[s03-M3a]], 6 seconds each. Everyone touches yes or no [[s03-M3b]].

Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A miss is a true result. Only a first unaided try counts.

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 4 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 5 observed

As group 1.

#### 3 min. Group 6 observed

#### 3 min. Read the captions

Each error group hears its caption strip once, read quietly, from day 27's assessment and today's. Write it on the mat.

#### 4 min. Return and revise

Day 26's exit showed errors D and E for some. Each child revises their own mat, at most two captions. For D, the child puts a finger on the touch on the repair cards' bending path; for E, lays the result picture beside the want picture and touches yes or no. No roll is repeated.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s03-X28a]] then [[s03-X28b]], once each; everyone touches.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper into three boxes. No lane: use the layout drawing and mark it.

### At home

**Swap.** No ball: a rolled-up sock. No blocks: two books as the bay. No eye protection for both: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The groups, the captions and the rug.

- **8 min. Roll and watch.** Roll gently, then harder, from one line. Ask which started faster.
- **12 min. Bumper.** Place a folded card where it must touch the ball. Your child draws [[s03-M2]].
  **Say.** "What made it turn?" Expect: it touched the card.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X28a]] and [[s03-X28b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one caption each. Never cut the rolls or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: faster, bumper, turn, touch, worked. Frame: "It turned where it touched." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Component marks are assigned the next school day. Day 29 carries both records, days 27 and 28, unchanged into the check on contact motion and unfamiliar weather information.
