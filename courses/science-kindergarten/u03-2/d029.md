# Day 29: New tests, new weather pages

## Teacher page

### Before class

You alone tie a short thick string, no loop, to each box. Lay five adjacent lanes you can see at once, each with a start line, a car, a box, a paper ball and two X marks. Put a mat and crayons at each lane. Keep form B covered for tomorrow.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and pull, you too. Floor only, no throwing, nothing small enough to mouth, string straight and never around a body. Nothing is carried to another lane. Stop any lane for unsafe play. With no eye protection run no trials: say the record is of pictures, which cannot show carrying out.

### The day

#### 4 min. Set-up and class transfer

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the three result cards for [[c1-T1]]; everyone touches; it is not scored. Seat group 1 at the lanes. The others sort weather pictures [[c1-P29-2]] or draw [[c1-P29-1]] on side 2.

#### 8 min. Group 1 observed

Five children, five lanes, mats in front. Ask each item once, aloud, with no coaching, and watch every child:
- 3 min 40 s, motion: [[c1-K1]], 30 s; [[c1-K2]], 60 s; [[c1-K3]], 45 s; [[c1-K4]], 45 s; [[c1-K5]], 40 s, 8 s each.
- 2 min 10 s, weather: [[c1-W1]], 35 s; [[c1-W2]], 35 s; [[c1-W3]], 60 s with a reason from each child.
- 2 min 10 s, forecast: [[c1-F1]], 8 s each; [[c1-F2]], 55 s; [[c1-F3]], 35 s.

Steady equipment but never give a plan or an answer. Mark each child on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only a first unaided try counts.

#### 1 min. Change-over

Balls into the bin, one wipe each, group 2 sits.

#### 8 min. Group 2 observed

As group 1.

#### 1 min. Change-over

As before.

#### 8 min. Group 3 observed

As group 1. Collect the mats. No exit and no repair today; day 31 repairs by letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper into the mat's boxes. Mats stay in the folder.

### At home

**Swap.** No toy car: a rolled sock. No box: a cereal box and a shoelace you tie on, no loop. No eye protection for both: the grown-up rolls.

**Skip.** The mat, the groups and the rug.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is invented. Ask the motion and weather questions once each; do not help. Mark each answer.
  **Say.** "Tell me what made it go." Expect: the touch.
- **15 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[c1-P29-1]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the observation is the day. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: harder, gentle, same, touch, cloudy, forecast. Frame: "I changed ___. I kept ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit: the record sheet is the evidence. Day 30 observes groups 4 to 6 in a parallel form with a toy van, a sled box and other records. Day 28's record and day 27's are carried unchanged and not scored again. Day 31 repairs by letter A to L.

Ours, all of it.
