# Day 31: Fix one part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count each letter A to L on the record sheet from days 29 and 30. Choose the three largest groups. Lay one table for each, with its repair cards. Put each child's mat at their seat.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. No rolling. Keep the walking space clear.

### The day

#### 3 min. Set-up and weather retrieval

Say once: "This is invented for practice." Point to the class chart. Children point to a cloudy day, then a sunny day. Say: "It varies, so we check each day."

#### 14 min. Return and repair

Teach at most three branches, the three largest letters: 4 minutes each with you, 1 minute to move. The rest work in pairs from the pictured move at their own letter's table. Repair by letter:
- A: two pairs of cards, the same car and a different car; point to the fair pair.
- B: pull one box gently and harder; show each start with hands.
- C: tap one ball from each side onto the X marks.
- D: order cards: come close, touch, changed.
- E: keep every mark; lay the guess beside it.
- F: five squares in a line; touch and slide each once.
- G: lay three day marks side by side and find the one that does not fit.
- H: put the word possible on the page picture.
- I: two town rows side by side; match the squares.
- J: two action cards in order, plan then shelter.
- K: sort shelter and not shelter cards.
- L: put the page and yesterday's squares in two piles.

Unit 3 letters left open on day 28 join the rotation. Give no answer; the picture is the repair.

#### 6 min. Revise your mat

Read each child's caption once; this hands back day 29's assessment and day 30's assessment. Each child redraws or recounts one part, keeping the first try beside the new one, at most two parts. Teacher circulates. No roll is repeated.

#### 7 min. Section exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask once each, everyone touches, and hear six: [[c1-X31a]], [[c1-X31b]], [[c1-X31c]], [[c1-X31d]].

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures by hand; fold paper into mats.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the pictures on paper.

**Skip.** The tables and the rug.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at your child's mat from days 29 and 30. Ask which part to fix.
  **Say.** "What would you change?" Expect: one part.
- **10 min. Fix it.** Your child redraws one part beside the first try.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X31a]] [[c1-X31b]] [[c1-X31c]] [[c1-X31d]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one table repaired. Never cut the record or the exit. Longer: draw two tests of one thing and say which was fair.

### Words and supports

Words: same, fair, varies, possible, shelter. Frames: "It varies, so we check each day." Pointing and any home language count; help is recorded as shown with help.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows which letters still stand; note them on the record sheet. Day 32 opens unit 4 and retrieves weather patterns in the warm-up. Records from days 27 to 30 pass on unchanged and are not scored again.
