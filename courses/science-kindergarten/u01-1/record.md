# Unit 1 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say or act it out. The Heard column is for the days, 1 to 7, on which you heard that child's reason (about six children a day, rotating). The End columns are the observation on day 8 or day 9, one cell for each look-for, and only a child's first unaided try at the sitting counts. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied equipment or read the card, and say so. Day 9 feedback and repair never change an End cell, and the day 9 fresh case is not scored.
