# Touch and no-touch cards (day 2 warm-up)

Six cards for pairs to sort into two piles, touch and no touch, then say "it moved when it was touched." Plain line drawings, one thing per card, no printed words. Pictures differ from the day 1 cushion and from the trials.

1. A hand pressing a rolling toy truck. The truck has motion lines. (touch)
2. A foot tapping a flat pebble across the floor. (touch)
3. A hand held far above a toy boat on a mat, with the boat at rest. (no touch)
4. A hand reaching toward a block with a clear gap, and the block at rest. (no touch)
5. A child's hand pulling a toy wagon by its string. (touch)
6. A toy train alone on a track with no hand anywhere, at rest. (no touch)

Key: the touching cards are 1, 2 and 5. A child who says a no-touch card is moving has met error C: ask them to point to what touched it.
