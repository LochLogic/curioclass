# Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks (points to, or places a counter on) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names, no faces drawn on the objects. Hands are shown as plain hands in different skin tones, never attached to a named child. Do not reuse a picture from the hook, the trials or the order cards.

## Day 1 cards: the cushion

**Card 1a.** A square cushion sitting on a floor. A hand presses on its side. A small arrow shows the cushion starting to slide.
**Card 1b.** A hand held well away from a cushion, with a gap. The cushion has motion lines behind it as if already sliding.
**Card 1c.** A cushion alone on the floor with a curved arrow beside it. No hand.

**Spoken:** "A cushion sat still on the floor. Then it slid. Touch the picture that shows what made it slide." **Key:** card 1a. Wrong 1b or 1c is error C.

## Day 2 cards: two prams

**Pair 1.** Two small panels, side by side. The same toy pram (a plain box on four wheels with a handle) is in both. A start tape is under the wheels in both panels. In the left panel a short arrow shows a gentle push. In the right panel a long arrow shows a harder push.
**Pair 2.** Two panels. The left panel shows a small pram with a short push arrow. The right panel shows a big pram with a long push arrow.

**Spoken:** "Pip wants to check whether a harder push sends a pram farther. Touch the two pictures that make a fair check." **Key:** pair 1. Wrong is error A.

## Day 3 cards: pulling and pushing

**Card 3a.** A hand holds the knob of a drawer pulling it toward the child. The drawer is partly open. A small arrow shows the opening direction.
**Card 3b.** A hand pushes a toy car with its flat palm. A small arrow shows the car going forward.

**Spoken:** "Pip says only pushing can start something moving. Touch the picture that shows Pip is not right." **Key:** card 3a. Wrong is error B.

## Day 4 cards: the tray and the gap

**Card 4a.** A top-down view. A tray slides up the page toward a wall with a gap at the left. A hand pushes the tray from its right side toward the left. An arrow points left.
**Card 4b.** The same wall and gap. A smaller, different tray is pushed straight ahead by a hand behind it. An arrow points up the page.

**Spoken:** "A tray is sliding toward a wall that has a gap at the left. Touch the push that could send it through the gap." **Key:** card 4a. Wrong is error F.
