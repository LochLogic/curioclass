# Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks (points to, or places a counter on) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names, no faces drawn on the objects. Hands are shown as plain hands in different skin tones, never attached to a named child. Do not reuse a picture from the hook, the trials or the order cards.

## Day 1 cards: the cushion

**Card 1a.** A square cushion sitting on a floor. A hand presses on its side. A small arrow shows the cushion starting to slide.
**Card 1b.** A hand held well away from a cushion, behind a line, palm turned away, with a wide gap. No motion lines anywhere; the cushion sits plain. The hand is not drawn as having just let go.
**Card 1c.** A cushion alone on the floor with a curved arrow beside it. No hand.

**Spoken:** "A cushion sat still on the floor. Then it slid. Touch the picture that shows what made it slide."

## Day 2 cards: two prams

**Card 2a.** One panel. A toy pram (a plain box on four wheels with a handle) sits on a start tape in a floor lane. No arrows, no second pram, nothing else drawn.

**Spoken:** "Pip wants to check whether a harder push sends a pram farther. Say Pip's plan: I change, I keep the same."

## Day 3 cards: pulling and pushing

**Card 3a.** One strip. A start tape at the left. Two stopping dots on two lines, one line labeled by a short pulling arrow and one by a long pulling arrow. The long-arrow dot is clearly farther (at least three tape pieces) from the start. A plain toy wagon with a string is drawn at the start.

**Spoken:** "Pip pulled a toy wagon from the same start, once gently and once harder. Say which pull moved the wagon farther."

## Day 4 cards: the tray and the gap

**Card 4a.** A top-down view. A ball's path goes straight up the page, then bends left at one place toward an X mark. At the bend a plain broom end touches the right side of the ball. A small box sits beside the straight part of the path with a visible gap between it and the path.

**Spoken:** "Pip's ball rolled straight, then turned toward the X mark. Say what touched the ball to make it turn."
