# Day 1: Where does our ball stop?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Clear three lanes a start tape each. Draw three empty boxes on the board and hang chart paper. The weather chart starts today, outside this lesson. Key: key-exits-1-4.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing, wait behind the line, no tasting or sniffing. Clean-up: balls into bins, one wipe each, eye protection to you. With none, run no rolls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 6 min. Hook

Three volunteers each roll one ball from the same start tape (a child may pass). Tape a piece where each stops. On the third roll tap its side and it turns.

**Ask.** "Why does our ball stop in different places, and why did it turn?" Write each idea on the board in the child's words; give no cause.

#### 6 min. Teaching first

Roll the ball once and, on the board, say each box as you draw it: before, the ball waiting; touch, the hand on it; after, the ball moving away.

**Ask.** "In the middle box, what happened to the ball?" Expect: the hand touched it, pushed it. Pointing counts.

#### 8 min. Roll and draw, in halves (4 + 4)

Three groups of five, one ball each. Each child has two strips: the page strip (own roll) and a paper strip (the hook). Half the class rolls once each (about 40 seconds), then draws their roll on the page strip while the next rolls. Read aloud: "Draw the ball before, the touch, and the ball after." The other half draws the hook on the paper strip. Swap after 4 minutes. If a middle box is empty, ask "what touched the ball?" Say the three words as they draw. Judge the page strip.

#### 5 min. Rug: notices and wonders

Pip asks one question at a time: "What did you notice?" then "What do you wonder?" Hear up to four of each; a child may pass. Tape three strips on the chart and write "touch" under the middle box.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the cushion cards, ask [[s01-X1]] once and everyone points. Hear a rotating six, 15 seconds each, while the rest draw on the back of their strip. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into three boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No tape: use a sheet of paper to mark the start. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The question board, the class chart and the rug talk.

- **5 min. Roll it.** On a bare floor, your child rolls the soft ball once from a mark. Put a piece of paper where it stops.
  **Say.** "What made the ball go?" Expect: my hand pushed it, I touched it.
- **10 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P1-1]]. Say each word as they draw: before, touch, after.
- **5 min. Wonder.** Say "I wonder why it stopped there." Write what your child wonders.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Say "this is invented." Draw three small pictures: a hand on a cushion, a hand held far away, a cushion alone. Ask [[s01-X1]] once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the wonders round. Never cut the touch box or the exit. Longer: roll again.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The ball moved when ___ touched it." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Error C (leaves out the touch) gets the touch and no-touch cards on day 2.
