# Day 4: Which way does it go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a start line and two X marks, left and right, in each of three lanes. Tie a short thick string, no loop, to a second box or a toy wagon. Key: key-exits-1-4.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll, pull and the clean-up. The string stays straight, never around a body, and nobody walks backward. Floor only, no throwing, similar slow pushes, wait behind the line. Clean-up: balls and strings into bins, one wipe each, eye protection to you. With none, run no rolls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pull the box and the wagon, each on its string. Children do [[s01-P4-3]] with their hands. If a third or fewer made error B on day 3's exit, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the push part.

#### 3 min. Plan

Show the keep, change, watch cards, the change card turned to two arrows toward the X marks. **Ask.** "This time we change which way. What do we keep?" Expect: the same ball, the same slow push, the same start. Watch: where the ball goes.

#### 12 min. Push toward each mark, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups of five, one ball each. Half the class pushes the ball slowly from the side toward each X (about 35 seconds a try). The waiting half draws the two paths on a path strip. The ball may miss an X; toward is enough. Swap after 6 minutes.

#### 2 min. Demonstration

Two volunteers, or you, pull the box toward each X while the class watches.

#### 3 min. Rug

**Ask.** "What touched the ball to make it turn?" Expect: the hand from the side. Add to the chart in the children's words: which way it is touched changes which way it goes. A child who says it turns before the hand arrives puts the order cards in order.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the broom card, ask [[s01-X4]] once. Pairs whisper the answer, then hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each; the rest draw the bent path. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, draw two X marks on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No tape: use two sheets of paper as the marks. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up pushes and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the box and the rug.

- **12 min. Push it.** Put two paper marks on the floor, left and right. Your child pushes the ball slowly from the side toward each mark.
  **Say.** "What did we keep the same? What changed?" Expect: the ball and the slow push; which way.
- **8 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P4-1]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4]] Say "this is invented." Draw a path that bends toward an X, and a broom touching the ball at the bend. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the demonstration. Never cut the exit. Longer: push each way twice.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The ball went ___ when it was touched ___." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who names a different ball or a near object as the cause (errors F and D). Day 5 opens with the same ball pushed two ways toward tape marks for them, then bumps two balls.
