# Day 2: A gentle push and a harder push

## Teacher page

### Before class

Clear three lanes with a start tape each. Lay out the dot strips, the keep, change, watch cards and six touch and no-touch cards for each pair. Key: key-touch-no-touch.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every roll and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing, wait behind the start tape. Clean-up: balls into bins, one wipe each, eye protection to you. With none, run no rolls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pairs do [[s01-P2-3]]. If many made error C on day 1's exit, all pairs sort; if a third or fewer, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the roll part. Pairs sort six cards into touch and no touch and say "it moved when it was touched." Ask a pair with a wrong card: "what touched it?"

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Pip guesses: "A harder push goes farther!" A guess is what we think before we test.

**Ask.** "What will we change, and what stays the same?" Show the cards: change, how hard; keep, the same ball, start tape and way; watch, the start and the stop. Roll gently, then harder, and lay a dot strip beside each stop. Say what you see, whatever it is.

#### 12 min. Gentle and strong, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups of five, one ball each. Half the class rolls gently, then harder (about 35 seconds a try) and marks a dot at each stop. The other half draws a guess. Swap after 6 minutes. A stop that is hard to read: roll that pair once more and keep both dots.

#### 3 min. Rug

Lay two strips side by side: nearer, farther or about the same (closer than one tape piece is the same). Add to the chart in the children's words: a harder push changes how it goes, and we watch how it starts.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the pram cards, ask [[s01-X2]] once and everyone points. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each, while the rest draw on the back of their strip. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, draw two lines on paper for the dot strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No large soft ball: roll a rolled-up sock. No tape: use paper to mark the start. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up rolls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up cards, the keep, change, watch cards and the rug.

- **5 min. Guess.** Your child draws [[s01-P2-1]].
- **12 min. Two rolls.** Roll the ball gently, then harder, from one mark, the same way. Your child marks each stop with a dot [[s01-P2-2]].
  **Say.** "What did we change? What stayed the same?" Expect: how hard; the ball, the start and the way.
- **3 min. Look.** Lay the two strips side by side. Ask nearer, farther or about the same.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2]] Say "this is invented." Draw two pairs of prams. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the rug. Never cut keep, change, watch or the exit. Longer: roll each push twice.

### Words and supports

Frame: "I changed ___. I kept ___ the same." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who changes the pram too (error A). Day 3 opens with the same or different cards for them, then asks whether a pull works like a push, with a box on a string.
