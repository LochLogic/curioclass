# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-1-4.


## Day 1 cards: the cushion

- Spoken: card 1a. Wrong 1b or 1c is error C (the motion begins with no touch).

## Day 2 cards: two prams

- Spoken: the push is what changes; the same pram, start or way is kept. Wrong is error A (a different pram, or nothing kept).

## Day 3 cards: pulling and pushing

- Spoken: the harder pull, the farther stop. A child who says a pull does not move the wagon is error B.

## Day 4 cards: the tray and the gap

- Spoken: the broom, on the side, at the bend. Wrong is error D (the box near the path) or F (a different ball).
