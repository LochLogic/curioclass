# Day 3: Gentle and harder pulls

## Teacher page

### Before class

You alone cut and tie a short thick string to each box, no loop. Lay three straight lanes with a start tape and a tape mark each. Lay out the before and after strips and the same or different cards. Keys: key-exits-1-4, key-same-or-different.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone present wears fitted eye protection for every pull and the clean-up. Floor only, no throwing. The string stays straight, never around a body, and nobody walks backward. No tasting or sniffing. Clean-up: you remove the strings, boxes into bins, one wipe each. With no eye protection, run no pulls: show dated photographs, said aloud to be from the day before.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Pairs do [[s01-P3-3]], pointing to the pair that checks a push fairly and saying "it is the same one, pushed two ways." If a third or fewer made error A on day 2's exit, hear just those children for two minutes at one lane in the pull part.

#### 5 min. Teaching first

Pip asks: "Does pulling work like pushing?" Pull a box by its string gently, then harder, from the same start tape. **Ask.** "What made the box start moving?" Expect: the pull, the string, your hand. Pointing counts. Draw before and after on the board.

#### 12 min. Gentle and stronger pulls, in halves (6 + 6)

Three groups of five, one box each. Half the class pulls gently, then harder, toward their own tape mark, standing still and drawing the string in (about 35 seconds a try). The waiting half draws before and after strips. Read aloud: "Draw the box before and after the pull." Write the words of the day's six on their strips as they tell them. Swap after 6 minutes.

#### 3 min. Rug

Lay strips side by side. Ask for each: did the box move? Add to the chart in the children's words: a pull can change how it moves.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice." Show the wagon strip, ask [[s01-X3]] once. Pairs whisper the answer, then hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each; the rest draw the wagon. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

With no printer, fold a paper into two boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No light box: use an empty cereal box. No string: use a shoelace you tie on, with no loop. No safety glasses or goggles for both of you: the grown-up pulls and your child watches from the side.

**Skip.** The warm-up cards and the rug.

- **8 min. Pull it.** Your child stands at a mark and pulls the box gently, then harder, drawing the string in. Never walk backward.
  **Say.** "What made the box start moving?" Expect: my pull.
- **12 min. Draw it.** Your child draws [[s01-P3-1]]. Write what they say under it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]] Say "this is invented." Draw two stopping dots on a line, one far and one near, labeled gentle pull and harder pull. Ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one half pulls and the other half draws. Never cut the pull, the strip or the exit. Longer: pull each way twice.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The box moved when ___ pulled it." Pointing, acting out and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who sees no link from a pull to motion (error B). Day 4 opens with two pulled objects, shown with hands, for them, then keeps the push the same and changes which way it goes.
