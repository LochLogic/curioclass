# Same or different cards (day 3 warm-up)

Two pairs of cards. A pair is two panels side by side. Pairs point to the fair pair and say "it is the same one, pushed two ways."

- **Pair 1, same object.** The same toy bus in both panels on the same start tape. A short push arrow in the left panel and a long push arrow in the right panel.
- **Pair 2, different object.** A small toy bus pushed with a short arrow and a large toy bus pushed with a long arrow.

Plain line drawings, no printed words.
