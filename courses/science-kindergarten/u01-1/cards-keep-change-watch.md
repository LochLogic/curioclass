# Keep, change, watch cards

Three large cards for a group or a mat: keep, change and watch. They are used on day 2, again on day 4 and day 6, and as the planning cards for end item 1 on days 8 and 9. Plain line drawings; the only words are the three words under the pictures, said aloud by the teacher.

- **Keep.** An open hand holding a small ball and a start tape together, with an equals sign. Word under it: keep.
- **Change.** A hand with a curved arrow around it, showing a gentle push and a harder push as two arrows of different length. Word under it: change.
- **Watch.** A pair of plain eyes above the start tape and the stop place. Word under it: watch.

For day 4 turn the change card to the second set: two arrows pointing to two tape X marks. For end item 1 add a small picture of a ball and a box so the child can place each card on the part it is about.
