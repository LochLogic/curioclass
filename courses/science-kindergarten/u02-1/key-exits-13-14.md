# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 13 and 14

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-13-14.


## Day 13 cards: five days in Denver

- Five day squares in a row, one date nume: rain on four of the five days.
- Spoken: four. Five, with one square touched twice, is error B; three means a square was missed (touch and slide with a partner, record "shown with help").

## Day 14 cards: July in Denver

- 14a. Spoken: thirteen (the teacher says the total, over ten). Fourteen or fifteen, with a square touched twice, is error B.
- 14b: one day. Two days is error C.
- 14c: more sunny days; the teacher says the totals, fourteen and eight, after the answer. "More rainy days" is a slip in reading the rows: no register letter, record "shown with help".

## Day 14 cards: the rule for the rows

- The rule used, checked against the archive on 2026-11-22: a day is the sky with the greatest coverage in any layer at 3 p.m. local time, sunny for few or clear and cloudy for broken or overcast; days whose greatest coverage is scattered, 2, 6, 11 and 20 July, sit in neither the sunny nor the cloudy row. Rainy is any report of rain in that day; windy is a sustained wind of 20 knots or more at any time that day.
