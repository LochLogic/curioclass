# Day 10: Two coat hooks and one day on the chart

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 9 left nothing to finish. Hang the two coat hook pictures beside today's chart row; the pattern sheet hangs empty. Put out six envelopes of five dated chart rows and the first ten squares. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; nothing to prepare.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

Say "these are pictures of our coat hooks" (or "we drew these; they are invented for practice"). **Ask.** "What do you notice? What are you wondering?" Expect: many coats in one, few in the other. Take six ideas, 20 seconds each; the rest point. Tell no answer. Post the big question: "Why do we need our coats at morning recess but not after lunch?"

#### 3 min. Review from unit 1

Show the before, touch and after strip; everyone points to each box. **Ask.** "What made the ball change?" Expect: the touch.

#### 6 min. Teaching first: one dated entry

Pip and you read today's chart row: date, morning picture, afternoon picture, arrow. Name the sky pictures together. Teach morning and afternoon by pointing left and right, and the arrow: up is warmer, down is cooler, two bars are about the same. **Ask.** "Where is the afternoon? Was it warmer, cooler or about the same?" Expect: a point to the right picture, then the arrow.

#### 8 min. Read the chart, in groups

Six groups of five, one envelope each. Each child points to one thing they notice (15 seconds, 75 a group, all at once), then draws the sky they would look at to find out what changed [[s02-P10-1]]. Hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Write wonders on slips; the class picks one.

#### 5 min. Begin the picture graph and the pattern sheet

Ten children each place one school-day square in a row (sunny, cloudy, rainy, windy), side by side as the stopping strips were; ten turns of 10 seconds. Show the pattern sheet: a pattern is what we see again and again, so one day is not a pattern. It stays empty.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "these pictures are Denver, from another observer's record." Ask [[s02-X10]] once; everyone points. Hear the six, 20 seconds each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw squares on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No coat hooks: lay two pictures of a row of pegs, eight coats on one and two on the other. No squares: draw a sun, cloud, raindrop and wind line on cut paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the graph.

- **8 min. Two pictures.** Show the two pictures. **Say.** "What do you notice? What do you wonder?" Expect: any idea, such as fewer coats. Give no answer.
- **12 min. Look at a day.** Draw the sky now, and the sky you would look at later to find out what changed. **Say.** "What would you look at again?" Expect: the sky, later.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X10]] Draw a morning and afternoon sky of one place, then three small pictures: which time was warmer, last year, next week. Ask once and your child points.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three ideas in the hook. Never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: morning, afternoon, warmer, cooler. Frame: "In the afternoon it was ___ than the morning." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 plans a morning look and an afternoon look at one taped spot. A child who touched last year or next week is marked not yet seen and sits by you at day 11's chart rows.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
