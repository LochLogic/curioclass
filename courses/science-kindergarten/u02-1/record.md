# Unit 2 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say or act it out. The Heard column is for days 10 to 19, on which you heard that child's reason (about six children a day, rotating). The End columns are the observations on day 18 (W1 to W3, in order) and day 19 (F1 to F3, in order), one cell for each look-for, and only a child's first unaided try at the sitting counts. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you steadied the page or read the card, and say so. Day 19 caption revisions never change an End cell, and the day 19 exits are response, not scored.
