# Weather squares and chart-row cards

Small square cards the same size as the class chart squares. One set for each group of five (an envelope each), plus a demonstration set for the teacher. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the date numeral on a date square. Scrap-paper squares do as well as bought cards.

**Sky squares.**
- **Sunny:** a round sun with straight rays and no cloud.
- **Cloudy:** a flat grey-outlined cloud covering most of the square.
- **Rainy:** a cloud with six short slanted rain strokes under it.
- **Windy:** two long curved lines streaming sideways with a small leaf blowing along.

A day can have more than one sky picture. A rainy and windy day uses a rainy square and a windy square, each with the same date square clipped to it.

**Date squares.** A blank square with a numeral for the day of the month in the corner. A gap in the class record is a date square with no sky square beside it, and it stays a gap.

**Warmth marks.** Three small cards for the afternoon against the morning: a plain arrow pointing up (warmer), pointing down (cooler), and a flat equals-shaped pair of bars (about the same). Used on day 10 for the teacher's chart row. They are marks about a feel; no number scale is used.

**Chart-row card (one row of the class chart).** A strip with, left to right, the date square, a morning sky square, an afternoon sky square and a warmth mark. Six table copies, each with five dated rows from the live chart.

**Teacher note:** the squares are used again on days 13, 14, 18 and 19 for the class's own live record. They are never mixed with the Denver exit squares.
