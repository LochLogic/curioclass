# Coat hook cards (day 10 hook)

Two pictures for the hook, shown beside that day's row of the class weather chart. Say first: "These are pictures of our coat hooks." If the teacher's photographs fit, use those with names covered and no child in view. If not, draw these two and say aloud: "We drew these two pictures. They are invented for practice." Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. The hooks are plain pegs on a wooden rail, never attached to a named child.

**Card 10a: morning recess.** A long wall rail with eight pegs. On six pegs hang plain coats of different sizes and colors, drawn as outlines. A row of small boots sits under the rail. Two pegs are empty.

**Card 10b: afternoon recess.** The same rail and eight pegs. Only two pegs hold a coat. Six pegs are empty. The boots are gone.

**Spoken:** "Here are our coat hooks at morning recess, and here they are at afternoon recess. What do you notice? What are you wondering?" Take ideas; tell no answer. Ask no child about their own coat, home or family.
