# Day 14: Our first month in rows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make six table copies of the live record from school day 1 to about day 20, gaps kept. Choose today's six. Hang the picture graph and the pattern sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. Squares go back in their envelopes in the clean-up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Day 13's exit showed error B for many. In pairs, five squares in a line: one child slides a square into a box as the partner counts it [[s02-P14-0]]; the last number says how many. Fifteen pairs at once, a minute each.

#### 3 min. Set-up

Read the month's dates in order and name the gaps. A gap stays a gap; we do not guess it.

#### 12 min. Count our first month

Six groups of five, each with a table copy. They sort the squares into four rows, sunny, cloudy, rainy and windy. One child for each row touches every square once and says the last number; the fifth checks. A day in two rows goes in both with the same date. Say any total over ten. Each group dictates one line under its row, "There are more ___ days than ___ days"; write it. Look for marks in the rows, each date counted once, and this month, not "always". Each child draws a row [[s02-P14-1]]. Hear the six, 20 seconds each, as groups dictate.

#### 5 min. Rug

Fill the graph from one group's rows; groups that counted differently recount once, touch and slide, and the class keeps the recount. Match two rows end to end. **Ask.** "What does the month show that one day did not?" Expect: a longest row, or many kinds of days. Revise the pattern sheet: the rows that stand out, and "it varies, so we check each day" for the afternoon claim.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Squares into envelopes.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "this month is Denver, from another observer's record." Ask [[s02-X14a]], [[s02-X14b]] and [[s02-X14c]] once each; everyone touches. Hear the six, one ask each, 15 seconds each.

### Other rooms

With no live record, use another observer's dated picture record, labelled and credited, from a month other than July to October 2024.

### At home

**Swap.** No record: draw ten squares of sunny, cloudy, rainy and windy days, one with rain and wind. No box: use a cup.

**Skip.** The groups and the graph.

- **5 min. Count.** Touch each rainy square once and slide it into a cup. **Say.** "How many?" Expect: the last number touched.
- **12 min. Rows.** Sort the squares into four rows and draw one row [[s02-P14-1]]. **Say.** "Which row is longest? How can we tell?" Expect: match them square to square.
- **8 min. What the month shows.** **Say.** "What does it show that one day did not?" Expect: any row that stands out.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X14a]], [[s02-X14b]] and [[s02-X14c]] Ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: count two rows, not four. Never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: row, count, longer. Frame: "There are more ___ days than ___ days." Dictated lines and pointing count.
