# Plan cards for the looks (days 11 and 12)

Four large cards the class chooses from, used on day 11 and again on day 12 for the bench. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words, no brand names. Children shown are different heights, hair and skin tones, never named.

**Card keep the place.** A child standing on a taped X on the ground, with the same X drawn again beside a second child, both standing on it. Says: keep the place.

**Card keep how we look.** Two children, one for the morning and one for the afternoon, both facing the same way with one finger up for one minute. Says: keep how we look.

**Card change the time.** A small clock showing the morning hour beside a small clock showing the afternoon hour, with a bent arrow between them. Says: change the time.

**Card watch.** An eye looking at a sky square, and a hand held flat in front of a face with a small dot beside it for the feel. Says: watch the sky and the feel.

**Day 12 bench set.** The same four cards with a plain bench in place of the taped X: a bench in sun and a bench in shade drawn side by side.

**Pip's guess.** A small card of Pip with a thought bubble holding a sun at the afternoon clock. Pip says his guess for the look. Children say what a guess is: what we think before we look.

**Used with.** The unit 1 keep, change, watch cards are the review on day 11's warm-up; these cards are new pictures for the new case, not the same cards.
