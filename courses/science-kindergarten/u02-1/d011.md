# Day 11: Plan a morning look and an afternoon look

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape one X at a spot in sight of the room. Choose three earlier chart rows (afternoon warmer, cooler, same) and today's six.

### Safety and set-up

The day before, check the weather and the spot. Outdoors: stay in sight, touch nothing unknown, never look at the sun. In thunder, ice, unsafe heat or poor air, look from a window away from the glass. Set-up for a later unit: you alone photograph a sidewalk weed or animal-dug hole if one is in sight.

### The day

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Show keep, change and watch. Ask [[s02-P11-0]]; everyone points. Expect: the ball and the start. Pip guesses the afternoon will be warmer; a guess is what we think before we look.

#### 6 min. Teaching first: plan the looks

Pip asks: "How can we find out if the afternoon is warmer than the morning?" The class picks from the four plan cards: keep the place, keep how we look (face one way, hand out for a slow count of ten), change the time, watch the sky and the feel. Model a morning mark: take the matching sky square, hold your hand out, mark a dot with rays for warm or a plain dot for cool. The dot is a start; it means more beside the afternoon dot.

#### 3 min. Line up

Say once: "Stay in sight. Touch nothing unknown. Never look at the sun."

#### 8 min. Morning look outdoors

All thirty stand around the X, facing one way, each with a sheet. Each draws the sky [[s02-P11-1]] and marks one dot [[s02-P11-2]], all at once. Hear the six, 20 seconds each: "How does it feel?"

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Sheets and crayons to baskets.

#### 5 min. Rug

Read your three chart rows; a child day 10's exit marked not yet seen sits by you. **Ask.** "Was the afternoon warmer, cooler or about the same?" Expect: a point to the arrow. Write on the pattern sheet what was seen: some warmer, some cooler, some the same. Say "it varies, so we check each day." With no cooler row, say "we have not seen every kind of day yet."

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "this one is invented for practice." Ask [[s02-X11a]] once; everyone points. Say "these are two days in Denver, from another observer's record" and ask [[s02-X11b]] once. Hear the six, 20 seconds each.

### Other rooms

At afternoon recess lead the same look at the X; children fill side 2 [[s02-P11-3]] [[s02-P11-4]]. Keep the sheets for day 12.

### At home

**Swap.** No taped X: use a doorstep. No sky squares: draw the sky.

**Skip.** The plan cards and the rug.

- **5 min. Plan.** **Say.** "How can we find out if the afternoon is warmer than the morning?" Expect: look in the morning, look again in the afternoon, same place.
- **8 min. Morning look.** Stand at the step and draw the sky [[s02-P11-1]]. Hold your hand out and mark a dot [[s02-P11-2]].
- **8 min. Later look.** Do the same on side 2 [[s02-P11-3]] [[s02-P11-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X11a]] [[s02-X11b]] Draw the cards and ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two plan cards.

### Words and supports

Words: guess, plan, warm, cool. Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow
