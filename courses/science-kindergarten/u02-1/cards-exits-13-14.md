# Exit cards, days 13 and 14

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except date numerals, no brand names. Squares are the size of the class chart squares. Do not reuse a picture from the class chart, the row mat or the live record.

**Credit, printed on every Denver master below:** "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University, Denver International Airport station (KDEN). Dates as marked." The teacher's readings are in brackets and are never printed for children.

## Day 13 cards: five days in Denver

Five day squares in a row, one date numeral on each: 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8 August. A raincloud square is clipped to the 4th, 5th, 7th and 8th. The 6th has a sun square only. (Teacher key: rain on four of the five days.)

**Spoken:** "Five days in Denver, made from another observer's record. Rain fell on some of them. Touch each rainy day once, and say the last number." **Key:** four. Five, with one square touched twice, is error B; three means a square was missed (touch and slide with a partner, record "shown with help").

## Day 14 cards: July in Denver

Four rows of squares for the whole month of July, side by side on a wide sheet, each square clipped to its date numeral. A day that sits in two rows has the same date square drawn in both rows.
- **Sunny row:** fourteen squares. (Dates 4, 5, 8, 10, 12, 13, 22, 23, 24, 25, 28, 29, 30, 31.)
- **Cloudy row:** thirteen squares. (Dates 1, 3, 7, 9, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 21, 26, 27.)
- **Rainy row:** eight squares. (Dates 2, 13, 15, 16, 20, 21, 27, 28.)
- **Windy row:** thirteen squares. (Dates 1, 3, 6, 7, 15, 16, 17, 19, 20, 25, 26, 27, 28.)

(Teacher key, the rule used: a day is the sky with the greatest coverage in any layer at the afternoon hour; rain and wind are reported at any time that day. Check the dates against the archive before printing.)

**14a. Spoken:** "A whole month of days in Denver, in picture rows. Touch each rainy day once, and say how many." **Key:** eight. Nine or ten, with a square touched twice, is error B.

**14b.** The 15th of July sits in the rainy row and in the windy row, marked with a ring. **Spoken:** "In the same record, one day sits in the rainy row and in the windy row. Is that one day or two days? Touch the card with one dot or the card with two dots." **Key:** one dot. Two is error C.

**14c.** The sunny row and the rainy row laid side by side, squares end to end. **Spoken:** "Match the sunny row beside the rainy row, one square to one square. Touch the longer row." **Key:** the sunny row; the teacher says the totals, fourteen and eight, after the match. A touch on the rainy row is a matching slip: no register letter, record "shown with help".
