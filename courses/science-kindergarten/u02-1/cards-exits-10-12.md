# Exit cards, days 10 to 12

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks (points to, or places a counter on) and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Say "this is invented for practice" first when it is. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except date numerals, no brand names. Do not reuse a picture from the hook, the class chart or the plan cards.

**Credit, printed on every Denver master below:** "Curio Class made these pictures from another observer's record: Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Iowa State University, Denver International Airport station (KDEN). Dates as marked." The teacher's readings are in brackets and are never printed for children.

## Day 10 cards: one place, two times

**Pair 10.** Two panels side by side, the same airport field, the same fence and windsock. Left panel: a sun with a few small clouds, headed by a morning clock and the date 1 August. Right panel: the same sun and a few small clouds, headed by an afternoon clock and the same date. A warmth mark between the panels: an arrow pointing up.

**Question cards (three).**
- **10a.** A small panel with two clocks, one morning and one afternoon, and a hand over a sun: which time was warmer.
- **10b.** A calendar page with a year-back arrow and a cloud: what was the weather last year.
- **10c.** A calendar page with a forward arrow and a raincloud: will it rain next week.

**Spoken:** "These two pictures are one place, Denver, in the morning and in the afternoon. Touch the question these two pictures can help us answer."

## Day 11 cards: the swing set and two pretend days

**Plan pair 11a.** Two panels. Left: a swing set with a morning clock above. Right: the same swing set, same fence, with an afternoon clock above. A bent arrow joins the clocks.
**Plan pair 11b.** Two panels. Left: the swing set with a morning clock. Right: a pond with reeds and an afternoon clock. The same bent arrow joins the clocks.

**Spoken (invented for practice):** "Pip wants to see if the afternoon is warmer than the morning. Touch the plan that is fair for finding out."

**Day pictures 11c.** Two calendar squares side by side, each holding a morning panel and an afternoon panel, and a feel dot under each panel. Left square: a plain morning dot, an afternoon dot with rays. Right square: a morning dot with rays, a plain afternoon dot. The skies are the same plain sun in all four panels. No dates, no place, no numbers.

**Spoken (invented for practice):** "These are two days from a pretend school's record. Say which day the afternoon was warmer than the morning."

## Day 12 cards: Pip's pretend picnic table

**Cards 12a and 12b.** The same picnic table drawn twice, same tree beside it. 12a has a morning clock above it and a small dot beside it with a thin radiating line (warm). 12b has an afternoon clock above it and a small plain dot (cool). Nothing else changes.

**Spoken (invented for practice, a pretend picnic table):** "Pip says every table is warmer in the afternoon. Here is Pip's table in the morning and in the afternoon. Rays mean warm and a plain dot means cool. Does this table say so? Touch yes or no."
