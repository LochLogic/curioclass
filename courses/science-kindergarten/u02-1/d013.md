# Day 13: Rows of days, counted once each

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill six envelopes with five dated squares each from the live chart (a gap stays a gap). Choose today's six. Choose a day with two kinds of weather; if the chart has none, make a pretend day and say aloud that it is invented for practice.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; paper only. Squares go back in their envelopes in the clean-up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Day 12's exit showed error A for many. Lay a second chart row where the afternoon was cooler or the same beside one where it was warmer; ask [[s02-P13-0]] and everyone points. Say "it varies, so we check each day."

#### 7 min. Teaching first: rows

You and Pip put five demonstration squares into rows, sunny, cloudy, rainy and windy. Touch each square once and slide it into the box; the last number says how many. Over ten, you say the total. Lay two rows side by side, square to square, as the stopping strips were in unit 1. **Ask.** "Which row is longer? How can we tell?" Expect: the one with squares left over.

#### 9 min. Our own rows

Six groups of five at a row mat, each with its own five days. Each child places one day [[s02-P13-1]]. Then one child for each row touches every square once and says the last number; the fifth child checks. Groups work at once, about 4 minutes. Hear the six, 20 seconds each, two minutes.

#### 4 min. One day, two rows

Put your two-kind day into both rows with the same date. Say "one day, two rows." **Ask.** "Which squares have the same date?" Expect: a point to both. Count each date once. Rows added together would give more squares than days.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Squares back into envelopes.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "these five days are Denver, from another observer's record." Ask [[s02-X13]] once; everyone touches. Hear the six, 20 seconds each. Mark what you see.

### Other rooms

With no live squares, use the five dated squares on the exit cards laid in rows as the teaching case, and write "no live rows" on the chart.

### At home

**Swap.** No squares: cut five paper squares and draw a sky on each. No box: use a cup.

**Skip.** The groups.

- **5 min. Make rows.** Lay the squares in rows by sky. **Say.** "Which row is longer?" Expect: the one with squares left over.
- **12 min. Count each once.** Touch each square once and slide it into a cup; the last number says how many. Draw one sky from a row [[s02-P13-1]].
- **8 min. One day, two rows.** Put a square with rain and wind in both rows. **Say.** "Is that one day or two?" Expect: one day, two rows.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X13]] Draw five day squares, rain on some; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three rows instead of four. Never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: row, count, longer. Frame: "The ___ row is longer." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 repairs error B, a square touched twice, with touch and slide in pairs. Then the class counts its first month in four rows.
