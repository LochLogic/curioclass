# Day 12: Is the bench warmer in the afternoon?

## Teacher page

### Before class

That morning you and today's six touch the bench and mark one dot each, rays for warm, plain for cool; put them on the chart as the morning bench. Teach after lunch recess.

### Safety and set-up

The day before, check the bench and weather. Stay in sight, touch nothing unknown, never look at the sun. You touch the bench first, back of a hand a few seconds; no touch on metal in sun. If unsafe, no child touches: the class marks sun or shade pictures instead. Wash hands after.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Lay a chart row where the afternoon was cooler or the same beside one where it was warmer; ask [[s02-P12-0]] and everyone points. Say "it varies, so we check each day." With no such row, say "we have not seen every day yet."

#### 4 min. Practice: read and plan

Read day 11's afternoon dots beside its morning dots on three sheets; everyone points to warmer, cooler or the same. Say the plan with the cards: keep the bench and its sun or shade, change the time, watch the feel. Nothing new is taught.

#### 3 min. Teacher check

Go out in sight. You touch the bench alone while the class waits.

#### 9 min. Afternoon touch

Waiting groups draw the bench with its sun or shade [[s02-P12-1]]. Groups of five lay the back of one hand on it for a few seconds and mark a dot [[s02-P12-2]]: six turns of 1 minute on a full bench, or ten turns of 40 seconds on a short one, three hands at a time, 7 and a half minutes with the swaps. Hear the six as they mark: "How does it feel?"

#### 4 min. Compare

On the rug lay the morning dots beside the afternoon dots. **Ask.** "Is it the same on every day? How do you know?" Expect: no, we only saw this day. Write on the pattern sheet in the children's words: "this bench, this afternoon". Say the bench is not the air.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

One wipe each at the door, wipes in the bin.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Say "this one is invented for practice, a pretend picnic table." Ask [[s02-X12]] once; everyone points. Hear the six, 20 seconds each.

### Other rooms

Morning dot missing, or a class that meets only in the morning: use the invented bench pair and write "no real reading today" on the chart.

### At home

**Swap.** No bench: use a stone step. No wipes: wash hands.

**Skip.** The wipes and the groups.

- **5 min. Look back.** **Say.** "Was it warmer in the morning or afternoon?" Expect: a point to the dots.
- **12 min. Touch the step.** Draw it [[s02-P12-1]]. A grown-up touches first. Your child lays the back of a hand on it and marks a dot [[s02-P12-2]].
- **8 min. Say the claim.** **Say.** "Is it the same on every day?" Expect: no, only this step, this afternoon.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X12]] Draw two picnic tables, one with a warm dot and one with a plain dot; ask once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear three, not six.

### Words and supports

Words: warm, cool, bench, sun, shade. Frame: "This bench, this afternoon, was ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow
