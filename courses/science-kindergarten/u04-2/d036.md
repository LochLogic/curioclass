# Day 36: What does a place give?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the place pictures, cards and marks for six tables. Put the three places on the board, the pattern sheet in reach and Pip ready. Air is only a swirl mark; do not say how it is used. Keys: key-exits-36-39, key-places.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No food, animal or soil in the room.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

Day 35's exit showed error D for some: for two minutes they match each animal to its food card; the rest touch the water, light and eats rows on the pattern sheet, then all say each row aloud.

#### 4 min. Hook

Put the forest, meadow and pond on the board with the deer, grass and fish cards, drawn for practice. Pip puts the fish in the meadow. Children say what is wrong.

#### 10 min. Build the links

Show the fish in the pond. Draw a crayon line from the fish to the water, and a second to the pond plants. Say "a line is a link: the fish gets this from here." Say where each lives: the deer at the forest edge by the stream, the grass in the sunny meadow where rain falls. Say "air is all around the deer; the swirl mark is our picture of it." Six tables then draw links for the deer (grass for food, water, the swirl for air) and the grass (sun for light, rain for water, land). Each child draws on the student page [[s04-P36-1]] and [[s04-P36-2]]. Hear a rotating six tell one link: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. What if

Pip puts the fish back in the meadow. **Ask.** "Which link is missing?" Children touch where the line stops. Say "no water link, no fish here." Then Pip puts the grass in the dark forest: which link is missing? Light.

#### 3 min. Chart

Each table sticks its place and links on the class chart; one child per table tells one link, 20 seconds each. Label it.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X36a]], then [[s04-X36b]], once each; everyone touches or draws. Mark the rotating six.

#### 1 min. Pack

Crayons into the tub.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw three boxes, and a fish, a deer and a tuft of grass.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a pond, a dry field and a living thing for each.

**Skip.** The chart.

- **8 min. Look.** Look at a picture of a pond. Ask what the fish could get there.
- **12 min. Links.** Your child draws a line from the fish to the water [[s04-P36-1]] and from the grass to the sun [[s04-P36-2]].
  **Say.** "Which link is missing if the fish is in a field?" Expect water.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X36a]] and [[s04-X36b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the fish and the grass only. Never cut What if or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: place, link, line, water, light, food. Frame: "The ___ gets ___ from here." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many touched the dry parking lot or said the duck can live anywhere, day 37's warm-up traces the missing link on the grass in the dark forest. Day 37 then adds people to a town map.
