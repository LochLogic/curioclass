# The town map and Pip's town (days 37 and 40)

Two kinds of map. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. No home, address or child is pictured or asked about.

## The teacher's printed town map (preferred)

A simple printed map of the school, the town water source named as the town's own water report names it, fields and trees. The report cannot be checked from here, so this map is used only when the teacher has the report in hand and has read it. A line is drawn from the source to the school tap and to a cup.

## Pip's town (the invented fallback, said aloud to be invented)

A map our own drawing, used whenever the report is not checked. A river bends along the left side. A water works building stands beside it. A line of pipes (a plain double line) runs to a school with a tap. Beyond the school are two fields, a patch of trees and a road with no houses drawn. Labels, written large by the teacher: "river", "pipes", "school", "tap", "fields", "trees".

## The person card

A person card is cut from paper: a simple figure, an adult, no face detail. It stands for "people", not for any child.

## What happens

The teacher traces water with a crayon from the source to the tap and to a cup. Pairs add a person card and draw lines to water (drinking), food grown on land, wood or soil for shelter, and air.

## Key

Lines from the person to the tap water, the field food, the trees for wood and a swirl for air. "The house makes the water" is error F; the line from the source shows where it comes from.
