# Day 37: Where do people get what they need?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the printed town map only if you have read the town's water report; otherwise use Pip's town and say aloud that it is made up. Either way no home is pictured. Cut a person card for each pair. Keep the place pictures and the chart in reach.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No home, address or family is asked about.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

Day 36's exit showed error E for some. For two minutes, lay the fish in the meadow beside the fish in the pond, and trace the missing link on the grass in the dark forest with a finger. Then everyone finds the water and eats rows on the pattern sheet and says what the fish needed.

#### 4 min. Look at the map

Hang the map. Say "this is a town, and its map is drawn for practice" if it is Pip's town. Children find the school and the water. Ask once, "Where does the school's water come from?" Accept any answer.

#### 8 min. Add people

Trace the water with a crayon line from the river, along the pipes, to the school tap and a cup [[s04-P37-1]]. Say "the house does not make the water; the line starts at the source." Pairs then put a person card on their map and draw links to drinking water, food from the fields, wood from the trees and the swirl for air [[s04-P37-2]]. Hear a rotating six explain one link: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Revise the model

People and their links go on the class picture chart beside the places. **Ask.** "If the river changed, which links would change?" The class says it aloud; scribe one link in a child's words.

#### 3 min. Share

Pairs tell a partner one link, all together, 1 minute. The class then hears three pairs: 20 seconds each, 1 minute.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X37a]], then [[s04-X37b]], once each; everyone touches or draws. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Pack

Maps into folders; crayons into the tub.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw a river, a line of pipes, a school and a tap.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: draw a tap and a line back to where the water starts.

**Skip.** The chart.

- **8 min. Look.** Look at a tap. Ask where the water could start. Any guess is fine.
- **12 min. Lines.** Your child draws a line from the river to the tap [[s04-P37-1]] and from a person to something from the land [[s04-P37-2]].
  **Say.** "Does the house make the water?" Expect no.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X37a]] and [[s04-X37b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the water line only. Never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: person, river, tap, source, land, link. Frame: "The person gets ___ from ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many touched the cottage or said the house makes the water, day 38's warm-up follows the line from the source to a cup with a finger. Day 38 then starts evidence: a hole, a nest and a tilted slab.
