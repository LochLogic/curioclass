# Eye card, thought card and claim strip (days 38 to 43)

For the teacher and for each pair. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. 

## The cards

- **The eye card.** A large eye drawn on a card. It means: what I saw.
- **The thought card.** A small cloud with a question mark in it. It means: what I think.

## The claim strip

A strip for the wall and a strip for each pair, with two blank lines and two small pictures: an eye, then a cloud. The teacher says aloud: "I think ___, because I saw ___." The strip is not read by the children; the teacher writes each pair's words under their drawing.

## Model on day 38

The teacher puts the eye card on the soil pile and says: "I saw a pile of soil beside the hole." Then the cloud: "I think the squirrel moved the soil." Then both: "I think the squirrel moved the soil, because I saw the pile."

For the weed: "I saw a weed in a crack. Did we see the crack form? No. So we cannot say the weed made it yet." The weed stays a "not yet" case.

## Sorts

On day 39 three pictures are sorted into eye (something I saw) and thought (something I think): a pile of soil, "the bird wanted a home", a hole in a trunk.
