# Teacher key: Place pictures, living-thing cards and marks (day 36)

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-places.


## What happens

Pip puts the fish in the meadow. Children say what is wrong. The teacher then models the fish in the pond: a line to the water and a line to the pond plants. The deer stands at the forest edge (grass beside the stream); the grass is in the meadow (sun and rain). Tables draw lines for the deer (to grass for food, to water, to a swirl for air) and the grass (to the sun for light, to the rain for water, to the land).

## Key

- The fish: lines to water and to pond plants.
- The deer: lines to grass, water and air.
- The grass: lines to the sun, the rain and the land.
- In the "what if" part, the fish in the meadow has no pond water and no pond plants, so no water line. The grass in the dark forest has no light line.
