# Day 38: What did you see?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out three evidence sets for six tables, two to a set. Hang the eye card and the thought card. Find the class's fall photographs of the squirrel's hole and the weed in the crack. No blame, no harm in any picture. Keys: key-evidence-38, key-exits-36-39.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. No live animal.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up and repair

Day 37's exit showed error F for some. Pairs put a finger on the town map and follow the line from the source to a cup. Then everyone touches the three rows on the pattern sheet.

#### 4 min. Hook and model

Pip says "the hole just appeared!" Show the squirrel-hole photograph. Put the eye card on the soil pile: "I saw a pile of soil." Put the thought card by it: "I think the squirrel moved the soil." Say both: "I think the squirrel moved the soil, because I saw the pile." Show the weed photograph: "I saw a weed in a crack. Did we see the crack form? No. So we cannot say the weed made it yet."

#### 7 min. Evidence sets

Each table gets its set: the hole and soil pile, a bird building a nest, or a tree root and a slab, flat then tilted. Children point to what changed and to what it gives: shelter, a safe place for eggs, the root reaching damp ground. Say "the slab is what we saw; the root grew."

#### 6 min. Say it

Each pair draws one changed part [[s04-P38-1]] and says "I think ___, because I saw ___"; the partner points to the picture that shows it [[s04-P38-2]]. Write the stem under their drawing. Hear a rotating six: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Class chart

Add an arrow from each changed place to what it gives (shelter, a safe place for eggs, water). Pin the claim strip beside it.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X38a]] once; pairs whisper, then hear the six, 15 seconds each. Ask [[s04-X38b]] once and all touch. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Pack

Pictures into envelopes; crayons into the tub.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw a hole, a nest and a slab on three cards, and an eye and a cloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: with a grown-up, look outside for a hole, a nest or a worn path; look from a safe distance and never touch holes or nests.

**Skip.** The claim strip.

- **8 min. Look.** Look at a hole, a nest or a crack in a path. Ask what changed.
- **12 min. Say it.** Your child circles the changed part [[s04-P38-1]] and draws it, saying "I think ___, because I saw ___" [[s04-P38-2]]. Write the words under it.
  **Say.** "Did you see it or think it?" Expect: the hole is seen; the digger is thought.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X38a]] and [[s04-X38b]] Say they are invented; ask once each; your child says the first sentence and touches the second.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two sets. Never cut the weed or the exit.

### Words and supports

Words: change, hole, nest, root, saw, think. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing and any language count.

### Tomorrow

If many said nothing changed or only people change things, day 39 opens with the slab flat and tilted, and the squirrel's pile beside the goose's grass. Day 39 then uses a dam and a nest.
