# Repair cards for errors E to I (days 37 to 40)

A wrong idea beside a right one. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. The teacher says it as a seeing, never as a blame.

- **E, "it can live anywhere".** The fish in the meadow with no line to water, beside the fish in the pond with the line. Trace the missing line on a second misplacement (the grass in the dark forest). Day 37.
- **F, "the house makes the water".** The map with a crayon line from the source to the tap and the cup. A finger follows the line. Day 38.
- **G, "plants cannot change places".** The paving slab flat, then tilted over the root. A finger on what changed. Day 39, with H.
- **H, "only people change things".** The squirrel's soil pile and the goose's short grass side by side. Who changed each? Day 39, with G.
- **I, "I like it".** The teacher says a liking and a seeing, such as "I like it" and "I saw chips under the hole." The child touches the one that is evidence. Day 40.
