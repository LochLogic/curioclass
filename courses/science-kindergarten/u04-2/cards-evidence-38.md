# Evidence sets, day 38

Three sets, two tables each. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. 

## Set 1. The squirrel hole

Two cards. The class's saved fall photograph of the squirrel's hole, and our drawing of a pile of soil beside a round hole at the foot of a fence. The need it meets: shelter.

## Set 2. A bird building a nest

Our own drawing in three small pictures: a small brown bird with a twig in its beak; the bird on a branch with a few twigs laid in a fork; a round cup of twigs in the fork with the bird sitting in it. The need it meets: a safe place for eggs.

A National Park Service video of two cactus wrens building a nest (Joshua Tree National Park, credit NPS / Brad Sutton, 2014) was considered. The page does not allow the video to be presumed public domain and says to ask the park who owns it, so the video is cut and these drawings are used instead.

## Set 3. A tree root and a paving slab

Our before and after in two pictures. First: a flat paving slab with a young tree beside it. Second: the same slab tilted up on one side, the tree's root showing under the raised edge. The need it meets: the root reaching water in the damp ground below.

## What happens

Children point to what changed and to the need it meets. The weed photograph is shown as a "not yet" case, not as evidence of a change.

## Key

Hole and soil pile: shelter. Nest of twigs: safe place for eggs. Raised slab over the root: reaching water. Changes seen, not guessed.
