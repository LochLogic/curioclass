# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 36 to 39

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-36-39.


## Day 36

- Spoken 36a: the pond edge. The dry parking lot, or any place, is error E.
- Spoken 36b: water, or the reeds and water plants to eat. Saying nothing is shown with help, no letter.

## Day 37

- Spoken 37a: the rain tank. The cottage is error F.
- Spoken 37b: the plot or the woodpile. Saying nothing and saying a house means no need of nature is error F.

## Day 38

- Spoken 38a: the vine covered the fence to get light, because the leaves reach up to the sun. "Nothing changed" is error G; "I like the vine" is error I.
- Spoken 38b: the goose (the middle picture shows it pulling at the grass). The person is error H. The kite is a card that is not evidence.

## Day 39 (section exit)

- Spoken 39a: the bird made a hole in the tree; the need is shelter, a safe place for its eggs. A miss with no letter is shown with help.
- Spoken 39b: a claim that names the bird and a picture, such as "I think the bird made the hole, because I saw chips." A liking offered as the reason is error I.
