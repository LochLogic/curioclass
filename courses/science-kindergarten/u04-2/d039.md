# Day 39: How does a dam or a nest change a place?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print our drawing of the dam (a low wall of sticks across a stream, a pond behind it). Pre-pair the class. Pull the slab pictures, the squirrel's pile and the goose's grass from the repair cards. Nothing new is taught today.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only. No live animal.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 38's exit showed errors G and H for some. In pairs, two minutes each: the slab flat and tilted side by side, a finger on what changed (error G); then the squirrel's pile and the goose's grass side by side, and who changed each? (error H). If fewer than a third missed, a small group sits with you and the rest draw a hole or a nest.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Touch the three rows on the pattern sheet. Then three quick sorts into eye (something I saw) or thought (something I think): a pile of soil, "the bird wanted a home", a hole in a trunk.

#### 9 min. A new photograph

Show the beaver dam drawing and the swallow's mud nest drawing, both made for practice. Pairs pick one and say "I think ___, because I saw ___"; the partner points to the picture that shows it [[s04-P39-1]]. Hear a rotating six: 20 seconds each, 2 minutes.

#### 4 min. Revise a link

The class changes one arrow on the chart: the dam or the nest goes in as a changed part, linked to shelter [[s04-P39-2]].

#### 3 min. Anchor question

At the question board: "Why would the squirrel dig by the fence?" Show the squirrel-hole photograph. Dictate one claim with its picture.

#### 3 min. Draw and tell

Each child draws one change and tells it to a partner, 30 seconds each, together. Write the stem under the drawings of the rotating six.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say "this is invented for practice, and not scored." Ask [[s04-X39a]], then [[s04-X39b]], once each; everyone touches or says. Mark the rotating six on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw a stick wall across a stream and a mud nest.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: with a grown-up, look outside for a nest, a hole or a worn path; look from a safe distance and never touch nests or holes.

**Skip.** The chart.

- **8 min. Look.** Look at a picture of a nest or a dam. Ask what it gives the animal.
- **12 min. Say it.** Your child circles the nest [[s04-P39-1]] and draws a line to what it gives [[s04-P39-2]]. They say "I think ___, because I saw ___".
  **Say.** "Did you see it or think it?" Expect: the nest is seen; the maker is thought.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X39a]] and [[s04-X39b]] Say they are invented; ask once each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the dam only. Never cut the repair or the exit. Longer: add a second line to a link and say which picture shows it.

### Words and supports

Words: change, dam, nest, shelter, saw, think. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

If many offered a liking as the reason, day 40 opens with a liking and a seeing: touch the one that is evidence. Day 40 then adds people clearing land for houses.
