# Section 4.2 opener: places give what living things need

## What the section does

Four days, 36 to 39. Day 36 builds a picture model with lines: the deer, the grass and the fish each get lines to what a place gives (food, water, air, light, land). Day 37 adds people, using the teacher's printed town map and its water source, or an invented town said aloud to be invented. Day 38 turns to change: a squirrel's hole, a root under a paving slab and a bird's nest are evidence that living things change places to meet a need, and children learn one sentence, "I think ___, because I saw ___." Day 39 is a fluency day with a beaver dam and a nest. The claim sentence is not taught earlier than day 38.

## How the days run

Each day is 30 minutes. A short warm-up and repair opens each day (the exit error of the day before), then a hook, a build at six tables, a share, the exit and a pack. A rotating six children are heard each day. Nothing is read by the children. The class picture chart and the pattern sheet stay on the wall through day 43.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A model shows relationships.** A line from a living thing to a thing in its place says "it gets this from here." A missing line is a missing need.
- **People are part of the picture.** Water goes from a source to a tap to a cup. A house does not make water.
- **Observation, then claim.** The eye card says what I saw. The thought card says what I think. A claim needs a picture that shows it.
- **Living things change their places** to meet needs: plants, animals and people. A like or dislike is not a seeing.
- **The weed stays a "not yet" case.** Nobody saw the crack form, so the class does not claim the weed made it.
- **Not here:** gases and breathing, food chains, growth rates, and judging how families use resources.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E:** "It can live anywhere." Find the line that is missing.
- **F:** "The house makes the water." Follow the line from the source.
- **G:** "Plants cannot change places." Look at the slab flat, then tilted.
- **H:** "Only people change things." Look at the squirrel's pile and the goose's short grass.
- **I:** "I like it." A liking is not a seeing.

## Safety

Pictures and printed maps only. No live animal and no animal handling. Draw and tell with no blame and no graphic picture. Crayons go back in the tub at clean-up.

## Supplies

Place pictures and cards, marks (drop, sun, bite, swirl, brown land), the town map, person cards, the evidence sets, the eye and thought cards, the claim strip, the dam and nest pictures, exit cards, repair cards and crayons. A room with nothing uses a hand-drawn place on paper, cut-out cards and crayon lines.
