# Beaver dam and swallow nest pictures (day 39)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. 

## The beaver dam photograph

A National Park Service photograph: a beaver lodge in the middle of a pond created by a large beaver dam in Bering Land Bridge National Preserve. Credit printed on the back and said aloud: NPS Photo / Andrea Willingham. The teacher shows the pond and says: "the dam made this pond." If the photograph is not printed, use our own drawing: a low wall of sticks across a stream with a pond behind it and a lodge of sticks in the pond.

## The swallow nest drawing

Our own drawing: a small bird at a half-round nest of mud stuck to the underside of a bridge, a few pellets of mud lying below.

## What happens

Pairs say "I think ___, because I saw ___" for one of the two; the partner points to the picture that shows it. The class changes one arrow on the chart: the dam or the nest is added as a changed part linked to shelter.

## Teacher background

The National Park Service says its photographs are generally in the public domain unless noted, and that third-party content may carry its own rules (page read 2026-11-19).
