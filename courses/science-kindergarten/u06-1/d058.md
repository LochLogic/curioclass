# Day 58: What would our action change somewhere new?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a reused sheet beside a fresh one. Post the class chart and the bin picture. Choose today's three pairs to hear.

### Safety and set-up

Pictures and paper only; no samples today.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many gave error B in day 57's exit, hold a sheet blank side up beside a fresh sheet: using the blank side means one less new sheet. If many gave error C, show an adult-bagged pick-up beside shrubs. At most two branches, 1.5 minutes each.

#### 3 min. Say it back

Ask: "Which tally row has more?" Then ask: "What did we start doing?" Children point to the class chart.

#### 5 min. A school corner we have not seen

Say once: "This corner is invented." Show the picnic table with paper under it and bottles by a drain. Together children point to the litter, you name our action, and they say what it changes.

#### 6 min. Pairs

A says "If we ___, then ___" and B points to the part of the picture that shows it; swap. Two minutes each, two to move. Hear three pairs.

#### 6 min. Draw it after

Each child draws the corner after our action. Scribe a label for six children as they dictate it.

#### 4 min. Is it clear?

A partner points to the part of the drawing that shows the action. Revise the class sentence from what was unclear. The corner is invented, so we say what it might change, not what it did.

#### 3 min. Section exit

Say it is invented. Show the two drawings, before and after. Ask [[s06-X58a]]; six touch in turn. Touching the puddle is shown with help, no letter. Ask [[s06-X58b]]; all draw an arrow in the box. "Every bottle goes to be recycled everywhere" is error F.

### Other rooms

Draw the corner on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed corner: draw a table, paper and two bottles by a drain.

**Skip.** The pairs and the class chart.

- **10 min. A new corner.** Your child points to the litter in your drawing.
  **Say.** "What would our action change here?" Expect: fewer bottles or less paper.
- **10 min. Draw it after.** Your child draws the corner after the action.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X58a]] [[s06-X58b]] Say it is invented.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pair each way. Keep the drawing and the exit. Longer: a second new corner.

### Words and supports

Words: litter, action, effect. Frames: "If we ___, then ___." "It might ___." Pointing and any home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 hears this exit first. If many gave error F, point to what our bin takes in the bin picture. Then the class counts the same area again and compares it with day 55.
