# Exit cards, days 55 to 58

Each exit has picture cards that every child marks or touches and a spoken question heard from the day's rotating six. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children in the pictures are of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Say aloud first that every case is invented for practice. Withhold each picture until the closing. Do not use the pictures of the four chains, the three actions or the class patch. Mark on the one scale. Nobody is blamed for the litter in any picture.

## Day 55: a paved corner and a puddle

**Picture.** A paved corner of a playground. Four scraps of crumpled paper lie on the pavement beside a puddle. A strip of grass and a fence run along the far side. No arrows.
**Spoken 55a:** "Invented for practice. Touch each scrap once and make a mark for each." **Key:** four marks, one for each scrap. A miscount is recounted with a partner, no letter.
**Spoken 55b:** "Draw an arrow for where one scrap might go." **Key:** an arrow from a scrap toward the puddle, the grass, the fence or another place in the picture. "It stays where it fell" is error A.

## Day 56: a school fair

**Picture.** A school fair on a field. Near a booth the grass is torn into bare, churned patches. A delivery van has a grey haze behind it. At the field edge three wrappers are blowing into a stream. A bird pecks at a bit of plastic on the ground. No cause arrows are drawn.
**Spoken 56a:** "Invented for practice. For land, then water, then air, then living things, touch what is hurt." **Key:** the torn grass, the wrappers in the stream, the haze, the bird. Touching the van itself instead of the haze is shown with help, no letter.
**Spoken 56b:** "Draw one thing the school could do so one of these is hurt less." **Key:** a feasible action and its benefit, such as a reminder card at a bin or a helper who picks up after lunch. "Put the wrappers in the shrubs, that keeps them safe" is error C.

## Day 57: a bench

**Picture.** A park bench with three loose wrappers beside it and wind lines pointing toward a row of shrubs.
**Spoken 57a:** "Invented for practice. Draw what our trial would do here." **Key:** the class's chosen action drawn on the bench scene, and a changed picture with fewer loose wrappers or less new material used. Wrappers pushed under the shrubs is error C.
**Spoken 57b:** "Tell why that helps." **Key:** "If we ___, then ___" naming an affected part. If the class chose to reuse paper, "reusing paper does not change how much new paper we need" is error B.

## Day 58 (section exit): bottles by a puddle

**Picture.** Two drawings of the same school corner. First: five bottles lie beside a puddle by a wall. Second: the same corner after a class's action, with a reminder card at a bin and two bottles by the puddle. A caption read aloud: "Before. After our class's action."
**Spoken 58a:** "Invented for practice. Touch what the action changed." **Key:** the bottles beside the puddle, fewer in the second drawing. Touching the puddle is shown with help, no letter.
**Spoken 58b:** "Finish the arrow that says why it helps." **Key:** an arrow from the action to less harm to the water or the living things near the puddle. "Every bottle goes to be recycled everywhere" is error F.

## Day 58 teaching corner (not an exit)

A picnic table with paper under it and bottles by a drain. Said aloud to be invented. Used with the whole class on day 58 to point to the litter, name the action and say what it changes.
