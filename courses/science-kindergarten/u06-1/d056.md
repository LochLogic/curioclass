# Day 56: What can litter touch, and what will we do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill in the bin picture: draw or paste what our bin takes and does not take. Post day 55's chart. Choose today's six to hear.

### Safety and set-up

Children work from pictures only. The break pick-up is yours alone, with the adult bag, gloves and grabber: dry paper or intact plastic only, then wash hands.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

If many gave error A in day 55's exit, draw three quick sketches, said to be invented: a scrap on a path, wind lines, the scrap by a puddle. Trace a dashed arrow with a finger: "might".

#### 2 min. Read back

Read back day 55's tally rows and drawing. Say: "I saw the litter. It might go."

#### 12 min. Four picture chains

Say once: "These pictures are invented for practice." Show each chain, 3 minutes, one actor each. Chains: tree, bottle and haze, wrapper in the wind, turtle and ring. Children point to what changes. Say "can" or "might", never "did". Mark land, water, air or living things on the class drawing with an arrow from the action, dashed for a might. A child may speak to one part.

#### 5 min. Three school actions

Say: "A solution is what we choose to do. Reuse: use it again. Recycle: our bin takes it and it is made into something new." Show the three action pictures beside the tally rows we counted most, and the bin picture: "Our bin takes this. It does not take that." Never "every bottle goes". Choose by hand, touching each hand once [[s06-P56-1]]; a tie goes to the pick-up routine.

#### 4 min. Start the trial

2 minutes: each child draws the chosen action on a reminder card: a hand and the place it goes [[s06-P56-2]]. 2 minutes: crayons to the tub, wipes, cards gathered. At the next break do the first pick-up (a set-up).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say it is invented. Show the school fair. Ask [[s06-X56a]], naming one part to each of six children in turn: they touch, 20 seconds each. Ask [[s06-X56b]]; all draw in the box and say which part it spares. Touching the bus for air is shown with help, no letter. Putting litter in the shrubs is error C.

### Other rooms

Draw each chain in three plain frames on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pictures: draw three frames.

**Skip.** The vote and the reminder cards.

- **10 min. A chain.** Draw a wrapper, wind and a stream.
  **Say.** "What might the wind do?" Expect: carry it to the stream.
- **10 min. What is hurt?** Name land, water, air, a living thing.
  **Say.** "Which one could litter touch here?" Expect: water, or any one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X56a]] [[s06-X56b]] Say it is invented.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the second chain. Keep the vote and exit. Longer: add a fourth action idea.

### Words and supports

Words: land, water, air, living things, solution, reuse, recycle. Frame: "The ___ can hurt the ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 hears this exit first. If many gave error C, bring the shrub picture, then the bagged litter. Then children show the chosen action with a clean bottle or paper and say how it lessens one effect.
