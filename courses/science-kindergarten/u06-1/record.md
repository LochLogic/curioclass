# Unit 6 record sheet: our class list

Write each child's name down the left. Use one scale in every cell: S for shown, H for shown with help, N for not yet seen. Each E column is one spoken exit and has a cell for each look-for listed under the table; a child may point, say, draw or act it out. The End columns are the observations on day 60 or day 61 (the T items, in order), one cell for each look-for; a child is seen once, on one of the two days. Only a child's first unaided try at the sitting counts. Never write N for a part you did not see; leave the cell blank and try another day. Write H when you read a card, steadied a page or placed a mark for the child, and say so. Day 60 work is scored on day 61 after the feedback and revision; day 61 work on the next school day. The day 61 exit is a response and is not scored. Keep one record across both days.
