# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 55 to 58

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece cards-exits-55-58.


## Day 55: a paved corner and a puddle

- Spoken 55a: the paper scraps (four against two bottles). Touching the bottles is a miscount, recounted with a partner, no letter.
- Spoken 55b: an arrow from a scrap toward the puddle, the grass, the fence or another place in the picture. "It stays where it fell" is error A.

## Day 56: a school fair

- Spoken 56a: land, the bare soil; water, the bag in the pond; air, the haze; living things, the duck. Touching the bus itself instead of the haze is shown with help, no letter.
- Spoken 56b: any feasible action that keeps litter or harm out of the pond. "Put the wrappers in the shrubs, that keeps them safe" is error C.

## Day 57: a classroom corner

- Spoken 57a: the part the chosen action changes: the three cups on the floor (pick-up), the half-used sheets (reuse), or the cups and sheets the school bin takes (recycle). The gap behind the cupboard, as a place to put the litter, is error C.
- Spoken 57b: "If we ___, then ___" naming an affected part. If the class chose to reuse paper, "reusing paper does not change how much new paper we need" is error B.

## Day 58 (section exit): bottles by a puddle

- Spoken 58a: fewer bottles beside the puddle in the second drawing. Saying the puddle changed is shown with help, no letter.
- Spoken 58b: an arrow from the action to less harm to the water or the living things near the puddle. "Every bottle goes to be recycled everywhere" is error F.
