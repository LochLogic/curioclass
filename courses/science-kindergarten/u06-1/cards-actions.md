# Three school action pictures (days 56 to 58)

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a caption, no brand names. Children and adults of many skin tones, hair and abilities. Each is a school action the class could choose, pictured with the kind of litter it touches. One actor in each picture.

## Action 1: a pick-up routine with a reminder

A child holds up a reminder card beside a bin at the edge of the playground. A grown-up in gloves, with a grabber and a bag, stands nearby (the grown-up is the actor). **Caption:** pick up, and remember. This action is for loose pieces of any kind and is the default when there is a tie.

## Action 2: reuse paper

A child turns a used sheet over and draws on the blank side. A new sheet stays in the pile (the child is the actor). **Caption:** use the other side. This action touches the paper row of the tally.

## Action 3: recycle what our bin takes

A child puts a bottle in the bin that the accepted-materials picture shows (the child is the actor). **Caption:** the right bin. This action touches the bottle or plastic row, and only for what our school's bin takes.

## How the class chooses

The teacher holds up the three pictures beside the day 55 tally rows. Each is tied to the kind of litter counted most. Children choose by hand, the teacher touching each hand once; a tie goes to the pick-up routine.

## Repair picture for error C: the shrubs

Used on day 57 for children who say litter is safe in the shrubs. A row of shrubs at the edge of a field, a nest among the branches, a wrapper pushed under the shrubs (the child is the actor). Beside it, the same edge with an adult in gloves bagging the wrapper. **Caption:** not under the shrubs.
