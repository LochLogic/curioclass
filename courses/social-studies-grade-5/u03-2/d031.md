### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X30a`, `s03-X30b`, `s03-P30`, `s03-F30`, `s03-T30a`, `s03-T30b`.

Return day 30's notice names, explanation, date ordering, scale work and retry. If many tickets show error B, compare earlier duties with the duty that remained; model five minutes without fresh notices. Reveal the invented hall-price case, [[s03-F31]], for five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain unresolved needs.

#### 10 min. Powers and consent

Review charter: an official grant, from day 1; assembly: a lawmaking body, from day 9. A legislature makes laws; review Congress as the national legislature from grade 4, while explaining that the Stamp Act Congress was a gathering of colonial delegates. Representation means acting through chosen representatives. Review consent: agreement.

Explain powers and eligibility separately. Colonial institutions developed before independence: charters granted powers, assemblies made laws, town meetings made local decisions. Participation was restricted. Read Congress's local tax-consent claim and Parliament's binding-law claim separately. Name "no taxation without representation." Contrast the isolated labels elected consent and no taxes without supplying a complete development/conflict explanation.

#### 12 min. Institution annotations

Model marking one power on the Parliament strip. **Ask.** [[s03-P31]] Expect local tax consent on Congress and broad lawmaking on Parliament. Children annotate separate teaching strips, not the held Penn, Boston and Virginia records. A separate teaching note distinguishes choosing officers from everyone's eligibility. No three-record integration or institution-development argument is rehearsed. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way, then each child writes. If many equate representation with no taxes, point to consent. If many infer everyone voted, separate powers from eligibility.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the three fresh summaries with [[s03-X31]]. All thirty explain alone on one half slip. Judge earlier governing experience and its link to parliamentary tax conflict. Restricted participation is context for feedback, not an extra unstated scoring demand. Neutral reading and transcribed dictation count as access. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 40 seconds for exits plus 30 times 15 seconds for practice/retry, 27.5 minutes. Return everything day 32, Return and repair. Review the single scale; support separately.
