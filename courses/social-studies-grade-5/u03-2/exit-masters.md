## Day 31 source card, reveal with the exit

Original summaries:
Penn charter, 1701: Eligible freemen choose an assembly yearly. It prepares bills for laws.
Boston town vote, 1768: Choose people to represent the town at a convention.
Virginia petition, 1764: Tax laws need representatives chosen by the people taxed.
Freemen means a legally eligible group, not everyone.
