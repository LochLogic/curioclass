# Day 30: A first pass through British policies

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use unit 3 policy-cards.md as a reference. The student page embeds the named cards and unit 3 scale strip and ruler at actual size; check its centimeter bar. Cut today's unit 3 section 2 fresh repair slips. From unit 3 section 2 private exits, put notices A to E and s03-X30a in the covered Exit box, s03-X30b alone on a half slip. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X29a`, `s03-X29b`, `s03-P29`, `s03-F29`.

Return day 29's explanations, arrow and map-limit retry. If many tickets show error A or error H, choose one: chronology or separating money pressure from Native land concerns. Model for five minutes, then reveal the invented store/delivery case, [[s03-F30]], for five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain other needs.

#### 10 min. Policy overview

Keep yesterday's promise: show the five named policy cards separately. Review tax. A duty taxes specified goods. Sugar, 1764: customs revenue and enforcement; Stamp, 1765: printed-paper tax; Townshend, 1767: import duties; Tea, 1773: special selling terms with a retained duty; Intolerable, 1774: punitive port and government restrictions. Retain the separate Proclamation reference.

Sketch separate actions: imported cargo, stamped paper, tea sale, closed port. Protests included petitions and refusal to buy; the punitive laws followed tea destruction. Review petition: a written request to officials. Criticism did not mean every colonist agreed. Teach facts without decoding unnamed notices or modeling a cheaper-price/authority explanation.

#### 12 min. Timeline and scale

Review chronology. **Ask.** [[s03-P30]] Children place already named dates, then annotate one response from the cards. No six-policy matching chain. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way. Review grade 4 map-scale: calibration stays at matching size. Give [[s03-T30a]] alone. Expect 600 feet. If missed, use five minutes measuring three centimeters and multiplying, then [[s03-T30b]], expecting 400 feet. Retrieval does not become newly scored mastery. Other pairs continue dated annotations.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover five fresh notices and [[s03-X30a]] in the student page's Exit box. Give s03-X30b on its half slip, referring to the single printed notice D. All thirty answer alone. Collect both with practice. After class: 30 times 40 seconds for six exit components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 30 minutes. Return everything day 31, Return and repair. Review the component scale; record access separately.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to paper. No printer: copy today's panels and stems exactly. Keep future sources covered.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or projector: copy today's named panels and point to paper. Use scrap paper. Keep held cards hidden.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read yesterday's cue, then [[s03-F30]] alone.
- **10 min. Policy overview.** Read the named cards one at a time.
- **12 min. Timeline and scale.** Do [[s03-P30]] and [[s03-T30a]].
  **Say.** "What distance does the scale give?" Expect 600 feet. If needed, demonstrate and try [[s03-T30b]], 400 feet. Without a ruler, copy a measured strip beforehand.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Uncover the Exit box and [[s03-X30a]]. Give [[s03-X30b]] on its half slip. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping repair and independent evidence. Longer: identify evidence still needed for one answer.

### Words and supports

Review tax, chronology, petition and map-scale. Duty: a tax on specified goods. Teach Townshend; accept Townsend. Preserve the bar calibration in large print. Everyone may pass in public talk. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. No personal disclosures or reenactment.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 returns every day 30 paper, including the Exit box and scale work. If many tickets show error B, contrast an earlier duty with a retained one. Then distinguish governing powers, voter eligibility and consent before a fresh institutional explanation.
