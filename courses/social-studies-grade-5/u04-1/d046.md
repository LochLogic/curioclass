# Day 46: Requests, actions, and rights

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 45 papers and deferred day 44 cues. Select one class repair priority. Separate teacher-key.md; current item-row keys govern additions and corrected practice. Cover the exit. Prepare a private alternative packet when requested: Records U/V only, with s04-P46-alt, without the shared sheet's comparison question.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 45's exits, annotations and repair; revisit deferred day 44 notes and s04-P44-2. Review protected repair. If many day 45 tickets show error B, use [[s04-K46-B]] then [[s04-F46-B]]; error J: [[s04-K46-J]] then [[s04-F46-J]]; error A: [[s04-K46-A]] then [[s04-F46-A]]. Deferred day 44 error W: [[s04-K46-W]] then [[s04-F46-W]]. Model one selected priority for five minutes; everyone answers its fresh row for five. Read the model steps after responses. Other gaps receive written cues and stay recorded.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Read day 46 teaching selections. Review petition, a written request to officials, and Hall's January 1777 freedom request. Review enslaved people as people forced to work and denied freedom. A request alone establishes no successful outcome. Review the gap between a promise and what happened, from grade 3 day 133, without rehearsing a complete gap explanation.

The Onondaga Nation's present timeline reports Haudenosaunee agreement to neutrality in 1775. Explain neutrality as remaining outside either side. Read its 1778 entry in plain words: Haudenosaunee leaders reject United States authority to draft their men. Nations' authority differs from an individual's legal rights. Do not turn this into one choice for every Native person. Women also faced unequal political participation; reserve voting details for the exit. No reenactment or personal disclosures.

#### 12 min. Action notes

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. **Ask.** [[s04-P46]]. Label requested action separately from maker/date on source labels; add today's dated teaching label to the timeline strip, reviewing equal year gaps. Hear a rotating six, one 45-second observation each, within six minutes. Record names; public passing is allowed. All thirty submit notes. No complete promise/voting comparison is modeled.

Alternative: give Records U/V and [[s04-P46-alt]] privately. Annotate makers and actions separately. Record unsampled history as not yet seen; this task cannot establish historical understanding.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s04-X46]] for independent paper: six minutes, collection two. Neutral reading and transcribed dictation give no content cues. Alternative users may decline historical passages; record unsampled components, without substitution claims. After class: thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, to mark all papers. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component; access separately. Return everything on day 47.

### Other rooms

No screen: use printed selections. No printer: copy only assigned stems and source panels. Keep future forms hidden.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy today's selections and assigned questions separately.

**Skip.** Public observations and class grouping.

- **10 min. Feedback.** Read one private cue. Use its model and fresh response from today's repair choices.
- **10 min. Read.** Read today's selections together.
  **Say.** “Does asking establish success?” Expect no; the request does not show its result.
- **10 min. Notes.** Complete [[s04-P46]], or the assigned alternative [[s04-P46-alt]]. Use source labels for maker and date.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X46]]. Your child answers independently. Honor the sensitive-passage choice; retain unsampled history as unknown. Save papers for day 47 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public observations, preserving individual notes and exit. Longer: name evidence needed to establish a request's outcome.

### Words and supports

Review equality, natural rights, legal rights and rule of law as needed. Enlarge print; allow neutral reading and private dictation. Study named nations without assigning children to represent them.
