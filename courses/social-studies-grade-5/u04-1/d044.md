# Day 44: A promise and our questions

## Teacher page

### Before class

Send the family note, including adult-student choice. Prepare only day 44 selections and covered exits. Separate teacher-key.md from every student copy; use current item-row keys for added questions. Prepare thirty private component records. Save day 43's retry and readiness papers for day 45.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

Pick up day 43's Tomorrow: open “Did the Revolution keep the Declaration's promise?” Read today's two teaching summaries. Review the Declaration's equality line and January 1777 Prince Hall petition from grade 3. Attribute the first to Congress, July 1776, addressing the world; the petition asks Massachusetts lawmakers for freedom. Read the original equality phrase and petition's freedom request aloud, without graphic passages. Neither summary establishes equal protection in practice.

#### 5 min. Founding retrieval

Review consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made, grade 3 day 127. **Ask.** [[s04-K44-1]] Pause, then [[steps:s04-K44-1]]. Pairs exchange one observation concurrently. If consent becomes agreement with every decision, distinguish choosing a process from liking its result. Participation alone does not establish freedom.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, from grade 4 day 19. Model separate labels, then [[s04-P44]]. Keep the key private. Children put the same dated labels on their timeline strip. Review equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks.

Explain that an inquiry can help other people examine public claims. Personal preference alone cannot explain that use. A promise states an ideal; a record of action helps test what happened. Keep these ideas separate, without modeling the held newsletter task.

#### 14 min. Question board

Review the question board routine. **Ask.** [[s04-P44-2]] Children write individually; fifteen pairs each take two concurrent 45-second turns, one per child. Passing publicly is allowed. Post questions under promises, wartime choices, and government. Attach answer days: promises 45 to 46; military events 47 to 49; choices 50 to 51; government 52 to 55. Reserve four minutes to revisit the consent distinction if needed. Collect both notes; blank source labels stay in folders.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal only [[s04-X44a]] and [[s04-X44b]]. Six minutes independent paper, two to collect all thirty. Neutral reading or privately transcribed dictation gives no content hints. After class, allow thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for notes and exits. Record each component as shown, shown with help, or not yet seen; access alone is not help. Return on day 45 with one written cue.

### Other rooms

No screen: point at printed sources. No printer: copy today's selections and exact stems separately, preserving covered exits.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy today's sources and questions. Use scrap paper for labels.

**Skip.** Class posting and public turns.

- **10 min. Sources.** Read only day 44 selections together. Review who made each source.
- **8 min. Consent.** Use [[s04-K44-1]].
  **Say.** “Must everyone like the result?” Expect no; choosing a process differs from liking its result.
- **10 min. Questions.** Your child completes [[s04-P44]] and [[s04-P44-2]]. Discuss possible evidence without answering the inquiry.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X44a]] and [[s04-X44b]]. Your child answers alone. Keep the earlier work for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce posting, keeping individual questions and exit time. Longer: group questions needing different evidence.

### Words and supports

Review petition: a written request to officials. Equality here extends equal votes to equal human standing. Offer large print, neutral reading, and private dictation. No personal reports or reenactment.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 reads the Declaration's argument. If many tickets show error W, give an audience cue; error A needs promise/action separation. Finance repair from day 43 takes priority. Deferred adult repair reaches day 46.
