# Day 45: The Declaration's argument

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare days 43 and 44 papers with one private cue each. Cover today's ticket and student-page Exit box. Keep teacher-key.md separate. Lay out counters and made-up supply labels. Keep all later finance selections hidden.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 43's retries and readiness, day 44 practice notes, s04-P44, s04-K44-1, s04-P44-2, s04-X44a and s04-X44b. Review protected repair: five-minute model, five-minute fresh response. Prioritize money/price and loan/interest from day 43. Review grade 3 money as an accepted exchange medium, price as the amount asked, loan as borrowed money repaid, interest as payment for using money.

Announce invented exchanges. **Ask.** [[s04-K45-1]] Then [[steps:s04-K45-1]]. Move counters between separate price and borrowing labels. Children answer [[s04-P45-1]] independently. Give day 44 error W an audience cue; error A a promise/action cue. Defer their adult repair to day 46; preserve other unit 3 needs.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Read day 45's separate teaching summaries, then the corresponding original Declaration phrases aloud. Congress addresses the world in July 1776. Grievances are Congress's accusations, not neutral findings. Label its premise, governments protect rights, separately from its conclusion, repeated abuses justify separation.

Teach natural rights as inherent human rights; rulers do not create them. Legal rights are protections established in law. Rule of law binds officials, too. Review equality as equal human standing and consent as agreement about decision-making. Representative houses concern lawmaking; armies without legislative consent concern limits on power. Do not introduce today's held judicial example. Religious wording expresses the makers' belief; students need not share it.

#### 12 min. Source annotations

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Review equal year gaps; label today's Congress date on the timeline strip. **Ask.** [[s04-K45-2]] Let children mark before [[steps:s04-K45-2]]. Then [[s04-P45]]. Fifteen pairs each take two concurrent 45-second turns, one each. Children may pass publicly. Collect all individual annotations. If many confuse principles and accusations, label their subjects separately; do not compose the exit's connected explanation.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s04-X45a]] on the half slip and [[s04-X45b]] in the student-page Exit box together. Six minutes independent paper; two minutes to collect slips and pages. Read neutrally or transcribe private dictation. After class, thirty times 60 seconds, thirty minutes, covers all papers. Review the component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. Return every paper on day 46.

### Other rooms

No screen: use printed sources. No printer: copy today's exact stems and selections. Counters can be paper squares.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use paper squares. No printer: copy only today's selections and stems.

**Skip.** Public pair turns.

- **10 min. Feedback.** Return earlier work. Use [[s04-K45-1]], then [[s04-P45-1]].
  **Say.** “What is added for borrowing?” Expect two tokens in the model, three in practice.
- **10 min. Read.** Read the teaching selections. Explain each principle separately.
- **10 min. Annotate.** Complete [[s04-K45-2]] and [[s04-P45]].
  **Say.** “Which statement gives government's purpose?” Expect protecting rights.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X45a]] on the slip and [[s04-X45b]] in the covered Exit box. Your child writes independently. Save both for tomorrow.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce pair reporting. Keep repair, teaching and exit. Longer: annotate another separate principle.

### Words and supports

Review petition and consent as needed. Review grievance: a complaint listed as a reason for acting, grade 3 day 126. Provide large print, neutral reading and private dictation. Never seek personal financial information.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 examines denied rights. If many day 45 tickets show error B, separate inherent rights from enacted protections; error J needs legal limits on officials; error A needs claim/outcome separation. Deferred day 44 error W shares one priority repair slot.
