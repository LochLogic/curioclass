# Section 4.1 teaching selections

## Day 44: hook and labels

Declaration, Congress, July 1776: people have equal human standing and rights government should protect.
Hall petition, January 1777: Black petitioners ask Massachusetts lawmakers to restore their freedom.
These are original summaries, not quotations. Review this earlier petition only as a hook.

Credits: s04-T-decl; s04-T-hall. Primary originals are public domain; renderings are ours.

## Day 45: separate premises

Congress says governments protect rights and receive legitimate power through consent.
Congress accuses the king of dissolving representative houses.
Another grievance accuses him of keeping armies without lawmakers' consent.
Mark each principle or accusation separately. Keep judicial dependence and jury trials covered.

Credit: s04-T-decl. Public-domain original; rendering ours.

## Day 46: separate actions

Hall's petitioners ask lawmakers for freedom; the request alone does not establish success.
Enslaved people were forced to work and denied freedom.
The Onondaga Nation's timeline reports Haudenosaunee agreement to neutrality in 1775.
This is the Nation's present historical account, not an eyewitness diary.
