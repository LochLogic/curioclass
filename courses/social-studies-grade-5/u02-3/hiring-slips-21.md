# Separate evidence slips, day 21
Original summaries. Keep craft, payment, and legal change separate.

## Craft and recipient, May 1795 payroll
Peter, Ben, Harry, and Daniel appear on a carpenter payroll.
Their enslaver James Hoban signed for their wages.
The record does not establish that they kept a share.

## Negotiated earnings, Smith, 1798, chapter 2
Smith hired himself out with permission and paid part of his earnings to his enslaver.
He describes a separate agreement concerning his freedom.
An earnings arrangement alone was not legal freedom.
