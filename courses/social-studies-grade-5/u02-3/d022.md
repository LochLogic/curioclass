# Day 22: Requests and legal changes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use only Smith's panel of unit 2 hiring slips. Print selected unit 2 procedure slips on today's page. Keep Side 2 covered. Print unit 2 private exit 22; separate R22 from unit 2 section 3 fresh checks. Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys. Use the existing unit 2 timeline and source prompts.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 21's exit, R21 and both practice notes. If many tickets showed error F, explain that an earnings arrangement is not legal release, using Smith's separate agreement. For error M, identify yesterday's explicit brickmaking payment recipient; error Q needs its mold detail. Explain one priority for five minutes. Reveal [[s02-R22]] for five minutes alone, without assuming its missing recipient. Collect; retain unresolved needs.

#### 12 min. Explain procedures

Review indenture: bound service for a term, from day 11. Explain its stated endpoint independently. Review hereditary: passing from parent to child, day 16. A fixed service endpoint is not hereditary bondage.

Teach manumission: an enslaver legally grants freedom. Follow Flora's 1772 deed, identifying the issuer before showing [[s02-P22-1]]. The deed, not payment alone, records release. Follow Quock Walker's freedom suit separately: he sought a legal judgment from courts, with cases from 1781 to 1783.

Review Elizabeth Freeman's 1781 jury decision from grade 3. Review Prince Hall's 1777 petition from grade 3: he asked lawmakers to act. Petition means a written request to officials. These earlier cases are reminders only.

#### 10 min. Process notes

Students annotate Flora's document first, then [[s02-P22-2]] beside Walker's summary. **Ask.** Read the questions before discussing. Expect: the enslaver issued the deed; courts decided Walker's claim. Pairs take 45 seconds each way, simultaneously. Add Flora and Walker dates to the timeline. Review: equal gaps represent equal years. Use the supplied maker, date, audience and purpose prompts. Read documents separately, without a four-record sort. Error N needs the institution's action; error G needs the endpoint.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First distribute [[s02-X22a]] and [[s02-X22b]] on one half slip. Reveal Side 2's Exit box [[s02-X22c]] simultaneously. Keep the four records visible for all three responses. Students write alone for six minutes; collect in two. Mark after class: 30 times 60 seconds, thirty minutes for exits and notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen, access separately. Return all on day 23.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner discussion, preserving individual notes. Longer: distinguish the maker of a document from its recipient.

### Words and supports

Grant: a legal release here. Court: an institution deciding legal cases. Legislature: lawmakers. Allow passing, a pause, large print and transcribed dictation. No role-play or personal disclosure.
