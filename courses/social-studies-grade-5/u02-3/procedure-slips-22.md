# One procedure at a time, day 22
Original summaries, not quotations.

## Fixed-term service
Indenture: bound service for a term. A stated term has an endpoint.
Hereditary slavery has no agreed service endpoint.

## Flora, 1772
Anthony carried a payment to Flora's enslaver. A deed legally released Flora.
The deed, not money alone, recorded manumission.

## Walker, 1781 to 1783
Walker brought a freedom suit. Courts heard claims and ruled on legal status.
Elizabeth Freeman also won a freedom suit; recall her as a reminder only.

## Hall, 1777, reminder only
Prince Hall petitioned the legislature. A request to lawmakers is not a court judgment.
