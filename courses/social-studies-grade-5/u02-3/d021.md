# Day 21: Skill, payment and legal freedom

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use unit 2 action cards for the escape symbol only. On unit 2 hiring slips, cover the payroll panel; use Smith's panel only. Print today's page and unit 2 private exit 21. Cut R21 from unit 2 section 3 fresh checks. Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys; today's data replace its old practice answers.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 20's paper privately: `s02-X20a`, `s02-X20b`, `s02-R20`, `s02-P20-1`, `s02-P20-2`. If many tickets showed error E, explain that escaping challenges denied control. For error L, use the escape symbol to show resistance without revolt; error B needs forced versus chosen movement. Review resistance: action against denied freedom. Teach one priority for five minutes; then reveal [[s02-R21]] for five minutes alone. Collect all papers; retain other gaps.

#### 10 min. Explain hiring

Teach artisan: a skilled craftsperson. Hired out means an enslaver sends someone to work for another. A work arrangement does not itself grant freedom. Revisit Venture Smith as a reminder from day 15. Read the original summary on Side 1, based on his 1798 account for readers of his life. Explain negotiated earnings and a separate freedom agreement, without acting them out.

Introduce the made-up brickmaking fragments. Model one arrow from mold-shaped bricks to craft knowledge. Leave payment and legal change separate; do not model a complete hiring explanation.

#### 12 min. Separate evidence

Pairs answer [[s02-P21-1]] and [[s02-P21-2]] separately. **Ask.** Read each question before discussing its detail. Expect: the enslaver receives payment; shaping bricks supplies skill evidence. Fifteen pairs take 45 seconds each way. Within five minutes hear six optional 45-second observations maximum. Record names and collect every child's notes. Error M needs the named recipient; error Q needs the craft detail. Add skill and payment labels separately to the carried labor map. Review legend: the map key.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X21]] only now. Read its made-up record neutrally. Students write alone for six minutes, reading included; collect in two. After class, allow 30 children times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes, to mark exit and notes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access separately. Return all work on day 22.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to the page. No printer: copy today's selected text and private checks. Transcribe dictation onto paper. Never show the covered payroll panel.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or display: copy Side 1 and keep the exit separate.

**Skip.** Public observations and class grouping.

- **10 min. Return.** Explain one need from yesterday. Then give [[s02-R21]] alone.
- **10 min. Hiring.** Read Smith's summary. Explain that permission to earn did not itself grant freedom.
- **12 min. Details.** Use [[s02-P21-1]] and [[s02-P21-2]]. Point to each separate fragment.
  **Say.** "Who receives this payment?" Expect: the enslaver named on the receipt.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X21]]. Read neutrally if needed. Your child answers alone; collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing. Preserve repair and exit. Longer: explain why a craft detail cannot establish a legal change.

### Words and supports

Review petition: a written request to officials. Offer passing, a pause, enlarged print and transcribed dictation. Use neutral summaries; no personal-history questions or role-play.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 returns day 21 work. Error F confuses hiring with freedom; error M ignores payment control; error Q denies skill. Explain one priority for five minutes using separate fragments, then give unseen R22. Next, distinguish legal procedures and requests.
