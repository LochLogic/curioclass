# Day 23: Delayed freedom and free lives

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use unit 2 procedure slips for repair and unit 2 change slips as teacher references. Print today's page, covering Side 2; print unit 2 private exit 23. Separate R23 from unit 2 section 3 fresh checks. Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys; use today's narrower X23b accept rule. Keep the unit 2 timeline and source prompts.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 22's three exit responses, R22 and process notes. If many tickets showed error G, revisit fixed-term service's endpoint, separately from day 16's maternal-status clause. Review indenture: bound service for a term. For error N, trace Flora's deed issuer and operative release without a selected-subset exercise. Teach one priority for five minutes, then reveal [[s02-R23]] alone for five. Collect and retain other needs.

#### 12 min. Explain changes

Teach emancipation: release from slavery. Gradual emancipation delays some people's freedom. Read Pennsylvania's 1780 rule separately: children born to enslaved mothers after the act owed service until 28. Those already enslaved were not all released immediately. Ask [[s02-P23-1]] before pointing to the endpoint.

Next read the Massachusetts summary and [[s02-P23-3]]. Courts held slavery incompatible with the state constitution by 1783. This was a court route, not Pennsylvania's gradual statute. Add separate dated labels to the timeline. Review: equal gaps represent equal years. Do not compare two people's labor cases during teaching.

#### 10 min. Free-life evidence

Revisit the Vassall home purchase as a reminder from day 15. Read the 1787 summary, then [[s02-P23-2]]. **Ask.** Read the item before discussion. Expect: property, without a voting guarantee. Pairs take 45 seconds each way, simultaneously. Annotate the home-purchase detail separately from the discrimination statement. Teach discrimination: unfair treatment based on group identity. Review limit: what the evidence cannot show. Family bonds existed during enslavement; legal freedom changed protections, not people's capacity to love. Error O needs rights distinguished from equal treatment; error D needs bonds distinguished from legal security.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Side 2, [[s02-X23a]], and half slip [[s02-X23b]] for the first time. Keep the two made-up cases visible. Students answer alone for six minutes, reading included; collect in two. Mark exits and notes after class: 30 times 60 seconds, thirty minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. Return everything on day 24.
