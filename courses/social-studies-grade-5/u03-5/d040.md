# Day 40: Which details answer our question?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Bring every day 39 paper. Prepare evidence-sort.md through today's integrated panels. Keep the merchants and governors selections covered until the exit. Cut today's fresh-slips.md panel. Read teacher-only keys and record sheets; print X40 alone on a half slip. Use the reached teaching summaries, replacing the unverified recollection.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X39a`, `s03-X39b`, `s03-P39`, `s03-P39-2`, `s03-F39`.

If many day 39 tickets show error L or error O, review location, documented action and intention. For error F, recover the recorded action first. Choose one priority. Model [[s03-K40-1]] for five minutes, asking before [[steps:s03-K40-1]]. Say the case is invented. Everyone then answers the separate invented notice case [[s03-F40]] independently for five minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain other needs.

#### 10 min. Evidence gathering

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Review Congress and Parliament from day 27, Wheatley's contradiction passage from day 38. Read the three summaries, each separately attributed. Congress claims elected consent; Parliament claims lawmaking authority; Wheatley challenges liberty claims alongside oppression. These selections replace the unavailable later recollection. Do not describe a late recollection as an immediate record.

Review condition from day 34: a circumstance under which a claim holds. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Ask no child to judge their own conduct. Work on colonial resistance only.

#### 12 min. Detail selection

Use the three question labels. **Ask.** [[s03-P40]] Everyone independently selects and attributes one detail. Then fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way, explaining relevance and one limit. Expect a detail connected to its question, with uncertainty kept. The documents state positions; they do not establish everyone's agreement. Review corroboration: independent tellers' agreement supports only what they actually report. These three positions are not three eyewitness confirmations. Give every folder a written cue. No complete conditional argument is modeled or rehearsed.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the unseen merchants/governors pair. Give [[s03-X40]] alone on the half slip. All thirty write independently; allow neutral reading and transcribed dictation. Collect papers. After class: 30 times 75 seconds for exit components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, about 48 minutes. Review one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access support separately. Return all on day 41, Return and repair. Error E means an expressed unsupported or unconnected argument, not silence.

### Other rooms

No screen: use paper panels. No printer: copy panels and stems exactly, keeping the exit pair separate.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the teaching panels and keep exit sources covered.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.

- **10 min. Repair.** Read yesterday's cue, discuss the clock-location model, then answer [[s03-F40]].
- **10 min. Gather.** Read the Congress, Parliament and Wheatley teaching summaries separately.
- **12 min. Select.** Do [[s03-P40]].
  **Say.** "What can this detail not establish?" Expect a specific limit, such as everyone's agreement.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal today's merchants/governors pair. Your child answers [[s03-X40]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping independent selection and exit time. Longer: identify a different question needing another source.

### Words and supports

Review condition, reasoning, source-sourcing, corroboration and limit: what evidence cannot show. Give neutral reading, large print and dictation. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 returns every day 40 paper. If many show error E, connect one attributed detail to its claim in Return and repair. Then prepare separate evidence labels and revise one reasoning link.
