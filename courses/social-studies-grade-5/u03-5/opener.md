# Section 3.5 opener

Teacher only. All times are for a 40-minute day.
All thirty submit paper. Dictation is transcribed onto paper; it is not a spoken item.
Fifteen pairs talk simultaneously, 45 seconds each way. Public sharing may be passed.
Hear at most six public turns in five minutes; record names, never infer everyone was heard.
Written feedback reaches every child; no full-class oral-answer queue.
Use one scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Record access support separately.
No content prompt is needed for reading, language, large-print or motor access.
A blank response is not evidence of a misconception.
Return and repair holds one priority: five minutes model, five fresh response.
More than ten sharing a gap: teach together. Otherwise meet one group while others use written cues.
Keep other gaps recorded, with a later date; never promise all needs are repaired.
Say every made-up case is invented. No child plays a nation, enslaved person or historical figure.
No reenactments, graphic images, slurs, sacred activities or personal disclosures.
A child may pause and use the neutral printed summary without explaining why.

## Continuity

Use source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose.
Corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing.
Reasoning explains how a detail supports a claim. A cause-effect chain connects reasons, not only dates.
Charter means an official grant. Tax means money many people put in together for what everyone uses.
Historical taxes did not benefit everyone equally. Supply terms afresh when earlier learning is uncertain.
No earlier-grade or earlier-unit check case becomes a new check.
