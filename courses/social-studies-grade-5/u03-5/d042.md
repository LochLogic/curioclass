# Day 42: Use evidence independently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 private end assessment using today's compact panels instead of the earlier master. Print two single-sided pages; cut the Q1 panel before exposing later questions. Prepare M's blank sites separately from its reference. Keep all answer keys teacher-only. Use today's updated location and portrayal keys. Hold every day 41 paper for day 43. Cover classroom source models.

### The day

#### 38 min. End-of-unit assessment

Day 41's exit stays unreturned. No warm-up, model, source checklist, peer help or repair precedes this assessment of earlier written work. All thirty answer on paper independently. Neutral reading preserves wording; transcribe private dictation without suggesting content. No public answer queue. Give access support separately from content support; do not infer an error from an omission.

Give A/B and [[s03-Q1]] only, four minutes. Collect those notes before releasing comparison directions. Keep A/B visible afterward. Q1 samples spontaneous sourcing, including inferred audience and purpose; the child receives no component checklist.

Release the remaining panels in order: Q2 [[s03-Q2a]] [[s03-Q2b]], four minutes; Q3 [[s03-Q3a]] [[s03-Q3b]] [[s03-Q3c]], six; Q4 [[s03-Q4a]] [[s03-Q4b]], four; Q5 [[s03-Q5]], four; Q6 [[s03-Q6a]] [[s03-Q6b]], three; Q7 [[s03-Q7a]] [[s03-Q7b]], three; Q8 [[s03-Q8a]] [[s03-Q8b]], eight; Q9 [[s03-Q9a]] [[s03-Q9b]], two. Reading is included. Short labels suffice for matches; explanations may be brief sentences.

A/B concern Lexington; C supplies six policy clues; D has separate activity records. D4/D5 are a clearly labeled 1780 preview, outside the main chronology. R is a different Congress selection, first seen here. M is a modern text schematic using new sites, not the day 39 targets. Authentic 1770s-map evidence remains not yet seen. P describes a different Revere print, not the practiced shooting portrayal.

#### 2 min. Collection

Collect every page, note and source. No self-marking and no extra exit. After class allow four minutes per child for assessment components, plus one minute per child for held day 41 feedback: 150 minutes total, ours, untested. Review one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. Keep each policy, event and role separate. Record a priority cue for everyone, including omissions. Return day 41 work and day 42's assessment on day 43, Feedback.

### Other rooms

No devices are needed. No printer: copy today's exact panels, withholding later directions until Q1 notes are collected.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy panels separately; cover all later questions.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public answer discussion.

- **38 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Give A/B and Q1 first. Collect notes after four minutes. Then follow Q2 to Q9 in classroom order, with the same sources and minutes. Read neutrally; your child answers independently. Give no source checklist or content hints.
- **2 min. Collection.** Gather all papers and source panels. Keep feedback for day 43. Use the separate teacher-only answer key afterward.

### Shorter or longer

Keep the independent window. If access needs require longer, record the extra time and resume privately without instruction.

### Words and supports

No content gloss or source prompt precedes Q1. Neutral reading, large print and private transcribed dictation preserve decisions. No child reports personal experiences.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 returns day 42's assessment and held day 41 papers. Select one priority per child from components, then use distinct repair models and a fresh retry. Money, price, loan and interest readiness comes last, without prior teaching.
