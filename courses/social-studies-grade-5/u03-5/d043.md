# Day 43: Respond, then look ahead

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare marked day 42 papers and held day 41 work, with one priority cue per child. Select at most four meeting priorities using repair-guide.md and today's model rows. Prepare unit 3 private priority retry cards, one assigned branch per child. Print stems from today's rows. Use shared case text except A, whose complete replacement row supplies its case. Keep retries and unit 3 next-unit readiness covered. Read teacher-only keys. Do not print the whole retry bank for anyone.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 42's assessment and all day 41 papers: `s03-X41`, `s03-P41`, `s03-P41-2`, `s03-F41`. Review the component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. Children privately read one priority cue. Keep other gaps recorded. If many day 41 or day 42 papers show error E, prioritize a detail-to-claim link. Blanks are unobserved components, not diagnosed errors.

#### 20 min. Response groups

Review protected repair and source-sourcing, reasoning and evidence limits. Hold at most four sequential five-minute meetings: two minutes for one isolated model, three for at most four single 45-second observations. Record names; no claim all thirty were heard. More than ten sharing a gap: use the same priority model together, then independent written responses. Other children use written cues on returned work; this is practice, never a new score from the same assessment case.

Choose models for observed needs only:
- Source clues, error C: [[s03-K43-C]]. Agreement/access, error D, error M or error R: [[s03-K43-D]]. Selected portrayal, error K: [[s03-K43-K]].
- Reasoning, error E: [[s03-K43-E]]. Reader use or interpretations, error U or error V: [[s03-K43-U]].
- Causal gaps, error A, error B, error H or error T: [[s03-K43-A]]. Political voice, error G, error I or error P: [[s03-K43-G]]. Local outcomes, error J, error Q or error S: [[s03-K43-J]].
- Agency and motive, error F, error N, error L or error O: [[s03-K43-F]].

Model fragments are invented and announced as such. Correct historical gaps from the returned factual key, one at a time; use separate named Lenape and Oneida evidence rather than one allegiance. Keep new retry cases hidden. Preserve unresolved components for day 45.

#### 5 min. Fresh retry

Each child answers just one new branch independently: [[s03-F43-C]], [[s03-F43-D]], [[s03-F43-E]], [[s03-F43-A]], [[s03-F43-G]], [[s03-F43-J]], [[s03-F43-F]], [[s03-F43-K]] or [[s03-F43-U]]. Give the matching case panel and response space, not this bank list. Collect all thirty papers. Dictation is transcribed as paper. Use the same component scale.

#### 10 min. Next readiness

Give [[s03-R43a]] and [[s03-R43b]] with neutral reading and independent paper responses. No instruction or explanation precedes collection. Review of earlier learning is being sampled, not presumed retained. Record money, price, loan and interest separately; saving was practiced in grade 4. No additional exit. After class: 30 times 60 seconds for retry and readiness, 30 minutes. Return all on day 45, Return and repair, within one ten-minute priority slot.

### Other rooms

No screen is needed. No printer: copy only assigned branch and readiness stems exactly, keeping cases separate.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy your child's assigned retry and the readiness questions separately.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public observations.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read the priority cue on returned days 41 and 42 work.
- **20 min. Response.** Discuss one different model for five minutes. Your child works with written cues afterward.
  **Say.** "What does your evidence leave unknown?" Expect the specific boundary in the selected model's answer key.
- **5 min. Retry.** Reveal only the assigned fresh case. Your child answers alone.
- **10 min. Next readiness.** Give [[s03-R43a]] and [[s03-R43b]] alone, without explanation before collection.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce public observations, keeping independent retry and the final ten minutes. Longer: revisit an unresolved component after collection.

### Words and supports

Review source-sourcing, reasoning and evidence limits only as needed. Public talk may be passed. Offer large print, neutral reading and transcribed dictation. No personal financial disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 opens the Declaration's promise question and retrieves founding ideas. Keep unresolved unit 3 needs recorded. Day 45 Return and repair receives today's retry and readiness papers, prioritizing money, price, loan or interest within one ten-minute slot.
