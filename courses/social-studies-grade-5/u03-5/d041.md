# Day 41: Make each evidence link clear

## Teacher page

### Before class

Bring every day 40 paper. Prepare closing-display.md through today's integrated panels, with blank labels and arrows. Keep the new two-city exit pair covered. Cut today's fresh-slips.md panel and X41 half slip. Read teacher-only keys. Reserve a folder for all displays and exit arguments, returned on day 43.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X40`, `s03-P40`, `s03-F40`.

If many day 40 tickets show error E, review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Model five minutes with the invented bench request [[s03-K41-1]], asking before [[steps:s03-K41-1]]. Everyone answers the different reading-room case [[s03-F41]] independently for five minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise meet one group while others use written cues. Keep other gaps open.

#### 10 min. Closing display

Review condition from day 34: a circumstance under which a historical judgment may hold. Review the claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Today use separate labels and arrows, as on day 35; do not supply a completed argument or writing frame.

Read the three teaching summaries. **Ask.** [[s03-P41-2]] Children work alone. Each adds one reasoning arrow between a selected detail and a possible claim label. The Congress detail concerns elected consent; Parliament asserts broad authority; Wheatley exposes a conflict within freedom claims. Retain attribution and uncertainty. These are positions, not proof that everyone agreed.

#### 12 min. Review and revise

Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way. One partner points to an unsupported leap; each may pass aloud and submit a written observation. **Ask.** [[s03-P41]] Expect a specific connection, not a copied source sentence. Children revise one separate label or arrow independently and leave the others visible. Collect all displays as practice for written cues. No final-argument score comes from this rehearsed display.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the unseen two-city source pair. Give [[s03-X41]] alone on the half slip. All thirty write a short argument independently, attributing both sources and connecting their details to a condition. Neutral reading and transcribed dictation preserve paper evidence. Collect all work. After class allow 30 times 90 seconds for exit and practice/retry, 45 minutes. Review the component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; support separately. Hold all day 41 feedback until day 43, leaving day 42 independent.

### Other rooms

No screen: point to paper summaries. No printer: copy the separate labels and sources. Keep the exit pair covered.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy panels and draw blank labels and arrows.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.

- **10 min. Repair.** Read yesterday's cue, discuss the bench model, then do [[s03-F41]].
- **10 min. Display.** Read the three teaching summaries. Do [[s03-P41-2]] on separate labels.
- **12 min. Revise.** Your child explains one arrow, then does [[s03-P41]].
  **Say.** "Why does this detail support your label?" Expect a connection, such as elected consent supporting political voice.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal today's two-city pair. Your child answers [[s03-X41]] alone. Hold feedback until day 43.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping revision and the exit. Longer: add an evidence limit to one separate label.

### Words and supports

Review condition, reasoning, claim sheet and limit. No writing frame today. Offer neutral reading, large print and dictation. Discuss historical choices, never personal conduct.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 independently assesses unit 3 using unseen sources. No day 41 handback, repair, example or answer discussion precedes it. Error E and every other day 41 need wait for day 43 Feedback and Response groups.
