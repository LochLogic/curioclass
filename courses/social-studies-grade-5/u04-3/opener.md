# Section 4.3: A union under strain

## Purpose and teaching

Days 52 to 55, forty minutes daily. Congress adopted the Articles in 1777; final ratification was 1781. A working government can have limited powers. Separate ordinary decisions, nine-state decisions, and unanimous amendments. The Ordinance shows capacity and contested claims over inhabited land. Inflation and deflation concern sustained general price movements. Seeking and availability distinguish unemployment from mere joblessness. Teach debt pressure without inventing a national historical price series. Several pressures, relief choices, and existing reform efforts matter in explaining unrest.

## Organization

Every day: repair ten, teaching ten, separate notes twelve, exit eight. Day 54 protects two minutes for R54, four for priority review, four for fresh work. The five-card exit is held; the debt response is a covered Exit box. Practice uses isolated trends and calculations, never the held sequential family case.

Teaching selections and recording labels are in this folder. Print only today's teaching block.

Use half-sheet exits; never make an item spoken to save space. Only day 54 has an additional covered Exit box.
Every child submits individual paper. Do not display held answers during teaching or repair.

## Continuity and access

Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Review perspective: two sides of one event.
Review map-scale: source and response calibration match. Modern boundaries orient geography, not eighteenth-century borders.
Review corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing.
Modern summaries do not become independent eyewitnesses merely by agreeing.
Earlier grade 5 drafts supply directions, not assumed learning. Reteach explicitly when evidence is absent.
Allow public passing, neutral reading, large print, pointing and private dictation without content cues.
Never ask about home, money, bodies, religion or immigration status. No historical role-play or sacred material.
Name continuing Native nations specifically. Enslaved people were forced and denied freedom.
For sensitive passages, offer an explicitly made-up public-rule comparison with the same evidence skill.
Record historical content as not yet seen when the alternative does not sample it.
