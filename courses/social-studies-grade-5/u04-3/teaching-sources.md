# Section 4.3 teaching selections

## Day 52: separate clauses

The Articles joined states for common defense while preserving substantial state power.
Congress approved them in 1777; all states completed ratification in 1781.
The Northwest Ordinance of 1787 established territorial government and a route to statehood.
US territorial claims concerned Native homelands, not empty land.
Article III promised respect for Native property; that promise did not establish actual consent.
Article VI prohibited slavery but permitted forced return of people claimed as fugitives.
Keep power, date, and retained authority on separate labels.

## Day 53: powers and voting rules

Congress requested state contributions. State legislatures collected taxes.
Congress lacked a direct tax on residents and broad control over trade.
Ordinary decisions required a majority; specified major decisions required nine states.
An amendment required agreement from every state.
Model only the amendment barrier, not a failed funding case.

Credit: s04-T-articles. Public-domain original; rendering ours.

## Day 54: invented mechanisms, separate records

Inflation: a sustained general rise in prices. Deflation: a sustained general fall.
Unemployment: an available person seeks paid work and cannot find it.
Made-up savings model: thirty tokens buy ten baskets priced at three tokens each.
At five tokens each, the same savings buy six baskets.
Separate receipt model: earnings fall from twenty to fifteen; the debt stays seven.
These quantities are invented, not historical measurements.
Historical context: postwar borrowers faced debt demands and scarce cash.
This does not establish a national price trend from one crop's price.

Credits: s04-T-prices; s04-T-jobs; s04-T-unrest; s04-T-cash. Facts paraphrased; figures ours.

## Day 55: separate historical notes

Massachusetts demanded taxes and debt payments amid scarce cash.
Some people faced seizure of property.
Petitions brought little relief; armed groups closed courts in 1786.
State forces suppressed the uprising in 1787.
Reform discussions already existed; unrest added pressure without making a Convention inevitable.
Keep actors, demands, and dates on separate notes, not a completed causal strip.

Made-up contingency model: a council postpones a bridge vote for a missing inspection.
It could seek another inspection; postponement alone does not determine its final choice.
