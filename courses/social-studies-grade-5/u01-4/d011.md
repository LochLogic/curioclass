# Day 11: Work, payment and freedom

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-exits.md and generated keys. Cover the half-slip. Print the source summaries once in the student frame. Prepare the labeled atlas with a transparent paper overlay: one band for crops, another for people's labor status. Use an open circle for a documented enslavement example, never quantity. Sort day 10's whole batch, including logs and repair paper.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 10's batch. Yesterday's map and growing conditions lead to human arrangements of work. For errors F/N, spend five minutes reading the atlas date and neighboring labels, then five on [[s01-R11a]]. For errors O/J, use the same five-plus-five split with [[s01-R11b]]. Climate affects crops; people made coercive choices. Choose one priority, whole class if more than ten miss. Others use written cues, not another meeting.

#### 10 min. Explain arrangements

Review hired: chosen to do a job. Extend this to wage employment, work for pay. Review forced labor: work people could not refuse. Review forced and chosen: people forced to work and not paid are named enslaved plainly.

Teach family work: work within a family. Teach indenture: bound service for a term. Read the Maryland fragment; some people did not choose these contracts. A stated endpoint distinguishes term service from slavery. Payment alone cannot establish freedom to leave. Never act out either arrangement.

Read the Cambridge record separately. Put its open circle on the teacher's Massachusetts overlay. Review legend: the map key, and region: places grouped by location and shared features. This northern record prevents a Southern-only account. It does not count everyone enslaved.

#### 9 min. Constraint annotations

Fifteen pairs read one fragment at a time, one 45-second turn each. Model finding a date in the Cambridge record without classifying four records. Each child annotates [[s01-P11]] independently. Keep source, location and constraint notes separate. Add the Maryland term-service note to the teacher-held overlay; retain original region bands. Neither symbols nor crops explain why slavery existed.

#### 5 min. Evidence collection

Hear six rotating observations of the printed term-service constraint, one 45-second ask each. Anyone may pass. Collect everyone's annotation, recording support separately. Keep the overlay; no additional spoken judgment is owed to the other twenty-four.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s01-X11a]] and [[s01-X11b]]. Say these records are made up. Allow one minute reading, four answering both, one collecting. Read neutrally or transcribe dictation. After class, thirty times 30 seconds gives fifteen minutes for tickets; allow ten more for annotations. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen per component. Blank work does not establish error G or K.

### Other rooms

Draw the overlay on an atlas copy. Hand-copy short source summaries with their makers; no live page is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy fragments and tasks. No overlay: add a separate labor band on paper.

**Skip.** Public observations and the class return.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the four separate fragments together. Explain each work arrangement.
  **Say.** "What limits term service?" Expect: a stated service period; leaving was constrained.
- **9 min. Annotate.** Take the partner turn, then your child completes [[s01-P11]].
- **5 min. Map.** Find Cambridge's colony on the labeled atlas and add the labor symbol together.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** First reveal [[s01-X11a]] and [[s01-X11b]]. Say the records are made up. Your child answers alone. Collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public observations. Keep all four distinctions and both exit items. Longer: reread a source's limits without adding categories.

### Words and supports

Term means a stated service period. Constraint means a limit on what someone can do. Provide enlarged fragments, neutral reading and dictation. Keep people separate from goods.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 returns day 11's whole batch. Errors G/K receive one five-minute explanation and five-minute fresh response. Then apply map keys and assemble separate claim components from two sources.
