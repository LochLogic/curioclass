# Day 13: Use new sources independently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-assessment.md, key-maps.md and generated keys. Cover every packet section. Remove practice displays; retain only each student's day 8 Penn findings, returned on day 9. Use assessment.md for ordering, not duplicate printing. The source sheet supplies all six cards, including the map and name bank. Sort X12 by one priority. Keep day 12 displays aside for tomorrow.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 12's exit and repair papers. Give one private cue for a key/source need, two minutes. Use [[s01-R13-1]] for three minutes. This supplied-symbol selection never constructs a key or previews check materials. Other gaps wait for day 14. Review legend: the map key.

#### 1 min. Directions

Distribute covered source sheets and response pages, with retained Penn findings. Explain neutral reading and dictation choices. Sources are summaries, not quotations. The town in the final item is made up. No presentations or peer marking.

#### 34 min. End-of-unit assessment

Reveal each portion at its own turn. Six minutes: [[s01-Q1a]], [[s01-Q1b]], [[s01-Q1c]], disease, attributed land practices and one war grievance. Four minutes: [[s01-Q2a]], [[s01-Q2b]], founding aims and research checked against new evidence. Three minutes: [[s01-Q3]], institutions and influence without ranking.

Twelve minutes: [[s01-Q4a]], [[s01-Q4b]], [[s01-Q4c]], [[s01-Q4d]]. Allocate five locating, four climate links, two labor meanings, one northern symbol. One minute: [[s01-Q5]], relevant evidence selection. Six minutes: [[s01-Q6]], claim, two attributed details and reasoning in a paragraph. Two minutes: [[s01-Q7]], two people's stakes. Collect portions within these allocations. Q5/Q6 share evidence, with separate judgments. Neutral reading fits inside each allocation; give no content clues. Accept exact dictation and equivalent labeled drawings where the same decision is shown.

After class, allow four minutes per child, 120 minutes for thirty, plus five minutes for repair papers. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, or not yet seen per component. Record access and content support separately. Blank work diagnoses no error. Select one priority for day 14; retain secondary needs.

### Other rooms

Use identical printed source summaries and an enlarged map. An adult reads neutrally and collects answers before opening keys.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve every assessment component and its time. Use access supports within the allocation. Finish feedback tomorrow.

### Words and supports

Review claim, evidence and reasoning in earlier words only before packets open. Offer neutral reading, enlarged map, movable labels or dictation. Do not judge reading speed or handwriting.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 returns day 13's assessment and repair paper, plus day 12's displays. Assign one priority, explain with different teaching fragments, then one fresh retry. The final ten minutes hold unit 2 readiness; route ideas may be new.
