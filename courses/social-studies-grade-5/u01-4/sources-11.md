# Sources and work, day 11

## Separate work fragments

Family: relatives worked small farms in the colonies.
Wage: Philadelphia craft journeymen earned wages.
Term service: Maryland records describe people bound to work for limited terms.
Not every term was freely chosen.
Enslavement: records document people forced to work in Cambridge, Massachusetts, in 1774.
People were forced, not goods or ordinary hired employees.
Family work: work within a family.
Hired: chosen to do a job; wage employment adds payment here.
Indenture: bound service for a term.
Forced labor: work people could not refuse.
Slavery did not provide the stated endpoint of term service.
