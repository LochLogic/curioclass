# Day 12: A sourced map and separate claim parts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-exits.md and generated keys; hide the inset and ticket. Withdraw completed claim prose. Keep the prepared atlas and teacher's labor overlay visible. Use sources-12.md summaries once below; map-display.md and claim-components.md provide the component layout. Sort day 11's entire batch by one priority. Prepare a paper strip with four equal bars, each ten imagined miles.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 11's tickets, constraint notes and repair paper. For error K, explain term limits and coercion five minutes, then [[s01-R12a]] five minutes. For error G, revisit the documented northern place five minutes, then [[s01-R12b]] five minutes. Each answers one assigned response. Whole-class priority means more than ten misses; others receive written cues. Review forced labor: work people could not refuse.

#### 5 min. Reopen references

Yesterday's labor distinctions join the crop map. Review legend: the map key. Review the scale-strip model: count equal ground-distance bars. Use the prepared regional labels and crop/labor symbols. Lay four equal bars along the invented route, forty imagined miles; this is a demonstration, not a geographic distance. Never measure the approximate atlas. Read the two Providence summaries without a completed argument model.

#### 10 min. Map display

All complete [[s01-P12a]] on the prepared labeled reference. Trace region bands; add one source note beside each separate crop/labor band, using the retained notes. Interpret the supplied key without constructing an empty key. Fifteen pairs confer simultaneously, 45 seconds each way, then annotate individually. Keep all colony names visible; do not practice full blank-outline labeling. Review region and climate in yesterday's words.

#### 9 min. Claim display

Review claim: what we say; evidence: what we saw or read that shows it. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Review the claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit.

Students complete [[s01-P12b]] and [[s01-P12c]] independently: banner, separate details and reasoning arrows. No paired underlining or integrated paragraph. Sachems are leaders; retain each source's description. Land-use rights and a land transaction are not identical wording. Hear six rotating 45-second observations during writing. Anyone may pass. Collect every display for unscored written feedback on day 14.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s01-X12]] and its New Jersey inset. Allow one minute reading, four drawing and labeling, one collecting. No key is shown. Accept distinct legible symbols, keeping people separate from crops. After class, thirty times 20 seconds gives ten minutes marking tickets; allow fifteen for display feedback. Record shown, shown with help, or not yet seen for ticket components; support separately. Give no display points.

### Other rooms

Use printed summaries if live pages fail. Hand-copy the labeled atlas and component spaces; the adult reads neutrally.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the source summaries, labeled map and component spaces. Fold paper for equal bars.

**Skip.** Public observations and the class return.

- **5 min. Reopen.** Read both Providence summaries. Count the demonstration bars together.
- **10 min. Map.** Your child completes [[s01-P12a]]. Take the partner turn.
  **Say.** "What can the supplied symbols show?" Expect: presence, without quantities.
- **9 min. Claim.** Use [[s01-P12b]] and [[s01-P12c]]. Keep the banner, details and arrows separate.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** First reveal [[s01-X12]] and the inset. Your child answers alone. Collect before discussing. Keep the display for later written feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public observations. Keep both sources and exit. Longer: add a source limit beside a reasoning arrow.

### Words and supports

Use the review definitions above. Offer neutral reading, large labels, movable symbols or exact dictation. Withdraw complete claim prose for everyone.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 receives day 12's exit and repair papers in five-minute Return and repair. Give one key/source cue, then new independent assessment sources. Return map and claim displays with written feedback on day 14.
