# Day 14: Repair one gap, then look ahead

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate retry/readiness keys and generated keys. Select one priority per child from day 13, reserving its retry unseen. Cut apart retries.md; Copy only R14i's stem onto a blank card; keep its generated key separate. For R14e, copy the dated neighboring footprints and give Delaware the wrong Pennsylvania label. Prepare at most four groups and written moves for others. Keep readiness covered. Return displays with written cues only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 13's assessment and s01-R13-1 paper, plus day 12's s01-P12a, s01-P12b and s01-P12c displays. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen per component; support separately. Name one priority privately. Review claim, evidence and reasoning in earlier words. Display feedback remains unscored. Blank work needs another opportunity, not an error diagnosis.

#### 20 min. Return and repair groups

Meet four groups maximum, five minutes each. Others follow written cues and revise their own returned work. No child waits for a public turn. Teaching annotations stay with students on their returned papers. Use fresh extraction fragments, then revisit the child's relevant component; never preview its retry.

Group one uses [[s01-P14-1]]: review living nations and specific source attribution. For shared-use needs, point to the returned land response and separate use from authority. Group two uses [[s01-P14-2]]: distinct rights; for influence, point to repeated institutions without ranking peoples. Group three uses [[s01-P14-3]]: climate alongside choices. Use labeled New York/New Jersey neighbors for location, or the day 11 term-service constraint for labor. Group four uses [[s01-P14-4]]: a relevant detail supports a claim. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Keep clues separate, without a full argument model. Children revise their own papers, not a practiced retry answer.

#### 5 min. Fresh retry

Give each child just one unseen item. Use [[s01-R14a]] for continuing government; [[s01-R14b]] for conflict bands; [[s01-R14c]] for distinct rights; [[s01-R14d]] for influence; [[s01-R14e]] for neighboring labels; [[s01-R14f]] for climate/choices; [[s01-R14g]] for labor constraints; [[s01-R14h]] for relevant stakes; [[s01-R14i]] for shared use and linked evidence. Collect all. This samples one component; it cannot certify the whole standard or a complete historical argument.

#### 10 min. Unit 2 readiness

Say the route idea may be new; do not claim completed grade 3 teaching. Reveal [[s02-R1a]], [[s02-R1b]], [[s02-R2]] only now. Allow four minutes routes, two coercion, three harbor, one collection, with neutral reading included. The harbor is made up. Do not teach directions before this diagnostic. People were forced; goods are objects. Record routes, coercion and port needs without points.

After class, thirty times 15 seconds gives about eight minutes for retries; thirty times 30 seconds gives fifteen for readiness. Return retries privately in day 15 Open inquiry, one minute. Readiness returns in day 16 Route and harbor instruction, ten minutes. Retain unsampled and unresolved gaps.

### Other rooms

Hand-copy only the assigned retry and needed diagram. Printed sources replace live pages. The adult explains privately, then collects independent work before feedback.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy one assigned retry and the readiness diagrams.

**Skip.** Four class groups and public turns.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return yesterday's work and the map/claim display. Choose one need.
- **20 min. Repair.** Use one teaching fragment, then revise the matching returned response.
  **Say.** "Which detail belongs with this response?" Expect: relevant evidence, with its source named.
- **5 min. Retry.** Give just the assigned fresh retry from the separate bank. Collect before discussing. Retain other needs for later teaching.
- **10 min. Readiness.** First reveal [[s02-R1a]], [[s02-R1b]], [[s02-R2]]. Your child answers alone; the route idea may be new. Collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Keep the selected repair, independent retry and final readiness. Longer: reread written display feedback, without claiming every gap repaired.

### Words and supports

Review forced labor and reasoning in earlier words. Offer neutral reading, dictation or enlarged maps. An access support alone does not lower a content judgment.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 opens the inquiry about enslaved and free Africans shaping colonies and seeking freedom. Return the selected retry privately in Open inquiry. Day 16 receives readiness in Route and harbor instruction; teach the simplified network as new.
