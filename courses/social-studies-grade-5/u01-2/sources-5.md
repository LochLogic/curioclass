## A different conflict

King Philip's War began in 1675.
Metacom was a Wampanoag leader whom the English called King Philip.
His alliances sought to protect homelands.
Maker: National Museum of the American Indian, modern chronology.

John Easton recorded a meeting intended to prevent war in 1675.
He was an English official, not a Native author.
His report mediates the words he heard.
Maker: John Easton, A Relation, 1675, introductory meeting passage.
