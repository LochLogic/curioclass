## Evidence and repair

Each child answers on paper, including dictation.
Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; support is recorded separately.
Blank evidence does not establish an error letter.
Handbacks.csv routes every judged batch to a receiving part with minutes.
Return one priority in ten minutes: five for explanation and five for fresh evidence.
Other gaps get written cues. Day 13 has five minutes total for return.
More than ten misses among thirty calls for a whole-class priority; otherwise small groups.
Day 14 has four five-minute adult groups maximum, then one five-minute retry per child.
Do not claim every missed component is repaired.
Fifteen pairs work simultaneously, one 45-second turn each way.
Public discussion hears six rotating children maximum, one 45-second ask each.
Record names and collect paper from all thirty; other children need no later oral assessment.
