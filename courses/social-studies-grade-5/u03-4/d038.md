# Day 38: Women acted in different ways

## Teacher page

Prepare unit 3 action-inventory.md as separate panels. Use unit 3 map-access reference and separate outlines only for navigation directions; keep Boston target labels hidden. ### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X37a`, `s03-X37b`, `s03-P37`, `s03-F37`.

If many day 37 tickets show error Q or error S, choose one priority. Review the British hill victory or city/war distinction for five minutes. Use teaching notes, not today's travel log. Reveal the new made-up log for [[s03-F38]], five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain unresolved needs.

#### 10 min. Women's actions

Review boycott from day 32: organized refusal to buy. Teach homespun: cloth made locally in homes; supply: provide needed materials. Kariofyllis's study describes women refusing imports and Lexington spinning gatherings. Making substitutes could sustain refusals. Phillis Wheatley's 1774 public letter challenged demands for liberty alongside oppression of others. Writing could challenge understandings of freedom.

Say the teaching adults are made up. Nell keeps a stall trading and a farm producing. Tessa gathers equipment for soldiers. Vera pools donations. Explain separate possible effects: continued production, supplies, pooled resources. These illustrate management, army support and fundraising, not historical documents. Later wartime illustrations sit on an undated preview inset, beyond March 1776.

#### 12 min. Source inventory

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. **Ask.** [[s03-P38]] Children inventory one attributed action and limit. Contrast boycotts with the Wheat sisters' relocation to Halifax in the study. Their private motives remain uncertain. Error F erases actions; error N assumes all women shared an allegiance.

Review Adams's organizing letters from day 32, Revere's selected engraving and Attucks's uncertain motives from day 34. Later Black advocates used Attucks's memory in citizenship arguments; mark a separate later-history inset. Influence means how an action changes events or understandings. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way about one action; all write.

Use three minutes for navigation: draw a circle labeled pond beside a square labeled shed. Say the reference is invented. Draw the same unlabeled shapes separately; transfer both labels, then cover the reference. Review legend and source-date checks. Historic maps need inspected labels and orientation clues. Schematic transfer is practiced; authentic historic-map reading stays not yet seen. Keep tomorrow's Boston targets hidden.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the invented five-adult case with [[s03-X38a]] in the Exit box. Give [[s03-X38b]] alone on the half slip. All thirty write independently; accept neutral reading and transcribed dictation. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 70 seconds for both components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 45 minutes. Return day 39, Return and repair. Review the component scale; support separately.
