# Day 37: Fighting, siege and a city departure

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 route-record.md and unit 3 teaching route diagram. Keep the unit 3 private siege diagram hidden. Cut today's unit 3 section 4 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 4 private exits. Use today's covered Exit box and one-item half slip. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Bring day 36 papers. Keep the later-war inset separate from the main timeline.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X36`, `s03-P36`, `s03-F36`.

If many day 36 tickets show error A or error Q, select one priority. For five minutes review date order versus cause, or protest method versus military result. Use the separate teaching dates and action types, never today's fresh notice. Reveal the invented Monday/Thursday notice for [[s03-F37]], five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain unresolved needs.

#### 10 min. Early fighting

Keep yesterday's promise: fighting began at Lexington and Concord in April 1775. Britain won the Battle of Bunker Hill in June, with heavy losses. Review siege: surrounding a place to force surrender, from grade 4 day 69. Teach evacuation: leaving a place. Fortifying Dorchester Heights with cannon threatened British control of Boston and its shipping routes. British forces left Boston on March 17, 1776.

Teach from separate park readings, one event at a time. Put March 1776 at the main timeline's endpoint. A separate later-war inset says fighting continues elsewhere. Holding one city, winning one battle and ending a war are different outcomes. No reenactment, assigned sides or graphic detail.

#### 12 min. Route and record work

Review source records and date placement. Trace only the Boston, Lexington, Concord route on route-teach.svg; it is not to scale. **Ask.** [[s03-P37]] Children annotate one teaching event/date on a separate slip. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way; everyone writes. Do not combine all consequences with a map. If many show error Q, point to the costly British victory. For error S, distinguish Boston's endpoint from the later-war inset.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the private siege diagram and fresh dispatches only now. Uncover [[s03-X37a]] in the page's Exit box. Give [[s03-X37b]] alone on the half slip. All thirty write independently; neutral reading and transcribed dictation count as paper. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 70 seconds for both components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 45 minutes. Return day 38, Return and repair. Review the component scale; support separately.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping repair and independent evidence. Longer: identify evidence still needed for one answer.

### Words and supports

Review siege, source records and timeline placement. Evacuation means leaving a place. Fortify means strengthen for defense. Offer neutral reading, dictation and large print. Everyone may pass in public talk. No reenactment or personal disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 returns every day 37 paper. If many tickets show error Q or error S, repair one outcome or the city/war distinction, then a fresh geographic limit. Afterward examine women's different activities, without assuming shared private motives.
