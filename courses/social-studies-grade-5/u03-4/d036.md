# Day 36: Resistance across 1773 and 1774

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 resistance-strips.md as separate dated panels with source credits. Cut today's unit 3 section 4 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 4 private exits. Use the covered source panel and one-item half slip. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Bring every day 35 paper and the retained timeline. Keep the shuffled notes hidden.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X35a`, `s03-X35b`, `s03-P35`, `s03-F35`.

If many day 35 tickets show error D, error E, error M or error R, select one for five minutes. Review evidence limits, reasoning, usefulness or maker access. Model with a made-up cellar inspection: a report on one dry corner cannot establish every wall's condition. The report still informs that corner. Reveal the new stair case and [[s03-F36]] for five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Keep unresolved needs recorded.

#### 10 min. Resistance chronology

Move forward from 1766 to 1773 and 1774 on the timeline. Review boycott from day 32: organized refusal to buy. Review representation from day 31. The Tea Party destroyed tea in protest against British policy. Parliament responded with punitive laws restricting Boston and Massachusetts government. Suffolk delegates rejected these laws and urged boycott and defense. The First Continental Congress endorsed their resolves, extending cooperation. Endorsed means formally supported.

Teach one reading at a time: Parliament's history for destruction/punishment; Suffolk's delegates for their decisions; the park's Adams account for Congress's endorsement. Explain each local link orally, never complete a written multi-event chain. Review the cause-effect chain from day 29: a cause explains why; a date alone does not.

#### 12 min. Date and action work

Review timeline placement: equal gaps represent equal years. **Ask.** [[s03-P36]] Children place one named date/action tag at a time and annotate its source. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way; everyone writes. Keep tags separate, without the held note order or an integrated chain response. If many treat all actions as battles, point separately to a protest action and a delegates' decision. Politics and military outcomes differ.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover only today's shuffled notes and give [[s03-X36]] on the half slip. All thirty independently order and explain connections. Accept neutral reading and transcribed dictation. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 60 seconds for exits plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 40 minutes. Return day 37, Return and repair. Review the component scale; access support separately. A chain need not claim that war was inevitable.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping repair and independent evidence. Longer: identify evidence still needed for one answer.

### Words and supports

Review boycott, representation, timeline and cause-effect chain. Resolves are formal decisions; endorsed means formally supported. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. Everyone may pass in public talk. No reenactment, purchasing disclosures or assigned national roles.
