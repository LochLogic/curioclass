#### 12 min. Leader survey

Read eight separate profiles. Mark 1786 as a preview beyond the war. Apply the established action/effect skill to these new selections. Source prompts are withdrawn; children identify attribution independently.

Model [[s04-K51-team]] with [[steps:s04-K51-team]] after responses. Announce both teams are made up. Their shared project lets different actions have different effects; titles alone establish neither. Keep historical profiles separate, without modeling the held pair. Distinguish Franklin's diplomacy from Washington's army role orally, without composing their assessment comparison. Teach Lafayette's muskets only.

#### 10 min. Individual action arrows

Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Locate the dated 1786 preview beyond wartime entries. **Ask.** [[s04-P51]]. Children write four separate attributed action arrows, not a paired paragraph. No paired historical paragraph. Discuss current item-row look-fors after responses.

Fifteen pairs speak concurrently, each child taking one 45-second turn. Public passing is allowed. Collect all thirty individual notes. Keep source prompts withdrawn; neutral reading and private dictation provide access.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s04-X51]] on the independent half-sheet master. Allow six minutes for paper responses, two for collection. Neutral reading and private dictation give no content cues. After class: thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; fifteen more for notes and repairs. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately. Return everything on day 52.
