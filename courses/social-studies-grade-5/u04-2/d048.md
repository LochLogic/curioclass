# Day 48: Camp, siege, surrender, and peace

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare returned papers and one assigned fresh repair card per child from current item-row stems. Keep teacher-key.md and corrected current item-row keys separate from student copies. ### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 47's s04-X47a, s04-X47b, s04-P47, day 47 practice notes and s04-K47-A/s04-F47-A. If many day 47 tickets show error X, trace corrected state labels privately, then use [[s04-K48-X]] and [[s04-F48-X]]. For error Y, distinguish the dated consequences in feedback, then use [[s04-K48-Y]] and [[s04-F48-Y]]. Model one priority five minutes, revealing steps after responses; give its fresh response five.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Read each logistics note beside its labeled teaching map. Valley Forge is in southeastern Pennsylvania; Yorktown is in eastern Virginia. Review map-scale: source and response retain matching calibration; use each map's own bar after resizing.

Explain an encampment as a place an army stays; supplies are needed materials. Valley Forge's shortages and illness caused hardship; training improved coordinated action. Hardship itself did not train soldiers. Review siege: surrounding a place to force surrender. Explain surrender as giving up to the opposing force. At Yorktown, American and French land forces and French naval power combined. Separate Cornwallis's 1781 surrender from the 1783 peace. No reenactment.

#### 12 min. Logistics annotations

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Keep source dates distinct from event dates. **Ask.** [[s04-P48]]. Check individual annotations against the source afterward; do not compose a camp/siege comparison. Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks; add separate dated labels.

Hear a rotating six: one 45-second observation each, 4.5 minutes within six minutes. Record names. Others annotate individually; all thirty submit notes. Passing is allowed; unheard children still receive paper feedback.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s04-X48a]] [[s04-X48b]] on the independent half-sheet master. Allow six minutes for paper responses, two for collection. Neutral reading and private dictation give no content cues. After class: thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exits; fifteen more for notes and repairs. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately. Return everything on day 49.
