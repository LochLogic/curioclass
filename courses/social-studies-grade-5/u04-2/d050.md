### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 49's s04-X49a, s04-X49b, s04-P49, day 49 practice notes and s04-K49-C, s04-F49-C, s04-K49-L, s04-F49-L. If many day 49 tickets volunteer error K, review aid versus agreement with [[s04-K50-K]], then [[s04-F50-K]]. Error L: [[s04-K50-L]], then [[s04-F50-L]]. Otherwise use [[s04-K50-resource]] and [[s04-F50-resource]] for the recorded resource gap. Model one priority five minutes, reveal steps after responses, then allow five for a fresh response.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Define Loyalist as supporting Britain. Review neutral: remaining outside either side. Review perspective: two sides of one event; evidence must support each view. Recall day 46's Onondaga Nation timeline as a reminder. Read the separate Oneida teaching selection. Nations continue today; one alliance never establishes every individual's allegiance.

Review enslaved people as people forced to work and denied freedom. Distinguish legal status, military side and eventual freedom. Free and enslaved Black people fought on both sides; service did not guarantee freedom. Model unknown motive with the made-up notice, withholding the exit household's reason. No historical sides assigned; no personal disclosures.

#### 12 min. Profile annotations

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Distinguish a modern nation's account from its historical action. **Ask.** [[s04-P50]]. Mark the teaching notice's missing reason; label a single historical action separately. Do not sort a whole status/allegiance matrix. Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks; attach separate dates.

Fifteen concurrent pairs each take two 45-second turns, one per child. Passing is allowed. Collect all individual notes. Printed Oneida/Onondaga selections supply the access fallback; no visitor or photo is needed.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Review the covered-paper routine. Reveal the covered Exit box [[s04-X50a]] and the half-sheet ticket [[s04-X50b]] together. Both are independent paper work. Allow six minutes for paper responses, two for collection. Neutral reading and private dictation give no content cues. After class: thirty times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for exits; fifteen more for notes and repairs. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately. Return everything on day 51. Read the browser-check limitation aloud. Accept Oneida counterevidence without the pending ancestry detail. Honor the family note passage choice, including students 18 or older. Alternative users answer [[s04-P50-alt]] privately instead; record historical coverage as not yet seen.
