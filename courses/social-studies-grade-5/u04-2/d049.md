# Day 49: Resources and a widening war

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare returned papers and one assigned fresh repair card per child from current item-row stems. Keep teacher-key.md and corrected current item-row keys separate from student copies. Print only s04-X49a on the half-sheet ticket. Cover Side 2 and the ticket until the exit part. This student page replaces duplicate teaching-selection, recording and practice masters.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 48's s04-X48a, s04-X48b, s04-P48, day 48 practice notes and s04-K48-X, s04-F48-X, s04-K48-Y, s04-F48-Y. Review protected repair. If many day 48 tickets show error C, use [[s04-K49-C]], then [[s04-F49-C]]; error L: [[s04-K49-L]], then [[s04-F49-L]]. Model one priority five minutes, revealing steps after responses; give its fresh inference five. Correct error X locations privately; record unresolved needs for day 59.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Read separate resource records. Review alliance: an agreement between groups to help one another. Distinguish earlier French assistance from the formal 1778 agreement. French supplies, soldiers and naval support were different resources. Dutch merchant trade supplied arms; bankers provided finance. Spanish campaigns against Britain widened the war; Texas cattle fed Spanish troops along the Gulf.

American persistence and training kept resistance possible. Britain's long Atlantic supply journey and widening war strained resources. Teach contributions separately, without assembling the held route argument or a complete two-cause assessment response.

#### 12 min. Resource inventory

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Demonstrate a resource label, keeping resource and origin in separate boxes. **Ask.** [[s04-P49]]. Children complete one inventory entry, not a winning-alone explanation. Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Add supplied aid/agreement dates; sequence alone does not establish cause.

Fifteen pairs work concurrently, each child taking one 45-second turn. Collect individual notes; public passing is allowed. Check origin labels after responses.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Review the covered-paper routine. Reveal the covered Exit box [[s04-X49b]] and the half-sheet ticket [[s04-X49a]] together. Both are independent paper work. Allow six minutes for paper responses, two for collection. Neutral reading and private dictation give no content cues. After class: thirty times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for exits; fifteen more for notes and repairs. Use one component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record access separately. Return everything on day 50.
