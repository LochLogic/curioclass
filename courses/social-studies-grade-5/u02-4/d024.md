# Day 24: A claim supported by two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use unit 2 change slips for repair. Print today's page and unit 2 private exit 24. Separate R24 from unit 2 section 4 fresh checks. Use unit 2 claim display as a teacher reference; today's page supplies its student frame. Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys. Remove complete worked claims; retain unit 2 timeline and source prompts.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 23's paper: `s02-X23a`, `s02-X23b`, `s02-R23`, `s02-P23-1`, `s02-P23-2`, `s02-P23-3`. If many tickets showed error P, reread Pennsylvania's delayed-service clause, without drawing the held retry timeline. For error O, annotate the home purchase separately from discrimination; error D needs existing family bonds distinguished from legal security. Teach one priority for five minutes. Reveal [[s02-R24]] for five minutes alone; collect and retain other gaps.

#### 5 min. Reopen sources

Review claim: what we say; evidence: what we saw or read that shows it. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim, from day 12. Reopen Smith chapter 2 and the Vassalls' 1781 request, already read in separate fragments. Identify makers, dates and audiences using supplied prompts. These are original summaries of their words, not quotations. Smith addresses readers of his life; the Vassalls address lawmakers. Do not supply a completed claim or integrated paragraph.

#### 10 min. Independent claim display

Students complete [[s02-P24-1]] alone: claim banner, separate attributed details and reasoning arrows, then a limit. Review limit: what the evidence cannot show. Point students to source headers when needed. For error H, ask them to locate a detail rather than supply their link. No paired underlining or paragraph model. Preserve individual work for collection.

#### 5 min. Share observations

Hear six rotating volunteers maximum, one 45-second comment each, within five minutes. **Ask.** Read the display question before inviting observations. Expect one source detail or one reasoning arrow per comment. Keep the display components separate; do not rehearse a complete argument. Every student may pass. Record names; everyone submits a written display, reaching the other 24 too. Seal displays before the exit.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s02-X24]] on its half slip. Read the new source summaries neutrally, without completing any reasoning. Students write alone for eight minutes; collect in two. Mark exit, R24 and displays after class: 30 times 90 seconds, forty-five minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. Return on day 26, after the assessment, without coaching it tomorrow.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to the page. No printer: copy the two teaching summaries and frame; keep the exit separate. Dictation is transcribed without adding links.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or display: copy the teaching summaries and draw the empty frame.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class groups.

- **10 min. Return.** Explain one need from yesterday; give [[s02-R24]] alone.
- **5 min. Sources.** Read Smith's summary and the Vassalls' 1781 summary. Point to makers and dates.
- **15 min. Display.** Your child completes [[s02-P24-1]], keeping claim, details and arrows separate.
  **Say.** "Which detail supports your claim?" Expect: a relevant source detail and an explanation of its link.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Put the display away. Reveal [[s02-X24]]. Your child writes alone. Read neutrally if needed. Collect before discussion.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear fewer public comments. Preserve both source links and independent writing. Longer: explain a limit without adding an unsupported claim.

### Words and supports

Review petition: a written request to officials. Practice frame: "This detail supports my claim because ..." Offer passing, a pause, large print and transcribed dictation; never request personal history.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 uses unseen sources and a map independently. Keep day 24 feedback sealed until day 26. Error H means missing relevant details or links, not a wrong opinion. Day 26 combines that repair with one assessment priority.
