# Day 26: Repair one gap with fresh evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys. Prepare private feedback on unit 2 assessment packet and unit 2 claim display. Select one priority per child; use unit 2 repair cues with earlier teaching fragments. Keep unit 2 section 4 fresh checks hidden until Fresh retry. Print today's page and cut one assigned retry per child. Retain unit 2 timeline and source prompts.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Return day 24's exit, R24 and display in two minutes; day 25's assessment in three. Every child reads one private next-step cue. If many tickets showed error H, prioritize a missing detail or link. Otherwise use assessment needs. Keep unrepaired components recorded. Review claim: what we say; evidence: what we saw or read that shows it.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Run at most four five-minute adult groups, one priority per child. Other students use matching written cues in pairs, with simultaneous 45-second turns each way. No public round. **Ask.** "What evidence is still missing?" Expect: the detail or link their feedback identifies.

Use one earlier teaching fragment at a time. A/I: reread the maternal clause or place the arrival label separately. B/J: point to one completed teaching route. C/K: separate one household record from totals; S: point to Washington's list. D/E/L: revisit Smith's marriage detail, then the escape symbol separately. F/M/Q: use the taught brickmaking recipient or craft detail, never the held metalworker. G/N: follow Flora's deed, then Hall's request recipient separately. O/P: identify Pennsylvania's release age or a separate Vassall property detail. H: draw one brickmaking-detail arrow to a supplied skills claim. R: connect a source date to a researcher's selection. Do not repair from fresh cases.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry

Reveal one assigned paper item: A/I, [[s02-R26a]]; B/J, [[s02-R26b]]; C/K/S, [[s02-R26c]]; D/E/L, [[s02-R26d]]; F/M/Q, [[s02-R26e]]; G/N, [[s02-R26f]]; O/P, [[s02-R26g]]; H, [[s02-R26h]]; R, [[s02-R26i]]. Students work alone, including neutral reading. Label invented records made up. Collect every response; one successful component does not clear other gaps.

#### 5 min. Handoff

Inspect 30 short responses at about eight seconds each, four minutes. Return each with a brief private cue in the remaining minute. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately. When longer reading is needed, leave judgment not yet seen and return a written follow-up cue today. Retain unresolved needs. Students keep timelines, labor maps and source prompts.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to written cues. No printer: hand-copy only the assigned item; transcribe dictation onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or projector: copy one repair fragment and one assigned fresh item.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public return.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read yesterday's work and day 24's display. Choose one need.
- **20 min. Repair.** Use the matching earlier teaching fragment. Keep fresh cases hidden.
  **Say.** "What evidence is missing?" Expect: the detail or link named in feedback.
- **10 min. Fresh retry.** Give only your child's assigned bank item, using today's mapping. Your child answers alone on the page.
- **5 min. Handoff.** Read the answer together. Write one next step and retain other needs.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner discussion, preserving independent work. Longer: revise after collecting fresh evidence.

### Words and supports

Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim. Offer passing, a pause, large print and transcribed dictation. No personal disclosure or reenactment.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens the historical question, "When is breaking a law the right thing to do?" Students write supporting questions. Carry unresolved unit 2 needs forward without promising an extra repair slot.
