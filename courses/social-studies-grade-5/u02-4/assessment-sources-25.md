# Reserved source sheet, day 25
Original summaries, not quotations. Read only during the assessment.

## A, Alexander Falconbridge, ship surgeon, 1788
People were confined below deck. The space grew hot without fresh air.
Many became sick and died.

## B, Felix, petition to the Massachusetts provincial government, January 1773
The petition describes children under others' control and a lack of secure property.
It asks government for relief from slavery.
These statements concern denied legal security, not the absence of family bonds.

## M4, made-up record
Mara is an enslaved Black adult. Her enslaver sells her away from her family.
He blocks her reading lessons. He beats her and threatens punishment for escape.
Mara makes wooden bowls.
