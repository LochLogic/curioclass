# Evidence, assessment, and response

Teacher only. Label every fictional person and record made up before reading.
No child represents a group or reports personal or family history.
No reenactments, auctions, simulations, graphic images, slurs, or sacred activities.
Offer a pause and the same neutral factual summary without requiring an explanation.
Read neutrally or transcribe dictated answers onto paper, preserving the historical decisions.
Do not diagnose an error from blank work.

All thirty students submit paper. Fifteen pairs talk simultaneously, 45 seconds each way.
Hear at most six rotating public observations, each 45 seconds, within five minutes.
Public talk is optional. Record names heard; collected paper reaches everyone.
Five minutes explaining one priority plus five on fresh paper uses the protected repair slot.
Teach together for more than ten shared errors; otherwise meet one adult group.
Others annotate different evidence with written cues. Keep unresolved needs recorded.
One scale: shown; shown with help; not yet seen. Record access support separately.

Claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, and a limit.
Reasoning means how a detail supports a claim.
Day 24 uses Smith chapter 2 and Vassall 1781 as separate display components.
No complete paragraph or paired underlining is modeled before E24.
Seal the display before E24's new Portsmouth 1779 and Vassall 1780 selections.
The earlier-grade Smith case remains teaching only. Retain supplied source prompts.

Day 25: directions one minute, neutral sources five, assessment thirty, collection four.
Q1 four; Q2 four; Q3 four; Q4 three; Q5 two; Q6 three; Q7 three; Q8 six; Q9 one.
Subletters separate asks within those allocations. Q8 selection and argument share one response.
The historical source sheet prints separately. Prompts and response space fit two student sides.
Read remaining context panels neutrally within their item allocation; never supply the reasoning.
Students retrieve law effects, Georgia's year, and abolition effects from instruction.
Withhold all assessment wording, sources and map layout until day 25.
Mark after collection, about ninety minutes for thirty papers, an untested estimate.

Return E24 and assessment privately on 26. Identify one priority and retain other needs.
Twenty repair minutes hold at most four five-minute adult groups; others use written cues.
Ten fresh-retry minutes hold one assigned paper item per child, never thirty oral turns.
Inspect all selected responses during Handoff and give a private written next-step cue.
Carry unresolved needs forward without inventing a promised later repair slot.
Before class: print claim-display-24.md, exit-24.md, assessment-sources-25.md,
assessment-25.md, context-25.md, fresh-checks.md and repair-cues-26.md.
Read teacher-only key-u2.md in section 2.1. Every student receives an individual map.
If printing fails, copy summaries and maps individually; do not test from an answered projection.
