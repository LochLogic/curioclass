# Day 18: Presence, legality and how common ownership was

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 2 scope cards as separate teacher displays; print student pages and unit 2 private exit 18 half slips. Cut R18 alone from unit 2 section 2 fresh checks; keep it unseen. Cover the student-page Exit box until the exit. Read teacher-only ../u02-1/key-u2.md and generated keys. Retain unit 2 completed network for feedback. Set the timeline beside the regional reminders.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 17's exit tickets, Exit box and written tasks: `s02-X17a`, `s02-X17b`, `s02-R17`, `s02-P17-1`, `s02-P17-2`, `s02-P17-3`. If many tickets showed error B, spend five minutes separating people forced to cross from objects exchanged. For error J, explain one documented link without assuming later destinations. Select one priority; retain others. Give unseen [[s02-R18]] for five minutes of independent annotation; collect.

#### 10 min. Explain legality

Read single records separately from scope-cards-18.md. Georgia's ban ended in 1751; other colonies already permitted slavery. Use [[s02-P18-1]] on the timeline. Separately locate Cambridge, Massachusetts: northern slavery was real. Do not combine invented northern-household and leader records before the exit.

Read Washington's 1799 list separately from Jefferson's 1774 record. Both enslaved people. Washington's list also includes people held through other arrangements; do not call every listed person his personal property. Widespread ownership differed by place; individual records cannot count all households.

Teach household proportion: the number of households in a group compared with the total. Demonstrate marked roofs, explicitly made up, with [[s02-P18-2]]. This is not a historical slavery statistic.

#### 12 min. Record scope

Review legend, the map key, from grade 4. Supply overlay instructions: regions group nearby colonies; symbols mark presence, never household totals. Review day 11's separate crop and people bands. Keep people apart from goods. On a teacher sketch, locate Cambridge in the north and Georgia in the south; children use location labels on their notes.

Pairs complete [[s02-P18-3]], one record at a time. **Ask.** [[s02-P18-4]] Expect: no, only presence. Review limit: what the evidence cannot show. Fifteen pairs take 45 seconds each way. Hear six optional 45-second observations within five minutes; record names and collect all paper. No two-record synthesis.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

First uncover the student-page Exit box [[s02-X18a]], then give half slip [[s02-X18b]]. Allow seven minutes alone, neutral reading included, then one to collect. After class, thirty times 60 seconds gives thirty minutes to mark all responses. Use the same shown scale, support separately. Return everything on day 19.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to teacher-held teaching panels. No printer: hand-copy the student text and separate held checks, preserving covered exit work. Dictated responses are transcribed onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or map: copy the single-record summaries and draw the named north/south locations.

**Skip.** Public observations and class grouping.

- **10 min. Return.** Explain one need from yesterday. Give [[s02-R18]] alone afterward.
- **10 min. Legality.** Read each record separately. Use [[s02-P18-1]] and [[s02-P18-2]]. Explain the roofs are made up.
  **Say.** "How many households have marked roofs?" Expect: twelve of thirty.
- **12 min. Scope.** Use [[s02-P18-3]], one record at a time.
  **Say.** "Does a presence symbol count households?" Expect: no.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** First uncover the student-page Exit box [[s02-X18a]], then give half slip [[s02-X18b]]. Your child answers alone. Collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing, preserving individual work, repair and exit time. Longer: name one detail the source cannot establish, without previewing another case.

### Words and supports

Legal means allowed by law, which does not mean just. Review forced labor: work people could not refuse. Review limit and legend in the earlier words above. Use neutral reading, enlargement or private dictation; permit a pause.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 returns all day 18 responses. If many give error C, locate northern presence; error K needs the missing household total. Error S denies named leaders' enslavement: reread one record. Choose one priority, then fresh R19. Day 19 studies separate rights and family constraints before an independent comparison.
