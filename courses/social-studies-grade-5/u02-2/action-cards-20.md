# Actions against denied freedom, day 20
Original summaries, not quotations.

## Locator, not to scale
Carolina plantations → Florida: St. Augustine, with Fort Mose nearby.
Fort Mose was established in 1738 by people who escaped enslavement.
Spanish freedom required service to the king and religious conversion.
This is historical context. No one acts out these events or performs religious activities.

## Separate action symbols
Footpath: escape from captivity.
Paper: petition, a written request to officials.
Raised flag: revolt, an armed uprising.
Revolts challenged enslavement and faced violent suppression.
