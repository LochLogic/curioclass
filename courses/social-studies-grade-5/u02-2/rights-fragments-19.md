Original summaries of different historical records, not quotations.

Smith's 1798 account, chapter 2: Smith married Meg while both were enslaved.
A later sale separated him from his wife and child.
Relationship: ______________________________________

Vassall 1781 petition: Anthony and Cuba requested support after years in slavery.
Recipient: _________________________________________

Education context: Some enslaved people learned to read. Access was limited and varied.
Enslavers could block learning. Do not assume one universal colonial education law.
Smith also describes violence by enslavers, told here without graphic detail.
Virginia's 1705 law allowed physical punishment of captured people escaping enslavement.
This law concerns escape, not a universal prohibition on learning.
Sale: treating someone as property. Restriction: a limit on what someone can do.
