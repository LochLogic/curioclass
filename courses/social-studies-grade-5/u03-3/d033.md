# Day 33: Source clues before evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 component-records.md as three model panels. Copy the different practice records below. Cut today's unit 3 section 3 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 3 private exits. Keep the source panel on Side 2 covered. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Gather day 32 papers. Remove model panels and checklists before the exit.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X32a`, `s03-X32b`, `s03-P32`, `s03-F32`.

Honor yesterday's promise. Review the ten-minute repair routine from grade 4: model five, fresh response five. If many day 32 tickets show error J, contrast a request with a threat. Review petition and intimidation from day 32. Model five minutes using its teaching actions, never its exit notes. Reveal today's made-up committee timetable note for [[s03-F33]]; allow five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise meet one group while others follow written cues. Retain other needs.

#### 10 min. Component model

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, from grade 4 day 19 and day 27. Audience means intended readers; purpose means what the maker seeks. Today's new demand is starting without prompts.

Say the three model records are invented. **Ask.** [[s03-K33]] Pause for a separate response before revealing each component. [[steps:s03-K33]] Never combine these different records into a complete source note. Teach testimony: an account given as evidence; engraving: a made print with selected visual features; repeal: ending a law.

#### 12 min. Guided annotations

Use the different invented practice records. Assign maker/date only to the label, audience only to the notice, purpose only to the request. **Ask.** [[s03-P33]] Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way; everyone records all three separate annotations. If many give an audience without a clue, point back to the addressee. Collect practice. Cover models and fold Side 1 away.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal only today's source panel and give [[s03-X33]] on its half slip. All thirty initiate notes independently, without a checklist. Collect spontaneous notes before any content prompts. Neutral reading and transcribed dictation preserve paper evidence. After class: 30 times 40 seconds for exits, plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 30 minutes. Return all work day 34, Return and repair. Review the component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record access support separately.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to paper. No printer: copy today's panels and stems exactly. Keep future sources covered.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy today's separate records and keep the source panel folded away.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read yesterday's cue, then do [[s03-F33]].
- **10 min. Model.** Read the invented model records. Do [[s03-K33]], revealing one component at a time.
- **12 min. Practice.** Use the different records for [[s03-P33]].
  **Say.** "Which words identify the readers of this notice?" Expect library volunteers from the addressee.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Hide the models and practice. Reveal only today's source panel. Give [[s03-X33]] alone, without naming the components first.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping repair and independent evidence. Longer: identify evidence still needed for one answer.

### Words and supports

Review audience, purpose and the four-part source model. Testimony, engraving and repeal have the meanings introduced above. Everyone may pass in public talk. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. No personal disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 returns every day 33 paper. If many tickets show error C, use separate audience and purpose clues before a fresh audience inference. Then examine differently made accounts of the King Street shooting.
