# Day 34: Different records, bounded evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 portrayal-notes.md as separate teaching panels. Use the additional Murray detail below. Cut today's unit 3 section 3 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 3 private exits. Replace the two-item master with today's covered Exit box and one-item half slip. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Bring day 33 papers. Use text descriptions, without an uninspected image.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X33`, `s03-P33`, `s03-F33`.

If many day 33 tickets show error C, review audience and purpose from the source model. For five minutes, use these separate invented fragments: "Seed volunteers" addresses readers; "Please water seedlings so they survive" expresses purpose. Reveal the new trail-helper note for [[s03-F34]], five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain unresolved needs.

#### 10 min. Two portrayals

Mark the return to 1770. British soldiers fired into a Boston crowd; people were killed, including Crispus Attucks. Avoid graphic detail. Review engraving and testimony from day 33. The park account identifies Paul Revere's engraving; a maker selected a portrayal. Matthew Murray testified for the defense in October 1770. His words survive in an anonymous summary among a lawyer's papers. Describe the source form, without inventing image features. Attucks's later symbolic influence does not establish his private motives.

Review corroboration: two tellers who each saw it agree; a copy adds nothing, from grade 4 day 22. Agreement strengthens only the supported detail. A purposeful source can still answer a bounded question.

#### 12 min. Evidence links

Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim, from grade 4 day 48. Review the claim sheet from grade 4 day 158: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Teach condition: a circumstance under which a claim holds. **Ask.** [[s03-K34]] Reveal separate labels and one arrow, not a paragraph. [[steps:s03-K34]]

**Ask.** [[s03-P34]] Each child annotates one source's feature and limit. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way; both write. If many show error K, distinguish a selected portrayal from a camera record. No shared-fact response or full two-source argument is modeled.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover the new port pair with [[s03-X34a]] in the Exit box. Give [[s03-X34b]] alone on the half slip. Each child writes independently; accept neutral reading and transcribed dictation. The letter anticipates closure, not a closure already operating. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 50 seconds for both components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 35 minutes. Return day 35, Return and repair. Review the component scale; support separately.

### Words and supports

Review audience, purpose, testimony, engraving, corroboration and reasoning. Condition means a circumstance under which a claim holds. Offer neutral reading, dictation and large print. Anyone may pass or use the neutral summary without explaining. No reenactment or personal disclosures.
