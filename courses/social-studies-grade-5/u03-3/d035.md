# Day 35: Weigh evidence and explain a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 3 evidence-display.md as blank labels only; retain yesterday's separate teaching summaries. Cut today's unit 3 section 3 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 3 private exits. Use today's covered Exit box and one-item half slip. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Bring every day 34 paper. Cover completed labels, practice frames and later sources before independent work.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X34a`, `s03-X34b`, `s03-P34`, `s03-F34`.

If many day 34 tickets show error C, error D or error M, or practice shows error K, select one priority for five minutes. Review clue-based sourcing, bounded agreement, question-specific usefulness or selected portrayal. Use made-up labels: a clerk records two blue bins; a caretaker records two blue bins with lids unchecked. Agreement supports count and color, leaving lid condition open. For source-clue needs, identify each recorder and observation scope. Reveal the fresh red-crate receipts and [[s03-F35]] for five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain other needs.

#### 10 min. Evidence selection

Apply yesterday's work without a new model. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Review corroboration and reasoning. Review the claim sheet: claim, two sources with makers, a detail from each, a limit. Children return to the Revere account and Murray teaching selections from day 34. They independently select one relevant detail from each and note each origin. A selected portrayal and a summarized witness statement offer different kinds of evidence. Nationality alone cannot establish accuracy.

#### 12 min. Evidence display

**Ask.** [[s03-P35]] Each child makes separate claim, detail, reason and uncertainty labels on evidence-display.md, using the summaries printed here. Accept a bounded claim about Murray's direct knowledge after departure, or about how the engraving presents an event. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way about one connection. Everyone writes; teacher gives each a written cue. If many show error E, point to yesterday's separate reason arrow. Do not assemble a complete comparison paragraph. Withdraw finished labels before the exit.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Review repeal: ending a law. Reveal the new trade pair with [[s03-X35a]] in the covered Exit box. Give [[s03-X35b]] alone on the half slip. All thirty write independently. Accept neutral reading and transcribed dictation. The pair supports trade concerns as a reason, not the only reason. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 70 seconds for both components plus 30 times 20 seconds for practice/retry, 45 minutes. Return day 36, Return and repair. Review the component scale; support separately.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to paper. No printer: copy today's panels and stems exactly. Keep future sources covered.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy blank labels and yesterday's separate teaching summaries. Keep today's trade pair hidden.

**Skip.** Class grouping and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Read yesterday's cue, then [[s03-F35]].
- **10 min. Select.** Your child chooses separate details from yesterday's teaching sources, naming each origin.
- **12 min. Connect.** Do [[s03-P35]] with separate labels.
  **Say.** "How does that detail support the claim?" Expect an explained link, such as departure limiting direct observation afterward.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Cover completed labels. Reveal the trade pair with [[s03-X35a]] and give [[s03-X35b]] on its half slip. Your child writes alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce sharing, keeping repair and independent evidence. Longer: identify evidence still needed for one answer.

### Words and supports

Review source-sourcing, corroboration, reasoning, claim sheet and repeal from days 33 and 34. Keep blank organizers; remove completed responses. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. Everyone may pass in public talk. No personal disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 returns every day 35 paper. If many show error D, error E, error M or error R, repair one evidence limit, connection, usefulness claim or access reason. Then move forward to 1773 and 1774 resistance events.
