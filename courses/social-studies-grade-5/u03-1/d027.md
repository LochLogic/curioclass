# Day 27: Questions about authority

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print unit 3 family note and component records; send family.md once. Use today's covered Exit box and one-item half slip instead of the old two-item master. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Use unit 3 question-board.md as a reference, copying only today's teaching panels below. Cut today's unit 3 section 1 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 1 private exits; keep later cards hidden. Bring day 26 feedback and the earlier timeline.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Pick up day 26's unresolved needs: review claim, evidence and reasoning. Model one missing source clue for five minutes using an already taught petition address, not today's retry. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use private written cues. For five minutes reveal the invented roof log, [[s03-F27]], and collect paper. Review the component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; access separately.

#### 8 min. Hook

Keep day 26's promise: open "When is breaking a law the right thing to do?" This concerns history, never children's conduct. Display separately attributed Congress and Parliament summaries. Authority is claimed power to make binding decisions. Consent means agreement. Review tax, the grade 3 word for money pooled for public uses; benefits were unequal. Invite silent noticing of the two claims. Do not model a supporting question or reader-use answer.

#### 7 min. Prompted retrieval

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose, from grade 4. Review Prince Hall's petition from grade 3 and day 22, a reminder, never a new check. Supply its date beside the address and request summary. **Ask.** [[s03-T27]] Expect the petitioners, January 13, 1777, Massachusetts lawmakers and a freedom-law request. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way; everyone records clues. Model a missed clue after responses. Mark the timeline's backward return from 1777 to 1754.

#### 8 min. Question board

Review the question board from unit 2. Model placing the supplied war-cost question beside days 28 to 30. **Ask.** [[s03-P27]] Expect consent near 31 and resistance near 32/36. Pairs sort, then record independently. Identify authority as an interpretive idea and a date as factual. Do not supply paired interpretations. Retain the board.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Uncover the archive-club card and [[s03-X27a]] in the student page's Exit box. Give only s03-X27b on the half slip. All thirty answer both independently; dictation stays paper. Collect all work. After class: 30 times 30 seconds for exits plus 30 times 15 seconds for practice, 22.5 minutes. Return everything on day 28, Return and repair. Use the single scale; omissions receive cues, not automatic error letters.

### Words and supports

Review source-sourcing and petition: a written request to officials. Authority: claimed decision power. Interpretive: open to different meanings. Everyone may pass in public talk. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. No personal disclosures or reenactment.
