# Day 29: Two pressures after the war

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use unit 3 cause-chain.md as a reference, copying only source notes and blank arrows. Cut today's unit 3 section 1 fresh repair slips and unit 3 section 1 private exits. Use today's covered Exit box and one-item half slip instead of the old two-item master. Read teacher-only key-u3.md and generated keys. Keep new exit panels hidden. Bring day 28 papers and the timeline.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back `s03-X28`, `s03-P28`, `s03-F28`.

Return day 28's explanation, annotations and retry. If many tickets show error H or error T, contrast the separately attributed teaching notes for five minutes: alliances differed; location is not a political choice. Reveal the fresh trade-route map description and [[s03-F29]] for five independent minutes. More than ten sharing a gap: teach together; otherwise one group, others use cues. Retain other needs.

#### 10 min. Policy model

Keep yesterday's promise: debt is money owed; revenue is government receipts. Britain retained war debts and defense costs after 1763. Review tax from grade 3, then explain new revenue laws. The Proclamation of 1763 restricted western settlement and private purchases in reserved Native lands. Its text names security and land abuses. This imperial rule did not create Native rights.

Review the grade 4 cause-effect chain: a reason leads to a decision; dates alone show order. Model a money-pressure label on one arrow. Separately label a land-pressure arrow. Do not write a paired explanation. Read the original teaching summaries aloud.

#### 12 min. Chain work

Review equal-year chronology and the earlier timeline: place 1754, 1763 and 1765 with unequal gaps. **Ask.** [[s03-P29]] Expect one source-supported arrow, such as debt toward revenue demands or land abuses toward purchase restrictions. Children label one arrow at a time, then add an evidence phrase. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way. If many connect land restrictions only to debt, point back to the land note and keep the branches separate. Retain the organizer for revision.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Uncover today's new panels and [[s03-X29a]] in the student page's Exit box. Give only s03-X29b on the half slip. Children answer both alone. Accept short sentences, neutral reading and transcribed dictation. Collect all papers. After class: 30 times 30 seconds for exits plus 30 times 15 seconds for practice/retry, 22.5 minutes. Return everything day 30, Return and repair. Review the component scale; access separately.

### Words and supports

Debt: money owed. Revenue: government receipts. Review tax, homeland and cause-effect chain. Frame: "The detail connects ___ because ___." Everyone may pass in public talk. Offer neutral reading, large print and transcribed dictation. No personal disclosures or reenactment.
