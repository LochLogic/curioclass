# Separate law slips, day 16
Original summaries, not quotations.

1662, Virginia General Assembly: A child's status follows the mother's status.
1667, Virginia General Assembly: Baptism does not change an enslaved person's legal status.
Hereditary: passing from parent to child.
Baptism: a religious ceremony, discussed here only as a subject of historical law.

Event slips: Virginia arrival, 1619; maternal law, 1662; baptism law, 1667.
Atlantic forced transport existed before 1619.
