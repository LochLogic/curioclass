# Day 15: Whose work and freedom belong in our inquiry?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare unit 2 source noticing as a teacher reference, student pages and unit 2 private exit 15 half slips. Read teacher-only key-u2.md and generated keys; keep both separate. Send the family note, including the adult-student choice. Set out the question board and day 14 retry feedback. Keep the student-page Exit box and half slips covered until the exit.

### The day

#### 6 min. Open inquiry

Return day 14's selected retry privately in the first minute; retain unresolved needs. Read both student summaries. Review Venture Smith's freedom story from grade 3, reminder only. Anthony and Cuba Vassall are real people, too. Post: "How did enslaved and free Africans shape the colonies, and how did they seek freedom?" Explain that people shaped places through work while seeking control over their lives.

#### 5 min. Retrieve choice and force

Review grade 4's forced and chosen: people forced to work and not paid, enslaved named plainly. All answer [[s02-K15-1]] privately. **Ask.** Read that question before discussing. Expect: forced labor cannot be refused; freely chosen work involves choice. Correct error B without acting out either condition.

#### 9 min. Question sorting

Review big question: a question too large to answer at once. Review supporting question: a smaller question that helps answer the big one. Model sorting the first supplied question into laws; children sort [[s02-P15-1]]. Use [[s02-P15-2]] separately. Never model generating two questions or an audience-benefit response.

Review the question board routine. Post supplied topics and evidence days: laws, 16/18; routes, 17; daily control, 19/21; resistance, 20; freedom and community, 22/23.

#### 10 min. Source noticing

Review source log: maker, date, audience, purpose. Supply those prompts explicitly. Smith addresses readers of his life account; the modern National Park Service history addresses history readers. Model distinguishing an event year from a text date. Pairs complete [[s02-P15-3]] and [[s02-P15-4]]. Fifteen pairs speak simultaneously, 45 seconds each way. Hear six optional 45-second observations within five minutes; record names. Collect every paper.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

First uncover the student-page Exit box [[s02-X15a]], then give half slip [[s02-X15b]]. Children work alone for eight minutes, neutral reading included; collect in two. After class, allow thirty times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes, to mark exits and practice. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; record support separately. Return everything on day 16.

### Other rooms

No projector: point to teacher-held teaching panels. No printer: hand-copy the student text and separate held checks, preserving covered exit work. Dictated responses are transcribed onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or board: copy the summaries and questions onto paper.

**Skip.** Public observations and the class question board.

- **6 min. Open.** Return yesterday's retry. Read the real people's summaries.
- **5 min. Review.** Use [[s02-K15-1]].
  **Say.** "How does forced labor differ from chosen work?" Expect: people cannot refuse forced labor.
- **9 min. Sort.** Use [[s02-P15-1]] and [[s02-P15-2]]. Place each question under its topic.
- **10 min. Notice.** Point to maker and date labels. Use [[s02-P15-3]].
  **Say.** "Which date describes the home purchase?" Expect: 1787, an event year.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X15a]] and [[s02-X15b]]. Your child answers alone. Collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing, preserving individual work, repair and exit time. Longer: name one detail the source cannot establish, without previewing another case.

### Words and supports

Review big question, supporting question and forced labor in the earlier words above. Read neutrally or transcribe private dictation. Permit passing, a pause and the same factual summary. Never request personal or family history.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 receives all day 15 paper in Importance return. If many tickets show irrelevant questions, reconnect topics to the inquiry. For error R, connect missing information to a reader's need. One priority only. Day 16 receives day 14 readiness, then teaches separate Virginia laws.
