# Read the completed network, day 17
This original diagram simplifies a network. It is not to scale.
Actual voyages varied. Not every ship sailed all three links.

**Goods:** Europe → Africa: manufactured goods.
**People forced:** Africa → Americas: enslaved Africans, the Middle Passage.
**Goods:** Americas → Europe: plantation goods.
