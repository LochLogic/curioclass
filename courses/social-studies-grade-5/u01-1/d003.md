# Day 3: What agreements establish

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate teacher pages and key-exits.md. Teacher-only exit look-fors appear below. Keep exits covered. The source block is on the student page; print no separate sources sheet. Prepare only needed timeline and source-log panels. Prepare the question board and unit record sheet. Sort yesterday's papers by one priority need.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 2's written tasks and exit tickets together. If many day 2 tickets showed error A, spend five minutes tracing authority in its diagram, then five on fresh [[s01-R3-1]]. For error B, reread the present-tense sentence on the retained sources-1 sheet then give five minutes for fresh [[s01-R3-2]]. Review: nation, a people with their own government. Choose one priority; others use written cues. A timeline gap may replace this explanation, never add another lesson.

#### 10 min. Explain agreements

Yesterday's disease and rights annotations lead to agreements. Review treaty: agreement between governments (grade 4). Review trade: goods move from where they are made to where they are sold (grade 4). Trading an object records that transaction; it supplies no automatic authority over surrounding land.

Review the 1621 mutual-protection agreement (grade 3), using sources-3. No harvest retelling. Explain that the named parties promised mutual protection. A record has a stated subject and scope. **Ask.** [[s01-K3-1]] Wait, then show [[steps:s01-K3-1]]. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose.

#### 9 min. Dated sequence

Pairs use [[s01-P3]] on the retained timeline. Review equal gaps for equal years, with ten-year marks. One 45-second turn each; every child places labels independently. Distinguish the event from the modern account. Keep the labeled line for the next section.

#### 5 min. Source limits

Everyone completes [[s01-P3-2]] and puts the maker in their source log. The shop receipt is invented. Its maker is the made-up shop. Hear six rotating 45-second observations about its recorded object. Explain that silence leaves a question unanswered; it does not establish the opposite. If many infer land authority from a transaction, point to the named object. Do not rehearse a promise-plus-consent answer.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s01-X3]] and the covered Exit box [[s01-X3b]] only now. Cedar and Harbor are made-up non-Native councils. Review consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made (grade 4). Children answer alone on the half-slip and Exit box; offer neutral reading or dictation. Collect all paper for day 4. After class, thirty times 30 seconds gives fifteen minutes for exits; allow ten more for practice. Record shown, shown with help, or not yet seen, with support separate.

Teacher only, mark each component: [[steps:s01-X3]] [[steps:s01-X3b]]

### Other rooms

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the agreement summary and questions. No strip: draw equal ten-year marks.

**Skip.** Public sharing and class return routines.

- **10 min. Agreement.** Read the dated reminder. Use [[s01-K3-1]], then reveal its steps.
  **Say.** "Who made this modern account?" Expect: Law Library of Congress.
- **10 min. Sequence.** Take a partner turn in [[s01-P3]]. Your child places both labels.
  **Say.** "How far apart are the events?" Expect: one year.
- **9 min. Records.** Use [[s01-P3-2]]. Explain that the invented receipt records only its named transaction.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** First reveal [[s01-X3]] and [[s01-X3b]]. Your child works alone. Read without hints if needed. Collect before discussing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing; retain explanation, individual work, and exit. Longer: revisit the existing source annotation with support withdrawn.

### Words and supports

Review consent: people agreeing to how decisions are made. Scope means what a record covers. Offer movable labels, neutral reading, or dictation; no land claim is inferred from silence.

Day 4 returns this batch in Return and repair. Error H treats the repair promise as consent to later land claims; error A explicitly denies land rights. Address one priority within ten minutes, then begin the Pequot War.
