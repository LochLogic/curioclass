# Day 8: Researching Penn through separate notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-exits.md and the generated teacher answer key. Keep the day 8 slip covered. Return yesterday's Penn research notes with the other papers. The sources-8 sheet block is included once on the student page. Prepare a folder for collecting research notes separately from exits.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 7's whole batch, including Exit boxes. If many day 7 tickets showed error D, spend five minutes locating a stated purpose, then five on [[s01-R8-1]]. For error I, instead separate a welcome from a restriction, then use [[s01-R8-2]]. These town and club cases are made up. Choose one priority; give written cues for other needs. Religious protection does not establish equal political rights.

#### 8 min. Reopen Penn

Yesterday's separate research moves lead to today's independent notes. Review founder: a person the town's record names as starting the town. Review finding: an answer supported by research evidence. Read fragments A and B without supplying a completed finding. Penn discusses purposes of government; these fragments alone do not describe all colonial rights.

**Ask.** [[s01-P8-2]] Locate the detail before checking the teacher answer. Accept B. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Keep the author's aim separate from proof that government achieved it.

#### 10 min. Research notes

All complete [[s01-P8-4]] privately on Penn research notes, then locate a relevant fragment. Fifteen pairs exchange source-location observations, one 45-second turn each. Each child completes [[s01-P8]] alone, recording key words rather than a report paragraph. Accept either fragment with accurate provenance. Hear six rotating source-location observations, one 45-second ask each, during this part; anyone may pass. No one models a complete question-plus-finding response. If many show error H, point back to their selected fragment before adding information.

#### 5 min. Source inventory

Everyone completes [[s01-P8-3]]. Each child checks labels against the printed source identity. Collect every practice page and research note before distributing the exit. Keep the timeline and source log in folders. The collected preface notes return after the exit, with teacher feedback tomorrow, and remain available on day 13.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s01-X8]]. Explain freemen as eligible voters here, not everyone. Children write independently: four minutes to read and form a question, three for the sourced finding. Collect before discussion. Redistribute only preface notes afterward. Return all judged work in day 9 Return and repair. After class, thirty times 45 seconds gives 22.5 minutes for the three exit components; allow fifteen more for notes. Use shown, shown with help, or not yet seen per component; record support separately. Blank work establishes no error.

Teacher only: [[steps:s01-X8]]

### Other rooms

Hand-copy the fragments with their identity line. No live research or home internet is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy fragments and tasks. No class folder: use a folded paper envelope.

**Skip.** Class return and public observations.

- **8 min. Reopen.** Read A and B. Use [[s01-P8-2]].
  **Say.** "Which fragment concerns administering laws?" Expect: B.
- **12 min. Notes.** Your child chooses a question with [[s01-P8-4]], then completes [[s01-P8]]. Take the partner's source-location turn.
  **Say.** "Where is that detail?" Expect: a point to A or B.
- **8 min. Labels.** Use [[s01-P8-3]]. Collect the notes before the exit.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** First reveal [[s01-X8]]. Your child answers alone. Collect before discussing. Return the preface notes separately and keep them for day 13.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear fewer public observations; retain independent notes and the complete exit. Longer: revisit an existing note with support withdrawn.

### Words and supports

Review evidence: information used to support a claim. Review: a preface introduces a text. Offer neutral reading, enlarged print, bilingual notes or dictation. No personal belief reports.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 returns day 8's batch. Error H uses an irrelevant detail or substitutes a name for support. Reconnect a different preface detail for five minutes, then make a fresh annotation for five. Keep preface notes for day 13. Next study continuing English language and institutions.
