# Day 9: Institutions that continued

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-exits.md and generated teacher keys. Keep the half-slip covered. The sources-9 sheet block below supplies dated labels, with qualified continuity. Prepare the equal-year timeline and source log. Sort day 8's papers by one priority. Keep individual Penn notes separate from exits for day 13.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 8's whole batch with written feedback on the preface notes. If many day 8 tickets showed error H, spend five minutes separating an author's name from a supporting detail. Then use fresh [[s01-R9-1]] for five minutes. Evade means avoid obeying. Other gaps receive written cues. Review finding: an answer supported by research evidence. Keep research notes in folders for day 13.

#### 10 min. Explain continuity

Yesterday's government purposes lead to practices that continued. Review voyage: a trip by ship. Review colony: a settlement far from the home country, which claims it. A voyage and sustained government are different. Read the Virginia record. Teach assembly: a lawmaking body. Institution means an established organization or practice.

**Ask.** [[s01-K9-1]] Wait before [[steps:s01-K9-1]]. Model the authority change only. Continuing government creates repeated occasions to use its language and procedures. Repetition helps explain influence; arrival order alone does not. Read the Dutch context separately. Other European powers established colonies too. English official texts do not prove that everybody spoke English or had equal rights. Influence does not rank peoples.

#### 9 min. Sequence work

Review timeline: equal gaps for equal years and ten-year marks. Fifteen pairs use [[s01-P9]], one 45-second turn each. Each student places the dated labels on the carried line, then marks continuation after takeover. Continuation need not mean uninterrupted meetings. Students independently complete [[s01-P9-2]]. Keep date, language and institution annotations separate; compose no causal paragraph. If many show error E, point to repeated government after arrival. For error L, replace ranking words with the record's institution labels.

#### 5 min. Board connection

Hear six rotating observations, one 45-second ask each, about a date or source-heading location. Anyone may pass. All submit sequence annotations. Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. The modern histories explain earlier events; their publication and event dates differ. Retain the timeline for day 10 maps and return all judged annotations then.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s01-X9]]. Review charter in its historical sense: an official grant. This royal charter is written in English. Gloss oath as a formal promise; allegiance as loyalty to a ruler or government. Children explain independently, including neutral dictation. Collect for day 10. After class, thirty times 30 seconds gives fifteen minutes for exits; allow ten more for annotations. Mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen per component; record support separately. Blank work establishes no error.

Teacher only: [[steps:s01-X9]]

  **Say.** "Which dated label comes first?" Expect: 1619 assembly.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear fewer public observations. Keep explanation, sequence work and exit. Longer: reread the record's continuation qualification with support withdrawn.

### Words and supports

Use the definitions above. Continuity means something continues across time. Offer enlarged headings, movable dated labels, neutral reading or dictation. Review reasoning: how a detail supports a claim.
