# Day 7: Founding aims and their limits

## Teacher page

### Before class

Separate key-exits.md and generated teacher keys. Cover the Exit box; hold half-slips until the exit. Prepare three category headings: Beliefs, Trade, Government safeguards. The sources-7 sheet block is printed once on Side 1. Retain sources-6 for reminders. Prepare Penn research notes with only its source fields exposed today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 6's entire batch. Review nation: a people with their own government. If many day 6 tickets showed error B, spend five minutes locating present actions in the dated records. For error C, instead separate each nation heading from other nations' headings. Everyone then completes fresh annotation [[s01-R7-1]] in five minutes. These records are reminders, not another check. Other gaps receive written cues.

#### 10 min. Explain aims and limits

Yesterday's living governments remain on the board. Now investigate colonial founders' aims without treating their claims as permission from Native nations. Review colony: a settlement far from the home country, which claims it. Review founder: a person the town's record names as starting the town. Apply this word to colonial founding.

Review trade: goods move from where they are made to where they are sold. Read the separate fragments. Explain dissent as disagreement with authorities. Williams opposed official control of belief; Hutchinson questioned ministers. Study beliefs historically; ask nobody about personal beliefs. An aim states a purpose, not a guarantee of equal rights.

**Ask.** [[s01-K7-1]] Wait, then [[steps:s01-K7-1]]. Model this category only.

A preface introduces a text. Review finding: an answer supported by research evidence. Teach separate research moves: choose an answerable question, note relevant key words, attach a source label. **Ask.** [[s01-K7-2]] Wait before [[steps:s01-K7-2]]. Leave its answer unwritten.

#### 9 min. Aim sorting

Fifteen pairs work simultaneously on [[s01-P7-2]] and [[s01-P7-3]], one 45-second turn each. Each child then completes [[s01-P7-4]] privately. Keep category placement and note fragments separate; compose no comparison paragraph. If many show error D, return to the specific aim words, rather than assigning every fragment one motive.

#### 5 min. Source labels

Review source-sourcing: maker, date, audience, purpose. Everyone completes [[s01-P7]] on Penn research notes. Hear six rotating observations about label locations, one 45-second turn each; others may pass. Collect all notes and annotations for day 8. Keep the timeline and source log in folders.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

First reveal [[s01-X7a]] and Exit box [[s01-X7b]]. Gloss banishment as being ordered to leave. Review profit: what is left after costs. An investor supplies money hoping for a gain. Children answer independently, including neutral dictation. Allow three minutes per task. Collect all paper for day 8. After class, thirty times 30 seconds gives fifteen minutes for exits; allow fifteen more for annotations. Mark each component shown, shown with help, or not yet seen; record support separately. Blank work establishes no error.

Teacher only: [[steps:s01-X7a]] [[steps:s01-X7b]]
