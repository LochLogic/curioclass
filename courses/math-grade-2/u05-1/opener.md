# Building and naming hundreds

Review Grade 1 ones, tens, digit and bundle. Fit ten ones to a ten-strip. Ten strips use precisely the same amount of paper as a hundred grid. A trade changes the unit being counted while keeping the quantity fixed. Connect the concrete exchange to the paper picture and then to its numeral. Recall Grade 1's one hundred and show how a zero records an empty place here. When both small-unit places are empty, both zeroes are needed. By day 78, lift the place cards to connect the digit positions with expanded form.

Bring back Exchange and check in a paired paper bank from section 2.2 (planned only). Recall Mika from section 1.1 in a new paper scene. Review equal-interval length from Unit 4. Day 74 has retrieval pictures. Keep their first marks for day 75 and use the separate fresh pictures on day 76 as needed. Return Unit 4 records privately in the named Return blocks on days 74 and 75. Send the bilingual family note on day 74.

Use fitted stock, a place mat, pencils and hand-labeled cards. Adults cut ahead. A ruled sheet and matching paper scraps replace the kit. Read one cue at a time and allow tactile outlines or a scribe for directed marks. Have students make their own marks. The private exit and practice keys belong in Before class.

For error A, cover a strip with ones and make the hundred exchange. For error B, pair each digit with its own units. For error C, keep the empty column covered by its zero card. For error D, lift the place cards to uncover the addends. A five-minute move includes a fresh independent retry. Save private first marks and revisions. Hear six 45-second turns during Practice, allowing 30 seconds for the roster. Rotate through the class by day 78 and carry missed names forward.
