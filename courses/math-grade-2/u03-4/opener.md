# Applying and checking measurement

On day 54 each pair measures a pencil case twice per child and plots its own readings. If all readings agree, keep consecutive ticks on both sides and stack the repeated marks at their place. Compare two named readings, keeping length and count distinct. Return the exit privately on day 55; carry any remaining needs to day 58.

On day 55 give all children four independent estimates, four tool turns and a five-strip plot. Use verified paper rulers, a yardstick, a meter strip, flexible paper tape and supported physical routes. Verify actual-size stock. Adults cut it and keep aisles clear. If a verified measure is missing, defer that observation and date the make-up. Pause after first forecasts to keep them separate from checked readings. Use two cohorts of 15. Keep 30 tool places during the overlap, at most eight per lane. Assign pupils 1 to 8 to cohort A and pupils 9 to 15 to cohort B. Hear them in those groups. Other children keep working. Give one private explanation turn per child, two minutes including switches. On day 56 hear pupils 16 to 30 using one retained tool picture during construction. Date the make-up for any absence before returning that child’s key. With one child, divide the 60 minutes into two sittings and use the same private paper records.

On day 56 return T, E and P for revision. Preserve first predictions and measured data. Join three verified foot strips on separate backing paper. If there is no adhesive, trace joins and endpoints onto it. Keep the yardstick covered before giving C. Return T and C for final revisions on day 57. Keep the two C counts and the reason from the first observation. Score the revised plot and original forecasts, allowing a new estimate only on an unseen matching-unit object. Use D, the short cable cue, L and S from the reserved stock. F uses their written evidence without adding a cue. Return private final feedback on day 58.

For response, use two five-minute rounds each on days 58 and 59. Rank errors A, B, C, J, I, D, E, F, G, H, K, L and M. The teacher leads one group per round. Others take a pictured repair and fresh recheck. Date outstanding needs. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. In Before class list the private station and closing stock key, the private closing assessment key and any applicable exit or repair key.
