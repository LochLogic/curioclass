# Private exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher. Use only the entry for the current ticket. Assign a letter only when the paper or explanation shows its reasoning.

**s03-X54.** 76 − 71 = □ or 71 + □ = 76, with □ = 5 cm. Locate the shortest and longest occupied ticks and mark their span, keeping the answer in centimeters. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error H for 76 cm and error C for 6 cm.
