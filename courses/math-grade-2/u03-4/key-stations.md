# Private station and closing stock key

Before class, check the four station lengths and tools. P stock, in strip order A through E, is 78, 109, 82, 98 and 112 millimeters. C is a separate, untouched 24-inch strip. Never put the target lengths on student stock. A supported flat tabletop route can be made with joined paper. Use flexible paper tape on the curve. For the conferences use one retained T picture per child. Ask how the tool shows the length. Allow two minutes each including notes and changeover. Hear children 1 to 15 on day 55 and 16 to 30 on day 56. The separate driving schedule is in the section opener.

**s03-E-cm.** About 20 cm, using two spans of a checked 10 cm reference. Use a relevant benchmark on the picture and save the prediction before any measurement. Accept a defensible approximation. Keep the first prediction when benchmark marks are revised. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label.

**s03-E-ft.** About 6 ft, using six spans of a checked foot reference. Use a relevant benchmark on the picture and save the prediction before any measurement. Accept a defensible approximation. Keep the first prediction when benchmark marks are revised. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label.

**s03-E-m.** About 2 m, using two spans of a checked meter reference. Use a relevant benchmark on the picture and save the prediction before any measurement. Accept a defensible approximation. Keep the first prediction when benchmark marks are revised. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label.

**s03-E-in.** About 18 in, using three spans of a checked 6 inch reference. Use a relevant benchmark on the picture and save the prediction before any measurement. Accept a defensible approximation. Keep the first prediction when benchmark marks are revised. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error I for Uses a centimeter for one inch.

**s03-T-cm.** 19 cm. One suitable choice is a centimeter ruler. Choose a usable tool, place it accurately along the named route, and record the start, end and unit in the picture. Accept another accurate tool choice with defensible use. Hear one private explanation across all four products, during day 55 or 56. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error B for 20 cm after starting at 1 and error C for 20 cm from counting boundaries.

**s03-T-ft.** 6 ft. One suitable choice is a yardstick with foot marks. Choose a usable tool, place it accurately along the named route, and record the start, end and unit in the picture. Accept another accurate tool choice with defensible use. Hear one private explanation across all four products, during day 55 or 56. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label.

**s03-T-m.** 2 m. One suitable choice is a meter stick. Choose a usable tool, place it accurately along the named route, and record the start, end and unit in the picture. Accept another accurate tool choice with defensible use. Hear one private explanation across all four products, during day 55 or 56. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label.

**s03-T-in.** 17 in. One suitable choice is a flexible measuring tape. Choose a usable tool, place it accurately along the named route, and record the start, end and unit in the picture. Accept another accurate tool choice with defensible use. Hear one private explanation across all four products, during day 55 or 56. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error J for Measures straight across the curve.

**s03-P.** Readings 8, 11, 8, 10, 11 cm. Positions 8, 9, 10, 11 have 2, 0, 1, 2 marks. Verify the personally measured list before looking at the plot. Use consecutive even ticks, one mark per observation, a title and a unit. Extra empty end positions are fine. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error F for One mark per distinct reading and error E for Omits 9 on equal ticks.

**s03-C.** 24 inches and 2 feet. Each foot is longer, so fewer cover the unchanged strip. Save the forecast before giving out tools. Observe both measurements with the same endpoints. The picture and one reason show why feet need fewer units. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error D for More feet because a foot is larger.
