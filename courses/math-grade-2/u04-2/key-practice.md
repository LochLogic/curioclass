# Private practice key



Keep this copy with the teacher.

- Day 64. 51 to 58 adds 7; 58 to 51 removes 7. Practice moves 32 to 45 and 47 to 38.
- Day 65. The model goes 54 to 84 to 89. On the first sheet a single rightward jump of 35 reaches 89. The supplied second route shows 24 + 22 = 46.
- Day 66. The invented 7 cm and 84 cm strips differ by 77 cm. From 84 back 7 reaches 77. From 7 up to 84 travels 77. On the practice sheet, 82 − 9 = 73 and 9 + 73 = 82.
- Day 67. 60 + 17 = 77 along either pictured addition route. 72 − 18 = 54 along either pictured subtraction route.
