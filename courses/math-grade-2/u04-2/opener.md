# Operations and equations on the number line



Review the ruler-built whole-number line. Trace a move before adding its arrow. The tail starts, the tip finishes and the spaces between them give distance. When you subtract by counting back, the landing position is the difference. When you find a missing addend by counting up, the distance from the smaller number to the larger is the difference. Review the Labeled tape diagram, count back and count up, and connect the extra span to the square in an equation.

Keep equal-tick paper lines and pencil tapes handy. Use a repeated paper edge when there is no ruler. A pencil tip can replace a counter. Draw two aligned rectangles when there is no tape master. For day 63, use the Written partner check by a related calculation again. Keep locating and calculating on separate sheets. On day 65 compare spoken equations to completed arrows and review the equal sign before swapping sides. Keep the child's end-first drawing for the exit.

Cover spaces for error A. Overlay ten equal units for error G. For error E, mark the initial amount before adding. For error F, trace the increase toward larger numbers. For error C, put the smaller amount and gap together; for D bracket the distance between endpoints. Use private folders and give a fresh individual retry whenever a repair is needed.

In Before class name the private exit ticket key and the applicable practice and repair keys. Print the pictured exit master at half-letter size, two per Letter sheet. Read prompts aloud. Hear six per day in Practice at 45 seconds each plus 30 seconds for notes and switches. Continue the same roster and prioritize any child missed or absent. Give a private return to every child.
