# Private exit ticket key



Keep this key with the teacher. Read evidence before assigning a letter.

**s04-X63.** 98, two ten-unit moves past 78. Look for a mark at 98. Use any accompanying marks to distinguish a tens error, a starting-tick error or an unexplained miss.

Shown example. Uses the decade anchors and marks the tick two units left of 100.

Not yet seen example. Marks 80 and shows two single spaces from 78.

Possible errors. 80 (error G when the work shows changes two ones in place of two tens); 97 (error A when the work shows counts 78 as the first traveled unit).

**s04-X64.** Subtract 16 from 85 to reach 69. Look for a minus sign and a distance of 16 on the arc, or a clear equivalent removal label. Both direction and amount matter.

Shown example. Marks −16 on the leftward arc.

Not yet seen example. Marks +16 on the leftward arc.

Possible errors. +16 on the leftward arrow (error F when the work shows treats the decrease as an increase); Subtract 17 (error A when the work shows counts both endpoints as traveled spaces).

**s04-X65.** Start at 48 and jump right 29 spaces to 77, for example right 20 then right 9. Accept any correct rightward partition, either addend first, or a route from zero that builds both addends. The diagram must account for the whole sum.

Shown example. Draws 48 to 68 to 77 and labels the jumps 20 and 9.

Not yet seen example. Draws only from 0 to 29 and calls that the sum.

Possible errors. Only 0 to 29 (error E when the work shows leaves out the first addend); 48 to 19 (error F when the work shows uses a leftward move for the addend 29).

**s04-X66.** Up. Back is 91 to 3, traveling 88 spaces. Up is 88 to 91, traveling 3 spaces. The difference is 3. Read Back and Up. The child draws each route in its labeled panel and marks the shorter route. Accept partitioned moves whose distances agree with their scales.

Shown example. Draws a left arrow 91 to 3 labeled 88 and a right arrow 88 to 91 labeled 3, and marks Up.

Not yet seen example. Draws only the Back route and rules out the rightward route because the equation uses subtraction.

Possible errors. Back only; Up ruled out (error C when the work shows insists that a subtraction strategy must move left); 91 spaces on Up (error D when the work shows labels the gap with the larger endpoint).

**s04-X67.** Remove 23 starting at 87 and end at 64 by a different route, for example 87 to 84 to 64. Accept a different valid decomposition (including one jump) to the same result. Check places, direction and total distance.

Shown example. Draws left 3 to 84 and left 20 to 64.

Not yet seen example. Draws left 3 to 84 and then moves only two units left.

Possible errors. 84 then 82 (error G when the work shows treats two tens as two ones); Ends at 65 (error A when the work shows counts the starting 87 tick as the first of three spaces).
