# Length stories and measurement data

On days 49 and 50, briefly name the skilled silk sari weavers of Kanchipuram, Tamil Nadu, and the weavers at the Kalakshetra craft center in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. The district and the center’s own pages describe this work. The math pictures show invented paper textiles. If the background is unavailable, use only the invented scenes. Use plain paper and no dress-up or personal reports.

Review the labeled tape diagram with one known length hidden. Use the parts of the story to place the square. Then draw a new tape on blank paper and mark which action is first. Recall **Day 34 two peg actions** as a planned reminder. Show on a separate fresh case how the first result becomes the next start. Have pairs sort one-step and two-step textile prompts before an independent solution. On day 51 rehearse measured lengths and taught calculation strategies without a timer or blocks.

On day 52 recall a categorical bar graph on fresh classroom data, then put it away. Align a model strip at zero and read the nearer whole mark at its other end. Choose ends clearly nearer one mark. Transfer successive ruler marks to a line plot and match one paper X to each child's measurement. Keep empty ticks and stack repeated readings. Teach how the position names a length and the height counts measurements. On day 53 use blank grids and let children draw the consecutive positions. Preserve actual measurements even when revising the scale. Use checked paper tapes, tactile marks, cut paper Xs and a plain grid without a kit.

Use the scheduled returns. Shade spaces for error C, bracket only excess for error H, locate the unknown before solving for error K, carry both actions for error L and include any new ten for error M. Restore ticks for error E and give one mark per reading for error F. Read one cue aloud and let every child mark a picture. Hear six during the named part and date absences. In Before class list the private exit ticket key and the applicable textile, repair or calculation key.
