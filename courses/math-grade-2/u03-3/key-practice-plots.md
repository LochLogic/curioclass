# Private practice plot key

Day 52. The strips give 40, 40 and 42 cm. From 40 through 42, the counts are 2, 0 and 1. Give one mark to each measurement, keep 41 empty and include a title and centimeter label.

Day 53. The strips give 44, 46 and 46 cm. From 44 through 46, the counts are 1, 0 and 2. Keep 45 empty. Empty ticks at either end may also appear on an evenly spaced scale.
