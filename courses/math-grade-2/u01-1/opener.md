# Section 1.1 opener: Related facts and useful strategies

## The mathematical work

Review Partner turns and Return and repair from kindergarten. Start with the hook and
the nine grade 1 retrieval tasks. If the old facts record or dated work is available,
read the supports used and the next practice need. Otherwise file day 1’s marks for the
scheduled returns. Send the family note home on day 1.

A related fact uses the same two parts and total. Cover or remove one part on a paper
ten-frame and point to the other. Read equals as “is the same amount as.” Show the
part and whole for each equation before switching its sides. On day 3 use two frames
for totals within 20. Locate an unknown change in a single arrival or removal before
looking for its value.

Withdraw the supplied frames on day 4. Ask children to compare recall, counting on,
doubles, a nearby double and making a ten. A child who recalls a fact may say “I just
know.” For an addition with no recalled result, start from the amount already held and
count only the new ones. On day 5 bundle tens and loose ones before drawing groups.
On days 5 and 6 addition extends through 50. Keep subtraction and mixed facts within 20.

## If a first mark needs repair

For error A mark only the advances. For B use a known fact to find a nearby one.
For I compare equal quantities on both sides. For J cover the part being removed.
For F place the square in the missing part before choosing an operation. For G join
the parts to check the whole. Ask a child privately about the route before assigning a
letter. Keep the first mark and return it with the private cue on the named day.

## Before the section

Prepare the hook, retrieval pictures, ten-frames, number and part cards, route strip,
ten-cell strips, two-pile record and game strip. Fold wide paper scraps for cards,
rule equal boxes for frames and use large paper squares for counters. Keep pieces on
the table. Choose paired move cards and mixed-fact prompts in advance. Keep all
reserved checks, including related equations in other orders, off the practice deck.
Read each cue aloud and give each child a paper copy or a hand-drawn version.
