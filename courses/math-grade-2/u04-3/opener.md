# Strategies and stories on the number line



On day 68 review the two routes from section 4.2. Show a jump of ten on an equally spaced line and beside it on an open number line. The written jump label fixes the distance on the open sketch. Preserve equal tick spacing in assessment evidence. Review unknown, equal sign and the Labeled tape diagram. Use Cover one quantity in a labeled model again before children draw their own lines. On day 72 give pairs blank paper to draw their own tapes for partner testing.

Use the even and open pictures, covers, separate retrieval strips, sorting cards, practice lines and individual assessment packs on their named days. Use plain paper and a repeated edge to make ticks. Draw tapes as two aligned rectangles. Mark with a pencil tip when a pointer is unavailable. Adults prepare all loose pieces. A tabletop path provides the same moves as a floor path.

For errors A and B, rebuild equal intervals. For C and D, distinguish the endpoint from the traveled gap. For E and F, mark the start and trace the increase. For G, match a ten to ten unit intervals. For H, cover the unknown and check that the answer fits. For I, draw the two changes in order. Keep private first marks and revisions as Separate dated copies for retained work. Record F and Q maintenance separately from R, N, D and any S. Read or sign one cue at a time and record a child's directed marks if a scribe is needed.

In Before class name the private exit, assessment and applicable repair keys. Print the pictured exit master at half-letter size, two per Letter sheet. Hear six per day in Practice, Partner testing or familiar Facts, with 45 seconds each and 30 seconds for switches. Log who was heard and continue through any names missed. After day 73, score the dated evidence and organize the two response days using the record.
