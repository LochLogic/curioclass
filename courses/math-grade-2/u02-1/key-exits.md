# Exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-X20.** 30 + 7 = 37 open holes.
  Marks nine tens less six tens and eight ones less one one, leaving three tens and seven ones.
  Examples: Crosses out 6 tens and 1 one, labels 37. / Writes 37 with no place-value marks.
  Error D. 159. Adds the covered holes to the whole.
  Error H. 37 because I subtracted. Names a procedure without linking it to tens or ones.

- **s02-X21.** 89 − 56 = 33. The same unknown can be shown by 56 + □ = 89 with □ = 33.
  Marks the missing part and gives an equation that identifies and solves it.
  Examples: Marks the open space and writes 89 − 56 = 33. / Labels 145 as the missing part.
  Error D. 145. Joins a known part to its whole.
  Error I. 34 missing, with 56 and 34 labeled as 89. The marked parts actually fill 90.

- **s02-X22.** □ − 32 = 45 with □ = 77, or 45 + 32 = 77 with 77 labeled as the start.
  Marks the restored start and matches it to a correct equation.
  Examples: Marks 45 left and 32 removed, writes □ − 32 = 45 and marks 77 at first. / Subtracts 32 from the end and labels 13 as the start.
  Error D. 13. Treats the end count as the start.
  Error I. 76 as the start, marked as 45 and 32. The two parts actually total 77.

- **s02-X23.** 50 + 30 = 80 and 4 + 2 = 6, so 86 lines.
  Shows a valid written strategy for the combined count and labels the total.
  Examples: Records 8 tens and 6 ones, then writes 86. / Writes 86 without a strategy.
  Error D. 22. Finds the difference instead of the whole.
  Error H. 86, I added. Gives no value reason for the total.
