# Two ways to add key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-P20-explain.** Each way combines 30 and 20 to get 50 and 4 and 5 to get 9, for 59. The order leaves the parts unchanged.
  Points to or marks the same tens and ones in both records. Treat naming alone as a vocabulary cue and accept matching marks.
  Examples: Matches both tens groups and both ones groups and explains they stayed the same. / Both are 59 because I added.
