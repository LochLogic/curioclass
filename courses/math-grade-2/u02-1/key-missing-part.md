# Missing part practice key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-P21-check.** The 41 removed and the 26 left fill the original 67, so 41 + 26 = 67.
  Relates the two parts to the whole with a drawing or equality and an indication that the whole is restored.
  Examples: Marks 41 and 26 in the original outline with 67 on the whole. / Labels 26 but leaves the whole unmarked.

- **s02-P21-1.** 62 − 31 = 31. Check: 31 + 31 = 62.

- **s02-P21-2.** 78 − 36 = 42. Check: 36 + 42 = 78.

- **s02-P21-3.** 68 − 46 = 22. Check: 46 + 22 = 68.
