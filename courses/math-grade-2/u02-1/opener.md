# Section 2.1 opener: Tens, ones and missing parts

## The mathematics

Review tens, ones, total, difference and the square for an unknown. Match ten paper
ones to one rod. When no exchange is needed, join or remove like units. Show why the
same groups make the same sum in either order. On day 21 find a missing part by
subtracting the known part from the whole. Check by joining both parts again. Review
the tape diagram on day 22 and match each length to the quantity it shows. Act the
story first, then cover one label and use a square for that amount. Guide the
transition from pieces to pictures to equations. Put the supplied pieces away for
written work on day 23.

## Returns and preparation

Send the family note home on day 20. Review the survey and Keep first paper marks and
date actual hearings from Unit 1. Return the dated originals in two rounds, one
move per round. Start with fresh paper evidence if the previous record is missing.
Use the retrieval marks as an opening review. Label whole and part for error D.
For error H connect a mark to its value. For error I rejoin the drawn parts inside
the old whole. Use Return and repair and follow the private roster.

Set out the hook, arrow votes, rods, ones, ten-frame, retrieval papers, seal sequence,
practice tape paper, mixed cards, exit tickets and private keys. Rule a ten-frame as
two rows of five, draw rods and equal unit marks, or copy the cards by hand. Keep
paper on the table. Read a cue at a time. Offer larger marks, tactile outlines or neutral
scribing and record the help.
