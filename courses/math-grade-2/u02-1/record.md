# Unit 2 evidence record

Use shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Save the first paper mark before any
feedback. Record the support and date each actual hearing. Give a child who passes
another private turn. Use error letters only when a mark or explanation shows the
method. Keep all cues, revisions and support beside the dated original.

## Class rotation

Hear six children in five minutes on an ordinary teaching day. Each turn gets
45 seconds, with 30 seconds total to switch and date the names. Put missed names
first on the next roster. On days 36 and 37 the separate rotation gives each
child one closing conference. Schedule any missed closing turn during later
independent work.
