# Exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s01-X12a.** 0, or a picture matching every cell with no extra.
  Error E. Joins both equal bars.
  Error D. Starts the narrow bar at 1 and the wide bar at 0.
- **s01-X12b.** 10 - 10 = 0 or 0 = 10 - 10.
  The equation uses both bar lengths and shows that their difference is 0.
  Error E. Uses the joined total as the comparison.
- **s01-X13.** Cone tape 16, hoop tape 5, same start; square on the 11-unit cone gap. The square is sufficient without 11 written.
  Both tapes have a common start and appropriate relative lengths. names and given lengths match the report. square is on the cone extension
  Error E. Joins the two bars to show extra.
  Error F. Uses the given numbers without locating the missing span.
- **s01-X14.** Mark all of Paz’s shorter tape on A, the unknown 11 - 2 = 9. No numeral is required.
  Marks Paz’s entire shorter tape on A; a card letter alone does not locate the missing amount.
  Error K. Attaches the extra to the wrong owner, making Paz larger.
  Error F. Fills a gap without locating the requested whole shorter tape.
- **s01-X15.** 17, represented by a starting tape made of 2 lost and 15 left.
  Identifies 17 as the start. labels or clearly distinguishes the removed 2 and the remaining 15
  Error F. Subtracts the two given numbers instead of locating the starting whole.
  Error A. Counts 15 as the first of two advances.
- **s01-X16a.** Bea.
  Marks Bea as the larger owner.
  Error K. Assigns the extra five to Ivo.
- **s01-X16b.** 17
  Error H. Uses fewer as an instruction to subtract with the larger owner correctly named.
