# Section 2.4 opener: Two steps and closing evidence

## The mathematics

Review the tape diagram with the child drawing the quantities. Say first and then
as a learner marks each action. The first result becomes the starting amount
for the next step. A question about the end needs the actions that change that
amount. Check by reversing or recombining the changes. In Partner turns, an author
describes one actor's scene, and the partner draws or labels the quantities before
checking both steps.

## Returns and preparation

For error F mark the second action on the first drawing. For errors A through C
review place value with one fresh case. For errors D and E name the owner and
relationship. For G track every count. For H connect a reason to a drawing or
equality. For I rejoin both parts inside the original outline.

Set out the peg pictures, blank action spaces, partner cards, mixed stories and
private ticket keys. Draw strips or boxes on paper if needed. Keep the addition
explanation, subtraction explanation, inventory and star tray on separate pages.
Read each cue aloud. Two minutes per private conference gives time for 15 children
on each observation day. Collect every child's two paper explanations, then keep
the originals beside their later revisions. Schedule any missed conference and
record the actual date.

On day 38 read the short stories one at a time. Use only bare numerals for the
independent calculation sample. File the earlier products and their revisions
without repeating the conference. Use the short marking copy after work is saved.
Keep the other keys with the teacher. Send each child one private cue into the
scheduled response rounds on days 39 and 40.
