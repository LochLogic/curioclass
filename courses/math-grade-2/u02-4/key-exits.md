# Exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-X34.** 83 − 7 = 76, then 76 − 19 = 57. Check: 57 + 19 + 7 = 83. Combining removals first also works.
  The plan accounts for both removals. The check restores the initial quantity.
  Examples: Marks both removals and verifies 57 + 19 + 7 = 83. / Marks only the flag removal and writes 76.
  Error F. 76. Stops at the result of the first removal.
  Error B. 63. Reverses the ones on the second removal.

- **s02-X35.** One actor starts with 32, removes 7 to leave 25 and adds 4 to end with 29.
  A paper story or labeled picture uses one actor and both changes, ending with 29. Neutral scribing may record the child’s response.
  Examples: I set 32 flags out, take in 7, then add 4. Now 29 flags are out. / I set out 32 flags, took 7 in, and had 25 left.
  Error F. 25 as the ending count. Uses only the removal.
  Error C. 39. Keeps three tens beside twelve ones before subtracting.

- **s02-X36.** Show 55 shared beats and 18 extra beats on the longer strip, for 73 beats.
  Marks the long tune as the larger count; the shared and extra amounts and the total agree.
  Examples: Draws strips labeled 55 shared and 18 extra and marks 73 at the longer end. / Labels the long tune 37 by subtracting 18 from 55.
  Error E. 37. Uses fewer as a cue to subtract.
  Error A. 63. Drops the new ten from the combined ones.

- **s02-X37.** 56 + 7 = 63 and 63 + 21 = 84. Grouping 7 + 21 = 28 first also works.
  Marks both arrivals and accounts for every dot in a plan with the correct final count.
  Examples: Marks 7 and 21 added to 56 and labels 84. / Marks only the arrival of 7 and labels 63.
  Error F. 63. Stops after the first arrival.
  Error A. 74. Drops a composed ten on the first arrival.
