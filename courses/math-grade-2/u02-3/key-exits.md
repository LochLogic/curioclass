# Exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-X29.** 46 + 54 = 100 and 27 + 38 = 65. Whole-plate label: 165. Other valid groups fit.
  Uses each of the four row counts once, shows a grouping and writes the correct total by the plate.
  Examples: Marks 46 with 54 and 27 with 38, then writes 165. / Groups only three rows and labels the plate 127.
  Error G. 127. Omits the row of 38.
  Error A. 155. Drops a newly composed ten from 27 + 38.

- **s02-X30.** 17 + 43 and 28 + 42 make 60 + 70 = 130. 17 + 28 and 43 + 42 make 45 + 85 = 130.
  Makes two distinct pairings, shows each addend once in each, and shows both totals are equal.
  Examples: Circles the two pairs in each record and calculates 130 both times. / Writes 130 in both spaces with no pairs.
  Error G. 88. Omits 42.
  Error H. Both 130 because I added. Does not show how the same addends make both totals.

- **s02-X31.** Choose the split tape and correct exchange: 41 = 30 + 11 and 41 − 17 = 24.
  Error D. 58 card. Joins the whole and a part. A choice alone does not diagnose the error.
  Error C. 34 card. Keeps four tens beside eleven ones. Confirm the child’s reasoning privately.

- **s02-X32.** The strip with 42 and 45, since 42 + 45 = 87.
  Error I. 42 and 46 strip. Accepts parts making 88. A choice alone does not diagnose the method.
  Error I. 41 and 45 strip. Accepts parts making 86. A choice alone does not diagnose the method.

- **s02-X33.** □ right + 38 extra = 92 left, so there are 54 notches on the right.
  Labels the smaller amount, the extra and the larger amount, with a correct equation and value.
  Examples: Marks 54 right + 38 extra = 92 left. / Uses 92 + 38 to find the right count.
  Error E. 130. Uses more as a cue to add.
  Error B. 66. Reverses the ones.
  Error C. 64. Keeps nine tens after exchange.
