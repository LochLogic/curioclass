# Covered tape and comparison key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-P32-covered.** 22, since 51 + 22 = 73.

- **s02-P32-more.** 35 more; 24 + □ = 59.

- **s02-P32-fewer.** 31 fewer; 41 + □ = 72.

For the open 73 strip the allowed pairs are 49 and 24, 50 and 23, 51 and 22, or 52 and 21. In the 51 and 22 comparison the gap is 29.
