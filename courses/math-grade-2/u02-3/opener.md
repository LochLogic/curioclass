# Section 2.3 opener: Flexible calculation and story diagrams

## The mathematics

Review accurate written methods and the four-addend grouping from day 27. Change
the order or the grouping and account for every original number. Use untimed facts
and written rehearsal. During independent fluency work, children may make their own
marks. Record accuracy and visible efficiency separately.

Review the tape diagram. For part and whole, fit the two lengths together. For
comparison, align the starting ends so the exposed length shows the extra.
Use more and fewer to name the relationship. A smaller amount plus the extra
equals the larger amount. Use the owner labels and the square before choosing
how to calculate. Compare opposite wordings with fresh quantities.

## Returns and preparation

Return error A to ten ones circled and transferred to the tens. For error G tick
each addend once. For error H connect a mark to its value. For errors D and E name
what each part or owner has. For error I rejoin the known parts inside the whole.
For errors B and C draw the original amount and cancel the traded ten before
removing anything. Use Return and repair and date the actual private turn.

Prepare screened cards, written practice, the sorted sheets, covered tape, separate
comparison slips, quantity labels, tickets and private keys. Draw proportional
strips and large blanks on scrap if needed. Withdraw supplied teaching tapes after
day 33. Read all captions aloud.
