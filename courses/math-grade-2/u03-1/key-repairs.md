# Private repair and recheck key

Show the model first. Cover it during the fresh try. Use one move per child per round. Use shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Enter the date of the next turn if a child needs another.

- **Error A.** Move the work row together. The new row needs all its seams touching and both ends covered. Fresh check. Cut fresh edges for six and eight copies of a new unit. In each, show full coverage with all seams touching.
- **Error B.** First mark the zero at the object's beginning. On the fresh edge, place the object at zero and show the ending mark. Fresh check. Give new verified markers and paper edges of 8 and 14 cm. Zero aligns at the start and the end reads 8 cm or 14 cm.
- **Error C.** Worked panel has 7 spaces. Recheck has 9 spaces, bounded by 10 marks. Fresh check. 11 and 15 spaces. There are 12 and 16 boundary marks.
- **Error D.** The model has 8 short spans or 4 long spans. The new row takes 5 long spans. Endpoints match. Fresh check. Give wide units that each span two narrow ones. The full covers take six and eight wide pieces.
- **Error E.** The fresh blank tick labels are 26 and 28. Equal intervals keep one place for each length. Fresh check. The blank positions are 16 and 18, or 36 and 37.
- **Error F.** In the model there are two separate marks at 12. The fresh plot has two marks at 45 and one at 48. Fresh check. First case gives one X at 49 and two at 50. Next case gives one at 65 and two at 67.
- **Error G.** The recheck shows a joined 61 cm, from 36 + 25. There is no overlap. Fresh check. The totals are 61 cm and 55 cm. Each bracket covers both parts.
- **Error H.** The fresh bracket begins where the short row ends and stops where the long row ends. Fresh check. The uncovered parts are 11 cm and 24 cm. Bracket from the shorter end to the longer end.
- **Error I.** From the same start the verified inch must extend beyond the centimeter. Fresh check. Give different checked inch and centimeter stock. An inch always reaches past a centimeter when both start at the same place.
- **Error J.** Tape follows the full second curve rather than crossing its opening. Fresh check. The flexible paper tape follows the named route all the way. A straight chord misses part of the route.
- **Error K.** 69 − □ = 47 gives 22 cm in the model. 67 − □ = 43 gives 24 cm in the recheck. Fresh check. 82 − □ = 65 gives 17 cm. 72 − □ = 33 gives 39 cm. The square marks the cut.
- **Error L.** Model: 78 − 16 = 62, then 62 − 21 = 41. Recheck: 88 − 35 = 53, then 53 − 16 = 37. Fresh check. First reach 66, then 54 cm. In the next case first reach 61, then 49 cm.
- **Error M.** 23 + 29 = 52 and 54 + 27 = 81. Each sum includes one composed ten. Fresh check. 48 + 27 = 75 and 56 + 29 = 85. Include the new ten.
