# Private fact observation card

Adult copy. Use days 17 and 18 with different children. Wait for each oral reply before the next cue. Spend 60 seconds on the six bare answers, about 40 seconds on one method and about 20 seconds to log and switch.

What is 7 + 1?

What is 6 + 6?

What is 9 + 5?

What is 16 - 9?

What is 20 - 8?

What is 12 - 6?

How did you find that one?

Record the selected fact and the method actually heard. Keep the first response and any support separate.

## Teacher key

- F1. 8
- F2. 12
- F3. 14
- F4. 7
- F5. 12
- F6. 6
- F7. Accept recall or a coherent mental method for the selected saved fact. Record accuracy and method separately.
