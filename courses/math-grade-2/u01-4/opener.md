# Section 1.4 opener: Applying ideas and gathering evidence

## The mathematical work

Review Counting collections and the anonymous survey. In the grouping practice,
circling fives or tens and tracking leftover singles checks that every object is
accounted for. Keep this evidence separate from the private facts sample. Hear the
first half of the roster on day 17 and the other half on day 18. Use the same adult card
and wait for each reply. Save initial accuracy, the method actually heard, help used
and later changes separately. Only the initial unaided recall enters the sum inventory.

The day 18 ballot asks about classroom drawing marks. Each group tallies its unsigned
votes and each child makes a picture graph and a bar graph. Read the three arithmetic
questions separately. The tally is the teacher’s key. Recount the slips if the tally goes
missing. If both are lost, collect a fresh anonymous ballot before scoring the graph work.

Day 19 returns F and G with written feedback. Give seven shared minutes to revise and
keep the originals. Then read one new prompt at a time and provide room for paper
marks. In the tape task, look for the common start, the extra span on the larger tape
and a reason using the quantities. In the related-fact task, ask how one subtraction
keeps the same parts and whole. Preserve each first answer before the next cue.

## If a first mark needs repair

Use the register and the recorded method. Have a child mark revisions in a separate
space beside dated feedback. Record each judgment as shown, shown with help or not
yet seen. Return E17 on day 18 and E18 on day 19. Send the original, feedback and pending
private turns to day 20, with the next repair rounds noted for days 20 and 21.

## Before the section

Prepare the practice collection record, unsigned slips, tally, two survey graph
forms, private facts card and individual story pictures. Use large paper squares,
handwritten ballots and blank equal-cell grids if the print pieces are unavailable.
One shared screen is optional. At home, shuffle invented response slips for the same
four choices, tally and mark both graphs. Keep the private facts card with the adult.
