# Private exit key

### s05-X84

895 < 902 becomes 902 < 905. Ten tens form a new hundred. Look for coherent before and after rows and a trade mark or unit words on the arrow.

805 < 902 after the change. Error G. Cycles the tens without exchanging a hundred. Puts 895 after 902 before the change. Error E. Uses the tens first.

### s05-X85

618 > 589, with six hundreds and the 5 in 589 marked. Look for a correct direction and visible evidence of the first different place. A matching notation or directed mark counts.

Chooses 589 as greater and points to its tens. Error E. Compares tens before unequal hundreds. Names the drawn amount greater but writes <. Error F. Reverses the comparison sign.

### s05-X86

796 < 802 < 814 becomes 802 < 806 < 814. The extra ten completes a hundred. Look for the replacement card inserted at its correct new position, with a trade mark or words.

Leaves 706 first after the change. Error G. Cycles the tens without forming a hundred. Writes 802 > 806 > 814 on the final row. Error F. Orders correctly but reverses signs.

### s05-X87

692 < 727 < 772, with hundreds marked for the first link and tens for the second. Look for correctly placed labels, correct signs and first-difference marks for each link independently.

Puts 692 last and marks its nine tens. Error E. Uses tens before unequal hundreds. Uses greater-than signs in the correct ascending row. Error F. Reverses the signs after ordering correctly.

### s05-X88

230; three tens or six fives added to two sealed hundreds. Look for equal groups only in the loose part and a correct total. Accept drawn jumps.

23 Error C. Loses the empty ones place. 32 Error B. Treats two hundreds as two ones.
