# Comparing and changing quantities

Review greater than, less than, equal to, ten more and ten less from Grade 1. Recall ordering from kindergarten. When hundreds differ, the lower places cannot overturn their comparison. Compare tens only if hundreds tie and ones only if both higher places tie. A sign faces the greater amount on its wide side. Ten more can bundle ten tens into a hundred. Ten less can open a hundred into tens. A change of one hundred preserves the tens and ones. Model both crossings with fitted pieces on day 84. Withdraw supplied drawings and lines for routine work on day 85 and let children sketch after answering.

Keep the targets silent during the number-name check.
