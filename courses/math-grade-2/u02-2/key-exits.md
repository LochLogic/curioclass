# Exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher.

- **s02-X24.** 71 = 60 + 11. Cross out 8 ones to leave 63.
  Shows one ten replaced by ten ones before crossing off 8; preserves the whole before removing.
  Examples: Shows 6 rods and 11 dots, crosses off 8 dots, and marks 63. / Crosses off a dot from 8 and reports 77.
  Error B. 77. Reverses the ones subtraction.
  Error C. 73. Keeps the old ten when ten ones appear.

- **s02-X25.** On 92, open a ten. Draw 8 tens and 12 ones, then cross off 5 of the ones. The 85 strip already has 5 loose ones.
  Marks the trade only on 92; the mark shows 8 tens and 12 ones before removing 5.
  Examples: On the 92 strip crosses one rod and adds ten dots to the two there. / Circles 92 but keeps all nine tens when adding the ten dots.
  Error B. Neither strip, 5 minus 2 is 3. Reverses the ones instead of exchanging.
  Error C. 92 strip with 9 tens and 12 ones, leaving 97. Keeps the traded ten.

- **s02-X26.** 86 = 70 + 16; remove 40 and 9, leaving 30 + 7 = 37.
  Matches the ten in the picture to 10 added ones and one fewer ten in the calculation.
  Examples: Writes 86 = 70 + 16 and 30 + 7 = 37 after removal. / Writes 86 = 80 + 16.
  Error B. 43. Subtracts smaller ones from larger ones.
  Error C. 47. Keeps the traded ten.

- **s02-X27.** 90 = 80 + 10. Remove 5 tens and 4 ones, leaving 36.
  Starts with no loose ones, opens one ten and removes the stated tens and ones.
  Examples: Draws 8 tens and 10 ones after exchange, then crosses off 5 tens and 4 ones. / Removes 5 tens but writes 44 from 4 minus 0.
  Error B. 44. Subtracts 0 from 4 in the ones.
  Error C. 46. Keeps nine tens during exchange.

- **s02-X28.** For 74 − 39, the first trades in 74 = 60 + 14. The second takes 30 first and trades in 44 = 30 + 14. Both leave 35.
  Marks one ten becoming ten ones in each note; the timing of the two trades is visible.
  Examples: Marks 60 + 14 before removing in the first and 30 + 14 after removing 30 in the second. / Writes 35 twice without showing either trade.
  Error H. 35 on both notes. Gives the result without the value link.
  Error C. 45 after renaming 44 as 40 + 14. Keeps the traded ten in the second note.
