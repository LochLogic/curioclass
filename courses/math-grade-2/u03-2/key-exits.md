# Private exit ticket key

Keep this copy with the teacher. Use only the entry for the current ticket. Assign a letter only when the paper or explanation shows its reasoning.

**s03-X44.** 42 + □ = 73 or 73 − 42 = □, with □ = 31 cm. Locate the uncovered span on the picture or a matching tape. Put the square at the extension. Check the unit. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error H for 73 cm and error C for 32 cm.

**s03-X45-estimate.** About 30 cm, using about three spans of the 10 cm reference. Save the forecast and its benchmark marks before the ruler is uncovered. Accept a defensible approximation. A number changed after measuring earns no estimate credit. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error A for About 20 cm from two spaced reference copies and error C for About 40 cm from counting four boundary marks.

**s03-X45.** 87 cm. The extension measures 28 cm. 59 + 28 = 87. Uncover the ruler only after s03-X45-estimate is saved. Look for a correct tool reading and the total across both pieces. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error G for 31 cm and error M for 77 cm.

**s03-X46.** About 13 inches, using the inch reference on both impressions. Repeat the inch benchmark along each of the two impressions and account for the total. Accept a defensible approximation. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error I for About 33 inches from centimeter spaces and error A for About 10 widely spaced inches.

**s03-X47.** The 84-mark record counts inches. Shorter units take more copies to cover the same ramp. Mark the inch tally and show how small and large spans cover the same endpoints. The count difference needs a unit-size reason. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error D for 84 marks count feet because feet are bigger and error A for Fewer feet because there are gaps.

**s03-X48.** 43 cm. 76 − 33 = 43. Mark the span between the two exposed endpoints and label the difference. Record the support given. Accept a clear drawing or a scribed label. Watch for error H for 76 cm and error C for 44 cm.
