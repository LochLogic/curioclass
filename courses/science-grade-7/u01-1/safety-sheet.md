# Year safety sheet (days 1 and 3)

1. Splash goggles on before anything is opened, and on until everything is cleaned up. Goggles sit over the eyes, not on the head.
2. Never taste or sniff what is in the lab, and keep hands and powders away from your face. Citric acid dust and splashes irritate eyes.
3. Hair tied back. Sit at the set distance during a teacher-only demonstration.
4. Wipe spills at once and tell the teacher. Waste goes where the teacher collects it.
5. Wash hands with soap after every lab.
6. Teacher-only: the first demonstration, anything closed in a container, and anything that heats. Why: these are the steps where splashes and pressure can surprise us. Students mix only the named household powders, in open cups, when the teacher says to begin.
7. Write one rule in your own words and what it protects.
