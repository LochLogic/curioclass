# Day 3: Safety rehearsal and tests that could count against us

## Teacher page

### Before class

Clean and dry 30 pairs of goggles and set one at each seat. Between periods the first row wipes the goggles (wipes, or a soap and water tub; dry before storing). Print the safety scenario sheets. Mark day 2's exits for errors C and D. Head the class chart of observations "Supports" and "Counts against".

### Safety and set-up

No mixing and no chemicals out today. Each student fits and removes their own goggles without touching the face, and the teacher checks seal, strap and hair. 2 minutes of the day clean and store them. Today is the full fitting; day 1 had a quick check.

### The day

#### 5 min. Goggle fitting

Fit in rows of 10, at about 10 seconds a student, while the other rows reread the safety sheet. Check the seal, the strap and tied-back hair. Show taking them off by the strap. Goggles go over glasses.

#### 9 min. Scenario cards

Each pair takes one scene, not five, and writes it up as [[s01-P3-1]]: what is wrong and what to do. The pairs share the other scenes in the next part.

**Ask.** What is wrong in your scene, and what is the first thing to do? Expect: Scene 1 goggles over the eyes first; Scene 2 stand back and watch from the set distance; Scene 3 wipe it up and tell the teacher; Scene 4 no sniffing, say what you see; Scene 5 nothing is closed in a container, and only the teacher decides.

#### 5 min. Share

Each pair gives one rule it used. Any student may pass. No one is named as the example of a mistake.

#### 4 min. Teacher-only boundaries

Say what stays teacher-only and where students stand: first demonstrations, anything closed in a container and anything that heats. Rehearse the stop signal once.

#### 2 min. Clean and store goggles

Students wipe their goggles as shown and store them where the teacher says.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 2's exits (s01-X2). Error C, 3 minutes: [[s01-P3-2]], an open-cup record beside a closed-pouch record. **Ask.** Where did cup A's missing grams go, and why did pouch B not change? Expect: the 0.4 g left cup A with the gas; pouch B kept its gas inside. Say aloud that these numbers are invented. Error D, 2 minutes: each student counts the particles in their day 2 drawing, inside the cup before, inside after and outside after. The totals should match.

#### 4 min. Teaching first

A testable question names what you would observe or measure and a result that would change your mind. Model [[s01-K3-1]]: use the same powders in 25 mL and 50 mL of water and measure how long bubbles keep rising; if both last about the same time, more water does not matter. Plan only: nobody mixes. A plan with no result named, "try it and see", gets the question: what result would change your mind?

#### 9 min. Groups

Groups of three take the day 1 explanations in turn: the gas was already in the powders; the gas came from the water; the gas formed when the powders met. Each student writes [[s01-P3-3]] on one explanation, and the group fills the table.

**Ask.** What would you need to see to change your mind about your explanation? Expect: an observation that counts against it. Bubbles alone do not prove any explanation (error E): an observation that all three explanations predict cannot choose between them.

#### 4 min. Class chart

Merge the tables on one chart under Supports and Counts against. The chart stays up for day 4.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

[[s01-X3]], alone, on paper. The student page stays in the notebook.

### Other rooms

Without enough goggles for everyone, run the scenario sheets and the planning, and do not hold a fitting. Without a printer, read the scenes aloud and students write on notebook paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No goggles: skip the fitting and read the safety sheet's rule 1 aloud. No printer: read the scenes aloud.

**Skip.** Fitting, pairs, and the class chart.

- **8 min. Scenes.** Read the safety sheet, then do [[s01-P3-1]].
  **Say.** "What is wrong, and what do you do first?" Expect: name the fault and one safe step.
- **6 min. Repair.** Do [[s01-P3-2]]; say the numbers are invented.
- **6 min. Testable.** Try [[s01-K3-1]], then read its answer.
- **10 min. Explanations.** Do [[s01-P3-3]].
  **Say.** "What would count against it?" Expect: something seen that the idea says should not happen.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two scenes, not five. Never cut the goggle fitting, the repair, the result that counts against or the exit. Longer: write the same table for a second explanation.

### Words and supports

Test: something you do or measure to check an idea. Count against: show an idea is less likely to be right. Frame: "If ___ were true, we would see ___; if we see ___ instead, it counts against it." Read the scenes aloud. Students may answer orally.

### Tomorrow

Error E on [[s01-X3]], bubbles proving an explanation, opens day 4: sort four supplied bubble cases. A test with no result named gets a written comment. Day 4 builds three competing models with the day 3 chart and the day 1 posted explanations, begins the question board, and has each student write one claim with evidence and reasoning.
