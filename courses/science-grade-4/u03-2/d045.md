# Day 45: Watch two directions of local motion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print today's fields only; cover Side 2. Read teacher-key.md and today's generated item key privately. Adult inspects coil ends, rope and grips; use the maker's printed stretch limit. If unavailable, use fallback C instead of stretching. Adult wears goggles and holds both ends at floor level. Mark a clear recoil and trip zone. Prepare correct strips and day 44 notes. No child handles either apparatus.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 44 s03-X44, s03-P44-labels and s03-P44-travel. Review local motion, five minutes for error A, using a familiar held point rather than a paired repair diagram. Review independent height and spacing, five minutes for error B, using correct teaching feature strips. If many, more than ten, show an error, lead that move; ready children label correct strips. Keep the cork question open on the question board and pattern chart.

#### 15 min. Coil and rope observation

Two seated groups of fifteen get four-minute demonstrations each, eight minutes total. Adult makes repeating sideways floor-rope waves, then lengthwise coil disturbances. Marked points stay near fixed floor marks; bunches pass along the coil. Observers remain outside recoil reach; never release ends or wrap apparatus around anyone. Stop for damage or slipping grip.

Review day 44's rope analogy. A floor-rope point moves across the overall direction; a coil point moves along it, back and forth. Keep amplitude and wavelength labels on rope or water, not bunches. Retry blurred motion once within eight minutes. Then announce fallback C is invented for practice, not observed evidence. Seven minutes remain for everyone to complete [[s03-P45-motion]], using words, not paired explanatory drawings. Preserve unclear actual readings with date, collector and conditions.

#### 10 min. Evidence sort

Review wave energy from day 42. Point separately at across, along and onward direction words in individual records. **Ask.** [[s03-P45-energy]]. Expect energy and pattern pass along; marked material moves nearby. Everyone writes. Hear rotating six at 45 seconds each within five minutes; passing is allowed. Use correct isolated statements during practice. An arrow models a direction, not an observed moving object. Record unresolved motion honestly.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult releases tension slowly, stores apparatus and washes, two minutes. Dry observers file and clear places, three. The clear floor zone stays empty until storage ends.

#### 5 min. Exit E45

Reveal Side 2 now; records are invented for practice. Each child completes [[s03-X45]] independently on a half slip. No rehearsal of the paired diagram. Collect all thirty. After class allow 30 × 25 seconds, 12.5 minutes for exit marking; 30 × 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes practice notes. Mark components shown, shown with help, or not yet seen, one formative scale.

### Other rooms

No kit: use fallback C and familiar correct rope features, explicitly weaker supplied evidence. No printer: copy direction fields first and reserved frames only at exit. A board replaces a projector.

### At home

**Swap.** Without inspected equipment and its stretch limit, use invented fallback C and correct rope strips. Copy fields without printing. Point at paper without a projector.

**Skip.** Group viewing and public sharing.

- **10 min. Repair.** Read day 44 notes; review nearby motion and separate height from spacing.
- **10 min. Observe.** Adult alone demonstrates safe rope and coil, or reads supplied records. Do [[s03-P45-motion]].
- **10 min. Explain.** Do [[s03-P45-energy]], then clear apparatus.
  **Say.** "What travels along the materials?" Expect: energy and pattern; the marked material stays nearby.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the two reserved records. Give [[s03-X45]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce public sharing, retaining observations and exit. Extend by naming one limit of using a coil to model a wave.

### Words and supports

Review local, pattern, energy and evidence. Along follows the apparatus; across goes side to side. Read neutrally; a scribe preserves reasoning. No wave-kind names are needed.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 returns s03-X45 and s03-P45-motion with notes, plus s03-P45-energy. If error A carries material onward, its five-minute Return and repair points again to a familiar coil marker. Day 46 then gathers independent model evidence without showing its solution.
