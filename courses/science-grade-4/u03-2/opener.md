# Section 3.2: Testing wave models

Days 45 to 47 use tagged rope and coil observations to distinguish material motion from pattern/energy travel. Lengthwise coil movement is an analogy for back-and-forth sound motion, never air crests. Keep height and spacing comparisons qualitative and on rope/water. Developing and revising models joins observed patterns to the wave idea.

Only the adult handles apparatus, on the floor within a clear recoil/trip zone, wearing goggles. Inspect ends and use the maker's printed safe stretch limit. Stop for slipping grip, damage or blurred motion. Two four-minute demonstrations use eight of fifteen minutes. Retry once inside those eight; use fallback C if still unclear. Seven minutes remain for individual direction records. Cleanup: adult releases tension/stores/washes two minutes; observers file three. Rope and coil are never wrapped around people.

The optional wave illustration is unavailable. Correct single-feature paper uses the same five minutes on day 46. Print motion-record.md, coil-fallback.md, feature-matching.md, portfolio-wave.md, portfolio-feedback.md, evidence-rehearsal.md and reserved exits. Day 46 fabric/bird evidence is independent and held out of all teaching. Give component-only feedback, revise day 47, first score day 55.

Day 47 retains the required kind; its revision activity is a review point. Never teach the pool/ring, rubber-arrow or fabric/bird responses in advance.

Every day lasts 45 minutes. Each child keeps individual paper evidence. Read directions neutrally; a scribe may record the child's own reasoning. Public talk is optional. Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each within five minutes. Partner turns take 45 seconds each, not thirty serial turns. All papers, including those of children not heard, receive written component feedback on the named hand-back day. Many means more than ten of thirty.

Teach from observations before naming explanations. Label and announce every supplied record: invented for practice. Keep actual observations with collector, date and conditions; mark unclear readings. Retry once inside the existing observation slot, then use the named fallback separately. No simulation or paper record proves practical performance.

Before class, print only that day's student pages, its reserved exit sheet, and the teacher-only key. Keep future exits and portfolio transfer cases sealed. Never project the teacher-only key. Packets use 14 point type and at most 350 student words a day, including questions. Exits use half sheets. Day 50 and day 54 have two judged exit rows; other exits each ask one task with at most two parts.

Use the formative record's three categories. Preserve originals and second-color revisions. Assessment points go only on assessment-record.md. No public grading or child used as a mistake example.
