# Day 60: Repeat the slope comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest stable gentle and steep trays with matched damp sand. Check allergies and protection. Mark each sediment front and fix rulers outside wet edges, aligned along travel. Arrange dry reading positions, catch bucket and cleaning supplies. Number six groups of five. Integrate trial-sheet.md; conceal exit-60.md prompts. Keep teacher-key.md private. Prepare day 59 feedback for every child.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 59's written tasks (s04-P59-response, s04-P59-plan and s04-P59-baseline), half slip s04-X59-plan and Exit box s04-X59-start with feedback. If many show error C, review fair test from day 52: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Identify matched water, sand, moisture and pouring on their own plans. Review baseline from day 59; point to each actual initial record. Repair missing labels within this slot, without rehearing the exit cases. Children ready to proceed label today's record.

#### 24 min. Tray turns

Review erosion from grade 2 and ruler reading from day 3. Read whole-centimeter marks; travel is end minus start. Six groups take four minutes each. Adult alone handles wet apparatus. Each turn: 45 seconds pouring equal cupfuls at the same marked positions and observing; 150 seconds for five independent paired readings and writing, 30 seconds per child; 45 seconds resetting matched sand. Others work dry, retaining their plans. All present wear goggles through cleanup. No sand is blown; nothing is tasted.

Each child completes [[s04-P60-measure]] with date, trial, slopes, starts, ends, units and an observation of carrying. Read the marked front, not the water edge. One retry stays inside that child's slot; otherwise mark unresolved and why. Never supply expected readings. Record no detected movement when that is observed. If resets or readings cannot fit, record fewer complete trials, not imaginary ones. Pool all available group records under trial numbers.

#### 6 min. Compare records

Children compare their own labels, units and observation notes with the pooled record. Preserve different readings and observer names; do not erase disagreement. Partners each take 45 seconds, simultaneously, to locate their own trial in the class record. Review cause-and-effect reasoning: comparison requires matched conditions and repeated evidence. A tray moves loose sediment; it cannot measure a solid-rock canyon's cutting rate. Do not introduce a supplied conflicting-trial claim before Exit.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult and any second handler contain sediment, wipe spills, clean goggles and wash with soap and water. Dry observers file pages. Settle runoff; never send sediment down sinks. Keep protection on until cleanup ends.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X60-record]] on the half slip and [[s04-X60-cause]] in the Exit box. Children answer independently on paper. Collect both and actual measurement pages. After class allow 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for both exit responses, plus 30 times 25 seconds, 12.5 minutes, for practical records. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing firsthand evidence stays missing. Return all day 61.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: mark actual measurement not run. Say aloud: invented for practice, gentle front starts at zero and ends at four centimeters; steep starts at zero and ends at eight. Label these supplied on a separate record. They cannot earn firsthand credit. No printer: copy record fields; reveal exit text only at Exit.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe kit: use the labeled invented record. Copy fields without printing.

**Skip.** Six-group rotation and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 59 feedback and match controls.
- **12 min. Observe.** Adult alone pours. Child uses [[s04-P60-measure]], following class safety.
  **Say.** "Where did you read the front?" Expect a ruler mark beside sediment, not water.
- **5 min. Record.** Keep starts, ends, units and any unresolved reading.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Contain sediment, wipe, clean protection and wash.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X60-record]] and [[s04-X60-cause]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Use fewer complete live trials, preserving individual readings, cleanup and exit. Extend record annotations, without adding a second changed condition.

### Words and supports

Review erosion, fair test and baseline. A repeat runs the same conditions again. Enlarge ruler marks; a scribe records the child's reading.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 observes thawed clay and enclosed rock abrasion. Return day 60's practical page and both exit responses in Compare controls. If many show error C, retain all repeats and limit the cause claim. Missing measurements receive reading support; no invented result fills them.
