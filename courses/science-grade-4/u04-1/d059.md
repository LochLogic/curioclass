# Day 59: Plan what changes and what stays matched

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest the tray; prepare matched sand patches and clay samples. Check ingredient allergies. Cover tables, mark the splash boundary, prepare catch bucket and everyone's goggles. Check freezer access and plastic containers. Integrate barrier-response.md, trial-sheet.md and freezing-sheet.md; conceal exit-59.md prompts. Keep teacher-key.md private. Sort readiness and day 58 papers for return.

### The day

#### 15 min. response

Return day 58's sort and exit feedback and day 56 readiness. Preserve fifteen minutes: five carrying, five barrier comparison, five explanation. Review erosion and barrier from earlier grades; teach a child's missing idea explicitly.

Adult pours over a marked damp patch; dry observers trace grains leaving for [[s04-P59-response]]. If many showed day 58 error A, distinguish carrying from new breakage here. Reset matched sand and pouring; compare bare and cloth-barrier paths. Ready children justify controls. Observe actual results, including no detected difference. Pairs each take 45 seconds, simultaneously, before finishing individual explanations. No expected result replaces an observation.

#### 15 min. Comparison planning

Review fair test from day 52: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Individually, [[s04-P59-plan]]. Change slope; match sand amount, moisture, water cupfuls, pouring position and manner. Review ruler reading from day 3: read whole centimeters; travel is end minus start. Describe measuring a marked sediment front, not canyon-cutting speed. For error C, identify what must stay matched without rehearsing a confounded-plan repair.

Introduce baseline: a starting record. Before any freezing, observe both clay samples for [[s04-P59-baseline]]: date, labels, cracks and moisture. Draw actual starting features, including no visible cracks. Review freeze from grade 2. Clay is an analogue, not rock; its behavior cannot prove rock behavior.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X59-plan]] on the half slip and [[s04-X59-start]] in the Exit box. Children answer alone; no oral replacement. Collect both and practice records. After class allow 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, for both exit responses, plus 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all day 60.

#### 5 min. Freezer setup

Adult labels identical plastic containers A and B, matching clay, moisture and loose covers. Leave headspace. Freeze A; keep B unfrozen. Record actual setup time. Adult arranges thawing before day 61. Children observe from dry places. If no freezer, retain the initial records and mark the freezing comparison not run.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Goggles stay on through cleanup. Adult and any second handler contain solids, wipe spills, clean goggles and wash with soap and water. Dry observers file papers. Settle runoff; never send sediment down sinks. Nothing is tasted; damp sand is never blown.

### Other rooms

No wet kit or protection: say aloud, invented for practice: bare patch loses grains; cloth catches some, with fewer escaping under equal pouring. Label responses supplied, with no firsthand credit. No freezer: draw the proposed setup and record not run. No printer: copy today's fields and conceal exit prompts.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe wet kit: use the invented barrier record. No freezer: draw the planned setup. Copy sheets if needed.

**Skip.** Class rotations and public reports.

- **8 min. Observe.** Adult handles kit; use [[s04-P59-response]]. Follow class safety.
  **Say.** "What left the patch?" Expect actual grain movement or an honest no-movement account.
- **10 min. Plan.** Use [[s04-P59-plan]]. Match everything except slope.
- **5 min. Start.** Use [[s04-P59-baseline]] before any treatment.
- **4 min. Setup.** Adult prepares containers, or labels the test not run.
- **3 min. Cleanup.** Contain solids, wipe and wash.
- **5 min. Exit.** Child completes [[s04-X59-plan]] and [[s04-X59-start]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Cut partner reports, preserving readiness response, both records, exit and cleanup. Extend the plan's measurement detail.

### Words and supports

Review erosion, barrier, fair test and freeze. Baseline means starting record. Dictation and labeled drawings preserve personal observations.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 returns all records in Return. If many show error C, repair controls and starting observations there. Repeat paired slope trials with independent ruler readings; preserve failed readings.
