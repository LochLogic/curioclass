# Teacher-only answers, section 1.4

Teacher-only answer key, continued.

## s01-A1

Prompt: Invented for practice. Same spool, hanging flap, surface, flap start and contact arrangement each time. The spool slows but keeps moving after contact; the flap swings. Flap travel, in centimeters: slower 4, 5, 4; faster 11, 12, 11. The same spool hits the same hanging flap. Explain how the repeated flap movements support a speed-energy link.

Expected response: The faster spool has more motion energy. Every faster flap travel, 11, 12 and 11 centimeters, exceeds every slower travel, 4, 5 and 4 centimeters. Energy passes at contact and changes flap motion. Repeated greater controlled effects support more energy available to transfer; centimeters measure movement, not energy.

Look for: Same spool: faster motion has more energy.; Use the repeated difference between both speed sets.; Explain energy transfer at contact into flap motion.; Link greater controlled movement to more energy available to transfer.

Expected wrong responses: Same spool means equal starting energy (A); The spool has 12 centimeters of energy (B).
