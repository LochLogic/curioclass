# Day 13: Agree on the anchor explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 12 ticket and classification feedback. Lay out question board, original marble records, scientific account and class energy-arrow chart. Use Ramp record R if records are absent, labeled invented for practice. Print Teacher-only key separately. Keep Bottle record hidden. Prepare blank evidence tags on each page; no live collisions.

### Safety and set-up

Paper records only. Bottles and orange are pictures, not food or equipment. No tasting, heating or touching for warmth.

### The day

Use class record book categories: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 12 tickets and the written task, s01-Ps12, with classification feedback. If many show error C, trace destinations for five minutes with [[s01-Pr13]]. Error J gets five minutes locating pushes on objects. More than ten is many. Others act on feedback; confer on blank work. Review grade 3 force arrows and day 7 energy arrows.

#### 20 min. Anchor consensus

Return to the original marble/cup question, not a new explanation packet. Read retained records and account aloud. Review grade 3 claim, evidence and reason: I think, because I saw, so.

**Ask.** [[s01-K13]]

Each child writes an evidence tag, then adds it beside an answered question. Allow ten seconds each, five minutes for thirty. Hear six reasons at 45 seconds, four and a half minutes; others submit notes or pass publicly. Review observation and inference, then connect speed, energy transfer and changed motion with [[steps:s01-K13]]. The new consensus joins these earlier pieces into one shared answer. Keep unresolved questions visible. If records overlap, retain uncertainty and propose more controlled trials. Never change observations to fit the explanation.

#### 10 min. One receiver prediction

Read [[s01-Pp13]] aloud, invented for practice. Pairs take one simultaneous 45-second turn each. Each child writes only a conditional motion phrase. Confer during remaining time. Conditional prediction practice stays in the folder. No new trial record to explain and no second receiver. Review energy destinations, including account-based warming; we have no temperature evidence.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Bottle record only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X13]] exactly. Children answer alone on paper. Mark after class: thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes. Tags take thirty times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return with written feedback day 15; no day 14 repair.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: children copy tags; write the covered exit separately. Use R explicitly as invented when anchor records are absent.

### At home

**Swap.** No class board or records: use Ramp record R and the account. Label R invented. No printer: copy tags and hide Bottle record.

**Skip.** Public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Use day 12 feedback; do [[s01-Pr13]].
- **15 min. Connect.** Do [[s01-K13]], pointing to record and account lines.
  **Say.** "Which question can this answer?" Expect a speed/energy or destination answer supported by its named source.
- **5 min. Predict.** Do [[s01-Pp13]].
  **Say.** "What depends on contact?" Expect tube motion can change if the ball reaches it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Bottle record; [[s01-X13]], independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, retaining every tag and individual prediction. Longer: choose an unresolved question and name evidence still needed.

### Words and supports

Consensus: a shared explanation supported by evidence. Review motion energy, transfer, destination, observation and inference. Read aloud; accept labels or child-directed scribing.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 is an independent end-of-unit assessment on an unseen case. Do not return day 13 feedback or reteach then. Error A needs speed/evidence links, error C destinations and error D motion alongside sound on day 15.
