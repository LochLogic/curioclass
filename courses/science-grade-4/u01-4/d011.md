# Day 11: Fill the missing trial

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 10 exit and source-choice feedback. Print the Teacher-only key separately. Keep Wagon record covered until the exit. Pretest gentle floor rolls; check car wheels, block edges and containment. Prepare a comparison with one slower row unfilled, completed during today's trials. Choose the next six on the heard roster, including day 10's remaining six.

### Safety and set-up

One lane, two handlers; others watch behind its boundary. Everyone present wears impact eye protection through setup and cleanup. Without it, use C, explicitly invented for practice. Never throw or taste; keep objects from mouths. Stop rolling before adult retrieval. No heating or touching to test warmth.

### The day

Use one scale in the class record book: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Return day 10 tickets and source choices. If many show error C, D or E, use [[s01-Pr11]] for five minutes each: destinations, motion beside sound, detection limits. Many means more than ten. Others follow written feedback; blank work gets a conference. Review grade 3 observation and inference: what we notice and what we work out.

#### 15 min. Missing trial investigation

Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Keep car, block, surface, start and contact fixed. Setup/protection takes three minutes. Run six two-minute trials, alternating slower and faster, visibly different approaches. Rotate two handlers within the six; four observers track contact, both motions, conditions and recording. All thirty record [[s01-Po11]], filling the missing slower row.

Mark unclear observations; repeat once within the slot. Otherwise copy the matching C row separately, labeled invented. Do not replace a real entry. Record motion qualitatively; no instrument measures energy or temperature here.

#### 5 min. Evidence notes

Alone, [[s01-Pn11]]. Hear six at 45 seconds each, four and a half minutes; public passing or private notes count. Collect everyone's page. Review motion energy, transfer, contact and destination from days 4 to 10. No integrated question/prediction/explanation practice card.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop rolls; count cars and blocks. Clear lane. Wipe and bag protection with wipes and storage bags. Retain observations in folders after feedback.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Wagon record, say invented for practice, then read [[s01-X11]] exactly. Each child answers alone on paper. Mark after class: thirty times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes; notes take thirty times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return both day 12.

### Other rooms

No devices needed. No safe apparatus: use C. No printer: copy the trial table and keep the exit separate.

### At home

**Swap.** No protected lane or room-wide eye protection: use Car record C, invented for practice. No printer: copy the table; hide Wagon record.

**Skip.** Public reports and group roles.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 10 feedback; use [[s01-Pr11]].
- **15 min. Observe.** Use six C rows for [[s01-Po11]]; leave one slower row covered first.
  **Say.** "What goes in the missing row?" Expect observations of both motions, labeled invented here.
- **5 min. Notes.** Do [[s01-Pn11]].
  **Say.** "Which question could this help answer?" Expect a link to the chosen observation.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Wagon record; [[s01-X11]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, keeping all records and repairs. Longer: identify an uncontrolled condition that would weaken the comparison.

### Words and supports

Review: prediction, observation, motion energy, transfer, contact, destination. Review rotating roles from grade 3. Read aloud; scribe only the child's words.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 returns tickets and notes in Return and repair. Error F needs an observable question, error A the speed contrast, error C destinations, five minutes each. Then classify correct force and energy arrows.
