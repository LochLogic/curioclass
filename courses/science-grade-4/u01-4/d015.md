# Day 15: Use feedback to revise

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and both teacher-only key pieces. Prepare day 14 packets with gap feedback and day 13 feedback. Put station-page.md at desks, with retained anchor evidence, correct-overlays.md, warming-card.md and evidence-tags.md. Keep readiness.md covered until the last ten minutes. Use the linked record sheet. Reuse the original assessment; print no second test.

### Safety and set-up

Paper stations only; no heat, sunlight test or live collisions. Cloth squares are pictures. Use existing pencils; an added symbol replaces a second color.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback

Return day 14 assessment and day 13 tickets and full pages, including s01-Pp13. If many show day 13 errors A, C or D, route them to the matching station. Read gap notes privately; each child identifies a next step. Read station directions aloud here. No public error examples from children's work. Review the grade 1 record scale, also used on day 14: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Response stations

Four five-minute rounds at desks; rotate task numbers, including page changes. Everyone completes all four. Teacher stays with the highest need. Children point, discuss privately and jot labels in their station page spaces. Each pair takes two 45-second turns per task, three minutes; two remain for labels, checking and page changes.

Station 1, A/B: [[s01-P15-1]], then [[s01-P15-distance]]. Review grade 3 claim, evidence and reason. Compare retained anchor effects, separating travel units from energy.

Station 2, C/D: [[s01-P15-2]]. Trace destinations on the anchor model; keep motion beside sound.

Station 3, E: [[s01-P15-3]]. Sort source labels; account-based warming is not a temperature measurement. No touching to test warmth.

Station 4, F/J: [[s01-P15-4]], then [[s01-P15-push]]. Use day 12 correct overlays. Read the two question choices on the page. Review grade 3 force arrows and day 7 energy arrows. Locate an object receiving a push before classifying energy destinations.

End each round with a 20-second oral check from the teacher-only key, after children commit their labels. Include this within the two minutes for labels, checking and changes. More than ten similar gaps selects teacher placement, not an additional serial repair.

#### 10 min. Revise responses

Children return to the three original day 14 responses for [[s01-P15-revise]]. Review grade 3 revision: keep original observations and initials; use a second color or added symbol. Add the revision note. Confer on blank or unrelated work; record content help. Collect the explanation, question, model and revision note together. Record revised explanation as s01-A3 and revised question/model as s01-A4. These record the same ten components once, not a second test or extra marks.

After class allow thirty times two minutes, sixty minutes, for final component categories and revision feedback. Return revised packets during day 16 Cleanup, without repair. Mark station pages after class: thirty times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Return them during day 16 Cleanup.

#### 10 min. Next unit readiness

Reveal the covered readiness panel on side 2 only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s02-R1]] exactly. Eight minutes independent paper; two minutes collection. Do not teach or discuss its answer first. After class mark thirty times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Keep categories for section 2.1; return day 18 for ten minutes across Response and repair and Light and warming comparisons. Missing sunlight cause goes there, not to day 16.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: copy station choices once on the board; children record privately. Copy readiness below the revision note; fold it out of view until the final part.

### At home

**Swap.** No class records: use invented Ramp record R. No printer: copy station choices and the covered readiness panel. No second-color pencil: add changes with a symbol and initials.

**Skip.** Group rotations. Stay at the table; point to each task.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read the notes on the returned papers together.
- **10 min. Repair.** Use tasks 1 to 4, including [[s01-P15-distance]] and [[s01-P15-push]]. Use the six labels on the returned day 12 page.
  **Say.** "Which source supports your change?" Expect a relevant observation or account line.
- **10 min. Revise.** Child does [[s01-P15-revise]]; keep originals. Use [[s01-A3]] and [[s01-A4]] for the returned work.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Reveal picture; child answers [[s02-R1]] alone. Save sunlight feedback for day 18.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: concise station explanations; retain all rotations, both revisions and final readiness. Longer: explain one unresolved anchor question using the limits of available evidence.

### Words and supports

Review: claim, evidence, reason, transfer, destination, force arrow, energy arrow and revision. Read aloud. Scribe only the child's response. No home or family disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 opens the crank-flashlight anchor, questions and initial model. Return revised packets during Cleanup without repair. Sunlight-warming readiness reviews kindergarten knowledge; missing cause receives day 18 support, never extra work on the hook day.

### Credit

Ours, all of it. Account: Source U1-collision and Source U1-assessment-account. Readiness background: Source U1-sunlight.
