# Day 15: Use feedback to revise

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 14 component-gap feedback and day 13 tickets/tags. Lay out original marble records, class energy-arrow chart, scientific account and correct overlays. Set out the day 14 assessment packet for revision. Use Ramp record R explicitly as invented if needed. Print Teacher-only key separately; fold side 2 over the readiness panel. Form four groups of seven or eight; mark each child's highest-need station from feedback. Set out second-color pencils and station page.

### Safety and set-up

Paper stations only; no heat, sunlight test or live collisions. Cloth squares are pictures. Use existing pencils; an added symbol replaces a second color.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback

Return day 14 assessment and day 13 tickets and evidence tags. If many show day 13 errors A, C or D, route them to the matching station. Read gap notes privately; each child identifies a next step. Read station directions aloud here. No public error examples from children's work. Keep day 14's component scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Response stations

Four simultaneous five-minute rotations; everyone visits all. Teacher stays at the highest-need station. Moving and checking fit each five-minute slot. Children point, discuss privately and jot labels in their station page spaces. Each pair takes two 45-second turns per station, three minutes; two minutes remain for labels, checking and moving.

Station 1, A/B: [[s01-P15-1]]. Review grade 3 claim, evidence and reason. Compare retained anchor effects, separating travel units from energy.

Station 2, C/D: [[s01-P15-2]]. Trace destinations on the anchor model; keep motion beside sound.

Station 3, E: [[s01-P15-3]]. Sort source labels; account-based warming is not a temperature measurement. No touching to test warmth.

Station 4, F/J: [[s01-P15-4]]. Use day 12 correct overlays. Read the two question choices on the page. Review grade 3 force arrows and day 7 energy arrows. Locate an object receiving a push before classifying energy destinations.

Give brief oral feedback using the teacher-only look-fors. More than ten similar gaps selects teacher placement, not an additional serial repair.

#### 10 min. Revise responses

Children return to both original day 14 items for [[s01-P15-revise]]. Review grade 3 revision: keep original observations and initials; use a second color or added symbol. Add the revision note. Confer on blank or unrelated work; record content help. Collect both responses and note. Record revised explanation as s01-A3 and revised question/model as s01-A4. These record the same ten components once, not a second test or extra marks.

After class allow thirty times 90 seconds, 45 minutes, for final component categories and revision feedback. Return revised packets during day 16 Cleanup, without repair. Station practice stays in folders.

#### 10 min. Next unit readiness

Reveal the covered readiness panel on side 2 only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s02-R1]] exactly. Eight minutes independent paper; two minutes collection. Do not teach or discuss its answer first. After class mark thirty times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Keep categories for section 2.1; return day 18 for ten minutes across Response and repair and Light and warming comparisons. Missing sunlight cause goes there, not to day 16.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: copy station choices once on the board; children record privately. Copy readiness below the revision note; fold it out of view until the final part.

### At home

**Swap.** No class records: use invented Ramp record R. No printer: copy station choices and the covered readiness panel. No second-color pencil: add changes with a symbol and initials.

**Skip.** Group rotations.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Return day 14 assessment and day 13 feedback.
- **10 min. Repair.** Use [[s01-P15-1]], [[s01-P15-2]], [[s01-P15-3]] and [[s01-P15-4]].
  **Say.** "Which source supports your change?" Expect a relevant observation or account line.
- **10 min. Revise.** Child does [[s01-P15-revise]]; keep originals. Use [[s01-A3]] and [[s01-A4]] to record these revisions once.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Reveal picture; child answers [[s02-R1]] alone. Save sunlight feedback for day 18.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: concise station explanations; retain all rotations, both revisions and final readiness. Longer: explain one unresolved anchor question using the limits of available evidence.

### Words and supports

Review: claim, evidence, reason, transfer, destination, force arrow, energy arrow and revision. Read aloud. Scribe only the child's response. No home or family disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 opens the crank-flashlight anchor, questions and initial model. Return revised packets during Cleanup without repair. Sunlight-warming readiness reviews kindergarten knowledge; missing cause receives day 18 support, never extra work on the hook day.
