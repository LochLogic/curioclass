# Day 14: Explain a new collision independently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener, assessment.md and both teacher-only key pieces. Print one two-sided student packet per child; the day's student page is that packet. Remove earlier models and practice records; blank the screen. Keep the spool case unseen until launch. Prepare component gap notes: four for item 1, two for item 2a and four for item 2b. Use the linked record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Paper case only. No spool, swinging apparatus, metal stop or heating is used.

### The day

#### 3 min. Independent launch

Reveal packet, say all trial records are invented for practice, and read directions without explanation. Read the shared record aloud neutrally. Review the familiar independent-work routine: everyone works alone. Offer large print, neutral read-aloud or child-directed scribing. Help with access, not science choices.

#### 17 min. Assessment item 1

Children read the table and answer [[s01-A1]]. This samples explaining the speed-energy relationship with repeated evidence, transfer and reasoning, 4-PS3-1. Circulate for access needs. No demonstration, peer talk, model, feedback or answer categories yet.

#### 5 min. Assessment item 2a

Point back to the shared record. Children answer [[s01-A2]] independently, asking one testable speed-and-transfer question. Read wording neutrally; give no science suggestions.

#### 10 min. Assessment item 2b

Read the printed slower-repeat definition and background neutrally. Children answer [[s01-A2b]] with an evidence-based prediction model. Keep both objects' motion, sound and account-based warming for their own decisions.

#### 7 min. Independent review

Children reread all three responses and add their own missing details. Read printed wording again if requested, without cues. No keys, partners or day 13 repair.

#### 3 min. Collection

Collect both sides together. After class, allow 30 times three minutes, ninety minutes, to identify component gaps and write next-step feedback. Use Teacher-only key; keep original responses. Return day 15, then record final component categories after revision. No points or second marking scale.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: hand-copy the shared record once per child and provide two blank response sides. Keep it unseen until launch.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the shared record and all three stems. No class record book: keep the original with component notes in a folder.

**Skip.** Class collection routines.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Hide earlier models; read directions and invented record neutrally. Child does [[s01-A1]] alone.
- **5 min. Question.** Child does [[s01-A2]] alone.
- **10 min. Predict.** Child does [[s01-A2b]] alone.
- **7 min. Review.** Child rereads without hints.
- **3 min. Collect.** Retain the response. Afterward, use Teacher-only key to write one next step for each missing component. Save revision for day 15.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both items and independent review. If access needs require more time, pause and resume independently before feedback. Do not add an extra test.

### Words and supports

Read any printed word aloud neutrally. Describe the arrangement without interpreting the results. No model or sentence frame supplies the answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 returns day 14 work and day 13 feedback. Error A/B needs controlled evidence, C/D destinations, E detection limits, F/J questions or arrow meanings. Four simultaneous five-minute stations precede ten minutes revising both responses; readiness is last.

### Credit

Ours, all of it. Background account informed by Source U1-assessment-account.
