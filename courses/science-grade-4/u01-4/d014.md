# Day 14: Explain a new collision independently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print assessment packet and Teacher-only key separately. Remove earlier models and practice records; blank the screen. Keep all records unseen until launch. Do not return day 13 feedback today. Prepare four item 1 and six item 2 component cells per child in the class record book: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Safety and set-up

Paper case only. No spool, swinging apparatus, metal stop or heating is used.

### The day

#### 3 min. Independent launch

Reveal packet, say all trial records are invented for practice, and read directions without explanation. Read the shared record aloud neutrally. Everyone works alone. Offer large print, neutral read-aloud or child-directed scribing. Help with access, not science choices.

#### 17 min. Assessment item 1

Children read the table and answer [[s01-A1]]. This samples explaining the speed-energy relationship with repeated evidence, transfer and reasoning, 4-PS3-1. Circulate for access needs. No demonstration, peer talk, model, feedback or answer categories yet.

#### 15 min. Assessment item 2

Point back to the same shared record. Children answer [[s01-A2]] independently. This samples a testable speed question and an evidence-based collision energy prediction, 4-PS3-3: both objects' motion, sound and warming from the background account. Do not explain the account or suggest destinations.

#### 7 min. Independent review

Children reread both responses and add their own missing details. Read printed wording again if requested, without cues. No keys, partners or day 13 repair.

#### 3 min. Collection

Collect both sides together. After class, allow 30 times two minutes, sixty minutes, to identify component gaps and write next-step feedback. Use Teacher-only key; keep original responses. Return day 15, then record final component categories after revision. No points or second marking scale.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: hand-copy the shared record once per child and provide two blank response sides. Keep it unseen until launch.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the shared record and both stems. No class record book: keep the original with component notes in a folder.

**Skip.** Class collection routines.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Hide earlier models; read directions and invented record neutrally. Child does [[s01-A1]] alone.
- **15 min. Predict.** Child does [[s01-A2]] alone.
- **7 min. Review.** Child rereads without hints.
- **3 min. Collect.** Retain the response. Afterward, use Teacher-only key to write one next step for each missing component. Save revision for day 15.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both items and independent review. If access needs require more time, pause and resume independently before feedback. Do not add an extra test.

### Words and supports

Read any printed word aloud neutrally. Describe the arrangement without interpreting the results. No model or sentence frame supplies the answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 returns day 14 work and day 13 feedback. Error A/B needs controlled evidence, C/D destinations, E detection limits, F/J questions or arrow meanings. Four simultaneous five-minute stations precede ten minutes revising both responses; readiness is last.
