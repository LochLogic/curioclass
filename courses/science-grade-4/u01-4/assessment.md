# Unit 1 end-of-unit assessment

## Student

Invented for practice. Work alone.
Same spool, hanging flap, surface, flap start and contact arrangement each time.
The spool rolls right, slows after contact, and keeps moving.
The flap starts still and swings right.

<pre>Rolling spool  -->  | Hanging flap |</pre>

| Trial | Slower flap travel, cm | Faster flap travel, cm |
|---|---|---|
| First | 4 | 11 |
| Second | 5 | 12 |
| Third | 4 | 11 |

Listeners stay in the same place.
Slower contacts sound quieter; faster contacts sound louder.
Slower repeat means slower than the recorded faster rolls.

Scientific background: A rolling cylinder meets a metal stop.
At contact, some energy can pass into objects and the nearby air.
This can warm them slightly.
Our classroom equipment cannot detect this tiny change.
The spool trials have no temperature readings.
