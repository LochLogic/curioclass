# Day 12: Read two kinds of arrows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return day 11 tickets and notes. Print Teacher-only key separately. Copy correct overlays on the board or student page, keeping Bead panel covered. Lay out scientific account and meaning labels; no live objects today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper models only; no rolling, heating or touching for warmth. Keep printed bead pictures separate from small objects.

### The day

Continue the class record book scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Return and repair

If many day 11 tickets show error F, A or C, spend five minutes each with [[s01-Pr12]]: observable outcome, speed contrast, destinations. More than ten is many. Return day 11 observation-note feedback too. Do not repeat its exit case. Others follow feedback; confer on blank work.

#### 10 min. Correct arrow overlays

Say the truck/block case is invented for practice. Pairs point, [[s01-Pa12]], before teacher feedback.

Review grade 3 force arrow: starts on the thing pushed, points the way the force goes. Review day 7 energy arrows: initial motion energy connects to destinations. Review day 10 observation versus scientific account: warming is explained, not a temperature reading. These are correct overlays to classify, not flawed models to repair.

**Ask.** [[s01-Pa12]]

Then show [[steps:s01-Pa12]]. Pushes on truck and block differ in direction. Energy arrows do not follow those directions or measure force.

#### 15 min. Meaning sort

Alone, classify labels for [[s01-Ps12]]. Pairs explain with two simultaneous 45-second turns each, three minutes total. Hear the next six for 45 seconds each, four and a half minutes; passing or private response allowed. Use remaining time for individual checking with teacher feedback. Collect every classification. If many confuse meanings, point first to the object acted on, then to an energy destination. Do not write a repaired account in practice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Bead panel only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X12]] exactly. Everyone answers alone on paper. After class mark thirty times 40 seconds, twenty minutes, and classifications thirty times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return both day 13.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: copy overlays and labels; each child makes two label columns. Copy covered exit separately.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy overlays and labels. No class records: use day 11's invented C notes. No board: point on paper.

**Skip.** Group reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Use day 11 feedback and [[s01-Pr12]].
- **10 min. Arrows.** Read correct overlays; do [[s01-Pa12]].
  **Say.** "What receives each push?" Expect block in A's right arrow, truck in its left arrow.
- **10 min. Sort.** Do [[s01-Ps12]] aloud, then point to each destination.
  **Say.** "What does the energy arrow connect?" Expect starting energy and a destination.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Bead panel; do [[s01-X12]] independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear fewer public turns, preserving each classification. Longer: explain why energy-arrow length cannot measure the push strength.

### Words and supports

Review: force arrow, energy arrow, contact, transfer, destination. Describe both overlays aloud. Pointing, large print and child-directed scribing are available.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 returns day 12 work. In Return and repair, error C gets five minutes tracing destinations; error J gets five locating pushes on objects. Then attach evidence to the anchor consensus. Day 13's exit receives feedback day 15, preserving independent assessment day 14.
