# Section 1.4: Connect, check and revise

## What the section does

Children use observations, predictions and explanations together, then independently apply the model. Energy and matter connects the changing motions, sound and account-based warming. A second receiver gains motion only if contact occurs; preserve uncertainty. Consensus means a shared explanation supported by evidence. Remove the class model for the assessment.

Use the operational Day blocks in the section plan. Every meeting is 45 minutes.

## Before class

Print missing-trial.md; correct-overlays.md; meaning-labels.md; evidence-tags.md; one-receiver.md; station-page.md. Print the section's exit cards separately, half a sheet each. Keep each covered until its day. Keep key-unit-2.md, key-unit.md teacher-only. Read the single record scale in ../u01-1/record.md. Do not distribute answers with cards.

## Equipment, records and access

Day 11 uses the protected car/block station, six cleaning places and the five-minute cleanup. Otherwise use ../u01-3/car-record-c.md. Other meetings use retained records, correct overlays and paper. No temperature instrument or deliberate warming.

A ruler reads nearest whole centimeters; less than one centimeter is not zero. Retain unreadable entries; repeat once within the trial time, then use a separate invented record if unresolved. Print and say invented for practice. Six two-minute trials need twelve minutes; the class observes one station. Two handlers and four observers rotate; all thirty keep records. Protection stays on through packing. Cleanup: two minutes counting and clearing, three for six captains to clean five student pairs each while the adult cleans their own, following product directions.

Every child may pass during public talk. Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each inside an existing five-minute work portion. Name those heard on the record; use the next meeting for the next six. Papers from everyone receive written next steps on the next named hand-back day. Read stems aloud, describe pictures, enlarge print, or scribe only the child's own response. No thirty-child oral round.

## Common errors and feedback

A/B: controlled effects support qualitative energy, not energy units. C/D: retain motion and multiple destinations. E: distinguish detection from the account. F/J: observable questions and correctly located contact pushes.

More than ten misses among thirty is many. Use the receiving Day block and handbacks.csv minutes; never add a second serial repair. Blank work gets a conference during independent work. One scale throughout: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Item choices and error letters are in key-unit.md.

## Protect independent evidence

Exit cards are assessment-only. No teaching sheet uses their cases or complete response forms. Keep the unseen assessment packet covered until day 14. Paper replay and destination labels fill the unavailable simulation slots; a simulation cannot establish warming. One child can use the same labeled records with an adult; no purchase is needed.
