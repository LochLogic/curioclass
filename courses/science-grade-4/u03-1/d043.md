# Day 43: Compare height, spacing, and motion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-key.md and today's generated item key privately. Print today's fields only, including the receiving-drum record. Cover Side 2. Pretest and clamp the ruler; adult wears goggles, clears recoil space and gently plucks. Keep faces back. Fill the tub, cover its table and place the spill cloth. Aim the flashlight at a card, away from eyes. Keep sound gentle. Prepare day 41 readiness and day 42 notes.

### The day

#### 15 min. Older-row response

Return day 42 s03-P42-observe and s03-P42-question with notes. Review its wave labels. Day 41's C-R41-source and C-R41-receiver select needed sound responses; teach both missing causal directions as new.

First five minutes: adult gently plucks the secured ruler, then stills it. Define vibrate: move back and forth repeatedly. Children answer [[s03-P43-source]] on paper. Expect sound with vibration, ending when stilled. Retry once; if inaudible, use the labeled invented row. The ruler shows vibration producing sound, not the reverse.

Next five: read receiving-drum.md as invented for practice. **Ask.** [[s03-P43-receiver]]. Expect untouched grains move during sound and rest during silence. Separate supports reduce a direct contact explanation. This is supplied evidence, not our trial.

Last five: review message, signal, sender and receiver from grade 1. Agree steady light means LOOK; show it toward a card. Every child writes [[s03-P43-signal]]. Confirm reception without increasing brightness. No serial spoken round. Ready children label familiar wave features between responses.

#### 20 min. Water comparison

Review day 42's amplitude from rest and matching-peak spacing, five minutes with setup. Adult varies gentle repeating dips. Six groups of five get two-minute views; three minutes finish [[s03-P43-table]]. Keep separate height, spacing and local movement columns; no paired drawing practice. Date actual records and retain unclear marks. Settle reflections and retry once within the slot; then announce invented fallback W separately. An unclear contrast stays unclear.

Hear at most four volunteers, 45 seconds each during recording; passing is allowed. On the pattern chart distinguish observations from explanations. If many bind spacing to height, point at the separate columns without coaching the exit case.

#### 5 min. Model explanation and E43

Uncover Side 2 now. The record is invented for practice. Each child completes [[s03-X43]] on a half slip, alone. Read neutrally; collect all thirty. After class: 30 × 25 seconds, 12.5 minutes exit marking, plus 30 × 20 seconds, ten minutes for practice notes. Record shown, shown with help, or not yet seen by component, never a second scale.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult empties, dries, stores equipment and washes, two minutes. Dry observers file, three. Wipe spills immediately. Keep observers out of splash and recoil zones throughout.

### Other rooms

Without kit use invented sound rows and fallback W; mark firsthand evidence unavailable. No printer: copy fields and reveal reserved frames only at exit. A board replaces a projector.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe equipment use the invented ruler row, receiving record and fallback W. Copy fields without printing. Point at paper without a projector.

**Skip.** Group views and public sharing.

- **10 min. Sound.** Adult demonstrates the secured ruler if safe. Do [[s03-P43-source]] and [[s03-P43-receiver]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports each direction?" Expect: vibration and sound together; the untouched receiver moves during sound.
- **5 min. Signal.** Agree steady light means LOOK. Do [[s03-P43-signal]]. Keep light away from eyes.
- **15 min. Compare.** Do [[s03-P43-table]], using actual tub views or separate invented fallback W. Keep unclear entries, then clear equipment.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the reserved frames; give [[s03-X43]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce public sharing, preserving both sound directions and wave observations. Extend with an uncertainty in the actual record.

### Words and supports

Review wave labels, pattern and energy arrows from day 42. Review signal words from grade 1. Read aloud; a scribe preserves individual reasoning. Public speech is optional.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 returns s03-X43 and s03-P43-table. Error A carries the float onward; error B binds height to spacing. Use five minutes per error in Return and repair. Sound responses return with written notes there, without extra repair time. Keep day 42's initial exit alongside these records.

### Credit

Ours. Sources U3-waves, U3-patterns and U3-vibration support these accounts.
