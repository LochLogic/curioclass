# Separate environmental details

## Reservoir detail

A reservoir can cover natural land areas. Record this detail and its source location.



Source: [Hydropower and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/hydropower/hydropower-and-the-environment.php), Dams have some environmental impact. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-25; reopened 2026-11-26.

## Surface-mine detail

Surface mining removes soil and rock above coal. Record this detail and its separate source location.



Source: [Coal and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/coal/coal-and-the-environment.php), Effects of coal mining. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-25; reopened 2026-11-26.

## Burning detail

Burning coal leaves ash. Record this detail and its source location.



Source: [Electricity and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/electricity-and-the-environment.php), Some power plants also produce liquid and solid waste. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-25; reopened 2026-11-26.
