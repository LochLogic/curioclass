# Day 31 held sources

## Source A

Wind is naturally replenished. Available wind can vary.

Source: [Wind energy and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/wind/wind-energy-and-the-environment.php), Wind is an emissions-free source of energy. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.

## Source B

Natural-gas deposits are limited. They form over very long times. Extracting gas uses up part of that deposit.

Source: [Sources of energy](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/what-is-energy/sources-of-energy.php), Nonrenewable energy. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.
