# Day 30: Finding natural origins

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Return day 29 papers with written feedback. Prepare teaching masters and blank note columns, omitting typed Name lines. Keep Day 30 exit ticket and Day 30 held sources concealed. Print origins and retrieval within the packet; keep archived designs for day 35. No apparatus or natural samples are handled.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 29's s02-X29, s02-P29-1 and s02-P29-repair. First five minutes: if many showed error D, locate the actual comparable retest supporting improvement. Many means more than ten of thirty. Next five: each child reads Private design feedback and marks one target for day 35. Review the individual design record routine; archive originals without revising today.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review grade 3's changed condition and needs meet conditions. Review habitat: a place providing living things' needs. Read Changed-condition retrieval, explicitly invented for practice. Alone, answer [[s02-P30-retrieval]]. If habitat is omitted, connect shrub removal to fewer nesting places before considering a retained strip. No personal stories.

#### 8 min. Teach origins

Ask [[s02-K30-1]] before explaining. Model from Source U2-origins, source categories, and Source U2-generation, solar cells: sunlight supplies energy; the made cell changes it into electricity. Review energy arrows from input to recipient, not force arrows. Teach natural resource: material or energy supply found in nature. Review source in grade 3's words: a card or page that tells you something. An energy source supplies energy; an information source supplies information.

#### 15 min. Resource categories

Pairs use Natural origins and made carriers for [[s02-P30-1]]. Model one source-statement/location note, then children complete their own categories. Each partner takes one 45-second explanation turn. Review rotating six: six public turns, at most 45 seconds each inside five minutes; passing is allowed. Everyone submits paper. If many show error I, separate made object from natural supply; batteries store energy and wires conduct current. Do not repair practice captions.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X30]] and Day 30 held sources now. Read neutrally for one minute; allow five alone and one to collect. Use the exact stem. After class: thirty tickets times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes; thirty practice sheets times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for, one scale only. Blank work gets a conference. Return all papers day 31; retain design feedback for day 35.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy blank fields and read the source masters aloud.

**Skip.** Public turns and pair sorting.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 29 feedback; choose a revision target for day 35.
- **5 min. Retrieve.** Do [[s02-P30-retrieval]].
- **8 min. Origins.** Read the sunlight example; do [[s02-K30-1]].
  **Say.** "Which part comes from nature?" Expect sunlight; the cell is made.
- **10 min. Notes.** Do [[s02-P30-1]] with the origins cards. Point to each source location.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal held sources and [[s02-X30]]. Read without explaining. Child responds alone. Save for day 31.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns, keeping individual notes, feedback target and exit. Longer: identify where an existing source supports a category.

### Words and supports

Natural resource; review source, habitat and energy arrows. Read stems aloud neutrally. Accept labeled drawings. Frame on the student page: "Source ___ says ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 31 returns s02-X30 and s02-P30-1. Error I, a made battery called natural, needs natural-origin sorting. Then combine source notes about replenished and limited supplies.
