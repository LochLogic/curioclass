# Seven resource cards

Cut apart. Add each source location to your notes.

| Resource | Detail |
|---|---|
| Sunlight | Renewable; available sunlight changes. |
| Wind | Renewable; available wind changes. |
| Flowing water | Renewable; available flow changes. |
| Coal | Limited deposits form very slowly. |
| Oil | Limited deposits form very slowly. |
| Natural gas | Nonrenewable; extracting it reduces the deposit. |
| Uranium | Limited mined fuel; its used waste needs special storage. |

Renewable means naturally replenished. Nonrenewable supplies are not replaced on human timescales. A turbine has blades turned by flowing water or air. It can turn a generator.
