# Particle-filter comparison

These are original diagrams of a generic coal facility.

## Without a particle filter

```text
coal burned --> particle-containing gas --> stack --> outside air
```

## With a particle filter

```text
coal burned --> bag-house filter --> gas with fewer particles --> stack
                         |
                  captured particles
```

Trace each path. Mark particles caught by the filter. The filter captures particles; it does not remove every gas. Mining still supplies the coal. No fuels or pollutants are handled.

Source: [Electricity and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/electricity-and-the-environment.php), Power plants reduce air pollution emissions in various ways.
