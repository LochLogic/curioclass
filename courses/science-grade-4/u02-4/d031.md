# Day 31: Replenished and limited supplies

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Seven resource cards as a teacher read-aloud master. Children receive seven resource labels and blank note columns within their page, not duplicate card copies. Keep Day 31 exit ticket and Day 31 held sources concealed. Sort day 30 papers for return.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 30's s02-X30, s02-P30-1 , s02-P30-retrieval and s02-K30-1 on the retrieval page. If many showed error I, separate the made battery from its natural material origins. Many means more than ten of thirty. Children point to a natural-origin entry in their own notes; keep feedback with the returned sheet.

#### 8 min. Teach combining

Review grade 3's source: a card or page that tells you something. Model [[s02-K31-1]], asking before revealing the two note locations. Explain renewable: naturally replenished during human lifetimes. Nonrenewable: limited supplies not replaced during human lifetimes. These are supply labels, not promises of constant output. Join a label to its supporting statement and location; do not model the held community comparison.

#### 15 min. Seven resource cards

Read Seven resource cards once, under 160 words of science text. Children label their seven rows for [[s02-P31-1]]. Teach turbine: blades turned by moving water or air, connected to a generator. Review generator conversion from motion to electrical energy. Uranium supplies nuclear fuel; used fuel needs special storage, without an atomic model. If many show error J, distinguish nature replenishing a flow from extracting a limited deposit. No actual fuels are handled.

#### 10 min. Source-note exchange

Pairs point to each other's statement/location links, one 45-second turn each. Review rotating six; hear six at most 45 seconds each inside five minutes, with passing allowed. Children explain one resource's label using its own source section, not a wind/gas comparison. Collect individual notes from everyone. Unsupported claims need a source location before another detail.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X31]] with Day 31 held sources only now. Read neutrally for one minute; five alone, one collection. After class: thirty tickets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes; thirty note sheets times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return every paper day 32.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy resource labels and blank columns; adult reads the master.

**Skip.** Public turns and pair exchange.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 30 feedback and locate a natural-origin note.
- **8 min. Notes.** Do [[s02-K31-1]]. Point to both source sections.
  **Say.** "Where did that detail come from?" Expect the title and named section.
- **15 min. Resources.** Read Seven resource cards. Do [[s02-P31-1]]. Let the child explain one label.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal Day 31 held sources and [[s02-X31]]. Read neutrally. Child answers alone; save papers for day 32.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns, retaining seven labels, attributed notes and exit. Longer: identify an availability limit for one replenished supply.

### Words and supports

Renewable, nonrenewable, turbine; review natural resource and source. Read labels aloud; accept drawing plus dictated attribution. Frame: "Source ___ says ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 32 returns s02-X31 and s02-P31-1. Error J, gas renewing during human lifetimes, needs the deposit-formation source. Then trace environmental effects from evidence.
