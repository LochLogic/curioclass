# Day 32 held sources

## Source A

Mountaintop removal and valley fills change land. Polluted drainage can harm animals living in streams.

Source: [Coal and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/coal/coal-and-the-environment.php), Effects of coal mining. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-25; reopened 2026-11-26.

## Source B

Burning fuel releases air pollutants. Some cause acid rain, harming plants and animals living in water.

Source: [Electricity and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/electricity-and-the-environment.php), Fossil fuel, biomass, and waste burning power plants. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-25; reopened 2026-11-26.
