# Day 33: Following particle paths

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Particle-filter comparison, one per pair, and blank source-note fields. Conceal Day 33 exit ticket and Day 33 held sources. Sort day 32 papers. Use the paper diagrams throughout; the resource-path tool is specification only. Do not conduct a smoke or filtering demonstration.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 32's s02-X32 and s02-P32-1, including s02-K32-1 on that sheet. If many showed error K, point to the returned burning source's air-pollutant statement. Many means more than ten of thirty. Review pollutant, a substance that can harm surroundings, before tracing today's paths.

#### 8 min. Teach filter mechanism

Show Particle-filter comparison. Explain the coal facility supplies electricity for human needs. Knowledge of particles in combustion gas helps people change equipment to reduce what escapes. **Ask.** [[s02-K33-1]] before following the collection branch. A bag-house particle filter catches particles; gases can continue along the gas path. Review cause and effect: the new barrier changes which particles reach outside air. No claim that every pollutant disappears.

#### 15 min. Particle paths

Pairs trace the two correct diagrams for [[s02-P33-1]]. Use four minutes to follow each branch slowly; this paper comparison replaces the unbuilt tool minute for minute. Each partner takes one 45-second turn pointing along a path. Children mark their own captured/leaving labels. If many show error F, follow the gas path past the filter and name one effect outside this device's reach. No wind trials or improvement-verdict case appears in practice.

#### 10 min. Source notes

Teacher reads Source U2-effects, air-pollution controls, and separately Source U2-coal, surface soil/rock removal. Children add each source location beside its matching diagram note. Mining still supplies coal even when particles are caught at the facility. Review rotating six: six 45-second turns within five minutes, passing allowed. Each locates one supporting detail, not a combined trial response. Everyone returns notes.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X33]], Day 33 held sources and its invented matched-trial record now. Read neutrally for one minute; five alone, one collection. After class: thirty tickets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes; thirty annotated papers times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank work gets a conference. Return all papers day 34.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw both correct paths from the master. No tool is needed.

**Skip.** Public turns and partner exchange.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 32 feedback and locate the burning-source statement.
- **8 min. Follow.** Do [[s02-K33-1]] using the diagrams.
  **Say.** "Where do caught particles go?" Expect the collection branch.
- **15 min. Annotate.** Do [[s02-P33-1]]. Adult reads source details; child records locations.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal held sources and [[s02-X33]]. Read neutrally. Child answers alone; save papers for day 34.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns, retaining both particle paths and source notes. Longer: identify a claim the filter diagram alone cannot establish.

### Words and supports

Particle: a tiny piece of material. Filter: equipment that catches particles here. Review pollutant, effect and source. Frame: "Source ___ says ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 34 returns s02-X33 and s02-P33-1. Error F, one change removes every effect, needs the remaining-effect evidence. Then read a dated ten-share generation graph.
