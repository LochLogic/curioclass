# Day 35: Revising explanations from evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Retrieve the crank drawing/question board, My individual design evidence and day 30 Private design feedback. Return only needed handwritten evidence rows; keep supporting printed frames covered in the teacher archive. Print Familiar model revision labels within today's page, without Name lines. Conceal Day 35 exit ticket. No powered test or new build.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 34's s02-X34 and s02-P34-1. If many showed error E, point to the renewable label and the separately documented effect. Many means more than ten of thirty. Replenishment describes supply, not absence of environmental effects. Return every note sheet with its written feedback.

#### 12 min. Anchor consensus

Review the familiar sealed hand-crank flashlight anchor using its original drawing, not apparatus. **Ask.** [[s02-P35-1]] before attaching labels. Review generator, current, circuit and energy arrows. Link the actual earlier observations to supported arrows: motion at the handle, generator conversion, current to light. Keep an unsupported claim as a question. Review consensus: agree only as far as evidence allows. Review rotating six; hear six 45-second contributions within five minutes, passing allowed. Every child submits an evidence label in Familiar model revision labels. Supplied records remain supplied; missing observation stays not observed.

#### 15 min. Portfolio revision

Return day 29's My individual design evidence and day 30's Private design feedback. Review the feedback-then-revision routine. Alone, complete [[s02-P35-2]] in a second color: three minutes locate feedback and evidence, nine revise, three attach and check identities. Review criterion, limits, refinement and matched retest. Use handwritten rows and adult implementation initials. If evidence is missing, label that gap; do not invent a successful trial or recreate performance on paper. Preserve the original. Collect for one later portfolio score after day 38, not a score today.

#### 5 min. Resource branches

Return to [[s02-P35-1]]. Children add separate natural-resource/use/effect labels from their earlier attributed notes. A crank uses the operator's motion; do not claim it burns the pictured generating fuel. Review cause and effect to connect resource use with its supported consequence. Keep unresolved board questions visible. Do not model a new device with a matched refinement and resource consequence together.

#### 8 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X35]] now. State that the toy record is invented for practice. One minute neutral read, six alone, one collection. After class: thirty tickets times 90 seconds, 45 minutes; thirty model labels times 20 seconds, ten minutes; thirty revised portfolios times two minutes, 60 minutes of descriptive feedback, no points. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Blank work gets a conference. Retain portfolios for day 36; return model and ticket feedback day 38.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No second pencil color: add revisions in a bordered area. No board: use the retained crank drawing.

**Skip.** Public turns and class agreement.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 34 feedback.
- **10 min. Model.** Do [[s02-P35-1]] with earlier evidence and resource notes.
  **Say.** "Which record supports this arrow?" Expect a dated observation and its connection.
- **12 min. Revise.** Do [[s02-P35-2]] using day 30 feedback. Preserve originals; label missing evidence honestly.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X35]]. Read neutrally. Child answers alone. Keep the portfolio for day 36 and ticket feedback for day 38.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns, preserving independent revision and the full exit. Longer: label an unresolved model claim and the evidence needed.

### Words and supports

Review consensus model, natural resource, cause and effect, current and criterion. Scribe without supplying reasoning. Printed frame: "Evidence for this arrow: ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 36 is an end-of-unit assessment using unfamiliar stations and a converting device, plus the already revised portfolio. No repair from day 35 occurs there. Retain s02-P35-2 with the portfolio. Return s02-X35 and s02-P35-1 with written feedback in day 38 Read feedback; its revisions address input, matched tests and remaining resource effects.
