# Section 2.4: Resources and effects

## Before class

Distinguish energy source from information source, a card or page that tells you something. Teach natural resource, renewable, nonrenewable, effect and pollutant explicitly. Use teaching cards and attributed notes, not held exit cards. A renewable supply can vary. A limited deposit is depleted by extraction. Judge evidence about technology and effects without judging jobs or families. Day 34 uses checked Colorado 2024 generation as an example; no location is assumed.

Read this section's teacher-key.md privately. Print each held exit only on its own day. The combined daily student print, including sources and exits, stays within 350 words. Every student sentence has at most 15 words. All teaching days take 45 minutes.

## Evidence and access

Every child returns paper. Partner talk permits one 45-second turn each, 90 seconds total. Hear a named rotating six publicly, 45 seconds each within five minutes; anyone may pass. These public turns are unjudged. Everyone's paper is returned on the handbacks.csv day.

Pretest compatibility, visibility and temperature changes before class. Use whole-degree thermometer marks; an unresolved change stays unclear. Announce invented records aloud and keep them separate from measured rows. Fallbacks cannot replace missing live observation or individual performance. Use the controls and timed return slots below; pretest before class.

## Evidence sources

U2-origins; U2-effects; U2-coal; U2-state. Government summaries are paraphrases; source titles, sections and URLs are on the relevant cards. No apparatus is bought or optional activity added by this piece.

## Printed sources, graph and returns

No fuels, pollutants, food, soil or heated apparatus are handled. Use source-notes.md and the separately attributed source cards. The day 30 road/shrub record is invented, with no personal disclosures. Day 31 uses the seven resource cards; renewal and local availability differ. Define turbine as blades turned by flowing water or air, coupled to a generator. Read nuclear fuel as limited, with waste needing management; do not teach atoms.

Day 32 teaching uses only reservoir land coverage, surface soil/rock removal and coal ash. Reserve the mining-drainage/combustion causal pair for E32. Day 33 follows captured/leaving particles on filter-diagrams.md, never the held wind trial. A filter catches particles, not all gases or mining effects. The unavailable resource tool is replaced by those paper paths for four minutes.

Day 34 uses state-record.md as a table, not a completed graph. Its checked Colorado 2024 ten-share rounding is gas three, coal three, wind three, sunlight one. Children draw an ordered picture graph with a key. Zero rounded shares is not absence; this is generation, not household use. state-calculation.md is private. If missing, P is invented: coal five, gas three, water two. The held regional graph supplies different counts and asks an environmental judgment, not graph construction.
