# Day 32: Evidence of environmental effects

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Separate environmental details for teacher reading, not an extra student copy. Prepare three blank source rows. Conceal Day 32 exit ticket and Day 32 held sources; those source selections are separate from teaching details. Sort day 31 papers. No samples or burning demonstrations.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 31's s02-X31 and s02-P31-1, including s02-K31-1 on that sheet. If many showed error J, reread the limited-deposit detail: extraction reduces what remains, while formation takes much longer than human lives. Many means more than ten of thirty. Review renewable and nonrenewable labels without comparing the returned community case again.

#### 10 min. Source demonstration

We evaluate factual claims, never workers or families. No personal experiences are requested. Review cause and effect and habitat, a place supplying living things' needs. Read only the reservoir and surface-removal teaching details with titles and sections. **Ask.** [[s02-K32-1]] before modeling the land-to-water habitat change. Teach pollutant: a substance that can harm surroundings. An effect is a change resulting from a cause; a source must support the link.

#### 15 min. Evidence annotations

Read Separate environmental details, under 140 words of science text. Children do [[s02-P32-1]] in Statement and source-location notes fields. Point to each separate title/section while reading. Keep notes separate: reservoir coverage, soil/rock removal and solid-fuel ash. Do not use the held mining-drainage or acid-rain selections. If a note claims every dam has identical effects, return to the source's word "can."

#### 8 min. Oral connections

Partners use their reservoir note for one condition-to-organism explanation each, 45 seconds a turn. Review grade 3's needs meet conditions. Review rotating six: hear six within five minutes, at most 45 seconds each; anyone may pass. Everyone submits the separate-detail table. This rehearses one oral link, not a written pair of extraction/use links.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X32]] and Day 32 held sources now. One minute neutral read, five alone, one collection. The invented community record and factual summaries remain distinct. After class: thirty tickets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes; thirty tables times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank work gets a conference. Return papers day 33.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy three blank rows; adult reads the environmental master.

**Skip.** Public turns and partner exchange.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 31 feedback; locate the deposit detail.
- **10 min. Habitat.** Read the reservoir detail. Do [[s02-K32-1]].
  **Say.** "What condition changed?" Expect dry land becoming covered by water.
- **13 min. Sources.** Read the three separate details. Do [[s02-P32-1]], recording each title and section.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal held sources and [[s02-X32]]. Read neutrally; child answers alone. Save papers for day 33.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public sharing, preserving individual source rows and the exit. Longer: identify which source wording makes an effect possible rather than certain.

### Words and supports

Pollutant, effect; review habitat, needs meet conditions and source. Use neutral read-aloud and a scribe. Frame: "Source ___ says ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 33 returns s02-X32 and s02-P32-1. Error K, burning releases no pollutants, needs the burning-source detail. Then compare particle paths with and without a filter.
