# Day 33 held sources

## Source A

Wind turns blades connected to a generator. The generator converts motion into electrical energy.

Source: [How electricity is generated](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/how-electricity-is-generated.php), Turbine driven generators. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.

## Source B

Turning blades can be noisy. Making turbine components can also affect the environment.

Source: [Wind energy and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/wind/wind-energy-and-the-environment.php), Wind turbines can affect the environment. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.
