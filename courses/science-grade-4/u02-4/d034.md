# Day 34: Reading ten-share generation records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Colorado 2024 generation record and State example preparation and fallback. Draw clear outline squares from the counts if glyphs print poorly. Prepare ten counters per child. Keep Day 34 exit ticket and Day 34 held sources concealed. Use the filter master only for a separate effect note; return day 33 papers.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review return and repair. Return day 33's s02-X33 and s02-P33-1, including s02-K33-1 on that sheet. If many showed error F, distinguish the documented noise change from an unchanged effect on the returned record. Many means more than ten of thirty. Do not generalize one improvement to every environmental effect.

#### 8 min. State record

Read the completed Colorado 2024 generation record. It describes that state's electricity generation that year, not this classroom's location or any household mix. Review grade 3's picture graph, key and scale. Here one square means one rounded share out of ten. Teacher handles raw totals and rounding; zero rounded shares need not mean absence. Read labels, then point to existing rows. If the prepared state record is unavailable, print and announce fallback P as invented for practice: coal five, gas three, water two. Never call P local.

#### 15 min. Read and represent

Alone, children do [[s02-P34-1]] using Graph counters. Count ten first; group counters by resource in any order. Check strip totals against the completed graph, without constructing another ordered graph. Review renewable/nonrenewable resource labels. If a child treats zero as absent, point to the rounding note. Record which source was actually used: Colorado or invented P.

#### 10 min. Source-location work

Children match each nonzero label to its natural origin in their notes. Reuse the teacher's Particle-filter comparison to locate one coal-effect detail separately, citing Source U2-effects. Do not rehearse the exit's no-effects claim or its held wind detail. Review rotating six: six 45-second source-location turns within five minutes, passing allowed. Everyone submits their counter drawing and notes. Evidence concerns resources, never family choices or jobs.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X34]] and Day 34 held sources now. One minute neutral reading, five independent work, one collection. The half slip reserves its upper response area for the graph, lower area for the claim judgment; use the exact stem once. After class: thirty tickets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes; thirty practice sheets times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank work gets a conference. Return all papers day 35.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and teaching labels; reveal the exact exit and sources only at its turn. No projector: use the teacher master. Dictation or a scribe preserves each child's science decisions.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use ten pencil circles. No prepared state copy: use invented P from the teacher master.

**Skip.** Public sharing and pair talk.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 33 feedback.
- **8 min. Read.** Point to the state, year, resource labels and key.
  **Say.** "What does one square represent?" Expect one rounded share out of ten.
- **15 min. Represent.** Do [[s02-P34-1]]. Child chooses the counter order. Locate one separate effect detail.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held wind source and [[s02-X34]]. Read neutrally. Child answers alone; save for day 35.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public turns; keep each strip, source note and full exit. Longer: explain why this generation chart cannot identify a household mix.

### Words and supports

Review picture graph, key, scale, renewable and environmental effect. Read graph labels aloud. Frame: "Source ___ says ___." Supply a scribe without selecting the claim judgment.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 returns s02-X34 and s02-P34-1. Error E, renewable means no effects, needs separating replenishment from impact. Then revise the familiar crank model and day 29 record.
