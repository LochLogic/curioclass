# Section 2.4 private keys

TEACHER ONLY. Keep this piece separate from all child pages. Read privately in Before class. All cases/counts described as invented are supplied practice records, never promised measurements. These keys were solved cold for data entry; the independent answer-key read follows.

## s02-X30

Shape: repair a caption by tracing natural origin to useful energy.

Prompt: Invented for practice. Card A describes coal burned at a plant for town lighting. These lamps use town electricity. The caption calls a battery the natural resource. Repair the caption using a resource-to-use explanation.

Key and look-fors: Coal is the natural resource; a battery is made. Coal stores energy, converted at the generating plant into electricity for lamps. Do not insert a battery into this town-lighting path.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X31

Shape: two-source renewal comparison linked to continued community use.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A coastal community considers wind or a natural-gas deposit. Combine Sources A and B to explain renewal and its importance for continued use.

Key and look-fors: A identifies wind as naturally replenished, although output varies. B explains limited gas deposits and very long formation. Extraction uses up the deposit, not replaced on human timescales. Identify both sources in the comparison.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X32

Shape: combine extraction/use sources into two causal links.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A community uses coal from mountaintop removal and valley-fill mining. Combine the mining and burning sources into two environmental cause-and-effect links.

Key and look-fors: A: this mining changes land; polluted drainage may harm downstream aquatic wildlife. B: burning releases air pollutants, including gases causing acid rain that harms plants/aquatic animals. One supported causal link per source; not every mine has identical effects.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X33

Shape: source-and-trial synthesis of useful output and changed/remaining effects.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A wind generator supplies clinic lights. Matched blade trials show less noise afterward. Bird collisions stay unchanged. Use both sources and the trial record to explain improvement and a remaining effect.

Key and look-fors: A connects wind motion, generator and electricity/light. B identifies blade noise and component-production effects. Matched invented trials support reduced noise, not fewer collisions. Component-production effects remain; bird outcomes come from the invented record.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X34

Shape: ordered graph plus two-source claim judgment.

Prompt: Invented for practice. Regional chart: gas six shares, wind three, sunlight one. Claim: all this electricity uses resources with no environmental effects. Draw an ordered picture graph with a key. Use the chart and wind-effects source to judge the claim.

Key and look-fors: Descending rows gas six, wind three, sunlight one; key one picture per share. Chart includes wind; effects source names bird/bat collision risk, contradicting claim. Chart alone cannot show effects.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X35

Shape: new-device model joining matched refinement evidence and resource consequence.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A hand-wound buzzer toy uses a generator. Bent contact: sound on one of five winds. Straightened contact: sound on five of five. Operator, winds, toy and listening point stay the same. A case card reports mining its materials cleared habitat. Model its energy path and improvement evidence, including one resource consequence.

Key and look-fors: Winding supplies motion converted by generator to electrical energy; current carries it to sound. Straighter contact maintains loop more reliably; matched one-of-five versus five-of-five supports improvement. Mining cleared habitat; contact repair does not restore it.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## Practice tasks, private answers

### s02-P30-1

Sunlight, wind, oil and uranium are natural origins. Wires and batteries are made carriers. Source means energy origin or, separately, a card/page supplying information; not caption repair.

### s02-P31-1

Sunlight, wind, flowing water: renewable but variable. Coal, oil, gas, uranium: nonrenewable limited supplies. Nuclear used waste needs special management. Seven separate notes, not written coastal synthesis.

### s02-P32-1

Reservoir can cover natural land; surface mining removes soil/rock; burning solid fuel leaves ash. Attribute details separately, no combined two-link account of held mine/combustion facility.

### s02-P33-1

Bag-house captures particles from combustion gas. Fewer particles escape, not every pollutant removed; mining remains. No wind-trial improvement verdict.

### s02-P34-1

Colorado 2024 rounded gas three, coal three, wind three, solar one. Hydro/remaining round to zero, not absent. Counter order unrestricted; no ordered graph construction or no-effects claim critique.

### s02-P35-1

Use earlier actual transfer/circuit observations and separately supported resource/effect labels. Familiar class model, no unseen generator/refinement/resource integrated scenario.

### s02-P35-2
