# Day 99: From information to action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Set separate role and input cards, a token and the unit 6 formative record. Keep the touch exit covered. Prepare day 98 papers with feedback and retained Light L. No light apparatus today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 98's tickets and s06-P98, s06-P98-repair, s06-P98-talk, s06-P98-handoff. If many day 98 tickets show error A, spend five minutes on the incoming-light label. Error B gets five minutes on Light L's source-off/on rows, said to be invented for practice. Review day 95's reflected light. Children answer [[s06-P99-repair]] using separate labels; no new integrated drawing.

#### 3 min. Grade 3 retrieval

Review grade 3's structure and function: the shape fits the job, with part, job, who it helps. Review variation, alike in; different in. Use [[s06-K99-retrieval]] as a verbal picture, invented for practice. Children complete the row individually. One part does not guarantee survival.

#### 7 min. Brain link

Reopen day 98's question: light labels leave sound-guided action unexplained. Review grade 1's eyes and ears sense, legs move. Introduce brain, information and response. A sense organ receives information; the brain processes it and guides action. **Ask.** [[s06-K99-link]] before explaining. Then [[steps:s06-K99-link]]. Demonstrate separate eye/light and ear/sound matches. Information arrows inside the animal differ from incoming light arrows.

#### 10 min. Roles and inputs

Individuals do [[s06-P99]] on role and input cards. Keep eye and ear matches separate. Check receiving versus processing. If many omit processing, pause at the brain card and name its job before continuing. Do not rehearse a complete touch drawing.

#### 10 min. Interaction

Move the token among receiving, processing and action stations for a visible paper arrow. Children annotate separate role matches. The token stands for information, not a small object inside an animal. Review grade 3's rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes during work; record names. Public talk may be passed; all thirty submit notes. Retain original models; review second-color revision from grade 3.

#### 5 min. Exit E99

Reveal [[s06-X99]] for the first time. Read the invented case neutrally; each child draws independently. Collect tickets, role matches and page. Look for receiving touch information, brain processing and guided movement. Error D directly commands movement from the receiving part. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only; describe omissions without guessing an error.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy separate roles and prompts onto notebook paper; keep the exit covered. Use a board for stations. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy cards and prompts without a printer. Use a paper scrap as the token.

**Skip.** Group stations and the speaking rotation.

- **8 min. Return.** Read feedback and do [[s06-P99-repair]].
- **7 min. Part and job.** Child does [[s06-K99-retrieval]].
- **10 min. Roles.** Ask [[s06-K99-link]], then do [[s06-P99]].
  **Say.** "Which part processes information?" Expect the brain; eyes receive light and ears receive sound. Move the token between separate role cards.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X99]]. Child draws independently. Keep the first response.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated matches, preserving repair, brain teaching and exit. Extend by explaining what the paper token cannot show.

### Words and supports

Brain: processes information. Response: guided action. Read prompts aloud, enlarge cards, offer tactile pointers or faithful scribing. Accept home-language explanations with an individual paper record.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes. Return all day 99 papers in day 100's Return and repair. Error D gets five minutes contrasting receiving and processing. Then observe a protected catch or invented Catch C, without ranking children.
