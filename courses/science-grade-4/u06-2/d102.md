# Day 102: Different incoming information

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only keys and Sources U6-owl/U6-snake. Set animal cards and original schematic, role chart and retained signal counts. Keep the two-record exit private. No live animals, prey scenes or apparatus. Read one animal card at a time; do not display an integrated two-animal answer.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 101's tickets, tallies, s06-P101, s06-P101-repair, s06-P101-meaning and s06-P101-compare. If many day 101 tickets show error D, spend five minutes on separate receiving and processing roles with [[s06-P102-repair]]. Error E gets five minutes using actual early/later counts or invented S. Review day 101's remembered meaning; counts can be consistent with learning without directly showing processing. Keep valid non-improvement.

#### 15 min. Owl and snake evidence

Read Card O aloud: owl hearing can locate sound. Children label the incoming information on that card using [[s06-P102]]. Source U6-owl supports this fact. Then put O aside and read H: some snakes sense heat through facial pits; not all snakes have them. Source U6-snake supports this fact. Label this input separately. Never call heat sound or ordinary visible light. Review the earlier unit's heat word; sensing heat here is a new use, without mechanism.

**Ask.** [[s06-P102-source]]. Expect a pointer to the corresponding card statement. Source cards report scientific information; these are not classroom animal observations. If labels are confused, reread just that card and point to its receiving part.

#### 10 min. Model parts

Review day 99's system-model and day 101's remembered meaning. On the role chart, replace one incoming-information label at a time. Leave the processing role present. Children annotate their individual labels, not two complete animal pathways. Review incoming light from day 95: light reaches eyes; internal information arrows have a different job. Do not practice the exit's two-input/action revision.

#### 5 min. Evidence questions

Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns pointing to one card and its source statement. Review grade 3's rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes concurrently; record names. Others submit labels and source notes. Public talk may be passed. Keep a visual label beside each oral explanation.

#### 5 min. Exit E102

Reveal [[s06-X102]] for independent paper revision. The two hidden-input records are first seen now, labeled invented. Look for the different inputs and actions while retaining processing in both systems. Error D omits processing. An unsupported memory denial is error E; do not require memory details this case does not supply. Collect pages, animal labels and tickets. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the original schematic and copy one card at a time. Cover the exit. One projector may show cards; everyone still labels paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the cards and original schematic without a printer. No animal or equipment needed.

**Skip.** Group sharing and speaking rotation.

- **8 min. Return.** Read feedback, use [[s06-P102-repair]] and retained counts.
- **12 min. Cards.** Adult reads each animal card separately. Child does [[s06-P102]].
- **5 min. Evidence.** Do [[s06-P102-source]].
  **Say.** "Which statement supports this input label?" Expect a pointer to that card's hearing or heat statement. Change one input label on the role chart.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X102]]. Child revises independently. Keep the first response.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated label matching, keeping both source readings and exit. Extend by identifying something the schematic does not show directly.

### Words and supports

Facial pits: heat-sensing parts in some snakes. Review brain and information. Enlarge diagrams, read cards aloud and allow faithful scribing. Keep an individual explanation, including home-language responses.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes. Return every day 102 paper in day 103's ten-minute Return and repair. Error D gets five minutes of separate roles; error E gets five minutes of learned-meaning evidence. Then match visible flag meanings and completed actions, before a new prediction/test exit.
