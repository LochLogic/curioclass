# Day 103: Use meaning, then test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only keys. Set visible flag meanings, completed-action records and tokens. Fold each student page so its Exit box stays hidden. Keep prediction tickets private until the exit. Prepare feedback on day 102 papers and retained signal counts. No sound apparatus, live animals or hidden cues in practice.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 102's tickets, animal labels, s06-P102, s06-P102-repair and s06-P102-source. If many day 102 tickets show error D, spend five minutes on separate receiving and processing cards with [[s06-P103-repair]]. Error E gets five minutes on learned meanings using retained signal counts, actual or explicitly invented S. Review day 101's remembered meaning. Keep processing inferred rather than directly observed.

#### 10 min. Present-cue model

Use visible flag action cards, explicitly invented for practice. Keep meanings and completed actions visible. Stripes first means token up; dots means down. After relearning, stripes means down. Children move paper tokens to match each already-shown action. Review day 99's system-model: the receiving input stays visual while the meaning used in processing changes. No prediction or cue disappearance.

#### 15 min. Record matching

Individuals complete [[s06-P103]] on the flag piece. **Ask.** [[s06-P103-meaning]]. Expect the last stripes row's learned down meaning to match its completed down action. Point to that visible record before interpreting it. If many confuse meanings, reread one row at a time; never erase the first meaning from its earlier row.

Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns. Review grade 3's rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes during work; record names. Public talk may be passed; all thirty submit paper. Review second-color revision: retain the first matching notes.

#### 5 min. Limits and handoff

Individuals answer [[s06-P103-limit]]. Paper token movement represents action; it does not expose brain processing or show every possible animal response. Review the question-board routine: leave tomorrow's plant question open. How does a plant get water, protection and young without walking? No plant answer yet.

#### 5 min. Exit E103

Reveal [[s06-X103]] only. Give two minutes for each child's independent prediction on the half slip. Preserve that response. Then uncover Side 2's [[s06-X103-test]] Exit box for three minutes, recording confirmation or revision against the new invented observation. No answer or record appears before prediction. Look for hearing, processing and remembered meaning, with a limit on certainty. Error D skips processing; error E denies remembered meaning. Collect both responses, flag matches and page; use shown, shown with help, not yet seen only.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy visible flag rows and prompts, reserving both exit stages on separate covered papers. No device needed. Use paper scraps as tokens.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy visible flag rows without a printer. Use a paper scrap as a token.

**Skip.** Group matching and speaking rotation.

- **8 min. Return.** Read feedback and do [[s06-P103-repair]].
- **12 min. Match.** Keep flag rows visible; do [[s06-P103]] and [[s06-P103-meaning]].
  **Say.** "Which meaning matches the last completed action?" Expect the relearned down meaning. Compare the visible row; do not predict.
- **5 min. Limits.** Do [[s06-P103-limit]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X103]] for an independent prediction. Then uncover [[s06-X103-test]] in the Exit box. Child tests against the record. Keep both responses.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated token matching; keep repair and both exit stages. Extend by describing evidence needed beyond this one record. Optional extension: [[s06-C103]], not collected; stays in the folder.

### Words and supports

Review learned meaning, response and model. Enlarge records, read neutrally and offer faithful scribing. Home-language explanations count; retain each child's paper explanation.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 75 seconds each, 37.5 minutes including both exit responses and notes. Return all day 103 papers on day 104 in ten-minute Return and repair. Errors D/E get five minutes each using separate roles and familiar counts. Then open the colored-flower question about plants obtaining resources without walking.
