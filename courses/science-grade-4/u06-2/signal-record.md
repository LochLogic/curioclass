## Signal S

Invented for practice. Early: two correct actions from five opportunities.
Later: four correct actions from five opportunities.

Keep actual results even when later success is unchanged or lower.

Teacher: early cues are one, two, one, two, two claps.
Later cues are two, one, two, one, one claps.
Demonstrate one opportunity, then run equal rounds with practice between them.
Six groups work simultaneously for eighteen minutes. Do not rank children.
If a record cannot be recovered within the round, mark unclear and use S.
Collect cards/tallies during two-minute cleanup.
