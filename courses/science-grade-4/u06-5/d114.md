# Day 114: Rehearse one link

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return every day 113 paper with feedback. Choose its two common errors. Set out familiar contrasts below. Print only named record excerpts, without earlier questions or signal meanings. Keep s06-X114 and its teacher-only answer key concealed. No specimens, apparatus or new investigation. All practice pages stay in the folder; collect and record nothing today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-X113, s06-X113-response, s06-P113-plant, s06-P113, s06-P113-roots, s06-P113-inside and s06-P113-question with their annotations. If many day 113 tickets show error A, compare Light L's source-on/off rows. Error D: match receiving, processing and action roles separately. Error F: connect source card U's roots to internal transport. Give the two common letters five minutes each; unused time retrieves established labels. Children point or speak, without marking the page or revising writing.

#### 15 min. Evidence retrieval

Review day 95's reflect and source dependence; day 99's system-model and day 101's remembered meaning. **Ask.** [[s06-P114-retrieve]] Expect one relevant date or supplied-record label. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns, one label each. Those needing another contrast use these same familiar records independently. Keep actual unclear entries; if absent use Light L or Signal S, announced invented for practice. Review only components; do not evaluate a weak account against a new observation yet.

#### 10 min. Components

Use [[s06-P114-match]] orally. Review day 104's internal/external distinction and day 105's life-function labels. Present source card U, animal card C and separate flower events one at a time. Expected matches include roots/uptake and stomach/processing. Retain separate plant and animal functions; avoid a complete four-function argument. Compare only after each spoken attempt. No writing is drafted, revised or edited.

#### 5 min. Oral rehearsal

Use [[s06-P114-link]]. Each child explains one familiar connection to a partner. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each, within 4.5 minutes; keep no assessment evidence. Passing public talk is allowed. Other children rehearse simultaneously; do not claim all were heard by the teacher.

#### 5 min. E114 self-comparison

Reveal [[s06-X114]] for the first time. Announce invented for practice. Each child chooses one account and writes a private response. Then reveal only its corresponding self-comparison-key.md answer. Children compare silently; optional self-correction is oral. No score, collection, teacher marking or record. Keep this private response and today's practice in the child's folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy familiar labels and exact self-comparison stem onto covered paper. Reveal at the named time. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the record excerpts and private stem onto paper when printing is unavailable.

**Skip.** Class reports and rotating-six hearing.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 113 feedback and point to a familiar contrast.
- **10 min. Retrieve.** Child answers [[s06-P114-retrieve]] orally.
  **Say.** "Which record supports that label?" Expect one relevant date or supplied label.
- **10 min. Components.** Use [[s06-P114-match]] and [[s06-P114-link]] orally. Reveal each answer afterward.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X114]]. Child writes privately, then compares with its answer. Keep it; no written revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated oral reports, preserving the private self-comparison. Longer: explain another familiar component orally.

### Words and supports

Review reflect, information, internal, external and evidence. Read directions neutrally; enlarge labels. Home-language explanations or a faithful scribe preserve the private response.

### Tomorrow

Day 115 begins unfamiliar independent paper models. Hold all teaching feedback through days 115 and 116; repair resumes on day 117. Today's private comparison is neither collected nor used to assign a new assessment score.
