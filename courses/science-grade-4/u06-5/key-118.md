# Day 118 answer key: teacher only

Keep away from children and the display. Use the private record and single scale in checks-key-118-120.md.
The successful answer below is one sample; accept other evidence-linked forms.

## C-E118-table

**Look for; successful sample.** W: ocean ridge and trench, two earthquakes and one volcano. X: coastal mountains, two earthquakes and one volcano. Y: inland mountains, one earthquake. Z: inland plain, one volcano. Keep each event at its mapped location.

**Unsuccessful sample.** W has no features because the ocean floor is flat. (error AA).

## C-E118-audit

**Look for; successful sample.** Y has inland mountains as well as X coastal mountains. W has a ridge and trench. Earthquakes cluster at W/X with an inland Y exception; volcanoes occur at W/X and separately at Z. Associations do not establish causes or cover the whole Earth.

**Unsuccessful sample.** All events must be coastal, even those marked inland. (error AA).

## C-E118-limit

**Look for; successful sample.** Nothing certain about future safety. An empty space means no selected event is plotted there in this record; other events or future hazards remain possible.

**Unsuccessful sample.** Every empty space is safe forever. (error AB).

## C-E118-add

**Look for; successful sample.** For example, removable door seals proposed at four tokens, with no digging. Seals could reduce water entering stores; their performance needs tests. Accept another feasible solution with a proposed cost of at most six tokens and a causal harm-reduction account. A warning alone needs protective action and does not keep stores dry.

**Unsuccessful sample.** Dig a new river channel despite the no-digging limit. (error AD).

## C-E118-compare

**Look for; successful sample.** Wall and raised storage meet the six-token/no-digging limits. Both keep nine model stores dry in small flow. Large flow wets seven behind the wall and four with raised storage: raising reduces wet stores more here, but neither meets the all-dry goal in large flow. The proposed third solution needs comparable tests; do not invent its performance.

**Unsuccessful sample.** The wall meets the goal in every flow because small flow wets no stores. (error AD).

## C-E118-harm

**Look for; successful sample.** Solutions can reduce wet stores or help people move away, but do not stop flooding. Both tested designs leave wet stores in large flow. A warning needs delivery and protective action; it does not remove the water.

**Unsuccessful sample.** A warning stops the river from flooding. (error AC).

## C-E118-model

**Look for; successful sample.** Lamp → dial → mirror → eye. The lamp illuminates the dull dial; reflected light from the dial reaches the mirror and is redirected into the eye. The screen prevents direct dial-to-eye viewing. Direction arrows represent light, not sight emitted by eyes.

**Unsuccessful sample.** Eye → mirror → dial supplies the light. (error AE).

## C-E118-predict

**Look for; successful sample.** The dial disappears from this view although the lamp still illuminates it. The only reflected viewing path is interrupted; an opaque card blocks that segment.

**Unsuccessful sample.** The dial stays visible because light passes through the opaque card. (error AF).

## C-E118-test

**Look for; successful sample.** Keep lamp-to-dial illumination. Mark dial-to-mirror blocked and no dial light reaching the eye through that route. Retain or revise the prediction visibly, citing disappearance despite continued illumination. Do not remove illumination merely because viewing failed.

**Unsuccessful sample.** Erase the lamp because an unseen dial cannot be illuminated. (error AF).
