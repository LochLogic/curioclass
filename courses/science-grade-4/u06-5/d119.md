# Day 119: Test the organism brief

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read key-119.md. Prepare a flap over the silent Test record. Keep day 118's assessment closed; it returns on day 120. Print the organism records with the invented label intact. Leave teacher answers out of sight.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and pencils only. No organisms, foods, bodily comparisons or personal disclosures. These are fictional organisms near the invented town. No senses game or live sound trial. Filing fits Collect.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Continue day 118's evidence audit with new organism records. Announce invented for practice. Read neutrally without naming a preferred model. Each child works independently. Explain where predictions are boxed and when the covered record opens.

#### 14 min. Model critique

Allow five minutes for [[C-E119-select]], five for [[C-E119-predict]] and four for [[C-E119-test]]. Keep the Test record covered until predictions are boxed. Children select and annotate an existing model, then test a changed input and revise from the revealed result. This contrasts with day 115's blank-page model. Note unsupported selection, missing brain processing or learned meaning for day 120, without correcting it now. No individual speaking round; every child returns paper.

#### 25 min. Paired repairs

Children repair two claims independently: thirteen minutes for [[C-E119-plant]] and twelve for [[C-E119-animal]]. Read the record labels and briefs without explaining links. The plant guide and rootless observation have different evidence roles. Accept short linked sentences, labeled arrows or a table. Judge inside/outside cooperation across survival, growth, behavior and reproduction, with relevant evidence. These are critiques of supplied claims, not new versions of day 116's paired essays. If many omit interactions, preserve their responses for day 120's structure route; do not teach during the check.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect both sides. After class allow ninety minutes for thirty papers using the same shown/shown with help/not yet seen scale as day 118. Keep initial results separate. Write one missing link and combine both days' feedback into at most two repair moves per child for day 120. No portfolio is scored again.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy models and both records on paper, with the Test record covered. One projector shows records only, never answers or another child's work.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the records and model choices on paper. Use a spare sheet as a flap.

**Skip.** Classroom collection and group routing.

- **3 min. Assessment directions.** Say all records are invented for practice. Read without hints.
- **14 min. Sensing.** Child answers [[C-E119-select]] and [[C-E119-predict]] alone. Box predictions; uncover [[C-E119-test]] afterward.
- **25 min. Claim repairs.** Child answers [[C-E119-plant]] and [[C-E119-animal]] using the supplied records.
- **3 min. Keep evidence.** Save first answers for day 120. Keep adult answers separate.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both organisms and the prediction-before-test order. Two sittings may preserve the full independent work. Add no extra scored task.

### Words and supports

Review sense organ, brain, remembered, internal, external and the four life functions. Read labels without choosing evidence. Faithful dictation or labeled links count. Language fluency does not substitute for scientific interactions.

### Tomorrow

Day 120 returns day 118 and today's checks once, then repairs selected gaps and preserves evidence. Errors AG and AH need receiving/processing/action or remembered meaning; errors AI and AJ need interacting structures. Keep these needs distinct from day 118's map and light errors.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Sources U6-sensing, U6-structures, U6-vegetative and U6-reproductive. Outcomes invented for practice.
