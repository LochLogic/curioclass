# Day 118: Audit the town advice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read key-118.md and prepare the private record. Copy maps and records exactly. Cover the final Test record with a paper flap. Keep answers concealed. Day 117's fresh-response notes inform access arrangements, not hints. No old work is scored again.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and pencils only, no light apparatus or wet model. The town, maps and trials are invented for practice. No personal disaster accounts. Collect paper and pencils within Collect.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Pick up day 117's handoff: use evidence to test claims on unfamiliar records. Announce all records invented for practice. Children work alone. Read text neutrally; a faithful scribe may record a child's own links. Show the flap and response spaces without interpreting evidence.

#### 24 min. Town audit

Allow eight minutes for [[C-E118-table]], [[C-E118-audit]] and [[C-E118-limit]]. Children table the aligned relief/event maps before correcting the advice. The symbols are locations, not event predictions. Allow sixteen minutes for [[C-E118-add]], [[C-E118-compare]] and [[C-E118-harm]]. Children propose a third option, then compare all options under both flows. Leave proposed performance unknown. Record missing map locators, limits, condition evidence or action links privately; give no teaching or correctness feedback. If many struggle, retain independent evidence and route them to day 120.

#### 15 min. Ray repair

The illuminated dial recalls unit 2's device context, but this dull dial reflects light. This is a mirror view, not day 115's illumination-around-screen case. Give six minutes for [[C-E118-model]] and four for [[C-E118-predict]]. Children draw arrows with labels, then box predictions. Reveal the final Test record only afterward; allow five minutes for [[C-E118-test]], annotating the same model box and preserving first ideas. Judge what evidence changes or supports the model, not diagram neatness. All submit paper; no individual speaking round.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect both sides and store them for day 120. Count papers while children file pencils. After class allow ninety minutes to mark with the teacher key's single scale. Record each item and one next-step cue. Select at most two five-minute repair moves per child; do not total scores.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy both maps and records onto two sheets, keeping the Test record covered. One projector shows only the current neutral record; answers remain private.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the records onto paper; cover the Test record with another sheet.

**Skip.** Class collection and group routing. Keep all independent responses.

- **3 min. Assessment directions.** Say these records are invented for practice. Read without hints.
- **24 min. Town audit.** Child answers [[C-E118-table]], [[C-E118-audit]], [[C-E118-limit]], [[C-E118-add]], [[C-E118-compare]] and [[C-E118-harm]] alone.
- **15 min. Light model.** Child answers [[C-E118-model]] and [[C-E118-predict]]. Box the prediction before revealing [[C-E118-test]].
- **3 min. Keep evidence.** File first answers for day 120. Keep the adult answer key out of sight.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both records and predictions intact. Use the same independent work across two sittings if needed. No added assessment items.

### Words and supports

Review map key, ridge, trench, opaque and reflect, from days 78 and 95 to 96. Enlarge text or read neutrally. Accept labeled diagrams, concise sentences or faithful dictation, without supplying science links.

### Tomorrow

Day 119 critiques sensing models and organism claims. Keep today's work closed until day 120; return feedback there once. Errors AA to AF route to day 120's map, design or light repair.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Sources U5-map and U6-seeing. All records invented for practice.
