# Section 6.5: Connect, check and repair the models

Days 113 to 117.

## Science and evidence

Day 113 closes separate light, animal-information and plant charts. Checks on 115/116 use new cases. Day 114 is oral rehearsal and one private paper self-comparison; collect nothing. Day 117 returns both assessments once. Sensitive-plant folding shows behavior; its protective value is not established by the given records. A whole pod does not show seeds inside. Sea-otter fur helps warmth while internal resources and reproduction also matter.

## Expected ideas and response

A/B/C/I route to light; D/E to sensing; F/G/H to plants; F to animals. Missing evidence gets descriptive feedback, not an invented letter. Two common letters get five minutes each on 114. No repair or feedback occurs on 115/116. Day 117: feedback five, simultaneous groups ten, revision/verification twenty, fresh explanation five, handoff five.

## Before class and equipment

Print assessment-115.md and assessment-116.md only for their day. Cover evidence-test flaps until predictions are recorded. Print teacher-only assessment-key.md, assessment-record.md and repair-cards.md. No equipment queues. Neutral directions/labels may be read; no scientific hints during checks.

## Fallback and record

Mark after each check, about sixty minutes for thirty papers. Original four cases total 44 points; component rows split single asks. Keep 0/1/2 scoring only on assessment-record.md. Route one fresh response per child on 117; review it in Handoff without rescore.

## Participation and access

Read labels and directions aloud without supplying the answer. Offer enlarged diagrams, tactile arrows, a scribe or home-language explanation. Public talk is optional. No home, pet, illness or body disclosures. Pairs take simultaneous forty-five-second turns. Teacher hears a rotating six, forty-five seconds each, within five minutes during work. Everyone else submits the paper record; the next named hand-back returns it. Use record.md to note who was heard. Initial ideas are descriptive. Other formative responses use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep first models and revise in a second color. Do not rank children.

## Boundaries and held cases

Use only the practice pieces named here before the exit. Exit/end prompts come from items.csv, never an improvised parallel. Dedicated assessment sheets stay teacher-held until their day. Print exit: half; at most two judged paper asks per slip. Day 113 puts its plant response in an Exit box. Day 117 assigns one route only. Factual background is in U6 source rows; all situation sequences and fallback outcomes are invented for practice.
