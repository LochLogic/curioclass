# Day 113: Evidence for our models

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Return every day 112 paper and annotation. Prepare separate light, information and plant charts. Use dated class records first; retain unclear entries. If missing, show only supplied Light L or Plant P, announced invented for practice. Print only the named record excerpts, without earlier prompts. Conceal exit diagram, half slips and Side 2. Paper records only; no specimens or apparatus.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 112's papers, including the written task, with their feedback inside this existing slot. Return s06-X112, s06-X112-animal and all day 112 practice annotations. For recurring error H in day 112 practice, use five minutes on [[s06-P113-roots]]: review day 105's missing-root limit. For error F on either day 112 exit, use five minutes on [[s06-P113-inside]]: review inside stem paths for the plant and day 109's inside processing for the animal. Missing comparison evidence or a limit gets a private note. Missing functions receive descriptive feedback. Every child marks both responses.

#### 5 min. Reopen anchors

Honor day 112's Tomorrow: connect the two original anchors through separate charts. Review day 94's invented cat sequence and day 104's flower record. Point to seeing, sound response and water-movement questions separately. Children choose a chart for [[s06-P113]]. Keep unanswered questions visible; invite no home or pet accounts.

#### 15 min. Evidence consensus

Review consensus from day 91: an evidence-supported account that can change. **Ask.** [[s06-K113]] Expect source on/off, with view unchanged; reveal the answer afterward. Place that evidence tag beside source dependence. Review day 99's system-model: receiving, brain processing, action. On the separate information chart, attach the familiar response-order record; brain processing is supplied, not directly observed. On the plant chart, attach dated internal-color evidence and a separate source card U tag. Children finish [[s06-P113]]. Hear rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes; log names. Everyone submits a locator.

#### 10 min. Closing questions

Children complete [[s06-P113-question]] independently, then file dated evidence. Review day 95's light-ray model, day 101's remembered meaning and day 104's inside/outside distinction only on their separate charts. Summarize the anchors: seeing needs incoming light; information is processed before action; interacting plant parts move resources. Cut flowers show transport, not roots. Retain uncertainty; do not rehearse a complete new-scene account.

#### 5 min. Exit E113

Reveal the invented scene, half-sheet [[s06-X113]], and Side 2's [[s06-X113-response]] and [[s06-P113-plant]]. Read exact stems neutrally. Children answer independently with brief labeled links. Collect slips, Exit box and practice. Mark afterward: 30 sets at 90 seconds, 45 minutes, including locator feedback. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy record excerpts and cover the exact exit stems and diagram until exit. Each child answers on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the named record excerpts and held diagram if printing is unavailable. Label supplied records invented for practice.

**Skip.** Rotating-six hearing and class reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Use [[s06-P113-roots]] and [[s06-P113-inside]].
- **15 min. Evidence.** Read the separate charts. Child answers [[s06-K113]] and [[s06-P113]].
  **Say.** "Which condition changed?" Expect the lamp switched off while the view stayed clear.
- **5 min. Question.** Child completes [[s06-P113-question]]. Keep the uncertainty.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the scene and [[s06-X113]], [[s06-X113-response]] and [[s06-P113-plant]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeat reports, preserving repairs and all independent responses. Longer: locate another date supporting an existing chart label.

### Words and supports

Review reflect, information, internal, external and evidence from this unit. Read labels aloud; enlarge diagrams. Allow a faithful scribe without supplying links. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Return every paper on day 114. Use two common letters, five minutes each: error A reverses light; error D omits processing; error F excludes inside parts. Remaining letters use familiar contrasts during retrieval. Day 114 rehearses orally and keeps its self-comparison private.
