## Science and evidence

Day 118 joins map data analysis with Earth patterns, design comparison with cause and effect,
and light modeling with cause and effect. Day 119 joins sensing models and structure arguments
with interacting systems. Symbols at different map sites are separate events.
The dull dial is viewed in a mirror; it is not a light source.
The plant's rootless observation cannot establish root uptake; the rooted guide supplies that link.
The animal is a fictional mammal, not a named species or a measured animal.
Its stipulated record describes interacting structures, not guaranteed natural outcomes.

## Day 120 repair directions

Each child works at most two five-minute moves in Targeted repair, on parallel cases.
AA/AB map: trace R's inland event and ridge; contrast a blank with a safety claim.
AC/AD design: separate gentle/strong conditions; cross out guarantees, preserving failed results.
AE/AF light: mark illumination and viewing separately; trace which segment the flap blocks.
AG/AH sensing: separate receiving, processing and action; add the remembered tap meaning.
AI/AJ structures: join outside entry to inside work. For AJ, first use day 117's familiar
flower-event cards: stamen supplies pollen, pistil receives it, internal ovules later form seeds.
For several missed routes choose the two most important moves; list others as remaining needs.
For complete work, identify a limit in either parallel record.
If more than ten children share a letter, lead its five-minute move during Targeted repair;
other routes use these directions and printed records. No extra return cycle is created.
AK gets immediate evidence-label feedback in Handoff, alongside any AE cue from C-X120.
