# Day 120: Repair and preserve the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use both checks' feedback to assign at most two five-minute moves per child. Read key-120.md and the repair directions in checks-key-118-120.md. Set four simultaneous routes: map/design, light, sensing, structures. Lay out returned papers and familiar flower-event cards for error AJ. Keep fresh exit tickets concealed. All parallel records are invented for practice.

### Safety and set-up

Paper, pencils and cards only. No plant or animal handling, apparatus or personal accounts. Missing folder pieces stay marked missing; no investigation is recreated.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return feedback

Return day 118's assessment in two minutes and day 119's assessment in three. Children locate feedback, keeping first responses visible. No assessment is rescored. Review day 117's second-color revision routine and the established independent-paper routine. If many share a gap, lead its move during Targeted repair; other routes work from teacher-key directions.

#### 10 min. Targeted repair

All routes work simultaneously, at most two five-minute moves each. Use fresh parallel cases: [[C-P120-map]], [[C-P120-design]], [[C-P120-light]], [[C-P120-sense]] or [[C-P120-structure]]. Teacher leads the largest-need route; the others use the printed evidence and key's directions, with adult verification next. Map/design addresses AA/AB then AC/AD; light AE/AF; sensing AG/AH; structures AI/AJ. For AJ, first order familiar flower-event cards before the new structure comparison. Establish any unsupported prerequisite as new learning, never presume a planned-only row was taught. If a record is unreadable, recopy its exact supplied text; do not fabricate observations.

#### 15 min. Preserve evidence

Children annotate the parallel explanation and complete [[C-P120-preserve]]. Keep original checks unchanged alongside revisions. Select the water-location map, roots/internal-transport model, energy-arrow model and design-test-refinement record; mark missing pieces missing. Label evidence origin, dates, changed links and unresolved needs. Teacher verifies four routes for two minutes each. During the remaining seven minutes, hear a rotating six at forty-five seconds each, recording names; all others hand back paper. These papers are reviewed now and stay in folders. No claim that every child was heard.

#### 7 min. Fresh explanation

Close repair cards. Give [[C-X120]] on a half-sheet ticket. Announce the museum record invented for practice. Each child repairs one new model and identifies evidence origin independently. Preserve the flawed original beside the correction. This fresh case differs from the parallel blocked-view record. No assessment rescore.

#### 8 min. Handoff

Review and return thirty exit tickets at sixteen seconds each. Use only shown, shown with help or not yet seen, on a separate fresh-evidence column. Give immediate error AE or AK feedback on incoming light or supplied evidence. Children attach tickets and unresolved needs to folders while waiting. File practice with students after review. Keep day 118 and day 119 results distinct from today's revisions. No later return day is promised.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the assigned parallel case and museum prompt on separate paper. No second color: box additions. One projector shows neutral records, with fresh case hidden until needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Box changes instead of using a second color. Copy the assigned case and exit ticket.

**Skip.** Groups and the teacher hearing round.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read both returned checks. Choose one missing link.
- **10 min. Repair.** Use the assigned [[C-P120-map]], [[C-P120-design]], [[C-P120-light]], [[C-P120-sense]] or [[C-P120-structure]].
  **Say.** "Which evidence changes this claim?" Expect the specific link in that case's answer.
- **10 min. Preserve.** Complete [[C-P120-preserve]]. Mark missing pieces missing.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** Child answers [[C-X120]] alone. Review together afterward and attach it to the folder.

### Shorter or longer

Repair one priority link, retaining fresh evidence and handoff labels. Longer: name a future observation that could resolve one remaining question, without claiming it occurred.

### Words and supports

Review map key, reflected light, brain processing, interacting structures and evidence. Preserve supplied versus observed distinctions. Offer faithful dictation and labeled arrows. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

This is the course's final day. Today's exit tickets return in Handoff. Preserve water maps, plant transport, energy arrows and design revisions for the grade 5 handoff. Keep unresolved needs explicit, with no promised summer work or missing measurements invented.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Sources U5-map, U6-seeing, U6-sensing, U6-structures, U6-vegetative and U6-reproductive.
