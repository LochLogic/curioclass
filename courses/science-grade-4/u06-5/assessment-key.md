## s06-E1-model

Lamp to mirror to tile to eye. Mirror redirects illumination onto tile; tile reflects light directly toward eye, not an image seen in mirror.

**Components:** Source-to-mirror link | Mirror-to-tile-to-eye links | Reflection explanation. Each: 2 complete, 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect; six points. | Shape: illumination-around-screen model

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Lamp to mirror to tile to eye. Mirror redirects illumination onto tile; tile reflects light directly to my eye. | Does not meet: Eye lights tile. (error A).

## s06-E1-predict

Tile becomes unseen because its only illumination is interrupted, despite the open view.

**Components:** Two points for supported prediction; 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect. | Shape: blocked-source prediction

**Sample comparison:** Meets: The tile disappears. Blocking lamp-to-mirror light removes its only illumination, even though my view stays open. | Does not meet: Tile stays visible without illumination. (error B).

## s06-E1-test

Confirm or revise to show dependence on illumination: lamp to mirror to tile to eye. Keep original prediction visible.

**Components:** Two points for evidence-linked confirmation/revision; 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect. | Shape: seeing evidence test

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Disappearing with the card supports my illumination model. Reappearing without it supports the restored lamp-mirror-tile-eye route. | Does not meet: Light crosses the opaque card. (error I).

## s06-E2-model

Noses receive odor; brains process it with different remembered experiences. Brain-guided responses differ despite same present odor.

**Components:** Information/receiving organs | Brain processing | Different responses | Remembered-experience interpretation. Each: 2 complete, 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect; eight points. | Shape: same-odor different-history model

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Both noses receive the same odor. Their brains process different remembered experiences and guide approach for P but retreat for Q. | Does not meet: Nose commands legs directly. (error D).

## s06-E2-predict

Approach is reasonable as experience changes learned meaning; justified uncertainty about an individual is acceptable.

**Components:** One point for reasoned changed-history prediction, zero absent/incorrect. Prediction plus test retain the original two-point component. | Shape: changed-history prediction

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Q may approach now because its safe food experiences can change the odor's remembered meaning. | Does not meet: Remembered experience cannot change action. (error E).

## s06-E2-test

Confirm or revise: experience changed learned meaning and response without a changed nose or odor. Do not generalize inevitable approach to all animals.

**Components:** One point for evidence-based test, zero absent/incorrect. | Shape: changed-history evidence test

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Q's approach supports changed remembered meaning. The odor and nose need not change for experience to guide a different action. | Does not meet: Same odor must always cause the same action. (error E).

## s06-E3

Roots, inside paths and leaves supply resources supporting survival. Larger leaves support growth. Touch folding is behavior; these records do not establish protection. Flower cooperation and pollen transfer followed by pod support reproduction. Whole pod does not show seeds or every formation step. No guaranteed survival.

**Components:** Survival | Growth | Behavior | Reproduction | Internal/external interactions | Relevant evidence and limit. Each: 2 complete, 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect; twelve points. Never require unsupported protection. | Shape: new-plant four-function argument with interaction and limit

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Roots and inside paths supply leaves with water for survival. Larger leaves support growth. Touch folding shows behavior, but not that it protects the plant. Flowers cooperate in reproduction after pollen transfer. The whole pod does not reveal seeds, so seed development remains incompletely shown. | Does not meet: Petals alone make seeds. (error G).

## s06-E4

Fur and air retain warmth for survival. Milk and digestion nourish growing young. Senses, brain and paws guide grooming behavior. Uterus supports prenatal development; milk glands nourish young after birth, supporting reproduction and growth. Fur alone does not suffice; records do not guarantee every pup survives.

**Components:** Survival | Growth | Behavior | Reproduction | Internal/external interactions | Relevant evidence and limit. Each: 2 complete, 1 partial, 0 absent/incorrect; twelve points. | Shape: new-mammal four-function argument with interaction and limit

**Sample comparison:** Meets: Fur traps air for warmth and survival. Milk and digestion nourish growing young. Brain, senses and paws guide grooming. Uterus supports development before birth; milk glands support young afterward. These inside and outside parts cooperate, but do not guarantee every pup survives. | Does not meet: Only fur matters. (error F).
