# Section 5.4 exit answers

Teacher only. Name in Before class; keep outside student print.

## s05-X90

Use raised structures with an alert and a usable inland route for the river-and-coast setting. Raising needs materials and the route needs testing; neither stops an event or guarantees protection. Other justified combinations count.

Connect at least two solutions to mapped/test evidence and name a cost, access or untested-condition tradeoff. Trench alone does not predict timing. Shape: two-sentence combination argument with tradeoff.
