# Day 93: Use feedback, then show what you already know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-key.md. Prepare privately returned day 92 reserved assessment packet and portfolio and feedback sheet, plus day 91 separate consensus notes and both exit papers. Set unit 5 record sheet, unit 5 assessment record, parallel repair packets, familiar U/W records and T cards. Assign at most two five-minute moves per child. Side 2 is the next-unit readiness sheet plus fresh explanation; keep it covered. Keys remain private.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback

Return day 92's assessment and portfolio with component feedback, plus day 91's written task, diagnostic papers and separate consensus notes. Each child identifies one priority. Keep first marks; no rescore. If many show the same error on day 92's items, assign that repair route. Ready children identify an unsupported claim in their own work. No child demonstrates a mistake publicly.

#### 10 min. Targeted repair

Review the parallel packet routine. All complete [[s05-P93-repair]] on paper: at most two five-minute moves, drawn from four routes. Relief/belts handles errors A/H by U key/relief tracing, then B/G by event-location/cluster tags. Limits handles error C with selection labels and unsupported blank-area inferences. Conditions handles errors D/E on the child's own two-flow teaching record. Warnings handles error F with T's received-message/action arrows, then error I with separate three-plus-five strips. Review day 89 coverage; never use the lookout diagnostic as teaching.

Teacher visits four groups for two minutes each, plus two routing minutes. Children use familiar wrong/right guides between visits. Unresolved gaps remain noted; there is no third move or speaking queue.

#### 15 min. Revision

Alone, [[s05-P93-revise]] on original assessment papers and retained portfolio. Review grade 3's second-color routine: keep original observations and first answers. Box revisions if needed. Children cite evidence beside changes. Teacher checks marked components and writes dated notes, preserving first scores separately. Collect all repair and revision records; missing evidence remains unavailable.

#### 5 min. Fresh explanation

Reveal [[s05-D93]] now. Four minutes individually on paper, one collecting. Neutral dictation records the child's words. Formative delivery/action note only; no new score. Give no answer before readiness.

#### 10 min. Next readiness

Reveal [[s05-R93a]] for four minutes, then [[s05-R93b]] for four minutes. Read stems neutrally; no model, reminders or coaching. Last two minutes collect and sort. Keep illumination, reception and movement-response notes distinct from unit 5 error letters. These are diagnostics, not claims that grade 3 taught seeing or sensing.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy familiar repair evidence and covered diagnostic prompts separately. No second color: box revisions. No disaster, body or family disclosures.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy prompts without a printer. Box revisions without another color.

**Skip.** Group routing and public talk.

- **3 min. Feedback.** Return the child's original assessment and portfolio.
- **8 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P93-repair]] on one priority teaching record.
- **14 min. Revise.** Child does [[s05-P93-revise]] beside the original.
  **Say.** "Which evidence supports your change?" Expect a located observation addressing the feedback.
- **5 min. Fresh explanation.** Reveal [[s05-D93]], answered independently.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Child answers [[s05-R93a]] and [[s05-R93b]], four minutes each. Collect for two minutes. Give no hints or answers.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce revision volume to the priority component; retain diagnostics and readiness minutes. Extend by recording an unfinished evidence need.

### Words and supports

Review unit 5 map and protection words only during repair. Readiness receives neutral reading and faithful scribing, with no concept cues.

### Tomorrow

After class, read D93 and readiness: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes. Add descriptive notes only, no second scale. Return repairs, revised assessment paper and D93 day 94 with private notes; unresolved warning-action needs stay in the handoff notes. Day 94 opens the cat-at-night anchor, questions and initial seeing model. Send R93a illumination gaps and R93b reception/response gaps to day 95's shared 15-minute readiness response. No readiness answers taught today.
