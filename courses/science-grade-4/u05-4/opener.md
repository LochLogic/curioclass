# Section 5.4 opener: Joining evidence and judging protection

Days 89 to 93 join maps, causal protection and constrained arguments. Monitoring coverage means
where sensors observe; notice coverage means who receives information. Both differ from ability
to act. Add delay and travel from one starting event before comparing with arrival. Invented whole
minutes here are not forecasts or real route advice. Natural tsunami warnings can require action
without waiting for an official message; follow local official procedures (U5-alert/U5-route).

Review tradeoff, hazard/design/harm arrows, design-cycle chart, observation/inference and exception.
Teach monitoring, coverage and delay explicitly day 89. Keep timing strips separate from message
reach, never paired E89 verdict/sensor gap practice. Day 90 returns portfolio for second-color revision;
hearing considers single solutions for settlement T, no compact package account. Day 91 keeps map
tags and protection notes separate. All thirty return paper; hear six voluntary turns, 4.5 minutes.

Print interval/coverage strips, feedback sheet, consensus strips, four repair routes and reserved
assessment. Keep K/L/M, costs and wall-better-than-raising evidence hidden until day 92 Launch.
E90's raising-better record differs deliberately: read each new record. Before class name teacher-only-key.md
for assessment and teacher-only-exit-key.md for exits; never include them in student print.

Day 92: Launch 3, maps 20, packages 18, Collection 4 minutes; no repair or apparatus rotations.
Student packet is within 350 words with paired maps and compact tables. Neutral reading, enlargement,
tactile versions and dictation preserve independent reasoning. After class score six components each
for E1/E2 and two for revised portfolio, assessment-record.md uses one component scale: 2 complete, 1 relevant incomplete,
0 absent/incorrect, unavailable opportunity blank with note. Keep /24 and /4 separate. About four
minutes per child marking, untested. No additional portfolio sitting time or repeated score.

Day 93: private feedback 5, at most two parallel five-minute repair moves 10, Revision 15, fresh D93 5,
next readiness 10. Routes: relief/belts A/B/G/H; limits C; conditions D/E; warnings F/I. Four two-minute
teacher visits plus two routing minutes; every child returns repaired paper, originals retained. Revision
gets a note beside first marks. Readiness R93a/R93b four minutes each plus two collecting; do not teach
answers first. Carry separate illumination/sensing gaps to day 95, preserve day 94 anchor. No personal
history, blame or real evacuation planning. Public source cards are own-word paraphrases with locators.
