# Day 89: From information to protective action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md and generated answers. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, returned day 88 papers and larger-flow log, flood logs and Flood fallback W, cause and action strips. Lay out warning interval and coverage strips and four relevant cards from eight hazard research cards. Hide other cards. Cut today's cards; keep preparation notes on the teacher master. Print one-item half slips from items.csv; cover the Exit box. Do not distribute the shared two-item exit master.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 88's papers, including s05-X88-revise and s05-X88-improve, with their feedback inside this existing slot. Return day 88's written task, both exit papers and larger-flow log. If many show error D, mark the recorded water crossing, five minutes. For error E, compare both conditions, five minutes. Use [[s05-P89-repair]]; ready children identify untested conditions. Review failure and condition from days 84 to 88. Preserve observations; use W only as invented for practice.

#### 15 min. Warning teaching

Review grade 3's hazard, harm and warning chain: warning, get ready, less harm. Read volcano/tsunami cards aloud. **Ask.** [[s05-K89-monitor]] before revealing [[steps:s05-K89-monitor]]. Review monitor from day 83. Teach coverage: places observed or people reached, two different records. Monitoring can inform uncertain forecasts; information still requires delivery and usable action.

Model [[s05-K89-time]], then [[steps:s05-K89-time]]. Children label strips alongside. Delay is time before the alert arrives. The whole delay-plus-travel interval must fit before arrival; travel alone omits waiting, error I. No arrival verdict here. State: real natural tsunami warnings may require immediate action without waiting for official messages. Follow local official procedures; these invented minutes are never route advice.

#### 15 min. Coverage audit

Review invented inland settlement T. Pairs do [[s05-P89-action]], sorting delivery separately from action access. Partner turns: 45 seconds each, simultaneously. **Ask.** Who receives information but cannot act? Expect storehouse: blocked doorway. Model the mill's delivery-to-action-to-less-exposure arrows; the flood continues. If error F appears, trace what changes people's exposure. Hear a rotating six volunteers, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; passing is allowed. Collect every delivery/action record.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the new record now. Alone, [[s05-X89-time]] on the half slip; [[s05-X89-gap]] in the paper Exit box. Four minutes answer, one collect both. Read stems exactly; scribe only the child's words.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy cards, strips and blanks beforehand; conceal reserved stems. Paper only, no local routes or personal disaster accounts.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy cards and strips without a printer. Adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Public hearing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P89-repair]] with retained logs.
- **12 min. Information.** Read the four cards. Do [[s05-K89-monitor]] and [[s05-K89-time]]. Join the paper intervals.
  **Say.** "What must happen after information arrives?" Expect a usable protective action; the event continues.
- **13 min. Audit.** Child does [[s05-P89-action]]. Separate receiving a notice from being able to act.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal reserved record. Child answers [[s05-X89-time]] and [[s05-X89-gap]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten voluntary sharing, keeping repairs and exit. Extend by describing a blocked action's possible improvement.

### Words and supports

Review warning, hazard and harm. New: coverage, delay. Read cards neutrally; offer enlarged print and home-language rehearsal.

### Tomorrow

After class, mark both exit papers: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Practice notes take 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Return all papers day 90 Return and repair. Error F rebuilds action arrows; error I joins intervals, five minutes each. Monitoring-gap misses get a brief coverage conference during work. Day 90 also revises day 88's portfolio.
