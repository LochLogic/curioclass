# Section 5.1 opener: Reading Earth patterns

Days 77 to 82 join map observations, Earth features and patterns. Keys and record limits stay
with maps. Coastline means the water edge; continental edge can lie offshore. Ridge is raised
seafloor; trench a long deep depression. Elevation is height relative to sea level; depth is distance
below water surface. Dark shading has no universal meaning. Contour joins equal-height places.
Clustering is association, not proof of cause. No mechanisms or predictions are taught.

Review key, land-water model, observation/inference, exception and question board in their earlier
words. The ledger now records written units 3/4, including the day 70 consensus, unlike the section
plans' older absence notes. Use actual retained work if available; otherwise establish a fresh display.
Day 78 teaches any missing readiness water-state/location ideas as new, then retrieves land-water modeling.
Review question board: notices become questions; review keeping originals when revising in a second color.

Default Map fallback U is invented for practice, not a real world catalog. Separate earthquake and
volcano sheets have aligned coordinates and keys. Real-layer option, if supplied later: 2025 earthquakes
magnitude five or greater; 2006 atlas volcano key covers eruptions or possible activity within its past
10,000 years, including uncertain activity. Never call all dots 2025 eruptions. The atlas legend's four
categories are retained in sources.csv. No classroom downloads. Day 82 uses printed official location
and older regional catalog plots; unavailable copies use invented V. No damage accounts.

Before class: print day 77 family note, teaching maps, strips, water cards, slice sequence, audits and
only today's reserved half slip. Hide later exits and K/L/M. Add tracing paper, ruler, clay, blunt slicer,
tray, dry paper, splash goggles, individual approved wipes/bags and adult handwashing supplies day 78.
Ask clay allergies beforehand. Adult alone slices away from hands; children fold paper and trace dry
prepared outlines. Ruler step is one centimeter; record whole-centimeter heights only. Smearing gets
invented outline sequence, never fabricated actual measurements. Cleanup five minutes: parallel wiping
and dry filing, adult containment/handwashing. No child sink queue.

G/H repair traces a cluster or applies each map key. A needs seafloor relief; B a noncoastal example;
C the record span/selection limits. At most two five-minute moves each, preserving day 78 response.
Practice is single-row audits, separate tallies/strips and evidence tags, never a complete held response.
Every child returns paper. Hear rotating six, 45 seconds each, all may pass. Use record.md for descriptive
feedback, no initial-model score. End evidence is day 92. Before class name teacher-only-exit-key.md;
keep answers outside student print. See U5-map, U5-relief and U5-safety in sources.csv for background.
