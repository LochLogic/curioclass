# Day 79: Observe first, then make a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Set Map fallback U overlays, map row audit and tally sheets, movable feature and question strips and parallel repair packets. Return day 78 traces and land-water model with notes. Keep exit-79.md concealed. Prepare unit 5 record sheet. No clay apparatus today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Public talk may be passed; no personal disaster stories.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 78 exits. If many show error H, first five minutes use [[s05-P-keys-79]], reading each key separately. For persistent error G, next five use [[s05-P-band-79]], contrasting crowded and empty areas. Review map key from grade 2 and day 78 shading. Ready children locate the matching key before labeling. Missing evidence receives support, not an error letter by default.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Teach continental edge versus coastline: the coastline is the land-water boundary; a continental edge may lie offshore. On U, point to the dashed edge and the distinct coast. Review day 78 ridge, trench, elevation and depth. Review observation and inference from grade 3 day 94: what we see; what we work out from it.

**Ask.** [[s05-K-row-79]]. Wait before showing the location correction.

[[steps:s05-K-row-79]]

Error A: point to two distinct seafloor relief marks. A flat sea surface does not mean flat underwater ground.

#### 17 min. Row audit

Alone, [[s05-P-audit-79]] using the displayed map row audit and tally sheets. Annotate Side 2. Audit one supplied row at a time, underlining the map evidence. Then pairs arrange separate feature strips under near edge, interior or underwater. U supplies no interior mountain evidence; leave that category empty rather than inventing it. Review day 77's located strips. Children do not build a new full table and derive its pattern before exit. Collect audits for day 80 notes.

#### 5 min. Evidence talk

**Ask.** [[s05-P-talk-79]]. Expect the ridge/trench contrast, not a cause. Hear a different rotating six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; all return their page and annotated rows. A pass leaves the written evidence available.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-79.md now: [[s05-X79-table]], [[s05-X79-pattern]]. Four minutes alone, one collection. Accept location wording that faithfully distinguishes the three records. Do not require a cause.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the supplied rows and U's keys on the board. Draw reserved cards separately, covered until exit. No projector: use pair copies.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy U and supplied rows without a printer. Desk copies replace a projector; parent takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Public turns and a second repair route.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P-keys-79]] if needed.
- **8 min. One row.** Ask [[s05-K-row-79]] before reading its steps.
  **Say.** "Where is your evidence?" Expect the paired lines left of land.
- **12 min. Audit.** Do [[s05-P-audit-79]], one row at a time.
- **5 min. Talk.** Do [[s05-P-talk-79]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the cards; child answers [[s05-X79-table]] and [[s05-X79-pattern]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten strip discussion, preserving the single-row model and exit. Extend by identifying a location U cannot establish.

### Words and supports

Continental edge, coastline. Review observation, inference, ridge and trench. Pointing, neutral reading and child-directed scribing preserve the evidence task.

### Tomorrow

After class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes, to mark both exit parts. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Return all day 80 with row audits. Error A gets a depth-change trace; unresolved error H gets its own-key match, five minutes each. Then add event evidence.
