# Day 80: Patterns keep their exceptions

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md; X80-exception needs the chain and off-chain volcano, not an extra safety statement. Set unit 5 record sheet, Map fallback U overlays, map row audit and tally sheets, movable feature and question strips and parallel repair packets. Use only U's earthquake/relief layers today; T supplies an unambiguous volcano key. Cover exit-80.md. Prepare returned day 79 row audits.

### Safety and set-up

Paper/counters only. Keep counters on desks; no personal disaster disclosure. Public talk may be passed.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 79's written task with notes, including its student page and retained attachments.

Return day 79's exit tickets and row audits. If many show error A, first five minutes use [[s05-P80-1]]. Unresolved error H uses [[s05-P80-2]] in the next five. Review ridge/trench and separate keys from days 78 and 79. Ready children locate the evidence without a hint.

#### 10 min. Teaching first

Review observation and inference from grade 3 day 94. **Ask.** [[s05-K80-1]] before revealing counts.

[[steps:s05-K80-1]]

Review odd day, exception from grade 3 day 102: evidence outside a usual pattern stays in the record. Read T's dates and larger-event selection aloud. These are invented labels, not actual measured sizes. Different selections can leave different gaps. A blank cannot prove safety. Association does not establish a cause.

#### 15 min. Evidence challenges

Pairs place counters on U earthquake dots, then tally T independently: [[s05-P80-3]]. Display map row audit and tally sheets; children tally on their student pages only. **Ask.** [[s05-P80-4]]. Expect an ocean-dot locator, error B's counterevidence. In a separate spoken turn, [[s05-P80-5]]; expect a smaller or out-of-span event, not safety elsewhere, error C. Partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously. Teacher keeps the master feature/location table; children audit counts without extending that table and composing an exception account together.

#### 5 min. Model board

Hear rotating six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, explaining a retained or moved observation strip using the claim question. Others may pass. All submit student pages with tallies and one located evidence mark for feedback; return day 81. Keep unknowns beside the display, not erased.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal exit-80.md: [[s05-X80-table]], [[s05-X80-exception]]. Four minutes alone; one collection. Keep the isolated volcano in the account. Do not award a safety assertion in place of the requested pattern.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy T and U dots/keys before class. Keep J covered on a separate board panel until exit. Pencil ticks replace counters; desk copies replace a projector.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy maps without printer/projector. Use pencil ticks without counters. Parent takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Public turns and second repair route.

- **5 min. Repair.** Do [[s05-P80-1]] if needed.
- **8 min. Dots.** Ask [[s05-K80-1]], then read its steps.
- **12 min. Tallies.** Do [[s05-P80-3]] and [[s05-P80-4]].
  **Say.** "Which mark challenges all-on-land?" Expect an ocean earthquake dot.
- **5 min. Labels.** Do [[s05-P80-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal J; child answers [[s05-X80-table]] and [[s05-X80-exception]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten board sharing, retaining tallies, limits and exit. Extend by naming evidence needed before a cause claim.

### Words and supports

Review belt, exception, observation and inference. Read record labels neutrally. Large symbols and a scribe preserve the child's evidence choices.
