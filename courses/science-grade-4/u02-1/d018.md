# Day 18: Light and warming

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest Light and warming kit contact readings and visible paths. Check cloth allergies beforehand. Record whole-degree starts; pre-expose identical sun/shade patches and dark/pale patches under matching exposure for equal intervals. Prepare Light-material sort, Surface temperature record, concealed Day 18 exit ticket and Teacher-only key. Retrieve day 17 papers, day 15 readiness tickets and Teaching fallback records S. Keep actual and invented records separate.

### Safety and set-up

Never view the sun, including in mirrors. No lasers, lenses or concentrating sunlight. Use acrylic mirror. Aim flashlight at target, away from eyes. Adult handles surfaces and reads warmth by thermometer, never touch-testing.

### The day

#### 10 min. Response and repair

First five: return day 17's ticket, log, s02-P17-1, s02-P17-repair and s02-P17-path with feedback. Review [[s02-P18-repair]]; if many show error G, distinguish a solid support route from air. Next five: return day 15 readiness. Review kindergarten's sunlight-to-surface model for missing sunlight cause. Begin [[s02-P18-sun]] with those children; draw a sunlight-to-surface arrow. Others start [[s02-P18-2]] from shared starting records.

#### 20 min. Light and warming comparisons

First five complete the readiness group's ten-minute response using pre-exposed matched sun/shade patches; children explain the record. Remaining children record independently. Adult reads stable nearest whole-degree marks at matching contact positions, confirming six rotating observers' 30-second looks, three minutes. Everyone records [[s02-P18-2]] on side 1, Surface temperature record. Compare identical sun/shade patches, then dark/pale at matching exposure. Review grade 3 thermometer-to-nearest-mark reading and fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Temperature is not measured energy.

Turn to side 2. Reserve five minutes here for [[s02-P18-1]] on Light-material sort. Review grade 1: lets light through, lets some through, blocks light; turns the light. Test each material separately with flashlight and target. Introduce opaque, blocks light, and translucent, lets some through. Keep mirror turning distinct from transmission. Record each beam effect in the table.

If cloudy or unresolved, keep actual readings unclear. Print/say invented for practice: S, shade 22 to 23, sunlight 22 to 27 degrees Celsius. Supplied reasoning leaves live warming missing; reserve day 19's retry. A flashlight shows paths, never substitutes for sunlight warming.

#### 10 min. Evidence explanation and exit

First five: turn back to side 1. Pairs each take one 45-second turn on [[s02-P18-sun]], citing a reading. If many use appearance instead, point to recorded changes. Last five: reveal [[s02-X18]]; four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 40 seconds, 20 minutes; thirty practice pages times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Review the grade 1 record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Return all papers day 19.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult shades/stores cloth and mirror; wipe thermometer and target area. Children return papers and wipe hands. Record missing live warming for tomorrow; retain original records.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank frames, display only needed S if required, and reveal the exact exit later. No safe sunlight access: record missing observation; use S. A projector enlarges adult readings.

### At home

**Swap.** No sunlight, suitable thermometer or safe cloth comparison: use invented S; mark live evidence missing. No mirror or flashlight: retain missing path observations and discuss supplied material descriptions. No printer: copy blank tables and ticket.

**Skip.** Public turns and group repair.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 17 and day 15 papers; do [[s02-P18-repair]].
- **15 min. Compare.** Adult reads safe pre-exposed cloth or S; child does [[s02-P18-2]] and [[s02-P18-sun]].
  **Say.** "What did sunlight do?" Expect warming supported by the identified record.
- **10 min. Materials.** Test each material separately for [[s02-P18-1]]; avoid eyes and sun viewing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X18]]; child answers alone. Save papers for day 19 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce discussion repetitions, keeping readiness response, comparisons and exit. Longer: revisit [[s02-P18-2]]; annotate the equal-exposure pair.

### Words and supports

Review nearest mark, sunlight warms surfaces, fair test and grade 1 light labels. Opaque blocks light; translucent lets some through. Read stems aloud; adult supplies readings. Accept labeled paths with a dictated caption.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 returns today's papers and repairs error H, appearance replacing warming evidence. Investigate hand-to-spoon transfer; use Compare evidence for a supervised sunlight retry if needed. Preserve missing evidence if weather still prevents it.
