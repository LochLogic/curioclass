# Section 2.1: Following energy

## Before class

Observe the sealed crank light before explaining its inside. Keep initial ideas and question board: notices become questions. Explicitly teach source, recipient, transfer and heat. Source supplies energy; recipient receives it. Transfer means energy passes from one object to another. Keep force arrows distinct. Sound crosses a gap; sunlight warms surfaces; heat transfers warmer to cooler. Compare actual records, never temperature as measured energy. Use observation-log.md and the separate light sheets.

Read this section's teacher-key.md privately. Print each held exit only on its own day. The combined daily student print, including sources and exits, stays within 350 words. Every student sentence has at most 15 words. All teaching days take 45 minutes.
