# Day 17: Sound across a gap

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return day 16 tickets and practice, including s02-P16-1. Pretest Covered drums and contained receiver on separate supports. Check rice allergies beforehand. Verify Goggles and cleaning supplies for everyone through setup, use and cleanup. Without these, use Teaching fallback records D only; live evidence remains missing. Prepare Sound observation log, Energy-path labels and concealed Day 17 exit tickets from data. Print Teacher-only key below separately.

### Safety and set-up

No tasting. Gentle taps, ears back, enclosed grains over catch tray. Separate source and receiver supports. Eye protection throughout live setup, use and cleanup; an enclosure alone is insufficient.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review grade 3 return-and-repair routine. Return day 16 papers with feedback: s02-P16-connect, s02-P16-1, s02-P16-model and s02-P16-question. If many, more than ten, show error A, do [[s02-P17-repair]]; locate input without explaining inside machinery. Others retrieve the same input from their records. Blank work gets a private conference. Keep initial model questions open.

#### 5 min. Setup and safety

Review grade 1 sound off, sound on, and touching as another cause. Show separate supports, clear enclosure and receiver. Reuse grade 1's sound-causes-motion investigation as a reminder; today's focus is transferred energy. Review source, recipient and transfer from day 16. Retain unobstructed view; do not introduce a visual barrier. If protection/allergy checks fail, leave rice stored and use D.

#### 15 min. Sound investigation

Six groups of five observe for two minutes each; three minutes cover resets/movement. Adult taps one tub gently; the receiving cover carries contained grains. Keep positions fixed; compare silence, taps, silence. Everyone completes [[s02-P17-1]] on Sound observation log. Waiting groups predict privately. Retry unclear movement once within the group's two minutes. If still unclear, keep it unclear. Show D separately, printed and said: invented for practice; silence/still, taps/moving, silence/still. Record missing live evidence.

#### 10 min. Evidence discussion

Pairs use Energy-path labels for [[s02-P17-path]]. Each takes one 45-second turn. Hear a new rotating six for 45 seconds each, within five minutes; public talk is unjudged and passing allowed. Everyone writes labels and a support-path note. Review grade 3 evidence reasoning: connect the change to the recipient. If many attribute an air path to touching supports, point out the competing solid path. No rival-record ranking in practice.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X17]] only now; four minutes independent paper, one collect. After class allow thirty times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for tickets; thirty times 20 seconds, ten minutes, for practice. Review grade 1 record categories: shown, shown with help, not yet seen, per look-for. Return all papers day 18; preserve measured and supplied labels.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Keep protection on while adult brushes contained grains into tray, wipes tubs and stores equipment. Wipe goggles and hands; sweep spills. Paper-only route stacks records. Collect everyone's log and student page.

### Other rooms

No kit or full eye protection: display only invented D, with provenance copied on logs. No printer: copy blank log and exact exit at its turn. A projector enlarges observations, never replaces them.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe drum setup, protection or completed allergy check: use invented D; live evidence stays missing. No printer: copy log and ticket. No labels: write source and recipient on scraps.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 16 work; child does [[s02-P17-repair]].
- **15 min. Investigate.** Adult handles safe apparatus or reads D; child does [[s02-P17-1]]. Keep supplied rows separate.
- **10 min. Explain.** Do [[s02-P17-path]].
  **Say.** "Why keep supports separate?" Expect fewer ways for contact through a shared support to carry energy.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X17]]; child answers alone. Save papers for day 18 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: shorten public discussion, retaining each group's view and individual paper. Longer: revisit [[s02-P17-path]] using the last silent interval.

### Words and supports

Review sound off/on, source, recipient, transfer and evidence. Read directions aloud; use moving, still or unclear. Dictation preserves the child's ideas. Do not teach vibration's cause here.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 returns today's log, practice and ticket. If many show error G, claiming shared supports prove an air path, contrast possible solid and air routes. Then complete day 15 sunlight response and investigate warming.
