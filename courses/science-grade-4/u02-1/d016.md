# Day 16: Follow the first clues

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest the Sealed hand-crank light, including stopping behavior. Lay out First crank ideas, Energy-path labels and Question board strip. Cover Day 16 exit tickets. Prepare the original anchor drawing, class question board, record sheet and day 15 revised packets. Print Teacher-only key separately. Send family.md, the bilingual unit 2 family note, home today; students 18 or older receive it and choose for themselves. School rules apply.

### Safety and set-up

Adult operates the sealed low-voltage light; keep fingers clear, aim away from eyes. Never open it or use outlets.

### The day

#### 5 min. Connect

Honor day 15's Tomorrow: start the flashlight anchor; sunlight feedback waits until day 18. Review the original anchor drawing with [[s02-P16-connect]]. Review unit 1 motion energy, transfer, destination and energy arrows: energy passes between objects. Introduce source, where transferred energy comes from, and recipient, what receives it. Destination names where it goes. Review force arrows: they start on the object pushed; they mean something different.

#### 8 min. Hook

Adult turns, stops, waits, then repeats. Everyone watches; record actual light behavior, including persistence. [[s02-P16-1]]. Review grade 3 observation, what we notice, versus inference, what we work out. Hear a rotating six for 45 seconds each, four-and-a-half minutes; children may pass. Review this grade 3 talk routine. If light fails, record failure; do not invent observations.

#### 10 min. Initial model

Individually complete [[s02-P16-model]] on First crank ideas. Partners arrange Energy-path labels; each takes one 45-second turn simultaneously across pairs. Draw the tentative class model; distinguish observed handle/light from uncertain inside parts. Teach no inside mechanism. If many invent unseen parts as observations, surround those parts with question marks.

#### 12 min. Question board

Review grade 3's question board: notices become questions. [[s02-P16-question]] on Question board strip. Partners each speak once for 45 seconds; everyone writes. Teacher groups strips under paths, control and origins. Keep unanswered questions visible, especially how energy reaches another place. Do not rehearse an unsupported report or its test question.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X16]] only now; four minutes alone, one to collect. After class: thirty papers times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, plus thirty practice sheets times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Review the grade 1 categorical record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return tickets and practice with feedback on day 17.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult stores light. Collect strips and sketches; retain originals for day 19. Return day 15's revised packets, s01-P15-revise, s01-A3 and s01-A4, with final component feedback, without repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy practice frames; reveal the exact exit on fresh paper only at its turn. No light: draw its outline as a proposed model, mark live evidence missing and retain questions.

### At home

**Swap.** No crank light: use a proposed outline; mark live evidence missing. No printer: copy frames and a separate ticket. No class drawing: sketch a marble approaching a cup as a reminder.

**Skip.** Public turns and board sorting.

- **5 min. Connect.** Point to the drawing; child does [[s02-P16-connect]].
- **8 min. Observe.** Adult operates the light safely; child does [[s02-P16-1]].
  **Say.** "Which change did you actually notice?" Expect handle movement and observed light behavior.
- **10 min. Draw.** Child does [[s02-P16-model]]; keep uncertain parts as questions.
- **7 min. Wonder.** Child does [[s02-P16-question]]. Save the original.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X16]]; child answers alone. Keep for tomorrow's feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce strip sorting, keeping every child's question and initial model. Longer: refine [[s02-P16-question]] to name an observable outcome.

### Words and supports

Review observation, inference, motion energy, transfer, destination and energy arrows. Source supplies energy; recipient receives it. Read aloud; accept a dictated question. All public talk permits passing.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 returns today's ticket and practice. If many show error A, energy made without input, use the handle observation to locate input. Then investigate sound across a gap. Day 18 receives sunlight-readiness feedback.
