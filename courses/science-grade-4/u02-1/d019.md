# Day 19: Where warmth travels

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest Clean metal spoons and cloth with the thermometer's stable whole-degree contact reading. If unsuitable, use Teaching fallback records H. Prepare Hand-to-spoon observation, retained First crank ideas, Energy-path labels and Surface temperature record. Conceal Day 19 exit tickets; print Teacher-only key separately. Retrieve day 18 papers and class model. If sunlight retry is needed, prepare Light and warming kit patches using day 18's equal-time procedure and allergy checks.

### Safety and set-up

Adult uses warm palms only, never hot water or heaters. Clean spoons; nothing eaten. For sunlight retry: adult handles surfaces; no touch-testing, sun viewing, mirrors, lenses or lasers. Clean-up takes five minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 18 tickets, light sort and temperature records with feedback, including s02-P18-1 and s02-P18-2. Review [[s02-P19-repair]]. If many show error H, appearance replacing evidence, locate a recorded change instead. Others underline their own evidence. Review return-and-repair routine; preserve original observations.

#### 5 min. Prepare

Review grade 3 thermometer reading to the nearest mark; our marks are one degree Celsius apart. Adult checks two matching room-temperature spoons. Introduce heat: energy transfer from a warmer object to a cooler object. Review transfer, source and recipient from day 16. Temperature describes warming, not an amount of energy. Explain the held/untouched comparison before measuring.

#### 15 min. Contact investigation

Adult reads stable starts at matching positions. Hold one spoon in a warm palm; leave the matching spoon untouched on cloth for the same interval. Adult reads finishes; six rotating observers inspect one mark for 30 seconds each, three minutes. Everyone does [[s02-P19-1]] on Hand-to-spoon observation. Keep hands clear of the measuring contact. If unresolved, retry once within this block; log unclear, then separately print/say invented H: held 20 to 23, untouched 20 to 20 degrees Celsius. Do not substitute it for observation.

Reserve final four minutes for [[s02-P19-model]] on First crank ideas. Review grade 3 revision: second color or added symbol, originals and initials retained. Add a supported branch, keeping the crank's inside uncertain. Do not practice reversing a supplied model.

#### 10 min. Compare evidence

Pairs each take one 45-second turn with Energy-path labels; explain the source/recipient in [[s02-P19-1]]. If many omit input, point to the hand's contact. No spoon-to-spoon transfer.

If sunlight was missing on day 18, this block becomes the supervised retry. Adult reads prepared sun/shade patches; update Surface temperature record and [[s02-P18-2]]. Everyone records whole degrees or unclear; retain earlier provenance. Keep the spoon model revision already completed. Still cloudy: preserve missing evidence for day 20's named retry slot.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X19]]; four minutes alone, one collect. After class allow thirty times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for tickets; thirty times 20 seconds, ten minutes, for practice. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Return all papers day 20; blank/unrelated work receives a conference.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult wipes/stores thermometer and spoons, shades retry cloth if used. Children wipe hands and collect labels. Collect everyone's work; retain originals.

### Other rooms

No suitable instrument or spoon comparison: use invented H, marking live evidence missing. No printer: copy blank frames and reveal exact exit at its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No suitable thermometer or spoons: use invented H; live evidence stays missing. No printer: copy frames and ticket. No second color: add symbols. No sunlight for retry: retain missing evidence.

**Skip.** Group viewing and public turns.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 18 papers; child does [[s02-P19-repair]].
- **15 min. Compare.** Adult palm-warms one spoon and reads both safely, or supplies H. Child does [[s02-P19-1]].
  **Say.** "What changed at the recipient?" Expect a cited increase or unclear reading, never an invented measurement.
- **10 min. Revise.** Child does [[s02-P19-model]] on the retained drawing. Keep original marks. If needed, retry sunlight instead; preserve a brief spoon evidence label.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X19]]; child answers alone. Save papers for day 20 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: shorten pair discussion; retain revision, comparison readings and exit. Longer: name a record needed before claiming heat transfer occurred.

### Words and supports

Review source, recipient, transfer, evidence, nearest mark and revision. Heat transfers energy warmer to cooler. Read aloud; accept labelled arrows and dictated evidence. Passing in public talk remains available.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 returns today's ticket and practice. Error A, warmth created without input, needs locating the warmer source. Direction-only mistakes receive a conference. Then build a battery/bulb closed path; carry unresolved sunlight evidence to its comparison retry.
