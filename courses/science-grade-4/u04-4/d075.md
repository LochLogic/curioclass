# Day 75: Audit a basin record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the course check answer key, key-checks-74-76.md, and key-checks-75.md. Locate day 71's unchanged individual practical record, including unavailable observations. Hold day 74 papers for day 76. Prepare the integrated measurement table, time-sequence records and diagram strip on today's student page. Cover teaching displays and keep answers private.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only; no soil, plants, ice, water, food or stones. No child recounts a personal hazard. The final three minutes include collection and filing.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Day 74's assessment stays private until day 76. Explain today's independent audit. Say every basin, organism, layer and measurement record is invented for practice. Identify the before/after columns without interpreting them. Day 71's individual practical record transfers unchanged, with no second observed turn or score.

#### 20 min. Investigation audit

All work alone. Allow four minutes each for [[C-E75-plan]] and [[C-E75-measure]], then four each for [[C-E75-ice]], [[C-E75-move]] and [[C-E75-life]]. Children gather observations from the printed time sequences, complete the distance record and correct claims. Read each record neutrally when requested. Ruler marks are whole centimeters; do not treat end marks as distances. Do not supply this correction during assessment.

Keep ice, wind, gravity, roots, rain channels and plant-water comparisons separate. The record is supplied evidence, not a new firsthand investigation. Actual observation status from day 71 remains unchanged. No student apparatus, demonstration queue or serial interview round.

#### 19 min. History corrections

Alone: twelve minutes [[C-E75-history]], seven [[C-E75-match]]. Children correct the printed history strip and add brief evidence labels. Accept arrows or labeled drawings instead of an essay. The Cap environment remains unresolved. Correcting an order does not establish a numerical age. Provide access support without confirming conclusions.

#### 3 min. Collect

Collect and file all papers. After class allow thirty minutes for written component feedback using the private answer key. Use shown, shown with help or not yet seen, no total. More than ten of thirty sharing V/W/X prioritizes land on day 76; Y/Z prioritizes history. Keep missing opportunity in a separate note. Return both checks together on day 76.

### Other rooms

No printer: reproduce the records and strip on the board in two panels. Children copy only response fields. No apparatus or display is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the records and diagram strip without a printer. Use paper instead of a display or apparatus.

**Skip.** The class handoff of day 71 records and collection.

- **20 min. Assessment audit.** Child answers [[C-E75-plan]], [[C-E75-measure]], [[C-E75-ice]], [[C-E75-move]] and [[C-E75-life]] alone.
- **19 min. History.** Use [[C-E75-history]] and [[C-E75-match]] independently. Read words without explaining the science.
- **6 min. Look together.** Preserve originals for day 76.
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your correction?" Expect a specific before/after change or ordered-layer feature.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both audit and history. Early finishers check that each corrected arrow has evidence.

### Words and supports

Accept dictation and labeled drawings. Written earlier terms: weathering and erosion, day 58; fossil, marine and undisturbed order, day 67. Teach no answer-bearing definition before collection.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 returns day 74's assessment and today's assessment with written component notes, then repairs and revises both once. Its last ten minutes diagnose water locations.

### Credit

Ours. Source C74-76-original holds invented records. Background: Sources C75-process, C75-life, C75-order, C75-fossils and C75-cut.
