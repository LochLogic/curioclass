# Teacher answer key, day 75

TEACHER ONLY. Use the category and routing directions in key-checks-74-76.md. No student display.

## C-E75-plan

Answer: Keep slope, starting sediment, moisture, pouring position and trial interval the same; change only water amount. Repeat both conditions with reset matched beds. Alternatively hold water fixed and vary only slope. Observe marked grains before/after to distinguish movement from breakage.

Look for: Look for exactly one changed condition, explicit controls and repeated before/after observations.

Wrong: Changing slope and water together proves water caused the difference. Error V.

## C-E75-measure

Answer: Small-water front moved 8 centimeters, from 4 to 12. Large-water front moved 5 centimeters, from 2 to 7. Marked grains moved without visible breakage: carrying, or erosion. Unequal slopes prevent isolating water amount. Record both results, even when unexpected.

Look for: Look for end minus start, whole centimeters, both observations, carrying evidence and causal limit. Supplied sequence is not a new practical measurement. Day 71 original practical status stays unchanged.

Wrong: The distances are 12 and 7 centimeters because end marks are distances. Error x.

## C-E75-ice

Answer: The new split after freezing supports weathering even with pieces beside the rock. The matched unfrozen wet rock stayed unchanged, supporting a freezing effect here. This does not show carrying.

Look for: Look for new breakage in place and the unfrozen comparison. Supplied time sequence supports reasoning, not firsthand freezing performance.

Wrong: Weathering requires pieces to travel away. Error W.

## C-E75-move

Answer: Wind: marked grains changed position without visible breakage, evidence of erosion. Gravity: loose pieces slid downhill with still air and no water, also carrying. Neither alone shows new smaller pieces.

Look for: Look for each agent connected to recorded movement, distinguished from breaking. No rate calculation.

Wrong: Both records prove rock broke into smaller pieces. Error W.

## C-E75-life

Answer: The growing root widens a crack, changing land material. Rain cuts channels while sheltered soil stays unchanged. Watered plants stay upright; unwatered matches wilt and recover after watering. This links water availability to plant condition. Each record tests its own process.

Look for: Look for root/crack, rain/channel and water/plant-condition evidence links separately.

Wrong: Root growth and rain have no effects. Error X.

## C-E75-history

Answer: Root came first with land conditions, then Shell with sea conditions, then Cap with uncertain environment. Layers existed before bending and breaking. Basin cutting followed deformation because walls cut already shifted layers. Fossil positions and cross-cutting support relative order, not exact duration.

Look for: Look for layer order, marine-over-plant change, deformation after host layers, later basin cutting and one timing limit. Corrected arrows with labels count; no essay needed.

Wrong: The basin existed before its rock layers and never changed. Error Z.

## C-E75-match

Answer: Matching ordered Root-Shell-Cap formations and fossils near and far support correlation and an earlier extensive sequence. Color alone is insufficient. The distant site with different fossil order is not a supported match. Correlation is an inference, not proof of exact age or continuous connection.

Look for: Look for near/far sequence and fossil evidence, rejection of color-only certainty and a limit.

Wrong: Same colors prove a match even when fossil order differs. Error Y.
