# Teacher answer key, day 74

TEACHER ONLY. Use the category and routing directions in key-checks-74-76.md. No student display.

## C-E74-band

Answer: Compared with H, J is taller with unchanged spacing. K is equally tall as J but closer-spaced. Marked points move locally, farther from rest in J and K. Energy travels onward, not the marked points.

Look for: Look for independent rest-to-peak height and peak-spacing comparisons, local motion and separate energy travel. Accept labeled sketches. No numerical wave measures.

Wrong: K must be shorter because its peaks are closer. Error R.

## C-E74-float

Answer: Draw the float rising then lowering beside its marker as peaks pass onward. Label local float motion separately from rightward traveling pattern and energy. The record does not show a ride to the far wall.

Look for: Look for two moments near the marker and separate local-motion and pattern/energy arrows. Accept small local sideways motion; do not require perfectly stationary water.

Wrong: The float rides each peak to the far wall. Error Q.

## C-E74-create

Answer: One possible code: left-arrow card means LEFT; right-arrow card means RIGHT. Lower cards between messages as a boundary. Sender and receiver share the rule. Uses paper, no power or shouting. Accept other distinct feasible signals with both meanings and boundaries.

Look for: Look for an additional system, both meanings, agreed boundaries and compliance with materials and volume limits.

Wrong: Show the same blank card for both meanings. Error U.

## C-E74-compare

Answer: B passes: five correct within 35 seconds in both conditions. A passes quiet but fails conversation accuracy despite being faster. My new code has no results, so it cannot yet outrank B. Check its feasibility against paper/no-power/no-shouting limits.

Look for: Look for all three systems, accuracy and duration, both conditions and limits. Do not invent results for the new code.

Wrong: A wins because it is fastest. Error S.

## C-E74-test

Answer: Test five messages in quiet and five with conversation at identical positions. Keep an agreed message list. Record each decoded meaning and whole-second elapsed duration. Check five correct within 35 seconds in each condition. Retain failed trials.

Look for: Look for both conditions, fixed positions, decoded accuracy, elapsed duration and both success rules. An untested proposal has no claimed performance.

Wrong: One quiet success proves it works in every condition. Error T.

## C-E74-encode

Answer: 1011: black-white above black-black, top row first and left to right.

Look for: Look for four digits in stated order; correct digit-labeled cells count.

Wrong: 0100, using opposite color meanings. Error U.

## C-E74-decode

Answer: 0010 makes white-white above black-white, using the shared order.

Look for: Look for correct cell placement in a two-by-two tile, not only a black-cell count.

Wrong: A black cell can go anywhere because order does not matter. Error U.

## C-E74-voice

Answer: Voice is encoded as ordered digits, transmitted, then decoded with an agreed rule to recover voice. Digital information can travel far with little loss when digits and order are preserved.

Look for: Look for encoding into digits and decoding into sound, with agreed order preserved. No device or voice recording required.

Wrong: Digits become any voice without a decoding rule. Error U.

## C-E74-question

Answer: Does rolling the same cylinder faster make the same swinging panel move farther after contact? Keep cylinder, panel and surface unchanged. Other speed questions tied to observable motion or sound count.

Look for: Look for changed speed and observable collision effect; best or nicest alone is not observable.

Wrong: Which speed is nicest? Error C.

## C-E74-predict

Answer: Before: motion energy in cylinder, panel still. After: less cylinder motion energy as it slows; energy transfers to panel motion, sound and slight warming of objects and air. A slower repeat predicts less panel motion from the repeated record. Continuing cylinder motion retains energy. Warming is inferred, not measured.

Look for: Look for both objects before/after, evidence-linked slower prediction, sound/warming destinations and measured/inferred distinction. No unsupported sound-volume prediction required.

Wrong: Energy disappears when the cylinder slows. Error B.
