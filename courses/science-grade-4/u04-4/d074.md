# Day 74: Check models and messages

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the course check answer key, key-checks-74-76.md, and key-checks-74.md. Copy its private component record onto a class list. Prepare day 73's written feedback. Cover teaching displays. Check the tile's cell order, decoding box and message records. Keep answers and future papers concealed.

### Safety and set-up

Paper work only. No water, stretched materials, moving toys, lights or sound demonstrations. Collect pencils and file pages in the final two minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Return day 73's exit ticket s04-P73-fresh and written tasks with notes; they stay in folders without repair time. Explain independent work and neutral reading support. Say all records are invented for practice. Read records exactly, without explaining answers. This check uses earlier ideas on new cases; no portfolio rescore.

#### 15 min. Wave models

Alone, use seven minutes for [[C-E74-band]] and eight for [[C-E74-float]]. Children draw and label models from the records. Accept dictation within work time; record who was heard. No serial interview round. Observe evidence use without feedback.

#### 20 min. Codes

Alone: two minutes each for [[C-E74-encode]] and [[C-E74-decode]] on Side 1. Then use Side 2: four minutes [[C-E74-create]], five [[C-E74-compare]], three [[C-E74-test]] and four [[C-E74-voice]]. Each child generates one additional system and compares all three. Supplied tests never establish the child's practical performance. Record an unfinished component honestly; do not invent successful trials.

#### 5 min. Collision question and prediction

Alone: two minutes [[C-E74-question]], three [[C-E74-predict]]. The warming account is supplied science, not a temperature reading. Children may draw arrows with short labels. No live collision occurs.

#### 2 min. Collect

Collect every page. After class allow 35 minutes for written component feedback using the private answer key. Record shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with no total. More than ten of thirty sharing an error prioritizes its day 76 visit: Q/R waves, S/T/U codes, B/C collision within waves. Missing work alone gets no error letter. Hold papers for day 76.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy records and prompts onto two board panels, preserving grids and table columns. Children answer on paper. No display is required.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the records and grids without a printer. Point at paper instead of a display.

**Skip.** Class collection and public reporting.

- **15 min. Assessment wave models.** Child answers [[C-E74-band]] and [[C-E74-float]] independently.
- **20 min. Codes.** Use [[C-E74-encode]], [[C-E74-decode]], [[C-E74-create]], [[C-E74-compare]], [[C-E74-test]] and [[C-E74-voice]]. Read neutrally; give no science hints.
- **5 min. Collision.** Use [[C-E74-question]] and [[C-E74-predict]].
- **5 min. Look together.** Keep originals for day 76.
  **Say.** "What supports your model?" Expect a specific record linked to a model label.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve all components. Early finishers check evidence labels without another task.

### Words and supports

Read aloud neutrally; accept labeled drawings and scribing. Earlier written terms include amplitude, wavelength and local motion, day 42; encode and decode, day 48; contact and energy arrows, days 7 to 10. No definitions that answer the check.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 independently audits basin investigations and history. No repair tomorrow; return today's assessment on day 76 with written feedback.
