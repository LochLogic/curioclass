# Day 71: Observe a new erosion comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Reserve new liners, starting patches and slopes before today; pretest apparatus privately. Prepare assessment-71.md stems and practical-sheet.md fields once. Keep assessment-fallback.md sealed unless needed. Prepare a blank six-group results chart. Print assessment-record.md and teacher-key.md for yourself; generated answers hold current items. Hold day 70 feedback until day 73.

### Safety and set-up

Check sand ingredients and allergies. Adult pours and resets; children read rulers at dry edges. All present wear splash goggles through cleanup. Damp sand only; no tasting. Catch runoff and wipe spills. Five Cleanup minutes: adult washes, children clean goggles simultaneously; settle solids for outdoor disposal, never sinks.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

After day 70's consensus, this is independent work, without feedback. Name the single changed condition, slope. Show equal water, sand amount, moisture, patch size and pouring position. Read directions neutrally, without demonstrating answers. Each child records their own readings. Review is assessed, not retaught.

#### 24 min. Observation turns

Six groups of five take four minutes each: 45 seconds adult pours and children observe, five 30-second independent paired ruler readings, 45 seconds reset and record the repeat. Adults handle wet apparatus; children remain dry. Each independently reads both start and end marks in whole centimeters, [[s04-E1-measure]]. Record no detected movement when appropriate. Never substitute expected numbers. Post each completed group's paired distances without causal comments; keep all valid repeats visible.

Waiting children independently annotate the four invented process records printed on Side 2. Say aloud: these are invented for practice, not class observations. Do not discuss answers. Retry a failed reading once within its slot; otherwise label unresolved. Supply assessment-fallback.md separately if needed; never pool supplied and live readings. Supplied measurements never fill live cells or earn firsthand credit.

#### 13 min. Independent explanation

Four minutes: [[s04-E1-cause]], using all resolved repeats, including disagreements. Nine minutes: [[s04-E1-ice]], [[s04-E1-wind]], [[s04-E1-animal]], [[s04-E1-rain]]. No prompts beyond neutral rereading. Record access help separately from content evidence. Collect every paper before any feedback.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult contains solids, wipes apparatus and washes; dry children clean goggles as directed and file papers. After class allow 30 times three minutes, 90 minutes, for component marking and written feedback. Assessment-record.md uses one 0/1/2 scale, sixteen possible points. Blank means absent or not yet observed. Keep formative categories elsewhere. Return feedback day 73, originals in Revision. Retain each child's original practical-sheet.md record for day 75's Investigation audit.

### Other rooms

No apparatus or safe protection: interpret the labeled invented fallback, leaving live observation cells blank. No printer: copy exact stems and blank fields. No display: maintain the same plain paper results chart.

### At home

**Swap.** Adult pours into two safely supported trays; child reads both rulers. Without apparatus or splash protection, use the labeled invented fallback; it gives no firsthand credit. Copy fields without printing.

**Skip.** Group queues and class reports. Give no content help before collecting work.

- **22 min. End-of-unit assessment.** Read directions neutrally. Complete [[s04-E1-measure]] across three safe repeated comparisons. Record actual results, including failures.
- **13 min. Independent explanation.** Complete [[s04-E1-cause]], [[s04-E1-ice]], [[s04-E1-wind]], [[s04-E1-animal]] and [[s04-E1-rain]].
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Collect work before feedback; clean equipment and wash. Discuss feedback on day 73.

### Shorter or longer

Preserve independent readings and both effects in the rain record. Do not add scored questions.

### Words and supports

Read prompts neutrally; enlarge print or scribe the child's own explanation. Do not select measurements or evidence for them.

### Tomorrow

Day 72 independently checks a new landscape history. Hold day 71's assessment feedback for day 73. Route errors A to E by expressed evidence gaps; measurement gaps receive ruler support, not error C automatically. Preserve originals.
