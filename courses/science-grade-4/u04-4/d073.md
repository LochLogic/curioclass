# Day 73: Repair evidence links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare feedback-sheet.md notes from both assessments. Assign one repair per child's recorded gap using repair-cards.md. Gather day 70 papers and earlier practice records. Keep fresh-case.md hidden. Print teacher-key.md and generated answers for yourself, including new repair rows. Keep original scores on assessment-record.md and today's formative observations on record.md.

### Safety and set-up

Paper repair only; no wet apparatus or new practical round. Set out earlier records beforehand. Final collection fits within Fresh case. Keep private feedback private.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Read written component notes from day 71's assessment and day 72's assessment. Review evidence and inferred from day 66: observations support a proposed explanation. Review original-preserving revision from day 70. Children identify their assigned evidence gap without changing scores or measurements.

#### 10 min. Targeted repair

Return day 70's s04-X70, s04-P70-consensus and s04-P70-feedback within this part, including its five-minute exit response allocation. If many show error H, their assigned history repair places cutting after host material. Four groups work simultaneously; circulate two minutes per group, with two minutes for transitions. Children complete only their assigned row, following repair-cards.md.

Breakage/carrying: [[s04-P73-rock]]. Review weathering and erosion from day 66. Error A separates breaking from travel; error B points to the new split as visible change.

Controls and land/life: [[s04-P73-land]]. Review day 64's retained records. Error C compares matched conditions; error D links organism action to material change; error E compares watered and unwatered plant condition. Do only the recorded gap.

Layer order: [[s04-P73-layer]]. Review day 68's model and day 67's undisturbed order. Trace identity; use error F only for an explicit lowest-always-oldest claim.

Historical evidence: [[s04-P73-history]]. Review day 69's two-hill record. Error G uses ordered fossils rather than color; error H puts the groove after its upper host rock.

Measurement gaps replace that child's group task with [[s04-P73-ruler]]. Review ruler zero and end minus start from day 3. Use the teacher-only answer key; do not print its answer on the child's page.

#### 20 min. Revision

Return original day 71 assessment for ten minutes, then day 72 assessment for ten. Complete [[s04-P73-revision]] for one feedback link from each. Keep original readings, claims and scores visible beside additions. Missing firsthand evidence stays blank; no practical retry today. Preserve day 71's original practical record for day 75's Investigation audit.

#### 5 min. Explain change

Complete [[s04-P73-change]]. Review day 61's paired evidence pointing: partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously; public talk may be passed. Use the remaining 3.5 minutes for each child's written evidence note. Do not substitute thirty serial interviews.

#### 5 min. Fresh case

Reveal [[s04-P73-fresh]] only now. Say the footpath record is invented for practice. Children independently explain and state a limit. Collect every page. After class allow 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for written feedback, using record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all today's rows day 74 in Launch, without additional repair minutes.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy assigned stems and revision fields, preserving original papers. No display: use retained practice records. No apparatus is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy assigned stems and feedback fields without a printer. Adult takes the partner role. Use paper models without apparatus.

**Skip.** Group circulation and class reports. Keep original scores and observations.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read day 71 and 72 feedback.
- **7 min. Targeted repair.** Complete the assigned repair row from the student page.
- **15 min. Revision.** Complete [[s04-P73-revision]] once for each assessment, preserving originals.
- **3 min. Explain change.** Complete [[s04-P73-change]].
  **Say.** "Identify evidence that changed your explanation." Expect a specific observation linked to a corrected claim.
- **5 min. Fresh case.** Reveal [[s04-P73-fresh]]. Child answers before discussing the answer.

### Shorter or longer

Cut public reporting, preserving both revision allocations and Fresh case. Extend the same revision with evidence that could change its claim.

### Words and supports

Review fossil and layer from day 67. Accept drawings or dictation. Missing work alone never establishes a misconception.

### Tomorrow

Day 74 independently checks earlier wave, message-code and collision work. Return today's papers in Launch with written feedback. Error x on the fresh case needs cut-before/after evidence and an unknown duration recorded for later support; no extra assessment warm-up is added.
