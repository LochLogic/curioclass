# Day 76: Revise evidence links

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the course check answer key, key-checks-74-76.md, and key-checks-76.md. Write feedback on both checks; assign one priority route per child. Prepare earlier teaching records for code, collision and layer support. Fold the readiness map until its final slot. Keep original categories and evidence alongside revised evidence. Use the private record's separate readiness columns.

### Safety and set-up

Paper work only; apparatus stays stored. No family or disaster disclosure. Collect revised papers in Revision; collect remaining papers during Readiness.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return feedback

Return day 74's assessment and day 75's assessment with written component notes. Children locate their priority gap privately. Review preserving originals from day 73. Missing work prompts an access conversation, not an assumed error. Readiness remains covered.

#### 12 min. Parallel repair

Four routes work simultaneously. Visit each for 2.5 minutes, ten total, leaving two for transitions. Assign one route per child; more than ten with a gap prioritizes that visit, without adding a round. Everyone submits a written repair. No thirty-child oral turn.

Waves Q/R: [[C-P76-wave]]. Review local motion, height and spacing from day 42. Collision B/C uses earlier energy arrows from day 10 during this visit: identify observable speed effects, remaining motion, sound and inferred warming before revising the question or prediction.

Codes S/T/U: [[C-P76-code]]. Review shared rules from day 48 using day 51/52 logs. Point to accuracy and duration in both conditions. A missing test remains missing. Trace encode, transmit and decode from the earlier day 48 account, using its shared rule.

Land V/W/X: [[C-P76-land]]. Review breaking/carrying from day 58. Separate root, rain-channel and water/plant evidence; hold other conditions fixed.

History Y/Z: [[C-P76-history]]. Review day 68 tracing through deformation; compare fossil sequence before color. Explicitly teach any missing idea instead of calling planned-only work review. Ready children find one uncertainty in their own evidence.

#### 13 min. Revision

Alone, [[C-P76-revise]]: six minutes on day 74, seven on day 75, including collection. Add second-color evidence links without erasing originals. Partners may each take 45 seconds simultaneously within these minutes to point to a changed link; public talk may be passed. Assess [[C-E76-record]] after class from submitted work, preserving original practical and portfolio status. Use the same categories, never another total. Record unresolved support needs.

#### 5 min. Fresh transfer

Independently answer [[C-P76-fresh]]. This new cut-and-fill event chain is unscored. Keep concealed during repairs. Collect it for written feedback, returned day 77 without adding repair time to its hook.

#### 10 min. Readiness

Unfold the map. Six minutes answer [[C-R76-water]], four collect and sort states versus location/key gaps. Read neutrally; no definitions or hints first. Diagnostic only. Send findings to day 78's reserved five-minute state matching and five-minute locations/key response. Teach missing water-location ideas as new because lower skill entries remain planned only. Land-water modeling remains separate retrieval. Day 77 keeps its map hook.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy only assigned repairs, then the concealed readiness map. No second color: box changes. Draw grids and symbols large enough to distinguish.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy prompts and map without a printer. Box revisions without a second pencil color. Adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Group visits and a second repair route.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read both checks' notes.
- **8 min. Repair.** Use assigned [[C-P76-wave]], [[C-P76-code]], [[C-P76-land]] or [[C-P76-history]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your correction?" Expect an evidence-linked change.
- **10 min. Revision.** Complete [[C-P76-revise]] on both original papers. [[C-E76-record]] needs no new writing.
- **5 min. Fresh transfer.** Reveal [[C-P76-fresh]] alone.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Reveal [[C-R76-water]] alone, without hints. Keep for day 78.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both revisions and readiness. Reduce group talk first. Extend an existing correction with its uncertainty.

### Words and supports

Use only assigned route terms. Labeled drawings, neutral reading and scribing count. Content coaching is recorded as help. Preserve uncertain observations.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 opens earthquake and volcano map patterns, questions and an initial model. Return today's papers with written notes only. Day 78 receives the readiness findings for its reserved response; no new response block is added tomorrow.
