## s04-E1-measure (day 71)

Shape: Independently measure a new matched slope comparison.

Answer: Record your actual gentle and steep distances from start marks in centimeters. Describe observed grains leaving the patch, or accurately state no detected movement.

Look-fors: M, 2 points: both labeled distances, start marks and units. | T, 2 points: visible carrying evidence or accurate no-detected-movement account. Missing firsthand measurements stay blank, not fabricated.

Meets (illustrative invented readings only): Gentle 1 cm, steep 5 cm from their starts. Grains moved out of the patch. | Does not meet: I wrote expected readings without observing. (error x).

Hand back: day 73, Revision; day 75, assessment.

## s04-E1-cause (day 71)

Shape: Explain cause and limit from firsthand repeated records.

Answer: Use all valid repeats; support a slope effect only if observed. Match sand, moisture, water and pouring. A tray cannot give a canyon-cutting rate.

Look-fors: R, 2 points: repeated comparisons support or limit cause. | C, 2 points: maintained control and justified limitation.

Meets (illustrative invented readings only): Gentle 1, 2, 1 cm; steep 5, 6, 5 cm. All three repeats favor steep with equal water and sand. A tray cannot set a canyon rate. | Does not meet: One trial proves slope caused it even if water changed. (error C).

Hand back: day 73, Revision.
