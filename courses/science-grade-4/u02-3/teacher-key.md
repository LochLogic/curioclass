# Section 2.3 private keys

TEACHER ONLY. Read privately in Before class. Keep these keys off child desks and displays.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record. Blank work gets a conference, not an error letter. Return notes with the next named ticket repair.

## s02-X24

Test every opening at the doorway to check detection there before choosing. A costs five tokens and B eight, so both meet the budget. Neither beside-box test establishes the doorway criterion.

Wrong C: Any attractive alarm succeeds without a success rule.

## s02-X25

Show buildable contacts joining when opened and separating when shut, such as a released spring. Both terminals connect through the buzzer when joined. Current carries stored energy to sound.

Wrong B: One lead completes the circuit.

## s02-X26

B records all openings at the required point. Two of five fail when contacts miss. A hides failures and cannot establish success.

Wrong C: We need no rule for success.

## s02-X27

Disconnect the cell before any adjustment. Reshape and tape the attached guard clear of the blades, or give another feasible safe clearance repair using the listed parts. The repeated blade jams justify this change. The complete circuit transfers stored cell energy through current to fan motion. Keep the guard; do not guarantee success.

Paper case only. No fan activity or added equipment. A retest prediction is optional.

Wrong D: Painting the guard my favorite color fixes the jam without failure evidence.

## s02-X28

The trials do not isolate pad improvement because both pad and cell changed. Compare original and wider pads using the same working cell, seven matching presses, and the same setup and observation point. Record all outcomes before judging improvement.

Wrong D: More lights prove pad improvement even though the cell changed.

## s02-X29

Add six retest rows at the original desk point using the same cell, lamp and tilt method. Include light/dark/unclear and switch-state columns. Leave outcomes blank, labeled not yet collected. Moving the switch does not itself prove improvement.

Wrong D: The improvement claim needs no test evidence.

## s02-X25-build

The child makes a buildable contact decision on a fresh safe placement and connects both terminals through the buzzer. Contacts join on opening. Record actual implementation, adult assistance and missing hardware.

Wrong B: One terminal is enough.

## s02-X28-retest

The child implements a failure-based change on an unfamiliar safe loose-contact placement and operates a matched trial. Keep all outcomes, including failure; copying a team record is not performance.

Wrong D: A preferred change needs no retest.
