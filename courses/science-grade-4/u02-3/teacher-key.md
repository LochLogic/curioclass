# Section 2.3 private keys

TEACHER ONLY. Keep this piece separate from all child pages. Read privately in Before class. All cases/counts described as invented are supplied practice records, never promised measurements. These keys were solved cold for data entry; the independent answer-key read follows.

## s02-X24

Shape: evaluate two proposals on energy and budget with missing criterion evidence.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A flap signal must sound at the doorway on every opening. The limit is eight tokens. Both plans have complete battery-buzzer loops. A costs three plus two tokens. B adds a three-token decoration. Both were tested only beside the box. Compare their energy paths and budgets, identifying missing evidence before choosing.

Key and look-fors: Both use stored cell energy carried by current to sound. A costs five, leaves three; B costs eight, leaves none. Both fit, but neither establishes doorway detection every opening. Test every opening there before choosing.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X25

Shape: correct two mechanical/circuit states with transfer caption.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A door chime must sound when its door opens. Its contacts join only when shut. Available: cell, holder, buzzer, leads and card. Draw a corrected open-and-shut plan with an energy-path caption.

Key and look-fors: Show buildable contacts joining when opened and separating when shut, such as a released spring. Both terminals connect through the buzzer when joined. Current carries stored energy to sound.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X26

Shape: judge competing logs and diagnose correlated contact failure.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A flap signal must sound at the doorway on every opening. Log A leaves out silent trials. Log B records sound, silence, sound, silence, sound there. Contacts miss during both silent trials. Explain which log can judge success and what failure it identifies.

Key and look-fors: B records all openings at the required point. Two of five fail when contacts miss. A hides failures and cannot establish success.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X27

Shape: recommend a refinement justified by a failure mechanism.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A flap signal fails when its contacts miss. Partners suggest wider contacts or brighter paint. Recommend a refinement using failure evidence and the energy path.

Key and look-fors: Wider contacts may maintain the closed current path so stored energy reaches sound. Cite missed contacts, predict a retest, never guarantee success. Paint does not address this failure.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X28

Shape: critique mismatched conditions and specify corrective retest.

Prompt: Invented for practice. Wider contact: five signals in five openings beside the box. Original contact: two in five at the doorway. Explain whether this establishes improvement against the doorway rule, including a needed retest.

Key and look-fors: No: listening point changed. Retest refined device five openings at the original doorway with matching parts/opening method. Record all outcomes and contact conditions, then compare doorway results.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.

## s02-X29

Shape: diagnose missing energy reasoning and refinement evidence in a peer record.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A case-opening signal record has a drawing and a before-test table. It says, we improved it. It has no explanation or retest. List additions needed to communicate its energy path and support its improvement claim.

Key and look-fors: Add stored-energy to current to sound arrows; a failure-linked reason for the change; and a comparable retest including failures. A claim alone does not establish improvement.

Use the formative categories on record.md, one cell per component; never point scores.
