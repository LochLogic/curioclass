# Two buildable lid mechanisms

## Released spring, open lid

```text
cell (+) -- released foil touching pad -- buzzer -- cell (-)
```

The opening lid releases foil toward its pad.

## Slider, open lid

```text
lid -- card link -- slider touching pad
cell (+) -- slider and pad -- buzzer -- cell (-)
```

The opening lid pulls a guided slider onto its pad.

Choose one for your own trigger-state plan.
