# Day 25: Building the signal

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Return day 24 papers. Prepare six compatible kits: cell/holder, insulated leads, buzzer, adult-cut box/card, smooth foil contacts and tape. Prepare Two buildable lid mechanisms: a folded-card spring carrying foil or a guided card slider. Retrieve each Personal signal plan and Parts and token ledger. Copy plan fields into today's packet; no printed Name line. Conceal Day 25 exit ticket. Lay out storage trays and wiping supplies.

### Safety and set-up

Only low-voltage cells, never outlets. Adult checks smooth contacts and insulation. Disconnect before changes; buzzer always in the loop. Adult disconnects immediately if wires warm. Keep sound away from ears. No tasting. Two active handlers per kit; others observe beyond the work boundary.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 24's ticket, s02-P24-1, s02-P24-repair, s02-P24-budget, s02-K24-1 and s02-K24-2. Review return and repair. Do [[s02-P25-repair]] independently. If many tickets show error C, locate the measurable result before naming appearance. Many means more than ten of thirty.

#### 5 min. Plan and protect

Review day 24 criterion, limits and mechanisms using Two buildable lid mechanisms. Children begin [[s02-P25-1]] with one correct trigger-state drawing. Trace the loop through both terminals and buzzer. Review stored-energy to current to sound arrows separately from circuit lines. Adult approves each plan before assembly.

#### 20 min. Build

Six teams have five two-minute assembly turns each. Stagger teams by 30 seconds; start a new round every three minutes. Teacher spends the last 30 seconds of each turn observing that child: thirty checks take fifteen minutes, ending by minute seventeen. Keep other kits disconnected during each sound check. Remaining time supports safe adjustments and storage at desks.

At each child's own turn, reveal a fresh safe contact placement and read [[s02-X25-build]]. The child uses their plan, reserving the final connection decision for the teacher's observation. Record action, output and assistance on Individual s02-X25-build record. Do not infer performance from a teammate. If a connection fails, record it; adult checks once within the turn. Unfinished performance stays not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Function check

Teacher supervises six quiet checks, at most 30 seconds each, with two minutes for transitions. Only one buzzer sounds. Children note actual output or failure on [[s02-P25-1]]. **Ask.** What did this brief check establish? Expect output for this check only, not five-opening reliability. Do [[s02-P25-observe]] on the printed observation prompt. 

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X25]] now, exactly printed. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 90 seconds, 45 minutes; thirty plans times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Formative record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component; no points. Blank work gets a conference. Return papers and individual build feedback day 26.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect cells, count parts, label/store devices and wipe hands/tables. Teacher checks all stored circuits are disconnected.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank plan fields and reveal the exact exit at its turn. No projector: draw correct trigger states. Without kits, make paper plans; implementation stays not observed. Paper reasoning cannot earn hands-on evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw blank plan fields. No kit: use a paper plan; record building as not observed.

**Skip.** Group rounds and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 24 papers; do [[s02-P25-repair]].
- **10 min. Plan.** Begin [[s02-P25-1]]. Trace both terminals through the buzzer.
- **15 min. Build.** With a safe kit, adult prepares edges and checks connections. Observe the child using their plan during assembly; record one decision. Disconnect before changes. Check briefly; count/store and wipe within this block.
  **Say.** "What did one check show?" Expect only this observed output, not reliability.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X25-build]] is observed during work; no extra turn. Reveal [[s02-X25]]. Child answers alone. Save papers for day 26.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce team adjustments, preserving every child's assembly turn and exit. Longer: annotate an existing connection.

### Words and supports

Review criterion, limit, stored energy, current and terminal. Provide large contacts or grip assistance; record child's decision separately. Permit dictation and public passing.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 returns all papers and tests every opening at the fixed point. Error B, one terminal called complete, needs tracing both connections before testing.
