# Day 29: Preserving individual design evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Retrieve day 28 papers, Personal signal plan, original/retest All-opening test log and Personal refinement record. Prepare My individual design evidence within today's packet. Keep devices disconnected for display. Copy the exit master's revised circuit onto a covered board; conceal Day 29 exit ticket. Ready adult Private design feedback master for after-class notes; children receive feedback day 30. Prepare archive folders, trays and wiping supplies.

### Safety and set-up

No powered testing today. Keep cells disconnected and stored separately. Adult checks foil edges; children handle only checked disconnected devices. No tasting. Clear desks before archiving.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 28's ticket, s02-P28-1 and s02-P28-repair, with individual s02-X28-retest feedback. Review return and repair. Alone, do [[s02-P29-repair]]. If many tickets show error D, point to the original marked back-wall position and explain how keeping it fixes listening conditions. Many means more than ten of thirty. No new counts or live test.

#### 8 min. Share device

Six team representatives each take at most 45 seconds, four and a half minutes total, with three and a half for transitions and individual labels. Review rotating six; anyone may pass. Use disconnected devices and actual records. Each representative shows the stored-energy/current/sound path and one actual result. Everyone else adds their own evidence label to [[s02-P29-1]]. This is unjudged public talk, not thirty separate speaking tests. Review day 24 criterion and limits.

#### 22 min. Preserve individual evidence

Alone, children complete [[s02-P29-1]] in My individual design evidence, using their own plan, adult-initialed contributions and full logs. Use the first ten minutes for plan, energy arrows, criterion and limits; next eight for tests, failure-linked refinement reason and comparable conclusion; final four for evidence labels and attachment. Keep earlier printed prompts covered; consult handwritten rows. Circuit lines close through both terminals; energy arrows link stored input to sound, with some warming. If many omit input, locate the cell before tracing output. Mark supplied reasoning separately; absent implementation remains not observed. Collect originals and keep the revision area blank until day 35.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X29]] exactly as printed and uncover the revised circuit. Children may draw their retest table on the blank reverse of the same half slip. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, plus thirty records times three minutes, 90 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, no points today. Blank work gets a conference. Write private descriptive feedback using Private design feedback. Return ticket, packet and feedback day 30; teacher retains supporting originals for day 35 revision. Score the portfolio once after day 38 revisions.

#### 5 min. Archive

Collect initialed sheets with original logs and plans; check identities against teacher observation initials. Count/store disconnected devices. Wipe work area. Preserve all evidence and uncertainty.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank portfolio fields; reveal exact exit at its turn. No projector needed. Without devices, share records. Missing hands-on work remains not observed, even when paper reasoning is strong.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy portfolio fields and the exit. Copy the master's revised circuit and cover it until the exit. No device: use retained records and label missing performance honestly.

**Skip.** Representative turns and group display.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 28 papers; do [[s02-P29-repair]].
- **10 min. Explain.** Child traces the disconnected plan and shows one recorded observation.
  **Say.** "Which record supports your claim?" Expect the actual row and its energy-path connection.
- **15 min. Preserve.** Do [[s02-P29-1]]. Attach original plan, tests and initialed contributions. Keep revision space blank. Archive safely within this block.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X29]]. Child answers alone. Save all papers for day 30 descriptive feedback and day 35 revision.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public turns, preserving individual record and exit. Longer: add a location label to an existing evidence citation.

### Words and supports

Review criterion, limits, refinement, retest and energy arrows. Accept labeled drawings, dictation or an adult scribe; retain the child's independent decisions. Public passing is available.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 returns day 29's ticket and written design feedback. Error D, an improvement claim without test evidence, needs pointing to the actual comparable retest. Identify one revision target for day 35. Then investigate natural resources supplying useful energy.
