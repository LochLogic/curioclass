# Day 27: Planning a refinement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys, Alarm R teaching record and today's Teacher-only key. Retrieve day 26 papers, each Personal signal plan and All-opening test log. Prepare Assigned refinement and handoff within the student packet, omitting Name lines. Conceal Day 27 exit ticket. Display the retained design-cycle chart. Check disconnected devices and smooth contacts for inspection; store cells separately.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 26's ticket, s02-P26-1 and s02-P26-repair. Review return and repair. Alone, do [[s02-P27-repair]]. If many tickets show error C, compare the actual result with the measurable criterion before considering changes. Many means more than ten of thirty.

#### 8 min. Teach refinement

Teach refine: change to address test evidence. Teach retest: test again under matching conditions. Children read and use both words. Review current, stored energy and the complete circuit from day 20. Announce Alarm R teaching record as invented for practice. Original two detections in five, three failures with loose lead; after securing it, four in five, one loose-lead failure, same conditions. Teach the assigned lead repair through [[s02-K27-1]], then reveal its steps. It may maintain the path, but partial improvement is not five-of-five success. 

#### 10 min. Match evidence

Children locate their own failure or unclear rows. Review safe inspection: disconnect first, smooth contacts, no tasting. Pairs trace the affected connection, one 45-second turn each. Within parallel teams, each child inspects the disconnected build and chooses a feasible contact adjustment. Mark its location on the plan. Teacher checks feasibility without choosing for the child.

If all trials succeeded, choose a way to secure that successful placement; predict maintained performance. Unclear evidence stays unclear. Start the blank fields from Assigned refinement and handoff, labeling the change as chosen.

#### 12 min. Revision planning

Alone, do [[s02-P27-1]]: make a full-size contact-placement template for the chosen adjustment. Mark the existing and intended positions; keep the original record. Partners each predict the resulting output aloud for 45 seconds. Teacher hears a named rotating six within five minutes; anyone may pass. Collect every template for written feedback.

If many omit the input, locate stored cell energy before tracing the complete current path to sound. Use this energy transfer to explain the prediction. A planned change supplies no new test evidence.

#### 5 min. Team handoff

Each child records one identifiable contribution and matched retest conditions. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Keep observation point, parts and opening method. Teams agree on one compatible change and assign member steps. Adult stores the disconnected hardware; no powered tests. Post the unresolved energy-origin question on the question board for day 30.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X27]] now, exactly printed. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 75 seconds, about 38 minutes; thirty plans times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on the formative record. Blank work gets a conference. Return papers day 28.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank handoff fields; reveal exact exit at its turn. No projector: draw the assigned repaired loop. Missing live logs: use Alarm R only for reasoning, labeled supplied and invented; personal performance stays not observed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy handoff fields. No kit: inspect the paper plan. No live log: use invented Alarm R; personal testing remains not observed.

**Skip.** Group turns and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 26 papers; do [[s02-P27-repair]].
- **10 min. Learn.** Read the teacher-assigned loose-lead case, [[s02-K27-1]]. Explain refine and retest.
- **15 min. Plan.** Child chooses a feasible contact change and does [[s02-P27-1]]. Keep original evidence.
  **Say.** "How could this path carry energy?" Expect stored cell energy carried by current to sound through a complete circuit.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X27]]. Child answers alone. Save papers for day 28.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public turns; keep individual plan and exit. Longer: annotate another connection within the assigned repair.

### Words and supports

Refine, retest. Review criterion, fair test and energy arrows. Accept labeled paths or dictation; identify child's choices separately from assistance.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 returns all papers, implements the chosen refinement and retests. Error D, preference without evidence, needs matching the planned change to an observed failure before handling parts.
