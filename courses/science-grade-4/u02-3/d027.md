# Day 27: Planning a refinement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys, Alarm R teaching record and today's Teacher-only key. Today's key makes retest prediction optional on the exit; the stem does not ask it. Retrieve day 26 papers, each Personal signal plan and All-opening test log. Prepare Assigned refinement and handoff within the student packet, omitting Name lines. Conceal Day 27 exit ticket. Display the retained design-cycle chart. No hardware handled today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 26's ticket, s02-P26-1 and s02-P26-repair. Review return and repair. Alone, do [[s02-P27-repair]]. If many tickets show error C, compare the actual result with the measurable criterion before considering changes. Many means more than ten of thirty.

#### 8 min. Teach refinement

Introduce refine: make a change intended to improve a design. Retest: repeat the test under comparable conditions after a change. Review current, stored energy and the complete circuit from day 20. Announce Alarm R teaching record as invented for practice. Original two detections in five, three failures with loose lead; after securing it, four in five, one loose-lead failure, same conditions. Teach the assigned lead repair through [[s02-K27-1]], then reveal its steps. It may maintain the path, but partial improvement is not five-of-five success. Do not use wider-contact or paint alternatives.

#### 10 min. Match evidence

Children locate their own failure or unclear rows. Partners each trace one affected connection for 45 seconds, then annotate paper. Teacher assigns a feasible connection repair, without a child recommendation or proposal ranking. If no failure exists, assign securing an existing connection for durability; preserve five-of-five evidence and predict maintenance, not improvement already proved. Unclear evidence stays unclear. Complete the failure-row field of Assigned refinement and handoff.

#### 12 min. Revision planning

Alone, do [[s02-P27-1]] for the assigned change. Draw a complete resulting path and predict sound. Teacher hears a named rotating six, at most 45 seconds each within five minutes, unjudged; all return paper. Anyone may pass. If many omit energy input, locate cell storage before drawing the path. Review design-cycle chart and energy arrows: circuit lines close, energy arrows connect stored input to sound. No before-and-after success claim without a test.

#### 5 min. Team handoff

Each child records one identifiable contribution and matched retest conditions. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Keep observation point, parts and opening method. Teams assemble these written contributions; no powered tests. Post the unresolved energy-origin question on the question board for day 30.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X27]] now, exactly printed. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 75 seconds, about 38 minutes; thirty plans times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on the formative record. Blank work gets a conference. Return papers day 28.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank handoff fields; reveal exact exit at its turn. No projector: draw the assigned repaired loop. Missing live logs: use Alarm R only for reasoning, labeled supplied and invented; personal performance stays not observed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy handoff fields. No live log: use invented Alarm R for reasoning, marking personal testing not observed.

**Skip.** Group turns and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 26 papers; do [[s02-P27-repair]].
- **10 min. Learn.** Read the teacher-assigned loose-lead case, [[s02-K27-1]]. Explain refine and retest.
- **15 min. Plan.** Adult assigns a feasible change; child does [[s02-P27-1]]. Keep original evidence.
  **Say.** "How could this path carry energy?" Expect stored cell energy carried by current to sound through a complete circuit.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X27]]. Child answers alone. Save papers for day 28.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public turns; keep individual plan and exit. Longer: annotate another connection within the assigned repair.

### Words and supports

Refine, retest. Review criterion, fair test and energy arrows. Accept labeled paths or dictation; identify child's choices separately from assistance.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 returns all papers, implements the assigned refinement and retests. Error D, preference without evidence, needs matching the planned change to an observed failure before handling parts.
