# Day 28: Refining and retesting

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Retrieve day 27 papers, Assigned refinement and handoff, Personal signal plan and original All-opening test log. Precheck six retained kits for compatibility, smooth contacts and insulation. Prepare retest fields, Personal refinement record and concealed loose-contact placements with failure records. Keep Alarm R teaching record adult-only for missing-equipment reasoning. Conceal Day 28 exit ticket. Restore the same listening point, trays and wiping supplies.

### Safety and set-up

Only low-voltage cells, never outlets. Disconnect before changes; keep buzzer in the loop. Adult disconnects warm wires immediately. Smooth contacts, sound away from ears, no tasting. Two active handlers per kit; others remain beyond the boundary.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 27's ticket, s02-P27-1, s02-P27-repair and s02-K27-1. Review return and repair. Alone, do [[s02-P28-repair]]. If many tickets show error D, connect the change to actual evidence before parts are handled. Many means more than ten of thirty.

#### 5 min. Refine

Review refine and retest from day 27. Six teams work in parallel. Five children each implement a 45-second step, totaling three minutes 45 seconds, with 75 seconds for safe transitions. Partners note contributions; teacher verifies each child during the existing retest turn. Keep cells disconnected. Record each child's change and assistance on Individual s02-X28-retest record during their retest turn. Each child starts [[s02-P28-1]]; preserve original plans and failures. No unsupported success claim.

#### 20 min. Retest lane

Reuse day 26's six three-minute slots plus two transition minutes. Five 30-second operators and 30 seconds reset per team. Adult checks each circuit before powering. One buzzer only; teacher listens at the same back-wall point and signals detection visibly. Second handler observes contacts. Each active team member records all five results and contact/lead conditions on All-opening test log. Other teams prepare silently. Keep parts and opening method unchanged except the planned refinement. For [[s02-X28-retest]], reveal an unfamiliar safe loose-contact placement at the child's turn. Read its record: "Invented for practice. Contacts missed; no signal reached the original point." Allow ten seconds including reading and disconnected restoration, then twenty to operate the matching opening. Keep the planned refined geometry. Record action and outcome; unfinished performance stays not yet seen. For unclear readings, adult checks once within work time, then uses the invented fallback and records the gap. Use each team's thirty-second reset to finish five brief observation notes. Shared-kit evidence stays labeled shared.

#### 5 min. Compare

Alone, children compare their own matched records in Personal refinement record. **Ask.** What does your complete record support? Expect actual before/after results linked to the original criterion, including continuing failures. Review current carrying stored cell energy to sound. A change may fail or maintain an already successful result. If many claim improvement from preference, point to their actual rows. 

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal [[s02-X28]] now, exactly printed. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 75 seconds, about 38 minutes; thirty packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Record shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component, no points. Blank work gets a conference. Return papers and individual retest feedback day 29.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect and count/store every part. Wipe hands/tables. Teacher checks all devices are disconnected.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy blank fields and exact exit at its turn. No projector needed. One shared kit uses the same six slots. No kit: announce Alarm R as invented for practice; reason from its matched results. Mark personal implementation and live retest not observed, never as completed performance.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy blank fields. No kit: use invented Alarm R for reasoning; personal implementation and live retest stay not observed.

**Skip.** Team rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 27 papers; do [[s02-P28-repair]].
- **20 min. Refine and test.** Do [[s02-P28-1]]. Adult checks safe disconnected changes; observe the child's contact change and matching retest. Retest five openings at the original point. Record every result. Count/store parts and wipe within this block.
- **5 min. Compare.** Read both full logs.
  **Say.** "What does your evidence support?" Expect a criterion-linked conclusion, including any failures or uncertainty.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X28-retest]] is observed during work; no extra turn. Reveal [[s02-X28]]. Child answers alone. Save papers for day 29.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce comparison talk; retain every implementation turn, trial and exit. Longer: annotate another recorded uncertainty.

### Words and supports

Review refine, retest, fair test and criterion. Adult visible detection signals support access. Permit dictated evidence; record decisions separately from physical assistance.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 returns all papers and preserves individual evidence. Error D, unmatched results called improvement, needs restoring the fixed observation point before making claims.
