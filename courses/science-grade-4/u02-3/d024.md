#### 5 min. Retrieve criteria

Review grade 3 need: who needs what, so that; criterion: a way to check the need is met; limit: the most we allow; token and cost. Use [[s02-K24-1]], then reveal [[steps:s02-K24-1]]. Children do [[s02-P24-budget]] independently; retain responses.

#### 10 min. Mechanism reading

Read the original account aloud once. Review day 20 stored energy, current, circuit and terminal. Explain spring and slider mechanisms with the displayed correct trigger states. The folded card supplies spring action; foil conducts. No live assembly. **Ask.** [[s02-K24-2]] Expect the complete path through both terminals and buzzer. Reveal its steps after partner tracing, one 45-second turn each. The spring moves contacts; stored cell energy supplies sound. Some energy also warms parts.

#### 10 min. Define problem

Review the design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare. Set the criterion: detect all five lid openings from the marked back-wall point. Limits: one desk, listed parts, ten tokens and tomorrow's 20-minute build block. Constraint means this familiar limit. Children start [[s02-P24-1]] on their Personal signal plan. If many confuse criterion with limit, point to the result before resources.

#### 10 min. Own proposal

Children sketch one trigger state. Use Parts and token ledger: four plus three plus two plus one totals ten, leaving zero. Prices are invented for practice, not purchase prices. Children compare their own plan with the stated criterion and limits. Teacher checks feasibility without claiming untested reliability. Retain plans for tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal only now [[s02-X24]], exactly printed. Four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 90 seconds, 45 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on the formative record. Blank work gets a conference. Return all papers day 25.

**Stored energy and a signal.**

A cell stores energy. A buzzer sounds when current has a complete path through it. The path joins both cell terminals through the buzzer. A lid can control that path.

One design uses a spring contact. The shut lid holds the folded contact away from another contact. Opening releases it, joining the contacts.

Another design slides a contact. A card link pulls it into contact when the lid opens. The adult prepares that link.

Both designs convert stored energy into sound. A failed connection interrupts current. Tests tell whether the signal works at our agreed point.

Source: [Batteries, circuits, and transformers](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/batteries-circuits-and-transformers.php), circuits. Federal energy information, public domain; read 2026-11-23. Original mechanism designs.
