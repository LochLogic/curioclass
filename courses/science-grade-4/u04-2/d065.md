# Day 65: Compare two land surfaces

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check turf treatment, plant and soil allergies; use gloves as needed. Pretest two matched slopes with safe dry viewing positions. Place rulers beside wet edges, zero at each marked sediment front. Prepare fresh matched starting surfaces for six groups. Set out protection and cleanup supplies. Integrate plant-sheet.md; cover the Exit box. Prepare day 64 feedback. Keep teacher-key.md private.

### The day

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review erosion from grade 2: wind or water carries material away. Review the erosion solution tested on day 59, using the actual retained record. Children complete [[s04-P65-retrieve]] as a description of supporting evidence, not a prediction for the held root case.

#### 3 min. Controls

Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Match slope, starting sediment, moisture, water and pouring position as closely as possible. Record differences that remain: turf changes structure and water absorption as well as roots. All present wear goggles through cleanup. Adult alone pours, resets and handles plants. Nothing is tasted; keep sand damp.

#### 24 min. Plant-cover turns

Six groups of five, four minutes each. Adult pours equal cupfuls at matched positions, 45 seconds. Five children independently read both sediment fronts, 30 seconds each, totaling 150 seconds. Adult resets, 45 seconds. Each child completes [[s04-P65-measure]] with date, trial, controls, whole-centimeter distances and actual observations. Review ruler zero from day 3: today's fronts start at zero. Never read the water edge as sediment. A one-centimeter ruler supports whole centimeters only.

Retry an unclear reading once within the child's slot; otherwise mark unresolved with its reason. If safe turns cannot finish, record fewer actual trials. Supplied fallback-records.md is announced as invented for practice and filed separately, without measurement credit.

Waiting children annotate their own observation fields. If day 64 showed more than ten misses, use five waiting-paper minutes for one selected repair, without taking any apparatus turn: error A separates breakage from travel; error D traces an organism's changed feature; error E connects water to plant condition. Public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Explain

Return day 64's s04-P64-records and five exit responses with feedback. Those repaired during waiting time locate that correction. Others use feedback to annotate independently. Finish today's own evidence notes, preserving contrary findings. This comparison cannot isolate roots alone. Do not rehearse the exit's unequal-water audit or nonzero ruler starts.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Only adult and any second handler touch wet equipment. Contain sediment, wipe spills, clean protection and wash hands with soap. Dry observers file pages. Settle runoff; keep solids out of sinks. Remove goggles after cleanup.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X65-distance]] and [[s04-X65-cause]] on the half slip; reveal [[s04-XB65-roots]] in the Exit box. Children answer alone. After class allow 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exit marking, and 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for practical feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return exits day 66; return both practice responses for day 70 Feedback revision, seven minutes. Preserve originals.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: use the labeled invented plant panel; leave firsthand measurement unobserved. No printer: copy fields and reveal exact exit stems only at Exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe equipment or protection, use the labeled invented plant panel. Copy fields without printing.

**Skip.** Six-group rotation and public reporting.

- **5 min. Recall.** Complete [[s04-P65-retrieve]] and identify controls.
- **15 min. Compare.** Adult alone pours following class safety. Child completes [[s04-P65-measure]]. Keep supplied data separate.
  **Say.** "What do your observations support?" Expect actual movement evidence and the limit on isolating roots alone.
- **5 min. Explain.** Finish evidence notes and read day 64 feedback.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Contain solids, wipe, clean goggles and wash hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X65-distance]], [[s04-X65-cause]] and [[s04-XB65-roots]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Run fewer complete live trials, preserving individual readings, cleanup and exit. Extend notes on unmatched surface features.

### Words and supports

Review living things changing places from kindergarten and evidence from grade 3. Turf is grass with roots and attached soil. Use enlarged ruler marks; a scribe records the child's own reading.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 revises the canyon model. Return today's three exit responses in Return. If many show error C, match water before isolating cover; error D needs an organism-effect link. Error x on distance needs ruler-start support. Today's practice returns day 70, without changing original measurements.
