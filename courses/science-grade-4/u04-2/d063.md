# Day 63: How far does the evidence reach?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare written feedback on all day 62 pages and slips. Integrate claim-cards.md. Set out the record sheet. Keep exit-63.md and teacher-key.md concealed. Today's paper comparison is the complete route; the planned simulation is unavailable.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 62's s04-P62-retrieve, s04-P62-graph, s04-P62-arrows, s04-X62-graph, s04-X62-claim and s04-XB62-process. If many show error C, retain every result on their original records and narrow the claim. Error A needs review: weathering breaks rock material down; erosion carries it away. Review both from days 58 and 62. Use feedback rather than restaging the exit case.

#### 5 min. Paper comparison

Use claim-cards.md, [[s04-P63-paper]]. Announce invented for practice. Children alone underline conditions held equal and circle the changed condition. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. They identify repeated evidence already supplied; no apparatus turns occur.

#### 20 min. Judge claims

Use the same item, without adding new cases. Five minutes: each child explains what the matched repeats support. Five: locate the evidence for that statement in the stem. Five: partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously to explain one limit, then annotate independently. Five: compare the tested setting with the untested settings already named in their own responses. Public talk may be passed.

Use the teacher-only answer key after children respond. The repeats support a grain-size effect under these tested conditions. They do not establish an effect in every setting. Error C overstates the cause. Do not rehearse the exit's confounded pebble test or construct its correction.

#### 10 min. Repair

Children reread their own response beside feedback and improve its evidence link, preserving the original. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each, within five minutes while others work independently. Review evidence from grade 3: observations supporting an explanation. Spend the remaining five minutes on independent completion. A valid explanation names the changed grain size, matched conditions and tested-setting limit. A loose-grain model does not give a solid-rock canyon rate. These annotations stay on the same practice response.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X63]], answered independently on paper. Collect slips and practice pages. After class allow 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, for exits; practice feedback takes 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Use record.md's one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all day 64, including children not heard.

### Other rooms

No screen is needed. No printer: copy the practice stem and fields before class; reveal the exact exit stem only at Exit. Dictation records the child's reasoning.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the practice fields. The adult takes the partner's role.

**Skip.** Public reports and rotating-six observations.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 62 feedback.
- **10 min. Compare.** Read [[s04-P63-paper]]. Mark conditions held and changed.
- **10 min. Explain.** Child writes the evidence link.
  **Say.** "What is supported, and what remains untested?" Expect a grain-size effect in these conditions, without an every-setting claim.
- **5 min. Repair.** Child improves that same response, keeping the original.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X63]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce public reporting, preserving individual reasoning, correction and exit. Extend annotations of which stated conditions support the claim, without inventing new records.

### Words and supports

Review fair test, evidence and erosion. Review claim from kindergarten: what you think, supported by what you saw. Read the stem aloud neutrally; accept labeled evidence marks and dictated reasoning.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 compares ice, wind, roots, burrows and plant-water records separately. Return today's slip and practice in Return. If many show error C, identify changed conditions before allowing a cause claim; more repeats cannot remove a confound.
