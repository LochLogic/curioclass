# Day 61: What changed in the samples?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check clay ingredients and allergies. Thaw samples fully; retain day 59's initial records. Pretest matched jars; inspect lids. Place wet apparatus beyond observers' reach on a covered tray. Lay out eye protection and cleanup supplies. Print abrasion-sheet.md within today's page; prepare freezing-sheet.md and day 60 feedback. Keep teacher-key.md and exit-61.md hidden.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Pick up day 60's promise: observe thawed clay and enclosed rock abrasion. Reopen freezing-sheet.md. Review baseline from day 59: a starting record for comparison. Children locate their own initial cracks and treatments before viewing later samples. Review freeze from grade 2: cooling until water becomes ice. Missing starting observations remain missing.

#### 20 min. Demonstrations

Review weathering from day 58: breaking rock material down. Review sediment: loose pieces of rock or soil. Introduce abrasion: rubbing that wears material down. All present wear splash goggles through cleanup. Adult alone handles clay and jars; nothing is tasted.

First ten minutes: show initial records, thawed clay and unfrozen control. Each child answers [[s04-P61-clay]] through dated observation drawings. Clay models change but cannot prove rock behavior. Preserve no visible change. Retry a blurred view once inside this slot; then mark unresolved.

Next ten: show both wet jars before treatment. Adult gently shakes one secured jar 100 times, counting within demonstration time. Leave the matched jar unshaken. Stop for leakage. Let water settle before comparing grains and edges. Each child records [[s04-P61-abrasion]]. Do not promise chips. Supplied fallback-records.md is separate: say aloud, invented for practice. It never replaces a child's actual observation. Work each investigation separately, without rehearsing the exit's paired evidence judgment.

#### 10 min. Compare controls

For the first five minutes, return day 60 exit ticket s04-X60-record and written task s04-X60-cause with feedback. Repair the recorded causal-evidence gap instead of opening discussion; preserve original readings.

Return day 60's s04-P60-measure and both exit responses with feedback. If many show error C, use five minutes to retain all their repeats and identify the limits of their cause claims. Never discard an inconvenient measurement. Others locate trial labels independently.

For five minutes, children add control observations to today's records. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously to point to their own observations. Public sharing is optional. Error B denies any rock change after a failed short trial; distinguish an absent result from an impossible change. Keep keys out of view.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult and any second handler contain solids, wipe spills, clean protection and wash hands. Dry observers file records. Keep solids out of sinks. Remove goggles after cleanup.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X61]] for independent paper responses. Collect both practical records and slips. After class allow 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for exit marking; practical notes take another 30 times 30 seconds. Use record.md's single scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all day 62.

### Other rooms

No safe kit: use only today's invented fallback panel; mark firsthand observation unavailable. No printer: copy record fields and reveal the exact exit stem only at Exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe jars, clay or goggles, use the labeled invented fallback panel. Copy fields without printing.

**Skip.** Public reports and class feedback distribution.

- **5 min. Start.** Find the initial clay record.
- **15 min. Observe.** Adult follows class safety. Child completes [[s04-P61-clay]] and [[s04-P61-abrasion]]. Keep actual and supplied observations separate.
  **Say.** "What changed from the starting record?" Expect an observed feature, or an honest report of no change.
- **5 min. Compare.** Point to the control for each observation.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Contain solids, wipe, clean protection and wash hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X61]]. Child answers independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, preserving observation, controls, cleanup and exit. Extend dated detail in the same drawings.

### Words and supports

Review evidence: observations supporting an explanation. Offer enlarged views and a scribe for the child's observations. A control is the matched comparison left untreated.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 graphs erosion observations and revises process arrows. Return today's slip and practical pages in Return. If many show error A, separate breaking from carrying; error B needs changed rock evidence without claiming every short trial succeeds.
