## Day 61 clay and stones

Clay A and B start with similar cracks. After thawing, A has a wider crack; B stays unchanged. This is a clay model, not proof of rock behavior. Two matched jars start with rounded soft stones. After gentle enclosed shaking, jar A has loose grains and duller edges. Unshaken jar B looks unchanged. A real short trial may show nothing.
