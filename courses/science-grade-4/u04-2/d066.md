# Day 66: Revise the canyon explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare feedback on day 65 exits. Reopen canyon-photo.jpg and each child's earlier model. Gather dated observations from days 60, 61 and 65, keeping supplied records distinct. Integrate revision-sheet.md and process-cards-66.md text. Cover the Exit box; reassemble exit-66.md with only its model on the half slip. Use the generated answer key for practice and teacher-key.md only for exit look-fors.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 65's s04-X65-distance, s04-X65-cause and s04-XB65-roots. If many show error C, use feedback to match water before isolating cover. Error D needs review of living things changing places from kindergarten. Error x on distances needs review of ruler zero and end minus start from day 3. Correct their own work without new examples. Practice feedback and revision remain day 70's allocation.

#### 20 min. Revise canyon

Review day 57's tentative canyon model and question board. Show the same photograph. A still image records walls, stripes and a river, not visible movement through time. Review weathering and erosion from day 58, and cause-and-effect arrows from day 57. Review gravity from grade 3: a pull down.

Children complete [[s04-P66-revise]]: five minutes locating one dated observation, ten revising one material-removal link with evidence, five comparing original and revised models. Review two-column source notes from day 30: source statement and source location. Mark observed features separately from inferred processes. Partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously to locate supporting evidence; public sharing may be passed.

Review slow change from grade 2: land changes a little at a time. Connect that model to the scale limit: a loose-sand tray cannot establish a solid-rock canyon's rate. Source U4-canyon supports river carving and a very old Earth. State Earth's great age plainly; no numerical date follows from this model. Review layer from grade 3: a band laid with other bands. Defer fossil ordering to tomorrow. Do not rehearse rockfall diversion or historical questions before Exit.

#### 15 min. Process practice

Announce process-cards-66.md as invented for practice, not observations. Children complete [[s04-P66-records]], three minutes per record and three minutes to check evidence links. I2 shows widening cracks, without directly showing fresh chips. W2 shows longer dust streaks after wind; source and fragment-size changes remain unknown. R2 links root growth with spreading blocks, without isolating every cause. P2 supports plant-water effects through controls; the separate channel change lacks its own controlled comparison. Error E denies water effects on plants. Keep answers tied to what each record actually states. Keep each claim within its own record's evidence.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X66-model]] on the half slip and [[s04-X66-question]] in the Exit box. Children work alone: four minutes for the labeled model, one for a question. Collect both and practice pages. After class allow 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, for exit marking and 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, for practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits return day 67 in Return; practice returns day 70 for eight minutes in Feedback revision.

### Other rooms

No display: inspect the printed photograph. No printer: copy practice text and model fields; reveal exact exit stems only at Exit. Supplied practice never becomes firsthand evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** No display: use the printed canyon photograph. Copy fields without printing. Adult takes the partner's role.

**Skip.** Public reports and feedback distribution.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 65 exit feedback.
- **15 min. Revise.** Complete [[s04-P66-revise]] using one dated observation.
  **Say.** "Which observation supports your changed model?" Expect a named observation linked to material removal, without an exact cutting rate.
- **10 min. Records.** Complete [[s04-P66-records]]. Keep each evidence link separate.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X66-model]] and [[s04-X66-question]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Cut public sharing, preserving individual revision, process records and exit. Extend uncertainty labels in the same model.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, layer and gravity. Inferred means proposed from evidence rather than directly observed. Accept labeled drawings, dictation and home-language reasoning.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 builds layered clay with fossil tokens and reads environmental records. Return today's exits in Return. Error B needs visible change evidence; error x needs a question answerable from layer evidence. Practice feedback waits for day 70; preserve all originals.
