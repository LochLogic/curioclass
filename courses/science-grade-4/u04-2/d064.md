# Day 64: Trace one change at a time

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 63 feedback. Integrate process-cards.md dated records once; use process-64.svg only for enlarged viewing. Cover Side 2's Exit box. Keep exit-64.md and teacher-key.md concealed. These are invented records, not collected photographs. No child supplies a personal disaster or home story.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 63's s04-X63 and s04-P63-paper. If many show error C, use their feedback to distinguish matching conditions from merely repeating a comparison. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Review weathering and erosion from day 58. Keep original responses visible; do not rehearse a fresh confounded claim.

#### 25 min. Separate processes

Say aloud that process-cards.md and its dates are invented for practice. Five minutes per record I, W, R, B and P. Each child completes [[s04-P64-records]] by annotating changed features and adding evidence limits, rather than supplying the exit's process labels.

I: compare initial and later cracks and the unfrozen control. Changed surfaces support material breakdown. Review freeze from grade 2. W: locate the dust and sheltered comparison; arrival does not identify where the dust began or prove new breakage. No blowing dry sand. R: trace the marked gap beside the widening root. Review living things changing places from kindergarten; these particular records are new. B: trace the longer opening and sagging ground. Digging changes the opening; the record alone does not establish what triggered the sag. P: trace changed plant condition under matched water treatment, without inventing land measurements.

Children mark one supporting feature and one limit for each record. A blurred drawing gets one retry within its five minutes, then an unresolved note. Error D denies organism effects; return to the marked physical change. Public talk may be passed.

#### 10 min. Rain and life

Use the P annotations from the same item. Teach the rainfall connection from Source U4-surface: rain supplies water that affects which plants can live in a place. Review erosion: water can carry material. The P record supports a plant-water connection, but records no groove measurement. Keep these evidence strands separate; do not practice designing the exit's combined rainfall test.

Partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously to locate their water-treatment evidence. Children then finish annotations independently. Error E denies water effects on plants; contrast the matched plants' observed conditions. Missing observations remain missing, rather than becoming a cause claim.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X64]] on the half slip and four separate Side 2 items. Allow three minutes for the rainfall comparison and two for brief process labels: [[s04-XB64-ice]], [[s04-XB64-wind]], [[s04-XB64-root]], [[s04-XB64-burrow]]. Children answer alone; neutral reading is allowed. Collect all. After class allow 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, for the exit set, plus 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, for practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all day 65 in Explain.

### Other rooms

No display: use the printed dated text. No printer: copy records before class; reveal exact exit stems only at Exit. No live investigation is claimed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer or display: copy the dated records. Adult reads unclear text neutrally.

**Skip.** Public sharing and feedback distribution.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 63 feedback.
- **15 min. Records.** Complete [[s04-P64-records]], adding changed features and limits.
- **10 min. Water.** Revisit the P annotations.
  **Say.** "What can these plant records support?" Expect a water-treatment link to plant condition, without a groove claim.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X64]], [[s04-XB64-ice]], [[s04-XB64-wind]], [[s04-XB64-root]] and [[s04-XB64-burrow]]. Child answers independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public reports, retaining every separate record and exit. Extend evidence-limit annotations without adding a combined comparison.

### Words and supports

Review evidence, weathering and erosion. Burrow means an animal-dug opening. Allow pointing with dictated labels. Enlarge marked features; a missing observation does not earn an error letter.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 measures rooted and bare runoff. Return today's practice and five exit responses in Explain. If many show error D or error E, connect the changed feature to the organism or water evidence. Error A needs breaking separated from travel. Fit repair within five waiting-paper minutes.
