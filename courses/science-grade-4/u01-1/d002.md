# Day 2: What makes our comparison fair?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and teacher-only key. Return day 1 papers with one next step. Prepare control-cards.md and the retained question board. Keep the shared ring exit covered. Pretest visible approach speeds in the guarded lane; set protection and six cleaning places. Keep ramp-record-r.md separate from measured records. Use the shared templates for the matching student-page sections.

### Safety and set-up

Review day 1's stop signal, no throwing, no tasting and counted marble. Existing room-wide protection stays on through live setup and packing. Two handlers enter; four observers remain behind the boundary. Teacher retrieves escaped objects only after stopping. Missing protection or failed containment means paper records. Five cleanup minutes include wiping and bagging goggles.

### The day

Other practice stays in folders; discuss it without collecting.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review the Return and repair routine. Return day 1's exit and s01-Pq1 notes. If many showed error I, use [[s01-P2]]: distinguish something seen from a future expectation. Everyone labels independently; then discuss. Other children follow their feedback. Blank work gets a brief conference.

#### 8 min. Compare controls

Retrieve one board question about speed. Review fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing.

**Ask.** [[s01-K2]]

[[steps:s01-K2]]

Use control-cards.md for the supplied setups. Children cross out the extra change before discussion. Error G changes the receiver as well as speed. Review trial: one try. Keep marble, cup, surface, cup start and contact arrangement fixed.

#### 22 min. Try the comparison

The next rotating six have two handling roles and four observation roles. Fit protection within the first three minutes. Run three slower and three faster trials at two minutes each, alternating speeds. Reset the cup to its tape mark each time; watch approach speed. Use [[s01-Pr2]] individually. R, labeled and said invented for practice, replaces live work if conditions fail.

Remaining seven minutes: pairs compare observations, 45 seconds each, and flag accidental changes. Hear six for 45 seconds each while others continue. Record names and collect all pages for day 3. Revisit the board question without supplying an energy answer. Retain all records; no blank plan card in practice.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop, count marble back, store ramp and cup, clear tape. Wipe and bag protection. Review the same cleanup routine as day 1. Six captains each clean five student pairs; the adult cleans their own. Use two minutes to pack and three to clean, following wipe directions.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

First reveal the ring case now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X2]] exactly. Each child plans independently on paper. No live ring is used. Mark after class: 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes, using the teacher-only key and the single record scale. Flag error G; return day 3.

Also review the 30 collected practice pages after class: 15 seconds each, seven and a half minutes. Add one specific next step; discuss other practice without collecting it.

### Other rooms

Without safe equipment use R; this diagnoses comparisons but supplies no conducting evidence. No printer: copy the page and keep the exit separate until its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe equipment, protection or class records: read day 1's R as invented for practice. No printer: copy paper.

**Skip.** Group handling and public sharing.

- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s01-P2]].
- **8 min. Compare.** Use [[s01-K2]].
  **Say.** "Which extra change hides the cause?" Expect: cup size; either change could affect travel.
- **12 min. Try.** Watch safe rolls or read R; complete [[s01-Pr2]].
  **Say.** "What stayed fixed?" Expect: same marble, cup, surface, start and contact arrangement.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s01-X2]]; the child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce public discussion, preserving six trials and exit. Longer: explain why an off-center contact needs a flag.

### Words and supports

Review: trial, fair test, observation, expected. Read stems aloud. Children may label a drawing instead of writing full sentences. Any child may pass in talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 returns day 2's exit and comparison page. If many show error G, undo the extra cup change in Return and repair. Then retrieve ruler zero, whole centimeters and one line-plot mark before measuring cup travel.
