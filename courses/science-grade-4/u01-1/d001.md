# Day 1: What changes when a marble hits a cup?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and teacher-only answer key. Review the familiar categories: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Print exit-001.md once per child and keep it covered; its stem is the ticket. Send family.md home. Prepare the position strip, question board and teacher-only record sheet. Pretest visible speeds in the guarded lane. Fit room-wide protection. Set the ruler, tape, wipes and bags at six cleaning places. Use ramp-anchor-r.md, printed and said invented for practice, if live observation fails.

### Safety and set-up

Room-wide eye protection stays on through live setup and packing; otherwise use R. Review the grade 3 stop signal: raised hand, freeze and look. No throwing or tasting; keep the counted marble from mouths. Two handlers enter the lane; four observers stay behind its boundary. Teacher retrieves escaped objects after rolling stops. Review wiping and bagging goggles; five cleanup minutes.

### The day

Collect each student page for feedback next lesson. Review one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. More than ten misses is many.

#### 3 min. Protection and setup

Fit protection; choose the first six. Others watch outside the lane. Stop for damaged containment.

#### 7 min. Anchor

Roll the same marble twice into the same cup. Review faster and slower from kindergarten: watch approach motion, not release marks alone.

**Ask.** [[s01-K1]]

[[steps:s01-K1]]

If the contrast fails, stop; read R as invented for practice. Give no energy explanation.

#### 5 min. Review position patterns

Review grade 3 position tables: turn, side, mark. Use the position-strip.md diagram with [[s01-P1]] on the student page. Trace intervals for misses. Retrieve predict and pattern from grade 3.

#### 20 min. Notice and question board

Alone: [[s01-Pq1]]. Review grade 3 claim, evidence and reason: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Keep reasons tentative. Review cause and effect: a push or pull changes motion. Review the question board, using the retained question-board.md template: notices become questions. Group evidence and unanswered questions.

Review grade 3's rotating six and named roster. Each partner speaks for 45 seconds. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each, inside five minutes; record names. Anyone may pass. Collect all notes for day 2. Sketch the class's observed cup changes and attach children's labels. Leave the cause open for testing.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop; count the marble back, pack ramp and cup, clear tape. Wipe and bag protection; retain notes and board. Six captains each clean five student pairs; the adult cleans their own. Use two minutes to pack and three to clean, following wipe directions.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal each child's covered panel now. Say invented for practice. Read [[s01-X1]] exactly; independent paper, no coaching. After class: 30 children times 20 seconds, ten minutes marking with the teacher-only key. Blank work gets a conference. Error I treats a guess as a result.

Also review the 30 collected practice pages after class: 15 seconds each, seven and a half minutes. Add one specific next step; return the whole page in Return and repair next lesson.

### Other rooms

No safe equipment: read R without live rolls. No printer: copy page and covered panel onto paper. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe ramp, protection or records: read R as invented for practice. No printer: copy panels.

**Skip.** Group handling and class board.

- **8 min. Notice.** Watch or read two rolls; use [[s01-K1]].
  **Say.** "What changed?" Expect: cup travel or sound, separate from possible causes.
- **7 min. Review.** Do [[s01-P1]]; trace equal gaps.
- **10 min. Questions.** Do [[s01-Pq1]].
  **Say.** "What still needs testing?" Expect: a question, not a proven guess.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the train panel; [[s01-X1]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public notices; keep individual notes and exit. Longer: sort questions by observable outcomes.

### Words and supports

Review: faster, slower, predict, pattern. Read aloud as needed. Accept labeled sketches or a scribe; keep ideas tentative.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 returns day 1's exit and notes. If many, over ten, show error I, separate observed from expected in Return and repair. Others receive a specific next step there. Then diagnose fair comparisons.
