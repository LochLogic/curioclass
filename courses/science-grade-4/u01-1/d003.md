# Day 3: How far did the cup travel?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and teacher-only key. Prepare day 2 feedback, measurement-strip.md and trial-sheet.md. Keep the shared washer exit covered. Align the whole-centimeter ruler with cup travel; pretest visible speeds and readable movement. Set protection and six cleaning places. Retain the question board; keep ramp-record-r.md separate. Use line-plot.md for the review mark and tomorrow's display.

### Safety and set-up

Review day 2's guarded lane, stop signal, no throwing, no tasting and count-back. Two handlers enter; four observers stay behind the boundary. Live setup through packing requires room-wide protection. Teacher retrieves escaped objects after stopping. Otherwise use R, printed and said invented for practice. Five cleanup minutes include wiping and bagging goggles.

### The day

Other practice stays in folders; discuss it without collecting.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 2's exit and s01-Pr2. If many show error G, diagnose [[s01-P3]]: remove the extra cup change. Everyone responds; other children use their next step. Retain comparison records in folders.

#### 5 min. Review measurement

Review centimeter, ruler zero and whole centimeter from grade 3.

**Ask.** [[s01-K3]]

[[steps:s01-K3]]

Review the familiar mathematics line plot: teacher places one given ribbon length, then children do [[s01-Pp3]]. Equal steps, axis label, one mark. This is not a travel-and-plot practice sheet.

#### 25 min. Measure repeated trials

First four minutes: fit protection and demonstrate measurement. Match ruler zero to the cup's starting front edge. Mark the stopped front edge, reading nearest whole centimeter; below one centimeter, record less than one centimeter. Keep the ruler still and aligned. Do not measure energy.

Next twelve minutes: the next six run three slower and three faster rolls, alternating speeds, two minutes per trial. Reset the same cup each time; verify visible approach speeds. All thirty complete [[s01-Pr3]] on trial-sheet.md or its matching student-page table.

Remaining nine minutes: review the grade 3 routine, repeat an unclear try once and keep both. Enter unreadable, repeat once, retain both entries. Flag off-center contact; never erase an inconvenient reading. Pairs check units while teacher hears six, 45 seconds each. Collect pages for day 4; retain class records. Use separate R if a safe readable comparison fails.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop; count and pack the marble, ramp and cup. Clear tape. Wipe and bag protection. Retain the dated records. Six captains each clean five student pairs; the adult cleans their own. Use two minutes to pack and three to clean, following wipe directions.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the covered ruler strip now. Say invented for practice. Read [[s01-X3]] exactly; independent paper. The two blanks hold travel records with units. Mark after class: 30 times 25 seconds, twelve and a half minutes, using the teacher-only key. Error H uses the endpoint; error B labels distance as energy. Return day 4.

Also review the 30 collected practice pages after class: 15 seconds each, seven and a half minutes. Add one specific next step; discuss other practice without collecting it.

### Other rooms

Without safe equipment, record R as invented, without claiming class measurements. No printer: copy tables, a blank plot space and covered exit strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe station, protection or class record: read day 1's R, invented for practice. No printer: copy tables.

**Skip.** Group handling and public sharing.

- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s01-P3]].
- **8 min. Review.** Use [[s01-K3]] and [[s01-Pp3]].
  **Say.** "What does the ruler measure?" Expect: distance in centimeters; count intervals, not the endpoint.
- **12 min. Trials.** Watch safe rolls or read R; do [[s01-Pr3]].
  **Say.** "What if a reading fails?" Expect: keep unreadable, repeat once, keep both.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the strip; [[s01-X3]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner discussion, keeping trials, unclear-reading repair and exit. Longer: explain why zero must match the starting edge.

### Words and supports

Review whole centimeter and ruler zero. Line plot: one mark per record on equal intervals. Enlarge the ruler and axis; a scribe records the child's own response. Passing in talk is allowed.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 returns day 3's exit and trial table. If many show error H, count start-to-stop intervals for five minutes; error B gets five minutes labeling distance. Then build the class plot and consider repeated speed effects.
