# Day 85: Two motions, one matched comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, returned day 84 causal strips and exits, shaking log and Shake fallback S. Set nested trays and contained latex-free balls, matched buildings, tape, loads and ruler; pretest safe endpoints. Lay out impact eye protection and wipes/bags and adult handwashing supplies. Prepare one-item half slips; cover Exit boxes. Ask sand/clay allergies before day 86.

### Safety and set-up

One adult-controlled station; no climbing. Check latex allergies; use latex-free balls. Everyone nearby wears impact eye protection through setup/work/cleanup, faces back. Stop if anything escapes or jams; one safe reset, then S. No flying loads. Five-minute cleanup; adult contains parts.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair, criteria

Return day 84's causal strips and both exit papers. If many show error D, [[s05-P85-1]], teaching overflow only, five minutes. Unresolved error F: [[s05-P85-2]], five minutes. Ready children check matching starts. Review criterion: standing means upright with load supported. Review whole-centimeter ruler readings from day 3. Adult marks two pretested gentle stroke endpoints; record starts/ends at one-centimeter steps, never earthquake magnitude. Goggles on in final minute.

#### 24 min. Matched shaking

Six groups of five visit four minutes each. Each visit: two adult 30-second motion runs including resets, 45 seconds recording, 60 seconds condition comparison, 75 seconds inspection/exchange. Both buildings ride together. Restore starts/load before each motion; repeat marked strokes each visit. All thirty observe and record [[s05-P85-3]], including failures. Waiting groups audit one cause and action strip, then copy visible trial outcomes. No individual apparatus queue.

Review two-condition comparison from day 53. Partners use [[s05-P85-4]], without recommendation-plus-next-test responses. If motion jams or a load escapes, stop. One safe reset within the visit; if unsafe/unreadable again, announce Shake fallback S is invented for practice. Mark supplied rows separately; note missing firsthand evidence. S standing totals are braced six/five, unbraced six/two. Promise no live winner.

#### 1 min. Comparison board

Post actual whole-model counts by motion, preserving unreadable entries. Results concern these models/motions. Children keep the log for feedback; it is unscored teaching for later comparison. Public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Two minutes: wipe/bag goggles simultaneously after apparatus stops. Three: dry children file logs while adult contains balls/loads, wipes station and washes hands. No sink queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the invented broad/narrow-base record, never practiced. Alone, [[s05-X85-recommend]] on one-item half slip; [[s05-X85-challenge]] in Exit box. Four minutes answer, one collect both. Read neutrally.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy today's texts and blank records beforehand; cover reserved work. Use the named invented fallback if safe observation is unavailable, noting missing practical evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe apparatus or eye protection, use S, announced invented. Without printer/projector, copy the log. Adult handles apparatus; child observes with eye protection.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public board.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P85-1]] if needed.
- **15 min. Observe.** Copy S using [[s05-P85-3]], or follow the adult-controlled class procedure.
- **5 min. Compare.** Child does [[s05-P85-4]].
  **Say.** "What changes between columns?" Expect actual differences, or S's stronger counts, five versus two.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Adult contains parts; wipe/bag eye protection and wash hands after handling.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the held record. Child answers [[s05-X85-recommend]] and [[s05-X85-challenge]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, retaining repairs, evidence and exit. Extend by naming an untested condition.

### Words and supports

Review condition, criterion and whole centimeter. Braces are the only model-design difference. Enlarge logs; provide seated views and neutral scribing. Unreadable differs from fallen.

### Tomorrow

Return the student written task with descriptive notes. Practice feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Mark both exit responses: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Note language or missing evidence separately.

Return unscored shaking log and both exit papers on day 86. Error E adds a missing test condition; unresolved error D inspects teaching overflow, five minutes each. Then prepare dry flood templates and matched controls.
