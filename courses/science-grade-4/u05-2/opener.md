# Section 5.2 opener: Comparing hazard protection

Days 83 to 85 join hazard solutions, causal reasoning and comparison. Protection reduces damage
or exposure while processes continue. Monitoring supplies information; delivery and usable protective
action must follow. Braces can reduce shaking damage, not stop shaking. U5-braces is browser check:
both automated sources refused; keep this for review. Models do not certify real buildings.

Review hazard, harm, lessen, criterion, limit and design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test,
compare, from grade 3 and this course's written units 2/3. Review constraint, the familiar limit on a
design, taught day 24; establish missing routines afresh. Research cards have source locators. Eight
90-second readings take twelve minutes; eight remain for notes. Practice single-hazard chains, never
E83's four-sketch account. Inland teaching settlement T has no package budget. Offer oral shaking
alternatives and record single causal links, never three workshop dry-floor drawings.

Print cards, causal strips, shaking log and Shake fallback S. Adult sets two same-base models differing
only in braces, equal nonbreakable loads too large to swallow, tape, nested trays, four contained latex-
free balls, ruler and impact eye protection. Check latex allergies beforehand. Pretest small motions;
record actual whole-centimeter endpoints at the ruler's one-centimeter step, never earthquake magnitude.
Adult alone moves tray, faces back, eye protection during setup/work/cleanup. Jam or escape: one safe
reset, then stop practical work and use S, announced invented. No flying load is acceptable.

Six groups of five visit four minutes each, 24 minutes. All keep logs; no individual apparatus queue.
S has six matched pairs: braced models stand six gentler/five stronger; unbraced six/two. Record
interpretation is not observation skill. Actual tests promise no winner. Day 85 logs are unscored teaching
before portfolio. Five cleanup minutes: parallel wiping/bagging, adult containment and handwashing.

E82 C/B returns day 83, E83 F/C day 84, E84 D/F day 85, E85 E/D day 86. Two five-minute moves
maximum per child. Hear rotating six voluntary turns, 4.5 minutes total; others return paper.
Use record.md's component feedback scale. Keep held base-design recommendation hidden until day 85.
No personal disaster, household inspection or evacuation-route homework. Before class name teacher-only-exit-key.md;
keep it outside student print. Background U5-monitor, U5-alert, U5-divert, U5-flood, U5-safety.
