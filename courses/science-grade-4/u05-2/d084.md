# Day 84: Several ideas and a fair comparison

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, eight hazard research cards, cause and action strips, shaking log and Shake fallback S. Hide S outcomes today. Return day 83 annotations and both exit papers. Prepare one-item half slips; cover Exit boxes. Pretest tomorrow's station, check latex allergies, inspect loads and arrange eye protection.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only today. No personal routes or building inspections. Adult-only pretesting uses contained parts and impact eye protection.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 83's exit tickets, both half slip and paper Exit box, with source annotations. If many show error F, [[s05-P84-1]] with the mill repair card, five minutes. Unresolved error C uses [[s05-P84-2]] with teaching U, five minutes. Ready children revisit one card locator. Public talk may be passed.

#### 8 min. Teaching first

Sketch T: inland stream beside footpath, storehouse farther inland. Say invented for practice. Review criterion and limit from grade 3. Review day 24's constraint: a limit on design choices. Review design-cycle chart: define, sketch, build, test, compare.

**Ask.** [[s05-P84-3]]. Then model [[steps:s05-P84-3]]. Introduce channel, a water route; platform, raised support; inlet, where water enters; failure, the observed goal not met. Review day 51's condition: a test setting. Show separate water-route sketches of channel and platform, without solving any dry-floor task. One input can succeed while another fails; no workshop or package-cost table.

#### 12 min. Generate alternatives

Partners take simultaneous 45-second turns offering shaking alternatives, [[s05-P84-4]]. Each records one mechanism strip and a material/access question. These are proposals needing tests, never building instructions. Hear rotating six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes inside this block; collect everyone's strips. If ideas repeat, distinguish changing support connections from changing stored-object positions.

#### 10 min. Compare and prepare

Review comparison from day 49: hold a common criterion, seek matching evidence, check limits. Exchange one strip, [[s05-P84-5]], using a research-card detail. Rehearse separate two-condition reasoning without a recommendation-plus-next-test response. Label dry logs, [[s05-P84-6]]. Tomorrow's equal-base models differ only in braces; load/start match across both motions. Adult completes station inspection after class.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the riverside workshop record for the first time. Say invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X84-ideas]] on one-item half slip; [[s05-X84-limits]] in Exit box. Four minutes draw/label, one collect both. Do not propose solutions.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy today's texts and blank records beforehand; cover reserved work. Use the named invented fallback if safe observation is unavailable, noting missing practical evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy T and the log without printer/projector. Paper replaces strips; adult is partner. Use records instead of tomorrow's apparatus if protection is unavailable.

**Skip.** Public turns and class station preparation.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P84-1]] if needed.
- **8 min. Trace.** Child does [[s05-P84-3]]; adult then explains its key.
- **12 min. Propose.** Do [[s05-P84-4]], then [[s05-P84-5]].
  **Say.** "What observation checks your idea?" Expect a result tied to its benefit, under matching conditions.
- **5 min. Prepare.** Child labels [[s05-P84-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the workshop. Child answers [[s05-X84-ideas]] and [[s05-X84-limits]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, retaining repairs, evidence and exit. Extend by naming an untested condition.

### Words and supports

Review criterion, limit, constraint, design-cycle chart and condition. Channel redirects water; platform raises support; inlet admits water; failure means the goal was not met. Enlarge T; allow neutral scribing.

### Tomorrow

Return the student written task with descriptive notes. Practice feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Mark both exit responses: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Note language or missing evidence separately.

Return causal strips and both exit papers on day 85. Error D examines the teaching overflow diagram; unresolved error F restores the message-action chain, five minutes each. Then compare braced/unbraced models under both gentle motions.
