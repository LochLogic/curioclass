# Eight hazard research cards

## Shaking 1

Shaking can push a building sideways.
Bracing strengthens vulnerable walls against that movement.

Source: [bracing guidance, page 1, opening](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/how2012_brace_cripple_walls.pdf).

## Shaking 2

Braces must connect parts securely to support the building.
Our cardboard model cannot show whether a real building is safe.

Source: [bracing guidance, page 1, anchoring tips](https://www.fema.gov/sites/default/files/2020-07/how2012_brace_cripple_walls.pdf).

## Volcano 1

Scientists compare a volcano's new readings with its quieter readings.
Changes can help them judge possible eruption activity.

Source: [monitoring, background-measurement paragraph](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP/vhp-uses-monitoring-data-and-volcanic-history-forecast-eruptions).

## Volcano 2

Past eruptions and current monitoring help scientists consider possible outcomes.
A forecast has uncertainty; monitoring does not stop an eruption.

Source: [monitoring, history and final forecast paragraphs](https://www.usgs.gov/programs/VHP/vhp-uses-monitoring-data-and-volcanic-history-forecast-eruptions).

## Tsunami 1

A tsunami warning tells people to act.
Moving to a safe place can reduce exposure while waves continue.

Source: [alerts, Warning paragraph](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tsunami-alerts).

## Tsunami 2

A usable route leads outside the hazard zone, inland or to high ground.
Strong natural warnings may leave no time to wait for an official message.

Source: [preparation, Plan for Evacuation](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tsunami-before); [alerts, natural-warning paragraph](https://www.weather.gov/safety/tsunami-alerts).

## Flood 1

A diversion takes water from its usual route toward another route or storage.
It can reduce floodwater reaching an exposed place.

Source: [diversion guidance, definition and purpose](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/hmsdocs/hmstrm/diversion-modeling).

## Flood 2

Raising a building can reduce its exposure to floodwater.
Walls can fail or be overtopped; protection depends on conditions.

Source: [flood measures, Building Elevation and Ringwalls](https://www.hec.usace.army.mil/confluence/CHART/UserGuide/chart-sct-the-conceptual-coastal-risk-framework).
