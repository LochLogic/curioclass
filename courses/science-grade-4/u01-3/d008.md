# Day 8: What did both objects do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the Teacher-only key below; exclude it from child copies. Print each exit panel with its exact stem and response space. Prepare day 7's exit and motion-note feedback. Lay out Car record C, question board and class record book. Inspect toy cars, wooden blocks and contained tape floor lane. Prepare protection, wipes and storage bags. Cover the can/cork panel until the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Use day 7's gentle contained floor rolls. Everyone present wears impact eye protection through setup and cleanup, or use C. No throwing or tasting. Count objects; stop rolls for adult retrieval, broken containment or missing protection.

### The day

Practice stays in folders after feedback. Review one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Return day 7's exit and motion notes. If many show error F, spend five minutes naming a visible outcome with [[s01-Pr8]]. Error C gets five minutes following changed motion to its energy destination. Error J gets five minutes locating the block's contact push. More than ten misses triggers shared repair; others use feedback. Blank work gets a conference. Keep exit revision cards covered.

#### 15 min. Compare collisions

Three-minute protected setup; six two-minute trials, alternating slower and faster. Keep car, block, surface, block start and contact alignment fixed. Two handlers and four observers form today's rotating six. Every child completes [[s01-P8]]. Record actual approach speed and both motions. Retain failed rows; use separate C events if needed, saying invented for practice. Push lines alone cannot establish speed. No supplied wrong prediction/model pair.

#### 5 min. Update from evidence

**Ask.** [[s01-Pu8]]

Review grade 3: revise the record in a second color, keeping original observations and initials. Partners take two simultaneous 45-second turns. Teacher hears the next six, 30 seconds each, within three minutes; continue yesterday's roster. Allow public passing or private response. Update class energy destinations from both motions. Collect records for day 9.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop rolls; count and pack cars and blocks. Clear lane. Wipe and bag protection. Keep records, including unclear rows.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal panel now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X8]] exactly. Children revise alone on paper. Mark after class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes; records take 30 times 15 seconds, seven and a half minutes. Return both day 9 using the same scale.

### Other rooms

No kit or protection: read C one event at a time, predicting before each reveal. No printer: copy blank log and keep exit panel covered. A scribe records the child's choices.

### At home

**Swap.** No kit or protection: use C, invented for practice. No second color: add a star. No printer: copy six log rows; hide panel.

**Skip.** Roster and live trials.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback and [[s01-Pr8]].
- **12 min. Compare.** Reveal C events separately; do [[s01-P8]]. Predict before each reveal.
- **8 min. Update.** Do [[s01-Pu8]], keeping first marks.
  **Say.** "What changed in each object's motion?" Expect: in C, car slows and block starts moving.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal panel; [[s01-X8]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: omit public reports, retaining six records, evidence note and exit. Longer: explain why changing the block weakens the comparison.

### Words and supports

Review: prediction, observation, contact, transfer, destination, motion energy. Read logs aloud; describe both motions. Offer a scribe and high-contrast added marks.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 returns day 8's exit and records. Error C needs a receiving destination, five minutes. Error I needs observed and expected separated, five minutes. Then record chronological listening events alongside motion. Carry the heard roster forward.
