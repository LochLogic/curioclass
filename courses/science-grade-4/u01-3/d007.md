# Day 7: Questions we can test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the Teacher-only key below; exclude it from child copies. Print each exit panel with its exact stem and response space. Prepare day 6's exit and matching-page feedback. Pretest car wheels, smooth blocks and contained floor lane. Lay out protection, wipes, storage bags and Ramp record R. Copy C on side 2; cover the exit panel.

### Safety and set-up

Gentle floor rolls only. Everyone present wears impact eye protection through setup and cleanup; otherwise use C. Keep faces back and observers outside the lane. No throwing or tasting; count objects. Stop rolls for adult retrieval, broken containment or protection.

### The day

Review one class record book scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Other practice stays in folders.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 6's exit and matching pages. If many show error A, use [[s01-Pr7]] and retained marble records for five minutes. Error B gets five minutes labeling travel. Use day 1's R, invented for practice, if earlier records fail. Others act on feedback. More than ten misses is many; blank work gets a conference. Carry day 6's destination question on the question board, a review routine.

#### 5 min. Review contact pushes

Review grade 3's force arrow, balanced, unbalanced and cancel with [[s01-K7]]. Ask first, then show [[steps:s01-K7]]. Trace for misses. Equal pushes mean no motion change, not necessarily rest.

#### 5 min. Contact and energy

**Ask.** [[s01-Ke7]]

[[steps:s01-Ke7]]

Review day 5's transfer and day 4's motion energy. Start labeled class energy arrows separately from force arrows. No energy quantities.

#### 10 min. Question clinic

Pairs sort [[s01-Pq7]]; reveal its key afterward. Review grade 3's testable-question routine. Hear six children, 45 seconds each, within five minutes; public passing is allowed. Record names for tomorrow's rotating six. Separately, each partner predicts block motion orally, two simultaneous 45-second turns. No combined question/prediction/arrow practice card.

#### 5 min. One collision

Two-minute protected setup; three-minute car/block trial. Two handlers and four observers form today's rotating six. Everyone completes [[s01-Po7]]. Observe approach speed directly, not from push lines. Retain unclear rows; use one separate C event if needed, saying invented for practice. Collect motion notes for day 8.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop rolls; count and pack cars and blocks. Clear lane; wipe and bag protection. Keep records.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal panel now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X7]] exactly. Children answer alone on paper. Mark after class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes. Motion notes take 30 times 15 seconds, seven and a half minutes. Return both day 8 using the same scale.

### Other rooms

No kit or protection: read one C event. No printer: copy page and separate covered exit panel. Describe positions; a scribe records the child's ideas.

### At home

**Swap.** No kit or protection: use C, invented for practice. No class records: use day 1's R. No printer: copy tasks; hide the exit panel.

**Skip.** Roster turns and live rolling.

- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s01-Pr7]].
- **8 min. Arrows.** Do [[s01-K7]] and [[s01-Ke7]].
  **Say.** "Where does the push arrow start?" Expect: on the thing pushed.
- **7 min. Questions.** Sort [[s01-Pq7]] together.
- **5 min. Observe.** Read one C event; do [[s01-Po7]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the panel; [[s01-X7]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, retaining individual observations and exit. Longer: explain why a push arrow cannot stand for an energy destination.

### Words and supports

Review: predict, testable, force arrow, balanced, unbalanced, cancel. New: contact, destination, energy arrow. Read aloud; offer tactile arrows or private talk.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 returns day 7's exit and motion notes in fifteen-minute Return and repair. Error F needs an observable outcome; error C needs a destination; error J needs a push on its receiver. Five minutes each if many miss. Then compare repeated collisions.
