# Teacher-only answers, section 1.3

Teacher-only answer key, continued.

## s01-X9

Prompt: Invented for practice. The same wheel hits the same box under matching conditions. Listeners stay in the same place. Event order: slower quiet; faster loud; slower quiet; faster loud; slower quiet; faster loud. Record sound tallies for each speed.

Expected response: Slower: three quiet, zero loud. Faster: zero quiet, three loud.

Look for: Slower row: three quiet, zero loud.; Faster row: zero quiet, three loud.; A merged or incomplete tally is a recording gap, without an energy misconception letter.

A merged or incomplete tally is a recording gap; give descriptive feedback.

## s01-Pr10

Prompt: Name an energy destination besides sound from a retained car trial.

Expected response: Block motion or remaining car motion, as supported by the retained record.

Look for: Name a motion destination besides sound.

Expected wrong responses: All collision energy became sound. (D); The same car has equal energy at both speeds. (A).

## s01-K10

Prompt: Sort the supplied details by observation or scientific account.

Expected response: Motion and sound come from observations or labeled reports. Slight warming comes from the account, not measured temperatures.

Look for: Identify information sources.; Collisions can warm objects and surrounding air.; Our records contain no temperatures; no reading does not prove no warming.

Expected wrong responses: We did not feel warmth, so warming cannot happen. (E).

## s01-Pa10

Prompt: Attach each destination label beside its information source on the class chart.

Expected response: Block motion and sound attach to observation or observer report; slight warming to account. Keep remaining car motion energy.

Look for: Connect initial motion energy to destinations, without equal-size claims.; Motion and sound use records.; Warming uses account, not a measured temperature.

Expected wrong responses: Energy disappears when the car slows. (C).

## s01-Ps10

Prompt: Sort labels A, B and C under observation or scientific account.

Expected response: A and B: observation or observer report. C: scientific account; no class temperature measurement establishes warming.

Look for: A block motion: observed record.; B contact sound: heard or observer report.; C slight warming: scientific account.; Account does not replace unclear observations.

Expected wrong responses: Warming is impossible because hands did not detect it. (E).

## s01-X10

Prompt: Invented for practice. A sphere slows but keeps moving after striking a paper cone. The cone moves; a click sounds. The supplied after-drawing shows only remaining sphere motion. Background account: collisions can slightly warm objects and nearby air. This setup cannot detect that tiny warming. Revise the sphere's before-and-after energy drawing using the observations and background card.

Expected response: Before: sphere motion energy. After: remaining sphere motion energy and energy passed to cone motion, sound and slight warming of objects and air. Motion and sound are observed; warming comes from the account.

Look for: Retain motion energy in the slowed sphere that continues moving.; Connect initial energy to cone motion, sound and slight warming.; Distinguish observed destinations from account-based warming.

Expected wrong responses: Energy disappeared when the sphere slowed. (C); All energy became the click. (D); No warmth was felt, so no warming happened. (E).
