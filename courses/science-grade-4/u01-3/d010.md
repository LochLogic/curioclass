# Day 10: Follow every energy destination

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and both teacher-only key pieces. Prepare day 9 feedback, warming-card.md and destination-labels.md. Retain the energy chart and a listening log; use car-record-c.md separately if needed. Keep the shared sphere exit covered. Warming comes from the account; no temperature reading or live collision occurs. Use the class record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No live collisions, heating or touching to test warmth. Point to labels without cutting.

### The day

Use one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Collect every page for day 11 feedback, then keep it in the folder.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 9's exit and full page. Give descriptive tally feedback; use work evidence for energy repairs. If many show error D, spend five minutes using [[s01-Pr10]] to keep block motion beside sound. Error A gets five minutes comparing starting motion energy at different speeds. More than ten misses triggers shared repair; others use feedback. Blank work gets a conference.

#### 10 min. Scientific account

Read the warming-card.md account reproduced on side 1 aloud beside a day 9 log. Without a readable log, use C, explicitly invented for practice. The account remains scientific background, separate from invented observations. Use the destination details below as the supplied details.

**Ask.** [[s01-K10]]

Then show [[steps:s01-K10]]. Review grade 3's observation and inference: what we notice and what we work out. Warming here comes from the scientific account. Motion and sound records do not measure temperature. No touching to test warmth, deliberate heating or invented temperature numbers.

#### 5 min. Destination annotations

Use the destination-labels.md menu reproduced on side 1.

**Ask.** [[s01-Pa10]]

Children point labels to class energy arrows and information sources. Review day 7's energy arrows. Give feedback from the teacher-only key. No device is needed; a simulation could not establish warming.

#### 15 min. Sort evidence sources

Children sort [[s01-Ps10]] alone, then explain in pairs, one 45-second turn each. Hear the next six, 45 seconds each, within five minutes; allow public passing or private response. Record names; day 11 reaches the remaining six. Collect everyone's source choices for day 11 feedback.

Annotate the class chart with observation or account labels. Retain remaining car motion energy when it still moves. Arrows show destinations, not equal shares. If many deny warming, contrast missing measurements with the account, without claiming warmth was felt.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal panel only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X10]] exactly. Children revise alone on paper. Mark after class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes. All page responses take 30 times 20 seconds, ten minutes. Give one written next step; return full pages with tickets day 11.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. No printer: copy account and labels once; keep exit panel separate. Describe drawing for a scribe; child supplies destination choices.

### At home

**Swap.** No class log: use C, invented for practice. No printer: copy account and labels; hide exit panel. No chart: write car motion energy and add labeled arrows.

**Skip.** Public turns.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback and [[s01-Pr10]].
- **8 min. Read.** Read account; do [[s01-K10]].
  **Say.** "Which destination comes from the account?" Expect: slight warming, not a temperature reading we made.
- **5 min. Labels.** Do [[s01-Pa10]] together.
- **7 min. Sort.** Do [[s01-Ps10]]; point to supporting sources.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal panel; [[s01-X10]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, retaining individual choices and exit. Longer: explain why arrow lengths cannot assign energy amounts from these records.

### Words and supports

Review: observation, inference, motion energy, transfer, destination. Distinguish observed and explained by account. Read aloud; offer tactile arrows or a scribe. No hearing or family disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 returns day 10's exit and source choices in fifteen-minute Return and repair. Error C needs destinations, error D needs motion beside sound, error E needs detection limits, five minutes each if many miss. Test a new slow car collision, then hear the remaining six during evidence notes.
