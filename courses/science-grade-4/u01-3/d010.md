# Day 10: Follow every energy destination

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the Teacher-only key below; exclude it from child copies. Print each exit panel with its exact stem and response space. Prepare day 9's exit and log feedback, class energy-arrow chart, class record book and Car record C. Copy scientific account and destination labels on side 1. Keep sphere/cone panel covered. No thermal equipment or live rolling today.

### The day

Review one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Other practice stays in folders.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 9's exit and logs. If many show error D, spend five minutes using [[s01-Pr10]] to keep block motion beside sound. Error A gets five minutes comparing starting motion energy at different speeds. More than ten misses triggers shared repair; others use feedback. Blank work gets a conference.

#### 10 min. Scientific account

Read the account aloud beside a day 9 log. If unavailable, use C, explicitly invented for practice.

**Ask.** [[s01-K10]]

Then show [[steps:s01-K10]]. Review grade 3's observation and inference: what we notice and what we work out. Warming here comes from the scientific account. Motion and sound records do not measure temperature. No touching to test warmth, deliberate heating or invented temperature numbers.

#### 5 min. Destination annotations

Destination annotations is a planned light tool, not built; use paper labels for these five minutes. Children point labels to class energy arrows and their information sources using [[s01-Pa10]]. Review day 7's labeled energy arrows. Give feedback from the teacher-only key. Model output could not establish warming either.

#### 15 min. Sort evidence sources

Children sort [[s01-Ps10]] alone, then explain in pairs: two simultaneous 45-second turns. Hear the next six, 45 seconds each, within five minutes; allow public passing or private response. Record names; day 11 reaches the remaining six. Collect everyone's source choices for day 11 feedback.

Annotate class chart with observation or account labels, without a before-and-after revision card. Retain remaining car motion energy when it still moves. Arrows show destinations, not equal shares. If many deny warming, contrast missing measurements with the account, without claiming warmth was felt.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal panel only now. Say invented for practice; read [[s01-X10]] exactly. Children revise alone on paper. Mark after class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes. Source choices take 30 times 15 seconds, seven and a half minutes. Return both day 11 using the same scale.

### Other rooms

No device is needed. No printer: copy account and labels once; keep exit panel separate. Describe drawing for a scribe; child supplies destination choices.

### At home

**Swap.** No class log: use C, invented for practice. No printer: copy account and labels; hide exit panel. No chart: write car motion energy and add labeled arrows.

**Skip.** Public turns and planned tool.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback and [[s01-Pr10]].
- **8 min. Read.** Read account; do [[s01-K10]].
  **Say.** "Which destination comes from the account?" Expect: slight warming, not a temperature reading we made.
- **5 min. Labels.** Do [[s01-Pa10]] together.
- **7 min. Sort.** Do [[s01-Ps10]]; point to supporting sources.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal panel; [[s01-X10]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, retaining individual choices and exit. Longer: explain why arrow lengths cannot assign energy amounts from these records.

### Words and supports

Review: observation, inference, motion energy, transfer, destination. Distinguish observed and explained by account. Read aloud; offer tactile arrows or a scribe. No hearing or family disclosures.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 returns day 10's exit and source choices in fifteen-minute Return and repair. Error C needs destinations, error D needs motion beside sound, error E needs detection limits, five minutes each if many miss. Hear the remaining six, then test a new slow car collision.
