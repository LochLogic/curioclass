# Day 9: Listen and record honestly

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the linked opener and both teacher-only key pieces. Prepare day 8 feedback and listening-log.md. Set a fixed safe listening place and name the reporting observer. Pretest cars, blocks and containment; set room-wide protection and six cleaning places. Keep car-record-c.md separate from actual records. Keep the shared wheel exit covered. Use the class record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

Gentle floor rolls; no throwing or tasting. Everyone present wears impact eye protection through setup and cleanup, or use C. Count objects. Stop for broken containment or protection; adult retrieves after rolls stop. Nobody moves closer to hear.

### The day

Review one record scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Other practice stays in folders.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 8's exit and records. If many show error C, use [[s01-Pr9]] for five minutes following energy to changed motion. Error I gets five minutes separating observed from expected. More than ten misses triggers shared repair; others act on feedback. Blank work gets a conference.

#### 5 min. Listening record

Review collision sound noticed earlier; extend it into a chronological event log.

**Ask.** [[s01-K9]]

Then show [[steps:s01-K9]]. A tick counts an event, not sound strength. Model unknown speed because none was supplied. Compare quiet/loud from the same safe place; unclear stays unclear.

#### 20 min. Listen and observe

Three-minute protected setup; six two-minute trials, alternating slower and faster. Today's rotating six take two handler and four observer roles. Keep car, block, surface, block start and alignment fixed. Every child completes [[s01-P9]] on listening-log.md or its matching page section. Observe approach speed directly. Retain failed rows; use separate C events if needed, saying invented for practice. Indistinguishable sound is unclear. Hearing access uses the named observer's report, labeled reported.

Use the final five minutes for [[s01-Pc9]]. Hear the next six, 45 seconds each, within four and a half minutes. Others talk in pairs, two simultaneous 45-second turns. Allow public passing or private response. Review speed-energy and transfer from days 4 and 5. No grouped tally/explanation practice card. Collect every log for day 10.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Stop rolls. Count and pack cars and blocks; clear lane. Wipe and bag protection. Keep unclear records. Six captains each clean five student pairs; the adult cleans their own. Use two minutes to pack and three to clean, following wipe directions.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal panel now; say invented for practice. Read [[s01-X9]] exactly. All thirty answer alone on paper. Mark after class: 30 times 30 seconds, fifteen minutes. Logs take 30 times 15 seconds, seven and a half minutes. Return both day 10 using the same scale.

### Other rooms

No kit or protection: reveal C events chronologically. No printer: copy blank log and separate covered exit panel. Read sound reports without imitating louder impacts. Offer a scribe.

### At home

**Swap.** No kit or protection: use C, invented for practice. No printer: copy six rows; hide panel. No sound access: read observer reports.

**Skip.** Public turns and live rolling.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback and [[s01-Pr9]].
- **5 min. Log.** Do [[s01-K9]] together.
- **12 min. Observe.** Read C events separately; complete [[s01-P9]].
- **3 min. Connect.** Discuss [[s01-Pc9]].
  **Say.** "Does sound tell the whole energy story?" Expect: no, the block moves too.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal panel; [[s01-X9]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, retaining six event entries and exit. Longer: explain why unclear sound cannot support a quiet/loud comparison.

### Words and supports

Review: motion energy, transfer, observation, prediction, repeated trials. Event tick means one contact. Read observer reports; never require hearing disclosures. Describe both motions.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 returns day 9's exit and logs. Error D needs block motion alongside sound, five minutes. Error A needs the same-object speed-energy link, five minutes. Then distinguish observations from the warming account. Carry the heard roster forward.
