# A collision account

Scientific account.

Collisions can pass energy into another object's motion.
Some energy can also reach sound and warm objects and nearby air slightly.
We cannot measure that tiny warming with our classroom setup.
No temperature reading was taken.

This account explains possibilities; it is separate from the recorded observations.
