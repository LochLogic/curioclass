# Day 67: Layers hold earlier environments

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare six clay sets with three distinguishable bands and two large buttons each. Mark buttons as stand-ins, not fossils. Print integrated fields from layer-sheet.md and the environment-cards.md record. Keep exit-67.md sealed. Print teacher-key.md for exit look-fors and use the generated answer key for practice. Prepare day 66 exit feedback.

### Safety and set-up

Check clay ingredients and allergies beforehand. Six groups of five share blunt slicers; cut away from hands. Count tokens out and back. Nothing is tasted. Stagger handwashing during final group work minutes; five Cleanup minutes finish washing and storage. Use paper if washing cannot fit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 66's s04-X66-model and s04-X66-question. If many show error B, point to changed rock features already recorded. For error x, help locate evidence a layer question could use. Honor yesterday's layer question. Review fossil from grade 3: what is left of a living thing from long ago, kept in rock. Children answer [[s04-P67-retrieve]]. Marine means sea-living here; an embedded marine shell suggests sea conditions, not an exact age.

#### 15 min. Build layers

Review layer, older, younger and undisturbed from grade 3: layers laid one on another, without later movement. Review day 59's clay model limit: clay is not rock.

Six groups place plain lower clay, button-bearing middle clay, then plain upper clay. Buttons represent unspecified finds, without environment claims. Each child presses one band edge or places a token, about 30 seconds each within five group minutes. Spend five minutes recording construction and five tracing exposed edges, [[s04-P67-build]]. Each child draws the sequence. First placed is oldest in this undisturbed model. Construction minutes cannot date rock.

#### 15 min. Environment cards

Say aloud: the environment-cards.md record is invented for practice. Review inferred from day 66: proposed from evidence rather than directly observed. Children complete [[s04-P67-cards]] using the single printed table: five minutes reading, five linking evidence, five comparing with a partner. Review paired evidence pointing from day 61; partners each have 45 seconds simultaneously. Public talk may be passed. Roots in place support earlier land; marine fossils above support later sea. Error G needs fossil positions, not color. Keep the exit's unknown interval hidden.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Count buttons back, store intact models for day 68, wipe tables and finish staggered washing. Children file individual drawings.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X67]] on the half slip, alone. Allow all five minutes for its labeled history model. Collect slips and practice. After class: 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, marking exits; 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes, practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return everything day 68 in Return.

### Other rooms

No clay: draw the same three construction stages, labeled model record. No printer: copy the table and fields; reveal the exact exit stem only at Exit.

### At home

**Swap.** Replace clay with stacked paper strips and large drawn token marks. Copy the table without a printer. Adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Class reports and paper distribution.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 66 feedback and complete [[s04-P67-retrieve]].
- **10 min. Build layers.** Complete [[s04-P67-build]] and keep the construction drawing.
- **10 min. Environment cards.** Read the invented table and complete [[s04-P67-cards]].
  **Say.** "Use land-to-sea cards. Link environment claims to layer evidence." Expect lower rooted plants before higher marine shells, without an exact date.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Store layers and wash after clay handling.
- **5 min. Exit.** Reveal [[s04-X67]]. Child draws alone before reading the answer together.

### Shorter or longer

Cut public reports, preserving construction records and Exit. Extend the same model's material limits.

### Words and supports

Review evidence from day 66. Accept labeled drawings, dictation and home-language reasoning. Read directions neutrally.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 bends and shifts saved models, then traces a real photograph. Return today's papers in Return. Error x on an assumed middle environment needs its unsupported claim crossed out, without filling the gap. Preserve originals.
