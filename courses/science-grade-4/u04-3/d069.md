# Day 69: Compare evidence-supported histories

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare feedback on day 68 papers. Integrate canyon-cards.md, source-notes.md fields and history-practice.md into the student page; history-practice.svg enlarges the same practice record only. Keep exit-69.md concealed. Print teacher-key.md for exit look-fors and the generated answer key for practice. Keep future assessment records sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Paper work only; no wet equipment. Put cards in labeled folders beforehand. Final collection fits within Exit. No child supplies a personal family or disaster story.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 68's s04-X68, s04-P68-model and s04-P68-photo. If many show error F, use their feedback to follow one labeled band through its changed position. Do not use screen height alone. Review day 30's two-column source notes: source statement and source location. Their inference stays separate.

#### 20 min. Research accounts

Review day 57's Grand Canyon anchor and day 66's revised explanation. Review evidence and inferred from day 66. Children read the single canyon-cards.md text for six minutes and keep attributed notes for six, [[s04-P69-notes]]. Card A supports river cutting over long time; Card B identifies different earlier environments; Card C gives an evidence method. Sources distinguish real canyon facts from tomorrow's or today's invented cases.

Spend eight minutes connecting notes to competing explanations in history-practice.md, without seeing Exit. Review fossil, layer and undisturbed from day 67. Review lake from grade 2: water collected in a hollow. Review weathering, erosion and gravity from day 66. Science assumes natural processes operated consistently in the past. Cutting requires material already present; a cut crossing an older feature provides relative-order evidence. Earth is very old; a tray or layer drawing does not date it.

#### 15 min. Independent history

Say the history-practice.md record is invented for practice. Children independently complete [[s04-P69-accounts]] for ten minutes. Then partners each take 45 seconds simultaneously, reviewing day 61's evidence pointing; use the remaining time to annotate uncertainty. Public talk may be passed.

The lower lake evidence supports water before upper rooted land plants. The repeated order and fossils at the distant hill support a tentative link; they do not guarantee it. The groove postdates the upper rock it cuts. Error G needs converging evidence rather than color alone. Keep the exit's break-and-channel accounts hidden.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X69]] on the half slip. Children choose independently, with an evidence link and timing limit. Collect slips and practice. After class: 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes, exit marking; 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Return all papers day 70 in Return.

### Side 1

I can compare landscape accounts and identify supporting evidence.

**Canyon evidence cards**

Card A: The Grand Canyon has layered walls. The Colorado River cut into older rock. Weathering and gravity also contribute to landscape change. These changes took a very long time.

Card B: Some canyon layers contain marine fossils. Other layers hold traces of land life. Embedded fossils provide evidence about earlier environments. A loose surface object gives different evidence.

Card C: Matching sequences and fossils can link separated exposures. Color alone is weak evidence. Later cuts can expose older layers. Keep uncertainty where evidence is incomplete.

Sources: U4-canyon, U4-fossils, U4-order, U4-cut, U4-match.
