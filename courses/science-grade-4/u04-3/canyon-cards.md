# Canyon evidence cards

## Card A: walls and removal

The Grand Canyon has layered walls. The Colorado River cut into older rock. Weathering and gravity also contribute to landscape change. These are changes over a very long time.

## Card B: environments

Some canyon layers contain marine fossils. Others contain traces of land life. Fossil evidence helps explain earlier environments. A loose object on today's surface is different from a fossil within its layer.

## Card C: using evidence

Matching layer sequences and fossils can support a link between separated exposures. Color alone is weak evidence. Later cutting can expose earlier layers. A model should retain uncertainty where the evidence is incomplete.
