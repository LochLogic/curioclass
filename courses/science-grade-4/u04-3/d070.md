# Day 70: Join the canyon evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 69 feedback and separate day 65 and 66 practice feedback. Reopen canyon-photo.jpg and retained dated observations. Display the five representations: cause-and-effect arrows, pattern chart, data graph, scale explanation and stability/change model. Print consensus-sheet.md fields once. Conceal exit-70.md. Keep teacher-key.md and generated practice answers private.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and photograph work only. Arrange originals beside revision fields before class. Final collection and clearing fit inside Exit. Use supplied landscape records, never children's personal disaster experiences.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Return day 69's s04-X69, s04-P69-notes and s04-P69-accounts. If many show error H, feedback points to a cut through already-existing material. Error x on duration needs unknown time stated, without adding a date. Review day 57's question board: notices become questions. Reopen its remaining layer question.

#### 20 min. Consensus

Review consensus from day 13: a shared explanation supported by evidence. Review day 66's weathering, erosion, gravity, evidence and inferred. Review slow change from grade 2: land changes a little at a time. Review fossil and undisturbed layer order from day 67. Review day 69's two-column source notes, keeping statements beside locations.

Use [[s04-P70-consensus]]: six minutes for each child to attach one evidence link; eight to join the five representations; six to retain an uncertainty. Review day 62's bar graph and day 57's cause-and-effect arrows. Keep actual measurements distinct from invented records. Water carried grains in trays; weathering breaks material and gravity can move it. Matching walls and ordered fossils help reconstruct earlier landscapes. Reopen canyon-cards.md. Source U4-canyon supports river carving and Earth's great age; a tray cannot establish a solid-rock canyon's cutting rate.

Review paired evidence pointing from day 61: each partner has 45 seconds simultaneously. Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each within the final six minutes; others leave paper links. Public sharing may be passed. Agreement follows evidence, not a vote. Do not introduce the exit's abandoned upper channel.

#### 15 min. Feedback revision

Return day 65's s04-P65-retrieve and s04-P65-measure for seven minutes. Children annotate their own evidence links without changing readings, [[s04-P70-feedback]]. Measurement gaps use the original start/end marks; error C needs maintained conditions, error D an organism-material link.

Use eight minutes for day 66's s04-P66-revise and s04-P66-records. Evidence must match its particular record. Error A separates breaking from travel; error B marks visible change; error E links water to plant condition. Preserve originals beside revisions.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reveal [[s04-X70]] on the half slip. Children independently compare the claims. Collect all pages. After class: 30 times 45 seconds, 22.5 minutes, exit marking; 30 times 35 seconds, 17.5 minutes, practice feedback. Use record.md: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. All today's work returns day 73 in Targeted repair; do not interrupt independent assessment days.

### Other rooms

No display: use printed canyon-photo.jpg and paper models. No printer: copy fields, then uncover the exact exit stem at Exit. Retained originals supply the graph and source notes.

### At home

**Swap.** Use the printed photograph and retained papers without a display. Copy fields without a printer. Adult takes the partner role.

**Skip.** Class reports and paper distribution.

- **5 min. Return.** Read day 69 feedback.
- **15 min. Consensus.** Complete [[s04-P70-consensus]] using earlier evidence.
  **Say.** "Attach evidence to a shared canyon explanation and retain one unsettled question." Expect a dated observation or identified source supporting a claim, plus an unknown exact duration.
- **10 min. Feedback revision.** Complete [[s04-P70-feedback]]. Divide time between day 65 and day 66 records; preserve measurements.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s04-X70]]. Child answers alone before discussing the answer.

### Shorter or longer

Cut public reports; protect both feedback allocations and Exit. Extend the same model's uncertain link with evidence that would change it.

### Words and supports

Review older and younger from day 67. Accept labeled models or dictation; evidence links matter more than length.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 independently checks a new erosion comparison and separate process records. Hold today's feedback until day 73: error H needs evidence against unchanged stream position; error x needs a justified limit. No assessment rehearsal or new practical round follows today.
