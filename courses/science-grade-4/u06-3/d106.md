# Day 106: Which observation belongs?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Return day 105's papers and notes. Prepare C1/D1 cards and complete reason strips. Keep the transport exit private. Have inside/outside view cards ready for separate repair. No plants or preparation equipment today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 105's exit, matches and practice: s06-X105, s06-P105, s06-P105-repair, s06-P105-reason, s06-P105-evidence and s06-P105-limit. If many day 105 papers show error F, spend five minutes on [[s06-P106-inside]]. Review day 104's internal role. For error H, spend five minutes on [[s06-P106-roots]], reviewing the missing-root limit from day 105. Keep these separate pointing tasks, without comparing transport records. No new content or written revision.

#### 15 min. Relevance rehearsal

Read the already-taught C1 and D1 cards, invented observations for practice. Review day 105's relevant and sufficient evidence. Individuals complete [[s06-P106]] by matching, one claim at a time. C1 concerns a grazer stopping; D1 concerns a wind-moved seed. A true observation may concern a different claim. If many reverse matches, cover one claim and reread only the observation bearing on it. Keep the paired internal-transport records unseen.

#### 10 min. Evidence matching

Individuals do [[s06-P106-reason]] with already-written reasons. They circle or point; no answer is drafted, revised or edited. Review grade 3's part-and-job reasoning: the evidence must connect the structure with the claimed job. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns reading their matches. Review the rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes within this part. Record names; everyone submits matching papers. Public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Oral confidence check

Use [[s06-P106-say]]. Each pair reads the same completed reasons and points to supporting evidence. These are rehearsed links, not a new explanation. Teacher checks unvisited pairs' paper matches. A moving seed supports dispersal, without showing that every seed grows. If matches remain uncertain, put each reason beside the observation it describes; do not supply the exit records.

#### 5 min. Exit E106

Reveal [[s06-X106]] for independent paper answers. Read neutrally; allow a short labeled explanation. The response identifies relevant inside-path evidence and states the missing-root limit. Error F explicitly denies inside transport. Error H claims roots were tested. Collect the exit, matches and reasons; use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing detail gets descriptive feedback.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: copy C1/D1 and complete reasons for pointing. Copy the transport exit onto covered paper; uncover only at the child's own turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Without printing, copy C1/D1 and the two complete reasons. Child points or circles.

**Skip.** Group rotation and repeated pair readings.

- **8 min. Return.** Use feedback with [[s06-P106-inside]] and [[s06-P106-roots]].
- **10 min. Match.** Child completes [[s06-P106]].
- **5 min. Reasons.** Do [[s06-P106-reason]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports protection?" Expect C1: the grazer stops at spines.
- **2 min. Read.** Do [[s06-P106-say]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X106]]; child answers independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated reason readings, keeping both repairs and the unseen exit. Longer: repeat [[s06-P106-say]] using the other already-written reason.

### Words and supports

Review relevant, sufficient, internal, transport and part/job. Read cards neutrally; allow pointing to complete reasons during rehearsal. Scribe the child's own exit explanation faithfully when needed.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets times 50 seconds, 25 minutes, on the same formative scale. Return all day 106 papers on day 107 in ten-minute Return and repair. Give errors F and H five minutes each using separate internal-role and missing-root contrasts. Then view dated inside sections and distinguish observation from supplied root information.
