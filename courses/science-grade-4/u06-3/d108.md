# Day 108: Flower parts and later seeds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Check pollen allergies before plants arrive; verify untreated white snapdragon identity. Adult dissects before class with splash goggles, blunt tweezers, cutting tool and covered tray; seal six sets. Use diagrams from the start if exposure, identity or preparation is unsuitable. No dye today. Keep teacher keys and exit private. Children never open, cut or taste.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return every day 107 paper, including dated observations, tags and links. If many day 107 tickets show error F, spend five minutes on [[s06-P108-inside]], reviewing useful internal paths. For error H, spend five minutes on [[s06-P108-roots]], reviewing missing roots. Keep contrasts separate; never relabel a supplied record observed. Reopen the question of how plants produce new young.

#### 15 min. Sealed parts

Allow three minutes for directions and six two-minute group views. Everyone begins diagram labels in [[s06-P108]] while waiting. Review grade 3's flower, fruit, seed; pollen is not a seed. Introduce petal, stamen, pistil and ovule using separate labels. Petals may attract visitors; stamens supply pollen. The pistil contains an ovary with ovules inside. Use the supplied enlarged diagram for hidden ovules, not an observed label. Children label only parts actually visible as observed; unclear stays unclear. Source U6-reproductive supports anatomy. Stop for damaged seals or discomfort; switch to diagrams.

#### 5 min. Event order

Read separate cards A, B and C. Explain pollination as pollen transfer to the pistil's top. Review grade 3's pollen-transfer setup. Fertilization names a further joining step inside the flower, needed for seed development. No cells or detailed mechanism. Pollen transfer does not guarantee fertilization. After fertilization, ovules can become seeds; the ovary develops into fruit. Children finish the A/B/C ordering in [[s06-P108]]. Do not model the exit's cooperation-and-insufficiency account.

#### 5 min. Fruit section

Show original paper section D and use [[s06-P108-fruit]]. Review grade 3's fruits holding seeds. D is an inside view of fruit at C, not a fourth event in reproduction. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns pointing out a seed. Public talk may be passed. All thirty return labels/order papers; teacher reads them after class. If many label pollen as a seed, separate a pollen label from the fruit's seed label.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult stores sealed containers, cleans tweezers, tray, cutting tool and goggles, wipes the table and washes hands. Children file paper views. No pupil handles plants or tools.

#### 5 min. Exit E108

Reveal [[s06-X108]]. Children independently explain interacting parts and the evidence limit on the half slip. Read neutrally. Error G assigns seed production to petals alone. Error F explicitly denies useful internal parts. Incomplete lists receive descriptive feedback. Collect all labels, sequences, seed labels and exit tickets. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No specimens: use original diagrams, marking anatomy supplied. No printer: draw separate labelled parts and copy cards, covering the exit until its own turn. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Use the original diagrams instead of flowers or cutting tools. Copy labels and cards without printing.

**Skip.** Group rotation and specimen preparation.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback with [[s06-P108-inside]] and [[s06-P108-roots]].
- **15 min. Parts.** Read the part table. Child labels the diagram and orders A/B/C in [[s06-P108]].
  **Say.** "Which part supplies pollen?" Expect the stamen. Pollen is not a seed.
- **5 min. Fruit.** Point to D; child does [[s06-P108-fruit]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X108]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated label discussion; keep anatomy, sequence, cleanup and independent exit. Longer: revisit the observed/supplied labels already requested in [[s06-P108]].

### Words and supports

Petal, stamen, pistil, ovary, ovule, pollination, fertilization. Review reproduce, pollen and seed. Enlarge separate labels; read neutrally and offer faithful scribing. Keep an individual explanation.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, on the same formative scale. Return all day 108 papers on day 109 in ten-minute Return and repair. Errors F/G get five minutes each: useful inside roles, then familiar pollen-to-seed sequence. Day 109 connects inside and outside animal structures with four life functions.
