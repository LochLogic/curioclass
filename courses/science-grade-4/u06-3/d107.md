# Day 107: Inside marks and supplied facts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Recheck allergies, untreated snapdragon identity and actual dye instructions; otherwise use diagrams. Adult cuts dated plain/dyed flower-stem sections before class with splash goggles and required gloves, then seals them. Prepare optional celery sections separately, labeled leaf stalk. Keep first records and unclear results. Reserve the exit. Children never open, cut or taste.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return all day 106 papers: s06-X106, s06-P106, s06-P106-inside, s06-P106-roots, s06-P106-reason and s06-P106-say. If many day 106 tickets show error F, spend five minutes on [[s06-P107-inside]]. Review the internal role taught day 104. For error H, spend five minutes on [[s06-P107-roots]], reviewing day 105's missing-root limit. Keep role and missing-part contrasts separate. Retrieve day 104's dated records for comparison.

#### 15 min. View inside

Allow three minutes for directions and six two-minute group views, twelve minutes total. Children record dates, conditions and colored/uncolored/unclear, then begin [[s06-P107]] on their table. Review day 104's observed/supplied tags. Inspect actual inside marks, comparing the dated plain and dyed flower sections. Celery, if present, is a leaf stalk, not stem evidence.

If color fails or specimens spoil, retain the actual unclear record. Read Plant P aloud as invented for practice; label it supplied. Color marks a possible water route, not water quantity. Stop for damaged seals, spills or discomfort; switch to drawings. No pupil handles specimen, dye or cutting equipment.

#### 5 min. Root source

Read source card U aloud; children finish [[s06-P107]] with a separate source tag. Review grade 1's roots taking in water and day 104's internal transport. Review grade 3's source distinction: a supplied fact differs from what this class observed. Source U6-vegetative supports the card. Preserve original marks; review grade 3's second-color revision for added labels only. Do not turn a supplied fallback into an observation.

#### 5 min. Single interaction

**Ask.** [[s06-P107-link]]. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns explaining one interaction from U, without a joint-source argument. Review the rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, and record names. Public talk may be passed. Everyone writes one short link for return, including children not heard. If many list unrelated parts, trace just one water-moving connection between two parts.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult stores sealed specimens, cleans equipment and goggles, wipes tables and washes hands. Children retain originals while filing their labeled records. No thirty-child washing queue is needed because pupils touched only paper.

#### 5 min. Exit E107

Reveal [[s06-X107]] only now. Children independently connect the two supplied sources and state the rootless limit on the half slip. Look for connected parts and honest provenance. Error F denies useful internal parts; error H treats a cutting as root evidence. Collect exit, tables, tags and link notes; use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Missing evidence receives descriptive feedback.

### Other rooms

No kit: use supplied views and invented Plant P with its label. No printer: copy dated spaces and U; keep the exit covered. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Use supplied diagrams and invented Plant P without a kit. Copy spaces without printing.

**Skip.** Group rotation and optional celery.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback with [[s06-P107-inside]] and [[s06-P107-roots]].
- **15 min. View.** Point to inside marks; child records [[s06-P107]]. Retain unclear; tag any fallback supplied.
- **5 min. Link.** Read U; child completes [[s06-P107-link]].
  **Say.** "What does U say passes toward leaves?" Expect water moving through inside stem paths.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X107]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated viewing talk, keeping each group's view and provenance labels. Keep cleanup and exit. Longer: revisit uncertain tags in [[s06-P107]], preserving first marks.

### Words and supports

Review transport, internal, source and observed/supplied. Enlarge inside views; offer neutral reading and faithful scribing. Keep the child's own explanation and first marks.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, on the same formative scale. Return every day 107 paper on day 108 in ten-minute Return and repair. Errors F/H get five minutes each on separate internal-role and missing-root contrasts. Then examine adult-prepared flower parts and learn their reproduction roles.
