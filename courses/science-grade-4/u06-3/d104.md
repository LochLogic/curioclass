# Day 104: A plant cannot walk to water

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Check pollen/celery allergies before plants arrive. Verify untreated white snapdragon identity and actual dye instructions; otherwise use diagrams. Adult prepares overnight pair and inside view with splash goggles and required gloves, then seals containers. Keep returned flag/signal records ready. Hide Side 2 and the exit. Print only named student portions of shared pieces, without teacher notes or source card U.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return all day 103 papers, including prediction, test, matches and notes. If many day 103 tickets show error D, use five minutes on [[s06-P104-repair]] with separate role cards. Review day 99's receiving, processing and action. For error E, use five minutes on [[s06-P104-memory]] with visible flag meanings and early/later signal counts. Review day 101's remembered meaning; counts show actions, not brains. Label Signal S invented if used. No child reenacts a mistake.

#### 5 min. Plant hook

Keep day 103's plant question: how can a plant get water without walking? Reveal the sealed plain-water and dyed-water pair. **Ask.** [[s06-P104]]. Children begin their dated table. Accept colored, uncolored or unclear. If color fails, retain unclear, then read Plant P aloud as invented for practice, supplied rather than observed. Review the question-board routine; leave explanations open.

#### 5 min. Grade 3 retrieval

Review grade 3's life cycle, flower, fruit and seed; pollen is not a seed. Individuals do [[s06-P104-cycle]]. Review grade 3's variation and part-and-job reasoning with [[s06-P104-jobs]]. Explain any missing link with a seed-to-seedling arrow or root/job match. Wider roots can help in that pictured place; they do not guarantee survival.

#### 12 min. Sealed comparison

One minute sets groups; six groups view for ninety seconds each, nine minutes total. Everyone completes [[s06-P104]] while waiting. Compare dates, water conditions and visible marks, without making a full plant pathway. Children never open, cut or taste. Stop for damaged seals, spills or discomfort; use supplied views. During the final two minutes, adult sets a fresh matched pair on the covered table with goggles, then seals it; children record conditions. Retain both dated pairs for day 107.

#### 3 min. Inside and outside

Introduce external as outside and internal as inside using separate views, [[s06-P104-label]]. Transport means movement through plant parts. Source U6-vegetative supports internal water paths; color is a marker, not water quantity. Do not complete an intact-plant water-path drawing for children. The first drawing belongs to their exit turn.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult stores containers, cleans equipment and goggles, wipes the table and washes hands. Children file comparison tables. No child handles dye, plants or cutting equipment.

#### 5 min. Exit E104

Uncover Side 2's [[s06-X104]] for three minutes. Then give [[s06-X104-question]] for two minutes. Read neutrally; children work alone. Keep first models and questions descriptive, without correctness marks. An unfinished arrow is not error F; note F only for an explicit denial of useful inside parts. Collect both pieces, labels and comparison tables.

### Other rooms

No kit: enlarge supplied views and announce invented Plant P. No printer: copy tables and prompts, keeping the intact-plant record covered until exit. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Without specimens, use supplied Plant P and the two views. Say it is invented. Copy tables without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and fresh-pair setup.

- **5 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s06-P104-memory]].
- **5 min. Review.** Do [[s06-P104-cycle]] and [[s06-P104-jobs]].
- **12 min. Observe.** Point to each view; child completes [[s06-P104]].
  **Say.** "What color did you see?" Expect a visible color or unclear, without an invented observation.
- **3 min. Label.** Do [[s06-P104-label]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X104]] in the Exit box, then [[s06-X104-question]]. Child works alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated table discussion; keep retrieval, safety and the independent model/question. Longer: revisit [[s06-P104-label]] beside the enlarged views.

### Words and supports

Internal, external, transport. Review roots, stems, leaves and part/job. Enlarge views; read neutrally and offer faithful scribing. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

After class allow 30 sets times 50 seconds, 25 minutes, for descriptive notes. Return every paper on day 105 in five-minute Return and repair. Error F receives a familiar inside/outside contrast. Then connect separate plant structures with their jobs, including growth and response.

### Credit

Ours. Sources U6-own, U6-vegetative and U6-snapdragon support original records and plant background.
