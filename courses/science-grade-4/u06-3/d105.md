# Day 105: Separate parts, useful jobs

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Prepare single-job plant cards and separate part/job strips using their labels. Keep composite exit cards private. Return day 104 tables, labels, first models and questions with descriptive notes. Use drawings only; no allergy exposure or sharp specimens today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return all day 104 papers. If many day 104 tickets state error F, use [[s06-P105-repair]] with the familiar separate inside/outside views. Review internal, external and transport from day 104. Incomplete first models receive questions, not an error label. Carry the plant-resource question forward to separate part/job links.

#### 12 min. Plant case reading

Read Cards C, T and D aloud, one at a time. Children complete those links in [[s06-P105]]. Review grade 3's part-and-job chart and its thorn protection idea. Review grade 3's structure and function: shape fits the job. Sharp spines can deter eating; thorns are different structures with a similar job. **Ask.** [[s06-P105-reason]]. Expect stored water to help during dry conditions; no guarantee. Review grade 3's flower, fruit and seed: moving an existing seed helps dispersal, not seed formation. Cite Sources U6-cactus, U6-thorn and U6-dandelion.

#### 8 min. Functions and sufficiency

Define survival as staying alive, growth as getting larger, and behavior as a response. Review grade 3's reproduce, making new individuals; call that function reproduction. Keep these as separate labels, not a four-function practice account.

Teach relevant evidence as information bearing on a claim; sufficient evidence is enough for that particular claim. Model [[s06-P105-evidence]] after children choose: stopping at spines supports protection, without proving universal safety. Then separately use [[s06-P105-limit]] with the rootless setup drawing. Missing roots prevent testing root uptake, error H. Never compare two transport records or construct the exit's four-function account.

#### 10 min. Single-job matches

Individuals finish [[s06-P105]], one card at a time. Review grade 1's roots taking in water and leaves facing light. Source U6-vegetative adds that leaves use light to make food; water and food support growth. Inside paths connect resources to other parts, review day 104. Match roots to water, leaves to light, thorns to protection and seed structures to travel separately. If many miss a job, point to its source sentence and retry only that match.

#### 5 min. Plant response

Read Card B and finish its [[s06-P105]] link. Teach bending toward light as a growth response, without an animal brain. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns explaining just that link. Review grade 3's rotating-six routine: hear six at 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes total, and record names. Public talk may be passed; all thirty submit paper. Source U6-bending supports the response.

#### 5 min. Exit E105

Reveal [[s06-X105]] for independent work. Read neutrally. Look for evidence-linked water functions, response and seed movement. Do not demand an internal label the stem does not ask for. Collect all practice and exit papers. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; incomplete links receive descriptive feedback. Error F is an explicit denial that internal parts can contribute.

### Other rooms

No device needed. No printer: copy the separate supplied facts and matching spaces; keep the composite exit on covered paper until its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the cards and matching spaces without a printer. Use drawings rather than plants.

**Skip.** Group strips and speaking rotation.

- **5 min. Return.** Review feedback; do [[s06-P105-repair]].
- **10 min. Read.** Read separate cards; child begins [[s06-P105]] and does [[s06-P105-reason]].
  **Say.** "How can stored water help?" Expect water available during dry weather, helping survival.
- **5 min. Evidence.** Do [[s06-P105-evidence]] and [[s06-P105-limit]].
- **5 min. Response.** Read Card B; finish its match in [[s06-P105]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X105]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated matching talk. Keep definitions, the absent-root limit and the unseen exit. Longer: reread Card B and repeat its separate match.

### Words and supports

Survival, growth, behavior, reproduction, relevant, sufficient. Review part/job and internal/external. Enlarge one card at a time; offer neutral reading or faithful scribing. Keep individual explanations.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes, using the same formative scale. Return all day 105 papers on day 106 in ten-minute Return and repair. Error F receives inside-role work; error H from setup practice receives the missing-root contrast, five minutes each. Then rehearse matching familiar claims to evidence.
