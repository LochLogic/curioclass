# Section 6.3: Plant parts work together

Days 104 to 108.

## Science and evidence

Evidence-based argument connects inside and outside plant structures as systems. Root uptake and transport are different claims. A rootless colored stem supplies no root observation. Color is a marker, not a quantity of water. Celery is a leaf stalk; use a flower stem for a stem claim. Introduce four life functions and sufficient evidence on 105 before 106 rehearsal. Stamens supply pollen; following fertilization, ovules may become seeds. Pollen is not a seed.

## Expected ideas and response

F excludes internal parts; G assigns seed production to petals alone; H claims uptake from a rootless cutting. D/E receive ten minutes on 104 before the plant hook. F returns on 105; F/H each receive five minutes on 106 to 108. F/G return on 109.

## Before class and equipment

Check pollen/celery allergies before bringing plants. Adult verifies untreated thorn-free white snapdragon identity and actual dye safety sheet. If either is unavailable, use diagrams. Adult alone cuts/prepares with splash goggles and any required gloves, then seals all containers. Children do not open, touch or taste specimens. Adult dissects before 108 with blunt tweezers/tray; seal six sets. Cleanup, wiping and adult handwashing take five minutes.

## Fallback and record

Print plant-record.md, plant-cards.md and flower-parts.md. Six two-minute views plus three setup minutes fit fifteen on 107/108; 104 uses six ninety-second views. Preserve unclear color, then announce invented Plant P. Optional reach uses an outside ruler with one-centimeter steps, nearest whole centimeter.

## Participation and access

Read labels and directions aloud without supplying the answer. Offer enlarged diagrams, tactile arrows, a scribe or home-language explanation. Public talk is optional. No home, pet, illness or body disclosures. Pairs take simultaneous forty-five-second turns. Teacher hears a rotating six, forty-five seconds each, within five minutes during work. Everyone else submits the paper record; the next named hand-back returns it. Use record.md to note who was heard. Initial ideas are descriptive. Other formative responses use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep first models and revise in a second color. Do not rank children.
