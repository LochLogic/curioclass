# Day 37: Independent source explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the private key for s02-E3. Prepare Day 37 independent source explanation with Day 37 held sources as one packet. Keep Single 28-point assessment record private. Conceal sources until their reading blocks. Keep day 36's papers and feedback filed until day 38. Arrange neutral read-aloud, scribing and enlarged-print access already used in class.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Day 36's assessment continues with a fresh source task, not feedback. Review the grade 3 assessment routine: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. Describe paper handling only. No apparatus today. Read the invented facility record neutrally, keeping it separate from factual sources.

#### 8 min. Source A

Reveal Source A on Day 37 held sources. Read it and its attribution aloud once without interpreting. Alone, children annotate source statements they might use in [[s02-E3]]. Do not supply causal connections, renewal labels or model arrows. The water-cycle context is information supplied by this source, not a prior-learning claim.

#### 8 min. Source B

Reveal Source B; read neutrally. Children annotate independently. Keep both sources available for writing. Answer word-pronunciation or direction questions without explaining the science. No partner turns or public sample answers.

#### 20 min. Explanation and model

Children independently complete [[s02-E3]], joining the sources with the invented facility record. Accept writing and a labeled drawing, or dictated decisions recorded by a scribe. The printed component labels identify the task, not the answers. Observe silently; record incomplete evidence for later feedback rather than repairing it now.

#### 4 min. Review

Children reread their own work and source attributions unaided. No key, partner checking or adult hints. They may finish their original response.

#### 2 min. Collection

Collect every packet privately. Retain it for written feedback and day 38 revision. Do not display answers or add points today.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy both exact sources and the data stem onto concealed board sections. Reveal each at its reading turn. Children draw and write on paper. One projector enlarges sources, never the private key. No device is required.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy both sources and the exact stem onto paper. No projector: point to the page. Keep the parent's answer view covered.

**Skip.** Class collection and public discussion.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Read each source aloud neutrally. The child annotates both, then completes [[s02-E3]] alone. Allow a labeled drawing and dictation without suggesting content. Identify the facility record aloud as invented for practice. Give no help or feedback today.
- **5 min. Collection.** Save the sources and original response together. Prepare private written feedback before day 38, without showing the key now.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: preserve both sources and independent response time; record unfinished work. Longer: child rereads the same evidence without a new task.

### Words and supports

Review: natural resource, renewable, generator, turbine, reservoir, habitat, source, and cause and effect. Read unfamiliar words neutrally. Judge science rather than spelling. No completed frame or example.

### Tomorrow

Return day 37's items and day 36's assessment in day 38 Read feedback. Route error E, renewable means harmless, and error F, one change removes all effects, to their distinct repair strips. Blank or unrelated work gets a conference. Use the shared 120-minute feedback allowance, thirty children times four minutes across both assessments. Final scoring follows day 38 revisions, not today's collection.
