# Day 40: Repair transport-fuel claims

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read Course check answer key. Print sources and drawings within the student page. Keep source-gap notes from day 38 private; they inform access arrangements, not hints. Cover earlier explanations and check answers. Prepare a private component record using day 39's same categories.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Receive day 38's source-gap note in this opening, informational only. Keep day 39's assessment sealed for day 41; no feedback or repair today. Explain that today's two sources are new; truck and comparison drawings are invented for practice. Read directions without examples. No family fuel use or jobs are discussed.

#### 35 min. Source and claim repairs

Students work alone. Eight minutes to read Source A and answer [[C-E40-origin]]; seven for [[C-E40-use]]. Ten to read Source B and answer [[C-E40-effects]]; ten for the before-and-after comparison and [[C-E40-change]]. Sources remain available. Read neutrally or scribe without content help. No interviews or scored public turns. Label source gaps for later rather than supply answers.

#### 7 min. Review and collect

Students check source labels silently for five minutes. Collect every page in two. Do not show answers. After class, allow twenty minutes for key components: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No total or point scale. Write a specific evidence gap beside each selected revision. More than ten of thirty misses selects priority within day 41's existing resource group, with no extra response period.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy both sources, drawings and four prompts onto board panels. Children work on two paper sides. No device is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Without a printer, copy sources, drawings and questions onto paper. Point at paper instead of a projector.

**Skip.** Class collection and private source-gap handoff.

- **40 min. Assessment claims.** Read both sources and the invented truck record neutrally. Your child answers [[C-E40-origin]], [[C-E40-use]], [[C-E40-effects]] and [[C-E40-change]] alone. Keep original writing.
- **5 min. Look together.** Save evidence notes for day 41.
  **Say.** "Does a second ship wall change the drilling site?" Expect: no; it addresses spill risk, while drilling may still disturb land.

### Shorter or longer

Keep both sources and all four responses. Early finishers check whether corrections have evidence.

### Words and supports

Review natural resource, renewable and nonrenewable. Access glosses: refinery, a place separating useful products from crude oil; hull, a ship's outer body. Neutral read-aloud and labeled drawings count.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 returns days 39 and 40 with written component feedback. Errors I/J concern origin and renewal; error K effects; error L overclaims one improvement. Use its resource group and preserve original work for revision. No portfolio rescore.

### Credit

Ours. Sources C40-A, C40-B and C40-use support the original source summaries.
