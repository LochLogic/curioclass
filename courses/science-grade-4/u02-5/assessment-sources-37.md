# Day 37 held sources

## Source A

Sunlight helps water cycle. Rain and snow replenish river flow. Flowing water turns a turbine connected to a generator. That generator supplies electricity.



Source: [Hydropower explained](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/hydropower/), water cycle and moving water. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.

## Source B

Dams can block fish traveling upstream. Reservoirs can cover natural habitats. Passages can help fish reach upstream breeding places.



Source: [Hydropower and the environment](https://www.eia.gov/energyexplained/hydropower/hydropower-and-the-environment.php), environmental impacts and fish passages. U.S. Energy Information Administration, public domain. Original summary; read 2026-11-23.
