# Day 38: Revise from evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read private keys and Private day 38 routing. Use current data for the generator key and missing-personal-evidence rule. Prepare Private end-of-unit feedback and Revise with evidence within today's page. Assign at most three five-minute letter strips. Select any feasible light, heat or current retry through pretest before class; no sound retry. Assemble archived teaching records and covered originals. Keep Single 28-point assessment record private and separate from the formative record.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback

Return day 36's assessment and portfolio, day 37's items, and day 35's s02-X35, s02-P35-1 and s02-P35-2. Review feedback-then-revision. Two minutes for transfer/design feedback, three for resources; day 35 notes sit beside these. Children read private component notes, not public scores. Blank work gets a conference, not a letter.

#### 15 min. Targeted repair

Review familiar representations only. Four parallel packet groups use paths A/B/L, design C/D, resources E/F/I/J/K, or observations G/H. Use only assigned strips, each five minutes. Without retry, teacher visits each group three minutes, twelve total; children continue independently meanwhile. If many show one error, prioritize that letter; many means more than ten of thirty.

Data tasks [[s02-P38-A]], [[s02-P38-B]], [[s02-P38-L]], [[s02-P38-C]], [[s02-P38-D]], [[s02-P38-E]], [[s02-P38-F]], [[s02-P38-I]], [[s02-P38-J]], [[s02-P38-K]], [[s02-P38-G]] and [[s02-P38-H]] match the selected strips. Error A traces crank input/output; error B both terminals; error L the single gap, using a drawing. Error C locates the criterion; error D matching tests. Error E separates renewal/effects; error F traces captured particles and remaining extraction. Error I sorts origins/carriers; error J compares formation/extraction; error K finds documented pollutant release. Error G uses earlier unobstructed separate-support/silence records; error H actual plain-patch readings. No held assessment configuration becomes a practice example. Strips stay in the folder.

Retry branch: teacher supervises one selected mode for five minutes, then five cleanup minutes. Other children use assigned paper moves without teacher visits during these ten minutes. Remaining five minutes permits one priority move and one three-minute group visit. Do not promise four visits too. Record unaided new observations separately; never replace original data. Unresolved groups get written prompts during revision.

#### 20 min. Revise both responses

**Ask.** [[s02-P38-1]]. Children work alone in second color: ten minutes transfer/device, ten resource explanation/model. Review keeping original observations and implementation initials. Feedback guides reasoning, not copied keys. Keep day 35's portfolio revision unchanged for its single score. Log unavailable evidence separately; coached reasoning never becomes an unaided observation.

#### 5 min. Reflect and hand off

Everyone completes the evidence note within [[s02-P38-1]]. Review rotating six: six 45-second readings fit five minutes; passing allowed. Listen while others write. Collect every revision. After class, thirty children times three minutes gives 90 minutes final marking. Use one 0/1 component scale on the 28-point record, with unavailable opportunities unscored and identified. Keep formative categories on their separate record.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy only assigned strips and response fields. Missing notes: read the relevant archived teaching source aloud. No apparatus: retain missing live evidence. A scribe preserves the child's reasoning.

### At home

**Swap.** No second color: border revisions. No apparatus: use paper moves; keep live gaps. No printer: copy one assigned strip and response fields.

**Skip.** Group visits and public reflections.

- **5 min. Read.** Return both assessments and day 35 notes privately.
- **10 min. Repair.** Choose two five-minute letter moves using familiar records.
  **Say.** "Which evidence supports your change?" Expect a specific record detail linked to the revised claim.
- **20 min. Revise.** Complete [[s02-P38-1]]. Preserve original observations. Divide time equally between transfer/device and resources.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Finish the evidence note. Keep unsupported or missing evidence labeled honestly. Save the revised response for day 39.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one priority repair, preserving both revisions. Longer: explain an unresolved evidence gap using the same records.

### Words and supports

Review: input, output, generator, circuit, terminal, criterion, limits, refinement, retest, natural resource, renewable, nonrenewable, pollutant, cause and effect. Use earlier notes rather than a completed answer frame.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 audits a fresh moving-toy report. Send day 36's unchanged observation record and return s02-P38-1 with a written evidence note in its opening, informational only. Send unresolved source-gap notes to day 40. No additional repair, interview or portfolio score is promised there.
