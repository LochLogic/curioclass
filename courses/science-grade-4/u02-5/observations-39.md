# Day 39 observation alternatives

TEACHER ONLY setup, read with station-setup.md. Same apparatus and controls, new arrangements. All watch from marked seats, no handling turns. Five comparisons, two minutes each including a written evidence jot. Pretest before class. Show every initial/final temperature reading, using an instrument with one-degree Celsius steps. Never report a value between marks. Adult removes the warming hand before contact and disconnects before circuit changes.

Sound: change the barrier position without touching either separate support. Use enclosed small paper pieces instead of rice. Compare gentle tapping and silence; no shared board or air blowing. Everyone wears existing goggles through setup, use and cleanup. Without enough goggles, use the invented record. Never increase loudness to obtain movement.

Light: pre-expose matching cloth regions from a common starting temperature, one under a triangular opaque mask. Measure marked regions with the same suitable surface probe before removing the mask. No sun viewing, lenses or hot lamps. A thermometer unsuitable for surface readings means use the supplied record. Keep unresolved actual readings; do not improve them.

Heat: palm-warm source spoon, remove hand, touch cooler spoon in a new side-by-side arrangement. Third matching spoon stays untouched. Record start/finish with the same probe at fixed contact positions. Use a clean dry cloth for handling; no hot water or tasting. Temperature stays below body temperature. If contact or resolution fails, preserve that note and use the supplied record.

Current: same battery, bulb and two series foil breaks, arranged as a rectangle. Compare neither, either one and both closed. Keep bulb in path. Disconnect before changes and at any wire warming; adult only, no outlets.

Generator: sealed crank light aimed away from eyes. Compare turning and stopping. Let stored light fade before repeating. Never open its casing or infer energy creation from delayed light.

Display ONLY needed fallback lines. Print and say: "Invented for practice. These are supplied observations, not our measurements."
