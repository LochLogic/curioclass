# Section 2.5 private keys

TEACHER ONLY. Keep this piece separate from all child pages. Read privately in Before class. All cases/counts described as invented are supplied practice records, never promised measurements. These keys were solved cold for data entry; the independent answer-key read follows.

## s02-E1-sound

Shape: visual-barrier sound inference from separated supports/silence control.

Prompt: At the sound station, record one observation with its evidence-linked energy-path explanation.

Key and look-fors: Separate-support receiver moves during sound, remains still during silence behind visual barrier. Sound carries energy across the gap.

Marking: One actual-observation point plus one evidence-linked account point. Record-based reasoning earns at most the account point; missing live evidence remains not observed.

Key and look-fors: Exposed patterned-cloth regions warm more than shaded regions from common start. Light carries energy to exposed cloth. Use actual readings.

Marking: One actual-observation point plus one evidence-linked account point. Record-based reasoning earns at most the account point; missing live evidence remains not observed.

## s02-E1-heat

Shape: detached warmer source and second recipient with untouched control.

Prompt: At the heat station, record one observation with its evidence-linked energy-path explanation.

Key and look-fors: After hand removal, warmer source spoon cools; cooler recipient warms; untouched control stays unchanged. Energy transfers warmer to cooler.

Marking: One actual-observation point plus one evidence-linked account point. Record-based reasoning earns at most the account point; missing live evidence remains not observed.

## s02-E1-current

Shape: joint condition of two interruptions in fresh loop.

Prompt: At the current station, record one observation with its evidence-linked energy-path explanation.

Key and look-fors: Only closing both breaks completes the loop through load and both terminals. Current carries stored cell energy to light.

Marking: One actual-observation point plus one evidence-linked account point. Record-based reasoning earns at most the account point; missing live evidence remains not observed.

## s02-E2

Shape: unfamiliar motion-to-current explanation plus constrained failure-based refinement.

Prompt: Invented for practice. A pull-cord turns a generator powering a marker lamp. The signal must light on every pull. Keep the listed parts and one box. Two failed pulls coincide with a joint opening. Explain conversion and a justified improvement within these limits, including a retest prediction.

Key and look-fors: Pulling supplies motion; generator converts motion to electrical energy; complete-path current carries energy to lamp light. Secure the joint because openings coincide with failed signals. Predict fewer failures under matching pulls, then retest all pulls, without guaranteeing success.

Marking: Six points: motion input; generator conversion; complete current path; light output; matching failure evidence; feasible change with comparable retest prediction.

## s02-E2-portfolio

Shape: locate already revised personal performance evidence.

Prompt: Locate your revised personal design evidence for the four labeled components.

Key and look-fors: Individual energy-based plan; criterion and limits; interpreted before/after tests; failure-based refinement with comparable retest. Use days 25 to 29 evidence, day 30 feedback, day 35 revision and teacher implementation initials. Team success alone earns no individual performance credit.

Marking: Four portfolio points, scored once after day 38 from preserved evidence; no second build or interview. Descriptive feedback precedes revision.

## s02-E3

Shape: two-source hydroelectric model joining origin/use/renewal/effects and partial improvement.

Prompt: Invented for practice. An unnamed river facility supplies lighting. After a passage is added, some fish travel upstream. Flooded habitat remains. Combine both sources to explain its energy use and environmental effects. Revise its model to show the improvement and what remains.

Key and look-fors: A: sunlight supports water cycling; rain/snow replenish river flow, turning turbine/generator for lighting. B: dams may block fish travel and reservoirs cover habitat. Passage helps upstream travel; flooded land remains. Attribute mechanisms to A/B and facility outcomes to invented record. Renewal is not constant availability.

Marking: Ten points: origin 1; need 1; renewal timescale 1; technology 1; two causal effects 2; changed effect 1; remaining effect 1; accurate identified A contribution 1 and B contribution 1.
