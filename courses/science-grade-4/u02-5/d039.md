# Day 39: Audit a toy report

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read Course check answer key and Day 39 observation alternatives. Pretest the five comparisons with day 36 controls; record actual whole-degree temperatures. Pre-expose masked cloth. Warm one spoon with a palm only. Position demonstrations for seated visibility. Prepare invented records for unavailable or unresolved comparisons; retain failed readings. Cover teaching displays. Keep the unchanged day 36 individual observation record private.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Return day 38's s02-P38-1 and final s02-E38-record summary with written notes; they go in folders. Receive day 36's unchanged observation record, informational only. Explain independent report auditing. Read the invented toy record and scientific account neutrally. No hints or model answers.

#### 10 min. Transfer demonstrations

Whole class observes five adult comparisons, two minutes each: sound, pre-exposed cloth, contact warming, open/closed lamp circuit, turning/stopped sealed generator. Use Day 39 observation alternatives for setup, controls and failed readings. Students jot evidence beside matching items; all write simultaneously. Never force a result or increase sound. Print and say that fallback observations are invented for practice. They support reasoning, never firsthand performance credit.

#### 28 min. Report repairs

Alone: eight minutes for [[C-E39-speed]], [[C-E39-question]] and [[C-E39-predict]]; twenty for [[C-E39-sound]], [[C-E39-light]], [[C-E39-heat]] and [[C-E39-current]]. Keep observations accessible. Read words without explaining science. Accept labeled drawings or a scribe within these minutes. No individual interview round. Record unavailable observations explicitly.

#### 4 min. Collect and disconnect

Collect every paper. Adult disconnects, counts parts and contains paper; wipe tables and clean goggles. After class, allow thirty minutes for component feedback using the private key. Keep one categorical record, with no total: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No observation or portfolio rescore. More than ten misses on a component selects its day 41 repair group.

### Other rooms

No apparatus or safe sightline: display the corresponding invented record. No printer: copy the page across two board panels; children answer on paper. Preserve evidence provenance.

### At home

**Swap.** Use supplied observation records instead of apparatus or a projector. Copy prompts onto paper without a printer.

**Skip.** Class cleanup and observation-record handoff.

- **40 min. Assessment audit.** Read the invented toy record, account and selected observation records without explaining. Your child answers [[C-E39-speed]], [[C-E39-question]], [[C-E39-predict]], [[C-E39-sound]], [[C-E39-light]], [[C-E39-heat]] and [[C-E39-current]] alone.
- **5 min. Look together.** Keep original writing for day 41.
  **Say.** "Where did energy go when the toy slowed?" Expect: gate motion, sound and slight warming; remaining motion still holds energy.

### Shorter or longer

Keep all components and collection time. An early finisher checks evidence labels without another task.

### Words and supports

Review motion energy, transfer, circuit, terminal and generator. Read directions and records aloud neutrally. Explain only access words, not science answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 audits two fresh transport-fuel sources. Hold today's papers for day 41 feedback and revision. Errors A to H, O and P route to motion or paths groups then; no repair during tomorrow's assessment.

## Student page

### Side 1

I can repair a report using evidence.

**Toy record, invented for practice**

The same toy tram hits the same sliding gate on the same surface.
Each speed has repeated trials.
Every slower trial moves the gate less than every faster trial.
The toy slows after contact; the gate moves.
Slower contacts sound quieter at the same listening point.

**Scientific account**

Collisions also transfer energy into slight warming of objects and nearby air.
Our toy record has no temperature reading.
