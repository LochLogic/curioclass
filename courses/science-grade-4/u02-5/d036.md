# Day 36: Independent observations and design evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the private answer keys, including both generator responses. Pretest Day 36 private station setup for visibility, compatibility and stable whole-degree readings. Prepare common-start cloth readings and separate spoon temperatures. Check full-room eye protection and allergies before choosing live sound. Conceal all cases and Side 2. Lay out named kits, cleanup supplies and Day 36 independent evidence. Retrieve day 35's revised My individual design evidence. Keep Single 28-point assessment record private.

### The day

#### 3 min. Launch

Day 35's model and portfolio revision lead to today's independent check. Review the grade 3 assessment routine: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. State equipment boundaries only. Children work alone; read directions neutrally. Keep Side 2 covered until Generator record. No day 35 repair or answer display.

#### 20 min. Four live observations

Run sound, light, heat and current in that order, five minutes each. Reveal [[s02-E1-sound]], [[s02-E1-light]], [[s02-E1-heat]], then [[s02-E1-current]] only at each turn. Each slot holds two one-minute seated views for fifteen children, including a thirty-second repeat, then three writing minutes. Adult reads actual thermometer marks aloud. Follow private configurations exactly, including silence, shade, untouched spoon and both circuit breaks. Keep supports separate. Record unclear changes honestly. If a pretest fails or protection is unavailable, reveal only that Assessment fallback O row. Read aloud: "Invented for practice, supplied evidence." Preserve actual readings and missing live evidence; no coaching.

#### 5 min. Generator record

Reveal [[s02-E2]] and [[s02-E2b]]. Children read the invented record and begin an independent explanation. No apparatus or worked example for this case.

#### 5 min. Portfolio evidence

Return day 35's preserved portfolio. Alone, children locate [[s02-E2-portfolio]] components in existing evidence. No new build, interview or revision. Missing implementation remains missing.

#### 7 min. Synthesis

Children finish the generator response, improvement and predicted comparable retest unaided. Collect each packet and its portfolio separately.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

For two minutes, adult disconnects, counts and stores equipment, then brushes contained grains. Protection stays on. Children return papers and wipe hands/tables. For three minutes, six cleaning places each serve five children cleaning goggles with wipes and storing them in bags. No sink queue.

### Other rooms

No printer: reveal exact stems and assigned records on the board at their turn. No apparatus: supplied reasoning only, not firsthand evidence. No projector: large teacher-read marks. A scribe records decisions without supplying them.

### At home

**Swap.** No apparatus or full eye protection: use assigned fallback rows, said aloud as invented. No printer: copy exact stems. No portfolio: preserve the gap.

**Skip.** Class waves and public turns.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Adult alone operates safe, pretested equipment. Child answers [[s02-E1-sound]], [[s02-E1-light]], [[s02-E1-heat]], [[s02-E1-current]], [[s02-E2]], [[s02-E2b]] and [[s02-E2-portfolio]] independently. Preserve actual unclear readings. Read neutrally; give no hints. Keep all answers and missing opportunities for day 38.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** Disconnect, store and wipe. Keep protection through grain cleanup. Feedback waits until day 38.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: no content cut; record unfinished components. Longer: quiet completion only, without hints.

### Words and supports

Review: energy, sound, light, heat, current, generator, circuit, criterion, limits, refinement and retest. Read unfamiliar words neutrally; accept labeled drawings or dictation.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 combines two unfamiliar sources independently, without day 36 feedback. Return today's assessment and portfolio with written component feedback in day 38 Read feedback. Allow thirty children times four minutes across days 36/37: 120 minutes after class. Final points wait until after revision. Unchanged observation records go to day 39.
