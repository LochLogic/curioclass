## C-E39-speed

Answer: The same faster toy has more motion energy to transfer. Every faster contact moves the gate farther. Greater gate motion indicates greater transfer in these controlled comparisons; distance is not energy units.

Look for: Look for speed-energy link, repeated slower/faster evidence and transfer reasoning. One category per component: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No total.

Wrong: The same toy always has equal motion energy. Error A.

## C-E39-question

Answer: Does changing this toy from slower to faster increase gate movement after contact? Keep toy, gate and surface unchanged. Accept other speed questions with observable transfer effects.

Look for: Look for changed speed and observable collision outcome. One category for the whole question.

Wrong: Which speed is nicest? Error C.

## C-E39-predict

Answer: Compared with a faster repeat, predict less gate motion and usually quieter sound. The toy loses motion energy as it slows, transferring energy to gate motion, sound and slight warming of objects and air. Retain motion energy for any continuing motion. Warming is account-based, not measured here.

Look for: Look for both objects and motion-energy changes, slower/faster comparison, sound/warming destinations and evidence/account distinction. One category per component.

Wrong: Energy disappears when the toy slows. Error B.
Wrong: All energy becomes sound. Error D.

## C-E39-sound

Answer: Separate supports remove the contact bridge. Receiving paper moves during gentle sound, rests during silence. This supports sound carrying energy across the gap; a shared board leaves another path possible.

Look for: Look for comparison and source-to-recipient account. Keep day 36 observation status unchanged. Supplied evidence never becomes firsthand.

Wrong: A shared board proves sound crossed air. Error F.

## C-E39-light

Answer: Use actual whole-degree readings from equal starts. Greater warming of exposed than shaded cloth supports light carrying energy to cloth. Appearance alone is insufficient. Honest unresolved readings supply no resolved warming evidence.

Look for: Look for comparative readings and light-to-cloth reasoning. Separate actual and supplied observations.

Wrong: Dark color proves this patch is warmer. Error O.

## C-E39-heat

Answer: Energy passes warmer spoon to cooler spoon. After hand removal, source cooling and recipient warming, beside an unchanged untouched spoon, support this direction. Unresolved readings do not prove no transfer.

Look for: Look for warmer-to-cooler direction with comparative evidence, not claimed measured energy.

Wrong: No resolved change proves no energy passed. Error E.
Wrong: The cooler spoon makes its own warmth. Error P.

## C-E39-current

Answer: A complete path through bulb and both battery terminals is needed. Current carries stored battery energy to light. Turning the sealed generator supplies motion energy converted into electrical energy carried by current to the lamp. Cite closed/open and turning/stopped evidence. Delayed light may use stored energy.

Look for: One category for circuit with evidence; one for generator input, conversion and output with evidence. No design or portfolio score.

Wrong: One wire to one terminal completes the lamp circuit. Error G.
Wrong: The generator creates energy. Error H.
