# Single 28-point assessment record

TEACHER ONLY. One point scale: each component earns 1 if shown independently, 0 if an attempted component is not shown. Absence or an unavailable observation remains unscored, with a dated missing-evidence note. Do not turn missing equipment into a zero. Report points earned out of observed/attempted points and list missing points; use /28 only with full evidence opportunity. No categorical marking on this sheet.

Give written feedback before day 38. Final scoring follows day 38 revision; portfolio is scored once from day 35 revision and earlier actual implementation evidence. Revised explanations may show supported reasoning; do not relabel coached or supplied accounts as unaided live observations.
