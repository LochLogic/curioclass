# Day 41: Revise, then check sound ideas

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read Course check answer key. Write feedback on days 39 and 40 before returning them. Assign motion, paths or resources from actual gaps, at most two groups per child. Fold Side 2 below the revision directions, hiding readiness until the last block. Keep originals, revisions and unchanged day 36 observations distinct. Use one categorical component record, no total.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return feedback

Return day 39's assessment and day 40's assessment with written evidence gaps. Children read privately and locate assigned components. Review feedback-then-revision. Blank work gets a brief conference next block, not an assumed error letter. No public scores or mistake examples.

#### 12 min. Parallel repair

Three groups work simultaneously. Teacher visits motion, paths and resources for three minutes each, leaving three minutes for transitions or a blank-work conference. Children use [[C-P41-motion]], [[C-P41-paths]] or [[C-P41-resources]], with assessment originals covered. Motion then uses [[C-P41-question]], written by all assigned children during its visit. One paper response per group, no thirty-child spoken round. If many share an error, prioritize that visit without adding time. Ready children identify an uncertainty from their evidence.

Motion covers errors A, B, C and D through repeated puck effects and energy destinations. Paths covers errors E, F, G, H, O and P through familiar teaching observations, the fan drawing and generator input. Resources covers errors I, J, K and L using day 40 sources with answers covered. At paths, select the archived sound/silence and separate-support log for error F; warming table for errors E/O/P; circuit layouts and energy chart for errors G/H. These are teaching reminders, never new checks. Supply unfamiliar words explicitly rather than call them review. No live retry or interview is promised.

#### 18 min. Revise and collect

Alone: eight minutes revise marked day 39 components, eight revise day 40, two collect. **Ask.** [[C-P41-revise]]. Preserve originals; add evidence notes in second color. Score [[C-E41-record]] from submitted work after class using the same component categories. Content help means shown with help. Missing firsthand observations remain missing. No portfolio or observation rescore.

#### 10 min. Readiness

Uncover the Readiness block on Side 2. Four minutes each for [[C-R41-source]] and [[C-R41-receiver]], two to collect. Read records neutrally; say they are invented for practice. No feedback or science definitions before answers. Diagnostic only, no grade 4 achievement score. No grain or ruler demonstration today. Record which causal direction needs teaching; earlier causal ledger entries remain planned only.

### Other rooms

No printer: show group records first, cover them, then write readiness records. Children answer on paper. No second color: box revisions.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the page without a printer. Box pencil changes without a second color. Point at paper instead of a projector.

**Skip.** Group rotations and a second repair case.

- **5 min. Read notes.** Find feedback on days 39 and 40.
- **10 min. Repair.** Choose [[C-P41-motion]], [[C-P41-paths]] or [[C-P41-resources]] for the main gap.
  **Say.** "Which evidence supports your change?" Expect: a named observation or source statement, not preference.
- **10 min. Revise.** Do [[C-P41-revise]] on original papers. [[C-E41-record]] requires no new writing.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Give [[C-R41-source]] and [[C-R41-receiver]] alone, without hints. Keep these for day 43's sound work.

### Shorter or longer

Choose one priority repair, preserving both revisions and readiness. Extend with an evidence-limit note.

### Words and supports

Group practice stays in the folder. Return it with a written note next opening. Use words needed by assigned repairs. Review transfer, circuit, generator, natural resource and nonrenewable. Scribing and labeled drawings count; access help supplies no science answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 keeps the cork hook, questions and initial wave model together. Send both readiness records to day 43's reserved response: five minutes per causal direction within its fifteen minutes. Missing causal directions are taught as new, not claimed as earlier completed learning. No signal-device check today. Return revised work with a written note next opening, informational only.

### Credit

Ours. Sources C39-energy, C40-A, C40-B and C41-sound support the accounts.
