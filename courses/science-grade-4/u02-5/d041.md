# Day 41: Revise, then check sound ideas

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read Course check answer key. Write feedback on days 39 and 40 before returning them. Assign motion, paths or resources from actual gaps, one priority group and one main error per child. Select its archived record in advance. Fold Side 2 below the revision directions, hiding readiness until the last block. Keep originals, revisions and unchanged day 36 observations distinct. Use one categorical component record, no total.

### Safety and set-up

Paper records only. Prepare selected records the day before; no grains, fuels or live circuits.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return feedback

Return day 39's assessment and day 40's assessment with written evidence gaps. Children read privately and locate assigned components. Review feedback-then-revision. Blank work gets a brief conference next block, not an assumed error letter. No public scores or mistake examples.

#### 12 min. Parallel repair

Three groups work simultaneously for twelve minutes. Visit each for three minutes; reserve three for transitions or a blank-work conference. Each child completes one main repair on paper, with assessment answers covered. Others keep working during visits. No individual speaking queue. More than ten misses prioritizes the matching visit; remaining gaps stay on the handoff note.

Motion: use [[C-P41-motion]] for A, B or D. Use [[C-P41-question]] instead for C, with the puck record visible. Review repeated evidence, energy destinations and testable questions from unit 1.

Paths: [[C-P41-paths]], with one assigned earlier teaching record. F uses the separate-support sound log; O the cloth readings; E/P the spoon table; G the circuit layout; H the familiar crank drawing. Review the selected source-to-recipient account, retaining unresolved readings. Teacher key gives each version's look-fors.

Resources: I/J use [[C-P41-origin]] with day 40 Source A. K/L use [[C-P41-resources]] with Source B. Cover the child's old answers, keeping source paragraphs available. Review natural origin, formation time or changed and remaining effects as needed. Ready children identify an uncertainty in their own record.

#### 18 min. Revise and collect

Alone: eight minutes revise marked day 39 components, eight revise day 40, two collect. **Ask.** [[C-P41-revise]]. Preserve originals; add evidence notes in second color. Score [[C-E41-record]] from submitted work after class using the same component categories. Content help means shown with help. Missing firsthand observations remain missing. No portfolio or observation rescore.

#### 10 min. Readiness

Uncover the Readiness block on Side 2. Four minutes each for [[C-R41-source]] and [[C-R41-receiver]], two to collect. Read records neutrally; say they are invented for practice. No feedback or science definitions before answers. Diagnostic only, no grade 4 achievement score. No grain or ruler demonstration today. Record which causal direction needs teaching; review written grade 1 sound models without assuming individual mastery.

### Other rooms

No printer: show group records first, cover them, then write readiness records. Children answer on paper. No second color: box revisions.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the page without a printer. Box pencil changes without a second color. Point at paper instead of a projector.

**Skip.** Group rotations and a second repair case.

- **5 min. Read notes.** Find feedback on days 39 and 40.
- **10 min. Repair.** Choose the matching main repair: [[C-P41-motion]], [[C-P41-question]], [[C-P41-paths]], [[C-P41-origin]] or [[C-P41-resources]].
  **Say.** "Which evidence supports your change?" Expect: a named observation or source statement, not preference.
- **10 min. Revise.** Do [[C-P41-revise]] on original papers. [[C-E41-record]] requires no new writing.
- **10 min. Readiness.** Give [[C-R41-source]] and [[C-R41-receiver]] alone, without hints. Keep these for day 43's sound work.

### Shorter or longer

Choose one priority repair, preserving both revisions and readiness. Extend with an evidence-limit note.

### Words and supports

Group practice stays in the folder. Return it with a written note next opening. Use words needed by assigned repairs. Review transfer, circuit, generator, natural resource and nonrenewable. Scribing and labeled drawings count; access help supplies no science answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 keeps the cork hook, questions and initial wave model together. Send both readiness records to day 43's reserved response: five minutes per causal direction within its fifteen minutes. Review grade 1 days 31 and 32; explicitly reteach missing causal directions. No signal-device check today. Return revised work with a written note next opening, informational only.
