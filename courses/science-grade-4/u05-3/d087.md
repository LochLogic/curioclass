# Day 87: Record small-flow evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, returned day 86 preparation guides and exits, dry flood templates and controls guides, flood tray set, flood logs and Flood fallback W. Hide W's larger-flow page. Retrieve shaking log and Shake fallback S. Check sight lines. Lay out splash goggles and approved wipes/bags and adult handwashing supplies. Prepare one-item half slips; cover Exit boxes.

### Safety and set-up

One covered adult-only wet station. Check sand/clay allergies beforehand; use paper if needed. Children handle dry paper only. Everyone nearby wears splash goggles through setup/work/cleanup. Pour below faces, keep sand damp, wipe spills immediately; no tasting. Adult settles sediment, disposes outdoors where permitted, never in drains, and washes hands. Cleanup: five minutes.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair, controls

Return day 86's preparation guides and both exit papers. If many show error E, [[s05-P87-1]], five minutes. Unresolved error D: [[s05-P87-2]], five minutes including final-minute goggles/sight-line check. Ready children audit shared starts. Review fair test and failure locations from day 86. Do not predict larger-flow results.

#### 20 min. Small-flow tests

Adult alone tests wall, channel and platform twice each. Six two-minute trials take twelve minutes; eight minutes cover resets/design changes. Use one level cup each trial: same cup, inlet, marked height, pace, slope, restored bed and house marks. Replace wet cardboard with matched dry pieces. Wet means visible water at a floor mark. Each child enters whole-house counts and observed routes, [[s05-P87-3]], from dry places. No individual apparatus turns.

If water soaks in or the route cannot be read, retain unreadable; try one safe reset inside reset budget, then announce Flood fallback W is invented for practice. Use small-flow page only, in separate supplied rows. W lacks house identities and one wetting cause: leave those entries unknown. Never invent a route from a wet count. Note missing firsthand evidence. Actual trials promise no winner.

#### 5 min. Condition reasoning

Explicitly review day 85's comparison with separate shaking log columns, [[s05-P87-4]]. Ask before revealing stronger column; demonstrate comparing both outcomes. Both records qualify a claim. Children mark larger-flow evidence missing in flood logs; do not guess it. This is unscored teaching before portfolio, with no completed ideal product. Public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

After pouring stops, two minutes parallel goggle wiping/bagging. Three minutes adult sediment containment, surface cleanup and handwashing; dry children file logs. Keep failures and original entries.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal invented two-cluster record. Alone, [[s05-X87-compare]] on one-item half slip; [[s05-X87-failure]] in Exit box. Four minutes answer, one collect both. No cause beyond the given wetting evidence is required.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy today's texts and blank records beforehand; cover reserved work. Use the named invented fallback if safe observation is unavailable, noting missing practical evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe wet kit or goggles, use small-flow W, announced invented. Without printer/projector, copy logs. Adult handles wet work; child records from a dry place.

**Skip.** Class viewing and a second repair route.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P87-1]].
- **15 min. Observe.** Use [[s05-P87-3]], with W or adult-run class procedure.
- **5 min. Compare.** Review [[s05-P87-4]] with the shaking log.
  **Say.** "What flood evidence is missing?" Expect larger-flow results, not a predicted winner.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** File records. Adult settles sediment, disposes outdoors where permitted, cleans surfaces and washes hands; wipe/bag goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the cluster record. Child answers [[s05-X87-compare]] and [[s05-X87-failure]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, retaining repairs, evidence and exit. Extend by naming an untested condition.

### Words and supports

Review condition, fair test, inlet and failure. Keep unknown causes unknown. Enlarge floor marks and logs; provide seated views or neutral scribing. Read reserved stems neutrally.

### Tomorrow

Return the student written task with descriptive notes. Practice feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Mark both exit responses: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Note language or missing evidence separately.

Return small-flow log with notes and both exit papers on day 88. Error E uses five-minute missing-condition repair before larger-flow trials. Keep raw small-flow evidence for independent comparison. No score today.
