# Day 88: Larger flow and an evidence-linked change

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, returned day 87 small-flow logs and exits, flood logs and Flood fallback W, dry flood templates and controls guides, flood tray set, improvement labels, portfolio and feedback sheet. Restore starts; check sight lines. Lay out splash goggles and approved wipes/bags and adult handwashing supplies. Hide reserved channel-end record. Prepare one-item half slips; cover Exit boxes.

### Safety and set-up

One covered adult-only wet station. Check sand/clay allergies beforehand; use paper if needed. Children handle dry paper only. Everyone nearby wears splash goggles through setup/work/cleanup. Pour below faces, keep sand damp, wipe spills immediately; no tasting. Adult settles sediment, disposes outdoors where permitted, never in drains, and washes hands. Cleanup: five minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 87's small-flow log and both exit papers. If many show error E, [[s05-P88-1]] before testing. Ready children check starts. Keep unaltered small-flow record; review condition and failure from days 84 to 87. Goggles on before wet work.

#### 20 min. Larger-flow tests

Adult tests each unchanged design twice: six two-minute trials plus eight reset/change minutes. Two successive level cups per run, same inlet, marked height, pace, slope, restored bed and house positions. Replace wet houses with matched dry pieces. Children observe from dry places and enter [[s05-P88-2]]. Count whole cupfuls/houses; unreadable is not dry.

If soaking/hidden routes prevent reading, keep unreadable; one safe reset within budget, then announce W is invented for practice. Larger-flow W shows wall overtopping, channel bank spill and platform edge wetting; use separate supplied rows. Never infer house identities from W. Compare actual with actual, or both W pages. If mixed, label mismatch and limit the claim. No promised winner.

#### 5 min. Improvement labels

Alone, [[s05-P88-3]] on improvement labels. Link one observed failure to a proposed effect, without recommendation revision. If none appears, mark an untested weakness. Review grade 3's second-color revision: keep the first drawing and observations. A second protection repair may use this block, never portfolio time. Public explanation is optional.

#### 5 min. Individual portfolio

Independently complete [[s05-P88-compare]] and [[s05-P88-improve]] on portfolio and feedback sheet. Use own separate small/larger records; show no ideal comparison. Collect before feedback, unscored today. Failed designs can supply strong observation evidence; W cannot show firsthand observation skill.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

After pours stop, two minutes parallel goggle wiping/bagging. Dry children file logs while adult settles sediment, wipes surfaces and washes hands, three minutes. No sink queue.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal reserved invented channel-end record for the first time. Alone, [[s05-X88-revise]] on one-item half slip; [[s05-X88-improve]] in Exit box. Four minutes answer, one collect both. Keep transfer separate from portfolio.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy today's texts and blank records beforehand; cover reserved work. Use the named invented fallback if safe observation is unavailable, noting missing practical evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Without safe wet kit or goggles, use W's separately labelled flow pages. Without printer/projector, copy logs and portfolio. Adult alone handles wet work.

**Skip.** Public explanation and second repair route.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P88-1]].
- **12 min. Observe.** Do [[s05-P88-2]] with W or adult-run trials.
  **Say.** "Where did water pass protection?" Expect an observed route, or W's overtopping, bank spill or edge wetting.
- **3 min. Label.** Do [[s05-P88-3]].
- **5 min. Portfolio.** Child answers [[s05-P88-compare]] and [[s05-P88-improve]] independently.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** File logs; adult contains sediment, cleans surfaces and washes hands. Wipe/bag goggles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal held record. Child answers [[s05-X88-revise]] and [[s05-X88-improve]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, retaining repairs, evidence and exit. Extend by naming an untested condition.

### Words and supports

Review condition, failure, channel, platform and second-color revision. Keep observed routes distinct from proposed effects. Enlarge logs; neutral scribing preserves the child's reasoning.

### Tomorrow

Return the student written task with descriptive notes. Practice feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Mark both exit responses: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Note language or missing evidence separately.

Return larger-flow log and both exit papers on day 89. Error D annotates failure; error E contrasts conditions, five minutes each. Then compare monitoring and warning evidence. After class, give portfolio comparison feedback and one improvement question: 30 times two minutes, 60 minutes. Return portfolio on day 90 for revision; first score day 92.
