# Section 5.3 opener: Comparing flood models

Days 86 to 88 join matched investigation, flood protection and cause/effect. Control input water
and restore the starting bed before comparing designs. The tray shows routes and model wetting,
not natural discharge or real-building certification. Review design-cycle chart, hazard/design/harm
arrows and second-color revision keeping original observations, from grade 3 and earlier units.

Print dry templates/guides, logs, portfolio/feedback sheet and Flood fallback W. Adult prepares one
covered station: tray, damp clean sand, clay, three houses, equal cups, bucket and cloth. Retain safety
sheets and ask sand/clay allergies in advance; use paper if needed. Children handle dry templates only.
Adult installs wet designs, pours below faces and restores bed, inlet, slope, house locations and pace.
Splash goggles for everyone nearby during setup/work/cleanup. No tasting. Wipe spills immediately.
Adult settles sediment, disposes outdoors where permitted, never in drains, then washes hands.

Day 86: six parallel groups make wall/channel/platform dry templates plus house/pour/log guides.
Each child records contribution. Day 87 uses one level cup, day 88 two successive level cups. Six
2-minute trials plus eight reset/change minutes fit twenty each day. Read whole cupfuls and whole
houses. Unreadable routes or soaking: one safe reset within budget, then W, announced invented.
Keep actual and invented rows separate. Five-minute cleanup includes parallel goggle wipes/bags,
dry filing and adult wet work/handwashing. Dry student handling avoids a sink queue.

Keep W's larger-flow page hidden day 87. Two trials per flow/design, three houses. Overtopping,
bank spill and edge wetting support different improvements. The practice channel's bank spill is
not held E88's end bypass. No live winner promised. Day 87 explicitly reviews two-condition reasoning
from day 85 before day 88 independent portfolio. Collect it, give written comparison feedback and an
improvement question after class, about two minutes per child. Revise fifteen minutes day 90;
first score day 92, four points. Design failure itself loses no reasoning credit. No ideal completed
portfolio for copying. E88 separately returns day 89 D/E repair. Before class name teacher-only-exit-key.md.

Practice keeps raw logs separate from improvement labels, never held recommendation revision.
Hear six voluntary turns only, 4.5 minutes; all return paper. Record descriptive feedback and missing
firsthand evidence when W replaces testing. Background U5-divert, U5-flood, U5-safety.
