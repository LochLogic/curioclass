# Day 86: Prepare three flood models

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only-exit-key.md. Prepare unit 5 record sheet, parallel repair packets, day 85 unscored shaking log and exits, dry flood templates and controls guides, flood logs and Flood fallback W. Hide W outcomes. Pretest flood tray set; mark bed, inlet, slope, house positions and pour height. Lay out splash goggles and approved wipes/bags and adult handwashing supplies. Cover Exit boxes; prepare one-item half slips with reserved diagram.

### Safety and set-up

One covered adult-only wet station. Check sand/clay allergies beforehand; use paper if needed. Children handle dry paper only. Everyone nearby wears splash goggles through setup/work/cleanup. Pour below faces, keep sand damp, wipe spills immediately; no tasting. Adult settles sediment, disposes outdoors where permitted, never in drains, and washes hands. Children move to dry seats; adult keeps goggles through wet cleanup. Cleanup: five minutes.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 85's exit tickets, both half slip and paper Exit box, with the unscored shaking log and notes. If many show error E, [[s05-P86-1]], five minutes using separate motion columns. Unresolved error D uses [[s05-P86-2]], teaching overflow only, five minutes. Ready children inspect blank guides. One successful condition cannot establish every outcome.

#### 5 min. Controls

Review grade 3's fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Review barrier and hazard, design, harm arrows. Review day 84's channel, platform, inlet and failure; day 51's condition. Dry-house criterion: no visible water at a floor mark. Alone, [[s05-P86-3]]. Adult models restoring starts, changing only design at a given input. Input step: one whole level cupful; counts: whole houses, not natural discharge.

#### 15 min. Prepare the shared set

Six parallel groups of five use [[s05-P86-4]]. Groups 1 to 3 make wall, channel and platform dry templates. Groups 4 to 6 prepare house-position, cup/pour and recording guides. Each records a specific contribution. Adult checks guides and installs designs successively in the shared tray without showing trial outcomes. Restore bed, slope, inlet and house positions each time. Tomorrow's small flow uses one cup; later larger flow uses two successive cups at unchanged height/pace. Children never handle sand/clay.

#### 5 min. Compare preparations

**Ask.** [[s05-P86-5]]. Hear rotating six volunteers, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; all return contribution notes. Public passes are allowed. Link a possible effect to a water route. If multiple starts change, fix the guide before testing. No playground-entrance or two-sketch transfer practice.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Parallel goggle wiping/bagging, two minutes. Dry children file templates/guides while adult contains wet material, cleans surfaces and washes hands, three minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal invented playground-entrance diagram on one-item half slip. Alone, [[s05-X86-ideas]] there; [[s05-X86-control]] in Exit box. Four minutes answer, one collect both. Read directions without proposing designs.

### Other rooms

No printer/projector: copy today's texts and blank records beforehand; cover reserved work. Use the named invented fallback if safe observation is unavailable, noting missing practical evidence.

### At home

**Swap.** Paper templates and blank logs replace a class set. Without printer/projector, copy them. Without safe wet equipment or goggles, use dry guides; hide W outcomes today.

**Skip.** Group rotation and wet installation.

- **5 min. Repair.** Use [[s05-P86-1]] with the earlier log.
- **5 min. Controls.** Child does [[s05-P86-3]].
- **15 min. Prepare.** Child makes one dry template and guide, [[s05-P86-4]].
  **Say.** "What must match when designs change?" Expect input, bed, inlet, house marks and pouring setup.
- **5 min. Cleanup.** File guides; adult handles wet cleanup and handwashing if used.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the entrance diagram. Child answers [[s05-X86-ideas]] and [[s05-X86-control]] alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public sharing, retaining repairs, evidence and exit. Extend by naming an untested condition.

### Words and supports

Review fair test, barrier, criterion, condition, channel, platform, inlet and failure. Enlarge guides and mark dry routes by texture. Neutral scribing preserves individual contributions.

### Tomorrow

Return the student written task with descriptive notes. Practice feedback after class: 30 times 15 seconds, 7.5 minutes. Mark both exit responses: 30 times 30 seconds, 15 minutes. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on unit 5 record sheet. Note language or missing evidence separately.

Return preparation guides with notes and both exit papers on day 87. Error E distinguishes one flow from untested conditions; unresolved error D retains teaching overflow, five minutes each. Then run small-flow tests without predicting larger-flow outcomes.
