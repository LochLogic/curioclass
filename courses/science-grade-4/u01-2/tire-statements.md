# Tire observation and statement cards

Invented for practice.

The same toy tire hits the same index card under matching conditions.
Three slower contacts each move the card a little.
Three faster contacts each move it farther.

| Record strip | Observation |
|---|---|
| S1, S2, S3 | Tire approaches slower; card moves a little. |
| F1, F2, F3 | Tire approaches faster; card moves farther. |

Statement cards:

- The tire moved faster.
- The card moved farther.
- Energy passed into card motion.
- The faster tire had more motion energy.
- The tire had equal energy at both speeds.

Match each complete statement to its supporting tire observation.
