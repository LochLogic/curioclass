# Teacher-only answers, section 1.2

Teacher-only answer key. Keep separate from every child copy.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen; no points.

## s01-P5

Prompt: Label what centimeters measure in our marble record.

Expected response: Centimeters measure cup travel distance, not energy.

Look for: Label the measured quantity as travel distance.

Expected wrong responses: Same marble means equal energy. (A); Centimeters measure energy. (B).

## s01-K5

Prompt: Invented for practice. Two listeners stood at different distances during otherwise matching rolls. Explain aloud why their sound reports cannot isolate speed.

Expected response: Listening distance also changed, so the sound difference cannot isolate the effect of approach speed.

Look for: Name the changed listening distance.; Connect that extra change to a possible alternative cause of the sound reports.

Expected wrong responses: Changing listener distance still isolates speed. (G).

## s01-Pn5

Prompt: Write three short evidence notes from our repeated marble trials.

Expected response: Three relevant notes, not a complete written explanation.

Look for: Name repeated outcomes.; Name a fixed condition.; Describe cup motion without energy units.

Expected wrong responses: The cup traveled centimeters of energy. (B).

## s01-X5

Prompt: Invented for practice. One cylinder strikes matching fresh spring receivers. Other contact conditions stay fixed. Compression, in centimeters: slower 2, 3, 2; faster 6, 7, 6. Explain what the spring record suggests about the cylinder's motion energy.

Expected response: The faster cylinder has more motion energy. All faster compressions exceed all slower compressions. Greater compression under matching conditions supports more energy available to transfer.

Look for: Connect faster motion to more motion energy.; Cite the repeated greater compression.; Explain that the controlled greater effect supports more energy available to transfer.

Expected wrong responses: Same cylinder means equal energy. (A).

## s01-P6

Prompt: Point to repeated marble effects supporting more motion energy at faster speed.

Expected response: Use separating outcomes at both speeds, conditions fixed.

Look for: Cite repeated effects at both speeds.; Same object does not imply equal energy when speed differs.

Expected wrong responses: One marble always has equal motion energy. (A).

## s01-K6

Prompt: Invented for practice: the same toy tire hits the same index card. Each speed has three trials. Every faster trial moves the card farther. Choose the explanation using transfer.

Expected response: Statement A connects energy passing to the card with its changed motion.

Look for: A: Faster tire motion has more energy available to transfer at contact; that changes card motion.; B: Faster tire motion has more energy because that is what faster means.; A supplies a mechanism; B restates the claim.

Expected wrong responses: B supplies evidence by repeating faster. (x).

## s01-Pm6

Prompt: Match each complete statement to its supporting tire observation.

Expected response: Greater repeated faster effects support the speed claim; fixed conditions support comparability; motion after contact supports transfer.

Look for: Match independently first.; Repeated outcomes support the speed claim.; Fixed conditions support fairness, not energy quantity.; Keep the note brief; do not rewrite an explanation.

Expected wrong responses: Same tire proves equal energy. (A).

## s01-X6

Prompt: Invented for practice. A hoop hits the same cardboard tube under matching conditions. Repeated faster rolls move it farther. Draft: "The faster hoop has more energy because fast means more energy." Strengthen this explanation with trial evidence and a transfer reason.

Expected response: Repeated faster hoop rolls move the tube farther. Energy passing from hoop motion changes tube motion. Greater controlled effects support more motion energy available to transfer.

Look for: Add repeated greater tube movement.; Explain transfer at contact into tube motion.; Connect the controlled greater effect to more available motion energy.

Expected wrong responses: The same hoop always has equal energy. (A); Tube travel measures energy in centimeters. (B).
