# Day 5: Why does target motion support our claim?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the opener and today's teacher-only key below; use the shared key for the exit. Prepare day 4 feedback, listening-comparison.md and evidence-notes.md. Retain the question board and controlled anchor records; use ramp-record-r.md separately if these fail. Keep the shared cylinder-and-spring exit covered. The guided listening argument concerns changed listener distance. Use today's printed observation labels; keep the record sheet teacher-only.

### Safety and set-up

Paper records only. No collision equipment is handled today.

### The day

Collect each student page for feedback next lesson. Review one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. More than ten misses is many.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 4's exit and whole annotation page: s01-P4, s01-Pr4 and s01-K4. If many show error A, spend five minutes using [[s01-Pr5]] with retained marble effects. Review motion energy from day 4; unchanged marble does not mean equal motion energy. Next five minutes address error B with [[s01-P5]]: label centimeters as travel distance. Others use their feedback. Blank work gets a conference.

#### 10 min. Connect evidence labels

Use listening-comparison.md. Review claim, evidence and reason; partners help assemble the oral argument about changed listening distance.

**Ask.** [[s01-K5]]

[[steps:s01-K5]]

Name transfer: energy passes from one object to another. Children say the word and use it to label the cup-motion account. Sort the printed observation labels from the energy-account label; then show one cup record beside its transfer account. Keep observations separate from the scientific explanation. 

#### 15 min. Partner evidence matching

Use [[s01-Pm5]]. Pairs match the printed observation labels to specific retained marble rows, keeping conditions and units visible. Each child takes two 45-second turns, three minutes per pair concurrently. Hear six for 45 seconds each during the same work; record names. Public passing is allowed. Collect each matched set with evidence notes. Keep unresolved questions on the retained question-board.md. Each child records the source trial beside each match.

#### 5 min. Evidence notes

Every child completes [[s01-Pn5]], three separate short notes. Collect all pages for day 6, including children not heard. Teacher checks relevance during work; one record scale only.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the cylinder record now. Say invented for practice. Read [[s01-X5]] exactly; independent paper explanation. Mark after class: 30 times 25 seconds, twelve and a half minutes, using the teacher-only key. Error A claims equal energy for the same object. Return day 6 with a next step.

Also review the 30 collected practice pages after class: 15 seconds each, seven and a half minutes. Add one specific next step; return the whole page in Return and repair next lesson.

### Other rooms

No equipment or device is needed. Missing records: read R as invented for practice. No printer: copy notes spaces and the covered record onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class records: read ramp-record-r.md, invented for practice. No printer: copy paper.

**Skip.** Public sharing and roster turns.

- **8 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s01-Pr5]] and [[s01-P5]].
- **10 min. Connect.** Use listening-comparison.md and [[s01-K5]] aloud.
  **Say.** "Which extra change hides the cause?" Expect: listening distance changed, so sound reports cannot isolate approach speed.
- **7 min. Notes.** Match [[s01-Pm5]]; do [[s01-Pn5]] as separate notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the cylinder record; [[s01-X5]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, keeping both partner turns, notes and exit. Longer: explain why one trial would be weaker evidence than repeats.

### Words and supports

Review motion energy, claim, evidence and reason. Transfer: energy passes between objects. Read records aloud. Offer a scribe for the child's own explanation. Rehearse the frame aloud; keep evidence notes separate.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 returns day 5's exit and evidence notes. If many show error A, use repeated marble effects in five-minute Return and repair. Then select complete tire explanations and match evidence, reserving written strengthening for the exit.
