# Day 5: Why does target motion support our claim?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use the Teacher-only key below; exclude it from child copies. Prepare day 4's exit tickets and annotations with one next step. Lay out retained trial records, question board and class record book. Keep the cylinder record panels covered until the exit. Keep the data key teacher-only. If records are missing, unreadable or overlapping, prepare day 1's R separately, labeled invented for practice.

### Safety and set-up

Paper records only. No collision equipment is handled today.

### The day

Other practice stays in folders; discuss it without collecting.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 4's exit and s01-Pr4 annotations. If many show error A, spend five minutes using [[s01-P5]]: compare the repeated outcomes while keeping the marble unchanged. Review motion energy from day 4; unchanged marble does not mean equal motion energy. Next five minutes address error B: relabel centimeters as distance, not energy. Others use their feedback. Blank work gets a conference.

#### 10 min. Connect the oral frame

Review grade 3 claim, evidence and reason. Give the oral frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Children point to records before speaking.

**Ask.** [[s01-K5]]

Name transfer: energy passes from one object to another. At contact, energy passing from marble to cup changes cup motion. Greater repeated effects with conditions fixed support more energy available from faster marble motion. Distance itself is not energy.

[[steps:s01-K5]]

Model the four-sentence oral explanation from the teacher-only key after children respond. If actual records overlap, state the limit and use separately labeled R; never claim the real record separates.

#### 15 min. Partner evidence chains

Pairs build oral chains using retained observations. Each child takes two 45-second turns: claim and evidence first, then reason after a partner points to the relevant trial. Four turns total three minutes per pair, concurrently. No complete written explanation frame is practiced. Hear the remaining six on the roster for 45 seconds each inside five minutes; record names. Everyone may pass publicly and give a private response.

Partners question the fixed conditions and repeated effects. Children revise their oral chain, preserving what the record actually says. Teacher leaves unresolved destination questions on the board for section 1.3.

#### 5 min. Evidence notes

Every child completes [[s01-Pn5]], three separate short notes. Collect all pages for day 6, including children not heard. Teacher checks relevance during work; one record scale only.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal the cylinder record now. Say invented for practice. Read [[s01-X5]] exactly; independent paper explanation. No cylinder practice occurred. Mark after class: 30 times 25 seconds, twelve and a half minutes, using the teacher-only key. Error A claims equal energy for the same object. Return day 6 with a next step.

### Other rooms

No equipment or device is needed. Missing records: read R as invented for practice. No printer: copy notes spaces and the covered record onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No class records: read day 1's R, invented for practice. No printer: copy paper.

**Skip.** Public sharing and roster turns.

- **8 min. Return.** Read feedback; do [[s01-P5]].
- **10 min. Connect.** Use [[s01-K5]] aloud, pointing to the record.
  **Say.** "Why does cup motion matter?" Expect: energy passes at contact and changes motion; repeated greater effects support more available energy.
- **7 min. Notes.** Do [[s01-Pn5]] as separate short notes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal the cylinder record; [[s01-X5]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer public reports, keeping both partner turns, notes and exit. Longer: explain why one trial would be weaker evidence than repeats.

### Words and supports

Review motion energy, claim, evidence and reason. Transfer: energy passes between objects. Read records aloud. Offer a scribe for the child's own explanation. Rehearse the frame aloud; keep evidence notes separate.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 returns day 5's exit and evidence notes. If many show error A, use repeated marble effects in five-minute Return and repair. Then select complete tire explanations and match evidence, reserving written strengthening for the exit.
