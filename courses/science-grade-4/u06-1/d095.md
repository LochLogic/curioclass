# Day 95: What does the box show?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 93's actual s05-R93a/b readiness papers, not the unused parallel readiness prompts. Prepare next-unit readiness sheet feedback and all day 94 records. Read teacher-only keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Set light condition sheet and Light L, role and input cards and unit 6 formative record. Pretest light-box apparatus: flashlight illuminates only the toy, never the viewer; exclude stray light. Stage six groups of five. Keep the bat ticket private.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair: readiness

Return written tasks: s06-X94-model, s06-P94, s06-P94-parts, s06-P94-materials, s06-P94-sort, s06-P94-link.

Return day 94's ticket, initial-model Exit box and component records descriptively. Use day 93's illumination and reception notes separately. First five minutes: compare source-on/off with a clear view. If many day 94 ideas show error A, or readiness omits illumination, review grade 1's light shines on it: open eyes do not supply illumination.

Next five: review grade 1's eyes and ears sense, legs move. Match only eye/light and ear/sound role cards; distinguish receiving from movement. Last five: alone, [[s06-P95-ready]]. Ready children compare condition labels independently. Both gaps receive both moves; no brain mechanism yet.

#### 20 min. Box observations

Keep aisles lit. Adult handles flashlight, box, toy and opaque card below eye level. No sun, lasers or beams toward eyes. Dry apparatus has no splash/flying hazard; goggles unnecessary. Review opaque: blocks light, from day 18.

Six visits of three minutes plus two transition minutes. Each visit: one preparation minute, five 20-second individual looks, 20 seconds for the group record check. During each look adult presents source-on/clear, source-off/clear, source-on/blocked conditions. No speaking queue. Alone, [[s06-P95]]; waiting children prepare separate labels, [[s06-P95-labels]].

If unclear, retain that reading; check stray light once within the turn. Then read Light L aloud as invented for practice. No kit uses L from the start, marked supplied. Do not invent light levels.

Within each preparation minute, connect source-to-toy and toy-to-eye as separate proposed links. Reflect means light bounces off; eyes receive object light. In each final 20-second group check, **Ask.** [[s06-P95-change]] Expect changed visibility with view unchanged. Locate supporting observations. No flawed-claim repair practice or complete drawing.

#### 5 min. Exit E95

Reveal [[s06-X95]]; say it is invented for practice. Alone on paper. One challenging observation supports a changed claim. No hints. Collect all condition records, component responses and tickets. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; describe missing evidence separately. Error A emits eye-light; error B omits illumination.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult switches off and stores flashlight, box, toy and card; wipes surfaces. Children file returned papers and clear walkways. Keep actual and supplied records distinguishable.

### Other rooms

No apparatus: read invented Light L, with every row marked supplied. No printer: copy its table and separate prompts; keep the exit covered. No child needs a device.

### At home

**Swap.** Without a pretested box, use invented Light L. Copy the table without a printer.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public talk.

- **10 min. Return.** Read readiness feedback and day 94's first ideas. Match eyes/light and ears/sound using [[s06-P95-ready]].
- **15 min. Conditions.** Adult changes box conditions safely, or reads supplied L. Child does [[s06-P95]] and [[s06-P95-labels]].
  **Say.** "What changed when only the source changed?" Expect visibility changed while the view stayed clear. Explain reflect as bounces off. Keep the separate labels.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Child answers [[s06-X95]] independently. Collect papers, then adult stores equipment.

### Shorter or longer

Reduce label rehearsal, never observation turns or the exit. Extend by identifying one condition the records kept unchanged.

### Words and supports

Reflect: bounces off. Review source, receiver and opaque. Read condition headings; enlarge the viewing record. A child may observe supplied records instead of approaching equipment.

### Tomorrow

After class, 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, reads all papers on the one formative scale. Return day 95's tickets, records and individual notes day 96. Repair errors A/B together for five minutes; recheck readiness receiving for five. Then investigate single mirror segments.
