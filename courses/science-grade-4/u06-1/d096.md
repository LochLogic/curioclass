# Day 96: Which way does light go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read today's teacher-only item keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv. Return day 95 papers and unit 6 readiness sheet feedback. Set light condition sheet and Light L, role and input cards, mirror segment cards and apparatus, and unit 6 formative record. Pretest separate mirror segments. Inspect acrylic edges; no glass. Arrange reflection onto a blank screen below eye level. Keep the star ticket covered. Use paper arrows if a safe reflection cannot be shown.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 95's tickets, condition sheets, component responses and individual notes. First five: [[s06-P96-repair]]. If many day 95 tickets show error A or B, review grade 1's light shines on it. Match the separate source and receiver labels to Light L; source-off changes illumination even with open eyes. Use toy records, never the bat claim again.

Next five: review grade 1's eyes and ears sense, legs move. [[s06-P96-input]] with eye/light and ear/sound cards rechecks day 93 readiness after day 95 response. No brain link. Ready children locate supporting conditions independently.

#### 15 min. Mirror segments

Review grade 1 day 44's mirror: turns the light. Review day 18's opaque: blocks light. A mirror may turn light while blocking transmission; those are different tests.

Three setup minutes plus six two-minute group visits. Adult controls flashlight and mirrors; five children each get a 15-second look at separate segments, leaving 45 seconds for reset and recording. [[s06-P96]]. Show M1 flashlight-to-mirror, M2 mirror-to-wall and M3 lit-toy-to-mirror separately. Never join a complete viewing chain. A visible spot supports a segment; light need not be visible in the air. **Ask.** Which way does this segment carry light? Expect toward its receiving surface.

Keep aisles lit; no sun, lasers or eye-directed beams. Intact dry apparatus has no splash/flying hazard. If the spot fails, mark unclear and use the invented paper card, labeled supplied. No angle measurements.

#### 5 min. Predict a segment

The planned light-path tool is unbuilt; paper is today's complete route. Use M2 alone. [[s06-P96-predict]]: turn the mirror card and move the outgoing arrow toward a different wall spot before showing the new arrangement. Do not insert a blocking card or practice a full path.

#### 5 min. Segment audit

Pairs revisit their single-segment response, not a complete drawing. Hear six children for 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes; public talk may be passed. Everyone submits paper. Accept direction consistent with the shown segment. If many reverse it, point from the supplying end toward the receiving end.

#### 5 min. Exit E96

Reveal [[s06-X96]] for independent drawing and explanation. The full mirror-view case and interruption are first seen now. Read neutrally; collect tickets and all component notes. Use one formative scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Error A reverses light; error I sends light through the opaque card.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult switches off the flashlight, stores mirrors and screens and wipes the table. Children clear dry paper arrows and aisles. Stop using damaged equipment.

### Other rooms

No kit uses the separate invented M cards throughout, marked supplied. No printer: draw one card at a time and copy prompts. Keep the full exit withheld.

### At home

**Swap.** Use paper M cards without mirrors or a flashlight. Adult handles any real apparatus below eyes.

**Skip.** Group visits and public speaking.

- **8 min. Return.** Child uses [[s06-P96-repair]] and [[s06-P96-input]] with feedback.
- **12 min. Segments.** Show M1, M2 and M3 separately; child does [[s06-P96]].
  **Say.** "Which way does this segment carry light?" Expect toward its receiving surface. A mirror turns light; it does not supply it.
- **5 min. Predict.** Child does [[s06-P96-predict]] with M2 only.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X96]]. Child draws and explains alone. Adult collects papers and stores equipment.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten audit discussion; preserve visits, prediction and exit. Extend with a second mirror direction on M2 alone.

### Words and supports

Review reflect, source, receiver and opaque. Use straight arrows without the geometry term. Offer tactile arrows, enlarged labels or faithful scribing.
