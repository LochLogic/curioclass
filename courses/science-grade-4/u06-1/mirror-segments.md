## Five-minute paper fallback

Use M1, then M2 separately.

Rotate the mirror card. Move the outgoing arrow to the new wall direction.

Leave M3 separate. Never join these into the held star path.

Teacher: demonstrate a single segment, then repeat its direction with a paper arrow.
Six two-minute visits plus three setup minutes fit fifteen minutes.
The five-minute paper extension changes mirror direction, not card interruption.
Adult uses intact acrylic mirrors, screens and flashlight below eye level.
No sun, glass or lasers. Store equipment during five-minute cleanup.
