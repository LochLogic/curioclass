# Section 6.1: Light reaches the eye

Days 94 to 98.

## Science and evidence

Modeling connects reflected light, seeing and cause and effect. Light must reach a dull object, then enter an eye. A mirror may redirect illumination or redirect a view; distinguish these uses. An open view alone cannot provide illumination. Eyeshine reflects incident light; complete darkness means no visual seeing. No retina, colors or cell mechanisms.
