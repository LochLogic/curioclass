# Day 97: Where does the shine come from?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare cat picture sequence, light condition sheet and Light L, and unit 6 formative record. Read teacher-only keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv, plus Source U6-cat and U6-shine background notes. Keep the raccoon ticket and student Exit box covered. Return day 96's tickets, component records and individual notes. Preserve dated models; have second colors ready. No live animals, eye-directed lights or required pet stories.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return written tasks: s06-P96, s06-P96-repair, s06-P96-input, s06-P96-predict.

Return all day 96 papers. If many day 96 tickets show error A, use five minutes on source-on/off toy records and separate source/eye labels. Review grade 1's light shines on it; eyes receive light. No complete mirror redraw.

Use five minutes for error I, [[s06-P97-repair]]. Review day 18's opaque: blocks light. Contrast clear and blocked viewing rows while illumination stays on. Children mark the changed condition. Ready children locate supporting rows. Do not reuse the star as teaching.

#### 10 min. Eye-shine evidence

Return to day 94's cat puzzle. Say the sequence is invented for practice. Supply the checked background in our words: cat eyes reflect incoming light. Some returns toward a viewer near the light source. A flash supplies that light; the eye does not make it. This explains reflected shine without adding eye anatomy.

Review reflect: bounces off, from day 95. Children do [[s06-P97-flash]], keeping the supplying flash label separate from returned-light label. **Ask.** What supplies the returning light? Expect the flash. Point to the supplied account as background, not a classroom measurement. If many show error C, distinguish supplies from reflects with the separate labels. Do not rehearse the reserved shine-and-zero-light account.

#### 15 min. Condition comparison

Use Light L and its three cards, read aloud as invented for practice. [[s06-P97]]. L1 changes source-on, L2 source-off, L3 blocked-view. Alone classify, then compare in pairs. Keep actual day 95 observations marked observed; L is supplied. Label visibility as recorded; an unseen traveling light path is inferred.

Explain that dim means some light is available; complete darkness means none. Review grade 1's seeing needs illumination: an open view alone cannot supply it. Children revisit separate condition labels, without an animal prediction or integrated drawing.

#### 5 min. One revised link

Review grade 3's second-color routine: keep original observations and first answers. [[s06-P97-link]]. Each revises or confirms one earlier arrow with a located observation. Pairs take two 45-second turns, simultaneously across the room; teacher hears a rotating six during work, 4.5 minutes maximum. Everyone submits paper; public talk may be passed.

#### 5 min. Exit E97

Reveal [[s06-X97]] and the separate Exit box [[s06-X97-dark]]. Say the scene is invented for practice. Two and a half minutes each, individually. The raccoon case is first seen now. Collect both plus component records. Error C makes shine; error B predicts seeing without illumination. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen; missing evidence gets descriptive feedback.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy practice labels and keep both independent prompts hidden until exit. No projector: show drawn cat panels. No equipment experiment is required.

### At home

**Swap.** Box revisions without another color. Copy pictures and prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Group discussion and speaking rotation.

- **8 min. Return.** Child does [[s06-P97-repair]] using condition records.
- **10 min. Shine.** Read the cat background; child does [[s06-P97-flash]].
  **Say.** "What supplies the returning light?" Expect the flash. Eyes reflect incoming light.
- **7 min. Conditions.** Use invented L for [[s06-P97]]. Child completes [[s06-P97-link]] with one dated arrow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X97]] and [[s06-X97-dark]]. Child answers alone. Collect both without coaching.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten pair comparison, keeping both repair slots and exits. Extend by identifying which single condition could test a revised arrow.

### Words and supports

Review reflect, source and opaque. Dim has some light; complete darkness has none. Read records aloud. Accept enlarged diagrams, tactile arrows, faithful scribing and home-language explanations.

After class, read all papers: 30 times 60 seconds, 30 minutes, using the one formative scale. Return day 97's tickets, Exit boxes and individual notes day 98. Repair errors B/C for five minutes each, then connect separate light labels to evidence for a visual explanation.
