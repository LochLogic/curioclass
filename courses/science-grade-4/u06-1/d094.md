# Day 94: A cat at night

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare the cat picture sequence, question board and unit 6 formative record. Read teacher-only keys in items.csv and wrongs.csv for today's rows. Cover the Exit box and hold the fox ticket. Send family.md. Put day 93's revised assessment paper, student written task, repair record and s05-D93 with private notes in folders before class. Retain unfinished unit 5 needs privately. No readiness answers today.

### The day

#### 10 min. Cat hook

Keep day 93's promised new anchor. Show the original cat sequence; say it is invented for practice. Read its two compelling questions before explanations. Alone, [[s06-P94]]; pairs compare visible changes. Separate a pictured notice from a proposed cause. No pets, bodies or family experiences requested. Preserve uncertainty about seeing in complete darkness.

#### 8 min. Parts and notices

Use an invented room-lamp, wall and observer scene, separate from the cat. Review grade 1's words: makes its own light; light shines on it. Introduce source, object and receiver beside separate labels. Alone, [[s06-P94-parts]]. Keep labels apart; no complete path drawing. If many reverse receiver and source, point to what supplies light without completing their seeing models.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review grade 1 day 43 and this year's day 18: lets light through, lets some through, blocks light. [[s06-P94-materials]], using fresh material pictures. If many miss, match the thick card to blocks light. Review day 16's source: where transferred energy comes from; light carries energy. This does not establish seeing mastery.

#### 14 min. Question board

Review grade 3's question board: group notices, retain unanswered questions. Pairs do [[s06-P94-sort]], sorting supplied questions rather than composing the reserved exit response. Put seeing and hearing headings on the board. Connect notices to these headings. Hear six children, 45 seconds each, within five minutes; others finish their individual records. Public talk may be passed. All thirty hand in notice records, including children not heard.

#### 5 min. First links

On the unrelated wall scene, show one arrow from lamp toward wall. [[s06-P94-link]]. **Ask.** Which end receives this light? Expect the wall end. Show the arrow before adding the direction word. No full seeing drawing or eye-emission correction today. The new light-path model begins with one directed segment; initial complete models stay tentative.

#### 5 min. Exit E94

Reveal the fox ticket [[s06-X94]] and student Exit box [[s06-X94-model]] together. Two minutes for one question; three for the initial drawing. Neutral reading or faithful scribing only. Collect both. Keep initial ideas descriptively, without correctness marks. Missing evidence stays missing; error A records only explicit eye-produced light.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy separate practice prompts and keep the fox scene covered. No projector: draw the cat panels from the original sequence. No apparatus needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy pictures and prompts without a printer.

**Skip.** Public talk and the class speaking rotation.

- **8 min. Notice.** Read the invented cat sequence. Child does [[s06-P94]].
  **Say.** "What visibly changed?" Expect a head turn or shine during the flash, without a required cause.
- **8 min. Parts.** Child does [[s06-P94-parts]] and [[s06-P94-materials]]. Keep labels separate.
- **9 min. Sort.** Use [[s06-P94-sort]] and [[s06-P94-link]]. Point from the lamp toward the wall.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X94]] and [[s06-X94-model]] together. Child answers alone. Save both first ideas without correcting them.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten board discussion; keep the hook, first link and independent exit. Extend by separating an observation from an explanation in a notice.

### Words and supports

Source supplies light; receiver takes it in. Read labels aloud; offer tactile arrows, enlarged pictures and a scribe. Public sharing is optional.

### Tomorrow

Read all records after class: 30 times 40 seconds, 20 minutes. Return tickets, Exit boxes and component records day 95. Day 95 responds to day 93's seeing and sensing readiness, including explicit error A from today's first ideas, before box observations. No initial-model correctness score.
