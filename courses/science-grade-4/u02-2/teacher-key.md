# Section 2.2 private keys

TEACHER ONLY. Read privately in Before class. Keep these keys off child desks and displays.

Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the formative record. Blank work gets a conference, not an error letter. Return notes with the next named ticket repair.

## s02-X20

Connect the free lamp terminal to the other cell terminal, making a complete loop through the lamp. Current carries stored battery energy to light.

Wrong B: One lead from one terminal completes the path.

## s02-X21

Draw a complete loop through both cell terminals and the target buzzer. Silence the nearby buzzer; compare target sound with its path complete and interrupted. Hearing sound near another sounding buzzer cannot establish the target current path.

Wrong B: One lead is enough to power this buzzer.

## s02-X22

The intact wire still completes the path when the parallel switch opens. Put the switch in a gap in that wire, so opening interrupts the loop.

Wrong L: A switch across an uncut wire must control the lamp.

## s02-X23

Pedaling supplies motion energy converted by the generator to electrical energy. Current carries energy to lamp and speaker; light to cloth; heat from warmer casing to cooler clip; sound to the separate receiver. The generator creates no energy.

Wrong A: The generator creates energy without an input.

## s02-X20-wire

Independent wiring on an unfamiliar safe terminal layout, both cell terminals through the lamp; actual output and safe disconnection. Record failed equipment, assistance and missing turns explicitly.

Wrong B: One terminal is enough.
