# Day 20: A path that lights

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest rated parts; inspect insulation. Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key separately. Print Individual wiring log and Day 20 exit ticket. Retrieve day 19 papers and Surface temperature record. Prepare Circuit teaching fallback. List five children per set; with one set, assign ten today, ten tomorrow, ten day 22. For sunlight retry, check allergies and prepare Light and warming kit. Record equal whole-degree starts before matched sun/shade exposure.

### Safety and set-up

Low-voltage holders only; never outlets or bare battery connections. Keep the bulb in the loop. Disconnect before changes and after tests. Adult disconnects warm parts immediately. Eat nothing; wipe tables and hands. Retry: adult handles cloth; no sun viewing, lenses or reflectors.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 19's ticket and all practice: s02-P19-1, s02-P19-repair, s02-P19-model and any s02-P19-retry. Review return and repair. Do [[s02-P20-repair]] using yesterday's record. If many tickets show error A, locate the warmer input; a reversed direction alone gets a separate conference. Many means more than ten of thirty. Others retain original observations and label their evidence.

#### 5 min. Teach and protect

Teach terminal: a connection point; circuit: a complete conducting loop; current: electric charge moving along a path; stored energy: energy kept for later transfer. Children read side 2 definitions and use them while describing the demonstration. Review unit 1 motion energy beside stored cell energy. Adult traces an intact cell-lamp loop and demonstrates safe disconnection. Review day 16 energy arrows: battery toward output, distinct from circuit lines. Energy does not return unchanged to the battery.

#### 15 min. Wiring turns

Turn to side 2 for Individual wiring log: [[s02-P20-1]]. Six sets: five two-minute turns per set within fifteen minutes. Stagger thirty 30-second adult checks across all fifteen minutes; others prepare disconnected. Adult checks safety and initials personal manipulation. Children draw their tested complete loop. One set: ten ninety-second turns plus five setup/reset minutes use Comparison's first five minutes. Children without a turn remain not yet seen.

Failed output: disconnect; adult checks once, retries within turn. If unresolved, print/say invented record C from Circuit teaching fallback. Keep it separate from observations; it establishes no wiring performance.

During the same turn, reveal [[s02-X20-wire]] with a fresh safe terminal layout. Record independent action, output, disconnection and assistance on Individual s02-X20-wire record. These thirty 30-second checks use fifteen minutes above, not another round. Return each performance note the following day; one-set turns continue through day 22.

#### 10 min. Comparison

Pairs each take one 45-second turn citing their log's output and input. Teacher checks labels. If many omit input, return to stored cell energy. Retain the log with feedback due day 21.

With six sets, missing sunlight replaces this block: [[s02-P20-retry]] on side 1, comparing retained Surface temperature record. Review nearest-mark reading from grade 3. Adult shares recorded starts and reads finishes, one degree Celsius per mark, matching contact positions. Unresolved readings stay unclear. One-set rooms defer retry to day 23. Missing evidence carries forward.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 20 exit ticket, [[s02-X20]], exactly as printed, including its one-terminal drawing. Four minutes alone; one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 30 seconds, 15 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on record.md. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 21.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect and count parts. Adult stores holders and shades retry cloth. Children wipe tables/hands; collect papers.

### Other rooms

No sets: use invented C; personal wiring remains missing. No printer: copy blank log and exact ticket drawing, revealing the ticket only at exit.

### At home

**Swap.** No compatible circuit set: use invented C; wiring remains missing. No printer: copy frames and ticket. No safe sunlight or thermometer: defer retry.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 19 papers; do [[s02-P20-repair]].
- **15 min. Wire.** Use matched low-voltage parts in holders. Keep the lamp in the loop. Disconnect before changes. Child does [[s02-P20-1]]; observe [[s02-X20-wire]] during this turn. Without a kit, wiring stays unobserved.
  **Say.** "Where did the output's energy come from?" Expect stored battery energy carried to the lamp.
- **10 min. Compare.** Discuss the log, preserving actual results. Optional [[s02-P20-retry]] uses matched cloth and adult whole-degree readings. Disconnect and wipe hands before the ticket.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X20-wire]] is observed during work; no extra turn. Reveal [[s02-X20]] with its drawing. Child answers alone. Save all work for day 21 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner repetition, retaining every wiring turn and exit. Longer: trace another connection in the same tested loop.

### Words and supports

Review source, recipient, transfer and evidence. Current carries energy; the circuit is its conducting path. Read aloud; allow motor assistance, labeling it honestly. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 returns today's papers. Error B, one terminal called complete, needs tracing the physical loop. Trace stored-energy transfer to a buzzer's sound. Preserve any sunlight gap for the next eligible retry.
