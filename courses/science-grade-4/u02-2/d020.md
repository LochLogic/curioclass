# Day 20: A path that lights

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest rated parts; inspect insulation. Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key separately. Print Individual wiring log and Day 20 exit ticket. Retrieve day 19 papers and Surface temperature record. Prepare Circuit teaching fallback. List five children per set; with one set, assign ten today, ten tomorrow, ten day 22. For sunlight retry, pre-expose matched cloth from Light and warming kit as on day 18; adult checks allergies before handling.

### Safety and set-up

Low-voltage holders only; never outlets or bare battery connections. Keep the bulb in the loop. Disconnect before changes and after tests. Adult disconnects warm parts immediately. Eat nothing; wipe tables and hands.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 19's ticket and all practice: s02-P19-1, s02-P19-repair, s02-P19-model and any s02-P19-retry. Review return and repair. Do [[s02-P20-repair]] using yesterday's record. If many tickets show error A, locate the warmer input; a reversed direction alone gets a separate conference. Many means more than ten of thirty. Others retain original observations and label their evidence.

#### 5 min. Teach and protect

Introduce terminal, a battery connection point; circuit, a conducting path through both terminals and the bulb; current, electric flow carrying energy. Stored energy is available in the battery before light appears. Review unit 1 motion energy beside this stored input. Adult assembles an intact loop, tracing through battery and bulb. Review energy arrows from day 16: battery toward output, distinct from circuit lines. Energy does not return unchanged to the battery. Model safe disconnection before touching connections.

#### 15 min. Wiring turns

Turn to side 2 for Individual wiring log: [[s02-P20-1]]. Six sets: five two-minute turns per set, ten minutes, plus five for checks/resets. Adult checks before connection; initials only personal manipulation. Others predict and record independently. Each child draws only their tested complete circuit, never a missing-lead puzzle. One set: ten two-minute turns continue through Comparison's first five minutes. No turn remains not yet seen.

Failed output: disconnect; adult checks once, retries within turn. If unresolved, print/say invented record C from Circuit teaching fallback. Keep it separate from observations; it establishes no wiring performance.

#### 10 min. Comparison

Pairs each take one 45-second turn citing their log's output and input. Teacher checks labels. If many omit input, return to stored cell energy. Retain the log with feedback due day 21.

With six sets, missing sunlight replaces this block: [[s02-P20-retry]] on side 1, comparing retained Surface temperature record. Review nearest-mark reading from grade 3. Adult reads matched starts/finishes, one degree Celsius per mark, same exposure time/contact position. Unresolved readings stay unclear. One-set rooms defer retry to day 23. Missing evidence carries forward.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 20 exit ticket, [[s02-X20]], exactly as printed, including its one-terminal drawing. Four minutes alone; one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 30 seconds, 15 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on record.md. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 21.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect and count parts. Adult stores holders and shades retry cloth. Children wipe tables/hands; collect papers.

### Other rooms

No sets: use invented C; personal wiring remains missing. No printer: copy blank log and exact ticket drawing, revealing the ticket only at exit.

### At home

**Swap.** No compatible circuit set: use invented C; wiring remains missing. No printer: copy frames and ticket. No safe sunlight or thermometer: defer retry.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public sharing.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 19 papers; do [[s02-P20-repair]].
- **15 min. Wire.** Adult follows classroom safety. Child does [[s02-P20-1]], or labels C as supplied evidence.
  **Say.** "Where did the output's energy come from?" Expect stored battery energy carried to the lamp.
- **10 min. Compare.** Discuss the log, preserving actual results. Optional [[s02-P20-retry]] uses matched cloth and adult whole-degree readings. Disconnect and wipe hands before the ticket.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X20]] with its drawing. Child answers alone. Save all work for day 21 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce partner repetition, retaining every wiring turn and exit. Longer: trace another connection in the same tested loop.

### Words and supports

Review source, recipient, transfer and evidence. Current carries energy; the circuit is its conducting path. Read aloud; allow motor assistance, labeling it honestly. Public talk may be passed.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 returns today's papers. Error B, one terminal called complete, needs tracing the physical loop. Trace stored-energy transfer to a buzzer's sound. Preserve any sunlight gap for the next eligible retry.
