# Day 23: Adding the current path

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Separate transfer cards, Complete circuit comparison cards, Circuit teaching fallback and Day 23 exit ticket. Retrieve day 22 papers, crank record/model and observation logs. No circuit or crank apparatus today. For the final sunlight retry, use Light and warming kit; check allergies. Record matched whole-degree starts before equal sun/shade exposure. Retrieve Surface temperature record.

### Safety and set-up

Only a sunlight retry uses apparatus. Adult reads safe surfaces; never touch-test warmth or look at the sun. No lenses, lasers or reflectors. Adult shades/stores cloth and wipes thermometer within retry time. Nothing eaten.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 22's ticket, later s02-X20-wire performance notes, s02-P22-1, s02-P22-repair and s02-K22-1. Review return and repair; do [[s02-P23-repair]] on the retained correctly installed switch drawing. If many tickets show error L, trace that correct switch in its gap; never rehearse the exit's bypass repair. Many means more than ten of thirty. Others annotate their tested state/output evidence.

#### 5 min. Generator bridge

Review day 22's turning-handle example beside the retained crank account. Both inputs supply motion, converted by the generator to electrical energy. Current carries energy to light; none is created. Review unit 1 motion energy, day 20 stored cell energy and circuit lines. Distinguish the battery's stored input from the crank's motion input. Explain from Source U2-generation without claiming anyone saw inside the sealed device.

#### 10 min. Separate-mode retrieval

Use side 1. Pairs do [[s02-P23-1]] using Separate transfer cards, one observation at a time. Review day 17 sound, day 18 light and day 19 heat. Point to actual class logs; a card's observation is not a new measurement. If a live record is missing, mark it missing. Circuit teaching fallback C is invented for practice, announced aloud; it supports reasoning only.

Four minutes here: trace displayed Complete circuit comparison cards and label the current path orally. These correct layouts replace the unavailable light tool. If many omit input, locate the cell before tracing output.

#### 10 min. Evidence comparison

Partners each take one 45-second turn comparing current with one earlier mode, using the card and log for [[s02-P23-1]]. Talk is unjudged. Children complete their paper labels.

Final sunlight retry replaces this block: [[s02-P23-retry]] on side 2, comparing Surface temperature record. Five minutes: adult shares recorded starts and reads finishes at matching positions, nearest whole-degree marks. Review grade 3 thermometer reading. Record unclear honestly; keep supplied rows separate. Five minutes: shade/store cloth and wipe thermometer, hands and tables. Carry missing live warming to day 38.

#### 10 min. Anchor revision

Review second-color revision, preserving originals and initials. Use side 2. Do [[s02-P23-model]]. Hear a named rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes; anyone may pass. Teacher draws proposed current-path changes on the familiar crank model; earlier heat/light/sound branches remain evidence-tagged. Circuit lines form a complete loop; energy arrows connect input to output. Everyone writes one evidence label in the remaining five minutes. Do not claim source-based internal parts were directly observed.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 23 exit ticket, [[s02-X23]], exactly as printed. Children revise the model for this new account on the slip: four minutes alone, one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 90 seconds, 45 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per component. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 24.

### Other rooms

No projector: draw the familiar model on the board. No printer: copy blank labels/cards and reveal the exact exit at its turn. No sunlight: preserve missing live evidence for day 38.

### At home

**Swap.** No projector: use the retained model. No printer: copy cards and ticket. No sunlight equipment: preserve the gap. No second color: add a symbol beside revisions.

**Skip.** Public turns and unavailable tool.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 22 papers; do [[s02-P23-repair]].
- **10 min. Retrieve.** Do [[s02-P23-1]], using earlier logs.
  **Say.** "What supplied each output?" Expect a source-to-recipient account supported by the named log.
- **15 min. Model.** Do [[s02-P23-model]]. Expect motion into generator, electrical energy carried to light, with evidence cited. Optional [[s02-P23-retry]] replaces ten minutes; include five for adult readings and five cleanup. Keep the brief model label.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X23]]. Child answers alone. Save all papers for day 24 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce comparison repetitions; retain model label and exit. Longer: support an existing familiar-model branch with another retained record.

### Words and supports

Review current, circuit, generator, motion energy, transfer and source/recipient. Read cards aloud; accept labeled arrows and dictated evidence. Keep public passing available.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 returns today's papers. Error A, generator energy created without input, needs tracing motion into conversion. Then retrieve design criteria and compare lid-alarm proposals with a materials budget. Carry sunlight gaps to day 38.
