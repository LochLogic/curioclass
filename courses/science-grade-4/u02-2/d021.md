# Day 21: Current to sound

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest rated buzzer polarity and voltage; inspect leads. Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Circuit teaching fallback, Complete circuit comparison cards, Individual wiring log and Day 21 exit ticket. Retrieve day 20 papers and Surface temperature record. Retain the lamp loop and personal-turn list. One set: select ten who missed yesterday. For optional sunlight retry, check allergies and prepare Light and warming kit. Record equal whole-degree starts before matched sun/shade exposure.

### Safety and set-up

Low-voltage holder only, never outlets. Keep a load in the loop. Disconnect before changes. Adult disconnects warm equipment immediately. Buzzers stay away from ears. No tasting; wipe hands and tables. Retry: adult handles cloth; no sun viewing, lenses or reflectors.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 20's ticket, personal s02-X20-wire feedback, s02-P20-1, s02-P20-repair and any s02-P20-retry. Review return and repair. Do [[s02-P21-repair]] on the retained tested drawing. If many day 20 tickets show error B, trace the physical complete lamp loop through both terminals. More than ten of thirty is many. Others locate the same route on their own drawing.

#### 5 min. Trace

Review day 20's circuit, current, terminal and stored energy. Adult traces a correctly connected buzzer loop without a missing-wire drawing. Only one buzzer sounds at a time. Review grade 1 sound off/on: sound is evidence, but another cause must be controlled. Adult establishes quiet before each test; children do not design a competing-source test. Current carries energy to the buzzer, which produces sound.

#### 15 min. Buzzer turns

Turn to side 2 for Individual wiring log: [[s02-P21-1]]. Six sets: five two-minute turns plus five checking/reset minutes. Each reconnects and logs complete/interrupted states; initial observed actions. Allow each group five quiet testing seconds per round; others stay silent. One set: ten ninety-second turns plus five setup/reset minutes use Evidence comparison's first five minutes. First observe the remaining day 20 performance, s02-X20-wire, on a fresh lamp layout for today's ten. Log on Individual s02-X20-wire record; return notes day 22. Record any missing buzzer test.

Failure: disconnect, adult checks polarity/compatibility once; retry within turn. If unresolved, print/say invented C from Circuit teaching fallback. It describes lamp evidence only; do not invent a measured buzzer result. Mark missing sound observation and wiring performance separately.

#### 10 min. Evidence comparison

Pairs each take one 45-second turn linking their observed states to energy transfer. If many repeat error B, finger-trace both terminals before labeling input/output. Three minutes: trace displayed Complete circuit comparison cards and compare with own log. The planned light tool is unavailable; these correct layouts replace it. No one-lead report or competing-buzzer design card appears.

With six sets, unresolved sunlight replaces this block, including cards: [[s02-P21-retry]] on side 1, comparing Surface temperature record. Review grade 3 nearest-mark reading. Adult shares recorded starts and reads finishes, one degree Celsius per mark, matching contact positions. Keep unclear and supplied rows separate. One-set rooms defer to day 23.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 21 exit ticket, [[s02-X21]], exactly as printed. Four minutes alone; one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for on record.md. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 22.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect, count and inspect parts. Adult shades retry cloth. Collect records; wipe hands/tables.

### Other rooms

No apparatus: use C as lamp reasoning evidence; buzzer observation and personal manipulation remain missing. No printer: copy blank records and correct layouts; reveal exact exit only at its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No matched buzzer set: use the lamp record C and mark sound evidence missing. No printer: copy log, correct layouts and ticket. No suitable sunlight equipment: defer retry.

**Skip.** Group rotations and the unavailable tool.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 20 papers; do [[s02-P21-repair]].
- **15 min. Test.** Use matched low-voltage parts in holders. Keep the buzzer away from ears. Child does [[s02-P21-1]]; disconnect before changes.
  **Say.** "What changed when the connection opened?" Expect sound stopped if the apparatus worked; preserve actual failures.
- **10 min. Compare.** Trace correct layouts and discuss the log. Optional [[s02-P21-retry]] uses adult whole-degree readings. Disconnect and wipe hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X21]]. Child answers alone. Save all papers for day 22 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated oral explanations, retaining turns and exit. Longer: trace the same tested loop from its other terminal.

### Words and supports

Review source, recipient, current, circuit, stored energy and sound off/on. Read aloud; allow assisted connections with assistance recorded. A child unable to hear observes the adult's visual sound indication; do not mark unaided hearing.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 returns today's papers. Error B needs a complete physical loop; unsupported attribution of nearby sound needs quiet comparison. Then use a switch in a prepared gap and learn how motion supplies a generator. Preserve sunlight gaps.

Ours. Background: Source U2-circuit and Source U2-energy.
