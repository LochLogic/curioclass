# Section 2.2: Controlling current

Teach terminal, circuit, current and stored energy before children wire. A complete path joins both cell terminals through a load. Current carries energy to output; energy does not return unchanged to the cell. Teach generator conversion in day 22's final five comparison minutes; retrieve it on day 23. Correct-layout cards replace the unavailable tool. Six sets permit five two-minute turns inside fifteen minutes. One set reaches ten children per day over days 20 to 22; teacher tracks all thirty.
