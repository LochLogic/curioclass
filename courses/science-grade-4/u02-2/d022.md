# Day 22: Controlling the path

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest compatible parts, inspect leads and fold foil edges smooth. Prepare one switch in an existing gap per set. Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Individual wiring log, Circuit teaching fallback and Day 22 exit ticket. Retrieve day 21 papers, crank record/model and Surface temperature record. Identify remaining children needing personal turns. Optional Light and warming kit: prepare matched pre-exposed cloth; check allergies before handling.

### Safety and set-up

Use low-voltage holders only, never outlets. Keep a load in the loop. Disconnect before changes and after testing. Adult checks foil and disconnects warm equipment immediately. Keep buzzers away from ears. Nothing eaten; wipe hands/tables.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 21's ticket, s02-P21-1, s02-P21-repair and any s02-P21-retry. Review return and repair; do [[s02-P22-repair]]. If many tickets show error B, trace the physical loop through both terminals. More than ten of thirty is many. For unsupported sound attribution, recall yesterday's quiet before testing. Others label their own recorded evidence.

#### 5 min. Switch preparation

Review grade 3 switch on/off; children watched control then, but wiring is new here. Adult supplies smooth folded contacts in an existing gap, with no alternate route. Review terminal, circuit, current and stored energy from day 20. Joined contacts complete the path; separated contacts interrupt it. Model safe disconnection and one correct open/closed test. No bypass drawing or relocation problem.

#### 15 min. Switch turns

Turn to side 2 for Individual wiring log: [[s02-P22-1]]. Six sets: five two-minute turns, plus five checking/reset minutes. Each operates contacts and records state/output. Prioritize remaining children for disconnect/reconnect actions; initial only actual manipulation. Others record observations independently. One set: ten two-minute turns continue through Compare states' first five minutes. Check the three-day list: all thirty need a personal turn.

If output fails, disconnect; adult checks once and retries within turn. If unresolved, print/say invented C from Circuit teaching fallback. Supplied lamp evidence never establishes personal wiring. Preserve all missing opportunities.

#### 10 min. Compare states

First five: pairs each take one 45-second turn linking a tested state to output. One-set rooms use these minutes for remaining turns. If many cannot link a gap with output, trace the actual correctly placed switch.

Turn back to side 1. Last five, every route: **Ask.** [[s02-K22-1]]. Introduce generator: a device converting motion energy to electrical energy. Review motion energy from unit 1. Using Source U2-generation, trace handle motion, generator conversion, current, light. [[steps:s02-K22-1]] Review the sealed crank record; inside parts are a source-based explanation, not observed internals. Keep energy arrows distinct from circuit lines.

With six sets, sunlight retry replaces only the first five: [[s02-P22-retry]] on side 1, comparing Surface temperature record. Review grade 3 nearest-mark reading. Adult reads matched cloth starts/finishes at whole-degree marks, equal exposure/contact position. Preserve unclear readings. Cleanup supplies the retry's other five minutes. Keep generator teaching. One-set rooms defer sunlight to day 23.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 22 exit ticket, [[s02-X22]], exactly, including its uncut-wire diagram. Four minutes alone; one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 23.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect, count/store parts and collect switches. Adult shades retry cloth. Children wipe hands/tables; collect records.

### Other rooms

No sets: invented C supplies reasoning only; wiring remains missing. No printer: copy frames, generation question and exact held exit at its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe circuit set: use invented C; keep wiring missing. No printer: copy frames/ticket. No suitable sunlight equipment: defer retry.

**Skip.** Group rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 21 papers; do [[s02-P22-repair]].
- **15 min. Switch.** Adult prepares safe contacts in a gap. Child does [[s02-P22-1]]. Disconnect before changes.
  **Say.** "What changed when contacts separated?" Expect output stopped because the conducting loop opened, if the apparatus worked.
- **10 min. Explain.** Discuss actual results, then [[s02-K22-1]]. Expect motion input converted to electrical energy, never created energy. Optional [[s02-P22-retry]] uses adult whole-degree readings. Disconnect and wipe hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X22]] with its drawing. Child answers alone. Keep all papers for day 23 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated discussion, keeping all turns and generator teaching. Longer: trace the same actual loop starting elsewhere.

### Words and supports

Review switch on/off, circuit and current. Generator converts motion energy to electrical energy. Read aloud and accept labeled drawings or dictated reasons. Record motor assistance honestly.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 returns today's papers. Error L, treating a bypassed switch as controlling an intact route, needs tracing a correct switch in a gap. Rehearse generator conversion, compare transfer modes, and revise the familiar crank model. Keep sunlight gaps for the final retry.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Source U2-circuit and Source U2-generation.
