# Day 22: Controlling the path

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pretest compatible parts, inspect leads and fold foil edges smooth. Prepare one switch in an existing gap per set. Read section private keys and today's Teacher-only key. Prepare Individual wiring log, Circuit teaching fallback and Day 22 exit ticket. Retrieve day 21 papers and crank record/model. Identify remaining children needing personal turns.

### Safety and set-up

Use low-voltage holders only, never outlets. Keep a load in the loop. Disconnect before changes and after testing. Adult checks foil and disconnects warm equipment immediately. Keep buzzers away from ears. Nothing eaten; wipe hands/tables.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 21's ticket, later s02-X20-wire performance notes, s02-P21-1, s02-P21-repair and any s02-P21-retry. Review return and repair; do [[s02-P22-repair]]. If many tickets show error B, trace the physical loop through both terminals. More than ten of thirty is many. For unsupported sound attribution, recall yesterday's quiet before testing. Others label their own recorded evidence.

#### 5 min. Switch preparation

Review grade 3 switch on/off; children watched control then, but wiring is new here. Adult supplies smooth folded contacts in an existing gap, with no alternate route. Review terminal, circuit, current and stored energy from day 20. Joined contacts complete the path; separated contacts interrupt it. Model safe disconnection and one correct open/closed test. No bypass drawing or relocation problem.

#### 15 min. Switch turns

Use side 1 for Individual wiring log: [[s02-P22-1]]. Six sets: five two-minute turns plus five checking/reset minutes. Each operates contacts and records state/output. Initial observed actions. One set: ten ninety-second turns plus five setup/reset minutes use Compare states' first five minutes. First observe the remaining day 20 performance, s02-X20-wire, on a fresh lamp layout for today's remaining ten; record on Individual s02-X20-wire record. Return notes day 23. Preserve missing switch tests and unfinished personal turns.

If output fails, disconnect; adult checks once and retries within turn. If unresolved, print/say invented C from Circuit teaching fallback. Supplied lamp evidence never establishes personal wiring. Preserve all missing opportunities.

#### 10 min. Compare states

First five: pairs each take one 45-second turn linking a tested state to output. One-set rooms use these minutes for remaining turns. If many cannot link a gap with output, trace the actual correctly placed switch.

Turn to side 2. Last five, every route: **Ask.** [[s02-K22-1]]. Introduce generator: a device converting motion energy to electrical energy. Review motion energy from unit 1. Using Source U2-generation, trace handle motion, generator conversion, current, light. [[steps:s02-K22-1]] Review the sealed crank record; inside parts are a source-based explanation, not observed internals. Keep energy arrows distinct from circuit lines.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Reveal Day 22 exit ticket, [[s02-X22]], exactly. Four minutes alone; one collect. After class: thirty tickets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes; thirty practice packets times 45 seconds, about 23 minutes. Review shown, shown with help, not yet seen per look-for. Blank/unrelated work gets a conference. Return all papers day 23.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Disconnect, count/store parts and collect switches. Children wipe hands/tables; collect records.

### Other rooms

No sets: invented C supplies reasoning only; wiring remains missing. No printer: copy frames, generation question and exact held exit at its turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No safe circuit set: use invented C; keep wiring missing. No printer: copy frames/ticket. 

**Skip.** Group rotations and public turns.

- **5 min. Repair.** Return day 21 papers; do [[s02-P22-repair]].
- **15 min. Switch.** Adult folds foil edges smooth and prepares contacts in a gap. Keep a load in the loop. Child does [[s02-P22-1]]. Disconnect before changes.
  **Say.** "What changed when contacts separated?" Expect output stopped because the conducting loop opened, if the apparatus worked.
- **10 min. Explain.** Discuss actual results, then [[s02-K22-1]]. Expect motion input converted to electrical energy, never created energy. Disconnect and wipe hands.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s02-X22]] with its drawing. Child answers alone. Keep all papers for day 23 feedback.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reduce repeated discussion, keeping all turns and generator teaching. Longer: trace the same actual loop starting elsewhere.

### Words and supports

Review switch on/off, circuit and current. Generator converts motion energy to electrical energy. Read aloud and accept labeled drawings or dictated reasons. Record motor assistance honestly.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 returns today's papers. Error L, treating a bypassed switch as controlling an intact route, needs tracing a correct switch in a gap. Rehearse generator conversion, compare transfer modes, and revise the familiar crank model. Keep sunlight gaps for the final retry.
