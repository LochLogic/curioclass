# Day 112: Audit the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Return every day 111 paper, including plant repair notes and animal locators. Keep student Side 2 folded closed; hold diagram sheets and half slips until exit. Prepare actual flower records and source card U. If records are absent or unclear, use supplied Plant P, announced invented for practice. Preserve actual unclear entries.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-P111, s06-P111-inside, s06-P111-flower, s06-P111-roots, s06-P111-talk and s06-P111-coverage with their annotations. If many day 111 tickets show error F, spend five minutes on [[s06-P112-inside]], reviewing day 109's inside processing. Use five minutes for recurring error H from day 111's setup notes, [[s06-P112-roots]]. Review day 105's missing-root limit. Return all papers; missing functions receive descriptive feedback rather than an invented error letter. Keep Side 2 closed.

#### 15 min. Locate support

Read Plant A/B and Animal A/B separately from the claim evidence locator sheet. Begin [[s06-P112]], one valid claim at a time. Review observed/supplied labels from day 104 and relevant/sufficient evidence from day 105. Children locate dates or supplied card labels. Familiar flower parts and event cards remind them which flower labels were supplied. The animal part and reproduction cards remain separate sources, not observations of living young. No weak teaching argument is rewritten.

#### 10 min. Interaction audit

Finish [[s06-P112]] with one supporting interaction per claim. Review day 99's system-model for Animal B. Pairs take two simultaneous 45-second turns pointing to one connection. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes; log names. Children then do [[s06-P112-audit]] on their returned day 111 paper. Judge whether the underlined evidence supports that function; an organ list alone is not enough. Keep any missing-function note beside the original, without composing a replacement argument.

#### 5 min. Keep evidence

Children date and file their original models, labels, source tags and today's locator notes for day 113. **Ask.** [[s06-P112-question]] Expect a genuine open question, not an unsupported claim. Each child records one on today's page. Class measurements, supplied models and invented fallbacks retain separate labels. Collect today's annotations with the exit for feedback, then return them for the evidence folder.

#### 5 min. Exit E112

Reveal Diagram A/B, the half slip [[s06-X112]], and Side 2's [[s06-X112-animal]]. Announce invented for practice. Read each exact stem neutrally. Children repair each independently, using concise sentences or labeled links. This is their first sight of these claims. Error H overclaims rootless evidence; error F explicitly excludes inside parts. An unwarranted guarantee is error x. Collect both responses and practice papers; use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw and cover the held diagrams; copy exact exit stems only at exit. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy claims and diagrams if printing is unavailable. Use supplied Plant P for absent class records; say it is invented for practice.

**Skip.** Pair turns and group hearing.

- **5 min. Return.** Use [[s06-P112-inside]] and [[s06-P112-roots]].
- **15 min. Locate.** Read valid claims separately; child does [[s06-P112]] and [[s06-P112-audit]].
  **Say.** "Which observation supports this sentence?" Expect the dated flower entry, or a supplied card supporting that particular claim.
- **5 min. Keep.** Child records [[s06-P112-question]] and dates the evidence folder.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal diagrams, [[s06-X112]] and [[s06-X112-animal]]. Child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeat reports; preserve independent evidence audits and both exit responses. Longer: locate support for another valid claim already offered.

### Words and supports

Review internal/external, interaction, relevant/sufficient and observed/supplied. Read directions and enlarge diagrams. A faithful scribe may record the child's explanation. Fade frames; keep scientific links independent.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 75 seconds each, 37.5 minutes. Return every day 112 paper on day 113 in ten-minute Return and repair: error H five minutes, then F five. Include both exit responses and open questions. Day 113 joins the seeing, information and structure models to the two anchors.
