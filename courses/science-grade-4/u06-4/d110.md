# Day 110: Find support for a plant claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Prepare returned day 109 labels, route notes and exits. Open actual dated flower records and source card U. If observations are absent or unclear, use Plant P, labeled and announced invented for practice; keep actual unclear entries. Display only Card B from single-job plant cards. Hold the exit privately.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return s06-P109, s06-P109-inside, s06-P109-flower, s06-P109-route and s06-P109-young with their annotations. Return every day 109 paper. If many day 109 tickets show error F, use five minutes on [[s06-P110-inside]], reviewing yesterday's intake/inside processing. Use five minutes for recurring error G from day 108, [[s06-P110-flower]], reviewing stamen, pistil and ovules. Children without these errors annotate the same cards independently. Review the four life functions from day 105.

#### 5 min. Model an argument

Review grade 3's claim, evidence and reason: I think, because I saw, so. Provide a complete oral model using familiar records; assume no earlier unit's argument lesson. Read [[s06-K110]] aloud, then reveal [[steps:s06-K110]]. Point to each source as its step arrives. Read Card B's supplied bending response; do not present it as a class measurement. Keep the full argument oral, without a written four-function model.

#### 15 min. Single-claim workshop

Use the claim evidence locator sheet, Plant A and Plant B separately. Review relevant and sufficient evidence from day 105. Each child chooses one valid claim and does [[s06-P110]]. Keep the claim printed once on the locator sheet. Partners each take a simultaneous 45-second turn locating support, then annotate date or source label and observed/supplied. A label alone is insufficient: they must point to the supporting observation or source statement. Error H wrongly treats the rootless cutting as a root test.

#### 10 min. Evidence questions

**Ask.** [[s06-P110-talk]] Expect a matching observation or controlled comparison, with its limits, as in the key. Partners discuss once each, 45 seconds simultaneously, then add an evidence-needed note. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes; log names. Others check their own locator. Return to the same single claim when evidence is irrelevant; do not rehearse the exit brief.

#### 5 min. Exit E110

Reveal [[s06-X110]] on its half slip. Announce invented for practice. Each child writes a concise independent argument, using the new brief. Read neutrally without supplying links. Seek evidence-linked inside/outside interaction and four functions. Explicit outside-only or petals-only claims indicate errors F/G. Collect all papers; use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy valid claims and locator fields; conceal the exact exit until its turn. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy claims and fields without printing. Use supplied Plant P when class records are unavailable. Say it is invented for practice.

**Skip.** Pair turns and the rotating six.

- **5 min. Return.** Use [[s06-P110-inside]] and [[s06-P110-flower]].
- **5 min. Model.** Read [[s06-K110]] and its steps aloud.
- **15 min. Locate.** Child chooses one claim for [[s06-P110]]. Point to its date or source label.
  **Say.** "What evidence would strengthen that claim?" Expect a relevant observation or controlled comparison, as in [[s06-P110-talk]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X110]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated pair reports; keep the oral model, individual locator and exit. Longer: annotate the other valid claim separately.

### Words and supports

Review claim, evidence, reason, internal, relevant and sufficient. Frame: "The observation ___ supports ___ because:

____________________________________________________________" Read claims aloud; offer enlarged labels or faithful scribing.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes. Return all day 110 papers on day 111. Its fifteen-minute Return and repair gives errors F/G/H five minutes each: inside processing, flower cooperation and root-evidence provenance. Include H from locator notes. Day 111 develops animal evidence links.

### Credit

Ours. Sources U6-vegetative, U6-reproductive and U6-bending support the plant models.
