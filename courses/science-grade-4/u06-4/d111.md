# Day 111: Support one animal interaction

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Prepare every day 110 paper for return, including locators and evidence-needed notes. Display separate animal cards and familiar flower/setup diagrams. Keep the new exit brief hidden. Read claims aloud without supplying their links. No live animals or personal body comparisons.

### The day

#### 15 min. Return and repair

Return s06-P110, s06-P110-inside, s06-P110-flower and s06-P110-talk with their annotations, plus the s06-K110 evidence-location note. If many day 110 tickets show errors F/G/H, return all papers and give each letter five minutes. Error F: [[s06-P111-inside]], reviewing day 109's intake and inside processing. Error G: [[s06-P111-flower]], reviewing day 108's stamen/pistil jobs. Error H: [[s06-P111-roots]], reviewing day 105's missing-root limit. Include H from day 110 locators. Others complete the same separate annotations independently. Review relevant/sufficient evidence from day 105.

#### 10 min. Animal claim hearing

Review grade 3's claim, evidence and reason, as used yesterday. Read Animal A and Animal B from the locator sheet separately. **Ask.** [[s06-P111-talk]] Expect an explicit card label and supported interaction, not an organ list. Pairs choose one claim and take two simultaneous 45-second turns. Public talk may be passed. Point to supplied cards C/F after partners explain; a source label is not a substitute for reasoning.

#### 10 min. Written locators

Children complete [[s06-P111]] on the locator sheet, one claim at a time. Animal A connects intake and inside processing. Animal B connects receiving information, brain processing and action. Review day 99's system-model; cards supply the hidden brain role. Do not label brain processing observed from action order. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes. Record names while all others annotate source labels and interactions. For error F, reconnect one inside part.

#### 5 min. Feedback and coverage

Use [[s06-P111-coverage]] orally in pairs; each records a function and one gap. Review survival, growth, behavior and reproduction from day 105. A feeding interaction can support both survival and growth. It does not explain every function. Read only the separate reproduction labels to clarify a gap if needed. Children revise one locator, keeping its first version. Do not assemble a full written four-function animal model.

#### 5 min. Exit E111

Reveal [[s06-X111]] exactly as printed; announce invented for practice. Children independently write on the half slip. Read neutrally. Seek evidence-linked inside/outside interactions supporting all four functions. Error F is an explicit outside-only claim. Other missing functions receive descriptive feedback. Collect every page and locator. Record only shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy separate claims and fields; keep the exact exit concealed until its turn. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy claims, card labels and fields if printing is unavailable. Use only paper diagrams.

**Skip.** Pair turns and the heard six.

- **10 min. Return.** Use feedback with [[s06-P111-inside]], [[s06-P111-flower]] and [[s06-P111-roots]].
- **10 min. Claim.** Read Animal A/B separately. Child answers [[s06-P111-talk]].
  **Say.** "Which source supports that interaction?" Expect card C for digestion or card F for brain processing.
- **10 min. Locate.** Child completes [[s06-P111]], then [[s06-P111-coverage]]. Keep each claim separate.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X111]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated oral reports; retain repairs, individual source links and exit. Longer: improve the same locator's interaction label.

### Words and supports

Review inside/outside, brain, digestion, claim, evidence and four functions. Read labels aloud. Use enlarged cards, a faithful scribe or home-language paper explanation.

### Tomorrow

After class mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each, 30 minutes. Return all day 111 papers on day 112. Its ten-minute Return and repair gives error F five minutes and recurring error H five minutes, including missing-root notes. Day 112 audits evidence and missing interactions before fresh claim repairs.
