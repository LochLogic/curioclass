# Day 109: Parts working together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read teacher-only keys. Return all day 108 papers, including diagram labels, event order and seed labels. Display only separate organ symbols and familiar flower parts. Keep the exit private. Read card labels aloud; the independent response remains each child's. No live animals, eggs or personal body comparisons.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Return day 108's papers, including the written task, with their feedback inside this existing slot. Pick up day 108's flower-to-young question. If many day 108 tickets show error F, spend five minutes on [[s06-P109-inside]]. Review internal/external from day 104 and the ovule's job from day 108. For error G, use five minutes on [[s06-P109-flower]], reviewing day 108's pollen transfer, fertilization and later seeds. Everyone records; return feedback without naming children's errors publicly.

#### 10 min. Animal parts

Introduce skin, heart, lungs, stomach and intestine using separate animal-part cards. Read the supplied jobs, one at a time. Begin [[s06-P109]]. Skin protects. Teeth begin processing food; the stomach and intestine continue digestion. The intestine absorbs nutrients. Lungs exchange gases with blood; the heart circulates blood carrying resources. Review day 99's brain processing and system-model. These are source-supplied functions, not classroom observations of organs.

#### 10 min. Resource and information roles

Use [[s06-P109-route]]. Pairs choose one route; each takes a simultaneous 45-second turn explaining it. Public sharing may be passed. Hear a rotating six, 45 seconds each within five minutes; note names. Review day 105's survival, growth and behavior: resources can support both staying alive and getting larger. For error F, reconnect an intake label with inside processing. Do not combine routes into a complete animal account.

#### 10 min. Bird reproduction

Use reproduction cards, five minutes per model. Review grade 3's egg-to-young sequence. Chicken: the ovary supplies egg material; internal parts add contents and shell. Fertile means capable of development; contents nourish and shell protects. Not every egg develops. Nonhuman mammal: uterus, also called womb, holds young before birth; milk glands nourish after birth. Finish [[s06-P109]] and [[s06-P109-young]]. Review reproduction as producing young, from day 105. Keep these models separate from the feeding and information routes.

#### 5 min. Exit E109

Reveal [[s06-X109]], exactly as printed. Say the record is invented for practice. Children work independently on the half slip. Read neutrally; permit concise sentences or labeled links. Seek all four functions and interactions using the supplied record. Error F requires an explicit outside-only claim; omissions receive descriptive feedback. Collect all papers. Use only shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy separate labels and response spaces; reveal the exact exit at its turn. No device needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy organ labels onto paper instead of printing. Use diagrams, not animals or eggs.

**Skip.** Group hearing and pair rotation.

- **5 min. Return.** Use feedback with [[s06-P109-inside]] and [[s06-P109-flower]].
- **10 min. Parts.** Read the supplied cards. Child begins [[s06-P109]].
- **10 min. Routes.** Child does [[s06-P109-route]].
  **Say.** "Which inside part helps this job?" Expect inside digestion with food intake, or another supported route.
- **5 min. Young.** Read both reproduction models; finish [[s06-P109-young]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s06-X109]]; child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten repeated matching discussion, keeping both reproduction models and exit. Longer: annotate a second route already offered.

### Words and supports

Heart, lungs, stomach, intestine, skin, uterus, milk glands. Review brain, internal, external and four life functions. Enlarge diagrams; offer a faithful scribe or home-language explanation on paper.

### Tomorrow

Mark 30 sets at 60 seconds each after class, 30 minutes. Return every paper on day 110. Its ten-minute Return and repair holds five minutes for error F, plus five for recurring error G from day 108. Day 110 links plant claims to evidence.
