# Section 3.4: Comparing tested systems

Days 51 and 52 test two systems at fixed positions, quiet then conversation. Record accuracy as whole-message correctness, duration in whole seconds, code, condition and every sent/received message. Compare systems rather than ranking children. Criteria: five correct messages within thirty seconds. Near/far cases are reserved transfer, not a description of our classroom route.

One station, six teams of five. Each three-minute visit includes two five-message runs, at most thirty seconds each, and two minutes checking/reset. Eighteen minutes plus two contingency minutes fit the test block. Swap sender/receiver between systems; log actual roles. Everyone records, not everyone independently sends. Use private BEND/ROLL/PRESS sender-cards.md. Match message frequencies and keep positions, output and rules fixed. Permute private orders across systems and conditions. Receivers commit before seeing sent entries. A missed message stays a miss. Repeat one failed timing once within contingency, retain both readings, then use T separately. Round to whole seconds; obscured/borderline timing is unclear, not pass.

Pretest kit and route. Gentle taps/conversation, steady light aimed at cards; no shouting, rapid flashing, mirrors, lasers or sun viewing. Adult handles batteries. Clear walking path, no running. Cleanup five minutes: kit two, filing three. Paper cannot establish sound-system performance.

Print test-log.md or test-log-background.md, private sender-cards.md, test-fallback.md, comparison-notes.md, portfolio-codes.md, portfolio-feedback.md and reserved exits. Day 53 transfer is independent; component-only feedback follows, revision day 54. On day 55 identify earlier revised records for first scoring after class: wave eight, code twelve, engineering four points. Fresh diagnostics earn no points. No new build, interview or practical round.

Day 56 returns feedback/E54, uses four parallel repair packets for at most three five-minute moves per child, then individual revision and reflection. Teacher makes four three-minute visits plus three routing minutes. Next-unit readiness uses six independent paper minutes and four collection/sort; no erosion repair before day 59. Print next-readiness.md, revision-sheet.md and all four repair packets. Keep every remaining gap visible.

Every day lasts 45 minutes. Each child keeps individual paper evidence. Read directions neutrally; a scribe may record the child's own reasoning. Public talk is optional. Hear a rotating six at 45 seconds each within five minutes. Partner turns take 45 seconds each, not thirty serial turns. All papers, including those of children not heard, receive written component feedback on the named hand-back day. Many means more than ten of thirty.

Teach from observations before naming explanations. Label and announce every supplied record: invented for practice. Keep actual observations with collector, date and conditions; mark unclear readings. Retry once inside the existing observation slot, then use the named fallback separately. No simulation or paper record proves practical performance.

Before class, print only that day's student pages, its reserved exit sheet, and the teacher-only key. Keep future exits and portfolio transfer cases sealed. Never project the teacher-only key. Packets use 14 point type and at most 350 student words a day, including questions. Exits use half sheets. Day 50 has one square exit; its boundary diagnostic occurs during Assemble. Day 54 has one grid-rule exit; the pond model occurs during Anchor consensus.

Use the formative record's three categories. Preserve originals and second-color revisions. Assessment points go only on ../u03-1/assessment-record.md. No public grading or child used as a mistake example.

Use pond-model.md only during day 54 Anchor consensus. X54b alone is the exit. On day 55 print assessment-answer-key.md and ../u03-1/assessment-record.md for the adult. Review retest from unit 2 and consensus from unit 1.
