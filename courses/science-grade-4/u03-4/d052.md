# Day 52: Test with background conversation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Prepare day 51 exit and log feedback, including s03-P51-return. Pretest kit and timer. Retain yesterday's position marks and rule versions. Print today's integrated fields and separate exit. Keep future cases sealed. Set a gentle conversation source away from the receiver's work. No one shouts over it.

### Safety and set-up

One active kit; six teams of five. Adult services equipment. Gentle taps and conversational voices only; stop for discomfort. Keep ears back and walking route clear. No liquids, loose striking pieces or light handling. Five cleanup minutes include adult wiping/storage.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return day 51's exit, s03-P51-return and annotated s03-P51-log. Use [[s03-P52-return]] with a familiar actual run. If many, more than ten of thirty, show error C, review correctness before elapsed time; a fast wrong reception remains wrong. Others annotate their own quiet log. Preserve observations; do not fill missing data from memory.

#### 20 min. Background tests

Review day 51 condition, accuracy and duration. Review grade 3 fair test: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Change background conversation; keep positions, signal output, meanings and message frequencies fixed. Use a different private sender-card order for each run. Both systems still need five correct within thirty seconds.

Complete [[s03-P52-log]]. Six three-minute visits use eighteen minutes: two runs of at most thirty seconds, two minutes checking/resetting. Swap sender/receiver between systems and name actual roles. Everyone records. The receiver commits detected messages, with ? for unclear signals, before the private sender card is revealed. Familiar-sequence findings remain limited.

Start timer with first signal; stop at the final agreed message boundary. Record whole seconds. Interrupted or boundary-time readings are unclear. Use two contingency minutes for one retry, retaining both readings. If still unclear, use fallback T separately and say it is invented for practice. Never raise sound to force a success. Waiting teams check their own rule labels silently.

#### 10 min. Changed outcomes

Return day 51 annotated quiet logs beside today's records. Children mark actual changes for five minutes: each system's reception and duration, retaining unchanged outcomes too. Compare observed signal patterns and reception under both settings. A difference suggests a condition effect; this short trial cannot prove every cause. Hear a rotating six, at most forty-five seconds each, during the next five minutes. Others may pass publicly; everyone's paper receives feedback. Add condition-tagged evidence to the question board and pattern chart. No missing-record puzzle or near/far example.

#### 5 min. Cleanup

Adult counts, wipes and stores kit for two minutes. Children attach both-condition logs and file for three. Keep actual, unclear and supplied records separate.

#### 5 min. Exit E52

Reveal [[s03-X52]]. Children answer alone on half slips. Collect all logs and slips. After class allow thirty times thirty seconds for each set, thirty minutes total. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen by component; return on day 53.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy fields and reveal the exit only at its slot. No kit: interpret fallback T as invented, with practical evidence missing. No projection is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy fields. Without a kit, use fallback T, announced invented. Use supplied durations separately; do not time the child.

**Skip.** Team rotation and public reports.

- **5 min. Return.** Do [[s03-P52-return]] on the quiet record.
- **15 min. Tests.** Repeat both systems with gentle adult conversation; complete [[s03-P52-log]]. Keep positions and rules. Mark unmeasured durations unavailable.
- **10 min. Compare.** Mark changes from quiet. Put equipment away.
  **Say.** "Which outcome changed?" Expect a cited reception or duration change, or no detected change.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Reveal [[s03-X52]]; child answers independently.

### Shorter or longer

Shorten public reports, preserving all trials, written comparisons and exit. Extend by naming another condition that remains untested.

### Words and supports

Review condition, accuracy, duration and code key. Read tables neutrally; a scribe records the child's observations. Frame: "With conversation, our record shows ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 53 returns day 52's exit and logs. If error D extends one condition's success everywhere, use five Return minutes to limit the claim. Children write personal evidence notes and independently attempt the reserved transfer. No new test or pause-change demonstration occurs.

### Credit

Ours. Background: Source U3-patterns and Source U3-design.
