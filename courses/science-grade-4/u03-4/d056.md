# Day 56: Revise once and prepare for the next unit

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 55's assessment component notes and separate diagnostic observations. Assign at most three five-minute moves per child, earliest gap first. Prepare four private packet routes: waves, comparisons, shared rules, failure/change. Return E54 with the feedback. Use integrated fields and familiar correct evidence. Keep readiness covered and adult answers private.

### Safety and set-up

Paper work only; equipment stays stored. No soil, water or cloth experiment today. No personal stories are requested. Final four minutes include paper collection and sorting.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read feedback

Return day 55's assessment and s03-D55-wave, s03-D55-codes and s03-D55-picture with diagnostic observations. Return day 54 s03-X54a and s03-X54b. Separate portfolio points from formative categories. Read personal next steps; do not compare children's results. Review grade 3 second-color revision: preserve original observations and initials. Identify the first assigned move. Missing firsthand evidence stays missing.

#### 15 min. Targeted repairs

Four parallel routes; each child completes at most three five-minute moves. Visit each route for three minutes, twelve total, with three routing minutes. Children awaiting a visit use their familiar evidence. More than ten showing a gap gives that route priority, without adding a whole-class round.

Waves: [[s03-P56-A]] and [[s03-P56-B]]. Repair error A by separating local motion from crest travel; error B ties height to spacing. Review day 42 local motion, amplitude and wavelength, using cork records and separate correct teaching feature strips. Model a rest line, local cork arrow and separate onward energy arrow. Mark matching peaks separately; spacing need not determine height. If using fallback W, announce invented for practice.

Comparisons: [[s03-P56-C]] and [[s03-P56-D]]. Review day 49 accuracy/duration on complete actual logs: both must pass in each tested setting. Error C ignores correct reception; error D exceeds tested conditions. Label fallback T invented when used.

Shared rules: [[s03-P56-E]]. Review day 48 code key with day 50 vertical three-cell strips. Model top-first order and agreed color meanings. Error E omits agreement. No new square-grid case.

Failure/change: [[s03-P56-F]]. Review day 27 retest. Connect a recorded failure to a possible change; error F substitutes decoration. If none occurred, retain that fact and identify an untested setting. No new practical round or performance credit.

#### 10 min. Individual revision

Complete [[s03-P56-revision]] from personal feedback, preserving originals. Review grade 3 second-color revision. Revisit relevant research notes, logs and comparison notes. Record unresolved needs. This is the single response to day 55, with no portfolio rescoring. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen for today's work only.

#### 5 min. Reflection

Complete [[s03-P56-reflect]]. Each child marks evidence connected to a changed idea. Partners may each take one forty-five-second explanation turn; passing is allowed. No thirty-child oral round. Attach a revised evidence tag to the question board and pattern chart.

#### 10 min. Next-unit readiness

Reveal [[s03-R56]]. Announce invented for practice. Six independent paper minutes, four to collect and sort. Neutral reading only; give no answer or erosion repair. Record carrying or design-evidence gaps without unit 3 error letters. Preserve uncorrected readiness for day 59's response. After class allow thirty times forty-five seconds, 22.5 minutes, for selected repairs/revision feedback, plus thirty times twenty seconds, ten minutes, for readiness sorting. Written response notes return day 57 without an added repair; readiness waits until day 59.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy only selected move prompts and response fields; conceal readiness. A board can hold familiar correct strips. No apparatus or projection is needed.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy selected prompts. Mark revisions with a different pencil pattern.

**Skip.** Group rotation and public reports.

- **5 min. Feedback.** Read day 55 comments and both day 54 exit notes.
- **10 min. Repairs.** Use the assigned wave, comparison, shared-rule or failure prompts.
  **Say.** "Which familiar evidence helps?" Expect a relevant observation, code rule or complete trial record.
- **10 min. Revise.** Do [[s03-P56-revision]]; preserve originals.
- **5 min. Reflect.** Do [[s03-P56-reflect]].
- **10 min. Readiness.** Reveal [[s03-R56]]. Child works alone for six minutes. Collect without correcting; retain for day 59.

### Shorter or longer

Choose fewer repair moves, keeping individual revision, reflection and readiness. Extend revision by stating a remaining evidence need.

### Words and supports

Review local motion, amplitude, wavelength, code key, criterion and retest. Neutral reading and a scribe preserve personal reasoning. Frame: "I changed ___ because the evidence shows ________________________________________________."

### Tomorrow

Day 57 opens with canyon walls and a river, questions and an initial landscape model. Day 56 written feedback returns without a new repair. Keep readiness carrying/design-evidence gaps for day 59. Do not reteach erosion before the next unit's hook.
