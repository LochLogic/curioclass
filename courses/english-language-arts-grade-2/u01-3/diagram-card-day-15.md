# Diagram card and label cards (day 15)

The diagram of the article is printed large with the title and three rows. Beside it, print the reading frame: "Read the title. Read the labels. Follow the arrows. Say the order." Pairs trace each row with a finger and say "In the ___, pupils have ___ ."

Label cards: loose cards for the labels, the arrows and the shapes, so that pairs can take the labels off and put them back. A room with no printer can draw the diagram by hand on scrap paper.
