# Cycle 3 written check (day 15, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 11 to 14.

1. "Put the ____ of paper on the desk." (sheet)
2. "A ____ fell from the tree." (seed)
3. "It is a ____ day." (windy)
4. "The ____ is on the shelf." (teapot)

Tag wrong spellings: "shet" or "sead" (Q, spelled by sound, pattern not carried over); "wundy" (M, a short vowel read or spelled in place of the team); "windee" (Q, y at the end of a word). Record other spellings separately.
