# Exit cards, days 13 to 15

Only day 13 has a card. Days 14 and 15 reread an article part with its photograph or diagram (items.csv and each day page name it).

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Photographs are staged with no children; diagrams are drawn from the description. You read each card twice (day 13 once, then once more), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 13 (s01-X13).** "Pupils put question slips in a bag. The teacher reads a slip. Partners find its answer in a book." Ask: "Tell me the key ideas of the card in order."
