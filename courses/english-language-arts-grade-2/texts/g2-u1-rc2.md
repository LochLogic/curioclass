# Reading Card 2

*invented for practice*

Tess and Omar sweep the path by the rows. They put each leaf in a bin. They stop before the bin is full. Omar carries the bin to the heap. Tess checks that no leaf is on the path. They leave a note on the bin: "Fill the bin to the line." The next pair reads the note before starting. Now each pair knows how much to put in.

*Picture: A swept path between two garden rows, with a green bin beside it and a line marked near the bin's top.*
