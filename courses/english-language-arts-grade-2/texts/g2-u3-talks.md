- form: two invented model talks, spoken by the teacher (one good, one weak)
- word count: 58
- use: Day 109: the class builds the three-line chart of a good talk (a voice the back row can hear, whole sentences, facts from the source) by comparing the two talks; the delivery notes are acted by the teacher. Teaching only; not a check.
- days: 109

## Text

**Good talk (spoken clearly, to the back row, facing the poster and the listener).**
Hello, everyone. My poster is about the honey bee. A honey bee drinks nectar from a clover flower. Pollen sticks to her legs. When she visits the next clover flower, some pollen rubs off. That is how the bee helps the clover make seeds.

**Weak talk (spoken softly, looking at the floor, with gaps; the teacher says it is invented and nobody's real talk).**
Um. This is a bee. It has legs. Um. Flowers. Yeah. That is it.
