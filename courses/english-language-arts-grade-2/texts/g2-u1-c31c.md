# The Paper Tower

*invented for practice*

Nia's group set paper cups on the floor to build a tower. Their first tower fell when the top cup tipped. Nia looked at the bottom row. "These cups are too close together," she said. She spread the bottom cups farther apart and started again. Ben passed her one cup at a time. This tower stood while they counted all its cups. Nia drew the wide bottom row in her notebook so the group could remember what had changed.

*Picture specification: Two panels show the same group. In the first, a tower has fallen and two bottom cups touch. In the second, the tower stands on three bottom cups with a gap about one cup wide between each. This is a reading case, not a construction activity or a claim that every tower is steadier with wider spacing.*
