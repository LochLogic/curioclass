## Text

**Page 1.** Ned finds a map in the pocket of his coat.

*Picture: Ned, a boy, pulls a folded map from a coat pocket.*

**Page 2.** The map has a path, a red ribbon and a big X.

*Picture: The map shows a winding path, a red ribbon and a big X.*

**Page 3.** Ned grabs his wagon. The path runs past a pond and a shed.

*Picture: Ned pulls a wagon along a path beside a pond toward a shed.*

**Page 4.** The X is on the shed. Ned looks inside.

*Picture: Ned peeks into a shed.*

**Page 5.** An insect hums. A kitten naps on a sack. It has a button nose!

*Picture: A small kitten sleeps on a sack. A bug hovers near it.*

**Page 6.** Ned ties the ribbon on the kitten.

*Picture: Ned ties a red ribbon around the kitten's neck.*

**Page 7.** Ned sets the kitten in his wagon. It will impress Nan!

*Picture: The kitten sits in the wagon as Ned pulls it home.*
