## Text

**Page 1.** At dusk, Kit and Dad sit in the garden.

*Picture: Kit, a girl, and Dad sit on a bench in a garden at dusk.*

**Page 2.** A moth flaps along the path. It lands on a bloom.

*Picture: A pale moth lands on a pink flower beside a path.*

**Page 3.** The moth sips and sips. It is not asleep.

*Picture: The moth drinks from the flower, its tongue curled out.*

**Page 4.** Dad says, "Turn on the lamp."

*Picture: Dad holds up a small lamp.*

**Page 5.** Kit and Dad disagree. "A bright lamp can distract the moth," says Kit.

*Picture: Kit points at the lamp and the moth. Dad looks puzzled.*

**Page 6.** "It will not sip. I will defend the moth."

*Picture: Kit puts her hand between the lamp and the flower.*

**Page 7.** Dad grins. "I agree." They sit in the dark. The moth sips and sips.

*Picture: Kit and Dad sit in the dark garden; the moth is still on the flower.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).

## Sources

Written for Curio Class. One real-world fact: moths sip nectar from flowers at night, and bright artificial light can draw a moth away from flowers (so a lamp may distract it).
