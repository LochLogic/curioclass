## Text

**Page 1.** Rob is skipping. Skip, skip, skip!

*Picture: Rob skips along a path.*

**Page 2.** Ann is hopping. Can Rob beat her?

*Picture: Ann hops beside Rob.*

**Page 3.** Rob is running fast. Then he is tripping on a rock!

*Picture: Rob stumbles over a rock.*

**Page 4.** Rob is sitting on the path. "I was wrong to rush," he said.

*Picture: Rob sits on the path, looking down.*

**Page 5.** Rob stopped. He got up and went on, slower.

*Picture: Rob stands and walks on.*

**Page 6.** Finally, Rob has won! Even Ann is clapping.

*Picture: Rob at the end of the path; Ann claps.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
