## Text

**Page 1.** Dad has a stable. The gate is stuck.

*Picture: Dad stands by a closed gate at a small stable.*

**Page 2.** Rob and his robot run to the gate. A spider sits on the handle!

*Picture: A spider sits on the gate handle; Rob and a small robot look at it.*

**Page 3.** "A puzzle!" said Rob. The robot can tickle it. The spider hops off. "Do not sit on the gate again!" said Rob.

*Picture: The robot tickles the spider; it hops away.*
