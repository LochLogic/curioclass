## Text

**invented for practice**

At the outdoor fossil table on Tuesday morning, Lina wanted to read her fossil notes to the class. A gust lifted the loose pages. Lina caught them, but the wind kept blowing. She asked her teacher for a box. Then she put the pages inside it, where its sides blocked the wind. She took out one page at a time to read. At last the class heard all her notes without a page blowing away.
