# Reading Card 5

*invented for practice*

Kai and Rosa pick beans from the school garden vines. They put each pod in a basket. They stop before the basket is too heavy. Kai carries the basket to the school kitchen. Rosa checks that each pod is green. They leave a note by the door: "Pick only green beans." The next pair reads the note before starting. Now each pair knows which beans to pick.

*Picture: Green bean vines on a frame, a basket on the ground beside them with a few pods in it.*
