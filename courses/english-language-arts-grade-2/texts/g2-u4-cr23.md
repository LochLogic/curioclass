## Text

**Page 1.** Jade and Pete play pirates in the garden.

*Picture: Jade and Pete, two children, wear cardboard hats in a garden.*

**Page 2.** The gate is the ship.

*Picture: The children stand at a garden gate as if it were a ship's rail.*

**Page 3.** "Rotate the latch," says Pete.

*Picture: Pete points at the gate latch.*

**Page 4.** Jade rotates the latch. The gate swings!

*Picture: Jade turns the latch and the gate swings wide.*

**Page 5.** Pete can imitate a pirate. "Ho ho!" he yells.

*Picture: Pete puts on a gruff pirate face.*

**Page 6.** Jade creates a flag. She draws a flag on a rag.

*Picture: Jade draws on a cloth with a marker.*

**Page 7.** The pirates wave the flag. The gate is the best ship!
