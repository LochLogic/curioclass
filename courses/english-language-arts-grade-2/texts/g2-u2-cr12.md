## Text

**Page 1.** Dan has a mission in class. He must make a decision.

*Picture: Dan stands in a classroom, thinking.*

**Page 2.** The class has a collection of games. Dan will pick a game for his team.

*Picture: A shelf of games; Dan beside it.*

**Page 3.** Dan checks the first section. It has chess and cards.

*Picture: Dan looks at a shelf of chess sets and cards.*

**Page 4.** Dan checks the next section. It has a map game of the nation.

*Picture: Dan holds a board with a map.*

**Page 5.** "Which game would the class like best?" Dan asks.

*Picture: Dan taps his chin.*

**Page 6.** Dan asks the class. Ten hands go up for the map game! Dan made a fine decision.

*Picture: Children raise their hands; Dan grins.*
