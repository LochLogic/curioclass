# The Joyous Bus

- title: The Joyous Bus
- text_id: g2-u4-cr26
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable cycle 26 reader, 7 pages, one to three short sentences a page with a plain one-line picture description
- word count: 59 words (title and picture lines not counted)
- use: first reading after the cycle 26 patterns are taught (teacher reads page 1, pairs echo-read the rest); reread for accuracy, then smoothly; matched reader for spelling and sentences
- days: 150 (first reading), 151, 152, 153; reread through 157 as the matched reader
- decodable through: cycle 26
- high-frequency words used (taught by the first day): a, to, the, are, they, sing, and, an, of, is, in, yellow, by, says, it
- new patterns of this cycle: -ous: joyous, enormous, marvelous; -us: bus, crocus, bonus, Gus
- Title: read by the teacher (children read the pages only). The plan's title was The Curious Bus. It is changed because the plan title holds a word the plan reserves for a check; the new title holds none.
- Invented names, spelled with taught patterns: Gus.
- Earlier patterns (grade 1 and cycles 1 to 19, review): big, zips, park, children, at, patch, bloom, yells, white, bees, buzz, look, then, hop, on, trip, home.
- Reserved words (the unit plan's isolated, dictated and oral-passage words, the common irregular word of each cycle, and the dictation sentences) stay out of the body, the title and the picture lines. Said, does, people, again, their, could and would are not used. No word could not be made decodable.

## Text

**Page 1.** A big bus zips to the park.

*Picture: A yellow bus drives toward a park.*

**Page 2.** The children are joyous. They sing and sing.

*Picture: Children sing inside the bus.*

**Page 3.** At the park, an enormous patch of crocus is in bloom.

*Picture: A big patch of crocus flowers in a park.*

**Page 4.** "Marvelous!" yells Gus. "Yellow and white crocus!"

*Picture: Gus, a boy, throws up his hands.*

**Page 5.** Bees buzz by the crocus. "A bonus!" says Gus.

*Picture: Bees hover over the crocus flowers.*

**Page 6.** The children look and look.

*Picture: The children crouch beside the flowers.*

**Page 7.** Then they hop on the bus. It is a joyous trip home.

*Picture: The children climb back onto the bus.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
