## Text

In this imagined visit, a butterfly unrolls its long tongue into a narrow flower tube to reach nectar. The tongue tip touches the pollen-bearing part, and grains stick near the tip. At another flower of the same kind, the tongue tip touches the pollen-receiving part. Some grains stay there. The narrow tube puts the tongue tip against both parts.
