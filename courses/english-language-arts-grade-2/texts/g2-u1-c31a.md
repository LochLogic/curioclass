# The Welcome Card

*invented for practice*

At Cedar School, each new pupil receives a welcome card. One side shows a classroom map. The other side lists where to put a coat and where to find books. A class partner reads the card with the newcomer. Then both pupils walk the route shown on the map. The card stays in the pupil's desk so it can be checked again.
