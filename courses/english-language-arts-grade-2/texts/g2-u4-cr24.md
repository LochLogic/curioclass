## Text

**Page 1.** Jo and Dad plant seeds outside.

*Picture: Jo, a girl, and Dad drop seeds into holes in the soil.*

**Page 2.** They dig holes along the path and fill a flowerpot.

*Picture: Jo and Dad dig along a garden path next to a flowerpot.*

**Page 3.** Sunshine and rain help the seeds grow.

*Picture: Sun and rain fall over the planted path.*

**Page 4.** Soon a sunflower sprouts. It is tall!

*Picture: A green sprout with a tall sunflower stands by the path.*

**Page 5.** A bird splashes in the birdbath. A grasshopper hops on the path.

*Picture: A bird in a birdbath; a grasshopper on the path.*

**Page 6.** Jo and Dad go up to the hilltop.

*Picture: Jo and Dad climb a small grassy hill.*

**Page 7.** They see the sunflower path in the daylight. "The path is gold!" says Jo.

*Picture: A long path lined with gold sunflowers in bright daylight.*
