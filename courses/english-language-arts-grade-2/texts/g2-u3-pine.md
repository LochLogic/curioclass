## Text

**Card 1 (text).** Some trees, such as pine trees, do not need an animal to move their pollen. The wind carries it from tree to tree.

**Card 2 (picture and caption).** Picture: a pine tree in spring with a yellow cloud drifting from its branches. Caption: Wind carries yellow pollen away from a pine tree.

Print on each card: Curio Class original, invented for practice.
