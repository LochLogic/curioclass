## Text

Paragraph 1: An animal dies near a river. Mud slowly covers its bones. The mud protects them from wind and rain.

Paragraph 2: Water seeps through the mud. Tiny bits of mineral from the water fill spaces in the bones. Over a long time the bones turn hard like stone.

Paragraph 3: Much later, the rock above wears away. A walker may spot the fossil bone at the surface. A scientist then digs it out with care.
