## Text

**Page 1.** Ted and Meg were going on a trip. They would leave at six o'clock.

*Picture: Ted and Meg stand by a van with a map.*

**Page 2.** Ted picked a map, a lamp and two hats.

*Picture: Ted holds a map; a lamp and two hats on a table.*

**Page 3.** Mom lifted the bag into the van. Meg asked, "Did you get the snacks?"

*Picture: Mom lifts a bag into the van.*

**Page 4.** "Yes," said Ted. "I'd never skip snacks!"

*Picture: Ted grins with a snack in his hand.*

**Page 5.** The van left at six o'clock. Meg played a game with the map.

*Picture: Meg in the van, a map on her lap.*

**Page 6.** At last, the van stopped by a lake. Ted called, "We made it!"

*Picture: The van beside a lake; Ted waves from the door.*
