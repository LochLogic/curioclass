## Text

**invented for practice**

On Monday morning, Theo wanted to carry a full cup of water to the class plant. The cup was too full, and water splashed over the rim. Theo stopped and poured some out. Then he walked slowly, holding the cup with both hands. At last the plant had its water, and the floor stayed dry.
