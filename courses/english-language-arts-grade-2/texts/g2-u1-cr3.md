Teacher note. The title is read aloud by the teacher; children read the pages. The reader is made only from what children have been taught by day 12: grade 1 patterns (short vowels, blends, digraphs, the endings s, es, ed and ing) and the patterns of cycles 1 to 3, plus the high-frequency words named below. Map each high-frequency word on the sound-spelling-map and say it plainly; do not let children guess from the picture or the first letter. First reading follows the day's pattern teaching, then reread with feedback through the cycle. The people in the reader are invented. The reader keeps clear of every word reserved for a check, a probe or an exit in the plans (the Pattern Card, Spelling Card and Dictionary Card words, the dictated words of days 20 and 25, and the Reading Cards).

Words checked against the patterns taught by day 12:

- closed syllables with a short vowel, with grade 1 blends, digraphs and endings (review): sits, next, desk, on, it, runs, in, is, can, sit, asked, grins, yes, sinks, and, at, this, best, am
- silent-e (a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e): tale
- open syllables (he, me, we, she, go): he, me
- ee, ea and y as long e: green, beanbag, sleepy, reads, funny, peeks, happy
- invented names, spelled with taught patterns: dee, lee
- high-frequency words: a, to, the, i, said

# The Green Beanbag

**Page 1.** A green beanbag sits next to the desk. Dee sits on it.

*Picture: A big green beanbag by a desk. Dee, a girl, sits on it.*

**Page 2.** Lee runs in. He is sleepy.

*Picture: Lee, a boy, runs through the classroom door and rubs his eyes.*

**Page 3.** "Can I sit on the beanbag?" asked Lee.

*Picture: Lee stands by the beanbag and points at it.*

**Page 4.** Dee grins. "Yes! Sit next to me," said Dee.

*Picture: Dee moves over and pats the beanbag beside her.*

**Page 5.** Lee sinks in and sits next to Dee.

*Picture: Lee sits on the beanbag beside Dee.*

**Page 6.** Dee reads a funny tale. Lee peeks at it.

*Picture: The two children side by side on the beanbag, a book open between them.*

**Page 7.** "This is the best beanbag," said Lee. "I am happy."

*Picture: Lee smiles. The beanbag is soft and green.*
