# Reading Card 1

*invented for practice*

Pat and Kim water the school garden beds. They fill a small can at the tap. They stop before it spills over. Pat carries the can to the dry soil. Kim checks that water reaches each plant. They leave a note by the tap: "One small can for each bed." The next pair reads the note before starting. Now each pair knows how much water to use.

*Picture: Plants growing in a rectangular soil bed, with a can beside it.*
