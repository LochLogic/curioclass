## Text

**Page 1.** Barb has a cart. It is a big yellow cart.

*Picture: Barb stands at the edge of a park beside a big yellow cart.*

**Page 2.** Dad gets jars of corn and a fern for the cart.

*Picture: Dad loads three jars of corn and a pot with a fern onto the cart.*

**Page 3.** "Start!" said Dad. Barb turns the cart up the path.

*Picture: Barb pulls the cart up a path; Dad walks behind.*

**Page 4.** Bump! The cart hit a rock. A jar fell!

*Picture: The cart wheel hits a rock and a jar tips off.*

**Page 5.** Dad and Barb sort the jars. The corn is not hurt.

*Picture: Dad and Barb kneel on the path sorting the jars; the corn is whole.*

**Page 6.** "That is great!" said Barb. "We can go on."

*Picture: Barb smiles and points up the path.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
