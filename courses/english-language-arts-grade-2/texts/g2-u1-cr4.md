Teacher note. The title is read aloud by the teacher; children read the pages. The reader is made only from what children have been taught by day 17: grade 1 patterns (short vowels, blends, digraphs, the endings s, es, ed and ing) and the patterns of cycles 1 to 4, plus the high-frequency words named below. Map each high-frequency word on the sound-spelling-map and say it plainly; do not let children guess from the picture or the first letter. First reading follows the day's pattern teaching, then reread with feedback through the cycle. The people in the reader are invented. The reader keeps clear of every word reserved for a check, a probe or an exit in the plans (the Pattern Card, Spelling Card and Dictionary Card words, the dictated words of days 20 and 25, and the Reading Cards).

Words checked against the patterns taught by day 17:

- closed syllables with a short vowel, with grade 1 blends, digraphs and endings (review): has, it, is, but, will, up, shelf, with, cloth, sets, box, can, on, clicks, class, that, spot
- open syllables (he, me, we, she, go): so, she
- ee, ea and y as long e: reach, cheers
- igh, ie and y as long i: light, tiny, bright, my, high, dries, right
- invented names, spelled with taught patterns: kay, ray
- high-frequency words: a, i, put, said, the, by

# A Light Up High

**Page 1.** Kay has a light. It is tiny, but it is bright.

*Picture: Kay, a girl, holds a tiny lamp that gives off a bright glow.*

**Page 2.** "I will put my light up high," said Kay.

*Picture: Kay looks up at a high shelf on the wall.*

**Page 3.** Kay dries the shelf with a cloth.

*Picture: Kay wipes the shelf with a cloth.*

**Page 4.** Ray sets a box by the shelf so she can reach.

*Picture: Ray puts a sturdy box on the floor under the shelf.*

**Page 5.** She sets the light on the high shelf. Ray clicks it on.

*Picture: The lamp on the shelf. Ray, a boy, reaches to a switch.*

**Page 6.** The light is bright! The class cheers.

*Picture: The room is full of light. Children smile and clap.*

**Page 7.** "That is the right spot," said Ray.

*Picture: Ray and Kay look up at the shining lamp.*
