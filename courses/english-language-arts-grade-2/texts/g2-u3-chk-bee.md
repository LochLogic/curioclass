## Text

In this imagined visit, a bee clasps a flower and shakes its body very fast. The shaking makes the pollen-bearing part let go of its grains, and they fall onto the bee's belly. At the next flower of the same kind, the bee clasps and shakes again, and its belly touches the pollen-receiving part. Some grains stay on that flower. The shaking sends the grains to the belly.
