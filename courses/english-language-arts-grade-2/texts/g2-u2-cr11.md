## Text

**Page 1.** It is hot. Gus needs a cool drink.

*Picture: Gus fans himself on a sunny day.*

**Page 2.** Gus gets plums, a spoon and a big cup.

*Picture: Gus on a counter with plums, a spoon and a cup.*

**Page 3.** He mixes the plum juice. Oops! The juice drips on his suit.

*Picture: Juice drips onto a suit.*

**Page 4.** Mom says, "Do not fuss. It is true, a spill can be fixed."

*Picture: Mom pats Gus's shoulder.*

**Page 5.** They rub the suit clean.

*Picture: Mom and Gus rub the suit with a cloth.*

**Page 6.** Gus mixes a fresh cup. They sip the cool juice by the pool.

*Picture: Mom and Gus sit by a pool with cups of juice.*
