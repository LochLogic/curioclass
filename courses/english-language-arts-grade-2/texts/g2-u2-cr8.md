## Text

**Page 1.** Jill has a gold ring. Gran gave it to her.

*Picture: Jill wears a small gold ring; Gran stands beside her.*

**Page 2.** Jill is sad. "Where's my gold ring?" she said.

*Picture: Jill holds her bare finger, frowning.*

**Page 3.** She checks behind the bed. She checks in an old box.

*Picture: Jill kneels by a bed, then opens an old box.*

**Page 4.** "Hello, Jill," said Gran. "Why are you sad?"

*Picture: Gran steps into the room.*

**Page 5.** "It's weird," said Jill. "My gold ring is lost."

*Picture: Jill shrugs, upset.*

**Page 6.** Together, Gran and Jill check each sock. The gold ring is in the last sock!

*Picture: Gran and Jill hold a row of socks; a gold ring drops from one.*

**Page 7.** Jill hugs Gran. Gran is kind. Jill will hold the ring tight.

*Picture: Jill hugs Gran with the ring on her finger.*
