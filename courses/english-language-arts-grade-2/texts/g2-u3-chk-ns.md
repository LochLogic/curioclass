## Text

**New plants.** Successful pollination can help a flower make seeds. A seed can grow into a new plant. The new plant can make flowers.

**New food.** These flowers can provide nectar, a sweet liquid that feeds visiting pollinators. The visitors can help these flowers make seeds in turn.

Diagram (a picture to draw, arrows between four boxes): pollinated flower, arrow, seed, arrow, new flowering plant, arrow, nectar for a later visitor. The diagram crosses seasons; it does not show one insect's flight.
