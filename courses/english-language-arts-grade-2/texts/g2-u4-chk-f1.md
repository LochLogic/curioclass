**F1, Small Hops**

Sparrow wanted the cherries on the high branch. "It is too far," she said, and she sat down. Finch said, "Try one hop." Sparrow hopped to a low branch. She hopped again, and again. At last she reached the cherries. "Small hops carried me," she said.
