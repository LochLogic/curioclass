## Text

**invented for practice**

Jo and her class were looking at a fossil display with their teacher. In one stone, she noticed a leaf shape. She wondered whether the thin lines belonged to the leaf. She pointed them out. The teacher showed Jo the matching diagram and caption. Jo traced the lines in her own drawing, then wrote a label. At last she placed the drawing beside the stone so another reader could compare them.
