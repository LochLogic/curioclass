## Text

Mira's garden has a bean plant. Its flowers are small and white. A bee lands on one and drinks the sweet juice inside. Yellow pollen sticks to her fuzzy legs. The bee is careless about the pollen; she does not mind it. She flies to the next bean flower, and some pollen falls onto its sticky top. Soon that flower starts a bean pod.
