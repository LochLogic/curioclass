## Text

**Page 1.** A crack runs along the road.

*Picture: A long crack in a gray road.*

**Page 2.** A seed lands in the crack.

*Picture: A small seed drops into the crack.*

**Page 3.** Rain falls. The seed drinks.

*Picture: Rain drops on the crack.*

**Page 4.** A sprout pokes out. It is bent and comical.

*Picture: A bent green sprout pokes up from the crack.*

**Page 5.** Kit sees it. "It is not logical," she says. "A plant in a crack!"

*Picture: Kit, a girl, crouches to look at the sprout.*

**Page 6.** Kit tells the whole class. She is vocal.

*Picture: Kit points at the sprout while her class gathers.*

**Page 7.** "It is a miracle," says Kit. She grins.

*Picture: Kit smiles beside the sprout.*
