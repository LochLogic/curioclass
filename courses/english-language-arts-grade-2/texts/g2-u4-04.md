## The Lizard and the Gray Morning

**Narrator:** One morning in the valley, the sun did not come. The sky was gray, and the air was damp and cold. A thick mist lay over the grass. The animals gathered on the flat stones.

**Crow:** "The sun is angry with us! We must bring it gifts. I will gather shiny things and leave them on the hill."

**Squirrel:** "No, no! The sun is just asleep. We must make a lot of noise to wake it up. Everybody stomp and shout!"

**Tortoise:** "Neither one. The sun always comes back. We should sit still and wait."

**Narrator:** The crow brought shiny pebbles. The squirrel banged on a hollow log. The tortoise sat still. The mist stayed. A little lizard shivered on the flat stone. She was very cold.

**Lizard:** "Crow, I can't see why the sun would be angry. Squirrel, I'm not sure that noise reaches that far. Tortoise, I'm cold now. I want to see for myself."

**Narrator:** The lizard began to climb the tall rock at the edge of the valley. It was slow work, and the stone was wet. She climbed up and up. Soon the mist was thick all around her. Then she climbed out of it.

**Lizard:** "Oh! It is bright up here!"

**Narrator:** The sun was shining on the top of the rock. It had never gone away. The mist was only sitting low in the valley.

**Lizard:** "Everyone! The sun is here! Climb up and see!"

**Narrator:** The animals looked up. One by one they climbed. They felt warm light on their backs. Soon the sun warmed the mist, and it floated away. The whole valley was bright.

**Crow:** "I was wrong about the gifts."

**Squirrel:** "My noise didn't help either."

**Tortoise:** "Waiting was not enough. But I'm glad I waited near you."

**Lizard:** "I'm just glad I looked."
