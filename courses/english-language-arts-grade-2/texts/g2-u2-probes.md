## Text

**invented for practice.** Reserved: used only in the two-minute reading turn after each cycle, never for teaching. Give the reading purpose before the first reading; ask the question and check the target word after rereading.

**Cycle 6.** Mark went to the park with his cart. He put the cart under a tree. The dark clouds came, so he went home before the rain.

- Purpose: Read to find why Mark went home.
- Key: Clouds warned of rain.
- Target word to confirm: park

**Cycle 7.** Joy put dry soil in a pot. She set a seed in it. Then she poured water on the soil. She put the pot by the window so the seed could get light.

- Purpose: Read to find why Joy chose the window.
- Key: To give the seed light.
- Target word to confirm: soil

**Cycle 8.** The child saw a wild duck by the pond. He stayed back. The duck had room to walk to the water. The child was glad he had not blocked its path.

- Purpose: Read to find how the child helped the duck.
- Key: Stayed back and left its path clear.
- Target word to confirm: wild

**Cycle 9.** Kim was patting the wet clay flat. She made a small dish from it. Then she set the dish on a mat to dry. She did not lift it while it was soft.

- Purpose: Read to find why Kim left the dish on the mat.
- Key: It was soft and needed to dry.
- Target word to confirm: patting

**Cycle 10.** Max jumped over a puddle on the path. His bag fell in the water. He picked it up and checked inside. The bag was wet, but his book was dry.

- Purpose: Read to find what happened to the book.
- Key: It stayed dry inside the bag.
- Target word to confirm: jumped

**Cycle 11.** Sue drew a blue moon on her card. She gave the card to her friend. Her friend had missed the night walk. Now the card showed what Sue had seen.

- Purpose: Read to find why Sue gave the card.
- Key: It showed the moon the friend had missed.
- Target word to confirm: drew

**Cycle 12.** A fan set the paper boat in motion. It slid across the tray of water. When the fan stopped, the boat slowed down. Pat turned the fan on to move the boat again.

- Purpose: Read to find what moved the boat.
- Key: Air from the fan.
- Target word to confirm: motion
