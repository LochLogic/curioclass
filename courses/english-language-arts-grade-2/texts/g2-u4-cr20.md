# Pennies and Bunnies

- title: Pennies and Bunnies
- text_id: g2-u4-cr20
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: decodable cycle 20 reader, 7 pages, one to three short sentences a page with a plain one-line picture description
- word count: 74 words (title and picture lines not counted)
- use: first reading after the cycle 20 patterns are taught (teacher reads page 1, pairs echo-read the rest); reread for accuracy, then smoothly; matched reader for spelling and sentences
- days: 120 (first reading), 121, 122, 123; reread through 157 as the matched reader
- decodable through: cycle 20
- high-frequency words used (taught by the first day): in, a, the, to, and, i, have, his, he, they, we, go, says
- new patterns of this cycle: pennies, ponies, bunnies (consonant y to ies); days, plays (vowel y adds s)
- Title: read by the teacher (children read the pages only). The plan's title was Puppies and Pennies. It is changed because the plan title holds a word the plan reserves for a check; the new title holds none.
- Invented names, spelled with taught patterns: Tess, Ben.
- Earlier patterns (grade 1 and cycles 1 to 19, review): has, six, jar, at, farm, card, tells, deal, ten, pet, yells, need, digs, coat, grins, now, run, munch, hop, pets, with, will, back, on, rainy.
- Reserved words (the unit plan's isolated, dictated and oral-passage words, the common irregular word of each cycle, and the dictation sentences) stay out of the body, the title and the picture lines. Said, does, people, again, their, could and would are not used. No word could not be made decodable.

## Text

**Page 1.** Tess has six pennies in a jar.

*Picture: Tess, a girl, holds a glass jar with six pennies in it.*

**Page 2.** At the farm, a card tells the deal: "Ten pennies to pet the ponies and bunnies."

*Picture: A hand-lettered card hangs on a farm fence. Ponies and bunnies stand behind it.*

**Page 3.** "I have six," yells Tess. "I need ten!"

*Picture: Tess waves the jar and looks worried.*

**Page 4.** Ben digs in his coat. He grins. Pennies!

*Picture: Ben, a boy, holds up a fistful of pennies from his coat.*

**Page 5.** Now they have ten pennies. They run to the farm.

*Picture: Tess and Ben run down a dirt path toward the farm.*

**Page 6.** The ponies munch and the bunnies hop.

*Picture: Two ponies eat hay while bunnies hop in the grass.*

**Page 7.** Tess pets the bunnies. Ben plays with the ponies. "We will go back on rainy days!" says Tess.

*Picture: Tess strokes a bunny and Ben pats a pony.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
