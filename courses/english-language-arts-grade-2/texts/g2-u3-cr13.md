## Text

**Page 1.** Tess has a little table. One leg is too short.

*Picture: Tess looks at a small table with one short leg.*

**Page 2.** "We've got a plan," said Dad. "A block can fix it."

*Picture: Dad holds up a wooden block.*

**Page 3.** Dad sets a block under the leg. The table sits still.

*Picture: The block is under the short leg; the table stands flat.*

**Page 4.** "Can you guess?" said Dad. "I've fixed it!" Tonight, Tess will eat at her little table.

*Picture: Tess smiles at the table; a bowl is on it.*
