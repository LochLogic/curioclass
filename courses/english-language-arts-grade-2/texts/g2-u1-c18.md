# School Reading Partners

*invented for practice*

At Bridge School, pupils read with partners each Tuesday after lunch. A partner is someone who works with another person. The teacher pairs pupils at a low shelf. Each pair chooses a book. They read in turns so both can follow the print. If a word is hard, they look at its letters and blend the sounds. Then they reread the sentence to check that it makes sense. At the end, each partner tells one thing the book explains.
