## Text

**Cycle 20** (oral days 120 to 123; isolated words: babies, berries, open, said)

The babies sat by the open gate. May had two berries in a cup. She said, "These are for my snack." The babies had their own food. May put her cup on a high shelf.

**Cycle 21** (oral days 124 to 128; isolated words: away, defrost, robot, does)

A robot does one job in the kitchen. It can defrost cold food. Jan puts the food away when the job is done. Jan dislikes a messy table, so she wipes it before she eats.

**Cycle 22** (oral days 129 to 133; isolated words: chicken, lemon, music, people)

People hear music near the chicken coop. Len has a lemon in his bag. An impatient child asks him to open the bag now. Len says the fruit is for lunch. They sit and listen until lunch begins.

**Cycle 23** (oral days 134 to 138; isolated words: delicate, locate, paper, again)

We locate the paper box again. Inside is a delicate plate. We lift it with care. A short note says that the plate is for Gran. We put it back until she comes to visit.

**Cycle 24** (oral days 139 to 143; isolated words: backpack, sunset, tulip, their)

At sunset the children pack their backpack. They put a tulip drawing inside it. A classmate checks the side pocket for a notebook. Then they close the bag and walk home together before it gets dark.

**Cycle 25** (oral days 144 to 148; isolated words: medical, obstacle, silent, could)

A silent cart could carry the medical box. An obstacle blocks its path. Kim moves a chair out of the way. The cart can pass now. Kim pushes it to the room and leaves the box there.

**Cycle 26** (oral days 149 to 153; isolated words: curious, campus, basic, would)

A curious child would like a basic map of the campus. A teacher points to the path on the map. The child follows the path to the art room. There is a small cactus by its door.
