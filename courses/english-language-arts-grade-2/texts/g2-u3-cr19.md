## Text

**Page 1.** At dawn, Nan yawns. She runs to the garden.

*Picture: Nan, in pyjamas, runs into a garden at sunrise.*

**Page 2.** She sees a bee crawl on a flower. It has yellow dust on its legs.

*Picture: A bee crawls on a flower.*

**Page 3.** Nan will pause to see it.

*Picture: Nan crouches a little way off, still.*

**Page 4.** "I saw a bee!" said Nan. She draws it on a page.

*Picture: Nan draws a bee on a sheet of paper.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
