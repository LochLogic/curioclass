Teacher note. The title is read aloud by the teacher; children read the pages. The reader is made only from what children have been taught by day 7: grade 1 patterns (short vowels, blends, digraphs, the endings s, es, ed and ing) and the patterns of cycles 1 to 2, plus the high-frequency words named below. Map each high-frequency word on the sound-spelling-map and say it plainly; do not let children guess from the picture or the first letter. First reading follows the day's pattern teaching, then reread with feedback through the cycle. The people in the reader are invented. The reader keeps clear of every word reserved for a check, a probe or an exit in the plans (the Pattern Card, Spelling Card and Dictionary Card words, the dictated words of days 20 and 25, and the Reading Cards).

Words checked against the patterns taught by day 7:

- closed syllables with a short vowel, with grade 1 blends, digraphs and endings (review): hits, glass, and, in, on, grass, plan, gets, big, mat, it, has, tank, red, cab, at, last, stops, let, us, with
- open syllables (he, me, we, she, go): we
- two-syllable words built from known halves: cannot
- ai and ay: rain, stay, play, wait, paint, tray, train, gray
- invented names, spelled with taught patterns: jay, may
- high-frequency words: the, said, i, have, a, they

# The Rainy Day

**Page 1.** The rain hits the glass. Jay and May stay in.

*Picture: Two children, Jay and May, at a classroom window. Rain runs down the glass.*

**Page 2.** "We cannot play on the grass," said May.

*Picture: May points out the window at a wet, empty grass patch.*

**Page 3.** "Wait!" said Jay. "I have a plan."

*Picture: Jay holds up one finger and grins.*

**Page 4.** Jay gets paint and a tray. May gets a big mat.

*Picture: The two children carry a paint tray and roll out a large mat on the floor.*

**Page 5.** They paint a train on the mat. It has a gray tank and a red cab.

*Picture: A long painted train on the mat with a gray tank and a red cab.*

**Page 6.** At last, the rain stops. "Let us play with the train!" said May.

*Picture: The window is bright. The two children kneel by the painted train and play.*
