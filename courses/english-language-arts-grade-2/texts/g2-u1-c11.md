# The Class Sign

*invented for practice*

After lunch on Monday, Mira's new class made a sign for its book corner. Mira stayed by the table while the others chose paint. "I can print the small letters," she said. The paper curled as she began. Mira stopped and waited. Then she pressed tape on each corner. Mira finished the words. She moved her chair so Leo could paint the border. They carried the sign to the book corner together.

*Picture specification: Mira prints at a table with a long sheet of paper. Its far end curls up and tape rolls lie beside her. Leo paints at the side. The book corner is behind them. The image adds that the sheet is long and curls at the far end. Neither the scene nor this picture is taught before day 11.*
