## Text

**Paragraph 1.** Scientists measure fossils. A ruler shows how long a bone is. Measuring two bones tells which one is bigger. The numbers go in a notebook so others can check them.

**Paragraph 2.** Scientists clear loose dirt from fossils with soft brushes. A small brush is gentle on the fossil. Careful cleaning lets the scientist see its shape.

**Paragraph 3.** Every find gets a label. The label tells where the fossil was found. Later, a scientist can read the label and know the place.

Caption under a picture of a brush beside a bone: "A soft brush clears dirt from a bone."

Note: general science teaching, ours, kept consistent with the field practices on the pages below; the text never tells a child to collect fossils.
