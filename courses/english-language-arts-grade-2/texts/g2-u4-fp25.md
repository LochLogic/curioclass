## Text

Kit has a bird named Bo. Bo is vocal. Bo sings and chirps all day. Bo is comical and sweet. He hops and flaps on the rug. Kit reads an article on birds of tropical lands. It is logical that Bo likes the sun. A sunny day is a miracle for Bo!
