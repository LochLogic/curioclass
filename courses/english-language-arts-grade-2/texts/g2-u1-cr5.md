Teacher note. The title is read aloud by the teacher; children read the pages. The reader is made only from what children have been taught by day 22: grade 1 patterns (short vowels, blends, digraphs, the endings s, es, ed and ing) and the patterns of cycles 1 to 5, plus the high-frequency words named below. Map each high-frequency word on the sound-spelling-map and say it plainly; do not let children guess from the picture or the first letter. First reading follows the day's pattern teaching, then reread with feedback through the cycle. The people in the reader are invented. The reader keeps clear of every word reserved for a check, a probe or an exit in the plans (the Pattern Card, Spelling Card and Dictionary Card words, the dictated words of days 20 and 25, and the Reading Cards).

Words checked against the patterns taught by day 22:

- closed syllables with a short vowel, with grade 1 blends, digraphs and endings (review): and, tub, has, red, it, is, on, in, end, not, fast, but, can, grin, hit
- silent-e (a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e): glides
- ai and ay: sail
- oa and ow as long o: boat, slow, go, blows, floats, float
- invented names, spelled with taught patterns: mo, joan
- high-frequency words: have, a, said, the, to

# The Slow Boat

**Page 1.** Mo and Joan have a tub. Mo has a red boat. It is slow.

*Picture: Mo, a boy, and Joan, a girl, kneel by a tub of water. A small red boat floats in it.*

**Page 2.** "Go, boat, go!" said Mo.

*Picture: Mo leans over the tub and waves his hands.*

**Page 3.** Joan blows on the sail.

*Picture: Joan, a girl, blows on the boat's small paper sail.*

**Page 4.** The boat floats in a tub. It glides to the end.

*Picture: The boat sails across the tub toward the far end.*

**Page 5.** It is not fast. But it floats and floats!

*Picture: The boat sits calmly on the water while the two children watch.*

**Page 6.** "It can float!" said Mo.

*Picture: Mo has both hands in the air.*

**Page 7.** Joan and Mo grin. The slow boat is a hit!

*Picture: The two children smile at the little red boat.*
