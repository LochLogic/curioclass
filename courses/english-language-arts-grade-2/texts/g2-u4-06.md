Teacher note: 06-a is for beats and repeated lines; 06-b is for rhyme and alliteration, and it also has a repeated chorus. In both, the sound carries the meaning: the beat is steady like wings, and the repeated line is the poem's request. Clap the beats. No tune is fixed for the song; any steady tune with four beats a line will do.

## 06-a Room for Wings

Clover, clover, bloom and grow,
Little bees are on the go.
Room for wings, room for wings,
Room for all the flying things.

Petals open, one by one,
Butterflies drink in the sun.
Room for wings, room for wings,
Room for all the flying things.

Leave a leaf and leave a stem,
Safe and warm, a home for them.
Room for wings, room for wings,
Room for all the flying things.

Pot and seed and water, too,
Something small will visit you.
Room for wings, room for wings,
Room for all the flying things.

## 06-b The Garden Refrain (a song)

Bright bees buzz by the blossoms,
Busy, busy, bumping blooms.
Soft wings sail on the sunny breeze,
Sipping sweet in the flower rooms.

*Chorus:*
Plant a flower, plant a flower,
Plant a flower, let it grow.
Pollen, petals, pollinators,
Planting helps the garden glow.

Moths come in with moonlit manners,
Gentle, gliding, gray and white.
Hummingbirds hum, humming high,
Dip and dart in the soft twilight.

*Chorus*

Little leaf and little stem,
Tiny seeds in tiny rows.
Water gently, wait awhile,
Up the whole wide garden goes.

*Chorus*
