## Text

**Page 1.** Roy sees a toy boat in the shop. He needs it!

*Picture: Roy looks through a shop window at a toy boat.*

**Page 2.** But the jar has just a bit of cash.

*Picture: Roy holds a jar with a few coins at the bottom.*

**Page 3.** Roy gets a job. The people on his street pay him to pick up their sticks.

*Picture: Roy on a street; neighbors point to sticks on their lawns.*

**Page 4.** Roy picks up sticks round the house and down the road.

*Picture: Roy carries an armful of sticks past a house.*

**Page 5.** Roy drops the cash in the jar. Clink!

*Picture: Roy drops coins into the jar.*

**Page 6.** Now the jar is up to the top! Roy gets the toy boat.

*Picture: Roy holds the toy boat, grinning.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
