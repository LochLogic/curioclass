Teacher note. A poem for choral reading. The teacher reads it aloud first on day 2; children join in on the two refrain lines. On day 21 children reread it for expression (a pause at each line end, a lift on "together"). The poem follows a school day from the bell to home time, the same day the story and the articles show from other sides. It names no country and no child, so any class can read it as its own. Verse has no usable measure; its difficulty rests on the qualitative read (clear sequence, repeated lines, everyday words, a few sound-pattern words such as "bell", "tell", "chime", "time", "sun" and "run").

# Our School Day

Bell, bell, bell, the morning bell,
Feet on the floor and a tale to tell.
Hello to the teacher, hello to the door,
Hello to the friends who are here once more.

*That's our school day, that's our school day,*
*Together, together, all the way.*

We read, read, read, and the pages turn,
We find a new word and we learn, learn, learn.
We ask a question, we wait, we hear,
We say, "Tell me more," and the answers are clear.

*That's our school day, that's our school day,*
*Together, together, all the way.*

Spoons go clink, and the napkins fold,
We pass the fruit and the stories told.
We share a laugh and a joke or two,
And the hum of lunchtime hums right through.

*That's our school day, that's our school day,*
*Together, together, all the way.*

Out the door and into the sun,
Skip, skip, skip, and then we run.
Round and round and up and down,
Laughing and laughing, a happy sound.

*That's our school day, that's our school day,*
*Together, together, all the way.*

Pack up, pack up, the last bells chime,
Wave goodbye, it's going-home time.
Tomorrow we'll come back, the same and new.
We saved a place, a place for you.

*That was our school day, that was our school day,*
*Together, together, all the way.*
