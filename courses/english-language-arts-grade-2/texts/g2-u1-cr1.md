Teacher note. The title is read aloud by the teacher; children read the pages. The reader is made only from what children have been taught by day 2: grade 1 patterns (short vowels, blends, digraphs, the endings s, es, ed and ing) and the patterns of cycle 1, plus the high-frequency words named below. Map each high-frequency word on the sound-spelling-map and say it plainly; do not let children guess from the picture or the first letter. First reading follows the day's pattern teaching, then reread with feedback through the cycle. The people in the reader are invented. The reader keeps clear of every word reserved for a check, a probe or an exit in the plans (the Pattern Card, Spelling Card and Dictionary Card words, the dictated words of days 20 and 25, and the Reading Cards).

Words checked against the patterns taught by day 2:

- closed syllables with a short vowel, with grade 1 blends, digraphs and endings (review): sits, at, his, desk, has, pen, and, pad, is, glad, can, with, hands, grins, nods, yes, six, mix, hot, yum
- silent-e (a_e, e_e, i_e, o_e): writes, note, bake, cupcakes, take, cupcake
- open syllables (he, me, we, she, go): he, me, we
- invented names, spelled with taught patterns: sam, pete
- high-frequency words: a, you, the, to, i, said, are, they

# A Note for Sam

**Page 1.** Sam sits at his desk. He has a pen and a pad. Sam is glad.

*Picture: Sam, a boy, sits at a desk with a pen in his hand and a pad in front of him. He smiles.*

**Page 2.** Sam writes a note. "Pete, can you bake cupcakes with me?"

*Picture: The pad with a short note on it and Sam's hand holding the pen.*

**Page 3.** Sam hands the note to Pete. Pete grins and nods.

*Picture: Sam holds out a folded note. Pete, a boy at the next desk, takes it and nods.*

**Page 4.** "Yes! I can bake," he said. "We can bake at six."

*Picture: Pete talks to Sam. A clock on the wall shows six o'clock.*

**Page 5.** Sam and Pete mix and bake. The cupcakes are hot!

*Picture: Two boys at a table with a mixing bowl and a tray of small cakes.*

**Page 6.** They take a cupcake. Yum!

*Picture: The two boys each hold a cupcake and smile.*
