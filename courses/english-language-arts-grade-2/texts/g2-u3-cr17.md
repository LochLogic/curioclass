## Text

**Page 1.** Kim and Nick play in the garden. Kim will pitch. Nick will catch.

*Picture: Two children stand on grass with a ball.*

**Page 2.** Kim pitches fast. Nick catches it. "Faster!" said Nick.

*Picture: Kim throws; Nick holds out a glove.*

**Page 3.** Kim pitches her fastest pitch. The ball sails past Nick and lands on a bench.

*Picture: A ball flies over Nick toward a garden bench.*

**Page 4.** Nick runs to the bench. "Now I pitch," said Nick. "My pitch is slower, and Kim can catch it!"

*Picture: Nick picks up the ball by the bench.*

All pictures are original commissions (to draw).
