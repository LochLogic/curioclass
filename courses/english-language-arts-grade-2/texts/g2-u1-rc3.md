# Reading Card 3

*invented for practice*

Rob and Nia plant bean seeds in pots. They press one seed into each pot. They stop before the soil gets too wet. Rob carries the pots to the sunny sill. Nia checks that each pot has a seed. They leave a note by the sill: "One seed for each pot." The next pair reads the note before starting. Now each pair knows how many seeds to plant.

*Picture: A row of small pots on a sunny sill, each with a little green shoot or a seed beside it.*
