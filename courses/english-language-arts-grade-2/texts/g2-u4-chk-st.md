## Text

**Card S, Rocks in the Sun**

Add a rock or a log to a pollinator garden. Butterflies warm up in the sun, so set it in a sunny spot. A warm butterfly can fly on to the flowers.

**Card T, Lights Out for Moths**

Keep outdoor lights off when you do not need them. Bright lights confuse moths. Moths visit flowers at night, and lit places get fewer visits. Dark nights help moths find food.
