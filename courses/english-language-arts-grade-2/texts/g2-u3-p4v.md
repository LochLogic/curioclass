## Text

**Form 1, Above the Petal** (day 113)
I glide over the grass.
I swoop to the flower.
The petal feels warm.
The stone feels hot.

**Form 2, Over the Leaf** (day 114)
I drift above the leaf.
I dart to the bloom.
The water is cool.
The ice is cold.

**Form 3, Along the Stem** (day 115)
I creep along the stem.
I rush to the bud.
The rock is big.
The hill is huge.
