# Picture cards, days 126 to 128

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with the passage it goes with named in small letters at the foot (for example "The Garden Refrain, chorus"). These are drawings of the unit's own texts, not invented cases. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 126 (s04-X126v2).** card 1, children planting flowers one after another in a row; card 2, one child sitting still beside a bare pot.
- **Day 127 (s04-X127v2).** card 1, a whole garden growing up from tiny rows of seeds; card 2, a bare, empty field.
- **Day 128 (s04-X128v2).** card 1, the ant shutting her door; card 2, the grasshopper wrapping her wings while ants carry grain inside.
