# The poem and the song in two colours (days 126, 127 and 128)

Layout only; the words are the text g2-u4-06 (06-a Room for Wings and 06-b The Garden Refrain), printed unchanged.

- Print the rhyming words in one colour and the repeated lines in another, and underline them as well so colour is not the only clue.
- Leave a small beat mark above each stressed word in 06-a, so children can clap the beat.
- One copy a pair; keep 06-a and 06-b on separate sheets. Head both "made for our class".
