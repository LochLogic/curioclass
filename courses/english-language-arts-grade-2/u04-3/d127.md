# Day 127: Hear what rhyme and repeated sounds add to a song

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark today's six for the exit and the six for the oral card (children 19 to 24). Print 06-a and 06-b in two colors and hang the sound chart (beat, rhyme, repeat, same first sound). Copy the fresh passage, one a pair, and keep it face down until the foundations block. Write six word-pair slips: moon and spoon, seed and weed, sun and sand, tiny and tall, ring and king, sing and sweet. Say the song is made for our class.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Day 126's exit ticket was s04-X126v2. Read the chorus of 06-b again twice (1 minute). Write two answers side by side: "It repeats" and "It keeps asking us to plant, with a steady beat" (1 minute). Ask "Which one says what the words add?" and "What do the repeated words make you hear or see?" Chant the chorus twice together (3 minutes). Saying "it repeats" names the line and leaves out what it adds (error O).

#### 12 min. Read 06-b

Say the words pollen, petals, pollinators and refrain, and show each in the song (1 minute). A refrain is a part that comes back, like the chorus. Read or sing the song once through (2 minutes). The class sings the chorus together, and no child sings alone (2 minutes). Pairs read the verses by turns, one verse each (5 minutes). Then the class reads the whole song with one clap a beat (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Model

Mark two rhyme pairs on the printed song: grow and glow, white and twilight (2 minutes). Rhyme is words that end the same sound. Review: rhyme, words that end the same, ball and hall (kindergarten, unit 1, day 16). Then mark the beginning sounds in "Bright bees buzz by the blossoms": the b sound starts five words (2 minutes). Alliteration is words that start with the same sound. Say what each adds: rhyme ties the ends of lines together; repeated beginning sounds make the bees seem to buzz (2 minutes). Add rhyme and same first sound to the sound chart under "What it adds" in the class's words. Pairs sort the six word-pair slips into rhyme and same first sound, and say why each pair belongs there (4 minutes). Keys: moon and spoon, seed and weed, ring and king rhyme; sun and sand, tiny and tall, sing and sweet start the same.

#### 7 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "What does repeating a sound emphasize?" (3 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I would add ___" (3 minutes). Share two in the whole class (1 minute). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Independent: write the recount

On the strip from day 126, each child writes or draws first, next and last for 01 or 02 (12 minutes), then writes the frame "The lesson is ___ because ___." (4 minutes). Confer with the six heard during the last 4 minutes, about 40 seconds each; the rest read their strip to a partner. Look for four things: the events in order, a lesson that rests on one event, the child's own words, one sentence holding lesson and reason. A recount that ends with the lesson before the events is error A; a lesson with no event behind it is error B. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 7 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice the last verse of 06-b (1 minute): [[s04-X127v2]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X127bv2]] (90 seconds). Then reread twice the hummingbird line of verse 2 (30 seconds), and hear the same six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X127cv2]] (90 seconds). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Saying "they rhyme" names the sound and leaves out what it adds (error O); saying "they start the same" and stopping there is the same miss.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed to this part. Use it to retrieve: children write awake and defend on slates and tell a partner the meaning of de-. Review: de- means undo or take away (grade 2, unit 4, day 124).

#### 15 min. A fresh decodable passage

Hand out the passage (not the reader). Pairs read it aloud, one sentence each (4 minutes), then the class reads it once together (3 minutes). Confirm the meaning after decoding (RF.2.4c): who disagree, what each wants, why the dog matters (5 minutes). Any word guessed from its first letter is error Q: touch each tile, blend. Ask what Pam and Dan do at the end (3 minutes).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Children 19 to 24 read the cycle 21 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap (12 minutes). The other twenty-four use tiles, a sort and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column. A printed word misread is error AE.

#### 15 min. Spell on tiles

One word at a time, built and not listed: children build awake, along, defend and disagree with tiles, say where the weak uh sound sits and write each under its tiles (7 minutes). Cloze strips with a bank card of distract and disagree: "Dan will ___ the dog." and "Pam and Dan ___ about the dog." Pairs choose the word that fits each strip and spell it from the bank (4 minutes). Children compare each word with the pattern on the chart, circle the weak uh sound and fix a word if needed (4 minutes). Spelling awake as ewake is error R.

#### 7 min. Dictionary

Review: check a spelling in a dictionary entry: find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters, correct the draft (grade 2, unit 1, day 27). Each pair takes a word slip from the dictionary page, writes it from memory, finds the entry, compares and corrects (6 minutes, L.2.2e). Share two pairs (1 minute). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Ask: "Which part of defend is not stressed?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No color printing: underline the rhyming words twice and circle the same first sounds. No tiles: paper squares. No dictionary page: write the entries on the board. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed song: read the chorus from the board or from memory.
**Skip.** The dictionary and the word pairs.

- **10 min. Hear it.** Read the chorus aloud together, clapping one beat a word.
  **Say.** "Which two words rhyme?" Expect: grow and glow.
- **10 min. Starts alike.** Say "Bright bees buzz by the blossoms" slowly.
  **Say.** "What sound do those words start with, and what does it make you hear?" Expect: b, and it makes the bees buzz.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes awake and defend and says the part not stressed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X127v2]], [[s04-X127bv2]] and [[s04-X127cv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the song, the model, the exit and the passage. Longer: a pair finds a third rhyme pair in the song and says what it adds.

### Words and supports

Rhyme, refrain, pollen, petals, pollinators, same first sound, adds. Frames: "The words ___ and ___ rhyme, so ___." "The sound ___ is repeated, so ___." Say it in the home language first; clapping or pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 128 compares two tellings and rehearses voices, and begins by repairing today's exit if needed. Today's exit tells you who can say what rhyming words add: if many said only "they rhyme" (error O), day 128 opens with a five-minute repair, asking what the rhyme does to the pace or the link. The six heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
