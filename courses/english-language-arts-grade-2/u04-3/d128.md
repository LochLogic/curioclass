# Day 128: Compare two tellings and read each speaker in a voice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark the six for the exit and the oral card (children 25 to 30). Write six strips: The grasshopper sings all summer. The ant pulls a heavy grain and asks her to sing along. The cold wind comes and the grasshopper has nothing to eat. She asks to borrow grain and promises to repay. The ant does not give her food. The ant says, Now you can dance. Print the sheets. Say aloud that practice cards are made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Day 127's exit ticket was s04-X127. Read it twice (1 minute). Say the rhyming words slowly and ask "What do they do to the way the lines move?" (2 minutes). Write a right answer beside "they rhyme": they link the two lines and make the pace feel quick (2 minutes). Saying "they rhyme" names the sound and leaves out what it adds (error O).

#### 8 min. Reread

Pairs reread the grasshopper's request in 01 and in 02 aloud from the side-by-side print, one telling each, then say in one sentence how the asking is different (8 minutes). Walk and listen to one pair under their breath.

#### 12 min. Compare

Pairs sort the six strips into only in 01, only in 02 and in both, using the side-by-side print (5 minutes). Keys: in both, the grasshopper sings, the cold wind comes, the ant does not give food; only in 01, the ant pulls a grain; only in 02, borrowing with a promise, and "Now you can dance." Each pair says one shared event and one difference with the frame "Both ___. But ___." on the board (3 minutes). Review: you compared two versions with the frame Both ___. But ___. (grade 2, unit 4, day 122). Then show a comparison that leaves a difference out: "Both tellings have a grasshopper and an ant." Pairs mark what is missing and finish it with a difference from the strips (4 minutes). A comparison with no difference is error D.

#### 8 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "How does the ant sound different in the two tellings?" (3 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I would add ___" (3 minutes). Share three in the whole class (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Voices

Six groups of five take a short piece of dialogue: groups 1 and 2 the summer talk in 01, groups 3 and 4 the winter talk in 01, groups 5 and 6 the door talk in 02. Three read (narrator, ant, grasshopper); two give one tip on how each voice sounds and say the tip after the reading. Each group reads for 75 seconds, the class names one difference in the voices for 30 seconds, and groups change places for 15 seconds: 2 minutes each, 12 minutes in all. Say a voice may be quiet or loud, but never mocking, and never an accent or a costume. Error C is the same voice for both speakers: say what each wants first, then read again. In the last 8 minutes pairs fix their compare chart and the beliefs table with what the ant wants in each telling.

#### 7 min. Exit

Say aloud that the cards are invented. Read the first card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X128]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X128b]] (90 seconds). Then read the second card twice (30 seconds) and hear the same six, 15 seconds each, read one line aloud in the voice that fits: [[s04-X128c]] (90 seconds). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Naming a line both versions share as the change is error D; giving two characters one view is error C.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed to this part. Use it to retrieve: children write disagree and distract on slates and tell a partner what dis- means. Review: dis- means not or the opposite (grade 2, unit 4, day 124).

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 21

Hand out the check sheet. Read each part aloud as the items give it, five minutes each. Words: [[s04-A63]]. Sentence: [[s04-A64]]. Reference with the printed entry: [[s04-A65]]. Walk the room, give no hints, and mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep the sheets: the commonest miss goes into day 131's sentences part. The check words stay unseen until now.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Children 25 to 30 read the cycle 21 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap (12 minutes). The other twenty-four use tiles, the sort and the reader in two stations of twelve. Ask in order: [[s04-A56]] [[s04-A57]] [[s04-A58]] [[s04-A59]] [[s04-A60]] [[s04-A61]] [[s04-A62]]. A printed word misread is error AE.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws one sentence with a cycle word from the dictionary page (8 minutes), checks one spelling in the entry (3 minutes), and reads it to a partner (4 minutes).

#### 13 min. Close

Pairs read A Moth in the Garden once more for ease: first an echo reading with you, page by page (4 minutes), then partners read a page each (5 minutes), then the class reads the whole smoothly together (2 minutes). Ask "Which part of disagree is not stressed?" (2 minutes). Mark the heard column.

### Other rooms

No printed side-by-side print: read both tellings aloud from one copy and point to the strips on the board. No tiles: paper squares. Pointing is an answer; the check sheet may be answered aloud to a scribe.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strips: tell the two tellings aloud and say "same" or "different" for each event.
**Skip.** The written check sheet and the reader.

- **10 min. Two tellings.** Read the summer and winter talk in 01 and the door talk in 02 aloud, a different voice for each speaker.
  **Say.** "How does the ant sound different?" Expect: kind and firm in the first; cold and short in the second.
- **10 min. Same and different.** Say one thing that happens in both tellings and one that happens in only one.
  **Say.** "Both ___. But ___." Expect: Both have a cold wind; but only 02 has borrowing with a promise.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes disagree and says the part not stressed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X128]], [[s04-X128b]] and [[s04-X128c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the compare, two groups reading and the exit. Longer: a group reads its piece twice, changing the voice for the ant the second time.

### Words and supports

Version, same, different, both, only, voice, request. Frames: "Both ___. But ___." "In 02, the ant ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 129 is the first check: a new card, a recount, a lesson and two voices, plus the start of cycle 22. It begins by repairing today's exit if needed. Today's exit tells you who can say what changes between two versions: if many marked the agreeing pair (error D), day 129 opens with a five-minute repair, marking the one detail found only in one version. The check sheets tell you the commonest spelling miss for day 131. The six heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
