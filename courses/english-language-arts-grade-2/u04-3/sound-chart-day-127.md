# Sound chart (a wall chart, from day 127)

Four short rows headed Beat, Rhyme, Repeat and Same first sound, each with a picture clue (a hand clapping, two matching words joined by a line, a line printed twice, three words starting with one letter). To the right of each row, leave a column headed "What it adds", blank at the start. The class fills it in as each sound is met.

The headings are the devices the standard names; do not put the exit's lines on the chart.
