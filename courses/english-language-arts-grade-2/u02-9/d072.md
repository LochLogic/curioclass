# Day 72: One page for the whole class

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark each letter card with one mistake and each revised story with one editing mistake. Set up your device and the screen, or the large paper. Open the contribution record sheet with 15 pairs. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 6 min. Revision retry

Say: "This draft is made up." Write the feedback "Tell what Tess felt." and the draft "Tess saw a smudge. Tess cleaned it. Her bus was late." Children ring the sentence that does not answer the feedback (error V) and say so to a partner (4 minutes). Hand back day 70's assessment, the revisions; each child retries one revision from the feedback (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Editing retry

Say: "These words are made up." Hand back day 71's assessment, the edited stories, and the day 67 exit tickets (items s02-X67a and s02-X67b). Ask "Which is right?" for Pip's cap and Pips cap (error AG), then couldn't and couldnt (error AH), then ring the special name that needs a capital on one strip (error AE), 1 minute each. Each child retries one marked mistake on the story (2 minutes).

#### 4 min. Letter card retry

Hand back the letter cards. Show the commonest miss of errors AE to AH on one fresh strip (2 minutes). Each child retries one marked mistake on the card (2 minutes).

#### 4 min. Plan the piece

Say: "This slip is made up." Show a slip with a sentence, a picture and a number for its place. Children point to the sentence, the picture, then the number. Say: "Each pair brings one sentence and one picture from its story and agrees where it goes."

#### 30 min. Pairs choose and agree (digital)

Fifteen pairs, about 2 minutes each in rotation, come to you with their sentence and picture. Each pair gives one wording or placement choice, and the partners say "agree" or "I would change it" (item s02-A53). Type it and show it; children do not type. The other pairs arrange their strip and picture on paper and tell a partner their choice. Record each child on the contribution record sheet: shown (proposes and agrees), shown with help (after a prompt), not yet seen (watches, error AI). With no device, write on the large paper.

#### 6 min. Display and read

The class reads the typed piece aloud once.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X72 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AI.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six cards from yesterday's group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Reblend any wrong ending (error Y).

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from yesterday's notes, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, reread Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "The session has a map game."), partner reread, and tiles (build session, direction, decision, mansion). No oral queue today.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

Partners reread A Choice of Actions, a page each.

### Other rooms

No device: write the class piece on large paper while pairs call out words, and mark the digital part open on the record sheet. A child who cannot say a choice points to a card.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the typed page.

- **15 min. The assessment at home.** Your child picks one sentence and one picture from their story and says where each goes on a page.
  **Say.** "Where would you put the picture, and why?" Expect: a place with a reason, such as beside the sentence so a reader sees both.
- **10 min. A letter.** Your child writes a short letter to a friend with a greeting, a closing and commas.
- **5 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say session and direction. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X72]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision retry, the pairs and the exit. Longer: a child writes a caption for the class piece.

### Words and supports

Review: shared typing, show why (day 66); the talk routine. Frames: "I would put ___ here because ___." "I agree because ___." "I would change it because ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class record each child on the contribution record sheet. Day 73 repairs error AI if many missed the exit, then refines illustrations.
