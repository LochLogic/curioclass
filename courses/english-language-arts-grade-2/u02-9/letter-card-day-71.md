# Letter card (days 71 and 72)

An invented letter printed as lines for the child to correct. Print "invented for practice" at the foot, and say aloud that Quillwick is a name invented for practice.

dear sam
On arbor day, our class in cardiff saw a fossil under a quillwick lamp. The case is noras project. She didnt cover the label.
best wishes
Kai

Prompts, read aloud one at a time on day 71: the holiday, the place, the lamp name, the greeting, the closing, the owner of the project, and the short word for did not. Keep the card out of teaching. Day 72 returns it with one marked mistake to retry.
