# Day 71: Edit a story and a letter

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark each sentence card with one unfinished sentence, and write one editing note on each revised story. Write the four checklist lines on the board. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Revise the sentence card

Say: "These words are made up." Write "When Jo waited" and "Jo waited by the case." Read each aloud and ask: "Is it a whole thing?" Children answer together; add the missing part to the first (2 minutes). Hand back day 70's assessment, the sentence cards; each child fixes the marked sentence (3 minutes). Tag error AD.

#### 20 min. W.2.5 editing

Hand out the editing checklist card and the revised stories with your notes (item s02-A45). Each child edits alone, one read for each checklist line (8 minutes), then trades with a partner, who checks one line (6 minutes). You and any helper hear the six, one minute each (6 minutes). The event stays the same. Keep the story with its revision; day 72 scores it. Tag error AE for a special name still in lowercase.

Check: the partner finds the marks one checklist line names.

#### 10 min. L.2.2a letter item

Say: "This letter is made up for practice. Quillwick is a name made up for practice." Hand out the letter card and read it aloud once. Read each prompt once and children correct it on the card: the holiday (item s02-A46), the place (item s02-A47), the lamp name (item s02-A48). Tag error AE.

#### 8 min. L.2.2b letter item

Read the prompts once: the greeting (item s02-A49), the closing (item s02-A50). Tag error AF.

#### 12 min. L.2.2c letter item

Read the prompts once: who owns the project (item s02-A51), the short word for did not (item s02-A52). Collect the letter cards. Tag error AG for the owner and AH for the short word.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand over the printed card of item s02-X71, read it aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AG.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six cards from yesterday's group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Reblend any wrong ending (error Y).

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from yesterday's notes, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, reread Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "Dan made a fine decision."), partner reread, and tiles (build collection, section, nation, mission). No oral queue today.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

Partners reread A Choice of Actions, a page each.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the letter large and read the checklist from the board. A child may say a correction instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups.

- **20 min. The assessment at home.** Your child edits their story with four reads: capitals, commas, apostrophes, spelling. Then write "dear mo" and "best wishes" on lines; your child writes each one correctly.
  **Say.** "What does a greeting need after it?" Expect: a comma.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say nation and mission. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X71]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: edit with two checklist lines and keep the letter card. Longer: a child edits a page of a book at home.

### Words and supports

Review: a capital letter for a special name (day 65); a letter's greeting, closing and commas (day 66); an apostrophe for a contraction or belongs to (day 67); the editing checklist (day 67). Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class mark the letter cards (errors AE, AF, AG, AH) and write the editing feedback. Day 72 retries them and the day 70 revision.
