# Day 74: Share the narratives and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the records of days 68 to 73 by error letter and set four groups: A, C and D; G to J; K to P and V; Q to U and AD to AH. Put the retry cards on four tables. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (feedback)

Say: "These details are made up." Hand back the written feedback from days 71 and 72. Show the commonest miss of error B from day 73 (item s02-X73v2) on a fresh slip: a picture with one detail the words do not say. Children point to the detail and say if the words show it (3 minutes). Each child reads their feedback and tells a partner one thing to fix (2 minutes).

#### 8 min. Group 1: reading

For errors A, C and D. Say: "This paragraph and this story are made up." Read a fresh paragraph twice, then a four-line story twice. For the paragraph, children name the topic (error C; items s02-A29 to s02-A32 were its day 68 check). For the story, they point to the line that finishes the opening (error A; items s02-X68v2, s02-A33 and s02-A34 were its check). The teacher moves from table to table, 4 minutes each.

#### 8 min. Group 2: words

For errors G, H, I and J. Say: "These words are made up." Show retie, unzip, riverbank and sandbox. Children sort the cards into "prefix and word" and "two words joined", then say if the meaning you read is right (error H or I). No word is spelled today.

#### 8 min. Group 3: narrative writing

For errors K to P and V. Say: "This draft is made up." Give the invented draft with one missing part and the five-box plan sheet. Each child points to the box that is empty and says the missing thought, feeling or time word (errors K to P; item s02-X69 was day 69's check). The teacher group draws the missing part and tells it; the rest write a few words.

#### 8 min. Group 4: conventions and sentences

For errors Q to U and AD to AH. Say: "These strips are made up." Each child gets one strip with one error. They find it and mark it (errors AG and AH, items s02-X71; error AD, item s02-X70). Then they say whether the fixed strip is right when you read it.

#### 8 min. Retry and record

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X74 aloud twice and ask it; the six answer to you, about 20 seconds each, the rest tell a partner (5 minutes). Record each child: shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. The earlier mark stays (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Sharing

Pairs read their narratives aloud to another pair, 3 minutes a pair, listeners ask "What did you want your reader to see?" or "Which part do you mean?" (6 minutes). The class reads the typed piece aloud (4 minutes). Where day 72's assessment (item s02-A53) was missed, give those pairs the two-choice card of two placements and ask "Which lets a reader see the picture first, and why?" (5 minutes). Any child may pass.

Check: each pair names one thing the listening pair heard.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six cards from yesterday's group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Reblend any wrong ending (error Y).

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from the latest notes, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, reread Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "A session has a mission."), partner reread, and tiles (build nation, section, decision, collection). No oral queue today; days 75 and 76 hold that.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups on day 75.

#### 5 min. Reread

The class rereads A Choice of Actions aloud together, for expression.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards large. Pointing, drawing and speaking are answers.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the sharing.

- **10 min. Read your story.** Your child reads their story to you, then tells you one thing they wanted you to see.
  **Say.** "What did you want me to see?" Expect: one detail from the story.
- **10 min. Fix one thing.** Find the mark a teacher made on the story or the letter and fix it with your child.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say nation and section. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X74]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the groups matched to the records, the retry and the exit; cut the second listening round. Longer: a child adds a caption to a partner's picture.

### Words and supports

Prefix, caption, detail, clarify. Frames: "First I ___. Next I ___." "I felt ___." "I would put ___ here because ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 begins the word and oral collection, and day 76 finishes it. Children who moved groups today start there.
