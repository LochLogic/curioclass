# Day 70: Make the story stronger

## Teacher page

### Before class

Match each story to its feedback line and each grammar card to its marked prompt. Write the five fresh sentence pairs and one fresh draft large. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Revise the grammar card

Say: "These sentences are made up." Show the commonest misses of errors Q to U (at most three letters), each as a wrong sentence beside a right one, and ask "Which is right?": the birds flew, the flock flew; two tooths, two teeth; Ben asked herself, Ben asked himself; Mia sitted, Mia sat; he ran quick, he ran quickly. One minute each. Hand back day 69's assessment, the cards; each child fixes the marked prompt (2 minutes).

#### 25 min. W.2.5 revision

Show a fresh draft: "Rae saw a smudge. Rae felt sad. Rae cleaned it." Children point to the empty box on the five-box plan sheet (3 minutes). If many missed day 69's exit (error P), make the empty box the ending. Hand back the stories with their feedback lines. Partners swap, read, and write one thing to add on the strip (6 minutes). Each child revises the story (item s02-A42) for 16 minutes: use the feedback line, decide about the strip, keep the event. Keep the original, the feedback and the revision together. Tag error V for an unrelated addition. After class write feedback on each revision; no score today.

Check: the partner's strip names one thing to add.

#### 25 min. L.2.1f sentence card

Say: "This text is made up." Reread "A Shape in the Stone" once (1 minute). Hand out the sentence card and the phrase strips. Read each prompt once, 4 minutes each: items s02-A43, s02-A54, s02-A55, s02-A44, s02-A56 and s02-A57. Collect the cards. Tag error AD for a part left unfinished.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X70 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AD.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six cards from yesterday's group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Reblend any wrong ending (error Y).

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from yesterday's notes, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, reread Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "The class made a decision."), partner reread, and tiles (build mansion, session, decision, explosion). No oral queue today.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

Partners reread A Choice of Actions, a page each.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the six prompts large and use paper strips for the phrases. A child may say a sentence instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the strips.

- **20 min. The assessment at home.** Your child reads their story and adds one thing a reader needs, keeping the same event. Then they say a simple sentence about the story, make it longer, and say it in a different order.
  **Say.** "What did you add, and why?" Expect: an action, a thought or a feeling that helps the reader.
- **10 min. A joined sentence.** Your child says two short sentences about the story, then joins them with a comma and and.
- **5 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions for expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say decision and session. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X70]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the simple sentence and its longer form, and keep the revision and the exit. Longer: a child revises a second event.

### Words and supports

Review: revise with feedback and a partner suggestion (day 64); produce, expand and rearrange a sentence (day 60). Frames: "I added ___ because ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class mark the sentence cards (error AD) and write one editing note on each revision. Day 71 opens with an AD repair, then edits the story and a letter.
