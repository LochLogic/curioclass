# Day 77: Fix the gaps from the mid-year checks, then a fresh try

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 75's assessment and day 76's assessment are handed back here. Sort the marks by gap letter, from the errors register A to N, R, S and T. Make groups of up to four by letter; a group of one joins the nearest letter. Put the missing-evidence children first for the spoken try.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "Today we fix what the checks showed, then try one fresh card. Nothing is graded." Show each child their group.

#### 50 min. Groups

Three rounds of 15 minutes. Each group gets a one-minute move for its letter, then practice on the same pattern with new words, never the check words. A, B and C: cover the first letter and blend; echo a line; read a line with one planted wrong word. D, E, F and G: sort cards by pattern; build the base then the ending with tiles; say the usual sound then the other; star the unusual part. H and I: sort, then build a new word with tiles; a guide-word hunt in the printed dictionary. J, K, L, M, N, R, S and T: point to the line that says it; say each question word with the kind of answer it wants; ask what happened, then what the character did; say the message in two parts; the frame "I read this to find out ___." Pairs not in a group reread A Choice of Actions. A group never takes spelling dictation.

#### 15 min. Written try

All thirty, new cards, only the first by turns of talk: build and say. In pairs, one child builds each word with tiles as the partner says it once [[c1-X77e]], 6 minutes; then the partner says one question about the note, read twice [[c1-X77f]], 9 minutes. You listen to as many pairs as you can and mark them.

#### 40 min. Spoken try

Ten children, 4 minutes each: those on the missing-evidence list first, then those whose letters are most often marked. Ask the reading [[c1-X77a]], 90 seconds, then the three word cards, 15 seconds each [[c1-X77b]] [[c1-X77c]] [[c1-X77d]]. The rest read and build quietly. A child not reached goes on the missing-evidence list for unit 3 small groups, and is heard there.

#### 5 min. Record

Mark every ask shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record which letters changed.

#### 5 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one thing they fixed today.

Day 78 begins unit 3. All retrieval of unit 2 and grade 1 routines starts there, not today.

### Other rooms

One child: take the first group move you need, then the written try and the spoken try in 12 minutes. Pointing and a scribe count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on scraps of paper.
**Skip.** The note.

- **10 min. Fix.** Say the word "wild" and ask your child to write it with the letters from scraps, then to read it back. Reread one favourite page so it sounds like talking.
  **Say.** "Tell me the part that does not sound the way it looks." Expect: the unusual part of the word.
- **20 min. Read.** Read a short page aloud together. Your child asks you who, what, where, when, why or how about it.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X77a]] [[c1-X77b]] [[c1-X77c]] [[c1-X77d]] [[c1-X77e]] [[c1-X77f]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the written try. Longer: pairs write one more question about their reader and trade.

### Words and supports

Words: question, topic, check, fix. Frames: "I read this to find out ___." "The text says ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where a card allows.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 begins unit 3. The marks from days 75 to 77 stay on the unit 2 record sheet; children who still show a gap are in the unit 3 small groups.
