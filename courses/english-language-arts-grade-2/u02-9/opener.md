# Section 2.9 opener: write, revise, edit and publish a narrative (days 69 to 74)

## What the section does

Six days finish the unit. On day 69 each child hears a fresh text twice and writes an illustrated story about one child at a display who meets a problem, then answers a short grammar card about the same text. On day 70 they revise the story from written feedback and a partner suggestion, and write, lengthen and rearrange sentences. On day 71 they edit the story and a short letter. On day 72 the class types one piece together, and you record each child's contribution. On day 73 children refine an illustration so the event is clear. On day 74 they share their stories and retry the part they missed.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60 for the topic and the foundations. Days 69 to 72 are check days, with an exit at the end of each. The foundations block is flexible groups of ten, by the latest evidence, and a reread of the cycle 12 reader. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask. Same-day fresh practice is progress evidence, not a second score.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A story keeps one event and finishes it.** Revising adds what the reader needs without changing the event.
- **Editing is a separate pass.** Capitals, commas, apostrophes and spelling come after the ideas.
- **Publishing is a team job.** Each child brings a wording or placement choice and a reason.
- **A picture adds what the words leave out.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **K to P, V:** the narrative gaps. Redo one part and keep the event.
- **Q to U, AD to AH:** grammar and convention slips. Fix one recorded error on a fresh card.
- **AI:** a child who watches the typing and offers nothing. Offer a two-choice card.
- **B and H:** a picture that contradicts the words, or a prefix mistaken for its opposite.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown with help, shown, not yet seen. The unit plan scores each part 0, 1 or 2 after class; record 0 as not yet seen, 1 as shown with help and 2 as shown. Exits are unscored; record the exit looks on the unit record sheet.
