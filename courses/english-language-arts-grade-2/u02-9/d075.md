# Day 75: Mid-year reading and word check, first fifteen children

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the thirty children in a fixed hearing order and mark fifteen for today; the other fifteen are heard on day 76. Nobody is heard twice. The children who moved groups on day 74 come first. The day 67 -tion and -sion record and the day 68 and 69 to 72 records are cited, not collected again.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is taught today. You may pass any ask." Mark every ask shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Directions

Seat today's first seven near you. The rest read A Choice of Actions and build words with tiles, quietly, and never see a check card.

#### 35 min. Conferences, first seven

Seven children, 5 minutes each: 4 minutes of asks and 1 minute to change the child. In order: a purpose from the title [[c1-A75a]], 20 seconds; the first reading [[c1-A75b]], 60; mostly about [[c1-A75c]], 30; the sense check [[c1-A75d]], 30; the reread [[c1-A75e]], 25; then five word cards, 15 seconds each [[c1-A75f]] [[c1-A75g]] [[c1-A75h]] [[c1-A75i]] [[c1-A75j]]. Give no hint beyond the stuck word. A child who cannot finish in 5 minutes stops: mark what was heard and put the rest on the missing-evidence list for day 77, never in a squeezed turn.

#### 15 min. Written station

All thirty write; all are marked. Say each word twice and give one minute: sway, praise, loan, moist, shrimp, clasp [[c1-A75k]] (6 minutes with the saying). Then the strip on side 2, with the printed dictionary [[c1-A75l]] (9 minutes). Collect the pages.

#### 40 min. Conferences, last eight

Eight children, 5 minutes each, the same asks. The rest read and build quietly.

#### 15 min. Reread

Pairs reread A Choice of Actions, a page each, for expression. Unmarked. Mark the fifteen heard today in the heard column.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs say one hard word they read and how. Collect every card; tomorrow's fifteen must not see them.

After class, list the gaps by letter: a guessed word A, word by word B, a misread left C, a vowel pattern D, an ending E, an unusual sound F or G, a spelling H, the dictionary I, the topic R, a liking for a purpose S, home for the title K. They go to day 77.

### Other rooms

One child: hear the whole battery in 5 minutes. No printed cards: copy each card's words large by hand. A child may say the strip answer and you write it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write five short words on paper.
**Skip.** The strip.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read a short paragraph about a rainy day aloud once. Your child says what to find out from its title, then reads it to you. Help only with a stuck word, after three seconds.
  **Say.** "What is it mostly about?" Expect: the one topic of the paragraph, not one detail.
- **10 min. Words.** Write drift, spoon, boiled, wild and thumb. Your child reads each.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads the last three sentences so it sounds like talking.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the first reading, the sense check and the words. Longer: a child reads a second article for fun, unmarked.

### Words and supports

Words: title, sentence, dictionary, wrong. Frames: "I want to find out ___." "It is mostly about ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 hears the other fifteen on a second set and the whole class answers questions about a new story, article and message. Day 75's marks go to day 77 with day 76's.

### Credit
