# Two-choice card (days 73 and 74)

A card with two options for one piece of the class narrative, for a child to pick from and give a reason. Option A: "Put the picture above the sentence." Option B: "Put the picture beside the sentence." Beneath: "I choose ____ because ____." On day 73 children only point to the option that fits the need you name. On day 74 a child whose day 72 record needs a retry picks one and says why.
