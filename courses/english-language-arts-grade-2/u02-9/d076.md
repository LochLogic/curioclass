# Day 76: Mid-year reading and word check, last fifteen children, and questions on a story, article and message

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hear the fifteen children not heard on day 75, in the same hearing order. Nobody is heard twice. Day 75's assessment marks stay on the record sheet; today's are added beside them, and both go to day 77.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is taught today. You may pass any ask." Mark every ask shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Directions

Everyone reads the story, the article and the message once with you, then writes on the student page. There is no second spelling station today.

#### 20 min. Class station

All thirty. Read the story "Cora and the Squeaky Door" aloud twice and give out its printed card; ask the story items [[c1-A76k]] [[c1-A76n]] [[c1-A76o]] [[c1-A76t]], saying each aloud. Then the article "Three Bells", twice, with its card: [[c1-A76l]] [[c1-A76p]] [[c1-A76q]]. Last the message, read aloud twice and never printed: [[c1-A76m]] [[c1-A76r]] [[c1-A76s]] [[c1-A76u]]. Allow about 90 seconds an ask. Children write short answers; a scribe or a drawing is allowed where an ask says so. Collect the pages.

#### 35 min. Conferences, first seven

Seven children, 5 minutes each: 4 minutes of asks and 1 minute to change the child. In order: a purpose from the title [[c1-A76a]], 20 seconds; the first reading [[c1-A76b]], 60; mostly about [[c1-A76c]], 30; the sense check [[c1-A76d]], 30; the reread [[c1-A76e]], 25; then five word cards, 15 seconds each [[c1-A76f]] [[c1-A76g]] [[c1-A76h]] [[c1-A76i]] [[c1-A76j]]. A child who cannot finish in 5 minutes stops: mark what was heard and put the rest on the missing-evidence list for day 77, never in a squeezed turn.

#### 40 min. Conferences, last eight

Eight children, 5 minutes each, the same asks. The rest read and build with tiles quietly.

#### 10 min. Reread

Pairs reread A Choice of Actions a page each. Unmarked. Mark the fifteen heard today in the heard column.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs say one question they wrote and who could answer it. Collect every card; none is kept for later.

After class, list the gaps by letter: a guessed word A, word by word B, a misread left C, a vowel pattern D, an ending E, an unusual sound F or G, evidence from home K, the topic R, a liking for a purpose S, the wrong detail T, plus a question J, a wrong question word L, a trait M and a message ask N. They go to day 77 with day 75's.

### Other rooms

One child: do the station on its own in 20 minutes, then the whole battery in 5. No cards: write the five words large by hand. A child may say an answer and you write it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write five short words on paper.
**Skip.** The station.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read a short note about a class trip aloud twice. Your child writes one question about it. Help only with a stuck word, after three seconds.
  **Say.** "Why should we bring that?" Expect: the reason the note gives.
- **10 min. Words.** Write stride, stray, napped, rind and couple. Your child reads each.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads the last three sentences of any page so it sounds like talking.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the first reading and the words. Longer: a child reads a second article for fun, unmarked.

### Words and supports

Words: question, article, message, character. Frames: "Who ___?" "The story says ___." Pointing, a scribe, drawing and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 reteaches the gaps of days 75 and 76 in groups and ends with a fresh try. Today's marks go there with day 75's.

### Credit

Ours: written for Curio Class. The patterns were taught earlier; the cards are new.
