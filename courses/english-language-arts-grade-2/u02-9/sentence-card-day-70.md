# Sentence card (days 70 and 71)

The child's card about the day 69 text, with six prompts and ruled lines, read aloud one at a time.

1. Write one simple sentence about Jo.
2. Make your simple sentence longer with a detail.
3. Say your longer sentence again with the parts in a different order.
4. Write one compound sentence about Jo.
5. Make your compound sentence longer with a detail.
6. Say your longer compound sentence again with its two parts swapped.

Phrase strips are available. Keep the card out of teaching.
