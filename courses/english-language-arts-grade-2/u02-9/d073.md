# Day 73: A picture that shows the event

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang page 2, page 3 and page 5 of The Mark in the Stone with the pictures covered. Write the six lines of the reread large. Write Cal's line large with a blank space under it. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These choices are made up." Hand back day 72's assessment, the record notes. If many missed day 72's exit (error AI), give each pair the two-choice card and say: "Which one lets the next reader see the picture first?" Pairs point together and a partner says "agree" (4 minutes). Otherwise reread the typed class piece aloud.

#### 5 min. Words

Say each word once and point to the wall. New: illustration is a picture that adds a detail the words leave out. Review: caption (day 47), detail, evidence and support (day 43), clarify (day 1). Children say each back once.

#### 10 min. Reread with the pictures

Say: "Amara and Dr. Lee are made up." Read pages 2, 3 and 5 aloud with the pictures covered, then uncover them (4 minutes). Read each line below aloud; for each, children point to the words or to the picture, and a partner says if it matches (6 minutes): "She picked up a small, flat stone." "Amara wears a yellow jacket." "Her heart began thumping." "Her eyes are wide." "Dust fell away." "Sunlight shines through the dust."

Check: words, picture, words, picture, words, picture.

#### 10 min. Model: does the picture match the words?

Show Cal's line: "First he brushed the ridge gently." Draw Cal and a stone. Add a brush, a puff of dust and the ridge. For each addition ask: "Is it in the line?" Expect: the brush and the ridge are in the line; the puff of dust is only in the picture. Then draw Cal holding a chalk stick and ask: "Does the picture match the words?" Expect: no. Say: "An illustration adds a detail. It never changes what the words say."

#### 15 min. Refine your illustration

Each child reads one action line in their story, checks the picture shows it, and adds what is missing. You move and give no content. A child who cannot write draws and tells you.

#### 5 min. Caption

Each child writes a caption of a few words under the picture that does not repeat the sentence.

#### 5 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "Which thing did you add, and which word in your story does it match?" Partners turn and talk, then link: "I agree because ___." or clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Hear the six, about 20 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X73 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error B for a detail that disagrees with the card.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six cards from yesterday's group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Reblend any wrong ending (error Y).

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from yesterday's notes, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, reread Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "Dan asks for a collection of games."), partner reread, and tiles (build collection, section, nation, mission). No oral queue today.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

The class rereads A Choice of Actions aloud together, for expression.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the six lines large and use a sketch of the pages. A child may say a caption instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the class talk.

- **10 min. In the words or the picture?** Open a picture book page. Read one sentence aloud and name a thing in the picture. Your child says if it is in the sentence or only in the picture. Do three.
  **Say.** "Is it in the words, or only in the picture?" Expect: one of the two, with the sentence pointed to.
- **10 min. Improve a picture.** Your child checks their story's picture shows one action, adds what is missing, and writes a caption of a few words.
- **5 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say collection and decision. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X73]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four lines and keep the refining and the exit. Longer: a child adds a second picture with its caption.

### Words and supports

Illustration, caption, detail, evidence, support, clarify. Frames: "The picture shows ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class mark the exits (error B). Day 74 opens with a repair of error B, hands back the day 71 and 72 feedback, then runs four groups, a retry and sharing.
