# Day 69: A story about a find

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the time-word cards and the five-box plan sheet. Keep the day 68 notes on errors A and C for day 74. Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 40 min. W.2.3 narrative

Say: "This story is made up for practice. Jo is made up." Read "A Shape in the Stone" aloud twice, then put it away (3 minutes). Hand out the narrative prompt page and read its prompt aloud once (item s02-A35). Children draw and write (37 minutes); the wall cards stay up. You give no wording. A child who cannot write dictates to you. Collect the pages. Day 72 scores each story shown, shown with help or not yet seen; tag errors K to P and B.

#### 2 min. L.2.1a item

Hand out the grammar card. Read prompt 1 once (item s02-A36). Tag error Q.

#### 2 min. L.2.1b item

Read prompt 2 once (item s02-A37). Tag error R.

#### 2 min. L.2.1c item

Read prompt 3 once (item s02-A38). Tag error S.

#### 2 min. L.2.1d item

Read prompt 4 once (item s02-A39). Tag error T.

#### 7 min. L.2.1e item

Read prompt 5 once (item s02-A40), then prompt 6 once (item s02-A41). Collect the cards. Tag error U.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X69 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error P.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Hand back day 68's assessment with its feedback row; children read the row and keep it for day 74. Reread six -tion and -sion cards from the day 68 group notes: the ending alone, then the word. Tag error Y or Z.

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from the day 68 results, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, read Dan's Stamp Collection, spell "Dan has a mission."), partner reread, and tiles (build collection, section, nation, mission). No oral queue today.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

The class rereads A Choice of Actions aloud together, for expression.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the prompts large. A child may say an answer instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups.

- **20 min. The assessment at home.** Make up and read aloud twice a short story about a child at a museum display whose label falls. Your child draws and writes a story about one child with a problem at a display: what the child does, thinks and feels, time words, an ending.
  **Say.** "How does the end finish the problem?" Expect: the problem from the start is solved at the end.
- **10 min. Describing words.** Your child says one sentence about a sled with a describing word, then one about how the sled rides.
- **5 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say collection and section. Your child writes each and says which letters make the ending sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X69]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the story is a drawing and spoken sentences. Longer: a second event after the first.

### Words and supports

Review: collective noun, irregular plural, reflexive pronoun, irregular past verb, adjective, adverb (days 54 to 56). Review: the five boxes of an event (days 59 to 63). Frames: "First ___. Next ___." "I felt ___." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class write one feedback line on each story (what is missing) and mark one grammar prompt to fix on each card. Day 70 hands both back and repairs errors K to P and Q to U.
