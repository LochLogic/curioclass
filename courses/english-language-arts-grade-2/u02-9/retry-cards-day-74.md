# Retry cards (day 74)

Short fresh cards for the four groups, one case each, none used earlier in the unit. Group 1: a four-line story and its opening line on two boxes side by side. Group 2: four word cards, retie, unzip, riverbank and sandbox: sort them under prefix and word, or two words joined, then say if a given meaning is right. Group 3: one invented draft with one missing part (a thought, a feeling or a time word) beside the five-box plan sheet. Group 4: one invented sentence strip with one error to find and mark, kept by the teacher from the days 69 to 72 records. Each child does one retry and the teacher records the result; the earlier mark stays.
