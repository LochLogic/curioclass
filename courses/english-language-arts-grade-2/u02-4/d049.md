# Day 49: Glossary, index, menu and icons

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 48's exit marks show whether error D needs the repair. Mark today's six (group 2 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Give each pair article C, the three route cards, the dictionary card and a pencil. Set three stations: tiles, the ten cycle 9 sort cards, a reread of Rob Is Skipping. If a shared screen can show page 4 exactly as printed, set it up; if not, the printed page does the whole job.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read twice: "How to Feed the Class Fish. Put in one pinch of food. Wait until it is gone." Say: "Someone might say the author wants to sell fish food. What does the author say in the first line?" Cover the invented purpose with a hand. Expect: it says how to feed the fish, so the author wants to explain how. Children tell a partner the words that show it. Hear two (error D).

#### 6 min. Words

Review: caption, the line under a picture (unit 1, day 22); glossary (grade 1, day 83); the index is new today. New today: process, steps in order that make something happen, as article B showed. Show page 4 of article C and say each word, point to it and use it in a sentence: "The glossary tells what a word means." "The index tells what page to look on." "The caption tells what a picture shows." "A fossil forms by a slow process." Partners say one sentence back.

#### 10 min. Read aloud: article C

Read Molds, Casts and Tracks aloud (524 words, about 4 minutes), pointing to each of the three diagrams and its caption. Say aloud: "Fossils on public land belong to everyone. We never dig for them or take them. If we find one, we leave it, take a picture and tell a ranger or a teacher." Say that page 4 is a different kind of page: it helps us look things up. Partners each tell one thing the article taught them (3 minutes). Hear three.

#### 10 min. Model: the reference page

If the screen exists, show page 4 on it beside the printed page for six of these minutes to show that an icon is a label and a page number; if it does not, use the printed page alone. Show the three parts of page 4. The glossary tells what a word means. The index lists where to look, with a page number. The menu has three icons: a seashell for Shapes, a footprint for Tracks and a magnifying glass for Scientists; each icon is a small picture with a label and a page to go to.

Walk three routes on the print. First the menu: "Where is the part about shapes in stone?" The seashell icon, Shapes, page 1 (3 minutes). Then the index: "Where is cast explained?" The index says cast, 1; turn to page 1 and find the bold word (3 minutes). Then the glossary: "What does specimen mean?" The glossary says one fossil or sample kept to be studied, and the bold word specimen is on page 3 (3 minutes). Say what each tool is for. The glossary says what a word means, the index says where to look, the menu icon jumps to a part by its picture and label. Do not trace the Tracks icon today. Say the limit: a word can be missing from the index or glossary, and then we try a feature we already know, such as a subheading (1 minute).

#### 12 min. Pairs use the route cards

Pairs take the three route cards in turn. One child traces a finger on the print from the tool to the page, says the tool and the page, and reads the first line they land on; the partner checks in the print and says "yes" or "which part do you mean?" Swap on each card (9 minutes). Walk among pairs, about 20 seconds each. Hear three pairs (3 minutes). Watch for error E, a pair that reads page after page instead of choosing a tool.

#### 7 min. Beginning dictionary

Review: a dictionary entry, find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters (unit 1, day 27). Pairs look up specimen on the beginning dictionary card, read its meaning and the glossary's, and say whether both mean the same (2 minutes). Expect: yes, both say it is kept to be studied. Then look up riverbed, which is bold on page 2 and not in the glossary (2 minutes). Last, read a sentence not on the card: "The wave knocked over the tower." Pairs find wave, read both meanings and choose the one that fits (3 minutes). Expect: a line of moving water.

#### 5 min. Write one sentence

Each child writes on side 1: "I found ___ by using ___." in a few words, such as a word and the tool. A child may draw and say the words for you to scribe.

Check: a fact named and the tool that found it.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X49 aloud twice (40 seconds). Ask item s02-X49, then s02-X49b, then s02-X49c. After each ask, children tell a partner. The six then answer all three to you, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Shown means the index, the glossary and the bag icon, each with a word of reason. Shown with help means the right tools after one prompt: "What does the card say about the page numbers?" Not yet seen means the glossary or the bag icon for track facts, or a search of every page (error E). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six word cards: rubbing, stopped, bigger, running, hoping, sitting. Children read each. If an ending is dropped or read wrongly, underline the ending, read it alone, then the whole word (error Z). If hoping is read as hopping, compare hope with hop (error AB).

#### 15 min. Read: the cycle 9 reader

Review: even and finally, mapped on day 48. Two words are new and mapped before anyone reads. Wrong: wr, where the w is silent and wr says /r/; star wr. Won: the o says /u/, as in sun; star the o (error AA). Put both on the word wall (3 minutes). Say the title yourself; children do not read it. Read page 1 aloud and the class echoes it. Pairs read pages 2 to 6 turn about, a page each (54 words in all, 6 minutes), covering the picture until they have read the line (error X). Check meaning after decoding (3 minutes).

**Ask.** "Why did Rob trip, and what did he do next?" Expect: he rushed, then got up and went on slower.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). All four lines are used. Each says the vowel sound in each word and the ending. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build running, sitting and hopping by doubling, then hoping by dropping the e. Sort: the ten cycle 9 cards under double the last letter, drop the e and just add the ending, a partner naming a word and the child pointing to its pile. Reread: pairs read Rob Is Skipping twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child in a station reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which word needed a double letter?" Expect: skipping, hopping, running, sitting or stopped, and the short vowel that explains it.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw page 4 on the board with three icons and write the index on paper. No screen: use the printed page. One copy of article C: show page 4 to the class and let pairs point on the board copy. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: use any book with an index, or write a three-line index on paper. No cards: write each word on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations, the sort and the oral card.

- **10 min. Tools that find.** Show a book with an index and a glossary, or use the day's page 4. Your child shows how to find a word.
  **Say.** "Which tool tells you what a word means?" Expect: the glossary.
  **Say.** "Which tool tells you what page to turn to?" Expect: the index.
- **10 min. Look up a word.** Find riverbed or specimen in any dictionary; your child reads the meaning.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say running, sitting and stopped. Your child writes each and says why the last letter doubles.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X49]] [[s02-X49b]] [[s02-X49c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs do two route cards and skip the sentence about wave; keep the model, the exit and the reader. Longer: a child writes a question for a partner, and the partner says which tool to use first.

### Words and supports

Process, caption, glossary, index, menu, icon, page. Frames: "I used the ___ to find ___." "The ___ tells ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing and tracing are answers. A child may pass any turn.
