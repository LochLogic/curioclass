# Exit cards, days 46 to 49

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards; you read each twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 46 (s02-X46).** "Paragraph 1: A fern print can stay in rock. Paragraph 2: A burrow can show where an animal dug." Ask: "Tell me one topic that fits both paragraphs." Then (s02-X46b), same card: "What is paragraph 2 about?"
- **Day 47 (s02-X47).** "Subheading: A Track. Sentence: A track is a line of **prints** made by feet. Caption: These prints show one step. A separate subheading: Shell Shapes." Ask: "Which feature on this card leads you to the track part fastest?" Then, same card: s02-X47b "Which feature on this card shows that prints is a key word?" and s02-X47c "Which feature on this card tells what a picture shows?"
- **Day 48 (s02-X48).** "First, put soil in the pot. Next, press in a seed. Last, add water." Ask: "Tell me what the author wants to do." Then a second card (s02-X48b): "The beach pebble is round and grey. It has a white stripe and a smooth edge." Ask: "Does the author want to answer a question, explain how, or describe what?"
- **Day 49 (s02-X49).** "Index: labels 2; tracks 4. Menu: bag icon, Labels; paw icon, Tracks. Glossary: label, a note naming something." Ask: "Which tool sends you to a page number for track facts?" Then, same card: s02-X49b "Which tool tells you what the word label means?" and s02-X49c "Which icon takes you to the part about labels?"
