# Beginning dictionary card (days 49 to 53)

A one-page mini dictionary with guide words at the top, shared by pairs. Entries, each with a short meaning and an example sentence:

- **bark** (noun) the rough outside of a tree. *The bark felt rough.* **bark** (verb) to make a sharp sound, as a dog does. *The dog will bark at the gate.*
- **wave** (noun) a line of moving water. *A wave rolled onto the sand.* **wave** (verb) to move a hand to say hello. *I wave to my friend.*
- **bold** (adjective) not afraid, or easy to see. *She was a bold climber.*
- **fan** (noun) a thing that blows air; a shape like a spread fan. *The fan cooled the room.*
- **riverbed** (noun) the ground a river flows over. *The riverbed was dry.*
- **specimen** (noun) one thing kept to be studied. *The specimen sat in a box.*

Pairs use the card to find a word, say its meanings, and choose the one that fits a sentence you read. Do not add cast, trace, location, notebook, collector or marked.
