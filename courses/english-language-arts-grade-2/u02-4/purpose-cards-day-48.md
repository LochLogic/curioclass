# Purpose cards (day 48)

Three cards: **to answer a question**, **to explain how**, **to describe what**. Pairs lay the card that fits beside a text and say the words that show it. The teacher models with the article on how a bone becomes a fossil: the author explains how, because the opening says the article tells the steps in order and the diagram has five boxes.
