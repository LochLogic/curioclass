# Feature route cards (day 47)

Three cards. Each has a question and an empty route of three steps: **subheading**, **bold word**, **caption**. Pairs walk a finger along the route on the printed article and say the route aloud. A route may skip a step the question does not need.

1. What do wind, rain and rivers do to the rock above the bone?
2. How long does a fossil bone take to form?
3. What do the five boxes in the diagram show?

Key for the teacher: 1 subheading Rock Wears Away (no bold word; the caption box 4 agrees). 2 subheading Five Steps (no bold word). 3 the caption under the diagram: five steps from living animal to fossil. The day's model uses minerals and the last step, and the marked page uses Buried Fast and layer, so no card repeats them.
