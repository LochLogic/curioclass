# Three invented blurbs (day 48)

Say aloud that these three blurbs are made up. Pairs sort them by purpose and say the words that show it.

**Card A.** Wash the apples. Cut them in four pieces. Put the pieces in a bowl and add a spoon of honey.

**Card B.** The red fox has thick fur, a long tail and sharp ears. It lives in the woods.

**Card C.** How many legs does a spider have? Turn the page to find out.

Key: A explains how (steps in order). B describes what (the fox's parts and home). C answers a question (the question and its answer).
