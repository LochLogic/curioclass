# Section 2.4 opener: reading a fossil article (days 46 to 49)

## What the section does

Four days turn to the articles in *Fossil Clues*. On day 46 children name the topic of an article and the focus of each paragraph, using a topic chart. On day 47 they use subheadings, bold words and captions to find a fact fast. On day 48 they say what the author wants to answer, explain or describe, and sort three invented blurbs by purpose. On day 49 they use a glossary, an index, a menu and its icons to find where a fact is. A teacher-held screen shows how an icon menu works; children choose a link orally and check it on the printed page. Paper does the whole job when no screen is available.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a repair, the model, pair work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block continues cycle 8 on day 47 with a 15-minute written check, then moves to cycle 9 on day 48: doubling the last consonant before an ending that starts with a vowel, with the reader *Rob Is Skipping*. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask; you hear the day's rotating six.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A topic is bigger than any one paragraph.** Name the focus of each paragraph, then say what joins them.
- **A feature is a road to a fact.** A subheading leads to a part, a bold word to a name, a caption to a picture, an index to a page.
- **An author wants to answer, explain or describe.** Point to the words that show it.
- **Paper and screen do the same job.** The icon is a label and a page number.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** one detail named as the whole topic. Read the title, name the topic, then check that each paragraph fits it.
- **D:** a purpose the text does not support. Ask what the author says in the first line.
- **E:** a useful feature ignored. Walk a finger from the subheading to the bold word to the caption.
- **AB, X, Y, Z:** a doubled letter dropped, a word guessed from the picture, a vowel team misread, or an ending dropped. Compare hop and hope, cover the picture and blend.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits are unscored; record the exit looks on the unit record sheet.
