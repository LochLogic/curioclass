# Reference route cards (day 49)

Three cards, each with a question about the third article and the tools: **glossary**, **index**, **menu icon**, **bold word**. Pairs trace a finger from the tool to the page, say the tool and the page, then read the first line they land on.

1. What is a paleontologist?
2. Where is a riverbed explained?
3. Where do you go to read about scientists at work?
