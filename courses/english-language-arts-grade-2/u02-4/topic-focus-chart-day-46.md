# Topic and focus chart (day 46)

A sheet with one wide box at the top labeled **Topic** and four narrow boxes in a row beneath it labeled **Focus 1**, **Focus 2**, **Focus 3** and **Focus 4**, each joined to the topic by an arrow. The teacher fills two of the focus boxes from an article on the wall chart as the model, then names the topic. Partners hold their own copy from day 46 and fill boxes 3 and 4.
