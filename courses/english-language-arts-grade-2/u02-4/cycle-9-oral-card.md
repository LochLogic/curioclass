# Cycle 9 oral card (days 48 to 52)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 48 only the first two lines are used. Children read the words and say each ending; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 8 patterns (ild, old, ind, ost).** child, wild, gold, hold, find, kind, post
- **Cycle 9 patterns (doubling before a vowel suffix).** rubbing, stopped, bigger, running, sitting, dropping, hoping, riding
- **A sentence.** The child was riding and stopped at the gate.

Partner word card for day 52: rubbing, stopped, bigger, running, sitting, hoping, riding, hiding. Dictated words: making, stepped.
