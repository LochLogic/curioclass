# Day 124: Read a made-up tale in parts and say what each animal believes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 123's exit tells you who claims more than a source says; run the opening repair below if many did. Cut four quote strips (invented from the tale): "I was wrong about the gifts." "My noise didn't help either." "Waiting was not enough." "I'm just glad I looked." Draw the large beliefs table on the board. Make six groups of five; give each child a part. Mark today's six and the six for the oral card (children 1 to 6).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Reread aloud the note after the second ant telling, then say only what it says about who told the fable: La Fontaine told it in French from older fables credited to Aesop; it does not say he began it (3 minutes). Pairs say one more sentence that the note does say and one that it does not (2 minutes). Saying more than the source says is the miss to repair. Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Cultural introduction

Say plainly that this tale is a story we made up. The tale first thought of for this lesson could not be cleared for us to use, so Curio Class wrote one to practice reading in different voices. It claims no people and no tradition, and it is not from any real place (4 minutes). Review: the credit habit from yesterday (grade 2, unit 4, day 123): say who wrote it and what is known. Pairs tell each other what a made-up tale can still teach us (4 minutes). Share two (2 minutes).

#### 15 min. Read 04 in parts

Preview four words with a picture: mist, gathered, shiny, hollow (1 minute). Read the whole script aloud, all voices, while groups follow (3 minutes: 312 words). Six groups of five, each child one of Narrator, Lizard, Crow, Squirrel and Tortoise, read it once together (6 minutes). Swap parts and read the last page again (5 minutes). Groups read at once; walk and listen, and ask each group to say the speaker's name before the line.

#### 8 min. Model: what each animal believes

A point of view is what a character thinks and wants. Review (kindergarten): you said what a character wants, and believe is to think something is true. On the large beliefs table fill two rows aloud: the crow believes the sun is angry, so she brings gifts; the lizard is not sure, so she goes to look (6 minutes). Ask the class to point to the line in the tale that tells it (2 minutes).

#### 8 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which animal's idea does the Lizard test?" (3 minutes). Link: "You said ___; I would add ___" (3 minutes). Share two (2 minutes). Expect: all three ideas; she says she is not sure of each and goes to look.

#### 9 min. Pairs

Each pair gets the four quote strips and the tale. They match each strip to the animal that says it, using the colors, and say what that animal wanted at first (6 minutes). Then they say which event shows the message (1 minute): the lizard climbs and finds the sun. Fill the squirrel and tortoise rows of the large table from what pairs say (2 minutes). Confer with the six heard as you walk. Say aloud again that the tale is made up.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X124]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X124b]] (2 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Marking the cave with the smile sign gives the owl's view to the mouse, error C.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Children write stories and plays on slates and tell a partner the rule for each: change the y to i and add es, or just add s (5 minutes). Review: cycle 20 (grade 2, unit 4, days 119 to 123).

#### 15 min. Model, map, blend (cycle 21)

New today. The schwa is the weak "uh" sound in a word part you do not stress, and it is often spelled with a: ahead, around, alone (7 minutes). The prefixes de- and dis- come before a base word and change its meaning: dis- means not or the opposite, de- means undo or take away: disagree, distrust, decode, decrease (6 minutes). Say each word, touch a tile for each part, blend, then spell. Review: prefixes un- and re- on a known base word (grade 2, unit 2, day 50). Reading ahead as head is error Q; spelling ahead as ehead is error R. Ask: "Which part do I not stress?" (2 minutes).

#### 15 min. First reading of the cycle 21 reader

Say the title A Moth in the Garden yourself. Read page 1 aloud, touching the words (2 minutes). Pairs echo-read pages 2 to 7: one reads a page, the other reads it back (9 minutes). After decoding, each pair tells what Kit and Dad disagree about and points to the words that say it (4 minutes). Cover the picture and read from the letters.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Children 1 to 6 read the cycle 21 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap (12 minutes). Say aloud that the card is for practice of this cycle. The other twenty-four use tiles, a sort and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column. A word misread and left is error AE.

#### 13 min. Spell

Map the irregular word says with no guessing from the picture or first letter: tap the sounds and put a heart over ay, which says the short e sound (4 minutes). Dictate: ahead, alone, along, disagree (5 minutes). Then dictate the sentence in short parts: Ben sits alone in the dark. (4 minutes).

### Other rooms

No color printing: put the speaker's name in bold before each line, as the script piece does. No tiles: paper squares. One copy: read it aloud yourself, with the class taking the animals' lines. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: you read the Narrator and Crow, your child reads Lizard, Squirrel and Tortoise.
**Skip.** The quote strips, the beliefs table and the tiles.

- **10 min. Read in parts.** Read the tale aloud in parts and say it is made up.
  **Say.** "What does each animal think?" Expect: Crow thinks the sun is angry; Squirrel thinks it is asleep; Tortoise thinks it will come back.
- **10 min. The Lizard.** Your child says what the Lizard does and what she finds.
  **Say.** "Why does she climb?" Expect: she is cold and wants to see for herself.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads ahead, alone and disagree and says the part not stressed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X124]] and [[s04-X124b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the two-row model and the exit; read the last page once. Longer: a pair writes one more line for the tortoise.

### Words and supports

Mist, gathered, shiny, hollow, believe, point of view, schwa, dis-, de-. Frames: "Crow thinks ___ because ___." "Lizard wants ___." Say a belief in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 125 voices the animals and fills the beliefs table. Today's exit tells you who can say what an animal believes: if many marked the smile cave (error C), day 125 begins with a five-minute opening repair: read each animal's line and say what it wants. The six heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
