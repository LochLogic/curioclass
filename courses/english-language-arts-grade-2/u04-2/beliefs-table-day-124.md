# Beliefs table, days 124 and 125

One table a pair. Two columns headed "Who" and "What the animal thinks or wants". Four rows, one for each speaker in the tale (the crow, the squirrel, the tortoise and the lizard), with a small picture of each animal in the first column. The second column is blank.

The teacher fills a large copy on day 124 for one animal. On day 125 pairs fill their own and use it to read in parts. The tale is made up and claims no culture; say so aloud.
