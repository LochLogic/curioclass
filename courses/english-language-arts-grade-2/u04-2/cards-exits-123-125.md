# Exit cards, days 123 to 125

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Where a day has two rows, ask them one after another, one ask each. The first row of each day is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 123 (s04-X123).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A tale about a goose is told in many places. One book says a baker named Ana told it. Nobody knows where it began." Show two picture cards: a book with Ana telling the tale; Ana at the start of a long road beside a banner that reads The Beginning. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what the book tells us."
- **Day 123 (s04-X123b).** Same card. Say: "Say who told the tale, as the card says."
- **Day 124 (s04-X124).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Owl says, The cave is safe. Mouse says, The cave is not safe. Mouse runs out." Show two picture cards: a cave with a smile sign and a small owl; a cave with a warning sign and a running mouse. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what Mouse believes about the cave."
- **Day 124 (s04-X124b).** Same card. Say: "Say what Mouse believes about the cave."
- **Day 125 (s04-X125).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Snail says, Let us wait for the rain. Beetle says, Let us move before the rain." Show two picture cards: a waiting snail; a moving beetle. Say: "Mark the picture that matches what Beetle wants."
- **Day 125 (s04-X125b).** Same card. Say: "Say what Snail wants."
