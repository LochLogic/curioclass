# Exit cards, days 123 to 125

Rows that open "This card is invented for practice" are made up: say so aloud and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. The other rows reread a passage of the unit's own texts, named in the row. Read the card or passage twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Where a day has two rows, ask them one after another, one ask each. The first row of each day is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 123 (s04-X123v2).** Reread aloud twice the note after the second telling, "Where this fable comes from." Show two picture cards: La Fontaine writing the fable in French; La Fontaine at the start of a long road beside a banner that reads The Beginning. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what the note tells us."
- **Day 123 (s04-X123bv2).** Same note. Say: "Say who told this telling, as the note says."
- **Day 124 (s04-X124).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Owl says, The cave is safe. Mouse says, The cave is not safe. Mouse runs out." Show two picture cards: a cave with a smile sign and a small owl; a cave with a warning sign and a running mouse. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what Mouse believes about the cave."
- **Day 124 (s04-X124b).** Same card. Say: "Say what Mouse believes about the cave."
- **Day 125 (s04-X125).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Snail says, Let us wait for the rain. Beetle says, Let us move before the rain." Show two picture cards: a waiting snail; a moving beetle. Say: "Mark the picture that matches what Beetle wants."
- **Day 125 (s04-X125b).** Same card. Say: "Say Snail's line in a voice that fits what Snail wants."
