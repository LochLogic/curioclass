# Day 123: Read the hummingbird tale and say who told it, as the book says

## Teacher page

### Before class

If many children in day 122's exit showed error E (a shared detail missed), the foundations opening repair below holds it. Write three credit sentences on slips for each pair: 1. "Wangari Maathai told it as a hummingbird doing the best she can." 2. "A film in a language of the Amazon forest tells it." 3. "The tale began in one village long ago." Set out the check sheets face down. Mark today's six and the seven for the oral card (children 24 to 30).

### The day

#### 10 min. Cultural introduction and credit

Show the map. Say the credit in its own words: Wangari Maathai, a Kenyan woman who worked to plant trees, told this tale as a hummingbird doing the best she can; a film adapted from a folk tale was made in Hatxa Kuĩ, a language of the Amazon forest in Brazil, and was shared by a language-heritage group at a university in Spain. Nobody has shown where it began. Curio Class wrote the words from the shared idea, not from one person's telling (3 minutes). Children name the credit as part of the reading. Each pair sorts the three slips into "the book says" and "nobody knows" and reads one aloud (5 minutes): the first two go under "the book says", the third under "nobody knows". Share (2 minutes). No accents, no costumes, no guess about a people.

#### 12 min. Read 03

Preview the words with a picture each: flames, perched, scooped, part (1 minute). Read the tale aloud while pairs follow (3 minutes: 280 words). Pairs read it again by turns, a paragraph each (5 minutes). The class rereads the hummingbird's lines together (3 minutes). Fire is a story event only: everyone in the tale is safe at the river. Do not act it out and do not ask about fires children have seen.

#### 8 min. Model

Recount the tale with the story-map, in order (4 minutes). Review: story-map and recount (grade 2, unit 4, day 119). Find the event that holds up the message: the other animals help only after they see the hummingbird try. Show how to say what the hummingbird does and what she says: she carries a drop, and she says "I am doing my part." (4 minutes).

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: turn, pair, link, clarify (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Turn and talk on "What do the other animals do after they watch the hummingbird?" (4 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I would add ___" (3 minutes). Share three (3 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Pairs

Each pair fills a story-map strip for 03 with a few words or drawings, then puts a dot on the event that shows the message and says why (11 minutes). Confer with the six heard (4 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X123]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X123b]] (2 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Claiming more than the card says, such as that Ana began the tale, is a wrong answer to repair tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Two repairs from earlier days. Error E from day 122 (3 minutes): on the side-by-side print find one thing that happens in both tellings and touch it in each column. Then the oral records of days 120 to 122 (2 minutes), error AB: for the children whose answer did not match the passage, reread the sentence that answers the question aloud together and answer again.

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 20

Cycle 20 is complete, so today's written check closes it. Say aloud that the words are for practice of this cycle. Three parts of five minutes each, sheet face up. Spelling: [[s04-A53]]. Sentence: [[s04-A54]]. Reference: [[s04-A55]], with the dictionary page and the printed entry on the sheet. Children use only the sheet and their pencil. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Spelling the sentence with ies on the vowel-y word, or adding s to a consonant-y word, is error R. A plural left unchecked is error S.

#### 14 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Children 24 to 30 read the oral card to you at your table, two minutes each including the swap (14 minutes): [[s04-A46]], [[s04-A47]], [[s04-A48]], [[s04-A49]], [[s04-A50]], [[s04-A51]]. Ask [[s04-A52]] only if the child has not checked a misread word. Say aloud that the card is for practice of the cycle. The other twenty-three sort slips and reread the reader in two stations of eleven and twelve. Mark the heard column and the oral card sheet.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws one sentence with a plural from today's check, then reads it to a partner (10 minutes). Move around and hear the sentences. Five volunteers read theirs aloud (5 minutes). The sentence need not be about a story.

#### 11 min. Close

The class reads Pennies and Bunnies once more together for ease (4 minutes). Pairs reread it to each other (4 minutes). Ask: "What do you do when a consonant comes before the y?" Expect: change the y to i and add es (3 minutes).

### Other rooms

No printer: write the three slips on torn paper; draw the strip on the board and have pairs point. No map: say the places. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: say where the tale comes from.
**Skip.** The check sheet, the slips and the oral card.

- **10 min. The tale.** Read or tell the tale of the hummingbird.
  **Say.** "What does the hummingbird do? What do the other animals do after?" Expect: she carries drops of water; they help after they see her try.
- **10 min. Who told it.** Your child says where the tale was told, as the credit says.
  **Say.** "Does anyone know where it began?" Expect: no, nobody has shown that.
- **10 min. Plurals.** Your child writes a sentence with a plural and reads it to you.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X123]] and [[s04-X123b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the credit, the model and the exit; do the written check as two parts. Longer: a pair writes a second credit sentence for a tale they know.

### Words and supports

Flames, perched, scooped, part, credit, tale. Frames: "The book says ___." "Nobody knows ___." "The animals help after ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 124 reads a tale we made up, in parts. Today's exit tells you who can say only what the card says about the teller: if many claimed more than the card (that Ana made up the tale first), day 124 begins with a five-minute opening repair. The written check and the seven heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
