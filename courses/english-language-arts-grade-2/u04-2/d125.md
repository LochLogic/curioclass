# Day 125: Voice each animal's view in the tale

## Teacher page

### Before class

If many children in day 124's exit showed error C (the same view given to two animals), run the opening repair below. Write the cycle 21 dictionary page by hand, nine entries with a short meaning: agree, ahead, alone, along, around, asleep, decode, decrease, disagree. Find the commonest spelling miss on the day 123 check sheets and write it beside the right form on the board. Groups as on day 124. Mark today's six and the six for the oral card (children 7 to 12).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Read each animal's line from the tale aloud and say what it wants: Crow wants to bring gifts, Squirrel wants noise, Tortoise wants to wait (3 minutes). Then read the two lines again in two different ways, so the class hears that Squirrel and Tortoise do not sound alike (2 minutes). Giving two animals the same view is the miss to repair. Any child may pass.

#### 8 min. Reread 04

Pairs reread the tale silently with a finger or a pencil, finding each animal's own line and putting a small mark beside it (8 minutes). Walk and listen to a pair read one line aloud under their breath.

#### 10 min. Rehearse in groups

Six groups of five take the same parts as yesterday. Each group marks the line where each animal's view shows and decides how its voice changes there: higher, slower or louder (10 minutes). Say a voice may be loud or quiet, but never mocking, and never an accent or a costume.

#### 14 min. Perform

Six groups each read the first eight lines, about 100 seconds each, and the class names one difference in voice after each group, 20 seconds each (12 minutes), with two minutes for the groups to change places. The listeners say "Squirrel's voice was ___ because ___."

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "How is Tortoise's voice different from Squirrel's?" (4 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I would add ___" (3 minutes). Share three in the whole class (3 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 8 min. Pairs

Say aloud Squirrel's line "No, no! The sun is just asleep" in a slow, soft voice. Each pair judges which animal that voice fits and fixes the voice so it fits Squirrel: faster, higher, louder (3 minutes). Then each pair fills the squirrel and tortoise rows of its beliefs table with a few words and a drawing for what each wants (4 minutes). Check one line from the tale in each row and confer with the six heard (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X125]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X125b]] (2 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Marking the waiting snail for Beetle is error C: read the line, then say who wants what.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Children write ahead and decode on slates and tell a partner the part that is not stressed in ahead and the meaning of the prefix de- (5 minutes). Review: schwa a, de- and dis- (grade 2, unit 4, day 124).

#### 12 min. Reread the cycle 21 reader

Pairs read A Moth in the Garden aloud for accuracy, one page each (6 minutes). Then read the whole once more smoothly, with a pause at each full stop, so it sounds like talking (6 minutes, RF.2.4b). Confirm what Kit wants for the moth after decoding. Reading a word from its first letter is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 21 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap (12 minutes). The other twenty-four use tiles, a sort and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column. A printed word misread is error AE.

#### 16 min. Spell

Repair first (5 minutes): show the commonest cycle 20 spelling miss beside the right form and say the link. Then say each word, use it in a sentence, say it again, and children write: around, asleep, decrease, distrust (6 minutes). Dictate: The moth is asleep on the path. (5 minutes). Spelling asleep as esleep is error R: compare the new word with the schwa a on the chart.

#### 10 min. Dictionary

Review: check a spelling in a dictionary entry: find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters, correct the draft (grade 2, unit 1, day 27). Each pair takes a word slip, writes the word from memory as a draft, finds the entry on the dictionary page, compares letters, corrects it if needed and says what the prefix or the first a means (8 minutes, L.2.2e). Share two pairs (2 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Ask: "Which part of alone is not stressed?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No colour printing: use the bold speaker names. No tiles: paper squares. Perform to the nearest pair if a group of five cannot be made. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No group of five: take two parts each and read the first eight lines.
**Skip.** The dictionary page, the tiles and the groups.

- **10 min. Voices.** Read the first eight lines of the tale with a different voice for each animal.
  **Say.** "How is Tortoise different from Squirrel?" Expect: Tortoise is slow and calm; Squirrel is fast and loud.
- **10 min. Wants.** Your child says what each animal wants, using words from the tale.
  **Say.** "What does Crow want? What does Lizard want?" Expect: Crow wants to bring gifts; Lizard wants to see for herself.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes around and asleep and says the part not stressed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X125]] and [[s04-X125b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the rehearsal, one performance, the pairs part and the exit. Longer: a group reads the last page in two different voices for Crow.

### Words and supports

Voice, higher, slower, louder, wants, believes. Frames: "Squirrel's voice is ___ because ___." "Tortoise wants ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Today's exit tells you who can say what each animal wants: if many marked the waiting snail for Beetle (error C), day 126 begins with a five-minute opening repair: read each animal's line and say what it wants. The six heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
