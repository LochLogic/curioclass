# Section 4.4 opener: checking the tales, then retrying (days 129 to 131)

## What the section does

Days 129 and 130 are the first two checks of the unit: day 129 recounts a tale, names its lesson and voices its characters (card F1), and day 130 compares two versions (card V1 or V2). Day 131 repeats the work with fresh cards for any child who needs another try. The checks are not shown beforehand.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. On the check days the topic block holds a short repair, a briefing and the check, then pairs and a close; the foundations block continues cycle 22 (spelling pattern, reader, rotation and an oral card). Day 131 opens with a repair chosen from the records of days 129 and 130, then the retry, then a fresh exit. Read the check texts only on their days; they are kept unseen.

## The ideas at an adult's level

- **A check shows what is secure.** It is marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. It is not a grade.
- **A retry is practice.** A fresh card, a new try and a repair at the start of the next day. The check marks stay as they were.
- **What the checks sample.** Order of events, a lesson that events support, different voices for different characters, and a difference found in only one version.

## The errors in plain words

- Events are told out of order (A).
- A lesson is stated with no event that holds it up (B).
- Different characters are given the same viewpoint (C).
- A difference between versions is left out (D).

Show the right answer beside the wrong one, say the link together, and let the child try again. Repairs are five minutes at the start of the next day.

## Marking

One scale for every look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record it on the record sheet. The checks on days 129 and 130 are marked on the record; the retry on day 131 is not scored.
