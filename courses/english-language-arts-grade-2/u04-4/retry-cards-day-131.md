# Retry cards, day 131

Fresh short cards for the retry, each marked "invented for practice" and read twice. The teacher chooses the card from the commonest miss on the records of days 129 and 130, not from one child's mistake.

- **Recount.** Three event pictures to put in order, then one event to mark as the one that holds a lesson, in a new story that does not reuse the words of the exit cards.
- **Voices.** Two lines of dialogue in which two animals think different things; the child says each line in a different voice and says what each animal wants.
- **Versions.** Two short versions of one new story; the child marks one detail that is only in one version.

Write each card to the same size as the exits, one ask a card. The retry is practice: it is not scored and it does not replace the check.
