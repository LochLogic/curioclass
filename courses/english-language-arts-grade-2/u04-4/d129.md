# Day 129: Show a recount, a lesson and two voices on a new card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make six groups of five. Mark the six for the oral card (children 1 to 6). Keep card F1 face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Day 128's exit ticket, s04-X128: read it twice. Point to the words only version B has. Marking the agreeing pair is error D.

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say the check is a new card, invented for practice: a recount, a lesson and two voices. Any child may pass. Collect the student pages at day's end.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups come to the table in turn, five minutes each, 60 seconds a child with the swap, the recount and the voices as one turn; the next group waits at the edge. Ask [[s04-A1]] and [[s04-A2]], then [[s04-A3]], [[s04-A4]] and [[s04-A5]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A recount that reaches the cherries first is error A; an unrelated lesson is error B; one voice for both is error C. The other children reread 03 or 04 in pairs and draw a favourite event.

#### 15 min. Pairs

Pairs from one group ask each other one question about the card (SL.2.3). Hear the six.

#### 5 min. Close

The class names one thing that goes well in a recount.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Children write alone and defend on slates and name the part not stressed. Review: schwa a, de- and dis- (grade 2, unit 4, days 124 to 128).

#### 15 min. Model, map, blend (cycle 22)

Say kitten, ribbon, taken: the weak uh sound sits in the second part, spelled e or o. Tap and blend each on tiles, then today. Say inside, insect, impress: the front part is i-m before p, b or m, and i-n before other letters. Children blend each and tell a partner what the front part says.

#### 15 min. First reading of the cycle 22 reader

Read page 1 aloud. Pairs echo-read the rest, then confirm what Ned finds (RF.2.4c). A first-letter guess is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 1 to 6 read the cycle 22 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The others use tiles in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 13 min. Spell

Map was (review: kindergarten, unit 3, day 96). Build kitten, ribbon, inside and impress with tiles, one at a time, and say where the weak sound sits (6 minutes). Cloze strips, bank ribbon and kitten: "Ned tied a ___ on the box." "The little ___ naps." Pairs fill each and spell it from the bank (7 minutes). Kitten as kiten is error R.

### Other rooms

No printer: read card F1 aloud twice. No tiles: paper squares.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: read card F1 aloud twice.
**Skip.** The tiles.

- **15 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A1]] [[s04-A2]] [[s04-A3]]
- **10 min. Tell it.** Tell a short story in order: first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What is the lesson, and which event shows it?" Expect: a lesson that one event holds up.
- **10 min. Two voices.** Read two lines in two different voices.
  **Say.** "What does each one think?" Expect: two different views.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes kitten and ribbon and says the part not stressed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the rotation. Longer: a child reads card F1 again.

### Words and supports

Recount, lesson, voice, first, next, last. Frames: "First ___. Next ___. Last ___." "Sparrow thinks ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 130 is the second check, comparing two versions, with no repair for today. Day 130 hands back day 129's assessment: the records go to the repair groups on day 131.
