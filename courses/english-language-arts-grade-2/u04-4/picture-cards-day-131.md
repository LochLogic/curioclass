# Picture cards, day 131

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 131 (s04-X131).** card 1, a covered basket; card 2, an uncovered basket.
