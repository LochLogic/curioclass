# Day 130: Compare two versions of one short story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark the six for the reasons and the six for the oral card (children 7 to 12). Keep V1 and V2 face down. Collect the student pages at the end of the day.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Day 129's assessment is handed back on day 131, so no repair is routed here. Show the blank two-box sheet, name the boxes Both and Only one, and say the texts are made up for practice. Collect the student pages at the end of the day.

#### 10 min. Retrieval

Pairs recount 01 and 02 in three events each, reading the printed strips. This is practice only.

#### 20 min. T3

Pairs read V1 and V2 aloud, one each (6 minutes). Each child fills the two boxes (10 minutes): [[s04-A6]] and [[s04-A7]]. Hear the six give a reason, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes): [[s04-A8]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A shared detail given as the difference is error D; no shared event is error E.

#### 15 min. Pairs

Pairs read their boxes to each other and each asks one question. Not marked.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "What was hard about finding one thing different?" (4 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___" (4 minutes). Share two (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Children write kitten and ribbon on slates and name the part not stressed. Review: schwa e and o (grade 2, unit 4, day 129).

#### 12 min. Read the cycle 22 reader

Pairs read A Map in a Pocket aloud for accuracy, one page each (6 minutes). Then the class reads it for meaning: where the map leads (6 minutes). A first-letter guess is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 22 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The others use tiles and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 16 min. Spell

Fix-the-word strip: buten, mitin, insekt, impolit. Pairs say each word, find the sound on the chart and write it right (7 minutes). Then sort the four words by schwa e or o and front part i-m or i-n (6 minutes). Compare each word with the chart (3 minutes). Spelling button as buten is error R.

#### 10 min. Map irregular words

Map was and his again, no guessing from pictures or first letters (review: kindergarten, unit 3, day 96). Children point to the part to remember and read each in a sentence.

#### 5 min. Close

Ask: "Which part of mitten is not stressed?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: read V1 and V2 aloud twice; children draw in two folded boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed versions: read V1 and V2 aloud twice.
**Skip.** The tiles.

- **15 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A6]] [[s04-A7]] [[s04-A8]]
- **15 min. Two tellings.** Tell a short story twice, changing one ending.
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: the first part is the same; the ending is different.
- **15 min. Words.** Your child writes button and mitten and says the part not stressed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep T3. Longer: a child says which ending they like.

### Words and supports

Version, both, only, same, different, ending. Frames: "In both, ___." "Only in V2, ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing or drawing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 131 opens with a repair read from the records of days 129 and 130, then a retry.
