# Day 131: Reread and retry what the checks showed

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the records of day 129 and day 130's assessment, and the cycle 21 sheets from day 128. Sort children into three groups of up to ten by need: events and lesson; voices; versions. Mark today's six for the retry and the six for the oral card (children 13 to 18). Copy the retry cards below. Say aloud that every card is made up and a retry never replaces a check.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 129's and day 130's assessment: repair the commonest miss in the record. For events (error A), lay three event drawings of card F1 in order and say first, next, last (3 minutes). For versions (error D), hold V1 and V2 side by side and point to the words only one has (2 minutes). A lesson with no event behind it is error B.

#### 10 min. Reread

Pairs reread 03 and 04 and find the line that fits the item they missed: the lines in order, the line a lesson rests on, the line each animal says, or a line found in only one tale.

#### 15 min. Teacher repair rotation

Three groups of up to ten, five minutes each, about 25 seconds a child and the rest for changing places. The events and lesson group lays three drawn events in order and puts a sticky dot on the event a lesson rests on. The voices group says what each animal wants, then reads the two lines in two voices. The versions group holds two versions side by side and marks one detail only here and one in both. The other children practise with the strips in pairs.

#### 5 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which event shows the lesson?" Use card R1: pairs judge the given lesson "Bears are big" against the two events and fix it (2 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I would add ___" (2 minutes). Share one (1 minute).

#### 20 min. Retry

Each child retries the task that was missed with a fresh card, to a partner (12 minutes, six minutes each), then to you for the six heard (8 minutes, about 80 seconds each). Record it as a second sitting beside the first. It is practice and does not replace the check.

**R1, events and lesson.** Read twice: "Bear could not move a big log. Bear asked Mouse and Otter. They pushed together, and the log rolled." Child puts three event drawings in order and says the lesson that one event holds up.
**R2, voices.** Read twice: "Rabbit says, The storm is near, let us hide. Deer says, The storm is far, let us play." Child reads each line in its own voice and says what each wants.
**R3, versions.** Read twice: "Version A: Duck found a pond. Duck splashed in. Her friends came, and they swam. Version B: Duck found a pond. Duck splashed in. Her friends did not come, and Duck swam alone." Child marks one detail only in one version and one thing in both.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X131]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X131b]] (2 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Marking the uncovered basket fastens the lesson to the wrong event (error B).

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed to this part. Use it to retrieve: children write button and impress on slates and name the part not stressed. Review: schwa e and o and the front parts i-m and i-n (grade 2, unit 4, day 129).

#### 12 min. Reread the cycle 22 reader

Pairs read A Map in a Pocket aloud for accuracy, one page each (6 minutes). Then read the whole once more smoothly, pausing at each full stop (6 minutes, RF.2.4b). Reading a word from its first letter is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 13 to 18 read the cycle 22 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The others use tiles, the sort and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Sort

Pairs sort twelve slips into schwa e or o and the front part i-m or i-n: kitten, ribbon, button, mitten, pocket, taken; impress, impolite, impossible, inside, insect, indoor (9 minutes). Children say each word and where the weak sound sits. Each child then fixes four wrong spellings on a strip, checking each against the chart: pockit, takin, indor, impres (6 minutes).

#### 11 min. Use the pattern in sentences

Repair first (5 minutes): show the commonest cycle 21 miss from the day 128 sheets beside the right form and say the link. Then each child writes one sentence with a cycle 22 word (6 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Ask: "Which part of pocket is not stressed?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No strips: fold paper in three or in two. No tiles: paper squares. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: tell a short story you know.
**Skip.** The sort and the tiles.

- **10 min. Retry.** Tell a short story in order. Ask which event shows the lesson.
  **Say.** "Which event shows the lesson?" Expect: the event the lesson rests on.
- **10 min. Voices.** Read two lines in two voices and ask what each speaker wants.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes pocket and taken and says the part not stressed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X131]] and [[s04-X131b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the retry and the exit. Longer: a child makes a fresh card for a partner.

### Words and supports

Event, lesson, voice, version, only, both. Frames: "The lesson is ___ because ___." "Only in B, ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 132 opens with a five-minute repair on today's exit: if many marked the uncovered basket (error B), connect the lesson to one event. The six heard on the oral card tell you who needs a group.
