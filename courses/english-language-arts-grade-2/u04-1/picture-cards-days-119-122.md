# Picture cards, days 119 to 122

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 119 (s04-X119).** card 1, Beaver gathering sticks; card 2, Beaver building a wall; card 3, rain falling on the wall.
- **Day 120 (s04-X120).** card 1, Jay hiding acorns; card 2, Jay eating.
- **Day 121 (s04-X121).** card 1, a hand giving a crumb; card 2, a hand giving a crumb with an arrow back.
- **Day 122 (s04-X122).** card 1, a leaf in both scenes; card 2, a leaf in one scene only.

On day 119 print three beaver cards in a mixed row; the order of the story is not marked on the front. Keep each set in its own envelope for the day.
