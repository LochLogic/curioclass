# Picture cards, days 119 to 122

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with the passage it goes with named in small letters at the foot (for example "the fable, winter part"). These are drawings of the unit's own texts, not invented cases. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 119 (s04-X119v2).** card 1, the ants handing out grain at their nest; card 2, the grasshopper asking at the nest; card 3, the grasshopper wrapping her wings around herself while ants carry grain inside.
- **Day 120 (s04-X120v2).** card 1, the ant, carrying corn, speaking to the grasshopper; card 2, ants opening their store of grain in winter.
- **Day 121 (s04-X121v2).** card 1, the ant at her door asking the grasshopper a question; card 2, the grasshopper holding out an empty hand, asking for grain.
- **Day 122 (s04-X122v2).** card 1, a cold wind blowing over a bare field; card 2, the ants handing out grain at their nest.

On day 119 print three winter cards in a mixed row; the order of the story is not marked on the front. Keep each set in its own envelope for the day.
