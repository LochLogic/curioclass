# Sort strips, day 121

Six strips, each one line about the two tellings, and a three-part mat headed "Only in the first telling", "Only in the second telling" and "In both". Pairs sort the strips and read their mat aloud, differences first.

Write the strips from the two texts as follows, one per strip, in plain words a second grader can read:
1. the grasshopper sings all summer (both);
2. the grasshopper asks for a few grains (only in the first);
3. the grasshopper asks to borrow and promises to repay (only in the second);
4. a narrator says something about the ant (only in the second);
5. the ant gives a short answer about singing (both, with different words);
6. the grasshopper is hungry when the cold comes (both).

Check each strip against the two texts before printing. Mark the mat only with the headings, not the answers.
