# Story-map strips, days 119 and 120

One strip a pair. Three boxes in a row, headed Summer, The season changes and Winter, with room under each for a small drawing or a few words, and a line at the foot that reads "The event that shows the lesson". Leave a small circle in each box for the dot. The strip is blank and the story is the fable we read, so write "from the fable we read" in small letters.

Day 119: the teacher fills a large copy on the board while children draw one way to get ready. Day 120: each pair fills a strip and puts one dot on the event they think holds the lesson. Day 121: the strip is kept to find what changes in the second telling.
