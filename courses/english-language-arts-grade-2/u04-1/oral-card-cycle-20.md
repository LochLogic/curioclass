# Oral card, cycle 20 (days 120 to 123)

The card each of the day's rotating children reads to you, two minutes each, in this order: children 1 to 8 on day 120, 9 to 16 on day 121, 17 to 23 on day 122, 24 to 30 on day 123.

The card is the cycle 20 section of g2-u4-oral. Print it from there: the four isolated words, then the passage. The words and the passage are not copied into this piece, so that the check words stay unseen until they are used. Mark reading, the answer to the question and the reread on the record sheet.
