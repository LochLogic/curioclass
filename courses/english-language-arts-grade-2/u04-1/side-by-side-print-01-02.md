# Side-by-side print of the two tellings (days 121 and 122)

Layout only; the words are the texts g2-u4-01 (left) and g2-u4-02 (right), printed unchanged.

- Two columns on one landscape page, the left headed "First telling" and the right "Second telling". Keep the source note on each: the first follows a telling credited to Aesop in a book by Joseph Jacobs (1894); the second follows La Fontaine as put into English by Elizur Wright, a translation from the 1800s. Curio Class wrote the words of both.
- Line up the same moments across the page: the grasshopper singing, the season changing, the knock at the door, the request, the ant's reply. Where one telling has a moment the other does not, leave a gap on the other side.
- Print one highlighter color for the first telling and a different one for the second in the key at the top.
- Print the pair on one sheet per two children, large type, wide spacing between lines.
