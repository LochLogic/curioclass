# Task sheets for the response groups, day 116

Three named tasks for the children not at the teacher table. Each child chooses the one the written feedback names.

- **Rewrite one sentence.** Read your sentence aloud. Does it end? Write it again as a whole sentence.
- **Redraw one label.** Look at your label. Does it name the part? Draw it again so the part is clear.
- **Sort fact cards.** Sort your fact cards into: from a source, and not from a source. Put the second pile aside.
