# Section 3.11 opener: presenting the posters (days 113 to 116)

## What the section does

Thirty children present their posters over three days, ten a day, three minutes each including one listener question. Each child explains the poster in whole sentences, connects a day word to real use, contrasts two pairs of close words from a verse, uses a describing word for a thing and for an action, and, on day 115, explains a short process with linking words. Day 116 gives each child written feedback and one component to retry.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The teacher marks the presentation sheet for the ten children of the day while a panel of children marks the criteria chart. In the foundations block six more children read fresh words to the teacher each day. Nothing is recorded in these four days.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A whole sentence** has an end, not a trailing fragment.
- **A word is more than repeated.** The child says a real time to use it.
- **Close words differ in strength or manner:** whisper and shout, chilly and freezing.
- **Steps are joined,** with so, because or then, and not listed.

## The errors, in plain words

- A fragment where a sentence is asked (O).
- A word repeated with no real use (P), or two close words said to be the same (Q).
- A meaning the word does not have (AP).
- A describing word for an action put on a thing (R).
- Steps listed with no connection (B).
