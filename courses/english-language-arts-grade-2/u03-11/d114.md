# Day 114: Observe the second group presenting and using precise words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Seat children 11 to 20 at the front and a panel of ten from the rest, one question card each. Keep the criteria chart up. Make the three word cards for [[s03-A102]]. Mark today's six, set 2. Nothing new is taught.

### The day

#### 12 min. P6 decoding sample

Six children come to your table one at a time, 2 minutes each: ask [[s03-A102]]. The other twenty-four sort the au and aw sort cards in pairs.

#### 23 min. Independent matched reading

Each child reads Dawn in the Garden alone and tells a partner the card's answers (10 minutes), rereads one page and tells you one word fixed (8), then reads a page to a partner for expression (5). Circulate and note the support used.

#### 25 min. Flexible groups

Four groups at your table, 6 minutes each: S and T (silent, tulip, rival: cover the picture, map each syllable, blend); AM, Y and J (words from their own feedback); AO and AN (dawn, yawn, pause, saw on tiles). One minute to move. The others reread.

#### 30 min. Ten presentation turns (group 2)

Read the verse Over the Leaf once to the front group. Each turn is 3 minutes: start 15 seconds; the poster in whole sentences, observed (40); the word slender and a poster word, [[s03-A85]], [[s03-A86]] (30); the verse pairs, [[s03-A91]], [[s03-A92]] (40); one adjective and one adverb, [[s03-A96]], [[s03-A96b]] (20); the panelist holds up a card and you read it aloud, [[s03-A81]] (25); thanks and moving 10. Say aloud that every question is invented. The panel marks the chart; you fill the sheet. A child may pass any ask.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X114]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A word repeated with no real time is error P; a meaning the word lacks is error AP.

#### 25 min. Quiet work

Hand back day 113's assessment: children revise the poem drawing from the written feedback (5 minutes), then revise the written story (15). Read a band text from 01-b to 01-e quietly (5). While they work, hear the ten presenters, 30 seconds each, on the poem's three asks from day 113 (what happens, the feeling at the start, the feeling at the end). Collect the revisions at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards by hand. A child who cannot speak to a group talks to one panelist; pointing is a start.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: draw a flower on paper. No texts: read any short page.
**Skip.** The panel and the word cards.

- **10 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X114]]
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you what a visitor does at a flower.
  **Say.** "What does the visitor do?" Expect: a whole sentence, such as The bee drinks nectar and carries pollen.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud and tells what happened.
- **10 min. Fix.** Your child reads their bee story and adds one thing a listener could see.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws that thing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reread to partners. Longer: add a second contrast of the child's own.

### Words and supports

Poster, listener, slender, real use. Frames: "The poster says ___." "A ___ is slower than a ___." A child may rehearse in the home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 115 holds the last ten posters and adds a short process joined with so or because. Today's exit tells you who gives a real time for a word. Written feedback on the talks goes back on day 116.
