# Day 115: Observe the last group and a linked process

## Teacher page

### Before class

Seat children 21 to 30 at the front and a panel of ten from the rest, one question card each. Keep the criteria chart up. Make the three word cards for [[s03-A103]]. Mark today's six, set 3. Nothing new is taught.

### The day

#### 12 min. P6 decoding sample

Six children come to your table one at a time, 2 minutes each: ask [[s03-A103]]. The other twenty-four sort the au and aw sort cards in pairs.

#### 23 min. Independent matched reading

Each child reads Dawn in the Garden alone and tells a partner the card's answers (10 minutes), rereads one page and tells you one word fixed (8), then reads a page to a partner for expression (5).

#### 25 min. Flexible groups

Four groups at your table, 6 minutes each: S and T (human, moment, frozen: cover the picture, map each syllable, blend); AM, Y and J (words from their own feedback); AO and AN (crawl, launch, fawn, vault on tiles). One minute to move. The others reread.

#### 30 min. Ten presentation turns (group 3)

Read the verse Along the Stem once to the front group. Each turn is 3 minutes: start 10 seconds; the poster, then the process in three to five steps, observed (35); the steps joined with so or because, [[s03-A100]] (15); the word settle and a poster word, [[s03-A87]], [[s03-A88]] (30); the verse pairs, [[s03-A93]], [[s03-A94]] (35); one adjective and one adverb, [[s03-A97]], [[s03-A97b]] (20); the panelist holds up a card and you read it aloud, [[s03-A82]] (25); thanks and moving 10. Say aloud that every question is invented. The panel marks the chart; you fill the sheet. A child may pass any ask.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X115]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A list of steps with no joining word is error B; a seed planted by the visitor is error A.

#### 25 min. Quiet work

Hand back day 114's assessment: each child reads a band text from 01-b to 01-e quietly (10 minutes), then pairs tell the planned bee story with its drawing, no recorder (15). While they work, hear the ten presenters, 30 seconds each, on the poem's three asks from day 113, and score the poem from the day 113 reading and day 114 revision. Collect the revised stories at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards by hand. A child who cannot speak to a group talks to one panelist; pointing is a start.

### At home

**Swap.** No texts: read any short page. No partner: tell it to a toy.
**Skip.** The panel and the word cards.

- **10 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X115]]
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you how a flower visit goes, step by step.
  **Say.** "How does one step lead to the next?" Expect: a sentence joined with so or because, such as Pollen sticks to the bee, so it carries it away.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud and tells what happened.
- **10 min. Story.** Your child tells their bee story to you with the drawing.
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says what to change.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reread to partners. Longer: add a second contrast of the child's own.

### Words and supports

Poster, listener, step, settle, real use. Frames: "The poster says ___." "A ___ is slower than a ___." A child may rehearse in the home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 116 hands back the written feedback with the one part to retry and works in four response groups. Today's exit tells you who joins steps (a bare list is error B). The poem is scored today.
