# Day 116: Use the feedback and retry one part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the four response groups from the record sheets of day 113, day 114 and day 115: meaning (error P, error Q, error AP), sentences (error O, error R), facts (error A, error C, error D, error E) and process (error B). Write each child's feedback note with one part to retry. Set the recording corner for the final ten. Make three word cards for [[s03-A119]]. Mark today's six, set 4; the last six are heard on day 117.

### The day

#### 12 min. P6 decoding sample

Six children come to your table one at a time, 2 minutes each: ask [[s03-A119]]. The other twenty-four sort the au and aw sort cards in pairs.

#### 23 min. Independent matched reading

As day 113: read Dawn in the Garden alone and tell a partner the card's answers (10 minutes), reread one page and tell you one word fixed (8 minutes), read a page to a partner for expression (5 minutes).

#### 25 min. Recorder window

The final ten children each make one rehearsal recording of their planned story with the drawing beside them, 2 minutes each plus 5 minutes of changeovers; you stay at the corner. The other twenty tell their story to a partner and choose one wording to change. Say aloud that rehearsal recordings stay in class and a child may record in a pair. A live telling in place of a recording is error U; a voice the recorder cannot pick up is error AA, so say one phrase a hand away from the device. Written feedback follows after class. If the device fails, hear each of the ten live and record on day 117.

#### 5 min. Hand back

Each child gets the written feedback on the talk (day 113's assessment, day 114's assessment and day 115's assessment go back today) with the one part to retry circled, and reads it to a partner. Collect the revised stories at the end of the day. Say the four groups aloud.

#### 32 min. Response groups

Four teacher turns of 8 minutes, from the record sheet; the others do the named task their note names from the task sheets: rewrite one sentence, redraw one label, or sort fact cards. Say aloud that every case is invented.
- Meaning: put "Dusty means dusty" beside a real place the word fits (error P); say which of whisper and shout is quieter (error Q); point to the words in a line that give a meaning (error AP).
- Sentences: lay a doer card and an action card side by side and say the whole line (error O); name the action first, then choose its describing word (error R).
- Facts: hold up a pollen card and a seed card and sort the lines (error A); finger on the line, read it, fix the fact (error C); ask of each fact, "Does it answer?" (error E); find one new fact and point to it (error D).
- Process: lay two step cards with an arrow and join them with a word of your choice (error B).

#### 3 min. Retry

Pairs, all at once, 1 minute a child: each retries the part the note circled; the partner ticks it on the note. The original observation stays; the retry is marked shown with help.

#### 15 min. Story

Children revise the written story from the feedback (7 minutes), then tell the planned story to a partner with the drawing (8 minutes). The first twenty do not record yet.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X116]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A fragment is error O; a fact about the pollen's color is error E.

### Other rooms

No device: hear the ten live and record on day 117. A child who cannot write dictates. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the lines on paper. No recorder: say it aloud.
**Skip.** The group tasks and the recording.

- **10 min. Fix.** Your child reads the note on their talk and says the part again.
  **Say.** "What will you change?" Expect: one named part, such as a whole sentence or a fact from the source.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud and tells what happened.
- **10 min. Story.** Your child tells their bee story with the drawing.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X116]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the hand back, the child's own group, the retry and the exit; the story is a single revision. Longer: a child retries a second part.

### Words and supports

Feedback, retry, doer, action, step. Frames: "The poster says ___." "First ___, so ___." A child may point to the words that give a meaning.

### Tomorrow

Day 117 records the first twenty stories. Today's retries and exit tell you who still needs a whole sentence, a source fact or a joined step; story feedback goes back for the last revision on day 117, and the final ten's rehearsal recordings get written feedback after class.
