# Exit cards, days 113 to 116

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 113 (s03-X113).** This card is invented for practice. Show the poster note: "A beetle walks on the petals. Pollen sticks to its body." A listener asks: "What does the beetle carry?" Say: "Answer the listener in a whole sentence."
- **Day 114 (s03-X114).** Say: "Tell about a real time you would use the word scratchy."
- **Day 115 (s03-X115).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A moth rests on a pale flower at night. Pollen sticks to its body. It flies to a new flower. Some pollen falls off." Say: "Tell how the moth helps the new flower, joining the steps with so or because."
- **Day 116 (s03-X116).** This card is invented for practice. Show the feedback: "Say where the pollen goes." Show the source: "The beetle lands on the next flower and some pollen rubs off there." Say: "Say one whole sentence that answers the feedback, using the source."
