# Exit cards, days 113 to 116

Say aloud each time a card is invented: "This card is made up for practice." Day 115 reads a true paragraph of g2-u3-01 instead. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 113 (s03-X113).** This card is invented for practice. Show the poster note: "A beetle walks on the petals. Pollen sticks to its body." A listener asks: "What does the beetle carry?" Say: "Answer the listener in a whole sentence."
- **Day 114 (s03-X114).** Say: "Tell about a real time you would use the word scratchy."
- **Day 115 (s03-X115v2).** Read aloud twice article 01-d (A Bat Visits at Night), paragraph 2 (A long nose and a long tongue), from the teacher copy of The Secret World of Pollination (g2-u3-01); it is true, not invented. Say: "Tell how the bat helps the next flower, joining the steps with so or because."
- **Day 116 (s03-X116).** This card is invented for practice. Show the feedback: "Say where the pollen goes." Show the source: "The beetle lands on the next flower and some pollen rubs off there." Say: "Say one whole sentence that answers the feedback, using the source."
