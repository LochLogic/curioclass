# Day 113: Observe the first group presenting posters

## Teacher page

### Before class

Seat children 1 to 10 and a panel of ten from the rest at the front, one question card each. Put up the criteria chart. Review: the chart (grade 2, unit 3, day 109). Make the three word cards for [[s03-A101]]. Mark today's six, set 1. Nothing new is taught.

### The day

#### 12 min. P6 decoding sample

Six children come to your table one at a time, 2 minutes each: ask [[s03-A101]]. The other twenty-four sort the au and aw sort cards in pairs.

#### 23 min. Independent matched reading

Each child reads Dawn in the Garden alone and tells a partner the card's answers (10 minutes), rereads one page and tells you one word fixed (8), then reads a page to a partner for expression (5).

#### 25 min. Flexible groups

Four groups at your table, 6 minutes each: S and T (tiger, spider, lilac: cover the picture, map each syllable, blend); AM, Y and J (words from their own feedback); AO and AN (hawk, claw, fault, cause on tiles). One minute to move. The others correct their day 112 spelling from the feedback (scored on 114) and reread.

#### 30 min. Ten presentation turns (group 1)

Read the verse Above the Petal once to the front group. Each turn is 3 minutes: start 15 seconds; the poster, [[s03-A78]], [[s03-A79]], [[s03-A79b]] (30); the word dusty and a poster word, [[s03-A83]], [[s03-A84]] (25); the verse pairs, [[s03-A89]], [[s03-A90]] (40); one adjective and one adverb, [[s03-A95]], [[s03-A95b]] (20); the panelist holds up a card and you read it aloud, [[s03-A80]] (25); thanks and moving 25. Say aloud that every question is invented. The panel marks the chart; you fill the sheet. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X113]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A fragment such as "On its body" is error O.

#### 25 min. Quiet work

Each child reads the poem Before I Fly alone first, then draws what happens and the bee at the start and at the end (5 minutes). Revise the day 112 comparison answer from your feedback (5). Draft the written story from the plan page (15); error V from day 112 redraws by what a listener needs to see, error E gives a reason from the story. While they draft, hear the ten presenters, 30 seconds each: [[s03-A98]], [[s03-A99]], [[s03-A99b]]. Read the poem aloud only after a first try, as help. Collect the work at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards by hand. A child who cannot speak to a group talks to one panelist.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: draw a flower and a bee on paper. No reader: read any short page.
**Skip.** The panel and the word cards.

- **10 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X113]]
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells you two facts about a bee and a flower.
  **Say.** "What does the bee carry?" Expect: a whole sentence, such as The bee carries pollen on its legs.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud and tells what happened.
- **10 min. Story.** Your child tells a bee story with a start, a middle and an end.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws one part of it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: reread to partners. Longer: add a second contrast.

### Words and supports

Poster, listener, contrast, real use. Frames: "The poster says ___." "A ___ is slower than a ___." A child may rehearse in the home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 114 holds the second ten posters. Today's exit and sheet tell you who answers a listener in a whole sentence (a fragment is error O). Talk feedback goes back on day 116; the poem drawings are revised tomorrow.
