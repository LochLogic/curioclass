# Note slip and notes sheet (days 88 to 118)

**Note slip.** A small slip with two boxes. Box 1: the fact in a few words (no more than about five). Box 2: the tag, which says which card or article gave it.

**Notes sheet.** A page for one child that holds the slips, one under the other, with the question written across the top.

A slip is torn paper in a room with nothing.
