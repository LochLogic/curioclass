# Exit cards, days 88 to 91

Day 88's cards are invented: say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at their foot. Days 89 to 91 use parts of g2-u3-01, which are true. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 88 (s03-X88).** This exit is invented for practice. Give two cards. Card 1 (text): "A bean seed sleeps in a dry jar. When it gets wet, it swells. Then a tiny root pushes out." Card 2 (picture of a bean seed in damp soil; caption): "Water and warmth help a seed wake up." Read card 1 aloud twice and read the caption. Say: "Write one note, in a few words, that answers: What does a bean seed need to start growing?"
- **Day 88 (s03-X88b).** Same cards. Say: "Say where you found your note."
- **Day 89 (s03-X89v2).** Show the teacher copy of article 01-e (A Hummingbird Visits) in The Secret World of Pollination (g2-u3-01) and read only its three bold headings aloud. This is true, not invented. Say: "You want to know what colors a hummingbird likes. Which heading do you try first?"
- **Day 90 (s03-X90v2).** Read aloud twice article 01-c (A Butterfly Visits), paragraph 2, from "A butterfly perches", without its bold heading; nothing printed. This is true, not invented. Say: "Tell me what this paragraph is mostly about."
- **Day 91 (s03-X91v2).** Two slips cut from article 01-e, paragraph 2: "The hummingbird pushes its bill deep into the flower." and "Its face is dusted with pollen." These are true, not invented. Say: "Say how the first step leads to the second, with so or because."
