# Exit cards, days 88 to 91

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 88 (s03-X88).** This exit is invented for practice. Give two cards. Card 1 (text): "A bean seed sleeps in a dry jar. When it gets wet, it swells. Then a tiny root pushes out." Card 2 (picture of a bean seed in damp soil; caption): "Water and warmth help a seed wake up." Read card 1 aloud twice and read the caption. Say: "Write one note, in a few words, that answers: What does a bean seed need to start growing?"
- **Day 88 (s03-X88b).** Same cards. Say: "Say where you found your note."
- **Day 89 (s03-X89).** The page "Ants at Work" with two subheadings, "Where ants live" and "What ants eat", each over a short paragraph. Say: "You want to know what ants eat. Which subheading do you try first?"
- **Day 90 (s03-X90).** Read aloud twice, nothing printed: "Ants build nests under rocks. The nest has many small rooms. Ants keep food in some rooms and raise their young in others." Say: "Tell me what this paragraph is mostly about."
- **Day 91 (s03-X91).** Two slips: "The seed gets wet." and "The seed swells." Say: "Say how the first step leads to the second, with so or because."
