# Joint chart sheet (days 88 to 118)

A sheet for the board. The class's question across the top. Under it, a space for slips from every child, in rows. Beside the chart a column for arrow cards, so the slips can be read as a chain.

Each slip stays on the chart under the question it answers. A slip that does not answer the question is moved to a side column called "true but not for this question".
