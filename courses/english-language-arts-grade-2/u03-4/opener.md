# Section 3.4 opener: asking, finding and noting (days 88 to 91)

## What the section does

The class learns the four moves of a small research cycle: ask, find, note and share. Children write a short note, in their own few words, with a tag that says where it came from. They use headings and captions to find a fact fast, name the topic and focus of a paragraph, and read the joint chart of the class's notes as a chain. Cycle 15 teaches the endings -ck, -k and -ic and the prefixes un- and re-.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 88 sets up the research-cycle chart and the first note slip. Days 89, 90 and 91 have no exit; the evidence is the note slips, the notes sheet and the heard column. In the foundations block the cycle 15 reader *The Attic Trunk* is read, sorted and spelled.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A note is a few words, not a copied sentence.** The tag says which card or article gave the fact.
- **A feature leads straight to a fact.** A heading tells where to look.
- **A joint chart shows the class's thinking.** Slips under one question can be read in order, with arrows.
- **A fact must answer the question.** A true fact that does not answer it is put to the side.

## The errors, in plain words

- The note is copied word for word (D).
- A fact is true but does not answer the question (E).
- A fact is tagged to the wrong source (AE).
- A prefix is treated as if it meant nothing (AL).
