# Day 91: Join notes into steps with so and because

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the research-cycle chart and the step chain up, with the joint chart and its slips from days 88 to 90. Put out the note slips and notes sheets from days 89 and 90. Print a draft strip for each child; cut so and because arrow cards for each pair. Pull three model slips from the joint chart (the anther makes pollen; the bee brushes the anther; pollen reaches the stigma). Mark today's six (group 4 of five in cycle 15) on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 90's notes sheets and its exit (s03-X90). Run at most two branches, the two most missed. If many named one detail for the whole paragraph (error AC), read the sentences again, then name what joins them before naming one detail. If many copied a sentence (error D), cover the lines and say the fact in four words before writing it. If many laid notes in a row with no joining word (error B), lay an arrow card, so or because, between two slips and say the link aloud. If many gave the right fact with the wrong tag (error AE), put a finger on its line and read the tag from it. Children repair one slip on scrap paper.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today we join our notes into steps. A good explanation says why each step happens, not only what comes next." Review: the step chain and arrow cards (grade 2, unit 3, day 79).

#### 12 min. Model the chain

Whole class. Lay the three model slips in order on the board with the two arrow cards between them (2 minutes). Read each link aloud and children echo it: "The bee brushes the anther, so pollen sticks to the bee. Because pollen sticks to the bee, some pollen reaches the stigma." (4 minutes). Point to each arrow and ask: "Does this arrow say why the next step happens?" Children say "The arrow shows ___ because ___" (3 minutes). Hold up a card with a row of the same three slips and no arrow card between them: say a row only shows what comes next, and an arrow tells why (3 minutes). Review: say a step connection with so (grade 2, unit 3, day 85).

#### 15 min. Pairs build a chain

Pairs choose three of their own slips about how pollen moves from the anther to a second flower, using the slips from days 89 and 90 and the joint chart if they need a third (3 minutes). They order the slips, lay an arrow card between each two, and say each link with so or because (4 minutes). Each child then writes one sentence for each arrow on the student page, a few words each (5 minutes). Hear six pairs, about 1 minute each, during the building and writing (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help (after "Why does the next step happen?") or not yet seen. Scribe for any child who asks.

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Does each arrow say why the next step happens?" Children say "The arrow shows ___ because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class naming one arrow that really gives a reason (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child writes the explanation on the draft strip, a few words in each box: First ___. Then ___. So ___. A child who is ready writes the ending in the last box. Children use their own words, not the slips' words. Scribe for any child who asks.

#### 5 min. Share

Partners read their strips to each other, and each puts a tick beside the link they believe (3 minutes). Collect the strips (2 minutes). Judge them later with three look-fors: each note answers the question and is in the child's words; each note has a right tag (from the slips); the arrows say why the next step happens, not only what comes next. Record shown, shown with help or not yet seen. No score.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only. Write yesterday's commonest miss beside the right word and read it aloud. For attic read as house (error S), cover the picture and map each letter. For magic with a long a (error T), say the short vowel before ic, then blend. For pak or duk (error Y), write pack and duck with the ck whole. For unlock as lock or replay as play (error AL), cover un- or re- and say what is left. For would as wood (error AM), star the odd part and write it twice from memory.

#### 15 min. Fresh passage and dictation

Read the title aloud: A Picnic on the Deck. Say the passage is new and made up for practice. Pairs read it, one sentence each, then swap (5 minutes). Ask after reading: "What does Rick refill, and who waddles up?" Children answer from the words, never from a picture or a first letter (2 minutes). Dictate stick, unfold and then the sentence "We could unpack the sack" (5 minutes); children write on the student page and check each word on the word wall. Star the odd part in could (3 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 4 of five in cycle 15) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: the six join, ten to a station; groups rotate. Tiles: build stick, unfold, picnic and comic. Sort: the eleven cycle 15 cards by ending. Reread: A Picnic on the Deck and The Attic Trunk. Mark the heard column.

#### 4 min. Exit

Hand out nothing. Say: "These slips are made up for practice." Show the two seed slips and read each aloud. Ask [[s03-X91]]: every child tells a partner; hear the six, about 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "Why does the second step happen?") or not yet seen. Tag error B for a link with no reason.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which part of would is the odd part?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strip as four boxes with arrows on a sheet and write the passage on a sheet. No pair sets: lay the model slips on a table and work with one pair at a time. A child who cannot write says each box and you write it, recorded as help. A tag may be a drawn symbol. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No arrow cards: write so and because on two scraps. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Slips.** Write three slips: the anther makes pollen; the bee brushes the anther; pollen reaches the stigma.
- **10 min. Link.** Your child lays the slips in order with so between the first two and because between the last two, and says each link.
  **Say.** "Does the arrow say why the next step happens?" Expect: yes, it says why.
- **10 min. Strip.** Your child writes First, Then, So in a few words each.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say stick and unfold. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X91]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second round of stations and the share; keep the model, the chain and the strip. Longer: a child adds a fourth slip and a third arrow card.

### Words and supports

Step, chain, arrow, link, so, because, reason, pollen, anther, stigma. Frames: "The arrow shows ___ because ___." "So ___." "Because ___, ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may lay the slips and point to the arrow instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The draft strips, the slips on the chart, the oral card and the heard column are the evidence for section 3.4. If many laid steps with no reason (error B), carry the arrow card into day 92's gathering. Day 92 gathers bee facts from a new article without the teacher choosing the answers, and gives the cycle 15 written check in the foundations block.
