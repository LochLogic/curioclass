# Day 89: Use features and a diagram to find facts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the research-cycle chart and the step chain up. On a new joint chart sheet write the question: What do the anther and the stigma do? Print 01-a with its diagram for each pair. Write eight word slips for each group (kick, mask, comic, unpack, replay, desk, duck, attic). Mark today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 15) on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 88's exit (s03-X88). Run at most two branches, the two most missed. Hold up pine card 1 and say it is made up for practice. If many copied the sentence (error D), cover the card; children say the fact in four words to a partner, then write it on a slip and tag it. If many gave a fact that does not answer (error E), hold up the question card and ask "Does it answer?". If many gave the wrong card (error AE), put a finger on the line and read the tag from it. Children write the repaired slip on scrap paper.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today our question is What do the anther and the stigma do? The answer is in Pollen on the Move. We use its features to find it fast." Review: the anther and the stigma (grade 2, unit 3, day 78). Review: the feature route card (grade 2, unit 2, day 47): subheading, bold word, caption and labels.

#### 10 min. Find with features

Pairs have the 01-a page and the route card. Children read only the features: subheadings, bold words and diagram labels; you read every line aloud. Model one route for anther (3 minutes): the subheading The parts of a flower that matter, then the bold word anther, then read aloud "The anther is a part that makes pollen. It sits on a thin stalk." Each pair traces the same route on its card with a finger (3 minutes). Children say "The feature that led me was ___" to a partner (2 minutes). Hear two pairs (2 minutes).

#### 14 min. Pairs gather and tag

Pairs find the line for the stigma by its own route: the subheading, then the bold word stigma. Read aloud only "The stigma is a part at the center. It is sticky at the top." Then they find how the anther is used in step 2 under How a bee helps: the steps, and how the stigma is used in step 4. Read aloud step 2 up to "pollen sticks to its fuzzy body" and step 4 from "Some pollen rubs off". Each pair writes two slips, one for the anther and one for the stigma, in a few of their own words, each with a tag: the subheading or the bold word that led them (6 minutes). Each slip says what the part does, not only what it is (error AF): the anther makes pollen and brushes it onto the bee; the stigma is sticky and takes pollen from the bee. Pairs say each slip aloud (2 minutes). Hear six pairs, about 1 minute each (6 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help (after "What does it do?") or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Add from the diagram

Review: reading a diagram's arrows (grade 2, unit 3, day 81). Pairs look at the labels and the four numbered arrows. Model arrow 3 (3 minutes): "The words say pollen rubs off on the stigma. The arrow adds where: from the bee onto the stigma of a second flower." Write the slip "bee to second flower's stigma" (tag: diagram). Pairs read arrow 4, which runs from the stigma down into the flower, and write one slip with the tag diagram saying what that arrow adds to the words (4 minutes). Say aloud what each arrow adds (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Which feature led you to your fact fastest, and how do you know?" Children say "The ___ led me because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class saying which feature came up most (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 4 min. Share on the chart

Each pair puts one slip on the joint chart (15 pairs); read each aloud with its tag (2 minutes). The class moves any slip that tells only what a part is, or is another fact, to the side column, true but not for this question (error E), and says why (1 minute). Read one tag aloud, finger on its line (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only. Write yesterday's commonest miss beside the right word. For reading attic as house from a picture (error S), cover the picture, map the letters and blend. For magic with a long a or comic with a long o (error T), say the short vowel before ic, then blend. For pak or duk (error Y), write pack and duck with the ck whole. For unlock as lock or replay as play (error AL), cover un- or re-, say what is left, then say what the prefix adds. For would as wood (error AM), star the odd part and read it.

#### 15 min. Read The Attic Trunk

Say the title aloud; it is not decoded. Read page 1 aloud as the first reading (3 minutes). Pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4: you read a page, they read it back, one sentence at a time, with the picture covered (7 minutes). Ask after each page: "Who is in it, and what is there?" Children answer from the words (2 minutes). Say again a word that tripped many: map the letters, blend, then check it makes sense in the sentence. Dictate kick and unpack and children write each (3 minutes), then check against the word wall.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 15) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: the six join, ten to a station; groups rotate. Tiles: build unpack, replay, mask and comic. Sort: the eight word slips by ending, then spell two. Reread: The Attic Trunk, pages 1 to 4. Mark the heard column.

#### 4 min. Exit

Hand out nothing. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show the Ants at Work page and read its two subheadings aloud. Ask [[s03-X89]]: every child tells a partner; hear the six, about 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "What do you want to know?") or not yet seen. Tag error AB for a subheading that does not lead to the fact.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What does the picture not tell you that the letters did?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the diagram by hand, with its four arrows and six labels. No pair sets: one 01-a page on the board, a pair at a time. A child who cannot write says the slip and you write it; the tag may be a drawn symbol (a heading box, a word box, an arrow). Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No printed page: draw a flower with a bee, an anther and a stigma.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Read.** Read your child the lines about the anther and the stigma from 01-a, or tell them: the anther makes pollen; the stigma is sticky.
- **10 min. Slips.** Your child writes two slips, anther and stigma, each saying what the part does, with a tag.
  **Say.** "What does the stigma do?" Expect: it is sticky and takes pollen from the bee.
- **5 min. Diagram.** Your child points to arrow 3 and says where it goes.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads The Attic Trunk aloud to you, one page at a time, then spells kick.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X89]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the diagram part and the second round of stations; keep the features, the slips and the reader. Longer: a child finds a feature that leads to nectar and says the route.

### Words and supports

Feature, subheading, bold word, label, diagram, arrow, anther, stigma, pollen. Frames: "The feature that led me was ___." "The ___ led me because ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the feature instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The slips on the joint chart and the exit are today's evidence, with the oral card and the heard column. If many wrote what a part is but not what it does (error AF), or gave the right fact with the wrong tag (error AE), day 90 opens with a 5-minute repair: trace one fact back to its line and read the tag, or join the word anther to the step it helps explain. Day 90 reads 01-a for its gist and takes two notes from one paragraph.
