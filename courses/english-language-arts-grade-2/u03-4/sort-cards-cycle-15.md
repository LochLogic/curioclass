# Sort cards, cycle 15 (day 90)

Eleven cards, one word on each, sorted by the ending: **-ck**, **-k**, **-ic**. Then four prefix cards are sorted by **un-** or **re-**.

pack, kick, duck, deck, desk, mask, tusk, attic, magic, comic, picnic

unlock, unpack, replay, rewind

Key: -ck: pack, kick, duck, deck. -k: desk, mask, tusk. -ic: attic, magic, comic, picnic. un-: unlock, unpack. re-: replay, rewind.

Each child spells two words and uses each in a sentence.
