# Day 88: Find a fact and say where it came from

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the research-cycle chart; keep the step chain and arrow cards on the wall. Put the joint chart sheet on the board and write the question on it: How does the wind help a pine tree? Cut the slips from the lists below, and write eight word slips for each group (pack, kick, desk, mask, attic, magic, comic, picnic). Keep the exit cards 88 to 91 aside. Mark today's six on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 4 min. Retrieve

Practice only, no marks. Review: the research-cycle chart (grade 1, unit 2, day 50): ask, find, note, share. Say each line twice and children point to the box it belongs to: "I wonder what a bat eats." (Ask) "I look in a card for the line." (Find) "I write the fact in a few words." (Note) "I tell the group what we found." (Share). Then everyone says the four words with a hand move each.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today we ask one question, find a fact in two cards and judge notes. The cards are made up for practice." Point to the question on the joint chart sheet. Say that a note holds a fact in a few words and a tag, and that the tag says where the fact came from. Review: the note slip and source tag (grade 2, unit 1, day 16).

#### 8 min. Hear two cards

Read the pine cards aloud. Card 1 twice (3 minutes): "Some trees, such as pine trees, do not need an animal to move their pollen. The wind carries it from tree to tree." Then describe card 2, a picture of a pine tree with a yellow cloud drifting from its branches, and read its caption once: "Wind carries yellow pollen away from a pine tree." Nothing on either card is marked. Partners tell each other what each card says, card 1 first (3 minutes). Hear two pairs, then ask the class which card has a picture (2 minutes). Wind is one carrier of pollen; most of this unit is about animals, so say so.

#### 12 min. Model: ask, find, judge notes

Whole class. Point to Ask on the chart and read the question (1 minute). Model Find on card 1: read it again slowly, put a finger under the line that tells how the wind helps, and say the fact in four words aloud, wind carries pollen along (2 minutes). Then hold up four ready slips, one at a time, and children judge each with a thumb:
- "wind carries pollen" (tag: card 1). Ask: "Does this note answer the question?" Children say "The note answers the question because ___". Put it on the joint chart (2 minutes).
- "a pine is a tree" (tag: card 1). True, but it does not answer. Children say "The note does not answer the question because ___" (error E). It goes in the side column called true but not for this question (2 minutes).
- "Some trees, such as pine trees, do not need an animal to move their pollen." (tag: card 1). Every word is copied. Children say how it differs from the first slip: a note is a few words of your own (error D) (2 minutes).
- "wind carries pollen from tree to tree" (tag: picture). Say: "Show me where the tag says this came from." Children see that tree to tree is on card 1, so the tag is wrong (error AE) (3 minutes).

No child writes a note in this part.

#### 12 min. Pairs judge and place slips

Pairs have both cards, ten counters and six practice slips. Say these are made up for practice. The slips, each with its tag:
1. "pollen goes tree to tree" (card 1)
2. "yellow pollen drifts away" (picture)
3. "a pine has branches" (picture)
4. "yellow pollen" (card 1)
5. "Wind carries yellow pollen away from a pine tree." (picture)
6. "wind takes pollen off" (picture)

For each slip, the pair sets a counter on the line or the picture it came from and puts the slip on ready, fix it or side. Slip 3 is true but does not answer (error E); slip 4 has the wrong tag, since yellow is only in the picture's caption (error AE); slip 5 is copied word for word (error D); slips 1, 2 and 6 are ready. A pair that finishes early says what it would change on each fix it slip. Walk and hear six pairs, about 1 minute each. Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "Does it answer the question?") or not yet seen. Each pair then swaps one ready slip with another pair, who judge it with a thumb and say why.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Does your note answer the question, and why?" Each child picks one slip from their six. Children say "It answers the question because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 8 min. Exit

Hand out the two bean exit cards and say: "This card is made up for practice." Read card 1 aloud twice and read the caption once (2 minutes). Ask one thing at a time: [[s03-X88]]. Children write one note in the first box of the student page (2 minutes). Then ask [[s03-X88b]]. Children write the tag in the second box (1 minute). Hear the six say the tag aloud, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes); the others finish writing. Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "Does it answer the question?") or not yet seen. Collect the pages. Tag error E for a fact that does not answer, D for a copied sentence and AE for the right fact with the wrong card.

#### 5 min. Share

Each pair puts one slip from ready on the joint chart. Read each fact aloud, and the class says whether it answers the question. Slips in the side column stay there. The joint chart stays up to day 118.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only. This follows day 87's check (items s03-A25 to s03-A28). Write the commonest miss beside the right word. If many left off a last syllable (error Y), write noodl beside noodle and wrinkl beside wrinkle, and children clap each word's syllables, then point to the le that finishes it. For swapped last letters (error AN), spell the word from the letters aloud while you point. For a card answer that contradicted the card (error C), reread the card and ask what it says. A copied draft (error J) goes back to the dictionary entry.

#### 15 min. Teach: -ck, -k, -ic and un-, re-

Retrieve the short vowel patterns: children say cat and duck, then say the vowel sound in each (2 minutes). Review: ck after a short vowel at the end of a word (grade 1, unit 2, day 49): write pack, kick and duck, map each sound, blend and say the word (2 minutes). Teach -k after a consonant: desk, mask (2 minutes). Teach -ic at the end of a longer word, which says ik: attic, magic, comic, picnic; map each, then blend one syllable at a time (3 minutes). Review: the prefixes un- and re- (grade 2, unit 2, day 50): cover the prefix, read the base, then add the prefix and say what it adds, for unpack, replay and rewind (2 minutes). Review: would (grade 1, unit 4, day 122). Map could the same way and star the odd part in each (2 minutes). Dictate "I can pack a bag" and children write it, then check pack on the word wall (2 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 1 of five in cycle 15) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Round 2 is 12 minutes: the six join, ten to a station. Round 3 is 12 minutes: groups rotate again. The six have the table and two stations; the others have all three. Tiles: build pack, kick, desk and attic. Sort: the eight word slips by -ck, -k and -ic. Reread: The Stable Gate. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What two letters end pack?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards and slips by hand on torn paper. No pair sets: one set on the board, a pair at a time. A child who cannot write dictates the note and tag and you write them, recorded as help. A tag may be a drawn symbol. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use small scraps. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Cards.** Read your child the two pine cards, card 1 twice.
- **10 min. Judge.** Show the slip "wind carries pollen" and the slip "a pine is a tree".
  **Say.** "Which note answers how the wind helps?" Expect: wind carries pollen.
- **5 min. Chart.** Your child says ask, find, note, share, with a hand move for each.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say pack and desk. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X88]] [[s03-X88b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the stations' second round and the share; keep the judging, the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child finds one more fact on card 2 and says which slip pile it belongs in.

### Words and supports

Question, note, tag, card, picture, source, answer, wind, pine. Frames: "I found it in ___." "The note answers the question because ___." "The note does not answer the question because ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the line or the card instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can write a fact in a few words and say its card. If many copied the sentence (error D), day 89 opens with a 5-minute repair: cover the card, say the fact in four words, then write it and tag it. If many gave a true fact that does not answer (error E) or the wrong card (error AE), use the question card or a finger on the line in the same slot. Day 89 gathers facts for a new question from the features and diagram of 01-a.
