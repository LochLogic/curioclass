# Research-cycle chart (days 88 to 118)

A tall sheet for the wall with four boxes joined by arrows: **Ask**, **Find**, **Note**, **Share**. Under each box one short line.

- **Ask:** What do we want to know?
- **Find:** Where does a source say it?
- **Note:** Write it in a few of my own words. Write where it came from.
- **Share:** Tell the group what we found.

The chart stays up to day 118.
