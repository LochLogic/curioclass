# Day 90: Read for the gist and take brief notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the research-cycle chart, the step chain and the unit 2 chart (topic and focus) up. On a new joint chart sheet write the question: Why do pollinators matter? Print 01-a for each pair and the notes sheet for each child. Put each group's eleven word cards and four prefix cards in a bag. Mark today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 15) on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 89's slips on the joint chart and its exit (s03-X89). Run at most two branches, the two most missed. If many chose a subheading that does not lead to the fact (error AB), read the question and both subheadings aloud and ask which one names the thing asked about. If many gave the right fact with the wrong tag (error AE), put one chart slip beside 01-a; a child puts a finger on its line and reads the tag from it. If many wrote what a part is but not what it does (error AF), join the word anther to the step it helps explain: it makes pollen, so a bee can carry it. If many copied a sentence (error D), cover the lines and say the fact in four words before writing. Children repair one slip on scrap paper.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Gist means what a whole text is mostly about. It is the topic you named on the topic and focus chart." Review: the topic and focus chart (grade 2, unit 2, day 46). Today we find the gist of Pollen on the Move first. Then we take two notes in a few words from one part of it.

#### 10 min. Read for gist

Read all of 01-a aloud once, about 330 words, without stopping (3 minutes). Review: Pollen on the Move (grade 2, unit 3, day 78). Partners say "It is mostly about ___" to each other, and point to the topic box on the chart (3 minutes). Hear the six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). The class agrees on a gist: how pollen moves from flower to flower with help from pollinators (1 minute). Children point to a heading that fits it.

#### 12 min. Ask and find

Point to the question on the chart: "Why do pollinators matter?" Review: the research-cycle chart (grade 1, unit 2, day 50). Pairs find the heading Why does it matter? and put a finger on it (1 minute). Read the paragraph under it aloud twice; children follow with a finger and read only the heading and the bold words (3 minutes). Each pair writes two slips from the paragraph in a few of their own words, each with the heading as its tag (5 minutes). They say each slip aloud (1 minute). Hear six pairs, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help (after "Does it answer why they matter?") or not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Trade and check

Each pair trades slips with another pair (1 minute). The reader says "Does it answer the question? Which source?" and points to the line the tag names (4 minutes). The owner fixes the slip or says why it stands (3 minutes). Hear the six, about 30 seconds each (2 minutes). Tag error E for a true fact that does not answer, AE for the wrong tag and D for a copied sentence.

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Which note answers the question best, and why?" Children say "I think ___ because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class saying what made a note good (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Notes sheet

Review: the notes sheet (grade 2, unit 1, day 24). Each child sets two slips on the sheet, and for each writes the fact, the tag, what the fact means, and a picture idea in a few words (5 minutes). A child may draw the picture idea. Scribe for any child who asks. Walk and look at slips; mark nothing aloud (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only. Write yesterday's commonest miss beside the right word and read it aloud. For attic read as house (error S), cover the picture and map each letter. For magic with a long a or comic with a long o (error T), say the short vowel before ic, then blend. For pak or duk (error Y), write pack and duck with the ck whole. For unlock as lock or replay as play (error AL), cover un- or re- and say what is left. For would as wood (error AM), star the odd part, read it, and write it twice from memory.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Pairs reread The Attic Trunk for accuracy: one reads a page, the other the next, then swap (4 minutes). Say: "A smooth second reading is the aim. Take your time." Groups sort the eleven word cards by ending: -ck (pack, kick, duck, deck), -k (desk, mask, tusk) and -ic (attic, magic, comic, picnic), then the four prefix cards by un- or re- (unlock, unpack, replay, rewind) (6 minutes). Children spell deck and magic on the student page and use each in a spoken sentence to a partner (5 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 15) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: the six join, ten to a station; groups rotate. Tiles: build deck, tusk, rewind and picnic. Sort: the eleven cards again, then spell two. Reread: The Attic Trunk and The Stable Gate. Mark the heard column.

#### 4 min. Exit

Hand out nothing. Say: "This text is made up for practice." Read the ants paragraph twice and ask [[s03-X90]]: every child tells a partner; hear the six, about 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "What is the whole paragraph about?") or not yet seen. Tag error AC for one detail named for the whole.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which prefix means again?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards on torn paper; read 01-a aloud from the teacher copy only. No pair sets: one 01-a page on the board, a pair at a time. A child who cannot write says the notes and you write them, recorded as help. A tag may be a drawn symbol. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the words on torn paper. No printed page: read the paragraph aloud from the teacher page.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Gist.** Tell your child 01-a in a few sentences, then ask what it is mostly about.
  **Say.** "What is it mostly about?" Expect: how pollen moves from flower to flower.
- **10 min. Notes.** Read the paragraph Why does it matter? twice. Your child writes two notes in a few words, each with the heading as its tag.
- **5 min. Check.** Your child says how each note answers why pollinators matter.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads The Attic Trunk aloud, then spells deck and magic.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X90]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the trade and check and the second reader station; keep the gist, the notes sheet and the sort. Longer: a child writes a third slip and says if it answers the question.

### Words and supports

Gist, topic, focus, heading, note, tag, matter, pollinator. Frames: "It is mostly about ___." "I think ___ because ___." "Does it answer the question? Which source?" "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the topic box or the heading instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The notes sheets, the slips and the exit are today's evidence, with the oral card and the heard column. If many copied a sentence (error D), or put notes in a row with no link (error B), day 91 opens with a 5-minute repair: cover the card and say a fact in four words, or lay an arrow card (so, because) between two slips and say the link. Day 91 joins notes into a step-by-step explanation with the step chain and arrow cards.
