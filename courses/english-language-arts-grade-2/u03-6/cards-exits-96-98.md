# Exit cards, days 96 to 98

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 96 (s03-X96).** Show the reserved photograph of a bumblebee with yellow pollen among the hairs of its head, once, and read its caption aloud: "Yellow pollen grains lie among the hairs on this bee's head." Show the note: "The yellow grains are on a wing." Say: "Do the photograph and caption support the note?"
- **Day 96 (s03-X96b).** Same photograph and caption. Say: "What would you change in the note?"
- **Day 96 (s03-X96c).** Same photograph and caption. Say: "What does the caption add to the picture?"
- **Day 97 (s03-X97).** This card is invented for practice. A partner says: "An insect crawls over a flower." Say: "Ask your partner one question about that."
- **Day 97 (s03-X97b).** Show the note card: "Pollen sticks to its body." Your partner asks: "Where does the pollen stick?" Say: "Answer your partner from the note card."
- **Day 97 (s03-X97c).** Same card. Say: "Say which card told you."
- **Day 98 (s03-X98).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Beetles visit wide flowers. A beetle is an insect with hard wing covers. It walks across the flower and eats. Pollen sticks to its body. So the beetle helps the flower." Say: "Point to the definition."
- **Day 98 (s03-X98b).** Same card. Say: "Say what the definition is for."
- **Day 98 (s03-X98c).** Same card. Say: "Say what the last sentence does."
