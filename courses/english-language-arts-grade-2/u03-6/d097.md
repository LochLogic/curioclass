# Day 97: Talk in pairs and fives about how pollinators help plants

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write twelve question strips, two sets. Set A: What does a hummingbird drink? How does a bat reach nectar? Where does a bee carry pollen? When do butterflies visit? What color is a pine cone? How fast can a rabbit run? Set B: What does a hummingbird hover by? Do bats feed by day? What does a bee gather with her legs? Where do butterflies land? How tall is a bus? What does a cow eat? Mark today's six.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 96's exit (item s03-X96). If many kept a note the photograph and caption did not support (error C), children read the caption aloud and underline the words that tell where the grains lie. Each child then writes the slip again with those words in it, in a few words.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today you talk with a partner and then in a group of five. We use the talk card: turn, pair, link, clarify. Nothing today is scored; it is practice for the talk you will do in a few days." Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1) and the talk card with a tally child (grade 2, unit 1, day 25).

#### 8 min. Model the talk

You and one child model with the child's slip, "she gathers pollen with her hairy legs" (tag: 01-b). Model waiting: you stop until the child has finished and say nothing (1 minute). Model linking: "You said hairy legs; I add: she mixes it with nectar." (1 minute). Ask the class to name the four moves on the talk card: turn, pair, link, clarify (2 minutes). Children practice waiting and linking in pairs with one slip each, one minute each way (4 minutes). Do not model a question or an answer; that is for the practice and the exit.

#### 16 min. Partner rounds

Two rounds of 8 minutes. Each pair has a set of six question strips and its own slips. Partner A takes three strips, reads one aloud and puts a counter on a slip that answers it, or says "my slips do not answer that" and puts the strip aside. Then partner B does the same with the other three (4 minutes each). In round 2 children swap partners and use set B. The question about a pine cone, a rabbit, a school bus and a cow are not answered by any slip. Walk and hear six pairs a round, about 1 minute each (6 minutes with the moves); the other 24 children work in pairs. Mark each of the six: shown, shown with help (after "Show me the slip") or not yet seen. Interrupting is error K.

#### 12 min. Science talk in fives

Six groups of five with a talk card and a tally child (review: grade 2, unit 1, day 25). Ask: "How do pollinators help plants grow and survive?" Say it twice (1 minute). Each child speaks about 1 minute from a slip while the others wait; the next child links with "You said ___; I add ___" (6 minutes with the moves). The tally child counts who spoke and who linked and reports to you (2 minutes). Hear one child in each group for 1 minute (3 minutes). Practice only; no score. A child may pass.

#### 9 min. Write

Each child writes one sentence from the child's own slip that tells how a pollinator helps a plant, a few words, with its tag, such as "a hummingbird carries pollen on its face, 01-e" (5 minutes). The child reads it to a partner, who puts a finger on the line that holds it (3 minutes). Collect the sentences (1 minute). A child who cannot write says the sentence and you scribe it, recorded as help. Look for: the fact is the child's own, the tag is right, the sentence says how the pollinator helps.

#### 7 min. Exit

Tell the class that the cards are made up for practice. You are the partner. Say aloud: [[s03-X97]]. Children each tell a partner their question (1 minute); hear the six, two on each ask (1 minute). Show the note card and say the partner's question: [[s03-X97b]]. Children tell a partner, then the six answer aloud (2 minutes). Ask: [[s03-X97c]]. Children tell a partner and the six answer (1 minute). Note hands for the rest. Mark each of the six: shown, shown with help (after "What would you like to know about the insect?" or "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error M for no question, N for an answer that ignores the question and L for an unrelated reply.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

This is the cycle 16 check. It teaches nothing new; give no hints. Children use the written check sheet and the student page. Spelling, 5 minutes: say each word, the sentence and the word again, twice through, then children write: [[s03-A35]] [[s03-A36]]. Reading, 5 minutes: read the card on the student page to the class once, then children read it again and write the answer in a few words: [[s03-A37]]. Reference, 5 minutes: children read the entry on the student page, check the draft against it and write the word again: [[s03-A38]]. Mark the written answers: shown, shown with help (a child who says the word aloud and you write it) or not yet seen. Tag error Y for a missing ending, AN for swapped letters and J for a draft copied without the entry. Collect the sheets.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread A Package at the Bridge, one page each, then swap, for expression: read it as the children in the story would say it. Walk and listen; no marks.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 16, the last group) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each including the swap (12 of your minutes). The other 24 are at stations, eight each. Round 2 is 12 minutes: the six join, ten to a station. Tiles: build lodge, smudge, range and strange. Sort: the ten word slips; each child reads a slip to a partner and the partner says its ending before turning it over. Reread: A Package at the Bridge. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

The six first heard on day 93 read a fresh card to you, two minutes each including the swap (12 minutes, 3 minutes for the moves): lodge, strange, smudge, range, edge, bridge, and the phrase "a strange lodge". Read the card to the child first. Mark: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The other 24 sit in pairs: one reads the ten word slips to the other, then you dictate dodge and large and each child writes them (about 6 minutes), then pairs check each other's with the word wall (about 4 minutes). Collect nothing; no marks.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the card and entry by hand. No groups of five: one group of five at a time talks while the others write. A child who cannot write says the sentence or spelling aloud and you write it, recorded as help. A tag may be a drawn symbol. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Slip.** Your child reads one slip from school aloud.
- **10 min. Ask.** Ask your child one question about the slip and listen to the answer all the way through.
  **Say.** "Where did you find that?" Expect: the article it came from.
- **10 min. Words.** Say lodge and strange. Your child writes each and uses it in a sentence.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X97]] [[s03-X97b]] [[s03-X97c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second partner round and the stations' second round; keep the talk in fives, the exit and the written check. Longer: a group of five adds a question of its own and the group finds a slip that answers it.

### Words and supports

Question, answer, partner, link, clarify, tally, note card, slip, tag. Frames: "My slips do not answer that." "You said ___; I add ___." "Which part do you mean?" "The note card says ___." A child may point to the slip or the card instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can ask a question about what a partner said and answer from a card. If many asked none (error M), day 98 opens with a 5-minute repair: ask one question the card could answer, then answer from the card. The written check also routes to day 98: the commonest cycle 16 miss in the foundations opening, with the taught ending beside the new word. Day 98 turns to the model paragraph Bats Help Flowers at Night and begins cycle 17.
