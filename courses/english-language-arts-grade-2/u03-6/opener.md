# Section 3.6 opener: photographs, questions and a model paragraph (days 96 to 98)

## What the section does

Children check a note against a photograph and its caption, ask one question of a partner and answer from a note card, and meet a short model paragraph with four jobs: a topic sentence, facts, a definition and an ending. They fill a plan page for their own paragraph. Day 98 closes with the assignment of the paragraph that the unit scores on days 99 to 101.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 96 uses photograph pages held by pairs. Day 97 uses the talk card and the tally child, and no recording is made. Day 98 lays the model's sentences in order and names each job. In the foundations block, cycle 16 closes with a written check on day 97 and cycle 17 begins on day 98 (-tch, -ch, -er, -est).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A picture can support a fact or not.** Ask what the picture shows and what only the caption says.
- **A question can be answered from a card.** The answer comes from the card, not from a guess.
- **A definition has a job.** It tells the reader a word so the next fact makes sense.
- **An ending tells why the facts matter.**
- **An optional outdoor look** only if the school plan allows it; no child touches an insect.

## The errors, in plain words

- A note is kept that the photograph does not support (C).
- No question is asked, or the answer ignores the speaker (M, N).
- The definition is read as one more fact (AF).
- No topic or no ending (F, G).
