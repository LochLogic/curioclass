# Model paragraph strips (day 98)

Six strips, one sentence on each, cut from the model paragraph *Bats Help Flowers at Night* and shuffled. Pairs lay them in order and name each sentence's job: **topic sentence**, **fact**, **fact**, **fact**, **fact**, **ending**. One of the facts is a definition (nectar, a sweet juice inside a flower).

- Bats help flowers at night.
- Some bats drink nectar, a sweet juice inside a flower.
- Their long noses and tongues reach deep inside.
- Pollen sticks to the bat while it feeds.
- Some pollen brushes onto the next flower.
- So bats help flowers while they eat.
