# Cycle 5 written check (day 25, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word taught on days 21 to 24.

1. "I put jam on my ____." (toast)
2. "A frog can ____ at night." (croak)
3. "Please ____ me the ball." (throw)
4. "I have a ____ of fruit." (bowl)

Tag wrong spellings: "tost", "croke", "thro", "bol" (Q, spelled by sound, pattern not carried over). Record other spellings separately.
