# Exit cards, days 21 to 25

Days 22 and 23 have no card: their exits reread article parts (items.csv and each day page name them).

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. You read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 21 (s01-X21).** Stanza (not from the poem): "The rain taps soft upon the glass. / We watch the little drops slide past. / We curl up with a book and stay, / And wait for clouds to drift away." Ask: "Read the stanza aloud so that a listener understands."
- **Day 24 (s01-X24).** "Pupils carry their reading books in a satchel. A satchel is a bag with a long strap." Ask: "Tell me what a satchel is, using the card."
- **Day 25 (s01-X25).** You say aloud: Lee says, "They carried those to the back before the bell." Ask: "Lee's words are not clear. Say what you would ask Lee."
