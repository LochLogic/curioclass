# Day 24: Gathering notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a notes sheet for each child. Put the five-row paragraph card from section 1.4 away: it is not used again. Have the printed articles ready in three piles. Mark today's six (group 4 of five). Have day 23's exit marks beside you: error A is the miss to repair.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 23's exit (error A, a difference that the card contradicts), check each detail against its card. Use the two captions of 02-a and 02-b, read aloud. Say a made-up claim: "Both photographs show a cushion." Children check each caption and find that only the 02-b photograph has a cushion. Say: "This is made up for practice." Otherwise retrieve the both and but sheet: partners say one Both ___ . But ___ . sentence.

#### 4 min. Words

Two words. To complete is to finish all the parts: "I complete my notes sheet." Review: to revise is to change your work to make it better (day 19). Partners say each word and its sentence. Tell children the five-row paragraph card is put away today and the notes sheet takes its place.

#### 11 min. Model the notes

Read aloud 02-c, the third article, while children follow the printed copy (about two minutes). Show the notes sheet and fill one example on the board, say each row aloud. Fact 1: "Many public schools serve every pupil a meal." Source tag: 02-c. Word and meaning: shift; the meaning copied from the box of words to know and checked against the sentence "A group that goes at one part of the day is called a shift." Picture idea: "the diagram's three rows, to show who is at school at what time." Think aloud: the fact answers the book question, the tag says where it came from, and the meaning is the article's own. Children read each row with you. Review: you used a note slip on day 16; the notes sheet works the same way with a place for a picture.

#### 10 min. Reread a chosen article

Each child chooses 02-a, 02-b or 02-c. Children who chose the same article form a pair or a trio. They reread with the printed copy, its captions, photographs or diagram, and its words to know. You read aloud to any pair that asks.

#### 20 min. Gather notes

Each child fills a notes sheet: two facts with source tags, one word with its meaning, and one picture idea. A fact must answer the book question, "What do schools help children do?" Cross out any that does not. For the meaning, find the article's own sentence. Children who cannot write tell you and you write; they may draw the picture idea. Meanwhile hear today's six, about two minutes each: ask "Show me where it says it." Collect the notes sheets at the end. After class, judge each against three look-fors: (1) each fact answers the book question; (2) each carries its source tag; (3) the meaning matches the article. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet. Nobody gets a score.

#### 5 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which fact helps explain what schools help children do?" Turn: partners tell each other (1 minute). Hear the six, about 30 seconds each; the next child links ("You said ___; I would add ___") or asks "Which part do you mean?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s01-X24 aloud twice and ask: "Tell me what a satchel is, using the card." The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a bag with a long strap. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the card say?" Not yet seen means a meaning the card contradicts, such as "A hard box with a lid" (error E). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Repair the commonest miss from day 23's sort and sentences. Say the word, map the whole team, check the wall for the spelling, and write it again. Use the words the class missed yesterday.

#### 15 min. Fresh passage

Give each child the passage Joan and the Toad, which no child has seen. Five minutes: read it silently, then to a partner, a sentence each. Four minutes: confirm the meaning after decoding. Ask: "Who rode in the snow?" "What sat in the road?" "What did the toad do?" Expect: Joan; a toad; it jumped off. If a word was hard, a child says the team's sound and rereads the sentence. Six minutes: say "Joan will show me the toad." twice, children write it, then match each word to the map.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each, three rounds. Tiles: spell glow, soak and slow. Write: write two oa or ow words from the wall, each in a short sentence, and read them to a partner. Reread: partners read The Slow Boat, a page each, for a smooth second reading. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 5 oral card (group 4 of five). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups swap stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one fact from their notes sheet and its source tag.

### Other rooms

No printer: rule a page in six rows for the notes sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed article: read it aloud to your child. No notes sheet: rule a page in rows. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pairs and the stations.

- **10 min. Read an article.** Read 02-a, 02-b or 02-c aloud to your child.
- **10 min. Notes.** Your child tells you one fact, where it comes from and a hard word. Write them on the sheet.
  **Say.** "What does the article say that word means?" Expect: the meaning the article gives.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say show and toad; your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X24]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fact instead of two. Longer: a child adds a third fact with its source tag.

### Words and supports

Complete, revise. Frames: "The article says ___ ." "A ___ is ___ ." A child may draw the picture idea and tell you the fact. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 rehearses a discussion. Its opening repair is for error E on today's exit (a meaning the card contradicts, such as "A hard box with a lid"): reread the source sentence and say the meaning from it. Children keep their notes sheets, which come back on days 26 to 28.
