# Day 26: Check: a school discussion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the cards and sheets. Set six groups of five, each mixing talkers. Put Reading Card 1 at your corner.

### The day

#### 48 min. Small-group discussion

Six groups of five, 8 minutes each with you. The other groups write a fact card from their day 24 notes, then reread 02-a to 02-c quietly. Read the question card twice: "What do 02-a and 02-b show that schools help children do?" (s01-A19). Say nothing for two minutes; then you may ask once, "Which part do you mean?" On the discussion record mark each child shown, shown with help or not yet seen: H waits and speaks one at a time, pointing to a fact; I links to a partner's remark (s01-A20); J asks which part when a remark is unclear (s01-A21); K asks or answers about the speaker's idea (s01-A22). If no remark was unclear by minute five, say one yourself. A child may pass.

#### 12 min. Whole-class return

One reporter per group reads a fact and its tag (1 minute each). Then the class links or asks (6 minutes), using the wait signal. Mark the last column for every child who speaks. Collect the fact cards at the end.

#### 5 min. Opening retrieval (foundations)

Each child finds oa and ow on the map and says one word for each. No reteaching.

#### 24 min. Reading conferences

Six children, four minutes each, in your corner. Give Reading Card 1. One minute: "Why are you reading this, and why does the note go by the tap?" (s01-A23). Two minutes: "Read the first four sentences aloud, and then read them again." (s01-A24). One minute: "Check the word beds from its letters and the sentence, and then reread the sentence." (s01-A25). Give no word and no meaning. Unscored; no speed target. The others reread The Slow Boat and spell cycle 5 words with tiles.

#### 28 min. Flexible groups

Two 14-minute groups with you, picked from the oral record and day 25's check. Group one, a spelling made by sound (such as tost): map the team, blend, spell again. Group two, oa or ow words missed in reading: map, blend, reread. The others rotate through reread and tiles.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one fact from today and its source tag.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the question on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: write on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups, the other stations and the conference.

- **25 min. The assessment: talk and read.** Ask: "What do 02-a and 02-b show that schools help children do?" Wait. Your child answers and points to a fact. Link to their answer, then ask them to link to yours. Say something unclear and see whether they ask which part. Then give Reading Card 1: ask why they are reading it, let them read the first four sentences twice, and have them check the word beds. Tell them no word.
  **Say.** "You said ___ ; I would add ___ ." Expect: a link that names your idea first.
- **15 min. Fact card.** Your child writes one fact, its tag and a picture idea.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the fact card to a fact and its tag. Longer: a group tries a second question on 02-c.

### Words and supports

Frames: "You said ___ ; I would add ___ ." "Which part do you mean?" A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens section 1.6. Errors H to K and N to P go to day 32's groups; error Q (day 25) is repaired at day 27's foundations opening. Cards 2 to 5 follow, six children a day.
