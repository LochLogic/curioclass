# Day 22: Choosing facts for the school book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the note slips and the reader. Mark today's six from the record sheet (group 2 of five on the oral card). Have day 21's exit marks beside you: error O is the miss to repair. Mark day 21's rotation misses by letter, L or M.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 21's exit (error O, lines read so fast they run together), reread at the phrase boundaries. Read aloud the opening of 02-b: "In Finland, children start school in the year they turn seven. A lesson is usually 45 minutes, and then pupils have a 15-minute break." Say it once running together, then once resting at each full stop. Children say which was easier to follow, then read the two sentences with you, resting at the full stops. Otherwise retrieve the source tag: a child names the article a fact came from.

#### 5 min. Words

Four words, each with a sentence. Review: to gather is to collect facts (day 16). To select is to choose (review: you met select in kindergarten, day 106): "I select the facts that answer the question." A caption is the line under a picture that tells what it shows: "The caption names the pot and the bowls." To be careful is to look closely so nothing is missed: "I am careful with the facts." Partners say each word and its sentence.

#### 6 min. The book

Say that the class will make a book. Write and read aloud its question: "What do schools help children do?" Children say it to a partner in their own words. Explain that a book has a question and each fact in it must help answer it. Review: a fact comes with its source tag (day 16); on a slip, the tag is the article, such as 02-a. Hand out the frame card and say the book will use its rows later.

#### 12 min. Model selecting

Read aloud 02-a, the first article, while children follow the printed copy (about two minutes). Write three facts on the board, each with its tag 02-a: (1) "Pupils serve the lunch." (2) "Pupils clean their classroom as a group." (3) "Japan is made of islands." Ask: "Which facts help answer the book question?" Think aloud for each: fact 1 shows a way pupils help each other and the school, fact 2 shows the same, so keep both; fact 3 is true but does not help answer, so cross it out. Say that selecting means keeping what helps the question. Children point to the line in the article for each fact kept. Then fill one note slip together: the fact, its tag. Mark each fact kept with a tick.

#### 8 min. Choose a clarifying picture

Show the 02-c diagram, "Three kinds of school day in Brazil." Review: on day 14 a photograph shows something the words do not say. Ask: "What does the diagram show that the words do not?" Expect: one building has a morning group and an afternoon group, laid out side by side, so you can see the hours do not overlap. Write the frame: "The diagram shows ___ so ___ ." Children say it to a partner. A picture belongs in the book when it shows what the words do not.

#### 14 min. Pairs select

Each pair takes 02-a or 02-b. They choose two facts that answer the book question and write each on a note slip with its source tag (first two lines of the slip). Then they choose one picture from that article, a photograph or its caption, and say a reason with the frame "The picture shows ___ so ___ ." About 8 minutes of work. Then hear today's six, about 40 seconds each: they read one fact and its tag. Others cross out any unrelated fact they chose.

#### 5 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which fact belongs in a book about what schools help children do?" Turn: partners tell each other (1 minute). Hear the six, about 30 seconds each; the next child links ("You said ___; I would add ___") or asks "Which part do you mean?" Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s01-X22 aloud twice, and ask: "Which mark means the window seat?" The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means the moon. Shown with help means the moon after one prompt: "What does the card say?" Not yet seen means the star (error A, a mark the card contradicts), or no answer. Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Many children may read the vowel team wrongly. If many read coat with a short o (error L), write coat, map oa as one long o, then blend c-oa-t. If many read snow with the vowel of cow (error M), write snow and read the ow words slow, grow and show: here ow says long o. Children read each word aloud.

#### 15 min. First reading

New today: The Slow Boat, the cycle 5 reader, read after the pattern teaching. Say the title and its high-frequency words, and map each on the sound-spelling-map: have, a, said, the, to. Say each plainly; do not guess from the picture or the first letter. Read page 1 aloud, children point under the words. Then the class reads page 1 together. Pairs read pages 2 to 7, a page each, in turn. Last, spell: go, slow and road, then dictate "Go slow on the road." Children write it, then match each word to the map.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each, three rounds. Tiles: spell slow, grow and foam. Hunt: with a printed copy of the reader, find the oa and ow words and underline each team (boat, slow, go, blows, floats, float). Reread: partners read The Slow Boat again, a page each. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 5 oral card (group 2 of five). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups swap stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one fact they kept today and its source tag.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the three facts of the model on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed articles: read the article to your child. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pairs, the stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Choose a fact.** Read the 02-a article aloud. Say the book question: "What do schools help children do?" Your child tells you one fact that answers it and says where it comes from.
  **Say.** "Does that fact help answer the question?" Expect: yes, because it shows a way pupils help.
- **10 min. Read The Slow Boat.** Your child reads it to you, a page each.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say slow and road; your child writes them.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X22]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the picture part to one example. Longer: a pair adds a third fact and crosses out any that does not answer the book question.

### Words and supports

Gather, select, caption, careful. Frames: "The fact helps because ___ ." "The picture shows ___ so ___ ." A child who cannot write tells you and you write. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 applies the picture to two articles. Its opening repair is for error A on today's exit (a mark the card contradicts, such as "the star"): put the picture beside the line and check each detail. Today's foundations repair carries error L or error M into day 23's reread and sort.
