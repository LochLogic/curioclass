# Sort cards (day 23)

Twelve cards, one word on each. Pairs sort them under two headings: **oa** and **ow**. Then each child spells two sentences you say.

road, coat, foam, soak, toad, goal, snow, slow, grow, show, flow, glow

Key: oa: road, coat, foam, soak, toad, goal. ow: snow, slow, grow, show, flow, glow.
