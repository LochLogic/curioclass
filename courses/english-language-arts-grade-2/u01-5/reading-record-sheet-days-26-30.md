# Reading record sheet (days 26 to 30, and day 32)

A sheet with the children's names down the left and one row for each, in the order they are heard (six a day, days 26 to 30, so all 30 once). Four minutes a child.

| Name | Day heard | Card | Purpose and understanding (1 minute) | First four sentences, twice (2 minutes) | Confirm or correct a word (1 minute) | Miscues noted apart |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Cards: Reading Card 1 on day 26, card 2 on day 27, card 3 on day 28, card 4 on day 29, card 5 on day 30. Each is read cold by the child in their own turn, so no child hears a card before the turn. You supply no word and no meaning during the turn. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen; record miscues apart from phrasing; set no speed target.
