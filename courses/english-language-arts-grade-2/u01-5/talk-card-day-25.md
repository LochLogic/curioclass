# Talk card with a tally (days 25 and 26)

A card for each group of five, with four lines, and a box for the tally child.

1. **Wait.** Raise your hand or use the signal; do not talk over a speaker.
2. **Link.** "You said ___; I would add ___."
3. **Ask.** "Which part do you mean?"
4. **Answer.** Use the speaker's idea in your answer.

**Tally** (one mark each time a child links or asks): ____________________

The tally child counts and does not speak for the group.
