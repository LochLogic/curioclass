# Day 23: Alike and different

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the both and but sheets and the sort cards, cut apart. Set the 02-a and 02-b photographs side by side. Make the exit photograph: a globe on a stand with four wheels on a classroom floor, with no children in it. Mark today's six (group 3 of five). Day 22's exit marks tell you who made error A. Key: key-sort-cards-day-23.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 22's exit (error A, a mark the card contradicts), put the picture beside the line. Show the 02-b photograph and read its caption: "A quiet corner of a library, ready for readers." Say a made-up claim, "The photograph shows a playground," and ask children to check each part against the photograph. They point to what is there: a low shelf, a cushion, a lamp. Then say a claim that fits. Say: "This is made up for practice." Otherwise retrieve the frame card: partners name its rows.

#### 4 min. Words

Three words. Review: in kindergarten you compared two things and said how they are the same and how they are different. Similar means almost the same. Different means not the same. To connect is to join two ideas, as a link joins your idea to a partner's. Say the frame aloud with the class: "Both ___ . But ___ ." Partners say one Both sentence about two objects in the room.

#### 8 min. Reread with photographs

Reread aloud the lunchtime paragraph of 02-a and the sentence of 02-b that tells what a library is (about one minute). Show the two photographs side by side with their captions. Ask for each: "What does the photograph show that the words do not?" Expect: for 02-a, bowls, a pot and a ladle on a cart, so you see how a lunch for a whole class is carried; for 02-b, a cushion and a lamp, so you see a place made for comfortable reading. If many only name a thing ("It has bowls", error C), ask the question again and say the frame: "The photograph shows ___ so ___ ." Children say it to a partner.

#### 14 min. Model Both/But

Show the both and but sheet. Write on the board one detail from the words, with its tags: "Both articles say pupils have a meal at school" (02-a, 02-b). Say it with the frame: "Both ___ ." Write one detail from a photograph, with its tags: "But the 02-a photograph shows bowls and a ladle for serving, and the 02-b photograph shows a cushion and a lamp for reading" (the two photographs). Say it: "But ___ ." Think aloud: the words give what is alike, the photographs give what is different, and each detail has a tag so a reader can find it. Children say the Both and But sentences with you, then read the tag beside each. Say that no school is better; a book that compares says how they are alike and how they are not.

#### 14 min. Pairs compare

Each pair finds one similar and one different detail, one from each article, and writes each in a column of the both and but sheet with its source tag. They may use a word detail or a photograph detail, not the pair on the board. Walk and ask: "Which article does that come from?" About ten minutes of work. Then hear today's six, about 40 seconds each: they say the Both and But sentences from their sheet.

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which details make the two schools similar and different?" Turn: partners tell each other (1 minute). Hear the six, about 60 seconds each. After each speaker, the next child links ("You said ___; I would add ___") or asks "Which part do you mean?" Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the two cards of item s01-X23 aloud twice, show the photograph, and ask: "Tell me one way the two classes are different, and what the photograph shows about the second globe." The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a difference the cards give and the stand with four wheels from the photograph. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the photograph show?" Not yet seen means "Both keep the globe on a shelf" (error A), or a place the photograph does not show, such as a table. Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Repair the commonest miss from day 22, error L (oa read with a short o) or error M (ow read like the vowel in cow), using the reader. Children find the word in The Slow Boat, map the whole team, blend, and read the sentence again. Use boat, slow and floats.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Five minutes: the class rereads The Slow Boat together, a page at a time. Five minutes: pairs sort the twelve cycle 5 sort cards under oa and ow, reading each word. Say each word aloud; the team is a spelling of long o. Five minutes: say "The toad has a coat." and "The foam can grow and grow." Children write each, mapping every word on the wall. Look together: some oa words and some ow words have the same sound, and the wall shows which spelling a word uses.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each, three rounds. Tiles: spell show, goal and glow. Reread: partners read The Slow Boat, a page each. Write: write two oa or ow words from the wall, each in a short sentence. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 5 oral card (group 3 of five). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups swap stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner a Both ___ . But ___ . sentence from today.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a page in two for the both and but sheet; write the sort words on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No second photograph: use the caption of each article. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pairs, the sort and the stations.

- **10 min. Alike.** Read the lunchtime paragraph of 02-a and the library paragraph of 02-b. Your child says one way they are alike.
  **Say.** "Both ___ ." Expect: pupils have a meal at school, or pupils do something together.
- **10 min. Different.** Your child says one way they are different, using the words "But ___ ." Then they look at a photograph and tell what it shows.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say toad and snow; your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X23]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sort to six cards. Longer: a pair finds a second alike and a second different detail, each with its tag.

### Words and supports

Similar, different, connect. Frames: "Both ___ . But ___ ." "The article says ___ ." "The photograph shows ___ ." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws and you write. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 starts the notes sheet and puts the frame card away. Its opening repair is for error A on today's exit (a difference the card contradicts): check each detail against its card.
