# Cycle 5 oral card (days 21 to 25)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 21 only the first two lines are used, because no cycle 5 pattern was taught before that day. Children read the words and say each long vowel; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 4 patterns (igh, ie, y).** light, night, high, right, pie, tried, my, fly, why
- **Cycle 5 patterns (oa, ow).** road, coat, foam, soak, snow, slow, grow, show

Partner word card for day 25 (read to each other, then spell two words you say): road, coat, foam, soak, snow, slow, grow, show, toad, glow. Dictated words: oak, low.
