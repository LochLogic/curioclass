# Poem stanza cards (day 21, and day 25)

Six cards for six groups of five, in large print. Five cards hold a stanza of *Our School Day* each, in the poem's order; the sixth holds the refrain. Each child reads one line to a partner first.

**Card 1.** Bell, bell, bell, the morning bell, / Feet on the floor and a tale to tell. / Hello to the teacher, hello to the door, / Hello to the friends who are here once more.

**Card 2.** We read, read, read, and the pages turn, / We find a new word and we learn, learn, learn. / We ask a question, we wait, we hear, / We say, "Tell me more," and the answers are clear.

**Card 3.** Spoons go clink, and the napkins fold, / We pass the fruit and the stories told. / We share a laugh and a joke or two, / And the hum of lunchtime hums right through.

**Card 4.** Out the door and into the sun, / Skip, skip, skip, and then we run. / Round and round and up and down, / Laughing and laughing, a happy sound.

**Card 5.** Pack up, pack up, the last bells chime, / Wave goodbye, it's going-home time. / Tomorrow we'll come back, the same and new. / We saved a place, a place for you.

**Refrain card.** That's our school day, that's our school day, / Together, together, all the way.
