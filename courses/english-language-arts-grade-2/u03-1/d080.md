# Day 80: Read the steps alone, then with help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the step chain and arrow cards and the pair step-chain set from day 79 at hand. Print the steps page of Pollen on the Move (steps 1 to 5, about 95 words, the diagram beside it) and the beetle card for every child. Put two columns on the unit 3 record sheet for today, "alone" and "with help". Mark today's six and set the cycle 13 oral card by your table. Cut the eight word cards for each pair.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These cards are made up." Use the commonest miss from day 79's exit. If many listed the steps with no order words (error B), say the four steps together with First, Then, Next and Finally, pointing along the arrow cards. If many named seeds or kernels (error A), show a pollen-dust card and a seed card and pairs match each to its caption. If many pointed to the wrong sentence (error C), reread the printed card and put a finger on the line. Otherwise ask pairs to say the step order from memory.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today you read the steps page alone first. Then I help, and you say it again. Both are marked, apart. The steps page is true; the exit card is made up." A child may pass at any time.

#### 10 min. Read alone

Each child reads the steps page of 01-a alone, quietly, with a finger and the diagram beside it. They put a star by a word that stopped them and a pencil bracket by the line that says what the bee reaches in for. Give no help at all in these 10 minutes: walk, watch, and do not read a word for anyone. Pencil down at the end.

#### 10 min. Explain to a partner

Partners take turns, without the page, saying how step 3 follows from step 2 and how step 5 follows from step 4 (4 minutes). Then hear the six, about 40 seconds each, and record alone, with help or not yet seen in the "alone" column (4 minutes). The other 24 keep talking in pairs, then swap (2 minutes). A child outside the six is "not yet heard".

#### 12 min. Supported reread

Read each starred word aloud with its sentence. Check each one against the sentence round it, as you did in unit 2 with "I know because the text says ___" (4 minutes). The class rereads the steps in two voices, half to each step, stopping at each arrow card and reading the step before it (4 minutes). Pairs then explain the same two links again with the page open; hear the same six, 40 seconds each, and record this second explanation in the "with help" column (4 minutes).

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask: "Which words tell you the order of the steps?" Children say "The word ___ tells me ___" to a partner (2 minutes). Hear the six (4 minutes). The class names the five order words together: First, Then, Next, After that, Finally (2 minutes).

#### 7 min. Write

Model one: "Because the bee lands, she can reach in." Each child chooses one pair of neighbouring steps and writes "Because ___, ___." with a few words each; you scribe for a child who asks.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give every child the printed card. Each reads it quietly to a partner; you listen to the six read it aloud, about 20 seconds each, and mark: shown (every word right), shown with help (right after one prompt, such as a finger under the word) or not yet seen (2 minutes). Then children turn the card over and you ask the second ask; everyone tells a partner and you listen to the six (1 minute). [[s03-X80]] [[s03-X80b]]

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six -le word cards: handle, giggle, waddle, table, little, puddle. The children read them in turn, a partner checking. Remap the first syllable of any missed word: open says its name, closed is short (error T).

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Pairs reread A Little Table for accuracy, one page each, a partner ticking the pages read right; a smooth second reading is the goal, with no timer (4 minutes). Then pairs sort the eight word cards by the first syllable: open and says its name (table, title, cable, ladle) or closed and short (little, puddle, handle, giggle) (5 minutes). Every child spells title and puddle on paper, checks each on the word wall, and tells a partner a sentence for each (6 minutes). If a child writes litl (error Y) or tabel (error AN), compare the word with the card and say the last syllable's letters in order before writing it again.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 2, day 59. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the day's six read the cycle 13 oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes), and the other 24 work at the stations, eight to a station. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: all stations fill to ten and the six take the two stations they have not had. Tiles: build title, cable, ladle and puddle. Sort: the day 79 cards by the letters of the last syllable. Reread: A Little Table, a smooth second reading, and the phrase cards. Group 3 of five at the oral card; today's new words are title, cable and ladle. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Say the ending of table: which two letters?" Children tell a partner, then say it together: l, e.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the steps onto the board and the beetle card by hand. No pair sets: use the wall chain. Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws and you scribe.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No printed steps: read from the book or screen.
**Skip.** The two-voice reread and the stations.

- **10 min. Read alone.** Your child reads the steps of Pollen on the Move alone with a finger, then you help with any stuck word.
  **Say.** "How does step 3 follow from step 2?" Expect: the bee flies to a second flower because the pollen stuck to her.
- **5 min. Order words.** Ask which words tell the order of the steps. Expect: First, Then, Next, After that, Finally.
- **5 min. Write.** Your child writes "Because ___, ___." for two steps.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child spells title and puddle and reads table, little and cable.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X80]] [[s03-X80b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read alone, the supported reread, the exit and the oral card; cut the write. Longer: a child reads the steps page to a younger child and says which step needs the one before it.

### Words and supports

First, Then, Next, After that, Finally; adheres and clings are the same idea. Frames: "The word ___ tells me ___." "Because ___, ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may read the card with a finger and ask for help; asking is not a miss, it is recorded as with help.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether a child reads a new card accurately and what the beetle carried. If many guessed a word from the picture (error S), day 81 opens by rereading the card with a finger under each word and blending any guessed word. If many named seeds (error A) or nectar (error C), a finger goes on the line that says it. In foundations, a child who writes litl or tabel (errors Y and AN) spells table from memory on day 81 and checks it on the word wall. Day 81 connects the steps in the words with the arrows in a flower diagram.
