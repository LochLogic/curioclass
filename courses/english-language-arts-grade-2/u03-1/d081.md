# Day 81: What the arrows show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the step chain and arrow cards and the pair step-chain set from day 79 at hand. Set the large flower diagram card on the board with its labels and four numbered arrow slips ready to move. Give each pair a blank flower diagram, the arrow slips and a step-chain set. Print the exit picture with three numbered arrows for every child. Mark today's six on the unit 3 record sheet and set the cycle 13 oral card by your table. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-13.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Use the commonest miss from day 80's exit. If many guessed a word (error S) or read pale short (error T), reread the bat paragraph from 01-d with a finger under each word and blend any word that was guessed. If many named seeds (error A) or the pale color (error C), reread it and have each child put a finger on the line that says when the flowers open. Otherwise ask pairs to say the step order from memory.

#### 7 min. Words for the diagram

Say each word, the class says it back, and you show it once, about 45 seconds a word. Review: anther and stigma (named on day 78), diagram (a drawing that explains with labels, from the diagram card of unit 1), label (a word written beside a part), arrow, explain (say how or why) and result (what happens because of an action). New: cause (the thing that makes another thing happen) and adheres (sticks on, the same idea as cling). Show each on the flower card or act it.

#### 8 min. Look at the diagram

Show the 01-a diagram: the title, the labels and the caption "Each arrow shows one step." Model arrow 1 (anther to bee): "The arrow shows pollen moving from the anther to the bee." Then say three statements and pairs show thumbs for each: "Arrow 2 goes from the bee away to a second flower and shows pollen moving there." "Arrow 3 shows the bee drinking nectar." "Arrow 4 goes from the stigma down and shows pollen helping seeds start." Pairs check the arrow and its label; the class fixes the wrong statement aloud (arrow 3 goes from the bee onto the sticky stigma and shows pollen moving there).

#### 12 min. Match steps to arrows

Pairs lay the four numbered arrow slips on their blank flower picture in the order of the steps and read the labels aloud to check (5 minutes). They lay the five step cards beside the arrows and find the step with no arrow, step 1, the bee lands (4 minutes). Ask pairs why: the words tell why the bee comes, and the diagram shows where the pollen goes (3 minutes). Take one pair's answer, about 40 seconds.

#### 8 min. Words say, diagram shows

Review: a picture shows what the words do not, from unit 1. Draw two columns, "The words say" and "The diagram shows". Model one of each. Words: the bee smells the flower. Diagram: pollen moves from the bee to a second flower. Pairs add one to each column, one child each, in turn (6 minutes).

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask: "What does the diagram clarify?" Children say "The diagram shows ___ that the words ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I would add ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close by saying one answer together (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Write

Each child draws a flower, writes one label (anther, stigma, nectar or pollen) on it, and writes one sentence with a cause and its result, "Because ___, ___." Model one: "Because the stigma is sticky, the pollen stays on it."

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give every child the printed flower picture with three numbered arrows. Read the card twice, then ask the first ask; every child points, and you note the six (2 minutes). Ask the second ask; everyone tells a partner and you stand by each of the six for about 10 seconds and mark: shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen (2 minutes). #### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Each child spells table from memory on paper, then checks it on the word wall. If a child writes tabel (error AN) or litl for little (error Y), say the letters of the ending in order, l, e, then write it again.

#### 15 min. Fresh passage and dictation

Read the title of A Candle in a Jar aloud. Pairs read the passage, one reading and one pointing, then swap (4 minutes). Each pair confirms the meaning after decoding by saying who has what (3 minutes). Never guess from the picture or the first letter; map any stuck word. Dictate waddle and cable, then the sentence "I guess one table is little." (6 minutes). Children check each against the word wall and fix any mistake (2 minutes). Tricky words: guess and one are starred on the word wall.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 2, day 59. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the day's six read the cycle 13 oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes), and the other 24 work at the stations, eight to a station. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: all stations fill to ten and the six take the two stations they have not had. Tiles: spell the dictation words waddle and cable and build table. Sort: the day 80 cards, open or closed first syllable. Reread: A Candle in a Jar and A Little Table. Group 4 of five at the oral card; no new word today. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which letter in one is the odd part?" Children tell a partner, then say it together: the o, which says /w/ and /u/, and the e is silent.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the flower and four arrows large on the board and copy the exit picture by hand. No pair sets: one chain on the wall, a group at a time. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No diagram card: use the diagram in the article. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pair matching and the stations.

- **10 min. Look.** Find the diagram in Pollen on the Move. Say a wrong thing about an arrow and ask your child to fix it.
  **Say.** "Arrow 3 shows the bee drinking nectar. Is that right?" Expect: no, it shows pollen going from the bee onto the sticky top.
- **10 min. Say and show.** Your child says one thing the words say and one thing only the diagram shows.
- **5 min. Write.** Your child draws a flower, writes one label and writes "Because ___, ___."
- **5 min. Words.** Your child spells waddle and cable and reads A Candle in a Jar.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X81]] [[s03-X81b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the look at the diagram, the match, the exit and the dictation; cut the second column share. Longer: a child draws a fifth arrow for what happens after step 5 and says what it would show.

### Words and supports

Anther, stigma, diagram, label, arrow, explain, cause, result, adheres. Frames: "The diagram shows ___ that the words ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Because ___, ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the arrow instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can match an arrow to the words and say what moves along it. If many pointed to the wrong arrow or said the bee flies home (error C), or said seeds fly (error A), day 82 opens by pointing to the arrow, saying what moves along it and checking it against the card. Day 83's opening repair carries the cycle 13 check of day 82. Day 82 chooses a strategy to read a pollination paragraph and explain its process.
