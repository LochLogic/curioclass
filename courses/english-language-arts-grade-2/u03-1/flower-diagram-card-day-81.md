# Flower diagram card with movable labels (day 81)

A large picture of one flower cut open so the inside shows, drawn in thick plain lines. The labels are on slips so they can be moved: petal, anther, pollen, stigma, nectar, stem. Four numbered arrows on slips too. The arrows come from the article *Pollen on the Move*: from the anther to the bee, from the bee away to a second flower, from the bee onto that flower's stigma, and from the stigma down toward the seed.

Pairs place each label on the part it names, then lay each arrow and say what moves along it. The question to ask at every arrow: "What moves here?" The answer is pollen.

Also print a blank copy (the flower with no labels) for each pair, with the four numbered arrow slips, for the match on day 81. Print the three-arrow flower picture for the exit for every child.
