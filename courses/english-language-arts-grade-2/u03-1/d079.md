# Day 79: Each step makes the next one possible

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the wall step chain: five strips, First, Then, Next, After that, Finally, with a label card under each (lands, reaches in, flies on, rubs off, seeds start) and the arrow cards in an envelope. Set a small chain set for each pair. Print paragraph 3 of 01-c (How pollen moves) for every child. Mark today's six on the unit 3 record sheet and set the cycle 13 oral card by your table. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-13.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These cards are made up." If many missed day 78's exit (error B, two statements with no joining word, or error C, the link run backwards), write the two butterfly facts from 01-c, "A butterfly probes a flower for nectar. Nectar is its energy food.", beside "A butterfly probes a flower for nectar because nectar is its energy food." Pairs point to the joined one and to the joining word. Then show two made-up lines, "Raj plants a seed in a pot, so a green shoot can push up" and "A green shoot pushes up, so Raj plants a seed in a pot", and pairs point to the one whose link runs the right way (5 minutes in all). Otherwise show one new pair of cards and pairs choose the right link of two.

#### 8 min. Model connected rereading

Reread steps 1 to 3 of 01-a aloud. Then reread step 1 before step 2 and say: "Step 2 can happen because step 1 happened." Say one link for steps 3 and 4 that is right ("Step 4 can happen because step 3 happened") and one that is wrong ("Step 4 can happen because step 1 happened"). Children show thumbs, and for the wrong one point to the step that must come just before step 4. The bee does not mean to carry pollen; she comes for food and some pollen goes along.

#### 12 min. Build the step chain

Say: "This is the step chain. First, then, next, after that, finally." Build arrows 1 and 2 on the wall chain yourself, laying an arrow card and pointing along it (5 minutes). The 15 pairs build arrows 3 and 4 with their own sets, laying each arrow card and pointing along it (7 minutes). Review: order words from unit 2, day 33 (First, Next, Then, At last); After that and Finally are new here.

#### 5 min. Read the cards

Pairs read the five labels and their order words aloud, one reading and one pointing, then swap (about 15 printed words).

#### 5 min. Take one away

Take the card "lands" off the wall chain. Ask: "Can the next step still happen?" Thumbs. The class reads the text line together, finger on the card: "If the bee never lands, the pollen never sticks." Then pairs turn "flies on" face down and point to the step after it that cannot happen now. Say: "Each step makes the next one possible."

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask: "How does one step make the next possible?" Each child points to two neighbouring steps on the small chain and says "Step ___ happens because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I would add ___", or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Say one link together to close (2 minutes). If a child says seeds, say: "That is a real thing; today we follow pollen" (error A).

#### 10 min. Write: two linked steps

Each child picks two neighbouring cards, copies the two labels in order on the page with an arrow drawn between them, and draws the two steps with the pollen shown; you scribe for a child who asks (6 minutes). Look for: the two steps are neighbours in 01-a and in its order; the arrow runs from the first to the second; the drawing shows pollen, not seeds. Then pairs get a chain with two neighbouring cards swapped, judge it and put it right (4 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This is from the true butterfly article." Read paragraph 3 of 01-c (How pollen moves) twice, then ask the first ask. Every child tells a partner; you stand by each of the six for about 10 seconds and mark: shown, shown with help (after "What does it say?") or not yet seen. Ask the second ask the same way. Then hand out the printed paragraph and ask the third ask; every child points, and you note the six. [[s03-X79v2]] [[s03-X79bv2]] [[s03-X79cv2]]

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

If many read table or little with the wrong vowel (error T), remap the first syllable: open says its name, closed is short. If many read one or guess from the letters wrongly (error AM), star the odd part again and read it. Otherwise reread yesterday's -le words from the cards (handle, giggle, waddle, table, little, puddle).

#### 15 min. Read: A Little Table

Map three new words first (3 minutes): we've (we and have, with the letters ha gone and an apostrophe, like the would-words you met in unit 2), I've (the same) and tonight (to and night). Read the title and page 1 aloud (1 minute). Pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4: one reads a page, the partner echoes it, then swap (7 minutes). Cover the picture while mapping any stuck word; never guess from the picture. After each page, the pair covers the picture and says what happened (4 minutes). The words use only -le, earlier patterns and the tricky words I've, we've, guess, one and tonight.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 2, day 59. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the day's six read the cycle 13 oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes), and the other 24 work at the stations, eight to a station. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: all stations fill to ten and the six take the two stations they have not had. Tiles: build table, little, puddle and giggle. Sort: the cards by the letters of the last syllable, dle (handle, waddle, puddle), gle (giggle), ble (table), tle (little). Reread: pairs reread A Little Table and yesterday's phrase cards. Group 2 of five at the oral card; today's new words are we've, I've and tonight. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What did the picture not tell you that the letters did?" Children tell a partner; take two answers (for example, the word on page 2 was we've).

### Other rooms

No printer: write the strips on torn paper and copy the paragraph by hand. No pair sets: use the wall chain with a group at a time. Pointing and drawing are answers.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the five order words on scraps. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The wall chain and the stations.

- **10 min. Read the steps.** Read steps 1 to 3 of Pollen on the Move aloud, then ask what if the bee never lands.
  **Say.** "What if the bee never lands?" Expect: the pollen never sticks.
- **10 min. Make a chain.** Write First, Then, Next, After that and Finally on scraps. Your child puts the steps in order with a drawing for each and lays an arrow between them.
- **5 min. Read it.** Your child reads A Little Table aloud, and you read each page back.
- **5 min. Words.** Say ta-ble and lit-tle in parts. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X79v2]] [[s03-X79bv2]] [[s03-X79cv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the chain build, the exit, the reader and the oral card; cut the second judging of a swapped chain. Longer: a child adds a sixth step, seeds can grow, and says which step it needs before it.

### Words and supports

Order words: First, Then, Next, After that, Finally. Labels: lands, reaches in, flies on, rubs off, seeds start. Frames: "Step ___ happens because ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point along the arrows instead of saying a step.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can put steps in order and say what lets the last step happen. If many said steps as a list with no order words (error B), day 80 opens by saying the steps again with First, Then, Next, Finally along the arrow cards. If many named seeds (error A), day 80 shows a pollen-dust card and a seed card and pairs match each to its caption. If many pointed to a wrong sentence (error C), a finger goes on the line that says it. In foundations, a wrong vowel in a -le word (error T) is remapped on day 80. Day 80 reads an illustrated pollination explanation independently, then with support.
