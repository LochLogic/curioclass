# Step chain and arrow cards (days 79 to 118)

Five long strips of paper, each with one order word printed large: **First**, **Then**, **Next**, **After that**, **Finally**. Between the strips, four arrow cards drawn on torn card. A wall copy stays up to day 118.

Below each strip is room for a short label card the class writes with the article *Pollen on the Move*: lands, reaches in, flies on, rubs off, seeds start. One strip can be turned face down so the class says what the missing step must have been, and why the next step could not happen without it.

Say "so" or "because" while pointing along each arrow.

Make a small set of the same strips, arrows and label cards for each pair (the pair step-chain set), used on days 79, 80 and 81; the wall chain stays up.
