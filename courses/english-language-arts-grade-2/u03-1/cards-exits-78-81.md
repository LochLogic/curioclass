# Exit cards, days 78 to 81

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each. Print the day 79 card large enough for each child to point to a line.

- **Day 78 (s03-X78).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Sam fills a bowl with water and sets it on the step. Later a sparrow lands on the rim and drinks." Say: "Say one sentence that joins the two things with so or because."
- **Day 78 (s03-X78b).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A small fly lands on a daisy and drinks its nectar. Pollen sticks to its legs. Then it flies to another daisy and some pollen rubs off." Say: "Say the word for an animal like the fly that moves pollen from flower to flower."
- **Day 79 (s03-X79).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Pollen on the Wind. A corn plant has a tassel at the top. The wind shakes the tassel and pollen floats out. Some pollen lands on the silk of another corn plant. Then that plant can start making kernels." Say: "Say the steps in order with First, Then, Next, Finally."
- **Day 79 (s03-X79b).** Same card, read again. Say: "Why can the last step happen? Say because."
- **Day 79 (s03-X79c).** Same card, printed for every child (the title and four lines, marked "invented for practice"). Say: "Point to the sentence that says where the pollen lands."
- **Day 80 (s03-X80).** This card is invented for practice. Give the child the printed card: "A beetle lands on a big white flower in a garden. Pollen sticks to its body. It walks to the same kind of flower on a new plant. Some pollen rubs off." Say: "Read the card aloud." Record alone, with help, or not yet seen.
- **Day 80 (s03-X80b).** Same card, covered. Say: "What did the beetle carry to the second flower?"
- **Day 81 (s03-X81).** This card is invented for practice. Show a flower picture with three numbered arrows. Read aloud twice: "A bumblebee reaches into a bell-shaped flower. Pollen sticks to her. Then she flies to a second bell-shaped flower. Pollen touches its sticky top." Say: "Point to the arrow that matches Then she flies to a second bell-shaped flower."
- **Day 81 (s03-X81b).** Same card and picture. Say: "Say what moves along that arrow."
