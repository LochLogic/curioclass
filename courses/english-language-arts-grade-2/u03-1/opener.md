# Section 3.1 opener: pollen on the move (days 78 to 81)

## What the section does

For four days the class reads *Pollen on the Move*, the first of five short true articles about how flowers get help from animals. Children learn what pollen, pollination, nectar and a pollinator are, tell how one step leads to the next with so or because, put the five steps in order with First, Then, Next, After that and Finally, and match each arrow on a flower picture to its step. The unit's question, "How do plants and pollinators help each other?", goes up on day 78 and stays on the wall until day 118. Say aloud that the article is true and that every practice card is made up.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a short opening repair, the day's words, a reading, a model, pair work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds a repair, new work on words that end in a consonant plus le (cycle 13), the reader *A Little Table*, a 37-minute rotation of three stations (tiles, sort, reread) and a close. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask. The child answers aloud; you hear the day's rotating six and the rest tell a partner. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Pollination moves pollen.** Pollen is a tiny powder. Pollination is moving it from one flower to another flower of the same kind. Animals, wind and water can do it. An animal that does it is a pollinator.
- **Seeds travelling is a different thing.** Seed dispersal moves seeds. Do not let the two run together. If a child says seeds fly, say "That is a real thing; today we follow pollen."
- **Each step makes the next one possible.** Say "so" or "because" and point along the arrow. A list of steps with no link is not yet an explanation.
- **A diagram says it with arrows.** Each arrow is one step. Read the words, then follow the arrow.

## The errors, in plain words

- Seeds are named where pollen belongs (A).
- The steps are said as a list with nothing linking them (B).
- A fact is said that the article does not give (C).
- A picture is used to guess a word instead of the letters (S).

Show the right answer beside the wrong one, say the link together, and let the child try again. Repairs are five minutes at the start of the next day.

## Marking

One scale for every look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record it on the record sheet. Nothing is scored on days 78 to 81 beyond the exits; they tell you what to repair.
