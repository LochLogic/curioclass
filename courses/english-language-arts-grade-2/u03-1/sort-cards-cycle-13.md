# Word and phrase cards, cycle 13 (days 78 to 81)

Word cards, one word on each, and phrase cards for the reread station. Words kept for the cycle check on day 82 are not on any card here.

**Day 78 sort (ends in a consonant plus le, or not).** Eight cards: picnic, handle, napkin, giggle, sunshine, table, rabbit, waddle.

**Day 78 phrase cards (reread station).** a little table; a wet puddle; the handle; a giggle.

**Day 79 sort (the letters of the last syllable).** Six cards: table, puddle, little, giggle, handle, waddle.

**Day 80 sort (the first syllable, open or closed).** Eight cards: table, title, cable, ladle, little, puddle, handle, giggle.

Each child spells two of the words from memory (title and puddle) and says a sentence for each.

**Day 81.** The day 80 cards are sorted again at the sort station. Dictation words: waddle and cable, and the sentence "I guess one table is little."
