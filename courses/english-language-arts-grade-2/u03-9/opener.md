# Section 3.9 opener: a bee-view story, a poster and a short presentation (days 104 to 108)

## What the section does

The class hears One Bee, Two Flowers, a made-up story told by a bumblebee, and checks which parts match the articles. Children then build a labelled poster from two articles, plan three spoken sentences, practise them in pairs, and improve the poster with a fact from each of two provided sources.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 104 sorts story lines against the articles and orders three picture cards. Day 105 has poster stations and the shared keyboard. Day 106 plans and rehearses. Day 107 rereads and takes the cycle 18 written check. Day 108 revises the poster with sources and begins cycle 19 (au and aw).

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A story can hold both facts and imagined thoughts.** The articles say which.
- **A label and a fact go together.** A label is a short word that names a part.
- **A sentence a listener can follow** is whole, in order and clear.
- **A source gets a small letter** wherever a child changes a fact.

## The errors, in plain words

- A fact in neither source or one the source contradicts (C).
- A true fact that does not answer how the visitor helps the flower (E).
- Sentences out of order, a fragment, or no detail (B, O, Z).
- A word read from its first letters, or a vowel misread (S, T).
- A poster line said again with no change (I).
