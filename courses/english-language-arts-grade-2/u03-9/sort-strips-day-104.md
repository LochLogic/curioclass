# Sort strips, real and imagined (day 104)

Six strips to cut apart, from the story One Bee, Two Flowers. Sort under two heads: **true of real bees (the articles)** and **imagined**. The articles are in hand.

- A bumblebee drinks nectar from the flower.
- The bee says, "Today is a good day for flowers."
- Some pollen rubbed off on the sticky stigma.
- The bee likes the girl on the bench.
- Seeds can grow after pollen reaches a flower.
- The nectar tastes like a warm, sunny day.
