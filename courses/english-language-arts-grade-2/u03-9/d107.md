# Day 107: Reread, practice clear sentences and take the cycle 18 check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one line of feedback on each poster (after class on 106) and set the posters out. Mark today's six, group 5 of five, children 25 to 30; choose six other children not yet heard reading this unit for the reading observation. Cut the stop strips. Keep the check cards out of sight until the check.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hand back

Return the posters from day 105, each with the feedback line written after day 106 (1 minute). Each child reads the line to a partner (2 minutes), then circles on the poster the one part it names (2 minutes). Error C: a fact the sources do not give; the child puts a finger on the source line first. The fixing is on day 108.

#### 20 min. Reread paragraph in pairs

Today is a fluency day: the class rereads, nothing is taught as new. Read the model paragraph Bats Help Flowers at Night three times (3 minutes): once slowly for the words, once stopping at each full stop, once like talking. Each child reads their research paragraph to a partner three times (8 minutes): for the words, for the pauses at the full stops, for sounding like talking. The partner follows with a finger, makes a dot on the stop strip at each pause heard, and says one thing the reader did well. Swap partners, and each reads once more for sounding like talking (8 minutes). Any child may read only part. Collect the strips (1 minute).

#### 18 min. Presentation sentences in fives

Six groups of five. Each child says two sentences from the plan card, 3 minutes a speaker (15 minutes): the group links ("I add...") and one child asks "Which part do you mean?" or another clear question. Changing speakers takes about 30 seconds, so you may end the last speaker early (3 minutes for changes). Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Independent reading

Children read the child-read texts 01-b, 01-d and 01-e on their own, a finger under the line. You hear the six chosen for the reading observation, 90 seconds each, a finger under the line, a few lines of one article (9 minutes). Write what you hear on the record sheet. Change over costs the last minute.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show the line and ask the one question (1 minute): [[s03-X107]]. All read the line to a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag a word read from its first letters error S, a vowel misread error T.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children change blocks and take the student page.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Nothing is routed here, so this is a warm-up, not a repair. Children read the cycle 18 words on the wall (2 minutes), then say what the ending means and say one word in a sentence (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Cycle 18 written check

Say the cards are invented. Each part is 5 minutes. Spelling: say each word, the sentence, the word again, and children write it ([[s03-A45]], [[s03-A46]]). Reading: read the card to the class once, hand out the printed card and ask the one question; children answer in a few words ([[s03-A47]]). Reference: hand out the draft and the entry; children correct and mark what they changed ([[s03-A48]]). Tag a last letter out of order error AN, a spelling copied from a neighbour error J, an answer that contradicts the card error C. Mark each on the scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Feedback goes back after class; correction is on day 108.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the cycle 18 oral card to you, two minutes each, and answer one more ask ([[s03-A49]]); 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: playable, breakable, flexible, readable. Sort: slips by -able or -ible. Reread: the reader for expression. Mark the heard column. Tag an ending treated as meaningless error AL.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which letters end movable?" Children tell a partner, then say them in order together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the check cards by hand. A child who cannot write answers the check aloud to a scribe; pointing to the entry is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No paragraph: use the poster. No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card.

- **10 min. Read it three times.** Your child reads the research paragraph aloud three times.
  **Say.** "Did it sound like talking?" Expect: a yes after the last reading.
- **10 min. Say two sentences.** Your child says two sentences from the plan card.
  **Say.** "Which part do you mean?" Expect: a clearer detail.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads breakable, movable and lovable and says what the ending means.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X107]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two readings of the paragraph, not three; keep the sentences in fives, the check and the exit. Longer: a child reads a second article aloud.

### Words and supports

Reread, pause, full stop, strip, entry. Frames: "I paused at ___." "The entry says ___." A child may point to the word.

### Tomorrow

Day 108 improves the poster with facts from two sources and starts cycle 19. Today's exit and the cycle 18 check tell you which misses to put beside the right word at the start of the foundations block.
