# Day 106: Plan a short presentation in complete sentences

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put each child's day 105 poster at their place. Mark today's six, group 4 of five, children 19 to 24; pair each of them with a child outside the six. Draw the three-box model on the board with a picture in each box. After class, write one line of feedback on each poster for day 107.

### The day

#### 10 min. Reread

Children reread the poster to themselves, then to a partner, a finger under each fact (4 minutes). Each picks three things on it to talk from: a label, a drawing and a fact, and circles them (4 minutes). Pairs say which three they picked (2 minutes).

#### 18 min. Write the plan

Today is an application day: the class plans, nothing is taught as new. The plan card has three boxes: what the visitor is, what it does to the flower, why that helps. Model with the board boxes, each holding a picture and no words: a hummingbird, a bill with yellow dots, a flower with seeds. Say the three sentences aloud as a talk: "A hummingbird visits the flower. Its bill gets dusted with pollen. The pollen reaches the next flower, so seeds can grow." Ask the class which box each sentence came from (4 minutes). Each child draws or writes a few key words in each box from their own poster (8 minutes). Each says the three sentences aloud to a partner before moving on, without reading the card out as words (6 minutes). A child who needs a frame uses: "The ___ is a ___." "It ___ the flower." "That helps because ___."

#### 18 min. Rehearse in pairs

Review: turn, pair, link, clarify (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Round 1 is 5 minutes: all pairs, each speaker says the three sentences (2 minutes each), the listener asks one detail with "Which part do you mean?" (SL.2.3). Round 2 is 13 minutes. The six sit near you, each one drawing a picture on the plan card while waiting; you hear each one at your table for 2 minutes and change over in 10 seconds, 13 minutes in all. The other 24 children work in 12 pairs: speak again to a new partner, 3 minutes each way, then use the last 7 minutes to add one detail to a box. A speaker may pass.

#### 7 min. Volunteers

Three volunteers say their three sentences to the class, about 1 minute and 30 seconds each with the change-over (5 minutes). The class links ("I add...") or asks (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show the plan words and ask the one question (1 minute): [[s03-X106]]. All tell a partner their sentence (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag a string of plan words with no verb error O, a fact the words do not give error C.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children change blocks and take the student page.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Nothing is routed here, so this is a warm-up, not a repair. Children read the cycle 18 words on the wall (2 minutes) and write one from memory, then check it letter by letter (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Fresh passage and dictation

Pairs read the passage A Map of the Trail, about 40 words, a sentence each; the title is read by you (5 minutes). Confirm the meaning after decoding: "What did Dad fix?" and "What does whole tell us about the map?" (3 minutes). The word whole is a mapped word. Review: -ible and -able mean able to be (grade 2, unit 3.8, day 103). Dictate two words and a sentence (7 minutes): flexible, playable. "The map is visible." The word, the sentence, the word again; children check each letter against the board after.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the cycle 18 oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: playable, breakable, flexible, readable. Sort: slips by -able or -ible. Reread: the passage for expression. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Say a word that ends in -able." Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the passage by hand. A child who cannot write dictates to a scribe; saying the three sentences is the plan.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: draw the three pictures. No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The passage.

- **10 min. Make three pictures.** Your child draws what a visitor is, what it does to the flower and why that helps.
  **Say.** "Tell me three sentences from your pictures." Expect: a sentence for each box.
- **10 min. Listen.** Hear them once. Ask one question.
  **Say.** "Which part do you mean?" Expect: a clearer detail.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads the day 105 sort cards and spells visible aloud.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X106]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one round of pairs; keep the plan, the hearing of the six and the exit. Longer: a child adds a fourth sentence that answers a listener's question.

### Words and supports

Plan, visitor, sentence, detail, box. Frames: "The ___ is a ___." "That helps because ___." A child may point to the picture and say the sentence.

### Tomorrow

Day 107 rereads the research paragraph, practices the presentation sentences in groups and takes the cycle 18 written check. Today's exit tells you who says a whole sentence from words. Each poster gets one line of feedback to hand back.
