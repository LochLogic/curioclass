# Sort cards, cycle 18 (day 105)

Ten cards to cut apart. Sort into two piles: -ible and -able.

- visible
- flexible
- audible
- reversible
- enjoyable
- movable
- breakable
- valuable
- lovable
- huggable

Then use one word in a sentence.
