# Day 108: Improve the poster with facts from two sources

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the posters, each with its feedback line, at the places. Skim the day 107 check sheets and write the commonest miss on the board beside the right form. Mark today's six, group 1 of five, children 1 to 6. Put the small letters b and c beside the two passages.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read the feedback

Today is an application day: the class revises, nothing is taught as new. Each child rereads the feedback line to a partner (2 minutes), then circles on the poster the fact or word the line asks them to change (3 minutes). With 01-b or 01-c in hand, each finds the source line they may change it from, a finger under it (5 minutes).

#### 25 min. Revise

Review: naming the article a fact came from (grade 2, unit 3, day 99). Handed back from day 105: each child changes at least one fact or word on the poster using a line from one of the two passages, and writes the small letter of that passage, b or c, beside the change (error AE: the letter must name the passage the line is really in). A second change comes from the other passage and gets its own letter (about 18 minutes). Then each adds an adjective from the passages, such as hairy, sweet or long, to one label, and an adverb that tells how, such as slowly or quickly, to one sentence on the poster (review of grade 2, unit 2, day 56) (about 7 minutes). A change is a new word or fact, not a recopy (error I). Errors: a fact in neither passage error C. A child who needs more time keeps the second change for day 112.

#### 15 min. Pairs read the change

Pairs sit side by side. One child reads the changed line aloud; the partner asks, "Which source said that?" and the writer puts a finger on the line in the passage and says the letter (4 minutes each way, 8 minutes). Swap to a new partner and read one change only (7 minutes). Any child may pass the question.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show the poster line and the two sources, read aloud twice (1 minute): [[s03-X108]]. All say the line to a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag the line said again unchanged error I, a fact neither source gives error C.

#### 5 min. Transitions

Children change blocks, take the student page and put the poster in the folder.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

The commonest miss of the day 107 check is on the board beside the right form. Children name the letters that differ (2 minutes), then write the right form from memory, cover it and check it letter by letter (3 minutes). Review: -ible and -able mean able to be (grade 2, unit 3.8, day 103).

#### 15 min. Cycle 19 pattern and reader

Review: reading a vowel team as one sound (grade 2, unit 2). Model (3 minutes): write saw, draw, yawn and say that aw says the sound you hear in saw; then pause, cause, fault and say that au says the same sound. Both teams say one sound, so you cannot hear which one; look, and check the entry when unsure. Map and blend (3 minutes): saw, draw, yawn, crawl, pause, cause; children say the sounds, then the word. Reader (5 minutes): read page 1 of Dawn in the Garden aloud; pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4, a sentence each, then say what Nan saw. The word yellow is mapped. Cover the pictures until after reading. Spell five (4 minutes): dawn, saw, draws, pause, crawl; the word, a short sentence, the word again.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the cycle 19 oral card to you, two minutes each, with 30 seconds of the vowel card and three irregular words in every turn; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: dawn, crawl, yawn, pause. Sort: eight slips by au or aw. Reread: the reader for accuracy. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Say au and aw words you heard." Children tell a partner, then say them together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the passages by hand. A child who cannot write dictates the change to a scribe; pointing to the source line is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No passage: use a fact your child knows. No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The words.

- **10 min. Change one thing.** Your child reads one line of their poster and changes a word or fact.
  **Say.** "Where did the new fact come from?" Expect: the article, with its letter.
- **10 min. Tell it again.** Your child says the new line aloud to you.
  **Say.** "What changed?" Expect: a word that was not there before.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads saw and pause and says which letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X108]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one change; keep the pairs reading the change and the exit. Longer: a child makes a second change from the other passage.

### Words and supports

Revise, source, adjective, adverb, letter. Frames: "I changed ___ to ___." "Source ___ said that." A child may say the new line first and then write it.

### Tomorrow

Day 109 rehearses the pollinator talk and asks for detail. Today's exit tells you who can change a line with a word from each source.
