# Picture cards, a fly visits two flowers (day 104)

Three drawn cards for each pair, in an envelope. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Cards are shuffled.

- **Card 1.** A fly rests on a white daisy.
- **Card 2.** Dust sits on the fly's body.
- **Card 3.** The fly lands on a yellow flower and some dust falls off.

Each child lays the cards in order and says first, then, last as the cards go.
