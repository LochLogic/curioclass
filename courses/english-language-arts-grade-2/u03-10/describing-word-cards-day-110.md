# Describing word cards (day 110)

Eight cards to cut apart, with a drawing of a fuzzy visitor on a flower. Sort the cards onto the drawing: which tell how the visitor looks, and which tell how it moves?

- fuzzy
- yellow
- round
- small
- sticky
- bright
- soft
- quick

A word may go in more than one place. Say a short phrase for each.
