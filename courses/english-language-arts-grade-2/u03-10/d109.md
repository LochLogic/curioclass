# Day 109: Rehearse the poster talk and ask for a detail

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put each child's revised poster with its feedback line at the groups of five. Seat today's six, group 2 of five, one to each group. Draw three empty lines on chart paper for the talk chart. If many of day 108's exits showed error I (the line said again unchanged) or error C (a fact neither source gives), start the rehearsal with those posters: each speaker names the source letter for one fact in the first minute.

### The day

#### 15 min. Build the criteria

Today is an application day: the class uses what it knows, nothing is taught as new. Say aloud that both talks are invented and nobody's real talk. Say the good talk from g2-u3-talks, clearly, facing the back row, but in place of its pollen lines say "Her body is covered in soft hair" and "That hair helps her carry what she gathers" (no other pollen fact is used today), then the weak talk, softly, looking at the floor (3 minutes). Pairs tell each other what they could hear and what helped (3 minutes). Draw the class out, one line at a time, and write each on the chart with a small picture: a voice the back row can hear (an ear); whole sentences (a sentence strip); facts from the source (a page). Review: a whole sentence (grade 2, unit 3, day 104). The class says each line in the voice it names (6 minutes). Looking at the listener or at the poster is allowed; say so (1 minute). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Rehearse in fives

Six groups of five, all at once. Review: turn, pair, link, clarify (grade 2, unit 3, day 87). The child holding the turn card is the speaker: a two-minute talk from the poster. The listener to the speaker's left asks one question using the strip, and the speaker answers in a whole sentence. That is 40 seconds; passing the card takes 20 seconds; each turn is 3 minutes and five turns are 15 minutes. Speaking while a partner talks is error K; gaining the floor means waiting for the card. A remark in place of a question is error L; a question about something else is error M; an answer about another pollinator is error N; a fragment such as "On the flower" is error O. Move between groups and listen to today's six, one minute each. In the last 5 minutes each group gives each speaker one star (a chart line done well) and one next step (a chart line to try), a minute a child.

#### 8 min. Volunteers

Three or four volunteers give the first two sentences of their talk to the class (30 seconds each). After each, the class links ("I heard...") or asks one question (90 seconds). Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Independent reading

Each child reads one passage from 01-b to 01-e quietly, the one they have not yet read. Circulate; mark nothing.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X109]]. All tell a partner their question (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Tag a remark error L, a question about something else error M.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children put the posters in the folder, take the student page and move to the foundations block.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No hand-back is routed to this slot. The five words spelled on 108 are on the board, mixed. Children read them to a partner (2 minutes), then cover one, write it from memory and check it letter by letter (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Reader

Review: au and aw say one sound (grade 2, unit 3, day 108). Model (2 minutes): write claw and fault and say that both say the sound you hear in saw; look to tell which, and check the entry when unsure. Map and blend (3 minutes): yawn, claw, cause, fault, hawk; children say the sounds, then the word. Reader (5 minutes): pairs read Dawn in the Garden, one page each, swap, and say what Nan saw. The word yellow is mapped; cover the pictures until after reading. Spell five and a sentence (5 minutes): yawn, claw, cause, fault, hawk, then Nan saw a hawk; the word, a short sentence, the word again.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six read the cycle 19 oral card to you, two minutes each, with 30 seconds of the vowel card and three irregular words in every turn; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: yawn, claw, cause, fault. Sort: eight slips, claw, hawk, fawn, drawn, cause, fault, vault, launch, by au or aw. Reread: the reader for accuracy. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which sound do au and aw share?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the strip and the turn card by hand. A child who prefers not to speak aloud rehearses to one listener and is heard live later; pointing to a poster part is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No poster: use a drawing of a flower and a bee. No chart: say the three jobs aloud.
**Skip.** The groups of five and the reader.

- **10 min. Say your talk.** Your child tells you two sentences about the poster.
  **Say.** "Say it so I can hear you from the door." Expect: a clear voice and whole sentences.
- **10 min. Ask a question.** You say one sentence about a bee. Your child asks one question about it.
  **Say.** "Where? What? How? Which part?" Expect: a question about what you said.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads claw and fault and says which letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X109]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three-child groups with two turns each; keep the chart, the volunteers and the exit. Longer: a speaker adds a second fact from a passage and names its letter.

### Words and supports

Rehearse, listener, detail, star, next step. Frames: "Where does it ___?" "I will tell you about ___." A child may rehearse in the home language first. Home ways of speaking are never ranked.

### Tomorrow

Day 110 practises describing words and says an unclear sentence clearer by talk. Today's exit tells you who asks about what was said: a remark is error L, a question about something else is error M.
