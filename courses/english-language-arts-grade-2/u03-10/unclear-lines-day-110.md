# Unclear lines, in pairs (day 110)

Three pairs of invented lines. In each pair, one line tells more. Pairs choose the line that tells more and say what the added words tell.

- A fly went. / A tiny fly went quickly to a daisy.
- A bee sat. / A fuzzy bee sat on a soft petal.
- Dust fell. / Yellow dust fell on a green leaf.
