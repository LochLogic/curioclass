# Day 110: Practise describing words and make a sentence clearer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the two poems and the drawing of the fuzzy visitor where all can see them. Mark today's six, group 3 of five. If many of day 109's exits showed error L (a remark, not a question) or error M (a question about something else), begin the pairs part by having each pair ask the partner one question about "A fly went" with the question strip, then choose the line that tells more.

### The day

#### 18 min. Poems

Today is a fluency day: the class practises, nothing is taught as new and nothing is written. Read 03-b, A Dusty Visitor, aloud once; the class reads it with you twice more (4 minutes). Then volunteers read a line each while the class echoes (4 minutes). Poem 03-a, Land, Cling, Carry, is read for sound only: the class claps the rhythm and says the chorus, "Land, cling, carry," with you (4 minutes). The poems are read together, never alone. Ask: "Which words help you see the visitor?" Children tell a partner, then the class (5 minutes). Gaining the floor means waiting for the turn card; speaking over a partner is error K (1 minute).

#### 15 min. Describing words

Review: an adjective is a describing word for a thing; an adverb is a describing word for an action (grade 2, unit 2, day 56). Pairs sort the eight cards onto the drawing of the visitor: words that tell how it looks beside the visitor, words that tell how it moves beside its action; a word may go in both places and the pair says a short phrase for it (6 minutes). Then each child looks at the drawing and makes a sentence of their own about the visitor with one describing word and one word for how it moves, and says it to a partner, who says which word was for the visitor and which was for the moving; swap, 30 seconds each, simultaneous (3 minutes). Five volunteers say their sentence to the class (4 minutes). A describing word put on the wrong job is error R. Hear one sentence from each of today's six if they were not chosen (2 minutes).

#### 15 min. Make it clearer, by talk

Pairs take the three pairs of invented lines; in each, one line tells more. They choose the line that tells more and say what the added words tell (3 minutes each, 9 minutes). Whole class: which added word did you like, and what does it help you see? (4 minutes). Link: "I heard..." (2 minutes). Nothing is written. The same sentence said again with no new word is error Z.

#### 5 min. Independent reading

Each child reads one passage from 01-b to 01-e quietly, a different one from yesterday. Circulate; mark nothing.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X110]] and then [[s03-X110b]]. All say both sentences to a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each for the two asks (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A new word put on the wrong place is error R; no new word at all is error Z.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children put the cards in the envelope, take the student page and move to the foundations block.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No hand-back is routed to this slot. Children read the five words spelled on 109 to a partner (2 minutes), then cover one, write it from memory and check it letter by letter (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Reread and use the pattern

Review: au and aw say one sound (grade 2, unit 3, day 108). Pairs reread Dawn in the Garden aloud, one page each, then again, making it sound like talking; the partner says one thing that sounded good (5 minutes). Sort the ten cards into au and aw (4 minutes). Spell four (3 minutes): shawl, flaw, launch, vault; the word, a short sentence, the word again. Write one sentence (3 minutes): I saw a hawk.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six read the cycle 19 oral card to you, two minutes each, with 30 seconds of the vowel card and three irregular words in every turn; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: shawl, flaw, hawk, vault. Sort: eight slips by au or aw. Reread: the reader for accuracy. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Say an aw word you heard today." Children tell a partner, then say one together.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards by hand. Pointing to a card is an answer; a child may whisper the sentence to you.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the eight words on torn paper. No poem: skip it.
**Skip.** The poems and the sorting of cards.

- **10 min. Describing words.** Your child looks at a toy or a flower and says one sentence with a word for how it looks and a word for how it moves.
  **Say.** "Which word is for the thing, and which is for the moving?" Expect: the two words named.
- **10 min. Make it clearer.** You say "A fly went." Your child says it again so it tells more.
  **Say.** "What do the new words tell me?" Expect: what the fly looks like or how it went.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child reads hawk and shawl and says which letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X110]] and [[s03-X110b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two of the three pairs of lines; keep the poems, the sort and the exit. Longer: a child says the sentence again with a third describing word and says what it tells.

### Words and supports

Describing word, how it looks, how it moves, clearer, line. Frames: "The ___ visitor ___." "The new words tell ___." A child may point to the card and say the word.

### Tomorrow

Day 111 compares talk for a visitor with talk for a friend. Today's exit tells you who can add a word for the thing and a word for the action: a word on the wrong job is error R, no new word is error Z.
