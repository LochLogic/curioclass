# Exit cards, days 109 to 112

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 109 (s03-X109).** This card is invented for practice. A speaker says: "The beetle walks on the flower and the pollen sticks." Say: "Ask the speaker one question to get one more detail."
- **Day 110 (s03-X110).** This card is invented for practice. Say: "The beetle came to the flower." Say: "Say it again with one word that tells how the beetle looks."
- **Day 110 (s03-X110b).** Same sentence. Say: "Say it again with one word that tells how the beetle came."
- **Day 111 (s03-X111).** This card is invented for practice. Say two lines: "Let me tell you about the moth's visit." and "The moth went there, you know." Say: "Which line would you use to open a talk for a visitor who has not seen your poster?"
- **Day 111 (s03-X111b).** Same two lines. Say: "Say why that line suits the visitor."
- **Day 112 (s03-X112).** This card is invented for practice. Read the plan line: "Then the bee hung under the leaf while the rain tapped." Describe two drawings: a close view of the bee's feet gripping the leaf, and a wide view of the whole hedge. Say: "Say which drawing would help a listener."
- **Day 112 (s03-X112b).** Same plan line and drawings. Say: "Say why."
