# Day 112: Plan a bee-view story and record one sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the recording corner at a side table with the device and the recording list. Draw the five boxes large on the board. Make the check cards for A52 to A54 by hand from the day's check items. Mark today's six, group 5 of five. If many of day 111's exits showed error Z (the other line) or error X (a line ranked as bad), say before the comparison what each line suits and who is listening.

### The day

#### 8 min. The model

Today is an application day: the class plans, nothing is taught as new. Review: a story told by an imagined narrator (grade 2, unit 3, day 104). Read pages 1, 3, 4, 8 and 12 of One Bee, Two Flowers aloud, stopping at each to fill one box with the class: where the bee is, what it wants, what it does in order, what it notices with its body, how it ends. The five boxes stay on the board.

#### 4 min. Show why

Say a sentence of your own about a bee, record it, and play it back. Ask: "What can you hear?" and "What would make it clearer?" Children tell a partner, then the class. Recordings stay in the class; a child may record in a pair or be heard live instead.

#### 35 min. Plan and record

Each child fills the plan page, a word or a drawing in each of the five boxes, with a drawing for the box about the body and one other box. A child asks two partners before asking you. Children go one at a time to the recording corner in the order of the list and record one planned sentence, one minute a child: 30 minutes, plus 5 minutes of changeovers, which is the whole 35. Name the next child while the last one records. You stay at the corner; the others plan quietly. A child who has not reached the corner by the end records on day 113. If the device fails, each child says the sentence into a paper tube and you listen live. Error O is a fragment such as "On the flower"; say who did what.

#### 5 min. Comparison collected

Say aloud that the lines are invented. Read the two lines twice: "I will describe our next flower visit." and "Here is our next stop." Each child says or writes on the sheet which line is for a visitor and which for a friend, and one word that told them. Collect the sheets; give written feedback after class; children revise on 113 and the revision is scored on 118.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read the plan line and describe the two drawings (1 minute): [[s03-X112]] and then [[s03-X112b]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each for the two asks (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A drawing picked for how it looks is error V; "I like it" is error E.

#### 3 min. Transitions

Children put the plan page in the folder, take the check sheet and move to the foundations block.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No hand-back is routed to this slot. Children read the au and aw words on the wall to a partner (2 minutes), then read the three irregular words and the vowel card together (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Cycle 19 written check

Only the cycle's own patterns and spelling; the cards are invented and you say so aloud. Written (5 minutes): [[s03-A50]] and [[s03-A51]]; say the word, the sentence, the word again, and children write it on the spelling part of the check sheet. Write down the support each child needed. For the other 10 minutes the class rereads Dawn in the Garden quietly in pairs while you hear a sampled six, the six of group 4, at the teacher table, about a minute and a half each: [[s03-A52]], [[s03-A53]] and [[s03-A54]], which are spoken items. Hand the printed card or the draft and entry card as each item says. The rest of the class is sampled in later foundations blocks. Feedback on the spelling after class; the correction is offered on 113 and scored on 114. A neighbour's spelling copied is error J; the letters out of order is error AN; a vowel team swapped for another with the same sound is error AO; a guess from the first letter is error S.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six do the check cards with you instead of the oral card, about two minutes each: [[s03-A55]], [[s03-A56]], [[s03-A57]], then [[s03-A58]] with [[s03-A59]] and [[s03-A59b]] on the reader; 24 are at stations. A child who needs a little longer finishes in round 2. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: hawk, claw, fault, cause. Sort: eight slips by au or aw. Reread: the reader for accuracy. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Say the word you spelled with aw." Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No device: use a paper tube and listen live. A child who cannot write answers the check aloud to you; pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say the sentence to a grown-up and ask for it back. No book: tell the bee story from memory.
**Skip.** The recording corner and the written check.

- **10 min. Plan.** Your child tells a short bee story in five parts: where the bee is, what it wants, what it does, what it notices with its body, how it ends.
  **Say.** "What does the bee notice with its body?" Expect: a touch or a feel, such as pollen on its fur.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws one thing a listener could not see from words alone.
- **10 min. Say it.** Your child says one planned sentence aloud.
  **Say.** "Who did what?" Expect: a doer and an action.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X112]] and [[s03-X112b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill the boxes for where, does and ending; keep the recording, the exit and the check. Longer: a child plans a second flower visit and draws it.

### Words and supports

Plan, box, notice, drawing, record. Frames: "The bee ___." "A listener needs to see ___." A child may draw a box instead of writing it.

### Tomorrow

Day 113 holds the first posters. Today's exit tells you who picks a drawing by what a listener needs to see (error V is a pick by looks) and gives a reason from the story (error E is a reason with none). Today's comparison sheets get written feedback after class and are revised on 113; the check papers are corrected on 113.
