# Day 132: Find the point an author makes and the reasons for it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print 05-a large and one arrow sheet for 05-a a pair; copy the exit cards for days 132 to 136. Mark the six for the exit's spoken ask and the six for the oral card (children 19 to 24). Say aloud that the exit card is made up and that 05-a is a true article. Keep the fresh passage face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval (practice only, no check evidence)

Review: you learned a point (what the author wants us to do or think) and a reason (tells why the point is a good idea) in grade 1. Pairs tell each other one thing an author of a book they know wants a reader to do or think, and one reason the author gives. Listen and note one miss. Nothing is marked.

#### 10 min. Read 05-a

Point to the unit question on the wall. Read 05-a aloud (2 minutes, about 250 words). Pairs read it by turns, a paragraph each (5 minutes). The class rereads the first and last paragraphs together (3 minutes). Words previewed: pollinator, nectar, bloom, clump.

#### 10 min. Model

Say what the day teaches. A point is what the author wants us to do or think. A reason tells why the point is a good idea. A joining word such as because, also or so ties a reason to its point. On the large copy, box the point: "plant flowers that bloom in every season". Then draw an arrow from each reason to the box and say it aloud:

- "Plant flowers in every season because early bees need food."
- "Plant flowers in every season, and fall flowers give food before the cold."
- "Also, a clump is easy for a pollinator to find."

Ask once: "If the reason were not true, is the point still a good idea?" Review: in kindergarten you put a point in a box and drew an arrow from each reason (a box with arrows).

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which reasons support the point?" Turn: each child tells a partner one reason (3 minutes). Pair: two pairs join and agree on the reasons (3 minutes). Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (2 minutes). Clarify: ask a speaker "Which part of the article tells that?" (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Pairs

Each pair gets the arrow sheet, which already has one arrow drawn: from "Fall flowers give food before the cold" to a box that reads "Bees wake up in early spring". Pairs judge the arrow and fix it (5 minutes). Then they draw the arrows from the other reasons to the point and add one reason of each pair's own from 05-a in the blank box (10 minutes). Confer with the six heard (about 90 seconds each, 5 minutes inside the 20). A reason tied to a detail instead of the point is error G.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X132]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X132b]] (1 minute). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 131's exit (error B). Lay the two basket pictures from day 131 side by side, point to the event that holds the lesson up and say the link: "This event shows the lesson." (3 minutes). Then show the commonest cycle 22 slip from the oral cards and say the link between letters and sound (2 minutes).

#### 15 min. A fresh decodable passage

Turn over Bell and the Button. Read the title. Children read it silently once, then pair-read it, a sentence each (8 minutes). Then confirm meaning aloud: what Bell's problem is, and where she finds the button (4 minutes). Where a child misreads, say: "Check the letters and the sense, and reread" (RF.2.4c, 3 minutes). A first-letter guess is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 19 to 24 read the cycle 22 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The same asks as on day 133 apply (the items are filed on 133). The others use tiles and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Dictation

Today's dictation is one word at a time, built and not listed: the shape of the day 133 check is kept clean. Say ribbon. Children build it with tiles, map the weak sound, and write it on the slate (3 minutes). Do the same for wagon and problem (6 minutes). Then each pair fixes four wrong drafts, buton, ribbin, wagen and insekt, saying which part is weak (6 minutes). Spelling button as buten is error R.

#### 8 min. Dictionary

Pairs draw one word slip (pocket, ribbon, wagon or button), find the first letter, find the entry in the printed beginning dictionary, read the meaning aloud and point to the entry (L.2.2e, 6 minutes). Review: you found an entry in a beginning dictionary in unit 1. Share one meaning (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Close

Reread the first and last page of the cycle 22 reader together, pausing at each full stop. Then each child tells a partner one word with a weak sound.

### Other rooms

No large print: copy 05-a on the board in three short paragraphs. No tiles: card squares. Pointing or drawing an arrow is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: read any short true text that tells a reader to do something.
**Skip.** The dictionary and the slate.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the sheet sent home aloud together, twice.
  **Say.** "What does the author want us to do?" Expect: plant flowers that bloom in every season.
- **10 min. Reasons.** Ask for one reason and draw an arrow from it to the point.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes ribbon and pocket and says which part is weak.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X132]] and [[s04-X132b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the arrow fix and the exit. Longer: a child finds a second reason for the point in 05-a and says why it fits, or writes a reason that does not fit and says why.

### Words and supports

Point, reason, because, also, pollinator, nectar, bloom, clump. Frames: "The author wants ___." "My reason is ___ because ___." Say a reason in the home language first. The exit pictures are described aloud for a child who cannot see them.

### Tomorrow

Day 133 opens with a five-minute repair on today's exit: if many marked the grey jacket or gave a reason the article does not state (error G), draw an arrow from each reason to the point it explains and read the pair aloud. Day 133 reads 05-b.
