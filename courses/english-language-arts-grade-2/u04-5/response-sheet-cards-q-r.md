# Response sheet for cards Q and R (day 156)

One sheet a child. Space for one point and the reason that supports it from card Q, one from card R, and a line for the child to say, or the teacher to write, what both cards say. At the top, a line for the name and a line for the support used. The cards are the check texts g2-u4-chk-q and g2-u4-chk-r; keep them unseen until day 156. It is first used in the last section of the unit and is made here with the others.
