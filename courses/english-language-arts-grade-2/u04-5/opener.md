# Section 4.5 opener: points and reasons in articles (days 132 to 137)

## What the section does

For six days the class reads short true articles about helping pollinators. Children find the author's point, match each reason to the point it explains, compare the important points of two articles, and build an opinion with a topic, a position, reasons, a link and an ending. On day 137 two new articles (cards M and N) are read and answered in writing. The cycle 22 written check is on day 133.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a short opening repair, the day's reading, a model, talk, pair work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds a repair, work on the cycle's spelling pattern, a reader, a rotation of stations with the oral card, and a close. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask on paper or aloud. A child outside the group heard is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Say aloud that every practice card is made up. The articles are true; the exit cards are made up.

## The ideas at an adult's level

- **A point is what the author wants you to think or do.** A reason tells why. A fact that supports it is a detail.
- **Each reason belongs to a point.** A reason tied to the wrong point is not yet checked against it.
- **Two articles can share a point.** Say the point both articles make, not a small detail.
- **An opinion has parts.** A topic, a position, reasons joined by because, and an ending. A reason must fit the position.

## The errors in plain words

- A reason is attached to the wrong point (G).
- A small detail replaces the important point (F).
- The writing states no opinion (I).
- A reason does not fit the opinion (J).
- The topic is never named (H).
- Reasons are not joined with linking words (K).

Show the right answer beside the wrong one, say the link together, and let the child try again. Repairs are five minutes at the start of the next day.

## Marking

One scale for every look-for: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record it on the record sheet. Nothing is scored beyond the exits and the oral and written cycle checks; they tell you what to repair.
