# Day 136: Draft an opinion and show which reason supports it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the frame with prompts only (no filled words), three reason strips, the shared-writing strips and twelve word slips; copy the exit cards for days 132 to 136. Mark the six for the exit's spoken ask and the six for the oral card (children 13 to 18). Say aloud that the exit card is made up. Today the frame is shown for the last time.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 135's exit (error I). Lay a label-only strip, "Flowers", beside the strip "We should ___". Add the writer's position with "should" and say it: "We should plant flowers." If H, K or L was commoner, use its register fix instead.

#### 8 min. Reread

Read the shared opinion from day 135 aloud, stressing because and also. Children point to each strip as it is read.

#### 10 min. Model

Show the frame with prompts only, for a new topic from 05-c: a place to live. The opinion: "We should leave a few dry stems standing." Say which text reason supports it: many bees nest in hollow stems. Selected evidence is withdrawn: hide the reason strip and have children find the line in 05-c that gives it. Say the sentence with because. Review: you built an opinion with a topic, a position, a reason and an ending on day 135. Today the Link strip (because, and, also) sits between the opinion and each reason.

#### 7 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which reason fits your opinion?" Turn (2 minutes), pair (2 minutes), link (2 minutes), clarify with "Which line of the article tells that?" (1 minute).

#### 25 min. Pairs

Each pair gets three given reasons for the opinion about dry stems, one of which does not fit; they find it and cross it out (5 minutes). A reason that is only a liking is error J. Then pairs draft a shared opinion on another need from 05-c (food, shelter or a place to nest, not the dry stems) with the frame and one reason from the article: a topic, an opinion with "should", one reason with because, and an ending (14 minutes). Pairs read the draft to another pair (6 minutes). Confer with the six heard (about 90 seconds each, inside the 25). Judge four look-fors: the topic is named; an opinion is stated; a reason from the article is joined by because or also; an ending sentence. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X136]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X136b]] (1 minute). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed to this part. Slates: children write gate and pirate and say which ending is strong and which is weak.

#### 12 min. Reread the cycle 23 reader

Pairs reread The Garden Gate for accuracy, one page each (6 minutes). Then read the whole once more smoothly, pausing at each full stop (6 minutes, RF.2.4b).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 13 to 18 read the cycle 23 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The others use tiles and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Sort

Pairs sort twelve slips under the schwa -ate and the long a -ate: pirate, accurate, moderate, ultimate, separate, fortunate; rotate, imitate, pollinate, create, migrate, vibrate (9 minutes). Children say each word and where the strong sound sits. Each child then takes four slips, looks, covers, writes from memory and checks: pirate, rotate, pollinate, migrate (6 minutes).

#### 11 min. Use the pattern in sentences

Each child writes one sentence with a cycle word (7 minutes) and reads it to a partner (4 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Ask: "Which ending is strong in rotate?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No frame sheet: write the four prompts on the board. No tiles: card squares. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: ask your child for a view on a family choice and a reason.
**Skip.** The sort and the slate.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the sheet sent home aloud together.
  **Say.** "What do you think we should do?" Expect: a sentence with should.
- **10 min. Reason.** Ask for one reason and whether it fits the view.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes rotate and migrate and says which ending is strong.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X136]] and [[s04-X136b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the cross-out and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second reason that fits and says why.

### Words and supports

Topic, opinion, reason, link, ending, fits. Frames: "We should ___ because ___." "This reason fits because ___." Say it in the home language first; pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 137 opens with a five-minute repair on today's exit: if many wrote "I like herbs" (error J), replace it with a fact from the source. Day 137 is the check on two new articles, cards M and N.
