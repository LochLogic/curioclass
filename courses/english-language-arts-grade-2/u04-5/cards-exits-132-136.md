# Exit cards, days 132 to 136

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Where a day has two rows, ask them one after another, one ask each. The first row of each day is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 132 (s04-X132).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Article: Children should wear bright jackets on the walk to school. Drivers can see bright colours from far away." Show two picture cards: a child in a bright jacket seen by a driver; a child in a grey jacket not seen. Say: "Mark the picture that shows the author's point."
- **Day 132 (s04-X132b).** Same article. Say: "Say the reason the author gives."
- **Day 133 (s04-X133).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Article: The class should plant radishes. Radishes sprout fast. The class should also water early. Early water stays in the soil." Show two picture cards: a radish sprout up after a few days; wet soil in cool morning light. Say: "Mark the picture that shows the reason for watering early."
- **Day 133 (s04-X133b).** Same article. Say: "Say the reason the article gives for watering early."
- **Day 134 (s04-X134).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Article A: Birds need a dish of water. Birds get thirsty. Wash the dish each week. Article B: Birds need a dish of water too. A shallow dish lets birds drink and splash." Show two picture cards: birds at a dish of water; a brush washing a dish. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what both articles say is most important."
- **Day 134 (s04-X134b).** Same articles. Say: "Say one thing both articles say."
- **Day 135 (s04-X135).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Our school hallway has a slippery floor. Children run in it. A sign could remind everyone to walk." Show two strips: a strip with spaces for a topic, an opinion, two reasons and an ending; a strip with a topic label only. Say: "Mark the strip that could hold a complete opinion from this card."
- **Day 135 (s04-X135b).** Same card. Say: "Say one sentence for the opinion strip that gives the opinion and a reason, using because."
- **Day 136 (s04-X136).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A window box holds herbs that stay green in winter. A visitor says it will help a winter cook." Show two picture cards: green herbs in snow; bare soil. Say: "Mark the picture that supports the opinion."
- **Day 136 (s04-X136b).** Same card. Say: "Say a reason that supports the opinion."
