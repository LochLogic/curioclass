# Exit cards, days 132 to 136

Rows that open "This card is invented for practice" are made up: say so aloud and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. The other rows reread a passage of the unit's own texts, named in the row. Read the card or passage twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Where a day has two rows, ask them one after another, one ask each. The first row of each day is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 132 (s04-X132v2).** Read aloud twice 05-c, A Place to Live, paragraph 4, about nests. Show two picture cards: dry stems standing and a patch of bare soil left alone; a raked bed with every stem cut down. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what the author wants people to do."
- **Day 132 (s04-X132bv2).** Same paragraph. Say: "Say the reason the author gives."
- **Day 133 (s04-X133v2).** Read aloud twice 05-c, A Place to Live, paragraph 3, about water. Show two picture cards: tiny mosquito wrigglers in old water; a bee standing on a stone in the dish. Say: "Mark the picture that shows why the article says to change the water."
- **Day 133 (s04-X133bv2).** Same paragraph. Say: "Say the reason the article gives for changing the water."
- **Day 134 (s04-X134v2).** Read aloud twice the first paragraph of 05-d, Planning a Flower Pot, then reread the first paragraph of 05-a. Show two picture cards: flowers in bloom with a bee visiting; a pot with holes in the bottom. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what both articles say is most important."
- **Day 134 (s04-X134bv2).** Same paragraphs. Say: "Say one thing both articles say."
- **Day 135 (s04-X135).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Our school hallway has a slippery floor. Children run in it. A sign could remind everyone to walk." Show two strips: a strip with spaces for a topic, an opinion, two reasons and an ending; a strip with a topic label only. Say: "Mark the strip that could hold a complete opinion from this card."
- **Day 135 (s04-X135b).** Same card. Say: "Say one sentence for the opinion strip that gives the opinion and a reason, using because."
- **Day 136 (s04-X136).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A window box holds herbs that stay green in winter. A visitor says it will help a winter cook." Show two picture cards: green herbs in snow; bare soil. Say: "Mark the picture that supports the opinion."
- **Day 136 (s04-X136b).** Same card. Say: "Say a reason that supports the opinion."
