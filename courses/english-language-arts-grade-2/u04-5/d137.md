# Day 137: Read two new articles and compare the points

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep cards M and N and their response sheet face down. Mark the six for the spoken ask and the six for the oral card (children 19 to 24). Collect the student pages at the end of the day.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 136's exit (error J). Replace "I like herbs" with a fact from the source: the herbs stay green in winter.

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: a point and its reason, and the most important points. The cards are new and made up for practice. Hand out the sheets. Collect the student pages at the end of the day.

#### 25 min. I1 and I2

Pairs read cards M and N (5 minutes). I1 (10 minutes): each child writes a reason from card M and a reason from card N, [[s04-A9]] and [[s04-A10]]; hear the six tell how a reason supports its point, 40 seconds each (4 minutes inside the 10), [[s04-A11]]. I2 (10 minutes) on the same sheet: [[s04-A12]] and [[s04-A13]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A reason on the wrong point is error G; a detail for a point is F.

#### 15 min. Pairs

Pairs read the sheets to each other and each asks one question. Not marked.

#### 10 min. Close

The class names one reason and the point it supports.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Slates: pirate, rotate, pollinate; name the strong ending.

#### 15 min. A fresh decodable passage

Read The Pirates Plan in pairs, then confirm its meaning aloud.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 19 to 24 read the cycle 23 oral card, two minutes each including the swap. Others use tiles.

#### 15 min. Dictation

One word at a time, built and not listed: children build skate and create with tiles and write each. Pairs complete two sentence strips with the missing -ate word.

#### 8 min. Dictionary

Pairs find pirate or rotate in the printed beginning dictionary, read the meaning and point to the entry.

#### 5 min. Close

Reread The Pirates Plan together, pausing at each full stop.

### Other rooms

Read the cards aloud twice; answers are aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read a short true text aloud twice and use it.
**Skip.** The dictionary and the tiles.

- **15 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A9]] [[s04-A10]] [[s04-A11]] [[s04-A12]] [[s04-A13]]
- **15 min. Two texts.** Read two short true texts on one topic.
  **Say.** "What do both texts want, and what is different?" Expect: one shared aim and one difference.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes pirate and rotate and says which ending is strong.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep I1 and the spoken ask. Longer: a child writes one more reason for a point.

### Words and supports

Point, reason, both, different. Frames: "The reason is ___ so ___." "Both want ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 138 opens with a ten-minute repair on the sheets (error G): a written note on each sheet, then a short retry. It rereads a model opinion.
