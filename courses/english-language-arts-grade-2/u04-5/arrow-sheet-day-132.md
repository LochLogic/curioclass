# Arrow sheet, day 132

One sheet a pair. The author's point is printed in a box at the top. Under it, each reason from the article is printed as a strip with room beside it. The children draw an arrow from a reason to the point it explains. A last box is blank for the pair to add a reason of their own from the article.

The sheet carries only the words of the article of the day (g2-u4-05), not the exit card.
