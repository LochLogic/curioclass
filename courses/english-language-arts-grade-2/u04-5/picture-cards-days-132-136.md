# Picture cards and strips, days 132 to 136

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 132 (s04-X132).** card 1, a child in a bright jacket seen by a driver; card 2, a child in a grey jacket not seen.
- **Day 133 (s04-X133).** card 1, a radish sprout up after a few days; card 2, wet soil in cool morning light.
- **Day 134 (s04-X134).** card 1, birds at a dish of water; card 2, a brush washing a dish.
- **Day 135 (s04-X135).** card 1, a strip with spaces for a topic, an opinion, two reasons and an ending; card 2, a strip with a topic label only.
- **Day 136 (s04-X136).** card 1, green herbs in snow; card 2, bare soil.
