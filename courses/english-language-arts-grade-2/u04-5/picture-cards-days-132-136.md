# Picture cards and strips, days 132 to 136

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, one idea a card, with "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the invented ones and the passage named at the foot of the others. Print each pair so the two cards are the same size and side by side in a mixed order that does not follow the answer. Draw only what the card says; add no objects the card does not name.

- **Day 132 (s04-X132v2).** card 1, dry stems standing and a patch of bare soil left alone; card 2, a raked bed with every stem cut down.
- **Day 133 (s04-X133v2).** card 1, tiny mosquito wrigglers in a dish of old water; card 2, a bee standing on a stone in a water dish.
- **Day 134 (s04-X134v2).** card 1, flowers in bloom with a bee visiting; card 2, a pot with holes in the bottom.
- **Day 135 (s04-X135).** card 1, a strip with spaces for a topic, an opinion, two reasons and an ending; card 2, a strip with a topic label only.
- **Day 136 (s04-X136).** card 1, green herbs in snow; card 2, bare soil.
