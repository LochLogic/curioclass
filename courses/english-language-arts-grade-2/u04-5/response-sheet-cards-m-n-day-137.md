# Response sheet for cards M and N (day 137)

One sheet a child. Space for the child to write one supported point and one reason, and a line for the child to say, or the teacher to write, how the two cards are alike or different. At the top, a line for the name and a line for the support used. The cards themselves are the check texts g2-u4-chk-m and g2-u4-chk-n; keep them unseen until day 137.
