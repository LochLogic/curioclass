# Shared-writing strips, days 135 and 136

One strip a child, four kinds: Topic, Opinion, Reason and Ending, each with a colored edge and its own heading. The class places the strips on the board in order. On day 136 the class adds a Link strip (because, and, also) between an opinion and a reason. The strips are blank.
