# Day 135: Build an opinion with a topic, reasons and an ending

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the scrambled four-strip set about 05-b, print the shared-writing strips and copy the exit cards for days 132 to 136. Mark the six for the exit's spoken ask and the six for the oral card (children 7 to 12). Say aloud that the exit card is made up. An opinion is what the writer thinks should be done. Its parts are a topic, an opinion with "should", reasons joined by because or also, and an ending.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 134's exit (error F). Name the point the details add up to, cover the details and say it. Use 05-c: the details about dishes, stems and leaves add up to "give pollinators a habitat". Children say it to a partner.

#### 10 min. Reread 05-b as an opinion

Pairs reread 05-b and find its topic, its opinion, its reasons joined by first, second and third, and its ending sentence. They mark each with a colored edge (RF.2.4b, W.2.1).

#### 10 min. Model

On the board build a four-strip opinion for 05-a with the paragraph frame. Topic: flowers for pollinators. Opinion: "We should plant flowers that bloom in every season." Reasons: "because early bees need food" and "also, fall flowers give food before the cold". Ending: "So flowers from spring to fall will keep our pollinators fed." Read it aloud and stress the linking words because and also. Every reason comes from the article. A reason tied to the opinion by a linking word is easier to follow than two bare sentences. New: the parts of an opinion piece. Review: in grade 1 you built a four-part opinion paragraph with a topic, an opinion, a reason and a closing.

#### 8 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on "Which reason do you want to add, and why?" Turn (2 minutes), pair (2 minutes), link with "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (2 minutes), clarify with "Which part of the article gives that?" (2 minutes).

#### 22 min. Shared writing

Pairs get the scrambled four strips about 05-b, put them in order and label each strip (6 minutes). Then each child writes one reason with because on a blank strip, chosen from 05-a (8 minutes). Children bring strips to the board in tables of five, about 15 seconds a child, and read each aloud (6 minutes). The class chooses an ending together (2 minutes). A draft that starts with a reason is error H.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X135]]. Each child marks one of the two strips (2 minutes). Hear the six, 15 seconds each: [[s04-X135b]] (1 minute). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Slates: children write rotate and pirate and tell which ending is strong and which is weak. Review: the ending -ate with two sounds (grade 2, unit 4, day 134).

#### 12 min. Read the matched decodable

Pairs reread The Garden Gate for accuracy, a page each (7 minutes). Confirm the meaning: who rotates the latch and what happens (5 minutes). A first-letter guess is error Q.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 23 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. The others use tiles and the reader in two stations of twelve. Mark the heard column.

#### 16 min. Spell

Repair first (5 minutes): show the commonest cycle 22 miss from the day 133 sheets beside the right form and say the link (error R). Then build and write rotate, imitate and pollinate on slates, one at a time (6 minutes). Pairs complete four sentence strips with the missing -ate word from a choice of two (5 minutes).

#### 10 min. Map irregular words

Map says and have with the sound-spelling map: ay says a short e in says; the e after v is silent in have. Children trace each letter group, say the word and write it. There is no guessing from the picture or the first letter.

#### 5 min. Close

Reread the first two pages of the reader together, pausing at each full stop. Each child names one word with the strong ending.

### Other rooms

No strips: fold a sheet in four and label the parts. No tiles: card squares. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No article: ask your child for one thing the family should do and why.
**Skip.** The slate and the sentence strips.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the sheet sent home aloud together.
  **Say.** "What does the author think we should do?" Expect: plant flowers that bloom in every season.
- **10 min. Opinion.** Make a sentence with should and because about a household job.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes rotate and says whether the ending is strong or weak.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X135]] and [[s04-X135b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the strip order and the exit. Longer: a child adds a second reason with also and says why it fits.

### Words and supports

Topic, opinion, reason, because, also, ending. Frames: "We should ___ because ___." "Also, ___." "Let us ___." Say the opinion in the home language first; pointing to a strip is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 136 opens with a five-minute repair on today's exit: if many marked the label-only strip or wrote no opinion (error I), add the writer's position with "should". Day 136 drafts a shared opinion on 05-c with the frame, which is withdrawn after that day.
