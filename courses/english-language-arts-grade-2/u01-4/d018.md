# Day 18: Check: questions and a paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print 02-d (marked "invented for practice"), a frame card and a lined page for each child, the cycle 4 sort cards and the four-row feedback sheet. Day 17's errors wait for day 20: D for the retry, L, M and P for the groups.

### The day

#### 15 min. Directions and access

Read aloud: "Today you read a new passage, answer questions and begin a paragraph. I will not help with answers. The frame card stays on your desk." No teaching. A child who cannot read 02-d hears it once; pointing is an answer.

#### 15 min. RI.2.1 on 02-d

Four minutes: children read 02-d alone. Three minutes: each writes a question the passage answers, and its answer (s01-A10). Six minutes: read each stem twice and children write a few words: "Who reads together?" (s01-A11) "What do they choose?" (s01-A12) "Where does the teacher pair them?" (s01-A13) "When do they read with partners?" (s01-A14) "Why do the partners read in turns?" (s01-A15) "How do pupils read a hard word?" (s01-A16). Two minutes: collect the pages. Shown means an answer from the text; shown with help, right after a third reading of the stem; not yet seen, a wrong or no answer ("Every morning" for when is error A).

#### 30 min. W.2.2, begin the paragraph

Five minutes: each tells a partner a plan from the frame card. Twenty minutes: each writes on the lined page; give no help with words. A child who cannot write tells it to you and you write it, supplying none. Five minutes: each reads it aloud to a partner (s01-A17). Unscored. Collect the pages; after class write one sentence a row on the back: topic, facts, meaning, ending.

#### 5 min. Opening retrieval (foundations)

Each child points to a team on the map and says a word. No reteaching.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Partners reread A Light Up High (five minutes); pairs sort the twelve cards into igh, ie and y (five); spell two sentences you say: "The sky is high." "Kay tried a bright light."

#### 37 min. Rotation

Tiles, sort and reread stations, two rounds. Round 1, 19 minutes: your six read the cycle 4 oral card to you, two minutes each (group 3 of five; the five groups cover all 30 children). Round 2, 18 minutes: a second station.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one word with igh, ie or y.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the frame card on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: read 02-d from the view. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The sort, the stations and the oral card.

- **15 min. The assessment: questions.** Read 02-d aloud once; ask the six questions on the student page, one at a time.
  **Say.** "Who reads together?" Expect: pupils, in pairs.
- **20 min. The assessment: begin the paragraph.** Your child tells you a plan, then writes on side 2.
- **10 min. Igh, ie and y.** Say night, pie and sky; your child spells each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sentences. Longer: a second fact in the plan.

### Words and supports

Partner, topic, fact. Frame: "The passage says ___ ." Spelling may be answered with tiles. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 returns your four lines and the paragraph; children revise and finish it, and you score it once, with no reteaching. Error A on "when" waits for day 20's round four.
