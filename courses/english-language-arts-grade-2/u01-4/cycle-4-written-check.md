# Cycle 4 written check (day 20, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word taught on days 16 to 19.

1. "The cat got a ____ and ran." (fright)
2. "Dad ____ an egg in the pan." (fried)
3. "The wet cloth will ____ soon." (dry)
4. "The lid is too ____ to open." (tight)

Tag wrong spellings: "frite", "frid", "dri", "tite" (Q, spelled by sound, pattern not carried over). Record other spellings separately.
