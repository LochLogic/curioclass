# Day 20 group record

A sheet with the children's names down the left and four columns: **Letter from days 16 to 19** (A, D, E, F, G, H to K, L, M, O, P or none), **Group round** (1 to 4), **Fresh retry card** (apron card, or the envelope card for no letter) and **Shown, shown with help, not yet seen**. Fill in the first column from your notes before the class arrives.
