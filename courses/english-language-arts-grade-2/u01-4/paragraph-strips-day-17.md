# Six strips for day 17 (from the article A Place to Read)

Cut six strips, one sentence on each. Pairs slide them into a paragraph, read it aloud and cross out the one strip that does not belong.

1. **Pupils in one Finnish town read every school day.**
2. **In 2025, a town called Riihimäki started a reading time in its schools.**
3. **At about ten o'clock each school day, pupils read a book for ten minutes.**
4. **A library is a place where people borrow books and read.**
5. **A little reading every day adds up.**
6. **Children start school in the year they turn seven.**

Order for the teacher: 1 topic, 2 fact, 3 fact, 4 definition, 5 conclusion. Strip 6 is true but is about when school starts, so it is crossed out. Say "in 2025" as the article does; say "one Finnish town", not "Finland".
