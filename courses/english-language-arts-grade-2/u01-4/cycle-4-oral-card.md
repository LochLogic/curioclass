# Cycle 4 oral card (days 16 to 20)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 16 only the first two lines are used, because no cycle 4 pattern was taught before that day. Children read the words and say each long vowel; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 3 patterns (ee, ea, y).** tree, feet, green, beach, reach, funny
- **Cycle 4 patterns (igh, ie, y).** light, night, high, right, pie, tried, my, fly, why

Partner word card for day 20 (read to each other, then spell two words you say): light, night, pie, tried, my, fly, sky, high. Dictated words: sight, cry.
