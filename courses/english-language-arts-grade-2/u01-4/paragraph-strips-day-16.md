# Paragraph strips (day 16 retrieval)

Four strips cut from scrap paper, one sentence on each, for pairs to put in order and name. This is a grade 1 task; it is practice only.

1. **A reading corner helps pupils read.** (topic)
2. **It has a rug and a low shelf of books.** (fact)
3. **Pupils pick a book and sit on the rug.** (fact)
4. **A reading corner is a quiet place to enjoy books.** (closing)

Pairs lay the strips in order and say which part each one is: topic, fact, fact, closing.
