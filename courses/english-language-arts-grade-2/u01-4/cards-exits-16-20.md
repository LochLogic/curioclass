# Exit cards and retry cards, days 16 to 20

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. You read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Day 18 and 19 have no exit; the cold passage "School Reading Partners" and the paragraph are the check.

- **Day 16 (s01-X16).** "Pupils keep their drawing books in a cubby. A cubby is a small open space with a shelf for one person's things. Each cubby has a name card." Ask: "Tell me what a cubby is, using the card."
- **Day 17 (s01-X17).** "The job board lists names. It names who wipes the tables each day. Pupils check it every morning." Ask: "These facts have no first sentence. Say a first sentence that tells what the paragraph explains."
- **Day 20, a child with no recorded letter (s01-X20).** Draft: "Pupils save used envelopes in a box. They draw on the blank backs." Printed feedback: "End by explaining what saving the envelopes helps pupils do." Ask: "Say an ending for the draft so that a listener understands."
