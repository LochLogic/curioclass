# Day 44: Reading for a listener

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the passages and the exit card large, one set for each pair. Mark today's six (group 2 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Day 43's exit marks show whether error AJ needs the repair. Read Find the Gold yourself first: its page 5 holds the word lost, which breaks the ost rule. Set three stations: tiles, the twelve sort cards, a reread of Find the Gold.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 43's exit (error AJ, a feeling or thought given to Anning), read three strips aloud, saying they are made up for practice: "In 1828 Mary found the skeleton of a flying reptile." "I wonder how wide its wings were." "Mary was amazed." Ask: "Which strip says something the account does not say?" Expect: the third. The first is the account's; the second says "I wonder", so it is a reader's idea; the third gives Mary a feeling no source holds. Otherwise pairs retrieve the chart and say one fact and one idea to each other.

#### 8 min. Model expression

Review: a question mark means the sentence asks (grade 1), and you rested at the full stops in unit 1, day 22. Today the voice shows it. Read page 3 of The Mark in the Stone aloud twice (48 words, 40 seconds each). The first time read flat and fast, with no pause. The second time pause at each full stop and let the voice rise at each question mark. Ask children what changed (3 minutes), then the class echoes page 3 a sentence at a time (3 minutes).

**Ask.** "What did my voice do at the question marks?" Expect: it went up; it sounded like asking.

#### 10 min. Partner reread: story page 4

Pairs reread page 4 (39 words) three times, turn about, each reading to the other (about 20 seconds a reading, 6 minutes). The listener gives one thumbs-up each time the reader pauses at a full stop. Then each pair says which reading was best and why (2 minutes). Move among pairs and listen to three (2 minutes).

#### 12 min. Partner reread: Learning to Look

Read the 56-word part aloud once (30 seconds). Pairs reread it twice, turn about, each time reading all of it (about 30 seconds a reading, 5 minutes). After each reading the listener says one word the reader read smoothly (2 minutes). Hear three pairs read aloud to you, a minute each (3 minutes). Review: mark a slash where a reader pauses (unit 1, day 21): each pair slashes one sentence on their copy and reads it (1 minute).

#### 8 min. The poem Look Again

Read the 29-word poem aloud twice, slowly, children following (1 minute): "I see a curve. I look again. A row of ridges, thin as rain. I draw the shape. I mark the place. Then read the name beside its case." Say that thin as rain compares the ridges to thin lines of falling rain, and that a case is the glass box that holds a display. The class reads it together once, pausing at each line end (review: pause at each line end, unit 1, day 21; 2 minutes). Then pairs read it once each (3 minutes). Hear two pairs (2 minutes).

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which reading helped you understand the most?" Turn: each child tells a partner one reading and why (2 minutes). Pair: two pairs join and say if they chose the same one (2 minutes). Link: "You said ___; I would add ___." Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Hear today's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), and after each one child links or asks. Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

#### 4 min. Mark the pauses

Each child slashes the pauses on the printed line on the student page, "I draw the shape. I mark the place.", then reads it to a partner (2 minutes each way).

Check: a slash after each full stop.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give each child in the six the printed card of item s02-X44. Say: "Read the card aloud for a listener." The six read to you, twice each, about 20 seconds a reading (2 minutes); the others read to a partner, twice each. Shown means the words read accurately, a pause at each full stop and the question mark, a rising voice at the question, and a smoother second reading. Shown with help means after one reminder. Not yet seen means one breath through the whole card (error W). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Retrieve ild, old, ind and ost: show six word cards one at a time, mild, hold, bold, kind, mind, post, and children read each aloud, saying the chunk first. If one reads a long vowel as short, put the chunk on the sound-spelling-map and blend it again (error Y). Review: together and whole from yesterday; map any missed with a star. Review ghost from yesterday: star gh, which says /g/ here. One more word for the oral card, modelled now: held, the past of hold, h-e-ld, with a short e.

#### 15 min. Read: the cycle 8 reader

Review: hello and weird, mapped yesterday. Put a finger on the starred part of each and say the word (2 minutes). Say the title yourself; children do not read it. Read page 1 aloud, a sentence at a time, and the class echoes it (2 minutes). Then pairs read pages 2 to 7 turn about, a page each (about 64 words, 6 minutes), covering the picture until they have read the line, then checking the line against the picture (error X guesses from the picture). Where's, It's and Together are mapped words and read whole. On page 5 the word lost does not follow the ost rule: try the name sound, hear that it makes no word, then use the short o (error Y). Check meaning after decoding (5 minutes).

**Ask.** "Where was the gold ring, and who helped look for it?" Expect: in the last sock; Gran and Jill looked together.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). Today all four lines are used: the grade 1 words, the cycle 7 words, the cycle 8 words and the sentence on the card Each says the vowel sound in each word. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build mild, hold, mind and host, then change one letter. Sort: the twelve cards under ild, old, ind and ost, then a partner says a word and the child points to its pile. Reread: pairs read Find the Gold twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child in a station reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which word did you decode, then check?" Expect: any word, such as lost or weird, and how they checked it against the meaning.

### Other rooms

No printer: read each passage from one copy held up and let pairs say the lines after you; write the poem on the board and the exit card on a large sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed story: use any short story you have. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The poem, the stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read for a listener.** Read three sentences of any story twice, once fast and once with a pause at each full stop. Your child copies the second way.
  **Say.** "What did I do at the end of each sentence?" Expect: you stopped for a moment; at a question your voice went up.
- **10 min. Read.** Show hold, bold, kind and host on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel's name.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say gold, mind and post. Your child writes each and checks the chunk.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X44]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hold the model, one partner reread and the exit, and skip the poem; keep the reader. Longer: pairs record how many pauses they hear in the poem and read it once to another pair.

### Words and supports

Pause, full stop, question mark, listener, smooth. Frames: "I paused at ___." "My voice went up at ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing and drawing are answers. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 turns to writing: the story's pages 4 and 5 are reread for an action, a thought and a feeling, you model one event and its closing from a picture card, and each child writes one. Today's exit (error W, reading with no pause) is repaired at its opening.
