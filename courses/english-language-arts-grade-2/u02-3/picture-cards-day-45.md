# Picture cards (day 45)

Four cards, one picture on each, drawn in thick plain lines.

- **Model card (teacher).** A child finds a ridged pebble by a stream. Say aloud that the case is invented.
- **Shell print.** A spiral shell shape pressed in a flat stone.
- **Leaf print.** A leaf shape with its veins pressed in a flat stone.
- **Fish in rock.** A small fish shape in a slab of gray rock.

Each child chooses one of the last three and writes in a few words: a time word and an action, a thought, a feeling and a closing.
