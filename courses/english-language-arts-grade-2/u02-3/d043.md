# Day 43: Facts and ideas

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 42's marks: the commonest exit miss (error B) and the cycle 7 check misses (error Y) are today's repairs. Mark today's six (group 1 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Hang the two-column fact and idea chart. Cut four strips for each pair (listed below). Set three stations: tiles, the twelve sort cards, the four phrase cards. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-8.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 42's exit (error B, a detail the story does not give), read this card twice and say it is made up for practice: "Dara needs the class to look at her chart, but the room is noisy. She holds up a red flag. Everyone turns to look." Ask: "Tell me how the ending solves the opening problem." Children say "I know because the card says ___" and name the flag. Otherwise partners say what the Beginning card and the Ending card of the story map each do: "The beginning introduces ___." "The ending concludes ___."

#### 10 min. Words

Eight words, each said once by the class, then used in one sentence about Mary or the story; partners say each sentence back (about 75 seconds a word).

New today: account, a true telling of what happened, in order. The Mary Anning text is an account. Sentence: "An account tells what happened, in order."

Review, in the words you used before: compare, look at two things and say how they are the same and different (kindergarten); detail, one fact under a topic (kindergarten); observe, from the observer in kindergarten, a person who looks and listens closely; describe (kindergarten); explain (kindergarten); collect, gather a fact from a reading (kindergarten); support, the line of the text that supports what you say (unit 1, day 28). Sentences: "Dr. Lee held the stone beside a picture card to compare them." "Every line matched, detail for detail." "Mary studied each fossil with care; she observed it closely." "Describe the fan on Amara's stone." "Dr. Lee explained what the picture card showed." "Each child had a bag to collect stones." "A line of the account supports what you say."

#### 10 min. Reread Big Finds

Pairs hold the 101-word Big Finds part, page 2 of the account. Read it aloud once while they follow (1 minute). Then they read it turn about, a paragraph each (2 minutes). Each pair then ticks on the chart sheet every time word (In 1811, In 1823, In 1828, later) and every thing Mary did (dug out the skeleton, kept looking, found a skeleton, cleaned, fixed, drew) (5 minutes). Say: "The account tells what Mary did and when. It never tells what she thought or felt, because no source does."

**Ask.** "What did Mary do in 1811?" Expect: she found and dug out the rest of the skeleton, after her brother found the skull; the account puts the date first. Share three pairs' marks (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Fact or imagined

Read Learning to Look aloud (56 words, 30 seconds). Say: "I am a made-up reader. My ideas are mine, not Mary's." On the wall chart, left column "What the account says": strips "Her father showed her how to look." "Mary looked closely." Right column "What a reader can imagine": "I wonder how small it was." "I imagine the stones were warm." Say: a fact is a line you can point to in the account; an idea is what a reader imagines, and we call it "my idea" and never give it to Mary. Then hold up the red strip: "Mary was scared of the cliffs." Ask children to find it in the account. They cannot. Turn it face down: the account never says it, so it is no fact (this is error AJ).

Pairs now (5 minutes): each has four strips, two facts from Big Finds ("Mary studied each fossil with care." "She made drawings of them.") and two ideas ("I wonder how big the skull was." "I imagine the cliff was steep."). They sort the strips into the two columns on the folded sheet, read each aloud with "The account says ___" or "A reader can imagine ___", and write one more idea in the right column starting "I wonder" or "I imagine".

Check: each strip sits under the right heading and every right-column strip starts with "I".

#### 12 min. Recount one discovery

Each child writes on the student page, in a few words, two sentences from Big Finds: "In 1811 ___." and "Later ___." (the account's 1823 and 1828 finds are "later"; 4 minutes). Review: the time words First, Next, Then and At last (unit 2, day 33); a date and the word later are time words too. Then one sentence for the made-up reader, starting "My idea: I" and holding a thought or a feeling (3 minutes). Say aloud that this reader is invented and the sentence is never Mary's. Partners check that each fact sentence has a time word and an action, and that the idea sentence says "my idea" (3 minutes). You scribe for any child who needs it (2 minutes). Watch for error O (events with no time word) and error AJ (a feeling written as Mary's).

Check: two fact sentences with a time word and an action, one labelled idea.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which words are Mary's facts and which are our idea?" Turn: each child reads a fact and the idea to a partner (2 minutes). Pair: two pairs join, and each pair says if the other's idea stays out of the account (2 minutes). Link: "You said ___; I would add ___." Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Hear today's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), and after each one child links or asks. Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X43 aloud twice, the fact card first. Ask: "Which feeling belongs to the invented reader and not to Anning?" The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means the feeling (eager) given to the reader with its words, "I felt eager". Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the card say?" Not yet seen means a feeling or thought given to Anning (error AJ). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Retrieval (foundations)

Review: oi, oy, ou and ow, from cycle 7 (day 38). Write on the board the four spellings the day 42 check showed most, without naming any child: fownd, foyl, croun, joyn. Put the word wall card beside each: found, foil, crown, join. Children say which two letters make the vowel sound, then fix the word with a partner (error Y). Then read their and people from the word wall, putting a finger on the starred parts.

#### 15 min. Teach: ild, old, ind, ost

Add four chunks to the sound-spelling-map. Say: "ild says /ild/ with the long i, as in mild. old says /old/ with the long o, as in gold. ind says /ind/ with the long i, as in kind. ost says /ost/ with the long o, as in post. Before these chunks the vowel usually says its name."

Model mild: map /m/ /ild/, a bar under ild, blend, build it with tiles. Say that ild is the smallest team: mild is the only one we use today. Do gold the same way (/g/ /old/), then change g to h for hold, f for fold and b for bold. Do kind (/k/ /ind/), then change k to m for mind. Do post (/p/ /ost/), then change p to h for host. Say the limits: a few words break the rule, such as wind (moving air), lost and cost. "Try the name sound first, then the short sound, and keep the one that makes a word that fits," as with ow on day 38. Write "The kind boy held the gold." and read it with the class.

Then five words mapped, never guessed from the picture or the first letter. Together: t-o, g-e-th, er; star the o, which says /oo/. Whole: wh-o-le; star wh, which says /h/ here, and the o-le. Hello: h-e-ll-o; star the e, which says /e/, and the o. Weird: w-ei-r-d; star ei, which says /ee/. Ghost: g-h-o-st; star gh, which says /g/ here, and the o says its name before st. Put all five on the word wall. Last, write where's, there's, it's and she's: the mark is an apostrophe and stands in for letters left out (where is, there is, it is, she is). Review: you read don't and doesn't as whole words on day 38. Today they are read only, not scored.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). Today only the first two lines are used, the grade 1 words (shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest) and the cycle 7 words (oil, point, boy, toy, out, loud, house, cow), saying the vowel sound in each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build mild, gold, kind and post, then change one letter (gold to fold, kind to mind, post to host). Sort: the twelve cycle 8 cards under ild, old, ind and ost, saying each word aloud. Reread: pairs read the four phrase cards twice, the second time smoothly: "a kind boy", "a mild day", "hold the gold", "a bold cow". Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child in a station reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which chunk is in kind?" Expect: ind. Children point to it on the sound-spelling-map.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a sheet into two columns, write the strips and the twelve words on torn paper. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. One copy of the account: read Big Finds aloud twice and let pairs point to each time word on the class copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed account: tell three true things about a person you know and add one idea of your own. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The strips, the stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Fact or idea.** Read the Big Finds part of Mary Anning Looks Closely together. Your child says what Mary did in 1811. Then say an idea of yours that starts "I wonder".
  **Say.** "Is that in the account, or is it our idea?" Expect: it is our idea; the account tells what Mary did and never what she thought.
- **10 min. Read.** Show mild, gold, kind and post on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel's name.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say mind, host and fold. Your child writes each and checks the chunk.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X43]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the extra idea strip; keep the model, the recount, the exit and the chunk teaching. Longer: a pair writes a second fact strip and a second idea strip for a partner to sort.

### Words and supports

Account, collect, compare, observe, support, describe, explain, detail, fact, idea. Frames: "The account says ___." "A reader can imagine ___." "In 1811 ___." "Later ___." "My idea: I ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing and drawing are answers. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 is a fluency day: children reread a story page and a biography part with a partner, pausing at full stops, and read the poem Look Again. Today's exit (error AJ, a feeling given to Anning) is repaired at the opening of day 44.
