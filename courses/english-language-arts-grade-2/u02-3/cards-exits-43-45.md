# Exit cards, days 43 to 45

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards, except on day 44, when each child reads the card aloud from a large print; you read each of the others twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 43 (s02-X43).** "Fact card: Anning collected fossils and sold her finds. Separate card from an invented reader: First I read about her collecting. Next I wondered how she noticed tiny details. At last I felt eager to look closely at a fossil picture." Ask: "Which feeling belongs to the invented reader and not to Anning?"
- **Day 44 (s02-X44).** "Yusuf found a fine line. He stopped. Was it part of the shell? He checked the picture once more." Ask: "Read the card aloud for a listener."
- **Day 45 (s02-X45).** "First I saw a tiny mark. I leaned close and wondered what it was. I felt puzzled. Then I looked at my labelled drawing." Ask: "Write one ending sentence that fits what happened."
