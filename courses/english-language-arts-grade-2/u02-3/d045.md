# Day 45: An event and its closing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 44's exit marks show whether error W needs the repair. Mark today's six (group 3 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Draw the Noor picture on the board. Give each pair three colours. Set three stations: tiles, the twelve sort cards, a reread of Find the Gold. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-8.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 44's exit (error W, one breath through with no pause), read this made-up card twice, once racing and once pausing, and say it is for practice: "Nia lifted the lid. Was it empty? She looked inside." Ask: "Which reading let you hear the question?" Expect: the second. Children echo it with the voice rising at the question mark. Otherwise pairs retrieve how the story of Amara ends, using the Ending card.

#### 10 min. Reread story pages 4 and 5

Review: time words First, Next, Then and At last (unit 2, day 33). Read pages 4 and 5 of The Mark in the Stone aloud (39 and 51 words, 1 minute together). Pairs reread them turn about (2 minutes). Amara is a made-up character; Mary Anning is real. Then each pair marks three things on their copy with three colours (5 minutes): one action in red (brushed), one thought in blue ("A crack would not be so neat"), one feeling in green (grinned). Hear three pairs (2 minutes).

**Ask.** "Which word on page 5 tells what Amara felt?" Expect: grinned.

#### 12 min. Model one event and its closing

Say: "This case is made up for practice." Show the Noor picture: a child by a stream with a ridged pebble. Build one event on the board, one box at a time, thinking aloud (7 minutes):

- Time word and action: "First, Noor spotted the pebble and brushed it clean."
- A thought: "The ridges are in a neat row."
- A feeling: "She felt hopeful."
- A closing that fits: "In the end, Noor wrapped the pebble in a leaf and carried it home."

Say: "A closing fits when it finishes what happened." Then write a closing that does not fit, "A bird sat on a roof.", and ask: "Does this finish what Noor did?" Expect: no; it is about something else. Cross it out (2 minutes). Hold up the five-box event checklist and read the boxes: time word and action, a thought, a feeling, a closing that fits, a capital and an end mark. Partners hold it. Children say each box back (3 minutes). Say aloud: the thought and the feeling are Noor's, an invented child; in an account of a real person, we keep our own ideas labeled as ours.

#### 15 min. Write an event and a closing

Each child chooses one of three pictures: shell print, leaf print or fish in rock, and writes in a few words, one box at a time: a time word and an action, a thought, a feeling and a closing. A feeling list is on the board: glad, nervous, proud, curious. A child may draw first and say the words for you to scribe; scribe for any who need it. Walk among children, about 20 seconds each, and mark nothing yet. Watch for error P, a closing that does not fit what happened, and for error O, events with no time word.

Check: four boxes filled, a time word first, a closing that finishes the event.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Does the ending fit the event?" Turn: each child reads their event to a partner, who ticks the first four boxes (3 minutes). Pair: two pairs join and each says if one ending fits (2 minutes). Say: "Your ending fits because ___." Link: "You said ___; I would add ___." Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Hear today's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), and after each one child links or asks. Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

#### 5 min. Check the writing

Each child checks the fifth box: a capital at the start, an end mark on each sentence, and one spelling against the word wall or the cycle 8 dictionary page. Say: "Find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters." The child writes the correction above the word.

Check: a capital, an end mark and one spelling checked.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X45 aloud twice, then give it printed. Say: "Write one ending sentence that fits what happened." Children write; the six read theirs to you, about 20 seconds each, and the others read to a partner. Shown means a sentence that finishes the event, such as looking at the drawing again or writing a label. Shown with help means after one prompt: "What did the writer do last?" Not yet seen means an ending about something else (error P). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six word cards one at a time: mild, hold, kind, mind, post, ghost. Children say the chunk first, then the word. If a vowel is read short, put the chunk on the sound-spelling-map and blend again (error Y). Review ghost: star gh.

#### 15 min. Reread, sort and spell

Pairs reread Find the Gold, a page each, then again smoothly (6 minutes). Review: where's, it's and together are read whole. Sort the twelve cards under ild, old, ind and ost, saying each aloud (4 minutes). Then dictate two words, bold and host, and the sentence "A kind boy sold the gold." Say each twice; children write with tiles or pencil and check each against the sound-spelling-map (3 minutes). Each child then finds one of the words on the cycle 8 dictionary page and says which guide words it sits between (2 minutes).

Check: each word has the right chunk.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). All four lines are used. Each says the vowel sound in each word. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build bold, kind and post, then change one letter. Sort: the twelve cards under ild, old, ind and ost, a partner naming a word and the child pointing to its pile. Reread: pairs read Find the Gold twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child in a station reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which spelling did you check?" Expect: any word, and where they found it.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the Noor picture and the three pictures on the board or paper; fold a sheet into five boxes. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. One copy of the story: read pages 4 and 5 aloud twice and let pairs point to the three marks on the class copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw a small picture of something you found outdoors. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations, the sort and the oral card.

- **10 min. An event and an ending.** Look at a picture of something found outdoors. Your child says what happened, one thing they thought, one feeling, and an ending that finishes it.
  **Say.** "Does the ending fit what happened?" Expect: yes, because it finishes what the child did.
- **10 min. Read.** Show bold, mind, host and gold on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel's name.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say fold, kind and post. Your child writes each, then checks one in any dictionary.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X45]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: model one event, write only the time word, action and closing, and keep the exit and the reader. Longer: a child writes a second closing that does not fit, and a partner says why.

### Words and supports

Event, thought, feeling, closing, fits, checklist. Frames: "First ___." "I thought ___." "I felt ___." "In the end ___." "Your ending fits because ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing and drawing are answers. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 begins section 2.4. Today's exit (error P, an ending that does not fit) is repaired at its opening: you read an event and two endings and children choose the one that finishes it.
