# Fact and idea chart (day 43)

Two columns on folded paper. The left is headed **What the account says**; the right is headed **What a reader can imagine**. Under each heading, draw four lines. Pairs sort sentence strips cut from the account into the left column, and write one thing a reader can imagine in the right column, starting "I wonder" or "I imagine". The right column is always labeled as made up; nothing on it is given to Mary Anning. The teacher holds the wall chart.
