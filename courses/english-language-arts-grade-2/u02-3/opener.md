# Section 2.3 opener: a true account and a first event (days 43 to 45)

## What the section does

Day 43 sets facts beside imagination. Children read the true account of Mary Anning, chart what it says and what a reader can imagine, and meet an invented reader whose thoughts are labeled as made up. They learn that no source says what Mary felt. Day 44 is a fluency day: children read a short passage and a four-line poem, *Look Again*, with pauses at full stops and a rising voice for a question. Day 45 turns to writing: the teacher models one event and its closing from a picture card, and each child writes a time word and an action, a thought, a feeling and a closing, and checks them against a five-box card.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a repair, the day's work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block teaches the cycle 8 vowel chunks ild, old, ind and ost, with the reader *Find the Gold*, a 37-minute rotation and a close. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask. You hear the day's rotating six; the rest tell a partner.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A fact is something the source says.** A reader's thought is something a reader imagines. Say which is which every time.
- **Never give a real person a thought the record does not hold.** Mary Anning's account lists deeds and dates. We may imagine as readers, and we say so.
- **An event needs five parts.** A time word and an action, a thought, a feeling and a closing that fits.
- **Read for the listener.** Pause at a full stop and let the voice rise at a question mark.

## The errors, in plain words

- **AJ:** a feeling or thought given to a real person with no source. Cover the thought bubble, read the fact card, and ask who has the idea.
- **W:** rushing past the full stops. Model one line racing, then pausing; the child copies the pause.
- **K to P:** an event with nothing added, no action, no thought, no feeling, no time words or no closing. Add one card to the first sentence.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Writing is unscored here; record what each child wrote against the five boxes.
