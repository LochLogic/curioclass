# Cycle 8 oral card (days 43 to 47)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 43 only the first two lines are used. Children read the words and say each vowel; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 7 patterns (oi, oy, ou, ow).** oil, point, boy, toy, out, loud, house, cow
- **Cycle 8 patterns (ild, old, ind, ost).** bold, gold, hold, fold, kind, mind, post, ghost
- **A sentence.** The bold host held the gold.

Partner word card for day 47: bold, gold, hold, kind, mind, post, fold, ghost. Dictated words: behind, told.
