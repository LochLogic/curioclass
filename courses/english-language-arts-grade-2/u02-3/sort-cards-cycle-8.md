# Sort cards, cycle 8 (days 43 to 45)

Twelve cards, one word on each. Pairs sort them under four headings: **ild**, **old**, **ind**, **ost**. On day 45 each child then spells two words and a sentence you say, and checks one spelling in the beginning dictionary. The words child, wild, cold, find and most are held for the cycle 8 check and are not on these cards.

mild, gold, hold, fold, bold, sold, kind, mind, bind, post, host, ghost
