# Beginning dictionary page, cycle 8 (day 45)

One page for each pair, with nine entries in alphabetical order. Guide words at the top: **bold** and **post**. Children use it to check one spelling.

**bold**, adjective. Brave and not afraid to try.

**fold**, verb. To bend something over so one part lies on another.

**gold**, noun. A shiny yellow metal.

**hold**, verb. To keep something in your hands.

**host**, noun. A person who welcomes guests.

**kind**, adjective. Gentle and caring toward others.

**mild**, adjective. Not strong; gentle, as in mild weather.

**mind**, verb. To be careful about something.

**post**, noun. A tall piece of wood or metal standing in the ground.

Steps to say: find the first letter; find the entry; compare the letters with your spelling; correct it. "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
