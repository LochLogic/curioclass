# Day 30: Check: the finished school book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return each book sheet with the written feedback on the conclusion. Print the student page and a fresh book sheet for each child. Mark today's six on the reading record (the last group; a child absent on an earlier day is heard on day 32). Have day 29's exit marks beside you: error E is read at the start of the revision. Reading Card 5 stays at your corner.

### The day

#### 10 min. Independent revision

Each child reads the written feedback on the day 29 conclusion and revises it alone. No teaching. If a child asks what to write, point to the feedback line. For a child whose day 29 exit showed error E (an ending that adds a fact), read the one written sentence first: "Use only what the facts say." Collect nothing yet.

#### 50 min. Publish

25 minutes: the fair copy. Each child copies page 1 to page 4 onto the fresh book sheet: the topic, two fact pages with source tags and meanings, and the conclusion. They check each hard word on the dictionary page or the definitions page before they write it. 15 minutes: pictures and captions. 10 minutes: arrange the pages in order, write the title and "By" on the cover and fold. Then partners read the finished book to each other and point from each fact to its article, the ask of item s01-A32: "Read me your book, and show me which fact comes from which article." No reteaching; point to the step, not the word. Collect the books at the end.

After class, about 90 minutes, read each book once and mark four rows on the record sheet, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen: a topic that says what the whole book explains (error D); facts that come from two named articles (error E); a meaning for each hard word (error F); a conclusion that follows from the facts (error G). Shown with help means the row was met only after a feedback line was read aloud. A book that meets all four reads, for example: "Schools help children learn together. Pupils serve lunch (02-a). To cooperate is to work together (02-a). Pupils read for ten minutes each day (02-b). So schools help children learn together." A book with a fact and no meaning for its hard word is error F. Revision came first, so every book is eligible.

#### 15 min. Spelling and dictionary performance (foundations)

Unscored; no help. Side 1, 8 minutes. Read the Spelling Card aloud and do not show it: "The coach brings water for clay. We carry it to the art table." Then say: "The coach fills a pail." Say it twice more, slowly. Say (s01-A33): "Write the sentence." Children write on side 1. Mark pail by letter: Q for pal (the ai team not carried over). Side 2, 7 minutes. Give the page with the draft and three entries (s01-A34). Say: "Check the spelling in this draft with the page, and fix it." Mark by letter: R for a word copied from a partner without opening the page. Walk and watch each child find the entry, compare the letters and write the word.

#### 24 min. Reading conferences

The last six children, four minutes each, in your corner. Give Reading Card 5. One minute (s01-A35): "Why are you reading this, and why does the note go by the door?" Two minutes (s01-A36): "Read the first four sentences aloud, and then read them again." One minute (s01-A37): "Check the word vines from its letters and the sentence, and then reread the sentence." Give no word and no meaning. Unscored; no speed target. Mark the reading record. Across days 26 to 30 all 30 children are heard once; keep the other 24 records. The others reread The Slow Boat in pairs and spell oa and ow words with tiles.

#### 21 min. Independent matched reading

Each child reads The Slow Boat alone and rereads one page of their choice aloud, softly, to the desk. Float. Collect the student pages at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the draft sentence and the three entries on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No fresh book sheet: fold a plain sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The reading conference and the matched reading.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Say "The coach fills a pail." (s01-A33) Your child writes it. Then give a draft with seet for seat, and ask your child to check it on a page of three entries (s01-A34). Give no help.
- **20 min. Make the book.** Your child copies the four pages onto a folded sheet, with pictures and captions, and a cover.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the book aloud and points from each fact to its article.
  **Say.** "Which article does this fact come from?" Expect: the child points to the article.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: copy the topic page and one fact page, with the conclusion. Longer: add a title page picture and read the book to someone else.

### Words and supports

Publish, arrange, correct, entry. Frames: "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write tells you and you write. The spelling item may be answered with tiles.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 is the second check day. It begins by handing back day 30's assessment marks on the record sheet. Errors F and G on the book, and Q and R on the spelling and dictionary items, wait for day 32's fresh retry and its round four.
