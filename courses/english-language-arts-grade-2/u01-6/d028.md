# Day 28: Facts, meanings and pictures

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, a dictionary page and a definitions page for each pair. Mark today's six on the reading record (the third group) and for the talk. Have day 27's exit marks beside you: error E is the miss to repair. Reading Card 3 stays at your corner.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 27's exit (error E, a page tied to the wrong line of the card), read aloud an invented card: "Pupils keep aprons on hooks. Each hook has a name tag. Aprons go home on Friday." Say: "This is made up for practice." Show a page: "Aprons go home at the end of the week." Ask: "Which sentence of the card does this page come from?" Children point to the third and say why. Otherwise retrieve the topic statement: partners read page 1 to each other.

#### 4 min. Words

Two words. Review: in grade 1, day 102, you strengthened a piece of writing. To strengthen is to make something stronger: "I strengthen my page with a fact." Evidence is the line of the text that supports what you say: "My evidence is the line in the article." Review: you pointed to your evidence in kindergarten and grade 1. Partners say each word in a sentence about their book.

#### 8 min. Model

Show the invented book from day 27 (it is made up for practice): page 1, "Schools help children stay healthy." Write page 3: "Fact: Pupils drink water from a jug at the table. (source: Notes page B)." Then the meaning, in the frame: "A jug is a tall container with a handle, for pouring." Say that the meaning comes from the notes, in the same words. Review: a caption is the line under a picture that tells what it shows (day 22); on day 2 and in grade 1 you told what a picture shows that the words do not. Hold up the picture idea: a jug beside a cup. Ask: "What does the picture show that the words do not?" Expect: the cup, so we see the water is for drinking. Write the caption: "A jug and a cup wait on the table." Children read it with you.

#### 24 min. Write

Each child writes page 3 on the day 28 student page: a second fact and its source tag from the other article. Then the meaning of a hard word from either fact page, in "A ___ is ___.", taken from the definitions page or the article's words to know; the meaning is the article's, not a new one. Last, one picture with a caption. Walk and ask: "Show me where it says it." After class, write one line of feedback on each new page (error E for a fact with no line, error F for a hard word with no meaning). Do not score.

#### 8 min. Partner check

Partners swap pages. Each asks "Which line?" and "What does your picture show that your words do not?" and the author answers, pointing. Each partner takes four minutes. Both fix one thing.

Check: each fact points to its line, and the picture shows something the words do not.

#### 6 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "What does your picture show that your words do not?" Turn: partners tell each other (1 minute). Hear today's six, about 30 seconds each; after each, the next child links ("You said ___ ; I would add ___") or asks "Which part do you mean?". Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s01-X28 aloud twice. Ask: "Say the caption for a picture of a stencil, using the card." The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a caption that gives the card's meaning. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the card say a stencil is?" Not yet seen means a meaning the card contradicts (error E) or a caption with no meaning. Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Repair the commonest miss from yesterday's dictionary model. If many looked in the wrong place for two entries with the same first letter, write rain and road side by side: children say the second letter of each, then find the right entry. Otherwise repair the commonest spelling made by sound against the word wall, mapping the team.

#### 10 min. Dictionary guided practice

Pairs with the dictionary page. Say the four steps with the class: find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters, correct the draft. Three drafts, one at a time, in a sentence on side 2: "We walk on the rode." "I made a mape." "Put it in the baig." For each, the pair finds the entry and fixes the word, then explains: "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." Walk around and listen to each pair once. Do not tell a pair the word; point to the step.

#### 24 min. Reading conferences

Six children, four minutes each, in your corner. Give Reading Card 3. One minute: "Why are you reading this, and why does the note go by the sill?" Two minutes: "Read the first four sentences aloud, and then read them again." One minute: "Check the word pots from its letters and the sentence, and then reread the sentence." Give no word and no meaning. Unscored; no speed target; mark the reading record. The others reread The Slow Boat in pairs, spell slow, snow, show and grow with tiles, and sort word cards by oa or ow.

#### 18 min. Flexible groups

Two 9-minute groups of about six from the evidence of days 26 and 27, 90 seconds a child. Group one: a spelling made by sound: map the team, blend, spell again. Group two: oa or ow words missed in reading: map, blend, reread. The others rotate through reread and tiles.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner what their picture shows that their words do not.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the definitions page's seven entries on the board and have children draw the picture box on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: write the entries on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pairs, the conference and the groups.

- **10 min. Second fact.** Read a second article aloud. Your child tells you one fact and where it comes from, then writes it with its source tag.
  **Say.** "What does your picture show that your words do not?" Expect: a detail the words leave out.
- **10 min. Meaning and picture.** Your child writes "A ___ is ___" for a hard word, in the article's words, then draws a picture and a caption.
- **5 min. Dictionary.** Write mape on a sheet. Your child finds map on the page, compares the letters and fixes it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X28]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the fact and the meaning, and draw the picture without a caption. Longer: add a third fact page from a third article, with its source.

### Words and supports

Strengthen, evidence, caption, entry. Say: "A ___ is ___." and "My picture shows ___, so ___." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write tells you and you write.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 returns the written feedback on days 27 and 28 and writes the conclusion. Error E on today's exit (a caption that contradicts the card) is repaired at day 29's opening.
