# Definitions page (days 28 to 30)

A second page of entries for the words a book may define, taken from the articles' own words to know.

**caption**, noun. Words under a picture that tell what it shows.

**cooperate**, verb. To work together to get something done.

**diagram**, noun. A drawing that explains something with labels.

**library**, noun. A place where people borrow books and read.

**schedule**, noun. A plan that tells what happens and when.

**shift**, noun. The part of the day when a group of pupils is at school.

**volunteer**, noun. A person who helps without being paid.
