# Exit cards, days 27 to 29

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. You read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 27 (s01-X27).** "Pupils use a whiteboard to practise letter shapes. They wipe it clean before writing another letter. Each pupil has a marker on a string." Ask: "Say one fact page for our book from this card, in your own words, then say the card sentence it comes from."
- **Day 28 (s01-X28).** "Pupils use a stencil to trace letters. A stencil is a thin sheet with a letter shape cut out of it." Ask: "Say the caption for a picture of a stencil, using the card."
- **Day 29 (s01-X29).** Draft: "Pupils stand wet umbrellas open by the door. Drips fall onto a cloth on the floor." Printed peer suggestion: "Explain what the cloth helps." Ask: "Say an ending that follows from the draft."
