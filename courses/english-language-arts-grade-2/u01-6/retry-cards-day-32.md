# Day 32 retry cards, by recorded letter

Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" on every card. Give the card of the child's recorded letter only; do not give every card to every child. A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark.

- **A, B, C, N, O, P (s01-X32v2).** No card: reread aloud twice the 02-a paragraph that begins "Pupils also clean." Ask: "Tell me how pupils clean their school." For O, the child rereads the paragraph aloud in phrases. For P, the child decodes "clean" from its letters and rereads the sentence to check it.
- **D, E, F, G (s01-X32d).** "Pupils hold notices on the board with magnets. A magnet is a small piece of metal that sticks to iron. Each notice has its own magnet." Ask by letter: D, a first sentence; E, a fact; F, what a magnet is; G, an ending.
- **H, I, J, K (s01-X32h).** You say aloud: "The class keeps the spare keys in that cupboard." Ask: "Wait for the speaker to finish, then say what you would ask." When asked which cupboard, the speaker says: "The tall cupboard by the window."
- **L, M (s01-X32l).** Two labelled pictures: a sheep labelled "sheep" and a ship labelled "ship". Ask: "Read the word with the long e sound."
- **Q, R (s01-X32q).** For R: the draft "A gote eats grass." and the entry "goat, noun. An animal with hooves and often horns." Ask: "Check the spelling of the draft with the entry and write the word correctly." For Q: say "Write this sentence: The soap is on the sink."
