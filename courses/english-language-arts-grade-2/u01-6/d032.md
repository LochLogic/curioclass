# Day 32: Respond: fix and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill the day 32 group record from your record sheet, the day 26 discussion and reading records and the day 29 to 31 marks: each child's letters, one round and one retry card. Put the retry cards, the sort cards and Reading Cards 1 to 5 where you can reach them. Count the children not yet heard in a reading conference.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back day 31's assessment marks and day 30's assessment marks together. Give the class one result by letter, in words: for example, "Many of us gave a meaning the passage does not give (error A)." Put the week 6 words on the wall: respond, clarify, support, define, conclude, revise, evidence and pattern. Review: you met these words earlier in the unit. Partners say each once, in a sentence, while they work.

#### 40 min. Four 10-minute teacher groups

Each round, seat up to six children with you, 90 seconds each. A child with letters in two rounds goes to the round with more of them; the rest join the partners. Round one, errors O, P and day 26's N (reading): each child rereads the card they read in their own conference. For O, mark a slash at each full stop and read in phrases. For P, read the letters of each word first, then check with the picture. For N, say in one sentence what the card is for. Round two, errors L and M (vowels): map the whole team to one sound, then blend: need, toad, slow, night, tried. Round three, errors H to K (talk), with error J from day 25's exit (s01-X25): rehearse the wait signal, a link ("You said ___ ; I would add ___") and "Which part do you mean?" on an invented remark, "She put it in that box by the door." Round four, errors Q and R: for Q, write tail and coat with the ai and oa teams from the wall; for R, find the entry on the dictionary page and correct the drafts "rowd" and "tay".

The others work as partners. For A, B, C and day 31's N (meaning): reread the passage or the page, say what the text says, and repair one sentence of their answer. For D, E, F, G (writing): reread their book page, repair one sentence, and add the missing meaning, source or ending. Walk between groups; do not teach the whole class.

#### 15 min. Fresh retry

Unscored. Give each child with a recorded letter the card for that letter only, and ask its one ask; each child takes about 40 seconds with you, up to 20 children. A child not reached gives the answer to a partner with the same card, unmarked. For each card, say: "This card is made up for practice." A, B, C, N, O, P (s01-X32v2): reread the 02-a cleaning paragraph and ask its question. D, E, F, G (s01-X32d): the ask by letter on the card. H, I, J, K (s01-X32h): "Wait for the speaker to finish, then say what you would ask." L, M (s01-X32l): "Read the word with the long e sound." Q, R (s01-X32q): the writing or entry ask on the card. A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. Mark the group record.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Each child finds ee, oa, ow, igh and ie on the map and says one word of their own for each. If a word does not match its team, find the team that does.

#### 15 min. Pattern retrieval

Pairs sort the pattern sort cards by team (8 minutes). The class reads each group aloud (3 minutes). Then say green and foam; children write each, map the team and check the wall (4 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Reading conferences for any child not yet heard, four minutes each, on a Reading Card that child has not seen: the same three asks as day 26. The others rotate through three stations, tiles (spell road, coat, glow and show), reread (The Slow Boat, a page each) and sort. Mark the heard column. Collect the student pages at the end.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner what a card or a group helped them fix.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the sort words on torn paper and the retry cards by hand. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: say each aloud. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups, the stations and the conference.

- **10 min. Reread.** Your child rereads one page of their book and says what they would fix.
  **Say.** "What would you fix, and why?" Expect: one change that matches a source.
- **15 min. Fix one thing.** Your child makes the change, then writes two long vowel words with different teams.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say green and foam; your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask the first that fits what your child found hard: [[s01-X32v2]] [[s01-X32d]] [[s01-X32h]] [[s01-X32l]] [[s01-X32q]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two rounds, for the two letters with the most children. Longer: a pair rereads the whole book and swaps one question.

### Words and supports

Respond, clarify, support, define, conclude, revise, evidence, pattern. Frames: "You said ___ ; I would add ___ ." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer. A child may pass a turn.

### Tomorrow

This is the last day of unit 1. Day 33 opens unit 2: the retry results go to its opening. The finished books stay in class.
