# Dictionary page for teaching (days 27 to 30)

One page for each pair, with six entries in alphabetical order. Day 27 models three; day 28 guides three draft spellings; day 29 is independent. The page used for the check on day 30 is a different page.

**bag**, noun. A soft holder for carrying things.

**light**, noun. What lets us see in the dark.

**map**, noun. A drawing that shows where places are.

**rain**, noun. Water that falls from clouds in drops.

**road**, noun. A hard path where cars and buses go.

**tray**, noun. A flat piece with edges, for carrying things.

Steps to say: find the first letter; find the entry; compare the letters with the draft; correct the draft. "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
