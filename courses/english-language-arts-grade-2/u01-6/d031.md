# Day 31: Check: reading about school

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page and both cold texts for each child. Keep 02-f covered. Have day 30's marks beside you.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions and access

Hand back day 30's assessment marks on the record sheet. Read the directions aloud: nothing is taught; drawing, pointing or a few words all count. Read any stem again on request.

#### 8 min. SL.2.2 on 02-f

Read "The Welcome Card" once aloud, text covered. Say (s01-A45): "Tell your partner the key ideas of what you heard." Partners tell each other (3 minutes). Then each fills the three boxes on side 1 with words or drawings (5 minutes).

#### 15 min. RI.2.1 on 02-e

Children read "The Return Shelf" with its diagram (3 minutes). Say (s01-A38): "Ask one question that this text can answer." They write it (3 minutes). Then say each stem once and children write a few words: 1 "Who uses the return shelf?" 2 "What does the shelf hold?" 3 "Where is the label?" 4 "When are books returned?" 5 "Why do the covers face out?" 6 "How is a missing title card replaced?" (s01-A39 to s01-A44; 1 minute and a half each).

#### 7 min. RI.2.7 on the 02-e diagram

Say (s01-A46): "Tell me what the arrow in the diagram helps us understand." Children write or draw on side 2.

#### 8 min. RL.2.3 on 01-check31

Children read "The Paper Tower" with its panels (3 minutes). Say (s01-A47): "Describe how Nia responds when the first tower falls." They write or draw (5 minutes).

#### 7 min. RL.2.7 on the two panels

Say (s01-A48): "Use the words and both pictures to explain what changed." Children write one line for the words and one for the second picture.

#### 10 min. Sharing and reflection

Partners swap books. Each reads one fact page aloud and names its source (3 minutes each). Each writes one improvement the sources support (4 minutes). Collect the student pages and books.

Check: the source said matches the fact page.

#### 5 min. Opening retrieval (foundations)

Each child finds oa and ow on the map and says one word for each. No reteaching.

#### 20 min. Flexible group one

About six children with you, cycle 5 only: map, blend, read the matching decodable, spell, 3 minutes each. The others reread and sort.

#### 20 min. Flexible group two

The same for the second six. The others reread, use tiles and sort.

#### 12 min. Paired reread

Partners reread The Slow Boat for expression, a page each.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one thing they did well today.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the diagram and panels by hand. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed text: read each aloud and draw the picture. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The sharing and the groups.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Read a short text aloud and ask your child to tell you its key ideas (s01-A45). Then give 02-e with its diagram: ask a question and answer six (s01-A38 to s01-A44), and say what the arrow shows (s01-A46). Give 01-check31: ask how Nia responds and what changed (s01-A47, s01-A48). Give no help.
  **Say.** "Tell me what you heard." Expect: three ideas in order.
- **15 min. Share.** Your child reads one fact page of their book to you and says its source.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the six stems after the first three. Longer: a child asks you one more question about 02-e.

### Words and supports

Question words, character, response, diagram. Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write tells you and you write.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 hands back day 31's assessment marks. Errors A, B, C and N go to its partners and retry cards.
