# Book pages (days 27 to 31)

One sheet folded in half and again, so that it makes a cover and four pages. Each child makes one.

**Cover.** Title: ______________________  By: ______________________

**Page 1, topic.** Schools help children ______.

**Page 2, first fact.** Fact: ______ (source: ______). A ______ is ______. Picture and caption.

**Page 3, second fact.** Fact: ______ (source: ______). A ______ is ______. Picture and caption.

**Page 4, conclusion.** So ______.

Each fact comes from one of two articles the child names. No outside fact goes in the book. A child who cannot write dictates to you or an approved scribe.
