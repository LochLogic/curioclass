# Day 27: Drafting our school book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a book sheet and the student page for each child and a dictionary page for each pair. Put the three printed articles and each child's notes sheet out. Mark today's six on the reading record (the second group of six) and for the talk. Have day 25's cycle 5 check marks and day 26's discussion record beside you. Reading Card 2 stays at your corner.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 26's assessment: write each child's letters (H to K for talk, N to P for reading) on the day 32 group record. There is no repair slot today; those letters wait for day 32's groups, and so does error J from day 25's exit. Then retrieve the notes sheet: partners read each other one fact and its source tag.

#### 4 min. Words

Four words, each in a sentence. To arrange is to put things in order: "I arrange my pages in order." To consult is to look in a book to find out: "I consult the dictionary page." To correct is to fix a mistake: "I correct my spelling." A source is the article a fact comes from. Review: you wrote a source tag after each fact in grade 1 and again on day 16. Partners say each word in a sentence about the book.

#### 8 min. Model

Show the book question and say it: "Explain what schools help children do, using facts and definitions from two articles." Review: you met it on day 22. Hold up a page of notes and say: "This is made up for practice." Its fact: "Pupils wash their hands before lunch." Its source tag: Notes page A. Write page 1: "Schools help children stay healthy." Then write "Schools are fun" beside it and ask which one says what schools help children do. The first does; the second is error D, a topic that explains nothing. Write page 2: "Fact: Pupils wash their hands before lunch. (source: Notes page A)." Think aloud: the fact comes from the notes, not from my head, and the tag says where. Children read each page with you.

#### 6 min. A second article

Pairs swap: each child names the article their partner chose on day 24, rereads one part of it with its words to know, and adds one row to the notes sheet, a fact and its source tag. The book then has facts from two articles. Walk and ask: "Show me where it says it."

#### 20 min. Draft

Each child folds a book sheet and keeps it. On the day 27 student page each writes page 1, the topic page, and page 2, the first fact page, with a source tag and a picture idea. The fact comes from the notes sheet; no outside fact goes in. A child who cannot write tells you and you write, adding no words. After class, write one line of written feedback on each page: the topic (error D) and the facts with their tags (error E). Do not score.

#### 8 min. Partner source check

Partners swap pages for a partner check, four minutes each. The partner finds the fact's line in the article and says "found" or "which line?". The author points to it. Then both fix a tag if it is wrong.

#### 4 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which line in the article does this fact come from?" Turn: partners tell each other (1 minute). Hear today's six, about 20 seconds each; after each, the next child links ("You said ___ ; I would add ___") or asks "Which part do you mean?". Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s01-X27 aloud twice. Ask: "Say one fact page for our book from this card, in your own words, then say the card sentence it comes from." The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a fact page in the child's words and the card sentence it comes from. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "Listen to the card again. Which sentence says it?" Not yet seen means a page the card does not support (error E), or none. Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Many children spelled day 25's check words by sound (error Q: tost, croke, thro, bol). Write the four on the board. For each ask: "Which word on the wall sounds like the end of it?" Children find road, coat, snow and show, see oa and ow, and mend each word to toast, croak, throw and bowl. Children write the four again and map the team under each.

#### 10 min. Dictionary teaching

New today. An entry is one word and its meaning on a dictionary page, in alphabetical order. Give each pair a page (bag, light, map, rain, road, tray). To consult it is four steps: find the first letter; find the entry; compare its letters with the draft; correct the draft. Model three, children following with a finger. Draft one: "lite" for the word in "The light is on." Find l, find light, compare: the entry has igh. Draft two: "rane". Two entries start with r, so look at the second letter: a, then rain. Draft three: "trai". Find t, find tray, compare the ending. After each, the class says: "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." Last, say that copying a neighbour's spelling is not consulting (error R); children say why the page is the better check.

#### 24 min. Reading conferences

Six children, four minutes each, in your corner. Give Reading Card 2. One minute: "Why are you reading this, and why does the note go on the bin?" Two minutes: "Read the first four sentences aloud, and then read them again." One minute: "Check the word rows from its letters and the sentence, and then reread the sentence." Give no word and no meaning. Unscored; no speed target; mark the reading record. The others reread The Slow Boat in pairs, spell road, coat, toad and glow with tiles, and sort word cards by oa or ow.

#### 18 min. Flexible groups

Two 9-minute groups of about six, from the cycle 5 evidence, 90 seconds a child. Group one: a spelling made by sound (error Q): map the team, blend, spell again. Group two: oa or ow words missed in reading: map, blend, reread The Slow Boat. The others rotate through reread and tiles.

#### 3 min. Close

Each child tells a partner which line their fact comes from.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the dictionary page's six entries on the board and copy them on index cards. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed page: write the six entries on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The pairs, the conference and the groups.

- **10 min. Read and choose.** Read one article from School Days aloud. Your child picks one fact and tells you where it comes from.
  **Say.** "Which line says it?" Expect: your child reads or points to the line.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes "Schools help children ___" and one fact with its source tag.
- **5 min. Dictionary.** Write rane on a sheet. Your child finds rain on the six entries, compares the letters and fixes it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X27]] Read the whiteboard card twice. Your child says one fact page from it and the card sentence it comes from.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second article row and write one fact page. Longer: a pair checks both fact lines against the article.

### Words and supports

Arrange, consult, correct, source, entry. Say: "The article says ___." and "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." Pointing is an answer. A child who finds the page hard works with a partner who reads the entries aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 adds the second fact page, a meaning and a picture. Error E on today's exit (a page tied to the wrong line of the card) is repaired at day 28's opening. Written feedback on today's pages goes back on day 29.
