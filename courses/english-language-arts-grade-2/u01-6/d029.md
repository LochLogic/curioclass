# Day 29: The conclusion and a stronger book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return each child's book sheet with the written feedback on days 27 and 28. Print the student page and a suggestion strip for each child. Mark today's six on the reading record (the fourth group). Have day 28's exit marks beside you: error E is the miss to repair. Put Reading Card 4 at your corner and the four picture cards where you can reach them.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 28's exit (error E, a caption whose meaning contradicts the card), read aloud an invented card: "Pupils hang bags on a rail. A rail is a long bar to hang things on." Say: "This is made up for practice." Say a made-up caption: "A rail: a soft cushion." Ask: "Where does the card say that?" Children find that it does not, point to the card line that gives the meaning, and say the caption again. Otherwise retrieve a caption: partners say the caption of their own picture.

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back day 27's and day 28's feedback together. Each child reads their lines, or you read them aloud to a child who needs it. Each child puts a tick by one line they will use today.

#### 10 min. Model the conclusion

Show the invented book from days 27 and 28 (made up for practice): page 1, "Schools help children stay healthy"; the hand-washing fact; the jug fact. Review: on day 17 you learned that a conclusion follows from the facts and goes back to the topic. Ask: "What do these two facts show?" Expect: that schools help children stay healthy. Write page 4: "So schools help children stay healthy." Check it with the class: it uses no new fact, and it matches page 1. Then show a peer suggestion strip. Review: in grade 1 you used peer question strips with a partner. This strip says: "Explain why washing helps." Say that a suggestion is about the book, not the child. Change page 2 by adding a line from the notes: "Clean hands help stop sickness from spreading. (source: Notes page A)." Children say what changed.

#### 14 min. Write

Each child writes page 4, the conclusion, on the day 29 student page: "So ___." It says what the facts show and goes back to the topic. No new fact goes in. A child who cannot write tells you and you write. After class, write one line of feedback on each conclusion (error G for a book that stops after the last fact). Do not score.

#### 8 min. Peer suggestions

Partners swap books. Minutes one to four: the first partner reads the other's book and writes a strip, "Explain why ___", while the author rereads their own book quietly. Minutes five to eight: swap roles. The strip asks about the book and names a page.

Check: the strip asks one "Explain why" question and names a page.

#### 13 min. Revise

Each child reads their feedback and their strip, picks one, and changes one thing in the book: adds a line from an article, fixes a tag or a meaning, or rewrites the conclusion. On the student page they write what they changed, a few words or a drawing. Check one fact line with a partner when a fact is added. Give no teaching.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the draft of item s01-X29 aloud twice and show its printed suggestion. Ask: "Say an ending that follows from the draft." The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means an ending built only from the draft's lines. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the draft say about the drips?" Not yet seen means an ending that adds a fact the draft does not give (error E), or none. Mark the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Pattern transfer (foundations)

Written and unscored, to see each child alone (items s01-A26 to s01-A28). Give side 2. Five minutes: hold up the kit and kite cards, each labelled, and say: "Mark the label whose word has a long vowel sound, and the label whose word has a short vowel sound." Do the same with hop and hope. Children circle the long word and underline the short one on side 2. Five minutes: children read the passage silently. Say these ten words one at a time, with a number: 1 loaf, 2 snail, 3 sigh, 4 weed, 5 crow, 6 clay, 7 shy, 8 bead, 9 tie, 10 silly. Say: "Match each of these ten words to its sound label." Children write the number on the line of its label. Give no help. Collect the sheets. Mark each by letter on the record sheet: L for one vowel sound given to a pair, M for a team read with a short vowel.

#### 24 min. Reading conferences

Six children, four minutes each, in your corner. Give Reading Card 4. One minute (s01-A29): "Why are you reading this, and why does the note go on the shed door?" Two minutes (s01-A30): "Read the first four sentences aloud, and then read them again." One minute (s01-A31): "Check the word tools from its letters and the sentence, and then reread the sentence." Give no word and no meaning. Unscored; no speed target. Mark the reading record: purpose, rereading, correction. The others reread The Slow Boat and spell road, coat and toad with tiles.

#### 5 min. Dictionary independent practice

Each child picks the hard word they defined on day 28, finds its entry on the definitions page or the dictionary page, compares the letters with their own spelling and corrects it if it differs. They say: "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." or "I checked the page and it was right."

#### 21 min. Matched reading

Partners read The Slow Boat, a page each, three times, resting at the full stops. Then each writes two oa or ow words from the wall in a short sentence. Float and listen; spell with children who ask, by mapping the team.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four picture cards on paper and write the sound labels on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strip: write one line on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The conference, the matched reading and the pattern page.

- **10 min. Conclusion.** Your child reads their book and tells you what the facts show. They write "So ___."
  **Say.** "What do the facts show?" Expect: a sentence that goes back to the topic.
- **15 min. Suggestion.** You write one strip, "Explain why ___", about their book. They change one thing.
- **5 min. Dictionary.** Your child checks one hard word on the definitions page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X29]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the conclusion and skip the strip. Longer: a pair reads each other's conclusions and says which fact each follows from.

### Words and supports

Conclusion, suggestion, strengthen, pattern. Say: "So ___." and "Explain why ___." A child who cannot write tells you and you write.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 is a check day. In its first 10 minutes each child revises the conclusion alone from your written feedback. Error E on today's exit (an ending that adds a fact) is read as one written sentence at the start of that revision. Errors L and M on the pattern transfer wait for day 32's round two.
