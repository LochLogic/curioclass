# Section 3.8 opener: sharing the paragraph and repairing (days 102 and 103)

## What the section does

Day 102 returns the written feedback, lets each child correct one thing and finish a final copy, and then children share the paragraph in fives and say which card answered which part. A shared keyboard station lets fifteen pairs type one sentence of a class paragraph. Day 103 sorts the children by their commonest error letter into four repair tables and ends with a fresh source line answered on paper.

## How the days run

Day 102 closes the cycle 17 foundations check with a written fifteen minutes. Day 103 begins cycle 18 (-ible and -able) and starts the day with a short spoken repair. The repair tables work from named tasks while the teacher takes four turns of about nine minutes each.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A listener who has not read the paragraph** is the best test of whether it explains.
- **Repair is by pattern.** A child goes to the table named for the child's commonest letter.
- **A fresh source gets a fresh answer.** It is not the class paragraph again.
- **The keyboard is a station,** two minutes a pair, with an adult beside it.

## The errors, in plain words

- A fact taken from the wrong card, or from neither (AE, E).
- A fact stated with no link to the next flower (B), or one the source does not give (C).
- A word copied from a neighbor or kept after a look at the entry (J, Y).
- An ending ending the paragraph with no why (G).
