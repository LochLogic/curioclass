# Fresh source strip, day 103

Read aloud by the teacher, then handed to each pair.

Question: Where does the dust stick on the hummingbird?

Source line: A hummingbird hovers at a red flower. It dips its long bill inside. Yellow dust sticks to the feathers on its head.

The child draws a line under the words that answer the question and draws or writes where the dust is. The words and the drawing are kept.
