# Day 103: Repair the gaps and answer a new source question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the records, paragraphs and slips of days 99 to 101 into four piles by each child's commonest error letter. Skim the day 102 check sheets and write the commonest miss on the board beside the right form. Mark today's six, group 1 of five, children 1 to 6. Put a task card at each of four tables.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hand-back

Return the folders: the paragraph, slips and record sheet from days 99 to 101, with day 101's edited paragraph on top (2 minutes). Each child learns the table named for their commonest letter and moves there (3 minutes). Sources: C, D, E, AD, AE. Writing: F, G, H, I, AF, B. Talk: K, L, M, N. Spelling: J, Y, AN. A child with no letter goes to the writing table for the extension.

#### 38 min. Repair in groups

Review: a wrong example beside a right one, then a new try (kindergarten, unit 1, day 6). Four turns of yours, largest group first, while the other tables work the task on their card; a child who finishes writes a second sentence that links a second card to the first. No child sits through a repair they do not need.

Sources, 10 minutes. Error C: a fact the card does not give. Show "The butterfly carries pollen in its wings" beside "on its tongue"; the child puts a finger on the card line, reads it and rewrites the fact. Error D: a copied slip beside one with the child's own line; each finds a different fact on the card and points to it. Error E: "A butterfly has wings" beside "A butterfly's tongue touches the pollen"; match each fact to the question on the board. Error AD: give the cards unmarked and the child finds the line. Error AE: trace a fact to its sentence and read the tag. Review: naming the card a fact came from (grade 2, unit 3, day 99).

Writing, 10 minutes. Error F: say what the paragraph explains, then write it first. Error G: a paragraph that stops beside "So both help flowers"; add an ending. Error H: "Pollen" unexplained beside "Pollen is the yellow powder flowers make"; choose the word a reader needs and explain it. Error I: read the feedback line and change one idea; a recopy is not a change. Error AF: join the defined word to the fact it helps explain. Error B: "A bat has a long tongue. A bat is brown." beside "A bat has a long tongue, so it reaches the nectar"; the child joins two of their own facts with so or because. Review: the because link (kindergarten, unit 3, day 82).

Talk, 5 minutes. A turn card in hand. Error K: rehearse waiting for the card. Error L: "You said ___; I add ___." Error M: ask "Which part do you mean?" Error N: say the partner's question back, then answer. Review: the talk card (grade 2, unit 1, day 25). Turns are counted: six children at 45 seconds is under 5 minutes; if more come, hear six and the rest rehearse in pairs.

Spelling, 5 minutes. Error J: a neighbour's spelling beside one found in the entry; find the entry first, then compare letters, with "bunch". Error Y: "fech" beside "fetch"; write the whole ending beside the base. Error AN: "softre" beside "softer"; say the letters in order and write them.

Second pass, 8 minutes: the group that has a second letter at another table meets you there, the largest first.

#### 10 min. Fresh source

Give each child a strip. Read the question and the line aloud twice (1 minute). Each child draws a line under the words that tell where the dust sticks (3 minutes), then draws the dust on the hummingbird in the box on the page (5 minutes). Collect the strip and the page (1 minute). It is a different case from the exit; it is scored as retried, shown with help.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show the question and the line, read aloud (1 minute): [[s03-X103]]. All tell a partner one sentence (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag a sentence that stops at the sticking error B, a fact the line does not give error C.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children change blocks and take the student page.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write the commonest miss of the day 102 check on the board beside the right form. Children name the letters that differ (2 minutes), then write the right form from memory, cover it and check it letter by letter (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Cycle 18 pattern

Review: an ending changes what a word means (grade 1, unit 2, day 61). Model (3 minutes): write play | able and say "able to be played"; then break | able. Write flex | ible and vis | ible. The endings sound the same, so you cannot hear which one; look, and check the entry when unsure. Map and blend (3 minutes): playable, breakable, flexible, visible, fixable, readable; children say the parts, then the word. Reader (5 minutes): read page 1 aloud; pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4, a sentence each, then say who made the label. Cover the pictures until after reading. Spell five (4 minutes): playable, flexible, readable, fixable, visible; the word, a short sentence, the word again.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the cycle 18 oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: playable, breakable, flexible, visible. Sort: six slips by -able or -ible. Reread: the reader for accuracy. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What does -able mean at the end of a word?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the strip and cards by hand. A child who cannot write dictates to a scribe; pointing to the words is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: copy the hummingbird line on paper. No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The tables and the oral card.

- **10 min. Fix one thing.** Read your child's paragraph aloud.
  **Say.** "What is this paragraph about?" Expect: how pollinators carry pollen. Your child adds the missing topic sentence or ending.
- **10 min. Words.** Say playable and flexible. Your child writes each and underlines the ending.
  **Say.** "What does -able mean?" Expect: able to be.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the label reader aloud.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X103]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second pass; keep the repair, the strip and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second sentence that links a second source to the first.

### Words and supports

Repair, table, source, strip, label, flexible. Frames: "The line says ___, so ___." "I will fix ___." A child may say the sentence first and then write it.

### Tomorrow

Day 104 reads the bee-view story and retrieves coherent speaking with descriptive details. Today's exit tells you who joins the sticking to the landing in one sentence. The cycle 18 reader comes back for rereading.
