# Table task cards for the repair groups, day 103

Four cards, one for each repair table. The tasks are named; the teacher takes one table at a time.

- **Sources table.** Reread your two slips. Draw a line under each fact. Write which card it came from.
- **Writing table.** Point to your topic sentence. Point to your ending. Write what is missing.
- **Talk table.** Take turns. One says a fact, the next links to it, the next asks to hear one part again.
- **Spelling table.** Find your word in the entry. Cover it. Write it. Check each letter.
