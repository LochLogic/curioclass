# Day 102: Share the paragraph and say which card told each part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Open the class page on the keyboard under the class question. Cut slips marked B (Bee Visit) and F (Butterfly Visit). Mark today's six, group 5 of five, children 25 to 30, one in each share group. Write the three station tasks on the board.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and correct

Hand back day 101's assessment, the edited paragraphs with the written feedback, and day 98's definition slips (2 minutes). Review: written feedback by row (grade 2, unit 3, day 100). Each child points to the word they defined, says the word it explains and says what the next fact does (3 minutes): the fix for a definition that stood alone, error AF. Each child then finds the one spelling you circled, finds its entry first and compares the letters one at a time (4 minutes), error J. Review: checking a spelling in an entry (grade 2, unit 1, day 27). Last minute: each child starts the final copy. Corrections are offered here and scored on day 103.

#### 30 min. Publishing stations

The shared keyboard is new today. Fifteen pairs come in seating order, 2 minutes a pair with the move included, so a pair types for about a minute and a half. The next pair stands ready behind. The pair picks one sentence from either paragraph that names its card, eight words at most. One child points, the other types, and they swap if time allows. An adult sits beside the keyboard, either another adult or you; the rest of the room works alone from the board. The adult writes one line of feedback for the pair, kept for day 105.

The other pairs do three tasks in this order, 28 minutes in all. Copy the final paragraph in pencil (12 minutes). Review: final copy and pictures with captions (grade 2, unit 1, day 30). Draw one fact and label it (10 minutes). Read the paragraph aloud to a partner, twice (6 minutes). A pair that returns from the keyboard picks up where it stopped.

#### 12 min. Share in fives

Six groups of five, a talk card and a tally child in each. Review: the talk card and tally child (grade 2, unit 1, day 25). Share in fives is new today. Each reader reads the paragraph aloud while the four listeners write on a slip the letter of the card each fact came from: B, F, or a question mark when it came from neither. Then one listener links ("You said ___; I add ___.") or asks to hear a part again, and the reader answers. Review: asking to hear a part again (grade 2, unit 3, day 99).

Turns are counted. Two-minute slots, moving included: about 80 seconds to read, 40 seconds to link or ask, five readers, so ten minutes. Slot 6 is for the group to compare slips with the reader. Your visit: sit with group 1 in slot 1, group 2 in slot 2, on to group 6 in slot 6. Each group puts its child of the day in the slot you arrive; group 6's child reads last. Ask the group: "Which card did that part come from?" Mark the child of the day: names the card for each fact (shown, shown with help, not yet seen), in the heard column.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X102]] and [[s03-X102b]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, both asks, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag a fact taken from the card that does not answer the question error AE, a fact from neither card error E.

#### 3 min. Transitions

Children change blocks, put away paragraphs and take the student page and a pencil.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Model on the board (2 minutes): the draft "mutch" and the entry "much, a lot". Finger under the entry, say it, then compare letters one by one: the draft has an extra t. A few common words, such as much and such, do not follow the rule that -tch comes after a short vowel, so the rule is not enough: the entry decides (error J). Pairs (3 minutes) correct "sich" against the entry "such, of this kind": find it, compare, write.

#### 15 min. Cycle 17 written check

Children use the check sheet on the student page. Say the card is invented and that you will not help with the words. Spelling (5 minutes): say each as the card says, the word, the sentence, the word. [[s03-A39]] [[s03-A40]] Reading (5 minutes): read the hen card aloud once, then children read the printed card and write a few words (2 minutes): [[s03-A41]]. Ask the two meaning questions one at a time; children write a few words for each (3 minutes): [[s03-A43]] [[s03-A44]]. Reference (5 minutes): children find the entry, compare, correct the draft and mark what changed: [[s03-A42]]. A child who asks has the card read aloud again after a first try (shown with help); a spoken spelling is not accepted; extra time stays inside the part. Collect. Scoring is on day 104.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. On the card, read patch for catch. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: fetch, bunch, softer, softest. Sort: the day 99 slips. Reread: each child reads the reader alone. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which ending means the most of all?" Children tell a partner, then say it together. Review: -er and -est (grade 1, unit 2, day 61).

### Other rooms

No keyboard: write the class page on chart paper, a sentence a pair, by hand. No printer: copy the check sheet by hand. A child who cannot write dictates to a scribe for the share; pointing to the card is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: letters on torn paper. No cards: say the page the fact came from.
**Skip.** The keyboard, the groups and the oral card.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child reads their paragraph aloud. Write B or F beside each fact you hear.
  **Say.** "Which card told that part?" Expect: the card named in the paragraph.
- **10 min. Words.** Say fetch and softest. Your child writes each and underlines the ending.
- **10 min. Fix a word.** Write "sich" and "such, of this kind". Your child finds the entry first, then writes the word.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X102]] [[s03-X102b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the drawing task; keep the share, the check and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second sentence that links a second card to the first.

### Words and supports

Share, listener, link, card, keyboard, beetle. Frames: "That part came from card ___." "You said ___; I add ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the card instead of naming it.

### Tomorrow

Day 103 sorts the class into four tables by the commonest letter on the record sheets. A child who named the wrong card (error AE) or a fact from neither (error E) goes to the sources table. It opens with the commonest miss of today's check beside the right form, and starts the next cycle.
