# Exit cards, days 102 and 103

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 102 (s03-X102).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Card X says: a beetle walks on a flower and pollen sticks to its body. Card Y says: the beetle walks to a new flower and the pollen falls off there." The question on the board: "Where does the pollen end up?" Say: "Say which card answered the question."
- **Day 102 (s03-X102b).** Same cards and question. Say: "Say what that card told."
- **Day 103 (s03-X103).** This card is invented for practice. Show the question: "How does a moth get pollen to the next flower?" Show the fresh source line: "A moth rests on a pale flower. Pollen sticks to its body. When it rests on the next flower, some of the pollen rubs off." Say: "Say one sentence that answers the question, using the line."
