# Exit cards, days 117 and 118

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 117 (s03-X117).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A butterfly story. First I landed on a leaf. Then a drop of water fell on my wing." Say: "Say the next line of the story so it tells what the butterfly feels."
- **Day 118 (s03-X118).** This card is invented for practice. Say: "Hey, wait till you hear this!" Say: "Say that line again so it suits a class visitor you do not know."
- **Day 118 (s03-X118b).** Same line. Say: "Say what you added for the visitor."
