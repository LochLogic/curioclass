# Day 118: Finish the stories and compare ways of speaking

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the last ten on the changeover list. Make six groups of five. Make one word card for [[s03-A121]]. Nothing new is taught or freshly scored: the earlier work, after feedback and a chance to revise, is scored from the retained record.

### The day

#### 15 min. Fresh word

Up to six children with an insecure vowel-team pattern read one fresh word each at your table, 90 seconds a child with moving: ask [[s03-A121]]. Never a new test. The rest reread Dawn in the Garden in pairs.

#### 30 min. Flexible groups

Three groups from the P6 and cycle records, 8 minutes each at your table, 3 minutes of moving between: map, blend, read the matched decodable, spell, reread. Remap the vowel first (error T); cover the picture (error S). The others reread.

#### 15 min. Independent reading

Each child reads a band text or Dawn in the Garden alone, then rereads a page. All thirty P6 records are now kept.

#### 25 min. Recording, last ten

The last ten record the publication recording, 2 minutes each plus 5 minutes of changeovers. Today's recording is not scored; the one made on day 117 is. The others read or rehearse. A voice that cannot be heard is error AA. Recordings stay in class and are never posted.

#### 18 min. Formal and informal story lines

Six groups of five, 3 minutes each at your table; the rest rehearse or read. Review: formal and informal talk (grade 2, unit 3, day 111). Say aloud that every line is invented and that no way of speaking is wrong. Each child points to the word in their day 112 comparison that told who the line was for and fixes it from your note (45 seconds a group). Then ask each child, 10 seconds a child, [[s03-A116]] and [[s03-A117]]. Ranking a home way of talking is error X: say what each suits and who is listening.

#### 15 min. Feedback window

Hand back day 117's assessment: every child has your written note on the recording. Collect the fixed pictures and comparisons at the end. Up to 20 children rerecord one short phrase, 30 seconds each, 5 minutes of changeovers; the others fix a drawing or caption from the note. Add one thing you can see or feel (error Z) or say it with a doer and an action (error O).

#### 2 min. Exit

Read the card once: [[s03-X118]] and [[s03-X118b]]. All say it to a partner. Hear two of the six, 45 seconds each; list the other four on the record sheet for day 119. Say it is invented. Error Z: a line said again unchanged.

### Other rooms

No device: tell stories live; a signed teacher checklist replaces the recording. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: say it aloud.
**Skip.** The recording and the word card.

- **10 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X118]] and [[s03-X118b]]
- **10 min. Two ways.** Say the same news to a friend and to a visitor.
  **Say.** "How did you change it?" Expect: more careful words for the visitor; neither way is wrong.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud.
- **10 min. Fix.** Your child fixes one thing in the story.
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child tells you their favorite part.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the comparison, the window and the exit. Longer: add a second visit.

### Words and supports

Formal, informal, visitor, friend. Frames: "For a visitor I say ___." "A friend knows ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 119 opens unit 4 with the first Ant and Grasshopper telling. Today's exit tells you who can shape a line for a stranger; all the unit's records now stay with you.
