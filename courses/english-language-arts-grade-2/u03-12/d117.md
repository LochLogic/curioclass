# Day 117: Record the first twenty stories

## Teacher page

### Before class

Review: the recorder (grade 2, unit 3, day 112). Set the recording corner and write children 1 to 20 on the changeover list. Make three word cards for [[s03-A120]]. Mark the last six of the thirty. Nothing new is taught. The bee story is not reread today.

### The day

#### 12 min. P6 decoding sample

The last six children come to your table one at a time, 2 minutes each: ask [[s03-A120]]. The other twenty-four sort the au and aw sort cards in pairs.

#### 23 min. Independent matched reading

As day 113: read Dawn in the Garden alone and tell a partner the card's answers (10 minutes), reread one page and tell you one word fixed (8 minutes), read a page to a partner for expression (5 minutes).

#### 25 min. Final ten revise and rerecord

The ten who made rehearsal recordings on day 116 revise their story from your written note and rerecord, 2 minutes each plus 5 minutes of changeovers. The other twenty read or rehearse in pairs. Say aloud that the bee's thoughts are imagined.

#### 50 min. Recording, first twenty

Children 1 to 20 record in turn: 2 minutes each, 30 seconds between turns, which is 50 minutes. Each turn: the story, [[s03-A104]] (70 seconds); play it back, [[s03-A105]] (10); the drawing, [[s03-A106]] (15); a listener question, [[s03-A107]] (10); the partner choice, [[s03-A108]] (10). The partner chooses one wording or place of the drawing aloud. The others read, rehearse in pairs with the drawing checklist, or wait; name the next child while the last records. A live telling is error U; no picture of what is said is error V; a partner who only watches is error W; a voice that cannot be heard is error AA. After class mark [[s03-A109]] from the retained poster sheet and [[s03-A110]] to [[s03-A115]] from the retained written story; no child is asked unless a page is unclear.

#### 5 min. Last story revision (C3)

Hand back day 116's story feedback. Each child changes the written story once, from the feedback note. Collect the stories at the end. A missing action, thought, feeling, time word or ending is errors AG to AK.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X117]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. An event with no feeling is error AI.

### Other rooms

No device: tell the story live and mark error U. A child may record in a pair, each contribution named.

### At home

**Swap.** No recorder: tell it aloud. No paper: use any blank page.
**Skip.** The recording and the checklist.

- **10 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X117]]
- **10 min. Tell.** Your child tells their bee story in order and shows the drawing.
  **Say.** "What does your drawing show a listener?" Expect: something that happens or a feeling the story names.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short page aloud and tells what happened.
- **10 min. Fix.** Your child reads the story and changes one thing.
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says who the story is for.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: record one minute. Longer: add a second visit.

### Words and supports

Story, record, listen, feel, imagine. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "I felt ___." "The drawing shows ___." A child may record first in the home language; the English recording is scored.

### Tomorrow

Day 118 records the last ten, compares a formal and an informal line, and opens a feedback window. Today's exit tells you who can name a feeling; every child gets a written note on the recording for the window.
