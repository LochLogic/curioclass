# Exit cards, days 93 to 95

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 93 (s03-X93).** This card is invented for practice. Show the source line: "Some flowers open early in the morning, and bees visit them then." Show the note: "Bees visit flowers late in the evening." Say: "Does the note match the source?"
- **Day 93 (s03-X93b).** Same card. Say: "Which word would you change in the note?"
- **Day 93 (s03-X93c).** Same card. Say: "Say a true note from the source line, in your own words, and say where it came from."
