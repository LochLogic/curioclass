# Day 95: Groups of five gather facts from two articles

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put out one record sheet for each of six groups of five (30 children; if the class is smaller, make the last group four). Write the question on a card for each group: Which flowers does each animal visit, and why? Keep the research-cycle chart up and hang a pairs list for you to fill in. Mark today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 16) on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 94's slips and chart. Run at most two branches, the two most missed. If many copied a sentence (error D), cover the article and say the fact in four words (the hummingbird hovers). If many wrote a true fact that does not answer the question (error E), hold up the question card and ask whether the slip answers it. If a note does not say what its line says (error C), point to the line, read it aloud, and compare the note word by word. Children repair one slip on scrap paper.

#### 3 min. Say the plan

Say: "Today six groups of five do their own research. Each group chooses two articles and asks: Which flowers does each animal visit, and why?" Review: discussion in groups of five with a talk card and a tally child (grade 2, unit 1, day 25). Roles in each group: reader, finder, noter, checker, sharer. The sharer is also the tally child and counts who spoke. After each slip the roles pass one place to the left, so every child writes one slip. Nobody touches an animal.

#### 6 min. Choose a pair

Each group talks for 3 minutes about which two of the articles 01-b to 01-e they will use. The sharer brings the choice to you and you write it on the group record sheet, about 30 seconds a group (3 minutes). You choose none. A pair may repeat across groups. A group that takes two articles that give the same kind of fact is told so, and may keep them.

#### 20 min. Gather and note

Five rounds of about 4 minutes. In each round the reader reads a paragraph of one article aloud, the finder puts a finger on the line that answers the question, the noter writes a slip with its tag, and the checker confirms the finger is on the line. Nobody reads for the group more than the paragraph in hand; you name no line. After the round the roles pass left, and the new noter copies the slip onto the record sheet row. By the end each child has written at least one slip. Visit all six groups and listen to one child in each for 1 minute (about 7 minutes with the moves); mark the child's slip on the sheet: shown, shown with help (after "Where did you find it?") or not yet seen. Tag error D, E, C, AD or AE.

#### 10 min. Check and repair

The checker in each group traces each slip back to its line and reads the tag from it (5 minutes, about 1 minute a slip). The group fixes any D, E, C or AE by rewriting the slip in a second colour (5 minutes). Visit any group that is stuck.

#### 10 min. Share

Each group gives the class one fact and its tag, 1 minute a group (6 minutes). After each, the class says with a thumb whether it answers the question and the group says where it found it (4 minutes in all, about 40 seconds a group).

#### 6 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Which article gave your group the surest fact, and how do you know?" Children say "The article ___ was surest because ___" to a partner (1 minute). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (1 minute). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only. Write yesterday's commonest miss beside the right word and read it aloud. For badge read as pin from the picture (error S), cover the picture, map each letter, blend, then check the meaning. For huge with a short u (error T), remap the vowel as long before ge. For baj or ridg (error Y), write the dge whole. For bridge with a hard g (error AM), star the odd part and write it twice. For bridg (error AN), say d, g, e in order.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Pairs reread A Package at the Bridge for accuracy, one page each, then swap (4 minutes). Each group of five sorts the eleven cycle 16 cards by ending: -dge (badge, edge, judge, bridge, ridge, dodge) and -ge (rage, huge, range, large, plunge), and reads each pile aloud (6 minutes). Each child spells ridge and rage on paper as you dictate them (2 minutes), then says a sentence with each to a partner (2 minutes). Collect the papers; they are unscored. Hear no one for a mark: the oral card is in the rotation.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 16) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each including the swap (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: the six join, ten to a station; groups rotate. Tiles: build ridge, rage, large and dodge. Sort: the cycle 16 cards again, by the sound before the ending. Reread: A Package at the Bridge and the phrase cards. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which two letters come before e in badge?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the articles by hand or read them aloud twice, rule the record sheet on paper and write the cycle 16 cards on slips. No groups of five: work with one group at a time, the others reading. A child who cannot write says the slip and a groupmate or you write it, recorded as help. A tag may be a drawn symbol. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Slip.** Your child reads the group slip aloud and says where it came from.
- **10 min. Ask.** Ask: "Which flowers does that animal visit, and why?" Your child answers in a few words from the slip.
  **Say.** "How do you know?" Expect: the article it came from.
- **10 min. Words.** Write ridge and rage on slips. Your child says each, writes it from memory and uses it in a sentence.
- **5 min. Reread.** Your child reads the school reader title aloud and says what the picture does not show.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the share to one fact a group and the second round of stations; keep the gathering and the check. Longer: a group adds a third article and finds one fact that one article leaves out.

### Words and supports

Group, article, question, slip, tag, reader, finder, noter, checker, sharer, surest. Frames: "The article ___ was surest because ___." "I found it in ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the line instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

There is no exit today. The group record sheet with one slip a child, the oral card records and the heard column are the evidence for section 3.5. Mark the slips later with three look-fors: each note answers the question; each note matches its line and has a right tag; the child, not an adult, found the line. Day 96 begins section 3.6: children use observation photographs and captions to confirm or correct their research notes.
