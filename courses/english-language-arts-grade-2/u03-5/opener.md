# Section 3.5 opener: four visitors and a group record (days 92 to 95)

## What the section does

Children read short true articles about how a bee, a butterfly, a bat and a hummingbird each move pollen. They write notes from them, check a note against its line, compare two visitors with a Both and But sheet, and then work in groups of five with two articles, one question and one note slip each. Day 93's exit asks whether a note matches its source. Days 92, 94 and 95 have no exit.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the note work and a short talk; the foundations block holds cycle 15 and then cycle 16 (-dge and -ge). Day 92 closes cycle 15 with a written check. Day 95 uses the group record sheet.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Each visitor moves pollen in its own way.** The bee gathers it on purpose; butterflies, bats and hummingbirds move it by accident while they feed.
- **A note is checked against its line.** Point to the line that says it.
- **Both and But.** What two visitors share, and what separates them.
- **No insect is touched.** Photographs and drawings are enough.

## The errors, in plain words

- A note contradicts its source (C).
- A fact does not answer the question (E).
- A fact is tagged to the wrong source (AE).
- A useful heading is passed over (AB).
