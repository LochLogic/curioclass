# Written check sheet, cycle 15 (day 92)

Fifteen minutes, three parts of five minutes each; the prefix lines go inside the reading part. The words and cards are read out or handed out from the day's check items; nothing is printed here that gives them away.

- **Spelling.** Write the two words you hear. 1. ___ 2. ___
- **Reading.** Read the card. Answer the question in a few words: ___
- **Prefixes.** Whisper each answer to your partner. Then write the prefix you talked about. 1. ___ 2. ___
- **Reference.** Look at the entry. Correct the word. Write it again: ___

Then mark what you changed.
