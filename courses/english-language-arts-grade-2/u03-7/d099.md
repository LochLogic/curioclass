# Day 99: Gather new facts alone and begin a paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write eight word slips: hatch, pitch, itch, match, lunch, bench, munch, pinch. Put the four jobs on the board. Mark today's six, group 2 of five.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No repair is routed here: retrieval, no marks. Children write hatch, itch, lunch and bench from memory, underline tch or ch and check the board.

#### 15 min. Reader

Review: -tch, -ch, -er and -est (day 98); one word each (3 minutes). Read the title and page 1 of Pitch and Catch in the Garden aloud (1 minute). Pairs echo-read pages 2 to 4 (4 minutes). Map said and garden (2 minutes). Spell pitch, bench, catch, faster and slower; write "Kim can pitch faster." (5 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: match, bench, faster, fastest. Sort: the slips by -tch or -ch. Reread: the reader. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which two letters end the word longer?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

#### 10 min. R2: gather alone

Each child reads the Bee Visit and Butterfly Visit cards (about 145 words) and fills two slips: how each carries pollen, and the card named. No one picks a fact. A child who asks has the card read aloud after a first try (shown with help). Marked from the slips: [[s03-A61]] [[s03-A62]] [[s03-A63]] [[s03-A64]]; the joint-report record gives [[s03-A60]].

#### 25 min. R3: begin the paragraph

Each child writes a topic sentence, two or more facts and a word to define, and notes an ending idea; no frame. After 3 minutes take 30 stops of 45 seconds, moving included (22 minutes): ask [[s03-A65]], [[s03-A66]], then [[s03-A67]]. Drafts are kept.

#### 18 min. R6: discussion in fives, children 1 to 15

Review: the talk card and tally child (grade 2, unit 1, day 25). Three groups of five at your table, 6 minutes each: round 1, a fact and its card, 30 seconds each, for [[s03-A74]]; round 2, 30 seconds each, a link or a request to hear a part again, answered, for [[s03-A75]], [[s03-A76]] and [[s03-A77]]; one minute seats the next group. The other 25 reread slips quietly.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read it twice (1 minute): [[s03-X99]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 20 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children change blocks; collect drafts.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards by hand. A child who cannot write dictates. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X99]]
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads a short paragraph about an animal and says one fact.
  **Say.** "What is this paragraph about, in one sentence?" Expect: one sentence that names the animal and what it does, not one fact.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes two facts from it, and which book or page each came from.
- **10 min. Words.** Say pitch and bench. Your child writes each and says which has the t.
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child asks you one question about the paragraph; answer it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second stations round. Longer: back a fact with a second card line.

### Words and supports

Pollen, carry, tongue, topic, fact, ending. Frames: "The card says ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may say the fact in the home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 100 hands back day 99's assessment, the slips with written feedback; children correct each against its card, then finish and revise. Where a first sentence is one card fact (error F), the feedback names the topic first.
