# Day 100: Finish the paragraph and revise it after feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Staple each child's day 99 slips to the written feedback. Cut the ten sort cards. Mark today's six, group 3 of five; children 16 to 30 are at the table for talk.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No repair is routed here: retrieval, no marks. Children write patch, such and colder from memory, underline the ending and check the board.

#### 15 min. Reread and use the pattern

Pairs reread Pitch and Catch in the Garden for expression (4 minutes). Pairs sort the ten cards into -tch, -ch, and -er or -est (5 minutes). Spell patch, such, colder and fastest (3 minutes), then write "Nick can run faster." (3 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: patch, such, colder, coldest. Sort: the day 99 slips by -tch or -ch. Reread: the reader. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which ending makes a word mean the most?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

#### 5 min. R2: correct against the cards

Return day 99's assessment, the slips with the written feedback (a first sentence that is one card fact is error F). Each child reads each slip against its card line and corrects it, so the note says what the card says and names the right card. Kept for scoring on day 101.

#### 15 min. R3: complete the paragraph

Children add facts, a word to define and an ending from the corrected slips. A child who asks has the feedback line read aloud. From minute 6 take 30 stops of 15 seconds, moving included (8 minutes): [[s03-A68]].

#### 5 min. Partner feedback

Review: the four-row feedback sheet (grade 2, unit 1, day 18). Pairs read each other's paragraph aloud and fill the four rows in a few words each.

#### 10 min. R3: revise

Each child changes one idea using your written feedback and the partner's sheet, circles the change and staples the before page to the after page. Mark [[s03-A71]] from the pages; ask at a stop only a child whose change is not clear.

#### 18 min. R6: discussion in fives, children 16 to 30

As day 99, with its four asks. The other 25 write quietly.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show draft, feedback and source line, read aloud (1 minute): [[s03-X100]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Tag a draft said again as I.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Children change blocks; collect the paired pages.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the sheet by hand. A child who cannot write dictates; the revision still names the card. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X100]]
- **15 min. Read.** Read your child's paragraph aloud and ask what one sentence they would add.
  **Say.** "What does the last sentence tell the reader?" Expect: it says why the facts matter together.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes that sentence and underlines it.
- **10 min. Words.** Say patch and colder. Your child writes each and says which ending means more.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sort cards, keep the stations. Longer: a pair adds a second revision from the partner's sheet.

### Words and supports

Feedback, revise, closing sentence, pollen, tongue. Frames: "The feedback asks ___." "I add ___ because ___." A child may say the new sentence first.

### Tomorrow

Day 101 hands back day 100's assessment, the revised paragraph, to edit from a checklist, and checks one spelling in an entry (error J: a spelling copied from a neighbor). Where a draft was said again (error I), the feedback names the missing idea.
