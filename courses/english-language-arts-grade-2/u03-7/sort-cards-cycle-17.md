# Sort cards, cycle 17 (day 100)

Ten cards to cut apart. Sort into three piles: -tch, -ch, and -er or -est.

- ditch
- fetch
- patch
- such
- much
- rich
- faster
- colder
- fastest
- coldest

Then spell one word and use it in a sentence.
