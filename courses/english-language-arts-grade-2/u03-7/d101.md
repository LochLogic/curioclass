# Day 101: Edit the paragraph and check one spelling

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the entry strip. Write the six retry groups on the board. Mark today's six, group 4 of five.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

No repair is routed here: retrieval, no marks. Children write ditch, much and longer from memory and check the board.

#### 15 min. Fresh passage and dictation

Pairs read the passage on the student page, a sentence each (4 minutes, about 40 words). Ask what happened (2 minutes). Review: the last letter doubles in big before -est (grade 1, unit 2, day 61). Dictate hatch, then bigger, then "Ben digs a bigger ditch." (9 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the oral card to you, two minutes each; 24 are at stations. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes. Tiles: ditch, lunch, bigger, longest. Sort: the day 99 slips. Reread: the passage and the reader. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which letter is doubled in bigger?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

#### 15 min. R4 and R5: edit and check

Return day 100's assessment, the revised paragraphs. Review: the edit checklist (grade 1, unit 1, day 30). Children edit from the sheet (8 minutes). Say wedge, the sentence "A wedge holds the door open." and wedge; children write it (2 minutes): [[s03-A72]]. Give the entry strip; each child checks and corrects (3 minutes): [[s03-A73]]. Read the entry to a child who asks (shown with help). Collect (2 minutes); scoring is on day 103.

#### 18 min. R6 retry in six groups

One group of five at your table, 3 minutes each, moving included. Each retries a missed turn, link, clarification or answer, feedback in hand. A right retry is shown with help and never replaces the first mark. The other 25 read their paragraph to a partner.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Show the caption and entry (1 minute): [[s03-X101]] and [[s03-X101b]]. All tell a partner (1 minute). Hear the six, both asks, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Tag a spelling kept without the entry J.

#### 22 min. Quiet work

Pairs read band texts 01-b to 01-e (10 minutes). Each child tells a partner what they added to the joint question; the partner writes it (7 minutes). Reread the paragraph for sharing (5 minutes). Take 30 stops of 20 seconds, moving included (10 minutes): [[s03-A69]], then [[s03-A70]].

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the passage and entry by hand. A child who cannot write spells aloud to a scribe. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Assessment: the exit ticket.** [[s03-X101]] [[s03-X101b]]
- **15 min. Read.** Your child reads their paragraph aloud and points to the sentence that says what a word means.
  **Say.** "What does that sentence help a reader do?" Expect: it lets the reader understand the next fact.
- **15 min. Edit.** Your child checks each sentence for a capital and an end mark.
- **10 min. Words.** Say ditch and lunch. Your child writes each and says which has the t.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the second stations round. Longer: a child edits a partner's paragraph for one spelling and checks it in a book.

### Words and supports

Edit, checklist, entry, caption, definition. Frames: "I checked ___ in the entry." "The entry says ___." A child may spell the word aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 102 shares the paragraph and ends the cycle check. If the exit showed a spelling kept without the entry (error J), find the entry first, then compare the letters.
