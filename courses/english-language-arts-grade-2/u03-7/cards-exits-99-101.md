# Exit cards, days 99 to 101

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 99 (s03-X99).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A moth visits a flower at night. Its body touches the pollen. It flies to a new flower and some pollen stays there." Say: "Say one sentence that tells what a paragraph about this moth is about."
- **Day 100 (s03-X100).** This card is invented for practice. Show the draft: "A moth helps flowers." Show the written feedback: "Say how the pollen gets to the new flower." Show the source line: "Pollen sticks to its body and goes with the moth." Say: "Say one sentence you would add."
- **Day 101 (s03-X101).** This card is invented for practice. Show the caption: "A moth rests on the lach." Show the entry: "latch: a catch that holds a gate shut." Say: "Use the entry. Say the word the way it should be spelled, letter by letter."
- **Day 101 (s03-X101b).** Same caption and entry. Say: "Say which letter the caption was missing."
