# Section 3.7 opener: the new research paragraph (days 99 to 101)

## What the section does

Children gather facts alone from two new cards, begin a research paragraph with a topic sentence, facts, a word to define and an ending, revise it after feedback, and edit it. The three days are the first scored checks of the unit: the teacher watches how each child gathers, revises and edits, and how each child takes part in a small talk group.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the writing and, in three groups of five at the teacher table, six minutes of observed talk. The foundations block teaches the endings -tch, -ch, -er and -est, and six children a day read the cycle 17 oral card to the teacher. No child is asked to do the same task as the exit on the same day; the exit is a short spoken card at the end of the topic block.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Gathering alone.** The child reads the cards and writes slips. No one picks the facts for the child.
- **A revision changes an idea.** It is more than a neater copy.
- **Editing is a separate pass.** Capitals, end marks and spelling are checked against a reference, not guessed.
- **Talk is observed, not recorded.** Turn, link, clarify and answer are marked on a sheet.
- **No insect contact.** Photographs and cards only.

## The errors, in plain words

- A paragraph with no topic, or a fact that does not answer the question (F, E).
- A draft said again in new words with no new content (I).
- A spelling kept after the entry was seen, or copied with no look (Y, J).
- Facts attributed to the wrong card or a fact the card does not give (AE, C).

## Marking

Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Every exit is marked on that scale, and the support used is written down.
