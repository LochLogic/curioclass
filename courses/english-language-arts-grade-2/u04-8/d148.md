# Day 148: Revise the invitation and lay it out so a guest can find the room

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 147's exit strips with their marks. Read the contribution cards and mark the pairs with a blank column. Set the device with each pair's typed lines open. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 1 to 6) and the six for the oral card (children 25 to 30). Say aloud that the exit card is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Error AA, from yesterday's contribution cards: each child tells the partner one choice they made, such as a word or a place, 45 seconds each way (2 minutes). Four pairs then say theirs to the class, about 45 seconds each (3 minutes). A child who says "my partner typed it" is asked what they would type next.

#### 10 min. Peer feedback

Pairs swap invitations. Each child says one kind thing, one specific thing and one helpful thing, and asks one question: "Could a guest find the room quickly?" (4 minutes, 2 minutes each way, SL.2.1). Each child then writes the two notes on the student page (6 minutes). Review: kind, specific and helpful feedback from unit 2 (day 64) and day 145. The talk question for the day: **Ask.** "How can a guest find the room quickly?" Expect: put the room line in big print, near the top.

#### 30 min. Revise and lay out

Each child revises after the two notes and chooses a word order and where the picture goes (W.2.5). If many missed a capital or a comma on day 147's exit, each pair first rereads its greeting and closing against the letter-part card and the checklist (2 minutes inside the 30). Fifteen pairs take the shared device in turn, 2 minutes each, to move the room line or make it bigger and place the picture (W.2.6). At the device each pair says the choice it made and why, and the pair writes it on the contribution card. Others work by hand: draw the layout on the student page, with the title, the room line and the picture where they want them, and say which line a guest reads first. A child who only watches the typing has not made a choice (error AA): hand over the keyboard and ask what to move.

#### 5 min. Share

Pairs read their invitation aloud to each other, 45 seconds each way (2 minutes); the listener points to the room line. Three pairs read to the class, about 45 seconds each (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Print and place

Print the final invitations. Each child folds theirs into the packet envelope, which goes back in the folder. Anything not printed is copied by hand tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice, show the two layouts and ask: [[s04-X148]]. Call the first of the six to your table at once. The class marks one layout at their seats while you hear the six, about 45 seconds each: [[s04-X148b]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed here. On slates, each child taps and writes vocal and tentacle, then says which ending each takes. Review: the -cal and -cle endings from day 144.

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 25

Hand out the check sheet. Every child writes the words and the sentence you say, then uses the printed dictionary entry (5 minutes each): [[s04-A100]], [[s04-A101]], [[s04-A102]]. Say each word, use it in a sentence, say it once more, and wait. Give no help. Collect the sheets. Keep the check words unseen. Score after class; the commonest miss goes back to the class on day 151.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 25 to 30 read the cycle 25 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap: [[s04-A94]], [[s04-A95]], [[s04-A96]], [[s04-A97]], [[s04-A98]]. Ask [[s04-A99]] only if a word was misread. Model one word on the board before the first turn: tentacle, tapped in three beats and blended. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws a sentence that uses a word from the cycle: vocal, comical, tropical, logical, vertical, typical, article, miracle, vehicle, particle or tentacle (9 minutes). Then each reads it to a partner (4 minutes). Walk and check one spelling against the sound-spelling map (2 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 13 min. Close

Pairs reread A Little Miracle for accuracy, a page each (6 minutes). The class reads it once more smoothly, pausing at each full stop (4 minutes, RF.2.4b). Each child finds one -cal word and one -cle word in the reader and tells a partner (3 minutes).

### Other rooms

No device: write the final invitation by hand and rule the layout; the pair says aloud what it would move. Read the check words and the entry aloud; the child writes on loose paper. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is a mark on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: draw the layout on paper. No slate: use scrap paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the check sheet.

- **10 min. Notes.** Your child reads the invitation to you, and you tell them one kind thing and ask one question.
  **Say.** "Could a guest find the room quickly?" Expect: yes, if the room line is big and near the top.
- **15 min. Layout.** Your child draws where the title, the room line and the picture go.
- **5 min. Sentence.** Your child writes a sentence with article or miracle.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X148]] and [[s04-X148b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the feedback, the layout choice and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second line for the invitation and says where it goes and why.

### Words and supports

Invitation, layout, guest, kind, specific, helpful, choice. Frames: "Please come to ___." "I moved ___ because ___." "I like ___. Could a guest find ___?" Enlarge print, read cards aloud, allow a scribe and a partner to type; extra time on the exit and the line read again.

### Tomorrow

Day 149 is a fluency day. Pairs reread their printed invitation, and if many chose the small room line or said a partner typed it on today's exit, each pair also tells where a guest finds the room. The class then rehearses a tale in two voices. Today's check sheets are scored after class and the commonest miss goes back on day 151. Today's exit marks go to the record sheet.
