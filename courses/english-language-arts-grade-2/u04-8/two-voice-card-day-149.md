# Two-voice card (day 149)

A strip for the rehearsal, two boxes side by side. Left box: a quick-voice mark (a short fast arrow). Right box: a slow-voice mark (a long slow wave). Groups read their own tale lines and point to the mark that fits each speaker. The card holds no sentence of the exit.
