# Printed invitation model (days 147 and 148)

One page, printed once, used as a model of layout and not of words.

- At the top, large: **Come to our pollinator show**
- Under it, large: **Library room, Thursday, 10:30**
- At the bottom, small: **Your helpers**
- A drawing of a bee at one side.

Children point to what a guest looks at first and what each line tells.
