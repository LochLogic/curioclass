# Letter-part card (days 147 and 148)

One card for each pair, three boxes in a row with a small label:

1. Greeting: says hello to the reader; a comma follows.
2. Body: says what, when and where.
3. Closing: says who it is from; a comma follows.

The card shows no sentence and no place name.
