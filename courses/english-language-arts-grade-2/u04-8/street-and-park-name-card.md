# Street and park name card (day 147)

A list of four made-up places for practice, each printed once with its capitals: Mill Street, Bridge Road, Pine Park, River Park. Beside each, the rule: every word of a name begins with a capital. Children copy one name and add the kind of place in small letters (a road, a park). The card never shows the place used in the exit.
