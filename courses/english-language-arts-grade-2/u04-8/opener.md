# Unit 4.8: An invitation and a tale in two voices (days 147 to 149)

## What the section does
The class writes a short invitation to the pollinator show, then uses capital letters and commas, lays the page out so a guest can find the room, and rehearses a tale with two different voices.

## How the days run
- Day 147: the invitation; capitals for names and commas in a greeting and a closing.
- Day 148: peer feedback, revision and layout; cycle 25 written check.
- Day 149: rehearse the tale in parts, each speaker in their own voice.

## The ideas at an adult's level
A greeting and a closing take a comma; the name of a place begins with a capital letter, every word of it. A page is easy to read when the facts a reader needs are large and near the top. In a tale, two characters want different things, and the reader's voice should show it.

## The errors in plain words
- Capitals or commas left out of an invitation.
- Watching a partner type or draw in place of making a choice.
- Two characters read in one voice.

## Marking
Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Each pair keeps a contribution card showing who made which choice.
