# Day 1 word sort

Twelve printed words in three columns. Children look at the letters after the vowel and say what they notice.

| Closed (short vowel) | Silent-e (long vowel) | Open (long vowel) |
|---|---|---|
| jump | bone | go |
| stop | smile | she |
| chin | mule | no |

For the sound sort, the teacher says eight words aloud (nest, bone, we, jump, tide, no, stop, tune) and children hold up the short card or the long card.
