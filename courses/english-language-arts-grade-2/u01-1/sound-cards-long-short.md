# Long and short sound cards

Two cards for each child, printed large on stiff paper. One card shows a small arc over a vowel (a short sound) and the word **short**. The other card shows a long line over a vowel (a long sound) and the word **long**. Children hold up one card for each word the teacher says. Use them on days 1, 3, 4 and 5.
