# Day 6: How does her response change?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each pair a before and after strip and the six line cards. Write rain, wait, paid, chain, day, play, stay and may on cards, one set a pair. Today's six start cycle 2: the first group on the record sheet again. Their oral card: smoke, plane, trap, he, lunch, trim; and "We can ride a bike to the lake." Have the exit passage list, tiles and the record sheet to hand.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 5's exit (error A, a reason that is not in the card), reread the Leo lines of chapter 5 with a finger on each line and replace the reason with the one the story gives. Otherwise partners say what a challenge is and what a response is, and give one example from chapter 3.

#### 3 min. Words

Say each word, the class says it, then act it out once. Hesitate: stop with one foot in the air, not sure. Approach: walk toward something. Children say a sentence with each to a partner: "I hesitate at the top of the slope." "I approach the gate."

#### 12 min. Read chapter 6, Ada's Empty Desk

Show the picture. Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Ada, Leo and Ms. Park are invented." Read the chapter once, about four minutes, pausing after "Ada is home with a cold", after "Mira hesitated" and at the end. At each pause say "I wonder ..." and let a child add one. Look at the picture for two minutes: "What do you notice about Mira's chin and hands?"

#### 10 min. A response can change

Say: "A response can change when a character tries again." Show the strip: Before on the left, After on the right. Model with one card from each chapter. Before, chapter 3: Mira folded her hands and said, "I will just watch." After, chapter 6: with Ada away, she stood up and walked across the room to Leo. Say the frame: "Her response changes when Ada is away."

**Ask.** What did Mira do before? Expect: she folded her hands and said she would just watch.

**Ask.** What does Mira do after? Expect: she stands, walks to Leo and asks him to be her partner.

If a child says "Mira is brave now", say: "That tells what she is like. What does she do?" (error B).

#### 15 min. Sort and place

Pairs read the six line cards aloud, three from chapter 3 and three from chapter 6. They place each card on the strip, in Before or After, and point to the line in the chapter that tells them. Six minutes. Then each pair picks one card from each box and reads the pair aloud to another pair, three minutes. Partners link: "I agree because ..." or "I would add ...". Hear your six, half a minute each. Expect Before: "I do not know how," Mira said quietly. Mira folded her hands on her desk. "I will just watch," Mira said again. Expect After: She stood up. "Can I be your partner for turn and talk?" "Yes. I accept." If a pair cannot decide, ask them to find the chapter that has the line.

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask the day's question: "How does her response change after the next challenge?" Run the four steps: turn (write or draw one change), pair, link, clarify, two minutes a step. Hear your six, half a minute each. Expect: before, she folds her hands and watches; after, she stands, walks over and asks Leo. If someone talks over a partner (error H), rehearse the wait signal once.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice from "Mira's hands started to fold" to "a warm desk," and ask (s01-X6v2): "Tell me what Mira looks at that helps her change." Your six answer aloud, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means the name card, linked to her standing up. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What does she look at?" Not yet seen means a thing she does not look at, or no answer.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write on the board the word most children missed on yesterday's check. If it was slid for slide (error L), blend slid, then slide, with the e hidden and shown. If it was sloide (error Q), set slide beside its family on the wall (ride, hide, bike), and read each. Pairs spell it again with tiles and say why the e is there. Otherwise reread the family words together.

#### 15 min. Ai and ay

Retrieve open and closed syllables, three minutes. Say he, go, sit, hot; children hold up open or closed. Closed: sit, hot. Open: he, go.

Teach two ways to spell the long a sound, twelve minutes. Say: "Two letters together can make one sound. Ai in the middle and ay at the end say long a." Write rain, wait, paid, chain for ai and day, play, stay, may for ay. Model rain: map /r/ /ā/ /n/, three sounds and four letters, with the ai on one tile, then blend, then spell with tiles. Pairs map, blend and spell wait and day. Read all eight cards in chorus, one at a time. Add ai and ay to the sound-spelling-map on the wall. Say: "You mapped said on day 2. It has ai, but it does not say long a. It is a tricky word." If a child reads rain with a short a (error M), map the whole team to its sound first, then blend.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each. Tiles: spell rain, wait, day, stay, may, paid. Sort: sort the eight cards by ai or ay, then say where the sound sits. Reread: partners read A Note for Sam, a page each, then Jess and Pete. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each (the oral card: six words and one sentence, cycle 1 patterns only; this starts the cycle 2 record); the others work in stations. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Say: "Tell your partner one thing Mira did before and one thing she does after." Expect: before, she folded her hands; after, she walked to Leo.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the six lines on torn card. No wall: draw the strip on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No strip: fold a sheet in two and head the sides Before and After.
**Skip.** The stations and the sort.

- **5 min. A response can change.** Say a character can respond one way and then another.
- **10 min. Ada's Empty Desk.** Read chapter 6 aloud. Say this story is made up.
  **Say.** "What does Mira do when Ada is away?" Expect: she stands, walks to Leo and asks him to be her partner.
- **10 min. Ai and ay.** Write rain, wait, day and play. Read each.
  **Say.** "Where does ai sit, and where does ay sit?" Expect: ai in the middle, ay at the end.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6v2]] Reread those chapter 6 lines twice. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the sort to four cards and shorten the third rotation round; keep the strip, the question and the exit. Longer: a child writes "Before, Mira ___; after, Mira ___" and draws both.

### Words and supports

Hesitate, approach, before, after. Frames: "Her response changes when ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 begins the next section. If many children gave error A on today's exit (a thing she does not look at, such as Leo), its opening repair rereads the name card lines aloud and replaces it. If many read ai with a short a (error M), the foundations opening maps the whole team before blending.
