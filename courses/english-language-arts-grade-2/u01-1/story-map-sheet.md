# Story-map sheet (days 1 to 31)

Fold a sheet in thirds. Draw three boxes across the top and label them **Who**, **Where and when**, **What happens**. Under them draw two wider boxes labeled **Challenge** (a hard thing that happens that a character has to deal with) and **Response** (what the character says or does about it). The challenge and response boxes are added on day 3. The teacher holds the map on days 1 to 3; partners hold their own from day 4. Children draw or write a few words in each box.
