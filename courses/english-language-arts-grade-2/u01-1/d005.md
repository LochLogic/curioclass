# Day 5: Why does Mira say no?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each pair the six why and how question cards and a talk routine card, and each child a cycle 1 written check sheet. Today's six for the oral card are the fifth group on the record sheet: pole, theme, brush, glad, we, flame; and "Jake and Beth can bake a cake." Gather the setting chart, event-response cards, long and short sound cards, the exit passage list, and the record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 4's exit (error A, a detail that is not in the picture), reread the first two paragraphs of chapter 3 while showing its picture, and replace the detail with one a child can point to. Otherwise partners pick one place from chapter 4 and say: "The words say ___; the picture shows ___."

#### 12 min. Read chapter 5, The Card Game

Show the picture. Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Leo and Ada are invented." Read the chapter once, about four minutes, pausing after "I invite you", after "I am fine" and at the end. At each pause say "I wonder why ..." and let a child add one. Look at the picture for two minutes: "What do you notice about her face and her feet?"

#### 5 min. Reread

Reread from "Mira's stomach did a flip" through the napkin paragraph, slowly, with a finger under the lines. Partners say: "What did Mira want? What stopped her?" Expect: she wanted to say yes; a worry stopped her.

#### 15 min. Why and how stations

Say: "Why asks for a reason. How asks how you can tell, from what a character says or does." Model one why: "Why does Leo walk away?" Say: "I know because the words say, No, thank you, and then he picked up his tray." Model one how: "How does Leo's table sound after the card falls?" Say: "I know because a girl shouted and everyone cheered."

Pairs choose three of the cards 1, 2, 3, 4 and 6; card 5 is saved for the next part. They answer aloud, point to the line or the picture, and say "I know because ___." They take turns, one card at a time, five minutes a card. Walk and listen. Expect for card 1: she is worried and says no though she wants to say yes. Card 2: her feet point toward Leo's table, and she watches the table. Card 3: she does not want to interrupt Ada. Card 4: he tells her his name and invites her. Card 6: they laugh and cheer. Accept any answer a child can point to.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask the day's question: "Why does Mira twist her napkin?" Run the four steps: turn (write or draw one reason), pair, link, clarify, two minutes a step. The link frame is "I agree because ___" or "I would add ___". Hear your six, half a minute each. Expect: she wants to go to the table but is afraid, so her hands keep busy. If a comment does not connect to the last one (error I), say the last idea first, then add: "You said ...; I would add ...".

#### 10 min. Write one answer

Say: "Write one sentence about Mira: Mira ___ because ___. Then draw it." Show the frame on the board, with a finished one beside it: "Mira says no because she is worried." Look for three things: it names what happened; it says what Mira did, not what she is like; it points to a line or the picture. A child who cannot write draws and tells you; you write the words. Hear the six during the writing.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice from "A boy with a striped hat" to "I remember," and ask (s01-X5v2): "Tell me why Mira almost smiles." Your six answer aloud, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means the reason from the lines: Leo is the thumbs-up boy. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What does Leo say about Monday?" Not yet seen means a reason that is not in the lines.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Review: you have read short, long and silent-e words for five days. Hand out the sheet with four numbered gaps. Say: "This is not a test of you. It tells me what to teach next." Read each sentence twice and say the missing word. Children write the word in the gap. Sentences: "Jo has a ripe ____ ." (plum) "Kim can ____ down the hill." (slide) "It is ____ late." (so) "Let us go ____ and sit." (inside). About eleven minutes. Collect the sheets.

Then, four minutes, unscored, on a slip: dictate "stripe" and then "napkin". Say each twice. Children write what they hear. Do not mark these. Tag a wrong spelling on the check with the letter that fits: L for a vowel misplaced ("plume", "slid"), Q for a pattern not carried over ("sloide").

#### 5 min. Reader

Partners reread A Note for Sam, a page each, for expression. Say: "Read it as if you were Pete saying yes." Ask them to make the question sound like a question.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Two rounds. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each (the oral card: six words and one sentence; new material modelled first); half the others work at tiles (spell pole, flame, brush, we, glad, bake) and half reread A Note for Sam. Round 2, 12 minutes: the stations swap. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

Day 1's six read to you, two minutes each, twelve minutes in all: chop, fresh, thank, hike, flame, me, and the sentence "Jake can ride home." Note who reads each cycle word by its letters. The others partner-read a pattern card (hike, gate, tone, me, bell, up) and spell two words you say twice: "bake" and "pole". No new teaching.

### Other rooms

No wall card: write the six question words on the board. No printer: write the six questions on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the question on a slip. No sheet: your child writes the word on paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the reread.

- **5 min. Why and how.** Say why asks for a reason and how asks how you can tell.
- **10 min. The Card Game.** Read chapter 5 aloud. Say this story is made up.
  **Say.** "Why does Mira say no, thank you?" Expect: she is worried, though she wants to say yes.
- **10 min. One sentence.** Your child writes "Mira ___ because ___" and draws it.
  **Say.** "Where did you find the reason?" Expect: a line or the picture.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X5v2]] Reread those chapter 5 lines twice. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reread and answer only two cards; keep the turn, pair, link, clarify part, the written answer, the exit and the check. Longer: a child writes a second sentence with how and points to the line.

### Words and supports

Why, how, reason. Frames: "I know because ___." "Mira ___ because ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 6 reads chapter 6 and traces how Mira's response changes. If many children gave error A on today's exit (a reason that is not in the lines, such as winning the card game), its opening repair rereads the Leo lines aloud and replaces the reason. The foundations opening repairs the commonest error on today's written check, L or Q.
