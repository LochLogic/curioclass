# Setting chart (day 4)

A sheet with two columns. The left column is headed **The words say** and the right column **The picture shows**. Under the columns print the frame: "The words say ___; the picture shows ___ ." Partners fill it for a place in the story: they write or draw one fact in each column and point to the line or the part of the picture that gave it.
