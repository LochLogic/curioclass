# Exit cards, days 1 to 6

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards; you read each twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 1 (s01-X1).** "Leo sorts word cards into two piles. He says each word out loud. Then he writes one new word on a slip." Ask: "Tell me what Leo does, in the order the card gives."
- **Day 2 (s01-X2).** "Ada waits by the art-room sink. Mira is still at her desk." Ask: "Say who waits by the sink, using the words of the card."
- **Day 3 (s01-X3).** "The window is stuck and Leo cannot push it open. He asks Mira to push with him." Ask: "Tell me what Leo does about the stuck window."
- **Day 4 (s01-X4).** "Leo steps into the reading tent. Its cloth roof hangs above him." Picture: a tent with a blue cloth roof covered in painted stars, a floor lamp and two cushions. Ask: "Tell me one thing the picture shows about the tent that the words do not say."
- **Day 5 (s01-X5).** "A truck is too loud for Mira to hear Ada. Mira asks Ada to move to a quiet bench so they can talk." Ask: "Tell me why Mira asks Ada to move."
- **Day 6 (s01-X6).** "Leo waits quietly when the line is full. Ada makes room. Now Leo steps in and tells his idea." Ask: "Tell me what changes for Leo after Ada makes room."
