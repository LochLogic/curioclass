# Exit passages, days 1 to 6

A teacher list, not printed. Each exit rereads a part of Mira's New Classroom that the day does not work through, read aloud twice, then asks the one question. Show the named chapter picture where one is listed. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 1 (s01-X1v2).** Chapter 1, the paragraph that begins "I wonder what it is like inside." Ask: "Tell me what Mira wonders, in the order the story gives."
- **Day 2 (s01-X2v2).** Chapter 2, from "On the desk was a small card" to "In case you came today." Ask: "Say who made the card and where its star is. Use the words of the story."
- **Day 3 (s01-X3v2).** Chapter 3, from "Ada thought for a moment" to "You can watch first." Ask: "Tell me what Ada says and what she does when Mira says she will watch."
- **Day 4 (s01-X4v2).** Chapter 3, the first two paragraphs, with the chapter 3 picture. Ask: "Tell me one thing the picture shows about the room that the words do not say."
- **Day 5 (s01-X5v2).** Chapter 5, from "A boy with a striped hat" to "I remember." Ask: "Tell me why Mira almost smiles, and how Leo helps her remember him."
- **Day 6 (s01-X6v2).** Chapter 6, from "Mira's hands started to fold" to "a warm desk." Ask: "Tell me what Mira looks at that helps her change."
