# Day 3: The challenge of a new school

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have the story-map sheet on the board with room for two more boxes. Write twelve word cards for each pair: hut, plan, fish, lamp, hide, game, rope, tune, be, go, she, he. Today's six are the third group on the record sheet. Their oral card: pine, shape, cone, we, trip, bunch; and "We hide the note in a safe spot." Gather the event-response cards, talk routine cards, long and short sound cards, the exit passage list.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 2's exit (error A, the wrong person at the place), reread the two name card sentences of chapter 2 with a finger on where, and have the class say the right answer. Otherwise retrieve the six question words: partners say them and ask each other one question about chapter 2 with any of them.

#### 3 min. Words

Say each word, the class says it, then each goes in a sentence you say and the class echoes. Respond: "I respond when my friend waves; I wave back." Belong: "My coat belongs on the hook." Children say a sentence with a word to a partner. Challenge is taught below.

#### 12 min. Read chapter 3, The Big Blue Chart

Show the picture. Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Ada and Ms. Park are invented." Read the chapter once, about three minutes, pausing after "That was the challenge", after "I will just watch," Mira said again, and at the end. At each pause say "I wonder ..." and let a child add one.

#### 12 min. Challenge and response

Show the story-map. Review: in grade 1 you used the story-map for who, where and when, and what happens. Add two boxes below: Challenge and Response. Say: "A challenge is a hard thing that happens that a character has to deal with. A response is what the character says or does about it." Model on turn and talk, where Ada asks Mira to go first: draw a hard thing in the Challenge box, and write Mira's words and what her hands do in the Response box. Frame: "The challenge is ___, so Mira ___."

**Ask.** What is the challenge? Expect: everyone else already knows how to turn and talk and Mira does not.

**Ask.** What does Mira do about it? Expect: she folds her hands and says "I do not know how. I will just watch."

Write "Mira is shy" beside the box and ask: "Does this say what she says or does?" Say: "That tells what she is like. The response is what she says and does" (error B).

#### 13 min. Pairs fill the map

Pairs turn to their event-response card, cross out Event and write Challenge. Say: "Now fill it for the Quiet Signal part of chapter 3 and the end of the day." Reread aloud the paragraph that begins "At Maple Street School" and the last paragraph. They draw the challenge, then write or draw what Mira does. They say the frame to each other. Run the four steps: turn (write or draw), pair, link, clarify. Hear your six, half a minute each. Expect: the challenge is that everyone else already knows the names and rules and Mira does not; Mira walks to the bus with her hands in her pockets and says "I will just watch." If a comment does not connect to the last (error I), say the last idea first, then add: "You said ...; I would add ...".

#### 10 min. The poem again

The class reads Our School Day together, three minutes, each part with its lines that come back. Partners link: "Which part of the poem tells what pupils do together at lunch? I agree because ..." Expect: the part about spoons, passing the fruit and sharing a laugh; the pupils pass the fruit together. Share two answers.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice from "Ada thought for a moment" to "You can watch first," and ask (s01-X3v2): "Tell me what Ada does when Mira says she will watch." Your six answer aloud, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means what Ada does, linked to Mira's words. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What does Ada do?" Not yet seen means a trait or no answer.

#### 5 min. Opening (foundations)

Each child holds the long and short cards. Say each word once: mad, made, bite, hid, me, pet, zone, fun. Children hold up the card for the vowel sound. Long: made, bite, me, zone. Short: mad, hid, pet, fun.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Review: you read A Note for Sam yesterday. Partners reread it a page each, three minutes. Then pairs sort the twelve word cards into closed, open and silent-e columns on the mat, four minutes: closed hut, plan, fish, lamp; silent-e hide, game, rope, tune; open be, go, she, he. Say the rule for each column.

Spell two sentences, four minutes. Say each twice and the class writes: "Pete can hide a note." and "She can bake a cake." Then write on the board "Jake can ride his bik." Children say the wrong word and how to fix it, four minutes. Expect: bik is wrong; add an e, bike (error L).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each. Tiles: spell bake, ride, home, he, drip, flat. Sort: sort the twelve cards again, then add one new word to each column. Reread: partners reread A Note for Sam with expression, one reading as Sam. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each (the oral card: six words and one sentence, patterns of days 1 and 2 and grade 1); the others work in stations. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which word needed its e today?" Expect: bike, hide, bake or any silent-e word.

### Other rooms

No wall card: write the six question words on the board. No sound cards: stand up for long, crouch for short. No printer: draw the three-column mat on the table with chalk or paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No sound cards: stand up for long, crouch for short.
**Skip.** The poem and the stations.

- **5 min. Challenge and response.** Say a challenge is a hard thing that happens, and a response is what a character says or does about it.
- **10 min. The big blue chart.** Read chapter 3 aloud. Say this story is made up.
  **Say.** "What does Mira do when Ada asks her to go first?" Expect: she folds her hands and says "I do not know how. I will just watch."
- **10 min. Words with e.** Write hide, rope, tune and bike. Read each.
  **Say.** "What does the e at the end do?" Expect: it is silent and the vowel says its name.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3v2]] Reread those chapter 3 lines twice. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the poem to one reading and shorten the third rotation round; keep the challenge and response part and the exit. Longer: a child draws a second challenge from chapter 3 and what Mira does.

### Words and supports

Respond, belong, challenge, response. Frames: "The challenge is ___, so Mira ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 reads chapter 4 and describes the setting from both words and picture. If many children gave error B on today's exit (a trait with no action, such as "Ada is nice"), its opening repair lays a slip beside two action cards, and the child picks the one that answers it.
