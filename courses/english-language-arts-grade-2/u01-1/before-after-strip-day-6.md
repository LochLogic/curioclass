# Before and after strip with line cards (day 6)

A long strip of paper folded into two boxes. The left box is headed **Before** and the right box **After**. Under it print: "Her response changes when ___ ."

Six line cards, three from chapter 3 and three from chapter 6. Pairs place each card in the box where it belongs and read the pair they choose aloud.

- Chapter 3: "I do not know how," Mira said quietly. | Mira folded her hands on her desk. | "I will just watch," Mira said again.
- Chapter 6: She stood up. | "Can I be your partner for turn and talk?" | "Yes. I accept."
