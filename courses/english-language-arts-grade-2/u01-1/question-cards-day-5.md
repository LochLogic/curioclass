# Why and how question cards (day 5)

Six printed cards about chapter 5. Pairs choose three, answer aloud, point to the line or the picture, and say "I know because ___ ."

1. Why does Mira say "I am fine"?
2. How can you tell Mira wants to go?
3. Why does Mira stay at the end of the long table?
4. How does Leo show he is friendly?
5. Why does Mira twist her napkin?
6. How do the children at Leo's table act?
