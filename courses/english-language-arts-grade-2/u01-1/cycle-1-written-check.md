# Cycle 1 written check (day 5, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap, and nothing else. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. Four independent spellings; none is a word from days 1 to 4.

1. "Jo has a ripe ____ ." (plum)
2. "Kim can ____ down the hill." (slide)
3. "It is ____ late." (so)
4. "Let us go ____ and sit." (inside)

Tag a wrong spelling with the letter that fits what the child did. "Plume", "slid" and "sloide" show a long or short vowel misplaced (L) or a pattern not carried over (Q). Record other spellings separately. After the check is marked, dictate "stripe" and "napkin" as unscored practice.
