# Six question words card (wall card, days 2 on)

Print six large words down the card, each with one example question about a place outside the story, such as a pond.

- **Who** fed the ducks?
- **What** floated on the water?
- **Where** is the pond?
- **When** did the ducks come?
- **Why** did the ducks swim away?
- **How** did the children count the ducks?

Keep it on the wall. Children point to the word they use when they ask a question.
