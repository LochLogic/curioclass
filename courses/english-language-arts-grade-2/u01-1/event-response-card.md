# Event and response card (day 1 on)

Two boxes side by side on a half sheet. The left box is labeled **Event** and the right box **Response**. Under them print the frame: "The event is ___, so ___ ." The child draws or writes the event from a chapter in the left box and what the character says or does in the right box. A trait word ("shy", "kind") does not go in the right box; ask "What does she do?".
