# Chorus wall strip (days 86 and 87)

A long strip of paper for the wall. The refrain of the first poem is printed large, two lines:

Land, cling, carry,
Take the pollen, do not tarry.

The whole class says it together. Each of the six groups of five says one stanza and the class joins the refrain.
