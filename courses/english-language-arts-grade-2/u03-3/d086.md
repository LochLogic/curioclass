# Day 86: Choose exact words to describe a pollinator

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the chorus wall strip; keep the step chain on the wall. Make each pair's cards and strips from the lists below, and each group's twelve -le cards from day 85's list. Mark today's six (group 4 of five) on the record sheet. The exit is spoken. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-14-day-86.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 85's exit (s03-X85), where the commonest miss was two near words said to be the same (error Q). Hang the word ladder. Say: "Two near words are not the same." Lay damp and soaked on the rungs and say the difference with your hands: a few drops, then water dripping from all of it. Pairs lay sip and gulp on a small ladder of their own and show the difference with their hands too (2 minutes). Say aloud: "Today we choose words that show exactly what a pollinator does."

#### 8 min. Reread the poem

Read 03-a, Land, Cling, Carry, aloud once with the pictures, then reread it with the class: you read each stanza and everyone joins the refrain from the wall strip. Stop at the hummingbird line and point to the picture; say: "The poem picked hover to show that picture." Children hear the exact words and see the pictures; they do not read the stanzas and no pair of verbs is compared today. Review: the words from day 85, fuzzy, sticky and tremble, are all in the poem; children put a finger up when they hear one (2 minutes).

#### 14 min. Pick the exact word

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Each pair has four sentence cards with a gap and two word cards for each: "The bird ___ at the red flower." (hovers, is) "The bee ___ on the petal." (lands, is) "The leaf ___ in the wind." (trembles, is) "The ___ pollen stays on her legs." (sticky, nice). Model the first aloud: lay hovers, read the sentence, and say "I chose hovers because it shows the bird stays in the air" (3 minutes). Pairs lay a card in each gap and say "I chose ___ because it shows ___" for each sentence (6 minutes). Hear six pairs, about 50 seconds each (5 minutes), and ask each pair one of: "Which part do you mean?" or "What does that word show you?"

#### 12 min. Describe a pollinator

Review: the thing card and the action card (grade 2, unit 2, day 56): a word that describes a thing is an adjective; a word that describes an action is an adverb, often ending in -ly. Pairs match the six word cards (fuzzy, smooth, prickly, sticky, soft, hard) to the six describing-word picture cards (3 minutes). Then pairs sort six more word cards onto the thing card or the action card: yellow, softly, bright, busily, fuzzy, brightly (3 minutes). Hand out the four sentence strips, one for each pollinator: "The fuzzy bee lands softly." "The bright hummingbird hovers busily." "The orange butterfly flaps lightly." "The dark bat flies quietly." Pairs circle the word for the thing in one colour and underline the word for the action in the other (4 minutes). Pairs read the four strips to each other (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Why is hovers a better word than is?" Children say "My word hovers shows ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class saying what a good word does (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child copies one of the four strips onto the student page, circles the thing word and underlines the action word, and draws the picture the sentence makes. A few words; the teacher scribes for a child who asks.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the slug card aloud twice. Ask one thing at a time: item [[s03-X86]], then [[s03-X86b]] on the same card, read once more. Partners whisper each answer, then you stand by the six, about 10 seconds an item (2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help (after "Is that for the slug or for the leaf?") or not yet seen. Tag error R for a describing word for a thing put on an action, or for the wrong noun.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write yesterday's commonest miss (error S, T or Y) beside the right word and read it aloud, pointing at each letter.

#### 15 min. Fresh passage and dictation

Read the title of Rain on the Stable aloud. Pairs read the passage, one sentence each, then swap (5 minutes). Ask: "Who has the candle, and what hurts?" Children answer from the words; never from a picture or a first letter (2 minutes). Dictate tiger, table and then the sentence "I said it again" (5 minutes); children write on the student page and check each word on the word wall. Star the odd parts in said and again (3 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the day's six (group 4 of five in cycle 14) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: stations fill to ten and the six take the two they have not had. Tiles: build tiger and table. Sort: the twelve -le cards, then spell two. Reread: Rain on the Stable and The Stable Gate. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "Which letters in again are the odd part?" Children tell a partner, then say it together.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six pictures on torn paper, write the strips by hand. No pair sets: one set on the board, a pair at a time. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Read.** Your child reads the sentence on the student page with a finger.
- **10 min. Words.** Write six words on slips: fuzzy, sticky, soft, softly, busily, brightly. Your child puts each under "a thing" or "an action".
  **Say.** "What does softly tell us about?" Expect: an action.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the sentence on the page and points to the word that shows the picture.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say tiger and table. Your child writes each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X86]] [[s03-X86b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reread of the poem and the matching of words to pictures; keep the sort, the strips and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second strip about a pollinator that is not on the cards.

### Words and supports

Describing word, thing, action, hover, land, tremble, fuzzy, sticky, busily, softly. Frames: "I chose ___ because it shows ___." "My word hovers shows ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the card instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can give a describing word for a thing and a different one for an action. If many wrote crawls slow or gave a describing word to the leaf (error R), day 87 opens with the thing card and action card. Day 87 rereads the pollinator poems and revisits the process explanation for meaning.
