# Describing-word picture cards (day 86)

Six cards, one picture each, drawn in thick plain lines: a fuzzy chick, a smooth stone, a prickly leaf, a sticky label, a soft pillow, a hard floor.

Pairs say one describing word for each card, then act one action word of their own with the arms. The thing card and the action card from unit 2 come back on this day and sit beside these.
