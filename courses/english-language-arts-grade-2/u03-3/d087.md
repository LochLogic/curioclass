# Day 87: Say the poems so the words show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the chorus wall strip; keep the step chain on the wall. Cut three line strips from 03-b for each pair, and write each group's stanza (see Perform a stanza). Mark today's six (group 5 of five, the last of the cycle) on the record sheet. There is no exit today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Practice only, no marks. This follows day 86's exit (s03-X86), where the commonest miss was a describing word on the wrong thing (error R). Say: "This card is made up for practice. A duck swims across a pond." Hold up the thing card and the action card. Name the thing, the duck, and children say one word that describes it. Then name the action, swims, and children say a word for how it swims, ending in -ly. Hear the six, about 10 seconds each, and have everyone say each pair aloud.

#### 12 min. Hear and name the visitor

Read 03-b, A Dusty Visitor, aloud twice with the pictures (3 minutes). The poem does not name its visitor. Partners tell each other who it is and the words that show it (fuzzy, yellow, round, small, buzzed), and point to the line they mean on the poem page (4 minutes). Hear the six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), then the class agrees on the visitor and the line that shows it (2 minutes). Children do not decode the stanzas yet; the printed page is for the finger. The title word is not drawn out today.

#### 12 min. Rehearse for expression

Model one line two ways, flat and then with the voice made to fit, and children say which shows the picture (1 minute). Pairs reread 03-b stanza by stanza and the refrain of 03-a from the wall strip, making the voice fit each line (11 minutes). While they rehearse, hear the six, about 1 minute each, at your table: one stanza of 03-b, read aloud on a second reading. Mark for the oral reading record: smooth, fits the words, accurate. Shown, shown with help (after one reread) or not yet seen. No timer.

#### 12 min. Poem lines and steps

Review: the step chain (grade 2, unit 3, day 79) and the steps in Pollen on the Move. Read the chain aloud, First, Then, Next, After that, Finally. Pairs have three line strips from 03-b: "It dipped in a flower, deep and sweet," "Gold on its legs and gold on its back," and "And dusted the stigma with gold, just right." Review: the stigma is the sticky top part of the flower in the middle (day 78). Pairs lay each strip beside the chain word it goes with and say "This line goes with ___ because ___" (6 minutes). Check one match aloud with the whole class: dipped in a flower is Then, because the bee reaches in and the pollen sticks (3 minutes). Each child writes the chain word beside each line on the student page (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine (grade 2, unit 1, day 1). Ask: "Which describing word in 03-b tells you most about the next flower?" Children say "The word ___ tells me ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I think ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class naming the word that came up most (2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 9 min. Perform a stanza

Review: perform a stanza (grade 2, unit 1, day 21): one pace keeper per group raises a hand at each line end, and everyone joins the refrain. Six groups of five each read two lines: 03-a stanza one lines 1 and 2, then 3 and 4; 03-a stanza two lines 1 and 2, then 3 and 4; 03-b stanza one lines 1 and 2, then 3 and 4 (set up 1 minute). Each group reads once (30 seconds), the class says the 03-a refrain from the wall strip (15 seconds), and one listener says one thing they heard (15 seconds): 1 minute a group, 6 minutes. Close with one reading of the refrain all together (2 minutes).

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Unscored. Give each child the cycle 14 check sheet and say each part twice. Spelling (5 minutes): items [[s03-A25]] and [[s03-A26]]. Reading (5 minutes): hand out the printed card and ask [[s03-A27]]; children write a few words. Reference (5 minutes): hand out the draft and the dictionary entry and ask [[s03-A28]]; children write the corrected word and mark what they changed. Collect the sheets. Tag error AN for swapped last letters, Y for a missing final syllable, C for an answer that contradicts the card and J for a copied draft.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread The Stable Gate for expression: one reads a page, the other the next, then swap. Say: "Read it like talking." Listen to any pair for one page.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 14, the last of the cycle) read the cycle 14 oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes), and the other 24 work at the three stations, eight to a station. Round 2 is 12 minutes: the six join, ten to a station. Every child has two stations. Tiles: build handle and puzzle. Sort: the twelve -le cards. Reread: The Stable Gate and a poem page of 03-b. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

No new teaching. Day 83's six read all six cycle 14 words to you, wrinkle and drizzle included, two minutes each (12 minutes). The other 24 partner-read the cycle 14 oral card words, then spell two words as you dictate them: noodle and wrinkle (unscored).

### Other rooms

No printer: write the stanzas, the three line strips and the dictionary entry on slips. No pair sets: one poem page on the board, read together. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Hear.** Read your child A Dusty Visitor from the poem page, then ask who the visitor is.
- **10 min. Voice.** Your child reads the three lines on the student page twice: once flat, once so the words show their picture.
  **Say.** "Which way shows the gold better?" Expect: with the voice.
- **10 min. Chain.** Your child says the chain, First, Then, Next, After that, Finally, and points to the word that goes with each line.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child writes noodle and wrinkle.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the reader and the second round of the stations; keep the rehearse, the performance and the written check. Longer: a child chooses a second poem line and says which chain word it goes with.

### Words and supports

Visitor, stanza, refrain, stigma, expression. Frames: "This line goes with ___ because ___." "The word ___ tells me ___." "You said ___; I think ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the line or the chain word instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

Mark the written check after class (AN, Y, C, J). There is no exit today: the oral reading record, the heard column and the check are the evidence. Day 88 opens with a repair of the commonest cycle 14 check miss in the foundations block and starts shared research on two pollinator texts.
