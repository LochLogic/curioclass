# Exit cards, days 85 to 86

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 85 (s03-X85).** Say: "Say where you could find something bumpy."
- **Day 85 (s03-X85b).** Same thing. Say: "Say why the word bumpy fits it."
- **Day 85 (s03-X85c).** Say: "Show with your arms which is faster, a stroll or a dash."
- **Day 85 (s03-X85d).** Say: "Which is colder, chilly or freezing?"
- **Day 85 (s03-X85e).** Same pair. Say: "How do you know?"
- **Day 86 (s03-X86).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A slug crawls onto a leaf." Say: "Say one describing word for the slug."
- **Day 86 (s03-X86b).** Same card. Say: "Say one describing word for how the slug crawls."
