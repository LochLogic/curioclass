# Section 3.3 opener: near words and two poems (days 85 to 87)

## What the section does

Children learn that words close in meaning are not the same: they sit at different places on a ladder from least to most. They hear two short poems about pollinators, notice the exact verbs and describing words, tell a describing word for a thing from a word for an action, and perform a stanza with the class. Day 87 holds the cycle 14 written check.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 85 builds the word ladder with real or pictured things in a row. Day 86 brings back the thing card and the action card and asks for one describing word for a thing and a different kind of word for an action. Day 87 is the performance: six groups of five say a stanza together with the chorus. In the foundations block, cycle 14 continues words ending in a consonant plus le, and a written check closes the cycle.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Near words differ in strength.** Damp, wet and soaked are ordered; the child can show the order with the hands.
- **A word for a thing and a word for an action are different jobs.** A word for an action often ends in -ly.
- **A poem uses exact verbs.** Land, settle, hover and swoop are four different pictures.
- **Pollen, not seeds.**

## The errors, in plain words

- Two near words are said to mean the same (Q).
- A word is used with no real example behind it (P).
- A describing word for a thing is put on an action, or the other way round (R).

## Marking

Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The ladder and the acting are quick looks, not scores.
