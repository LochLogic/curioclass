# Word ladder (days 85 and 86)

A row of three picture cards from least to most: **damp**, **wet**, **soaked**. A cloth, a sponge and a puddle make three real things for the same row. The class says the ladder aloud and then shows "a little" and "a lot" with the arms.

Pairs keep a small copy. A second ladder is built on day 86 from the poem 03-a with pictures: settle, land.
