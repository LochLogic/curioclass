# Day 82: Choose a clue for a hard word

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the flower diagram card and the step chain cards where all can see them. Give each pair a strategy fan and the six clue cards, cut apart. Give each child the day 82 student page and the cycle 13 check sheet. Mark today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 13) on the unit 3 record sheet and keep the cycle 13 oral card by your table. Keep the exit cards 82 to 84 at hand (spoken, nothing printed). Key: key-clue-cards-day-82.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Use the commonest miss from day 81's exit. If many pointed to the wrong arrow or said the bee flies home (error C), or said seeds fly (error A), show the flower diagram card. Trace arrow 2 with a finger and ask pairs what moves along it. Then each pair puts a finger on the line of the card that says so: pollen moves from the bee to the second flower. Otherwise pairs say the step chain from memory, one step each, with First, Then, Next, After that and Finally.

#### 5 min. Say the plan

Hold up a strategy fan, the same as each pair's. Say: "Today you read a new paragraph and choose your own clue for a hard word. Nothing is new; you choose." Three new names go with old work, about 40 seconds each. A strategy is a way to work out a word. Review: sentence clue, the sentence beside a word tells what it means (grade 1). Review: word parts, a small word you know inside a longer one, as care in careful (unit 2, day 37), and un- and re- on a base word (unit 2, day 50). Review: a dictionary page entry, where you find the word and compare the letters (unit 1, day 27). A dictionary strip is the same idea cut small: a few entries on a slip. Say: "This fan holds the three cards. Each pair holds one set, today and on days 83 and 84."

#### 8 min. Listen and read

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read "Mira's Beans" aloud twice (3 minutes). Children then read it once on side 1 of the student page, a finger under each word (3 minutes), and star one word that stops them (1 minute). Ask by show of hands who starred careless, mind or pod; there is no right word to star (1 minute). Content: a bee visits a bean flower; no sting or injury, and nothing is handled. Say aloud that mind here means care about, not the part of you that thinks.

#### 14 min. Choose and use

Lay the three fan cards on the board as three piles. Then:

- Model one clue card, "care + less", with think-aloud (2 minutes): "Two small parts. I know care. This is a word parts clue." Place it under word parts. Review: in grade 1, day 146, you met the ending -less. Say: careless means without care, and the card sentence "she does not mind it" backs that up.
- Pairs sort the other five clue cards under the three piles (5 minutes). While they work, sit with today's six as three pairs, about 80 seconds a pair, and ask only "Which pile, and what is on the card?"
- Check each pile aloud (5 minutes): sentence clue holds "The bee is careless about the pollen" and "Soon that flower starts a bean pod"; word parts holds "care + less" and "hunt + ing"; dictionary strip holds "pod: a case that holds seeds" and "twig: a small thin branch". Say the meaning each clue gives: careless about the pollen shows mind is care about, pod is a case that holds seeds.
- Pairs point to the starred word in the card that each pile helped (2 minutes).

If many put a card in the wrong pile, ask what is printed on the card, and move it to the pile that names that kind of clue. Frame: "This card goes with ___ because it shows ___."

#### 12 min. Explain the process

Do not retell yet. Read four claims about the card, one at a time (the claim strips of key-clue-cards-day-82). Pairs put a finger on the line of the card that shows the claim is right or wrong and show a thumb up or down.

- Model claim 1 (2 minutes): "The bee carries pollen from one bean flower to the next." Thumbs up; the finger goes to the line about the next bean flower.
- Claims 2, 3 and 4 (6 minutes): "The bee carries seeds to the next bean flower." (pollen, not seeds, error A); "The bean pod starts before the bee lands." (the card puts the landing first); "The bee drinks the sweet juice, and pollen sticks to her legs." Pairs fix each wrong claim aloud and say the fixed claim together.
- Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes): each says to you one fixed claim, from any of the four, and touches the line that says so. Others whisper theirs to a partner.

Show the step chain on the wall and ask where the wrong claim breaks it.

#### 11 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from unit 1, day 1. Ask: "Which fan card would you hand a friend first when a word stops them, and why?" Children say "I would hand ___ because ___" to a partner (2 minutes). The partner links with "You said ___; I would add ___" or asks "Which part do you mean?" (2 minutes). Hear the six, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Close with the class naming one reason that came up twice (3 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." There is no printed card. Read the moth card twice, then ask one question at a time. Item [[s03-X82]]: each pair holds up its fan card; any of the three is fair, because each can work on uncurls; note the six. Items [[s03-X82b]], [[s03-X82c]] and [[s03-X82d]]: partners whisper the answer to each other; you hear the six on X82b and X82c, about 10 seconds a child, and mark each, and mark X82d from what partners whisper near you: shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AB for no strategy named, AL for un- treated as meaningless, B for steps as a list and A for seeds carried.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Unscored. Give each child the cycle 13 check sheet and say each part twice. Spelling (5 minutes): items [[s03-A21]] and [[s03-A22]]. Reading (5 minutes): hand out the printed card and ask [[s03-A23]]; children write a few words. Reference (5 minutes): hand out the draft and the dictionary entry and ask [[s03-A24]]; children write the corrected word and mark what they changed. Collect the sheets. Tag error Y for a missing final syllable, AN for swapped last letters, C for an answer that contradicts the card and J for a copied draft.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread A Little Table for expression: one reads a page, the other reads the next, then they swap. Say: "Read it like talking." Listen to any pair for one page.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 13, the last of the cycle) read the cycle 13 oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes), and the other 24 work at the three stations, eight to a station. Round 2 is 12 minutes: the six join, ten to a station. Every child has two stations. Tiles: build and spell paddle and ladle. Sort: the day 80 cards, open or closed first syllable. Reread: A Little Table and the phrase cards. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

No new teaching. Day 78's six read the cycle 13 words to you, two minutes each (12 minutes). The other 24 partner-read the cycle 13 phrase cards, then spell handle and ladle as you dictate them (unscored).

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three fan cards on card, copy the six clues on slips, and write the dictionary entry for the check on a slip. No pair sets: one fan on the board, a pair at a time. Pointing is an answer.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The oral card and the stations.

- **5 min. Read.** Your child reads Mira's Beans on the student page with a finger, then stars one word that stops them.
- **10 min. Clue.** Ask which clue helps with the starred word: the sentence beside it, a part inside it, or a dictionary. Write the clue words down.
  **Say.** "What does the sentence say about it?" Expect: a few words from the card.
- **10 min. Reread.** Your child reads Mira's Beans aloud a second time, slowly and then like talking, and says what the bee carries.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say paddle. Your child writes it, then checks the ending in a book or on a slip you write.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X82]] [[s03-X82b]] [[s03-X82c]] [[s03-X82d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sort, the claims, the exit and the written check; cut the second round of the stations. Longer: a child writes one clue card of their own for a word in the card and a partner picks its pile.

### Words and supports

Strategy, clue, strip, careless, mind, pod. Frames: "This card goes with ___ because it shows ___." "I would hand ___ because ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" A child may point to the card or the line instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you whether children can name a strategy with its clue, say what a word part does, and say the steps with why the pollen reached the second flower. If many named no strategy (error AB), or treated un- as meaningless (AL), day 84 groups them. Mark the written check after class (Y, AN, C, J). Day 83 is a check on a new card: its opening repair is the commonest miss of this written check. Day 83 reads a new pollination explanation and describes how its steps connect.

**I can pick a clue for a hard word.**

**Mira's Beans**

Mira's garden has a bean plant. Its flowers are small and white. A bee lands on one and drinks the sweet juice inside. Yellow pollen sticks to her fuzzy legs. The bee is careless about the pollen; she does not mind it. She flies to the next bean flower, and some pollen falls onto its sticky top. Soon that flower starts a bean pod.
