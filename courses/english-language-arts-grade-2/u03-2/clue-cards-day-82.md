# Clue cards and claim strips, day 82

Say aloud each time: "These cards are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each sheet. One set of six clue cards a pair, cut apart. The three fan cards (strategy-fan) are the three piles.

**Six clue cards (words only, no pictures).**

1. "The bee is careless about the pollen."
2. "Soon that flower starts a bean pod."
3. "care + less"
4. "hunt + ing"
5. "pod: a case that holds seeds"
6. "twig: a small thin branch"
