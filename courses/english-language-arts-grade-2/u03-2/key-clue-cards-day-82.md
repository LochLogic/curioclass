# Teacher key: Clue cards and claim strips, day 82

Teacher only. Never print this page; it answers the printed piece clue-cards-day-82.

Key: cards 1 and 2 go under sentence clue, 3 and 4 under word parts, 5 and 6 under dictionary strip. Meanings the teacher says after the sort: mind here means "care about" (not the part of you that thinks); careless means without care; pod is a case that holds seeds.

Card labels: 1 sentence clue (the clue to mind in Mira's Beans: careless about the pollen, so mind is care about); 2 sentence clue (the clue to pod); 3 word parts (the clue to careless); 4 word parts (a small word you know, and what the ending adds); 5 and 6 dictionary strip.

**Four claim strips (the teacher reads each aloud; they are not printed for children).** About Mira's Beans.

1. The bee carries pollen from one bean flower to the next. (the card says it; used as the model)
2. The bee carries seeds to the next bean flower. (pollen, not seeds)
3. The bean pod starts before the bee lands. (the card puts the landing first)
4. The bee drinks the sweet juice, and pollen sticks to her legs. (the card says it)

Children put a finger on the card's line that shows each claim is right or wrong.
