# Repair cards, day 84

Say aloud each time: "These cards are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each sheet. Plain words, no pictures on the strips. One set a group, cut apart.

**Group A (seed or pollen).** Two heading cards, "pollen dust" and "seed". Four caption strips for the pair to sort under them:

1. "Yellow dust sticks to the bee's legs." (pollen dust)
2. "A seed drops on the ground." (seed)
3. "Some dust rubs off on the next flower." (pollen dust)
4. "A seed grows into a new plant." (seed)

**Group B (so or because).** Two small cards, "so" and "because", and six sentence strips, each with a gap. The pair lays the right small card in each gap and reads the strip. Nobody explains; the pair only places the cards.

1. "The cup tipped ___ the juice spilled." (so)
2. "The juice spilled ___ the cup tipped." (because)
3. "The bell rang ___ the class lined up." (so)
4. "The class lined up ___ the bell rang." (because)
5. "The bee was hungry ___ it flew to the flower." (so)
6. "It flew to the flower ___ the bee was hungry." (because)

**Group features and focus.** No new card: the group uses the headings and paragraphs of Pollen on the Move (01-a of g2-u3-01).

**Group word strategies.** The day 83 word card and its strip; two fresh words are said aloud by the teacher: "unwrap" in the sentence "Mia will unwrap the gift" and "refill" in "Please refill the cup."

**Group diagram.** The day 83 Next Spring card and its diagram.
