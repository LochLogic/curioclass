# Day 83: A check on a new pollination card

## Teacher page

### Before class

A check: teach and hint at nothing. Cover the step chain and wall charts. Give each child two pencil colours and the answer sheet. Mark today's six (group 1 of five in cycle 14).

### The day

#### 25 min. A1: explain the steps

Say: "This is a check. Pollination moves pollen; seeds travelling is different. I will not help." Children read the Next Spring card alone (3 minutes). Ask item [[s03-A2]]; they write a few words in pencil. Record: read alone. Read item [[s03-A1]] twice. Children write the steps in boxes and so or because on arrows, in pencil (10 minutes). Scribe for a child who asks. Read the card aloud once. Children reread and add or change work in the second colour (7 minutes). Record: supported. Collect the papers; feedback comes after class (3 minutes).

#### 20 min. C1: the card's parts

Read item [[s03-A3]] twice; a few words or a drawing (3 minutes). Read items [[s03-A4]] and [[s03-A5]] twice each (2 minutes). Ask items [[s03-A6]] and [[s03-A7]]; pointing counts (2 minutes). Ask items [[s03-A8]], [[s03-A9]] and [[s03-A10]]; answers go on side 2 (3 minutes). Hand out the word card and strip. Ask items [[s03-A11]] to [[s03-A20]] one at a time, twice each; children write the meaning and the clue (10 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit

Unscored; no item. Ask: "Which fan card would you try first on a hard word?" Children tell a partner, then write one word.

#### 10 min. Quiet reread

Children reread Pollen on the Move alone.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Practice only, no marks. Children read three cycle 13 word cards aloud to a partner and say what each means. The day 82 spelling misses wait for day 84.

#### 15 min. Review: C-le continued

Retrieve -le: table, little (day 78; 1 minute). Model, map, blend and spell marble, saddle, middle and noble (6 minutes), then the first four oral card words once each (3 minutes; wrinkle and drizzle wait for day 84). Review: an open first syllable says its name (day 80); read spider and tiger (2 minutes). Star the odd part of again (1 minute). Dictate "The spider sat on the table." (2 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the six read the first four cycle 14 oral card words at your table, two minutes each; the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes: stations fill to ten and the six take the two they have not had. Tiles: marble, saddle. Sort: the day 80 cards. Reread: A Little Table.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What are the last two letters of marble?" Children say l, e.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the cards by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. The assessment at home.** Read three sentences about any plant growing, aloud twice. Your child says how the steps connect with so or because.
  **Say.** "Why does the next step happen?" Expect: because of the step before.
- **10 min. Reread.** Your child rereads Pollen on the Move, a finger under each word.
- **10 min. A hard word.** Your child names a word that stopped them and the clue that helped.
- **10 min. Words.** Say marble and saddle. Your child writes each and says the last two letters.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the quiet reread to five minutes. Longer: add an arrow to the diagram.

### Words and supports

A child may say, point or draw an answer.

### Tomorrow

After class mark, write feedback and sort papers by error letter for day 84's groups. Day 84 retries a connection on a different paragraph.
