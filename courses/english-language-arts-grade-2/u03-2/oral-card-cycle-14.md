# Oral card, cycle 14 (days 83 to 87)

The card each of the day's six reads to you, two minutes each, on cycle 14's words.

Read each word aloud: marble, middle, saddle, noble, wrinkle, drizzle. Day 83's six stop after noble; wrinkle and drizzle are taught on day 84.

Read the phrase: a noble saddle.

Tricky words, with the odd part starred: again, said.

Marks: reads each word by its syllables; keeps the letters before le in view; does not guess from the picture.
