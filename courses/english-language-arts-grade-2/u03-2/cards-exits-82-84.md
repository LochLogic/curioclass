# Exit cards, days 82 to 84

The moth card of day 82 is invented: say aloud "This card is made up for practice" and print "invented for practice" in small letters at its foot. The other rows use paragraphs of article 01-b (A Bee Visits) in The Secret World of Pollination (g2-u3-01), read from the teacher copy; they are true. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card or paragraph twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 82 (s03-X82).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A moth lands on a pale flower at night. Her long tongue uncurls and reaches the sweet juice deep inside. Pollen dusts her body. She flies away, and some pollen brushes onto the next flower." Say: "Look at the word uncurls. Which strategy card could help you with it: sentence clue, word parts or dictionary strip? Hold up one."
- **Day 82 (s03-X82b).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A moth lands on a pale flower at night. Her long tongue uncurls and reaches the sweet juice deep inside. Pollen dusts her body. She flies away, and some pollen brushes onto the next flower." Say: "What does uncurls mean here?"
- **Day 82 (s03-X82cv2).** Read aloud twice article 01-b, paragraph 3 (A special way to shake a flower). Say: "Say the steps in order."
- **Day 82 (s03-X82dv2).** Same paragraph. Say: "Why does the pollen fall out?"
- **Day 84 (s03-X84v2).** Read aloud twice article 01-b, paragraph 2 (How a bee moves pollen). Say: "Say how the steps connect, with because or so."
- **Day 84 (s03-X84bv2).** Same paragraph, read once more. Say: "Where does the pollen brush off?"
