# Exit cards, days 82 to 84

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv. Where a day has more than one row, ask them one after another, one ask each.

- **Day 82 (s03-X82).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A moth lands on a pale flower at night. Her long tongue uncurls and reaches the sweet juice deep inside. Pollen dusts her body. She flies away, and some pollen brushes onto the next flower." Say: "Look at the word uncurls. Which strategy card helps you with it: sentence clue, word parts or dictionary strip?"
- **Day 82 (s03-X82b).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A moth lands on a pale flower at night. Her long tongue uncurls and reaches the sweet juice deep inside. Pollen dusts her body. She flies away, and some pollen brushes onto the next flower." Say: "What does uncurls mean here?"
- **Day 82 (s03-X82c).** Same card, read again. Say: "Say the steps in order."
- **Day 82 (s03-X82d).** Same card. Say: "Why does the pollen reach the second flower?"
- **Day 84 (s03-X84).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "In spring a fruit tree has white flowers. A bee drinks from one, and pollen sticks to her fuzz. She flies to a flower on a second tree. Some pollen rubs off on its sticky top. Later that flower starts a small fruit." Say: "Say how the steps connect, with because or so."
- **Day 84 (s03-X84b).** Same card, read once more. Say: "Where does the pollen rub off?"
