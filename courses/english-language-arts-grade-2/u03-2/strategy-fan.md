# Strategy fan (days 82 to 84)

Three cards on a ring, each with a short instruction and a small drawing.

1. **Sentence clue.** Read the sentence around the word. What do the other words say about it?
2. **Word parts.** Cover the part at the front or the end. What is left? What does the part add?
3. **Dictionary strip.** Find the word in the strip. Compare the letters. Read what it says.

Each pair holds one set. Say: "My clue is ___."
