# Day 84: Fix a connection and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 83 papers by error letter into five piles (A, B, AB and AC, AL and AP, C) and write each child's group on the feedback row. Set out the repair cards by group. Mark today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 14) on the unit 3 record sheet. Hang the step chain. Print nothing for the exit: it is spoken. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-13.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Retrieve

Practice only, no marks. Review: look, peek and glance, verbs that differ in how you do them (grade 1, unit 1, day 22). Ask: "When would you use gigantic, and when big?" Pairs say one real use for each (2 minutes). Say aloud that day 85 begins from this.

#### 2 min. Return the papers

Hand back each child's day 83 papers, which are day 83's assessment. Each reads the feedback row and circles the one thing to change.

#### 10 min. Groups A and B (teacher)

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Group A (error A), 5 minutes: two heading cards, pollen dust and seed, and four caption strips. The group sorts each strip under its card and puts a finger on the word in the strip that tells which. Pollen sticks and rubs off; a seed grows. Group B (error B), 5 minutes: two small cards, so and because, and six strips with a gap. The group lays the right small card in each gap and reads the strip; nobody explains yet. Review: so and because in a pair of sentences (grade 1, unit 2, day 54).

Other children revise in the second color, from the feedback row. They check each arrow word on their A1: does the step before make the next step happen? If not, they change it. They also fix the C1 cells the row names. No child explains the connection aloud here.

#### 10 min. Group features and focus (teacher)

Errors AB and AC. Use the headings and paragraphs of Pollen on the Move. Ask: "Which heading takes you straight to what the bee gets? Point there." The group answers three such questions, then finds the focus of the first two paragraphs and says what joins them. Revising children work as above.

#### 6 min. Group word strategies (teacher)

Errors AL, AP, C and AB from day 82. On the word card, children cover a prefix, say what is left and what the part adds, then point to the clue words that support a meaning. For a fresh word, say it in its sentence and have the group name the clue before the meaning: unwrap (Mia will unwrap the gift), refill (Please refill the cup). Revising children work as above.

#### 6 min. Group diagram (teacher)

Error C and the diagram item. Reread the Next Spring card with the group. Each child puts a finger on the line that says what the arrows show. Others finish revising.

#### 15 min. Fresh retry

Say: "This is from a true article about bees." This is the retry; it is scored with A1 on day 85. Read paragraph 2 of 01-b (How a bee moves pollen) twice. Ask item [[s03-X84v2]]. Each child writes how the steps connect on a clean organizer, then tells a partner. While they write, stand by the six about 10 seconds each and mark: shown, shown with help (after "What does it say?") or not yet seen. Then read the paragraph once more and ask item [[s03-X84bv2]]; children tell a partner (2 minutes). Collect every organizer. Tag error A for seeds carried, B for a list of steps and C for an answer the paragraph contradicts.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write yesterday's commonest miss (error S, T or Y) beside the right word and read it aloud. Repair the commonest cycle 13 check miss (error Y): write table beside marble and say the letters of the ending, l then e, in order.

#### 8 min. Teach: -kle and -zle

Retrieve: table, marble, saddle (day 83; 1 minute). Say: "Some -le words have k or z before the le." Model, map, blend and spell ankle and puzzle, with the letters before le in view: kle and zle (4 minutes). Children read wrinkle and drizzle from the oral card, then spell puzzle on a slate (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Read the cycle 14 card

Read the title of The Stable Gate aloud. Read page 1, then pairs echo-read pages 2 and 3 (6 minutes). Pairs confirm the meaning after decoding by saying who is at the gate (3 minutes). Never guess from the picture or the first letter; map any stuck word. Dictate ankle and puzzle, then children check each on the word wall (4 minutes). Star the odd part of again (2 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, review from unit 3, day 78. Round 1 is 13 minutes: the day's six (group 2 of five in cycle 14) read the oral card at your table, two minutes each (12 of your minutes); the other 24 are at stations, eight each. Rounds 2 and 3 are 12 minutes each: stations fill to ten and the six take the two they have not had. Tiles: build stable and handle. Sort: the day 80 cards. Reread: The Stable Gate and A Little Table. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Ask: "What does the picture not tell you that the letters did?" Children tell a partner, then say it together: the words say who did what.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the strips on torn paper and copy the organizer by hand. A child may point or say an answer instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the stations.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the numbered steps in Pollen on the Move with a finger.
- **10 min. Fix it.** Say a wrong link, "The bee flies to the next flower because the pollen sticks." Your child fixes it.
  **Say.** "Which step comes first?" Expect: the pollen sticks, so she carries it.
- **5 min. Words.** Your child writes ankle and puzzle.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X84v2]] [[s03-X84bv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the retrieve and the close; keep the groups and the retry. Longer: a child says what the bee does with the pollen she takes to her nest and what links it.

### Words and supports

Pollen, seed, so, because, connect. Frames: "The pollen sticks, so ___." "The flower starts a fruit because ___." A child may point to the line instead of saying it.

### Tomorrow

The retry tells you whether children can link steps with so or because and keep pollen apart from seeds. If many said the bee carries seeds (error A), day 85 opens with a fresh card said with so or because. After class, score nothing yet: day 85 scores A1 and C1 from the day 83 papers and today's revision, outside class. Day 85 connects pollinator words with real uses and tells nearby verb and adjective meanings apart.
