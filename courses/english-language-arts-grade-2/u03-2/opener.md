# Section 3.2 opener: working out new words, and the first check (days 82 to 84)

## What the section does

Day 82 teaches three ways to work out a new word: the sentence around it, the parts inside it, and a dictionary strip. Day 83 is the first check of the unit: each child reads a new short card about pollination alone, then again with help, and explains how its steps connect and what a few words mean. Day 84 is a response day: children are grouped by what the marking showed and work on that gap.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 83 is a check day: nothing new is taught, and the child's independent reading and supported reading are kept as two records, pencil for alone and a second colour for with help. Days 82 and 84 follow the usual shape of opening repair, words, reading, model, pair work and a five-minute exit. In the foundations block the cycle 13 written check is on day 82 and the reader for cycle 14 begins on day 83.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A strategy is chosen, not guessed.** The child names the clue that helped: the sentence, a word part, or the dictionary.
- **A word part has a job.** A prefix such as un- or re- changes the meaning. It is never just letters at the front.
- **Independent and supported reading are different records.** Both are true and both are kept. Neither is a grade.
- **Pollen, not seeds.** The unit keeps pollination and seed travel apart.

## The errors, in plain words

- A strategy is named with no clue (AB).
- A word part is treated as if it meant nothing (AL).
- A meaning is given that the words do not support (AP).
- A fact contradicts the card (C).

## Marking

Shown, shown with help, not yet seen, and the two pencil colours on the check card. No reteaching happens on day 83; day 84 is where the marking is used.
