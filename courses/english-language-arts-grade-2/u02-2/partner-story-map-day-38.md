# Partner story map (day 38)

Each pair folds a sheet into a map. Across the top, one wide box: **Beginning** (who, where and when, what starts). In the middle, four narrow boxes: **First**, **Next**, **Then**, **At last**. Across the bottom, one wide box: **Ending**. Pairs fill the boxes with strips cut from card, using words from *The Mark in the Stone*. The teacher checks the beginning and ending boxes first, then holds up two maps and the class names one thing both beginning boxes hold. The map stays on the wall for days 40 to 42.
