# Day 40: Telling a new story's beginning and ending

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the strip, Lina's Pages and the student page. Mark today's six (group 3 of five) on the record sheet. No help during the check.

### The day

#### 20 min. Check: 40-RL.2.5

Say: "This story is made up for practice." Read Lina's Pages twice, children following (3 minutes). They cover it, fill the strip, then write two sentences under it (8 minutes). Partners tell each other the two sentences (4 minutes). The six come to you one at a time (5 minutes): ask item s02-A1, then item s02-A2. The others reread quietly. Collect the strips.

Shown means both sentences hold with a detail. Shown with help means one holds, or both after a prompt. Not yet seen means the jobs are swapped (error A). Mark the other strips after class.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X40 twice. Ask: "Tell me how the ending solves what the beginning started." The six answer, 20 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means the unreadable label and the clear one. Shown with help means after the prompt "What did Hana start with?" Not yet seen means a detail the card contradicts (error B).

#### 35 min. Independent reading and conferences

First, 5 minutes for day 39's exit (error B). Read: "What did Dr. Lee take from her pocket? A: A magnifying glass. B: A picture card." Children find page 7, put a finger on the line, say "I know because the text says ___" and choose. Then each child rereads one of pages 2 to 7 of The Mark in the Stone and writes "I read ___; I noticed ___." Review: the reading conference from unit 1. Hold six conferences of four minutes: the six read four sentences twice; unscored.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six cards: oil, boy, out, cow, point, toy. Children read each and say the vowel sound.

#### 15 min. Reread, sort and spell

Pairs reread Coins for a Toy once and sort the twelve cards under oi / oy and ou / ow (6 minutes). Say boy and mouse; children write each, then "The boy has a toy." (4 minutes). Review: dictionary pages. Each child checks one word on the cycle 7 page: find the first letter, find the entry, compare, correct (5 minutes).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Group 3 of five at the oral card, two minutes each; stations of tiles, sort and reread.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which word did you check, and where?" Expect: a word and its entry on the dictionary page.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a sheet into three boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed story: read a short story you know aloud twice. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The strip, the stations and the oral card.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Read a short story you know aloud twice. Your child says what the beginning introduces and what the ending concludes, with no help.
  **Say.** "What does the beginning introduce, and what does the ending conclude?" Expect: the beginning starts a job or a problem and the ending finishes it.
- **10 min. Read.** Show oil, boy, out and cow on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say boy and house. Your child writes each and checks the vowel spelling on the cards.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X40]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the sort; keep the check and the exit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Beginning, ending, introduces, concludes. Frames: "The beginning introduces ___." "The ending concludes ___." Drawing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 returns to Lina's Pages and asks the six question words. Day 41 hands back day 40's assessment strips. Day 42 groups children by the letters recorded on days 40 and 41.
