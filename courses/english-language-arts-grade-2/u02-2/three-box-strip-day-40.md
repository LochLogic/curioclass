# Three-box strip (day 40)

A strip of paper folded into three boxes labelled **Beginning**, **Middle** and **Ending**. After the story is read aloud twice, the child draws or writes one key idea in each box, with the text covered. Below the strip, two lines: **The beginning introduces ___** and **The ending concludes ___**. The teacher collects the strip as evidence and does not help.
