# Day 39: Going back to the line

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child, and pages 1 to 5, two choice question cards and Coins for a Toy for each pair. Mark today's six (group 2 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Have day 38's exit marks beside you: error A is the miss to repair. Cover the five return-card steps on the board. Set three stations: tiles, sort cards, a reread of Coins for a Toy. Keys: key-question-cards-day-39.md, key-sort-cards-cycle-7.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 38's exit (error A, the ending named as the start), lay two strips beside the large story map's Beginning box, which holds "Amara finds a curved mark and wonders what it is": "Dr. Lee compares the stone with a picture card" and "Amara tells Ben what the mark is." Ask: "Which strip finishes the job the Beginning started?" Children point to the second and say why: it answers what the mark is. The first is a middle step, At last. Otherwise partners say to each other what each step of the talk routine asks them to do: turn, pair, link, clarify.

#### 8 min. Reread pages 8 and 9

Read pages 8 and 9 aloud while children follow the pictures (about 165 words, 90 seconds): the shell lying in the mud, then the stone with the hollow in it. Do not stop on trace or mold; day 57 returns to them. Pairs reread page 9 turn about, a sentence each (about 60 words, 3 minutes). Review: the question words who, what, where, when, why and how, from unit 1 and day 35. Ask the class (2 minutes): "What does Dr. Lee say she cannot tell Amara for sure?" Expect: what kind of shell animal it was.

#### 12 min. Return to the text

Uncover the return card and read it once: 1. Hear the question and both choices. 2. Find the page. 3. Put a finger on the line. 4. Say "I know because the text says ___." 5. Choose A or B. Say: "A choice is only right if a line says so. If you cannot find the line, read the page again."

Model with a who question. Read: "Who said 'Good eyes'? A: Amara. B: Dr. Lee." Find page 7, put a finger on "At last Dr. Lee smiled. 'Good eyes,' she said." Say "I know because the text says Dr. Lee smiled and she said good eyes." Choose B.

Model with a why question. Read: "Why will Dr. Lee send a picture of the stone to the museum? A: A scientist there may be able to name it. B: She wants to keep the stone there." Page 9: "She may be able to name it." Choose A.

**Ask.** "Which line proves your choice?" Class does a where question: "Where did Dr. Lee write the place and the day? A: On a paper label. B: In Amara's notebook." Expect page 10, "on a paper label"; the notebook is where Amara draws. Error B is a choice the line contradicts: if a child picks B, say "Show me the line" and let the line decide.

#### 12 min. Pairs practise

Each pair has two of the six choice question cards and pages 1 to 5. For each card both children take a turn: one finds the page and puts a finger on the line, the other says "I know because the text says ___" and chooses (about 5 minutes a card). No answer is passable without the line. Move among pairs and ask one for the line. Key: card 1, page 1, "A guide named Dr. Lee met the group at the gate." Card 2, page 1, "Each child got a soft brush and a paper bag." Card 3, page 2, "Amara walked to a pile of broken gray stones." Card 4, page 1, "On a cool, bright morning, Amara's class went to a fossil park." Card 5, page 3, "But first she wanted to be sure." Card 6, page 5, "Next, Amara brushed the stone gently."

Check: each pair names a page and reads the line before it chooses.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which line proves your choice?" Turn: each child tells a partner the line from a card (2 minutes). Pair: two pairs with different cards join, and each reads its line while the other pair says if the line proves the choice (2 minutes). Link: "You said ___; I would add ___." Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Then hear today's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), and after each one child links or asks. Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Write one answer

Each child writes on the student page "I know because the text says ___.", copying a few words from the line they found (3 minutes). Partners check the words against the page (2 minutes). A child may point to the line and tell you.

Check: the words come from the page and the sentence ends with a mark.

#### 5 min. Compose a question

Each pair writes on a slip one question with a question word and its answer in a few words, about the story and not a choice question (4 minutes). They read it to the next pair, who answer it (1 minute). Unscored: the slips stay in the folder and prepare day 41.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X39 aloud twice. Ask: "Say the words that tell why Arlo numbers the sketches." The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means the words of the card (so he can find its note). Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the card say?" Not yet seen means a reason the card does not give (error B). Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Review: the ending -s, from grade 1. Show six word cards one at a time: toys, boys, cows, points, oil, loud. Children read each aloud. If one drops the s, underline the ending, read it alone, then read the whole word (error Z). For oil and loud, say the vowel sound before blending.

#### 15 min. Read: the cycle 7 reader

Write their and people. Review: yesterday you starred eir in their and eo and le in people. Children say the word with a finger on each starred part (2 minutes). Say the title of the reader yourself; children do not read it. Read page 1 aloud, a sentence at a time, and the class echoes it (2 minutes). Then pairs read pages 2 to 6 turn about, a page each (about 57 words, 6 minutes), covering the picture until they have read the line, then checking the line against the picture (error X guesses from the picture). Check meaning after decoding (5 minutes).

**Ask.** "What does Roy want, and how does he get the cash?" Expect: he wants a toy boat; he does a job, picking up sticks for the people on his street.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes): the grade 1 words, the cycle 6 and cycle 7 words, then the sentence "The boy put the toy in the house." Each says the vowel sound in each word. Put is a grade 1 word; if a child stalls on it, say it and mark the child on boy, toy and house. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build point, Roy, loud and how, then change one letter. Sort: the twelve cards under oi / oy and ou / ow, then a partner says a word and the child points to its pile. Reread: pairs read Coins for a Toy twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child in a station reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "What did the letters tell you that the picture did not?" Expect: any word the words gave and the picture did not, such as that the jar has just a bit of cash.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the six question cards on torn paper and the return card on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. One copy of pages 1 to 5: read each line aloud and let pairs point to it on the class copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed story: read the lines aloud from a story you have. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The slips, the stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Back to the line.** Read page 6 of The Mark in the Stone together. Ask the question with two choices: "Who held up a magnifying glass? A: Dr. Lee. B: Amara." Your child finds the line and says the frame.
  **Say.** "Which line proves your choice?" Expect: Dr. Lee held up a magnifying glass; the child says "I know because the text says ___."
- **10 min. Read.** Show point, loud, how and toy on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say oil, cow and house. Your child writes each and checks the vowel spelling.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X39]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card each in pairs practise and a short reread of the reader; keep the return card, the model and the exit. Longer: a pair writes a choice question with a wrong choice the page contradicts and trades it with another pair.

### Words and supports

Line, page, choose, question, because, their, people. Frames: "I know because the text says ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing and drawing are answers. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 is the first check day: a new short story is read aloud twice, children fill a three-box strip with no help, and the six explain it to you. Today's exit (error B, a reason the card does not give) is repaired on day 40 after the check, in the reading part.
