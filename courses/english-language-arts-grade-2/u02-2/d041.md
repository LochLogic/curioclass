# Day 41: Six questions, back to the text

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the strip, Lina's Pages, the student page and The Loud Band. Mark today's six (group 4 of five) on the record sheet. No help during the check. Key: key-sort-cards-cycle-7.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 40's assessment strips, no marks shown. Review: the question words, from day 35. Each child asks a partner one question about The Mark in the Stone.

#### 3 min. Read again

Say: "This story is made up for practice." Read Lina's Pages; children follow.

#### 17 min. Six questions

Ask items s02-A3 to s02-A8 in order, each said twice; children write or draw each answer in its box, text in view (8 minutes). Partners point to the line for one box (3 minutes). The six answer two questions each aloud (6 minutes). Collect the strips.

Shown means the text's answer. Shown with help means after "Show me the line." Not yet seen means a contradicted answer (error B) or a fragment (error AD).

#### 8 min. Ask and answer your own question

Model with page 10 of The Mark in the Stone: "How did Amara keep her stone safe?" She wrapped it in soft paper. Each child writes a who, what, why or how question and its answer on the strip's back (6 minutes); a partner answers it aloud (2 minutes). Unscored.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 11 of The Mark in the Stone aloud twice, showing the picture. Ask (s02-X41v2): "Ask me a where or when question that page 11 answers." The six answer, 20 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a question page 11 answers (where are Amara and Ben: on the bus). Shown with help means after "Which word asks where?" Not yet seen means a question page 11 does not answer (error B).

#### 12 min. Paired reread

Pairs reread Coins for a Toy twice, a page each, the second time smoothly.

#### 10 min. Discussion practice

Question: "Which line proves your answer?" Turn, pair, link ("You said ___; I would add ___."), clarify ("Which part do you mean?"). Use the wait signal.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write their and people; star eir, then eo and le. Children say each word with a finger on each starred part (error AA).

#### 15 min. Read and write the transfer passage

Pairs read The Loud Band, a sentence each (6 minutes), and say who plays (3 minutes). Dictate "The people sit down." (6 minutes). Unscored.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Group 4 of five at the oral card, two minutes each. Stations: tiles, sort and reread.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which question word was hardest?" Expect: a word and a page line.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a sheet into six boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed story: read a short story you know aloud twice. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The strip, the stations and the oral card.

- **20 min. Assessment.** Read a short story you know aloud twice. Your child answers a who, what, where, when, why and how question about it, with no help.
  **Say.** "Which line gives your answer?" Expect: a line that matches the answer.
- **10 min. Read.** Show loud, point, boy and cow on cards. Your child reads each and says the vowel sound.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say toy, out and house. Your child writes each and checks the vowel spelling.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X41v2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the discussion practice.

### Words and supports

Who, what, where, when, why, how, line. Drawing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 hands back day 41's assessment strips and groups children by the letters recorded (A, B or AD) for a retry.
