# Six-box response strip (day 41)

A sheet folded into six numbered boxes. Under each number, one question word: **1 Who**, **2 What**, **3 Where**, **4 When**, **5 Why**, **6 How**. After the story is read aloud, the teacher asks one question for each box; the child writes or draws the answer in the matching box. The strip is collected as evidence. The child also writes one question of their own on the back, with its answer.
