# Choice question cards (day 39)

Six cards about *The Mark in the Stone*. Pairs take two, find the page, put a finger on the line, say "I know because the text says ___", then choose A or B.

1. Who met the group at the gate? A: Dr. Lee. B: Amara.
2. What did each child get? A: a soft brush and a paper bag. B: a shovel and a pail.
3. Where did Amara find the mark? A: at a pile of broken gray stones. B: at the edge of the water.
4. When did the class go to the park? A: on a cool, bright morning. B: at night.
5. Why did Amara not shout at once? A: She wanted to be sure first. B: She was afraid of Dr. Lee.
6. How did Amara clean the stone? A: She brushed it gently. B: She washed it in a stream.

Key: 1 A (page 1), 2 A (page 1), 3 A (page 2), 4 A (page 1), 5 A (page 3), 6 A (page 5). Mix the order of A and B when you print.
