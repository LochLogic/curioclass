# Cycle 7 oral card (days 38 to 42)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 38 only the first two lines are used, because no cycle 7 pattern was taught before that day. Children read the words and say each vowel sound; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 6 patterns (ar, or, er, ir, ur).** car, park, corn, horn, her, bird, turn, fern
- **Cycle 7 patterns (oi, oy, ou, ow).** oil, point, boy, toy, out, loud, house, cow, how
- **A sentence.** The boy put the toy in the house.

Partner word card for day 42: oil, boy, loud, house, cow, how, point, toy. Dictated words: round, enjoy.
