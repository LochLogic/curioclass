# Beginning dictionary page, cycle 7 (day 40)

One page for each pair, with nine entries in alphabetical order. Guide words at the top: **boil** and **toy**. Children use it to check one spelling.

**boil**, verb. To heat water until it bubbles.

**boy**, noun. A young male child.

**cow**, noun. A large farm animal that gives milk.

**house**, noun. A building where people live.

**loud**, adjective. Having a big sound.

**oil**, noun. A thick, smooth liquid that is slippery.

**out**, word. Not inside.

**point**, noun. The sharp end of something.

**toy**, noun. A thing that a child plays with.

Steps to say: find the first letter; find the entry; compare the letters with your spelling; correct it. "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
