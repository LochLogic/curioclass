# Day 42 group record

A sheet with the children's names down the left and four columns: **Recorded letter** (from days 40 and 41: A, B, AD or none), **Group** (1 to 4), **Fresh retry** (strip or six-box sheet, and the date) and **Shown, shown with help, not yet seen**. Group 1 is letter A, group 2 is letter B, group 3 is letter AD or shown with help, and group 4 is no letter (extension). Keep it in your folder.
