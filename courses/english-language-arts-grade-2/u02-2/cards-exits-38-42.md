# Exit cards, days 38 to 42

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards; you read each twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 38 (s02-X38).** "Ivy wants to find the fossil sketch that fits her label. She checks the curves in two sketches. She chooses the matching one and pins it beside the label." Ask: "Tell me how the first part and the last part of this card work together."
- **Day 39 (s02-X39).** "Arlo puts a number beside each fossil sketch so he can find its note." Ask: "Say the words that tell why Arlo numbers the sketches."
- **Day 40 (s02-X40).** "Hana cannot read a smudged label. She compares it with her clean note, then writes it clearly. A visitor can now read it." Ask: "Tell me how the ending solves what the beginning started."
- **Day 41 (s02-X41).** "Raj draws the fossil at the table before lunch. He draws it twice to include the faint lines." Ask: "Ask me a where or when question that this card answers."
- **Day 42 (s02-X42).** "A label falls from a drawing. Pia reads her note, then replaces the label. The display is ready again." Ask: "Tell me how the ending solves the opening problem."
