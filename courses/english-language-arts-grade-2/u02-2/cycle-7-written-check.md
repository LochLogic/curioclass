# Cycle 7 written check (day 42, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 38 to 41.

1. "The pup ____ a bone." (found)
2. "Wrap the food in ____." (foil)
3. "The king wore a ____." (crown)
4. "Will you ____ us at the table?" (join)

Tag wrong spellings: "fownd", "foyl", "croun" and "joyn" each write a taught vowel correspondence wrongly (Y). After the check is marked, dictate "round" and "enjoy" as unscored practice.
