# Day 159: A school story, and the year-end reading check for the last fifteen children

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hear the fifteen children not heard on day 158, in the same order. Nobody is heard twice. Day 158's assessment marks stay on the record sheet; today's are added beside them. Keep independent and supported reading apart.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is taught today. You may pass any ask." Mark every ask shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Directions

Everyone writes a short story on the prompt [[c2-A159n]]. Today's first six children are heard while they write; the rest write on. The last nine are heard after.

#### 35 min. Narrative with first conferences

Read the prompt aloud twice. Give 5 minutes to draw or plan, then 30 minutes of writing. In those 30 minutes hear six children, 5 minutes each: 4 minutes of asks and 1 minute to change the child. In order: the purpose [[c2-A159a]], 20 seconds; what happens [[c2-A159b]], 40; mostly about [[c2-A159c]], 25; the start read aloud [[c2-A159d]], 45; the sense check [[c2-A159e]], 25; the reread [[c2-A159f]], 20; then three of the seven word cards, 15 seconds each, from the child's place in the order: [[c2-A159g]] [[c2-A159h]] [[c2-A159i]] [[c2-A159j]] [[c2-A159k]] [[c2-A159l]] [[c2-A159m]]. A child who cannot finish in 5 minutes stops; mark what was heard and put the rest on the missing-evidence list for day 160.

#### 45 min. Conferences, last nine

Nine children, 5 minutes each, the same asks. The rest read the cycle 26 reader quietly, then write on.

#### 10 min. Finish writing

All finish their story. A child who has no ending writes one last line. Collect the pages.

#### 10 min. Records

Fill in the range record: a story or poem and an article or steps for each child heard, with independent and supported reading marked apart.

#### 5 min. Reread

Children reread their own story to a partner. Unmarked.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs say the best moment in a partner's story. Collect every card.

After class, list the gaps by letter: a guessed word A, word by word B, a misread left C, a vowel pattern D, an ending E, an unusual sound F or G, the topic R, a liking for a purpose S, evidence from home K, a dropped prefix W, a story with no action or feeling O, no time words P, and no ending Q. They go to day 160 with day 158's.

### Other rooms

One child: write for 25 minutes, then hear the battery in 6. A child may say the story and you write it, or draw and label; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write five short words on paper.
**Skip.** The word list.

- **30 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read the prompt: "The lights go out for a few minutes. Write what happens, what you do, what you feel and how it ends." Your child writes or tells a short story. Help only with a stuck word.
  **Say.** "How did it end?" Expect: a last event, such as the lights came back.
- **10 min. Words.** Write stake, bawl, deny, patted and hood. Your child reads each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tell the story aloud and write the ending. Longer: draw the best moment and label it.

### Words and supports

Words: story, ending, first, then, at last. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. At last ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 160 reteaches the gaps of days 158 and 159 in groups and ends with a fresh try and a record for grade 3.
