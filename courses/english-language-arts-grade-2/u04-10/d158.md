# Day 158: Year-end reading and word check, first fifteen children

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the thirty children in a fixed hearing order; mark fifteen for today and fifteen for day 159. Nobody is heard twice. The day 153 -ous and -us record is cited; those words are not sampled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Day 157's exit (E157, s04-X157 and s04-X157b) comes back. Show two fresh sentences: "We should add a jar. It saves a long walk." and "We should add a jar because it saves a long walk." Children say which has the link word and read it. Mark nothing.

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is taught today. You may pass any ask." Seat today's first seven near you; the rest reread the cycle 26 reader.

#### 15 min. Silent reading

The fifteen of today each read their own two cards silently: the story for an odd place in the order, the poem for an even one, and the informational card by the place rule on the cards file. Others read the reader.

#### 15 min. Written station

All thirty write; all are marked. Say each word twice and give about 40 seconds a word [[c2-A158n]]. Then the strip on side 2, with the printed dictionary [[c2-A158o]].

#### 35 min. Conferences, first seven

Seven children, 5 minutes each: 4 minutes of asks and 1 minute to change the child. In order: the purpose [[c2-A158a]], 20 seconds; what happens [[c2-A158b]], 40; mostly about [[c2-A158c]], 25; the start read aloud [[c2-A158d]], 45; the sense check [[c2-A158e]], 25; the reread [[c2-A158f]], 20; then three of the seven word cards, 15 seconds each, the three starting at the child's place in the order: [[c2-A158g]] [[c2-A158h]] [[c2-A158i]] [[c2-A158j]] [[c2-A158k]] [[c2-A158l]] [[c2-A158m]]. A child who cannot finish in 5 minutes stops; mark what was heard and put the rest on the missing-evidence list for day 160.

#### 40 min. Conferences, last eight

Eight children, 5 minutes each, the same asks. The rest read and build quietly.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs say one word they read today and how. Collect every card; tomorrow's fifteen must not see them.

After class, list the gaps by letter: a guessed word A, word by word B, a misread left C, a vowel pattern D, an ending E, an unusual sound F or G, a spelling H, the dictionary I, the topic R, a liking for a purpose S, evidence from home K and a dropped prefix W. They go to day 160.

### Other rooms

One child: hear the battery in 6 minutes. No printed cards: copy the words large. A child may say the strip answer and you write it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write five short words on paper.
**Skip.** The strip.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read a short paragraph about how rain fills a puddle aloud once. Your child says why someone would read it, then reads it to you. Help only with a stuck word, after three seconds.
  **Say.** "What is it mostly about?" Expect: the one topic of the paragraph, not one detail.
- **10 min. Words.** Write plump, haunt, spooky, remind and lifted. Your child reads each.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads the last two sentences so it sounds like talking.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the first reading and the words. Longer: a child reads a second article for fun, unmarked.

### Words and supports

Words: story, poem, article, steps, wrong. Frames: "I read this to find out ___." "It is mostly about ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where an ask allows; record the support used and keep independent and supported reading apart.

### Tomorrow

Day 159 hears the other fifteen on a second set and the whole class writes a short story. Day 158's marks go to day 160 with day 159's.
