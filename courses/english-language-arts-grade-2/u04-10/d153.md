# Day 153: Recount a tale, say its lesson and read two voices

## Teacher page

### Before class

Carry day 152's exit marks to the record sheet. Make six groups of five and mark children 25 to 30 for the oral card. Keep card F2 face down. Asks by group: 1, [[s04-A29]] and [[s04-A30]]; 2, [[s04-A31]] and [[s04-A32]]; 3, [[s04-A33]] and [[s04-A34]]; 4, [[s04-A29]] and [[s04-A31]]; 5, [[s04-A30]] and [[s04-A33]]; 6, [[s04-A32]] and [[s04-A34]].

### The day

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: a recount, a lesson and two voices, in whole sentences, on a new made-up card. No help; any child may pass. Collect the student pages at the end of the day. Review: whole sentences from day 60.

#### 5 min. Read F2 aloud

Read card F2 aloud once (30 seconds) and say where each group's copy waits. The rest is quiet reading. Review: recount from day 119.

#### 30 min. Group sittings (T4, T5, T6)

Groups 1 to 4 come in turn, 7.5 minutes each: five children, two asks of 45 seconds. Each ask is heard from ten children; the sitting record names them. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Out of order is error A, a lesson no event holds is error B, one voice or a swapped want is error C, a fragment is error P. The others read a tale.

#### 15 min. Quiet reading

Groups 5 and 6 sit the same way, 7.5 minutes each. The others read a tale or poem they choose. The heard column comes from the voice reading in [[s04-A31]].

#### 5 min. Close

Each child names a liked tale to a partner.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

None is routed here. On slates build bonus and joyous and say each ending. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 26

Five minutes each, alone. Say each of four words, use it, say it again [[s04-A109]]. Then the sentence in short parts [[s04-A110]]. Then the entry strip: check the draft and correct it [[s04-A111]]. A pattern not carried over is error R; a draft left alone is error S.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 25 to 30 read the oral card, two minutes each as one turn holding [[s04-A103]] to [[s04-A108]]; no new word, so no model. Others reread the reader with tiles. A first-letter guess is error Q, a misread error AE, an unchecked misreading error AD.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws a sentence with a cycle word from the reader, then reads it to a partner. Not scored.

#### 13 min. Close

Reread the reader in pairs.

### Other rooms

Read card F2 aloud twice; a child may answer aloud or by gesture.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: read card F2 aloud twice.
**Skip.** The tiles.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Read card F2 aloud twice. Your child tells the story in order, says the lesson using one event, and reads Heron's and Duck's lines in two voices.
- **10 min. Tell it.** Tell a short story in order: first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What does Heron want, and what does Duck want?" Expect: two different wants.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes bonus and joyous and says the ending of each.
- **5 min. Last.** Your child reads one favourite tale line aloud in two voices.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sittings and the written check. Longer: a child reads the narrator's lines too.

### Words and supports

Recount, lesson, voice, want. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "The lesson is ___ because ___." Pointing is an answer; repeat an ask when asked and record the support.

### Tomorrow

Day 154 is the second check: Fox in the Well, V3 and V4, one thing the same and one different. The check sheets are marked after class and the commonest miss goes to day 155; the commonest F2 miss goes to a table on day 157.
