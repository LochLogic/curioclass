# Day 160: Fix the year-end gaps, a fresh try, and the record for grade 3

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 158's assessment and day 159's assessment are handed back here. Sort the marks by gap letter A to I, K, O to S and W. Make groups of up to four by letter; a group of one joins the nearest letter. Put the missing-evidence children first for the spoken try.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "Today we fix what the checks showed, then try one fresh card. Nothing is graded." Show each child their group.

#### 40 min. Groups

Three rounds of 13 minutes. Each group gets a one-minute move for its letter, then practice on the same pattern with new words, never the check words. A, B and C: cover the first letter and blend; echo a line; read a line with one planted wrong word. D, E, F and G: sort cards by pattern; build the base then the ending with tiles; say the usual sound then the other; star the unusual part. W: put the prefix on a flap over the base word. H and I: sort, then build a new word with tiles; a guide-word hunt. K, R and S: point to the line that says it; ask what all the sentences are about; the frame "I read this to find out ___." O, P and Q: add one thing you did and one thing you felt; number the events and add a time word; ask how it ended. A group never takes spelling dictation.

#### 15 min. Pair try

All thirty, new cards. In pairs, one child builds each word with tiles as the partner says it once [[c2-X160f]], 6 minutes. Then the partner reads the two cards aloud and says one thing both share and one thing that is different [[c2-X160b]], and the pencil jar choice with its two facts [[c2-X160a]], 9 minutes in all. You listen to as many pairs as you can and mark them.

#### 40 min. Spoken try

Ten children, 4 minutes each: those on the missing-evidence list first, then those whose letters are most often marked. Ask the reading [[c2-X160c]], 90 seconds, then the two word cards, 15 seconds each [[c2-X160d]] [[c2-X160e]]. The rest read the cycle 26 reader quietly. A child not reached goes on the grade 3 handoff as not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Practice only, on the earlier school text, never check evidence. A child says one comment; the partner links to it, then asks a clarifying question; swap.

#### 7 min. Handoff record

Fill in the grade 3 record: independent and supported band reading apart, any incomplete digital contribution or recording, patterns still insecure, and what changed on the retry.

#### 5 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one thing they can do now that they could not in September.

### Other rooms

One child: take the first group move you need, then the tile words and the reading card in 10 minutes. Pointing and a scribe count; record the support used.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on scraps of paper.
**Skip.** The pair try.

- **10 min. Fix.** Say a word with a prefix, such as unlock. Your child covers un, reads lock, then reads the whole word.
  **Say.** "Which part is the prefix?" Expect: un, the part before the base word.
- **20 min. Read.** Read a short page aloud together. Your child tells you what it was mostly about.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X160a]] [[c2-X160b]] [[c2-X160c]] [[c2-X160d]] [[c2-X160e]] [[c2-X160f]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the spoken try and the record. Longer: write one more story ending and trade it.

### Words and supports

Words: share, different, link, ask. Frames: "Both ___, but ___." "I agree because ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count; record the support used.

### Tomorrow

The course ends. The handoff record goes forward with the marks from days 158 to 160 for the grade 3 teacher.
