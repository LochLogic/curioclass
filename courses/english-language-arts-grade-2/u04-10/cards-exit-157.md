# Exit card, day 157

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 157 (s04-X157).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Choice: set a shallow water dish with stones by the school bed. A: Pollinators need a safe place to sip. B: The dish is round." Show the two facts on strips. Say: "Mark the fact that helps the choice."
- **Day 157 (s04-X157b).** Same card. Say: "Say one sentence with an opinion, a link word and the fact that helps."
