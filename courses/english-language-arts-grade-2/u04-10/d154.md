# Day 154: Say what two versions share and where they differ

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 153's assessment is handed back to the record sheet: the sitting marks, the oral card and the check sheets. Make six groups of five. Keep the V3 and V4 card face down. Put each packet in its envelope on the desk.

### The day

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: one thing both versions share and one thing that differs, with the words that show it. The card is new and made up. No help; any child may pass. Collect the student pages at the end of the day. Review: Both ___, but ___ from day 23.

#### 5 min. Read V3 and V4 aloud

Read V3, then V4, aloud once. Say where each group's printed card waits. Review: versions from day 121.

#### 30 min. Group sittings (T7)

Groups 1 to 4 come in turn, 7.5 minutes each: five children, two asks of 45 seconds, each child hearing both. Ask [[s04-A35]], then [[s04-A36]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only differences named is error E; only a shared detail named is error D. The others read a tale or article alone.

#### 15 min. Quiet reading

Groups 5 and 6 sit the same way, 7.5 minutes each. The others read a tale or article they choose; no heard column today.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs tell one story they heard this week.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

None is routed here. On slates build marvelous and chorus with tiles and say each ending. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 30 min. Independent work and W3 scoring

Pairs reread the cycle 26 reader, then sort the word cards crocus, bonus, enormous, joyous, circus and octopus under -us or -ous, and look one up in the printed dictionary. No help. Score each packet at one minute a child on [[s04-A41]] to [[s04-A45]]: topic, opinion, reasons, link words, ending. A missing topic is error H, no opinion I, an irrelevant reason J, no link word K, no ending L.

#### 18 min. Flexible groups

Three rounds of 6 minutes. Groups of ten by the latest recorded letter (R, S, Q, AB to AE). One group sits with you: map are, they and says, blend, read Gus and the Log, spell with tiles. The other twenty reread the reader by pairs or build with tiles.

#### 7 min. Close

Pairs read a reader sentence aloud.

### Other rooms

Read V3 and V4 twice; a child may point to a line.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: read the two versions aloud twice.
**Skip.** The word sort.

- **15 min. The assessment.** Read two short versions of one story aloud, twice each. Your child says one thing that happens in both and one thing that differs.
  **Say.** "What is the same and what is different at the end?" Expect: one shared event and two different endings.
- **10 min. Sort.** Your child sorts crocus, bonus, enormous and joyous under -us or -ous.
- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads one favorite page aloud.
- **5 min. Tell.** Your child says one story they heard this week.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sittings. Longer: a child names a second difference with the words that show it.

### Words and supports

Version, same, different, ending. Frames: "Both ___." "In one ___, but in the other ___." Repeat an ask when asked and record the support.

### Tomorrow

Day 155 is the packet celebration: sharing and reading for classmates, the cycle 26 sheets handed back, and the poem retry for children 16 to 30. The commonest V3 and V4 miss goes to a table on day 157, and the packet notes to day 157 for the link word.
