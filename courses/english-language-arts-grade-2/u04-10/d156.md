# Day 156: Compare two articles and say why a reason fits its point

## Teacher page

### Before class

Carry day 155's retry marks to the record sheet. Mark the six children for the heard column. Keep cards Q and R face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say what is checked: the most important point of two new articles and a reason for each. The cards are new and made up. No help; any child may pass. Collect the student pages at the end of the day. Review: main idea and details from day 138.

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say how the four strips work: each is a line to finish with a few words. Say that none is shown filled. Say that they should read each card with a finger under the words.

#### 25 min. Sitting (I3, I4)

Each child reads Q and R, then writes the strips for [[s04-A37]] and [[s04-A38]] (15 minutes) and for [[s04-A39]] and [[s04-A40]] (10 minutes). Mark them after class from the page: a point both cards share, the two main points, a reason from the same card. A single detail for a point is error F; a reason from the other card is error G; only differences or only likenesses is error D or E. The six of the heard column read their strips to you, 1 minute each, inside the 25. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 15 min. Pairs

Pairs read their strips to each other and each says one thing the partner wrote that they did not. Practice, not marked. Review: the talk routine from unit 1.

#### 10 min. Close

Pairs draw one thing a pollinator could use at the school, and label it. Not scored.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

None is routed here. On slates build chorus and fungus with tiles and say each ending. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three rounds of 15 minutes. Groups of ten by the latest recorded letter (R, S, Q, AB to AE). One group sits with you: map the irregular words, blend, read Gus and the Log, spell on tiles, reread. The other twenty reread the cycle 26 reader in pairs, or build words on tiles with the printed dictionary. The cycle 26 written and oral records are scored after class.

#### 10 min. Close

Pairs read one new cycle word sentence aloud.

### Other rooms

Read cards Q and R aloud twice; a child may answer each strip aloud, and the scribe writes it.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: read two short paragraphs about different ways to help bees.
**Skip.** The tiles.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read two short articles aloud twice each. Your child says what each is mostly about and finishes: "Both ___." "One says ___, but the other says ___."
  **Say.** "How does one reason help its point?" Expect: a reason from the same article.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws and labels something a bee could use.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child builds chorus and bonus and says each ending.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the strips. Longer: a child names a second reason.

### Words and supports

Point, reason, both, different. Frames: "Both ___." "Q ___, but R ___." "A clump helps because ___." Read the cards aloud, accept a drawing and a scribe, and record the support.

### Tomorrow

Day 157 is the closing day. Four tables take the commonest miss from days 152, 153, 154 and today, new cards S and T are read cold, and one last opinion sentence is the exit. The unit ends.
