# Unit 4.10: Group sittings, a celebration and the retry (days 153 to 157)

## What the section does
The class closes the unit. Groups sit with the teacher for a story card, a pair of versions, a pair of articles and a poem. Children share their packets, read for classmates, and finish with one fresh opinion sentence.

## How the days run
- Day 153: read F2; group sittings; cycle 26 written check.
- Day 154: read V3 and V4; group sittings; independent work.
- Day 155: packet sharing and reading for classmates (a celebration; no new evidence).
- Day 156: sitting on two articles, Q and R.
- Day 157: tables of the commonest misses, a reading retry on two new cards, and the closing exit.

## The ideas at an adult's level
A closing check samples what a child can do alone, then once more with a table's help. A right retry shows the child has learned the move; it never replaces the first mark.

## The errors in plain words
- A comparison that leaves out a difference.
- A reason fastened to the wrong point.
- A sound device named without its effect.
- Characters read in one voice.

## Marking
Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 157 records the retry beside, never over, the first mark.
