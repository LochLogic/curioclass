# Day 157: Fix what the checks showed and say one last opinion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 156's assessment is handed back to the record sheet: the strip marks. Sort the marks into the commonest miss from day 153 (the story card, error C), day 154 (the two versions, error D), day 156 (the two articles, error G) and day 152 (the poem, error O). If one shows no clear miss, give that table to the next largest count. Mark the six for the heard column.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Each child sees their own written marks and notes; say which table they sit at. Then the link-word repair from day 154's packet notes (3 minutes): show two printed sentences about a made-up class job, "We should keep the lid on. Bees get in." and "We should keep the lid on because bees get in." The class circles the word that joins the reason to the opinion in the second and reads it aloud. Review: link words because and so from grade 1, day 134. A missing link word is error K.

#### 40 min. Tables and retry

Four tables of up to ten children take a 10-minute turn each, 40 minutes in all. At the table show the wrong sentence beside the right one from the pairs sheet, and the children match each wrong sentence to its right pair, then read the right one aloud. Table C, day 153's miss: Cat and Bird. Table D, day 154's: the boy and the dog. Table O, day 152's: "Tap, tap". Table G, day 156's, a case for today: three points, "Pots need holes." "Pots need a sunny place." "Pots need soil." and two reasons, "Water runs out so roots stay dry." "Leaves use light to make food." Children draw an arrow from each reason to the point it explains, then read the pair aloud. Review: the most important point and its reason from day 135. The pairs for D and G stay in the folder.

While a table waits, pairs read cards S and T cold and fill the response sheet: each child draws one thing card S asks a garden to add and one thing card T asks a garden to keep, labels each with a word from its card, and reads the two labels to the partner (1 minute each). A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark.

#### 10 min. Exit and opinion retry

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice, show the two fact strips, and ask [[s04-X157]]; children mark on their page (2 minutes). The six of the heard column say [[s04-X157b]] to you, 1 minute each. A fact that does not help is error J; no link word is error K; a fragment is error P. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Close

Each child tells a partner one thing they can now do. The unit ends.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

None is routed here. On slates build circus and chorus with tiles and say each ending. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three rounds of 15 minutes. Groups of ten by the latest recorded letter (R, S, Q, AB to AE). One group sits with you: map the irregular words, blend, read Gus and the Log, spell on tiles, reread. The other twenty work at two stations: partner reread of the cycle 26 reader, and tile builds with the printed dictionary. Words for retry come from each group's own taught patterns: bonus, crocus, enormous, joyous, octopus.

#### 10 min. Close

Pairs read one new cycle word sentence aloud.

### Other rooms

Read cards S and T aloud twice; answers are said and a scribe writes. The pairs sheet is read aloud and the right sentence is pointed to.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strips: say the two facts aloud.
**Skip.** The words.

- **10 min. Match.** Read the wrong and right pair: "Cat says, come and play. Bird says, I must rest." Your child says who wants what.
  **Say.** "What does each one want?" Expect: Cat wants to play and Bird wants to rest.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child builds crocus and joyous with scraps and says each ending.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X157]] and [[s04-X157b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep your child's own table and the exit. Longer: a child writes the sentence from the exit as an opinion about another choice.

### Words and supports

Opinion, link word, fact, reason. Frames: "We should ___ because ___." "The card asks for ___." Read the strips aloud, accept a drawing or a gesture, and record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 158 begins the year-end reading and word check. The exit ticket marks go back to day 158 for a fresh repair, and the record sheet and packets stay in class.
