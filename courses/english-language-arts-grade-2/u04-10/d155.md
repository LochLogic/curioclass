# Day 155: Share the packets and read a favorite for the class

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make six groups of five. Put each child's packet on the desk. Set the P1 notes for children 16 to 30 on your table. Mark each check sheet with the commonest miss of the class, and sort the sheets by group. Day 154's assessment is handed back to the record sheet: the sitting marks and the packet scores, with the link-word notes kept for day 157. Nothing is scored today; say so aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Agree how to listen: wait for the speaker, add to what was said, ask when you do not follow. Review: turn, pair, link, clarify from unit 1. Say aloud that nothing is scored today except one short question at the end of the reading, and that the packets stay in class. Mark the six for that exit ask (children 25 to 30).

#### 20 min. Packet sharing

Six groups of five, two rounds. Round one (10 minutes): each child shows the drawing and reads the opinion card aloud, 2 minutes each with the swap. Round two (10 minutes): each child answers one listener question from the group in a whole sentence, 2 minutes each. Review: a whole-sentence answer from day 151. A listener asks "Which part do you mean?" if a point is not clear. Families are not asked to come.

#### 10 min. P1 retry

Children 16 to 30 come to your table one at a time and retry the one effect that was missing in their day 152 notes, 45 seconds each: name the sound, then say what it adds. Poem P is not read aloud again. Hear the thirteen with the most missing first; hear the other two in the closing minutes. A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. Children 1 to 15 read a favorite tale or poem to a partner.

#### 20 min. Read for classmates

Each group picks a tale from 01 to 04 or the poem or song in 06 and reads it in parts, a line or a character each (8 minutes to prepare). Then each group reads for the class, 2 minutes each (12 minutes). Voices only; no costumes or mocking accents. Say each tale's credit from its page before it is read. Review: two voices from day 149.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read aloud twice to the class, in two voices and leaving out who says each line, the winter talk of the first ant telling (1 minute). Then hear the six, one at a time, about 40 seconds each, while the rest of the class draws one thing they noticed from another group: [[s04-X155v2]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Revise the cycle 26 record

Give back each child's written check sheet from day 153. The word most children missed is shown on the child's own sheet, not on the board. Each child finds that word in the printed dictionary, compares the letters and corrects it in pencil beside the first try, which stays. Children who missed no word read a page of the reader to a partner. Review: ending -ous and -us from day 149. A pattern still not carried over is error R.

#### 42 min. Flexible groups

Three rounds of 14 minutes. Groups of ten by the latest recorded letter (R, S, Q, AB to AE). One group sits with you: map the irregular words, blend, read Gus and the Log, spell on tiles, reread. The other twenty work at two stations: partner reread of the cycle 26 reader, and tile spelling of bonus, chorus, circus, octopus, enormous and joyous with the printed dictionary for checking. Move the groups each round.

#### 10 min. Close

Pairs read one new cycle word sentence aloud, then choose one word from it to build on tiles. Hear the last two P1 retries from the notes, 45 seconds each, while the pairs work.

### Other rooms

Read a tale aloud in turns, a line each. The listener question is answered aloud, or by a gesture and a drawing.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use any drawing and a sentence your child wrote.
**Skip.** The tiles.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child shows a drawing and reads a sentence about it. Ask one question about it.
  **Say.** "Which part do you mean?" Expect: your child points to the part and answers in a whole sentence.
- **15 min. Read it in parts.** Pick a short tale or poem and read it in turns, one line each, in two voices.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child builds bonus and enormous with scraps of paper and says each ending.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X155v2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sharing and the reading for the class. Longer: a group adds a second tale to read.

### Words and supports

Share, listener, question, parts, voice. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "Which part do you mean?" Accept a drawing with an explanation, repeat a question when asked, and record the support used.

### Tomorrow

Day 156 is the last reading check: two short articles, Q and R, read cold, and four strips on their most important points and reasons. The notes on poem P go to a table on day 157.
