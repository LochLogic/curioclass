# Wrong-and-right pairs, day 157

One pair for each table, printed large, a wrong and a right answer side by side. The table says why the right one is right. These cases are new and are not exit or end-item cases.

- **Table for the story card (C).** Cat says, "Come and play!" Bird says, "I must rest." Wrong: "Cat and Bird both want to play." Right: "Cat wants to play; Bird wants to rest."
- **Table for the two versions (D).** Version 1: the boy gives the dog a bone and the dog wags. Version 2: the boy gives the dog a ball and the dog runs. Wrong: "The two versions are the same." Right: "In both the boy gives the dog a gift; in one it is a bone and in the other a ball."
- **Table for the two articles (G).** Points: "Wear a hat in the sun." "Drink water on a hot day." Reason: "Water keeps the body cool." Wrong: the arrow goes from the reason to the hat. Right: the arrow goes from the reason to the water.
- **Table for the poem (O).** Line: "Tap, tap, drum on the drum." Wrong: "Tap, tap is said twice." Right: "Tap, tap is said twice, so it sounds like a drum beat."
