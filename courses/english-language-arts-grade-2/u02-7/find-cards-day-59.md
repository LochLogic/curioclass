# Find cards (day 59)

Eight cards, each with a plain drawing and a few words naming an invented find. Each child takes one and plans an event about it.

1. a smooth stone with a pale stripe
2. a feather by the creek
3. a curled leaf in a puddle
4. a pebble with a hole through it
5. a paw print in soft mud
6. a seed pod on the path
7. ripples in wet sand
8. a bent twig with a bud

Say aloud that every find is made up.
