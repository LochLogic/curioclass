# Day 61: Acting out a find before writing it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang pages 3 and 9 of The Mark in the Stone and a large five-box sheet. Lay out the feelings word bank for each child and the six story slips and three-box strip for each pair. Mark today's six (group 4 of five in cycle 11) on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This strip is made up for practice." Read aloud twice: "While the class walked." Ask: "Does it tell a whole thing?" Hear three, then add the missing strip together: "While the class walked, Dev found a bone." If many missed day 60's exit (error AD), read each strip aloud and ask it again; otherwise retrieve the phrase strips.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: action, thought and feeling are three boxes of the five-box sheet you made on day 45, and rehearse means to say your plan aloud before you do it (grade 1). Elaborate: to say more about one moment. Point to each on the large sheet and say it once. A child says one of them about Amara.

#### 8 min. Reread

Read page 3 aloud (Amara wonders about the mark), then page 9 (Amara is disappointed and excited), 3 minutes. Pairs lay the six story slips onto the strip's three boxes, What she did, What she wondered and How she felt (4 minutes).

Check: walked and carried are in What she did; was the mark a leaf and a crack would not be so neat are in What she wondered; disappointed and excited are in How she felt.

If a slip is put in the wrong box, ask: "Is that something she did, or something she wondered?" (1 minute).

#### 12 min. Model: rehearse before you write

Stand for Cal, an invented child, with the ridge on a river stone. Act the action: kneel and brush the air. Say the thought aloud: "Is it a root?" Say the feeling: "I felt curious." Say: "Now I can write each one." One actor performs; the class watches, and as each box happens a child ticks it on the large sheet. Then do it once more, slower, and say: "Rehearsing first means the words are ready when I write."

#### 12 min. Pairs rehearse

In each pair one child performs their own find (an action, a thought said aloud, a feeling) while the partner holds the five-box sheet and ticks each box as it happens; then they swap (6 minutes each). You move and give no wording. Say aloud: "Every find is made up."

If a child tells the feeling but no thought (error M), hold up a thought bubble and say: "I wonder ___."

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "When you act it out, what does your face or your voice show that words on a page do not?" Pairs talk (2 minutes), then link and clarify. Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child adds a thought and a feeling to the plan sheet, choosing the feeling from the word bank, with a few words. Point to the bank for a child with no feeling (error N).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X61a aloud twice and ask it of two of the six. Do the same with s02-X61b and with s02-X61c, two of the six each. The rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error M, N or L on a miss.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six ew and mixed vowel team cards (new, grew, crew, clue, suit and zoo). Reblend any wrong vowel team (error Y): map the whole team first, then blend.

#### 15 min. Transfer to a fresh passage

Review: would is a mapped word (day 54); star its unexpected part. Pairs read the fresh passage twice, smooth and clear, and say what the crew grew and what Sue found (8 minutes). Confirm each word after decoding it: ask whether it makes sense. Dictate one sentence: "Sue grew new food." Children write it with tiles or paper (4 minutes). Never guess a word from its picture.

Check: grew and new are spelled with ew; food with oo; a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 4) sit with you for the cycle 11 oral card, two minutes each; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build room, youth, new, bruise), sort (the twelve cards by spelling, then say the sound) and reread (the fresh passage and Blue Fruit Soup with a partner). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which word did you decode, then check?" Expect: a word from the passage, such as would or clue.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the six story lines on slips, write the feelings words on the wall and draw the large sheet on paper. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No word bank: say a feeling word aloud.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Act it out.** Your child picks something small to find, then acts what they would do, says what they would wonder and says how they would feel.
  **Say.** "What did you do? What did you wonder? How did you feel?" Expect: one action, one question, one feeling word.
- **10 min. Fill the boxes.** Your child adds the wondering and the feeling to the plan sheet.
- **10 min. Words.** Read the sentence "Sue grew new food." Your child writes it from your voice.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X61a]] [[s02-X61b]] [[s02-X61c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs rehearse for 4 minutes each and skip the write; keep the exit and the teaching. In a shorter year the rehearsal can fold into day 62's model, but every foundations teaching turn and oral card turn stays. Longer: a child rehearses a second find and tells which was easier to act.

### Words and supports

Elaborate, rehearse, action, thought, feeling, curious, puzzled, eager, proud, unsure, relieved. Frames: "I wondered, ___?" "I felt ___." "First I ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 62 opens with a repair of today's exit (errors M, N and L, a thought, a feeling or an action left out): a thought bubble, the feelings word bank and the question "What did the child do?" It then turns to drafting an opening, First and Next on the draft page, followed by the cycle 11 written check.
