# Story slips: did, wondered, felt (day 61)

Six slips for each pair, one line on each from The Mark in the Stone, and a strip with three boxes headed **What she did**, **What she wondered** and **How she felt**. Pairs sort the slips onto the boxes.

- Amara walked to a pile of broken gray stones. (did)
- Amara carried the stone to Dr. Lee. (did)
- Was the mark a leaf? (wondered)
- A crack would not be so neat, she thought. (wondered)
- Amara felt a little disappointed. (felt)
- Amara felt a little excited. (felt)
