# Phrase strips (days 60 and 70)

A set of paper strips for each pair. Pairs lay out a simple sentence, add one detail strip, move a strip, and join two simple sentences with a joining strip.

- Who strips: Wren, Dev, my friend, the class
- Action strips: found a shell, sorted the bones, drew a line
- Detail strips: small, on the beach, with a brush
- Joining strips: , and

Example the teacher builds first: "Wren found a shell" then "Wren found a small shell on the beach" then "On the beach, Wren found a small shell." Pairs build their own about their find.
