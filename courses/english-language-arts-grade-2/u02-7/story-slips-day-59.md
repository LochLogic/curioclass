# Story slips (day 59)

Four slips for each pair, one line on each, taken from the pages of The Mark in the Stone, and a strip with two boxes headed **Beginning** and **Ending**. Pairs sort the slips onto the boxes, then draw one arrow from the slip that asks a question to the slip that answers it.

- The class arrives at the fossil park.
- Amara finds a curved mark and wonders what it is.
- Amara draws her stone and Dr. Lee writes a label.
- Amara tells Ben what the mark is.
