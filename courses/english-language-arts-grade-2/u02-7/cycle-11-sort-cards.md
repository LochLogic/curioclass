# Sort cards, cycle 11 (days 59 and 60)

Twelve cards, one word on each. On day 59 the sort station uses only the eight cards of the four spellings taught so far (the ew cards stay in the envelope). On day 60 pairs sort all twelve under five headings by spelling: **oo**, **ou**, **ui**, **ue** and **ew**. Then each child spells two words and a sentence you say.

moon, food, soup, youth, fruit, suit, blue, clue, new, grew, crew, flew
