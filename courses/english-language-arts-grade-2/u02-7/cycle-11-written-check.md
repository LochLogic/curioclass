# Cycle 11 written check (day 62, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 58 to 61, and none is moon, soup, fruit, blue, flew, juice, pool or spoon.

1. "She swept with a ____." (broom)
2. "A ____ sat down." (cougar)
3. "We go on a ____." (cruise)
4. "Put on the ____." (glue)

Tag wrong spellings: "brume" or "brum", "coogar", "cruze" and "gloo" put a different spelling on the vowel sound (Y); a plain "brm" or "cgr" tag the letter that fits. After the check is marked, dictate "cool" and "new" as unscored practice.
