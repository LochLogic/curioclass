# Feelings word bank (days 61 to 63)

One card each, with twelve words in two columns and a small face beside each: curious, puzzled, proud, worried, excited, unsure, surprised, nervous, eager, calm, hopeful, relieved.

Children point to a word, say it and use it in the plan sheet's feeling box.
