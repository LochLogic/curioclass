# Section 2.7 opener: plan, build and draft a discovery narrative (days 59 to 63)

## What the section does

Five days build one short narrative event. Day 59 rereads the fossil-dig story and plans one event in five boxes: a time word, an action, a thought, a feeling and a closing. Day 60 makes sentences, makes them longer and moves their parts without changing the meaning, including a sentence joined with and. Day 61 rehearses the event aloud before any writing. Day 62 drafts an opening and the first two events with First and Next. Day 63 finishes the draft with Then, At last and a closing that answers the start.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a repair, a reread of the story, one model, a pair task and a five-minute exit. The foundations block follows cycle 11 (oo, ou, ui, ue and ew) with the reader *Blue Fruit Soup*, a fresh passage on day 61, a 15-minute written check on day 62 and a rotation in which you hear the day's six. Day 63 begins cycle 12 with -tion. Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask; the writing exits (day 63) are on paper.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A good ending finishes what the beginning started.** The last box answers the first.
- **A sentence can grow and move.** More detail and a new order keep the meaning.
- **Thought and feeling are different boxes.** What a character wonders is not how they feel.
- **Time words hold events in order.** First, next, then and at last.

## The errors, in plain words

- **P:** no closing, or one unrelated to the find. Put the first box beside the last.
- **AD:** half a sentence. Ask "Does it tell a whole thing?"
- **M and N:** a retell with no thought, or no feeling. Add the thought bubble and point to the feelings bank.
- **O:** actions with no time words. Slide a time-word card before each action.
- **Y:** a wrong vowel team spelled on the sound.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits are unscored; record the exit looks on the unit record sheet. The plan sheets and drafts are kept for feedback after class.
