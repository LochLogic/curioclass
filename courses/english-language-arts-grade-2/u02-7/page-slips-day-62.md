# Page slips (day 62)

Four slips for each pair, one line on each from pages 4 to 7 of The Mark in the Stone, mixed up, and the pair's time-word cards. Pairs put the slips in the order the story tells them and slide the matching time-word card (First, Next, Then, At last) in front of each.

- Amara looked at the mark carefully. (First)
- Amara brushed the stone gently. (Next)
- Amara carried the stone to Dr. Lee. (Then)
- Dr. Lee smiled and held up a picture card. (At last)
