# Day 59: Planning one event in five boxes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang pages 1, 10 and 11 of The Mark in the Stone and the story-map. Lay out a five-box plan sheet for each child, the find cards and story slips for each pair, and the eight sort cards (the ew cards stay in the envelope). Mark today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 11) on the unit 2 record sheet. Keys: key-cycle-11-sort-cards.md, key-story-slips-day-59.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud twice: "Rosa untied the bag. The knot came loose. Now the bag was open." Ask: "Use the next two sentences to say what untied means here." Hear three. If many missed day 58's exit (error H), flip the prefix card (un- means not, or undoing) and say not tied; otherwise retrieve the word-part cards. Every child may pass.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: a narrative is a story someone made up, and an event is what happens in it (kindergarten); the beginning introduces a story and the ending concludes it (days 34 and 36). Say each once and point to it on the story-map: narrative, event, beginning, ending. A child says one sentence about The Mark in the Stone with one of them. Say aloud: "Amara, Dr. Lee and the fossil park are made up."

#### 10 min. Reread the story

Read page 1 aloud (the class arrives at the fossil park), then pages 10 and 11 (the label and the bus ride home), 2 minutes. Children point to who is there at the start and to what Amara knows at the end (3 minutes).

Pairs lay the four story slips onto the strip's two boxes, Beginning and Ending (3 minutes).

Check: the class arrives and Amara finds a mark and wonders are in the Beginning box; the drawing and label and telling Ben are in the Ending box.

Each pair draws one arrow from the slip that asks to the slip that answers it (2 minutes). Draw the same arrow on the wall story-map and say: "An ending finishes what the beginning started."

#### 10 min. Model: plan one event

Review: you made the five-box event sheet on day 45. Plan a find for Cal, an invented child: a ridge on a river stone. Fill the boxes as you say each aloud: First (a time word), brushed (an action), "Is it a root?" (a thought), curious (a feeling), drew it: a ridge, not a root (a closing). Draw an arrow from the last box to the thought box. Say: "One event stays in the boxes. A trip to the shop would not go here." Children watch; nobody writes yet.

#### 12 min. Pairs plan

Each child takes a find card (a pebble, a paw print, a seed pod) and fills the five boxes with a few words and a small picture, saying each box to a partner (9 minutes). The partner draws an arrow from the last box to the box it answers (3 minutes). You move and give no wording. Say aloud: "Every find is made up."

If many arrows have nowhere to go (error P), hold the first box beside the last box and ask: "Did the end answer the start?"

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "Which find card would be hardest to give a last box, and why?" Pairs talk (2 minutes), then link ("You said ___; I would add ___") and clarify ("Which part do you mean?"). Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Draw and label

Each child draws the event's end in the last box and labels it with a few words. Watch for a closing that has nothing to do with the find.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read Rob Is Skipping aloud twice and ask item s02-X59v2 of three of the six. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X59b aloud twice and ask it of the other three. The rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the story say?" or "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error P on a miss of s02-X59v2.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six oo and ou cards (zoo, room, food, youth, coupon and one more). Reblend any wrong vowel team (error Y): map the whole team first, then blend. Never guess a word from its picture (error X).

#### 15 min. Teach: ui and ue, then the first reading

Review: a vowel team is two letters that make one sound (unit 1), and oo and ou say /oo/ (day 58). Model mapping suit and bruise: ui is one sound, /oo/ (4 minutes). Model clue and Sue: ue is one sound, /oo/ (3 minutes). Children say each. Spell suit and clue with tiles (2 minutes). Review: says is a mapped word (day 55); star its unexpected part.

Hand each pair Blue Fruit Soup and read its title to them. Pairs echo-read pages 1 to 3 after you, then finish pages 4 to 6 together (6 minutes). Never guess a word from its picture.

Check: suit and clue spelled with ui and ue.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 2) sit with you for the cycle 11 oral card, two minutes each; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build zoo, food, suit, bruise, clue, Sue), sort (the eight cards under oo, ou, ui and ue) and reread (Blue Fruit Soup with a partner). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which two letters make the sound in clue?" Expect: ue.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a sheet into five boxes, and write the four story lines and the find cards on slips. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No find cards: use any small thing from a walk, such as a leaf.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. A story and its ending.** Your child tells you a story they know in a few lines.
  **Say.** "How did the end finish what the start began?" Expect: it answers the question or finishes the job the start set up.
- **10 min. Plan one event.** Your child fills the five boxes for the small thing, then draws an arrow from the last box back.
- **10 min. Words.** Say suit and clue. Your child writes each and says which two letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X59v2]] [[s02-X59b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs plan in 8 minutes and skip the draw and label; keep the arrow, the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child plans a second ending for the same find and says which fits better.

### Words and supports

Narrative, event, beginning, ending, suit, clue, Sue. Frames: "First ___." "I wondered, ___?" "I felt ___." "It ended when ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn. A child who cannot write dictates a few words to you.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 opens with a repair of today's exit (error P, an ending that does not finish the start): the first box beside the last box. It then turns to sentences about finds, made longer and moved, with ew and the second reading of Blue Fruit Soup.
