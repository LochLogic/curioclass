# Day 60: Making sentences longer and moving their parts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang page 5 of The Mark in the Stone. Print the sentence cards and cut the phrase strips for each pair. Write the four model pairs on the board, covered. Mark today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 11) on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud twice: "Ria sees muddy tracks by the gate. She wonders what made them. She follows them and finds a snail on a leaf." Ask: "Did the end answer the start?" Hear three. If many missed day 59's exit (error P), put the first box of a plan sheet beside the last box and ask it again; otherwise retrieve the plan sheets.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: expand means make a sentence longer with a detail (kindergarten), and a sentence joined with and is known from grade 1, and a simple sentence is a sentence with one idea (new today). Say each once and write a sentence for it on the wall: produce (make a sentence), expand, rearrange (move its parts and keep its meaning), and (joins two whole sentences). A child says one sentence with a word.

#### 8 min. Reread

Read page 5 of The Mark in the Stone aloud, where Amara brushes the stone. Children echo the sentence "Next, Amara brushed the stone gently." and point to the detail words in the page: gently and careful.

#### 12 min. Model: make it longer, move it, join it

Uncover the pairs one at a time. Children ring or bracket on their own sheets as you do on the board; nobody writes a new sentence yet. Say aloud: "Wren and her friend are made up."

First pair: "Wren found a shell." and "Wren found a small shell on the beach." Ring the added words, small and on the beach. Ask: "Is it still a whole sentence?"

Second pair: "Wren found a small shell on the beach." and "On the beach, Wren found a small shell." Ring the part that moved and the comma after it. Ask: "Is it a whole sentence? Does it say the same thing?"

Third: "Wren found a shell, and her friend found a bone." Put a bracket round each part. Say: "Each part could stand alone. The word and, after a comma, joins them." Ring the comma and and.

Fourth: "On the beach, Wren found a shell, and her friend found a bone." Ring the moved part and say the whole thing aloud.

#### 12 min. Pairs sort and mark

Each pair has the sentence cards and phrase strips. Part A, 4 minutes: sort cards 1 to 4 into whole sentence and not yet a whole sentence. Part B, 4 minutes: lay the strips Dev, found a shell, on the beach; ring the strip that can move to the front and read the new order aloud. Part C, 4 minutes: on card 5, ring the joining word.

Check: cards 1 and 4 are whole; cards 2 and 3 are not yet; on card 5 the ring goes round and.

If many sorted card 3 as whole (error AD), read it aloud and ask: "Does it tell a whole thing?"

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "Does the sentence still mean the same thing after you moved the strip?" Pairs use their Part B strips (2 minutes), then link and clarify. Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child writes one simple sentence about their own find from the plan sheet, with a capital letter and an end mark, then underlines who and circles the action. A child who cannot write dictates it to you.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X60 aloud twice and ask it of two of the six. Do the same with s02-X60b and then s02-X60c, two of the six each. The rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AD on a part of a sentence given as a whole one.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six ui and ue cards (suit, bruise, clue, Sue and two more). Reblend any wrong vowel team (error Y): map the whole team first, then blend.

#### 15 min. Teach: ew, then reread and sort

Review: ue says /oo/ in clue (day 59). Model mapping new, grew and crew: ew is one sound, /oo/ (4 minutes). Children say each. Spell crew with tiles (2 minutes). Pairs reread Blue Fruit Soup, one page each in turn, smooth and clear (4 minutes). Pairs sort the twelve cards under oo, ou, ui, ue and ew; map one card aloud with the class first (5 minutes).

Check: new, grew and crew are sorted under ew.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 3) sit with you for the cycle 11 oral card, two minutes each; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build new, grew, crew, clue), sort (the twelve cards again, then say the sound each spelling makes) and reread (Blue Fruit Soup with a partner). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which spellings can make the sound in new?" Expect: ew, and also oo, ou, ui and ue.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the five sentence cards on slips and the strips on torn paper. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the parts of a sentence on three slips of paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Make it longer.** Write "The dog ran." Your child adds a detail and says the new sentence.
  **Say.** "Is it still a whole sentence? Does it say the same thing?" Expect: yes, a whole sentence, and it says more.
- **10 min. Move it.** Your child moves a part to the front and reads it.
- **10 min. Words.** Say new and crew. Your child writes each and says which letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X60]] [[s02-X60b]] [[s02-X60c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep Part A and Part C of the pairs task, drop Part B, and keep the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child writes a second sentence about the find and joins the two with and.

### Words and supports

Produce, expand, rearrange, and, new, grew, crew. Frames: "It tells a whole thing." "It is not yet a sentence." "I moved ___ to the front." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 opens with a repair of today's exit (error AD, a part of a sentence given as a whole one): read the strip aloud and ask "Does it tell a whole thing?" It then turns to acting out a find, with the action, the thought and the feeling, before drafting, with ew mixed with the other four spellings and a fresh passage.
