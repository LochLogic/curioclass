# Fresh passage, cycle 11 (day 61)

Written to cycle 11 patterns only, with the mapped word would. Pairs read it, confirm the meaning, then you dictate "Sue grew new food."

A crew at the zoo grew food. Sue said, "We would boil it in a pot." Sue found a clue, a spoon in the room. Sue said, "That spoon is new!"
