# Exit cards, days 59 to 63

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. You read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Where a day has two or three asks, the rotating six split them, two children to an ask. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 59 (s02-X59b).** Card: "Kofi finds a smooth pebble. He brushes it clean. He wonders, Is it a bone? He feels curious. Then he tells the class all about his birthday cake." Ask: "Say which part does not belong to this one find."
- **Day 60 (s02-X60).** Card: "Pru holds the lamp. Her friend sorts the stones." Ask: "Say both jobs as one sentence that uses the word and."
- **Day 60 (s02-X60b).** Card: "Leo painted a door." Ask: "Say the sentence again with one detail added."
- **Day 60 (s02-X60c).** Card: "In the mud, Omar found a claw." Ask: "Say the same thing again, with Omar first."
- **Day 61 (s02-X61a).** Card: "Mei lifted the lid of an old tin. Was something moving inside? She felt scared, and then she felt glad when it was a wind-up frog." Ask: "Tell me what Mei thought."
- **Day 61 (s02-X61b).** Card: "Mei lifted the lid of an old tin. Was something moving inside? She felt scared, and then she felt glad when it was a wind-up frog." Ask: "Tell me how Mei felt."
- **Day 61 (s02-X61c).** Card: "Mei lifted the lid of an old tin. Was something moving inside? She felt scared, and then she felt glad when it was a wind-up frog." Ask: "Tell me one thing Mei did."
- **Day 62 (s02-X62).** Card: "Zuri needed a clean edge on her drawing. She checked the rock. She traced the line. Last she shaded the edge." Ask: "Say the first two actions using First and Next."
- **Day 62 (s02-X62b).** Say aloud: "A story is about a girl named Nia, who finds a shell." Ask: "Say one opening sentence for this story."
- **Day 63 (s02-X63).** Card: "First Ivo saw a shiny coin in the sand. Next he brushed it off." Ask: "Write one closing sentence that finishes the story."
