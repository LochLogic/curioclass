# Day 62: Drafting an opening, First and Next

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write Cal's finished draft large: "On a cool morning, Cal went to the river." "First he brushed the ridge gently." "Next he wondered, Is it a root?" Lay out a draft page and time-word cards for each child or pair. Mark today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 11) and day 58's six on the unit 2 record sheet. Key: key-cycle-11-sort-cards.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud twice: "Lia knelt by the creek. She wondered if the shape was a leaf. She felt eager." Ask three children: "Tell me what Lia wondered." Ask two: "Tell me how Lia felt." If many missed day 61's exit (errors M, N and L), put a thought bubble beside the boxes and point to the feelings word bank; ask "What did the child do?" for an action. Otherwise retrieve the plan sheets.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: the beginning introduces a story and tells who, where and when (day 34); the time words First, Next, Then and At last come from grade 1. An opening is the first lines of your own draft. Say each once with its time-word card: opening, First, Next, Then, At last.

#### 8 min. Reread

Read pages 4 to 7 of The Mark in the Stone aloud, the pages that begin First, Next, Then and At last (3 minutes). Pairs put the four page slips in the order the story tells them and slide the matching time-word card in front of each (4 minutes).

Check: the slips run looked carefully (First), brushed (Next), carried to Dr. Lee (Then), smiled and held up a picture card (At last).

If a slip lands out of order, ask: "What happened before it?" (1 minute).

#### 10 min. Model: read a finished draft

Show Cal's finished draft and read each line aloud. Say aloud: "Cal is made up." Children underline the time words First and Next on their own draft page, number the order 1 and 2, and write the box each line came from (action, thought) in the margin, as you do on the large page. Ask: "What does the time word tell us?" Expect: which thing happened first and which came after. Say: "The opening tells who, where and when."

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "Why does First go before Next?" Pairs talk (2 minutes), then link ("You said ___; I would add ___") and clarify ("Which part do you mean?"). Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Draft

Each child drafts an opening, First and Next on the draft page, from their plan sheet, with a picture in the box (a drawing first, then the lines). Slide the time-word cards in front of the lines when a child is stuck. You move and give no wording. A child who cannot write dictates to you.

If a draft has an opening followed by events that are not about one find (error K), return to the plan sheet and cross out the one that does not belong. If a draft has a thought and a feeling but no action (error L), ask: "What did the child do?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X62 aloud twice and ask it of three of the six. Read item s02-X62b aloud twice and ask it of the other three. The rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error O on a miss.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Hand out the four-line check sheet and say: "Write the word you hear." Read sentence 1 twice and say its missing word, and so on through the four sentences (10 minutes). Children write in the gaps. Unscored: note what each child tried. After it, dictate "cool" and "new" as practice for the child to compare with the wall (5 minutes). Hold the marks for day 63's opening.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread Blue Fruit Soup for expression: smooth, with the voices of Gus and Mom.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 5) sit with you and read the oral card for the whole cycle, two minutes each; mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build bruise, grew, crew, clue), sort (the twelve cards by spelling) and reread (Blue Fruit Soup). Round 2, 12 minutes: groups move on and the six join a station.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

Day 58's six read the cycle's words to you, two minutes each (12 minutes). The others partner-read the partner word card and spell two words you dictate, cool and new, without new teaching. Review: look at the word wall if unsure. Do not teach.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the draft page as a box with five lined groups and write the four story lines and the time words on slips. Large group: each child answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No time-word cards: write First and Next on two slips.
**Skip.** The written check and the oral card.

- **10 min. Say it in order.** Your child tells you two things they did today, using First and Next.
  **Say.** "What does the word First tell me?" Expect: it was the first thing.
- **10 min. An opening.** Your child says and writes one opening line about a find: who, where and when.
- **10 min. Draft.** Your child writes First and Next lines from the plan sheet.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X62]] [[s02-X62b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drafts get a picture and one opening line, and the second line waits for day 63; keep the exit and the written check. Longer: a child writes the Then line early and slides its time-word card into place.

### Words and supports

Opening, First, Next, Then, At last. Frames: "On a ___ morning, ___ went to ___." "First I ___." "Next I ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn. A child who cannot write dictates a few words to you.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 opens with a repair of today's exit (error O, actions with no time words): slide a time-word card in front of each action. The foundations opening rereads the commonest cycle 11 misses from today's check (errors Y and AA). Then the class finishes the draft with Then, At last and a closing that answers the start, and begins -tion.
