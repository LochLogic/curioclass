# Sentence cards (day 60)

Five printed cards for each pair, about invented finds, and the pair's phrase strips. Part A: pairs sort cards 1 to 4 into **whole sentence** and **not yet a whole sentence**. Part B: pairs lay the strips Dev, found a shell and on the beach in that order, ring the strip that can move to the front and read the new order aloud. Part C: card 5 has two brackets drawn over its two parts; pairs ring the joining word.

1. Dev found a bone. (whole)
2. On the beach, (not yet)
3. and her friend drew a line (not yet)
4. Mina held a brush. (whole)
5. Wren sorted shells, and her friend drew a line. (two parts, joined by the word and)

Say aloud that the finds are made up.
