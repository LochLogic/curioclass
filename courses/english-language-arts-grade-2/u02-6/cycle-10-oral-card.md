# Cycle 10 oral card (days 53 to 57)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On days 53 and 54 only the first two lines are used. Children read the words and say each ending; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 9 patterns (doubling before a vowel suffix).** rubbing, stopped, bigger, running, sitting, dropping
- **Cycle 10 patterns (three sounds of -ed; would-contractions).** asked, played, lifted, planted, called, tossed, he'd, I'd, you'd
- **A sentence.** He'd lifted the box and asked for the lid.

Partner word card for day 57: asked, played, lifted, planted, called, tossed, she'd, we'd. Dictated words: they'd, called.
