# Day 57: A check on reading a fossil article

## Teacher page

### Before class

A check: nothing is taught, held up or hinted at. Print the article and chart for each child. Mark today's six (group 5 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 12 min. RI.2.2 item

Say: "This article is made up." Read Fossil Notes aloud once, children following (3 minutes). Ask items s02-A9 to s02-A12 once each; children write a few words in the chart box (9 minutes). Reread an ask once on request. Mark shown, shown with help (after the reread) or not yet seen; tag error C.

#### 6 min. RI.2.6 item

Ask items s02-A13 and s02-A14; children write or circle. Tag error D.

#### 10 min. RI.2.5 item

Ask items s02-A15 to s02-A20 once each; children point and write the answer. Tag error E for a long way round or a wrong place.

#### 4 min. RI.2.4 item

Ask item s02-A21. Tag error F.

#### 4 min. L.2.4a item

Ask item s02-A22. Tag error G.

#### 4 min. L.2.4b item

Ask item s02-A23. Tag error H.

#### 4 min. L.2.4c item

Ask item s02-A24. Tag error I.

#### 4 min. L.2.4d item

Ask item s02-A25. Tag error J.

#### 7 min. L.2.4e item

Ask item s02-A26 with the printed glossary and item s02-A27 with the beginning dictionary card (6 minutes). Item s02-A28 stays open for lack of a digital copy; note it. Tag error G.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X57 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, 30 seconds each; tag error C.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Unscored. Say each sentence of the written check twice and the missing word once (8 minutes). Collect the sheets. Dictate stopped as practice (5 minutes). Tag Y, Z or AH as the card says.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread Packed and Ready for expression (3 minutes), then read the partner word card and spell they'd and called (2 minutes).

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, two rounds of 12 minutes. Round 1: today's six read the cycle 10 oral card to you, two minutes each, saying each -ed ending; mark the heard column. The other 24 are in two stations: tiles and sort, and a reread of Packed and Ready. Round 2: the groups swap.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

No new teaching. Day 53's six read the cycle 10 words to you, two minutes each. The others partner-read the pattern card and spell planted and we'd.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the paragraphs and menu cards by hand. A child may say an answer to you instead of writing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No reader: any short page with a heading. No tiles: write letters on scraps.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. The assessment at home.** Read a short page with a heading aloud once. Your child says the topic and what each paragraph is about, in a few words.
  **Say.** "What does the writer mainly explain?" Expect: a few words that name the topic.
- **10 min. Small words.** Say unlock, painter and bookshelf. Your child says the small words inside and what each word means.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads Packed and Ready for expression, a page at a time.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say tossed and planted. Your child writes each and says which sound -ed makes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X57]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the reader. Longer: a child says how one answer was found.

### Words and supports

Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 hands back this check, repairs the commonest miss and day 56's error U, mends C to J in four groups, and starts oo and ou.
