# Day 55: Himself, herself and what happened yesterday

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 54's exit marks show whether errors Q and R need the repair. Mark today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 10) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Give each pair the strips, the six pictures, the verb cards, the story pages and tiles. Set three stations: tiles, the ten cycle 9 sort cards, a reread of Packed and Ready.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 54's exit, repair with fresh made-up cards. Error Q (3 minutes): read "A bunch of flowers sat on the shelf." and ask pairs for the one word that names all the flowers as a group; say "one group word, many flowers". Error R (2 minutes): read "One mouse ran by the shelf. Then a second mouse ran by too." and ask pairs to say how many ran, in one sentence; flip the mouse and mice cards: the whole word changes, so no -s. Otherwise pairs retrieve the picture cards and say one group word and one changed plural.

#### 5 min. Words

Say each word, show it and use it in a sentence yourself, then one child says a sentence: himself, herself, ourselves and themselves on the strips, and held and met written on the board. Say, "I held the brush." "We met at the gate." Hear three sentences. The strips are read to the children today; they match by the small first word (him, her, our, them).

#### 8 min. Reread

Say: "This story is made up; Amara and Dr. Lee are not real people." Read page 1, page 5 and page 6 of The Mark in the Stone aloud once, children following (3 minutes). Pairs point to herself, held and met and answer in a few words: What did Amara say to herself? What did Dr. Lee hold up? Who met the group at the gate? (3 minutes). Hear three pairs (2 minutes).

**Ask.** "What did Dr. Lee hold up?" Expect: a magnifying glass.

#### 12 min. Model: pointing back, and what happened yesterday

Himself, herself, themselves, myself and ourselves point back to the person who does the action. Draw a girl alone and say, "The doer is one girl, so the word is herself: Amara said it to herself." Draw a boy alone: himself. Draw two children: themselves, as scientists ask themselves questions in article C. Point to yourself: myself. Point to the class: ourselves. Say a gap sentence and have children choose the word from the strips: "The boy hurt ___." (3 minutes for the five). Then flip the verb cards. Today I get a shell; yesterday I got a shell. Write and wrote, lay and laid, feel and felt, then hold and held and meet and met from the story. The yesterday word is a different word, not "-ed"; we do not add -ed to these (6 minutes). Children say each pair back. Use the word card to finish "Today I get; yesterday I ___."

Check: each strip is chosen for the doer; each pair is said with the new word, not -ed.

#### 12 min. Pairs match and sort

Pairs lay each of six strips beside the picture it points back to and say a sentence, such as "The boy hurt himself." (5 minutes). Then they sort the eight verb cards into today and yesterday and lay each pair side by side (4 minutes). Last, read three gaps and pairs hold up the card that finishes each: "Yesterday we ___ a long letter." "Today I ___ happy." "Last week she ___ a map on the table." (3 minutes). Walk among pairs, about 20 seconds each. Watch for error S, a strip matched to the wrong person, and error T, an -ed added to a yesterday word.

Check: six strips matched; eight cards sorted into pairs; three gaps finished.

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: turn, pair, link, clarify, from unit 1. Question: "Why do we say herself for one girl and themselves for two children?" Turn: each child tells a partner (1 minute). Pair: two pairs join and one says what the other said, with "You said ___; I would add ___" (2 minutes). Hear today's six, about 40 seconds each including a link or "Which part do you mean?" from another child (4 minutes). Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

Expect: the word has to match the person or people who did the thing.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child writes "Yesterday I ___ a stone." with got or found, then "I carried it ___." with myself, a few words each. Write found on the board for copying. A child may draw and say the words for you to scribe. Judge: a yesterday word that is not -ed; myself for the doer. Mark nothing aloud.

Check: a yesterday word and a pronoun that fits, written or scribed.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X55a aloud twice (30 seconds). Ask item s02-X55a. Children tell a partner. Then read the card of item s02-X55b twice (30 seconds), ask it, and children tell a partner. The six then answer both to you, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes); mark the record sheet. Shown means the first answer is Bea and the second says both hid and told. Shown with help means after one prompt: "What does the card say about Bea?" Not yet seen means the friend named for herself (error S), or an -ed added, as hided or telled (error T).

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six cards: asked, played, lifted, planted, stopped, rubbed. Children read each and say the ending alone. If the ending sound is wrong, say it alone, then the whole word again (error Y).

#### 15 min. Reread, sort and spell

Pairs reread Packed and Ready, a page each, then again (4 minutes). Then sort the twelve cycle 10 cards under /t/, /d/ and /id/ by the sound of -ed; all three are spelled -ed (4 minutes). Spell planted, called and tossed with tiles, then write "Ted tossed the hat to Meg." from your dictation (4 minutes). Map says and once as words to know by heart: in says the ay says /e/ and the s says /z/; in once the o says /wu/ and ce says /s/. Star them and put them on the word wall (3 minutes).

Check: planted and called spelled with -ed; a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes): the cycle 9 words, the cycle 10 words, then the sentence "He'd lifted the box and asked for the lid." Each says the ending sound of each -ed word. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build planted, called and tossed, then say the ending sound. Sort: the ten cycle 9 sort cards. Reread: pairs read pages 4 to 6 of Packed and Ready twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "What are the three sounds of -ed?" Expect: /t/, /d/ and /id/.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six people on card and write the strips by hand; write the eight verbs on folded slips. One copy of the story: read the three pages aloud from it. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. Large group: pair children so each pair speaks for 40 seconds, or hear the rotating six and the rest tell a partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each word and draw a girl alone and two children. No tiles: write letters on scraps.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Himself and herself.** Draw a girl, a boy and two children. Say "The girl hurt ___." Your child picks herself, himself or themselves for each picture.
  **Say.** "Why do we say herself for one girl and themselves for two children?" Expect: the word matches the person or people who did the thing.
- **10 min. Yesterday.** Say get and got, write and wrote, feel and felt. Your child says "Today I ___. Yesterday I ___." for each pair. Expect a different word, not -ed.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say planted and tossed. Your child writes each and says the sound -ed makes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X55a]] [[s02-X55b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs match the strips and sort the verb cards, and skip the three gaps; keep the exit, the reread and the oral card. Longer: a child writes a sentence for each pair of verb cards and reads one aloud.

### Words and supports

Himself, herself, myself, ourselves, themselves, held, met, got, wrote, felt, laid. Frames: "The boy hurt ___." "Today I ___. Yesterday I ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 opens with a repair of today's exit (error S, a pronoun matched to the wrong person; error T, an -ed on a word that changes), and its foundations block rereads six -ed cards and he'd, I'd and you'd with the ending sound reblended (errors Y and AH). Then children choose between a describing word for a thing and one for an action, with gentle and gently.
