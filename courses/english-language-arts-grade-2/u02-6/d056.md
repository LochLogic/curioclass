# Day 56: Describing a thing and describing an action

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 55's exit marks show whether errors S and T need the repair. Mark today's six (group 4 of five in cycle 10) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Give each pair the thing card, the action card, the eight sentence cards, the fresh passage and tiles. Write he'd, I'd and you'd on cards beside six -ed cards. Set three stations: tiles, the twelve cycle 10 sort cards, a reread of Packed and Ready. Keys: key-cycle-10-sort-cards.md, key-fossil-sentence-cards-day-56.md, key-verb-cards-day-55.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 55's exit, repair with fresh made-up cards. Error S (3 minutes): read "Ben sits by the gate. His sister Ivy hums. Ben pats himself on the back." and ask pairs who himself means; draw one arrow from Ben back to himself. Error T (2 minutes): say "Today we find a shell. Yesterday we ___ a shell." and children say found, not finded; flip the verb cards. Otherwise pairs retrieve the verb cards and say one pair, today and yesterday.

#### 5 min. Words

Say each word, show it, children say it back, and one child says a sentence: precise (exact, just right), gentle (soft and careful), gently, and suggest (to offer an idea). Use each in a sentence yourself first, such as "I suggest a gentle brush for the rock." Hear three sentences about a brush or a rock.

#### 8 min. Reread

Read aloud, children following the print: the sentence in article C about brushing the rock gently, and the sentence on page 4 of The Mark in the Stone where Amara brushed the stone gently. Say: "This story is made up; Amara is not a real person." Pairs point to the word gently in each and tell a partner what is being done gently (3 minutes). Then they point to the word careful on page 4 and say who was careful (2 minutes). Hear three pairs (3 minutes).

**Ask.** "What did Amara do gently?" Expect: she brushed the stone.

#### 12 min. Model: a describing word for the thing or for the action

A gentle brush: gentle describes the brush, a thing. The brush moves gently: gently describes the move, an action. Hold up the thing card for the first and the action card for the second, and ask each time, "A thing or an action?" Children point to a card. Add loud and loudly, with a loud bell and the bell rings loudly, then quick and quickly, with a quick step and the girl steps quickly; the word with -ly goes with the action (6 minutes). Say once the word adverb for the word that tells how an action is done; no child is asked to define it. Say: "A describing word for a thing has no -ly; a describing word for an action usually adds -ly." Say each pair aloud with the class: slow, slowly; neat, neatly (3 minutes). Then say a gap, "The ___ box sat on the shelf, strong or strongly?" and children point to a card and say the word (3 minutes).

Check: children say thing or action for each model and say the right word for the gap.

#### 12 min. Pairs choose the word

Pairs take the eight fossil sentence cards. For each card they read the two printed words, choose the one that fits the blank and point to the thing card or the action card to say why: "It tells about a thing" or "It tells about an action" (8 minutes). Then pairs read each finished sentence aloud to the pair beside them (2 minutes). Hear three pairs, one card each (2 minutes). Walk among pairs, about 20 seconds each. Watch for error U, an -ly word chosen to describe a thing, or a plain word chosen for an action. The key is on the piece file.

Check: eight cards finished; each choice explained with a card.

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: turn, pair, link, clarify, from unit 1. Question: "Does the word tell about the thing or about the action?" Say it about "A quiet rock." and "She spoke quietly." Turn: each child tells a partner (1 minute). Pair: two pairs join and one says what the other said, with "You said ___; I would add ___" (2 minutes). Hear today's six, about 40 seconds each including a link or "Which part do you mean?" from another child (4 minutes). Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

Expect: a word for a thing says what the thing is like; an -ly word says how the action is done.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child writes about a brush: "A ___ brush." with a describing word for a thing, then "I brush ___." with a describing word for an action, a few words each. Write gentle and gently on the board for copying. A child may draw and say the words for you to scribe. Judge: no -ly on the thing; -ly on the action. Mark nothing aloud.

Check: a word for a thing and a word for an action, written or scribed.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X56 aloud twice (30 seconds). Ask item s02-X56. Children tell a partner. Then read the card of item s02-X56b aloud twice (30 seconds), ask it, and children tell a partner. The six then answer both to you, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes); mark the record sheet. Shown means the first answer is the -ly word and the second names the word for the coat. Shown with help means after one prompt: "What does the card say about the coat?" Not yet seen means the thing word chosen for the action, or the action word chosen for the thing (error U).

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show six -ed cards: asked, played, lifted, planted, called, tossed. Children read each and say the ending alone. Then show he'd, I'd and you'd: say each, and say d stands for would (he would, I would). If an ending sound or the d is wrong, say it alone, then the whole word again (errors Y and AH).

#### 15 min. Transfer to a fresh passage

Review: the three sounds of -ed, and he'd, I'd and you'd for would (day 53). Review: would and two, words to know by heart (day 54). Say: "This passage is new; read it with what you know." Pairs read the fresh passage, a sentence each, then again (5 minutes). After each sentence they say in a few words what happened, to confirm meaning after reading the word (3 minutes). Say she'd and we'd as whole words, with the d for would (2 minutes). Dictate one sentence: "Kim said we'd pick it." (3 minutes). Children say what Kim would do, in a few words (2 minutes).

Check: -ed words and 'd words read; we'd spelled with an apostrophe; a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes): all four lines, the grade 1 words, the cycle 9 patterns, the cycle 10 patterns and the sentence "He'd lifted the box and asked for the lid." Each says the ending of each -ed word and what the d stands for. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build lifted, called and tossed, then say the ending sound. Sort: the twelve cycle 10 sort cards under /t/, /d/ and /id/. Reread: pairs read pages 4 to 6 of Packed and Ready twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which word did you read, then check?" Expect: a word from the fresh passage, checked against what the sentence meant.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the thing card and the action card by hand and write the eight sentences on folded slips. One copy of article C: read the sentence aloud and show it on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. Large group: pair children so each pair speaks for 40 seconds, or hear the rotating six and the rest tell a partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a thing on one sheet and a child walking on another. No tiles: write letters on scraps.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Thing or action.** Say "a quiet rock, she spoke quietly". Your child says which word tells about a thing and which about an action. Add loud and loudly, slow and slowly.
  **Say.** "Does the word tell about the thing or about the action?" Expect: a word for a thing, or an -ly word for how it is done.
- **10 min. Choose the word.** Say "The girl carried the tray (careful, carefully)." Your child picks the word and says why.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say lifted and we'd. Your child writes each and says what the d stands for.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X56]] [[s02-X56b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs finish six of the eight cards; keep the exit, the fresh passage and the oral card. Longer: a child writes a sentence about a rock with a word for a thing and one for an action, and reads it aloud.

### Words and supports

Precise, gentle, gently, suggest, careful, carefully, slowly, quickly, loudly. Frames: "It tells about a ___." "A ___ brush." "I brush ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Today's exit error U (a word for an action chosen for a thing, or the other way round) is repaired on day 58. Day 57's foundations block is a written check on the three sounds of -ed and the would-contractions with fresh words.
