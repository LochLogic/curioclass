# Cycle 11 oral card (days 58 to 62)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. Children read the words and say the vowel sound in each; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen. Day 58 uses lines one to four; the writers of days 59 to 62 add the patterns each day teaches.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 10 patterns (three sounds of -ed; would-contractions).** asked, played, lifted, planted, called, tossed, he'd, I'd, you'd
- **Cycle 11 patterns, day 58 (oo and ou saying /oo/).** zoo, room, food, youth, coupon
- **A sentence.** A moon shone on the zoo.
