# Cycle 10 written check (day 57, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 53 to 56, and none is packed, filled or rested.

1. "We ____ the wall blue." (painted)
2. "She ____ me find it." (helped)
3. "He ____ the table." (cleaned)
4. "He said ____ come." (he'd, a whole word)

Tag wrong spellings: "paintid", "paintd", "helpt" and "cleand" put the wrong letters on the sound of -ed (Y); "paint", "help" or "clean" for the whole word omits the ending (Z); "hed" omits the apostrophe (AH). Tag the letter that fits. After the check is marked, dictate "stopped" as unscored practice (double, then -ed).
