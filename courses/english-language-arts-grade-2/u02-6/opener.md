# Section 2.6 opener: group words, doing words and describing words (days 54 to 58)

## What the section does

Three days of grammar, one check day and one day to mend. On day 54 children use a word that names a group (a flock, a herd, a bunch) and the plurals that change their whole shape (child and children, tooth and teeth). On day 55 they use himself, herself and themselves for the person doing the action, and the common verbs that change when they tell of yesterday (get and got, write and wrote). On day 56 they choose between a describing word for a thing and one for an action (gentle and gently). Day 57 is a mid-unit check on a new article. Day 58 gives each child a fresh paragraph to try the strategy they missed.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a repair, a short reread, one model, pair work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block follows cycle 10: the three sounds of -ed (/t/, /d/ and /id/) and the contractions he'd, I'd and you'd, with the reader *Packed and Ready*, a fresh passage on day 56, a 15-minute written check on day 57, and a rotation in which you hear the day's six read the oral card. Day 58 begins cycle 11 (oo and ou). Every exit is one card you read aloud twice and one ask.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **One word can name a whole group.** A flock of birds is one group of many.
- **Some plurals change instead of adding -s.** Mice, teeth and children are whole new words.
- **Herself points back to the doer.** The pronoun matches the person who acts.
- **Some verbs have their own past form.** Hid, told and wrote are different words, not "-ed" words.
- **A describing word is chosen for what it describes.** Gentle fits a thing; gently fits an action.

## The errors, in plain words

- **Q and R:** a group word replaced by a plural, or a plural made with an extra -s. Hold the cards against each other.
- **S and T:** herself matched to the wrong person, or hided and telled said for hid and told. Draw the arrow; sort today and yesterday.
- **U:** a thing word used for an action. Ask "a girl or speaking?"
- **C to J:** the article-reading misses of the unit; the day 58 groups mend them.
- **Y, Z and AH:** a wrong ending sound written, an ending dropped, or an apostrophe left off he'd.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits are unscored; record the exit looks on the unit record sheet.
