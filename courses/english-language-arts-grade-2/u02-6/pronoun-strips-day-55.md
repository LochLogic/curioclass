# Pronoun strips (day 55)

Six paper strips, one word on each, to match to six person pictures.

Strips: herself, himself, themselves, myself, ourselves, yourself.

Pictures: a girl alone, a boy alone, two children, a child pointing at their own chest (me), two children with an arrow joining them (we), a child being spoken to (you).

Pairs lay each strip beside the picture it points back to and say a sentence, such as "The boy hurt himself." Key: herself with the girl, himself with the boy, themselves with the two children, myself with me, ourselves with we, yourself with you.
