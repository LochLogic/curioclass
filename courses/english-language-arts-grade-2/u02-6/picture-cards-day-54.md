# Picture cards (day 54)

Ten small cards, each with a plain drawing and the word under it, and three larger group pictures. Pairs sort the ten into **one** and **more than one**, then pick a group word for each group picture. You say every word aloud as you show its card; children match by picture and by ear and do not decode or spell tooth, goose or mouse today (oo is taught on day 58). The sheep, cow and grape pictures are yours, drawn on the board for the model, so the pairs' three pictures are new.

Ten cards: child, children, tooth, teeth, mouse, mice, goose, geese, fish (one), fish (many). Print the word on the back too, so a pair can check.

Three group pictures: birds in the sky, horses in a field, bananas on a stem. Group words to offer: flock, herd, bunch, crowd.

Key: one: child, tooth, mouse, goose, fish (one). More than one: children, teeth, mice, geese, fish (many). Group words: flock of birds, herd of horses, bunch of bananas. Crowd is for people, so no group picture takes it.
