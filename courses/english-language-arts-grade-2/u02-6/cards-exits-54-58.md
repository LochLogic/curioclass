# Exit cards, days 54 to 58

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. You read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 54 (s02-X54a).** Card: "A pack of dogs ran over the bridge and barked." Ask: "Say the word that names all the dogs as one group."
- **Day 54 (s02-X54b).** Card: "One woman waved from the bridge. Then a second woman came to wave too." Ask: "Say who waved from the bridge, counting them all, in one sentence."
- **Day 55 (s02-X55a).** Card: "Bea sits by the creek. Her friend Tam watches the water. Bea tells herself it is a secret." Ask: "Say who the word herself means in the last sentence."
- **Day 55 (s02-X55b).** Card: "Bea hides a shell in her bag. Then she tells Tam a secret." Ask: "Say those two things again as if they happened last week."
- **Day 56 (s02-X56).** Card: "Opal is a calm girl. She speaks calmly to the guide." Ask: "Say which word tells how Opal speaks."
- **Day 56 (s02-X56b).** Card: "Kai wore a bright coat. He waved cheerfully." Ask: "Say which word tells what Kai's coat was like."
- **Day 58 (s02-X58).** Card: "Lena unbuckled the case. A buckle keeps a case shut. Now the case was open." Ask: "Use the next two sentences to say what unbuckled means here."
