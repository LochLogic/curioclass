# Fresh passage, cycle 10 (day 56)

Written to cycle 10 patterns only, with the mapped words said, would and two. Pairs read it, confirm the meaning, then you dictate "Kim said we'd pick it."

Jed and Kim planted a seed in a pot. They filled the pot and rested. Kim said, "We'd pick it in the sun." Jed asked, "Would Mom lift the pot?" Mom lifted it. She'd set two pots by it.
