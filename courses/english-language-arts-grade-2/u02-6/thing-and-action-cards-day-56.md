# Thing card and action card (day 56)

Two large cards for each pair. The **thing card** shows a drawing of a child, a rock and a brush, with the words "a thing". The **action card** shows a child walking, a child talking and a child painting, with the words "an action".

Pairs hold up one card to explain each choice on the sentence cards: "It tells about a thing" or "It tells about an action."
