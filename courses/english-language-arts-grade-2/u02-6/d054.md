# Day 54: One word for a group, and plurals that change

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 53's exit marks show whether error D needs the repair. Mark today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 10) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Give each pair the picture cards, article C, Packed and Ready and tiles. Write stopped, rubbed and wanted on cards. Draw sheep on a hill, cows in a field and grapes on a stem on the board. Set three stations: tiles, the ten cycle 9 sort cards, a reread of Packed and Ready.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 53's exit (error D), say: "Read the title first." Hold up a made-up article called Nest Parts whose first line says "This article tells what a nest is made of." Ask the pair: "What does the author want to explain, and which words show it?" Expect: what a nest is made of; they point to those words. If many missed error E, a search of every page, say a made-up question, "Where do the birds go in winter?", and pairs put one finger on the subheading that answers it. Hear two pairs. Otherwise pairs lay one feature route card beside article C and walk the route with a finger.

#### 5 min. Words

Say each word, show it, children say it back, and one child says a sentence: flock (sheep, birds), herd (cows), bunch (grapes) and crowd (many people; draw a crowd at a fair on the board). Use each in a sentence yourself first, such as "A herd of cows crossed the field." Hear three sentences about animals or fruit.

#### 8 min. Reread part of article C

Read the Scientists at Work part aloud twice, children following the print (about 3 minutes). Stop before the sentence about fossil feet and skip it aloud; it waits for a later day. Pairs point to the word group and tell a partner who is in that group (2 minutes). Then they point to teeth and to fish and say each word: teeth means more than one tooth; fish here means one fish (3 minutes). Hear three.

**Ask.** "Who is in the group of scientists?" Expect: the scientists who brush the rock.

#### 12 min. Model: a name for many, and plurals that change

Say "a flock of sheep" and point to the board; say "a herd of cows" and "a bunch of grapes". One word names the whole group, and children say each phrase back (4 minutes). The word fits the thing: a herd of cows, not a herd of grapes. Then show the picture cards in pairs and say each aloud: child and children, tooth and teeth, mouse and mice, goose and geese, fish and fish. Review: you learned in kindergarten that a plural means more than one and usually adds -s or -es. These words are different: the whole word changes, so no -s is added; fish stays fish. Children say "one ___, two ___" after you for each pair (6 minutes). The words are heard and matched to the picture, not sounded out; the letters for oo are taught on day 58. Then say two sentences yourself: "The flock flew over the hill." "Two geese swam in the pond." (2 minutes)

Check: children say each group phrase and each pair back without adding -s.

#### 12 min. Pairs sort and say

Pairs sort the ten picture cards into two piles, one and more than one (5 minutes). Then they take the three group pictures (birds in the sky, horses in a field, bananas on a stem), pick the group word from flock, herd, bunch and crowd for each, and say a sentence about it (5 minutes). Pairs turn a card over to check the word on the back. Walk among pairs, about 20 seconds each. Hear three pairs, one picture each (2 minutes). Watch for error Q, a plural said where a group word fits, and error R, an extra -s such as mouses.

Check: ten cards sorted; a group word that fits each picture; a sentence for each.

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: turn, pair, link, clarify, from unit 1. Question: "How does a group word help a reader picture the scene?" Turn: each child tells a partner (1 minute). Pair: two pairs join and one says what the other said, with "You said ___; I would add ___" (2 minutes). Hear today's six, about 40 seconds each including a link or "Which part do you mean?" from another child (4 minutes). Use the wait signal. A child may pass.

Expect: it tells many things together as one, so a reader sees a flock in the sky.

#### 6 min. Write

Each child writes: "I saw a ___ of ___." with a group word and what it names, then "I saw two ___." with a plural from the cards, a few words each. Children may copy the words from the cards; they copy tooth, goose and mouse and do not sound them out. A child may draw and say the words for you to scribe. Judge: a group word that fits the thing; a plural that changed. Mark nothing aloud.

Check: a group word and a changed plural, written or scribed.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X54a aloud twice (30 seconds). Ask item s02-X54a. Children tell a partner. Then read the card of item s02-X54b twice (30 seconds), ask it, and children tell a partner. The six then answer both to you, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes); mark the record sheet. Shown means the first answer is the group word and the second says the whole plural with no -s. Shown with help means after one prompt: "What does the card say first?" Not yet seen means the plural said for the group word (error Q), or a plural with an extra -s (error R).

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Show the three cards: stopped, rubbed, wanted. Children read each and say the ending alone. If the ending sound is wrong, say the ending alone, then the whole word again (error Y).

#### 15 min. Read the reader after pattern teaching

Review: -ed has three sounds, /t/, /d/ and /id/ (day 53). Model asked, played and lifted: map each sound and say the ending alone, /t/, /d/ and /id/ (3 minutes). Review: said, the ai that says /e/ (day 33). Map were, would and two as words to know by heart: star the tricky parts, the ere in were, the ould in would, and the w and o in two. Put them on the word wall (4 minutes). Read the title, Packed and Ready, to the children. Pairs read pages 1 to 3 of Packed and Ready, echo-reading: you read a line, they read it back, then each pair rereads. After each page they say in a few words what happened; do not let them guess a word from the picture (5 minutes). Spell asked and lifted with tiles, then write "Ted asked for a lamp." from your dictation (3 minutes).

Check: asked and lifted spelled with -ed; a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). Only the first two lines are used, the grade 1 words and the cycle 9 patterns. Each says the vowel sound in each word and the ending. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: build asked, played and lifted, then say the ending sound. Sort: the ten cycle 9 sort cards. Reread: pairs read pages 1 to 3 of Packed and Ready twice, the second time smoothly. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which sound does -ed make in asked?" Expect: /t/.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the picture cards on card with the word on the back. One copy of article C: read the part aloud and show it on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. Large group: pair children so each pair speaks for 40 seconds, or hear the rotating six and the rest tell a partner.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw sheep, cows and a bunch of grapes on paper. No tiles: write letters on scraps.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. A name for many.** Draw or point to sheep, cows and grapes. Say "a flock of sheep, a herd of cows, a bunch of grapes" and your child says each back.
  **Say.** "How does a group word help someone picture the scene?" Expect: it tells many things together as one.
- **10 min. Plurals that change.** Say one child, two children; one tooth, two teeth; one mouse, two mice. Your child says each pair and then says what you would call two geese.
- **5 min. Spell.** Say asked and lifted. Your child writes each and says which sound -ed makes.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X54a]] [[s02-X54b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs sort the ten cards and pick group words for two pictures; keep the exit, the reader and the oral card. Longer: a child writes three group-word sentences about a trip, or finds a plural in article C and says it as a riddle.

### Words and supports

Flock, herd, bunch, crowd, children, teeth, mice, geese, fish. Frames: "A flock of ___." "One ___, two ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Then children use himself, herself and themselves and say what happened yesterday with got, wrote and felt.
