# Fossil sentence cards (day 56)

Eight cards, each with a sentence that has a blank and two printed words. Pairs pick the word that fits and point to the thing card or the action card.

1. The ____ rock sat by the path. (quiet, quietly)
2. The cat crept ____ to the door. (soft, softly)
3. The ____ child held the shell. (proud, proudly)
4. She carried the tray ____. (careful, carefully)
5. A ____ stone lay on the table. (smooth, smoothly)
6. He wrote his name ____. (neat, neatly)
7. The ____ dog sat by the door. (sad, sadly)
8. The girls waited ____. (patient, patiently)

Key: 1 quiet (thing), 2 softly (action), 3 proud (thing), 4 carefully (action), 5 smooth (thing), 6 neatly (action), 7 sad (thing), 8 patiently (action).
