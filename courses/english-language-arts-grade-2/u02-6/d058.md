# Day 58: Mending the article-reading gaps

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 57 charts and slips by the error letters you tagged (C to J) into four piles: topic and purpose (errors C and D), features and reference (errors E and G), word parts (errors H, I and J), sentence context (errors F and G). A child with two letters joins the group of the first. Mark today's six (group 1 of five in cycle 11) on the unit 2 record sheet. Hang the sound-spelling-map. Keys: key-cycle-10-sort-cards.md, key-compound-cards-day-51.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair (feedback)

Hand back day 57's assessment, the charts and slips, one comment on each. Then repair the commonest miss of the day 57 check together, and day 56's exit error U, with a fresh card: "The girl hummed a ___ tune. (sweet, sweetly)" Ask: "Which word fits the gap?" Children say which word and what it tells about. Hear three.

#### 10 min. Group 1: topic and purpose (errors C and D)

Read a fresh made-up paragraph: "Scientists use brushes of many sizes. A wide brush sweeps away dust. A thin brush cleans small cracks. Each size has a job." Ask: "What is the topic?" Each child answers (about 30 seconds). Then ask: "What does the author explain?" Each child answers. Expect: brushes; why a scientist uses different sizes.

#### 10 min. Group 2: features and reference (errors E and G)

Pairs use article C. Each child walks a finger from the index to the page, then from a glossary word to its meaning, then from a menu icon to its page. Say aloud: "No digital copy is in the room, so we use the print one." Watch for the long way round; say "Which feature is nearest?" once.

#### 10 min. Group 3: word parts (errors H, I and J)

Predict, then check the sentence, with three fresh words. Unfold: the prefix flip card, "She unfolded the map on the table." Refill: the flip card, "Please refill the cup." Doghouse: a compound slip, "The dog sleeps in his doghouse." Children say the parts, predict, then check against the sentence. Hear each child once.

#### 10 min. Group 4: sentence context and definitions (errors F and G)

Read: "The bark of the old tree was rough. The dog's bark was loud." Ask: "Which meaning of bark fits the first sentence?" Each child answers, with the clue word. Then read the second and ask again. Keep the one that fits.

#### 10 min. Fresh paragraph retry

Hand each pair Fossil Work, Three Short Paragraphs. Each child tries the strategy tagged as a miss, one ask, in writing or aloud, and not the day 57 article: for C, "What is paragraph 3 about?"; for D, "What does paragraph 1 mainly explain?"; for E, "Use the caption to say what the brush does."; for F, "Say what measure means from what comes after it."; for G, "Does brush in the caption mean a tool for clearing dirt or a hairbrush?"; for H to J, "Use the small word in cleaning to say what cleaning means." Collect the slips.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X58 aloud twice and ask it. The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; tag error H on a miss.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Return the cycle 10 check. Reread its commonest misses with fresh words: sorted, wished, snowed, and you'd. Children say the ending alone, then the whole word (errors Y, Z and AH).

#### 15 min. Teach: oo and ou saying /oo/

Review: a vowel team is two letters that make one sound, as ai in rain (unit 1). Review: ou says /ow/ in house (cycle 7). Model zoo, room and food: map oo as one sound, blend, say the word (6 minutes). Model youth and coupon: here ou says /oo/ (3 minutes). Children say each. Spell zoo and room with tiles, then write "The zoo has a big room." from your dictation (6 minutes). Never guess a word from its picture. The reader begins on day 59.

Check: zoo and room spelled with oo; a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 1) sit with you for the cycle 11 oral card, two minutes each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build zoo, room and food), sort (the twelve cycle 10 cards), reread (Packed and Ready). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which letters make the sound in zoo?" Expect: oo.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the three paragraphs large on one sheet and the words on folded slips. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 30 seconds; a group of more than ten splits.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on scraps.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read it again.** Hand back the check; your child tells you one answer they would change and why.
- **10 min. A fresh paragraph.** Read two sentences about any animal. Your child says the topic and what the writer explains.
  **Say.** "What is the topic?" Expect: a few words naming the animal or thing.
- **10 min. Words.** Say zoo and room. Your child writes each and says which letters make the sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X58]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups meet for 8 minutes each; keep the retry, the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child writes a sentence with a word that has two meanings.

### Words and supports

Topic, caption, bark, zoo, room, food. Frames: "The topic is ___." "The word means ___ because ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 opens with a repair of today's exit (error H, a prefix word whose meaning was not checked against the sentence), then turns to the story The Mark in the Stone and planning one event, with more cycle 11 patterns and the first reader.
