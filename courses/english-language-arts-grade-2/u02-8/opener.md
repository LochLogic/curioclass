# Section 2.8 opener: revise, edit and check (days 64 to 68)

## What the section does

Four days of mending writing and one check day. On day 64 each child reads one line of feedback on their draft and changes one thing, keeping the event the same. Day 65 edits capital letters for holidays, places and made-up product names. Day 66 writes a short letter with commas in the greeting and the closing. Day 67 edits contractions and words that show who has something. Day 68 is a check day: a fresh article for its topic and the focus of each paragraph, and a fresh story for how its ending finishes its beginning.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds a repair, a short reread, one model, pair work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block follows cycle 12: -tion on day 63 and -sion on day 64, with the reader *A Choice of Actions*, a fresh passage on day 66, a 15-minute written check on day 67 and a rotation. On day 68 the foundations block moves to flexible groups. The exits on days 64 and 67 are written on paper.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Revising changes the writing, not the story.** One new line answers what the reader asked.
- **Special names take capital letters.** A holiday, a place and a product name.
- **A letter has a greeting and a closing, each with a comma.**
- **An apostrophe does one of two jobs.** It stands for missing letters, or it shows who has something.

## The errors, in plain words

- **V:** an added line that leaves the story. Read the feedback and ask "Does this answer it?"
- **AE, AF:** a special name left lowercase, or a missing comma. Hold up a common-name card and a special-name card; walk a finger down the letter.
- **AG, AH:** a missing apostrophe in a belongs-to word or a contraction.
- **A:** the opening and the ending mixed up.
- **Y and Z:** a wrong ending written, or an ending dropped.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Exits are unscored; record the exit looks on the unit record sheet. The check day rows are scored after class.
