# Day 64: Revising a draft after feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Last night you wrote two lines on each draft page: one on focus (one find only) and one on elaboration (an action, a thought or a feeling). Hand them back with a partner suggestion strip. Write Cal's draft large with the feedback "Tell the reader how Cal felt." Cut three strips: "He felt curious.", "Cal's shoes were blue.", "The bus came at noon." Mark today's six (group 2 of five in cycle 12) on the unit 2 record sheet. Key: key-cycle-12-sort-cards.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Hand back the day 63 exit tickets (item s02-X63) and repair them (errors P and O) with fresh cards. Error P (3 minutes): read aloud twice, "Mara found a gray feather in the grass. First she picked it up. Next she held it to the sun." Read two closings: "At last she saw it was a gull's feather." and "Mara drank some juice." Ask: "Which closing answers the start?" Hear three children. Error O (2 minutes): read, "Jun found a seed. He rinsed it. He dried it." Pairs slide a time-word card in front of each action.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: revise means change work to make it better (day 24). Review: elaborate means say more about one moment (day 61). Review: feedback is kind, specific, helpful words about a draft (grade 1). Review: focus is what one paragraph is about (day 46); a story keeps a focus too, one find. Say each once and point to the sentence on the wall: "Feedback helps me revise my draft."

#### 8 min. Read your feedback

Review: in unit 1 you changed a paragraph after written feedback (day 19). Each child reads the two lines on their draft to a partner. The partner points to the part of the draft each line fits. The child circles the line to use today. Pointing is an answer. Go to any child who cannot read a line and read it aloud.

#### 12 min. Model: revise one thing

Show Cal's draft (made up) and read it aloud with the feedback "Tell the reader how Cal felt." Lay the three strips beside it. Ask: "Which strip answers the feedback?" Children point; hear four. Slide "He felt curious." in after "Next he wondered, Is it a root?" and read the whole page again. Ask: "What stayed the same?" Expect: the event, the time words, the ending. Ask: "What is new?" Expect: one line about how Cal felt. Say: "A new line answers the feedback and stays on the find." Set the other two strips aside and say why: they leave the find.

#### 18 min. Revise

Partners write one thing for the child to explain on the strip, "My partner suggests: Explain why ___" (3 minutes). Each child then changes or adds one thing that the circled feedback names, keeps the event the same, and puts a star by the new line (12 minutes). Each child reads the new line aloud to a partner (3 minutes). You move and give no wording. A child who cannot write dictates to you.

If a new line leaves the find (error V), read the feedback aloud and ask: "Does this new line answer it?"

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "What did your partner suggest, and how did it help the reader?" Partners turn and talk, then one links: "I agree because ___." or asks: "Which part do you mean?" Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the exit ticket, read the card of item s02-X64 aloud twice, and children write one sentence (3 minutes). While they write, call the six to you, read the card of item s02-X64b aloud twice, and ask it; each answers in about 20 seconds. Mark each as shown, shown with help (after "What does the feedback say?") or not yet seen. Tag error V on a miss.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Hang nation, section and collection from the word wall. Review: tion says /shun/ (day 63). Children say each ending alone, then the whole word by chunk (na tion). Hear three. On a wrong ending (error Y), cover the first chunk, read the ending alone, then read the word.

#### 15 min. Teach: -sion, then the first reading

Model mapping mission, session and mansion: sion says /shun/ here too. Write the chunk under the sound, blend by chunk (mis sion), say the word (4 minutes). Model decision and explosion: after a vowel letter, sion says /zhun/. Children say which sound they hear in mission and in decision (3 minutes). Spell mission with tiles (3 minutes). Pairs echo-read pages 1 to 3 of A Choice of Actions, and after each page say what a mission is, a job you must do (5 minutes). Never guess a word from its picture.

Check: mission spelled with -sion, and a child says which sound it makes.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 2) sit with you for the cycle 12 oral card, two minutes each; the card is modeled today, so read every word except direction. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build mission, session, mansion), sort (the eight cycle 12 cards by ending) and reread (pages 4 to 6 of A Choice of Actions with a partner). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Which letters say the ending sound in mission?" Expect: sion.

### Other rooms

No printer: write Cal's draft and the three strips on a large sheet and rule a sheet for the exit. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read it aloud.** Your child reads the two lines of feedback on their draft.
  **Say.** "Which line will you use?" Expect: one line, and the part of the draft it fits.
- **10 min. Change one thing.** Your child adds one line the feedback asks for. The story stays the same.
- **10 min. Words.** Say mission and decision. Your child writes each and says which letters make the ending sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X64]] and [[s02-X64b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the star-and-read-aloud step in revise and keep the model, the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child revises a second thing from the other feedback line.

### Words and supports

Revise, feedback, focus, mission, decision, session. Frames: "My partner suggests ___." "I added ___ because ___." "Which part do you mean?" Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

After class read each revised draft and mark any new line that leaves the find. Day 65 opens with a repair of today's exit (error V, a sentence that leaves the find), then edits capital letters for holidays, places and product names in the draft. It reads A Choice of Actions again and sorts all twelve cycle 12 cards.
