# Exit cards, days 64 to 68

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. You read each card twice (or hand over the printed card where the day says so), then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 64 (s02-X64).** Card: "Draft: I saw a bird. It flew away. Feedback: Tell what the bird looked like." Ask: "Write one sentence to add that follows the feedback."
- **Day 64 (s02-X64b).** Card: "Draft: Ria found a smooth stone. She brushed it clean. Her bus was late. She saw a curved line. Feedback: Keep to one find." Ask: "Say the one sentence that should come out." The six answer aloud.
- **Day 65 (s02-X65).** Printed card: "Hugo and Rita saw a flinkbrush bag at lake huron on earth day." Ask: "Say each special name the way it should be written."
- **Day 66 (s02-X66a).** Printed card: "dear wes" / "The fossil trays came today." / "see you soon" / "Opal" Ask: "Say the greeting as it should be written."
- **Day 66 (s02-X66b).** Printed card: "dear wes" / "The fossil trays came today." / "see you soon" / "Opal" Ask: "Say the closing as it should be written."
- **Day 67 (s02-X67a).** Printed card: "This is Finns map. We havent used it yet." Ask: "Write Finns so it shows the map belongs to him."
- **Day 67 (s02-X67b).** Printed card: "This is Finns map. We havent used it yet." Ask: "Say havent the correct way, letter by letter, with the apostrophe."
