# Cycle 12 written check (day 67, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 63 to 66, and none is action, vision, motion, mission, expression or fiction.

1. "We each had a ____ of pie." (portion)
2. "That ____ is new." (invention)
3. "We had a ____ in class." (discussion)
4. "He had a look of ____." (confusion)

Tag wrong spellings: "porshun", "inventshun", "discushun" or "confuzhun" put the wrong letters on the ending sound (Y); "port" or "invent" omit the ending (Z). Tag the letter that fits. After the check is marked, dictate "fiction" as unscored practice.
