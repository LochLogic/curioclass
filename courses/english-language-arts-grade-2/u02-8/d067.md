# Day 67: Apostrophes and an editing checklist

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read each letter from day 66 and keep two that miss a comma for the opening repair. Hang page 1 and page 9 of The Mark in the Stone. Write the four checklist lines on the board. Mark today's six (group 5 of five in cycle 12) on the unit 2 record sheet. Keep the cycle 12 written check ready to read aloud. Keys: key-apostrophe-cards-day-67.md, key-cycle-12-sort-cards.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This letter is made up for practice." Hand back the day 66 exit cards (items s02-X66a and s02-X66b) and repair error AF with a fresh letter. Read aloud: "Dear Lin" then "My stone is gray." then "Yours truly" then "Tam". Walk a finger down the model letter on the wall and stop at each comma. Then children point to where the fresh letter needs a comma. Hear three, and say: "A comma follows the greeting and the closing."

#### 3 min. Words

Review: you read he'd, I'd and we'd as whole words (day 53). Today the words are apostrophe, contraction and belongs to. An apostrophe is a small mark that looks like a comma set high. A contraction squeezes two words into one. Belongs to means a thing has an owner. Say each once and show it on an apostrophe card.

#### 6 min. Reread

Reread "Amara's class went to a fossil park" from page 1 and "I can't tell you for sure" from page 9 of The Mark in the Stone. Say: "Amara and Dr. Lee are made up." Children point to each apostrophe. Ask: "What do you notice about Amara's and can't?" Hear three. Do not name the two jobs yet.

#### 12 min. Model: two kinds of apostrophe

Write can't, isn't, it's and we're. Say each as two words and cover the letters that are gone with a finger: can not, is not, it is, we are. Say: "A contraction squeezes two words, and the apostrophe stands where letters are gone." Write Kofi's bag and the guide's brush. Ask: "Does the bag belong to someone?" Say: "A belongs-to word shows who has something." For each word on the board ask: "Gone letters, or belongs to someone?" Children answer together. Go back to Amara's and can't and say which kind each is.

#### 10 min. Pairs sort and fix

Pairs sort the eight apostrophe cards under gone letters and belongs to someone. Then they take the four printed sentences, ring the one word in each that needs an apostrophe, and say which kind it is to a partner. No apostrophe is written on the card; it is written in the draft. You move and ask each pair about one sentence.

#### 8 min. Edit with a checklist

Review: a checklist helps you edit your own writing (grade 1, day 30). Hand out the editing checklist card with its four lines: capital letters, commas, apostrophes, spelling checked. Review: you check a spelling in the dictionary or on the word wall (day 27). Each child reads their draft four times, once for each line, and marks a box when that line is checked. Say: "Editing mends the marks and the spelling. It does not change the story."

#### 6 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "How can you tell a contraction from a belongs-to word?" Partners turn and talk, then link: "I agree because ___." Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Write

Each child writes one sentence with a belongs-to word and one with a contraction, a few words each, and rings the apostrophe in each. A child who cannot write dictates to you.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give each child the printed card for items s02-X67a and s02-X67b and the exit ticket, and read the card aloud twice. Ask item s02-X67a, and children write the answer on the ticket. Collect the tickets and mark each from the page as shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Tag error AG for a possessive with no apostrophe. Then ask item s02-X67b aloud: your six say it letter by letter to you, about 20 seconds each, and the rest say it to a partner. Mark the six as shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and tag error AH for a contraction said with no apostrophe.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Unscored. Say each sentence of the cycle 12 written check twice and the missing word once (8 minutes). Children write the word in the gap. Collect the sheets. Dictate fiction as practice and children write it (3 minutes). Review the four words aloud with their endings (4 minutes). Tag error Y or Z as the card says.

#### 5 min. Reader

Pairs reread A Choice of Actions for expression, once each way, one reads a page and the other reads the next.

#### 25 min. Rotation

Standard rotation, two rounds of 12 minutes. Round 1: today's six (group 5) read the cycle 12 oral card to you, two minutes each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in two stations of twelve: tiles with the sort cards, and a reread of A Choice of Actions. Round 2: the groups swap.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

No new teaching. Day 63's six read the cycle 12 words to you, two minutes each (12 minutes). The others partner-read the cycle 12 partner word card and spell vision and direction as you dictate them.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the eight apostrophe words on slips and the four sentences on one sheet, write the checklist on the board, and rule a sheet for the exit. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Find the mark.** Look at a page of a book at home. Your child finds an apostrophe and says if letters are gone or something belongs to someone.
  **Say.** "Gone letters, or belongs to someone?" Expect: one of the two, with a reason.
- **10 min. Check a draft.** Your child reads their draft once for capitals, once for commas and once for apostrophes.
- **10 min. Words.** Say mission and session. Your child writes each and says which letters make the ending sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X67a]] and [[s02-X67b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards instead of eight and keep the checklist, the exit and the written check. Longer: a child sorts ten more words from a book into gone letters and belongs to someone.

### Words and supports

Apostrophe, contraction, belongs to, can't, isn't, it's, we're. Frames: "Letters are gone because ___." "It belongs to ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

After class mark the exit tickets (errors AG and AH) and the written check (errors Y and Z). Day 68 is the end-of-unit check: an article read aloud once and a story read twice, with an exit. It has no topic repair slot, so its first five minutes in the foundations block repair AG, AH, Y and Z, and then flexible groups follow.
