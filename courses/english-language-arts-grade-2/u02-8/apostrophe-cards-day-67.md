# Apostrophe cards (day 67)

Eight cards and four printed sentences for each pair. Pairs sort the cards under **gone letters** (a contraction) and **belongs to someone**, then ring the one word in each sentence that needs an apostrophe and say which kind it is. No apostrophe is written on the card.

Cards: can't, isn't, it's, we're, Kofi's bag, the guide's brush, Mari's tray, the teacher's pen.

Sentences, printed with the missing apostrophe: "That isnt my cup." "The dogs bone is under the chair." "Were ready to go." "Is this Levs hat?"
