# Day 65: Capital letters for special names

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read each revised draft from day 64 and keep the ones with a new line that leaves the find, for the opening repair. Hang page 10 of The Mark in the Stone. Write the eight sorting names on slips for the board. Mark today's six (group 3 of five in cycle 12) on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand back the day 64 exit tickets (item s02-X64) and repair error V with a fresh card. Read aloud twice: "Draft: Lee found a long gray bone. He brushed it clean. Feedback: Tell what Lee thought." Read two new sentences: "Lee wondered if it was a leg bone." and "Lee likes pizza." Ask: "Does this new sentence answer the feedback?" for each. Hear three children and say why the second leaves the find.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: a capital letter starts a name (grade 1, day 50). Today the words are capital, holiday, place and product. A holiday is a special day, a place is where something is, and a product is a thing a maker sells. Say each once and show it on its card. Zipfold and Brightmark are names invented for practice.

#### 8 min. Reread

Reread page 10 of The Mark in the Stone aloud, where Dr. Lee writes the place and the day on a label. Say: "Dr. Lee and Amara are made up." Children point to the capital letters in Dr. Lee and in Amara, and say why a person's name has one. Hear four. Say: "A special name begins with a capital."

#### 12 min. Model: one special name gets a capital

Sort the eight cards on the board in four pairs: a holiday and Thanksgiving; a lake and Lake Erie; a state and Utah; a pen and a Zipfold pen. Each time ask: "One special holiday, place or product?" The special card goes under special and the common card under common. Say: "A special name is one special holiday, place or product, and it begins with a capital." Write "on thanksgiving we went to lake erie." large, read it aloud, and underline the two special names. Children say what kind each is.

#### 12 min. Pairs fix

Pairs get the six lowercase sentences on the capital cards. For each, they underline the special name and write beside it H for a holiday, P for a place or B for a product. The partner checks it against the sorted cards and says: "One special holiday, place or product?" You move and ask each pair about one sentence. No capitals are written on the card; they are written in the draft.

#### 7 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Review: the talk routine from day 1. Ask: "How do you know a word needs a capital letter?" Partners turn and talk, then link: "I agree because ___." Hear the six (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Edit

Each child checks their draft for a holiday, a place or a product name, and writes each with its capital. If the draft has none, the child writes one sentence with a holiday and a place. Mark each fixed name with a pencil tick.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give each child the printed card of item s02-X65 and read it aloud twice. Say: "Flinkbrush is a name made up for practice." The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each (2 minutes); the rest tell a partner. Mark shown, shown with help (after "What does the card say?") or not yet seen. Tag error AE on a miss.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Hang mission, session, mansion, decision and explosion from the word wall. Children say the ending alone, then the whole word by chunk. Hear three. On a wrong ending (error Y), cover the first chunk, read the ending alone, then read the word.

#### 15 min. Reread, sort and spell

Pairs reread A Choice of Actions (4 minutes). Pairs sort the twelve cycle 12 cards under -tion, -sion saying /shun/ and -sion saying /zhun/; read direction, action, motion, fiction and vision by chunk first (5 minutes). Spell fiction, direction and session with tiles (3 minutes). Dictate "The class has a collection." and children write it (2 minutes). Review: would is a mapped word with a starred part, as were and two (day 54) (1 minute). Never guess a word from its picture.

Check: the sentence has a capital, a period, and collection spelled with -tion.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six (group 3) sit with you for the cycle 12 oral card, two minutes each, with direction now added. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 are in three stations of eight: tiles (build nation, direction, session), sort (the twelve cycle 12 cards) and reread (A Choice of Actions with a partner). Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: groups move on.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "How many sounds can -sion say?" Expect: two, /shun/ and /zhun/.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the eight names on slips and the six sentences on one large sheet, and copy the exit card by hand. Large group: each child in a group answers once, about 20 seconds; the six hear you in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Find names.** Look at a food box or a calendar. Your child points to a holiday, a place or a maker's name and says which kind it is.
  **Say.** "Which letter starts it?" Expect: a capital.
- **10 min. Fix it.** Your child checks their draft for a holiday, place or product name and writes it with a capital.
- **10 min. Words.** Say direction and session. Your child writes each and says which letters make the ending sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X65]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards instead of eight and keep the underline, the exit and the teaching. Longer: a child writes two sentences, one with a holiday and one with a place, and checks each capital.

### Words and supports

Capital, holiday, place, product, direction, session. Frames: "It is one special ___." "It begins with a capital because ___." Pointing is an answer. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 opens with a repair of today's exit (error AE, a special name left lowercase), then reads a model letter and writes a short fossil letter with a comma in the greeting and in the closing. It reads Nell at the Station in the foundations block.
