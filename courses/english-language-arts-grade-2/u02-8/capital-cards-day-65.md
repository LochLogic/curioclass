# Capital cards (days 65 and 66)

Eight sorting cards and six sentences for each pair.

Sorting cards, four pairs of a common name and a special name: a holiday and Thanksgiving; a lake and Lake Erie; a state and Utah; a pen and a Zipfold pen (an invented product name). Pairs sort them under **common** and **special**.

Six sentences printed in lowercase. Pairs underline each special name and write beside it H (holiday), P (place) or B (a product, from a maker) to say what kind it is; no capital is written on the card: "We saw fireworks on fourth of july." "Rae went to texas in the summer." "My brightmark crayons are new." "On labor day we walked by lake michigan." "Ravi has a plinkit kit." "We ate pie on thanksgiving."
