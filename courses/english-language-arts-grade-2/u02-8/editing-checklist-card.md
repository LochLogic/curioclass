# Editing checklist card (days 67 and 71)

A card with four lines, each with a box for a pencil mark: **Capital letters on names, holidays and places**, **Commas in a greeting and a closing**, **Apostrophes where letters are gone or something belongs to someone**, **Spelling checked in the dictionary or word wall**. Each child marks the boxes while editing their own narrative.
