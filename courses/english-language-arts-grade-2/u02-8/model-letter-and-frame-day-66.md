# Model letter and letter frame (days 66 and 67)

The model letter, kept on the wall: "Dear Pip," then "I found a smooth stone by the creek." then "Your pal," then "Rhys". Read aloud as an invented model.

The letter frame for each child: a greeting line that begins "Dear" with a blank for the name, three ruled lines, a closing line with a blank, and a line for the writer's name. No comma is printed. A child who needs it gets a copy with the two commas printed in grey. Children write to a classmate about their find.
