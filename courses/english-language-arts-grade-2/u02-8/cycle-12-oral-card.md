# Cycle 12 oral card (days 63 to 67)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 63 only the first two lines are used. Children read the words and say the ending sound; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 11 patterns.** moon, soup, fruit, blue, flew, zoo, room
- **Cycle 12 patterns (-tion, -sion).** nation, section, collection, direction, mission, session, mansion, decision, explosion
- **A sentence.** The nation made a decision at the session.

Partner word card for day 67: nation, section, collection, direction, mission, session, decision, explosion. Dictated words: fiction, vision.
