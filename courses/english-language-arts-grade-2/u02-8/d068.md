# Day 68: A check on an article and a story

## Teacher page

### Before class

A check: nothing is taught, held up or hinted at. Print the article with no headings. Sort the day 67 sheets by error letter (Y, Z, AG, AH). Mark today's six on the unit 2 record sheet.

### The day

#### 12 min. RI.2.2 item

Say: "This article is made up." Read Notes From the Rock aloud once, children following the print (3 minutes). Ask items s02-A29 to s02-A32 once each; children write a few words in the chart box (9 minutes). Reread an ask once on request. Collect the charts. Mark shown, shown with help (after the reread) or not yet seen; tag error C.

#### 20 min. RL.2.5 item

Say: "This story is made up." Read Dev's Puzzle aloud twice, children following the print (4 minutes). Ask item s02-A33 once; children draw or write on the student page, and the six tell you aloud, about 30 seconds each (8 minutes). Ask item s02-A34 the same way (8 minutes). Collect the pages. Mark shown, shown with help (after one reread) or not yet seen; tag error A.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read A Choice of Actions aloud twice, children following their copy, and ask item s02-X68v2. The six answer to you, about 30 seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Tag error A on a miss.

#### 23 min. Independent reading

Each child reads A Choice of Actions alone and writes one line about it. Unscored; no reteaching.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Reread six -tion and -sion cards from the day 67 misses (errors Y and Z): the ending alone, then the word. Then repair errors AG and AH with fresh words: write hasn't and Rae's boat, cover the apostrophe, and ask: "Which letters are gone?" and "Does the boat belong to someone?"

#### 45 min. Flexible groups

Three groups of ten, set from the day 67 check and oral record, three rounds of 15 minutes: the teacher group (map, blend, read A Choice of Actions or Nell at the Station, spell two words), partner reread, and tiles (build mission, session, nation, decision). No oral queue today; days 75 and 76 hold that.

Check: each child reads or builds each word and a partner says if it matches the card.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Note which children move groups tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Reread

The class rereads A Choice of Actions aloud together, for expression.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the texts large. A child may say an answer instead of writing or drawing it.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The groups and the independent reading.

- **10 min. The assessment at home.** Read a short story aloud twice. Your child says what the beginning tells and what the ending finishes.
  **Say.** "How does the end finish what the start began?" Expect: the problem from the start is solved at the end.
- **10 min. A short article.** Read two or three sentences about any animal or thing aloud once. Your child says the topic in a few words.
- **10 min. Read it again.** Your child rereads A Choice of Actions, a page at a time, with expression.
- **5 min. Words.** Say mission and decision. Your child writes each and says which letters make the ending sound.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X68v2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the independent reading and keep the article, the story and the exit. Longer: a child draws the beginning and the ending of a story they know.

### Words and supports

Answers may be drawn or spoken.

### Tomorrow

Group children by error letter (A and C) for day 74. Day 69 is cold: it drafts a new discovery narrative with event details, time words and a closing.
