# Cycle 6 oral card (days 33 to 37)

The day's rotating six read the card to you, two minutes each. On day 33 only the first line is used, because no cycle 6 pattern was taught before that day. Children read the words and say each vowel sound; you mark shown, shown with help, or not yet seen.

- **Grade 1 words.** shelf, class, cloth, lunch, thank, chest
- **Cycle 6 patterns (ar, or, er, ir, ur).** car, park, jar, corn, horn, born, her, bird, turn, fern
- **A sentence.** A bird sat on the corn.

Partner word card for day 37 (read to each other, then spell two words you say): car, jar, horn, her, bird, turn. Dictated words: park, fern.
