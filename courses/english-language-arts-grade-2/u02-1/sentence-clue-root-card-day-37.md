# Sentence clue and small word card (day 37)

A card folded in half. On the left, step one: **Read the sentence around the word.** On the right, step two: **Look for a small word inside it.** Under the steps, three lines to fill: the word, the clue in the sentence, the small word.

Practice words for the teacher to use, none from the check: **careful** (care), **patient** (patience is a wait without hurry), and **fan** in "a fan-shaped mark" against "a fan that blows air". Say each in its sentence, then reject the meaning that does not fit.
