# Beginning and ending match cards (day 36)

Eight cards. Pairs lay each beginning card beside the ending card that finishes it and say why they fit.

**Beginning cards**
1. Mira stops in the doorway of a new classroom and does not know where to go.
2. Amara's class goes to a fossil park, and she finds a curved mark in a small stone.

**Ending cards**
1. Mira opens the door for the newest pupil and says welcome.
2. Amara tells her friend what the mark in the stone is.

Keep two spare cards blank.
