# Cycle 6 written check (day 37, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 33 to 36.

1. "The cow sleeps in the ____." (barn)
2. "Please ____ the soup with a spoon." (stir)
3. "A rose has a sharp ____." (thorn)
4. "Do not ____ your hand on the hot pan." (burn)

Tag wrong spellings: "bern", "ster", "thron" and "birn" each write a taught vowel correspondence wrongly (Y). After the check is marked, dictate "market" and "tiger" as unscored practice (closed and open syllables).
