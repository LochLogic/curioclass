# Beginning dictionary page, cycle 6 (day 35)

One page for each pair, with nine entries in alphabetical order. Guide words at the top: **bird** and **turn**. Children use it to check one spelling.

**bird**, noun. An animal with feathers and wings.

**car**, noun. A machine with wheels that carries people.

**corn**, noun. A yellow food that grows in rows on a tall plant.

**curl**, noun. A bit of hair that bends round.

**fern**, noun. A green plant with soft, feathery leaves.

**her**, word. Belongs to a girl or a woman.

**jar**, noun. A glass pot with a lid.

**orange**, noun. A round fruit with a thick skin.

**turn**, verb. To go round or change direction.

Steps to say: find the first letter; find the entry; compare the letters with your spelling; correct it. "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___."
