# Story map with beginning and ending boxes (days 34 to 42)

Take the unit 1 story map (Who, Where and when, What happens, Challenge, Response). Fold a new sheet in thirds and add two wide boxes: **The beginning introduces** at the top and **The ending concludes** at the bottom, with the four middle steps **First**, **Next**, **Then** and **At last** between them. The beginning box has three prompts: who, where and when, and what starts. The teacher holds the large map on days 34 and 36; partners hold their own from day 36.
