# Text chart (day 33)

Fold a sheet in half. Label the left column **The text tells** and the right column **The text leaves open**. Under the headings, draw one line for each entry. Children add one entry to each column in turn, in a few words or a drawing, and say where the story gives it. The teacher holds the wall chart; pairs keep their folded sheet for day 34.
