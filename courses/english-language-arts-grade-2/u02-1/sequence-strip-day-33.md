# Sequence strip (day 33)

Four cards cut from a sheet, labelled **First**, **Next**, **Then** and **At last**, and one strip of paper long enough to lay them in order. While you reread pages 4 to 7, pairs lay the four cards on the strip and say aloud what Amara does at each.
