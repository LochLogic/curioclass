# Day 35: Mary Anning looks closely

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the student page for each child and the account for each pair. Mark today's six (group 3 of five) on the unit 2 record sheet and its heard column. Have day 34's exit marks beside you: error A is the miss to repair. Write four fact slips by hand (see the sequence part). Set three stations: tiles, the sort cards, the reader reread. The story map stays on the wall. Keys: key-sequence-strip-day-33.md, key-sort-cards-cycle-6.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 34's exit (error A, the middle or end named for the beginning), point at the story map and read two lines from The Mark in the Stone: "Amara picked up a small, flat stone" (page 2) and "Amara told her friend what was in her bag" (page 11). Ask: "Which line goes in the beginning box?" Children point to the first and say why: it starts the story. Otherwise partners tell each other what the beginning of The Mark in the Stone introduces.

#### 9 min. Read aloud: Mary Anning Looks Closely

Say: "This is a true account of a real person, Mary Anning. It tells what happened and does not say what she thought." Read the whole account aloud (about 370 words), then pause on the headings. Say plainly, in one sentence each and without lingering, that her father died and that the family did not have much money; and that a club for scientists did not let women join. Then go on. Say that her animals lived very long ago, with no number. Review: careful, said in unit 1 (look closely so nothing is missed). Children listen for where and when things happened.

#### 8 min. Question words

Write the six question words on the board: who, what, where, when, why, how. Review: you asked question words in unit 1 on day 1 and in kindergarten. Reread "Growing Up by the Sea" (about 85 words). Model a question and its answer from the text: "Where was Mary born? In Lyme Regis, a town on the coast of England." Pairs then ask one where question and one when question about Mary, answering each in a whole sentence with a finger on the line (5 minutes). Hear three pairs (3 minutes). Do not ask about her father; keep to places and dates.

**Ask.** "Say one where question and its answer." Expect: for example, "Where did Mary look for fossils? On the beach."

#### 12 min. Sequence cards

Pairs have the strip (First, Next, Then, At last) and four fact slips you wrote by hand. Each slip holds one fact: (a) in 1811 her brother Joseph found a strange skull in the cliff; (b) then Mary dug out the rest of the skeleton; (c) in 1823 she found a nearly whole plesiosaur; (d) in 1828 she found a flying reptile skeleton. Pairs read each slip, lay them in the order the account gives, and say "First, in 1811, ___." (6 minutes). Rearrange once if the numbers disagree. Review: the strip and First, Next, Then and At last from yesterday and from unit 1. Then say each aloud, one child a card, and the class repeats (4 minutes). Last, ask: "Which card tells the last find?" (2 minutes). Expect: the 1828 one, so At last.

#### 5 min. Say-back

Each child tells a partner one fact in a whole sentence with its time word: "In 1826, Mary ___." (2 minutes). Then each pair swaps and says the other's fact back to the class if asked (3 minutes). Do not mark.

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Question: "Which part of Mary's work took careful looking, and what in the text shows it?" Turn: each child tells a partner (2 minutes). Pair: partners find the line and read it aloud (3 minutes). Link: "You said ___; I would add ___." Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" Then hear today's six, about 30 seconds each (3 minutes), and after each, one child links or asks. Use the wait signal. Expect: she cleaned the bones and drew them (page 2).

#### 6 min. Write one answer

On the student page each child writes one question beginning with where, when or what about Mary, and its answer from the account (4 minutes). Partners check a capital, an end mark, a noun and a verb in each (2 minutes).

Check: the question begins with a capital and ends with a question mark, the answer ends with a mark, and each has a noun and a verb.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card of item s02-X35 aloud twice. Ask: "Say one question about Mary that this card answers." The six answer to you, about 20 seconds each; the others tell a partner. Shown means a question whose answer is in the card. Shown with help means that after one prompt: "What does the card tell about Mary?" Not yet seen means a question the card does not answer (error B) or a statement. Mark the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Review: you mapped yellow and orange on day 33. Say each word and children write it from memory, then check against the word wall and star the odd part. If many missed the o or the ge in orange, map it again, never from the first letter or the picture. Watch for spellings made by sound (yelo, orenge).

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Reread The Park Cart in pairs, the second time smoothly (3 minutes). Then pairs sort the twelve cards under ar, or and er, ir, ur, saying each word aloud (4 minutes), and sort the six syllable words by whether the first syllable is open or closed (2 minutes). Next, each child writes two words you say, jar and curl, then the sentence "The bird sat on her car." (3 minutes). Last, pairs use the dictionary page (nine entries with guide words, bird to turn) to check one spelling: find the first letter, find the entry, compare the letters, correct it, and say "I checked the page and changed ___ to ___." (3 minutes). Review: you used a dictionary page in unit 1, on day 27. Expect: jar, curl, and a capital and an end mark.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Standard rotation. Round 1, 13 minutes: today's six sit with you for the oral card, two minutes each (12 minutes). Each reads the cycle 6 words (car, park, jar, corn, horn, born, her, bird, turn, fern), then the sentence "A bird sat on the corn.", saying the vowel sound in each. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the heard column. The other 24 children are in three stations of eight. Tiles: jar, curl, her, then change one letter (jar to car, her to hen). Sort: the twelve cards again, then say a word and a partner points to its pattern. Reread: pairs read The Park Cart and one reads the other's page back for meaning. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each: the groups move on and the six join the two stations they have not met.

#### 3 min. Close

**Ask.** "Where do we look to check a spelling?" Expect: the dictionary page, the word wall or the sound-spelling-map.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the twelve words and the six syllable words on torn paper, and draw the strip on a long sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper. One copy of the account: read it aloud and write the four fact slips on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed account: read from your own words and use the strip drawn on a sheet. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The sorting, the stations and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read the account.** Read Mary Anning Looks Closely aloud together.
  **Say.** "Where did Mary look for fossils, and who showed her how?" Expect: on the beach at Lyme Regis; her father.
- **10 min. In order.** Your child writes two facts on slips and lays them in order with First and At last, saying one whole sentence for each.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say jar, curl and her. Your child writes each and checks it against the word card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X35]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read the first two headings only and lay two fact slips; keep the exit and the sort. Longer: a pair writes a third question and answer and says what time word goes with it.

### Words and supports

Collect, careful, fossil, discover. Question words: who, what, where, when, why, how. Frames: "Where ___? ___." "In 1811, Mary ___." "First ___. Then ___." "You said ___; I would add ___." Pointing and drawing are answers. A child may pass any turn.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 rereads pages 8 to 11 of the story for what the ending concludes. Error B on today's exit (a question the card does not answer) is repaired at its opening, with strips from the account.
