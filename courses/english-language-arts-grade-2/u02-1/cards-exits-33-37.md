# Exit cards, days 33 to 37

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards; you read each twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 33 (s02-X33).** "Zara notices a cone shape in a stone. She draws it before reading its label. The label says it is a fossil. She wonders what lived here long ago." Ask: "Tell me what Zara does, in the order the card gives."
- **Day 34 (s02-X34).** "Idris needs a label for a fossil drawing. He takes out his notes. Later he finds the name and labels the drawing." Ask: "Tell me what the beginning of this card introduces."
- **Day 35 (s02-X35).** "Mary Anning said she learned fossil collecting from her father. She became a fossil collector and dealer. Many people who knew fossils wanted her advice." Ask: "Say one question about Mary that this card answers."
- **Day 36 (s02-X36).** "Kemi wants a drawing to show a fossil's edge. She adds its missing notch. Now another reader can match the drawing to the fossil." Ask: "Tell me how the ending finishes what the beginning started."
- **Day 37 (s02-X37).** "Pat will sort the fossil finds in the lab. Pat is the sorter." Ask: "Use a small word you know to say what sorter means here."
