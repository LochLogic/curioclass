# Exit cards, days 50 to 53

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading. Children do not read the cards; you read each twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 50 (s02-X50).** "The fossil sketch is unfinished. Finished means complete. Mateo adds the missing edge." Ask: "Use un- and finished to tell me what unfinished means."
- **Day 51 (s02-X51).** "The worker keeps a fieldbook. Dictionary: field, open land; an area of study. Here the worker writes about an outdoor site." Ask: "Put the two parts together and tell me what fieldbook means here." Then, same card family (s02-X51b), a second card: "Dictionary: pen. One, a tool for writing with ink. Two, a small fence that keeps animals in. The farmer shut the sheep in the pen." Ask: "Which meaning of pen fits the sentence?"
- **Day 52 (s02-X52).** "The first paragraph says a drawing shows the shape of a find. The second paragraph says a photograph shows its colour." Ask: "Say the whole topic that both paragraphs share." Then, same card, s02-X52b: "Say what the second paragraph is about."
- **Day 53 (s02-X53).** "Title: Why Take Photographs? Subheading: Colour. Caption: This photograph records the colour of the rock." Ask: "Tell me what this text is meant to do." Then a second card (s02-X53b): "An article called Sea Stars has three subheadings: How They Move, What They Eat and Where They Live. It also has a glossary and an index." Ask: "A child wants to read about what sea stars eat. Which feature should the child use?"
