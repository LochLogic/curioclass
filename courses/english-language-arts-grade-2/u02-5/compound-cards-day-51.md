# Compound word cards (day 51)

Five slips, one whole word printed on each in 18 point, with a fold down the middle so that the two parts show apart when the slip is opened out. Pairs hold all five.

seashell (sea | shell), sandstone (sand | stone), hillside (hill | side), raincoat (rain | coat), sunlight (sun | light)

Pairs open a slip, read each part, say what each part means, predict what the whole word means, then close the slip and check the prediction against the sentence you say or the article sentence. The first three words are in article C (read to the class on day 49); the last two are spoken practice words. Every part is a word children can read from taught patterns.
