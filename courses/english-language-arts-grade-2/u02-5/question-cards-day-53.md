# Question cards (day 53)

Three cards about the third article. Pairs take each card, name the most useful feature among the subheading, bold word, caption, glossary, index and menu icon, and find the answer on the print.

1. What does a specimen mean?
2. Which page tells about footprints?
3. What does the caption under the track picture say?

Key for the teacher: 1 the glossary (or the bold word on page 3). 2 the index, page 2, or the footprint subheading. 3 the caption under the picture: a fossil track, and each print is a mold of a foot. Do not use notebook or location as a question.
