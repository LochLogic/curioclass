# Cycle 9 written check (day 52, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 48 to 51.

1. "The cat is ____ on the mat." (napping)
2. "She ____ her baby." (hugged)
3. "We are ____ the floor." (mopping)
4. "We are ____ a trip." (planning)

Tag wrong spellings: "naping", "huged", "moping" and "planing" each omit the doubling (AB); "nap" or "plan" for the whole word omits the ending (Z); tag the letter that fits. After the check is marked, dictate "making" and "stepped" as unscored practice (drop the e against double).
