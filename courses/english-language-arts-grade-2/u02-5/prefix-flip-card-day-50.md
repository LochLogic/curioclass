# Prefix flip card (day 50)

A slip of card folded so that a flap covers the front of a word. Write **cover** on the card, and on the flap write **un-**. Lift the flap to read **uncover**, then say "un- means not, so uncover means not covered". Make a second slip with **re-** over **play**: replay means play again. The flap lifts to show the prefix and covers it again. Pairs hold one card each.

Teacher note: only uncover is in the article. The other practice words (unlock, replay, rebuild) are spoken for practice.
