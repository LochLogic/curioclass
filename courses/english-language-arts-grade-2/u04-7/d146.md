# Day 146: Edit the packet and explain planting steps

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write first, next, then, after that, last on a wall strip. Shuffle the four step strips for each pair. Write the twelve sort words on cards. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 25 to 30) and the six for the oral card (children 13 to 18).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Hand back day 145's exit marks. Point to the wall strip: first, next, then, after that, last. Review: time words from unit 2, and the chain of steps from unit 3. Pairs say the five words in order, then each says one thing they do first at school.

#### 10 min. Reread 05-d

Pairs reread 05-d by turns, one paragraph each, and mark each sequence word they find with a pencil dot (5 minutes, RF.2.4a, RI.2.3). Then they put the shuffled strips in the article's order and say, for each strip, what the step changes in one word, such as "drains" for the holes (5 minutes). Visit pairs for about 20 seconds each.

#### 15 min. Shared writing

Repair first, 5 minutes, whole class, from day 143's exit (error Z): act the pot with and without stones, with a cup and torn paper for water, and say what goes wrong. Write "Stones go first because it is first" beside "Stones go first so extra water can drain out", and ask which one says what the step does. Then write five class planting steps on chart paper (3 minutes): choose a pot with holes, add stones and soil, scatter the seeds and cover them, water gently, put the pot in a sunny spot. Pairs each take one step, three pairs to a step, and each child says a complete sentence with the frame "The ___ helps ___" for it, 45 seconds each way (2 minutes). Eight children share, about 40 seconds each, and you write the best sentence by each step (5 minutes), for example "The stones help extra water drain out." The class reads the chart together.

#### 20 min. Edit the packet

Editing is its own pass, after the revision on day 145. Review: capital letters for names, the apostrophe and the comma in a greeting and closing, from unit 2 (days 65 to 67). With the editing checklist, each child makes four looks at the revised card, one pen color for each (W.2.5, L.2.2a to c, L.2.2e): (1) a capital for the start of each sentence and for a name, such as Sam or a park's name; (2) an end mark on each sentence; (3) the apostrophe in a word that has one, and the comma after "Dear planter" and before the closing; (4) one spelling checked in the printed dictionary. A box is ticked only after that look. Nobody scribes the conventions. The envelope and drawing are finished now. Read six cards in full, one minute each, and glance at the rest; the edited card is kept for the scoring on day 154. Error N: a mark left uncorrected; tap the line and ask what the checklist box asks.

#### 5 min. Pairs check

Pairs check each other's card against the checklist, one look at a time (2 minutes each way, 1 minute to swap). The partner may point but not change the card.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice the experts paragraph of 05-b and show the three step strips (1 minute): [[s04-X146v2]]. Each child marks one of the three strips (1 minute). Hear four of the six, 45 seconds each with the swap: [[s04-X146bv2]] (3 minutes); the other two are heard on day 147. The rest read their edited card to themselves. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From the commonest foundations miss on yesterday's spelling (error Q, a first-letter guess; error R, a pattern not carried over; error S, a spelling left unchecked): on slates, fix artical and tropicle, and say which ending each word takes.

#### 12 min. Reread the cycle 25 reader

Pairs reread A Little Miracle for accuracy, a page each (5 minutes). The class reads it again smoothly, pausing at each full stop (4 minutes, RF.2.4b). Pairs read it once more with the voice of someone who is surprised (3 minutes). No timer; accuracy comes first.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 13 to 18 read the cycle 25 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. Model one -cal word on the board before the first turn. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Sort

Pairs sort twelve word cards by ending (7 minutes): -cal, vertical, tropical, comical, logical, typical, vocal; -cle, article, miracle, vehicle, particle, bicycle, tentacle. Model tentacle and particle first. Then each child picks four cards, looks, covers, writes from memory and checks (8 minutes): this is not dictation.

#### 11 min. Use the pattern in sentences

Each child writes one sentence with a cycle word (7 minutes) and checks one spelling against the sound-spelling map (2 minutes). Two children read theirs to the class (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs read one sentence aloud to each other.

### Other rooms

Write the checklist on the board and tick on paper. The acting uses a cup and torn paper. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No four pens: use two and a pencil. No word cards: write the words on scraps.
**Skip.** The oral card and the sort.

- **10 min. Steps.** Read the planting steps on the page together.
  **Say.** "Why do the stones go first?" Expect: so extra water can drain out.
- **15 min. Edit.** Your child looks at the card four times with the checklist: capitals, end marks, apostrophe and commas, one spelling checked.
- **5 min. Sentence.** Your child writes a sentence with article or vertical.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X146v2]] and [[s04-X146bv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the shared writing, the edit and the exit. Longer: a child writes a because for all five steps and reads the chart aloud.

### Words and supports

First, next, then, after that, last, because, edit, capital, apostrophe, comma. Frames: "We ___ so ___." "___ comes before ___ because ___." Read the chart aloud, enlarge print, accept acting or a labelled drawing for a reason. Conventions are not scribed in the edit.

### Tomorrow

Day 147 opens section 4.8 with a class invitation: commas, and capital letters for place names. The edited card and the envelope go into the folder for the scoring on day 154. Today's exit marks go to the record sheet.
