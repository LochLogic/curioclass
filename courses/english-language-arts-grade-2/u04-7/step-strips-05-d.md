# Step strips for 05-d (day 143 and day 146)

Four strips, one for each step of planning a pot from the text, printed large and cut apart. Pairs order the strips on the table, then say why one step must come before the next, pointing at the change the first step makes. Use the steps the article gives, in the article's words. The strips are never the exit case.
