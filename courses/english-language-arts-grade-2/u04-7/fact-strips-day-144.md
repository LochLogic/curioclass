# Fact strips, day 144

For the "Find facts" part, pairs get four strips taken from article 05 (never from the exit card). Each pair sorts them into "helps the opinion" and "does not help" for the class opinion the teacher states, and draws an arrow from a fact to the opinion it helps. The strips are copied from 05 in the article's words.
