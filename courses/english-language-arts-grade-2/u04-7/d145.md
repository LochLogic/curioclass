# Day 145: Revise the reasons, links and ending after feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return each card with its one-line note from day 144 and the drawing. Make a list of each child's recorded letter for the five tables (H and I, J, K, L, M). Make six groups of five. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 19 to 24) and the six for the oral card (children 7 to 12). Say aloud that the exit card is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the note

Hand back day 144's exit marks with the cards. Each child reads the note on the card and tells a partner what it asks. A note that names a part (a missing link word, a missing ending) is read with the parts wall card beside it. Ask: "Which part is the note about?" Children point to it on the wall card. Hear the answer from any child who offers.

#### 10 min. Pair feedback

Review: kind, specific, helpful words about a draft, from unit 2 (day 64) and grade 1. Pairs swap cards; each says one kind, specific, helpful thing about the writing and asks one question (2 minutes each way, 4 minutes; 15 pairs at once). Then each writes one note for the partner on the back: the part that works and one change (6 minutes). **Ask.** "What is one change that would help a reader?" Expect: a part named with a reason, such as "add an ending so the reader knows what to do".

#### 10 min. Group critique

Groups of five set the card and the drawing out on the table. Each child reads the card aloud (45 seconds) and the group fills the critique strip with the child's name: one thing that works, one question and one idea to try. Each child hears one comment about the writing and one about the drawing from the group: 2 minutes a child, 5 x 2 = 10 minutes. Sit with two groups in turn, five minutes each. Rules: speak one at a time; name the part (writing or drawing) before the comment; the speaker answers the strip before revising. Any child may pass.

#### 25 min. Revise

Each child revises the reasons, links and ending after the notes, on the same card in a second colour so the first draft stays readable (about 20 minutes, W.2.5). A revision changes an idea; an edit fixes a convention, and that waits for day 146. Do not erase: cross out and write beside. Take five tables by recorded letter, five minutes each (25 minutes in all); every child takes one table turn inside the 25. H and I (name the topic; add the writer's position with "should"). J (swap in a fact from 05-a or 05-c that answers the question). K (choose a link word that joins the reason to the opinion). L (add an ending that completes the position). M (change one idea the note asked about instead of copying it again). Tables use the child's own card, never the exit strips. Error M: copying the old words in a new colour; ask "What is different in your card now?" Collect the revised cards.

#### 5 min. Share

Pairs read the revised card aloud and each says one thing they changed (2 minutes each way, 1 minute to swap).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X145]]. Each child marks one of the two ending strips (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each: [[s04-X145b]] (3 minutes). The rest read their revised card to themselves. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A right retry after help is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From the commonest foundations miss on yesterday's tiles (error Q, a first-letter guess; error R, a pattern not carried over): build vertical and vehicle, tap the syllables, blend every letter and say which ending each takes.

#### 8 min. Revise the cycle 24 record

Hand back the day 143 check sheets with the right form written beside each miss (error R). Each child corrects the sheet. A child who missed an oral turn on 143 reads the card now. Scored afterward on day 146.

#### 12 min. Read the matched decodable

Pairs reread A Little Miracle, a page each (RF.2.4a), and confirm each page from print and sentence (RF.2.4c). **Ask.** "Why does Kit say it is not logical?" Expect: a plant is growing in a crack. The class then reads pages 4 to 7 together once, pausing at each full stop.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 25 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. Model one -cle word on the board before the first turn. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 16 min. Spell

Words are built, fixed and used, not listed. Model typical, logical, particle and bicycle on tiles, tapping the syllables (4 minutes); each child builds and writes two of them (4 minutes). Fix the word (4 minutes): you write artical, tropicle and vehical on the board; pairs correct each with the pattern and the sound-spelling map. Then each child writes one sentence about a plant with a -cal or -cle word (4 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 7 min. Map irregular words

Map says (review of day 124) and whole (the w is silent; o and e say the long o). Take a sentence from the reader: Kit tells the whole class. Tap each word, mark the part that does not say what you expect, and read the line. No guessing from pictures or first letters.

### Other rooms

The critique strip is a folded paper with three lines. A second colour can be a different line style. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No second colour: write the change in a different place on the card. No tiles: paper squares.
**Skip.** The group critique and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read.** Your child reads the note aloud.
  **Say.** "What is one change that would help a reader?" Expect: a part named with a reason.
- **15 min. Revise.** Your child changes one reason, one link word or the ending and reads the card again.
- **5 min. Words.** Build typical and particle from paper squares.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X145]] and [[s04-X145b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the note, the revision at the table and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second ending and says which finishes the position better.

### Words and supports

Revise, note, kind, specific, helpful, link, ending. Frames: "One thing that works is ___." "My question is ___." "One idea to try is ___." "I changed ___ because ___." A scribe is allowed for content.

### Tomorrow

Day 146 edits the card in its own pass: names with capitals, end marks, apostrophes, the comma after "Dear planter" and before the closing, and a spelling checked in the dictionary. The cycle 24 check scores come on day 146.
