# Day 144: Draft a wildflower opinion with facts from the articles

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back each child's day 143 plan. Cut the fact strips (one set a pair) and set out the sticky strips, lettered a and c. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 7 to 12) and the six for the oral card (children 1 to 6). Say aloud that the exit card is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From day 138's exit, two quick repairs. Error X, 3 minutes: act sprinkle, pour and dump with the cup of torn paper, then put the three words in order by how much comes out. Error Y, 2 minutes: write "The gently seeds" beside "Water the pot gently". Ask what the word tells about, the seeds or the watering, and choose its form. A describing word for a noun is not the same as one for an action.

#### 12 min. Find facts

Pairs sort the four fact strips under "helps" and "does not help" for a class opinion you state: "Our school bed should keep a patch of bare soil." Draw an arrow from a helping fact to the opinion (2 minutes). The strips, in the article's words, are: "Some bees nest in bare ground that nobody digs up." "So people can leave a few dry stems standing and a patch of soil alone." "Bees, butterflies, moths, bats and hummingbirds are all pollinators." "A shallow dish with some stones in it gives them a safe place to land and sip." Say aloud that a fact helps only if it speaks to the question. Then pairs reread 05-a and 05-c and each child marks two facts with sticky strips lettered a or c, each fact an answer to "Why should someone plant a suitable wildflower?" (10 minutes, W.2.8). Use only 05-a and 05-c, never anything else. Visit each of the 15 pairs for about 40 seconds. Error J: a fact that does not answer the question; ask "What does a planter learn from this fact?"

#### 23 min. Draft

Each child writes the card on side 1 (W.2.1): "Dear planter,", the topic, the opinion, two facts with the letter of their article, a link word for each, and an ending. Pick exact verbs and describing words from the wall ladder. Review: precise words from day 138. Read the parts wall card aloud once. Facts are copied or put in the child's own words; no fact from anywhere else. A child may draw the card or have it scribed. Confer with six children, one minute each, inside the 23. Ask each: "What does your fact tell a planter?" Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen on the record sheet.

#### 10 min. Draw

On the drawing sheet in pencil: a wildflower and one pollinator visitor, drawn from what you imagine and from the articles, as if watched from a little way off. Under the picture, one line says what it shows. Nobody handles an insect.

#### 5 min. Pairs

Pairs read the card aloud, one at a time; the listener names the fact they heard (2 minutes each way, 15 pairs at once). Review: turn, pair, link, clarify from unit 1.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X144]]. Each child marks one of the two fact strips (1 minute). Hear the six, 30 seconds each: [[s04-X144b]] (3 minutes). The rest finish the drawing line. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Cycle 24 words on tiles: build sunflower and birdbath, tap the two parts and blend every letter.

#### 15 min. Model, map, blend

Review: the -le syllable from unit 3 (table, stable). New today: the ending -cal says /kul/ (vertical, tropical, comical) and the ending -cle says /kul/ too (article, miracle, vehicle). The c says /k/, and in -cle the e is silent. For each word on tiles: tap the syllables, blend the whole, then spell it. Check each word in a sentence: "A vertical stem stands straight up." "A bus is a vehicle." "The clown was comical." Map one irregular word each: says (review of day 124) and whole (the w is silent, and o and e say the long o). No guessing from pictures or first letters.

#### 15 min. First reading of the cycle 25 reader

Read A Little Miracle after the pattern is taught. You read page 1 aloud; pairs echo-read pages 2 to 7, a page each (RF.2.4a). Then confirm meaning aloud from the print: what grows in the crack, and what does Kit say? (RF.2.4c). Model one unfamiliar word on the board before pairs begin.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 1 to 6 read the cycle 25 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. These are first-day samples, diagnostic; the cycle's check items are on day 148. Model one -cal word on the board before the first turn. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 13 min. Spell

One word at a time, built and not listed: children build tropical, comical, article and miracle with tiles, tap the syllables and write each (8 minutes). Then they compare each with the pattern on the board. Pairs complete three cloze strips on side 2 (5 minutes): "A stem that stands straight up is ___." "A bus is a ___." "The funny clown was ___." Error S: a spelling left unchecked.

### Other rooms

Copy the card frame on the board. Sticky strips are folded paper. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No sticky strips: underline facts in two colours. No tiles: paper squares.
**Skip.** The oral card and the cloze strips.

- **10 min. Find.** Read the two articles on the page together; your child picks two facts that tell a planter why to plant a wildflower.
  **Say.** "What does your fact tell a planter?" Expect: what the flower gives pollinators.
- **15 min. Write.** Your child writes the card on side 1 and draws a wildflower and one visitor.
- **5 min. Words.** Build tropical and article from paper squares.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X144]] and [[s04-X144b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the facts, the card and the exit; draw tomorrow. Longer: a child adds a third fact from 05-c and says why it fits.

### Words and supports

Fact, tag, link word, ending, pollinator, suitable. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "A planter should ___." "Let us ___." Read the articles aloud, enlarge print, accept a scribe or a labelled drawing for a reason.

### Tomorrow

Day 145 opens with each child reading the written note: after class write one line on each note slip (error J, a reason that does not help, when it applies). The cycle 24 sheets come back on day 145 with the right form beside the commonest miss. Day 145 is pair feedback, a group critique and revision.
