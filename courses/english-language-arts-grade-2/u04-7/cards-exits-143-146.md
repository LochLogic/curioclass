# Exit cards, days 143 to 146

Rows that open "This card is invented for practice" are made up: say so aloud and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. The other rows reread a passage of the unit's own texts, named in the row. Read the card or passage twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 143 (s04-X143v2).** Reread aloud twice the last two paragraphs of 05-d, from "Last" to the end. Show two small pictures: open wildflowers in the pot with a bee feeding; a pot of bare soil just sown. Say: "Mark the picture that shows what can come after the waiting."
- **Day 143 (s04-X143bv2).** Same paragraphs. Say: "Tell me why the flowers must open before pollinators come."
- **Day 144 (s04-X144).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Opinion: Our class should put a mat by the door. Fact A: Mats catch the mud from our shoes. Fact B: The mat is blue." Show the two facts on strips. Say: "Mark the fact that helps the opinion."
- **Day 144 (s04-X144b).** Same card. Say: "Say how that fact helps the opinion."
- **Day 145 (s04-X145).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Our class should make a suggestion box. A box can hold good ideas." Show two ending strips. A: "Let us make a suggestion box." B: "A box can hold good ideas." Say: "Mark the ending that finishes the opinion."
- **Day 145 (s04-X145b).** Same card. Say: "Say the draft again, joining the reason to the opinion with a link word."
- **Day 146 (s04-X146v2).** Reread aloud twice the experts paragraph of Room Without Sprays. Show strips: A "Find out what the pest is." B "Change what drew it." C "Accept a few holes." Say: "Mark the step right before B."
- **Day 146 (s04-X146bv2).** Same paragraph. Say: "Say why finding out the pest comes before changing what drew it."
