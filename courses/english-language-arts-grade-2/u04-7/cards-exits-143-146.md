# Exit cards, days 143 to 146

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 143 (s04-X143).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Washing a brush. A: Dip the brush in paint. B: Rinse the brush in water. C: Dry the brush on a towel. D: Put the brush in the jar." Show two small pictures: a brush dripping clean water, and a brush with thick paint on the bristles. Say: "Mark the picture that shows the brush after the rinse."
- **Day 143 (s04-X143b).** Same card. Say: "Tell me why rinsing comes before the towel."
- **Day 144 (s04-X144).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Opinion: Our class should put a mat by the door. Fact A: Mats catch the mud from our shoes. Fact B: The mat is blue." Show the two facts on strips. Say: "Mark the fact that helps the opinion."
- **Day 144 (s04-X144b).** Same card. Say: "Say how that fact helps the opinion."
- **Day 145 (s04-X145).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Our class should make a suggestion box. A box can hold good ideas." Show two ending strips. A: "Let us make a suggestion box." B: "A box can hold good ideas." Say: "Mark the ending that finishes the opinion."
- **Day 145 (s04-X145b).** Same card. Say: "Say the draft again, joining the reason to the opinion with a link word."
- **Day 146 (s04-X146).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Putting on a coat. A: Put one arm in a sleeve. B: Put the other arm in. C: Zip the coat. D: Go out the door." Show four step strips. Say: "Mark the step that comes right before the zip."
- **Day 146 (s04-X146b).** Same card. Say: "Say why both arms go in before the zip."
