# Day 143: Read planting instructions and plan a seed packet

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print 05-d large and cut the four step strips, one set a pair (the words are under Steps and why). Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 1 to 6) and the six for the oral card (children 25 to 30).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

If many chose the strip with no link word on day 142's exit (error K), say the draft aloud with and without the link word (3 minutes). Then point to the five parts on the parts wall card; each child says one part to a partner (2 minutes). Review: the parts of an opinion piece from day 135 and section 4.6.

#### 17 min. Read 05-d

Words previewed (3 minutes): native (a plant that grows in your area), drain (water runs out), thinly (a few, spread out), regularly (again and again, on a schedule), and suitable (it fits the place; native plants suit the weather and the soil). Read 05-d aloud once, finger under the words on the big print (2 minutes; the plan says about 190 words, the text measures about 210). Pairs reread by turns, one paragraph each, finger under the words (8 minutes, RF.2.4a; 15 pairs read at once, you listen at three pairs). The whole class rereads the first and last paragraphs together (4 minutes, RF.2.4b). Ask when done: "What is the article telling you to do?" Expect: grow flowers for pollinators in a pot, in steps.

#### 15 min. Steps and why

Review: you learned a chain of steps in unit 3, where each step leads to the next. The strips, in the article's words: A "Choose a pot with holes in the bottom." B "Add a layer of stones, then fill the pot with potting soil." C "Scatter the seeds thinly and cover them with a thin layer of soil." D "Water the pot gently and put it in a sunny, warm spot." Lay strips A to D on the wall in order. Say what the first step changes and what the next step needs. A to B: "The holes let extra water out. Now stones and soil can go in the pot, and the water has a way to drain." C to D: "The seeds sit under a thin cover of soil. Now water can go on gently and the seeds stay put." Say aloud that a step is not "next" just because it is on the list: each step changes something the next step needs (5 minutes, RI.2.3). Then pairs get a scrambled set and do two things (10 minutes). First they put the strips in the order of the article and check against 05-d (3 minutes). Then they swap two neighbouring strips and act what goes wrong with the cup, the scraps and the stones, and draw it in the box on side 2 (7 minutes). No sentence about why is asked for here. Visit each of the 15 pairs for about 30 seconds. Error Z: a pair that only says "it is next"; ask "What does the step before it change?"

#### 6 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on **Ask.** "What would you tell a friend who skips the stones?" Turn: each child speaks for 45 seconds in the pair, so a round is 2 minutes with the swap, 15 pairs at once, with the frame "If you skip ___, then ___" (2 minutes). Link, with "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (2 minutes). Clarify, with "Which part do you mean?" (1 minute). One pair shares to the class (1 minute). Any child may pass. Review: turn, pair, link, clarify from unit 1. Expect: a step named with what goes wrong, such as "the water has nowhere to go".

#### 10 min. Plan the packet

Show the four parts of the packet in a hand, one at a time (2 minutes): the envelope, the drawing sheet, the opinion card and the class steps. Say the unit question: "Why should someone plant a suitable wildflower?" Each child fills the plan strip on side 1 in a few words (8 minutes, W.2.1): topic, my opinion, the two facts to find (write only which article you will look in: a or c; the facts are not chosen yet), my link words, my ending. The place is a class pot, a window box, a school bed or a park, never a home yard. A child may draw or have a few words scribed. No drafting today. Read the parts wall card aloud while they work. Hear the three not reached on day 142's exit, 45 seconds each with the swap, at your table.

#### 7 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice the last two paragraphs of 05-d (1 minute): [[s04-X143v2]]. Each child marks one of the two pictures (1 minute). Hear the six, 45 seconds each with the swap: [[s04-X143bv2]] (4 minutes 30 seconds). The rest finish their plan strip. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From the commonest foundations miss on yesterday's dictation (error Q, a first-letter guess; error R, a pattern not carried over; error S, a spelling left unchecked): on slates build weekend, sandbox and starfish, tap the two parts, blend every letter and check the word against the pattern.

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 24

Hand out the check sheet. Every child writes the words and the sentence you say, then uses the printed dictionary entry (5 minutes each): [[s04-A91]], [[s04-A92]], [[s04-A93]]. Say each word, use it in a sentence, say it once more, and wait. Give no help. Collect the sheets. Keep the check words unseen.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 25 to 30 read the cycle 24 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap: [[s04-A85]], [[s04-A86]], [[s04-A87]], [[s04-A88]], [[s04-A89]], [[s04-A90]]. Ask the last only if a word was misread. Model one compound, flowerpot, on the board before the first turn. Keep each ask short. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws a sentence that uses a compound word from the cycle (sunflower, flowerpot, birdbath, grasshopper, hilltop, daylight, sunshine, outside) (9 minutes), then reads it to a partner (4 minutes). Walk and check one spelling against the sound-spelling map (2 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 13 min. Close

Pairs reread The Sunflower Path for accuracy, a page each (6 minutes). The class reads it once more smoothly, pausing at each full stop (4 minutes, RF.2.4b). Ask: "Which compound word do you see on page 5?" Children tell a partner, then say it together (3 minutes).

### Other rooms

Copy the four steps on the board and number them; the acting uses a cup and torn paper. Read 05-d aloud twice instead of in pairs. Read the check words and the entry aloud; the child writes on loose paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the four steps on four scraps. No stones: use paper balls.
**Skip.** The written check and the oral card.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the planting steps on the page together, a step each.
- **10 min. Why.** Swap two steps and act what goes wrong with a cup and scraps.
  **Say.** "Why must the steps come in this order?" Expect: a step named with what it changes.
- **10 min. Plan.** Your child fills the five boxes on side 1 in a few words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X143v2]] and [[s04-X143bv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the strips with the acting, the plan strip and the exit. Longer: a child makes a fifth step and says what the step before it changes.

### Words and supports

Step, because, native, drain, thinly, regularly, suitable. Frames: "___ comes before ___ because ___." "I think ___ because ___." Read 05-d aloud, enlarge print, accept acting or a labelled drawing as an answer. Say a word in the home language first.

### Tomorrow

Day 144 opens with a five-minute repair on day 138's exit, from the routed hand-back: act sprinkle, pour and dump once more, then name the word "gently" tells about. A wrong answer on today's exit (error Z) is repaired on day 146. Day 144 drafts the card: two facts from articles a and c, each tagged with its letter, and a drawing.
