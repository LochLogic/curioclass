# Day 139: Draft an introduction and opinion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a W1 sheet a child. Hang the parts wall card. Mark the six heard and the oral card children (1 to 6). Collect the student pages and W1 sheets at the end of the day for written notes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many missed day 138's exit (error X), act set and toss once more.

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: a topic and an opinion, from cards M and N. No help today; a note comes back tomorrow.

#### 7 min. Reread cards M and N

Read both aloud once (2 minutes); pairs read by turns (5 minutes). Practice.

#### 20 min. W1, sitting 1

Each child begins the piece on the question "Should our garden include suitable native plants and night-blooming flowers?" with a topic sentence and an opinion in the two boxes: [[s04-A14]] and [[s04-A15]]. No frame, no help. The six heard read their opening aloud, 1 minute each inside the 20. No topic is error H; no position is error I. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Collect the student pages and sheets.

#### 8 min. Draw

A picture to go with the piece. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Partner reading

Pairs reread 05-c with a finger under the words (RF.2.4b). Practice.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs say their opening sentence aloud; the partner says what the topic was.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Slates: write pirate and rotate and say which ending is strong.

#### 15 min. Model, map, blend (cycle 24)

Review: a compound word is two known words joined; predict the meaning, then check it in the sentence. New: the ending of a compound word is the word it was, with no letter lost. Join tiles for cupcake, popcorn, goldfish and bathtub: join, tap the two parts, blend the whole. Each part is a syllable.

#### 15 min. First reading of the cycle 24 reader

Read page 1 of The Sunflower Path aloud; pairs echo-read the rest. Confirm: what do Jo and Dad plant?

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 1 to 6 read the cycle 24 oral card, 2 minutes each (diagnostic). Model one compound first. The others use tiles. Mark the heard column.

#### 13 min. Spell

Tile builds, not dictation: build flowerpot and birdbath from their parts and write each (6 minutes). Then cloze strips from the reader: "Jo and Dad plant seeds ___." and "___ and rain help the seeds grow." Fix the word "sunshyne" (4 minutes). Map says, with no guessing from pictures or first letters (3 minutes).

### Other rooms

Read the cards aloud twice; answer on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read two short true texts about a garden aloud twice.
**Skip.** The oral card and the rotation.

- **20 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A14]] [[s04-A15]] Give no help.
- **15 min. Reader.** Read The Sunflower Path with your child.
  **Say.** "What do Jo and Dad plant?" Expect: seeds.
- **10 min. Words.** Join flower and pot, bird and bath on paper squares.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the briefing, W1 and the reader. Longer: a child adds a picture label.

### Words and supports

Topic, opinion, native, night-blooming. Frames: "Our garden can ___." "I think our garden should ___." A child may say the opening first and have it scribed.

### Tomorrow

Each draft gets a one-line note after class. Day 140 starts by reading it, then adds reasons joined with because, and or also.
