# W1 sheet, days 139 to 141

One sheet kept across three sittings. Cards M (Plants That Fit) and N (Flowers After Sunset) are reread before each sitting.

- **Sitting 1 (day 139).** A box for the topic sentence. A box for the opinion.
- **Sitting 2 (day 140).** A box for a reason from card M or N. A box for a link word.
- **Sitting 3 (day 141).** A box for an ending. A small box for the one flagged convention.

Space for a picture at the foot. Nothing is printed in the boxes.
