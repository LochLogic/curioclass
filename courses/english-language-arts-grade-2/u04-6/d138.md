# Day 138: Reread a model opinion and choose exact words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 137's sheets, each with a one-line written note. Print the model opinion large (below), hang the parts wall card and the two ladders, and cut the bike strips and the exit strips. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask and the six for the oral card (children 25 to 30). Say aloud that the bike case and the exit card are made up for practice.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 137's assessment, the sheets with their notes, and say what a note asks. Read the bike case twice: "This card is made up for practice. Our school should keep bikes on the rack. Bikes block the hall. A bike on the rack leaves the hall clear." Add a second strip pair: "Riders should wear helmets. A helmet protects the head." Whole class, 4 minutes, on the commonest miss from the sheets. Error F: show the point named as "the hall" beside the right point, "keep bikes on the rack"; cover the details and say the point they add up to. Error G: show an arrow drawn from "A helmet protects the head" to the rack point beside the arrow to "wear helmets"; draw the arrow to the point the reason explains and read the pair aloud. Then two 3-minute teacher turns, an F table and a G table, while the rest redo their answer on the sheet from the written note. A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. The bike case is for repair only.

#### 15 min. Reread a model opinion

Words previewed: precise and shade. Review: you learned precise in unit 2 (a precise word is the exact one). Review: shades of meaning, from grade 1, and the word ladder from unit 3 (damp, wet, soaked). Read the model aloud once (1 minute). Review: the parts of an opinion piece from day 135.

> Pollinators need flowers. We should plant flowers that bloom in every season. Early bees need food because few flowers are open in spring. Also, fall flowers give butterflies food before the cold comes. So flowers from spring to fall will keep our pollinators fed.

Pairs read it by turns, one sentence each (6 minutes). Then pairs color the parts with five crayons matching the wall card: topic blue, opinion red, reason green, link word yellow, ending purple (8 minutes, RF.2.4b, W.2.1). **Ask.** "Which sentence says what the writer thinks should be done?" Expect: We should plant flowers that bloom in every season. **Ask.** "Which words join a reason to the opinion?" Expect: because, also. The main idea is the opinion; each reason is a detail that supports it.

#### 10 min. Model exact words

Draw the two ladders tall, weakest word at the bottom: drink, sip, gulp (a bee at a flower on a warm morning) and cold, chilly, freezing (an early spring morning). Act each word before you place it: a sip is small and slow; a gulp is big and quick; chilly is a shiver in a coat; freezing is hands tucked in and teeth chattering. Review: sip and gulp on the word ladder, from unit 3. Then swap words in the model. Write "A bee drinks from a flower" and change drink to sip; write "Early bees wake on a cold morning" and change cold to chilly. Say why: choose the word that shows the picture in your head. A precise word is not always the strongest one; it is the one that fits. Review: describing words for a thing and for an action, from unit 3.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on **Ask.** "Which word makes the picture clearer?" Round 1 uses the bee ladder, round 2 the morning ladder. Each child takes a turn of 1 minute in the pair, so a round is 2 minutes and the two rounds are 4 minutes with all 15 pairs speaking at once. Then link, with "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (3 minutes), and clarify, with "Which part do you mean?" (3 minutes). Any child may pass. Expect: a word named with its picture, such as "sip, because I see a small slow drink".

#### 10 min. Independent

Sort first (3 minutes, no picture): each pair puts word cards on a line from weak to strong, once for how tired (sleepy, tired, worn out) and once for the wind (breezy, gusty, fierce). Then each child takes the flat-word piece (7 minutes): "The pony was tired. It walked up the road. The wind was strong." Rewrite two flat words with exact ones from the word bank (weary, plodded, trudged, strolled, gusty, fierce, and the words the class gathered from 05 and 06), add one describing word, and circle the two new words. Confer with the six heard, about 1 minute each. Error X: two near words treated as the same; have the child act both.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X138]]. Each child marks one of the two sentence strips (2 minutes). Hear the six, 20 seconds each: [[s04-X138b]] (2 minutes). Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Slates, from the commonest foundations miss on day 137 (error Q, a first-letter guess; error R, a pattern not carried over; error S, a spelling left unchecked): write gate, rotate and pirate, tap each word, and say which ending is strong.

#### 15 min. Written check, cycle 23

Hand out the check sheet. Every child writes the words and the sentence you say, then uses the printed dictionary entry (5 minutes each): [[s04-A82]], [[s04-A83]], [[s04-A84]]. Say each word, use it in a sentence, say it once more, and wait. Give no help. Collect the sheets. Keep the check words unseen.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 25 to 30 read the cycle 23 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap: [[s04-A76]], [[s04-A77]], [[s04-A78]], [[s04-A79]], [[s04-A80]], [[s04-A81]]. Ask the last only if a word was misread. Model one -ate word on the board before the first turn. Keep each ask short. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. A fresh response

Each child writes or draws a sentence that uses an -ate word from the cycle (pirate, rotate, imitate, migrate, pollinate, accurate) (9 minutes), then reads it to a partner (4 minutes). Walk and check one spelling against the sound-spelling map (2 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 13 min. Close

Pairs reread The Garden Gate for accuracy, a page each (6 minutes). The class reads it once more smoothly, pausing at each full stop (4 minutes, RF.2.4b). Ask: "Which ending is strong in rotate?" Children tell a partner, then say it together (3 minutes).

### Other rooms

Copy the model on the board; the ladders are acted and spoken. Word cards are folded paper strips. Read the check words and the entry aloud; the child writes on loose paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No crayons: underline with five different marks. No word cards: write the words on scraps.
**Skip.** The written check and the oral card.

- **10 min. The model.** Read the model on the page aloud together and color its five parts to match the key.
  **Say.** "Which sentence says what the writer thinks should be done?" Expect: We should plant flowers that bloom in every season.
- **10 min. Exact words.** Act sip and gulp, then chilly and freezing.
  **Say.** "Which word makes the picture clearer?" Expect: a word with its picture.
- **10 min. Rewrite.** Your child rewrites two flat words in the piece on the page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X138]] and [[s04-X138b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, one ladder, the rewrite and the exit. Longer: a child chooses an exact word for the ending and says what it shows.

### Words and supports

Precise, shade, sip, gulp, chilly, freezing, topic, opinion, reason, link word, ending. Frames: "A ___ is stronger than a ___." "The word ___ shows ___." Say a word in the home language first; acting a word is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 139 opens with a five-minute repair on today's exit: if many marked the wrong strip or called near words the same (error X), act both words once more. Day 139 is the first sitting of the opinion piece: a topic and an opinion from cards M and N, with no help.
