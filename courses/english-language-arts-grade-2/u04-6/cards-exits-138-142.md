# Exit cards, days 138 and 142

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 138 (s04-X138).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "A: Pat set the full tub on the shelf. B: Pat tossed the full tub on the shelf." Show two sentence strips. Say: "Mark the sentence for a careful helper carrying a tub full of water."
- **Day 138 (s04-X138b).** Same card. Say: "Say the word in that sentence that shows how the helper put the tub down."
- **Day 142 (s04-X142).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Our pot should have flowers. Flowers can offer food." Show two draft strips. Strip 1: "Our pot should have flowers. Flowers can offer food." Strip 2: "Our pot should have flowers because they can offer food." Say: "Mark the strip that links the reason to the opinion."
- **Day 142 (s04-X142b).** Same card. Say: "Say one sentence that finishes the opinion."
