# Word ladders, day 138

Two ladders for the model, drawn tall on chart paper, weakest word at the bottom. Each word is acted before it is placed.

- **drink, sip, gulp.** A bee at a flower on a warm morning. A sip is small and slow; a gulp is big and quick.
- **cold, chilly, freezing.** An early spring morning. Chilly is mild; freezing is the strongest.

A third strip stays blank for the pairs: "Which word makes the picture clearer?" Words come from the model opinion, never from the exit.
