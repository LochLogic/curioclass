# Bike case, day 138 (return and repair)

A fresh invented case for the first ten minutes, read aloud twice. Say: "This card is made up for practice."

"Our school should keep bikes on the rack. Bikes block the hall. A bike on the rack leaves the hall clear."

Two strips: the point (bikes on the rack) and the reason (the hall stays clear). Children draw an arrow from the reason to the point it explains. The case is for repair only and is never an exit or an end item.
