# Day 141: Write an ending, revise and edit

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return the W1 sheets with a link note and one circled convention. Print a clean copy a child. Mark the six heard and the oral card children (13 to 18).

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the note

Hand back day 140's assessment, the drafts with notes. Each child reads the note and tells a partner what it asks (error K's repair).

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: an ending, then reasons revised after the note, then a separate editing pass. No help on the first part.

#### 30 min. W1, sitting 3

Each child writes an ending and revises earlier reasons after the note: [[s04-A18]] and [[s04-A19]]. The six heard read their ending aloud, 1 minute each inside the 30. No ending is error L; a draft copied neatly with no change is error M. Score only earlier parts that got a note; the ending is scored after the day 142 revision. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Collect the student pages and drafts.

#### 10 min. W2, editing pass

Review: edit with the checklist, one box each (unit 2). On a clean copy, with a second color, each child checks the four boxes and corrects the circled mark: [[s04-A20]]. Use one adult note and one partner note, and the dictionary for a spelling. Keep the draft before and after. A mark left unchanged is error N.

#### 10 min. Share

Pairs read their finished piece aloud and say one thing they changed. Practice.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

The commonest foundations miss from yesterday (error Q, R or S): blend all the letters, check against the pattern.

#### 12 min. Reread the cycle 24 reader

Pairs reread The Sunflower Path for accuracy (6 minutes), then smoothly (6 minutes, RF.2.4b).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 13 to 18 read the cycle 24 oral card, 2 minutes each. Model one compound before the first turn. The others use tiles and the reader. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Sort

Pairs sort twelve compound words by the first word and tap the two parts: sunflower, sunshine, daylight, birdbath, flowerpot, outside, hilltop, grasshopper, cupcake, goldfish, bathtub, popcorn (9 minutes). Each child covers, writes four and checks: flowerpot, hilltop, goldfish, cupcake (6 minutes).

#### 11 min. Use the pattern in sentences

Each child writes one sentence with a cycle word (7 minutes), reads it to a partner (4 minutes). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs read one sentence aloud.

### Other rooms

Write the checklist on the board; loose paper for the clean copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No second pencil color: use a pen.
**Skip.** The oral card and the sort.

- **25 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A18]] [[s04-A19]] [[s04-A20]] Give no help.
  **Say.** "What did the note ask you to change?" Expect: one idea.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child writes goldfish and flowerpot from memory.
- **10 min. Read.** Reread The Sunflower Path together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the ending, the revision and the edit. Longer: a child reads the piece aloud and says what each reason adds.

### Words and supports

Ending, revise, edit, checklist, note. Frames: "Let us ___." "I changed ___ because ___."

### Tomorrow

Pieces get a note on the ending after class. Day 142 reads it, models a fresh revision, then each child revises, retries a missing part and edits the circled mark.
