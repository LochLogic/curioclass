# Day 140: Add reasons and linking words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return the W1 sheets with the note slip on each. Write beside the commonest cycle 23 miss its right form (for the Spell part). Mark the six heard and the oral card children (7 to 12).

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the note

Hand back day 139's assessment, the drafts with notes. Each child reads the note and tells a partner what it asks (error H's repair).

#### 5 min. Briefing

Say what is checked: reasons joined with because, and or also. The sources are cards M and N. No help today.

#### 30 min. W1, sitting 2

Each child revises the introduction after the note and adds reasons from M and N joined with because, and or also: [[s04-A16]] and [[s04-A17]]. The six heard read one reason aloud and say which card it comes from, 1 minute each inside the 30. Collect the student pages and drafts. An irrelevant reason is error J; reasons with no link word are error K. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Newly made reasons are not scored.

#### 10 min. Pairs

Pairs read their revised opening and one reason to each other; the listener asks one question. Practice, not marked.

#### 10 min. Quiet reading

Each child rereads 05-a, 05-b or 05-c alone or with a partner.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

The commonest foundations miss from yesterday (error Q, R or S): blend all the letters of a compound, then check it against the pattern.

#### 12 min. Read the matched decodable

Pairs reread The Sunflower Path, a page each, then confirm meaning from print and sentence: what grows by the path? (RF.2.4a, RF.2.4c).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 24 oral card, 2 minutes each. Model one compound before the first turn. The others use tiles and the reader. Mark the heard column.

#### 16 min. Spell

First 5 minutes: the commonest miss from the day 138 check sheets, written beside the right form (error R). Then tile builds: sun + flower, grass + hopper, day + light (7 minutes), and a cloze strip for each, such as "Jo and Dad see the ___ path." (4 minutes). Keep both parts whole.

#### 10 min. Map irregular words

Review: map they and says, with no guessing from pictures or first letters (days 119 and 124). Say each word, tap its sounds, mark the letters that do not say what you expect.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs read one sentence of the reader aloud.

### Other rooms

Read the cards aloud twice; answers are aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read two short true texts aloud first.
**Skip.** The oral card and the pairs.

- **25 min. The assessment.** [[s04-A16]] [[s04-A17]] Give no help.
  **Say.** "Which text does your reason come from?" Expect: card M or card N.
- **10 min. Words.** Build sunflower and hilltop on paper squares.
- **10 min. Read.** Reread The Sunflower Path together.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep W1 and the reader. Longer: a child adds a second reason and its card.

### Words and supports

Reason, because, and, also, link. Frames: "Our garden should ___ because ___." "Also, ___." A child may say a reason first.

### Tomorrow

Each draft gets a note on its link after class. Day 141 starts by reading it, then writes an ending, revises earlier reasons and edits in a separate pass.
