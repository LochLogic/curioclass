# Parts wall card (five parts of an opinion)

One large card for the wall, five boxes in a row, each with a color and a small word:

1. Topic (blue): what the piece is about.
2. Opinion (red): what we think should happen.
3. Reason (green): a fact that helps.
4. Link word (yellow): because, so, and, also.
5. Ending (purple): finishes the opinion.

The card shows no sentence. Pairs use it on days 138, 142, 143, 145 and 146 to point to the parts of a model.
