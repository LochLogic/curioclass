# Day 142: Improve the opinion after feedback

## Teacher page

### Before class

Return each child's draft with the note on the ending. Write your sprays draft large (below) and keep a clean copy. Make a list of each child's recorded letter for the five tables (H and I, J, K, L, M and N). Mark the six for the spoken exit ask and the six for the oral card (children 19 to 24). Say aloud that the exit card is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the note

Hand back day 141's assessment, the drafts with notes. Each child reads the note on the ending and tells a partner what it asks (error L's repair).

#### 10 min. Model on a fresh case

Not the assessed piece. Write your own draft in one pen color:

> our school garden should use fewer sprays because I like green leaves. Also, sprays are bad. Let us use fewer sprays.

Read it aloud and say the notes you gave yourself: a reason about you does not help the opinion (error J), one idea is unclear (error M) and the first sentence starts with a small letter (error N). Revise in a second color: cross out "I like green leaves" and write the fact "some sprays can kill bees right away" from 05-b; change "sprays are bad" to "a spray can kill the insects that songbirds eat". Then, in a separate pass on the clean copy, write the capital for Our. Say aloud that a revision changes an idea and an edit fixes a convention, and that they are two passes. Last, pairs find the five parts of your revised draft on the parts wall card (4 minutes of the 10).

#### 8 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on **Ask.** "What did the note ask you to change, and why?" Turn (2 minutes), pair (2), link, with "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (2), clarify, with "Which part do you mean?" (2). Any child may pass. Expect: the part named with a reason, such as "to add an ending, so the piece finishes what I think".

#### 32 min. Revise, retry and edit

Each child revises the piece after the note (14 minutes), retries the missing part on a fresh strip with no help (8), edits the circled mark in a separate pass with the checklist (6, the repair for error N) and has a partner check it (4). A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. Call five tables by recorded letter, 5 minutes each (25 minutes): H and I (name the topic; add the writer's position with "should"), J (swap in a fact from card M or N or 05), K (choose a link that joins the reason to the opinion), L (add an ending that completes the position), M and N (change one unclear idea from the note; correct the circled mark with the checklist). Tables use the child's own garden piece, never the exit strips. Every child takes one table turn inside the 32. Collect the revised piece, the fresh strip and the clean copy.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice (1 minute): [[s04-X142]]. Each child marks one of the two draft strips (2 minutes). Hear three of the six, 45 seconds each with the swap: [[s04-X142b]] (2 minutes 15 seconds); the other three are heard on day 143. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

The commonest foundations miss from yesterday (error Q, R or S): blend every letter of a compound, then check it against the pattern.

#### 8 min. Revise the cycle 23 record

Hand back the day 138 check sheets with the right form written beside each miss (error R). Each child corrects the sheet. A child who missed an oral turn on 138 reads the card now. Scored afterward on day 143.

#### 15 min. A fresh decodable passage

Not the reader. Read Rob and Jo on the Hilltop in pairs, then confirm its meaning aloud: where do Rob and Jo go, and what do they see? (RF.2.4c). Model one unfamiliar compound on the board before pairs begin.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 19 to 24 read the cycle 24 oral card, 2 minutes each. Model one compound before the first turn. The others use tiles and the passage. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Dictation

One word at a time, built and not listed: children build anthill, lunchbox, popcorn and hilltop with tiles, tap the two parts and write each (8 minutes). Then they compare each with the pattern on the board. Pairs complete two cloze strips from the passage with it covered: "An ___ is in the grass." and "Rob and Jo make ___." (7 minutes).

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs read a completed strip aloud.

### Other rooms

Copy your draft on the board. Strips are folded paper. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is aloud or on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No second pencil color: use a pen. No tiles: paper squares.
**Skip.** The tables and the oral card.

- **15 min. Revise.** Your child reads the note and changes one idea.
  **Say.** "What did the note ask you to change, and why?" Expect: the part named, with a reason.
- **10 min. Edit.** Your child corrects the circled mark.
- **10 min. Words.** Build anthill and lunchbox from paper squares and write them.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X142]] and [[s04-X142b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the revision and the exit. Longer: a child writes a second reason and says which card it comes from.

### Words and supports

Revise, edit, note, link, ending. Frames: "The note asks me to ___ because ___." "I changed ___." A child may say the change first and have it scribed.

### Tomorrow

Day 143 opens with a three-minute repair on today's exit: if many chose the strip with no link (error K), say the draft aloud with and without the link word. Day 143 reads planting instructions and plans a seed packet.
