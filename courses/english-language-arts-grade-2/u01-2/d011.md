# Day 11: Show what you know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print "The Class Sign" with its picture, and a new event card and student page a child. Note the commonest error (M or Q) on the day 10 check. Today's six are group 1. Their grade 1 card: cake, flag, thick, glad, stop, frog; "The frog sat on a big log."

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Write the check word most children missed. Error Q: set it beside the ai and ay wall words. Error M: map the whole team to one sound, then blend. Children spell it again on tiles.

#### 6 min. Read the cold text

Say: "This text is made up for practice." Show the picture. Read "The Class Sign" twice. Do not explain it.

#### 12 min. Ask and answer (11-1)

Unscored; no help. Each child asks one question the text can answer (s01-A1); the partner answers. Hold up the question words card and ask who, what, where, when, why and how (s01-A2 to s01-A7). Hear the six, twenty seconds each. Shown is a right answer from the text; shown with help follows "What does the text say?"; a wrong answer is error A.

#### 8 min. Response (11-2)

Ask (s01-A8): "How does Mira respond when the paper curls?" Shown names what she does, linked to the paper. "She is kind" is error B.

#### 8 min. Words and picture (11-3)

Ask (s01-A9) for Mira's problem and what she did, picture in view. Shown takes the problem from the words and a detail from the picture. "The picture is pretty" is error C.

#### 15 min. Written response

Read the new event card twice and show its picture. Children write or draw "Mira ___ because ___" and name the picture detail. Look for the event, what Mira did, and the detail. Collect the pages.

#### 6 min. Reread to a partner

Partners reread the day 10 passage in phrases, pausing at each full stop (error O). No scoring.

#### 5 min. Retrieval (foundations)

Spell rain, day, wait, stay on tiles.

#### 15 min. Cycle 3 opens

Write need, week, beak, reach, happy and baby. Map each, blend, then spell on tiles. Add ee, ea and y to the sound-spelling-map.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Round 1, 13 minutes: your six read their grade 1 oral card with you; others work at the tiles and sort ee, ea and y word cards. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Say: "Tell your partner one word with ee, ea or y."

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the picture with pencil. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed picture: draw one. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

**Skip.** The tiles.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read "The Class Sign" aloud twice and show its picture. Ask the questions the view prints, one at a time. Your child answers aloud; give no hints.
  **Say.** "How does Mira respond when the paper curls?" Expect: what she does, not "she is kind".
- **15 min. Written response.** Read the new event card twice. Your child fills the student page.
- **10 min. Ee and ea.** Write need, week, beak and reach. Your child reads each.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: a drawing for the written response. Longer: a second event card.

### Words and supports

Frames: "The text says ___ ." "Mira ___ because ___ ." Pointing is an answer.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 hands back day 11's assessment by letter: error A, B or C goes to its group. If many children gave error M or Q on the check, repair it again on day 12 in foundations.
