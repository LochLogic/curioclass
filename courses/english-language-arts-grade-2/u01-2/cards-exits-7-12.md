# Exit cards, days 7 to 12

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines. You read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv.

- **Day 7 (s01-X7).** "Mira and Ada carry a heavy crate of books." Picture: each girl holds one handle and the crate hangs level between them. Ask: "Tell me what the picture adds about how they carry the crate."
- **Day 8 (s01-X8).** "The ribbon is too short, so Mira ties on a second piece. Wind lifts her paper, so she closes the window." Ask: "Tell me what Mira does after each event."
- **Day 9 (s01-X9).** Ada says: "Leo waited until I finished my turn." Ask: "Say one comment that links to what Ada said. Start with the words You said."
- **Day 10 (s01-X10).** Printed card: "Leo puts his empty basket by the door. He slips the notes inside. At last, his notes have a place." Ask: "Read the card to me so I can hear every sentence."
- **Day 12 (s01-X12).** "Mira's mitten drops behind a heavy shelf. She asks the teacher to move the shelf." Ask: "Tell me how Mira responds when the mitten drops behind the shelf."

## Day 12 retry cards, by recorded letter

- **Letter A, tin card (s01-X12a).** "Ben puts crayons in a tin. He sets the tin on the top shelf." Ask: "Tell me where Ben sets the tin."
- **Letter C, chairs card (s01-X12c).** "Ben stacks the chairs by the door." Picture: three stacks of chairs, their legs fitted together, leaving a clear path to the door. Ask: "Tell me what the picture adds that the words do not say."
- **Letter B or no letter.** The day 12 shelf card above (s01-X12).
