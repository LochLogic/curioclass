# Day 12: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill the first column of the day 12 group record from your notes on day 11's assessment: A, B, C or none. Make four groups of up to eight. Mark the three retry passages: the gym paragraph (chapter 4), the "Not now" lines (chapter 7) and the end of chapter 8. Today's six are the second group on the record sheet. Their oral card: feed, deep, leaf, team, sunny, jelly; and "Dee and Lee keep a big green leaf."

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Give the class one result by letter, never by name: "Many of us found the action that answers the event." If your notes show a different strength, say it in the same shape. Then name what we will work on: "Some of us will reread, some will map a vowel team, some will practise talking, and some will practise answering from the text or the picture. Everyone tries a fresh card at the end." A child may pass at any time. No one is the example of a mistake.

#### 40 min. Four 10-minute teacher groups

Make the groups from your notes: round one by letters O and P (the day 10 exit and reading notes), round two by L and M (the day 10 written check), round three by H to K (talk notes from days 8, 9 and 11), round four by A, B or C. A child in more than one group goes to the group they need most. Sit with one group at a time, ten minutes each, in the order below. The other children work as partners at their desks: reread a chapter of their choice, sort the event and action cards, practice the link card, and tell each other "What does the challenge make Mira do?", using the story-map sheet and the talk routine card. Post these four tasks. Each teacher group repairs one letter. In each group, take about a minute and a half for every child to say or read one thing.

Round one, O and P (reading), 10 minutes. Read the day 10 passage aloud in phrases with the slash marks; the group echoes you, then the partner reads, then each child reads one phrase. Pause at each full stop. Ask: "What does Mira do for Tomas?" Expect: she opens the door and says she will be his partner. For error P, cover the picture on page 3 of The Rainy Day, read the letters first, then check against the picture.

Round two, L and M (vowels), 10 minutes. For error M, map the whole team to one sound before blending: rain, play, wait. For error L, blend a matched pair with the e hidden and then shown: mat, mate. Children map one word each on tiles and read it.

Round three, H to K (talk), 10 minutes. Rehearse the wait signal once. Practice a link: say a remark, such as "Ada missed a day of subtraction," and each child says "You said ___; I would add ___ ." Then say an unclear remark: "Mira took the thing to the place." Children ask "Which part do you mean?" and wait for the answer. For error H, one child speaks at a time. For error J, ask for more before saying yes. For error K, say the speaker's idea back before asking.

Round four, A, B or C, by letter, 10 minutes. Error A: read "Mira walked across the room to Leo" and show the word where on the question words card. Ask "Where did she go?" The child puts a finger on the line that says. Error B: lay the card "Leo asks Mira to play cards" beside two cards, "Mira says, 'No, thank you.'" and "Mira is shy." The child picks the card that says what she does. Error C: show the chapter 4 picture, cover the words and ask what the picture shows; the child says "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ."

#### 15 min. Fresh retry

Unscored. Reread aloud twice the passage for each child's recorded letter: the gym paragraph of chapter 4 (s01-X12av2) for letter A, the "Not now" lines of chapter 7 with its picture (s01-X12cv2) for letter C, and the end of chapter 8 (s01-X12v2) for letter B or no letter. Your six answer to you, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner, then draw what the story says in the box on the student page. Shown means the answer is right and uses a word of the story or a detail of the picture. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What does the story say?" Not yet seen means a wrong answer, or none. Write the result on the group record. If the same answer is wrong in a whole group, note the letter for day 13.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Retrieve what children met on day 11: write need, week, beak and reach. Children map the team in each word (ee or ea, both long e), blend and spell it on tiles. If a child reads ee or ea with a short vowel (error M), map the whole team first, then blend.

#### 15 min. Reader

Say the title, The Green Beanbag, and read it aloud once as a model, pointing to each word. Map the tricky words said, to and the on the wall and say them plainly; no one guesses from the picture or the first letter (error P). Children then read it in chorus, one page at a time. Ask after pages 1 and 4: "Who is on this page?" Dictate two words and a sentence for children to spell on paper or with tiles: need and week, and "We need to feed the baby." They underline the team in each word.

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each. Tiles: spell need, week, beak and reach. Sort: sort the ee, ea and y word cards and say where the team or the y sits. Reread: partners read The Green Beanbag, a page each. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 3 oral card; the others work in stations. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Say: "Say one word with ee, ea or y to your partner and tell where the team sits." Expect: ee or ea in the middle of a word, or y at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the passage on the board; read the retry passages from the story. No wall: draw the sound-spelling-map on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No cards: write each card's sentence on paper and read it aloud.
**Skip.** The groups and the stations.

- **5 min. The passage.** Read the passage from chapter 8 with the slash marks, in phrases.
  **Say.** "What does Mira do for Tomas?" Expect: she opens the door and says she will be his partner.
- **15 min. Try a new card.** Read your child this made-up card: "Mira's marker dries out. She asks Ada for the blue one." Ask your child to tell you how Mira responds. Say the frame "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ."
- **5 min. Ee and ea.** Write need, week, beak and reach. Read each.
  **Say.** "Where does the team sit in each word?" Expect: in the middle of the word.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X12v2]] [[s01-X12av2]] [[s01-X12cv2]] Reread the end of chapter 8 twice; your child answers alone. The chapter 4 and chapter 7 asks are for a child who missed where, or what a picture adds.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut each teacher group to eight minutes, keep the repair and the fresh retry. Longer: a child writes "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ." for two cards and draws both.

### Words and supports

Event, response, picture, link. Frames: "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ." "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ." "You said ___; I would add ___ ." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws, and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 begins the next section and reads the first schools article. Its opening repair is for the commonest retry letter from today: error B (a trait with no response), error A (a detail the story does not give) or error C (a picture named without saying what it adds). Any ee or ea miss from the reader goes to day 13's foundations opening.
