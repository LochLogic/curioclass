# Section 1.2 opener: pictures, events and a first check (days 7 to 12)

## What the section does

Days 7 to 10 finish the story *Mira's New Classroom*. Children explain what a picture adds to a character's response, describe how Mira responds to two events in one paragraph, link comments in a discussion, and reread a passage for understanding and expression. Day 11 is a check day: a short invented text, "The Class Sign", is read aloud twice, and children ask and answer who, what, where, when, why and how questions, tell how Mira responds when the paper curls, and use the words and the picture together. Day 12 is a respond day: you give the class one result by letter, teach four small groups and set a fresh retry. Day 11 and day 12 results are unscored.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes: a 60-minute topic block and a 60-minute foundations block. Cycle 2 (ai and ay) runs on days 7 to 10 and ends with a 15-minute written check on day 10. Cycle 3 (ee, ea and y) opens on day 11, and the cycle 3 reader, *The Green Beanbag*, is first read on day 12. On day 11 the topic block is the check: opening repair 5, read the cold text 6, ask and answer 12, response 8, words and picture 8, written response 15 and a partner reread 6. The foundations block is retrieval 5, cycle 3 opens 15, rotation 37 and close 3. On day 12 the topic block is feedback 5, four 10-minute teacher groups 40 and a 15-minute fresh retry. Every exit is one question you read aloud, and you hear the day's rotating six.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A picture adds something.** Ask what the picture shows that the words do not say, then say how that changes what we know about the character: "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ."
- **Two events, two responses.** Put each event beside what the character does about it, one at a time.
- **A link uses the last speaker's words.** "You said ___; I would add ___ ." A comment that could follow anything is not a link.
- **Read for the listener.** A reader pauses at each full stop so that the listener can follow the meaning.
- **A result is a letter.** Day 12 groups are set by the letter recorded for each child, not by a score.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail that contradicts the card. Reread with a finger on each action.
- **B:** a trait where a response is wanted. Lay the event beside two actions.
- **C:** a picture named without saying what it adds. Cover the words and ask what the picture shows.
- **I, O:** an unrelated comment, and a rushed read with no pause at the full stops. Say the last word first; read in phrases.
- **M, Q:** a vowel team read or spelled with the wrong sound or the pattern dropped. Map the team, then compare with the word wall.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet.
