# Picture card (day 7 on)

A printed illustration cut from a chapter page, pasted on a half sheet, with a strip under it that reads: "The picture shows ___ so ___ ." Pairs take the pictures of chapters 1, 4 and 6 and say what each adds to how Mira responds. On days 14 and 15 the same card holds a photograph or a diagram.
