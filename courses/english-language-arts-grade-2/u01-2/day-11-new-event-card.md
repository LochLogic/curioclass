# Day 11 new event card

For the written response (15 minutes, unscored). Print one card for each child. Read it aloud twice.

**Event.** "The line for the water fountain is long, and the bell will ring soon." Picture: a line of six children at a fountain, a wall clock that shows almost nine, and a drawn bag on the floor.

Children write or draw "Mira ___ because ___ " for this event and say which picture detail helped. The teacher scribes for a child who needs it. Unscored.
