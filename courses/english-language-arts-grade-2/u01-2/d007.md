# Day 7: What does the picture add?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make three picture cards a pair: the pictures of chapters 1, 4 and 6, each under the strip "The picture shows ___ so ___ ." Copy the eight word halves on cards. Today's six are the second group on the record sheet. Their oral card: sail, tail, hay, lay, flat, stone; and "We stay and wait at the gate." Have the exit card, the exit picture and The Rainy Day ready.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 6's exit (error A, a change that did not happen), reread the card aloud with a finger on each action and replace the wrong one with the one the card gives. Otherwise partners use the before and after strip again: one sentence for what Mira did before Ada was away and one for after.

#### 3 min. Words

Review: you taught approach on day 6; children walk toward a spot and say "I approach the door." New word, offer: hold out a pencil and say "I offer you a pencil." Children offer a crayon to a partner and say the sentence.

#### 10 min. Reread chapters 5 and 6 with their pictures

Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Ada, Leo and Ms. Park are invented." Show the chapter 5 picture and reread the napkin lines: "Mira twisted her napkin around her finger. She twisted it the other way." Then show the chapter 6 picture and reread the walk: "She stood up. She walked across the room, past the blue chart and the plant shelf, to the table where Leo sat." Reread each twice. Ask only what children see: "What do you notice?" Take three answers, then move on.

#### 12 min. What a picture adds

Hold up the picture card. Say: "You told what a picture shows that the words do not on day 2, and that is review. Today we use it to see how a picture adds to what Mira does. We say it with a frame." Point to the strip: "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ."

Model with the chapter 6 picture. Read the words again: "She stood up. She walked across the room." Say: "The words say she walks to Leo. Now I look at the picture. Her chin is up and Leo is waving ahead of her. The picture shows her chin up and Leo waving, so Mira walks over ready to ask." Point at each detail as you name it.

**Ask.** What does the picture show that the words do not say? Expect: her chin is up, Leo is waving, or the small empty chair behind her.

**Ask.** What do we say after "so"? Expect: what Mira does or how she walks. If a child says "the picture has a girl" or only names a thing, say: "That names what is there. What does it show about what she does?" (error C).

Children say the frame once with a partner while you point to the picture.

#### 15 min. Pairs use three picture cards

Pairs take the cards for chapters 1, 4 and 6, three minutes each, and say the frame for each: what the picture shows, then what Mira does. For chapter 6 the pair finds a different detail from the one you used. Partners link: "I agree because ..." or "I would add ...", and clarify with "Which part do you mean?" in the last three minutes. Hear your six, half a minute each, while pairs talk.

Expect chapter 1: the picture shows Mr. Davis waiting a few steps behind her, so Mira has time to open the door herself. Expect chapter 4: the picture shows her looking up, not at her feet, so Mira walks beside Ada. Expect chapter 6: the picture shows the empty chair and name card small behind her, so Mira leaves Ada's desk and walks on. If a pair names only the snowflake or the fish, ask: "What does it show about what Mira does?" (error C). If a pair says what Mira is like, such as "she is scared", say: "That tells what she is like. What does she do?" (error B).

#### 10 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask the day's question: "What does the picture show that the words do not?" Run the four steps, two minutes each: turn (draw or write one picture detail), pair, link, clarify. Two minutes remain for you to call two pairs back to the class. Listen to your six during the pair and link steps. Expect: a detail of a face, a path, a chair or a person, and what Mira does because of it. If someone talks over a partner (error H), rehearse the wait signal once.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice and show the picture (s01-X7): Mira and Ada carry a heavy crate of books; each girl holds one handle and the crate hangs level between them. Ask: "Tell me what the picture adds about how they carry the crate." Your six answer aloud, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means the answer says what the picture shows and what that adds. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What does the picture show?" Not yet seen means only a thing named, or no answer.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Retrieve ai and ay on the sound-spelling-map: say rain and day, children say the sounds, map the team on tiles (r, ai, n; d, ay), blend, and spell each with tiles. If many read ai with a short a (error M), map the whole team to one sound first, then blend.

#### 15 min. Open and closed syllables

Three minutes of review: children sorted closed and open syllables on days 1 and 3, and built napkin from nap and kin on day 2. Say nap-kin and clap two beats. Lay out the halves: nap, kin, pa, per, mu, sic, he, ro. A syllable that ends in a consonant is closed, and the vowel says its short sound: nap, kin. A syllable that ends in a vowel is open, and the vowel says its name: pa, mu, he, ro. Children sort the halves into open and closed, then join pairs: nap-kin, pa-per, mu-sic, he-ro. Say each word and clap it.

Four minutes: show that the teams stay whole across a break: ex-plain and dis-play. Underline plain and play; each has a team that says long a. Say: "Explain means tell how or why." Children say each word, find the team and say its sound.

Eight minutes: model the first reading of The Rainy Day, pointing to each word, then read it again in chorus, one page at a time. Map the tricky words on the wall (said, have, they) and say them plainly; do not let anyone guess from the picture or the first letter (error P). After each page, ask: "Who is on this page?"

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each. Tiles: spell wait, stay, may and paid. Sort: sort the eight word halves into open and closed, then sort word cards into ai and ay and say where the team sits. Reread: partners read The Rainy Day, a page each, then swap. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 2 oral card; the others work in stations. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Say: "Tell your partner which team you mapped first and where it sat in the word." Expect: ai in the middle of a word, or ay at the end.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the chapter pictures with pencil on half sheets. No wall: draw the sound-spelling-map on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No picture cards: look at the pictures in the story book page by page.
**Skip.** The stations and the open and closed sort.

- **5 min. A picture adds.** Show one picture from the story. Say the frame "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ."
  **Say.** "What does the picture show that the words do not say?" Expect: a chin, a chair, a path or a person, and what Mira does.
- **10 min. Two pictures.** Read the two chapter lines aloud. The child points to a detail in each picture and says the frame.
- **10 min. Ai and ay.** Write rain, wait, day and stay. Read each.
  **Say.** "Where does ai sit, and where does ay sit?" Expect: ai in the middle, ay at the end.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]] Read the card twice and show the picture. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the pairs to two cards (chapters 1 and 6), shorten the sort and keep the model, the exit and the reader. Longer: a child draws a new detail in the chapter 4 picture and says the frame for it.

### Words and supports

Offer, approach, picture, shows. Frames: "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ." "You said ___; I would add ___ ." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws, and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 reads chapter 7. If many children gave error C on today's exit (a picture named without saying what it adds), its opening repair covers the words and asks what the picture shows, then says it with the frame. If many read ai with a short a (error M), the foundations opening maps the whole team before blending.
