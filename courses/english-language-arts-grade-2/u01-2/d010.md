# Day 10: How does the story end?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the passage from the end of chapter 8 (69 words) with its slash marks, one a pair. Print the pattern card with two dictated words and the exit card. Today's six are the fifth group on the record sheet. Their oral card: pain, play, shape, flip, chest, them; and "Jay and May wait in the rain." Day 6's six also read a fresh card: stain, gray, stripe, crisp, chunk, thin; and "Jay will stay and play with May."

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 9's exit (error I, a comment that does not use the last speaker's word), say Ada's word first, then add: "You said waited; I would add ..." with a new line of your own, and let two children try. Otherwise partners hold a line card from day 9 and link to it with the link card.

#### 3 min. Words

Review: offer, from day 7, and accept, from day 8; pairs offer a crayon and accept it. New word, return: give back. Children say "I return your crayon" and give it back. Then they tell a partner one thing Mira offered, and one thing she accepted, in the story so far.

#### 10 min. Read chapter 8, The Newest Pupil

Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Tomas, Ada and Leo are invented." Show the picture, then read the whole chapter once, about four minutes. Pause after "It was the sound of a person standing very still," after "Mira knew that look" and at the end. At each pause say "I wonder ..." and let one child add one. Then ask: "What do you notice in the picture?" and take three answers. Ask nothing about children's own first days or homes.

#### 15 min. Reread the child passage

Give each pair the passage. Read it aloud once, about a minute, and stop at each slash mark. Review from grade 1: read in smooth phrases, as in talking, not word by word. Pairs read it twice, a part each, then swap. The first reading is for meaning, five minutes: after it ask, "What does Mira do for Tomas?" Expect: she opens the door, says welcome and says she will be his partner. The second reading is for expression, five minutes: read each phrase, pause at each full stop, and let the voice get softer for the last line, "I will be your partner." Walk round and log the child reading of your six, half a minute each, on the record sheet. If a pair reads in one breath (error O), read the first line with them in phrases.

#### 12 min. Tell the story with the map

Pairs use the story-map sheet. Each pair chooses one challenge and response from chapter 3 and one from chapter 7 and tells both: "The challenge is ___, so Mira ___ ." Chapter 3: she folds her hands and says "I will just watch" when everyone talks. Chapter 7: she steps back, then asks "Did you mean no?" Partners link ("I agree because ..." or "I would add ...") and clarify with "Which part do you mean?" Pairs then tell the class in one sentence how her responses changed. Expect: first she watched, later she asked a question. If a pair names how Mira feels instead of what she does, say: "That tells how she is. What does she do?" (error B).

#### 10 min. Perform the passage

Partners read the passage once more in phrases, a part each, for four minutes. Then show the picture of chapter 8 and say the frame from day 7: "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ." Pairs say what it adds. Expect: Ada waving and Leo with his thumb up behind them, so Mira welcomes Tomas with her whole class behind her, or Mira holds the door with an open hand, so she welcomes him. Take three pairs.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give your six the printed card (s01-X10): "Leo puts his empty basket by the door. He slips the notes inside. At last, his notes have a place." Ask: "Read the card to me so I can hear every sentence." About twenty seconds each; the rest read it to a partner. Shown means an accurate read, a pause at each full stop and a warm last sentence. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "Read it again and stop at each full stop." Not yet seen means one breath for all three sentences, or many misread words.

#### 15 min. Written check (foundations)

Unscored, to see what each child can do without help. Give each child the check sheet. Read each sentence twice and say its missing word; children write the word in the gap. 1. "Mira put the letter in the mail." The missing word is mail. 2. "The hose can spray water." The missing word is spray. 3. "The trail goes up the hill." The missing word is trail. 4. "She made a braid in her hair." The missing word is braid. Give no help with spelling and no hint about the team. Collect the sheets. Mark each word by letter on the record sheet: Q for mail written mal or mayl, spra or braed, L for brade or trale, and M when a short vowel is read in place of the team. Write other spellings down as they are.

#### 5 min. Reader

Partners reread The Rainy Day for expression, a page each, pausing at each full stop. Say: "Read it so your partner enjoys the story."

#### 25 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each, two rounds. Tiles: spell the ai and ay words on the wall. Sort: sort the ai and ay cards and say where the team sits. Reread: partners read The Rainy Day again. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 2 oral card; the others work in stations. Round 2, 12 minutes: every group visits a second station. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Fresh response

No new teaching. Day 6's six read the fresh card to you, two minutes each, 12 minutes. The others work in pairs with the pattern card: they read it, then take turns spelling the two dictated words you say, pray and fail, on paper or with tiles. For the last three minutes, everyone chooses a word from the wall that was hard for them and reads it to a partner.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the passage on the board with the slash marks. No wall: draw the sound-spelling-map on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed passage: write it on paper with a slash after each phrase. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.
**Skip.** The stations and the written check.

- **5 min. Chapter 8.** Read the chapter aloud. Say this story is made up.
  **Say.** "What does Mira do for Tomas?" Expect: she opens the door and says she will be his partner.
- **10 min. Read the passage.** Your child reads the passage twice, once for meaning and once so you enjoy it.
- **10 min. The picture.** Look at the chapter 8 picture. Your child says "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X10]] Give your child the printed card and listen to the reading.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one reading of the passage for each purpose, a shorter tell-the-story part and the exit; the written check stays. Longer: a child reads the passage to a younger child and says what the second reading changed.

### Words and supports

Offer, accept, return, expression. Frames: "The challenge is ___, so Mira ___ ." "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws, and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 is the first check: a short invented text read aloud twice. If many children gave error O on today's exit (a read with no pause at the full stops), read the passage in phrases in group time on day 12, and in day 11's partner reread. The written check shows who needs the wall words for ai and ay, and day 11's opening repair uses them.
