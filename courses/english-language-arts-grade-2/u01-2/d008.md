# Day 8: What does Mira do after each event?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write four event cards and four action cards a pair (listed in the pairs part) and twelve word cards: rain, wait, paid, chain, day, play, stay, may, nap, kin, pa, mu. Today's six are the third group on the record sheet. Their oral card: maid, bay, plain, brave, slip, chin; and "May and Jay play in the sand."

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

If many children missed day 7's exit (error C, a picture named without saying what it adds), cover the words of the chapter 4 picture card, ask what the picture shows, and say it with the frame "The picture shows ___ so Mira ___ ." Use the chapter 4 picture, not the exit picture. Otherwise partners use the challenge and response boxes of the story-map (review from day 3): each tells one challenge from chapter 3 and what Mira did about it.

#### 5 min. Words

Review: hesitate, from day 6; children stop with one foot in the air. New word, accept: when someone offers you a thing and you take it, you accept. Pairs act it out: "I offer you a crayon." "I accept." Then they say one sentence with hesitate and one with accept.

#### 12 min. Read chapter 7, Not Now

Say aloud: "This story is made up. Mira, Ada and Ms. Park are invented. In this chapter two words hurt Mira's feelings for a moment, and the chapter ends well." Show the picture, then read the chapter once, about four minutes, pausing after "Not now," after "Mira felt her face go hot" and after "did you mean no?". At each pause say "I wonder ..." and let one child add one. Look at the picture for two minutes. Ask only: "What do you notice about Ada's eyes and Mira's shoulders?" Ask nothing about children's own friends or homes.

#### 12 min. Two events, two responses

Say: "Review from grade 1: major events are the important things that happen. Two major events happen to Mira, one in chapter 6 and one in chapter 7. We put each event beside what she does." Show the story-map row with two boxes: Event one, Event two. Event one, from chapter 6: Ada's desk is empty. Write it, and beside it write what Mira did: she stood up, walked to Leo and asked him to be her partner.

Model event two, from chapter 7: Ada says "Not now." Say: "Here is the event. Now what does Mira do? First she steps back and folds her hands. Then she remembers the chart, unfolds her hands, walks back and asks, 'Did you mean no?'" Say the frame: "The event is Ada says 'Not now,' so Mira steps back, and then she asks."

**Ask.** What is the event in chapter 7? Expect: Ada says "Not now."

**Ask.** What does Mira do about it? Expect: she steps back and folds her hands, then asks "Did you mean no?"

If a child says "Mira is hurt", say: "That tells how she feels. What does she do?" (error B).

#### 13 min. Pairs place cards

The event cards read: Ada's desk is empty. Ada says "Not now." Leo asks again about the cards. Ada says "I meant after." The action cards read: Mira walks to Leo and asks to be his partner. Mira asks, "Did you mean no?" Mira says, "Yes. I accept." Mira starts the map. Pairs match each event card to its action card and say the frame for each: "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ." Then they answer two questions for the "Not now" card, a why and a how. Why: "Why does Mira ask?" Expect: because "Not now" sounded like no. How: "How does she ask?" Expect: in a steady voice, after she walks back to Ada's desk. Each pair then reads its four matches to another pair, and the listeners link: "I agree because ..." or "I would add ...". Hear your six, half a minute each, while pairs work. If a pair matches Leo's invitation to "Mira starts the map", ask them to find which chapter has the line (error A).

#### 8 min. Turn, pair, link, clarify

Ask the day's question: "How are her two responses alike or different?" Run the four steps, two minutes each: turn (draw or write one way they are alike or different), pair, link, clarify. Listen to your six during the pair and link steps. Expect: alike, she acts both times and does not stay silent; different, the first time she walks to a friend, the second time she asks a question. If someone talks over a partner (error H), rehearse the wait signal once.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card twice and ask (s01-X8): "Tell me what Mira does after each event." Your six answer aloud, about twenty seconds each; the rest tell a partner. Shown means both events with their actions. Shown with help means right after one prompt: "What happens first? What does she do? What happens next?" Not yet seen means an action that contradicts the card, or no answer.

#### 5 min. Opening repair (foundations)

Write two ai or ay words that many children missed on day 7 (wait and stay if none were). Children map each team to one sound, blend and spell it on tiles. If a child reads a team with a short vowel (error M), map the whole team first, then blend.

#### 15 min. Reread and sort

Three minutes: partners reread The Rainy Day, a page each, for accuracy first. Then six minutes: sort the twelve word cards into four columns, ai, ay, open halves and closed halves (rain, wait, paid, chain; day, play, stay, may; pa, mu; nap, kin), and say where the team sits. Three minutes: dictate two sentences for children to spell on paper or with tiles, saying each word slowly: "I can explain the plan." "Dale will display a big tray." Children underline the team in explain and display. Three minutes: show the sentence "Jay can stai in the rain." on the board Children find the one wrong word, say why it is wrong (ay sits at the end of stay) and fix it (error Q).

#### 37 min. Rotation

Three stations, ten children each. Tiles: build rain, wait, day and play, then plain and display. Sort: sort the twelve word cards again and add one new word to each column. Reread: partners read The Rainy Day, a page each, then swap. Round 1, 13 minutes: your six sit with you, two minutes each, for the cycle 2 oral card; the others work in stations. Rounds 2 and 3, 12 minutes each. Mark the heard column.

#### 3 min. Close

Say: "Show your partner one event card you placed and say the frame: 'The event is ___, so Mira ___ .'" Expect: any matched pair, such as Ada's desk is empty, so Mira walks to Leo.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards on torn paper. No wall: draw the sound-spelling-map on the board. No tiles: write letters on torn paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on torn paper. No cards: write each event and action on its own slip.
**Skip.** The stations and the wrong-word hunt.

- **5 min. Two events.** Say that Mira has a big event in chapter 6 and another in chapter 7.
- **10 min. Not Now.** Read chapter 7 aloud. Say this story is made up.
  **Say.** "What does Mira do when Ada says 'Not now'?" Expect: she steps back, then asks "Did you mean no?"
- **10 min. Place the cards.** Lay out the event and action cards. The child matches each pair and says "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]] Read the card twice. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: match only the two cards for chapters 6 and 7, cut the wrong-word hunt and keep the model, the question and the exit. Longer: a child draws both events and writes "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ." under each.

### Words and supports

Hesitate, accept, event, response. Frames: "The event is ___, so Mira ___ ." "You said ___; I would add ___ ." Pointing is an answer; a child who cannot write draws, and you write the words. A child may pass.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 reads the exchange in chapter 7 again. If many children gave error A on today's exit (an action that contradicts the card), its opening repair rereads the card with a finger on each action and replaces the wrong one. If many read ai with a short a (error M), the foundations opening maps the whole team before blending.
