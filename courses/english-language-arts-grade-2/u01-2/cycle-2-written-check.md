# Cycle 2 written check (day 10, 15 minutes, unscored)

The child's sheet has four numbered lines with a printed gap. You read each sentence twice and say the missing word; the child writes the word in the gap. None is a word from days 6 to 9.

1. "Mira put the letter in the ____." (mail)
2. "The hose can ____ water." (spray)
3. "The ____ goes up the hill." (trail)
4. "She made a ____ in her hair." (braid)

Tag wrong spellings: "mal" or "mayl" for mail (Q, the team ai is not carried over or ay is used in the middle); "spra" (Q); "trale" (L); "braed" (Q); "brade" (L, a long sound spelled with the silent-e pattern). A short vowel read in place of the team is M. Record other spellings separately.
