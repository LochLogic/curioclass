# Day 150: Assemble the packet and check that the steps follow a clear order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Carry day 149's exit marks to the record sheet. Get the packet envelopes from the folder. Print poem P once for each child in the first fifteen and keep the copies in the folder. Make three groups of five from children 1 to 15. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 13 to 18) and the six for the oral card (children 7 to 12).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Hold up the four-finger card: beat, rhyme, repeated sound, repeated line. Review: you learned these four sound devices on days 126 and 127. Each pair names the four on its fingers and says one thing a device does. Then point to the sequence words on the wall: first, next, then, after that, last. Review: time words from unit 2 and the chain of steps from unit 3. Put the card away before the first group sits at the table.

#### 35 min. Assemble and P1

P1 comes first, before any poem work. Children 1 to 15 come to your table in three groups of five, six minutes each (18 minutes inside the 35). Give each child a printed copy of poem P, read it aloud once tapping a steady beat (30 seconds), then ask each child the four asks one at a time, about 65 seconds a child: [[s04-A21]], [[s04-A22]], [[s04-A23]], [[s04-A24]]. Give no help beforehand and do not read the poem to the class. Record the support used on the P1 record sheet; a prompt such as "What does that do for the poem?" is recorded as shown with help. A sound named without what it adds is error O; a missing sound is error AF. Collect the copies.

The other children put the envelope, the drawing, the edited card and the printed invitation together in the packet envelope. They check that the five class planting steps on the chart keep their order, and the first group back from the table joins them. Walk among them and say nothing about the poem.

#### 10 min. Sequence check

Pairs check the class steps against 05-d: choose two steps, swap them, act the swapped steps with the cup and torn paper, and say in a few words what goes wrong (RI.2.3; review of days 143 and 146, where you learned that each step changes what the next step needs). Pairs then swap back and say the right order with the sequence words. Listen to six pairs, about 90 seconds a pair. A pair that says only "because it is next" has stated the order with no connection (error Z): ask what the step changes.

#### 5 min. Read together

Pairs reread the five steps aloud, finger under the words, one step each by turns. If many read both lines in one voice on day 149's exit, they reread the steps again with a slower, clearer voice on the reason words.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud twice the first-reason paragraph of 05-a and show the three strips: [[s04-X150v2]]. Call the first of the six to your table at once. The class marks one strip at their seats while you hear the six, about 45 seconds each: [[s04-X150bv2]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed here. On slates, build enormous and bonus with tiles, tap the parts and say which ending each takes. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 12 min. Read the matched reader

Pairs reread The Joyous Bus, a page each, for accuracy (6 minutes, RF.2.4a). Confirm the meaning from the print and the sentence (RF.2.4c, 6 minutes): **Ask.** "Why are the children joyous?" Expect: they are going to a park, and they sing. **Ask.** "What does Gus say about the crocus?" Expect: it is marvelous. A child who guesses a word from the picture reads it again, letter by letter, with the tiles.

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 7 to 12 read the cycle 26 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. Model one word on the board before the first turn: octopus, tapped in three beats and blended. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 16 min. Spell with a sort and sentence strips

This is not dictation. Pairs sort eight word cards by ending (6 minutes): joyous, enormous, marvelous and dangerous go under -ous; bus, circus, bonus and chorus go under -us. Then each child completes two sentence strips with a gap, choosing a word from the sorted cards and spelling it (6 minutes), and fixes two wrong spellings on the board: joyus, bonous (4 minutes). Error R: a pattern not carried over; ask which ending the word needs.

#### 10 min. Map irregular words

Map are, they, says and of from the reader. Say each word, circle the letters that do not say what you expect, and write it on a slate from memory (10 minutes). Do not guess a word from a picture or its first letter.

#### 5 min. Close

Pairs read one page of The Joyous Bus to each other, 45 seconds each way with the swap.

### Other rooms

Write the five class steps on the board and act with a cup and torn paper. Poem P is read aloud by you at the table; the child may answer aloud or with a gesture. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is a mark on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the steps on scraps. No cup: use any bowl and torn paper.
**Skip.** The poem check at the table, the oral card and the sorting.

- **10 min. Steps.** Read the five planting steps together, swap two and act them.
  **Say.** "What goes wrong?" Expect: for example, water has nowhere to drain, or the seeds wash away.
- **10 min. Packet.** Your child puts the drawing, the edited card and the invitation in the envelope.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child builds enormous and bonus with scraps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X150v2]] and [[s04-X150bv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the table sittings, the assembly and the exit. Longer: a child writes the five steps with first, next, then, after that and last.

### Words and supports

Packet, assemble, sequence, first, next, then, after that, last, because. Frames: "___ comes before ___ because ___." "Without ___, ___." Read the strips aloud, enlarge print, accept acting or a labeled drawing for a reason. At the table a question may be repeated; a prompt is recorded as help.

### Tomorrow

Day 151 opens by acting hand washing in the wrong order, from the commonest wrong answer on today's exit where the order is stated with no connection. P1 notes for children 1 to 15 are written after class and handed back on day 151 for a ten-minute retry. Today's exit marks go to the record sheet.
