# Day 151: Rehearse a packet reflection and answer a listener in a whole sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Carry day 150's exit marks to the record sheet. Write the P1 notes for children 1 to 15 after day 150's table and mark each child's missing or unexplained effect. Hand back day 150's items with the notes. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 19 to 24) and the six for the oral card (children 13 to 18). Say aloud that the exit card is made up for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

From the commonest wrong answer on day 150's exit, where the order is stated with no connection (error Z): pairs act washing hands with the rinse first and with the soap first, and say in a few words what is left on the hands. Then say the rule together: each step changes what the next step needs. Review: you learned this on days 143 and 146.

#### 10 min. Model a reflection

Say a two-sentence reflection, invented for practice: "My packet is for people who want a pot of wildflowers. I moved the room line on my invitation so a guest can find it." Then ask the listener question aloud and give two answers side by side: "Where did you move it?" A fragment, "Up." A whole sentence, "I moved it up under the title." A fragment is never marked wrong in conversation; it is a request for detail that needs a sentence (SL.2.6). Pairs say each answer back and say which one tells the listener more (4 minutes). Then pairs sort six printed starters into whole sentence and not yet a whole sentence: "My packet.", "I changed my ending.", "On the card.", "Now a reader can plant a pot.", "In the envelope.", "I added a label." (4 minutes). Review: whole sentences from day 60. Children say the whole one for each fragment (2 minutes).

#### 20 min. Reflect in groups

Six groups of five. Round one, each child gives a two-sentence reflection on their packet, two minutes a child (10 minutes), using the reflection strips: "I changed ___." "I changed it because ___." "Now a reader can ___." Round two, each child asks one listener question and gets an answer, two minutes a child (10 minutes). **Ask.** "Which words did you change, and why?" Expect: a whole sentence that names the words and the reason. Listen in four groups, about 3 minutes each (12 minutes), and record SL.2.3 and SL.2.4 on the record sheet. Error W: an answer that ignores the question; repeat the question and ask for the answer again.

#### 10 min. P1 retry

Hear children 1 to 15 whose notes name a missing or unexplained effect, 45 seconds each, at your table: the child names the effect and says what it adds, with no new reading of poem P (up to 13 children, 45 seconds each with the change-over counted, so the 10 minutes are full). A right retry is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. A child not reached is written on day 152's P1 record and heard there after the P1 sittings. Children whose notes show all four effects, and the rest, reread poem 06-a in pairs, finger under the words, once for accuracy and once smoothly. Review: 06-a from days 126 and 150.

#### 10 min. Write

Each child writes the two reflection sentences by hand on a strip for the packet and reads them to a partner (7 minutes, W.2.5). Check one spelling against the sound-spelling map (2 minutes). The strip goes in the packet envelope (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say aloud that the card is made up. Read the card twice and show the two answer strips: [[s04-X151]]. Call the first of the six to your table at once. The class marks one strip at their seats while you hear the six, about 45 seconds each, a second listener asking the question: [[s04-X151b]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed here. On slates, build bus and circus with tiles, tap the parts and say which ending each takes. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 8 min. Revise the cycle 25 record

Return each child's day 148 check sheet with the commonest miss marked (error R, a pattern not carried over). On the revision sheet the child writes the right form beside the miss and reads it aloud once. A child who still needs an oral turn on cycle 25 reads the card to you now; the score is made afterward.

#### 10 min. Reread the cycle 26 reader

Pairs reread The Joyous Bus for accuracy, a page each (5 minutes). The class reads it again smoothly, pausing at each full stop (5 minutes, RF.2.4b).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 13 to 18 read the cycle 26 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. Model one word on the board before the first turn: chorus, tapped in two beats and blended. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Sort

Pairs sort twelve word cards by ending (7 minutes): joyous, enormous, marvelous and dangerous under -ous; bus, circus, bonus, chorus, octopus, crocus, fungus and Gus under -us. Then each child picks four cards, looks, covers, writes from memory and checks (8 minutes). This is not dictation.

#### 10 min. Use the pattern in sentences

Each child writes one sentence with a cycle word (7 minutes) and checks one spelling in the printed dictionary (2 minutes). Two children read theirs to the class (1 minute). A spelling left unchecked is error S.

### Other rooms

No printed strips: write the starters on the board. Hear the retry at your desk. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is a mark on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the starters on scraps. No dictionary: use any book with word lists.
**Skip.** The group rounds, the retry and the oral card.

- **10 min. Say it.** Your child says two sentences about their packet and answers one question from you.
  **Say.** "Which words did you change, and why?" Expect: a whole sentence naming the words and the reason.
- **10 min. Write.** Your child writes the two sentences on a strip.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child looks, covers, writes and checks four words on the page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X151]] and [[s04-X151b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, one round of reflection and the exit. Longer: a child answers two listener questions in whole sentences.

### Words and supports

Reflect, listener, whole sentence, changed, because. Frames: "I changed ___." "I changed it because ___." "Now a reader can ___." "The listener asked ___." Read the strips aloud, enlarge print, let a child say a sentence in the home language first, and accept a drawing with a spoken sentence.

### Tomorrow

Day 152 opens by replaying the question and answering again in a whole sentence for the commonest wrong answer on today's exit, a fragment or an answer that ignores the question. It runs the second sitting on poem P for children 16 to 30 and hears any child from 1 to 15 not reached today. Today's exit marks go to the record sheet; the cycle 25 score is made after class.
