# Day 152: Rehearse a poem and explain how its sounds help its meaning

## Teacher page

### Before class

Carry day 151's exit marks to the record sheet and the retry marks to the P1 record sheet. Print poem P once for each child from 16 to 30 and keep the copies in the folder. Make three groups of five from children 16 to 30. Write the six sound cards. Mark the six for the spoken exit ask (children 25 to 30) and the six for the oral card (children 19 to 24).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

If many answered with a fragment or ignored the question on day 151's exit, replay the question and answer again in a whole sentence. Say a listener question, invented for practice: "Which part of your packet did you finish first?" A fragment answer, "My drawing." Then a whole sentence, "I finished my drawing first." Then an answer that ignores the question, "It has a bee on it", and the class asks for the answer to the question that was asked. Review: whole sentences from day 60. Pairs each give one whole-sentence answer to the same question, 45 seconds each way.

#### 35 min. P1 and rehearsal

P1 comes first, before any poem work. Children 16 to 30 come to your table in three groups of five, six minutes each (18 minutes inside the 35). Give each child a printed copy of poem P, read it aloud once tapping a steady beat (30 seconds), then ask each child the four asks one at a time, about 65 seconds a child: [[s04-A25]], [[s04-A26]], [[s04-A27]], [[s04-A28]]. Give no help beforehand; a prompt such as "What does that do for the poem?" is recorded as shown with help. Hold the four-finger card out of sight. A sound named without what it adds is error O; a missing sound is error AF. Collect the copies.

While you sit at the table, children 1 to 15 rehearse 06-a and 06-b in three groups of five, reading a line each in a steady beat and tapping each beat with a finger (RF.2.4b). Then the pair sorts the six sound cards under rhyme, first sound and repeated line: the cards read: grow and go; one and sun; bright bees buzz; soft wings sail on the sunny breeze; room for wings, room for wings; plant a flower, plant a flower (6 minutes). Review: the four sound devices from days 126 and 127. The rehearsal runs on its own while you sit, and the sittings do not overlap it for you. After P1, children 16 to 30 join the rehearsal, and in the 17 minutes left you hear the children from 1 to 15 listed as not reached on day 151 (at most two), 45 seconds each with the change-over: name the effect and say what it adds, with no new reading of poem P. A right retry is marked shown with help.

#### 10 min. Sing and read

The class reads or sings 06-b with a steady beat, standing, and a partner taps the repeated line each time it comes back. Review: 06-b from day 127. Say aloud that nobody is scored; the song is for the room.

#### 5 min. Talk

Turn, pair, link, clarify on **Ask.** "How does the repeated line help the song?" Expect: it comes back so you remember it and it asks again for the same thing. Turn: each child speaks 45 seconds in a pair, 2 minutes with the swap. Link: "I heard ___ say ___; I want to add ___" (1 minute). Clarify: "Which part do you mean?" (1 minute). One pair shares, about 45 seconds (1 minute). Any child may pass. Review: turn, pair, link, clarify from unit 1.

#### 5 min. Exit

Show the two printed lines from the last verse of Room for Wings and read them twice: [[s04-X152v2]]. Call the first of the six to your table at once. The class circles one pair of words at their seats while you hear the six, about 45 seconds each: [[s04-X152bv2]]. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Opening repair

No repair is routed here. On slates, build joyous and chorus with tiles, tap the parts and say which ending each takes. Review: -ous and -us from day 149.

#### 15 min. A fresh passage

Read the title Gus and the Log and nothing else. Pairs read the passage, finger under the words (5 minutes, RF.2.4a). It is not the reader. Confirm the meaning (RF.2.4c, 5 minutes): **Ask.** "What grows on the log?" Expect: a fungus. **Ask.** "What does Gus sketch?" Expect: the fungus and a crocus. A child who guesses from the first letter reads the word again, tile by tile (error Q). The class rereads one sentence each by turns (5 minutes).

#### 12 min. Rotation

Children 19 to 24 read the cycle 26 oral card to you, two minutes each including the swap. Model one word on the board before the first turn: fungus, tapped in two beats and blended. The others use tiles and the reader at stations. Mark the heard column.

#### 15 min. Spell with tiles and cloze strips

This is not dictation; the check is on day 153. Pairs build enormous, marvelous, circus and octopus on tiles, tap the parts, blend every letter and check the ending (6 minutes). Then each pair takes the cut copy of Gus and the Log with six words covered: bus, enormous, fungus, marvelous, crocus and joyous. Each child chooses a word from the bank, spells it in the gap and reads the sentence to confirm it fits (9 minutes). Error R: a pattern not carried over; ask which ending the word needs.

#### 8 min. Dictionary

Each pair picks one cycle word from circus, chorus, octopus, bonus, crocus or fungus, finds it in the printed beginning dictionary and checks the spelling, then says what they compared (L.2.2e). If a word has no entry, pick another. A spelling left unchecked is error S.

#### 5 min. Close

Each child reads one sentence from Gus and the Log to a partner, 45 seconds each way with the swap.

### Other rooms

Tap the beat on the table. Write the six sound cards on the board and point to each one. Poem P is read by you at the table; the child may answer aloud or with a gesture. Read the exit card aloud twice; the answer is a circle on a folded sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No tiles: write letters on scraps. No song: say poem 06-a with a steady clap.
**Skip.** The poem check at the table, the oral card and the sorting.

- **10 min. Clap and read.** Read poem 06-a together with a steady clap.
  **Say.** "How does the repeated line help the poem?" Expect: it comes back so you remember it and it asks again for the same thing.
- **10 min. Words.** Your child builds enormous and circus with scraps and fills in two covered words.
- **10 min. Sound cards.** Your child sorts the six sound cards into rhyme, first sound and repeated line.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X152v2]] and [[s04-X152bv2]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the table sittings, the song and the exit. Longer: a child says a fifth effect, such as a quiet last line, or finds a repeated line in a favorite song.

### Words and supports

Beat, rhyme, repeated sound, repeated line, effect. Frames: "The ___ helps because ___." "The ___ makes me think ___." Tap the beat, accept a gesture or a drawing with an explanation, read the exit lines aloud, and at the table repeat a question when asked; a prompt is recorded as help.

### Tomorrow

Day 153 is the unit check day: groups recount a tale and read two characters in two voices, with the poem and the song retained from today. Today's exit marks go to the record sheet. P1 notes for children 16 to 30 are written after class and handed back on day 155 for a retry; the cycle 25 score is made after class.
