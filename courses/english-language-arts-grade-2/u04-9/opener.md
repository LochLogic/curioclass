# Unit 4.9: Pot labels, a poem and a reflection (days 150 to 152)

## What the section does
The class assembles its packet, makes a label by steps, reflects aloud on a change made after feedback, and learns how a poem uses sound. Poem P is read cold for the first sitting and again for the second.

## How the days run
- Day 150: assemble the packet; the first sitting on poem P (children 1 to 15); a four-finger count of sound devices.
- Day 151: model a reflection; groups answer a listener's question; retry.
- Day 152: second sitting on poem P (children 16 to 30); sing and read.

## The ideas at an adult's level
Steps in a task are ordered by what each one makes possible or prevents. A reflection answers a listener in a whole sentence and says what changed and why. A sound device in a poem (beat, rhyme, repeated sound, repeated line) is understood by what it does for the rhythm or the meaning.

## The errors in plain words
- Steps put in order with no reason why.
- A reply that is a few words, not a sentence.
- An answer that ignores the question the listener asked.
- A sound named without saying what it adds.

## Marking
Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Poem P is observed and recorded; it is never rehearsed before the sittings.
