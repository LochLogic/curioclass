# Exit cards, days 150 to 152

Rows that open "This card is invented for practice" are made up: say so aloud and print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. The other rows reread a passage of the unit's own texts, named in the row. Read the card or passage twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 150 (s04-X150v2).** Reread aloud twice 05-a, Flowers Through the Seasons, paragraph 3, the first reason. Show three strips: A "Bees wake up hungry in early spring." B "Early flowers bloom, so more bees can eat." C "There are more bees later in the year." Say: "Mark the strip that comes right after the early flowers."
- **Day 150 (s04-X150bv2).** Same paragraph. Say: "Say why early flowers come before more bees later."
- **Day 151 (s04-X151).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Before: Our poster is nice. Written feedback: Say what makes it nice. After: Our poster has a red kite in the corner. A listener asks: Why did you change the first sentence?" Show two answer strips. A: "I changed it to say what makes the poster nice." B: "To say." Say: "Mark the answer that is a whole sentence."
- **Day 151 (s04-X151b).** Same card. A second listener asks: "What did you put in the new sentence?" Say: "Answer the listener in a whole sentence."
- **Day 152 (s04-X152v2).** Show the first two lines of verse 3 of Room for Wings (06-a), printed, and read them aloud twice: "Leave a leaf and leave a stem, Safe and warm, a home for them." Say: "Circle the two words that rhyme at the ends of the lines."
- **Day 152 (s04-X152bv2).** Same lines. Say: "Say what those two words do for the poem."
