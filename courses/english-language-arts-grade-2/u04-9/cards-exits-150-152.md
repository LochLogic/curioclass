# Exit cards, days 150 to 152

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Read each card twice, then ask the one question. Answers and wrong answers are in items.csv; they are not printed here. Each day has two rows: the first is marked on paper; the second is spoken and is heard from the rotating six.

- **Day 150 (s04-X150).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Making a shelf sign. A: Write the shelf name in pencil. B: Check the spelling in the dictionary. C: Go over the letters in ink. D: Tape the sign to the shelf." Show four step strips. Say: "Mark the step that comes right after the spelling is checked."
- **Day 150 (s04-X150b).** Same card. Say: "Say why checking the spelling comes before the ink."
- **Day 151 (s04-X151).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Before: Our poster is nice. Written feedback: Say what makes it nice. After: Our poster has a red kite in the corner. A listener asks: Why did you change the first sentence?" Show two answer strips. A: "I changed it to say what makes the poster nice." B: "To say." Say: "Mark the answer that is a whole sentence."
- **Day 151 (s04-X151b).** Same card. A second listener asks: "What did you put in the new sentence?" Say: "Answer the listener in a whole sentence."
- **Day 152 (s04-X152).** This card is invented for practice. Read aloud twice: "Pitter, patter, rain on the pane. Puddles splash along the lane." Show the printed lines. Say: "Circle the two words that rhyme at the ends of the lines."
- **Day 152 (s04-X152b).** Same card. Say: "Say what those two words do for the poem."
