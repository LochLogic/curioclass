# Retrieval cards (day 39)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Three cards, each marked by every child, then six rotating children say why, one card each. A miss: the teacher models observation to claim on a second picture. No heredity and no light mechanism.

**Card 1: a toy in a dark box.** Two small pictures. Left: a closed box with a toy inside and no lamp, labelled dark. Right: the same box with the lid off and a lamp shining on the toy, labelled lamp on.
**Say:** "Mark the picture that shows why we can see the toy. Tell us why."
**Key (teacher):** The lamp-on picture; we see the toy when light shines on it (grade 1 review).

**Card 2: a seedling and its parent plant.** A big plant and a small seedling beside it, both with leaves of the same pointed shape, the seedling smaller.
**Say:** "Mark what is alike. Tell us what tells you."
**Key (teacher):** The leaf shape is alike and the size is different (grade 1 review: alike, not exact copies).

**Card 3: two pictures of water.** Left: an ice cube on a plate, keeping its shape. Right: water poured from a jug into a bowl, flowing to fit it.
**Say:** "Mark the solid water and the liquid water. Show what tells you."
**Key (teacher):** The ice cube is solid water and keeps its shape; the poured water is liquid water and flows (units 1 and 3 review).
