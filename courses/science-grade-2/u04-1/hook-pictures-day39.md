# Hook pictures (day 39; also days 40, 43, 44 and 47)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

These stand in for photographs. The castle and boat also serve the day 40 string repair for error A. The egg set is used again on days 43 and 44 and in the day 44 repair for error C.

## Set 1: the castle and the boat

Six large blocks, each marked with a small letter a to f so that the same block can be found in both pictures: a and b are cubes, c and d are long bricks, e is a triangle block and f is a half-round block.
**Picture 1, labelled castle:** a and b stand as two towers, c and d lie across the top as walls, e sits on a tower as a roof and f stands at the front as a gate.
**Picture 2, labelled boat:** c and d lie side by side as the deck, a and b stand at the two ends, e stands upright as a sail and f sits in the middle as a cabin.

## Set 2: the chocolate

A piece of chocolate bar in a sealed clear bag. Three pictures in a row.
**Picture 1, labelled before:** the bag lies on a tray, the chocolate in a hard block.
**Picture 2, labelled warm water:** the bag floats in a bowl of warm water; the chocolate inside is liquid and has flowed to the bottom. A grown-up in goggles stands at the bowl; children stand behind a line on the floor.
**Picture 3, labelled cooled:** the bag lies flat on the tray, the chocolate a hard flat sheet, a different outline.

## Set 3: the egg

The teacher cooked this egg in a kitchen. Three pictures in a row.
**Picture 1, labelled before:** a raw egg in a dish, a runny clear white and a round yolk.
**Picture 2, labelled cooked:** the same egg after cooking, the white firm and white, the yolk firm, with wavy lines for heat.
**Picture 3, labelled cooled:** the cooked egg on a plate with small snow marks for cool, still firm and white.

**Say:** "Look at all three. Pip says: our block castle became a boat, and melted chocolate hardened again, but a cooked egg never runs again. Which changes can be undone?"
