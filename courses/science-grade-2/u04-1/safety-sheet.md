# Safety sheet (days 39, 42, 43)

Read each line aloud with the picture. Children point to the picture for each line.

1. Sealed things stay sealed. We never open them. (Picture: a closed bag and a closed cup on a tray.)
2. We never taste anything. (Picture: a mouth with a line through a spoon.)
3. Only a grown-up does the warm water. We stay behind the line. (Picture: a grown-up with goggles at a bowl, children behind a floor line.)
4. Blocks and card stay on the table. (Picture: blocks on a table.)
5. If water spills, tell a grown-up and wipe it at once. (Picture: a cloth on a drop of water.)
6. Tell a grown-up if you cannot be near an egg or milk. There is always a picture to use instead. (Picture: a photograph card.)
