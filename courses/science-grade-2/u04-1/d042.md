# Day 42: Did the water go back?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Before children enter, an adult in fitted splash goggles warms the sealed chocolate bag in the warm-water bath, cools it, photographs each step, then clears the bath and any spill. Freeze one sealed water cup the day before and photograph it. Check egg, milk, soy and nut allergies; a child on the list uses the photographs. Put a tray with the sealed water and ice cups on each table. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Warm tap water, not hot, in a sealed tub out of reach, emptied and cleared before children enter; no kettle or heater in the room. Children never open a sealed item or use the freezer. Nothing is eaten.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 41's exit showed who said the floppy strip holds as well (error E). If over a third did, hold up stiff and floppy strips for everyone: which stays standing? Expect: the stiff one; it keeps its shape. Otherwise pass table by table, one minute each.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Say the pond cards are invented. All hold up one finger for solid water, two for liquid water (1 minute). Today's six say what tells them, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Expect: ice keeps its shape; liquid flows. A miss: tilt a sealed cup.

#### 7 min. Chocolate

Lay out the chocolate strip. Each group passes its sealed cooled sample, 15 seconds each child (5 x 15 s = 75 seconds). **Ask.** What did the grown-up do to try to bring it back? Expect: cooled it. Is it hard again? Expect: yes. What is different? Expect: the outline, not the state (error B).

#### 8 min. Water

Each group looks at its tray. Children draw both cups now (3 minutes); the ice cup stays on the tray. Show the water strip: liquid, frozen in the grown-up's freezer, then warmed. An attempted return is the temperature changed back, then looked at again. **Ask.** What was done to change the temperature back, and what do you see? Expect: warmed; liquid again.

#### 6 min. Return arrows

On the change-arrow model add a return arrow for the water and one for the chocolate, and write what was done to change the temperature back (3 minutes). Today's six say "I think ___ went back, because I saw ___," 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 2 min. Look again

Groups look at the sealed ice cup a second time. Any liquid? Looked at, not measured. If unchanged, the water strip supplies the melt; say so aloud.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Put the three exit cards on the wall. Read [[s04-X42]] once; all point to the two pictures (1 minute). Today's six say what was done, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Clean up

The adult takes the cups and bag and wipes spills. Children wipe trays and hands with a damp cloth. Hands are washed before the next food or break, said aloud.

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the third strip: does changing the temperature back restore things? Sometimes.

### Other rooms

No freezer or cups: use the photographs only. No printer: draw the cards.

### At home

**Swap.** A zip bag of water for the sealed cup.

**Skip.** The sketch.

- **10 min. Look.** A grown-up freezes a cup of water in a zip bag overnight. Draw it liquid, then frozen.
- **15 min. Look again.** Let it warm on a table. Draw it again.
  **Say.** "What did we do to change the temperature back? What do you see?" Expect: warmed it; liquid again.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X42]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the water strip; never cut the third look or the exit. Longer: another thing that melts and sets.

### Words and supports

Solid, liquid, warm, cool, freeze, melt, hard, back. Frame: "I think ___ went back, because I saw ___." Read captions aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says freezing alone sent the water back (error D); day 43 repairs it with two cards and a third turned over. Day 43 opens section 4.2 with the cooked egg, a thawed leaf and heated paper, to see changes that remain.
