# Exit cards, days 39 to 42

Each exit has picture cards every child marks, and a spoken stem read once. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. The teacher hears a rotating six.

## Day 39 exit card: five toy train cars

**Picture:** Two cards. Card 1: five toy train cars in a straight row, each a different simple shape. Card 2: the same five cars joined in a curving line. Under them, two circles: yes, no.
**Say:** "Pip says the curving line needed new cars. Is Pip right? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No, with the words that it is the same five cars in a new arrangement. Yes is error A.

## Day 40 exit card: a round piece and a flat piece

**Picture:** Two pieces drawn side by side: a round piece and a long flat piece. A toy stage with no floor drawn behind them.
**Say:** "Pip is building a toy stage and wants a flat floor for it. Touch the piece Pip should use."
**Key (teacher):** The long flat piece, because its flat shape makes a floor and the round one rolls. Any piece, or the round piece, is error E (nearest letter).

## Day 41 exit card: two bookends

**Picture:** Two cards. Card 1: a stack of toy books with a stiff card strip folded into an L, standing straight beside it. Card 2: the same books with a floppy paper strip folded into an L, drooping, the books leaning. Under them, two circles: yes, no.
**Say:** "Pip says the floppy strip holds the books up as well as the stiff one. Is Pip right? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No, with the words that the stiff card keeps its shape and the paper droops. Yes is error E.

## Day 42 exit cards: a rain bucket

**Picture:** Three cards laid left to right and labelled first, then, last. First: a rain bucket with liquid water, marked with a line at the top of the water. Then: the same bucket with the water frozen solid. Last: the same bucket with liquid water again, a wavy line for warm.
**Say:** "Touch the two pictures that show the water went back."
**Key (teacher):** The first and the last, either liquid picture at either end, with the words that the water was warmed and looked at again. The first two is error D.
