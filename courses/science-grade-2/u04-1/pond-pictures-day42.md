# Pond pictures (day 42)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

New pond pictures for the water retrieval. Two cards.

**Card A:** A pond in cold weather covered by a flat sheet of ice, a fallen log at its edge, labeled cold.
**Card B:** The same pond in warm weather, its water rippling, a duck swimming, labeled warm.

**Say:** "Mark the solid water and the liquid water, and show what tells you."
**Key (teacher):** Card A is solid water; the ice keeps its shape. Card B is liquid water; it flows and ripples. If a child misses, tilt an adult-prepared sealed cup.
