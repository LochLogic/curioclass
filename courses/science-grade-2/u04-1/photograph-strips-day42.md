# Photograph strips (day 42)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

These stand in for the adult's photographs. The bath was cleared before children came in.

## Strip 1: chocolate

A piece of chocolate bar in a sealed clear bag, as on day 39.
**Frame 1, labelled hard:** the bag on a tray, the chocolate in a hard block.
**Frame 2, labelled warmed by the grown-up:** the bag in a bowl of warm water, the chocolate liquid. A grown-up in goggles at the bowl.
**Frame 3, labelled cooled:** the bag on a tray, the chocolate a hard flat sheet, a different outline.
**Say:** "What did the grown-up do to try to bring it back? What do you see?"
**Key (teacher):** The grown-up cooled it. It is hard again; the outline is different, and the outline is not the state (error B).

## Strip 2: water

A plastic cup of water with a line drawn at the top of the water, in three frames marked day 1, day 2 and day 3.
**Frame 1, day 1, liquid:** the cup of liquid water.
**Frame 2, day 2, frozen:** the cup in a freezer, the water a solid block. Only the grown-up uses the freezer.
**Frame 3, day 3, warmed:** the cup on a table, the water liquid again.
**Say:** "What was done to change the temperature back? What do you see?"
**Key (teacher):** The cup was warmed, then looked at again. The water is liquid again, so the change went back (the third look, error D).
