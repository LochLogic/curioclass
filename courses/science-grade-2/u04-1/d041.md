# Day 41: Can another set make different things?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put six rounded card shapes and a before-and-after sheet at each child's place, a stiff and a floppy strip on each group's table. Keep the day 40 model record and sketch in view. Choose Today's six for the record sheet with a heard column. Key: key-stiff-floppy-strips.

### Safety and set-up

Adults have rounded the card corners. Shapes stay on the table, and are counted back to six at the end.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 40's exit showed who said any piece does the same job (error F) or that a new arrangement needs a new piece (error A). Hold up the flat and the round piece. **Ask.** Which makes a floor? Expect: the flat piece; the round one rolls. For error A, string from each castle block to its place in the boat. If over a third said F, everyone does this; otherwise a small group at your table for 2 minutes while the rest start Retrieval. Then hold up the stiff and floppy strips, each with a card square taped on top. **Ask.** Which stays standing, and why? Expect: the stiff strip; it keeps its shape (error E).

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Partners say their day 40 sentence, "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces," 45 seconds each way (1.5 minutes). Then each tells what one piece's shape did, 45 seconds each way (1.5 minutes).

#### 4 min. Stiff and floppy

Each group has one stiff card strip and one floppy paper strip. The five children try both, 15 seconds each strip (5 x 2 x 15 s = 150 seconds). Use unit 3's words: stiff, bends easily.

#### 10 min. The card set

Each child alone. Build any first whole from the six card shapes and draw it as Before (3 minutes). Take it apart and build a different whole (3). Draw it as After (2). Draw a line from each old piece to its place (2). Mark the three look-fors from the sheets: names both wholes; shows the same pieces by lines; names a piece and a use. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 6 min. Second-set account

Pairs tell the account, "same pieces, a different whole, this shape does ___, the stiff card ___," 1.5 minutes each child (3 minutes). Today's six say it to you, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Children may finish the second frame on the sheet.

#### 4 min. Revise the parts model

Put the card set beside the block set on the class sketch. **Ask.** What can each set do, and not do? Expect: blocks stack high; flat card makes a floor or a wall; a shape suits a use, and so does a stiff card.

#### 3 min. Pack up

Count the shapes back to six, then into the tray.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the exit card and read [[s04-X41]] once; all point to yes or no (1 minute). Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the second strip: can another set make different objects? Yes, and the set matters.

### Other rooms

No card: use six pieces of packaging cut in rounded shapes. No printer: draw the sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** Six cut shapes from a cereal box.

**Skip.** The strips.

- **10 min. Build.** Build a whole from six card shapes, draw it, take it apart and build a different whole. Draw it.
- **8 min. Match.** Draw a line from each old piece to its place.
  **Say.** "Same pieces, a different whole. This shape does ___." Expect: the same six, and a use for one shape.
- **7 min. Strips.** Hold up a stiff and a floppy strip of paper.
  **Say.** "Which keeps its shape?" Expect: the stiff one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X41]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the strips try-out; never cut the card set or the exit. 

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, shape, use, stiff, bends easily. Frame: "Same pieces, a different whole. This shape does ___." Read captions aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the floppy strip holds as well (error E); day 42 repairs it with the strips again. Day 42 looks at photographs and sealed cups of water and chocolate, warmed and cooled.
