# Day 39: Can we change it back?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check egg, milk, soy and nut allergies first; any child may use photographs only. Put large blocks, six to a group, and the arrow sheet with cut-apart hook pictures on each table. Set the sealed chocolate and cooked-egg displays on a tray at the front. Hang paper for the sketch, the change-arrow model and the question board. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Sealed things stay sealed; nothing is eaten. Blocks stay on the table. Ask no child what is eaten at home.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Day 38 ended unit 3; today starts unit 4. Say the retrieval cards are invented. Show each; all hold up one finger for the left picture or two for the right (1 minute). Today's six say why, one card each, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes): light shines on the toy; the leaves are alike, the size differs; solid water keeps its shape, liquid water flows. A miss: model "I saw ___, so ___" on a second picture (1 minute).

#### 6 min. The hook

Build a castle of six blocks in view, take it apart and build a boat. Show the hook pictures and point to the sealed displays. Read Pip's line: "Our block castle became a boat, and melted chocolate hardened again, but a cooked egg never runs again." **Ask.** Which changes can be undone? Take guesses only.

#### 7 min. Take it apart

Six groups of five, all at once. Each builds anything, takes it apart and builds something different. Each child points from one block in the first build to the same block in the second, 15 seconds each (5 x 15 s = 75 seconds). That line from old to new is matching: it counts the pieces, no number said. Visit each group. **Ask.** What stayed the same? Expect: the six blocks. A new arrangement is not new pieces (error A).

#### 5 min. Parts and whole

Each child draws the two builds and six pieces on the parts page (4 minutes). Start the class parts-and-whole sketch from three pages (1 minute).

#### 8 min. Three changes

Each group lays the before and after pictures in the arrow sheet, draws an arrow in each row and circles "goes back" or "stays changed" (3 minutes). Each child says, as in grade 1, one "I think ___, because I saw ___" to the group, 20 seconds each (5 x 20 s, under 2 minutes). Today's six say theirs to the class, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Pip says: "The egg will go back if we cool it." Do not answer; write it on the board.

#### 3 min. Question board

Read five strips, each with its day: Where did the castle pieces go (day 40)? Can another set make different objects (41)? Does changing the temperature back restore things (42)? Does that always work (43)? What evidence settles a disagreement (44)? Children place their notices under one.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read [[s04-X39]] once; all point to yes or no (1 minute). Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack up

Blocks go back to the tray, counted to six.

### Other rooms

No blocks: six boxes or cups. No printer: draw the pages.

### At home

**Swap.** Six cups or spoons for blocks.

**Skip.** The board and arrow sheet.

- **10 min. Build.** Build anything from six objects, take it apart, build something different.
  **Say.** "What stayed the same?" Expect: the same six things.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws both builds and the six pieces.
- **5 min. Guess.** Name a change, such as ice melting or bread toasting.
  **Say.** "Will it go back? What did you see?" Any guess is fine.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X39]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two changes on the arrow sheet; never cut the exit. Longer: a third whole, matched.

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, same, different, take apart, change, back. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Read captions aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a new arrangement needs new pieces (error A); day 40 opens with a string from each castle block to the boat. Day 40 rebuilds six blocks as a new whole, drawn before and after.
