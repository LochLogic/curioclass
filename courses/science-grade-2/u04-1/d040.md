# Day 40: Where did the castle pieces go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put six large blocks and a before-and-after sheet at each child's place. Keep a spare six for the model. Hang the day 39 sketch and the question board in view. Choose Today's six for the record sheet with a heard column. Key: key-hook-pictures-day39.

### Safety and set-up

Large blocks only, kept on the table below faces. Blocks are counted back to six at the end.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 39's exit showed who said a new arrangement needs new pieces (error A). If more than a third of the class said it, do this with everyone: show the castle and boat pictures and lay short string, never near necks, from each block to its place in the boat. Otherwise a small group at your table for 2 minutes, while the rest start the Retrieval.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

**Ask.** What was the castle made of? What was the boat made of? Pairs point at the day 39 sketch and say it, 45 seconds each way (1.5 minutes), then the Ask (1.5 minutes). Expect: six blocks, the same six.

#### 4 min. A modeled record

With your spare six, draw a first whole and say its name, take it apart, build a different whole, draw it, draw a line from each old piece to its place in the new whole, and say: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Pin the record on the wall. Matching is counting by lines.

#### 12 min. My own take-apart

Each child alone. Build any first whole from the six and draw it as Before (2 minutes). Take it apart (1). Build a different whole (3). Draw it as After (2). Draw a line from each old piece to its place (2). Check lines with a neighbor (2). While they work, walk round. After class, mark one look-for on the record sheet for Today's six from the collected sheets: shows the same pieces by lines. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. A piece and its use

Shape and job, as in grade 1 and unit 3. Pairs pick one piece in the new whole and tell a partner its job, 1 minute each way (2 minutes). Today's six say it to you, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). **Ask.** What does the flat piece do that the round one cannot? Expect: its job is a floor; the round one rolls.

#### 4 min. One sentence

Partners say the sentence to each other first (1 minute). Then each child writes or has scribed on the sheet: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." (3 minutes).

#### 2 min. Pack up

Count the blocks back to six on the table, then into the tray.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the exit card. Read [[s04-X40a]]; all touch the piece (1 minute). Read [[s04-X40b]]; all touch the piece (1 minute). Today's six say why for the second, 20 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the first strip on the question board: where did the castle pieces go? Into the boat.

### Other rooms

No blocks: six boxes, cups or card squares. No printer: draw the sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** Six cups or spoons for blocks.

**Skip.** The sentence.

- **10 min. Build.** Build a whole from six objects and draw it. Take it apart and build a different whole. Draw it.
- **10 min. Match.** Draw a line from each old piece to its place.
  **Say.** "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Expect: the same six, in a new arrangement.
- **5 min. Use.** Pick one piece and say what its shape does.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X40a]] and [[s04-X40b]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the neighbor check; never cut the matching or the exit.

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, same, different, line, shape, use. Frame: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Read captions aloud; a scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a different whole needs new pieces (error A) and who says any piece does the same job (error F). Day 41 opens with the flat and the round piece held up and a stiff and a floppy strip held up. It takes six card shapes apart and rebuilds them as a different whole.
