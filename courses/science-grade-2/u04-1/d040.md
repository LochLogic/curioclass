# Day 40: Where did the castle pieces go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put six large blocks and a before-and-after sheet at each child's place. Keep a spare six for the model. Hang the day 39 sketch and the question board where all can see them. Choose Today's six for the record sheet with a heard column.

### Safety and set-up

Large blocks only, kept on the table below faces. Blocks are counted back to six at the end.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 39's exit showed who said a new arrangement needs new pieces (error A). If more than a third of the class said it, do this with everyone: show the castle and boat pictures and string from each block to its place in the boat. Otherwise pass table by table, one minute each, with the same pictures.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

**Ask.** What was the castle made of? What was the boat made of? Pairs point at the day 39 sketch and say it, 1.5 minutes each child. Expect: six blocks, the same six.

#### 4 min. A modelled record

With your spare six, draw a first whole and say its name, take it apart, build a different whole, draw it, draw a line from each old piece to its place in the new whole, and say: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Pin the record on the wall. Matching is counting by lines.

#### 12 min. My own take-apart

Each child alone. Build any first whole from the six and draw it as Before (2 minutes). Take it apart (1). Build a different whole (3). Draw it as After (2). Draw a line from each old piece to its place (2). Check lines with a neighbour (2). While they work, mark three look-fors on the record sheet from the sheets: names both wholes; shows the same pieces by lines; names a piece and a use. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. A piece and its use

Pairs pick one piece in the new whole and tell a partner what its shape does, 1.5 minutes each child (3 minutes). Today's six say it to you, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). **Ask.** What does the flat piece do that the round one cannot? Expect: it makes a floor; the round one rolls.

#### 4 min. One sentence

Partners say the sentence to each other first (1 minute). Then each child writes or has scribed on the sheet: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." (3 minutes).

#### 3 min. Pack up

Count the blocks back to six on the table, then into the tray.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the exit card and read [[s04-X40]] once; all touch the piece (1 minute). Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the first strip on the question board: where did the castle pieces go? Into the boat.

### Other rooms

No blocks: six boxes, cups or card squares. No printer: draw the sheet in two boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** Six cups or spoons for blocks.

**Skip.** The sentence.

- **10 min. Build.** Build a whole from six objects and draw it. Take it apart and build a different whole. Draw it.
- **10 min. Match.** Draw a line from each old piece to its place.
  **Say.** "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Expect: the same six, in a new arrangement.
- **5 min. Use.** Pick one piece and say what its shape does.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X40]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the neighbour check; never cut the matching or the exit. Longer: build a third whole and match again.

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, same, different, line, shape, use. Frame: "My ___ became a ___. I used the same pieces." Read captions aloud; a scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says any piece does the same job (error E, the nearest letter). Day 41 opens with a stiff and a floppy strip held up as a sign. It takes six card shapes apart and rebuilds them as a different whole.
