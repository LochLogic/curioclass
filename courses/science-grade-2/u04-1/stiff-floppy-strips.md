# Stiff and floppy strips (days 41, 42 and 47)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two pairs, for the repair of error E. Each strip is held up as a sign: a small card square is taped to the top of the strip and the strip stands on a table.

**Pair 1:** a stiff card strip standing straight with its sign up, and a floppy paper strip drooping with its sign leaning over.
**Pair 2:** a thick cardboard strip standing straight with its sign up, and a thin ribbon drooping with its sign flat on the table.
**Say:** "Which one stays standing? What is it about the strip?"
