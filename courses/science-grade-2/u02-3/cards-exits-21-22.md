# Exit cards (days 21 and 22)

Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture. Cards stay in the envelope until the exit.

## Day 21 exit card: the tall tubes

**Picture:** Three cards in a row. Card 1: a row of tall cardboard tubes standing in sand, drawn large. Card 2: four bars from matched fan tests, one square for each centimeter, labelled tubes and flat board: tubes 6 and 7, flat board 3 and 3. Card 3: a close picture of the tubes, tidy and bright.

**Say:** "Pip says the tall tubes will protect best because they are tallest. Which card decides? Point."

**Key (teacher):** Card 2, the matched fan-test bars. Card 3 or card 1, because the tubes look strong, is error C.

## Day 22 exit card: hedge and fence

**Picture:** Two bar cards for the same dune strip, one square for each centimeter. Hedge H: lost 1 then 4, path used 9. Fence F: lost 3 then 3, path used 2. A line across the lost bars at 5 and a line across the path bars at 6, each labelled limit. Under them, two circles: H, F.

**Say:** "The limits are: lose at most 5, and use at most 6 of the path. Which one meets both limits? Point."

**Key (teacher):** Fence F: 3 and 3 are at or under 5, and 2 is at or under 6. Hedge H, because it lost least at first, is error D: its 9 of path is over the limit of 6.
