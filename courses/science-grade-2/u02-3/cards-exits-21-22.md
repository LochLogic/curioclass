# Exit cards (days 21 and 22)

Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture. Cards stay in the envelope until the exit.

## Day 21 exit card: the tall tubes

**Picture:** Three cards in a row. Card 1: a row of tall cardboard tubes standing in sand, drawn large. Card 2: four bars from matched fan tests, one square for each centimeter, labeled tubes and flat board: tubes 6 and 7, flat board 3 and 3. Card 3: a close picture of the tubes, tidy and bright.

**Say:** "Pip says the tall tubes protect best. Which lost less sand, the tubes or the flat board? Point to a card and say the numbers."

## Day 22 exit card: hedge and fence

**Picture:** Two bar cards for the same dune strip, one square for each centimeter. Hedge H: lost 1 then 4, path used 9. Fence F: lost 3 then 3, path used 2. A line across the lost bars at 5 and a line across the path bars at 6, each labeled limit. Under them, two circles: H, F, to point to after the answer is said.

**Say:** "The limits are: lose at most 5, and use at most 6 of the path. Which numbers show that one of them meets both limits?"
