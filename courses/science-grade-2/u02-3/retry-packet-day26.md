# Day 26 retry packet

A fresh packet for children who still need a part. Read the directions once and mark one component at a time. Print "invented for practice" on each record. No people in any picture.

## Four records

- Cliff record 1: a cliff with a block gone, words: it came away in one storm.
- Cliff record 2: a second cliff with a block gone, words: it came away in one storm.
- Smooth record 1: a rocky surface, rough then smooth, words: it wore away little by little.
- Smooth record 2: a second rocky surface, rough then smooth, words: it wore away little by little.

## Water bars (soil lost in centimeters, first run and second run)

- Barrier R: 7 and 8, path used 9.
- Barrier S: 10 and 11, path used 1.
- Barrier T: 12 and 13, path used 11.

Limits: lose at most 9, and use at most 10 of the path.

## Wind bars

- Barrier X: 2 and 8.
- Barrier Y: 5 and 5.

Limit: lose at most 6 in each run.

## Key (teacher)

The two cliff records are the fast change, one storm. The two smooth records are the slow change, little by little. Barrier R meets both limits: 7 and 8 are 9 or less, and 9 is 10 or less. R does poorly on path: it uses 9 of the 10 it is allowed. For wind, choose Y: both runs, 5 and 5, are at or under 6, while X's second run, 8, is over. Y does poorly because it loses more than X in the first run, 5 against 2.
