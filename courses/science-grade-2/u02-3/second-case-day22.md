# A second soil tray (day 22)

Six minutes of second-case reasoning. Print "invented for practice" under each card.

## Two screens for a new tray

**Picture:** A new tray of the same soil, with the same wetness and the same fan place. Beside it a reed screen and a board panel, each with two bars from matched fan tests. Reed screen: 2 and 3, path 4. Board panel: 4 and 4, path 1.

Two limits: lose at most 4 in a run, and use at most 3 of the path.

**Say (teacher):** "Does the choice from our own trays carry over to this tray? Mark each barrier against both limits. Which meets both?"

**Key (teacher):** The reed screen loses 2 and 3, at or under 4, but uses 4 of the path, over the limit of 3, so it fails the path limit. The board panel loses 4 and 4, at the limit, and uses 1, so it meets both limits. Choose the board panel.
