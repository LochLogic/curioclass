# Day 23 packet: four records

A reserved packet printed only on day 23. Read the directions once. Children read only labels and the key. Print "invented for practice" on each record. No people in any picture.

## Record A (a picture pair)

**Picture:** A flat shore with a few rocks, marked "just before", and the same shore with a mound of dark new rock added, marked "just after".

## Record B (a separate account)

**Words:** The melted rock spread and hardened during the eruption.

## Record C (a picture pair)

**Picture:** A different shore. Older: the edge is near a row of stakes. Later: the edge is farther inland and the stakes stand in the water.

## Record D (a separate account)

**Words:** Repeated waves carried small pieces away, little by little.

## Tasks

1. Use A and B to explain a fast land change.
2. Use C and D to explain a slow land change.
3. Revise your change model so that it shows the fast route and the slow route, each with the records that support it.

## Key (teacher)

Task 1: A with B show new land added suddenly: the shore is flat in the picture before and the mound is there after, and the words say during the eruption. Task 2: C with D show a slow change: the edge is farther inland in the later view, and the words say little by little. Task 3: a model with a fast arrow labelled A and B, a slow arrow labelled C and D, and source labels on each. Look-fors are on the record sheet.
