# Day 26: Fix it, try again, and answer the path question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one next step on each day 23, 24 and 25 record, for one component. Sort the papers into three piles: error A with error B; error C with error D, adding the day 22 exit slips with error D; error E. Pin the class change model and the design-cycle chart beside a clean sheet headed "Why our path became a ditch". Draw picture directions. Keys: key-repair-set-day26, key-retry-packet-day26.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and read feedback

Hand back the records from day 23's items, day 24's items and day 25's assessment; children read or hear their next step. Hear six children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "My next step is ___." Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Repair by letter groups

Each group sits with you 4 minutes in turn (12), then 3 to move. The others follow the picture directions and fix one thing on their record. All cards are invented.

- **Error A with error B** (words-that-say-when card). **Ask.** Which words tell us if it was fast or slow? Expect: in one night, so fast; little by little, so slow.
- **Error C with error D** (which-card-decides card, then the at-under-over card). **Ask.** Which card decides? Expect: the bars from matched tests. Then mark one limit the chosen barrier misses.
- **Error E.** Lay the day 24 water list and wind list side by side. **Ask.** What do we compare with what? Expect: water with water, wind with wind; each test starts alike.

If many made one letter, it takes the first 5 minutes with the whole class and each group turn shrinks to 2.5 minutes.

#### 10 min. Retry

Say the packet is invented and unscored. Each child answers only the missed component, alone, one step at a time. Events: [[s02-X26a]]. Water barriers: [[s02-X26b]], then [[s02-X26c]]. Wind barriers: [[s02-X26d]], then [[s02-X26e]]. Children point, mark or say a few words. Record supported and independent evidence apart, on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Hear six rotating children (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Consensus

At the sheet; you draw, they tell. Join the revised change model and the design models.

**Ask.** Why has the bare path become a ditch, and how could we stop it? Expect: water ran down the bare slope and carried soil away; plants or a barrier slow the water, and each does well and poorly.

Draw solid arrows for what we saw and measured. Draw dotted arrows for what we think but did not measure. Answer the board card; leave one open.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on the board and read them aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a cliff with a block gone, a rough rock then a smooth one, and three barriers with two numbers each.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the record sheet.

- **5 min. Look back.** Read the next step on the records.
  **Say.** "What is your next step?" Expect: one fix from the records.
- **10 min. Fast and slow.**
  **Say.** "How do you know a change was fast?" Expect: the words say in one storm, and two records agree.
- **10 min. The barriers.**
  **Say.** "Which barrier meets both limits, and what does it do poorly?" Expect: the one under both lines; it may use much of the path.
- **5 min. The path.**
  **Say.** "Why did the path become a ditch?" Expect: water carried the soil down the bare slope.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X26a]], [[s02-X26b]], [[s02-X26c]], [[s02-X26d]] and [[s02-X26e]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one component, never the retry. Longer: a third barrier.

### Words and supports

Fast, slow, limit, meets, strength, weakness, dotted arrow. Frame: "I choose ___ because the bars show ___, and it is poor at ___." A scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow
