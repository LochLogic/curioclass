# Day 24 packet: matched tests and a hillside pair

A reserved packet printed only on day 24. Read the directions once. Children read only labels and the key. Print "invented for practice" on each record. No people in any picture.

## Water tests (soil lost in centimeters, first run and second run)

- Bare ground: 7 and 8
- Wall: 3 and 4
- Rock barrier: 4 and 5
- Rooted mat: 1 and 2

## Wind tests (soil lost in centimeters, first run and second run)

- Bare ground: 8 and 9
- Wall: 4 and 5
- Stick windbreak: 2 and 3

## Hillside pair

**Picture 1:** A bare hillside above a lane, with a deep gully down it and a fan of soil on the lane. Water arrows run down the gully.

**Picture 2:** The same hillside with a rooted mat across the slope and a row of stones at the foot. A small gully is left at one side and a wall stands at the lane edge; water arrows go round the end of the wall.

## Tasks

1. Compare the water solutions.
2. Compare the wind solutions.
3. Use arrows on your model to show how protection changes the land and the land around it, including the water that runs round the end of the wall.

## Key (teacher)

Task 1: all three lose less than bare ground; the wall loses less than the rock barrier, 3 and 4 against 4 and 5; the rooted mat loses least of all, 1 and 2. Task 2: both lose less than bare ground; the stick windbreak loses less than the wall, 2 and 3 against 4 and 5. Task 3: arrows show less soil moving where a barrier or plants stand, and the water turned round the end of the wall goes on to wear land beside it. Look-fors are on the record sheet.
