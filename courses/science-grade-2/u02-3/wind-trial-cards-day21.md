# Six wind trials (day 21)

You run six trials with the teacher fan on side-shielded damp-sand trays, two each for bare sand, the stick windbreak and the card wall. Use the cards below as the matched results if no fan is available, or as the model for your own record. Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture.

## Result cards

Bars from matched fan tests, one square for each centimeter of sand lost, first run and second run:

- Bare sand: 9 and 8.
- Stick windbreak: 3 and 4.
- Card wall: 2 and 6.

Path width used, in whole centimeters: stick windbreak 3, card wall 5.

## Close-up card

**Picture:** A close picture of the stick windbreak with sand piled against the windward side and a thin line of sand behind it.

**Say (teacher):** "Read each strip against the fixed ruler. Say one thing each barrier did well. Say one thing each did poorly."

**Key (teacher):** Both lose less than bare sand. The stick windbreak lost 3 and 4, steady. The card wall lost 2 then 6: it did very well once and poorly the second time, and it takes up more path, 5. Accept any strength or weakness the numbers support.
