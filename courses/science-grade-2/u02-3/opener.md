# Section 2.3 opener: which protection fits the problem?

## What the section does

Six days, 21 to 26. Children read wind-trial results that you run, compare two designs against two limits, and then show what they have learned. Day 21 you run six wind trials with a fan while children watch, record and read the strips. Day 22 asks whether the choice carries over to a second soil tray. Days 23, 24 and 25 are the end of the unit: three short observation tasks done from a packet. Day 26 returns the feedback, repairs by letter group, and runs a fresh retry of only the parts a child has not yet shown. Days 21 and 22 end with an exit; days 23 to 25 do not.

## How the days run

Days 21, 22 and 26 open with five minutes of return and repair. Days 23 to 25 open with directions, then the packet. Children never run the fan; you do. Read each stem aloud, one ask at a time. Mark with one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. On days 23 to 25 score each look-for 2, 1 or 0 on the record sheet.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A fair comparison keeps everything equal except the design.**
- **A limit is the most we allow.** A design that is at or under the line meets that limit.
- **Every design has a strength and a weakness.** Name both, using the numbers.
- **A tray shows how soil moves, not how long a real change takes.**
- **Protection changes the land around it.** Water that cannot go through a wall goes round the end.

## Safety

You run the fan. Children wear eye protection near the trays and stand back from the fan. Keep a cloth at hand for spills, wash hands after handling sand or soil, and tip soil into a lined bin, never a drain.
