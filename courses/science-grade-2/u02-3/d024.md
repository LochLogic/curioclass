# Day 24: Water, wind and a hillside

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the wall covered and children seated apart. Print the packet and the student page. Set the record sheet with six columns, three for the water item, two for the wind item and one for the arrows. Day 23 papers are read before day 26: no reteach today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Nothing to wear, nothing to clean.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say the packet is invented for practice. Read the prompt neutrally, one step at a time, with a pause after each. Step 1: look at the water tests; read each once. Step 2: [[s02-E4]]. Step 3: look at the wind tests; read each once. Step 4: [[s02-E5]]. Step 5: [[s02-E6]]. Name no test as the best.

#### 20 min. The packet

Each child works alone on their own paper: compares the water tests, compares the wind tests, and draws arrows on blank model paper. No group talks. You may read a line again on request, once. A scribe or an oral answer is allowed for any child whose plan says so, 30 seconds each, six at most (3 minutes). Mark six look-fors on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only "shown" counts a point; record help apart from independent work.

1. Compares the wall with the rock barrier in both runs, 3 against 4 and 4 against 5.
2. Compares the rooted mat with the wall and the rock barrier: the mat lost least.
3. Says the water carried the loose soil.
4. Compares the stick windbreak with the wall in both runs, 2 against 4 and 3 against 5.
5. Says the wind carried the loose soil.
6. Draws arrows for less soil moving behind a barrier and for water turned round the end of the wall onto the land beside it.

#### 10 min. The hillside pair

Not scored. Pairs may talk. Show the two hillside pictures from the packet, a bare hillside and the same hillside with a rooted mat, stones and a wall. No dates are given.

**Ask.** Which feature of our model fits what you see? Expect: the mat and stones hold soil; water goes round the end of the wall.

**Ask.** What can the two pictures not tell us? Expect: how fast the land changed; they show no dates.

Hear six pairs (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Any pair may pass.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the tests as bars on the board and read them aloud; children write on plain paper. One adult: children work alone anyway.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: the numbers are in the questions. Draw a bare hillside, then the same hillside with a mat of plants, a row of stones and a wall.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the scribe.

- **8 min. Water.** Your child does [[s02-E4]] alone. Give no hint.
- **8 min. Wind.** Your child does [[s02-E5]] alone.
- **8 min. The hillside.** Look at the two drawings.
  **Say.** "What can the pictures not tell us?" Expect: how fast the land changed; they show no dates.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-E6]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the check is not shortened. Longer: none; nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Water, wind, wall, mat, windbreak, arrow. Read aloud, large print, a scribe, an oral answer or pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 is not scored in class. Each child gets their record back on day 26 with one written next step. Error C goes to the design group and error E to its own group on day 26. Day 25 is the last observation: two screens tested for one problem.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
