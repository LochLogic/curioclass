# Day 23: Fast and slow on a new shore

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the wall, the class change model included. Seat children so each sees only their own paper. Print the packet, the anchor path sheet and the student page. Set the record sheet with six columns, two for each item below. Day 22's slips and today's papers are read before day 26: no reteach, no hints.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Nothing to wear, nothing to clean.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say the packet is invented for practice. Read the whole prompt neutrally, one step at a time, with a pause after each. Step 1: look at records A and B; read each once. Step 2: [[s02-E1]]. Step 3: look at records C and D; read each once. Step 4: [[s02-E2]]. Step 5: [[s02-E3]]. Name no record as the right one.

#### 20 min. The packet

Each child works alone: reads or listens to the four records, finishes the two frames on the student page, and revises their own change model on blank paper with source labels and arrows. No group talks. You may read a line again on request, once. A scribe or an oral answer is allowed for any child whose plan says so, 30 seconds each, six at most (3 minutes). Mark six look-fors on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only "shown" counts a point; record help apart from independent work.

1. Cites record A: the flat shore before and the mound after.
2. Corroborates with record B: the words during the eruption.
3. Cites record C: the edge farther inland in the later view.
4. Corroborates with record D: the words little by little.
5. Explains each speed by what happened to the land, a mound added and an edge worn back, not by how the shore looks today.
6. Revises the model with a fast route labelled A and B and a slow route labelled C and D.

#### 10 min. Revise the anchor

Not scored. Hand out the anchor path sheet. Each child uses their own earlier results to draw one protection for the worn path, label what it is made of, and draw an arrow for the water. Teach nothing; read the two lines on the sheet once.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four records on the board and read them aloud; children write on plain paper. One adult: children work alone anyway.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a flat shore, then the same shore with a mound; draw a shore edge near stakes, then farther back with the stakes in water. Write "The melted rock spread and hardened" and "Waves carried small pieces away little by little" on two cards.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the scribe.

- **8 min. Fast.** Read records A and B aloud. Your child does [[s02-E1]] alone. Give no hint about which record.
  **Say.** "Which records show a fast change, and how do you know?" Expect: A and B, a flat shore then a mound, and the words during the eruption.
- **8 min. Slow.** Read records C and D aloud. Your child does [[s02-E2]] alone.
  **Say.** "Which show a slow change?" Expect: C and D, the edge farther inland and the words little by little.
- **8 min. The path.** Your child draws one protection for a worn path and an arrow for the water.
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-E3]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the check is not shortened. Longer: none; nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Record, fast, slow, shore, before, after. Read aloud, large print, a scribe, an oral answer or pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 is not scored in class. Each child gets their record back on day 26 with one written next step for one component, and error A and error B go to the day 26 groups. Day 24 is the next observation: matched water and wind tests and a hillside pair.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
