# The river story

A made-up story, one page, for day 6. It is pleasant and cannot tell where water is. Say aloud that it is a story somebody made up.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A boy with light brown skin sits on a log by a wide river at sunset, with a small dog beside him. Birds fly over. No map marks.

**Read:** Once there was a boy and a dog who loved a lovely river. Every evening the river sang to them. They said it was the nicest river in the world.
