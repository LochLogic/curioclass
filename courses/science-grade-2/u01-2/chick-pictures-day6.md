# Chick pictures

Retrieval of two-source practice from Grade 1. Two captioned pictures, as before. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A round yellow chick standing in grass beside an egg shell. Caption below: This chick hatched today.

**Read:** This chick hatched today.

## Page 2

**Picture:** A hen sitting on a nest of straw. Caption below: A hen keeps her eggs warm.

**Read:** A hen keeps her eggs warm.
