# Water places

A keyed map with captions for day 6, read aloud by the teacher. Children read only the labels and key. Print "invented for practice" beneath the map.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Only the key and labels carry words.

## The map

**Picture:** A map of an imagined place seen from above. Hills are three rounded humps at the top. A bending river runs from the hills down the middle of the page to the ocean along the bottom edge. A round lake sits to the left of the river. A small round pond sits on the right of the river, near the ocean. Each water place has a filled dot beside it. A key box in the corner reads: filled dot = liquid water.

**Read:** Caption 1: The river runs from the hills to the ocean. Caption 2: The lake lies in a low place left of the river. Caption 3: A small pond lies near the ocean, right of the river. All of these are liquid water.
