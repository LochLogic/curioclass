# Frozen water places

A keyed map with captions for day 7, read aloud by the teacher. Children read only labels and key. Print "invented for practice" beneath the map.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names.

## The map

**Picture:** A map of an imagined mountain place seen from above. Three tall peaks at the top, drawn as pointed shapes. White patches on the peaks are snow. A wide band down one valley between two peaks is a sheet of ice. A frozen pond near the foot of the valley is a round shape with cracks. Each frozen place has an open dot beside it. A key box in the corner reads: open dot = solid water.

**Read:** Caption 1: Snow lies on the high peaks. Caption 2: A sheet of ice fills the high valley. Caption 3: A small pond near the foot of the valley is frozen. Snow and ice are solid water.

## Key (teacher)

The two pages together show liquid water and solid water in imagined places. Match each open dot to the key. Do not give amounts or sizes.

## Ice-water pictures (if a cup fails)

**Picture:** Two small pictures side by side, each a sealed clear cup on a tray. Cup 1 holds ice pieces and carries an open dot. Cup 2 holds flat liquid water and carries a filled dot. Key box: filled dot = liquid water, open dot = solid water. Print "invented for practice" beneath.

**Read:** Cup 1 holds ice. Cup 2 holds liquid water. Ice is solid water.
