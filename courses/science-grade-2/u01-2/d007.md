# Day 7: Water that flows and water that is solid

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, check each seal. Just before class, put fresh ice in one cup of each pair. Set the sealed ice and water cups in trays, with cloths and goggles, at the tables. Hang the chart. Choose today's six. You speak for Pip. Keys: key-frozen-water-places, key-cards-exits-5-7, key-water-places.

### Safety and set-up

Sealed ice and water cups only: held briefly, never opened, never tasted. Wipe spills at once. Goggles on during handling. The adult hands out and collects the trays and empties the cups into a tray, not a drain. A child who dislikes cold holds the cup with a cloth or uses the pictures.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 6 exit and written task (the marked map) with one next step each. Teach the one error most children made, error A first: each pair holds up the page that can answer and points to its caption or key.

#### 3 min. Recall

Reread column 1 and its source label, Water places, as you point.

#### 5 min. Hold the cups

Goggles on first (1 minute). Each child holds the ice cup, then the water cup, 20 seconds each (5 x 2 x 20 s, about 3.5 minutes), all groups at once. Each group does [[s01-P7-1]]. Children wipe the condensation. If a cup leaks or the ice has melted, use the ice-water pictures, said to be invented.

#### 2 min. Talk

Pip says "the ice cup has no water in it." This is error C. Children do [[s01-P7-2]], testing Pip against what they held. Expect: ice is solid water; the other cup holds liquid water.

#### 7 min. Second source

Read Frozen water places aloud and point to the key. Write Frozen water places at the top of column 2. Pairs find the snow and the ice, and all do [[s01-P7-3]], pointing to the dot and the key. Add each fact to column 2 with its source label.

**Ask.** What does the second page tell us that the first did not? Expect: snow, ice and a frozen pond. A second page adds places the first lacks.

#### 8 min. Combine

Pairs mark every place from both pages on one chart under liquid and solid, with page names; each child does [[s01-P7-4]], at least four marks. Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "Show me one mark, its page and what it says." Mark who was heard. Error B card: read the dot against the key before marking.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Condensation wiped, goggles wiped and bagged, cups emptied.

#### 3 min. Exit E7

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-5-7. Every child does [[s01-X7]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Question board

Reread the board: where water is, and what it can be.

### Other rooms

No cups: use the ice-water pictures, or two closed plastic containers held briefly by an adult. One projector: show one page at a time, then the chart.

### At home

**Swap.** No sealed cups: two closed plastic containers, never glass, one with ice and one with water, held briefly by both of you, never opened or tasted. Or draw them. No page: draw a mountain with snow and a river.

**Skip.** The pairs and the chart.

- **6 min. Hold.** Your child does [[s01-P7-1]].
  **Say.** "Which is solid, and which is liquid?" Expect: ice solid, water liquid.
- **4 min. Pip.** Your child does [[s01-P7-2]].
  **Say.** "Pip says the ice has no water in it. What do you say?" Expect: ice is water that is solid.
- **8 min. Second page.** Your child does [[s01-P7-3]].
- **7 min. Mark.** Your child does [[s01-P7-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X7]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hold the cups once each. Never cut the cups, the key or the exit. Longer: add a third place from a made-up caption and say which page gave it.

### Words and supports

Liquid: water that flows. Solid: water that keeps its shape, like ice and snow. Frame: "___ is liquid (solid) because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who chooses claim Y (error C) and who marks from one page only (error F). Day 8 is a check day with no reteach, so the slips are read before day 10, where those children join a letter group. Day 8 uses new pages and no help.
