# Day 8: Where is the water in Pine Coast?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover everything on the wall, the day 7 chart included. Seat children so each sees only their own paper. Print the packet, chart and student page. Key: key-packet-day8. Set up the record sheet with six columns, one for each component below. The day 7 exit slips are read before day 10, not today: no reteach, no hints.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say the packet is invented for practice. Say once: "A record is a page that tells what someone saw." Read the prompt neutrally, one step at a time, pausing after each: "Where is water in Pine Coast, and in what states?" Step 1: look at records A, B and C; read each caption once. Step 2: choose the records that answer, [[s01-E1]]. Step 3: fill in the chart, [[s01-E2]]. Step 4: say whether the solid water is higher or lower than the liquid water, [[s01-E3]]. Name no record as the right one.

#### 25 min. The packet

Each child works alone on their own paper: chooses records, fills in the chart, and finishes the frame "In Pine Coast the solid water is ___ and the liquid water is ___." No answer key and no hint are supplied (record A's own key box stays on the page and tells the dots of record B too), and there are no group talks. You may read a line again on request, once. A scribe or an oral answer is allowed for any child whose plan says so, 30 seconds each, six at most (3 minutes).

After class, mark the six components in the record sheet's six columns from the collected packets, on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only "shown" counts a point.

1. Chooses records A and B and leaves out C, because C is Other Cove.
2. Uses record A's key to read the ocean, the river and the ice.
3. Records the ocean, river, lake and pond in the places the records show.
4. Finds the ice in A and the snow in B, each with its record letter.
5. Marks each place liquid or solid correctly.
6. Says the solid water is higher and the liquid water lower, for Pine Coast only, not for every place.

#### 5 min. Check and collect

Check names and collect the packets. Score nothing in front of the class.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the records on the board and read them aloud; children write on plain paper. One adult: children work alone anyway.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a keyed map with a river, an ocean and ice, and two small picture logs, one of them from a different place.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the scribe.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read the prompt aloud one step at a time and wait. Your child does [[s01-E1]], [[s01-E2]] and [[s01-E3]] alone. Give no hint about which record.
- **5 min. Look together.** Read the chart together.
  **Say.** "Which records told about Pine Coast, and how do you know?" Expect: A and B, because C is a different place.
- **10 min. Where the water is.**
  **Say.** "Where is the liquid water, and where is the solid water?" Expect: the ocean, river, lake and pond are liquid; the ice and snow on the ridge are solid, in Pine Coast.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter or longer: none.

### Words and supports

Record (a page that tells what someone saw), chart, liquid, solid, ocean, river, lake, pond, ice, snow. Read aloud, large print, a scribe, an oral answer or pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 is not scored in class. Each child gets their record back before day 10, with one written next step for one component. The day 7 exit errors, error C and error F, and the day 8 letters go to the letter groups of day 10. Day 9 begins section 1.3 with a model check of a new bay.
