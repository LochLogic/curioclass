# Day 6: Which page tells where water is?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Rule the two-column chart large and write at the top: Where is water? Lay the river story and Water places side by side. Have the day 5 models and exits ready. Choose today's six. You speak for Pip, one line at a time. Keys: key-cards-exits-5-7, key-water-places.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Nothing to wear, nothing to clean.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 5 exit and written task (model and pattern frame), each with one next step. Teach error D first: draw the land beside the water on the shared Dry Ridge picture.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Show the two new chick pages, invented for practice, like the grade 1 robin chick pages. Read each caption. Everyone does [[s01-P6-1]] and points on your signal; any child may keep their arm down. A miss: point to each caption and say one fact from each.

#### 5 min. Choose a source

Read the river story aloud and say a person made it up. Read the Water places captions aloud and point to the key. A source is a page or picture that gives information; it is not a source of light or sound from grade 1. A caption says what a picture shows. A map key says what each dot means.

#### 4 min. Talk

Pairs do [[s01-P6-2]] aloud and point to a caption or the key. Pip takes the story: "it is nicer." This is error A.

**Ask.** Which page can tell where water is, and what shows it? Expect: the map, because its key and captions say where. A story can be nice and still not say where. Ask a child who drew the water spread over the meadow on day 5: "Which part of the meadow was lowest?"

#### 8 min. Gather facts

Write Water places at the top of column 1. Model caption 1: the river runs from the hills to the ocean, so write river and ocean with a filled dot. Pairs find the lake and the pond. All do [[s01-P6-3]], pointing to the dot, then the key. Fill the chart as they speak. Error B card: a partner reads the dot against the key first.

#### 8 min. Mark your page

Each child circles every water place on their own copy of the map and draws its label, [[s01-P6-4]]. Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "Show me a dot, and what the key says." Scribe the words; mark who was heard.

#### 3 min. Exit E6

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-5-7. Every child does [[s01-X6]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "Where else is water, including ice?"

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map on the board and have children copy the four water places. One projector: show the river story, then the map.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: draw a place with a river, a lake and a pond, and a key that says filled dot = liquid water. No story page: tell a short made-up story about a river.

**Skip.** The chick pages, the pairs and the chart.

- **5 min. Two pages.** Show the story and the map.
  **Say.** "Which page can tell where water is? How do you know?" Expect: the map, because its key and captions say where. Your child does [[s01-P6-2]].
- **8 min. Read the map.** Your child does [[s01-P6-3]].
  **Say.** "What does the key say that dot means?" Expect: liquid water.
- **12 min. Mark it.** Your child does [[s01-P6-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X6]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the chick pages. Never cut the key, the caption or the exit. Longer: a second place on another page.

### Words and supports

Source: a page or picture that gives information. Caption: words under a picture. Map key: says what each dot means. Pond: a small body of water. Frame: "This page tells where water is because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who picks the page that sounds nicer (error A) and who marks a dot without the key (error B). Day 7 opens with Return and repair of the day 6 exit, error A first, and error B gets a picture card in its combine part. Day 7 also rereads column 1 of the chart.
