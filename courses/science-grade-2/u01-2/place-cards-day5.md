# Stone Bay and Dry Ridge

Two place cards for day 5. Show both at once. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** Stone Bay: a rounded bay with a small sandy beach and a rocky point, a stream running between two low hills into the bay. A man with a fishing pole stands on the beach.

**Read:** In Stone Bay a stream runs between two hills into the bay.

## Page 2

**Picture:** Dry Ridge: a long flat-topped ridge with a few bushes. A shallow stream runs down between two gentle slopes to a long lake in a low place, with slopes rising on every side of the lake. There is no ocean on the page. A woman with gray braids walks along the ridge.

**Read:** On Dry Ridge a stream runs down to a lake. The lake lies in a low place with slopes all round it.
