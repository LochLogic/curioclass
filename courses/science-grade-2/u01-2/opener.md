# Section 1.2 opener: where is the water?

## What the section does

Four days, 5 to 8. Day 5 looks at a second place and checks whether the class model fits it. Day 6 asks which page can tell where water is, and the class reads a keyed map with captions. Day 7 adds a second page about frozen water and combines both on one chart. Day 8 is the check of the section: each child chooses records, fills in a chart and says a pattern, with no help from you.

## How the days run

Days 5 to 7 open with five minutes of return and repair (nine on day 6, which also retrieves two-source practice). Read each stem aloud, one ask at a time. Children mark, point, draw or say a few words. Day 8 is thirty-five minutes: directions, the packet, and collecting the work. Mark with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. On day 8 there is no help.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Liquid and solid water.** Ocean, river, lake and pond are liquid water. Ice and snow are solid water. The same water can be either.
- **A page that tells is not the same as a page that is pleasant.** A keyed map and its caption can answer "where is water." A story about a river cannot.
- **A pattern from two records is a claim about those places.** Children say "in this place" and never "everywhere."
- **Mark what you saw; mark a guess differently.** A dotted arrow means unobserved.

## Safety

Day 7 uses two sealed cups, one with ice and one with liquid water, on a tray. Never open or taste them. An adult checks the seals and brings fresh ice. Wipe the tray when the ice has melted.
