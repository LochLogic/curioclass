# Retrieval cards (day 16)

Print "invented for practice" under each card.

## Design cycle card

**Picture:** A circle of four small pictures with arrows: a large question mark for the problem, a pencil sketch, a built shape, a test. Caption: Problem, sketch, build, test.

**Say (teacher):** "We used a circle like this in unit 1. Which step comes before build?"

**Key (teacher):** Sketch.

## Daylight chart card

**Picture:** A chart with three columns for morning, noon and afternoon, and a row of suns of different heights. Caption: The sun is low in the morning and high at noon.

**Say (teacher):** "Point to the column where the sun is highest."

**Key (teacher):** Noon.

## Signal card

**Picture:** A flag on a pole and a lamp beside it. Caption: A signal sends a message without words.

**Say (teacher):** "Point to the picture that sends a message by light."

**Key (teacher):** The lamp.
