# Retrieval cards (day 16)

Print "invented for practice" under each card.

## Design cycle card

**Picture:** A circle of four small pictures with arrows: a large question mark for the problem, a pencil sketch, a built shape, a test. Caption: Problem, sketch, build, test.

**Say (teacher):** "We used a circle like this in grade 1. Which step comes before build?"

**Key (teacher):** Sketch.

## Daylight chart card

**Picture:** A chart of two bars of daylight hours, one short bar labeled day 1 and one long bar labeled day 2. No caption.

**Say (teacher):** "Point to the day with more daylight."

**Key (teacher):** Day 2, the longer bar.

## Signal card

**Picture:** A flag on a pole and a lamp beside it. Caption: A signal is a light or sound that goes across.

**Say (teacher):** "Point to the picture that sends a message by light."

**Key (teacher):** The lamp.
