# Exit cards, days 16 to 20

Each exit has a picture card every child marks, and a spoken stem read once. Say aloud that every card is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each card. None of these pictures is used in a hook or a lesson picture. No person is in any picture. Children point or circle; the teacher hears a rotating six.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so.

## Day 16 exit card: dry sand and damp sand

**Picture:** Two small trays drawn from the side on the same stack of two books, each with one cup beside it marked "one cup". The left tray is marked dry sand and the right tray damp sand. Under each tray a short bar of lost sand, one longer than the other. Under them, two circles: yes, no.

**Say:** "Pip poured the same cup on both and they lost different amounts. Does it matter that one was dry and one was damp? Point to yes or no."

**Picture, part b (item 16b):** A paper strip with a centimeter ruler beneath it. The first pencil mark is at 0 and the second pencil mark is at 4. Labelled sand edge before and after.

**Say (part b):** "The sand edge was at 0. After the pour it was at the second mark. How far did the edge move back? Write the number."

**Key (teacher):** Yes, with the words that the start must be the same. No, because the cup was the same, is error E. Part b: 4 centimeters. Reading the second mark without starting at 0, or giving a number that is not 4, is not yet seen (no letter).

## Day 17 exit card: the fence

**Picture:** Three cards in a row. Card 1: a tall wooden fence drawn large. Card 2: three bars from equal tray tests, labelled fence, hedge and mesh. Card 3: a close picture of the fence boards.

**Say:** "Pip picks the fence because it looks strongest. Which card does Pip need before choosing? Point."

**Key (teacher):** Card 2, the tray result bars. Card 1 or card 3, because they show the fence best, is error C.

## Day 18 exit card: wood chips

**Picture:** Three cards in a row. Card 1: a tray from above with wood chips spread across the whole path. Card 2: one short bar with the words lost the least. Card 3: a tidy picture of a heap of chips.

**Say:** "The chips lost the least sand, and Pip says they have no weakness. Point to the card that shows something the chips might do poorly."

**Key (teacher):** Card 1, with the words that the chips use up the path. Card 2, because it says lost the least, is error D. Card 3, because the chips look good, is error C.

## Day 19 exit card: mulch and gravel

**Picture:** A two-bar picture graph on squares, one square for each centimeter. A line across both bars at 7. The mulch bar is 5 squares tall and the gravel bar 9 squares tall, each labelled. Under it, two circles: mulch, gravel.

**Say:** "A limit is the most we allow. Which bar goes past the limit line? Point to it."

**Key (teacher):** The gravel bar. The mulch bar, or both bars, has no letter in the register: the teacher notes it and day 20 opens with error G.

## Day 20 exit card: two shapes for one garden strip

**Picture, part a:** Two small pictures of the same garden strip from above, each with wind arrows blowing at it. Picture 1: a curved screen with its middle toward the wind. Picture 2: a staggered row of posts, the second row standing in the gaps of the first. A blank box under each.

**Say, part a:** "Sketch both shapes. Draw an arrow for each to show how it turns the wind."

**Say, part b:** "How could we compare their protection fairly? Say it or point to a card." Cards: same ground and same fan place; different ground; the one that looks stronger.

**Key (teacher):** Part a: both shapes drawn, each with an arrow that bends around or slows at the shape; arrows straight through both have no letter, so note it and show day 21 an arrow bending. Part b: the same ground and fan place, then measure the retreat. The one that looks stronger is error C. A different ground or fan place is error E.
