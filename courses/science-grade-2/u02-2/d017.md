# Day 17: A wall and a row of rocks against a bare slope

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, set two trays a group to the slope card, with a taped line and an X. Tape the books under trays. Count stones. Draw the results chart. Choose two sixes: Two runs, exit. Keys: key-bare-strips-day16, key-cards-exits-16-20.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on before any cup is filled, until clean-up ends. Sand stays below faces. Stones stay out of mouths and are never thrown; an adult counts them back. Wipe spills. No goggles for all: only you pour; groups use the invented water records.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 16 exits, s02-X16 and s02-X16b. Error E: write "No, because the slope was the same, so the start does not matter." Lay two trays on four books and two, then a dry tray beside a damp one.

**Ask.** Can we compare these? Expect: no; reset both to the slope card, make the sand equally wet, then pour.

Groups reset their two trays to the slope card together: same books, same X.

#### 3 min. Roles and plan

Keep the same: slope, cup, pour place. Change one thing: the barrier. Watch one thing: retreat. Groups 1 to 3 build the card wall, groups 4 to 6 the rock row. Each group keeps a bare tray. Everyone takes a different job than day 16.

#### 4 min. Set up

Goggles on. Place the barrier across the path. Fill the cups to the line.

#### 8 min. Two runs

Two pourers pour the bare tray and the barrier tray at once. Mark the strips; read them with a ruler at 0; write the numbers. Reset both trays to the slope card; pour again. Hear one child per group say "bare ___, barrier ___" (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 3 min. Width

Path width used is how much of the path the barrier takes. Each group lays a strip across the barrier and reads the whole centimeter.

#### 4 min. Class chart

Recorders post their group's numbers, two at a time. For each barrier, order the three second-run numbers; the middle number is the class number.

Lay the day 16 strips beside the chart: equal trays differed that much. About the same is from kindergarten; here it means numbers 1 centimeter apart.

#### 5 min. Well and poorly

Show the card wall and stone row pictures. For each barrier the chart decides what it did well. Expect: the wall sends water round its ends, onto the land beside it; the rocks let water and soil through the gaps.

**Ask.** What did each barrier do well and poorly? Expect: a strength from the numbers, a weakness from the numbers or the picture.

Pairs tell each other; hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Trays without clean numbers: use the invented water records, said aloud to be invented.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Goggles wiped and bagged. Hands washed in two waves at two sinks. An adult counts the stones and tips the sand into the lined bin.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read [[s02-X17]]; everyone says both numbers. Hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No trays: use the invented water records and the result bar card. No printer: draw the cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** One tin: pour it bare, smooth it, then pour it with the barrier. No stones: a row of large bottle caps, counted back and kept out of mouths. No goggles: a grown-up pours while the child steps back.

**Skip.** The class chart and the groups.

- **8 min. Bare.** Pour one cup on a damp-sand tin on two books; mark a strip and read it.
- **10 min. Barrier.** Smooth to the same slope, lay a card across the path, pour, mark and read again.
  **Say.** "What do we keep the same, and what do we change?" Expect: keep slope, cup and pour place; change the barrier.
- **8 min. Well and poorly.**
  **Say.** "What did the barrier do well and poorly?" Expect: it lost less, but water ran round its ends.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X17]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one barrier, one run. Never cut the bare tray, the numbers or the exit. Longer: a stone row with wider gaps.

### Words and supports

Barrier, retreat, width. Frame: "It did well at ___ and poorly at ___."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who picks a barrier because it looks strongest (error C). Day 18 opens with error C: put the result bar next to each barrier before choosing. Then mat and plants, and the class chart grows.
