# Key: cards-exits-16-20

Teacher only. Do not print for children.

## Day 16 exit card: dry sand and damp sand

**Key (teacher):** Yes, with the words that the start must be the same. No, because the slope was the same, is error E. Part b: 4 centimeters. Reading the second mark without starting at 0, or giving a number that is not 4, is not yet seen (no letter).

## Day 17 exit card: the fence

**Key (teacher):** 6 centimeters less: bare tray 7 centimeters, fence tray 1 centimeter. Choosing the fence because it looks strongest, without reading the bars, is error C. Another number has no letter: lay the bars side by side and count the squares between the tops.

## Day 18 exit card: wood chips

**Key (teacher):** A change that answers the gaps, such as closing or filling them, with the picture of water through the gaps or the loss growing from 2 to 5 named as the support. No change because the pile lost little is error D. A change because the pile looks messy is error C. A change the card does not show, such as a taller pile, has no letter: ask what the picture shows.

## Day 19 exit card: mulch and gravel

**Key (teacher):** A mulch bar 5 squares tall and a gravel bar 9 squares tall, both from the bottom line, with the tick above the gravel bar. A tick on both bars, or on the mulch bar, is error G: day 20 opens with error G. Bars of other heights have no letter: count the squares up from the bottom line.

## Day 20 exit card: two shapes for one garden strip

**Key (teacher):** Part a: both shapes drawn, each with an arrow that bends around or slows at the shape; arrows straight through both have no letter, so note it and show day 21 an arrow bending. Part b: the same ground and fan place, then measure the retreat. The one that looks stronger is error C. A different ground or fan place is error E.
