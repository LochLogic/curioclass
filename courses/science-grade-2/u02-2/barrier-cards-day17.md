# Barrier cards and result bars (day 17)

Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture.

## Card wall and stone row

**Picture 1:** A soil tray from above with a card wall standing across the middle of the path, water arrows going round its ends.

**Picture 2:** A soil tray from above with a row of smooth stones across the middle of the path, water arrows going between and round the stones.

## Result bar card

**Picture:** Three bars from equal water tests, one square for each centimeter: bare tray 6 squares, card wall 3 squares, stone row 4 squares. Each bar is labelled with its barrier.

**Say (teacher):** "The bars are retreat in centimeters. Point to the barrier that lost the least. Say one thing it did well. Say one thing it did poorly."

**Key (teacher):** The card wall lost the least, 3. Both barriers did better than the bare tray. A card wall can turn soft and fall in water; a stone row lets water through the gaps. Accept any weakness the record or the picture supports.
