# Shape-to-job cards (day 20)

Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture.

## Four shapes

1. A flat card wall facing the wind.
2. A card wall bent into a curve, with its middle toward the wind.
3. A single row of sticks in a line.
4. Two rows of sticks with the sticks of the second row standing in the gaps of the first.

**Say (teacher):** "A shape has a job. Draw an arrow for the wind. Draw a second arrow to show where the shape turns the wind."

**Key (teacher):** The flat wall turns the wind up and over; the curve pushes it to the sides; one row lets wind through the gaps; the two staggered rows slow wind in the gaps. Accept any arrow that matches the shape.

## Fair-plan card

**Picture:** Two soil trays side by side, the same size, the same soil, the same wetness, a fan place marked with the same X for each.

**Say (teacher):** "These trays start equal. What would you change to compare two windbreaks fairly: the fan place or the windbreak?"

**Key (teacher):** The windbreak. The fan place stays the same.
