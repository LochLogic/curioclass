# Day 16: An equal start, and how far the edge moved back

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, set two trays for each group: damp sand on two books, a taped line across the top, an X at the pour place. Fill the cups to one line. Choose two different sixes, for Retrieval and the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on before any cup is filled, kept on through clean-up. Sand stays below faces. Wipe spills; wash hands. No goggles for all: only you pour; groups use the invented strips.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 15 exits. Teach the letter more children made. Error F: lay a unit 1 pour record beside the Alder Creek flood record.

**Ask.** What can the pour record not show? Expect: how long a real change took.

Error B: "in one night" settles it. If B was more missed, it takes these minutes; F goes to a card at the table.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Lay the three retrieval cards. Everyone points; hear six on the last (6 x 20 s).

**Ask.** Which step comes before build? Expect: sketch.

**Ask.** Where is the sun highest? Expect: noon.

**Ask.** Which picture sends a message by light? Expect: the lamp.

#### 3 min. The problem

Show the day 11 path sketch. Problem: keep the soil on the path. On the design-cycle chart add "compare" after "test".

#### 6 min. An equal start

Goggles on. Pour one cup on a tray on four books and one on two. Pip says: "Both got one cup, so it is fair."

**Ask.** What was not the same? Expect: the slope; the steeper tray lost more.

**Ask.** What must match before we pour? Expect: slope, cup and pour place.

Keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Groups check their trays against the slope card.

#### 9 min. Mark the retreat

Teach ruler zero once: put the strip's first mark at 0; read the second mark to the whole centimeter.

Jobs in each group of five: pourer (unit 1's), second pourer, marker, measurer, recorder. The marker marks the sand edge on the strip. Both pourers pour one cup at the X. The marker marks the new edge; the measurer reads the strip; the recorder writes both numbers. Hear the measurers (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

Write each pair on the board. A pair more than 2 apart: pour again; if it still differs, that group uses the invented strips on day 17.

#### 4 min. Two limits

Say: retreat is how far the edge moved back; path width used is how much path a barrier takes; a limit is the most we allow.

Each child sketches a barrier on the path with an arrow from shape to job. Hear six: "This ___ stops soil because ___" (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Goggles wiped and bagged. Hands washed in two waves at two sinks. An adult tips the sand into the lined bin. Groups keep their strips.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read [[s02-X16]] once; everyone points. Hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on the board. No sand: read the invented strips with a ruler.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a baking tin of damp sand on two books. No goggles: use sunglasses, or watch a grown-up pour.

**Skip.** The retrieval cards, the groups and the clean-up count.

- **10 min. An equal start.** Pour one cup on the tin, then tilt the books and pour again.
  **Say.** "What must match before we pour two trays?" Expect: the slope, the cup and the place we pour.
- **12 min. Mark the retreat.** Lay a strip from a taped line to the sand edge, mark it, pour, mark again, and read the strip against a ruler at 0.
  **Say.** "How far did the edge move back?" Expect: a whole number of centimeters.
- **5 min. A barrier.** Sketch one barrier on a path.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X16]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one tray, one strip. Never cut the equal start, ruler zero or the exit. Longer: a steeper tray.

### Words and supports

Retreat, barrier, limit. Frame: "The edge moved back ___ centimeters."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the start does not matter if the cup is the same (error E). Day 17 opens with error E: reset two trays to the slope card, then pour.
