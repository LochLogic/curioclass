# Day 16: An equal start, and how far the edge moved back

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, set two trays a group: damp sand on two books, a taped line, an X at the pour place; cups filled to one line. Choose two sixes, for Retrieval and the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on before any cup is filled, until clean-up ends. Sand stays below faces. Wipe spills. No goggles for all: only you pour; groups use invented strips.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 15 exits. Teach the letter more children made; the other goes to a table card.

Error F: lay a unit 1 pour record beside the Alder Creek flood record. **Ask.** What can it not show? Expect: how long a real change took.

Error B: lay the Alder Creek record beside a day 14 slow card. **Ask.** Which words tell fast or slow? Expect: "in one night"; "little by little".

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Lay the three retrieval cards. Everyone points; hear six on the last.

**Ask.** Which step comes before build? Expect: sketch.

**Ask.** Which day has more daylight? Expect: the longer bar.

**Ask.** Which picture sends a message by light? Expect: the lamp.

#### 3 min. The problem

Show the day 11 path sketch. Problem: keep the soil on the path. Add "compare" after "test" on the design-cycle chart.

#### 6 min. An equal start

Goggles on. Pour one cup on a tray on four books, one on two. Pip says: "Both got one cup, so it is fair."

**Ask.** What was not the same? Expect: the slope; the steeper tray lost more.

**Ask.** What else could differ at the start? Show a dry scoop beside the damp tray. Expect: how wet it is.

**Ask.** What must match? Expect: slope, wetness, cup, pour place.

Keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Groups check their trays against the slope card.

#### 9 min. Mark the retreat

Say: retreat is how far the edge moved back. Teach ruler zero: strip's first mark at 0; read the second to the whole centimeter.

Jobs in each group of five: pourer (unit 1's), second pourer, marker, measurer, recorder. Both pourers pour one cup at the X; the marker marks the old and new edge on the strip; the measurer reads it; the recorder writes both numbers. Hear the measurers (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

Write each pair on the board. Pairs more than 1 apart pour again; if still apart, use the invented strips on day 17.

#### 3 min. Set-up for day 19: a limit

Say: a limit is the most we allow. Each child sketches a barrier with an arrow from shape to job, as in grade 1 (1 minute). Hear six: "This ___ stops soil because ___" (2 minutes).

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Goggles wiped and bagged. Hands washed in two waves at two sinks. An adult tips the sand into the bin. Groups keep strips.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read [[s02-X16]]; everyone points. Show the strip and read [[s02-X16b]]; everyone writes the number. Hear six on the first (2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on the board. No sand: read the invented strips.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a baking tin of damp sand on two books. No goggles: use sunglasses, or watch a grown-up pour.

**Skip.** The retrieval cards, the groups and the clean-up count.

- **10 min. An equal start.** Pour one cup on the tin, then tilt the books and pour again.
  **Say.** "What must match before we pour two trays?" Expect: the slope, the cup and the place we pour.
- **12 min. Mark the retreat.** Lay a strip from a taped line to the sand edge, mark it, pour, mark again, and read the strip against a ruler at 0.
  **Say.** "How far did the edge move back?" Expect: a whole number of centimeters.
- **5 min. A barrier.** Sketch one barrier on a path.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X16]] and [[s02-X16b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one tray, one strip. Never cut the equal start, ruler zero or the exit.

### Words and supports

Retreat, barrier, limit, wet and dry. Frame: "The edge moved back ___ centimeters."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the start does not matter if the cup is the same (error E). Day 17 opens with E: reset two trays to the slope card, pour; then a dry tray beside a damp one.
