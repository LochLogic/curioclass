# Day 18: A mat and plants against the same water

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, set two trays for each group to the slope card. Stake a fabric mat across one tray, edge not pegged. Check the seedlings: untreated, clean. Choose two different sixes, for Two runs and the exit. Key: key-cards-exits-16-20.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on before any cup is filled, kept on through clean-up. Sand stays below faces. Seedlings are not eaten and return to the adult. Wipe spills; wash hands. No goggles for all: only you pour; groups use the invented water records.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 17 exits. Error C: write "The tall wall will win, it looks strongest." Lay the class chart bars beside the wall and rock pictures.

**Ask.** What do we need before we choose? Expect: the result bar for each barrier.

Pairs match each barrier picture to its bar; the bars decide.

#### 3 min. Roots hold soil

Show the four protection pictures: grass mat, low shrubs, tall tree, fabric mat. Say: established roots hold soil; a new seedling's roots do not yet. Our fabric stands in for a grass mat.

**Ask.** Which hold soil with roots? Expect: grass mat, shrubs and tree.

#### 4 min. Set up

Goggles on. Each group checks both trays against the slope card and fills the cups to the line.

#### 11 min. Two runs

Two pourers pour the mat tray and the plant tray at once. Mark both strips; read them with a ruler at 0; write the numbers. Reset to the slope card, restake the mat, replace the seedlings; pour again. Jobs turn; everyone has a different one. Hear one child per group say "mat ___, plants ___" (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 3 min. Record

Each group measures the path width used by the mat and by the plants with a strip. Recorders post all six numbers, two at a time (3 x 20 s = 1 minute).

#### 5 min. Improve

Each group picks one change because the record says so, such as pegging the mat edge, and sketches it, as in grade 1, with an arrow from shape to job. It is not retested. Hear six: "I would change ___ because the record says ___" (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Accept a change the record or the picture supports.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Goggles wiped and bagged. Seedlings return to the adult. Hands washed in two waves at two sinks. An adult tips the sand into the lined bin.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented. Read [[s02-X18]] once; everyone points to what on the card supports their change and whispers the change to a partner. Hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No trays: use the invented water records for the fabric mat (2 then 3) and the seedlings (6 then 7), said aloud to be invented. No printer: draw the pictures on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** One tin. A cloth stands in for the mat; a clump of grass or a sprig pressed into damp sand stands in for plants. No goggles: sunglasses.

**Skip.** The class chart and the groups.

- **6 min. Roots.** Look outdoors or at a picture.
  **Say.** "Which plants hold soil with roots?" Expect: grass, shrubs and trees.
- **14 min. Test.** Pour a cup on the tin with the cloth staked on; mark a strip and read it. Smooth the sand, press the plant in, pour again.
- **6 min. Improve.** Sketch one change because the record says so.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X18]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one run each. Never cut the numbers, the change from the record or the exit. Longer: peg the mat edge and test it.

### Words and supports

Roots, mat, seedling, improve. Frame: "I would change ___ because the record says ___."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the design that lost the least has no weakness (error D). Day 19 opens with error D: mark one thing the chosen design does poorly in its own record. An error C goes to a card at the table. Then the numbers become bars.
