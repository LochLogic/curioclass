# The slope card and the strip (day 16)

Two pieces for the day 16 test. Print "invented for practice" under each.

## Slope card

**Picture:** Two soil trays drawn from the side, one beside the other. Each rests on the same stack of two books. A line from the tray bottom to the book top is marked "same slope". A cup beside each is marked "one cup".

**Say (teacher):** "Both trays sit on the same books. Both get one cup. We change one thing at a time."

## Paper strip card

**Picture:** A tray drawn from above with a paper strip laid along the top edge, and a pencil mark on the strip where the soil edge begins. A second picture shows the same tray after pouring, with a second pencil mark farther back.

**Say (teacher):** "Mark the edge before. Pour. Mark the edge after. The space between the two marks is how far the edge moved back. Read it with the ruler to the whole centimeter."

**Key (teacher):** Zero means no move. A strip that reaches the 3 mark is 3 centimeters of retreat. Children read whole centimeters only.
