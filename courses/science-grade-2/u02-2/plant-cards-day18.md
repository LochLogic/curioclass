# Mat and plant cards (day 18)

Print "invented for practice" under each card. No people in any picture.

## Four protection pictures

1. A grass mat laid across a bare path on a slope, roots in the soil.
2. A row of low shrubs along the edge of a bank.
3. A tall tree with wide roots holding a slope.
4. A fabric mat staked down across a bare path.

**Say (teacher):** "These are four ways to hold soil. Point to the ones that hold soil with roots. Point to the one that holds it with cloth."

**Key (teacher):** Roots: 1, 2 and 3. Cloth: 4.

## Result bar card

**Picture:** Three bars from equal water tests, one square for each centimeter: bare tray 7 squares, fabric mat 2 squares, grass mat 1 square. A note under the path width reads: the fabric mat covers 8 centimeters of the path and the grass mat covers 3.

**Say (teacher):** "Which lost the least? Which covers more of the path? What does a barrier give up when it covers more of the path?"

**Key (teacher):** The grass mat lost the least, 1. The fabric mat covers more path, 8, so less of the path can be used.
