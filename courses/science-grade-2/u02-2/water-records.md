# Invented water records (days 17 to 19)

For a room where the trays will not give clean numbers. Say aloud that every record is invented for practice, not observations, and print "invented for practice, not observations" on each card.

## Retreat records

**Picture:** For each design, two ruler bars side by side, first run and second run, one square for each centimeter. Retreat in centimeters:

- bare tray: 9 and 10
- card wall: 2 and 4
- rock barrier: 5 and 6
- fabric mat: 2 and 3
- seedlings: 6 and 7

## Path width used

**Picture:** One ruler bar for each of two designs, in centimeters: card wall 8, rock barrier 3.

**Say (teacher):** "These records stand in for our trays. Read each bar with the ruler. Which design lost less? Which takes more of the path?"

**Key (teacher):** The card wall lost less than the rock barrier, 4 against 6 in the second run, but takes more of the path, 8 against 3.
