# Invented bare-slope strips (day 16)

For a room where the sand will not behave. Say aloud that these strips are invented for practice, not observations, and print "invented for practice" under each.

## Two strips

**Picture:** Two paper strips drawn side by side, each marked with a centimeter ruler beneath it and two pencil marks. Strip 1: the second mark is at 9. Strip 2: the second mark is at 10. Each is labelled bare tray, one cup.

**Say (teacher):** "These two trays started the same and were poured the same. Read each strip with the ruler. Do they match exactly?"

**Key (teacher):** Strip 1 is 9 centimeters and strip 2 is 10. Equal trays still differ a little, so a gap of 1 centimeter is about the same.
