# Day 20: Plan a fair wind test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set two soil trays and a count of sticks for each group. Check every stick is blunt with no splinters. Keep shape cards 2 and 4 in the envelope for the exit; do not show them. Choose six children for the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Soil stays damp and below faces. Goggles on for the build and the clean-up. Sticks have blunt ends, stay below faces and are counted back. Wash hands after soil. No fan today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 19 exits. Error G: write "The short bar is the one past the line." Lay a ruler along the limit line on the pinned graph.

**Ask.** Which bars rise above the ruler? Expect: the ones past the limit.

Pairs read each pinned bar as over or under.

#### 4 min. The wind problem

Show the fan-tray card from day 15. Same problem, a different mover: wind carries dry soil. On the board write: against wind.

**Ask.** What moves the soil now? Expect: the wind.

#### 6 min. Shape and purpose

Show shape card 1, a flat card wall, and card 3, a single row of sticks. Each child draws a wind arrow at each shape, and a second arrow where the shape turns the wind, as in grade 1's shape-to-job arrow. Hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

**Ask.** Where does the wind go at each shape? Expect: up and over the wall; through the gaps in the row. Land behind may get less wind; we will test.

#### 14 min. Build and sketch

Two children build the card wall in one tray, two stand the sticks in a row in the other, and one measures. Choose a gap of 1, 2 or 3 centimeters between sticks and say why. Everyone sketches both builds on design paper with a wind arrow. Hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

Each group puts a counter on 1, 2 or 3. The most counters is the class gap. A tie: you pick the middle. Write it on the board for day 21. Nobody knows the best gap yet.

#### 3 min. Fair wind plan

Show the fair-plan card: two trays alike, one fan place X.

**Ask.** To compare two windbreaks fairly, do we change the fan place or the windbreak? Expect: the windbreak. Keep the same tray, fan place and wetness; change the barrier; watch the retreat.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count the sticks back. Tip the soil into the lined bin. Wash hands in two waves at two sinks.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented. Hand out the day 20 cards. Read [[s02-X20a]] once; everyone sketches. Read [[s02-X20b]] once; hear six (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No soil trays: build with paper and draw the trays. No printer: draw the shape cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** One baking tin of damp soil. Card strips stand in for sticks. No fan.

**Skip.** The counters and the vote.

- **8 min. Shapes.** Look at a card wall and a row of sticks.
  **Say.** "Where does the wind go at each one?" Expect: over the wall; through the gaps in the row.
- **12 min. Build.** Stand a card wall in the tin, then a row of sticks, and sketch both again, with the shape-to-job arrow from grade 1.
- **6 min. Plan.**
  **Say.** "What do we change to compare fairly?" Expect: the windbreak; the tray and fan place stay the same.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X20a]] and [[s02-X20b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: build one tray and sketch both. Never cut the arrows, the fair plan or the exit. Longer: draw where the soil behind each may stay.

### Words and supports

Shape, job, windbreak, gap. Frame: "This shape turns the wind ___."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who picks the one that looks stronger (error C) or changes the ground or fan place (error E); arrows straight through both shapes have no letter. Day 21 opens with error C or E, whichever more children made, in 5 minutes: match two barriers to their result bars. Then you run the wind trials with the class gap.
