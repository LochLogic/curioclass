# Extension card

For a child who showed everything. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A picture of a bay with a tall hill on the left and flat ground on the right, and a stream that runs down to the ocean. A key box is empty.

**Read:** Make a picture model. Where does the rain water go? Use a dotted arrow for any route you did not see.
