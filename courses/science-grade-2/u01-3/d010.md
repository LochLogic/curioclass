# Day 10: Fix it, try again, and say where the water goes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one next step on each day 8 and day 9 record. Sort the records into four piles: error A with error F; error B with error D and error G; error C; error E. Add day 7 exit tickets for error C and error F. Pin the dated day 1 sketch beside a clean sheet headed "Our schoolyard after rain". Draw picture directions on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and read feedback

Hand back day 8's assessment and day 9's items; children read or hear their next step. Hear six children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "My next step is ___." Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Repair by letter groups

Each group sits with you 3 minutes in turn (12), then 3 to move. The others follow the picture directions and fix one thing on their record; a child who showed everything does the extension card. All pages are invented.

- **Error A with error F** (page 1). **Ask.** Which page can tell us where the lake is? Expect: the keyed map. For F, add a board log, "a pond by the shed": it adds a pond.
- **Error B with error D and error G** (pages 2 and 5). **Ask.** What would a partner not know without a key? Expect: what the arrow means. Then add the hill, dip and lake.
- **Error C** (page 3). **Ask.** Which is solid and which liquid? Expect: snow solid, puddle liquid, both water.
- **Error E** (page 4). Trace the lowest way. **Ask.** Which way did the water go? Expect: down to the pool.

If many made one letter, it takes the first 5 minutes whole class; each turn shrinks to 2.5 minutes.

#### 10 min. Fresh retry

Say the packet is invented and unscored. Each child works alone; read one step at a time. Step 1: mark the water places on pages 1 to 3, liquid or solid, [[s01-X10]]. Step 2: draw the ridge, hollow and lower stream, with a key, [[s01-X10b]]. Step 3: "I know the stream is low because ___", [[s01-X10c]]. Note only the repaired components.

#### 5 min. Anchor explanation

At the consensus chart; you draw, they tell. **Ask.** Which routes did we see? Expect: blacktop to the low corner, along the curb to the drain cover, along the groove into the grass. Draw solid arrows, naming each picture. **Ask.** Which routes did we not see? Expect: down at the drain cover, and into the grass slope. Draw dotted arrows: "A dotted arrow means we think the water goes this way, but we did not see it." **Ask.** Where does the water on our schoolyard go after rain? Expect: downhill to the low corner, where it collects.

#### 2 min. Close the board

Answer the board card "Where does the water go?" from the chart. Leave open: "Where does it go inside the drain?"

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a ridge with a hollow holding a pool, a stream below, snow on top, and a page that says "My favourite colour".

**Skip.** The letter groups and the record sheet.

- **5 min. Look back.** Lay your child's day 9 model beside the pictures.
  **Say.** "What would you change?" Expect: one fix, such as the key, the hills, the ocean or the water running down the valley.
- **10 min. Seen and not seen.** Draw a street after rain. Use solid arrows for water you saw, dotted for a guess.
  **Say.** "Which way did the water go that we did not see?" Expect: a dotted arrow and "I think ___, but we did not see it."
- **15 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X10]], [[s01-X10b]] and [[s01-X10c]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the extension card, never the retry. Longer: a second invented bay.

### Words and supports

Uncertain: we did not see it. Frames: "I know the ___ because ___." and "I think ___, but we did not see it."

### Tomorrow

Unit 1 ends here. The retry shows who still marks the colour page (error A), leaves out the snow (error C) or skips the key (error B). Day 11 opens unit 2 with a worn path and retrieves source practice and the schoolyard runoff.
