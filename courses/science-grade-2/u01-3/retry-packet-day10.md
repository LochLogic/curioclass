# Day 10 retry packet

A fresh unscored retry, printed only on day 10. Read one step at a time. Print "invented for practice" on each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names.

## Page 1: the ridge

**Picture:** A rounded ridge with a hollow holding a pool, and a lower stream running along the foot of the ridge. A woman with short hair and a green coat stands by the pool.

## Page 2: the close-up

**Picture:** A close view of snow lying on the top of the ridge.

## Page 3: the colour page

**Picture:** A page with a large patch of orange and the words My favourite colour.

## Steps

**Read:** Step 1: "Mark the water places and say whether each is liquid or solid." Step 2: "Draw a model of the same ridge, hollow and lower stream, with a key." Step 3: "Say why the stream is where it is, with the words: I know the stream is low because."

## Key (teacher)

Step 1: pool and stream liquid; snow solid; the colour page is not a source. Step 2: ridge and hollow with the pool, the stream low and away from the ridge, a key. Step 3: I know the stream is low because the picture shows it below the hollow and the ridge.
