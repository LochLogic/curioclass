# Section 1.3 opener: a model of a new place

## What the section does

Two days, 9 and 10. Day 9 is the model check: each child gets a packet of two views of an unfamiliar bay and draws a picture model of it with a key, then shows and says the route the rain water takes. Day 10 is the repair day, with a fresh unscored retry for the children who need it and a class model with dotted arrows for what nobody saw.

## How the days run

Day 9 is thirty-five minutes: directions, the model, and collecting. There is no help. Day 10 opens with five minutes of returning feedback. You hand back each child's day 8 and day 9 record with one written next step. Then three-minute repair groups by letter: group the children who made the same error letter, and work with each group on a fresh set of cards. Mark with the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A model of a new place uses the same parts: land shapes, water, a route and a key.** The shapes are different, because the place is different.
- **The water goes to the low ground.** That is the pattern, and it is a claim about the places we have seen.
- **A map of another area uses its own shapes.** Do not copy this area onto it.
- **A dotted arrow means unobserved.** Use it for the route nobody saw.

## Safety

No new materials. Keep the day 9 packets with you until you have scored them.
