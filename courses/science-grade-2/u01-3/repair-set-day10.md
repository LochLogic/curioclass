# Repair set

Fresh cards for the three-minute letter groups on day 10. None is from the day 8 or day 9 packets. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** Group A: two pages. One says Rivers are fun. The other is a keyed map with a filled-dot lake. A child picks the page that can answer where water is.

**Read:** Which page can tell us where the lake is?

## Page 2

**Picture:** Group B and D: a blank tray picture with a hill and a dip, no key, and an arrow. A child adds the key and the land around the water.

**Read:** Add what the arrow means, and draw the hill and the dip.

## Page 3

**Picture:** Group C: a drawing of snow on a step beside a drawing of a puddle of melted water on the same step.

**Read:** Snow and the puddle are both water. Which is solid and which is liquid?

## Page 4

**Picture:** Group E: a drawing of a slope with a pool at its foot and an arrow pointing up the slope.

**Read:** Trace the lowest way with a finger. Which way did the water go?

## Page 5

**Picture:** Group G: a drawing of a pictured lake and a model of the same area with the lake left off.

**Read:** Find the lake in the picture. Add it to the model.
