# Day 9: A model of Willow Bay

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover everything on the walls. Seat children so each sees only their own paper. Print the packet, template and student page. Give the record sheet six columns, one for each component below.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say the packet is invented for practice. Read the prompt neutrally, one step at a time, with a pause after each, because it has several asks. Step 1: look at both views of Willow Bay; read the caption once. Step 2: make a model of this area, with a key, [[s01-E4]]. Step 3: use it to show and say the way the water goes, [[s01-E5]]. Step 4: say how a map could show a different area, [[s01-E6]]. Name no part as the right one.

#### 25 min. The model

Each child works alone and draws the model on the template. Nothing is placed for them, and there are no group talks. Read a line again on request, once. A scribe or an oral answer is allowed for a child whose plan says so, 30 seconds each, six at most (3 minutes). Mark the six on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only "shown" counts a point.

1. Draws both hills and the valley in recognisable shapes.
2. Draws the bending river and the ocean in recognisable shapes.
3. Places the river in the valley between the hills, opening into the ocean.
4. Adds a key that says what each mark means.
5. Shows the water running along the low valley toward the mouth, not over the hills, because the valley is lower.
6. Says a map of a different area has that area's own shapes and places.

#### 5 min. Check and collect

Check names and collect everything. Score nothing in front of the class.

After class, mark the six. With day 8's six that is twelve components on one scale; only "shown" counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: no key is error B, no hills error D, no ocean error G, water over the hills error E. Feedback names one component and one next step. A child who showed all six gets the extension card.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two views on the board and read the caption. One adult: children work alone anyway.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a bay with two hills, a valley, a bending river and an ocean, seen from the side and from above, with a caption. No template: draw a big box and a key box under it.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the scribe.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Read the prompt aloud one step at a time and wait. Your child does [[s01-E4]], [[s01-E5]] and [[s01-E6]] alone. Give no hint.
- **10 min. Look together.** Lay the model beside the pictures.
  **Say.** "Where does the rain water go, and how do you know?" Expect: along the low valley to the river mouth, because the valley is lower.
- **10 min. A different place.** Pick any place your child knows.
  **Say.** "How would a map of that place look?" Expect: its own hills and water in its own places, with its own key.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the check is not shortened. Longer: none; nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Model, key, valley, river, ocean, route. Frames: "The water goes ___ because ___." and "A map of a different area would show ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking or pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 is not scored in class. On day 10 each child gets day 9's items and day 8's assessment back, with one written next step. Day 9's letters go to the letter groups: error B with error D and error G, and error E. Day 10 also takes day 8's error A, error C and error F, then a fresh unscored retry.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
