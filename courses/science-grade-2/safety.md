# Our science safety rules (Grade 2, the whole year)

A teacher page: each rule once, with the days that use it. Each day's Safety and set-up says what that day needs. The unit safety sheets are the child versions. Give a short safety talk before every hands-on part; watch every task throughout.

## The stop rule (every day)

Anyone, child or adult, may say "Stop." Everyone freezes, hands down, and you look. Stop for a spill, a hurt or upset child, a sting, rising dust, a loose cord, thunder, or anything unknown. Start again only when you say so.

## Eye protection (days 2, 7, 16 to 18, 20, 21, 27 to 30, 32 to 34, 36, 42, 49 to 55, 60 to 63, 75)

- Everyone in the room wears it for every pour, trial and clean-up, you too.
- Splash goggles, indirectly vented and marked Z87 and D3, for sand, soil, water and the fan.
- Impact glasses or goggles marked Z87 for strips, blocks and model pieces.
- One pair per child, in a named bag; wash with soap and water and dry before the next use.
- Sunglasses are not eye protection. Without enough for all, only you pour and groups use the invented records.

## Hand washing (days 2, 16 to 18, 20, 21, 30, 42, 43, 49 to 56, 60, 62, 66, 70, 75)

Wash with soap and water after sand, soil, plants and outdoor looks. When thirty cannot wash at once, they wipe hands with a damp cloth, then wash before the next food or break, said aloud.

## Allergies: plants, foods, stings and latex (every hands-on day)

- Before the year, get the nurse's list: plants and pollen, foods, stings, mold, dust and asthma, latex.
- A child on the list uses the pictures and handles nothing they react to; any child may choose the pictures.
- Bands, gloves and rubber samples are nonlatex (days 27, 30, 34).
- Food appears only as sealed displays (days 39, 42, 43): double-sealed, kept cold until class, discarded unopened the same day. Nothing is tasted.

## Plants, soil and mold (days 16 to 18, 20, 49 to 56, 60 to 63, 75)

- Use play sand or sterilized bagged potting soil, never soil dug from the yard.
- Grow plants from grocery or school-garden seed, not coated garden seed (days 49, 75). Plants and seeds are never eaten.
- If a cup grows fuzzy mold, the adult, wearing gloves, puts it unopened in a sealed bag, then washes hands; a child with a mold allergy or asthma moves away (days 50 to 56, 75). Tubs from home are rinsed of food first.

## Outdoor looks and adult ratios (days 4, 66, 70)

- A second adult comes outdoors (an aide, a volunteer or a nearby teacher), one at each spot. With none, use the photographs.
- The day before, an adult walks the spots for glass, sharp things, animal waste, standing water, nests, and poison ivy or nettle; if found, use the photographs.
- The adult carries the nurse's phone number and any listed child's epinephrine pen.
- Closed shoes. Stay on the path and in sight; touch and collect nothing. Count heads out and back.
- Sun: water and hats. Ice or storms: photographs indoors.

## Heat and lamps (days 28, 29, 42, 43)

- An adult heats and freezes before class, away from the room; items come in cooled. No kettle, heater or flame in the room.
- Lukewarm water is tap water that feels barely warm on the adult's wrist, never from a kettle or a hot tap (day 29). Day 42's warm water is in a sealed tub out of reach, emptied before children enter.
- Lamps are LED or a flashlight, never a bulb that gets hot, and never aimed at eyes (day 28). A hand lens is never aimed at the sun or a bright window (day 60).

## Electricity (days 21, 28)

Only you plug in or touch the fan or lamp. Cords stay dry, taped flat, away from water and sand. The fan runs on low; children stay behind the taped line.

## Magnets, small parts and string (days 2, 17, 40, 47, 61 to 63)

No magnets are used this year. Pebbles, stones, counters and model seeds stay out of mouths and are counted back (days 2, 17, 61 to 63). String is short and kept away from necks (days 40, 47). Pipe cleaner ends are folded back. Floor mats are taped down (day 61).

## Glass and sharp edges (days 7, 27, 28, 33, 34, 36)

No glass anywhere, home swaps included: plastic containers only; a picture card stands in for glass (day 28). Pieces are new and blunt; foil edges are folded over by an adult (day 30). The adult checks wood pieces for splinters or cracks before class (day 36). Blocks are under 250 grams and placed by the adult while children sit (day 33).

## Clean-up (every hands-on day)

- Wipe spills at once. Trays sit on the table, books under them taped (days 16 to 18).
- Damp sand is scooped into a lined bin, never dry-swept, never poured down a drain (days 2, 21).
- The adult hands out and collects trays and sealed cups (days 7, 29).
