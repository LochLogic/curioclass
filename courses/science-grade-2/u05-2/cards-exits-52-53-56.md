# Exit cards, days 52, 53 and 56

Each exit has picture cards every child touches and a spoken stem read once. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. The teacher hears a rotating six.

## Day 52 exits: two chive cards

**Picture (a):** two sage plants started May 16 at 5 centimeters with three green upright leaves each. On May 18 the plant kept in the dark and watered is 5 centimeters with three green upright leaves, and beside it its control, kept in the sun and watered, is also 5 centimeters with three green upright leaves.
**Say:** "Pip says the dark plant is the same as its control, so plants do not need light. Compare the two plants: is Pip right yet? Say yes or not yet."
**Key (teacher):** not yet, with the words that the dark plant matches its control only so far and we keep recording. Error C is saying yes.
**Picture (b):** a second chive plant, started May 9 at 7 centimeters with four green upright leaves. On May 11 the plant kept in the sun with no water is still 7 centimeters with four green upright leaves.
**Say:** "Pip says the sunlit plant looks fine with no water, so sunlight is all it needs. Is Pip right yet? Say yes or not yet."
**Key (teacher):** not yet; keep recording. Error D is saying yes.

## Day 53 exit: basil cards

**Picture (a):** the June 2 basil cards: the shaded plant 10 centimeters with two pale leaves and a leaning stem, the sunlit plant 7 centimeters with four green upright leaves; three small cards: a ruler, the leaves and stem, the pot.
**Say (a):** "Pip says the shaded basil is healthier because it is taller. Touch the card that shows Pip is wrong."
**Key (teacher):** the leaves and stem card. The ruler card is error B.

**Picture (b):** a basil light test with equal water. June 2: sunlit plant 7 centimeters with four green upright leaves; shaded plant 10 centimeters with two pale leaves and a leaning stem. The shaded plant is moved into the sun. June 9: sunlit plant 8 centimeters with five green leaves; the moved plant still 10 centimeters with four green leaves and a firmer stem.
**Say (b):** "Did the moved plant get healthier? Say yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** yes; more green leaves and a firmer stem, though its height did not change. Error B is no, because the height is the same.

## Day 56 exit: three spinach cards

**Picture:** spinach plants all started April 1 at 3 centimeters with two green leaves. April 15: sun with water, 8 centimeters, four green leaves. Dark with water, 11 centimeters, two pale leaves. Sun with no water, 3 centimeters, two wilted leaves.
**Say (a):** "Touch the two pairs that test light and water fairly."
**Key (teacher):** sun with water beside dark with water, and sun with water beside sun with no water. Error A is dark with water beside sun with no water.
**Say (b):** "Tell us what in each fair pair shows what a plant needs."
**Key (teacher):** the dark plant is taller but pale, so it needs light; the dry plant wilted with no gain, so it needs water. Errors: the dark one is taller so it is healthier (B); water is all a plant needs (C); sunlight is all a plant needs (D).
