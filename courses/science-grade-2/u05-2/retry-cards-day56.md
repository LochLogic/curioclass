# Retry cards (day 56)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Fresh cards for the missed component only, two asks each, answered by touch or yes or no, unscored. Items s05-X56c to s05-X56h hold the asks and keys.

**Retry A (a fair pair):** two matched lettuce seedlings: L in a sunny window, watered every day; M in a closet, no water. Pip says L and M show that light matters. Ask: "Touch the two things that are different." Then three more cards: a watered plant in the closet, a plant in the window with no water, a second plant like M. Ask: "Touch the plant we could add to compare with L for light alone."
**Key (teacher):** the light and the water; the watered plant in the closet. Error A is saying only the light is different.
**Retry B (taller is not healthier):** two nasturtium plants. Left: 17 centimeters, pale, leaning, two thin leaves. Right: 12 centimeters, green, firm, four leaves. Ask: "Touch the healthier plant." Then: "Touch what tells you."
**Key (teacher):** the shorter one on the right; leaf color, number of leaves or stem. Error B is the taller.
**Retry C (water alone):** a mustard plant watered every day and kept in a closet: Day 1 green, Day 12 pale and limp. Pip says water is all a plant needs. Ask: "Touch the picture that tells."
**Key (teacher):** the Day 12 picture. Error C is agreeing with Pip.
**Retry D (sun alone):** two pepper plants in a sunny window, one limp with no water, one green and watered. Pip says sun is all a plant needs. Ask: "Touch the picture that tells."
**Key (teacher):** the limp plant. Error D is agreeing with Pip.
