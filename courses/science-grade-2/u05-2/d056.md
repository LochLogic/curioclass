# Day 56: Repair, retry and say what we found

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 54's assessment and day 55's assessment by error: A with E, B, C, D, and write each child's group on their feedback form. Set a repair set at each group table. Pin the chart. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. After class the adult restores the treatment plants (dark plants to the window, dry plants watered) and discards any mouldy cup.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and read the feedback

Each child reads or hears the one written next step on their record. Today's six say theirs back, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 12 min. Repair by letter groups

Four groups, each with you for 2.5 minutes in turn while the rest work from picture directions at their table; 2 minutes to move (4 x 2.5 + 2). Group A with E: the dill cups, then the modelled kale entry. Group B: the day 53 oat cards beside the day 51 corn cards, every difference besides height. Group C: the violet in a closet, Day 1 beside Day 10. Group D: the geranium in a sunny window, Day 1 beside Day 10. One child follows the same steps one-to-one with an adult. A child with no gap plans another comparison.

#### 10 min. Fresh retries

Each child retries only the missed component: group A [[s05-X56c]] and [[s05-X56d]], group B [[s05-X56e]] and [[s05-X56f]], group C [[s05-X56g]], group D [[s05-X56h]]. Read the asks aloud (2 minutes); children answer by touch or yes or no (4). Six rotating children say their reason, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Collect (1). A retry is recorded apart and never rescores an item.

#### 5 min. Class explanation

The class dictates and you write: missing light plausibly explains the pale grass; our seedlings show what happens without light or without water; the board alone did not isolate a cause, and warmth is still a question. **Ask.** Which day's record shows it? Expect: a day number from the chart (50, 52, 55). Say where a record was inconclusive.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented for practice. Read [[s05-X56a]] (0.5 minute); all touch the two pairs (0.5). Read [[s05-X56b]]; six rotating children answer, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark on the one scale.

### Marking

After class, 20 minutes: mark the exit; record each retry apart. Days 57 to 59 carry the unit record unchanged.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Two drawn plants for any card.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the class explanation.

- **5 min. Next step.** Read the written next step aloud together.
- **15 min. Repair.** Draw two plants that differ in one thing only. **Say.** "Which is the healthy plant to compare with, and what is different?" Expect: the green watered sunny one; only one thing is different.
- **5 min. Explain.** **Say.** "What do plants need, and how do we know?" Expect: light and water; a plant without light grew pale, without water it wilted.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X56a]] and [[s05-X56b]], then the retry that was missed: [[s05-X56c]], [[s05-X56d]], [[s05-X56e]], [[s05-X56f]], [[s05-X56g]] or [[s05-X56h]]. The child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair for 2 minutes a group; never cut the retry or the exit. Longer: what result would surprise you?

### Words and supports

Healthy plant, pale, wilted, same, different, record. Frames: "The ___ plant is different in ___." "I cannot tell yet, because ___." Pointing is accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who still cannot choose a fair pair, calls the taller plant healthier, or says water alone or sun alone is enough: note each name on the unit 5 record, since the unit ends here and no later day repairs these. Day 57 is the first mid-year check: a new landscape, new water sources and new heating and cooling records, with the unit 5 records left unchanged.
