# Day 57: A new coast, new water cards and two warm bowls

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 56 hands on no gap to this check. Print one card sheet for each child (folded so side 2 stays closed until the second block), and the day 58 set sealed. Cover the walls. Seat each child so they see only their own page and card sheet. On the record sheet choose Today's six for each block, not heard in the last two weeks. The unit 5 records stay in their folders unchanged and are not used today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Directions

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. Every card is new and invented for practice. You may pass." Read the Tern Coast caption on the card sheet twice. Show where answers go on the page. Practice one touch with two plain cups: "Touch the bigger cup."

#### 9 min. A model of a new place

Read [[c1-M57a]] once. Children draw a model with a key (4 minutes). Read [[c1-M57b]]; they draw the arrow (2 minutes). Read [[c1-M57c]]; every child writes or draws one line (1 minute). Reading the three stems takes the other 2 minutes. Today's six are heard quietly at their places, 20 seconds each, as the others write; any child may pass.

#### 9 min. Where is the water?

Open side 2 of the card sheet. Read [[c1-S57a]]; children circle the cards (2 minutes). Read [[c1-S57b]]; they draw or write the liquid and the solid (3 minutes). Read [[c1-S57c]]; every child writes a few words (2 minutes) while Today's six are heard quietly at their places, 20 seconds each; any child may pass. Reading takes the last 2 minutes.

#### 9 min. Heating, cooling and undoing

Show the two record strips on side 2. Read [[c1-H57a]]; children circle returned or stayed changed and draw what shows it (3 minutes). Read [[c1-H57b]] the same way (3 minutes). Read [[c1-H57c]]; every child writes a few words (2 minutes) while Today's six are heard quietly at their places, 20 seconds each. Collect (1 minute).

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the nine items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: A to D in the model, E to G in the sources, H and I in the heating records. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each. Days 54 and 55 are not re-scored.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the Tern Coast picture and the three Fox Valley cards on the board; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a coast with a hill, a river, a lake and the ocean. Any soft food stands in for the noodle (check food allergies first, do not eat it, and a grown-up does any warming).

**Skip.** The sealed sets and Today's six.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each ask aloud once and wait. Your child draws the model and answers [[c1-M57b]], then the water cards and the two bowls, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Did the snowball come back, and did the noodle?" Expect: the snowball did, the noodle did not.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Model, key, route, liquid, solid, returned, stayed changed. Frame: "It returned, because ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 is not scored in class. Day 58 is the second part of the check, at the next check meeting: fast and slow events, two barriers and a material choice. Keep the day 58 set sealed until then. Day 59 hands both days back with one written next step.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
