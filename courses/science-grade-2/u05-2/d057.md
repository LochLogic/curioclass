# Day 57: A new coast, new water cards and two warm bowls

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 56 hands on no gap to this check. Print the cards in three sealed sets. Cover the walls. Seat each child so they see only their own page. On the record sheet choose Today's six for each block, not heard in the last two weeks. The unit 5 records stay in their folders unchanged and are not used today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Directions

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. Every card is new and invented for practice. You may pass." Read the Tern Coast caption twice. Show where answers go on the page. Practise one touch with two plain cups: "Touch the bigger cup."

#### 9 min. A model of a new place

Read [[c1-M57a]] once. Children draw a model with a key (5 minutes). Read [[c1-M57b]]; they draw the arrow (2 minutes). Read [[c1-M57c]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words or draw.

#### 9 min. Where is the water?

Open the Fox Valley set. Read [[c1-S57a]]; children circle the cards (2 minutes). Read [[c1-S57b]]; they draw or write the liquid and the solid (3 minutes). Read [[c1-S57c]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words (2 minutes).

#### 9 min. Heating, cooling and undoing

Open the last set. Read [[c1-H57a]]; children circle returned or stayed changed and draw what shows it (3 minutes). Read [[c1-H57b]] the same way (3 minutes). Read [[c1-H57c]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (2 minutes); the rest write a few words. Collect (1 minute).

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the nine items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: A to D in the model, E to G in the sources, H and I in the heating records. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each. Days 54 and 55 are not re-scored.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the Tern Coast picture and the three Fox Valley cards on the board; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a coast with a hill, a river, a lake and the ocean. Any soft food stands in for the noodle.

**Skip.** The sealed sets and Today's six.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each ask aloud once and wait. Your child draws the model and answers [[c1-M57b]], then the water cards and the two bowls, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Did the snowball come back, and did the noodle?" Expect: the snowball did, the noodle did not.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Model, key, route, liquid, solid, returned, stayed changed. Frame: "It returned, because ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 is not scored in class. Day 58 is the second part of the check: fast and slow events, two barriers and a material choice. Day 59 hands both days back with one written next step.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
