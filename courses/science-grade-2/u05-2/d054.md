# Day 54: Read a plant, graph a date and plan a test

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, the adult sets labeled healthy, dark and dry cups, a tray and five rulers at three tables and checks for mold. Put an observation table, a graph sheet and a plan sheet at each place. Key: key-plan-sheet-day54. Day 53 was the last practice; today samples days 48 to 53 and teaches nothing. Name the first three tables. 

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on for each plant turn. Soil stays in the tray, rulers below faces, spills wiped at once, hands washed at the next washing. The adult lifts a table's cover for its window only and says how long. The adult removes any moldy cup; no child touches it.

### The day

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Read directions neutrally; no hint, no reteaching. Three windows of 4 minutes, one table of five each (15 children). Two rounds of 2 minutes: three children read an assigned plant at the soil, then the other two. Each enters the date, condition, height and leaves and stem, and says which plant it is compared with ([[s05-E1]], [[s05-E2]], [[s05-E3]]). Watch each child about 30 seconds (5 x 30 s = 2.5 minutes a window) and mark the look-fors. A child with day 52 procedure feedback redoes the corrected steps uncoached; append what you see to the day 52 record. Unscored. The other 15 draw [[s05-E4]] from their day 52 record on the graph sheet (5 minutes), then add color symbols. Collect the observation tables at the end.

#### 3 min. Moves and wipe-down

Two moves of 30 seconds. Goggles wiped and bagged, tables, spills and hands wiped; hands are washed at the next washing, said aloud.

#### 5 min. Graph

The 15 who watched draw [[s05-E4]]: bars up from the shared zero line, one square a centimeter, three condition labels. The 15 who graphed earlier check theirs against their records. Unscored.

#### 3 min. Revisit the anchor

Show the board pictures. **Ask.** What do we now think, and what can we not tell? Six rotating children answer, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Write nothing as correct.

#### 12 min. Draft the fresh plan

All 30 children alone, on the plan sheet of eight boxes. Say it is invented for practice. Read the pea-seedling problem one sentence at a time, once (about 1 minute). Give no hint and no repeat. The boxes ask [[s05-E5]] to [[s05-E12]]. Answers are drawn or a few words; a scribe writes only the child's words. Collect the sheets.

### Marking

After class, 45 minutes: read each draft against the look-fors of [[s05-E5]] to [[s05-E12]] and mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write one next step per child on a feedback form for day 55. 

### Other rooms

No plants: use dated measured photographs said aloud to be kept records; the conducting look-fors are not marked, and the record says so.

### At home

**Swap.** Any small plant for the class cups, or a drawn plant with its height written.

**Skip.** The windows, the class graph and the table talk.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Read the pea problem one sentence at a time, once, and give no hint. The child fills the eight boxes of the plan sheet alone.
  **Say.** "What could explain why P is tall and pale and why Q wilted?" Expect: P may lack light and Q may lack water.
- **10 min. Look at a plant.** Read it at the soil to the whole centimeter and fill a dated line, [[s05-E1]] and [[s05-E2]].
- **5 min. Look back.** **Say.** "What do we think about the grass under the board, and what can we not tell?" Expect: missing light may explain it, but the board alone does not show the cause.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer words per box; never cut a window, the graph or the plan. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Healthy plant, pale, wilted, same, different, record. Pointing and drawing are accepted. Directions are read aloud. A scribe writes only the child's words.

### Tomorrow

No exit today; the drafts and the observation lines are marked. Day 55 takes the other three tables to the plants, hands back the written next step on each plan for a revision, and opens the invented later records.
