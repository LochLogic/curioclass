# Day 55: Finish the record, revise the plan and explain

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, the adult sets the labelled cups, a tray and five rulers at the other three tables and checks for mould. Seal the invented record in an envelope. Day 54's assessment, the plan sheet, comes back marked with a next step: put each at its place. Today samples days 48 to 54 and teaches nothing.

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on for each plant turn. Soil stays in the tray, rulers below faces, spills wiped at once, hands washed at the next washing. The adult lifts a table's cover for its window only and says how long. The adult removes any mouldy cup. The first 15 children handle no soil or cover today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Read aloud, neutrally: no hint, no reteaching, chart covered. Say the envelope stays sealed until the plans are revised, and that its pictures are invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Three windows of 4 minutes for the other three tables (15 children), as on day 54: two rounds of 2 minutes, a plant each at the soil. Each child enters the last dated line of the record, [[s05-E13]], and watch each about 30 seconds (5 x 30 s = 2.5 minutes a window). Unscored. The first 15 add their final line from the measured photograph of their own plant, alone at their seats. Their day 54 records are kept once, not redone. Where a child's day 52 steps were corrected, append what you see to the day 52 record.

#### 3 min. Moves and wipe-down

Two moves of 30 seconds. Goggles wiped and bagged, tables, spills and hands wiped; hands are washed at the next washing, said aloud.

#### 15 min. Revise and interpret

All 30 children alone, on paper. They read the written next step on the plan sheet and revise it (8 minutes). Then open the envelope and read the three cards aloud once (1 minute). The children show on the student page what the comparison with the sun-and-water plant shows about light, [[s05-E15]], and about water, [[s05-E16]], by drawing or a few words (6 minutes). Unscored. You hear no one; a child who needs a scribe has an adult write the words and add none. Collect the plan sheets, record sheets and student pages.

### Marking

After class, 45 minutes: mark the eight plan components of the revised plan and the two conducting components from the day 52 record with its appended steps, [[s05-E14]]: shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point (ten points). Sort [[s05-E13]] for error E, [[s05-E15]] for error C and [[s05-E16]] for error D. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No plants: use dated measured photographs said aloud to be kept records; the two conducting components are not marked, and the record says so. The eight planning components and the interpretation run as written.

### At home

**Swap.** Any small plant for the class cups, or a drawn plant with its height written.

**Skip.** The windows and the sealed envelope.

- **25 min. Assessment.** The child reads the written next step and revises the plan sheet alone. Give no hint.
- **15 min. Interpret.** Read these invented records aloud: sun with water, 10 centimeters, green, firm; P behind a screen with water, 15 centimeters, pale, weak; Q in sun with no water, 4 centimeters, wilted. **Say.** "What does sun with water against P show about light?" Expect: only light differs; P is pale and weak, so a plant needs light. The child answers [[s05-E15]] and [[s05-E16]].
- **5 min. Plant.** Read a plant at the soil, [[s05-E13]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Healthy plant, pale, wilted, same, different, record. Pointing and drawing are accepted. Directions are read aloud.

### Tomorrow

No exit today. Day 56 returns each record with one written next step and repairs by error: A and B from the plans, C from [[s05-E15]], D from [[s05-E16]] and E from the records, then retries and writes the class explanation.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
