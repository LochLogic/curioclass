# Puppet-stage record (day 55)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Sealed in an envelope. Opened only after children have revised their plans on day 55, and read aloud.

**Three cards, dated after fourteen days:**
**Card S, the added plant:** in sunlight with two spoonfuls daily, 10 centimeters, green, five leaves, firm stem.
**Card P, behind the screen with water:** 15 centimeters, pale, three leaves, a weak leaning stem.
**Card Q, in sunlight with no water:** 4 centimeters, wilted, three limp leaves.
**Say:** "Use these later records and your revised model to explain each completed comparison." Unscored; the teacher hears no one.
