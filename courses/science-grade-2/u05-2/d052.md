# Day 52: What is different from the healthy plant?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set cups, a tray and a damp cloth at each table of five, and a dated table and feedback form at each place. Pin the chart. Sort day 51's tickets by error. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on to measure. Soil stays in the tray, rulers below faces, spills wiped. Nothing is tasted; hands washed before the next food or break. A child with a plant allergy uses the pictures. The adult handles covers and, gloved, bags any moldy cup unopened. The cover is up only to measure; say how long when reporting the dark plant.

### The day

#### 3 min. Read the feedback

Each child reads or hears the next step on their day 50 line (1 minute). Today's six say theirs back, 20 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Error A and error B, 3 minutes each; the commoner runs whole class, the other in a small group.
Error A: lay pair M beside pair N. **Ask.** What is different in each pair? Expect: M, only the light; N, the cups and soil too.
Error B: lay the Day 12 corn cards side by side. **Ask.** What is different besides height? Expect: color, leaves and stem.

#### 17 min. Revise and gather

Each child revises their day 50 line from their next step (3 minutes) and holds the look-for they chose on day 51 beside their reading; at the table they share which one a reading could challenge. Goggles on (1). The adult lifts the covers (1). Two rounds of 3 minutes (6): at each table three children read a plant each at the soil and write the whole centimeter beside a sketch while two sketch; then the other two read. The adult covers the dark cups (1). The table shares its readings aloud; each child fills three dated lines and writes how the dark and dry plants differ from the healthy plant, never dark against dry (5). Watch Today's six, 30 seconds each (3 minutes): follows the measuring steps; makes a dated line. A gain under 1 centimeter is "same". A failed plant is swapped for a spare and the line says "replaced".

#### 6 min. Discuss the evidence and revise the model

Six tables answer (3 minutes). **Ask.** What is different from the healthy plant, and what is the same? Expect: the table's own records, one plant against the healthy, no repeats. An arrow turns solid only where the records show a difference; else it stays dotted: "I cannot tell yet."
Pip says, "One week is enough to be sure about every plant." **Ask.** Do our records show that? Expect: only where a record differs from the healthy (3 minutes).

#### 3 min. Exit

Say: invented for practice. Read [[s05-X52a]] once (0.5 minute); all show yes or not yet. Read [[s05-X52b]] (0.5 minute); all show. Three of Today's six give reasons (2 minutes). Mark on the one scale.

#### 5 min. Clean up

Bag wiped goggles (1 minute). Wipe tables, spills, then hands (3). The adult takes trays and covers (1).

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark each line on the two look-fors; write one next step per child for day 53.

### Other rooms

No plants: use dated measured photographs said aloud to be kept records; the conducting look-fors are not marked, and the sheet says so.

### At home

**Swap.** A small plant of any kind for the class cups, or two drawn plants, one green and one pale.

**Skip.** The goggles, the covers and the table talk.

- **5 min. Repair.** Draw two plants that differ in one thing only. **Say.** "What is different, and what is the same?" Expect: one thing is different; the cup, soil and water are the same.
- **15 min. Measure.** Read the plant at the soil to the whole centimeter and fill the dated line. Write what is different from a healthy plant.
- **10 min. Talk.** **Say.** "What is different from the healthy plant, and what is the same?" Expect: the answer from the line, even if nothing is different.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X52a]], then [[s05-X52b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer table reports; never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Healthy plant, different, same, replaced (a spare swapped in and marked). Frames: "The ___ plant is different in ___." "I cannot tell yet, because ___."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says yes too soon: error C for the dark plant, error D for the dry plant. Day 53 opens with both, 3 minutes each, then reads a second case on paper and hands back each child's day 52 line.
