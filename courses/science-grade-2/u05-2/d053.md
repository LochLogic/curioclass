# Day 53: Why might the taller plant be less healthy?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set one oat card set on each table of five. Pin the chart with its condition row. Sort the day 52 tickets by error C and error D. Hand out each child's day 52 line with your written next step. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only; no plants and no goggles today. Count each card set back at the end.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Error C and error D, 3 minutes each; if many made one, it runs whole class and the other goes to a small group.
Error C: lay the corn Day 1 card beside the Day 12 card of the covered plant. **Ask.** Was the covered plant pale on Day 1? Expect: no, it looked fine at first; touch the pale one on Day 12.
Error D: draw on the board a dry plant upright on Day 3 and limp on Day 12, beside a watered green one on both days. **Ask.** How did the dry plant look at first, and later? Expect: upright, then limp; standing up today is not the end of the record.

#### 3 min. Retrieve the records

Each child finds their day 52 line and the next step written on it.

#### 7 min. Revise the day 52 records

Alone, on paper, from the next step (4 minutes). Move among the tables and note revisions. Today's six say what they changed, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 12 min. Read a second case

Say the cards are invented for practice. Tables mark what changed, what stayed the same and what is watched on the oat cards (7 minutes); visit each of six tables for 1 minute. Then the class answers (5 minutes). **Ask.** Which oat plant is taller on Day 14? Expect: the shaded one, 22 and 20. **Ask.** Why might the taller plant be less healthy? Expect: it is pale, bent and falling over with thin leaves; the lit one is green and firm. Only the light is different; the water is the same.

#### 9 min. Discuss and revise the model

Pip says, "The taller plant is the healthier plant." **Ask.** Does that fit the Day 14 oat cards? Expect: no; error B is saying yes (3 minutes). Add the oat case to the condition row: taller, pale, weak (2 minutes). Today's six say, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___," 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Hand the cards back (1 minute).

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented for practice. Read [[s05-X53]] once over the basil cards (0.5 minute); all show yes or no (0.5 minute). Today's six give a reason, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark on the one scale.

### Marking

After class, 20 minutes: note the revised day 52 lines and mark the exit. Error B goes to day 56.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three oat cards on scrap paper, a shaded plant and a lit plant for each day.

### At home

**Swap.** Two drawn plants for the oat cards, one tall and pale, one shorter and green.

**Skip.** The table marking and the class chart.

- **5 min. Repair.** Draw a plant that looks fine today and limp later. **Say.** "Is it fine for good?" Expect: not yet sure; keep looking.
- **15 min. Cards.** Read the Day 1, Day 7 and Day 14 oat cards. Fill the three boxes: what changed, what stayed the same, what we watch.
- **10 min. Say.** **Say.** "Which plant is taller, and which looks healthier?" Expect: the shaded one is taller; the lit one is healthier.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X53]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read Day 14 only; never cut the exit. Longer: say what else could be watched.

### Words and supports

Shaded, lit, taller, healthier, pale, firm, bent. Frame: "The ___ plant is taller, but ___." A scribe is allowed.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says no because the height is the same: error B. Error B goes to day 56. Day 54 is a check day: it observes a class plant and graphs one date.
