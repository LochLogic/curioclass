# Day 53: Why might the taller plant be less healthy?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set one oat card set on each table of five. Pin the chart with its looks row. Sort the day 52 tickets by error C and D. Hand out each child's day 52 line with your written next step. Choose Today's six. Key: key-oat-cards-day53.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only; no plants and no goggles today. Count each card set back at the end.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Error C and error D, 3 minutes each; if many made one, it runs whole class and the other goes to a small group.
Error C: lay the corn Day 1 card beside the Day 12 card of the covered plant. **Ask.** Was the covered plant pale on Day 1? Expect: no, it looked fine at first; touch the pale one on Day 12.
Error D: draw on the board a dry plant upright on Day 3 and limp on Day 12, beside a watered green one on both days. **Ask.** How did the dry plant look at first, and later? Expect: upright, then limp; standing up today is not the end of the record.

#### 3 min. Retrieve the records

Each child reads their written next step to a partner (1 minute) and says what they will fix (1 minute); a child who is waiting for a small group starts finding their day 52 line.

#### 7 min. Revise the day 52 records

Alone, on paper, from the next step (4 minutes). Note revisions as you move. Today's six say what they changed, 30 seconds each (3 minutes).

#### 12 min. Read a second case

Say the cards are invented for practice. Say: "This is the fair test from unit 2." Tables mark "We kept the same / We changed / We watch" on the oat cards (4 minutes): same is the water, cup and soil; changed is the light; watch is colour, leaves and stem. "What changed since last time" is only for dated growth. Visit six tables, 1 minute each. The class fills the Day 14 table together at the chart and circles taller and healthier (3 minutes). Then the class answers (5 minutes). **Ask.** Which oat plant is taller on Day 14? Expect: the shaded one, 22 and 20. **Ask.** Why might the taller plant be less healthy? Expect: it is pale, bent and falling over with thin leaves; the lit one is green and firm. Only the light is different; the water is the same.

#### 9 min. Discuss and revise the model

Pip says, "The taller plant is the healthier plant." **Ask.** Does that fit the Day 14 oat cards? Expect: no; error B is saying yes (3 minutes). Add the oat case to the looks row: taller, pale, weak (2 minutes). Today's six say, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___," 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Hand the cards back (1 minute).

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented for practice. Read [[s05-X53a]] over the June 2 cards; all touch one card (0.5 minute). Read [[s05-X53b]] over the four basil cards; all show yes or no (0.5 minute). Today's six give a reason for the second, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Mark on the one scale.

### Marking

After class, 20 minutes: note the revised day 52 lines and mark the exit. Error B goes to day 56.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three oat cards on scrap paper, a shaded plant and a lit plant for each day.

### At home

**Swap.** Two drawn plants for the oat cards, one tall and pale, one shorter and green.

**Skip.** The table marking and the class chart.

- **5 min. Repair.** Draw a plant that looks fine today and limp later. **Say.** "Is it fine for good?" Expect: not yet sure; keep looking.
- **15 min. Cards.** Read the Day 1, Day 7 and Day 14 oat cards. Fill the three boxes: we kept the same, we changed, we watch.
- **10 min. Say.** **Say.** "Which plant is taller, and which looks healthier?" Expect: the shaded one is taller; the lit one is healthier.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X53a]] and [[s05-X53b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read Day 14 only; never cut the exit.

### Words and supports

Shaded, lit, taller, healthier, pale, firm, bent. Frame: "The ___ plant is taller, but ___."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the taller plant is healthier or says no because the height is the same: error B. Error B goes to day 56. Day 54 is a check day: it observes a class plant and graphs one date.
