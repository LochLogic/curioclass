# Repair, retry and exit cards (day 59)

Held back. No case from days 57 or 58. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except a date or where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. One repair set for each of five tables.

- **Group A.** Hills, flat land and a lake as loose cut-outs, a river arrow card and a photograph of a made-up river valley.
- **Group B.** The snowball, a puddle and ice in a row; a page that can answer and one that cannot.
- **Group C.** A lump and a sheet of one thing; a noodle before, heated and cooled.
- **Group D.** An old and a new photograph side by side; words that tell how fast.
- **Group E.** Runs with a printed limit of 7; a job card matched to a test card.
- **Retries.** One card for each group: landforms, the snowball and puddle, the boiled potato slice, the beach dune photograph, and lids A and B.
- **Exits.** A Pell Hollow arrow card, a map card and a poster card, a juice pop card and a pancake card, and fence P and fence Q.
