# Check cards, days 57 and 58

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 57. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except a date or where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Day 57 and day 58 each print one card sheet a child; the day 58 sheets stay sealed until day 58.

## Day 57: Tern Coast, Fox Valley, the snowball and the noodle

Printed one card sheet a child: side 1 holds Tern Coast in a box; side 2 holds the three Fox Valley cards as three small boxes labeled map, caption and poster, and the snowball and noodle records as two strips of three small pictures. The day 58 sheets stay sealed.

- **Tern Coast.** A picture of a coast: a long high hill on the left, flat land in the middle with a bending river that runs into the ocean and a lake in a hollow beside it, and the ocean on the right. Caption: Rain that falls on the long hill ends up in the ocean.
- **Fox Valley.** Three cards. The map card: a keyed map with a blue line (the river), a blue patch (a pond) and a white patch (a snowfield). The caption card: In winter the top of the pond is solid ice and the river still flows. The poster card: a poster for the Fox Valley Fair that shows a prize cow, with no facts about water.
- **Snowball record.** Three small dated pictures: a snowball on a plate, a puddle on the plate after a day in a warm room, and a sheet of ice on the plate after a cold night outside.
- **Noodle record.** Three small dated pictures: a hard dry noodle, the noodle soft after a grown-up heated it in a bowl of hot water, and the soft noodle after the bowl cooled.

## Day 58: Marl Point, two barriers and a cover

Printed one card sheet a child: side 1 holds the Marl Point cards and the sandbar; side 2 (folded closed until the barriers) holds the two barriers and the three cover strips with their numbers and limits. Every number a child must use is printed on the sheet as well as read aloud.

- **Marl Point.** Three cards, each a before picture and an after picture drawn side by side. Size and speed cross: one small fast change, one large slow change and one large fast change.
  - Gate card: a small mound of dirt by a gate, then the same mound with a notch in its side. Caption: one hard rain in one morning cut this notch.
  - Ridge card: a long rocky ridge with sharp points, then the same ridge with rounded points. Caption: wind and rain rounded this ridge little by little.
  - Field card: a wide smooth field, then the same field with a long deep cut across it. Caption: one night of heavy rain made this cut.
  - Sandbar card: a sandbar seen on Monday, Tuesday and Wednesday, three identical small pictures, and an older photo beside them with the sandbar much farther out. Caption: the sandbar looks the same each day.
- **Two barriers.** Barrier H and barrier J, each drawn as a small bar card with three numbers (first run, second run and path used) and both printed limits (lose at most 4 centimeters each run, use at most 6 centimeters of the path). Barrier H: lost 1 then 2, path used 9. Barrier J: lost 6 then 3, path used 2.
- **Cover strips.** Straw, bark and plastic, each with two numbers and the two printed limits (water climbs 3 centimeters or more; slides at most 2). Straw: climbed 5, slid 1. Bark: climbed 2, slid 4. Plastic: climbed 0, slid 8.
