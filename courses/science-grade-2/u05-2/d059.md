# Day 59: Repair, retry and say what we can do now

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 57 and day 58 pages by letter into five groups: A with B, C and D (the model); B with F and G (the sources); C with H and I (the changes); D with J and K (fast and slow); E with L and M (the barrier and the cover). Write each child's group and one next step on their page. Set one set of repair cards at each group table. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and read the feedback

Each child gets day 57's assessment and day 58's assessment back and reads or hears the one written next step on their page. Today's six say theirs back, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 13 min. Return and repair

Five groups, each with you in turn while the rest work from picture directions at their table: A 3 minutes, B 3, C 2, D 2, E 2, then 1 minute to move. Group A: place hills and flat land around the water, trace the low way with a finger, set the model beside the photograph. Group B: hold up the page that can answer, lay the snowball, puddle and ice in a row, find what only the caption tells. Group C: a lump and a sheet of one thing, a noodle before, heated and cooled. Group D: the old and new photo side by side, the words that tell how fast. Group E: mark each run against its limit, match the job to its test. A child with no gap draws a fresh coast.

#### 8 min. Fresh retries

Each child retries only the missed group: A [[c1-X59e]], B [[c1-X59f]], C [[c1-X59g]], D [[c1-X59h]], E [[c1-X59i]]. Read the asks aloud (2 minutes); children touch or say yes or no (3). Six rotating children say their reason, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Collect (1). A retry is recorded apart and never rescores a day 57 or 58 item.

#### 6 min. Exit

Say every card is invented for practice. Read [[c1-X59a]], [[c1-X59b]], [[c1-X59c]] and [[c1-X59d]] one at a time; all touch (3 minutes). Six rotating children say why for [[c1-X59c]], 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Collect (1).

#### 4 min. Record the gain

Each child draws or ticks one thing they can do now that they could not on day 57 or 58 (3 minutes). Collect (1).

### Marking

After class, 20 minutes: mark the four exits on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record each retry apart. Record what was shown on their own and what was shown with help in separate columns; never rescore days 57 and 58.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on scrap paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Any drawn arrow, map and two bowls for the cards.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the retry.

- **5 min. Next step.** Read the written next step aloud together.
- **15 min. Repair.** Draw a hill, a river and a lake with a key.
  **Say.** "Which way does the water go, and how do you know?" Expect: down the low way, because water runs downhill.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[c1-X59a]], [[c1-X59b]], [[c1-X59c]], [[c1-X59d]] and the retries [[c1-X59e]], [[c1-X59f]], [[c1-X59g]], [[c1-X59h]], [[c1-X59i]] aloud, one at a time. The child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair for 2 minutes a group; never cut the retry or the exit. Longer: a fresh coast with two rivers and a key.

### Words and supports

Model, key, route, source, liquid, solid, limit, returned. Frames: "I can now ___." "It stays under the limit, because ___." Pointing is accepted.
