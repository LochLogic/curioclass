# Oat cards (day 53)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Printed for each table. Two matched oat seedlings, equally watered, one in a shaded corner and one in the sun, on three dated cards.

**Day 1:** both seedlings the same size, 5 centimeters, two green leaves.
**Day 7:** the shaded one is 18 centimeters, pale, bent, with two thin leaves. The lit one is 15 centimeters, green, upright, with four leaves.
**Day 14:** the shaded one is 22 centimeters, falling over, with two pale leaves. The lit one is 20 centimeters, with five green leaves, firm.
**Say:** "This is the fair test from unit 2. Mark we kept the same, we changed and we watch. Why might the taller plant be less healthy?"
