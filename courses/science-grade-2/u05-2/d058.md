# Day 58: Marl Point, two barriers and a cover

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 57 hands on nothing to day 58. Set the sealed sets. Cover the walls. Seat each child so they see only their own page. On the record sheet choose Today's six for each block, not heard on day 57. No plant task and no unit artifact is re-scored today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 8 min. Directions

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. Every card is new and invented for practice. You may pass." Read the three Marl Point captions twice. Show where answers go on the page and where a circle goes.

#### 12 min. Fast and slow

Read [[c1-F58a]]; children circle two cards (2 minutes). Read [[c1-F58b]]; they circle one card and write the words that tell (2 minutes). Read [[c1-F58c]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words (2 minutes). Read [[c1-F58d]]; everyone writes or draws one fast and one slow (3 minutes). Collect (1 minute).

#### 15 min. Two barriers and a cover

Read the barrier card aloud twice with its limits (2 minutes). Read [[c1-B58a]], [[c1-B58b]], [[c1-B58c]] and [[c1-B58d]] one at a time; children write a few words with a number for each (8 minutes). Today's six say [[c1-B58b]], 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes of those 8). Read the cover card with its two limits twice; read [[c1-W58a]]; children circle a strip and write the two numbers that tell (3 minutes). Read [[c1-W58b]]; they write a few words (2 minutes). Collect as each child finishes.

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the ten items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: J and K in the events, L in the barriers, M in the cover. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each. Response on day 59 only.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three Marl Point cards and the barrier and strip numbers on the board; answers go on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a bank cut by a flood and a worn step. Write the barrier numbers on paper.

**Skip.** The sealed sets and Today's six.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each ask aloud once and wait. Your child answers [[c1-F58b]] and [[c1-W58b]], then the sandbar, the two barriers and the cover, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "What is a fast change and what is a slow one?" Expect: one night or one afternoon, and little by little.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fast, slow, limit, barrier, strength, weakness, straw. Frames: "It does well because ___." "It does poorly because ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 is not scored in class. Day 57's assessment waits for day 59, which hands the pages of days 57 and 58 back with one written next step, repairs by letter group, retries and takes four fresh exit cards.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
