# Repair set (day 56)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One set for each letter group, 2.5 minutes with the teacher in turn while the others work from picture directions at their tables. The cases here are new and are not the exits or the retries.

**Group A with E:** three dill cups: S in the sun, watered; T under a box, watered; U in the sun, no water. Touch the plant to compare with S for light, and the plant to compare with S for water; T and U are never compared. Then a modeled complete entry for a kale seedling: date, sun with water, 13 centimeters, six green upright leaves drawn in the leaf box, a straight stem drawn in the stem box.
**Group B:** the day 53 oat cards beside the day 51 corn cards. Touch each difference besides height.
**Group C:** a violet watered every day and kept in a closet, dated Day 1 green and Day 10 pale and limp. Touch the picture that tells whether water is all a plant needs.
**Group D:** a geranium in a sunny window with no water, dated Day 1 firm and Day 10 limp. Touch the picture that tells whether sun is all a plant needs.
**Key (teacher):** A: S with T for light, S with U for water; the entry has leaves and stem beside the height. B: the taller covered corn and oat plants are pale and weak, so look at color, leaves and stem. C: the Day 10 picture. D: the Day 10 picture. One child follows the same steps one-to-one with an adult.
