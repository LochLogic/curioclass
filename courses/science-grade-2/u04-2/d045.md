# Day 45: The rocket made new

## Teacher page

### Before class

Round every card corner. Set each child's rocket (two rectangles, two triangles, one square) flat on their desk in the same arrangement, under the record, and check it yourself. Tables 1 to 3 are watched today. Put the familiar cards in an envelope for tables 4 to 6. Keep the day 46 bus sets sealed.

### Safety and set-up

Pieces have rounded corners and stay on desks. No goggles are needed. Nothing is heated or tasted.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Read aloud, neutrally, one step at a time. Say the case is invented for practice. Give no hints and do not teach today. Read [[s04-E1]] once; all thirty draw the rocket as it is.

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Tables 1 to 3 are watched in three windows of 4 minutes. In a window the five read [[s04-E2]] and [[s04-E3]] and take the rocket apart and make a different whole while you watch all five. Hear one brief reason from each, [[s04-E6]], when its whole is made: 5 x 30 s = 2.5 of the 4 minutes. Mark live: apart, a different whole, the reason heard. Tables 4 to 6 work alone on the familiar cards: chocolate and egg, marking went back or stayed changed. Builds stay private.

#### 3 min. Moves and pack up

Two moves of 30 seconds (1 minute). Then 2 minutes to count five pieces in each new whole and cover it with the record.

#### 20 min. Accounts

The fifteen watched finish their record (14 minutes): draw what changed, [[s04-E4]]; match the pieces by lines, [[s04-E5]]; finish "This shape does ___ because it is ___" (a scribe where wanted). Tables 4 to 6 draw and match a familiar build on the same sheet, unscored. Then everyone, in pairs, explains the three anchor changes with the models on the wall (6 minutes): castle to boat, chocolate, egg, 1 minute each child for each. Unscored; listen, mark nothing, and do not point to the models. Then collect the records.

### Marking

After class, allow 45 minutes to mark both records once. Mark each look-for on the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only shown counts; keep helped and independent work apart. The observation sheet names who was watched and who was heard. Tables 4 to 6 are assessed on day 46.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two boxes on folded paper. No card: cut five pieces from packaging.

### At home

**Swap.** Packaging card for the card pieces.

**Skip.** The anchor talk with a partner.

- **25 min. The assessment: build and tell.** A grown-up cuts two rectangles, two triangles and a square from card and rounds the corners. Set a rocket, then ask aloud, one at a time: draw it, take it apart, make a different thing from the same five, draw what changed, match the pieces, say what suits a piece. Give no hints. **Say.** "Which pieces did you use?" Expect: the same five.
- **20 min. Talk.** Look at a picture of the castle and the boat. Ask what stayed the same and what changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: never cut a window or the record; shorten the pair talk to 4 minutes. Longer: none; day 47 takes extensions.

### Words and supports

Piece, same, different, stiff, floppy, round, flat. Frame: "My rocket became a ___. I used the same pieces." Read every label aloud; pointing and a scribe are allowed.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 finishes the same record: tables 4 to 6 build an unseen bus in windows, and everyone argues from five heating and cooling records. Nothing is taught. Day 47 returns this record with one next step.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
