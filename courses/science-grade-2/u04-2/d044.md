# Day 44: What would you need to see?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count eight evidence cards into an envelope for each group. Draw a blank consensus chart with three boxes: the account, the parts and whole, and the cause. Have Pip ready. Pin the day 43 change model where all can see it. Choose Today's six. Key: key-evidence-cards-day44.

### Safety and set-up

Paper cards only. Nothing is heated, opened or eaten. Count the cards back at the end.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 43's exit showed who said cooling fixes it (error C). Show the egg before cooking and after cooking and cooling. **Ask.** Did cooling make the egg runny again? How do you know? Expect: no; it stayed firm after cooling. Cooling did not undo the cooking.

#### 4 min. Pip says one line

Pip says: "Everything we heat goes back when it cools." The class checks the line against two cards from its own records, the chocolate and the paper, and says what each shows. Expect: the chocolate went back; the paper stayed changed. Pip is right only sometimes.

#### 9 min. Evidence cards

Groups of five sort their eight cards into went back, stayed changed and not enough yet. A card with no attempted return cannot go in went back. The teacher stays about 1.5 minutes at each of six tables (6 x 1.5 = 9 minutes). Say what a child's pile shows; do not move cards for them.

#### 8 min. Say the argument

Pairs say "I think ___ went back, or stayed changed, because I saw ___, so ___," each child 1 minute, all pairs at once (2 minutes). The so is the cause: heating or cooling. Today's six say it to the class, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). The class says whether the evidence supports it (3 minutes). The claim, seeing and reason are spoken first.

#### 7 min. Consensus

Join three things on one chart: our account of the pieces, our parts-and-whole sketch, and our cause. Write: the same pieces can make a different whole; heating or cooling can cause a change that goes back or stays. Children say it in their words; the teacher writes.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Put the three glue cards on the wall. Read [[s04-X44]] once; all point to yes or no and whisper what changed to a partner (1 minute). Today's six say what they see, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack up

Count the cards back into the envelopes.

#### 2 min. Board

Answer the fifth strip: what evidence settles a disagreement? A record that tried to bring it back.

### Other rooms

No printed cards: draw each on a folded paper, before, after, and a third box only if one was tried.

### At home

**Swap.** Drawn cards for the printed set.

**Skip.** The chart.

- **10 min. Sort.** Lay out four cards: the chocolate, the egg, the water cup, the paper. Sort into went back, stayed changed, not enough yet.
  **Say.** "What did we do to try to bring it back?" Expect: warmed or cooled it, then looked again.
- **10 min. Say it.** Say: "I think ___ stayed changed, because I saw ___, so ___."
- **10 min. The card with no third look.** Hide the third picture of the water cup. **Say.** "Can we say it went back?" Expect: not yet.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X44]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards; never cut the card with no third look or the exit. Longer: draw a new card for a change at home and sort it.

### Words and supports

Went back, stayed changed, not enough yet, evidence, cause. Frame: "I think ___ went back, or stayed changed, because I saw ___, so ___." Read every card aloud; pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a new outline means the glue did not go hard again; day 47 repairs that with a solid lump and a solid sheet. Days 45 and 46 are checks: the teacher gives no hints and the chart is not pointed to.
