# Familiar cards (days 45 and 46)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Unscored pair talk before the end items. Two cards per pair, from the unit, none of them the end-item cases.

**Card 1:** the chocolate set from day 39. **Card 2:** the egg set from day 39.
**Say:** "In pairs, tell each other which one went back and which one stayed changed. Say what you saw and what was done."
**Key (teacher):** Chocolate went back, the egg stayed changed. Take only a listening note; this talk is not scored.
