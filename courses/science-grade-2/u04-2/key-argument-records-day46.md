# Argument records (day 46)

**Key (teacher):** Butter and juice returned; cookie and cucumber remained; the second butter is not enough yet because there is no cooling record. Causes: heating made the solid butter liquid; heating made the soft dough a firm cookie; cooling made the liquid juice solid; cooling made the firm cucumber slice soft, and it stayed soft after thawing. A general claim: heating and cooling can cause some changes that go back and some that stay.
