# Evidence cards (day 44)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Eight cards per group. Each card has a short label and a small picture sequence. Children sort them into three piles: went back, stayed changed, not enough yet.

1. **Castle, boat, castle:** blocks as a castle, then a boat, then the castle again. Went back.
2. **Chocolate:** hard, warmed and liquid, cooled and hard. Went back.
3. **Water cup:** liquid, frozen, then warmed and liquid again. Went back.
4. **Egg:** raw, cooked, then cooled and still firm. Stayed changed.
5. **Leaf:** firm green lettuce, frozen by a grown-up, then warmed and still limp. Stayed changed.
6. **Paper:** white, heated with a brown mark, then cooled and still marked. Stayed changed.
7. **Ice cup after waiting:** liquid, frozen, then a card marked "waited, no third look" with the cup still frozen. Not enough yet.
8. **Blocks, castle to boat, no rebuild:** a castle, then a boat, with no card showing the castle again. Not enough yet.

**Say:** "Pip says the ice cup went back. Check it against the card. Sort all eight, then tell us one claim with evidence and a reason."
