# Day 46: The bus made new, and what heat can do

## Teacher page

### Before class

Round every card corner. Set each bus (two rectangles, two circles, one square) flat on the desks of tables 4 to 6 under the record, and check each yourself. Keep day 45's assessment records apart and untouched. Put the argument records on every desk face down. Tables 1 to 3 get the familiar cards.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Pieces have rounded corners and stay on desks. Nothing is heated, opened or tasted. No goggles are needed.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Read aloud, neutrally, one step at a time. Say every case is invented for practice. Give no hints and do not teach. Open the sealed bus now. Read [[s04-E7]] once; tables 4 to 6 draw the bus. Then read the label of each of the five argument records once.

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Tables 4 to 6 are watched in three windows of 4 minutes, as on day 45. The five read [[s04-E8]] and [[s04-E9]] and take the bus apart and make a different whole. Hear one brief reason from each, 5 x 30 s = 2.5 of the 4 minutes. Mark live: apart, a different whole, the reason heard. Tables 1 to 3 finish the familiar cards alone; nothing from day 45 is rescored or repeated.

#### 3 min. Moves and pack up

Two moves of 30 seconds (1 minute), then 2 minutes to count five pieces in each new whole and cover it with the record.

#### 5 min. Finish the bus record

Tables 4 to 6 draw what changed, [[s04-E10]]; match the pieces by lines, [[s04-E11]]; and finish "This shape does ___ because it is ___" [[s04-E12]] (a scribe where wanted). Tables 1 to 3 finish their familiar cards.

#### 15 min. The argument

All thirty, alone, on paper, at once; same time for everyone. Show the five records. Re-read each ask in turn (about 3 minutes): [[s04-E13]], [[s04-E14]], [[s04-E15]], [[s04-E16]], [[s04-E17]], [[s04-E18]], [[s04-E19]], [[s04-E20]], [[s04-E21]] and [[s04-E22]]. Children mark and write a few words; a scribe or spoken answer is allowed. Hear no one and give no hint. Collect the records at the end.

### Marking

Allow 45 minutes after class to mark both records once. Mark each look-for on the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only shown counts; keep helped work apart. The sheet names who was watched and who was heard. A card with no cooling record is not enough yet, never no.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each record as three boxes. No card: cut packaging.

### At home

**Swap.** Packaging card for the card pieces.

**Skip.** The windows and the record sheet.

- **20 min. The assessment: the bus.** Cut two rectangles, two circles and a square. Set a bus and have the child draw it, take it apart, make something different from the same five, draw what changed and match the pieces. Give no hints. **Say.** "Which pieces did you use?" Expect: the same five.
- **20 min. The records.** Read the five record cards aloud. Ask for each: did it go back, and what shows it? Butter and juice went back; cookie and cucumber stayed; the second butter needs a cooling picture.
- **5 min. Talk.** Look at the answers together. Name one thing to try next.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: never cut a window, the bus record or the argument. Longer: none; day 47 takes extensions.

### Words and supports

Returned, remained, enough, not enough yet, cooled, heated, same pieces. Frame: "It returned, because ___." Read every label and ask aloud; pointing and a scribe are allowed.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 returns the day 45 and day 46 records with one next step each. Group A with E, group B, group C and group D repair with the teacher, and a fresh unscored retry follows. Day 46's items are answered there, not today.
