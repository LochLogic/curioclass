# Day 47: Back to the evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write one next step on each feedback form from the day 45 and day 46 records, and put each child in one letter group: A with E (pieces), B, C or D (heating and cooling), from the record sheet and the day 44 exit slips. Set each group's repair set at its table. Pin the consensus chart. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper, string and card only. Nothing is heated, opened or tasted. Keep blocks on tables below faces and string short, away from necks and mouths. Count strips and cards back at the end.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and read feedback

Each child gets day 45's assessment record and day 46's assessment records, and a form with one next step for one component, and reads or hears it. Today's six say theirs back, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Repair by letter groups

Each group tries its cards at its table from the pictures. Then each has the teacher for 3 minutes in turn: group A with E, then B, C, D (4 x 3 = 12 minutes) with 3 minutes to move.
A and E: string from each block of the castle to the boat; then a stiff and a floppy strip as signs for an upright sign.
B: a solid lump and a solid sheet from one melted chocolate sample; both hard, so the state came back.
C: the egg before cooking and after cooking and cooling; cooling did not undo it.
D: two cards, then a third card turned face up after the temperature is changed back.
If over a third made one letter (error A, B, C, D or E), its first 5 minutes are whole class and the four turns shrink to 2 minutes.

#### 10 min. Fresh retries

Each child answers only the component their feedback names, on the retry cards, unscored. Read the asks aloud, one at a time: [[s04-X47a]], [[s04-X47b]] for pieces and [[s04-X47c]], [[s04-X47d]] for heating and cooling (2 minutes). Children mark or touch on paper (4 minutes). Today's six say their reason, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Collect the cards (1 minute). Record each retry apart; it never rescores the item.

#### 5 min. Closing explanation

The class dictates and you write: the same pieces can make a different whole (days 40, 41); heating and cooling sometimes restore what we saw, tried by changing the temperature back (days 42, 44); cooling did not uncook the egg (day 43). **Ask.** Which day's record shows it? Each child copies or has scribed one line.

### Other rooms

No string: draw the lines. A child working one to one with an adult follows the same steps.

### At home

**Swap.** Packaging card and wool for the strips and string.

**Skip.** The letter groups.

- **10 min. Next step.** Read the written next step with your child. Try it with the cards from day 45 or 46.
- **10 min. Repair.** Match each piece of the castle to the boat with string. Look at the egg before and after cooking and cooling. **Say.** "Did cooling undo it?" Expect: no; it stayed firm.
- **10 min. Say it.** Say together: same pieces; heating or cooling can change things back; the egg stayed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X47a]], [[s04-X47b]], [[s04-X47c]] and [[s04-X47d]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: shrink each turn to 2 minutes; never cut the retry or the closing explanation. Longer: after the retry, make a third arrangement from the check set and say which piece suits its use.

### Words and supports

Same pieces, went back, stayed changed, not enough yet, because. Frame: "I know ___ because ___." Read every card aloud; pointing and a scribe are allowed.

### Tomorrow

Unit 4 is done. Day 48 starts unit 5 with pale grass under a board and a plan for separate light and water comparisons. Keep the explanation chart: unit 5 asks for claims with evidence again.
