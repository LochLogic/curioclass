# Day 43: Does every change go back?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, an adult freezes one lettuce leaf in a sealed bag overnight, thaws it, and photographs it before and after. Keep a never-frozen leaf. An adult cooks the egg at home or in a kitchen and photographs it. Seal both displays and set each on its tray. Check egg and lettuce allergies. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

All heating and freezing is done before class by an adult. Children look at sealed displays and photographs, and open nothing. No flame is in the room. Nothing is eaten. Clean-up is three minutes below.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 42's exit showed who said freezing alone sent the water back (error D). Show the cup of liquid water and the cup frozen. **Ask.** Did the water go back? What do we need to see first? Expect: warm it and look again. Then turn the third card: liquid again. That third look is the attempted return.

#### 8 min. The egg

Each group has the sealed cooked-egg display and the three egg photographs. Pass the display, 30 seconds a child (5 x 30 s = 2.5 minutes). **Ask.** What did the heat do? We cooled it. Did cooling undo it? Expect: firm and white; no. Groups draw the egg's third look on the change-arrow sheet and circle stays changed.

#### 8 min. The leaf

Each group has two sealed leaves, one frozen then thawed, one never frozen, and the leaf photographs. Pass the pair, 30 seconds a child (2.5 minutes). **Ask.** What did the cold do? We warmed it back. Is it firm again? Expect: limp; no. Cooling caused a change that stayed.

#### 6 min. The paper

Heated paper is photographs only. Groups lay the three in a row: pale, browned by an adult's heating, cooled and still brown. They mark each: changed, tried cooling, stayed changed.

#### 6 min. Lasting branches

Add a stays changed branch to the class change model and pin the egg, leaf and paper on it (3 minutes). Today's six say "I think ___ stays changed, because I saw ___," 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Say the claim and the seeing aloud before anyone draws.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Put the three corn cards on the wall. Read [[s04-X43]] once; all point to yes or no with both hands (1 minute). Today's six say what they see, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Clean up

The adult collects the sealed displays and discards them (1 minute). Children wipe hands with a damp cloth (1 minute). Nothing was opened. Hands are washed at the next scheduled washing, said aloud. Wipe the tables (1 minute).

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the fourth strip: does that always work? No.

### Other rooms

No sealed displays: use the day 39 egg set and the leaf and paper photographs. No printer: draw the three-card rows.

### At home

**Swap.** Drawn pictures for any item you cannot get.

**Skip.** The class model.

- **10 min. Egg.** A grown-up cooks an egg the day before. Look at it cooled. **Say.** "Did cooling make it runny again?" Expect: no; it stayed firm.
- **10 min. Leaf.** A grown-up freezes a lettuce leaf in a bag overnight, then thaws it. Look at it beside a fresh leaf. **Say.** "Is it firm again?" Expect: no; limp.
- **10 min. Paper.** Look at the paper photographs. Mark: changed, tried cooling, stayed changed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s04-X43]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pass the displays 20 seconds a child; never cut the attempted return or the exit. Longer: another thing a grown-up heated that stayed changed.

### Words and supports

Changed, tried cooling, stayed changed, lasting. Frame: "I think ___ stays changed, because I saw ___." Read every label aloud; pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says cooling fixes it (error C); day 44 repairs it with the egg before and after cooking. Day 44 sorts eight evidence cards into went back, stayed changed and not enough yet.
