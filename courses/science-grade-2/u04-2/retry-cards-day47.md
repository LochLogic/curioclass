# Retry cards (day 47)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Fresh cards for the missed component only, read one ask at a time, unscored. The cases are new and are not the end items or the exits. Items s04-X47a to s04-X47d hold the asks and keys.

**Retry A (the same pieces):** a card windmill of four triangles and one rectangle, beside a card turtle made from the same five pieces. Asks: touch the pieces that are the same in both; then pick one piece and say why its shape or the stiff card suits its new job.
**Retry B (heating and cooling):** three cards: an ice star that melted and froze again as a block; pale bread heated into brown toast that stays brown after it cools; a melted chocolate sample whose cooled picture is hidden. Asks: touch each change that came back; then say yes or no, does cooling always make things go back, and touch the picture that tells.
**Key (teacher):** Retry A: the rectangle and the four triangles are in both, and one piece has a shape or stiffness reason. Retry B: only the ice block came back; no, because the toast stayed brown; the chocolate is not enough yet.
