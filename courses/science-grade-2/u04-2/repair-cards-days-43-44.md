# Repair cards (days 43 and 44)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Used for the five-minute repairs of errors D and C. The egg pictures come from the day 39 hook pictures.

## Error D repair (day 43): two cards and a third face down

**Card 1, labelled cup first:** a cup of liquid water. **Card 2, labelled cup second:** the same cup with the water frozen. **Card 3, face down, labelled third:** the cup on a table with a wavy line for warm and liquid water again; the teacher turns it over only after the class says what a third card must show.
**Say:** "The first two cards: did the water go back? Why or why not? Now the third."
**Key (teacher):** Two cards alone do not show it. The third card, after warming and a look, shows it went back.

## Error C repair (day 44): the egg

Use Set 3 of the day 39 hook pictures: raw, cooked, cooked and cooled.
**Say:** "Did cooling make the egg runny again? How do you know?"
**Key (teacher):** No. The cooked egg stayed firm after cooling, so cooling does not undo everything.
