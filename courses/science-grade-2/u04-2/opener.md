# Section 4.2 opener: heating, cooling and the third look

## What the section does

Five days, 43 to 47. Day 43 asks what a third card must show before a change counts as going back, using photographs of a leaf and heated paper beside the egg. Day 44 sorts eight evidence cards into went back, stayed changed and not enough yet, and the class agrees one claim with evidence and a reason. Days 45 and 46 are the end of the unit, two cases of rebuilding from the same pieces and an argument about five records. Day 47 returns feedback, repairs by letter group and tries a fresh retry.

## How the days run

Days 43, 44 and 47 open with five minutes of return and repair or feedback. Days 45 and 46 have no opener. Read every stem aloud, one ask at a time, and say that every picture is invented for practice. Mark with one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Children read only short labels. On days 45 and 46 do not coach: the look-for lists decide what is marked.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Some heating changes can be undone and some cannot.** Cooling returns melted solids and warmed liquids; cooling does not return a cooked or burned thing.
- **A claim needs a record, a reason and a third look.** Say what you saw before and after, and what was done to try to bring it back.
- **Not enough yet is an honest answer.** A picture with no attempted return cannot sort as went back.
- **A new outline need not mean a new state.** A cooled solid can be hard in a different shape.

## Safety and set-up

All heating is done by an adult before the children come in; children see photographs and sealed samples. Nothing is eaten and nothing sealed is opened. Check egg, milk and nut allergies first, offer the photograph path to any child, and ask no one what is eaten at home. Cards have rounded corners, spills are wiped at once, and hands are washed at the next scheduled washing.
