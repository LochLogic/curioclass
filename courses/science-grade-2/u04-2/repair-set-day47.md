# Repair set (day 47)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

For the repair by letter groups. Each group has the cards for its letter.

**Group A:** the castle and boat pictures with string, one piece per place.
**Group B:** a solid lump and a solid sheet, both made from one melted chocolate sample.
**Group C:** the egg cards, before and after cooking and cooling.
**Group D:** two cards and a turned third card, as on day 43.
**Group E:** a stiff and a floppy strip and a card record each.
**Say:** "Show your group's answer. Tell why."
