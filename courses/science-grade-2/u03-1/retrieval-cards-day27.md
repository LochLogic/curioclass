# Retrieval cards (day 27)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Three cards, shown one at a time while you read the line aloud. These are earlier-grade ideas only, not tests of this unit.

## Card 1: a day-length chart

**Picture:** A bar chart of hours of daylight, one bar for a winter month and one for a summer month, the summer bar longer.
**Say:** "Which month has more daylight? Point."
**Key (teacher):** The summer bar.

## Card 2: a shape and its job

**Picture:** A drawing of a spoon and a fork side by side.
**Say:** "Point to the one with a bowl that holds soup."
**Key (teacher):** The spoon. Its shape fits the job.

## Card 3: a light test

**Picture:** A window with a sheet of clear plastic wrap held in front of one half and a wooden board held in front of the other half. Light shows through the wrap and not through the board.
**Say:** "Which one let the light through? Point."
**Key (teacher):** The plastic wrap.
