# Day 29: Ice and water

## Teacher page

### Before class

Before class an adult checks every seal and the lukewarm water by hand, and keeps a spare sealed cup of each on a cold pack. Fill the trays. Pin the pattern chart and the question board. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Splash goggles on for all. Lukewarm water only, sealed; cups are never opened or tasted. Ice is held briefly. A child who dislikes cold uses a mitt. Wipe condensation and any drop at once. Clearing trays is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 28's exit, error D takes this part. Show the day 29 repair card: plan A tapes the lamp and swaps a clear and a frosted sheet; plan B moves the lamp. **Ask.** Which plan is fair? Expect: plan A; the lamp stays, only the sheet changes. A child tapes the lamp on the picture.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Show the pond pictures: a winter pond with a skin of ice, a summer pond with flowing water. Say they are invented. **Ask.** Point to the one where water flows. Expect: the summer pond; the winter pond holds its shape. Solid water and liquid water are review. A miss: compare a second pair on the board.

#### 4 min. Hook

Hold up the sealed ice cup and the sealed lukewarm cup. **Ask.** Are these the same stuff? Take guesses. Pip says: "The ice cup is not water." The class waits for evidence (error C).

#### 12 min. Observe

Each child tilts each cup once and holds the ice cup for 20 seconds; five children, about 3 minutes of handling in all, the rest looking and talking. The cups stay sealed. The group draws on its observation chart what each cup does when tipped: keeps its shape, or flows to fit the cup. Spare cup or the water cup pictures if a seal leaks or the ice has melted; say they are pictures.

#### 6 min. Classify

Groups mark each cup solid or liquid and say what shows it. Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes): "The ___ cup is ___ because it ___." Add to the pattern chart: the same water has different properties when cooler and when warmer. Cooler and warmer are words only; nothing is measured.

#### 3 min. Shared record

One sentence a group, which you scribe on the class record: "The ice ___ but the warmer water ___." (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 4 min. Clear trays

Wipe condensation with the cloth, cups into the return tray, hands wiped.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 29 exit picture, read [[s03-X29]] once; everyone points to yes or no. Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No sealed cups: a zip bag of ice and a zip bag of lukewarm water, sealed. Or the water cup pictures.

### At home

**Swap.** No cups: a sealed bag of ice and a sealed bag of lukewarm tap water, never hot. A cold hand: use a mitt.

**Skip.** The groups and the class record.

- **10 min. Tip and hold.** Tilt each sealed bag. Hold the ice bag for 20 seconds. Never open or taste.
  **Say.** "What does each one do when it tips?" Expect: the ice keeps its shape; the warm water flows.
- **8 min. Solid or liquid?** Your child draws both on the student page.
  **Say.** "Which is solid, and which is liquid?" Expect: ice is solid; the warmer water is liquid.
- **8 min. Same water.** Let the ice melt in a bowl.
  **Say.** "Is it still water?" Expect: yes; ice is water that is solid.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X29]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tilt only, no drawing. Never cut the tipping or the exit. Longer: watch a bag of ice melt.

### Words and supports

Solid, liquid, flows, keeps its shape, cooler, warmer. Frame: "The ice ___ but the warmer water ___." Pointing is fine; a mitt for the ice.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the ice is not water (error C). Day 30 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then tests how far water climbs strips and which hat pieces keep a paper tab dry.
