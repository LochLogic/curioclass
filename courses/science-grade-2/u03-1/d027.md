# Day 27: The soggy hat

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, dampen a paper hat on a paper doll in a tray for each group. Fill the six-sample trays; keep the exit's felt and foam out. Hang the word cards face down; pin the question board. Choose Today's six. Unit 2 ended on day 26 and hands nothing on.

### Safety and set-up

Samples are new and blunt: no glass, no sharp metal, never near mouths. The hat is damp, not dripping; nothing is poured, so goggles stay on the shelf. Wipe drops at once.

### The day

#### 7 min. Hook

Say you dampened the hats to show what a drizzle did. Each child touches the hat with one finger (5 x 10 s, 1 minute).

**Ask.** What happened to the hat and the doll? Expect: wet, soft, soggy. 

Ask once, leave it open: "Why are rain boots made of rubber and mittens of wool, and not the other way round?" Correct nothing.

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Show the retrieval cards one at a time; children point: which month has more daylight; which has a bowl for soup; which lets light through. Read the unit 2 strip, "Everything stays the same except one thing"; the class says it back. A miss: draw a second pair.

#### 10 min. Look, touch, name

Read safety sheet lines 1 and 5. Children touch the samples gently and sort them any way they notice (4 minutes). Turn the word cards over: colour, rough, smooth, hard, soft, bends, stiff. A property is something we can see or feel about a material, and a test can show more. Children say each word as they touch a sample (3 minutes); tests come tomorrow.

Pip says, one line: "These two are both blue, so they are the same all over." Groups feel their same-colour pair side by side.

**Ask.** Are they the same all over? Expect: no; colour tells only colour (error B).

Pip says: "Rubber is my favourite, so make everything from rubber." Show a pillow card and a door prop card; children point to the word card each job needs.

**Ask.** What do we decide first? Expect: the job, then what to test (error E).

#### 8 min. First choice

On the first-choice hat sheet each child draws a hat from one sample, all the same size, and copies its label. No right answer. Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each, and write their "because" (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes); the rest tell a partner. Any child may pass. Collect the sheets, unscored; they return on day 35. This is step one of the design cycle.

#### 4 min. Question board

Each group adds one wonder (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Post: What properties can we observe? The class agrees four tests for tomorrow, drawn on the board: press, gentle scratch, bend, light through.

#### 2 min. Pack up

Samples counted back, trays wiped, hands wiped.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 27 exit picture, read [[s03-X27]] once; everyone points to yes or no. Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No trays: pass six household pieces.

### At home

**Swap.** No doll: draw one. No sample set: use a wooden spoon, a metal spoon, a plastic lid, a rubber band, a cloth, a paper towel.

**Skip.** The trays and the question board.

- **5 min. The soggy hat.** Dab a paper hat on a paper doll with a little water.
  **Say.** "What happened to the hat?" Expect: wet, soft, soggy.
- **10 min. Feel and name.** Touch each thing; your child circles two words on the page for each.
  **Say.** "Is it rough or smooth? Hard or soft?" Expect: honest words.
- **5 min. Same colour.** Find two things of one colour.
  **Say.** "Are they the same all over?" Expect: no; colour tells only colour.
- **8 min. First choice.** Your child fills the first-choice hat sheet.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X27]] aloud, alone: "Two squares are both blue. Do they feel and bend the same? Yes or no?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one Pip line. Never cut the first choice or the exit. Longer: sort by a second property.

### Words and supports

Material, property, job, and the seven word cards. Frames: "It is ___ but that one is ___." "I chose ___ because ___." Pointing is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says two things of one colour feel and bend the same (error B). Day 28 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then runs the four agreed tests.
