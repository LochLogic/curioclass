# Safety sheet (days 27, 28, 29, 30, 33, 34, 36)

Read each line aloud with the picture. Children point to the picture for each line.

1. Samples stay on the tray. We never taste them.
2. We press gently with the blunt stick, down low, away from faces. (Picture: a stick pressing a flat sample on a table.)
3. The light shines on the sample, never at eyes. (Picture: a flashlight pointing at a paper screen.)
4. Sealed cups stay shut. (Picture: a closed cup with a lid on a tray.)
5. If water spills, tell a grown-up and wipe it at once. (Picture: a cloth on a drop of water.)
6. Hands and feet stay clear when a block is placed. (Picture: hands behind the back.)
