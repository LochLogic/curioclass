# Repair cards, days 28 to 31 (return and repair)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Each repair takes five minutes at the start of the next day, for the letter most children showed. None of these pairs is the pair on the exit.

## Day 28 repair, letter B: two yellow samples

**Picture:** A yellow cloth square and a yellow plastic lid, side by side, with a hand bending the cloth and a hand pressing the lid.
**Say:** "These are both yellow. Bend one, press one. Do they feel the same? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No. The cloth bends and the lid stays stiff. The class says colour told only the colour.

## Day 29 repair, letter D: two light plans on a different sheet pair

**Picture:** Plan A: a lamp taped to a book stack with a clear sheet and a frosted sheet taking turns in front. Plan B: the lamp drawn at two places, one for each sheet.
**Say:** "Which plan is fair? Tape the lamp on the picture."
**Key (teacher):** Plan A. The child puts a paper tape on the lamp in plan A.

## Day 30 repair, letter C: one cup of water in three pictures

**Picture:** Three cards of one cup: a solid block of ice shaped like the cup, the same ice partly melted, and the cup full of flowing water.
**Say:** "Is it the same water in all three? Point to yes or no, then tell a neighbour what stayed the same."
**Key (teacher):** Yes. The water stayed the same; only how it holds its shape changed.

## Day 31 repair, letter F: two pictured strips

**Picture:** Strip 1 with a wet mark climbing inside it. Strip 2 with a pool of water at the bottom and a dry strip above.
**Say:** "Trace where the water went. Which strip took water in? Point."
**Key (teacher):** Strip 1; the water climbed up into it. In strip 2 the water stayed outside.
