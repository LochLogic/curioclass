# Invented strip and hat records (day 30, fallback)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Used if the class numbers fail. Read it as if the class had measured it.

| Piece | Water climb (cm) | Tab under the hat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| paper towel | 8 | wet |
| cotton fabric | 5 | wet |
| wool | 3 | dry |
| foil | 0 | dry |
| plastic | 0 | dry |
| rubber | 0 | dry |

**Key (teacher):** Any piece with 0 took no water in. The paper towel took in the most, and the doll got wet under it. For the rain hat, foil, plastic and rubber kept the tab dry.
