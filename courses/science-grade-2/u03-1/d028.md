# Day 28: Test it and sort it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape each flashlight on a stack of books, beam level, a paper screen behind. Lay out the trays, sticks, charts and book pages. Hang the property word cards, the pattern chart and the glass picture card (a window pane and a jar). Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Impact eye protection stays on from the first test to the count-back. Press and scratch gently with the blunt stick, down low on the tray, away from faces. Bend gently; never stretch rubber. The light never points at eyes. No glass in hands. Samples never go near mouths. Pack-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 27's exit, error B takes this part. Show the day 28 repair card, a yellow cloth and a yellow plastic lid. Children bend one and press one at the table. **Ask.** Do they feel the same? Expect: no; the cloth bends and the lid stays stiff; colour told only colour.

#### 4 min. Model the tests

Show each test once, slowly. Press: the stick, gently; a dent or none. Scratch: gently; a mark or none. Bend: gently; bends or stays stiff. Light: the flashlight stays on its stack, only the sample is held in front of it; the light words are review: lets light through, lets some through, blocks light. This is a fair test: keep one thing still, change only the sample. Show rough beside hard: a rough fabric dents, a smooth wooden block does not (error A). Show the glass card.

#### 12 min. Test

Six groups of five, in two passes of 6 minutes. In pass one each child owns one property for all six samples, about 1 minute a sample: colour, rough or smooth, hardness (press and scratch), bends or stiff, light. The stick child and the light child work at the same time. In pass two each child takes the next property round. A dent or a scratch mark goes in the press box.

#### 4 min. Record

Each child ticks their tests on the group's sorting chart, one column a property. Leave a box blank if unsure.

#### 6 min. Sort

Each group sorts the six samples by one property it chooses and finds one property two different materials share. Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes): "These are alike in ___ but different in ___." If many say rough means hard, press one more rough-soft and smooth-hard pair. Add a shared property to the pattern chart.

#### 3 min. Book page

Each group fills one sorting book page: a picture, a label, and one sentence you scribe (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 3 min. Pack up

Samples counted back, sticks and flashlights returned, eye protection off, hands wiped.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 28 exit picture, read [[s03-X28]] once; everyone touches a plan. Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No flashlight: a lamp on a stack of books. No chart: rule one on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No stick: the blunt end of a wooden spoon. No flashlight: a lamp, never at eyes. No chart: rule one on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the book page.

- **12 min. Test.** Pick six safe things. Press with the spoon end, bend, shine the light through, feel.
  **Say.** "Does it dent? Does it bend? Does light get through?" Expect: honest yes or no for each.
- **8 min. Sort.** Your child sorts them by one property and tells you which two share one.
  **Say.** "What is alike, and what is different?" Expect: alike in one property, different in another.
- **5 min. Page.** Your child draws two of them on the student page and fills the frame.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X28]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three properties, not five. Never cut the fair light test or the exit. Longer: sort by two properties at once.

### Words and supports

Dent, scratch mark, bends, stiff, lets light through, blocks light. Frame: "These are alike in ___ but different in ___." A helper presses while the child decides.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who moves the flashlight for each sample (error D). Day 29 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then watches water in a sealed ice cup and a sealed lukewarm cup.
