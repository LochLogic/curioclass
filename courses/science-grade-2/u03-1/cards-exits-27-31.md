# Exit cards, days 27 to 31

Each exit has a picture card every child marks, and a spoken stem read once. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Day 27 is practice only. The teacher hears a rotating six. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

## Day 27 exit card: two blue squares

**Picture:** A blue sponge and a blue board side by side, the same shade of blue. Under them, three cards: a colour swatch (look at the colour), a finger pressing down (press), and a ruler beside a square (look at the size).
**Say:** "Pip wants to know which one is softer. Which test tells: look at the colour, press, or look at the size? Point."
**Key (teacher):** Press, with the words that colour tells only the colour. Look at the colour is error B. Look at the size tells how big, not how soft.

## Day 28 exit card: two light plans

**Picture:** Two cards side by side. Plan 1: a flashlight on a stack of books, a thin paper sheet drawn between the flashlight and a screen, and a thick card sheet leaning beside it. Plan 2: the same flashlight drawn closer to the card sheet than to the paper sheet, with an arrow showing it moved.
**Say:** "We want to know which sheet lets more light through. Touch the fair plan."
**Key (teacher):** Plan 1, with the words that the flashlight stays put and only the sheet changes. Plan 2, because closer shows it better, is error D.

## Day 29 exit card: one puddle

**Picture:** Two cards. Card 1: a puddle at dawn with a thin skin of ice, labelled morning. Card 2: the same puddle at the same place, flowing, with ripples, labelled noon. Under them, two circles: yes, no.
**Say:** "Pip says the ice in the puddle is not water. Is Pip right? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No, with the words that ice is water that is solid and the same water flows at noon. Yes is error C.

## Day 30 exit card: two new strips

**Picture:** Two cups with a strip standing in each, drawn from the side, each with a cup line marked on the strip. Strip K: a clean strip with no wet mark above the cup line. Strip L: a strip with a wet mark climbing to the 6 line above the cup line. A small ruler beside each, zero at the cup line.
**Say:** "Touch the strip that took water in."
**Key (teacher):** Strip L, because the water climbed up into it. Strip K, because nothing climbed so it took in the most, is error F.

## Day 31 exit cards: two bars, then two hats

**Part a picture:** Blank axes on squared paper, one square for each centimeter, and a bottom line labelled zero. Two labels under the axes: strip M, strip N. Say: "Strip M and strip N both had water climb 4 centimeters. Draw the two bars."
**Key (teacher):** Two bars of equal height, each 4 squares, one for M and one for N, both starting at the bottom line. A different height has no letter: note it on the drawing and the child redraws at the table.

**Part b picture:** Two cards. Hat M: a doll under a hat with a dry paper tab beneath. Hat N: a doll under a hat with a wet tab beneath. Under them, two circles: hat M, hat N.
**Say, part b:** "The water climbed the same in both strips. Touch the hat to use."
**Key (teacher):** Hat M, because the doll stayed dry. Hat N, because it takes water in, is error H.
