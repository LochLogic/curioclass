# Property word cards (days 27 and 28)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures.

Seven large cards for the wall, each with one word and one picture. Read each word aloud when you hang it.

- **color**: a row of crayons.
- **rough**: a close picture of a coarse sack cloth.
- **smooth**: a close picture of a clean, flat tile.
- **hard**: a stone being pressed by a stick, no mark on it.
- **soft**: a cushion with a dent from a stick.
- **bends**: a ruler-sized strip curving over a hand.
- **stiff**: a strip held out flat and straight.

**Key (teacher):** A property is something we can see, feel or test. Do not hang any card with a sample's name on it; the words belong to the sample's properties, not to one material.
