# Bar retrieval (day 31)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

**Picture:** A two-bar picture graph on squared paper, one square for each unit, labeled "children who like apples" and "children who like pears". The apple bar is 5 squares tall and the pear bar is 3 squares tall.
**Say:** "Which bar is taller? How many squares taller? Point and say."
**Key (teacher):** The apple bar, 2 squares taller.
