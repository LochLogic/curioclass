# Water pictures (day 29, fallback if the cups fail)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Used only if a sealed cup leaks or the ice melts before class. Say aloud that these are pictures, not the cups.

**Picture 1:** A sealed cup tipped on its side with a block of ice that stays in the same shape.
**Picture 2:** A sealed cup tipped on its side with the water flowing to the lower side.

**Say:** "Which cup holds its shape? Which flows?"
**Key (teacher):** Picture 1 holds its shape (solid). Picture 2 flows (liquid). The same water can be either.
