# Day 49: Three cups

## Teacher page

### Before class

Seven days before, the adult sows radish seeds; the day before, the adult chooses three matched radish plants, dampens the soil and checks each cover for light leaks, stability and heaters. Label cups healthy, dark and dry; set cups, tray and spoon at each table. Pin the day 48 chart. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on. Soil stays in the tray, rulers below faces; wipe spills at once. Nothing is tasted. Wash hands after soil and plants. Check the nurse's list for plant and pollen allergies; a child who reacts uses the pictures. The adult places the covers; stop for an unstable one.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many made error A on day 48's exit, take it whole class; if few, a small group at your table. Lay the dark cup beside the dry cup. **Ask.** What is different? Expect: the light and the water, so not fair. Lay the dark cup beside the healthy cup. **Ask.** What is different now? Expect: only the light.

#### 3 min. Retrieve

Show the plant strip, 9 centimeters.
**Ask.** Point to where zero meets the soil. Expect: the soil line.
**Ask.** What number does the top reach? Expect: 9.
**Ask.** Which bar is that long? Expect: the middle bar.
A miss gets the second strip, top at 6.

#### 4 min. Plan the cups

Name the three cups on the chart. Hold up one plant and put the ruler's zero at the soil once. Read the safety sheet's eight lines with the pictures. Table roles: soil, plant, water, measure, record. Each missing-need plant is compared only with the healthy plant, never dark with dry.

#### 13 min. Set up

Goggles on (1 minute). Level the soil, set and firm the plant, tray under the cups (5 minutes). The water child gives the healthy and dark cups the agreed level spoonfuls and the dry cup none; the table writes the amount (1 minute). Measure in two rounds of 2 minutes (4 minutes): three children read a plant each at the soil, then the other two read the same plants. The adult covers the dark cups and sets trays in the window (2 minutes); say the cover lifts only to measure. Visit six tables, 2 minutes each.

#### 7 min. Starting records and model

Each child fills the starting record for the plant they read: every box on the sheet (4 minutes). A height alone is error E: ask for leaves and stem. Today's six say their entry, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the others check the boxes. Add the three cups to the chart (1 minute).

#### 3 min. Exit

Read [[s05-X49a]] once over the bean cups; all show 1, 2 or 3 fingers (0.5 minute). Three of Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (1 minute). Read [[s05-X49b]]; all draw on the ticket (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Clean up

Wipe goggles with one damp cloth a table and bag them (1 minute). Wipe tables and spills (3 minutes). The adult takes trays and covers (1 minute). Hands are washed straight after, table by table.

### Other rooms

No plants: use dated measured photographs, said aloud to be kept records.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw three cups for seedlings, and a box with holes for the cover.

**Skip.** The clean-up part and the table roles.

- **5 min. Repair.** Show three cups: healthy, dark, dry. **Say.** "Which two make a fair test of light?" Expect: healthy and dark; only light differs.
- **12 min. Cups.** Fill the student page for each cup. If you have seedlings, set up three, with a ruler at the soil. Do not taste soil or plants, and wash hands after.
- **8 min. Record.** Fill the starting record: date, cup, height, leaves, stem.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X49a]] aloud, then [[s05-X49b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three spoken entries; never cut the measuring or records. Longer: write the spoonful schedule.

### Words and supports

Healthy, dark, dry, soil, ruler, centimeter, zero, leaf, stem. Frame: "My plant is the ___ plant." A scribe is allowed.

### Tomorrow

Error A: leaving the dark cup dry. Error E: writing only a height. Day 50 repairs both, then measures the same cups and fills the dated table.
