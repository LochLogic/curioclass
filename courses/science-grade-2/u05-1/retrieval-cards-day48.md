# Retrieval cards (day 48)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Four cards, one ask each, marked in turn, about a minute each. A miss gets a modeled second picture in the last minute.

**Card 1:** a plant, a rock, a toy car and a puppy. Ask: "Mark what a plant needs." Key (teacher): the sun and water pictures hold the answer; the card shows a sun and a watering can beside the four, mark sun and water.
**Card 2:** a drawing of a small plant with five arrows. Ask: "Point to the roots. Point to the leaves." Key: the roots are under the soil; the leaves are above.
**Card 3:** two pictures of a lamp-and-cloth test, like the one from first grade. In the left picture two cloths are held, one after the other, at the same spot in the same lamp beam, one thick and one thin. In the right picture the lamp is also moved closer for the second cloth. Ask: "Touch the picture that changes only the cloth." Key (teacher): the left picture; only the cloth changes, the lamp and the spot stay the same.
**Card 4:** two bars side by side, one long and labeled summer day, one short and labeled winter day. Ask: "Mark the longer daylight bar." Key: the summer bar.
