# Plant strip (day 49)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One paper strip for the teacher to hold up, with a drawn plant standing in a drawn cup. Beside it a ruler is drawn in whole centimeters from 0 to 12. The plant's top reaches the 9 mark. The soil line is at 0.

**Say:** "Show where zero meets the soil. Read this whole number. Match it to a bar." Next to the strip hang three bars of 5, 9 and 12 squares.
**Key (teacher):** zero is at the soil line; the number is 9; the middle bar. A miss gets a second strip with the top at 6.
