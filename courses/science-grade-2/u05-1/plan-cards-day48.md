# Plan cards (day 48)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One set per table of five. Each card shows one potted seedling with a label. The planning chart outline is drawn once on large paper by the teacher: three columns headed healthy plant, no-light plant and no-water plant, and rows headed what we keep the same, what we change and what we watch.

**Card H, the healthy plant:** a seedling in a cup in a sunny window, three drops falling into the cup.
**Card L, the no-light plant:** a seedling in a cup under a box with air holes, three drops falling into the cup.
**Card W, the no-water plant:** a seedling in a cup in a sunny window, no drops, a small crossed-out drop.
**Say:** "Place one card in the chart and say what it keeps and what it changes." Each child places and says one card, 30 seconds each.
**Key (teacher):** Card L changes only the light, so it is compared with Card H. Card W changes only the water, so it is compared with Card H. Card L and Card W are never compared with each other, because they differ in two things.
