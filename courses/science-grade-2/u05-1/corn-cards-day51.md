# Corn pair and series cards (day 51)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Printed for each table of five. Labels only.

## Three pair cards

**Pair 1:** two corn seedlings in matching cups with the same soil, both in a sunny window. One gets a level spoonful of water and one gets none.
**Pair 2:** a corn seedling and a marigold seedling in matching cups with the same soil and the same water. The corn sits in the window and the marigold sits under a box with air holes.
**Pair 3:** two corn seedlings in matching cups and soil. One sits in the window with a spoon of water; one sits under a box with no water.
**Say:** "Mark fair or not fair. Then touch what is different."
**Key (teacher):** Pair 1 is fair; only the water is different. Pair 2 is not fair; the kind of plant is different as well as the light. Pair 3 is not fair; the light and the water are different. Error A is calling pair 2 or pair 3 fair.

## The series

Two matched, equally watered corn seedlings, one covered and one lit, on three cards.

**Day 1:** both 2 centimeters, two small green leaves.
**Day 6:** covered 8 centimeters, pale, two thin leaves. Lit 7 centimeters, green, three leaves.
**Day 12:** covered 19 centimeters, pale, leaning, two thin leaves. Lit 16 centimeters, green, firm, four leaves.
**Say:** "Mark colour, leaves and stem with the symbols. Which is taller? Which looks healthier? Say why."
**Key (teacher):** the covered plant is taller; the lit plant is healthier, because it is green and firm with more leaves. Error B is saying the taller plant is the healthier one. Pip says "the plant that grew the most is the healthiest"; check the line against the Day 12 card.
