# Day 48: Why is the grass pale?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 47 ended unit 4 and hands on nothing. Pin the board pictures. Draw the planning chart large: columns healthy, no-light and no-water plant; rows keep the same, change, watch. Set plan cards on each table. Choose Today's six. Key: key-plan-cards-day48.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only; no goggles today. Count the plan cards back.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

Show the same lawn on its first and last day, board lifted (1.5 minutes). Say the pictures are invented. **Ask.** Why is the grass under the board yellow and pale when the grass around it is green? Offer no cause. Partners tell what they see (1 minute). Four children say, 30 seconds each (2 minutes). Write ideas unsorted.

#### 5 min. Retrieve

Four review cards, a minute each, all pointing. Mark misses on the record sheet.
**Ask.** Mark what a plant needs. Expect: sun and water.
**Ask.** Point to the roots. Then point to the leaves. Expect: under the soil; above.
**Ask.** Touch the picture that changes only the cloth. Expect: the left picture.
**Ask.** Mark the longer daylight bar. Expect: the summer bar.
Draw a second picture for a miss.

#### 12 min. Plan fair comparisons

Class (3 minutes): put the healthy-plant and no-light cards on the big chart; say the grade 1 words again: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. **Ask.** What is the one thing we change? Expect: the light.
Tables (6 minutes): five children, five turns of 30 seconds. Three place the healthy, no-light and no-water cards and say what each changes; two say who the no-light and no-water plants are compared with. You visit six tables, 1 minute each.
Show (3 minutes): two rotating tables show theirs, 45 seconds each. Pip says, "No light and no water is a fair test." **Ask.** What is different between the no-light plant and the no-water plant? Expect: light and water, so not fair (error A). Each child marks one choice.

#### 9 min. Needs-arrow model and question board

Draw a plant with a dotted arrow from the sun and one from the water; dotted means not seen yet. Children draw theirs (4 minutes). Sort the ideas (2 minutes): light and water can be tested with plants; warmth and weight stay as questions. The board changed light, rain and perhaps warmth together, so no one cause shows. Today's six say, "I think ___, because I saw ___," 30 seconds each (3 minutes).

#### 4 min. Exit

Read [[s05-X48]] once over the three pots (1 minute). All show the light pair, then the water pair, with fingers for the pot numbers. Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Show the bucket-lawn picture and read [[s05-X48b]]; every child says what they see to a partner (0.5 minute); the teacher hears the six as they pair up.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards. No photographs: use the drawn pair.

### At home

**Swap.** Draw the grass picture for the board photographs.

**Skip.** The table show and the question board.

- **6 min. Look.** Draw grass that is green all round, with a pale patch where a board lay. **Say.** "What do you see?" Expect: pale under the board, green around it.
- **10 min. Plan.** Fill the chart on the page for a healthy plant, a no-light plant and a no-water plant. **Say.** "Which two do we compare to test light?" Expect: healthy and no-light; only the light differs.
- **8 min. Arrows.** Draw a plant with two dotted arrows, sun and water.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X48]] and [[s05-X48b]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: show one table; cut the board sort, never the card placing or the exit.

### Words and supports

Plant, light, water, dark, dry, pale, same, different. Frames: "The ___ plant has ___ but not ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___."

### Tomorrow

Error A: joining the two plants that each lack a need. Day 49 opens with a 3-minute return and repair, then sets up three real cups.
