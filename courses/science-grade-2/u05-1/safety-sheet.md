# Safety sheet (days 49, 50, 52, 54, 55)

Read each line aloud with the picture. Children point to the picture for each line.

1. Goggles on before we touch soil or plants. (Picture: a child with goggles, a bag with a name on it.)
2. Soil stays in the tray. (Picture: a cup of soil in a tray.)
3. We never taste seeds or plants. (Picture: a mouth with a line through a leaf.)
4. Rulers stay below our faces. (Picture: a child holding a ruler low beside a cup.)
5. The grown-up lifts the dark cover. It has air holes. (Picture: a grown-up lifting a box with holes.)
6. If water spills, tell a grown-up and wipe it at once. (Picture: a cloth on a drop of water.)
7. If you see fuzzy mould, do not touch. Tell a grown-up. (Picture: a cup with a fuzzy spot, a hand held back.)
8. Hands are washed at the next washing time. (Picture: hands under a tap.)
