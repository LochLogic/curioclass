# Board pictures (day 48)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two pictures of the same patch of lawn, labelled first day and last day. Use the teacher's own dated photographs where there is a lawn; where the ground is frozen, use these drawn pictures.

**Picture 1, first day:** a lawn of even green grass with a flat wooden board laid in the middle, a ruler beside it, a date card reading "Day 1".
**Picture 2, last day:** the same lawn two weeks later, the board lifted and leaned on a fence. Under the board's outline the grass is pale, drawn with thin dashed blades. Around it the grass is green. A date card reads "Day 14".
**Say:** "Here is the same grass on two days. What do you see?" Take ideas, accept none, write them unsorted.
**Key (teacher):** the grass under the board is pale and the grass around it is green. No cause is offered. The board changed light, rain and perhaps warmth together, so it cannot show which one mattered.
