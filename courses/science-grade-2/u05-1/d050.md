# Day 50: Measure and write it down

## Teacher page

### Before class

The adult inspects the plants, labels the cups and keeps the agreed care between meetings. Set cups, tray and spoon at each table. Put one dated table at each place. Sort the day 49 tickets by error A and error E. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on. Soil stays in the tray, rulers below faces; wipe spills at once. Nothing is tasted. Wipe hands with a damp cloth; wash before the next food or break, said aloud. The adult lifts and replaces the covers. The adult, gloved, bags any moldy cup unopened and washes; a child with a mold allergy or asthma moves away. Clean-up is the last 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair, then the procedure

If many made one error on day 49's exit, that error takes its 3 minutes whole class and the other goes to a small group.
Error A (3 minutes): lay the dark cup beside the dry cup, then beside the healthy cup. **Ask.** What is different? Expect: light and water, then only light.
Error E (3 minutes): draw a cup with only "7 centimeters". **Ask.** What belongs in the leaf box and the stem box? Expect: the leaves and the stem, drawn.
Procedure (2 minutes): each child tells a partner the ruler steps, 1 minute each way: zero at the soil, read the top, write the whole centimeter.

#### 16 min. Measure, sketch and record

Goggles on; the adult lifts the covers (1 minute). Two rounds of 3 minutes (6 minutes): three children read a plant each at the soil while two sketch it, then the other two read the same plants while the first three sketch. The adult covers the dark cups after round two. Each child enters the date, condition, height to the whole centimeter and appearance, with the sketch (5 minutes); hear Today's six at the tables, 25 seconds each (6 x 25 s = 2.5 minutes). The water child gives the healthy and dark cups the agreed spoonfuls, the dry cup none; the table writes the amount (2 minutes). The adult sets the trays in the window (2 minutes).

#### 8 min. Discuss the evidence and revise the model

Six tables answer in turn, 40 seconds each (6 x 40 s = 4 minutes). **Ask.** What changed since day 49? **Ask.** What stayed the same? A gain under 1 centimeter reads "same"; say so as true. Add the dated heights to the chart. Keep each arrow dotted unless a record shows a difference (4 minutes).

#### 3 min. Exit

Read [[s05-X50]] once over the ruler photograph (0.5 minute). Each child fills the line on the ticket (2 minutes). Collect (0.5 minute). Mark on the one scale.

#### 5 min. Clean up

Wipe goggles, one damp cloth a table, and bag them (1 minute). Wipe tables, spills, then hands (3 minutes). The adult takes trays and covers (1 minute). Say hands are washed at the next washing time.

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: read each dated line for date, condition named, height from the soil, leaves and stem. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Write one next step on each feedback form for day 52.

### Other rooms

No plants: use dated measured photographs, said aloud to be kept records.

### At home

**Swap.** Any small plant, or a drawn one, for the class cups.

**Skip.** The covers and the table talk.

- **5 min. Repair.** Show the ruler steps. **Say.** "Where does zero go?" Expect: at the soil.
- **15 min. Measure.** Read the plant at the soil to the whole centimeter. Draw its leaves and stem. Fill the dated line.
- **5 min. Talk.** **Say.** "What changed, and what stayed the same?" Expect: the answer from the record, even if nothing changed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X50]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sketch while waiting, and say fewer table reports; never cut the reading or the line. Longer: fill a second line for another cup.

### Words and supports

Date, condition, height, same, changed, leaf, stem. Frame: "It is ___ centimeters and ___." A scribe is allowed.

### Tomorrow

Error E: a height with nothing else. Day 51 opens with the height-only line and the filled one, then asks what makes a test fair and what besides height tells us a plant is healthy. Day 52 hands back the feedback.
