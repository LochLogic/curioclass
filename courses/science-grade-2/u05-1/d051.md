# Day 51: Is taller healthier?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set one corn card set on each table of five. Pin the chart. Choose Today's six. Sort the day 50 exit tickets by error E. Key: key-corn-cards-day51.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only; no plants and no goggles today. Count each card set back at the end.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

If many made error E on day 50's exit, take it whole class; if few, a small group at your table. Draw two lines for one pot: "5 centimeters" alone, and "5 centimeters, two green leaves, a straight stem". **Ask.** What does the first line leave out? Expect: the leaves and the stem.

#### 3 min. Retrieve the model

**Ask.** Which arrows are still dotted? Expect: those our records do not yet show.
**Ask.** Which table lines do we have? Expect: day 49 and day 50.
**Ask.** What can we still not tell? Expect: whether a plant needs light or water, until the records differ.

#### 17 min. Fairness and height evidence

Say all cards are invented for practice. Say: "A fair test keeps everything the same and changes one thing, as in unit 2."
Pairs (8 minutes): each table marks the three pair cards fair or not fair and touches the difference on each; the cards are the water-only pair, the corn and marigold pair, and the light-and-water pair. Visit each of six tables for 1 minute. Error A is calling pair 2 or pair 3 fair.
Series (9 minutes): tables lay the Day 1 and Day 6 cards and read them aloud, then mark color, leaves and stem for the covered and the lit plant on Day 12 with symbols, 6 minutes. The class answers for 3 minutes. **Ask.** Which plant is taller on Day 12? Expect: the covered plant, 19 and 16. **Ask.** Which plant is healthier? Expect: the lit plant. **Ask.** What tells you? Expect: green, firm, four leaves.

#### 12 min. Discuss and revise

Pip says, "The plant that grew the most is the healthiest." **Ask.** Does that fit the Day 12 cards? Expect: no; the covered plant grew most and is pale and leaning (error B is saying yes) (4 minutes). Add a looks row to the model: color, leaves, stem beside height. Pairs choose what they will look at on day 52 that could support or challenge "plants need light and water" (5 minutes); each child keeps a look-for card. Today's six say, "I will look at ___ because ___," 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 3 min. Exit

Read [[s05-X51a]] once over the sunflower pairs; all show M or N with 1 or 2 fingers (1 minute). Show the sunflower card and read [[s05-X51b]]; all touch one card (20 seconds); Today's six say why, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark on the one scale.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pairs and the series.

### At home

**Swap.** Two drawn plants for the corn cards.

**Skip.** The pair cards and the pairs' choices.

- **5 min. Repair.** Draw a height-only line and a full line. **Say.** "What does the first leave out?" Expect: leaves and stem.
- **12 min. Series.** Fill the color, leaves and stem rows for both plants on the page.
- **8 min. Say.** **Say.** "Which plant is healthier, and what tells you?" Expect: the lit plant; green, firm, more leaves.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s05-X51a]], then [[s05-X51b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark Day 12 only; never cut the series or the exit. Longer: say what else could be different between two pots.

### Words and supports

Fair test (keep the same, change one thing, as in unit 2), different, taller, healthier, green, pale, firm, leaning. Frame: "I will look at ___ because ___." A scribe is allowed.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who calls an unfair pair fair (error A) and who says taller is healthier (error B). Day 52 opens with both for 3 minutes each, then records the second week's growth and compares each plant with the healthy plant.
