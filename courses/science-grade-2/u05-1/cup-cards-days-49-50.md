# Cup cards (days 49 and 50)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Three cup cards for the return and repair, and the model for the chart.

**Cup H:** a cup with soil and a seedling, in a sunny window, a spoon pouring water.
**Cup D:** the same cup with the seedling under a box with air holes, a spoon pouring water.
**Cup R:** the same cup in the sunny window, no spoon, a small crossed-out drop.
**Day 49 repair (error A):** lay Cup D beside Cup R. Ask: "Touch what is different." Then lay Cup D beside Cup H and ask again.
**Key (teacher):** Cup D against Cup R differ in light and in water, so it is not a fair test. Cup D against Cup H differs only in light. Cup R against Cup H differs only in water.
**Day 50 repair (error A):** the same cards, in the order Cup D with Cup R, then Cup D with Cup H.
