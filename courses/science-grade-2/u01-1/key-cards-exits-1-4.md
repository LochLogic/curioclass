# Key: cards-exits-1-4

Teacher only. Do not print for children.

## Day 1 exit card: the rock slope

**Key (teacher):** An arrow starting high on the slope, running down the rock and into the hollow, which is drawn holding water. An arrow going up from the hollow is gap E.

## Day 2 exit card: the tray

**Key (teacher):** Two hills, the valley pool, the arrow from the hilltop down to the pool, and the key box filled in so a partner could read the arrow.

## Day 3 exit card: the model

**Key (teacher):** A hollow between the hills holding water, an arrow from its low edge into the river, and the ocean kept.

## Day 4 exit card: the corner and the tray

**Key (teacher):** The path sloping to the dip with water in it, the same mark in the dip and between the tray hills, and a key saying the mark means the water collected.
