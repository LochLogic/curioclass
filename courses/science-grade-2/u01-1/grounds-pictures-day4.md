# School-grounds corners

Three drawings of grounds corners for day 4, stand-ins for teacher photographs. Show one to each group. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A corner with a play structure, a slope of grass and a low path that bends toward a dip full of water. A boy with brown skin and a cap kneels at the edge looking at the water.

**Read:** There is a low path and a dip with water.

## Page 2

**Picture:** A corner with a fence, a flat blacktop and a puddle at the lowest edge where the blacktop meets grass. No people.

**Read:** The puddle is at the low edge of the blacktop.

## Page 3

**Picture:** A corner with a garden bed raised on a bank. A stream of water runs from the bed along a shallow groove into the grass. A girl in a wheelchair watches from the paved path.

**Read:** Water ran from the garden bed along the groove.
