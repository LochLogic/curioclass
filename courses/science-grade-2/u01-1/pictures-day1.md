# Schoolyard after rain

A picture sequence for day 1. Four pictures. These are stand-ins for teacher photographs of the real schoolyard (no identifiable children). Show one at a time and say who drew it: "We made these pictures. They are not our school, but a lot like it." Children read no words. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A schoolyard during a light rain: a low brick wall, a flat blacktop, a grassy slope that runs down to a paved path, and a bench. Gray curved lines show falling rain. No people.

**Read:** It is raining on the schoolyard. The rain falls on the grass, the blacktop and the path.

## Page 2

**Picture:** The same yard after the rain. Puddles lie on the blacktop near a low corner. A thin stream of water runs along the edge of the path. The grass slope is dark and wet.

**Read:** Now the rain has stopped. There are puddles. A little stream runs by the path.

## Page 3

**Picture:** A close view of the curb at the low end of the yard. A thin stream of water runs along it and ends in a puddle against a drain cover. A girl with dark curly hair crouches to look, with her hands on her knees.

**Read:** Mina looks at the water by the curb. She sees where it is going.

## Page 4

**Picture:** The yard seen from above as a plain drawing: the slope, the blacktop, the path and the bench, with no water drawn. A blank space shows around the low corner.

**Read:** Where did the water go? Where was it before? What do you notice? We will use these questions to start our question board.
