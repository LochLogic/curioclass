# Day 3: Fix the model

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put pieces 1 to 6 in an envelope for each pair, and count them. Have the class model chart and each group's day 2 pour record sheet ready. Choose today's six. Nothing new is taught today; the day uses day 2's key and pour. Key: key-cards-exits-1-4.

### Safety and set-up

No hazards: paper, pencils and tape. The pieces are counted back into envelopes at the end (2 minutes), so none is lost. Scissors stay with you.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 2 exit and the pour record sheets. Teach the one error most children made, error E first. On a pour record, trace with a finger from the hilltop down to the pool, then redraw the arrow on the exit card. Anyone with error B or error D gets a picture card at their table; they use it in the next part.

#### 4 min. Show one revision

Redraw one group's day 2 model on the chart after reading its pour record. Say it aloud with the frame "I changed ___ because I saw ___". The old drawing stays beside the new one, dated. Nothing new is taught.

#### 12 min. Build with picture pieces

Pairs place the pieces in the box on one student page: hills, a hollow holding water, a river, a lake in the hollow and the ocean. The river runs downhill, toward the ocean at the page edge; its piece is narrower at the high end. Not every river reaches the ocean. Draw an arrow only where a pour record showed the water going, then fill the key box. Each pair does [[s01-P3-1]]. Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "What does your model show, and what does it leave out?" Scribe their words.

#### 8 min. Revise from your own pour

Each group takes its day 2 pour record and asks: "What did our water do that our model does not show?" Each pair does [[s01-P3-2]]: one change, said with the frame.

Pip says: "I left the ocean off because it is not on our page." This is error G.

**Ask.** Where does the river end? Expect: at the ocean, at the page edge. Pip adds the ocean with its mark in the key.

#### 4 min. Gallery

One pair shows its model to another pair, new today and built on the grade 1 partner share, and says: "I saw ___, I think ___." Swap. About 2 minutes each way.

#### 3 min. Exit E3

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-1-4. Every child does [[s01-X3]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Put pieces away

Count the pieces back into envelopes. Models and student pages stay with students, in their folders; the chart stays with you.

### Other rooms

No pieces: draw hills, a hollow, a river and the ocean on scrap paper and cut them out.

### At home

**Swap.** No pieces: draw and cut the shapes from paper. No chart: use one sheet.

**Skip.** The gallery (new today, built on the grade 1 partner share) and the pair work.

- **5 min. Look.** Find the day 2 pour picture, or pour a little water on a sand hill again.
  **Say.** "Which way did the water go?" Expect: downhill, into the low place.
- **10 min. Build.** Your child places two hills, a hollow with water, a river and the ocean, and does [[s01-P3-1]].
  **Say.** "Which way does the river run, and how do you know?" Expect: downhill, toward the ocean, because the piece narrows at the high end.
- **10 min. Fix one thing.** Your child does [[s01-P3-2]].
  **Say.** "What did you change, and what did you see?" Expect: a change and the picture that shows it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X3]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: leave out the lake. Never cut the pour record check, the key box or the exit. Longer: add a second hollow.

### Words and supports

Revise: change a model because of what we saw. Frame: "I changed ___ because I saw ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who draws the new route climbing (error E), who adds water with no land round it (error D) and who drops the ocean (error G). Day 4 opens with Return and repair of the day 3 exit, error E first, then error D and error G.
