# Pour records (supplied)

Three supplied pour record cards for day 2, used when a group's own pour fails or is unclear. Each card is a tray drawn from the side with a key box under it. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** Card A: a tray with a sand hill on the left and a flat sand floor on the right. A cup tips water onto the top of the hill. An arrow runs down the hill and ends in a small pool at the foot. The key box says: arrow = the water went this way.

**Read:** The water ran down the hill and made a pool at the foot.

## Page 2

**Picture:** Card B: a tray with two sand hills and a dip between them. An arrow runs from the top of each hill down into the dip. A pool sits in the dip. Key box as on card A.

**Read:** The water ran down both hills and gathered in the dip between them.

## Page 3

**Picture:** Card C: a tray with one tall sand hill and a long flat shelf. An arrow runs down the hill and along the shelf, ending at the far edge in a small pool. Key box as on card A.

**Read:** The water ran down the hill, then along the flat part to the far end.
