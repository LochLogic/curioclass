# Day 1: Where did the water go?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the doorway route the day before. Wet yard: show it from the dry threshold. Dry yard: use the pictures. Tape up chart paper for the question board you used in grade 1, and the class model. Choose today's six. You speak for Pip, a paper puppet, one line at a time.

### Safety and set-up

Look at runoff only from the dry doorway or a safe path. No child goes near runoff or a drain. Pencils only, so no goggles.

### The day

#### 6 min. Hook and question board

Say: "These pictures are invented for practice." Show pictures 1 and 2.

**Ask.** What do you notice? Expect: puddles, a little stream, wet grass. Take about eight; any child may pass. Write each as a question. Read aloud: "After a rainstorm, where does the water on our schoolyard go?"

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Review the grade 1 map words. Draw on the board a wavy band, a rounded hump and a key box: wavy band = river, hump = hill. In pairs, one child does [[s01-P1-1]] and the other [[s01-P1-2]], then swap. Note on the record sheet who needed the symbol shown. For a miss, show the symbol beside the feature, then let the child try the other. Those children sit beside a partner on day 2.

#### 8 min. Worked example

Show picture 3, Mina at the curb. Say: "We look from here, not near drains." The arrow follows the stream in the picture. Say each word once: runoff, rain that runs over the ground; downhill, toward lower ground; hollow, a low place; collect, gather in one place. [[steps:s01-K1-1]]

**Ask.** Where did the water stay? Expect: in the low place at the end, the puddle in the hollow.

Pip draws only a puddle: "That is where the water is." This is error D.

**Ask.** What is missing from Pip's picture? Expect: the high ground the water came down, and the low place round the puddle. Pip adds the slope and hollow outline. The arrow means the water went this way.

#### 10 min. Sketch the routes

Alone, with the picture card (pictures 2 and 4). Each child does [[s01-P1-3]]. Say: "Draw only routes the pictures show." Hear today's six for 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "Where did the water go, and where did it stay?" Scribe their words; mark who was heard.

#### 5 min. Partner share

Pairs, 1 minute each: one child points to the other's sketch and says where the water went; the author does [[s01-P1-4]]. Swap. Then 3 minutes: three volunteers pin sketches as the first class model, dated.

#### 3 min. Exit E1

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-1-4. Every child does [[s01-X1]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Pack up

Collect picture cards. Sketches stay with students, in their folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures on the board and sketch on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: with a grown-up, look at a street after rain from a dry doorway, away from traffic and drains, or draw one yard from memory. No chart: use one sheet.

**Skip.** The class chart and the partner share.

- **5 min. Look.** Show a picture of rain on a yard and the yard after.
  **Say.** "What do you notice?" Expect: puddles, a stream, wet ground.
- **8 min. Trace.** Trace a finger down the picture from high ground to the low puddle. Draw one arrow the same way and the hollow.
  **Say.** "Where did the water go, and where did it stay?" Expect: down the slope, and in the low place.
- **10 min. Sketch.** Your child does [[s01-P1-3]] on the student page.
- **5 min. Change one thing.** Point at the sketch and say where the water went. Your child does [[s01-P1-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X1]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fewer notices. Never cut the tracing, sketch or exit. Longer: add a second low place.

### Words and supports

Frame: "The water goes downhill to the ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who draws the route climbing (error E) or only a full hollow (error D). Day 2 opens with Return and repair of the day 1 exit, error E first, then error D.
