# Day 4: A map of our grounds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the paths, the weather, insects and allergies the day before. If the corner is not safe, use the pictures. Tape up the class model chart. Have each group's day 2 pour record sheet ready. Choose today's six. You speak for Pip. Key: key-cards-exits-1-4.

### Safety and set-up

Stay on dry paths and in sight. Touch no plants or animals. Offer the indoor picture path to any child with an allergy to stings. A real look is from the doorway only, if the ground is dry and safe. Set-up for unit 6, teacher alone, during the draw part and outside class minutes: dry seed heads in a bag, check burrs, and store the bag out of reach. Children never collect. Clean-up is the 3 minute pack up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 3 exit. Teach the one error most children made, error E first. Trace with a finger the low route on the day 2 pour record, then redraw the arrow on the exit card. Anyone with error D or error G gets a picture card at their table; they use it in the next making part.

#### 3 min. Look

Show grounds pictures 2 and 3, invented for practice, or the real corner from the doorway. For each, do [[s01-P4-1]] together: everyone points with an arm on your signal. Any child may keep their arm down.

#### 5 min. Show how to draw a picture map

On the chart, draw picture 2 from above, with an arrow along the route seen and a key box. Say it is not to scale: a map does not have to be exact in size. Pip draws his map with no key. This is error B.

**Ask.** What can you not tell from Pip's map? Expect: what the arrow means. Pip adds a key box.

#### 12 min. Draw the grounds map

Groups of five. Each child does [[s01-P4-2]] from picture 3, with one arrow along a route the picture shows. Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "Where is lower on your map?" Scribe their words and mark who was heard.

#### 6 min. Compare with the tray

Each group lays one map beside its day 2 pour record sheet and does [[s01-P4-3]] together, with the frame "the same is ___, different is ___".

**Ask.** What does the school map show that the tray did not? Expect: a bank, a groove, grass; the water kept running instead of gathering. The tray left out the bank and the grass.

#### 3 min. Exit E4

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-1-4. Every child does [[s01-X4]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Collect pencils. Maps stay with students, in their folders. Collect picture cards.

#### 2 min. Question board

Answer "Does our map match the school?" with what the pictures showed, and leave it open for day 5.

### Other rooms

No pictures: draw the corner on the board and have children copy it from above. One projector: show the pictures there.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture: look at a path or a slope near home after rain, or draw one. No tray record: use the day 2 drawing or a bowl of damp sand.

**Skip.** The group work and the seed heads.

- **5 min. Look.** Show grounds picture 3 or a real slope.
  **Say.** "Which way is lower?" Expect: toward the low ground, where the water ends.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child does [[s01-P4-2]]. Say that a map need not be exact.
- **10 min. Compare.** Lay the map beside the day 2 tray drawing. Your child does [[s01-P4-3]].
  **Say.** "What is the same, and what is different?" Expect: both run downhill; one gathers, one runs on.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X4]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one arrow, no compare frame. Never cut the look, the key box or the exit. Longer: draw a second corner.

### Words and supports

Map, as in grade 1: a drawing of a place from above. Frame: "The same is ___, different is ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who draws the path running up (error E), who draws a dip and water alone (error D) and who skips the key (error B). Day 5 opens with Return and repair of the day 4 exit, error E first, then error D and error B.
