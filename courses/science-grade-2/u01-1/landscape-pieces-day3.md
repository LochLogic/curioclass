# Landscape picture pieces

A set of cut-out picture pieces for day 3, one envelope for each pair. Print each piece on its own card and put the label under it. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair, never from a real photograph.

## Page 1

**Picture:** Two rounded hill pieces, one tall and one low, each with a flat bottom edge. Label: hill.

## Page 2

**Picture:** A hollow piece: a bowl-shaped dip in the ground with a curved edge. Label: hollow.

## Page 3

**Picture:** A river piece: a long bending band that narrows toward one end. Label: river.

## Page 4

**Picture:** A lake piece: a rounded water shape with a wavy edge. Label: lake.

## Page 5

**Picture:** An ocean piece: a wide band for the edge of a page, with a wavy top line. Label: ocean.

## Page 6

**Picture:** Eight small arrow pieces and one key box piece. The key box reads: arrow = the water went this way.

## Page 7

**Picture:** A lesson strip showing rain gathering in a dip, the water rising, and the water running over the low edge.

**Read:** Rain gathers in the dip. The water rises. It runs over the low edge.
