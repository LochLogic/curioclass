# Day 2: The tray and the pour

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, check sand, trays and tops. Set one tray, cup and cloth per group, and the demonstration tray. Write the key chart: arrow, hill, hollow, water. Choose today's six. Key: key-cards-exits-1-4.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone nearby wears splash goggles from the start, as in kindergarten. Play sand only, never yard soil. Keep sand and cups below faces. Never throw or taste sand or pebbles; count pebbles back. Pour only over the tray. Wipe spills. Wash hands after sand. Tip sand into a lined bin, never a drain. Without goggles for all, only you pour and groups use the pour record cards.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 1 exit and cards. Teach the one error most children made, error E first. On picture 2, trace from the high grass down to the low corner. Children trace it on their card, then redraw the arrow on their exit card. Anyone with error D gets an outline card; they use it in Build.

#### 5 min. Worked example: a model, its key and the tray

Say: "The sand stands for land. The cup of water stands for rain." A model stands for the real yard and leaves things out. Sand soaks in more water than pavement, so trust it only for the route. Down means lower, not the bottom of the page. [[steps:s01-K2-1]]

Pip draws arrows and dots and says: "Everybody knows what they mean." This is error B.

**Ask.** What can you not tell from Pip's drawing? Expect: what the arrow and dots mean. Pip then adds a key box.

#### 7 min. Build

Goggles on first (1 minute). Groups of five shape two hills and a valley with three pebbles. Jobs: shaper, pourer, pointer, drawer, wiper. Each group does [[s01-P2-1]] before any water, said aloud as a guess, as in kindergarten.

#### 6 min. Pour and watch

All six groups pour at once. Each pours one cup slowly from a hilltop, twice, 2 minutes each, by two pourers. The drawer does [[s01-P2-2]] on the pour record sheet. Watch for an arrow drawn uphill. If the water soaks in, dampen the sand and pour slower. If it still fails, use card A, B or C, said aloud to be invented.

#### 6 min. Draw the pour record

Alone. Each child does [[s01-P2-3]]. Hear today's six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "What does the tray show, and what does it leave out?" Scribe their words and mark who was heard.

#### 7 min. Clean-up

Wipes and goggles first, 1 minute. Two waves of three groups wash at two sinks, 2.5 minutes a wave (15 children, 20 seconds each) while you tip trays into the bin. Waiting groups finish drawing.

#### 3 min. Exit E2

Read the card once, as printed in cards-exits-1-4. Every child does [[s01-X2]]. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 1 min. Question board

Add one question for day 3: "Why did the water gather there?" Keep the pour records; drawings go in folders.

### Other rooms

No sand: trace cards A, B and C; all do [[s01-P2-3]] from one card.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: use a baking dish of damp sand, or bagged potting soil, not soil from outside. No goggles for both of you: use a spoon of water poured over a drawn hill, or the cards. No chart: write the key on the page.

**Skip.** The groups, the jobs and the clean-up count.

- **5 min. Shape.** Make one hill and a low place in the sand.
  **Say.** "Show me with a finger where the water will go." Expect: down from the hill top to the low place.
- **6 min. Pour.** Wear goggles. Pour a small cup slowly from the hill top and watch.
  **Say.** "Where did the water go, and where did it stay?" Expect: down the hill, and in the low place.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child does [[s01-P2-3]], with a key box.
- **5 min. Clean up.** Tip the sand in the bin, wipe, wash hands.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s01-X2]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one pour. Never cut the goggles, guess, key box or exit.

### Words and supports

Model (review from grade 1): a drawing or tray that stands for the real thing. Key: a box that tells what each mark means. Valley: low land between hills. Frame: "The arrow means ___".

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who draws the arrow climbing (error E), who draws only the pool with no hills (error D) and who leaves out the key box (error B). Day 3 opens, as in kindergarten, with Return and repair of the day 2 exit, error E first, then error B and error D.
