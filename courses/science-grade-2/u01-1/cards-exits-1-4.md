# Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Each exit has a picture card every child draws on or marks, and a spoken stem read once. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card. None of these pictures is reused in a hook, a read-aloud or a lesson picture.

## Day 1 exit card: the rock slope

**Picture:** Two small drawings side by side. Left: a rock slope with a hollow at its foot, the hollow dry. Right: the same slope with the hollow full of water and wet streaks down the rock.

**Say:** "In the first picture the hollow is dry. In the second it is full. Draw the way the water went from the slope to the hollow."

**Key (teacher):** An arrow starting high on the slope, running down the rock and into the hollow, which is drawn holding water. An arrow going up from the hollow is gap E.

## Day 2 exit card: the tray

**Picture:** A tray drawn from the side with two hills and a pool in the valley. An arrow runs from the pool up one hill. Beside it, a pour record card shows the water running from a hilltop down to the pool.

**Say:** "The arrow is wrong. The record shows the water came down from the top. Draw the tray with the arrow fixed, and fill in the key."

**Key (teacher):** Two hills, the valley pool, the arrow from the hilltop down to the pool, and the key box filled in so a partner could read the arrow.

## Day 3 exit card: the model

**Picture:** A model of two hills, a river and the ocean, with no hollow. Three small pictures beside it: rain gathering in a hollow between the hills, the water rising, and the water running over the low edge into the river.

**Say:** "The model has no hollow. Change it to match the three pictures."

**Key (teacher):** A hollow between the hills holding water, an arrow from its low edge into the river, and the ocean kept.

## Day 4 exit card: the corner and the tray

**Picture:** A playground corner where a low path leads water into a dip. Beside it, a tray record showing water gathered between two hills.

**Say:** "Draw this corner. Then mark one thing its water did that the tray water did, and add a key box that says what the mark means."

**Key (teacher):** The path sloping to the dip with water in it, the same mark in the dip and between the tray hills, and a key saying the mark means the water collected.
