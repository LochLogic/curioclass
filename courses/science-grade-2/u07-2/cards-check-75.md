# Check cards, day 75

Held back. Not shown before day 75. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except a date or where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. One sealed set, plus plan cards.

- **Plan cards.** A plain control seedling in the sun with water; blank spaces beside it for a second seedling.
- **Later records.** Radish P (no light, 11 centimeters, pale and leaning) and radish Q (no water, 3 centimeters, wilted) beside the 9 centimeter green control, labelled supplied and invented.
- **At the station.** A tray of five seedling cups and a ruler for each child.
