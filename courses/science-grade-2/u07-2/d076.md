# Day 76: Repair, retry and a hand-off folder

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 74 and day 75 pages by letter into five groups: A (pieces and a new whole, N and O); B (heating and cooling, P, Q and I); C (the plan beside a control, R); D (what a plant needs, S, T and U); E (our own records and supplied ones, V). Write each child's group and one next step on their page. Set one set of repair cards at each group table. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and read the feedback

Each child gets day 74's assessment and day 75's assessment back and reads or hears the one written next step on their page. Today's six say theirs back, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 12 min. Return and repair

Five groups, each with you in turn while the rest work from picture directions at their table: A 2 minutes, B 3, C 2, D 2, E 2, then 1 minute to move. Group A: lay out the same pieces in two wholes, point to what is joined and what is separate, tap the stiff piece. Group B: lay a warmed thing beside its start, and look for what is truly the same. Group C: put one change beside the control and say the one thing that differs. Group D: set a sun-only and a water-only plant beside the control. Group E: set a measured record beside a supplied one. A child with no gap draws a fresh plan.

#### 7 min. Fresh retries

Each child retries only the missed group: A [[c2-X76f]], B [[c2-X76g]], and groups C, D and E [[c2-X76h]]. Read the asks aloud (2 minutes); children touch or say yes or no (3). Six rotating children say their reason, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). A retry is recorded apart and never rescores a day 74 or 75 item.

#### 6 min. Exit

Say every card is invented for practice. Read [[c2-X76a]], [[c2-X76b]], [[c2-X76c]], [[c2-X76d]] and [[c2-X76e]] one at a time; all touch (3 minutes). Six rotating children say one thing that is different for [[c2-X76e]], 20 seconds each (2 minutes). Collect (1).

#### 6 min. Hand-off folder

Each child files the unit 5 conducting record, the unit 6 model record and the day 72 record, unchanged, with the day 74 and day 75 pages (4 minutes). They write or draw one thing that is better now (2 minutes). Collect the folders.

### Marking

After class, 20 minutes: mark the five exits on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record each retry apart. Record what was shown on their own and what was shown with help in separate columns; never rescore days 74 and 75. Note one line per child for grade 3.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on scrap paper; the folder can be a sheet folded in half.

### At home

**Swap.** Any craft pieces, soft food and a seedling for the cards.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the retry.

- **5 min. Next step.** Read the written next step aloud together.
- **15 min. Repair.** Lay out your own plant records, first and latest, side by side.
  **Say.** "What can you do now that you could not at the start?" Expect: a measure, a plan or a fair comparison.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[c2-X76a]], [[c2-X76b]], [[c2-X76c]], [[c2-X76d]], [[c2-X76e]] and the retries [[c2-X76f]], [[c2-X76g]], [[c2-X76h]] aloud, one at a time. The child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair for 2 minutes a group; never cut the retry, the exit or the folder. Longer: a fresh plan for a new seedling.

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, control, evidence, measured, supplied, returned, stayed changed. Frames: "I can now ___." "It suits the job, because ___." Pointing is accepted.

### Tomorrow

The check ends here. The folder goes with the child to grade 3.
