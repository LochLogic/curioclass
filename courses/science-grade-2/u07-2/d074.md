# Day 74: A raft, a fence and three warm records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 73 hands on no gap to this check. Print the cards in two sealed sets. Cover the walls. Seat each child so they see only their own page. On the record sheet choose Today's six for each block, not heard in the last two weeks. The day 72 record stays in its folder unchanged and is not used today.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. Every card is new and invented for practice. You may pass." Show where answers go on the page. Practise one touch with two plain cups: "Touch the bigger cup."

#### 15 min. Pieces and a new whole

Show the raft card, the table card and the fence card; read the captions twice (2 minutes). Read [[c2-P74a]]; children touch the pieces in both (2 minutes). Read [[c2-P74b]]; they say or write what held them (3 minutes). Read [[c2-P74c]]; they say or draw how one piece suits its job (3 minutes). Read [[c2-P74d]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words or draw (3 minutes). Collect (1 minute).

#### 15 min. Heating, cooling and undoing

Open the second set. Read [[c2-H74a]]; children circle returned or stayed changed and say or draw what shows it (3 minutes). Read [[c2-H74b]] the same way (3 minutes). Read [[c2-H74c]] the same way (3 minutes). Read [[c2-H74d]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words (3 minutes). Collect (1 minute).

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the eight items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: N to Q. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each. Earlier unit records are not re-scored.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the raft, the table and the fence on the board; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: build a raft and a fence from six craft sticks or straws and two nonlatex bands or strings (check latex allergies; never stretch or snap them near faces). Any soft food stands in for the toast (check food allergies first, do not eat it, and a grown-up does any warming).

**Skip.** The sealed sets and Today's six.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each ask aloud once and wait. Your child answers [[c2-P74d]], then the wax, the toast and the peach, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Did the wax come back, and did the toast?" Expect: the wax did, the toast did not.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Piece, whole, joined, separate, stiff, returned, stayed changed. Frame: "It stayed changed, because ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 74 is not scored in class. Day 75 is the second part of the check: a plant plan, a measure and later records. Day 76 hands both days back with one written next step.
