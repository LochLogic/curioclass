# Day 70: Would another look agree?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Walk both spots the day before: paths, weather, stinging insects, harmful plants, allergies, mown or trampled ground. Lay the day 66 loops, six in each spot. Put the day's question on the question board: Would another look agree? Pick the next six to be heard.

### Safety and set-up

Closed shoes. Watch only: touch, move and collect nothing. Unsafe or frozen site, or an allergy: use the second-date photographs and say aloud they are weaker. A mown or trampled spot goes on the record.

### The day

#### 3 min. Recall

Hold up the day 66 inventory sheets. **Ask.** We look at the same two spots again. What do we keep the same? Expect: the loop, five minutes, the labels; the same time of day if we can. Pip says "use a bigger loop this time." Expect: no, a bigger loop shows more, so we could not tell if the place or the looking changed.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 69's exit. Cards are invented. Error A (3 minutes): the circle-and-count card, a grass strip; the child circles the same kind and counts circles (three), beside "seven pictures, so seven kinds." Error B (2 minutes): the equal-count cards, feeder corner and vegetable bed; touch each kind on one card, then the other. Put "same number, so the same" beside "same number, different kinds." If many made one letter it takes the whole class; the rest lay out their day 66 graphs.

#### 4 min. Prepare and move

Read the safety sheet once: path, sight, watch only, tell an adult. Same spots, same loops, same order. The class walks together; an adult counts heads.

#### 10 min. Survey

Six groups of five, a loop in each spot. Five minutes at spot 1, five at spot 2, the change called from your watch (a pace cue, so every group looks equally long). Each child draws every living thing seen on a new inventory sheet dated today, repeats too, not each other. Nothing is touched or collected.

#### 9 min. Compare

Each child circles repeats and draws one picture per kind on a second graph template, with day 67's labels; new kinds get a label agreed aloud (5 minutes). Groups lay it beside the day 66 graph: a tick by a kind on both dates, a ring around a kind on one date only (4 minutes).

**Ask.** Did every kind come back? Expect: some did and some did not; do not ask why yet.

#### 3 min. Model

Each group sticks one graph row for each spot under the day 66 row on the class sheet, 6 x 15 s = 1.5 minutes. With the class mark each kind "seen again" or "seen this date only" (1.5 minutes). Add the date.

#### 2 min. Exit

Say the picture is invented for practice. Read [[s07-X70]] once; all touch a card (0.5 minute). Six rotating children say why, 15 seconds each (6 x 15 s = 1.5 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Loops and boards collected (1 minute); clothing checked in pairs in the line; two sinks. If the line runs over, the last wash comes at the next scheduled washing, said aloud.

### Other rooms

No outdoor spot: the photographs. No printer: draw each card on scrap.

### At home

**Swap.** One spot only, such as a plant pot; a book on the knee for a board.

**Skip.** The class sheet and the groups.

- **3 min. Keep.** Pick one spot and a time.
  **Say.** "What do we keep the same when we look again?" Expect: the same spot, the same time, the same way of drawing.
- **5 min. Repeats.** Circle repeats on a drawing; count circles.
- **10 min. Look.** Five minutes: draw every living thing. Go outdoors with a grown-up, and check the spot for unsafe ground and stinging insects first. Watch only, touch nothing, wash hands after.
- **9 min. Compare.** Lay it beside an earlier drawing; tick kinds seen both times.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X70]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one spot, the graph and the exit. Never cut the exit. Longer: a third look.

### Words and supports

Keep the same (size, time, labels). Frame: "Both days we saw ___. Only on ___ we saw ___." Pointing and drawing are accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the box lost two kinds when the look was shorter (error C). Day 71 repairs it with the flap card, then reads the two dated records and answers the unit's question.
