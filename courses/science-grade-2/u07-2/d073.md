# Day 73: Fix one thing and look at two more places

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy each child's day 72 record; clip copy, original and feedback slip together. Sort by letter: A, B and C from day 72; D from the day 69 notebook and the day 71 exit. Set out each group's cards. Choose today's six. Keys: key-hedge-and-dune-cards-day73, key-wet-and-dry-pair-days-71-73.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; pencils only. No hazard. Count the cards back at the end.

### The day

#### 8 min. Read feedback and revise

Return day 72's assessment with its slip. Read each note aloud, or an adult does for any child whose record says so (3 minutes). Each child revises one thing in pencil on the copy; the original stays whole and attached (3 minutes). Six rotating children say their change, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 12 min. Repair by letter groups

Cards are invented. Groups A, B, C and D each meet you 2.5 minutes in turn while the rest work with a partner from the picture directions; 2 minutes to move (4 x 2.5 + 2). Group A, error A: the circle-and-count card; circle the same kind, count circles. Group B, error B: the equal-count cards; touch the members that differ. Group C, error C: flap card 2, the shrub; first view, then lift the flap. Group D, error D: the wet and dry pair, three kinds each. If many made one letter, it takes the first 3 minutes with the whole class (3 + 3 x 2 + 3). One child follows the steps with an adult; a child with no gap tells a partner what each card shows.

#### 7 min. Fresh comparison

Two cards, invented for practice: a hedge row and a sand dune. Each child draws one picture per kind on both cards and one graph (4 minutes); six rotating children say a kind on both cards and a kind on one, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Unscored.

**Ask.** Which kind is on both cards? Expect: the beetle; the other kinds are on one card only.

#### 3 min. Consensus

The class revises the model once more; you write it. Aim for: count kinds once; equal counts can hide different kinds; a kind not seen may be hiding; "we cannot tell" when records do not decide; wet is not always more.

**Ask.** What can two looks not tell us? Expect: every kind.

#### 3 min. Exit

Pictures are invented. Read [[s07-X73a]] once; all touch a number (0.5 minute). Read [[s07-X73b]] once; all touch yes or no (0.5 minute). Six rotating children say why, 15 seconds each (6 x 15 s = 1.5 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Papers into folders; cards counted.

### Marking

After class, 45 minutes (ours, untested): score the revised version once on the ten components, original attached: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the hedge row (2 thorn bushes, 1 berry vine, 2 finches, 1 beetle) and the dune (2 beach plants, 1 mouse, the same beetle).

### At home

**Swap.** Any two drawings of two places for the cards.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the consensus.

- **8 min. Note.** Read the written next step together; redo one thing in pencil.
- **12 min. Repair.** Do the weakest card again.
  **Say.** "How many kinds, and are the kinds the same?" Expect: circle the same kind and count circles; equal counts can hide different kinds.
- **7 min. Compare.** Draw one picture per kind on two places; mark one kind on both.
- **3 min. Say.** What can two looks not tell us?
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X73a]] and [[s07-X73b]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the revision and the exit. Longer: a third card.

### Words and supports

Equal counts, cannot tell. Frame: "Both have ___. Only ___ has ___." Pointing is accepted.

### Tomorrow

The day 72 record goes unchanged to year-end. Day 74 is the first year-end check: a craft raft and fence, and wax, toast and fruit records, with the day 72 record left unchanged.
