# Repair, retry and exit cards (day 76)

Held back. No case from days 74 or 75. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except a date or where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. One repair set for each of five tables.

- **Group A.** The same pieces laid out as two wholes; a piece card for stiff and floppy.
- **Group B.** A thing warmed beside its start; a dated three-picture strip.
- **Group C.** A control seedling with a card for the one thing that differs.
- **Group D.** A sun-only plant and a water-only plant beside the control.
- **Group E.** A measured record beside a supplied record.
- **Retries.** A stick and a ribbon for a fence; a banana slice dated Monday, Tuesday and Friday; two marigold seedlings; three okra seedlings, sun and water, sun and no water, water in the dark; our dated chard record beside an invented card.
- **Exits.** Four tubes and three boards as a shelter and a bench; a bowl of gelatin dessert warmed and cooled; a pea seedling with a control; the first and the latest plant record.
