# Section 7.2 opener: repeating the look and explaining what we found

## What the section does

Four days, 70 to 73. Day 70 repeats the schoolyard survey one week later at the same two spots, the same size, the same five minutes and the same labels, and lays the new graph beside the old one. Day 71 reads the two dated records, finds what was seen both times and what was seen once, and answers the unit's question in the class's own words, with "we cannot tell" wherever the records do not decide. Day 72 is the unit's one unaided record: each child alone with three new printed scenes. Day 73 returns the feedback, lets each child revise, repairs by letter group and ends with a fresh comparison.

## How the days run

Days 70, 71 and 73 open with a return and repair of the exit before or the written feedback. Read every stem aloud, one ask at a time, and say that every picture is invented for practice. Six rotating children are heard each day, 15 to 20 seconds each, and the record sheet names who. Mark with one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Children read only short labels. On day 72 do not coach: read each prompt aloud once, explain nothing, point to no chart, and accept pointing and dictated answers through a scribe. The day 72 paper is unscored on the day; written feedback follows, day 73 begins with an individual revision, and the retained revised version is scored once on ten components with the original attached. Scientific names earn nothing.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **One look can be luck, so we look again the same way.** Same size of patch, same five minutes, same labels. Then a difference is less likely to be made by the looking.
- **A second date may differ for three reasons.** The place changed, the weather or the time of day changed, or the looking changed. Keeping area, time and labels the same lets us say the looking did not change. It never lets us say all the kinds were found.
- **"We cannot tell" is a finished answer.** Where the two records do not decide, the class says so. If a spot was mown or trampled, the record says so and the comparison is reported as inconclusive.
- **No ranking and no wet-means-more rule.** A damp place and a dry place can hold the same number of kinds. What the cards show is only what they show.
- **A kind not seen may be hiding.** The class model carries this limit all the way to the end of the unit.
- **Equal counts can hide different kinds.** Review, never new on day 72.

## Safety and set-up

Day 70 repeats the survey rules of day 66: the adult checks the paths, the weather, stinging insects, harmful plants, allergies and access the day before; closed shoes, stay on the path and in sight, touch and collect nothing; the same twelve rope loops and thirty boards; clean-up of about four minutes with clothing checked in pairs and hands washed at two sinks. For an unsafe or frozen site, or an allergy, use the second-date photographs and say aloud that they are weaker. Days 71 to 73 are paper only, with papers into folders at the end in two minutes. Day 72 needs only the printed scenes and the template, one set a child, with nothing shared.
