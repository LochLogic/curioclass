# Day 75: Radish windows and later records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the tray of five seedling cups at the window station the day before. Seat the other groups at tables with the student page. On the record sheet choose Today's six, not heard on day 74. The unit 6 model record and the day 72 record stay unchanged in their folders.

### Safety and set-up

Fitted goggles on, rulers below faces, soil stays in the tray. Nothing is tasted. An adult removes any moldy cup. Wash hands at the next washing. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The plant cards are invented for practice. You may pass." Show the station and where the plan goes on the page.

#### 24 min. Plant windows

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each at the station, one group at a time, while the rest draw at their tables. At the station read [[c2-L75c]]; each child measures their own cup to the whole centimeter and says the number (5 children x 30 seconds = 2.5 minutes) and then writes [[c2-L75d]] (1.5 minutes). You watch each child for 30 seconds. At the tables, read [[c2-L75a]] once and then [[c2-L75b]] once; children draw both plans beside the control (6 minutes in all, finishing before their turn). Collect each page as the group leaves the station.

#### 3 min. Moves and wipe-down

Groups move back to their seats. Wipe the rulers and the tray with a dry cloth. Hands are washed at the next washing.

#### 5 min. Records and later data

Read [[c2-L75e]] and [[c2-L75f]] once each; children say or write what the comparison shows (2 minutes). Read [[c2-L75g]]; Today's six say it, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes); the rest write a few words. Collect (1 minute).

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the seven items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: R in the plans, S to U in the later records, V in the strength of evidence. Write one next step on each page. Keep one record each. Response on day 76 only.

### Other rooms

No seedlings: lay out dated measured pictures of one plant with a ruler; mark that record weaker evidence than a plant measured by the child, and say why.

### At home

**Swap.** Any small seedling or a grass shoot in a cup of soil (do not taste soil or plants; wash hands after).

**Skip.** The tray and Today's six.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the plant cards are invented. Read each ask aloud once and wait. Your child draws the plans, measures a cup with a ruler and writes [[c2-L75d]], then answers the later records, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "What did the plant without light look like?" Expect: pale and leaning.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Control, light, water, measure, centimeter, evidence, supplied. Frames: "The control has ___." "Our own record is stronger, because ___." Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 is not scored in class. Day 74's assessment waits for day 76, which hands the pages of days 74 and 75 back with one written next step, repairs by letter group, retries, four fresh exit cards and a hand-off folder.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
