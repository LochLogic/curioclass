# Day 72: Three new places, on your own

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print one set of three scenes and one template a child. Do not show the scenes before today. Cover day 71's class sheet. Set out the watched and heard columns. Plan a scribe for any child who dictates. Key: key-scenes-day72.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; pencils only. No hazard. Count the scenes and templates back at the end.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Each child alone, nothing shared or discussed. Say the scenes are invented for practice, not survey results. Read each prompt aloud once and explain nothing; point to no chart. A child may point, draw or dictate to a scribe; scientific names earn nothing. Today is unscored; written feedback follows.

#### 5 min. Item 1

Read: "Mark the land and the water in each scene" ([[s07-E1]]). Then: "Show how you will look for the plant kinds and the animal kinds" ([[s07-E2]]).

#### 15 min. Item 2

Read each once: draw one picture for each plant kind in each scene ([[s07-E3]]); one for each animal kind ([[s07-E4]]); write how many kinds the meadow shows, counting each kind once ([[s07-E5]]); make a graph with one picture for each kind in each scene ([[s07-E6]]).

#### 10 min. Item 3

Read: which scene shows the fewest kinds ([[s07-E7]]); how the meadow and the stream edge are alike and different in their kinds ([[s07-E8]]); the meadow and the stream edge each show four kinds, are they the same kinds, and say why ([[s07-E9]]). While children work, six rotating children are heard: each says their answer to the last ask, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes), no hint. Write on the heard column.

#### 2 min. Item 4

Read: "Could the marsh have a kind that is not in this picture? Say why." ([[s07-E10]]). Collect every scene and template, named, at the end.

### Marking

After class, 60 minutes of teacher time (ours, untested): note on each child's slip, 12 words or fewer with a small picture, for each part that needs one. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No letter, no score; the ten components are scored once after day 73. Keep the record whole: it goes unchanged to day 73 and the year-end days.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the scenes from scenes-day72.

### At home

**Swap.** No scenes: use three photos of a yard, a pot and a window box, said to be invented.

**Skip.** The heard turns and the slips.

- **35 min. Assessment.** Read each ask aloud once and explain nothing; give no hints. Your child marks land and water and shows how to look (5 minutes), draws each plant and animal kind once, counts the kinds and makes a graph (15), then compares the scenes and says whether the pictures show every kind (15).
  **Say.** "The meadow and the stream edge each show four kinds. Are they the same kinds, and why?" Expect: not necessarily; equal counts can hide different kinds.
- **5 min. Look back.** Write one next step of 12 words or fewer, with a picture.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; it is the unit's one record. Longer: none; do not add a task.

### Words and supports

Kind, same, different, plant, animal, count, graph. Pointing, drawing and a scribe are accepted. Prompts are read aloud without added content.

### Tomorrow

Day 73 returns each record with one written next step, then repairs kinds counted by pictures (error A), equal counts read as the same kinds (error B) and not seen read as not there (error C), by letter group.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
