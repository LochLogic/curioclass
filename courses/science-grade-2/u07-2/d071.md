# Day 71: What can our two looks tell us?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print claim strips and six wet and dry pairs. Lay each group's dated graphs side by side. Close the flap card. Board question: What can we claim? Pick today's six; the exit six are the next six. Key: key-wet-and-dry-pair-days-71-73.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; pencils only. No hazard. Count the cards back at the end.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Day 70's exit; the card is invented. If many made error C (the box lost two kinds), it takes the whole class; otherwise a small group while the rest read their graphs. Show the flap card, the leaf pile. **Ask.** Pip drew no toad. What can Pip say? Expect: Pip did not see one; one may be hiding. Lift the flap: a toad. Put "no toad here" beside "not seen is not there."

#### 5 min. Read the records

Groups read their dated graphs aloud, a reader a spot: kinds on each date, kinds on both dates, kinds on one only (3 minutes). Six rotating children say one thing the records show, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Compare

Groups find kinds on both dates, kinds on one date only, and whether the counts match; where counts are equal, touch whether the kinds match (6 minutes). Say three reasons a kind may be missing: it was hiding, the place changed (mown, trampled, weather, time of day), or we looked differently. Keeping loop, time and labels the same does not mean we saw every kind. Each group names one kind on both dates and one on one date, 6 x 30 s = 3 minutes.

#### 12 min. Anchor answer

The wet and dry pair, invented (3 minutes, whole class; error D from day 68). Groups circle the kinds on the rain garden and the gravel lot and count circles. **Ask.** Which place has more kinds? Expect: neither, three each, and the kinds differ. Two cards, not a rule.

Then the class dictates the answer to the unit's question and you write it (5 minutes): kinds seen both times, kinds seen once, and "we cannot tell" where the records do not decide. Use the frame on the student page. If a spot was mown or trampled, or photographs stood in, the comparison is inconclusive. Each child writes or draws a claim strip, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___" (4 minutes); a child may dictate to a partner or adult.

#### 6 min. Revise the model

The class sheet and question board get the answer and its limits (3 minutes). **Ask.** What can our two looks not tell us? Expect: all the kinds, or why a kind is missing. Read the sheet aloud (1 minute). Children add questions to the board (2 minutes).

#### 2 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented for practice. Read [[s07-X71]] once; all whisper their answer to a partner (0.5 minute). Six rotating children say why, 15 seconds each (6 x 15 s = 1.5 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Sheets into folders; cards counted.

### Marking

After class, 10 minutes: read each strip for kinds counted once, a shared or different kind named, a limit named, no ranking of places. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Practice only.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the rain garden (2 irises, 2 dragonflies, 1 water strider) and the gravel lot (2 thistles, 2 crickets, 1 pigeon).

### At home

**Swap.** No graphs: use two drawings of one spot made on different days.

**Skip.** The groups, the chart and the exit rotation.

- **3 min. Flap.** Hide a toy under a cloth; draw what you see.
  **Say.** "Can we say nothing is under it?" Expect: no, we did not see it.
- **6 min. Read.** Read the two drawings: which kinds on both days.
- **10 min. Strip.** Write or say "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___."
  **Say.** "What can our looks not tell us?" Expect: every kind, or why one is missing.
- **8 min. Pair.** Wet and dry cards: circle kinds, count circles.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X71]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the strip and the exit. Never cut "we cannot tell". Longer: one new board question.

### Words and supports

Cannot tell (the records do not decide). Frame on the page. Pointing and a scribe are accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says wet places always have more kinds (error D). Day 72: each child alone, three new scenes. Day 73 repairs by letter.
