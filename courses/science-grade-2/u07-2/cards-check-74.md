# Check cards, day 74

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 74. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except a date or where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Two sealed sets.

- **Raft set.** The raft card (six craft sticks side by side held by two bands), the table card (the eight pieces laid out) and the fence card (the sticks standing in a row).
- **Records set.** A spoon of wax dripping and cooling into a round then warmed again; a muffin half warmed to toast then chilled; a peach slice dated Monday, Tuesday and Friday.
