# Exit cards, days 70, 71 and 73

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Each exit has picture cards every child touches and a spoken stem read once. The teacher hears a rotating six, 15 seconds each. Day 72 has no exit.

## Day 70 exit: a flower box twice

**Picture:** Pip, a child, beside a flower box. Monday's page shows four different drawings and a clock at five minutes. Friday's page shows two drawings and a clock at one minute. Two cards: yes and no.
**Say:** "Pip drew a flower box for five minutes on Monday and saw four kinds. On Friday Pip looked for one minute and saw two kinds. Touch yes or no: can we say the flower box lost two kinds?"
**Key (teacher):** no; Friday's look was shorter. Error C is touching yes.

## Day 71 exit: a creek bank and a hillside

**Picture:** a damp creek bank card with a broad-leaf plant, a newt and a water bug. A dry hillside card with a sage bush, a pale flower, a bee and a grasshopper. Two cards: yes and no.
**Say:** "Pip says wet places always have more kinds. Touch yes or no: do these two cards agree with Pip?"
**Key (teacher):** no; the dry hillside shows four kinds and the damp bank shows three. Error D is touching yes.

## Day 73 exit: a park and a ditch

**Picture a:** a park picture showing three identical grass tufts and two identical ants. Three number cards: two, three and five.
**Say (a):** "Pip counted five kinds. Touch the right number of kinds."
**Key (teacher):** two. Error A is touching five or three.
**Picture b:** a ditch picture showing two identical reed clumps and three identical frogs. Two cards: yes and no.
**Say (b):** "Pip says the park and the ditch show the same kinds because both have two. Touch yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** no; the park shows grass and ants, and the ditch shows reeds and frogs. Error B is touching yes.
