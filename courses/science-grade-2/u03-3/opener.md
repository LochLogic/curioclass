# Section 3.3 opener: choosing from the records

## What the section does

Two days, 37 and 38. Day 37 is the second half of the end of the unit: each child works through a packet of new records for three unfamiliar materials and chooses one for each of five jobs, saying what the records show. Day 38 returns written feedback on days 36 and 37, repairs by letter group, and runs a fresh retry of only the parts a child has not yet shown. Day 38 ends with an exit that is practice and unscored. Day 36 carries forward and is not re-scored.

## How the days run

Day 37 opens with directions, then the packet, then collect. Read each task aloud once, one ask at a time. Children read only labels, numbers and the key. Mark with one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen; on the record sheet score each look-for 2, 1 or 0. Day 38 opens with five minutes of return and read feedback. Give each child one written next step for one component only.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A choice rests on a result, not on a favorite or a color.**
- **Each job needs its own property.** The ledge needs a small drop, the wrapper needs to wrap a curve, the stamping base needs no dent, the lining needs to hold a block, the mat needs the farthest climb.
- **A different job can change the best choice,** because it asks for a different property.
- **People test materials from nature before they design with them.** Wood from a tree is tested for bend or dent before it is chosen for a block.
- **Only length is a number.** 1 centimeter apart is about the same.

## Safety

Day 37 is paper only; there is no safety step. On day 38 the repair sets are pictures only. Keep the sealed cups, samples and sticks put away until the next unit.
