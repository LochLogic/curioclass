# Retry cards, day 38

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Fresh cases, read one step at a time, only for the component a child has not yet shown. Practice and unscored. These are the pictures for the exit items on day 38.

## Card a (hardness): two equally rough samples

**Picture:** Two rough squares of the same size, labelled left and right. A blunt stick presses each one. The left one has a dent. The right one has none.
**Say:** "These are equally rough. The same stick pressed each one the same. Touch the harder one."
**Key (teacher):** The right one, with no dent.

## Card b (color tells the bend)

**Picture:** Two brown strips, one curving over a hand and one held out straight.
**Say:** "Both are brown. Do matching colors tell us how they bend? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No. Same color, different bend.

## Card c (water, solid or liquid)

**Picture:** A row of four pictures of water in a sealed tray: liquid water flowing, then the same water cooler with ice crystals forming, then mostly ice, then a solid block of ice holding its shape.
**Say:** "It cooled slowly. Is it still water at the end? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** Yes. It changed from flowing to holding its shape, and it is still water.

## Card d (fair test)

**Picture:** Two plans. One: a lamp fixed in one place with a different sample each time. Two: the lamp moved closer for one sample and a different sample.
**Say:** "Touch the plan that keeps the light comparison fair."
**Key (teacher):** The plan with the lamp fixed.

## Card e (the strip that bends more)

**Picture:** Strip P is smooth and bends in a curve. Strip Q is rough and stays straight.
**Say:** "Touch the strip that bends more."
**Key (teacher):** Strip P.

## Card f (job choice from a test)

**Picture:** Two bars for water climb: P at 1, Q at 7. Two hat pictures: a doll dry under P and a doll wet under Q.
**Say:** "Touch the strip to make the replacement rain hat."
**Key (teacher):** Strip P, because the doll stayed dry under P.

## Card g (water entering a strip)

**Picture:** Two equal strips. One has no wet mark. The other has a wet mark climbing 4 centimeters.
**Say:** "Touch the strip that shows water going into it."
**Key (teacher):** The 4 centimeter strip.

## Card h (a level ledge)

**Picture:** Two ledge strips with equal loads. One drops 2 centimeters. The other drops 4 centimeters.
**Say:** "Touch the stiffer ledge."
**Key (teacher):** The strip that dropped 2 centimeters.
