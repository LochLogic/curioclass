# Day 38: Repair it, then explain the hat

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write each child's one next step on the feedback form from your marking of days 35 to 37. Sort the records into four piles: errors A with B, C, D, and E with F, G and H. Set a table for each pile with its repair picture and picture directions. Put the retry cards in order a to h on your desk. Mark the six children you will hear.

### Safety and set-up

None: pictures and paper only. Samples, sticks and cups stay put away.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and read feedback

Hand back each child's day 36 record, day 37's assessment (the packet) and feedback form. Hear the rotating six say their next step back, 30 seconds each (3 minutes); any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Repair by letter groups

Four turns of 3 minutes with you and 3 to move; the other groups work from picture directions. Hand-backs: the day 35 slips (s03-X35) and day 36 items s03-E3 and s03-E6 for error A with error B; s03-E5 for error C; s03-E1 for error D; day 37 items s03-E14, s03-E13 and s03-E9 for error E with errors F, G and H, where the first letter most children made takes the time. The paper moves are in the repair set. If many made one letter, teach it to the whole class for 5 minutes and shrink each turn to 2. A child with nothing to repair writes a test record for one more material and says which job it fits.

#### 10 min. Fresh retries

Only the missed component, on paper, one step at a time. Read cards a to h (s03-X38a to s03-X38h) once each and pause 45 seconds. A child marks or says only the card on their feedback form. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen; record help apart. The retry never rescores day 37.

#### 5 min. Closing explanation

With the materials chart, day 35, and the pattern chart in view, ask: "What changed between the hat that failed and the hat we chose?" Partners talk 30 seconds, then three children answer. Expect days 30 and 31: some materials let water pass and some do not, so the chosen hat keeps the doll dry under our tests. Then ask: "What did our tests show about boots and mittens, and what did they not show?" Same turns. Expect: water was shown, warmth was not tested. Add "warmth" to the question board.

### Other rooms

Teaching one child: go over the feedback, redo the weakest piece, then say why the hat keeps the doll dry and what was not tested.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw the two strips on paper.

**Skip.** The letter groups and the question board.

- **10 min. Look back.** Read the day 37 answers and pick one that had no reason.
- **15 min. Try again.** Draw two strips, one smooth and bendy, one rough and stiff.
  **Say.** "Which strip bends more? How can you tell?" Expect: the one that bends easily, not the rough one.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Your child answers the one the day 37 answers missed, alone: [[s03-X38a]], [[s03-X38b]], [[s03-X38c]], [[s03-X38d]], [[s03-X38e]], [[s03-X38f]], [[s03-X38g]] or [[s03-X38h]].

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: repair only the letter most children made; never cut the retries or the closing question. Longer: a test record for one more material and its job.

### Words and supports

Tested, result, because, job, bends, dent, climb, leak, warmth. Frame: "The tests showed ___, but they did not show ___." Read every card aloud; pointing or drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

The retries show who has mended error A, B, C, D, E, F, G or H, and nothing is rescored. Unit 3 ends today. Day 39 opens the next unit with a block castle becoming a boat, chocolate hard again and a cooked egg, to ask what can change back.
