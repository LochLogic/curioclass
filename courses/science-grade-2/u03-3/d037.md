# Day 37: Choose from the records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a packet and an answer sheet for each child. The packet key (key-packet-day37) is for you only; never print it for children. Seat children so each sees only their own paper. Keep the choice model and the materials chart, day 35, on the wall but do not point to them. Put the answer chart on your clipboard. Mark the six children you will hear today on the record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

None: paper only. No samples, sticks or water are out.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say aloud that every record is invented for practice. Read the steps neutrally, one at a time: look at the packet, answer each task on the answer sheet in a few words or numbers, you may pass, and nobody can help. Hand out the papers.

#### 25 min. The item

Each child works alone. Read each task once, one ask at a time, when most hands are still, then read it again only if asked: [[s03-E7]], [[s03-E8]], [[s03-E9]], [[s03-E10]], [[s03-E11]], [[s03-E12]], [[s03-E13]], [[s03-E14]], [[s03-E15]]. Give no hints and no conferences; a scribe or a spoken answer is allowed and noted. In the last 9 minutes hear the rotating six say their answer to [[s03-E14]] at your desk, 1 minute each (6 children at 60 seconds is 6 minutes); say nothing about it. Mark each look-for on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Record help apart from independent work.

#### 5 min. Collect

Collect the packets and answer sheets, and count them back.

### Other rooms

Same paper path in a room with nothing. Teaching one child: read the packet in two short sittings; the child points and explains; give no help.

### After class

Read the answer sheets against the answer chart. Score the item out of 14: one for the length records, one for the other results, then for each of five jobs one for a supported choice and one for naming the test, one for why a different job changes the choice and one for the wood link. Only "shown" counts a point. Write for each child one component and one next step, about 60 minutes in all, on the feedback form.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the table from the packet onto paper.

**Skip.** The rotating six and the clipboard.

- **5 min. Assessment directions.** Say the numbers are invented for practice. Read each task once.
- **25 min. The assessment.** Your child does the nine tasks alone, with no hints.
- **10 min. Look together.** Read the answers after.
  **Say.** "Why can a different job change the best choice?" Expect: the job decides which test result to look at.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the check is not shortened. Longer: none; the day teaches nothing new.

### Words and supports

Tested, result, because, job, bends, dent, climb, held, slipped. Frames: "I choose ___ because ___." "It is not good for ___ because ___." Read all prompts aloud; pointing, saying, drawing or a scribe all count. Rehearse the property words with gestures before the day, never during the item.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 returns each packet, and day 36's assessment records, with one next step each; day 36 is not rescored. The error letters to watch are error A (rough means hard), error E (a favourite as the reason), error F (no climb means it took in the most), error G (bends most means stiffest) and error H (takes in water means best hat). They go to the group that repairs them, and a fresh short retry follows.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
