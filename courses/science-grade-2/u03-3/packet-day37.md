# Day 37 packet: three unfamiliar materials

A reserved packet printed only on day 37. Read the directions once. Children read only labels, numbers and the key. Print "invented for practice" on each record. No people in any picture. Do not reuse any picture from the unit.

## The records

Three materials: card X, sheet Y and cloth Z. All three were tested the same way.

| | Card X | Sheet Y | Cloth Z |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Water climb along a strip (cm) | 3 | 0 | 6 |
| Drop at the middle under the same load (cm) | 1 | 5 | 3 |
| Gentle press with a blunt stick | no dent | dent | dent |
| Feel | rough | smooth | rough |
| Same block on the same slope | held | slipped | held |
| Wrapped around a curve | cracked | wrapped | wrapped |

## Tasks

1. Put the water numbers and the drop numbers in the right boxes of your record.
2. Put the press, feel, slope and curve results in the right boxes.
3. A display ledge must not bend much. Choose a material and show what tells you.
4. A parcel wrapper must wrap around a curve. Choose a material and show what tells you.
5. A stamping base must not dent when it is pressed. Choose a material and show what tells you.
6. A lining on a sloping surface must stop a block from sliding. Choose a material and show what tells you.
7. A mat must draw water farthest along a strip. Choose a material and show what tells you.
8. Look at your choices for the ledge and the wrapper. Why can a different job change the best choice?
9. **Picture:** A bamboo plant, a bamboo pole beside it, and a rail on a bridge. How could knowing about bamboo help people design a rail that does not bend?
