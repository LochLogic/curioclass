# Repair set (day 38)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Group sheets for the repair by letter, three minutes with you for each group. Each case below is new; none is the case on a day 36, 37 or 38 item.

## Group A: rough is not hard

**Picture:** A rough sponge square with a stick pressing in a deep dent. A smooth stone tile drawn in plain lines with a stick pressing and no dent.
**Say:** "Which one is harder? Why?"
**Key (teacher):** The tile, because it did not dent. Rough is how it feels.

## Group B: the same colour, a different bend

**Picture:** Two black ribbons. One is limp over a finger. The other is a stiff band held straight.
**Say:** "Both are black. Do they bend the same?"
**Key (teacher):** No. Colour tells only the colour.

## Group C: ice is water

**Picture:** A spoon of ice, the same spoon after melting, the same spoon of water poured in a cup.
**Say:** "What stayed the same? Is the ice water?"
**Key (teacher):** The water. The ice is water that is solid.

## Group D: tape the lamp

**Picture:** Two plans for a light test of a leaf and a cloth. One moves the lamp for each. One tapes the lamp.
**Say:** "Which plan is fair? Tape the lamp."
**Key (teacher):** The plan with the lamp taped.

## Group E, F, G, H: one picture each, taken in the order most children named

- **E:** "A job card says a bag needs a stiff base. Pip says the favourite is best for everything. Pair the job with a test result." Key: the job decides.
- **F:** "Strip 1 had water climb 5. Strip 2 had none. Which took water in?" Key: strip 1.
- **G:** "A ramp dropped 1 under a load. Another dropped 6. Which stays more level?" Key: the drop of 1.
- **H:** "A cover soaked water in and the doll got wet. Another stayed dry. Which is the better hat?" Key: the one that stayed dry.
