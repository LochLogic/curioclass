# Retrieval cards (day 66)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Unscored. Two cards, one ask each, about five and three minutes. A miss gets a modeled second try in the last minute.

## Card 1: needs and places (the needs-and-places picture mat)

**Picture:** a schoolyard corner. A child sits on a bench. A bird stands on the ground next to a shallow puddle. A small plant grows out of the soil. Printed beside three of the drawings: "air" (a curl of wind lines), "puddle" and "soil". A blank line is drawn from nothing.
**Say:** "Draw lines from each living thing to the things it gets from this place."

## Card 2: a root and leaves (a new picture)

**Picture:** a young pea plant in a window box. Its roots are drawn below the soil line with a dark wet patch around them. Its leaves face a bright sun drawn at the top corner.
**Say (a):** "Mark the root that takes in water."
**Say (b):** "Mark the leaves that get light."
