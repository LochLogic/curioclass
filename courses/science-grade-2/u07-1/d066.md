# Day 66: What lives in these two spots?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, an adult walks both spots for glass, sharp things, waste, standing water, nests, poison ivy or nettle; if found, use the photographs. Check the nurse's sting-allergy list. Pick two equal spots side by side; lay six loops in each. Pin day 65 chart. Post the compelling question. Key: key-retrieval-cards-day66.

### Safety and set-up

Closed shoes. A second adult comes, one at each spot; without one, use the photographs. The adult carries the nurse's phone and any listed epinephrine pen. In sun, water and hats; on ice, stay in. Unsafe site or an allergy: use the matched schoolyard photographs, two a group; say a photograph is weaker. Read the safety sheet aloud.

### The day

#### 4 min. Doorway look and predictions

From the door the class looks at the two spots. **Ask.** Do these two spots hold the same living things? Expect: any guess. Hands up: same, partly, different (1 minute). Three of today's six say why (1 minute).

#### 2 min. Carry in from unit 6

Read the day 65 consensus chart aloud. Two children say one link each. If many missed day 65's exit (errors A, B and C), trace one link again.

#### 8 min. Retrieval (unscored)

Say the mat is invented for practice. Card 1 (5 minutes): "Draw lines from each living thing to the things it gets from this place." A child counts as an animal. Miss: trace one link on the earlier picture model. Card 2, the root and leaf card (3 minutes): "Mark the root that takes in water." Then: "Mark the leaves that get light." Miss: use unit 5's needs-arrow drawing.

#### 4 min. Prepare and move

A habitat is a place where living things find what they need. Say: five minutes sees only some of what lives there. Four rules: stay on the path, stay in sight, watch and touch nothing, tell an adult if unsure. The class walks together; an adult counts heads.

#### 10 min. Survey

Six groups of five, a loop in each spot. Five minutes at spot 1, five at spot 2, the change called from your watch (a pace cue). Each child draws every living thing seen on their own sheet, repeats too (sorted tomorrow). Nothing is touched, moved or collected.

#### 5 min. Habitat picture model

Each group sticks one drawing from each child on the class sheet, a box a spot (2 minutes). Six rotating children say what lives in one spot and its link (2 minutes). Read the sheet aloud (1 minute).

**Ask.** No one drew a bee at spot 1. Can we say there are no bees there? Expect: we did not see one; one may be hiding.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Collect loops and boards; check clothing for insects in pairs; wash hands, two sinks. Any late wash is at the next washing, said aloud.

#### 2 min. Exit

Say the picture is invented for practice. Read [[s07-X66]] once; all touch a card (0.5 minute). Read [[s07-X66b]]; all draw while six say why for the first (1.5 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No outdoor spot: the photographs. No printer: draw each spot on folded paper.

### At home

**Swap.** A book on the knee for a board; a loop of string for a rope loop.

**Skip.** The class sheet and the class hands.

- **4 min. Guess.** Look at two spots from a door.
  **Say.** "Do these two spots hold the same living things?" Expect: any guess, with a reason.
- **5 min. Needs.** Draw lines from a plant, a bird and a child to air, water and soil.
  **Say.** "What does each one get from this place?" Expect: air for all, water for each, soil for the plant.
- **13 min. Look.** Five minutes at each spot, watch only: draw every living thing. Go outdoors with a grown-up, and check the spot for stinging insects and harmful plants first. Wash hands after.
- **5 min. Tell.** Tell what lives in each spot.
  **Say.** "Did you see everything that lives here?" Expect: no, some may be hiding.
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X66]] and [[s07-X66b]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one spot, the model and the exit. Never cut the exit or watch-only rules. Longer: a third spot.

### Words and supports

Habitat, survey (a careful look at a place), inventory (a picture list of what we saw). Frame: "I saw ___ in spot ___." Pointing and a scribe are accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says there are no worms when none were seen (error C). Day 67 repairs it, then sorts the drawings: circle repeats, one picture a kind.
