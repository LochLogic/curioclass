# Flap cards (days 67, 71 and 73)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

For repairing error C, a kind that was not seen is not a kind that is not there. Each card has a paper flap that lifts. Two cards; the cases are new and are not any exit. One child follows the same steps one to one with an adult.

**Flap card 1, a leaf pile.** First view: a patch of ground with a pile of leaves and a stone. Under the flap, which sits over the leaf pile: a toad sitting still.
**Flap card 2, a shrub.** First view: a shrub with a few berries on a fence. Under the flap, which sits behind the shrub: a rabbit crouched low.
**Say:** "Pip looked at the first view and drew no toad. What can Pip say?" Lift the flap. "Now what can Pip say?"
**Key (teacher):** first view: Pip did not see a toad, and one may be hiding. After the flap: there is a toad, and Pip's first look missed it. The same steps are used for the rabbit. The wrong claim, "there is no toad here", is shown beside the right one. Use card 1 on days 67 and 71 and card 2 on day 73.
