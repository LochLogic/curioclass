# Day 68: A pond and two forests

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print five sets of the three evidence cards a group, one set a child. Have the circle-and-count card ready. Put the day's question on the question board: Are all places of one kind alike? Choose today's six. Key: key-pond-and-forest-cards-day68.

### Safety and set-up

Indoors; pencils only. No hazard. Count the cards back at the end.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Day 67's exit (error A). Say the card is invented for practice. Show the circle-and-count card: five identical ladybugs, a fern and a swallow. "Circle the pictures that are the same kind. Count your circles." Put "seven kinds" beside "three kinds: five ladybugs are one circle." If many missed it, the whole class; otherwise a small group while the rest start.

#### 12 min. Observe and record

Say the cards are invented for practice, not real ponds or forests. Each child has the pond card, forest A and forest B: four minutes a card (3 x 4 minutes). On a folded scrap, one row a card: circle repeats, draw one picture per kind, count the kinds. Reuse day 67's labels; agree a new label aloud for each new kind, such as "round shiny back swimmer".

#### 10 min. Compare

Groups find the kind that is on the pond and on forest A, a kind only the pond has, and the three counts (7 minutes).

**Ask.** Why do forest A and forest B differ? Expect: different kinds, and a different number; one has a squirrel and ferns.

Six groups each report one finding, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes). Expect: dragonfly on both; pond five, forest A four, forest B two.

#### 11 min. Revise the model

Add the pond and both forests to the class sheet, one picture a kind, and count each row together (4 minutes). The class states a pattern: places differ in kinds, and one forest is not another forest (3 minutes). Pip says "every forest has four kinds." The class tests the line against the cards (2 minutes). Six rotating children say "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___," 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Three cards say nothing about every pond or forest; say so.

#### 2 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented for practice. Read [[s07-X68a]] once; all touch a card (0.5 minute). Read [[s07-X68b]] once; all touch yes or no (0.5 minute). Six rotating children say why to the second one, 10 seconds each (6 x 10 s = 1 minute). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards back into the sets and counted.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pond (3 lily-pad plants, 2 edge plants, 2 fish, a dragonfly, a water beetle), forest A (3 trees, 2 ferns, a squirrel, 2 dragonflies) and forest B (2 trees, 2 beetles).

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: look at two places you know, or draw a pond and a forest with the kinds your child names.

**Skip.** The class sheet, groups and Pip.

- **3 min. Look back.** Five ladybugs, a fern and a swallow are drawn.
  **Say.** "How many different kinds?" Expect: three; five ladybugs are one kind.
- **12 min. Cards.** Draw one picture for each kind in each place; count.
  **Say.** "Which kind is in both places? Which is in only one?" Expect: a kind named in each.
- **8 min. Pip.** Pip says every forest has four kinds.
  **Say.** "Is Pip right? Why?" Expect: no; one forest shows fewer kinds.
- **5 min. Say it.** "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___."
- **3 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X68a]] and [[s07-X68b]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the pond and one forest, and the exit. Never cut the exit. Longer: draw a third forest and compare.

### Words and supports

Shared kind (in both places), different kind (in only one). Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Pointing and drawing are accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says no kind is shared when one is (error B) and who says every pond holds five (error D). Day 69 repairs both, then compares a desert card and a shore card.
