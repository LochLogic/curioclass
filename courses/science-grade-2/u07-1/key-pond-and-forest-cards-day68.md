# Pond card, forest A and forest B (day 68)

**Key (teacher):** pond five kinds, forest A four kinds, forest B two kinds. The kind on both the pond and forest A is the dragonfly. Only the pond has lily-pad plant, edge plant, fish and water beetle. The tree is on both forests. Forest A has four and forest B has two, so one forest is not another forest. Pip says every forest has four kinds; the cards show it is not so. Nothing here ranks places: these are three invented cards. Mushrooms are left out on purpose, so that every kind is a plant or an animal.
