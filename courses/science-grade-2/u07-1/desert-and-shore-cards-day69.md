# Desert card and shore card (day 69)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two cards, five paper sets a group, one set a child. Every kind is drawn the same way wherever it appears, and nothing is hidden or cut off. They are invented for practice, not survey results and not claims about every desert or shore. Kinds are described by shape, not by name.

**Desert card, six pictures, three kinds.** Sandy ground with rocks. Two identical tall cacti, one lizard with a long tail and three identical stout dark beetles that tip their back ends up.
**Shore card, six pictures, three kinds.** Wet sand at the edge of the sea. Three identical strands of seaweed, two identical gulls and one long pink sand worm lying in the wet sand.

**Say:** "Circle the pictures that are the same kind. Draw one picture for each kind. Count your kinds."
