# Safety sheet (unit 7)

Read each line aloud with the picture. Children point to the picture for each line.

1. Closed shoes on. (Picture: a pair of shoes.)
2. We stay on the path and in sight. (Picture: children in a line on a path.)
3. We watch. We touch nothing. (Picture: a child with hands behind the back looking at a plant.)
4. We move and collect nothing, not even a stone. (Picture: a stone left in place.)
5. We tell a grown-up if we are unsure. (Picture: a child with a raised hand.)
6. We check our clothes for insects in pairs. (Picture: two children looking at a sleeve.)
7. We wash our hands. (Picture: hands under a tap.)
