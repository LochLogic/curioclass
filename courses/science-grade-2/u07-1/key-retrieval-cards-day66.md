# Key: retrieval-cards-day66

Teacher only. Do not print for children.

## Card 1: needs and places (the needs-and-places picture mat)

**Key (teacher):** accept the child as an animal. The bird goes to the air and to the puddle. The plant goes to the air, the puddle and the soil. The child goes to the air; the child's drinking water comes from a tap, not this puddle, so one line is enough. Land is a place and a source for some living things. Do not imply that every living thing needs soil. A miss is traced on the earlier picture model, one link, then the child tries another.

## Card 2: a root and leaves (a new picture)

**Key (teacher):** the roots in the dark wet patch; the leaves facing the sun. A miss is traced on unit 5's needs-arrow drawing: part, what it takes in, what it does for the plant. Retrieval is never scored.
