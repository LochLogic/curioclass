# Circle-and-count card (days 68, 70 and 73)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

For repairing error A, counting pictures of one kind is counting individuals; we count kinds. One card for a small group at the teacher's table, and a copy for the partners working from the picture directions. The case is new and is not any exit. One child follows the same steps one to one with an adult.

**Picture:** a grass strip. Five identical ladybugs in a loose row, one fern and one swallow in flight. Seven pictures in all.
**Say:** "Circle the pictures that are the same kind. Count your circles. How many kinds does the card show?"
**Key (teacher):** the five ladybugs are one circle, the fern one circle and the swallow one circle: three kinds. The wrong answer is seven, counting every picture, or five, counting the ladybugs. Beside it, the right idea: five ladybugs are one kind, so circle the same and count circles.
