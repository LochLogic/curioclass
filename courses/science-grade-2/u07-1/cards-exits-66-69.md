# Exit cards, days 66 to 69

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Each exit has picture cards every child touches and a spoken stem read once. The teacher hears a rotating six, 15 seconds each. Every card is invented for practice.

## Day 66 exit: Pip and the worms

**Picture:** Pip, a child kneeling beside a bare patch of ground with a hand lens held away from it. Pip's page shows a drawing of the patch and no worms. Two sentence cards: "there are no worms here" and "Pip did not see worms, and some may be hiding".
**Say:** "Pip looked at a patch for one minute and drew no worms. Touch what Pip can say."
**Key (teacher):** Pip did not see worms, and some may be hiding. Error C is touching "there are no worms here".

## Day 67 exit: a patch card

**Picture:** one patch card showing four identical pill bugs, two identical moss clumps and one dandelion. Three number cards: three, four and seven.
**Say:** "Touch how many different kinds the card shows."
**Key (teacher):** three. Error A is touching seven or four.

## Day 68 exit: two cards, then Pip

**Picture a:** card X, a garden bed, shows a tall flower, a worm and a sparrow. Card Y, a path edge, shows a worm and moss. The worm is drawn the same on both.
**Say (a):** "Touch the kind that is on both cards."
**Key (teacher):** the worm. Error B is saying none are the same, because X has three kinds and Y has two.
**Picture b:** a pond card with five kinds on it, and a new pond card with three kinds. Two cards: yes and no.
**Say (b):** "Pip says every pond has five kinds because our pond card has five. A new pond card shows three kinds. Touch yes or no: is Pip right?"
**Key (teacher):** no. Error D is touching yes.

## Day 69 exit: eelgrass bed and tidepool

**Picture:** two cards. A sheltered coastal eelgrass bed: three identical eelgrass blades, one crab, one clam and one snail, six pictures. A rocky tidepool: one sea star, two identical mussels, one snail and one hermit crab, five pictures. Every kind is drawn clearly and the hermit crab is not drawn like the crab. Three cards: the eelgrass bed, the tidepool and neither, they show the same number.
**Say (a):** "Touch which card shows more kinds."
**Key (teacher):** neither; each shows four kinds. Error A is touching the eelgrass bed because it has more pictures.
**Say (b):** "Touch one kind that only one of the cards shows."
**Key (teacher):** eelgrass, crab, clam, sea star, mussel or hermit crab, any kind that is on one card only; the snail is on both cards. Error B is saying both show the same kinds because each shows four.
