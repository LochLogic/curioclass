# Day 63: Build a model for a new animal

## Teacher page

### Before class

An adult folds and covers the pipe-cleaner ends, then labels and sets out one jay set a child on a tray. Cover the carry-arrow chart. Split the class: tables 1 to 3 are watched today, tables 4 to 6 on day 64. Print the record sheet's watched and heard columns. Key: key-retrieval-card-day63.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on. Pieces below faces, away from mouths. Tie hair back. Stop for any bare wire and use paper. No live animals. Clean-up is inside the last 3 minutes: wipe and bag eye protection, wipe tables.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieve alone

Each child works alone on the retrieval card. Read once, no hints: "Place each word card on the picture. Then mark the piece that is a seed and the piece that is pollen." [[s06-E11]] and [[s06-E12]]. Unscored. Collect.

#### 3 min. The new card

Show the jay card, invented for practice. Captions give places and parts only. Say the acorn is a seed. Read once: "Design and build something that does this animal's plant job. Show what your parts represent and try it. Use the result to improve your model and explain its limits."

#### 2 min. Sketch

Each child sketches a design on the model record.

#### 10 min. Build and try

Each child alone builds from their own set and tries it on the tray. No hints, no reteaching; read the card again if asked.

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Tables 1 to 3 (15 children), one window of 4 minutes a table. Each child about 45 seconds (5 x 45 s = 3.75 minutes). Say one ask at a time, about 7 seconds each: [[s06-E1]], [[s06-E2]], [[s06-E3]], [[s06-E5]], [[s06-E6]], [[s06-E7]]. Mark the look-fors on the watched column, and write error A (pollen taken for a seed) or error B (parts drawn on, not moved) if it shows; scoring is of the retained day 64 version. The other 15, alone, label the sock and flower models on their day 61 and 62 sheets and draw one limit; unscored.

#### 3 min. Moves and clean-up

Two moves of 30 seconds, then eye protection wiped, tables wiped. The adult labels and stores each set with its record.

### Marking

After class, 60 minutes: mark [[s06-E4]] from each drawing. Write one note of 12 words or fewer, with a small picture, on a feedback slip clipped to each record. Use shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No set: draw each part on card.

### At home

**Swap.** Any card for the beak halves; any small card for the acorn; torn paper for leaf litter.

**Skip.** The windows and the retrieval card.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Show the jay card and read it once. Your child designs and builds with paper, gives no hints, and says what each part stands for. [[s06-E1]], [[s06-E2]], [[s06-E3]], [[s06-E5]], [[s06-E6]] and [[s06-E7]].
  **Say.** "Show me where your model starts, and how it carries the acorn." Expect: at the oak outline; the halves close on the acorn and open over the leaf litter.
- **10 min. Seed or pollen.** [[s06-E12]] with a brown and a yellow paper piece.
  **Say.** "Which piece is the seed, and which is pollen?" Expect: brown is the seed; yellow is pollen.
- **5 min. Look back.** Write one note of 12 words or fewer.

### Shorter or longer

Cut the retrieval card, never the build or the asks. Longer: none today.

### Words and supports

Jay, acorn, beak, oak, plant outline, stands for. Frame: "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___." Pointing and drawing are accepted.

### Tomorrow

Each child reads one note and revises. Day 64 watches tables 4 to 6 and keeps the records; errors A to D go to day 65.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
