# Day 61: Make a sock that carries a seed

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, build each sock on its leg, trial that a hooked seed clings, tape a zero line beside the ruler. Swap a failing set. Return the day 60 tickets. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on for every piece. Pieces stay below faces and away from mouths; hair tied back. Walk slowly; no scattering. The mat edge is taped down. Clean-up, 4 minutes: wipe and bag eye protection (1), count pieces into trays (2), wipe tables (1).

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Say the cards are invented. Error A: "Touch the seed path. Touch the powder path. Say which is which." Expect: brown is the seed; yellow is powder. Many: whole class. A few: a small group.

#### 3 min. Ruler retrieval

Pairs use the ruler strip: mark where zero starts, write where the end stops. Expect: the start line; 9 centimeters. After a miss, lay the strip's left end at the 1 mark, then slide it to zero.

#### 4 min. Define the job

On the chart: carry a hooked card seed from the zero line to a new place. The limit, the most we allow, is 30 centimeters. Say the seed is a model, not a real burr. Walk the design-cycle chart: the problem now; sketch, build, test, compare follow.

**Ask.** What caught, and what moved? Expect: the hooks caught the fuzz; the seed moved.

#### 7 min. Build and sketch

Groups set the sock on the mat beside the ruler (3 minutes). Each child sketches it and fills "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___." (4 minutes); a scribe helps a child who needs one. Expect: sock for fur; hooks catch loops.

#### 8 min. Trials

One walk each, about 90 seconds (5 x 90 s = 7.5 minutes): touch the seed to the sock, walk it, let go, read where the edge stops at zero, to the whole centimeter. The next child writes it; the other three hold the zero line, call "stop", and read the ruler. No cling: record 0; the whole walk: "rode the whole walk, 30". A seed that will not cling: swap the set; if it still fails, read the trial cards, said to be invented.

#### 3 min. Record and compare

Each child writes their distance and stuck or fell, draws one change that would help the seed hold, and names one thing the model cannot show. Expect: growing; a real animal's walk. Like the tray on day 15: it showed how soil moves, not how long.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented. Read [[s06-X61a]]; all touch (0.5 minute). Read [[s06-X61b]]; six rotating children answer, 15 seconds each (6 x 15 s = 1.5 minutes). Read [[s06-X61c]]; all write the number (0.5). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Clean up

As in Safety and set-up.

### Other rooms

No sock or seeds: use the trial cards and draw the sock.

### At home

**Swap.** No ruler: count finger widths and say it is a model. No seed: use a paper square.

**Skip.** The group walks and the class tally.

- **5 min. Route.** Show a brown arrow and a yellow arrow.
  **Say.** "Which path is the seed?" Expect: the brown one; the yellow powder is not a seed.
- **15 min. Sock.** Put a fuzzy sock on a hand and touch a card seed to it. Walk it along a table, let go, and write how far it went.
  **Say.** "What caught, and what moved?" Expect: the hooks caught the fuzz, and the seed moved.
- **10 min. Sheet.** Draw the model and fill "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X61a]], [[s06-X61b]] and [[s06-X61c]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two walks a group. Never cut the ruler reading or the exit.

### Words and supports

Stuck, fell, limit, centimeter, ruler zero, stands for. Frame: "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___." Pointing is accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who thinks a prettier model carries better (error B). Day 62 returns error B, then tests a smooth seed beside a hooked one and carries paper dots between card flowers.
