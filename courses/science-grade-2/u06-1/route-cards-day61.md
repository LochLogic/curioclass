# Route cards (day 61 and day 65, group A)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two cards for the error A repair: a seed path and a powder path. The teacher says they are invented for practice.

**Card 1, the seed path (brown arrow):** a burr plant at the left, its prickly seed heads showing. A dog walks past with a burr caught in the fur of its leg. A brown arrow runs from the plant to the dog's leg and on to a patch of bare ground at the right where one burr lies on the ground. Labels: "plant", "animal", "new place".
**Card 2, the powder path (yellow arrow):** a flower with yellow powder at its middle at the left, a hummingbird hanging in front of it with yellow marks on its forehead, and a second flower at the right with a few yellow marks on its middle. A yellow arrow runs from the first flower to the hummingbird's forehead to the second flower. Labels: "flower", "animal", "next flower".
**Say:** "Touch the seed path. Touch the powder path. Say which is which."
**Key (teacher):** the brown arrow is the seed path and starts a new plant; the yellow arrow is the powder path and rides the animal and may be left on the next flower. Pollen is not a seed.
