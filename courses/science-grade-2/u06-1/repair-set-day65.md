# Repair set (day 65)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One set for each letter group, 2.5 minutes with the teacher in turn while the others work with a partner from these picture directions. The cases here are new and are not the exits, the scored case or the bat card. One child follows the same steps one-to-one with an adult.

**Group A (pollen is not a seed):** the day 61 route cards. Touch the brown seed path and the yellow powder path, write "seed" and "powder" on the matching labels, then revise the labels on your own model.
**Group B (a decoration is not a model):** a cardboard owl with big painted eyes standing on a table beside a small dish, and a loose card lying at its feet. Test whether it moves the card from the table to the dish, then repair it so it does.
**Group C (animals visit for food):** a bumble bee at a star-shaped flower with an orange nectar spot in the middle and yellow powder around it. Trace the bee's visit, put a finger on the nectar, and touch where the powder stuck on the way.
**Group D (not every seed sticks):** on the same cloth, three hooked card seeds and three smooth card seeds, each touched once. Touch which kind stuck, and say what the claim "every seed sticks" leaves out.
**Key (teacher):** A: the brown path is the seed, the yellow path is the powder. B: the test moves the card; the model needs a part that holds and lets go. C: the bee goes for the nectar, which is food; powder sticks on the way. D: the hooked seeds stuck and the smooth seeds fell; only hooked seeds stick, so not every seed sticks.
