# Ruler strip (day 61)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One paper strip for the teacher to hold up, and a copy for each pair. A thick line at the left end of the strip is labelled "start". Beside it a ruler is drawn in whole centimeters from 0 to 12. The strip's right end reaches the 9 mark. The start line is at 0.

**Say:** "Show where zero starts on the strip. Read where the end of the strip stops, to the whole centimeter."
**Key (teacher):** zero is at the start line; the end is at 9 centimeters. A miss gets the strip again, with its left end laid at the 1 mark and then moved back to zero, so alignment is shown at the ruler.
