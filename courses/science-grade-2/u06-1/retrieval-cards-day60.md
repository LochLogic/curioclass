# Retrieval cards (day 60)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two cards, one ask each, marked in turn, about two minutes each. A miss gets a modeled second picture in the last minute.

**Card 1:** three coverings in a row: a fuzzy wool cloth drawn with tiny loops, a smooth sheet of shiny plastic, and a smooth stone slab. Ask: "Mark the covering that could catch a hook."
**Key (teacher):** the fuzzy cloth with loops. A miss is modeled with the grade 1 picture of a part of nature copied for a job, and the teacher says: here a model copies what the animal does, not its parts.
**Card 2:** five picture cards, each with one picture and one word: problem (a child pointing at a problem), sketch (a pencil and paper), build (hands joining pieces), test (a model being tried), compare (two results side by side). They are laid face up out of order.
Ask: "Show the pictures in order, marking one at a time: problem, sketch, build, test, compare."
**Key (teacher):** problem, sketch, build, test, compare. A miss is modeled against the unit 2 design-cycle chart.
