# Day 62: Does every seed stick? How does pollen travel?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, trial each group's seeds on its sock and its flowers, felt body and dots. Swap a failing set. Pin the day 60 pictures. Choose today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on. Pieces below faces, away from mouths. Test on the tray. Dots stay paper: no blowing. Clean-up, 4 minutes: wipe and bag eye protection (1), count seeds and dots back to trays (2), wipe tables (1). Hands are washed at the next scheduled washing, said aloud.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Say the cards are invented. Error B from the day 61 exit: "Touch the model that moved the seed." Expect: the plain sock. We test a model, not its looks. If many made error B on day 61's exit, whole class; a few, a small group.

#### 6 min. Smooth beside hooked

Side 1: each child circles a guess for each seed (1 minute). Each child touches one hooked and one smooth seed to the sock, 45 seconds (5 x 45 s = 3.75 minutes), and the group tallies stuck of 5 (1 minute). Expect: hooked 4 or 5; smooth 0. If a trial fails, read the trial cards, said to be invented.

#### 4 min. Revise

Each child adds "not every seed sticks" and a limit to their sketch. The chart gets hooks and fuzz fit the job.

**Ask.** What does your model leave out? Expect: growing; smooth seeds; a real animal. If many guessed every seed sticks (error D), rerun one seed of each at the front instead.

#### 9 min. Pollen, tried

Give each group two card flowers, a felt body and dots on the first flower; show nothing first. **Ask.** How could a bee's body move dots to the other flower? Each child tries one carry of about a minute (5 x 60 s = 5 minutes) while others watch, then counts the dots left on the second flower and writes the whole number on side 2 (1). Three groups tell what they found (3), unscored. If the dots will not move, swap the set, else use the drawn sequence.

#### 6 min. Pollen, named

Name it: the dots are pollen, carried by the body from a source flower to a receiving patch. Each child draws their own carry's arrow, source to body to receiver, on side 2 (2). Lay a brown seed arrow beside a yellow powder arrow: pollen is not a seed (1). Pip says, "The bee goes to help the flower" (error C). The class checks the day 60 pictures. Expect: the bee goes for food; powder sticks on the way (2).

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the cards are invented. Read [[s06-X62a]]; all touch (0.5 minute). Read [[s06-X62b]]; six rotating children answer, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 4 min. Clean up

Dots are counted back into trays.

### Other rooms

No seeds or flowers: use the trial cards and draw two flowers.

### At home

**Swap.** No hook-and-loop square: look at the trial pictures instead. No felt: use a cotton ball.

**Skip.** The group tally and the repair.

- **10 min. Test.** Press a hook-and-loop square and a paper square on a fuzzy sock.
  **Say.** "Which one stuck?" Expect: only the one with hooks.
- **15 min. Pollen.** Use two paper flowers, a piece of felt and five yellow paper dots. Carry dots from one flower to the other and count what is left.
  **Say.** "Where did the dots start, ride and stay?" Expect: the first flower, the felt, the second flower; pollen is not a seed.
- **5 min. Draw.** Draw the arrow from flower to felt to flower.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X62a]] and [[s06-X62b]]. Your child answers alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one seed each, one carry a group. Never cut the exit. Longer: test a bigger smooth seed.

### Words and supports

Stuck, fell, source, body, receiver, pollen. Frame: "The ___ seed stuck, and the ___ seed fell." Pointing is accepted.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who thinks every seed sticks (error D); day 65 repairs it. Days 63 and 64 are observed work: each child builds a model for a new animal.
