# Exit cards, days 60, 61, 62 and 65

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Each exit has picture cards every child touches and a spoken stem read once. The teacher hears a rotating six, 15 to 20 seconds each. Days 63 and 64 have no exit.

## Day 60 exit: a fly and yellow powder

**Picture:** a fly standing on a small flower with yellow powder on its legs, beside a second flower of the same kind, and a card with a brown seed beside the picture. Two cards, one with the yellow powder and one with a brown seed.
**Say (a):** "Touch what the fly carried: the yellow powder or a seed."
**Key (teacher):** the yellow powder. Error A is touching the seed.
**Say (b):** "Draw an arrow for where the powder can go next."
**Key (teacher):** an arrow from the fly to the second flower. A missing or half arrow gets descriptive feedback and no letter.

## Day 61 exit: a mitten model and a ruler

**Picture:** a smooth plastic mitten model with a card seed that keeps sliding off. Two cards: swap in fuzzy cloth where the seed touches, and paint the mitten red. Also a paper bird on a string, wings up. A ruler drawn in whole centimeters from 0 at a taped line, with a card seed's edge stopping at the 17 mark.
**Say (a):** "Touch what to change."
**Key (teacher):** swap in fuzzy cloth where the seed touches. Error B is touching paint it red.
**Say (b):** "Say or draw one thing this paper bird model cannot show."
**Key (teacher):** anything it leaves out: how a real bird flies, its weight, what it eats. An omission gets descriptive feedback.
**Say (c):** "Read where the edge stops, to the whole centimeter."
**Key (teacher):** 17 centimeters. A misread ruler gets descriptive feedback and no letter.

## Day 62 exit: three records

**Picture:** three record cards. Card 1: a hooked seed falls from bare card and stays on fuzzy cloth. Card 2: a smooth seed falls from the bare card. Card 3: the same smooth seed falls from the fuzzy cloth. Two sentence cards: every seed sticks to fuzzy cloth, and only the hooked seed stuck.
**Say (a):** "Touch the sentence the records support."
**Key (teacher):** only the hooked seed stuck. Error D is touching every seed sticks.
**Say (b):** "Say or draw one thing these records cannot tell us."
**Key (teacher):** whether the seed would grow. An omission gets descriptive feedback.

## Day 65 exit: a model moth

**Picture:** a model moth made of card and felt that carries yellow dots away from a first card flower. At a second card flower the receiving patch lies off to one side and never touches the dots on the moth. Three part cards: the receiving patch, the moth's wings, and a brown seed to swap for the dots. Then two cards: food, and to help the plant.
**Say (a):** "Touch the part to move so the dots can be left there."
**Key (teacher):** the receiving patch. Error B is the wings. Error A is swapping the dots for seeds.
**Say (b):** "Why did the moth go to the flower?"
**Key (teacher):** food. Error C is to help the plant.
