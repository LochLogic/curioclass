# Safety sheet (unit 6)

Read each line aloud with the picture. Children point to the picture for each line.

1. Eye protection on before we touch model pieces. (Picture: a child with eye protection, a bag with a name on it.)
2. Pieces stay large and below our faces, never in our mouths. (Picture: a child holding a card seed low over a tray.)
3. We walk the sock model slowly on the cleared mat. (Picture: a child walking beside a taped line.)
4. Only the grown-up handles burrs and seed heads. (Picture: a grown-up holding a sealed bag.)
5. Paper dots stay paper. We never blow on them. (Picture: a dot on a tray, a mouth with a line through it.)
6. We never touch live bees or other animals. (Picture: a bee on a flower, a hand held back.)
7. The grown-up covers the ends of the pipe cleaners. (Picture: a pipe cleaner with a covered end.)
8. Hands are washed at the next washing time. (Picture: hands under a tap.)
