# Trial cards (days 61 and 62)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Used only when a trial cannot run, because a seed will not cling or the dots will not transfer. Say aloud that the readings are invented, and that no reading is scored.

**Day 61, five walks of a sock model:** five pictures, each of a fuzzy sock on a card-tube leg walked along a mat beside a ruler from 0. A hooked card seed rides on the sock and stops. The edge of the seed stops at 8 centimeters, 14 centimeters, 26 centimeters, 11 centimeters and 19 centimeters.
**Say:** "Read where the seed stops, to the whole centimeter. Which walk carried the seed farthest?"
**Key (teacher):** 8, 14, 26, 11 and 19 centimeters; the third walk went farthest.
**Day 62, a smooth seed beside a hooked seed on the same sock:** two tally cards. Hooked seeds: five touched to the sock, four stuck and one fell (it touched too little cloth). Smooth seeds: five touched to the sock, none stuck.
**Say:** "Read how many stuck out of five for each kind."
**Key (teacher):** hooked 4 of 5, smooth 0 of 5. Not every seed sticks; the smooth seed fell every time. The cards say nothing about growing.
