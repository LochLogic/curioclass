# Day 64: Improve the model and say what it cannot show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Clip each child's feedback slip to their record and set each set, model and record on a tray. Cover the carry-arrow chart. Tables 4 to 6 are watched today; tables 1 to 3 were watched on day 63 and are not watched again. Print the watched and heard columns.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on. Pieces below faces, away from mouths. Tie hair back. Stop for any bare wire and use paper. No live animals. Clean-up is inside the last 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 20 min. Feedback, revise, try, record

Return day 63's assessment records. Children work alone; do not teach. Each child reads their note and picture with a partner, and an adult reads it aloud to any child whose record says so (3 minutes). Revise the drawing and the build (9). Try the model again and write what changed and why, and one limit: [[s06-E9]] and [[s06-E10]] (6). Name each part: "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___." Tell how the halves hold the acorn and let it go: [[s06-E8]] (2). A child may draw, point or dictate to a scribe. Collect the records at the end of the part.

#### 12 min. Observation windows

Tables 4 to 6 (15 children), one window of 4 minutes a table. Each child about 45 seconds (5 x 45 s = 3.75 minutes). The six asks of day 63, one at a time, about 7 seconds each: the beak, the oak, what it carries, where the route starts, how it carries, where it leaves the acorn. Mark the look-fors on the watched column, and write error C (the animal helps the plant) or error D (hooks on every seed) if it shows; windows corroborate and are not scored. The other 15 finish their labelled accounts alone.

#### 3 min. Moves and clean-up

Two moves of 30 seconds, then eye protection wiped, tables wiped. The adult bags each child's model with its drawing and record, to be scored together.

### Marking

Before class on day 65, 60 minutes: score each retained version on the ten components, from the model, drawing and record, using shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Only shown counts. Write one next step of 12 words or fewer, with a picture, on each slip.

### Other rooms

No set: draw each part on card.

### At home

**Swap.** Any card for beak halves, a small card for the acorn.

**Skip.** The windows and the partner read.

- **30 min. Assessment.** Read your note aloud. Your child improves the jay model, tries it, and writes or says what changed, why, and what the model cannot show. Give no hints. [[s06-E9]] and [[s06-E10]].
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: a change because of what the try showed, or a reason to keep a part.
- **10 min. Say the parts.** [[s06-E8]].
  **Say.** "How do the halves hold the acorn and let it go?" Expect: closing holds it; opening lets it go.
- **5 min. Look back.** Write one next step of 12 words or fewer.

### Shorter or longer

Cut the partner read, never the try or the asks. Longer: none today.

### Words and supports

Change, try, limit, hold, let go, stands for. Frame: "This stands for ___. It moves ___ because ___." Pointing and drawing are accepted.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 returns each model and record with one written next step, then repairs errors A, B, C and D by letter group, and closes both anchor questions.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
