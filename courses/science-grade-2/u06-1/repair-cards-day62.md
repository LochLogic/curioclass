# Repair cards (day 62, and day 65, group B)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Two cards for the error B repair: a drawn-on animal that cannot hold a seed beside a plain sock that does.

**Card 1:** a flat card cut-out of a smiling fox with a bow tie and bright painted stripes, standing on a mat beside a start mark and a stop mark. A card seed lies at the start mark and has not moved. Label: "painted fox".
**Card 2:** a plain fuzzy sock on a card-tube leg on the same mat, with a hooked card seed stuck to its fuzzy side. The seed's edge is at the stop mark, 10 centimeters from the start. Label: "plain sock".
**Say:** "Touch the model that moved the seed."
**Key (teacher):** the plain sock. Error B is touching the painted fox: a model must move something from one place to another, so it is tested, not judged by how it looks.
