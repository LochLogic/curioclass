# Retrieval card (day 63)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One card for each child, read neutrally with no hints. The arrow is scored (pollen was first tried on day 62); the seed and pollen marks are unscored. A picture of the class flower model on a tray: a large card flower at the left where the dots start, with yellow dots at its middle and a printed start dot, a felt body in the middle with a few dots on it, and a second large card flower at the right with a patch and two dots on it. Space above the picture for one arrow. At the bottom, two loose pieces to mark: one brown seed piece, a large seed shape, and one yellow dot piece, a small round dot, each drawn with a black outline and a box.
**Say:** "Draw one arrow to show where the pollen starts, what carries it and where it is left. Then mark the piece that is a seed and the piece that is pollen."
