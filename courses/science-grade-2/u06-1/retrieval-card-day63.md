# Retrieval card (day 63)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One card for each child, read neutrally with no hints, unscored. A picture of the class flower model on a tray: a large card flower at the left with yellow dots at its middle, a felt body in the middle with a few dots on it, and a second large card flower at the right with a patch and two dots on it. Under the picture, three empty label boxes with the words "source", "body" and "receiver" on loose word cards. At the bottom, two loose pieces to mark: one brown seed piece and one yellow dot piece, each with a box.
**Say:** "Place each word card on the picture. Then mark the piece that is a seed and the piece that is pollen."
**Key (teacher):** source on the first flower, body on the felt, receiver on the second flower's patch; the brown piece is the seed and the yellow dot is pollen. Items s06-E11 and s06-E12.
