# Picture sort (day 36)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

For the groups that wait, six small cards of familiar samples: a metal spoon, a rubber band, a cotton sock, a wooden block, a clear plastic cup, a paper napkin. Children sort them into two groups by any one property they choose, and say what it is. Unscored; nothing from this task goes on the checklist.

**Key (teacher):** Any sort with one rule that fits every card. A sort by a rule that fails for a card is noted for the next day, not marked.
