# Day 36: Test it and tell what you found

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the answer chart from the three samples. Turn the wall charts to the wall. Post the order of the six groups. Set each group's tray; keep sealed ice cups on the cold pack with spares. Seat children so each sees only their own paper. Every child is marked today.

### Safety and set-up

Impact eye protection on at the station. The stick presses below faces; the flashlight never shines at a person; sealed cups stay sealed and are never tasted. Samples are new, edges rounded, no glass or sharp metal. Wipe condensation and spills at once.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say the picture cards are invented for practice. Read one step at a time, neutrally: each group comes to the station for 4 minutes; each child tells one test and what stays the same ([[s03-E1]]), then does it gently ([[s03-E2]]); then the group finishes the paper record ([[s03-E3]], [[s03-E4]], [[s03-E5]], [[s03-E6]]) at its table. Waiting groups sort the picture cards, unscored. Give no help on the test.

#### 24 min. Observation windows

Six windows of 4 minutes, one group at a time. Five children in 4 minutes: each gets about 45 seconds, 15 for the spoken test and 30 for one gentle action (5 x 45 s = 3 minutes 45 seconds); in the last 15 seconds peers say what they saw. Each child takes a different thing from the tray: the stick, a sample to bend, the flashlight, the ice cup, the lukewarm cup. Read [[s03-E1]] once, then [[s03-E2]] once. Mark live on the checklist, two points for each child: proposes a relevant test with a steady condition; does it safely. Give no feedback. 

After its window a group takes its tray to its table. Each child alone fills the record: [[s03-E3]] for the sample they tested, [[s03-E4]], [[s03-E5]], [[s03-E6]]. A child may pass. Waiting groups sort the picture cards alone; nothing from them goes on the checklist.

#### 3 min. Swaps and clean-up

Between windows, 30 seconds to swap trays, wipe condensation and swap a melted ice cup for a spare. At the end, one minute: damp wipes at tables, sticks and samples counted back.

#### 5 min. The hat again

Hand back each child's day 35 hat drawing. Each child alone draws or says on the back one thing they would keep or change, and what shows it. Unscored; no feedback. The last group finishes its record. Collect every record and drawing at the end; read the records against the answer chart after class.

### Other rooms

No equipment: use the test photographs and the observation sheets. No child action can be credited from paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ice: a sealed jar from the freezer, no tasting.

**Skip.** The posted order and the checklist.

- **10 min. The assessment, first part.** Pick three clean things: a wooden spoon, a plastic lid, a cloth. Your child does [[s03-E1]], then [[s03-E2]], gently, alone.
- **10 min. Record.** Your child fills [[s03-E3]].
- **10 min. Sort.** Your child does [[s03-E4]] and [[s03-E6]].
- **10 min. Cups.** A sealed cup of ice and a sealed cup of cool water: [[s03-E5]]. No tasting.
- **5 min. Look.** Read the answers with them; give no hints first.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; the check is not shortened. Longer: none; nothing is added.

### Words and supports

Test, steady, dent, rough, smooth, bends, stiff, solid, liquid. Read all prompts aloud; pointing, saying, drawing or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 is not scored in class. Records, errors B, C and D and the unit item go to day 38 in letter groups. Day 37 is the second check: new packet, choose materials for jobs.
