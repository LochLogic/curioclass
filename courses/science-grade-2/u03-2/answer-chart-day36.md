# Day 36 answer chart

Make this before class from the three samples. If a sample differs from the line below, change the line, not the child.

| Sample | Color | Feel | Press | Bend | Light |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| sanded pine | pale | smooth | no dent | stays stiff | blocks the light |
| clear flexible plastic | clear | smooth | no dent | bends | lets light through |
| new cotton | white | rough | dents when pressed | bends | lets a little light through |

**Ice and bath:** the ice cubes are solid; they keep their shape. The bath water is liquid; it flows to fit the tub.
**Safe action:** the stick presses below face height; the light never points at a person; nothing is tasted.
