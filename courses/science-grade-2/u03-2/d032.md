# Day 32: Is the hat winner best for everything?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the day 30 class record chart where all can see it. Set the tray at the front with the towel strip, the plastic piece, a small cup of water and the cloth. Copy the choice model on the board with the hat row filled. Hand back the day 31 folders. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

The teacher alone pours, with a helper, both in goggles; children stay seated. Pour a little, over the tray. Wipe spills at once.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 31's exit, error H takes this part. Draw two covers on the board, said to be invented: cover A took water in and its tab is wet; cover B took none in and its tab is dry. Children point to the cover that keeps the doll dry. **Ask.** Which cover took water in? Expect: A; but B keeps the doll dry. A child whose day 31 graph has a note redraws it at the table meanwhile.

#### 6 min. A second job

Pour a little water onto the tray. Lay the paper towel strip in it, and the plastic piece beside it. Pip says: "Our hat winner is best for everything." Draw the two jobs side by side: a hat, a pad. **Ask.** Does the hat keep water out or take it in? Expect: keep it out. **Ask.** What must a pad do with the spill? Expect: take it in. Pip is not sure yet (error E).

#### 10 min. Records to choices

Pairs copy the day 30 climbs and tab marks onto the records sheet (4 minutes), using their day 31 graph. They circle the best piece for the pad and the best for the hat and complete the two lines (6 minutes). Visit pairs. Expect: the pad is the piece the water climbed farthest; the hat has a dry tab. If the class numbers failed, use the invented records, said aloud.

#### 6 min. Second-purpose argument

Three pairs say one line each, one minute with the check (3 minutes): "For the ___ I choose ___ because ___." After each, the class points to the number or tab that backs it. **Ask.** Does the record support it? Expect: yes or no, with a bar or a tab. The other pairs whisper their own line to a partner (3 minutes).

#### 5 min. Choice model

Add the second row on the board: job, a pad; the property we tested, water climb; what the test showed; our choice. Pairs copy it beside the hat row.

#### 3 min. Say it

Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes): "For the ___ I choose ___ because ___." Judge with three look-fors: names the job; uses a result, not liking; the two jobs differ.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 32 exit picture, read [[s03-X32]] once; everyone touches the mat strip and says why (1 minute). Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Board

Return to the question board: Would a different job change our choice? First answer: yes, the pad and the hat.

### Other rooms

No tray: draw the two jobs and read the climbs from the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No records: use the numbers 8, 5, 3, 0, 0 and 0, said to be invented.

**Skip.** The pairs and the question board.

- **10 min. Two jobs.** Lay a paper towel and a plastic bag over a puddle of spilled water, adult wiping up.
  **Say.** "Which soaks it up? Which keeps the table dry?" Expect: the towel soaks it up; the plastic keeps it dry.
- **8 min. Choose.** Your child fills the choice model for a pad and a hat.
  **Say.** "Is one material best for both?" Expect: no; the job decides.
- **7 min. Student page.** Your child finishes the frames.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X32]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pad only, then the hat. Never cut the two jobs or the exit. Longer: name a third job and choose.

### Words and supports

Job, pad, hat, climb, dry, wet, choose. Frame: "For the ___ I choose ___ because ___." A scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the hat winner is best for the pad too (error E). Day 33 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then the class tests how far equally loaded strips bend.
