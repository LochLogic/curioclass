# Day 35: Why did the first hat fail?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Find each child's dated day 27 drawing and set the paper doll beside the board. Copy the materials chart on the board: rain hat, pad, shelf, cover, grip, with the columns job, the property we tested, the evidence. Choose Today's six. Key: key-evidence-cards-day35.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Nothing is poured, pressed or loaded.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 34's exit, error A takes this part. Show the repair card, said to be invented: a rough knitted square with a deep dent, and a smooth stone slab with none. **Ask.** Which one is hard, and what shows it? Expect: the slab; the press left no dent. Rough is how it feels. A child who touched the wrong lining on the grip card touches it again where the block stayed.

#### 5 min. Look back

Hand back each child's day 27 drawing. Show the doll and the paper hat. **Ask.** What did we learn that the first drawing did not know? Expect: tests show which material keeps the doll dry, and the job decides. Pairs tell a partner (3 minutes); three children tell the class.

#### 10 min. Revise

Read the five evidence cards aloud once. Each child alone revises the drawing with a material chosen from the cards, and labels the job, the tested property and the result. The written task: "I changed the ___ to ___ because the test showed ___." (scribed if wanted). Judge with three look-fors: names the job; names the property that was tested; uses a result, not liking. Visit tables.

#### 7 min. The pattern chart

Pairs copy the materials chart as the class fills one row for each job from the cards. Then the class adds what different materials share: the paper towel and the wool both took water in; the rubber and the fabric both gave a result that fits one job. Add each to the pattern chart.

#### 6 min. From nature

Draw a tree, a plank and a block on the board. People test a material before they choose it, and many materials come from nature, some changed by people. **Ask.** Which is the natural material, and which is made from it? Expect: the tree; the plank and block. Then show the natural materials picture: a sheep, wool yarn, a mitten. Children name one pair.

#### 3 min. Say it

Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes): "I changed the ___ to ___ because the test showed ___."

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 35 exit picture, read [[s03-X35]] once; everyone points to the sheet Pip should use and whispers what the test shows to a partner (1 minute). Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 1 min. Board

Answer the question: what explains the failed hat? The paper took the water through. Revised drawings go in the folder, kept for day 36.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the five evidence cards and the chart on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read the five results aloud from the page.

**Skip.** The pairs and the pattern chart.

- **10 min. Look back.** Find or draw a hat for a doll or a toy.
  **Say.** "What would you change, and what test shows it?" Expect: a material that kept a tab dry.
- **8 min. Revise.** Your child draws the new hat and writes the frame.
- **7 min. Nature.** Name something made from a tree or from wool.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X35]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise only the hat. Never cut the evidence or the exit. Longer: choose a second job and revise it.

### Words and supports

Job, test, evidence, result, natural, hat. Frame: "I changed the ___ to ___ because the test showed ___." A scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says same colour means same result (error B); it is not repaired on day 36, but on day 38. Day 36 is the observation window: each child plans and does one gentle test of three new samples, and sorts what they find.
