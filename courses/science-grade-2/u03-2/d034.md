# Day 34: What makes a good cover and a good grip?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, an adult checks the four samples for edges and sets one four-sample set and one blunt stick for each group. Put the choice model on the board with the hat, pad and deck rows. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Impact eye protection on for all. Press gently with the blunt stick below faces; bend gently by hand, never stretch the rubber. No glass or sharp metal. Count sticks and samples back; wipe hands.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 33's exit, error G takes this part. Show the repair card, said to be invented: two strips across supports, one dropped 1 centimeter, one 5, and the words "stays level". **Ask.** Which strip stays level? Expect: the one that dropped 1; less drop, more level.

#### 8 min. Guided press and texture

Read one step at a time, two minutes a sample: press once, feel, bend gently, mark. Five roles turn each sample: presser, feeler, bender, marker, caller (20 seconds each for the first three). The group fills one sorting mat for wood, rubber, fabric and plastic. Rough and hard are separate columns (error A): dent shows hard or soft, touch shows rough or smooth.

#### 8 min. A cover

The job: a cover for a corner that must not dent when pressed. Pairs circle the sample from the mat and say which result decides. **Ask.** Which column decides? Expect: dent, not rough. Three pairs say it to the class.

#### 9 min. A handle grip

Show the grip pictures, said to be invented: one block on one slope on four linings, two hold, two slip. No hands are tested; grip is read from the pictures only, and feeling a sample does not decide it. **Ask.** Which linings held the block still? Expect: the two where the block stayed at the top. Pairs choose a material for a handle grip and point to the picture. Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 3 min. Framed page

"The ___ is ___, so it is good for ___." The teacher scribes three sentences, a cover and a grip from three pairs, about 1 minute each; the other pairs copy one onto their framed page. The class adds the cover and grip rows to the choice model.

#### 3 min. Clear up

Sticks and samples counted back; hands wiped, one group at a time.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 34 exit pictures, read [[s03-X34]] once, then [[s03-X34b]] once; everyone touches one each time (1 minute). Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each, both asks (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). A grip slip gets a note, no letter. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 1 min. Board

Name the five tests behind the choice model rows: tab dry, water climb, drop, dent, slope grip.

### Other rooms

No samples: use the grip pictures and a drawn press result for each sample.

### At home

**Swap.** No pressing stick: the blunt end of a spoon, gentle, below the face.

**Skip.** The group mat and the framed page.

- **12 min. Press.** Gently press a wooden spoon, a cloth, a plastic lid and a rubber band. **Say.** "Which dented? Which feels rough?" Expect: they are different questions.
- **8 min. Choose.** **Say.** "Which would make a cover that must not dent?" Expect: one that did not dent.
- **5 min. Grip.** Look at the grip pictures. **Say.** "Which lining holds the block still?"
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X34]] and [[s03-X34b]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two samples, then the cover. Never cut the press, the separate columns or the exit. Longer: choose a third job.

### Words and supports

Press, dent, rough, smooth, hard, soft, cover, grip, slip. Frame: "The ___ is ___, so it is good for ___." A scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says rough means hard (error A). Day 35 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then the class returns to the failed hat and revises its first choice.
