# Natural materials picture (day 35)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

**Picture:** Three cards in a row. Card 1: a sheep with a thick coat. Card 2: a ball of wool yarn. Card 3: a knitted mitten.

**Say:** "The mitten starts with the sheep. People test the yarn before they choose it. What would you test the yarn for if you wanted a mitten to keep hands warm and dry? Say it."
**Key (teacher):** Any test of a property the job needs, such as the press, the bend, or how water moves in it. This is a talking point, not marked. The idea is that people choose a material using tests.
