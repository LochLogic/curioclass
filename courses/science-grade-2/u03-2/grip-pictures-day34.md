# Grip pictures (day 34)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

One block on one sloping board, drawn from the side on four cards. No hands are tested. Each card has a lining sheet under the block.

- **Card 1, wood lining:** the block has slid to the bottom of the slope.
- **Card 2, rubber lining:** the block stayed at the top.
- **Card 3, fabric lining:** the block stayed at the top.
- **Card 4, plastic lining:** the block has slid to the bottom.

**Say:** "Same block, same slope. Point to the linings where the block held still."
**Key (teacher):** Rubber and fabric. Wood and plastic let the block slip. A lining does not have to feel rough to hold; the slope test shows it.
