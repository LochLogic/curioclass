# Day 33: Which strip stays most level?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, an adult cuts equal strips and sets each group's supports over a catch tray, same span. Groups 1 and 4 take card, 2 and 5 plastic, 3 and 6 fabric. Draw the class chart. Choose Today's six.

### Safety and set-up

Impact eye protection on for all. Low supports sit over the tray; the adult places every block, each under 250 grams; children sit, feet back. Strips are new; no glass or sharp metal. Wipe hands after.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 32's exit, error E takes this part. Show the repair card, said to be invented: a shoe lace holder and a lunch box lid, soft cotton cord and hard plastic. Children pair each job with a material. **Ask.** Can one material be best for both jobs? Expect: no; the job decides which result matters.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Show the day 17 card wall and stone row pictures, said to be invented. **Ask.** What did the card wall do well, and what did it do poorly? Expect: it lost less water than the bare tray, but water ran round its ends. A miss: say one aloud, pointing, and the child says the other.

#### 4 min. Plan

The job: a deck for a toy bridge that must stay level under a block. Fair test: same span, same strip size, same block. Change only the material. Watch how far the middle drops. We compare how far it bends, not when it would break. Less drop means more level.

#### 14 min. Test

Each group has one material. Roles turn between the two trials: measurer, recorder, caller, tray keeper, level checker (is the strip on both supports?). In 2 minutes the measurer holds the ruler beside the middle, zero at the top of the strip. The adult then places each group's block in turn, 30 seconds a group, 3 minutes a round. The measurer reads the drop to the whole centimeter; the recorder writes it. Roles turn (1 minute), then trial 2 (4 minutes). The group's number is the second trial. If a strip slips or none drops a whole centimeter, use the invented bend records, said aloud.

#### 5 min. Compare

Both groups' numbers for each material go on the class chart. Within 1 centimeter is about the same; if two groups differ by more, ask where the block sat. Pairs draw three bars, one square a centimeter, bottom line ruler zero, and add the deck row to the choice model. **Ask.** Which strip would you use for the bridge deck, and what shows it? Hear Today's six, 30 seconds each (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

#### 4 min. Clear blocks

Blocks into the tray, supports stacked, strips returned, hands wiped.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the pictures are invented. Show the day 33 exit picture, read [[s03-X33]] once; everyone touches a strip (1 minute). Hear Today's six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Board

Children name a job that wants a strip to bend; offer a strap if none comes. The question stays open for day 35.

### Other rooms

No blocks or supports: use the invented bend records and the pictures; no child action can be credited from paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No ruler: a strip of paper marked in centimeters. Use two stacks of books as supports.

**Skip.** The six groups and the class chart.

- **12 min. Test.** Lay a card strip across two stacks of books over a tray. The adult sets a block on the middle; the child reads the drop with a ruler. Then a cloth strip and a plastic strip.
  **Say.** "How far did the middle drop?" Expect: a whole number of centimeters.
- **8 min. Bars.** Draw the three bars, one square for each centimeter.
- **5 min. Choose.** **Say.** "Which strip would you use for the bridge deck?" Expect: the one that dropped least.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask [[s03-X33]] aloud, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two materials, one trial. Never cut the block, the reading or the exit.

### Words and supports

Drop, level, bends, stiff, strip, block, support. Frame: "The ___ strip dropped ___ centimeters, so it stays ___." A scribe is fine.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says the strip that bent most is stiffest (error G). Day 34 opens with 5 minutes of return and repair for it, then the class presses and feels four samples and chooses a cover and a handle grip.
