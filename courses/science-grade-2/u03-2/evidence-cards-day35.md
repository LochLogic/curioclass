# Evidence cards (day 35)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one.

Five printed cards, one test result each, for the child to read with the teacher's help. Read each card aloud once.

- **Hat:** Plastic: water climbed 0. Tab dry. Paper towel: water climbed 8. Tab wet.
- **Pad:** Paper towel: water climbed 8. Wool: water climbed 3.
- **Shelf:** Card strip dropped 2. Fabric strip dropped 9.
- **Cover:** Wood pressed with the blunt stick: no dent. Rubber pressed with the blunt stick: dent.
- **Grip:** Rubber lining: block held still. Wood lining: block slipped.

**Key (teacher):** For the rain hat, plastic. For the pad, paper towel. For the shelf, card. For the cover, wood. For the grip, rubber. Each choice rests on a result; the colour or the feel is not the evidence.
