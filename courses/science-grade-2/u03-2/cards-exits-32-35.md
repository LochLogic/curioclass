# Exit cards, days 32 to 35

Each exit has a picture card every child marks, and a spoken stem read once. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. The teacher hears a rotating six. Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures.

## Day 32 exit card: a mat for a plant pot

**Picture:** Two cups with a strip standing in each, drawn from the side, each with a cup line. Strip T: a wet mark climbing to the 8 line. Strip V: no wet mark above the cup line, and beside it a doll under a hat with a dry tab. A small ruler beside each, zero at the cup line. A third picture: a plant pot dripping onto a flat mat.
**Say:** "A mat under a dripping plant pot must soak up drips. Why does the job change which strip we use for the mat and which for a hat?"
**Key (teacher):** Strip T for the mat, because the water climbed 8 and the mat needs to take water in; strip V for a hat, because the hat job needs the doll kept dry, so the job changes the choice. Strip V for the mat as well, because it was the best hat, is error E.

## Day 33 exit card: a shelf that stays level

**Picture:** Two strips lying across two low block supports, each with one block on the middle, drawn from the side with a ruler beside it. Strip R dropped 3 centimeters at the middle. Strip S dropped 8 centimeters.
**Say:** "Pip needs a shelf that stays level. Touch the strip to use."
**Key (teacher):** Strip R, because it dropped less. Strip S, because it bent the most so it is the stiffest, is error G.

## Day 34 exit card: a cover that must not dent

**Picture:** Two close pictures. A woven doormat piece with a deep dent in it where a blunt stick pressed. A smooth tile with a blunt stick pressing it and no dent.
**Say:** "A cover must not dent when pressed. Which one would you use, given what the press test showed?"
**Key (teacher):** The tile, with the test result that the press left no dent. The mat, because it is rough so it is hard, is error A.

## Day 34 exit card, second: a lining for a handle grip

**Picture:** Two close pictures of one block on one sloping board, drawn from the side. Lining M: the block stayed at the top. Lining N: the block slid to the bottom. No hands in the picture.
**Say:** "Look at two new lining cards. The same block sat on the same slope on each. A tool handle needs a grip that holds. Which lining would you use, given what the slope test showed?"
**Key (teacher):** Lining M, with the test result that the block stayed at the top. Lining N, because it looks rougher, is a slip with no letter: a grip is read from the slope picture, not from the feel.

## Day 35 exit card: two green sheets

**Picture:** Two cards. Sheet 1: a green sheet over a book box, the box dry underneath. Sheet 2: a green sheet over a book box, the box wet underneath. Under them, two circles: sheet A, sheet B. Sheet A is marked as Pip's choice because it is the greener one.
**Say:** "Pip chose sheet A because it is the greener one. The box stayed wet under sheet A and dry under sheet B. What should Pip change to fit what the test shows?"
**Key (teacher):** Change to sheet B, with the words that the box stayed dry under it and wet under sheet A. Keeping sheet A because both sheets are green is error B.
