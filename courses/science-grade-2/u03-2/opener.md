# Section 3.2 opener: which material fits which job?

## What the section does

Five days, 32 to 36. The class uses the day 30 records for a second job, tests how far equally loaded strips bend, presses and feels four samples for a cover and a grip, and returns to the failed hat to revise the first choice. Day 36 is the first end-of-unit observation window. Days 32 to 35 end with an exit. Day 36 does not.

## How the days run

Days 32 to 35 open with five minutes of return and repair of the last exit, one error letter. Read every stem aloud, one ask at a time. Mark with one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Children read only short labels. On day 36, six groups of five come to you at four minutes each; the others sort pictures of familiar samples on their own. Mark each child live on the checklist, two points for each look-for, and give no feedback until day 38.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The job decides which result to look at.** A material that takes in water is right for a mat and wrong for a hat.
- **Greater drop under the same load means more bend.** Less drop stays more level.
- **Rough does not tell us hard.** Pressing shows hard or soft; touching shows rough or smooth.
- **Different tests show different properties.** Color, feel, press, bend and light each need their own test.
- **People use what tests show, and many materials come from nature,** such as wool from sheep.

## Safety and set-up

The adult places every block. Low supports sit over a tray, with hands and feet clear. Samples are new; no glass or sharp metal in student hands. Press gently with the blunt stick below faces, and never shine a light into eyes. Sealed cups stay sealed; warm water is lukewarm. Keep a cloth at hand and wipe spills at once.
