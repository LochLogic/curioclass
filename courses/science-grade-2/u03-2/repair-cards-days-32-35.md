# Repair cards, days 32 to 35 (return and repair)

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so. Children in any picture are of different ages, skin tones and hair, shown as simple figures. Say aloud that every picture is invented for practice, and print "invented for practice" beneath each one. Each repair takes five minutes at the start of the next day, for the letter most children showed. None of these cases is the case on the exit.

## Day 33 repair, letter E: two jobs, two materials

**Picture:** Two job cards: a shoe lace holder and a lunch box lid. Two material cards: soft cotton cord and hard plastic.
**Say:** "Pair each job with a material from the records. Can one material be best for both jobs? Point to yes or no."
**Key (teacher):** No. The job decides which test result matters. Cotton cord for the lace holder, plastic for the lid.

## Day 34 repair, letter G: two pictured drops

**Picture:** Two strips across supports. One drops 1 centimeter. The other drops 5 centimeters. Beside them the words "stays level".
**Say:** "Line the drops up beside the words stays level. Touch the strip that stays level."
**Key (teacher):** The strip that dropped 1.

## Day 35 repair, letter A: a rough soft sample and a smooth hard sample

**Picture:** A rough knitted square with a stick pressing in and leaving a deep dent. A smooth stone slab with a stick pressing and no dent.
**Say:** "Which one is hard? What shows it? Point."
**Key (teacher):** The stone slab, because the press left no dent. Rough is how it feels, not how hard it is.

## Day 36 note

No repair on day 36. Day 36 opens with directions.
