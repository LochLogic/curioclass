# The worn path (day 11)

A picture sequence of four pages for the day 11 hook, then three path-edge cards. These are stand-ins for teacher photographs of a real worn path beside grass (creator, date and permission recorded before any photograph is used). Show one page at a time and say: "We made these pictures. They are not our school, but a lot like it." Children read no words. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each page.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a page says so. Children in any picture are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair.

## Page 1

**Picture:** A school slope seen from the side. A bare path runs straight down it from a paved walk at the top to a low fence at the bottom. Grass stands on both sides of the path. A boy in a cap and a girl with braids stand at the top and look down.

**Read:** This is a path down a slope. The path is bare. The grass beside it is green.

## Page 2

**Picture:** A close view of the path. The soil is smooth and bare, with a few small stones. Short blades of grass stand all along both edges. Nothing grows in the middle.

**Read:** Look close. Nothing grows on the path. Grass grows right beside it.

## Page 3

**Picture:** The same slope in the rain. Grey slanting lines show rain. A thin stream of water runs down the middle of the path. The grass on both sides is wet and still stands.

**Read:** It is raining. Water runs down the path. The grass beside it stays.

## Page 4

**Picture:** The path after the rain. A groove runs down the middle, a ditch with small stones at its low end and a fan of soil at the bottom by the fence. The grass at both sides looks the same as before.

**Read:** Now the path has a ditch. The grass beside it has not changed. Why? How could we stop it?

## Path-edge cards (steady, yet changed)

Three cards, printed apart and shuffled. Each shows one spot at the top of the path edge, drawn from above, and a short caption. Children order them by the caption words, not by any date.

**Card 1:** Grass grows right up to a narrow flat strip. Caption: At the start of term. The grass touches the strip.

**Card 2:** A bare band lies beside the strip, wider than before, with a few grass stubs. Caption: Later. A bare band lies beside the strip.

**Card 3:** The bare band has a small shallow hollow in it. Caption: Latest. The bare band has a small hollow.

**Say (teacher):** "On any one day the edge looked the same as the day before. Put the cards in order. What is different between the first and the last?"
