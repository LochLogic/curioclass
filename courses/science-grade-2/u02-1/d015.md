# Day 15: Explain the path, and what a tray can show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a set of the three path cards and two captions for each group. Hang filled change chart and a clean sheet headed "Our change model". Choose today's six for Say it and a different six for exit. Keys: key-path-cards-day15, key-cards-exits-11-15.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No person is in any card.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 14 exit tickets (s02-X14). Teach error A: write "No, because nobody saw it change." Order the terrace drawings from the exit card and point to what differs.

**Ask.** What is different? Expect: the flat ledge is now a rounded slope. The difference is the change.

#### 8 min. The path again

Show the older, later and latest path cards and read the two captions: "Children walked here all term" and "After a big rain this week." Groups sort the pictures and words into fast and slow, two sources each (3 minutes). Visit each group 30 seconds (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

**Ask.** What shows a slow change? Expect: the older and later pictures differ, and the words say all term.

**Ask.** What shows a fast change? Expect: the latest picture, and the words say after a big rain this week.

Do not say how to stop it yet.

#### 9 min. What a model can show

Lay the three unit 1 pour records and the fan-tray card on the board. Say once: watching changes now helps us think about how land changed before.

**Ask.** What do the pour records and the fan tray show? Expect: which way water and wind move sand and soil.

**Ask.** What can they not show? Expect: how long a real change takes. Say: the tray moved sand in minutes; a hillside may take a night or a term.

#### 8 min. Revise the class model

On "Our change model" join the day 11 sketch, the jagged fast arrows, the stepped slow arrows and a model limit line, written by the class: "A tray shows how soil moves. It does not show how long a real change took." Each child finishes their own sketch on the student page: a fast change and a slow change, each labeled, and the frame "I know ___ because ___." Look for: one fast and one slow change named; a source for each, a picture or words; no number of years.

#### 4 min. Exit

Say the card is invented for practice. Read [[s02-X15a]] once; everyone circles a record and whispers the telling words to a partner. Read [[s02-X15b]] once; everyone circles a card. Hear a rotating six on both, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Board

Read "What changed? How quickly?" and answer it from the chart: the path edge changed slowly, the ditch quickly. Add the next question: what protects the land? Tomorrow, trays.

#### 3 min. Say it

Hear six children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "I know ___ because ___" about the path. Mark who was heard.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards large on the board and read the captions aloud. One projector: show one card at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the path three times, older, later and latest, with a caption under each. No pour records: draw a tray of sand with a fan blowing across it.

**Skip.** The groups, the class model and the board.

- **8 min. The path.** Show the three pictures and captions.
  **Say.** "Which picture shows the quick change?" Expect: the latest, "after a big rain this week".
- **8 min. The tray.** Look at the tray drawing.
  **Say.** "What can a tray show, and what can't it?" Expect: which way sand moves, not how long a real change takes.
- **9 min. Your path.** Your child finishes the student page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X15a]] and [[s02-X15b]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort only the latest card. Never cut the model limit or the exit. Longer: find a third source.

### Words and supports

Fast, slow, model, tray. Frame: "I know ___ because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a tray tells how long a real change took (error F) and who says erosion is always slow (error B). Day 16 opens with Return and repair of the day 15 exit tickets, whichever of error F or error B more children made, in 4 minutes. Then trays.
