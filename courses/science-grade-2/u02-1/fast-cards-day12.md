# Four fast changes (day 12)

Four card pairs. Each pair is two picture cards, printed apart and shuffled, and one separate caption card. Read each caption aloud. Children order the pair, point to the words that say when, and mark the change chart. Print "invented for practice" beneath pairs 2 to 4. No picture shows a hurt person; the places in all four are empty of people.

Drawing style for pairs 2 to 4: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no printed words except where a card says so.

## Pair 1: the mountain

**Pictures:** Two photographs of one mountain from the same side. In the first the mountain has a tall rounded top. In the second the top is gone and a wide notch is in its place. Use the U.S. Geological Survey pages "Mount St. Helens Before the 1980 Eruption" and "Mount St. Helens after the 1980 Eruption", EROS Center, public domain, linked in sources.csv (U4 and U5). Do not print the photograph dates for children; the dates are not the evidence.

**Caption card (second source):** The top of the mountain slid away in one day, during the eruption.

**Words that say when:** in one day.

## Pair 2: the fence line

**Pictures:** A field with a long straight wire fence drawn from above. Card 1: the fence is straight from edge to edge. Card 2: the fence is cut in two by a crack in the ground and the two halves are no longer in line.

**Caption card:** The ground shook once. Afterward the fence was out of line.

**Words that say when:** once.

## Pair 3: the roadside hill

**Pictures:** A grassy hill beside an empty lane, drawn from the side. Card 1: the hill is steady with bushes on it. Card 2: a patch of earth and rocks has slid down onto the lane and a bare scar is left on the hill. No vehicles and no people.

**Caption card:** After a night of hard rain, a patch of the hill slid down all at once.

**Words that say when:** all at once.

## Pair 4: the creek bank

**Pictures:** A creek called Reed Creek, drawn from above. Card 1: grass grows to the creek edge. Card 2: a big bite is gone from the bank and brown water fills the notch.

**Caption card:** A flood came in one night and cut a big piece off the bank.

**Words that say when:** in one night.

## The word to teach from pair 4

Erosion: wind or water wearing land away and carrying it off. Erosion can be fast or slow. Say this once and write it on the change chart.

## Key (teacher)

Order: first card, then the card with the change, for every pair. Each pair has two sources, the pictures and the caption words, and the two agree. A mark on the chart is: picture | words | source label.
