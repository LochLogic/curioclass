# Four slow changes (day 14)

A hook card and four older and later pairs, each with a separate caption card. Read each caption aloud. Children order each pair, point to the words, and add to the slow column of the change chart. Say aloud: "These pictures are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. No people in any picture, and no waves in any picture.

## Hook card: the worn stone step

**Picture:** A stone step at a school door, with a smooth dip worn in its middle.

**Say (teacher):** "Did anyone see this step change in a day?"

## Pair 1: the worn step

**Pictures:** Older: a flat stone step with sharp edges. Later: the same step with a dip in the middle.

**Caption card:** Many feet stepped here, a little each season.

**Words:** a little each season.

## Pair 2: the smooth rock

**Pictures:** Older: a rough rock with sharp corners lying in a shallow stream. Later: the same rock with round corners.

**Caption card:** Water slid over the rock, and its corners wore smooth little by little.

**Words:** little by little.

## Pair 3: the river bank

**Pictures:** A wide river called Long River, drawn from above, a different place from Reed Creek on day 12. Older: a tree stands well back from the bank edge. Later: the bank edge is at the tree's roots.

**Caption card:** The river took a little of the bank each season.

**Words:** a little each season.

## Pair 4: the sea cliff

**Pictures:** The top edge of a sea cliff above a beach, drawn from the side, with a fence post near the top. Older: the post stands well back from the cliff edge. Later: the cliff edge has moved inland until it is close to the post.

**Caption card:** Wind and rain wore at the cliff top, a little each season.

**Words:** a little each season.
