# The path again, and a fan tray (day 15)

Three path picture cards with three caption cards, then one fan-tray card. Read each caption aloud. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no people.

## Path cards

The class sorts the evidence into fast and slow, two sources each, and does not yet say how to stop the loss.

**Picture older:** The lower part of the path, with grass on both sides and a flat narrow strip of path.

**Picture later:** The same lower part, the path wide and bare, the grass on both sides standing.

**Picture latest:** The same lower part with a ditch down the middle of the path and a fan of soil at its foot.

**Caption older and later:** Children walked here all term.

**Caption latest:** After a big rain this week.

## Fan-tray card

**Picture:** A flat tray of dry sand with a small hand fan at one end, drawn from the side, with wind lines blowing across. A pile of sand lies against the far end of the tray.

**Caption:** Wind moved the sand in this tray.

**Say (teacher):** "The pour record cards and this tray show soil moving the way water or wind goes. What can they show? What can they not show?"
