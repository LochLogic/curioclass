# A second place, Marsh Hill (day 13)

A second slope and six source cards for day 13. Say aloud: "Marsh Hill is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" beneath every card.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. No people in any picture.

## Marsh Hill, two ordered pictures and a caption

**Picture 1:** A grassy slope called Marsh Hill, drawn from the side, with a flat field at its foot. The slope is smooth and green.

**Picture 2:** The same slope. A deep gully runs down it and a fan of mud lies on the field.

**Caption:** The flood came down Marsh Hill in one afternoon and cut a gully.

**Words that say when:** in one afternoon.

**Say (teacher):** "Put the pictures in order. Run the evidence check: ordered pictures, words that say when, a second source that agrees. Is this a fast change like the creek bank on day 12?"

## Six source cards: sort them

Print each card on its own square. Pairs sort them into two piles: "tells when it happened" and "only a story or a liking".

1. Two ordered pictures of a field, before and after a flood. Label: before, after.
2. A caption: The flood came in one afternoon.
3. A sentence: I like the muddy field. It looks like fun.
4. A made-up tale: Once a giant dug the gully with a spoon.
5. An observer's note: The water rose fast and the slope slid down.
6. A sentence: My cousin says this is the best hill.
