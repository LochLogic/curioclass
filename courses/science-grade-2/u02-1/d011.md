# Day 11: A bare path and the grass beside it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the day 10 consensus model pinned. Mix up each group's three path-edge cards. Hang a question board headed "What changed? How quickly?" and chart paper headed "Our path". Choose today's six. Put each child's day 10 retry tickets (s01-X10, s01-X10b, s01-X10c) at their place. Key: key-cards-exits-11-15.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No one in the pictures is hurt. Nobody tells about a flood or storm; any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook

Show pictures 1 to 4 of the worn path and read each aloud. Say: "We made these pictures. They are not our school, but a lot like it." Anyone points and says what they notice. Read picture 4's last lines once: "Why? How could we stop it?" Do not answer.

#### 3 min. Source practice (retrieval)

Read bird cards 1 and 2. Each card is a source, as on days 6 and 7. Every child draws or writes one fact from each in the boxes on side 1.

**Ask.** Which fact helps the young bird stay alive? Expect: it calls, so the adult knows it is hungry (card 1); the adult brings food (card 2). Missed: model one labeled column on the board.

#### 5 min. Forecast practice (retrieval)

Read helper cards A, B and C.

**Ask.** Remember the kindergarten forecast: a forecast warns of strong wind. Point to the helpers getting ready before it arrives. Expect: card A. Everyone points; any child may pass.

**Ask.** How does the warning help? Expect: helpers put loose things away before the wind comes. Missed: read cards A and B together, "before" and "after", and name one more safe way to get ready.

#### 10 min. Sketch what made the path

Each child draws the path before and now. Add a solid arrow where picture 3 showed water running and a dotted arrow where they only think it goes. Fill in "I think ___ made the path." Nothing is marked right or wrong; accept feet, rain, both or more, and do not say what made it.

#### 8 min. Steady, yet changed

Six groups order their three path-edge cards by the captions "At the start of term", "Later", "Latest". Visit each group 30 seconds (6 x 30 s = 3 minutes).

**Ask.** What is different between the first card and the last? Expect: the bare band is wider and has a small hollow in it.

Say: "On any one day the edge looked the same as the day before. Over the term, it changed." Write steady and changed on the chart. Children draw the difference on side 2.

#### 6 min. Question board

Hear six children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "I wonder ___." Write each on the board; the rest follow. Mark who was heard.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented for practice. Read [[s02-X11]] once; everyone points to a picture. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures large on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pictures: look at a worn path, a worn step or a bare patch of lawn together, or draw one. No cards: draw the same spot three times.

**Skip.** The bird and wind cards, the groups and the question board.

- **5 min. Look.** Look at a worn place together.
  **Say.** "What do you notice?" Expect: nothing grows on the path, and grass grows beside it.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the place before and now, and an arrow where water would run.
  **Say.** "What do you think made it?" Expect: any answer, such as feet or rain. Do not correct it.
- **10 min. Steady, yet changed.** Draw the same edge three times: first, later, latest.
  **Say.** "What is different between the first and the last?" Expect: the bare band is wider and has a hollow.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X11]], alone. Draw three ground pictures marked Monday, Friday and one month later, only the last with a hollow, and ask which picture shows a change.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: draw only the "now" picture. Never cut the card ordering or the exit. Longer: compare a worn place outdoors, seen from the doorway, with picture 2.

### Words and supports

Steady: looks the same from one day to the next. Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a place that looked the same every day did not change (error A). Day 12 opens with Return and repair of the day 11 exit tickets, error A first, then four fast changes.
