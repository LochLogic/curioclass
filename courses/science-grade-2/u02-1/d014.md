# Day 14: Slow changes nobody sees in a day

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a set of the four pairs for each group, each caption on its own card. Hang the day 12 chart with its Fast side filled and the Slow side empty. Put out the day 11 sketches. Choose today's six for Say it and a different six for the exit.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No person and no waves are in any card. No years are counted.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 13 exit tickets (s02-X13). Teach error B: write "Slow, because wind and water wear things away slowly." Lay the sand-pile exit card beside it.

**Ask.** Which words say how fast the wind moved the sand? Expect: "one afternoon", so it was fast. The words about when decide how fast, not the word wind.

#### 3 min. Hook

Show the worn stone step, said aloud to be invented for practice.

**Ask.** Did anyone see this step change in a day? Expect: no.

**Ask.** The step has a dip in it. What does that tell us? Expect: it changed.

#### 14 min. Four slow changes

Six groups, each with a set. For each pair, 3 minutes plus 2 to mark: the group orders the older and later pictures; you read the caption aloud and every child points to the words that say when; every child marks the Slow side of the change chart with a small picture, the words and a source label, and the class chart gets the mark with a stepped arrow and the word "slow".

The four pairs, with the words that say when:
- Pair 1, the worn step: "a little each season".
- Pair 2, the smooth rock: "little by little".
- Pair 3, the river bank: "a little each season". Long River is a different place from Reed Creek on day 12.
- Pair 4, the sea cliff: "a little each season".

#### 6 min. Fast beside slow

Read the day 12 Fast side beside the new Slow side.

**Ask.** How are the words different? Expect: "in one night" and "once" against "little by little" and "a little each season". Children write one fast and one slow phrase on side 2.

**Ask.** Why can a slow change look steady on any one day? Expect: each day it changes only a tiny bit, so today looks like yesterday, but the older and later pictures differ.

#### 6 min. Revise the model

Each child takes out their day 11 sketch, keeps the water arrows and adds a stepped arrow with the word "slow" where the path-edge cards showed the bare band growing. Do not say what made the path.

#### 3 min. Say it

Hear six children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "I know it changed because ___." Mark who was heard.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented for practice. Read [[s02-X14]] once; everyone circles yes or no. Hear a different rotating six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pairs large on the board and read the captions aloud. One projector: show one pair at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a worn step or a smooth stone as it was and as it is now. Or look at a rounded old step together.

**Skip.** The groups, the chart and the model.

- **8 min. Older and later.** Draw an older and a later picture of one worn thing.
  **Say.** "Did anyone see it change in a day?" Expect: no, but the later picture is different.
- **10 min. Words.** Say a caption such as "a little each season".
  **Say.** "How do you know it changed?" Expect: "the later picture is different and the words say little by little."
- **7 min. Fast and slow.** Your child says one fast and one slow change.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X14]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pairs, the step and the cliff. Never cut the words that say when or the exit. Longer: add a third source for one pair.

### Words and supports

Slow, older, later, steady. Frame: "I know it changed because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a ledge nobody saw change did not change (error A). Day 15 opens with Return and repair of the day 14 exit tickets, error A, then the path again with fast and slow evidence.
