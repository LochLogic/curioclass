# Day 12: Fast changes and the words that say when

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the four pairs for each group, each caption on its own card. Hang the change chart, only Fast headed. Choose two sixes: Say it, exit. Keys: key-cards-exits-11-15, key-fast-cards-day12.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No card shows a hurt person. No child retells a real event; thank anyone who starts and talk after class (unit opener).

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 11 exit tickets (s02-X11). Teach error A, the commonest: write "The swing ground looked the same every day, so it did not change." Lay the three swing-ground pictures side by side: Monday, Friday, then one month later.

**Ask.** What is different? Expect: the last month has a scuffed hollow. "The difference is the change."

#### 2 min. Open the board

Read "What changed? How quickly?" Today: changes that were quick.

#### 18 min. Four fast changes

Six groups, each with a set. For each pair, 4 minutes (the first, 6): the group orders the two pictures, before then after (1 minute); you read the caption aloud and every child points to the words that say when; every child marks the change chart with a small picture, the words, and the source label "pair 1" (2 minutes); the class chart gets the same mark and a jagged arrow with the word "fast". Say each time: "Two sources agree: the pictures and the words."

The four pairs, with the words that say when:
- Pair 1, the mountain: "in one day".
- Pair 2, the fence line: "once".
- Pair 3, the roadside hill: "all at once".
- Pair 4, the creek bank: "in one night".

Show the photograph credit on pair 1.

#### 5 min. The word erosion

Hold up pair 4.

**Ask.** What carried the bank away? Expect: the flood water. Say: "Erosion is wind or water wearing land away and carrying it off." Say it together and write it. Say once: "Erosion can be fast or slow," and write that. The word fits the creek bank best. Children draw the water carrying the bank on side 1.

#### 4 min. What the four share

**Ask.** What is the same in all four? Expect: the words say it was quick. Write the evidence check on the chart: ordered pictures, words that say when, a second source that agrees. Check pair 1 against all three, aloud; children copy the check on side 1.

#### 3 min. Say it

Hear six children (3 minutes): "I know it was fast because ___." Mark who was heard.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented for practice. Read [[s02-X12]] once; everyone circles fast or slow and the words that tell. Hear a different six (2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pairs large on the board and read the captions aloud. One projector: show one pair at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a beach or a creek bank before and after, and say a caption such as "It happened in one night."

**Skip.** The groups, the chart and the credit line.

- **8 min. Two pictures.** Draw or show a before and an after.
  **Say.** "Which picture is first? What words say when?" Expect: the before; "in one night".
- **10 min. Erosion.** Draw water carrying a bank away.
  **Say.** "What is erosion?" Expect: wind or water wearing land away and carrying it off.
- **7 min. Check.** Your child names the three checks on the page.
  **Say.** "How do you know it was fast?" Expect: "the words say ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X12]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two pairs, including the creek bank. Keep the words that say when and the exit. Longer: find a third source for one pair.

### Words and supports

Erosion, fast, before, after. Frame: "I know it was fast because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count. If a child asks whether anyone was hurt in the eruption, answer plainly: yes, some people were. Scientists now watch volcanoes so people can get away in time. Then return to the pictures.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a fast change is slow because erosion takes a long time (error B) and who says a beach that is still a beach did not change (error A). Day 13 opens with Return and repair of the day 12 exit tickets, the more missed letter first, then a second place.
