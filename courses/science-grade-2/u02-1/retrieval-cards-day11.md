# Retrieval cards (day 11)

Two sets for the two retrieval parts of day 11. Read each caption aloud. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

Drawing style: plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names. Children and adults are drawn with a range of skin tones and hair. No one is hurt in any card.

## Source practice: two bird cards

**Card 1 picture:** A nest in a bush with a young bird, its beak wide open. Caption: The young bird calls out.

**Card 2 picture:** An adult bird on the edge of the same nest, holding a worm toward the young bird. Caption: The adult brings food to the young bird.

**Say:** "Each card is a source. Mark one fact from each source that helps the young bird stay alive. Write a label for each source on your two-column chart."

**Key (teacher):** Card 1: the young bird calls, which tells the adult it needs food. Card 2: the adult brings food. A label for each source is written on the chart.

## Forecast practice: three picture cards

**Card A picture:** A school yard with a forecast page pinned to a wall, showing a cloud with wind lines. A woman in a green scarf and a boy carry a bench and a bin toward a shed. Caption: Before the wind comes, helpers put loose things away.

**Card B picture:** The same yard windy, with leaves and an empty bin tumbling across it. No one is in the picture. Caption: After the wind: loose things blown across the yard.

**Card C picture:** Children sit at tables inside a bright room. Caption: Indoor time.

**Say:** "A forecast warns of strong wind. Mark the picture of helpers getting ready before it arrives. Then say how the warning helps."

**Key (teacher):** Card A. The warning tells the helpers to put loose things away before the wind comes.
