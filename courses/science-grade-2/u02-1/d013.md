# Day 13: A second place, and what counts as evidence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the day 12 chart. Print a set of the four pairs and the six source cards for each pair of children. Choose today's six for the pairs, and a different six for Say it. You speak for Pip. Keys: key-marsh-hill-day13, key-cards-exits-11-15, key-fast-cards-day12.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. No person is in any picture. No child retells a real event.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the day 12 exit tickets (s02-X12). Teach the letter more children made, 5 minutes; the other goes to a picture card at the table.

Error B: write "The flood-cut bank is erosion, and erosion is slow." Lay pair 4, the creek bank, beside the day 11 path-edge cards.

**Ask.** Which words say how fast? Expect: "in one night" for the bank; erosion can be fast or slow, and the words about when decide.

Error A: lay the before and after cards side by side and point to what differs.

#### 8 min. Order and point again

Pairs mix the day 12 pairs, order each one and point to the words that say when. Hear three pairs, six children, 1 minute each pair, 3 minutes (3 x 1 min), while the rest work. Mark who was heard.

#### 6 min. A second place, Marsh Hill (invented for practice, said aloud)

Show Marsh Hill pictures 1 and 2 and read the caption: "The flood came down Marsh Hill in one afternoon and cut a gully."

**Ask.** Which picture comes first? Expect: the smooth green slope.

**Ask.** Which words say when? Expect: "in one afternoon".

**Ask.** Does a fast explanation still fit? Expect: yes. The pictures are in order, the words say when, and the two sources agree, as with the creek bank. Add Marsh Hill to the Fast side of the chart with its jagged arrow.

#### 8 min. Choose the evidence

Pairs sort the six source cards into two piles, "tells when it happened" and "only a story or a liking", as with evidence and story in unit 1. Each child writes the card numbers in the two boxes on side 1.

**Ask.** Why is card 3, "I like the green field," not evidence? Expect: it is a liking, not when it happened. Cards 1, 2 and 5 tell when; cards 3, 4 and 6 are a liking or a story.

#### 5 min. Pip's claim

Pip says, one line at a time: "I looked at one picture, so I am sure."

**Ask.** Is one picture enough? Expect: no. We need words that say when and a second source that agrees. Pip fixes it: "I looked at the pictures and the words, and they agree." No child is the mistake.

#### 5 min. Say it

Hear six more children, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes (6 x 30 s): "I know ___ because ___." The rest write the frame on the page (2 minutes). Mark who was heard.

#### 3 min. Exit

Say the card is invented for practice. Read [[s02-X13]] once; everyone circles fast or slow and the words that tell. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each (6 x 20 s = 2 minutes). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures large on the board and read the cards aloud. One projector: show Marsh Hill there.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write six short slips yourself, three that say when something happened and three that are only a story or a liking.

**Skip.** The pairs, the chart and Pip.

- **10 min. Second place.** Draw a made-up hill before and after a flood, with a caption such as "in one afternoon".
  **Say.** "Which words say when?" Expect: "in one afternoon".
- **10 min. Sort.** Your child sorts the slips into "tells when" and "only a story or a liking".
  **Say.** "Why is this one not evidence?" Expect: it is a liking, not when it happened.
- **5 min. Say it.** Your child writes "I know ___ because ___."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Your child does [[s02-X13]], alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four cards, not six. Never cut Marsh Hill or the exit. Longer: write one new card of each kind.

### Words and supports

Evidence: tells when it happened. Story: made up. Frame: "I know ___ because ___." Pointing, speaking or a scribe all count.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows who says a fast change is slow because wind and water wear things away slowly (error B). Day 14 opens with Return and repair of the day 13 exit tickets, error B first, then four slow changes.
