## Night Skies

[page 1]
Look up at the night sky! What do you see?
*Picture: a child on a hill, head tipped back, under a dark sky.*

[page 2]
Sometimes the sky is dark. Stars shine bright.
*Picture: a dark sky with many stars.*

[page 3]
Sometimes the moon is thin. Sometimes the moon is round.
*Picture: two pictures: a thin moon, then a round moon.*

[page 4]
You might see the moon in the day, too.
*Picture: a pale moon over a bright blue daytime sky.*

[page 5]
Stars are far away. The sun is a star, too!
*Picture: a small sun beside a field of distant stars.*

[page 6]
Some birds fly at night. Some fly high in the sky.
*Picture: a few dark birds in a night sky near the moon.*

[page 7]
You might see a plane. The plane has lights that blink.
*Picture: a small plane with blinking lights in the night sky.*

[page 8]
Look through a window at the night sky.
*Picture: a window with a child's shape; the night sky and a star beyond.*

[page 9]
Have you seen a shooting star? If you are lucky, you might!
*Picture: a streak of light across the sky.*

[page 10]
What will you see in the sky tonight?
*Picture: an open night sky with a few stars and a crescent moon.*
