# Cones and Tubes

**Decodable through cycle 17.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 17 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practiced (cycle 17):** u_e and e_e (cycle 17). Words: cone, cones, cute, mule, tube, tubes, tune. Names with the pattern: june, pete, steve.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, has, is, the, to.
- **Names (all decodable):** June, Pete, Steve.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
