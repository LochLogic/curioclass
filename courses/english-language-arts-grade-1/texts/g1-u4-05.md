Keys for day 147 (the plan's keys fit this text): point = birds help people; reasons = eggs or meat give food, and birds eat insects that harm food plants; "Birds live all over the world" is a true detail, not a reason (error C). Words in the text: harmful, destroy, protect, useful. The words helps, helped, helping, harmless and bank are not in the text (they are reserved for the new cards).

---

[page 1]
Birds live all over the world. They are important to people in many places.
*Picture: a world map with a small bird drawn on each land shape.*

[page 2]
Many people eat eggs from chickens. They also eat chickens and turkeys. Eggs and meat from birds are food for many people.
*Picture: a basket of eggs beside a hen, and a family table with a plate of food.*

[page 3]
Birds also help the plants that people eat. Some insects are harmful. Beetles and caterpillars can chew and destroy plants on farms. Many birds eat these insects, so the plants stay safe.
*Picture: a bird on a plant stem with a caterpillar in its beak, a half-eaten leaf, and a healthy plant with fruit.*

[page 4]
These are some reasons why people should help protect birds, so birds can live and grow. Birds are very useful to people!
*Picture: a child filling a bowl of water for a bird, a few birds nearby, a green garden behind.*
