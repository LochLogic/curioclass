# Ben's Donut

**Decodable through cycle 14.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 14 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practised (cycle 14):** open syllables with the long vowel (cycle 14). Words: be, donut, hi, no, robot, we. Names with the pattern: bo.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, has, he, his, is, said, the, to, was.
- **Names (all decodable):** Ben, Bo.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
