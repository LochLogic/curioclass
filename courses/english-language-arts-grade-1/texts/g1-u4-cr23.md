## A Little Seed

[page 1]
See the little seed under the tree?
*Picture: a tree in a field; a small brown seed on the ground beneath it.*

[page 2]
It is a windy day. The wind picks up the seed. Up, up, up!
*Picture: wind lines curl through the grass; the seed floats up.*

[page 3]
The seed lands in the grass, deep in the dirt.
*Picture: a seed tucked in brown dirt between grass blades.*

[page 4]
The seed sleeps. Rain falls on the dirt.
*Picture: rain drops fall on the dirt over the seed.*

[page 5]
The sun is on the dirt, too. The dirt heats up.
*Picture: a bright sun over the same patch of dirt.*

[page 6]
Soon the seed cracks! A tiny root reaches deep.
*Picture: the seed splits and a thin white root points down.*

[page 7]
A green shoot reaches up. The shoot grows and grows.
*Picture: a short green shoot pokes through the dirt.*

[page 8]
Every week, the plant gets taller. It has one leaf, then three.
*Picture: three pictures of a plant with one leaf, then two, then three.*

[page 9]
Then the plant has a sweet, red bloom. A bee sits on it.
*Picture: a red flower with a bee resting on it.*

[page 10]
It really is a plant! It is not a little seed anymore.
*Picture: the full plant with its bloom beside the small seed.*
