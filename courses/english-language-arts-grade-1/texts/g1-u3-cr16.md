# Mike and Dave Hike

**Decodable through cycle 16.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 16 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practised (cycle 16):** i_e and o_e (cycle 16). Words: hike, hole, home, like, mole, nine, smile, stone, time.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, has, his, is, the, to.
- **Names (all decodable):** Dave, Mike.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
