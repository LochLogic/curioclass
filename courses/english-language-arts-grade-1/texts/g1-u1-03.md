Teacher note. The teacher reads the poem first with the sounds; children then join the refrains "Tap, tap, tap" and "Bang, bang, bang" and echo each line. The word "bang" is echoed, not decoded (the ng spelling comes in unit 2). The poem uses the hammer from the building-site article (Builders at Work), so day 5 can connect its words to that article. It is not a decodable text.

## Text

Tap, tap, tap,
the little hammer sings.
Tap, tap, tap,
on a nail for tiny things.

Bang, bang, bang,
the big hammer booms.
Bang, bang, bang,
on boards for new rooms.

Hold the handle, steady, slow.
Tap, tap, tap, and in they go.
Hush. The hammer rests at last,
and the shelf stands strong and fast.
