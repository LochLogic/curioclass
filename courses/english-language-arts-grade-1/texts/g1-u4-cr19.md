## Looking for Mars

[page 1]
It is dark. It is time to look for Mars!
*Picture: a night sky over a park with a few small stars; two children, Pat and James, stand on the grass.*

[page 2]
Pat and James go to the park. It is not far.
*Picture: the two children walk along a path toward a park gate.*

[page 3]
Pat has a scarf and a hat. James has a map of the stars.
*Picture: Pat in a scarf and hat; James holding a paper map with star dots on it.*

[page 4]
Some stars are big. Some stars are just dots.
*Picture: a wide sky with a few big stars and many tiny dots.*

[page 5]
"Mars is a red dot," says James. "Look hard!"
*Picture: James points up at a small red dot among the stars.*

[page 6]
Pat looks and looks. Pat can not spot Mars.
*Picture: Pat, head tipped back, turns slowly on the grass.*

[page 7]
"Would Mars hide?" asks Pat. "No," says James. "But it is hard to spot."
*Picture: the two children sit side by side and look up.*

[page 8]
Then Pat grabs his arm. "Mars!" says Pat. "I see it!"
*Picture: Pat holds James by the arm and points at the red dot.*

[page 9]
"I knew it!" says James. "Does it look red?" "Yes!" says Pat.
*Picture: both children grin up at the sky; the red dot glows.*

[page 10]
"Can we look again?" asks Pat. "Yes," says James. "Bye, Mars!"
*Picture: the children wave at the sky as they stand up from the grass.*
