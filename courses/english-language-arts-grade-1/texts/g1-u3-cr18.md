# A Day at the Lake

**Decodable through cycle 18.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 18 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practised (cycle 18):** silent-e bases with -ed, -ing, -s, e dropped before the vowel ending (cycle 18). Words: hiked, hoped, joked, making, named, smiled, taking.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, the, to, was.
- **Names (all decodable):** Bob, Dave, Kate.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
