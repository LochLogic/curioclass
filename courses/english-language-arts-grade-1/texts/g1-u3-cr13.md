# Pam's Backpack

**Decodable through cycle 13.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 13 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practiced (cycle 13):** compound words and closed two-syllable words (cycle 13). Words: backpack, catfish, hilltop, hotdog.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, has, is, she, the, was.
- **Names (all decodable):** Dad, Pam.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
