# Sam Wants a Pet

- title: Sam Wants a Pet
- text_id: g1-u2-cr6
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 6 decodable reader, 6 pages, one or two short sentences a page
- word count: 51 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 6 reader: first read on day 40 once its patterns are mapped; matched rereads on the days after (days 40 to 43); a decodable the child reads, not a read-aloud
- days that read it: 40 to 43
- high-frequency words: a, and, i, in, is, said, see, the
- patterns: short e (bed, get, pet, net); short o review (not, got); short a, i and u review (Sam, sad, can, big, bug)
- note: Cycle 6 on its first reading day (40): short e is mapped on day 39. -ank, -ink, -unk and -onk are not mapped until day 43, so no nasal-ending word is used; no -ed, -ing, action -s or clusters. Avoids the cycle's reserved words (hen, web, pen, bank, sink) and the retry sentence Rob has a pet. The title is adult-read (wants has an untaught pattern).

## Pages

1. Sam is in bed. Sam is sad.
   Picture: Sam sits in bed with his chin on his knees.

2. "Can I get a pet, Dad?" said Sam.
   Picture: Sam leans out of bed toward Dad in the doorway.

3. Dad said, "Not a big pet."
   Picture: Dad holds up his hands, smiling and shaking his head.

4. Sam got a net. Sam got a bug in the net!
   Picture: Sam holds a net with a green bug inside.

5. Dad and Sam see the bug. Dad is not mad.
   Picture: Dad crouches next to Sam to look at the bug.

6. The bug is a pet. Sam is not sad.
   Picture: Sam grins with the bug in the net.
