# The City Nest (opinion model)

- **title:** The City Nest (a model opinion paragraph)
- **text_id:** g1-u4-opm
- **author:** Curio Class
- **year:** 2026
- **kind:** informational (opinion)
- **form:** one model paragraph of four parts (topic, opinion, reason, closing), read aloud and printed for the class to mark
- **word count:** 45 words
- **use:** the model read on day 136 (children point to the topic, the opinion, the reason and the closing; four strips are ordered to match); kept by each child, marked, for 137 and 138
- **read on days:** 136 (reread 137, 138 as the marked model)
- **side taken:** take the nest down. The day 139 key takes the other side (the nest stays) and uses a different reason, so this model is not the key's paragraph.
- **reason used:** pieces of the nest fall to the sidewalk below, where people walk. This is one of the reasons for taking the nest down in the base text's nest-debate strips, so *A City Nest: Two Views* (g1-u4-04) must hold this reason on its take-down side; if it does not, change the sentence here to a reason it does hold.
- **source note:** New writing; the reason's idea is adapted from the nest-debate supporting strips of EL Education Grade 1 Module 4 Unit 2 Lesson 4 (CC BY 4.0), the sidewalk-and-walkers reason. No sentence is taken from them.
- **content note:** a class talks about a nest on paper; no child goes near a nest. Hawks are named plainly and nothing is graphic.

---

## The City Nest

[the four parts, printed one to a line for marking]

Hawks have a nest on a tall city building. *(topic)*
I think the nest should come down. *(opinion)*
I think so because bits of the nest fall onto the sidewalk below, and people walk there. *(reason)*
So the city nest should come down, to keep the sidewalk safe. *(closing)*

*Picture: a tall building with a big stick nest high on a ledge; below it a sidewalk with a few small bits on the ground and people walking by.*
