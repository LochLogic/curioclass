# Kate and Jake Bake a Cake

**Decodable through cycle 15.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 15 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practiced (cycle 15):** a_e (cycle 15). Words: bake, cake, make, plate.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, has, is, of, the, was.
- **Names (all decodable):** Jake, Kate.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i, and the cycle patterns above).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
