## Nia Calls for Help

[page 1]
Nia is waiting for her team to finish stretching after soccer practice. She walks to the old bench by the big glass window of the sports hall. Something gray is lying on the ground under the window.

Nia sees a hurt bird by a bench. She fetches her coach.

"Coach Lee! Coach Lee! Come and look!" Nia calls, and she runs across the field.
*Picture: a girl of about seven with dark curly hair and a soccer shirt points at the ground beside a wooden bench. A big window of a sports hall shines behind it. A gray dove lies still on the grass.*

[page 2]
Coach Lee walks back with Nia. They stop a long way from the bird. The bird is a gray dove. It blinks. It tries to fly, but it only flaps and falls back down.

"Do not touch it, Nia," Coach Lee says kindly. "A wild bird that hurts itself is scared. It could be hurt more if we pick it up."

Nia nods. Her heart feels fast. She wanted to help the dove with her own hands, but she knows that is not the best help.
*Picture: Nia and a tall coach with a whistle on a cord stand far from the dove. The dove flaps one wing against the grass.*

[page 3]
"What can we do?" Nia asks.

"We can ask for help," says Coach Lee. "Birds like this need a trained helper. A trained helper has learned how to look after hurt wild birds. I will phone the wildlife center."

Coach Lee takes a phone from her bag. Nia stands very still and quiet. She does not run. She does not shout. A bird needs a quiet place.
*Picture: Coach Lee holds a phone. Nia stands next to her with her hands pressed together. The dove sits small and quiet near the bench.*

[page 4]
Nia waits away from the bird. Her coach phones a trained helper.

"Hello. A dove hit our window and cannot fly. We are staying back. Yes, we will keep everyone away. Thank you," Coach Lee says into the phone.

Nia watches the dove from the other side of the field. She sits on the grass. The phone call is over, but the helper is not here yet. Nia still waits, and waiting is hard. She holds her knees and counts the clouds.
*Picture: Nia sits on the grass at a distance with her knees pulled up. Coach Lee, still holding the phone, stands beside her. The dove and bench are far off.*

[page 5]
At last a small blue car comes slowly up the drive. A woman gets out with a soft towel and a box. She kneels by the dove, very slowly, and lifts it with care.

"It is stunned, but it is breathing well," she says. "I will take it to the center, where it can rest and be checked. You called the right people."

Nia smiles. The dove is in careful hands now. The gray bird is safe, and Nia knows what to do next time. She calls a grown-up and keeps her distance.
*Picture: a woman in a green jacket kneels with a towel-wrapped dove. Nia and Coach Lee watch from a few steps away. Other children in soccer shirts wave from the field.*
