# On the Pond

- title: On the Pond
- text_id: g1-u2-cr10
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 10 decodable reader, 7 pages, one or two short sentences a page
- word count: 48 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 10 reader: first read on day 60 once its patterns are mapped; matched rereads on the days after (days 60 to 63); a decodable the child reads, not a read-aloud
- days that read it: 60 to 63
- high-frequency words: a, and, in, is, the
- patterns: l- and r-blends (frog, crab, drip, plop, grins, swish); s-blend sw (swims, swish, swim); -es (kisses); action -s; short o, i, u, a review
- note: Cycle 10 on its first reading day (60): initial l- and r-blends were taught on day 59 and -es on day 60. -er and -est are taught on day 61, so none is used. The title word pond (a final -nd cluster, cycle 11) is adult-read and never appears in the body. Avoids fish, dishes, boxes, faster and hummed.

## Pages

1. A frog sits on a log in the sun.
   Picture: A green frog sits on a log; the sun is bright above the water.

2. A crab sits on the log.
   Picture: A red crab climbs onto the log beside the frog.

3. The sun is hot. Drip, drip, drip!
   Picture: Drops run down the frog's back.

4. Plop! The frog swims.
   Picture: The frog dives; rings spread out on the water.

5. Plop! The crab swims. Swish, swish!
   Picture: The crab pushes after it, trailing ripples.

6. The frog kisses the crab.
   Picture: The frog touches its nose to the crab's claw.

7. The frog and the crab swim and swim. The crab grins.
   Picture: Both swim in the water; the crab's wide grin shows.
