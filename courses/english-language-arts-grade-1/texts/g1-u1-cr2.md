## Pages

3. Pat has a map.
   Picture: Pat holds a map unfolded wide.

4. That cat can tap.
   Picture: A striped cat sits on a table, one paw raised.

5. The cat can tap the pan.
   Picture: The cat taps the pan; it tilts.

6. The cat can tap the hat.
   Picture: The hat is knocked to the floor.

7. The cat can tap the map.
   Picture: The map slides off the table in a heap.

8. Pat can pat the cat.
   Picture: Pat, beside the heap of hat, pan and map, pats the cat.

9. The cat can nap.
   Picture: The cat curls up asleep on the heap.

10. Pat can nap.
   Picture: Pat sits beside the cat, eyes closed, in a happy jumble.
