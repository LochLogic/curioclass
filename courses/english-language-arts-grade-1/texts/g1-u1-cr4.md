# Pat's Lunch

- title: Pat's Lunch
- text_id: g1-u1-cr4
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 4 decodable reader, nine pages, one sentence a page
- word count: 50 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 4 reader: first read on day 22 once u, qu and plural -s are mapped; matched rereads on days 23 to 25
- days that read it: 22 to 25
- high-frequency words: the, a, is, has, look, and, in, his, with
- patterns: short u (tub, bun, run, hug); qu (quit); plural -s on the taught base bun (buns); short a and i review (ham, jam, it, in)
- note: Cycle 4: short u, qu, plural -s on taught bases (cats, mats, pans, tubs, buns); this reader uses buns only. No clusters, short o or e. The title "Pat's Lunch" is adult-read (lunch has an untaught pattern).

## Pages

1. Pat has a tub.
   Picture: Pat carries a round tub with a lid.

2. The tub has buns and ham.
   Picture: Inside the open tub: buns and slices of ham.

3. It has jam with the buns.
   Picture: A small jar of jam sits beside the buns.

4. A cat can run at the tub.
   Picture: A striped cat runs toward the tub.

5. Quit it, cat!
   Picture: Pat, hands up, calls to the cat.

6. The cat has a bun.
   Picture: The cat has a bun in its mouth.

7. Pat can hug the cat.
   Picture: Pat hugs the cat anyway.

8. Look at the cat with the bun!
   Picture: Close up: the cat, bun in its mouth, looks pleased.

9. Pat has buns with ham and jam.
   Picture: Pat sits with a plate of buns, ham and jam; the cat naps nearby.
