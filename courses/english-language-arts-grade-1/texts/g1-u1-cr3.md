## Pages

1. Look at this map.
   Picture: A child, Pat, holds a map with a dotted path and a dish drawn at the end.

2. It has a path and a pit.
   Picture: Close up of the map: the dotted path passes a round pit.

3. Pat and his cat sit with the map.
   Picture: Pat and a striped cat sit side by side with the map spread out.

4. Pat can tap at the path.
   Picture: Pat taps the path on the map with one finger.

5. That cat is in a pit!
   Picture: The cat has hopped into a shallow pit; only its ears show.

6. Pat can pat the cat.
   Picture: Pat reaches down and pats the cat.

7. The map has a dish with fish.
   Picture: Close up of the map: a dish with a fish drawn on it.

8. Pat and his cat dash at the dish.
   Picture: Pat and the cat run along the path at a real dish.

9. The cat has the fish.
   Picture: The cat holds the fish; the dish is empty.

10. Pat has the map.
   Picture: Pat, smiling, holds the map up.
