## Sam's Hammer

[page 1]
Sam's class will act in a skit. It is a skit about an old farm.
*Picture: a classroom stage of painted paper: a flat farm field and a few chairs; children stand in a line.*

[page 2]
Nell will be the first girl in the skit. James will be a man on the farm.
*Picture: Nell and James in simple costumes on the paper stage.*

[page 3]
Sam is sad. He does not want to act.
*Picture: Sam sits at the side with his chin on his hand.*

[page 4]
"I can not act," says Sam.
*Picture: Sam shakes his head and looks at the floor.*

[page 5]
Dad has a plan. "Sam, you can use your hammer," says Dad.
*Picture: Dad kneels beside Sam and holds out a small hammer.*

[page 6]
"I will help with the set," says Sam. He hammers a barn and a bench.
*Picture: Sam at a table hammering a red cardboard barn.*

[page 7]
Bam, bam, bam! Sam hammers and hammers. The barn is red.
*Picture: a finished red barn and a small bench stand on the stage.*

[page 8]
Ouch! The hammer hits his hand. Sam is hurt, but just a bit.
*Picture: Sam holds up one hand and winces; Dad looks on kindly.*

[page 9]
The skit is a hit! Nell and James act. Sam turns the barn around.
*Picture: Sam turns the barn on the stage while Nell and James act.*

[page 10]
The class claps over and over. "The set is the best part!" says Dad.
*Picture: children clap; Sam smiles beside the barn.*
