# Sam's Rock

- title: Sam's Rock
- text_id: g1-u2-cr8
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 8 decodable reader, 7 pages, one or two short sentences a page
- word count: 65 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 8 reader: first read on day 50 once its patterns are mapped; matched rereads on the days after (days 50 to 53); a decodable the child reads, not a read-aloud
- days that read it: 50 to 53
- high-frequency words: a, and, has, in, is, said, see, the
- patterns: ck (rock, sock, deck, tick, tock); wh (which); action -s (digs); short e, o, i, a, u review (red, tan, hid, hug)
- note: Cycle 8 on its first reading day (50): wh and -ck are mapped that day. -ed said /t/ is not taught until day 53, so no -ed word is used. The name Nell (a doubled -ll, cycle 9) is not used; the friend is Jan. Avoids whip and peck (the day 53 sample words) and kick, kicked. The title is adult-read only for its apostrophe.

## Pages

1. Sam has a rock. Sam has a red sock and a tan sock.
   Picture: Sam holds a smooth rock; a red sock and a tan sock lie beside him.

2. Sam hid the rock in a sock.
   Picture: Sam tucks the rock into the tan sock behind his back.

3. Jan is on the deck. Jan can not see the rock.
   Picture: Jan sits on a wooden deck, frowning at the two socks.

4. "Which sock has the rock?" said Jan.
   Picture: Jan points from one sock to the other.

5. Jan digs in the red sock. It is not the rock!
   Picture: Jan pulls the red sock open; it is empty.

6. Jan digs in the tan sock. Tick, tock! It is the rock!
   Picture: Jan lifts the rock out of the tan sock.

7. Jan and Sam hug.
   Picture: Jan and Sam hug on the deck, the rock in the middle.
