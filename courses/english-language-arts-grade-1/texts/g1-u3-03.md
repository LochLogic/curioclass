# Poems About Birds

- **title:** Poems about birds (Feather Coat, Wing Clues)
- **text_id:** g1-u3-03
- **author:** Curio Class
- **year:** 2026
- **kind:** literary
- **form:** two short rhymed poems; Wing Clues is a riddle poem
- **word count:** about 77 words (text only, not picture notes)
- **use:** paired (read aloud, then reread chorally; clapped for beat; children do not decode them alone)
- **read on days:** both whole on 108; Feather Coat reread 108 to 111; Wing Clues 111, 118, 119.

Teacher note: the coat is imagined (a bird has no buttons or sleeves); the facts under it are real (feathers cover a bird; a bird can fluff them to keep warm). Wing Clues gives four true clues about one bird without naming it, so children match clues to a picture, then say what a clue tells.

---

## Feather Coat

[page 1]
My coat has no buttons.
My coat has no seams.
Feathers grow all over me,
soft as morning dreams.
*Picture: a small round bird on a branch, drawn as if wearing a coat, with no buttons or seams.*

[page 2]
When the cold wind blows,
I fluff up my feathers
and keep the warm air close,
whatever the weather.
*Picture: the same bird puffed up on a snowy branch, a scarf of wind swirling past.*

---

## Wing Clues

[page 1]
Two small wings, quick and round,
push the air with a whirring sound.
Four toes grip a branch up high.
A thick beak cracks seeds nearby.
*Picture: four small clue panels in a row, a wing, a foot on a branch, a beak and a seed, a question mark. The bird itself is hidden behind a leaf.*

[page 2]
Chirp, chirp, chirp! Guess my name.
Match each clue, then play the game.
*Picture: the leaf lifts to show a sparrow hopping on a fence.*

Teacher key (not read to children before they guess): a sparrow.
