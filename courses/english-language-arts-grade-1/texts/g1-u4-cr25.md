## Reading Together

[page 1]
It is a rainy morning. Pat and James are inside.
*Picture: rain on a window; two children indoors.*

[page 2]
They sit on a beanbag. James has a story about a rabbit.
*Picture: Pat and James on a beanbag with a picture story open.*

[page 3]
James reads. Pat looks at the page. They read together.
*Picture: the two children read, their heads close.*

[page 4]
Sam comes in with a basket. It has paper and a crayon in it.
*Picture: Sam at the door with a basket.*

[page 5]
"Can I read, too?" asks Sam. "Yes," says James. "Sit by me."
*Picture: Sam sits down next to James on the beanbag.*

[page 6]
James reads a page by himself. Pat tries a hard line.
*Picture: James turns a page; Pat points at a word.*

[page 7]
"Sound it out," says Sam. "Pat, you can do it!"
*Picture: Sam leans in and points with a smile.*

[page 8]
Soon the rain stops. "Let us go outside," says Sam.
*Picture: a window; the rain stops and a patch of sun shows.*

[page 9]
"Not yet," says Pat. "We have to finish the story!"
*Picture: Pat holds the story close.*

[page 10]
They read together. A good story is fun with a pal!
*Picture: all three children read on the beanbag.*
