## Pat's Birthday

[page 1]
Today is Pat's birthday. She is six!
*Picture: a bedroom with a paper chain of flags; Pat sits up in bed.*

[page 2]
Pat gets up at six. "Can we play?" asks Pat. "Not yet," I say.
*Picture: Pat in pajamas bounces at the door; an older child holds up a hand.*

[page 3]
"I want rain!" says Pat. "I want a rainbow!"
*Picture: Pat with her arms spread wide.*

[page 4]
"It may not rain today," I say. "But we will see."
*Picture: the older child kneels and speaks to Pat.*

[page 5]
We look out the window. The sun is up. No rain.
*Picture: two children at a window; the sky is bright and clear.*

[page 6]
Gran has a tray. On the tray is a cake!
*Picture: Gran carries a tray with a round cake.*

[page 7]
On top of the cake is a rainbow!
*Picture: a close picture of the cake with a bright rainbow in frosting.*

[page 8]
"A rainbow for me!" says Pat. "It will not blow away!"
*Picture: Pat claps beside the cake.*

[page 9]
Pat blows out the flame. I hand Pat a gift. It is a red box.
*Picture: Pat blows out a candle; a small gift in a red box.*

[page 10]
We both laugh. Pat always has fun on her birthday!
*Picture: the two children laugh together by the cake.*
