# A Sunset Picnic

**Decodable through cycle 12.** Only letters and patterns taught through cycle 12 of the Skills Block order, plus the high-frequency words below.

- **Pattern practised (cycle 12):** closed two-syllable words, two consonants between the vowels (cycle 12). Words: basket, napkin, picnic, rabbit, sunset.
- **High-frequency words used:** a, the, was.
- **Names (all decodable):** Kim, Pat.
- **Other words:** earlier cycles only (short vowels, digraphs, blends, final blends, -s, -es, -ed, y as long i).
- **Kept out on purpose:** the reserved words of the foundations samples and exits (cobweb, muffin, catnip, bedbug, tulip, moment, plane, brave, rope, kite, cube, these, ride, riding, wave, drape).
