## Lights Out

Bright lights at night can pull birds toward windows.
People can turn off lights they do not need.
Then birds are not pulled toward the glass.
Please turn the lights out for birds.

*Picture: a tall building at night; half its windows dark, half lit; a few small birds flying past the dark windows.*
