# I Look Out

- title: I Look Out
- text_id: g1-u2-cr11
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 11 decodable reader, 6 pages, one or two short sentences a page
- word count: 38 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 11 reader: first read on day 65 once its patterns are mapped; matched rereads on the days after (days 65 to 68); a decodable the child reads, not a read-aloud
- days that read it: 65 to 68
- high-frequency words: a, i, in, look, see, the
- patterns: ou (out, found); -ed said /id/ (planted, lifted); final clusters -nt, -nd, -ft (tent, wind, planted, lifted); l- and r-blends (flag, flap, glad, planted); short a, e, i, o, u review
- note: Cycle 11 on its first reading day (65): final clusters and ou are taught on day 64 and -ed /id/ on day 65. The child only looks at a hill, never at the sun. Avoids kicked, sand, dusk, rested, looks, looking, filled, boxes, faster and fastest (the day 68 samples) and the base words rest and fast.

## Pages

1. I look out.
   Picture: A child at a window looks out at a hill.

2. I see a tent on a hill.
   Picture: A small tent stands on the hill.

3. Dad found a flag.
   Picture: Dad holds up a bright flag beside the tent.

4. Dad planted the flag on the hill.
   Picture: Dad pushes the flag pole into the ground.

5. The wind lifted the flag. Flap, flap!
   Picture: The flag streams out in the wind.

6. I see the flag in the wind. I am glad.
   Picture: The child at the window smiles.
