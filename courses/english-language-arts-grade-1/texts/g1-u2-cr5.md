- high-frequency words: a, and, has, in, is, look, the
- patterns: short o (box, pot, mop, dog, on, not, nod); x as two sounds in box; short a review (Sam, mad); no clusters and no endings

## Pages

1. Sam has a box.
   Picture: Sam holds a plain cardboard box with its lid open.

2. In the box is a pot.
   Picture: Inside the box: a small pot.

3. In the box is a mop.
   Picture: Inside the box: a mop with a long handle.

4. Look! In the box is a dog!
   Picture: A small dog sits up in the box with a mop beside it; Sam stares.

5. The dog is on the mop.
   Picture: The dog sits on the mop head and wags its tail.

6. Sam is not mad. Sam and the dog nod.
   Picture: Sam smiles and nods; the dog nods too.
