## Sam's Throat Hurts

[page 1]
Sam wakes up. His throat hurts!
*Picture: Sam sits up in bed, one hand on his neck.*

[page 2]
"I do not want to eat," says Sam. "I am very sad."
*Picture: a plate of food on a tray; Sam turns away.*

[page 3]
Dad has a coat on. "You should see a doctor," says Dad.
*Picture: Dad in a coat holds out Sam's coat.*

[page 4]
The doctor has a stick and a lamp. She looks at his throat.
*Picture: a doctor with a small lamp on her head looks at Sam, who has his mouth open.*

[page 5]
"Open wide," says the doctor. "Rest and wait. It is just a bug."
*Picture: the doctor smiles and Sam holds his mouth wide.*

[page 6]
Sam gets back in bed. He can only rest. Dad makes a mug of tea.
*Picture: Sam in bed under a blanket; Dad sets a mug on a small table.*

[page 7]
Sam waits and waits. He naps in bed.
*Picture: Sam asleep, with a window and a moon outside.*

[page 8]
After a good nap, Sam is well.
*Picture: Sam stretches in bed, smiling.*

[page 9]
"Can I eat?" asks Sam. Dad hands him a plate of beans.
*Picture: Dad passes a plate of beans to Sam.*

[page 10]
Sam eats the beans. Then he is glad. His throat is not so bad!
*Picture: Sam eats at the table, with a big smile.*
