# Classroom Sign Model

- **title:** Classroom Sign Model (a four-sentence tool paragraph)
- **text_id:** g1-u1-04
- **author:** Curio Class, adapted from EL Education
- **year:** 2026
- **kind:** informational
- **form:** model paragraph (topic, two facts, closing), one page
- **word count:** 26 words (text only, not picture notes)
- **use:** paired. The teacher reads it aloud whole; children then point to each sentence and say it. Children are not asked to decode it alone, though the short words (crayon, tape, sign) may be read chorally on days 22 and 28.
- **read on days:** 7 (first reading, as the paragraph-frame model), 22 (shared reading, four strips: topic, fact, fact, closing), 28 (analyze the parts and write a topic sentence), 33 (returned to with the hammer poem).
- **source and licence:** Adapted from EL Education K-5 Language Arts, CC BY 4.0, file G1M1U3L8_ModuleLessons-SupportingMaterials-0419.doc (the classroom display sign teacher model, grade 1 module 1 unit 3 lesson 8). The sentences are newly worded and rewritten about a crayon and tape rather than scissors and glue; changes made, no pictures reused. Licence: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Teacher note: sentence 1 is the topic, sentences 2 and 3 are facts about what each tool did, and sentence 4 is the closing. Classroom tools only; no outside fact needs checking.

[page 1]
We used tools to make our classroom sign.
A crayon made the letters dark.
Tape joined the paper pieces.
The sign helps people find our room.
*Picture: a paper classroom sign taped together from three pieces, dark crayon letters on it, a crayon and a roll of tape lying beside it.*
