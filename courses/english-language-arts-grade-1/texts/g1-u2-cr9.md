# Sam and Nell on the Hill

- title: Sam and Nell on the Hill
- text_id: g1-u2-cr9
- author: Curio Class
- year: 2026
- kind: literary
- form: cycle 9 decodable reader, 7 pages, one or two short sentences a page
- word count: 49 (title not counted; the teacher reads the title)
- use: Cycle 9 reader: first read on day 55 once its patterns are mapped; matched rereads on the days after (days 55 to 58); a decodable the child reads, not a read-aloud
- days that read it: 55 to 58
- high-frequency words: a, and, but, of, the, was
- patterns: doubled finals -ll and -ss (Nell, hill, spilled, yelled, mess); s-blend st (stop); -ed said /d/ (spilled, yelled); -ed said /t/ (licked); short a, e, i, o, u review
- note: Cycle 9 on its first reading day (55): doubled finals, s-blends and -ed /d/ are taught on day 54 and -ed /t/ on day 53. The Skills Block title word with the vowel sound of all and ball is not a taught pattern, so this reader is about a spilled jug and has its own title. Avoids fill, filled, buzz (the day 58 sample words), the day 57 sentence about Stan, and rocked. The title is adult-read.

## Pages

1. Sam and Nell sat on a hill.
   Picture: Two children sit at the top of a grassy hill.

2. Nell had a jug of jam. Sam had a bun.
   Picture: Nell holds a jug; Sam holds a bun.

3. Nell spilled the jam!
   Picture: The jug tips and jam runs down the grass.

4. Sam yelled, "Stop, Nell!"
   Picture: Sam holds up a hand.

5. The jam was on the hill. It was a mess.
   Picture: Jam smears the grass, the jug on its side.

6. Sam and Nell licked the jug.
   Picture: The two children lick jam off the jug.

7. It was a mess, but it was fun.
   Picture: Both children laugh, jam on their chins.
