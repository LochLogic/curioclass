# Day 59 calendar card and sentence contrasts

**Calendar card.** A large card printed with the date for the day, written with its comma between the day and the year, for example "May 2, 2026". Each child copies the date from the card onto the page.

**Contrasts for the board (each wrong one is shown beside the right one, once, then taken down).**
- "Stars above" and "The stars are above the roof." (a fragment and a sentence)
- "sun moon stars" and "sun, moon, stars." (no commas and with commas)
- "May 2 2026" and "May 2, 2026." (no comma and with the comma)

The contrasts are the day's teaching and stay off the exit cards. Children say which is written right and why; nobody is the example of a mistake.
