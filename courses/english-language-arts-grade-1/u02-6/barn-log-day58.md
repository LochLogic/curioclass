# Day 58 day-and-night barn log

One barn view drawn twice on a folded sheet, drawn by the teacher before class.

- **Left, day.** A barn, a fence, a hill and a bright sky with a sun. No star marks anywhere.
- **Right, night.** The same barn, fence and hill under a dark sky with a handful of star dots and a moon.
- **Arrow card.** Joins the text fact "The sun lights the sky, so we cannot see the stars" to the day picture.

Each child draws their own two log pictures (day, night) and an arrow from a text fact to its log picture, with the label "Stars in Daylight and Darkness" as the only writing. Night is shown gently, with the moon and stars lit. In a room with nothing, fold a page in two and draw both views.
