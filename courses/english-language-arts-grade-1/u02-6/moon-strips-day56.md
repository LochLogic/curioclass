# Day 56 moon strips and arrow card

Two picture strips go on the wall side by side. Each has a label card. A pencil-line arrow card (an arrow drawn on a small card) joins a fact to the page where it is read.

- **Strip one, "moves" (where the moon is).** Two pictures: a full moon above a barn roof, then the same moon above a tall tree. Label card: "moves". The moon is a plain round disc in both pictures.
- **Strip two, "changes" (its shape).** Two pictures taken from the text's own page 3 and page 4: a thin curve and a half. Label card: "changes".
- **Arrow card.** An arrow from a picture of the moon with a small circle of Earth beside it to the words "The Changing Moon". This is the page 5 note: the moon goes around Earth, so we see more or less of it.
- **Source label for the notes.** "The Changing Moon".

The strips show one pattern each, so a child can say which pattern a picture tells. Nobody looks at the sun. In a room with nothing, draw the strips on folded paper and use a pencil line for the arrow.
