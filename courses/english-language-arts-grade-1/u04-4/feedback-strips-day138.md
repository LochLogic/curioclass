# Feedback strips

Slips of paper for a peer to leave one written question about a draft, headed "I wonder" with a line. Pairs trade drafts, write one question, then read it aloud and the author answers it.

## How the strips run (replaces the first paragraph)

Teacher-held, printed three to a sheet and cut apart, each headed "I wonder": (1) "I wonder what the source says about this." (2) "I wonder how you will end." (3) "I wonder why you think so." On day 138 the teacher picks one for each child's draft in about 20 seconds, adds a word or two in pencil if it helps, and clips it to the draft; a peer may read it aloud for a child who cannot. A strip asks a question; it never corrects. On day 140 the teacher clips one to each day 139 paragraph for the part still missing. The child answers by adding one new detail, never by copying the draft again.
