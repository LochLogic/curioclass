# Four-part strips

Four strips for a model opinion paragraph: Topic, Opinion, Reason, Closing. Each carries one sentence you read aloud. Children set the strips in order and say what each does. The model sentences are never a held exit case.

## The strips, as written

**Model (day 136, the marked model, from The City Nest):**

- Topic: Hawks have a nest on a tall city building.
- Opinion: I think the nest should come down.
- Reason: I think so because bits of the nest fall onto the sidewalk below, and people walk there.
- Closing: So the city nest should come down, to keep the sidewalk safe.

**Ordering set (day 136, cut apart and shuffled, one set a pair; invented for practice, the plant corner):**

- Topic: Our class has a plant by the window.
- Opinion: I think we should water it on Monday.
- Reason: I think so because the soil is dry after the weekend.
- Closing: So we should water the plant on Monday.

**Day 140 sets (invented for practice; four cards laid in order, a wrong page beside a right one):**

- Wrong page: Help birds. I like cake.
- Right page: Hawks have a nest on a tall building. / I think the nest should stay. / I think so because the young need it as they grow. / So give the nest space.
- Retry set, the fish tank: Our class has a fish tank. / I think the tank should stay on the low shelf. / I think so because the low shelf is steady. / So the tank should stay on the low shelf. For the reason part, two loose strips: "The low shelf is steady." "The tank is blue."

None of these is an exit or check case. The day 139 mobile and the exit cards stay out of every strip.
