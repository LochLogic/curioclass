# Section 4.4 opener

## What the section does

Days 136 to 140 move an opinion paragraph from model to joint draft to independent writing. Day 139 is the second check and day 140 shares opinions with care and responds to the evidence. Day 139 holds the fourth cycle sample.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** Gives a preference without an opinion paragraph. Say the opinion in a full sentence before any reason.
- **C:** Calls an unrelated fact a supporting reason. Ask whether the fact backs the point; sort it again.
- **N:** Recopies without responding to feedback. Read the feedback strip first, then change one thing.
- **AA:** Offers an unrelated reading response. Ask what the story says, then what the child thinks.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
