# Day 140: Share opinions kindly and fix my paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the day 139 paragraphs by the part each still lacks: the topic, the position, the reason or the closing. Name the groups of five, today's heard six and the catch-up group. Name the children who answered a question by copying the draft again on day 138 (error N, s04-X138a). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "This page is made up for practice: Close the gate. I like gates." **Ask.** "What does this page still need?" Pairs decide in 60 seconds. Hear three pairs, 20 seconds each. Expect: a reason, with because. A child who says only "a picture" hears: "Which part is missing, the topic, the opinion, the reason or the closing?" Then say: "Today we share our opinions kindly and fix our paragraphs."

#### 15 min. Reread

Reread a made-up page, 3 minutes: "Hawks have a nest on a tall building. I think the nest should stay. I think so because the young need it as they grow. So give the nest space." Then reread pages 2 and 4 of A City Nest: Two Views, pointing to each picture, 4 minutes. Each child points to the reason each side gives, one reason on each page, 4 minutes. Review: an opinion paragraph has four parts (day 136). Touch the four parts of the made-up page as the class names them, 2 minutes: topic, opinion, reason, closing. Say what the made-up page shows that "Close the gate" lacked, 2 minutes.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the first two asks and the group turns. Pass is allowed. Use the turn card.

1. **Ask.** "What is the writer's opinion on the made-up nest page?" Expect: the nest should stay. Heard: two.
2. **Ask.** "Where should the reason connect?" Expect: it comes after the opinion and goes with the word because. Heard: two.
3. Review: share in groups of five (unit 3, day 106). Six groups of five share at once, 9 minutes. Each child reads or says one sentence from their own paragraph and points to the line it came from, 20 seconds. The listeners say it back, 20 seconds (review: listeners say it back, grade 1 days 27 and 134, and kindergarten day 141). Then one listener asks "I wonder ..." in a kind voice, 20 seconds, and the writer answers. **Ask.** "How can your reply use the page and be kind?" Expect: "I wonder what the page says" or "I wonder why you think so." A reply such as "that is bad" is set aside: ask for a question instead. You stand with six children in turn, one in each group, the heard six, 60 seconds each, and hear the rest from the edge.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Review: Return and repair (unit 1, day 20). Hand each child back day 139's assessment, their own paragraph. Three parts, 20 minutes.

1. Answer a question, 5 minutes. Say: "This page is made up for practice. Our class has a bean pot. A writer wrote: Water the beans on Monday. A peer asks: I wonder why you think so." **Ask.** "Say a new line that answers the peer." Pairs say one in 90 seconds. Hear three pairs, 20 seconds each. Expect: a new reason, such as "I think so because the soil is dry." Copying "Water the beans on Monday" again is error N: ask "What does the page say that is new?" This repair is for day 138's exit (s04-X138a).
2. Rebuild, 10 minutes. Groups of five go by the part their paragraphs still lack, one group per part, with a wrong page beside a right page: "Help birds. I like cake." beside the four cards of the nest page, laid in order. Each group lays the four loose cards in order, says the name of each, and says what the wrong page lacks, 6 minutes. Then the fish tank retry, 4 minutes: "Our class has a fish tank. I think the tank should stay on the low shelf. I think so because ..." The group puts one of two loose strips in the reason place and tells why it fits: "The low shelf is steady." or "The tank is blue." This repairs the paragraph on day 139 (s04-A13, error B). Walk between the groups and mark each child: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
3. Fix your own, 5 minutes. Each child adds the missing part to their own paragraph, by printing, drawing or saying it to a scribe. Hear three children read the new part, 40 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which revised page shows a reason from the page?" ([[s04-X140a]]) While the rest finish, the heard six come to your table, 35 seconds each, with the card in view. **Ask.** "What would you write to support the opinion?" ([[s04-X140b]]) **Ask.** "Say a question you would ask this writer. Start with I wonder." ([[s04-X140c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A liking in place of a reason is error B. An unkind remark in place of a question is error x.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cut and cute: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /m/ /oo/ /n/. Say the first, middle and last sound of seed. Segment green.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 23, lesson 116. New today: oo says its sound in moon, ee says the long e in tree, and a y at the end of a longer word says long e in happy. Review: ea is another spelling of the long e sound (day 131). Map each, 2 minutes, and chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it: see, seed, feed, feet, sleep; then zoo, moon, food, cool. Then happy, bunny, windy: clap the two parts, find the y at the end and say each word, 3 minutes. Cycle 23 has no reader yet, so the rotation rereads Pat's Birthday.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print two words from the chain, 4 minutes. Dictate "The bunny is by the tree." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map oo, ee and final y, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Pat's Birthday pages 1 to 10, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. Pat's Birthday has no oo, ee or final y words, so this reread practices fluency only; the new patterns are practiced on today's word cards and chains, and the cycle 23 reader, A Little Seed, starts on day 141. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record, today's exit marks and the day 139 results with each repair filed.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the pages aloud and write the four boxes on a sheet. One child: say the paragraph again with the missing part and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each part on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child reads or says one sentence from the day 139 paragraph. **Say.** "Say it back. Then ask: I wonder ..." Expect: a kind question, not a remark.
- **10 min. Fix.** Find the missing part: topic, position, reason or closing. **Say.** "Say a line that adds it." Expect: a line that goes with the rest. Write it for your child to copy.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "see" and "happy."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X140a]] [[s04-X140b]] [[s04-X140c]] Read a short made-up page and one line from a class page your child has not seen. Your child points to the revised page with a reason, says a reason from the class page, and asks a kind question starting with I wonder.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the group share, one repair and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes asks a second question that sends the writer back to the page.

### Words and supports

Words: opinion, reason, closing, share, respect, I wonder, tree, moon, happy. Frame: "I wonder ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the page with a reason, say a reason from a class page and ask a kind question. Day 141 starts a new task, the window bird portrait, with information on bird-safe windows. A child who gave a liking in place of a reason (error B, s04-X140b) hears the opinion and its reason said as a pair of sentences first.
