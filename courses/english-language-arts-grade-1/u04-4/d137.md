# Day 137: Draft a nest opinion together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the joint draft chart: four boxes marked Topic, Opinion, Reason and Closing. Open A City Nest: Two Views to page 2. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who gave the topic words where the reason was asked on day 136 (error C, s04-X136b). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This paragraph is made up for practice. Our class has a sand table. I think we should keep the lid on. Here is one reason I could give: the sand stays clean." **Ask.** "Say the reason with I think so because. Do not say what the paragraph is about." Pairs say it in 60 seconds. Hear three pairs, 20 seconds each. Expect: "I think so because the sand stays clean." This repairs error C (s04-X136b): a child who says "our class has a sand table" hears "That tells what it is about. What tells why?" Then say: "Today we write a paragraph together."

#### 15 min. Reread the debate

Review: an opinion paragraph has four parts, the topic, the opinion, the reason and the closing (day 136). Reread pages 2 and 4 of A City Nest: Two Views, pointing to each picture, 4 minutes. Lay the six point-and-reason strips from day 132 on the chart tray. Read each strip aloud and children echo it, 3 minutes. Then children vote by moving to the left side of the room for stay or the right side for come down, 2 minutes; the side with more children is the class position, and the other side keeps its reasons for day 138. Back at their places, each child points to one strip that matches the class position, 4 minutes: pairs agree on a strip and three pairs show theirs, 30 seconds each. Say what the source says on each: "Page 2 says the young need the nest as they grow."

Review: the because frame (day 134). Say it once for the class: "I think the nest should ___ because ___."

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the three asks. Pass is allowed. Use the turn card.

1. **Ask.** "What position will our class explain?" Expect: the side the vote chose, in the words stay or come down. Heard: two.
2. **Ask.** "Which word joins the opinion to the reason: and, so or because?" Expect: because. Say: "Because tells why." Heard: two.
3. **Ask.** "Look at page 2 or page 4. Which line of the page tells why for our side?" Expect: a line from the page, such as "the young need it as they grow" for stay, or "bits of the nest fall to the sidewalk" for come down. A child who names a liking, "I like hawks," hears: "Point to the line on the page." Heard: two.

#### 20 min. Shared writing

Review: shared writing, where pairs give lines aloud and the teacher writes them on a chart (unit 2, days 66 and 67, and unit 3, day 93). Work down the joint draft chart one box at a time, 4 minutes a box, with 4 minutes left over for the whole reread. For each box pairs say a line in 60 seconds, three pairs are heard, 20 seconds each, and the class picks one by hands. You write it. After each line ask the class one question, the source check: **Ask.** "Does the source say this?" Thumbs up for yes. A line the page does not say, such as "hawks are cute," is set in the margin and one the page does say goes in the box.

1. Topic. Pairs say what the paragraph is about. Expect: "A hawk has a nest on a tall building in the city."
2. Opinion. Say: "I think the nest should ___." The class position fills it.
3. Reason. Each pair finds the line on the page that gives its reason, points to it, and says it with "I think so because." Review: because (day 134).
4. Closing. Say: "The closing goes back to the topic. Which words from the topic box can we use?" Children point at the topic line, and the class says the closing, such as "So the city nest should stay."

Children copy the topic line and the closing line onto the paragraph frame, 4 minutes. At the end the class rereads the whole draft with you pointing, 2 minutes. Leave the chart up for day 138.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which source picture gives a reason that matches the draft?" ([[s04-X137a]]) While the rest finish, the heard six come to your table, 35 seconds each, with the card in view. **Ask.** "Say a reason from the source that could finish the draft." ([[s04-X137b]]) **Ask.** "Say a closing for the draft that goes back to the topic." ([[s04-X137c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A liking in place of a reason is error B, for day 138's opening.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cod and code: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /g/ /r/ /ow/. Say the first sound of snow. Segment slow.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 22, lesson 113. Review: ay and ow say long vowel sounds (day 135), and ai is another spelling of the long a sound (day 130). Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it: play, pray, tray, stay, stray; then grow, glow, blow, slow, snow. **Ask.** "Which two letters say the long a at the end of stay?" Expect: ay. Then map two irregular words, 2 minutes each: both and always. Page 10 of Pat's Birthday waits for day 138.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the closing line from the joint draft as a copy, one letter form checked, 5 minutes. Dictate "Stay away from the nest all day." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map ay and ow, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Pat's Birthday pages 1 to 9, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 138's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the four boxes on the board and copy the lines on scrap paper. One child: write a joint draft with the parent as scribe and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: write the four labels on scraps of paper.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Choose.** Say: "Should a nest stay or come down? Say what you think." **Say.** "I think the nest should ___ because ___." Expect: a reason from the debate, not a liking.
- **10 min. Write together.** Say each part aloud, then write it for your child to copy: what it is about, what you think, why, the ending.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "stay" and "snow."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X137a]] [[s04-X137b]] [[s04-X137c]] Read a short made-up draft and one source line your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture that gives a reason, then says a reason and a closing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the vote, the reason box and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes draws the reason in the frame's reason box.

### Words and supports

Words: opinion, position, topic, reason, because, closing, stay, come down. Frame: "I think the nest should ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick a reason that matches the draft, say a reason from the source and say a closing that returns to the topic. A child who gave a liking in place of a reason (error B, s04-X137b) goes to day 138's opening repair. Day 138 has each child draft and revise an individual nest opinion.
