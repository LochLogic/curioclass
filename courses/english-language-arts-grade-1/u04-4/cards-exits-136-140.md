# Exit cards, days 136 to 140

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 136 card (s04-X136a, W.1.1)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Quiet voices matter. I think children should use quiet voices because they help everyone listen. Let us use quiet voices." Show three pictures: children using quiet voices, a child shouting, a child alone at a plain desk. Say: "Which picture shows what this writer wants?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Children using quiet voices.

## Day 136 card (s04-X136b, W.1.1)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "Quiet voices matter. I think children should use quiet voices because they help everyone listen. Let us use quiet voices." Say: "Which words give the reason?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** "they help everyone listen"

## Day 136 card (s04-X136c, W.1.1)

**Right answer:** It goes back to the topic and says what to do: use quiet voices.

## Day 137 card (s04-X137a, W.1.1)

**Right answer:** A child tripping on a bag on the stairs.
