# Day 139: Check my opinion paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six and the catch-up group from day 138's exit marks. Keep the sample words in your folder. Put away the draft chart and the strips. No help, no hints.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "This is made up for practice." Hand out the response sheets. Rules: work alone, no frame, pass if you wish.

#### 20 min. Check sitting

Review: the check-sitting routine (day 129). Read twice, no help: "Our class has a paper-star mobile over the door. View A: Keep it over the door, because everyone sees it as they come in. View B: Move it to the reading corner, because the corner is quiet and nobody will bump it." Say: "Where should the mobile hang? Name the topic, say what you think, give a reason from the two views and finish." ([[s04-A13]]) Either position counts when its reason matches. A scribe may write for a child whose printing is not yet fluent. After class mark the topic, the position, the reason and the closing: shown, shown with help (the scribe), not yet seen.

#### 12 min. Reread

Unscored. Partners reread the day 137 class draft for expression, then swap. Hear six pairs.

#### 18 min. Draw and tell

Unscored. Each child draws the city nest and tells a partner one thing the writers said. Write nothing.

#### 5 min. Close

Collect the sheets. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say tap and tape: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /f/ /l/ /ow/. Say the first, middle and last sound of window. Segment pillow.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 22, lesson 115. No new teaching; the sample runs in the rotation. Warm-up chain, 20 seconds a word: stay, grow, slow.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Copy "The nest is by the window." Dictate "The tray is on the shelf." whole, then a word at a time.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The cycle 22 sample runs here. The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: map, blend, read, spell, then the sample. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Pat's Birthday, each child heard 20 seconds in the book and 20 on the sample. Show the held word card: "Read this word." ([[s04-A7]]) Then the card goes away: "Write the word I say." ([[s04-A8]]) Never say the words aloud to the class. The others reread.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the marks to the record sheet. Regroup for cycle 23. List each miss for day 140.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the question on a plain sheet. One child: say the paragraph aloud for a parent to write.

### At home

**Swap.** No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The sample and the rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment: one paragraph.** Read twice, with no help: "Our family has a calendar by the fridge. View A: Keep it by the fridge, because everyone passes it. View B: Move it to the hall, because the fridge is crowded."
- **15 min. Write.** **Say.** "Where should the calendar hang? Say what it is about, what you think, why, then the ending." ((s04-A13)) Expect: either place, with a reason that matches, and an ending that goes back to the calendar. Write what your child says if printing is hard.
- **15 min. Print.** Your child prints "The tray is on the shelf." from your reading.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sitting and the sample. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: position, reason, closing, mobile. A scribe, a home language and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Each miss waits for day 140, which hands back each paragraph (s04-A13) and repairs it by rebuilding the four parts from four cards. The sample marks give the cycle 23 groups.
