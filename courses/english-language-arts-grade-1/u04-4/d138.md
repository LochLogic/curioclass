# Day 138: Draft and revise my own nest opinion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the feedback strips, three to a sheet, and cut them apart. Hand out the paragraph frames with only the four labels showing. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who gave a liking in place of a reason on day 137 (error B, s04-X137b). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say: "This is made up for practice. Our class has a rain barrel. A draft reads: Keep the lid on. The source reads: A barrel with a lid keeps leaves out of the water." Lay two loose strips under the card: "I like barrels." and "A barrel with a lid keeps leaves out of the water." **Ask.** "Which strip goes in the reason box?" Pairs decide in 60 seconds. Hear three pairs, 20 seconds each. Expect: the strip that matches the source. A child who picks the liking hears: "Does the source say that?" This repairs error B (s04-X137b). Then say: "Today you write your own."

#### 15 min. Reread the reasons

Review: an opinion paragraph has four parts (day 136), and the class wrote one together on the joint draft chart (day 137). Reread the joint draft chart, pointing to each part, 2 minutes. Reread pages 2 to 5 of A City Nest: Two Views, pointing to each picture, 5 minutes. Spread the point-and-reason strips on the tray. Each child chooses a position, stay or come down, and a reason strip that matches it. The position does not have to be the class position. Children tell a partner their position and reason in 90 seconds, then place a counter on a chart under stay or come down, 3 minutes. Review: view and position (days 132 and 136). Say the count aloud, 1 minute. Pairs check each other's reason against the page, pointing to the line, 3 minutes.

#### 10 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time, then three children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the two asks. Pass is allowed. Use the turn card.

1. **Ask.** "What is your position?" Expect: "I think the nest should stay" or "come down." Heard: three.
2. **Ask.** "Which line on the page gives your reason?" Expect: a pointed line with its words. Heard: three.

#### 25 min. Draft and revise

Draft, 15 minutes. Each child fills the four labeled boxes, in their own words from the page: the topic, the opinion, one reason and the closing. Review: the because frame (day 134). A child who cannot print yet says each part to you or a partner, who writes it; the child copies one line. While they draft, walk the room and clip one "I wonder" strip to each draft, choosing it from what the draft lacks, in about 20 seconds a draft: what the source says about this, how you will end, or why you think so. You may add a word or two in pencil. A strip asks; it never corrects. Thirty drafts at 20 seconds each fit inside the 15 minutes.

Revise, 10 minutes. Review: a peer asks a question and the writer answers with a new detail (unit 2, days 65 and 67, and unit 3, day 117). Pairs trade drafts. Each child reads the strip on their partner's draft aloud, or a partner reads it for them, 2 minutes. Then each writer answers it with one new detail from the page, said aloud first, then added to the draft by printing or by a scribe, 6 minutes. A writer who only copies the draft again has not answered the question; ask "What does the page say that is new?" Hear three drafts read, 40 seconds each, in the last 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which revised page answers the peer?" ([[s04-X138a]]) While the rest finish, the heard six come to your table, 35 seconds each, with the card in view. **Ask.** "What ending would keep this topic?" ([[s04-X138b]]) **Ask.** "Which source picture shows the writer's reason?" ([[s04-X138c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the page copied again is error N, for day 140's repair. A child who points to the topic picture for the reason is error C.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say pin and pine: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /s/ /l/ /ow/. Say the first, middle and last sound of tray. Segment grow.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 22, lesson 114. Review: ay and ow say long vowel sounds (day 135), and both and always were mapped on day 137. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it: say, stay, tray; then low, slow, show. Read one sentence aloud and check it makes sense: "We go slow on the tray." **Ask.** "Does that make sense?" Expect: no. Fix it with the class: "We go slow." Then map one irregular word, 3 minutes: laugh. The first part says /l/ and /a/, and gh says /f/. Now page 10 of Pat's Birthday can be read, because both, always and laugh are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print two sentences from the draft, the opinion and the closing, as a copy, 5 minutes. Dictate "The nest is by the window." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map ay and ow, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Pat's Birthday pages 1 to 10, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record, today's exit marks and the feedback strips each draft got. Keep the drafts for day 139.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four boxes on a sheet and say the questions aloud. One child: draft aloud with a parent as scribe, answer one "I wonder" question with a new detail, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say a question aloud.

**Skip.** The sound chain.

- **10 min. Draft.** **Say.** "Should a nest stay or come down? Say your position." Expect: "I think the nest should stay (or come down) because ..." Write it for your child as four lines: what it is about, what you think, why, the ending.
- **10 min. Ask.** **Say.** "I wonder what the page says about your reason." Expect: one new detail from the page, not the same sentence again. Your child answers with it.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "say" and "show."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X138a]] [[s04-X138b]] [[s04-X138c]] Read a short made-up page and one comment your child has not seen. Your child points to the page that answers the comment, then says an ending and points to the picture that shows the reason.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft and one answer to a strip. Longer: a child who finishes answers a second strip.

### Words and supports

Words: position, reason, closing, revise, I wonder, slow, show. Frame: "I think the nest should ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the revised page, say an ending and match a source picture to a reason. A child who copied the page again instead of answering (error N, s04-X138a) goes to day 140's repair. Day 139 is the check: each child writes a paragraph alone.
