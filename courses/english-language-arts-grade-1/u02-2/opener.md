# Section 2.2 opener: feeling words, sense words and the second tale

## What the section does

Days 39 to 42 teach the class to find words that say how someone feels and words that tell what can be seen, heard or touched. Children use them on the Efik and Ibibio tale, then hear a second tale from China and retell it with a story map. Day 42 repeats the lesson work on a short tale so that the move is shown twice.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A feeling word says how someone feels.** A sense word says what is seen, heard, felt, smelled or tasted. A word that only names a thing is neither.
- **A picture shows a thing; the word does the work.** Point to the word first.
- **A traditional tale is told, not invented here.** Name its people when you read it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** an object name is called a feeling word. Reread and mark the word that says how someone feels.
- **A, B, D, E:** see section 2.1.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
