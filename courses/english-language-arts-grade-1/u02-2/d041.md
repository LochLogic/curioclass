# Day 41: A tale from China, who and where

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sky-and-earth picture, the character cards and the story map, the exit card with its pictures and a copy of Sam Wants a Pet for each child. Draw three picture cards for the repair: Ant climbing a hill, Ant waving a feeler, Ant running down. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read the second tale whole

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a traditional Chinese tale. People in China have told it for a very long time, and they still tell it." Content note: the tale holds a family kept apart by a river of stars, and a happy meeting; no death, no injury, no violence. No costumes, accents or acting out.

Words first (3 minutes), each with its picture: weaver, a person who weaves threads together; cowherd, a person who looks after cows; ox; bridge; magpie, a black-and-white bird. Say the two names once, as best you can, and the class echoes: Zhinu (roughly JUR-nyoo) and Niulang (roughly NYOH-lahng). If a family in your class speaks Chinese, ask them to say the names.

Read *The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd* (g1-u2-04, about 460 words), showing each picture, with a pause after pages 3, 5 and 7 (8 minutes in all). At each pause say: "What do you see?" and take two answers. Last, say gently: "They are sad to be apart, and glad to meet" (2 minutes). Do not ask a child about their own family.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair first (4 minutes, from day 40's exit, error A). Read a made-up line: "Ant climbed a hill. Ant waved a feeler. Ant ran down." Lay the three pictures in the wrong order. A pair says first, next, last and fixes it, with a finger on the time words. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the questions the whole ten minutes. A few children may also have named an object (error D) or a claim the card never made (error E) on yesterday's exit: work with them at the rug edge now.

Reread pages 1 to 3 (2 minutes). **Ask.** "Who are the two characters?" Expect: Zhinu and Niulang. **Ask.** "Where do they live?" Expect: Zhinu in the sky, Niulang on the earth. Partners point to the picture (4 minutes).

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. **Ask.** "What does each character care about?" Starter: "Zhinu cares about ___. Niulang cares about ___." Expect: her clouds and her weaving; his ox and his kind work. Hear four pairs. Say gently that the two are sad to be apart and glad to meet; nobody is asked about their own family. Look-fors: each child waits; each names one thing a character cares about.

#### 20 min. Opening story-map

Review: you used a story map for the Efik and Ibibio tale on days 36 to 38. This map is a fresh one for the new tale. Model (10 minutes). Hang the sky-and-earth picture. Place the character cards: Zhinu in the sky, Niulang and his ox on the earth. Write on the map where the tale comes from: a traditional Chinese tale. Then three first events, one box each: Zhinu weaves the clouds; Niulang takes care of his ox; they meet on the earth. **Ask.** "Which event is first?" Expect: Zhinu weaves.

Each child draws one of the three events on a card (6 minutes): the room splits into three, about ten children to an event. Children add a word label with the map or ask you to write it. Last, children hang the cards in order and say first, next, last with the class (4 minutes). Hear the six as they draw, 40 seconds each, and mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Look-fors: names the characters; names where each lives.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice. This is an invented story, not a traditional tale." Read aloud: "In a park in China, Lin found a torn paper boat. Lin carried it to Grandfather. Together they patched it." Show two pictures: Lin finding the boat, and Lin and Grandfather patching it. Say: "Point to the picture that starts this story." Every child points ([[s02-X41a]]). Then read the card again and show two pictures: a park with a path and a bench, and a room with a table. Say: "Point to where this story starts." Every child points ([[s02-X41b]]). A good answer is Lin finding the boat, then the park. The patching picture is error A. The room picture is error E. Mark the rotating six on each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to day 42.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: segment and blend short e words and count the sounds with a finger for each: ten (three), step (four), tent (four), spend (five). Say each sound, then the word. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Decode (3 minutes): get, set, wet, jet, bed. Spell on sound boxes (3 minutes): wet and met. Mapped words (2 minutes): said and see. Review: you mapped these on day 39. The class reads each card and names the part to remember. Then read pages 1 to 3 of Sam Wants a Pet with a finger under each word (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: each child revises W to Z in the letter-form record with a finger on the page (3 minutes), then makes any form still missing. A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong. Dictation (5 minutes): say "Dad can get the net." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Then each child points to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads Sam Wants a Pet aloud, one page each, then spells two words, met and set, on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Sam Wants a Pet with a partner; chain bed, bet, get, got and not with letter cards; spell wet and jet on sound boxes; draw the cowherd and his ox and label it. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the map marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name tomorrow's six.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the sky above and the earth below on a sheet, and the two characters on scrap paper. One child: hear the tale in two sittings, draw one event, say who and where, answer both exit questions by pointing, read pages 1 to 3 of Sam Wants a Pet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the sky and the earth on one page. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The talk turns and the rotation.

- **10 min. Listen.** Read the tale aloud, with its pictures, in two sittings. Say it is a traditional Chinese tale.
  **Say.** "Who are the two, and where do they live?" Expect: Zhinu in the sky, Niulang on the earth.
- **10 min. Map.** Your child draws one first event in the map and adds a word.
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child revises W to Z, then prints "Dad can get the net."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X41a]] [[s02-X41b]] You read a new invented story: in a kitchen in Mexico, Tomas dropped an egg, called Mother, and together they swept it up. Show Tomas dropping the egg and Tomas and Mother sweeping; your child points to the picture that starts the story, alone. Then show a kitchen and a beach, and your child points to where it starts.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the map, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk. Longer: a child who finishes draws the sky and earth places with one more detail for each.

### Words and supports

Words: weaver, cowherd, ox, bridge, magpie, sky, earth, first events. Frames: "___ lives in the ___." "First ___, next ___, last ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 rereads the second tale and finds a lesson in its ending. If many children laid the picture of patching first (error A) on today's exit, the opening of day 42's repair part goes to a four-minute repair: reread the time words and place the picture cards. If a few pointed to the room (error E), work with them at the rug edge.
