# Day 39 feeling and sense chart

A wall chart with three boxes: a face for a word that says how someone feels, a hand or an ear for a word that tells what can be touched or heard, and a small house for a word that only names a thing. Word cards read aloud and sorted from the tale: "broad smile", "knee-deep", "house", "huge", "friend", "safe". Children say why each card goes in its box.
