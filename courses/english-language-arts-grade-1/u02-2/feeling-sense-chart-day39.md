# Day 39 feeling and sense chart

A wall chart with three boxes: a face for a word that says how someone feels, a sense box with an ear, a hand and an eye drawn on it for a word that tells what can be heard, touched or seen, and a small house for a word that only names a thing. Six word cards read aloud and sorted from the tale: "broad smile" (face), "knee-deep" (sense, hand), "house" (house), "huge" (sense, eye), "splash" (sense, ear) and "fish" (house). Children say why each card goes in its box.
