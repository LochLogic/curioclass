# Day 41 story-map cards for the Chinese tale

A two-part picture, sky above and earth below, with character cards to place: the weaver girl Zhinu in the sky, the cowherd Niulang with his ox on the earth. A fresh story-map folded in three parts for characters, place and events. The teacher says aloud that this is a story people in China still tell, and says the two names once for the children to echo.
