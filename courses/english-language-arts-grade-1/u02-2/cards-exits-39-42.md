# Exit cards, days 39 to 42

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 39 card (s02-X39, RL.1.4)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Mouse felt excited. Rough bark scratched her paws. Leaves rustled overhead." Show three pictures: an excited face, a hand on rough bark, and leaves on a branch. Say: "Point to the picture that shows how Mouse felt inside." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The excited face. The hand on bark shows a touch word; the leaves show a thing.

## Day 40 card (s02-X40a, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Hedgehog lost a bead. Mole found it by the fence. Hedgehog thanked Mole, and they put it back." Show two strips of three pictures: lost, found, put back; and put back, found, lost. Say: "Point to the strip that tells the whole story." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The strip lost, found, put back.

## Day 40 card (s02-X40b, RL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Use the same card, read aloud again. Read three reason cards aloud: "Hedgehog and Mole do things, and it ends." "It names a bead." "It tells how big a mole is." Say: "Point to the card that says why this is a story." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The card "Hedgehog and Mole do things, and it ends."

## Day 41 card (s02-X41a, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. This is an invented story, not a traditional tale. Read aloud: "In a park in China, Lin found a torn paper boat. Lin carried it to Grandfather. Together they patched it." Show two pictures: Lin finding the boat, and Lin and Grandfather patching it. Say: "Point to the picture that starts this story." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of Lin finding the boat.

## Day 41 card (s02-X41b, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Use the same card, read aloud again. Show two pictures: a park with a path and a bench, and a room with a table. Say: "Point to where this story starts." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The park picture.

## Day 42 card (s02-X42, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Crab tried to lift a stone. Turtle pushed beside her. Together they rolled it to the shore. Crab felt grateful as the stone splashed into the water." Show three pictures: Crab and Turtle pushing the stone together, the stone alone, and Crab asleep. Say: "Point to the picture that shows what Crab could learn." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of Crab and Turtle pushing together. Any lesson the events support is accepted.
