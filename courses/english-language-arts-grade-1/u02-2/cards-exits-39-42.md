# Exit pictures, days 39 to 42

Each exit reads a named passage of the unit's texts aloud; this page holds only the pictures and cards to show. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 39 exit pictures (s02-X39v2, RL.1.4)

Passage: Moon's Walk, page 4, first two lines, read aloud once; the poem is first read whole on day 64; day 39 works the tale's pages 3 and 4. Read two lines from the poem Moon's Walk, page 4: "At last, the morning comes. I fade to pale, and smile." Say: "Find the feeling word in these lines and tell what it helps you imagine about the moon." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Smile: it helps me imagine the moon is happy, glad that the morning comes. Pale tells what the moon looks like; morning is a thing.

## Day 40 exit pictures (s02-X40av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: Sun's Day, pages 1 to 4, read aloud once; the poem is worked from day 63; day 40 works the Efik and Ibibio tale. Read the poem Sun's Day aloud once. Say: "Retell the whole poem: tell what the sun does first, next, then and at last." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Climbs above the hill; high and bright; slides down the sky; sinks out of sight.

## Day 40 exit pictures (s02-X40bv2, RL.1.5)

Passage: Sun's Day, pages 1 to 4, read aloud once; day 40 works the Efik and Ibibio tale. Read Sun's Day again. Say: "Tell why Sun's Day tells a story." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The sun is a character that talks and does things in order, and the poem has an ending.

## Day 41 exit pictures (s02-X41av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 5 (Niulang runs; the river of stars); day 41 works pages 1 to 3. Reread page 5 of the tale aloud. Show two pictures: Niulang running up the hill, and the river of stars between the two. Say: "Point to the picture that comes first on this page." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Niulang running up the hill.

## Day 41 exit pictures (s02-X41bv2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 5; day 41 works pages 1 to 3. Read page 5 again. Show two pictures: a hill under the sky, and a room with a table. Say: "Point to where Niulang runs." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The hill under the sky.

## Day 42 exit pictures (s02-X42v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 4 (the gray clouds and the call home); day 42 works the magpie bridge on page 7. Reread page 4 of the tale aloud: "Nobody was weaving them. Zhinu had forgotten the loom." Say: "Tell the lesson Zhinu could learn, and what happens on the page that shows it." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Any lesson the events support, with the event that shows it, such as: keep doing the work others need, because she forgot the loom and the clouds went gray.
