# Exit pictures, days 39 to 42

Each exit reads a named passage of the unit's texts aloud; this page holds only the pictures and cards to show. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 39 exit pictures (s02-X39v2, RL.1.4)

Passage: Moon's Walk, page 4, first two lines, read aloud once; the poem is first read whole on day 64; day 39 works the tale's pages 3 and 4. Read two lines from the poem Moon's Walk, page 4: "At last, the morning comes. I fade to pale, and smile." Show three pictures: a smiling face, a pale moon, and a morning sky. Say: "Point to the picture that shows how the moon feels inside." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The smiling face (smile). Pale tells what the moon looks like; the morning sky is a thing.

## Day 40 exit pictures (s02-X40av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: Sun's Day, pages 1 to 4, read aloud once; the poem is worked from day 63; day 40 works the Efik and Ibibio tale. Read the poem Sun's Day aloud once. Show two strips of three pictures: the sun climbing over the hill, the sun high over children, the sun sinking out of sight; and the same three in reverse. Say: "Point to the strip that tells the whole poem." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The strip climbing, high, sinking.

## Day 40 exit pictures (s02-X40bv2, RL.1.5)

Passage: Sun's Day, pages 1 to 4, read aloud once; day 40 works the Efik and Ibibio tale. Read Sun's Day again. Read three reason cards aloud: "The sun talks and does things, and it ends." "It names a hill." "It tells how hot the sun is." Say: "Point to the card that says why this tells a story." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The card "The sun talks and does things, and it ends."

## Day 41 exit pictures (s02-X41av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 5 (Niulang runs; the river of stars); day 41 works pages 1 to 3. Reread page 5 of the tale aloud. Show two pictures: Niulang running up the hill, and the river of stars between the two. Say: "Point to the picture that comes first on this page." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Niulang running up the hill.

## Day 41 exit pictures (s02-X41bv2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 5; day 41 works pages 1 to 3. Read page 5 again. Show two pictures: a hill under the sky, and a room with a table. Say: "Point to where Niulang runs." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The hill under the sky.

## Day 42 exit pictures (s02-X42v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 4 (the gray clouds and the call home); day 42 works the magpie bridge on page 7. Reread page 4 of the tale aloud: "Nobody was weaving them. Zhinu had forgotten the loom." Show three pictures: Zhinu weaving the clouds again, the gray clouds alone, and Zhinu asleep. Say: "Point to the picture that shows what Zhinu could learn." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Zhinu weaving the clouds again. Any lesson the events support is accepted, such as keep doing the work others need.
