# Day 42: A lesson from the ending

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the exit pictures and a copy of Sam Wants a Pet for each child. Draw three picture cards for the repair: Snail crawling onto a rock, Snail yawning, Snail sliding into the water. Make the chain cards: event, result and a blank. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread pages 4 to 9

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a traditional Chinese tale." Review: you heard pages 1 to 3 yesterday and drew where Zhinu and Niulang live. Read pages 4 to 9, showing each picture: the call home, the river of stars, the magpie bridge, the yearly meeting (6 minutes). Content note: the two are kept apart, then meet; no one is hurt. Pause after pages 5 and 7 and ask what the class sees.

**Ask.** "How do Zhinu and Niulang feel when the river comes between them?" Expect: sad. Hang the feeling and sense chart. Review: you sorted words on day 39. **Ask.** "Which words help you picture the river?" Expect: wide, shining. Put them in the sense box, with the eye (9 minutes).

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair first (4 minutes, from day 41's exit, error A). Read a made-up line: "Snail crawled onto a rock. Snail yawned. Snail slid into the water." Lay the three pictures in the wrong order. A pair says first, next, last and fixes it, with a finger on the time words. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the questions the whole ten minutes. A few children may have pointed to a place the page did not give (error E): work with them at the rug edge, and point to the words that tell.

**Ask.** "What help comes near the ending?" Expect: the magpies make a bridge. **Ask.** "How does it change the two?" Expect: they meet and hold hands. Partners point to the picture on page 7 (6 minutes).

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. **Ask.** "Which event shows a lesson?" Starter: "The event is ___." Expect: the magpies make a bridge. Then teach joining with but. Say: "They are apart, but the magpies help." The word but joins two parts that pull against each other. Review: you joined parts with and yesterday. Each child says a joined sentence to a partner, such as "The river is wide, but the bridge is strong." Hear four pairs.

#### 20 min. Lesson from the ending

Review: you made an arrow chain on day 37. Model (8 minutes). Lay the event card: "The magpies made a bridge." Lay the arrow, then the result card: "Zhinu and Niulang met." Lay a second arrow and the blank card. **Ask.** "What could this tale teach?" Expect: when you care about someone, you keep caring, even far apart. Write it on the blank card and read it. Say: "A lesson is something a tale could teach. Some listeners say other lessons, and any lesson the events support is right."

Each child draws the event that shows a lesson and adds a feeling or sense word as a label (6 minutes), with the map or by asking you to write it, such as sad, wide or shining. Then each says "The lesson is ___ because ___" to a partner (2 minutes, one turn each, 60 seconds). Hear the six, 40 seconds each (4 minutes), while the others draw a second event. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Look-fors: names an event from the ending; says a lesson, not only a topic; the label is a feeling or sense word.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 4 of the tale aloud: "Nobody was weaving them. Zhinu had forgotten the loom." Say: "Tell the lesson Zhinu could learn, and what happens on the page that shows it." Every child tells a neighbor. The rotating six then say it, 20 seconds each ([[s02-X42v2]]). Any lesson the events support is accepted, with the event that shows it. A thing in the tale given as the lesson, such as the gray clouds, is error B. A claim the page never makes, such as Zhinu was sleepy, is error E. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to day 43.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: the class says the vowel in each of ten, bed, get and met; a fist for the short e. Blend and segment five sounds: /s/ /p/ /e/ /n/ /t/, "spent"; tap each sound, then say the word. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Decode (3 minutes): bed, net, wet, jet, set. Spell on sound boxes (3 minutes): met and pet. Then read Sam Wants a Pet whole with the class, a finger under each word, one page at a time (4 minutes). Mapped words said and see: the class reads each card twice before the first page.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: collecting finished on day 40. Each child opens the letter-form record, revises any form with a feedback mark, and makes any form still missing (4 minutes). A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong. Dictation (4 minutes): say "Sam can see the red bed." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped words can and see are named aloud. Then each child points to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads Sam Wants a Pet aloud with expression, one page each, then spells two words, bed and get, on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Sam Wants a Pet with a partner; chain pet, pot, not, net and wet with letter cards; spell set and met on sound boxes; draw the bridge event again and say its lesson to a partner. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the lesson marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the new foundations groups for day 43.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three exit pictures on scrap paper. One child: reread pages 4 to 9 in two sittings, make the chain with a grown-up, draw the bridge event with a label, answer the exit by pointing, read Sam Wants a Pet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three boxes on a page for event, result and lesson. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The joined sentences and the rotation.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 6 and 7 of the tale aloud.
  **Say.** "What help comes near the ending, and what changes?" Expect: the magpies make a bridge, and the two meet.
- **10 min. Lesson.** Your child draws the bridge, adds a word such as sad or wide, and says, "The lesson is ___ because ___."
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child spells met and pet, then prints "Sam can see the red bed."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X42v2]] You read page 4 of The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, where the clouds go gray because Zhinu forgot the loom. Your child tells the lesson Zhinu could learn and what happens that shows it, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the chain, the exit and the rotation; cut the joined sentences. Longer: a child who finishes says a second lesson and points to the event that supports it.

### Words and supports

Words: lesson, event, result, bridge, ending, but. Frames: "The lesson is ___ because ___." "___, but ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 reads the night-sky poems and collects feeling and sense words. If many children pointed to the gray clouds alone (error B) or to Zhinu asleep (error E) on today's exit, the opening of day 43's repair part goes to a four-minute repair: connect one action to its result, then say a lesson.
