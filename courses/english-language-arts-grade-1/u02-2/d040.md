# Day 40: Retell the tale and say why it is a story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the two-column sheet, the exit card with its strips and three reason cards, and a copy of Sam Wants a Pet for each child. Set out a story map and a set of tale picture strip cards for each pair. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read the tale whole

Whole class on the rug. Opening repair first (4 minutes, from day 39's exit, error C). Read page 3 again: the Moon met the Sun at the door with a broad smile. Partners point to the face on the words that tell how the Moon feels. Then read page 2: the Sun lived in a house. **Ask.** "Is house a feeling word?" Expect: no, it only names a thing. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and keep the minutes for the reading.

Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Read the tale whole, showing each picture (5 minutes). Say the repeated question with the class: "Is it still safe?" "Yes." (2 minutes). **Ask.** "What stories do people tell about the sky?" Expect: this one, about why the sun and the moon live there (2 minutes). Last, hold up the feeling and sense chart and each child says one word from the tale (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Retell with the map

Review: you used the story map on days 36 to 38. Partners take a story map and a set of tale picture strip cards. One child retells with the map, first, next, last, in about two minutes; then the partner retells (4 minutes). Walk and ask, "Which card is first?" Hear three pairs (4 minutes). Look-fors: three events in their order; the characters named.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. Hang the two-column sheet from day 35. **Ask.** "Why is this a story?" Starter: "It is a story because ___." Expect: characters talk and act; a lesson. Point to the tale column. Then teach joining aloud. Say: "The Sun asked, and the Water answered." The word and joins two parts. Each child says one joined sentence about the tale to a partner, such as "The Sun built a compound, and the Water came." Hear four pairs.

#### 20 min. Complete retelling

Each child folds a page into three and draws the tale's first, next and last events (8 minutes), adds a word label to each with the map or asks you to write it (3 minutes), then retells to a partner (3 minutes, one turn each, 90 seconds). Say: "End with: It is a story because ___." Hear the six, 40 seconds each (4 minutes), while the others add a joined sentence to a panel with a grown-up scribe. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Judge with three look-fors: (1) three events in their order; (2) the characters named; (3) a reason it is a story.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud: "Hedgehog lost a bead. Mole found it by the fence. Hedgehog thanked Mole, and they put it back." Show two strips of three pictures: lost, found, put back; and put back, found, lost. Say: "Point to the strip that tells the whole story." Every child points ([[s02-X40a]]). Then read the card again and read three reason cards: "Hedgehog and Mole do things, and it ends." "It names a bead." "It tells how big a mole is." Say: "Point to the card that says why this is a story." Every child points ([[s02-X40b]]). A good answer is the strip lost, found, put back, then the first reason card. The strip in reverse is error A. A reason that names an object is error D. A claim the card never makes is error E. Mark the rotating six on each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to day 41.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: the class says the first sound, the vowel and the last sound in ten, bed and get; a finger up for each. Blend five sounds: /s/ /p/ /e/ /n/ /d/, "spend"; tap each sound, then say the word. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Decode (3 minutes): bed, red, net, ten, get. Spell on sound boxes (3 minutes): pet and get. Mapped words (2 minutes): said and see. Review: you mapped these on day 39. The class reads each card, then names the part to remember. Then read page 1 of Sam Wants a Pet with the class, a finger under each word (2 minutes). You read the title to the class. Children read the rest in the rotation.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: each child prints W to Z into the letter-form record (4 minutes) and adds the day. That makes all 52 forms. Revise O to V with a finger on the page. A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong. Dictation (4 minutes): say "Dad has a red bed." Children print a word at a time with the map, then point to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads Sam Wants a Pet aloud, one page each, then spells two words, net and bed, on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Sam Wants a Pet with a partner; chain get, got, pot, pet and net with letter cards; spell wet and jet on sound boxes; retell the three panels to a new partner. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the retell marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name tomorrow's six.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips and reason cards on scrap paper. One child: hear the tale, retell with the three panels to a grown-up, answer both exit questions by pointing, read page 1 of Sam Wants a Pet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the three panels on a long page. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The linked turns and the rotation.

- **10 min. Retell.** Your child tells the tale from the three panels, first, next, last.
  **Say.** "Why is this a story?" Expect: people talk and act, and it ends.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws three panels and adds a word to each.
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child prints W to Z, then "Dad has a red bed."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X40a]] [[s02-X40b]] You read a new card: Otter dropped a shell, Heron spotted it in the reeds, Heron lifted it out. Show two strips of three pictures, in order and in reverse, and your child points to the whole story. Then read three reasons, and your child points to the one that says why it is a story, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling, the exit and the rotation; cut the joined sentences. Longer: a child who finishes retells to a new partner using a joined sentence between two panels.

### Words and supports

Words: retell, story, first, next, last, reason, and. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "It is a story because ___." "___, and ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 reads a second tale, from China, and traces its first events. If many children laid the strips in the wrong order (error A) on today's exit, the opening of day 41's repair part goes to a four-minute repair: reread the time words and place the picture cards. If a few named an object (error D) or a claim the card never makes (error E), work with them at the rug edge.
