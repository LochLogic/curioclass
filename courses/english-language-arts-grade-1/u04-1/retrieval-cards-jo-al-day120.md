# Day 120 retrieval cards, Jo and Al

Two teacher-made cards for the opening retrieval, a model and not an exit. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each. Card one: "Jo sees a hurt bird. Jo calls Dad. Dad phones a bird helper. The bird needs trained care." Card two: "Al sees a hurt bird. Al calls Mum. Mum phones a bird helper. The bird needs trained care." Each has one plain drawing of a child beside a grown-up with a phone. Read a card aloud, then ask what is the same and what is different, which sentence tells why the helper is phoned, and who gets each call. A story-map with three boxes (who, where and when, what happens) is drawn on the Jo card for the problem ask. These two cases are used only here.
