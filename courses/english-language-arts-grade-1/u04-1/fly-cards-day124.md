# Fly cards

Two picture cards and two sentences you read aloud, both from page 8 of Ben and the Bird. Card one: a small insect near a gate post; "A fly buzzes past the bench." Card two: a bird high in a tree with its wings spread; "Another bird lifts its wings and begins to fly." The children point to the card that fits each sentence and say which words told them.
