# Day 123: Nia calls for help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 122's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the repair card below and its two pictures. Hand back yesterday's printing sets, each with one written line, and day 122's exit tickets (s04-X122c). Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Cut the six story-map pictures apart and mix them. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 122's exit tickets (s04-X122c); each child looks at what Ben and Dad do in their drawing. Repair for day 122's exit (error AC): a child who ordered two events wrongly. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Mo's cup tips over. Mo gets a cloth and wipes the table." Lay two pictures down: the cup tipped over, Mo wiping the table. Say: "Pat puts the wiping first. Sam puts the tipped cup first. Whose order fits the card?" Children show a thumb for Pat or for Sam (about 10 seconds). Expect: Sam. Then you point to the tipped cup and say first, point to the wiping and say next. Pairs say it back with first and next, in 15 seconds, and three pairs are heard. A child who thumbs for Pat has put the answer before the problem.

#### 15 min. Read Nia Calls for Help

Say: "This is a different story about a different girl and a different bird." Read the whole story aloud with the pictures (about 390 words, three minutes). Pause after page 2 and ask: "Who says not to touch the dove?" Expect: Coach Lee. Read on. Reread page 3 slowly. Review: a sentence clue, the sentence beside a word tells what it means (day 121). Say the sentence aloud: "A trained helper has learned how to look after hurt wild birds." Children say what trained helper means in 10 seconds with a partner. Say the safety words together: "We never pick up a wild bird. We call a grown-up." A child upset by the hurt dove may look at the picture of Nia and her coach.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds, then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Three asks, about 2 minutes 30 seconds each, with the words dove, coach, stunned and window said once with their pictures.

1. **Ask.** "Touch the words on page 4 that show how Nia waits." Expect: waits away from the bird; sits on the grass; holds her knees.
2. **Ask.** "Lee says Nia stays back because the dove is scared. Mo says Nia stays back because the bench is old. Whose answer fits page 2?" Expect: Lee's.
3. **Ask.** "Kai says Ben and Nia are alike because an adult tells each to keep away from the bird. Roz says they are alike because each bird is a robin. Whose answer fits both stories?" Expect: Kai's; Nia's bird is a dove.

#### 8 min. Pronouns

Today's new idea: a pronoun takes the place of a noun, so we do not say the same name again and again. Say: "Nia is quiet. She is very still." The word she takes the place of Nia. Say the list with a gesture each: I, me, he, she, it, they, them. Show the pronoun cards he, she, it and they. Read one line from page 3 and children point to the pronoun card that replaces the noun: Nia is she; the dove is it; Nia and Coach Lee are they. Then lay down the noun cards Pip and Tim: Pip is he, Pip and Tim are they. Pairs then take one story page and draw an arrow from each pronoun to its noun on the picture, 2 minutes, with six pairs heard, 20 seconds each. We say it today and do not write it.

#### 12 min. Nia's story-map

Review: a story-map has three boxes, who, where and when, and what happens (day 120). Each child has a story-map sheet. Six small pictures lie on the table. Children sort the pictures into the three boxes, 6 minutes: the girl into the who box, the bench and window into the where and when box, and the others into the what happens box. Say: "Show me the box for the problem." Then each child copies one word label from the card, dove or coach, and copies the page number, 1, 2 minutes (copied, not decoded; you say the word first). Partners lay Nia's map beside the Ben map and say one thing the same, 40 seconds each, and you hear six, 15 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture records what Bea does?" ([[s04-X123a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, with the card in view, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Which event explains why she does it?" ([[s04-X123b]]) The rest also draw alone. **Ask.** "Draw what Nia does when she sees the dove." ([[s04-X123c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who picks or draws an action the story does not give (error AB) goes to day 124's opening repair. A drawing that picks up the dove gets the rule said again: we never pick up a wild bird.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Segment sharp. Blend /k/ /or/ /d/. Say the last sound of fork. Say hat and hate: which has the long vowel sound?

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 19, lesson 99. Review: ar and or each make one sound (day 120). Read and write on small boards, 20 seconds a word: art, cart, sharp, short, sport. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Then read a short sentence on the board with a finger under each word and check it makes sense: "A short cart is in the barn." Then map two irregular words: knew (the k is silent, and ew says /oo/) and does (the oe says /uh/); read each whole, never sound it out. The reader Looking for Mars is used in the rotation today, the whole book; would and bye were mapped yesterday.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print Pp, Qq, Rr, Ss, Tt, ten forms, 5 minutes, with p and q printed side by side: the p tail goes down on the left and the q tail goes down on the right, so check the two together. While they print, walk and read each child's feedback line on yesterday's set aloud, about 8 seconds each. Dictate "A sharp fork is in the cart." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes. No new letter forms are taught; new-letter teaching finished on day 27.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Looking for Mars, the whole book, each child heard in turn, 40 seconds; the heard-six column names them. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with A Day at the Lake for the rest.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 124's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud and draw the pictures on paper. One child: read the story together, sort the six pictures into the three boxes, say the pronoun for each noun, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort, the rotation and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Story.** Read Nia Calls for Help aloud, with the pictures. **Say.** "Who says not to touch the dove?" Expect: Coach Lee.
- **10 min. Pronouns.** Say: "Nia is quiet. She is very still." **Say.** "Who is she?" Expect: Nia. Then say: "The dove is gray. It is quiet." Your child says what it is.
- **5 min. Print.** Five pairs, Pp to Tt. Dictate: "A sharp fork is in the cart."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X123a]] [[s04-X123b]] [[s04-X123c]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture, says which event explains it, then draws what Nia does when she sees the dove.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the story, the pronouns, the exit and the printing. Cut the map to three pictures. Longer: a child who sorts quickly says one thing the same and one thing different between the Ben map and the Nia map.

### Words and supports

Words: dove, coach, stunned, window, pronoun, she, it, they. Frames: "Nia sees ___." "She ___." "They ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say what a character does and which event explains it. A child who picked or drew an action the story does not give (error AB) gets a fresh card in day 124's opening repair, and the drawings (s04-X123c) come back. Day 124 reads chapter 3 of Ben and the Bird and compares one event with the short story, teaching his, her and their, and something and nothing.
