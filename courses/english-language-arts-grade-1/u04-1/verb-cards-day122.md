# Verb cards

Six picture cards from pages 4 and 5 of chapter 2: Dad listens, Dad and Ben listen, Ben nods, Ben and Dad nod, Dad waits, Dad and Ben wait. Two sentence strips you read aloud: "Dad listens." "Dad and Ben listen." Children match the verb you say to the picture it fits and say it for one, then for two.
