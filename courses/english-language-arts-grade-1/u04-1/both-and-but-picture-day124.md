# Both and But picture

A two-column wall frame. The left column is headed Both and holds a picture of Ben and Nia each waiting away from a bird; the right is headed But and holds two small pictures, Ben on a bench with Dad and Nia on the grass with Coach Lee. The frame holds experiences, what each child does, never how the birds look. The class says, "Both wait away from the bird, but Ben sits on a bench and Nia sits on the grass." The frame is reused on days 125 and 130.
