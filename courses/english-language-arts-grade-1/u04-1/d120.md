# Day 120: The bird-care question and Ben's robin

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pin the question card: How can people help birds that need it? Copy day 119's retry marks: a child who named a detail for the whole topic, or pointed to the cover for a feature, is named for the opening. Name today's heard six and the ten-minute catch-up group from the cycle record. Mix the order cards. Keep the story pictures face down until page 1.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Day 119's retry showed who still names one detail for a whole topic (day 119's error A), or points to the cover for a feature (day 119's error D). Those children stand at the board with you for one minute: read the whole question card with a finger under each word, then point to the one word that names the topic (birds), not a detail. Everyone says the question back. Say: "We will read a long story, a chapter at a time, about a boy and a bird. We will look for an answer." Show page 1, then read it aloud up to "It sits very still." Review: notice and wonder (kindergarten, days 80 and 81). Partners say what they notice and what they wonder, 20 seconds a pair, one turn each; five pairs are heard. The pictures preview notice and help.

#### 10 min. Retrieval task

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read the Jo card, then the Al card, from the card sheet. Nine asks, one at a time. For each, partners answer in 20 seconds and two pairs are heard (about 55 seconds an ask, 9 minutes in all). The record names each miss by row.

1. **Ask.** "Say one thing the same on the two cards." Expect: both children call an adult. (review of unit 1, day 18)
2. **Ask.** "Say one thing different on the two cards." Expect: Jo calls Dad, Al calls Mum.
3. **Ask.** "Touch the sentence that tells why a helper is phoned." Expect: The bird needs trained care. A child who touches a first sentence only has not read for the reason.
4. **Ask.** "Say: I think calling a helper is good because ___." (opinion with a reason, unit 2)
5. **Ask.** "Say what you think trained means, and touch a word on the card that helps." (review of kindergarten, days 37, 43 and 49: a word near an unknown word can help)
6. **Ask.** "Say who gets the call, to finish: Jo calls ___." Expect: Dad.
7. **Ask.** "Put the topic, reason and closing cards in order." (paragraph-frame, unit 1, day 7)
8. **Ask.** "I put the problem in the who box. Is that right? Say which box it goes in." Expect: no, the box for what happens.
9. **Ask.** "Put the source label beside the note." (unit 2, day 50)

A preference with no reason, an unrelated fact as the reason, a word guessed without its sentence, and a fragment each go on the record.

#### 15 min. Read chapter 1

Read pages 1 and 2 of chapter 1 aloud, showing each picture, up to "Then he stops." and stop. Partners say what Ben could do (one turn, 20 seconds a pair, five pairs heard). Then read on to the end of page 3 (about 330 words in all, three minutes with the pictures). Reread page 2 slowly while children point to the hanging wing in the picture. Say the safety words together: "We never pick up a wild bird. We call a grown-up." Write on the chart under the question card: Ben calls his dad. Say: "That is one way to help." A child upset by the hurt bird may look at the picture of Ben and Dad instead.

#### 10 min. Talk

Partners use the turn card (review: grade 1, day 1), two turns each, then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Three asks, one at a time, about 2 minutes 25 seconds each.

1. **Ask.** "Kim says Ben's problem is that the robin cannot fly. Lou says it is that the gate is green. Whose answer fits page 2? Show with a thumb, and point to the page." Expect: Kim; one wing hangs low.
2. **Ask.** "Two sentences are on the strip. Which words show what Ben does? Touch them." Expect: comes, stops, calls.
3. **Ask.** "Four wing-job cards lie on the robin's picture: fly, find food, get away, sing. Touch each job the robin cannot do." Review: wings help a bird fly, find food and get away (unit 3). Expect: the first three; sing is a trap, since wings do not sing.

#### 15 min. Story-map

Review: a story-map has three boxes, who, where and when, and what happens (kindergarten, day 49; grade 1, days 10 and 18). Each child draws chapter 1 in the three boxes, 9 minutes. You walk and say, "Show me the box for the problem." Then each child copies one word label under the problem, bird or wing, from the card, and copies the page number, 2 (copied, not decoded; you say the word first), 1 minute. Partners tell their map, 40 seconds each, with 3 minutes to change over and settle. Collect the maps.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 3 aloud and show the three pictures from the exit sheet. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows what Dad does?" ([[s04-X120av2]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, with page 3 open, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Why does Dad say they need a trained helper?" ([[s04-X120bv2]]) The rest also do two more things on the page, alone. **Ask.** "Draw one way a person can help a bird that needs it." ([[s04-X120c]]) **Ask.** "Put a star on the box of your story-map that shows Ben's problem." ([[s04-X120d]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the page once), not yet seen. A child who picks or says an action the page does not give (error AB) goes to day 121's opening repair. A drawing that picks up the bird gets the rule said again: we never pick up a wild bird.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only, all at once. Say cap and cape: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /j/ /ar/. Say the first, middle and last sound of cork. Segment park.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Today's new pattern: ar and or. On the sound-spelling map, map ar (jar) and or (corn). Review: two letters can make one sound (digraphs sh, th and ch, grade 1, days 6 to 9); here a vowel and r make one sound. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: car, card, cart, part, park, dark; then for, fork, fort, short. Children tap the sounds, say the word and write it. Each is one syllable, so one clap checks one vowel sound. Then decode and spell two words, card and fork. The reader waits for the rotation.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

First dated printing set: print Aa, Bb, Cc, Dd, Ee, ten forms, on the sheet, 5 minutes. No new letter forms are taught; new-letter teaching finished on day 27. Dictate "A car is in the dark." whole, then a word at a time; children tap each sound and print, 4 minutes. Written feedback goes back tomorrow.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with the reader A Day at the Lake (cycle 19's own reader waits until its spellings and irregular words are mapped, tomorrow). Each child is heard in turn, 40 seconds, and the heard-six column names them. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, a finger under each word.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record, today's printing set and the retrieval misses by row. Mark each exit: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Name the opening repair for tomorrow.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three boxes on paper and say every card aloud. One child: read the chapter aloud together, draw the three boxes, print five letter pairs, and answer the exit aloud. A device in the room is not needed.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: say each card aloud and draw it on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the rotation and the sound chains.

- **5 min. Question.** Read aloud: How can people help birds that need it? **Say.** "What do you notice and wonder about a hurt bird?" Expect: anything true that your child sees or asks.
- **10 min. Story.** Read chapter 1 aloud, pages 1 to 3, with the pictures. **Say.** "What problem does Ben notice?" Expect: the robin cannot fly; one wing hangs low. Say together: never pick up a wild bird, call a grown-up.
- **5 min. Map.** Your child draws three boxes: who, where and when, and what happens.
- **5 min. Print.** Five pairs, Aa to Ee. Dictate: "A car is in the dark."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X120av2]] [[s04-X120bv2]] [[s04-X120c]] [[s04-X120d]] Reread page 3 of the story. Your child points to the picture of what Dad does, says why Dad wants a trained helper, draws one way to help a bird, then stars the box of the story-map that shows Ben's problem.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the story-map, the exit and the printing. Cut the retrieval to asks 1, 2, 5 and 8. Longer: a child with a clear map tells the story in order to a partner using first, next and last.

### Words and supports

Words: notice, wonder, help, wing, gate, problem. Frames: "I notice ___." "I wonder ___." "Ben sees ___. He calls ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can match a picture to an action and say why. A child who chose an action the page does not give (error AB) gets a fresh card in day 121's opening repair. Day 121 rereads chapter 1 for what happens to Ben and teaches how the sentence beside a word helps its meaning; retrieval misses go to its repair groups, and the printing set comes back with written feedback.
