# Day 122: What Ben and Dad do next

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 121's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the repair card below and its three pictures. Hand back yesterday's printing sets, each with one written line, and day 121's exit tickets (s04-X121c). Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Make the four sort pictures. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 121's exit tickets (s04-X121c); each child looks at the Ben in their drawing. Repair for day 121's exit (error E): a child who kept the first meaning that came to mind over the sentence. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Tess has a pup, a young dog that is six weeks old." Say: "Max says a pup is a cup. Lee says a pup is a young dog. Whose meaning does the sentence give?" Children show a thumb for Max or for Lee (about 10 seconds). Expect: Lee. Then you read the whole sentence again and say: "That sentence is the clue." Pairs say which words told them, in 15 seconds, and three pairs are heard. A child who thumbs for Max chose the meaning that came first.

#### 15 min. Read chapter 2

Read pages 4 to 6 aloud with the pictures (about 330 words, three minutes). Pause after page 4 and say: "The helper told Dad what to do while they wait." Partners guess what the helper said, one turn each, 20 seconds a pair, five pairs heard. Read on. Reread page 5 slowly while children touch the picture of Ben and Dad a good way back from the robin. Say the three rules together: "We stay back. We keep it quiet. We give no food or water." Safety words again: "We never pick up a wild bird. We call a grown-up." Write on the chart: Ben and Dad stay back. Say: "That is one way to help." A child upset by the hurt bird may look at the picture of Ben and Dad.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds, then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Three asks, about 2 minutes 30 seconds each, with the words space, quiet, strength and helper said once with their pictures.

1. **Ask.** "Four picture cards lie on the table: stay back, keep quiet, give food, pick up. Sort them into what the helper said and what the helper did not say." Expect: stay back and keep quiet are what the helper said; give food and pick up are not.
2. **Ask.** "Find the little word on page 5 that joins the two parts: If we come close, the bird may use up its strength. So we give it space. Touch the word that joins them." Expect: so. Review: you met so in kindergarten. Say: "So tells us the second part happens because of the first."
3. **Ask.** "Mia says Ben stays back because the bird is scared. Jon says Ben stays back because the gate is green. Whose answer fits page 5?" Expect: Mia's.

#### 8 min. Agreement

Today's new idea: a verb must match its noun. Review: a naming word and an action word (kindergarten, unit 2.1). Say: "Dad waits. Ben and Dad wait." One person gets the verb with s; more than one gets the verb without it. Say each pair twice with the picture. Show the six verb cards from pages 4 and 5 (Dad listens, Dad and Ben listen, Ben nods, Ben and Dad nod, Dad waits, Dad and Ben wait). For each, children say the sentence for one, then for two. Then lay down the noun cards Pip and Tim and say: "Pip nods. Pip and Tim nod." Partners take two cards, say a sentence that matches, and the other partner says thumb up or thumb down (20 seconds a pair, six pairs heard). We say it, we do not write it today.

#### 12 min. Two events

Each child has a two-event strip: two small pictures are already in the boxes, a robin on the ground with one wing hanging, then a boy and a man waiting well back from it. An arrow joins the boxes. Children do three things, 2 minutes each, with you reading each one aloud. First, circle the box that shows the problem. Then find and mark so on the board and copy it onto the arrow, a word you say first. Then judge a given strip: you hold a second strip with the arrow turned backward, and children say thumb up or thumb down for whether the arrow goes from the problem to what Ben and Dad do. Expect: down. Partners then tell the order with first and next, 40 seconds each, and you hear six, 15 seconds each. Review: first, next and last tell the order (kindergarten, day 51).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture strip shows the two events in order?" ([[s04-X122a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, with the card in view, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "How does the second event answer the first problem?" ([[s04-X122b]]) The rest also draw alone. **Ask.** "Draw one thing Ben and Dad do while they wait for the helper." ([[s04-X122c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the strip in the wrong order goes to day 123's opening repair for error AC. A drawing that feeds the robin or picks it up (error AB) gets the rule said again: we stay back and give no food.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Blend /f/ /ar/ /m/. Segment short. Say the first, middle and last sound of barn. Say tap and tape: which has the long vowel sound?

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 19, lesson 98. Review: ar and or each make one sound (day 120). Build, take apart and write ar words on small boards, 20 seconds a word: farm, harm, barn, yarn, card, hard, yard. Say each, tap the sounds, clap the syllable, write it. Then map two irregular words: would (the ou says /oo/, and the l is silent) and bye (the y and e say one long vowel sound); read each whole, never sound it out. The reader Looking for Mars is used in the rotation today, pages 1 to 8; again was mapped yesterday.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print Kk, Ll, Mm, Nn, Oo, ten forms, 5 minutes. While they print, walk and read each child's feedback line on yesterday's set aloud, about 8 seconds each. Dictate "A storm is far off." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes. No new letter forms are taught; new-letter teaching finished on day 27.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Looking for Mars, pages 1 to 8, each child heard in turn, 40 seconds; the heard-six column names them. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with A Day at the Lake for the rest.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 123's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud and draw the two pictures on paper. One child: reread pages 4 and 5 together, find so, say the verb pairs, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort, the rotation and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 4 and 5 of chapter 2 aloud. **Say.** "What does the helper say to do while they wait?" Expect: stay back, keep quiet, give no food or water.
- **10 min. So.** Say: "If we come close, the bird may be scared. So we give it space." **Say.** "Which little word joins the two parts?" Expect: so.
- **5 min. Verbs.** Say Dad waits, then Ben and Dad wait. Your child says which has the s.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X122a]] [[s04-X122b]] [[s04-X122c]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the strip in order, says how the second event helps, then draws one thing Ben and Dad do while they wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the so word, the exit and the printing. Cut the verb cards to four. Longer: a child who finds so quickly finds a second joining word on page 5 and says what it joins.

### Words and supports

Words: space, quiet, strength, helper, wait, so, verb. Frames: "First ___. Next ___." "___ because ___." "___ waits." "___ and ___ wait." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can put two events in order and say how the second answers the first. A child who ordered the strip wrongly (error AC) gets a fresh pair in day 123's opening repair, and the drawings (s04-X122c) come back so each child sees what they drew. Day 123 reads the second bird-help story whole and records its character's experience, and teaches the pronouns he, she, it and they.
