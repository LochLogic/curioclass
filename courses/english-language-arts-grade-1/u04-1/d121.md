# Day 121: Ben's experience and a clue in the sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 120's retrieval misses and exit marks; find the commonest one. Make the repair card below and its three pictures. Hand back yesterday's story-maps and printing sets, each with one written line. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 120's exit tickets (s04-X120c and s04-X120d) and the story-maps; each child looks at the star. Repair for day 120's exit (error AB): a child who picked or said an action the card does not give. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Pia's toy bird rolls under the sofa. Pia reaches in with a long spoon and rolls it out." Lay three pictures down: Pia rolling the bird out, Pia throwing the bird away, Pia sweeping the floor. Children sort them in pairs into two piles, on the card and not on the card, then say one action word from the card. Expect: the first on the card; the other two not. A picture sorted as on the card because it looks likely is error AB.

#### 15 min. Reread chapter 1 and a sentence clue

Reread pages 2 and 3 aloud with the pictures (about 270 words, 2 minutes 30 seconds). Today's new idea is a sentence clue: the sentence beside a word can tell what it means. Review: the three-card routine for an unknown word, word, picture, sentence (kindergarten, unit 2.1). Point to trained helper on page 3. Say: "Ola thinks trained means learned how to care for hurt wild birds. Rae thinks trained means a thing on rails." Read the next sentence aloud: "A trained helper has learned how to care for hurt wild birds. That is her job." Children show a thumb for the child whose meaning fits the sentence (about 10 seconds). Then you show a wrong clue and the class judges it: "I think the clue is Dad comes to the gate. Thumb up or down?" Expect: down. Say: "The clue is the sentence beside trained helper, so Ola's meaning came from there." Children say thumb up or down once more for your fixed clue. Review: -ed tells it already happened (kindergarten, day 53): trained is train and -ed, someone who was taught. Pairs then take the burrow card. Two strips lie beside it: "A rabbit lives in a burrow, a hole in the ground." and "The rabbit is brown." Pairs sort the strips into tells what burrow means and does not tell (3 minutes, six pairs heard, 20 seconds each). Say the sentence clue card line: cover the word, read the sentence around it. Use the words after decoding, never to guess how a word sounds. A child upset by the hurt robin may look at the picture of Ben and Dad.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds, then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Three asks, about 2 minutes 30 seconds each, with the words gate, wait, trained, helper and care said once with their pictures.

1. **Ask.** "What does Ben do on pages 2 and 3?" Expect: he sees the robin, stops, calls his dad.
2. **Ask.** "Mia says the sentence about learned how to care explains trained. Jon says Ben likes the green gate explains it. Whose sentence?" Expect: Mia's.
3. **Ask.** "Before the sentence you may have thought one thing about trained. Now say what you think." Frame: "I thought ___. Now I think ___." Expect: the meaning from the sentence, not the first guess.

#### 6 min. Nouns

Today's new idea: three kinds of noun, a word for a person, place, thing or animal. Common nouns name any one: gate, robin, bird, garden. Proper nouns name one special one and begin with a capital: Ben, Saturday. Review: a name begins with a capital letter (grade 1, day 50). A possessive noun shows what belongs to someone: Ben's gate is the gate that belongs to Ben. Say each on page 1, then say Ben's gate together. Show the six noun cards (dog, nest, Pip, Tim, Omar's scarf, Sam's hat). Pairs lay them under common, proper and possessive, two each, saying "the name of one" or "any". The sort carries into the next part.

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Review: Return and repair groups (grade 1, day 20): a wrong example beside a right one, then a fresh card. Day 120's retrieval misses sort into three groups of about six at your table, 2 minutes 30 seconds each, with 2 minutes 30 seconds to move. Reasons (errors B and C): wrong, "I like calling." beside right, "I think calling a helper is good because the bird needs trained care." Name the model: the paragraph-frame. Word (error E): wrong, "trained means on a train" beside right, learned how to care. Name the model: the sentence clue. Sentence (error Q): wrong, "The helper." beside right, "The helper cares for the bird." Each child then says a fresh one. The other pairs finish the noun sort and say why each card sits in its group.

#### 4 min. Sort and print

Each child prints their own name and two noun words from the cards at the foot of today's dated sheet, copying from the card; a name begins with a capital.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the kit?" ([[s04-X121a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, with the card in view, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Which words on the card tell what a kit is?" ([[s04-X121b]]) The rest also draw alone. **Ask.** "Draw one thing that happens to Ben in chapter 1." ([[s04-X121c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who picks the kitten has used the first meaning that came to mind over the sentence (error E) and goes to day 122's opening repair. A drawing of the robin alone, with no Ben, is error A: a look is drawn where an experience was asked.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Segment farm. Blend /k/ /or/ /n/. Say the middle sound of horn. Say cod and code: which has the long vowel sound?

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 19, lesson 97. Review: ar and or each make one sound (day 120). Connect the spellings to words: read the chain on word cards, then write on small boards, 20 seconds a word: corn, horn, born, storm, stork, fork. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Then map one irregular word: again (the first a says /uh/ and ai says /e/; read it whole, never sound it out). The reader Looking for Mars is first used in the rotation today, pages 1 to 6 only; its later pages wait until their irregular words are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print Ff, Gg, Hh, Ii, Jj, ten forms, 5 minutes. While they print, walk and read each child's feedback line on yesterday's set aloud, about 8 seconds each. Dictate "Park the car in the barn." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes. No new letter forms are taught; new-letter teaching finished on day 27.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Looking for Mars, pages 1 to 6, each child heard in turn, 40 seconds; the heard-six column names them. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with A Day at the Lake for the rest.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 122's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud and draw the pictures on a board. One child: reread pages 2 and 3 together, find the sentence beside trained, sort the six cards and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No noun cards: write six words on scraps.

**Skip.** The retrieval repair, the rotation and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 2 and 3 of chapter 1 aloud. **Say.** "What does Ben do?" Expect: he sees the robin, stops, calls his dad.
- **10 min. Clue.** Read the sentence after trained helper. **Say.** "What does trained mean?" Expect: has learned how to care for hurt wild birds.
- **5 min. Nouns.** Say Ben, Ben's gate, gate. Your child says which names one.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X121a]] [[s04-X121b]] [[s04-X121c]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture, points to the words that tell, then draws one thing that happens to Ben.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the sentence clue, the exit and the printing. Cut the noun sort to three cards. Longer: a child who meets the clue easily finds a second word on page 3 and the sentence beside it.

### Words and supports

Words: gate, wait, trained, helper, care, clue, noun. Frames: "I thought ___. Now I think ___." "The clue is ___." "___ names one." "___ names any." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can use the sentence to choose a meaning and point to the clue words. A child who kept the first meaning (error E) gets a fresh card in day 122's opening repair, and a child who drew only the robin (error A) is shown a drawing with Ben in it. Day 122 reads chapter 2 and connects what Ben and Dad do to the problem in chapter 1, and teaches the verb that matches its noun.
