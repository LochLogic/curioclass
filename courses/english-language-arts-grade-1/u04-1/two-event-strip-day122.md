# Two-event strip

A paper strip with two boxes marked First and Next and an arrow between them, with a short blank line on the arrow. On day 122 the boxes already hold two small pictures, so no child draws: First, a robin on the ground with one wing hanging; Next, a boy and a man waiting well back from it. The child prints so on the arrow (copied from the board) and circles the problem. On day 128 the same strip holds two other pictures from chapter 4. No printed words except First, Next and the arrow's line.
