# Sentence-clue card

A frame for pointing to a clue. The card shows a sentence line with a gap beside a picture word box, and an arrow marked "clue" pointing at the line. Use it with any word from the reading: the child covers the word, reads the sentence around it, and points to the words that tell what it means.
