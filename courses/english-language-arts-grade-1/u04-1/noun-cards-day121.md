# Noun cards

Six cards for pairs to sort into three groups: common, proper and possessive. Words, each with a small drawing: dog, nest (common); Pip, Tim (proper); Omar's scarf, Sam's hat (possessive). Every word is decodable from the taught letters. Two cards per group. Pairs say why a card goes in its group.
