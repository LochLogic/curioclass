# Day 147: Show what I know about birds as helpers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six and the catch-up group. Keep the held card face down in your folder. No help, no hints, no feedback today. Keep the clock to yourself.

### The day

#### 40 min. Sitting

Say: "This is a sitting. I will not help. Pass if you wish." Hand out the response sheets. Say each stem once and wait. Mark each: shown or not yet seen (no shown with help, since none is given). Collect the sheets at the end.

- **Row 1, 12 minutes.** Read *Birds as Human Helpers* aloud whole, then slowly again, 4 minutes. [[s04-A14]] [[s04-A15]] [[s04-A16]] Birds living all over the world is error C.
- **Row 2, 6 minutes.** [[s04-A17]] [[s04-A18]] A question about color is error D.
- **Row 3, 7 minutes.** [[s04-A19]] Then show the held card and its two pictures. [[s04-A20]] Bank as money in both sentences is error E.
- **Row 4, 7 minutes.** [[s04-A21]] The held card stays in view. [[s04-A22]] Harmless as full of harm is error F.
- **Row 5, 8 minutes.** The held card stays in view. [[s04-A23]] [[s04-A24]] Helped as unrelated to help is error G.

A child who cannot yet write an answer says it to you in the next part, 40 seconds each, and you write it as said.

#### 20 min. Familiar reading

Each child rereads a familiar decodable or a poem from an earlier unit, alone or with a partner. No teaching, no feedback. Take the said answers.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say pie, high, kite and sky: which have the long i? Segment cried.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 24, lesson 123. No new teaching. Read the word cards, then chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: night, high, sigh, right, pie, lie.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate "The kite is high." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: map igh and ie, blend, read, spell, reread Night Skies. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Night Skies reread for expression, each child heard for 35 seconds. The others reread, chain and spell in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the sitting marks to the record sheet. None is returned before day 157.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the row boxes on a plain sheet. One child: do the rows with a grown-up who writes the answers as said.

### At home

**Swap.** No text: use any short page.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. The assessment: read twice.** Read, with no help: "Cows help people. Cows give milk to drink. Some cows pull carts on farms. Cows are very important." **Say.** "What point does the writer make?" Expect: cows help people.
- **10 min. Reason.** **Say.** "Say one reason that supports the point." Expect: cows give milk, or cows pull carts.
- **10 min. Question.** **Say.** "Ask a question about the word important." Expect: a question about its meaning, such as what does it mean. Write it as said.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "high" and "kite."
- **5 min. Close.** Give no hints and do not mark; the class sitting is the record.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: ask every row; shorten the familiar reading. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: point, reason. A scribe and a home language count; give no hint.

### Tomorrow

The sitting shows what each child can do alone: point and reasons, a question about a word, a meaning from its sentence, a word part, a root. Nothing is repaired today. Day 148 opens with day 146's repair, then analyzes a model portrait paragraph. Sitting misses go to the day 157 repair.
