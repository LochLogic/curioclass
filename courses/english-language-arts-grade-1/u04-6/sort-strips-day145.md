# Point card and sort strips: Birds Help Plants

A point card and four strips, read aloud by you, sorted under the point card into a "tells why" place and a set-aside corner.

- Point card: Birds help plants grow in new places.
- Strip: A bird eats fruit with seeds inside.
- Strip: Seeds fall far from the plant.
- Strip: A sprout starts.
- Strip: Bright feathers.

Three word cards with a small picture each: fruit, plant, bird (the word seed is held for the day 150 reading check, so it is not on a card; the strips are read aloud by you and children do not read them). The strips come from *Birds Help Plants* (g1-u4-03). The sorted strips stay in the portrait folder.
