# Section 4.6 opener

## What the section does

Days 145 to 147 finish the reading of the unit information pages. Day 145 reads why plants need birds and sorts reasons from facts that do not tell why. Day 146 reads why animals need birds and uses roots and word parts to work out word meanings. Day 147 is the first observation sitting of the unit: five rows on a new short text, with no help and no hint; its response is on day 157 only. The sitting words and cards are held and never shown before day 147.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** Calls an unrelated fact a supporting reason. On 145 test whether the fact explains the stated point. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **D:** Asks a question unrelated to word meaning. On 142 model a meaning question about a highlighted word. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **F:** Reverses an affix's meaning. On 146 connect -less to without using a known root. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **G:** Treats an inflected word as unrelated to its root. On 146 attach ending cards to the same root. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **AA:** Offers an unrelated reading response. On 157 return to the line before answering anew. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **E:** Uses one meaning despite contrary sentence clues. On 135 mark the sentence clue before choosing the meaning. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.

On day 147 do not teach, hint or give feedback. Anything a child cannot yet do is marked "not yet seen" and returns on day 157.
