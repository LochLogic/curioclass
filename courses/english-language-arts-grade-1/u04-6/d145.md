# Day 145: Why plants need birds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group and the pairs. Set out each pair's plant sort strips and word cards. Write the bike card (below) as you will say it. Keep the exit pictures face down. The cycle record from day 144's regrouping names the catch-up six.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 144's exit (error S, s04-X144bv2), 3 minutes. Say: "This card is made up for practice. A bike has a bell and a basket. The bell is for warning people. The basket is for carrying a lunch." Show two small pictures. Pairs say to each other one thing the bell does that the basket does not, 90 seconds; hear two pairs. If many missed, say a second made-up card (a kite has a tail and a string; the tail keeps it steady, the string is held in a hand) and ask again, 1 minute. Then, 2 minutes: lay out the point and reason strips from day 142 and say: "Review: a point is what the writer wants and a reason is a fact that tells why (days 132 and 142). Today the strips come from a new page, about plants."

#### 15 min. Read

Teach three words with pictures, 3 minutes: fruit, seed (the small part inside fruit that can grow into a new plant) and sprout (a tiny new plant). Read *Birds Help Plants*, pages 1 to 3, aloud once, whole, 2 minutes. Say: "The seeds come out in the bird's droppings. That is what a bird leaves behind; the page says it plainly." Reread page 3 with its picture, 3 minutes, and say the point: "Birds help plants grow in new places." Children point to the fruit, the seed and the sprout on the pictures of pages 1 and 2, 3 minutes. Then think aloud with the because test (review, days 133 and 142), 4 minutes: "Birds help plants grow in new places because seeds land far from the plant. That tells why, so it is a reason."

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time with the turn card, 60 seconds, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the asks. Pass is allowed.

1. **Ask.** "What does the writer say birds do for plants?" Expect: they help plants grow in new places.
2. **Ask.** "Say a fact from the page that tells why." Expect: the seeds fall far from the plant, or birds carry seeds.
3. **Ask.** "How does a seed get far from the plant?" Expect: a bird eats the fruit and flies on.
4. **Ask.** "Point to the line on page 3 about bright feathers." Expect: the child points to it. Say: "The page calls this a fun fact. It is not why birds help plants."

#### 20 min. Writing and language

Model, 4 minutes. Put the point card on the board with a tells why place and a set-aside corner. Say: "This strip is made up for practice: Birds like to sing. Does it fit after because? Birds help plants grow in new places because birds like to sing. No, it does not tell why, so it goes in the corner." Then lay the page strip "A bird eats fruit with seeds inside" under the point card: it tells why. Pairs sort the other three strips, 5 minutes: seeds fall far from the plant, a sprout starts, bright feathers. Two tell why and the feathers strip goes in the corner. Hear two pairs say the because test aloud. Each child says "Birds help plants because ___" to a partner, 3 minutes, 40 seconds each. Then, 5 minutes, alone: each child draws a small picture on one reason strip and prints one word label from the word cards (fruit, plant or bird). Last, 3 minutes: strips go in the portrait folder. A child who puts feathers under tells why is error C; ask them to say the because test aloud.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud page 2 of Marks Outside and show the three pictures from the card sheet, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" ([[s04-X145av2]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 2 minutes. The spoken asks are heard in the rotation below. The five windows are error C. The smiling child or "Dots are pretty" is error AA.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say sit and night, then pin and high, then kit and sigh: which word in each pair has the long i? Blend /n/ /i/ /t/ with the long i. Segment high.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 24, lesson 121. New today: igh says the long i sound in night, high, sigh and right, three letters, one sound. Map it on the sound-spelling map, 2 minutes. Review: i_e and y also say long i (kite, bike; my, fly). Pairs sort sixteen cards by spelling, 4 minutes. Then chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it: high, sigh, night, right, 4 minutes. Today the rotation rereads A Little Seed; cycle 24's own reader waits until its words are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. All children write the dictated sentence "A bright night." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map igh, blend, read, spell, reread A Little Seed. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with A Little Seed pages 1 to 10 reread for expression, each child heard in turn for 35 seconds. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With page 2 of Marks Outside open: [[s04-X145bv2]]
- **Ask.** With page 3 of *Birds Help Plants* in view: "Say why plants need birds." ([[s04-X145c]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread a page once), not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the cycle record for day 145 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 146's opening.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips aloud and draw the two places on a sheet. One child: say the point, find the reason strips and say the because test with a grown-up.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each line aloud.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Tell.** Say: "Birds eat fruit. Seeds pass out of the bird and land far away. A new plant can start there. Bright feathers are a fun fact, not why." **Say.** "Why do plants need birds?" Expect: birds carry seeds to new places.
- **10 min. Because.** **Say.** "Finish: Birds help plants because ..." Expect: seeds land far away.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "night" and "high."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X145av2]] [[s04-X145bv2]] [[s04-X145c]] Read page 2 of Marks Outside. Your child points to the picture of the writer's reason, says why a fact does not tell why, and says why plants need birds.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the sort, the exit and the rotation. Longer: a child who finishes writes a second label on a strip.

### Words and supports

Words: fruit, seed, sprout, point, reason, fun fact, because. Frame: "Birds help plants because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once. Note for the day 150 hearing: the reader A Little Seed and the page text carry "seed" and "day"; both stay, and no word card for seed is shown.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the rotation tell you who can pick a reason and who can say why an unrelated fact does not tell why. Day 146 reads why animals need birds and uses word parts. A child who took the five windows (error C) tests a fresh card with because first. The cycle record goes to day 146's regrouping.
