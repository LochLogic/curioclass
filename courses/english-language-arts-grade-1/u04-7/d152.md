# Day 152: Edit and publish the window bird portrait

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs, today's five hearing children (hearing sheet, day 3 of six) and today's ten for the device (first ten on the class list). Open the shared page with ten numbered frames. Write the turn order on the board. Keep the held cards in your folder and the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 151's exit (error N, s04-X151a), 3 minutes, on a page made up for practice. Say: "This page is made up for practice. Our class has a pencil jar. Children take a pencil. A peer asks, 'Where do we put the pencils back?'" Pairs say one new line that answers the peer, 40 seconds each; a child who says the page again is error N. Then, 2 minutes, two new words with paper stars and a paper line: during tells when something is going on (we clap during the song) and beyond tells where, farther than a place (the star is beyond the line). Say each twice and move a star. Review: toward means moving to it (day 141); move a star toward the line.

#### 10 min. Language items

An observation: these are the unit's grammar items, so give no help. Say each stem once. Children whisper the answer to a partner, 15 seconds each; you listen at the pairs of two children per part, so six are heard across the parts. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- **2 min.** Show a picture of one paper star and print: ___ star shines. Say: "Choose a or these to finish the caption." ([[s04-A47]]) Show three paper stars and print: ___ stars shine. Say the same. ([[s04-A48]]) "A stars shine" is error O.
- **2 min.** Say: "Tell something we do during our song. Use the word during." ([[s04-A49]]) Show a paper star past a line: "Use the word beyond to tell where the star is." ([[s04-A50]]) Show a paper star moving to a mark: "Use the word toward to tell how the star moves." ([[s04-A51]]) Toward with no relation is error P.
- **6 min.** Four pictures, each said twice, 45 seconds a prompt. Girl watering a plant and boy sweeping: "Say a compound statement" ([[s04-A52]]) and "Now say it again with more detail." ([[s04-A53]]) Boy with a ball and girl with a mitt: "Ask a compound question" ([[s04-A54]]) and again with more detail ([[s04-A55]]). Open door and chair: "Give a compound command" ([[s04-A56]]) and with more detail ([[s04-A57]]). A very big hat and a very tiny hat: "Say a compound exclamation" ([[s04-A58]]) and with more detail ([[s04-A59]]). One word alone as a sentence ("The bird.") is error Q.

#### 15 min. Digital contribution

An observation. Ten children, 75 seconds each, with 15 seconds between turns for the next child to step up (10 times 75 seconds is 12 minutes 30 seconds, and 10 moves of 15 seconds fill the other 2 minutes 30 seconds). A child not reached when the 15 minutes end goes first at the device on day 153, and the log shows who is still to be seen. Each child brings a partner's paper window drawing, adds one label in the frame beside it on the shared page, a word with its page tag, such as marks and Marks Outside, and says it aloud. No accounts, no names on the screen. You stand at the device and log name, time and what was added ([[s04-A63]]). A child who only watches is not logged as a contribution and is error R. The rest finish their paper window drawing on the final sheet and the next ten go on day 153. If the device cannot be used, no record is made and the contribution is not yet seen.

#### 25 min. Edit and publish on paper

Return the revised paragraphs and say today's jobs: edit, then publish, 2 minutes. Edit, 8 minutes: with the edit checklist at the foot of the final sheet, each child touches the first letter of each line for a capital, the end of each line for an ending mark, and each word against the sound-spelling map; one fix is marked with a different color. Review: capital, ending mark and mapped words (unit 1). Publish, 10 minutes: copy the edited paragraph onto the four lines of the final sheet under the paper window drawing. A child who cannot yet write a line says it to you and you write it word for word, 30 seconds each, for up to six. Partners read aloud, 60 seconds each, 3 minutes. Last, 2 minutes: file the final sheet in the portrait folder; it goes on display on day 154.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its printed caption, "These ribbons waves," 1 minute. **Ask.** "Edit the caption so it fits: circle the word that is wrong and write it the right way above it." All children edit on the page, 2 minutes ([[s04-X152a]]). Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 1 minute. The spoken ask is heard in the rotation below. Waves left as it is is error I; "A ribbons wave" is error O.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Clap the parts of cupcake, inside, mistake and pancake. Count the vowel sounds in each: two. Say the long vowel sound you hear in the second part.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 25, lesson 128. New today: the silent-e syllable, a part with a vowel, a consonant and a silent e (cake, side, take). Review: silent-e spellings (units 2 and 3) and the closed part cup in cupcake. Read and divide cup-cake, in-side, mis-take and pan-cake, 4 minutes, saying each and tapping the parts. Compare a silent-e part with a closed part: cap and cape, 2 minutes. Chain by syllable on small boards, 20 seconds a part: cup-cake, in-side, mis-take, pan-cake, 4 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate cupcake and inside, then "A pancake is inside." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The hearing first, 15 minutes, at your table: today's five children, 3 minutes each (hearing sheet). Show the held card: "Read these words" ([[s04-A60]]), 1 minute. Then: "Count the vowel sounds in each word," with the five words ([[s04-A61]]), 30 seconds. Then: "Read each word one part at a time," the five words and cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A62]]), 1 minute 30 seconds; score the reading apart from the counting. No help is given: mark each shown or not yet seen. Ai read as two sounds is error T; sailboat counted as four is error U; stopping after sun is error V. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Then three groups of 5 minutes at your table: the catch-up six first, then two groups of six with *Reading Together* read for expression, each child heard for 40 seconds (6 times 40 seconds is 4 minutes, then 1 minute for the ask and changing). The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say a caption for the ribbons that starts with these." ([[s04-X152b]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A group not met today goes first tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the hearing sheet marks, the contribution log, the cycle record for day 152 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 153's opening. After class, score from the kept work: the paragraph drafted on day 150 and revised on day 151 ([[s04-A81]]); the bluebird response ([[s04-A82]] and [[s04-A85]]); the source notes ([[s04-A83]] and [[s04-A84]]). Nothing is returned before day 157.

### Other rooms

No device: publish on paper only; the digital contribution stays not yet seen and nothing is invented. One child: edit the paragraph with a grown-up, copy it to the final sheet and add a label with its page.

### At home

**Swap.** No paper bird: say each sentence aloud.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Edit.** Read your child's paragraph back. **Say.** "Touch each capital letter. Touch each ending mark." Expect: a touch at the start and the end of each line.
- **10 min. Say.** Show one paper bird, then three. **Say.** "Say a caption: ___ bird flies. ___ birds fly." Expect: a bird flies; these birds fly.
- **10 min. Words.** Say cupcake in two parts. Print "inside."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X152a]] [[s04-X152b]] Write "These ribbons waves." Your child fixes the wrong word and says a caption that starts with these.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the edit, the final sheet and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes adds a second label with its page to the drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: edit, publish, during, beyond, toward, these. Frame: "These ___ ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the log tell you who matches these with a plural and who has made a contribution. Day 153 opens with a 3-minute repair for error I on a fresh caption, then the next ten children go to the device.
