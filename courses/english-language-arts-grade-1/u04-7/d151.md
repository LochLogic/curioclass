# Day 151: Revise the paragraph after a question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs and today's five hearing children (hearing sheet, day 2 of six; different from day 150). Clip each draft's question strip: most are "Where do the marks go?" or "How close are the marks?". Keep the held cards in your folder and the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 150's exit (error X, s04-X150a), 3 minutes, on a page made up for practice. Say: "This page is made up for practice. Our class has a plant. It sits by the window. We water it on Monday." Pairs say one thing the page says, not a feeling, 40 seconds each, and touch the line. A child who says "The plant is pretty" is error X; ask, "Does the page say it?" and reread. Then, 2 minutes: return each draft with its question strip and say: "A question is a way to make your writing better."

#### 10 min. Read

Reread *Marks Outside* pages 1 and 2 aloud with their pictures, 3 minutes. Review: you heard these pages on days 143 and 149. Each child reads the question strip with you, 2 minutes, and points to the line on the page that answers it. Children say the answer to a partner, 40 seconds each, 2 minutes. Take 3 minutes for a few children to say where on the page they found it.

#### 10 min. Language items

An observation: these are the unit's grammar items, so give no help. Say each stem once. Children answer with their five word cards held up or a whisper to a partner, 20 seconds; you hear two children, 20 seconds each, per part, so six are heard across the parts. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- **3 min.** Say: "Yesterday I ... Finish the sentence with the word walk." ([[s04-A34]]) Say: "Today I ... Finish the sentence with the word walk." ([[s04-A35]]) Say: "Tomorrow I ... Finish the sentence with the word walk." ([[s04-A36]]) "Tomorrow I walked" is error K.
- **2 min.** Show a picture of one small bird that hops. Say: "Add a describing word to this sentence: The bird hops." ([[s04-A37]]) "The bird small" is error L.
- **5 min.** Print each caption with a blank, read it aloud, and ask for one of the five word cards, 1 minute each. The paper star is red, ___ the paper moon is red too. ([[s04-A38]]) The box is small, ___ the lid is big. ([[s04-A39]]) Shall we make a card ___ draw a map? ([[s04-A40]]) The floor was wet, ___ we walked slowly. ([[s04-A41]]) We wear coats ___ it is cold. ([[s04-A42]]) Using or as a reason is error M.

#### 15 min. Revise

An observation: this is the revision step. Each child answers the strip with one new line from *Marks Outside*, written under the draft with its page tag (page 1 or page 2 of *Marks Outside*), 10 minutes. A child who cannot yet write it says it once to you and you write it word for word, 30 seconds each, for up to six. Then partners read the new line against the strip, 5 minutes. Recopying the draft with no new line is error N. Keep the strip, the draft and the new line in the record folder. ([[s04-A46]]) You record the focus, the response and the strengthening separately, and score it on day 152.

#### 15 min. Complete

Each child checks the four parts against the plan, 5 minutes: topic, fact, fact, closing; add what is missing. Partners read each other's paragraph aloud, touching each line, 6 minutes. Then, 4 minutes: children take out the bluebird response from day 149, reread its label and details, and add one more detail or word label to it, then file the draft and the response in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three page pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which revised page answers the peer?" ([[s04-X151a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 2 minutes. The spoken ask is heard in the rotation below. The same page copied again is error N.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Count the vowel sounds, not the letters, in paper, music, open and tulip: two in each. Clap each word in parts.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 25, lesson 127. New today: the words come, himself and together are mapped for *Reading Together*, 3 minutes: say each, point to the part that does not say its usual sound, and write it from memory. Review: an open syllable ends in a vowel that says its name (day 89). Show o-pen, pa-per, mu-sic and tu-lip, 3 minutes, and compare the first part of each with the closed part nap in nap-kin (day 150). Chain by syllable on small boards, 20 seconds a part, saying each: o-pen, pa-per, mu-sic, tu-lip, 4 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate music and paper, then "The paper is open." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The hearing first, 15 minutes, at your table: today's five children, 3 minutes each (hearing sheet). Show the held card: "Read these words" ([[s04-A43]]), 1 minute. Then: "Count the vowel sounds in each word," with the five words ([[s04-A44]]), 30 seconds. Then: "Read each word one part at a time," the five words and cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A45]]), 1 minute 30 seconds; score the reading apart from the counting. No help is given: mark each shown or not yet seen. Ai read as two sounds is error T; sailboat counted as four is error U; stopping after sun is error V. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Then three groups of 5 minutes at your table: the catch-up six first, then two groups of six with *Reading Together* read for expression, each child heard for 40 seconds (6 times 40 seconds is 4 minutes, then 1 minute for the ask and changing). The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say the line you would add to answer the peer." ([[s04-X151b]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A group not met today goes first tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the hearing sheet marks, the cycle record for day 151 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 152's opening.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the question on a scrap and say the pages aloud. One child: answer the question aloud to a grown-up, who writes the new line as said, and say which page it came from.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each line aloud.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Question.** Say: "Marks Outside says marks go on the outside of the glass, close together." **Say.** "Where do the marks go?" Expect: on the outside.
- **10 min. Add.** Say a short paragraph about windows. Your child adds one new line that answers a question you ask. **Say.** "Say the line you added." Expect: a fact about marks.
- **10 min. Words.** Say paper in two parts. Print "paper."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X151a]] [[s04-X151b]] Read a short made-up page about a book cart and a question. Your child points to the page that answers the question, and says the line they would add.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the question, the revision and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes answers a second question in another new line.

### Words and supports

Words: question, revise, detail, source. Frame: "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the revision tell you who answers a question with a new line instead of copying. Day 152 opens with a 3-minute repair for error N on a fresh page, returns the revised paragraph, then edits it and publishes it on paper and at the shared device.
