# Section 4.7 opener

## What the section does

Days 148 to 152 turn the sources into a written paragraph. Day 148 reads a model paragraph and tags each fact with its page. Day 149 plans the portrait from two pages. Day 150 drafts it, day 151 revises it after a peer question and day 152 edits and publishes it on paper, with one shared drawing device for ten children a day on days 152 to 154. Days 150 to 152 also observe the grammar items, and the foundations hearings run five children a day from day 150.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The errors, in plain words

- **Y:** Names a detail as the main topic. On 149 test which topic holds all the page's details. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **X:** Writes praise instead of informative facts. On 157 replace nice with one verified protection fact. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **W:** Records an unsupported research fact. On 157 replace it with a detail located on a named page. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **N:** Recopies without responding to feedback. On 157 answer the peer's written question with one new detail. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **I:** Uses a singular verb with a plural subject. On 157 move one bird then two and match hops to hop. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **O:** Uses a singular determiner with a plural noun. On 157 match these to two paper birds. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **AA:** Offers an unrelated reading response. On 157 return to the line before answering anew. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **R:** Counts watching as a digital contribution. On 157 add one child-directed label on the shared device. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
