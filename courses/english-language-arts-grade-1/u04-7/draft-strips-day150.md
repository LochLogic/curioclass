# Draft sheet and closing strips

A draft sheet with four numbered lines (topic, fact, fact, closing) and three closing strips to choose from: "Make glass visible." / "Windows are big." / "I like windows." One closing returns to the topic; the other two do not. A child who cannot yet write a line says it once to you and you write it word for word.
