# Day 150: Draft the window bird paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs and today's five hearing children (hearing sheet, day 1 of six). Make two held cards for your folder: boat, train, clean, day, snow, feet, seed, tie, light; and sunset, robot, bedtime, garden, sailboat with cor-ner, bird-bath, tur-nip. Never practice words. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 149's exit (error W, s04-X149a), 3 minutes, on pages made up for practice. Say: "These pages are made up for practice. Page A: A kite flies in the wind. Page B: A string holds the kite." Say the fact "A string holds the kite." Children find the page that says it, point to the line and say its page, 40 seconds, then each pair sticks a page tag on a scrap beside the fact. A child who names page A, or who writes no tag, is error W; reread both pages and find the line together. Then, 2 minutes: retrieve the four parts of the paragraph, topic, fact, fact, closing. Return each plan page and say today's goal: draft the paragraph from the plan.

#### 10 min. Read and talk

Reread *Windows Birds Can See* pages 1 and 3 aloud with their pictures, 3 minutes. Partners, 7 minutes: each says the topic and the two facts from the plan, in order, with a finger on each box, 60 seconds each, and points to the closing label without saying it. Then three children are heard, 40 seconds each, for a total of six heard across the day's asks. Pass is allowed.

#### 10 min. Language items

An observation: these are the unit's grammar items, so give no help. Each stem is said once, children whisper the answer to a partner, 20 seconds each, and you hear two children, 20 seconds each, so six are heard across the three parts. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

- **3 min.** Show a picture of a bird near a fence at Theo's house and the name card Theo. Say: "Write a caption with the words bird, Theo and Theo's." ([[s04-A25]]) A child who says the bird as a name is error H.
- **3 min.** Show a picture of one bird. Say: "Finish this sentence with hop: The bird ..." ([[s04-A26]]) Show a picture of two birds. Say: "Finish this sentence with hop: The birds ..." ([[s04-A27]]) "The birds hops" is error I.
- **4 min.** Say: "Theo looks at the birds' home." Say: "Say it again with he in place of Theo." ([[s04-A28]]) Say it again, then: "Say it again with their in place of the birds'." ([[s04-A29]]) Last: "Ask a question that uses the word anyone." ([[s04-A30]]) Their for Theo alone is error J.

#### 30 min. Draft

Each child writes four lines on the draft sheet from the plan page, with the Lights Out card in view as the model. Topic line, 5 minutes: copy the topic word from the plan page. Two fact lines, 14 minutes: set the plan's two fact strips beside each line, say a fact once, then write it with the mapped words and sound spelling, one word at a time. Keep the page tag on each line. A child who cannot yet write a line says it once to you, and you write it word for word, 30 seconds each, for up to six children. Closing, 7 minutes: pairs sort the three closing strips into goes back to the topic and does not, then each child copies the strip that goes back. Partners touch each line and read it aloud, 4 minutes. The draft leaves out where the marks go. Praise in place of a closing, "I like windows," is error X. The draft goes to you for written feedback by the end of the day.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three page pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which page shows a draft with a topic and two facts from the source?" ([[s04-X150a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 2 minutes. The spoken ask is heard in the rotation below. The page that says "I like helmets" is error X.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Clap the parts of napkin, picnic, rabbit and basket. Count the vowel sounds in each: two. Say each word in parts, then whole.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 25, lesson 126. New today: two-syllable words and the five syllable types. Say: every part of a word has a vowel sound, so count the vowel sounds to count the parts, 2 minutes. Review: you chopped nap-kin and pic-nic on day 79; divide between the two consonants. Show the five-type chart, 3 minutes, one known word each: closed (cat), open (go), silent-e (cake), vowel team (tree), r-controlled (farm). Say that today only closed parts are spelled. Chain by syllable on small boards, 20 seconds a part: nap-kin, pic-nic, rab-bit, bas-ket, 5 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate napkin and basket by syllables, then "A rabbit sits." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The hearing first, 15 minutes, at your table: today's five children, 3 minutes each (hearing sheet). With the held cards, 1 minute: "Read these words" ([[s04-A31]]). Then, 30 seconds: show sunset, robot, bedtime, garden and sailboat and say "Count the vowel sounds in each word." ([[s04-A32]]) Then, 1 minute 30 seconds: "Read each word one part at a time," the five words and cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A33]]); score the reading apart from the counting. No help is given: mark each shown or not yet seen (no shown with help). Ai read as two sounds is error T; sailboat counted as four parts is error U; stopping after sun is error V. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Then three groups of 5 minutes at your table: the catch-up six first, with the syllable type cards, then two groups of six with Night Skies read for expression, each child heard for 40 seconds (6 times 40 seconds is 4 minutes, then 1 minute for the ask and changing). The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say a closing for the draft." ([[s04-X150b]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. "I like helmets" is error X. A group not met today goes first tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the hearing sheet marks, the cycle record for day 150 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 151's opening.

### Other rooms

No printer: write four numbered lines on a plain sheet and say the pages aloud. One child: say the topic and two facts to a grown-up, who writes each line as said, then choose the closing that goes back to the topic.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each line aloud.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Say.** Draw four boxes in a column. **Say.** "Say a topic and two facts about bird-safe windows." Expect: the topic, then a fact each from the pages you heard. Write one word in each box.
- **10 min. Closing.** Say: "Windows are big. I like windows. Make glass visible." **Say.** "Which one goes back to the topic?" Expect: make glass visible.
- **10 min. Words.** Say rabbit in two parts. **Say.** "How many parts?" Expect: two. Print "napkin."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X150a]] [[s04-X150b]] Read a short made-up page about helmets. Your child points to the page with a topic and two facts from the source, and says a closing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the draft, the exit and the language items. Longer: a child who finishes adds a second closing in other words.

### Words and supports

Words: draft, topic, fact, closing, source, syllable. Frame: "The topic is ___. The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the draft tell you who writes a topic, two facts and a closing. Day 151 opens with a 3-minute repair for error X on a fresh draft, then returns each draft with one written question from a partner's read-through and asks for one new detail.
