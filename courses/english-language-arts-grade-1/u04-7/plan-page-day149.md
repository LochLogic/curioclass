# Portrait plan page

One page per child with four boxes in a column (topic, fact, fact, closing), a small page-tag box beside each fact, and a large box underneath for a drawing of a paper window with marks. Word labels only; sentences are said to a partner. The page leaves out where the marks go: that is the detail the peer question on day 151 asks for.
