# Peer question strips

A small stack of strips, each with one written question: "Where do the marks go?" "How close are the marks?" and a few others you write after a partner's read-through (what, where or how). Each strip is clipped to the child's draft. The child answers it with one new line from *Marks Outside*, keeping the page tag.
