# Lights Out model card

One large card with the four-sentence model paragraph *Lights Out* (g1-u4-lo) printed in large type and the picture of a tall building at night with half its windows dark and a few small birds. You read it aloud. It is the model for a topic, two facts and a closing. It is about lights at night, not about marks on glass.
