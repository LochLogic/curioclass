# Day 69: Say our plan and pick our words

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back the sun-poem planning pages. Lay out the three hoops, labeled time, feeling and sense, and these word cards: First, Then, At last, warm, quiet, sleepy, bright. Put up the arc chart. Pick the heard six and the five groups of six; copy day 68's marks for errors A, E, L, M and N.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From day 68's exit, error A (reversed order, 3 minutes) and error E (an invented part named as a fact, 2 minutes). Say: "These cards are made up for practice." For error A, reread the time words on the arc chart, then read "First a bird sat on a branch. Then the bird flew off." Two picture cards go under First and Then. **Ask.** "Which picture goes first?" Expect: the bird on the branch. For error E, read from page 4 of Sun Across the Sky, "Our shadows grow long again, because the sun is low." Say a plan, "The sun is low. A bird sings." **Ask.** "Which part is in the article?" Expect: the sun is low.

#### 15 min. Rehearse the plan

Review: the writing-process chart; the plan comes first, and a plan can be said aloud. Rehearse the class moon plan aloud in front of the children with First, Then and At last (3 minutes). Partners then use their planning pages (6 minutes). Each tells the plan once, pointing at the pictures (45 seconds each), then once more with the time words (45 seconds each). The listener points to the box that was told and says "yes" or asks "Which comes first?" Hear the heard six, 60 seconds each (6 minutes), while the others draw one more detail. Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Time, feeling and sense words

Review: a feeling word tells how someone feels; a sense word tells what you see, hear, touch, taste or smell, from day 39. Read Sun's Day, pages 1 to 3, aloud. Show the cards one at a time, say each, and place it in a hoop with the children: First (time), Then (time), sleepy (feeling), warm (sense, touch), quiet (sense, hearing). **Ask.** "Which hoop for bright?" Expect: sense, seeing. Partners then choose one time word, one feeling word and one sense word from the poem and write each as a word label on the planning page, copied from a card (6 minutes).

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: groups of three talk, maker, listener and asker. Ten groups of three. Turn card: "Which two events? Which ending? Which fact stays true?" Three rounds of 3 minutes, so each child is the maker once: the maker tells (1 minute), the listener says what they heard (1 minute), the asker asks one question (1 minute). Pip asks one question too. Hear three groups share (3 minutes). A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Name the ending

Read page 4 of Sun's Day aloud. Say: "At last, the poem comes to rest. The ending tells how." Each child says an ending line to a partner, such as "At last the sun sinks and the sky is quiet", 30 seconds each (3 minutes). Then each child draws it as a picture in the At last box and adds a face or a sense label (7 minutes). Walk round and hear the heard six.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the cat poem aloud and show the two strips; every child holds up one finger or two ([[s02-X69a]]). Then reread the first line of page 5 of the Chinese tale, "Zhinu was sad to go," and show two pictures, a sad face and a silver pin; one finger or two ([[s02-X69bv2]]). Then ask the heard six the time words in the cat poem ([[s02-X69c]]), 25 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say tub and tube; **Ask.** "Which word has the sound of the letter u's name?" Expect: tube. Blend /s/ /w/ /i/ /ng/, "swing". Say "bank": first sound /b/, middle sound /a/, last sound /k/. Segment "grunt" with counters: /g/ /r/ /u/ /n/ /t/, five sounds.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidation, no new pattern, whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review: -ll, s-blends, l- and r-blends, final clusters, ou and -ed said /id/ from cycles 9 to 11. Chain with the cards (4 minutes): spill, stamp, crab, lift, hunt, found, planted. Read each; spell every third on sound boxes. Pairs reread I Look Out for meaning and expression: you read a page, the pair echoes it, then each reads a page smoothly (6 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27. Dictation (8 minutes): "The sun is out." Children print a word at a time with the map; the words is and the are named aloud, and the capital T. Then each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. First (3 minutes) one repair from day 68's marks, the one most marks in this group need. Error L: read pack, add -ed, reread (s02-A32). Error M: stretch hand, mark each sound with a counter, write it (s02-A33, s02-A34). Error N: follow a phrase of I Look Out with a finger and voice it (s02-A35, s02-A36). Then each child reads one line of Sun's Day, page 1, with you, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out with a partner; chain spill, stamp and crab with letter cards; spell lift and hunt on sound boxes; read the word cards look, see, a and the to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet. Keep the planning pages for day 70.

### Other rooms

No chalk or rope: lay three paper circles on the floor. One child: tell the plan twice, place the word cards in the three circles, point at the exit strips, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No word cards: use scraps of paper.

**Skip.** The groups of three and the rotation.

- **15 min. Plan.** Your child tells their sun plan twice, then with First, Then and At last. **Say.** "What happens first? Then? At last?" Expect: two events and an ending in order.
- **10 min. Words.** Read Sun's Day aloud. Your child points to a feeling or sense word (warm, quiet).
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads lift, hunt and found and prints "The sun is out."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X69a]] You read: "First a pup ran to the gate. Then its soft nose touched my hand. Last the pup went to sleep. I feel calm." Draw run, touch, sleep and sleep, touch, run. Ask: "Point to the strip that follows the poem." Expect: run, touch, sleep. [[s02-X69bv2]] You read from the Chinese tale: "Zhinu was sad to go." Draw a sad face and a silver pin. Ask: "Point to the feeling word." Expect: the sad face. [[s02-X69c]] Ask: "Which time words tell the order?" Expect: First, Then, Last.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the rehearsal, the exit and the rotation table; cut one round of three. Longer: a child who finishes adds a third event with a time word.

### Words and supports

Words: rehearse, time word, feeling word, sense word, ending. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. At last ___." "The word ___ tells how it feels." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Any child may pass. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who points to the strip that follows the order (error A) and who picks the feeling word, not the object (error C). Days 70 and 71 are independent, so nothing is repaired in them; error A and error C wait for day 75's response. Day 70 begins the independent sun poem from the planning page.
