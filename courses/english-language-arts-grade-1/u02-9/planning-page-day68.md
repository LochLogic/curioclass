# Day 68 sun-poem planning page

A page of five boxes: event one, event two, an ending, a fact label, an invented label. Children fill the boxes with a small picture each and a word label only. Children carry the page to days 69 and 70.

The prompt is given orally: "Tell what the imagined sun sees. Use two events in order, details, time words and an ending. Mark a fact from our information and an invented detail." In a room with nothing, draw five boxes on folded paper.
