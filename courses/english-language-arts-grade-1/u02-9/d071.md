# Day 71: Finish my sun poem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep every chart down. Put each child's day 70 draft and drawing on their desk with a grid card. Seat the scribes. Copy the heard six. This is the same sitting as day 70's assessment, scored once today.

### The day

#### 35 min. Finish the poem

Each child finishes the poem from their day 70 draft ([[s02-A38]]): events, time words, an ending, and the drawing. A scribe writes what the child says, as spoken, 40 seconds a child; two scribes take 15 children each. With one adult, take the children not scribed on day 70 first. Give no feedback and no model.

#### 15 min. Reread against the five rows

Each child rereads the poem, with the scribe if needed, and points to the five boxes on the grid card: two events, order, details, time words, ending. One minute a child; give no other help.

#### 5 min. Mark the poem

Each child adds the fact pointer, a label on the sun fact, and the invented-detail label, a label on what the poet made up.

#### 5 min. Submit

Each child hands in the poem and the drawing. Score nothing in front of children.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say rid and ride; **Ask.** "Which has i's name?" Expect: ride. Blend /g/ /r/ /a/ /s/ /p/, "grasp". Say "loud": /l/ /ow/ /d/. Segment "slip": /s/ /l/ /i/ /p/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidation, no new pattern. Uncover the map. Small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread: found, loud, planted, flag, sink, hill. Each child then reads one sentence of The Changing Moon, page 2, with support.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictation (8 minutes): "Dad found a loud drum." Children print a word at a time; the capital D and a are named aloud. Each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. The group reads I Look Out, a page each (3 minutes), then spells found and loud (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out; chain lifted, planted and hunted; spell nest and king; read the word cards look, see, a and the.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the folder. After class, score each poem once on the score grid from the record of days 70 and 71: five rows, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen, and the two labels apart. A list of sun facts with no events is error F; it is not repaired until day 75.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the grid card as five boxes in a row.

### At home

**Swap.** No scribe: you write what your child says.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **30 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A38]] Your child finishes the poem and tells it to you; you write it as spoken. Your child points to five parts: two events, order, details, time words, ending. Give no hints. **Say.** "Tell me your poem and point to First, Then and At last." Expect: two events in order with details, and an ending.
- **10 min. Mark.** Your child says which part is a sun fact and which is made up.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads loud, found and drum and prints "Dad found a loud drum."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the finish and the reread. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: poem, event, ending, fact, invented. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

Nothing is given back today. Day 72 hands each poem back, and children exchange a partner question about it.
