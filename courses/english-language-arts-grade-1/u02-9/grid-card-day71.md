# Day 71 grid card and score grid

**Grid card (for each child).** Five picture boxes in a row: two events, order, details, time words, ending. The child points to each part on their own poem while rereading, with a scribe if needed.

**Score grid (teacher record).** Five rows per child, marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with one line each for the fact pointer and the invented-detail label. The poem is scored once from the whole independent record of days 70 and 71.
