# Section 2.9 opener: planning and writing the sun poem

## What the section does

Days 68 to 71 return to the sun facts and make a sun narrative poem. On 68 and 69 the class plans with the charts up; on 70 and 71 each child works alone, with no chart and no model, in one continuous sitting that is scored once on 71. Day 70 is not scored and gets no feedback.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and the work; the foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Days 70 and 71 are independent: nothing is displayed, no one helps beyond the read-aloud prompt, and a scribe writes only what the child says.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A plan has two events, an ending and two labels.** One label marks a fact from our information; the other marks an invented detail.
- **A poem is checked against five rows:** two events, order, details, time words and an ending.
- **A fact stays true.** Invented details go beside the fact, never against it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** events in the wrong order.
- **E:** a claim the sources do not make.
- **F:** a list instead of events told in order.
- **C:** an object name taken for a feeling word.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
