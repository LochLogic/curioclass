# Day 70: Begin my sun poem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Take down every chart and the moon poem; cover the sound-spelling-map until the foundations parts. Put each child's planning page and the picture pages on their desk. Seat the scribes. Copy the heard six. Day 69's marks for error A and error C are not repaired today; they wait for day 75.

### The day

#### 10 min. Source rereading

Each child looks alone at their planning page and the picture pages. Read nothing aloud. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Planning

Read the prompt aloud twice: "Tell what the imagined sun sees. Use two events in order, details, time words and an ending. Mark a fact from our information and an invented detail." Each child checks their page against it. Give no other help.

#### 30 min. Drafting

Each child draws and labels two events in order and tells the poem to a scribe, who writes it as spoken, 40 seconds a child; two scribes take 15 children each ([[s02-A37]]). With one adult, scribe the heard six today and the rest on day 71, the same sitting. One word label is the only writing the child does alone. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A list of sun facts is error F; record it, do not repair it.

#### 10 min. Drawing

Each child draws the scene to match the events. Give no feedback.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say cut and cute; **Ask.** "Which has the sound of the letter u's name?" Expect: cute. Blend /s/ /l/ /e/ /d/, "sled". Say "nest": /n/ /e/ /t/. Segment "brick": /b/ /r/ /i/ /k/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidation, no new pattern. Uncover the map. Small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread: tent, bank, wind, flag, crab, hill, found, lifted. No sample is taken.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictation (8 minutes): "The tent is soft." Children print a word at a time; the and is are named aloud. Each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. The group reads I Look Out, a page each (3 minutes), then spells tent and soft (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out; chain bank, sink and king; spell nest and hunt; read the word cards look, see, a and the.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the drafts and drawings into a folder for day 71. Move the marks to the unit 2 record sheet. Nothing is scored or handed back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw on folded paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No scribe: you write what your child says.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **30 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A37]] Say twice: "Tell what the imagined sun sees. Use two events in order, details, time words and an ending." Your child draws the events and tells you the poem; you write it as spoken. Give no hints. **Say.** "Tell me your poem." Expect: a first event and a detail.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the scene.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads tent, bank and wind and prints "The tent is soft."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the drafting and the drawing; cut the stations to two. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: poem, event, ending. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count. Read the prompt twice.

### Tomorrow

Nothing is given back. Day 71 continues the same sitting: each child finishes the poem, then checks it against five parts.
