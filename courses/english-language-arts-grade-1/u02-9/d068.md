# Day 68: Plan our sun poem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a planning page for each child. Put up the arc chart, the writing-process chart and the class moon poem. Open the shared notebook to its sun notes. Keep the cycle sample word cards sealed: kicked, filled, rested, looks, boxes, looking, faster and fastest stay out of sight until the rotation. Write the five groups of six. Copy the heard six from day 67.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From day 67's exit, error P (a detail about a different topic): the exit rows (s02-X67a) for 3 minutes and (s02-X67b) for 2. Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read: "A girl sat by a pond." A friend asks, "What was in the pond?" Show two cards, a duck on the water and a girl eating lunch. **Ask.** "Which card answers the friend?" Expect: the duck. Reread the question, then add only the detail it asks for. Partners each say one detail that answers it, 20 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Reread the sun article

Review: you heard Sun Across the Sky before. Read pages 3 and 4 aloud with the pictures. **Ask.** "What do the shadows do when the sun is low?" Expect: they grow long. Partners use the turn card, 20 seconds each, and point in the pictures to one sun fact that must stay true. Review: a source label says where a fact came from, as in our notebook. Point to the notebook's sun note and read its label, article. Say: "A fact stays true in our poem."

#### 10 min. Reread the sun poem

Review: a narrative poem tells what happened, in order. Read Sun's Day aloud, then again with the children joining. Point to the four pictures and say First, Next, Then, At last. Say: "A poet can imagine; imagined means made up." **Ask.** "Can a real sun stretch its arms?" Expect: no, the poet imagined it. Partners point to one more made-up phrase in the poem (6 children say theirs aloud).

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: partner talk with the turn card. Say the question twice: "Which two events will the imagined sun see? Which fact must stay true?" Partners point at the event pictures from the arc chart and tell each other, 90 seconds each. Pip asks one clarifying question, such as "Which comes first?" Hear three pairs. Any child may pass.

#### 20 min. Plan

Say aloud: "Tell what the imagined sun sees. Use two events in order, details, time words and an ending. Mark a fact from our information and an invented detail." Teach two labels (4 minutes). Fill a blank page from the class moon poem: its two events under First and Then, its ending line, a fact box with the word article (the moon is seen in the dark sky), and an invented box (what the moon feels). Say: "The fact pointer shows what stays true. The invented-detail label shows what the poet made up." Then each child fills the planning page (12 minutes): a small picture in each of the First, Then and At last boxes, a fact picture with the word article, and an invented picture with one word label. Hear the heard six while the rest draw, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). A page that only lists sun facts is error F: draw First and Then beside it with the child.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the model events ([[s02-X68a]]); every child holds up one finger for the first strip or two for the second. Then reread *Sun's Day*, page 2, to the heard six and ask for the sun fact ([[s02-X68bv2]]), 25 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say cub and cube; **Ask.** "Which word has the sound of the letter u's name?" Expect: cube. Blend /f/ /l/ /i/ /ng/, "fling". Say "tent": first sound /t/, middle sound /e/, last sound /t/. Segment "sink" with counters: /s/ /i/ /n/ /k/, four sounds.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 11, lesson 60, the progress sample, whole class on side 2 of the page. Teach nothing new. Write out, sand, rested and the (5 minutes, [[s02-A33]]): say each word once in a sentence and once alone; sentences: "The cat ran out." "The sand is soft." "The dog rested." "Put on the hat." Then say "dusk" once in "We see stars at dusk" and once alone (3 minutes, [[s02-A34]]); the word is not shown. Walk round and collect the pages (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27. Children print the letter pairs Ss, Hh and Tt, capital then small (2 minutes). Dictation of the sun-plan labels (3 minutes): sun, hill, tent, each under its picture. Then "The sun is up." (3 minutes), the named. Each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. The sample first (2 minutes, [[s02-A32]]): each child reads the group's pair from the cards, 20 seconds each. Pairs: kicked and filled; rested and looks; looking and boxes; faster and fastest; filled and boxes. Then the kept readings, 20 seconds each: a line of Moon's Walk (2 minutes, [[s02-A35]]) and a sentence of The Changing Moon, page 2 (2 minutes, [[s02-A36]]); prompt, never read for the child. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out with a partner; chain planted, lifted and tent with letter cards; spell hunt and sing on sound boxes; read the word cards look, see, a and the to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the sample marks and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet. Keep the planning pages in the room for day 69.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a paper into five boxes and draw the two exit strips on the board. One child: read the article and poem pages, plan with pictures, point at the exit strips, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: fold a paper into five boxes.

**Skip.** The partner talk and the rotation.

- **10 min. Poem.** Tell or read the sun poem Sun's Day. **Say.** "Which part is made up?" Expect: the sun stretching its arms, or talking.
- **15 min. Plan.** Your child draws a plan in five boxes: first, then, at last, a true sun fact, and something made up. **Say.** "Tell me your plan." Expect: two events in order, then an ending.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads tent, lifted and planted and prints "The sun is up."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X68a]] You say: "A boy puts on a coat. Later the boy goes out." Draw a coat then a door, and a door then a coat. Ask: "Point to the first thing." Expect: the coat. [[s02-X68bv2]] You read Sun's Day, page 2: "Their shadows hide beneath their feet, tiny, at midday." Ask: "Say the part that is a sun fact." Expect: the shadows are tiny at midday.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the plan, the exit and the sample; cut the poem reread to one reading. Longer: a child who finishes adds a time word label to each box.

### Words and supports

Words: plan, event, ending, fact, invented, imagine. Frames: "First ___. Then ___. At last ___." "A fact: ___. An invention: ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Any child may pass. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who points to the strip that follows the order (error A) and who names the sun fact, not the imagined part (error E). Day 69 opens with a repair for each, 3 minutes for error A and 2 for error E, then rehearses the plan. The sample marks go to day 69's rotation: error L (kick for kicked), error M (a vowel left out) and error N (only listening).
