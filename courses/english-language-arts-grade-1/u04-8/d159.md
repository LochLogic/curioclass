# Day 159: Reading, a text, a book and a story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 158's assessment is not handed back; its marks wait for day 160. Keep the check cards covered. Today's individual readings are the other fifteen children, from the hearing order of day 158; nobody is heard twice. The marks of day 147 (author reasons) and day 153 (comparison) are cited from the record sheet, not collected again. Mark the six children for the pair work from the foot of the hearing order.

### The day

#### 6 min. Set-up and moves

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is graded. You may pass any ask." Seat today's fifteen where you can call each in turn; the others sit in pairs. Mark every ask on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 9 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Three groups of five at your table, 3 minutes each, with the new words on the day 159 card: long or short ([[c2-F159h]]), blend ([[c2-F159i]]), first, middle or last sound ([[c2-F159j]]) and finger taps for plunk ([[c2-F159k]]), 40 seconds each and 20 seconds to change the group.

#### 15 min. Printing and dictation

All thirty write; only today's fifteen are marked. Say each word twice: dream ([[c2-F159l]]), would ([[c2-F159m]]) and yawn "the way it sounds" ([[c2-F159n]]), one minute each and 2 for the saying. For the next 10, a child whose all-letter sheet misses a form prints it with your written feedback; the rest finish side 2. Not a new alphabet test.

#### 30 min. Topic response

All thirty, new texts. Read the shears text aloud once with its picture, 2 minutes; children whisper to a partner, and you hear the heard six: what it is about ([[c2-T159a]]) and two things learned ([[c2-T159b]]), 3 minutes. Show the Garden Tools book card, 5 asks, 1 minute each, children point and you watch the heard six: the heading ([[c2-T159c]]), the contents page for shears ([[c2-T159d]]), what tells a word's meaning ([[c2-T159e]]), what the label points to ([[c2-T159f]]) and the icon you touch to hear the words ([[c2-T159g]]); that is 5 minutes, with 3 more to swap the six, 8 minutes in all. Last, 17 minutes: each child writes, or draws and labels, or tells a scribe a short story about Mei and her bicycle from the bicycle card: first and next ([[c2-T159h]]), a detail and a time word ([[c2-T159i]]) and the ending ([[c2-T159j]]). A scribe takes six children in 12 minutes at most; children needing one go first; a child with no piece goes on the missing-evidence list.

#### 60 min. Individual readings

Fifteen children, 4 minutes each at your table, the same order as day 158 with the day 159 cards: purpose ([[c2-F159a]]), first reading ([[c2-F159b]]), check the sense ([[c2-F159d]]), second reading ([[c2-F159c]]), five words ([[c2-F159e]]), five endings ([[c2-F159f]]) and ten mapped words ([[c2-F159g]]). A child who cannot finish in 4 minutes stops; the rest goes on the missing-evidence list. Collect the student pages at the end. The rest of the class reads the known reader and finishes side 2.

After class, mark the pieces and list who needs a second hearing. Gaps to look for: from day 158 (errors A to K and M), a detail named as the topic or another detail given (error L), a book part mixed up (error Q), events out of order (error O), no closing or an opinion (error P).

### Other rooms

One child: hear the readings, then the shears and book card asks, about 20 minutes, and skip the pair work. No scribe: the adult writes the child's words once, read back.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read a short made-up story aloud.

**Skip.** The group sounds.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read aloud: "Shears cut stems. Shears have two blades." Do not help; write what you saw.
  **Say.** "Tell me two things you learned about shears." Expect: two facts from the words, such as cutting stems and having two blades.
- **10 min. Story.** Say: "Tell me what you do first and next when you ride a bicycle." Expect: first and next in order.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say "would" and have your child write it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the readings and the story; hear the oral sounds on day 160. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second tool and labels one part.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, label, heading, glossary. Frames: "The page says ___." "First ___. Then ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count. Give one prompt only where an item says.

### Tomorrow

Day 160 uses both days' marks: you group children by the gap you recorded on days 158 and 159, teach one move and try a fresh text or word task, and fill the grade 2 handoff.

### Credit
