# Day 155: Review the portrait and finish the sound record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs and the last five hearing children (day 6 of six). Put the two large pages and the portrait display in place.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 154's exit (error S, s04-X154bv2), 3 minutes. Say: "Page 1: To wash a cup, fill the bin. Rub the cup with a cloth. Page 2: To dry a cup, take a towel." Partners each hold a page and a counter and say "My page adds ___," 40 seconds. Only "both are about a cup" is error S. Then 2 minutes: retrieve topic, two facts with pages, closing; no exit today.

#### 15 min. Supported reading

Not scored. Choral reread of two class-chosen pages, such as *Marks Outside* page 1 and *Windows Birds Can See* page 3, 4 minutes a page, then pairs reread *Reading Together*, 7 minutes.

#### 15 min. Review the portrait evidence

Review: page tag (unit 4, day 143). Each child finds one portrait fact's page, 3 minutes; partners check, 3 minutes; hear six, 1 minute each; fill portrait log gaps, 3 minutes.

#### 20 min. Finish the display

Add missing labels and tags, 7 minutes; set final sheets, 3 minutes; silent walk in two halves, 4 minutes each, pointing to a fact and its page; pairs say that page, 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Closing

Partners say what the portrait taught, 2 minutes; hear six, 30 seconds each. No marks.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Say a word, clap its parts, name its type from the chart: cupcake, paper, farmer, teacher.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Review: the five types (day 154). Pairs sort cards onto the chart, 5 minutes: napkin, paper, cupcake, seaweed, farmer, basket, music, inside; each pair spells one by part, 5 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Printing set, 5 minutes. Dictate "The sister is in the market.", 5 minutes. Not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Hearing first, 15 minutes, no help. Held card: "Read these words" ([[s04-A77]]), 1 minute; "Count the vowel sounds in each word" ([[s04-A78]]), 30 seconds; "Read each word one part at a time," with cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A79]]), 1 minute 30 seconds, scored apart. Errors T, U, V. Then three groups of 5 minutes: catch-up six, then two of six, *Reading Together*, 40 seconds a child.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, hearing sheet, portrait log, cycle record; list any missing hearing; collect the student pages, 1 minute.

### Other rooms

No printer: read from the book. One child: reread two pages and check one fact.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: say the words aloud.

**Skip.** The sorting.

- **10 min. Assessment words.** Print two-part words on paper cards, such as pan-cake and far-mer, and ask your child to read each part, then say how many vowel sounds each has. Expect: two.
- **10 min. Reread.** Reread a page of the portrait book with your child pointing. **Say.** "What does the page say people can do?" Expect: one thing from the page.
- **10 min. Portrait.** Your child finds one portrait fact and shows the page it came from.
- **10 min. Parts.** Clap the parts of cupcake and paper.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the portrait review and the hearing. Longer: a child who finishes adds a label with its page to a classmate's drawing, with permission.

### Words and supports

Words: portrait, page, fact, label, display. Frame: "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The hearing sheet now holds all thirty children. Day 156 is a silent sitting with no hint; read the choice card aloud, leave the pages on the tables and give no help.
