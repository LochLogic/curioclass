# Day 154: Compare two pages and set up the portrait display

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs, today's five hearing children (hearing sheet, day 5 of six) and today's ten for the device (children 21 to 30 on the class list). Cut the four picture cards. Set out the display. Keep the held cards in your folder and the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 153's exit (error A, s04-X153b), 3 minutes, on a card made up for practice. Say: "This card is made up for practice. Mo and Lin each plant a bean. Mo waters his bean. Lin puts her bean in the sun." Four small cards: Mo waters his bean, Lin moves her bean to the sun, Mo has a red cap, Lin has a long braid. Pairs sort the cards into two piles, what a child does and how a child looks, 90 seconds, then point to the pile that says how Mo and Lin are different and say one line, 90 seconds. You listen at three pairs. A look the card never gives is error A: ask "What does the card say they do?" Then 2 minutes: say the day's three jobs, a comparison of two pages, the last ten turns at the shared device, and setting up the display. Say: "No model comes first for the comparison."

#### 15 min. Compare two pages (observation)

An observation, unrehearsed: show no model and give no help. Review: Both ___. But ___. (unit 4, day 131). Say: "These two pages are made up for practice." Show the two large cards and read each twice, 3 minutes in all. Page A, Pouring: "To pour water into a cup, hold the cup still. Tip the pitcher slowly. Stop at the line." Page B, Mixing: "To mix a drink, take a spoon. Stir the powder in. Count to ten." Each child places the picture cards on the Both and But frame and prints a word label on each, 6 minutes. The sheet is the record for every child: the card under Both ([[s04-A71]]), the card under But ([[s04-A72]]) and a crayon ring round one line on each page card ([[s04-A73]]). Then hear six children, 1 minute each (6 minutes): say each ask once, A71, then A72, then A73 with the child pointing at the words. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Naming only what both pages share, with no difference, is error S. Collect the sheets, 1 minute.

#### 15 min. Digital contribution

An observation. The last ten children, 90 seconds each (10 times 90 seconds is 15 minutes). Each child brings a partner's paper window drawing, adds one label in the frame beside it, a word with its page tag, and says it aloud (the same record as day 153). No accounts, no names on the screen. Log name, time and what was added. A child who only watches is error R. The log is checked for each child's first complete contribution. A child who missed a turn on 152 or 153 goes after the ten if time is left, and otherwise takes a 90-second turn during the display part below while the others place their sheets. No one is held to a second conference. Score day 153's entries from the log now, not today's during the work. If the device cannot be used, the contribution is not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Prepare the display

Review: topic, two facts with their pages, closing (unit 4, day 148). Place, 5 minutes: in groups of six, one minute a group, each child sets the final sheet and the paper window drawing on the display. Say the plan, 2 minutes: topic, two facts each with its page, closing. Partners take turns, 90 seconds a child, 3 minutes for fifteen pairs at once, naming each page from the display. Then 1 minute to move, new partners and the second turn, 3 minutes. Then hear six children at the front, 40 seconds each, 5 minutes; the class names each page. Last, 1 minute: the display stays up for days 155 to 157. Content note: the pages are about paper windows only; no one touches a real window.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture shows what both pages are about?" ([[s04-X154a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 2 minutes. The box alone is error Y. The spoken ask is heard in the rotation below.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Clap the parts of farmer, sister, thunder and market, 1 minute each. Say the vowel sound and the r sound in the second part.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 25, lesson 130. New today: the r-controlled syllable, a part where r changes the vowel sound (far, ter, der, mar). Review: closed, open, silent-e (days 150 to 152) and vowel team (day 153). Read and divide far-mer, sis-ter, thun-der and mar-ket, 4 minutes, saying each and tapping the parts. Chain by syllable on small boards, 20 seconds a part, 3 minutes. Then touch the five types on the chart and say each with one known word, 3 minutes: closed, open, silent-e, vowel team, r-controlled.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate farmer and thunder, then "The sister is at the market." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The hearing first, 15 minutes, at your table: today's five children, 3 minutes each (hearing sheet, day 5 of six). Show the held card: "Read these words" ([[s04-A74]]), 1 minute. Then: "Count the vowel sounds in each word," with the five words ([[s04-A75]]), 30 seconds. Then: "Read each word one part at a time," the five words and cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A76]]), 1 minute 30 seconds; score the reading apart from the counting. No help is given. Ai read as two sounds is error T; sailboat counted as four is error U; stopping after sun is error V. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Then three groups of 5 minutes at your table: the catch-up six first, then two groups of six with *Reading Together* read for expression, 40 seconds a child; for each of the heard six who is not one of today's five, the 40 seconds is the cycle 25 sample instead: show the held card to read, take it away, then the held word to write on the small board. The five hearing children already have this record, and no second cycle assessment is made. The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds each ask:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say one way the pages are different." ([[s04-X154b]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. Change turns by pointing, 10 seconds. A group not met today goes first tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the hearing sheet marks, the contribution log, the Both and But sheets, the cycle record for day 154 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 155's opening.

### Other rooms

No device: the digital turns wait and the contribution is not yet seen; a table is the display. One child: read the two pages aloud, fill the sheet without help, then stand the portrait up and explain it with its pages.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the two pages.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Pages.** **Say.** "Two made-up pages: one tells how to hang a coat. One tells how to put a hat on a shelf. Say what both are about, then one difference." Expect: putting things away; coat on a hook and hat on a shelf.
- **10 min. Explain.** Your child says the portrait: topic, two facts with their pages, closing.
- **10 min. Words.** Say farmer in two parts. Print "far."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X154a]] [[s04-X154b]] Say: "The first page tells how to ring a hand bell. The second page tells how to put the bell away in its box." Your child points to what both are about and says one way they are different.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the comparison, the display and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes adds a third difference to the sheet and rings the words.

### Words and supports

Words: page, display, topic, both, different. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The sheets, the exit and the spoken asks tell you who names what each page adds and who names only a likeness. Day 155 opens with a 3-minute repair for error S on a fresh pair of pages, then the class rereads, reviews the portrait evidence and finishes the display; the last five children are heard.
