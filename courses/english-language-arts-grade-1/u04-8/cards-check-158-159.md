# Days 158 and 159 check cards (teacher only, unseen until the day)

Say each time: "These cards are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings, no shading. Keep the cards covered; collect every card after each child. Day 158 uses the first set (children 1 to 15 of the hearing order), day 159 the second set (children 16 to 30). Each child reads one of three passages by rotation: children 1, 4, 7, 10, 13 read prose; 2, 5, 8, 11, 14 read the poem; 3, 6, 9, 12, 15 read the information. The words use only patterns taught by cycle 25 (short and long vowel teams, igh and ie, ar, or, er, ir, ur, silent-e, two-syllable words by part) and the mapped words the, a, is, has, this, look, like, and, said, see, says, she, would, about, good, very, only, after, little, every, have, come.

## Day 158 reading passages

**Prose, "Mei on the Coast".** "Mei sits by the coast. The moon is up. A beam gleams on the foam. Mei dreams of a boat. "I can reach the moon," she says." Picture: a child on a shore at night.

**Poem, "A Bird Can".** "A bird can tweet, tweet, tweet. / A bird can creep on two small feet. / A bird can dip and sweep and speed. / Then a bird can rest and feed." Picture: a small bird on a branch.

**Information, "A Flask".** "A flask is a tool. A flask can hold tea. The flask keeps the tea hot on a trip. A flask can keep milk cold, too. A flask helps a man on a trek." Picture: a flask and a cup beside a path.

## Day 158 words (cover until the ask)

- Digraph and vowel-team words: tooth, sheep, spark, swirl, charm.
- Words with endings: raced, hiking, hottest, tamer, booths.
- Mapped words: would, about, good, very, only, after, little, every, have, come.

## Day 158 oral sounds (three groups of five)

- Long or short vowel, children hold up the long card or the short card: beet, stub, thigh, trot, groan.
- Blend and say, one word to each child: steep, skim, growl, stint, dunk.
- Say the sound asked: the first sound of tooth; the middle sound of sheep; the last sound of spark (the three words go round the group).
- Say each sound in the word with a finger tap: trench (five sounds).

## Day 158 dictation (whole class, twice each)

creep; every; crew. For crew, say "Spell it the way it sounds."

## Day 159 reading passages

**Prose, "Idris and the Bug".** "Idris sits in the yard. The moon gleams. A spark of light zips by. "It is a bug!" says Idris. The bug glows in the night." Picture: a child in a yard at night, a tiny glowing bug.

**Poem, "Parts at Rest".** "The wing goes flap, flap, flap. / The tail goes sway, sway, sway. / The beak goes peck, peck, peck. / Then the bird can rest in a tree." Picture: a bird in a tree.

**Information, "A Spade".** "A spade is a tool for the yard. A spade has a tall handle and a flat end. A man steps on the spade and digs. A spade helps a man make a pit for a tree." Picture: a spade standing in a pit by a small tree.

## Day 159 words (cover until the ask)

- Digraph and vowel-team words: brain, peach, thorn, speed, scorn.
- Words with endings: racing, hotter, tamest, coasted, swirled.
- Mapped words: would, about, good, very, only, after, little, every, have, come.

## Day 159 oral sounds (three groups of five)

- Long or short vowel: moan, vent, loaf, cob, speed.
- Blend and say: vent, hound, glint, cob, dunk.
- Say the sound asked: the first sound of thorn; the middle sound of peach; the last sound of brain.
- Say each sound in the word with a finger tap: plunk (five sounds).

## Day 159 dictation (whole class, twice each)

dream; would; yawn. For yawn, say "Spell it the way it sounds."

## Day 159 topic cards (whole class)

**Information read aloud once, "Shears".** "Shears are a tool for the garden. Shears have two sharp blades and two handles. You squeeze the handles, and the blades snip. Shears trim a hedge and cut tall grass. Shears help a gardener keep a yard neat." Picture: shears at a hedge. The picture carries three one-word labels: handles, blades, hedge.

**Tool book card, "Garden Tools".** A one-page picture of a small book. Its heading: "Garden Tools". Its table of contents: "Trowel, page 2; Shears, page 3; Pitchfork, page 4." A bold word, "pitchfork", on the page with a glossary box: "pitchfork: a tool with long points for lifting hay." A picture of a trowel with a label "blade". A printed screen with an icon of a speaker, an icon of a magnifying glass and an icon of a house, each with one word beneath it: "listen", "find", "home". Read the words aloud when asked; children point.

**Narrative card.** A picture of a bicycle whose kickstand will not stay up; beside it a small wrench and a pump. The teacher says, "This is made up. Think of the bicycle story from the start of the year: a child looked at the part first, then picked the tool. Now a different child, Mei, has this bicycle." Children draw three boxes and label or dictate.

## Notes for the teacher

The child gets the card face down, turns it, and the clock starts at the first ask. Idris is the only person in the prose; the bug does not speak.
