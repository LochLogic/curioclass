# Retry cards, day 157

Two retries reread named pages of the unit's texts; the third is a fresh card marked "invented for practice" at the foot. Use the one that matches the commonest missed component in the record.

**Reason or unrelated fact (error C, s04-X157av2).** Reread aloud page 1 of Windows Birds Can See. Show three pictures: a bird flying at a window full of reflected trees, a person looking out a window, a smiling child. Say: "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" Right answer: the bird flying at the window full of reflected trees: the glass looks like open space.

**Likeness and difference (error A, s04-X157bv2 and s04-X157cv2).** Reread aloud page 8 of Ben and the Bird and the end of page 4 of Nia Calls for Help. Show three pictures: each child sitting far from a bird, waiting; two children in blue shirts; a bird flying alone. Say: "Which picture shows how Ben and Nia are alike?" Right answer: each child sitting far from the bird, waiting for the helper. Then say: "Say one way they are different." Right answer: Ben watches another bird fly and wishes the robin could fly; Nia holds her knees and counts the clouds.

**Opinion, reason and closing (error B).** Read aloud: "A writer wrote: Share the markers. I like blue. The class page reads: Sharing means everyone gets a turn." Show three pictures: a page with the reason added, a page that repeats I like blue, a page with a blue drawing only. Say: "Which revised page shows a reason from the class page?" Right answer: the page with the reason added.
