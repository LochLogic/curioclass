# Retry cards, day 157

Three fresh cards for the retry, each marked "invented for practice" at the foot. Use the one that matches the commonest missed component in the record.

**Reason or unrelated fact (error C).** Read aloud: "A writer says, 'Wipe your feet.' Mud makes the floor slippery. The doormat is brown." Show three pictures: a child slipping on a muddy floor, a brown doormat, a child smiling. Say: "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" Right answer: the child slipping on a muddy floor.

**Likeness and difference (error A).** Read aloud: "Ida and Pilar each build a tower. Ida's tower stands. Pilar's tower tips over." Show three pictures: both children building, two tall towers, a tipped tower alone. Say: "Which picture shows how Ida and Pilar are alike?" Right answer: both children building. Then say: "Say one way they are different." Right answer: Ida's stands and Pilar's tips over.

**Opinion, reason and closing (error B).** Read aloud: "A writer wrote: Share the markers. I like blue. The class page reads: Sharing means everyone gets a turn." Show three pictures: a page with the reason added, a page that repeats I like blue, a page with a blue drawing only. Say: "Which revised page shows a reason from the class page?" Right answer: the page with the reason added.
