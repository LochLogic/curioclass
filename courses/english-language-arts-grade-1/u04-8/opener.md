# Section 4.8 opener

## What the section does

Days 153 to 157 finish the portrait and close the unit. Day 153 compares two stories and gives ten more children a turn at the shared device. Day 154 compares two pages and prepares the display. Day 155 reviews the portrait and the sound record. Day 156 holds a silent opinion transfer. Day 157 shares the portraits, takes any missing observation and gives each child a fresh retry on what the record showed. Day 154 holds the sixth cycle sample.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** Compares birds' appearance instead of characters' experiences. On 125 contrast feathers with a child calling an adult; rebuild two event maps. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **S:** Gives only a likeness in an information comparison. On 157 find what one page adds to the other. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **B:** Gives a preference without an opinion paragraph. On 140 rebuild topic, position, text reason and closure from four cards. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **Y:** Names a detail as the main topic. On 149 test which topic holds all the page's details. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **AA:** Offers an unrelated reading response. On 157 return to the line before answering anew. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **R:** Counts watching as a digital contribution. On 157 add one child-directed label on the shared device. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.

On days 155 and 156 give no hint. On day 157 a retry that is now right is marked "shown with help" and never replaces the first mark.
