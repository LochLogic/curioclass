# Opinion sheet

One printed page per child with four empty boxes in a column, labeled only with a small picture clue for each part (topic, opinion, reason, closing). No frame words, no hint. Place the sheets in the unit folder at the end of the day. A scribe takes the sentences of a child who cannot yet print them.
