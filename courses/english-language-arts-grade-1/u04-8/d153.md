# Day 153: Compare two stories and finish the device turns

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group, the pairs, today's five hearing children (hearing sheet, day 4 of six) and today's ten for the device (children 11 to 20 on the class list). Cut the six picture cards for the Both and But sheet. Keep the held cards in your folder and the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 152's exit (error I, s04-X152a), 3 minutes, on a card made up for practice. Say: "This card is made up for practice. It shows three paper kites." Children hold up fingers for how many kites, then whisper to a partner, 40 seconds each pair: a line that starts with these and says what the kites do. Pairs work at once; you listen at three pairs. "These kites flies" is error I: show one kite, then three, and say it again. Then 2 minutes: say the day's three jobs, a comparison of two stories, ten more turns at the shared device, and practice explaining the portrait. Say: "No model comes first for the comparison."

#### 15 min. Compare two stories (observation)

An observation, unrehearsed: show no model and give no help. Review: Both ___. But ___. (unit 4, day 124). Say: "These two stories are made up for practice." Read each twice, 2 minutes in all. Story A: "Remy waits in line for the water fountain. The line moves and soon he drinks." Story B: "Ruth waits in line for the water fountain. The bell rings and the line is still long." Hand out the sheets and the six picture cards (Story A: waits, line moves, drinks; Story B: waits, bell rings, line still long). Each child sorts the cards onto the Both and But frame and prints one word label on each card they place, 7 minutes. The sheet is the record for every child: the card under Both ([[s04-A64]]), the card under But ([[s04-A65]]) and one event from each story ([[s04-A66]]). Then hear six children, 1 minute each (6 minutes): say each ask once, A64 first, then A65, then A66. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A look the stories do not give ("both have brown hair") is error A. Content note: these stories hold no bird and nothing sad.

#### 15 min. Digital contribution

An observation. Ten children, 90 seconds each (10 times 90 seconds is 15 minutes, so the next child stands beside the device before the last one finishes). Each child brings a partner's paper window drawing, adds one label in the frame beside it on the shared page, a word with its page tag, such as marks and Marks Outside, and says it aloud ([[s04-A70]]). No accounts, no names on the screen. You stand at the device and log name, time and what was added. A child who only watches is not logged and is error R. The children from day 152 are not repeated. The rest finish their paper window drawings on the final sheet. Score today's entries from the log on day 154, not while the children work. If the device cannot be used, no record is made and the contribution is not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Rehearse an explanation

Review: page tag (unit 4, day 143) and the topic, two facts and closing of day 148. Each child picks two facts from their portrait paragraph and prints a word label with its page tag on the small board, 4 minutes, using *Windows Birds Can See* pages 1 and 3 and *Marks Outside* page 1. Say the frame once: "The page says ___." Partners take turns, 90 seconds a child (60 seconds to explain with the drawing, 30 seconds for the partner to name the page), 3 minutes for fifteen pairs at once. Then new partners and the second fact, 3 more minutes, after 1 minute to move. Each child speaks twice. Then hear six children at the front, 45 seconds each, 5 minutes; the class names the page each time. Close with 2 minutes: a thumb up when a partner named the right page. No grammar or revision conference today.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture shows how Zara and Hugo are alike?" ([[s04-X153a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file their folders, 2 minutes. Two square puzzle boxes is error A. The spoken ask is heard in the rotation below.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Clap the parts of playground, teacher, seaweed and raincoat, 1 minute each. Say the vowel sound you hear in each part.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 25, lesson 129. Review: the vowel-team syllable, a part where two letters make one vowel sound (unit 4, day 131), the five syllable types chart (day 150) and the vowel teams ay, ea, ai and oa (cycles 21 and 22). The new work today is reading and spelling two-syllable words whose second or first part is a vowel team. Read and divide play-ground, teach-er, sea-weed and rain-coat, 4 minutes. Say each, tap the parts, then touch the team that makes the vowel. Chain by syllable on small boards, 20 seconds a part, 4 minutes. Read the sentence "A teacher is on the playground." together and check that it makes sense, 2 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate seaweed and raincoat, then "A teacher is on the playground." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The hearing first, 15 minutes, at your table: today's five children, 3 minutes each (hearing sheet, day 4 of six). Show the held card: "Read these words" ([[s04-A67]]), 1 minute. Then: "Count the vowel sounds in each word," with the five words ([[s04-A68]]), 30 seconds. Then: "Read each word one part at a time," the five words and cor-ner, bird-bath and tur-nip ([[s04-A69]]), 1 minute 30 seconds; score the reading apart from the counting. No help is given: mark each shown or not yet seen. Ai read as two sounds is error T; sailboat counted as four is error U; stopping after sun is error V. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. Then three groups of 5 minutes at your table: the catch-up six first, then two groups of six with *Reading Together* read for expression, each child heard for 40 seconds (6 times 40 seconds is 4 minutes, then 1 minute for the asks and changing). The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds each ask:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say one way they are different." ([[s04-X153b]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A group not met today goes first tomorrow.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the hearing sheet marks, the contribution log, the Both and But sheets, the cycle record for day 153 and the exit marks. The exit marks go to day 154's opening.

### Other rooms

No device: the digital turns wait and the contribution is not yet seen; those ten finish their paper drawings. One child: read the two stories aloud, sort the cards on the sheet without help, then explain two portrait facts and name their pages.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the two stories.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Stories.** **Say.** "Two made-up stories: Ana waters a plant and it grows. Bo waters a plant and it wilts. Say how they are alike, then different." Expect: both water a plant; one grows and one wilts.
- **10 min. Explain.** Your child shows a drawing and says one fact with "The page says ___."
- **10 min. Words.** Say playground in two parts, then print "rain."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X153a]] [[s04-X153b]] Say: "Zara and Hugo each put a puzzle together. Zara's is done. Hugo's is not done yet." Your child points to a picture of both at puzzles and says one way they are different.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the comparison, the exit and the hearing. Longer: a child who finishes adds a second likeness or difference and names the event.

### Words and supports

Words: alike, different, both, but, page, label. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The sheets, the exit and the spoken asks tell you who names what each child does and who names a look. Day 154 opens with a 3-minute repair for error A on a fresh card, then the last ten children go to the device and the portraits go on display.
