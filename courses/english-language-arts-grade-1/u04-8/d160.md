# Day 160: Reteach from the year-end check and hand off

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 159's assessment and day 158's assessment are not handed back as papers; use the marks. Sort the marks from day 158 and day 159 by the gap recorded for each child, one list per gap: a long vowel or r-controlled word, a two-syllable word, fluency, a book part, an opinion, a word part. At most ten children in a group. A child with no gap reads earlier books. Put the missing-evidence list at the front of your folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Set-up and records

Say: "Today you may try again. A try that is now right is marked shown with help. The first mark stays." Seat the groups. Mark every ask: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 40 min. Reteach groups

Each group comes to your table for 8 minutes in turn, five groups at most, and the rest work in pairs on earlier books. One move for one gap, one minute: long vowel, underline the team and say its sound; two-syllable word, clap, cover one part, read each, then the whole; fluency, echo a line, then read it together; book part, four cards, you name a job and the child holds the card; opinion, the frame "I think ___ because ___"; word part, lock beside unlock. The other 7 minutes the group practises the move on known words. One child speaks at a time, and a child that did not speak goes next.

#### 20 min. Fresh retry

Each group comes back for 4 minutes with its card from the retry card sheet. Long vowel group: read the words ([[c2-X160a]]). Two-syllable group: read and clap ([[c2-X160b]]). Fluency group: read the passage ([[c2-X160c]]). Book part group: point on the book card ([[c2-X160d]]). Opinion group: say an opinion with a reason ([[c2-X160e]]). Word part group: say what un- does ([[c2-X160f]]). Hear one child at a time at 40 seconds each; a group of ten takes two turns. A child who cannot finish stops; the rest goes on the missing-evidence list. Mark each: shown with help or not yet seen; the first mark stays.

#### 25 min. Reread earlier texts

Pairs and small groups reread Reading Together and earlier books they know, then draw a favourite part and label it. You walk and hear the children on the missing-evidence list for one minute each.

#### 20 min. Words and spelling

Small groups by gap, 10 minutes each in turn: sound-by-sound spelling with counters for four short words, then a letter for each; a cycle word read twice. No new pattern. Everyone else prints the dated all-letter sheet.

#### 10 min. Records and handoff

File the retry marks, the missing-evidence list and the check marks of both days in the unit 4 record. Write each child's one remaining need on the grade 2 handoff sheet: what is shown, what is shown with help, what is not yet seen.

### Other rooms

One child: one retry card on the missed part, then stop. No printer: write the six cards by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No retry card: use a made-up short list of words.

**Skip.** The group moves.

- **10 min. Retry.** Show five new words such as float, steep, bright, snail and burnt, then sunset, napkin, cobweb and bedtime.
  **Say.** "Read each word, then clap the parts." Expect: each word read by its sounds, with the parts clapped.
- **10 min. Read.** Read this once: "Jo has a kite. The wind lifts it high in the sky." Your child reads it back.
- **15 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X160a]] [[c2-X160b]] [[c2-X160c]] [[c2-X160d]] [[c2-X160e]] [[c2-X160f]] Use the card for the part your child missed. For the opinion, ask "Which digs a hole, a spoon or a spade? I think ___ because ___." For the word part, ask what un- does to lock.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reteach and the retry. Longer: a child with no gap reads a favourite book to a partner.

### Words and supports

Words: try, again, part, opinion. Frame: "I think ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The year ends. Today's records go to the grade 2 handoff.
