# Day 158: Year-end reading and spelling check, first fifteen children

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the check cards covered. Put the thirty children in the fixed hearing order and mark the first fifteen for today; the other fifteen are heard on day 159. Nobody is heard twice. Give each child a long card and a short card. Copy from the records of day 149 and day 155 which children still miss a cycle entry: they are heard first. Nothing from day 157 is given back.

### The day

#### 6 min. Set-up and moves

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is graded. You may pass any ask." Seat today's fifteen where you can call each in turn. The other fifteen go to their tables with the reader and the student page. Mark every ask on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 9 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only, no print. Three groups of five at your table, 3 minutes each. In each group: hold up long or short ([[c2-F158h]]), 40 seconds; blend one word each ([[c2-F158i]]), 40 seconds; the first, middle or last sound, one each ([[c2-F158j]]), 40 seconds; tap the sounds of trench, each child in turn ([[c2-F158k]]), 40 seconds; 20 seconds to change the group. Children with a missing cycle entry go in the first group.

#### 15 min. Printing and dictation

All thirty write; only today's fifteen are marked. Say each word twice and give one minute to write it: creep ([[c2-F158l]]), every ([[c2-F158m]]), and crew "the way it sounds" ([[c2-F158n]]). That is 5 minutes with the saying. For the next 10, a child whose dated all-letter printing sheet misses a form prints it now with your written feedback; the rest print on side 2. This is not a new alphabet test.

#### 30 min. Known-text reading and written tasks

All thirty. Pairs read Reading Together, a page each, then swap, then draw and label a tool on the student page. You hear no one for a mark. Walk and listen to the six children named at the foot of the hearing order, one minute each, for the supported-reading record only.

#### 60 min. Individual readings

Fifteen children, 4 minutes each at your table: 3 minutes for the asks and 1 minute to change the child (point to the next). Each child reads the passage for their place in the order (prose, poem or information). In order: purpose ([[c2-F158a]]) 15 seconds; first reading ([[c2-F158b]]) 45; check the sense ([[c2-F158d]]) 15; second reading ([[c2-F158c]]) 45; five words ([[c2-F158e]]) 20; five endings ([[c2-F158f]]) 20; ten mapped words ([[c2-F158g]]) 20. Give no hint beyond the stuck word. A child who cannot finish in 4 minutes stops; mark what was heard and put the rest on the missing-evidence list for the catch-up group, never into a squeezed turn. Collect the student pages at the end. Everyone else keeps reading the known reader and finishes the student page.

After class, from the marks, list who needs a second hearing and which gaps recur: a word read from the picture (error A), a word by word reading (error B), a misread left (error C), a changed vowel (error D), a dropped ending (error E), a mapped word sounded out (error F), the wrong card for a vowel (error G), a dropped sound (error H), the wrong place of a sound (error I), a spelling short of a sound (error J), a mapped word spelled as it sounds (error K), an answer from home (error M).

### Other rooms

One child: hear the whole battery in about 14 minutes and skip the groups. No printed cards: copy each card by hand, large.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: say the words aloud and write them yourself.

**Skip.** The group sounds.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Write five short words, such as chain, feet, barn, spot and creep, and have your child read each. Do not help; write what you saw.
  **Say.** "Read the word." Expect: each word read by its sounds.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say "creep" and have your child write it, then say the sounds as they write.
- **10 min. Reread.** Your child reads any known book once and tells you the part they liked.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the readings and the dictation; hear the oral sounds on day 160. Longer: a child who finishes reads a second passage aloud for fun, unmarked.

### Words and supports

Words: read, again, sound, spell. Frame: "I read to find out ___." Pointing, a scribe and a home language count where the ask allows. Give one prompt only where an item says.

### Tomorrow

Day 159 hears the other fifteen children on the same asks with new cards, and the whole class answers new questions about a text, a book and a story. Day 158's marks go to day 160 with day 159's.
