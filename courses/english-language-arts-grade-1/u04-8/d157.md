# Day 157: Share the portrait and fix what the record showed

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the six sharing groups of five. Make the three repair groups from the record (ten children each at most): the day 147 reason (error C), the day 153 comparison (error A) and the day 156 opinion (error B). A child who missed none keeps to the share and the display. Put the missing-evidence list in your folder (five children at most).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Say the day's order: share, finish any missing observation, then repair and retry. Say that a retry that is now right is marked shown with help and never replaces the first mark. A child may pass at any time.

#### 20 min. Share in six groups of five

Review: share in groups of five (unit 3, day 106). Say the plan, 2 minutes: show the portrait and say one sentence with its source page. Each child shows the paper portrait and says one sentence with its page, 60 seconds, and the group names the page, 30 seconds: 5 children times 90 seconds is 7 minutes 30 seconds, so the groups finish together. Move from group to group and note who was heard. Then 4 minutes: show the shared page of window labels on the device or the printed copy and let each group say one label and its page. Then 4 minutes: groups swap and walk the display in silence, each child pointing to one classmate's fact and its page. Last, 2 minutes: thanks and put the portraits in the folder. 

Check: each sentence names a page of *Windows Birds Can See* or *Marks Outside* that holds the fact; ask the group to point to it in the source.


#### 15 min. Missing observations

Take only what fits a 3-minute turn from the missing-evidence list, five children at most: a missing hearing (the nine words, the five-word count and the part reading, the same words and key as day 155) or a missing device turn (90 seconds, the same record as day 153). A missing sitting for the opinion sheet needs 20 minutes and is left not yet seen. No new reading, grammar, opinion or digital conference is held. The rest read familiar books or add to the display.

#### 20 min. Return and repair, then retry

One response allocation for the unit. Review: Return and repair (unit 4, day 140). First, 3 minutes: form the three groups and hand back day 156's assessment, the opinion sheet, and day 153's comparison sheet. Then 9 minutes: each group comes to your table for 3 minutes while the others work a repair card in pairs.

- **Error C group (a fact that does not explain the point).** Show "A writer says, 'Put the cups away.' The cups are blue. Cups on the floor can break." Children sort the two facts: which one explains the point. Say each reason with "because."
- **Error A group (a look in place of what a character does).** Show "Kai and Dee each carry a bucket. Kai carries it to the shed. Dee sets hers down by the door." Children point to what each child does and set aside the cards about how they look.
- **Error B group (a liking in place of an opinion).** Four loose cards: a topic, an opinion, a reason and a closing about snack time. Children lay them in order and say the name of each.

Then 8 minutes for the retry, which is the day's exit: each group comes back for 2 minutes in turn, 6 minutes. Read its card once from the retry card sheet and children mark a picture on their page. Error C group: "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" ([[s04-X157a]]). Error A group: "Which picture shows how Ida and Pilar are alike?" ([[s04-X157b]]), then "Say one way they are different," whispered to a partner ([[s04-X157c]]). Error B group: "Which revised page shows a reason from the class page?" ([[s04-X157d]]). Hear two children in each group. Mark each: shown with help or not yet seen; the first mark stays. Last, 2 minutes: collect the pages.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Segment and blend words from the five syllable types, clapping the parts: basket, paper, inside, farmer. 1 minute each, 1 minute to change.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidate cycles 19 to 25 in small groups by the cycle record; no new pattern. Read the matched decodable and divide long words, 5 minutes, then spell two words by part, 5 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate "A rabbit is in the basket." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the cycle record names: map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, *Reading Together* and the matched decodable, heard one child at a time, 40 seconds each (6 times 40 seconds is 4 minutes, then 1 minute for changing; change by pointing). The heard-six column names them. The rest reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

File the sheets, the heard-six column, the cycle record and the retry marks. Put in the unit record: the hearing sheet, the contribution log, the portrait folder, the opinion sheets and the response record. Score from the kept work: the paragraph on day 150 and 151 and the bluebird response from days 149 and 151 and the source notes if day 152 left them unscored.

### Other rooms

No device or printer: share the paper portrait and keep a missing device turn not yet seen. One child: share the portrait with a grown-up, then one retry card on the missed part, and stop.

### At home

**Swap.** No retry card: use a made-up pair of stories.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child shows the portrait and says one sentence with its page.
- **10 min. Retry.** Read aloud: "Ida and Pilar each build a tower. Ida's tower stands. Pilar's tower tips over." **Say.** "Which picture shows how they are alike?" Expect: both children building. **Say.** "Say one way they are different." Expect: one stands and one tips over.
- **10 min. Words.** Say basket in two parts. Print "paper."
- **5 min. Reread.** Reread *Reading Together* once.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X157a]] [[s04-X157b]] [[s04-X157c]] [[s04-X157d]] Use the retry card for the part your child missed, or the Ida and Pilar card. Your child points to how Ida and Pilar are alike and says one way they are different.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the share and the retry. Longer: a child who is not retrying adds a label with its page to the display.

### Words and supports

Words: share, page, repair, retry. Frame: "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The unit's records are filed. Day 158 is a check: you collect the cumulative reading record once and combine it with the cycle 19 to 25 record.
