# Day 160 retry cards (made up for practice)

Say each time: "These cards are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Use only the card for the child's recorded gap; hear each child once.

**Card 160a, words.** float, steep, bright, snail, burnt. For children whose gap was a long vowel or r-controlled pattern.

**Card 160b, two-syllable words.** sunset, napkin, cobweb, bedtime. For children whose gap was a two-syllable word.

**Card 160c, passage.** "Jo has a kite. The wind lifts it high in the sky. Jo runs and runs. The kite dips and rises. Jo is very glad." Picture: a child running with a kite. For children whose gap was fluency.

**Card 160d, book card.** A picture of a small book. Contents: "Moon, page 2; Stars, page 3; Sun, page 4." A picture of a star with the label "point". For children whose gap was a book part.

**Card 160e, opinion.** Pictures of a spoon and a spade beside a small hole in the dirt. For children whose gap was an opinion.

**Card 160f, word part.** Two word cards, lock and unlock, with a picture of an open lock and a closed lock. For children whose gap was a word part.

Repair moves, one minute each: long vowel, underline the team and say its sound; two-syllable word, clap, cover one part, read each, then the whole; fluency, echo a line, then read it together; book part, four cards, the teacher names a job and the child holds the card; opinion, the frame "I think ___ because ___"; word part, lock beside unlock.
