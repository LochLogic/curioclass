# Day 156: Write an opinion about helping birds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the three pages on every table and the opinion sheets face down. Name the catch-up six and four groups of six. Plan the scribe.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Show the five children heard on day 155 their hearing marks, 1 minute: day 155's assessment handed back, nothing taught. Read the choice card aloud once: "Our class can make a paper window-pattern message or a nesting-space message. Which should we explain first?" Say it is made up for practice, the sitting is silent, no hints, and a child may pass. Review: topic, opinion, reason, closing (unit 4, day 136); no model.

#### 10 min. Read

Hand out sheets, 2 minutes. Reread *Windows Birds Can See* pages 1 and 3 and *Nests Need Space* page 3 once each, no comment, 4 minutes. Silent looking, 3 minutes; 1 minute for the rule. Birds are shown only from far away or in adult care.

#### 20 min. Opinion transfer (observation)

Say: "Name the topic, say what you think, give a reason from a protection page and close." Each child fills four boxes ([[s04-A80]]); no frame, help or hint; either choice counts if the reason matches. Children print by sound. A scribe (you or a helper, up to ten children at 2 minutes each) writes word for word; any other child without a sheet goes on the missing-evidence list. "I like blue" is error B. Mark: shown, shown with help (scribe only), not yet seen.

#### 20 min. Finish the portrait

Finish labels and tags, 8 minutes. Partners practise what they will say on day 157, 90 seconds a child, 3 minutes; move 1 minute; one more turn, 3 minutes. Display and silent reading, 5 minutes. Review: day 148.

#### 5 min. Closing

Collect sheets and folders into the unit folder, 3 minutes. Make the missing-evidence list, 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Segment and blend, clapping: cupcake, rabbit, teacher, farmer.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Small groups by the cycle record; no new pattern. Matched decodable, divide long words, spell two words by part.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Printing set with written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate "The farmer sits inside.", 5 minutes. Not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Catch-up six first, 10 minutes. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each, *Reading Together* and the matched decodable, 40 seconds a child, changing by pointing. The rest reread and spell in pairs. Nothing is scored.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, cycle record, marked sheets, missing-evidence list.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw four boxes by hand. One child: read the card, leave the pages open, scribe if asked.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: use the pages on a library or class copy at a later time.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **15 min. Assessment: opinion.** This comes first. Read aloud: "Our class can make a paper window-pattern message or a nesting-space message. Which should we explain first?" Read two pages aloud. **Say.** "Say your topic, what you think, a reason from the page, and a closing." Give no hint. Expect: a choice with a matching reason.
- **10 min. Portrait.** Your child says the portrait with its pages.
- **10 min. Words.** Say rabbit in two parts. Print "inside."
- **10 min. Reread.** Reread *Reading Together* once.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sitting and the portrait. Longer: a child who finishes draws the nest or window and labels it.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, opinion, reason, closing, page. No frame is given today. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

Day 157 begins with the share, then the missing-evidence list and the single response. Bring the opinion sheets, the portrait log and the record. Repair groups form from the day 147, 153 and 156 records.
