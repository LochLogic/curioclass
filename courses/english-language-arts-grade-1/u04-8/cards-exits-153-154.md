# Exit cards, days 153 and 154

These exits use named pages of the unit's texts: reread those pages aloud and show the pictures listed. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 153 pages (s04-X153av2, RL.1.9)

Reread aloud page 15 of Ben and the Bird and the end of page 5 of Nia Calls for Help. Show three pictures: each child beside a picture of calling a grown-up, two children in green shirts, an empty gate. Say: "Which picture shows how Ben and Nia are alike?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Each child beside the picture of calling a grown-up: both know to call a grown-up next time.

## Day 153 pages (s04-X153bv2, RL.1.9)

The same two pages stay open; reread them if the child asks. Say: "Say one way they are different." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Ben picks up his shovel to dig in the garden; Nia smiles as the dove goes with the helper.

## Day 154 pages (s04-X154av2, RI.1.9)

Reread aloud page 5 of Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes and page 3 of Feeders Need Adult Care. Show three pictures: a bird at a nest box beside a bird at a clean feeder, a nest box alone, a coat hook. Say: "Which picture shows what both pages are about?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The bird at the nest box beside the bird at the clean feeder: people helping birds.

## Day 154 pages (s04-X154bv2, RI.1.9)

The same two pages stay open; reread them if the child asks. Say: "Say one way the pages are different." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** One page is about nest boxes that give birds places to raise their young; the other is about a feeder that helps only if someone cares for it.
