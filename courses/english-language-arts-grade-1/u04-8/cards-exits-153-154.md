# Exit cards, days 153 and 154

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 153 card (s04-X153a, RL.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Zara and Hugo each put a puzzle together. Zara's puzzle is done. Hugo's puzzle is not done yet." Show three pictures: both children working at puzzles, two square puzzle boxes, a finished puzzle alone. Say: "Which picture shows how Zara and Hugo are alike?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Both children working at puzzles.

## Day 153 card (s04-X153b, RL.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "Zara and Hugo each put a puzzle together. Zara's puzzle is done. Hugo's puzzle is not done yet." Say: "Say one way they are different." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** "Zara's puzzle is done, and Hugo's is not done yet."

## Day 154 card (s04-X154a, RI.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "The first page tells how to ring a hand bell. The second page tells how to put the bell away in its box." Show three pictures: a child holding a hand bell, a box alone, a coat hook. Say: "Which picture shows what both pages are about?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** A child holding a hand bell.

## Day 154 card (s04-X154b, RI.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "The first page tells how to ring a hand bell. The second page tells how to put the bell away in its box." Say: "Say one way the pages are different." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** "One page is for ringing the bell and one is for putting it away."
