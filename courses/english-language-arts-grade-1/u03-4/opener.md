# Section 3.4 opener: feather research and a first paragraph

## What the section does

Days 90 to 94 ask a research question a provided page can answer, gather source-labelled notes from two articles and a picture the class observes, and jointly build a short paragraph with a topic, facts and a closing. The class also finds a planted wrong fact by checking against its notes.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A research question is one a provided page can answer.**
- **A note keeps its source.** A small picture, one word and a label are enough.
- **A paragraph names its topic, gives facts and ends.** A liking is not a fact.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail is named as the topic. Sort under one topic again.
- **F:** the paragraph stops with no ending. Add a closing that returns to the topic.
- **M:** a vowel is left out of a spelling. Stretch and map every sound.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
