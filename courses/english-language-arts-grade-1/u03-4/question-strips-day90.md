# Day 90 question strips

Four strips, each with a picture and a printed question starter: beak, "How does ___ help the bird eat?"; feather, "How does ___ help the bird stay warm?"; wing, "How does ___ help the bird move?"; foot, "How does ___ help the bird hold on?" A child copies one strip and fills the blank with a word from the board. The research-cycle chart (ask, find, note, share) stays up.
