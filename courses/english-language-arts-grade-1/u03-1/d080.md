# Day 80: What the picture shows and what the words tell

## Teacher page

### Before class

Color the sparrow's head brown on the class copy of page 2. Put out the eye and ear signs, the pointer cards and the repair cards, and the exit pictures face down. Copy the day 79 exit marks from the record sheet and name today's heard six. Day 79's exit is repaired in the first part: for those children, today's repair card is the fresh case.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show a topic card, "A rain coat", and two detail cards, "keeps you dry" and "has a hood". Say the repair for error A from day 79's exit, in which a detail was taken for the topic. Put "has a hood" on top as if it were the topic and ask: "Is this the whole page, or one fact about it?" Move it down. The class sorts both details under the topic and says: "This is about a rain coat. One detail is it keeps you dry." Children who need the repair stand near you; everyone says it. Nobody is named.

#### 8 min. Reread page 2 of Beaks Find Food

Read the sparrow paragraph from page 2 aloud: "A sparrow has a thick, strong beak. The beak cracks hard seeds. Then the sparrow eats the food inside." Show the diagram beside it, a sparrow's head with a line to the word beak. Review: a label is a word written beside a part of a drawing, from kindergarten. Say the new word: diagram, a drawing that shows the parts of something. Children point to the label "beak" on the diagram. Say: "The label tells the name of the part." Children say "label" and "diagram" with you. A child follows with a finger while you read it a second time.

#### 12 min. Picture facts and word facts

Review: in unit 1 you used the eye for what a picture shows and the ear for what the words tell. Hold up the eye sign beside the brown sparrow in the diagram and the ear sign beside the sparrow paragraph. Say: "The sparrow is brown. Is that in the picture or in the words?" Expect: the picture. Say: "It cracks hard seeds. Is that in the picture or in the words?" Expect: the words. Say the wrong sentence aloud and ask: "Brown is in the article." **Ask.** "Is that right?" Expect: no; brown is in the picture; cracks seeds is in the words. A child who says brown is in the article has taken a picture fact for a words fact (error C).

Pairs get the two-column sheet and four pointer cards you read aloud: "the heron's feathers are gray", "the sparrow is on a fence", "the heron stands still in shallow water" and "the hawk tears meat". They look at the four-head picture on page 1, sort each card under the picture symbol or the word symbol, and say which. Walk among the pairs for six minutes; hear two pairs say a card and its column at the end. Expect: the gray feathers and the fence are picture only; standing still and tearing meat are words only.

#### 10 min. Three questions

Pairs ask and answer these three spoken questions, one at a time, with two turns each; read each twice.

1. "What does the beak help the sparrow eat?" Expect: hard seeds.
2. "What does only the words tell you?" Expect: it cracks hard seeds, then eats the food inside.
3. "How do the picture and the words together explain feeding?" Expect: the picture shows the beak; the words tell what it does.

Fifteen pairs take two turns of about 20 seconds each. Hear four pairs, one minute each, as the others rest their voices.

#### 20 min. Draw and label

Hand out the draw-and-label page. Say: "Draw one bird head from the article. Copy the label beak from the board onto the beak." Children draw for 14 minutes, then copy the label. A scribe or you hears six children, one minute each, in the last six minutes: each says one words-only fact, such as "It cracks hard seeds", and you or a scribe write it under the drawing. Beside each fact the child puts the eye or the ear sign. Look for: the label sits on the beak; the fact comes from the words; the symbol matches. The word beak is copied, not read from sound spellings. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child who gives the bird's color as the fact has used the picture (error C).

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud the part of Beaks Find Food headed Flat Beaks for Mud. The others add color to their drawing; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the duck picture, drawn green and brown, with three labels on it: beak, wing and foot. Say: "Point to the labeled part these words tell about." ([[s03-X80av2]]) Then, with the page in view: "What do the words tell you that the picture does not?" ([[s03-X80bv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the part once), not yet seen. A child who says it is green has given a picture fact (error C).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say mad and made; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: made. Blend /f/ /l/ /a/ /t/, "flat". Say drip and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /d/, /i/, /p/. Segment trunk: /t/ /r/ /u/ /n/ /k/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 12, lesson 62. First map the irregular word was: write it, say it, and show that the a says /u/ and the s says /z/; children trace it in the air and say it. The words a and the are already mapped. Then connect the spellings to words: chop each word on the board between its two middle consonants and read the parts: basket (bas | ket), kitten (kit | ten), magnet (mag | net), hidden (hid | den). Say: "Each part has one vowel sound." Children clap and read each with you. Spell two words: say kitten, clap, chop, and children print the parts on their page. The sound-spelling-map stays up. Today the reader A Sunset Picnic is read for the first time in the rotation, now that its words are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Children decode, then spell, two words from the chain: basket and hidden. Print the pairs K k and B b. Dictate: "A kitten is in a basket." a, is and in are mapped words. Say it whole, then a word at a time; children stretch each word, print it and read it back. Walk round for a missing vowel (error M).

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first: the six children the record names meet you to map, blend, read, spell and reread. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, with the reader A Sunset Picnic: each child reads one or two pages and spells one chain word, 50 seconds each, one child at a time. The heard-six column names the six whose reading you write up in full. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread A Sunset Picnic, chain basket, kitten, magnet and hidden with letter cards, spell a word on sound boxes, read the words a, is, the and was. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Keep the draw-and-label pages with the label, fact and symbol marks.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the bird head and the two columns on scrap paper and read the article from the page. One child: reread the sparrow paragraph, sort the four cards, draw and label, and do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed article: read the sparrow paragraph from a book about birds. No signs: draw an eye and an ear on paper.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **10 min. Picture and words.** Read a short bird book page. **Say.** "Is that in the picture or in the words?" Expect: colors and where the bird sits are in the picture; what the bird does is in the words.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws a bird head, copies the word beak on its beak, and tells one fact from the words.
- **5 min. Words.** Clap and chop kitten and basket, then print "A kitten is in a basket."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X80av2]] [[s03-X80bv2]] Read the part of Beaks Find Food headed Flat Beaks for Mud. Your child points to the part, then tells what the words say.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sort, the draw-and-label and the exit. Cut the second question. Longer: a child draws the second picture fact beside the first.

### Words and supports

Words: label, diagram. Frames: "The picture shows ___." "The words tell ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say what only the words tell. If many pointed to the label wing or gave a picture fact such as the color (error C), day 81 opens with a five-minute repair of day 80's exit: mark a picture symbol or a word symbol beside each source on a fresh pair. Day 81 then uses a contents and a heading to find a beak fact.
