# Day 79 topic card and detail cards, beak pairs

Two sets for the main-idea-details picture. Set one (modelled by you): topic card "A sparrow's beak" with a picture of the sparrow's head; detail cards "thick and strong" and "cracks hard seeds". Set two (pairs): topic card "A duck's beak" with a picture of the duck's head; detail cards "flat" and "strains mud and water". Each detail card has a small picture. Children place two detail cards under the topic card and say "This is about ___. One detail is ___."
