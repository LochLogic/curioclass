# Section 3.1 opener: the bird-body question and four articles

## What the section does

Days 79 to 83 open the unit's question: how do a bird's body parts help it survive? The class hears four short articles, one on beaks, one on wings, one on feathers and, in the next section, one on feet. Children name a topic and its key details, tell what only the picture shows from what only the words say, and use a contents page, a heading, a label and a glossary to find a fact. Day 83 holds the first cycle sample in the rotation.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A topic is what every detail is about.** Say "the topic" for the one thing, and "details" for the facts about it.
- **A picture and the words do different jobs.** A color can be only in the picture; what a part does is in the words.
- **A contents page leads to a heading, and the heading leads to the fact.** The cover only names the book.
- **Talk has rules.** Listen with care, one speaker at a time, ask when confused.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail is named as the topic. Sort two details under the topic card again.
- **C:** a picture fact is taken for a words fact. Mark a picture symbol or a word symbol beside each source.
- **D:** the cover is used to find a fact. Follow the contents to the page, then the heading, with a finger.
- **L:** two sounds are counted as one. Move one counter for each sound.
- **M:** a word is written with a vowel missing. Stretch it and map every sound.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
