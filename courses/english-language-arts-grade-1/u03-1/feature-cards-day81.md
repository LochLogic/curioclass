# Days 81 to 83 text-feature cards

Four large cards taken from Beaks Find Food, Wings in Motion and Feathers Protect: a contents entry ("Strong Beaks for Seeds, page 2"), a heading, a diagram label ("beak") and a glossary line ("crack: to break open."). Each card has a small icon. Pairs use them to say what each feature finds. On day 83 add the glossary line for down and the label for wing feathers from Feathers Protect.
