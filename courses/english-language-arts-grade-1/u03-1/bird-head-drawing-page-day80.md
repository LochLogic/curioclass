# Day 80 draw-and-label page

A page with a large empty frame and a line at the foot. Each child draws one bird head from the article, copies the label "beak" from the board onto the beak, and tells one words-only fact for you or a scribe to write. A picture symbol or word symbol is marked beside each fact.
