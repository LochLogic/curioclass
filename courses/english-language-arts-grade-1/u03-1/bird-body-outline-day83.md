# Day 83 bird-body outline

A plain line outline of a bird body with a wing, a chest and a tail. Each child copies two labels from the board, "down" and "wing feathers", onto the right places. The glossary line for down ("soft feathers that lie close to a bird's body", as the article's glossary says) and the diagram of Feathers Protect stay up for the class.
