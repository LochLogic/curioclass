# Exit pictures, days 79 to 83

Every exit here rereads a named part of the unit's own texts (texts/g1-u3-01); this sheet holds only its pictures. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except a label a picture names. Children do not read them; you read the passage aloud.

## Day 79 pictures (s03-X79av2, RI.1.2)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 2, Hooked Beaks for Meat (day 79 works the sparrow and duck parts).

Reread aloud the part of Beaks Find Food headed Hooked Beaks for Meat, page 2. Show three pictures: a hawk's hooked beak, a small bit of meat, a pile of seeds. Say: "Point to the picture this part is mostly about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The hawk's hooked beak.

## Day 79 pictures (s03-X79bv2, RI.1.2)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 2, Hooked Beaks for Meat (day 79 works the sparrow and duck parts).

The same part stays in view. Say: "Tell me two things this part says about the hawk's beak." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** It is hooked; it tears meat into small bits.

## Day 80 pictures (s03-X80av2, RI.1.6)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 2, Flat Beaks for Mud (day 80 works the sparrow part and its diagram).

Reread aloud the part of Beaks Find Food headed Flat Beaks for Mud, page 2. Show a duck drawn green and brown, with three labels on it: beak, wing, foot. Say: "Point to the labeled part these words tell about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The label beak.

## Day 80 pictures (s03-X80bv2, RI.1.6)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 2, Flat Beaks for Mud (day 80 works the sparrow part and its diagram).

The same part stays in view. Say: "What do the words tell you that the picture does not?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The duck dips its beak in the mud; the beak strains the mud and water out and keeps bits of food.

## Day 81 pictures (s03-X81av2, RI.1.5)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 1 contents and page 2, Long Beaks for Fish (day 81 routes the seed, mud and hawk facts).

Show page 1 of Beaks Find Food and read its contents aloud. Show three pictures: the cover, page 2 with the heading Long Beaks for Fish, page 3 with the glossary. Say: "Point to where you look to find the fish fact." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Page 2, the heading Long Beaks for Fish.

## Day 81 pictures (s03-X81bv2, RI.1.5)

Passage: Beaks Find Food, page 1 contents and page 2, Long Beaks for Fish (day 81 routes the seed, mud and hawk facts).

The contents stays in view. Say: "How does the contents help you?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The entry Long Beaks for Fish tells the page, page 2, and leads to the heading.

## Day 82 pictures (s03-X82av2, RI.1.2)

## Day 82 pictures (s03-X82bv2, RI.1.2)

Passage: Wings in Motion, page 2, Wide Wings Glide (day 82 sorts details under the whole article's topic; this part's own topic is not worked).

The same part stays in view. Say: "Tell me two things this part says." Hear the day's rotating six.

## Day 83 pictures (s03-X83av2, RI.1.5)

Passage: Wings in Motion, page 3 glossary line wing, and the page 2 diagram labels wing and feathers (day 83 works down, trap and the Feathers Protect labels).

Read aloud the Wings in Motion glossary line: "wing: the part of a bird that moves to push air." Show its diagram, a sparrow seen from above, with two labels: wing, feathers. Say: "Point to the label for the part the glossary tells about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The label wing.

## Day 83 pictures (s03-X83bv2, RI.1.5)

Passage: Feathers Protect, page 1, the sentence about cold, wind and rain, and the page 3 glossary line protect (day 83 works down and trap).

Reread aloud page 1 of Feathers Protect: "Feathers protect a bird in cold, wind and rain." Say: "What does protect mean? Use the glossary." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** To keep safe: feathers keep a bird safe in cold, wind and rain.
