# Exit cards, days 79 to 83

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 79 card (s03-X79a, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A hummingbird has a long, thin beak. It drinks nectar, a sweet drink inside flowers." Show three pictures: a hummingbird with its long beak, a flower alone, a pile of seeds. Say: "Point to the picture the card is mostly about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The hummingbird (with its beak).

## Day 79 card (s03-X79b, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Tell me two things the card says about the hummingbird." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** It has a long, thin beak; it drinks nectar from flowers.

## Day 80 card (s03-X80a, RI.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A swan dips its neck under the water to reach plants." Show the swan drawn white, with three labels on it: neck, wing, foot. Say: "Point to the labelled part that the card tells about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The label neck.

## Day 80 card (s03-X80b, RI.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "What do the words tell you that the picture does not?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The neck reaches plants under the water.

## Day 81 card (s03-X81a, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Page 1 contents: Legs, page 2. Page 2 heading: Running Legs. The words under the heading: An ostrich has long, strong legs. They help it run fast. The cover shows the whole ostrich." Show three pictures: the cover, page 2 with the heading Running Legs, page 4 with the glossary. Say: "Point to where you look to find the leg fact." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Page 2, the heading Running Legs.

## Day 81 card (s03-X81b, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "How does the contents help you?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** It tells the page, and leads to the heading.

## Day 82 card (s03-X82a, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A penguin uses its wings to swim underwater. It cannot use them to fly in the air." Show three pictures: a penguin's wings, a swimmer in water, a penguin's foot. Say: "Point to the picture the card is mostly about." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The penguin's wings.

## Day 82 card (s03-X82b, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Tell me two things the card says." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** It uses its wings to swim underwater; it cannot use them to fly in the air.

## Day 83 card (s03-X83a, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A tail feather has a stiff middle shaft. Thin branches spread from the shaft. Glossary: shaft, the stiff middle part of a feather." Show a drawing of a feather with three arrows: one to the middle stem, one to the thin branches, one to the tip. Say: "Point to the arrow that shows the shaft." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The arrow to the middle stem.

## Day 83 card (s03-X83b, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Where is the shaft? Use the glossary and the words." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** In the middle, with thin branches spreading from it.
