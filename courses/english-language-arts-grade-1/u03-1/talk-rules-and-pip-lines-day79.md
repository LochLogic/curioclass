# Day 79 talk-rules card and Pip's lines

The talk-rules card has three pictures and three lines you read aloud: "Listen with care." "One speaker at a time." "Ask if you are confused." Pip shows a turn one line at a time: "I will wait until you finish." "I will add to your idea." "What do you mean by that?" Pairs use two linked turns and one clarifying question on "How does a beak help a bird?" Practice only; nothing here is scored.
