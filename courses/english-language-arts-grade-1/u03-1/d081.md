# Day 81: Find a beak fact with the contents and a heading

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print Beaks Find Food with its cover, contents, page 2 and page 3, and put the contents on the board. Lay out the text-feature cards, the Bird Wings heading card, the repair pair and the exit pictures face down. Copy day 80's exit marks and name today's heard six. Today's repair is the commonest miss on day 80's exit.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This is made up for practice." Show the picture of a red bucket and read: "A bucket holds water." Say the repair for error C from day 80's exit, in which a picture fact is taken for a words fact. **Ask.** "Which fact is only in the picture, and which is only in the words?" Expect: red is in the picture; holds water is in the words. Children put the eye sign beside the picture and the ear sign beside the sentence, and say it: "Red is in the picture. Holds water is in the words." Say each once more as a class.

#### 8 min. Words and reread

Show each word with its picture or a finger on the printed page, say it, and have the class say it: page, part, locate (find where something is), explain (tell how or why), select (choose), connect (join one thing to another), heading, contents. About 15 seconds a word. Then reread page 1 and page 2 of Beaks Find Food aloud, children following with a finger. Say: "Where is the seed fact?" Hold the question; the next part answers it.

#### 15 min. Contents and heading, taught

Show the printed contents on the board. Model with a finger, aloud: "I want the seed fact. The contents says Strong Beaks for Seeds, page 2. It tells me the page. I turn to page 2. I look for the heading Strong Beaks for Seeds. The fact is under the heading: The beak cracks hard seeds." Show the heading card feature: a heading is the words at the top of a part that tell what it is about.

Review: a label names a part of a picture, from yesterday. Say: "The contents leads to a page. The heading leads to the fact." Show the contents card and the heading card beside the label card; children say "contents, heading, label" with the icon. Contrast: the cover shows the book's title and picture; the contents tells the route.

Pairs have the contents page. Say: "Find the mud fact. Put a finger on the entry. Then go to page 2, then the heading, then the fact." Walk among the pairs for six minutes: each finger moves entry, page, heading, fact. Expect: Flat Beaks for Mud, page 2, then "The duck dips its beak in the pond mud." Hear three pairs say the route, one minute each. A child who goes to the cover first has used the cover instead of the contents (error D): put the cover away and start again from the contents.

#### 10 min. Three questions

Pairs ask and answer these spoken questions, two turns each, read twice.

1. "Where is the seed fact?" Expect: page 2, under Strong Beaks for Seeds.
2. "What does the contents tell you to do next?" Expect: go to the page, then find the heading.
3. Show the heading card that reads Bird Wings, as a title only. "Why is it the wrong place to look for a beak fact?" Expect: it is about wings, not beaks.

Fifteen pairs take two turns of about 20 seconds; hear four pairs, one minute each.

#### 17 min. Draw a route

Hand out the route sheet: four boxes in a row joined by arrows, contents, page, heading, fact picture. Say: "Find the hawk fact in the article. Draw the four steps." The hawk fact is under Hooked Beaks for Meat on page 2. Children draw for 12 minutes: the contents entry, page 2, the heading, and the hooked beak. They copy one word label, heading or page, from the board. In the last five minutes hear six children trace it with a finger and say it, 50 seconds each. Look for: the four steps in order; a finger on each; the label copied correctly. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Show page 1 of Beaks Find Food and read its contents aloud. The others finish their route drawing; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the three pictures: the cover, page 2 with the heading Long Beaks for Fish, page 3 with the glossary. Say: "Point to where you look to find the fish fact." ([[s03-X81av2]]) Then, with the contents in view: "How does the contents help you?" ([[s03-X81bv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the contents once), not yet seen. A child who points to the cover has used the cover (error D).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say hop and hope; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: hope. Blend /s/ /p/ /i/ /n/, "spin". Say glad and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /g/, /a/, /d/. Segment plan: /p/ /l/ /a/ /n/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 12, lesson 63. Build and take apart words with the cycle's spellings. Lay letter cards for nap and kin, push them together to make napkin, and pull them apart; read the parts, then the word. Do the same for rabbit (rab, bit), sunset (sun, set) and cactus (cac, tus). Say: "In each part the vowel is short and a consonant closes it." Children clap each word and say the vowel sound in each part. Then each child writes napkin part by part on their page. The sound-spelling-map stays up.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the pairs H h and R r. Dictate: "A rabbit has a basket." Say it whole, then a word at a time. Children stretch each word and mark every sound with a dot, print it and read it back. Repair for a missing vowel (error M): say the word slowly again and have the child tap each sound. has is a mapped word.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first: the six children the record names meet you to map, blend, read, spell and reread. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, with A Sunset Picnic: each child reads a page, then spells napkin or cactus on a card, 50 seconds each, one child at a time. The heard-six column names the six whose reading you write up in full. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread the reader, build napkin, rabbit and cactus with letter cards, spell a word on sound boxes, trace a route on the contents. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Keep the route sheets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the contents and page 2 on the board and read the article from the page. One child: do the finger route for the seed and the mud fact, draw the route, do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed article: use the contents of any short non-fiction book. No signs: draw an eye and an ear.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **10 min. Contents and heading.** Open a book with a contents. **Say.** "Which page has the fact about ___? Find the heading." Expect: your child follows the entry to the page, then to the heading.
- **10 min. Draw a route.** Four boxes: contents, page, heading, fact. Your child draws each step.
- **5 min. Words.** Clap and chop napkin, then print "A rabbit has a basket."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X81av2]] [[s03-X81bv2]] Show the contents of Beaks Find Food. Your child points to where to find the fish fact and says how the contents helps.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the finger route, the exit and the route sheet. Cut the third question. Longer: a child finds the heron fact by the same route and draws it.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, page, locate. Frames: "The contents says ___." "I find it under ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who follows the contents to a heading. If many pointed to the cover (error D), day 82 opens with a five-minute repair of day 81's exit: follow a fresh contents entry to its heading with a finger, the cover put away. Day 82 then reads Wings in Motion and retells its details.
