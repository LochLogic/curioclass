# Day 79 feeding-sentence cards and beak drawings

Eight teacher-made cards for pairs. Four plain line drawings of a bird head, close up, each with a different beak: thick and short, flat and wide, long and thin, curved at the tip. Four sentence cards you read aloud: "It cracks hard seeds." "It strains mud and water." "It catches a fish." "It tears meat into small bits." Pairs match each sentence to a drawing, then say why. The drawings carry no printed words.
