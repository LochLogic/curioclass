# Day 82 wing detail cards

One topic card with a picture of a flying sparrow and the words "Wings help a bird fly". Four detail cards, each with a small picture: "push the air", "quick turns", "glide", "stay in one place in the air". The class sorts two under the topic and pairs sort the other two. A wrong card for the contrast: a single feather picture beside "the article is about a feather".
