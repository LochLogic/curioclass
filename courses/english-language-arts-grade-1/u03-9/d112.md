# Day 112: Draw a fact from each page and say where it came from

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 111's exit marks and name today's heard six, the six catch-up children and the pairs for the talk. Put each child's two pages and choice in front of them. Copy the two model pages: Feathers for Water (Feathers Protect, label F) and Feathers (Duck at the Pond, label DP). Make the repair page below. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 111's exit (error D, and s03-X111bv2; or error B, and s03-X111av2). Use the commoner. Say: "This page is made up for practice." Show a page whose contents line reads "Duck Feet, 2. Duck Feathers, 4." and a cover with a duck picture and a title only. Read page 4 once: "Duck Feathers. Down feathers lie close to the duck's body. Down helps keep the duck warm." All children at once, point. **Ask.** "Which line sends you to the fact that down keeps the duck warm? Point to it." Expect: the contents line Duck Feathers, 4. Follow it with a finger to page 4 and read the sentence. If the slip was error B, **Ask.** "Does this page say the duck flies? Say yes or no." Expect: no. Put a finger on the last word of the sentence: the page stops there.

#### 8 min. Reread

Review: a note shows where its fact is with an arrow (day 110). Read page 1 of each of the four birds once, about 15 words each, from the page sets: "Strong Beak, page 2. A sparrow has a thick, strong beak. The beak cracks hard seeds." and the duck, heron and hawk pages the same way, two minutes. Children listen for their own bird. Then all at once, with a partner, each points on page 1 to a fact that could be drawn, three minutes. Hear six children, thirty seconds each: "Show me the fact you will draw." Expect: a finger on a sentence that names a part or what the part does.

#### 12 min. Drawing from one source

Review: an annotated drawing has a drawing, a word label and a source (day 87); a label is checked in three steps, read it, follow its line, find the words in the sentence (day 109); a source label says where a fact came from (day 110). Model, three minutes, on Feathers for Water, label F (Review: read on day 83): "A duck has feathers that keep water out. The duck stays dry in the pond." Draw a duck on water and one thin line to its back. Print feathers at the end of the line and F beside it. Say: "The page says feathers, so I label feathers. It does not say beak, so I label no beak." Check the label in the three steps. Then each child, five minutes, draws one fact from page 1 on the student page: a part, a thin line, a word label copied from the page and the source label (P1, D1, H1 or K1) beside it. Last, four minutes: partners check each label in the three steps, then each child fills a note strip for the fact, the source label in the box and the arrow on the line.

#### 20 min. A second source

Model, four minutes. Review: a second page adds to the first (day 99). Read Feathers, label DP: "Down feathers lie close to the duck's body. Down helps keep the duck warm." Add a second line to the model duck's chest, label down, DP beside it. Say: "The first source gave feathers that keep water out. The second adds down, and what down does." Then each child, twelve minutes, finds a fact on page 2 (P2, D2, H2 or K2), adds that part to the drawing with a line, a word label and the source label, and fills a second note strip. Read page 2 aloud again for any child. Hear six children, one minute each, inside the twelve: **Ask.** "Show me where the second page says it." Expect: a finger on the sentence, then on the label. A child who draws a part the page does not give has made error B; point to the exact sentence first. Last, four minutes, each child shows a partner the sentence for every label.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn card, pairs from different tables, all at once, three minutes, then six pairs heard, fifty seconds each, five minutes (two minutes to set up): "What does the second source add to the drawing's explanation?" Expect: "It adds the toes, and what they do." Review: listen, restate ("You said ___"), then reply (day 107). A child may pass; the partner then points to the second label.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet. **Ask.** "Gather one fact for your note from source Y1: draw it on your page and label it Y1." All children draw (2 minutes) ([[s03-X112a]]). Then, with the card in view, the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each: **Ask.** "Which source says where the crane looks for food? Say Y1 or Y2." ([[s03-X112b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who draws the crane's wings has chosen a part the sources do not state (error B). A child who says Y1 has taken the source that names the legs for the one that names the water. Name day 112's misses for day 113's repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say hope and hoped, use and used, note and noted; what do you hear added? Blend skate: /s/ /k/ /a/ /t/, long a. Give the first, vowel and last sound of joke. Segment baked.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 18, lesson 94: the three sounds of -ed. Review: -ed can say /t/ and /d/ (unit 2, days 53 and 54), and the e drops before -ed (days 109 and 111). New today: after a base that ends in t or d, -ed says /id/ and adds a part. Lay three columns, /t/, /d/ and /id/. Sort the word cards by the sound heard, all at once on desks, three minutes: baked, hoped, hiked and joked under /t/; used, named and smiled under /d/; hated, skated and noted under /id/. Say each word as it goes down and clap its parts: hated has two, baked has one. Map and decode two from each column. Spell hated and named, about 40 seconds a word. Reread A Day at the Lake for expression.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print Y y and Z z, two pairs, dated; the page goes in the separate folder kept for 118. These are the last two pairs: the record now holds all 52 forms (every form was taught by day 27; feedback written after class). Dictate: "Kate skated and smiled." Say it whole, then a word at a time; Kate is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Kate the last e is silent, and in skated and smiled the e of the base is dropped. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names: map, blend, read, spell and reread; today hope, hoped, name, named. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, 50 seconds each, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. Each child reads one page of A Day at the Lake, then spells one word, hoped or skated. The rest reread A Day at the Lake in pairs for expression, sort the -ed cards by sound and chain note, noted, name, named with letter cards, in pairs at stations. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six. Write each miss in the cycle record.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the drawing marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Put each drawing, note strip and route card in the child's riddle folder. List by name the children who chose a part the sources do not state (error B), for day 113.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages on paper and read each aloud. One child: draw one fact from each page of the chosen bird and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No pages: use any two short bird pages and call them page 1 and page 2.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **10 min. One fact.** Read page 1 of a short bird page. Your child draws one fact with a word label and the page. **Say.** "Show me where the page says it." Expect: a finger on the sentence.
- **10 min. A second page.** Read page 2. Your child adds one more fact, labeled, to the drawing.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say hope, hoped, note, noted. Your child says what was added.
- **5 min. Print.** Y y, Z z and "Kate skated and smiled."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X112a]] [[s03-X112b]] Read the short card the page shows. Your child draws one fact from the first source and labels it, then says which source tells where the bird looks for food.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the first drawing, the exit and the second fact; skip the talk. Longer: a child adds a third fact from the model pages to a second drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: drawing, label, source, page, second. Frames: "The page says ___." "My label is ___." "You said ___. I think ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the drawing a source states and name the source that tells where the bird looks for food. A child who pointed to the crane's wings (error B) gets a five-minute repair on fresh sources on day 113. Day 113 sorts each child's four notes under topic cards and turns two of them into clues.
