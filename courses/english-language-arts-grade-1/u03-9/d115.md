# Day 115: Check the topic, the details and the six routes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the Feathers Protect pages out of sight. Take down every card and chart. Name the heard six. No teaching, no repair, no help. Day 114's misses (error D) wait for day 116.

### The day

#### 5 min. Setup

Say: "This is a check." Hand out the student page and, face down, the Feathers Protect pages and the printed menu. Read Feathers for Flight, page 2, once (not worked in class). Show three pictures: the wing feathers, a branch alone, the bird's feet. Say: "Mark the picture that this part is mostly about." ([[s03-A27v2]]) Children mark one.

#### 15 min. Retell

Feathers for Flight stays in view. Call children one at a time, 30 seconds each: "Tell me the two things this part says." ([[s03-A28v2]]) The others draw any bird quietly. Mark: shown, not yet seen. Record error A for a detail named as the topic and error B for a fact the part does not give.

#### 40 min. Six-feature trace in Feathers Protect

Children turn the pages and the menu over. Say: "On your sheet, trace the six routes to the flight fact: the contents entry, the heading, the label, the glossary line, the menu choice and the icon." ([[s03-A29v2]]) Ten minutes alone on paper. Then six groups of five at your table, 5 minutes each, one minute a child: each shows one route with a finger while the rest finish. Shown: all six routes end at the words that carry the fact. Otherwise not yet seen; record error D for a cover-only route.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only, familiar words. Say hid and hide; the child says which has the long vowel sound. Blend /f/ /l/ /a/ /g/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of rides. Segment baked.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Familiar words: hoped, named, skated, nicer and safest. No repair.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictate "Duke ate a plum." Children tap, print and read it back. Then print D d and P p. No repair.

#### 30 min. Rotation

No catch-up group on a check day. Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table, one minute a child: read one page of A Day at the Lake and spell one familiar word. The rest reread and spell in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the sheets. Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet; list each miss by name for day 119.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages. One child: do each item aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: read Feathers Protect from its page.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment, topic and details.** Read Feathers for Flight once, with no help. **Say.** "What is it mostly about? Tell me two things it says." Expect: the main topic, then two details the page gives.
- **15 min. The assessment, the routes.** Your child points to the contents entry, the heading, the label and the glossary line, with no help.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "Duke ate a plum."
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says how the contents led to the page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling and the traces. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, label, glossary, menu, icon. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who can name the topic, retell two details and trace six routes alone. Each miss waits for day 119: error A, error B or error D. Day 116 opens with the day 114 repair (error D).
