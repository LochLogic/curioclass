# Day 115: Check the topic, the details and the six routes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep card C out of sight. Take down every card and chart. Name the heard six. No teaching, no repair, no help. Day 114's misses (error D) wait for day 116.

### The day

#### 5 min. Setup

Say: "This card is made up for practice. This is a check." Hand out the student page and, face down, the card C pages. Read card C once: "A nightjar has brown patterned feathers. The pattern helps the bird blend in where it rests." Show three pictures: the patterned feathers, a branch alone, a bird in flight. Say: "Mark the picture that the card is mostly about." ([[s03-A27]]) Children mark one.

#### 15 min. Retell

Card C stays in view. Call children one at a time, 30 seconds each: "Tell me the two things the card says." ([[s03-A28]]) The others draw any bird quietly. Mark: shown, not yet seen. Record error A for the place named as the topic and error B for a fact the card does not give.

#### 40 min. Six-feature trace on C

Children turn the pages over. Say: "On your sheet, trace the six routes to the feather facts: the contents entry, the heading, the label, the glossary line, the menu choice and the icon." ([[s03-A29]]) Ten minutes alone on paper. Then six groups of five at your table, 5 minutes each, one minute a child: each shows one route with a finger while the rest finish. Shown: all six routes end at the words that carry the fact. Otherwise not yet seen; record error D for a cover-only route.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only, familiar words. Say hid and hide; the child says which has the long vowel sound. Blend /f/ /l/ /a/ /g/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of rides. Segment baked.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Familiar words: hoped, named, skated, nicer and safest. No repair.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictate "Duke ate a plum." Children tap, print and read it back. Then print D d and P p. No repair.

#### 30 min. Rotation

No catch-up group on a check day. Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table, one minute a child: read one page of A Day at the Lake and spell one familiar word. The rest reread and spell in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the sheets. Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet; list each miss by name for day 119.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages. One child: do each item aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: use a short bird page with a contents line, a heading, a label and a glossary line.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment, topic and details.** Read a short bird page once, with no help. **Say.** "What is it mostly about? Tell me two things it says." Expect: the main topic, then two details the page gives.
- **15 min. The assessment, the routes.** Your child points to the contents entry, the heading, the label and the glossary line, with no help.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "Duke ate a plum."
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says how the contents led to the page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling and the traces. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, label, glossary, menu, icon. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who can name the topic, retell two details and trace six routes alone. Each miss waits for day 119: error A, error B or error D. Day 116 opens with the day 114 repair (error D).
