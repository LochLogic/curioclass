# Exit cards, days 112 to 114

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 112 card (s03-X112a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Source Y1: A crane has long legs. Source Y2: A crane looks for food in shallow water." Say: "Gather one fact for your note from source Y1: draw it on your page and label it Y1."

**Right answer:** A drawing of the long legs, labeled Y1.

## Day 112 card (s03-X112b, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Which source says where the crane looks for food? Say Y1 or Y2." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Y2.

## Day 113 card (s03-X113b, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "An avocet has a long beak that curves up. It sweeps the beak side to side to find food." Say: "Say the first clue of a riddle, without the bird's name." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** I have a long beak that curves up.

## Reading exits of days 113 and 114

These exits are read from the unit's texts and have no card. Day 113 (s03-X113av2): Fluffing Up in Feathers Protect, with the feathers and wing topic cards as the two pictures. Day 114 (s03-X114av2, s03-X114bv2): Wings Push the Air in Wings in Motion; the two pictures are its contents line Wings Push the Air, page 2, and its glossary line wing.
