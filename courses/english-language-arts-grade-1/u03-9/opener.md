# Section 3.9 opener: gathering, organizing and verifying, and the observation check

## What the section does

Days 112 to 114 turn the expert-bird facts into clues: a drawing from one source, a second source added, notes sorted under topic cards, each clue found again by a feature route. Day 115 is an observation check on a fresh card.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Days 112 to 114 end with a five-minute exit read aloud to you; you hear the day's six children. Day 115 gives no help and no exit. A child outside the six is "not yet heard". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A clue is a verified fact with the name held back.**
- **A second source adds to the first.**
- **Verifying means finding the fact again by a route.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail named as the topic. Sort again.
- **B:** a fact no source gives. Point to the sentence.
- **D:** the glossary or cover used to find a fact. Follow the contents and heading.
- **E:** a liking in place of a fact. Say a fact with the name held back.
- **N, O:** a reversed letter form, or an irregular word sounded as regular. Model it again.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
