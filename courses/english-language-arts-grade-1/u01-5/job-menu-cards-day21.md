# Day 21 job menu cards

Three invented picture cards. A child points to the job the class could help. Under each picture print one word. You read it aloud.

| Card | Word | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | roll | Pencils rolling off the edge of a desk onto the floor. |
| 2 | carry | A child walking to the rug with an armful of pencils, some dropping. |
| 3 | reach | A glue stick on a high shelf and a child stretching up toward it. |

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.
