# Paragraph-frame cards, funnel (days 21 to 24)

Four cards for each pair, one sentence on each, printed in a column. A child reads each sentence with you, then puts the cards in order. Leave the last line of each card empty for one word label.

| Card | Sentence | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Topic | A funnel is a tool for pouring. | ______ |
| Fact | Its wide top catches the rice. | ______ |
| Fact | Its narrow end fits the jar. | ______ |
| Closing | The funnel helps us pour. | ______ |

**Picture:** a small funnel above a jar, rice falling through it, drawn on the topic card only.
