# Day 21 fresh teller card

Two panels for the opening repair. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice."

**First panel:** "I am Pia. My paint jar falls over. Blue paint runs on the table."

**Second panel:** "Mr. Cole brings a cloth. 'Hold the jar,' Mr. Cole says. I hold it. He wipes up the paint."

**Pictures:** first panel, a girl and a tipped jar with blue paint on a table; second panel, a man with a cloth wiping the table while the girl holds the jar upright.

Ask: "Who is telling this story?" **Right answer:** Pia (Mr. Cole speaks inside the marks). Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.
