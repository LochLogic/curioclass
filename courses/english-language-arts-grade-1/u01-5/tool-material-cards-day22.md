# Day 22 tool and material word cards

Six word cards and two heading cards for each pair. A child sorts the words under the headings and says why.

| Heading card | Word cards |
|---|---|
| tool | hammer, ladle, hoe |
| material | wood, dirt, dough |

Print each word large, with a small picture above it: a hammer, a ladle with a deep bowl, a hoe, a plank of wood, a handful of dirt, a ball of dough. The heading cards carry the word and a picture of one hand holding something (tool) or a heap of the stuff (material).
