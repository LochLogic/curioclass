# Exit cards, days 21 to 24

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Every child points to or marks a picture; you hear the day's rotating six.

## Day 21 card (s01-X21, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "Our paper scraps scatter. A tray with low sides holds things in." Show three pictures. Ask: "Which job could a tray with low sides help with?"

**Pictures:** (1) paper scraps in a neat pile inside a low tray; (2) a cat asleep on a chair; (3) three books standing in a row.

**Right answer:** The scraps staying together. A child who gives a reason: the low sides hold things in.

## Day 22 card (s01-X22, W.1.2)

Read aloud, four sentences: "Our box holds chalk. Its sides keep pieces together. Its lid keeps dust inside. The box helps us tidy." Ask: "Point to the closing sentence and say it."

**Picture:** a box of chalk with its lid beside it; the four sentences printed in a column, one to a line, so a child can point.

**Right answer:** The box helps us tidy.

## Day 23 card (s01-X23, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "A tray's sides stop balls rolling away. A paper handle helps lift the tray." Ask: "What does the handle do?"

**Picture:** a tray with a paper handle on top and two balls in it.

**Right answer:** It helps lift the tray.

## Day 24 card (s01-X24, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "A paper pocket holds cards. Tape joins its sides. Its open top lets cards slide in." Ask: "How does the open top help the pocket?"

**Picture:** a paper pocket with an open top and a card halfway in.

**Right answer:** Cards slide in.
