# Section 1.7 opener: a written explanation, a check, and a talk

## What the section does

Days 28 to 33 take the paper tool to a finished piece. Children write a topic sentence, then facts, then a closing sentence, revise after a partner's question, edit capital letters and ending marks, and submit. Four rotations of observation (days 28 to 31) finish the foundations record. Day 32 is a check day. Day 33 is a group discussion, a share, and a response time.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. On days 29 and 30 you hear three groups of five on word work for 6 minutes each, 18 minutes in all, while the others draft quietly. Day 32 has no teaching: you read Cards C and D aloud once, then hear six groups of five while the rest answer on paper. Day 33 starts the discussions early, before the midpoint of the day, then the share, then a response in which each child retries one item on a fresh card.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The topic sentence names the tool.** "My ___ is a tool for ___."
- **Two facts, each from the model or an article.** The picture alone does not count.
- **A closing sentence says the job.** It does not repeat a fact.
- **A discussion builds.** One speaks, one adds, one asks for clarity.

## The errors, in plain words

- **AB:** the tool is not named. **AC:** no closing. **AA:** no facts.
- **A and C:** a detail not on the card, or one only the picture shows.
- **AD, AE, AF, AG:** interrupting, an unrelated turn, changing the subject instead of asking, ignoring the answer.
- **The foundations letters (G to T, AH) and the word letters (U to Z):** recorded as they show. The repair for each is on day 33.

## Marking

Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may pass. Each child's note is a next step, not a grade. The W.1.2 score sheet scores the explanation once: topic, two accurate facts, closing, each 0 or 1.
