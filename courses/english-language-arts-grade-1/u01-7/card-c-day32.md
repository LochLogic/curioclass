# Card C (day 32)

Read aloud once to everyone. For item s01-A44 only; s01-A42v2, s01-A43v2, s01-A51v2 and s01-A52v2 use the tractor paragraph of Tools on the Farm. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

Print in a column: "A ramp slopes up to a platform. A worker rolls a barrel along the ramp. The ramp helps the barrel reach the high platform."

**Picture:** a ramp with green sides beside a brick wall, a platform at the top, a barrel on the ramp and a worker beside it.
