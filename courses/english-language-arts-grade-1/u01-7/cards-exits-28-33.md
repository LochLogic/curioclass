# Exit cards, days 28 to 33

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Every child says, points to or marks a picture; you hear the day's rotating six.

## Day 28 card (s01-X28, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "Our paper rack is a tool for our drawings. Folded feet help it stand. Slots hold each drawing. The rack keeps our work together." Ask: "Say a sentence that names the tool and what it is for."

**Picture:** a paper rack with folded feet and three slots, a drawing in each.

**Right answer:** A sentence that names the rack and its job with the drawings, such as: The rack is a tool for our drawings.

## Day 29 card (s01-X29, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "A ruler is a tool for our paper. Its marks show how long. Its straight edge guides our pencil." Ask: "Tell me two facts that explain how the ruler helps."

**Picture:** a ruler with marks, laid along a sheet of paper, a pencil beside its edge.

**Right answer:** The marks show how long, and the straight edge guides the pencil.

## Day 30 card (s01-X30, W.1.2)

Read aloud: "Our paper tube carries rolled posters. Its closed ends keep them from sliding out." Say as a partner: "How does that help when we walk to the rug?" Ask: "Say a closing sentence that answers your partner."

**Picture:** a paper tube with closed ends and the tip of a rolled poster showing at one end.

**Right answer:** A closing that says the tube lets us carry the posters without losing them, such as: The tube helps us carry the posters to the rug.

## Day 31 card (s01-X31, W.1.2)

Show one strip under a picture. Print the strip: "the folder holds paper" (no capital, no ending mark). Say: "Edit the strip so it is ready to share: point to each place you would fix and say what you change."

**Picture:** joined papers inside a folder, beside a few loose papers.

**Right answer:** A capital T and an ending mark: "The folder holds paper." Record which mark was missed, with no error letter.

## Day 33 card (s01-X33, SL.1.1b)

A speaker (you) says: "A tub in the yard has a hole near the top. Extra rain goes out of the hole." A partner (you) says: "I heard where the extra rain goes." Show two pictures. Ask: "Which picture supports both speakers?" The rotating six then add a fact or ask a question.

**Pictures:** (1) a tub with rain running out of a hole onto the grass; (2) a tub with rain sitting on its lid.

**Right answer:** The picture of rain running out of the hole.
