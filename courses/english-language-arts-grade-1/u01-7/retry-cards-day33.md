# Day 33 retry cards

One fresh card of the same kind for each unit item, for the one-item retry in the response. The child retries the item the written note names. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

**Card G (for the reading and story rows).** Print: "Gus has a mug. It has jam. The mug hits the mat. Gus is mad." Pictures: Gus beside a mug lying on a mat with jam; Gus beside an upright mug. Two panels for the story rows. First panel: "I am Raj. Gran and I are in the hall. Our shoes are wet." Second panel: "Gran gets a mat. 'Step on it,' Gran says. I step. The mat dries my shoes." Pictures: Raj and Gran in a hall; Gran laying a yellow mat while Raj steps on it.
