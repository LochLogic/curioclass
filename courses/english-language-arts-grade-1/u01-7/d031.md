# Day 31: Edit and share the tool explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the booklets, the checklists, the score sheet, the exit card with its picture and two strips, and the observation sheet. Write the six names for rotation 5, the last six children not yet heard. Have the four texts at hand. Real tools: teacher only. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class on the rug. Read the Classroom Sign Model aloud once (2 minutes). Read the hammer poem aloud and have the class echo the sound word, "bang," on each line (3 minutes). Read one page of Kofi and the Bicycle (3 minutes) and one short line of the article Tools at Work (2 minutes). After each, ask the same question: "What job does the tool do here?" Hear one answer each. Say: "Review: a fact tells one thing a part does. Today you check that every fact in your booklet is a fact, not a guess." Pairs choose one sentence from a text that is a fact and read it to each other (5 minutes, inside the 15).

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card. Child A reads the booklet aloud to the partner, a page at a time. Child B listens for two things and says them back: "Your topic names the tool." "Your facts say what a part does." Swap (8 minutes). Hear two children say one fact to the class (2 minutes). Look-fors: the listener says what was heard, not what to fix; the facts come from the model or the texts. A child may pass the reading turn and point to the part instead.

#### 10 min. Edit and submit

Four minutes of repair first. If many children gave a closing that repeats a fact (error AC, "Its closed ends keep them in"), a feeling (error AA, "I like the tube") or a fact only the picture shows (error C, "The poster is red") on day 30's exit, say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "A bucket is a tool for water. Its handle lets us carry it. Its sides hold the water." Lay the strips: "The bucket helps us move water." "Its sides hold the water." "I like buckets." **Ask.** "Which strip closes the job?" Expect: the first, which says the job. Point to the strip that repeats a fact and the strip that is a feeling, and say why each does not close. Close a fresh piece together. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge.

Edit (6 minutes). Review: in kindergarten you began each sentence with a capital letter and ended it with a mark. Show a made-up sentence on the board, "the basket holds scraps", and ask the class what to change. Expect: a capital letter at the start and an ending mark. Children read their booklet to themselves, pointing to the first letter and the last mark of each sentence, and fix any that are missing. Each child then puts the booklet in the basket to submit. Score each submitted explanation once on the score sheet for W.1.2 while the children work at the next part: topic named, two accurate relevant facts, closing, each 0 or 1 ([[s01-A39]]). Artistic quality is not scored. Do not score a second time and do not hold a writing conference.

#### 20 min. Prepare to share

Pairs and then small groups. Each child chooses one page of the booklet to share and practices reading it aloud twice, once to a partner and once to a group of four (8 minutes). The listener asks one question from a strip, "What does the ___ do?" and the reader answers aloud. Then each child draws the job done in the share box on the student page and prints a short label (8 minutes). Hear two children read to the class (4 minutes). Walk and ask, "Which fact are you sharing?" Look-fors: the reader looks up at the end; the answer says what a part does. A child may share by pointing and saying.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show one strip under the picture of two papers joined in a folder: "the folder holds paper". Say: "Edit the strip so it is ready to share: point to each place you would fix and say what you change." Every child points, then the rotating six say the changes, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. ([[s01-X31]]). A good answer makes the first t a capital and adds an ending mark. Record which mark a child missed.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend: /w/ /i/ /g/, "wig"; /f/ /u/ /n/, "fun". Segment with counters: wig, three counters. Isolate the last sound of fun, /n/. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

No new pattern. The class reads Pat's Lunch together once (3 minutes). Pairs reread it, a page each (4 minutes). The class reads the cards hugs, buns and tubs, then each child spells buns on the sound-box sheet (3 minutes). Walk and sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Each child prints any letter still missing on the letter sample sheet (4 minutes). Dictation: say "Sam has a big wig." Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds: the capital S, sam, has, a, big, wig and the period. The sample is scored only; it is not a lesson. Walk and look at b and d.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Observation rotation 5 of 5, the last. Six different children, 5 minutes (300 seconds) each, at your table; no new teaching. The other twenty-four read known texts and spell independently.

*Order and seconds, one child at a time, as on day 27 (275 seconds).* Card F carries items 1, 12, 13 and 14. 1. s01-A16, 10: on Card F, point to the first word, the capital letter and the end mark of "The cup tips." 2. s01-A17, 10: cap and cape, which has the long vowel. 3. s01-A18, 10: blend /s/ /l/ /i/ /p/. 4. s01-A19, 10: cup, first, middle and last sounds. 5. s01-A20, 15: stamp, every sound. 6. s01-A21, 15: th, ch, sh on cards. 7. s01-A22, 15: read chip and shut. 8. s01-A23, 10: read the and is. 9. s01-A24, 5: read cap and caps. 10. s01-A26, 20: spell chin and the. 11. s01-A27, 15: spell thud, not shown. 12. s01-A28, 25: read Card F to find why the pup is sad, then point. 13. s01-A29, 90: read Card F aloud, then once more. 14. s01-A30, 25: find what the cup does, reread to check.

Hold the quiet pace cue yourself, never shown or heard by children. Mark each item on the observation sheet and move on at once. A child who is not yet seen gets the stem once more, then the first sound as help; if still not seen, record it and teach nothing new. The repair waits for day 33. The letter samples (s01-A25) and the earlier reading records (s01-A31, s01-A32) take no minutes. After this rotation every child has supplied the evidence once. Do not repeat the observation on a second day.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the observation marks and the W.1.2 scores to the record sheet and file the booklets, checklists, letter samples and score sheet. Mark the exit marks and the six heard. List the children not yet seen on any item for the make-up on day 32.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips on scraps and the score table on a lined page; record on a lined page. One child: read the booklet to a grown-up, edit the capital letters and marks, do the exit and the letters; skip the observation.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: write the two sentences on scraps. No booklet: use the drawing.

**Skip.** The observation.

- **10 min. Read it.** Your child reads the booklet aloud to you.
  **Say.** "What job does the tool do?" Expect: the job in a sentence, such as "It helps us tidy."
- **10 min. Check it.** Your child points to the capital letter and the ending mark of each sentence and fixes any that are missing.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints "Sam has a big wig."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X31]] You write "the folder holds paper" on a strip; your child shows the two places to fix and says the changes, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the edit, the exit and the rotation; cut the second sharing round. Longer: a child who finishes edits a classmate's sentence strip and says why it is ready to share.

### Words and supports

Words: edit, capital letter, ending mark, share. Frames: "My fact is ___." "It is ready because ___." Scribing for a child who cannot yet print; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. A quiet corner for the observation, and extra seconds where the dialect differs. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 is the unit check: a short hearing for each child on the sounds, words, sentences and reading taught across the unit, and a paper sheet. Children not yet seen on any rotation item are heard first. Keep the booklets safe for day 33.
