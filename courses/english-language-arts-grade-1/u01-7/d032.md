# Day 32: Check: listen to a story and an article

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the paper item sheets. Write six groups of five on the observation sheet. Day 31's scores stay filed. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 8 min. Setup and read-aloud

Say: "Today I listen to you, a few at a time. The cards are made up for practice." Read Card D aloud once, then Card C once. Show the pictures. Explain the paper sheet: drawing, marking or one printed word. Nothing is taught today. Send group one to your table, the rest to the sheet.

#### 30 min. Hearings

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each, at your table: 8 group minutes on Card D, 7 on Card C, 15 for each child's own question and answer. Card D: s01-A40, who and where; s01-A41, "Who clears the path?" Card C: s01-A42 and s01-A43, "What rolls along the ramp?" Questions: s01-A44, "Ask your partner a question about Card C"; s01-A45, the child answers "What does Nana pick up?" Help only by rereading once. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 17 min. Make-up

Children away, or not yet seen, are heard now at your table on the items they lack. The rest finish the paper sheet: s01-A46 and s01-A47 (the big things, in order), s01-A48 and s01-A49 (the teller, the speaker), s01-A50 (words and picture), s01-A51 and s01-A52 (the facts the words tell and the picture shows, and the ramp's job). Invented spelling counts.

#### 5 min. Close

Collect the sheets. Name tomorrow: share and a short response. Teach nothing new.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend: /r/ /a/ /m/, "ram"; /b/ /u/ /g/, "bug". Segment with counters: ram, three counters. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

Familiar words only. The class reads Pat's Lunch once; pairs reread a page. Each child spells tubs on the sound-box sheet.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Each child prints any letter still missing on the letter sample sheet. Dictation: say "Pat has a big mat." Children print a word at a time with the map.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Make-up observation: children not yet seen on a foundations item are heard 5 minutes each on Card F and the day 27 items they lack. The others read known texts.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the marks to the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the seven boxes on a lined page and read the cards from a lined page. One child: hear the cards at home with a grown-up and do the paper sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: draw the story on plain paper. No cards: retell the story from memory.

**Skip.** The hearings.

- **15 min. The assessment: tell it.** Your child tells you a story from the unit, with a beginning, middle and end, and says who tells it.
  **Say.** "Who is telling the story?" Expect: a name or a "me", such as "Min tells it."
- **15 min. Ask it.** You read a short tool article aloud; your child asks you one question and hears your answer.
- **10 min. Show it.** Your child draws one fact the words tell and one fact only the picture shows.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints "Pat has a big mat."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear every child on s01-A41 and s01-A43 only. Longer: a second question about the cards.

### Words and supports

Words: story, teller, question, picture. Frames: "I want to know ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 holds the share, the discussion groups and a short response on the evidence from days 27 to 33.
