# Word card V and the sort cards for the day 29 and 30 observation

Items s01-A33 to s01-A38. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Word card V

Print in a column: "A scoop is a tool with a bowl. Mina uses it to move sand. She looks at two scoops. She peeks through a gap to see them, then glances at them for a moment. Her little scoop sits beside the tiny scoop."

**Picture:** two scoops side by side, a little one and a tiny one, with sand beside them.

## Sort cards

Four word cards for the sort: scoop, sieve, sand, flour. Print each large with a small picture: a scoop, a sieve, a heap of sand, a bag of flour.
