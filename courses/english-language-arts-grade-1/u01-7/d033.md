# Day 33: Share, talk and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the exit card with its picture and the fresh retry cards. Write six groups of five on the observation sheet and the six sharers for the exit. Before the Response, write a short note for each child from the record sheet: one item and its error letter, from days 27 to 33, including day 32's assessment. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 5 min. Setup

Whole class on the rug. Say: "Today you share, talk in small groups and try one thing again." Read the Classroom Sign Model aloud once, a finger under the words, and the hammer poem once, with the class echoing "bang." Name the talk rules: one person speaks at a time, listen to the end, add to what was said. Send group one to your table and the rest to their seats with the booklets.

#### 30 min. Observed discussions

Six groups of five, 5 minutes (300 seconds) each, at your table, in the first 40 minutes of the day. Reread the sign model aloud to each group. Then say: "Talk about how the sign model shows that tools help. Wait for the person speaking." Keep quiet and let the five talk. Mark on the observation sheet, once per child: s01-A53, waits and speaks one at a time; s01-A54, adds a linked comment over several exchanges; s01-A55, asks a clearing question; s01-A56, asks a question about what a partner said, and answers one. If a child has not yet made a move, ask once, "What do you want to add?" and record shown with help. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with an error letter if one fits (errors AD, AE, AF, AG). Cite the W.1.2 score from day 31 and the word marks from days 29 and 30 from the record sheet; do not score or hear them again. The other twenty-five practice sharing in pairs from their booklets and draw.

#### 5 min. Regroup

Whole class. Pairs tell each other one thing a tool in their booklet does. Hear two.

#### 15 min. Share

The rotating six read their chosen booklet page aloud to the class, 2 minutes each: the page, then one listener question from the class, such as "What does the ___ do?" The reader answers aloud. Look-fors: the reader looks up; the answer says what a part does. A child may share by pointing and saying. Use the last 3 minutes to thank the readers and name one thing each did well.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Speaker (you): "A tub in the yard has a hole near the top. Extra rain goes out of the hole." Partner (you, in another voice): "I heard where the extra rain goes." Show two pictures: rain running out of the hole onto the grass, and rain sitting on the lid. Ask: "Which picture supports both speakers?" Every child points. The rotating six then add a fact or ask a question, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X33]]). A good answer points to the rain leaving the hole. An unrelated turn, such as "My tub is red," does not build on what was said (error AE).

#### 40 min. Independent reading and spelling

Children read known texts, including Pat's Lunch and their booklets, and spell on sound-box sheets, alone and in pairs. Sit with each child for a minute: hear one page of reading, one spelling. Use the time to finish each child's note for the Response. Nothing new is taught.

#### 20 min. Response

Each child gets the written note naming one item and its error letter. The notes carry what was sent: from day 27, s01-A19 to s01-A31 (errors J to T, AH and A); from day 29, s01-A33 to s01-A38 (errors U to Z); from day 31, s01-A39 (AA, AB, AC); from day 32, s01-A40v2, s01-A46bv2, s01-A47v2, s01-A48v2, s01-A50v2 and s01-A51v2 (A, D, E, F, C, AI). The child reads the note to a partner, then retries that one item on the fresh retry card, inside the 20 minutes, alone. Group the notes by error letter and sit with the largest groups first; the rest wait with a partner and rehearse aloud. The child retries once, about 40 seconds each. Use the retry card for the letter, as follows.

Reading and spelling errors J, K, L, M, N, AH, O, P, Q, R, S and T: the retry card names the word or line to read or spell, fresh and unheld. Word work errors U, V, W, X, Y and Z: the fresh word cards and the question on the retry card. Writing errors AA, AB and AC: "Explain how a broom helps our classroom. Name it, give two facts and finish." Story and article errors A, C, D, E, F and AI: Card G and its two panels, with the question for that letter. Discussion errors AD, AE, AF and AG: a fresh group talk about a mat at the door, with the matching move.

Mark the retry on the record sheet: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Teach nothing new in a retry. A child with no note reads a booklet page to a partner.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the retry cards on a lined page; record on a lined page. One child: talk with a grown-up about how a tool helps, read a booklet page aloud, answer the exit by pointing, and do the one retry.

### At home

**Swap.** No booklet: use the drawing. No exit picture: draw two pictures.

**Skip.** The discussion groups.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child reads one booklet page aloud to you, then you ask one question: "What does the ___ do?"
  **Say.** "What does the ___ do?" Expect: what a part does, such as "It holds the scraps."
- **10 min. Talk.** You and your child take turns saying one thing about a tool. Each adds to what the other said.
- **10 min. Try again.** Your child reads or spells one thing from the unit again, with you listening.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X33]] You say two sentences about a rain tub and show two pictures; your child points, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the discussion, the exit and the Response; cut the independent time to 25 minutes. Longer: a child who finishes the retry reads a second booklet page to a new partner.

### Words and supports

Words: share, add, ask, listen. Frames: "I add that ___." "Which ___ did you mean?" Scribing for a child who cannot yet print; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. A quiet corner for the groups. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 begins unit 2 with the sky question: children connect what they see in the sky with a fact from a read-aloud. If many children made an unrelated turn (error AE) on today's exit, open the first talk on day 34 with four minutes: contrast a new subject with adding to a comment, and respond to a fresh comment.
