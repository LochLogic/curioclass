# Day 29: Draft facts about how the tool helps

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the booklets, word card V with the four sort cards, the exit card with its picture and the observation sheet. Name the six groups of five: groups 1 to 3 today, groups 4 to 6 on day 30. Write the six names for rotation 3 across the observation sheet. Draw your own basket model, with a wide mouth and a handle, for the demonstration. Real tools: teacher only. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 18 min. Word observation

Three groups of five, 6 minutes (360 seconds) each, at your table. The other twenty-five open the booklet, copy their topic sentence from yesterday onto page 1 and draw their tool, quietly. Per group, read word card V aloud once (20 seconds). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Each child then takes about 68 seconds, in this order; all five children answer the same stem in turn. Adult guidance is allowed; record each part once.
1. s01-A33, 12: four word cards, scoop, sieve, sand, flour. "Sort the tool words and the material words."
2. s01-A34, 12: "What is a scoop? Use the card."
3. s01-A35, 12: "Tell me one use for a scoop at school."
4. s01-A36, 12: "Act out look, peek and glance."
5. s01-A37, 8: "Point to the little scoop and to the tiny scoop. Which one is smaller?"
6. s01-A38, 12: "Tell me why Mina uses a scoop. Use the words scoop, sand and because."

Mark each item shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet, with the error letter if one fits: flour with the tools (error U), "a thing" with no attribute (error V), the word repeated with no use (error W), peek and glance acted the same (error X), tiny and little the same size (error Y), words listed with no because (error Z). A child who is not yet seen gets the stem once more; if still not seen, record it and teach nothing new. The repair waits for day 33. A child who is away is heard on day 30.

#### 12 min. Text questions and talk

Whole class, then pairs. Review: you heard Tools on the Farm on day 3. Read the hoe paragraph aloud: "A farmer uses a hoe. A hoe has a long handle and a flat head. The farmer pulls the hoe through the dirt to cut weeds and to loosen the soil. The long handle lets the farmer stand up tall. So the farmer does not have to bend over all day." (1 minute) **Ask.** "Which part helps the farmer, and what does it do?" Expect: the long handle lets the farmer stand up tall. Say: "A fact names a part and tells what it does. Here the words tell us." (2 minutes)

Pairs, with the turn card. Child A says the fact in the frame, "The ___ helps because ___" (the long handle helps because it lets the farmer stand tall). Child B asks, "Which part?" and A names it again. Swap (4 minutes). Then each child says a fact about the paper model, in the same way, and the listener asks "Which part?" (4 minutes). Hear two pairs aloud (1 minute). Look-fors: a part and what it does; the fact can be tested on the model or found in the article; the answer to "Which part?" is the part, not a color. A child may pass and point to the part.

#### 20 min. Draft

Four minutes of repair first. If many children gave a sentence that names no tool (error AB, "It is good") or a fact only the picture shows (error C, "The slots are brown") on day 28's exit, write It helps and My basket helps on the board. Say: "Which one names the tool?" Say the second again. Then each child opens with a fresh sentence that names the model, to a partner. Cover the picture first when the slip was a picture fact. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge.

Model (3 minutes), with your basket. Write page 1 on the board: My basket is a tool for paper scraps. Touch the wide mouth. Ask yourself aloud: "What does this part do? It lets scraps drop in." Write page 2: Its wide mouth lets scraps drop in. Say: "A fact names a part and what it does. I can test it: watch." Drop in a scrap. Then touch the handle and write page 3: Its handle lets us carry it. Say: "Nice is a feeling. A fact is something you can see or test. A color is only in the picture." Compare with the sign model on the wall: "A crayon made the letters dark."

Children draft (12 minutes). Each child draws the part that does the job above the line on page 2, says the fact, and prints it, with sound boxes if needed. Then page 3: a second part and its fact. The frame is "Its ___ ___." Four to six words are enough; a child who cannot yet print says the fact and you scribe it for copying. Walk and ask, "Which part?" and "What does it do?" Sit with three children, 1 minute each. Look-fors: the topic sentence is on page 1; page 2 names a part and says what it does; no feeling or color stands in for a fact. Use the last minute for three children to read a fact aloud.

#### 5 min. Letter samples

Each child prints the pairs U u to Z z on side two of the letter sample sheet, and you write the date. Forms still missing are recorded as incomplete, never wrong. Watch b and d.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "A ruler is a tool for our paper. Its marks show how long. Its straight edge guides our pencil." Show the picture: a ruler with marks, laid along a sheet of paper, a pencil beside its edge. Ask: "Tell me two facts that explain how the ruler helps." Every child says two facts to a partner, one finger for each, then the rotating six say them to you, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X29]]). A good answer gives both: the marks show how long and the straight edge guides the pencil. "It is nice" is a judgment with no fact (error AA). "It is yellow" is a fact only the picture shows (error C). "It cuts paper" is a part the card did not give (error A). These marks go to day 30.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend: /p/ /i/ /g/, "pig"; /f/ /u/ /n/, "fun". Segment with counters: pig, three counters. Isolate the first sound of fun, /f/. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

No new pattern. The class reads Pat's Lunch together once, a finger under the words (3 minutes). Pairs reread it, a page each (4 minutes). The class reads the cards buns, tubs, nuts and hugs, then each child spells nuts and hugs on the sound-box sheet (3 minutes). Walk and sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

No new letter. Dictation: say "Tim has a tub." Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds: the capital T, tim, the mapped word has, a, tub and the period. Then each child prints the line again, smaller, below it. Walk and look at b and d.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Observation rotation 3 of 5. Six different children, 5 minutes (300 seconds) each, at your table; no new teaching. The other twenty-four read known texts and spell independently, as on day 28. Every child supplies this evidence once in the window of days 27 to 31.

*Order and seconds, one child at a time, as on day 27 (275 seconds).* Card F carries items 1, 12, 13 and 14. 1. s01-A16, 10: on Card F, point to the first word, the capital letter and the end mark of "The cup tips." 2. s01-A17, 10: cap and cape, which has the long vowel. 3. s01-A18, 10: blend /s/ /l/ /i/ /p/. 4. s01-A19, 10: cup, first, middle and last sounds. 5. s01-A20, 15: stamp, every sound. 6. s01-A21, 15: th, ch, sh on cards. 7. s01-A22, 15: read chip and shut. 8. s01-A23, 10: read the and is. 9. s01-A24, 5: read cap and caps. 10. s01-A26, 20: spell chin and the. 11. s01-A27, 15: spell thud, not shown. 12. s01-A28, 25: read Card F to find why the pup is sad, then point. 13. s01-A29, 90: read Card F aloud, then once more. 14. s01-A30, 25: find what the cup does, reread to check.

Hold the quiet pace cue yourself, never shown or heard by children. Mark each item on the observation sheet and move on at once. A child who is not yet seen gets the stem once more, then the first sound as help; if still not seen, record it and teach nothing new. The repair waits for day 33. The letter samples (s01-A25) and the earlier reading records (s01-A31, s01-A32) take no minutes.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the observation marks and the word marks to the record sheet and file the observation sheet, the booklets, the dated letter samples and the models. Mark the exit marks and the six heard. Name tomorrow's six and groups 4 to 6.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the four sort words on cards, read word card V from a lined page, draw sound boxes on a lined page and read Card F from a lined page; record on a lined page. No booklet: fold three lined sheets. One child: say the hoe fact with a grown-up, draft the topic sentence and one fact, do the exit and the letters; skip the observation.

### At home

**Swap.** No model: draw it and use the drawing. No booklet: use a lined page.

**Skip.** The word groups and the observation.

- **10 min. Find a fact.** Your child shows the model, points to one part and says what it does: "Its ___ ___."
  **Say.** "Which part helps, and what does it do?" Expect: a part and an action, such as "Its handle lets me carry it."
- **10 min. Draw and print.** Your child draws the part and prints the fact, one letter for each sound.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints "Tim has a tub."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X29]] Your child answers alone, aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the word observation, the draft of one fact, the exit and the rotation; cut the second pairs turn. Longer: a child who finishes drafts a third fact about another part.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, part, helps, because, handle, mouth. Frames: "Its ___ ___." "The ___ helps because ___." "Which part?" Sound boxes; scribing for a child who cannot yet print; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. A quiet corner for the observation, and extra seconds where the dialect differs. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 hears groups 4 to 6 on word work, then a partner asks one specific question about each draft and the maker answers aloud, and each child adds a closing sentence and revises one fact. Rotation 4 hears six more children. If many children gave a part the card did not give (error A, "It cuts paper"), a picture fact (error C, "It is yellow") or a judgment with no fact (error AA, "It is nice") on today's exit, the revise part opens with four minutes: reread the card, point to the sentence that says it, cover the picture first for error C, give one fact for each finger for error AA, and revise one fact in the draft.
