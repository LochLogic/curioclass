# Day 43 poem chart

A wall strip with line cards from *Quiet Sky* (g1-u2-02): "The air is cool and still."; "I hear a cricket sing."; "I feel my blanket, warm."; "The sky is soft and blue." Under them, a face, a hand, an ear and an eye. Children place each line under the one that fits and say which word in the line tells.
