# Day 46: Tell it with the map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the exit card with its three picture strips and three lesson cards. Fold each story map so only the three event boxes show, one for each tale, and make a set for each pair. Write a repair card: "Ted gets a bug." with a picture of Ted holding a net over a bug. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read both tales

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Reread pages 2, 3 and 5 of that tale, showing each picture (6 minutes). Say aloud: "This is a traditional Chinese tale." Reread pages 5, 7 and 9 of that tale (6 minutes). Review: you heard both tales on days 35 to 42. Partners lay the tale picture strip cards for the first tale and the three event pictures for the second tale on their folded maps (3 minutes). Neither tale is acted out.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair first (4 minutes, from day 45's exit, error J). Show the card "Ted gets a bug." with the picture. Cover the picture. Say: "Map each spelling, blend, then check." The class maps g-e-t-s and blends the word. Uncover the picture and read the sentence. Say: "The picture comes second." If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the questions the whole ten minutes.

**Ask.** "Which event shows the lesson in the first tale?" Expect: the Sun and the Moon say yes again and again as the water rises. **Ask.** "Which event shows the lesson in the second tale?" Expect: the magpies make a bridge. Partners point to the event on each map (6 minutes). Any lesson the events support is right.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. Review: you used so to join two facts on days 34 and 35, and and and but to join parts on days 40 and 42. Say aloud: "The water rose, so the Sun and the Moon climbed up." Say: "The word so tells a result." Say aloud: "The magpies made a bridge, so the two met." **Ask.** "What is the lesson, and which event shows it?" Starter: "The lesson is ___, so ___." Expect: when you care you keep caring, so the two met; or, listen to a warning, so say yes slowly. Each child joins two parts with so aloud. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 20 min. Independent retelling

Each child picks a tale. Partners retell it with the folded map, first, next, last, then say the lesson and the event that shows it, one turn each, about two minutes a child (4 minutes). Then each child draws first, next and last of the tale they picked, three small pictures in a row, and labels the event that shows the lesson (16 minutes). While they draw, hear the six at your table, one child at a time, one minute each (6 minutes): the child retells the tale with the folded map, and says a lesson with one event. Look-fors: events in order; a lesson supported by one event; the child says the words of the tale, not only a topic. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud: "Wren could not carry the sticks. Dove lifted some with Wren. Together they built the nest." Show three picture strips of the events, and three lesson cards, reading each aloud: "Nests." "Helping together can get a hard job done." "Wrens sleep all day." Say: "Point to the lesson." Every child points. The rotating six then retell the three events with the folded map, half a minute each ([[s02-X46]]). A good answer is the card Helping together can get a hard job done. The card Nests is error B, a topic. The card Wrens sleep all day is error E, a claim the card does not make: point to the words that tell. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: the class isolates sounds in blends: the first sound of blink, the last sound of plank, the middle sound of stamp. Segment five sounds: /b/ /l/ /i/ /ng/ /k/, "blink"; /p/ /l/ /a/ /ng/ /k/, "plank"; /s/ /t/ /a/ /m/ /p/, "stamp"; tap each sound, then say the word. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Decode then spell taught words (5 minutes): read fits, hugs, nods, fixing and sky from the cards; spell sets and fly on sound boxes. Then partners reread Dad's Plan for expression, one page each, a finger under each word (5 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: collecting finished on day 40. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Ted fits the red lid." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Watch the forms T, d, f, r and l. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group rereads Dad's Plan aloud with expression, one page each, then spells nods on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Dad's Plan with a partner; chain fit, fix, six, sit and sip with letter cards; spell hugs and my on sound boxes; retell a tale to a partner with the folded map. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the retell marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 47.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the picture strips on scrap paper and fold a sheet in three for each map. One child: reread both tales in two sittings, retell each with the map, say a lesson for each, draw three pictures, answer the exit by pointing, then reread Dad's Plan.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: fold a page in three. No cards: draw the three events on a page.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Retell.** Your child picks a tale and tells it from the pictures, first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What is the lesson, and which event shows it?" Expect: the magpies make a bridge, so the two meet; or, the water kept rising after they said yes.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws first, next and last in three boxes.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child spells sets and fly, then prints "Ted fits the red lid."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X46]] You read a new card: Mole could not reach the jar. Hare held the stool. Together they got it down. You show three lesson cards: Jars. Friends can help you reach what you cannot reach alone. Hares can fly. Your child points to the lesson, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to one pair each. Longer: a child who finishes retells the second tale and says a different lesson.

### Words and supports

Words: retell, lesson, event, first, next, last, so. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "The lesson is ___, so ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 checks ordered retelling, key details and the central message of one sky tale, cold. If many children pointed to the card Nests (error B) on today's exit, day 49's response part carries the repair: connect one action to its result, then say a lesson. No repair opens day 47, so the check stays cold.
