# Section 2.3 opener: poems, facts and a retelling alone

## What the section does

Days 43 to 46 add poems about the night sky. The class sorts poem lines by what they make us feel or sense, compares a poem with a fact about the same thing, reads a short decodable card on its own and retells a tale with a story map. Day 43 also holds the cycle 6 sample and the scoring of the letter-form record.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A poem is made of lines and sound.** It may have no events, so it is not retold the way a tale is.
- **A fact can be checked; a poem line is a feeling or a picture.**
- **A child reads a word by its sounds, then checks it against the picture.** The picture comes second.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C, G:** a touch word taken for a feeling word, or a picture that has nothing to do with the words.
- **D, J:** sorting by topic; guessing a word from its picture. Map each spelling and blend.
- **K, M, AA:** a different middle vowel, a missing vowel, a reversed letter.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
