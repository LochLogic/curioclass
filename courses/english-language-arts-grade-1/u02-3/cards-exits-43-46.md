# Exit cards, days 43 to 46

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

On day 45 the child reads the card; all the others are read aloud by you.

## Day 43 card (s02-X43, RL.1.4)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "The night was hushed. A cold wind brushed my cheek. I felt peaceful." Show three pictures: a calm smiling face, a hand on a cheek, and a house. Say: "Point to the picture that shows how the speaker felt inside." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The calm face. The hand shows the touch words, cold wind and brushed my cheek.

## Day 44 card (s02-X44, RL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud two cards about the same topic. The poem card: "Sleepy star, blink, blink, blink. Hush, hush, goodnight." The fact card: "Stars look small because they are far away." Say: "Point to the card that tells a fact you could check." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The fact card.

## Day 45 card (s02-X45, RF.1.3b)

This card is invented for practice. The child reads this card: "Ben hums. Ben naps. Ben digs." Show four pictures out of order: Ben humming, Ben napping, Ben digging in sand, Ben planting seeds. Say: "Read the card. Point to the picture of what Ben does last." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of Ben digging in sand.

## Day 46 card (s02-X46, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Wren could not carry the sticks. Dove lifted some with Wren. Together they built the nest." Show three picture strips of the events, and three lesson cards read aloud: "Nests." "Helping together can get a hard job done." "Wrens sleep all day." Say: "Point to the lesson." The day's rotating six retell the three events with the map.

**Right answer:** The card "Helping together can get a hard job done."
