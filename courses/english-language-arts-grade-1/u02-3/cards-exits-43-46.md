# Exit cards, days 43 to 46

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card still marked "invented for practice" below; the exit pictures read a named passage and carry no foot line. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

On day 45 the child reads the card; all the others are read aloud by you.

## Day 43 exit pictures (s02-X43v2, RL.1.4)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 6, last two sentences; day 43 works the poem Quiet Sky. Reread the end of page 6 of the Chinese tale aloud, from "But they looked across". Show three pictures: a sad face, a loom, and a house. Say: "Point to the picture that shows how the people on the earth felt inside." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The sad face (felt sad). The loom names a thing.

## Day 44 exit pictures (s02-X44v2, RL.1.5)

Passage: Quiet Sky, page 1, lines 3 and 4, beside Stars in Daylight and Darkness, page 3 (first read on day 58); day 44 works the Quiet Sky line on page 2, the Chinese tale and Sky Patterns, page 5. Hold up two pages and read a part of each. Quiet Sky, page 1: "The first small star blinks on, as bright as morning dew." The star article, page 3: "The stars are still up there." Say: "Point to the page that tells a fact you could check." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The star article page.

## Day 45 card (s02-X45, RF.1.3b)

This card is invented for practice. The child reads this card: "Ben hums. Ben naps. Ben digs." Show four pictures out of order: Ben humming, Ben napping, Ben digging in sand, Ben planting seeds. Say: "Read the card. Point to the picture of what Ben does last." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of Ben digging in sand.

## Day 46 exit pictures (s02-X46v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 8 (the yearly meeting); day 46 works pages 5, 7 and 9 of that tale and pages 2, 3 and 5 of the Efik and Ibibio tale. Reread page 8 of the Chinese tale aloud. Read three lesson cards aloud: "Magpies." "People who care about each other can wait to meet again." "The stars sleep all day." Say: "Point to the lesson." The day's rotating six retell page 8 with the map.

**Right answer:** The card "People who care about each other can wait to meet again."
