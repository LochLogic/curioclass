# Day 45: Words that describe the tale

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the exit card and picture row for each child and Dad's Plan for each child. Sort the tale picture strip cards into a set for each pair. Write two repair cards: the poem line "The sun has gone to sleep." and the fact "Every evening, the sun goes down." Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the Efik and Ibibio tale

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Review: you heard this tale on days 35 to 38 and sorted two of its words on day 39. Reread it in its five pages, showing each picture (7 minutes). Say the Water's question with the class: "Is it still safe?" and the class answers, "Yes." (3 minutes). The tale is told, never acted out. **Ask.** "What happens to the water?" Expect: it comes in and rises, up to the knees, the heads and over the roof (3 minutes). **Ask.** "What happens at the end?" Expect: the Sun and the Moon go up into the sky (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair first (4 minutes, from day 44's exit, error D). Lay the two cards. Read: "The sun has gone to sleep." Read: "Every evening, the sun goes down." Say: "Point to the line that tells something you could check." Pairs point first, then say why: the sun goes down is a fact. Say: "The poem gives a picture; the fact can be checked." If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the questions the whole ten minutes.

Each pair lays the picture strip cards in order and groups them: first, next, last (6 minutes). **Ask.** "What happens first?" Expect: the Sun asks the Water to visit, and the Water says the house is too small. **Ask.** "What happens last?" Expect: the water rises over the roof and the Sun and the Moon go up to the sky.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. Review: in kindergarten you found words that describe; on day 39 you sorted broad smile and knee-deep. Hang the feeling and sense chart. **Ask.** "Which word told you what it was like?" Starter: "The word ___ tells what ___ was like." Expect: huge compound; knee-deep; broad smile. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 20 min. Words that describe

Reread three pages with expression, in pairs, in your own voice; nobody plays a character (6 minutes). The heard six each say one line after you, with expression, half a minute each (3 minutes). Each child draws a moment from the tale and labels one describing word, with the map or by asking you to write it (11 minutes). Hang the drawings under the feeling and sense chart.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Give each child the card: Ben hums. Ben naps. Ben digs. Show four pictures out of order: Ben humming, Ben napping, Ben digging in sand, Ben planting seeds. Say: "Read the card. Point to the picture of what Ben does last." Every child reads with a finger under each word and points. The rotating six then read the card aloud, 20 seconds each ([[s02-X45]]). A good answer is Ben digging in sand. Ben planting seeds is error J, a guess from the picture. Ben humming is error A, the first event. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to day 46.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: the class taps and says the sounds in my, fly, sky, cry and try, then blends them: /f/ /l/ /ie/, "fly". Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Read the cards from day 44: gets, sets, fixing (1 minute). Same endings, new bases: fits, nods, hugs (2 minutes). Show page 3 of Dad's Plan, the picture of Dad fitting a lid, with the sentence under it covered. Say: "Cover the picture, map each spelling, blend, then check." Map g-e-t-s, blend, uncover the picture (1 minute). Spell fits and nods on sound boxes (1 minute). Read Dad's Plan with the class, a finger under each word, one page at a time; the class reads the mapped words has and look twice first (5 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: collecting finished on day 40. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Jen sets the wet mat." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Watch the forms J, e, s, w, t and m. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads Dad's Plan aloud with expression, one page each, then spells hugs on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Dad's Plan with a partner; chain wet, set, get, gets and sets with letter cards; spell fits and nods on sound boxes; tell a partner the tale with the drawings, first, next, last. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 46.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strip cards on scrap paper and write the exit card on a sheet. One child: reread the tale in two sittings, lay the strips with a grown-up, draw a moment with a describing word, answer the exit by reading the card and pointing, then read Dad's Plan.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three boxes on a page for first, next and last. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read the last two pages of the tale aloud. Your child tells it, first, next, last.
  **Say.** "Which word told you what it was like?" Expect: huge, knee-deep or broad smile.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws one moment and labels one describing word.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child spells fits and nods, then prints "Jen sets the wet mat."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X45]] You write a new card: Tim nods. Tim fits a lid. Tim sets a mat. You show four pictures, and your child reads the card and points to what Tim does last, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to one pair each. Longer: a child who finishes labels a second describing word and says what it was like.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, huge, knee-deep, broad smile, first, next, last. Frames: "The word ___ tells what ___ was like." "First ___, next ___, last ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud except the exit card.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 uses a story map to rehearse a retelling of each tale. If many children pointed to Ben planting seeds (error J) on today's exit, the opening of day 46's repair part goes to a four-minute repair: map each spelling, blend, then check with the picture.
