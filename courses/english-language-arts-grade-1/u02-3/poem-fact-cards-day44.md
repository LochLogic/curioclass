# Day 44 poem and fact cards

Two sets on the same topic. **A poem line** from *Quiet Sky*: "The first small star blinks on". **A fact** from *Sun Across the Sky*: "The sun is a star." Children point to which card gives something they could check and which gives a picture or a feeling. A third column is added to the day 35 sheet for the poem.
