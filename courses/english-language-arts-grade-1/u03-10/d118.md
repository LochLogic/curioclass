# Day 118: Finish the scientific drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 117's marks. Name today's heard six, the partner pairs and cohort 3 (children 21 to 30 on the class list). List the children who gave a liking on day 117's exit (error E) for the repair. Make the repair strip below. Keep the cohort word cards out of sight. Day 117's error B waits for day 119. After class, the unit's folder work is scored out of class from the folders; the page names the items.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 117's exit (error E): a child who gave a liking in place of a clue. Say: "This strip is made up for practice." Show a fresh strip on the chart: "Source H2: A heron's toes spread out wide. The toes spread its weight. Strip: How does it help the bird?" Read it aloud. All children at once, a finger on the sentence that answers the strip. **Ask.** "Which line answers the strip?" Expect: the toes spread its weight; "I like herons" is a feeling, not an answer. Then each child says one fact from their own bird's page to a partner, with the label named. Hear three children. Review: a fact has a source label (day 117).

#### 14 min. Review the drawing card

Review: a label is a word beside a part, and it is checked against the sentence, not the picture (grade 1, day 109). Show the hawk model with its swapped labels. Walk the three-step label check: read the sentence, point to the part, say whether the label matches. The class finds both swapped labels and swaps them back. Review: a riddle's clues come from facts (day 116); reread Wing Clues from the poem pages, a line at a time, and say which line is the shape clue and which is the use clue. Review: the fact line (kindergarten, day 93). A fact line sits under the drawing and gives one fact from the page, with its source label, and never names the bird. Chart one: "The claws grasp food. K2". Children say it back. Then a partner check plan: you draw, you label, a partner checks.

#### 26 min. Complete the drawing

Each child finishes the drawing from day 117's riddle folder: the bird part the riddle names, drawn large, with one or two labels, and one fact line with its source label. The fact line never names the bird. Give no help with the facts: point to the page and the label. The partner label check comes at minute 18: swap, read the fact line, point to each label, say whether it matches the sentence. Fix any label that does not. A child who cannot print says each label and the fact to you or a scribe, and your hand writes it. Hear six children, one minute each, as you walk: "Show me one label and where the page says it." Expect: a finger on the label and a finger on the sentence. A label from the picture alone is error C; a fact no page gives is error B. Five minutes at the end: the drawing and the riddle card go in the folder together.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn card, pairs from different tables, all at once, two minutes, then six pairs heard, fifty seconds each, five minutes, with three minutes to set up and move. Review: listen, restate, reply (day 107). **Ask.** "How does the finished drawing support the riddle's clues?" Expect: "The drawing shows the part the clue names, and the label matches the clue." A child may pass; the partner then points to the label.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its two pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the part the sentence is about?" ([[s03-X118a]]) Then the heard six come to your table, with the card in view, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Say the label for the part." ([[s03-X118b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who says the beak has picked a part the sentence does not give (error B). A child who says a color from the picture has trusted the picture over the words (error C). Both wait for day 119's Repair by gap.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say can and cane; which has the long vowel sound? Blend /f/ /r/ /o/ /z/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of tune. Segment flame.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidate; no new pattern. Whole class on small boards, then six tables of five at the map, a minute a table. Review: the e dropped before -ing and -ed (day 109). Chain skate, skated, skating. Decode hated, sunrise, widest and napkin, counting the vowel sounds, not the vowel letters. Spell skated and sunrise, about 40 seconds a word. Reread A Day at the Lake, pages 9 to 12, for expression. Then each child echo-reads the fact line after you, a finger under each word.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictate "Dave rides a mule." Say it whole, then a word at a time; Dave is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Dave and mule the last e is silent, and in rides the e stays. Then each child prints two pairs the written feedback marked (practice, not collected). Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Observation cohort

Cohort 3, ten children not yet recorded, one at a time at your table, 3 minutes each. Cards stay in the folder: sunhat, windmill and mudslide; their divided forms sun-hat, wind-mill and mud-slide; and the ending list socks, benches, camping, wished, pulled, lasted, paler, palest, tapes, taping, taped. First, half a minute. **Ask.** "Count the parts in each word by its vowel sounds." ([[s03-A36]]) Review: every syllable has a vowel sound (days 79 and 99). Expect two, two, two; mudslide has two vowel sounds, though its letters u, i and e look like three (error H). Second, 45 seconds. **Ask.** "Read each word, part by part." ([[s03-A37]]) Expect each part read, then blended; a word said from a picture or a guess is error I. Third, one minute and three-quarters. **Ask.** "Read each word." ([[s03-A38]]) Expect every ending kept: wished ends /t/, pulled /d/, lasted adds a part, and taping is not tape (error J). Fill only the cells a recent record has not shown, and cite that record. A child may ask for a word again: shown with help.

The other twenty work in pairs at three stations, ten minutes each, and you hear no one else: reread A Day at the Lake a page each; chain skate, skated, skating and spell hated and widest with letter cards; say the fact line to a partner, who points to the label it matches.

Ten children at 3 minutes fills all 30 minutes with no room to walk, so seat the next child as the last one leaves. A child with a recent shown entry takes less. A child not finished at minute 30 is recorded as unfinished and heard on day 119; do not hurry one.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the cohort rows, the exit marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Write one line for each cohort child who showed error H, error I or error J. After class, score the folder work out of class, with no child waiting: oral sounds [[s03-A39]] [[s03-A40]] [[s03-A41]] [[s03-A42]], the cycle sample [[s03-A43]] [[s03-A44]] [[s03-A45]] [[s03-A46]] [[s03-A47]], the drawing and the beak sentence [[s03-A48]] [[s03-A49]] [[s03-A50]], printing [[s03-A51]], and the supported reading [[s03-A52]] [[s03-A53]] [[s03-A54]] [[s03-A55]] [[s03-A56]]. Cite the day each was collected. Put every drawing and card in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the model on paper and say the exit cards aloud. One child: draw one part with one label and a fact line, take the three observation tasks with a grown-up, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No drawing card: use plain paper with two label lines and one fact line.

**Skip.** The observation, the stations and the talk.

- **10 min. Look.** Your child rereads the riddle and the page, and finds the part the first clue names.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws that part large and labels it. **Say.** "Where does the page say it?" Expect: a finger on the sentence.
- **5 min. Fact line.** One fact from the page, with its source label, and no bird name.
- **5 min. Print.** "Dave rides a mule."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X118a]] [[s03-X118b]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture the sentence is about, then says the label for the part.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one label and the fact line; keep the partner check and the exit. Longer: a child with a finished drawing adds a second labeled part from the page.

### Words and supports

Words: drawing, label, fact line, source, match. Frames: "The label is ___." "The page says ___." "It matches." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can find the part a sentence names and give its label. A child who named a part no page gives (error B) or took a color from the picture (error C) is in day 119's Repair by gap, with a symbol marking, picture or word. Day 117's error B and day 116's error A are there too. Cohort 3's misses in counting (error H), blending (error I) and endings (error J) are retried on day 119. Day 119 plays the riddle game and finishes the unit.
