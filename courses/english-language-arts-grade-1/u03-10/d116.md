# Day 116: Write the expert-bird riddle

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 115's assessment, the marked records, and copy its marks. Name today's heard six, the pairs for the talk and cohort 1 (children 1 to 10 on the class list). Make the repair page below. Make the cohort word cards from your folder and keep them out of sight. Put each child's riddle folder on the desk. Day 115's misses (error A and error B) wait for day 119. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 114's exit (error D, and s03-X114av2): a child who used the glossary alone. Say: "This page is made up for practice." Show a fresh page: "Contents: Wing Beats, 2. Heading: Wing Beats. Glossary: hover, to stay in one place in the air. A hummingbird's wings beat very fast. A hummingbird can hover." (restates Wings in Motion). All children at once, a finger in the air. **Ask.** "Show me the line that sends you to the hovering fact, then the heading, then the sentence." Expect: the contents line, the heading, then the sentence. The glossary line says what hover means; it does not prove the fact. Hear three children say which line they used. Review: contents, heading and glossary (days 81 to 83 and 111).

#### 5 min. Riddle frame

Say: "These riddles are made up for practice." Review: a riddle gives clues and each clue narrows the bird (kindergarten, day 13; grade 1, day 108). Hold up the riddle card from day 108: the frame "I have ___. I ___. What am I?", two clue lines, the name on the back and the drawing under a flap. Read the model of day 113 from its beak topic card: "I have a flat beak. My beak strains food from mud. What am I?" Lift the flap and show a duck. Review: the first clue is the shape and the second is the use (day 113); the name stays back until the answer. Name the task: "Today you write your riddle. Tomorrow a partner asks one question and you change a clue. On day 118 you finish the drawing and on day 119 we play the game."

#### 10 min. Choose two clues

Each child takes the verified clues from day 114 with their source labels. Six minutes alone: pick the best two, one for shape and one for use. Review: a source is where a fact came from; a clue with no source label is checked again on the route card (day 114). A liking, such as "I like my bird", is not a clue; the child goes back to the page (error E). Give no help with the facts. Four minutes in pairs: each child says both clues without the bird's name and the partner says which is shape and which is use. Hear six children, one minute each, inside the six minutes: "Say your clue and point to its source label." Expect: a plain fact and a finger on its label.

#### 25 min. Draft the riddle independently

Three minutes to set up: show the frame again and say that a child who cannot print says each clue to you or to a scribe, and your hand writes it. Eighteen minutes: each child prints the two clues on the riddle card under the frame, with the bird's name small on the back, and draws the bird in the box on the student page. Neither clue names the bird. Give no help with the facts: if a child asks "Is this true?", point to the source label and the page. Walk round and hear six children, two minutes each, inside the eighteen: "Read me your clues and show me where the page says them." A child who wrote a liking goes back to the page (error E). A child who wrote a fact no page gives is shown the sentence (error B). Four minutes: riddle card, sketch and clues go in the riddle folder.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn card, pairs from different tables, all at once, two minutes, then six pairs heard, fifty seconds each, five minutes, with three minutes to set up and move. Review: listen, restate ("You said ___"), then reply (day 107). **Ask.** "How can the bird's name be held until the answer?" Expect: "I say what the part looks like and what it does, and the name stays under the flap." A child may pass; the partner then reads one clue aloud.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet. Show the two pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture should answer a riddle with these clues?" ([[s03-X116a]]) Then, with the card in view, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each: **Ask.** "Say the first clue, without the bird's name." ([[s03-X116b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the fish alone has named a detail, not the bird (error A). A child who says "I like fish." has given a liking, not a fact (error E). Name day 116's misses for day 117's repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say tap and tape; which has the long vowel sound? Blend /k/ /l/ /o/ /z/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of mule. Segment flute.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Consolidate; no new pattern. Whole class on small boards, then six tables of five at the sound-spelling map, a minute a table. Review: silent-e endings, the e dropped before -ing and -ed (day 109); closed two-part words, clap then chop between the middle consonants (days 79 and 81); two-part silent-e words (day 106). Chain bake, baked, baking, bakes; say each -ed word's last sound. Decode sunset, napkin, pancake and inside, counting the vowel sounds, not the vowel letters. Spell baked and sunset, about 40 seconds a word. Reread A Day at the Lake, pages 1 to 4, for expression. Then each child echo-reads one sentence of the chosen bird page after you, a finger under each word.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictate "Pete rides a bike." Say it whole, then a word at a time; Pete is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Pete and bike the last e is silent and in rides the e stays. Then each child prints two pairs the written feedback marked (every form was taught by day 27; this page is practice and is not collected). Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Observation cohort

Cohort 1, ten children not yet recorded, one at a time at your table, 3 minutes each. Your cards stay in the folder: catnip, pilot and cupcake; their divided forms cat-nip, pi-lot and cup-cake; and the ending list jumps, dishes, resting, missed, yelled, rusted, softer, softest, waves, waving, waved. First, half a minute. **Ask.** "Count the parts in each word by its vowel sounds." ([[s03-A30]]) Review: every syllable has a vowel sound (days 79 and 99). Expect two, two, two; cupcake has two vowel sounds, though its letters u, a and e look like three (error H). Second, 45 seconds. **Ask.** "Read each word, part by part." ([[s03-A31]]) Expect each part read, then blended; a word said from a picture or a guess is error I. Third, one minute and three-quarters. **Ask.** "Read each word." ([[s03-A32]]) Expect every ending kept: missed ends /t/, yelled /d/, rusted adds a part, and waving is not wave (error J). Fill only the cells a recent record has not shown, and cite that record. A child may ask for a word again: shown with help. Say what you saw in one line to the child; the written note goes on the record.

The other twenty work in pairs at three stations, ten minutes each, and you hear no one else: reread A Day at the Lake a page each; chain bake, baked, baking, bakes and sunset, napkin with letter cards; say the riddle to a partner, who says back the shape clue and the use clue.

Ten children at 3 minutes fills all 30 minutes with no room to walk, so seat the next child as the last one leaves. A child with a recent shown entry takes less. A child not finished at minute 30 is recorded as unfinished and heard on day 119; do not hurry one.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the cohort rows, the exit marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Write one line for each cohort child who showed error H, error I or error J; the retry is on day 119. List by name the children who gave a liking (error E) for day 117, and those who picked a detail (error A) for day 119. Put every riddle and sketch in the riddle folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a paper in half for the riddle card, two clues on the front and the name inside, and say the exit card aloud. One child: write one riddle, take the three observation tasks with a grown-up at the table, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No riddle card: fold a paper, two clues on the front, the name inside.

**Skip.** The observation, the stations and the talk.

- **10 min. Choose.** Your child picks two true clues about a bird from a short page, one for what a part looks like and one for what it does. **Say.** "Where does the page say it?" Expect: a finger on the sentence.
- **10 min. Write.** Print the two clues under "I have ___. I ___. What am I?" and draw the bird. The name stays on the back.
- **5 min. Say it.** Your child says the riddle aloud, and you guess.
- **5 min. Print.** "Pete rides a bike."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X116a]] [[s03-X116b]] Read a short bird card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture the clues fit, then says the first clue without the bird's name.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the talk; keep the clues, the draft and the exit. Longer: a child with two clues drafts a third from the second page of the chosen bird.

### Words and supports

Words: riddle, clue, shape, use, source, flap. Frames: "I have ___." "I ___." "What am I?" "The page says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick the bird a clue set describes and say a clue with the name held back. A child who gave a liking (error E) gets a five-minute repair on three fresh cards on day 117. A child who picked the detail (error A) waits for day 119, with day 115's error A and error B. Cohort 1's misses in counting (error H), blending (error I) and endings (error J) are retried on day 119. Day 117 is the peer question and the revision.
