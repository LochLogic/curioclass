# Exit cards, days 116 to 119

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 116 card (s03-X116a, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A gannet has a long, pointed bill. It dives into the sea to catch fish." Show two pictures: the gannet, a fish alone. Say: "Which picture should answer a riddle with these clues?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The gannet.

## Day 116 card (s03-X116b, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Say the first clue, without the bird's name." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** I have a long, pointed bill.

## Day 117 card (s03-X117a, W.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Source: An emu has long legs. They help it run fast. Draft: I fly with my legs. Peer strip: What do the legs help it do?" Show two pictures: the running legs, the flying picture. Say: "Which picture fits the peer's question?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The running legs.

## Day 117 card (s03-X117b, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Say a clue that answers the question." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** I run fast with strong legs.

## Day 117 card (s03-X117c, L.1.2d)

This card is invented for practice. Show the draft card "A bird eats a grb." and two word cards, grb and grub. Read the sentence aloud with the word grub spoken. Say: "Which spelling matches the sound-spelling map? Point to it." Everyone marks it on the page; the heard six may point at your table.

**Right answer:** grub. A child who points to grb has left the vowel out (error M).

## Reading exits of days 118 and 119

These exits are read from the unit's texts and have no card. Day 118 (s03-X118av2, s03-X118bv2): the Feathers part of Duck at the Pond, page 2; draw the duck's feathers, and the duck's beak.

## Day 119 retry (the exit)

The retry reads the opening of Heron by the Water and Where a Heron Lives (s03-X119av2, s03-X119bv2); draw the heron in shallow water by a marsh, and a fish alone. Each child retries only the missed component; check the answer against the page. Topic and retell: "Which picture is this part about?" (the heron in shallow water). Features: "Point to where the glossary says what marsh means." Writing: "Say a topic sentence." or "Say a closing sentence." Participation: "Say one fact and point to its source." Foundations and conversation retries use the reserved word lists and a partner exchange about the part (restate, then reply). They never rescore earlier records.
