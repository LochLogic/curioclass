# Section 3.10 opener: the riddle, the drawing, the observation and the unit response

## What the section does

Days 116 to 119 finish the expert-bird task. Children draft a riddle from verified facts, revise one clue after a partner's question, finish the scientific drawing and share the riddle game. Three groups of ten children give the foundations observation on 116, 117 and 118. Day 119 completes missing evidence and retries one missed part.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60 (day 119 differs). Days 116 to 118 end the topic block with a five-minute exit read aloud to you; you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A clue holds back the name.** A fact stands alone; the name comes at the answer.
- **A peer question asks for one change.** Answer it from the source.
- **A drawing names only what is there.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail named as the topic. Sort again.
- **E:** a liking in place of a fact. Say a fact with the name held back.
- **B:** a fact no source gives. Point to the sentence.
- **C:** a picture trusted over the words. Check the label against the sentence.
- **D:** the cover used to find a fact. Follow the contents and heading.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
