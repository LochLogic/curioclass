# Day 89: Return the misses and find a fact by its feature

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the marks into groups: day 86's assessment (errors A, B and D), day 87's exit (error C) and day 88's grid and exit (error K, and error M on the sample). Write each child's missed component on a repair note. Draw the fresh repair cards. Put out the flamingo cards, the question cards and the exit pictures face down.

### The day

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." The class first, 4 minutes: name the miss the most children made, if more than a third made it, and model it once on a fresh card. For a detail named as the topic (error A): a swing card, "A swing has a seat and two chains"; the topic is the swing, the seat is one detail. For a fact the page does not say (error B): a teapot card, "A teapot has a spout"; say what the card says, not what you know. Then, in the same four minutes, the class traces each route with a finger in the air.

Then up to four repair groups, 2 minutes each, at most six children, about 20 seconds a child, 8 minutes in all: topic and detail (error A), a fact the page gives (error B), the picture against the words (error C: a boat picture with the words "The boat is blue"; the picture shows a red boat; the words win), and a heading match (error D: three fresh toy pictures and three headings on separate strips, the cover face down; the child lays each heading under the picture it names). Each child names the step on the repair note, then tries it once. Up to 24 children are seen this way; the others get the class model above and the partner try below. The rest of the room works on the retry.

Retry on paper, 8 minutes: each child does only the missed component on the flamingo card, drawing or placing the detail (error A), tracing the sentence with a finger (error B), marking a picture symbol or a word symbol beside each source on a fresh pair (error C), or matching the page-2 picture of the flamingo card to its heading with a finger, the cover put away, on a fresh page (error D). Mark each repair note: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 86's assessment stays marked as it was; only the retry is new.

#### 15 min. Supported rereading

Review: page 2 of Feet That Help, from day 87. Pairs reread it aloud, taking a paragraph each, for three minutes, then again swapping. Hear six children, 90 seconds each, nine minutes in all, one at a time at your table while the others reread; a child who is stuck is given the word once. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Look for: the child points under each word and rereads a stuck sentence.

#### 10 min. Partner exchange

Pairs use the flamingo card. Say: "A partner says something about the page. Say back what the speaker said, then add or ask." Review: restate before you reply, from day 88. One pair model: the speaker says "A flamingo has a curved beak." The listener answers "You said the beak is curved. What does it do?" An unrelated turn, such as "I like pink", is error K. All fifteen pairs talk at once for four minutes, two turns each; hear six pairs, 40 seconds each, four minutes in all. Mark: shown, shown with help (you say the first words), not yet seen.

#### 10 min. Find and point

Four question cards, each with a picture: which part holds a branch, which part helps a bird stay warm, which part moves a bird through the air, which part helps a duck eat. A pair takes one card, 4 minutes, and finds the fact in the bird articles by the heading over each paragraph, the cover put away, and points to the sentence. Then they swap. The teacher listens to six children, 50 seconds each, five minutes in all, while the others work. A child who goes to the cover has used the cover alone (error D): ask which feature of the page tells them where to look.

#### 5 min. Exit

Show page 1 of Wings in Motion and read its contents aloud, then the part headed Round Wings Flap. The others finish; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the three pictures: a sparrow's round wings flapping, the cover with its flock of birds, a nest. Say: "Point to the picture that marks the fact you found." ([[s03-X89av2]]) Then, with the pages in view: "Which feature helped you find the fact?" ([[s03-X89bv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the page once), not yet seen. A child who points to the cover has used the cover alone (error D).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say me and men; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: me. Blend /s/ /m/ /a/ /sh/, "smash". Say chop and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /ch/, /o/, /p/. Segment chest: /ch/ /e/ /s/ /t/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 14, lesson 71. New: an open syllable ends in a vowel that says its name. Show the Vowel House pairs on cards: me and met, we and wet, go and got, no and not. Say each and tap the sounds: one vowel, then a consonant shuts it in, or no consonant and the vowel is open. Review: a closed syllable, from day 79; he and she, from day 85. Words: me, we, be, go, no. Then a two-part chain, clap, chop and read, one at a time: ro-bot, o-pen, la-bel. The first part is open, the second closed. The first time the class sees an open syllable in a longer word is today. Silent e and vowel teams are not taught yet. About 40 seconds a word.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the pairs R r and G g (every letter form was taught by day 27). Dictate two words, we and go, then: "Pat has a robot." Say it whole, then a word at a time; a and has are mapped. Children say each word slowly, tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; robot is two parts, ro and bot. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap the sounds again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names. Three minutes: for error M on day 88's sample, stretch and map every sound, then write the word, on fresh taught words: mud, flag, crab, one at a time, 30 seconds a child. Then they map, blend, read, spell and reread for the rest of their ten minutes. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table with Pam's Backpack, the cycle 13 reader: each child reads a page and spells a closed word on a card, 50 seconds each, one child at a time. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread the reader, chain words with letter cards, sort open and closed words. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the repair marks, the exit marks, the partner marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. List by name the children whose route still used the cover only (error D) for day 90's repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards on scrap paper and read the articles from the page. One child: retry the missed step alone on a fresh card, find one fact by its heading, and do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: use a library book with a contents page. No partner: say both parts aloud.

**Skip.** The repair groups, the rotation and the stations.

- **10 min. Retry.** Your child tries the step on the repair note: topic and detail, a fact the page gives, the picture against the words, or a picture matched to its heading.
- **10 min. Find it.** Open a nonfiction book. **Say.** "Which page tells about feet? Which feature told you?" Expect: your child names the contents or a heading.
- **5 min. Words.** Clap and chop robot, then print "Pat has a robot."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X89av2]] [[s03-X89bv2]] Show the contents of Wings in Motion and read Round Wings Flap. Your child points, then says which feature helped.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair, the exit and the sample; cut the partner exchange. Longer: a child finds a second fact by a different feature and says which.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, feature, source. Frames: "I found it by ___." "You said ___. What ___?" Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once. Enlarged cards for any child who wants them.

### Tomorrow

Day 89 repaired day 86, 87 and 88's misses and took the exit. A child who used the cover alone (error D) and a fact the page does not say (error B) are named for day 90's five-minute repair. Day 90 begins the beak research.
