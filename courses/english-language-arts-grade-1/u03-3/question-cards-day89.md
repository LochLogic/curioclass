# Day 89 question cards

Four cards, each with a picture and a spoken question: "Which part holds a branch?" "Which part helps a bird stay warm?" "Which part moves a bird through the air?" "Which part helps a duck eat?" Each child finds the fact by a feature route in the four articles.
