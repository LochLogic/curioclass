# Day 89 flamingo card (retry)

The retry card for the repair groups and the partner exchange. Page 1: the cover with a picture of a flamingo, the contents (Curved Beaks, page 2; Glossary, page 3) and a printed menu with Beaks and Feet. Page 2: the heading Curved Beaks, the label beak and two sentences: "A flamingo has a curved beak. It sifts tiny animals from the water." Page 3: the glossary (sift: to keep small bits of food and let the water go). Each child retries only the component the check showed was missed.
