# Section 3.3 opener: an annotated drawing, a science talk and the first response

## What the section does

Days 87 to 89 turn what the class found into a labeled drawing, a talk and a response. Each child draws a bird foot, labels two parts, adds a source note and changes one detail by pointing to a sentence. On day 88 the class hears a short story about a child at a window and talks with the teacher in small groups, one child at a time. Day 89 is a response day: the teacher returns the check work, repairs what it showed and sets a fresh retry.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A story is not research evidence.** It gives a thing to notice; the article gives the fact that explains it.
- **A good reply answers the speaker.** Say back what the speaker said, then add or ask.
- **A retry asks only the part that was missed.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** a picture fact is taken for a words fact. Mark each source with a picture or word symbol.
- **K:** a reply is unrelated. Restate the speaker's point first.
- **M:** a vowel is left out of a spelling. Stretch and map every sound.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
