# Poem page

The poem Feather Coat from the unit 3 text g1-u3-03, printed on one page in large type with a small drawing of the round bird in its feather coat.

My coat has no buttons.
My coat has no seams.
Feathers grow all over me,
soft as morning dreams.

When the cold wind blows,
I fluff up my feathers
and keep the warm air close,
whatever the weather.

It is reread for fluency with the beat clapped; the words are heard and said, not decoded.
