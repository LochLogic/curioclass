# Day 127: Reread, then check the details

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 126's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the repair card and its three pictures below. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Mix the eight sort cards. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 126's exit tickets. Repair for day 126's exit (s04-X126a, error AB): a child who picked a person or an action the card never gives. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Mia feels hot in the sun. Mia tells Coach Bo. Coach Bo gives her a cup of water." Lay three pictures: Mia telling Coach Bo, Mia running to the bus, Coach Bo giving her a cup of water. **Ask.** "Sort the pictures: on the card, or not on the card." Pairs sort in 60 seconds; then you touch the sentence on the card for each picture on the card, and three pairs say which picture is not on the card, 15 seconds each. Expect: telling and giving are on the card; running to the bus is not. A child who keeps the bus picture took an event the card never gives.

#### 12 min. Reread difficult passages

Review: echo and choral reading, a phrase at a time (unit 1, day 5). Today you reread the words that are hard to say well: Dad's list on page 5 and Ms. Torres's words on page 10; her last words on page 11 are judged, not echoed. You say each phrase with its pause and the class echoes it, no one reads alone. Do page 5 first, "We stay back." (pause) "We keep it quiet." (pause) "We do not give it food or water." Then page 10, a phrase at a time, 7 minutes in all. Then judge a given reading, with no one saying the line: you read the last line of page 11 twice, once in one flat rush and once kindly with its pause, and children say thumb up or thumb down for which reading is kind and which keeps the pause, 3 minutes, six children saying why in one word each.

Check: the kind reading is the second one, with a pause between the two parts.

#### 5 min. Poem reread

Review: Feather Coat (day 108). Show the printed page. Read it once, then the class joins in a choral reading with the beat clapped. No new teaching of the poem: the words are heard and said, not decoded.

#### 13 min. Confirm details

Today you check three things children say about Nia Calls for Help. Reread the page you need each time, then ask. Three asks, 3 minutes each, thumbs up or thumbs down, and a partner says one word of why.

1. **Ask.** "Kit says Nia's story says do not touch the dove. Is Kit right?" Expect: yes; page 2.
2. **Ask.** "Lou says Nia phones the helper in Nia's story. Is Lou right?" Expect: no; Coach Lee phones.
3. **Ask.** "Max says both stories say a hurt wild bird needs a trained helper. Is Max right?" Expect: yes. Reread page 3 of Ben and the Bird and page 3 of Nia Calls for Help.

Then six children are heard, 20 seconds each, on the reason for their thumb on one ask.

#### 20 min. Detail check

Hand out the two-column sort sheet: Ben's story on the left, Nia's story on the right. Mix the eight cards, each with a small picture; you read every card aloud and no child decodes one. Say: "Sort each card to the story it comes from." Each child sorts the eight cards, 8 minutes, and a partner checks one card against the story's picture, 20 seconds a pair, six pairs heard. Then **Ask.** "Find the two cards, one from each column, that show the same thing." Children match four pairs: the robin and the dove (a hurt bird on the ground), Dad holds up a hand and Coach Lee says do not touch (the adult keeps the child back), Dad phones and Coach Lee phones, and Ben swings his feet and Nia holds her knees (the child waits). Pairs lay the pairs side by side in 6 minutes. No writing is drafted.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which paired-event picture matches the words?" ([[s04-X127a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each, the card in view for the first three. The first three are asked stems b and c. **Ask.** "What is the same?" ([[s04-X127b]]) **Ask.** "What is different?" ([[s04-X127c]]) The last three have page 11 of Ben and the Bird open and are asked stem d, and then the three Nia pages are laid out for stem e. **Ask.** "Say Ms. Torres' last words, the way she says them." ([[s04-X127d]]) **Ask.** "Which page shows the trained helper coming to the dove?" ([[s04-X127e]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card or the page once), not yet seen. A child who puts the events in reverse order (error AC) goes to day 128's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say pin and pine: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /ch/ /ur/ /ch/. Isolate the last sound of church. Segment curl.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 20, lesson 103. Build, take apart and write er, ir and ur words with the map. Map one irregular word, over: o says /oh/, then v, then er; read it whole. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: turn, burn, burst, curb, curl, church. Say each, tap the sounds, write it.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Copy two sentences from the day's reading, "Ben waits. Dad listens.", one letter form checked, 5 minutes. Dictate "The girl can stir the fern." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Hammer, all ten pages, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with Looking for Mars and A Day at the Lake for the rest.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 128's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud, draw the pictures on paper and make the two columns on a page. One child: judge the two readings of page 11, sort the eight cards into two columns, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read the last line of page 11 twice, once flat and rushed, once kindly with a pause. **Say.** "Which reading is kind?" Expect: the second, with a pause between the two parts.
- **10 min. Check Nia.** Read page 2 of Nia Calls for Help. **Say.** "Does Nia's story say do not touch the dove?" Expect: yes, Coach Lee says it.
- **5 min. The poem.** Say a line of Feather Coat to clap with.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X127a]] [[s04-X127b]] [[s04-X127c]] [[s04-X127d]] [[s04-X127e]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the matching picture and says what is the same and what is different, then says Ms. Torres' last words and points to the page in Nia's story where the helper comes.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the page 11 judging, the sort and the exit; cut the poem reread. Longer: a child who matches the four pairs quickly says one more detail Nia's story has that Ben's does not.

### Words and supports

Words: reread, check, kindly, helper, phone. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say Ms. Torres' words well and who can confirm a detail against the short story. A child who put the events in reverse order (error AC) gets a fresh pair in day 128's opening repair (s04-X127a). Day 128 reads chapter 5 and builds a whole-story map.
