# Exit cards, days 125 to 130

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 125 card (s04-X125a, RL.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Ari sits on the step. Bea sits on the wall. Both wait for the library to open." Show three pictures: both children waiting for the library, two children in matching blue scarves, two open libraries. Say: "Which comparison picture fits?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Both children wait for the library to open.

## Day 125 card (s04-X125b, RL.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "Ari sits on the step. Bea sits on the wall. Both wait for the library to open." Say: "What is one thing the same?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Both wait for the library to open.

## Day 125 card (s04-X125c, RL.1.9)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "Ari sits on the step. Bea sits on the wall. Both wait for the library to open." Say: "What is one thing different?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Ari sits on the step; Bea sits on the wall.
