# Exit pictures, days 125 to 130

Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read anything here; you reread the named story pages aloud and show their pictures. Nothing on this sheet is made up: every exit asks about a page of Ben and the Bird or Nia Calls for Help.

## Day 125 pictures (s04-X125av2, RL.1.9)

Reread page 3 of Ben and the Bird and page 2 of Nia Calls for Help, to "if we pick it up," aloud. Three pictures: each grown-up standing back from the bird, a robin beside a dove, a grown-up holding the bird. The same pages answer s04-X125bv2 and s04-X125cv2.

**Right answer:** Each grown-up standing back from the bird.

## Day 126 pictures (s04-X126av2, RL.1.9)

Reread the first lines of page 5 of Ben and the Bird, to "while we wait," aloud. Three pictures: a woman talking on a phone, Ben giving advice, the girl with the dog. The same lines answer s04-X126bv2 (why she is the one to ask).

**Right answer:** The woman on the phone, the bird helper.

## Day 127 picture pairs (s04-X127av2, RL.1.9)

Reread the first lines of page 1 of each story aloud: Ben and the Bird to "It sits very still," Nia Calls for Help to "under the window." Three pairs: each child going to a place, then finding a bird; each child finding a bird, then going to the place; both children shouting. The same lines answer s04-X127bv2 and s04-X127cv2.

**Right answer:** Each child goes to a place, then finds a bird.

## Day 128 picture strips (s04-X128av2, RL.1.9)

Reread pages 4 and 5 of Nia Calls for Help aloud. Three strips: Coach Lee phones, Nia waits, the helper lifts the dove; the helper lifts the dove, then Coach Lee phones; Nia carries the dove away. The same pages answer s04-X128bv2 (how the ending fixes the dove's problem).

**Right answer:** Coach Lee phones, Nia waits, the helper lifts the dove.

## Day 130 pictures (s04-X130av2, RL.1.9)

Reread page 6 of Ben and the Bird and the last lines of page 3 of Nia Calls for Help, from "Nia stands very still," aloud. Three pictures: Ben speaking quietly to a girl with a dog beside Nia standing still; a big dog beside a gray dove; both children shouting. The same pages answer s04-X130bv2 and s04-X130cv2.

**Right answer:** Ben speaking quietly and Nia standing still.
