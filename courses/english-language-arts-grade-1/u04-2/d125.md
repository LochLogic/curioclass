# Day 125: Two children wait while an adult phones

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 124's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the repair card and its two strips below. Hand back day 124's drawings (s04-X124dv2) and the dated printing sets, each with its written line. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Lay out the six sort cards and the six sentence cards, mixed. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back the drawings (s04-X124dv2); each child looks at what Ben does in theirs. Repair for day 124's exit (s04-X124bv2, error A): a child who named how things look, not what the children do. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Tia waters a plant with a can. Rao waters a plant with a cup. Each plant is tall." Lay two strips beside it: "Kit says: both children water a plant." "Lou says: both plants are tall." Say: "Which strip tells what the children do?" Children show a thumb for Kit or for Lou (about 10 seconds). Expect: Kit. Then pairs touch the words on the card that Kit used, in 15 seconds, and three pairs are heard. A child who thumbs for Lou took how the plants look, not what the children do.

#### 15 min. Reread two phone scenes

Review: you heard both stories whole (days 120 to 124). Today you reread only the phone scenes. Read page 4 of Ben and the Bird (about 65 words) and pages 3 and 4 of Nia Calls for Help (about 110 words) aloud with their pictures, 3 minutes in all. Then three asks, about 3 minutes each, in pairs with the turn card.

1. **Ask.** "Touch the person who phones in each picture." Expect: Dad; Coach Lee.
2. **Ask.** "I say four lines. Hold up one finger for a line from Ben's story and two fingers for Nia's." Say: "We found a robin in the garden." "A dove hit our window and cannot fly." "Yes. Yes. I see." "Yes, we will keep everyone away." Expect: 1, 2, 1, 2.
3. **Ask.** "Touch the part of each picture that shows what the child does while the adult phones." Expect: Ben holds Dad's sleeve; Nia stands very still, hands together.

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: the Both and But picture (day 124). Model aloud, with no child speaking. Lay the picture on the wall. Take the sort card an adult phones the helper and think aloud: "Dad phones in Ben's story. Coach Lee phones in Nia's. A grown-up phones in both." Place it in the Both column. Take Ben holds Dad's sleeve and place it in the But column, with Ben: "Only Ben does this." Then partners sort the other four cards onto the picture, one card at a time, thumbs up or down for each placement, 3 minutes. Then two more asks, about 3 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Sort the six cards onto the Both and But picture." Expect: an adult phones the helper and the child waits beside the adult go under Both; Ben holds Dad's sleeve and Dad and Ben listen go under But on Ben's side; Nia stands very still and Coach Lee and Nia wait go under But on Nia's side.
2. **Ask.** "Pip says Ben and Nia are different because Ben's bird has an orange chest and Nia's bird is gray. Does that tell what happens to the children?" Expect: no.
3. **Ask.** "What does each child do while the adult phones?" Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds; then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Expect: Ben holds Dad's sleeve and listens; Nia stands very still and quiet.

#### 8 min. Practise nouns, agreement and pronouns

Review: a matching verb agrees with its noun (day 122) and a pronoun takes the place of a noun (day 123). Show six sentence cards from the two pages: Dad phones. Dad and Ben listen. Coach Lee phones. Nia and Coach Lee wait. She stands still. He holds the sleeve. Three asks, about 2 minutes 30 seconds each.

1. **Ask.** "Sort the cards: one person acts, or more than one acts." Expect: Dad phones, Coach Lee phones, She stands still and He holds the sleeve are one; Dad and Ben listen and Nia and Coach Lee wait are more than one.
2. **Ask.** "Say the matching verb: Dad ___ ; Dad and Ben ___ ." (phones; phone) Expect: phones, then phone.
3. **Ask.** "Show the card She stands still. Which name does she take the place of?" Show the pronoun cards (he, she, it, they, their) for support. Expect: Nia.

#### 12 min. Compare on paper

Hand out the event pictures sheet: two boxes headed Both and But, with the small pictures from the phone scenes at the top. Say the word labels once with their pictures: phone, sleeve, still. Each child draws what an adult does in both stories in the Both box, and what one child does in the But box, then copies one word label under a box (6 minutes). Partners point to their own box and say which box the other child's picture belongs in, 15 seconds a pair, and six pairs are heard. You scribe the labels for six children who ask. The drawing shows what a child or adult does, never how a bird looks (error A).

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 3 of Ben and the Bird and page 2 of Nia Calls for Help, to "if we pick it up," and show the three pictures from the exit sheet. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which comparison picture fits?" ([[s04-X125av2]]) All children also mark one of three small pictures from Nia Calls for Help. **Ask.** "Mark the picture where Coach Lee phones the helper." ([[s04-X125d]]) While the rest finish, the heard six come to your table, with both pages open, 30 seconds each; three are asked stem b and three stem c. **Ask.** "What is one thing the same?" ([[s04-X125bv2]]) **Ask.** "What is one thing different?" ([[s04-X125cv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the pages once), not yet seen. A child who names how things look, not what the grown-ups do (error A), goes to day 126's opening repair. A child who marks the page where Nia only fetches her coach has not yet found the phone scene: reread page 3 once.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say fin and fine: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /h/ /er/. Say the first, middle and last sound of herd. Segment turn.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 20, lesson 101, explicit teaching: er, ir and ur are new. Three spellings, one sound, /er/. Add them to the sound-spelling map with fern, bird and fur. Review: ar and or say one sound each (cycle 19). Map one irregular word, about: a says /uh/, then b, then ou says /ow/, then t; read it whole. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: her, herd, hurt, turn, burn, burst. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Read each word whole once it is written.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the four forms the written feedback on the day 120 to 124 sets marked, twice each, 5 minutes; this is the last revision before day 129 (nothing new is taught). While they print, walk and read each child's feedback line aloud, about 8 seconds each. Dictate "Her fur is soft." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map er, ir and ur, blend, read, spell, reread, and the irregular word about. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Looking for Mars, the whole book, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell er, ir and ur words in pairs at stations, with A Day at the Lake for the rest. Cycle 20's reader waits until its spellings and words are mapped; it opens on day 126.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 126's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud, draw the pictures on paper and the Both and But boxes on a page. One child: reread the two phone scenes, point to the person who phones in each, sort the six cards into Both and But, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort, the sentence cards and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Ben.** Read page 4 of chapter 2 of Ben and the Bird aloud. **Say.** "What does Ben do while Dad phones?" Expect: holds Dad's sleeve and listens.
- **10 min. Nia.** Read pages 3 and 4 of Nia Calls for Help. **Say.** "What does Nia do while Coach Lee phones?" Expect: stands very still and quiet.
- **5 min. Both and But.** Write "an adult phones" and "holds a sleeve" on two slips. Your child puts each slip in the Both pile or the But pile.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X125av2]] [[s04-X125bv2]] [[s04-X125cv2]] [[s04-X125d]] Reread page 3 of Ben and the Bird and the start of page 2 of Nia Calls for Help. Your child points to the picture that fits, says one thing the same and one thing different, then marks the picture where the adult phones.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the sort, the exit and the printing; cut the pronoun card to one sentence. Longer: a child who sorts quickly finds one more card that goes under Both.

### Words and supports

Words: phone, still, quiet, sleeve, both, but, fern, bird, fur, about. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can compare what happens to two children and who can find the phone scene. A child who named how things look (error A) gets a fresh card in day 126's opening repair ([[s04-X125bv2]]). Day 126 reads chapter 4 and finds who can safely help the robin.
