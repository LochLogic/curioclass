# Tense strip

A wall strip in three panels headed Yesterday, Today and Tomorrow, with a small sun picture low, high and rising. Under each panel is a space for a verb card. The model verb is call: called, calls, will call. Six sentence cards are sorted under the panels on day 126 and the strip is used again on day 128 (lifted, lifts).
