# Day 128: The whole story, from the gate to the van

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 127's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the two repair pictures below. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Mix the four event cards for the class chart. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 127's exit (s04-X127a, error AC): a child who put two events in the wrong order. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Ola unpacks her bag. Then Ola sits at her desk." Lay the two-event strip, with first and next, and two pictures: Ola sitting at her desk and Ola unpacking her bag. **Ask.** "Put the pictures on the strip, first and next." Pairs build it in 60 seconds, then you read the card once more and touch each word that tells the order, and three pairs say the order aloud, 15 seconds each. Expect: unpacks first, sits next. A child who puts the desk picture first took the likelier order, not the one the card gives.

#### 15 min. Read chapter 5

Read pages 12 to 15 aloud with the pictures (about 400 words, four minutes). Say these words once with their pictures: towel, carrier box, careful (from day 126: full of care). Reread page 13 slowly. Then two asks, about 4 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Touch the words on page 13 that show how Ms. Torres moves." Read the page again a sentence at a time; children touch the picture and you point to the sentence. Expect: slowly; gently; both hands.
2. **Ask.** "Hold up one finger when Ben stays far away, two when Ben talks to a grown-up." Reread page 12, then page 14. Expect: one finger on page 13, two on page 14.

Say the safety words together: "We never pick up a wild bird. We call a grown-up." A child upset by the hurt bird may look at the picture of Ben smiling up at Ms. Torres on page 14.

#### 15 min. Talk

Three asks, about 4 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Lia says that at the end Ben picks up the robin. Max says that at the end Ben has called a grown-up and the helper has come. Whose answer is right?" Expect: Max's. Touch page 15.
2. **Ask.** "Max also says that at first Ben wants to pick up the robin, and at the end he does not. Does Max tell how the ending is different from the beginning?" Thumbs. Expect: yes. Reread page 2, "Then he stops," and page 15, "Today he did not pick up a wild bird."
3. **Ask.** "Which words on page 14 tell what Ms. Torres thanks Ben for?" Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds; then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Expect: you called a grown-up, and you stayed back.

#### 8 min. Adjectives

Review: describing words such as tiny and little (day 22). Today's new idea: a word that describes a thing is called an adjective. Show four cards from the pages, each with a small drawing: orange chest, soft towel, small white van, careful hands. Point to the describing word in each and say what it tells: the color, the feel, the size, the way. Model aloud, with no child speaking: "The robin has an orange chest. Orange tells what color." Then **Ask.** "Touch the describing word on each card." Expect: orange, soft, small and white, careful. Then a second ask. **Ask.** "Finish the sentence with a describing card: Ms. Torres has ___ hands." Expect: careful. Say it in the past with the tense strip: "Today Ms. Torres lifts the robin. Yesterday Ms. Torres lifted the robin."

#### 12 min. Whole-story map

Hand out the whole-story map: three boxes in order, Beginning, Middle and End. Mix the four event cards: sees, calls, waits, helper comes. Say: "Draw one picture in each box that shows the story, and copy one word label from the cards under it." Each child works alone for 8 minutes. The class chart holds the pairs of events: sees and calls, then waits, then helper comes. Walk and read each map aloud, 10 seconds a child. The map shows what Ben and the helper do, never how the robin looks (error A); a child who puts the helper first has the order reversed (error AC).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three picture strips. All children mark a strip on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture strip fits from beginning to ending?" ([[s04-X128a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, with the card in view, 30 seconds each; three are asked stem b and three the stem about page 13, with page 13 open. **Ask.** "How does the ending answer the first problem?" ([[s04-X128b]]) **Ask.** "Ms. Torres kneels by the robin. What does she do to keep it safe?" ([[s04-X128c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card or the page once), not yet seen. A child who reverses the strip (error AC) goes to day 130's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cub and cube: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /th/ /ir/ /d/. Say the first, middle and last sound of hurt. Segment curb.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 20, lesson 104. Read and write short sentences with er, ir and ur, and check that each one makes sense. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: fern, herd, third, shirt, hurt. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Read the sentence "The herd is on the fern." once, whole.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Copy a two-line sentence from the day's reading, "Ms. Torres lifts it. The robin is safe.", each form checked against its model, 5 minutes. Dictate "Her bird is not hurt." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Hammer, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with Looking for Mars and A Day at the Lake for the rest.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Day 129 is the first check: set out the response sheet, keep the sample words in your folder and name day 130's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud, draw the pictures on paper and fold a page into three boxes. One child: reread page 13, touch the describing word on each card, draw the three boxes and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The adjective cards and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Chapter 5.** Read pages 12 and 13 aloud. **Say.** "What does Ms. Torres do to keep the robin safe?" Expect: a towel, both hands, the carrier box.
- **10 min. The whole story.** Your child draws three boxes: the start, the middle, the end of Ben's story. **Say.** "How does the ending answer the first problem?" Expect: the helper takes the robin and Ben did not pick it up.
- **5 min. Describing words.** Say: "A soft towel. An orange chest." Your child points to the describing word in each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X128a]] [[s04-X128b]] [[s04-X128c]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the strip that fits from beginning to ending, says how the ending answers the first problem, and says what Ms. Torres does to keep the robin safe.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the whole-story map and the exit; cut the tense sentences. Longer: a child who finishes the map says one more thing Ben learned.

### Words and supports

Words: towel, carrier box, careful, adjective, beginning, middle, end. Frames: "At first ___. At the end ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can follow the whole story in order and say what the helper did. Day 129 is the first check: both new stories are read aloud with no help, and the cycle 20 sample runs in the rotation. A child who reversed the strip (error AC) gets a fresh strip on day 130 (s04-X128a).
