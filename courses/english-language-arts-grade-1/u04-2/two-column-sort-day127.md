# Two-column sort

A sheet with two columns headed Ben's story and Nia's story, and eight small cards to place: a robin on the ground by the gate, Dad holds up a hand, Dad phones, Ben swings his feet on the bench (Ben's story); a dove on the ground by the bench, Coach Lee says do not touch, Coach Lee phones, Nia holds her knees on the grass (Nia's story). Children sort each card, then match four pairs, one from each column, that show the same thing.
