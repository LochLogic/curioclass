# Day 129: Check the two stories

## Teacher page

### Before class

Use day 128's exit marks to name the heard six and the catch-up group. Keep the sample words in your folder. Close every folder. No help, no hints.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "These stories are made up for practice." Hand out both pages. Rules: work alone, draw, label, pass if you wish.

#### 20 min. Check sitting

Read twice, no help: "Story A: Ivy finds a lost bottle by the swings. She hands it to the teacher on duty. Story B: Dale finds a lost hat by the slide. He puts it in the lost-and-found box." Say: "Draw and label one thing that happens in both stories." ((s04-A10)) Then: "Draw and label one thing that is different in the two stories." ((s04-A11)) A scribe may write the labels. Walking, say: "Point to or tell one thing that happens in each story that shows your drawing." ((s04-A12)) Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 12 min. Reread a favourite page

Unscored. Each child says one page of Ben and the Bird to a partner, then they swap. Hear six pairs.

#### 18 min. Draw and tell

Unscored. Each child draws a favourite page and tells a partner why. Write nothing.

#### 5 min. Close

Collect the sheets. Fill the heard-six column.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say tub and tube: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /ch/ /ur/ /n/. Segment burst.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 20, lesson 105. No new teaching; the sample runs in the rotation. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: her, bird, hurt, fern.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Copy "Ben waits.", 4 minutes. Dictate "The bird sat on the fern." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes. After class score the dated printing sets of days 120 to 124 from the folders: each of the 52 forms ((s04-A9)).

#### 30 min. Rotation

The cycle 20 sample runs here. The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: map, blend, read, spell, then the sample. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Hammer, each child heard 20 seconds in the book and 20 on the sample. Show the held word card: "Read this word." ([[s04-A3]]) Then the card goes away: "Write the word I say." ([[s04-A4]]) Never say the words aloud to the class. The others reread in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the marks to the record sheet. Regroup for cycle 21. List each miss by name for day 130 and keep the sheets.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two boxes on paper. One child: do each item aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No crayons: use a pencil.

**Skip.** The sample and the rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment: two stories.** Read twice, with no help: "Rosa finds a dropped ball by the fence. She gives it to the coach. Tom finds a dropped cap by the bench. He hangs it on a hook."
- **15 min. Draw.** **Say.** "Draw and label one thing that happens in both stories." ((s04-A10)) Expect: each child finds a dropped thing and puts it where it belongs. **Say.** "Draw and label one thing that is different." ((s04-A11)) Expect: Rosa gives the ball to the coach; Tom hangs the cap on a hook. **Say.** "Tell one thing that happens in each story that shows your drawing." ((s04-A12))
- **10 min. Print.** Your child prints the alphabet, capitals then small letters. ((s04-A9))

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the sitting and the sample. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: same, different, label. A scribe, a home language and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Each miss waits for day 130, which hands back each sheet (s04-A10) and repairs it with a new pair of passages. The sample marks give the cycle 21 groups.
