# Event pictures

One sheet, used on days 125 and 130. A page with two large boxes headed Both and But, and a row of small plain line pictures under them of the phone scenes in the two stories: a child standing back while a grown-up phones the helper (one picture from Ben and the Bird, one from Nia Calls for Help), one actor per picture. Children draw or place what the children do in the Both box, and copy one word label. The pictures show events from the texts only, never an exit case and never how a thing looks.
