# Day 126: Who can safely help the robin

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read day 125's exit marks and find the commonest miss. Make the repair card and its four strips below. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Set out the tense strip with its six cards and the four sort cards, mixed. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Hand back day 125's exit tickets (s04-X125d). Repair for day 125's exit (s04-X125bv2, error A): a child who named how things look, not what the children do. If another miss was commoner, use that one. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Dev shares an apple with Nan. Eli shares an apple with Pam. Each apple is red." Lay two pictures on the table, a hand and an eye. Mix four strips: "Dev shares an apple." "Eli shares an apple." "Each apple is red." "The apples are round." **Ask.** "Sort the strips: what the children do under the hand, how things look under the eye." Pairs sort in about 90 seconds and three pairs say why one strip went where it did, 15 seconds each. Expect: the two shares under the hand; red and round under the eye. A child who puts "Each apple is red" under the hand took a look for something the children do.

#### 15 min. Read chapter 4

Read pages 10 and 11 aloud with the pictures (about 230 words, two minutes). Say these words once with their pictures: job, check, safe, quiet. Reread page 10 slowly. **Ask.** "Hold up one finger each time Ms. Torres says one thing she does at her job." Expect: four fingers over the page (checks the wing, finds out what is wrong, gives the right food, gives a quiet safe place). Reread page 11 and say the safety words together: "We never pick up a wild bird. We call a grown-up." Say: "Ms. Torres will come to the garden in chapter 5." A child upset by "I cannot promise" may look at the picture of Ben smiling at the phone.

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: the sentence clue (day 121): the sentence beside a word tells what it means. Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Who can safely help the robin?" Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds; then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Expect: Ms. Torres, the trained helper.
2. **Ask.** "Touch the words on page 10 that tell what Ms. Torres's job is." Read the page again, a sentence at a time; children touch the picture of the phone and you point to the sentence. Expect: to care for hurt wild birds. Pairs touch it in 20 seconds; four pairs are heard.
3. **Ask.** "Sort the four cards under Ms. Torres or Ben." Expect: checks the wing and gives the right food go under Ms. Torres; calls a grown-up and stays back and keeps quiet go under Ben. Pairs sort in 2 minutes and three are asked why one card went where it did.

#### 7 min. Tense

Today's new idea: a verb can tell when something happens, in the past, the present or the future. Hang the tense strip with its three panels: Yesterday, Today, Tomorrow. Model aloud, with no child speaking: "Yesterday Ben called Dad. Today Ben calls Dad. Tomorrow Ben will call Dad." Move one card into each panel and point to the word that changed: called, calls, will call. Then lay six sentence cards you read aloud: "Ben called his dad." "Dad phoned the helper." "Ben waits on the bench." "Dad listens." "Ben will call again." "Ms. Torres will come." **Ask.** "Sort the six cards under Yesterday, Today or Tomorrow." Expect: the first two under Yesterday, the next two under Today, the last two under Tomorrow. Pairs sort in 3 minutes and three pairs say which word told them, 15 seconds each.

#### 6 min. Word parts

Today's new idea: a word part at the end can change a word. Review: -ed tells it already happened (trained, day 121). Show the part cards care, -ful and -less. Say: "Care and -ful make careful: full of care. Care and -less make careless: without care." Say careful again: chapter 5 has it. Then lay the root card wait and the ending cards -s, -ed and -ing. **Ask.** "Add the ending card to wait and say the new word." Pairs build waits, waited and waiting in 90 seconds, and three pairs say one aloud, 15 seconds each. Say: "-ed already happened; -ing is happening now."

#### 7 min. Who can help

Hand out the helper card: a page folded in two halves, headed Calls and Cares. Each child draws the child and adult in the first half and the trained helper in the second, then copies one word label from the board, call or care (5 minutes). Walk and ask each child, 10 seconds, "Who is this, and what do they do?" The card never shows a child touching a bird.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread the first lines of page 5 of Ben and the Bird, to "while we wait," and show the three pictures from the exit sheet. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows who tells Dad what to do?" ([[s04-X126av2]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each; three are asked stem b, with page 5 open, and three the stem about chapter 4, with page 11 open. **Ask.** "Why is she the one to ask?" ([[s04-X126bv2]]) **Ask.** "Ms. Torres tells Ben and Dad what to do. What does she tell them to do?" ([[s04-X126c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the page once), not yet seen. A child who names a person or an action the page never gives (error AB) goes to day 127's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Blend /s/ /t/ /er/. Segment first. Isolate the first sound of shirt. Say mat and mate: which has the long vowel sound?

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 20, lesson 102. Connect er, ir and ur to the words on the map and to Sam's Hammer. Map one irregular word, old: o says /oh/ before ld. Read it whole. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: stir, sir, first, girl, bird, shirt, fern. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Read the first pages of the reader once the map holds.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Copy one sentence from the day's reading, "Dad's phone rings.", each form checked against its model, 4 minutes. Decode, then spell, two words from the chain. Dictate "The bird is on the third fern." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map er, ir and ur, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Hammer, pages 1 to 9, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. If the map is not secure for a group, keep Looking for Mars. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations, with Looking for Mars and A Day at the Lake for the rest. Page 10 waits for day 127.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 127's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud, draw the pictures on paper, and fold a page for the helper card. One child: reread pages 10 and 11, say who can safely help, build waits, waited and waiting, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort, the part cards and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Read chapter 4.** Read pages 10 and 11 aloud. **Say.** "What does Ms. Torres tell Ben and Dad to do?" Expect: stay back and keep the robin quiet.
- **10 min. Who can help.** **Say.** "Who can safely help the robin?" Expect: Ms. Torres, the trained helper. Your child draws her on a page and one word, care.
- **5 min. Yesterday and tomorrow.** Say: "Yesterday Ben called Dad. Tomorrow Ben will call Dad." Your child says "Today Ben calls Dad."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X126av2]] [[s04-X126bv2]] [[s04-X126c]] Reread the start of page 5 of the story. Your child points to who tells Dad what to do and says why she is the one to ask, then says what Ms. Torres tells Ben and Dad to do.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the sort, the exit and the printing. Cut the part cards to waits and waited. Longer: a child who sorts quickly says one more sentence about each day on the strip.

### Words and supports

Words: job, check, safe, quiet, trained, careful, careless, waits, waited, waiting. Frames: "A trained helper ___." "Yesterday ___. Today ___. Tomorrow ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can say who safely helps and why. A child who picked a person or an action the page never gives (error AB) gets a fresh card in day 127's opening repair (s04-X126av2). Day 127 rereads Ben's and Ms. Torres's words, then confirms details against Nia Calls for Help.
