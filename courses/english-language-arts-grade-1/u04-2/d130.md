# Day 130: Fix the comparison with a new pair of pages

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 129 sheets and list each miss by name: a likeness of looks (error A), a drawing for an event the story never gives (error AB), events in the wrong order (error AC), an adjective without a sentence (error L), a letter form reversed (error Z). Make the fresh pair of stories and its four strips below. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Show one anonymous drawing from the 129 sitting in which the thing drawn is how something looks, with the name covered. Say: "This is one way a drawing can go. It shows how things look." **Ask.** "What could we draw instead to say what the children do?" Partners say one idea, 20 seconds, and three pairs are heard. Expect: the children finding a thing and giving it to a grown-up. No one is the example of a mistake.

#### 15 min. Reread the new pair

Review: you reread two pages from each story (days 125 to 128). Today's pair is new: page 11 of Ben and the Bird and page 5 of Nia Calls for Help. Read both aloud with their pictures (about 220 words, 2 minutes). Three asks, about 4 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "I say four lines. Hold up one finger for page 11 and two fingers for page 5." Say: "You did exactly what helps most." "You called the right people." "Stay back and keep it quiet." "It is stunned, but it is breathing well." Expect: 1, 2, 1, 2.
2. **Ask.** "Pip says the helper on both pages tells the child they did well. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: yes.
3. **Ask.** "Touch the part of each picture that shows the child is glad." Expect: Ben smiling; Nia smiling.

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: the Both and But picture (day 124). Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "How would you repair the comparison?" Show the anonymous drawing again. Partners use the turn card, two turns each, 40 seconds; then six children are heard, 15 seconds each. Expect: say what the children do, not how things look.
2. **Ask.** "Lia says Ben and Nia are alike because each is glad at the end. Is Lia talking about what the children feel?" Thumbs. Expect: yes.
3. **Ask.** "Sort the four cards onto the Both and But picture." Expect: the child is glad at the end and a grown-up says the child did well go under Both; Ben smiles as he listens goes under But on Ben's side; Nia smiles as she watches goes under But on Nia's side. Pairs sort in 3 minutes and three pairs say why one card went where it did.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 129's assessment, each sheet with one written line (s04-A10), 2 minutes. Sort the class by the misses you listed. If most of the class missed the same thing, run that group whole class in the same minutes. The rest practise the adjective cards in pairs: each pair picks a card and says a whole sentence, "The towel is soft."

First, 5 minutes, the order group (s04-X128a, error AC): set three pictures on the table for a fresh strip: Mo opens a box, Mo takes out a book, Mo reads the book. **Ask.** "Put the pictures in a strip, first, next and last." Expect: opens, takes out, reads. Then you read the card once and touch each word that tells the order.

Then, 10 minutes, the likeness group (s04-A10, error A). Say: "These stories are made up for practice." Read twice: "Story C: Juno finds a spilled cup by the door. She tells the cleaner. Story D: Ravi finds a broken chair by the stairs. He tells the caretaker." Lay a hand and an eye. Mix four strips: "Both children tell a grown-up." "Juno tells the cleaner and Ravi tells the caretaker." "The cup is yellow." "The chair is brown." **Ask.** "Sort the strips: what the children do under the hand, how things look under the eye." Pairs sort in 90 seconds and three pairs say why one strip went where it did. Then each child fixes the drawing on their own sheet: they use the event pictures sheet and a hand strip, draw what the children do in the Both box and copy one word label, 5 minutes.

Last, 3 minutes: the printing and the adjective pairs finish.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which comparison picture fits?" ([[s04-X130a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three have the card in view and are asked stems b and c. **Ask.** "What is one thing the same?" ([[s04-X130b]]) **Ask.** "What is one thing different?" ([[s04-X130c]]) The last three have page 11 of Ben and the Bird and page 5 of Nia Calls for Help open. **Ask.** "On one of these pages the helper talks on the phone. Point to that page." ([[s04-X130d]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card or the page once), not yet seen. A child who names how things look, not what the children do (error A), goes to day 131's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cap and cape: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /g/ /oa/ /t/. Say the first, middle and last sound of rain. Segment coat.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 21, lesson 106, explicit teaching: oa, ai and ea are new. Each spelling says a long vowel sound. Add them to the sound-spelling map with goat, rain and tea. Review: er, ir and ur say one sound (cycle 20). Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: coat, goat, road, toad, load; then rain, tail, mail, wait, pail. Say each, tap the sounds, write it.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the forms the day 129 scoring marked, each twice, 5 minutes; the printing group traces the model first. Dictate "The goat is on the road." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map oa, ai and ea, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Hammer, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. Cycle 21's reader waits until oa, ai and ea are mapped; it opens on day 131. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. File the day 129 results. Name day 131's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the cards aloud and draw the pictures on paper. One child: reread both pages, sort the four cards into Both and But, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each pair aloud and act it out.

**Skip.** The sort and the sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read page 11 of chapter 4 of Ben and the Bird and page 5 of Nia Calls for Help. **Say.** "How would you repair a drawing that shows only how things look?" Expect: draw what the children do.
- **10 min. Fix.** Your child draws what a child and a grown-up do in one of the stories, with one word label.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "coat" and "goat."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X130a]] [[s04-X130b]] [[s04-X130c]] [[s04-X130d]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture that fits, says one thing the same and one thing different, then points to the page where the helper talks on the phone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the repair and the exit. Longer: a child who sorts quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: repair, both, but, helper, goat, rain, tea. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can name what two children do and who can find a page. A child who named how things look (error A) gets a fresh card in day 131's opening repair (s04-X130a). Day 131 turns to bird-protection information and compares two information pages.
