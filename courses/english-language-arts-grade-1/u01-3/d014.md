# Day 14: Why does Gran sit on the stool?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures. Lay out a strip, the event cards, counters, a turn card and the teller and speech cards for each pair, the three sentence cards, the fresh teller panel, the picture cards for the exits, the reader Pat's Mess, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 13's exit marks give today's repair group (error F). Today applies what days 10 to 13 taught; nothing is new.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read pages 8 to 12

Whole class on the rug. Pairs lay out the three event cards from days 12 and tell the first try with a counter on each (2 minutes). Say: "Now we hear the later try." Act out "stool" and "watch" with a hand (1 minute).

Read pages 8 to 12 with each picture shown (about 3 minutes), pausing at page 8 and at page 10. Each pause is one ask, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds:

- After page 8. **Ask.** "What does Gran say she will do?" Expect: watch.
- After page 10. **Ask.** "What does Kofi do when the big screwdriver does not fit?" Expect: he finds a little screwdriver with a thin tip.

Then pairs tell the later events with three counters and add them to the back of their story-map strip: the big screwdriver does not fit; the little screwdriver fits and the bell rings; the basket strap is tight (5 minutes). Sit with three pairs. The pairs hold the strip and the turn card from today on.

#### 10 min. Partners find the evidence first

Read page 8 aloud once, with its picture, to everyone (1 minute). Then 4 minutes for the repair for day 13's exit (error F). Children who called Grandpa the teller because he talks sit with you, with the teller strip and the speech card. Read the fresh panel, made up for practice: "I am Rosa. Uncle Bo sets up a new shelf. 'Pass me the screws,' Uncle Bo says. I pass them." Picture: Rosa handing a tin of screws to Uncle Bo beside a shelf on the floor. **Ask.** "Who tells this story, and which word shows it?" Expect: Rosa; I. Place the teller strip on "I am Rosa" and on "I pass them", and the speech card on Uncle Bo's words. If many children made this error, run it with the whole class.

The other pairs work now, 5 minutes for the repair group and 9 minutes for the others, on one job: find the words and the picture on page 8 that tell why Gran sits on the stool. Partners point and talk; do not tell them the answer. The repair group joins its partners for the last 5 minutes. Walk and listen. Say only: "Show me where." A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, 5 minutes, turn card, two turns each of about 30 seconds. **Ask.** "Which words and which picture explain Gran's action?" Frame: "I know because the words say ... and the picture shows ..." Hear today's six at their pairs, about 40 seconds each. Last 5 minutes: you answer. The words say "This time you do the work"; the picture shows Gran sitting with her hands folded. Gran sits so Kofi can do the work. Pairs tell a neighbor the frame once more.

#### 20 min. Draw and describe

Each child picks an action from pages 8 to 12 by Kofi or by Gran, draws it, prints one word label and says the frame: "I know because the words say ... and the picture shows ..." Draw for 8 minutes and print the label for 3. Pairs say the frame to each other (3 minutes). Then hear today's six, 40 seconds each (4 minutes), and write each one's spoken sentence under the drawing while the others add color. Keep the last 2 minutes for a neighbor to point to the page that holds the word detail.

Look-fors for the six: (1) names the character; (2) gives a word detail from the page; (3) gives a picture detail. The drawing stays in the child's folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "I cannot reach the pump. Grandpa lowers it. I fill the tire." Show the picture: Ali, Grandpa and a pump on a low stand. Ask: "Why does Grandpa lower the pump? Use the words." Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X14]]). A good answer is that Ali cannot reach it, from the first sentence. A child who says "Grandpa is tall" gave a picture fact (error C). A child who says "Grandpa wants to fill the tire" gave a reason the card does not give (error A).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Spoken blends. Say the sounds, with a small gap, and the class blends: /b/ /r/ /a/ /g/, "brag"; /s/ /w/ /a/ /m/, "swam"; /s/ /t/ /r/ /a/ /p/, "strap". Push a counter for each sound on swam and strap. Watch for a dropped consonant (error I): one counter for each sound.

#### 10 min. Map and read

Connected practice. The class reads Pat's Mess together with expression (2 minutes). Then three sentence cards: "A tan cat has a hat." "That is a pan." "This is the tan cat." Read each once to the class with a smooth phrase. Pairs read each card with a finger under the words, once each way, and tap the part to remember on a, has, is and the (6 minutes). Point out the capital letter at the start and the period at the end. Sit with three pairs. Do not time anyone.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 13 (the letter L or l, or "I tell"): that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach M and m. Say: "M is a tall stick, a slant down to the line, a slant back up, and a tall stick down. The little m is a short stick with two humps." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "a man at a map". Man is like pan with a new first sound. Children print a word at a time.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table each child reads the cat's refrain from Pat's Mess, "The cat can tap the ...", on pages 5 to 7 with expression, then reads one new sentence card (about 40 seconds each). The other places: sound counters (brag, strap, swam), word building with tiles (man, pan, tan), draw and label (finish the day's drawing), tool pictures, quiet pairs retelling the story with the strip. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, the day 13 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the student pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the picture for the exit and the cards by hand. No counters: use buttons. One child: reread page 8, find the words and the picture that explain Gran's action, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No event cards or strip: tell the events on your fingers. No turn card: pass a pencil.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Read and find.** Read pages 8 to 12 of Kofi and the Bicycle aloud.
  **Say.** "Why does Gran sit on the stool?" Expect: the words say "This time you do the work"; the picture shows her hands folded.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child picks what Kofi or Gran does, draws it on the day 14 page and prints one word. Then your child says: "I know because the words say ... and the picture shows ..."
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints M m and "a man at a map".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X14]] Read "I cannot reach the pump. Grandpa lowers it. I fill the tire." Show a picture of Ali, Grandpa and a pump on a low stand. Ask: "Why does Grandpa lower the pump? Use the words."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the later-events retell and the sentence cards to one; keep the evidence search, the drawing and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes describes a second character's action or a second picture detail.

### Words and supports

Words: stool, watch, explain, words say, picture shows, evidence. Frames: "I know because the words say ... and the picture shows ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 rereads a supported story passage and the hammer poem for meaning and expression. If many children gave a picture detail only on today's exit (error C) or a reason the card does not give (error A), its reread part opens with four minutes: add a word detail to a picture detail, and point to the sentence before answering.
