# Day 11: Describe Kofi and Gran

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures. Lay out the character cards with their word fact and picture fact cards, a strip and a turn card for each pair, counters, tiles, the three word cards, the reader Pat's Mess, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 10's exit marks give today's repair group (error D).

### The day

#### 15 min. Read pages 1 to 7

Whole class on the rug. Review: on day 2 you learned the eye and the ear. The picture shows; the words tell. Today we describe a character, which means saying what the character does or looks like, using both. Content note: a boy, his grandparent and some tools in a shed; nothing frightening.

Reread pages 1 to 7 (about 3 minutes) with each picture shown. Pause after pages 4, 5 and 7. Each pause is one ask, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds:

- After page 4. **Ask.** "What do the words say Kofi tries first?" Expect: his hands; he twists and pushes the seat.
- After page 5. **Ask.** "The picture shows Kofi's tongue sticking out. What do the words say he does?" Expect: he turns and turns the screwdriver on the bolt.
- After page 7. **Ask.** "What do the words say Kofi learned?" Expect: look at the part first, then pick the tool.

Leave 3 minutes to act out "twist", "push" and "turn" and to say the tool names again.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

First 4 minutes: the repair for day 10's exit (error D). Children who pointed inside the shed sit with you; the others start the pairs' task below. If many children did so on day 10's exit, run it with the whole class instead and shorten the pairs' task. Reread the end of page 1 and the middle of page 2. **Ask.** "Point to the sentence that names the place." Expect: "Behind her house is a shed full of tools," or "Rain taps on the shed roof, so Gran and I will work inside." Then reread page 12. **Ask.** "Which words tell that Kofi goes out?" Expect: "The rain stops. I buckle my helmet." Show its picture: the wet garden. The place can change, so check the page you are on.

Then pairs, 6 minutes. Review: on day 2 you asked a question the words can answer and pointed to the sentence. Model one: "What does Gran lift off her old toolbox?" Expect: the cover. Each child asks a partner one question about Gran or Kofi, and the partner points to the page and answers; then swap. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Describe two characters

Make two columns on the board: "The words say" and "The picture shows". Hold up the Kofi card and place it in box 1 of a strip. Model: the words say "I twist the seat and I push the seat"; the picture shows a green raincoat. Then the Gran card: the words say "Gran picks a wrench"; the picture shows gray curls and a blue apron.

The class adds one more of each for each character. Kofi: the words say "Hands are not enough"; the picture shows a scrunched face. Gran: the words say "A screwdriver turns a screw"; the picture shows her hands on her hips. You hold the cards and place them. The class says the frame once for each character: "The words say ... and the picture shows ..." Pairs say it for Kofi and for Gran to each other (2 minutes). A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Character cards

Hand out the day 11 student page. Say: "Draw Kofi or Gran." Introduce the page (2 minutes). Draw for 8 minutes. Pairs say the frame to each other once each way (2 minutes). Then hear today's six, 40 seconds each (4 minutes) while the others add color and print one word label (3 minutes). Look-fors for the six: names the character; says one thing the words give; says one thing the picture shows. The page stays in the child's folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 10 aloud and show its picture. Ask: "What does Kofi do on this page? Use the words." Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X11v2]]). A good answer is that he finds a little screwdriver and turns the screw tight. A child who says "He smiles" gave a picture detail with no word detail (error C). A child who says "Gran turns the screw" gave an action from another character (error A).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Count sounds, not letters. Blend a four-sound word: say /f/ /l/ /a/ /t/ with a small gap and the class says "flat". Blend a five-sound word: /k/ /l/ /a/ /m/ /p/, "clamp". Then isolate in flat: the first sound, /f/; the middle vowel sound, /a/; the last sound, /t/. Segment clamp with five counters, one for each sound. Last, "that": push three counters, because th is one sound. Watch for a consonant in the middle (error J): stretch f-l-a-t on four fingers and the third finger says the vowel. Watch for a dropped consonant (error I) and for fl counted as one sound (error K): one counter for each sound.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Chain with tiles or paper squares, one change at a time: pat, pan, can, cat, hat. For each, pairs say the sounds, build the word and read it (6 minutes). Then the, is, a. Review: look at the card, cover it, say the letters, uncover to check (mapped words, day 7). Partners check each other (4 minutes); sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 10 (the letter I or i, or "this is a hat"): that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach J and j. Say: "J is a tall stick that curls to the left at the bottom, like a hook. The little j is a short stick that goes below the line, hooks to the left, and has a dot above." Children trace in the air, then print. Dictation: say "Pat has a hat" and children print a word at a time. Check the mapped words has and a against the cards.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table the group reads Pat's Mess page by page, one child a page (about 20 seconds each), then finds has and a on page 1 and says the part to remember. The other places: sound counters (segment pat, that and clamp), word building with tiles (pat, pan, can), draw and label Kofi or Gran, tool pictures, quiet pairs where each asks one question about a page. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard, the day 10 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the student pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the character cards by hand. No counters: use buttons. One child: reread pages 1 to 4, describe Kofi, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters or tiles: use buttons or paper squares.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **5 min. Reread.** Read pages 1 to 4 of Kofi and the Bicycle.
  **Say.** "What do the words say Kofi tries first?" Expect: his hands; he twists and pushes the seat.
- **10 min. Words and picture.** Look at page 1 together.
  **Say.** "Tell me one thing the picture shows about Gran, and one thing the words tell." Expect: gray curls or a blue apron; she waves, or she has a shed full of tools.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws Kofi or Gran on the day 11 page and prints one word.
- **5 min. Sounds and letters.** Say flat slowly; your child holds up a finger for each sound (four). Print J j and "Pat has a hat".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X11v2]] Read page 10 of the story and show its picture. Ask: "What does Kofi do on this page? Use the words."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the pairs' questions and the chain to three words; keep the description cards, the drawing and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes describes the other character on the back.

### Words and supports

Words: describe, character, words say, picture shows, raincoat, apron, wrench. Frames: "The words say ... and the picture shows ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 puts the first events in order. If many children said a picture detail only on today's exit (error C) or an action from another character (error A), its Reread and question part opens with four minutes: add a word detail to a picture detail, and point to the sentence before answering.
