# Day 13 teller strip and speech cards

**Teller strip:** a long strip with the word I and a small mouth. **Speech card:** a card with quotation marks. You read four lines from the bicycle story and children hold up the strip for the telling and the speech card for the quoted words. Lines: "I am Kofi." (telling) "Let's see what your bicycle needs," says Gran. (speech) "Hands are not enough," I say. (speech) I look in the toolbox. (telling).
