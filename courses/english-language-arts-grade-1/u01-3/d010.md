# Day 10: A story about fixing a bicycle

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its twelve pictures. Lay out the tool picture cards, the large story-map strip, a strip for each pair, the map picture cards, the turn cards, Tools in the Kitchen, the watering can card, the five word cards, Pat's Mess, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 9's marks give today's repair group (error AI). The error AA group was held to day 9's Talk part.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read the story whole

Whole class on the rug. First show the picture cards one at a time and say each name together: screwdriver, wrench, toolbox, seat, bell (3 minutes). Act out "loose" and "wobble" with a hand. Content note: a boy and his grandparent mend a bicycle in a shed on a rainy day, with a small cat; nothing frightening, and real tools stay with you. Say: "Curio Class made up this story. Kofi tells it."

Read all twelve pages (433 words) with each picture shown (about 4 minutes). Pause after pages 3, 6 and 10. Each pause is one ask, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds, fifteen pairs at once:

- After page 3. **Ask.** "Tell your neighbor one thing wrong with Kofi's bicycle." Expect: the seat is loose, the bell goes thunk, the strap hangs down.
- After page 6. **Ask.** "Which tool does Gran pick for the bolt?" Expect: a wrench.
- After page 10. **Ask.** "What does Kofi use to fix the bell?" Expect: the little screwdriver.

Finish pages 11 and 12. Children tell a neighbor their favorite page (1 minute). A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Retrieval and repair

Six minutes with the whole class on the rug, then four for the repair.

Review: you learned the story-map in kindergarten, a strip with a box for who, a box for where and when, and a box for what happens. Hold up the large strip. The people and animals in a story are its characters. The place and time of a story is its setting, which you learned in kindergarten. Pairs have a strip and the map cards.

**Ask.** "Who is in the opening of the story, where are they, and what happens first?" Each child puts one character card and the place card in a box, and orders the two event cards: Kofi brings the bicycle into the shed, then Kofi twists the seat with his hands. Walk the pairs. If a pair misses an entry, model that one entry and let the child complete another. Expect: Kofi and Gran; Gran's shed on a rainy Saturday; Kofi brings the bicycle.

Then the repair, 4 minutes, for the children day 9's exit marked error AI (a fact from the picture given as a fact from the words). They sit with you; everyone else tells a neighbor the three entries from the strip. Reread the ladle paragraph of Tools in the Kitchen and show its picture. **Ask.** "Do the words say the pot is steaming?" Expect: no, only the picture shows the steam. **Ask.** "What do the words say about the long handle?" Expect: it keeps the cook's hand away from the hot pot. Now the fresh watering can card, made up for practice. Words: "A watering can has a long spout. It sends water to the plants." Picture: a red can on a step beside a pot of flowers. **Ask.** "Point to one fact only the picture shows, then say one fact only the words tell." Expect: the red can or the step; it sends water to the plants.

#### 10 min. Discussion-protocol, made explicit

Review: you used the turn card in kindergarten, two turns each, and a child may pass. Today the card gets three starters. Write them on the board and say each twice with the class: "I agree because ...", "I would add ...", "Which part?"

Model in 2 minutes. Hold the turn card in one hand and say a line, then pass it to your other hand and answer. First hand: "Kofi wants a bicycle that works." Second hand: "I agree because the seat wobbles. I would add: he wants to fix it himself." First hand: "Which part?" Second hand: "Page 11. 'I do it all by myself.'"

Pairs, 6 minutes. **Ask.** "What does Kofi want?" Each child takes two turns of about 30 seconds, so a pair needs 2 minutes; a child may pass. Hear today's six at their pairs, about 45 seconds each. Last 2 minutes: two pairs tell the class. Expect: to fix his bicycle; to do the work himself.

#### 20 min. The story-map

Each child has the day 10 student page. Build the large strip with the class, one box at a time, 6 minutes a box: you draw and say it, the children draw and print one word label (Kofi, shed, bicycle).

Box 1, who: Kofi and Gran are the characters. Box 2, where and when, the setting: Gran's shed, on a rainy Saturday. Box 3, what happens first: Kofi brings his bicycle to the shed. In the last 2 minutes each child points at the three boxes and tells a neighbor. Watch for a box that holds something the story does not give, and ask: "Which page says so?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 12 with its picture hidden. Then show two story pictures: page 12, the wet garden, and page 2, inside the shed. Say: "Point to the picture that shows where Kofi and Gran are at the end." Every child points. Then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X10v2]]). A good answer is the garden picture: the rain stops and Kofi buckles his helmet. A child who points inside the shed gives the place from the start, not the end (error D).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Read Pat's Mess aloud once with its pictures (about 30 seconds). The class retells it in order: Pat has a hat, a pan and a map; the cat taps them; Pat and the cat nap. As they name each thing, stretch it: "h-a-t" and the class blends it. Then rhymes. Review: rhyming words end alike (day 5). **Ask.** "Which word rhymes with hat, cat or pan?" Expect: cat. Pairs give a word that rhymes with map: tap or nap.

#### 10 min. Mapped words

Review: mapped words have a part to remember (you learned them in kindergarten and used them on day 7). Hold up each card. Say the word, tap a finger for each sound and point to the part to remember. the: th is one sound, and the e is the part to remember. a: one letter that says /uh/ when it stands alone. is: the s says /z/. has: the s says /z/. this: th, i, s say their usual sounds, but we have not read short i words yet, so learn it as a whole word.

Partners: one holds up a card, the other reads it, then swap (3 minutes). Then look, cover, say the letters and check (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 9 (the letter H or h, or "that hat"): that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach I and i. Say: "Capital I is a tall stick with a short bar on the top and one on the bottom. The little i is a short stick with a dot above it." Children trace in the air and on a palm, then print I i on the page. A capital I alone is also the word a teller uses for himself.

Dictation: say "this is a hat" and children print it a word at a time, saying the sounds. Check the mapped words against the cards.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table the group rereads Pat's Mess for meaning: read it together (1 minute), then each child reads a page alone (about 20 seconds each). Ask: "Who is Pat?" (a child) "What does the cat tap?" (the pan, the hat, the map) "Who naps at the end?" (Pat and the cat). The other places: sound cards, word building with tiles (at, cat, hat, that), draw and label a character from today's story, tool pictures (sort the screwdriver, wrench and toolbox from the seat and bell), quiet pairs telling the story-map. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard, the day 9 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the student pages in the children's folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the story pictures and cards by hand. No sound-spelling map: write the letters on the board. One child: hear the story, fill the three boxes, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No story-map strip: fold a sheet in three. No pictures for the exit: draw a porch and a kitchen.

**Skip.** The rotation, the class talk and the repair.

- **10 min. Read the story.** Read Kofi and the Bicycle aloud, showing the pictures.
  **Say.** "What does Kofi want?" Expect: to fix his bicycle, and to do the work himself.
- **10 min. Three boxes.** Your child draws who, where and what happens first on the day 10 page.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints I i, then "this is a hat".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X10v2]] Read page 12 of the story with its picture hidden. Show the pictures of pages 12 and 2. Ask: "Point to the picture that shows where Kofi and Gran are at the end."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read the story and cut the second turn of the talk; keep the story-map, the repair and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second detail about Gran or Kofi from a picture.

### Words and supports

Words: character, setting, story-map, Kofi, Gran, shed, screwdriver, wrench, toolbox, wobble. Frames: "Kofi wants ..." "I agree because ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 11 describes Kofi and Gran from the words and the pictures. If many children pointed inside the shed on today's exit (error D), its Reread and question part opens with four minutes: find the words and the picture that show where Kofi and Gran are.
