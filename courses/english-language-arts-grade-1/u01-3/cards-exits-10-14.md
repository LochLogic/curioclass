# Exit cards, days 10 to 14

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 10 card (s01-X10, RL.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "I am Ali. We sit on the porch. My wagon wheel squeaks. Grandpa brings an oil can." Show two pictures: a porch, and a kitchen. Say: "Point to the picture that shows where this story happens."

**Right answer:** The porch picture.

## Day 11 card (s01-X11, RL.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "I am Ali. I hold the lid. Grandpa pours the oil." Show the picture: Ali holding a lid; Grandpa in a blue hat pouring oil. Ask: "What does Grandpa do? Use the words."

**Right answer:** He pours the oil (a word detail from the card).

## Day 12 card (s01-X12, RL.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "We stop at the gate because my wagon falls over. Grandpa finds a rope and ties the load. We leave with the wagon straight." Show three scrambled pictures: the wagon fallen over, Grandpa tying a rope, the wagon straight. Say: "Tell me, in order, what happens at the gate."

**Right answer:** The wagon falls over; Grandpa ties the load with a rope; the wagon is straight.

## Day 13 card (s01-X13, RL.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. Read two panels aloud. First panel: "I am Ali. Grandpa opens the box." Second panel: "'Here is the oil,' Grandpa says. I take it." Ask: "Who is telling this story?"

**Right answer:** Ali tells it (Grandpa speaks inside the marks).

## Day 14 card (s01-X14, RL.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "I cannot reach the pump. Grandpa lowers it. I fill the tire." Show the picture: Ali, Grandpa and a pump on a low stand. Ask: "Why does Grandpa lower the pump? Use the words."

**Right answer:** Because Ali cannot reach it (the first sentence).
