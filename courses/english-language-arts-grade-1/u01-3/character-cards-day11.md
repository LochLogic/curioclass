# Day 11 character cards

Two cards from the bicycle story. **Kofi:** a boy in a green raincoat who says I. **Gran:** a woman with short gray curls and a blue apron who speaks in quotation marks. Children place a word detail from the story (for example, Gran lifts the cover off her old toolbox) and a picture detail (for example, the blue apron) beside each card and say who it describes.
