# Section 1.3 opener: a story about fixing a bicycle

## What the section does

Days 10 to 14 read one story, whole and then again, about a child and a grandparent mending a bicycle. The class names who is in the story, where it happens and the big events, and finds out who tells it. Children use the pictures and the words together to say what a character does.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60: a topic block with a story reading, a talk and a drawing activity ending in a five-minute spoken exit, and a foundations block of sounds and letters. You hear the day's six children each day. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Characters, setting and events.** Who, where, what happens, in order.
- **The teller says I.** The one who tells the story says I. A character who speaks speaks inside quotation marks. You read the telling in one voice and the quotation in another.
- **Words and pictures work together.** A detail from the words and a detail from the picture make a fuller description.

## The errors, in plain words

- **D, E, F, C, A:** the place is taken from another story, an event is left out, the speaker is called the teller, a detail comes only from the picture, or a detail is invented. A short repair routine follows each exit; you may use a story-map strip with three boxes.
