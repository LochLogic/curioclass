# Day 12: The rain, the problem and what happens

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures. Lay out the three event cards, a strip and a turn card for each pair, counters, tiles, the character cards, the page 8 to 10 pictures, the two word cards, the reader Pat's Mess, scrap paper, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 11's exit marks give today's repair group (errors C and A).

### The day

#### 15 min. Read pages 1 to 7 for the place

Whole class on the rug. Review: the setting is where and when a story happens (kindergarten). Today we find the place and the problem. A problem is something that is wrong, that a character wants to fix. Act out "loose" and "wobble" again with a hand.

Read pages 1 to 3 slowly with the pictures (2 minutes), then pages 4 to 7 straight through (2 minutes). Pauses are one ask each, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds:

- After page 2. **Ask.** "Where do Kofi and Gran work, and what is the weather?" Expect: in Gran's shed; it is raining.
- After page 3. **Ask.** "What is wrong with the bicycle?" Expect: the seat is loose, the bell goes thunk, the strap hangs down.
- After page 7. **Ask.** "What did Gran do about the seat?" Expect: she used a wrench and the bolt is tight.

Use the rest for the page 3 picture: Kofi sits with his arms out and the seat leans to one side. Say what the words give and what the picture adds.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

First 4 minutes: the repair for day 11's exit (errors C and A). Children who gave only a picture detail, or an action from another character, sit with you with the Kofi card. A child who named another character's action points to the sentence first: "Gran picks a wrench." Then show the page 6 picture, with the tiny gray cat on the shelf. **Ask.** "The picture shows a cat. What do the words say Gran does?" Expect: she picks a wrench and turns it. Add the word fact to the picture fact: "The picture shows a cat, and the words say Gran turns the wrench." If many children made these errors, run it with the whole class.

The others work in pairs and point. **Ask.** "Where does the work happen?" Partners point to the sentence: "Rain taps on the shed roof, so Gran and I will work inside." Then **Ask.** "What problem is there?" Partners point to a sentence on page 3. Sit with three pairs (6 minutes in all).

#### 10 min. Setting and events

Say the sentence aloud: "Rain taps on the shed roof, so Gran and I will work inside." The rain is part of the setting, and it makes them work in the shed, where the tools are. The big things that happen in a story, in order, are its major events. Review: in kindergarten you told a story with first, next and last.

Pairs, 3 minutes, turn card. **Ask.** "Why do Kofi and Gran work inside?" Frame: "They work inside because ..." Expect: it is raining. Hear four pairs (2 minutes). Then model: the seat is loose, so Kofi tries his hands first. Class, 2 minutes. **Ask.** "Why does Kofi try the screwdriver after his hands?" Expect: his hands did not fix the seat; it was still loose. Teach the link: the problem makes the events happen. Leave 3 minutes for a neighbor to tell one more "because" from page 5 or 6.

#### 20 min. Story-map, events

Pairs get the three event cards, shuffled: Kofi tries with his hands (page 4); Kofi tries a screwdriver and the tip slips off (page 5); Gran fits the wrench and the bolt is tight (page 6). Pairs lay the cards in order and put a counter on each (5 minutes). Sit with three pairs and ask them to tell it: "First ..., next ..., last ..." (3 minutes). Show the three events in the third box of the large strip.

Then each child draws one event on the day 12 page, a different one from their partner, and prints one word label (8 minutes). Each child tells the event to a neighbor with its place in the order (3 minutes). The page stays in the child's folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread pages 8 to 10 aloud. Show their three pictures, mixed up. Say: "Tell me, in order, what happens with the bell." Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X12v2]]). A good answer names all three events in order: thunk, the big screwdriver too wide, the little one fits. A child who leaves out the little screwdriver, or puts it first, has missed a major event (error E).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

As day 11, with counters. Blend a four-sound word: /g/ /r/ /a/ /b/, "grab". Blend a five-sound word: /p/ /l/ /a/ /n/ /t/, "plant". Isolate in grab: first /g/, middle vowel /a/, last /b/. Segment plant with five counters. Then chat: three counters, because ch is one sound. Watch for the same errors as day 11 (errors J, I and K) and use the same repairs.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Spell from memory, with no card in view. Say each word, children push a counter for each sound, then print it on scrap paper: hat, pan, tap, that, the. Say: "the is a mapped word: print th and remember the e."

Partners swap papers and check each word against the sound-spelling map and the word cards (4 minutes). Sit with three pairs.

Check: hat, pan, tap, that, the (th, then the part to remember).

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 11 (the letter J or j, or "Pat has a hat"): that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach K and k. Say: "K is a tall stick, then a slanted line in toward the stick and a slanted line out from the middle. The little k is the same with a short arm and a short leg." Children trace in the air, then print. Dictation: say "a tan cat". Tan is like pan with a new first sound: pan, tan. Children print a word at a time.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table the group rereads Pat's Mess, one child a page (about 20 seconds each), and says what happens on that page, with a counter for each thing the cat taps. The other places: sound counters (grab, plant, that), word building with tiles (tan, pan, pat), put the three event cards in order and tell them, tool pictures, quiet pairs. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard, the day 11 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the student pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three event cards by hand. No counters: use buttons. One child: reread pages 2 to 7, order the three events, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No event cards: draw three small pictures on a folded sheet. No counters or tiles: use buttons.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **5 min. Reread.** Read pages 2 and 3 of Kofi and the Bicycle.
  **Say.** "Why do Kofi and Gran work inside?" Expect: it is raining, so they use the shed.
- **10 min. Events in order.** Draw the hands, the screwdriver and the wrench on a sheet folded in three.
  **Say.** "What happens first, next and last?" Expect: Kofi tries his hands; the screwdriver slips off; Gran uses the wrench and the bolt is tight.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say grab slowly; your child holds up a finger for each sound (four).
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints K k and "a tan cat".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X12v2]] Read pages 8 to 10 of the story. Ask: "Tell me, in order, what happens with the bell."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear only four pairs say the "because"; keep the event order, the drawing and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes draws a fourth event, the basket, from page 11.

### Words and supports

Words: problem, setting, major events, first, next, last, because, loose, wobble. Frames: "They work inside because ..." "First ..., next ..., last ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 finds who tells the story. If many children left out the little screwdriver or put the events out of order on today's exit (error E), its Reread and question part opens with four minutes: lay three event cards and two, ask which is missing, put it back, and tell all three with three counters.
