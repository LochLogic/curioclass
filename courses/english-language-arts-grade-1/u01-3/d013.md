# Day 13: Who tells the story?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures. Lay out the teller and speech cards, the three-panel strips, a story-map strip and the event cards for each pair, counters, tiles, the picture cards for the exits, the six word cards, the reader Pat's Mess, scrap paper, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 12's exit marks give today's repair group (error E).

### The day

#### 15 min. Read pages 1 to 7 with two voices

Whole class on the rug. Show the two cards (3 minutes). The teller strip has the word I and a small mouth. The speech card has quotation marks. Say: "Kofi tells this whole story, and the teller says I. When a person talks in the story, the words they say sit inside quotation marks." The teller is also called the narrator. A speaker is a character whose words are in the marks. Partner A holds the teller strip, partner B the speech card.

Practice with four lines (2 minutes): "I am Kofi." (teller strip) "Let's see what your bicycle needs," says Gran. (speech card) "Hands are not enough," I say. (speech card) "I look in the toolbox." (teller strip). Read each line and the pair holds up the right card.

Read pages 1 to 7 in two voices (5 minutes): a plain voice for the telling, a different voice for the words inside the marks. The pairs hold up the card that fits. Then pause for two asks, 20 seconds each, to a neighbor:

- After page 4. **Ask.** "How does the story show that Gran is talking?" Expect: her words are inside the marks; the words say "she says".
- After page 7. **Ask.** "When Kofi says 'Now I know', who is telling the story?" Expect: Kofi.

Use the last 3 minutes for any lines the pairs got wrong. Note that "says Gran" and "I say" are part of the telling, not inside the marks.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

First 4 minutes: the repair for day 12's exit (error E). Children who left out the rope or put the events out of order sit with you and the three event cards. Lay two cards, the hands and the wrench. **Ask.** "Which event is missing?" Expect: the screwdriver slips off. Put it back and tell all three with three counters: first, next, last. If many children made this error, run it with the whole class.

The others work in pairs and point, 6 minutes. **Ask.** "Who tells the story on page 1, and which words show it?" Expect: Kofi; "I am Kofi." Then **Ask.** "On page 6, who speaks inside the marks?" Expect: Gran. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Talk

Model for 2 minutes with page 4, using the turn card. Say: "Gran speaks, and Kofi speaks, but only Kofi tells." Pairs, 5 minutes, two turns each of about 30 seconds; a child may pass. **Ask.** "Who tells, and who speaks?" Expect: Kofi tells; Gran speaks; on page 4 Kofi also speaks, "Hands are not enough," I say. Hear today's six at their pairs, about 40 seconds each. The last 3 minutes: two pairs tell the class. Say: "A person who speaks is not always the one who tells."

#### 20 min. Mark the teller

Each pair has the three-panel strip, copied from the story with its pictures. Panel 1 (page 1): I am Kofi. Panel 2 (page 4): Gran lifts the cover off her old toolbox. / "We will fix the seat first," she says. / First I try with my hands. / "Hands are not enough," I say. Panel 3 (page 7): I sit on the seat. / "Now I know," I say. / "Good thinking," says Gran.

Model panel 1 with the class (4 minutes): place the teller strip on the telling. Pairs do panels 2 and 3 (8 minutes): partner A places the teller strip on the telling lines, partner B places the speech card on the quoted words. A neighbor pair checks (3 minutes). Go through the answers together (3 minutes): in panel 2 the teller strip goes on lines 1 and 3, in panel 3 on line 1; the speech card goes on the quoted words. Last 2 minutes: pairs place the teller strip on the Kofi card in box 1 of their story-map strip.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the first panel: "I am Ali. Grandpa opens the box." Read the second panel: "'Here is the oil,' Grandpa says. I take it." Ask: "Who is telling this story?" Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X13]]). A good answer is Ali, the one who says I. A child who says Grandpa, because he talks, has called the speaker the teller (error F).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Segment and blend with counters. Blend a four-sound word: /k/ /l/ /a/ /p/, "clap". Blend a five-sound word: /g/ /r/ /a/ /s/ /p/, "grasp". Segment grasp with five counters. Then bath: three counters, because th is one sound. Watch for errors J, I and K and use the day 11 repairs.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Review the mapped words: the, a, is, has, this and I (day 10). Pairs turn the six cards face down, flip one, read it and tap the part to remember (4 minutes). Dictate the, has and I: children print each on scrap paper, remembering the tricky part (3 minutes). Then find every I on page 1 in the large print and color it (3 minutes). Those are the places Kofi says I.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 12 (the letter K or k, or "a tan cat"): that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach L and l. Say: "L is a tall stick with a foot that goes to the right. The little l is just a tall stick, with no bars and no dot." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "I tell", stretching t-e-l. Children print the capital I and the sounds they hear; tel is right for today. Then show tell: two l's end this word, and we will meet that spelling soon.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table the group reads Pat's Mess with expression, one child a page (about 20 seconds each). Then play telling or speaking: read the four lines from the first part of the day on cards, and each child holds up the teller strip or the speech card. The other places: sound counters (clap, grasp, bath), word building with tiles (tan, tap, that), draw and label the teller, tool pictures, quiet pairs taking turns as the telling voice and the speaking voice. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard, the day 12 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the student pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two cards and the panel strip by hand. No cards at all: raise one hand for the telling and two hands for the quoted words. One child: read page 4 in two voices, mark the three lines, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: raise one hand for the telling and two hands for the quoted words.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Two voices.** Read page 4 of Kofi and the Bicycle aloud: a plain voice for the telling, a different voice for the words inside quotation marks. Your child raises a hand at the quoted words.
  **Say.** "Who tells the story?" Expect: Kofi, who says I.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws the teller on the day 13 page and prints one word.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say clap slowly; your child holds up a finger for each sound (four).
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints L l and "I tell".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X13]] Read "I am Ali. Grandpa opens the box." Then read "'Here is the oil,' Grandpa says. I take it." Ask: "Who is telling this story?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark only panel 2 and keep the model, the talk and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes finds one more quoted line on page 11 and says who speaks.

### Words and supports

Words: teller, narrator, speaker, quotation marks, says, tell. Frames: "Kofi tells the story." "Gran says ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 explains why Gran sits on the stool, with the page and the picture. If many children called Grandpa the teller on today's exit (error F), its Partners find the evidence first part opens with four minutes: track the I in a fresh panel.
