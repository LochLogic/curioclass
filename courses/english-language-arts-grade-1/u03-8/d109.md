# Day 109: Check a drawing's labels against its sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 108's exit marks and name today's heard six, the six catch-up children and the six children for the drawing. Print the Duck at the Pond page 2 for each child. Make the repair set below. Set out the model card, the two pair cards, the word cards, the sound-spelling map and the record sheet. The exit diagram stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 108's exit (error A, and s03-X108av2): a child who named one detail as what the cards share. Use the one component most children missed. If error A, say: "These cards are made up for practice." Lay three cards: story, "Noor saw a heron stand still in the water."; poem, "Still as a post, it waits and waits."; information, "A heron stands very still. It waits for a fish to swim close." Read each once. Show two pictures: the heron standing still, the fish alone. All children at once, point. **Ask.** "Which picture shows what all three cards are about?" Expect: the heron standing still. Then one child says a fact the information card gives. If error K was commoner, use the puppet: it says "Wings help it fly", a child replies with "You said wings help it fly. I think ..." and a pair tries once. Review: a topic is what the whole card is about; a detail is one fact under it (kindergarten).

#### 10 min. Words, then the card

Show a picture card for each word and say it once, children repeating: label (review: a word written beside a part of a drawing, from kindergarten and day 83), part, scientific drawing, accurate. A scientific drawing names only parts that are really there. Accurate means right in every part. Then show the model card: a hawk on a post, with two labels, claws and beak, each on a line to a part. Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read its sentence once: "A hawk has a hooked beak. The hawk has sharp claws." Do not say yet whether the labels are right.

#### 15 min. Analyze the card

Model, five minutes, with the model card. Say the three steps while pointing: read the label; follow its line to the part; find the words about that part in the sentence. Label claws: its line goes to the beak; the sentence says a hooked beak. "The picture shows a beak, so the label must be right" is error C; the label is checked against the sentence, not the picture. The words do not match. Label beak: its line goes to the claws; the sentence says sharp claws. The two labels are swapped. Swap them and check again: each now matches its words.

Pairs, all at once, with pair card 1 for three minutes and pair card 2 for four minutes, using the three steps. **Ask.** "Is any label on card 1 wrong?" Expect: none; the sentence says toes and branch. Then card 2: **Ask.** "Which label can the sentence not prove?" Expect: toes; the sentence names only the beak. A pair crosses it out and leaves the neck without a label, because a drawing may have parts the page does not name. Walk round and hear six pairs, 40 seconds each, inside the seven minutes. For any pair that trusts the picture, point to the words first. Last, three minutes: **Ask.** "Why did we cross it out, not swap it?" Expect: the sentence has no word for the neck.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn card, pairs, all at once, three minutes, then six pairs heard, fifty seconds each, five minutes (two minutes to set up): "What does the diagram show beyond the sentence?" Expect: where the part is, how big it is, what shape it is. Review: listen, restate ("You said ___"), then reply. A pair may pass; the partner then says what they noticed.

#### 15 min. Draw and label

Read the first two sentences of the Beak part of Duck at the Pond aloud: "A duck has a flat beak. It dips its beak in the water and the mud." Each child draws a duck's beak on the student page, eight minutes, and prints one word label, beak, and a source line, "Duck, 2". A scribe prints for a child who asks. Pairs check the label against the sentence, three minutes: "The label says ___. The page says ___." Hear six children, 50 seconds each, about five minutes, while the rest finish: "Show me the words that say it." Keep the drawing in the riddle folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Show the Duck at the Pond diagram with a paper label, wing, laid over the label on the foot, and read aloud the Feet part of page 2, not yet worked: "A duck has webbed feet. Webbed feet push the water. A duck swims well." All children mark the wrong label on the page. **Ask.** "Which label is wrong?" ([[s03-X109av2]]) Then the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each: **Ask.** "Say the label that should be there." ([[s03-X109bv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread once), not yet seen. A child who points to the bird picture, not a label, has trusted the picture (error C). A child who offers a label the sentence does not give has made error B. Name day 109's misses for day 110's repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say ride and rides, bake and baked; what do you hear added? Blend /h/ /o/ /p/ /t/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of rides. Segment baked.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 18, lesson 91: explicit teaching first. Review: -s and -ing on short-vowel bases (day 44), -ed said /t/ and /d/ (days 49 and 56) and plural -s on silent-e bases (day 107). Show bake. Add s: bakes; the e stays because s is a consonant. Add ing: baking; the e drops, because ing begins with a vowel. Add ed: baked; the e drops again, and the ending sounds /t/. Do the same with ride: rides, riding, and with use: used (the ending sounds /d/). Never double a letter here. Map each word, count the vowel sounds (baked has one, baking has two; error H counts letters) and decode: bakes, baking, baked, rides, riding, used. Spell baked and rides, about 40 seconds a word. The reader Cones and Tubes is reread today; the cycle 18 reader comes on day 110.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print M m, N n, O o and P p, four pairs, dated; the page goes in the separate folder kept for 118 (every letter form was taught by day 27; feedback written after class). Dictate: "Pete baked cakes." Say it whole, then a word at a time; Pete is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Pete and cakes the last e is silent, and baked ends with the sound /t/. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names: map, blend, read, spell and reread; today bake, bakes, baked, tube, tune. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, 50 seconds each, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. Each reads one page of Cones and Tubes and one word card from today (bakes, baking, baked, rides, riding or used), then spells one taught word. The rest reread Cones and Tubes in pairs for expression, chain bake, bakes, baked, baking with letter cards and sort the cards bakes, baking, baked and rides, riding by their ending, in pairs at stations. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six. Write each miss in the cycle record.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the drawing marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Put each drawing in its folder. List by name the children who trusted the picture (error C) or gave a label the sentence does not state (error B), for day 110.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the model card on paper and read each sentence aloud; children draw the duck's beak on any paper. One child: check the model card with the three steps, draw the beak and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a bird's foot, write two labels and read a bird page sentence.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **10 min. Check a label.** Draw a bird part with a label. Read one sentence about it. **Say.** "Does the page say that word? Show me." Expect: a finger on the sentence, not the picture.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a duck's beak and prints the label beak.
- **5 min. Words.** Say bake, bakes, baking and baked. Your child says which ones lose the e.
- **5 min. Print.** M m, N n, O o, P p and "Pete baked cakes."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X109av2]] [[s03-X109bv2]] Read the duck's Feet part and show the diagram with the page's wrong label. Your child points to the wrong label, then says the label that should be there.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the pair cards and the exit; skip the discussion. Longer: a child draws a second part and checks its label with a partner.

### Words and supports

Words: label, part, scientific drawing, accurate, flat beak. Frames: "The label says ___." "The page says ___." "You said ___. I think ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can find a label the sentence does not support and say the label that belongs. A child who trusted the picture (error C) or gave a label the sentence does not state (error B) gets a five-minute repair on a fresh drawing card on day 110. Day 110 chooses an expert bird and finds facts on two pages.
