# Expert bird page sets, day 110

Four birds, two pages each. Every fact is from Beaks Find Food and Feet That Help (g1-u3-01). Each page has a heading, one label, a source label (P1 and P2 for the sparrow, D1 and D2 for the duck, H1 and H2 for the heron, K1 and K2 for the hawk), a printed contents line at the top and one glossary line at the foot. The first page of each bird is numbered 2 and the second 4, so a child must use the contents line to tell them apart. The contents line lists both entries, for example "Strong Beak, 2. Four Toes, 4." The glossary lines restate the glossaries of Beaks Find Food, Feet That Help and Heron by the Water (g1-u3-01); no new fact is added.

**Sparrow, P1, Strong Beak.** A sparrow has a thick, strong beak. The beak cracks hard seeds. **P2, Four Toes.** A sparrow has four toes on each foot. The toes grip a branch.

**Duck, D1, Flat Beak.** A duck has a flat beak. The beak strains food from mud. **D2, Webbed Feet.** A duck has webbed feet. The feet push the water.

**Heron, H1, Long Beak.** A heron has a long beak. The heron catches fish with its beak. **H2, Long Toes.** A heron has long toes. The toes spread its weight.

**Hawk, K1, Hooked Beak.** A hawk has a hooked beak. The beak tears meat into small bits. **K2, Sharp Claws.** A hawk has sharp claws. The claws grasp food.

**Contents lines and glossary lines.** Sparrow: Strong Beak, 2. Four Toes, 4. Glossary: P1 crack, to break open; P2 perch, to rest on a branch. Duck: Flat Beak, 2. Webbed Feet, 4. Glossary: D1 strain, to let water drain out and keep the food in; D2 webbed, joined with skin. Heron: Long Beak, 2. Long Toes, 4. Glossary: H1 beak, the hard part of a bird's mouth; H2 wade, to walk in shallow water. Hawk: Hooked Beak, 2. Sharp Claws, 4. Glossary: K1 hooked, curved like a hook; K2 grasp, to take hold and grip.
