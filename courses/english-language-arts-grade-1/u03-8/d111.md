# Day 111: Follow the route from the contents to the fact

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 110's exit marks and name today's heard six, the six catch-up children and the pairs for the route. Print a route card for each child and enlarge the sparrow page P1. Put each child's two pages and riddle folder on the desk. Make the repair sources below. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Repair for day 110's exit (error A, and s03-X110a; or a child who said the other source for what a part does). Use the commoner. Say: "These sources are made up for practice." Read: "Source M: A duck has webbed feet. Source N: The feet push the water. The duck swims." Show two pictures: the duck swimming, the water alone. All children at once, point. **Ask.** "Which picture fits both sources?" Expect: the duck swimming. Then, if the slip was the sources: **Ask.** "Which source tells what the feet do? Say M or N." Expect: N. Point to the exact sentence before answering.

#### 10 min. Words

Show a picture card for foot and wing and say each once. Say match: to be the same. Say route: the way we follow, one step at a time, to a fact. Show the three route words on cards, contents, heading and glossary, and say each (Review: days 81 to 83 and 105; the contents leads to the page, the heading leads to the fact, the glossary says what a word means). Children say each word and point to where it sits on the sparrow page.

#### 15 min. Model the route

Show the sparrow page P1, enlarged: the contents line "Strong Beak, 2. Four Toes, 4.", the heading Strong Beak, the sentences and the glossary line at the foot. Read the last clue pair of Wing Clues page 1 aloud (Review: day 108): "Four toes grip a branch up high. A thick beak cracks seeds nearby." Say: "I want to check the clue about the beak: how does a sparrow eat seeds?" Trace aloud with a finger. First, the contents: the entry Strong Beak is on page 2. Second, the heading: Strong Beak, and under it the sentence "The beak cracks hard seeds." That sentence is the fact. Third, the glossary: crack means to break open. Say: "The glossary tells what a word means. It does not prove the fact. The sentence on the page proves it." Then show the cover, a sparrow picture and a title only: "The cover does not say how a sparrow eats seeds." That is error D. Number the route, one, two, three, on an enlarged route card: contents, heading, glossary. Do not ask the exit's question; model the steps aloud.

#### 15 min. Try the route

Pairs at the table of their expert bird, all at once. Each pair picks one fact on one of its two pages and traces it by the three steps on one route card per child: number the boxes 1, 2, 3 in the order used, print the page number on the line and say the fact. Three minutes to set up and see the model again, eight minutes to trace, four minutes to compare with the next pair. Hear six pairs, one minute each, inside the eight: **Ask.** "Show me your route, one, two, three." Expect: a finger on the contents line, then the heading, then the glossary line. A pair that reaches for the cover is shown the contents line first (error D). A pair that says the glossary proves the fact is shown the sentence.

#### 10 min. Talk

Turn card, pairs, all at once, three minutes, then six pairs heard, fifty seconds each, five minutes (two minutes to set up): "Why is this route stronger than guessing from a cover?" Expect: it shows the page and the words, and a cover shows only a picture and a title. Review: listen, restate ("You said ___"), then reply. A pair may pass; the partner then shows the three steps with a finger.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet. Show the two pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture matches the fact on this page?" ([[s03-X111a]]) Then, with the card in view, the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each: **Ask.** "Point to the page you would turn to for this fact." ([[s03-X111b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the albatross on a rock has named an action the page does not state (error B). A child who points to the cover has looked at the cover and used no feature (error D). Name day 111's misses for day 112's repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say bake and bakes, ride and rides; what do you hear added? Blend /s/ /l/ /i/ /d/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of joked. Segment named.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 18, lesson 93: -ed, -ing and -s on taught silent-e bases. Review: the e stays before -s and drops before -ing and -ed (day 109). Lay three columns, -s, -ing and -ed. Sort the word cards by their ending, all at once on desks, three minutes: bakes and rides under -s; baking, riding, making and taking under -ing; baked, hoped, hiked, named, smiled, joked and used under -ed. Say each -ed word as you hear it; the three sounds of -ed are sorted on day 112. Map and decode two from each column. Spell baking and hoped, with the e dropped, about 40 seconds a word. Reread A Day at the Lake for expression.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print U u, V v, W w and X x, four pairs, dated; the page goes in the separate folder kept for 118 (every letter form was taught by day 27; feedback written after class). Dictate: "Mike baked a cake." Say it whole, then a word at a time; Mike is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Mike and cake the last e is silent, and in baked the e of bake is dropped. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names: map, blend, read, spell and reread; today bake, baking, baked, make, making. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, 50 seconds each, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. Each child reads one page of A Day at the Lake, then spells one word of the day (baking or hoped). The rest reread A Day at the Lake in pairs for expression, chain bake, baked, baking with letter cards and sort the cards by ending, in pairs at stations. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six. Write each miss in the cycle record.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the route marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Put each route card with the child's two pages in the riddle folder; the choice and the route card go forward to day 112. List by name the children who looked at the cover (error D), for day 112.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the route card as three boxes on paper and read each page aloud. One child: trace one fact on the sparrow page, number the three boxes and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: use a short bird page with a contents line, a heading and a glossary line.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **10 min. Route.** Read a short bird page. **Say.** "Show me the contents line, then the heading, then the glossary line." Expect: three fingers in that order. Your child numbers the three steps 1, 2, 3.
- **5 min. Words.** Say foot, wing, match and route. Your child points to each on the page.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say bake, bakes, baked, baking. Your child says which ones lose the e.
- **5 min. Print.** U u, V v, W w, X x and "Mike baked a cake."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X111a]] [[s03-X111b]] Read the short page the card shows. Your child points to the matching picture, then shows which page to turn to.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, the exit and the route on one page; skip the talk. Longer: a child traces a second fact on the other page.

### Words and supports

Words: foot, wing, match, route, contents, heading, glossary. Frames: "The contents says ___." "The heading says ___." "You said ___. I think ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can match a fact on a page to a picture and use the contents line to find its page. A child who looked at the cover (error D), or named an action the page does not state (error B), gets a five-minute repair on a fresh page on day 112. Day 112 gathers expert-bird facts with labelled drawings and source references; each child's expert-bird choice and route card go forward.
