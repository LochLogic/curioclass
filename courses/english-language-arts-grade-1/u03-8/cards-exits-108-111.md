# Exit cards, days 108 to 111

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 108 card (s03-X108a, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Story card: Lena saw a cormorant sit on a rock. Poem card: Wings held wide, in the sun to dry. Information card: A cormorant holds its wings out after it swims. This helps dry its feathers." Show two pictures: the cormorant holding its wings out, the water alone. Say: "Which picture shows what all three cards are about?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The cormorant holding its wings out.

## Day 108 card (s03-X108b, RI.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Tell me one thing the information card says." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** It holds its wings out, or it helps dry its feathers.

## Day 109 card (s03-X109a, RI.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Drawing card: a cardinal's head with a crest, and the crest labeled foot. Sentence: A cardinal has a crest on its head." Show two pictures: the cardinal picture, the labels on the card. Say: "Which label on the card is wrong?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The word foot.

## Day 109 card (s03-X109b, RI.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Say the label that should be there." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Crest.

## Day 110 card (s03-X110a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Source U: A woodcock has eyes set far back on its head. Source V: The eyes help it watch for danger." Show two pictures: the woodcock, a plant on the ground. Say: "Which expert-bird picture fits both sources?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The woodcock.

## Day 110 card (s03-X110b, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Which source tells what the eyes help it do? Say U or V." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** V.

## Day 111 card (s03-X111a, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Contents: Wings, 3. Heading: Long Wings. Glossary: glide, move through air without flapping. An albatross has long wings. It can glide over the sea." Show two pictures: the albatross gliding over the sea, the albatross on a rock. Say: "Which picture matches the fact on this page?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The albatross gliding over the sea.

## Day 111 card (s03-X111b, RI.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view. Say: "Point to the page you would turn to for this fact." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Page 3, from the contents line Wings, 3.
