# Day 108 riddle card

A card with the frame "I have ___. I ___. What am I?" Two clue lines, the bird's name written on the back, and the drawing under a flap. The teacher's sample riddle, invented for practice: "I have a flat beak. I have webbed feet. What am I?" (a duck). The children keep their card in the folder from day 108.
