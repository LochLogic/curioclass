# Poem pages, days 108, 109, 110, 111, 118 and 119 (g1-u3-03)

Read aloud, whole, twice. The text is the written poems in courses/english-language-arts-grade-1/texts/g1-u3-03.md; print each page with its picture note.

**Feather Coat, page 1.** My coat has no buttons. / My coat has no seams. / Feathers grow all over me, / soft as morning dreams.

**Feather Coat, page 2.** When the cold wind blows, / I fluff up my feathers / and keep the warm air close, / whatever the weather.

**Wing Clues, page 1.** Two small wings, quick and round, / push the air with a whirring sound. / Four toes grip a branch up high. / A thick beak cracks seeds nearby.

**Wing Clues, page 2.** Chirp, chirp, chirp! Guess my name. / Match each clue, then play the game.

Teacher key (not read before children guess): a sparrow. Credit: ours.
