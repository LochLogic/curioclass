# Day 111 route card

A one-page card with three boxes in a row: contents, heading, glossary. Under each box a number space and a line for the page. The model route on the sparrow page P1 (expert-bird-pages-day110): the contents line Strong Beak, 2, then the heading Strong Beak, then the glossary line crack (to break open). The glossary gives a word's meaning; it does not prove a fact. A child traces the route for a fact on the chosen bird and numbers the three boxes.
