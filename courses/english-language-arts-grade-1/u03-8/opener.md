# Section 3.8 opener: the riddle launch, the drawing card and the contents route

## What the section does

Days 108 to 111 launch the riddle and drawing task. Children hear two poems, see how a story, a poem and an article can share one clue, check a drawing's labels against a sentence, choose an expert bird and find two facts, and learn the contents, heading and glossary route.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Each ends its topic block with a five-minute exit read aloud to you; you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A drawing names real parts.** A label is a word beside the part, checked against the page.
- **A poem imagines; an article explains.** A line break puts a clue at a line end.
- **The glossary explains a word.** It does not prove a fact.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail named as the topic. Sort again.
- **C:** a picture trusted over the words. Check the label against the sentence.
- **B:** a fact no source gives. Point to the sentence.
- **D:** the cover used to find a fact. Follow the contents and heading.
- **J:** an ending left off in reading. Read the base, add the ending.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
