# Day 8: Mid-unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out Card A and paper item sheet (the card face down with its picture), the sound-spelling map, the sort cards: tool words and picture symbols, the place signs, the chime and the record sheet. Today teaches nothing new. Give no hint and no model. Tomorrow's repair groups come from today's marks.

### The day

#### 10 min. Setup and read-aloud

Say: "Today shows what you can do. I will not help. This card is made up for practice." Read Card A once, aloud: "A sieve has little holes. A cook shakes flour through the sieve. The sieve keeps the lumps back." Then show the picture. Hand out the sheets, names and date on top.

#### 30 min. Hearings

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each. At the teacher table each child takes about a minute with Card A and its picture: ask one question the words answer; "What stays in the sieve?"; ask your partner a different question; "Who shakes the flour through the sieve?"; say how the sieve helps, using one picture detail and one word detail. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The rest of the room works at the seat on the paper items: put a word symbol or a picture symbol on each fact card, and draw and label the sieve.

#### 15 min. Make-up

Children who missed a hearing or a sheet, and any child not yet heard, take it now, the same way.

#### 5 min. Close

Collect every sheet, with name and date. Say: "I will look at your pages tonight."

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend and segment pat, pan, tan; the class holds up a finger for each sound. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

Chain on word cards: pat, pan, tan, tap. The class says each word. No reteaching.

#### 10 min. Letters

Teach G and g. Say: "G is a curl with a short bar. The little g is a circle with a tail." Children trace and print. Not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each. Each group rereads Pat's Mess; at the teacher table each child reads a line. The chime moves everyone along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Sort the sheets by error letter. Record error AA from day 7 on the sheet. Name tomorrow's repair groups.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the sheet by hand. One child: do the five items one at a time; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: draw two boxes and a large box on plain paper.

**Skip.** The rotation, the make-up and the class talk.

- **10 min. Assessment: Card A.** Read Card A once and show the picture. No help.
  **Say.** "What stays in the sieve?" Expect: the lumps.
- **10 min. Ask.** Your child asks two different questions about the sieve.
- **10 min. Who.** Ask: "Who shakes the flour through the sieve?" Expect: the cook.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the sieve, labels it and says how it helps, with one thing from the picture and one from the words.
- **5 min. Symbols.** Your child puts a word symbol (ear) or a picture symbol (eye) on each fact.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear A1 and A2 only. Longer: no extension; the check is the day.

### Words and supports

Words: sieve, flour, lumps, cook. Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 returns day 8's items. The repairs match the errors the marks show.
