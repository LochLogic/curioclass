# Day 8 Card A

Read aloud once to the class: "A sieve has little holes. A cook shakes flour through the sieve. The sieve keeps the lumps back."

**Picture:** a cook shakes a round sieve above a bowl; a spoon rests beside the bowl. Show it after the reading. This is the card for items A1 to A6. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. The card is not modeled before the day.
