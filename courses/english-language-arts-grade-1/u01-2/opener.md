# Section 1.2 opener: words and pictures, talking and writing about a tool

## What the section does

Days 5 to 9 connect a poem to the articles, teach how to take a turn in a talk, and show how a short explanation names a tool and gives a fact. Day 8 is a check day: you read a card aloud once and hear small groups. Nothing is taught on the check day. Day 9 returns the check with a repair and a revision.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 8 is two parts: hearings in small groups, and a make-up time. A child outside the group you hear is "not yet heard". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A puppet can show a mistake safely.** Pip says a line that stays on the topic and one that leaves it. No child is the example.
- **An explanation has a topic, facts and an end.** Name the tool, give a fact the words support, close with its job.
- **Words and pictures give different facts.** Sort each fact to its source before answering.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, C, AI:** a detail is invented, or a fact is sorted to the wrong source, or only the picture is used. Lay the sentence beside the picture.
- **AE, AD:** a turn leaves the topic, or a partner is spoken over. Pip shows the turn, then the children add to it.
- **AA:** a sentence has no fact. Ask what the tool does, then say two facts on fingers.
