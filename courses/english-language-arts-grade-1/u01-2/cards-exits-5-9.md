# Exit cards, days 5 to 7

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 5 card (s01-X5, RF.1.4b)

Say: "Wipe, wipe, wipe, little sponge. Wipe the table clean." (invented for practice) and ask: "Say it back the way a sponge would wipe."

**Right answer:** Three phrases, Wipe, wipe, wipe / little sponge / wipe the table clean, said with a steady repeated beat.

## Day 6 card (s01-X6, SL.1.1b)

This card is invented for practice. Pip the puppet reads one line from a card: "A mop has soft strings. It soaks up spills." Pip adds: "It helps with cleaning." Show two pictures: a mop on a wet floor, and a broom. Ask: "Add one thing about the mop to what Pip said."

**Right answer:** Any fact from the card, such as Its soft strings soak up spills.

## Day 7 card (s01-X7, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A clip holds our drawings. Its jaws grip the paper. We can find our drawings together." Show two pictures: clipped drawings, and a loose pile. Ask: "Tell me one fact about the clip that I could write after the topic."

**Right answer:** Its jaws grip the paper, or It keeps the drawings together.

The day 9 exit uses the ladle paragraph of Tools in the Kitchen (texts/g1-u1-02.md); it needs no card.
