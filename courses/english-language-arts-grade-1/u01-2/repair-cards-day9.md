# Day 9 repair cards

Three fresh cards, read aloud, each with a picture that holds one fact the words do not give. Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot.

**Glue stick card.** Words: "A glue stick has a cap. The cap keeps the glue from drying." Picture: a glue stick with a purple stick and a green cap.

**Stapler card.** Words: "A stapler holds a row of staples. Pressing it joins two sheets of paper." Picture: a gray stapler on a desk beside two sheets.

**Stool card.** Words: "A stool has three legs. It lets a child reach a high shelf." Picture: a small round stool on a rug beside a shelf.

Use: cover the picture, reread the sentence, ask which fact is still there; then uncover and name one fact only the picture shows.
