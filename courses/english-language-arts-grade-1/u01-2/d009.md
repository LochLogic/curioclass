# Day 9: Back to the article, and fix it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 8's sheets into repair groups by error letter. Lay out Card A and paper item sheet, the repair cards (glue stick cap, stapler, stool), the sort cards: tool words and picture symbols, the topic card and detail cards, the picture cards for the exits, Tools in the Kitchen, the move strips, the word cards, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the article

Whole class on the rug. Reread the funnel paragraph in Tools in the Kitchen with the picture shown (about 20 seconds). Content note: a cook, a bottle of oil; nothing frightening. Ask the class: "What keeps the oil off the table?" Pairs find the sentence that answers it and point to it in the large print (5 minutes); sit with three pairs. Then you point: "The oil goes into the bottle, not onto the table." Say: "The words told us." Each pair tells a neighbor one more fact the words give about the funnel (3 minutes). A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Revise an answer

Return day 8's paper sheets. Say: "These are day 8's items. They are yours to fix." Review: Read, Point, Talk, Draw is the routine from kindergarten: read the card, point to the words, talk to a partner, draw what you found. Show the four pictures of the move strip. Say them together. Children look at their own sheet, with a partner, and say one thing to fix (4 minutes).

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Four groups, 4 minutes each, then 4 minutes for changes on the sheet. Groups follow day 8's marks; the rest work in pairs from the move strip.

- Error A (A2): Card A, "What stays in the sieve?" Contrast lumps with chops: point to the words. Then the stapler card, cover the picture and ask what the words say.
- Error B (A5): lumps in the words against the pictured spoon. Then sort one fresh fact from the glue stick card, word symbol or picture symbol.
- Error C (A6): the bowl alone against the bowl and the lumps. Then describe the stool card with one picture detail and one word detail.
- Error AI: the pictured spoon against the lumps words. Then find the source of a pictured fact on the stapler card (the gray stapler).

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs give one word fact and one picture fact for an article tool, with a turn card (4 minutes). Meanwhile a small group for error AA (day 7 and day 8): show the clip card, cover the word "nice", say two facts on your fingers (6 minutes).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "A hose has a long tube. It carries water to the garden." Show the picture: a green hose across flower beds. Ask: "Tell me one thing the words say about the hose that you cannot see in the picture." The class tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X9]]). A good answer is that it carries water to the garden. A child who says "It is green" has named something the picture shows (error AI).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend and segment tap, trap. Watch for a dropped consonant: a child who says "tap" for trap gets the /r/ back. The class holds up a finger for each sound.

#### 10 min. Chain and spell

Chain from memory, one change at a time: cat, hat, that. For each, the class says the sounds and spells it with tiles; they point to th as one sound. Pairs check each other (4 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap (the letter G or g, or a dropped sound). Otherwise teach H and h. Say: "H is two sticks and a bridge. The little h is a tall stick and an arch." Children trace and print. Then dictation: say "that hat" and children print it, a word at a time.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each. At the teacher table the group retells Pat's Mess: who, then what happens (about 40 seconds each). Then map the mapped words the, a, is, has on the reader. The other places: sound cards, word building, draw and label, tool pictures, quiet pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet. Collect the pages.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards and the move strip by hand. One child: reread the funnel paragraph, fix one item with the move strip and do the exit; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No move strip: say the four moves aloud.

**Skip.** The rotation and the class talk.

- **5 min. Reread.** Read the funnel paragraph.
  **Say.** "What keeps the oil off the table?" Expect: the funnel sends it into the bottle.
- **10 min. Fix an item.** Read the sheet from day 8 together. Your child fixes one answer: read, point, talk, draw.
- **10 min. Words and picture.** Look at the stapler card. Your child says one fact the words give and one the picture shows.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say cat, hat, that slowly. Print H h.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X9]] Read "A hose has a long tube. It carries water to the garden." Ask: "Tell me one thing the words say that you cannot see in the picture."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the groups with a mark get the full repair; the others work from the strip. Longer: a child who finishes finds a second fact the picture shows and the words do not.

### Words and supports

Words: sieve, lumps, funnel, repair, picture, words. Frames: "The words say ..." "The picture shows ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 opens with four minutes for children who named something the picture shows, not the words, on today's exit (error AI): find the source of one more fact.
