# Day 6 Pip lines

Pip is a sock puppet. Pip says each line from the kitchen article, then you ask the class: does this stay on the topic, or leave it? Lines: "A ladle has a deep bowl." (stays) "My cat has a ball." (leaves) "A funnel pours oil into a bottle." (stays) "I like the color blue." (leaves). Children add one fact that stays on the topic. No child is the example of a mistake: Pip makes every mistake.
