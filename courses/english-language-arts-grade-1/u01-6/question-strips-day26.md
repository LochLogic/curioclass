# Day 26 question strips

Three strips for each pair, cut apart. Print the word large on each, with a small picture of a pointing hand. A child chooses one strip and points it at one part of the partner's model.

| Strip | Word |
|---|---|
| 1 | How? |
| 2 | Why? |
| 3 | What? |
