# Day 26: Answer a partner's question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the question strips, one set a pair, the letter sample sheets, the exit card with its picture, and the kitchen lines. Lay out the day 25 models and change drawing sheets, the day 24 model record sheets, the precut paper and pre-torn tape, and the hearing note slips. Choose cohort 3 for the retry. Real tools: teacher only.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Read day 25's marks. If many children gave a judgment with no fact (error AA, "A new sheet is nice"), a picture fact (error C) or a part the card did not give (error A) on day 25's exit, take four minutes. Write two words on the board: nice and fact. Review: on day 24 you said a fact is something you can see or test. Say: "A change follows a fact." Then a new card, made up for practice: "Our paper crown falls down over the eyes. Its band is loose." **Ask.** "What change would help the crown stay up?" Expect: a tighter band. Hold up one finger for the fact (the band is loose) and one for the change (a tighter band). For error C, cover the picture and reread. For error A, reread and point to the sentence that holds the fact. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge. Use any minute left to name today's plan: answer a partner's question.

#### 10 min. Reread

Whole class on the rug. Read the Classroom Sign Model aloud, a finger under the words (2 minutes). Review: it has a topic, two facts and a closing, the paragraph-frame from day 7. Point to each sentence and the class says what it is. Each child then rereads their own sentence from the day 24 model record sheet, "The ___ helps because ___", to a partner, and their day 25 sentence, "I changed my ___ because ___" (5 minutes). A child may point instead. Hear three children read a sentence to the class (3 minutes).

#### 15 min. Partner questions

Pairs with the question strips: How, Why and What. Show the move with the lantern from day 25 (3 minutes). A child points the strip How? at the holes and asks, "How does this part help?" You answer, "The round holes let the light shine out." The Why? strip at the string: "Why is this part here?" "The string lets the lantern hang." The What? strip at the tape: "What is this part?" "It is tape. It joins the sides." Say: "A question points at one part. The maker answers with a fact." Pairs take turns (8 minutes): one child is the maker with the day 25 model, the partner points one strip at a part and asks, the maker answers aloud; each maker answers three questions, then they swap. Review: ask "Which part?" if a question is not clear. Sit with three pairs, 1 minute each, and hear six children (3 minutes). Look-fors: the question points at one part; the answer is a fact about that part, not a feeling. A child may pass; the partner then answers for the model with a point.

#### 25 min. Revise

Each maker revises the model or the drawing after the partner's question: add a part, change a part or redraw a box on the change drawing sheet, with precut paper and pre-torn tape (15 minutes). Say: "Use the question. What did your partner ask about? Make that part better and say why." Walk and ask, "What did your partner ask?" and "Which fact says the new part helps?" Each child then tells a partner, "I revised my ___ because ___" (4 minutes). Hear today's six during the making, 1 minute each, and write the sentence each says. Walk the class past three revised models (3 minutes). On the model record sheet each child adds a label for the new part (3 minutes). Look-fors: a revision that answers the question; a fact after because. A child who revises with no fact (error AA, "I made it better") hears: "Better is a feeling. What can you see or test?"

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "Our paper bin holds our books. Its sides are tall, so a hand cannot reach in." Show the picture: a paper bin with tall sides, books inside and a hand above it. Say: "A partner points at the bin and asks, Which part stops my hand?" Ask: "Answer your partner: change that part so a hand can take a book out. What do you change?" Every child draws or points to the change, then hear today's six say it, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X26]]). A good answer lowers the sides or opens the front or top. "Make it nicer" gives a judgment with no fact (error AA). "Color it red" is a picture change with no fact behind it (error C). "Make it bigger" is a change with no fact behind it (error A). These marks go to day 27.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend: /m/ /u/ /g/, "mug"; /y/ /u/ /m/, "yum". Segment with counters: yum, three counters. Isolate the first sound of yum, /y/. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map

No new pattern. Pairs reread Pat's Lunch aloud, two pages each, a finger under the words (5 minutes). Then build buns, tubs and nuts on tiles, say each, and add the s (3 minutes). The class reads the cards buns, tubs, nuts, mugs, rugs and hugs (2 minutes). Walk and sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Teach Y and y. Say: "Y is a V sitting on a tall stick. The little y is a small v with a long tail below the line." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "Yum, a bun." Children print a word at a time, with the map: the capital Y, yum, the comma, the mapped word a, bun and the period. Then letter samples (4 minutes): each child prints the pairs A a to F f on side one of the letter sample sheet, and you write the date beside the group. Forms still missing are recorded as incomplete, never wrong. Watch b and d.

#### 30 min. Rotation

**Retry, cohort 3 (15 minutes).** Cohort 3 is the ten children heard yesterday. Each rereads the line their written note named, with a finger under the words, 90 seconds each. The other twenty reread Pat's Lunch with a partner. Update the note: what changed and what comes next. Read the note to the child.

**Matched group reading (15 minutes).** Six groups of five, matched by how they read Pat's Lunch. Sit with the two groups who read least smoothly, 5 minutes each: tap the sounds in a word, blend it, read a page of Pat's Lunch, spell one word on tiles, then reread the page. With each, read the kitchen lines from Tools in the Kitchen with them: "A cook makes meals. A cook uses tools to get food ready." and ask, "What does a cook use?" The other groups reread in pairs. Use 5 minutes for the third group.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the models in the labelled box and file the dated letter samples. Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, the retry notes. Name tomorrow's six.

### Other rooms

No strips: cut three paper strips and print How, Why and What. No tape: fold the paper and join with a glue stick. One child: answer three questions from a grown-up about the model, revise one part, do the exit and the letters; skip the retry and the group reading.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: ask the three questions aloud. No tape: use a glue stick.

**Skip.** The retry and the group reading.

- **10 min. Questions.** Your child shows their model. Point at one part and ask, "How does this part help?" then "Why is this part here?" then "What is this part?"
  **Say.** "How does this part help?" Expect: a fact about what the part does.
- **10 min. A change.** Your child changes one part because of a question and says "I revised my ___ because ___."
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints Y y and "Yum, a bun."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X26]] Read "Our paper bin holds our books. Its sides are tall, so a hand cannot reach in." Ask: "A partner asks which part stops a hand. What do you change so a hand can take a book out?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the questions, one revision and the exit; cut the matched reading to the two groups you sit with. Longer: a child who finishes answers a third question and tells which fact it came from.

### Words and supports

Words: question, part, revise, fact, because, How, Why, What. Frames: "I revised my ___ because ___." "How does this part help?" "Why is this part here?" "What is this part?" Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. A scribe may write a word label. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 finishes the model, rehearses two facts with because in trios and says a closing. If many children gave a judgment with no fact (error AA, "Make it nicer"), a picture change (error C, "Color it red") or a change with no fact behind it (error A, "Make it bigger") on today's exit, its first part opens with four minutes: contrast "nicer" with a fact and a change that follows it, and retry on a new bin.
