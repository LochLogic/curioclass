# Card F and the item cards for the day 27 observation

Card F is a picture-and-sentence card for items s01-A16 and s01-A28 to s01-A30. The item cards are printed one at a time for s01-A21 to s01-A24. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line.

## Card F

Print the five sentences in a column, large, one to a line: "The pup sits. It has a cup. The cup tips. The pup is sad. It sits in the sun."

**Pictures (two alternatives under the text):** (1) a pup sitting beside a cup lying on its side with a puddle; (2) a pup sitting beside a cup standing upright.

Print each on its own card, large, black on white.
