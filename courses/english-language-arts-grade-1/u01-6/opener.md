# Section 1.6 opener: a design change, a partner's question, and the first observation

## What the section does

Days 25 to 27 ask children to change a paper tool on purpose, because a fact says the change will help. On day 25 each child draws one change and says why. On day 26 a partner points a question strip at one part and the maker answers and revises. On day 27 children finish the model, rehearse in trios and say a closing sentence. The first of five observation rotations runs on day 27.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 27 holds the first rotation inside the foundations block: six different children, 5 minutes each, while the other 24 read known texts and spell. Item times are on the observation sheet, about 275 seconds in all. Nothing new is taught on day 27; you record, and the repair waits for day 33. Hearing cohorts 3 and the retries of cohorts 2 and 3 run on days 25 and 26 as before.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A change on purpose has a reason.** "Because a fact says it will help" is the whole idea.
- **A partner's question points at a part.** How, Why and What each ask about one part; the maker answers from what the model has.
- **A closing says the job.** One sentence, no new fact.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a fact that is not on the card.
- **C:** a fact only the picture shows.
- **AA:** a judgment with no fact.

The other letters of the unit show up only in the day 27 observation. Record the item and the letter and teach nothing new. The repair for each is on day 33.

## Marking

Shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child may pass. The observation sheet and the record sheet stay with you.
