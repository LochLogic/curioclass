# Exit cards, days 106 and 107

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 106 card (s03-X106a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card, with a cover picture of a merganser on its front and the sentences inside, and read it aloud: "A merganser has a long, thin beak with edges like tiny teeth. The edges help it grip fish. Source label H1." Show two pictures: the beak holding a fish, the merganser's wings. Say: "Which picture matches the fact on the card?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The beak holding a fish.

## Day 106 card (s03-X106b, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view with its cover showing. Say: "Show me where a listener could check that fact." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Points to the label H1 and the sentence.

## Day 107 exit pictures (s03-X107av2, s03-X107bv2)

The exit is read from Waiting in Heron by the Water, page 2 (g1-u3-01); it has no card. Draw two pictures: the heron standing still in the water, and a fish alone.

## Note for day 107

The swallow card is the fresh card for the retry only: it is read once before the retry and is never the exit. It is not an item. Its text: "Contents: Tail, 2. Heading: Forked Tail. Glossary: forked, split into two points at the end. A swallow has a long, forked tail. It catches insects in the air." Its contents, heading and glossary pages stay with the teacher until day 107 and are printed only on this card; no day teaches them.
