# Day 106 share card

One card per child: a line for the chosen fact, a small drawing box, and a "my source line" box with a pointer arrow. The child holds it up to a listener, reads or says the fact and points to the source line. The listener may ask to see the source line. The card is for listening, not a performance mark.
