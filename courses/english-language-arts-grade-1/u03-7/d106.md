# Day 106: Share a fact and show its source

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's heard six and the six catch-up children. Draw the page Webbed Feet on a large sheet. Put each child's revised paragraph and organizer on the desk. Seat six groups of five. Sort the share cards by group. Hand back day 105's assessment, the finished paragraph, scored out of class, with its written note. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This page is made up for practice. Its facts come from Feet That Help." Day 103's exit showed some children pointing only at the cover (error D). Show the page: contents, Feet, page 2; heading, Webbed Feet; glossary, "webbed: joined with skin"; sentence, "A duck has webbed feet. Skin joins the duck's toes." Point to the cover and ask: **Ask.** "Does the cover say what webbed means?" Expect: no. Then: **Ask.** "Where could a listener check the word webbed?" Expect: a finger on the glossary line; the heading only helps you find the page. Say the rule: "Point to the page before you explain." Review: heading, contents, glossary and label as ways to find a fact (days 81 to 83). Partners take turns, three minutes, all at once: one points to the heading and the glossary line, the other says "I can see it."

#### 8 min. Words, then a story page

Review: Mina at the Window, read on day 88. Hold up the eight picture cards: tree, window, notice, remain, wonder, respond, perch, observer. Review: tree, window, notice, wonder and observer from kindergarten and day 88, remain from day 36 and perch from day 84. Respond is new: to answer. Reread pages 1 and 2 aloud (about 80 words), two minutes; they hold tree, window, notice and observer, so children point to those four cards as they hear them. The other four words (remain, wonder, respond, perch) go in made-up sentences with a gesture, and you say that they are not on those pages. Say each word in a made-up sentence, for example "I respond when a friend asks me something," and children point to its card, five minutes, all at once. The words are practised, not tested. Children say the word back; no one reads them.

#### 7 min. Rehearse

Each child picks one fact from the revised paragraph and its source label. At the share card, draw the fact in the box and write the source label in the "my source line" box, two minutes. Then partners take turns, two minutes each, all at once: say the fact once, point to the source line, and the partner asks, "Where can I check that?" This is new: a listener can ask where to check. Ask it once with the whole class, then partners use it.

#### 25 min. Share in six groups of five

Each group takes about 4 minutes at your table: five children at 45 seconds, 3 minutes 45 seconds in all, with a short move between groups. Six groups make 24 minutes, and one minute sets up. A child holds up the share card, says the fact and points to the source line. The listener asks, "Where can I check that?" and the child points again. Write one listening note per group: who pointed to a line, who named only the cover. The note is not scored. Any child may pass and listen. The groups waiting at their desks finish the drawing, then say a second fact to a partner (the extension). Say first: "Sharing is for listening. A listener can check."

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: the turn card. Hold it up. **Ask.** "How does that fact support the paragraph's explanation?" Partners take turns, four minutes, all at once, with the frames "My fact is ___." and "It comes from ___." Then hear six pairs, one minute each. Expect: a fact joined to the paragraph's topic, with a finger on its source. Pass is allowed.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet, cover showing. Show the two pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture matches the fact on the card?" ([[s03-X106a]]) Then, with the card in view, the heard six come to your table, 25 seconds each (2 minutes 30 in all): **Ask.** "Show me where a listener could check that fact." ([[s03-X106b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points only at the cover has not used a source line (error D). A child who picks the wings has chosen a part the card does not give (error B). Name the heard six for day 107's repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say can and cane; which has the long vowel sound? Blend /k/ /l/ /o/ /z/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of cute. Segment June.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 17, lesson 88: u_e and e_e, review of day 104, with silent e words from i_e and a_e (days 94 to 100). New today: a two-syllable word can join two syllables you know. Show pancake. Clap it: pan-cake. Chop between the parts. Map each part: pan has one vowel sound, cake has one vowel sound with a silent e. Do the same with inside (in-side) and sunrise (sun-rise). Count vowel sounds, not letters (error H): "How many vowel sounds? Two, so two parts." Decode each word, then spell pancake and sunrise on desks, about 40 seconds a word. The reader Cones and Tubes was mapped on day 104; it is reread in the rotation.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print A a, B b, C c and D d, four pairs, dated; the page goes in the separate folder kept for 118 (every letter form was taught by day 27; feedback written after class). Dictate: "Duke ate a pancake." Say it whole, then a word at a time; a is mapped, Duke is a name, so it begins with a capital, and ate and pancake were just mapped. Children tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names: map, blend, read, spell and reread. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, 50 seconds a child, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. Each reads a page of Cones and Tubes, then spells a taught word (tube). The rest reread, chain tub, tube, tune with letter cards, and sort the cards pancake, inside and sunrise by their two parts, in pairs at stations. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the heard-six column, the cycle record, the exit marks and the six listening notes to the record sheet. List by name the children who pointed only at the cover. The sort of the day 104 and 105 records into at most four gaps (topic and evidence, features, writing and participation, foundations, one gap each child) is done after class, before day 107.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the share card on folded paper. One child: share one fact, point to its source line and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No paragraph: use any short bird page with a heading and a label.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **10 min. Share.** Your child picks one fact and its source line, says it and points. **Say.** "Where can I check that?" Expect: a finger on the line, not the cover.
- **5 min. Words.** Point to a picture for tree, window, notice, wonder, respond.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Clap pancake, inside and sunrise: two parts each.
- **5 min. Print.** A a, B b, C c, D d and "Duke ate a pancake."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X106a]] [[s03-X106b]] Read aloud: "A merganser has a long, thin beak with edges like tiny teeth. The edges help it grip fish. Source label H1." Show a picture of the beak holding a fish and one of the wings. Your child points to the picture, then shows where to check it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the share, the talk and the exit; skip the words. Longer: a child shares a second fact with its source line and says how it supports the same explanation.

### Words and supports

Words: share, source, check, listener, respond, remain. Frames: "My fact is ___." "It comes from ___." "Where can I check that?" Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can point to the source line of a fact. A child who pointed only at the cover (error D) gets a five-minute repair on a fresh card on day 107. Day 107 is response time: repair by gap from the 104 and 105 records, one fresh retry on a swallow card, then the exit. Day 105's list of misses is made after class today and answered tomorrow.
