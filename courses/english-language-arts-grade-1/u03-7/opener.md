# Section 3.7 opener: sharing the paragraph, responding to the check

## What the section does

Day 106 children share one fact from their paragraph and show the source line. Day 107 uses the 104 and 105 records: you repair by gap in small groups, then each child retries only the missed part on one fresh card.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Each ends its topic block with a five-minute exit read aloud to you; you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A listener can check.** Sharing means pointing to the source line.
- **The cover is not a source.** Contents, heading, label or glossary lead to the fact.
- **A retry changes one thing.** Only the missed part is tried again, on a new card.

## The errors, in plain words

- **D:** the cover used to find a fact. Follow the contents and heading.
- **A:** a detail named as the topic. Sort again.
- **B:** a fact no source gives. Point to the sentence.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
