# Day 62: Respond to the check and fix the note

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the day 61 grid, sort the children by the one part not yet seen (the detail, the idea and feeling, or the sentence) and note the lapses most marked. Draw three feedback cards: a pointing hand for "add one detail", a face for "say a feeling", a bar with a dot for "say a whole sentence". Have the talk card and listener card ready. Pick the heard six and the two five-child groups from day 60.

### The day

#### 10 min. Opening repair

Run at most two branches. First, error E from day 60 (5 minutes). Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read: "A bell rings when school starts." Say two things: "The bell rings when school starts." and "I like bells." **Ask.** "Which says only what the line says?" Expect: the first. Reread the line, then say only what it says. Second, the lapse the grid marks most from H and G (5 minutes). For H, say "Bell rings" beside "The bell rings when school starts." and **Ask.** "Which tells who or what and what happens?" Expect: the second. For G, draw a bold border beside a drawing of the bell with ring lines; the class says what a listener needs to see.

#### 15 min. Revise by feedback

Hand back day 61's assessment work first. First, if many missed error E on the day 61 connection (4 minutes): read "To see a puddle shrink, look at it in the same spot each day." Say the made-up reason "Because puddles are fun" and reread the line. **Ask.** "Which says only what the line says?" Expect: we see what changed. Second, if many missed error O in the notebook (4 minutes): show a page with only a star border beside "Stars are still up in the day. (Stars in Daylight and Darkness, page 3)". Point to the one fact, then name its source.

Then give feedback by group, not child by child (3 minutes): show each group of children its feedback card and say it in a sentence, such as "Add one detail you can see in your drawing." Each child revises only that part (4 minutes): a drawing or note on a fresh strip stapled beneath the first; a spoken part said again to a partner while you note it on the grid. Keep the revision with the original. A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Visual retry and edit

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." First the drawing (5 minutes): "Draw two views of the school puddle, morning and noon, so a listener can see it shrank." Say what the listener needs to see, then draw only that. Then each child says one whole sentence about it to a partner: who or what and what happens (3 minutes).

Then the response card (7 minutes). Say: "The notebook draft says moon bright." Read the source line once: "We see sunlight reflected by the moon." A peer asks, "What makes it bright?" Show two pictures: an arrow from the sun to the moon, and a lamp inside the moon. Say: "Point to the picture that answers the peer." Every child points; the rotating six say the source line again ([[s02-X62a]]). A lamp inside the moon is error E. Children draw what the source line says with an arrow, and the six tell how it helps a listener ([[s02-X62e]]); a border is error G. Hear the six: "Say the draft again as a finished sentence that uses the source line" ([[s02-X62b]]); "moon bright" is error H. Last, on the page: print the name from "ana saw a star" with its capital ([[s02-X62c]]) and put the end mark on ([[s02-X62d]]). Walk round. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Teach nothing new.

#### 15 min. A later linked exchange

Run only the moves the grid shows many missed, 3 minutes each, at most four. Twenty children rehearse each move in threes with the talk card and the listener card, wrong beside right: waiting with a hand held still beside calling over a partner (error W); "I agree with Ravi because his note says stars shine at night" beside "Yes" (error X); asking "Which picture do you mean?" beside saying nothing (error Y); naming the bell drawing beside repeating "Which drawing?" (error Z).

The two five-child groups from day 60 come to your table, 6 minutes each: one more linked exchange on "Which source detail should our note include?" Write a note of each wait, link, question and answer. First teaching is not scored; write "not observed" rather than guess.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Keep each revision beside the original. Mark the grid and the unit 2 record sheet. Put aside the capital and end mark pages for day 63.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Say like and lick; **Ask.** "Which word has the sound of the letter i's name?" Expect: like. Blend /s/ /p/ /l/ /i/ /t/, "split". Say "grill": first sound /g/, middle sound /i/, last sound /l/. Segment "stripe" with counters: /s/ /t/ /r/ /i/ /p/, five sounds.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Cycle 10, lesson 54: review of the cycle's patterns, no new one. Read split, strip, stress, grill and press from the cards, then the -es words from lesson 52 and the -er and -est words from lesson 53: kisses, wishes, taller, biggest and slimmest (4 minutes). Partners read On the Pond again for meaning and expression: the teacher reads a page, the pair echoes it with the same voice, then each reads a page smoothly (6 minutes). The class says what the frog and the crab do.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (6 minutes): say "The frog is the biggest." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped words the and is are named aloud. Watch the capital T and the double g. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, first (3 minutes): children who made error L read the base, add the ending, and reread; error M, stretch the word and mark each sound; error I, one counter for each sound in split. Then the group reads On the Pond, a page a child, and spells press on sound boxes (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread On the Pond with a partner; chain split, strip and stress with letter cards; spell grill and press on sound boxes; read the word cards was, of, but and the to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the revision marks and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet with the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 63.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three feedback pictures on paper and write the response card on the board. One child: revise the one part, answer the card by pointing and speaking, print the name and the end mark, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No crayons: draw with a pencil and label.

**Skip.** The rounds and the rotation.

- **10 min. Fix.** Your child fixes one thing from the check, then tells you what changed. **Say.** "What did you change?" Expect: one thing, in a whole sentence.
- **10 min. Edit.** You write "mia has a red cap" with no capital and no end mark. Your child fixes it.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads split, kisses and biggest and prints "The frog is the biggest."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X62a]] You read: "A lamp lights the table." A child asks, "What lights it?" Draw a lamp over a table and a moon over a table. Ask: "Point to the picture that answers." Expect: the lamp. [[s02-X62b]] Ask: "Say it as a finished sentence." Expect: "The lamp lights the table." [[s02-X62c]] Your child prints "mia" with its capital. [[s02-X62d]] Your child puts the end mark on "mia has a red cap". [[s02-X62e]] Your child draws an arrow from the lamp to the table and says how it helps.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision, the response card and the rotation; cut one repair branch. Longer: a child who finishes asks a partner a question about a revised note and answers it.

### Words and supports

Words: revise, source, listener, question. Frames: "The line says ___." "I changed ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Any child may pass. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who points to the picture the source line supports (error E), who says a finished sentence (error H), who prints the capital on a name (error Q) and who ends with a mark (error R). Day 63 opens a day-and-night poem. Its foundations rotation gives the first group three minutes each on the capital for a name and on the end mark, from today's page.
