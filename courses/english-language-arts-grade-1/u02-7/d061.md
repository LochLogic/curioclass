# Day 61: Check a sky connection, a note and a talk with a drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print card M and the score grid. Write six groups of five.

### The day

#### 6 min. Setup and read-aloud

Say: "This is a check, made up for practice. You may point, draw, say or pass." Read card M once, its pictures in view.

#### 10 min. RI.1.3

Read page 6 of *Sun Across the Sky* and ask [[s02-A19v2]]; then page 6 of *The Changing Moon* and ask [[s02-A20v2]], each page with its picture and each question read twice. Children circle one of two pictures on side 1. Go round once, 8 seconds a child. Mark the grid: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A reason the page does not give is error E.

#### 10 min. W.1.8

Ask [[s02-A22]]. Children draw on side 2, box 1. Scribe a label on request. Go round, 15 seconds a child; record help.

#### 10 min. SL.1.5, the drawing

Ask [[s02-A24]]. Side 2, box 2. A border earns no mark (error G).

#### 24 min. Oral rotation

Six groups of five, four minutes each at your table. Ask [[s02-A23]], then [[s02-A25]]; 40 seconds a child. Mark detail, idea and feeling, and sentence separately. A fragment is error H. Waiting groups finish drawing.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Not scored. Slid and slide: which has i's name? Expect: slide. Blend /g/ /r/ /a/ /s/. Sounds of "drop". Segment "stripe".

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Explicit teaching first (5 minutes). Review from day 60: kiss, kisses. New: -er and -est on a known base: two things, taller; three or more, tallest. Map tall and small whole (a says /aw/). In big and slim the last letter doubles before an ending. Read tall, taller, tallest; big, bigger, biggest; slim, slimmer, slimmest. Spell big on sound boxes (2 minutes); partners read On the Pond (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictation (6 minutes): "The frog is bigger." The and is are named aloud. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table: read taller, biggest and On the Pond (3 minutes); spell slim (3 minutes). Stations: reread On the Pond; chain tall, taller, tallest; spell slimmer; read was, of, but, the.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the student pages. Mark [[s02-A21]] from the notebook and [[s02-A26]] to [[s02-A29]] from the dated discussion records; no child sits for them. Write "not observed" where a record is missing.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the grid on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No crayons: use a pencil and label the two pictures.

**Skip.** The group talk.

- **15 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A22]] You say: "To see a seed grow, draw the pot each day. This is made up." Ask: "How can drawing the pot each day show the seed grew? Draw it and label it."
- **15 min. Draw.** [[s02-A24]] Your child draws two pots, first and later, and labels them. **Say.** "Tell about your pictures and say a feeling." Expect: a detail and a feeling, in a whole sentence.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads taller and biggest and prints "The frog is bigger."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** Ask: "Why draw the pot each day?" Expect: so we can see what changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep each item; cut the stations to two. Longer: a second sentence about the drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: check, record, label. Pointing, drawing and a home language count. Read every card twice.

### Tomorrow

The grid shows which part each child has not yet seen: detail, idea and feeling, or sentence. Day 62 repairs the lapse most seen first, then groups by lapse: E and O in the revise part, G and H in the visual part, W, X, Y and Z in the linked exchange. Each child revises one part.
