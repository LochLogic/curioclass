# Day 60 talk card and listener card

**Talk card.** A strip with three spaces drawn left to right: a small picture for the fact, a small picture for the drawing, and a small speech bubble for the sentence. The maker fills the three spaces with a note, a drawing and a spoken sentence.

**Listener card.** A card with a large question mark on one side and a small ear on the other. The asker holds the question mark to ask one question; the listener holds the ear while the maker speaks.

**Roles.** Groups of three: maker, listener, asker, one minute each, three rounds. The agreed turn signal is a raised hand held still until the speaker finishes. Pip (the class puppet) asks one clearing-up question the way a partner does.
