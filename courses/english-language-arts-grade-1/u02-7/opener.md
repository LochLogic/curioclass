# Section 2.7 opener: a talk, a check and a response

## What the section does

Days 60 to 62 rehearse a short science talk in threes, check the sky-pattern connection, the notebook and a talk with a drawing on day 61, and respond to what the check showed on day 62. Day 61 is a check: no new teaching, no help beyond the read-aloud. Day 62 revises only the part not yet seen, and the revision is kept with the original.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and the work; the foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Day 61 is run on a score grid: the detail, the idea and feeling, and the sentence are marked separately.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A talk has a fact, a drawing and a sentence.** The listener asks, and the speaker answers the question that was asked.
- **A source line supports a claim.** "Moon bright" is a draft; the source line says what makes it bright.
- **Take turns.** Wait, link your reply to the last idea, ask when something is unclear, and answer the question you were asked.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E, G, H, O:** an unsupported claim, an unhelpful drawing, a fragment, a decoration in place of a fact.
- **Q, R:** a name with no capital, a sentence with no end mark.
- **W, X, Y, Z:** taking another turn, an unlinked reply, confusion left unasked, a question repeated instead of answered.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
