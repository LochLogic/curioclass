# Exit cards, days 60 to 62

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Where a line says "heard in the day's rotating six", no card is printed. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed".

## Day 60 card (s02-X60a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud the notebook line: "Stars are easier to see in a dark sky." A speaker says, "Our night log shows stars above a tent." Show two pictures: stars above a tent, and an empty bright sky. Say: "Point to the picture that supports the talk." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of stars above the tent.

## Day 60 card (s02-X60b, SL.1.6)

Heard in the day's rotating six. A partner asks, "What does your drawing help us see?" Say: "Say one finished sentence that answers your partner." 

**Right answer:** A finished sentence about the child's own drawing, such as "The drawing helps us see the stars above the tent."

## Day 62 card (s02-X62a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. The notebook draft says "moon bright." Read aloud the source line: "We see sunlight reflected by the moon." A peer asks, "What makes it bright?" Show two pictures: an arrow from the sun to the moon, and a lamp inside the moon. Say: "Point to the picture that answers the peer." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of the arrow from the sun to the moon.

## Day 62 card (s02-X62b, L.1.1j)

This card is invented for practice. Same draft, "moon bright." Say: "Say the draft again as a finished sentence that uses the source line." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** A finished sentence such as "The moon reflects sunlight" or "The moon is bright."

## Day 62 card (s02-X62c, L.1.2a)

This card is invented for practice. Show a sentence card without an end mark: "ana saw a star". Say: "Write the name again so it is written right." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** Ana, with a capital A.

## Day 62 card (s02-X62d, L.1.2b)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card "Ana saw a star" with no end mark. Say: "Put the end mark on." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** A period after star.

## Day 62 card (s02-X62e, SL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. The notebook line says "moon bright." Say: "Draw what the source line says, using an arrow, and tell how the drawing helps a listener." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** An arrow from the sun to the moon, with a short telling of how it shows what makes the moon bright.
