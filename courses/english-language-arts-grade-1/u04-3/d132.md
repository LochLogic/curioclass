# Day 132: Find each side's point and reason in a city nest debate

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read A City Nest: Two Views. Make the two strips and the frame for the opening repair below, and the claim frame with its three strips. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who gave only a likeness on day 131 (error S, s04-X131cv2). Keep the exit pictures face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These pages are made up for practice." Read: "Page C: Our class feeds the fish. Page D: Our class wipes the tables." **Ask.** "Pip says both pages are about the class. Does that tell what page C adds?" Thumbs. Expect: no, it is a likeness. Lay two strips under the headings page C adds and page D adds. **Ask.** "Put each strip under the page it belongs to: feeds the fish, wipes the tables." Pairs sort in 90 seconds and three pairs say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: feeds the fish under C, wipes the tables under D. This is the repair for error S (s04-X131cv2); watch those children.

#### 15 min. Read A City Nest: Two Views

Say the words and touch each picture: view, hawk, ledge, sidewalk. Read the text whole, with its pictures (about 235 words, 2 minutes): the first view is on pages 2 and 3, the second on pages 4 and 5, and page 6 gives the summary. Review: you learned point and reason in kindergarten. The point is the one sentence the writer wants us to remember; a reason is a sentence that tells why. Three asks, about 4 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Pip says the first view wants the nest to stay up. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: yes.
2. **Ask.** "Pip says the second view wants the nest to stay up. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no, it wants the nest taken down.
3. **Ask.** "Pip says page 6 says both sides care about the nest. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: yes. Read page 6 once more.

#### 15 min. Talk and Claim-evidence-reasoning

New: claim-evidence-reasoning, said aloud only, nothing written. Claim is what the writer wants. Evidence is words from the text. Reasoning is the because that ties them. Show the claim frame with three boxes and model on the first view: put "The nest should stay" in claim, "A tall building is like a tree" in evidence, and "The young need the nest as they grow" in reasoning; read it with the word because between the last two. Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Pip put 'The street is crowded' in the claim box of the first view. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no.
2. **Ask.** "Pairs, fill the frame for the second view: sort three strips into claim, evidence and reasoning." Strips: "The nest should come down." "Bits of the nest fall to the sidewalk." "People walk there, so it is not safe." Pairs sort in 5 minutes. Expect: the first in claim, the second in evidence, the third in reasoning. For the third ask, expect: the third strip.
3. **Ask.** "Mark the word so on the strip that has it, and say which strip tells why." Pairs mark in 4 minutes; three pairs are heard, 20 seconds each.

#### 8 min. Determiners

New: a word that comes before a noun and tells which one or how many is a determiner. Review: one and more than one (plural) from unit 1 of this year. Show the cards a, an, the, this, that, these, those. Hold one paper bird near and then far: "this bird, that bird." Hold two near and then far: "these birds, those birds." Say "an egg" and "a nest." **Ask.** "Pip says these nest. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no, this nest. **Ask.** "Match the cards: this and that to one paper bird, these and those to two." Pairs match in 4 minutes. A child who puts a one-word card with two birds (error O) counts the birds aloud first.

#### 12 min. Point and reason

Give each child the point and reason frame, with three strips: "The nest should stay." "The young need the nest as they grow." "The ledge is gray." **Ask.** "Put one strip under Point, one under Reason, and the strip that is not a reason in the set-aside corner." Then each child copies the word because between the Point and Reason strips, 12 minutes. Expect: Point, Reason, set-aside in that order. Walk and mark the heard six as you pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 5 of A City Nest: Two Views aloud and show the three pictures from the card sheet. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the author's point?" ([[s04-X132av2]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three keep page 5 open. **Ask.** "What reason does the author give?" ([[s04-X132bv2]]) The last three have pages 2 and 4 of A City Nest: Two Views open. **Ask.** "Which page is the side that wants the nest taken down?" ([[s04-X132c]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread a page once), not yet seen. A child who calls an unrelated fact a reason (error C) goes to day 133's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say not and note: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /b/ /ea/ /k/. Say the first sound of tail. Segment rain.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 21, lesson 108, explicit teaching: build, take apart and write oa, ai and ea words. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: beak, bean, mean, meat, seat; then pain, paid, maid, mail. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Review: a vowel-team syllable (day 131): clap raincoat and mailbox.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print "The nest should stay." as a caption for the point card, 5 minutes. Dictate "The bean is on the seat." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Throat Hurts, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 133's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips aloud and draw the frames on a sheet. One child: sort the three strips, say which is the point and which the reason, and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each sentence aloud and your child holds up one finger for a point and two for a reason.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 2 and 3 of A City Nest: Two Views. **Say.** "Pip says the nest should come down because the young need it. Is Pip right?" Expect: no.
- **10 min. Sort.** Say "The nest should stay" and "The young need the nest." Your child holds up one finger for the point and two for the reason.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "bean" and "seat."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X132av2]] [[s04-X132bv2]] [[s04-X132c]] Read page 5 of A City Nest: Two Views again. Your child points to the picture of what that side wants, says its reason, then points to the page that wants the nest taken down.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the frame and the exit. Longer: a child who sorts quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: view, hawk, ledge, sidewalk, point, reason, because, determiner, claim, bean. Frames: "The point is ___." "A reason is ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can find an author's point and say a reason, and who can find the side that wants a nest taken down. A child who called an unrelated fact a reason (error C, s04-X132bv2) goes to day 133's opening repair. Day 133 rereads both views and sorts reasons by the point they support.
