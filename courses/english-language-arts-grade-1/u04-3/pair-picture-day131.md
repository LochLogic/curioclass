# Pair picture

A sort mat with three boxes, Nests only, Both and Boxes only, and a two-page frame with a Same space between the two pages. It holds three sets of strips, all read aloud by you.

Six detail strips, one set for each pair: "Do not touch a nest." "Young birds need their parents to come back." "A cavity is a hole in a tree." "People can build nest boxes." "Staff cut the wood." "Volunteers write a welcome."

Three picture cards for each child to place on the frame: a hole in a tree, children standing back from a nest, a child writing welcome on a box.

Four strips for the opening repair, a hand picture and an eye picture: "Both children tell a grown-up." "Mia tells the helper and Omar tells the guard." "The glove is red." "The scarf is long."

Day 135 uses the same frame with the six protection strips: "Look from far away." "The law protects wild birds." "Steps inside help baby birds climb." "Twenty boxes were built." "A cavity is a hole in a tree." "Boxes help birds that nest in holes." Its three picture cards are boards with labels, children far from a nest looking up, and a bluebird at a box.
