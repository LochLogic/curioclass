# Day 134: Gather the reasons and talk them over

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the strips for the opening repair, the six spoken cards, and the groups of three; seat the heard six in two groups at your table. Name the catch-up group and the children who matched a reason to the wrong writer on day 133 (error C, s04-X133bv2). Put the sample word cards in your folder. Keep the exit pictures face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This is made up for practice. A writer wants the class to cover the sandbox." Lay Pip's sort: under Reason, "The sandbox is square"; under Not a reason, "Cats dig in it." Say the test: "Cover the sandbox because ___." **Ask.** "Pip's sort has two strips in the wrong place. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no. **Ask.** "Fix Pip's sort: which two strips swap?" Pairs fix in 90 seconds and three pairs say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: cats dig in it goes under Reason and the square one goes under Not a reason. This is the repair for error C (s04-X133bv2); watch those children.

#### 15 min. Locate reasons

Read pages 2 to 5 of A City Nest: Two Views again (about 140 words, 1 minute). Lay six strips with link words: "A hawk needs a nest because the young need it as they grow." "People walk there, so it is not safe." "The street is crowded because so many bird watchers came." "A tall building is like a tree." "Food is easy to find in the city." "People need room to walk." Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Mark the link word on each strip that has one: because or so." Pairs mark in 5 minutes. Expect: because on the first and third, so on the second; none on the others.
2. **Ask.** "Pip says the strip with so supports the side that leaves the nest up. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no, it supports the side that takes it down. About 3 minutes.
3. **Ask.** "Find the because on page 5 and put your finger on the words after it." Expect: so many bird watchers came to see the hawks. Pairs do this in 4 minutes.

#### 15 min. Discussion

Groups of three (maker, listener, asker; review of unit 1, day 27) sit with two strips and the two point cards. In each round, the maker puts one strip under a point card and says which point it supports. The listener says the maker's choice back: "You put ___ under ___." The asker asks one question: "Which word links it to the point?" and the maker points to because or so. Rounds are about 4 minutes, the roles turn, and three rounds fill the time, so each child is heard about 40 seconds. Respectful turns: the listener says the speaker's choice back before anyone answers. Sit with the two groups of the heard six and mark each child on the listening record: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Walk past the rest.

#### 10 min. Practise grammar

Review: the joining words (day 131), the determiners (day 132), the prepositions and the sentence kinds (day 133). Say six cards aloud and each pair holds up the card that fits, about 1 minute each. "The hawk is big ___ the wren is small." (but) "I feed the fish ___ they are hungry." (because) "___ birds, the two near me, fly." (these) "The cat sits ___ the fence." (on, by or behind) "Stay back." (command) "Is the nest high?" (question)

#### 10 min. Opinion with a reason

Each child gets the opinion picture, picks a point card (stay or down) and one reason strip that supports it, and draws the picture for it, with one word label (stay or down) copied, 5 minutes. Then partners say it, 20 seconds each: "I think the nest should ___ because ___." Walk and listen; a child may pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud the first part of page 4 of Nests Need Space and show the three pictures from the card sheet. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which picture shows a useful reason to leave nests alone?" ([[s04-X134av2]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three keep that page open. **Ask.** "Which words on the card support that choice?" ([[s04-X134bv2]]) Then say: "Two children answer a classmate. The classmate says the nest should stay because the young need it. Child one says: I like hawks. Child two says: You said the young need the nest." **Ask.** "Which child says the classmate's reason back?" ([[s04-X134c]]) The last three have pages 2 and 3 open. **Ask.** "One side says the nest should stay. Say one reason that side gives." ([[s04-X134d]]) Then turn to pages 4 and 5. **Ask.** "One side says the nest should come down. Say one reason that side gives." ([[s04-X134e]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread a page once), not yet seen. A child who calls an unrelated fact a reason (error C) goes to day 135's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say hop and hope: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /r/ /ai/ /n/. Say the first, middle and last sound of goat. Segment coat.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 21, lesson 110: no new teaching. Pairs reread the chains on small boards and spell seat, road, rain, tail and mail, 20 seconds a word. The progress sample runs in the rotation below.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print "I think the nest should stay." as a copy, one letter form checked, 5 minutes. Dictate "The seat is on the road." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The cycle 21 sample runs here. The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: map, blend, read, spell, then the sample: each child reads one reserved word from your folder ([[s04-A5]]); then the card goes away and each writes one word you dictate ([[s04-A6]]), 1 minute each. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Throat Hurts, each child heard 20 seconds in the book and 20 seconds on the sample, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. Never say the words aloud to the class. The others reread in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Record the sample marks and the exit marks. Regroup for cycle 22 by the sample and the cycle record. Name day 135's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips aloud and draw the point cards on a sheet. One child: sort the strips, say the opinion sentence and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each reason aloud and your child says stay or down.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 2 and 3 of A City Nest: Two Views. **Say.** "Pip says 'The nest is brown' is a reason to leave it up. Is Pip right?" Expect: no.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws what they think should happen to the nest and one reason, with one word label.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "seat" and "road."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X134av2]] [[s04-X134bv2]] [[s04-X134c]] [[s04-X134d]] [[s04-X134e]] Read the first part of page 4 of Nests Need Space. Your child points to the picture that shows a useful reason, says which words tell why, then says one reason from each side of the nest talk.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the link words, the opinion and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: reason, point, because, so, listen, ask, stay, down, seat, road. Frames: "I think the nest should ___ because ___." "You put ___ under ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick a useful reason, say a reason from each side and hear a reason said back. A child who called an unrelated fact a reason (error C, s04-X134av2) goes to day 135's opening repair. Day 135 rereads the nest page and the bluebird account and uses sentence clues for fly and nest.
