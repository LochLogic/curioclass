# Day 135: Compare the two pages and use sentence clues

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the three strips for the opening repair, the six protection strips and the clue cards fly and nest. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who called an unrelated fact a reason on day 134 (error C, s04-X134a). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This is made up for practice. A writer wants the class to wipe shoes on the mat." Lay Pip's Reason line with three strips: "Mud makes the floor slippery." "Mud is hard to clean." "The mat is brown." Say the test: "Wipe your shoes because ___." **Ask.** "Pip's Reason line has one strip that does not belong. Which strip comes off?" Pairs decide in 90 seconds and three pairs say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: the mat is brown. This is the repair for error C (s04-X134a); watch those children.

#### 15 min. Reread both pages

Review: in day 121 you used the sentence beside a word to learn what it means, and in day 124 you did the same for fly. Reread Nests Need Space and Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes, pointing to each labelled picture (about 495 words, 4 minutes). Three asks, about 3 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Point to the protection fact on page 3 of Nests Need Space." Expect: stay back, look from far away, do not touch a nest.
2. **Ask.** "Point to the picture on page 5 of the account that shows how people give birds a place to nest." Expect: the row of twenty finished boxes.
3. **Ask.** "Pip says both pages tell how people help birds that nest in holes. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: yes.

#### 15 min. Talk

Lay the three-box mat: Nests page only, Both, Account only. Read six strips once each: "Look from far away." "The law protects wild birds." "Steps inside help baby birds climb." "Twenty boxes were built." "A cavity is a hole in a tree." "Boxes help birds that nest in holes." Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Sort the six strips onto the mat." Pairs sort in 6 minutes. Expect: look from far away and the law under Nests page only; steps inside and twenty boxes under Account only; a cavity and boxes help under Both.
2. **Ask.** "Pip put 'Twenty boxes were built' under Nests page only. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no. About 3 minutes.
3. **Ask.** "Choose one strip you put under Both and point to its words on each page." Pairs find them in 4 minutes and three pairs show, 20 seconds each.

#### 10 min. Sentence clues

Review: day 121 and day 124, as above. Read the four cards aloud with their small drawings: "The robin will nest in the tree." "The nest is full of eggs." "The kite can fly high." "Do not let the fly in." Lay two picture pairs: a bird settling to make a home and a nest of eggs; a bird in the sky and an insect on a window. Two asks.

1. **Ask.** "Put each card under the picture it fits." Pairs sort in 4 minutes. Expect: robin will nest and nest is full of eggs, one under each nest picture; kite can fly high under the sky picture; let the fly in under the insect.
2. **Ask.** "Pip put 'Do not let the fly in' under the bird in the sky. Is Pip right? Mark the words next to fly that show it." Pairs mark in 3 minutes. Expect: no; the words let and in point to the insect. A child who keeps one meaning against the clue (error E) rereads the sentence and marks the clue first.

#### 10 min. Pair of pages

Give each child the two-page frame and three picture cards: boards with labels, children far from a nest looking up, a bluebird at a box. Each child places one under the Nests page, one under the Account, one in Same, then copies one word label, 5 minutes. Then pairs match the part cards: sort the roots lock, wrap, build and plant under un-, re- or both, 4 minutes. Review: un- and re- (day 131). Expect: lock to un-; wrap to both; build and plant to re-.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which pair of pictures shows the two meanings of bat?" ([[s04-X135a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three have the card in view. **Ask.** "What is one thing the pages share?" ([[s04-X135b]]) **Ask.** "What is one difference?" ([[s04-X135c]]) The last three keep the card in view. **Ask.** "Which words on page A tell you what kind of bat it is?" ([[s04-X135d]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who gives only a likeness when a difference was asked (error S) goes to day 136's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cub and cube: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /p/ /l/ /ay/. Say the first, middle and last sound of say. Segment stay.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 22, lesson 111, explicit teaching: ay and ow are new. Each spelling says a long vowel sound. Add them to the sound-spelling map with play and show. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: say, play, stay, tray, pay; then show, slow, grow, blow, low. Say each, tap the sounds, write it.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print "Look from far away." as a copy, one letter form checked, 5 minutes. Dictate "I can play and I can show you." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map ay and ow, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Throat Hurts, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. Cycle 22's reader waits until ay and ow are mapped. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 136's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips and cards aloud and draw the boxes on a sheet. One child: sort the six strips onto the mat and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each strip aloud and your child points left, middle or right.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read page 3 of Nests Need Space and page 2 of the account. **Say.** "Pip says both pages tell about a youth club. Is Pip right?" Expect: no, only the account does.
- **10 min. Sort.** Say "Look from far away" and "A cavity is a hole in a tree." Your child points to the nest page only or Both.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "play" and "show."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X135a]] [[s04-X135b]] [[s04-X135c]] [[s04-X135d]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the pair of pictures for the two meanings, says one thing the same and one thing different, then says which words tell the meaning on the first page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the sort and the exit. Longer: a child who sorts quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: protect, both, but, clue, nest, fly, play, show. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can name a likeness and a difference between two pages and who can find the clue words for a word with two meanings. A child who gave only a likeness (error S, s04-X135c) goes to day 136's opening repair. Day 136 looks at an opinion paragraph for its topic, opinion, one reason and closing.
