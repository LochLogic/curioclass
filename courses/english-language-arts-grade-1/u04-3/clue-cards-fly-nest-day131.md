# Clue cards, fly and nest

Four sentence cards you read aloud, with small drawings: "The robin will nest in the tree." "The nest is full of eggs." "The kite can fly high." "Do not let the fly in." Two picture pairs: a bird settling to make a home beside a nest of eggs, and a bird in the sky beside an insect on a window. Pairs sort each card under the picture it fits, then mark the clue words next to nest or fly. Used on day 135.
