# Day 133: Sort the reasons by the point they support

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the three strips for the opening repair, the six reason strips with two point cards, and Pip's finished sort with two strips swapped. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who called an unrelated fact a reason on day 132 (error C, s04-X132b). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This is made up for practice. A writer wants the class to close the window." Lay three strips: "Rain comes in." "The window is square." "The sill is white." Say the test: "Close the window because ___." A reason fits after because and tells why. **Ask.** "Pip put 'The window is square' under Reason. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no. **Ask.** "Sort the three strips: Reason or Not a reason." Pairs sort in 90 seconds and three pairs say why, 20 seconds each. Expect: rain comes in under Reason; the other two under Not a reason. This is the repair for error C (s04-X132b); watch those children.

#### 12 min. Reread the two views

Reread pages 2 to 6 of A City Nest: Two Views (about 190 words, 2 minutes). Three asks, about 3 minutes each; each child marks the words on a printed copy or points on the big page.

1. **Ask.** "Mark the words after because on page 2." Expect: the young need it as they grow.
2. **Ask.** "Mark the words after because on page 5." Expect: so many bird watchers came to see the hawks.
3. **Ask.** "Mark the words after because on page 6 for each side." Expect: the hawks need a home; the sidewalk should be safe and clear.

#### 18 min. Talk and sort

Show the two point cards: Leave the nest up, Take the nest down. Read the six strips once each: "A tall building is like a tree." "The young need the nest as they grow." "Food is easy to find in the city." "Bits of the nest fall to the sidewalk." "The street is crowded." "People need room to walk." Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Sort the six strips under the point each one supports." Pairs sort in 6 minutes. Expect: the first three under Leave the nest up; the last three under Take the nest down.
2. **Ask.** "Pip's finished sort has 'People need room to walk' under Leave the nest up. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no. Then: "Fix Pip's sort: which two strips swap?" Pairs fix in 4 minutes. Expect: people need room to walk goes under Take the nest down, and the young need the nest goes under Leave the nest up.
3. **Ask.** "Review: you used But for what is different on day 124. Pip says both sides want the same thing. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no, they want different things. Read page 6: "But they do not want the same thing."

#### 10 min. Prepositions

New: a word that shows where one thing is next to another is a preposition. Review: you learned the position words on, under, beside and in in kindergarten. Lay the marks and show the cards: on, near, away from, by, under, behind. Move the paper bird and say each: "The bird is on the nest. The bird is near the nest." Two asks.

1. **Ask.** "Put the bird where I say: by the nest, behind the nest, away from the nest." Pairs move the bird in 4 minutes.
2. **Ask.** "Pip says 'The bird near.' Does it tell where? Say it so it does." Expect: The bird is near the nest. A child who leaves out the where word (error P) moves the bird first, then says it.

#### 10 min. Sentence forms

Review: the four kinds of sentence (day 35): a statement, a question, a command and an exclamation; and joining two with and (day 72). Each kind has a card. Say six sentences about the nest: "The hawk sits on the nest." "Is the nest high?" "Stay back from the nest." "What a big nest!" "The nest is high and the street is crowded." "Stay back and look up." **Ask.** "Put each sentence under its kind card." Pairs sort in 6 minutes and three pairs say why, 20 seconds each. Nothing is written.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which paired-reason picture fits?" ([[s04-X133a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three have the card in view and are asked stems b and c. **Ask.** "Which reason goes with the writer who wants a quiet corner?" ([[s04-X133b]]) **Ask.** "Which reason goes with the writer who wants the corner moved away from the door?" ([[s04-X133c]]) The last three have page 3 of A City Nest: Two Views open. **Ask.** "Some bird watchers say it is great to see the hawks. Which side are they on?" ([[s04-X133d]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card or the page once), not yet seen. A child who names an unrelated fact or the other writer's reason (error C) goes to day 134's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cut and cute: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /g/ /oa/ /t/. Say the first, middle and last sound of tail. Segment road.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 21, lesson 109, explicit teaching: read and write connected sentences with oa, ai and ea and check the meaning. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: toad, road, load; then rain, main, tail. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. Then read "The toad is on the road." and check that it makes sense.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print "Leave it up." and "Take it down." as two copied sentences, 5 minutes. Dictate "The goat eats the leaf." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Throat Hurts, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 134's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips aloud and draw the two point cards on a sheet. One child: sort the six strips under the two points and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each reason aloud and your child says leave it up or take it down.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 4 and 5 of A City Nest: Two Views. **Say.** "Pip says 'People need room to walk' helps the side that leaves the nest up. Is Pip right?" Expect: no.
- **10 min. Sort.** Say "The young need the nest" and "The street is crowded." Your child says which side each helps.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "road" and "rain."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X133a]] [[s04-X133b]] [[s04-X133c]] [[s04-X133d]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture that pairs each reason with its writer, then names the reason for each writer, then says which side the bird watchers on page 3 are on.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the sort and the exit. Longer: a child who sorts quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: reason, point, because, preposition, near, behind, road, rain. Frames: "Leave the nest up." "Take the nest down." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pair a reason with its writer and who can find a side on a page. A child who named an unrelated fact or the other writer's reason (error C, s04-X133b) goes to day 134's opening repair. Day 134 gathers the reasons for each nest decision and talks them over in groups of three.
