# Day 131: Read about nests and compare two pages

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read both texts. Make the new pair of stories and its four strips below, and the six detail strips on the pair picture piece. Name today's heard six and the catch-up group, and the children who missed day 130's exit stem a (error A). Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These stories are made up for practice." Read twice: "Story E: Mia finds a lost glove by the gate. She tells the helper. Story F: Omar finds a lost scarf by the fence. He tells the guard." Lay a hand and an eye. Mix four strips: "Both children tell a grown-up." "Mia tells the helper and Omar tells the guard." "The glove is red." "The scarf is long." **Ask.** "Sort the strips: what the children do under the hand, how things look under the eye." Pairs sort in 90 seconds and three pairs say why one strip went where it did, 20 seconds each. Expect: the first two under the hand, the other two under the eye. This is the repair for error A (s04-X130a); watch those children.

#### 15 min. Read both pages

Say the new words and touch each labelled picture: nest, cavity, protect, volunteer, nest box (you met protect in kindergarten). Read Nests Need Space (about 220 words, 2 minutes) and then Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes (about 275 words, 2 minutes). Three asks, about 3 minutes each.

1. **Ask.** "Review: in day 121 you used a sentence clue to find what a word means. Which words on page 2 tell what a cavity is?" Expect: a hollow space, like a hole in a tree.
2. **Ask.** "Point to the picture on page 3 of Nests Need Space that shows how to help a nest." Expect: the children and the adult far from the tree.
3. **Ask.** "Pip says the staff cut the wood before the children came. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: yes.

#### 15 min. Talk

Review: you sorted what is Both and what is But on a picture in day 124. Today the mat has three boxes: Nests only, Both, Boxes only. Give each pair the six strips, read each aloud once: "Do not touch a nest." "Young birds need their parents to come back." "A cavity is a hole in a tree." "People can build nest boxes." "Staff cut the wood." "Volunteers write a welcome." Three asks.

1. **Ask.** "Sort the six strips onto the mat." Pairs sort in 5 minutes. Expect: do not touch a nest and young birds need their parents under Nests only; a cavity and nest boxes under Both; staff cut the wood and a welcome under Boxes only.
2. **Ask.** "Pip put 'Do not touch a nest' under Both. Is Pip right?" Thumbs. Expect: no. Then: "Pip put 'People can build nest boxes' under Both. Is Pip right?" Expect: yes. About 3 minutes.
3. **Ask.** "Choose one strip you put under Both and point to its words on both pages." Pairs find them in 4 minutes and three pairs show, 20 seconds each.

#### 7 min. Conjunctions

New: a word that joins two parts of a sentence is a conjunction. Review: you learned because in kindergarten, so on day 122, and but with Both and But on day 124. Read page 3 of Nests Need Space: "Nests need space, because a nest is not safe if people come too close." **Ask.** "Which part tells why: the part before because or the part after?" Expect: the part after. Then give each pair the four conjunction cards (and, but, so, because). Say a sentence with a gap and each pair holds up the card that joins it, 1 minute each: "The dog is wet ___ I get a towel." (so) "The bird is small ___ it is loud." (but) "We stay back ___ the nest needs space." (because) "A hawk sits on the ledge ___ a pigeon sits on the roof." (and) After the last, say it wrong on purpose: "We stay back so the nest needs space." Say the link aloud with the class: "We stay back, and why? The nest needs space." Expect: because. A child who picks a card that does not fit the link (error M) says the link aloud first.

#### 6 min. Word parts

Review: you learned un- (the opposite) and re- (again) in kindergarten, and the ending -ed on day 126. Lay the part cards un- and re-. **Ask.** "Sort each root card to un-, re-, or both: safe, load, pack, paint, fill." Pairs sort in 2 minutes. Expect: safe to un-; load and pack to both; paint and fill to re-. Check with thumbs, 2 minutes. Then show protect and protected: "protected is protect with -ed, in the past." Say: "A bird nest is protected by law." Read page 5 of the account aloud and touch replace: "place again."

#### 7 min. Pair of pages

Give each child the pair picture mat and three picture cards: a hole in a tree, children standing back from a nest, a child writing welcome on a box. Each child places one card in the box for Nests only, one in Same, and one in Boxes only, then copies one word label under any card, 7 minutes. Walk and mark the heard six as you pass.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures. All children mark a picture on the page. **Ask.** "Which paired picture fits the two pages?" ([[s04-X131a]]) While the rest finish the page, the heard six come to your table, 30 seconds each. The first three have the card in view and are asked stems b and c. **Ask.** "What is one thing the pages share?" ([[s04-X131b]]) **Ask.** "What is one difference?" ([[s04-X131c]]) The last three have page 4 of Nests Need Space open. **Ask.** "A nest is in a place that causes a problem. What does a child do?" ([[s04-X131d]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card or the page once), not yet seen. A child who gives only a likeness when a difference was asked (error S) goes to day 132's opening repair.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say rod and rode: which has the long vowel sound? Blend /s/ /ai/ /l/. Say the first, middle and last sound of seat. Segment coat.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 21, lesson 107, explicit teaching: connect each spelling to the words and the reader. Warm-up chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word: eat, seat, beak, leaf, bean, read. Say each, tap the sounds, write it. New: a vowel-team syllable is a syllable whose vowel sound is spelled with two letters. Say, clap and write the two parts of raincoat, beanbag and mailbox, 3 minutes. Map the tricky words should, good, very, only and after: touch the part that does not follow the map, say it, trace it, 4 minutes. Review: should is mapped like would (day 122).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print "Stay back from a nest." as a copy, one letter form checked, 5 minutes. Dictate "A goat is on the road." whole, then a word at a time, 4 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map, blend, read, spell, reread. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with Sam's Throat Hurts, first use now that should, good, very, only and after are mapped, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with 1 minute to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Fill the heard-six column, the cycle record and today's exit marks. Name day 132's opening repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the strips aloud and draw the three boxes on a sheet. One child: sort the six strips onto the three boxes and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: say each strip aloud and point to a left, middle or right place for Nests only, Both, Boxes only.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Reread.** Read pages 1 to 3 of Nests Need Space. **Say.** "Which words tell what a cavity is?" Expect: a hollow space, like a hole in a tree.
- **10 min. Sort.** Say "Do not touch a nest" and "A cavity is a hole in a tree." Your child points to Nests only or Both for each.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "seat" and "good."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X131a]] [[s04-X131b]] [[s04-X131c]] [[s04-X131d]] Read a short made-up card your child has not seen. Your child points to the picture that fits, says one thing the same and one thing different, then says what a child does when a nest is in a place that causes a problem.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the sort and the exit. Longer: a child who sorts quickly writes one more word label.

### Words and supports

Words: nest, cavity, protect, volunteer, nest box, conjunction, because, seat, bean. Frames: "Both ___." "But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can name a likeness and a difference between two pages and who can say what a child does about a nest in trouble. A child who gave only a likeness (error S, s04-X131c) goes to day 132's opening repair. Day 132 reads A City Nest: Two Views and finds each author's point and reason.
