# Point-and-reason strips

Day 132: the claim frame (claim, evidence, reasoning) with three strips for the second view: "The nest should come down." "Bits of the nest fall to the sidewalk." "People walk there, so it is not safe." Each child also gets a frame marked Point, Reason and a set-aside corner, with three strips: "The nest should stay." "The young need the nest as they grow." "The ledge is gray." Day 133 uses the six reason strips from the text: "A tall building is like a tree." "The young need the nest as they grow." "Food is easy to find in the city." "Bits of the nest fall to the sidewalk." "The street is crowded." "People need room to walk." Day 134 uses six link strips: "A hawk needs a nest because the young need it as they grow." "People walk there, so it is not safe." "The street is crowded because so many bird watchers came." "A tall building is like a tree." "Food is easy to find in the city." "People need room to walk." Reuse on days 136 and 137.
