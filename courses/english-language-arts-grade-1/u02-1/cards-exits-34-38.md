# Exit cards, days 34 to 38

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 34 card (s02-X34, RI.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Our rain log shows light rain, then heavy rain, then a dry yard. Our note says: the rain grew, then stopped." Show three picture strips: light rain, heavy rain, dry yard; dry yard, heavy rain, light rain; light rain, heavy rain, heavier rain. Say: "Point to the strip that this note goes with." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The first strip: light rain, heavy rain, dry yard. A child who says why: the rain grew, then stopped.

## Day 35 card (s02-X35, RL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud two cards. The story card: "Moon carried a silver cup to Wind." The fact card: "The moon rises on one side of the sky and sets on the other." Show two pictures: a moon with arms carrying a cup to a puff of wind, and a moon low over a hill with an arrow along the sky. Say: "Point to the picture that goes with the story words." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of the moon with arms carrying a cup to the wind. A child who says why: in the story the moon does something, as a character.

## Day 36 card (s02-X36a, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "At the creek, Otter found a cracked bucket. Otter asked Beaver for mud. Beaver brought a lump, and Otter patched the bucket." Show two pictures: a creek with reeds, and a hill with a tree. Say: "Point to where this story happens." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The creek picture.

## Day 36 card (s02-X36b, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Use the same card, read aloud again: "At the creek, Otter found a cracked bucket. Otter asked Beaver for mud. Beaver brought a lump, and Otter patched the bucket." Show three picture cards: Otter patching the bucket, Otter finding the bucket, Otter asking Beaver. Say: "Put the three picture cards in order." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** Finding the bucket, asking Beaver, patching the bucket.

## Day 37 card (s02-X37, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Badger rushed to stack logs. The logs fell with a crash. Badger felt upset. Next time Badger stacked slowly, and the logs stayed up." Show three pictures: Badger stacking slowly with the logs standing, a pile of fallen logs, and Badger asleep in the sun. Say: "Point to the picture that shows what Badger could learn." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of Badger stacking slowly with the logs standing. A child who says why: going slowly keeps the logs up.

## Day 38 card (s02-X38a, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Bear shut the shed. Finch waited outside. Bear opened it, and Finch brought a song. They listened together." Show three scene cards: Bear and Finch listening together, the shed shut with Finch outside, the shed open. Say: "Put the three scenes in order." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The shed shut with Finch outside, the shed open, Bear and Finch listening together.

## Day 38 card (s02-X38b, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Use the same card, read aloud again. Show two pictures: two friends listening together, and Bear shutting the shed with Finch outside. Say: "Point to the picture that shows a lesson this story could teach." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The picture of two friends listening together.
