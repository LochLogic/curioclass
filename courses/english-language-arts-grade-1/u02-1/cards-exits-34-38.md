# Exit pictures, days 34 to 38

Each exit reads a named passage of the unit's texts aloud; this page holds only the pictures and cards to show. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 34 exit pictures (s02-X34v2, RI.1.3)

Passage: Sky Patterns, page 5 (the moon's pattern), read aloud again; day 34 works pages 3 and 4. Reread page 5 of Sky Patterns aloud: "The moon has a pattern too. Some nights it is round and bright. Some nights it is a thin curve. Then it grows round again." Show the class record card: a thin moon, and a week later a round moon, read aloud: "We saw a thin moon. Later we saw a round moon." Say: "Connect what we saw to the fact on the page. Use because or so." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The moon looked thin and then round because the moon has a pattern: it is a thin curve, then it grows round again.

## Day 35 exit pictures (s02-X35v2, RL.1.5)

Passage: the tale, page 5 (the Sun and the Moon climb to the roof), beside Sky Patterns, page 1; day 35 works the tale's page pauses and Sky Patterns, page 4. Reread two parts aloud. From the tale, page 5: "The Sun and the Moon climbed up to sit on the roof." From Sky Patterns, page 1: "The sky has the sun, the moon and the stars." Show two pictures: the Sun and the Moon on a roof, and children looking at the sky. Say: "Point to the picture that goes with the story words." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The Sun and the Moon on the roof. A child who says why: in the tale the Sun and the Moon do things, as characters.

## Day 36 exit pictures (s02-X36av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: the tale, page 4 (the Water at the gate, to knee-deep), read aloud ahead of day 37; day 36 works pages 1 to 3. Read page 4 of the tale aloud, from "The Water came to the gate" to "knee-deep." Show two pictures: the Sun's big compound with its gate, and a hill with a tree. Say: "Point to where this part happens." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The compound with its gate.

## Day 36 exit pictures (s02-X36bv2, RL.1.2)

Passage: the tale, page 4 (the Water at the gate, to knee-deep); day 36 works pages 1 to 3. Read page 4 again. Show three picture cards: the water knee-deep, the Water at the gate, the Water flowing in with the fish. Say: "Put the three picture cards in order." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The Water at the gate, the Water flowing in with the fish, the water knee-deep.

## Day 37 exit pictures (s02-X37v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: the tale, page 2 (the Water's warning, "it must be huge"); day 37 works the rising water on pages 4 and 5. Reread the end of page 2 aloud, from "Build a very big compound" to "it must be huge." Show three pictures: the Sun listening to the Water, the fish, and the Sun asleep. Say: "Point to the picture that shows what the Sun could learn here." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The Sun listening to the Water. A child who says why: listen when a friend warns you.

## Day 38 exit pictures (s02-X38av2, RL.1.2)

Passage: the tale, page 3 (the Moon at the door, the building, the invitation); day 38 orders the whole tale with the seven strip cards. Reread page 3 of the tale aloud. Show three scene cards: the Sun and the Moon building the wall, the Moon smiling at the door, the Sun inviting the Water. Say: "Put the three scenes in order." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The Moon smiling at the door, the Sun and the Moon building, the Sun inviting the Water.

## Day 38 exit pictures (s02-X38bv2, RL.1.2)

Passage: the tale, page 3 (the Sun keeps his promise); day 38 works the lesson of the whole tale. Read page 3 again. Show two pictures: the Sun building the big compound he promised, and the Sun's small house. Say: "Point to the picture that shows a lesson this page could teach." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The Sun building the compound he promised. A lesson: do what you promise.
