# Day 37 water rising strip

A drawn house with a long blue strip that slides up behind it. Three marks on the house: at the knee, at the head, at the roof. Children move the strip as the class says the repeated question and the answer from the tale. Beside it, a face card and an ear card for a first look at a feeling word and a sense word. No acting out of the tradition.
