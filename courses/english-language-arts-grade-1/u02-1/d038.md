# Day 38: Put the tale in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print Pip's turn lines, the exit cards with their pictures and the word card for the sample. Write the six names for today's exit, rotation group 1 and the sample. Sort the tale picture strip cards into a set for each pair. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the tale whole

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Review: you have heard every page of the tale, and you moved the blue strip yesterday. Read the tale whole, showing each picture (5 minutes). Then say the repeated question again with the class: the Water asks, "Is it still safe?" and the class answers, "Yes." (3 minutes). **Ask.** "What happens at the end?" Expect: the Sun and the Moon go up into the sky. Last, pairs tell each other the tale in three sentences, with the map in view (7 minutes); hear three pairs.

#### 10 min. Order the story

Each pair gets a set of tale picture strip cards, mixed: the Sun visits the Water; the Water says the house is too small; the Sun promises a big compound; the Water comes in; the water rises to the head; the water rises over the roof; the Sun and the Moon go up. Pairs place the cards in order and say first, next, last (6 minutes). Walk and ask, "Which card is first?" Hang one set on the class map and check it with the class (4 minutes): the class reads the order with you. Review: the story-map from days 36 and 37.

#### 10 min. Talk with linked turns

Groups of three: one speaks, one adds, one asks "Which part?" Pip shows three habits, one line at a time, and the class echoes each: "I will wait until you finish." "I will add to your idea: Sun said yes again." "What do you mean by it?" Say: "Which event matters most for the lesson?" Starter: "I think ___ matters because ___." Each group has about three minutes; the six move to your table for the last two. This is practice only; nothing here is scored. Look-fors: each child waits; one child adds to what was said; one child asks a question about it.

#### 20 min. Retell and lesson

If many children picked the fish, a topic with no lesson, on day 37's exit (error B), open with four minutes. Read a made-up line: "Hare rushed, and the pots fell." Say: "A topic is pots. A lesson is go slowly so the pots stay up." Lay an arrow card between the action and the result. Pairs say the action, the result and a lesson. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge.

Partners retell the tale with the map, first, next, last (4 minutes). Then each child draws the event that best supports the lesson, adds a word label, and says "The lesson is ___ because ___" (6 minutes). Hear the six, one minute each (6 minutes), while the others draw a second event. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Judge with three look-fors: (1) names an event in its place in the order; (2) states a lesson, not only a topic; (3) points to the picture or the words that show it.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 3 of the tale aloud. Show three scene cards: the Sun and the Moon building the wall, the Moon smiling at the door, the Sun inviting the Water. Say: "Put the three scenes in order." The rotating six each lay the cards and say first, next, last, 20 seconds each ([[s02-X38av2]]). Then read page 3 again and show two pictures: the Sun building the big compound he promised, and the Sun's small house. Say: "Point to the picture that shows a lesson this page could teach." Every child points ([[s02-X38bv2]]). A good order is door, building, inviting; a reversed pair is error A. A good lesson picture is the Sun building what he promised: do what you promise. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. These marks go to day 39.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: the class says each sound of "stop", "swim" and "frogs" with a finger up for each. Blend and segment five sounds: /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/ /s/, "stops". Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 5 sample. Review: you have spelled short o words since day 34. At the teacher table, group 1 (the six) is sampled one child at a time, about 40 seconds each (4 minutes in all). Show the word card and say: "Read this word." The child sounds it out and reads it ([[s02-A1]]). Remove the card and say: "Write the word I say: job." Say it once in a sentence, then once alone ([[s02-A2]]). The child writes the whole word on a sound-box sheet. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen, with an error letter if one fits. The other children, in pairs at their seats for the 10 minutes, spell mop, pot and box on sound boxes and read Sam's Box to each other. Groups 2 to 5 are sampled in the same way at the table during the rotation. The next foundations group on day 39 starts from today's results.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: each child prints capitals G to N into the letter-form record (4 minutes) and adds the day. Revise y, z and A to F with a finger on the page. A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong. Dictation (4 minutes): say "Sam has a mop." Children print a word at a time with the map, then point to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; group 1 has been sampled, so it reads Sam's Box and spells with you. Groups 2 to 5 are sampled first (4 minutes: read, then write the word) and then read one page of Sam's Box aloud. Regroup by the results, for the next foundations block: children who read and wrote the word go on together; children who need the vowel again meet in one group. The other children rotate through stations: reread Sam's Box with a partner; chain mop, map, mad and sad with letter cards; spell dog and pot on sound boxes; reorder the tale cards with a partner. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the retell marks and the sample marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the new foundations groups for day 39.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the scene cards on scrap paper and write the sample word on a card. One child: reread the tale, order the seven cards with a grown-up, draw the event that supports the lesson, answer the exit by pointing, and read and write the sample word.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the seven events on a long page. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The linked turns and the rotation.

- **10 min. Order.** Your child tells the tale from the pictures, first, next, last.
  **Say.** "Which part matters most for the lesson, and why?" Expect: the Sun said yes and the water kept rising, so listen to a warning.
- **10 min. Lesson.** Your child draws that event, adds a word and says, "The lesson is ___ because ___."
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child prints G to N, then "Sam has a mop."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X38av2]] [[s02-X38bv2]] You read page 3 of the tale aloud. You show three scenes, the Moon at the door, the building and the invitation, and your child puts them in order, alone. Then you show two pictures, the Sun building what he promised and a small house, and your child points to the one that shows a lesson, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the order, the retell, the exit and the sample; cut the linked turns to one group. Longer: a child who finishes retells the tale to a new partner with the cards.

### Words and supports

Words: order, retell, lesson, first, next, last, matters. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "The lesson is ___ because ___." "I think ___ matters because ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 teaches feeling and sense words. If many children laid the scenes in the wrong order (error A) on today's exit, the opening of day 39's repair part goes to a four-minute repair: reread the time words and place the scenes. If many children missed the sample (error K, reading the vowel wrong, or error M, a letter left out of the written word), the next foundations group on day 39 starts with a three-minute repair: isolate the spoken vowel, map it and blend again.
