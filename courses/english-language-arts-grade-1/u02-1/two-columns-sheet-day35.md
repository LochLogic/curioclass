# Day 35 two columns: tale and information

A sheet folded into two columns. Heading one: **A tale.** Heading two: **An information text.** Two features go under each. Under the tale: characters who talk and act; a lesson. Under the information text: real facts; the word "so" joining two facts. Children point to the page that shows each feature and say how they know. The sheet returns on day 40 and day 44.
