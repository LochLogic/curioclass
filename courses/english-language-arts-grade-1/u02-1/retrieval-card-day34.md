# Day 34 retrieval card: Sana's Drawing

Read aloud: "Sana looks out of the window at the evening sky. She sees a pale moon and one bright star. Sana draws the moon and the star on a card. Then she adds a thin cloud." Show one picture: a child at a window with a drawing pad and a pale moon in the sky. Then run the kindergarten tasks, one ask at a time: name the title and author of the card; retell it; tell how seeing led to drawing; sort "moon" with sky words; describe the picture; add one detail; give a note. Hear the rotating six; a miss is modelled at the next small group.
