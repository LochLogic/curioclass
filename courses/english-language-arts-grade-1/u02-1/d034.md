# Day 34: What does the sky show us?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sky record cards, the Sana's Drawing card, the exit strips and a letter-form record for each child. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Hang the sunrise and sunset cards apart, with room for the arrow card. Never look at the sun: the pictures are the record, and no child is asked to watch the sky at home. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Hook and read

Whole class on the rug. Words first (4 minutes), each with its picture: sky, day, observe, connect, notice, pattern, sunrise, sunset. Say each word once; the class says it back. Review: you used observe and notice in kindergarten, and you made a connection arrow there. New today: pattern, sunrise and sunset.

Read *Sky Patterns* whole (about 130 words), showing each page. Pause after page 3, 4 and 5 (4 minutes in all). **Ask.** after page 3: "What does the sun do in the morning?" Expect: it comes up; it rises. After page 4: "What does the sun do in the evening?" Expect: it goes down; it sets. After page 5: "What does the moon do again and again?" Expect: it is round, then thin, then round.

Match the page sentences to the class sky picture record (5 minutes). Hold up the sunrise card and the sunset card. Read page 3's first sentence; a child points to its card. Read page 4's first sentence; a child points. Then hang the sky fact cards under them: The sun rises. The sun sets. Say: "A pattern happens again and again in the same order. The sun rising and setting is a pattern." Last, say the question the unit will answer (2 minutes): "What patterns can we see in the sky, and what stories do people tell about them?" Children say it back once. No answer is wanted yet.

#### 8 min. Retrieval

Review: these are seven kindergarten tasks, all done before. Read the Sana's Drawing card aloud once, with its picture (1 minute). Then seven asks, one at a time, partners saying the answer to each other (about 1 minute each; each ask is 30 seconds for partners and 20 for a heard child). Hear one of the six per ask; the seventh goes to the child who passed or to a volunteer, and only the six are marked. 1. "Say the title and who made the card." Expect: Sana's Drawing, Curio Class. 2. "Retell it, first, next, last." 3. "How did seeing lead to drawing?" Expect: Sana saw the moon, so she drew it. 4. "Sort moon with sky words": cards star, cloud and sun go in the sky pile, hat and cup in the other. 5. "Describe the picture." 6. "Add one detail Sana could draw." 7. "Give me one note about the evening sky." A miss is modelled at the next small group; teach nothing new here.

#### 7 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

If many children on day 33's exit gave an unrelated turn (error AE, "My tub is red"), open with four minutes. Say a made-up line: "A cup on the shelf holds pencils." Show two replies: "My shoes are blue" and "I add that the cup holds pencils for all of us." **Ask.** "Which reply builds on what was said?" Expect: the second, because it names the first idea. Pairs say one of each, then the class does the reread in three minutes, page 3 only. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the reread the whole seven minutes.

Reread pages 3 and 4 aloud. **Ask.** "Which words in the page tell what the sun does?" Expect: comes up, rises (page 3); goes down, sets (page 4). Partners point to the words in the large print.

#### 5 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed. Say: "What do you notice about when the sun comes up and goes down?" Starter: "I notice ___." Expect: it comes up in the morning and goes down in the evening. Hear three pairs. Look-fors: each partner gives a turn and a child builds on the other's idea with "I add that ___."

#### 20 min. Make a connection

Review: a connection arrow joins two things with so or because. Model on the class record (6 minutes). Step 1: hold up the sunrise card: "I see the sun just above the roofs." Step 2: read page 3 aloud: "The sun comes up, so light fills the sky." Step 3: hold the fact card "Light fills the sky." Step 4: lay the arrow card between the picture and the fact, and the word so on it. Say: "I see the sun come up, so light fills the sky." **Ask.** "What does the arrow join?" Expect: what we see in the picture and a fact from the page. **Ask.** "Which word joins them?" Expect: so. Put a sticker marked 3 beside it: "Sky Patterns, page 3." That sticker is the source note, and it tells where the fact is found.

Each child draws a sunrise or a sunset in a box (8 minutes), adds a one-word label (sun, sunrise or sunset), and says the frame: "The sun ___, so ___." A sunset record uses page 4: the sun sets, so the sky grows dim. The child places the page sticker, 3 for a sunrise or 4 for a sunset. Walk and ask, "Which page tells it?" Write one source note for each group on the wall record: Sky Patterns, page 3; Sky Patterns, page 4. Last (6 minutes), three children show their record and say the frame; the class points to the fact card that fits.

Look-fors: the picture shows what is seen; the sentence has a fact from the page; the sticker matches the page.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud: "Our rain log shows light rain, then heavy rain, then a dry yard. Our note says: the rain grew, then stopped." Show three strips: light rain, heavy rain, dry yard; dry yard, heavy rain, light rain; light rain, heavy rain, heavier rain. Say: "Point to the strip that this note goes with." Every child points to a strip. The rotating six then say or show which words in the note tell what changed, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s02-X34]]). A good answer points to the first strip, with the changes in the order the note gives. The strip in the reverse order is error A. The strip that adds a change the note never says is error E. These marks go to day 35.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken pairs; children tap the knee for a short vowel and slide the hands apart for a long vowel: not and note, tub and tube, pin and pine. Blend four sounds: /b/ /o/ /k/ /s/, "box"; the class taps each sound, then says the word. Segment six, "six": /s/ /i/ /k/ /s/. Say: "x holds two sounds." Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Short o (3 minutes): the picture is an octopus; the sound is /o/, as at the start of ox. Add o to the map. Then j with jam and w with wig join the picture list for these words. Review: you printed J j on day 11 and W w on day 24, and x says /k/ /s/ together, from day 25.

Decode a chain with letter cards (3 minutes), one sound changing: mop, top, tip, tap. Each time, the class says what changed. Spell on sound boxes (2 minutes): pot and box; x takes one box and says two sounds. Mapped words, the part to remember (2 minutes): from and wants, then says. The class reads the three cards twice.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: each child prints a to h into the eight boxes of the letter-form record and dates it (4 minutes). Count aloud: the line first, then the bump, for b; round first for d (see tomorrow). A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong; the record is scored on day 43. Dictation (4 minutes): say "Tom has a box." Children print a word at a time, with the map: the capital T, Tom, the mapped word has, a, box and the period. Last, each child points to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads one page of Pat's Lunch aloud, then spells two words, pot and mop, on sound boxes. Use the previous cycle's reader until the o words and mapped words are secure; the cycle 5 reader starts tomorrow. The other children rotate through stations: reread Pat's Lunch with a partner; chain mop, top, tip and tap with letter cards; spell box and pot on sound boxes; draw and label a sunrise. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 3 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record: who was heard at the table and at the exit. Name tomorrow's six.

#### 2 min. Retrieval

Read sun and segment it: /s/ /u/ /n/. Then explain suns with two pictures, one sun and two suns: the s says more than one. Children point to the picture for suns.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on scrap paper and the eight boxes on a lined page. No sticker: write 3 or 4. One child: read *Sky Patterns* with a grown-up, draw a sunrise record, say the frame, do the exit by pointing and print a to h; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a sunrise on paper. No stickers: write 3 or 4.

**Skip.** The retrieval, the rotation and the page sticker.

- **10 min. Notice.** Look at a morning picture, not at the sun. Ask what your child sees.
  **Say.** "What do you notice about the sun in the morning?" Expect: it comes up, it is above the roofs.
- **10 min. Connect.** Your child draws the sun coming up and says, "The sun comes up, so ___."
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child prints a to h, then "Tom has a box."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X34]] You show the strips and read the card. Your child points, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the connection, the exit and the rotation; cut retrieval asks 5 to 7. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second record for the other half of the day and says the frame with so.

### Words and supports

Words: sky, day, observe, connect, notice, pattern, sunrise, sunset, so. Frames: "The sun ___, so ___." "I notice ___." "I add that ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; scribing for a child who cannot yet print a label. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 reads the tale of the sun and the water and compares it with *Sky Patterns*, then joins two facts with so. If many children placed the strips in the wrong order (error A, dry yard first) or picked the strip that adds a change the note never says (error E, heavier rain) on today's exit, the first minutes of day 35's Compare part go to a four-minute repair: reread the note, point to each change, and lay the strips in order.
