# Day 36 story-map cards for the Efik and Ibibio tale

Unit 1's story-map, folded into three parts, with cards to place. **Characters:** Sun, Water, Moon. **Place:** the earth, Sun's small house. **First events:** Sun visits Water; Water says Sun's house is too small; Sun promises to build a big compound. Each child draws one event on a card and places it.
