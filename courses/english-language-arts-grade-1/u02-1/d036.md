# Day 36: Who, where and what first

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story map and the tale picture strip cards, the exit cards with their pictures, and a copy of Sam's Box for each child. Write the six names for today's exit and rotation group 1. Hang the story map in three parts: who, where, what first. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the tale

Whole class on the rug. Words first (4 minutes), each with its picture: house, remain, compound. Review: you met tale, promise and warn yesterday. New today: remain means to stay, and a compound is a big yard with houses in it. Say each once; the class says it back.

Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Reread pages 1 to 3, showing each picture (3 minutes). Pause after Sun's promise and ask one thing at a time (4 minutes). **Ask.** "Who are the friends?" Expect: the Sun and the Water. **Ask.** "Where do they live?" Expect: on the earth. **Ask.** "What does the Sun promise?" Expect: to build a big compound. Children point to the picture for each answer. Leave the rest of the tale for tomorrow.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

If many children picked the fact picture on day 35's exit (error D), open with four minutes. Read two made-up cards: the story card "Hen asked Duck for a ride." and the fact card "A hen has two legs." Show two pictures: a hen on a duck and a hen standing still. **Ask.** "Which words are the story words?" Expect: the card where someone does something. Point to the words first, then to the picture. Pairs say it to each other. Then do the rest in six minutes. If only a few slipped, work with them at the rug edge and give the questions the whole ten minutes.

**Ask.** "Who is in the tale?" Expect: the Sun, the Water and the Moon. **Ask.** "What does the Water say about the house?" Expect: it is too small. **Ask.** "Who can show me the picture where the Water listens?" A child points. Partners point to the picture for each answer.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed. Say: "Why does the Water warn the Sun?" Starter: "The Water warns the Sun because ___." Pip says one line at a time, and the class echoes each: "I wait until you finish." "I add that the house is small." Hear three pairs. Expect: the house is too small for the Water's people. Look-fors: each partner gives a turn; a child builds on the other's idea with "I add that ___."

#### 20 min. Story-map

Review: unit 1's story-map, with three parts. Model on the class map (8 minutes). Who: hang the cards Sun, Water, Moon. Where: the earth, the Sun's small house. What first: lay three picture cards in order: the Sun visits the Water; the Water says the house is too small; the Sun promises a big compound. Say first, next, last for the three cards. **Ask.** "Which card is first?" Expect: the Sun visits the Water.

Each child draws one event on a blank card (8 minutes), adds a one-word label and places it on the map in order, saying "First ___, next ___, last ___" with the class. Walk and ask, "Which part of the tale is that?" Last (4 minutes), three children say their event and place it.

Look-fors: the picture shows one event; the card is in its place in the order; a one-word label.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud: "At the creek, Otter found a cracked bucket. Otter asked Beaver for mud. Beaver brought a lump, and Otter patched the bucket." Show two pictures: a creek with reeds, and a hill with a tree. Say: "Point to where this story happens." Every child points ([[s02-X36a]]). Then read the card again and show three picture cards: Otter patching the bucket, Otter finding the bucket, Otter asking Beaver. Say: "Put the three picture cards in order." The rotating six each lay the cards, and say first, next, last if they speak, 20 seconds each ([[s02-X36b]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. A good answer is the creek picture and the order finding, asking, patching. A wrong order is error A. These marks go to day 37.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words: blend /b/ /o/ /k/ /s/, "box"; tap each sound; four sounds, and the class holds up four fingers. Segment "wax": /w/ /a/ /k/ /s/. Say: "x holds two sounds." Pairs: the class says the first sound, the vowel and the last sound of mop and jam. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Spell on sound boxes (4 minutes): box, wax and jot. x takes one box and says two sounds. Decode (2 minutes): mop, top, dot, not. Mapped words (4 minutes): from, wants, says. Review: you read from and wants yesterday. The class reads each card, then spells the part to remember: the s in says. The class reads Sam's Box, pages 1 to 3, with a finger under each word.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: each child prints q to x into the letter-form record (4 minutes) and adds the day. Revise i to p with a finger on the page. A form not yet made is incomplete, never wrong. Dictation (4 minutes): say "Sam says the dog is mad." Children print a word at a time with the map; the capital, the mapped words says and the, and the period are named aloud. Last, each child points to the capital and the period (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table, matched reading and spelling at the cycle's level: the group reads Sam's Box aloud, one page each, then spells two words, box and mop, on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Sam's Box with a partner; chain box, wax and mix with letter cards; spell dog and pot on sound boxes; draw their tale event again and label it. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks to the unit 2 record sheet and write the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name tomorrow's six.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the story map on a page folded in three and the cards on scrap paper. One child: reread the tale in two sittings, draw the three first events and say first, next, last, answer the exit by pointing and print q to x.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three small pictures on a page. No reader: use the cards from class.

**Skip.** The talk and the rotation.

- **10 min. Who and where.** Retell the first part of the tale.
  **Say.** "Who is in the tale, and where do they live?" Expect: the Sun, the Water and the Moon, on the earth.
- **10 min. First events.** Your child draws the Sun's visit and the Sun's promise and says first, next.
- **10 min. Letters.** Your child prints q to x, then "Sam says the dog is mad."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X36a]] [[s02-X36b]] You read a new card: a goat finds a lost glove in a meadow, asks a crow for string, and ties it on. You show two pictures, a meadow and a snowy peak, and your child points to where it happens, alone. Then you read it again and lay three small pictures of finding, asking and tying in a mixed row. Your child puts them in order, alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reread, the story-map, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to one pair. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second event and places it.

### Words and supports

Words: house, remain, compound, who, where, first, next, last. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "The Water warns the Sun because ___." Pictures first; pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers; a scribe for labels. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 rereads the difficult events and connects them to a lesson. If many children laid the three picture cards in the wrong order (error A) on today's exit, the opening of day 37's reread and question part goes to a four-minute repair: reread the card, say first, next, last and lay the cards again.
