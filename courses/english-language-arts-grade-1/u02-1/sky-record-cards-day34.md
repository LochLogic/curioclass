# Day 34 class picture record cards

Teacher-made picture cards for the class record: a sun just above a row of roofs with long light across a street (sun coming up); an orange sky with the sun touching the roofs (sun going down); a fact card "The sun rises." and a fact card "The sun sets."; and an arrow card. Children match the sentences from pages 3 and 4 of the read-aloud to the pictures, then place the arrow and say "so". Each child draws a sunrise or sunset, adds a one-word label, and the teacher writes the page sticker as the source note.
