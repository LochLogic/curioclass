# Day 35 sentence-kind lines

Pip, the class puppet, says four lines, one at a time, with the class showing the end mark by gesture: a statement, "The Sun has a small house."; a question, "Where is the Water's house?"; a command, "Tell the tale again."; an exclamation, "What a huge compound!" Then two short sentences are joined with "and": "Sun came. Moon came." becomes "Sun came and Moon came."
