# Section 2.1 opener: the sky question and the first tale

## What the section does

Days 34 to 38 open the unit's question: what patterns can we see in the sky, and what stories do people tell about them? The class hears a short information text, *Sky Patterns*, connects a picture record with a fact, then hears the Efik and Ibibio tale of why the sun and the moon live in the sky. By day 38 each child retells the tale in order and says a lesson it could teach. Day 38 also holds the first cycle sample and practice with taking turns in talk.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A pattern is something that happens again in the same order.** Say "first, next, last" with the pictures.
- **A fact and a story do different jobs.** A fact can be checked; a story has characters who do things.
- **A lesson is not a topic.** "Listen to a warning before you promise" is a lesson; "water" is only the topic.
- **The tale belongs to the people who tell it.** Say aloud that the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** two events are swapped. Reread the time words and place the picture cards.
- **B:** a topic is given as the message. Connect one action to its result, then say a lesson.
- **D:** a card is picked because it names the same thing. Point to what the words do first.
- **E:** a claim the card does not make. Point to the sentence.
- **W to Z:** talk habits. Practice only until day 61.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
