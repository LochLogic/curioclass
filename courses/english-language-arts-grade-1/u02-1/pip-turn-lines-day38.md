# Day 38 Pip's turn lines

Pip shows three habits, one line at a time. **Waiting:** "I will wait until you finish." **Linking:** "I will add to your idea: Sun said yes again." **Asking:** "What do you mean by 'it'?" The groups of three use the same three lines when they discuss which event matters most for the lesson. Practice only; nothing here is scored before day 61.
