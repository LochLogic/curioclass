# Day 72: Show what I know about my poem and my sentences

## Teacher page

### Before class

Charts down. Each child's poem, sheets and student page on the desk. Seat the scribes. Copy the heard six. Say each stem twice; give no hints.

### The day

#### 6 min. Setup and collect

Hand back day 71's assessment, each child's poem.

#### 15 min. W.1.5

Review: a peer question helps the writer add a detail (day 67). Partners swap poems and ask one question each (4 minutes); you write it if needed. Say: "Add what your partner asked about. Keep your poem on the same topic." ([[s02-A39]]) Each child draws or tells the detail (10 minutes); scribes write it for the heard six first, 40 seconds a child.

Check: My question is ___.

#### 24 min. L.1.1j

Review: statement, question, command and exclamation, joined with and (days 35 and 66). Show the moon picture. Three minutes a stem; each child writes it in its box or tells a scribe. In order: a statement ([[s02-A40]]), longer with and ([[s02-A41]]); a question ([[s02-A42]]), longer ([[s02-A43]]); a command ([[s02-A44]]), longer ([[s02-A45]]); an exclamation ([[s02-A46]]), longer ([[s02-A47]]).

#### 5 min. L.1.2a

Read card E aloud: "mina, november 14 2026"; "I drew the moon"; "Did you see it"; "Look at my drawing"; "My list: bell rope pail". Say: "Fix the name in the first line." ([[s02-A48]]) Then: "Fix the month in the first line." ([[s02-A49]])

#### 4 min. L.1.2b

Say: "Put the right end mark on each of the next three lines." ([[s02-A50]]) Then: "Write an exclamation about the picture, with its end mark." ([[s02-A51]])

#### 6 min. L.1.2c

Review: commas in a list and a date (day 59). Say: "Write the date from the first line again, with its commas." ([[s02-A52]]) Then: "Write the last line again, with its commas." ([[s02-A53]])

#### 10 min. Missing cycle 11 evidence

Only children with no day 68 record, groups of six, 3 minutes each; the others reread I Look Out in pairs. Each reads loud, bank and hunted, then writes shout and gift. An unheard child goes first in the rotation.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Say kit and kite; **Ask.** "Which has i's name?" Expect: kite. Blend /d/ /r/ /u/ /m/, "drum". Say "ship": /sh/ /i/ /p/. Segment "grin": /g/ /r/ /i/ /n/.

#### 10 min. Familiar map and spell

Small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread: mount, count, drift, clamp, sunk, hunt.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table: read I Look Out, a page each, then spell sunk and count. The others rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out; chain mount, count and drift; spell clamp and hunt; read look, see, a and the.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold paper; card E on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No scribe: you write what your child says.

**Skip.** The sounds.

- **20 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A39]] Your child tells you the poem. You ask one question about it. **Say.** "Add what I asked about. Keep your poem on the same topic." Expect: a detail that answers it.
- **15 min. Sentences.** Show a picture of a dog on a porch. Your child says a statement, a question, a command and an exclamation.
- **10 min. Fix.** Write "sam, may 9 2027" and "pen, book, bag". Your child fixes the name, the month and the commas.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the poem and the caption. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: question, detail, capital, comma. A scribe counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 73 opens the shared file; a child whose detail left the poem (error P) rereads the question and adds only its detail. Day 75 retries fragments, capitals, end marks and commas (errors H, Q to V).
