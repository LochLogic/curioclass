# Day 74: Tell about my poem with my drawing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep every chart down. Lay each child's poem, book page and a clean drawing page on their desk. Open the shared file and the shared device and device record. Seat the scribes. Copy the heard six. Number six groups of five children. Today observes: read each stem twice, give no hints, and no reteaching.

### The day

#### 6 min. Setup and record

Hand out the poems and pages. On the unit 2 record sheet, cite the day 73 device record and the day 61 description record for each child. Today scores only the new clear reading.

#### 24 min. Oral rotation

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each at your table; the heard six go first. Each child gets about 48 seconds: 36 seconds for the poem and the scene, 12 seconds for whole sentences. Say: "Read your poem to us, then tell about your sun scene and how it makes you feel." ([[s02-A56]]) A scribe may help a child read. Then say: "Tell about your poem in whole sentences." ([[s02-A58]]) That is 18 minutes of the first and 6 of the second across the six groups. Mark each on the oral rotation score grid: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A fragment such as "Hill. Sun." is error H: record it, do not repair it. The other children read their poem to a partner, softly, with the drawing in view.

#### 10 min. Improve the drawing

Say: "Draw the picture that shows the order of your poem's events, so a listener can follow." ([[s02-A57]]) Each child prepares or improves the poem drawing from their page. Decoration is not marked. A drawing that only adds stars or a border is error G: record it. Give no feedback.

#### 20 min. Finish the digital contribution

Only children without a full day 73 device record work at the device, one minute each, in this order: first the children marked error T, 5 minutes, each given one thing to enter and check (a sentence typed or told, or a picture placed), then the rest. The others reread the book pages with a partner and rehearse reading their poem. Mark the text editor, the drawing tool and the placing apart on the device record. A child still unfinished is marked not yet seen. Count: up to 20 children by 1 minute.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say tap and tape; **Ask.** "Which has a's name?" Expect: tape. Blend /f/ /r/ /o/ /g/, "frog". Say "rug": /r/ /u/ /g/. Segment "swim": /s/ /w/ /i/ /m/.

#### 10 min. Familiar map and spell

Consolidation, no new pattern. Uncover the map. Small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread: sound, trunk, raft, tank, bend, bump.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; the heard six go first. The group reads I Look Out, a page each (3 minutes), then spells sound and tank (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out; chain trunk, tank and bend with letter cards; spell raft and bump on sound boxes; read the word cards look, see, a and the.

#### 15 min. Handwriting, dictation and record

Dictation (8 minutes): "The frog sat on a log." Children print a word at a time; the capital T and the end mark are named aloud. Each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes). Move the marks for SL.1.4, SL.1.5 and SL.1.6 and the device record to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column (5 minutes).

### Other rooms

No device: the digital contribution is recorded as not observed. Oral reading and drawing run on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No scribe: you write what your child says.

**Skip.** The sounds.

- **20 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A56]] Your child reads you the poem, then tells the scene and how it feels. **Say.** "Read me your poem, then tell about your sun scene and how it makes you feel." Expect: the scene, one event and a feeling. Then [[s02-A58]]: "Tell about your poem in whole sentences."
- **10 min. Draw.** [[s02-A57]] Your child draws the events in order so a listener can follow.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads sound, trunk and raft and prints "The frog sat on a log."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading and description and the drawing. Longer: a child adds a second feeling word and says why.

### Words and supports

Words: scene, feeling. Frames: "In the picture ___." "I feel ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 shares the book. You read two fresh cards aloud once, a story and an information piece, and the children answer on paper and by pointing. The last 20 minutes repair the day's marked errors, then a fresh retry.
