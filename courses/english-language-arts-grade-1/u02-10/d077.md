# Day 77: Reading, questions and a tool explanation

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 76's assessment is not handed back; its marks wait for day 78. Keep the check cards covered. Today's individual readings are the other fifteen children, from the hearing order of day 76; nobody is heard twice. The marks of day 72 (sentences) and day 75 (retell, message and connection) are cited from the record sheet, not collected again. Mark the six children for the pair work from the foot of the hearing order.

### The day

#### 6 min. Set-up and moves

Say: "These cards are made up for practice. Nothing is graded. You may pass any ask." Seat today's fifteen where you can call each in turn; the others sit in pairs. Mark every ask on one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 9 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Three groups of five at your table, 3 minutes each, with the new words on the day 77 card: long or short ([[c1-F77h]]), blend ([[c1-F77i]]), first, middle or last sound ([[c1-F77j]]) and finger taps for scalp ([[c1-F77k]]), 40 seconds each and 20 seconds to change the group.

#### 15 min. Printing and dictation

All thirty write; only today's fifteen are marked. Say each word twice: flush ([[c1-F77l]]), look ([[c1-F77m]]) and stunt "the way it sounds" ([[c1-F77n]]), one minute each and 2 for the saying. For the next 10, a child whose all-letter sheet misses a form prints it with your written feedback; the rest finish side 2. Not a new alphabet test.

#### 30 min. Topic response

All thirty, new texts. Read the pliers text aloud once with its picture, 2 minutes. Partners ask and answer: one asks ([[c1-T77a]]), the other answers ([[c1-T77b]]), then swap, 4 minutes; you listen to the three pairs of the heard six, 80 seconds a pair. Show the picture: point to what the words did not say ([[c1-T77c]]), then say what pliers are for ([[c1-T77d]]) to a partner, 3 minutes. Read Tavi's Pedal aloud once, 3 minutes. Six asks, 1 minute each, children whisper to a partner or point, you hear the heard six: the feeling ([[c1-T77e]]), the place ([[c1-T77f]]), the three pictures in order ([[c1-T77g]]), who tells ([[c1-T77h]]), who speaks ([[c1-T77i]]), the last picture ([[c1-T77j]]). Last, 12 minutes: each child writes, or draws and labels, or tells a scribe a piece about tongs: the tool ([[c1-T77k]]), two facts ([[c1-T77l]]) and a closing line ([[c1-T77m]]). A scribe takes six children in 12 minutes at most; children needing one go first; a child with no piece goes on the missing-evidence list.

#### 60 min. Individual readings

Fifteen children, 4 minutes each at your table, the same order as day 76 with the day 77 cards: purpose ([[c1-F77a]]), first reading ([[c1-F77b]]), check the sense ([[c1-F77d]]), second reading ([[c1-F77c]]), five words ([[c1-F77e]]), five endings ([[c1-F77f]]) and ten mapped words ([[c1-F77g]]). A child who cannot finish in 4 minutes stops; the rest goes on the missing-evidence list. Collect the student pages at the end. The rest of the class reads the known readers and finishes side 2.

After class, mark the pieces and list who needs a second hearing. Gaps to look for: from day 76 (errors A to K and M) and a detail named as the topic or another detail given (error L), an answer from home (error M), the teller and the speaker mixed (error N), events out of order (error O), no closing or an opinion (error P).

### Other rooms

One child: hear the readings, then the pliers and Tavi's Pedal asks, about 20 minutes, and skip the pair work. No scribe: the adult writes the child's words once, read back.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read a short made-up story aloud.

**Skip.** The group sounds.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Say it is made up. Read aloud: "Tongs lift hot corn. Tongs keep hands cool." Do not help; write what you saw.
  **Say.** "Tell me two facts about tongs." Expect: two facts from the words, such as lifting hot corn and keeping hands cool.
- **10 min. Story.** Read a short made-up story told by a child.
  **Say.** "Who is telling it?" Expect: the child in the story.
- **10 min. Spell.** Say "look" and have your child write it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the readings and the tongs piece; hear the oral sounds on day 78. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second tool and labels one part.

### Words and supports

Words: question, fact, picture, tell. Frames: "The words say ___." "The picture shows ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count. Give one prompt only where an item says.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 uses both days' marks: you group children by the gap you recorded on days 76 and 77, teach one move and try a fresh text or word task. Nothing from today is given back before then.

### Credit

Ours, all of it. The patterns, tools and story ideas were taught earlier from adapted lessons; the cards here are new and written by Curio Class.
