# Day 75 card N and the star log (W.1.8 fallback)

Print "invented for practice" at the foot of card N. Read it aloud only for a child with no notebook answer on file (s02-A61); no teaching. The reading items of day 75 read named passages of the two tales and Stars in Daylight and Darkness.

**Card N, information (about a telescope).** "A telescope gathers more light than our eyes alone. This lets us see faint stars that our eyes alone cannot show us."

**Star log (companion to N).** "Our first night drawing shows the stars seen with eyes alone. Our telescope drawing adds faint stars between them." Two drawings: a few stars; the same view with more stars.

**Pictures and stickers.** Three event pictures for the Efik and Ibibio tale, pages 4 and 5 (the Water at the gate, the Sun and the Moon on the roof, the Sun and the Moon going up into the sky); a face sticker and a hand sticker for each child; a story mark and an information mark. Answers are not printed here.
