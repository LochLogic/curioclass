# Day 75 cards U and N and the star log (end-item cards)

Print "invented for practice" at the foot of cards U and N. Read each aloud once; no teaching.

**Card U, a story ("Star's Ribbon").** "Star pulled a ribbon of soft cloth. It caught on a twig and tore. Star felt worried. Moon brought a needle and mended it. Then Star spread the cloth for them both to sit on. Their work was done."

**Card N, information (about a telescope).** "A telescope gathers more light than our eyes alone. This lets us see faint stars that our eyes alone cannot show us."

**Star log (companion to N).** "Our first night drawing shows the stars seen with eyes alone. Our telescope drawing adds faint stars between them." Two drawings: a few stars; the same view with more stars.

**Pictures and stickers.** Three event pictures for U (the torn cloth, Moon mending it, the cloth spread for both); a face sticker and a hand sticker for each child; a story mark and an information mark. Answers are not printed here.
