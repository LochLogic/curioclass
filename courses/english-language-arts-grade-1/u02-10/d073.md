# Day 73: Add to my poem and put it in our book

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the writing-process chart up. Open the shared file on the device and save it under the day's date. Lay a book page and the day 71 poem on each desk. Seat the scribes. Copy the heard six and the device record. Make six groups of five children and number them. Today is the book: no accounts, no names or photographs on the device, and the book stays in class.

### The day

#### 10 min. Setup and save

Hand back day 72's assessment, each child's marked sheets; say nothing about errors, they wait for day 75. Point to the writing-process chart: plan, draft, share, revise, publish. Review: our writing-process chart from day 65. Say: "Today we revise, then publish." Open the shared file and show the pages in it, then save it, so each child sees the save happen once. Read the six group lists aloud. Review: shared device turns, text editor and drawing tool, one child at a time (day 67). Say that each child gets one minute at the device: thirty seconds to type or tell one sentence, thirty seconds to make or change one picture. A child may pass.

#### 20 min. Revision period

This part holds two jobs, in this order.

First, 5 minutes, only for children marked error P on day 72 (a detail about another topic). Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read: "A boy rode a sled." A friend asks, "What was on the hill?" Show two cards, snow and trees on a hill, and a boy eating lunch. **Ask.** "Which card answers the friend?" Expect: snow and trees. Each child rereads their own peer question from day 72 and says one detail that answers it, nothing else, then draws it on the book page.

Second, 15 minutes, only for children with no day 72 W.1.5 record ([[s02-A54]]). A partner asks one new question about the sun events in the poem; write it on the question and detail sheet if the child cannot. Say: "Add what your partner asked about, and keep the sun event." The child draws or tells the added detail; a scribe writes it, 40 seconds a child, heard six first. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child with a day 72 record is cited, not asked again: that child finishes the book page, copying the poem's best line under the picture.

#### 30 min. Shared device

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each at the device; the other five groups finish and assemble their paper pages. Say: "Type or tell one sentence of your poem, then make or change its picture and help a friend place it in the book." ([[s02-A55]]) Text editor first, about 30 seconds: the child types, or says the sentence and a partner types, and you read it back. Drawing tool next, about 30 seconds: the child makes or changes one picture while the child before them helps place it in the book. On the device record, mark the text editor, the drawing tool and the placing separately, with the help given. A child who only watches is error T: give that child one thing to enter and check, and note it. The count: 5 children by 1 minute is 5 minutes. A child not finished in the minute is marked not yet seen and finishes on day 74.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say hop and hope; **Ask.** "Which has o's name?" Expect: hope. Blend /s/ /t/ /a/ /m/ /p/, "stamp". Say "fish": /f/ /i/ /sh/. Segment "plan": /p/ /l/ /a/ /n/.

#### 10 min. Familiar map and spell

Consolidation, no new pattern. Uncover the map. Small groups map, blend, read, spell and reread: grunt, plant, mound, stamp, lift, hunt.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; the heard six go first. The group reads I Look Out, a page each (3 minutes), then spells mound and stamp (3 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out; chain grunt, plant and lift with letter cards; spell hunt and plant on sound boxes; read the word cards look, see, a and the.

#### 15 min. Handwriting, dictation and record

Dictation (8 minutes): "The sun sank on the hill." Children print a word at a time; the capital T and the end mark are named aloud. Each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes). Move the marks for W.1.5 and the device record to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column (5 minutes). Save the shared file.

### Other rooms

No device: the pages are paper only. Record W.1.6 as not observed, because paper alone does not complete it. Keep the shared file as the paper book.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: write on paper and draw the picture.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **20 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A54]] Your child tells you the sun poem. You ask one new question about it. **Say.** "Add what I asked about, and keep the sun event." Expect: a detail that answers it.
- **10 min. Type and draw.** [[s02-A55]] Your child types or tells one sentence of the poem and draws its picture. You help place the picture on a page.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads grunt, plant and lift and prints "The sun sank on the hill."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the revision period for any child without a day 72 record, and the device turn. Longer: a child adds a second detail and reads the poem clearly.

### Words and supports

Words: revise, publish, book. Frames: "I add ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; a partner may type.

### Tomorrow

The device record tells you who typed, drew and placed a page. Day 74 hears each child read the poem and describe the scene. A child not finished at the device finishes in a 20-minute part, with one thing to enter and check.
