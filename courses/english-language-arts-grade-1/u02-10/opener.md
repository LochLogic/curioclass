# Section 2.10 opener: revising, publishing and the unit check

## What the section does

Days 72 to 75 collect the unit's evidence. Day 72 observes peer feedback, revision and sentence editing. Day 73 revises and publishes the class sun-poem book on paper and, for typing and drawing, on a shared device. Day 74 observes a clear reading with a drawing. Day 75 shares the book and responds to the end-of-unit cards. The book stays in class: no accounts, no public names, no photographs.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk and the work; the foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time. Days 72 and 74 are observation days, so no teaching enters them; day 75 ends with a repair and a fresh retry, marked on paper.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A peer's question asks for a detail.** The writer answers it and keeps the topic.
- **Four kinds of sentence, then longer.** Each is said simple and then joined with "and".
- **A caption has a name, a date and sentences that end.** Capitals open names and months; commas sit in a list and a date.
- **Story and information are marked differently.** A story has events and a problem; information explains how something works.

## The errors, in plain words

- **P, T:** a topic change, watching a device without entering.
- **A, B, F:** sequence errors, a topic taken for a message, a list for events.
- **C, H, Q, R, U, V, S:** feeling words, fragments, capitals, end marks, a month, commas.
- **D, E, G, O:** classifying by topic, an unsupported claim, an unhelpful drawing, decoration for a fact.
- **W to Z:** turn-taking, linking, asking and answering.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
