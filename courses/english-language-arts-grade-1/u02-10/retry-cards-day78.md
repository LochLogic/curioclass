# Day 78 retry cards (made up for practice)

Say each time: "These cards are made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Use only the card for the child's recorded gap; hear each child once.

**Card 78a, words.** chick, plank, flick, thrill, quilt. For children whose gap was a map or blend.

**Card 78b, endings.** dusted, melting, quacks, wetter, softest. For children whose gap was an ending.

**Card 78c, passage.** "Gus has a rug. Gus bangs it. Thud, thud, thud! A chick hops in the sand. The chick is not glad." Picture: a child with a rug and a chick. For children whose gap was fluency.

**Card 78d, information, read aloud once.** "A trowel is a small tool for digging. A trowel has a short handle and a pointed blade. A gardener holds the trowel and digs a small hole for a bulb." Picture: a trowel alone. For children whose gap was comprehension.

Repair moves, one minute each, taught before the retry: map and blend, cover the picture, tap each sound, then blend; endings, read the base, then add the ending; fluency, echo a line, then read it together; comprehension, hear it again and point to the picture that answers; spelling, one counter for each sound, then a letter for each.
