# Day 75 fresh retry card

Used in the last 15 minutes of the response allocation, on paper, after the five minutes of repair.

- **Visual retry (response 5).** Two pictures for a fresh line, one that helps a listener and one that does not. The child points and says how it helps.
- **Editing retry.** Fresh lines to fix, using a name, a month and an end mark.

Marks are written on the record sheet by the teacher: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Reasons are heard from a rotating six.
