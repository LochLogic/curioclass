# Day 78: Reteach from the checks and try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 77's assessment and day 76's assessment are not handed back as papers; use the marks. Sort the marks from day 76 and day 77 by the gap recorded for each child, as one list per gap: a map or blend, an ending, fluency, comprehension. Put at most ten children in a group. A child with no gap reads the known readers. Put the list of children whose evidence is missing at the front of your folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Set-up and records

Say: "Today you may try again. A try that is now right is marked shown with help. The first mark stays." Seat the groups. Mark every ask: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 40 min. Reteach groups

Each group comes to your table for 8 minutes in turn, five groups at most, and the rest work in pairs on known readers. One move for one gap, one minute: map and blend, cover the picture, tap each sound, then blend; endings, read the base, then add the ending; fluency, echo a line, then read it together; comprehension, hear it again and point to the picture that answers; spelling, one counter for each sound, then a letter for each. The other 7 minutes the group practises the move on the known words. Each group makes its turn count: one child speaks at a time, and a child that did not speak goes next.

#### 20 min. Fresh retry

Each group comes back for 4 minutes with its card from the retry card sheet and reads once each. Map or blend group: read the words ([[c1-X78a]]). Ending group: read the words ([[c1-X78b]]). Fluency group: read the passage ([[c1-X78c]]). Comprehension group: hear the text and answer aloud ([[c1-X78d]]). The comprehension group hears the card together once, then each child answers in one short phrase. For the others, hear one child at a time at 30 seconds each, at most six children a turn; the rest reread known readers. A child who cannot finish stops; the rest goes on the list of missing evidence. Mark each: shown with help or not yet seen; the first mark stays.

#### 25 min. Reread tools texts

Pairs and small groups reread On the Pond and I Look Out and the tools texts they know, then draw a tool and label one part. You walk and hear the children on the list of missing evidence for one minute each.

#### 20 min. Words and spelling

Small groups by gap, 4 minutes each in turn, five groups: sound-by-sound spelling with counters for two short words, then a letter for each; a cycle 11 word read twice. No new pattern. Everyone else prints the dated all-letter sheet, then rereads a known reader.

#### 10 min. Records

File the retry marks, the list of missing evidence and the check marks of both days in the unit 2 record, and note who needs the first weeks of the next unit's help. Put each child's one gap on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

One child: one retry card on the missed part, then stop. No printer: write the four cards by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No retry card: use a made-up short list of words.

**Skip.** The group moves.

- **10 min. Retry.** Show five new words such as chick, plank, flick, thrill and quilt, then dusted, melting, quacks, wetter and softest.
  **Say.** "Read each word." Expect: each word read by its sounds, with the ending read.
- **10 min. Read.** Read this once: "Gus has a rug. Gus bangs it. A chick hops in the sand." Your child reads it back.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X78a]] [[c1-X78b]] [[c1-X78c]] [[c1-X78d]] Use the card for the part your child missed. For the last, read aloud all three sentences of card 78d and ask "What does a trowel dig?" Expect: a small hole.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reteach and the retry. Longer: a child with no gap helps a partner read a known reader.

### Words and supports

Words: try, again, ending, point. Frame: "I try again." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count.
