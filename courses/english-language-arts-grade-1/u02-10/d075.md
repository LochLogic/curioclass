# Day 75: Share our book and show what I know

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep every chart down. Put each child's book page and kept notebook answer on their desk. Lay the tales, the star article, card N, the star log, the event pictures and the stickers out of sight. Day 74's marks are already on the record sheet. Copy the heard six. Number six groups of five children. Seat the scribes. Today observes first and repairs last: read each stem twice, give no hints, and teach nothing until the response part.

### The day

#### 4 min. Setup and read-aloud

Read pages 4 and 5 of the Efik and Ibibio tale aloud once, showing the pictures. Read page 4 of *The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd* once, then page 5 of *Stars in Daylight and Darkness*. Teach nothing.

#### 24 min. Oral groups

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each at your table; the heard six go first. In each group: one minute for the star page, two for the Efik and Ibibio tale, one for the feeling and touch words. Review: central message, and feeling word and sense word, from days 37 and 39. Reread the star page's last sentence. Say: "Why does the group of stars rise and set?" ([[s02-A59v2]]) Show the three event pictures out of order: the Sun and the Moon on the roof, the Water at the gate, the Sun and the Moon going up into the sky. Say: "Put the three pictures in order." ([[s02-A62v2]]) Then: "Tell the three events, and tell what the tale teaches." ([[s02-A63v2]]) Show page 4 of the Chinese tale and give a face sticker. Say: "Put the face on the feeling word." ([[s02-A64v2]]) Then: "Say the feeling word." ([[s02-A64bv2]]) Show page 4 of the Efik and Ibibio tale and give a hand sticker. Say: "Put the hand on the word that tells what you could feel by touch, and say it." ([[s02-A65v2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The other children sit at their tables and quietly reread their kept notebook answer. These parts, to the end of the RL.1.5 part, are the observation and fill the first 40 topic minutes.

#### 7 min. W.1.8

Review: a source label says where a fact came from (day 68). Each child reads the kept two-source notebook answer and points to its two source labels. Say: "How can we answer our question with the article and the log?" ([[s02-A61]]) A child with no answer on file uses card N and the star log: "Our first night drawing shows the stars seen with eyes alone. Our telescope drawing adds faint stars between them." Read card N and the log aloud once and show its two drawings. Walk round and mark. A child with no source-labelled note in the group answer (W.1.7) adds one now, then says: "Show me the note you added to the group's answer, with its source label." ([[s02-A60]]) W.1.7 is cited from the day 61 record, not repeated.

#### 5 min. RL.1.5

Review: a tale and information, in two columns (day 35). Give each child a story mark and an information mark. Lay the tale's page 5 beside the star article's page 5. Say: "Mark the story picture for the tale and the information picture for the star page." ([[s02-A66v2]]) Then: "Tell why, drawing on our earlier tales, poem and articles." ([[s02-A67v2]]) Children answer with a word label (problem, ending, facts) or to a scribe; hear a rotating six aloud.

#### 20 min. Share the sun-poem book

Two rounds of 10 minutes, new groups of five each round. Each child shows or reads a page from the book to the group (about a minute); then one listener says one thing they noticed. Mark the reading: "Read your page to the group." ([[s02-A68]]) Five children by 2 minutes is 10 minutes. The book stays in class: no accounts, no public names, no photographs.

#### 10 min. Reread

Review: echo and choral reading (days 5 and 63). Read Quiet Sky, Sun's Day and Moon's Walk aloud once each, then the class joins with expression, about three minutes each. Read pages 1 and 2 of Sun Across the Sky aloud once.

#### 25 min. Familiar reading

Pairs read On the Pond and I Look Out, a page each, then swap. Hear the heard six, 3 minutes each, as they read a page. No new sample and no foundations teaching today.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the marks and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet. Cite the cycle records of days 38 to 68. The cumulative reading and spelling record is added after days 76 and 77.

#### 20 min. Response allocation

Five minutes of repair, then 15 of fresh retry. The repair takes the gaps sent on from day 69 (errors A and C), day 71 (error F), day 73 (error P) and day 72 (errors H and Q). Each child works in one group by the gap you recorded; give each group about 75 seconds. Every example is made up for practice and is not an assessed card.

- **Sequence (errors A, B, F).** Read "First Ana put on her coat. Then she went out in the snow." Show two pictures, the coat and the snow. **Ask.** "Which goes first?" Expect: the coat. Place them under First and Then. A child who listed facts draws First, Next and At last in three boxes.
- **Language (errors C, H, and the conventions).** Read "A pup ran to the pond. The pup felt happy." Show a happy face and a pond. **Ask.** "Which is the feeling?" Expect: the happy face; mark the word. Say "pup run", then "The pup runs." and ask for a whole sentence. Write kim and Kim; point to the capital.
- **Source connection (errors D, E, G, O).** Read "A shell is hard." A friend says a shell is pink. **Ask.** "Does the line say pink?" Expect: no, it says hard. Point to the purpose of two cards: one tells facts, one tells a story.
- **Revision (errors P, T, W to Z).** Read "A cat sat on a mat." A friend asks, "What color was the mat?" Show a blue mat and a cat eating lunch. **Ask.** "Which card answers the friend?" Expect: the blue mat. A child who only watched enters one thing.

Then the fresh retry, one prompt each, one ask at a time, marks on paper, reasons from a rotating six. Read the fresh card: "First Moon saw a child open the gate. Then the child carried a red basket inside. Last the gate shut, and the path was quiet. Moon felt calm." and "The moon can sometimes be seen in daylight." Show gate open, basket carried, gate shut and a daytime moon.

1. Sequence: "Which pictures go in order?" then "What could the story teach?" Expect: open, carry, shut; finishing a job brings quiet. A child who listed facts draws two events and an ending.
2. Feeling: "Which picture shows calm?" then "Which word tells how Moon felt?" Expect: the calm face; calm, quiet.
3. Source: "Which picture fits the fact?" then "Say one whole sentence that tells the fact." Expect: the daytime moon; the moon can be seen in daylight.
4. Revision: "Which event picture needs a detail?" then "What did the child carry?" Expect: the carrying picture; a red basket, events unchanged. A child marked error T enters one sentence in the text editor and one picture in the drawing tool, or on paper without a device.
5. Visual: show two pictures, the child carrying a basket through the gate and a border of flowers. "Which helps a listener?" then "How does it help?" Expect: the first; it shows what was carried.
6. Discussion, errors W to Z: with a peer and an adult, "Which words should our note use?" then "What needs clarifying?" Hear one linked turn and one question.

Editing retry for errors Q to V: print Eli's lines without end marks: "eli, april 6 2026"; "I drew a moon"; "Did you draw stars"; "Look at this picture"; "My list: moon stars sun". Say: "Edit the part you missed." Use only the missed component. The fresh card is used only after the repair, never on the item just taught.

### Other rooms

No device: a child marked error T draws one contribution and places it on a paper page. Cards are read from the page.

### At home

**Swap.** No stickers: circle with a pencil.

**Skip.** The reading pairs.

- **20 min. The assessment.** [[s02-A66v2]] Read your child page 5 of the Efik and Ibibio tale and page 5 of the star article, Stars in Daylight and Darkness. Your child marks the story and the information. **Say.** "Tell why." Expect: the tale has a problem and an ending; the star page tells facts.
- **10 min. Book.** Your child reads or shows you the book page and says one thing they noticed.
- **5 min. Poems.** Reread Quiet Sky together, with expression.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the oral groups, the book share and the response. Longer: a child tells a second reason for the story mark.

### Words and supports

Words: story, information, feeling word. Pointing, drawing, a scribe, stickers and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 hears cumulative reading and spelling once, with new passages and the recent cycle records. Nothing from today is given back; the marks go to the unit 2 record.
