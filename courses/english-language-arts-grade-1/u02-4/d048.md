# Day 48: Feeling words, touch words and facts

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make a face and hand sticker pair for each child. Write five groups of six and six groups of five on the record sheet. Day 47's assessment marks stay there for day 49. Give no model and no chart.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read

Say: "I will not help." Read, in large print, page 8 of *The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd*, page 1 of *Sun's Day* and page 4 of *The Changing Moon*, with its picture. Hand out the stickers.

#### 20 min. Check: feeling and touch words

Review: a face goes on a word that tells how someone feels, a hand on a word you can touch (day 39). Five groups of six, 4 minutes each, the two large-print pages in view. Each child puts the face on page 8's feeling word ([[s02-A9v2]]) and says it ([[s02-A9bv2]]); puts the hand on the touch word of Sun's Day and says it ([[s02-A10v2]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The rest draw the moon at night.

#### 25 min. Check: story and facts

Review: you sorted tales and facts on days 35 and 44. Six groups of five, 4 minutes each, both pages in view. Each child points to the page that gives facts ([[s02-A11v2]]), then says or points to how they know ([[s02-A12v2]]). Mark as before.

#### 5 min. Collect and compare

Collect the drawings and stickers.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Not scored. Blend /w/ /i/ /n/, "win". Segment "tent" and "drum" into four sounds. Isolate the middle sound of tent.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Read runs, rubs and wins. **Ask.** "What does the s change in run and runs?" Expect: one person does it now. Spell runs on sound boxes. Pairs reread Dad's Plan.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictation: say "Jen rubs the wet pan." Children print a word at a time; the is named aloud. Each child points to the capital and the period.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each; today's heard six go first. The cycle 7 sample is taken at your table, one minute a child: show the card hops, "Read this word." ([[s02-A13]]). Remove it. Say "Write the word I say: jogs." once in the sentence "Dad jogs." and once alone ([[s02-A14]]). Mark each. Stations: reread with a partner; chain run, rub, rug and rig; spell wins.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Write the marks and the sample on the record sheet with the heard-six column. Reading hop for hops is error L.

### Other rooms

No printer: show the pages from the book or a screen. One child: the four asks, then the sample.

### At home

**Swap.** No stickers: draw a small face and a small hand.

**Skip.** The sounds.

- **15 min. Assessment: two pages.** Read page 8 of The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, with no help. **Say.** "Which word tells how Zhinu and Niulang feel?" Expect: glad. Read Sun's Day, page 1. **Say.** "Which word tells what you could feel by touch?" Expect: warm.
- **15 min. Which gives facts?** Read The Changing Moon, page 4. **Say.** "Which page gives facts, this or the tale's page 8, and how do you know?" Expect: the moon page; it tells what is true about the moon's light.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the moon at night.
- **5 min. Sounds.** Your child reads runs and prints "Jen rubs the wet pan."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear [[s02-A9v2]] and [[s02-A11v2]] only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: glad, warm, glows, facts. Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 returns to the moves on fresh cards. Use days 47 and 48: errors A, B, C and D go to its Return and repair, and a dropped s (error L) to its rotation.
