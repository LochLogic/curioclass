# Day 47: Retell a new tale

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut card T's three event pictures apart for each group of five. Write six groups of five on the record sheet. Give no model, hint or story map. Read the student page aloud.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read and set up

Say: "Today shows what you can do. I will not help. This card is made up for practice." Say: "A lantern is a light you carry. Mist is thin cloud near the ground." Read card T twice, showing each picture. Hand out the pages.

#### 45 min. Check: retell the tale

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each at your table, one minute a child. Each child: puts the three pictures in order ([[s02-A6]]); tells what happens, first, next and last ([[s02-A7]]); points to what the story teaches from three cards you read aloud ([[s02-A8]]). Keep card T in view. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen; help is the item's own words said again. The rest draw the night sky, then look at Dad's Plan quietly. The last 15 minutes: absent children and any child who passed take the same asks.

#### 5 min. Collect and compare

Collect the drawings. Note which item most children found hard.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Not scored. Blend /p/ /e/ /t/, "pet". Segment "step" into four sounds. Say my, fly and sky; listen for the long i.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Review: you read gets, sets and fixing on day 44; -s tells what one person does now. Read pets, tugs, mops and mixing from the cards. Spell pets and tugs on sound boxes. Pairs reread Dad's Plan.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dictation: say "Meg tugs the red mop." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Each child points to the capital and the period.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each; today's heard six go first. At your table: reread Dad's Plan aloud, then spell mops. Stations: reread with a partner; chain pet, pen, pin and pig; spell tugs and my.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Write the marks on the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column. Name day 48's groups.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw card T's pictures on scrap. One child: do the three asks one at a time.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw three small pictures.

**Skip.** The sounds.

- **15 min. Assessment: a new card.** Read twice, with no help: "Lamb lost the way in the fog. Owl hooted from a tree. Lamb followed the hoot and got home."
  **Say.** "Tell it: first, next, last." Expect: Lamb is lost, Owl hoots, Lamb follows the hoot home.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the three events in order.
- **10 min. Teaches.** Read "Fog." "Owl calls so Lamb can find home." "Owls sleep." **Say.** "Which one is what it teaches?" Expect: the second.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads pets and tugs and prints "Meg tugs the red mop."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear [[s02-A6]] and [[s02-A8]] only. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: lantern, mist, path. Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 checks feeling words, touch words and a tale against facts, cold; card I is new. Day 47's assessment marks for order (error A) and the lesson (error B) go to day 49, with day 46's exit marks for error B.
