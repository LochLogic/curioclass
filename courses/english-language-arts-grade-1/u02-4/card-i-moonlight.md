# Card I: Moonlight (day 48)

Read aloud once on day 48 with card T beside it. For items s02-A11 and s02-A12. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading.

Print in a column: "The moon does not make its own light. Sunlight shines on it. We see the bright part that faces us."

Pictures: the sun shining on a round moon; a child looking up at a half moon from a window.

The facts rest on the NASA moon pages (read 2026-11-20): the moon does not make its own light, moonlight is reflected sunlight, and the sun always lights half the moon while we see part of the lit half.
