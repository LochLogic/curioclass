# Day 49: Try the tale moves again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 47's assessment and day 48's assessment marks by error letter: A, B, C, D and L. Day 46's exit marks for error B join B. Write the repair card: "Ant dropped a crumb in the stream. Ant felt worried. Beetle pushed a smooth leaf to the crumb. Ant rode the leaf and got the crumb." with three pictures. Write the fact card: "An ant is an insect. An ant has six legs." Fold the maps.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the Efik and Ibibio and Chinese tales

Whole class on the rug. Say aloud: "This is a story the Efik and Ibibio people of southern Nigeria tell." Reread pages 4 and 5 of that tale, showing each picture (5 minutes). Say aloud: "This is a traditional Chinese tale." Reread pages 5, 6 and 7 (6 minutes). Review: you heard both tales on days 35 to 46; a bridge is a path over a gap. Partners lay the strip cards on their folded maps (4 minutes). Neither tale is acted out.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Give each child one spoken note; no scores. Begin with the miss most children made (4 minutes) on the repair card Ant's Crumb; the rest of the class draws their own three pictures of it. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Then three small groups of up to eight, 4 minutes each, from the other letters; use the last 4 minutes for second tries.

Error A, order. Read the card, then lay the pictures pushed, dropped, rode. **Ask.** "Which came first?" Expect: dropped. Lay first, then, last words over the pictures and tell it.

Error B, a topic for a lesson (with day 46's exit marks). **Ask.** "What did Beetle's push make happen?" Expect: Ant got the crumb. **Ask.** "What does the story teach?" Expect: a friend can help fix a problem. Say: "Ant is who it is about. A lesson says what an event shows."

Error C, a feeling word. Put the face on worried and the hand on smooth leaf; the chart stays in view. Read the card again; children put the face and hand on a fresh card.

Error D, a tale called facts. Read the repair card and the fact card. **Ask.** "What does Ant do in the story?" Expect: rode the leaf. Point to the story card. **Ask.** "What is a fact about ants?" Expect: an ant has six legs. Point to the fact card.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, pass allowed, about one minute a child. **Ask.** "What does the lesson help someone do?" Starter: "The lesson helps you ___." Expect: any lesson an event of the tale supports, such as say yes slowly when you hear a warning, or friends can help you meet. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 10 min. Retry the move

Partners retell one tale, one turn each, first, next, last with the folded map, about one minute a child (4 minutes). Each child draws first, next and last in three boxes and labels the lesson (6 minutes). While they draw, hear today's six, one minute each: events in order; a lesson one event supports; the character named. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 3 of the Chinese tale aloud. Show three strips of three pictures: talking and laughing, coming down, building a home; coming down, talking and laughing, building a home; building a home, coming down, talking and laughing. Say: "Point to the strip with the page in order." Every child points; the rotating six also tell it ([[s02-X49v2]]). A good answer is the second strip. The first and third strips are error A: lay first, then, last words along the strip. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Move one counter a sound: "stop" has four, "sop" has three; "flag" and "grab" have four; "slept" has five. If many count a cluster as one sound (error I), repeat with counters in the rotation. Say "when" and listen for the first sound, /w/. Hear the end sound /t/ in helped, jumped and asked; no -ed word is read yet.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review: you met sh, th and ch as two letters for one sound; wh is the same kind (2 minutes). Map when and whiz, read them from the cards. New on the map: c and k together say /k/ after a short vowel at the end of a word (2 minutes). Map back, pack and duck, read them. Spell sack and when on sound boxes (3 minutes). Review: you mapped said and see on day 39; point to the part to remember (3 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: collecting finished on day 40. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Jack has a duck." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word has is named aloud. Watch the forms J, c, k and d. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table: first a child who dropped the s on day 48 reads a fresh word card, the base, adds the ending, then rereads: pins (3 minutes); then the group rereads Dad's Plan aloud and spells back on sound boxes. The other children rotate through stations: reread Dad's Plan with a partner; chain back, pack, pick and pin with letter cards; spell duck and when on sound boxes. No child waits more than one rotation without a station.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the retry marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 50.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips on scrap paper and fold a sheet in three for each map. One child: reread both tales, do the repair for your child's own miss, retell one tale with the map, draw the three events, answer the exit by pointing, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No map: fold a page in three.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Retell.** Your child tells one of the two tales from the pictures, first, next, last.
  **Say.** "What is the lesson, and which event shows it?" Expect: friends can help you meet, so the magpies made a bridge.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws first, next and last in three boxes.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads back and duck and prints "Jack has a duck."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X49v2]] You read page 3 of The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd aloud. Your child tells it in order, first, next, last.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to one pair each. Longer: a child who finishes retells the second tale and says a different lesson.

### Words and supports

Words: retell, lesson, bridge, first, next, last, worried. Frames: "First ___, next ___, last ___." "The lesson is ___, so ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 opens the sun research question. Its first four minutes repair the strip order from today's exit (error A) for the children who need it: reread the time words and place the pictures.
