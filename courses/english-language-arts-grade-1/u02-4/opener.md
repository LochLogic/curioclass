# Section 2.4 opener: the mid-unit check and the response day

## What the section does

Days 47 and 48 are check days. On day 47 each group of five hears a new tale and retells it in order with its message. On day 48 children mark a feeling word and a touch word, and tell which of two cards gives facts. Nothing is taught during a check, and no map is given. Day 49 returns to the moves with a fresh card, and you use the check records as your list.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The cards are new.** Never read a taught text.
- **Help is recorded.** A prompt from the item makes the mark "shown with help".
- **Day 49 retries the move, not the card.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, E:** see section 2.1.
- **C, D:** see sections 2.2 and 2.1.
- **K, L:** the cycle 7 sample. Isolate the vowel, or read the base and add the ending.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
