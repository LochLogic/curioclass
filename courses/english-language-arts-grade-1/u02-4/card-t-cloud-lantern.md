# Card T: The Cloud's Lantern (days 47 and 48)

Read aloud twice on day 47 with the pictures in view, and again on day 48. For items s02-A6 to s02-A10. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading.

Print in a column: "Cloud kept a lantern under a leaf. 'It is all mine,' Cloud said. Soon cold mist hid the path. Cloud felt lonely and could not find the way home. Moon called, 'Lift your light, and we can both see.' Cloud lifted the lantern. Together they followed the lit path home. After that, Cloud shared the light."

Pictures, one beside each sentence group: a cloud hiding a small lantern under a leaf; a misty path with a cloud looking sad; the moon calling from above; the cloud holding the lantern up; the two travelers walking home on a lit path.

For day 47 cut the three event pictures apart: the lantern hidden under a leaf, the lantern raised, and the two travelers going home. Hand each group of five one set.
