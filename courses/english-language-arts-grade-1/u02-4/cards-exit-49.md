# Exit card, day 49

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 49 card (s02-X49, RL.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Wind found a lost hat by the swing. Wind lifted it to Girl. Girl waved and put it on." Show three strips of three pictures: lifted, found, waved; found, lifted, waved; waved, found, lifted. Say: "Point to the strip with the story in order." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The second strip: found, lifted, waved.
