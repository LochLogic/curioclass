# Exit pictures, day 49

Each exit reads a named passage of the unit's texts aloud; this page holds only the pictures and cards to show. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 49 exit pictures (s02-X49v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: The Weaver Girl and the Cowherd, page 3 (Zhinu comes down, they meet, they build a home); day 49 rereads pages 5 to 7. Reread page 3 of the Chinese tale aloud. Show three strips: talking and laughing, coming down, building a home; coming down, talking and laughing, building a home; building a home, coming down, talking and laughing. Say: "Point to the strip with the page in order." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The second strip: coming down, talking and laughing, building a home.
