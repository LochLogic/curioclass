# Day 63: Tell what happens in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the narrative-arc chart: four picture slots headed First, Next, Then and At last, and a wider slot at the end. Make fifteen sets of four event pictures and four time-word cards. Seal the cycle sample cards for two words in an envelope. Pick the day's heard six and the five foundations groups. From the day 62 grid, note who marked error Q (a name with no capital) and error R (no end mark).

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Run one branch, the lapse the day 62 exit marks show most (s02-X62e: a drawing that does not help a listener; draw one arrow and say it). For error E (a claim the line does not make): say "This is made up for practice." Read "A snail crawls up the wall." Say "The snail crawls up the wall." and "Snails are slow." **Ask.** "Which says only what the line says?" Expect: the first. For error H (a fragment): show "Snail crawls" beside "A snail crawls up the wall." **Ask.** "Which tells who or what and what happens?" Expect: the second. Errors Q and R wait for the rotation.

#### 15 min. Read Sun's Day

Say each word with its picture and have the class say it back: wake, climb, slide, sink, hum. Then read Sun's Day, whole (g1-u2-02), pausing after pages 2 and 3 to show the pictures. The children listen; they do not read it. Say: "The sun in this poem talks. A real sun cannot talk. That part is imagined." Then read the sun facts under it from Sun Across the Sky (g1-u2-01): the sun rises, climbs, sinks and sets. Say: "Those are facts." Read the poem once more, slowly. Review: in kindergarten and on day 51 you used First, Next, Then and Last for the order of the sun's day. Today's poem uses one more, At last. Say: "A poem that tells what happened, in order, is a narrative poem."

#### 10 min. Reread and question

Reread the poem a page at a time; the class echoes each line after you, as in the hammer poem reading (review of echo and choral reading from unit 1). **Ask.** "Which events happen?" Expect: the sun wakes and climbs, is high, slides down, sinks. **Ask.** "Which words tell the order?" Expect: First, Next, Then, At last. Partners point to those four words on the large-print page; they read only these four labels. Walk round; fifteen pairs, one look each.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: a feeling word and a sense word, from day 39. Hold up the face (a feeling) and the ear, eye and hand (what we hear, see, feel). Pip says one line: "The sun hums a quiet song. I hear that." **Ask.** "What does the sun see, hear or feel?" Partners use the turn card (review from kindergarten), point to a card and say one sentence, then swap (2 minutes). Hear the heard six alone, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes. Expect: sees children play; hears birds fly home or its own song; feels warm. Share three ideas with the class (4 minutes). Any child may pass.

#### 15 min. Order the events

Review: the story map from day 36 put a tale's first events in order. Today's arc chart does the same for a poem and adds an ending. Each pair has four event pictures (wake, climb, slide, sink; the card named climb shows the sun high and bright) and four time-word cards. Pairs set the pictures in order under the time words (6 minutes). Walk round and mark each pair: shown, shown with help, not yet seen (one minute each, 8 minutes; a pair that needs help rereads its page aloud with you). Then fill the ending slot with the class: "Good night" and the feeling, a sleepy face. **Ask.** "How does the poem come to rest?" Expect: the sun says good night and will come back. Say: "An ending says how a poem comes to rest." Cards are made up for practice; say so aloud.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read aloud the card [[s02-X63]], which is invented for practice. Show the two strips at the front, in either order. Say the stem once. Every child circles 1 or 2 on the student page. Hear the heard six: ask "Which words tell the order?" Expect: First, Next, At last. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say slim and slime; **Ask.** "Which word has the sound of the letter i's name?" Expect: slime. Blend /s/ /l/ /i/ /d/, "slid". Say "sled": first sound /s/, middle sound /e/, last sound /d/. Segment "slip" with counters: /s/ /l/ /i/ /p/, four sounds.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class, sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Cycle 10, lesson 55: review, no new pattern. Teach first (4 minutes): slide two counters for the blend in slim, then add the letters; show -es on wish and kiss; show the doubled last letter before -er and -est on big and slim. Then a word chain with the cards (6 minutes): slim, slimmer, big, bigger, biggest, wish, wishes, kiss, kisses, split, strip. Read each, then spell the odd ones on sound boxes. Fresh words for the progress sample stay sealed.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (7 minutes): say "The frog sits on a slim log." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Then each child prints their first name under the sentence with its capital (3 minutes). Watch the T and the capital in the name.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. Review: the cycle progress sample, as on day 58. At the table, first (3 minutes): the sample. Each child reads one word card from the envelope (10 seconds each), then you remove the card and say the second sample word once in a sentence and once alone; all six write it at the same time on their sheet while you watch each writer ([[s02-A30]], [[s02-A31]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Then the day 62 repairs, 3 minutes: use the one this group's marks need. Error Q: find the name in "ana saw a star", circle its first letter, print the capital (s02-X62c). Error R: point where the voice stops and add the mark to "Ana saw a star" (s02-X62d). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread On the Pond with a partner; chain slim, slimmer and wishes with letter cards; spell split and kisses on sound boxes; read the word cards a, and, in and the to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the sample marks and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet. Name the six who read aloud first tomorrow.

### Other rooms

No printer: fold a paper strip into five for the arc chart, draw the four pictures, and draw the two exit strips on the board. One child: sort the four pictures, say the order with the time words, point to the strip, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: draw four small suns on paper.

**Skip.** The pairs, the rotation and the sample words.

- **10 min. Poem.** Read Sun's Day aloud from the page. **Say.** "Which words tell the order?" Expect: First, Next, Then, At last.
- **10 min. Order.** Your child draws four suns in order and writes the four time words beside them.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads wish, wishes, slim and slimmer, and prints "The frog sits on a slim log."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X63]] You read: "First a duck waddled to the pond. Next the duck splashed. At last the duck dried off." Draw two strips of three pictures: waddle, splash, dry; and dry, splash, waddle. Ask: "Point to the strip that follows the poem." Expect: waddle, splash, dry.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the reading, the ordering, the exit, the sample and the dictation; shorten the talk to the heard six. Longer: a pair that finishes tells the poem again without the cards, using the four time words.

### Words and supports

Words: narrative poem, imagined, First, Next, Then, At last, ending. Frames: "First ___. Next ___." "The sun sees ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Any child may pass. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who puts events in order (error A: reversed order). Day 64 opens with a repair for error A: reread the time words and place two picture cards. The sample tells you who drops an ending (error L: "box" for "boxes") and who leaves out a vowel (error M: "fster"); the day 64 rotation gives each group 3 minutes on the one it needs. Day 64 reads the moon's poem and asks what the imagined moon might see.
