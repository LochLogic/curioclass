# Day 66 eight-box sentence sheet

A sheet of two rows of four boxes, picture side up. Row one: a simple statement, a simple question, a simple command, a simple exclamation. Row two: the same four, each joined with "and".

**Picture for day 66.** A pail by a gate and two goats in a field. Each child says or points to one box at a time; the teacher scribes; the answer is not corrected on the sheet.

Day 72 uses the same sheet with a fresh picture. Keep the day 66 picture off the day 72 sheet. In a room with nothing, fold a page into eight boxes.
