# Exit cards, days 63 to 67

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card still marked "invented for practice" below; the exit pictures read a named passage and carry no foot line. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud. Where a line says "heard in the day's rotating six", no card is printed. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed".

## Day 63 exit pictures (s02-X63v2, RL.1.2)

Passage: Quiet Sky, pages 1 and 2; day 63 works Sun's Day. Reread Quiet Sky, pages 1 and 2, aloud. Show two strips of three pictures: the sun gone down, the first star, the moon over the hill; and the moon over the hill, the first star, the sun gone down. Say: "Point to the strip that follows the poem." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The strip sun gone down, first star, moon over the hill.

## Day 64 exit pictures (s02-X64av2, RL.1.5)

Passage: Quiet Sky, page 2, lines 3 and 4 ("The moon peeks over the hill"); day 64 works Moon's Walk and The Changing Moon, page 2. Reread two lines of Quiet Sky, page 2: "The moon peeks over the hill, a pale and gentle ring." Show two pictures: a moon with eyes peeking over a hill, and a plain moon above a hill. Say: "Point to the picture that is imagined, not real." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The moon with eyes peeking over the hill.

## Day 64 card (s02-X64b, SL.1.6)

Heard in the day's rotating six. Show a picture of a child hanging a shirt on a line under the moon. Say: "Say one finished sentence about what the imagined moon sees." 

**Right answer:** A finished sentence such as "The moon sees a child hanging a shirt on a line."

## Day 65 card (s02-X65a, W.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud two cards. Card A: "First a bee flew to a red flower. Next the bee sipped from the flower. At last the bee flew home." Card B: "A bee is yellow. A bee is small. A bee is soft." Say: "Point to the card that tells what happened, in order." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** Card A.

## Day 65 card (s02-X65b, L.1.1j)

Heard in the day's rotating six. Say: "Here is a sentence: A bee flew. Say it again with one more detail." 

**Right answer:** A finished sentence with a detail, such as "A fuzzy bee flew to the flower."

## Day 66 card (s02-X66a, W.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud the notes: "In the evening a lamp is lit. A child comes out to sit. The child goes back in." Show two strips: sit, then in; and in, then sit. Say: "Point to the strip that guides the plan." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The strip sit, then in.

## Day 66 card (s02-X66b, W.1.3)

Heard in the day's rotating six, with the notes from the last card in view. Say: "Say the first event and the next, with a time word." 

**Right answer:** Any two events of the notes, in order, joined by time words, such as "First the child came out to sit. Next the child went in."

## Day 67 card (s02-X67a, W.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud the shared draft: "Moon saw a girl. Then the girl went to bed." Read the source note: "A girl swept the porch." Say: "Give the writer one kind, specific note about the draft." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** A kind note on the same topic, such as "I like that Moon saw a girl. What was the girl doing before bed?"

## Day 67 card (s02-X67b, W.1.5)

Heard in the day's rotating six, with the draft, the peer question and the source note in view. Say: "Say the detail the writer adds." 

**Right answer:** A sentence that adds the swept porch, such as "The girl swept the porch before bed."
