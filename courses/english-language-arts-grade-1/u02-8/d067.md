# Day 67: Give a kind note and fix our moon poem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Type the class moon poem from the day 66 chart into the shared file on the device, one line to a row, with space under each event. Open the drawing tool on a blank page. Write three peer questions on cards, one about each event the class chose, for example "What was by the barn?". Group the children in six groups of five and note which group goes to the device when. Copy the heard six from the day 66 column.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

From day 66's exit, two branches. First, error A (reversed order), 3 minutes: say "These cards are made up for practice." Reread the time words on the arc chart. Read "First the bread cooled. Next we ate it." Show two picture cards, the bread cooling and the plate. **Ask.** "Which picture goes first?" Expect: the bread. Children place them under First and Next. Second, error F (a list instead of events), 2 minutes: say "lamp, step" and then "First a bell rang. Next the school door opened." **Ask.** "Which tells what happened?" Expect: the second. Children draw First and Next in two squares and add an ending square.

#### 10 min. Read and feedback

Reread the class moon poem aloud (2 minutes), the children joining. Point to the writing-process chart: the class is at share, then revise. Review: a kind, specific, helpful note asks for a detail on the same topic, from day 65. Read one peer question card aloud, then the next. Model one note (2 minutes): think aloud, "Is it kind? Is it about one part of the poem? Does it ask for a detail on the moon?", then say, "I like the line where the moon rises. What color was the sky?" Partners choose one question and give one kind, specific note for the poem, in their own words (2 minutes). **Ask.** "Which note helps the writer, and why?" Expect: the one that asks for a detail about the poem's moon or its events. A note about lunch changes the topic (error P). Hear three pairs. Pip says one thing to add. Any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Revise on paper

Review: in kindergarten you answered a partner's question by adding one detail. Model the revision (1 minute): read your note's question, say the detail that answers it, "The sky was dark blue," and draw it beside its line. Each child picks one line of the poem and a question (a card or a partner's note), says the detail aloud to a partner, then draws it on a page and writes at most a label (error P: a different topic; the detail must answer the question). Walk round. Hear the heard six say their detail alone, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes; write what each says on the page for a child who cannot yet write it. A child who needs more time keeps the page for the device part.

#### 30 min. Shared device

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each at the device. The text editor first: each child dictates or types the sentence from their page, about 30 seconds, and you read it back. Then the drawing tool: each child makes or changes one picture, about 30 seconds, while the child before them helps place it. Each child takes both tools; the record shows each separately. A child who only watches (error T) is given one thing to enter and check; note it on the record. Keep the file private to the room. The other four groups work at their tables: finish the page, reread the poem with a partner, and match a detail to its line. Count: 5 children by 1 minute is 5 minutes.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the draft and the note aloud, invented for practice, and ask [[s02-X67a]]: every child gives a neighbor one kind, specific note. Hear the heard six give theirs, then ask them [[s02-X67b]], with the draft, a peer question and the note in view. Mark each: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A note about something else, such as lunch, is error P.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken, not scored. Say rod and rode; **Ask.** "Which word has the sound of the letter o's name?" Expect: rode. Blend /k/ /l/ /ow/ /d/, "cloud". Say "king": first sound /k/, middle sound /i/, last sound /ng/. Segment "think" with counters: /th/ /i/ /n/ /k/, four sounds.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 11, lesson 59. Review final clusters, -ed said /id/ and ou. Chain with the cards: planted, melted, sink, sing, bank, found, lost, hunt (4 minutes). Pairs reread I Look Out for meaning and expression: you read a page, the pair echoes it, then each reads a page smoothly (6 minutes). The class says what the child sees and feels.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27. Dictation (8 minutes): "Dad found a lost bag." Children print a word at a time with the map; the capital D is named aloud. Then each child prints their first name with its capital (2 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. Each child rereads yesterday's sentence for 20 seconds as the revised reading (2 minutes). Then the group reads I Look Out, a page each (2 minutes), and spells found and lost on sound boxes (2 minutes). The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread I Look Out with a partner; chain planted, sink and bank with letter cards; spell hunt and sing on sound boxes; read the word cards look, see, a and the to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the device record and the heard-six column to the unit 2 record sheet. Save the shared file and the pages for day 68.

### Other rooms

No device: use the large paper copy; children draw and paste their pictures, and the device entries are recorded as not observed. One child: say the detail, draw it, then dictate it to you as the entry, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: write the sentence on paper and draw the picture.

**Skip.** The pairs, the device rounds and the rotation.

- **10 min. Poem.** Read a short poem you and your child made, or Moon's Walk, aloud. Your child asks you one kind question about it. **Say.** "What do you want to know?" Expect: a detail on the same topic.
- **10 min. Add.** Your child adds one detail by saying it and drawing it.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads lost, hunt, sing and found and prints "Dad found a lost bag."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X67a]] You read the draft: "A boy saw a star. Then the boy went in." The note says "A boy lit a candle." Ask: "Give the writer one kind, specific note." Expect: a kind note that asks what the boy was doing. [[s02-X67b]] Ask: "Say the detail the writer adds." Expect: a sentence about the candle.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the peer notes, the paper revision, the exit and the device turns for one tool; cut the second tool turn only when no device is free. Longer: a child who finishes adds a second detail for another line.

### Words and supports

Words: feedback, kind, specific, helpful, revise, detail, share, device. Frames: "I would like to know ___." "I can add ___." "The note helps because ___." Pointing, drawing, dictating and a home language count as answers. Any child may pass. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who adds a detail that answers the question (error P: a different topic). Day 68 opens with a repair for error P: reread the peer question and add only its requested detail. Day 68 returns to the sun facts and chooses two or more events for a sun poem.
