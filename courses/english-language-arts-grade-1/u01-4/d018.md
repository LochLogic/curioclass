# Day 18: Tell the whole story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures and a story-map strip for each child. Lay out a turn card and a strip for each pair, the picture cards for the exit, the chain cards, the reader Pat's Map, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 17's exit marks give the opening repair (error A). Today applies days 10 to 17; nothing is new.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread pages 1 to 12 quickly

Whole class on the rug. Review: the people in a story are its characters; the place and time is its setting; the big things that happen, in order, are its events (day 10 and day 12). Read the whole story in order with each picture shown (6 minutes). Pause three times, one ask each, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds:

- After page 2. **Ask.** "Where do Kofi and Gran work?" Expect: in Gran's shed, because of the rain.
- After page 7. **Ask.** "What has happened so far?" Expect: Kofi tried his hands, the screwdriver slipped, Gran used the wrench and the seat is still.
- After page 12. **Ask.** "What is different at the end?" Expect: the bicycle works, and Kofi does the work himself.

Now show how three big events can tell the whole story, with three picture cards from the story and a hand on each (3 minutes): first Gran fixes the seat with the wrench, next Kofi fixes the bell with the little screwdriver, last Kofi fixes the strap by himself. The class says it back with you. Pairs tell it with the cards and a counter on each (4 minutes). Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Prepare

First 4 minutes: the repair for day 17's exit (error A). Children who said something the card did not say sit with you. Say a sentence about the story: "Gran uses a hammer on the bolt." **Ask.** "Which sentence says so?" They look; there is none. Then the sentence that does say it: "Gran picks a wrench." Say a second one: "Kofi pulls the strap tight." They find page 11: "I loop the strap around the handlebar and pull it tight." A good answer points to the sentence before it says the sentence. If many children made this error, run it with the whole class.

The others work in pairs, 6 minutes, with the story-map strip. Each pair chooses one character, the place and three events, puts a counter in each place on the strip, and says them to each other. Do not tell them which. Sit with three pairs. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Talk

Three asks, one at a time, each answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds. **Ask.** "Who is in the story?" Expect: Kofi and Gran, and a cat. **Ask.** "Where does it happen?" Expect: Gran's shed. **Ask.** "What happens?" Expect: the three events, with first, next and last. Then pairs, turn card, 3 minutes, one ask each in turn. Hear today's six pairs, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). In the last minute pairs say the three events once more.

#### 20 min. Draw the description

Each child uses the story-map strip. Box 1: draw one character. Box 2: draw the place. Box 3: draw the three events in order, and print a word label for each box (11 minutes). Pairs say it to each other (3 minutes). Hear today's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes): the child says it, and you write one spoken sentence under the strip. The others add color. The page stays in the child's folder.

Look-fors for the six: (1) names the character and the place; (2) tells three events in order; (3) gives a word detail (what the words say) and a pictured detail (what the picture shows).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "I am Ali. We are in the garage. My lamp will not light. Grandpa finds a new cord. The lamp lights." Show two pictures: a garage with a workbench, and a kitchen. Ask: "Where does this story happen?" Every child points to a picture, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each, and ask each one to say the sentence that gives the place. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X18]]). A good answer is the garage. A child who points to the kitchen named a place the card does not give (error D). These marks go to day 20, not day 19.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Spoken blending and segmenting. Blend: /w/ /i/ /sh/, "wish"; /ch/ /a/ /t/, "chat"; /m/ /a/ /th/, "math". Segment with counters: rich, three counters; dish, three counters. Isolate the middle sound of fish, /i/, and the last sound, /sh/. Watch for ch said for sh (error M) and a consonant given for the middle sound (error J).

#### 10 min. Map and read

Pat's Map reread for meaning, 6 minutes. Review: look, and, in, his and with are mapped words. Read the page, children echo, then pairs read with a finger under the words and answer "What does Pat do?" in one sentence on pages 5 to 9. Then a chain with tiles (4 minutes): chat, that, math, bath, one sound at a time. The class taps the digraphs, one sound for two letters. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 17 (the letter P or p): that small group works up to 5 minutes. Then teach Q and q. Say: "Q is a circle with a short tail at the bottom right. The little q is a small circle with a stick on its right side that drops below the line." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "that man is rich". Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table each child reads one page of Pat's Map for meaning (about 55 seconds each) and says why Pat does it. The other places: sound counters (rich, wish, fish), word building with tiles (chat, that, math, bath), retell the story with the three event cards, draw and label (finish the day's strip), quiet pairs echoing the hammer poem. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, the day 17 repair group. Name tomorrow's check groups. Keep the strips in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strip with three boxes by hand. No counters: use buttons. One child: reread the story with the adult, fill the strip, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No story-map strip: fold a sheet into three boxes. No turn card: pass a pencil.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Read and tell.** Read the bicycle story aloud, or its first, middle and last pages.
  **Say.** "Who is in the story, where does it happen, and what happens?" Expect: Kofi and Gran; Gran's shed; the seat, the bell and the strap get fixed.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws one character, the place and three events on the strip and prints one word.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints Q q and "that man is rich".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X18]] Read "I am Ali. We are in the garage. My lamp will not light. Grandpa finds a new cord. The lamp lights." Show two pictures: a garage with a workbench, and a kitchen. Ask: "Where does this story happen?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the Pat's Map reread to the chain and the rotation to four places; keep the retell, the strip and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes adds a second pictured detail to a box and says it.

### Words and supports

Words: character, setting, event, shed, first, next, last. Frames: "The story happens in ..." "First ..., next ..., last ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 is the story check: you read a new two-panel card aloud once, hear small groups, and teach nothing. Day 18's exit marks do not go to day 19; if many children pointed to the kitchen (error D), the repair waits for day 20's Return and repair part.
