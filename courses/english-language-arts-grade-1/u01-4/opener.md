# Section 1.4 opener: trying again, and a check on a fresh story

## What the section does

Days 15 to 20 return to the last pages of the bicycle story, show how a first try changes to a second, and ask children to say a story's change in order. Day 19 is a check day: you read a two-panel card aloud once and hear small groups. Day 20 returns the check with a repair on a fresh card.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. Day 19 holds the hearings and a make-up time; no teaching happens. Day 20 holds the return and the repair. A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A change has two tries.** The first try fails; the second succeeds because something changes. Say what changed.
- **The teller and the actor can differ.** The one who says I tells the story.
- **A fresh card shows what a child can do alone.** Do not model a check card before the day.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C, D, E, F, A:** a detail from the picture alone, a place from another story, an omitted event, the actor called the teller, or an invented detail. Repairs are in the day 20 groups.
- **R:** a line read one word at a time. Clap each phrase.
