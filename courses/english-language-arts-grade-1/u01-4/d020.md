# Day 20: Fix it on a fresh story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the fresh card and a strip for each child. Sort the day 19 sheets by the error letters you recorded: D (a place from another story), E (a missing event) and F (the doer called the teller). Day 18's exit marks for error D join group D. Lay out the place signs, the chime and the reader Pat's Map. Use a fresh card, never Card B; the check marks stay as they were.

### The day

#### 10 min. Read the fresh card

Whole class on the rug. Act out "crooked" and "straight" with a hand (1 minute). Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the two panels of the fresh card aloud with its pictures, then again (3 minutes): "I am Pia. Mr. Dane and I are in the hall. The door sign hangs crooked." "Mr. Dane brings a roll of tape. 'Hold the sign,' Mr. Dane says. I hold it. He tapes it straight." Children listen only. Then each child tells a neighbor one thing they heard (3 minutes). Page 2 of Kofi and the Bicycle waits for the exit.

#### 20 min. Return and repair

Hand each child day 19's assessment sheet, face down, with one spoken note (2 minutes). The children in no group draw their own description of the fresh card on a strip, the people, the place and three events, with a word label in each box, and sit at their seats. Groups of up to eight, one after another:

Group D (day 18's exit and the day 19 place), 6 minutes. **Ask.** "Where does this story happen?" Expect: in the hall. They put the hall sign on the picture and point to "Mr. Dane and I are in the hall." Say: "Kofi goes to Gran's house, and his story ends in the garden. Each story has its own place, and this one is the hall."

Group E (the missing event), 4 minutes. Lay two counters and two picture cards, the sign hanging crooked and the sign straight. **Ask.** "What happened in between?" Expect: Mr. Dane brings the tape and tapes it. Put the missing event between and tell all three, first, next and last. Say: "Two events leave a gap; three tell the whole story."

Group F (the doer called the teller), 4 minutes. Review: the teller says I (day 13). Read page 6 of the bicycle story, "Gran picks a wrench ... She turns it." Gran does it, but Kofi tells it. Then read a fresh panel, made up for practice: "I am Nia. My jar lid is stuck. Aunt Fay taps the lid with a spoon. 'Now try,' Aunt Fay says. The lid turns." **Ask.** "Who tells this story?" Expect: Nia. Place the teller strip on "I am Nia" and on "My jar lid is stuck", and the speech card on Aunt Fay's words. Say: "The one who does is not always the one who tells."

Use the last 4 minutes to check in with any child who needs a second try.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, turn card, 4 minutes. Each child shows the part they added or changed and says what it was, "I changed my picture to show ...". Partners say back what they heard. **Ask.** "Which part changed in your picture?" Hear today's six pairs, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). A child may pass.

#### 15 min. Draw a clearer description

Each child adds to or redraws the day 19 sheet with the part that was missing, the place, an event or the teller, and says the change to you or a partner. Draw for 9 minutes, then hear today's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes): write the child's spoken sentence under the sheet.

Look-fors for the six: (1) names the place from the words; (2) tells three events in order; (3) says who tells.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 2 of Kofi and the Bicycle aloud, a plain voice for the telling and another for Gran's words. Ask: "Who is telling this part of the story?" Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X20v2]]). A good answer is Kofi. A child who says Gran, because she talks, called the speaker the teller (error F). These marks go to day 21.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Spoken blending and segmenting. Blend: /w/ /i/ /sh/, "wish"; /ch/ /a/ /t/, "chat". Segment with counters: bath, three counters; rich, three counters. Isolate the first sound of dish, /d/. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

Pat's Map reread for meaning. Review: look, and, in, his and with are mapped words. The class reads together, then each pair reads one page and says what Pat does. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Teach S and s. Say: "S is a curvy snake: start at the top right, curve left, then curve right and finish at the bottom left. The little s is a small snake." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "a ship at a dish". Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. This is the cycle sample and regrouping. At your table each child reads one page of Pat's Map and the four words sit, ship, dish and with (about 55 seconds each); note who needs a small group in the next cycle. The other places: sound counters (wish, chat, bath), word building with tiles (sit, sip, ship, dish), the three events of the fresh card with counters, draw and label, quiet pairs echoing the hammer poem. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, and who made each repair. Name tomorrow's six, and who is still not seen on error F.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the fresh card's two pictures by hand. One child: hear the fresh card, repair the one part the sheet shows, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: fold a sheet into three boxes.

**Skip.** The groups, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Listen.** Read this made-up card twice: "I am Pia. Mr. Dane and I are in the hall. The door sign hangs crooked." "Mr. Dane brings a roll of tape. 'Hold the sign,' Mr. Dane says. I hold it. He tapes it straight."
  **Say.** "Where does it happen, and who tells it?" Expect: in the hall; Pia.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the people, the place and three events and says what changed from their first try.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints S s and "a ship at a dish".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X20v2]] Read page 2 of Kofi and the Bicycle in two voices. Ask: "Who is telling this part of the story?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the groups and the exit; cut the redrawing to one added part. Longer: a child who finishes tells a second panel that Pia could tell next.

### Words and supports

Words: crooked, straight, hall, sign, tape. Frames: "The story happens in ..." "First ..., next ..., last ..." "... tells the story." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 uses the story and the articles to find a classroom job a designed tool could help. If many children called Gran the teller on today's exit (error F), its first part opens with four minutes: contrast Kofi telling with Gran speaking, and track the I in a fresh two-panel card.
