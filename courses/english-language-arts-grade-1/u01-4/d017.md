# Day 17: How is the second try different?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures and the two-tries sheet. Rule a chart with two columns, first try and later try. Lay out a turn card, the page 5 and page 6 picture cards and the three repair cards for each pair, the picture cards for the exit, the word cards, the reader Pat's Map, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 16's exit marks give the opening repair (error E). Today applies day 16; nothing is new.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread pages 4 to 11

Whole class on the rug. First 4 minutes: the repair for day 16's exit (error E). Children who gave the last event and left out the one in the middle sit with you and three cards from the bell: the big screwdriver that is too wide (page 9), Kofi looking at the small screw (you draw it from the words "I look at the screw. It is small."), and the bell ringing (page 10). Lay the first and the last. **Ask.** "Which picture is missing?" Expect: Kofi looks at the screw and finds the little screwdriver. Put it between the other two and tell all three: first, next, last. If many children made this error, run it with the whole class. The others point with a partner to the page 9 picture and tell each other what went wrong.

Then reread pages 4 to 7, the seat (3 minutes), and pages 8 to 11, the bell (4 minutes), with each picture shown. Pause after page 5 and after page 6, one ask each, answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds. After page 5. **Ask.** "What happens when Kofi turns the bolt with the screwdriver?" Expect: the tip slips off; the bolt does not move. After page 6. **Ask.** "What does Gran use, and what happens?" Expect: a wrench; its mouth fits and the bolt is tight. The rest is told with the pictures, without pauses.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

Pairs find the first try and the later try on the seat, 6 minutes, with the page 5 and page 6 picture cards. **Ask.** "How is the second try different?" Partners lay the two cards side by side and point to the sentence on page 6 that says what is different: "Its mouth fits the bolt just right." Do not tell them. Say only: "Show me where." Sit with three pairs. Hear today's six at their pairs for 40 seconds each (4 minutes). A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Compare, spoken

Whole class and the chart. Two columns, first try and later try. Review: the setting is where a story happens, and a problem is something wrong that a character wants to fix; the problem here is the loose seat. Place the page 5 card in the first column and the page 6 card in the later column. Fill three rows by picture and by saying: who is trying (Kofi, then Gran); what tool (a screwdriver, then a wrench); what happens (it slips, then it is tight). Everyone says: "First Kofi uses a screwdriver, and it slips. Then Gran uses a wrench, and the bolt is tight." Pairs then place the page 9 and page 10 cards on their own chart and say the same kind of telling with "First ___, then ___" (4 minutes). Hear today's six pairs, 40 seconds each, in the same minutes.

#### 20 min. Draw and say

Each child gets the two-tries sheet and chooses the seat or the bell. They draw the first try and the later try and print a word label in each box, spelled by its sounds (10 minutes). Pairs say which detail changed (3 minutes). Hear today's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes), and write each one's sentence under the drawing. The others add color. Keep the last minute for a neighbor to point to the page that holds the change.

Look-fors for the six: (1) draws both tries; (2) names what is different, the tool or the person; (3) says it with first and then.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "First I tape the kite tail. It falls off. Then Grandpa ties it with string. It stays on." Show a picture of each try. Ask: "How is the second try different?" Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X17]]). A good answer says Grandpa uses string and it stays on. A child who says "Grandpa tapes it" gave something the card does not say (error A).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend and segment words with /i/, /ch/ and /sh/. Say the sounds and the class blends: /ch/ /a/ /t/, "chat"; /r/ /i/ /ch/, "rich". Then say a word and push a counter for each sound: dish (three counters, /d/ /i/ /sh/), fish (three counters). Say that sh and ch are each one sound, so one counter. Watch for sh counted as two (error L).

#### 10 min. Map and read

Review: look, like and and are mapped words from day 16, with a part to remember; in, his and at are older. Point to each on the map and tap the part to remember. Then Pat's Map, the cycle 3 reader, first read after its patterns are mapped. Read the title to the children; it is read by the adult. Page by page: you read, the class echoes, then each pair reads the page with a finger under the words. Ten pages, one sentence each (6 minutes); ask "What does Pat do?" on pages 4, 6 and 8, one answer to a neighbor each. Then the class reads the whole book together once (2 minutes). Do not time anyone. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 16 (the letter O or o): that small group works up to 5 minutes. Then teach P and p. Say: "P is a tall stick down, then a bump at the top: go around and come back to the stick. The little p is a stick that drops below the line, with a small circle at the top beside it." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "a fish in a dish". Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table each child reads one page of Pat's Map for meaning (about 55 seconds each) and says what Pat does. The other places: sound counters (rich, chat, dish), word building with tiles (sit, sip, ship, dish), the two-tries chart in pairs (place the cards and say first, then), draw and label (finish the day's sheet), quiet pairs retelling the seat with the page 5 and 6 cards. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, the day 16 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the cards by hand and rule the chart on the board. No counters: use buttons. One child: reread pages 4 to 7 with the adult, compare the two tries with first and then, do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: point to the pictures on pages 5 and 6. No turn card: pass a pencil.

**Skip.** The repair, the chart, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Read and find.** Read pages 4 to 7 of Kofi and the Bicycle aloud.
  **Say.** "How is the second try different?" Expect: Gran uses a wrench, and the bolt is tight.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the first try and the later try on the two-tries sheet and prints one word in each box.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints P p and "a fish in a dish".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X17]] Read "First I tape the kite tail. It falls off. Then Grandpa ties it with string. It stays on." Show a picture of each try. Ask: "How is the second try different?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the bell to one telling and the rotation to four places; keep the seat, the drawing and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes says a third way the work could change.

### Words and supports

Words: different, tool, slips, wrench, fits, tight. Frames: "First ..., then ..." "The second try is different because ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 rereads the whole story and prepares a description of the character, the place and three events. If many children told Grandpa taping on today's exit (error A), its Prepare part opens with four minutes: say a sentence, then point to the sentence in the story that says it.
