# Day 19 Card B

Two panels, read aloud once to the class. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice."

**First panel:** "I am Jo. Tess and I sit on the rug. My toy car is under the sofa."

**Second panel:** "Tess holds a long ruler. I say, 'Can I help?' I bring a flashlight. Tess slides the ruler behind the car and pulls it out. Now I can play."

**Pictures:** first panel, Jo and Tess on a green rug beside a sofa; second panel, Jo shining a flashlight into the space while Tess reaches behind the toy car with the ruler. This is the card for items A9 and A10; A7, A8 and A11 to A15b use pages 11 and 12 of Kofi and the Bicycle. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. The card is not modeled before the day.
