# Days 16 and 17 two-tries sheet

A page with two large boxes joined by an arrow. The first box has a small picture of a hand with a flat palm (the first try). The second box has a small picture of a hand holding a new tool (the second try). Children draw what the first try was and what changed, and say it to a partner. One word label.
