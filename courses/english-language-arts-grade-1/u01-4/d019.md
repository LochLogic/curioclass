# Day 19: Show what you know about a story

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have story pages 11 and 12, Card B and its pictures, and a check sheet for each child. Lay out the record sheet with six names a group, the place signs, the chime and the reader Pat's Map. Teach nothing new; give no hint or model.

### The day

#### 10 min. Setup and read-aloud

Say: "Today shows what you can do. I will not help." Reread pages 11 and 12 aloud, with their pictures. Say: "This card is made up for practice," and read Card B once with its pictures. Hand out the sheets.

#### 30 min. Hearings

Six groups of five, 5 minutes a group, about one minute a child. At the teacher table each child: asks one question pages 11 and 12 answer ([[s01-A7v2]]); answers "Who fixes the basket strap?" ([[s01-A8v2]]); asks a different question, about Card B ([[s01-A9]]); and answers "Who pulls out the car?" ([[s01-A10]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The rest work at their seats on the check sheet: draw who is in the pages and where they end, with a word label ([[s01-A11v2]], 10 minutes); draw the three big things that happen, in order ([[s01-A12v2]]); circle who tells each page ([[s01-A13v2]], 5 minutes) and who says Again! ([[s01-A14v2]]); and draw one word detail ([[s01-A15v2]], 3 minutes) and one pictured detail ([[s01-A15bv2]], 2 minutes) for Kofi, each with a label.

#### 15 min. Make-up

Absent children and blank items are taken now, the same way. A child may point to an unclear part and say it.

#### 5 min. Close

Collect every sheet.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Blend and segment dish, chat and math. Not scored.

#### 10 min. Map and read

Say each chain card: chat, that, math, bath.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Teach R and r. Say: "R is a tall stick, a bump at the top and a leg that slants down. The little r is a short stick with a small arm." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "a dish". Not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each. Each group rereads Pat's Map; at the teacher table each child reads a page and you sample the cycle (not scored). The chime moves everyone along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Sort the sheets by error letter: A, AI, C, D, E and F go to day 20's groups.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the check sheet by hand. One child: do the four spoken items, then the drawings, one at a time; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: use two plain sheets.

**Skip.** The rotation, the make-up and the class talk.

- **10 min. Assessment.** Read pages 11 and 12 once, with no help.
  **Say.** "Who fixes the basket strap?" Expect: Kofi, by himself.
- **10 min. Ask.** Your child asks a question the pages answer. Then read this made-up card once: "I am Jo. Tess and I sit on the rug. My toy car is under the sofa. Tess holds a long ruler. I say, 'Can I help?' I bring a flashlight. Tess slides the ruler behind the car and pulls it out. Now I can play." Ask "Who pulls out the car?"
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws who is in the story, where they are and the three big things, and says who tells it.
- **10 min. Describe.** Your child says one thing Kofi does, with one word detail and one picture detail.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear [[s01-A8v2]] and [[s01-A10]] only. Longer: no extension; the check is the day.

### Words and supports

Words: strap, handlebar, basket, ruler, flashlight. Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 returns day 19's assessment sheets and repairs on a fresh card, never Card B or pages 11 and 12. Errors A, AI, C, D, E and F go to its Return and repair groups, with day 18's exit marks for error D.
