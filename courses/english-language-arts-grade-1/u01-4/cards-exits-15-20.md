# Exit card, day 15

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the card; you read the line aloud. The exits for days 16, 17, 18 and 20 use named pages of Kofi and the Bicycle, not cards.

## Day 15 card (s01-X15, RF.1.4b)

Show the printed line "Sam has a pad. Sam has a map." Read it aloud once, then ask: "Say the line like talking, after me."

**Right answer:** A smooth phrase with the rhythm of the line: Sam has a pad (beat) Sam has a map.
