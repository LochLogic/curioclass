# Exit cards, days 15 to 20

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 15 card (s01-X15, RF.1.4b)

Show the printed line "Sam has a pad. Sam has a map." Read it aloud once, then ask: "Say the line like talking, after me."

**Right answer:** A smooth phrase with the rhythm of the line: Sam has a pad (beat) Sam has a map.

## Day 16 card (s01-X16, RL.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "I squeeze the horn. It will not sound. Grandpa clears the dust. Now it toots." Show three pictures: the horn silent, Grandpa wiping the horn, the horn tooting. Ask: "What happens between the first try and the second?"

**Right answer:** Grandpa clears the dust.

## Day 17 card (s01-X17, RL.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "First I tape the kite tail. It falls off. Then Grandpa ties it with string. It stays on." Show a picture of each try. Ask: "How is the second try different?"

**Right answer:** Grandpa ties it with string, and it stays on.

## Day 18 card (s01-X18, RL.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "I am Ali. We are in the garage. My lamp will not light. Grandpa finds a new cord. The lamp lights." Show two pictures: a garage with a workbench, and a kitchen. Ask: "Where does this story happen?"

**Right answer:** The garage.

## Day 20 card (s01-X20, RL.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. Read two panels aloud. First panel: "I am Lee. Mud is stuck on my scooter wheel." Second panel: "Mr. Ray takes a rag. 'Hold the wheel,' Mr. Ray says. I hold it. He wipes the mud off." Ask: "Who is telling this story?"

**Right answer:** Lee tells it (Mr. Ray speaks inside the marks).
