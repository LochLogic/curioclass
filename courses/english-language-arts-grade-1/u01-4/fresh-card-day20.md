# Day 20 fresh card

Read aloud, with its picture, for the day 20 repair. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice."

**First panel:** "I am Pia. Mr. Dane and I are in the hall. The door sign hangs crooked."

**Second panel:** "Mr. Dane brings a roll of tape. 'Hold the sign,' Mr. Dane says. I hold it. He tapes it straight."

**Pictures:** first panel, a hall with a crooked door sign and a child and a man; second panel, the man taping the sign while the child holds it. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot. Groups D, E and F answer where it happens, what the three events are, and who tells it.
