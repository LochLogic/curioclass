# Day 16: How does the work change?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the story with its pictures, the hammer poem and the two-tries sheet. Lay out a turn card and the page 9 and page 10 picture cards for each pair, the picture cards for the exit, the word cards, the chain cards, the reader Pat's Mess, the place signs and the chime. Hang the sound-spelling map. Day 15's exit marks give today's repair group (error R). Today is the first day of cycle 3.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read pages 8 to 11

Whole class on the rug. Act out "try" and "fit": hold a big hand over a small hole and a small hand that fits it (1 minute). Review: in the story so far, Gran turned the wrench and Kofi watched (pages 5 and 6).

Read pages 8 and 9 with the pictures (2 minutes). **Ask.** "What happens when Kofi tries the big screwdriver?" Expect: its tip is too wide; it will not fit. Read pages 10 and 11 (2 minutes). **Ask.** "How is the little screwdriver different from the big one?" Expect: it has a thin tip, and it fits. Each ask is answered to a neighbor in 20 seconds. Then **Ask.** "On these pages, who turns the screwdriver, and who watches?" Expect: Kofi turns it; Gran watches.

Now lay the two picture cards on the board, the big screwdriver on page 9 and the little one on page 10 (1 minute). Say the change as one telling, with a hand on each card: "First Kofi tries the big screwdriver, and it does not fit. Then Kofi tries the little one, and the bell rings." The class says it back with you. Pairs lay out their two cards and tell it to each other with "First ... then ..." (4 minutes). Sit with three pairs. What Kofi did to change his try comes in the next part.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

First 4 minutes: the repair for day 15's exit (error R). Children who read the line one word at a time sit with you. Say a line from the poem as a robot, one word at a time: "Tap. And. In. They. Go." Then like talking: "Tap, tap, tap, and in they go." Clap each phrase together: "Tap, tap, tap" (clap), "and in they go" (clap). Each child claps and says the line once. If many children made this error, run it with the whole class.

The others work in pairs for 6 minutes. **Ask.** "What does Kofi do differently the second time?" Partners find the sentence on page 10 and point. Do not tell them. Say only: "Show me where." Expect: "I look at the screw." A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, turn card, 4 minutes. **Ask.** "How does the work change?" Frame: "First ..., then ..." Expect: first Gran turned the wrench and Kofi watched, then Kofi turns the screwdriver and Gran watches; first the big screwdriver, then the little one. Partners take two turns each of about 30 seconds. Hear today's six pairs, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). In the last 2 minutes you say the change once and pairs tell a neighbor.

#### 20 min. Draw the change

Each child gets the two-tries sheet: a box for the first try, an arrow, a box for the later try. They draw the first try and the later try from pages 9 and 10 and print a word label in each box, spelled by its sounds (10 minutes). Pairs say "First ..., then ..." to each other (3 minutes). Hear today's six, 1 minute each (6 minutes), and write each one's sentence under the drawing while the others add color. Keep the last minute for a neighbor to point to what changed.

Look-fors for the six: (1) draws the first try; (2) draws the later try and what changed; (3) says first and then.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "I squeeze the horn. It will not sound. Grandpa clears the dust. Now it toots." Show three pictures: the horn silent, Grandpa wiping the horn, the horn tooting. Ask: "What happens between the first try and the second?" Every child tells a neighbor, then hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X16]]). A good answer is that Grandpa clears the dust. A child who says "the horn toots" gave the last event and left out the one in the middle (error E).

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Cycle 3 opens, spoken only. Say each word and the class says the middle sound and holds up a finger: sit, fish, chat, dish, rich. Sort them aloud by the first or last sound: /sh/ in fish and dish, /ch/ in chat and rich. Then say the sounds and the class blends: /s/ /i/ /t/, "sit"; /d/ /i/ /sh/, "dish".

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Review: the sound-spelling map has digraphs, two letters for one sound (th on day 6). Point to the new entries. Short i, with a keyword picture of an igloo: say i, i, igloo, and tap under the letter. The two letters c h make one sound, /ch/, as in chair. The two letters s h make one sound, /sh/, as in ship. Add look, like and and to the word cards as new words with a part to remember (in "like" the ending is a part to remember; the letters do not spell the long sound). Review: in, his and at are mapped words from before. "With" is read by its sounds, with the th. Chain with tiles, one sound at a time: sit, sat (the middle changes), sip (two sounds change, so go slowly), ship (the first sound becomes two letters). The class reads each word as it is built. Sit with three pairs.

#### 10 min. Letters and dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap from day 15 (the letter N or n): that small group works up to 5 minutes. Then teach O and o. Say: "O is a round circle: start at the top, go left, around and close it. The little o is a small circle." Children trace and print. Dictation: say "a ship in a dish". Children print a word at a time, with the map for the sounds.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At your table each child reads one page of Pat's Mess for meaning (about 55 seconds each), and says what the cat does on that page. The other places: sound counters (sit, dish, rich), word building with tiles (sit, sat, sip, ship), echo the hammer poem in pairs, draw and label (finish the day's two-tries sheet), quiet pairs telling the change with the two cards. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet: the exit marks, the six who were heard and their look-fors, the day 15 repair group. Name tomorrow's six. Keep the pages in the folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two pictures and the exit pictures by hand. No counters: use buttons. One child: read pages 8 to 11 with the adult, tell the change with "first, then", do the exit and the letters; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No picture cards: point to the pictures on pages 9 and 10. No turn card: pass a pencil.

**Skip.** The repair, the rotation and the class talk.

- **10 min. Read and find.** Read pages 8 to 11 of Kofi and the Bicycle aloud.
  **Say.** "How does the work change?" Expect: first the big screwdriver did not fit, then Kofi found a little one and the bell rang.
- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws the first try and the later try on the two-tries sheet and prints one word in each box.
- **5 min. Letters.** Your child prints O o and "a ship in a dish".
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X16]] Read "I squeeze the horn. It will not sound. Grandpa clears the dust. Now it toots." Show three pictures: the horn silent, Grandpa wiping the horn, the horn tooting. Ask: "What happens between the first try and the second?"

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cut the chain to sit and ship and the rotation to four places; keep the story reading, the drawing and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes draws a second try that could come next and says it.

### Words and supports

Words: try, later, first, then, different, fit, thin, wide. Frames: "First ..., then ..." "The work changes because ..." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 rereads pages 4 to 11 and compares a first try with a later try. If many children left out the event between the two tries on today's exit (error E), its Reread pages 4 to 11 part opens with four minutes: lay picture cards, find the one that is missing and put it between the two tries.
