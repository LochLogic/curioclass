# Day 97: Compare two pages and keep labeled notes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 96's exit marks and name today's heard six and the six catch-up children. Print the student pages, the page Heron by the Water and the shared answer chart. Write H on a new strip for the label chart. Make the repair cards, the bordered blank card, the labeled note and the word cards. Put out the syllable chart, letter cards, the sound-spelling map and the readers. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Day 96's exit showed the cover used in place of a feature (error D). Put a fresh page on the table with its cover picture of a flock of birds and a contents line, Fast Hummingbird Wings, page 2. Turn the cover face down. **Ask.** "Where do you start to find the fact about hummingbird wings?" Expect: the contents entry. Every child traces with a finger in the air as you point: the contents entry, page 2, the heading, the fact "A hummingbird can hover." Say the rule: "The contents and the heading lead to the fact. The cover does not." If many made error D on day 96's exit, repeat with Feet for Swimming, page 2.

#### 10 min. Words, then read Heron by the Water

Show six cards: seed, mud, verify, question, source, diagram. Review: seed and mud from the beak article, source from unit 2, question from day 90 and diagram from day 80. New today: verify, to check that a fact is true by finding it in a page. The class says each word and you say it in a sentence, 30 seconds a word, three minutes. Read Heron by the Water aloud, pages 1 and 2 and then the glossary, three minutes. This is the first reading. Then reread the feeding sentences, two minutes: "A heron has a long, pointed beak. The beak works like a spear. The heron uses it to catch fish." Last, two minutes: fingers on the heading Long Beak and on the label long beak on the diagram. Review: heading and label, from days 80 and 81.

#### 12 min. Compare two pages

Put the letter B on the beak article and H on Heron by the Water, and add H to the label chart. Review: a source label says where a fact came from, from day 91. Show the heron part of the article, Long Beaks for Fish, and the part of Heron by the Water headed Long Beak. Pairs point to one feeding fact on each page and then to where the two agree, all at once, four minutes. Hear three pairs, 30 seconds each. Expect: both pages say the heron's long beak catches fish. Say: "When two pages say the same fact, we can verify it."

Now the contrast for error G, three minutes. Lay down a card with a bright border and nothing inside beside a note with a picture of a spear, the word spear and the label H. **Ask.** "Which card gives a fact about how a beak gets food?" Expect: the labeled note. Say the rule: "A border is not a fact. A note has a picture, a word and a label." If a child picks the border, ask "What fact does it hold?"

#### 8 min. Talk

Review: the turn card. **Ask.** "What fact does each page give?" Pairs take turns, two minutes, all at once; pass allowed. Hear six pairs, one minute each. Expect: the article says the heron strikes fast and catches the fish; the page says the beak works like a spear. Say which page each fact came from, B or H.

#### 20 min. Notes

Review: a note has a picture, a word and a label, from day 91; a note with no label is not yet a note. Three minutes to set up. Nine minutes: each child makes two notes about the heron, one from page B and one from page H, using two note cards from the folder. Words for copying: spear, strikes, long, fish. Each card gets its label, B or H, and the child points to the line that backs it. Then eight minutes at the shared answer chart, headed "How does a beak help a bird get food?" Five tables of six come up one table at a time, a minute and a half each, and each child sticks one note strip with a located fact and its label on the chart. Hear the first six children as they stick: **Ask.** "Which page says that?" Expect: the label letter and a finger on the line. The tables waiting finish their cards. A child with a decorated card and no fact is asked to point to the line first (error G). Each child keeps both notes in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet. The others finish; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the three pictures. Say: "Point to the picture that goes with both sources." ([[s03-X97a]]) Then, with the card in view: "Say one note and its label." ([[s03-X97b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the spoonbill's wings has picked a part the sources do not tell about (error B). A child who says a fact with no label has a fact but not yet a note.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say fad and fade; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: fade. Blend /p/ /l/ /a/ /n/, "plan". Say plate and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /p/, /a/ (long), /t/. Segment skate: /s/ /k/ /a/ /t/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 15, lesson 79. Review: a_e from days 94 to 96, and the ending -s from unit 2 (tugs). Show cards in pairs: cake and cakes, make and makes, take and takes. Say each and tap the sounds; the -s at the end says /s/ in each of these words, so listen as you say it. Map each new word: makes, takes, bakes. Chain on letter cards, one at a time: make, makes, take, takes, bake, bakes. Decode, then spell, two words: takes and bakes. Say: "Words with silent e and -ing wait for a later cycle." Review: -ing and -ed on closed words from unit 2, mixing and kicked, read each with its ending sounded. About 40 seconds a word. The reader Kate and Jake Bake a Cake is reread in the rotation.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the pairs B b and K k (every letter form was taught by day 27). Decode, then spell, two words: takes and bakes. Dictate: "Jake makes a cake." Say it whole, then a word at a time; a is mapped, makes was mapped today, and Jake is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children stretch each word, tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Jake, makes and cake the e is silent. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap the sounds again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names: map, blend, read, spell and reread on make and makes, take and takes, cake and cakes, about 40 seconds a word. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table with Kate and Jake Bake a Cake, reread for meaning and expression: each child reads a page and spells a taught word on a card, 50 seconds each, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread Ben's Donut, chain words with letter cards, sort closed and silent e word cards. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the notes you saw, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. List by name the children who offered decoration in place of a located fact (error G) for day 98's repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the chart on scrap paper and read the two pages aloud from any two printed bird pages. One child: compare the two pages by pointing to the shared fact, make two labeled notes, and do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No second page: use the heron lines in the day's pages. No partner: say both parts yourself.

**Skip.** The shared chart, the rotation and the stations.

- **10 min. Read.** Read the page Heron by the Water aloud, then the heron part of the beak article.
- **10 min. Compare.** **Say.** "Where do the two pages agree?" Expect: the heron's long beak catches fish.
- **5 min. Note.** Your child draws one note, a picture, one word and the letter of its page, B or H.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X97a]] [[s03-X97b]] Read the card the page shows. Your child points to the picture, then says one note and its label.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the comparison, the labeled notes and the exit; the chart can be a small group chart. Longer: a child finds a fact the pages do not share and notes it with its label.

### Words and supports

Words: verify, source, question, diagram, label, agree. Frames: "Page B says ___." "Page H says ___." "The two pages agree: ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can match a located fact to two sources and who can say a fact with its label. A child who offered decoration in place of a located fact (error G) is named for day 98's five-minute repair. Day 98 explains how a beak helps its bird using the article's evidence.
