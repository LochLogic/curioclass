# Day 99 species page (second source), Bills at Work

A one-page source labelled W, written in other words than the beak article. Printed contents: Bills for Seeds, Bills for Mud, Bills for Fish, Bills for Meat, page 2 for each. Under Bills for Seeds: "A sparrow's thick bill breaks open hard seeds." Under Bills for Mud: "A duck's wide, flat bill sifts food from mud and water." Under Bills for Fish: "A heron's long bill spears fish." Under Bills for Meat: "A hawk's hooked bill rips meat." One label (bill) and a glossary line (rip: to tear). A child points to the line that matches the note and writes the label W; a note found in one source only is marked "check". Bill is another word for beak (day 95).
