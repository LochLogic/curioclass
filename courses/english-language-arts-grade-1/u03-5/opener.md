# Section 3.5 opener: beak notes, a second source and a connection sentence

## What the section does

Days 95 to 99 give each child a task: explain how one bird's beak helps it get food. Children name the topic and key details, locate facts with the contents, heading, label and glossary, keep notes labeled by source, compare two pages, write one connection sentence and check one fact in a second source. Written feedback comes on day 98 and the revision on day 99.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Two pages can agree.** A fact that two sources give is more trustworthy.
- **A border or a color is not a fact.** A note holds a located fact and its label.
- **A connection sentence names the part, the food and how.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a detail named as the topic. Sort again.
- **D:** the cover used to find a fact. Follow the route.
- **G:** decoration in place of a located fact. Add one located fact to the shared answer.
- **B:** a claim no source gives. Point to the source sentence.
- **M:** a vowel left out of a spelling. Stretch and map every sound.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
