# Day 99: Check a note in a second page and revise the sentence

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy day 98's exit marks and sample marks and name today's heard six and the six catch-up children. Print the student pages and the species page, one for each pair. Make the repair cards and the word cards. Put each child's day 98 sheet with its feedback in the folder, ready to hand back. Put out the syllable chart, letter cards, the sound-spelling map and the readers. The exit card stays face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "These cards are made up for practice." Day 98's exit showed a part named with no link (error A). Lay out three cards: a duck's webbed feet, skin joins the toes, and push the water. **Ask.** "Which card is the topic? The other two tell about it." Expect: a duck's webbed feet. Slide the two detail cards under it. Say the topic again with the class: "The topic is the duck's webbed feet." Say the rule: "The topic is what every detail is about." If many made error A on day 98's exit, repeat with a sparrow's four toes as the topic.

#### 8 min. Gather and verify

Review: the beak notes folder from day 96, and a source label, from day 91. Each child takes out the notes and the day 98 sheet. Say: "To verify is to check a fact in another page, from day 97." Hold up the species page Bills at Work and write its label, W, beside B and H on the chart of labels. Read the page aloud, one minute. Show the route with the hawk: the contents entry Bills for Meat, page 2, the heading, then the line, "The hawk's hooked bill rips meat." Say: "Bill is another word for beak, from day 95." Each child then rereads their notes and picks one to check, three minutes, all at once. Walk among them.

#### 12 min. Check against a second source

Model one that fails, first. Take a note from the article, "the heron strikes fast", and look under Bills for Fish on page W: the page says the bill spears fish, and nothing about striking fast. Say: "Found in one page only. We write check on the card." Then pairs, eight minutes. Each pair takes turns with their two notes: the child points to the line on page W that says the same fact, in other words, and writes the label W on the card beside the first label. A note with no matching line gets the word check. Hear three pairs at the end, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** "Which line on page W says the same as your note?" Expect: a finger on the line and the label W. A child who says the fact is true because it is in a picture has not verified it: ask "Which line says it?" Say the rule: "A note checked in two pages is a note you can trust."

#### 10 min. Revise the sentence

Hand each child the day 98 sheet with its feedback. Say: "Change one detail." The change may add the food, add the how, fix a claim no note backs, or swap a word for the exact one from the page. Each child says "I changed ___ because ___" to a partner, then writes the new sentence under the first, or says it for you to scribe. Keep both. Move among the children; pay most attention to those whose feedback said food? or how?. Mark shown, shown with help, not yet seen. This revision stays in the folder with the first sentence and the feedback for day 118.

#### 10 min. Talk

Review: the turn card. **Ask.** "Where else can you check that note?" Pairs take turns, three minutes, all at once; pass allowed. Hear six pairs, one minute each. Expect: in a second page, such as page W or the heron page, looking under the heading for the same bird. A child who says "ask a friend" is asked "Which page would say it?"

#### 10 min. Notes folder

Each child files the notes, the sheet and the revision in the folder, in that order, and writes a W or the word check on any card not yet marked. Ten minutes. Hear six children, one minute each, while the others file. **Ask.** "Where did you check your fact?" Expect: the label W with the label of the first page, B or H.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet. The others finish; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the three pictures. Say: "Point to the picture that fits a note you can check in both sources." ([[s03-X99a]]) Then, with the card in view: "Say that note, and tell me where you found it." ([[s03-X99b]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. A child who points to the worm alone has picked the food in place of the part (error A). A child who gives a note found in one source only has not checked it in both (error B).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say bit and bite; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: bite. Blend /h/ /o/ /m/, "home". Say bone and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /b/, /o/ (long), /n/. Segment smile: /s/ /m/ /i/ /l/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 16, lesson 81, explicit teaching first: i_e and o_e. New today: the silent e makes the i and the o say their names. Review: a_e from days 94 to 98, and closed syllables from day 79. Add i_e and o_e to the sound-spelling map. Show pairs, one at a time: bit and bite, hop and hope, rob and robe. Say each and tap the sounds: bit has /b/ /i/ /t/ with a short i; bite has /b/ /i/ /t/ with a long i, and the e is silent. Children say which column of the syllable chart, closed or silent e. Map the new words: bite, hope, home, bone, Mike.

Now a check on the silent e. Show bike. **Ask.** "How many vowel sounds do you hear in bike?" Expect: one. Say: "Two vowel letters, i and e, but one vowel sound: the i says its name, the e is silent." Mark only the sounded vowel in bike, bone and tape with a dot, all at once on cards. About 40 seconds a word. The reader Kate and Jake Bake a Cake is reread in the rotation until the cycle 16 patterns are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print the pairs H h and N n (every letter form was taught by day 27). Decode, then spell, two words: bite and bone. Dictate: "Mike has a bone." Say it whole, then a word at a time; has and a are mapped, and Mike is a name, so it begins with a capital. Children stretch each word, tap one finger for each sound and print a letter for each tap; in Mike and bone the last e is silent. Repair for a vowel left out (error M): tap the sounds again and ask "Where is the vowel?"

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first, the six children the record names. For their first three minutes, the repair for error M on day 98's sample: stretch and map every sound, then write the word, on fresh taught words, lake, snake, plate, one at a time, about 30 seconds a child and word. Then they map, blend, read, spell and reread for the rest of their ten minutes. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table with Kate and Jake Bake a Cake, reread for expression: each child reads a page and spells a taught word on a card, 50 seconds each, one child at a time; the heard-six column names them. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread Ben's Donut, chain words with letter cards, sort closed and silent e word cards. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the verified notes, the revision marks, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Check each folder holds the notes, the day 98 sheet with its feedback and the revision. List by name the children who gave a note found in one source only (error B) for day 100's repair.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the four lines of the species page onto scrap paper and fold a sheet into a folder. One child: pick one note, find the line that says the same in the second page, write W, revise the sentence, and do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No second page: use any bird page about the same bird. No partner: say both parts yourself.

**Skip.** The rotation and the stations.

- **10 min. Check.** Read your child one note from the folder. Read the second page aloud. **Say.** "Which line says the same as your note?" Expect: the line about the same bird, in other words.
- **10 min. Revise.** Read the sentence from day 98 and its feedback. Your child changes one detail and says "I changed ___ because ___."
- **5 min. Print.** Your child writes W or the word check on the note card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X99a]] [[s03-X99b]] Read the card the page shows. Your child points to the picture, then says the note and where it was found.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the check of one note, the revision and the exit; file the folder tomorrow. Longer: a child checks a second note and marks it W or check.

### Words and supports

Words: verify, check, source, bill, revise. Frames: "Page W says ___." "I found it in ___ and ___." "I changed ___ because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can fit a note in both sources and who tells one found in a single source (error B). A child who gave a note found in one source only is named for day 100's five-minute repair. Day 100 analyzes a bird paragraph for its topic, a factual middle and a closing.
