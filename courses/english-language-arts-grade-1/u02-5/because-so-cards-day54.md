# Day 54 because and so cards

Two fact cards and an arrow card, with the joining word on a third card. Pair one: "The sun is high" and "our shadows are short." Pair two: "The sun sets" and "the sky gets dark." Children lay the cards, put the arrow between them, and say the sentence with "because" or with "so".
