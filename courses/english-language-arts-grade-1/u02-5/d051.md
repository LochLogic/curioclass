# Day 51: The sun's day in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the four sun sequence cards, shuffled. Write the repair card Bakery Door: "In the morning the bakery door opens. A dog waits outside." with one picture. Write three fact cards from Sun Across the Sky: page 3 "Our shadows grow short, because the sun is high." Page 4 "Our shadows grow long, because the sun is low." Page 5 "The sun sets, so the sky gets dark." Pick today's heard six.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read Sun Across the Sky

Whole class on the rug. Words first (3 minutes), each with a gesture: sun, rise (hands up), climb (fingers climbing), sink (hands down), set (hand flat, gone), shadow, Earth. Say aloud: "Nobody looks at the sun. We learn about it from shadows and pictures." Read the article whole, pages 1 to 6, pausing after pages 2, 3 and 5 for the picture (10 minutes). On page 2 say: "The sun seems to rise. Earth is turning." Night is gentle: on page 5 the sky gets dark and quiet, and it is morning on the other side of Earth. Mark the next step on the research cycle chart: we found a source.

#### 10 min. Reread and question

Opening repair for the children whose day 50 exit showed error E (4 minutes). Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card Bakery Door. **Ask.** "Which words on the card make you wonder?" Each child points to the words or the picture. Expect: a dog waits outside. Then write the wondering as a question we can find out: "What is the dog waiting for?" Say: "I like the dog is a feeling. It does not send us back to the page."

Then partners reopen page 2 (6 minutes). **Ask.** "What is the first thing the sun does, and which word tells you it is first?" Expect: it rises; the word First. Partners point to the word on the page and to the picture. **Ask.** "Which words begin pages 3, 4 and 5?" Expect: Next, Then, At last. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, one turn each, about one minute a child, pass allowed. **Ask.** "What comes next after the sun sets?" Starter: "After the sun sets, ___." Expect: night, and then the sun rises again, as page 6 says. Hear four pairs, half a minute each (2 minutes). Then lay the four sun sequence cards in order as a class (4 minutes): four children, who may pass, each place one card; the class says first, next, then, last.

#### 20 min. Facts in order, and a connection

Say aloud, as a quick warm-up (3 minutes): "The sun climbs." Review: on day 35 you learned four kinds of sentence. Partners turn it into a question ("Does the sun climb?"); hear two. Then: "Watch the shadow grow short." is a command.

Model (5 minutes). Review: the connection arrow from day 34 and because from unit 1. Lay the page 3 fact card in two halves: "The sun is high." arrow "Our shadows are short." Say: "The article says our shadows grow short, because the sun is high. Because tells why. The arrow goes from the high sun to the short shadows." Then lay page 5: "The sun sets, so the sky gets dark." Say: "So tells what happened next." Point to the source on each card.

Each child draws two facts joined by an arrow (7 minutes), from the page 4 or page 5 card, and says the sentence with because or so. Pairs say it to each other, one turn each, half a minute a child (2 minutes). Then each of the day's six says theirs to you, half a minute each (3 minutes). Nothing is added that the article does not say.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 6 of *Sky Patterns* aloud: "After morning comes the afternoon. After the sun sets, the night comes." Show two strips of three pictures: morning, afternoon, night; and night, afternoon, morning. Say: "Point to the strip in the order of the page." Every child points; the rotating six also tell it ([[s02-X51v2]]). A good answer is the first strip. The second strip is error A: lay first, then, last along it. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Say "sock" and hear the last sound, /k/; do the same with rock, deck, luck and tick. Say "duck": the last sound is /k/ and it is written with two letters. Move one counter a sound for "clap" (four sounds) and "stamp" (five sounds).

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review: c and k together say /k/ after a short vowel at the end of a word, from day 49 (2 minutes). Read lock, luck, neck and dock from the cards (3 minutes). Spell luck and neck on sound boxes (3 minutes). Pairs reread Sam's Rock pages 1 to 4, pointing under each word (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Rick can lock the deck." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped words can and the are named aloud. Watch the forms R, c and k. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table: the group rereads Sam's Rock pages 5 to 7 with expression, a page a child, then spells dock on sound boxes. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread Sam's Rock with a partner; chain lock, luck, duck and dock with letter cards; spell neck and rock on sound boxes; read the word cards sock, which, tick and bank to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the repair marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 52.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four sequence cards on scrap paper and the arrow in crayon. One child: hear the article a page at a time, lay the four cards in order, join two facts with an arrow, answer the exit by pointing, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No sequence cards: draw four small suns on separate pieces of paper.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Read.** Read pages 3 to 5 of Sun Across the Sky aloud. **Say.** "What does the sun do first, next, then and last?" Expect: rises, climbs, sinks, sets.
- **10 min. Join.** Your child draws two facts and an arrow. **Say.** "Tell it with because or so." Expect: the sun sets, so the sky gets dark.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads luck and neck and prints "Rick can lock the deck."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X51v2]] You read page 6 of Sky Patterns: "After morning comes the afternoon. After the sun sets, the night comes." Your child tells it back in order: morning, afternoon, night.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, the model and one drawing, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to the cards. Longer: a child who finishes draws the other fact pair.

### Words and supports

Words: rise, climb, sink, set, shadow, Earth, because, so. Rise, climb, sink and set are what the sun seems to do. Frames: "The sun ___." "___ because ___." "___, so ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can keep a text's order (error A: events reversed). Day 52 rereads the article with the class log. Its repair part opens with four minutes on error A for the children who need it: reread first, then, last and place the pictures.
