# Day 52 what we saw and what we read

A sheet in two columns: **What we saw** and **What we read.** Models: under what we saw, "The sun was behind the roof at going-home time." Under what we read, "The sun sets on the other side of the sky." Children add one note to each column and write a source label: log or article.
