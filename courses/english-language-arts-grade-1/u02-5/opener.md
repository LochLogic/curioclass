# Section 2.5 opener: a sky question and a shared notebook

## What the section does

Days 50 to 55 open the unit's research. The class poses a question, reads the sun article, keeps a picture log and a shared notebook with a source label on every note, and learns to tell what we saw from what we read. Children join two ideas with "because" and "so", capitalize names and months, and add end marks. Day 55 prepares a short talk.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A question is something we can find out.** It is not a feeling.
- **A note names its source:** the picture log (what we saw) or the article (what we read).
- **"Because" and "so" join two ideas.** The first tells what happened; the second tells what followed.

## The errors, in plain words

- **E:** a claim the page does not make. Point to the sentence.
- **G:** a drawing that does not help a listener.
- **A:** events in the wrong order.
- **Q, U, R:** a name or month in lowercase; a sentence with no end mark.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
