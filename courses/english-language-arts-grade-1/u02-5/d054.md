# Day 54: Because and so

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make the because and so cards for each pair: pair one, "The sun is high" and "our shadows are short"; pair two, "The sun sets" and "the sky gets dark"; a new pair, "The bucket has a hole" and "the water runs out"; an arrow card; and cards because and so. Write the repair card: "ana drew the sun in march." Pick today's heard six.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread the sun article

Whole class on the rug. Reread pages 3 to 6 of Sun Across the Sky, one picture pause each (7 minutes). After pages 3 and 5, lay pair one and pair two beside the page, with the arrow card between the halves (4 minutes). Say aloud: "Nobody looks at the sun." Review the research cycle chart, ask, find, note, share: the notes in our notebook share what the sources say (4 minutes: read three notes the class made).

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair for the children whose day 53 exit showed error Q or U (4 minutes). Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card. Review: a name and a month begin with a capital letter, as on days 50 and 53. Each child circles the name and the month on a copy and prints the capital above each.

Then partners look at page 5 (6 minutes). **Ask.** "Which two ideas does the article connect?" Expect: the sun sets and the sky gets dark. **Ask.** "Is it one thing making the other happen, or one thing coming after another?" Expect: the sun sets makes the sky get dark. Say: "The first, next, then and last words show an order. Because and so show what makes a thing happen." Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, two rounds, pass allowed. Round 1 (3 minutes), one turn each: **Ask.** "What does because mean in this sentence: Our shadows are short because the sun is high?" Expect: because tells why. Round 2 (3 minutes), one turn each: **Ask.** "What does so mean in this sentence: The sun sets, so the sky gets dark?" Expect: so tells what happened next. Hear four pairs, half a minute each (2 minutes).

#### 20 min. Because and so

Model (4 minutes). Say: "This pair is made up for practice." Lay "The cup is full" arrow "the milk spills." Say it both ways: "The cup is full, so the milk spills." "The milk spills because the cup is full." The arrow goes from what makes it happen to what happens. Nothing is added that the cards do not say.

Pairs build sentences (7 minutes) with pair one, pair two and the new pair, the arrow and the card because or so, one turn each, saying each aloud both ways. Then each child places a period card at the end of one built sentence, and says in a few words what a sentence like that does, it tells something (3 minutes). Hear the day's six, 40 seconds each (4 minutes): one sentence from the article and one from the new pair. The rest draw their favorite pair on the student page. Keep 2 minutes for second tries.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread page 4 of *Sun Across the Sky* aloud: "Our shadows grow long again, because the sun is low." Show three pictures: a low sun, long shadows on a field, and a snowman. Say: "Point to the picture of what makes the shadows long." Every child points; the rotating six also say the sentence with because ([[s02-X54av2]]). A good answer is the low sun. The long shadows are error A: the cause and the result swapped. The snowman is error E: a thing the page does not give. Then say: "This card is made up for practice." Show two sentence cards and read each aloud: "The bread is warm, so we eat it" with no end mark, and "The bread is warm, so we eat it." Say: "Point to the card with a finished sentence." Every child points; the six say what the end mark does ([[s02-X54b]]). A good answer is the second card. The first card is error R: no end mark. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Blend /s/ /t/ /o/ /p/, "stop"; /s/ /p/ /i/ /n/, "spin"; /s/ /t/ /e/ /p/, "step". Say "spill" and hear the first two sounds, /s/ and /p/, side by side. Move one counter a sound for spill (four) and stop (four).

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. New today. At the end of a short word, l, s, f and z are often doubled and say one sound: hill, bell, mess, kiss, puff, fizz. Map and read them (3 minutes). New: an s-blend has the s and the next sound side by side, each with its own sound: stop, spin, spill (2 minutes). New: the ending ed can say /d/ after /l/: spill, spilled; yell, yelled. Cover the ending, read the base, add ed, reread (2 minutes). If a child drops the ending (error L), do the same. Map the part to remember in was (a says /u/, s says /z/) and of (f says /v/) (1 minute). Spell hill and spilled on sound boxes (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Nell has a bell." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word has is named aloud. Watch the capital N and the double l in Nell and bell. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. Each group at your table first sends the children who made error K on the day 53 sample (3 minutes): say the fresh card, when, slowly, isolate the vowel sound /e/, map it, and blend again. Then the group rereads Sam's Rock, a page a child, and spells bell on sound boxes. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread Sam's Rock with a partner; chain kiss, miss, mess and less with letter cards; spell puff and fizz on sound boxes; read the word cards hill, bell, mess and puff to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the repair marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 55.

### Other rooms

No printer: write each fact on a strip of paper and draw the arrow in crayon. One child: reread pages 3 to 6, build the three pairs with because and so, place a period, answer both exits by pointing, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each fact on a strip of paper and draw an arrow.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Because and so.** Read page 5 of Sun Across the Sky aloud. **Say.** "Tell it with so, then with because." Expect: the sun sets, so the sky gets dark; the sky gets dark because the sun sets.
- **10 min. End marks.** Your child prints "The sun sets." and puts the period at the end. **Say.** "What does the period tell us?" Expect: the sentence is finished.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads hill and bell and prints "Nell has a bell."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X54av2]] You read from Sun Across the Sky: "Our shadows grow long again, because the sun is low." Ask: "What makes the shadows long?" Expect: the low sun. [[s02-X54b]] You write "The grass is green" twice, once with a period. Ask: "Which is a finished sentence?" Expect: the one with the period.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, one pair built both ways, the exits and the rotation; cut the talk to one round. Longer: a child who finishes makes a new made-up pair and says it both ways.

### Words and supports

Words: because, so, connect, period. Because tells why; so tells what happened next; a period ends a sentence that tells something. Frames: "___, so ___." "___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who finishes a sentence (error R: no end mark). Day 55 shares an answer with a drawing. Its repair part opens with four minutes on error R for the children who need it: say the purpose of the sentence and place its end mark.
