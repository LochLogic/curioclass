# Day 55: Share what we found

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write two repair cards with no end mark: "The cat naps in the sun" and "Is the sun up". Cut a period card and a question mark card for each child. Have each child's day 53 answer and drawing, the notes sheet and the because and so cards ready. Pick today's heard six.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread with the notebook

Whole class on the rug. Reread Sun Across the Sky pages 2 to 6, one picture pause each (7 minutes). Read aloud three notes from the shared notebook page and the log boxes (3 minutes). Say aloud: "Nobody looks at the sun." Then pairs: each child reads one of their own notes to a partner, one turn each, about a minute a child (5 minutes). Say after each note where it came from, the log or the article.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair for the children whose day 54 exit showed error R (4 minutes). Say aloud: "These cards are made up for practice." Read the first repair card. **Ask.** "What is this sentence for?" Expect: it tells us something. Each child places a period card at its end. Read the second card. **Ask.** "What is this sentence for?" Expect: it asks. Each child places a question mark card. Say: "A sentence's job tells us its end mark."

Then partners look at their own answer and drawing (6 minutes). **Ask.** "Which detail does your listener need?" Each child points to one thing in the drawing a listener must see to understand the answer. Expect: for example, the low sun, or the short shadows. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 10 min. Model sharing

Say aloud: "This answer is made up for practice." Model one share (4 minutes), speaking in a voice that reaches the back, looking at the listeners, holding the drawing up: "The article says the sun climbs. Our log shows the sky was bright at midday. So the sun was high." Show a drawing of a high sun over a playground with short shadows. Say aloud what you did: loud voice, eyes on the listener, a full sentence, a drawing that shows the detail. Pairs then practice the same four moves with a made-up answer from the because and so cards, one turn each, half a minute a child (3 minutes). Hear three pairs (3 minutes). This is a brief model; the sharing is not yet observed.

#### 20 min. Share an answer

Each child finishes the drawing for their answer from day 53 (6 minutes): the detail a listener needs, a source label, and a name with a capital. Pairs share, one turn each, a minute a child (3 minutes); the listener says back one detail. Then new partners share again (3 minutes). The day's six come to you as a group (6 minutes), one after another, a minute each; the rest share a third time with a new partner. Mark each of the six: shown, shown with help or not yet seen, on the four looks: the source named, two details joined, nothing the source lacks, a drawing the listener needs. Keep 2 minutes for the class to put one drawing each on the shared notebook page.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud the sentence: "The whistle blows, so the children line up." Show the drawing for it: a whistle with children standing in a line. Say: "Share this answer with me as your listener: say it clearly and point to the part of the drawing that shows it." Every child shares with a neighbor; the rotating six share with you ([[s02-X55]]). A good answer says the sentence clearly and points to the whistle and the line. Pointing to a part that does not show the answer is error G. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Segment five-sound words with counters: stand, swept, trust. Blend /s/ /k/ /r/ /a/ /p/, "scrap"; /c/ /r/ /i/ /s/ /p/, "crisp". If a child counts a cluster as one sound (error I), repeat with a counter for each sound.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review from days 53 and 54: ed can say /t/, as in licked, and /d/, as in spilled and yelled; doubled l, s, f and z each say one sound, as in hill and mess (4 minutes: read hill, mess, licked, spilled and yelled from the cards, covering the ending first). Review the parts to remember in was, of and but. Read the cycle 9 reader Sam and Nell on the Hill (4 minutes): you say a page, the class echoes it, pointing under each word. Spell mess on sound boxes (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Jill hid the bell." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word the is named aloud. Watch the capital J and the double l in both Jill and bell. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table: the group reads Sam and Nell on the Hill aloud, a page a child, and tells what happened to the jam; then spells yelled on sound boxes. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread Sam and Nell on the Hill with a partner; chain hill, bill, bell and sell with letter cards; spell mess and spilled on sound boxes; read the word cards was, of, but and stop to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the repair marks and the sharing marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 56.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the two repair sentences on strips and make each end mark with a pencil. One child: reread the article and your notes, finish a drawing, share the answer aloud with the four moves, answer the exit by pointing, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet or cards: use plain paper.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Draw.** Your child draws what the sun does at midday, with the detail a listener needs. **Say.** "What does a listener need to see?" Expect: the high sun and short shadows.
- **10 min. Share.** Your child shares the drawing with you, in a clear voice, looking at you. **Say.** "Tell me what the article says and what we saw." Expect: a sentence with because or so.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads hill and mess and prints "Jill hid the bell."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X55]] You say: "The pot boils, so the lid shakes." Draw a pot with a shaking lid. Your child shares the answer with you and points to the part of the drawing that shows it.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, one share, the exit and the rotation; cut the model to one pair. Longer: a child who finishes shares a second answer with so.

### Words and supports

Words: share, listener, detail, drawing, period, question mark. A listener is a person who hears you; a detail is one small fact. Frames: "The article says ___." "We saw ___." "So ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who draws the detail a listener needs (error G: a drawing that adds nothing). Day 56 begins the moon. Its repair part opens with four minutes on error G for the children who need it: draw the detail a listener needs.
