# Day 50 shared notebook page

A wall page with the line "Our question: ______" at the top and two columns beneath: **Source** and **Note**. The class chooses one question about the sun from the pictures and the article, and the teacher models one note: a few words, a small picture and a source label. Each child then adds a note of their own to a page.
