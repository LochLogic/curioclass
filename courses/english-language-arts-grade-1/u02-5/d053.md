# Day 53: A two-source answer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the model answer on a chart: "Our shadows were short at lunch. The article says shadows grow short because the sun is high. So the sun was high at lunch." Make a sentence card for each child: "___ drew two sources in ___." Write the repair card Puddle Ice: a drawing of a bike by a frozen puddle and the fact card "A puddle can freeze in the cold." Pick today's heard six.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread

Whole class on the rug. Reread Sun Across the Sky pages 3 to 5, one picture pause each (6 minutes). Read aloud the log boxes the class has drawn and the notes on the shared notebook page (3 minutes). Say aloud: "This answer is made up for practice." Read the chart with the model answer twice, and point to the part that comes from the log and the part that comes from the article (6 minutes). Nobody looks at the sun.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair for the children whose day 52 exit showed error E (4 minutes). Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Show Puddle Ice. **Ask.** "Which card tells what someone saw, and which tells what we read?" Expect: the drawing is what was seen; the fact card is what we read. Each child points to the drawing and then to the fact card.

Then partners look at the article and the log (6 minutes). **Ask.** "Which source gives the first useful detail? Which log detail adds evidence?" Expect: page 3 says why shadows grow short; our log shows what the sky looked like at midday. Partners point to each. Hear four pairs, half a minute each.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, two rounds, pass allowed. Round 1 (3 minutes), one turn each: **Ask.** "What does the article say, and what did we see?" Starter: "The article says ___. We saw ___." Round 2 (3 minutes), same pairs: each joins the two ideas aloud with because, "We saw ___ because the article says ___." Expect only a reason the article gives. Hear four pairs, half a minute each (2 minutes).

#### 20 min. A two-source answer

Model (4 minutes). Say: "The question is still: what does the sun do in the sky every day?" Point to the chart: the log gives one part, the article gives the other. Each child makes the same kind of answer from their own notes (6 minutes): two boxes side by side, one picture from the article and one from the log, with a source label on each.

Pairs say their answer to each other with because or so, one turn each, half a minute a child (3 minutes). Then each child fills the sentence card (5 minutes): print a name and a month, copied from the name card and the month card, each beginning with a capital letter, and place the period. While they work, take the day's six one at a time, write their answer as they say it, and read it back (about 40 seconds a child). Last, children circle the capital letters on their card (2 minutes). If a name or month starts with a small letter (errors Q and U), circle it with the child and print the capital above it.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Show three sentence cards and read each aloud: "dev watched the sun in june." "Dev watched the sun in june." "Dev watched the sun in June." Say: "Point to the one written right." Every child points; the rotating six also say which letters begin the name and the month ([[s02-X53]]). A good answer is the third card. The first card is error Q: the name is lowercase. The second card is error U: the month is lowercase. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Which vowel sound do you hear? Say pairs: tick and tock; rock and rack; deck and duck; lick and lock. Children say the vowel they hear in each. Say "packed" and "picked": the ending sounds like /t/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review: on day 49 you heard the ending /t/ in helped and asked; today you read it. New: the ending ed can say /t/. Cover the ending, read the base, add ed, say the word, and reread. Model pack, packed (2 minutes). Children read picked and licked from the cards the same way (3 minutes). If a child reads pack for packed (error L), cover the ending, read the base, add it, reread. Spell packed on sound boxes: the base pack, then ed in one box (3 minutes). Pairs reread Sam's Rock page 1 (2 minutes).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Jan picked a rock." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word a is named aloud. Watch the capital J and the forms c and k. Then each child points to the capital, the period and the ending ed (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. The cycle 8 sample is taken at your table, one minute a child: show the card from the unit 2 fresh retry cards, whip, and say "Read this word." ([[s02-A15]]). Remove it. Say "Write the word I say: peck." once in the sentence "The hen can peck." and once alone ([[s02-A16]]). Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread Sam's Rock with a partner; chain pack, packed, pick and picked with letter cards; spell licked on sound boxes; read the word cards packed, picked, licked and which to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the repair marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the sample marks and the heard-six column. Sort the sample by error letter for tomorrow. Name the foundations groups for day 54.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the sentence lines on strips and fold a page in two for the notes. One child: reread the article, make a two-source answer, copy a name and a month with capitals, answer the exit by pointing, then the foundations parts and the sample.

### At home

**Swap.** No notes sheet: fold a page in two.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Two sources.** Read page 3 of Sun Across the Sky aloud. Your child draws it in one box and a sky seen from a window in the other. **Say.** "Tell it with because or so." Expect: the shadows are short because the sun is high.
- **10 min. Capitals.** Your child prints their name and the month, each with a capital letter, on a line.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads packed and picked and prints "Jan picked a rock."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X53]] You write three cards: "mia saw the moon in may." "Mia saw the moon in may." "Mia saw the moon in May." Ask: "Which is written right?" Expect: the third.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, the answer with one drawing, the exit and the rotation with the sample; cut the talk to one round. Longer: a child who finishes writes a second answer with so.

### Words and supports

Words: source, article, log, because, so, capital. A capital letter begins a name and a month. Frames: "The article says ___." "We saw ___." "___ because ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can capitalize a name and a month (error Q: a lowercase name; error U: a lowercase month); the sample tells you who needs the vowel work (error K: a different middle vowel) and the ending work (error L: a dropped ending). Day 54 opens with four minutes on error Q and U for the children who need it: circle each name and month and capitalize its first letter. Its rotation takes error K with the next foundations group: isolate the spoken vowel, map it and blend again.
