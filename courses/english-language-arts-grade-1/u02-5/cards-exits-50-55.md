# Exit cards, days 50 to 55

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 50 card (s02-X50, W.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Every night the street lamp comes on. A moth flies near the lamp." Show the page picture, then read three cards aloud: "Where does the moth go in the day?" "What color is the lamp?" "The moth is nice." Say: "Point to the question we could ask about this page." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The card "Where does the moth go in the day?" It asks to find something out that the page does not tell.

## Day 51 card (s02-X51, RI.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "First, the balloon goes up. Then it floats high. Last, it comes down." Show two strips of three pictures: up, high, down; and down, high, up. Say: "Point to the picture strip in the order of the card." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The strip up, high, down.

## Day 52 card (s02-X52, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show two cards: a class picture log card, a drawing of a flag with a long shadow beside it, and a fact card read aloud: "Shadows grow long when the sun is low." Say: "Point to the card that tells what we saw." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The drawing of the flag and its long shadow, our observation. The fact card is a source we read.

## Day 53 card (s02-X53, L.1.2a)

This card is invented for practice. Show three sentence cards and read each aloud: "dev watched the sun in june." "Dev watched the sun in june." "Dev watched the sun in June." Say: "Point to the one written right." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The third card: Dev and June each begin with a capital letter.

## Day 54 card (s02-X54a, RI.1.3)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "The oven is hot, so the bread is warm." Show three pictures: a hot oven, warm bread on a plate, and a snowman. Say: "Point to the picture of what makes the bread warm." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The hot oven. The word so joins what makes it to what happens.

## Day 54 card (s02-X54b, L.1.2b)

This card is invented for practice. Show two sentence cards and read each aloud: "The bread is warm, so we eat it" with no end mark, and "The bread is warm, so we eat it." Say: "Point to the card with a finished sentence." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The second card, with the period.

## Day 55 card (s02-X55, SL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud the sentence: "The whistle blows, so the children line up." Show two drawings for it: a border of stars, and a whistle with children standing in a line. Say: "Point to the drawing that helps a listener." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The drawing of the whistle and the children in a line.
