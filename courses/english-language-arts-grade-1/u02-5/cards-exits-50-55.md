# Exit cards, days 50 to 55

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of each card still marked "invented for practice" below; the exit pictures read a named passage and carry no foot line. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 50 card (s02-X50, W.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "Every night the street lamp comes on. A moth flies near the lamp." Show the page picture. Say: "Ask one question about this page that we could find out from a source." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** A question the page does not answer, such as "Where does the moth go in the day?"

## Day 51 exit pictures (s02-X51v2, RI.1.3)

Passage: Sky Patterns, page 6 (after morning, after the sun sets); day 51 works Sun Across the Sky, pages 2 to 5. Reread page 6 of Sky Patterns aloud: "After morning comes the afternoon. After the sun sets, the night comes." Say: "Tell how two facts on this page connect. Use so or because." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The sun sets, so the night comes.

## Day 52 card (s02-X52, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show two cards: a class picture log card, a drawing of a flag with a long shadow beside it, and a fact card read aloud: "Shadows grow long when the sun is low." Say: "Tell what we saw for the we-saw column, then the fact that goes beside it." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** We saw a flag with a long shadow; beside it goes the fact, "Shadows grow long when the sun is low."

## Day 53 card (s02-X53, W.1.8)

Made up for practice. Read two cards aloud: "In the morning, the sun comes up." "In the evening, the sun goes down." Say: "Answer our question, what does the sun do each day, in two complete sentences, one from each card." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Two complete sentences, one from each card: The sun comes up in the morning. The sun goes down in the evening.

## Day 54 exit pictures (s02-X54av2, RI.1.3)

Passage: Sun Across the Sky, page 4 (long shadows); day 54 works pages 3 and 5. Reread page 4 of Sun Across the Sky aloud: "Our shadows grow long again, because the sun is low." Show three pictures: a low sun, long shadows on a field, and a snowman. Say: "Point to the picture of what makes the shadows long." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The low sun. The word because joins what happens to what makes it happen.

## Day 54 card (s02-X54b, L.1.2b)

This card is invented for practice. Show two sentence cards and read each aloud: "The bread is warm, so we eat it" with no end mark, and "The bread is warm, so we eat it." Say: "Point to the card with a finished sentence." Hear the day's rotating six. 

**Right answer:** The second card, with the period.

## Day 55 card (s02-X55, SL.1.5)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud the sentence: "The whistle blows, so the children line up." Show the drawing for it: a whistle with children standing in a line. Say: "Share this answer with me as your listener: say it clearly and point to the part of the drawing that shows it." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Says the answer clearly and points to the whistle and the children in a line.
