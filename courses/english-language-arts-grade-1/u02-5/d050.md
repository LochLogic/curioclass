# Day 50: Ask a question about the sun and open our notebook

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the notebook page and the picture log strip low on the wall, with the research cycle chart beside them. Write the repair card: "First, Pup found a stick. Next, Pup ran with it. Last, Pup gave it to Dad." with three pictures: found, ran, gave. Write each child's name on a card for copying, and post the month card from the wall calendar. Pick today's heard six from the record sheet.

### The day

#### 15 min. Reread Sky Patterns and begin the notebook

Whole class on the rug. Review: you heard Sky Patterns on day 34; a pattern is something that happens again and again in the same order. Reread pages 1 to 6, one picture pause each (5 minutes). Say aloud: "Nobody looks at the sun. Pictures stand in for it." Show the class sky picture record and the picture log strip (3 minutes): the log holds what we see, and the article holds what we read. Review: the research cycle chart, ask, find, note, share, which you used in kindergarten and use again today. Point to ask and find: "Today we do these two steps." At their seats children draw box one of the log, the morning sky as it looks from the window today: a color, a cloud, light on the floor, with a one-word label (7 minutes). Never the sun. No window: draw the sky from the picture on page 1.

#### 10 min. Reread and question, with opening repair

Opening repair for the children whose day 49 exit showed error A (4 minutes): they sit at the front; the rest point to a favorite picture in Sky Patterns with a partner. Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Read the repair card, then lay the pictures out of order: ran, gave, found. **Ask.** "Which came first?" Expect: found. Lay the first, next, last cards over the pictures in order and tell it again, pointing. Reread the time words, then each child places the pictures once, and you note who needs it again.

Then partners reopen Sky Patterns (6 minutes). **Ask.** "Which fact makes you wonder?" Each child points to a picture or a sentence and says "I wonder ___" to a partner, one turn each, about half a minute a child, pass allowed. Hear four pairs, half a minute each. Expect: any wonder the page supports, such as where the sun goes at night.

#### 10 min. Talk

Pairs, with the turn card, in two rounds. Review: the turn card starters from unit 1, "I would add" and "Which part?". Round 1 (3 minutes), one turn each: **Ask.** "What would you like to find out about the sun?" Starter: "I would like to find out ___." Expect: where it goes at night, how it moves. Round 2 (3 minutes), new partners: each says back what the new partner told them, then adds "I would add ___" or asks "Which part?". Hear four pairs, half a minute each (2 minutes). While they talk, write five or six of the wonderings you hear on strips, in the children's words.

#### 20 min. A question and a labelled note

Read the strips aloud (4 minutes). Say: "A question is something we can find out. Ours must be one a picture and an article can tell us." Shape three of the wonderings into questions the log and Sky Patterns could answer; include "What does the sun do in the sky every day?" Children show a hand for the one they want; the most hands wins. Write it on the notebook page after "Our question:".

Model a note (5 minutes). Review: you met note and source in kindergarten. Say: "A note says what a source says, in a few words, and names its source. A source is where we found it." Show the sky fact card for page 3, "Every morning, the sun comes up." Draw a small sun over a row of houses, write two words, "sun up", tick the book picture for the article, and copy a name card and the month card, each starting with a capital letter. Say: "I read it in Sky Patterns, page 3."

Children make a note of their own (10 minutes), from a fact card or from their log box: a picture, one to three words, a tick at the book picture (the article) or the window picture (the log), and their own name and the month copied with capitals. Walk round and ask the day's six, half a minute each: "Where did you find it?" Add one article note and one log note to the wall page (1 minute).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read aloud: "Every night the street lamp comes on. A moth flies near the lamp." Show the page picture, then read three cards aloud: "Where does the moth go in the day?" "What color is the lamp?" "The moth is nice." Say: "Point to the question we could ask about this page." Every child points; the rotating six also say how they know ([[s02-X50]]). A good answer is the first card. The second card is error G: the picture already shows it. The third card is error E: a feeling, not a question to find out. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken words, not scored. Say "when" and "wet": both begin with /w/; wh is two letters for that one sound. Hear the first sound in web, when, whiz and which. Move one counter a sound: "frog" has four sounds, "dog" has three. If many count a cluster as one sound (error I), repeat with counters in the rotation.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Whole class with the sound-spelling-map and letter cards. Review: you mapped wh and ck on day 49 (when, whiz; back, duck). Map which: wh, i, ch, three sounds in five letters (2 minutes). Map sock, rock, deck and tick: c and k together say /k/ (2 minutes). Review: -ank on day 43; map bank, four sounds, with n and k saying /ng/ then /k/ (1 minute). Spell sock and bank on sound boxes (2 minutes). Read Sam's Rock (3 minutes): review the mapped words a, the, has, said and see; you say a page, the class echoes it, pointing under each word. Read the title aloud for them.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Letter forms: new-letter teaching finished on day 27, so today is use, not teaching. Review: a name begins with a capital letter, as in your edit on day 31. Dictation (6 minutes): say "Nick has a sock." Children print a word at a time with the map; the mapped word has is named aloud. Watch the forms N, c and k. Then each child points to the capital and the period (4 minutes).

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table; today's heard six go first. At the table: the group reads Sam's Rock pages 1 to 6 aloud, a page a child, then spells rock on sound boxes, about a minute a child. The other children rotate through four stations, 6 minutes each: reread Sam's Rock with a partner; chain sock, rock, lock and luck with letter cards; spell deck and tick on sound boxes; read the word cards which, when, whiz and sock to a partner.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks and the repair marks to the unit 2 record sheet with the heard-six column and the cycle record. Name the foundations groups for day 51.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the log boxes on scrap paper and fold a sheet in two for the notebook. One child: reread Sky Patterns, choose one question together, draw a note with its source, answer the exit by pointing, then the foundations parts.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall page: fold a sheet in two columns, Source and Note.

**Skip.** The talk pairs and the rotation.

- **10 min. Wonder.** Read pages 3 and 4 of Sky Patterns aloud. **Say.** "What do you wonder about the sun?" Expect: any question, such as where it goes at night.
- **10 min. A note.** Your child draws the sun coming up, writes two words, and ticks where you read it. **Say.** "Where did you find it?" Expect: in Sky Patterns.
- **10 min. Sounds.** Your child reads sock and rock and prints "Nick has a sock."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X50]] You say: "Every night the owl hoots. The owl sits in a tree." Ask: "Which could we find out: What does the owl eat? Or: The owl is lovely?" Expect: the first.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the repair for your own misses, the question and note, the exit and the rotation; cut the talk to one round. Longer: a child who finishes makes a second note from the other source.

### Words and supports

Words: question, note, source, notebook, log, article. A question is something we can find out; a source is where we found it; the log is our own pictures; the article is what we read. Frames: "I wonder ___." "I found it in ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

The exit tells you who can pick a question to find out (error E: a feeling taken for a question). Day 51 reads Sun Across the Sky for the first time. Its reread part opens with a four-minute repair of error E for the children who need it: point to the page words before choosing a question.
