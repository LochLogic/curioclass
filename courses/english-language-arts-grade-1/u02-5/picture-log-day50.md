# Day 50 picture log strip

A strip of boxes, one for each time of day, with a place for the date. Box one: the sun low in the morning sky. Box two: the sun high. Box three: the sun low in the afternoon sky. The class draws what they see from a window, never looking at the sun, and writes a one-word label. The log is what we saw; the article is what we read.
