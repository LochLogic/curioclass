# Highlighted word cards

Three word cards, each with the word in large print and a small picture on the back: glass, visible, reflection. Each word is highlighted with a yellow stripe. A frame card reads "What does ___ mean?" and is read aloud. Children ask the question to a partner and point to the page picture that answers it.
