# Day 143: Gather window facts and tag each one

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six and the catch-up group. Give each child an organizer with its two page tags. Mark page numbers on the read-aloud copies. Keep the exit card face down. Practise saying the fresh repair pair as made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 142's exit (error C, s04-X142a), 3 minutes. If many missed, repeat with a second point ("Wipe your feet" with "Mud makes the floor slippery" and "The mat is brown"), 2 minutes from the first Talk turn. Say: "These are made up for practice. The point is: Hang up coats." Read two strips, "Coats on the floor make children trip." and "The hooks are in a row." Pairs say, for each, "Hang up coats because ..." and decide whether it fits. Hear two pairs. Then, 2 minutes: show the organizer, a question box, two note boxes and a page tag beside each (review: research organizer, unit 3, day 103; a note with its page label, unit 3, day 110). Say: "Today we gather facts, which means we collect them from pages we are given. A page tag names the page a fact came from." Read the question: "How can people keep windows safe for birds?"

#### 15 min. Read

Reread *Windows Birds Can See* page 3, 2 minutes. Read *Marks Outside*, pages 1 to 3, whole and aloud with the pictures, 3 minutes; stop at barrier on page 1 and say it means something that blocks the way, 1 minute. Point to the five small windows on page 2 and say each label, 2 minutes. Then read *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes* aloud once, whole, 3 minutes, and put it aside: it is tomorrow's second way. Hold the two window pages up beside the organizer, 2 minutes: these are the two pages we use today. Say that the teacher provided them.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time with the turn card, 60 seconds, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the asks. Each answer comes from the page. Pass is allowed.

1. **Ask.** "Where do the marks go?" Expect: on the outside of the glass.
2. **Ask.** "How close are the marks?" Expect: close together.
3. **Ask.** "Which page told you where the marks go?" Expect: Marks Outside.
4. **Ask.** "Which page told you that marks show the glass?" Expect: Windows Birds Can See.

#### 20 min. Writing and language

Alone, with you walking. Model note 1, 4 minutes: reread the line on page 3 of *Windows Birds Can See*, "Marks on a window show a bird that the glass is there." Each child takes the strip "Marks show a bird that the glass is there" from the window fact strips in the folder. Draw a small marked window in the first note box, print the label marks, peel the tag Windows Birds Can See and write 3 beside it. Each child makes note 1 the same way, 6 minutes. Then note 2 from *Marks Outside*: the marks go outside, close together; a small drawing, one word label (outside is on the board), and the tag Marks Outside with its page number, 8 minutes. Check every note has a tag, 2 minutes: a note with no page is error W; send the child back to find the fact on a page and stick the tag. The organizer goes in the portrait folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which note names its page?" ([[s04-X143a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file the organizers, 2 minutes. The spoken asks are heard in the rotation below. The note with no tag is error W.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Clap and count the parts of moon-light, bee-hive and tree-top. Say bee, then hive: children say the whole word. Do the same for tree and top. Say the first part of each word, then its sounds: moon, bee, tree.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 23, lesson 119. Review: cut a two-syllable word into parts, read each, join them (day 106); ee, oo and final y (day 140). New today: read and spell beehive, treetop, moonbeam and bumpy: cut each word, read the parts, join them, 4 minutes. Then board work, 3 minutes: write bee, then hive, and join; the same for tree and top. Two more words for A Little Seed, 3 minutes: really (real and ly, y says /ee/) and anymore (any and more; map the irregular parts).

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate "A bee is in the treetop." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map oo, ee and final y, blend, read, spell, reread A Little Seed pages 1 to 8. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with A Little Seed pages 1 to 10, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with at least 20 seconds to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Which note answers the class's question?" ([[s04-X143b]])
- **Ask.** With page 2 of Marks Outside in view: "Say one way to put marks on a window." ([[s04-X143c]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. "Marbles are round" is error C.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks, and the organizers checked for two tags.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw two note boxes and a tag box on a sheet and say the page aloud. One child: find the line on the page, draw it and name the page.

### At home

**Swap.** No organizer: fold a paper in half, draw in each half.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Note.** Say: "Marks go on the outside of the glass, close together. This is from the page Marks Outside." **Say.** "Where do marks go?" Expect: on the outside. Draw it with a word label.
- **10 min. Tag.** Write the page name beside the drawing. **Say.** "Which page told you?" Expect: Marks Outside.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "beehive" and "treetop."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X143a]] [[s04-X143b]] [[s04-X143c]] Read a short made-up page. Your child points to the note that names its page, then to the note that answers the question, and says one way to put marks on a window.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: make note 1 and tag it; make note 2 at home. Longer: a child who finishes adds a third drawing of another way to make marks, with its tag.

### Words and supports

Words: gather, provided, page tag, outside, barrier, marks. Frame: "I got this from ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the rotation tell you who can find the note that names its page, pick the note that answers the question and name a way to make marks. Day 144 reads a second way to help birds and compares it with safe windows. A child who chose the note with no page (error W, s04-X143a) finds a fact on a named page and sticks its tag, on a fresh pair of notes, first.
