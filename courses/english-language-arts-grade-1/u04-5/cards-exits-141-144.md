# Exit cards, days 141 to 144

Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the day 141 and 143 cards. The day 142 and 144 exits use named pages of the unit's texts: reread those pages aloud and show the pictures listed. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line and any word a card says is printed. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 141 card (s04-X141a, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "Rafi's page is about the sandbox. It says, 'The sandbox is wide. The sandbox has a lid.' Rafi ends, 'The sandbox helps us dig at recess.'" Show three pictures: the sandbox, the lid alone, a shovel alone. Say: "Which picture is the topic of Rafi's page?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The sandbox.

## Day 141 card (s04-X141b, W.1.2)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "Rafi's page is about the sandbox. It says, 'The sandbox is wide. The sandbox has a lid.' Rafi ends, 'The sandbox helps us dig at recess.'" Say: "Say one fact from Rafi's page." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** "The sandbox is wide." or "The sandbox has a lid."

## Day 141 page (s04-X141c, RI.1.10)

Heard in the rotation, not on a card. Show page 2 of *Windows Birds Can See*, the picture of the lit building at night. Say: "What pulls birds toward windows at night?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Bright lights inside a building.


## Day 142 pages (s04-X142av2, RI.1.8)

Read aloud page 3 of Marks Outside. Show three pictures: birds flying past a dark building at night, children with paper windows, an empty room. Say: "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The birds flying past the dark building: with the lights off, birds are not pulled toward the glass.

## Day 142 pages (s04-X142bv2, RI.1.8)

Page 3 of Marks Outside stays open; reread it if the child asks. Say: "Say what the writer wants." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Turn off lights that are not needed at night.

## Day 142 page (s04-X142c, RI.1.10)

Heard in the rotation, not on a card. Show page 3 of *Windows Birds Can See*, the picture of the window with dots. Say: "What do the dots tell the bird?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The glass is there; it is not open sky.


## Day 143 card (s04-X143a, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. Show the card and read it aloud: "A class asks how to keep marbles from rolling off a table. The first note says, 'A low rim keeps marbles in.' It has a page tag: marble tray book, page 2. The second note says, 'Marbles are round.' It has no page tag." Show three pictures: the first note with its page tag, the second note with no tag, a blank note. Say: "Which note names its page?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The first note.

## Day 143 card (s04-X143b, W.1.8)

This card is invented for practice. The same card stays in view; read it again if the child asks: "A class asks how to keep marbles from rolling off a table. The first note says, 'A low rim keeps marbles in.' It has a page tag: marble tray book, page 2. The second note says, 'Marbles are round.' It has no page tag." Say: "Which note answers the class's question?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The first note.

## Day 143 page (s04-X143c, W.1.8)

Heard in the rotation, not on a card. Show page 2 of *Marks Outside*. Say: "Say one way to put marks on a window." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Any way the page names: dots, lines, tape, stickers, cords, a screen or paint that washes off.


## Day 144 pages (s04-X144av2, RI.1.9)

Reread aloud page 3 of Nests Need Space and page 3 of Windows Birds Can See. Show three pictures: people standing far back from a nest tree, a window with dots, an empty nest. Say: "Which picture shows what the nest page asks people to do?" Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The people standing far back from the nest tree.

## Day 144 pages (s04-X144bv2, RI.1.9)

The same two pages stay open. Say: "Say one thing the window page asks people to do that the nest page does not." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Put marks on windows so birds can see the glass.

## Day 144 pages (s04-X144cv2, RI.1.9)

The same two pages stay open; reread them if the child asks. Say: "Say one thing the two pages both do." Hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** Both tell how people can protect birds.
