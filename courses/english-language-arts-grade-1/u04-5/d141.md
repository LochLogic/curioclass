# Day 141: Start my window bird portrait

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six and the catch-up group from the cycle record. Pin the task card where all can see it. Give each child a folder, the topic card and three strips. Keep the exit card face down. The sound-spelling map already holds oo, ee and final y from day 140.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Show the task card and read it aloud once, 2 minutes: a topic box, two fact boxes, a closing box and a paper window with a paper bird. Say: "This is our window bird portrait. We make it on paper windows, due on day 152. Nobody needs a window or a yard at home." Review: a drawn plan (kindergarten, day 7) and the paragraph frame: topic, fact, fact, closing (unit 1, day 21). **Ask.** "What do you wonder about birds and windows? Start with I wonder." Pairs, 60 seconds; hear three pairs, 20 seconds each (review: kind reply, ask I wonder, day 140). Last, 1 minute: for the children whose day 140 exit gave a liking in place of a reason (error B, s04-X140b), say a made-up pair, "Stack the chairs. A stack leaves room to walk," and ask them to say the reason back.

#### 15 min. Read

Before reading, 2 minutes: glass (point to the window) and toward (moving to it; fly toward the door). Then read *Windows Birds Can See*, pages 1 to 3, whole and aloud, with the pictures, 3 minutes. Reread page 1 with its picture, 4 minutes: the class points to the glass, the reflection and the bird on the labelled drawing, a few children at a time. Say that a reflection is what glass shows back, like a puddle. Reread page 3 with its marks picture, 3 minutes: point to the marks. Show the page 2 picture, 2 minutes: bright lights inside a building at night can pull birds toward the windows. Say the number once: more than a billion is a very big number of birds.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time with the turn card, 90 seconds, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each. The six heard are spread over the three asks. Pass is allowed.

1. **Ask.** "What does the bird see in the glass?" Expect: trees and sky, the reflection.
2. **Ask.** "What can people put on a window?" Expect: marks, dots.
3. **Ask.** "Why does that help the bird?" Expect: the marks show the glass is there. A child who says "birds like dots" hears: "What does the page say the marks tell the bird?"

#### 20 min. Writing and language

Whole class, then alone. Put the topic card "bird-safe windows" on the board and read the three strips aloud, 3 minutes: reflection fools the bird; many birds hit glass; marks show the glass. Think aloud, 4 minutes: "The topic holds every fact, so the topic is bird-safe windows, not only marks. I choose two facts. Then a closing: Make glass visible." Touch each box of the task card as you say it. Each child puts the topic card on the plan and chooses two of the three strips, 3 minutes. Then each child draws the plan on the page: a topic box, two fact boxes with a small drawing each, a closing box, and writes one word label, glass or marks, 8 minutes. Partners then point to each box of the other's plan and say its name back, 2 minutes. The plan, the strips and the task card go in the portrait folder, kept to day 152.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture is the topic of Rafi's page?" ([[s04-X141a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file the folders, 2 minutes. The two spoken asks are heard in the rotation below, so heard work stays out of this slot. A lid alone or a shovel alone is error Y (one detail for the whole topic).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cub and cube: which has the long vowel sound? Then pin and pine. Blend /m/ /oo/ /n/. Say the vowel sound you hear in tree. Segment food.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 23, lesson 117. Review: oo says its sound in moon, ee says long e in tree, final y says long e in happy (day 140); touch each card on the map, 2 minutes. New today: three words for A Little Seed, 3 minutes. Little: lit and tle, the end says "tul". Under: un and der (er, day 126). Soon follows oo. Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it, 4 minutes: moon, soon, noon, food, pool; bee, see, deep, keep, tree. Keep feet and seed off the boards today.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set: m n o s t r e b d p, with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate moon, then tree, then "See the moon." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map oo, ee and final y, blend, read, spell, reread A Little Seed pages 1 to 3. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with A Little Seed pages 1 to 5, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with at least 20 seconds to spare. The other groups reread Pat's Birthday, chain and spell in pairs at stations. The heard six come one or two to a group; with the exit card in view, ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** "Say one fact from Rafi's page." ([[s04-X141b]])
- **Ask.** With the page 2 picture in view: "What pulls birds toward windows at night?" ([[s04-X141c]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. "Sandboxes are the best" is error X (praise in place of a fact).

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks, and each portrait folder checked for its plan.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the page aloud and draw the four plan boxes on a sheet. One child: read the three lines of the window page aloud, draw the plan together and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No page: say the idea.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Tell.** Say: "Glass can show trees and sky. A bird may fly toward it and hit the glass. Marks on the outside show the glass is there." **Say.** "What can people put on a window?" Expect: marks.
- **10 min. Plan.** Draw a topic box, two fact boxes and a closing box. **Say.** "Say your two facts." Expect: two lines from the telling.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "moon" and "tree."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X141a]] [[s04-X141b]] [[s04-X141c]] Read a short made-up page. Your child points to its topic and says one fact. Then your child says what pulls birds toward windows at night.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the read, the plan, the exit and the rotation. Longer: a child who finishes adds the third strip as a third fact box.

### Words and supports

Words: glass, reflection, toward, marks, topic, fact, closing. Frame: "The marks help because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the rotation tell you who can name the topic of a page, give a fact and say a window fact. Day 142 teaches the point and its reasons on the same window page. A child who chose one detail as the topic (error Y, s04-X141a) tests each strip against the topic on a fresh card first. A child who gave praise in place of a fact (error X, s04-X141b) is asked for something the page says.
