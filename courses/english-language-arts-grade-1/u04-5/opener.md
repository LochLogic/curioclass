# Section 4.5 opener

## What the section does

Days 141 to 144 launch the window bird portrait: a drawing and a short informative paragraph about bird-safe windows, built from two pages and made on paper windows. Day 141 launches it, day 142 teaches point and reason, day 143 gathers facts into an organizer with page tags, and day 144 adds a second way to help and compares it with safe windows. Nobody needs a window, a feeder or a yard at home, and no real glass is ever touched. Day 144 holds the fifth cycle sample.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The errors, in plain words

- **Y:** Names a detail as the main topic. On 149 test which topic holds all the page's details. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **X:** Writes praise instead of informative facts. On 157 replace nice with one verified protection fact. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **C:** Calls an unrelated fact a supporting reason. On 145 test whether the fact explains the stated point. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **D:** Asks a question unrelated to word meaning. On 142 model a meaning question about a highlighted word. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **AA:** Offers an unrelated reading response. On 157 return to the line before answering anew. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **W:** Records an unsupported research fact. On 157 replace it with a detail located on a named page. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.
- **S:** Gives only a likeness in an information comparison. On 157 find what one page adds to the other. If many miss, use five to ten group minutes within the allocated response.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
