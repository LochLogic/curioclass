# Window bird portrait: task card

A drawn plan like the kindergarten plans, one large card for the board and one small copy for each child's portrait folder. Five boxes in a column, each with a plain drawing and one printed label: topic (bird-safe windows), fact, fact, closing, and a picture of a paper window with marks and a paper bird. A line at the foot reads "due day 152, made on paper windows". You read the card aloud once; children do not read it. The card stays in the portrait folder to day 152. It shows the shape of the task, not a finished portrait.
