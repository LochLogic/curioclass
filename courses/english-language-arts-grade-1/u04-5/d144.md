# Day 144: A second way to help birds

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six, the catch-up group and the six children for the cycle sample (the heard six). Keep the two sample words from the sample card in your folder. Lay out the Both and But sheets. Keep the exit pictures face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 143's exit (error W, s04-X143a), 3 minutes. If many missed, repeat with a second made-up page (the Ant Page, page 2: "An ant carries food") for 2 minutes, taken from the first Talk turn. Say: "This page is made up for practice. It is the Bean Page, page 3: A bean needs water to grow." Show a note, "Beans need water," with no tag. Say the class finds the line on the page, names the page and you stick the tag. Show a second note, "Beans are green." Pairs look for the line; none is there, so the note gets the word check (review: a note with no line gets the word check, unit 3, day 100). Then, 2 minutes: children take out the organizer and each points to their two notes and their two page tags. Point to the portrait task card and say today's question: "How else do people help birds?" A second way is more to gather for our portrait.

#### 15 min. Read

Reread *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes*, pages 1 and 5, 3 minutes, and *Nests Need Space*, page 5, 2 minutes; point to the hole and the nest box on the pictures. Then read *Feeders Need Adult Care*, pages 1 to 3, aloud once as information only, 4 minutes. Say: "An adult does this job. Children never clean a feeder." Do not compare it with the bluebird account today. Teach two words, 3 minutes: cavity (a hole in a tree) and nest box. Last, 3 minutes: children point to the hole and the box on the pictures and name what each shows.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time with the turn card, 60 seconds, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the asks. Pass is allowed.

1. **Ask.** "What do nest boxes give birds?" Expect: a place to nest.
2. **Ask.** "What do window marks do for birds?" Expect: they show the glass so a bird does not hit it.
3. **Ask.** "Which one gives birds a place to nest, window marks or nest boxes?" Expect: nest boxes.

#### 20 min. Writing and language

Alone, with you walking. First, 6 minutes: note 3 on the organizer, below the dotted line. Say the fact from page 1 of *Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes*: when there are not enough holes, people can build nest boxes. Each child draws a small box, prints the label box, and sticks the tag Volunteers Build Bluebird Boxes with 1 beside it. Then, 14 minutes, the Both and But sheet (review: Both ___. But ___., days 124, 131 and 135). Model, 4 minutes: draw window marks in the first slot and a nest box in the second; say "Both protect birds. But marks keep birds from hitting glass and boxes give birds a place to nest." Children lay the three picture cards (helps birds, keeps birds from hitting glass, gives birds a place to nest) in the Both and But places, then say your sentence back to a partner, 6 minutes. Each child draws the two pictures in the slots and prints one word label for each, marks and box, 4 minutes. A child who says only "both are for birds" where a difference was asked is error S; ask what each page adds.

#### 5 min. Exit

Reread aloud page 3 of Nests Need Space and page 3 of Windows Birds Can See and show the three pictures from the card sheet, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture shows what the nest page asks people to do?" ([[s04-X144av2]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file the notes and sheets, 2 minutes. The spoken asks are heard in the rotation below. The window with dots or the empty nest is error AA.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Review. Clap and count the parts of windy, beehive and sunny. Blend /b/ /oo/ /m/. Segment tree.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 23, lesson 120, cycle progress sample. Whole class, 3 minutes: chain on small boards, 30 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it: boom, food, seen, need, funny, windy. Then read the word cards for A Little Seed, 1 minute: little, soon, every, one, really, anymore. Then 6 minutes: the rest chain the same words in pairs, each partner checking the other's board, while the six heard children come to your table one at a time, 35 seconds each (about 3.5 minutes): read the first sample word; take the card away; the child writes the second word on the board as you say it.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. All children write the dictated sentence "The bee is in the sunny treetop." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes. It is not scored.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map oo, ee and final y, blend, read, spell, reread A Little Seed. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with A Little Seed pages 1 to 10 reread for expression, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with at least 20 seconds to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the two pages open: [[s04-X144bv2]]
- **Ask.** With the two pages open: "Say one thing the two pages both do." ([[s04-X144cv2]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread a page once), not yet seen. "Both help birds" is error S.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, the cycle sample marks, the cycle record for days 141 to 144 and the exit marks. The record goes to the day 145 regrouping.

### Other rooms

No printer: say the pages aloud and draw two slots on a sheet. One child: tell the fact, draw the box and the marks, and say the Both and But sentence with a grown-up.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: fold a paper in half.

**Skip.** The sound work.

- **10 min. Tell.** Say: "When birds have too few holes in trees, people build nest boxes. Window marks help birds not hit glass." **Say.** "What do nest boxes give birds?" Expect: a place to nest.
- **10 min. Draw.** Draw marks on one half and a box on the other. **Say.** "Both help birds. But ..." Expect: marks help a bird see glass; a box gives a nest place.
- **5 min. Words.** Print "sunny."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X144av2]] [[s04-X144bv2]] [[s04-X144cv2]] Read page 3 of Nests Need Space and page 3 of Windows Birds Can See. Your child points to the picture, says one thing the window page adds, and says one thing both pages do.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep note 3, the sentence and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes tells the partner one more way marks and boxes are different.

### Words and supports

Words: nest box, cavity, marks, protect, place, both, but. Frame: "Both ___. But ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the rotation tell you who can say what a page asks people to do, say what one page adds that the other does not, and say what two pages share. Day 145 reads why plants need birds and tests reasons. A child who gave a likeness where a difference was asked (error S, s04-X144bv2) names one thing each part adds on a fresh card first. The cycle 23 sample and the cycle record go to the day 145 regrouping.
