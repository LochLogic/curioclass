# Day 142: What the writer wants and why

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the heard six and the catch-up group. Set out each pair's point card, three strips and word cards. Write the two practice strips as you will say them, marked invented for practice. Keep the exit card face down.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Repair for day 141's exit (error Y, s04-X141a), 3 minutes. If many missed, add a second pair of strips (a bike: "The bike has a bell." "The bike has two wheels.") for 2 minutes, taken from the first Talk turn. Say: "These are made up for practice." Put two strips on the board, "The swing has a seat." and "The swing has two chains.", beside two labels, swing and chains. Pairs test each strip against each label: "Does this fact fit under it?" The label that holds every strip is the topic, 90 seconds. Hear two pairs. A child who gave praise in place of a fact on day 141 (error X, s04-X141b) hears: "What does the page say?" Then say, 2 minutes: "Yesterday our plan had a topic and facts. Today the page gives a point and its reasons."

#### 15 min. Read

Reread *Windows Birds Can See*, pages 1 to 3, aloud, 3 minutes. Teach two words, 3 minutes: a point is what the writer wants; a reason is a fact that tells why. Review: point and reason strips, and the because test (days 132 and 133). Show the point card and read the last line of page 3: "Making windows visible is a way to protect birds." Think aloud, 6 minutes, with the strips: "Making windows visible protects birds because a bird sees trees in the glass. That tells why: a reason. Marks show a bird that the glass is there. Does that tell why? Yes, another reason." Place both under the point card. Reread page 1 and its picture, 3 minutes, so the class sees the trees in the glass.

#### 15 min. Talk

Partners take two turns each time with the turn card, 60 seconds, then two children are heard, 40 seconds each, so the six heard are spread over the asks. Pass is allowed.

1. **Ask.** "Finish the point: The writer wants windows to be ___." Expect: visible, easy for birds to see.
2. **Ask.** "Finish it with a fact from the page: Marks help birds because ___." Expect: the marks show the glass is there.
3. Say: "This strip is invented for practice: Some windows have curtains." **Ask.** "Say yes or no: does it tell why?" Expect: no.
4. Say: "This strip is invented for practice: A bird hits glass because it sees trees." **Ask.** "Say yes or no: does it tell why?" Expect: yes.

#### 20 min. Writing and language

Pairs, then alone. First, 8 minutes. Each pair sorts three strips under the point card: A bird sees trees in the glass. Marks show a bird that the glass is there. Some windows have curtains (invented for practice, set aside in the corner). Each says the because test aloud for each strip before placing it. Walk between pairs; a strip that tells why stays, a strip that does not goes in the corner (error C). Then, 12 minutes, meaning questions. Review: the sentence beside a word tells what it means (day 121). Show the card for reflection with its yellow stripe and model: "What does reflection mean?" Say: not "What color is it?" Each child chooses a word card (glass, visible or reflection), asks a partner the meaning question, and the partner points to the page picture that answers it, 6 minutes. Hear three children, 40 seconds each (error D if the question is about a look, not a meaning). Each child draws one kept strip in the reason box on the page, and the strips go in the folder.

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is invented for practice." Read the card once from the card sheet and show its three pictures, 1 minute. All children mark a picture on the page, 1 minute. **Ask.** "Which picture shows the writer's reason?" ([[s04-X142a]]) Collect the pages, 1 minute. Children file the strips, 2 minutes. The spoken asks are heard in the rotation below. The red shoes are error C (an unrelated fact); the long empty hall is error AA (leaves the line).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say the last sound in happy, then in baby. Clap the two parts of baby, funny and windy. Blend /h/ /a/ /p/ /ee/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 23, lesson 118. Review: final y says long e in happy and windy (day 140); y at the end of my and fly says long i (unit 2, day 44). Touch both cards, 2 minutes. New today, 3 minutes: funny, baby and tiny split into two parts, y says /ee/; the first part of baby and tiny says its own name. And two words for A Little Seed: every (ev and ry) and one (o says "wu"). Chain on small boards, 20 seconds a word, saying each, tapping the sounds and writing it, 3 minutes: sun, sunny, wind, windy, fun, funny. Then read and clap baby and tiny, 2 minutes.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Dated printing set with your written feedback, 5 minutes. Dictate "The baby is happy." whole, then a word at a time, 5 minutes.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The catch-up group first, 10 minutes: the six children the cycle record names; map y as long e, blend, read, spell, reread A Little Seed pages 1 to 5. Then four groups of six at your table, 5 minutes each, with A Little Seed pages 1 to 8, each child heard in turn for 40 seconds, so each group ends with at least 20 seconds to spare. The other groups reread, chain and spell in pairs at stations. The heard six come one or two to a group; ask each, 15 seconds:

- **Ask.** With the exit card in view: "Say what the writer wants." ([[s04-X142b]])
- **Ask.** With the page 3 picture in view: "What do the dots tell the bird?" ([[s04-X142c]])

Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the card once), not yet seen. "Halls are long" is error AA.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Heard-six column, cycle record, the exit marks.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the strips on scraps and say the pages aloud. One child: say the point, find the reason strip and answer the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: say each line aloud.

**Skip.** The sound chains.

- **10 min. Point.** Say: "A writer wants windows easy for birds to see. Marks show the glass." **Say.** "What does the writer want?" Expect: windows easy to see.
- **10 min. Because.** **Say.** "Finish: Marks help because ..." Expect: they show the glass is there.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "baby" and "happy."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X142a]] [[s04-X142b]] [[s04-X142c]] Read a short made-up page. Your child points to the reason picture, says what the writer wants, and says what dots on a window tell a bird.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the worked example, one sort and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes writes one more reason strip from the page.

### Words and supports

Words: point, reason, reflection, visible, glass, tiny. Frames: "The writer wants ___." "It tells why because ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow

The exit and the rotation tell you who can pick the reason, say what the writer wants and say what the marks tell a bird. Day 143 gathers facts from two pages into an organizer. A child who took an unrelated fact for the reason (error C, s04-X142a) tests whether it tells why on a fresh card first. A child who left the line (error AA, s04-X142b) goes back to the line before answering anew.
