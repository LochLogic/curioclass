# Fact strips and topic card

One topic card and three fact strips, large print, one sentence each, read aloud by you. Children order the strips under the topic card and choose two.

- Topic card: bird-safe windows
- Strip 1: Glass can show a reflection, and a bird may fly toward it.
- Strip 2: Many birds hit glass.
- Strip 3: Marks show a bird that the glass is there.

A closing strip, "Make glass visible.", is used only in your thinking aloud. All three strips come from *Windows Birds Can See* (g1-u4-03). They stay in each child's portrait folder.
