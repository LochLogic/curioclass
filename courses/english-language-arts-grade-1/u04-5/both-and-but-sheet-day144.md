# Both and But sheet: marks and nest boxes

The Both ___. But ___. frame from day 124, one per child, with two picture cards to set on it: window marks and nest boxes (from *Windows Birds Can See*, *Nests Need Space* and the bluebird account). The sheet gives only the two frame lines and two empty picture slots. Children say the likeness and the difference aloud and write one word label for each.
