# Research organizer

One page per child. A question box at the top ("How can people keep windows safe for birds?"), two note boxes each with room for a small drawing and a word label, and a small printed page-tag box beside each note. A third note box is added on day 144 below a dotted line. Tags are printed on peel-off labels: "Windows Birds Can See" and "Marks Outside", each with a page number to write. The organizer is the base of the day 149 plan.
