# Day 104: Check the topic, the facts and the paragraph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep cards B1 and B2 and the cold passage Penguin Wings out of sight; the class has never heard Penguin Wings. Name the heard six. No help, no hints.

### The day

#### 6 min. Penguin Wings, then B1 and B2

Hand out the student page. Read Penguin Wings twice. Show three pictures: the penguin's wings, the water alone, the penguin's feet. Say: "Mark the picture that this text is mostly about." ([[s03-A22v2]]) Mark: shown, not yet seen. Then say: "These cards are made up for practice." Read B1 once: "An oystercatcher has a long, strong beak. Its beak pries open shells." Read B2 once: "An oystercatcher eats shellfish. Its strong beak pries the shells open."

#### 24 min. Notes and retelling

Each child makes a note from B1 and one from B2, labeled B1 and B2, alone. Call children one at a time, about 28 seconds each, Penguin Wings in view: "Tell me two things this text says." ([[s03-A23v2]]) Mark: shown, not yet seen. Record error A for the water or the feet named as the topic and error B for a fact the part does not give.

#### 30 min. Transfer paragraph

Say: "Write a paragraph about the oystercatcher's beak. Name the topic, give two facts and finish it." Cards B1 and B2 stay in view. Unscored.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say cut and cute; which has the long vowel sound? Blend /m/ /u/ /l/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of June. Segment flute.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Explicit teaching: u_e and e_e are new. The silent e makes the u say its name (cut, cute) and the e say its name (Pete). Add both to the map. Map cute, mule, June, Pete, flute, one vowel sound each. Decode, then spell, cute and June.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print P p and E e. Dictate: "Pete has a mule." No repair.

#### 30 min. Rotation

No catch-up group. Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table, one minute a child: reread Mike and Dave Hike and spell a taught word. The rest reread, chain and sort in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the sheets and paragraphs. Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet. Score out of class, from the folders: the paragraph revised on day 103 ([[s03-A19]]), the shared note from day 97 ([[s03-A20]]) and the two-source note ([[s03-A21]]). List each miss by name for day 106.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the boxes on paper. One child: do each item aloud, no help.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: use a short bird page with two facts about its beak.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment, topic and details.** Read Penguin Wings twice, with no help. **Say.** "What is it mostly about? Tell me two things it says." Expect: the penguin's wings, then two facts the text gives.
- **15 min. The assessment, writing.** Your child prints a topic sentence, two facts and a closing, with no help.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "cute," "mule" and "June."
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says where each fact was found.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, fact, closing, source. A scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who can name the topic, retell two facts and write them. Each miss waits for day 106. Day 105 finishes the paragraph and traces six routes in a new text, Woodpeckers.
