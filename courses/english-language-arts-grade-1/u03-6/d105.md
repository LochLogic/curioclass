# Day 105: Finish the paragraph and trace the routes

## Teacher page

### Before class

No help, no hints.

### The day

#### 5 min. Setup

Hand out the student page, the trace sheet, Beaks Find Food, pages 1 to 3, the printed menu and cards B1 and B2. Give back each paragraph begun on day 104; day 104's assessment is scored out of class.

#### 15 min. Finish the transfer paragraph

Say: "Finish your paragraph about the oystercatcher's beak." Unaided. Collect it; it is scored out of class ([[s03-A57]]). Record error E for no facts and error F for a list that stops.

#### 40 min. Feature trace in Beaks Find Food

Read Strong Beaks for Seeds, page 2, aloud once. Show page 1, the contents; page 2, the heading and the diagram with its label beak; page 3, the glossary; and the printed menu with its bird icons and book icon. Say: "On your paper, trace the routes to the sparrow's seed fact: the contents entry, the heading, the label, the glossary line, the menu choice and the icon." ([[s03-A24v2]]) Then: "Tell me what each route finds." ([[s03-A24bv2]]) Review: this tracing, from day 86. Ten minutes, each child alone, six paper traces. Then six groups of five at your table, 5 minutes each, one minute a child: each child explains the routes while the others finish. Mark: shown for all six routes traced to the seed fact; not yet seen otherwise. Record error D for the cover only.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say tub and tube; which has the long vowel sound? Blend /r/ /u/ /l/. Give the first, vowel and last sound of tune. Segment prune.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 17, lesson 87, review of day 104, familiar patterns: i_e, o_e, u_e and e_e. Map hole, mule, cute and June, one vowel sound each. Decode, then spell, hole and mule. No repair.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print L l and T t (every letter form was taught by day 27). Dictate: "Luke has a tune." No repair.

#### 30 min. Rotation

No catch-up group. Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table, one minute a child: read Cones and Tubes, a page each, for the first time after day 104's patterns, and spell a taught word. The rest reread Mike and Dave Hike and sort closed and silent e cards in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the sheets. Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet. List each miss by name for day 106.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six boxes on paper. One child: trace aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed pages: read Beaks Find Food from its page and draw the menu.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment, finish.** Your child finishes the paragraph from day 104 with a closing, with no help. **Say.** "Tell me your closing." Expect: a sentence that goes back to the beak.
- **15 min. The assessment, the route.** **Say.** "Show me the contents entry, the heading, the label and the glossary line, and tell me what each finds." Expect: a finger on each, with no help.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "Luke has a tune."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the traces. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, label, glossary, menu, icon. A scribe and a home language count.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who can finish a paragraph and trace a route alone. Each miss is listed on day 106 and repaired on day 107. Day 106 opens with the repair for day 103's exit and shares the research paragraph.
