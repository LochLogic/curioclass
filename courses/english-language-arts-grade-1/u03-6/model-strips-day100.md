# Day 100 model strips

The model paragraph, four sentences, each on its own paper strip, with three labels (topic, facts and closing) to slide the strips under.

1. A hawk's beak helps it get food.
2. The hawk has a hooked beak.
3. The hooked beak tears meat into small bits.
4. That is how a hawk's beak gets its food.

Strip 1 goes under topic, strips 2 and 3 under facts, strip 4 under closing. Keep one extra strip, "I like hawks. They look fierce.", to show a liking is not a fact. The strips come from Beaks Find Food, Hooked Beaks for Meat (g1-u3-01). Credit: adapted from EL G1 M3 U2, our own words.
