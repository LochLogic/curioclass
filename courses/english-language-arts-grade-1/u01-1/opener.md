# Section 1.1 opener: tools that help us work

## What the section does

Days 1 to 4 open the unit's question: how do tools help people work? The class hears three short articles about builders, farmers and cooks, asks and answers questions about them, tells which facts the words give and which the pictures show, and sorts tools by kind. Day 1 also holds an unscored placement screen: you listen to each child say a short sentence and point to the print of its first word. Nothing on day 1 is graded.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a retrieval repair, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A fact has a source.** Some facts are only in the words and some are only in the picture. Say "the words tell" or "the picture shows".
- **A question the text can answer is a real question.** Point back to the sentence that gives the answer.
- **A tool is a thing with a kind and a use.** "A ladle is a kitchen tool for soup." Say the kind and the use together.
- **Everyone may pass.** Public talk is never a test of one child.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, AI:** a detail is invented, or a fact is given to the wrong source. Cover the picture, reread the sentence and ask which fact is still there.
- **V, U:** a tool is called only "a thing", or a food is sorted as a tool. Add the kind and the use.
- **R:** a line is read one word at a time. Clap each phrase, then say it.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
