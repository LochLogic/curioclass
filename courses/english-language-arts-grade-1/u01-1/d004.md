# Day 4: What is a tool?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print Tools in the Kitchen with its picture. Hang the eye and ear signs. Lay out the picture cards for the exits, the topic card and detail cards from day 2, the sort cards: tool words and picture symbols (the six kitchen cards) with the two hoops (tools, not tools), the two school tool cards, the exit card with its two pictures and the place signs. Today's six are the next six names on the record sheet.

### The day

#### 15 min. Read the kitchen article

Whole class on the rug. Read Tools in the Kitchen whole, once (158 words, about 80 seconds), with the picture shown. Content note: a cook, a hot pot of soup, no child handles anything hot.

Say a line from the article: "A ladle is a kitchen tool for soup." Say: "This sentence tells two things. It says the kind, a kitchen tool. It says what it is for, soup." Write it on chart paper and put a finger under each word.

**Ask.** Which category does the ladle belong to? Expect: kitchen tools. Review: you learned yesterday that a category is a group of things that go together. Every child says "kitchen tool" together.

Model one question and its answer. Ask yourself: "What does the funnel do?" Then touch the funnel paragraph and read: "The cook sets the narrow end in a small bottle and pours oil into the wide top." Say: "I asked, and the words answered."

#### 10 min. Reread and question

First 4 minutes, a repair for the day 3 exit (error B). Reread two kitchen facts: "The long handle keeps the cook's hand away from the hot pot." Say: "Did the picture tell this, or the words?" Then point to the white apron in the picture. Say: "Did the picture tell this, or the words?" Children point to the ear for the handle and the eye for the apron. Say it back: "The words tell the handle; the picture shows the apron."

Then 6 minutes in pairs. Partner A asks one question about a kitchen tool ("What does the rolling pin do?"); partner B points to its sentence in the large print. Swap. Fifteen pairs talk at once; sit with one pair. A child may pass.

#### 10 min. Sort and talk

Pairs lay out the six kitchen cards: ladle, rolling pin, funnel, dough, soup and oil. Read each aloud. They sort them into the hoops, tools and not tools, and say one use for each tool ("A ladle lifts soup"). Dough, soup and oil are what the cook works with, not tools (error U). Say a wrong sort back as a question: "Does the cook use soup to do a job, or work on the soup?" (6 minutes.)

Then show the eraser and the pencil sharpener. Each pair names one use for one of them (2 minutes). Say: "These tools are in our room." Tools stay in the room; nobody names a tool from home. Hear three pairs say it to the class, 40 seconds each (2 minutes).

#### 20 min. Define a tool

Hand out the student page. Say: "Draw a tool from the three articles. Then say what it is." Draw for 8 minutes. Say the pattern together: "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___." Model: "A hoe is a farm tool for weeds."

Each child fills the pattern and says it to a partner (3 minutes). Then each says it to you; you hear about 10 children at 40 seconds each while the others add color to the drawing (7 minutes). Write one word label under the drawing; a child may say it to a scribe. The look-fors are: names a category; gives a use taken from the text; the label matches the drawing. Mark on the record sheet. A child who says "A hoe is a thing" has said a tool is a thing (error V).

#### 5 min. Exit

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Read: "A whisk is a kitchen tool. Its wires mix the eggs." Show the two pictures: a whisk and a spoon. Say: "What is a whisk? Use the card." Hear today's six, 20 seconds each. Mark each: shown, shown with help or not yet seen ([[s01-X4]]). A good answer names the kind and the use. A child who says only "a whisk" has named it without saying what it is.

#### 5 min. Oral sound work

Review: first and last sounds and segmenting are kindergarten work.

- Segment and count the sounds in lap (3), fish (3: /f/ /i/ /sh/) and mat (3). The class holds up a finger for each sound.
- Say the first sound, then the last sound, of lap, fish and mat.

#### 10 min. Shared writing

Compose one sentence together about a tool: "A ladle is a kitchen tool for soup." Write it on chart paper, saying each word. Children say the first sound of tool, /t/, and print t in the air, then on their page. You finish the line. Review: the first word starts with a capital and the sentence ends with a dot.

#### 10 min. Retrieval repair, letters, dictation

Repair first, only if the record shows a gap: that small group works up to 10 minutes. Otherwise teach C and c.

Say: "C is a curl, open on the right. The little c is a smaller curl." Children trace C and c in the air, then print them on the student page. Then dictation: say "a cat" and children print it, a word at a time. Check as they go.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Six groups of five, six places, 5 minutes each. At the teacher table a group reads Sam sat a third time: "Sam sat. Sam sat at a mat." Each child reads one line alone (about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes in all). The other five places: sound cards, word building, draw and label, tool pictures, quiet pairs. The chime moves everyone one place along.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Mark the record sheet. Name tomorrow's six. Collect the pages.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six cards by hand and read the article from the page. One child: read the article, sort the six cards, fill the frame and do the exit; skip the rotation.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed article: read it from the page. No hoops: use two bowls.

**Skip.** The rotation and the class talk.

- **5 min. Read the kitchen article.** Read it with the picture.
  **Say.** "What is a ladle?" Expect: a kitchen tool for soup.
- **5 min. A question.** Your child asks one question about a kitchen tool and points to the sentence.
- **5 min. Sort.** Sort ladle, rolling pin, funnel, dough, soup and oil into tools and not tools.
- **5 min. Draw.** Your child draws a tool and says "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___."
- **5 min. Sounds.** Say lap, fish, mat slowly, one sound at a time.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4]] Read the card, show the two pictures and say: "What is a whisk? Use the card."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the school tools in Sort and talk and hear only five children say the frame. Keep the article, the frame and the exit. Longer: a child who finishes says the frame about a second tool.

### Words and supports

Words: tool, category, kitchen, ladle, rolling pin, funnel. Frame: "A ___ is a ___ tool for ___." Pointing, drawing and a home language count as answers. Say the English back once. Read every card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 reads the hammer poem with expression and connects its words to the tool articles. If many children said a tool is just a thing on today's exit (error V), day 5's join part opens with four minutes: say a tool is a thing, then add its kind and its use.
