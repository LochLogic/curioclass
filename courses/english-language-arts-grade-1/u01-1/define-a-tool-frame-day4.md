# Day 4 define-a-tool frame

A page with a drawing box and the pattern, read aloud by you, as a picture frame: "A ______ is a ______ tool for ______." The child draws a tool from the three articles, says the frame to a partner and then to you, and writes one word label under the drawing. A child may point or speak to a scribe.
