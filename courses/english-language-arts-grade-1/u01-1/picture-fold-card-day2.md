# Day 2 picture fold card

A sheet of paper folded in half. On the left half, the picture of the builders from the article. On the right half, a blank box. Children point to a fact in the picture, fold the sheet to hide it, and say what the words alone tell. Mark "the picture shows" with a small eye and "the words tell" with a small ear.
