# Day 3 sort cards: two sources

Two groups of cards on the farm article. **Picture-only cards:** a straw hat, a red tractor, a brown dog, green rows of plants. **Words-only cards:** "The long handle lets the farmer stand tall" and "The rose breaks the water into drops". Two hoops are drawn on paper: one with a small eye, one with a small ear. Pairs sort each card into the picture hoop or the words hoop and say how they know. Print "from the article, Tools on the Farm" in small letters on the back of each card.
