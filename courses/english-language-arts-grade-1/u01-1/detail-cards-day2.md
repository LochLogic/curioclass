# Day 2 topic card and detail cards

One topic card: a picture of a builder at a half-built wall, with the words "A builder uses tools". Four small detail cards, each a picture of one fact from the article: the flat end of a hammer pushing a nail, the claw pulling a bent nail, the bubble in the middle of a level, the wheel of a wheelbarrow. Pairs lay each detail card under the topic card and say what it tells. Print "from the article, Builders at Work" in small letters at the foot of the topic card.
