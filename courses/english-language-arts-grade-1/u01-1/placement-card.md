# Day 1 placement card

Unscored. Read aloud: "A jar holds pencils. The jar sits on a mat." Show the printed sentence in large letters. Say: "Point to the first word." Then: "Say the sentence back in your own words." Note on the record sheet what the child pointed to and said. This card is made up for practice. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot.
