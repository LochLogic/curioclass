# Day 4 kitchen sort cards

Six picture cards: ladle, rolling pin, funnel, dough, soup, oil. Two hoops are drawn on paper: "tools" and "not tools". Pairs sort the cards and say one use for each tool. Print "from the article, Tools in the Kitchen" in small letters on the back of each card.
