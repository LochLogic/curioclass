# Exit cards, days 1 to 4

Say aloud each time: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of every card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

## Day 1 card (s01-X1, RI.1.7)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A brush moves dust into a pan. The floor becomes clean." Show two pictures: a brush beside a dustpan, and a pencil beside a book. Ask: "Which picture shows the work in these words?" Every child points to or marks a picture; hear the day's rotating six.

**Right answer:** The brush and dustpan picture. A child who says why: they move the dust so the floor is clean.

## Day 2 card (s01-X2, RI.1.1)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A builder uses a ladder. Its rungs let her climb up to the roof." Show two pictures: a ladder against a roof, and a ladder lying on grass. Say: "Ask me one question that these words can answer." Hear the day's rotating six; answer by pointing to the sentence.

**Right answer:** Any question the card answers, such as What do the rungs let her do? or Where does she climb? The teacher answers by pointing to the sentence.

## Day 3 card (s01-X3, RI.1.6)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A farmer uses a bucket to carry water." Show the picture: a yellow bucket beside a pair of boots. Say: "Tell me one thing the picture shows that the words do not say."

**Right answer:** The bucket is yellow, or there are boots: any fact only the picture shows.

## Day 4 card (s01-X4, L.1.5b)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A whisk is a kitchen tool. Its wires mix the eggs." Show two pictures: a whisk, and a spoon. Say: "What is a whisk? Use the card."

**Right answer:** A kitchen tool with wires for mixing eggs (the category and the use, in either order).
