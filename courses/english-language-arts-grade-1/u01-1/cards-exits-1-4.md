# Exit card, day 4

Say aloud: "This card is made up for practice." Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the foot of the card. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no printed words except the foot line. Children do not read the cards; you read the lines aloud.

Days 1 to 3 exits use passages of Tools at Work (texts/g1-u1-02.md), reread aloud with the article picture; they need no card.

## Day 4 card (s01-X4, L.1.5b)

This card is invented for practice. Read aloud: "A whisk is a kitchen tool. Its wires mix the eggs." Show two pictures: a whisk, and a spoon. Say: "What is a whisk? Use the card."

**Right answer:** A kitchen tool with wires for mixing eggs (the category and the use, in either order).
