# Day 86 card A, teacher-held

Card A is the three pages of Duck at the Pond (texts/g1-u3-01), with the day 85 printed screen. On day 86 the class has not yet heard it; read only the part headed Feathers aloud, once, and keep the pages in the folder until day 92, when the whole text is first read. Page 1 is the cover with its picture and the contents (Feathers, page 2; Glossary, page 3). Page 2 is the headings, the diagram with its label feathers, and the feather sentences. Page 3 is the glossary (dry: not wet). The printed screen is the day 85 menu with its choice Feathers and bird icon. Mark the day 89 repair groups from what the children trace. The student page carries a strip of three pictures with a ring box under each: a duck with its feathers, drops of water alone, and a duck's webbed foot. Children mark one picture for the topic item.
