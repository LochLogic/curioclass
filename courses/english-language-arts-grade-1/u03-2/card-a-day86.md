# Day 86 card A, teacher-held

Teacher-held for the mid-unit check on day 86. It is printed once, read aloud once and kept in the folder; it is not used as practice before or after. Page 1 is the cover with a picture and the contents. Page 2 is the heading, a label and two sentences. Page 3 is the glossary. The printed screen has a two-choice menu with small icons and a book icon. Read it aloud only on day 86, and mark the day 89 repair groups from what the children trace. The student page carries a strip of three pictures with a ring box under each: the owl's fringed wing feather beside a flying owl, a fringed edge alone, and a swimming bird. Children mark one picture for the topic item.
