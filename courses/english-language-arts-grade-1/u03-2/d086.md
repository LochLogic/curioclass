# Day 86: Check the topic, the details and the route

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep card A out of sight. Take down every feature card and printed screen. Name the heard six. This is a check: no teaching, no repair, no help.

### The day

#### 5 min. Setup and read card A

Say: "This card is made up for practice." Hand out the student page. Read card A once: "An owl's flight feathers have fringed edges. The fringe can make the owl's flight quieter." Say: "Mark the picture that the card is mostly about." ([[s03-A4]]) Children mark one of three pictures on the strip.

#### 15 min. RI.1.2

Card A stays in view. Call children one at a time, 30 seconds each: "Tell me the two things the card says." ([[s03-A5]]) Others draw any bird quietly, nothing from the card. Thirty turns fill the 15 minutes; a scribe may take some. Mark: shown, not yet seen. Record error A for the edge alone and error B for a fact the card does not give.

#### 20 min. RI.1.5, six paper traces

Show card A's pages and screen. Say: "On your sheet, trace the contents entry, the heading, the label, the glossary line, the menu choice and the icon. Then tell me the two facts and what fringe means." ([[s03-A6]]) Each child works alone. Mark: shown for five or six traces right and the facts found; not yet seen otherwise. Record error D for the cover only.

#### 20 min. Route talk

Six groups of five, 3 minutes each, explain their routes; the others check their own traces quietly.

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only, familiar words. Say pin and pine; the child says which has the long vowel sound. Blend /g/ /l/ /a/ /d/. Say crab and give its first, vowel and last sound. Segment twin.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Familiar words: reread basket, kitten, backpack, catfish, hotdog and hilltop. No repair.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Print H h and T t. Dictate catfish and hotdog. Children stretch, print and read back. No repair.

#### 30 min. Rotation

Five groups of six, 6 minutes each at your table, 60 seconds a child: reread Pam's Backpack and spell a familiar word. The catch-up group resumes on day 87. The rest reread and spell in pairs.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Collect the sheets. Move the marks and the heard-six column to the record sheet; list each miss by name and component for day 89.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pages on paper. One child: do each item aloud, no help.

### At home

**Swap.** No card: use a short bird page with a contents, a glossary and a label.

**Skip.** The rotation.

- **15 min. The assessment, topic and details.** Read a short bird page once, with no help. **Say.** "What is it mostly about? Tell me two things it says." Expect: the main topic, then two details the page gives.
- **15 min. The assessment, the route.** Your child points to the contents entry, the heading, the label and the glossary line, with no help, and says what the glossary word means.
- **10 min. Words.** Print "catfish" and "hotdog."
- **5 min. Talk.** Your child says how the contents led to the page.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the retelling and the six traces. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Words: contents, heading, label, glossary, menu, icon. Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count. Stems read twice.

### Tomorrow

The marks tell you who can name the topic, retell two details and trace a route alone. Each miss waits for day 89: error A, error B or error D. Day 87 opens with the day 85 repair.
