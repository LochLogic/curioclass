# Day 84 three-box connection page

A page of three boxes in a row: part picture, arrow, job picture. A line at the foot for one joining word (because or so) copied from the board. Modeled on the sparrow: the toes box, an arrow, the box with the sparrow on a branch. The child draws the part and the job of a bird of their choice from the article and copies the joining word.
