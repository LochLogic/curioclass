# Day 86 trace sheet

A sheet with six empty boxes in a row, each headed by a picture only: contents entry, heading, label, glossary line, menu choice, icon. Below, two lines for the facts and one line for the meaning of the glossary word. Each child traces the route in each box with a pencil, alone, and tells you the facts and the meaning. No help is given.
