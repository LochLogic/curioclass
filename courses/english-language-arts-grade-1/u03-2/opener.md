# Section 3.2 opener: feet that help, menus and the first check

## What the section does

Days 84 to 86 connect a body part to what it helps the bird do, then use a printed menu with small icons to find a fact. Day 86 is the mid-unit check: each child works alone on one invented card and its printed pages, and no help is given. The check marks are used on day 89 to repair what is needed.

## How the days run

Each day is 120 minutes in two blocks of 60. The topic block holds the reading, the talk, the work and a five-minute exit. The foundations block holds oral sound work, letters and sounds, a rotation of small groups and a short record. Every exit is spoken to you: you read the card aloud, the child points, sorts, draws or speaks, and you hear the day's six children. A child outside the six is "not yet heard", never "missed". A child may pass at any time.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A connection joins two facts.** A part is one fact, a job is another, and the arrow says so.
- **A menu is a contents page on a screen.** A choice leads to a page, and an icon says what kind of page it is.
- **A check shows what a child can do alone.** Nothing is taught during it.

## The errors, in plain words

- **B:** a claim the source does not give. Point to the exact source sentence first.
- **D:** the cover is used instead of the route. Follow the menu or contents to the heading.
- **A:** a detail is named as the topic in the retelling. Sort again on a fresh card.

## Marking

One scale holds all year: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Mark on the record sheet and keep the sheet in your folder.
