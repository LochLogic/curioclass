# Day 85 printed screen menu and icons

A printed screen with a frame line like a screen and four choices, each with a small bird icon: Beaks, Wings, Feathers, Feet. A book icon at the corner points to the glossary. Each choice leads to a page of the article it names; the printed copies of page 2 of Beaks Find Food, Wings in Motion and Feathers Protect are kept beside it so that pairs can trace a route with a finger (the model route goes to the hawk fact on the Beaks page; pairs trace the wing glide fact and the down fact). The Feet choice is left for the day 85 exit, whose own card holds its menu and page; no practice route goes to a foot fact. It does not show live electronic operation.
