# Day 85: Use a menu and its icons to find a fact

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the screen, large for the class and one for each pair, with the page 2 copies beside each. Draw a menu card and an icon card for the text-feature cards. Lay out the practice card, the turn cards and the exit pictures face down. Copy day 84's exit marks and name today's heard six. If the room has a shared screen, open one local page with a menu for the three-minute demonstration.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening repair

Say: "This is made up for practice." Show the practice card: "A bucket has a handle. The handle lets Mina carry the water." A child says: "The bucket's handle helps it float." Say the repair for error B from day 84's exit, in which a job the card does not give was said. Put a finger on the exact sentence, "The handle lets Mina carry the water." Say it with the class: "Its handle helps Mina carry water." Say the rule: "Point to the sentence first, then explain." If many gave a job the card does not give on day 84's exit (error B), take a second card, the same way: "A pot has a lid. The lid keeps the soup warm."

#### 5 min. Words

Show each word with its picture and have the class say it: menu (a list of choices on a screen), icon (a small picture that tells what kind of page a choice opens), screen. About 25 seconds a word. Show the printed screen: four choices, Beaks, Wings, Feathers and Feet, each with a small bird icon, and a book icon in the corner. Say: "A bird icon says a bird page. The book icon leads to the glossary."

#### 15 min. A printed menu, taught

Model aloud, with a finger on the large screen. "I want the hawk fact. First I choose the menu word, Beaks, and I see its bird icon. The choice leads to the page. On the page I find the heading, Hooked Beaks for Meat. Then I read the fact: The hooked beak tears meat into small bits." Point to each step as you name it: choice, icon, page, heading, fact. Hold up the menu card and the icon card beside the heading and contents cards. Say: "The menu is like a contents page on a screen."

Contrast: show the cover of Beaks Find Food. Say: "The cover shows the whole book. It tells me which book, not which page. To find the fact I follow the menu." Everyone says: "Choose, follow the icon, find the fact." If the room has a shared screen, show the same route on it once (3 minutes); the children watch and do not touch it. Without one, the printed screen is all the class needs.

#### 15 min. Pairs trace

Pairs trace two routes on their printed screen, each with a finger. The first: find the wing fact about gliding. The second: find the fact about down for warmth. For each, the pair names the choice, the icon, the page and the heading, then reads the exact sentence aloud with a finger under it, one partner at a time (about 4 minutes a route, then 2 minutes to reread each sentence). Walk among the pairs; hear four pairs, one minute each. A child who points to the cover has used the cover, not the menu (error D): put the cover away and begin again at the menu.

#### 10 min. Three questions

Pairs ask and answer these spoken questions, two turns each, read twice. The printed screen is in view.

1. "Which choice leads to the wing fact about gliding?" Expect: Wings, with its bird icon.
2. "What does the icon do?" Expect: it tells what kind of page the choice opens; a bird icon, a bird page; the book icon, the glossary.
3. "Which sentence tells you?" Expect: the sentence under the heading Wide Wings Glide, found with a finger.

Fifteen pairs take two turns of about 20 seconds at once; hear four pairs, one minute each.

#### 5 min. Talk

Partners say the route aloud with the turn card: "First I ___. Then I ___." (2 minutes, all pairs at once). Then hear six pairs, 30 seconds each (3 minutes). The listening partner says whether the cover was used.

#### 5 min. Exit

Use today's printed screen. The others color their route sheet; the heard six come to your table, 40 seconds each (4 minutes). Show the three pictures: the menu choice Feet with its bird icon, the menu choice Beaks with its bird icon, the cover of Feet That Help. Say: "Point to the menu choice and icon that lead you to the fact about webbed feet." ([[s03-X85av2]]) Then reread aloud the part that choice opens, Feet for Swimming, and ask: "What does this page tell you about the duck's feet?" ([[s03-X85bv2]]) Mark each: shown, shown with help (you reread the part once), not yet seen. A child who points to the cover has used the cover, not the menu (error D).

#### 5 min. Oral sounds

Spoken only. Say tap and tape; **Ask.** "Which has the long vowel sound?" Expect: tape. Blend /s/ /t/ /e/ /p/, "step". Say blot and the class says the first sound, the vowel sound and the last: /b/, /o/, /t/. Segment frost: /f/ /r/ /o/ /s/ /t/.

#### 10 min. Map and spell

Cycle 13, lesson 67. First map the word she, for the reader: write it, say it, and show that sh says /sh/ and the e says /ee/; children trace it in the air. Review: she was mapped in kindergarten; the words a, the, is and has are mapped already. Then connect the spellings to words: chop each compound word on the board and read the parts: back | pack, cat | fish, sun | set, can | not. Children clap and read each with you. Spell catfish part by part on the page. The reader Pam's Backpack is read for the first time in the rotation today, now that its words are mapped.

#### 10 min. Letters, handwriting and dictation

Children decode, then spell, two compound words from the reader: hilltop and hotdog. Print the pairs P p and H h. Dictate: "Pat has a backpack." Say it whole, then a word at a time; has and a are mapped words. Children stretch each word, print it and read it back. Repair for a missing vowel (error M): tap the sounds again.

#### 30 min. Rotation

The ten-minute catch-up group first: the six children the record names meet you to map, blend, read, spell and reread. Then four groups of six, 5 minutes each at your table, with Pam's Backpack: each child reads a page and spells a compound word on a card, 50 seconds each, one child at a time. The heard-six column names the six whose reading you write up in full. The rest work in pairs at stations: reread A Sunset Picnic, chain words with letter cards, build and chop compound words, trace a menu route again. Thirty children: six catch-up, four groups of six.

#### 5 min. Organize and record

Move the exit marks, the route notes, the cycle record and the heard-six column to the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer or screen: draw the printed screen on the board with four choices and a small bird beside each, and keep the page 2 copies on the table. One child: trace one route with a finger, say it aloud, and do the exit aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed screen: draw a menu of three choices with a small bird beside each. No second page: use the contents of a bird book.

**Skip.** The rotation, the stations and the shared screen.

- **10 min. A menu route.** Draw a menu with Beaks, Wings and Feet, each with a small bird icon. **Say.** "Which choice leads to the fact about ___? What does the icon tell you?" Expect: the choice, then a bird page; your child points to the sentence.
- **10 min. Say the route.** Your child says: "First I choose ___. Then I read the fact."
- **5 min. Words.** Spell catfish part by part, then print "Pat has a backpack."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X85av2]] [[s03-X85bv2]] Show the printed menu, then read Feet for Swimming. Your child points to the choice and icon for the webbed-feet fact, then says what the page tells.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: keep the model, one pair route, the exit and the sentence. Cut the third question. Longer: a child traces a third route, to a heading on the Feet page.

### Words and supports

Words: menu, icon, screen, route. Frames: "First I choose ___." "The icon tells ___." "The sentence says ___." Pointing, drawing, a scribe and a home language count; say the English back once.

### Tomorrow
