- [W] Survey records floodwater on this part of the land.
- [D] Owner reports that water stayed below the shed floor.

- A place with no symbol has no observation recorded here.

**Caption.** One survey after Bellwether's fall flood shows water on Mill Street, the playing field, and a low stretch of Patch Lane. The map combines surveyed locations and one owner's report.
