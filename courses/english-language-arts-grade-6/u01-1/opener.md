# Section 1.1: What Mara sees in a door

## Reading the opening trial

Readers follow Mara’s thoughts. They infer what other people care about from what they say and do. Her two drawings make the town’s choice visible. Keep talk on the fictional town and let students pass. Hang on to the complete opening packet. Day 2 starts from the beginning even if a student missed day 1. Use chapters 3 and 4 to finish the trial before settling a theme.

## Routines to review

Review exact quotation, inference and response. Keep the chapter and place next to each copied sequence of words. Review the story map and mark where two events connect. Ask whether a draft theme can account for the end of the trial. In sentence combining, preserve both complete thoughts. During partner rereading, listen for phrasing and expression. Invite a retry in a steady voice.

## Return and repair

For error A, put a statement beside its exact line. For error B, mark the words that could support the inference. For error C, compare a topic and a message about choices. For error E, return two released event labels to their order. For error F, name the job a detail does in the explanation. Share individual cues before comparison.

## Set out

Print the novella packet, quotation notes, episode map, paragraph frame, reading log, and a family note for day 1. Give the family note directly to any student age 18 or older. They make their own pass or consent choice within school rules. Keep separate half-sheet exit tickets and release passages. Use complete print beside any passage audio. Record which support a reader used.
