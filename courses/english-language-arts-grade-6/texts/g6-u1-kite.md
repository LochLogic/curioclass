# The Kite on the Fence

Nico carried his kite to the empty field behind the school. Near the fence, he let the string slip through his fingers. The kite dipped, climbed, and dipped again. Before he could pull it back, its paper tail caught on a low rail. He tugged once. The tail stretched tight, and a thin tear opened at the edge.

Nico set the reel on the grass and walked toward the fence. A loose loop of string dragged behind him. With the pull gone, the tail hung just low enough for him to lift it off the rail. He pressed the torn edges together, but they parted as soon as he let go. He wound the string around the reel and tucked the kite under his arm. On the walk home, he tried to remember where he had left the roll of tape.
