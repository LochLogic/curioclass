# Choosing words for a meeting

**Jo**

We was keeping that board.

**Ana**

For the meeting note I will write, We were keeping that board. The note has a formal audience.

## Teaching notes

Print the transcript beside the matching exchange. Ask which words fit the conversation and which fit the meeting note. Discuss audience and setting with respect for both speakers and their language varieties.
