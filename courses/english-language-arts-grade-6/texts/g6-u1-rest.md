# The Rest

1. The town musicians rehearsed in the uphill meeting room while the riverside room remained closed after the flood. Dev had learned the drum part for the reopening concert. At the first rehearsal he began before the others had settled their pages. His sticks hurried over the drumhead. When the flute should have answered, there was only a thin breath. Dev filled the silence with another roll.
2. "Again," said the leader. Dev played more firmly. He knew his part; he had practiced it against the kitchen clock all week. Across the circle, Mei lowered her flute. "I missed the entrance," she said. Dev tapped the count on his knee. The entrance seemed plain enough to him. He wondered whether she had practiced as much.
3. During the break, Dev returned for his scarf. Mei was playing alone. Without the drum, he could hear the long note that began her phrase, so quiet that the scrape of his shoe almost covered it. He stopped beside the doorway. Mei lowered the flute. "I wait to hear the space," she said. Dev looked at the sticks in his hand.
4. When rehearsal resumed, Dev asked to hear the flute phrase once before joining it. He laid his sticks across his knees and counted without tapping. Then they tried together. At the entrance he let his hands remain still. Mei's note crossed the circle. His next stroke came after it, steady enough for the other players to follow.
5. On concert evening Dev lost sight of Mei behind a row of music stands. The familiar entrance approached. He could have filled it; his hands already knew the roll. Instead he counted the silent beats. A flute answered from the far side of the stands, and Dev waited until its last note before raising his sticks. For the first time that evening, he heard the whole room.
