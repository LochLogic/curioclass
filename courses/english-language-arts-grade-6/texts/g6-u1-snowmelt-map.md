# One Survey of the Stream

North is at the top.

| Row | West | Center | East |
|---|---|---|---|
| North row | Hill | Stream [W] | Hill |
| Middle row | Hill | Stream [W] | Low field [W] |
| South row | Hill | Stream [W] | Low road [W] |

**Key**

The patterned outline marks where water reached in this survey. In the grid, [W] marks places inside the outline.

**Caption**

The outline shows land reached by a past flood. It does not predict the next flood.

### Row-ordered map description

- North row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Hill.
- Middle row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Low field [W].
- South row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Low road [W].
