# Section 1.5 opener. A choice with consequences

Read chapters 13 to 16 in order. The competing deposits change what the family can promise.
The closing drawings carry forward the level landing and show what an open door lets visitors do.
Keep discussion on the invented town. Invite students to pass in public talk.

Review narrator, the teller of a story, and dialogue, words spoken by characters.
Review reader, purpose, form from the day 11 writing. Context is the situation the reader needs.
A sequence is the order of events. Pacing lets a writer linger or move quickly.
Review partner rereading with a feedback card. Listen for phrasing and expression.

Give each student the complete chapter and the noticeboard model. Use the annotated sample and narrative guide
to mark specific moves. The widened bench follows a request. The touch on the painted border holds the moment
before the request. On day 18, issue the story map and source note. Keep the writer's own work descriptive.

Before class, print the day’s held exit and keep the exit rows’ look-fors with the teacher record.
Keep chapter 16 in two segments. Day 19 stops at the hinge. Day 20 starts with Ms. Idris.
If audio is unavailable, use complete print or a teacher reread. Record the reading conditions.

Use error G to cue an opening action, I to restore the narrative purpose and H to link an outcome to a choice.
With error F, reread the released line and ask what it lets the reader see. Check one repair per pupil.
Use the existing observation record for the day 20 cohort. Match names to the day 10 feedback and date each
prepared contribution, context and private makeup before transferring the record to day 30.
