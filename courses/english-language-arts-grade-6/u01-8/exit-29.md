# The tile in the wall

**A. Story.** Imani saved a chipped tile from the old reading room. In the rebuilt room, she set it into the wall at eye level where old readers could find it.

**B. Test note.** The chipped tile cracked when the mason pressed weight on it.

**C. Plan caption.** Wall recess for a display tile. A separate beam carries the weight above it.

What claim about rebuilding can you support with Imani’s choice and the builders’ evidence?
