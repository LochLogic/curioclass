# Day 32 reading repair slips

### Find the exact action

Sela stacked the folded tables beside the stage. Rin carried the music stands to the closet.

Underline the words that tell what went into the closet.

### Match a conclusion to its evidence

The custodian measured the inside temperature at noon. A note says the building stayed that warm all night.

Mark the words that tell when the measurement was made.

### Test a statement about what matters

The sign painter left space for an arrow. A visitor could find the stairs after the arrow went up.

Choose the statement the actions support. Circle it.

- Signs use paint.
- A small mark can help someone find the way.

### Keep what happened

Lee clumsily scraped the wet paint off the bench. Underline the word that judges the action.

### Keep events in order

After the crew tested the latch, they hung the door. Write 1 for the first action and 2 for the next.

___ Hung the door.  ___ Tested the latch.

### Explain what a detail does

The new hallway had no signs. Abdul set a lamp outside the open meeting room.

Mark the action that would help someone find the meeting.

### Pick a relevant detail

One note gives the color of the stage. Another says the side stairs are closed.

Circle the note you would read to decide where to enter.
