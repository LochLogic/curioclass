# Section 1.8 opener. What Bellwether keeps by changing

Start from the complete novella and the earlier drawing record. Review theme within one text, a message
supported by its events and details. Review partner rereading with a feedback card, using phrases and
expression at a comfortable pace. On day 28 pair different held cards after practice. Keep reading accuracy,
phrasing and the paper interpretation in their own fields. Use conference replacement turns for private
listens on days 29, 32, 35 and 36. Carry a named, dated cue if a pupil is absent.

On day 29 give each pair the released story and both flood explainer selections. Yu’s story supplies
a literary response to the problem. The federal explanations help identify processes and limits. Keep
evidence, infer, episode, consequence and theme attached to a source detail. Post the released excerpts
from course print. Read the original lines from paper if a network connection or projector is unavailable.

On day 30 collect earlier scored portfolio work, the two discussion cohorts and actual reading records.
Save today's discussion and annotation as practice. For T or R, offer a private cue about a reason or a
location. Use the existing component record for the 2, 1, 0 marks on the earlier work.

Before day 31 class, print the complete held story, high-water excerpt, grid and response sheet.
Use the same words and labels in large print or an ordered map description. Give the glosses on the
excerpt page. Mark passage audio as supported reading. Keep all check materials sealed until cold reading.

Return the first responses on day 32. Follow letter A by locating exact words, B by matching scope to a
claim, C by testing a theme against an action, D by removing judgment, E by ordering events, F by
explaining a detail or V by choosing a relevant source. Give one priority. Practice first on the released
repair slips. Save the earlier-answer revision separately and record one next reading action.
