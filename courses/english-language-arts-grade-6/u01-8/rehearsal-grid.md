# Rehearsal Rooms and the River

North is at the top.

                  West           Center        East
    North row     Hill room      Hill path     Hill
                ///////////////////////////////////////////////
    Middle row  / Low room [W]   River [W]     Low field [W]  /
    South row   / Low road [W]   River [W]     Low field [W]  /
                ///////////////////////////////////////////////

**Key.** The patterned outline marks where water reached in this survey.
In the grid, [W] marks places inside the outline.

**Caption.** Observed flood extent in one invented survey.
Land outside the outline has no guarantee against future flooding.

**Row-ordered map description**

- North row from west to east. Hill room, Hill path, Hill.
- Middle row from west to east. Low room [W], River [W], Low field [W].
- South row from west to east. Low road [W], River [W], Low field [W].
