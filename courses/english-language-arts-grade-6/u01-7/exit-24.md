# An account for the volunteer

You and Jo made a label for your own box. The volunteer gave it to you alone.

Which first-person pronouns fit the first three blanks?

Jo and __________ made this label.

The volunteer handed it to __________.

The label is __________.

Which pronoun fills the last blank to emphasize who wrote the name?

I __________ wrote my name on it.
