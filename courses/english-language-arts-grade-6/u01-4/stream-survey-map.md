**Key.** The patterned outline marks where water reached in this survey. In the grid, [W] marks places inside the outline.

**Caption.** The outline shows land reached by a past flood. It does not predict the next flood.

**Map in reading order**

- North row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Hill.
- Middle row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Low field [W].
- South row from west to east. Hill, Stream [W], Low road [W].
