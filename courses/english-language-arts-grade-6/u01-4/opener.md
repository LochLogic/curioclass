# Section 1.4: A turning point and a survey

## Reading changes and their limits

Review consequence as the change an event brings about. Track what Mara sends and what each adult can commit to. On a map, the marks show what the survey recorded. A caption sets the limits of the record. Keep personal experiences private and let students pass.

## Routines to review

Review partner rereading for phrases and expression. Use an unmarked chapter passage and give the cue after the first attempt. Use supplied notes to consider which detail a neutral summary needs. In the response lesson, have each pupil correct earlier work first. Take away the paragraph frame before the new exit.

## Return and repair

For error D, remove an opinion and keep the event. For error A, locate exact words. For errors B and E, trace the supported claim and the order of released details. For error V, restate the question and select evidence that answers it.

## Set out

Carry the chapter 10 packet and drawing timeline forward. Print listener cards, quotation notes and feedback slips. Keep the day 15 excerpt, map and access copies together under cover until independent reading starts. Use the fixed offline copy. Preserve support conditions in the reading log and give private listening follow-ups in the scheduled conference slots.
