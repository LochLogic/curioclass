# Section 1.2: What the trial changes in two stories

## Reading the comparison

Follow the hall, the earlier journey, and the return to the hall in Yu’s story. Name Shun as the ruler. Yu’s father is Gun. Yi helps find food, and Ji helps people sow. Use the source note to distinguish the retelling’s imagined dialogue from its underlying traditional account. Talk about the characters’ displacement and food scarcity through the text. Offer a quiet pass.

## Routines to review

Review the discussion moves: mark a detail, listen, ask for a detail, link, and explain what became clearer. Agree on roles, a goal, and a deadline. Keep a first summary separate from the partner’s response. Confirm a paraphrase with the person who spoke. Use dated written feedback to revise the next preparation slip.

## Return and repair

For errors C and D, turn a topic into a message or take a judgment out of a summary. For errors R, S, T and U, locate a detail, rehearse a handoff, extend a response, or have a speaker confirm a restatement. Return check feedback before earlier-answer revision. Keep only one priority repair per pupil at a time.

## Set out

Print both literary texts, the episode map, discussion sheet, feedback slips and reading log. Keep the complete day 6 text and all access copies sealed until the check. Use the fixed text in the course packet. Day 7 follow-up uses the private reading copy and keeps incoming records for other pupils.
