# Day 4 exit ticket

**Tale A.** A fox wants to eat the last ripe plum.
Instead, she buries the whole fruit on a bare hillside. Years later, travelers rest in the tree’s shade.

**Tale B.** A boy wants to spend his last coin on a sweet.
He buys thread instead and mends a fishing net his cousins use all summer.

**RL.6.2.** What shared theme do the fox’s and the boy’s choices develop?
