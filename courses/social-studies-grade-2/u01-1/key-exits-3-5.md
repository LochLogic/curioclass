# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 3 to 5

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-3-5.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 3: south. North points right, so left is south. West, north or east is error B.
- Day 4: 18 blocks. Six blocks is the marks counted and the label ignored; 9 blocks is one length of three added to six; both are error C.
- Day 5 pointing: the key box; the title, rose or scale bar is error A. Day 5 marks: the boat ramp and the garden steps. A bare place, the water or the rock, or a feature named with no action, is error D. Boats are put into the water at the ramp; food grows on the cut slope.
