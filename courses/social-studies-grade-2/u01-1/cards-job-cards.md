# Job cards and map cards (day 2 repair; two more on day 12)

Used for error N, choosing a map for its job. Say first: "These cards are made up." Plain black line drawing on white, one picture and three words on each card.

## Three job cards

1. **find the gym door** (a door with a small figure beside it, no face)
2. **compare two big lands** (two blobs of different sizes)
3. **count the benches** (a row of small bench shapes)

## Three map cards for pairs to match

1. **A floor plan of a school** with a gym drawn and a door marked. Label: school plan.
2. **A world map** with two lands outlined. Label: world map.
3. **A park plan** with a row of benches. Label: park plan.

## For the teacher

Each pair lays a job card beside the map card that has what the job needs, and says: "This map has ___, so it helps." Praise a reason about what the map shows, never about how pretty it is. Pip says the prettiest map is best for any job; the class puts Pip's pick beside the right one.
