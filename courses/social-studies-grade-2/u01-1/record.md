# Unit 1 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into rotating sixes. The first six are heard on days 1, 6 and 11, the second six on days 2, 7 and 12, the third six on days 3, 8 and 13, the fourth six on days 4, 9 and 14, the fifth six on days 5 and 10, and any child not yet heard is heard on day 15 and again on day 16. Day 14 has two groups: the claim group and a second group for the exit, who must not be the same six. Each E column is one exit: one tick when the child marks the card by pointing, circling, placing a token or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. The R columns are the start-of-year looks (day 1, with item 6 heard on days 2 and 3): no score, only the support the child used. The End columns are the day 15 assessment, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt (shown with help), 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Items 13 and 14 are scored after class on day 15 from the revised map and the new graph made on day 14, not from a new attempt. Day 13 drafts are practice and are not scored. The four component groups for day 16 are map parts (items 1 to 6), sources (items 15 to 20), projections (items 7 to 12) and made map (items 13 and 14). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
