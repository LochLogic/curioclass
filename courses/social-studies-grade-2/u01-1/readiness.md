# Unit 1 start-of-year page

## Student

For the teacher. This is one printed page of four pictures, not a test. Children read only the one-word labels: say each prompt aloud. Every child marks the page on day 1, in the last ten minutes, after the hook and the retrieval question. Note on the record sheet what each child did and what help was used; give no score and no grade. Items 1, 2, 4 and 5 are heard from the page. Item 3 is asked on the page, and its why (item 6) is heard from pairs on days 2 and 3, eight pairs on day 2 and seven on day 3. The pictures are in cards-readiness-pictures.md.
