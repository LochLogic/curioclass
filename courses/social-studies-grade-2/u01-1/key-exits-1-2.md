# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 1 and 2

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-1-2.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 1: the trail map; it shows the paths. Choosing the world map for size or color is error N.
- Day 2: the key; the title tells the place and what the map shows. Circling the title, the rose or the scale bar is error A.
