# Exit cards, days 3 to 5

Each card is marked by every child (circle or point), then one spoken question is heard from the day's six. Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white, labels of one to three words.

## Day 3 exit card: Fairground

**Picture:** A fairground map. A compass rose in the corner has an arrow marked N that points to the right edge of the page. A stage is drawn on the left side. A snack stand is drawn to its right. Four boxes under the map hold the words north, south, east, west.

**Mark:** Circle the direction word from the snack stand to the stage. **Spoken:** "How do you know?"

## Day 4 exit card: a path in scale lengths

**Picture:** A path drawn as six equal boxes end to end under a scale bar. The scale bar shows one box and the words one box is three blocks. Three words below in a row: 6, 9, 18 blocks.

**Mark:** Circle how far the path is. **Spoken:** "What does the scale tell us?"

## Day 5 exit card: Lake Edge

**Picture:** A map of a lake edge with five numbered places: 1 a boat ramp with a fish stall beside it; 2 garden steps cut into a hillside; 3 a bare hill; 4 open water; 5 a rock. The card has all four parts: a title strip (Lake Edge), a key box that gives a small symbol for each numbered place, a compass rose and a scale bar.

**Mark:** Circle two places where people use or change the land or water. **Spoken:** "How do people use it?" Then: "Point to the part of the card that tells you what the garden steps symbol stands for."
