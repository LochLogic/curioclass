# Day 2: The title and the key

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the four-part card. Cover the title on one copy of Lantern Lane Market and the key on another. Choose today's six (group 2) and eight pairs for (s01-R6). Key: key-exits-1-2.md.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Day 1's marks give two routes: item 1 (s01-R1) and error N (s01-X1). Teach the more common to the class in 5 minutes; a small group takes the other in Pairs work.

Item 1: show a block beside its drawn square. Review: in grade 1 you learned a key is a box that says what each small picture stands for. Pairs read a new key (a triangle is a hut): "The triangle stands for ___."

Error N: Pip (review: you met Pip in kindergarten and grade 1) says, "The prettiest map is best for any job." Beside it: "The best map has what we need for the job." Show the school plan, the world map and the park plan; pairs whisper what job each does well, then turn over its job card.

#### 11 min. Title and key

Say: "We made this map; the market is made up." Show Lantern Lane Market. Review: in grade 1 you learned a symbol is a small picture that stands for a real thing. New word: title, the words that say what the map shows.

Read the title. Trace from the star to the key box and back: "The key tells me a star is a stall." Cover the title, then the key.

**Ask.** What can we not tell now? Expect: what the map shows (title covered); what the star means (key covered).

Frame on the board: "The title tells me ___. The key tells me ___." Partners say it once.

Pip says, "The title tells me what the star means." Beside it: "The key tells what the star means; the title tells what the map shows."

#### 15 min. Pairs work

Harvest Hill, one copy a pair (made up). Each pair chooses a title from three cards, matches three symbols to key words, then each partner says the frame once.

Minutes 0 to 4: the small group takes the repair not taught to the class.

Minutes 4 to 10: eight pairs, 40 seconds each, page item 3 open. **Ask.** Why could the old map of Boulder, made in 1904, show what Boulder looked like then? (s01-R6) Expect: it was made to show Boulder then.

Minutes 11 to 14: the six, 30 seconds each: mark the exit card (s01-X2), answer (s01-X2b). A child may pass or point.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card; two boxes are for later. Read aloud: "Circle the part that tells what the diamond means." Mark 1 minute; collect 1; add today's answer to the board 1. Circling the title, rose or scale bar is error A.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No flaps: fold paper over the title and the key. No job cards: say the three jobs.

**Skip.** The repair, the pairs and the old-map question.

- **8 min. A map.** Draw a market: a star is a stall, a wave is a pond. Give it a title and a key box.
  **Say.** "What does the title tell us? What does the key tell us?" Expect: what the map shows; what the star means.
- **10 min. Cover it.** Cover the title, then the key.
  **Say.** "What can we not tell now?" Expect: what the map shows; what the star means.
- **7 min. Match.** Your child picks a title for a farm map and matches three symbols to a key.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** "What does the title tell us?" [[s01-X2b]] Expect: what the map shows.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: cover only the key; hear four pairs. Longer: pairs invent a title.

### Words and supports

Words: title, key, symbol. Frames: "The title tells me ___." "The key tells me ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 teaches the compass rose. If many circled the title, rose or scale bar (error A), it opens with four new titles and keys matched in pairs; item 2 (s01-R2) goes to a small group.
