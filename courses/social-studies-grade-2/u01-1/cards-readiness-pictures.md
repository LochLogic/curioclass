# Start-of-year page: four pictures (day 1)

One printed page, four boxes, plain black line drawing on white, thick lines, labels of one to three words in 18 point type. Say first: "We made these pictures for our class. The places in them are made up, except the old map of Boulder." Boulder is a real map; the other three are invented, and the page prints "invented for practice" in small letters under them.

## Picture 1: a gate on a path

**Picture:** A small picture map of a field. A dotted line runs from the left edge to the middle. At the end of the dotted line is a square. A key box in the corner shows a small square and the word gate.

**Teacher says:** "What does the square stand for on this map?"

## Picture 2: a pond and a tent

**Picture:** A small picture map with an arrow pointing up the page and the word north beside it. A pond is drawn above a tent.

**Teacher says:** "Which direction is the pond from the tent?"

## Picture 3: two ways to find out about a place

**Picture:** On the left, a crop of the 1904 Boulder map (the contour lines and a river). On the right, a drawing of an imagined town with a castle and a rainbow. Under the right picture: "someone made this up".

**Teacher says:** "Which one could help us find out what Boulder looked like then?" The reason is heard on days 2 and 3, not on the page.

## Picture 4: a river and a hill

**Picture:** A small picture map. A river runs from the top of the box to the bottom. Beside it, on the right, is one round hill drawn with a few curved lines.

**Teacher says:** "Circle the land." Then: "Draw a box round the water."

## For the teacher

Nothing here is scored. Read each prompt once and again if asked, point to the picture when a child needs it, and write down the support used. Do not say which picture is right.
