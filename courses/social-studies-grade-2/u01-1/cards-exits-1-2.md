# Exit cards, days 1 and 2

Each exit has a picture card that every child marks (circle or point) and a spoken question heard from the day's six. Print "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom of each card. Neither card reuses a map from the hook or the day's teaching. Plain black line drawing on white, labels of one to three words.

## Day 1 exit card: two maps

**Picture:** Two boxes side by side. Left box: a trail map of a nature preserve with a dotted path from a parking square to a tower triangle, and a small key. Right box: a world map outline. Under the left: trail map. Under the right: world map.

**Mark:** Circle the map that helps you find the path to the lookout tower. **Spoken:** "Why does that map help?"

## Day 2 exit card: Orchard Paths

**Picture:** One card with four boxes, each holding one part: a title box (Orchard Paths), a key box (a diamond means a well; a leaf means a tree row), a small compass rose box and a scale bar box. The map itself is a small drawing of rows of trees and a diamond.

**Mark:** Circle the part that tells what the diamond means. **Spoken:** "What does the title tell us?"
