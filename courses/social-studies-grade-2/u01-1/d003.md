# Day 3: The compass rose

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the wall signs by compass if the room allows. Choose today's six (group 3) and seven pairs for (s01-R6). Draw the four repair maps. Key: key-exits-3-5.md.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Day 2's marks give two routes: item 2 (s01-R2) and error A (s01-X2). Teach the more common to the class in 5 minutes; a small group takes the other in Pairs work.

Item 2: turn the page so the arrow on picture 2 points right. Pairs put a finger on the tent and move it north: "What do you reach?" Expect: the pond.

Error A: pairs match four title cards to four maps and four symbols to their keys.

#### 11 min. Compass rose

Review: in grade 1 you learned the direction indicator and the four directions, north, south, east and west. New word: compass rose, the direction indicator with all four directions drawn.

Review: in grade 1 you set the four wall signs by compass. A compass needle settles toward north (USGS). Set the north sign by it; if steel spoils the needle, name one wall north and say aloud that you named it. Everyone points to each sign.

Say: "We made this map; the camp is made up." Show Birch Camp, its rose pointing down.

**Ask.** Start at the fire ring and walk north. Where do you get to? Expect: the well, because the rose points down.

**Ask.** Which way is south? Expect: up, the opposite way from north.

Turn the page: the rose turns with it. Board frame: "North is ___. The ___ is north of the ___."

Pip (review: you met Pip in kindergarten and grade 1) says, "North is the top of the page." Beside it: "North is where the rose points; turn the page and the rose turns."

#### 15 min. Pairs work

Quarry Hill, one copy and one paper rose a pair (made up). Turn the paper rose until its N arrow matches the map's. Partner A says the frame; partner B asks "What is south of the bench?" and A answers. Swap.

Minutes 0 to 4: the small group takes the repair not taught to the class.

Minutes 4 to 9: seven pairs, 40 seconds each, page item 3 open. **Ask.** Why could the old map of Boulder show what Boulder looked like then? (s01-R6) Expect: it was made to show Boulder then.

Minutes 10 to 13: the six, 30 seconds each: mark the exit card (s01-X3), answer (s01-X3b). A child may pass or point.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card. Read aloud: "Circle the direction word from the snack stand to the stage: north, south, east or west." Mark 1 minute; collect 1; add today's answer to the board 1. West, north or east is error B.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No compass: name one wall north. No paper rose: draw a cross, write N on one end and turn the sheet.

**Skip.** The repair, the pairs and the old-map question.

- **8 min. The rose.** Draw a camp: a cabin at the top, a fire ring, a well at the bottom, the rose pointing down.
  **Say.** "Start at the fire ring and walk north. Where do you get to?" Expect: the well.
- **7 min. Turn it.** Turn the page.
  **Say.** "Where does north point now?" Expect: where the rose points, not the top.
- **10 min. Quarry.** Draw a pit, a bench and a pond in a row, the rose pointing left. Ask what is south of the bench: the pond.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** "How do you know?" [[s01-X3b]] Expect: the rose says where north is.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear four pairs. Longer: pairs draw a map and a rose.

### Words and supports

Words: compass rose, north, south, east, west. Frame: "North is ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 teaches scale. If many gave error B, it opens with a paper rose turned on the desk and fresh cards, and a small group takes any more. Day 3's rose comes back on day 4.
