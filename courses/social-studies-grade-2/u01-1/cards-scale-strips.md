# Scale maps and strip cards (day 4, with the day 5 repair map)

Invented maps. Say first: "We made these maps for our class. The places are made up." Each prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. The scale bar is a row of equal boxes with a label.

## Map 1: Sunny Street (the teacher's worked example)

**Picture:** A short street seen from above with a bakery at the left end and a library at the right. A path between them turns one corner. The scale bar is a row of equal boxes; the label reads one box is one block. The path is exactly four boxes long.

**Teacher says:** "Each scale length stands for one block." Lay a paper strip as long as one box on the scale bar. Then: a walker went three blocks from the bakery; lay strips along the path to where she stopped (three strips).

## Map 2: Cove Road (the teacher's second example)

**Picture:** A short road along a cove with a dock sign at one end and a flag at the other. The label reads one box is five steps. The path is exactly four boxes long, so count by fives: 5, 10, 15, 20. Then: a walker went 15 steps from the dock sign; how many lengths (three)?

## Pairs work: two cards

Each pair gets six equal paper strips and two cards. Card 1: two paths from a school gate, one to a corner store (five boxes) and one to a pool (four boxes); the label reads one box is two blocks. Pairs lay strips along each path, say which path is longer (the corner store path), then which path is eight blocks (the pool path). Card 2: a path of five boxes from a swing to a slide; the label reads one box is five steps. A walker goes 15 steps from the swing: pairs count by fives, lay strips and stop where she stops (three lengths).

## Day 5 repair card (error C)

A fresh map with two paths, A and B, and a scale bar whose label reads one box is two paces; path A is three boxes long and path B is four. Pairs lay strips along each path and count the ground units aloud, then say which path is eight paces (B).

## For the teacher

A strip is not a ruler: it is as long as one box and nothing else. Count whole units only. Pip says three lengths means three paces whatever the label; beside it, three lengths of five paces is fifteen paces.
