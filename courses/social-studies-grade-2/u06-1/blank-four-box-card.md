# Four-box card

Four boxes in a row on one page, large print, for the teacher's wall. Each box has a label and a picture cue.

| where (a pin on a map) | how (a path with an arrow) | why (a speech bubble with a question mark) | when (a calendar) |
|---|---|---|---|
| &nbsp; | &nbsp; | &nbsp; | &nbsp; |

Under the boxes: "The teller may say: I would rather not say."

Use: day 73 filled by the teacher with the model; day 74 filled by the class as the visitor speaks; day 75 labels only, no example in view.
