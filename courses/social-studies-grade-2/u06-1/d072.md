# Day 72: What is culture?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the chart cards and Hana's. Say that Hana, Beck's street and the Aspen Street Fair are made up. Ask no child about home, family, money or beliefs; any may pass. No repair is routed: copy day 71's errors G and H onto the record sheet. Key: key-exits-72-75.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook, Music Here

Hand back day 71's group tasks (s05-P71-3, s05-P71-4, s05-P71-6) and exit ticket (s05-X71), one written cue each, 1 minute. Show the Music Here map and read the Posadas Card's first three sentences and credit line; it is one real man's telling, through an interpreter. **Ask.** "Can music travel without fitting in a suitcase?" Leave it open; review the question board from grade 1 and put the unit question on it.

#### 4 min. Retrieval, grade 1's ways of living

Review: custom, language, tradition, holiday and diverse, from grade 1. Read: "Two neighbors speak different languages and both help at school." **Ask.** "Thumbs up: can both belong?" Expect thumbs up. **Ask.** "Say one thing neighbors might share." Hear three.

#### 10 min. What is culture

Whole class. Read Hana's seven cards and put each picture on the chart: language, food, music, beliefs, holidays, art, ways of dress. Review: culture is what people make, do and share (unit 4); today it has seven parts. Beliefs: what people hold important; no child names one. **Ask.** "Which part of culture is this?" Expect the part's word; pointing counts. Pip says error A: "Culture is stuff, like a hat or a boat." Say beside it: "Good morning in two languages is culture too, and you cannot hold it."

#### 7 min. Sort in pairs

Pairs put Beck's eight cards on the chart [[s06-P72-1]]; a card may go in two places with a reason. Hear the rotating six, about 45 seconds each: [[s06-X72b]]. Mark by the scale; a pass counts as heard.

#### 4 min. Same or different

Draw two flatbreads, one with seeds. Pip says error G: "Everybody on Hana's street bakes flatbread the same way." Review: "Both ___, but ___" from unit 5. Class: both flatbread, but one has seeds. Pip says error K: "Beck's pancakes are better than our porridge." Class: different is not better.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Review: grade 1's exit ticket. Read [[s06-X72]] aloud once; each child marks it. [[s06-X72b]] was heard in the pairs work.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "What is culture?". Three children point to one part on the chart.

### Other rooms

No chart: draw seven boxes on the board. No tokens: a pencil dot.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart or cards: draw seven boxes on paper and each card on a slip. No token: a pencil dot.

**Skip.** The pairs and the six.

- **5 min. Hook.** Read the Posadas Card's first three sentences. **Say.** "Can music travel without fitting in a suitcase?" Expect: any idea; leave it open.
- **15 min. What is culture.** Sort Beck's eight cards onto the seven boxes [[s06-P72-1]]. **Say.** "Which part of culture is pancakes on Sunday?" Expect: food.
- **5 min. Same or different.** **Say.** "Two neighbors bake flatbread; one adds seeds. How are they the same and different?" Expect: both flatbread, one has seeds.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X72]] [[s06-X72b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs sort four cards. Longer: each pair invents a ninth card.

### Words and supports

Words: culture and its seven parts. Frames: "This is ___." "Both ___, but ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows error A (the token on the stew or the painted tile), error G (everyone keeps it the same way) or error K (one way is better). Day 73 (Understanding) opens with a 5-minute Return and repair for the letter most missed on day 72, then writes the four interview questions: where, how, why, when.
