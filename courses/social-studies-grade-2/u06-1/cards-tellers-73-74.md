# Ms. Bell and Mr. Okoye (invented tellers, models of days 73 and 74)

Every card prints "invented for practice". The teacher says aloud that Ms. Bell and Mr. Okoye are made up. Neither card is an exit case. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Ms. Bell, the crossing guard (day 73)

**First, one question and a thin answer.** The teacher asks "Why did you move?" and Ms. Bell says "It was time." The class says that is not enough to learn from.
**Then the four boxes, each ending "this town":**
- Where: "I lived in a town by a lake before I came to this town."
- How: "I came by train."
- Why: "I wanted to work near the school crossing."
- When: "I came in 2011."
**Pip says B:** "One question is enough: Where are you from?" The teacher says beside it: "Four questions: where, how, why and when."
**The rule:** the teller may say "I would rather not say".

## Mr. Okoye, a listener's model (day 74)

**The teacher asks:** "Why did you come?"
**Mr. Okoye says:** "To be near my sister."
**The teacher asks the follow-up:** "Could you explain that part?"
**Mr. Okoye says:** "She moved here first, and I wanted to live close."
**The teacher writes only:** "near sister" then "she moved here first". Pip says C: "He said sister. I will write that his sister has a big house." The teacher says beside it: "Only what he said. If he did not say, I write unknown."
