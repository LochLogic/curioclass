# Interview sheet

Name ______________________

Print on one page. The teacher reads the frames aloud. The child writes or draws a word in each blank. Questions are about a move; the teller may pass.

| Topic | Question frame |
|---|---|
| where | Where did ______ live before? |
| how | How did ______ get here? |
| why | Why did ______ move? |
| when | When did ______ come to this town? |

My own question about the move: ______________________

Teller's answer boxes (day 74 and day 75): one small box under each question, with the words "unknown" and "passed" printed at the bottom as choices.

Use: day 73 questions; day 74 answers and a revised question; day 75 the round.
