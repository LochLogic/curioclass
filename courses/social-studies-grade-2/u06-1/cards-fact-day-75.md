# Fact cards, day 75 (invented)

Six printed cards in an envelope for each group waiting for the round; every card prints "invented for practice". Children match each card to the question it answers (where, how, why or when). The teacher reads each aloud. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

| Card | Picture | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| ferry ticket | a ticket with a boat | how |
| the year 2009 | a calendar page | when |
| a hill farm | a small farm on a hill | where |
| a letter that says come help | a letter with a stamp | why |
| train ticket | a ticket with a train | how |
| the year 2014 | a calendar page | when |

**Teacher note:** none of these is a teller's real move. Say aloud that they are made up. Do not use these years or the ferry with any day 75 round teller.
