# Day 75: The interviews

## Teacher page

### Before class

Ask the visiting adult back, or plan your own safe move: one sentence opening, then answers on request, one "passed" and one "unknown" among them. Do not use a ferry, a train, 2009 or 2014 (fact cards), nor a bus, a job or a factory. Make five groups of six and mark the record sheet's "who was heard" column. Ask no child about their own move; any may pass. Key: key-exits-72-75.md.

### The day

#### 2 min. Opening

The four-box card shows labels only. Say the rule: a teller may pass, and a pass is a full answer. Hand back s06-P74-1 and s06-X74. Repair for day 74's letter: error C, a teller says "I came by ferry in the spring" and the class underlines what was said in two notes; or error D, six fresh questions sorted "about the move" and "not". Hear two; any may pass.

#### 27 min. The round

Five rounds of about 5 minutes 24 seconds, six children each: the teller's opening, about 30 seconds; six turns of 45 seconds each, with the swap; about 25 seconds to change groups. The teller opens with one sentence, then answers on request. Four children each ask one topic question [[s06-X75]], two ask a follow-up [[s06-X75b]], and all six record in the note box [[s06-X75c]]. Count the clock; a child who passes is recorded as heard. Mark the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

The five groups not in the round work three stations when they are out, about 21 minutes in all, moving on by the station strip with no timer shown: (a) match the fact cards to the question each answers [[s06-P75-1]], about 8 minutes; (b) say your own question to a partner, then ask "Could you explain that part?" [[s06-P75-2]], about 6; (c) prepare the home sheet, about 6.

#### 3 min. Record and reflect

Review: your own boxes from day 74. Children sit. Each says to a partner one question for their home teller and one follow-up. You finish the record sheet: who was heard, and who is left for day 76. No private detail goes on any display.

#### 3 min. Close

Remind: home sheets come back on day 76. Add "What can we ask a teller?" to the question board.

### Other rooms

No envelopes: write the six fact cards on the board. Stand or sit for the round.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: draw four boxes labeled where, how, why, when.

**Skip.** The five rounds and the stations.

- **5 min. Match.** [[s06-P75-1]] **Say.** "Which question does a hill farm answer?" Expect: where.
- **15 min. Ask.** A grown-up tells a safe move and may pass. Write your own question [[s06-P75-2]], ask the four topics and one follow-up. **Say.** "Could you explain that part?" Expect: more about something they said.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X75]] [[s06-X75b]] [[s06-X75c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear four rounds and bring the last six on day 76. Longer: a child asks the teller a fifth question and says who could answer it.

### Words and supports

Words: topic, follow-up, pass, unknown. Frames: "My question is ___." "The teller said ___." Drawing counts; a partner may hold the pencil.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows error B (one question for all four topics, or a topic asked twice while another is left out), error D (a follow-up that does not follow what was said) and error C (a detail the teller did not say, or a guessed year). Day 76 (Application) opens with 5 minutes: the letter most missed, with a fresh card. Children not yet heard ask their topic question aloud. Home sheets go in the folder. Then the visitor's account is read as a source.

### Credit

Ours. Every fact card is made up.
