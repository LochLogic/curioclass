# Beck's street cards (invented, the pair sort of day 72)

Every card prints "invented for practice". Each pair gets eight small cards and sorts them onto the culture chart. The rotating six are asked, one at a time: "Where does this one go?" The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

| Card (a label of one to three words, read aloud) | Chart place (teacher sheet only) |
|---|---|
| pancakes on Sunday | food |
| a clapping rhyme | music |
| a woven mat | art |
| Kite Day | holidays |
| the street's word for friend | language |
| red scarf day | ways of dress |
| we share the first berries | beliefs |
| a porch story | language or art; either, with a reason |

**Teacher note:** Cards stay on their own side of the chart; any card may go in more than one place if the child can say why. After the sort the teacher uses Pip for G, then K: Pip says "Everyone on Beck's street keeps Kite Day the same way", then "Their pancakes are better than our porridge". The class says: people who share something still do it differently, and different is not better.
