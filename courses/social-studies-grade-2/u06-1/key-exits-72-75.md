# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 72 to 75

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-72-75.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 72: the song; spoken: any real difference (a dish, a song, a game). Day 73: What year did you come to this town?; spoken: a question about where she lived before. Day 74: the first note; spoken: a question about the job, such as "What kind of job?". Day 75: a question for the topic given; a follow-up about the teller's words; a note box with only what the teller said, or passed or unknown. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
