# Culture chart: the seven parts

Seven large picture cards for a wall chart, kept up through day 81. Each card has the word and one plain picture. The teacher reads them aloud. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

| Card | Words under the picture | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| language | language: the words people speak and sign | two people greeting with speech bubbles of different shapes |
| food | food: what people cook and eat | a pot on a table with bowls around it |
| music | music: sounds people make for others | a person with a flute and a small drum |
| beliefs | beliefs: what people hold to be important | two neighbors carrying a box together |
| holidays | holidays: days many people keep together | a wall calendar with one day circled |
| art | art: pictures and made things | a chalk drawing on a sidewalk |
| ways of dress | ways of dress: what people wear | rain hats and sun hats on hooks |

**Teacher note:** Beliefs is a word on a card with one non-religious example. No child names a belief, and no religious observance, costume or tasting goes with this chart. A culture is what people make, do and share (review of unit 4), and people who share one still differ.
