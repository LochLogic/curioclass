# Exit cards, days 72 to 75

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 72 exit card: Aspen Street Fair

**Picture:** Three small pictures side by side: a pot of stew on a table, a painted tile on a wall, a person singing.
**Card line:** "At the Aspen Street Fair a family serves a stew, a neighbor hangs a painted tile and a neighbor sings a song."
**Mark:** Put a token on the one part of culture you cannot hold.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Two families at the Aspen Street Fair keep the same holiday. Say one way they could keep it differently."

## Day 73 exit card: School cook Ms. Dale

**Label:** "School cook Ms. Dale remembers her move to this town in 2018; interviewed today."
**Three question cards:** "Why did you come here?" "How did you get here?" "What year did you come to this town?"
**Mark:** Circle the question that asks when.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Ask Ms. Dale a question about where she lived before."

## Day 74 exit card: Mr. Quill

**Teller line:** Mr. Quill says: "I came to this town by bus. I came for a job."
**Three notes:** "He came by bus for a job." "He came by bus for a job at a big factory." "He came by train for a job."
**Mark:** Circle the note that holds only what Mr. Quill said.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Mr. Quill said he came for a job. Ask him one follow-up question about that."

## Day 75 exit (observed in the round)

**Spoken, in the round:** "Ask the teller the question for your topic: where, how, why or when." Then: "Ask the teller one follow-up question about something the teller just said."
**Paper, on the child's note box:** "In your box write or draw only what the teller said. If the teller passed or did not say, write passed or unknown."
