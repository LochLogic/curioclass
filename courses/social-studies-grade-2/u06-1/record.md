# Unit 6 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into five rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the teacher's table on days 72, 77 and 81, the second six on days 73 and 78, the third six on days 74 and 79, the fourth six on days 75 and 76; any child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block, and on day 76 children not yet heard on day 75 ask their topic question aloud at their table. Each X column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, boxing, placing a token or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Day 75 has a "who was heard" column: a note for each of the four topic questions asked, a follow-up asked, and whether the note holds only what the teller said, with "unknown" or "passed" written where it applies. The End columns are the day 80 sitting, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Items 1 to 7 are read from the day 75 round and the day 79 revised record, not from first attempts, and take no day 80 minutes; items 8 to 20 are on paper. The four groups for day 81 are items 1 to 7 (B, C, J), items 8 to 10 (D, I), items 11 to 15 and 20 (A, F, H) and items 16 to 19 (G, K, L, M). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders. Keep names and private details off any display.
