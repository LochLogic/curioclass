# Day 74: Listen, ask more, write what was said

## Teacher page

### Before class

Ask a willing school adult to tell a safe move; they may decline any question. They write, or dictate, a short account in their own words and approve it for day 76. Hang chart paper for the class copy. Keep no recording. Say aloud that Mr. Okoye is made up. Key: key-exits-72-75.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the interview sheets (s06-P73-1) with mark and cue, 2 minutes. Then 3 minutes for the letter most missed on s06-X73 and s06-X73b: error B, Mr. Ashby says "I lived by a lake" and the class names the topics missing; error D, sort six questions "about the move" or "not". A miss in day 73's retrieval is taught here.

#### 4 min. Listening and a follow-up, a model

Review: listening and asking about what a speaker said, from ELA unit 1. Mr. Okoye (made up) answers "Why did you come?" with "To be near my sister." A follow-up is a question about a part the teller already said; ask "Could you explain that part?" He says "She moved here first, and I wanted to live close." A faithful note holds only what the teller said: write "near sister", "she moved here first". Children say it back to a partner, starting "He said": [[s06-P74-2]]. Pip says error C: "He said sister. I will write that his sister has a big house." Say beside it: "Only what he said. If he did not say, I write unknown."

#### 14 min. The visitor

The visitor tells a safe move to the class. The rotating six ask, about 45 seconds each: four one topic each, two a follow-up; the record sheet names them. The visitor may pass; a pass is a full answer. Every child puts a mark in the four-box card for each topic the visitor answers [[s06-P74-1]]; P for a pass, a dash for no answer. You write the class copy on chart paper as the visitor speaks. No private detail goes on the display.

No visitor: tell an invented safe move, said aloud to be made up.

#### 6 min. Check our notes

Pairs underline the class-copy line each mark came from; rub out a mark with no line. Hear the rotating six, about 45 seconds each: [[s06-X74b]]. Mark by the scale.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read [[s06-X74]] aloud once; each child circles. [[s06-X74b]] was heard while pairs checked.

#### 2 min. Revise

Each child changes one question on their sheet, using the cue.

#### 3 min. Home interview

Hand out the home interview sheet: any trusted adult, who may pass; write only what is said. Back by day 76. A child with no home path uses the school round. Never ask about papers, money or why a family is sad.

### Other rooms

No chart paper: write the class copy on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: draw four boxes labeled where, how, why, when.

**Skip.** The class visitor and the six.

- **5 min. Model.** Read Mr. Okoye: "To be near my sister." **Say.** "Could you explain that part?" Expect: she moved here first. Say it back: "He said ___" [[s06-P74-2]].
- **15 min. A teller.** A grown-up tells a safe move and may pass. Ask four questions and one follow-up; record [[s06-P74-1]]. **Say.** "Could you explain that part?" Expect: more about something they said.
- **5 min. Revise.** Change one question on yesterday's sheet.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X74]] [[s06-X74b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: ask the visitor only where and why. Longer: a second follow-up.

### Words and supports

Words: follow-up, faithful note, pass. Frames: "The teller said ___." "Could you explain that part?" Drawing counts.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows error C (a detail the teller never said, or changed) and error D (a question not about what was said). Day 75 (Application) opens with 2 minutes: a fresh note, children underline what was said, or six fresh questions sorted. Then each child asks a topic question and a follow-up and records only what the teller says.

### Credit

Ours. Mr. Okoye and the repair cards are made up.
