# Section 6.1 opener: what culture is, and how to ask a teller

## What the section does

Four days, 72 to 75, of 35 to 37 minutes. The unit asks: when people move to a new place, do they change the place more, or does the place change them more? Day 72 teaches what culture is, seven parts on one wall chart (language, food, music, beliefs, holidays, art, ways of dress), and that people who share a culture still do things differently. Days 73 to 75 teach the interview: four questions (where, how, why, when), listening for what the teller really said, a follow-up, and a note that holds only what was said. The one real telling is the Posadas Card on day 72, a summary in our words with its credit line. Every other person in a card is invented, printed "invented for practice", and the teacher says so aloud.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a short repair or retrieval, then teaching, a part for pairs where the rotating six are heard at the teacher's table (about 45 seconds each, one spoken ask), an exit ticket and a question-board turn. The teacher reads every card aloud. Children read labels of one to three words. No child is asked about their own home, family, money, religion or how they came here, and any child may pass. A pass is a full answer and is recorded as heard.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Culture is what people share and do.** It is more than things you can hold: a language, a song or a holiday cannot be held. Beliefs here means "what people hold to be important", with a non-religious example; no child names a belief.
- **People who share a culture still differ.** Two families keep one holiday in two ways. Different is not better.
- **An interview is a spoken source.** The teller made it and the child writes it down. A faithful note holds only what the teller said. Unknown stays unknown, and a teller may say "I would rather not say".
- **Four topics.** Where, how, why and when ask the move the way unit 5's chain does. "We have always lived here" is a full answer to where and when.
- **A follow-up asks about a part the teller already said.**

## The errors, in plain words

Pip the puppet says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: culture is only stuff (A, day 72); everyone does it the same way (G, day 72); their song is better (K, day 72); one question is enough (B, day 73); I will ask about her shoes (D, day 74); I will write that he had a big old bus (C, day 74).

## What stays held back

The exit cards of days 72 to 75 are printed only on their days. Nothing a child tells in a home interview goes on a display.
