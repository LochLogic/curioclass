# Hana's street cards (invented, the worked example of day 72)

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. The teacher says aloud that Hana and her street are made up and reads each card once. Seven picture cards go onto the culture chart as the teacher says the part. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

| Card line (read aloud) | Part of culture the teacher names | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| "On Hana's street, two neighbors say good morning in two languages." | language | two neighbors waving, two speech bubbles |
| "On Fridays someone bakes flatbread and shares it." | food | a round flat loaf on a cloth |
| "At dusk a neighbor plays a flute tune on the steps." | music | a person on steps with a flute |
| "Once a year the street keeps Paper Boat Night, a holiday." | holidays | small paper boats on a long table |
| "Children draw chalk pictures on the walk." | art | a chalk flower on a sidewalk |
| "Many people on the street wear rain hats with wide brims." | ways of dress | hats on a row of hooks |
| "Neighbors on Hana's street think it is important to help each other carry things." | beliefs | two people carrying one box |

**Pip says A:** "Culture is stuff, like a hat or a boat." The teacher says beside it: "A flute tune is culture too, and you cannot hold it."
**Teacher note:** Day 72's exit uses a different street fair. Do not print any exit card with this piece.
