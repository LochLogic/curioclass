# Repair cards, days 73 to 75

Every card prints "invented for practice". One fresh card for the letter most missed on its day; do not reuse any exit case. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 73 repair (A, G or K)

- **A:** five pictures: a clay pot, a hello word, a kite, a story told aloud, a bedtime rhyme. Sort into "I can hold it" and "I cannot hold it"; both are culture.
- **G:** "The Rao family and the Voss family both keep the Harvest Supper." The Raos sing a table rhyme. The Vosses play a card game. Say one difference.
- **K:** "A rice dish with peas and a corn bread." Say what is different and why neither is the best.

## Day 74 repair (B or D)

- **B:** Mr. Ashby says "I lived by a lake." The class says which three topics are missing: how, why and when.
- **D:** sort six questions into "about the move" and "not about the move": Where did you live before? What is your dog's name? How did you travel here? How tall are you? Why did you move? What is your favorite game?

## Day 75 opening (C or D)

- **C:** teller line, "I came by ferry in the spring." Two notes: "She came by ferry in the spring." and "She came by ferry in the spring with her dog." Children underline what was said.
- **D:** the same six questions as day 74 on new slips with new topics, sorted again.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

A: the pot is held, the rest cannot be held. G: any real difference. K: any difference, no winner. B: how, why, when. D (day 74): Where, How travel and Why are about the move; the others are not. C: the first note.
