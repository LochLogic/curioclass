# Day 20: The five oceans

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fourth six on the record sheet. Tape two copies of the World Outline Cards outline edge to edge. Hand back day 19's cards (s02-X19). Set the repair outline beside the globe. Key: key-exits-17-20.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Error J from day 19 (s02-X19, s02-X19b, s02-X19c): lay the new outline, name the Alps or the Nile, a child draws a finger along it and shows it on the globe, north arrow beside it. Say together: "The ___ is in the ___ of ___." If no one needs it, rehearse the Alps and the Nile.

#### 11 min. The five oceans

Review: on day 17 you met the continents; in grade 1 you learned ocean. Read the key, then the ocean page. Each ocean is said by compass word and by what it lies between or round: the Pacific between the Americas and Asia and Australia; the Atlantic between the Americas and Europe and Africa; the Indian between Africa, Asia and Australia; the Arctic round the North Pole; the Southern round Antarctica.

Finger on the taped outlines: follow the Pacific across the tape, then round the globe.

Pip says: "The Pacific stops at the edge of the paper." Beside it: "On the globe the Pacific goes on round the back; the two edges are one water." **Ask.** Where does the Pacific go at the edge? Expect: on round the back.

Pip says: "There are four oceans." Beside it: "Five: the Southern Ocean is the water round Antarctica." **Ask.** Which ocean lies round Antarctica? Expect: the Southern.

Pip says: "The five oceans are five pools that never meet." Beside it: "Their salt water is joined; one finger follows it all round." **Ask.** Joined or apart? Expect: joined.

#### 10 min. Why oceans matter

Pairs hold four Ocean Cards, ours, with a credit line on each: homes and food; ships carrying goods; sea water making rain; most of the water on Earth. Read each label. Pip says: "Oceans matter only for holidays." Beside it: "Oceans are homes for living things, and their water makes rain." Each pair chooses one card and says "Oceans matter because ___" ([[s02-P20-2]]).

#### 8 min. Supported work

Pairs find the water Pip left unnamed on the practice map ([[s02-P20-1]]); first placements are practice. Meanwhile the six come to the globe one at a time, 40 seconds each, for two of (s02-X20c), (s02-X20d), (s02-X20e) and (s02-X20f), dealt: about 4 of the 8 minutes, so the rest is slack for a small group on any error J left over.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "oceans" and "why oceans matter" under "Where are these places?"

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Mark 2 minutes on the Indian Ocean map: "Write the number of each ocean beside its name" (s02-X20). The six were heard in supported work. Two numbers for one ocean missing is error B; four oceans is error M; separate pools is error L; holidays alone is error C. A feature on the wrong side has no new letter: error J.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The Ocean Cards and the tape.

- **10 min. Name the water.** [[s02-P20-1]] Your child finds the unnamed ocean on a map.
  **Say.** "Which ocean lies round Antarctica?" Expect: the Southern.
- **15 min. Why it matters.** Name one reason oceans matter, then point to the water on a globe.
  **Say.** "Why do oceans matter?" Expect: homes, food, ships, rain or most of the water.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X20]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X20c]] [[s02-X20d]] [[s02-X20e]] [[s02-X20f]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two oceans and one reason. Longer: a child says which oceans lie between two continents.

### Words and supports

Words: ocean, Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, Southern, joined. Frame: "Oceans matter because ___." Pointing counts.
