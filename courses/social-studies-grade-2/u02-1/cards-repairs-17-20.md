# Repair cards, days 18 to 20

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 18: country inside continent (error A)

**Picture:** A large outline of an invented continent with three smaller outlines inside it, each with a made-up name: Tarn, Brindle and Oso. A label under the card: A country lies inside a continent.
**Say:** "Country inside, continent around." Children point to a country, then trace the continent.

## Day 18: land and water outline sort (retrieval)

**Picture:** Eight small cards, four showing land shapes (hill, island, plain, mountain) and four showing water (river, lake, ocean, pond), and two bins labeled land and water.
**Say:** "How is a river different from an ocean?" A river is retraced from its start to where it meets a larger water.

## Day 19 and 20: trace the feature (error J)

**Picture:** A new outline of two continents with an empty line beside each, a north arrow top left. The teacher names a feature; the child draws a finger along it and says "The ___ is in the ___ of ___."

## Day 20: the Pacific join (error B)

**Picture:** Two copies of the world outline taped edge to edge so that the Pacific shows as one water across the join. A finger follows the water across the tape, then the same finger follows it round the globe.
