# Day 19: Features of the other continents

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the third six on the record sheet. Keep the feature pack face down until the exit. Set the new outline of two continents for the repair beside the globe. Hand back day 18's cards (s02-X18) and maps (s02-P18-1). Key: key-exits-17-20.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Error J from day 18 (s02-X18, s02-X18b, s02-X18c), the one repair this warm-up holds. Lay the new outline. Name the Andes or the Amazon; a child draws a finger along it on the outline, then shows it on the globe, the north arrow beside it. Say together: "The ___ is in the ___ of ___."

#### 14 min. Eight features

Review: you read the Atlas key on day 18; its signs are triangles for mountains, a wavy line for a river, dots for desert, a ring for a reef and white hatching for an ice sheet. Our signs add dots, a ring and hatching. Take each page about 1 minute 45 seconds: read the label, find the sign, place the feature on the page and the globe, and ask "Land, water or ice?"
- Europe: the Alps, mountains in central Europe, and the Danube, a river across central and southeastern Europe.
- Africa: the Sahara, a desert across northern Africa, and the Nile, a river in northeastern Africa.
- Asia: the Himalayas, mountains in southern Asia.
- Australia: the Outback, the dry land in its middle, and the Great Barrier Reef, in the sea off its northeast.
- Antarctica: the ice sheet that covers most of the land.

Each page also shows a city or people at work, so no page stands for its continent. The Alps were in day 17's hook.

Pip says: "The Nile is in South America." Beside it: "The Nile runs through northeastern Africa." **Ask.** Where does the Nile lie? Expect: in the northeast of Africa.

#### 3 min. Who made it

Review: the three questions from day 6. Read the credit line on the Africa page. **Ask.** Who made it? When? Where? Expect: Curio Class; the record does not say; the record does not say.

#### 7 min. Pairs work

Pairs place the eight labels on the practice map ([[s02-P19-1]]), then check each against the wall Atlas, saying "The ___ lies in the ___ of ___." Small groups: any error J left over. First placements are practice. The maps stay in the folder.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "mountains, rivers, deserts, a reef and ice" under "Where are these places?"

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Mark 1 minute on the new crop of Australia. Read aloud: "Circle where the Great Barrier Reef is" (s02-X19). Each of the six draws one card from the pack and comes to the globe, turned a new way, 45 seconds each with the swap, about 4.5 minutes, for one ask, (s02-X19b) for three children and (s02-X19c) for three. A feature on the wrong continent or side is error J. Land, water or ice swapped has no letter: record not yet seen.

### Other rooms

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The feature pack and the credit line.

- **10 min. Find them.** [[s02-P19-1]] Your child places eight paper labels on a world map.
  **Say.** "Where does the Nile lie?" Expect: in the northeast of Africa.
- **15 min. Land, water or ice?** Name each feature and say its sign from the key.
  **Say.** "Is the Great Barrier Reef land, water or ice?" Expect: water, in the sea off northeastern Australia.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X19b]] [[s02-X19c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one feature for each page. Longer: a child says which two continents the Nile and the Alps lie on, with a direction between them.

### Words and supports

Words: desert, reef, ice sheet, mountains, river, northeast. Frame: "The ___ lies in the ___ of ___." Pointing counts.
