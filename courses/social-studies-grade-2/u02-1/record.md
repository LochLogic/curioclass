# Unit 2 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the class globe on days 17, 22 and 26, the second six on days 18, 23 and 27, the third six on days 19, 24 and 29, the fourth six on days 20 and 25, the fifth six on days 21 and 26, and any child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block. Each E column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, boxing, writing or placing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The End columns are the day 28 sitting, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Globe placements are booked here across days 21 to 27: each child is heard three times on four places, covering all seven continents and the five oceans. The four component groups for day 29 are location, climate and livelihood, change at two dates and sourced claim. A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
