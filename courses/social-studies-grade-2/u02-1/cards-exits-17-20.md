# Exit cards, days 17 to 20

Each card is marked by every child (circle, box, write), then the spoken asks are heard from the day's six at the class globe. Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line on every card: Outline drawn by Curio Class.

## Day 17 exit card: World Outline Card F

**Picture:** The world, Atlantic in the middle, seven shapes lettered P, Q, R, S, T, U, V, a north arrow in the top left. No names are printed.

**Mark:** Circle Africa. Draw a box round the land that lies north of Africa. **Spoken (the six, at the globe):** "Show me Antarctica." "Is Antarctica north or south of Africa?" "What is Africa?"

## Day 18 exit card: a blank crop

**Picture:** A crop of the Americas and the edge of Africa, with no features drawn. Three small marked positions: a dot in the west of South America, a dot in the east of South America, and a dot on the edge of Africa. A north arrow top left.

**Mark:** Circle where the Andes are. **Spoken (the six, on a globe turned a new way):** "Show me the Amazon." "Is the Amazon in the north or the south of South America?" "Show me the Rocky Mountains." "Show me the Great Lakes."

## Day 19 exit card: a coastal crop of Australia

**Picture:** A crop of Australia with its coast and the sea round it. Three marked positions: one in the sea off the northeast, one inland, one in the sea off the west. A north arrow top left.

**Mark:** Circle where the Great Barrier Reef is. **Spoken (the six, on a new globe view, one card from the feature pack):** "Show me this feature." "Is the feature drawn land, water or ice?"

## Day 20 exit card: a map centered on the Indian Ocean

**Picture:** The world with the Indian Ocean in the middle. Six shapes numbered: 1 the large water on the right, 2 the thin water on the left edge, 3 the water left of Africa, 4 the water in the middle, 5 the water across the top, 6 the water across the bottom. A north arrow in the top left. A word bank of six words: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, Southern, Great Lakes.

**Mark:** Write the number of each ocean beside its name. **Spoken (the six, in supported work):** "Tell me one way oceans help living things." "Are the five oceans joined or apart?" "Does the ocean or all the other water on Earth together hold more?" "A ship sails from one land to a harbor in another land. What is it carrying?"
