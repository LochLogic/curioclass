# Day 17: The seven continents

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the first six on the record sheet. A person looks at the Aletsch images before layout; say only what you see. Set the globe beside Card A, north arrows matching. Draw three slips: "Drawn by Lin Okafor, 2026, made in a classroom." "Kept in the Oak Library." "Shows Cedar Pond." Key: key-exits-17-20.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review: on day 6 you sorted a credit. Errors E and J from day 16: say the credit is made up; children place each slip under who made it, who keeps it and what place it shows. **Ask.** Where was it made? Expect: in a classroom. Whole class if more than a third made error E or J, otherwise a small group in Pairs work. Error C: a small group in Pairs work, 4 minutes; three strips on a path of five paces a length make 15 paces.

#### 5 min. Hook

Show the Aletsch pair, one place in the Swiss Alps on 2 September 1984 and 6 August 2024. Read the credit: "NASA Earth Observatory images by Michala Garrison, using United States Geological Survey Landsat data." Set Palm Cove (made up) beside. **Ask.** Where on Earth would you choose to live? What do we need to find out? Expect: where places are, what each is like. Any choice is welcome; no one says where they live.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: in grade 1 you learned continent and ocean; in unit 1, the compass rose, whose north is the north arrow on our cards. Each child holds Card A. **Ask.** Point to a continent. Point to an ocean. Use the north arrow to say where one lies. Note misses.

#### 12 min. The seven continents

Say: "These outlines are drawn for our class from the real shapes of the lands." Read the key. Then one Atlas page a minute: North America, South America, Europe, Africa, Asia, Australia, Antarctica. Read its label, put a hand on its shape on Card A, then on the same land on the globe.

With a finger on the arrow first, say: "North America lies north of South America." "Asia lies north of Australia."

Pip says: "Asia is a country." Beside it: "Asia is a continent, a very large land area, with many countries inside." Review: on day 12 you saw a country inside a continent. **Ask.** What is Asia? Expect: a continent.

#### 6 min. Pairs work

Pairs place the seven tags on the blank card ([[s02-P17-1]]). Each partner then says one direction sentence from the dealt cards ([[s02-P17-2]]): North America with South America, or Asia with Australia. The cards stay in the folder. Small groups: error C, 4 minutes; error E or J if not taught to the class.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "seven continents" in the children's words.

#### 7 min. Exit ticket

Mark 2 minutes on card F. Read aloud: "Circle Africa" (s02-X17). "Draw a box round the land that lies north of Africa" (s02-X17b). The six come to the globe one at a time, 45 seconds each with the swap, each dealt one of (s02-X17c), (s02-X17d) or (s02-X17e), two for each. Africa called a country is error A. North and south reversed has no letter: record not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball with seven shapes drawn on it, or one world map.

**Skip.** The Aletsch pictures and the groups.

- **10 min. Name them.** [[s02-P17-1]] Your child lays seven paper tags on a world map, one on each continent.
  **Say.** "Which continent is this?" Expect: its name, a very large land area.
- **15 min. Which way?** Point to the map's north arrow. Say sentences with it.
  **Say.** "Which lies north, North America or South America?" Expect: North America lies north of South America.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17]] [[s02-X17b]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X17c]] [[s02-X17d]] [[s02-X17e]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read each label's first sentence. Longer: a direction sentence with east or west.

### Words and supports

Words: continent, north, south, east, west, north arrow. Frame: "___ lies north of ___." Pointing counts.
