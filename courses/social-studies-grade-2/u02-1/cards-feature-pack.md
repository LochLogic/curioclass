# Feature pack: eight cards (day 19 exit, spoken part)

One card for each feature, face down. The child draws one card at the class globe. Each card shows a small drawing, the feature's name and its sign from the key. Credit line on every card: Drawing by Curio Class.

| Card | Drawing | Name | Sign |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | white-topped peaks in a ring | the Alps | triangles |
| 2 | a river with a bridge | the Danube | wavy line |
| 3 | a wide sand desert | the Sahara | dots |
| 4 | a long river with green banks | the Nile | wavy line |
| 5 | very tall peaks with snow | the Himalayas | triangles |
| 6 | a dry red plain | the Outback | dots |
| 7 | a ring of coral under blue water | the Great Barrier Reef | ring |
| 8 | a white plain of ice | the Antarctic ice sheet | white hatching |

## Key (teacher)

Place on the globe: the Alps in central Europe; the Danube across central and southeastern Europe; the Sahara across northern Africa; the Nile in northeastern Africa; the Himalayas in southern Asia; the Outback in the interior of Australia; the Great Barrier Reef off northeastern Australia; the ice sheet covering Antarctica. Kind: land for the Alps, the Sahara, the Himalayas and the Outback; water for the Danube, the Nile and the Reef; ice for the ice sheet.
