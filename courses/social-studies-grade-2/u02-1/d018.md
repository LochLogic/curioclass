# Day 18: The Americas

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Lay out repair cards and Card C. Hand back day 17's cards (s02-X17) with one line each. Post the Atlas pages for North America and South America beside the globe, north arrows matching. Key: key-exits-17-20.md.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair

Two parts of 4 minutes. Part 1, error A from day 17: the country repair card is made up, an invented continent with three invented countries, Tarn, Brindle and Oso. Children point to a country and trace the continent, saying "country inside, continent around." Part 2, retrieval misses from day 17, a review of grade 1: sort eight cards into land (hill, island, plain, mountain) and water (river, lake, ocean, pond). **Ask.** How is a river different from an ocean? Expect: a river flows in a line and meets a larger water; an ocean is huge salt water. A part under a third needed becomes a small group in Pairs work.

#### 12 min. Features of the Americas

Review: in unit 1 you read a key, with a wavy line for a river and triangles for mountains; in grade 1 you learned river, lake and mountain. Read the key, then the North America and South America pages. Say each place with a compass word, a finger on the arrow:
- The Rocky Mountains lie in the west of North America.
- The Great Lakes lie along the border of the United States and Canada.
- The Andes lie along the west of South America.
- The Amazon river crosses the north of South America.

Show each on the page, on Card C, then on the globe. Pip says: "The Great Lakes lie in South America." Beside it: "They lie along the border of the United States and Canada, in North America." **Ask.** Where do the Andes lie in South America? Expect: in the west, along its edge. Last, read one page's credit line and ask the three questions from unit 1, 2 minutes: who made it (Curio Class), when and where made (the record does not say).

#### 8 min. Pairs work

Pairs place the four labels on the practice map ([[s02-P18-1]]), then check each against the wall Atlas and say "The ___ lie in the ___ of ___" ([[s02-P18-2]]). Small groups: north and south reversed, 2 minutes with the arrow on the globe; any part of the repair not taught to the class. First placements are practice.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "features of the Americas" under "Where are these places?"

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Mark 1 minute on the blank crop. Read aloud: "Circle where the Andes are" (s02-X18). The six come to the globe, turned a new way, one at a time, 45 seconds each with the swap, about 4.5 minutes, each dealt one of (s02-X18b), (s02-X18c), (s02-X18d) or (s02-X18e). A feature on the wrong side or another continent is error J.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball with the Americas drawn on it, or a map.

**Skip.** The repair cards and the credit line.

- **10 min. Find them.** [[s02-P18-1]] Your child places four paper labels on a map of the Americas.
  **Say.** "Where do the Andes lie?" Expect: in the west of South America.
- **15 min. Say it.** Point to the north arrow and say sentences together.
  **Say.** "Is the Amazon in the north or the south of South America?" Expect: the north.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X18]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X18b]] [[s02-X18c]] [[s02-X18d]] [[s02-X18e]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one feature for each continent. Longer: a child says which feature lies west of the Great Lakes.

### Words and supports

Words: feature, mountains, river, west, north. Frame: "The ___ lie in the ___ of ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 opens with error J: trace the Andes or the Amazon on a new outline, then the globe, with the north arrow. Then eight features of the other five continents.
