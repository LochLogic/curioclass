# Globe prompt cards (days 21 to 27)

Twelve cards, ours, each with the name on it and a small drawing. At each hearing a child draws four cards and shows each place at the class globe, 15 seconds a card. Each child is heard three times across days 21 to 27, covering all seven continents (and one feature each) and the five oceans. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

| Card | Name | Kind |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | North America and the Rocky Mountains | continent and feature |
| 2 | South America and the Andes | continent and feature |
| 3 | Europe and the Alps | continent and feature |
| 4 | Africa and the Nile | continent and feature |
| 5 | Asia and the Himalayas | continent and feature |
| 6 | Australia and the Great Barrier Reef | continent and feature |
| 7 | Antarctica and its ice sheet | continent and feature |
| 8 | the Pacific Ocean | ocean |
| 9 | the Atlantic Ocean | ocean |
| 10 | the Indian Ocean | ocean |
| 11 | the Arctic Ocean | ocean |
| 12 | the Southern Ocean | ocean |

## Key (teacher)

Place on the globe by compass word: Rocky Mountains in the west of North America; Andes along the west of South America; Alps in central Europe; Nile in northeastern Africa; Himalayas in southern Asia; the Reef in the sea off northeastern Australia; the ice sheet on Antarctica. Oceans as on the Atlas ocean page. Book each placement on the record sheet as shown, shown with help or not yet seen.
