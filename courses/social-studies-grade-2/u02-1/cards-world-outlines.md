# World Outline Cards

Say first: "These outlines are drawn for our class from the real shapes of the lands." Plain black line drawing on white, one card each, a north arrow in a corner of each. Credit line on every card: Outline drawn by Curio Class.

## Card A: the world, Atlantic in the middle (teaching, day 17)

**Picture:** The seven continents as one flat card with the Atlantic Ocean in the middle, so the Americas lie on the left and Europe, Africa and Asia on the right; Australia lower right; Antarctica a long strip along the bottom. A north arrow in the top left. The shapes are lettered by the key: A North America, B South America, C Europe, D Africa, E Asia, F Australia, G Antarctica. A key box lists the seven letters with the seven names.

**Say:** Name each shape with a hand on it, then find the same land on the globe.

## Card B: the blank card with lettered shapes (pairs work, day 17)

**Picture:** The same outline with the letters H, J, K, L, M, N, P in the seven shapes and no names. A north arrow in the top left. Seven name tags to cut out are on the blank sheet.

## Card C: the Americas crop (teaching crop, day 18)

**Picture:** North America and South America only, the Pacific on the left, the Atlantic on the right, a north arrow top left, no names and no features drawn.

## Card D: two flat copies for the Pacific join (day 20)

**Picture:** Two copies of Card A on separate sheets, each with the Pacific Ocean cut at its edge, so that taped edge to edge, the two copies show the Pacific as one water.

## Key (teacher)

Card A letters: A North America, B South America, C Europe, D Africa, E Asia, F Australia, G Antarctica. Card B letters: H North America, J South America, K Europe, L Africa, M Asia, N Australia, P Antarctica. Do not print this key on any card the children mark.
