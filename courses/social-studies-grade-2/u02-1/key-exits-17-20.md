# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 17 to 20

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-17-20.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 17: Africa is T; Europe is S; Antarctica is the strip along the bottom, south of Africa. Day 18: the Andes are the west position. Amazon: the long river across the north of South America. Day 19: the Reef is the sea position off the northeast; the card drawn gives the feature. Day 20: shapes 1 and 2 are the Pacific; 3 the Atlantic; 4 the Indian; 5 the Arctic; 6 the Southern; Great Lakes is left alone. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
