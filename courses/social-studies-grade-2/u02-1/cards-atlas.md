# World Feature Atlas

Each page has a drawing, one key, and a label of at most three short sentences read aloud. Every page carries the credit line: Drawing by Curio Class. Ask the three questions of one credit line on days 18 and 19: who made it, when, where made (the page does not say where; say "the record does not say"). Every page shows homes, farms, cities or work, not only a wild place, so no one page stands for a whole continent. The Europe, Africa and Antarctica labels do not say "north of Africa" or "south of Africa", because the day 17 exit asks exactly that.

## The key (read once; unit 1's key is read the same way)

**Picture:** A box with seven small signs: a triangle, mountains; a wavy line, a river; a blue shape, a lake; dots, desert; a ring, a reef; white hatching, an ice sheet; a small house, a city or town.

## North America

**Picture:** A harbour city with tall buildings, a wheat farm with a barn in the foreground, and a snowy mountain range far behind. Triangles run down the west of the page. Blue lake shapes lie along a line across the north.
**Label:** North America lies in the northwest of the globe. The Rocky Mountains run down its west. The Great Lakes lie along the border of the United States and Canada.

## South America

**Picture:** A city on a hillside beside a market square, a terraced farm, and a wide river across the north, bordered by green forest. Triangles run down the west edge.
**Label:** South America lies south of North America. The Andes run along its west. The Amazon river crosses its north.

## Europe

**Picture:** A town square with a clock tower, a farm field of green and gold, and a river with a bridge. A white-topped mountain range stands in the middle.
**Label:** Europe lies west of Asia. The Alps are mountains in central Europe. The Danube river flows across central and southeastern Europe to the Black Sea.

## Africa

**Picture:** A big city with towers and a train, a green field beside a river, a market with people buying fruit, and a quiet sand desert across the top. A long river runs up the east.
**Label:** Africa lies east of South America. The Sahara is a very large desert across northern Africa. The Nile river flows north through northeastern Africa.

## Asia

**Picture:** A busy city street with shops and a bus, a rice field with workers, and a row of snow peaks beyond.
**Label:** Asia lies east of Europe. The Himalayas, very high mountains, lie in southern Asia.

## Australia

**Picture:** A coastal city with a harbour and houses, a farm with sheep, a dry red plain in the middle, and a ring of coral in the sea off the northeast.
**Label:** Australia lies south of Asia. The Outback is the dry land in its middle. The Great Barrier Reef lies in the sea off its northeast.

## Antarctica

**Picture:** A white land with a station of small round buildings, a tractor on the ice and two people in warm coats. Hatching covers the land.
**Label:** Antarctica lies at the bottom of the globe, south of Australia and South America. A great ice sheet covers most of the land. Scientists live and work at research stations there.

## The ocean page (day 20)

**Picture:** The world in one piece with the five oceans in five tints and a name in each: Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Arctic, Southern. A north arrow top left.
**Label:** The Pacific lies between the Americas and Asia and Australia. The Atlantic lies between the Americas and Europe and Africa. The Indian lies between Africa, Asia and Australia. The Arctic lies round the North Pole. The Southern lies round Antarctica.
