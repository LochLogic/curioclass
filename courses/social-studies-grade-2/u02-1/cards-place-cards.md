# Place Cards (eight)

Each card is ours and invented for practice. Every card prints "invented for practice" and its maker, Curio Class. The teacher reads each card aloud once. No child is asked to choose against where their family lives.

## 1. Palm Cove

**Picture:** A warm bay with palm trees, a small beach, fishing boats, and a covered market by the water. A thermometer reads warm.

## 2. Cedar Hill Row

**Picture:** A town on a green hill with tall trees, a bakery, a school and a path to a lookout. A thermometer reads cool.

## 3. Dry Wash

**Picture:** A flat sandy valley with few plants, a well with a windmill, and low houses with thick walls. A thermometer reads hot.

## 4. Frost Basin

**Picture:** A wide dry valley covered with snow, a few low shrubs, a fuel store and thick-coated people. A thermometer reads cold.

## 5. Cove Harbor

**Picture:** A busy port with cranes, ships, a ferry and a street of shops. A thermometer reads mild.

## 6. Terrace Row

**Picture:** Green steps cut into a hillside with small farms, a footpath and a village with a market. A thermometer reads mild.

## 7. Snow Station

**Picture:** A small research station on an ice plain, round buildings, a tractor and two scientists in warm coats. A thermometer reads very cold.

## 8. Lantern Market

**Picture:** A big city square with lamps, stalls, a bus stop and a library. A thermometer reads warm.

## Key (teacher)

The cards are for the hook on day 17 (Palm Cove) and for day 27's choosing. None repeats a place used in an exit card, the day 28 packet or a day 29 fresh task.
