# Ocean Cards (day 20)

Four cards, ours, each with a drawing and a one-sentence label read aloud. Credit line on every card: Drawing by Curio Class, made for this lesson. Pairs choose one card and say "Oceans matter because ___."

## Card 1: Homes and food

**Picture:** A school of fish swimming over coral, a sea turtle, and a fisher in a small boat pulling in a net.
**Label:** Many living things make their homes in the ocean, and people gather food there.

## Card 2: Ships carry goods

**Picture:** A large ship far out at sea with stacked crates on its deck and a small port town on the horizon.
**Label:** Ships carry goods across the oceans from one land to another.

## Card 3: Sea water makes rain

**Picture:** The sea with a sun above it and thin lines rising from the water, a cloud over the land, and rain falling on a green hillside.
**Label:** Sun warms the sea and its water rises. Later it falls again as rain.

## Card 4: Most of the water

**Picture:** A ball of the Earth with most of its surface blue, and a small bar beside it: a tall blue bar for ocean water and a short bar for all other water.
**Label:** Most of the water on Earth is in the ocean.

## Key (teacher)

The four reasons rest on NOAA's ocean facts pages and the USGS water cycle page (sources.csv). The exit on day 26 uses a different Ocean Life Card with a harbour ship; the shared writing on day 26 uses cards 3 and 4 only.
