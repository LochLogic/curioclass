# Fresh task cards, day 57

Every card prints "invented for practice" and "model". This is a model story, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for. Plain black line drawing on white. Never from the assessment.

## Group 1: Madagascar Crop and a rice chain

Maps are redrawn by Curio Class in 2026 from Natural Earth map data (public domain). Production draws no border that anyone disputes as settled. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written).
**Madagascar Crop, physical:** An island in the Indian Ocean with a highland area marked and a coast. Key: a triangle is a mountain, blue is water. **Political:** the island as one country with a star for the capital, Antananarivo, and a thick line around its coast. Key: a star is a capital, a thick line is a border. (Source 57A.)
**Rice chain:** A bag of rice. Four pictured people: a farmer, a boat crew, a market stall and a cook at a school canteen. The group says maker, carrier, seller and user.

## Group 2: two country cards (invented)

**Country Wen:** A library and a lighthouse. The group matches the library and the lighthouse each to the picture of what it is for. The king opens the harvest fair and a council plans the roads.
**Country Oba:** Voters mark ballots for a leader. The group sorts Oba under voting and Wen under not voting.

## Group 3: three model labels

**Tin kettle:** Made in Ghana, sold in Spain. **Wool mat:** Made in Chile, sold in Japan. **Song-writing service:** done in Brazil, paid for by a theater in Canada. The group slides a counter along each label from made to sold, puts each in the IN box or the OUT box seen from where it was made, and points to the service.

## Group 4: Source Card E and a claim

**Source Card E:** A picture of a drum in a window. Credit: "Made by Curio Class", the date line is missing. **Say:** "The card does not say when." The group rebuilds a claim: "I think ___ because ___. Source Card E shows ___, made by ___."

## Key (teacher)

Group 1: the island is one country; the chain has four different people, the farmer the maker. Group 2: the library so people can read and borrow books, the lighthouse so ships can find the coast safely, the council runs the government; Oba under voting, Wen not. Group 3: kettle Ghana to Spain, mat Chile to Japan, service Brazil to Canada; the service is the song-writing. Group 4: a reason, the named card, the maker, and the date unknown.
