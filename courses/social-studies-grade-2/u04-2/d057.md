# Day 57: Try it again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 56 into four groups from the record sheet: items 1 and 5 (errors A, B, I and R); item 2 (errors C, D, E, M and N); items 3 and 4 (errors F, G, H and O); items 6 and 2b (errors K and P). Lay each group's cards. Name today's six, the children least heard. Say nobody's page is held up, and every card is a model made up by Curio Class. Key: key-exits-52-57.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opener

Read aloud the class answer on the question board. Hand back day 56's assessment and written task, marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen: (s04-E1) maps, (s04-E4) countries, (s04-E10) trade, (s04-E19) claim and source. Name the groups.

#### 10 min. Teaching by group

Meet each group in turn; the rest work in pairs from the group move strip. Group 1, 3 minutes: the Madagascar Crop; [[s04-P57-1]] Then the rice chain: [[s04-P57-2]] Group 2, 3 minutes: Country Wen and Country Oba; [[s04-P57-3]] Then [[s04-P57-4]] Group 3, 2 minutes: three labels; [[s04-P57-5]] Then [[s04-P57-6]] and [[s04-P57-7]] Group 4, 2 minutes: Source Card E; [[s04-P57-8]] Expect: the card does not say. Then [[s04-P57-9]] Pip says: "I think it because I like it." Say beside it: the card shows it. Group 2's Pip also says the ferry card's wrong idea (error N): the government keeps the ferry so people can cross. Say each group's right idea aloud once: one country, two maps; a government's work has a purpose; the same good goes out of one country and comes into another; a claim names its card and its maker.

#### 9 min. Fresh task

Each group works one new card written on the board, in pairs: the mango chain [[s04-P57-10]]; the clinic and the fire tower [[s04-P57-11]]; a bamboo chair label [[s04-P57-12]]; Source Card F, a kite photograph, maker named, date missing [[s04-P57-13]] Walk round; give feedback on each. Secure children say the same trade from the other country's side.

#### 5 min. Feedback and the self-check strip

Each child marks one box in each row of the strip and points to one piece of work: [[s04-P57-14]] Collect.

#### 2 min. Question board

Revise the class answer in the children's words.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X57]], 1 minute. The six stand at your table: [[s04-X57b]] for three, [[s04-X57c]] for the other three; six turns of 25 seconds, 2.5 minutes. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. What is still not shown goes to unit 5's retrieval.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: tell one thing you improved.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Your child finds the part of the check that felt hardest and says why. **Say.** "What was hard?" Expect: any honest answer.
- **15 min. Try again.** Your child picks the hardest part of the check and tries it again with you. **Say.** "What changed this time?" Expect: a reason or a named card. [[s04-P57-14]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X57]] [[s04-X57b]] [[s04-X57c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the teaching and the exit only. Longer: a secure child tells a trade from the other side.

### Words and supports

Words: import, export, source, maker, claim, purpose. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___ or unknown." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 opens unit 5: why people move to new places. What is still not shown today goes to retrieval there. A child shown on [[s04-X57]] and [[s04-X57b]] keeps the trade idea; error O shows as OUT circled or "it comes in"; error N as "it is a truck".
