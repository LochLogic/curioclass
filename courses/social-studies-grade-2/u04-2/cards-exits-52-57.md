# Exit cards, days 52 to 57

Every card prints "invented for practice" and "model" in small letters. Where a real country is named, the card says: "This is a model story, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for." Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. No brand name appears.

## Day 52 exit card: Tile Case

**Picture:** A tile workshop with a stack of tiles, a bag of clay and an empty pot of glaze. Beside it a designer at a table drawing a pattern on paper. A line of a plane flying between two countries.
**Mark 1:** Write G or S under the designer's pattern work. **Line added for mark 2:** "The workshop has all the clay it needs, its glaze pot is empty, and nobody near it draws patterns." **Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say why the workshop pays the designer."
**Spoken, three of the six:** "Say whether a tile workshop can buy a designer's pattern work from another country." **Spoken, the other three:** "Clay is dug near the workshop, and the workshop still buys clay from abroad. Say one reason it might."

## Day 53 exit card: two model labels

**Picture:** A basket with hands weaving, labelled "Made in Vietnam", sold in the United States. A bowl labelled "Made in the United States", sold in Italy. A world map card with the three countries marked.
**Mark 1:** Draw an arrow for the basket from where it was made to where it is sold. **Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Point with your finger from where the bowl was made to where it is sold."
**Spoken, three of the six:** "Say why a buyer in the United States wants a basket made in Vietnam." **Spoken, the other three:** "Say how the person who uses the basket is linked to a person far away."

## Day 54 exit card: Purchase Case

**Picture:** Three things on a table: a globe, an atlas and a poster, numbered in the order the class likes them (1, 2, 3). A check mark by the globe.
**Mark:** The class chooses the globe. Circle the best other thing the class gave up.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Country Dova and Country Pell agree to use one safety rule for the boxes on their trucks. Say whether they are now one country or still two." (Truck Case: two country outlines, two trucks and one rule sign.)

## Day 55 exit card: Repair Case

**Picture:** A workshop with a bag of screws and a technician in another country giving advice by telephone, a coin purse changing hands.
**Mark 1:** Circle the service. **Spoken, three of the six, two small cards:** "Repair Card: Made by Curio Class, 2026, invented for practice: the workshop's staff have not learned this repair." "Everyone knows workshops need advice." Point to the card that gives a source for the reason.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say what the workshop gets when it pays the technician."

## Day 57 exit card: a Kenya label

**Picture:** A label "Made in Kenya; sold in the United States", a world map card with the two places marked.
**Mark:** Circle IN or OUT for the good, seen from the United States.
**Spoken, three of the six:** "Seen from Kenya, does the same good come in or go out?" **Spoken, the other three:** "A government picks up the trash from every street. Say what that is for."
