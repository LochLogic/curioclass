# Repair cards, days 53 to 57

Every card prints "invented for practice" and "model". Plain black line drawing on white. Each repair is at most 5 minutes. Pip says the wrong idea and the right idea is said beside it.

## Two suppliers (error F, day 53)

**Picture:** A Kelda school needs thick paper. Tarn makes thick paper. Orrin also makes thick paper but sells it for more coins. The child matches the buyer's need to the two suppliers and says why the school may choose Tarn.
**Say:** "A country can make paper and still buy paper that costs less, or a skill it does not have." Pip: "A country trades only when it has nothing."

## Paid work (error G, day 53 small group)

**Picture:** A Tarn guide who leads a Kelda group over a mountain pass; a coin purse changes hands. The child follows the payment and the work along an arrow.
**Say:** "A guide's work can be bought from another country too." Pip: "Only things can be traded."

## Two-way route (error H, day 54)

**Picture:** A card "wool, Made in Kelda, sold in Tarn" and a card "salt, Made in Tarn, sold in Kelda", each with an arrow on a small map. The child labels each arrow from each country's viewpoint.
**Say:** "Where it is sold and where it was made are two places." Pip: "Sold here means made here."

## Maker-to-user chain (errors I and R, day 54 small group)

**Picture:** A clay bowl: a potter (maker), a cart driver (carrier), a shop clerk (seller), a child at school (user). The child builds the chain of four pictured people.
**Say:** "A maker made it, a carrier moved it, a seller sold it, a user uses it." Pip: "The shop made it all." Pip: "It comes from far, so we are linked."

## New route traced (error O, day 54)

**Picture:** A spoon "Made in Tarn, sold in Kelda" and a lamp "Made in Kelda, sold in Tarn". The child traces each with a finger and says in or out from Kelda.
**Say:** "An import comes into the buyer's country." Pip: "An import goes out of the buyer's country."

## Two-option purchase (error J, day 55)

**Picture:** A class can buy paint or clay but has room for one. It chooses paint. The child circles what it gave up.
**Say:** "The best other thing we gave up is the clay." Pip: "Opportunity cost is everything I want."

## Before and after an agreement (error L, day 55 small group)

**Picture:** Kelda and Orrin each have a border. They agree to use one pallet size. The picture shows the same two borders before and after, with one rule sign between them.
**Say:** "Two countries can share a rule and stay two." Pip: "An agreement makes two countries one."

## Claim from a card (error K, day 57)

**Picture:** A fresh invented card and the frame "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___ or unknown."
**Say:** "I think it because the card shows it, not because I like it." Pip: "I think it because I like it."

## Ferry card (error N, day 57)

**Picture:** A ferry crossing a river and a sign "the government keeps the ferry".
**Say:** "The government keeps the ferry so people can cross the river." Pip: "The government has a ferry."

## Key (teacher)

Each repair names its letter and the right idea as above. Paper from Tarn for the school; follow the coin and the work; wool arrow Kelda to Tarn, salt arrow Tarn to Kelda; four people along the bowl's arrow; the spoon comes in to Kelda and the lamp goes out; the clay is given up; Kelda and Orrin stay two countries.
