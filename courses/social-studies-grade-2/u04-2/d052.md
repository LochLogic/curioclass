# Day 52: Why one country buys from another

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the small group (at most six) and today's six, the children least heard. Put the five story cards in order. Say aloud: Kelda and Tarn are made up, and so is every story. Key: key-exits-52-57.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 51's written task, one cue each (1 minute). Repair for s04-X51 (error Q), 4 minutes, if more than a third missed: the Lune caption card. **Ask.** [[s04-P52-1]] Expect: the picture shows beads. Say: "Best is what someone thinks." Errors P and E go to the small group.

#### 3 min. Retrieval from grade 1

Review from grade 1: a good is a thing you can hold; a service is work done for you. Show the loaf and the haircut. Pairs: **Ask.** [[s04-P52-4]] Walk round; a pair that names a thing goes to day 53's warm-up.

#### 7 min. Four reasons

Review: resource (from nature, unit 3) and skill (kindergarten). Read the four cards. Nails: no iron hill. Cloth: a weaving skill. Apples: something different. Bricks: the Tarn ones cost fewer coins. **Ask.** [[s04-P52-6]] Pip says: "A country trades only when it has nothing." Say beside it: a country can buy what costs less, or a skill it does not have. Fifth card: act the sail mender, a paper coin purse passing from Kelda to Tarn. Say: "Work crosses a border too." Pip says: "Only things can be traded." Say beside it: a mender's work can be bought across a border.

#### 6 min. Arrows and the chain

New: trade between countries is people in one country buying from people in another. Draw Tarn, Kelda and an arrow for the nails. Say: "The arrow runs from where it was made to where it is sold. Into Kelda is an import. Out of Tarn is an export." Children trace the arrow in the air and say "import" as it lands. Four children hold the chain cards and pass the nails box along the arrow, each saying their word: maker (producer, unit 3), carrier, seller, user (consumer, unit 3). Two minutes in all.

#### 8 min. Work

Each pair holds a reason picture and draws a buyer story: [[s04-P52-9]] Then **Ask.** [[s04-P52-10]] Meanwhile the small group meets you for 5 minutes: error P, [[s04-P52-2]] (s04-X51b) Expect: the card does not say; error E, [[s04-P52-3]] (s04-X51c) Expect: no, some people.

#### 2 min. Question board

Read aloud: "Why exchange goods and services?" Add the four reasons.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X52]]; the Tile Case is new. The six stand at your table: [[s04-X52b]] for all six, then [[s04-X52c]] for three and [[s04-X52d]] for the other three. Twelve turns at 25 seconds each, with the step, make 5 minutes. Finished children look at a picture book. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four reason pictures on the board; children draw their buyer story on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: act the story with a cup and two spoons.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Four reasons.** **Say.** "Why does the builder buy nails from Tarn?" Expect: Kelda has no iron hill. [[s04-P52-6]]
- **15 min. My buyer story.** Your child draws a buyer story and tells it. [[s04-P52-9]] [[s04-P52-10]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X52]] [[s04-X52b]] [[s04-X52c]] [[s04-X52d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop apples and bricks. Longer: tell the nails story from Tarn's side.

### Words and supports

Words: trade between countries, import, export, maker, carrier, seller, user. Pointing and home-language reasons count.
