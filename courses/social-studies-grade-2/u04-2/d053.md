# Day 53: Where it was made, where it is sold

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the wall world map with the yarn. Name the small group (at most six) and today's six, the children least heard. Say aloud: every label is made up, a model, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for. Key: key-exits-52-57.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back s04-X52 and the buyer drawing s04-P52-9, one cue each (1 minute). Repair for s04-X52b (error F), 4 minutes, if F was missed most: the two paper cards, Tarn and Orrin. **Ask.** [[s04-P53-1]] Say: "A country can make paper and still buy paper that costs less." Pip says: "A country trades only when it has nothing." Error G goes to the small group.

#### 4 min. Read a label

Review: the three questions from unit 1 (who made it, when, where). Show the pencil cup label. **Ask.** [[s04-P53-3]] Expect: the label does not say. Say: "Made in Portugal, used in our class, so ___" and let a few children finish it with a reason from day 52. A label proves the stated origin, not the whole journey.

#### 8 min. Trace it

Review: the arrow runs from where it was made to where it is sold (day 52). Show the notebook label, "Made in the United States", sold in Canada. **Ask.** [[s04-P53-4]] Then **Ask.** [[s04-P53-5]] Pin the yarn between them. Say: "Out of the United States is an export; into Canada is an import." Pip says: "Sold here means made here." Say beside it: made and sold are two places. Show the drum, made in Mexico, sold in the United States; pin the yarn, say "into the United States, an import", and name who made, carried, sold and uses it. Pip says: "An import goes out of the buyer's country." Say beside it: an import comes in.

#### 11 min. Pairs work

Each pair has the rug, lamp and kite labels, a map and two counters. Pairs: [[s04-P53-6]] Then [[s04-P53-7]] (both stay in the folder). Each child, to the partner: **Ask.** [[s04-P53-8]] Each child fills the four boxes on the page: [[s04-P53-9]] (stays in the folder). The small group meets you for 5 minutes: error G, [[s04-P53-2]] Expect: the guide's work.

#### 3 min. Question board

Read aloud: "Why do countries trade for it?" Add a reason from the labels.

#### 5 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X53]], 2 minutes. The six stand at your table: [[s04-X53b]] for all six, then [[s04-X53c]] for three and [[s04-X53d]] for the other three. Twelve turns at 25 seconds each, with the step, make 5 minutes. Finished children look at a picture book. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map on the board and the labels on cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No boxes or counters: two cups and two buttons.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Slide and say.** Your child slides a button from where a label says it was made to where it is sold. **Say.** "Made in ___, used in ___, so ___." Expect: any reason from yesterday. [[s04-P53-8]]
- **15 min. Four people.** [[s04-P53-9]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X53]] [[s04-X53b]] [[s04-X53c]] [[s04-X53d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two labels. Longer: tell the lamp story from Egypt's side, import and export swapped.

### Words and supports

Words: import, export, maker, carrier, seller, user, IN, OUT. Frame: "Made in ___, used in ___, so ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 teaches that a choice gives up another thing, and that two countries can share a rule and stay two. It opens with one repair, 5 minutes: error H as the arrow started where it is sold for [[s04-X53]]; error O as the finger reversed for [[s04-X53b]]; error I as "the shop made it" and error R as only a place name for [[s04-X53d]]. A route is traced again from each country's side.
