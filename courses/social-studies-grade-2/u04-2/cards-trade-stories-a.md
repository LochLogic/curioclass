# Trade Stories A, day 55

Three model cases read once, each with a map outline of Japan and Saudi Arabia. This is a model story, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for. Credit on each card, read aloud: "Made by Curio Class, 2026, invented for practice." Each caption names its buyer's reason. Plain black line drawing on white. Held back and unseen until day 55. Maps are redrawn by Curio Class in 2026 from Natural Earth map data (public domain). Production draws no border that anyone disputes as settled. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written).

## Card 1: dates

**Picture:** A buyer in Japan, a grower in Saudi Arabia, a box of dates and an arrow between them. **Caption:** "A buyer in Japan buys dates from a grower in Saudi Arabia. The buyer wants a fruit that is different from what is on the shelf." (a different product)

## Card 2: camera

**Picture:** A buyer in Saudi Arabia, a workshop in Japan, a camera and an arrow. **Caption:** "A buyer in Saudi Arabia buys a camera from a workshop in Japan. The workshop knows how to make cameras." (a skill)

## Card 3: guide

**Picture:** A visitor from Japan, a guide in Saudi Arabia, a coin purse and a map. **Caption:** "A visitor from Japan pays a guide in Saudi Arabia. The guide knows the place." (knowledge, a service) This card's date line is missing: "the card does not say".

## Source questions

Who made it: Curio Class. When: 2026, and for the third card "the card does not say". Where: in our class. The teacher composes no claim.

## Key (teacher)

Reasons: a different product, a skill, knowledge of a place. A claim may stress resources, skills or cooperation; each child says why. No officeholder is named. The cards say these are model stories, not records of shipments or of what either country is known for.
