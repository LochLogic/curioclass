# Trade story cards, day 52

Kelda and Tarn are invented countries; each card prints "invented for practice" and "model story". One actor in each scene. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Five story cards

1. **Nails.** A Kelda builder at a doorway holds a box of nails from Tarn. Kelda has no iron hill. **Reason picture:** an empty hill with a question mark. **Say:** "The builder buys nails because Kelda has no iron." (a resource)
2. **Cloth.** A Kelda tailor holds cloth woven in Tarn. **Reason picture:** a hand weaving on a loom. **Say:** "The tailor buys cloth because Tarn weavers have a skill." (a skill)
3. **Apples.** A Tarn cook holds a basket of Kelda apples. **Reason picture:** two different plates of food. **Say:** "The cook buys apples for something different to eat." (a different product)
4. **Bricks.** A Kelda builder holds bricks from Tarn. **Reason picture:** two price tags, one smaller. **Say:** "The builder buys bricks that cost less." (a lower price)
5. **Sail mender.** A Tarn mender kneels beside a Kelda boat's sail with a needle; a coin purse changes hands. **Say:** "Work crosses a border too."

## Said aloud

Pip says F: "A country trades only when it has nothing." The right idea is said beside it: "A country can buy what costs less, or a skill it does not have." Then Pip says G: "Only things can be traded." Beside it: "A mender's work can be bought from another country too."

## Pairs work

Each pair matches the four story cards (nails, cloth, apples, bricks) to the four reason pictures.

## Key (teacher)

Nails with the empty hill; cloth with the weaving hand; apples with the two plates; bricks with the smaller price tag. The sail mender is the service. Price, skill, resource and variety are the reasons; none says a country has nothing.
