# Teacher key: Day 56 packet (kept back until day 56)

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (packet-assessment.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Item 1: the physical map; the political map by the thick border line in its key; Africa is a continent that holds many countries and Botswana is one country. Item 2: people, land with borders, a government with leaders, or laws; so people have clean water to drink; no, the job passes in the leader's family; elected representatives choose the prime minister; no, the card shows some people, not everyone. Item 3: the Peru workshop has the skill to weave caps or its cap costs less; the United States workshop has the skill to make pumps; the translator knows the language the instructions need. Item 4: an arrow from Peru to the United States for the cap; from the United States to Peru for the pump; the cap comes in; the pump goes out. Item 5: a weaver in Peru made the cap and sellers and carriers brought it; the workshop in Peru made it. Item 6: any reason that is a skill, product, price, resource or cooperation and fits the stories; a packet card named with what it shows; the card does not say who made Source Card D.
