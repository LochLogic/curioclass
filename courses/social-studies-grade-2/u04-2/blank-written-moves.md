# Written move for each group, day 57

Each group has one strip. The children work in pairs from the strip while the teacher meets other groups.

**Group 1 (A, B, I, R):** Look at the Madagascar Crop. Match each key picture to its meaning. Put the four rice people in order from maker to user.
**Group 2 (C, D, E, M, N):** Look at Country Wen and Country Oba. Match the library and the lighthouse to the picture of what each is for. Sort the two countries under voting or not.
**Group 3 (F, G, H, O):** Look at the three labels. Slide a counter along each label from made to sold. Put each label in the IN box or the OUT box, seen from where it was made. Point to the service.
**Group 4 (K):** Rebuild a claim from Source Card E. Name the card and who made it, or say the card does not say.

Secure children defend another viewpoint with the same source.
