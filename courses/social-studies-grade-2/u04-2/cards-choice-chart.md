# Choice chart cards, days 54 and 55

Invented for practice. Plain black line drawing on white. The chart has two boxes: "we chose" and "we gave up".

## The class's tokens

**Crayons card** and **ruler card**. The class has tokens for one. It chooses crayons. The ruler goes in the "we gave up" box: the best other thing.

## Tarn school

**Nails card** and **cloth card** (from Kelda). The school has one purchase. It chooses nails; the cloth is given up.

**Say:** "We chose ___ and gave up ___." The word is said and written: opportunity cost. Grade 1's choose-and-give-up mat is named as review. Pip says J: "Opportunity cost is everything I want."

## Key (teacher)

Crayons chosen, ruler given up; nails chosen, cloth given up. Opportunity cost is the best other thing given up, not everything wanted.
