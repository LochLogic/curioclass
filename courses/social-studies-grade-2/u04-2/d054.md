# Day 54: A choice gives up another thing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the choice chart on the wall: two boxes, "we chose" and "we gave up". Name the small group and today's six. Say aloud: Kelda, Tarn, Dova and Pell are made up, and so is every story. Key: key-exits-52-57.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 53's s04-X53, one cue each. Repair for s04-X53b (error H), 4 minutes, if H was missed most: the wool card, "Made in Kelda, sold in Tarn". **Ask.** [[s04-P54-1]] Say: "Made and sold are two places; wool comes into Tarn" (error O). Pip says: "Sold here means made here." Errors I and R (s04-X53d) go to the small group.

#### 3 min. Retrieval from grade 1

Review: reasons for choosing a map (unit 1) and the physical map from day 48. Show the physical map of the Africa pair. **Ask.** [[s04-P54-4]] A miss: show the political map.

#### 7 min. A choice gives up another

New word: opportunity cost. Say: "We can have crayons or a ruler, not both." The class chooses crayons; place the crayons card in "we chose" and the ruler card in "we gave up". Say: "The best other thing we gave up is the opportunity cost." Pip says: "Opportunity cost is everything I want." Say beside it: it is the one best other thing, not every wish. Then the Tarn school buys nails or cloth from Kelda, one only, and chooses nails. Pairs: [[s04-P54-5]] Then **Ask.** [[s04-P54-6]]

#### 6 min. Countries work together

Read the Shipping Story: Kelda and Tarn agree to use the same shipping form. **Ask.** [[s04-P54-7]] Expect: two. Say: "Two governments, two sets of borders, one shared form." Pip says: "An agreement makes two countries one." Say beside it: two countries can share a rule and stay two.

#### 6 min. A claim, modeled

Say and write: "I think the farm buys seed from Tarn because its own seed did not grow. The Seed Card shows the seed that did not grow, made by Curio Class." Review: the claim with a named source (unit 2). Pairs say the second case, each child in turn: **Ask.** [[s04-P54-8]] Meanwhile the small group meets you for 5 minutes: error I, [[s04-P54-2]] then error R, the same chain strip, 2 minutes. Pip says: "I think it because I like it." Say beside it: the card shows it.

#### 4 min. Revise day 51

Each child revises the four-row chart from the written cue: [[s04-P54-9]] The revised chart is kept for day 56.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X54]], 2 minutes. The six stand at your table: [[s04-X54b]] for each, 25 seconds with the step, 2.5 minutes. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Question board

Read aloud: "What do choices and agreements change?" Add one answer.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two boxes on the board and the cards as words; the chart is lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: two spoons for the choice, one drawn rule sign for the agreement.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Choose and give up.** **Say.** "We chose nails. What did we give up?" Expect: the cloth. [[s04-P54-6]]
- **15 min. A claim.** Your child says it with the frame. [[s04-P54-8]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X54]] [[s04-X54b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: drop the map review. Longer: a child tells the Tarn school's choice next year.

### Words and supports

Words: opportunity cost, choose, give up, agreement, source. Frames: "We chose ___ and gave up ___." "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.
