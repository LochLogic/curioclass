# Day 56 packet (kept back until day 56)

Read the whole packet aloud once, neutrally, and no more. One copy of each card for each child, laid flat, one pencil each. Do not teach, hint or confirm. No answer map is visible. Every card except Source Card D prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters, and each real country is a model: "This is a model story, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for." Plain black line drawing on white. No answer is discussed. Held back and unseen until day 56. The key is on the teacher's sheet only.

## Card 1: Africa Map Pair B

Maps are redrawn by Curio Class in 2026 from Natural Earth map data (public domain). Production draws no border that anyone disputes as settled. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written).
**Map A, physical:** Part of southern Africa. The Atlantic Ocean in blue. A large area of dots and tan is the Kalahari Desert. Key: dots on tan stand for desert, blue for water, green for grassland. No country lines.
**Map B, political:** The same part of southern Africa. Botswana, Namibia and South Africa are labelled. A thick black line is a border. The shades differ from map A's. Key: a thick line is a border, a shade is one country.

## Card 2: Country Cards C

**Japan, people card:** Six pictured people doing different things: a farmer, a student, a nurse, a bus driver, a singer, a cook. A flag in the corner. **Japan, land and borders:** an island outline with a coast. **Country Sund, government (invented):** voters, then elected representatives, then a prime minister who leads the government; the king in a side box marked "role set by the constitution". **Tokyo:** a city with a star. **Country Ovo, government (invented):** a leader and an arrow to the leader's child; the job passes in the leader's family. **Clean water supply:** a pipe, a tap and a tank, with a sign "the government keeps the water clean".

## Card 3: Trade Stories B

**Picture:** A map of the United States and Peru. Three scenes, one actor each. A cap woven by a workshop in Peru and sold to a buyer in the United States. A pump made by a workshop in the United States and used on a farm in Peru. A translator in Peru paid by a workshop in the United States to put its instructions into another language.

## Card 4: Source Card D

**Picture:** A photograph of a woven cap on a table. The credit lines read "Made by" and "Date" and both are left blank.

## The items, read aloud, one ask at a time

**Item 1 (4 minutes).** On Africa Map Pair B: "Point to the map that helps you find the Kalahari Desert." "Point to the map that helps you find Botswana's borders." "Say why Africa and Botswana are different kinds of places."
**Item 2 and item 2b (6 minutes).** On Country Cards C: "Say one thing that makes Japan a country." "Say how the clean water supply helps people." "Say whether the leader of Country Ovo is chosen by voting." "Say who chooses the prime minister of Country Sund." "On the people card, say whether the six pictured people show that everyone in Japan lives the same way." Item 2b is not asked: it is read from the retained day 54 revised four-row chart.
**Item 3 (4 minutes).** On Trade Stories B: "Say why the buyer in the United States chooses a cap made in Peru." "Say why the farm in Peru buys a pump from the United States workshop." "Say why the United States workshop pays a translator in Peru."
**Item 4 (4 minutes).** "On the Trade Stories B map, draw an arrow for the cap from where it was made to where it is sold." The same for the pump. "Seen from the United States, say which good comes in." "Seen from the United States, say which good goes out."
**Item 5 (3 minutes).** "A child in the United States uses the cap. Say how that child is linked to a person in Peru." "On Trade Stories B, say who made the cap."
**Item 6 (4 minutes).** Said to a partner for a minute, then recorded: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___ or unknown." "With Trade Stories B and Source Card D, say why these countries need each other." "Say a card you used and what it shows." "Say who made Source Card D."
