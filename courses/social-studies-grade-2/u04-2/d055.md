# Day 55: Why do countries need each other?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name the small group (at most six) and today's six. Read the three Trade Stories A cards once yourself. Say aloud: these are model stories made up by Curio Class, not records of real shipments or of what Japan or Saudi Arabia is known for. Hold the trade claim strip back until Say it. Key: key-exits-52-57.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 54's s04-X54 with a cue. Repair for s04-X54 (error J), 4 minutes, if J was missed most: the paint and clay cards; the class chose paint. **Ask.** [[s04-P55-1]] Say: "The best other thing we gave up is the clay." Pip says: "Opportunity cost is everything I want." Say beside it: one best other thing. Error L (s04-X54b) goes to the small group; the revised charts stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Read the question board's first answers from day 48, aloud. **Ask.** [[s04-P55-3]]

#### 8 min. Trade Stories A

Read each card once, a finger on its map: a Japanese buyer purchases dates from a Saudi grower, wanting a different fruit; a Saudi buyer purchases a camera from a Japanese workshop that knows how to make cameras; a Japanese visitor pays a Saudi guide who knows the place. Source questions for all three: who made them, Curio Class; when, 2026 for the dates and camera cards. **Ask.** [[s04-P55-4]] Expect: the card does not say. You compose no claim.

#### 6 min. Say it

Hand out the strip. Each child says the claim to a partner, three minutes each way: [[s04-P55-5]] The partner listens for a reason, a named card and the maker or "unknown". Children may stress resources, skills or cooperation, and each says why. Pip says: "I think it because I like it" (error K). Say beside it: the card shows it.

#### 8 min. Record it

This is the written task. Each child records the claim with the strip: [[s04-P55-6]] A partner or you write only the child's own words; spelling is not judged. The small group meets you for 3 minutes: error L, [[s04-P55-2]] Expect: two. Then scribe for children who ask.

#### 3 min. Another view

**Ask.** [[s04-P55-7]] This is practice: feedback only, no score.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X55]], 2 minutes. The six stand at your table: [[s04-X55b]] for three, [[s04-X55c]] for the other three; six turns of 25 seconds, 2.5 minutes. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the three stories from this page and draw each on the board; children write the claim on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: write the frame on a sheet.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Say it.** Your child reads or hears one story and says the claim. **Say.** "I think ___ because ___." Expect: a reason and a story card named. [[s04-P55-5]]
- **15 min. Write it.** Write only your child's words. [[s04-P55-6]] [[s04-P55-7]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X55]] [[s04-X55b]] [[s04-X55c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one story card only. Longer: tell the camera story from the other country's side.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, reason, source, maker, trade. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___ or unknown." Pointing and home-language reasons count.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 is the unit check; nothing new is taught and it has no repair. The claim of today is the written task practiced before it, and a fresh claim is scored on day 56. Error K shows as a liking in the frame for [[s04-X55b]]; error G as the screws circled for [[s04-X55]] or [[s04-X55c]].
