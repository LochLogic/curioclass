# Shipping Story, day 54

Kelda and Tarn are invented. The card prints "invented for practice" and "model story". A real agreement between two countries is not used: no page was readable in full, so only this model is told.

**Picture:** Two country outlines with a dashed border each. Two government houses, one in each country. A single shipping form drawn between them with a stamp from both.
**Story, read aloud:** Kelda and Tarn agree to use the same shipping form. The boxes cross the border with one kind of paper. There are two countries, one form, two governments and two sets of borders.
**Pip says L:** "An agreement makes two countries one." The right idea is said beside it: "Two countries can share a rule and stay two."

## Key (teacher)

Two borders, two governments and one shared form. A shared rule is cooperation, not a union.
