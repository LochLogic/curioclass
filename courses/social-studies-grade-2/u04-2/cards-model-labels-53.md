# Model labels, day 53

Every label prints "invented for practice" and "model". Where a real country is named, the card says: "This is a model story, not a record of real shipments or of what a country is known for." No brand name appears. Nothing is from a home. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Teacher's two labels

**Toy drum:** "Made in Mexico", sold in the United States. **Notebook:** "Made in the United States", sold in Canada.
The teacher draws each arrow on the wall world map, says in or out from the United States, and names who made, carried, sold and uses. Pip says H ("sold here means made here"), then O ("an import goes out of the buyer's country").

## Pairs' three labels

**Wool rug:** "Made in Turkey", sold in the United States. **Clay lamp:** "Made in the United States", sold in Egypt. **Kite:** "Made in Indonesia", sold in the United States.
Each pair draws an arrow from where made to where sold, writes IN or OUT seen from the United States, and says "Made in ___, used in ___, so ___" with a reason from day 52.

## Key (teacher)

Drum: Mexico to the United States, in. Notebook: the United States to Canada, out. Rug: Turkey to the United States, in. Lamp: the United States to Egypt, out. Kite: Indonesia to the United States, in. A label proves the stated origin, not the whole journey.
