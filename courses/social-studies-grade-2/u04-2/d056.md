# Day 56: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 55's written task, s04-P55-6, and exit ticket s04-X55, with a cue: that was practice; today a fresh claim counts. Split the class into thirds A, B and C. Say: answer alone, by pointing, circling, drawing, a few words or a scribe; pass any item. Each ask is read once. Every place on the cards is a model made up by Curio Class. Key: key-assessment.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the packet

Read the four cards aloud once: Africa Map Pair B, Country Cards C, Trade Stories B and Source Card D. Nothing is discussed.

#### 4 min. Item 1

On Africa Map Pair B, all point to the map for the Kalahari Desert (s04-E1) and for Botswana's borders (s04-E2). Everyone writes a few words on why Africa and Botswana differ (s04-E3).

#### 6 min. Item 2 and item 2b

On Country Cards C, all answer: what makes Japan a country (s04-E4); how clean water helps (s04-E5); is Country Ovo's leader chosen by voting (s04-E6); who chooses Country Sund's prime minister (s04-E7); do the six people show everyone in Japan lives alike (s04-E8). Item 2b (s04-E9) is not asked: after class read the retained day 54 chart; missing evidence stays not yet shown.

#### 4 min. Item 3

On Trade Stories B: why the buyer chooses the cap (s04-E10), why the farm buys the pump (s04-E11) and why the workshop pays a translator (s04-E12), answered by all on the page.

#### 4 min. Item 4

On the Trade Stories B map all draw an arrow for the cap (s04-E13) and the pump (s04-E14), then point to the good that comes in (s04-E15) and goes out (s04-E16), seen from the United States.

#### 3 min. Item 5

A child in the United States uses the cap: the link with a person in Peru (s04-E17) and who made the cap (s04-E18), written by all.

#### 4 min. Item 6

Each child says to a partner for a minute, then writes the frame: why these countries need each other (s04-E19), a card used and what it shows (s04-E20).

#### 5 min. Collect

Collect the work; a child still working finishes quietly. Each child writes who made Source Card D (s04-E21). After class mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Sort errors into day 57's four groups: items 1 and 5 (errors A, B, I and R); item 2 (errors C, D, E, M and N); items 3 and 4 (errors F, G, H and O); items 6 and 2b (errors K and P).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a cap from one country sold in another.

**Skip.** The thirds and the scribe.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read each question once; give no help.
  **Say.** "Who made the cap?" Expect: the workshop in Peru.
  **Say.** "Why do these countries need each other?" Expect: a reason and a named card.
- **20 min. Respond.** Talk about which part felt hardest; your child picks one to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one ask from each item.

### Words and supports

Words: continent, country, government, trade, import, export, source, maker. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___ or unknown." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 hands back day 56 in four groups, with the written task from today, and each child tries the hardest part fresh.
