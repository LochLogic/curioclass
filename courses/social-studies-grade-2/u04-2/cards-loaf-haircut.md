# Loaf and haircut cards, day 52 retrieval from grade 1

Invented for practice. Two cards for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white.

**Card 1:** A loaf of bread. **Card 2:** A person having a haircut.
**Say:** "Sort them: a thing you can hold, or work done for you."

## Key (teacher)

The loaf is a good, a thing you can hold. The haircut is a service, work done for you. A miss goes to the day 53 Return part when more than a third missed, otherwise to a small group.
