# Repair card, day 69

Printed "invented for practice". Each repair is at most 5 minutes. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## A notice and its short version (error F, day 69)

**Card 1, the complete notice (invented for practice):** "The library opens at nine. Bring your card. Children may borrow four books." **Card 2, the accurate short version:** "The library opens at nine." The group fills "Both say ___; only ___ says ___."
**Say:** "It is shorter. Leaving out is not disagreeing." Pip: "The short one left out the books, so it lied."

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Both say the library opens at nine; only the complete notice says bring your card and borrow four books. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
