# Fresh task cards, day 71

Every card prints "invented for practice" except the two real nation cards. Never from the assessment. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Group 1: Hill Road Card

"Bix's family loves the sea. The road to the sea town is steep and rocky. The family climbs it with all their bags and moves to a house by the sea. Afterward, Bix learns to swim." The group says chosen, with "could they have stayed?" yes. A second card: "Officials say the dam above Bix's old village may break. Everyone must leave today." forced. The group puts a reason, a move and afterward in order, and points to the line that supports each answer.

## Group 2: Valley Card

"Nia lived in a mountain village. The village had little water. Nia moved down to a city by a river, to have water to drink." A small map with a mountain and a river, no arrow. The group draws the arrow from the mountain to the river city and says the reason: little water.

## Group 3: six small cards and two tellers

Six cards to sort: Rio buys a ticket and takes a plane to a new job (invented); Taj is taken from home and put on a ship against his will (invented); the Chickasaw card from day 64 (real, here first); Una comes to join a sister (invented); Vee is told to leave by officials and cannot stay (invented); the Eastern Band card from day 64 (real, here first). Two tellers: "Odo hoped to see the sea." "Pia hoped to find a school." The group says "Both ___, but ___."

## Group 4: whale cards and bridge picture (written on slips)

A fisher on the pier saw a whale spout and wrote: a whale spouted by the pier. A shop flyer says the same words, copied from the fisher. A school poster says the same words, copied from the flyer. The group traces the poster back with a finger and says who saw the whale. A picture of a stone bridge with two lamps; the group says a new sign for the bridge with only things they can check on the picture.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Group 1: chosen; forced; yes, they could have stayed in the first card. Group 2: from the mountain to the city; little water. Group 3: chose: Rio, Una; brought by force: Taj, Vee; here first: the Chickasaw card, the Eastern Band card; the tellers hoped for different things. Group 4: the poster came from the flyer, the flyer from the fisher, and the fisher saw the whale; a sign such as a stone bridge with two lamps. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
