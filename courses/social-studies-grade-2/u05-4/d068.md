# Day 68: Two accounts of one arrival

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say aloud: Rin, Wen, Ola, Bram and Cora are made up; Catherine and Anna are real. Read the Catherine card from "In New York" on, and read both credit lines as "told in 1991" (the card's first line has an age and a year the page does not support). Name today's six. Ask no child where their family came from; any child may pass. Key: key-exits-68-71.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 67's exit ticket and student page (s05-P67-4). Error E (s05-X67c) is today's repair. Review: hoped and traveled, from day 66. [[s05-P68-1]] Pip says error E: "Wen came by bus, so that is how Wen felt." Say beside it: hoped and traveled are two questions. Review: Pip, from kindergarten. Error L (s05-X67b) goes to a small group in the pairs work.

#### 3 min. Review, the three questions

Review: the three questions from unit 1: who made it, when, where. Read the Catherine card. **Ask.** [[s05-P68-2]] Children point to each label.

#### 9 min. Two accounts, a model

Say it is made up. Read Account A, by a child, then Account B, a retelling of A (a new telling that takes its facts from an account). Words: account, retelling, both say, only one says. Place three detail cards in two columns. **Ask.** [[s05-P68-3]] Then: [[steps:s05-P68-3]] Pip says error F: "B is shorter, so B lied." Say beside it: shorter is not disagreeing; leaving out is not lying.

#### 8 min. Catherine and a retelling

Read the Catherine card and our retelling. **Ask.** [[s05-P68-4]] The class sorts four detail cards together. Pip says error K: "I do not need to name a card." Say beside it: "The ___ card shows ___." **Ask.** [[s05-P68-5]] Each child says it to a partner, 20 seconds each.

#### 7 min. Pairs work

Pairs fill the both-say chart for the Anna card and our retelling. [[s05-P68-6]] Meanwhile the error L group, at most 5 children, takes 4 minutes, about 45 seconds each with the swap: [[s05-P68-7]] Pip says error L: "They felt the same." Say beside it: two tellers, two hopes. Then hear three of the six, 45 seconds each: [[s05-X68b]] and [[s05-X68c]]. The other three are heard in the exit part; six turns do not fit beside the group.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X68]]. While they mark, hear the other three of the six, 45 seconds each: [[s05-X68b]] and [[s05-X68c]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

Draw the two columns on the board; write the cards on slips; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed chart: fold a page into two columns, both say and only one says.

**Skip.** The repair, the small group and the six.

- **10 min. Rin.** Read Account A and Account B from the page about Rin's class. **Say.** "What do both say?" Expect: the books and the fish tank.
- **15 min. Anna.** Read the Anna card and its retelling. [[s05-P68-6]] **Say.** "What does only Anna's card say?" Expect: she was 18; they hugged and cried.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X68]] [[s05-X68b]] [[s05-X68c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the three questions; keep Pip's error F. Longer: ask what a person who was there could add.

### Words and supports

Words: account, retelling, both say, only one says. Frames: "Both say ___; only ___ says ___." "The ___ card shows ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 69 opens with error F ("Yes, it lied", s05-X68c) on a notice beside its short version, sends error K (no card named, s05-X68) to a small group, then asks who saw it for themselves.
