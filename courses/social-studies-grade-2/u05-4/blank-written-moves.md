# Written move for each group, day 71

Each group has one strip. The children work in pairs from the strip while the teacher meets other groups.

**Group 1 (items 1 and 4: C, K, B, N, O):** Look at the Hill Road Card. Put "chosen" or "forced" on it with "Could they have stayed?" Put the reason, the move and afterward in order. Point to the card line that supports your answer.
**Group 2 (items 2 and 3: I, A):** Look at the Valley Card. Draw an arrow from where Nia left to where Nia arrived. Say the reason the card gives.
**Group 3 (items 5 and 6: J, E, L, D, M):** Look at the six small cards. Sort them under "chose to come", "brought by force" and "here first". Say what each of the two tellers hoped, not how they traveled.
**Group 4 (item 7: F, G, H):** Look at the whale cards and the bridge picture. Trace the poster back to the fisher and say who saw the whale. Say a sign for the bridge with only parts you can check.

Secure children defend another viewpoint with the same source.
