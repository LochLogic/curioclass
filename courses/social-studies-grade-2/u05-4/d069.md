# Day 69: Who saw it, and our claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say aloud: Lee, Kit, Mo, Nat, Fenn Town and the Hospital Card are made up. Lay out each child's three cards: Smith Life, Cherokee Today and one Arrival Voices card. Name today's six. Claims stay in the folder until day 71. Any child may pass. Key: key-exits-68-71.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 68's marked work (s05-P68-1, s05-P68-3, s05-P68-4, s05-P68-6) and exit ticket (s05-X68). If many made error F on day 68's exit (s05-X68c), this is a whole-class repair. [[s05-P69-1]] Pip says error F: "The short card left out the books, so it lied." Say beside it: leaving out is not disagreeing. Error K (s05-X68) goes to a small group in the closing claim.

#### 6 min. Two tellers, or one

Words: teller, own way of knowing (saw it for themselves), copy. **Ask.** [[s05-P69-2]] Pairs draw the two lines. Then Mo saw the balloon too. **Ask.** [[s05-P69-3]] Say: two tellers who each saw it, and agree, make that part more trustworthy; a copy adds nothing; different names alone do not make two tellers. Pip says error G: "Two papers say it, so two witnesses." Say beside it: the flyer copied Lee's note.

#### 4 min. A sourced claim, modeled

Review: the claim frame from unit 4, and "Could they have stayed?" from day 59. Read the Hospital Card; say and record a claim. **Ask.** [[s05-P69-4]] Pip says error H: "It is true because I like the hospital." Say beside it: a claim needs a reason a card shows. Review: opinion, from unit 1.

#### 12 min. The closing claim

[[s05-P69-5]] Minutes 1 to 4: pairs say their claims, 45 seconds each with the swap. A claim answers, names two cards, says chosen or forced, and points to a line. Meanwhile the error K group, at most 5 children, takes 4 minutes: [[s05-P69-6]] Pip says error K: "I do not need a card for my claim." Say beside it: "The ___ card shows ___." Minutes 5 to 12: children record the claim with the frame; write only a child's own words, if asked. Hear the six, 45 seconds each, as the rest record: [[s05-X69b]]. Practice with written feedback, not scored.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X69]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The six answered [[s05-X69b]] aloud.

#### 3 min. Question board

**Ask.** [[s05-P69-7]] Children point to the Lee, Kit and Mo sheet. Close the board.

### Other rooms

Write the cards on slips; draw the lines on the board; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each card's name on a slip of paper.

**Skip.** The repair, the small group and the six.

- **10 min. Who saw it?** Read about Lee, Kit and Mo from the page. **Say.** "Which two give a reason to trust the balloon?" Expect: Lee's note and Mo's note; each saw it.
- **15 min. Your claim.** [[s05-P69-5]] **Say.** "Why do people leave home for a new place?" Expect: a reason, chosen or forced, and a named card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X69]] [[s05-X69b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the modeled claim. Longer: ask what record could be a second teller.

### Words and supports

Words: teller, own way of knowing, copy. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Saying, pointing and having words written count.

### Tomorrow

Day 70 is the unit check; nothing is taught. Errors G (s05-X69) and H (s05-X69b) wait for day 71's fourth group; the claim comes back on day 71.

### Credit

Ours. The Smith Life, Cherokee Today and Arrival Voices cards are Curio Class summaries with their credit lines. Lee, Kit, Mo, Nat and the Hospital Card are made up.
