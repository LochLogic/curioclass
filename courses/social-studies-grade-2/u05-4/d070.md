# Day 70: Unit check, people on the move

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the packet, one copy each. Read each card once, neutrally; do not teach or hint; show no timers. Every ask is read once. Children answer on the packet by pointing, circling, underlining, drawing or a few words, since a whole class cannot be heard. A child who cannot mark says it to the teacher: at most 4 children, about a minute each, named on the record sheet. Any child may pass. Key: key-assessment.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Item 1

Read Antin A and the Forced Case. Ask [[s05-E1]], [[s05-E2]], [[s05-E3]] and [[s05-E4]], one at a time. Children circle chosen or forced on the strip, or underline the line.

#### 4 min. Item 2

Read the map card. Ask [[s05-E5]]; children draw the route in pencil. Ask [[s05-E6]]; they underline the words on Antin A.

#### 2 min. Item 3

Ask [[s05-E7]] and [[s05-E8]]. Children underline two lines of Antin A, or write a few words.

#### 3 min. Item 4

Ask [[s05-E9]], [[s05-E10]] and [[s05-E11]]; children circle or underline. Ask [[s05-E12]]; they fill the three boxes on the student page.

#### 4 min. Item 5

Read the Example Cards. Ask [[s05-E13]], [[s05-E14]], [[s05-E15]] and [[s05-E16]]; children circle a card each time. Ask [[s05-E17]]; they circle the cards or write a few words.

#### 3 min. Item 6

Read the Present Card. Ask [[s05-E18]], [[s05-E19]] and [[s05-E20]]; children underline a line or write a few words.

#### 6 min. Item 7

Read Antin B, C and D. Ask [[s05-E21]], [[s05-E22]], [[s05-E23]], [[s05-E24]] and [[s05-E25]]; children circle on the strip. Ask [[s05-E26]]; they circle one, two or three.

#### 5 min. Collection and self-reflection

Collect the packets. Review: the self-check strip from unit 1, three pictured choices and one piece of work that shows a mark. Children mark it. After class mark each part: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. No part is averaged away. Day 71 respond groups by error: O, C, K and B (items 1 and 4); I, K and A (items 2 and 3); P, D, J, E, L and M (items 5 and 6); K, F, G and H (item 7). Each item samples one code: item 1 chosen and forced; items 2 to 4 the route and the reasons; item 5 a nation of immigrants; item 6 then and now; item 7 two accounts. Goods and ideas are not sampled.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: copy each card onto paper.

**Skip.** The self-check strip and the lines said to the teacher.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Read each ask once; give no help. [[s05-E12]] [[s05-E20]] [[s05-E26]]
  **Say.** "Was Mary Antin's family's move chosen or forced?" Expect: chosen; Father sent for them.
  **Say.** "Did the teller of Antin B see it for themselves?" Expect: no, Antin B copied Antin A.
- **15 min. Talk it through.** Which part felt hardest? Your child picks one to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one ask from each item. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: chosen, forced, teller, copy, own way of knowing. Frame: "Both say ___; only ___ says ___." Pointing, drawing and saying to the teacher count.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 meets four groups from this record: items 1 and 4 (errors O, C, K, B; s05-E1), items 2 and 3 (errors I, K, A; s05-E5), items 5 and 6 (errors P, D, J, E, L; s05-E13) and item 7 (errors K, F, G; s05-E21).
