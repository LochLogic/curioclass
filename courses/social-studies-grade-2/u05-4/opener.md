# Section 5.4 opener: two accounts, who saw it, and the check

## What the section does

Four days, 68 to 71, of 35 minutes. The unit asks: why do people leave home for a new place? Day 68 compares two accounts of one arrival: a memory and a retelling. Children fill a two-column chart, "Both say ___; only ___ says ___." Day 69 teaches corroboration: when two accounts agree we trust that part more, but only after checking that each teller had their own way of knowing. Two copies of one account are one account. Children then answer the unit's question in a short claim that names its sources. Day 70 is the end-of-unit check on a packet held back until its day. Day 71 responds: four small groups re-teach what the check showed, and a brief fresh task follows.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a repair, then teaching, a part for pairs, the rotating six heard at the teacher's table, an exit ticket and a question-board turn. Day 70 is a check: the packet is read aloud once inside each item, nothing is taught, there is no repair and no timers are shown. The teacher reads every card, caption and item aloud. Children read labels of one to three words.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **An account is what one person tells or writes about an event.** A retelling takes its facts from an account, so it depends on it: it has no way of knowing for itself.
- **Leaving out is not lying.** A shorter account differs because it is shorter. Difference is not contradiction.
- **Corroboration.** Agreement of two tellers who each saw it makes that part more trustworthy. Agreement of an account and its copy adds nothing. Different names alone do not make independent tellers.
- **Checkable and opinion** are unit 1's words. "The bell rang" can be checked by asking someone who heard it. "Lovely" is what someone thinks.
- **A claim names its reason, its card and the card's maker.** "The card does not say" is a fair answer.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: the short one left something out, so it lied (F, day 68); two sheets, so two witnesses (G, day 69); it is true because I like it (H, day 69); I do not need a card for my claim (K, days 68 and 69).

## What stays held back

The day 70 packet (the Antin cards, the Forced Case, the Example Cards, the Present Card and the world map) is printed only on day 70. The Ribbons Card and the Retelling Card (day 68 exit), the bell models (day 69 exit) and the sandcastle models (day 71 exit) are printed only on their days.
