# Exit cards, days 68 to 71

Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 68 exit cards: Ribbons Card and Retelling Card

Credit: "Catherine Hannon English, interview EI-116a, 17 November 1991, Ellis Island Oral History Collection, National Park Service. Summary by Curio Class."
**Ribbons Card (the memory):** "Catherine had pictured children with ribbons in their hair, but they did not wear uniforms."
**Retelling Card (retelling, based only on Catherine's account, Curio Class 2026):** "Catherine had pictured children with ribbons in their hair."
**Mark:** Circle a detail both cards say: ribbons in their hair, or no uniforms.
**Spoken, the six one at a time (two asks):** "Say one detail that only the memory tells." And: "The Retelling Card is shorter than the Ribbons Card. Did it lie, yes or no?"

## Day 69 exit card: three bell models

Printed "invented for practice".
**Model 1:** "A writer on the dock says the bell rang."
**Model 2:** "A writer on the ship says the bell rang."
**Model 3:** "A third writer copies the dock writer."
**Mark:** How many tellers? Circle one, two or three.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "The bell rang. The bell was lovely. Say which one we can check."

## Day 71 exit cards: sandcastle models and the fountain

Printed "invented for practice".
**Model 1:** "A watcher on a hill writes about a sandcastle contest."
**Model 2:** "A watcher on the beach writes about it."
**Model 3:** "A poster copies the hill watcher."
**Mark:** How many tellers? Circle one, two or three.
**Fountain Card:** a picture of a fountain with no water, and the caption "the prettiest fountain in town".
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say the part we can check on the picture."
