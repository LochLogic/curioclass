# Day 71: Try the hard part again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Order the four groups from the day 70 record, most missed first (often item 7). Lay out each group's cards and the move strip. The fresh cards are made up except the two nation cards from day 64. Ask no child about their family; any child may pass. Key: key-exits-68-71.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back day 69's claim (s05-P69-5) and day 70's assessment, each with a written cue and a picture of the part to try next. No one is graded in public.

#### 15 min. Grouped reteaching

Review: respond-day groups, from day 45 and unit 4. Four groups, about 3.5 minutes each, most missed first; skip a group with no misses. At most 4 children take a turn of about 45 seconds; in a larger group the rest answer by pointing. The other children work in pairs from the group move strip; for group 4 use the whale cards and the bridge picture.
- **Group 1, items 1 and 4 (error O, error C, error K, error B; s05-E1).** Review: the test "Could they have stayed?" from day 59. [[s05-P71-1]] [[s05-P71-2]] [[s05-P71-3]] Pip says error O: "Forced, because the road was hard." Say beside it: a hard move can still be chosen.
- **Group 2, items 2 and 3 (error I, error K, error A; s05-E5).** [[s05-P71-4]] [[s05-P71-5]] Pip says error I: the arrow runs from the river city to the mountain. Say beside it: from where they left to where they arrived.
- **Group 3, items 5 and 6 (error P, error D, error J, error E, error L; s05-E13).** [[s05-P71-6]] [[s05-P71-7]] Pip says error J: "All six chose to come."
- **Group 4, item 7 (error K, error F, error G; s05-E21).** [[s05-P71-8]] [[s05-P71-9]] Pip says error G: "The flyer and the poster saw the whale too." Pip says error H: "The best bridge in town" can be checked.

#### 5 min. Fresh evidence

Children mark [[s05-X71]]. Hear the six, 45 seconds each: [[s05-X71b]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Self-reflection

Review: the self-check strip, from unit 1 and day 70. Each child marks progress and a next step. Children write the part to try again in the box on the student page. At home, talk through each miss instead.

### Other rooms

Write each fresh card on a slip; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each fresh card on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at the returned claim and packet. **Say.** "Which part do you want to try again?" Expect: one part, such as who saw it.
- **15 min. A fresh card.** [[s05-P71-8]] **Say.** "Who saw the whale?" Expect: the fisher; the others copied.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X71]] [[s05-X71b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: meet only the two most-missed groups. Longer: secure children defend another viewpoint with the same source.

### Words and supports

Words: chosen, forced, teller, copy, checkable. Frames: "Both ___, but ___." "The ___ card shows ___." Pointing and saying count.

### Tomorrow

Unit 6 opens on day 72 with retrieval only; no readiness check is reserved. Errors G (s05-X71) and H (s05-X71b) from today's exit go on the record sheet for that retrieval.

### Credit

Ours. The two nation cards are Curio Class summaries from day 64; every other fresh card is made up.
