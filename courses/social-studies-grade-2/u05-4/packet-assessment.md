# Day 70 packet (kept back until day 70)

Read the whole packet aloud once, neutrally, inside each item, and no more. One copy of each card for each child, laid flat, one pencil each. Do not teach, hint or confirm. No answer map is visible and no timers are shown. Every card prints its maker, date, place and how it knows. Invented cards print "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class". Real accounts are shortened wording, "not an exact quotation", with a credit line. Plain black line drawing on white. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

Natural Earth map data (public domain), redrawn by Curio Class in 2026. Names only: Europe, North America, Hamburg, the Atlantic Ocean and Boston. No other labels and no borders.

## The items, read aloud, one ask at a time

**Item 1 (3 minutes).** "Antin A. Point to chosen or forced for Mary Antin's family's move." "Forced Case. Point to chosen or forced for the family in the Forced Case." "Say or point to a line from Antin A that shows who asked Mary Antin's family to come." "Say or point to a line from the Forced Case that shows why the family left."
**Item 2 (4 minutes).** "On the world map, show with your finger the route of Mary Antin's family, from where they left to where they arrived." "Say how Mary Antin's family crossed the Atlantic Ocean, using Antin A."
**Item 3 (2 minutes).** "Say one possible reason for Mary Antin's family's move that Antin A gives." "Say a second possible reason that Antin A gives."
**Item 4 (3 minutes).** "Point to the reason Antin A supports better: joining Father, or seeing new sights." "Say or point to the line of Antin A that shows it." "Say who made Antin A." "Tell Mary Antin's move in order: the reason, the move, then what happened next."
**Item 5 (4 minutes).** "On the Example Cards, point to one card that supports a nation of immigrants." "Point to a second card that supports a nation of immigrants." "Point to the card of a nation that was here first." "Point to the card of a person who was brought here against their will." "If a nation of immigrants means everyone freely arrived from abroad, say who the phrase leaves out."
**Item 6 (3 minutes).** "Say what Mary Antin wanted from her move, using Antin A." "Say what Rahaf wanted from her move, using the Present Card." "Say one way what Mary Antin wanted and what Rahaf wanted are alike or different."
**Item 7 (6 minutes).** "On Antin A and Antin B, point to a detail both say: Father met them in Boston, or Mary felt excited." "Point to a detail only Antin A says: Father met them in Boston, or Mary felt excited." "Did the teller of Antin B have their own way of knowing? Point to yes or no." "Did the teller of Antin D have their own way of knowing? Point to yes or no." "Point to the agreement that is stronger about the meeting: Antin A with Antin B, or Antin A with Antin D." "How many tellers who knew it for themselves told Antin A, Antin B and Antin C? Say one, two or three."
