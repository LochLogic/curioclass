## Three questions, again (3 minutes)

Who made it, when, where, and when told, asked of the Catherine card (from day 65, see the newcomer summaries) and kept on the card.

## Rin model (9 minutes, invented for practice)

**Account A, by a child (invented for practice):** "Our class moved rooms. We carried our books and the fish tank. I felt scared."
**Account B, a retelling (invented for practice, based only on Account A):** "The class carried books and the fish tank to the new room."
The teacher fills the chart on the board: "Both say the class carried books and the fish tank; only Account A says the child felt scared." Terms: account, retelling, both say, only one says. Pip says F.

## Catherine and a retelling (8 minutes)

**The Catherine card:** the first-impressions card from day 65.
**Retelling, based only on Catherine's account (Curio Class, 2026):** "Catherine was ten in 1920. In New York she saw schoolchildren and dirty streets."
The class fills "Both say ___; only ___ says ___." together: both say schoolchildren and dirty streets; only Catherine's card says the children looked untidy and that she thought they might be orphans. "It is shorter; it left out what she thought. Left out is not lied."

## Anna's reunion and a retelling (pairs, 7 minutes)

**The Anna Arrival card** (day 58). **Retelling, based only on Anna's account (Curio Class, 2026):** "Anna was 18 in 1920. Her mother and her aunt met her in New York."
Each pair fills the chart. The six are heard.
