# Cards for day 69

Every card prints "invented for practice". The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Two tellers, or one (6 minutes)

**Sol (pier):** "Sol stands on the pier and writes: the boat came in at noon and a band played."
**Ada (boat):** "Ada is on the boat and writes: the boat came in at noon and a band played."
**The poster:** "A poster says: the boat came in at noon and a band played." The poster's maker did not see it and copied Sol.
**Picture:** A pier with Sol, a boat with Ada, a poster on a post. A line from each writer to the boat; no line from the poster to the boat, a line from the poster to Sol.
Each has their own way of knowing: Sol saw it from the pier, Ada from the boat, the poster did not see it. Agreement of two tellers who each saw it makes that part more trustworthy; the poster adds nothing. Different names alone do not make two tellers. Pip says G. Terms: teller, own way of knowing, copy.

## A sourced claim, modeled (4 minutes)

**Invented move:** "A nurse takes a job at a hospital in a new town." The teacher says and records: "I think the nurse moved for work because the card says the hospital needs nurses. The Hospital Card shows a job, made by Curio Class." Not a historical claim.

## The closing claim (12 minutes)

Frame strip, one per child: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Three source cards for each child: the Smith Life card (day 60), the Cherokee Today card (day 61), and an Arrival Voices page (the newcomer summaries of days 65 to 67). A child says the claim to a partner and then records it with transcription support only. A claim may stress safety, family or several reasons, must say chosen or forced, and must name a source. A card that is invented is marked practice. The six are heard. Practice with written feedback returned on day 71; not scored.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Tellers: two (Sol, Ada). Look-fors for the closing claim: answers the question; names two sources; says chosen or forced; links a line of a source to the reason. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
