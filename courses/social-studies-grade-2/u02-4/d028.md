# Day 28: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Split the class into three thirds from the record sheet. Say: answer alone, pass any item, no one helps, a scribe is ready. Each question is read once. Say every place on the new cards is invented for practice. Hand out packets, pages and pencils. Hide any answer map. Key: key-assessment.md.

### The day

#### 7 min. Item 1

On World Map B and the first word bank, read aloud: write each continent's letter beside its name (s02-E1); circle where the Himalayas lie (s02-E2); circle where the Great Lakes lie (s02-E3).

#### 5 min. Item 2

The oceans word bank: write each ocean shape's number beside its name (s02-E4); circle yes or no, are the Indian and Atlantic oceans joined (s02-E5); circle one picture that shows why oceans matter (s02-E6).

#### 5 min. Item 3

On Coast and Ridge: circle the many-year sentence (s02-E7). Six of third A are heard, one at a time, 40 seconds each, while the rest mark: (s02-E8) and (s02-E9). Then circle the plants in the east valley (s02-E10) and circle yes or no, is the valley hot (s02-E11).

#### 5 min. Item 4

Six of third B are heard, 40 seconds each: (s02-E12) and (s02-E13). The rest circle what the wet slope shares (s02-E14), yes or no, do all do the same work (s02-E15), and where a hiker needs the warmer coat (s02-E16).

#### 4 min. Item 5

On Change Pair B: the lake continent (s02-E17), the street continent (s02-E18), what changed at the lake (s02-E19), what is new on the street (s02-E20), a box round one thing that stayed (s02-E22), yes or no, does the 2000 picture show the street today (s02-E24), then physical or cultural for the tram (s02-E21) and for the smaller lake (s02-E29). Third C is heard, 10 seconds each: (s02-E23). Then two of third A's last four are heard, 40 seconds each, for (s02-E8) and (s02-E9).

#### 4 min. Item 6

Circle the place you would choose (s02-E25). Each child says a reason the card shows to a partner (s02-E26), then writes the frame: I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___. Then write the source (s02-E27) and who made it (s02-E28).

#### 5 min. Collect

Collect packets and pages. A child still working finishes quietly. Meanwhile hear the last two of third A and the last four of third B, 40 seconds each, for the same asks. After class, mark each look-for 2 (shown), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the record sheet, and add the revised maps and globe record from days 21 to 27. Sort errors into four groups: where on Earth (errors A, J, L, M); land, water and climate (errors C, D, E, F, K, O); change over time (errors G, H, N); my choice and my source (error I, also day 27's exits).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a place with a sea, a ridge and a wet side.

**Skip.** The thirds and the scribe.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read each question once; give no help.
  **Say.** "What does the sea do to the wet side?" Expect: rain falls there.
  **Say.** "Which place would you choose? Why?" Expect: a reason and a named card.
- **20 min. Respond.** Talk about which part felt hardest; your child picks one to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one question from each item.

### Words and supports

Words: continent, ocean, climate, region, physical change, cultural change, source, maker. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.
