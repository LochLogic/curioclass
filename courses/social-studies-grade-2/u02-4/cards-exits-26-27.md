# Exit cards, days 26 and 27

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters, except the Ocean Life Card, which prints its maker. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 26 exit card: Ocean Life Card

**Picture:** Two panels. Left panel: a fish home, a small reef with three fish and a crab. Right panel: a ship at a harbour with a crane lifting a crate on to the deck. Credit line under both: Drawing by Curio Class.

**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows oceans as homes for living things. **Spoken:** "Say a because sentence about one picture on the Ocean Life Card." "Say which card shows your reason." "Who made the Ocean Life Card?"

## Day 27 exit card: Rainy Bay

**Picture:** A wooded shore with tall trees. Rain falling on the trees and the bay. A covered path leading from the shore to a row of homes. A kite in the corner of the sky. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

**Mark:** Circle one picture that supports a choice to live here. **Spoken:** "Say a because sentence about one picture on the Rainy Bay card." "Say which card shows your reason."
