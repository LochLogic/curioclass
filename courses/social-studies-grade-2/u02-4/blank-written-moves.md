# The written move for each group (day 29)

Name ______________________

**Where on Earth.** Find the place on the globe first. Say where it lies with a compass word. Check the Southern Ocean.

**Land, water and climate.** Say today or many years. Say what the land and water do. Say what people there might need.

**Change over time.** Read both dates. Say what changed and what stayed. Read why from the card; if the card does not say, say that.

**My choice and my source.** I choose ______ because ______. The ______ shows ______, made by ______.
