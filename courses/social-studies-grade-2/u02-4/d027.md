# Day 27: Where would you choose to live?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the six the record sheet names for today. Lay the response pages, the three sets of claim, detail and source cards and the eight Place Cards, each naming Curio Class as its maker. Hand back day 26's cards (s02-X26) and pages (s02-P26-2). Say every place is invented for practice. Key: key-exits-26-27.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error I, from day 26 (s02-X26b). Pip says: "I choose it because it is best." Beside it: "A choice needs a detail and a named card." Pairs match each of three sets: a claim card, a detail card and a source card, each source naming its maker. Ask for the set that is only a liking: it has no detail and no source. This is the one repair today.

#### 3 min. The compelling question

Read aloud the big question: "Where on Earth would you choose to live, and why?" Lay out the eight Place Cards. No child is asked to choose against where their family lives; choosing home is welcome.

#### 6 min. Say it

Pip's claim card says: "I choose Cove Harbor because it is nice." Pairs look at the Cove Harbor Place Card and say what the claim lacks. **Ask.** "Does the claim name a detail the card shows?" ([[s02-P27-1]]) Expect: no. Then pairs say it fixed with the frame: "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___."

#### 10 min. Record it

Each child fixes Pip's claim on the response page, in three lines: one detail the card shows ([[s02-P27-3]]), the card that shows it ([[s02-P27-4]]) and who made it ([[s02-P27-5]]). The words are each child's own. A teacher or partner may write a child's own words; no teacher-made sentence. Spelling is never judged. The pages stay in the folder; the teacher marks them after class.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 6 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 60 seconds each, for (s02-X27b), (s02-X27c) and one ocean ([[s02-P27-2]]). Everyone else finishes the response page, then revises the map from the cue and fills the cue sheet. This block has about 6 minutes of slack.

#### 2 min. Another choice

Partners say: "Another good choice is ___ because ___."

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud: "Circle one picture that supports a choice to live here" (s02-X27) on the Rainy Bay card, an invented place. Mark 2 minutes. The six were heard in supported work. No picture circled has no letter: record not yet seen. "Because it is nice" is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No Place Cards: use any picture of a place in a book.

**Skip.** The cue sheet.

- **10 min. Choose.** Look at a picture of a place together.
  **Say.** "Would you choose to live here? Why?" Expect: a reason from the picture.
- **15 min. Fix it.** [[s02-P27-1]] [[s02-P27-3]] Fix a claim that says only "it is nice": add a detail and the card.
  **Say.** "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___." Expect: a place, a reason and a named picture.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X27]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X27b]] [[s02-X27c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the say-it part and one written line. Longer: a child defends a second choice from the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: choose, reason, because, card, shows, maker. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 is the unit check on a new map, a new place card and a new pair of pictures. If many circled no picture or gave a liking only, they say a because sentence on day 29.
