# Day 26: A claim with a source

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the six the record sheet names for today. Lay the dated school yard cards, the Ocean Cards for homes and for most of the water, and the response pages. Hand back day 25's cards (s02-X25) and charts (s02-P25-2). Say the school yard is invented for practice. Key: key-exits-26-27.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error N, from day 25 (s02-X25f). Pip says: "The old school yard picture shows the yard today." Beside it: "Read the date: this is 1990." Lay the yard dated 1990 and the yard dated 2015. Children put them in date order, say each date and point to the one nearer to today. This is the one repair today.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: on days 11 and 14 you said "I think the ___ map is best for ___ because ___." **Ask.** Give a reason for choosing the Peters map to compare sizes. Expect: it keeps the sizes. A child who misses rehearses a because sentence in pairs work.

#### 10 min. Claim with a named source

Read the homes and food Ocean Card aloud, with its maker: Curio Class made it. Model the frame, one blank at a time: "I think oceans matter because they are homes for living things. The Ocean Card shows fish. Made by Curio Class." Point to the fish as you say the third blank. A source is a named card with who made it. Pip says: "I choose it because it is best." Beside it: "A reason names a picture and a card."

#### 6 min. Shared writing

The class says a second claim about the most-of-the-water Ocean Card; never the exit's card. The teacher writes it in the frame while the children say each blank aloud. Each child then copies the class claim into the frame on the response page ([[s02-P26-2]], 3 minutes). The pages stay in the folder; the teacher marks them after class.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 6 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 60 seconds each, for (s02-X26b), (s02-X26d), (s02-X26c) and one ocean ([[s02-P26-1]]). Everyone else revises the map from the cue and fills the cue sheet. A child not yet fully heard is taken in the spare time. This block has about 6 minutes of slack.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "How can sources support our choice?" in the class's words.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud: "Circle the picture that shows oceans as homes for living things" (s02-X26) on the Ocean Life Card, one panel of a fish home and one of a ship at a harbour. Mark 2 minutes. The six were heard in supported work. The ship circled has no letter: record not yet seen. "Because I like oceans" is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The cue sheet.

- **10 min. Date order.** Put two dated photos in order.
  **Say.** "Which is nearer to today?" Expect: the later date.
- **15 min. Say the frame.** [[s02-P26-2]] Choose a picture in a book and say the frame.
  **Say.** "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___." Expect: a reason, a picture and a named card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X26]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X26b]] [[s02-X26d]] [[s02-X26c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the model only. Longer: a child says a different reason for the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, reason, because, source, maker, card, shows. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___." Pointing counts.
