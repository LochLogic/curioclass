# Two dated views of a familiar place (day 26 warm-up, error N)

Invented for practice. Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Our school yard, 1990

**Picture:** A yard with a wooden swing, a hopscotch painted on the ground and a low fence. A small tree.

## Our school yard, 2015

**Picture:** The same yard with a metal climbing frame, the hopscotch, a taller fence and the same tree, now taller.

**Say:** "Which is older? Which is nearer to today? Read the date."
