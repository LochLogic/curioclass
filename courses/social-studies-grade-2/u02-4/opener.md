# Section 2.4 opener: choosing with a source, and showing what we know

## What the section does

Four days, 26 to 29, of 40, 40, 35 and 32 minutes. Day 26 teaches a claim that names its source: "Oceans matter because ... The Ocean Card shows ...", modeled and then said together. On day 27 each child answers the unit's question, where on Earth would you choose to live, and why, by saying a claim and then recording it with the frame, naming a Place Card and its maker. Choosing home is welcome and no child is asked to choose against where their family lives. Day 28 is the end-of-unit assessment: six items on a fresh map, a fresh coast-and-ridge card and a fresh pair of dated pictures. Day 29 responds: small groups meet the teacher about what the sitting showed, then try a fresh task.

## How the days run

Day 26: a repair on dates, a retrieval, the claim with a named source, shared writing, supported work, the exit. Day 27: a repair, the compelling question, saying it, recording it, supported work, another choice, the exit. Day 28: set-up, six items read aloud, collect. Day 29: the question board read, teaching by group, a fresh task, feedback and a self-check strip.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A claim names its source.** "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." The reason and the source are each child's own.
- **A source has a maker.** Curio Class made each card; the record does not say more.
- **A preference is not a reason.** "Because it is best" gives no source; "because the card shows trees" does.
- **Day 28 samples the unit.** It scores the final revised location record, the continents, the oceans, climate and plants, region, change at two dates and a sourced claim.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once with the right idea beside it. An old picture shows the place today (N, repaired day 26). I choose it because it is best (I, repaired day 27). Every letter of the unit is answered on day 29 by group: location (A, B, J, L, M), climate and livelihood (C, D, E, F, K, O), change at two dates (G, H, N) and sourced claim (I).

## Supplies for the section

The Ocean Cards from day 20, the Ocean Life Card and Rainy Bay (exit cards), the eight Place Cards, claim, detail and source cards, frame strips, the day 28 packet (locked until the day), fresh task cards for day 29, a written move for each group, a self-check strip for each child, the globe, the record sheet and the question board.
