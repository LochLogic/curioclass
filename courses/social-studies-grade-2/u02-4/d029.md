# Day 29: Try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 28 into four groups from the record sheet, by the part with the largest gap: where on Earth (errors A, B, J, L, M), land, water and climate (errors C, D, E, F, K, O), change over time (errors G, H, N) and my choice and my source (error I). Today's six: the children least heard. Lay each group's fresh card and written move. Say nobody's page is held up.

### The day

#### 4 min. Opener

Read aloud the class answer to "Where on Earth would you choose to live, and why?" from the question board. Name the four groups. Hand back day 28's assessment, marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen, by group: (s02-E1) where on Earth, (s02-E7) land, water and climate, (s02-E21) change over time, (s02-E26) my choice and my source.

#### 10 min. Teaching by component

Meet each group 2 to 3 minutes, the largest gap first; the others work from the written move in pairs. Where on Earth: find the place on the globe, say its compass word, and check the Southern Ocean. Land, water and climate: say today or many years, then what the land and water do. Change over time: read both dates, say what changed and what stayed, and read why from the card. My choice and my source: say "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___."

#### 10 min. Fresh task

Each group works its card in pairs ([[s02-P29-1]] [[s02-P29-2]] [[s02-P29-3]] [[s02-P29-4]] [[s02-P29-5]]): the Nile label, the Mist Valley strips, the harbour's crane, then Heron Lake with its reason and card. Pip voices the wrong idea on the Nile label, the Mist Valley strips and the crane. The six come one at a time to your table, 60 seconds each: (s02-X29b), (s02-X29c), (s02-X29d) and (s02-X29e). The last 2 minutes, everyone marks the Sunny Reach card (s02-X29): "Circle one picture that supports a choice to live there."

#### 6 min. Feedback and the self-check strip

Write feedback on each fresh task. Each child marks one box in each row of the self-check strip ("I can show this now", "I improved this part", "I want another try at this part") and points to one piece of work that shows it (2 minutes). Collect 1 minute. A circle with no reason is error I; the same mistake as day 28 is tagged with its day 28 letter.

#### 2 min. Question board

Revise the class answer in the class's words.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No fresh cards: reuse a day 28 question.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at day 28 together; your child picks one part to try again.
  **Say.** "Which part do you want another try at?" Expect: one of the four parts.
- **15 min. Try again.** Teach that part with the written move.
  **Say.** "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Expect: a reason, a card and its maker.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X29]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X29b]] [[s02-X29c]] [[s02-X29d]] [[s02-X29e]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fresh card for each group. Longer: a child defends a second choice from the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: try again, claim, reason, source, maker, changed, stayed the same. Frames: "I choose ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 starts unit 3: how people use and change their environment, and why people settle where they do. Parts not yet shown on day 29 are recorded for unit 3.
