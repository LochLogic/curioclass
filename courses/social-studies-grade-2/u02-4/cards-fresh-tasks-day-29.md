# Fresh task cards, day 29

One card for each group, and one exit card for every child, never one from the assessment. Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Location: a new crop of Europe and Africa

**Picture:** A crop of Europe and Africa with the sea between and a north arrow top left. A label card that reads the Nile has been laid in Europe by Pip. Task: move the label to where the Nile runs.

## Climate and livelihood: Mist Valley

**Picture:** A green valley with a river and fog lying low, a ridge behind, a farmer with a hat and a bridge. Two sentence strips: Fog filled the valley this morning. Fog fills the valley most mornings of most years.
**Task:** Sort the two strips into one day and many years.

## Change at two dates: an invented harbour

**Picture:** Two panels dated 1975 and 2005. 1975: a light tower, a small wooden pier and a few boats. 2005: the light tower, a longer pier with a crane and more boats. Pip says: The crane is in the 1975 picture.
**Task:** Fix Pip's answer.

## Sourced claim: Heron Lake

**Picture, Heron Lake:** Quiet water with reeds, a boardwalk and a heron.
**Task:** Write one reason to live there that the card shows. Then write the name of the card that shows your reason.

## Exit card, every child: Sunny Reach

**Picture:** A sunny hillside with an orchard, a stream and a footpath. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.
**Mark:** Circle one picture that supports a choice to live there. **Spoken:** "Say a because sentence about one picture on the Sunny Reach card." "Say which card shows your reason." "Who made the Sunny Reach card?" "What did you do to your day 28 answer?"

## Key (teacher)

Nile: northeast of Africa. Mist Valley: the first strip is one day, the second many years. Harbour: the crane is in the 2005 picture. Heron Lake: any reason from the card (the quiet water, the reeds, the boardwalk, the heron), and Heron Lake. Sunny Reach: the orchard, the stream or the footpath, tied to a reason; Curio Class. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
