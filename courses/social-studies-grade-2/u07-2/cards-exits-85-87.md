# Exit cards, days 85 to 87

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. Exit and check cards are first seen at the child's own turn. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 85 exit card: Fennel Valley

**Picture:** a small map of Fennel Valley with a key box: a row of dashes is a river; a wavy line is a road; a triangle is a town. The map shows a row of dashes, a wavy line and two triangles.
**Mark:** Put a token on the river.
**Spoken, the six one at a time, two asks:** "How did the key tell you?" Then: "Read the sticker on one fact in your land box. Say where the fact came from."

## Day 86 exit card: chairs

**Caption:** "Workers use wood to make chairs."
**Two pictures:** a log; a finished chair.
**Mark:** Put a token on the resource.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say what work the wood helps with."

## Day 87 exit card: stories

**Caption:** "Some neighbors meet to tell stories."
**Two word cards:** some, everyone.
**Mark:** Put a token on the word the caption uses.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say what we can say about the people there."
