# Day 87: Gather facts about people today

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 86 record: error I. Have a school practice ready: "Our class lines up by tables." Say aloud that the Oakfield pages are made up. People are shown in cities and towns as well as the countryside. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error I from day 86 (s07-X86) (or the most missed letter, the others to a group of at most 6). Pip says: "A scarf is a resource." Show [[s07-R87-1]]: sort six pictures into from nature and made from it.

#### 9 min. Review, two witnesses and a copy

Review: you learned in unit 5 that two tellers who each saw it and agree make that part more trustworthy, and that a copy is not another teller. You used "Both ___, but ___" in unit 1. Nothing new is taught. About 4 minutes, then 5.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K87-1]] Show the three Oakfield pages, invented for practice. Pip says error E: "Everyone in Oakfield goes to the market." Say beside it: "Many families go; the page says many, not everyone."
- **Ask.** [[s07-K87-2]] Two practices; neither is the norm. Never use a child's home or family.

#### 16 min. Gather, people today

Groups of one to four read the People Today page in pairs. Each child takes two notes from a city or town, a language and an everyday practice, said as some or many, and writes or draws them in the people today box with a sticker on each. [[s07-P87-1]] A child who finishes may add the third as a drawing on the back. Time:
- Hear the first six, one ask each, 45 seconds and 10 to swap: show the day 87 exit card and ask [[s07-X87b]]. About 6 minutes.
- Read the page aloud for up to 6 children, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet.
- The rest is about 4 minutes to start.
A child who writes everyone is error E; ask what the page says.
After class, read each note page and fill a feedback note: maker named, helping part, fits the question. Return them on day 89.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the word the caption uses." [[s07-X87]] Some is right; everyone is error E.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add one answer to "How do people live today, and what varies?"

### Other rooms

No printer: write each Oakfield page on a slip. One projector: show the People Today page.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use a page about any town; find one thing many people there do.

**Skip.** The repair group and the hearing of six.

- **5 min. Sort.** Look at six things and sort them into from nature and made. [[s07-R87-1]]
- **15 min. Gather.** Read the People Today page together. [[s07-P87-1]] **Say.** "Does the page say some, many or everyone?" Expect: the word the page uses.
- **10 min. Both and but.** [[s07-K87-2]] **Say.** "Tell me one way two practices are alike and one way they differ." Expect: both ..., but ....
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X87]] [[s07-X87b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one note, then the second tomorrow. Longer: find a second page for the same fact and say whether it is another teller or a copy.

### Words and supports

Words: city, town, language, some, many, copy. Frames: "The page says some ___, so I can say ___." "Both ___, but ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A token on everyone, or "everyone there tells stories" (error E), goes on the record sheet. Day 88 turns the notes into a claim backed by two facts from named sources, and opens by replacing "everyone" in two fresh captions with what the page supports.

### Credit

Ours. The packet pages are Curio Class's own words from the offices each strip names; Oakfield is made up.
