# Day 85: Gather facts about land and water

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 84 record: error B. Lay out each packet, the note pages and the pre-cut stickers. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error B from day 84 (s07-X84). Pip says: "The note has the prettiest picture, so I pick it." Show [[s07-R85-1]]: find who made each, then the part that helps. More than a third missed: the whole class. Otherwise a group of at most 6 works with you during gathering.

#### 7 min. Review, key and region

Review: you read a map key in unit 1, and you used the region frame in units 2 to 4. Nothing new is taught. Show the Reedmark map, invented for practice, with its key: a star is a market; a diamond is a mill.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K85-1]] Trace from the symbol to the key and back. Pip says error G: "The star is a hill, because stars are bright." Say beside it: "The key says the star is a market."
- **Ask.** [[s07-K85-2]] Reedmark and its neighbor Hollin share a river. Use the frame "These places share ___, so ___."

#### 18 min. Gather, land and water

Groups of one to four read the packet map and Land and Work page in pairs, a sentence each. Each child writes or draws two facts in the land and water box of the note page, in their own words, and puts a source sticker on each: the page name, Curio Class and the office the credit strip names. [[s07-P85-1]] The note page stays in the folder. Each child also finds the water symbol on the packet map by its key. Time:
- Hear the first six, two asks each: show the day 85 exit card, then [[s07-X85b]] and [[s07-X85c]]. About 45 seconds an ask and 10 to swap: 10 minutes.
- Read the pages aloud for up to 6 children, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet.
- Two minutes to start.
A note about animals only is error C; sort it into the three parts on day 86. A fact with no sticker is error H. Judge after class: the fact is in the right box and has its sticker.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the river." [[s07-X85]] The row of dashes is right; the wavy line is error G.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add one answer to "What land and water are here?" from the class's notes.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the key by hand.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use any map with a key and find the water.

**Skip.** The hearing of six and the repair group.

- **5 min. Look at a key.** **Say.** "The map has a key. What does the star mean?" Expect: what the key says.
- **15 min. Gather.** Read the map and one page together. [[s07-P85-1]] **Say.** "Which page did that fact come from?" Expect: the page name and Curio Class.
- **10 min. Find the water.** Use the key to find the water on the map.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X85]] [[s07-X85b]] [[s07-X85c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fact, then the second tomorrow. Longer: find a second page for the same fact.

### Words and supports

Words: river, mountain, key, sticker, source, land, water. Frames: "The key says ___." and "The page says ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A token on the wavy line or "a river is always wavy" (error G) goes on the record sheet, and so does "I just wrote it" (error H). Day 86 opens with a repair of the most missed of error G, error H or error C, then gathers facts on resources and work.

### Credit

Ours. The packet pages are Curio Class's own words from the offices each strip names; the Reedmark and Pell Town cards are made up.
