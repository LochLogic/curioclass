# Day 86: Gather facts about resources and work

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 85 record: error G, error H and error C. Order them most missed first. Draw the three Tarn Row pictures. Say aloud that Tarn Row and the repair cards are made up. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

The most missed of error G, error H or error C from day 85 (s07-X85), one fresh card; others go to a group of at most 6 during gathering.
- Error G: [[s07-R86-1]] (stays in the folder) Pip says: "The cross is the pond, because a pond is round." Beside it: the key says the circle is a pond.
- Error H: [[s07-R86-2]] Pip says: "I just wrote it."
- Error C: [[s07-R86-3]] (stays in the folder) Pip says: "My report is only the animals." Beside it: land and water, resources and work, people today.

#### 6 min. Review, resource and trade choice

Review: you learned natural resource, industry and job in unit 3, and opportunity cost in unit 4. Nothing new is taught.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K86-1]] Show the three Tarn Row pictures. Pip says error I: "A wall is a resource." Say beside it: "The stone is the resource. The wall is what is made."
- **Ask.** [[s07-K86-2]] No assessment.

#### 19 min. Gather, resources and work

Groups of one to four read the Land and Work page's work lines in pairs. Each child writes or draws two facts in the resources and work box of the note page, one on what the land or water gives and one on the work people do, in their own words, with a sticker on each. [[s07-P86-1]] The note page stays in the folder. Time:
- Hear the first six, one ask each, 45 seconds and 10 to swap: show the day 86 exit card and ask [[s07-X86b]]. About 6 minutes.
- Read the page aloud for up to 6 children, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet.
- The rest is a start of 7 minutes.
A made thing named as the resource is error I. A fact with no sticker is error H. Judge after class: each fact is in the right box and has its sticker.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the resource." [[s07-X86]] The log is right; the chair is error I.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add one answer to "What resources support people's work?"

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three pictures. One projector: show the packet work lines.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use any page that names a job and what the job uses.

**Skip.** The repair group and the hearing of six.

- **5 min. Name it.** **Say.** "A resource comes from nature. What do people take from the land or water where you live?" Expect: such as water or stone.
- **15 min. Gather.** Read the work lines together. [[s07-P86-1]] **Say.** "What does the land give, and what work do people do?" Expect: a resource and a job.
- **10 min. Tarn Row.** [[s07-K86-1]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X86]] [[s07-X86b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fact, then the second tomorrow. Longer: ask what is given up if a resource is sold.

### Words and supports

Words: resource, work, job, made. Frames: "The ___ is the resource; ___ is made." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A token on the chair, or "the chair is the resource" (error I), goes on the record sheet. Day 87 opens with a repair of error I, then gathers facts on people today and reviews corroboration.

### Credit

Ours. The packet pages are Curio Class's own words from the offices each strip names; Tarn Row is made up.
