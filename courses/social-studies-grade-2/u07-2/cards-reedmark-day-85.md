# Reedmark map, day 85

Invented for practice. The teacher's model for reading a symbol against its key. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Reedmark map

A map titled "Reedmark" with a key: a star is a market; a diamond is a mill. A river is drawn flowing past the town and on to a neighbor, Hollin, also invented. Credit: "Invented for practice, made by Curio Class, 2026."

The teacher traces from a symbol to the key and back: "The map shows a market. I can find it because the key says the star is a market." Pip says G: "The star on the Reedmark map means a hill, because stars are bright." Beside: "The key says the star is a market."

## Region frame

"These places share ___, so ___." Reedmark and Hollin share a river, so both use the river water. Nothing new is taught.
