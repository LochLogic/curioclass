# Oakfield pages and a school practice, day 87

Invented for practice. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Three pages about Oakfield

- **A visitor's page.** "I went to the Oakfield market on Saturday. Many families go." Made by a visitor, invented.
- **A market worker's page.** "Our market opens on Saturday. Many families come." Made by a worker who was there, invented.
- **A third page.** "There is a market in Oakfield on Saturday. Many families go." A line says: copied from the visitor's page.

The teacher shows that the visitor and the worker each saw the market and agree; the third page copies the visitor, so it is not another teller.

## A school practice (teacher supplies one line)

Example line: "Our class lines up by tables." Compare with one everyday practice from the child's packet, with "Both ___, but ___". Two practices; neither is the norm. Never use a child's home or family.

Pip says E: "Everyone in Oakfield goes to the market." Beside: "Many families go; the page says many, not everyone."
