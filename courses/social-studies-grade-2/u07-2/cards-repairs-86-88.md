# Repair cards, days 86 to 88

Fresh invented cards, printed "invented for practice". Not exits. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 86: G, H or C (the most missed)

- G: a fresh key (a square is a school; a circle is a pond; a cross is a well). Match three symbols on a small map of Birchwood (invented).
- H: three notes with no stickers, each to be given its sticker.
- C: three report cards (land and water, resources and work, people today) and six small notes to sort onto them.

## Day 87: I (a resource and what is made from it)

Six small pictures to sort into "from nature" and "made from it": a field of wheat, a loaf, a pile of stone, a wall, a flock of sheep, a scarf.

## Day 88: E (some, many, not everyone)

Two fresh captions: "Everyone in Marlow walks to school." (page says many) and "Everyone in Marlow sings at the fair." (page says some). Replace "everyone" with what the page supports.
