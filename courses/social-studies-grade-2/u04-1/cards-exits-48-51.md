# Exit cards, days 48 to 51

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters where it is made up. Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card.

## Day 48 exit card: Nigeria Crop

Maps are redrawn by Curio Class in 2026 from Natural Earth map data (public domain). Production draws no border that anyone disputes as settled: a disputed edge is left as a plain edge of the shading with no line and no label. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written).
**Picture 1:** A physical map of a crop of West Africa with a little of the Atlantic Ocean. Title: "Part of West Africa, land and water". The Niger River as a wavy blue line inside Nigeria. Key: a wavy blue line is a river, a triangle is a mountain, tan is dry land.
**Picture 2:** The same crop as a political map. Nigeria in one shade, its neighbors in other shades. A dashed line is a border. Key: a dashed line is a border, a shade is one country.
**Mark 1:** On the political map, box the country of Nigeria. **Mark 2:** Finish the line: The physical map shows the river; the political map shows ___.
**Spoken, three of the six:** "Is Europe a country or a continent?" **Spoken, the other three:** "Point to the key line that shows a border."

## Day 49 exit card: three flags

**Picture:** Three small panels, each with a flag. Trail Club: a clubhouse with a rule sign and a row of members, no land drawn. Three Reef Islands: three small islands with people and fields; each village has its own sign and there is no shared leader or law book. Vandor: land inside a drawn border, a leader at a desk and a book of laws.
**Mark:** Circle the country. **Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Trail Club has a flag and members. Say one thing a country has that Trail Club does not."

## Day 50 exit card: three country cards

**Picture:** Three small cards. Country Brae: people mark papers; the picture shows a tally with the most marks on one side. Country Sael: the leader's younger sister takes the job when the leader steps down; the picture shows two chairs and an arrow between them. Country Tor: people drop slips in boxes; the picture shows the most slips in one box.
**Mark:** Circle the country whose leader was not chosen by voting.
**Spoken, three of the six:** "In Country Mira the queen cuts ribbons and the prime minister plans the roads. Say who runs the government." **Spoken, the other three:** "In Country Dune the government puts up street lights. Say how that helps people."

## Day 51 exit card: two captions

**Picture 1:** A person twisting rope in a workshop. Caption, read aloud: "Tarn rope-making. The picture shows a person twisting rope. Rope-making is the finest craft. Made by Curio Class, 2026, invented for practice."
**Picture 2:** A person holding a broom beside a doorway. Caption: "Kelda broom-making. Made by Curio Class, invented for practice." No date is printed.
**Mark:** On the rope caption, circle the sentence you can check on the picture. Then finish the row from the two captions: Tarn: a craft of ___. Kelda: a craft of ___.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "On the broom caption, no date is printed. Say what we write for the date." **Spoken, three of the six:** "Say whether the picture shows that everyone in Tarn makes rope."
