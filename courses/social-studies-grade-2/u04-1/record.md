# Unit 4 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into five rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the teacher's table on days 48 and 53, the second six on days 49 and 54, the third six on days 50 and 55, the fourth six on days 51 and 57, and the fifth six on day 52; any child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block. Each E column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, boxing or drawing an arrow, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The End columns are the day 56 sitting, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Item 2b is not asked on day 56: copy into its cells the day 54 revised four-row chart (a contrast in each row, a card pointed to, no row saying everyone does one thing, the maker and date or "the card does not say"), and keep the first draft too. Each child gives one spoken reason on item 3, item 4 or item 5, by thirds. The four groups for day 57 are items 1 and 5 (A, B, I, R), item 2 (C, D, E, M, N), items 3 and 4 (F, G, H, O) and items 6 and 2b (K). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
