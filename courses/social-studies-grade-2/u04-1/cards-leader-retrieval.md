# Leader cards, day 48 retrieval from grade 1

Invented for practice. Two cards on each pair's desk. Plain black line drawing on white.

**Card 1:** Room 3 picks a class captain. Classmates mark ballots; the picture shows ballots dropped into a box and a tally strip.
**Card 2:** Room 5's line leader is picked by the teacher. The picture shows a teacher pointing at a child.
**Say:** "Point to the leader chosen by voting." Before the cards, partner talk for one minute: "Why does our class have rules?"

## Key (teacher)

The captain, chosen by ballots, is the leader chosen by voting. A miss is met in the day 49 Return part, or in the small group, as the data holds. Grade 1 words reviewed: rule, vote, election, leader.
