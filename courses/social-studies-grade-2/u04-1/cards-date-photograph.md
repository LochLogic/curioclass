# Date and photograph cards, day 51 retrieval from grade 1

Invented for practice. Two cards for each pair. Plain black line drawing on white.

**Card 1:** A photograph of a school hall with a date written along the edge: "May 1998".
**Card 2:** A story told by a person with no date written anywhere.
**Say:** "Point to the one that helps us check when it happened." Then to a partner: "Why?"

## Key (teacher)

The dated photograph. A miss: the teacher models a different source choice and a because sentence before the next part.
