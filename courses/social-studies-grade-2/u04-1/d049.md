# Day 49: What makes a country

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet, find the repair for the first part and the small group (at most six) for pairs work. Lay out the paper outlines, the Kelda cards and each pair's chart and envelope. Pick today's six, the children least heard. Say Kelda and Tarn are invented. Key: key-exits-48-51.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 48's written task, the two key drawings, with a mark each. If many missed day 48's exit, do one repair, 5 minutes. First, if many missed the leader cards, lay them again and say "chosen by voting" with the ballot card. Else error A: **Ask.** [[s04-P49-1]] Expect: the big ring, then an inner ring. Say: "Africa is a continent. Egypt is a country in it." Else error B goes to the small group.

#### 4 min. Region revisited

Review: region, an area that shares something. On the wall pair, point to the tan land on map A, then the dashed lines on map B: on our map the tan land lies across more than one border. Say: "A physical region can cross country borders." **Ask.** [[s04-P49-2]] Expect: dry land, one region.

#### 9 min. What makes a country

Kelda is invented. Lay the six Kelda cards on the six-box chart, one at a time, reading each card's line: people, land and borders, history, culture (what people there make, do and share), languages, government. Review: a capital is where the government meets; Kelda's is Port Kelda. Show the flag card: "A capital and a flag are usual in a country. They are not enough: our class could make a flag and still be a class." Pip says: "A flag makes a place a country." **Ask.** [[s04-F49-1]] Expect: no. Say the four things with a finger on each card: people, land with borders, a government and laws. **Ask.** [[s04-P49-4]] Expect: the four things.

#### 8 min. Pairs work

Hand out charts and envelopes, 1 minute. Pairs put each Tarn card in its box and say "Tarn has ___" ([[s04-P49-3]]); the team shirt and the cake go aside. The small group meets you for 5 minutes: error B on the Kelda island pair, [[s04-P49-6]] Expect: a border, read from the key.

#### 3 min. Question board

Partner talk, 1 minute. **Ask.** [[s04-P49-5]] Expect: people, land with borders, a government, laws. Hear three, 30 seconds each. Add "What does government do for people?"

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X49]], 2 minutes. Meanwhile call the six one at a time for [[s04-X49b]], 30 seconds plus 15 to call the next, 4.5 minutes; finished children look quietly at the chart. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six boxes on paper; hold up each card and name it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw six small pictures of a made-up country.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Six things.** Draw or name a made-up country's people, land, history, culture, languages and government.
- **10 min. Say.** **Say.** "What makes a country?" Expect: people, land with borders, a government, laws. [[s04-P49-5]] **Say.** "A flag makes a place a country. Is that right?" Expect: no. [[s04-F49-1]]
- **5 min. The four.** **Say.** "Say the four things." [[s04-P49-4]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X49]] [[s04-X49b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three Tarn cards in pairs work. Longer: a child draws one more place with exactly one of the four things missing.

### Words and supports

Words: country, border, government, law, history, culture, language. Frame: "Tarn has ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error C shows as a circled Trail Club or Three Reef Islands, or "a flag" said for [[s04-X49b]]. If many made it, day 50 opens with one repair; fewer go to a small group. Day 50: what a government is for, and how leaders are chosen.
