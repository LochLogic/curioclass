# Day 51: Two countries, four rows

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet, choose the one repair (error D, M or N, the most seen) and the small group (at most six). Pick today's six, the children least heard. Say aloud that Wrenn and Hale are invented; the Japan and Saudi Arabia cards are made by Curio Class from named public pages and show examples, never everyone. Key: key-exits-48-51.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 50's written task with a mark each. If many missed day 50's exit, repair error D, 5 minutes: read four fresh cards (Hale marks ballots, Wrenn the eldest child takes the job, Ostry drops slips in boxes, Lune the leader's cousin takes the job). **Ask.** [[s04-P51-1]] Expect: the family. Say: "In Wrenn the leader's job passes in the family." Error M and error N go to the small group.

#### 4 min. Retrieval from grade 1

Review from grade 1: a source that lets us check when. Each pair gets the two cards. **Ask.** [[s04-P51-2]] Expect: the photograph. **Ask.** [[s04-P51-3]] Expect: it has a date. A miss: model the choice and a because sentence.

#### 7 min. Sourcing a card

Review: the three questions from unit 1 (who made it, when, where). Hold up the Japan card. **Ask.** [[s04-P51-4]] **Ask.** [[s04-P51-5]] Expect: Curio Class; 2026, in our class from named public pages. Fill the history row for both on the wall chart, a finger on each card: Edo was renamed Tokyo in 1868 (Source 51B); the kingdom took the name Saudi Arabia in 1932 (Source 51F). **Ask.** [[s04-P51-6]] Expect: 1932. Pip says: "Everyone in Japan does puppet theater." Say beside it: the card shows one thing some people do. If asked about belief, say people in every country follow many beliefs.

#### 10 min. Compare

Hand out the pages. Each child fills the four rows, one difference a row, a finger on the card each time: [[s04-P51-7]] [[s04-P51-8]] [[s04-P51-9]] [[s04-P51-10]], then [[s04-P51-11]]. The small group meets you for 5 minutes: error M, [[s04-P51-14]] Expect: the prime minister; error N, [[s04-P51-12]] Expect: cross the river.

#### 4 min. Draft saved

Charts go in as a draft. After class write one cue on each, about 90 seconds a chart, from four look-fors: a difference in each row, a card pointed to, no row says everyone, the maker and year. Question board: **Ask.** [[s04-P51-13]] Expect: history, culture, language or government.

#### 6 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X51]], then finish the row comparing Tarn and Kelda, 3 minutes. Meanwhile call the six one at a time, 30 seconds plus 15 to call the next, 4.5 minutes: [[s04-X51b]], and for three of them [[s04-X51c]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two cards on the board; children write the four rows on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: compare two made-up countries drawn by you.

**Skip.** The small group and the six.

- **10 min. Cards.** **Say.** "Who made the Japan card?" Expect: Curio Class. [[s04-P51-4]] Then [[s04-P51-5]].
- **15 min. Four rows.** Your child fills each row with a finger on the card. [[s04-P51-7]] [[s04-P51-8]] [[s04-P51-9]] [[s04-P51-10]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X51]] [[s04-X51b]] [[s04-X51c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two rows. Longer: a child names one thing the cards do not say.

### Words and supports

Words: compare, history, culture, language, government, source. Frame: "The ___ card shows ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

The chart is the written task: the cues come back on day 52 and are used for revision on day 54. Error Q shows as the finest-craft sentence circled for [[s04-X51]]; error P as a guessed year for [[s04-X51b]]; error E as "yes" for [[s04-X51c]]. Day 52 opens with one repair, 4 minutes; small groups take the others. It opens section 4.2: why people trade.
