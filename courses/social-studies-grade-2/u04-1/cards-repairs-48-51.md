# Repair cards, days 49 to 52

Every card prints "invented for practice" where it is made up. Plain black line drawing on white. Each repair is at most 5 minutes. Pip says the wrong idea and the right idea is said beside it.

## Nested outlines (error A, day 49)

**Picture:** A continent outline drawn on paper and three paper country outlines (Tanzania, Egypt and Ghana, from Natural Earth outlines) laid inside it. Each ring is named: continent, country.
**Say:** "Africa is a continent. Egypt is a country in it. Tanzania is a country in it." Pip: "Africa is a country."

## Kelda island pair (error B, day 49 small group)

**Picture:** The Kelda two-map pair, used as a repair. The child points to the key line, then to a border.
**Say:** "Read the key: the dashed line is a border." Pip: "The map with more colours shows the borders."

## Chess Club and Country Lune (error C, day 50)

**Picture:** Two cards side by side. Chess Club: a flag, members and a rule board. Country Lune: a flag, land inside a border, a leader and a law book. Four things matched: people, land with borders, a government, laws.
**Say:** "Chess Club has a flag. A country also has land with borders, a government and laws." Pip: "A flag makes a place a country."

## Voting and family strips (error D, day 51)

**Picture:** Two strips. Four fresh cards: Country Hale (people mark ballots), Country Wrenn (the eldest child takes the leader's job), Country Ostry (people drop slips in boxes), Country Lune (the leader's cousin takes the job). The child sorts each under voting or family.
**Say:** "In Wrenn the leader's job passes in the family." Pip: "Every leader is chosen by voting."

## Two authority diagrams (error M, day 51 small group)

**Picture:** Diagram 1: voters, then elected representatives, then the prime minister, who leads the government; the emperor in a box to the side marked "role set by the constitution". Diagram 2: a ruling family, then the leader. The child traces with a finger, who chooses and who runs.
**Say:** "Elected representatives choose the prime minister, who leads the government. The emperor has a role set by the constitution and does not govern." Pip: "Japan's emperor runs the government." Source 50A and 50C.

## Ferry card (error N, day 51 small group)

**Picture:** A ferry crossing a river with people and a cart; a sign "the government keeps the ferry".
**Say:** "The government keeps the ferry so people can cross the river." Pip: "The government has a ferry."

## Caption check (error Q, day 52)

**Picture:** Lune bead-stringing: a person stringing beads. Two sentences: "The picture shows a person stringing beads." "Bead-stringing is the best craft." Made by Curio Class, invented for practice.
**Say:** "I can check what the picture shows; best is what someone thinks." Pip: "It says best, so it is true."

## Two practices in one country (error E, day 52 small group)

**Picture:** Kelda with two cards: a spring boat parade and a harvest song night. Both shown as things some people do.
**Say:** "These are two things some people in Kelda do. The cards do not say everyone does them." Pip: "Everyone in Kelda does the boat parade." The child then compares two practices before comparing two countries.

## Missing credits (error P, day 52 small group)

**Picture:** Four fresh invented cards (a drum photo with no maker, a map with no date, a poster with no place, a toy with a full credit). Each missing credit is marked "unknown".
**Say:** "The card does not say." Pip: "No date on the card, so I will say 2020."

## Key (teacher)

Each repair names its letter and its right idea as above. The retrieval gap (grade 1 rules and elected leaders, a class rule beside a public rule, a leader chosen by voting matched to its election card) is met in the day 49 Return part when more than a third missed.
