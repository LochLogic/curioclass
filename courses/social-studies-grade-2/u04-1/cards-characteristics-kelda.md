# Kelda Characteristic Cards, Pond Club and the country card, day 49

Kelda is an invented country. Each card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Six cards go on a six-box chart.

## Six Kelda Characteristic Cards

**People:** A crowd of different faces, ages and clothes. **Say:** "Kelda has many kinds of people."
**Land and borders:** A map with hills, a river and a dashed line around the edge. **Say:** "Kelda has land inside a border."
**History:** A picture of a stone bell tower with a note "built long ago". **Say:** "Kelda has a past that people remember."
**Culture:** A boat parade, a bowl of soup being shared and a song. **Say:** "Culture is what people there make, do and share."
**Languages:** Two speech bubbles, one in Keld and one in Marrow (both invented). **Say:** "Kelda has two languages."
**Government:** A council house, ballots and a book of laws. **Say:** "Kelda has a government and laws."

## Capital and flag

**Capital card:** Port Kelda, a star on the map. **Say:** "A capital is where the government meets." **Flag card:** a flag with a blue wave. "A capital and a flag are usual. They are not enough."

## Pond Club (invented)

**Picture:** A pond clubhouse with a flag, a row of members and a rule board. No land drawn, no leader for everyone, no law book.
**Say:** "Pond Club has a flag, members and rules. It is not a country." Pip says C: "A flag makes a place a country."

## Four things that make a country

People, land with borders, a government and laws. Said as a chant of four, with a finger on each of the cards.

## Key (teacher)

Four things make a country: people, land with borders, a government and laws. The six cards on the chart also include history, culture and languages. A flag and a capital are usual, not enough.
