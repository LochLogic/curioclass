# Tarn eight picture cards, day 49

Tarn is an invented country. One envelope for each pair, eight cards. Each prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

**Six Tarn cards**
1. People: a group of different people at a market.
2. Land and borders: a map with a coast, hills and a dashed border.
3. History: an old harbor wall with a note "built long ago".
4. Culture: neighbors sharing a bean-soup supper with a fiddle.
5. Languages: two speech bubbles, Tarnish and Oru (both invented).
6. Government: a hall with a leader at a table and a law book.

**Two decoys:** a team shirt with a number; a birthday cake with candles.

**Say:** "Tarn has ___." The pair puts each card onto the six-box chart, or sets the decoys aside and says why.

## Key (teacher)

Six cards onto the six boxes: people, land and borders, history, culture, languages, government. The team shirt and the birthday cake belong to no box; a child who sets them aside has them right.
