# Tanzania Crop, day 48

One for each pair. Two maps on one sheet. Maps are redrawn by Curio Class in 2026 from Natural Earth map data (public domain). Production draws no border that anyone disputes as settled: a disputed edge is left as a plain edge of the shading with no line and no label. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written).

## Physical map

**Picture:** The country of Tanzania cropped from the continent, with a little sea along the east edge. Title: "Tanzania, land and water". A triangle for a mountain in the north, three blue lake shapes along the west and north edges, a wavy line for a river. Key: a triangle is a mountain, a blue shape is a lake, a wavy line is a river.
**Say:** "Circle the mountain."

## Political map

**Picture:** The same crop. Title: "Tanzania, countries". Tanzania in one shade, its neighbours in other shades and unnamed. A dashed line is the border. A star for the capital, Dodoma. Key: a dashed line is a border, a star is a capital, a shade is one country.
**Say:** "Box the country. Tell your partner which key line told you."

## Key (teacher)

Mount Kilimanjaro is in the north and is the highest mountain in Africa. Lake Victoria, Lake Tanganyika and Lake Nyasa lie partly in Tanzania. Dodoma is the capital (source 48D). The mountain is circled on the physical map; the box is round the shaded country on the political map; the dashed line in the key is how the border is known.
