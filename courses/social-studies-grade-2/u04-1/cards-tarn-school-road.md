# Tarn school and road-crew cards, day 50

Tarn is invented; each card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Supported work: each child labels one job and says how it helps.

**School card:** A building with a bell, children going in and a teacher at the door. **Label:** "A school."
**Road-crew card:** Workers filling a hole in a road and a cart rolling by. **Label:** "A road crew."

**Say:** "The government ___ so people can ___."

## Key (teacher)

Any job with a purpose meets: a school so children can learn; a road mended so people can travel safely. A job named with no purpose is not yet shown. Marked shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
