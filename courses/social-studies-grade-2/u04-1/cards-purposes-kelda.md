# Kelda purpose cards, day 50

Kelda is invented; each card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

**School:** Children at desks and a teacher. **Say:** "The government of Kelda runs a school so children can learn."
**Road crew:** Workers mending a road with a cart passing. **Say:** "The government mends the road so people can travel."
**Traffic signal:** A signal at a crossing and a person walking. **Say:** "The government puts up a signal so people are safe."

Grade 1 words reviewed: government, law, citizen. Pip says N: "The government has a school." The right idea is said beside it: "so children can learn".

## Key (teacher)

Each card is read with the frame "The government ___ so people can ___." A job named with no purpose is the wrong idea. Government purposes are responsibilities, not proof that every government does them well.
