# Day 50: What a government is for

## Teacher page

### Before class

From the record sheet, choose the one repair and the small group (at most six). Lay out the cards and chart. Pick today's six, the children least heard. Say aloud that Kelda, Orrin and Tarn are invented. Key: key-exits-48-51.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 49's written task, the card sort, with a mark each. If many missed day 49's exit, repair error C, 5 minutes: on the Country Lune card, **Ask.** [[s04-P50-1]] Expect: the four things. Say: "A flag is usual, not enough. A country also has people, land with borders, a government and laws." Fewer go to the small group.

#### 7 min. What a government is for

Review from grade 1: government, law, citizen. **Ask.** [[s04-P50-2]] Expect: rules, or things everyone uses. Show Kelda's school card, then the road crew. Say the frame: "The government ___ so people can ___." **Ask.** [[s04-P50-3]] Expect: learn. **Ask.** [[s04-P50-4]] Expect: travel. Show the traffic signal: "The government puts up a signal so people can cross safely." Pip says: "The government has a school." **Ask.** [[s04-F50-1]] Expect: so children can learn. Say: a purpose is what the job is for, and not every government does its jobs well.

#### 8 min. How leaders are chosen

Lay Kelda's card under "chosen by voting": ballots, the most marks wins (review of grade 1's election), and Orrin's card under "the job passes in the family." **Ask.** [[s04-P50-5]] Expect: Kelda's card. **Ask.** [[s04-P50-6]] Expect: Orrin's card. Pip says: "Every leader is chosen by voting." **Ask.** [[s04-F50-2]] Expect: no. Say: voting is one way, not the only way. You learned in grade 1 that the Navajo Nation elects its President today. Japan today (Sources 50A and 50C): voters elect representatives, who choose the prime minister, who leads the government; the emperor has a role set by the constitution and does not govern. **Ask.** [[s04-P50-7]] Expect: the representatives. Pip says: "Japan's emperor runs the government." Say beside it: the prime minister leads the government.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Hand out cards and strips, 1 minute. Each child chooses the school or road-crew card, labels one job and says the frame ([[s04-P50-8]]); a partner may write. Hear the six, 45 seconds each plus 15 to call the next, 6 minutes: three hear [[s04-X50b]], three hear [[s04-X50c]]. The small group meets you for 4 minutes for the letter most missed. Mark each on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Question board

Partner talk, 1 minute. **Ask.** [[s04-P50-9]] Expect: a purpose with "so people can". Hear three, 30 seconds each. Add "How do two countries differ?"

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s04-X50]]; read it once. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on the board; children label on lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a school and a road.

**Skip.** The six and the small group.

- **10 min. Jobs.** **Say.** "The government runs a school so people can ___." Expect: learn. [[s04-P50-3]] Then the road. [[s04-P50-4]]
- **10 min. Leaders.** **Say.** "Is every leader chosen by voting?" Expect: no. [[s04-F50-2]]
- **5 min. Label.** Your child draws a job and finishes the frame. [[s04-P50-8]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X50]] [[s04-X50b]] [[s04-X50c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Kelda's school and road only. Longer: a child draws a made-up country's job and its "so people can".

### Words and supports

Words: government, purpose, law, leader, vote, family, prime minister. Frame: "The government ___ so people can ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error D shows as a circled voting country; error M as "the queen" said for [[s04-X50b]]; error N as "it puts up street lights" for [[s04-X50c]]. If many made one, day 51 opens with one repair, 5 minutes; fewer go to a small group. Day 51 compares two countries.
