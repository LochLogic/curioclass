# Place Cards, day 31

Four taught cards and four sorting cards. Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

## Taught cards

**Stilt Bay.** *Picture:* A row of houses on tall posts above water, ladders to each door, a boat tied to a post. Caption: In Stilt Bay the water rises each year. The houses stand on posts.
**Snowy Pass.** *Picture:* Deep snow on a mountain road, houses with steep roofs and snow sliding off. Caption: Snowy Pass has deep snow. The roofs are steep.
**Hot Flats.** *Picture:* A flat dry land under a bright sun, a large cloth stretched on poles over a yard, people in thin clothes sitting in the shade. Caption: Hot Flats has strong sun. People make shade and wear thin clothes.
**Hill Village.** *Picture:* Deep snow on a hillside, a row of low houses, snowshoes by each door. Caption: Hill Village has deep snow. People keep snowshoes by the door.

## Region review

Snowy Pass and Hill Village share deep snow, so people there share a need. One has steep roofs and one keeps snowshoes by the door. Ask: "Must every home look alike?" The class says no. The frame from unit 2: "These places share ___, so ___."

## Four sorting cards

**Picture 1:** A covered porch on a house in a rainy village. Adapt.
**Picture 2:** A patch of ground cleared of stones and levelled for a school garden. Change the land.
**Picture 3:** A tunnel cut through a hill with a road going in. Change the land.
**Picture 4:** Boots lined up by a door in a muddy place. Adapt.

## Key (teacher)

Taught cards: adapt for Stilt Bay, Snowy Pass and Hot Flats. Region review: similar conditions, varied adaptations. Sorting: 1 and 4 adapt; 2 and 3 change the land. Any sort with a because is a good one.
