# Four communities, day 32

Every card is made up and prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Each card has a small map with a key, a compass rose, and two arrows into the community: a short blue arrow for water from nearby, and a long arrow for rice from far away. No community is ranked above another.

## The four cards

**Tern City.** *Picture:* Tall homes close together, a market square, a river at the edge. Caption: Tern City is a city. Many people live close together.
**Plum Lane.** *Picture:* Houses with small yards on curving streets, a school, a park. Caption: Plum Lane is a suburb. Homes have yards.
**Wren Town.** *Picture:* A main street with shops and homes mixed, a small library. Caption: Wren Town is a small town. Shops and homes stand together.
**Bean Hill.** *Picture:* Wide fields, a barn, a few houses far apart. Caption: Bean Hill is a farm community. Fields cover most of the land.

## Map reading

Each map says in its key which arrow is near and which is far. The compass rose is used once: "The farm lies ___ of the town." Say: "Every community gets some things from near and some from far."

## Frame

"The city and the farm both get ___ from near and ___ from far. The city has ___; the farm has ___." Pairs fill it, then the suburb and the small town.

## Key (teacher)

Every card: water from near, rice from far. City: tall homes close together. Suburb: homes with yards. Town: shops and homes together. Farm: fields. Pip says: "A farm gets everything it needs from its own fields." The right idea is said beside it.
