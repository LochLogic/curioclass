# Marlow and Gap Town, day 30

Both places are made up and print "invented for practice". The teacher says so aloud. Each map has a key and a compass rose, and its caption is read aloud.

## Marlow

**Picture:** A small map. A wide river runs from the north edge to the south edge. On the east bank a row of homes and a market square; on the west bank two flat green fields. A boat landing on the east bank with two boats tied. A bridge joins the banks. Key: a square is a home, a green block is a field, an anchor is a boat landing, a line across the river is a bridge. Compass rose in the corner.
**Caption:** Marlow is a city made up for practice. It lies beside a wide river. The river gives water, food and a way for boats.

## Gap Town

**Picture:** A small map. Two lines of mountains run east to west with a low gap between them. A road comes from the south, runs through the gap and goes on to the north. A small town sits in the gap: a few homes, an inn with a sign and a well. Key: a triangle is a mountain, a long line is a road, a square is a home. Compass rose in the corner.
**Caption:** Gap Town is a town made up for practice. A road crosses the mountains here through a low gap. Travelers stop in Gap Town.

## Model, said aloud

"People settled here because ___." For Marlow: they could get water, grow food and use boats. For Gap Town: the road through the gap brought travelers. Pip says: "People live by a river because it looks pretty." The right idea is said beside it.
