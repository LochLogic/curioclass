# Day 30: Why people settle

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the first six on the record sheet. Hand back day 29's exit tickets and written tasks, marked; leftover parts are recorded for this unit. Lay out the pictures, maps and cards. Keep the exit card back. Say aloud that every card marked invented for practice is made up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Retrieval

Review: in grade 1 you named land and water. Show the school picture: a hill, a stream, litter. Pairs answer: [[s03-P30-1]], [[s03-P30-2]], [[s03-P30-3]]. If more than a third needed help, day 31 opens with this picture.

#### 3 min. Hook

Show the Terraced Field beside the raised house and read both captions. Ask the three questions of the Terraced Field's credit: who, when, where. Ask: "Should people change the land, or change their ways to fit it?" Write every answer on the question board.

#### 9 min. Why people settle

Review: on day 2 you read a key and on day 3 a compass rose. Show Marlow; read its key and caption; use the rose once: the river runs from the north edge to the south edge. To settle means to make a home in a place and stay. Say: "People settled here because the river gives water, food and a way for boats" ([[s03-K30-1]]). Pip says: "People live by a river because it looks pretty" ([[s03-F30-2]], error B). Say beside it: pretty is not a reason; a reason is something the place gives. Show Gap Town and say: "People settled here because the road through the gap brings travelers" ([[s03-K30-2]]). **Ask.** "What helped people settle here?" Expect: the river's water or boats; the road through the gap.

#### 6 min. How people change the land

Review: on day 5 you said how people changed land or water. Show the Terrace card and fill the frame with the class: "People cut steps into a steep hill, so the land has level strips to plant" ([[s03-K30-3]]). Show the Dam card: "People build a dam across a river, so the land has a lake" ([[s03-K30-4]]). Pip says: "The dam. That is what it changed" ([[s03-F30-1]], error P). Say beside it: the dam makes a lake.

#### 11 min. Supported work

The first six come to your table, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X30b]]. The others, in pairs, write each picture card's number under Marlow, Gap Town, Terrace, Dam or none ([[s03-P30-4]]; it stays in the folder), then tell a partner why one card goes where it does ([[s03-P30-5]]). Card 6, leaves by a lake, goes under none.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card and read aloud: "Circle the picture that shows a reason people could settle here" ([[s03-X30]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A circled sunset or flower field is error B.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw and number the six cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw a river, a gap between two hills and a hill with steps.

**Skip.** The retrieval picture and the question board.

- **10 min. Two places.** Read the Marlow and Gap Town captions aloud.
  **Say.** "What helped people settle here?" Expect: the river's water, food and boats; the road through the gap.
- **15 min. Sorting cards.** [[s03-P30-4]] Your child writes each card number under its place.
  **Say.** "Why does this card go there?" Expect: a because that names water, boats, the road, steps or a lake.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X30]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X30b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs sort cards 1, 3, 4 and 6 only. Longer: a child draws a new place and says one reason people would settle there.

### Words and supports

Words: settle, reason, river, gap, land, change, because. Frames: "People settled here because ___." "People ___, so the land ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the flowers or the sunset on today's exit (error B), day 31 opens with a fresh Fern Landing card; a small group takes error P, the dam named but not what it did. Day 31 teaches how people adapt.
