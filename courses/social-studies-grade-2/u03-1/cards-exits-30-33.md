# Exit cards, days 30 to 33

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print these only on the exit of their day.

## Day 30 exit card: Reed Lake Town

**Picture:** Three pictures. A lake with fish jumping and a boat. A hillside covered in bright wild flowers. A pink sunset over the roofs.
**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows a reason people could settle here. Then finish the line: People could settle here because ___.
**Spoken, with a new card:** A town drains a wet, muddy field and plants grass there for a ball field. "What did draining change?"

## Day 31 exit card: Windy Shore Card

**Picture:** Three pictures. A cap with ear flaps. A person with a scarf wound round the face. A bridge over a creek.
**Mark:** Circle one picture that shows people adapting to the wind. Then finish the line: They adapt by ___.
**Spoken, with a new card:** A school in a rainy place builds a covered walkway between two buildings. "Did the school adapt, change the land, or both?"

## Day 32 exit card: Hill Farm Card

**Picture:** A small map with a key and a compass rose. Hill Farm in the middle with its own field of oats. An orchard one square away and a cloth town twelve squares away. Two arrows of equal width toward the farm, pears from the orchard and cloth from the town.
**Mark:** Finish the line: Hill Farm gets ___ from near and ___ from far away.
**Spoken:** "Which thing comes from far away?"

## Day 33 exit card: Library Flood Case

**Picture:** Three pictures. A wet library floor. Neighbors carrying dry books out to a table on the library steps. A child at a closed library door holding a book bag.
**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows people helping each other after the flood. Then finish the line: Because of the flood, the child cannot ___.
**Spoken, with a new card:** A flood closes the bridge in a town. "Who is affected?"
