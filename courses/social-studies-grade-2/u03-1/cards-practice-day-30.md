# Six picture cards, day 30

Pairs draw a line from each card to Marlow, Gap Town, the Terrace card or the Dam card, and say why to a partner. Every card prints "invented for practice".

## The six cards

**Picture 1:** A boat landing beside a river with a flat field behind it. Goes with Marlow.
**Picture 2:** Two flat fields beside a wide river. Goes with Marlow.
**Picture 3:** A road running through a low gap between two mountains. Goes with Gap Town.
**Picture 4:** Steep hillside cut into level strips. Goes with the Terrace card.
**Picture 5:** A river held back by a wall, a lake behind it. Goes with the Dam card.
**Picture 6:** A pile of red and yellow fallen leaves by a lake. Goes with no card; "a pretty sight is not a reason to settle".

## Key (teacher)

Pictures 1 and 2: Marlow; the river gives water, food or boats. Picture 3: Gap Town; the road through the gap. Picture 4: Terrace. Picture 5: Dam. Picture 6: no line; a pretty sight. Any line with a because is a good one. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
