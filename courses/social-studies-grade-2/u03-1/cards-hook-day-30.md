# Hook and retrieval pictures, day 30

Every picture prints "Drawing by Curio Class, 2026" under it. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Retrieval picture

**Picture:** A small school at the foot of a hill. A stream runs beside the school yard. Paper and wrappers lie on the grass by the stream. No people.
**Ask, one at a time, answered to a partner then shared:** "Name the land and water." "How could people make this smaller?" "How could a class choose together?" The teacher listens and tallies who needed help.

## Terraced Field

**Picture:** A steep hillside drawn as level steps, each step a narrow flat strip with rows of green plants and a low stone edge along its front. One small farmer with a basket on the middle step. No path.
**Caption, read aloud:** Steps cut into a steep hill make level places to plant. This is a drawing of a made-up hillside.
**The three questions:** Who made it? Curio Class. When? 2026. Where made? A drawing of a made-up hillside.

## The raised house

**Picture:** A small house standing on tall posts above shallow water, a short ladder from the door to a boat tied below. Reeds at the edge.
**Caption, read aloud:** A house raised on posts. This is a drawing of a made-up house.

## Hook

Show the Terraced Field beside the raised house. Ask: "Should people change the land, or change their ways to fit it?" The answers go on the question board, unexplained.
