# Day 33: How people help after a flood

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fourth six on the record sheet. Lay out the Pond Farm card, the disaster cards and helper pictures for a quiet task. Say aloud that Dune Ridge and Gull Point are made up, and that the real cards follow city pages named on the card.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 32's exit (error C), show Pond Farm: a short arrow from the next field and a long arrow from a town across the mountains. The class labels each arrow near or far from the key ([[s03-P33-1]]), then says what each carries ([[s03-P33-2]]).

#### 3 min. Calm opening

Say: "Today's cards are about how people help each other after a flood, an earthquake or a hurricane. The pictures show places and helpers. These cards are about other places and are told once. You may look at the helper pictures instead of the others, and you may pass."

#### 10 min. A flood

Show the Flood Card and read its caption and source line. Ask the three questions: who made it (Curio Class, from a City of Montpelier page), when (the page shows no date; the flood was July 2023), where made (Montpelier, Vermont). Fill two boxes with the class: what the flood did to things ([[s03-K33-1]]) and what it changed for people ([[s03-K33-2]]). Show Recovery Card 1, read its caption and source line, and fill a third box: how people helped ([[s03-K33-3]]). Pip says: "Only buildings get hurt in a flood" ([[s03-F33-1]], error D). Say beside it: a flood also changes what people can do.

#### 6 min. An earthquake and a hurricane

Three minutes each, told once. Read the Dune Ridge card and its source line, and say the page does not say what happened there; the town is made up. Fill two boxes: one thing that changed for people ([[s03-K33-4]]) and how people helped ([[s03-K33-5]]). Read the Gull Point card the same way: [[s03-K33-6]] and [[s03-K33-7]].

#### 9 min. Supported work

The fourth six come to your table one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X33b]]. The others draw or write one effect of the flood on people ([[s03-P33-3]]), one help ([[s03-P33-4]]) and the card's name ([[s03-P33-5]]); day 34 hands these back. Collect them and write a one-line picture cue on each (about 20 minutes after school). A child may look at helper pictures instead.

#### 3 min. Calm close

Read the help line of the Flood Card again. Put the cards away. Offer the quiet task again. No child tells a story of their own.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card and read aloud: "Circle the picture that shows how the flood changed what a person could do" ([[s03-X33]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A circled floor or chair is error D.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the boxes on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: look at one picture of helpers together and say what they are doing.

**Skip.** The earthquake and hurricane cards.

- **10 min. Helpers.** Read the Recovery Card 1 caption aloud; a child may look at the pictures instead.
  **Say.** "How did people help?" Expect: crews, volunteers and neighbors helped.
- **15 min. My explanation.** [[s03-P33-3]] [[s03-P33-4]] [[s03-P33-5]] Your child draws or writes one effect, one help and the card.
  **Say.** "What could people not do?" Expect: use the shops, or go near the water.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X33]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X33b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tell only the flood and the hurricane cards. Longer: a child names a second helper on Recovery Card 1.

### Words and supports

Words: flood, earthquake, hurricane, helper, help, effect. Frames: "The Flood Card shows ___ (an effect on people). It shows ___ (a help)." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the floor or the chair on today's exit (error D), day 34 opens with a Creek Road card. Explanations come back with a picture cue. Day 34 teaches reading published advice.
