# Repair cards, days 31 to 34

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Each repair is at most 5 minutes.

## Landing Card (error B, day 31)

**Picture:** Fern Landing: a small river landing, two boats tied, a cart with sacks, and a flat field of beans behind it.
**Say:** "People could settle at Fern Landing because boats could land and goods could move, and the field could grow food." Pip says people live there because it is pretty; the right idea is said beside it.

## Road Card (error P, day 31 small group)

**Picture:** A new road cut through a hill, the hill with a clear slice taken from it, a cart on the road and a short trip marked on a map.
**Say:** "What changed for travel? The trip is shorter. What changed on the hill? A slice was cut away."

## Roof and road sort (error A, day 32)

**Picture:** Two cards and a line between two headings, adapt and change the land. Card 1: a slanted roof on a rainy village house. Card 2: a road cut through a hill. Then the raised-house card is placed on the line between.

## Pond Farm Card (error C, day 33)

**Picture:** Pond Farm: a farm with a pond, a barn and a field. Two arrows come in. A short arrow from the next field brings seed. A long arrow from a town across the mountains brings tools. A key says which is near and which is far.
**Say:** "Seed comes from near. Tools come from far." Pip says a farm gets everything from its own fields; the right idea is said beside it.

## Key (teacher)

Landing: a landing, boats, goods and a field; pretty is not a reason. Road: shorter trips for travel, a slice cut from the hill. Sort: roof adapt, road change the land, raised house both. Pond Farm: seed near, tools far.
