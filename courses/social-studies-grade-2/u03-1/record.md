# Unit 3 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into five rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the teacher's table on days 30, 35 and 40, the second six on days 31, 36 and 41, the third six on days 32, 37 and 42, the fourth six on days 33, 38 and 43, and the fifth six on days 34 and 39; any child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block. Each E column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, drawing a line or boxing, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The End columns are the day 44 sitting, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Item 4 is not asked on day 44: copy into its cells the revised explanation of day 35 or 36 (an effect on people, a help, the card named), and keep the first explanation too. Each child gives one spoken reason on item 1, item 3 or item 5, by thirds. The four groups for day 45 are items 1 and 2 (A, B, P), items 3 and 4 with exits 35, 36 and 42 (C, D, E), items 5 to 7 (F to J, L to O) and item 8 (K). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
