# Section 3.1 opener: people and the land they live with

## What the section does

Four days, 30 to 33, of 36 to 38 minutes. The unit asks: should people change the land, or change their ways to fit it? Day 30 teaches why people settle where they do (a river city and a gap town, both made up for practice) and what it means to change the land (a terrace, a dam). Day 31 teaches how people adapt (a house on posts, a steep roof, shade and thin clothes) and where adapting and changing the land overlap. Day 32 compares four kinds of community, a city, a suburb, a small town and a farm community, and what each gets from near and from far. Day 33 tells, calmly and once, how a flood, an earthquake and a hurricane can affect people and how people helped.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a repair or a retrieval, then a short teaching part, a part for the children to work with, the day's rotating six heard at the teacher's table, and a one-picture exit. Day 33 adds a calm opening and a calm close, and no child tells a story of their own.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Adapt** means change what we do, wear or build to fit a place. **Change the land** means reshape land or water. A house raised on posts is both: people fit the water and put something new on the land.
- **A reason to settle** is something a place gives: water, food, a way for boats, a road through the mountains. A pretty view is not a reason.
- **A place offers possibilities.** It does not decide what every person there does.
- **Naming a thing does not explain it.** "The dam" names it; "the dam holds back river water and makes a lake" says what changed.
- **Every community gets some things from near and some from far.** No kind of community is ranked above another.
- **Disasters are told calmly.** The pictures show places and helpers, never a person hurt. A child may look at the helper pictures instead and may pass.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: people live by a river because it is pretty (B, day 30); the dam is all it changed (P, day 30); a house on posts is only adapting (A, day 31); a farm gets everything from its own fields (C, day 32); only buildings get hurt in a flood (D, day 33).

## Supplies for the section

The Terraced Field drawing and the raised-house drawing, the Marlow and Gap Town maps, five Changing Land Cards, four Place Cards, four community cards, the Flood Card, Recovery Card 1, the earthquake and hurricane cards, the repair cards, the exit cards, the practice explanation sheet, the record sheet and the question board. The teacher says aloud that every place and child on a card marked "invented for practice" is made up. Every real line on a card names its page, its date and its link, and the page itself is not copied.
