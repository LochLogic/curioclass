# Disaster cards, day 33

These cards are told once, calmly. The pictures show places and helpers, never a person hurt. A child may look at the helper pictures instead and may pass. The pages named are not copied; the cards are Curio Class's own words made from them.

## Flood Card

**Picture:** A downtown street with water lying in it, closed shop doors and a sign that says Keep Out. No people in the water. Credit line under the picture.
**Caption, read aloud:** In July 2023 a flood came to Montpelier, a city in Vermont. The rivers rose to more than 21 feet. Several feet of water stood downtown. Moving water can knock a person off their feet, so people were told to stay out of it.
**Source line:** City of Montpelier, Flood Guide, montpelier-vt.org/610/Flood-Guide. The page shows no date; the month and year of the flood come from the City's recovery page below. Made by Curio Class from these pages.
**Two boxes for the class:** What the flood did to things. What it changed for people.

## Recovery Card 1

**Picture:** Helpers in work gloves carrying a wet chair out of a shop to a truck, and a long line of helpers at a table.
**Caption, read aloud:** After the flood, crews took away more than 1,700 tons of debris. Volunteers were sent to hundreds of jobs. Neighbors helped each other.
**Source line:** City of Montpelier, Flood and Disaster Recovery Assistance, updated August 21, 2023, montpelier-vt.org/1407/Flood-Disaster-Recovery-Assistance. Made by Curio Class from this page.
**Then say:** "How people helped."

## Earthquake card

**Printed under the title:** Original, invented for practice.
**Picture:** A small town, a few shelves fallen inside a shop window, neighbors picking things up together.
**Caption:** In Dune Ridge, a town made up for practice, an earthquake shook the ground. Shelves fell and the shops closed for a day. Neighbors helped each other pick up.
**Source line:** Advice from Ready.gov, Earthquakes, updated September 9, 2026, ready.gov/earthquakes. The page does not say what happened in Dune Ridge.

## Hurricane card

**Printed under the title:** Original, invented for practice.
**Picture:** A small seaside town with bending trees, a school with its windows boarded and an empty bus yard, neighbors carrying a box of food to a door.
**Caption:** In Gull Point, a town made up for practice, a hurricane brought strong wind and rain. The school closed for three days. Neighbors checked on each other and shared food.
**Source line:** Advice from Ready.gov, Hurricanes, updated October 16, 2026, ready.gov/hurricanes. The page does not say what happened in Gull Point.

## Calm opening and close

Opening, said aloud: "Today's cards are about how people help each other after a flood, an earthquake or a hurricane. The pictures show places and helpers. These cards are about other places and are told once. You may look at the helper pictures instead of the others, and you may pass." Close: read the help line of the Flood Card again, put the cards away, and offer the quiet task again.

## Key (teacher)

Flood Card: things, water in the street and shops; people, they stayed out of the water. Recovery Card 1: crews took debris away; volunteers and neighbors helped. Earthquake card: shops closed for a day; neighbors helped. Hurricane card: school closed; neighbors shared food. Pip says: "Only buildings get hurt in a flood." The right idea is said beside it.
