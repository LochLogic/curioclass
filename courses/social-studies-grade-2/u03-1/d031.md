# Day 31: How people adapt

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Build Pip's sort with the four sorting cards: the porch and the school garden under change the land; the tunnel and the boots under adapt. Lay out the other cards. Say aloud that every card is invented for practice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 30's exit (error B), show Fern Landing: two boats, a cart of sacks, a bean field. Ask [[s03-P31-1]]. Say beside Pip's "pretty" that pretty is not a reason. If more than a third needed help with day 30's retrieval, show the school picture again instead.

#### 10 min. How people adapt

Adapt means we change what we do, wear or build to fit a place. Read the Place Cards. Ask [[s03-K31-1]] for Stilt Bay (posts), [[s03-K31-2]] for Snowy Pass (steep roofs) and [[s03-K31-3]] for Hot Flats (shade, thin clothes). Review: on day 23 you learned a region is an area that shares something. Show Hill Village beside Snowy Pass: both share deep snow, so they share a need. Ask [[s03-K31-4]]. **Ask.** "Must every home look alike?" ([[s03-P31-6]]). Expect: no; one has steep roofs and one keeps snowshoes. Similar places can adapt in different ways.

#### 8 min. Adapt, change the land, or both

Lay two headings: adapt and change the land. The class sorts the terrace, dam, steep roof, thick coat and raised house cards ([[s03-K31-5]]); the raised house goes on the line between. People adapted to the water and put something new on the land. Pip says: "A house on posts is only adapting; the land is the same" ([[s03-F31-1]], error A). Say the right idea beside it.

#### 11 min. Supported work

The second six come to your table one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X31b]]. A small group for error P takes the Road Card for 5 minutes: they fix Pip's answer, "The road" ([[s03-P31-2]]), then label the shorter trip and the cut slice ([[s03-P31-3]]). The others, in pairs, move the two pictures under the wrong heading in Pip's sort ([[s03-P31-4]]; it stays in the folder), then say why one goes under its new heading ([[s03-P31-5]]). Watch for only the tunnel moved (error A).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card and read aloud: "Circle the picture that shows people changing the land" ([[s03-X31]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A circled cap or scarf is error A.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four sorting pictures on the board under two headings.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a roof, a coat and a tunnel and sort them.

**Skip.** The small group and the region frame.

- **10 min. Places.** Read the Stilt Bay and Snowy Pass captions.
  **Say.** "How do people there adapt?" Expect: houses on posts; steep roofs.
- **15 min. Pip's sort.** [[s03-P31-4]] Your child moves the two pictures that sit under the wrong heading.
  **Say.** "Why does this go there?" Expect: it is built to fit a place, or it reshapes the land.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X31]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X31b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort only the roof, coat, terrace and dam cards. Longer: a child finds one more thing people both adapt to and change the land for.

### Words and supports

Words: adapt, change the land, both, region, share. Frames: "People adapt by ___." "People change the land by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the cap or the scarf on today's exit (error A), day 32 opens with a roof card and a road card sorted, then the raised house placed between. Day 32 teaches how a city, a suburb, a small town and a farm community meet daily needs from near and far.
