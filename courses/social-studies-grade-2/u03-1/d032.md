# Day 32: Near and far

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the third six on the record sheet. On the Bean Hill map, find the edge where the long rice arrow starts and note its direction word: the answer to [[s03-K32-3]]. Lay out the cards and a frame strip for each pair. Say aloud that every card is invented for practice and none is better than another.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 31's exit (error A), lay two headings, adapt and change the land. The class sorts the roof card, the road card and the raised house card ([[s03-P32-1]]); the raised house goes on the line between.

#### 10 min. Four communities

Read the four cards: Tern City, a city; Plum Lane, a suburb; Wren Town, a small town; Bean Hill, a farm community. Review: on day 2 you read a key. Each map's key says the short blue arrow is water from nearby and the long arrow is rice from far away. On Tern City the class labels each arrow near or far ([[s03-K32-1]]) and says what each carries ([[s03-K32-2]]). Use the compass rose once, on Bean Hill ([[s03-K32-3]]). Say: "Every community gets some things from near and some from far." **Ask.** "What does this place get from near and what from far?" Expect: water from near, rice from far.

#### 6 min. Compare

Pairs fill the frame: "The city and the farm both get ___ from near and ___ from far" ([[s03-P32-3]]) and "The city has ___ and the farm has ___" ([[s03-P32-4]]). Pip says: "A farm gets everything it needs from its own fields" ([[s03-F32-1]], error C). Say the right idea beside it. Write "How do communities meet needs?" on the question board.

#### 11 min. Supported work

The third six come to your table one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X32b]]. A small group for error A from day 31's six takes the roof, road and raised house cards for 5 minutes. The others, in pairs, label each arrow on the Plum Lane card ([[s03-P32-5]]) and the Wren Town card ([[s03-P32-6]]) near or far, and fill the strip; all four pages stay in the folder. Watch for near and far swapped (error C).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card. Read aloud: "Hill Farm's own field grows oats. Circle one thing the farm gets from outside its own fields" ([[s03-X32]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add the class's answers in its own words.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the frame on the board; children copy it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a house, a field and two arrows, a short one for water and a long one for rice.

**Skip.** The small group and the strip.

- **10 min. The city card.** Read the Tern City card and its key.
  **Say.** "What comes from near, and what from far?" Expect: water from near; rice from far.
- **15 min. Near and far.** [[s03-P32-3]] [[s03-P32-4]] Your child fills both frames.
  **Say.** "Does a farm get everything from its own fields?" Expect: no; some things come from near and some from far.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X32]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X32b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: compare the city and the farm only. Longer: a child names one more thing a place could get from near or far.

### Words and supports

Words: city, suburb, small town, farm community, near, far, arrow. Frames: "The city and the farm both get ___ from near and ___ from far." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the oats field on today's exit (error C), day 33 opens with a Pond Farm card, two arrows traced, near and far named. Day 33 tells, calmly, how a flood, an earthquake or a hurricane can affect people and how people help.
