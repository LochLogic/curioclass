# Exit keys, days 30 to 33 (teacher only)

For the teacher only. Never print this page for children; the exit cards carry no answers.

Day 30: the lake, because it gives water, fish or a way to travel; the field is dry now, so children can play on it. Day 31: the cap or the scarf, they adapt by covering up from the wind; both. Day 32: pears from near and cloth from far away; the cloth, because the map shows its town far off. Day 33: the neighbors carrying books; the child cannot go in to borrow a book; the people who cross the bridge. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
