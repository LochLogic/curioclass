# Changing Land Cards, days 30, 31 and 43

Five cards, kept for day 43. Each card ends "Made by Curio Class". Plain black line drawing on white. The terrace and dam cards say what changed; the other three are adapt or both.

## Card 1: Terrace

**Picture:** The Terraced Field steps in small.
**Caption:** People cut steps into a steep hill. The land now has level strips to plant. Soil stays on the strips.
**Model:** "People cut steps, so the land has level strips."

## Card 2: Dam

**Picture:** A river with a low wall across it, and a lake spread out behind the wall.
**Caption:** People build a dam across a river. The dam holds back river water and makes a lake.
**Model:** "People build a dam, so the land has a lake." Pip says: "The dam. That is what it changed." The right idea is said beside it: the dam holds back river water and makes a lake.

## Card 3: Steep roof

**Picture:** A house in deep snow with a steep roof and snow sliding off one side.
**Caption:** In a snowy place people build steep roofs, so the snow slides off.

## Card 4: Thick coat

**Picture:** A person in a thick coat, hat and boots in falling snow.
**Caption:** In a cold place people wear thick coats.

## Card 5: Raised house

**Picture:** The raised house of the hook, small.
**Caption:** In a place where water rises, people build a house on posts. People fit the water and put something new on the land.

## Sorting, day 31

Two headings: adapt, change the land. The terrace and dam go under change the land; the roof and coat go under adapt; the raised house goes on the line between: both.
