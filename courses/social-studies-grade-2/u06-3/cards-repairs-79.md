# Repair cards, day 79

Every card prints "invented for practice". One fresh card for the letter most missed on day 78. None is an exit case. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

- **F:** "A family moves to the harbor town. They learn the town's words. The town paints a new sign in two languages." Two change arrows: one for the family, one for the place.
- **G:** "On Cobb Street, two neighbors cook for the street party." Say one difference between the two neighbors.
- **K:** "A hat worn in sun and a scarf worn in snow." Say what is different and why neither is the best.
- **L:** the local nation's own page read again; find one sentence in the present tense (teacher-supplied; never another nation's page).
- **M:** a fresh town card for each group: a family that came this year, families who have always lived here, the nation (teacher-supplied line). A token for each line that shows something added.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

F: the family learned words; the town got a new sign. G: any real difference. K: any difference, no winner. L: a present-tense sentence from the nation's own page. M: a token on each group's line.
