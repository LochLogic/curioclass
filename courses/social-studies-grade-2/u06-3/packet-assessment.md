## Memory M

Label: "A Curio Class retelling based on Mary Antin, The Promised Land, 1912. Memories, not a complete account of every family member."
**Text:** "After we reached Boston, we had to learn English. In Chelsea, my mother worked in the family store. She learned English while helping customers. Father subscribed to Yiddish newspapers. At school I wrote a poem and read it to my classmates. Later a Boston newspaper printed my poem."

## TEACHER ONLY: day 80 key (detach before copying; never printed on a child's packet)

Item 8: a question about M or P that bears on life after moving, such as "Who took P, and when?" Item 9: M can tell that the family had to learn English, the mother worked in a store, the father subscribed to Yiddish newspapers or the writer's poem was printed; P can tell it shows a group of schoolchildren at a public school in Chelsea.
