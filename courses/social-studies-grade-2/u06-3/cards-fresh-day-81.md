# Fresh cards, day 81

One fresh case for each of the four groups. Every card prints "invented for practice". Fresh means not in the day 80 packet and not an exit. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Group 1 (items 1 to 7: B, C, J): a fresh interview record

Teller (invented): Ms. Reyes. "I came to this town in 2005. I came by boat. I would rather not say why." The record shows one topic missing (where), one guessed answer ("a big blue boat"), and two dated events out of order (2012 before 2008).

## Group 2 (items 8 to 10: D, I): a fresh photograph and caption

**Picture:** children around a market stall with a striped awning. **Caption:** "Market day on Ash Lane. Photographer and date unknown." Ask: a question that helps; what it can tell; what it cannot tell.

## Group 3 (items 11 to 15 and 20: A, F, H): a fresh memory

"I brought a flatbread recipe here. I learned to make the town's cheese. We bake together on Saturdays."

## Group 4 (items 16 to 19: G, K, L, M): a fresh town card

A town card with three lines: "A family that came in the spring sells paper lanterns." "Families who have always lived here run the Saturday book swap." "The nation's own page" (teacher-supplied, present tense, never another nation's page).

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Group 1: how and why are not the missing topic; where is. The guess is rubbed out. 2008 goes before 2012. Group 2: a question about the photograph; it shows a group at a market; it cannot tell what anyone said. Group 3: flatbread brought; cheese learned; a claim naming the memory and a line. Group 4: a token on each group's line; no ranking.
