# Exit cards, days 79 and 81

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other ask is spoken and heard from the rotating six. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. Day 80 has no exit; it is the check. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 79 exit card: Song Case

**Card line:** "The Orsa family moves to Hollis Lane. They keep singing their own songs. They learn the street's words for things. They teach the neighbors a new song."
**Three pictures:** the Orsas saying the street's words; the neighbors singing the new song; the Orsas singing their own songs.
**Mark:** Put a token on the one picture that shows something that did not change.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "On the Orsa card, say one thing that changed, starting: The Orsa card shows ___."

## Day 81 exit card: the weaving memory

**Card line:** "I wove cloth in my old town. Here I learned my neighbors' words and taught them to weave."
**Two pictures:** weaving; the neighbors' words.
**Mark:** Put a token on the one the writer brought.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "On the weaving memory, ask one question that helps us learn about life after the move."
