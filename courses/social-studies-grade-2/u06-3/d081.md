# Day 81: Try the hard part again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Order the four groups from the day 80 record, most missed first. Say aloud that the fresh cards are made up. Any child may pass. Key: key-exits-79-81.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back day 79's claim (s06-P79-1) and day 80's assessment, each with a written cue and a picture of the part to try next.

#### 15 min. Grouped reteaching

Review: respond-day groups, day 71. Four groups, about 3.5 minutes each (4, 4, 4 and 3), most missed first; skip a group with no misses. At most four children take a 45-second turn, one ask each; the rest point. The others work in pairs from the written move strip.
- **Group 1, items 1 to 7 (error B, error C, error J; s06-E1).** [[s06-P81-1]] [[s06-P81-2]] [[s06-P81-3]] Pip says error C: "She said boat. I will write a big blue boat." Say beside it: only what she said.
- **Group 2, items 8 to 10 (error D, error I; s06-E8).** [[s06-P81-4]] Pip says error I: "The photograph shows they were happy." Say beside it: "The photograph shows ___; it cannot tell ___."
- **Group 3, items 11 to 15 and 20 (error A, error F, error H; s06-E11).** Read the bee memory from the slip. [[s06-P81-5]] [[s06-P81-6]] Two change arrows: hats learned; honey table added. Pip says error H: "My claim is right because I like it."
- **Group 4, items 16 to 19 (error G, error K, error L, error M; s06-E16).** [[s06-P81-7]] Pip says error M: "Only the new family has culture." Say beside it: every group has culture, and none is the best.

#### 5 min. Fresh evidence

Children mark [[s06-X81]]. Hear the first six, 45 seconds each: [[s06-X81b]], reading the weaving card only at each turn. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 5 min. Reflection

Review: the self-check strip from unit 5 and day 80. Each child marks progress and a next step, and writes the part to try again on the student page. The question board stays closed. At home, talk through each miss instead.

### Other rooms

Write each fresh card on a slip; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each fresh card on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at the returned claim and the check. **Say.** "Which part do you want to try again?" Expect: one part, such as what the writer brought.
- **15 min. A fresh card.** Read the bee memory. [[s06-P81-5]] **Say.** "What did the writer bring?" Expect: keeping bees.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X81]] [[s06-X81b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: meet only the two most-missed groups. Longer: what other source could check a memory?

### Words and supports

Words: brought, changed, source, claim, memory. Frames: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." "The photograph shows ___; it cannot tell ___." Pointing and saying count.

### Tomorrow

The token on the neighbors' words (error F) or a question that does not ask about the memory (error D) or says more than it gives (error I) goes on the record sheet. Day 82 opens unit 7, Understanding: the big question and the menu of about 16 countries, and each child chooses one; it opens with retrieval only.

### Credit

Ours. Every fresh card and the bee memory are made up. The nation line is a Curio Class summary of the nation's own page, with its credit line.
