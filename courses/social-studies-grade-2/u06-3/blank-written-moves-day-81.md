# Written moves, day 81

One strip for each group, read by the pairs that are waiting. Large print.

| Group | The move |
|---|---|
| 1 (items 1 to 7) | Find the missing topic. Rub out the guess. Put the earlier date first. |
| 2 (items 8 to 10) | Ask a question about the photograph. Say "The photograph shows ___; it cannot tell ___." |
| 3 (items 11 to 15 and 20) | Say what was brought. Draw two change arrows. Say "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." |
| 4 (items 16 to 19) | Put a token on each group's line. Say one difference inside a group. Do not rank. |
