# Day 79: Answer the big question

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the two Odel lines on the board, covered. Say that Odel, Mr. Okoye and the bus-stop family are made up. Choose a shareable day 74 or 75 line. Mark the third six, and three each from the first and second sixes not yet heard. Key: key-exits-79-81.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Teach the letter most missed on s06-X78. Error F: board, "A girl's family moves to a hill town. They learn the town bus. The town adds a bus stop." Two change arrows. Errors G, K, L, M: the repair card.

#### 6 min. A claim, modeled

Review: the unit 5 claim frame. Uncover: "Odel moves to a cold town. Odel buys a warm coat at the town store." "The store starts a Friday table for Odel's kind of soup." Say and write: "I think both changed because Odel bought a coat and the store added a soup table. The Odel lines show both, made by Curio Class. These lines cannot measure which changed more." **Ask.** "Which source shows that, and what does it say?" Pip says error H: "My claim is right because I like it." Wipe the model.

#### 12 min. The closing claim

Pairs hold the frame and three cards, the Posadas Card with its day 78 line, Language Today and the shareable line. **Ask.** "When people move to a new place, do they change the place more, or does the place change them more?" [[s06-P79-1]] Said, then recorded. A pair may say either change, or that the cards cannot measure which; it names a card and a line, never ranking a culture. From your table, 45 seconds a child: hear the third six on [[s06-X79b]], reading the Orsa card only at each turn. Then hear three of the first six, about 90 seconds each, on s06-X77b and s06-X77c from the cook card. Mark by the scale; a pass is heard. Hearing takes 9 of these 12 minutes; in all, today's hearing is about 13.5 minutes.

#### 8 min. Supported work

The written cues come back. 3 minutes: revise the day 75 interview record and the day 76 dated strip. 5 minutes: revise the day 78 explanation, s06-P78-1. Both versions kept; the revision is scored. Hear three of the second six, 90 seconds each, on s06-X78b and s06-X78c. The last 3 minutes: repair one group of at most three, one minute each, on the letter most missed on s06-X78.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read [[s06-X79]] aloud; each writes the claim. [[s06-X79b]] was heard for six children.

#### 3 min. Question board

Say the unit's question; the class answers aloud. Close the board.

### Other rooms

No printed cards: write each on a slip.

### At home

**Swap.** No Posadas Card: use Language Today.

**Skip.** The six.

- **5 min. Model.** Read the two Odel lines. **Say.** "Did Odel change, or did the store?" Expect: both.
- **15 min. Your claim.** [[s06-P79-1]] **Say.** "Which card shows that, and what line?" Expect: a card and a line it gives.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X79]] [[s06-X79b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card, not three. Longer: what card could check your claim?

### Words and supports

Words: claim, source, changed, kept. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___, made by ___."

### Tomorrow

A token on a changed picture (error F) or a spoken line with no card (error H) goes on the record sheet. Day 80 is the unit check: no repair, no teaching.
