# Section 6.3 opener: the closing claim, the check and the response

## What the section does

Three days, 79 to 81. Day 79 answers the unit's question, "When people move to a new place, do they change the place more, or does the place change them more?", in pairs from the Posadas Card (Music Here), Language Today and a line of the day 74 account or a record a teller allowed to share. A child may use Language Today instead of Music Here. A modeled claim on a separate invented move comes first; its filled version is withdrawn before the pairs work. The same day, children revise the interview record and the day 78 explanation from the written cues; only the revisions are scored. Day 80 is the end-of-unit check on a short memory and a photograph not seen before. Day 81 responds: four small groups re-teach what the check showed, then a fresh task.

## How the days run

Day 79 follows the usual shape. Day 80 is a sitting: the packet read aloud neutrally inside each item, individual paper work, five minutes to collect and for each child to mark the self-check strip with one piece of work that supports the mark. Items 1 to 7 are read from retained records and take no day 80 minutes. Day 81 opens with feedback, then four groups of about 3.5 minutes, then a fresh task, then reflection. No child is asked about their own home, family, money, religion or how they came here, and any child may pass.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A claim may stress that the newcomers changed more, that the place did, or that these sources cannot measure which.** It names a source and the line it gives, and it never ranks a culture.
- **Unknown or declined answers are accurate recording,** never a gap to fill.
- **A photograph shows what is visible,** not what was said or felt.
- **Native nations are living governments** named by their own names in the present tense.
- **Scores are kept apart:** each topic, each element, each change, the question, what it can tell and what it cannot.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says "My claim is right because I like it" (H, day 79). The letters repaired on day 79 from day 78 are F, G, K, L and M.

## What stays held back

The day 80 packet (a memory, a photograph and a nation card) is printed only on day 80. The day 81 exit card prints only on day 81. The filled Odel model is withdrawn on day 79 before the pairs work.
