# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 79 and 81

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-79-81.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 79: the Orsas singing their own songs; the card shows the neighbors learned a new song or the Orsas learned the street's words. Day 81: weaving; a question about the memory, such as "How did you learn those words?" Never infer that everyone learned weaving. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
