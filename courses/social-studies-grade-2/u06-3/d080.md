# Day 80: Unit check, family stories and culture

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print one packet each, and large-print copies. Show no timers. Read each card once, neutrally; give no hint. Children underline, circle, point, draw or write a few words. A child who cannot mark says it: at most four, a minute each. Items 1 to 7 come from the day 75 round and the day 79 revised record, with no minutes today. Photograph P: before printing, a person looks at the photograph itself and checks it against its caption; read the caption only.

### The day

#### 3 min. Item 8

Read Memory M once, and the caption of Photograph P once; show P in large print. Ask [[s06-E8]]. Children choose M or P and draw or write a question.

#### 3 min. Item 9

Ask [[s06-E9]]. Children underline a line of M or point to a part of P, or write a few words.

#### 2 min. Item 10

Ask [[s06-E10]]. Children write a few words or draw.

#### 1 min. Item 11

Read M again. Ask [[s06-E11]]. Children underline a line of M.

#### 1 min. Item 12

Ask [[s06-E12]]. Children underline a different line.

#### 2 min. Item 13

Ask [[s06-E13]]. Children underline.

#### 2 min. Item 14

Ask [[s06-E14]]. Children underline.

#### 2 min. Item 15

Ask [[s06-E15]]. Children underline.

#### 1 min. Item 16

Ask [[s06-E16]]. Children draw or write a few words.

#### 1 min. Item 17

Ask [[s06-E17]]. Children draw or write a few words.

#### 2 min. Item 18

Read the Oneida card once. Ask [[s06-E18]]. Children underline a line of the card.

#### 1 min. Item 19

Ask [[s06-E19]]. Children draw or write a few words.

#### 4 min. Item 20

Ask [[s06-E20]]. Children say it under their breath, then record it with the frame; transcription support only.

#### 5 min. Collection

Collect the packets. Read the community part of items 16 to 19 also from the revised day 79 explanation, and the day 79 exit s06-X79 and claim s06-P79-1 for the record; first tries of days 73, 74, 77 and 78 were practice. The self-check strip from unit 5: three pictured choices and one piece of work that supports a mark. Children mark it. After class mark each part: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Scores stay apart: the question, what it can tell, what it cannot; each element; each change; the claim.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: read each card from the page, once.

**Skip.** The self-check strip and the lines said to a grown-up.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Read each card once; give no help. [[s06-E8]] [[s06-E9]] [[s06-E10]] [[s06-E20]]
  **Say.** "Say one thing Memory M can tell us." Expect: a line it gives, such as learning English.
  **Say.** "Say one thing the Oneida Nation adds today, from the Oneida card." Expect: it governs itself or its schools teach its language.
- **10 min. Talk it through.** Which part felt hardest? Your child picks one to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 8, 9, 14 and 20. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: memory, photograph, source, culture, brought, changed. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing, drawing and saying to the teacher count.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 takes no repair. Day 81 meets four groups from this record: items 1 to 7 (errors B, C, J; s06-E1), items 8 to 10 (D, I; s06-E8), items 11 to 15 and 20 (A, F, H; s06-E11) and items 16 to 19 (G, K, L, M; s06-E16). Day 79's claim and today's work come back with a written cue.

### Credit

Photograph P: the plate opposite printed page 230 of that book, public domain; the photographer is unknown. Oneida card: Oneida Nation of Wisconsin, oneida-nsn.gov, home page and Oneida Nation School System page; summary by Curio Class, not an exact quotation.
