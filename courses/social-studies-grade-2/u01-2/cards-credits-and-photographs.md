# Credit cards and photograph cards (days 6 to 8)

Say first: "We made these cards for our class. The photographs show no children, and the practice credits are made up." Photographs are taken in the school with no child in the picture. Each card prints its credit line in 18 point type under the picture.

## Photograph Card T1 (day 6, the teacher's worked example)

**Picture:** A photograph, taken from a hallway, of a playground seen through a window. **Credit:** Photograph by Curio Class, 2026, made in a school hallway.
**How it is used (day 6):** the credit is cut into three slips: Photograph by Curio Class | 2026 | made in a school hallway. The teacher lays each slip on the matching row of the three-question card as children say which row it goes on. Then: "What place does it show?" (a playground; the slips said where it was made, a hallway). "What is this photograph for?"

## Photograph Card T2 (day 7 warm-up)

**Picture:** A photograph of a hallway wall with coat hooks next to a door. **Credit:** Photograph by Curio Class, 2026, made in a school hallway.
**Caption:** The coat hooks are next to the door. This is the best hallway.

## Photograph Card T3 (day 8, source two cards)

**Picture:** A photograph of a classroom board with a clock above it. **Credit:** Photograph by Curio Class, 2026. (No place of making is printed on this card, on purpose: pairs write not printed.)
**Caption:** A clock hangs above the board. This is the best clock.

## Six credit cards for each pair (day 6), sorted into who made it, who keeps it, what place it shows

1. Made by Ana Brook, 1931. 2. Drawn by Ng and Sons, mapmakers. 3. Kept in the Maple Street Library. 4. Held by the County Archive. 5. Shows Pine Lake Campground. 6. Shows Bell Cove Beach.
Two extra slips for the day 8 sort (error M): "Kept in the Town Hall box. Shows Fern Meadow." and "Held by the Hill Library. Shows Stone Bridge Road." These print no maker; pairs write "not printed" under who made it.

## Pip's filled card for T2 (day 7 warm-up)

The three-question card for T2, filled in by Pip and wrong in each box: Who made it? The Hall Library. When? Last year. Where was it made? On a playground. Pairs check each box against T2's credit line, one box at a time, and say whether it is right.

## Caption repair card (day 8, errors F and L)

**Picture:** A drawn bulletin board with a calendar on the right and a plant on the left. **Caption:** The plant is on the left of the calendar. This is the best plant. Pairs put a pencil tick on the spot in the picture that proves a statement; a statement with no spot gets no tick. Then they say what the card can still be used for.

## Day 5 repair cards for error D (day 6 warm-up): five pairs

A human action card beside the feature it changed: a stone wall with a pasture, a pond ring with ducks, a ditch with a field, a raised bank with a creek, a mowed lawn with a hill.

## For the teacher

Pip says the library made the map; beside it, a person or firm made it, and the library keeps it. A maker named in the credit line, the place made in the credit line, and the place the picture shows are three different things. "The record does not say" is a full answer for a credit that is not printed.
