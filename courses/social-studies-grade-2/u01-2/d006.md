# Day 6: Who made it, when and where

## Teacher page

### Before class

Choose today's six: group 1. Cut T1's credit into three slips. Keep picture 3 of the start-of-year page for the small group. Key: key-exits-6-8.md.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 5's marks (3 minutes). Teach error D to the class (5 minutes); a small group takes item 3 in Pairs work.

Error D: review: on day 5 you said "People ___ the ___ so ___." Say: "These cards are made up." Show the five repair pairs, an action beside its feature. Pip says, "There is a creek." Beside it: "People built a raised bank beside the creek."

**Ask.** What did people do here? Expect an action. Ask it of each pair.

#### 12 min. Three questions on a card

Review: in grade 1 you asked who made it, when and why, and said "the record does not say." Today's card swaps why for where it was made.

Show the real Boulder map (review: the start-of-year page). Read its margin from the transcript; children point to each line. The library's barcode and label are not on the map.

**Ask.** Who made it? Expect: the U.S. Geological Survey. Write it on the card.

**Ask.** When? Expect: 1904.

**Ask.** Where was it made? Expect: the margin does not say. Write "not printed."

**Ask.** What place does it show? Expect: Boulder, Colorado. The library keeps the map.

Say: "We made this photograph." Show T1, its credit cut into three slips. Read each slip; children say which row it goes on.

**Ask.** What is this photograph for? Expect: to show the playground from the hallway.

Pip says: "The library made this map." Beside it: "A person or firm made it; the library keeps it." Pip: "It was made in Boulder, because it shows Boulder." Beside it: "It shows Boulder; the margin does not say where it was made."

#### 12 min. Pairs work

Six credit cards a pair (made up): lay each under who made it, who keeps it or what place it shows (s01-P6-1). It stays in the folder.

Minutes 0 to 4: the small group takes item 3 (s01-R3): picture 3. Which could help us find out what Boulder looked like then? Expect: the old map.

Minutes 4 to 8: circulate: no library card under who made it; shown is not made.

Minutes 8 to 11: the six, 30 seconds each: mark the exit card (s01-X6), then answer (s01-X6b) and (s01-X6c). A child may pass or point.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card. Read aloud: "Circle the date." Mark 1 minute; collect 1; tick the record sheet 1. A date, name or place not printed is error M.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed map: draw one and write a maker, a year and a place on its edge.

**Skip.** The sort.

- **8 min. A credit.** Your child draws a picture and writes under it "Drawn by ___, 2026, made at this table."
  **Say.** "Who made it? When? Where was it made?" Expect: your child, 2026, at this table.
- **8 min. Keeper.** Put the picture in a box labeled with a made-up keeper, such as "Kept in the Blue Box."
  **Say.** "Who made it, and who keeps it?" Expect: your child made it; the box keeps it.
- **9 min. Made or shown.** Draw a beach.
  **Say.** "What place does it show? Where was it made?" Expect: a beach; at the table.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X6b]] Then [[s01-X6c]] Expect the maker in the credit, not the box; the place in the credit, not the picture.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four cards. Longer: pairs write a credit for a drawing.

### Words and supports

Words: maker, date, place, made, keeps, shows. Frame: "It was made by ___. It was made in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 teaches fact and opinion. Today's marks go to day 8's credit sort: the keeper's tag as maker is error E, the room shown as place made is error J, a name or place not printed is error M.
