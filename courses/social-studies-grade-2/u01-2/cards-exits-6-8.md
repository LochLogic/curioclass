# Exit cards, days 6 to 8

Each card is marked by every child, then the spoken parts are heard from the day's six, one at a time. Photographs show no child. Every invented card prints "invented for practice" in small letters; Room Photograph A is a real photograph and prints its real credit. Do not name Room Photograph A, its maker or its credit line before day 6.

## Day 6 exit card: Room Photograph A

**Picture:** A photograph of an empty classroom with desks and a window. **Credit line printed under it:** Photograph by Curio Class, 2026, made in a school doorway. A separate tag at the card's edge reads: Kept in the supply room box. (The tag is the keeper's label, not a credit.)

**Mark:** Circle the date. **Spoken, two stems, one after the other:** "Point to who made it." "Point to where it was made."

## Day 7 exit card: boot tray

**Picture:** A photograph of a boot tray by a door with four boots on a mat. Two statements under it: Four boots sit on the mat. These boots are the coolest.

**Mark:** Put the check token on the sentence you can check. **Spoken:** "What can this source help us find?"

## Day 8 exit card: a window and a door

**Picture:** A photograph of a classroom wall with a window beside a door. **Credit line:** Photograph by Dana Whitfield, 2026, made in a school lobby (invented for practice). Two statements: A window sits beside a door. This is our best room.

**Marks:** Circle who made it. Place the check token on the statement that can be checked. **Spoken, two stems, one after the other:** "Point to where it was made." "Point to the date."
