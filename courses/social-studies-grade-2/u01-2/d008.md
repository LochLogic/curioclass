# Day 8: Source two cards

## Teacher page

### Before class

Choose today's six: group 3. Count day 6's misses (errors E, J, M) and day 7's (errors F, L). Key: key-exits-6-8.md.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Two parts of 5 minutes. Teach the part more children missed to the class; a small group takes the other in the next part. Freed minutes hand back marks.

Credit sort (errors E, J, M): lay out the six credit cards and two extra slips (made up): "Kept in the Town Hall box. Shows Fern Meadow." "Held by the Hill Library. Shows Stone Bridge Road." Children say where each goes. **Ask.** Who made these two? Expect: not printed. Pip says, "The library made it." Beside it: "A person or firm made it; the library keeps it."

Caption against its picture (errors F, L): the caption repair card (made up). Pairs put a pencil tick on the proof for each statement; a statement with no proof gets none. **Ask.** What can this card still help us find? Expect: where the plant is.

#### 19 min. Source two cards in pairs

Pairs hold the three-question card, T3 (made up) and the real Pickaway County map, printed in 1858. Do not point for them. Minutes 0 to 5: the small group takes the other repair.

Minutes 0 to 6, T3: fill the card, writing where it was made (s01-P8-2). For the caption, point to the proof of the statement that can be checked and say it.

Minutes 6 to 12, Pickaway: read the title block from the transcript, children pointing. Copy the maker as printed (s01-P8-1) and the date (s01-P8-3). **Ask.** Where was it made? Expect: Philadelphia, in the title block. The map shows Pickaway County, Ohio. R. P. Smith, named in the lower margin, entered the map with a court clerk; he is not the maker. Review: the title tells what a map shows; this map prints no key, so read its labels. Both stay in the folder.

Minutes 12 to 17: the six, 50 seconds each: mark the exit card (s01-X8) and (s01-X8b), then answer (s01-X8c) and (s01-X8d). A child may pass or point.

Three looks: words come from the credit; "not printed" where nothing is printed; maker, place made and place shown kept apart.

#### 3 min. Question board

**Ask.** Who made this source, and what can we check? Add the class's answer in its own words: the three questions, and the part that is not an opinion.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card: circle who made it (s01-X8); set the magnifier token on the statement that can be checked (s01-X8b). Read both aloud. Mark 2 minutes; collect and tick 1. The keeper as maker is error E; a token on both is error F.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed map: draw one.

**Skip.** The repairs.

- **8 min. No place.** Draw a picture. Write "Drawn by ___, 2026" and no place.
  **Say.** "Where was it made?" Expect: the credit does not say, so we write "not printed."
- **8 min. Proof.** Write two sentences under it, one you can check and one that is what someone thinks.
  **Say.** "Point to the proof for the one we can check."
- **9 min. Copy.** [[s01-P8-1]] [[s01-P8-3]] [[s01-P8-2]] Your child copies the words as printed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]] [[s01-X8b]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X8c]] Expect the place in the credit, not the room in the picture. Then [[s01-X8d]]: the year printed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: T3 only. Longer: read the Pickaway lower margin and say who it names.

### Words and supports

Words: maker, date, place made, fact, opinion. Frames: "It was made by ___." "It was made in ___." Pointing counts.
