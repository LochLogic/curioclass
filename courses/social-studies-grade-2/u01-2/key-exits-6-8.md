# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 6 to 8

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-6-8.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 6: the printed date; the maker named in the credit line; the printed making place, a doorway, not the classroom shown. The label on a box or archive is the keeper (error E). A name or place that is not printed is a guess (error M). The room in the picture is the place shown, not the place made (error J).
- Day 7: check token on Four boots sit on the mat; the other statement gets no check token. It can help find how many boots, not what everyone thinks is coolest. Check tokens on both is error F; throwing the card out is error L.
- Day 8: the maker in the credit line (Dana Whitfield); the check token on the window statement; the printed lobby as where it was made, not the room shown; the printed year 2026 as the date (a date not printed is error M).
