# Day 7: Fact or opinion

## Teacher page

### Before class

Choose today's six: group 2. Day 6's marks go to day 8, not here: note the letters on the record sheet. Put a set of tokens on each desk. Key: key-exits-6-8.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Warm-up

Show Photograph Card T2 (made up) and Pip's filled three-question card. Pairs check each box against the credit line and point to the words.

**Ask.** Is Pip's box right? Read each box. Expect: no; Curio Class, 2026, a school hallway.

#### 14 min. Fact or opinion

Review: in grade 1 a fact is something we can find out. New word: opinion, what someone thinks; it cannot be checked. Hold up the magnifier token: "I can check this on the picture." Then the thought token: "this is what someone thinks." Children say each.

On T2's caption, place no token.

**Ask.** How could we find out whether the coat hooks are next to the door? Expect: look at the picture and find the hooks and the door.

**Ask.** How could we find out whether this is the best hallway? Expect: we cannot; it is what someone thinks.

Show the pond map (made up): rose, barn, pond; caption "The pond is east of the barn. This is the prettiest pond."

**Ask.** How could we check the first statement? Expect: use the rose.

**Ask.** Using only what we checked, where is the pond? Expect: east of the barn. The opinion stays; the checked part still helps.

Pip says: "It is printed, so it is true." Beside it: "We can check some printed things; 'prettiest' is what someone thinks." Pip: "The caption says best, so I cannot use any of it." Beside it: "Check the part you can, and leave the opinion."

#### 14 min. Pairs work

The hallway picture and six statement cards (made up). Minutes 0 to 4: one reads a card, the other puts a pencil tip on its proof. A card with no proof goes face down.

Minutes 4 to 7: each pair takes a face-down card and says a new checkable statement about the shelf, bench or door (s01-P7-1); it stays in the folder.

Minutes 7 to 10: say the way from door to shelf using only the cards face up.

Minutes 10 to 13: the six, 30 seconds each: mark the exit card (s01-X7), then answer (s01-X7b). A child may pass or point.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card; a token sits on a statement as a counter sits on a square. Read aloud: "Put the check token on the sentence you can check." Mark 1 minute; collect 1; tick the record sheet 1. A token on both is error F; "it cannot help" is error L.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No tokens: two scraps of paper, one with a magnifier drawn.

**Skip.** The pairs.

- **8 min. Check it.** Draw a door, a shelf and a bench with two books.
  **Say.** "The shelf is next to the door. How could we check it?" Expect: look at the picture and find them.
- **8 min. What someone thinks.** Say, "This is the nicest shelf."
  **Say.** "How could we find out?" Expect: we cannot; it is what someone thinks.
- **9 min. Use it.** [[s01-P7-1]] Your child says one new statement that can be checked, then says the way from door to shelf with it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X7b]] Expect: how many boots, not what is coolest.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three cards. Longer: a checkable statement for each card face down.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, opinion, check. Frames: "I can check this on the picture." "This is what someone thinks." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 uses the three questions and fact or opinion on two new sources. It opens with two repairs: day 6's marks (errors E, J, M) and today's (a token on both, error F; the card thrown out, error L).
