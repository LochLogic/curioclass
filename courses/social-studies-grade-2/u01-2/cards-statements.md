# Statement cards (day 7)

Say first: "We made these cards for our class; the picture and the map are made up." Each prints "invented for practice".

## Pairs work: one hallway photograph and six statement cards

**Picture:** A drawn hallway seen from one end. A door on the left, a shelf beside the door, and a bench under a window farther along. Two books lie on the bench. Three boxes sit on the shelf.

**Six statement cards, one statement each:**
1. The shelf is next to the door.
2. This is the nicest shelf.
3. Two books lie on the bench.
4. Reading here is the best.
5. The bench is under the window.
6. The hallway is too long.

Pairs do not sort with tokens: one child reads a card and the other puts a pencil tip on its proof in the picture; a card with no proof is turned face down. Then they say the way from the door to the shelf using only the cards left face up. The tokens are shown by the teacher and used on the exit.

## Map caption for the worked example

A small map with a rose and a barn and a pond, and a caption: The pond is east of the barn. This is the prettiest pond. The teacher checks the first statement with the rose and says the second is what someone thinks.

## For the teacher

Pip says "It is printed, so it is true"; beside it, "We can check some printed things; 'prettiest' is what someone thinks." Pip says "The caption says best, so I cannot use any of it"; beside it, "Check the path and the door, and leave the opinion."
