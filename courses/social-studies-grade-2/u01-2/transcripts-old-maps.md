# Two old maps and the words printed on them (days 6 and 8)

Each map is shown large with its margin enlarged, and the teacher reads the transcript aloud. Keep the words printed on the map apart from what a library says about it. The margins below were read from the full images on 2026-11-20.

## Old map 1: Boulder, Colorado (day 6)

**Picture:** The whole 1904 map, brown paper with contour lines and a river, and the enlarged margins.

**Printed in the top margin:** "U.S. Geological Survey, Charles D. Walcott, Director." "Topography." "Colorado (Boulder County), Boulder Quadrangle."

**Printed in the bottom margin:** "Engraved April 1904 by U.S.G.S." "E. M. Douglas, Geographer in charge." "Triangulation and topography by Frank Tweedy." "Surveyed in 1902." "Edition of June 1904."

**Not part of the map:** a barcode and a label that says MAPS PCL, and handwriting that says COLO. 9 BOULDER Cop. 2, which are the library's own marks. The library keeps the map; it did not make it.

**Answers for the card:** Who made it? The U.S. Geological Survey. When? 1904, engraved April 1904. Where was it made? The record does not say; the margin does not print a place of making. What does it show? Boulder, Colorado.

**The bridge, "What is this map for?":** to show the land, its hills and its river, and where the roads and houses are.

## Old map 2: Pickaway County, Ohio (day 8)

**Picture:** The whole 1858 map, enlarged on the title block and the lower margin. It shows many small drawings of buildings and a table of numbers around its edge.

**Printed in the title block:** "Map of Pickaway County, Ohio. From surveys and county records. Kellogg and Randall, Publishers, 517, 519 and 521 Minor St., Philadelphia, 1858."

**Printed in the lower margin:** "Entered according to Act of Congress in the year 1858 by R. P. Smith in the clerk's office of the District Court of the eastern district of Pennsylvania."

**Answers for the card:** Who made it? Kellogg and Randall, the publishers (children copy this as printed). When? 1858. Where was it made? Philadelphia. What does it show? Pickaway County, Ohio. A child who writes Ohio for where it was made has named the place shown (error J).

## For the teacher

Day 8 has no printed key on this map, so children read it for its title and for the symbols it labels itself. Children copy the maker's name as printed, or write "not printed". Never read aloud what a library or seller says about the map as if it were printed on it.
