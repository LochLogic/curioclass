# Day 44 packet: Cedar Inlet (kept back until day 44)

Read the whole packet aloud once, neutrally, and no more. One copy of each card for each child, laid flat, one pencil each. Do not teach, hint or confirm. No answer map is visible. Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. No answer is discussed.

## Card 1: Inlet Map

**Picture:** A map with a key and a compass rose. A bay on the south side with a curved shore that shelters it, homes along the shore, boats tied along a landing, a forest on the hills to the north, a boatyard beside the landing, a new pier reaching out from the landing and a patch of marsh grass with birds beside it, partly covered by the pier. A line in the water marked high and low.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet is made up for practice. Boats land along its shore. Boat crews change their travel times when the water rises and falls. A new pier reaches out from the landing.

## Card 2: Work Card

**Picture:** A boatyard. A stack of timber, a saw, two workers at a half-built boat, a pond with young fish, a stove with a pile of coal, a lump of clay and a bale of wool on a shelf.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet is made up for practice. A forest covers the hills behind the yard. The yard crew shapes timber to make wooden boats. The yard keeps a pond where young fish are raised each year and caught. Coal is burned in the yard stove.

## Card 3: Connections Map

**Picture:** A map with two places. Cedar Inlet on the coast with a boat. A farming community inland with sheep, joined by a long arrow. Labels: boats, boat repair, wool blankets, animal health advice. A telephone drawn along the arrow.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet makes boats and repairs boats. The farming community makes wool blankets. An animal health worker there gives advice by telephone to animal keepers at Cedar Inlet.

## Card 4: Community B

**Picture:** Four small cards, each with a map, a key and two arrows into the community: a short arrow for wood from nearby and a long arrow for shoes from far away.
**Quill City:** tall homes close together, a bus stop. **Birch Lane:** houses with yards. **Ford Town:** shops and homes mixed on one street. **Sedge Farm:** fields and a barn.

## Card 5: Learning B

**Picture:** Three pictures in order. A potter at a table spins a lump of clay by hand on a flat board, with a thought bubble showing the lump turning and becoming a round bowl shape. The potter builds a wheel stand with shaping tools laid beside it. A shelf full of bowls.
**Caption, read aloud:** A potter at Cedar Inlet, in three steps: first, next, now.

## The items, read aloud, one ask at a time

**Items 1 and 2 (5 minutes).** On the Inlet Map: "Say why people settled by this bay." "Say how boat crews adapt their travel." "Say what the new pier changed for the boats." "Say what the new pier changed for the marsh." Paper: "At Cedar Inlet, did people adapt, change the land, or both? Circle one."
**Item 3 (3 minutes).** On Community B: "On the farm card, say one thing the farm gets from far away." "On the city card, say one thing the city gets from nearby." "Say one way the land is used in the city that is not how the farm uses its land."
**Item 4.** Not asked. Scored from the revised explanation of Recovery Card 2 kept from day 35 or 36.
**Item 5 (5 minutes).** On the Work Card: "Circle the natural resource the boats are made from: trees, clay or wool." "Say a job in the boatbuilding industry." "Say one job near the forest that is not building boats." "Circle the one that can be made again during our lives: pond fish or coal." "Say why the one you circled can be made again, or why the other cannot." "Say how the forest connects to the boatbuilding." "On the Inlet Map, say how the sheltered bay connects to the boatbuilding."
**Item 6 (5 minutes).** On the Connections Map: "Draw a box round the good Cedar Inlet makes." "Draw a circle round the service Cedar Inlet offers." "Draw a box round the good the farming community makes." "Draw a circle round the service the farming community gives." "A shop in Cedar Inlet sells wool blankets. Say where the blankets were made." "Say one thing the boat builders pay for to make a boat."
**Item 7 (3 minutes).** On Learning B: "Circle the picture that shows what the potter learned." "Circle one picture that shows equipment." "Say what the potter invented because of what the potter learned." "Say what the wheel stand helped the potter make."
**Item 8 (4 minutes).** Say to a partner for a minute, then record: "Should people at Cedar Inlet change the land, adapt, or do both? Circle one." "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." "Say who made the card you named."
**Sitting.** Each child gives a spoken reason on one of items 1, 3 and 5, by thirds. No aid but the packet and the frame strip.
