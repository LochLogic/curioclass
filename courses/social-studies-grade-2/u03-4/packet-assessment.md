# Day 44 packet: Cedar Inlet (kept back until day 44)

Read the whole packet aloud once, neutrally, and no more. One copy of each card for each child, laid flat, one pencil each. Do not teach, hint or confirm. No answer map is visible. Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. No answer is discussed.

## Card 1: Inlet Map

**Picture:** A map with a key and a compass rose. A bay on the south side with a curved shore that shelters it, homes along the shore, boats tied along a landing, a forest on the hills to the north, a boatyard beside the landing, a new pier reaching out from the landing and a patch of marsh grass with birds beside it, partly covered by the pier. A line in the water marked high and low.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet is made up for practice. Boats land along its shore. The water line in the bay goes up and down. A new pier reaches out from the landing.

## Card 2: Work Card

**Picture:** A boatyard. A stack of timber, a saw, two workers at a half-built boat, a pond with young fish, a stove with a pile of coal, a lump of clay and a bale of wool on a shelf.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet is made up for practice. A forest covers the hills behind the yard. Workers in the yard build boats. The yard keeps a pond with young fish. Coal is burned in the yard stove.

## Card 3: Connections Map

**Picture:** A map with two places. Cedar Inlet on the coast with a boat. A farming community inland with sheep, joined by a long arrow. Labels: boats, boat repair, wool blankets, animal health advice. A telephone drawn along the arrow.
**Caption, read aloud:** Cedar Inlet is on the coast. A farming community is inland. A telephone is drawn along the long arrow.

## Card 4: Community B

**Picture:** Four small cards, each with a map, a key and two arrows into the community: a short arrow for wood from nearby and a long arrow for shoes from far away.
**Quill City:** tall homes close together, a bus stop. **Birch Lane:** houses with yards. **Ford Town:** shops and homes mixed on one street. **Sedge Farm:** fields and a barn.

## Card 5: Learning B

**Picture:** Three pictures in order. A potter at a table spins a lump of clay by hand on a flat board, with a thought bubble showing the lump turning and becoming a round bowl shape. The potter builds a wheel stand with shaping tools laid beside it. A shelf full of bowls.
**Caption, read aloud:** A potter at Cedar Inlet, in three steps: first, next, now.
