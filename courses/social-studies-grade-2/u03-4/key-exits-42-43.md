# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 42 and 43

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-42-43.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 43: a claim that picks change, adapt or both with a because from the steep or muddy path and what the Hill Path card shows; case 2 adapt; case 3 both; adapt in case 3: they skip muddy days (digging the ditch is the change).
