# Day 44: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Say: answer alone, pass any item, no one helps, and a scribe is ready to write for any child who asks; every place on the cards is invented for practice. Hand back day 43's exit tickets with no comment: nothing is repaired today. Hand out packets, pages and pencils, and hide any answer map. The frame strip stays in its folder and is the one aid. Key: key-assessment.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Read the packet

Read each of the five cards' captions aloud once, neutrally. Discuss no answer.

#### 5 min. Items 1 and 2

Read each ask once on the Inlet Map: [[s03-E1]] [[s03-E2]] [[s03-E3]] [[s03-E4]] and, for the circle, [[s03-E5]]. Everyone writes or draws a few words on the response page.

#### 3 min. Item 3

On Community B: [[s03-E6]] [[s03-E7]] [[s03-E8]]. Everyone writes or draws a few words.

#### 6 min. Item 5

On the Work Card and Inlet Map: [[s03-E12]] [[s03-E13]] [[s03-E14]] [[s03-E15]] [[s03-E16]] [[s03-E17]] [[s03-E18]]. Everyone writes or draws a few words.

#### 5 min. Item 6

On the Connections Map, with pencils on the card: [[s03-E19]] [[s03-E20]] [[s03-E21]] [[s03-E22]]. Then on the page: [[s03-E23]] [[s03-E24]].

#### 4 min. Item 7

On Learning B: circle for [[s03-E25]] and [[s03-E26]]; write or draw for [[s03-E27]] and [[s03-E28]].

#### 4 min. Item 8

Circle for [[s03-E29]]. Then each child writes the frame for [[s03-E30]], alone. Then [[s03-E31]].

#### 3 min. Collect

Helpers collect packets and pages; a scribe finishes writing for any child who asked. A child still writing finishes quietly. After class, mark each of the eleven codes shown, shown with help or not yet seen on the record sheet, never averaged. Item 4 is not asked: score [[s03-E9]], [[s03-E10]] and [[s03-E11]] from the revised Recovery Card 2 explanation of day 35 or 36, looking for an effect on a person's day, one help and the card named. Sort errors into four groups for day 45: items 1 and 2 (errors A, B and P); items 3 and 4 (errors C, D and E); items 5, 6 and 7 (errors F to J, L to O); item 8 (error K).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a small bay with boats, a forest and a pier.

**Skip.** The scribe.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read the questions once; give no help.
  **Say.** [[s03-E30]] Expect: a choice, a named card and what it shows.
- **15 min. Talk.** Talk about which part felt hardest.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one question from each item. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: adapt, change the land, both, source, maker, resource, renewable. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 45 hands back day 44 in four groups: items 1 and 2 (errors A, B and P); items 3 and 4 (errors C, D and E); items 5, 6 and 7 (errors F to J, L to O); item 8 (error K). Each group meets you for 2 to 3 minutes, then tries a fresh card.
