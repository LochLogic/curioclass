# Day 46: Show what you know so far

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hand back day 45's exit tickets and written task, marked. Open day 45's record cells and the record sheet: one row a child, a column for each item 1 to 15, a heard column. Cover every model; keep the packet closed until now. Name four groups of six from children not yet heard: group 1 the scale, group 2 why oceans matter, group 3 who made the map, group 4 how to live with the land. Never ask where anyone lives. Key: key-packet-check-1-day-46.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say: "Today I find out what you remember from this half of the year. I will not help you choose." Say: "These cards are made up." Hand out the packet, the student page and pencils. Any child may pass. A scribe writes a child's own words.

#### 9 min. Item 1

Show the Fennick Plot Map and read it aloud once. Read each ask once: [[sc1-E1]] [[sc1-E2]] [[sc1-E3]] [[sc1-E5]] Everyone writes or draws a few words for each on the student page. Group 1 comes to you for [[sc1-E4]], one ask each, 45 seconds and 5 to swap, 5 minutes; the rest keep writing.

#### 8 min. Item 2

On World Map C, each child writes the letter or number beside each word in the bank: [[sc1-E6]] [[sc1-E7]] Then everyone writes a few words for [[sc1-E8]] [[sc1-E9]]. Group 2 comes to you for [[sc1-E10]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, 5 minutes.

#### 5 min. Item 3

Look at the corner note on the Fennick Plot Map. Everyone writes a few words for [[sc1-E12]]. Group 3 comes to you for [[sc1-E11]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes with moving.

#### 8 min. Item 4

Show the Dune Gate card and read its caption. Each child writes the frame on the student page: [[sc1-E13]] [[sc1-E15]] Group 4 comes to you for [[sc1-E14]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, 5 minutes.

#### 3 min. Hand in

Collect every packet and page. Four groups hear 24 children, one spoken ask each at 45 seconds plus 5 to swap, 20 of the 35 minutes. A child past 24 is marked on paper only and is heard first on day 47, Fresh task. Items 6, 7, 13 and 15 are scored from the work handed in. After class mark each item shown, shown with help or not yet seen, never averaged. A part neither marked nor heard is not yet seen. Sort errors into four groups for day 47: items 1 to 5 (errors A to E); items 6 to 10 (errors F, G and H); items 11 and 12 (errors I and J); items 13 to 15 (errors K, L and M).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a small map of a garden with a pond, a title, a key and a scale on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard column.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say the map and the place are made up. Your child tells what a map's title, key, compass rose and scale tell, places the continents and oceans, says who made a map and when, and says where they would choose to live with a reason that names a card. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What does the scale tell you? What makes the ocean matter? Who made this map?" Expect: how far one length stands for; it holds most of the water or makes rain; the name in the corner note.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child picks one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1 and 4 first; the rest wait for day 47. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: title, key, compass rose, scale, continent, ocean, maker, source. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe count.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 hands back day 46 in four groups: items 1 to 5 (errors A to E, most often scale); items 6 to 10 (errors F, G and H); items 11 and 12 (errors I and J); items 13 to 15 (K, L, M, most often the source named). Each group meets you for 2 to 3 minutes, then tries a fresh card.
