# Section 3.4 opener: looking back, choosing with a source, and showing what we know

## What the section does

Four days, 42 to 45, of 35, 36, 35 and 32 minutes. Day 42 looks back on the class's action: the Response Box is read, the Action Record is filled with what we did and what people said, and a made-up class's record (Room 2) is read beside ours. No sentence says the poster prevented flooding, and a missing answer is a record too. Day 43 answers the unit's question, should people change the land, or change their ways to fit it, with a claim that names its source, a good thing and a hard thing. Day 44 is the end-of-unit assessment on a held-back packet about Cedar Inlet, a place made up for practice. Day 45 responds: small groups meet the teacher on what the sitting showed, then try a fresh task.

## How the days run

Day 42: a repair, the Response Box, the Action Record, a model, supported work with a small group, the question board, the exit. Day 43: a repair, a retrieval, a claim with a named source, saying it, recording it, another choice, the exit. Day 44: the packet read aloud once, then eight sets of items, then collect. Day 45: the question board read, teaching by group, a fresh task, feedback and a self-check strip.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Looking back is not scoring the class.** An action that got no answer is still a record.
- **A claim is a choice among change, adapt or both with a reason.** Every supported choice meets. A tradeoff is one good and one hard thing that come together.
- **A source is named by its maker.** Curio Class made each card; the card does not say more.
- **Day 44 is a check:** nothing is taught and no repair is made on it. Marking is shown, shown with help or not yet seen, by part, never averaged.
- **No child is asked what their family does.**

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once: we voted, so our poster worked (E, day 42); the tray guide knows how to bake (J, day 41, traced in a small group on day 42); I choose it because I say so (K, day 43); the walkers who skip muddy days changed nothing, so it is only adapting (A, day 43). Every letter of the unit is answered on day 45 by group.

## Supplies for the section

The Response Box, the Action Record, the Room 2 record, a fresh three-box strip, the frame strip, the Terraced Field and Stilt Bay cards, the exit cards, the day 44 packet (locked until the day), a written move for each group, the fresh task cards for day 45, a self-check strip for each child, the record sheet and the question board.
