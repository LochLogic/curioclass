# Day 42: What did our action do?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the next six on the record sheet. Fetch the Response Box from the receiving class's teacher. Have the Action Record, Room 2's card (picture covered) and a fresh three-box strip ready. Keep the Room 6 exit card face down. Key: key-exits-42-43.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 41's exit tickets and the gardener strips (day 41's written task). Looking back is not scoring the class. **Ask.** "Say in order what our class did to share its message: first, next, last." ([[s03-P42-1]]) Expect: choose, share, leave the Response Box.

#### 4 min. The Response Box

Open the box. Read each slip aloud; tally: I read it, I heard it, a few words. **Ask.** "Say how many slips came back, or that none came back." ([[s03-P42-2]]) Expect: our count. If none came back, the record says so. A missing answer is a record too.

#### 8 min. Our Action Record

Fill the record on the board. **Ask.** "Say what we made or said, and where." ([[s03-P42-3]]) **Ask.** "Say what the slips tell us about who read our message." ([[s03-P42-4]]) Expect: only what they show. Pip says: "We voted, so our message worked." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F42-1]], error E) Expect: no; we voted to choose, and the slips tell us whether people read it. No line says the message kept anyone safe from a flood. **Ask.** "Say one thing we would change next time." ([[s03-P42-5]]) Write it on the record.

#### 5 min. A model

Read only the caption of Room 2's card, a made-up class: "Room 2 voted and chose a message. They hung it on the hall wall. No slips came back." **Ask.** "Room 2 voted, hung its message, and no slips came back. Say one thing Room 2 can say." ([[s03-P42-6]]) **Ask.** "Say one thing Room 2 cannot say." ([[s03-P42-7]]) Expect: that people read it.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Call today's six one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving. Hand each the Room 6 card to circle for [[s03-X42]], then ask [[s03-X42b]]. Then the small group, up to six, 5 minutes: children who said a tool holds the knowing (error J). Review: you learned human resource and learned skill on day 41. Tell one fresh case in three steps: Mina, an invented child, keeps hens. She learned that hens lay in a quiet corner, made a nest box with a lid, and gathered more whole eggs. The group places each step in a box on the strip ([[s03-P42-9]]). The rest, in pairs, say one change to our message and why ([[s03-P42-8]]), then draw it.

#### 3 min. Question board

**Ask.** "Say what our action did, in your own words." ([[s03-P42-10]])

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the Room 6 card. Everyone but today's six circles for [[s03-X42]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the three rows on the board and use word cards for slips.

### At home

**Swap.** No record: say what the family would change about any message.

**Skip.** The strip.

- **10 min. First, next, last.** Say what the class did to share its message.
  **Say.** [[s03-P42-1]] Expect: choose, share, leave the box.
- **15 min. What we would change.** [[s03-P42-8]] Your child draws it.
  **Say.** "What would you change, and why?" Expect: a change tied to the record.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X42]] [[s03-X42b]] Your child circles one picture and answers one question aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only our record and the exit. Longer: a child draws a second change.

### Words and supports

Words: record, slip, answered, change, voted. Frame: "We did ___. People answered ___. Next time we would ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A child who circled the vote cup or the wall, or said we voted so it worked (error E), starts day 43 with three pictures sorted. If more than a third made error J on day 41, day 43 repeats the three-box strip with everyone. Day 43 answers the unit's question with a written claim and a named source.
