# Room 2's Action Record, day 42

Invented for practice: Room 2 is a made-up class. Plain black line drawing on white. It is read aloud beside our own record.

**Picture:** A three-row record. Row 1, we chose: a cup with counters in it. Row 2, we did: a message hung on a hall wall. Row 3, people answered: an empty box with a label that reads slips, none.
**Caption, read aloud:** Room 2 voted and chose a message. They hung it on the hall wall. No slips came back.
**Ask:** "What can Room 2 say? What can it not say?"

## Key (teacher)

Room 2 can say it voted and hung its message. It cannot say people read it, since no slips came back. A missing answer is a record too. Pip says: "We voted, so our poster worked." The right idea is said beside it.
