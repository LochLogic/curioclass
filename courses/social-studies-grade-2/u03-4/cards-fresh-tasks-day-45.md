# Fresh task cards, day 45

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Never from the assessment.

## Sorting cards (group 1, change, adapt or both)

**Picture 1:** A bridge built across a creek. Change the land.
**Picture 2:** Villagers wearing snowshoes in deep snow. Adapt.
**Picture 3:** A town cuts a canal for boats and also ties its boats up for the winter freeze. Both.

## Stony Lake Card

**Picture:** A flat shore. Fishing boats pulled up on the sand. A pretty rainbow over the lake. A stone wall built along the water, with a dry road behind it and waves at the front.
**Caption, read aloud:** Stony Lake is a town made up for practice. The town built a stone wall along the water.
**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows a reason people settled at Stony Lake. **Spoken:** "What did the stone wall change?"

## Meadow Farm Card and Room 9's record

**Picture:** A map with a key. Meadow Farm near the middle. A short arrow takes milk jugs to the town. A long arrow brings seed bags from far away.
**Room 9's Action Record, picture:** A cup with counters in it. A message hung on a wall. A box labeled slips with one slip in it that reads I read it.
**Point:** Point to the arrow that shows something the farm gets from far away. **Spoken:** "Room 9's record shows a vote, a message hung up and one slip that says I read it. What can Room 9 say about its message?"

## Cotton Town Card

**Picture:** A field of cotton planted each year. A weaver at a loom. A thought bubble: knows how threads cross. A pile of coal beside the mill stove.
**Point:** Point to the one that can be grown again each year. Point to what the weaver learned.

## Valley Road Card

**Picture:** A road beside a river that floods each spring, a market at the end of the road, water over the road in one picture.
**Three claims:**
1. I think people at Valley Road should build a wall because I like walls.
2. I think people at Valley Road should move market day to summer because the river floods in spring. The Valley Road Card shows the river over the road, made by Curio Class.
3. Everyone knows what to do.
**Point:** Point to the claim that names its source.

## Key (teacher)

Sorting: change; adapt; both. Stony Lake: the shore with the boats; the waves stop at the wall and the road behind stays dry. Meadow Farm: the seed bag arrow; Room 9 hung its message and at least one person read it, but it cannot say everyone did. Cotton Town: the cotton; the thought bubble. Valley Road: the second claim. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
