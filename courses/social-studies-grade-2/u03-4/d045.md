# Day 45: Try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 44 into four groups from the record sheet, by the part with the largest gap, at most about eight children each: items 1 and 2 (errors A, B and P); items 3 and 4 (errors C, D and E); items 5, 6 and 7 (errors F to J, L to O); item 8 (error K). Lay out each group's cards. In groups 1 and 2, hear the children least heard first. Nobody's page is held up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opener

Read aloud the question board's class answer. Hand back day 44's assessment with its marks, shown, shown with help or not yet seen, by group, and the written task from day 43. Name the four groups.

#### 10 min. Teaching by group

Meet each group 2 to 3 minutes, the largest gap first; the others work in pairs from the written move ([[s03-P45-4]]). Group 1 (errors A, B and P): on the Road Card from day 31, **Ask.** "Say what the road changed for the hill." ([[s03-P45-1]]) Expect: a slice was cut away. Group 2 (errors C, D and E): trace the two arrows on the Hill Farm Card, then **Ask.** "Say what a closed road would do to the people who use it." ([[s03-P45-2]]) Expect: they are cut off. Group 3 (errors F to J, L to O): Pip says: "Everyone in this town is a fisher." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F45-1]]) Expect: no; a fisher and a baker are two jobs. Group 4 (error K): each child takes the day 43 strip from the folder and says the card and its maker ([[s03-P45-3]]). Expect: Curio Class.

#### 10 min. Fresh task

Each group works its new card in pairs. Everyone in group 1 circles for [[s03-X45]]. You hear two children from each group, 45 seconds an ask with the swap: group 1, Stony Lake, [[s03-X45b]], 1.5 minutes; group 2, Meadow Farm and Room 9's record, [[s03-X45c]] then [[s03-X45d]], 3 minutes; group 3, Cotton Town, [[s03-X45e]] then [[s03-X45f]], 3 minutes; group 4, Valley Road, [[s03-X45g]], 1.5 minutes. Pointing counts. Mark them on the record sheet. A child not heard is marked not yet seen and recorded for day 47's repairs. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 7 min. Feedback and the self-check strip

Children mark one box in each row of the strip, then point to one piece of work that shows a mark ([[s03-P45-5]]), 4 minutes. Hear [[s03-X45h]] from three children, 45 seconds each, about 2 minutes; the rest are recorded not yet seen. While they work, write one line of feedback on each fresh task, about 10 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Question board

**Ask.** "Say how you would answer the question board now." ([[s03-P45-6]]) Expect: a choice with a reason. Revise the board in the children's words.

### Other rooms

No printed cards: draw each card on the board and use lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No fresh cards: reuse one day 44 question.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at day 44 together; your child picks one part to try again.
  **Say.** [[s03-P45-6]] Expect: a choice with a reason.
- **15 min. Try again.** Your child tries that part again from the written move.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X45]] [[s03-X45b]] [[s03-X45c]] [[s03-X45d]] [[s03-X45e]] [[s03-X45f]] [[s03-X45g]] [[s03-X45h]] Your child tries the card they were given, says the answer aloud and says one thing they changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fresh card for each group. Longer: a child defends a second choice from the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: try again, change, adapt, both, source, maker, renewable. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 is the course check, on maps and places from all three units. Anything still not shown on today's cards is recorded for days 46 and 47.
