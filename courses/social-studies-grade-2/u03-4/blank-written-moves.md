# Written move for each group, day 45

Each group has one strip. The children work in pairs from the strip while the teacher meets other groups.

**Group 1 (A, B, P):** Look at the Road Card from day 31. Say a reason people could settle by it. Say what the road changed for travel and for the hill. Sort three new cards under change, adapt and both.
**Group 2 (C, D, E):** Trace the two arrows on the Hill Farm Card from day 32. Say who a closed road affects. On Room 2's record, point to what the class can say and what it cannot.
**Group 3 (F to J, L to O):** Compare the fisher and the baker in one town. Sort six resource cards into can grow again and cannot. Say what Ana learned and what she made.
**Group 4 (K):** Rebuild a claim from the card. Name the card and who made it.

Secure children defend another choice with the same source.
