# Day 43: Change the land or fit it?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the next six on the record sheet. Put up the Terraced Field and Stilt Bay cards, the Snowy Pass card and Changing Land Card 5. Lay out the three repair pictures and a folder for each child. Keep the Hill Path card face down. Key: key-exits-42-43.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 42's exit tickets and the three-box strips (day 42's written task). If many circled the vote cup or the wall (error E), put the three pictures under we chose, we did and people answered ([[s03-P43-1]]). If more than a third made error J on day 41, spend the time on a three-box strip for everyone instead.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: a choice with "because", from unit 2. **Ask.** "Give a reason for choosing a raincoat on a wet day." ([[s03-P43-2]]) Expect: it keeps me dry.

#### 8 min. A claim with a named source

Review: the unit 2 frame with a named source. Show the Terraced Field. **Ask.** "Who made the Terraced Field drawing?" ([[s03-P43-3]]) Expect: Curio Class. Model: "I think people at the Terraced Field should change the land because the hill is steep. The Terraced Field drawing shows level strips to plant, made by Curio Class. One good thing is level places to plant; one hard thing is that each strip is narrow." Pip says: "I choose it because I say so." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F43-1]], error K) Expect: no; a reason names what a card shows. Show Card 5, the raised house. Pip says: "The raised house only adapts, because the people did not change the land." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F43-2]], error A) Expect: no; both.

#### 6 min. Say it

Each child picks an open place: the Terraced Field, Stilt Bay or Snowy Pass. Partners say the frame, 3 minutes each way, and listen for a choice, a named source and a hard thing. Each says: a choice ([[s03-P43-4]]); why, from the card ([[s03-P43-5]]); the card and its maker ([[s03-P43-6]]); one good thing ([[s03-P43-7]]); one hard thing ([[s03-P43-8]]).

#### 9 min. Record it

Each child writes the claim on the frame strip ([[s03-P43-9]]); a partner may write a child's own words. Meanwhile call today's six, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving: each says change, adapt or both for [[s03-X43b]] and [[s03-X43c]] and answers [[s03-X43d]]. Each child keeps the strip in a folder.

#### 1 min. Another choice

One pair says, "Another good choice is ___ because ___." ([[s03-P43-10]])

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the Hill Path card. Everyone writes the claim, [[s03-X43]]; an adult may scribe. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A because with no card is error K.

### Other rooms

No printer: put the three places on the board in words and sketches.

### At home

**Swap.** No drawing: draw a house on posts and use it as the card.

**Skip.** The exit.

- **25 min. Say it, then write it.** Pick a place; say a choice, a reason, the card and its maker, one good thing and one hard thing; then your child fills the strip.
  **Say.** [[s03-P43-9]] Expect: all four parts.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X43]] [[s03-X43b]] [[s03-X43c]] Your child finishes the Hill Path claim, then answers [[s03-X43b]], [[s03-X43c]] and [[s03-X43d]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one place and the strip. Longer: a second choice from the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: change the land, adapt, both, source, good thing, hard thing. Frame: "I think people at ___ should ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___. One good thing is ___; one hard thing is ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 is the unit check on a packet not seen before: nothing is taught or repaired there, so what today's exit shows waits for day 45. Bring the strip in its folder; it is the one aid.
