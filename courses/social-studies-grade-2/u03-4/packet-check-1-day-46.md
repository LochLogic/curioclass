# Check 1 packet: a map, the world and a place (kept back until day 46)

Nothing here is taught before day 46. Read each card aloud once, neutrally, and no more. One copy of each card for each child, laid flat, one pencil each. Do not teach, hint or confirm. Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Every completed model in the room is covered.

## Card 1: Fennick Plot Map

**Picture:** A map of a village plot seen from above. The top of the card is the top of the map. Title across the top: Fennick Plot, a village garden and pond. A compass rose at the top right with N at its top. A key box: a row of short dashes means bean rows; a wavy line means the pond; a small roof means the shed; a ring means the well. A scale bar: one bar length is six paces. The pond is at the left. The well is in the middle, exactly three bar lengths from the pond, with bean rows between them. The shed is to the right of the well. A corner note in small print: Drawn by Curio Class for practice, spring 2026.

## Card 2: World Map C and word bank (a sheet each child writes on)

**Picture:** A fresh outline of the world, drawn new for this check, with seven land shapes marked with letters G, H, J, K, L, M and N, and six water areas marked with numbers 7, 8, 9, 10, 11 and 12. A word bank of twelve words under the map: South America, Asia, North America, Europe, Australia, Africa, Antarctica, Pacific, Arctic, Atlantic, Indian, Southern. A line beside each word to write a letter or number.

## Card 3: Dune Gate card

**Picture:** A small town of low houses with thick walls and small windows. A shade cloth stretched on poles over a market table. A well with water jugs beside it. A few palm trees. A path. **Caption, read aloud:** Dune Gate is a town made up for practice, in a desert region. Made by Curio Class.

## Check 1 record sheet

Names down the side; a column for each item 1 to 15; a heard column. Use the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Four groups of six, each heard on one spoken ask: group 1 the scale, group 2 why oceans matter, group 3 who made the map, group 4 how to live with the land. A part neither marked on paper nor heard is not yet seen and goes to day 47.
