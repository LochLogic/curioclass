# Day 47: Try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 46 into four groups from the check 1 record sheet, by the part with the largest gap, at most about eight children each: items 1 to 5 (errors A to E); items 6 to 10 (errors F, G and H); items 11 and 12 (errors I and J); items 13 to 15 (errors K, L and M). Lay out each group's cards. Say aloud that every card is made up. Today's six: the children least heard. No one is held up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Hand back day 46's assessment: the packets and pages with their marks, shown, shown with help or not yet seen, by group. Name the four groups by their cards. Nobody is named for a miss.

#### 10 min. Teaching by group

Meet each group 2 to 3 minutes, the largest gap first; the others work in pairs from the written move. Group 1 (errors A to E): on the Bench Row Map, trace a mark to the key, then **Ask.** [[sc1-P47-1]] Expect: eight. Group 2 (errors F, G and H): sort the six name cards, then Pip says: "The Atlantic is a continent." **Ask.** [[sc1-P47-2]] Expect: it is an ocean, water. Group 3 (errors I and J): on the corner note, **Ask.** [[sc1-P47-3]] Expect: Curio Class, winter 2026. Group 4 (errors K, L and M): Pip says: "I choose Mossy Row because it is nice." **Ask.** [[sc1-P47-4]] Expect: a picture the card shows and the card named. Each pair then does its written move strip ([[sc1-P47-5]]).

#### 10 min. Fresh task

Group 1 works the Rook Lane Map and group 4 the Mossy Row Card in pairs; everyone circles for [[sc1-X47]] and [[sc1-X47c]]. First hear any child not heard on day 46, their own ask. You hear six of group 1 for [[sc1-X47b]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 4.5 minutes. Then you hear today's six for [[sc1-X47d]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 4.5 minutes; groups 2 and 3 work their pair cards meanwhile. Mark each on the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 7 min. Feedback and the self-check strip

Children mark one box in each row, then point to one piece of work that shows a mark ([[sc1-P47-6]]), 4 minutes. While they work, write one line of feedback on each fresh task, about 10 seconds each. The strips and fresh tasks stay with the students in their folders.

#### 2 min. Close

Say what changed. Nothing from today is handed on except what the record sheet holds.

### Other rooms

No printed cards: draw each card on the board and use lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw a map with a bar and a number of paces on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at day 46 together; your child picks one part to try again.
  **Say.** "What does the claim lack?" Expect: a picture the card shows and the card named.
- **15 min. Try again.** Your child tries that part again from the written move.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc1-X47]] [[sc1-X47b]] [[sc1-X47c]] [[sc1-X47d]] Your child tries the card they were given, answers aloud and says one thing they changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fresh card for each group. Longer: a child defends a second choice on the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: try again, change, scale, bar length, maker, source, claim. Frame: "I choose ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 opens unit 4: a physical map and a political map of one place side by side. Anything still not shown on today's cards stays on the record sheet.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
