# Exit cards, days 42 and 43

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print only on the exit of their day.

## Day 42 exit card: Room 6's Action Record

**Picture:** Three pictures. A cup with counters in it. A message hung on a wall. A pile of slips that read I read it.
**Mark:** Circle the picture that shows people read it.
**Spoken:** "What would you change next time, and why?"

## Day 43 exit card: Hill Path cases

**Picture:** Three small panels.
**Case 1:** Logs are set into a steep path to make steps.
**Case 2:** Walkers move their trips to the dry months.
**Case 3:** A ditch is dug beside the path and walkers also skip muddy days.
**Mark:** Under each, circle change, adapt or both.
**Spoken:** "In case 3, what do the walkers do to adapt?"
