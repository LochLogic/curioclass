# Exit cards, days 76 to 78

Every invented card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six, at about 45 seconds each. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 76 exit cards

**Card 1 (paper):** Memory: "I came in 2013. The bike lane opened in 2019. I do not know when the skate park opened." A strip with 2013, 2019 and a box marked "I do not know when", and three cards: arrival, bike lane, skate park. **Mark:** Put the skate park card where it belongs.
**Card 2 (spoken):** Memory: "I opened my shop in 2021. The clock was fixed in 2017." Two cards, shop and clock. **Ask:** Say which card comes first on the strip.
**Card 3 (spoken):** Memory: "I started at the school in 2020. The pie contest began in 2023." **Ask:** Say one thing this memory cannot tell us.
**Card 4 (spoken, heard from the other three of the six):** "Lio saw a banner fall from the roof of the hall. Lio told Kay, and Kay wrote a note about it. Another note copies Kay's note." **Ask:** Say how many people saw the banner fall. Key: one, Lio.

## Day 77 exit cards

**Card (paper):** "A cook moves by bus to a new town. In her bag she packs a teapot, a rug and a rolled map. At the library she tells the neighbors a folktale from her old town. A neighbor shows her a family cooking custom." Four pictures: the teapot, the rug, the rolled map, the folktale told aloud. **Mark:** Circle the one thing the cook brought that was not in her bag.
**Spoken 1:** "The cook told the neighbors a folktale from her old town. How did the folktale get to the new town?"
**Spoken 2:** "The cook tells the neighbors a folktale from her old town. A neighbor shows the cook a family cooking custom. Say one way the folktale and the cooking custom are alike."

## Day 78 exit cards (Eddyford, invented, and one real card)

**Card 1:** "A family that came this year started a night market for foods."
**Card 2:** "Families who have always lived in Eddyford keep the old orchard open to all."
**Card 3 (real, from the Seneca Nation of Indians' own pages):** "The Seneca Nation is a government whose constitution was established in 1848. Its language departments offer Seneca language classes." Credit line: "Seneca Nation of Indians, sni.org, home page and Seneca language page. Summary by Curio Class, not an exact quotation."
**Card 4:** "The town hall bell rings at noon."
**Mark:** Put a token on the card that shows what the Seneca Nation adds today.
**Spoken 1:** "Look again at the four cards. Say what the families who have always lived in Eddyford added to the town."
**Spoken 2:** "Look again at the four cards. Say one change to Eddyford because of the family that came this year."
