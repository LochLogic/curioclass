# Repair cards, days 77 and 78

Every card prints "invented for practice". One fresh card for the letter most missed. None is an exit case. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 77 repair (C, E, I or J)

- **C:** a note: "I planted the apple tree in the spring." No year. The class says "unknown" and places the card in the box.
- **E:** Wren's card (she saw the parade), a notice board (it copies Wren) and Dov's card (he saw the parade). Trace the copy back to its teller; name the second teller.
- **I:** a note: "I came in 2012." Say "The source says she came in 2012; it cannot tell how she felt."
- **J:** two dates: 2011 and 2015. Order them on a strip.

## Day 78 repair (A or K)

- **A:** four cards: a lamp, a recipe told aloud, a basket, a lullaby. Sort in a bag or in a person.
- **K:** two customs: a hat worn in sun and a scarf worn in snow. Say what is different and why neither is the best.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

C: unknown, the box. E: the notice board copies Wren; Dov is the second teller. I: as stated. J: 2011 first. A: the lamp and basket in a bag; the recipe told aloud and the lullaby in a person. K: any real difference, no winner.
