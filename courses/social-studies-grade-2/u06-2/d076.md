# Day 76: What a telling can and cannot tell

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have the visitor's approved account and class copy from day 74. Lay a dated strip and five event cards at each table; keep the gate card for your own model strip. Say Ines and Tavo are made up. Ask no child about home or family; any may pass. Key: key-exits-76-78.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

For 4 minutes, teach the letter most missed on s06-X75, s06-X75b and s06-X75c with a made-up card: error B, a teller says only "I came by boat" and the class names the topics left; error C, two notes, one with a detail never said, and the class underlines what was said; error D, a teller says "I moved for a school" and the class picks the follow-up about it. Then 1 minute: home sheets go in the folder; none, the school record. Day 75's pages (s06-P75-1, s06-P75-2, s06-X75c) go back on day 79 with a mark and cue.

#### 6 min. A telling as a source

Read the account and class copy aloud. A memory is what one person remembers and tells; review: a source, from grade 1. Ask its three questions (review, unit 1): who made it, when, where. Say: "The source says ___; it cannot tell ___." **Ask.** "Why did the visitor come?" Expect what it says, or "unknown". Pip says error I: "The account says the year the visitor came, so the visitor liked it here." Say beside it: "It says the year; it cannot tell how the visitor felt."

#### 5 min. One teller or two

Review: teller, own way of knowing and copy, from unit 5. Retell the visit beside the account. **Ask.** "Who knew it firsthand?" Expect: the visitor; your retelling and the class copy are copies. Then Ines and Tavo (made up) each watched a fence painted; a poster copies Ines. Pip says error E: "Two sheets say it, so it is true twice." Say beside it: "The poster copied Ines, so it is one teller. Tavo was there too: a second teller."

#### 9 min. The strip

Place the visitor's dated cards on a strip as a model, and the gate card, which has no year, in the box "I do not know when". Tables build strips from five event cards [[s06-P76-1]]. Pip says error J puts 2022 before 2010: "The smaller year comes first." Pip says error C: "No year, so I will say 2016." At your table hear the rotating six on [[s06-X76b]] and up to four not yet heard on a topic question, about 45 seconds each.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

Read [[s06-X76]] aloud once; each child places the card.

#### 5 min. One more ask

Hear the rotating six, 45 seconds each: three on [[s06-X76c]] and three on [[s06-X76d]]. The rest finish their strip page.

#### 3 min. Close

Add "What does a memory show or leave unknown?" to the board. Mark each strip for day 79.

### Other rooms

No strips printed: draw a line with year marks and a box beside it.

### At home

**Swap.** No strip: draw a line with year marks and a box.

**Skip.** The tables and the six.

- **10 min. A telling as a source.** Read the account the grown-up told. **Say.** "The account says ___; it cannot tell ___." Expect: it says when they came; it cannot tell how everyone felt.
- **15 min. The strip.** [[s06-P76-1]] **Say.** "Where does the card with no year go?" Expect: the box, I do not know when.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X76]] [[s06-X76b]] [[s06-X76c]] [[s06-X76d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four cards. Longer: what could check the visitor's memory?

### Words and supports

Words: memory, source, timeline, unknown. Frame: "The source says ___; it cannot tell ___."
