# Strip event cards, day 76 (invented)

Five cards for each table; every card prints "invented for practice". The table places them on its strip. The teacher also places the visitor's dated cards, and a teller's arrival year from the class records may be added with permission, names off. The teacher's own model strip also has the gate card, which has no year and goes in the "I do not know when" box; the tables never get it. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

| Card | Year |
|---|---|
| a new crosswalk painted | 2014 |
| a mailbox put up | 2010 |
| a tree planted by the school | 2012 |
| a new gate at the playground (teacher model only) | no year given |
| the playground opens | 2015 |
| the library adds a reading corner | 2022 |

**Pip says J, then C:** Pip puts 2022 before 2010. Beside it: "The smaller year comes first." Pip says "No year, so I will say 2016." Beside it: "No year given: the box."
**Key (teacher sheet only):** order 2010, 2012, 2014, 2015, 2022 on the table strips; the gate, in the teacher's model, in the box.
