# Ines and Tavo cards (invented, corroboration review of day 76)

Every card prints "invented for practice". The teacher says aloud that Ines, Tavo and Fig Street are made up. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

**Ines's card.** Maker: Ines. How she knows: she was there. "Ines says: I watched the neighbors paint the Fig Street fence blue on a Saturday."
**Tavo's card.** Maker: Tavo. How he knows: he was there. "Tavo says: I watched too. The fence was painted blue, and the paint was wet."
**Poster.** Maker: a poster on the lamppost. How it knows: it copies Ines. "Fig Street Fence Day. The fence was painted blue on a Saturday."
**Questions of each card:** who made it, when, where.
**Pip says E:** "Two sheets say it, so it is true twice." The teacher says beside it: "The poster copied Ines, so it is one teller. Tavo was there too: a second teller."
**Key (teacher sheet only):** Ines and Tavo each saw it; the poster is a copy.
