# Town picture and town cards, day 78

The class's own town is the community. Pairs place a token beside each line that shows what a group added; a card with no line gets no token. The newcomers' and long-time residents' lines come from the day 75 interview records the teller allowed to share, names off, not read before. The nation's line comes from the nation's own page (teacher-supplied; see Language Today). If the class has too few records, the standby cards below stand in, said aloud to be invented. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

**Picture:** A town street with a library, a school, a playground and a bridge, and three labels above it: "newcomers", "long-time residents", "the nation".

## Standby cards (invented, "invented for practice")

- **Newcomers:** "A family that came in the spring teaches a bread-baking hour at the library."
- **Long-time residents:** "Families who have always lived in the town keep a Saturday swap table in the school yard."
- **The nation:** not invented. The teacher prints one present-tense line from the local nation's own page here. Never invent a nation's contribution.

## The explanation frame

"In our town, ___ added ___. ___ added ___. We could all ___ so that ___." The shared goal comes from the cards. Then, said to a partner: one change for the newcomers and one change for the place.

**Pip says M, then G:** "Only the new people have culture; the others just live here." Beside it: "Everyone's songs, words and ways are culture." Pip says "Everyone in the town does it the same way." Beside it: "Neighbors who share a town still differ."
**Meets (teacher sheet):** names a group that added; ties the addition to a card line; says one change to the newcomers and one to the place; says one shared goal.
