# Day 78: How a move changes people and a place

## Teacher page

### Before class

The exit and the unit assessment use other nations' own pages, never your local nation, and no item asks about the local page. Print the local nation's page with title and link; if none, read one sentence that the nation is a living government with its own language and schools, linked to its site. Choose two shareable day 75 lines, names off; too few, use made-up standby cards. Key: key-exits-76-78.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 77's sort (s06-P77-1). Teach error A or K, whichever more missed on day 77's exit (s06-X77, s06-X77b, s06-X77c): error A, sort a lamp, a recipe told aloud, a basket and a lullaby "in a bag" or "in a person"; error K, a sun hat and a snow scarf, no winner.

#### 6 min. Posadas again

Review: the Posadas Card from day 72 and the unit 5 chain strip. Read the card and its day 78 line: the National Endowment for the Arts says his arrival helped traditional Mexican music grow in California. Give the strip two "afterward" arrows. Review: newcomer (unit 5), change and cultural change (unit 2). **Ask.** "What changed for his gatherings, and what changed for California?" Expect: shorter, at homes and garages; Mexican music grew. **Ask.** [[s06-P78-2]] Expect: local laws limit all-night events. It is one man's telling; it cannot tell how he felt. Pip says error F: "Only the new family changes."

#### 6 min. Language Today

Read the Cherokee Nation card, a nation from unit 5: it governs itself today and runs the Cherokee Immersion School. Then read the local nation's own page, by its own name. Pip says error L: "The nation was here long ago." Say beside it: "The Nation's own page says it teaches its language today."

#### 7 min. Our town

Pairs get the town picture (newcomers, long-time residents, the nation) and its lines, and put a token beside each line that shows what a group added; a card with no line gets none. Pip says error M: "Only the new people have culture; the others do not." Say beside it: "Everyone's songs, words and ways are culture." Pip says error G: "Everyone does it the same way." Say beside it: "Neighbors still differ."

#### 10 min. Explain

Pairs say, then record, "In our town, ___ added ___. ___ added ___. We could all ___ so that ___." [[s06-P78-1]] Hear three of the rotating six, about 90 seconds each: ask [[s06-X78b]] and [[s06-X78c]], reading each card only at that child's turn. The other three are heard on day 79. Mark by the scale.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read [[s06-X78]] aloud once; each child puts a token. [[s06-X78b]] and [[s06-X78c]] were heard for three children in the pairs work.

#### 2 min. Close

Add "What can we all do together?" to the board.

### Other rooms

No tokens: pencil dots. No town picture: draw three labeled boxes.

### At home

**Swap.** No town cards: use two lines about your own town, names off, and the nation's own page if you find it.

**Skip.** The pairs and the six.

- **5 min. Posadas.** Read the card and its last line. **Say.** "What changed for his gatherings, and what changed for California?" Expect: shorter, at homes and garages; Mexican music grew. [[s06-P78-2]]
- **10 min. Explain.** [[s06-P78-1]] **Say.** "What did newcomers add, and what did people who have always lived here add?" Expect: lines from your cards.
- **5 min. Language Today.** Read the Cherokee Nation card. **Say.** "What does the Cherokee Nation do today?" Expect: it governs itself and teaches its language.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X78]] [[s06-X78b]] [[s06-X78c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: standby cards. Longer: a second shared goal.

### Words and supports

Words: newcomer, change, add.

### Tomorrow

The exit shows error L (a token on card 1 or 2) or error H (a token on card 4); the spoken asks show error M or F. Day 79 (section 6.3) opens with 3 minutes of repair for the letter most missed and returns today's explanations with a cue to revise.
