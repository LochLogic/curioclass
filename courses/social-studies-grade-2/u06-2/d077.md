# Day 77: What people bring when they move

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick the repair card for day 76's most missed letter. Say Tavan, Pell and Pia are made up. Leave the "recipe told aloud" card out of the envelopes. Ask no child about their own move; any may pass. Key: key-exits-76-78.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Teach the letter most missed on s06-X76, s06-X76b, s06-X76c and s06-X76d with its repair card: error C, a note with no year goes in the box; error E, trace a notice board back to Wren and name Dov as the second teller; error I, "I came in 2012": it cannot tell how she felt; error J, order 2011 and 2015. Day 76's strips (s06-P76-1) go back on day 79.

#### 3 min. Retrieval, the chain

Review: the chain from unit 5, because, moved, afterward. Fill the strip as you say a made-up chain: Pia's class grew, so it moved to the room by the library, and afterward it had a reading corner [[s06-P77-2]].

#### 9 min. Two examples, a model

Tavan moves to a new town and plays a drum rhythm on Fridays so neighbors can join. Pell keeps the language Pell grew up speaking and teaches a Saturday words class. For each, fill three lines: what moved (a rhythm; words), how (with a person and by teaching), why shared (so neighbors can join; so neighbors can learn). Terms: bring, in a bag and in a person; share; teach; tradition (review, grade 1). Pip says error A: "Only things in a bag move to a new town." Say beside it: "A rhythm moved with Tavan, and it did not fit in a bag."

#### 10 min. In a bag or in a person

Pairs lay out seven cards and each child says the frame, choosing from the cards [[s06-P77-1]]; the partner says whether the card could be packed. Hear three of the rotating six, about 90 seconds each for both asks, with swap time: [[s06-X77b]] and [[s06-X77c]]. The other three are heard on day 79. Mark by the scale; a pass counts as heard.

#### 4 min. Alike and different

Fill the chart for Tavan and Pell. The class says which part of the culture chart each is: music, language. Pip says error K: "Tavan's rhythm is better than a words class." The class says: different things; different is not better. Answer the hook: yes, music can travel without a suitcase.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read [[s06-X77]] aloud once; each child circles. [[s06-X77b]] and [[s06-X77c]] were heard for three children in the pairs work.

#### 2 min. Question board

Answer "What travels with people?" and add "What changes for whom?".

### Other rooms

No printed cards: write the seven words on slips.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each word on a slip of paper.

**Skip.** The pairs and the six.

- **5 min. Two examples.** Read: Pell teaches a Saturday words class. **Say.** "What moved with Pell, and how?" Expect: words, with Pell and by teaching.
- **15 min. Bag or person.** [[s06-P77-1]] **Say.** "Where does a spoon go?" Expect: in a bag.
- **5 min. Hook.** **Say.** "Can music travel without a suitcase?" Expect: yes, in a person.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X77]] [[s06-X77b]] [[s06-X77c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: say the frame for four cards. Longer: name one more thing that moves in a person.

### Words and supports

Words: bring, share, teach, tradition. Frame: "The ___ moved in a person."

### Tomorrow

The exit shows error A (only things in a bag travel), error H (a claim the card does not support), error K (one practice ranked above another) or error M (only the cook has culture). Day 78 (Understanding) opens with 3 minutes of repair for error A or K, whichever more missed, then teaches how a move changes newcomers and the place.
