# Tavan and Pell cards (invented, the worked examples of day 77)

Every card prints "invented for practice". The teacher says aloud that Tavan and Pell are made up. Day 77 uses invented examples because Posadas and the Cherokee Nation are the worked examples of day 78. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

**Tavan.** "Tavan moves to a new town. On Fridays Tavan plays a drum rhythm on the steps so neighbors can join." What moved: a rhythm. How: with a person and by teaching. Why shared: so neighbors can join.
**Pell.** "Pell moves to a new town. Pell keeps the language Pell grew up speaking and teaches a Saturday words class." What moved: words. How: with a person and by teaching. Why shared: so neighbors can learn them.
**Chain retrieval (a fresh invented chain):** Pia's class gets a new room. Because: the class grew. They moved: to the room by the library. Afterward: they had space for a reading corner.
**Alike and different chart:** both pass on something to neighbors; one is music and one is language.
**Pip says A, then K:** Pip says "Only things in a bag move to a new town." Beside it: "A rhythm moved with Tavan, and it did not fit in a bag." Pip says "Tavan's rhythm is better than a words class." Beside it: "Different things. Different is not better."
**Hook answered aloud:** yes, music can travel without a suitcase.
