# Statement cards, day 60

Six cards for the pairs work. A pair sorts them into two sheets, "Smith decided" and "others decided", then points to the line of the Smith Life card for one: "The Smith Life card says ___." The six are heard. No acting out. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

1. Men took Smith far from his family.
2. Smith saved his coins.
3. Men said Smith belonged to them.
4. Smith bought his own freedom.
5. Men put Smith on a ship.
6. Smith worked to buy freedom for his wife and children.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Smith decided: 2, 4, 6. Others decided: 1, 3, 5. Lines to point to: paragraph one for 1 and 5, paragraph two for 3, paragraph three for 2, 4 and 6. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
