# Exit cards, days 60 to 62

Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 60 exit card: Smith's later life

Credit line: "Venture Smith, A Narrative of the Life and Adventures of Venture, 1798, and the Library of Congress. Summary by Curio Class, read November 2026."
**Card line:** "Smith bought his freedom. Later he owned land by a river."
**Picture:** Three small pictures in a row: a man by a house at a river, a man alone in a small boat, a man in a fine hall with a crown (marked as a palace).
**Mark:** Circle the picture of Smith's life after he was free: owned land by a river, sailed for fun, lived in a palace. (The pictures are labeled with those three phrases.)
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Smith had to work for the men who held him. Whose choice was that, Smith's or the enslavers'?"

## Day 61 exit card: Seminole Card

Credit line: "Seminole Nation of Oklahoma, Our Story page, and National Park Service, Trail of Tears National Historic Trail, read November 2026. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card line:** "Officers of the United States government told Seminole families to leave their homes in the Southeast, and the families were made to go west."
**Picture:** A homeland map with an arrow west. No people shown.
**Mark:** Circle chosen or forced.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "The Seminole families were forced west. Is the Seminole Nation gone, or here today?"

## Day 62 exit card: Route Card

Printed: "Invented for practice. Made by Curio Class in 2026. This is a model of a family's flight, not a record of a real family."
**Card line:** "War made staying unsafe. A family fled Syria to Jordan, then flew to Luxembourg."
**Picture:** The world map with Syria, Jordan and Luxembourg marked and labeled, nothing else drawn.
**Mark:** On the world map, draw the route.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "On the Route Card, say why the family left Syria."
