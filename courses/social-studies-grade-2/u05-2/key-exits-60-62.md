# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 60 to 62

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-60-62.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 60: owned land by a river; the enslavers' choice. Day 61: forced; here today (the Seminole Nation of Oklahoma has its own pages). Teacher note: do not say every Seminole person left; Seminole people also live in Florida today as the Seminole Tribe of Florida, a separate nation (its own history page, semtribe.com/history/the-long-war, says several bands remained in Florida; not taught here). Day 62: an arrow from Syria to Jordan, then an arrow from Jordan to Luxembourg; war made staying unsafe. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
