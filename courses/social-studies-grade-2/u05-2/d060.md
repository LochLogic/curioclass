# Day 60: Smith's story: a forced move

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the third six on the record sheet. Say aloud that Venture Smith is a real person and the other children and towns are made up. No acting today; any child may draw instead, or go to a quiet drawing task. Key: key-exits-60-62.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 59's chain (s05-P59-7) and exit ticket (s05-X59), marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If many missed error B on day 59's spoken exit s05-X59c (the field planted first), reorder a fresh chain: [[s05-P60-6]] Say: reason first, then the move, then afterward. Error C (s05-X59, a forced move circled as chosen) goes to the small group below.

#### 3 min. The source

Smith Life is a Curio Class summary of Venture Smith's own account, printed in 1798. Review: you ask of a card who made it, when, where. **Ask.** Who made it? When? Where? Expect: Venture Smith told his life and it was printed; in 1798; New London, Connecticut. Review: you found the continents in unit 2. [[s05-P60-1]]

#### 9 min. Smith Life, told once

Read the three paragraphs once, slowly and calmly, with the credit line. Say "enslaved person" and "enslavers", never other words. Show no picture of capture. Let any child draw. He did not choose it. The card ends: his life went on after he was free.

#### 4 min. Whose wish

Fill the chain on the board: "Because the men wanted him to work for them, they took him. Afterward he was enslaved." Two columns: what the enslavers did; what Smith wanted. [[s05-P60-2]] The card does not say more about what he hoped that day. Pip says error C: "He rode a ship, so he chose." Say beside it: Could he have stayed? He could not say no: forced.

#### 4 min. The lives they built

Say: Smith was a person with family and skills. [[s05-P60-3]] This card tells only Smith's life. Do not rank hardships.

#### 7 min. Pairs work

Pairs sort the six statement cards onto "Smith decided" and "others decided" (and write S or O): [[s05-P60-4]] Each pair points to a line for one: [[s05-P60-5]] Pip says error K: "I think Smith liked it." Say beside it: the card says ___. Hear four of the six first, 45 seconds each with the swap, 3 minutes, for s05-X60b, then a small group of at most 6 takes 4 minutes: a trip planned freely beside a family told they cannot stay. [[s05-P60-7]]

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X60]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. While the rest mark, hear the other two of the six, 45 seconds each: [[s05-X60b]].

### Other rooms

Write the statements on slips; read the card aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheets: two piles on the table.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **5 min. The card.** Read Smith Life once, calmly. **Say.** "Who made it? When? Where?" Expect: Smith, 1798, Connecticut.
- **15 min. Who decided?** Your child writes S or O. [[s05-P60-4]] **Say.** "The card says ___." Expect: a line.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X60]] [[s05-X60b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three statements. Longer: what source would tell Smith's own wishes.

### Words and supports

Words: enslaved person, enslavers, forced, decided, freedom. Frame: "The Smith Life card says ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 tells another forced move. Error K shows as sailed or palace circled; error C as "Smith chose it". Day 61 repairs error C first and sends error K to its small group.
