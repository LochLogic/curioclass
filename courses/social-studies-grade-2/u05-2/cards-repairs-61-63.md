# Repair cards, days 61 to 63

Every card prints "invented for practice" except where a real nation is named. Each repair is at most 5 minutes. Pip says the wrong idea and the right idea is said beside it. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## A trip and a notice (error C, day 61)

**Picture:** Card 1: a family plans a trip to a harbor town for a shop job. Card 2: officials tell a different family to leave their house because the hillside is sliding. The child sorts "chosen" and "forced" with "Could they have stayed?"
**Say:** "A ship or a car is how they traveled. The question is whether they could have stayed." Pip: "They rode on a boat, so they chose."

## A supporting line (error K, day 61 small group)

**Picture:** A card: "Ming walks to the hills for fresh grass for the sheep." The child puts a finger on the line that shows a reason.
**Say:** "The card says ___." Pip: "Ming wanted a holiday, I think."

## The three Cherokee governments (error M, day 62)

**Picture:** A card map with the Cherokee Nation and the United Keetoowah Band in Oklahoma and the Eastern Band in North Carolina, each with a star, beside the removal arrow from the Southeast to the west. A dated line: "As of November 2026."
**Say:** "Three Cherokee governments govern themselves today." Pip: "The Cherokee are gone."

## Why a family left (error A, day 63)

**Picture:** Card: "Floods reach Yara's village and the water is not safe. Yara's family leaves for a camp in a dry town." The child points to the line that gives the reason.
**Say:** "The card says the water is not safe." Pip: "They went to see the dry town."

## A fresh arrow (error I, day 63)

**Picture:** A card: "Pavo lived by the sea in the east and moved to a farm in the west." A small map with no arrow. The child draws the arrow from where Pavo left to where Pavo arrived with a finger first and then a pencil.
**Say:** "Start where they left; end where they arrived." Pip: "Pavo went from the farm to the sea."

## Trip and notice again (error C, day 63)

**Picture:** The same two-card sort as day 61 with new names: Card 1, a family chooses a house near a school. Card 2, officials tell a family they cannot stay in their town.
**Say:** "Could they have stayed?"

## Key (teacher sheet only)

C: chosen; forced. K: the line about the grass. M: Oklahoma, Oklahoma, North Carolina. A: the water is not safe. I: from the sea in the east to the farm in the west. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
