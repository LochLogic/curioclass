# Day 62: Leaving because it is not safe

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the fifth six on the record sheet. Say aloud: Bel and every family on the practice cards are made up; Anna, Smith and the Cherokee people are real. No child is asked whether they or their family are refugees; any child may pass or draw. No acting out. Key: key-exits-60-62.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 61's strip (s05-P61-5), map (s05-P61-7) and exit ticket (s05-X61). If many missed error M on day 61's spoken exit s05-X61b (the nation is gone), do the three Cherokee governments on the card map beside the removal arrow, dated November 2026: [[s05-P62-1]] Error C (s05-X61) goes to the small group in the tracing part.

#### 9 min. A word met, then a chain

Say the word: a refugee is a person who had to leave their country because staying was not safe. [[s05-P62-3]] Read the Refugee Card for Bel, made up. The class fills a blank chain strip with no example in view. [[s05-P62-4]] **Ask.** [[s05-P62-5]] Pip says error C: "They went to another country, so they chose." Say beside it: choosing a road does not make leaving a choice.

#### 9 min. Which reason is better supported?

Three cards, each with two possible reasons: Anna, Smith, the Cherokee. Each pair points to the line that supports the better one: "The ___ card says ___." [[s05-P62-6]] The six come to your table, 45 seconds each with the swap, 4.5 minutes, for s05-X62b. Pairs not waiting go on to the next card.

#### 8 min. Trace the moves

Pairs trace Anna, Smith and the Cherokee on the continent map with a pencil and say each: [[s05-P62-7]] Pip says error I: "Anna went from America to Europe." Turn the arrow round: start where they left. Each child draws Anna's arrow: [[s05-P62-8]] The Cherokee arrow does not cross an ocean. A small group of at most 6 takes 4 minutes on error C: a bus trip planned freely beside a family told to leave. [[s05-P62-2]]

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X62]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The six answered [[s05-X62b]] aloud in the reasons part.

#### 1 min. Question board

Read "Who could stay, and who could not?" Tomorrow: why one country is called a nation of immigrants.

### Other rooms

Draw the continents on paper; write cards on slips; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed map: draw seven blobs and label them.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **10 min. Bel.** Read the card. Your child fills the boxes. [[s05-P62-4]] **Say.** "Could they have stayed?" Expect: no, it was not safe.
- **10 min. Arrow.** Your child draws Anna's arrow. [[s05-P62-8]] **Say.** "From where to where?" Expect: Europe to North America.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X62]] [[s05-X62b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Bel and one arrow. Longer: what source would tell Bel's own wishes.

### Words and supports

Words: refugee, safe, route, from, to, by. Frame: "From ___ to ___, by ___, because ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 63 asks who the word immigrant describes. Error I shows as the route reversed or Syria drawn straight to Luxembourg; error A as "they went to see Luxembourg"; error C as "they chose to go". Day 63 repairs error I first and sends error A to a small group.
