# Day 14: Our map, revised

## Teacher page

### Before class

Claim group: group 4. Exit group: group 5. Count day 13's misses on error I and error O. Set the four wall signs. Put each draft on its desk with its slip face up. Key: key-exits-13-14-16.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Return each draft with its cue and day 13's exit ticket. Error I (day 13's spoken answer): pairs trace the room route on their draft, a finger from the door, pointing to each feature in the order a visitor meets it. Pip puts the shelf in a corner to look neat. Beside it: "The shelf is where it is in the room." **Ask.** Where is the ___ in the room? Expect: where it was seen.

#### 12 min. Working time

Minutes 0 to 4: each child counts two other kinds of features and draws a new graph, one mark for each. A small group with error O recounts with you: each mark touches one counted thing. Minutes 4 to 12: children revise the map on a clean sheet, following the cue: set the rose by the north wall sign, and write "Made by ___ in our classroom, 2026." Review: on day 6 the three-question card asked who made it, when and where; this line answers all three. Minutes 5 to 10: the exit group come one at a time to your table, mark the exit card (1 minute), then answer (s01-X14b), 25 seconds each.

#### 9 min. Closing claim

Pip says: "The prettiest map is best for every job." Beside it: "The best map has what we need for the job." Review: on day 11 you said "I think the ___ map is best for ___ because ___." Each child circles one map and one job on the claim sheet, then writes or draws a because and what our map leaves out (4 minutes). The claim group say theirs aloud, 40 seconds each. Look for: the map fits the job; the because points to something the map shows; the child says what it leaves out. Choices: our map, the Riverbend Park map, either world card or the globe.

#### 5 min. What our map leaves out

Pairs talk 2 minutes. **Ask.** [[s01-P14-1]] Expect: a real thing not drawn. Hear three pairs. Add to the board what our map shows, and a first answer to "Which map shows the world best, and best for what?" Expect: it depends on the job.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card (s01-X14). Read aloud: "A visitor cannot tell what the little pictures mean. Circle the part to add." Mark 1 minute. Collect everything 2 minutes. The title, rose or scale bar circled is error P; the prettiest map for every job is error N.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: use the draft on plain paper.

**Skip.** The six and the claim aloud.

- **15 min. Revise.** Your child reads your cue, then draws the map again with a title, key, north arrow and five-box scale, and writes "Made by ___ in ___, 2026."
  **Say.** "Where is the ___ in the room?" Expect: where it was seen.
- **10 min. A claim.** Your child picks a map and a job and says why.
  **Say.** "What does your map leave out?" Expect: something not drawn.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X14]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X14b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise one part. Longer: a second scale on a map of a bigger place.

### Words and supports

Words: key, rose, scale, maker, leave out, claim, job. Frame: "It leaves out ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 15 is the unit check: read once, answer alone. Today's map, graph and claim are scored after class; the six not yet heard say their claim. Error P (day 14's ticket) goes to day 16, to add the missing part to a new map in a group.
