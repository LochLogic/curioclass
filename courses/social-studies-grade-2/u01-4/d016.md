# Day 16: Try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort day 15's assessment into four groups from the record sheet, by the part with the largest gap: map parts (errors A, B, C, D), sources (errors E, F, J, L, M), two world maps (errors G, K, N), our own map (errors I, O, P). Today's six: the children least heard. Lay each group's cards at its table. Hold no page up. Key: key-exits-13-14-16.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return findings

Hand back each page marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. Pairs say "I want another try at ___." 15 seconds each; a child may pass.

#### 10 min. Targeted teaching

Meet each group 2 and a half minutes, the largest gap first; the others work their first fresh card in pairs. Map parts: lay four strips on a path labelled ten paces a length and count by tens: 40 paces; turn a paper rose and say north is where it points; match a ditch, a hedge and a dock to what each changed. Sources: sort a made-up credit into who made it, who keeps it and what place it shows. Two world maps: lay the cut-outs on the Peters card and the globe; Mercator suits a straight course, Peters suits sizes. Our own map: check the four-part card against your returned map and add the part that does not work. Review: on day 14 you made a map with all four parts.

#### 10 min. Fresh task

Each group works its cards in pairs ([[s01-P16-1]] [[s01-P16-2]] [[s01-P16-3]] [[s01-P16-4]] [[s01-P16-5]] [[s01-P16-6]] [[s01-P16-7]]), then names the one thing that changed its mind. Map parts cards 1 to 3, sources 4 and 5, two world maps 6, our map 7. Say each card is made up for practice. Pip voices the wrong idea on cards 1, 5 and 6. The six come one at a time to your table, 1 minute each: mark the exit card, then answer (s01-X16b) and (s01-X16c). Six minutes in all, four spare.

#### 5 min. Review

The other 24 mark the card (s01-X16). Read aloud: "Circle how far: 7, 9 or 14 paces." Mark 2 minutes. Each child marks one box in each row of the self-check strip and points to a piece of work that shows it (2 minutes). Collect 1 minute. Seven or nine paces is error C; the keeper as maker is error E; the park as the place made is error J.

### At home

**Swap.** No strips: cut paper into equal bits.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at what day 15 showed; your child picks one part to try again.
  **Say.** "Which part do you want another try at?" Expect: one of map parts, sources, two world maps, our map.
- **15 min. Try again.** Teach that part with your hands: strips on a path, a credit sorted, or the map with its four parts.
  **Say.** "Each length stands for ___, so the distance is ___." Expect: the label, then the total.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X16]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X16b]] [[s01-X16c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one card for each group.

### Words and supports

Words: scale, maker, keeper, place made, rose, try again. Frames: "Each length stands for ___, so the distance is ___." "Made by ___ in ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 starts unit 2 with a short retrieval of directions and maps. If many circled 7 or 9 (error C), pointed to the keeper (error E) or the park (error J), repeat those in five minutes.
