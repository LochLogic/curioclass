# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 13, 14 and 16

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-13-14-16.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 13: two different symbols in the key, bench and tree, and the bench symbol on both benches where they stand; the observed positions stay. One symbol for both kinds is error P; any order is error I.
- Day 14: the key. Any true thing the map leaves out, such as the paths or the gates. The title, the rose or the scale bar is error P; "it shows everything" is error N.
- Day 16: 14 paces. Nia Okafor is the maker, a school workroom is where it was made, Heron Park is the place shown. 7 or 9 paces is error C; the keeper or the park's name as maker is error E; the park as the place made is error J.
