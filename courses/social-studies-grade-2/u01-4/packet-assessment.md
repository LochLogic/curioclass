# Day 15 packet (kept back until day 15)

Read each item once, neutrally. One copy of each card for each table of about six, laid flat where every child can see it, and one counter for each child. Do not teach, hint or confirm. Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters, except the Vermont map, which is old and real.

## Card 1: Willow Garden Map

**Picture:** An invented garden map, north up. Title: Willow Garden. A gate at the west end. A straight path of three equal lengths, each marked four paces, runs east to a tool shed at the east end. Rectangles are planting beds and circles are water barrels, with a bed beside each barrel. A small bridge crosses a stream. The key lists a rectangle for a planting bed and a circle for a water barrel. A compass rose and a scale label, one length is four paces.

**Caption, read aloud once:** The path runs from the gate to the shed. This is the nicest garden anywhere.

## Card 2: Map X and Map Y

**Picture:** Two world cards made from public domain coastlines. Map X shows one projection and Map Y the other, centered differently, and they differ by pattern, not color. Neither card is named.

## Card 3: the Vermont map

**Picture:** A crop of a printed map of Vermont, 1855, enlarged, with its imprint beneath. **Read aloud once from the transcript:** Colton's Map of the State of Vermont. J. H. Colton, New York, 1855. This transcript is provisional until a person reads the imprint off the full image.
