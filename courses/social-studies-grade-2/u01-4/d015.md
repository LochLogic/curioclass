# Day 15: Unit check

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the packet in its envelope until now. A person must read the Vermont imprint off the full image before this page prints; the transcript is provisional until then. Choose the six not yet heard from the record sheet; lay each child's day 14 claim sheet beside it. Count day 14's misses on error P.

### The day

#### 3 min. Set-up

Say: answer alone, pass any item you like, no one helps with answers, a scribe is ready. Each question is read once. Hand out response pages, counters and one packet set a table. Say the Willow Garden Map and Map X and Map Y are invented for practice; the Vermont map is old and real. Read the caption and imprint once, neutrally.

#### 20 min. Independent tasks

Children answer on the response page by circling, pointing, drawing or writing a word; a scribe writes what a child says. Say nothing about right or wrong. Read in this order, with about this long.
Willow Garden Map, 8 minutes: lines 1 to 7: title (s01-E1), key (s01-E2), rose (s01-E3), then turn each table's card so the rose points left: north now (s01-E22), scale (s01-E4), gate to shed (s01-E5), two ways people use the land (s01-E6).
Map X and Map Y, 5 minutes: lines 8 to 13: the Mercator map, with a counter on one clue (s01-E7); what it changes (s01-E8); what the Peters map changes (s01-E9); which compares sizes (s01-E10); why (s01-E11); what the chosen map gets wrong (s01-E12). Read each table's counters once.
Vermont map, 3 minutes: lines 14 to 16: who made it (s01-E15), when (s01-E16), where it was made (s01-E17).
Caption, 4 minutes: lines 17 to 19: which can be checked (s01-E18), what the map can help us find out (s01-E19), what it cannot show (s01-E20).
Items s01-E13 and s01-E14 are scored from day 14's revised map and new graph, not asked again.

#### 5 min. Hear the children not yet heard

The six: "Say your claim: I think the ___ map is best for ___ because ___" from their day 14 sheet (s01-E21), 40 seconds each, 4 minutes and a spare minute. Neutral prompts only. The others finish or pass items, then draw a place they like on the back, not scored.

#### 5 min. Collect

Collect response pages, revised maps and graphs with their drafts, and claim sheets. Mark nothing in the room. After class, mark each look-for 2 (shown), 1 (shown with help) or 0 (not yet seen) on the record sheet, about 3 minutes a child (ours, untested). If day 14's tickets showed error P, note it for day 16.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: draw a garden map with a title, key, rose and scale.

**Skip.** The scribe, the claims and the packet.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read each question once; give no help.
  **Say.** "What does the key tell you?" Expect: what each symbol stands for.
  **Say.** "How far is it from the gate to the shed?" Expect: the lengths counted by the label.
  **Say.** "Which can be checked on the map?" Expect: the path, not the nicest.
- **20 min. Respond.** Look at what was missed together; your child tries one part again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one item from each card.

### Words and supports

Words: title, key, rose, scale, maker, date, place, fact, opinion, Mercator, Peters. Frames: "The key tells me ___." "I think the ___ map is best for ___ because ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 hands back day 15's assessment by part: map parts (errors A, B, C, D), sources (errors E, F, J, L, M), two world maps (errors G, K, N) and our own map (errors I, O, P).
