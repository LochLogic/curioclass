# Day 13: Planning our map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: group 3. Count day 12's misses (error H). Pick two kinds of features every child can count, such as windows and shelves; keep two for day 14. Tape a line along the wall. Our place is never a home. Key: key-exits-13-14-16.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Warm-up and repair

Error H (day 12's exit ticket): pairs lay the country card on the North America card, and that on the world card. Review: in grade 1 you stacked address rings. **Ask.** Is North America a country? Expect: no, a continent holding several countries. Pip says: "North America is a country." Beside it: "North America is a continent with several countries."

#### 5 min. What should our map show?

**Ask.** What should our map show, and what can it leave out? Expect: the door, desks, shelf; small moving things can go. List three to show and two to leave out, each with "because". Pip puts the sink in the middle: "That looks nice." Beside it: "The sink is where it is in the room." Post it on the board.

#### 6 min. Scale in paces

Review: on day 4 you laid strips end to end and said "Each scale length stands for ___, so the distance is ___." Pace the long wall once, heel to toe, the class counting aloud. The class picks the scale length: two paces if the count is even, one if odd. Each child draws a scale bar of equal boxes, writes paces a box and counts the wall's boxes. Use your count if a child cannot pace.

#### 5 min. Count and graph

Review: in kindergarten you drew a two-row picture graph, one mark for each. Count each kind aloud as you point; each child draws one mark for each. Pip draws two marks too many: "More marks look better." Beside it: "One mark for each one counted." **Ask.** [[s01-P13-1]] Expect: no, four lamps, four marks.

#### 10 min. Draft the map

Each child drafts: a title, symbols with a key line each, a rose with north toward the north wall sign, the scale bar, and features where seen. No map is held up. Minutes 0 to 6: circulate for a key line for each symbol and each feature where it is in the room. Minutes 6 to 10: the six come one at a time, mark the exit card with you (1 minute), then answer (s01-X13b), 25 seconds each.

#### 4 min. Exit ticket

The other 24 mark the card (s01-X13). Read aloud: "Draw one more mark so the bench row matches the courtyard." Mark 1 minute; collect 1 minute; close the board 2 minutes. After class write one cue on each slip: a count that does not match, a feature in the wrong place, or a missing part (about 1.5 minutes a child, ours, untested). No mark added, or four marks, is error O. Any order will do is error I.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: fold paper for the map and draw five boxes for the scale.

**Skip.** The warm-up, the pacing and the six.

- **8 min. Count.** Pick a library, shop or school room you can see, not your home. Count two kinds of things aloud together.
  **Say.** "How many ___? How many marks?" Expect: one mark for each, the same number.
- **17 min. Draft.** Your child draws a title, a key, an arrow for north, five boxes for the scale, and things where you saw them.
  **Say.** "Where is the ___ in the room?" Expect: where it was seen.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X13]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s01-X13b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one kind. Longer: a third kind.

### Words and supports

Words: key, rose, scale, graph, mark, leave out. Frame: "It leaves out ___."

### Tomorrow

Day 14 returns each draft with its cue and opens with error I. Error O is met when a small group recounts and draws a new graph.
