# Teacher key: Day 15 packet (kept back until day 15)

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (packet-assessment.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Turned rose (item s01-E22): after the compass rose is read, each table turns its Willow Garden card a quarter turn so the rose points to the left of the card. The answer is toward where the rose points; the top of the card is error B.
- Map X and Map Y (item s01-E7): each child circles the Mercator map on the response page and puts a counter on one clue on the table's card; the teacher reads the counters on each table once.
- Vermont (items s01-E15 to s01-E17): children copy the printed words from the enlarged imprint, or point; a scribe writes what a child says.

- Map X is the Mercator card and Map Y the Peters card, told apart by how Greenland and Africa compare in size; the key stays on this page.
- Willow Garden answers: it shows a garden called Willow Garden; twelve paces from gate to shed; the path statement can be checked, nicest is what someone thinks.
- Vermont: maker J. H. Colton, date 1855, made in New York.
- Collect the pages at the end. The teacher scores the revised map and graph after class.
