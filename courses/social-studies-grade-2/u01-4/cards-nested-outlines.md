# Three nested outlines (day 13 warm-up, error H)

Say first: "We drew these outlines for our class from the real shapes of the lands." Plain black line drawing on white. Each outline is on its own card.

## Card 1: the world

**Picture:** A world outline with North America drawn thicker than the rest.

## Card 2: North America

**Picture:** An outline of North America with the borders of three countries shown by thin lines and no names printed.

## Card 3: one country

**Picture:** One country's outline, large, with a small dot for a town. Label: a country.

## For the teacher

Pairs lay card 3 on card 2 and card 2 on card 1, tracing with a finger: a country is inside a continent, and a continent is inside the world. Pip says North America is a country; beside it, North America is a continent with several countries. Five minutes, the one repair the warm-up holds. Retrieval inside it: "Which land or water features belong on our map?"
