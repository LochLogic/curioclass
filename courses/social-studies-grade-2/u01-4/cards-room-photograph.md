# Room photograph card (day 13 fallback)

Used only if the room cannot be counted or paced (a visitor, a closed door). Say so aloud: "We are counting from the photograph."

**Picture:** A photograph of the classroom taken from the door, showing the desks, the board, the shelf and the plants, with no child in it. **Credit line:** Photograph by Curio Class, 2026, made in a school doorway.

**For the teacher:** Children count two kinds of features from the picture, such as chairs and plants. A child who cannot pace uses the teacher's pace count. The scale length is two paces if the wall's count is even, one pace if it is odd.
