# Fresh tasks, day 16

Each card is invented for practice; say so aloud. Groups work one card at a time after the targeted teaching, in pairs. Plain black line drawing on white. No card repeats a map, a case or a question of day 15 or of the exit card.

## Card 1: a longer path (map parts, scale)

**Picture:** A made-up path with four equal lengths end to end. The label says one length is ten paces. Pip says: "Four lengths is four paces." Pairs lay strips, say whether Pip is right, and say why (P16-1).

## Card 2: four slips about the rose (map parts)

**Picture:** Four slips and a small made-up map with its rose pointing down the page. Pairs sort each slip true or not true (P16-2).

## Card 3: actions and features (map parts, land and water)

**Picture:** Three action slips (dug a ditch, planted a hedge, built a dock) and three picture cards (a stream, a field edge, a lake shore). Pairs match each action to the feature it changed (P16-3).

## Card 4: a credit to sort (sources)

**Picture:** A made-up credit cut into three slips: Drawn by Ravi Shah, 2026, made in a school hallway. Kept in the town library. Shows a pond. Pairs lay the slips on three rows: who made it, who keeps it, what place it shows (P16-4).

## Card 5: a printed caption (sources)

**Picture:** A made-up map of a pond with a caption: The pond is east of the barn. It is the loveliest pond. Pip says printed means true. Pairs circle the words that are what someone thinks (P16-5).

## Card 6: a flat map and the globe (projections)

**Picture:** A globe beside a flat card of the same lands. Pip says the Peters map is useless. Pairs use the globe to say whether Pip is right (P16-6).

## Card 7: my own map (made map)

**Picture:** None: the child's own returned revised map and the four-part card. Each child marks the one part that does not yet work and adds it (P16-7).

## For the teacher

Pip voices the wrong idea on cards 1, 5 and 6. Each group names the one thing that changed its mind. Groups take cards by component: map parts 1 to 3, sources 4 and 5, projections 6, made map 7.
