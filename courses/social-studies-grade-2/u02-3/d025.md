# Day 25: Another region

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the six the record sheet names for today. Lay the repair cards and the Harbor Street cards. Hand back day 24's cards (s02-X24) and charts (s02-P24-1). Say every place is invented for practice. Key: key-exits-24-25.md.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair

Two parts of 4 minutes. Part 1, error G from day 24 (s02-X24d): eight small cards, each with a date, sorted into two bins, physical and cultural. Say together: "Ice, water, land and plants are physical. What people build, do or write is cultural." Part 2, error H (s02-X24c): a river picture beside a reason card, "A flood cut into the bank," and a card, "The card does not say." Say: "The pictures show what changed; the reason card tells why." Teach the part more children needed to the whole class; the other goes to a group of at most 6 in pairs work.

#### 9 min. Another region

Find North America on the globe. Harbor Street is in Stone Landing, an invented town on a harbor in North America, not in Europe. Lay the two views and read each date: 1925 and 2025. Ask the three questions from day 6: who made it (Curio Class), when (the dates), where made (the record does not say).

Review: in grade 1 you filled a then-and-now chart. Read the changes only from the cards as drawn. Buildings: wood, then brick. Travel: a horse and cart, then a bus and bikes. Lamps: gas, then electric. Signs: English, then English and Spanish. The harbor and the tall clock stayed the same. People built every change here, so each is cultural. Pip says: "The 1925 street is the street today." Beside it: "Read the date: this is 1925."

#### 6 min. Pairs work

Pip's chart, with its columns changed and stayed the same, puts the clock under changed and the signs under stayed the same. Pairs move the two label cards that sit in the wrong column ([[s02-P25-2]], 3 minutes), then say "It changed from ___ to ___. It stayed the same: ___." The charts stay in the folder; the teacher marks them after class.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 8 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 70 seconds each, for (s02-X25c), two of (s02-X25d), (s02-X25g), (s02-X25b), (s02-X25e), (s02-X25f) or (s02-X25h), dealt, and one ocean ([[s02-P25-1]]). Everyone else revises the map from the cue and fills the cue sheet. This block has about 4 minutes of slack.

#### 2 min. Question board

The class says its answer to "What changes over time?" and the teacher writes it in the class's words.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud: "Circle one thing that is new in 2020" (s02-X25) on the town card, an invented town in South America dated 1980 and 2020. Mark about 1 minute. The six were heard in supported work. The stall circled has no letter: record not yet seen. One kind of change named is error G; a made-up cause is error H; 1980 called today is error N.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The cue sheet.

- **10 min. Two views.** Find an old and a new photo of one street.
  **Say.** "Which is nearer to today? How do you know?" Expect: the date.
- **15 min. Pip's chart.** [[s02-P25-2]] Move the two wrong labels on the Harbor Street chart.
  **Say.** "What changed, and what stayed the same?" Expect: buildings and signs changed; the harbor and clock stayed.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X25]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X25b]] [[s02-X25c]] [[s02-X25d]] [[s02-X25g]] [[s02-X25e]] [[s02-X25f]] [[s02-X25h]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: part 1 of the repair only. Longer: a child says one thing that could change in their street and one that would stay.

### Words and supports

Words: changed, stayed the same, date, street, harbor, physical change, cultural change. Frames: "It changed from ___ to ___." "It stayed the same: ___." Pointing counts.
