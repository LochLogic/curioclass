# Day 24: One region at two dates

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the six the record sheet names. Lay out cards. Hand back day 23's cards (s02-X23) and region frames (s02-P23-1). Say every place is invented for practice except Aletsch, which is real; a person looks at its images before layout. Key: key-exits-24-25.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error F, from day 23 (s02-X23b). Lay the three Sandpiper Town cards: a baker, a teacher and a ferry pilot. Pip says: "Everyone near the sea is a fisher." Beside it: "One place, three kinds of work." **Ask.** Do all three fish? Expect: no. This is the one repair today.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: in grade 1 you used a date to compare two pictures. Lay the classroom cards, dated 1998 and 2018, and read the dates. **Ask.** Which is older? Expect: 1998. A child who misses joins a small group in pairs work.

#### 11 min. One region at two dates

Find Europe on the globe, then the Alps. Lay the two satellite images together; review dates by reading them: 2 September 1984 and 6 August 2024. They are a month apart, so we compare ice, not snow. Read the credit: NASA Earth Observatory images by Michala Garrison, using United States Geological Survey Landsat data. Ask the three questions from day 6: who made it, when, where made (not said).

Two new words: a physical change is a change in land, water, ice or plants; a cultural change is a change in what people build, do or write. Review grade 1's then-and-now chart in three columns. Changed: the ice, whose end has moved back more than 1,300 meters. Stayed the same: the mountains and the valley. The card says: read the reason card once. "Summers in the Alps have grown warmer. Warm summers melt more ice than winter snow adds." Say it is slow, and no child has to fix it.

Pip says: "The two glacier pictures show cultural change too." Beside it: "Ice is physical; nobody built it." Pip says: "The picture proves why the ice shrank." Beside it: "The pictures show what changed; the card tells why."

#### 5 min. Pairs work

Pairs lay the Pine Cove cards, 1985 and 2010, and sort the five label cards under changed and stayed the same ([[s02-P24-1]], 3 minutes). Then they copy the words on the reason card into the third column and say "The card says ___." ([[s02-P24-3]], 2 minutes). The charts stay in the folder.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 7 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 70 seconds each, for (s02-X24f), one of (s02-X24b), (s02-X24c), (s02-X24d) or (s02-X24e), dealt, and two globe locations ([[s02-P24-2]]). The rest revise the map from the cue.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "What changes over time?"

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud: "Circle one thing that changed between the two pictures" (s02-X24) on the forest card, an invented forest in Europe, 1995 and 2015. Mark 2 minutes. The six were heard earlier. The hill or the path circled has no letter: record not yet seen. The picture for why is error H; cultural is G; the 1995 picture as today is N.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The cue sheet.

- **10 min. Two dates.** Find two dated photos of one place.
  **Say.** "Which picture is older? How do you know?" Expect: the date.
- **15 min. Changed and stayed.** [[s02-P24-1]] Sort the five Pine Cove labels, the boat ramp first, then read the reason card.
  **Say.** "What changed, what stayed the same, and what does the card say?" Expect: a change, a stay and the card's words.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X24]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X24b]] [[s02-X24c]] [[s02-X24d]] [[s02-X24e]] [[s02-X24f]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Pine Cove only, without the retrieval. Longer: a child says why an old photograph cannot show the place today.

### Words and supports

Words: changed, stayed the same, glacier, ice, physical change, cultural change. Frames: "It changed from ___ to ___." "It stayed the same: ___." "The card says ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 25 opens with errors G and H: physical and cultural cards in two bins, then a picture beside its reason card. Then another region.
