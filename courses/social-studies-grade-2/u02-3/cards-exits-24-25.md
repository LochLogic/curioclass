# Change Pair A: exit cards, days 24 and 25 (kept back until the day)

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Day 24: the forest, in Europe

**Picture:** Two panels side by side, a small map of Europe with a dot above them. Left panel, dated 1995: a hill with a path and a few small trees. Right panel, dated 2015: the same hill and path with many rows of trees.
**Caption under the pair:** Wetter years let young trees grow back across the hill.

**Mark:** Circle one thing that changed between the two pictures. **Spoken:** "What stayed the same?" "What printed words tell us why the trees changed?" "Is the change in the forest pictures physical or cultural?" "Which picture is nearer to today?" "Show me this continent on the globe."

## Day 25: the town, in South America

**Picture:** Two panels, a small map of South America with a dot above them. Left panel, dated 1980: a market stall, a path and a pond. Right panel, dated 2020: the same stall and path, a smaller pond and a new footbridge beside the stall.
**Caption under the pair:** Less rain fell, so the pond is smaller.

**Mark:** Circle one thing that is new in 2020. **Spoken:** "Show me this continent on the globe." "Say one thing that stayed the same on the diagram." "Which change on the diagram is physical?" "Which change on the diagram is cultural?" "What does the card say about why the pond is smaller?" "Which picture is nearer to today?"
