# Harbor Street, Stone Landing (day 25)

Invented for practice, in an invented town in North America. The two cards print the dates 1925 and 2025 in large letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Harbor Street, 1925

**Picture:** A harbour with a wooden bakery and a wooden ferry ticket office, each with a painted sign in English. A horse-drawn cart and people on foot. A tall clock on a post at the corner. Gas lamps.

## Harbor Street, 2025

**Picture:** The same harbour. A brick bakery and a brick ferry ticket office, with bright signs in English and in Spanish. A bus at a bus stop and people on bikes. The tall clock still stands at the corner. Electric lamps.

## Six label cards and Pip's chart (pairs work)

Labels: the buildings, the lamps, the bus, the signs, the clock, the harbour. Pip's chart, with its two columns changed and stayed the same: changed holds the buildings, the lamps, the bus and the clock; stayed the same holds the harbour and the signs.

## Key (teacher)

Changed: the buildings (wood to brick), the lamps (gas to electric), the bus (it was a cart), the signs (English, then English and Spanish). Stayed: the harbour and the clock. Pip's two mistakes: the clock sits under changed and the signs sit under stayed the same. All the changes on Harbor Street are cultural; nothing here is a physical change. The dates are the card's own; Harbor Street is not a real street.
