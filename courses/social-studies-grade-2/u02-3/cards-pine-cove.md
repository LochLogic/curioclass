# Pine Cove and the day 24 classroom cards (day 24)

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Day 24 retrieval: our classroom, two dates

**Picture, dated 1998:** A classroom with desks in rows, a chalkboard and a globe on a shelf.
**Picture, dated 2018:** The same classroom with desks in groups, a wall screen and the same globe on its shelf.
**Say:** "Which is older? Read the date."

## Pine Cove, 1985 (pairs work)

**Picture:** A small sandy beach in a cove, a boat ramp, a wooden fence along the top of the beach and a shed.

## Pine Cove, 2010

**Picture:** The same cove. The beach is wide, and the water's edge is farther out. The boat ramp, the fence and the shed are where they were.

**Reason card:** Storms carried sand into the cove.

## Five label cards

The beach. The edge of the water. The boat ramp. The fence. The shed.

## Key (teacher)

Changed: the beach (wider) and the edge of the water (farther out). Stayed: the boat ramp, the fence and the shed. Why: the reason card. The two pictures alone do not say why. The classroom pair is for retrieval only: the 1998 card is older, the 2018 card is nearer to today.
