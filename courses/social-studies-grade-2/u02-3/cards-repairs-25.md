# Repair cards, day 25

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

## Part 1: physical and cultural cards (error G)

**Picture:** Eight small cards and two bins. Physical: a river bends and cuts a new bank; a hill covered by snow in winter; a stream widens after rain; a lake edge with new reeds. Cultural: a new school with a red roof; a new road with a lamp post; a fence round a garden; a bakery with a new sign. Each card has a date in its corner. Two bins: physical (land, water, living things), cultural (made by people).

## Part 2: picture beside reason (error H)

**Picture:** A picture card of a river with a cut bank in the second panel, beside a reason card that reads A flood cut into the bank. A third card reads The card does not say.
**Say:** "The pictures show what changed; the reason card tells why." If there is no reason card, say "The card does not say."
