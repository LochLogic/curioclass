# Section 2.3 opener: what changes over time

## What the section does

Two days, 24 and 25, of about 40 minutes. The class looks at a place at two dates and says what changed, what stayed the same and, if the source says, why. On day 24 the place is a glacier in the Alps of Europe, seen in two satellite images, and an invented lake, Pine Cove. On day 25 it is an invented street, Harbor Street in Stone Landing, in North America. Children sort changes into physical (land, water, plants) and cultural (what people built or made), and each child learns to read the date and say which picture is nearer to today. A twelve-minute supported-work block each day hears about six children at the globe while the rest revise their maps.

## How the days run

Day 24: a repair, grade 1 retrieval on dates, Aletsch at two dates, a then-and-now chart with Pine Cove, supported work, the exit. Day 25: two repairs, Harbor Street, a chart for the street pair, supported work, the exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Two dates, two pictures.** What changed and what stayed are read from the pictures; why it changed is read from the printed reason, not from the picture.
- **Physical change** is a change in land, water or living things. **Cultural change** is something people built or made. A place usually holds both.
- **A date says how old the picture is.** An old picture does not show the place today.
- **Aletsch** is a real glacier in Europe. The two satellite images are federal and credited. The warmer summers in the Alps are the printed reason, told at grade level without alarm.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once with the right idea beside it. Everyone near the sea fishes (F, repaired day 24). The two glaciers show cultural change too (G). The picture proves why the ice shrank (H). An old picture shows the place today (N, repaired day 26).

## Supplies for the section

The Aletsch sheet and reason card (from section 2.1), the Pine Cove card, the Harbor Street pair, then-and-now charts, repair cards, exit cards (Change Pair A, kept back until the day), the globe, the record sheet and the question board. Every invented place and date is printed "invented for practice".
