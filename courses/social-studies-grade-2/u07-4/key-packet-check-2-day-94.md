# Teacher key: Check 2 packet, day 94: two accounts of one flood (kept back until day 94)

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (packet-check-2-day-94.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Both say: the water reached the second fence post by noon. Only the Letter: Dessa stood on the bridge at dawn and watched the river climb. Differences: two days of rain and three; nine boats and eleven. Own way of knowing: Dessa yes, she watched; Joss no, a neighbor told him.
