# Repair and fresh task cards, day 96

Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Never from the check packets. Read every card aloud once.

## Group 1: two accounts (errors N, O, P)

**Hill Fair notes (repair):** Two short notes. Note 1: It rained after lunch. Eight children ran for the barn. Note 2, told to its writer by a friend: It rained before lunch. Eight children ran for the barn. Pairs find the detail both say, then say how each writer knew.
**Orchard Gap notes (fresh task):** Two notes about a market-day wind. The first note, by a stallholder who stood at the gap: The wind took three awnings. The second note, by a visitor told it by a friend: The wind took three awnings and a cart.

## Group 2: a move (errors Q, R)

**Ice Road card (repair):** Three pictures: a frozen pond, a stopped ferry, a farmer carrying cheese over the ice. Pairs lay them first and next.

## Group 3: a claim with two facts (errors S, T)

**Larch Harbor Card (fresh task):** A harbor with many boats at a long dock, a crane, and a sign for a fish market. **Caption, read aloud:** Larch Harbor is a place made up for practice. Made by Curio Class.
**Pip's claim (repair):** The harbor is busy because it is good. Pairs say what is missing.

## Group 4: a label and a memory (errors U, V)

**Rice Label (repair):** Rice from the wet river fields of Marlowe, sold in Tolliver, a dry land where rice does not grow.
**Drum Memory (repair):** I brought my aunt's drum. Pairs say what the newcomer brought.

## Written move strips (one for each group)

Group 1: underline what both notes say; say how each writer knew. Group 2: lay the pictures first and next; say the cause. Group 3: say the frame with two fact cards: I think ___ because ___ and ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___. Group 4: say why Tolliver trades for rice; say what the drum added.

## Key (teacher)

Hill Fair: eight children ran for the barn; note 2 was told. Orchard Gap: three awnings. Ice Road: the pond froze, then the ferry stopped, then the farmer carried cheese. Larch Harbor: many boats at the dock; the card is named, made by Curio Class. Rice: it does not grow in dry Tolliver. Drum: a drum. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
