# Day 94: Two accounts of one flood

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 93's cells carry over, no repair: read error B (s07-X93) and error D off the sheet, and mark s07-P93-3 after class; hear each on day 96. Open the check 2 record sheet: one row a child, a column for each item 1 to 13, a heard column. Cover every model. Name three groups of six from children not yet heard: group 1 for item 2, group 2 for item 3, group 3 for item 5. Key: key-packet-check-2-day-94.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say: "Today I find out what you remember about comparing two accounts. I will not help you." Say: "These accounts are made up." Hand out the packet, student page and pencils. Any child may pass. A scribe writes a child's own words.

#### 6 min. Read the accounts

Read the Letter aloud once and the Notice aloud once, neutrally. Read both again only if asked. Discuss no answer.

#### 7 min. Items 1 and 2

Read each ask once: [[sc2-E1]] [[sc2-E2]] Everyone writes or draws a few words for each on the student page. Group 1 comes to you for [[sc2-E5]], one ask each, 45 seconds and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes; the rest keep writing.

#### 6 min. Item 3

Read: [[sc2-E3]] Everyone writes a few words. Group 2 comes to you for [[sc2-E5]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes; the rest finish earlier lines.

#### 7 min. Items 4 and 5

Read: [[sc2-E4]] Everyone circles yes or no on the student page. Group 3 comes to you for [[sc2-E5]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes.

#### 3 min. Hand in

Collect every packet and page. Three groups hear 18 children, one ask each, 15 of the 32 minutes. A child past 18 is marked on paper only and is heard first on day 96, Fresh task. After class mark each item shown, shown with help or not yet seen, never averaged. A part neither marked nor heard is not yet seen. Sort errors for day 96, group 1: items 1 to 5 (errors N, O and P).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: read two short notes aloud that you make up about one rainy day, one written by someone who was there and one by someone who was told.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard column.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say the accounts are made up. Read two accounts of one flood aloud once each. Your child says a detail both say, a detail only one says, a place they differ and whether each teller saw it themselves. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What do both accounts say? Did the writer see it?" Expect: a shared detail; yes if the writer was there, no if the writer was told.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child picks one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: items 1, 3 and 5 only; the rest wait for day 96. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: account, both, differ, teller, saw it, was told. Frame: "Both say ___. The Letter says ___, and the Notice says ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe count.

### Tomorrow

Day 95 is the second part of this check: a source set about a place, a move, a claim, a label and a memory. Anything from today stays on the record sheet for day 96.
