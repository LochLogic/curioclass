# Day 93 fresh cards for the four groups

One fresh case for each group, printed "invented for practice". Not exits. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

- **Group 1 (items 1 to 5: C, H, G).** A report card with only pictures of animals; a fact with no sticker; a small map of Lindenhurst (invented) with a key: a flag is a library, a bar is a bridge. Sort three report parts, add a sticker, find the bridge by the key.
- **Group 2 (items 6 to 13: A, B).** Two questions for Birchwood (invented): "What do people grow?" and "How many doors are on the bus?" Two sources: a map by Curio Class and a visitor's note. Link a question to the big one; find the maker then the helping part.
- **Group 3 (items 14 to 19: D, F).** Two small facts: "A clinic stands by the square." "A bus leaves hourly." Link them to a claim with "because"; the child directs their own entry on the display sheet.
- **Group 4 (resource and bounded claim: I, E).** Sort a field of reeds and a mat made from them; a caption "Many families go to the fair" beside "Everyone goes to the fair": say what the page supports.
