# Day 96: Try again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort days 94 and 95 into four groups from the check 2 record sheet, by the part with the largest gap, at most about eight children each: two accounts (errors N, O, P); a move (errors Q and R); a claim with two facts (errors S and T); a label and a memory (errors U and V). Lay out each group's cards. Say aloud that every card is made up. Today's six: the children least heard. No one is held up.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return

Hand back day 94's assessment and day 95's assessment with their marks, shown, shown with help or not yet seen, by group. Name the four groups by their cards. Nobody is named for a miss.

#### 10 min. Teaching by group

Meet each group 2 to 3 minutes, the largest gap first; the others work in pairs from the written move. Group 1 (errors N, O and P): on the Hill Fair notes, **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-1]] Expect: eight children ran for the barn. Then **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-2]] Expect: no. Group 2 (errors Q and R): on the Ice Road card, **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-3]] Expect: the pond froze, so they carried cheese. Group 3 (errors S and T): Pip says: "The harbor is busy because it is good." **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-4]] Expect: two facts and the cards. Group 4 (errors U and V): on the Rice Label, **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-5]] Expect: rice does not grow in dry Tolliver; then on the Drum Memory, **Ask.** [[sc2-P96-6]] Expect: a drum. Each pair then does its written move strip ([[sc2-P96-8]]).

#### 10 min. Fresh task

Group 1 works the Orchard Gap notes and group 3 the Larch Harbor Card in pairs; everyone circles for [[sc2-X96a]]. First you hear any child not yet heard on day 94 or 95, their own ask, then today's six for [[sc2-X96b]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes for six; a child still not heard stays not yet seen on the record; groups 2 and 4 work their pair cards meanwhile. Mark each on the record sheet: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Anything still not shown goes on the record.

#### 7 min. Feedback and the record

Children mark one box in each row of the self-check strip, then point to one piece of work that shows a mark, 4 minutes. While they work, write one line of feedback on each fresh task, about 10 seconds each. The strips and fresh tasks stay with the students in their folders. Then copy the record sheet's marks onto the page that goes to the grade 3 teacher ([[sc2-P96-7]]), after class.

#### 2 min. Close

Say what each group changed. This is the last day of the course.

### Other rooms

No printed cards: draw each card on the board and use lined paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: make up two short notes about one windy day on scrap paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the strip.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at days 94 and 95 together; your child picks one part to try again.
  **Say.** "What is missing from the claim?" Expect: two facts and the cards they came from.
- **15 min. Try again.** Your child tries that part again from the written move.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[sc2-X96a]] [[sc2-X96b]] Your child tries the card they were given, answers aloud and says one thing they changed.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fresh card for each group. Longer: a child tells a second claim from the same card.

### Words and supports

Words: try again, change, account, teller, cause, effect, claim, card, maker. Frame: "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

This is the last day of the course. The record, with the marks for every part, goes to the grade 3 teacher.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
