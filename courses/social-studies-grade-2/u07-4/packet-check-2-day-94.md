# Check 2 packet, day 94: two accounts of one flood (kept back until day 94)

Nothing here is taught before day 94. Read each account aloud once, then again if asked. Say first: "These accounts are made up." Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Every completed model in the room is covered.

## Account 1: the Letter

**Picture:** A bridge over a river and a woman standing on it. Read aloud: Dessa Orr kept the footbridge at Sallow Ford, a town made up for practice. She writes: It rained for two days. At dawn I stood on the bridge and watched the river climb. By noon the water reached the second fence post. I helped nine boats up the hill.

## Account 2: the Notice

**Picture:** A printed town notice and a desk. Read aloud: Joss Harrow, the town clerk, wrote this notice for the Sallow Ford paper. He was upriver that week. He writes: A neighbor told me it rained for three days. The water reached the second fence post by noon. Eleven boats were taken up the hill.

Names down the side; a column for each item 1 to 13; a heard column; a line at the foot for the page that goes to the grade 3 teacher. Use the one scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A part neither marked on paper nor heard is not yet seen and goes to day 96.
