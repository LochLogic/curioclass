# Teacher key: Check 2 packet, day 95: the Brannock set (kept back until day 95)

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (packet-check-2-day-95.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Cause: two dry summers, so the crops failed. Effect: the cannery got workers, or a second school room opened. A claim: Dellmouth grew because a cannery needed workers (Move Card) and more boats tied up at the dock (Ledger Card). The maker of every card is Curio Class. Trade: coffee does not grow in Brannock and Sennor grows it. Brought: a wool shawl, a recipe for bean bread, a fiddle. Added: bean bread at the supper, music at the dance.
