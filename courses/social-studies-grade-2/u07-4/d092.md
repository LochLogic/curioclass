# Day 92: End-of-unit check, a country up close

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 91 record: error H. Lay out each child's kept report, notes, map, spoken and digital records from days 85 to 91, newest versions first; first tries were practice. Set one sheet and a pencil at each place. Say aloud that Postcard P is made up. Show no timer. Any child may pass; no one is asked about home or family.

### The day

#### 5 min. Opening

Read the sheet aloud once, neutrally, a source strip first and then each step one ask at a time (packet-assessment.md). Do not teach, hint or confirm. If any of day 91's six were not heard, hear them first, 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, with the day 91 six question (s07-X91b); the day then runs 35 minutes and the rest look quietly at their kept report.

#### 8 min. Item 2

Individual work, no timer shown. Children mark with a pencil; the marks are their pointing. Item 2, error A: [[s07-E6]] [[s07-E7]] [[s07-E8]] [[s07-E9]] The line may be a few written words or dictated.

#### 8 min. Item 3

Item 3, error B: [[s07-E10]] [[s07-E11]] [[s07-E12]] [[s07-E13]] A child who needs it hears a stem again, never the answer.

#### 4 min. Spare

For a dictated line: at most four children who cannot mark say it to you, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet. Items 1 and 4 need no sitting minutes; score them after class from the kept work, the latest versions after feedback and revision. A part with no kept record is not yet seen.
- **Item 1** (errors C, E, G, H, I): [[s07-E1]] [[s07-E2]] [[s07-E3]] [[s07-E4]] [[s07-E5]] from the report page, its three boxes and stickers, and the map with its key.
- **Item 4** (errors D, F): [[s07-E14]] [[s07-E15]] [[s07-E16]] [[s07-E17]] [[s07-E18]] [[s07-E19]] from the report and the final spoken and digital records of day 91. Watching another child's turn is not a turn.

#### 5 min. Reflection

Each child marks the picture of the one part to look at again: the map, a resource and work fact, a people today fact, or my claim. Not scored. Collect the sheets.

### Other rooms

One adult: items 1 and 4 are scored the same way. No printer: read each page and card from the front; children answer on lined paper. No device: the digital records read not yet seen.

### At home

**Swap.** No sheet: write Page I and Postcard P on paper.

**Skip.** Anything that tires the child.

- **20 min. The assessment.** Read the page and the card aloud once, then each step one at a time. [[s07-E6]] [[s07-E7]] [[s07-E8]] [[s07-E9]] [[s07-E10]] [[s07-E11]] [[s07-E12]] [[s07-E13]] **Say.** "What do those two answers help us decide?" Expect: why fish work and city life matter there.
- **5 min. Why.** **Say.** "Why this source?" Expect: who made it and the part that helps.
- **5 min. Look.** Look at their report together; say the claim and two facts.

### Shorter or longer

No shortening: the check keeps all items. A child who needs more time takes the four spare minutes.

### Words and supports

Words: fact, source, maker, question, claim. Frames: "The ___ says ___." "I choose ___ because ___ made it." Read every stem aloud; accept pointing and a dictated line.

### Tomorrow

Day 93 hands back one written note to each child, meets the four largest groups for what the check showed, and ends with a short fresh task about a made-up place. Items 1 to 19 are marked on the record sheet tonight: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Credit
