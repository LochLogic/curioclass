# Exit cards, days 91 and 93

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other ask is spoken and heard from the rotating six. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. Day 92 has no exit ticket. Exit and check cards are first seen at the child's own turn. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 91 exit card: listener questions

**Three pictured questions:** "Where did that fact come from?"; "What color is your paper?"; "How long was your talk?"
**Mark:** Put a token on the question that helps make a report stronger.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say how that question helps the report."

## Day 93 exit card: garden record

**Record (made up by a school garden club):** "People grow pears here. A weekly stall sells the pears." Two parts: a source line "Made by the Garden Club, 2026" and a flower picture.
**Mark:** Put a token on the part that tells who made the record.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say a claim about what matters in this place, with two facts."
