# Day 92 sheet: the unseen page and the postcard (kept back until day 92)

Read the whole sheet aloud once, neutrally, a source strip first and then each step one ask at a time. Do not teach, hint or confirm. One copy for each child, laid flat, one pencil each; large-print copies on request. No timer is shown. Exit and check cards are first seen at the child's own turn. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Page I

**Title:** Work with fish in Iceland
**Illustration (labelled "illustration, not a photograph"):** a small drawing of a fish and a boat.
**Heading:** Catching, preparing and selling
**Text:** "People in Iceland catch fish. Other people prepare fish for sale. Iceland sells fish products to other countries."
**Second heading:** People today
**Text:** "Most people in Iceland live in or near the capital, Reykjavík."
**Source strip:** "Written by Curio Class in 2026 from two pages made by Statistics Iceland, Iceland's national statistics office in Reykjavík: its Fisheries page (page date not stated) and its news release on the population on 1 January 2024 (21 March 2024). The export figures on the Fisheries page come from customs declarations."

## Postcard P (invented for practice)

**Heading:** Scenery. **Picture:** a small mountain drawing.
**Text:** "I visited Iceland. I liked looking at the mountains best."
**Source strip:** "Invented visitor's postcard, made for this task by Curio Class in 2026; no real sender or place of writing."

## Sheet, item 2 (Page I only)

Three supporting-question cards: "What land and water are here?" "What resources support people's work?" "How do people live today, and what varies?"
1. Underline two facts on Page I.
2. Put a token on the card the first underlined fact answers.
3. Put a token on the card the second underlined fact answers.
4. Write or tell one line: How could those answers help us decide what matters about life in Iceland?

## Sheet, item 3 (Page I and Postcard P)

For our work question, "What resources support people's work?":
1. Circle Page I or Postcard P.
2. Point to who made the one you circled.
3. Circle one heading or feature on it that helps.
4. Underline two facts on it that answer the work question.

## Reflection (not scored)

Mark the picture of the part you want to look at again: the land and water map; a resource and work fact; a people today fact; my claim.

## TEACHER ONLY: day 92 key (detach before copying; never printed on a child's sheet)

Item 2: two facts, one from "Catching, preparing and selling" (people catch fish; other people prepare fish; Iceland sells fish products) and one from "People today" (most people live in or near Reykjavík). The work fact answers the resources and work card; the Reykjavík fact answers the people today card. A fact may answer more than one card if the child explains. The line: both answers help us see that fish work and city life matter there. "Both answers are Iceland" is A.

A helping part: the heading "Catching, preparing and selling" or the source strip. Two relevant facts: fish catching and fish preparing or selling. Choosing the postcard for its picture, or underlining the mountain line, is B. Score the stated maker-and-feature reason; the facts count toward relevance only.
