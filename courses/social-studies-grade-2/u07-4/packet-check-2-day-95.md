# Check 2 packet, day 95: the Brannock set (kept back until day 95)

Nothing here is taught before day 95. Read each card aloud once, then again if asked. Say first: "These cards are made up." Every card prints "invented for practice" and "Made by Curio Class" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Every completed model in the room is covered.

## Card 1: Brannock Page

**Picture:** A rough map of Brannock with a coast, cold hills and a port town, Dellmouth. Read aloud: Brannock is a country made up for practice. Its hills are cold and dry, and coffee does not grow there. Its port town is Dellmouth.

## Card 2: Move Card

**Picture:** Three small pictures: dry fields, a cart on a road, a cannery. Read aloud: Two summers were dry in the Brannock hills. The crops failed. Farm families left the hills and came to Dellmouth, on the coast. A cannery in Dellmouth needed workers. The families took jobs there. The town opened a second school room.

## Card 3: Ledger Card

**Picture:** A page of marks and two boat pictures. Read aloud: Dellmouth Dock Ledger. Boats that tied up at the Dellmouth dock in one year: in the first year, twelve. In a later year, thirty-one.

## Card 4: Sennor Coffee Label

**Picture:** A coffee tin label. Read aloud: Sennor Highland Coffee. Grown and packed in Sennor. Sold in Brannock.

## Card 5: Memory Card

**Picture:** A woman with a shawl, a bag and a fiddle. Read aloud: Ilsa Brandt remembers. I came to Dellmouth from the hill farms when I was nine. In my bag I carried my mother's wool shawl, her recipe for bean bread and my cousin's fiddle. At the first town supper I baked the bean bread, and people asked for more. Later my fiddle played at the dance.
