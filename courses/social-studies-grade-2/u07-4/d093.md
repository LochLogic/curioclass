# Day 93: Try the hard part again

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark items 1 to 19 on the record sheet; order the four groups, most missed first. Say aloud that the fresh cards are made up. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Feedback

Hand back one written note to each child from day 92's assessment (items 1 to 19): the part to fix. [[s07-R93-1]] Each child says the part to try again.

#### 15 min. Return and repair, groups

Four groups by component, about 3.5 minutes each (4, 4, 4 and 3), most missed first; skip a group with no misses. At most four children take a 45-second turn, one ask each, 5 seconds to swap; the rest point. The rest work in pairs from the written move strip.
- **Group 1, items 1 to 5 (errors C, G, H; s07-E1).** [[s07-P93-1]] [[s07-P93-2]] [[s07-P93-3]] Pip says error H: "Where a fact came from does not matter." Say beside it: the sticker names the page and maker.
- **Group 2, items 6 to 13 (errors A, B; s07-E6).** [[s07-P93-4]] [[s07-P93-5]] Pip says error B: "The visitor's note is nicest." Say beside it: find who made it, then the part that helps.
- **Group 3, items 14 to 19 (errors D, F; s07-E14).** Use the child's own report, not a card. [[s07-P93-6]] [[s07-P93-7]] Pip says error D: "A bus leaves hourly. That is my claim." Say beside it: "I think ___ because ___."
- **Group 4, a resource and a bounded claim (errors I, E; s07-E2).** [[s07-P93-8]] [[s07-P93-9]] Pip says error E: "Everyone goes to the fair."

#### 8 min. Fresh task

Show the garden record, invented for practice, now, not before. Read: "Put a token on the part that tells who made the record." [[s07-X93]] About 1 minute. Then each child says a claim with two facts to a partner and records it with the claim frame. Hear the first six during this, one ask each, 45 seconds and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes: [[s07-X93b]] Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Reflection

Each child marks the picture of one thing that went well. Not scored.

### Other rooms

Write each fresh card on a slip and read it aloud. No device: the child says the entry aloud and points to the display sheet.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write each fresh card on paper.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **10 min. Look back.** Look at the note. **Say.** "Which part do you want to try again?" Expect: one part, such as the claim or the label. [[s07-R93-1]]
- **15 min. A fresh try.** [[s07-P93-6]] **Say.** "Which two facts back your claim?" Expect: two facts from your report, each with its page.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X93]] [[s07-X93b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: meet only the two most-missed groups; never the claim and its two facts. Longer: what fact would weaken the claim?

### Words and supports

Words: fix, maker, question, claim, fact. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "I choose ___ because ___ made it." Pointing and saying count.

### Tomorrow

One fact given as a claim (error D) or the nicest picture chosen as the source (error B) goes on the record sheet. Unit 7 ends today. Day 94 is the course-level check: two short accounts not seen before of one event; what both say, where they differ, whether each teller had their own way of knowing.

### Credit

Ours. Every fresh card and the garden record are made up.
