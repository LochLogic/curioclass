# Day 95: A place, a move and a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 94's assessment is marked after class; its marks stay on the sheet and go back on day 96. Open the check 2 record sheet at its item 6 to 13 columns and cover every model. Name four groups of six from children not yet heard: group 1 for item 7, group 2 for item 8, group 3 for item 11, group 4 for item 13. Say aloud that the place is made up and is not a country the class studied. Key: key-packet-check-2-day-95.md.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Say: "Today I find out what you remember about places, moves and claims. I will not help you." Say: "These cards are made up." Hand out the packet, student page and pencils. Any child may pass. A scribe writes a child's own words.

#### 7 min. The Move Card

Read the Brannock Page and the Move Card aloud once. Read each ask once: [[sc2-E6]] [[sc2-E7]] Everyone writes or draws a few words on the student page. Group 1 comes to you for [[sc2-E7]], one ask each, 45 seconds and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes.

#### 8 min. The claim

Read the Ledger Card aloud once. Read each ask once: [[sc2-E8]] [[sc2-E9]] Everyone writes a few words. Group 2 comes to you for [[sc2-E10]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes.

#### 5 min. The label

Read the Sennor Coffee Label once. [[sc2-E11]] Group 3 comes to you, 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes; the rest finish earlier lines.

#### 6 min. The memory

Read the Memory Card aloud once. Read each ask once: [[sc2-E12]] [[sc2-E13]] Everyone writes a few words for [[sc2-E12]]. Group 4 comes to you for [[sc2-E13]], 45 seconds each and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes.

#### 3 min. Hand in

Collect every packet and page. Four groups hear 24 children, one ask each, 20 of the 32 minutes. A child past 24 is marked on paper only and is heard first on day 96, Fresh task. After class mark each item shown, shown with help or not yet seen, never averaged. Sort errors for day 96: items 6 and 7 (errors Q and R); items 8 to 10 (errors S and T); items 11 to 13 (errors U and V).

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: make up a short note about a family moving, a coffee tin and a newcomer's story, and read each aloud once.

**Skip.** The groups and the heard column.

- **25 min. The assessment.** Say the place is made up. Read a short card about a move aloud and ask what made it happen and what it caused. Ask for a claim with two facts and the cards they came from, why a country gets a product from elsewhere, and what a newcomer brought. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What made the families leave? What did the move cause? What did the newcomer bring?" Expect: a cause on the card; an effect on the card; a thing the memory names.
- **10 min. Respond.** Your child picks one part to try again.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the move card, the claim and the label only; the memory waits for day 96. Longer: none; this is a check.

### Words and supports

Words: move, cause, effect, claim, fact, trade, label, newcomer. Frame: "I think ___ because ___ and ___. The ___ and the ___ show it, made by ___." Pointing, drawing and a scribe count.

### Tomorrow

Day 96 hands back days 94 and 95 in four groups: two accounts (errors N, O, P); a move (errors Q and R); a claim with two facts (errors S and T); a label and a memory (errors U and V). Each group meets you for 2 to 3 minutes, then tries a fresh card.
