# Section 7.4 opener: showings, the end-of-unit assessment, and the response

## What the section does

Three days, 91 to 93, the course's last unit. Day 91: each child shows their report, about thirty seconds each (claim, two facts, any display correction the child directs, then one listener question), in a fixed circle order with a record column for who was heard. Two circles run at once: the teacher at one, a second adult or older helper at the other. A class over twenty-four sends the extra showings to day 90's practice. Day 92 is the unit's check: a printed sheet on an unseen source page (items 2 and 3, about eight minutes each), while items 1 and 4 are scored after class from the retained report, notes, map, spoken and digital versions. Day 93 responds: written notes, four small groups of about four minutes, then a fresh task. This is the course's last day of teaching unit work; nothing hands on from it except the course-level check on day 94.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Score the latest versions.** Items 1 and 4 use work after written feedback and revision; first attempts are practice.
- **A claim answers the big question.** Different claims can fit the evidence; none is ranked.
- **A source is chosen by its maker and a helping part**, not by color.
- **Read the page and the strip aloud neutrally, once.** Never hint, confirm or teach during the sitting.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says: I will ask what color the paper is (H, day 91); the flower picture is the nicest so I choose it (B, day 93); here are my pears, that is my claim (D, day 93). A child who needs help with an earlier idea meets it in one of four groups on day 93.

## What stays held back

The unseen page, the postcard and the sheet print only on day 92. The garden record prints only on day 93.
