# Day 91: Present the reports

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 90 record: error F. Set the showing order sheet with two circles in a fixed order and a Heard column. Brief the second adult: one question to a showing, no judgments; display sheet until the offline display exists. No child must say why they chose a country. Any child may pass and show on another day.

### The day

#### 3 min. Note cards

Hand back the note card from day 90 with the labels (s07-R90-1) and finished report (s07-P90-2). Each child reads the card, or hears it, and says the one thing to try next. [[s07-R91-2]]

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Error F from day 90 (s07-X90): [[s07-R91-1]] Show the card, invented for practice. Pip says: "I watched Mo's screen, so I did digital." Say beside it: "I said my own claim and two facts and checked my own screen." Each child's own correction is directed during their showing; any other letter goes to a small group of at most 6 at the end of the day.

#### 3 min. Listener question

Review: you learned on day 90 that a listener asks "Which page says that?" and says a question, never a judgment.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K91-1]]
Circle rules: one question to a showing.

#### 19 min. Showings

[[s07-P91-1]] Two circles of about 15 at once, in the order on the sheet. Each child shows the report: the claim, the two facts with their pages, any correction they direct on their own entry, then one listener question. About 1 minute each with the swap, 15 in the circle, 15 minutes; the seven in each circle who carry a turn from days 89 and 90 and direct their entry take about 30 seconds more, so about 19 minutes in all. Tick Heard and record claim, facts, display and question: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Keep the final spoken and digital versions; the fourteen with no day 89 or 90 turn direct their entry here.

#### 2 min. Transitions

Move to the six and hand out the exit cards.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Read: "Put a token on the question that helps make a report stronger." [[s07-X91]] "Where did that fact come from?" is right; the other two are error H.

#### 5 min. The six heard

The rotating six, one ask each, 45 seconds and 5 to swap, about 5 minutes: [[s07-X91b]] The rest draw the part of their report they will check tomorrow. If the time runs out, hear five and name the sixth on the sheet.

### Other rooms

One adult: run one circle of up to 24 showings, about 40 seconds each; name the rest on the sheet and hear up to six on day 92. No device: show from paper and record digital as not yet seen. One projector: show the report page of the child showing.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: show the paper report and the display sheet.

**Skip.** The repair.

- **5 min. Note card.** Look at the card and name one thing to try. [[s07-R91-2]]
- **15 min. Show it.** Show your report to the grown-up: the claim, two facts and their pages. [[s07-P91-1]] **Say.** "Which page says that?" Expect: the page and who made it.
- **5 min. The question.** [[s07-K91-1]] **Say.** "What do we ask a showing?" Expect: Which page says that?
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X91]] [[s07-X91b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: show the claim and one fact; never the label or question. Longer: what fact would weaken the claim?

### Words and supports

Words: show, listener, question, claim, label. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ says ___." Pointing counts; any language the teacher can record counts.

### Tomorrow

Error H (a question that does not ask where a fact came from) goes on the record sheet. Day 92 takes no repair: it is the end-of-unit check, a printed sheet on a page and a card not seen before, read aloud once, plus the report scored from your kept work. Any of today's six not heard are heard first.
