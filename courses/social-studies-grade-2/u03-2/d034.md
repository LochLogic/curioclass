# Day 34: Reading advice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fifth six on the record sheet. Published advice is a page by a town or state office that says what to do. If your town or state has a flood page, make Card A from at most three of its lines and name the page; if not, use Advice Card A and say it is another state's. Ask the principal which ways the school allows. Keep Card C back.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 33's exit (error D), show Creek Road. Pip says: "Only buildings get hurt in a flood." The class fixes it ([[s03-P34-1]]). If few missed it, the small group does this later.

#### 3 min. Cue return

Hand back day 33's written task, the explanations, with their cues. Each child adds or says one thing ([[s03-P34-2]]; it stays in the folder).

#### 9 min. Reading published advice

Review: in unit 2 you said "The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Show Advice Card A; read its lines and source line. Ask the three questions: who made the page ([[s03-K34-4]]), when ([[s03-K34-5]]), where ([[s03-K34-6]]). Model the check with the message "Stay off roads that are closed": read a line; the class holds up a thumb if it says the same or turns it down ([[s03-K34-2]], line 1; [[s03-K34-1]], line 3). Each child writes the card letter and line number under the message ([[s03-K34-3]]; it stays in the folder). Pip's rule: "Clap three times when the water rises. It is a good rule because we say so" ([[s03-F34-1]], error K). Say beside it: a rule is checked when a named card says it. 

#### 6 min. Ways to help

The class names ways to help the school get ready ([[s03-P34-3]]). Sort them onto at most three option cards the school allows, such as a poster or an announcement. Set aside, kindly, a way the school cannot allow. The class picks the cause.

#### 12 min. Supported work

First 4 minutes, everyone: read the Recovery Card 2 caption aloud once. Each child draws or labels one effect on people ([[s03-P34-4]]), one help ([[s03-P34-5]]) and the card's name ([[s03-P34-6]]). Collect for feedback; day 35 hands them back. While they draw, call the fifth six one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X34b]]. Then the small group (5 minutes) takes error D from day 33's six with the Creek Road card. The others, in pairs, fix Pip's glued messages on Card B ([[s03-P34-7]]; it stays in the folder); the lawn chairs message has no line.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card and read aloud: "Circle the poster that was checked against an Advice Card" ([[s03-X34]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A circled Poster 2 or 3 is error K.

### Other rooms

No printer: write Card A on the board; children use their own paper for the explanation.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: use a flood page from your own town or state and read three lines.

**Skip.** The small group and the options.

- **10 min. Advice.** Read Advice Card A aloud.
  **Say.** "Which line says the same as: Stay off roads that are closed?" Expect: the third line, from Card A.
- **15 min. Explanation.** [[s03-P34-4]] [[s03-P34-5]] [[s03-P34-6]] Your child draws one effect and one help and names the card.
  **Say.** "How do you know it is on the card?" Expect: the card names it.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X34]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X34b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip ways to help; day 35 starts them. Longer: a child checks one more message against Card A.

### Words and supports

Words: advice, card, line, message, check, source. Frames: "The card says ___." "The ___ shows ___ (an effect on people). It shows ___ (a help)." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled Poster 2 or 3 on today's exit (error K), day 35 opens with three message cards, a say-so, a liking and a checked line. Each child's explanation comes back with one line of written feedback. Day 35 checks each option against Cards A and B and votes.
