# Advice Cards C and D (exit only)

Kept back. Print Advice Card C on the day 34 exit and Advice Card D on the day 35 exit, and not before. Made by Curio Class in plain words from the same public page. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Advice Card C

**Lines:**
1. Do not walk through floodwater.
2. Stay away from fallen electricity lines.
3. Do not eat or drink anything that touched floodwater.
**Source line:** Vermont Department of Health, Stay Safe in a Flood, last updated October 29, 2026, healthvermont.gov/emergency/public-health-preparedness/stay-safe-flood. Made by Curio Class from this page.

## Advice Card D

**Lines:**
1. Do not drive around barriers.
2. Listen to public safety officials.
3. Stay tuned to the radio or television for updates.
4. Fill bathtubs, sinks and jugs with clean water.
**Source line:** Same page and date as Advice Card C. Made by Curio Class from this page.

## Key (teacher)

Day 34: the third line of Card C is the one the first poster uses. Day 35: the second line of Card D is the one Room 4's message says. A line on a nearby topic, such as the third line of Card D, is the wrong example. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
