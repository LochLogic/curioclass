# Exit keys, days 34 to 36 (teacher only)

For the teacher only. Never print this page for children; the exit cards carry no answers.

Day 34: Poster 1; the third line of Card C. Day 35: the second line of Card D; whether people read it. Day 36: the job picture the child was seen doing, with a line that says that job; the slips in the Response Box, or whether people stopped to look. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
