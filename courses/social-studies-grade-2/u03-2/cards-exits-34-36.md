# Exit cards, days 34 to 36

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print only on the exit of their day.

## Day 34 exit card: School Message Case

**Picture:** Three posters from another class, side by side.
**Poster 1:** Do not eat or drink what floodwater touched. Under it: Advice Card C.
**Poster 2:** Wear yellow boots on rainy days. Under it: Mr. Ray told me this rule.
**Poster 3:** Count to ten before you leave the room. Under it: We all think this is a good rule.
**Mark:** Circle the poster that was checked against an Advice Card.
**Spoken:** With Advice Card C in front of the child: "Point to the line on Advice Card C that says the same as the first poster."

## Day 35 exit card: Room 4's Message Card

**Picture:** A card with the words Room 4's message: Listen to the safety officials, and a drawing of a helper in a vest.
**Mark:** With Advice Card D in front of the child: "Circle the line on Advice Card D that says the same as Room 4's message."
**Spoken:** "Our poster is hung. What could we check later to see whether it worked?"

## Day 36 exit

**Mark:** On the class Action Record, each child circles the picture of the job they did, then finishes the line: I helped by ___. The teacher marks it against the job seen; a child with no circle, or a job not done, is not yet seen.
**Spoken:** "Our message is up. What could we check in two weeks to see if people read it?"
