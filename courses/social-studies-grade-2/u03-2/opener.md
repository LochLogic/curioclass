# Section 3.2 opener: checking advice and acting through the school

## What the section does

Three days, 34 to 36, of 36 to 40 minutes. The class reads published advice about floods, learns to check a message against a line of the advice, chooses one way to help the school get ready, votes fairly and carries the choice out through the school. Day 34 teaches the check: say a message, find the line that says the same, name the card. Day 35 applies it: each option is checked against the advice lines, then the class votes. Day 36 carries out the plan, with a job for every child, and starts the Action Record. The course picks no cause: the class chooses among ways the school allows. No safety rule is ever made up; every message uses a line of an Advice Card.

## How the days run

Day 34: a repair, the cue back on day 33's explanation, reading advice, ways to help, a first explanation of Recovery Card 2, the exit. Day 35: a repair, the fair vote steps, checking each option, the vote, a revised explanation, the exit. Day 36: a repair, jobs, making or rehearsing, doing it, the revised explanation completed, the exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Published advice** is a page by a town, state or national office that says what to do. The class's own town or state page replaces our cards when the teacher finds it.
- **A message is checked** when a line of a named card says the same.
- **A vote chooses; it does not show the action worked.** What worked is checked later, from the slips in the Response Box (day 42).
- **Two feedback loops** run: a cue back on day 33's explanation on day 34, and one line of written feedback on day 34's explanation before day 35. About 20 minutes each evening for 30 children.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: our rule is good because we say so (K, day 34); we voted, so our message worked (E, day 35). D from day 33 (only buildings get hurt) is repaired on day 34 with a Creek Road card.

## Supplies for the section

Advice Cards A and B (taught), C and D (exit only, kept back), the Recovery Card 2 caption, option cards, message cards, Pip's made-up rule card, repair cards, the exit cards, paper cups and 30 counters, the Action Record, the Response Box with picture slips, job pictures, poster paper and markers, the explanation sheet, the record sheet and the question board. At home, one child and one adult may compare two ways to tell the household about a flood and deliver a message in the card's own words; nothing is sent back and nothing about the home is reported.
