# Option cards and message cards, days 34 to 36

Plain black line drawing on white. Every card is ours.

## Option cards

At most three, made after the class names ways to help and the teacher sorts them onto what the school allows.
**Card 1:** A poster. *Picture:* A poster on a wall.
**Card 2:** A short announcement to another class. *Picture:* A child standing before a class, one hand raised.
**Card 3:** The school's own option, drawn when the class names it.
A way the school cannot allow is said kindly and set aside; the course picks no cause.

## Pip's made-up rule

**Picture:** Pip the puppet holding a card.
**Pip says:** "Clap three times when the water rises." Beside it: "The Advice Card says ___ and its page is named: that is how we know." The made-up rule has no card and no page.

## Message cards for day 34 pairs (match each to a line on Advice Card B)

1. Charge your phone before the storm.
2. Put photo albums up high.
3. Get a kit ready.
A fourth message, "Put the lawn chairs inside", has no line on Advice Card A or B: pairs say "no line says this".

## Day 35 check

For each option card pairs read the lines of Advice Cards A and B and say "a line says this" or "no line says this". An option no line supports is reworded to what a line says or set aside; nothing is invented.

## Key (teacher)

Message 1: line 1 of B. Message 2: line 2 of B. Message 3: line 3 of B. Message 4: no line.
