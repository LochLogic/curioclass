# Day 36: Doing our plan

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Confirm the principal's yes, the place for panels or the receiving class and its teacher, and that the teacher will keep the Response Box. Lay out four job pictures in four corners: make a panel, say a line, hold the card, keep the record. Use only lines from Advice Cards A and B.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 35's exit (error E), lay three Action Record pictures: hands raised to choose, two children hanging a poster, a child reading a note that came back. The class sorts them into we chose, we did and people answered ([[s03-P36-1]]). Say: we voted to choose; we check later whether people read or heard it. If few missed it, the small group does this in supported work.

#### 4 min. Jobs

Each child walks to the corner of the job picture they pick and names the job ([[s03-P36-2]]); about five stand at each. Write names by job, so all 30 have one and no one is left out. A child who cannot speak aloud holds the card or keeps the record.

#### 10 min. Make or rehearse

Poster plan: six groups of five make one panel each: a drawing, one checked line and the card's name. In each group two make the panel, one says the line, one holds the card, one keeps the record. Announcement plan: six children each say one checked line, about 20 seconds each, 2 minutes in all, while the others rehearse listening and hold the card. Each speaker says the line and names its card ([[s03-K36-1]]).

#### 6 min. Do it

Hang the panels where the school allows, or say the announcement in the other class. Leave the Response Box, an envelope with slips for "I read it", "I heard it" and a few words, with that class's teacher, who returns it by day 42. Fill in what we made or said, where, and the advice lines used on the Action Record.

#### 10 min. Supported work

Call the second six one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X36b]]. Everyone completes the revised Recovery Card 2 explanation ([[s03-P36-3]]); keep both versions for item 4. The small group (5 minutes) takes error E from day 35's six with the three Action Record pictures if that repair was not done in the opener.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Each child circles on the Action Record the picture of the job they did ([[s03-X36]]). This is a record of what happened and has no wrong answer. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A child with no circle is not yet seen. A vote alone is not completion.

### Other rooms

No poster paper: use the backs of used sheets. No other class: say the announcement to the class and record it.

### At home

**Swap.** No other class: say a message to the household in the card's own words.

**Skip.** The panels and the Response Box.

- **10 min. Choose a message.** Read one line of an advice card aloud together.
  **Say.** "Which card is it from?" Expect: the card's letter.
- **15 min. Revise.** [[s03-P36-3]] Your child completes the revised explanation.
  **Say.** "What did you add?" Expect: an effect on people, a help or the card.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X36]] Your child circles the job they did.
  **Say.** [[s03-X36b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one panel for the whole class. Longer: a child says a second checked line to the household.

### Words and supports

Words: job, panel, announcement, record, answered, box. Frames: "Our message says ___. The card says ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many answered "We already voted" on today's exit (error E), day 37 opens with the three Action Record pictures sorted; otherwise day 37 says the region frame again. The Response Box comes back by day 42. Day 37 starts section 3.3: natural resources and the jobs they support.
