# Advice Cards A and B, days 34 to 36

Made by Curio Class in plain words from one public page. The page itself is not copied. Each card has at most three short sentences, read aloud, and the three questions are asked of each card: who made it, when, where. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Advice Card A

**Picture:** A small sign shape with three lines and a small drawing of water rising by a step.
**Lines:**
1. Stay away from floodwater.
2. Water can be very deep and can rise quickly.
3. Do not use roads marked closed.
**Source line:** Vermont Department of Health, Stay Safe in a Flood, last updated October 29, 2026, healthvermont.gov/emergency/public-health-preparedness/stay-safe-flood. Made by Curio Class from this page.

## Advice Card B

**Picture:** A small sign shape with three lines and a small drawing of a phone, a box and a stair.
**Lines:**
1. Keep your phone charged.
2. Move important things to upper floors.
3. Pack an emergency kit.
**Source line:** Vermont Department of Health, Stay Safe in a Flood, last updated October 29, 2026, healthvermont.gov/emergency/public-health-preparedness/stay-safe-flood. Made by Curio Class from this page.

## How to teach the check

Model: say a message, find the line that says the same, name the card. "The card says keep your phone charged. Advice Card B." Pip's made-up rule sits beside it: Pip's wrong idea K.
