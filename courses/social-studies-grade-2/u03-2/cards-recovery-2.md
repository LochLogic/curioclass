# Recovery Card 2 caption, days 34 to 36

Held until day 34. Told calmly; the picture shows helpers and places, never a person hurt. Made by Curio Class from two pages and the pages are not copied.

**Picture:** A shop with a long table in front and a few boxes on it, a small van with a post sign parked beside it, and helpers in gloves carrying a box in.
**Caption, read aloud:** Floodwater damaged homes and shops in Montpelier, Vermont. A mobile postal unit served customers. Thousands of volunteers came.
**Source line:** Montpelier Alive, Visiting Montpelier after the July 2023 flood, September 14, 2023, montpelieralive.com/blog/visiting-montpelier-after-the-july-2023-flood, and City of Montpelier, Flood and Disaster Recovery Assistance, updated August 21, 2023. Made by Curio Class from these pages.
**Note for the teacher:** Neither page says that a road closed, so no line says so. The caption names an effect on people's day (shops filled with water, mail by a mobile unit) and a help (volunteers, the mobile unit).

## Task

Draw or label one effect on people, one help and the card's name. First explanation on day 34; revised on day 35 or 36 after one line of written feedback. Both versions are kept; only the revised one is scored.
