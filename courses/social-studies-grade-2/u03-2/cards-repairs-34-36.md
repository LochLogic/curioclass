# Repair cards, days 34 to 36

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Each repair is at most 5 minutes.

## Creek Road (error D, day 34)

**Picture:** Creek Road: a road crossing a creek on a low bridge with water over it, a child with a school bag turned back, a shop and a clinic on the far side, an arrow along the road.
**Say:** "The closed road is a route to the shop and the clinic. Who cannot get there? The people who use that road." Pip says only buildings get hurt in a flood; the right idea is said beside it.

## Three message cards (error K, day 35)

1. A say-so: "A grown-up told me this rule."
2. A liking: "It is a nice rule."
3. A card-checked line: "Stay away from floodwater. Advice Card A, line 1."
The checked one is found, and the class says how they know.

## Three Action Record pictures (error E, day 36)

**Picture 1:** Children raising hands to choose between two posters. We chose.
**Picture 2:** Two children hanging a poster on a hall wall. We did.
**Picture 3:** A child reading a note that came back in an envelope. People answered.
Sort into we chose, we did and people answered. Pip says: "We voted, so our message worked." The right idea is said beside it: we voted to choose, and we check later whether people read or heard it.

## Key (teacher)

Creek Road: shop, clinic, school; the people who use the road. Messages: the third. Pictures: 1 chose, 2 did, 3 answered.
