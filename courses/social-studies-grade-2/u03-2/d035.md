# Day 35: Check it and vote

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the first six on the record sheet. Write one line of feedback on each day 34 explanation (about 20 minutes after school). Write on each option card the message the class gave on day 34. Confirm the principal's yes and ask the receiving class. Set out one cup for each option and 30 counters. Keep Advice Card D back.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

If many missed day 34's exit (error K), lay three message cards: "A grown-up told me this rule", "It is a nice rule" and "Stay away from floodwater. Advice Card A, line 1". The class sorts them under checked against a card and not checked yet ([[s03-P35-1]]), then says what a not-checked message needs ([[s03-P35-2]]).

#### 4 min. A fair choice

Review: in kindergarten and grade 1 you chose together: listen, ask why, look for a change both accept, and vote only if still apart, with a counter in a closed cup or a pass. Ask [[s03-P35-3]] and [[s03-P35-4]].

#### 8 min. Check each option

Pairs get the option cards. Each card carries a message. Using Advice Cards A and B, pairs sort each card under "a line says this" or "no line says this", and write the card letter on any that a line says ([[s03-P35-5]]; it stays in the folder). Say a message for an option no line supports, if a line does say something close ([[s03-P35-6]]), or set that option aside. Nothing is invented.

#### 6 min. Vote

Each child puts one counter in the cup of one option, or passes: 30 children in a line, about 6 seconds each, 3 minutes; count aloud, 1 minute. Two children give a reason for an option that did not win ([[s03-P35-7]]). Fill the Action Record. Pip says: "We voted, so our message worked" ([[s03-F35-1]], error E). Say beside it: we voted to choose; later we check whether people read or heard it.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Hand back day 34's explanations with their line of feedback. First 4 minutes, everyone revises it on the student page ([[s03-P35-8]]). While they work, call the first six one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes, for [[s03-X35b]]. Then the small group (5 minutes) takes error K from day 34's six: Pip's new glued messages on Card B ([[s03-P35-9]]). The others keep revising or draw the job they would like tomorrow. Keep both versions of every explanation.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the exit card and read aloud: "Room 4's message says: Listen to the safety officials. On Advice Card D, circle the line that says the same" ([[s03-X35]]). Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. A circled third line is error K.

### Other rooms

No cups: use two boxes and paper slips. No printer: write Cards A and B on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No counters: use two pebbles or paper slips for a vote between two ways to tell the household.

**Skip.** The small group and the Action Record.

- **10 min. A fair choice.** Pick two ways to tell your household about a flood.
  **Say.** "What do we do first, and when do we vote?" Expect: listen; vote only if still apart.
- **15 min. Revise.** [[s03-P35-8]] Your child reads the feedback line and revises the explanation.
  **Say.** "What did you add?" Expect: an effect on people, a help or the card's name.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X35]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s03-X35b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: check two options only. Longer: a child checks a second message against Card B and says which line supports it.

### Words and supports

Words: option, vote, count, reason, checked, line. Frames: "A line says this." "I vote for ___ because ___." Pointing counts, and a child may pass.

### Tomorrow

If many answered the vote count on today's exit (error E), day 36 opens with three Action Record pictures sorted into we chose, we did and people answered. Day 36 carries out the plan through the school, with a job for every child.
