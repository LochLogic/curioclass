# Day 63: Who is an immigrant?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the sixth on the record sheet. Say aloud: Pavo, Tove, Rey, Yara, Kit, Lark, Moss, Wren, Finn and the family are made up; Anna and Catherine are real. Ask no child where they or their family came from; any child may pass or draw. Key: key-exits-63-67.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 62's chain (s05-P62-4), arrow (s05-P62-8) and exit ticket (s05-X62). Repair error I, or error C if more missed it. Error I: [[s05-P63-1]] Children follow the arrow with a finger, then draw it. Pip says error I: "Pavo went from the farm to the sea." Say beside it: start where they left. Error C: [[s05-P63-2]] Error A (s05-X62b) goes to the small group.

#### 3 min. Retrieval, grade 1

Review: you learned citizen and diverse in grade 1. Pairs talk a minute on each ask. **Ask.** [[s05-P63-4]] **Ask.** [[s05-P63-5]] Day 64 repairs a miss.

#### 8 min. Two examples

Say: an immigrant is a person who comes from another country to live; a visitor comes and goes home. **Ask.** [[s05-P63-8]] Pip says error P: "Anyone who comes for a week is an immigrant."

Anna: read her card again with its credit line; you met Anna on day 58. Review: the three questions (grade 2, unit 1). Fill them: Anna, told to an interviewer; arrived 1920, told 1991; Ellis Island. **Ask.** [[s05-P63-7]]

Catherine: read her card once with its credit line. Card: "Catherine came to New York City. In 1991 she told an interviewer about her first day in America." It does not say where she began, so mark a star at New York City, no arrow.

#### 6 min. The phrase

Review: a nation is a people with their own government (grade 1). The phrase a nation of immigrants: many families here came from another country, at many times, and families arrive today. Show the family card, made up: "A family arrived last month from another country to live here." Set Anna, Catherine and the family in a row. One family is an example; the phrase is about many. **Ask.** [[s05-P63-9]]

#### 9 min. Pairs work

Pairs read six cards to each other, sort them under immigrant, visitor and born here, then write the names: [[s05-P63-6]] Kit, Lark, Moss, Wren, Finn, the family. First, the small group of at most 6, 4 minutes, error A: [[s05-P63-3]] Then the six, 45 seconds each with the swap, 4.5 minutes, for s05-X63b with the Wei Card.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X63]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The six answered [[s05-X63b]] aloud.

#### 1 min. Question board

Read "Whose history does the phrase describe?"

### Other rooms

Write the cards on slips; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: write Kit, Lark, Moss, Wren, Finn and "a family" on slips.

**Skip.** The retrieval pairs, the groups and the six.

- **10 min. Pavo.** Read the card. Your child draws the arrow. [[s05-P63-1]] **Say.** "Which way does the arrow go?" Expect: from the sea in the east to the farm in the west.
- **10 min. Sort.** Read the six slips. Your child sorts them and writes the names. [[s05-P63-6]] **Say.** "Who goes home after a week?" Expect: Wren, the visitor.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X63]] [[s05-X63b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four sorting cards (Kit, Wren, Finn, the family). Longer: where could another arrow start?

### Words and supports

Words: immigrant, visitor, born here, nation, family. Frame: "An immigrant ___. A visitor ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow
