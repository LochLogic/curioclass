# Cards for day 66

Real parts are summaries in our words with credit lines. The Kai strip is invented for practice. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

A plain outline of the United States with a coast. A star at New York Harbor labelled "Ellis Island" and a star at San Francisco Bay labelled "Angel Island".

## Angel Island, told plainly (9 minutes)

Credit: "Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation, By the Numbers page; National Archives, Prologue; read November 2026. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card:** "A law, the Chinese Exclusion Act of 1882, was made to stop Chinese workers from coming to the United States. Newcomers came to Angel Island, in San Francisco Bay. Chinese newcomers were held there longer than others: weeks and months, and a few people about two years."
The chain strip gains a box: because they hoped ___, they came; a law ___; afterward they were held. Pip says N.

## Mock Ging Sing (7 minutes)

Credit: "Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation, Judy Yung Oral History Collection, interview of 9 April 1984. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card:** "Mock Ging Sing was held on Angel Island for ten months, in 1937 and 1938. In 1984 she told an interviewer she had big dreams about America."
**Three questions:** Who made it: Mock Ging Sing, to an interviewer. When: told on 9 April 1984; the hold was in 1937 and 1938. Where: Angel Island. Perspective: her hope beside Anna's.

## Kai strip (pairs work, 8 minutes)

Printed "invented for practice". **Card:** "Kai hoped to join an aunt. A law kept most Chinese people out. Officers held Kai on the island for weeks." Each pair fills the strip (because Kai hoped ___, Kai came; a law ___; afterward Kai was held) and says what Kai hoped for and what Anna hoped for. The six are heard.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Kai hoped to join an aunt; a law; held for weeks. Anna: the card tells that her mother and aunt met her; the card does not say what she hoped. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
