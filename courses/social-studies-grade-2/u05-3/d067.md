# Day 67: Long ago and told more recently

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the tenth on the record sheet. Say aloud: Pol, Ria, Sia and Oto are made up; Catherine, Mock Ging Sing and Rahaf are real. Read Rahaf's card as what she said in 2018, never as what newcomers think. Ask no child where they or their family came from; any child may pass or draw. Key: key-exits-63-67.md.

### The day

#### 8 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 66's strip (s05-P66-5) and exit ticket (s05-X66). Two repairs, 4 minutes each. Error N (s05-X66): [[s05-P67-1]] Pip says error N: "Ria chose to be held." Say beside it: a hold is not part of choosing the trip. Error L (s05-X66b): Review: Both ___, but ___ (kindergarten). [[s05-P67-2]] Pip says error L: "Everybody felt the same about moving." Say beside it: two tellers, two stated hopes.

#### 5 min. Arrival Voices

Read three cards once each with their credit lines: Catherine's first impressions (day 65) and Mock Ging Sing's card (day 66) again, and Rahaf: "Rahaf was born in Homs, Syria. She moved to the United States when she was 16 and a half. In 2018 she said she misses the loving and caring people in her neighborhood." Credit: Rahaf Barcudi, interviewed by Raghad, 2018, Learning for Justice interview transcript. Summary by Curio Class. **Ask.** [[s05-P67-3]]

#### 6 min. Revise two charts

Add Rahaf's row to Chart 1 (who, what they felt, when told) and to Chart 2 (newcomer long ago, told more recently). Words: alike, different, newcomer, told more recently. Model on Catherine and Mock Ging Sing: "Both told an interviewer about America, but Catherine told about what she saw and Mock Ging Sing told about big dreams." One teller is not every newcomer.

#### 10 min. Pairs work

Pairs write and say Both ___, but ___ for Mock Ging Sing and Rahaf, with a card line for each side, not about how they traveled: [[s05-P67-4]] Pip says error E: "Both came by boat." Pip says error L: "They felt the same." Hear four of the six, 45 seconds each with the swap, 3 minutes, with the two Arrival Voices paragraphs: two children [[s05-X67b]] and two [[s05-X67c]].

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X67]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. While the rest mark, hear the other two of the six, 45 seconds each: one [[s05-X67b]] and one [[s05-X67c]].

#### 3 min. Question board

Read "Did arrival feel the same to everyone?" Children point to a card line that helps.

### Other rooms

Draw the charts on the board; write cards on slips; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed charts: draw two columns, long ago and told more recently.

**Skip.** The repairs, the six and the charts.

- **10 min. Voices.** Read the Mock Ging Sing and Rahaf cards. **Say.** "When did Rahaf tell this?" Expect: in 2018, not today's view.
- **15 min. Both, but.** Your child finishes the sentence. [[s05-P67-4]] **Say.** "Which line shows it?" Expect: a card line for each.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X67]] [[s05-X67b]] [[s05-X67c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one chart and the Both, but sentence. Longer: what other voice would show a different view, and where could you find it?

### Words and supports

Words: alike, different, newcomer, told more recently. Frame: "Both ___, but ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 sets two accounts of one arrival side by side. Error E shows as a ship or plane for what a person said; error L as "they felt the same". Day 68 repairs error E with a fresh pair, and sends error L to a small group.
