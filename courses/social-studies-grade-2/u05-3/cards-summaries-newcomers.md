# Newcomer summaries

Real people, each a short summary in our words with a credit line. The teacher reads each once and says it is a real person's memory. Each card prints who told it and when. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Anna: the bread (day 65)

Credit: "Anna Zagar Klarich, interview EI-89, 12 September 1991, Ellis Island Oral History Collection, National Park Service. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card:** "Anna had little to eat on the boat. In the dining room at her aunt's place she liked the French bread and butter. Her aunt urged her to take more."
**Three questions:** Who made it: Anna, told to an interviewer. When: she arrived in 1920; she told it on 12 September 1991. Where: New York. "Who made it and when told" is added to the three questions.

## Catherine: first impressions (days 65, 67, 68)

Credit: "Catherine Hannon English, interview EI-116a, 17 November 1991, Ellis Island Oral History Collection, National Park Service. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card:** "Catherine was ten in 1920. In New York she saw schoolchildren who looked untidy to her. She thought they might be orphans. She also saw dirty streets with garbage."
**Three questions:** Who made it: Catherine, told to an interviewer. When: she arrived in 1920; she told it on 17 November 1991. Where: New York.

## Rahaf: what she misses (day 67)

Credit: "Rahaf Barcudi, interviewed by her sister Raghad, 3 June 2018, Teaching Tolerance (now Learning for Justice) interview transcript. Summary by Curio Class."
**Card:** "Rahaf was born in Homs, Syria. She moved to the United States when she was 16 and a half. In 2018 she said she misses the loving and caring people in her neighborhood."
**Three questions:** Who made it: Rahaf, to her sister. When: told on 3 June 2018, not what she thinks in November 2026. Where: the recording place is not given.

## Pairs work (day 65, 9 minutes)

Each pair underlines, in two colors, what Anna and Catherine hoped or felt (one color) and what each did or how each traveled (the other color). The six are heard. Pip says E.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Felt or hoped (color one): Anna liked the bread; Catherine thought the children looked untidy and might be orphans, saw dirty streets. Did or traveled (color two): Anna was on a boat; both arrived in 1920. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
