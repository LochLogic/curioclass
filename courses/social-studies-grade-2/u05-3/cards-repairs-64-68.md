# Repair cards, days 64 to 68

Every card prints "invented for practice" except where a real card from teaching is shown again. Each repair is at most 5 minutes (4 each on day 67). Pip says the wrong idea and the right idea is said beside it. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Visitor, immigrant, born here (error P, day 64 small group)

**Picture:** Three new cards: Tam visits a cousin for a week, Ivo moved here to live, Rue was born here. The child sorts them under the three words.
**Say:** "An immigrant comes to live. A visitor goes home." Pip: "Tam visits for a week, so Tam is an immigrant."

## Homeland map and dated card (errors D and M, day 65)

**Picture:** The Homeland and Removal Map (day 64) beside the Eastern Band card. The child follows the arrow west, then finds the star where the nation is today.
**Say:** "A nation moved by the United States government is not an immigrant. The dated card says the nation governs itself today." Pip: "The Cherokee became immigrants. The Cherokee are gone."

## Chose or brought by force (error J, day 65)

**Picture:** Six invented arrivals: Zel packs a suitcase and takes a train to a new job. Moro's family takes a boat to join a cousin. A girl, Aja, is taken from her home by men and put on a ship, and she does not choose it. Bo comes to study at a school. Dev was told by officials to leave and cannot stay. Pru comes with her aunt, because she wants a new school. The child sorts "chose" and "brought by force", with no ranking of who belongs.
**Say:** "Many chose. Some were brought by force." Pip: "Everyone came here because they chose to."

## Hoped and traveled (error E, days 66 and 68)

**Picture:** A fresh pair of cards. Card 1: "Dani hoped to see an old friend. Dani came by train." Card 2: "Efe hoped to start a farm. Efe came by boat." The child underlines what each hoped in one color and how each traveled in the other.
**Say:** "How they felt or hoped is one question. How they traveled is another." Pip: "Dani came by train, so that is how Dani felt."

## Two hopes, two tellers (error L, days 67 and 68)

**Picture:** Two fresh cards. "Sia hoped to join a brother." "Oto hoped to learn a trade." The child says "Both ___, but ___."
**Say:** "Two tellers, two stated hopes." Pip: "Everybody felt the same about moving."

## A chosen voyage and an imposed hold (error N, day 67)

**Picture:** Card 1: "Pol takes a boat to visit an aunt and plans the trip." Card 2: "Officers hold Ria at a dock while a law is checked, and she cannot leave." The child sorts "chosen" and "held by others".
**Say:** "A hold is not part of choosing the trip." Pip: "Ria chose to be held."

## Key (teacher sheet only)

P: Tam visitor, Ivo immigrant, Rue born here. D, M: the arrow runs west; the star is where the nation is today. J: chose: Zel, Moro, Bo, Pru; brought by force: Aja, Dev. E: Dani hoped to see a friend, traveled by train; Efe hoped to start a farm, traveled by boat. L: both hoped to be near or learn something, but different hopes. N: card 1 chosen; card 2 held. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
