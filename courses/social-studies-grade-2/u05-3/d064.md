# Day 64: Whose story the phrase leaves out

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the seventh on the record sheet. Say aloud: Sel, Nia, Tam, Ivo and Rue are made up; Anna, Mock Ging Sing and the two nations are real. Write one slip per pair: "Sel. Men took Sel from home and put Sel on a ship. Invented for practice." Leave the Smith Life card on the shelf. Ask no child about their family. Key: key-exits-63-67.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 63's arrow (s05-P63-1), sort (s05-P63-6) and exit ticket (s05-X63). If more than a third missed a retrieval ask on day 63, ask both again to the whole class (s05-P63-4, s05-P63-5). Otherwise repair error D or J, whichever was missed more: [[s05-P64-2]] Error P (s05-X63b) goes to the small group.

#### 3 min. Review, grade 1

Say: Review: you met the Lenape in grade 1 and the Cherokee Nation on day 61. **Ask.** [[s05-P64-1]]

#### 8 min. Here first

Read the Eastern Band and Chickasaw cards once each, with credit lines and "As of November 2026". Gloss sovereign: a nation that governs itself. On the map, show the Chickasaw homeland, the arrow west and the star at its capital today, and the Eastern Band's star in North Carolina. Say: an immigrant comes from another country to live (review, day 63); a nation that was here first did not immigrate; being moved by the United States government was removal (day 61). **Ask.** [[s05-P64-5]] Pip says error D: "The Eastern Band came from another country." Pip says error M: "The Chickasaw are gone." Children point to the card line that answers: [[s05-P64-3]] [[s05-P64-4]]

#### 7 min. The limits of the phrase

Read the Sel slip and Anna's card side by side; nothing on Anna's card says she was forced. Review: you learned the word enslaved on day 60. Say: the phrase describes people who came to live by their own choice; it does not describe people brought by force, or a nation that was here first. Everyone belongs; this is about how they arrived. **Ask.** [[s05-P64-6]] Pip says error J: "Everyone came here because they chose to." Say beside it: many chose; some were brought by force. **Ask.** [[s05-P64-7]]

#### 9 min. Pairs work

Pairs sort five cards under chose to come, brought by force and was here first, then write the names: [[s05-P64-8]] First the small group of at most 6, 4 minutes, error P: [[s05-P64-9]] Then the six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes: three hear s05-X64b, three hear s05-X64c with the Keetoowah Card. The next six hear the other ask.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X64]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The six answered [[s05-X64b]] and [[s05-X64c]] aloud.

### Other rooms

Write the cards on slips; read aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: write the five names on slips.

**Skip.** The retrieval pairs, the groups and the six.

- **10 min. Here first.** Read the Eastern Band card. **Say.** "Who was in that region first?" Expect: the Eastern Band, it is their homeland.
- **15 min. Sort.** Read the five slips. Your child sorts and writes the names. [[s05-P64-8]] **Say.** "Which card is a person who did not choose to come?" Expect: Sel.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X64]] [[s05-X64b]] [[s05-X64c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort three cards (Anna, Sel, Eastern Band). Longer: what else would you read about the Chickasaw Nation?

### Words and supports

Words: immigrant, nation, sovereign, here first, brought by force. Frame: "The phrase describes ___; it does not describe ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 turns to Ellis Island. Error D shows as yes circled; error J as "yes, they immigrated"; error M as "only in the past". Day 65 repairs the letter most missed of D, J and M and sends the rest to a small group.
