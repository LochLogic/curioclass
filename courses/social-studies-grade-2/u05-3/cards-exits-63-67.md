# Exit cards, days 63 to 67

Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 63 exit card: Wei Card

Printed "invented for practice".
**Card line:** "Wei came from China to join an uncle in the United States in 1990."
**Mark:** Circle the word for Wei: immigrant or Native.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say how Wei's card helps show why this country is called a nation of immigrants."

## Day 64 exit card: Keetoowah Card and Removal Caption

Credit: "United Keetoowah Band of Cherokee Indians in Oklahoma, official website, and National Park Service, Trail of Tears National Historic Trail, read November 2026. Summary by Curio Class. Dated November 2026."
**Keetoowah Card:** "The United Keetoowah Band says it is a sovereign nation headquartered in Tahlequah, Oklahoma."
**Removal Caption:** "From 1837 to 1839 the United States government removed thousands of Cherokee people from their homelands in the Appalachian Mountains."
**Mark:** Did Cherokee people become immigrants by being forced west? Circle yes or no.
**Spoken, the six one at a time (two asks):** "Enslaved African families were brought to the Americas by force. Say whether the phrase a nation of immigrants describes how they came." And: "The Keetoowah Card is dated November 2026. Is the United Keetoowah Band in the past, or here today?"

## Day 65 exit card: Cookie Card and Newcomer Card

Credit: "Anna Zagar Klarich, interview EI-89, 12 September 1991, Ellis Island Oral History Collection, National Park Service. Summary by Curio Class."
**Cookie Card:** "Anna remembers being so hungry for cookies that she took four big cookies the next morning. Her mother told her not to be embarrassed and to eat all she wanted."
**Mark:** Circle what Anna wanted in her memory: cookies, a hat, a boat.
**Newcomer Card (invented for practice):** "Zeb is new in town and has a cousin who lives there. Zeb says, 'I hope my cousin will be my friend here.'"
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "On the Newcomer Card, say what Zeb hopes for."

## Day 66 exit card: Lin Card

Printed "invented for practice".
**Card line:** "Lin hoped to find work. A law was made against Chinese workers coming, so officers held Lin on the island for months."
**Mark:** Circle why Lin was held: a law, a storm, Lin's wish.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Lin hoped to find work. A new girl hopes to join her mother. Say how their hopes are alike."

## Day 67 exit cards: two Arrival Voices paragraphs

Credit: Catherine Hannon English, interview EI-116a, 17 November 1991, and Rahaf Barcudi, interview of 3 June 2018, summaries by Curio Class.
**Paragraph 1:** "Before she came, Catherine expected New York City to be beautiful and clean."
**Paragraph 2:** "In 2018 Rahaf said that if Syria were safer she would probably go back to visit, but not to live there again."
**Mark:** Circle the paragraph that tells what Catherine expected before she came.
**Spoken, the six one at a time (two asks):** "Say what Rahaf said in her 2018 interview about going back to Syria." And: "Say one way that what Catherine expected and what Rahaf said are alike or different."
