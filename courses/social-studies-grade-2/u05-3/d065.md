# Day 65: What newcomers said about arriving

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the eighth on the record sheet. Say aloud: the cards for Zel, Moro, Aja, Bo, Dev, Pru and the 1900, 1950 and 2000 cards are made up; Anna and Catherine are real. Use Anna's reunion line only, never her food memory, and Catherine's streets only, never what she expected. Key: key-exits-63-67.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 64's sort (s05-P64-8) and exit ticket (s05-X64). Repair the letter most missed of D, J and M. Errors D and M: [[s05-P65-1]] Pip says error D: "The Cherokee became immigrants." Pip says error M: "The nation is gone." Say beside it: the dated card says it governs itself today. Error J: [[s05-P65-2]] Say: many chose; some were brought by force. The others go to the small group.

#### 3 min. Review, timeline

Review: timelines, kindergarten and grade 1. Three made-up cards go on the strip. **Ask.** [[s05-P65-3]]

#### 8 min. Ellis Island, and when it was told

Say: Ellis Island was an immigration station in New York Harbor, open from 1892 to 1924, the largest and most active in America. Show New York on the small world map. Read Anna's reunion card again with its credit line; you met Anna on day 58. Review: the three questions. Add a fourth: when was it told? Anna arrived in 1920; she told it in 1991. **Ask.** [[s05-P65-4]]

#### 7 min. Dated cards

Place four cards left to right: Smith's account printed, 1798, marked "when it was written down"; Cherokee removal begins, 1838; Anna arrives, 1920; Anna and Catherine tell their memories, 1991. Say: the move and the telling are two dates. **Ask.** [[s05-P65-5]] Start Chart 1 (who, what they felt, when told) with the two tellers and 1991.

#### 9 min. Pairs work

Review: two views (kindergarten). Perspective: what a person hoped, wanted or felt, in their own telling. Read the Catherine card: "Catherine came to New York City. She saw schoolchildren who looked sloppy to her, and she thought they might be orphans. She saw dirty streets with garbage. She told it to an interviewer in 1991." Pairs underline in color one what each felt or thought and in color two what each did, saw or how Anna traveled, then write: [[s05-P65-6]] Pip says error E: "Anna came by boat, so that is how she felt." First the small group of at most 6, 4 minutes, error J: [[s05-P65-2]] Then the six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, for s05-X65b with the Newcomer Card.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X65]]. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The six answered [[s05-X65b]] aloud.

### Other rooms

Draw the strip on a long sheet; write cards on slips.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall strip: lay the cards in a row on the table.

**Skip.** The review cards, the groups and the six.

- **10 min. Two dates.** Read Anna's card. **Say.** "Which date is when she arrived, and which is when she told it?" Expect: 1920 arrived, 1991 told.
- **15 min. Felt and traveled.** Read the Catherine card. Your child writes on the lines. [[s05-P65-6]] **Say.** "What did Anna feel?" Expect: they hugged and cried.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X65]] [[s05-X65b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Anna's two dates and one line for each teller. Longer: what would you ask Catherine?

### Words and supports

Words: Ellis Island, dated, told, felt, thought, traveled. Frame: "Anna felt ___. Anna traveled ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow
