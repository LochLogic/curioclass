# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 63 to 67

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-63-67.md); never hand to children.

## Key

Day 63: immigrant; it is one example of a person coming from another country to live here. Day 64: no; here today, the dated card says so; no, they were brought by force and did not choose to come. Day 65: cookies, and the A at 1920; that his cousin will be his friend. Day 66: a law; both hoped coming would bring something good (work for Lin, her mother for the girl), any likeness that rests on the two hopes. Day 67: paragraph 1; she would probably go back to visit, not to live there; a likeness or difference that rests on the two cards, such as Catherine looked ahead to a new place and Rahaf looked back at her home, not a claim about every newcomer.
