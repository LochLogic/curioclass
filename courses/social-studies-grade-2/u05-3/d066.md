# Day 66: Angel Island, told plainly

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the ninth on the record sheet. Say aloud: Mina, Orin, Dani, Efe, Ming and Kai are made up; Mock Ging Sing is real. Say Chinese newcomers by that name, plainly. Ask no child about family. Key: key-exits-63-67.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 65's lines (s05-P65-6) and exit ticket (s05-X65). Repair error E on a fresh pair, Dani and Efe: underline in one color what each hoped, in the other how each came. Pip says error E: "Dani came by train, so that is how Dani felt." [[s05-P66-2]] Say beside it: hoped is one question, came is another. Error K (s05-X65) goes to the small group.

#### 3 min. Review, the test

Review of 5.1: could they have stayed? **Ask.** [[s05-P66-1]]

#### 9 min. Angel Island, told plainly

Show the small map with two stars. **Ask.** [[s05-P66-4]] Read the card with its credit line: "A law, the Chinese Exclusion Act of 1882, was made to stop Chinese workers from coming to the United States. Newcomers came to Angel Island, in San Francisco Bay. Chinese newcomers were held there longer than others: weeks and months, and a few people about two years." Credit: Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation, National Archives and California State Parks, read November 2026. Summary by Curio Class. Review day 59's chain strip. It gains a box: because they hoped, they came; a law; afterward they were held. Pip says error N: "They chose to be held." Say beside it: a law and officers held them; it was not part of the trip they planned.

#### 7 min. Mock Ging Sing

Read the card with its credit line: "Mock Ging Sing was held on Angel Island for ten months, in 1937 and 1938. In 1984 she told an interviewer she had big dreams about America." Credit: Angel Island Immigration Station Foundation, interview of 9 April 1984. Summary by Curio Class. Fill who, when told and where. **Ask.** [[s05-P66-8]] Add her to Chart 1. **Ask.** [[s05-P66-7]] Say only that she came to Angel Island.

#### 8 min. Pairs work

Each pair fills the strip for Kai: [[s05-P66-5]] then says what Kai hoped for: [[s05-P66-6]] First the small group of at most 6, 4 minutes, on error K: [[s05-P66-3]] Then hear four of the six, 45 seconds each, 3 minutes, for s05-X66b with the girl's line.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X66]]. Mark by the scale. While the rest mark, hear the other two of the six, 45 seconds each: [[s05-X66b]].

### Other rooms

Draw boxes; write cards on slips.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed strip: draw three boxes and label them hoped, a law, held.

**Skip.** The review cards, the groups and the six.

- **10 min. Kai.** Read Kai's card. Your child fills the boxes. [[s05-P66-5]] **Say.** "What did Kai hope for?" Expect: to join an aunt.
- **10 min. Mock Ging Sing.** Read her card. **Say.** "When was she held, and when did she tell it?" Expect: held in 1937 and 1938, told in 1984.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X66]] [[s05-X66b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Kai's strip, then the exit. Longer: what other record might tell how a hold felt?

### Words and supports

Words: law, held, hoped, Angel Island, Ellis Island. Frame: "Because they hoped ___, they came. A law ___. Afterward ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 67 puts a newcomer long ago beside one told more recently. Error N shows as Lin's wish circled or "Lin chose to be held"; error E as a ship or plane for what a hope was; error L as "they felt the same". Day 67 repairs error N and error L in its Return and repair, 4 minutes each.
