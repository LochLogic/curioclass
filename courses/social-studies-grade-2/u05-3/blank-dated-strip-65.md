## Review (3 minutes): three dated cards, invented for practice

1900: a child and a horse and cart. 1950: a child and an old car. 2000: a child and a computer. The class places them left to right.

## Four dated cards (7 minutes)

- **1798:** Smith's account printed. Marked "when it was written down", not when he was taken.
- **1838 and 1839:** Cherokee removal.
- **1920:** Anna and Catherine arrive.
- **1991:** Anna and Catherine tell their memories (September and November).

The class places them left to right. "The move and the telling are two dates."

## Ellis Island (8 minutes)
