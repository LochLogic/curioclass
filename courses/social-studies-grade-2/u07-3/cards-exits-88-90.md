# Exit cards, days 88 to 90

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. Exit and check cards are first seen at the child's own turn. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 88 exit card: Alder Ford river barge

**Three fact cards, from the Alder Ford page:** "Boats carry vegetables to town." "Children fish in the river for supper." "The river is long."
**Mark:** Finish the claim with two facts that answer the question: The river matters for living in Alder Ford because ___ and ___.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say how your claim helps us learn what matters about living there."

## Day 89 exit card: the lake

**Fact card:** "The lake gives people water." Its source label: "Lake Page, made by Curio Class."
**Part box:** one empty part box of the report with a label slot under it.
**Mark:** Fill the part box: write the fact, then write its page name in the label slot.
**Digital turn (the day's fifteen, not the exit card), three asks:** "Say your claim for the adult to enter." "Say your two facts for the adult to enter." "Point to your entry on the screen and say if it is right."

## Day 90 exit card: two panels

**Panel 1:** only a country name and a drawing. **Panel 2:** a claim and two facts with labels.
**Mark:** Put a token on the panel that tells what matters and why.
**Digital turn (the day's remaining fifteen), the same three asks.**
