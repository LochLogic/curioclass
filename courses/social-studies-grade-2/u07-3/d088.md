# Day 88: From facts to a claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 87 record: error E. Have the note pages, the Japan card and the claim frame ready. Say aloud that Marlow is made up.

### The day

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: you learned in grade 1 to say what you think and why. Ask [[s07-R88-1]]; take three. A miss: add "because".

#### 6 min. A claim, Japan

Review: you used the claim frame "I think ___ because ___. The ___ shows ___, made by ___" in unit 5 and unit 6. New today: a claim stands on two facts, each answering a supporting question. Show the Japan card from day 82. Say and write the claim card: "I think the sea matters for how people live in Japan because Japan is islands with sea all around. The Japan page shows islands and seas, made by Curio Class." Then say, as thinking, not a fact the page gives: "So people there may live and work near the sea."
- **Ask.** [[s07-K88-1]] Point to the two facts on the card.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K88-2]] Then say: the big question asks what matters about how people live, and land and water may matter.
Pip says error D: "Here are my facts: islands, seas, mountains." Say beside it: "I think the sea matters because Japan is islands with sea all around."

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error E from day 87 (s07-X87): [[s07-R88-2]] Read two fresh captions and page lines aloud; children say what the page supports instead of "everyone". Return the day 87 sort (s07-R87-1) and note page (s07-P87-1). Any other letter goes to a small group of at most 6.

#### 17 min. Claims by pairs

[[s07-P88-1]] Each child picks two facts from the note page that answer different supporting questions, says a claim to a partner, then records it on the frame; an adult may write a child's words. Time:
- Pairs say claims: about 30 seconds each way, 2 minutes.
- Recording on the frame: about 8 minutes.
- Hear the first six during the recording, 45 seconds each and 10 to swap, about 5 minutes: show the day 88 exit card and ask [[s07-X88b]].
Facts listed with no "because" are error D: ask which question each fact answers.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card and read it aloud. [[s07-X88]] An adult may scribe. A bare fact list, or the long river, is error D.

#### 1 min. Board

Answer aloud once: "Which answers best support our claim?" The facts that answer the big question.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the Japan claim on the board and each Marlow caption on a slip. One projector: show the Japan card.

### At home

**Swap.** No note page: use any two facts from a book or a page about one town.

**Skip.** The repair and the hearing of six.

- **3 min. Think.** [[s07-R88-1]] **Say.** "What do you think, and why?" Expect: an answer with because.
- **8 min. Japan.** Read the Japan claim aloud: "I think the sea matters for how people live in Japan because Japan is islands with sea all around." [[s07-K88-1]] [[s07-K88-2]] **Say.** "Which two facts does the claim stand on?" Expect: islands, and sea all around.
- **15 min. Your claim.** Pick two facts from your note page and say a claim with because. [[s07-P88-1]] **Say.** "Which question does each fact answer?"
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X88]] [[s07-X88b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one fact and its question, then the second tomorrow. Longer: ask what fact would make the claim weaker.

### Words and supports

Words: claim, fact, source, because, matters. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts; any language the teacher can record counts.

### Tomorrow

Error D (a list taken for a claim) goes on the record sheet. Day 89 opens with one repair card that links two details to a claim, returns the written source feedback for a short revision, then children draft the report with its map.

### Credit

Ours. The Japan facts are Curio Class's own words from the Statistics Bureau of Japan handbook (2025) and the Geography of Japan page; Marlow is made up.
