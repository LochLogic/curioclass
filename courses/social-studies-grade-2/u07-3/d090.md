# Day 90: Finish the report and practise presenting it

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 89 record: error H. Name today's nine on the rotation sheet. Brief the adult as on day 89; when the offline display exists the adult uses it, until then the display sheet. Say aloud that the repair facts are made up. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error H from day 89 (s07-X89): [[s07-R90-1]] Show the three unlabeled facts, invented for practice. Pip says: "Where a fact came from does not matter, I just wrote it." Say beside it the same fact with its label. Any other letter goes to a small group of at most 6 during the pairs.

#### 5 min. Teaching, presenting

Review: you used the claim frame and the Japan claim card on day 88. New today: how to present. Show the Japan map, say the claim, say the two facts and name each page and maker (the Japan card, made by Curio Class). A listener may ask one question: "Which page says that?" Listeners say a question, never a judgment; any child may pass.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K90-1]]
Pip says error F: "I watched a screen, so I did digital." Say beside it: "I said my own claim and two facts and checked my own screen."

#### 7 min. Practice in pairs

[[s07-P90-1]] Each child says their claim and two facts to a partner; the partner asks one question; the pair changes places. About 45 seconds each way and 10 to swap, 3 minutes. Today's nine leave in turn for the device, about 2.5 minutes each (three asks at 45 seconds with swap), run by an adult; their partner waits or joins another pair.

#### 17 min. Finish the report

[[s07-P90-2]] Return the day 89 report draft (s07-P89-1) and source revision (s07-R89-2). Each child reads the feedback note and revises the paper report on coverage, source labels and claim support; keep both versions. The turns continue: nine at about 2.5 minutes is 22.5 minutes across this part and the pairs. The other fourteen make their entry at their day 91 showing. Each child directs their own entry and checks the screen. [[s07-X90b]] [[s07-X90c]] [[s07-X90d]] Record shown, shown with help or not yet seen; no child watches instead of making one.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the panel that tells what matters and why." [[s07-X90]] The second panel is right; the first is error D. After class write each child a note card (one line each: claim, labels, entry) for day 91.

### Other rooms

No device: record digital as not yet seen. One projector: show the Japan card and the presenting card.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: say the claim and facts to the grown-up, who writes them on the display sheet.

**Skip.** The repair and the display turn.

- **5 min. Labels.** Add a page name and who made it to three facts. [[s07-R90-1]]
- **15 min. Present.** Say your claim and two facts; the grown-up asks "Which page says that?" [[s07-P90-1]] **Say.** "Which page says that?" Expect: the page and who made it.
- **10 min. Finish.** Fix one thing in your report. [[s07-P90-2]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X90]] [[s07-X90b]] [[s07-X90c]] [[s07-X90d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: practise with one partner turn, not two; never the labels or the entry. Longer: add what fact would weaken your claim.

### Words and supports

Words: present, listener, question, label, claim. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ says ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error F (watching taken for a turn) goes on the record sheet. Day 91 opens with the note card, then each child directs a correction on their own entry, then reports are shown in two circles, about 30 seconds each.

### Credit

Ours. The Japan facts are Curio Class's own words from the Statistics Bureau of Japan handbook (2025); the repair facts are made up.
