# Day 89: Draft the report with its map

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 88 record: error D. Choose today's seven for display turns (nine on day 90, fourteen at their day 91 showing); name them on the rotation sheet. Brief the adult: no account, network or upload; the child says each box, the adult enters it; nobody types. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Error D from day 88 (s07-X88): [[s07-R89-1]] Show the card, invented for practice. Pip says: "A well. A pond. A school." Say beside it a claim with "because". Any other letter goes to a small group of at most 6 during the drafting.

#### 4 min. Revise source choices

Return the day 88 claim frame (s07-P88-1) and the day 87 feedback note. [[s07-R89-2]] Each child changes one source choice on the note page, using the sourcing card: who made it, then the helping part. Keep both; only the revision is scored.

#### 6 min. Teaching, the display

The new tool is the display. When the offline display exists the adult uses it; until then the adult writes on the display sheet. Use the Japan claim card from day 88. Before class the adult enters the Japan claim in the claim box, the two facts in the fact boxes and the page name with Curio Class in the label slot. Show the finished screen and name each box's job; the children's own turns come later in the rotation.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K89-1]]
- **Ask.** [[s07-K89-2]]
Say that each turn is the child's own; watching is not a turn.

#### 19 min. Draft the report

[[s07-P89-1]] Each child draws the map with its key box, fills the three part boxes from the note page with a sticker in each, and writes or dictates the claim card with two facts and a "because". Time:
- Today's seven take a turn at the device, run by the adult, about 2.5 minutes each (three asks, 45 seconds and swap): 17.5 minutes, while the rest draft.
- Each child directs the entry and checks the screen: claim, two facts, then "right" or one thing to change. [[s07-X89b]] [[s07-X89c]] [[s07-X89d]]
- No six are heard today.
Fill the rotation sheet: shown, shown with help (the adult supplied words), not yet seen; a child who only watched is not yet seen.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the picture that says where the sentence came from." [[s07-X89]] The label is right; the border is error H. Collect the drafts.

### Other rooms

No device: record the digital cells as not yet seen and keep the display sheet on paper. One projector: show the Japan claim card.

### At home

**Swap.** No device: say each box aloud to the grown-up, who writes it on the display sheet.

**Skip.** The repair and the display turn.

- **5 min. Revise.** Look at one source choice and change it by its maker and a helping part. [[s07-R89-2]]
- **20 min. Draft.** Draw a map with a key, write a fact in each of three boxes with where it came from, and a claim with two facts. [[s07-P89-1]] **Say.** "Which two facts back your claim?" Expect: two facts, each with its page.
- **5 min. Say it.** Say the claim, then the two facts; the grown-up writes them and the child checks. **Say.** "Is that what you said?" Expect: right, or one thing to change.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X89]] [[s07-X89b]] [[s07-X89c]] [[s07-X89d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two part boxes today; never the claim card or labels. Longer: add a third labeled fact.

### Words and supports

Words: report, map, key, label, claim, display. Frames: "I think ___ because ___." "The ___ shows ___, made by ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

Error H (a fact with no label) goes on the record sheet. After class write each child a one-line note on coverage, labels and claim; return it on day 90. Day 90 opens by adding labels to three facts, then finishes the report and gives nine more their turn at the device.

### Credit

Ours. The Japan facts are Curio Class's own words from the Statistics Bureau of Japan handbook (2025); Birchwood is made up.
