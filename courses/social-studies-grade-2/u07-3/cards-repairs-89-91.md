# Repair cards, days 89 to 91

Fresh invented cards, printed "invented for practice". Not exits. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 89: D (a claim, not a list)

A card for Birchwood (invented): "A well. A garden. A school." Link two details to a claim with "because": for example "I think water matters because a well and a garden are here."

## Day 90: H (add the label)

Three unlabeled facts on small cards: "Roads here are narrow." "A school stands by the pond." "Fish are sold at the dock." Add a label to each: page name and who made it.

## Day 91: F (direct your own entry)

Each child directs a correction on their own entry. No new card.
