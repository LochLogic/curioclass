## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** The Bay of Bengal lies east of India, and the Arabian Sea lies west. The Himalayas form the northern edge. One region of India is plains along the Ganga and the Indus.

**Work.** Coal and iron ore are found in India. In Darjeeling, more than 80 tea gardens grow tea.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from an Indian High Commission fact sheet and the national tourism office, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** New Delhi is the capital. Mumbai is a center of India's film industry.

**Languages.** India's Constitution recognizes 22 languages. Hindi is an official language, and English can also be used for official work.
