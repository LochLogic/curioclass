# Day 84: Pick a source by its maker and its helping part

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out each country's packet (a map, a Land and Work page and a People Today page, each with a credit strip). Draw the picture sheet. Read the day 83 record: error A. Choices today are practice and never scored.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Error A from day 83 (s07-X83). Show [[s07-R84-1]]: which helps, and how? Most missed: the whole class. Otherwise a group of at most 6 works with you during pairs.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Review: in grade 1 you named kinds of source, such as a photograph, a letter, a map and a story people tell.
- **Ask.** [[s07-P84-0]] Hear two answers. A miss: show two cards side by side with one maker named. Record, no score.

#### 7 min. Choose a source, Japan

Review: the three questions card from unit 1 (who made it, when, where made). It has one new line today, What part helps? A part that helps is a title, key, label, heading or caption. Show the Land and Water page, made by Curio Class in 2026 from the Statistics Bureau of Japan's handbook, and your picture sheet.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K84-1]] Fill the card aloud for the page, tracing each answer on the page itself. Teach before observing.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K84-2]] Pip says error B: "It is the prettiest, so I pick it." Say beside it: "I pick the page because Curio Class made it and its heading Mountains helps."

#### 17 min. Pairs choose

Country groups of one to four open their packet; each page names where its facts came from. The need: "What land and water are here?" Each pair picks one source and fills its sourcing card. [[s07-P84-1]] Practice, not scored; it stays in the folder.
- Hear the first six, two asks each: first show the day 84 exit card, then ask [[s07-X84b]] and [[s07-X84c]]. About 45 seconds an ask and 10 to swap: 10 minutes.
- Then read the page aloud for up to 6 children, about 1 minute each, named on the sheet; one minute starts the pairs. A family country child sits with you and a page you find together. Never ask why.
Judged with 3 look-fors: the maker is named; the helping part is a heading, key, label or caption, not a color; the source fits the need. Error B: "the nicest picture" or "the colors".

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the card that can show where the river is." [[s07-X84]] The map is right. The note or the border is error B.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the page names and credit strips by hand. One projector: show the page and the sourcing card.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use a page from a library book or a map, and find who made it.

**Skip.** The group packets and the hearing of six.

- **5 min. Name a source.** **Say.** "Tell me two kinds of source." Expect: any two, such as a photograph or a map.
- **15 min. Choose.** Look at one page together. **Say.** "Who made it, and what part helps?" Expect: the maker's name and a heading or label. [[s07-P84-1]]
- **10 min. Compare.** Find a second page; pick the one a maker made to show the place.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X84]] [[s07-X84b]] [[s07-X84c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear four, not six. Longer: ask which source two pages could answer.

### Words and supports

Words: source, maker, key, label, heading, caption. Frames: "I pick ___ because ___ made it and the ___ helps." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A token on the note or the border, or "the nicest picture" or "the colors" (error B), goes on the record sheet. Day 85 opens with a repair of error B, then reviews a map key and gathers land and water facts from the packet.

### Credit

Ours. The Japan page is our own words from the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025, Statistics Bureau of Japan. Packet facts are our own words from the offices each page names.
