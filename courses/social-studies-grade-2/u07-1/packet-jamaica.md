## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Jamaica is an island in the Caribbean Sea. The Blue Mountains are its highest area. The Black River runs through a plain in the southwest.

**Work.** Bauxite is dug from the ground. Mining and tourism bring in money from other countries.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Jamaica tourist board and a reference encyclopedia, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Kingston is the capital and the largest city. It is on the island of Jamaica.

**Languages.** English is the official language. Many people speak Jamaican Patois, and many use both.

**Everyday life.** Ackee and saltfish is a favorite dish in Jamaica.
