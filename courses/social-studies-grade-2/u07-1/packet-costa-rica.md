## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Costa Rica has the Caribbean Sea on its east side. It has the Pacific Ocean on its west side. The Talamanca Mountains are in the country.

**Work.** Many farmers grow coffee, which is a main export. Some people fish off the Pacific coast.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Costa Rica tourism board and a reference encyclopedia, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** San José is the capital and the largest city. It is in a part of the country called the Central Valley. Quepos is a fishing town on the Pacific coast.

**Languages.** Spanish is the official language, and many people speak it. Some people speak Bribri, a language the country recognizes.
