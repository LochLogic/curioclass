# Japan hook and locator map, day 82

Japan is the teaching example and is not on the menu. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Locator map

An original map (redrawn by Curio Class from Natural Earth public domain map data, row 82B) of Asia and the Pacific Ocean with Japan marked and named. Title: "Where is Japan?" Key: a dot is a country marked for today. Credit: "Map made by Curio Class, 2026, from Natural Earth public domain data."

## Japan card (read aloud by the teacher)

"Japan is a country of islands. Seas are around the islands. Mountains cover much of the land."
Credit line: "Our own words, from the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025, Statistics Bureau of Japan, read November 2026 (row 82J1)."

## The big question (written on the board)

"What matters most about how people live in the country I chose?" The children say aloud: "Could land and water matter?"

## TEACHER ONLY

Only the three card sentences are taught about Japan. No number is printed (the page also gives a share of mountainous land; it is not used). The foreign ministry kids page was the plan's first choice and refused an automated read; the facts rest on source row 82J1, the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025 from the Statistics Bureau of Japan, checked. Retrieval question after the hook: "Our big question asks what matters about life in a country. Would 'What work do people do?' help us answer it? How?" A reason that work meets needs is shown; "it is another question" is A. Record, no score.
