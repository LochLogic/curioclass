## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Peru has the Pacific Ocean on its west side. The Andes Mountains run through the middle of the country. Peru has three regions: Coast, Highlands and Jungle.

**Work.** Farmers grow potatoes, maize and mangoes. Mines produce gold and copper. Peru sells gold, copper and coffee to other countries.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Peru tourism board and a reference encyclopedia, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Lima is the capital and the largest city. It is on the coast. Cusco and Arequipa are other cities.

**Languages.** Spanish is the official language. Many people also speak Quechua, Aymara or other Indigenous languages.

**Everyday life.** Many people in Peru enjoy cebiche, a seafood dish made with lime.
