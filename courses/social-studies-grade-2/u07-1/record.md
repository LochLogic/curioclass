# Unit 7 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into five rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the teacher's table on days 82 and 87, the second six on days 83 and 88, the third six on days 84 and 91, the fourth six on days 85 and 93, the fifth six on day 86; a child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block. Each X column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, token or drawing, and a note when the spoken part was heard (two asks on days 84 and 85). Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. Days 89 and 90 have a digital column for the one-minute turn: claim said, two facts said, own entry checked; a child who only watched is not yet seen. Day 91 has a "who was heard" column for the showing circle order. The End columns are the day 92 look-fors: items 1 and 4 are read from the retained portfolio, report, spoken showing and digital entry after written feedback and revision, not from first attempts; items 2 and 3 are on the printed sheet. Day 84 source choices are practice. Score days 85 to 87 source choices only after written feedback and the day 89 revision. Write 2 when the child shows a look-for alone, 1 with a content prompt, 0 when it was seen and not yet shown. Component groups for day 93: items 1 to 5 coverage, sources and map (C, H, G); items 6 to 13 connection, maker and feature (A, B); items 14 to 19 claim and digital (D, F); resource and bounded claim (I, E). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Keep names and private details off any display.
