## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** South Korea is on the Korean Peninsula, with sea on three sides. The East Sea is deep and clear. About three-fourths of the peninsula is mountains.

**Work.** Farmers grow rice. In 2023, South Korea grew about 3.7 million tons of it. On the warm southern coast, bananas and mangoes can now be grown.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Seoul is the capital and the largest city. The Han River flows through it. More than half of the people live in the Seoul, Gyeonggi and Incheon area.

**Languages.** Many people speak Korean. It is written in Hangeul, a script first made in 1443.

**Everyday life.** In summer, many people go to streams, rivers and beaches to cool off.
