# Section 7.1 opener: choosing a country, asking questions, choosing sources

## What the section does

Three days, 82 to 84, of 35 to 36 minutes. The unit asks: what matters most about how people live in the country I chose? Day 82 puts the big question on the board with one real example, Japan, and each child chooses a country from a menu of sixteen. Day 83 teaches that a smaller question helps only if its answer tells us something about how people live, and the class writes three supporting questions: what land and water are here, what resources support people's work, how people live today and what varies. Day 84 teaches choosing a source by who made it and which part of it helps. Day 84 choices are practice and are never scored.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a short repair or retrieval, then teaching, a pairs part where the rotating six are heard at the teacher's table (about 45 seconds for each spoken ask), an exit ticket and a question-board turn. The teacher reads every card aloud. Children read labels of one to three words. Japan is the only real country taught here, and only three facts: it is a country of islands, seas are around them, and mountains cover much of the land (one credit line on the card). Every other place and person is invented and printed "invented for practice"; the teacher says so aloud.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A supporting question earns its place.** It helps when its answer tells how people meet a need or use what is around them. A question with no link to how people live (how many windows are in the room) does not help.
- **Three questions, three report parts.** Land and water is physical features, resources and work is resources, how people live today is people. The report is made on days 89 and 90.
- **A source is chosen by its maker and by a part that helps.** The maker is named (Curio Class made every page and map; the credit line also names the office whose records it used). A helping part is a title, key, label, heading or caption, never a color.
- **Choosing a country needs no reason.** No child is asked why. A country off the menu is welcome only with the teacher's help and a printed set; never required.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip the puppet says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: every question helps (A, day 83); pick the prettiest source (B, day 84). On day 82 a child who wants only the animals is met with land and water, work and people (C, repaired on 85).

## What stays held back

The exit cards of days 82 to 84 print only on their own days. The three small pictures of day 82 first appear on the exit card.
