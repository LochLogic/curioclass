## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** New Zealand has a North Island and a South Island. The Tasman Sea lies between New Zealand and Australia. Aoraki/Mount Cook, the highest mountain, is in the middle of the South Island. Its park has 19 peaks over 3,000 meters.

**Work.** Farms make dairy foods, beef and sheep meat to sell to other countries. Kiwifruit and wood from forests are sold abroad too.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Wellington is the capital. New Zealand's Parliament has its buildings there. About 422,000 people lived in Wellington in 2023.

**Languages.** Three languages are official: English, te reo Māori and New Zealand Sign Language. About 23,000 people use New Zealand Sign Language.

**Everyday life.** Children in Years 1 to 6 may get to learn a new language at school, such as te reo Māori.
