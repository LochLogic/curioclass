# Country packet layout (the sheet each country's pages use)

Every country packet has a map, a Land and Work page and a People Today page, about sixty words each, and one original illustration marked "illustration". The packets themselves are planned only, not yet written.

**Credit strip, printed under each page:**
"Made by Curio Class, 2026, from [office or page name], read [month and year]. Where made: [place]. What it is for: to help us learn about [land and water, work, or people today]."

**Page headings (one line each):** Land and water. Work. (Land and Work page) | Cities and towns. Languages. Everyday life. (People Today page).

**Words that say how many:** use "some" and "many" and never "everyone".
