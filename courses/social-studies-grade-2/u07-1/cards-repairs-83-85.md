# Repair cards, days 83 to 85

Fresh invented cards, printed "invented for practice", one small group or the whole class. Not exits. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 83 and 84: A (a question that helps)

- Day 83 pair: "Where do people get food?" beside "How many chairs are in the gym?" Say which helps and "helps because ___".
- Day 84 fresh Japan cards: "How do people get from one island to another?" beside "Which pencil is the longest?"

## Day 85: B (choose by maker and helping part)

Need: "Where is the harbor in Pell Town?" Two invented sources: a town map "made by Curio Class, 2026" with a title and a label "harbor", and a visitor's note, "The harbor was lovely." Find who made each, then the part that helps.
