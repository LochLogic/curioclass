## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** The Pacific coast runs the whole length of Chile's west side. The Andes mountains lie along the east and cover about one third of the country. The Bio-Bio is the major river in the south.

**Work.** Copper is the most important mineral dug in Chile. Many farms in the central valleys grow grapes, apples and wheat.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Santiago is the capital. It sits in a basin about 112 kilometers from the Pacific coast, with the Andes to the east.

**Languages.** Spanish is the official language, and many people speak it. Some people also speak Mapudungun, Aymara, Quechua or Rapa Nui.

**Everyday life.** Soccer, called fútbol, is the most popular professional sport in Chile.
