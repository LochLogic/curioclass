# Day 82: Choose a country

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put up the Japan card, the 16 country cards and the sign-up chart. After class, copy unit 6's day 81 results (s06-X81, s06-P81-3, s06-P81-7, error D, error F) onto the record sheet only; nothing from unit 6 is repaired today. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Hook, Japan

Review: you learned the continents on a locator card in unit 2. Show "Where is Japan?" and point to Asia. Read: "Japan is a country of islands. Seas are around the islands. Mountains cover much of the land." Credit: our own words from the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025, Statistics Bureau of Japan, read November 2026. Write the big question on the board: "What matters most about how people live in the country I chose?"
- **Ask.** "Say it with me: Could land and water matter?" Expect yes or maybe.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Review: grade 1's supporting question, a smaller question that helps answer the big one.
- **Ask.** [[s07-P82-1]] Expect: yes, work is how people meet needs. "It is another question" is error A; note it, repair on day 83.

#### 5 min. The menu

Review: the continents. Read the 16 names by continent area. Ghana, Canada and Mexico are not on the menu. For a family country off the menu say only: "We can look for a page together."

#### 7 min. Choosing

Tables of six go to the sign-up chart, one a minute, and each child puts a token in a circle. A full country (four circles) sends the child to a second pick, and the last two minutes serve those and any family country. Never ask why. A pass is a full answer.

#### 11 min. First look and exit

Each child opens their country card (1 minute). Then:
- **Mark, 2 min.** Hand out the exit card; read: "Put a token on the picture for what you most want to find out." [[s07-X82]] Any picture is right; the flag is error A.
- **Heard, 6 min.** The first six, one at a time at your table: 45 seconds each plus about 10 to swap. [[s07-X82b]] Any question about land, water, work or people is shown. "I like their flag" is error A; "I only want to see the animals" is error C, met again on day 85. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.
- **The rest, 2 min and while waiting.** Draw one thing they hope to learn. [[s07-P82-2]] It stays in the folder.

#### 3 min. Question board

Put up the big question and the three exit pictures. Say: "Tomorrow we ask smaller questions."

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the chart on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: write the sixteen names on paper and the child points.

**Skip.** The sign-up chart and the table turns.

- **5 min. Hook.** Read the Japan card aloud. **Say.** "Could land and water matter for how people live?" Expect: yes or maybe.
- **5 min. Choose.** Read the names of the sixteen countries. The child points to one. Never ask why.
- **10 min. Look and draw.** [[s07-P82-1]] [[s07-P82-2]]
- **10 min. Wonder.** Say the big question. The child says one thing they hope to learn.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X82]] [[s07-X82b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: hear three. Longer: find two on the map.

### Words and supports

Words: country, land, water, work, people. Frame: "I hope to learn ___."

### Tomorrow

A token on the flag, or a remark about the flag (error A), goes on the record sheet, and so does "I only want to see the animals" (error C). Day 83 opens with the repair of error A from the retrieval and the exit, then teaches the three supporting questions: land and water, resources and work, people today.

### Credit

Ours. The Japan card is our own words from the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025, Statistics Bureau of Japan. Maps are redrawn from Natural Earth public domain map data.
