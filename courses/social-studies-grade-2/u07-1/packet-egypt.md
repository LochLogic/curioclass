## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** The Nile river flows north to the Mediterranean Sea. The Red Sea lies to the east. About 90 percent of Egypt is desert.

**Work.** The Delta lies between the Nile's two branches, and its land is very good for farming. Main crops include cotton, rice and wheat. The Eastern Desert holds gold, coal and oil.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the State Information Service of Egypt and EBSCO Research Starters, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Cairo is the capital and the largest city. Alexandria is the second largest and the main port. Many people live in the Nile valley and delta.

**Languages.** Arabic is the official language. Some people also speak English or French.

**Everyday life.** Bread, beans and rice are staple foods for many people.
