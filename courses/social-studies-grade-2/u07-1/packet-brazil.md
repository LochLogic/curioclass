## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Brazil has the Atlantic Ocean on its east side. The Amazon River flows through the north. The Brazilian Highlands lie inland from the coast.

**Work.** Brazil sells coffee, soybeans and orange juice to other countries. Sugarcane is grown along the coast. Iron ore is found in the ground.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Brasília is the capital. It became the capital in 1960. Many people live in big cities such as São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Salvador.

**Languages.** Portuguese is the official language, and many people speak it. Brazilian Portuguese is a little different from the Portuguese spoken in Portugal.

**Everyday life.** Many people enjoy soccer, which many call football.
