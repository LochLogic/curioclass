# Japan question cards, day 83

Four large cards on the wall. The teacher models each with: "Does the answer help us decide what matters about life in Japan? Say how." The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

1. "What are the mountains like?" (land and water)
2. "What can people do with the sea?" (resources and work)
3. "Which places do many people live in today?" (people today)
4. "How many windows are in our room?" (does not help; invented for practice)

Each card has a small picture: a mountain, a boat, a town street, a window. The three that help each get a yarn line to the big question on the wall. The answers to cards 1 to 3 are not taught: the class has only the three Japan card sentences.

Pip says A: "Every question helps, even 'How many windows are in our room?'" Beside: "This question helps because the answer tells ___ about how people live."
