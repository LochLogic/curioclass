## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Kenya has a coast on the Indian Ocean in the southeast. Mount Kenya rises about 5,199 meters in the highlands. Lake Victoria is shared with Uganda and Tanzania.

**Work.** Tea and coffee are major exports from Kenya. People grow them on farms. Farming and tourism are big parts of Kenya's economy.

Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Kenya foreign ministry country profile and EBSCO Research Starters (Kenya article), read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Nairobi is the capital city of Kenya. It is a center for trade and business in East Africa.

**Languages.** English and Kiswahili are official languages. More than 40 local languages are also spoken, such as Kikuyu, Luo, Kalenjin and Kamba.

**Everyday life.** Many people eat ugali, a maize porridge, with stew or vegetables. Football is the national sport. Kenya is known for its long-distance runners.
