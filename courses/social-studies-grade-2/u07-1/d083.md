# Day 83: Smaller questions that help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Read the day 82 record: error A, and error C (only animals), met again on day 85. Put the four Japan question cards on the wall with the big question. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Review: Pip, from kindergarten, says a wrong idea and is corrected. Error A from day 82's retrieval and exit (s07-X82). Pip says: "Every question helps, even 'How many windows are in our room?'" Say beside it: "This question helps because the answer tells ___ about how people live." Show [[s07-R83-1]] and ask which helps and how. More than a third missed: the whole class, continuing into the next part. Otherwise a group of at most 6 works with you while the rest start the pair cards.

#### 8 min. Question connections, Japan

Review: grade 1's supporting question; the question board stays up. Model four wall cards, one at a time. Teach before observing: you say each answer.
- **Ask.** [[s07-K83-1]] [[s07-K83-2]] [[s07-K83-3]] [[s07-K83-4]] For each: "Does the answer help us decide what matters about life in Japan? Say how." Expect yes with how for the first three, no for the windows. Yarn goes from each helping card to the big question; no Japan answer is read today.

#### 9 min. Pairs sort

Each pair sorts the six cards into helps and does not help, tags each helper with the land, work or people picture, and says "helps because ___" to the partner. [[s07-P83-1]] It stays in the folder. While they work, hear the first six one at a time, 45 seconds each plus about 10 to swap, 5.5 minutes in all. Ask: [[s07-X83b]] The answer is that it tells how people meet a need, such as people need water. "It is a question" or "I like it" is error A. Mark shown, shown with help or not yet seen.

#### 8 min. Our three questions

Write together on the board: What land and water are here? What resources support people's work? How do people live today, and what varies? Draw a link from each to the big question. Children copy and link. [[s07-P83-2]] It stays in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the card. Read: "Put a token on the question that helps us learn how people live." [[s07-X83]] The water question is right; the pencil or number question is error A.

### Other rooms

No yarn: draw the links.

### At home

**Swap.** No yarn: draw a line with a pencil. No envelope: cut the six cards from paper.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the hearing of six.

- **5 min. Look.** Say the big question. **Say.** "Would How many windows are in our room? help?" Expect: no, it does not tell how people live.
- **10 min. Sort.** [[s07-P83-1]]
- **10 min. Write.** [[s07-P83-2]] **Say.** "How does one of those questions help?" Expect: it tells how people live.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s07-X83]] [[s07-X83b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: model two wall cards and sort four cards. Longer: ask for a fourth question that helps.

### Words and supports

Words: helps, question, land, work, people. Frame: "This question helps because the answer tells ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A token on the pencil or number question, or "it is a question" (error A), goes on the record sheet. Day 84 opens with a fresh question card for error A, then teaches choosing a source by who made it and which part helps.
