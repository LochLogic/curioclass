## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Vietnam is long and narrow. The South China Sea lies along its east and south. The Annamese Cordillera mountains run through the center. The Red River and the Mekong River each reach the sea in a wide delta.

**Work.** Rice fields grow on the Red River delta. The Central Highlands are coffee country.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Hanoi is the capital, in the north. Ho Chi Minh City is the largest city, in the south.

**Languages.** Vietnamese is the official language. Some people in the highlands speak other languages, such as Tai languages in the north.

**Everyday life.** Pho is a well-known Vietnamese noodle soup, especially linked to Hanoi and street vendors.
