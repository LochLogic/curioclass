# Exit cards, days 82 to 84

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other ask is spoken and heard from the rotating six. The teacher reads every ask aloud, one at a time. Exit and check cards are first seen at the child's own turn. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 82 exit card: country card

**Two questions:** "What do people eat?" and "Is my shirt red?"
**Mark:** Put a token on the question that helps our big question.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say one thing you hope to learn about how people live in your country." Never ask why the country was chosen.

## Day 83 exit card: three question pictures

**Three cards:** a tap and a bucket, "Where do people get water?"; a pencil, "What color is my pencil?"; a number, "What is my favorite number?"
**Mark:** Put a token on the question that helps us learn how people live.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say how 'Where do people get water?' helps us learn how people live."

## Day 84 exit card: Kestrel Bend

**Three cards:** a town map with a title, a key and labels, "made by Curio Class, 2026"; a visitor's note, "I loved the river, it was lovely"; a plain decorated border.
**Mark:** Put a token on the card that can show where the river is.
**Spoken, the six one at a time, two asks:** "Point to who made the town map." Then: "Point to the part of the town map that helps you find the river."

## TEACHER ONLY: key

Day 82: the eating question; a token on the shirt question is A. Spoken: any hope about how people live ("What are the mountains like?", "What do they eat?"); "I like their flag" or "What toys do they have?" is A; "I only want to see the animals" is C (met again on 85).
Day 83: the water question; the pencil or number question is A. Spoken: it tells how people meet a need ("people need water"); "it is a question" or "I like it" is A.
Day 84: the map; the note or the border is B. Spoken: the maker line "Curio Class, 2026"; then a key or a label; "the colors" or "the nicest picture" is B.
Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
