## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** France has coasts on the Atlantic Ocean and the Mediterranean Sea. The Pyrenees mountains are in the south and the Alps are in the east. The Seine river flows through Paris.

**Work.** France sells many farm products to other countries. Many farms grow wheat, corn and barley. Wine is made in regions such as Burgundy, Bordeaux and Champagne.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Paris is the capital and by far the largest city. Marseille is a large port on the Mediterranean Sea, and Lyon is also a large city.

**Languages.** French is the official language. Some people also speak regional languages such as Alsatian, Flemish or Catalan.

**Everyday life.** Football, rugby, tennis and cycling are popular sports. The Tour de France is a yearly bike race.
