# Pairs sort, day 83

Six invented question cards for each pair, printed "invented for practice". Pairs sort them into "helps" and "does not", tag each that helps with the land, work or people picture, and say "helps because ___" to the partner. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

Helps:
- "What jobs do people do?" (work picture)
- "Where do the rivers run?" (land picture)
- "What homes do people live in?" (people picture)

Does not help:
- "How many steps are in our hall?"
- "Which crayon is the longest?"
- "How many clocks are in the library?"

<!-- TEACHER-ONLY BLOCK: begins -->

## TEACHER ONLY

The first three help; the last three do not. A pair that says a jobs question "helps because it tells how people earn a living" is showing the link. Do not use these cards on the exit.
