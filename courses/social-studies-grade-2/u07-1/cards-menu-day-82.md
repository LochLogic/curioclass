# The menu and the sign-up chart, day 82

Sixteen country cards, one for each menu country, plus one sign-up chart. A country card shows only the country's outline and its name; the three small pictures are on the exit card and appear nowhere else on day 82 before the exit. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## The menu, by continent area

| Continent area | Countries |
|---|---|
| North America | Costa Rica, Jamaica |
| South America | Brazil, Peru, Chile |
| Europe | France, Norway |
| Africa | Egypt, Kenya, South Africa |
| Asia | India, Vietnam, South Korea |
| Australia and wider Oceania | Australia, Papua New Guinea, New Zealand |

Ghana, Canada and Mexico are left off the menu. Keep unit 2's continent name Australia; Oceania names the wider region. Continent grouping follows the continents taught in unit 2 (source row 10E, weak: Wikipedia only). Say each name as the country says it where known; pronunciation is not yet checked (planned only, not yet written: a speaker or an audio check before the day).

## The sign-up chart

A chart with sixteen boxes, each holding the country name and four small circles (at most four children for a country). Tables of six go one at a time. A full country sends the child to a second pick. A family country that is not on the menu is the teacher's job by day 84 and is never asked about: say only "we can look for a page together."

## Country card

A line outline of the country (redrawn by Curio Class from Natural Earth public domain map data, row 82B; a map keeps to the data's borders and shows no disputed border) and its name in 18 point type. Nothing else.
