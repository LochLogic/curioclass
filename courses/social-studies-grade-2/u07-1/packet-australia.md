## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Australia is the flattest continent, and apart from Antarctica the driest. A large part of it is dry. The Murray-Darling is its longest river system.

**Work.** Many farms grow wheat. Farms sold 34.8 million tonnes of wheat in 2024-25. Farms also keep cattle: 29.7 million at the end of June 2025.

Credit strip: "Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Australian Bureau of Statistics (Geography and Climate; Agriculture) and Tourism Australia, read November 2026. Where made: Curio Class. What it is for: to help us learn about land and water, and work."

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Canberra is the capital. Sydney is the largest city, with 5.2 million people in 2021. Almost 80 percent of people live in the east.

**Languages.** Some people also use other languages, such as Mandarin, Arabic and Vietnamese. In 2021, people used 167 Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander languages.

**Everyday life.** In one survey, walking for exercise was the most popular activity.
