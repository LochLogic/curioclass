# Japan set, day 84

Three printed cards for the teacher's choice model, each with the sourcing card. The need: where are Japan's mountains? The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

1. **Japan map.** Made by Curio Class, 2026, from Natural Earth public domain map data (row 82B). Title "Japan"; key: a triangle is a mountain area; labels for the islands.
2. **Japan Land and Water page.** Made by Curio Class, 2026, from the Statistical Handbook of Japan 2025, Statistics Bureau of Japan (row 82J1). Headings: "Islands", "Seas", "Mountains". Lines: "Japan is a country of islands." "Seas are around the islands." "Mountains cover much of the land." The page has no work facts; it is called "Land and Water" and not "Land and Work".
3. **A visitor's note (invented for practice).** "The paper lanterns were pretty." No maker's name.

Read the card aloud: who made it, when, where made, and "What part helps?" Pip says B: "The note has the prettiest picture, so I pick it." Beside: "I pick the map because Curio Class made it to show land, and its key shows the mountains."
