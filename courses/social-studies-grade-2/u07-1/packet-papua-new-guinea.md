## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Papua New Guinea has a mountain middle with a high, crowded Highlands region. Mount Wilhelm, at 4,509 metres, is the highest peak.

**Work.** The country sells coffee, logs and palm oil to other countries. Port Moresby is the capital, and the National Parliament is there.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Port Moresby is the capital and largest city. Lae is another city.

**Languages.** More than 700 languages are spoken. Many people speak Tok Pisin. English is an official language.

**Everyday life.** Many people use more than one language. They may use their own language, Tok Pisin and English at different times.
