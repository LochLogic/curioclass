## Land and Work page

**Land and water.** Deep fjords cut into Norway's coast, and thousands of islands lie offshore. Hardangervidda is a very large high plain. The highest point is Galdhøpiggen, at 2,469 meters.

**Work.** Many of Norway's resources come from the water: fish, oil and natural gas, and fast rivers that make electricity. Some factories make ships, aluminum and paper.

## People Today page

**Cities and towns.** Oslo is the capital. Bergen and Trondheim are also large cities. Most people live in towns and cities.

**Languages.** Norwegian is written in two ways, Bokmål and Nynorsk. Some people speak Sami, which is official in nine municipalities.

**Everyday life.** Skiing is the national sport, and many children learn to ski before school.
