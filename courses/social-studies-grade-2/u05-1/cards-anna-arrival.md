# Anna Arrival and route map

Real person, told as a summary in our words. Credit line printed under the card: "Anna Zagar Klarich, interview EI-89, 12 September 1991, Ellis Island Oral History Collection, National Park Service. Summary by Curio Class." The teacher reads the card once and says it is a real person's memory. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## The card (read aloud)

"Anna was 18 years old in 1920. She left Yugoslavia, the country named on the page where her memory is kept, and crossed the sea. She came to the United States. Her mother and her aunt met her. They hugged, and they all cried."

**Picture:** A girl with a bag at a ship's rail. A woman and an older woman waiting on a dock with open arms. A speech bubble marked "Anna remembers".
**Label (three questions of the card):** Who made it: Anna, told to a National Park Service interviewer. When: she arrived in 1920; she told it in 1991. Where: Ellis Island, New York Harbor. Printed as "who", "when", "where".
**Teacher note:** The country name is the one printed on the page; today that place is several countries, so the class says only Europe. The page does not name Anna's town or ship and does not say why she came. The class never says it was only for family: "the card tells us she was met by family; it does not say the rest". Other memories on the same page are held for day 65.

## Route map (day 58, day 63, day 64, day 68)

A printed world map naming only the seven continents (no countries). A pencil arrow is drawn on the map from Europe to North America. The three-question card sits beside it. The child's finger follows from where she left to where she arrived.
