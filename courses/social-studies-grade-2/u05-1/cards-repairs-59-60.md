# Repair cards, days 59 and 60

Every card prints "invented for practice". Each repair is at most 5 minutes. Pip says the wrong idea and the right idea is said beside it. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Two motives (error A, day 59)

**Picture:** Two cards. Ola's card: "Ola moves to the city to work at a bakery." Pim's card: "Pim moves to the city to live near a grandmother." The child points to the line that supports each: "The card says ___."
**Say:** "Two cards, two reasons." Pip: "Everyone moves for work."

## A claim and its card (error K, day 59)

**Picture:** A card: "Wes walks a day to a new mill town. He hears the mill needs workers." A second card says only "Wes was happy." The child puts a finger on the line that supports "Wes moved for work".
**Say:** "The card says ___." Pip: "Wes moved for a better life, because I think so."

## Reorder a chain (error B, day 60)

**Picture:** Three cut cards for a family in a smoky town: "Smoke fills the town and officials say to leave." "The family drives to a safe town." "Afterward, they sleep in a school gym." The child puts the cards in order on the chain strip.
**Say:** "Reason first, then the move, then afterward." Pip: "The family slept in a gym, so that is why they left."

## Trip and told to leave (error C, day 60 and day 61)

**Picture:** Two cards. Card 1: a family plans a trip to a lake town for a new job and packs the car. Card 2: officials say a family's house is not safe and they cannot stay. The child sorts each under "chosen" or "forced" with "Could they have stayed?"
**Say:** "A car or a ship is how they traveled. The question is whether they could have stayed." Pip: "They rode in a car, so they chose."

## Key (teacher sheet only)

A: work, family; the lines naming each. K: the mill needs workers. B: smoke, drive, gym. C: card 1 chosen; card 2 forced. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
