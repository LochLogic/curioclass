# Chain cards, day 59

Every card prints "invented for practice". One actor in each scene. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Two story cards (taught, 8 minutes)

- **Ravi:** Ravi's town has one small school. A big school opens in a town by the sea. Ravi's family decides to move there. Afterward, Ravi learns at the big school.
- **Hale:** The company that owns every house in Hale's town sells the land. The new owner tells every family to leave by the end of the month. Afterward, they rent rooms in the next town and the neighbors help.

The cards go on the chain strip as cut boxes: reason, move, afterward. "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." Test question: "Could they have stayed?" Ravi: they decided, chosen. Hale: they were told to leave, forced. Pip says C, then B.

## Pairs work (8 minutes)

Cut cards from three stories, each in three pieces (reason, move, afterward), mixed:
- **Fenn** is offered a job in a shop by a cousin. Fenn moves to the shop town. Afterward, Fenn sells cheese. (chosen)
- **Cass** lives in a dry town. The only well in Cass's town runs dry, and no water can be brought in. Cass's family leaves. Afterward, they live in a town by a river with plenty of water. (forced)
- **Dov**'s family likes the lake. A house by the lake costs less than their house. They move to the lake town. Afterward, Dov learns to fish. (chosen)

Each pair orders each strip and says "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." with chosen or forced. The six are heard.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Fenn: chosen; Cass: forced; Dov: chosen. Order: reason, move, afterward. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
