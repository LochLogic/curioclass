# Unit 5 record sheet: our groups

Write each child's name down the left. Group the class into five rotating sixes. The first six are heard at the teacher's table on days 58, 63 and 68, the second six on days 59, 64 and 69, the third six on days 60, 65 and 71, the fourth six on days 61 and 66, and the fifth six on days 62 and 67; any child not yet heard is heard in the next supported-work block. Each E column is one exit day: one tick when the child marks the card by circling, boxing or drawing an arrow, and a note when the spoken part was heard. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. The End columns are the day 70 sitting, one cell for each look-for: 2 when the child shows it alone, 1 when it needs a content prompt, 0 when the part was heard and not yet seen. Leave a cell blank when a part was not heard. Each child says the lines of items 1 to 7 to the teacher by sevenths across the sitting, and points on the packet for the rest. The four groups for day 71 are items 1 and 4 (C, K, B, N), items 2 and 3 (I, A), items 5 and 6 (J, E, L, D, M) and item 7 (F, G, H). A child may pass; try again another day and put the date in the cell. Never write a count of reminders.
