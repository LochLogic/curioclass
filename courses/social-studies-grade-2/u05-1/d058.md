# Day 58: Why do people move?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the first six on the record sheet. Say aloud: Anna is real; Teo, Dara, Juno, Imre, Pia, Nell, Orla, Bram and Sela are made up. No child tells about their family; any may pass. Key: key-exits-58-59.md.

### The day

#### 4 min. Hook

Hand back day 57's exit ticket (s04-X57) and marked written tasks (s04-P57-1 s04-P57-2 s04-P57-3 s04-P57-4 s04-P57-5 s04-P57-6 s04-P57-7 s04-P57-10 s04-P57-11 s04-P57-12 s04-P57-14). Children not yet shown (error O, error N) follow the arrow below with a finger. Read Anna Arrival once, with its credit line, as a real person's memory. Review: in unit 1 you asked of a card who made it, when, where. **Ask.** Who made it? When? Where? Expect: Anna told it to an interviewer; she arrived in 1920 and told it in 1991; Ellis Island, New York Harbor. Review: in unit 4 the arrow ran from where a thing is made to where it is sold. Today it shows where a person left and arrived. [[s05-P58-1]] Say Europe only.

#### 3 min. Question board

Write "Why do people leave home for a new place?" Children give possible reasons; write one a card. Passing is fine.

#### 3 min. Retrieval, unit 2

Review: you placed the seven continents in unit 2. Each pair points on the small map as you name each: [[s05-P58-0]] Scan. A miss joins the small group below.

#### 9 min. Five reasons

Read the five cards one at a time. A move is going to live in a new place. A reason is why a person moved (in unit 2 it was why you think something). A possible reason has a line of the card that shows it. After each card the class says the frame. [[s05-P58-2]] Pip says error A: "Everyone moves for work." Say beside it: Teo moved for work, Dara for family; two cards, two reasons. Pip says error K: "Juno moved for a better life, because I think so." Say beside it: the card says a hillside slid onto the road.

#### 11 min. Pairs work

Each pair sorts Nell, Orla and Bram under the five reason pictures (4 minutes): [[s05-P58-3]] Each child then tells the partner, for one card, [[s05-P58-4]] The six, 45 seconds each, 4.5 minutes, come to your table in turn for [[s05-X58b]]. A small group of at most 6 redoes the continents.

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X58]], 1 minute. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add the reasons. **Ask.** Could Anna's be one of these? Expect: the page tells us she was met by family; the card does not say the rest.

### Other rooms

Draw the pictures and route on the board; pairs sort word cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw five small pictures.

**Skip.** The pairs and the continent map.

- **5 min. Anna.** Read Anna's card. **Say.** "Where did she leave, and where did she arrive?" Expect: Europe, then North America.
- **10 min. Five reasons.** Read the five cards. **Say.** "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___." Expect: work, family, safe, land or free, with a line.
- **10 min. Sort.** Your child writes a word for each. [[s05-P58-3]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X58]] [[s05-X58b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three of the five cards. Longer: another reason the cards do not give, and what would show it.

### Words and supports

Words: move, reason, possible, work, family, safe, land, free. Frame: "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___." Pointing and drawing count.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 teaches chosen and forced and the chain. Error A shows as "everyone moves for work" on the spoken exit; error K as work with no line, or a reason the card does not give. Day 59's opening repair picks up error A and its small group error K.
