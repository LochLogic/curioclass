# Day 59: Chosen or forced, and the chain

## Teacher page

### Before class

Name today's six, the second six on the record sheet. Say aloud: Anna is a real person; Ravi, Hale, Fenn, Cass, Dov, Ola, Pim, Wes, Mina, Tavi and every town are made up. Any child may pass. Key: key-exits-58-59.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 58's sort (s05-P58-3) and exit ticket (s05-X58), marked shown, shown with help or not yet seen. If many missed error A on day 58's spoken exit s05-X58b (everyone moves for work), do the repair: Ola's and Pim's cards, each child pointing to the line that supports each. [[s05-P59-1]] Say: two cards, two reasons. Error K goes to the small group below.

#### 3 min. Retrieval, grade 1

Review: in grade 1 you put steps in order with first, then, so. Three pictures of baking go in order: [[s05-P59-3]] This is the bridge to reason, move, afterward.

#### 8 min. The chain, and who decided

Read Ravi's card alone. Fill the strip: "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." [[s05-P59-4]] **Ask.** Could they have stayed? Expect: yes, the family decided; chosen means they decided. Then read Hale's card alone. [[s05-P59-5]] Forced means they could not stay safely or freely. Pip says error C: "Hale's family rode in a car, so they chose." Say beside it: the question is whether they could have stayed. Pip says error B: "Hale learns at the new school, so Hale moved." Say beside it: reason first, then the move, then afterward.

#### 6 min. Which reason is better supported?

Two reason cards for Anna: to be with family; to see a big city. Review: in unit 2 you said "I think ___ because ___". [[s05-P59-6]] Say: the card does not say why she came. Her mother and aunt met her, so family has a line; a big city has none. Better supported is not certain.

#### 8 min. Pairs work

Each pair orders the cut cards of Fenn, Cass and Dov on a strip and says each chain, with chosen or forced and "could they have stayed" (answers: Fenn chosen, Cass forced, Dov chosen). Each child fills one strip: [[s05-P59-7]] Each child says the chain to the partner: [[s05-P59-8]] Hear four of the six, 45 seconds each with the swap, 3 minutes, two for [[s05-X59b]] and two for [[s05-X59c]]. A small group of at most 6 takes 4 minutes on error K: Wes's card, a finger on the line. [[s05-P59-2]]

#### 3 min. Exit ticket

Children mark [[s05-X59]], 1 minute. Mark: shown, shown with help, not yet seen. While the rest mark, hear the other two of the six, 45 seconds each, one for [[s05-X59b]] and one for [[s05-X59c]].

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "Who decided to move?"

### Other rooms

Draw three boxes on paper; write the cards on slips.

### At home

**Swap.** No chain strip: fold paper in three.

**Skip.** The groups and the six.

- **5 min. Bread.** Your child puts mix, bake, eat in order. **Say.** "First, then, so." Expect: first mix, then bake, so we eat.
- **10 min. Ravi and Hale.** Read each card. **Say.** "Could they have stayed?" Expect: Ravi yes; Hale no.
- **10 min. Dov.** Your child fills the boxes. [[s05-P59-7]]
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X59]] [[s05-X59b]] [[s05-X59c]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Cass alone in the pairs work. Longer: a chosen move that was hard.

### Words and supports

Words: chosen, forced, because, afterward, decided. Frame: "Because ___, they moved ___. Afterward ___." Pointing and drawing count.
