# Section 5.1 opener: why people move, and the chain

## What the section does

Two days, 58 and 59, of 35 minutes. The unit asks: why do people leave home for a new place? Day 58 gives five possible reasons for a move (work, family, safety, land, freedom) and the habit of pointing to the line of a card that shows a reason. Day 59 teaches chosen and forced with one test question, "Could they have stayed?", and a chain strip: because (the reason), they moved (the move), afterward (what happened next). Both days open with Anna, one real person whose arrival is told as a short summary in our words with a credit line. All other people in the stories are invented, and the teacher says so aloud.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a short repair or retrieval, then teaching, a part for pairs, the rotating six heard at the teacher's table, an exit ticket and a question-board turn. The teacher reads every card aloud. Children read labels of one to three words. No child is asked about their own family, home, money, religion or how they came here, and any child may pass.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A reason is possible when a card gives a line for it.** It is not certain unless the teller says so. The teacher says "the card does not say" aloud when a card does not.
- **People move for several reasons at once.** Some reasons push people from a place and some pull them to another. These are adult words and are not taught.
- **Chosen and forced.** One test: could they have stayed? Chosen means they decided. Forced means they could not stay safely or freely. A hard road is still a chosen trip, and choosing a road does not make a flight chosen.
- **The chain.** Because (the reason), they moved (the move). Afterward (what happened next). Reason first, then the move, then afterward.
- **Anna.** The page tells of meeting family at the end of a crossing. It does not say that was her only reason. The class never says it was.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip the puppet says each wrong idea once and the right idea is said beside it: everyone moves for work (A, day 58); I think so, so it is true, with no line of a card (K, day 58); the arrival came first (B, day 59); they rode a car or a ship, so they chose (C, day 59).

## What stays held back

The exit cards for days 58 and 59 (Weaver Card, Land Card and the two market cards) are printed only on their days.
