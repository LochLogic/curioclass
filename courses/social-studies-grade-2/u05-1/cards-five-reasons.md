# Five reason stories and sorting cards, day 58

Every card prints "invented for practice". One actor in each card. The teacher says aloud that Teo, Dara, Juno, Imre, Pia, Nell, Orla and Bram are made up. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Five reason stories (read aloud, 9 minutes)

- **Work:** Teo is a boat builder. His harbor has no boats to build this year, so a shipyard in the next bay gives him a bench. Teo moves to the next bay to build boats.
- **Family:** Dara's older brother lives in a town by the hills. Dara goes to live in the town where her brother lives.
- **Safety:** A hillside slides onto the road in Juno's village. Juno's family looks for a safer town and moves.
- **Land:** Imre keeps goats. A valley has more grass than his hill. Imre leads his goats to the valley.
- **Freedom:** Pia is a printer. Her town says nobody may print newspapers. Pia moves to a town where printers are free.

Each has one picture and one word label (work, family, safe, land, free). After each: "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___."

## Three cards for the pairs work

- **Nell** is a baker. A bakery in a seaside town needs a baker. Nell moves there. (work)
- **Orla** lives in a dry place. A river valley has wet fields. Orla moves to the valley and plants a garden. (land)
- **Bram** says, "My grandmother lives in the next town and she needs company." Bram moves to the next town. (family)

The pair sorts the three cards under the five reason pictures. For one: "A possible reason is ___. The card says ___." The six are heard.

## Key (teacher sheet only)

Nell: work. Orla: land. Bram: family. Sample line from each card: the line that names the need, the wet fields, the grandmother. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
