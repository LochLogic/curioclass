# Exit cards, days 58 and 59

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Reserved: print each only on the exit of its day. The key is on the teacher's sheet only and is never printed on a child's card. One judged paper row on each quarter page; the other asks are spoken and heard from the rotating six. The art is described here and has not been drawn or looked at yet (planned only, not yet written). Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 58 exit card: Weaver Card

**Picture:** A girl at a loom. A far town with a market. A sister waving at a gate.
**Card line:** "Sela hears that a far town needs weavers. Her sister lives in that town too. Sela moves there."
**Mark:** Circle a reason the card gives for Sela's move: work, safe, free.
**Spoken, the six one at a time:** "Say one reason a person might move that the Weaver Card does not give." The six mark the card line first, then hear this ask.

## Day 59 exit cards: Land Card and two market cards

**Land Card picture:** A girl in thin soil. A river plain with a green field. A cousin waving.
**Card line:** "Tavi's cousin visits each summer. Tavi says, 'My soil is thin; I want deep soil to grow food.' Tavi moves to a river plain and plants a field."
**Spoken, the six one at a time (two asks, one at a time):** "Say which reason the Land Card supports better: deep soil to grow food, or the cousin's visits." Then: "On the Land Card, tell what happened in order: first the reason, then the move, then what happened next."
**Card 1 picture:** A market stall being sold and a big market town. **Line:** "Mina's family sells their stall and moves to a bigger market town to sell more."
**Card 2 picture:** A river over the roofs of Wren Village. **Line:** "A river rises over Wren Village and the water is not safe. The families must leave tonight."
**Mark (the one judged paper row of the day):** Circle the card that shows a chosen move: Card 1 or Card 2.
