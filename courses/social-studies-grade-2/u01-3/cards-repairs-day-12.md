# Repair cards for day 12 (errors G and K)

Say first: "These cards are made up for practice."

## Error G: one land on the globe and on a flat map

**Picture:** A globe with the land of Australia on it, beside a flat world card with the same land. Each pair traces the land on both with a finger and says what changed (the shape or the size is changed on the flat map).

## Error K: two job cards matched to the two maps

1. **hold a ship's course** (a ship with a straight dotted line).
2. **compare two sizes** (two blobs, one clearly bigger).

Pairs match each job card to the World Projection Card that suits it: the Mercator card for holding a ship's course, the Peters card for comparing sizes. Say: "This map suits the job because ___."

## For the teacher

Pip says a flat map keeps everything the globe does; beside it, any flat map changes something. A ship's constant direction is not always the shortest route. Four minutes for K and five for G: the more common goes to the whole class, the other to a small group.
