# Teacher key: Exit cards, days 9 to 12

Teacher only. Keep apart from the child pieces (cards-exits-9-12.md); never hand to children.

## Key

- Day 9: the paper star. The balloon or the bowl is error G. Saying Earth gets gaps or holes is error Q; a round skin must be stretched, folded or opened to lie flat.
- Day 10: no. The map stretches lands far from the middle. Yes is error G. It keeps the shapes of very small places right; saying it keeps everything the globe does is error G.
- Day 11: the Mercator card. Credit: who drew it (Lena Hart), the year (2026), where made (a school gym); the coastline data is the data, not the maker (error E); the place shown is not the place made (error J). Circling the other card because the first is useless is error K.
- Day 12: five labels of the right kind, one for each arrow; the key's wavy line means river. Which ocean, river or range is not scored. Amazon River at the river arrow, like Brazil at the country arrow, is error H; Writing North America or Brazil at the country arrow is error H; reading a symbol as the wrong feature is error A.
