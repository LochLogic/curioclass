# Exit cards, days 9 to 12

Each card is marked by every child, then one spoken part is heard from the day's six. Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white, labels of one to three words. No exit card reuses a map or a picture from the day's teaching.

## Day 9 exit card: three pictures

**Picture:** Three boxes in a row: a flat paper star, a balloon, and a salad bowl. A label under each.

**Mark:** Circle the one that can lie flat with no gap and no stretch. **Spoken:** "Why can a balloon's skin not lie flat as it is?"

## Day 10 exit card: a Mercator map with two islands

**Picture:** A small Mercator-style card with a compass rose. Near the top edge, one made-up lake is drawn wide. Near the middle, another is drawn narrower. A key line says: both lakes are made up.

**Mark:** Does the drawing prove the top lake is bigger? Circle yes or no. **Spoken, two stems, one after the other:** "Why not?" "What does this kind of map keep right?"

## Day 11 exit card: two world cards

**Picture:** A pair of world cards, one Mercator and one Peters, both centered on a different ocean from the teaching cards (the Pacific). Each has a credit line printed under it: Drawn by Lena Hart, 2026, made in a school gym (invented for practice). Coastlines from Natural Earth, public domain.

**Mark:** Circle the map that keeps the shapes of small places near us right. **Spoken:** "Read me the credit line on one card."

## Day 12 exit card: North America outline

**Picture:** A blank outline of North America with five arrows. A key box shows a wavy line is a river, triangles are a mountain range, ripples are an ocean or lake. A word bank of fourteen words printed below: Canada, United States, Mexico, Pacific Ocean, Atlantic Ocean, Arctic Ocean, Great Lakes, Mississippi River, St. Lawrence River, Rocky Mountains, Appalachian Mountains, North America, Amazon River, Brazil.

**Mark:** Write a label from the word bank at each arrow: a country, an ocean, the Great Lakes, a river and a mountain range. **Spoken:** "How did the key help you find the river?" (heard while the others write)
