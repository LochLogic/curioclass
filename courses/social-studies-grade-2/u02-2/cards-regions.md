# Region cards (day 23)

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Three desert cards

**Picture:** Red Sand Flats (red sand and a single bush), Stone Basin (gray rock and a dry streambed) and Wind Mesa (flat hill and a few grasses). Each card shows a rain gauge nearly empty.
**Label:** Each place has little rain and few plants.

## Three rainforest cards

**Picture:** Fern Coast (tall trees and ferns), Rain Hollow (a wet green valley with a stream) and Palm Bend (palms along a river). Each card shows a rain gauge nearly full.
**Label:** Each place has much rain and thick plants.

## A wet coast and a dry coast (error E)

**Picture:** Windward Cove, a bay with green hills and clouds over the land, rain falling. Lee Cove, a bay with brown hills and a clear sky. Both lie beside the sea.
**Label:** Both are beside the sea. One is wet and one is dry.

## How life fits (read aloud)

A dry region's farmers bring water. A wet region's builders add roofs and covered paths. The class says: "These places share ___, so ___."

## Three people in one coastal town (error F)

**Picture:** Sandpiper Town, a harbour town. Three people: a baker with bread, a teacher with a book, a ferry pilot at the wheel. They live in the same town.
**Label:** One place. Three kinds of work.

## Key (teacher)

The cards are ours. A region is an area that shares something (grade 1: a big area made of many towns, with "that shares something" added). Do not name a real region's jobs.
