# Marsh Town, the sorting cards and the rain strip (day 21)

Every Marsh Town and sorting card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Marsh Town: two weather reports and one many-year card

**Picture:** A small town beside a marsh, a church tower, a row of houses and a boardwalk.
**Report 1:** Monday. Wind and light rain over the marsh.
**Report 2:** Thursday. Bright sun and a warm breeze.
**Many-year card:** Marsh Town. Over many years, most springs have many rainy days and winters are mild.

## Six labelled cards (day 21 teaching; new wrong labels on day 22's warm-up)

Each card carries Pip's label, W for today's weather or C for what usually happens over many years. Children say agree or fix, with a reason.
1. Thick snow fell this morning. Pip: C. (right: W, one day)
2. A cold wind blew at noon. Pip: W. (right)
3. The sky was gray at lunch. Pip: W. (right)
4. Winters here are usually cold. Pip: W. (right: C, many years)
5. This coast is rainy most of the year. Pip: C. (right)
6. Autumn is usually windy here. Pip: C. (right)
Day 22: Pip's new labels, cards 2 and 6 wrong.

## The sea, vapor, cloud and rain strip (day 21 repair, error C)

**Picture:** Four panels in a row. Panel 1: the sea under a sun. Panel 2: thin lines rising from the water. Panel 3: a cloud over land. Panel 4: rain falling on a green hill.
**Say:** A finger traces from the sea, up to the cloud and down to the rain.

## Key (teacher)

NOAA's weather and climate page: weather is short-term conditions of the atmosphere; climate is the average daily weather over a long time at a place. The strip rests on the USGS water cycle page. The exit cards use other sentences; do not read them here.
