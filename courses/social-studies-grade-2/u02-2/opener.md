# Section 2.2 opener: how land and water shape life

## What the section does

Three days, 21 to 23, of about 40 minutes. Day 21 separates weather, what the air is doing today, from climate, what usually happens over many years. Day 22 shows four ways the place of land and water shapes the weather and what grows: height, the sea beside a ridge, nearness to the equator, and a desert. Day 23 names a region, an area that shares something, and shows how life there fits it. Each day the class also tidies the errors the last exit showed, and about six children are heard at the globe in a twelve-minute supported-work block while the rest revise their maps from the written cues returned on day 21.

## How the days run

Day 21: two repairs from the oceans, weather and climate, pairs sorting, supported work, the exit. Day 22: a warm-up repair, four places, pairs matching, supported work, the exit. Day 23: two repairs, regions, supported work, the exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Weather is today; climate is the pattern over many years.** One snowy day is not a cold climate.
- **Higher is colder** in the lower air, because the warmth comes mostly from the ground. The card shows it; the Sun's distance is not the reason.
- **A ridge can split wet from dry.** Wet air from the sea rises and cools over a ridge, rain falls on the side the air climbs, and less is left for the far side. Not every coast is rainy.
- **Near the equator it is often warm and rainy;** a desert is dry and can be hot or cold.
- **A region is an area that shares something,** such as little rain. The place does not choose any person's job: people in one region do many kinds of work.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once with the right idea beside it. One snowy day means it is always cold (D). Every coast is rainy (E). Higher places are warmer because they are closer to the Sun (O). Every desert is hot (K). Everyone near the sea is a fisher (F). Pip also says the ocean ideas of the last section, B, C, L and M, which are repaired on day 21.

## Supplies for the section

The globe, the Marsh Town and sorting cards, the four-place cards, region cards, repair cards, exit cards, the region frame, the cue sheet, a paper arrow, the record sheet and the question board. Every invented place is printed "invented for practice".
