# Day 21: Weather and climate

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fifth six on the record sheet. Write one cue on each returned map from days 17 to 20: a tick or an arrow from the wall Atlas, and one line about one miss. Count which of day 20's errors (B, L, M or C) most children made. Hand back day 20's cards (s02-X20) and maps (s02-P20-1). Key: key-exits-21-23.md.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Two parts of 5 minutes. Part 1, errors B, L and M from day 20: a finger starts at one map edge and follows the water round the globe, through the Southern Ocean round Antarctica. Say: "One water. Five names." Part 2, error C: trace the rain strip with a finger, from the sea up to the cloud and down to the rain. The part more children needed is for the whole class; the other goes to a small group in the last 3 minutes of supported work.

#### 9 min. Weather and climate

Review: in grade 1 you learned environmental feature, which includes weather. Weather is what the air is doing today: sun, rain, wind, fog, snow. Show the two Marsh Town reports (invented for practice): Monday, wind and light rain; Thursday, bright sun and a warm breeze. Each is weather, one day. Then the many-year card: most springs have many rainy days and winters are mild. That is climate: what a place is usually like over many years, a pattern. Show the six labelled cards: Pip has labelled each W or C, and two are wrong. For each, say agree or fix, with a reason.

Pip says: "It snowed Tuesday, so it is always cold here." Beside it: "One day is weather; climate is what usually happens over many years." **Ask.** Is one snowy day the climate? Expect: no, climate is many years.

#### 4 min. Pairs work

Each pair says one sentence about Marsh Town's climate, then writes the end of it ([[s02-P21-2]]). First sentences are practice.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 9 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 90 seconds each, for a spoken reason (s02-X21b) and four globe locations from the prompt cards ([[s02-P21-1]]). Everyone else reads the cue on the returned map, changes the map and fills the cue sheet. The last 3 minutes go to the small group for the other repair. This block has about 3 minutes of slack; if a child needs longer, hear fewer locations.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "How do land and water shape life?"

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud the three sentences (s02-X21). Mark 2 minutes. The six were heard in supported work. One day taken as always, such as one dry day for a dry climate, is error D.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The cue sheet.

- **10 min. Find it.** Point to a continent your adult names.
  **Say.** "Where does it lie?" Expect: a compass word.
- **15 min. Weather and climate.** Say what the air is doing today, then what it is usually like in winter.
  **Say.** "Is today weather or climate?" Expect: weather.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X21]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X21b]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one report and the many-year card. Longer: a child says one weather sentence and one climate sentence of their own.

### Words and supports

Words: weather, climate, pattern, usually, today, many years. Frame: "That is weather because ___." Pointing counts.
