# Day 23: Regions

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the first six again, those least heard. Lay the repair cards and the region cards. Hand back day 22's cards (s02-X22) and matches (s02-P22-2). Say every place is invented for practice. Key: key-exits-21-23.md.

### The day

#### 10 min. Return and repair

Two parts of 5 minutes. Part 1, error O from day 22: Slate Hill (invented), 3 at the top, 11 at the foot. Say together: "Higher is colder." Part 2, error K: a hot, dry desert card and a cold, dry desert card. Say: "A desert is dry. Hot or cold?" The part more children needed is for the whole class; the other goes to a small group in supported work.

#### 13 min. Regions

Review: in grade 1 you learned region, a big area made of many towns. A region also shares something. Three desert cards, Red Sand Flats, Stone Basin and Wind Mesa, share little rain. Three rainforest cards, Fern Coast, Rain Hollow and Palm Bend, share much rain. Draw a pencil boundary round each three; a boundary can move.

Windward Cove and Lee Cove (4 minutes) lie beside the sea; one is wet and one is dry. Pip says: "Every coast is rainy." Beside it: "Both are beside the sea. One is wet and one is dry."

How life fits: a dry region's farmers bring water; a wet region's builders add roofs and covered paths. Pairs say "These places share ___, so ___" (3 minutes, [[s02-P23-1]]).

Sandpiper Town, invented: a baker, a teacher and a ferry pilot. Pip says: "Everyone near the sea is a fisher." Beside it: "One place, three kinds of work." **Ask.** Does the place choose a person's job? Expect: no.

#### 12 min. Supported work

About 9 minutes: the six come to the globe one at a time, 85 seconds each, for (s02-X23b), one of (s02-X23c) or (s02-X23d), dealt, and two globe locations ([[s02-P23-2]]). Everyone else revises the map from the cue, fills the cue sheet, and moves a pencil boundary on the region frame if a card was added. This block has about 3 minutes of slack.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add "A region is an area that shares something."

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Read aloud: "Circle the word for an area that shares something like this" (s02-X23) on Dune Flat and Salt Plain. Mark 2 minutes. The six were heard in supported work. Everyone there is a farmer is error F. Country or continent circled has no letter: record not yet seen.

### Other rooms

The materials line names each stand-in.

### At home

**Swap.** No globe: use a ball or a world map.

**Skip.** The cue sheet and the region frame.

- **10 min. Find it.** Point to a place your adult names.
  **Say.** "Where does it lie?" Expect: a compass word.
- **15 min. These places share.** [[s02-P23-1]] Say a place that shares little rain, and what farmers might do.
  **Say.** "What do these places share, so what?" Expect: little rain, so farmers bring water.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X23]] Your child marks the card alone.
  **Say.** [[s02-X23b]] [[s02-X23c]] [[s02-X23d]]

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the desert cards only. Longer: a child says what would change if the sea were on the other side of the ridge.

### Words and supports

Words: region, share, boundary, coast, dry, wet. Frame: "These places share ___, so ___." Pointing counts.
