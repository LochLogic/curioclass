# Repair cards, days 22 and 23

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

## Day 22 warm-up: Pip's labels on the six cards again (error D)

The six cards of day 21 with Pip's new labels, cards 2 and 6 wrong; children fix with a reason.

## Day 23 part 1: two thermometers at two heights (error O)

**Picture:** Slate Hill, an invented hill with a thermometer at the top and a thermometer at the foot, and the words top and foot. The top reads 3 and the foot reads 11.
**Say:** "Higher is ___." (colder)

## Day 23 part 2: a hot desert and a cold desert (error K)

**Picture:** A card of sand with a hot sun and the words dry and hot. A card of snow with frost and the words dry and cold.
**Say:** "A desert is dry. Hot or cold?"

## Day 24 warm-up: three people, one town (error F)

Sandpiper Town's baker, teacher and ferry pilot from the region cards.
