# Exit cards, days 21 to 23

Each card is marked by every child, then the spoken asks are heard from the day's six. Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Credit line: Drawing by Curio Class.

## Day 21 exit card: three sentences

**Picture:** A strip with three sentences each beside a blank box. Sentence one: Friday the air was clear and warm. Sentence two: Summers here are usually dry, year after year. Sentence three: This morning fog sat over the river.

**Mark:** Write W beside each weather sentence and C beside each climate sentence. **Spoken:** "How do you know the second sentence is climate?"

## Day 22 exit card: Birch Ridge

**Picture:** A mountain with two towns. High Birch near the top and Low Birch in the valley, each with its name printed and a worker in it. No weather words, no thermometers, no numbers.

**Mark:** Circle the town where a worker needs the warmer coat. **Spoken:** "Why does the worker in that town need the warmer coat?" "Can a desert be cold?" "Is every place beside the sea rainy?"

## Day 23 exit cards: Dune Flat and Salt Plain

**Picture:** Two cards. Dune Flat, a sand flat with scattered low shrubs and a nearly empty rain gauge. Salt Plain, a white flat with scattered grasses and a nearly empty rain gauge. Three words in a row under both cards: region, country, continent.

**Mark:** Circle the word for an area that shares something like this. **Spoken:** "Does the card tell us every person's job there?" "What do the two cards share?"
