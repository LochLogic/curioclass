# Day 37: From a resource to a job

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the third six. From your county's profile (Source 37B), or the state's, copy one line naming something that grows or is made there onto the Local Resource Card, with the profile's date. Cut the nine practice cards. Keep the Fresh Cards face down until the exit. Key: key-exits-37-41.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 36's written task, the revised Recovery Card 2 explanation, with its feedback line.

If two or more of the six on day 36's exit said "We already voted" (error E), sort the three Action Record repair pictures into we chose, we did and people answered.

Otherwise draw a cove with a boat and a lake town with a dock, and say the frame: "These places share ___, so ___" ([[s03-P37-1]]). Expect: water, so boats and docks.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

**Ask.** "A person catches fish for work. What is the job called?" ([[s03-P37-2]]) Expect: a fisher. **Ask.** "Which part comes from nature, a fish or a net?" ([[s03-P37-3]]) Expect: the fish.

#### 10 min. From resource to job

Review: job. You learned job in kindergarten as your part of our class work; here it is one person's part of a place's work. New: **natural resource** (something from nature that people use) and **industry** (all the work that gets or makes one kind of thing). Every place here is made up.

Show the Gull Cove picture and strip. Think aloud: fish are the resource, catching them is the fishing industry, one person's part is the job, a fisher. **Ask.** "On the Gull Cove strip, point to the box that names the industry." ([[s03-K37-1]]) Expect: the middle box.

Children take the strip card. Read the other three aloud, one mark each: box the industry on Wide Plain ([[s03-P37-4]]); underline the job on Iron Hill ([[s03-P37-5]]); circle the resource on Tall Pine ([[s03-P37-6]]).

Pip says: "Everyone at Gull Cove is a fisher." Show the cove: a fisher, a nurse, a bus driver. **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F37-1]], error F) Expect: no. Say beside it: people there do many jobs.

#### 5 min. Our region

Read the Local Resource Card's line aloud and set it beside Gull Cove's strip: the same idea, in our region. **Ask.** "What does the line name that comes from nature?" ([[s03-P37-7]]) Fill in resource, industry and job with the class. No one tells what their family does.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Pairs set the nine cards (blueberry, lake salt, sand) into three strips on the strip mat ([[s03-P37-8]]). Call the third six one at a time to the table, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving, for [[s03-X37b]], [[s03-X37c]] and [[s03-X37d]] on the Fresh Cards.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the Fresh Cards. Each child draws a line for [[s03-X37]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the strips on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No cut cards: write each word on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** The Local Resource Card and the table.

- **10 min. Three words.** Read the Gull Cove strip together: fish, fishing, fisher.
  **Say.** "Which box is the industry?" Expect: the middle box, fishing.
- **10 min. Mark the strips.** [[s03-P37-4]] [[s03-P37-5]] [[s03-P37-6]] Your child marks three strips.
- **10 min. Nine cards.** [[s03-P37-8]] Your child sets the cards in three strips.
  **Say.** "Is every person there a berry picker?" Expect: no, people do many jobs.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X37]] [[s03-X37b]] [[s03-X37c]] [[s03-X37d]] Your child draws a line, points to one worker and answers two questions aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two chains. Longer: a pair draws a place and its strip.

### Words and supports

Words: natural resource, industry, job (review). Frame: "The ___ is the resource. The ___ is the industry. The ___ is the job."

### Tomorrow

If many drew a line to a worker that does not use the resource (error O), day 38 opens with the Mill Card; error F joins day 38's small group. Day 38 sorts resources into renewable and not.
