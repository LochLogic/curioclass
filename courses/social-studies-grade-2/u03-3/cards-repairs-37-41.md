# Repair cards, days 38 to 41

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Each repair is at most 5 minutes.

## Mill Card (error O, day 38)

**Picture:** A mill by a river, logs floating down to it, a sawyer at a saw.
**Say:** "Trees and a river to float the logs support a mill."

## Fisher and baker (error F, day 38 small group)

**Picture:** Two workers in one town: a fisher with a net and a baker with a loaf.
**Say:** "One place, two jobs. People there do many jobs."

## Object and work cards (error H, day 41)

Two sorted piles. Things: a hat, a loaf, a toy. Work done for you: a haircut, a lesson, a bus ride.

## Maker and buyer cards (error I, day 41 small group)

Maker pays: clay, tools, workers' time. Buyer pays: the price at the shop. Cards are sorted into two columns.

## Three-box strip (error J)

See the blank three-box strip in section 3.4.

## Key (teacher)

Mill: trees, river, sawyer. Fisher and baker: both jobs in one place. Objects: hat, loaf, toy. Work: haircut, lesson, bus ride. Maker: clay, tools, workers' time. Buyer: the price.
