# One food, farm to table, day 39

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. A map has a key, a compass rose and arrows between the places; near and far are read from the arrows, never from what a child's family buys.

## Bread chain

**Picture:** A map. Wheat fields on a plain at the left. A mill far across the map, a bakery and a shop near each other in a town, a family at the end. Arrows from the wheat to the mill, the mill to the bakery, the bakery to the shop, the shop to the family.
Questions: Who grows? Who moves? Who sells? Who buys? Two words are named with the pictures: producer (grows or makes it) and consumer (buys and uses it).

## Milk to cheese chain (pairs build)

Cards: a dairy farm, a cheese maker far away, a shop, a family. Pairs set them in order and answer the same four questions.

## Return and repair (error G)

**Picture:** Two woodlot cards. Woodlot A: stumps and a row of young trees planted beside each. Woodlot B: stumps and no young trees, a few trees left. Compare use and regrowth: planted as fast as cut, trees renew; cut faster, they run low.

## Small group (errors L and N)

**Picture:** An old can traced back to the metal that was already there. A fuel line traced from a tank back through a pipe to a supply made long ago.

## Key (teacher)

Bread: grown on the plain, moved to the mill far away and on to the town, sold in the shop, bought by the family. Milk: dairy farm, far cheese maker, shop, family. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
