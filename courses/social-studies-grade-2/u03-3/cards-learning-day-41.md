# People are a resource too, day 41

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white.

## A tractor and a sewing machine

**Picture:** A farmer on a tractor in a field. A thought bubble: knows how to drive and mend it.
**Picture:** A person at a sewing machine. A thought bubble: knows how to sew.
Two boxes: equipment (the tractor, the sewing machine); learned skill (what the person knows). "A human resource is what people know and can do." Pip's wrong idea J.

## Retrieval

"A tailor is paid for making a coat. What is the job? What is the income?" If missed, model with a different worker: a farmer paid for selling vegetables.

## The gardener chain, practice only

**Picture 1:** Ana, a child gardener, looks at crowded seedlings. A thought bubble: seedlings need room.
**Picture 2:** Ana makes a planting board with holes set a hand apart.
**Picture 3:** A long bench of many healthy seedlings.
Boxes: learned, invented, more made. Pairs fill one word or drawing each.

## Key (teacher)

Tractor and sewing machine: equipment; the person's knowing is the learned skill. Gardener: learned, seedlings need room; invented, a planting board; more made, more seedlings. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
