# Resource, industry and job strips, day 37

Every card prints "invented for practice". Plain black line drawing on white. Each strip has three boxes: resource, industry, job.

## Four places

**Gull Cove.** *Picture:* A small cove with boats and nets. Resource: fish. Industry: fishing. Job: fisher.
**Wide Plain.** *Picture:* A flat field of wheat under a wide sky. Resource: wheat. Industry: farming. Job: wheat farmer.
**Iron Hill.** *Picture:* A hill with a mine door and a cart of dark rock. Resource: iron. Industry: mining. Job: miner.
**Tall Pine.** *Picture:* Tall pine trees and a saw. Resource: trees. Industry: lumber. Job: logger.
Words: natural resource, industry, job (review). Pip's wrong idea F.

## Nine practice cards (three chains), pairs

**Chain 1:** blueberry bushes; berry farming; berry picker.
**Chain 2:** lake salt; salt works; salt worker.
**Chain 3:** sand; glass making; glassblower.
Pairs put the nine cards into three strips: resource, industry, job.

## Region frame

"These places share ___, so ___" for Gull Cove and a lake town. Places in one region often share land and water, so they often share resources, but not everyone does the same job.

## Key (teacher)

Strips as above. Chains as above. Pip says everyone on the coast fishes; the right idea is said beside it: a resource supports some jobs; people there do many jobs. Marking scale: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.
