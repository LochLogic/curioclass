# Day 38: Renewable or not

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fourth six on the record sheet. Cut the six resource cards. Draw two bins on the board: renewable and non-renewable. Keep the Garden and Wire Card face down until the exit. Key: key-exits-37-41.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 37's exit tickets, the Fresh Cards, and its written task, the strip marks. Show the Mill Card, a mill by a river with logs floating down to a sawyer. **Ask.** "On the Mill Card, point to what carries the logs to the mill." ([[s03-P38-1]]) Expect: the river. Say: trees and a river to float the logs support a mill (error O).

#### 3 min. Opening

**Ask.** "What can happen to a tree after it is cut?" ([[s03-P38-2]]) Expect: a new one can be planted.

#### 10 min. Renewable or not

Review: two bins. You sorted weather cards into two bins on day 21. Today's bins are **renewable** (it can be made again in a time people can wait, if used with care) and **non-renewable** (it cannot be made again in our lives).

**Ask.** "Tall Pine loggers plant a young tree for each tree they cut. Which bin do the trees go in?" ([[s03-K38-1]]) Expect: renewable. Wind and river water are renewable too (Source 38A). **Ask.** "Coal takes far longer to form than a person lives. Which bin does coal go in?" ([[s03-K38-2]]) Expect: non-renewable. Say that oil is non-renewable (Source 38A, Source 38B).

Pip says: "A renewable resource can never run out." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F38-1]], error G) Expect: no. Say beside it: cut faster than planted, trees run low.

#### 5 min. Reuse and refilling

Show the old pan melted into a mold for a new pan. Pip says: "Melting an old pan makes new ore." **Ask.** "Is Pip right?" ([[s03-F38-2]], error L) Expect: no, the same metal. Show the tank filled at a station. Pip says: "Filling a tank makes new oil." **Ask.** "Is Pip right?" ([[s03-F38-3]], error N) Expect: no, the oil was already made.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Pairs sort the six resource cards (wind, river, young tree, oil well, coal, iron ore) into the two bins ([[s03-P38-3]]), then each tells a partner why one card is in the non-renewable bin ([[s03-P38-4]]). For iron ore say only that it is dug from the ground and not planted; give no time. Meanwhile call the fourth six one at a time, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving, for [[s03-X38b]] and [[s03-X38c]]. Then the small group takes error F from day 37's six, 5 minutes, with the Fisher and Baker card ([[s03-P38-5]]). Expect: two jobs, one town.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the Garden and Wire Cards. Each child circles for [[s03-X38]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two bins on the board and sort word cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No cut cards: write each name on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** The small group and the Mill Card.

- **10 min. Two bins.** Draw two bins. Say what renewable and non-renewable mean.
  **Say.** "Trees are planted as fast as they are cut. Which bin?" Expect: renewable.
- **15 min. Sort six.** [[s03-P38-3]] Your child sorts the six cards.
  **Say.** "Why is coal in the non-renewable bin?" Expect: it takes far longer to form than a person lives.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X38]] [[s03-X38b]] [[s03-X38c]] Your child circles one and answers two questions aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four cards. Longer: name a renewable thing near home.

### Words and supports

Words: renewable, non-renewable, resource (review). Frame: "It is non-renewable because ___."
