# Day 40: Goods, services and what it costs to make

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the first six on the record sheet again. Cut the eight sorting cards. Draw two hoops on the board: a thing you can hold, and work done for you. Keep Towns A and B face down until the exit. Key: key-exits-37-41.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 39's exit tickets, the apple-juice chains, and its written task, the milk chain marks. Show the wool hat card: sheep on a far farm, a hat maker far away, a shop near, and arrows between them. **Ask.** "Point to where the hat is made." ([[s03-P40-1]], error M) Expect: the far hat maker. Say: some goods come from far away.

#### 10 min. Goods and services

Review: goods and services. You learned them in kindergarten: a good is a thing you can hold, a service is work done for you. Every place here is made up.

Here, a bakery's bread and a haircut. Far away, a factory's shoes and a help line answered from another state. **Ask.** "Which hoop does a loaf of bread go in?" ([[s03-K40-1]]) Expect: a thing you can hold. **Ask.** "Which hoop does a haircut go in?" ([[s03-K40-2]]) Expect: work done for you.

Pip says: "A haircut is a thing." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F40-1]], error H) Expect: no, a service. Pairs then sort the eight cards into the two hoops ([[s03-P40-2]]): bread, shoes, a kite, jam, a haircut, a guitar lesson, a bus ride, a help line.

#### 8 min. What it costs to make

Show the baker's kitchen: flour, an oven, two helpers. Say: a **cost** is what a maker pays for to make it. The baker pays for flour (materials), an oven (equipment) and helpers' time (workers). **Ask.** "Point to the equipment." ([[s03-K40-3]]) Expect: the oven. **Ask.** "Point to the materials." ([[s03-K40-4]]) Expect: the flour.

Pip says: "The price is the only thing it costs to make." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F40-2]], error I) Expect: no. A cost is what the maker pays, never what a family pays.

#### 11 min. Supported work

Pairs fill the shoe factory cost chart (the card shows leather hides and thread), one word or drawing in each box ([[s03-P40-3]]). Call the first six one at a time to the table, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving, for [[s03-X40b]] and [[s03-X40c]]. Then one small group, 5 minutes, takes error C from day 39's six with the farm card: two arrows come in, one from near and one from far ([[s03-P40-4]]).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out Towns A and B. Each child marks [[s03-X40]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two hoops on the board and sort word cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No hoops: draw two circles on paper.

**Skip.** The farm card and the wool hat card.

- **10 min. Two hoops.** Sort bread, a haircut, a kite and a bus ride into two circles.
  **Say.** "Which is work done for you?" Expect: the haircut and the bus ride.
- **15 min. What it costs.** [[s03-P40-3]] Your child fills the cost chart.
  **Say.** "What does a baker pay for?" Expect: flour, an oven and helpers' time.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X40]] [[s03-X40b]] [[s03-X40c]] Your child marks one thing, points to one service and answers one question aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four sorting cards. Longer: name a good and a service near home.

### Words and supports

Words: good, service, cost, materials, equipment, workers. Frame: "A maker pays for ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many boxed a service or circled a good (error H), day 41 opens with the object cards and work cards sorted. A child who said the price is the only cost (error I) joins day 41's small group. Day 41 teaches that people are a resource too.
