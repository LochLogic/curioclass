# Section 3.3 opener: resources, work and learning

## What the section does

Five days, 37 to 41, of 30 to 40 minutes. The questions: how do resources and learning support work? Day 37 links a natural resource to an industry and a job on four made-up places and then to the class's own county or state. Day 38 sorts resources into renewable and non-renewable, and shows that melting an old metal object makes no new ore and that filling a tank moves fuel and does not make it. Day 39 follows one food, bread, from farm to table with a map and arrows, near and far. Day 40 sorts goods from services and names what a maker pays for. Day 41 teaches that people are a resource too: what they learn leads to tools and to more being made.

## How the days run

Each day opens with a repair or a retrieval, then a short teaching part, then the children sort, line, arrow or fill, the day's six are heard at the teacher's table, and one picture is marked at the end. Cards are read aloud; children read labels of one to three words.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A natural resource** is something from nature that people use. An **industry** is all the work that makes or gets one kind of thing; a **job** is one person's part of it.
- **A place offers possibilities; not everyone there does one job.**
- **Renewable** means it can be made again in a time people can wait, if it is used with care. Trees renew when planted as fast as they are cut; cut faster, they run low. **Non-renewable** things such as oil and coal form far too slowly. Renewable does not mean never runs out.
- **Reuse is not new ore.** Melting an old pan makes a new pan from the same metal. Filling a tank moves fuel that was already made.
- **A good** is a thing you can hold; a **service** is work done for you. A **cost** is what a maker pays for, such as materials, equipment and workers' time, and never a family's money.
- **A human resource** is what people know and can do. A tool is equipment; knowing how is a learned skill. Never a person's worth.

## The errors, in plain words

Pip says each wrong idea once: everyone on the coast fishes (F) and where a place is has nothing to do with the work (O), day 37; renewable can never run out (G), recycling makes new ore (L) and filling a tank makes new oil (N), day 38; everything we buy is made here (M), day 39; a haircut is a thing (H) and the price is the only cost (I), day 40; the sewing machine knows how to sew (J), day 41.

## Supplies for the section

Strip cards and the Local Resource Card (the class's own county profile, found before day 37), two bins, six resource cards, the bread and milk chains and the apple-juice chain, two hoops, goods and services cards, the cost chart, the gardener strip, the repair cards, the exit cards, the record sheet and the question board. No real company, price or brand appears. Every invented card prints "invented for practice".
