# Day 41: People are a resource too

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the second six on the record sheet. Lay out the object, work, maker and buyer cards. Give the tractor and sewing machine cards two boxes: equipment and learned skill. Keep the Beekeeper Card face down until the exit. Key: key-exits-37-41.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 40's exit tickets, Towns A and B, and its written task, the cost charts. If more than a third made error H on day 40's exit, sort the six cards: hat, loaf, toy and haircut, lesson, bus ride ([[s03-P41-1]]). If error I was larger, sort the four maker and buyer cards instead ([[s03-P41-2]]) and give the object cards to the small group.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: income. You learned income in grade 1: money you receive for work. **Ask.** "A person is paid for making a coat. What is the job called?" ([[s03-P41-3]]) Expect: a tailor. **Ask.** "What is the money a tailor receives for the work called?" ([[s03-P41-4]]) Expect: income. A miss is modeled with another worker: a farmer paid for selling vegetables.

#### 10 min. People are a resource

Say: a **human resource** is what people know and can do. It is never about how much a person matters. Show the tractor card: a farmer who learned to drive and mend it. Set the tractor in the equipment box and what the farmer knows in the learned skill box. **Ask.** "Which box does the tractor go in?" ([[s03-K41-1]]) Expect: equipment. **Ask.** "Which box does knowing how to drive and mend it go in?" ([[s03-K41-2]]) Expect: learned skill. Do the same with the sewing machine card.

Pip says: "The sewing machine knows how to sew." **Ask.** "Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F41-1]], error J) Expect: no, the person learned how; the machine helps.

#### 6 min. Learning leads to making more

Show Ana, an invented child gardener, in three pictures: seedlings crowded, a planting board with holes a hand apart, a long row of healthy seedlings. This is practice only. Pairs fill the gardener strip, one word or drawing in each box: what Ana learned, what Ana invented, what more was made ([[s03-P41-5]]).

#### 10 min. Supported work

Call the second six one at a time to the table, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving, for [[s03-X41b]]. Then one small group, 5 minutes: error H or I if it is left over, otherwise a retrieval gap on job and income with a new worker. The rest finish the strip and tell a partner what Ana learned.

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the Beekeeper Card. Each child circles for [[s03-X41]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two boxes on the board and use word cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No cut cards: draw the two boxes on paper.

**Skip.** The retrieval and the small group.

- **10 min. Two boxes.** Draw a tractor and what a farmer knows. Put each in a box: equipment or learned skill.
  **Say.** "Which one is the learned skill?" Expect: knowing how to drive and mend it.
- **15 min. Ana the gardener.** [[s03-P41-5]] Your child fills the strip.
  **Say.** "What did Ana make after she learned?" Expect: a planting board, then more seedlings.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X41]] [[s03-X41b]] Your child circles one thing and answers one question aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the tractor card. Longer: draw a tool someone learned to use.

### Words and supports

Words: human resource, equipment, learned skill, invented. Frame: "Ana learned ___, so she made ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

A child who said a tool holds the knowing (error J) joins day 42's small group on a fresh three-box strip. If more than a third made error J, the whole class takes it on day 43. Day 42 looks back on the readiness action.
