# Day 39: One food, farm to table

## Teacher page

### Before class

Today's six: the fifth six on the record sheet. Hang the bread map with its key. Cut the four milk cards. Put the two woodlot cards, the old can card and the fuel line card ready. Keep the apple-juice chain face down until the exit. Key: key-exits-37-41.md.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 38's exit tickets, the Garden and Wire Cards, and its written task, the sorted bins. Lay the two woodlot cards: A has a young tree planted beside each stump, B has stumps and no young trees. **Ask.** "Point to the woodlot where the trees will run low." ([[s03-P39-1]], error G) Expect: B. Say: planted as fast as cut, trees renew; cut faster, they run low.

#### 3 min. Two words

Name two words with the bread map: **producer** (grows or makes it) and **consumer** (buys and uses it). Point to the wheat fields and to the family. Children only listen and point; the words are not asked today.

#### 10 min. One food, farm to table

Review: a map key. You read a key on day 2. Say aloud that this chain is made up. The bread map shows wheat on a plain, a mill far across the map, a bakery and a shop near each other in a town, and a family. Arrows join each stop to the next. Read near and far from the arrows, never from what any family buys.

**Ask.** "Point to who grows the wheat." ([[s03-K39-1]]) Expect: the wheat fields. **Ask.** "Point to who buys the bread at the end." ([[s03-K39-2]]) Expect: the family. Children take the bread map; each writes near or far beside the mill ([[s03-K39-3]]) and beside the bakery ([[s03-K39-4]]). Expect: far, near.

Pip says: "Everything we buy is made right here." **Ask.** "Look at the bread map. Is Pip right? Say yes or no." ([[s03-F39-1]], error M) Expect: no, the mill is far.

#### 6 min. A second chain

Pairs set the four milk cards in order ([[s03-P39-3]]): dairy farm, cheese maker (far away), shop, family. Each child tells a partner who sells the cheese ([[s03-P39-4]]). Pairs write near or far beside the cheese maker ([[s03-P39-5]]).

#### 10 min. Supported work

Call the fifth six one at a time to the table, 45 seconds each, about 5 minutes with moving, for [[s03-X39b]]. Then one small group, 5 minutes, takes errors L and N from day 38's six: the old can card, where the new can's metal came from ([[s03-P39-7]]), and the fuel line card, where the fuel was made long ago ([[s03-P39-8]]). The rest tick each arrow of their chain that points the way the food goes ([[s03-P39-6]]).

#### 2 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the apple-juice chain. Each child marks [[s03-X39]]. Mark later: shown, shown with help, not yet seen.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the bread map on the board and use word cards for the milk chain.

### At home

**Swap.** No cut cards: write each stop on a scrap of paper.

**Skip.** The woodlot and small-group cards.

- **10 min. Read the map.** Look at the bread map together and follow the arrows.
  **Say.** "Which stop is far away?" Expect: the mill.
- **15 min. Milk to cheese.** [[s03-P39-3]] [[s03-P39-5]] Your child sets four cards in order and labels one stop.
  **Say.** "Who sells the cheese?" Expect: the shop.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X39]] [[s03-X39b]] Your child marks two stages and tells the chain aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: only the bread map. Longer: a child draws a short chain for a food of their choice.

### Words and supports

Words: producer, consumer, near, far, arrow, key. Frame: "First ___, then ___, then ___." Pointing counts.

### Tomorrow

If many circled the orchard (error M), day 40 opens with the wool hat card. A child who said it is all made in one place (error C) joins day 40's small group. Day 40 sorts goods from services.
