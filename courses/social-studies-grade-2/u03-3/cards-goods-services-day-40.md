# Goods, services and costs, day 40

Every card prints "invented for practice". No brand names. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Eight sorting cards (two hoops: a thing you can hold; work done for you)

**Good:** a loaf of bread; a pair of shoes; a toy kite; a jar of jam.
**Service:** a haircut; a guitar lesson; a bus ride; a help line answered from another state.
Shoes and the help line come from far away.

## What it costs to make

**Picture:** A baker's kitchen. A sack of flour. An oven. Two helpers kneading.
**Say:** "A baker pays for flour (materials), an oven (equipment) and helpers' time (workers). A cost is what a maker pays for to make it." Pip's wrong idea I.

## Shoe factory card and cost chart (pairs)

**Picture:** A factory with a stack of leather hides, a bag of thread, a cutting table, a stitching machine and workers at benches.
**Chart (blank):** Materials: ______ Equipment: ______ Workers: ______. Pairs fill one word or drawing in each.

## Repair cards (day 40 Return and repair, error M; small group, error C)

**Picture:** A wool hat card: sheep on a far farm, a hat maker far away, a shop near, arrows from the sheep to the maker to the shop. Follow the arrow. **Picture:** A farm card with two incoming arrows, tools and feed, from near and from far.

## Key (teacher)

Goods: bread, shoes, kite, jam. Services: haircut, lesson, bus ride, help line. Costs: flour, an oven, helpers. Chart: leather hides and thread; cutting table and stitching machine; workers at benches.
