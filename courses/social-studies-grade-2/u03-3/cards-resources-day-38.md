# Renewable and not, day 38

Every card prints "invented for practice" except where it names a real fuel. Plain black line drawing on white.

## Two bins

**Renewable:** can be made again in a time people can wait, if used with care. **Non-renewable:** made so slowly it cannot be made again in our lives.

## Six sorting cards

**Picture 1:** A windmill turning on a hill. Renewable.
**Picture 2:** A river running down a valley. Renewable.
**Picture 3:** A young tree planted in a row of stumps and young trees. Renewable.
**Picture 4:** An oil well pumping. Non-renewable.
**Picture 5:** A pile of coal beside a rail car. Non-renewable.
**Picture 6:** A mine with a cart of iron ore. Non-renewable.

## Reuse and refilling

**Picture:** An old pan melted in a hot dish and poured into a mold for a new pan. Same metal, no new ore.
**Picture:** A tank filled at a station from a long hose. Oil moved, not made.

## Say

Renewable does not mean never runs out. Trees renew when planted as fast as they are cut. Pip's wrong ideas G, L and N.

## Key (teacher)

Sort: 1, 2, 3 renewable; 4, 5, 6 non-renewable. The pan: the same metal. The tank: oil moved, not made.
