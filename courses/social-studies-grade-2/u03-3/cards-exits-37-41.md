# Exit cards, days 37 to 41

Every card prints "invented for practice" in small letters. Plain black line drawing on white. Reserved: print only on the exit of their day.

## Day 37 exit card: Fresh Cards

**Picture:** Three resources in a left column: a bunch of river reeds, a sheep with wool, a block of quarry stone. Four workers in a right column: a basket maker, a knitter, a stonecutter, a baker.
**Mark:** Draw a line from the river reeds to the worker whose job uses them.
**Spoken:** "Point to the worker whose job uses the sheep wool." "Which worker's job uses the quarry stone?" "A place has lots of river reeds. What jobs might people there do?"

## Day 38 exit card: Garden and Wire Card

**Picture:** A vegetable bed with seedlings, planted again each spring. A coil of wire made from copper dug in a mine.
**Mark:** Circle the one that is renewable.
**Spoken:** "An old bench is melted and made into a new bench. Did the new bench need new metal dug out of the ground? Say how you know." "A boat owner pumps fuel from a big drum into a boat's tank at a dock. Did the dock make new fuel? Say how you know."

## Day 39 exit card: apple-juice chain

**Picture:** A map. An orchard and a shop in the same town. A pressing plant far across the map. Arrows from the orchard to the plant and from the plant to the shop. A family at the end.
**Mark:** Circle the stage that happens far away.
**Spoken:** "Say how the juice gets from the orchard to the shop, from the arrows."

## Day 40 exit card: Towns A and B

**Picture:** Two small town panels. Town A: a stack of bricks and a person mending a wall. Town B: a roll of cloth and a person translating a letter.
**Mark:** Draw a box round the good Town A makes.
**Spoken:** "Point to the service Town B offers." "What must brickmakers pay for to make bricks?"

## Day 41 exit card: Beekeeper Card

**Picture:** A beekeeper at a hive. A thought bubble: knows that bees stay calm in smoke. A smoker with a puffing nozzle. A stack of honey jars.
**Mark:** Circle what the beekeeper learned.
**Spoken:** "What did the smoker help the beekeeper collect?"
