# Day 14: Check, a new swing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the weather. Hang the basket from a support you have secured, spare ready, four washers counted in. Write the waiting task on the board: your own notebook answer to "What makes a swing start, stop and keep coming back?"

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on at the station; low arcs below faces; strings off necks, washers out of mouths. Only the teacher secures and releases the basket. An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun; the thermometer is non-glass. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 6 min. Launch and transitions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Teach the stop signal. Day 14's assessment, the revised day 12 record (s01-E1 and s01-E2), is scored after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. Day 13's exit tickets go back on day 16. Name the order of the six groups.

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look; write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s01-P14-3]]. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 24 min. Station turns

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each, at the one basket; the teacher releases it. A group fits eye protection, watches three runs of ten turns, then wipes eye protection, 30 seconds inside the 4 minutes. Each child, on their own sheet, writes [[s01-P14-1]], starting with the side really seen. A turn too quick to read stays blank, never guessed. A partner may tick while the child calls the turns and watches. Waiting groups write their own notebook answer to the board question, unscored. No help, no feedback.

#### 6 min. Independent prediction

Each child alone marks the repeating order and writes [[s01-P14-2]]. Unscored practice. Collect the sheets.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count washers in, wipe eye protection, collect strips and notebooks. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No basket: a small light box hung from two strings of equal length, released by the teacher from one side. No invented record today: only the child's own observations count.

### At home

**Swap.** No washers: a key on a string held by you at arm's length, low arc. No thermometer: look out the window.

**Skip.** The groups, the waiting task and the revised record.

- **20 min. Assessment, the record.** You hold the key at one side and let go gently, three runs. The child alone does [[s01-P14-1]]. Give no help.
- **8 min. Assessment, predict.** The child alone does [[s01-P14-2]].
- **5 min. The weather.** Do [[s01-P14-3]].
- **8 min. Your revised record.** Read [[s01-E1]] and [[s01-E2]]; they are scored from the day 12 record. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who missed a turn writes what was seen and says so. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Turn, side, cycle, centre, repeat, evidence. Frame: "Next is ___ because ___." Read stems aloud; accept pointing, drawing or dictation; large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 15 is a second check day, on pushes: own work, no help, no feedback. Day 14's records (s01-P14-1, s01-P14-2 and s01-P14-3) go back on day 16.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
