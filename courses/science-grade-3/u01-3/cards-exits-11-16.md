# Exit cards, days 11 to 16

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 11

A can rolls back and forth in a curved skate dip. Record: left side 9 cm up, bottom, right side 8 cm up, bottom, left side 7 cm up.

## Day 12

A bucket hangs from a porch hook and is made shorter. Pat says it will stay on the left because it started there. The record: left, centre, right, centre.

## Day 13

One half sheet, two questions. 1: a wheeled cart at the bottom of a U-shaped track, with two equal arrows from opposite sides. 2: a second cart moved to a raised end, with its record of start, middle, far end, middle, start, middle, far end, middle. Asked aloud, no sheet: the cart passing the bottom to the right, with a new long arrow pointing right.

## Day 16

One card, two questions. 1: a box on a tray with two about equal pushes touching it from opposite sides. 2: a bead hanging on a string, with the record far left, middle, far right, middle, far left.
