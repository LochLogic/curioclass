# Day 15: Check, a new sled

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set one tray with a taped start line, the sled and a ruler; clear the floor around it. Write the plan frame on the board: "I change only ___. I keep the same ___. Our group tries each way ___ times." Set the order of the six groups.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges; nothing is thrown; floors stay clear. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch and transitions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Teach the stop signal. Today's own work is the assessment: s01-E3, s01-E4 and s01-E5 score the fresh sled plan, trials and explanation after class, outside lesson minutes. The revised day 7 record gets a repair mark beside the score. Day 14's assessment, s01-P14-1, s01-P14-2 and s01-P14-3, stays held: its checklist goes back on day 16, the scored paper on day 17. Show the sled and tray; name the order of the groups.

#### 30 min. Station turns and plans

Six groups of five, 5 minutes each, at the one station. Waiting children plan alone on their own sheet, [[s01-P15-1]]: about equal pushes from opposite sides against one stronger push, changing only the strength. At the station each child makes one trial of each way, a group-mate pushing from the other side, and writes initials, the pushes and where the sled ended in whole centimeters, [[s01-P15-2]]. A trial is about 25 seconds: ten trials, 250 of the group's 300 seconds, 50 to change places. A second station, if there is one, halves the waiting. An unexpected result is written as it happened, with a retest idea. Hand pushes are only about equal. No help, no feedback.

#### 6 min. Independent explanation

Each child alone draws the arrows for both ways and writes why one stayed and one moved, [[s01-P15-3]]. Scored after class. Collect the sheets.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Wipe and stack the tray and sled, collect the sheets. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No sled: a small light box on a flat tray or smooth table. No invented record today: only the child's own trials count.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a smooth table with a tape line. No box: any small light box.

**Skip.** The groups and the waiting plan.

- **10 min. Assessment, the plan.** The child alone does [[s01-P15-1]]. Give no help.
- **15 min. Assessment, the trials.** You push from one side with a finger and the child from the other, once about equal and once with you pushing harder. The child alone does [[s01-P15-2]].
- **8 min. Assessment, explain.** The child alone does [[s01-P15-3]].
- **8 min. What is scored.** Read [[s01-E3]], [[s01-E4]] and [[s01-E5]] together; they check today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who missed a trial says so. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Balanced, unbalanced, cancel, arrow, trial, fair test. Frame: "I change only ___." Read stems aloud; accept pointing, drawing or dictation; large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 16 is the respond day. In its first 5 minutes the day 14 and day 15 look-for checklists and day 13's exit tickets go back, and each child learns their group. The scored papers come back on day 17. Four groups then meet the teacher for 7 minutes each.
