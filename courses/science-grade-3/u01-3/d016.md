# Day 16: Respond to the checks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the children into four groups by their largest gap on the day 13 exit and the day 14 and 15 checklists: Forces (A, B and C), Fair testing (D and H), Alternating positions (error E), Evidence limits (F and G). List the groups on the board; set four spots. Fill one look-for checklist a child for day 15's assessment and day 14's: a tick a look-for and one question stamp, about 1 minute a child. Scores go on the record sheet by day 17.

### Safety and set-up

Gentle pushes inside the tray, away from table edges; nothing is thrown; floors stay clear. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back the look-for checklists for the day 14 and day 15 work (s01-P14-1, s01-P14-2, s01-P14-3, s01-P15-1, s01-P15-2 and s01-P15-3) and day 13's exit tickets (s01-X13d). The scored papers come back on day 17; no score goes back today. Error A, 2 minutes: hold a box still between two hands; each child draws both arrows. Error C, 2 minutes: label strength and distance on an arrow card as two different things. Last minute: each child reads their checklist and finds their group on the board.

#### 28 min. Four groups with the teacher

Four groups of about seven, 7 minutes each with the teacher, called by a signal. The other three groups work in pairs from the partner prompts: one partner reads a prompt aloud, the other answers on the page, then swap. Every child does all four prompts; the teacher's group does its own at the spot. Children may pass; nobody is the example of a mistake.
- Forces (A, B and C), [[s01-P16-1]]: hold the box still between two hands, draw both arrows, say what a longer arrow tells and what it does not.
- Fair testing (D and H), [[s01-P16-2]]: cross out every change but one, then run the same condition again.
- Alternating positions (error E), [[s01-P16-3]]: lay the cards in order and slide a finger along them.
- Evidence limits (F and G), [[s01-P16-4]]: compare successive marks, then highlight two earlier marks that support a prediction.

**Ask.** What did you check first when your prediction failed? Expect: our readings; then what changed.

#### 8 min. Fresh individual transfer

Alone, [[s01-X16c]]; read each question aloud and wait. Ask [[s01-X16d]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. The card is invented for practice. The first question is unit 2 retrieval, two contact pushes drawn as arrows on a resting book: equal arrows, no change. Unscored; used to plan later support. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Wipe and stack trays and boxes, collect the papers. Prompt pages stay in the folder. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No box or tray: a small box on a table. No printer: copy the four prompts onto the board and write the answers in notebooks.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the turns on six slips of paper. No tray: a small box on a table.

**Skip.** The groups, the partner reading and the repair.

- **8 min. Pushes.** Hold a box still between your two hands. Do [[s01-P16-1]].
  **Say.** "What do the two arrows tell you? Does a longer arrow mean farther?" Expect: "They cancel, so it stays; a longer arrow means a stronger push, not farther."
- **8 min. A fair test.** Do [[s01-P16-2]].
- **8 min. Turns.** Lay the six slips right, center, left, center, right, center. Do [[s01-P16-3]].
- **8 min. How sure.** Do [[s01-P16-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X16c]], answered alone. Then ask [[s01-X16d]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs do two prompts, not three, in partner time. Never cut the teacher's 7 minutes or the transfer. Longer: a group that finishes writes a wrong answer for a friend to fix.

### Words and supports

Arrow, cancel, strength, distance, fair test, order, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." A partner may read the prompt; pointing, drawing and dictation count.

### Tomorrow

Day 17 starts unit 2: the class observes a suspended paper clip and static paper attraction, then models forces that act without touching. Today's transfer shows who needs a quick look at contact pushes first. The scored day 14 and 15 papers come back in day 17's retrieval slot.
