# Day 13: Explain the swing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the weather. Write a short note on each day 12 record: a tick or one question, never a score. Draw the swing on the board. Set each pair's flowerpot record. Keep the day 8 class explanation in view.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun; the thermometer is non-glass. Hand wipes close the weather part. Paper only today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look; write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s01-P13-1]]. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 12's pages and exit tickets (s01-P12-1, s01-P12-2, s01-P12-3, s01-P12-4, s01-P12-5 and s01-X12). Error E, 2 minutes: lay the turning-position cards in order and slide a finger along them. Error H, 2 minutes: lay two runs of cards side by side, left, centre, right, centre each; say what one run shows and what two show. The last 2 minutes: read your note.

#### 7 min. Revise the record

The written task from day 12 comes back. Each child revises their record in a second colour, [[s01-P13-2]], keeping the first marks. A turn never seen stays blank. Collect the records: they are scored after day 14, outside lesson minutes.

#### 11 min. Class explanation

On the board, with force arrows, build one explanation from the box tests and the swing records. Children copy it as you go.
- Pushes can change motion.
- Two about equal pushes from opposite sides cancel.
- At the bottom the string's pull up and gravity's pull down cancel, so the washer hangs still.
- Gravity pulls the washer down the curve; the string guides its path.
- It keeps moving past the bottom because it is already moving.
- The order of the turns repeats while the swings get smaller.

An arrow is for a force on one thing, never for its travel. Each child then writes [[s01-P13-3]].

#### 5 min. Unfamiliar record

Pairs read the flowerpot record, said aloud to be invented, and write [[s01-P13-4]].

**Ask.** What does the table not show? Expect: nobody has watched past the fourth turn.

#### 8 min. Exit, section exit ticket

Alone, [[s01-X13]], [[s01-X13b]] and [[s01-X13c]]; read each card aloud, 2 minutes a card, and wait. A record may be read to a child. Look for: card 1, no, the equal pushes cancel; card 2, start (a little lower is fine), because the order repeated; card 3, its speed or direction; no, nothing new has been seen. Error A: "no force is on it, it is still". Error B: "yes, two pushes start it". Error C: "the long arrow means it goes far". Error E: "the far end, it stays there". Error G: "yes, the old pattern still tells us". A blank is an incomplete part, not a letter. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Question board

Mark answered questions with their evidence; keep the open ones. Today's pages stay in the folder.

**Ask.** When should we change a prediction? Expect: when something has changed or we see something new, not because we like another answer. Every child may pass.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the flowerpot record and the swing on the board. No thermometer: write sky and wind from the window, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No thermometer: look out the window. No pendulum: a key on a string, held still.

**Skip.** The pairs, the repair and the question board.

- **5 min. The weather.** Do [[s01-P13-1]].
- **8 min. Revise.** Read the note on the day 12 record. Do [[s01-P13-2]].
- **10 min. Explain.** Hold a key on a string still. Do [[s01-P13-3]].
  **Say.** "What pulls on the key while it hangs still? Why does it not move?" Expect: "The string pulls up and gravity pulls down; they cancel."
- **5 min. The flowerpot.** Do [[s01-P13-4]].
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X13]], [[s01-X13b]] and [[s01-X13c]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three lines of the explanation and one flowerpot sentence. Never cut the revision, the exit or the arrows. Longer: write what a heavier washer would change and what it would not.

### Words and supports

String pull, gravity, cancel, evidence, prediction, repeat. Frame: "The order ___ because ___." A partner may read the record aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 14 is a check day: own work, no help and no feedback. Each child watches an unfamiliar paper basket on two strings at a station turn and writes its record alone, while the revised day 12 record is scored after class. Today's exit letters go back on day 16.
