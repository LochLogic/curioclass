# Day 11: A ball in a bowl

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a strip up each bowl's inside wall: zero at the bottom, a mark every centimeter, 6 marked. Set each bowl on a tray with a soft ball and a ruler. Draw the class table and lay out the turning-position cards. Mark day 10's pages with a tick or a note.

### Safety and set-up

Roll the ball, never throw it. Bowls stand steady in trays, away from table edges; floors stay clear. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 10's pages and exit tickets (s01-P10-1, s01-P10-2, s01-P10-3, s01-P10-4 and s01-X10). Error E, 2 minutes: lay the turning-position cards in order and slide a finger along them. Error G, 2 minutes: on the board, highlight two right turns of the rocking toy record that were each followed by center.

#### 3 min. Set-up

Bowls on trays, floors clear. Roles: a releaser lets go with no push, a reader says each side and height; roles rotate every release. The middle of the bowl is the bottom, like the swing's center.

#### 6 min. Predict

Set a ball at the 6 mark on the left of one bowl. Each child writes [[s01-P11-1]] alone before it is let go. Then let go; the class watches four turns and a reader calls each height. If it will not roll cleanly, use an invented record, said aloud to be invented: left 6, bottom 0, right 5, bottom 0.

**Ask.** On the right, higher, the same or lower? What told you? Expect: lower or about the same; our day 9 far marks got a little smaller. Every child may pass.

#### 14 min. Observe the bowl

Six groups of five, one bowl each. The releaser lets go from the 6 mark on the left. Everyone writes four turns in [[s01-P11-2]]: turn 1 is the start, left, 6; turn 2 is the bottom, 0. Each child releases twice, about 40 seconds a release: ten releases, 7 of the 14 minutes. On the second release the child checks every row and writes a different height beside the first, keeping both. A turn too unclear to read is the side only. If a ball will not roll cleanly, repeat once and keep both. Watch for four turns all at the left (error E).

#### 6 min. Compare with the pendulum

New word: bottom. Put the invented table on the board. [[steps:s01-K11-1]]

**Ask.** Does the ball do what the washer did? How do you know? Expect: yes; the order repeats and each far turn is a little lower.

Say why: gravity pulls the ball to the bottom; it is already moving there, so it keeps going up the other side. Children write [[s01-P11-3]].

#### 5 min. Pattern list

Add the bowl to the pattern list. What repeats: the order of the turns. What changes: how high they reach, a little lower each time.

**Ask.** When should we change a prediction? Expect: when we see something new. Every child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s01-X11]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s01-X11b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) the bottom; (b) no, a little lower, because each turn was a bit smaller. Error E: "the right side again, it stays there". Error F: "yes, it always goes the same". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Dry the bowls, wipe the balls, collect the pages.

### Other rooms

No bowl: a curved book spine. No tape: stand a ruler inside the wall.

### At home

**Swap.** No tape: stand a ruler inside the bowl. No bowl: roll a soft ball along a curved book spine. No printer: copy the table onto paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the pattern list.

- **5 min. Guess.** Hold a soft ball on the left side of a bowl at the 6 mark. Do [[s01-P11-1]] before letting go.
  **Say.** "On the right, higher, the same or lower? What did you see?" Expect: "Lower, because the swings we watched got a little smaller."
- **12 min. Roll it.** Let go with no push, twice. Do [[s01-P11-2]].
- **8 min. Compare.** Do [[s01-K11-1]] and [[s01-P11-3]].
  **Say.** "Does the ball do what a swing does?" Expect: "Yes, it turns in order and each turn is a bit lower."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X11]], answered alone. Then ask [[s01-X11b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one release each. Never cut the guess, the four turns or the exit. Longer: release from the 4 mark and compare the first far turn with the 6 mark's.

### Words and supports

Bottom, turn, side, height, repeat, predict, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." A partner may release the ball and call heights.

### Tomorrow

Day 12 opens with a 2-minute measuring warm-up, then Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. Error E ("the right side again, it stays there") lays the cards in order; error F ("yes, it always goes the same") compares the heights on the class table. Then the teacher shortens the pendulum string and the class compares what it predicts with what it sees.
