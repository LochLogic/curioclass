# Day 12: The shorter pendulum

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a strip under each pendulum path: centre 0, a mark every centimetre each side, 10 marked. Hang each pendulum from a checked support, string shortened to 40 cm by you, four washers counted out. Draw the class table. Mark day 11's pages.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on while a pendulum swings; low arcs below faces; strings off necks, washers out of mouths. Only the teacher secures supports and changes strings. Nobody sits on a playground swing for this. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Measuring warm-up

Draw two strips above a ruler on the board. Children copy them and write [[s01-P12-1]]. A miss gets zero put at the strip's end and a second strip.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 11's pages and exit tickets (s01-P11-1, s01-P11-2, s01-P11-3 and s01-X11). Error E, 2 minutes: lay the turning-position cards in order and slide a finger along them. Error F, 2 minutes: on the class table, compare each height with the one before and say what changed.

#### 6 min. Safety and predict

Fit eye protection; teach the stop signal. Say the string is now 40 cm. Each child writes [[s01-P12-2]] alone. This starts a record that is scored later, so it gets written feedback first.

#### 14 min. Observe the changed pendulum

Six groups of five, one pendulum each. A releaser holds the four washers at the 10 cm mark and lets go gently, no push; a reader counts ten turns aloud, then the washers are stopped. Three runs, turns counted, not timed; the releaser changes each run. A short string turns quickly, so a child may miss turns: three runs let each child fill what a run missed. Each child alone, on their own sheet, writes [[s01-P12-3]]. A turn too quick to read stays blank, never guessed. A partner may call the turns while the child watches and writes. A child who could not read a turn says so.

#### 6 min. Predictions against observations

Each group writes its predicted and seen order and far marks on the class table, [[s01-P12-4]].

**Ask.** If a prediction failed, what do we check first? Then what? Expect: the readings first; then what changed, the shorter string.

#### 6 min. Mark and predict

Each child marks the repeating order on their record and writes [[s01-P12-5]] alone. Look for the opposite side and two earlier cycles. Give feedback as you pass; the record is collected.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s01-X12]]; read both cards aloud and wait. Look for: (a) no, it still goes left, centre, right, centre; (b) run it again and compare. Error E: "yes, it started on the left". Error H: "nothing, it worked". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count washers in, wipe eye protection, collect strips and records. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No pendulum: a key on a string at arm's length, low arc, over a strip on a desk. No invented record today: only the child's own observations count.

### At home

**Swap.** No washers: a key on a 40 cm string, held by you at arm's length, low arc. No printer: copy the table onto paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the class table.

- **3 min. Ruler.** Do [[s01-P12-1]].
- **6 min. Guess.** Do [[s01-P12-2]] before the key swings.
  **Say.** "Will the order of the turns stay the same? Will the marks be about the same?" Expect: "The order stays left, centre, right, centre; the marks are the same or a little smaller."
- **15 min. Watch alone.** You hold the key at the 10 mark and let go gently; the child watches. Do [[s01-P12-3]]. Keep the key from faces and mouths.
- **8 min. Check and predict.** Do [[s01-P12-4]] and [[s01-P12-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X12]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two runs. Never cut the guess, the child's own record or the exit. Longer: predict, then test, whether four washers or two change the order of the turns.

### Words and supports

Shorter, changed, turn, cycle, prediction, readings. Frame: "I guessed ___, but I saw ___." A partner may call turns.

### Tomorrow

Day 13 opens with a weather record, then Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. Error E ("yes, it started on the left") lays the cards in order; error H ("nothing, it worked") runs the same condition again. Records go back with feedback; children revise them in a second colour and the class builds one explanation of the swing.
