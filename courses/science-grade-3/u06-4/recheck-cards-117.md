# Recheck cards (day 117, unscored, fresh)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. One card for each group. Alone, on paper, 5 minutes.

## Card A: a display

Invented for practice. Stone Hill, rainy days in 30-day samples. Spring 16, summer 6, autumn 12, winter 2. Draw a bar graph with a scale of 2. Label it.

## Card B: one day

Invented for practice. Autumn at Pine Row is usually dry. One autumn day was stormy. Ro says autumn there is stormy. Is Ro right? One sentence.

## Card C: one year

Invented for practice. Last January had 4 snowy days. The town's 30-year January record runs from 3 to 15. Ro says last January is the town's climate. Is Ro right? One sentence.

## Card D: two sources

Invented for practice. Atlas card: Region X is cold in winter. Station card: it rains in only 2 months. Write one sentence about X using both.

## Card E: protection

Invented for practice. Goal: at least 4 of 6 paper figures stay dry. Limit: at most 7 tokens. A bus-stop cover costs 7 tokens. With it, 5, 5, 4 figures stay dry. Without it, 1, 1, 2. Does it have merit? Name one thing the trials do not show.
