# Day 113: One wall, three answers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Clear one wall. Mark three columns: seasonal graphs, climate ranges, hazard, design, harm. Put out the joined wall cards, the product H cards, the second colours and each child's product H with its six written notes. Stand the four day 98 pages with their headings showing. Highlight nothing; no one is named.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 112's exit tickets, s06-X112. Error E, 1 minute: draw one chain on the board, hazard, design, harm. Error F, 1 minute: read the limit box before any count. Error G is named in a word: say what was not tested. No child is named.

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P113-2]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 8 min. Product H revision

Alone, in a second colour, from the written notes. Each child changes one part and keeps the first answer, [[s06-P113-1]]. Do not supply the limits or the counts. Product H is scored after day 116, outside class, on s06-E13, s06-E14, s06-E15, s06-E16, s06-E17 and s06-E18.

#### 23 min. Consensus model

3 minutes: show the four day 98 pages again with their headings on. Close the anchor in four sentences: no two days are alike; each season has its usual weather; a place's climate is the range over many years; a design lessens harm and the hazard still happens.

10 minutes: every child places one wall card, [[s06-P113-3]]: a seasonal graph, a climate range, or a hazard, design and harm. Thirty children at 20 seconds each fill the 10 minutes. A child may place a card without a word.

7 minutes: pairs pick one question group (what repeats; does one year describe everywhere; how can harm be less) and write the sentence, [[s06-P113-4]].

4 minutes: read the question board back. **Ask.** Which of our three answers would you change if one new record came in tomorrow? Expect: "I would add it, then check if the usual or the range moved." Each answer raises the next question, for example what makes land wear away, next year.

#### 2 min. Notebook

Each child writes one line, [[s06-P113-5]]: today's goal, the evidence, what changed in the model.

#### 3 min. Exit E113

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X113]]. Look for: (a) no, one July is one year, and the climate is the range over many years, 3 to 9; (b) the sail lessened the scorching, within the limit, and the records do not show real playgrounds are safe. Error C says this July shows Harlow is hotter now. Error G says every real playground is safe. Note the letters.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards stay on the wall. Jar emptied by the adult, hands wiped. Product H collected.

### Other rooms

No wall: lay the cards on a table in three rows. No printer: copy the three card headings onto the board. One projector: show the four pages, then the wall; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall: lay three cards on a table. No class log: the parent writes four dated weather notes. No thermometer: write sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The helpers, the wall turns and the question board.

- **8 min. Product H.** Read the note. The child does [[s06-P113-1]].
- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside. The child does [[s06-P113-2]].
- **15 min. Three answers.** The child fills one card, [[s06-P113-3]], then [[s06-P113-4]].
  **Say.** "Which of our answers would you change if one new record came in?" Expect: "I would add it and see if the usual moved."
- **2 min. Notebook.** The child does [[s06-P113-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X113]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: place cards for one question group, keep the revision and the notebook. Longer: write the one record that would change your answer.

### Words and supports

Pattern, usual, range, hazard, harm, merit, limit. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow
