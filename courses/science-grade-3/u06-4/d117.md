# Day 117: Respond to the checks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have every paper back with its written feedback and scored components. Print the repair sheets for each group and one recheck card for each child's group. Set four group spots and write their names on the board: displays, evidence spans, source links, design reasoning. Keep day 114's exit tickets sorted by letter.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes. Clean-up is inside the last part.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P117-6]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 5 min. Return components

Hand back day 115's assessment and day 116's assessment with the scored components of products W, C and H, written feedback and the fresh work: day 115's records 2 minutes, day 116's records 2 minutes. Scored from the work you handed in. Each child marks the one component to work on, [[s06-P117-1]], 1 minute. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 30 min. Return and repair: four groups

This is the one response. Each child joins the group for the component marked. Each group meets you for 7 minutes; the rest work in pairs from the written repair on the repair sheets. A group of 7 or fewer says one revision each, 45 seconds a child; a larger group has a rotating seven speak while the rest write, and the record names who spoke. A child with several gaps works through them with an adult, then tries the recheck.
- Displays, [[s06-P117-2]]. Error A: lay day 114's graph beside one with the key used; one square is 2 days; rebuild one bar with counters grouped by the key.
- Evidence spans, [[s06-P117-3]]. Error B, 4 minutes: lay the odd day beside the season's records. Error C, 3 minutes: lay one year beside the range over many years.
- Source links, [[s06-P117-4]]. Error D: each child links one detail from each card.
- Design reasoning, [[s06-P117-5]]. Error E, 3 minutes: the design lessens what the hazard does. Error F, 2 minutes: the limit box first. Error G, 2 minutes: name one condition not tested.

**Ask.** What is the one thing you would now say differently? Expect: "I would say ___, because the record shows ___." Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Recheck

Alone, 3 minutes: [[s06-X117f]]. Unscored. Then each group hears its own fresh card and answers aloud, 2 minutes: evidence spans, [[s06-P117-7]] and [[s06-P117-8]]; source links, [[s06-P117-9]]; the other groups listen. Look for: bars at 16, 6, 12 and 2 with a scale; merit within the limit, and one thing the trials do not show; no, one day is not the season; no, one year sits inside the range; both cards in one sentence. Note the letters and give the papers to the class file.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the repair lines and the recheck card on the board. No groups: take each marked component with the whole class in turn. One projector: show the repair lines.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the parent takes the child's marked component alone. No second color: use a pen. No thermometer: write sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The groups, the helpers and the clean-up.

- **5 min. Look back.** Read the feedback together. The child does [[s06-P117-1]].
- **20 min. Revise.** Work the marked component only: [[s06-P117-2]], [[s06-P117-3]], [[s06-P117-4]] or [[s06-P117-5]].
  **Say.** "What would you now say differently?" Expect: "I would say ___, because the record shows ___."
- **5 min. The weather.** The child does [[s06-P117-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X117f]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one repair move in the marked group, then the recheck; never cut the recheck. Longer: write one record that would change your weather account, or the one trial that would test your design's limit.

### Words and supports

Revise, component, range, source, goal, limit, merit. Frame: "I would say ___, because the record shows ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 118 checks unit 5, fossils and environmental merit, on unseen records; nothing from today goes forward. Today's pages stay in each child's folder.
