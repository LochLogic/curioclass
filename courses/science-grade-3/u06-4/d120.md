# Day 120: Fix one slip, then say goodbye to the course

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the returned pages by child: day 118 and day 119, each with one written next step. Set four repair places, one for each group. Put a second-color pencil out for each child. On the record sheet list which children take which letters.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Stay indoors in a weather warning. Nobody looks at the sun. The adult alone handles any ice. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Paper and card only at the repair places.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[c3-P120-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 4 min. Return of feedback

Hand back day 118's assessment and day 119's assessment, each with one written next step. Children read their own, silently, and mark the one slip they will fix. Nothing is scored again. A child with no letter takes a fresh card and writes a question that can be tested.

#### 20 min. Repair groups

Four groups, 5 minutes each with the teacher; a child goes to the group for the letter on their note, or to two groups. The rest work from the station cards in a second color, [[c3-P120-2]]. Group 1, letters R and S, from c3-E118f: set the lower layer card beside this week's weather card, then mark the two places on a map. Group 2, letters T and U, from c3-E118c: match a torn strip to the whole strip, then sort three bone cards. Group 3, letters V and W, from c3-E118k: tick each limit for each trial, then add the words the card does not show to a claim. Group 4, letters X and Y, from c3-E119d: write the scale and two labels on a bare graph, then add "usually, not always" to a sentence. A group of up to eight gets about 30 seconds a child in a 5-minute turn.

#### 9 min. Exit

Alone, the exit ticket: [[c3-X120d]]. Read each card aloud twice, 3 minutes each. Note letters on the record sheet. Collect the tickets. Then ask aloud, 3 minutes, any child may answer: [[c3-P120-4]]; note the letters. The revised papers, [[c3-P120-2]], stay with the students in the folder.

#### 4 min. Hand-off

Children write [[c3-P120-3]] on the hand-off folder sheet and put it in their folder. The folder goes home with them.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Pencils and cards back to the folder. Wipe the tables. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the repair cards onto the board, one group at a time. No second color: write the change in pencil and draw a box round it. A small class: the teacher takes each group in order and no card stays out.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the exit cards onto paper. No second color: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The weather record and the groups.

- **5 min. Your notes.** Read the next steps on your day 118 and day 119 pages. Pick one slip.
- **24 min. Fix one slip.** Do [[c3-P120-2]], then a second slip if you can. Write [[c3-P120-3]] for your folder.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: "I changed one thing, and the first answer is kept."
- **9 min. Exit ticket.** [[c3-X120d]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fix one slip only. Never cut the new cards. Longer: write a second criterion sentence and say what it might pull against.

### Words and supports

Layer, fossil, extinct, criterion, limit, scale, label, usually. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

This is the last day of the course. The exit tickets are collected today and are not handed back in this course; the folder goes home.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
