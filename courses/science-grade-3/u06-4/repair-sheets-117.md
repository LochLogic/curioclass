# Repair sheets, day 117

Four seven-minute groups. The rest work in pairs from the written repair.

## Group 1: displays (A)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: 14 days, so 14 marks. Each mark is 2 days, so 7 marks. Rebuild one bar with counters grouped by the key.

## Group 2: evidence spans (B, C)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: one stormy day, so the season is stormy. The usual comes from many days. Someone might say: this year is the climate. Climate is the range over many years. Lay the odd day beside the season's records.

## Group 3: source links (D)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: Region Y is dry. Join both cards: warm all year and rain in only 3 months. Link one detail from each card.

## Group 4: design reasoning (E, F, G)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: the shelter stops the storm. It lessens what the wind does to the markers. Someone might say: cost does not matter if it works. Check the limit box. Someone might say: it worked in the model, so real places are safe. Name one condition not tested.

Each child states one revision, 45 seconds each. A larger group has seven speak while the rest write.
