# Check cards, days 118 and 119

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 118. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no words except where a card says so. Print "invented for practice" in small letters beneath each card. Read each card aloud twice.

## Dry basin card (day 118)

A dry, flat basin. Two cuts in the ground, North Cut and South Cut, two kilometers apart.

- North Cut, lower layer: fish bones of two kinds and water-plant leaf prints. One kind is Gar F. Upper layer: stump roots in place and a trunk piece 45 cm long. The layers lie flat and were never moved.
- South Cut, one layer: one fish bone and a snail shell.
- A guide card: Gar F lives nowhere now. The other bones match fish that live in rivers today.
- Mia's line: "This week is hot and dry, so the basin was always dry."

## Meadow pool card (day 118)

A meadow pool in a dry month. Pool depth fell from 40 to 10 cm. Iris flowers fell from 20 to 4. Tadpoles fell from 60 to 12. Dead tadpoles found: 30. 18 tadpoles were found in the creek this month; none were there before. No eggs were laid this month.

Goal: at least 8 of 10 iris patches in flower and at least 6 of 10 net scoops with tadpoles. Limits: 6 tokens, and the fence line stays clear.

| Trial | Iris patches in flower | Net scoops with tadpoles | Tokens | Fence line |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A, shade cloth | 8 of 10 | 7 of 10 | 5 | clear |
| B, rain barrel | 9 of 10 | 4 of 10 | 4 | clear |
| C, deep dig | 9 of 10 | 8 of 10 | 3 | blocked |

## Tucson cards (day 119)

Numbers invented for practice, written to look like a tidy weather summary for Tucson, Arizona. Say aloud that the numbers are made up for this check.

Source card, by season: spring 22 degrees Celsius, 4 rainy days in the season, west wind. Summer 34, 12 rainy days in the season, south wind. Autumn 26, 5, south wind. Winter 14, 6, east wind.

Faulty table: summer temperature 24, summer rainy days 8, autumn wind north; every other cell right.

Faulty graph: bars at 4, 8, 5 and 6 rainy days with no title, no labels and no scale.
