# Exit card (day 114)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Alone, on paper, one fresh card. Read aloud once.

Invented for practice. Marlow, a new town. Spring: 20 degrees Celsius, 8 rainy days. Summer: 28 degrees, 4 rainy days. Spring wind is east. Summer wind is west. Atlas card: warm summers. Station card: summer rainy days run from 2 to 9 over 30 years. A wind shelter costs 6 tokens. The limit is 6 tokens. Goal: keep at least 5 of 6 markers. With the shelter, 6, 6, 6 markers stay. Without it, 2, 1, 2.

(a) Make a table of the weather. Graph the rainy days with a scale of 2. Say what summer is usually like.

(b) Draw arrows for the shelter. Write a merit claim and one limit of the evidence.

# Exit card (day 113)

Invented for practice. Read aloud once.

Harlow's 30 Julys had 3 to 9 hot days. This July had 11. A model shade sail cut scorched benches from 7 to 2 for 5 tokens. The limit is 6. (a) Does this July change Harlow's climate? Why? (b) What do the records show, and not show?
