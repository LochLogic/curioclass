# Repair cards and exit cards, day 120

Four repair sets for day 120 and the exit cards. Plain line drawings, no brand names. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

## Repair sets

- **Set 1, letters R and S.** The lower layer card of the dry basin beside a card showing this week's hot, dry weather; a small map with the two cuts marked. Children say which shows long ago, and where else to look.
- **Set 2, letters T and U.** A strip of paper torn from a longer strip; three bone cards, one that matches a living fish, one that lives nowhere now and one unknown. Children say how long the whole strip was and sort the bones.
- **Set 3, letters V and W.** A trial table with a limits row. Children tick each limit for each trial, and add "the card does not show every living thing benefits" to a claim.
- **Set 4, letters X and Y.** A bare bar graph with one bar and no scale; a sentence about a season. Children write the scale and the two labels, read one bar, and add "usually, not always".

## Exit cards (answered alone, read aloud twice)

- **X120a.** A bar graph for Cedar Mesa winter with the side counting by 2, one bar drawn to 8, and no title.
- **X120b.** A dry canyon wall with a fish bone and a reed print low in the rock.
- **X120c.** A marsh gate with a tokens line and a goal line.

## Hand-off sheet

A folder sheet with three lines: a fossil sentence, a criterion sentence and the latest weather row.
