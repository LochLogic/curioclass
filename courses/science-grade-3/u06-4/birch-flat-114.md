# Birch Flat transfer case (day 114)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Pairs work this case. Teacher reads it aloud once.

## The town

Birch Flat. Seasons in this order: spring, summer, autumn, winter. Each season is a sample of 30 days.

- Average temperature, supplied: 12, 23, 15 degrees Celsius. Winter is below freezing, said in words.
- Days with rain or snow: 10, 6, 8, 4.
- Wind from most often: west, south, west, north.

## The atlas card and the station card

Atlas card: Birch Flat has cool, snowy winters. Station card: snowy January days over 30 years run from 3 to 12. Selected Januaries: 5, 11 and 7.

## A snow fence

A snow fence stops snow from drifting onto a model road. Goal: at most 3 of 10 cars blocked. Limit: at most 8 tokens. The fence costs 7 tokens. With it, cars blocked: 2, 3 and 2. Without it: 8, 7 and 8.

## Your task

8 minutes: make the table and graph the days with rain or snow. 6 minutes: describe the climate using both cards. 6 minutes: write a merit claim and one limit of the evidence.
