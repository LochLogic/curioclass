# Day 119: Mend a weather table and graph

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the source card and the student page. Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. Day 118's oral record stays on the record sheet unchanged and is not scored again today. The day 115 and day 116 observation marks stay as they are. Day 118's pages are not handed back until day 120.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "This is the second part of the last check, and day 118's was the first. I will not help. The Tucson numbers are made up for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages. Practise one touch: "Touch the word summer."

#### 25 min. Audit and correction

Show the source card and read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c3-E119a]] (6 minutes), [[c3-E119b]] (6 minutes), [[c3-E119c]] (5 minutes) and [[c3-E119d]] (5 minutes). A child who answers aloud is heard by the teacher, who ticks the heard column and says nothing.

#### 10 min. Explanation

Alone, [[c3-E119e]] (5 minutes) and [[c3-E119f]] (5 minutes). Collect every page.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Cards and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks.

### Marking

After class, 25 minutes: mark the six items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: X or Y. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the source card on the board and hide it between parts; children copy the faulty table onto plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Any printed weather summary may stand in; read the numbers yourself.

**Skip.** The directions practice and the clean-up.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the numbers are made up. Read the source card aloud once. Your child answers [[c3-E119a]] to [[c3-E119f]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Is every summer day in Tucson the same?" Expect: "No. It is usually hot, but one day can differ."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Season, source, table, bar graph, scale, label, usually, expect. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 120 hands day 118's assessment and day 119's assessment back, each with one written next step. It ends the course with a hand-off folder.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
