# Day 115: Check, two records and two cards

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the printed evidence records, the two fresh cards, and the student page for each child. Keep the score sheet in your folder. File each child's revised products W and C as repair evidence; nothing goes back today.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 2 minutes: papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. Day 114's exit tickets and pages stay in the folder; their letters wait for day 117. Today's fresh work is scored after class on s06-E1 to s06-E12; revised W and C get only a repair mark. Error A is read from s06-E3 and s06-E4, error B from s06-E6, error D from s06-E7 and error C from s06-E12. Read both tasks aloud: organize and graph the records; combine two cards. Read each card once; do not explain the numbers.

#### 20 min. Fresh check one

Alone on paper, the unseen Ridge records, [[s06-P115-1]], [[s06-P115-2]] and [[s06-P115-3]]. Give no help or feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: the four seasons in order with all three measures; bars at 6, 8, 2 and 4 on a stated scale that the bars use; summer usually warmer and wetter than winter, with different usual winds, and not a guarantee. Note error A (a scale named and ignored) and error B (one day taken for a season). Scored on s06-E1 to s06-E6.

#### 20 min. Fresh check two

Alone on paper, the unseen cards for regions M and N, [[s06-P115-4]], [[s06-P115-5]] and [[s06-P115-6]]. Give no help and no feedback. Look for: M warm all year and usually wetter in July, 14 to 26 days; N cold winters, mild summers, 1 to 9 days; the years change, with 15, 23 and 19 for M and 3, 8 and 5 for N; each claim tied to its card. Note error C (this year is the whole climate) and error D (one card only). Scored on s06-E7 to s06-E12.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write both cards on the board and read them aloud; children answer on notebook paper and rule their own graph. One projector: show each card while it is read, then take it down.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult copies each onto paper. No scored page: skip the last part.

**Skip.** The scoring and the clean-up.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Read the Ridge card aloud once. The child alone does [[s06-P115-1]], [[s06-P115-2]] and [[s06-P115-3]]. Then the cards for M and N, [[s06-P115-4]], [[s06-P115-5]] and [[s06-P115-6]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What do you expect in summer at Ridge, and how sure are you?" Expect: "Usually warmer and wetter than winter, not a guarantee."
- **5 min. What is scored.** Read [[s06-E1]] to [[s06-E12]]; they check today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the table and the expectation. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Season, scale, range, source, atlas card, station card, usually. Frame: "In ___ it is usually ___. The atlas says ___; the station says ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply the scale or the link between cards.

### Tomorrow

Day 116 is the second end check, on a design the class has not seen: own work, no feedback. Day 117 hands back the checklists; each child works on one component. Scored papers return on day 120.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
