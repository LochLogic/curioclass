# Day 118: A dry basin and a meadow pool

## Teacher page

### Before class

At the door, hand back day 117's exit tickets, s06-X117, s06-X117b, s06-X117c, s06-X117d and s06-X117e, without talk; none is used today. Print the two cards, each marked "invented for practice". Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. Open the heard column for any child who answers aloud.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions

Say: "This is the first part of the last check. I will not help. The cards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages and show where answers go. Practise one touch: "Touch the word layer."

#### 18 min. Dry basin

Show the dry basin card. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c3-E118a]] to [[c3-E118f]], 2 to 3 minutes each. At 17 minutes say: "Turn the page."

#### 18 min. Meadow pool

Show the meadow pool card. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c3-E118g]] to [[c3-E118l]], 2 to 3 minutes each. A child who answers aloud is heard by the teacher, who ticks the heard column and says nothing. Collect every page.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Cards and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks.

### Marking

After class, 40 minutes: mark the twelve items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: R, S, T, U, V or W. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on the board and hide it between parts; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** A dry streambed or a puddle that has shrunk may stand in; read the numbers yourself.

**Skip.** The directions practice and the clean-up.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each card aloud once. Your child answers [[c3-E118a]] to [[c3-E118l]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Is a hot, dry week proof the place was always dry?" Expect: "No. Layers and bones show what it was long ago."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Layer, fossil, extinct, evidence, goal, limit, token, trial. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 119 is the second part of the check: a Tucson weather table and bar graph to mend. Nothing from today is handed back until day 120.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
