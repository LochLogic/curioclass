# Day 118: A dry basin and a meadow pool

## Teacher page

### Before class

Day 117's exit tickets stay in the class file and go back on day 120 with the check results. Print the two cards, each marked "invented for practice". Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. Open the heard column for any child who answers aloud.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions

Say: "This is the first part of the last check. I will not help. The cards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages and show where answers go. Practice one touch: "Touch the word layer."

#### 18 min. Dry basin

Show the dry basin card. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c3-E118a]] to [[c3-E118f]], 2 minutes 30 seconds each. At 16 minutes say: "Turn the page."

#### 18 min. Meadow pool

Show the meadow pool card. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c3-E118g]] to [[c3-E118l]], 2 minutes 30 seconds each. A child who answers aloud is heard by the teacher, who ticks the heard column and says nothing. Collect every page.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Cards and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks.

### Marking

After class, 40 minutes: mark the twelve items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: R, S, T, U, V or W. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on the board and hide it between parts; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw each card on paper.

**Skip.** The directions practice and the clean-up.

- **35 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each card aloud twice. Your child answers [[c3-E118a]] to [[c3-E118l]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Is a hot, dry week proof the place was always dry?" Expect: "No. Layers and bones show what it was long ago."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Layer, fossil, extinct, evidence, goal, limit, token, trial. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 119 is the second part of the check: a Tucson weather table and bar graph to mend. Nothing from today is handed back until day 120.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
