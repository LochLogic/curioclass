# Item W fresh card (day 115, diagnostic, unscored)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Ridge records

Seasons are in this order: spring, summer, autumn, winter. Each season is a sample of 30 days.

- Average temperature, supplied: 16, 26, 32, 20 degrees Celsius.
- Days with rain or snow: 6, 8, 2, 4.
- Wind from most often: north, north, west, east.

## Your task

Make a labeled table. Make a bar graph of the days with rain or snow, with a scale. Use all three measures to write what to expect in a season. Say how sure you are.
