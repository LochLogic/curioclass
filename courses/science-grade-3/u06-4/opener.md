# Section 6.4 opener: joining the three models, checks and response

## What the section does

Five days, 113 to 117. The big question is: "What weather can we expect here, and how can people get ready for the dangerous kind?" Day 113 revises product H and joins the three class models on one wall: the seasonal expectation, the climate range and the hazard, design, harm arrows. Day 114 applies the joined model to a new town and a protection design, and holds the section exit. Day 115 is the end check for weather representation and climate descriptions. Day 116 is the end check for judging a design. Day 117 responds to the checks.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The seasonal expectation is likely, not certain.** It comes from many days.
- **Climate is the range over many years.** One year, or one day, is not the climate.
- **Two sources are joined.** The atlas card says what kind. The station card says how much.
- **Merit means the goal met within the limit.** The model shows the model. Name one thing not tested.
- **Weather cannot be stopped. Harm can be lessened.**

## Scoring, outside class

Day 115 and day 116 hold fresh diagnostics: 20 minutes each on day 115, 30 minutes on day 116, unscored. The scored work is the revised product W and the revised product C (after day 115) and the revised product H (after day 116). Eighteen points in all, one for each component. Score each point 1 when the component is complete and 0 when it is not. An unseen component is blank, not zero. Repairs happen in teaching slots only: day 113, then day 117. Never on days 115 and 116.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** the scale is ignored. **B:** one day guarantees the season. **C:** this year is the whole climate. **D:** one source is enough. **E:** the design stops the hazard. **F:** the budget is ignored. **G:** a model trial guarantees real protection.

## Supplies for the section

The score sheet, the wall cards, the Birch Flat sheets, the fresh cards for days 115 and 116, the repair sheets and recheck cards for day 117, squared paper, second colours. Weather equipment on days 113, 114 and 117.

## A room with nothing

Printed cards read aloud, said to be invented. The dated adult record for weather.
