# Item H fresh card (day 116, diagnostic, unscored)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## A wind screen

A wind screen protects a model waiting area. Goal: at least 7 of 9 lightweight markers stay in place. Limit: at most 13 tokens.

## The trials

With the screen: markers kept 7, 8 and 7. Without it: markers kept 2, 3 and 2. The screen costs 12 tokens.

## Your task

Annotate the hazard model. Judge merit against the goal and the limit. Name what the evidence cannot show.
