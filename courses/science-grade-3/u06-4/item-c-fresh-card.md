# Item C fresh card (day 115, scored)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Atlas card

Region M is warm throughout the year. Region N has cold winters and mild summers. Both have wetter and drier months.

## Station card

A 30-year record of rainy July days. M runs from 14 to 26 days. N runs from 1 to 9 days. Selected years: M had 15, 23 and 19. N had 3, 8 and 5.

## Your task

Describe the usual conditions of M and N. Compare their ranges. Show how the years change. Link each claim to its card.
