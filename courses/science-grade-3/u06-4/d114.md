# Day 114: A new town, the same model

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print one Birch Flat sheet for each pair and the exit tickets. Put the joined wall in view and squared paper at each pair. Decide six pairs to visit by lot, so each hears its claim for one minute; keep the record sheet to name them.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P114-1]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 20 min. Transfer practice

Pairs work Birch Flat, invented for practice. Read the sheet aloud once, 1 minute: four seasons, the winds, an atlas card, a station card, a snow fence on a model road. Winter is "below freezing", in words. Nothing is taught today; pairs use the wall model.

8 minutes: a labelled table, [[s06-P114-2]], and a graph of the days with rain or snow on a scale of 2, [[s06-P114-3]]. 6 minutes: the winters from both cards, [[s06-P114-4]]. 6 minutes: a merit claim and one limit on the evidence, [[s06-P114-5]]. Walk, and visit the six pairs chosen by lot, 1 minute each; each states one claim and the record names who. Do not supply the scale, the link between cards or the limit. If many pairs draw a bar for each day, hold the same page and ask what one square stands for: error A. If many call this winter the climate, ask how the 3 to 12 years compare: error C.

**Ask.** Which record gave you the most for the table, and which for the climate? Expect: "The season rows for the table; the station card for the climate." Any child may pass.

#### 12 min. Exit E114

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, on paper, [[s06-X114]]. Look for: bars at 8 and 4 with a scale; summer usually warm, less rainy, west wind, years change; the shelter meets the goal in every trial within the limit; it lessens markers lost, not storms. Error A: no scale. Error B: one day for a season. Error C: one year as the climate. Error D: one card only. Error E: the shelter stops the storm. Error F: cost ignored. Error G: the model means real places are safe. Note the letters.

#### 5 min. Notebook

2 minutes: the Ask above in pairs. 3 minutes: each child writes one line, [[s06-P114-6]]: the goal, the evidence, what changed in the model.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Jar emptied by the adult, hands wiped. Sheets and squared paper collected; today's pages stay in each child's folder; exits kept for day 117.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the sheet aloud and write its numbers on the board. No squared paper: rule a grid on notebook paper. One projector: show the sheet; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No squared paper: draw a grid on plain paper. No printed sheet: copy the numbers onto paper. No thermometer: write sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The helpers, the visits by lot and the clean-up.

- **5 min. The weather.** The child does [[s06-P114-1]].
- **20 min. Birch Flat.** Read the sheet. The child does [[s06-P114-2]] to [[s06-P114-5]].
  **Say.** "What do both cards say about winter?" Expect: "Cool, snowy, and the snowy days change from year to year."
- **5 min. Notebook.** The child does [[s06-P114-6]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** The paper card, [[s06-X114]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: table, graph and climate, then the merit claim as the exit; never cut the exit. Longer: write the one record that would change your climate account.

### Words and supports

Pattern, range, source, hazard, harm, merit, limit, trial. Frame: "In ___ it is usually ___. The atlas says ___; the station says ___. ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 115 is the first end check, on records the class has not seen: own work, no help, no feedback. Today's letters wait for day 117: error A, error B and error C to the display and evidence groups; error D to source links; error E, error F and error G to design reasoning.
