# Day 116: Check, a wind screen

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the printed evidence records, the fresh card and the student page for each child. Keep the score sheet in your folder. Put each child's revised product H in the folder; nothing goes back today.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while the card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### The day

#### 3 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. Day 115's assessment pages stay held. The scored work for this check is the revised product H, s06-E13, s06-E14, s06-E15, s06-E16, s06-E17 and s06-E18, scored after class, outside lesson minutes. Error F is read from s06-E15, error E from s06-E17 and error G from s06-E18. Read the task aloud: annotate the model and judge merit. Read the card aloud once, invented for practice; do not explain the numbers.

#### 30 min. Fresh diagnostic

Alone on paper, the unseen wind screen, [[s06-P116-1]], [[s06-P116-2]], [[s06-P116-3]] and [[s06-P116-4]]. Give no help or feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: arrows from the windstorm to the screen to fewer markers moved, with the wind still blowing; at least 7 of 9 markers stay in all three trials (7, 8 and 7) against 2, 3 and 2 without it, and 12 tokens within the limit of 13; a merit claim that says the screen changes how much wind reaches the markers; stronger winds and real places not tested. Note error E (the screen stops the storm), error F (the limit ignored) and error G (the model means real places are safe). The fresh work is a diagnostic and is not scored.

#### 9 min. Independent checking

Children reread each statement and tick it only where a card or table backs it, [[s06-P116-5]]. Give no hints.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the fresh card on the board and read it aloud; children answer on notebook paper. One projector: show the card while it is read, then take it down.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: the parent copies it onto paper. No scored page: skip the last part.

**Skip.** The scoring and the clean-up.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Read the wind screen card aloud once. The child alone does [[s06-P116-1]], [[s06-P116-2]], [[s06-P116-3]] and [[s06-P116-4]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "Does the screen have merit, and what can the trials not show?" Expect: "Yes, it meets the goal within the limit; stronger winds were not tested."
- **5 min. Checking.** The child does [[s06-P116-5]]. Then read [[s06-E13]] to [[s06-E18]]; they are scored from the revised product. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the claim and the limit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Goal, limit, token, trial, merit, claim, tested. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply the goal, the limit or the counts.

### Tomorrow

Day 117 gives back day 115's assessment and day 116's assessment with the scored components. Each child marks one component and joins its group: displays; evidence spans; source links; design reasoning for error E, error F and error G.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
