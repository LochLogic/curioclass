# Day 25: Who needs what?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape the carrier cards, need cards and design-cycle chart to the board. The chart is review. Print a need strip each.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar; ice is the adult's. Non-glass thermometer only. Stay indoors in a warning. Never look at the sun. Observers wash hands after the pots and the jar. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P25-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 24's exit tickets, s02-X24. Error A, 2 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap and say the screw leaped from 2 cm without touching. Error D, 2 minutes: write a plan with two changes on the board and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box.

#### 3 min. Retrieve grade 2 design words

Two new practice carriers; does well and limit are grade 2 review. A holds three blocks, B one. Both must hold two, each from one strip of paper, the limit. **Ask.** Which meets the need? Why? Expect: A does well, holding three when two are needed; B does poorly, it holds one. Children write [[s02-P25-2]]. A miss: lay the records against the need.

#### 4 min. Hook: the cupboard

Read the cupboard story aloud once; show the picture. **Ask.** Who needs what? Expect: the class needs the door to stay shut so the beads stay in; someone must still open it.

#### 3 min. What we know

List what a magnetic pull did on days 17 to 24: pulls across a gap, stronger when closer, the ends matter, only some metals such as steel, a switch turns it on and off. The question board asks: how could a magnetic effect serve people?

#### 3 min. The design-cycle chart

The grade 2 chart comes back: define, sketch, build, test, compare. Today is only define, the step that says the problem.

#### 7 min. Need, not equipment

A design problem starts with a need: who needs what, so that. It is not a list of things or an answer. Show the cupboard cards: "The cupboard door needs to stay shut." and "Magnets, tape and a door." **Ask.** Which says the need? Which lists things? Expect: the first; the second. Then the dry-paper pair, no magnets: "The class needs the paper to stay dry in the rain." and "A plastic sheet and a rope." Same answer. Say we do not name a magnet yet.

#### 6 min. Write the need

Each child fills the need strip, [[s02-P25-3]]: ___ needs ___ so that ___, and draws who needs it. A partner may scribe.

#### 3 min. Share

A rotating six read theirs, 20 seconds each. The class picks the clearest by hands. Any child may pass.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add how a magnet could serve the need; mark answered questions.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X25]]; read both cards aloud. Ask [[s02-X25b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) no, it lists things, not who needs what; (b) the class needs the tray to stay on the sill when the window opens. Error E: "yes, it says what we need" or "use a magnet to hold it." Note letters. Pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards on the board and the need on paper. One child reads the story, writes the need and draws who needs it.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the story's need on paper. No printer: draw the strip.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records, the share and the question board.

- **8 min. Designs.** Do [[s02-P25-2]].
  **Say.** "Which carrier meets the need? Why?" Expect: "Carrier A. It holds three, and two are needed."
- **10 min. The cupboard.** Read: a class cupboard door swings open and the beads slide out. Say: "Who needs what, so that?" Expect: "The class needs the door to stay shut so the beads stay in."
- **8 min. Write it.** Do [[s02-P25-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X25]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X25b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one need card pair. Never cut the need strip, the share or the exit. Longer: write the need for the paper in the rain.

### Words and supports

Need, who, what, so that, design problem, define, does well, does poorly, limit. Frame: "___ needs ___ so that ___." A partner may scribe; home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 26 opens with the weather record, then 2 minutes of Return and repair and hands back day 25's exit. Error E ("yes, it says what we need" or "use a magnet to hold it") rewrites a list of things as who needs what, so that. Then the class turns the need into ways to check it and limits.
