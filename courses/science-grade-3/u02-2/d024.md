# Day 24: What does a switch change?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Rehearse the demonstration. Tape the wire ends. Put eight clips on a tray. Draw a board in four piles: switch, gap, uses, cannot test here. Lay out sticky notes.

### Safety and set-up

The teacher alone runs the nail and coil. Wire ends stay taped, the cell is disconnected between uses, and the wire may warm: stop if it does. Never short the cell. Every child wears goggles and stays seated, devices put away. The teacher handles and counts the clips. Observers wash hands after the pots. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 23's exit tickets, s02-X23. Error A, 2 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap, trace the arrow, and say what none seen means. Error D, 2 minutes: write a test with two changes on the board and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 2 min. Goggles

Every child fits goggles; neighbours help. Sit, devices away.

#### 9 min. Demonstration

Say only that this nail and coil is a magnet while the switch is on. Nobody builds it. **Ask.** What will the clips do with the switch on? With it off? Expect: jump up; fall. Run it. Switch on: clips leap to the nail tip from about 1 cm and cling; off: they fall. The class counts the clips held aloud and the teacher writes each count on the board. Children copy the first two on and off cycles into the switch record, [[s02-P24-1]]; the third cycle is watched and compared. Disconnect the cell after each cycle. No demonstrator: read a record naming the apparatus, observer and date, say it is weaker evidence than seeing, and use the same counts.

#### 4 min. Notice and wonder

Pairs say what they saw. Each pair posts one wonder on a sticky note, 15 in all.

#### 8 min. Questions

Each child writes one question the switch could help answer, [[s02-P24-2]]. The teacher sorts the notes and questions into switch, gap, uses and cannot test here. Run one: do the clips stay with the switch off? Only the switch changes.

#### 6 min. Useful devices

**Ask.** Where is a pull you can switch on and off useful? Expect: a crane that lifts steel and lets it go. Pairs find a second place, such as a door held open until the switch is off. Children write one, [[s02-P24-4]]; the notes go in the uses pile. Nothing is built.

#### 4 min. Claim

Children finish [[s02-P24-3]]: when the switch is ___, the clips ___; I know because I saw ___. Read one frame aloud; any child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X24]]; read both cards aloud. Look for: (a) no, it pulls across a gap; (b) two things change, so we could not tell which did it. Error A: "yes, it has to touch." Error D: "nothing is wrong, a bigger screw shows more." Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipe and return goggles. The teacher counts the clips. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No demonstrator or no printer: read the record aloud and copy its counts from the board. One child reads the record and writes the claim alone.

### At home

**Swap.** No demonstration at home: use the record in Say. No printer: draw the table.

**Skip.** The nail and coil, the groups, the living records and the sorting.

- **8 min. The record.** Read: a teacher ran a nail and coil. Switch on, 4 clips held. Off, 0. Do [[s02-P24-1]].
  **Say.** "What changed, and what did the clips do?" Expect: "The switch changed. Clips held with it on, fell with it off."
- **8 min. A question.** Do [[s02-P24-2]].
- **6 min. Where.** Do [[s02-P24-4]].
- **8 min. Claim.** Do [[s02-P24-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X24]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one on and off cycle. Never cut the question, the claim or the exit. Longer: sort five more wonders.

### Words and supports

Switch, on, off, clips held, cling, question. Frames: "When the switch is ___, the clips ___." "I know because I saw ___." A partner may write the claim as the child says it. Describe the demonstration aloud for a child who cannot see it.

Then the class meets the cupboard that will not stay shut.
