# Static cards, day 23

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Card 1: rubbed

A plastic comb is held over a heap of ten paper bits. Two bits have jumped up and cling to the teeth. A small label says "rubbed". An arrow starts on a bit and points up to the comb.

## Card 2: not rubbed

The same comb over the same bits. No bit has moved. A small label says "not rubbed".

## Card 3: near and far

Two drawings side by side. Left: the comb 2 cm above the bits, six bits lifted, a long arrow. Right: the comb 6 cm above, one bit lifted, a short arrow. Each arrow starts on a bit.
