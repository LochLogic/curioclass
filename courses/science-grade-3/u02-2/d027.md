# Day 27: Try one latch

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count into each tray two magnets, three washers on a thread and tape. Set a cupboard model by each. Leave the statement and token card on the board. Put the flowering pots and swabs on one table.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on every child. Magnets (12) and washers (18) counted out and back; fingers clear of pinches; magnets and washers from mouths; magnets from phones and tablets. Trials stay over the tray. Check allergies before any child touches a flower; no touching faces; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 26's exit tickets, s02-X26. Error E, 2 minutes: rewrite a list of things as who needs what, so that. Error F, 2 minutes: lay a proposal card beside the printed allowance and check it line by line.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box.

#### 10 min. Pollination (set-up for unit 3), with sketching

A set-up for unit 3, not a test. A rotating six work in pairs at the flowering pots, one swab per plant: brush each open flower and carry pollen to a different plant, then wash hands (2 minutes inside the 10). Allergic children sketch instead. The rest sketch one latch on the sheet, [[s02-P27-1]]: where each magnet goes, which ends face, an arrow across the gap. Then each fills the statement strip, [[s02-P27-2]], from the board.

#### 3 min. Plan check

Each group settles one sketch and checks it against the criteria and limits: no more than two magnets, three tape lengths, seven tokens. The pollinators rejoin.

#### 8 min. Build

Eight making minutes, one attempt only, over the tray. No second try, no new parts.

#### 4 min. Test

Hang washers one at a time. Each child tries the one-hand open once, about 15 seconds, 75 seconds a group. Write the washers held, 0 or 3 or more as they are, and yes or no for one hand, [[s02-P27-3]]. A trial that fails to read is repeated once.

#### 7 min. Refine the problem

One speaker a group, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes. **Ask.** Did the criteria let us judge? Which criterion needs a clearer measure? Expect: opens with one hand did not say how hard to pull. Each group makes one change to the statement, not a rebuild: [[s02-P27-4]].

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X27]]; read both cards aloud. Ask [[s02-X27b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) no, two things changed, so the position may not be what fixed it; (b) no, it broke the materials limit. Error D: "yes, higher is better." Error F: "yes, it held, so it meets the problem." Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count 12 magnets and 18 washers back. Tape off. Wipe goggles. Stack models. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No models: judge two sketches against the statement on paper, say it is weaker evidence, and still fill the strip. One child tries a box lid, two fridge magnets and washers.

### At home

**Swap.** No model: a box lid and box, two fridge magnets, a few washers; keep magnets from phones. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. No printer: draw the sheet.

**Skip.** The flowers, the groups and the sharing.

- **8 min. Sketch.** Do [[s02-P27-1]].
  **Say.** "Which ends face? Where does the arrow go?" Expect: "Red faces blue. The arrow crosses the gap."
- **7 min. The statement.** Do [[s02-P27-2]].
- **10 min. Try it.** Make one latch. Hang washers one at a time. Do [[s02-P27-3]].
- **5 min. One change.** Do [[s02-P27-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X27]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X27b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the test only, no pollination. Never cut the one attempt, the one change or the exit. Longer: sketch a second idea and say which limit it meets.

### Words and supports

Sketch, attempt, trial, criterion, limit, changed, token. Frames: "We changed ___." "It showed ___." A partner may hang the washers as the child directs.

### Tomorrow

Day 28 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 27's exit. Error D ("yes, higher is better") crosses out every change but one. Error F ("yes, it held, so it meets the problem") checks a proposal line by line against the printed allowance. Then younger children need to open the same cupboard.
