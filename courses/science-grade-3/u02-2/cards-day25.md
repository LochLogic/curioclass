# Cards, day 25

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Grade 2 carriers

Two paper carriers. Carrier A is a strip folded in two and holds three equal blocks. Carrier B is a single flat strip and holds one. Both must hold two blocks. Ask which does well, which does poorly, and what the limit is.

## Need and equipment

Two cards side by side. Left: "The cupboard door needs to stay shut." Right: "Magnets, tape and a door."

A third card for a non-magnet example. Left: "The class needs the paper to stay dry in the rain." Right: "A plastic sheet and a rope."
