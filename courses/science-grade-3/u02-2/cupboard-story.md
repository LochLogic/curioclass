# The cupboard that would not stay shut (read aloud, day 25)

An original classroom story. The teacher reads it aloud once. Children look at a plain line drawing of a cupboard with a door slightly open.

Our class has a supply cupboard. It holds paper, glue sticks, rulers and a bag of beads. The door swings open when someone walks past. Last week the beads slid out and rolled under the tables. Everyone helped pick them up.

Mr. Okafor, who teaches the class next door, says the door is too loose. Ana says it needs a lock. Dev says that would be hard for little hands. Lin says, "We should say what we need first."

The class looks at the cupboard. Who needs the door to stay shut? Who needs to open it? What would we check, to know it works?
