# Day 23: Does a rubbed comb pull more when it is closer?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six trays: comb, cloth, ten bits, distance strip. Post the day 19 class table. Draw an empty bit table.

### Safety and set-up

Check allergies first. Comb and cloth are the default. A balloon on hair is by choice only: an adult alone inflates it and picks up fragments at once. Keep bits from mouths and devices away. No circuits. Observers wash hands after the pots. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 22's exit tickets, s02-X22. Error A, 2 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap, trace the arrow, and say what none seen means. Error D, 2 minutes: write a test with two changes on the board and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 3 min. Retrieve the gap table

Show the day 19 class table. **Ask.** When the gap got smaller, what did the lean do? Expect: in our trials it got bigger. All write [[s02-P23-1]]. Hear a rotating six, 20 seconds each.

#### 4 min. Ask the question

Show static card 2, then card 1: the same comb, not rubbed, then rubbed. On day 17 a rubbed comb lifted bits across a gap. **Ask.** Does the rubbed comb lift more bits when it is closer? Expect: maybe; test it. Children fill in the plan table, [[s02-P23-2]]: change the gap (1, 3 and 6 cm); keep the comb, cloth, 20 strokes and the ten bits; watch the bits lifted.

#### 2 min. Safety and set-up

Dry hands. Bits stay on the tray.

#### 15 min. Investigate

Six groups of five. Each trial: rub 20 strokes, hold the comb at the gap over the bits for 5 seconds, count the bits lifted or clinging. Nine trials of about 60 seconds (three gaps, three tries) take 9 of the 15 minutes; four children make two, one makes one. Everyone writes all nine counts in [[s02-P23-3]]. Write none seen when no bit lifts, never no pull. An unclear try is repeated once and both kept. If nothing lifts, the air may be damp: write none seen, use an invented record for the table (said aloud to be invented) and try another day.

#### 5 min. Class table

Groups post counts by gap. **Ask.** What changed when the gap changed? Expect: in our trials the closer gap lifted more bits. Hear a rotating six, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes: a count and [[s02-P23-4]].

#### 4 min. Compare with the magnet

Static is the word for a pull that starts with rubbing. Show static card 3. **Ask.** What is the same and different about a comb lifting paper and a magnet pulling a clip? Expect: same, a pull across a gap, bigger when closer; different, the comb needs rubbing and lifts paper, not only steel. Children draw [[s02-P23-5]]: the arrow starts on a bit, points up to the comb and is longer for the closer gap, labelled rubbed. Add rubbed to the class model.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X23]]; read the card aloud. Look for: (a) none seen at 5 cm, the pull may be too small to see, closer lifted more; (b) keep the gap, ruler, cloth and bits. Error A: "Pat is right, there is no pull." Error D: "move it closer too." Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Count combs and cloths back. Sweep bits into the bag. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the bit table on the board. One child tests a comb, a cloth and torn paper at two gaps.

### At home

**Swap.** No comb: a plastic ruler. No wool: a dry wool sock. No printer: draw the table on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the living records and the class table.

- **5 min. Plan.** Do [[s02-P23-2]].
- **15 min. Try three gaps.** Tear ten bits of paper. Rub the comb 20 strokes. Hold it over the bits 5 seconds at 1, 3 and 6 cm. Do [[s02-P23-3]].
  **Say.** "What changed when the comb was closer? What do you write if no bit lifts?" Expect: "More bits lifted. None seen, not no pull."
- **8 min. Draw it.** Do [[s02-P23-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X23]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two gaps, two tries each. Never cut the plan, none seen or the exit. Longer: add a fourth gap.

### Words and supports

Static, rubbed, lifted, gap, none seen. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___." "None seen at ___ cm." A partner may hold the comb.

### Tomorrow

Day 24 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 23's exit. Error A ("Pat is right, there is no pull") lays a ruler across the gap. Error D ("move it closer too") crosses out every change but one. Then a magnet is switched on and off.
