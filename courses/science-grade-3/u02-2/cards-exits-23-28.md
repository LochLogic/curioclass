# Exit cards, days 23 to 28

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 23

A plastic ruler rubbed on cloth is held over cereal bits. At 1 cm four bits are lifted. At 5 cm none are lifted. Pat says it has no pull at 5 cm.

## Day 24

A switched magnet over a steel screw. Switch on: the screw jumps up to the magnet from 2 cm. Switch off: it stays. A second card shows Pat's plan: turn the switch on and also use a bigger screw.

## Day 25

Card 1: Pat's list. A list that reads "a bucket, a rope and a hook". Card 2: a plant tray slides on a windowsill when the window opens.

## Day 26

Card 1: "Use a clip and one strip of tape." Card 2: a pencil holder. The limit is 5 tokens. Pat's holder costs 8 tokens and holds more pencils than needed.

## Day 27

Card 1: a window-blind cord that swings loose. A team moved the magnet higher and added more tape, and say the new position fixed it. Card 2: a team used a third magnet although the problem allows two, and the cord held.

## Day 28

A magnetic pickup tool for three steel washers. Allowed: two magnets, one card, two tape lengths, six making minutes, five tokens. Each supply costs one token.
