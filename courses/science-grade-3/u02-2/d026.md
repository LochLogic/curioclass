# Day 26: How will we know the latch works?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count into each tray two magnets, three washers on a thread and tape. Set a cupboard model by each. Post the token card and proposal cards. Draw the statement frame: need, criteria, limits.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on every child. Magnets (12) and washers (18) counted out and back; fingers clear of pinches; magnets from mouths, phones and tablets. Trials stay over the tray. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar; observers wash hands. Nobody looks at the sun. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P26-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 25's exit tickets, s02-X25. Error E, 2 minutes: rewrite a list of things, such as a bucket and a rope, as who needs what, so that.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box.

#### 3 min. Recap

Read the clearest need from day 25: the class needs the cupboard door to stay shut so that the supplies stay in.

#### 6 min. Criteria

**Ask.** How would we check the need is met? Expect: hang something from the handle and see if it stays shut; open it. A criterion is a way to check the need is met; the need alone cannot be checked, so we add several. Ours: stays shut with two washers hanging from the handle; opens with one hand. We count washers; no grams. Children write [[s02-P26-2]].

#### 9 min. Measure the hold

Six groups of five, goggles on. Tape one magnet to the door and one to the frame, red end to blue. Hang washers on a thread from the handle one at a time over the tray. Three runs of three adds, about 30 seconds each, take 4.5 of the 9 minutes; each child adds a washer or records. Everyone writes the washers held in [[s02-P26-3]]. Write 0 and 3 or more as they are. A pair too weak for the door uses a lighter card door.

#### 6 min. Limits and the statement

**Ask.** What is the most we allow? Expect: a limit. Show the token card. Materials: at most two magnets and three tape lengths. Time: eight making minutes. Cost: seven tokens; a magnet is 2, a tape length 1. Two magnets and three tapes cost exactly 7. Children fill [[s02-P26-4]]. Build the statement on the board: need, criteria, limits.

#### 5 min. Judge two proposals

Show proposal cards 1 and 2. **Ask.** Which meets our statement? Why? Expect: 1; it holds three, opens with one hand and costs 6, under 7. Card 2 needs two hands and costs 9, over 7. Pairs decide and write [[s02-P26-5]]; hear three pairs, 40 seconds each.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X26]]; read it aloud twice. Ask [[s02-X26b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) two checkable criteria and the limits: three materials, 5 minutes, 6 tokens; (b) no, over the limit. Error E: "use sticks, tape and clay; it looks good." Error F: "yes, it works better, so the limit does not matter." Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count 12 magnets and 18 washers back. Tape off. Wipe goggles. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No cupboard models: a box lid, two fridge magnets and a few washers. No printer: write the criteria and limits on the board. One child can test one lid and fill the statement.

### At home

**Swap.** No cupboard: a box lid and box with two fridge magnets and a few washers; keep magnets from phones. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. No token card: draw it.

**Skip.** The weather, the groups, the proposals and the living records.

- **6 min. Criteria.** Do [[s02-P26-2]].
  **Say.** "How could we check the door stays shut?" Expect: "Hang washers on the handle. See if one hand opens it."
- **12 min. The hold.** Tape one magnet to the lid and one to the box. Hang washers one at a time. Do [[s02-P26-3]].
- **7 min. Limits.** Do [[s02-P26-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X26]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X26b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two runs, not three. Never cut the criteria, the limits or the exit. Longer: judge a third proposal of your own.

### Words and supports

Criterion, limit, materials, time, cost, token, washers held. Frames: "The limit is ___." "We can check it by ___." A partner may place the washers as the child directs.

### Tomorrow

Day 27 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 26's exit. Error E ("use sticks, tape and clay; it looks good") rewrites a list of things as who needs what, so that. Error F ("yes, it works better, so the limit does not matter") checks a proposal line by line against the printed allowance. Then each group sketches and tries one latch.
