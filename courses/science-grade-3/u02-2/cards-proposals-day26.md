# Proposal cards, day 26

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Proposal 1

A cupboard door with one magnet on the door and one on the frame, red end to blue end. Two washers hang from the handle. Note: holds three washers, opens with one hand, costs 6 tokens.

## Proposal 2

A cupboard door with two magnets on the door and two on the frame, and extra tape. Note: holds two washers, needs two hands to open, costs 9 tokens.
