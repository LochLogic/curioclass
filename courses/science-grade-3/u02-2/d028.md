# Day 28: What changes when the user changes?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Leave day 27's statement and results on the board. Print a new user statement each.

### Safety and set-up

A paper day: no latch parts. Check allergies before any child touches the pots; the two observers wash hands after the pots. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 27's exit tickets, s02-X27. Error D, 2 minutes: write a plan with two changes on the board and cross out every change but one. Error F, 2 minutes: lay a proposal card beside the printed allowance and check it line by line.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 5 min. A new need

Say that younger children in the school need to open the same cupboard. They are invented users; nobody speaks about home. **Ask.** What changes? Expect: the door must open more easily. Children write [[s02-P28-1]] on the new user statement.

#### 6 min. Use the evidence

Read day 27's washer counts from the latch hold record, a rotating six at 20 seconds each. Recall day 19: a larger gap gave a weaker pull. **Ask.** What does that suggest for a lighter pull? Expect: a little more gap opens more easily, but may hold fewer washers. Children write [[s02-P28-2]].

#### 10 min. Revise the criteria

Pairs revise the holding and opening criteria. A changed need changes what we want, and holding more and opening easily can pull against each other: that is a tradeoff, from grade 2, where a design wins one thing and loses another. Three pairs share, 40 seconds each. The class agrees one change and a reason tied to the younger users. The limits stay: two magnets, three tape lengths, eight making minutes, seven tokens. Children write [[s02-P28-3]].

#### 8 min. Write the statement

Each child writes the full statement for the new users, [[s02-P28-4]]: need, two criteria that can be checked, and all three limits. A partner may scribe; dictation counts.

#### 8 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X28]], the section exit ticket; read the card aloud twice. Say that this card has its own price, one token a supply, not the class price card. Ask [[s02-X28b]] and [[s02-X28c]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Look for: (a) one criterion changed to fit the helper, a reason about the helper, the limits kept; (b) all the limits; (c) one limit named that stays, with the reason. Error E: "(a) a magnet, a card and tape." Error F: "(a) or (c) take away the token limit so it is easier." Note letters on the record sheet. Pages stay in the folder.

#### 2 min. Question board

Add how a magnetic effect could serve people; mark answered questions.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the frame on the board and the statement on paper. One child writes the whole statement alone from the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: draw the statement frame. No day 27 counts: use "held 3, opened with one hand."

**Skip.** The living records, the sharing and the question board.

- **5 min. A new need.** Say: "Younger children need to open the cupboard." Do [[s02-P28-1]].
  **Say.** "What changes?" Expect: "The door must open more easily."
- **6 min. The evidence.** Do [[s02-P28-2]].
- **8 min. One change.** Do [[s02-P28-3]].
- **8 min. The statement.** Do [[s02-P28-4]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X28]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X28b]] and [[s02-X28c]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one criterion changed, not two. Never cut the statement or the exit. Longer: write a second user, such as someone with a weak grip, and the change.

### Words and supports

User, need, criterion, limit, tradeoff, token. Frames: "___ needs ___ so that ___." "I changed ___ because ___." "The limit is ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 29 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 28's exit. Error E ("a magnet, a card and tape") rewrites a list of things as who needs what, so that. Error F ("drop the token limit so it is easier") checks a proposal line by line against the printed allowance. Then the class revises its model of magnets.
