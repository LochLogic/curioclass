# Outcome cards (day 70)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Pairs sort the first three cards by outcome. The outcomes are: does well, does poorly, cannot stay. The fourth card is read and discussed.

## Hare card

A hare on the tundra has new young. It is hard to see on the snow. It finds willow to eat.

## Musk ox bull card

A musk ox bull has lost most of its body fat. Bulls in this state have a greater risk in a severe winter. It is not certain to die. Many bulls live through the winter.

## Rainforest frog card

A rainforest tree frog is on the winter tundra. It needs warm, wet air. The tundra freezes. The frog cannot stay.

## Wood frog card

A wood frog shelters in leaf litter in a forest. Part of its body water freezes in winter. It thaws in spring and is well. Its place differs from the tundra. It is not doing poorly.
