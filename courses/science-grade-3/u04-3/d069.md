# Day 69: What does the place give?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the wall drawing of the tundra. Cut the word cards apart, one set a pair. Draw two columns on the board, Needs and Conditions.

### Safety and set-up

The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic, and no one looks at the sun. An adult prechecks the weather, goes with the two helpers, who alone touch them, empties the jar and handles ice. In a weather warning the class stays indoors. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 68's pages, s04-P68-1, s04-P68-2 and s04-P68-3, and tickets, s04-X68, with the item V look-for checklist, 1 minute. Then 2 minutes: Error E (a pair formed, so chicks hatched): lay the pair card beside the chick card. Error C (big thorns guarantee a bush lives): read the table's exception.

#### 32 min. Needs and conditions

6 minutes: read the hare card aloud, as invented. A need is something a living thing must get, as you learned in kindergarten (review); conditions are what a place is like. Write the hare's needs left on the board, the tundra's conditions right. It eats willow and hides in the snow in its white coat: needs meet conditions, so it does well. Children write [[s04-P69-1]]. **Ask.** What does the hare need, and what does the tundra give it? Expect: food and a place to hide; willow and snow.

8 minutes: read the polar bear card aloud: the coast of the tundra, where sea ice holds seals. Draw an arrow from its need, seals, to its condition, sea ice, and write so on it. Judge by needs and conditions, never by kind of animal. **Ask.** What does the bear need, and where does the place give it? Expect: seals, from the sea ice.

8 minutes: read the rainforest tree frog card aloud: it needs warm, wet air all year, and the winter tundra freezes. Draw its need with a cross where the link should be: a missing link (review from kindergarten), so it cannot stay. Children fill [[s04-P69-2]]. **Ask.** Which link is missing? Expect: warm, wet air; the tundra freezes instead. Error F sounds like: frogs live anywhere; point to the cross.

10 minutes: the wood frog is a separate comparison from a forest and wetland, never evidence about the tundra. Read its card aloud. In winter part of its body water freezes; in spring it thaws and moves to a pond. Its needs meet its own place. Say the frog words match reading. Pairs, all at once: lay the 14 word cards in two columns, 3 minutes; write the wood frog's needs and conditions, [[s04-P69-3]], 3 minutes; each child adds a Conditions column and one need arrow with so to the day 59 drawing in a second color, and crosses the frog's missing link, [[s04-P69-4]], 4 minutes. Hear the rotating six; any child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit E69

Read it twice. Alone, [[s04-X69]], 2 minutes. Look for: the mat plant, whose needs meet the conditions, and the banana plant's missing need, warmth all year. Error F says both, plants grow anywhere. Error G says neither, nothing can grow in the cold. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P69-5]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards and drawings away. Helpers wash hands.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the cards aloud and write the word cards on the board. No weather tools: read the dated adult record.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult says each card in plain words. No day 59 drawing: draw one hare and one frog. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The repair, the sort in pairs and the weather record.

- **5 min. Hare.** The child does [[s04-P69-1]].
  **Say.** "What does the hare need, and what does the tundra give it?" Expect: food and a place to hide; willow and snow.
- **7 min. Frog.** The child does [[s04-P69-2]].
  **Say.** "Which link is missing?" Expect: warm, wet air; the tundra freezes.
- **10 min. Wood frog.** The child does [[s04-P69-3]].
- **8 min. Drawing.** The child does [[s04-P69-4]].
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X69]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: do the hare, the frog and the drawing. Never cut the missing link or the exit.

### Words and supports

Need, conditions, needs meet conditions, does well, missing link. Frame: "It needs ___, and the place has ___, so ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 70 opens with the commoner of error F (both, plants grow anywhere: draw the missing link on the banana card) or error G (neither, nothing can grow in the cold: point to a second kind whose needs are met), 2 minutes. Then three outcomes in one habitat.
