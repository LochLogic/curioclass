# Day 73: Judge who does well in the schoolyard

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, the adult prechecks the route, weather, allergies and boundaries, and decides: walk or photographs. Set out the clipboards, one inventory sheet and one photograph for each group, and the wipes at the door, one tub for each table of five.

### Safety and set-up

Outdoors the class stays in sight, walks, wears closed shoes, looks only and touches no living thing, unknown organism or water. Hands are wiped at the door, 15 seconds a child, six tables at once. In a weather warning, with allergy symptoms or a missing adult, use the photographs. The thermometer is non-glass, the adult empties the jar and handles any ice, the jar is plastic, and no one looks at the sun; the weather is read at the usual place, not carried outside. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 72's pages, s04-P72-1, s04-P72-2, s04-P72-3 and s04-P72-4, and tickets, s04-X72; keep the revised item H for scoring. Error F ("it is there, so it is doing well"), 2 minutes: look in the inventory for a sign of doing well or poorly, not only what is there. Then the safety rules again, 2 minutes: each group reads one of its rules aloud.

#### 26 min. The schoolyard

3 minutes out: groups walk to their spots, an adult in front and one behind. 12 minutes at the spot: the group stands together, and one child at a time takes a turn of 2 minutes and writes one entry on the sheet: a kind seen, a condition and a sign of doing well or poorly. Five turns take 10 minutes; the last 2 the group reads its entries aloud. A kind is a picture word, such as a plant with narrow leaves; the sheet is evidence of needs and conditions, not a list of names. Any child may pass a turn while a partner points. 3 minutes back: each child copies their entry onto the page, [[s04-P73-1]].

8 minutes: **Ask.** Which entry shows doing well, and which doing poorly? Expect: a sign on the sheet, such as many new leaves or few small leaves; with no sign, the sheet cannot tell. Each child adds one need arrow marked so, or a cross on a missing link, to the day 59 drawing in a second color, using the group's entries, [[s04-P73-2]].

If the walk is unsafe, groups use their photograph and make the same entries indoors from it, 12 minutes; the sign must show in the picture.

#### 8 min. Exit E73

1 minute: read the card aloud twice. 6 minutes: alone, [[s04-X73]]. 1 minute: collect. Look for: the jewelweed does well, with many new leaves; the clover does poorly, with few small leaves; the cricket cannot stay, since every leaf is soaked. Error F says all three do equally well. Error G says the hollow is too wet for any kind. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P73-3]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud as supplied.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Clipboards stacked. Hands wiped at the door, one tub for each table. The adult empties the jar.

### Other rooms

No yard or no clipboards: use the photographs and a book as a board. No weather tools: read the dated adult record.

### At home

**Swap.** No yard: use a window view, a pot plant or a photograph. No clipboard: use a book. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The group turns, the repair and the weather record.

- **14 min. Look.** The parent stays close. Look only; touch nothing living or wet. Wear closed shoes, and wash hands after. The child writes entries, [[s04-P73-1]].
  **Say.** "What sign shows it is doing well or poorly?" Expect: many new leaves, or few small ones.
- **8 min. Drawing.** The child does [[s04-P73-2]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X73]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write two entries and the exit. Never cut the sign or the exit. Longer: say what change to the spot would move one kind from does poorly to cannot stay.

### Words and supports

Inventory, condition, sign, evidence, kind. Frame: "I saw ___, a condition ___, and a sign ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 74 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error F or error G from today's exit: draw the missing link on the cricket card, or point to the jewelweed whose needs are met. Then the class joins what it has found into one model of the tundra.
