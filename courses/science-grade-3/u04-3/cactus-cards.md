# Cactus cards (day 71)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Pairs argue on the frame.

## Saguaro in a desert

A saguaro cactus grows on a desert slope. The place is hot and dry. The sandy soil drains well. Summer rain comes. The saguaro does well.

## Saguaro in a bog

The same cactus is in a bog. The soil is waterlogged. Its roots stay wet. Wet soil can rot a cactus quickly. The saguaro cannot stay.
