# Item H cards (day 71)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Twelve minutes, alone. This is scored after day 77 from the revised work.

## Garden pond card

A garden pond has fresh water all year. It has oxygen and sunlight. The edges are waterlogged. There is no dry ground.

## Waterweed card

Waterweed needs light and wet roots. It makes new shoots and flowers.

## Marsh flower card

A marsh flower needs wet soil. It grows poorly when fully under water. It has fewer leaves.

## Burrowing mammal card

A burrowing grassland mammal needs dry burrows. Its burrows flood. It has no dry shelter.

## Your task

Which does well, which does poorly, which cannot stay? Use needs and conditions. Mark your copy of the model.
