# Day 72: Plan a schoolyard inventory

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the spot cards, one inventory sheet for each group and each child's item H with its feedback. The adult prechecks the route, weather, allergies and boundaries for day 73 today, and the photographs of each spot are ready as the fallback.

### Safety and set-up

Planning stays indoors. No equipment and no weather record today. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 71's pages, s04-P71-1, s04-P71-2, s04-P71-3 and s04-P71-4, with the item H written feedback, and tickets, s04-X71, 1 minute. Then 2 minutes on error F (yes, plants grow wherever there is soil): draw the missing link on the cranberry card.

#### 28 min. Plan the inventory

5 minutes: from grade 2, an inventory is a picture list of the kinds seen in one place at one time. Today each entry also names a condition and a sign of doing well or poorly. Show one made-up entry on the board: a plant with round leaves; on bare, dry ground; few small leaves. Say the sheet records evidence; the arguing comes after looking.

13 minutes: six groups of five, all at once. Show the four spot cards, A to D. Each group picks one spot, 3 minutes, and two groups may share a spot. Each group writes two conditions to look for, [[s04-P72-1]], then one sign of doing well and one of doing poorly, [[s04-P72-2]], 5 minutes, and agrees who records first. Visit each group for 1 minute.

10 minutes: preparation. Walk through the route and the boundary on the board. Groups write two rules for the walk, [[s04-P72-3]], 4 minutes: closed shoes, walk, stay in sight, look only and touch no living thing or water. Show the wipes, one tub for each table. Say that if weather or access stops the walk, each group works from a photograph of its spot on the same sheet. Rehearse the stop signal and the walk to the door, 2 minutes.

#### 8 min. Item H revision

Alone, in a second color, with the feedback in hand. Read your one question; add or fix one thing and keep the first writing, [[s04-P72-4]]. Collect every paper; the revised item H is scored after day 77.

#### 3 min. Exit E72

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X72]], 2 minutes. Look for: the fern and place kept; a condition that can be seen, shade or damp; a sign on the plant, such as many new fronds or brown tips. Error F writes "it is there, so it is doing well": being there is not a sign of doing well. Note letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, inventory sheets kept for day 73, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the spot cards aloud and copy the five columns on notebook paper. No photographs: draw each spot on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No yard: pick a window view, a pot plant or one street tree. No second color: use a pen. No wipes: wash hands after.

**Skip.** The group choice, the walk rehearsal and the repair.

- **8 min. Pick a spot.** The child does [[s04-P72-1]].
  **Say.** "What will you look for, and why?" Expect: two conditions, such as shade or damp soil.
- **6 min. Signs.** The child does [[s04-P72-2]].
- **6 min. Rules.** The child does [[s04-P72-3]].
- **8 min. Item H.** The child does [[s04-P72-4]], alone.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X72]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: plan one condition and one sign, and do the item H revision. Never cut the rules or the exit. Longer: say what a photograph cannot show that looking can.

### Words and supports

Inventory, condition, sign, evidence. Frame: "I will look for ___ because ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 73 opens with 4 minutes: error F from today's exit, 2 minutes (look in the inventory for a sign of doing well or poorly, not only what is there), and the safety rules again, 2 minutes. Then the groups visit their spot, or use the photographs, and judge who does well, poorly or cannot stay.
