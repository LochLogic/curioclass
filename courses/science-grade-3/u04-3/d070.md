# Day 70: Three outcomes in one place

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the wall drawing of the tundra. Set one set of outcome evidence cards on each pair's table and a needs frame sheet at each child's place. Write three heads on the board: Does well, Does poorly, Cannot stay.

### Safety and set-up

The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic, and no one looks at the sun. Only two helpers touch them; the adult empties the jar and handles ice. In a weather warning the class stays indoors. An adult prechecks the weather and goes with the helpers outdoors. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 69's pages, s04-P69-1, s04-P69-2, s04-P69-3, s04-P69-4 and s04-P69-5, and tickets, s04-X69, 1 minute. Then 2 minutes for the commoner of two repairs. Error F (both, plants grow anywhere): draw the missing link on the banana card. Error G (neither, nothing can grow in the cold): point to a second kind whose needs are met.

#### 32 min. Three outcomes

6 minutes: point to the hare on the wall drawing. Its needs meet the conditions, so it does well. Name the two other outcomes. Does poorly: the kind is there, and a sign shows it is not getting what it needs. Cannot stay: a need has no link, as with the frog's cross. **Ask.** What evidence shows doing poorly, not just different? Let children guess; do not answer yet.

10 minutes: pairs, all at once, read the hare card, the musk ox bull card and the rainforest frog card, invented for practice, and write each outcome and one piece of evidence, [[s04-P70-1]]. Then answer the Ask: a sign on the card, such as little fat left. Say the bull has a greater risk in a severe winter; it is not certain to die, and many bulls live through. Say this bull, never all musk oxen. Error C sounds like: the thin bull will die. Read the card's last line aloud.

8 minutes: read the wood frog card aloud; it is a separate forest and wetland comparison. Part of its body water freezes in winter, it thaws in spring and is well. **Ask.** Is it doing poorly because its trait is unusual? Expect: no; it is well in its own place, and that place differs from the tundra. Children write [[s04-P70-2]].

8 minutes: each child writes three claims, one for each outcome, on the frame sheet, [[s04-P70-3]]. Use the hare, the bull and the rainforest frog. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit E70

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X70]], 2 minutes. Ask [[s04-X70b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: the violet does well; the prairie flower lives but does poorly, with few small leaves; the tortoise cannot stay. Error F says they all do the same. Error G says no living thing lives in a dark forest. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P70-4]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud as supplied.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards and frame sheets away. The adult empties the jar; helpers wash hands.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the cards aloud and write the three heads on the board; children copy them. No weather tools: read the dated adult record.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult says each card in plain words. No frame sheet: write the frame on paper.

**Skip.** The repair, the pair sort and the weather record.

- **10 min. Three cards.** The child does [[s04-P70-1]].
  **Say.** "What shows doing poorly, not just different?" Expect: a sign such as little fat; the bull has a greater risk, not certain death.
- **6 min. Wood frog.** The child does [[s04-P70-2]].
- **10 min. Three claims.** The child does [[s04-P70-3]].
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X70]], answered alone. Then ask [[s04-X70b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write two claims, the first and the last. Never cut the evidence or the exit. Longer: name what would move the bull to cannot stay.

### Words and supports

Does well, does poorly, cannot stay, evidence, greater risk. Frame: "It needs ___, and the place has ___, so ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 71 opens with the commonest of error F, error G and error C from today, 2 minutes: match each need to what the forest floor gives, point to the violet whose needs are met, or read the bull card's last line. Then the class argues one cactus in two places.
