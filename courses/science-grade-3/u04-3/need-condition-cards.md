# Need and condition cards (day 69)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Each card is 50 words or fewer. Pairs sort the word cards into two columns: needs and conditions.

## Hare card

A hare lives on the winter tundra. It eats willow. It needs food, a place to hide and warmth. The tundra is cold, snowy and windy. Its winter coat is white.

## Polar bear card

A polar bear lives on the coast of the tundra. Its main food is seals. It hunts seals from the sea ice. It needs seals and the ice they rest on.

## Rainforest tree frog card

A rainforest tree frog needs warm, wet air all year. It never meets freezing weather. The winter tundra freezes.

## Wood frog card

This is a separate comparison. A wood frog lives in a forest and wetland. In winter it shelters in leaf litter. Part of its body water freezes. In spring it thaws and moves to a pond to breed.

## Word cards

Needs: food. Needs: a place to hide. Needs: warmth. Needs: seals. Needs: warm, wet air. Needs: leaf litter. Needs: a pond in spring.

Conditions: cold. Conditions: snow. Conditions: wind. Conditions: sea ice. Conditions: freezing. Conditions: forest floor. Conditions: spring ponds.
