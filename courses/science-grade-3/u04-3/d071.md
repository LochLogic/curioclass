# Day 71: A cactus in two places

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the cactus cards and one set of item H cards for each child. Plan 90 minutes after class for item H feedback, 3 minutes a child.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 70's pages, s04-P70-1, s04-P70-2, s04-P70-3 and s04-P70-4, and tickets, s04-X70, 1 minute. Then 2 minutes for the commonest of three repairs. Error F (they all do the same): match each need to what the forest floor gives. Error G (no living thing lives in a dark forest): point to the violet. Error C (the thin bull will die): read the bull card's last line aloud.

#### 24 min. Cactus in two places

6 minutes: read the desert card aloud, as invented for practice: a saguaro on a hot, dry, sandy slope that drains well, with summer rain. **Ask.** What does the saguaro need, and what does the desert give it? Expect: well-drained soil and summer rain; both are there. Children copy the claim from the board, [[s04-P71-1]].

8 minutes: read the bog card aloud: the same cactus in waterlogged soil, its roots staying wet. **Ask.** Which link is missing in the bog? Expect: well-drained soil; wet soil can rot a cactus quickly. Pairs say the claim to each other, 45 seconds each way, all at once. Each child writes it, [[s04-P71-2]], 4 minutes. Error F sounds like: a cactus is a plant, so it lives anywhere; point to the cross.

10 minutes: each child draws the saguaro in both places, joins its need to each condition with an arrow marked so, and crosses the missing link, [[s04-P71-3]]. The arrow says why: well-drained soil is the reason it does well. The model supports the argument; it does not replace the claim. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 12 min. Item H

Alone, with no help. Read the four pond cards and the task aloud once, as invented for practice. Children write [[s04-P71-4]] and mark a copy of the model. Four look-fors, 1 point each: a judgment from evidence that the waterweed does well, one that the marsh flower does poorly, one that the burrowing mammal cannot stay, and arrows that explain each need and condition link. A support names the card line, never the answer. Collect every paper.

After class, give written feedback, 3 minutes a child: mark each look-for supported or not yet and write one question for the weakest. Never write the answer.

#### 3 min. Exit E71

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X71]], 2 minutes. Look for: no, since the damp, sour soil it needs is not on the hill. Error F says yes, plants grow wherever there is soil. Note letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: read every card aloud; children answer on notebook paper and draw the model on its back.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the parent says each card in plain words.

**Skip.** The repair and the pair talk.

- **8 min. Saguaro.** The child does [[s04-P71-1]] and [[s04-P71-2]].
  **Say.** "Which link is missing in the bog?" Expect: well-drained soil; the bog is waterlogged.
- **6 min. Arrows.** The child does [[s04-P71-3]].
- **12 min. Item H.** The parent reads the four cards aloud once. The child does [[s04-P71-4]] alone, with no help.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X71]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write the bog claim, item H and the exit; say the desert claim aloud. Never cut item H or the exit. Longer: say what change to a place would move a kind from does poorly to cannot stay.

### Words and supports

Waterlogged: soaked with water. Drained: water runs off. Does well, does poorly, cannot stay. Frame: "It needs ___, and the place has ___, so ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 72 opens with a 3-minute Return and repair: item H's feedback, 1 minute, and error F (yes, plants grow wherever there is soil: draw the missing link on the cranberry card), 2 minutes. Then item H is revised in a second colour, and the class plans a schoolyard inventory.
