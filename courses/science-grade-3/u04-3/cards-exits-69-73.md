# Exit cards, days 69 to 73

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 69

Invented for practice. A cold, windy place with no trees and a short summer. A low mat plant grows well in short, cool summers. A banana plant needs warm weather all year. Which can live here? Say why.

## Day 70

Invented for practice. A shady forest floor. A violet grows many new leaves. A sun-loving prairie flower has few small leaves. A desert tortoise needs hot, dry ground and has none. Which does well? Which does poorly?

## Day 71

Invented for practice. A cranberry plant needs damp, sour soil. A dry, sandy hill has none. Would it do well on the hill? Say why.

## Day 72

Invented for practice. A class studies a damp, shady strip by the school gym wall. A fern grows there. A group writes its entry: "Fern. By the wall. It is there." Write the entry again. Add a condition of the place and a sign on the fern.

## Day 73

Invented for practice. A shaded, wet hollow. A jewelweed has many new leaves. A sun-loving clover has few small leaves. A dry-leaf cricket needs dry leaves to rest. Every leaf here is soaked. Argue who does well, poorly or cannot stay.
