# Spot cards (days 72 and 73)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Each group chooses one public spot from four.

- Spot A: the edge of the grass by the fence.
- Spot B: the base of a tree.
- Spot C: a flower bed or planter.
- Spot D: the sunny edge of the path.

If the walk cannot happen, each group uses a photograph of the same spot. They work indoors on the same sheet.
