# Group cards (day 60)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read each card aloud once. Each card is 45 words or fewer.

## Card 1: musk ox ring

Wolves come near. The musk oxen stand in a ring. Their horns face out. The calves stay inside the ring. The wolves meet horns, not calves.

## Card 2: wolf pack

A pack of wolves hunts elk. The wolves take different places. Some chase the elk. Others wait ahead. They hunt as one pack.

## Card 3: school of fish

Small fish swim close together in a school. They turn at the same time. A hunting fish sees so many fish at once. It is hard to pick just one.

## Card 4: honeybee colony

A honeybee colony has one queen and many workers. The queen lays eggs. Workers look after the young and guard the nest. Some workers bring food. The work is shared.
