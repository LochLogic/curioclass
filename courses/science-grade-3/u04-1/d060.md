# Day 60: How do groups help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Assign the card pairs: groups 1 to 6 take cards 1 and 2, 3 and 4, 1 and 3, 2 and 4, 1 and 4, 2 and 3. Draw the class chart on the board: card, how it helps, small or large, jobs. Check conditions for the weather record.

### Safety and set-up

The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic, and no one looks at the sun. An adult prechecks the weather, goes with the two helpers, who alone touch the equipment, empties the jar and handles any ice. In a weather warning the class stays indoors. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Grade 1 review: a chick calls and the parent brings food. Alone, [[s04-P60-1]]. **Ask.** Why does that help the chick survive? Expect: it gets the food it needs to grow. A miss is repaired in the next part.

#### 8 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 59's pages, s04-P59-1, s04-P59-2, s04-P59-3 and s04-P59-4, and tickets, s04-X59. Four repairs, 2 minutes each. If over a third missed one, run it with everyone; otherwise run it at your table while the others read a third group card.
- Pond and forest miss: lay the two lists side by side and match the frog; the other kinds belong to one place.
- Chick miss (review): put three pictures in order: the chick calls, the parent brings food, the chick stays alive.
- Error F (turtles live anywhere): draw a link from the turtle to open water, then find none on the tundra.
- Error G (nothing lives on the tundra): point to the hare and the musk oxen and say what each gets there.

#### 24 min. Four groups

6 minutes: model one link on the ring card. Say: "I think the ring protects calves, because I saw horns facing out and calves inside, so wolves meet horns, not calves." Name three ways a group can help: food, staying safe, handling a change such as cold. A group can also cost more mouths to feed.

13 minutes: six groups of five. Read each card aloud once, 1 minute. Each child reads one sentence from each of the group's two cards, 30 seconds a turn, 10 turns, 5 minutes. Then the group fills two chart rows and each child writes [[s04-P60-2]] and [[s04-P60-3]], 6 minutes. Small or large comes from the card's words: pack and ring are small; so many fish and many workers are large. Any child may pass.

5 minutes: the class chart. One child from each of the six groups gives one row, 30 seconds each, and you write it; compare answers for the same card. Look for a way it helps, small or large, and a job. Error C sounds like: the ring always saves the calves.

#### 3 min. Exit E60

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X60]]. Look for: the group pushes the fish into shallow water, so each pelican scoops food. Error B says all birds need a group to eat. Error C says the group always gets fish. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P60-4]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud and say it is supplied.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards are collected. The adult empties the jar; the helpers wash hands at two sinks.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the cards aloud; children copy two chart rows from the board. No weather tools: read the dated adult record aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the parent takes two cards and the child takes two. No printed cards: the parent reads them aloud from the page.

**Skip.** The repairs, the class chart and the weather record.

- **4 min. Chick.** The child does [[s04-P60-1]].
- **18 min. Two cards.** The parent reads two cards aloud, once each. The child does [[s04-P60-2]] and [[s04-P60-3]].
  **Say.** "How does this group help, and what job does each one do?" Expect: staying safe or food, and one job such as watching or guarding.
- **9 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X60]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups read one card each. Never cut the modeled link, small or large, or the exit. Longer: say what record would show a group has a cost.

### Words and supports

Group, protect, food, job, small, large, change. Frame: "The group helps by ___." Home-language answers count. Read every card aloud; a partner may read a sentence with the child.

### Tomorrow

Day 61 opens with a 4-minute Return and repair, 2 minutes each, for error B and error C from today's exit. Error B reads: all birds need a group to eat. Error C reads: the group always gets fish. Then the class reads a wolf ladder and a heron record, and draws a bar graph.
