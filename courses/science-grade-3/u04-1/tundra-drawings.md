# Tundra drawings and winter card (day 59)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Show these with no captions that explain. Read the winter card aloud.

## Drawing 1: the ring

Winter tundra, snow on the ground and a few low plants. Seven musk oxen stand in a ring, all facing outward. Their long dark hair hangs low. Two calves stand in the middle of the ring. Three wolves wait at the edge, a little apart. No animal is hurt. Wolves and musk oxen look at one another.

## Drawing 2: the hare

Winter tundra, the same snow. A white hare sits in the open in the front. A second white hare sits by a low mound of snow. Only its dark eye, its dark nose and the tips of its ears show.

## Winter card, words only

Cold. Snow. Strong wind. Few plants.
