# Day 59: What lives on the winter tundra?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Send the family note home. Write three heads on the board: Groups, Differences, Places. Check conditions for the weather record, and set out the weather record sheet.

### Safety and set-up

The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic, and no one looks at the sun. Only the two helpers touch the equipment; the adult empties the jar and handles any ice. In a weather warning the class stays indoors. An adult prechecks the weather and goes with the helpers outdoors. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Hand back day 58's pages, s03-P58-1, s03-P58-2, s03-P58-3, s03-P58-4, s03-P58-5, s03-P58-6, s03-P58-7 and s03-P58-8, the ticket, s03-X58c, and the scored day 55, day 56 and day 57 papers as children sit, without comment. Show the ring and hare drawings with no captions and read the winter card aloud. Conditions means what a place is like. Pairs talk for 1 minute, then the class says what it noticed. If a child says wolf, say a predator is an animal that hunts other animals for food.

**Ask.** Which animal did you notice first, and which nearly disappeared? Expect: oxen or wolves first; the second hare, hidden by the snow.

**Ask.** Could a rainforest tree frog stay here? Expect: probably not, with a reason about cold or snow. Do not explain. Any child may pass; no one is asked about home. Write each notice as a question on the question board (review from grade 1), 2 minutes.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Read aloud: pond, frog, snail, reed; forest, frog, fern, beetle. Alone, write [[s04-P59-1]]. **Ask.** Which kind lives in both? Expect: the frog. A miss goes to day 60; do not reteach today.

#### 24 min. First system drawing

4 minutes: on the board, draw a musk ox, low plants and a link line between them. Say the line goes from the animal to something the place gives it, here food: a musk ox is linked to low plants because it eats them. 13 minutes: pairs draw the musk ox, the wolf and the hare on the tundra with link lines, [[s04-P59-2]]; all 15 pairs work at once while you circulate. In the last 2 minutes each child writes one link, [[s04-P59-3]]. Accept any link the drawing supports: food, a place to hide, animals to hunt. If the wolf has no line, ask what it gets from the tundra. 3 minutes: each of the rotating six names one link, 30 seconds each. 4 minutes: sort the question board into Groups, Differences and Places.

#### 3 min. Exit E59

Read the meadow card twice. Alone, [[s04-X59]]; ask [[s04-X59b]] aloud. Look for: lines to the seeds, stream and bush; spoken: no, the turtle's water and plants are missing. Error F: yes, or no lines. Error G: nothing lives on the tundra. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P59-4]]. Say a temperature below zero in words, such as two below zero. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud and say it is supplied.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Drawings go in folders. The adult empties the jar; the helpers wash hands at two sinks.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the two scenes on the board, no captions. One projector: show the drawings; children draw on paper. No weather tools: read the dated adult record aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No partner drawing: the adult draws one animal and the child draws two. No weather tools: look out of the window and write the sky only. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The question board and the weather record.

- **5 min. Look.** Describe the two drawings aloud.
  **Say.** "Which animal did you notice first? Which nearly disappeared?" Expect: the oxen or wolves first; the hidden hare.
- **3 min. Pond and forest.** The child does [[s04-P59-1]].
- **17 min. Draw.** The child does [[s04-P59-2]] and [[s04-P59-3]].
  **Say.** "What does this animal get from the place?" Expect: low plants, snow to hide in, or animals to hunt.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X59]], answered alone. Then ask [[s04-X59b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: draw the musk ox and the hare only. Never cut the link lines or the exit. Longer: add a rainforest tree frog and say what it would need.

### Words and supports

Conditions: what a place is like. Predator: an animal that hunts other animals for food. Link: a line from an animal to what it gets. Frame: "A ___ is linked to ___ because ___." Home-language answers count. Enlarge the drawings; a partner may point instead of write.

### Tomorrow

Day 60 picks up the pond and forest miss, error F and error G in its 8-minute Return and repair, 2 minutes each. Error F reads: yes, turtles live anywhere. Error G reads: no, nothing can live on the tundra. Then the class reads four group cards.
