# Item G cards (day 62)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Twelve minutes, alone. This is scored after day 76 from the revised work.

## Meerkat card

A small group of meerkats looks for food. One is a lookout. The others dig. When the lookout calls, eight of ten trips end with food and a safe return. With no calls, four of ten do. After one call, the group runs to shelter.

## Penguin card

A much larger huddle of penguins stands in cold wind. Neighbors shelter one another. As the wind rises, birds change places.

## Your task

How can these groups help? Say food, staying safe and cold wind. Name the jobs. Which group is bigger? Mark your copy of the model.
