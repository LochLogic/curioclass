# Exit cards, days 59 to 63

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 59

Invented for practice. A pond turtle needs warm, open water and water plants. The winter tundra has ice, snow and no open water. Could the turtle live there? Say why.

## Day 60

Invented for practice. White pelicans swim in a half circle toward shore. They push fish into shallow water. How does the group help a pelican?

## Day 61

Invented for practice. A bar graph shows fish trips out of 10. One gull: 4. Two gulls: 6. Three gulls: 8. Six gulls: 3. Would you add more gulls to a group of three? Use the graph.

Picture: a bar graph, one bar for each group of gulls. The bars are 4, 6, 8 and 3 squares tall.

## Day 62

Invented for practice. A prairie dog stands on a mound and barks. The other prairie dogs stop, stand tall and look. Write: I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___.

## Day 63

Invented for practice. Two mice carry seeds home together. A lone owl finds food, a nest and safety all season on its own. What can grouping help? What shows not every animal needs a group?
