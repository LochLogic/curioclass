# Wolf ladder (day 61)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Six picture cards on one ladder, left to right. No numbers are printed anywhere.

## The cards

- Card 1: one wolf. A bar beside it, one step tall.
- Card 2: two wolves. A bar two steps tall.
- Card 3: three wolves. A bar three steps tall.
- Card 4: four wolves. A bar four steps tall.
- Card 5: five wolves. A bar still four steps tall.
- Card 6: six wolves. A bar still four steps tall.

## What the bar means

A taller bar means more hunts end in food. The bars rise up to four wolves, then stay level. Read it as a pattern, not as a count.
