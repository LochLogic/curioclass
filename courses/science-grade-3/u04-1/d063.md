# Day 63: Does the evidence hold up?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put each child's item G draft and look-for checklist on their desk, and the day 62 frame sentence beside it. Write the frame on the wall large enough to change. Print nothing extra. Clear desks of other papers.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment today and no weather record. Pencils stay down while a sentence is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected and desks cleared.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 62's pages, s04-P62-1, s04-P62-2, s04-P62-3, s04-P62-4, s04-P62-5 and s04-P62-6, tickets, s04-X62, and item G with its look-for checklist, 2 minutes. Each child marks one thing to fix, [[s04-P63-1]]. Then error B from the exit, 2 minutes: set the moose card beside the group cards and mark where each got food. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 23 min. Partner challenge

5 minutes: model a challenge with a teacher-written sentence. Say: "What did you see that shows that? Does your so say how it helps?" The answer to the first is a detail seen, not the claim said again.

8 minutes: pairs, all at once. A partner questions each child's day 62 sentence for 90 seconds, and the child answers and revises for 2 and a half minutes, 4 minutes a child. Children write [[s04-P63-2]] and [[s04-P63-3]] in a second color. Walk to the rotating six and hear each, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

6 minutes: revise the wall frame together. Keep: the claim says what the group does; the evidence says what was seen; the so says how it helps. Add: say can help or often, never always or all, unless a record shows it.

4 minutes: read three teacher-written revised sentences aloud, from the day 60 cards. No child's mistake is shown.
- I think the wolf pack helps wolves hunt, because I saw a pack catch more elk than one wolf, so hunting together helps.
- I think the school helps small fish stay safe, because I saw a hunting fish cannot pick just one, so it is harder to catch one.
- I think the colony shares its work, because I saw workers guard the nest and bring food while the queen lays eggs, so each bee has a job.
**Ask.** What did you see that shows that? Expect: a detail from the card.

#### 8 min. Item G revision

Alone, in a second color, from the checklist and its question stamp: [[s04-P63-4]]. Keep the first writing visible. Add or change only what a record on the cards supports. Do not give an answer; point to a card line. The revised paper is collected and marked as repair evidence after day 76.

#### 7 min. Exit E63

1 minute directions: read the card aloud twice. 5 minutes: alone, [[s04-X63]]. Ask [[s04-X63b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. 1 minute: collect. Look for: grouping can help the mice get food; the owl did well alone, so not every animal needs a group. Error B says every animal needs a group. Error C says the mice always get enough, so groups never fail. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the frame on the board; children use notebook paper. No second color: use a pen or a different line.

### At home

**Swap.** No partner: the adult asks the question. No second color: use a pen. No item G draft: the child writes the food link again.

**Skip.** The wall frame and the clean-up.

- **4 min. Feedback.** The child reads the checklist and does [[s04-P63-1]].
- **9 min. Question it.** The child does [[s04-P63-2]] and [[s04-P63-3]].
  **Say.** "What did you see that shows that?" Expect: a detail from the card or record, not the claim again.
- **10 min. Item G.** The child does [[s04-P63-4]], with no help.
- **7 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X63]], answered alone. Then ask [[s04-X63b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: question one sentence and revise item G. Never cut the partner's question, the item G revision or the exit. Longer: write one question that would test a claim a record cannot show.

### Words and supports

Evidence, reason, revise, feedback, can help. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Home-language answers count. Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 64 opens section 4.2 with a retrieval and an 8-minute Return and repair, 2 minutes for error B from today's exit: every animal needs a group. Error C reads: the mice always get enough, so groups never fail. The class asks how differences within one kind could change survival on the tundra. The revised item G stays in the folder, marked as repair evidence after day 76.
