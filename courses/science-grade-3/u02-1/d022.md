# Day 22: Questions and the arrow model

## Teacher page

### Before class

Lay out the ten question cards and card N in sets for pairs. Post the day 19 and day 20 class tables side by side on the board. Keep each child's day 17 pages in the folder, ready to hand out.

### Safety and set-up

No magnets today. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. After the pots, the mealworm box and the rain jar, the two observers wash hands. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P22-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 21's exit tickets, s02-X21. Error B, 2 minutes: read the day 21 compare tables side by side; the clip leaned and the foil did not, so say what that shows. Error D, 2 minutes: write a proposed test with two changes on the board, and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The day's two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box on the living record; the rest look from their seats.

#### 5 min. Sort questions

Pairs sort the ten question cards into testable and not yet, [[s02-P22-2]]. A card that cannot be tested, or needs a design, goes under not yet. For each testable card, say the one change. A wonder that fits none stays on the question board.

#### 10 min. Revise the model

Show the gap and arrow cards. Hand out the day 17 pages. Children redraw the clip and the magnet with the arrow, [[s02-P22-3]]. Use the class tables: day 19 made the arrow longer for the closer gap; day 20 pointed it away when red faced red. **Ask.** What changed since day 17? Expect: the arrow crosses the gap, its length follows our readings, and its direction depends on the ends. Children place the day 17 drawing beside the new one. Nobody is the example of a miss. If a child draws the arrow on the magnet, trace it back to the clip.

#### 8 min. A new card

Pairs read card N and write one testable question, [[s02-P22-4]], with what they change, keep the same and watch. Hear a rotating six pairs, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes in all. **Ask.** What do you change? What stays the same? Expect: the number of sheets; the magnet and the shelf; whether it stays on. The rest swap questions and cross out every change but one.

#### 6 min. Exit

Alone, the section exit ticket, [[s02-X22]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X22b]] and [[s02-X22c]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Look for: (a) change the gap, keep the two magnets and the ends facing, watch the lean; (b) change which end faces, keep the gap, watch toward or away; (c) none was seen at 6 cm, the pull may be too small to see. Error D: "change the gap and turn one magnet". Error A: "no force, it is too far". Note letters on the record sheet. A blank is an incomplete part, not a letter.

#### 5 min. Look back

Read the question board aloud. Name which wonders were tested and which were not yet. Say that unit 3 starts from the living records. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the ten questions on the board and sort on paper. One child can sort and draw alone.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write ten questions about magnets and sort them. No printer: draw the table on paper.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records, the pair work and the class model.

- **8 min. Sort.** Write four questions about magnets. Do [[s02-P22-2]].
  **Say.** "Which can we test? Which not yet?" Expect: "One we can test by changing one thing. One needs a design."
- **8 min. The arrow.** Draw a clip and a magnet with a gap. Do [[s02-P22-3]].
- **9 min. A new question.** Think of paper between a magnet and a steel door. Do [[s02-P22-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X22]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X22b]] and [[s02-X22c]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort six cards. Never cut the one-change rule, the arrow redraw or the exit. Longer: write a second question from card N.

### Words and supports

Testable, not yet, change, keep the same, watch, arrow, gap. Frames: "I would change ___." "In our trials ___." A partner may write the question as the child says it.

### Tomorrow

Day 23 opens, in section 2.2, with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 22's exit. Error A ("no force, it is too far") lays a ruler across the gap and says what none seen means. Error D ("change the gap and turn one magnet") crosses out every change but one. Then the class asks what static does at different distances.
