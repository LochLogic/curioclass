# Day 17: A pull across a gap

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a clip thread to each group's table edge; count clips and magnets into trays. Check allergies and the weather. Draw the parcel with two equal arrows on the board. Open the question board.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on before a clip moves. Count magnets and clips out and back. Fingers clear of pinches; magnets stay away from mouths, phones and tablets. Static: comb and cloth only. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. Clean-up is 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark, the rain jar, the direction card and the sky. Children write [[s02-P17-1]]. Last minute: hand washing.

#### 3 min. Retrieve unit 1

Hand back day 16's sheet, s01-X16c. The parcel on the board is invented for practice. **Ask.** Why does it stay still? Expect: the two equal arrows cancel. A small group that missed the box question holds a box still between two hands for 1 minute; the rest redraw both arrows on the back of their sheet and write one line.

#### 6 min. Hook

Demonstration. A clip on its thread leans toward a magnet held 3 cm away and hangs in the air, tethered. Then rub the comb on the wool cloth, 20 strokes, and lift tissue bits across a gap of about 2 cm. If the air is damp and nothing lifts, try a dry cloth once, then describe a record and say it is invented for practice. Say nothing about why.

#### 4 min. Draw first

Each child draws [[s02-P17-2]] on the drawing sheet before any word is taught. Nothing is marked wrong. Sheets stay in the folder for day 22.

#### 4 min. Talk: listen, build on, ask

Pairs swap drawings. The partner listens, then builds on ("I agree, and I think ___") or asks one question ("What makes you think ___?"). **Ask.** What do you think is making the clip lean? Hear four voices from the rotating six. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Gap, attract, repel, arrow

Show card 2. Teach the gap (review): the space with nothing between two things. Attract is a pull toward across a gap; repel is a push away across a gap. Each magnet has a red end and a blue end. Swing one hung magnet away with the red end of a second: a push across a gap. [[steps:s02-K17-1]] Show card 3. [[steps:s02-K17-2]] Children fill in the lines on their page.

#### 7 min. Turns at the clip

Six groups of five, goggles on, tethers secured. Each child takes one turn of about 80 seconds: bring the magnet slowly to the clip until it leans, hold, and draw [[s02-P17-3]] on the drawing sheet. Five turns of 80 seconds fit under 7 minutes. Watch for an arrow that starts on the magnet.

#### 3 min. Question board

Each pair gives one wonder, [[s02-P17-4]]. Post them unedited.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X17]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X17b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) an arrow that starts on the bolt and points across the gap to the magnet; (b) no, the bolt moved with a gap between. Error A: "yes, it has to touch". Note letters.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count magnets and clips back; wipe goggles; collect comb, cloth and tissue; take tethers off. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the arrow lines on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No magnet or clip: a fridge magnet and a steel paper clip on a thread. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. No printer: draw on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the weather, the talk routine and the counting.

- **6 min. Draw first.** Hang a clip on a thread and bring a magnet near. Do [[s02-P17-2]].
  **Say.** "What do you think makes the clip lean?" Expect: "Any idea is fine, such as the magnet pulls it."
- **10 min. The gap.** Look at the gap between the clip and the magnet. Do [[s02-K17-1]].
  **Say.** "Does the magnet touch the clip? Where does the arrow start?" Expect: "No. It starts on the clip and points to the magnet."
- **9 min. Your turn.** Do [[s02-P17-3]] and [[s02-P17-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X17]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X17b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one wonder a pair. Never cut the drawing first, the gap or the exit. Longer: draw the arrow for a magnet swung away by a second magnet.

### Words and supports

Gap, magnet, attract, repel, arrow, red end, blue end, lean. Frames: "I agree, and I think ___." "What makes you think ___?" A partner may move the magnet; a drawing may be dictated.

### Tomorrow

Day 18 opens with the weather record, then 2 minutes of Return and repair. Error A ("yes, the magnet has to touch the bolt") sits beside the clip leaning with a ruler laid across the gap. An arrow that starts on the magnet is no letter: trace the arrow along the ruler. Then the class turns today's wonders into questions it can test.
