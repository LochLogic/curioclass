# Day 21: What does a magnet pull?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a clip thread to each group's table edge, with lean strips under the clip and a distance strip beside it. Count two magnets, five clips and one foil square into each tray. Number the compare table's three questions on the board. Assign each group one question, two groups to each.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on every child. Magnets counted out and back; fingers clear; magnets away from mouths, phones and tablets. Flying clips: eye protection stays on. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. After the pots, the mealworm box and the rain jar, the two observers wash hands. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P21-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 20's exit tickets, s02-X20. Error C, 2 minutes: turn a magnet over near a hung clip, then near a hung magnet; say what differs.

#### 2 min. Living records

The day's two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box on the living record; the rest look from their seats.

#### 3 min. Choose the question

Each group reads its numbered question on the compare table. Nothing new is taught.

#### 2 min. Plan table

Each child writes [[s02-P21-2]]: what we change, keep the same and watch.

#### 2 min. Safety and set-up

Fit goggles. Count two magnets out. Check the lean strip and the distance strip. Hold every try at the same gap.

#### 16 min. Investigate

Six groups of five. Do the group question: three tries of each condition, A then B, about 40 seconds a try; six tries shared by five children (four make one, one makes two) take under 5 minutes. Everyone writes every try in [[s02-P21-3]], in whole centimeters. Then each child holds a magnet near the steel clip and then the foil square, 30 seconds each, and writes what leaned. A lean too small to see is written "none seen", never no force. An unclear try is repeated once and both are kept. If no lean can be read, use another group's table and say so; as a last resort use a record said aloud to be invented. Nobody is the example of a miss.

#### 5 min. Class table

Each group posts its three tries of A and B. Hear a rotating six, one child per group, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes in all: a try and [[s02-P21-4]]. **Ask.** What did you see? What can we not say yet? Expect: the clip leaned and the foil did not; two magnets are not promised to pull twice as hard. Put two groups' tables side by side.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X21]]; read both cards aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X21b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) no, only some metals are pulled; (b) no, two things changed, the object and the number of magnets. Error B: "yes, all metals are pulled". Error D: "yes, two pull more". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count magnets (2 a group), clips (5 a group) and foil squares back; wipe goggles; collect strips. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the compare table on the board. One clip, one fridge magnet and one foil square do every try in turn for one child.

### At home

**Swap.** No foil: a clean, empty aluminum drink can, held by an adult. A clip with a fridge magnet is one magnet; a second stuck on is two. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, keep your face back and wear glasses or sunglasses while the clip swings, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. Keep coins from mouths. No printer: draw the table on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the weather, the living records and the class table.

- **5 min. Plan.** Do [[s02-P21-2]].
- **15 min. Try it.** Hold a magnet at the same gap from a clip, then from a foil square. Do [[s02-P21-3]].
  **Say.** "What leaned? What can we not say yet?" Expect: "The clip leaned and the foil did not. All metals are pulled we cannot say."
- **5 min. What we saw.** Do [[s02-P21-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X21]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X21b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two tries of each condition. Never cut one change at a time, the foil check or the exit. Longer: test a second kind of metal or a stack of three.

### Words and supports

Steel, aluminum, metal, stacked, one change. Frames: "In our trials ___." "We cannot say ___ yet." A partner may hold the magnet and the reader may call the lean aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 22 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 21's exit. Error B ("yes, all metals are pulled") holds the same magnet end near a clip and a foil square, one then the other. Error D ("yes, two pull more") crosses out every change but one. Then the class sorts testable questions and revises the arrows.
