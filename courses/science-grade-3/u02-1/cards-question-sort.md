# Question cards, days 18 and 22

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain cards, one question each, printed in large letters.

## Set A: for pairs to sort (day 18 into four piles, day 22 into testable and not yet)

1. Does a bigger magnet pull from farther? (materials)
2. Does a closer gap make a bigger lean? (distance)
3. What does a magnet do to a plastic spoon? (materials)
4. Which way does the clip lean if the magnet is turned? (turning)
5. Why is a magnet magic? (none)
6. Does it pull through a sheet of card? (materials)
7. Can a magnet help hold a door shut? (uses)
8. Does a long gap show no pull at all? (distance)
9. Does turning the red end away change the push? (turning)
10. How could a magnet help a crane? (uses)

For day 22, cards 5 and 10 sort to "not yet": one cannot be tested, one needs a design.

## Card N: a new card for day 22 (unfamiliar)

A small magnet is stuck to a steel shelf edge. A single sheet of paper is between them. A second drawing shows two sheets between them. Pairs write one testable question and say what they change, keep and watch.
