# Day 18: Questions we can test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Alone, fill the pots with soil, set the wicks, reservoir and lamp, and sort the seeds. Check allergies the day before. Lay out the question cards in tens, the question strips and a ruler for each group. Keep the day 17 wonders on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Only the planter touches soil; everyone washes hands after soil and living things. Mealworms are looked at, never handled, and never released. The lamp is adult-only, and water stays away from it. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. Set-up clean-up is 3 of its 15 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P18-1]]. The last minute is hand washing, review from grade 2.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 17's exit tickets, s02-X17. Error A, 2 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap on the clip and trace the arrow along it; nothing touches the clip but the thread.

#### 15 min. Set-up for unit 3: living records

Two children from the rotating six sow the two fast-cycling flowering pots at the teacher's table. Each group sows two radish pots of six seeds: one crowded, about 1 cm apart, and one spaced, about 4 cm apart, measured with the ruler. Roles: planter, the only one who touches soil; spacer; labeler; date recorder; mealworm watcher. Time: 2 minutes for roles, 6 for sowing, 3 for labels and [[s02-P18-4]], 3 for clean-up and hand washing, 1 spare. The six planters wash in turn at two sinks, three turns. The groups visit the mealworm box in turn, 30 seconds each. Care stays equal for every pot. Pages stay in the folder.

#### 6 min. Model a question

Turn a day 17 wonder into a question we can test. [[steps:s02-K18-1]] **Ask.** What do we change? What do we keep the same? What do we watch? Expect: the gap; the magnet, the clip and the end facing; the lean. Set aside a question with no change to make.

#### 4 min. Sort the board

Pairs sort the ten question cards into four piles, [[s02-P18-2]]: distance, materials, turning, uses. A wonder that fits none stays on the board.

#### 7 min. Choose a change

Each child writes the gap question on the strip, [[s02-P18-3]]. Then the group agrees one strip: three gaps from 1 to 10 cm and what stays the same. Watch for two changes on one strip.

#### 3 min. Hear the groups

One child per group reads the strip aloud, 30 seconds each; six groups fit in 3 minutes.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X18]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X18b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) change the string; keep the nut, the magnet and the gap; watch the lean; (b) no, two things changed. Error D: "yes, moving the magnet closer shows more". "Watch the string" is no letter. Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No pots: describe the set-up on the board and sow one pot at the front. No printer: write the strip on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No question strip: draw the three lines on paper. No magnet: a fridge magnet and a clip on a thread. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets.

**Skip.** The planting, the weather, the card sort and the groups.

- **8 min. A testable question.** Hang a clip on a thread and bring a magnet near. Do [[s02-K18-1]].
  **Say.** "What do we change, keep the same and watch?" Expect: "Change the gap; keep the magnet and the clip; watch the lean."
- **12 min. Your strip.** Do [[s02-P18-3]]. Pick three gaps from 1 to 10 cm.
  **Say.** "Why change only one thing?" Expect: "Then we know which one caused it."
- **8 min. Wonders.** Write two more wonders about the magnet and ask which can be tested.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X18]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X18b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the groups read their strips to the teacher in pairs. Never cut the model question, the one-change rule or the exit. Longer: write a second strip for a different change.

### Words and supports

Question, test, change, keep the same, watch. Frames: "I wonder if ___ when we change ___." A partner may write the strip as the child says it.

### Tomorrow

Day 19 opens with a 2-minute measuring warm-up, then Return and repair, 2 minutes. Error D ("yes, moving the magnet closer shows more") crosses out every change but one in a proposed test. Then the groups ask the gap question, plan three gaps and test them.
