# Day 20: Does turning change the pull?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang one magnet on the clip thread from each group's table edge, red end out, with the lean strips and distance strip beside it. Count two magnets and five clips into each tray. Draw the class table on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on every child. Magnets counted out and back; fingers clear of the gap between two magnets; magnets away from mouths, phones and tablets. Flying clips and swinging magnets: eye protection stays on. After the pots and the mealworm box, the two observers wash hands. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 19's exit tickets, s02-X19. Error A, 2 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap and trace the arrow along it; say what none seen means. Error D, 2 minutes: write a proposed test with two changes on the board, and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The day's two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box on the living record; the rest look from their seats.

#### 4 min. Ask the question

Groups read their question strip and rewrite it for turning: one change, what stays the same and something to watch. Nothing new is taught.

#### 3 min. Plan table

Each child writes [[s02-P20-1]], the plan table from unit 1.

#### 2 min. Safety and set-up

Fit goggles. Count two magnets out. Hold the second magnet 4 cm from the hung one, using the distance strip.

#### 12 min. Investigate

Six groups of five. Hold the red end of the held magnet 4 cm from the hung magnet's red end, then its blue end. Say what the hung magnet does, toward or away, and read its move in whole centimeters. Six tries of about 40 seconds shared by five children (four make one, one makes two) take under 5 minutes; everyone writes every try in [[s02-P20-2]]. Then each child holds the red end, then the blue end, near a hung clip and writes [[s02-P20-3]]. A move too small to see is written "none seen". An unclear try is repeated once and both are kept. If no move can be read, use another group's table and say so; as a last resort use a record said aloud to be invented. Nobody is the example of a miss.

#### 5 min. Class table

Each group posts its tries. Hear a rotating six, one child per group, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes in all: a try and [[s02-P20-5]]. **Ask.** What changed when we turned the magnet? Expect: red toward red, they pushed apart; the clip leaned either way. Put two groups' tables side by side.

#### 5 min. Revise arrows

The arrow on the hung magnet points toward the held magnet when red faces blue, and away when red faces red. The clip's arrow points toward either end. Children draw [[s02-P20-4]].

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X20]]; read both cards aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X20b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) the blue end, because unlike ends pull; (b) no, steel is pulled by either end. Error C: "the blue end pushes it" or "the nail is pushed away". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count magnets (2 a group) and clips (5 a group) back; wipe goggles; collect strips. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the turn table on the board. Two fridge magnets and one clip do both turns in turn for one child.

### At home

**Swap.** No second magnet: use one magnet and a steel clip on a thread. A string loop can be a strip of paper. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, keep your face back and wear glasses or sunglasses while the clip swings, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. No printer: draw the table on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the living records and the class table.

- **5 min. Plan.** Do [[s02-P20-1]].
- **15 min. Turn it.** Hold one magnet near a clip on a thread, red end then blue end. Do [[s02-P20-3]]. With two magnets, turn one over and watch. Do [[s02-P20-2]].
  **Say.** "What happens when red faces red? What does the clip do with either end?" Expect: "They push apart. The clip leans toward both."
- **5 min. Arrows.** Do [[s02-P20-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X20]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X20b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three tries each way. Never cut one change, the clip turn or the exit. Longer: test a second gap and add it to the table.

### Words and supports

Toward, away, push, pull, red end, blue end, like ends. Frames: "In our trials ___." "When ___ faced ___, it ___." A partner may hold the magnet and the reader may call the move aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 21 opens with 2 minutes of Return and repair. Error C ("the blue end pushes it" or "pushed away") turns a magnet over near a hung clip, then near a hung magnet, and says what differs. Then the class tests which things a magnet pulls and what two magnets do.
