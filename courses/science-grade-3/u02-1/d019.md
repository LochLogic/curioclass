# Day 19: Does the gap change the pull?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a clip thread to each group's table edge, with a lean strip under the clip and a distance strip beside it. Count two magnets and five clips into each tray. Draw the class table on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Goggles on every child. Magnets counted out and back; fingers clear; magnets away from mouths, phones and tablets. Flying clips: eye protection stays on. After the pots and the mealworm box, the two observers wash hands. Mealworms are never handled. Clean-up is 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Measuring warm-up

Pairs read ruler pictures 1 and 2 and write [[s02-P19-1]] on the page. A miss gets zero lined up at the gap's start, and ruler picture 3 as one more strip.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 18's exit tickets, s02-X18. Error D, 2 minutes: write a proposed test with two changes on the board, and cross out every change but one.

#### 2 min. Living records

The day's two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box on the living record; the rest look from their seats.

#### 4 min. Ask the question

Groups read their question strip. Each needs one change, what stays the same and something to watch. Nothing new is taught.

#### 3 min. Plan table

Each child writes [[s02-P19-2]]: three gaps, three tries at each. It is the plan table from unit 1.

#### 2 min. Safety and set-up

Fit goggles. Count two magnets out. Check the lean strip under the clip and the distance strip beside it.

#### 13 min. Investigate

Six groups of five. Hold the red end at the first gap for 5 seconds, then read how far the clip tip moved toward the magnet, in whole centimeters. Nine tries of about 40 seconds shared by five children (four make two, one makes one) take about 6 minutes; everyone writes every reading in [[s02-P19-3]]. A lean too small to see is written "none seen", never no force. An unclear try is repeated once and both are kept. If no lean can be read, use another group's table and say so; as a last resort use a record said aloud to be invented. Nobody is the example of a miss.

#### 5 min. Class table

Each group posts its readings by gap. Hear a rotating six, one child per group, 40 seconds each, 4 minutes in all: a reading and [[s02-P19-4]]. **Ask.** What did you see change when the gap changed? Expect: in our trials, the closer gap leaned more. Put two groups' tables side by side.

#### 4 min. Revise arrows

The arrow across the gap is longer for the closer gap. It shows how strong the pull was in our trials, not how far the clip moved. Children draw [[s02-P19-5]].

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X19]]; read both cards aloud and wait. Ask [[s02-X19b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) no, the magnet changed too, so two things changed; (b) none was seen, the pull may be too small to see, closer gave a bigger lean. Error D: "yes, the gap caused the change". Error A: "there is no pull at that gap". Note letters.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count magnets (2 a group) and clips (5 a group) back; wipe goggles; collect strips. Pages stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the gap table on the board. One clip and one fridge magnet do three gaps in turn for one child.

### At home

**Swap.** No lean strip: tape a ruler beside the clip. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, keep your face back and wear glasses or sunglasses while the clip swings, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets. No printer: draw the table on paper.

**Skip.** The groups, the living records and the class table.

- **4 min. Read a gap.** Do [[s02-P19-1]].
- **6 min. Plan.** Do [[s02-P19-2]].
- **15 min. Try three gaps.** Hold a magnet at each gap for 5 seconds. Do [[s02-P19-3]].
  **Say.** "What happens to the lean as the gap gets smaller? What do you write if you see none?" Expect: "In our trials it got bigger. None seen, not no pull."
- **5 min. Arrows.** Do [[s02-P19-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X19]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X19b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two gaps, two tries each. Never cut one change, the none seen note or the exit. Longer: test a fourth gap and add it to the table.

### Words and supports

Gap, lean, whole centimeters, none seen, closer, farther. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___." "In our trials ___." A partner may hold the magnet and the reader may call the lean aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 20 opens with 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. Error A ("there is no pull at that gap") lays a ruler across the gap and traces the arrow along it, and says what none seen means. Error D ("yes, the gap caused the change") crosses out every change but one. Then the class tests what turning one magnet does.
