# Exit cards, days 17 to 22

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 17

A steel bolt lies on a smooth tray. A magnet is held close, with a small gap marked between them. The bolt has slid toward the magnet. A blank box below is for the arrow.

## Day 18

A nut hangs on string beside a magnet, with a ruler behind. Pat wants to know if a different kind of string changes the lean. Pat also moves the magnet closer. A strip below has three empty lines: change, keep the same, watch.

## Day 19

Card 1: a magnet at 2 cm leans a hanging screw 3 cm. A second magnet at 6 cm leans it 1 cm. Card 2: a screw hangs at a far gap and does not move. Pat says there is no pull.

## Day 20

Card 1: a magnet hanging on a string, its red end close to a held magnet whose end is left uncolored. Card 2: a steel nail on a tray with a magnet's red end held near it, the nail slid toward the magnet; Pat's claim in a speech balloon: the blue end will push the nail away.

## Day 21

Card 1: a magnet pulling a steel bolt, with a silver aluminum tray beside it. Card 2: one magnet on a clip, then two stacked magnets on a steel bolt, with Pat's claim that two pull more.

## Day 22

Two flat round magnets. One hangs on a loop of thread. The other is held near it at 2 cm, at 6 cm, and then turned over. Three empty strips below: gap test, turning test, and a note that at 6 cm no lean is seen.
