# Day 95: Check, two outcrops on a dry plain

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the fresh card and the student page for each child, and keep the score sheet, unit 5, in your folder. Put each child's day 94 pages in the folder; nothing goes back today.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. Today's fresh work is the scored work for this check, s05-E1, s05-E2, s05-E3, s05-E4, s05-E5 and s05-E6, scored after class on the score sheet, outside lesson minutes. Each child's revised product F, handed in on day 93, is marked beside it as repair evidence, not the score. Day 94's pages stay held. Read the task aloud: annotate the model with the kinds, sizes, places, order and limits. Read the fresh card aloud twice; it is invented for practice.

#### 32 min. Fresh check

Alone on paper, [[s05-P95-1]], [[s05-P95-2]], [[s05-P95-3]] and [[s05-P95-4]]. Give no help and no feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: shells at the west and tracks at the east favor a shoreline with land nearby, and moved shells are still possible; the trunk piece is younger than the tracks; no order for shells and tracks at one level; track length is not body length; the trunk piece is a minimum; this shell form is extinct and other shellfish are not; a week of weather is a short record. Note error A (the present taken as the past), error B (none here, so extinct), error C (deeper always older) and error F (size alone proves a hot place) for day 97. This fresh work is the score.

#### 6 min. Independent checking

Children reread and tick each statement a record backs, [[s05-P95-5]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the fresh card on the board and read it aloud; children answer on notebook paper and draw the model by hand. One projector: show the card; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: the adult copies it onto paper. No scored page: skip the last part.

**Skip.** The scoring and the clean-up.

- **36 min. Assessment.** Read the fresh card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s05-P95-1]], [[s05-P95-2]], [[s05-P95-3]] and [[s05-P95-4]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What do the shells and the tracks tell you about the place?" Expect: water and land were close together.
- **4 min. Checking.** The child does [[s05-P95-5]].
- **4 min. What is scored.** Read [[s05-E1]], [[s05-E2]], [[s05-E3]], [[s05-E4]], [[s05-E5]] and [[s05-E6]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the claim and the order. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Outcrop, fossil, layer, undisturbed, extinct, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___. The evidence cannot show ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply claims, order or limits.

### Tomorrow

Day 96 is the second check, on judging a response to a change, with a fresh card and no feedback. Day 95's checklist comes back on day 97, in the group for the component each child missed; the scored pages come back on day 98.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
