# Item S fresh card (day 96, the scored check)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A cold spell lowers a garden's temperature.

## The records

- Some flowering plants stop flowering.
- Moths move away.
- Exposed caterpillars die.
- Cold-tolerant beetles arrive and reproduce.

## The goal card

Keep at least 4 flowering plants and 9 pollinator visits, within 10 tokens. Structures stand at most 50 centimeters tall.

## Two model trials

- Tall shelter: 8 tokens, 6 plants, 12 visits. It stands 70 centimeters tall.
- Low cover: 10 tokens, 4 plants, 9 visits. It stands 40 centimeters tall.

## Your task

Make a merit claim. Draw system arrows. Write one limit on the evidence.
