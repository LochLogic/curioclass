# Day 94: What each record can and cannot show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print two comparison tables and the fresh pair, day 94, for each pair, and a system revision sheet for each child. Keep the product S card on your desk. Pin the one-week log strip beside the wall model. Set the weather tools.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 93's s05-P93-1, s05-P93-2, s05-P93-3, s05-P93-4, s05-P93-5 and the ticket s05-X93. If many made error A (it is cold in the log, so it always was), lay the one-week strip beside the rooted layers: a week is a short record, and rooted trunks tell a longer story. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P94-1]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record, said to be supplied.

#### 8 min. Product S revision

Hand back day 91's product S, s05-P91-3, with its written feedback and a fresh system revision sheet. Alone, in a second colour: change one thing the feedback points to, keep the first drawing, [[s05-P94-2]]. Give no help beyond reading a line again. Say nothing of points. Collect the sheets; they are scored after day 96.

#### 20 min. Comparison

6 minutes: pairs fill a table with a fossil record and a modern record, [[s05-P94-3]]: what was seen, who or what, what it supports, what it cannot show. The reminders are day 82's trunks in rock and day 87's highway; reminders only. **Ask.** What can this record tell us that the other cannot? Expect: the fossil record tells what lived there long ago; the modern record tells what living things do after a change and what a response costs. 8 minutes: read the fresh pair once, invented for practice: a quarry with reed prints low and grass seed prints high, and a stream bank fenced from cattle. Pairs fill a second table, [[s05-P94-4]]. Unscored. 6 minutes: six pairs each give one row, 1 minute each, one at a time; choose by lot; the record names who. Look for: the quarry supports marsh, then dry ground, with no date; the fence meets the goal for these spots. Error D says the fence helps every animal.

#### 7 min. Exit E94

1 minute: read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. 5 minutes: alone, [[s05-X94]]. 1 minute: collect. Look for: the ridges show sea at one place and land trees at the other, with no order; shade supports the pond life for these goals; neither gives a date. Error A says the ridges are as they are today, so they always were. Error D says all the pond animals will do well. The letters wait for day 97.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the table on the board and read the cards aloud; children answer on notebook paper. No weather tools: read the dated adult record. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed table: draw four rows and two columns. No second colour: use a pen. No thermometer: read a dated weather record, said to be supplied.

**Skip.** The pair rows and the helpers.

- **4 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P94-1]].
- **8 min. Revise.** Give back product S. The child does [[s05-P94-2]] alone.
- **8 min. Two records.** The child does [[s05-P94-3]].
  **Say.** "What can the rock tell us that the highway cannot?" Expect: what lived there long ago.
- **8 min. Fresh pair.** Read the cards once. The child does [[s05-P94-4]].
  **Say.** "What can neither record show?" Expect: a date, or that every animal benefits.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X94]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill the fossil column only for the fresh pair. Never cut the revision or the exit. Longer: write a third record that would change the table.

### Words and supports

Fossil record, modern record, supports, cannot show, date, trial. Frame: "The record shows ___. It supports ___. It cannot show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; a scribe may write for a child.

### Tomorrow

Day 95 is a check: each child works alone on a fresh card, with no help and no feedback, and day 94's pages stay in the folder. Error A (the present taken as the past) and error D (all organisms alike) from day 94's exit are not repaired on day 95; they wait for day 97, where s05-X94 is handed back.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
