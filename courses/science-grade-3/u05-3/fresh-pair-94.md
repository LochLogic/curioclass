# Fresh pair (day 94, unscored)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Card 1: a quarry

A quarry wall has two layers. The low layer has prints of reed stems, 30 centimetres long. A guide says reeds grow in marsh. The high layer has prints of grass seeds. A guide says these grasses grow on dry ground. The layers are undisturbed.

## Card 2: a modern record

Cattle are fenced off a stream bank. Grass grows back along the water. Insects that nest in the bank return. A fence trial costs 3 tokens and keeps 6 of 8 bank spots green. The goal is at least 4 of 8 spots, within 4 tokens.
