# Repair sheets, day 97

Four six-minute groups. The rest work in pairs from the written repair.

## Group 1: past-environment evidence (A, F)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: the place is dry now, so it always was. A fossil that needs water shows water was there. Someone might say: the leaf is big, so it was hot. Size plus a guide card says more.

## Group 2: extinction and time (B, C)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: none live here, so extinct. Extinct means none alive anywhere. Someone might say: the bottom layer is always older. Only when the layers are undisturbed.

## Group 3: organism responses (D)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: the change hurt every living thing the same. Each follows its own need.

## Group 4: merit against limits (E)

Wrong beside right. Someone might say: the biggest number wins. Check the limit box first.

Each child states one revision, 45 seconds each. A larger group has seven speak while the rest write.
