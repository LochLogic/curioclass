# Post-fire cards (day 92)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Fire is part of many places. The question is whether food comes back. Sharing is voluntary. No one is asked about a fire they know.

## Drawing

A hillside one season after a fire. Black trunks stand. Green shoots show in the ash. Birds sit on a trunk. Ten marked spots are drawn on the ground.

## The goal card

Goal: at least 6 of 10 spots with seed plants, within 6 tokens.

## Three matched plots

- Left alone: 7 of 10 spots, 0 tokens.
- Replanted: 9 of 10 spots, 4 tokens.
- Replanted and fenced: 10 of 10 spots, 8 tokens.

Pairs mark each plot against the goal. Left alone meets both. Replanted meets both. Replanted and fenced breaks the token limit.

## Argument

Each child writes a claim, evidence and reason. The record cannot say replanting always helps.
