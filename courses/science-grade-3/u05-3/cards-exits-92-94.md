# Exit cards, days 92 to 94

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 92

Invented for practice. After a flood, three meadow plots. Goal: at least 4 clover patches back, within 4 tokens. Plot P is left alone: 6 patches, 0 tokens. Plot Q is reseeded: 5 patches, 3 tokens. Plot R is reseeded and watered: 9 patches, 7 tokens. Which plots have merit? Does the record show reseeding always helps?

## Day 93

Invented for practice. A sandy hill has prints of fan-shaped leaves in a rock layer. A guide says these leaves belong to warm, wet forests. The class log for one week shows cold days. Aman says the log shows this place is cold, so it never had a forest. Is he right?

## Day 94

Invented for practice. Record 1: a map. Coral fossils are at one ridge. Rooted tree remains are at another ridge. No layers match, so no order is known. A guide says this coral lived in seawater. Record 2: a pond lost its shade, and the water warmed. A shade trial costs 4 tokens. It keeps 3 water plants and 2 insect larvae. The goals are 3 plants and 2 larvae, within 4 tokens. What does each record support about change? What can neither establish?
