# Day 97: Respond to the two checks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have every paper back with its written feedback and score. Print the repair sheets for each child and the transfer cards for each pair. Set four group spots and write four names on the board: past-environment evidence, extinction and time, organism responses, merit against limits. Mark a dated wind row in the class weather log.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment today. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers filed in each child's folder.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return components

Hand back day 95's assessment and day 96's assessment with written feedback, fresh work and scores: day 95's s05-P95-1, s05-P95-2, s05-P95-3, s05-P95-4 and s05-P95-5, 2 minutes; day 96's s05-P96-1, s05-P96-2, s05-P96-3, s05-P96-4 and s05-P96-5, 2 minutes. The exit tickets go back too: day 94's s05-X94, day 85's s05-X85c and day 92's s05-X92, 1 minute. Scored from the work you handed in. Each child marks the one component to work on, [[s05-P97-1]]. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 24 min. Four six-minute groups

This is the one response. Each child joins the group for the component marked. The teacher takes the groups in turn, 6 minutes each; the rest work in pairs from the written repair on the repair sheets. A group of 7 or fewer says one revision each, 45 seconds a child; a larger group has seven speak while the rest write, and the record names who spoke.
- Past-environment evidence, [[s05-P97-2]]. Errors A and F: lay s05-X94 and a right answer side by side; a water fossil shows water was here; size alone does not show the climate.
- Extinction and time, [[s05-P97-3]]. Errors B and C: lay s05-X85c beside the two stacks; extinct means none alive anywhere; a lower layer is older only when the layers are undisturbed.
- Organism responses, [[s05-P97-4]]. Error D: lay s05-X94 beside one record and trace each kind to its own need.
- Merit against limits, [[s05-P97-5]]. Error E: lay s05-X92 beside the limit box; read the limit first.

Any child may pass in public talk. **Ask.** What did you change, and what in the record said so? Expect: "I changed ___, because the record says ___."

#### 10 min. Fresh transfer

1 minute: read the two questions aloud, invented for practice. 8 minutes: alone, [[s05-X97c]]. Ask [[s05-X97d]] and [[s05-X97e]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. 1 minute: collect. Unscored. Look for: the low layer is older, still water then dry ground, and one frosty week is a short record; the brush fence has merit, and the concrete channel breaks the token limit.

#### 3 min. Retrieval for unit 6

Point to the marked row. Children find one dated wind record in the class weather log and say its direction, then say which line on transfer card 2 is the goal and which the limit, [[s05-P97-6]]. A miss is noted; day 98 hands the child a highlighted wind row, and goal and limit cards come before day 109.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers filed in each child's folder; cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the repair lines and the transfer cards on the board; answer on notebook paper. No groups: take each marked component with the whole class in turn. One projector: show the cards.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the adult takes the child's marked component alone. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The groups and the clean-up.

- **6 min. Look back.** Read the feedback together. The child does [[s05-P97-1]].
- **16 min. Revise.** Work the marked component only: [[s05-P97-2]], [[s05-P97-3]], [[s05-P97-4]] or [[s05-P97-5]].
  **Say.** "What did you change, and what in the record says so?" Expect: "I changed ___, because the record says ___."
- **4 min. A wind record.** The child does [[s05-P97-6]].
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** Read both questions aloud. [[s05-X97c]], answered alone. Then ask [[s05-X97d]] and [[s05-X97e]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one repair move in the marked group, then one transfer card. Never cut the transfer. Longer: write one claim the records cannot support, and the record that would be needed.

### Words and supports

Revise, component, fossil, layer, extinct, goal, limit, merit. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 98 opens unit 6 with the class weather-log puzzle. A child who missed today's wind row gets a highlighted row; a child who missed goal against limit gets matched cards before day 109. Today's pages stay in each child's folder.
