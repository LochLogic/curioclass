# Section 5.3 opener: joining the two scales, checks and response

## What the section does

Six days, 92 to 97. The big question is: "What do old records and new records each tell us, and what can neither tell us?" Day 92 argues from post-fire replanting records, a practice day different from the assessment. Day 93 revises the product F model, joins the day 82 drawings and the wall model, and sets a one-week log strip beside ancient change. Day 94 revises product S, compares a fossil record with a modern record, and holds the section exit. Day 95 is the end check for fossil evidence. Day 96 is the end check for judging a response. Day 97 responds to both checks.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A week of weather is a short record.** It cannot show ages. Fossil rock and a week of log are different records.
- **Each record has limits.** A fossil record gives kinds, sizes, places and order. It does not give exact dates, and a trunk piece gives only a minimum size.
- **Merit still means goals and limits.** Check the boxes before reading any count. Replanting does not always help. Fire is part of many places.
- **Voluntary sharing.** On day 92 no child is asked about a fire they know.
- **If a child tells or is upset.** If a child starts to tell about a fire they lived through, thank them, say "that sounds hard," do not ask for details, and keep the lesson on the paper example. If a child seems upset, let them step out with a helper or sit with you, and tell the school counselor or the family the same day. No one acts out a fire.
- **No climate-change teaching.** If a child asks, answer from evidence.

## Scoring, outside class

Day 95 and day 96 hold fresh diagnostics, 32 minutes each, unscored. The scored work is the revised product F (after day 95) and the revised product S (after day 96). Six points each, 12 in all. Score each point 1 when the component is complete, 0 when it is not. An unseen component is blank, not zero. Repairs happen in teaching slots only: days 93 and 94, then day 97. Never on days 95 and 96.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** it is this way now, so it always was. **B:** none here, so extinct. **C:** deeper is always older. **D:** everything responds alike. **E:** the biggest count wins. **F:** size alone proves a hot place.

## Supplies for the section

The score sheet, the product F and S cards, the printed evidence records for days 95 and 96, the model sheets, second colors. Weather equipment on days 93 and 94.

## A room with nothing

Papers are printed. The wall model is drawn on the board.
