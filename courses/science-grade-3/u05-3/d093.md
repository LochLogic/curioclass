# Day 93: One wall, two records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make 30 wall cards, one a child: 3 for the finds (trunk, leaf print, bark print), 1 guide card (tree), 3 solid and 3 dotted line cards, 2 order cards (older, younger), 5 shade-chain cards (shade lost, warmer water, fewer larvae, snails arrive, fish move), 7 log cards (a day each), 6 blank. Draw two halves on the wall: long ago, and now. Pin the week strip beside it. Print a model sheet each; have system sheets out.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 92's s05-P92-1, s05-P92-2, s05-P92-3 and the ticket s05-X92. Repair the commoner letter. If it is error E (the biggest count wins), put plot R beside the limit box: 9 is the most, and its 7 tokens break the limit of 4. If it is error D (every animal gets its food back the same way), say each follows its own need. One child restates it; any child may pass. The other letter waits for day 97.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P93-1]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record, said to be supplied.

#### 8 min. Product F revision

Hand back day 86's product F, s05-P86-3, with its written feedback and a fresh model sheet. Alone, in a second colour, on the model sheet: change one thing the feedback points to, keep the first drawing, [[s05-P93-2]]. Give no help beyond reading a line again. Say nothing of points. Collect the sheets; they are scored after day 95.

#### 27 min. Consensus model

3 minutes: uncover the day 82 drawings again: trunks with roots in rock, and a treeless snowy plain. Say what the finds show: a forest grew here, and a tree needs soil, water and enough warm days, so the place was warm long ago. How it became cold is a question for a later grade. 10 minutes: each child places one card on the wall, 20 seconds each, and says it, [[s05-P93-3]]. **Ask.** Is that card evidence or an inference? Expect: the trunk shows; "warm place" infers, so a dotted line. 6 minutes: pairs write the frame from the fossil records, [[s05-P93-4]]. 5 minutes: point to the one-week strip beside the wall, [[s05-P93-5]]. **Ask.** What can the week tell, and what can it not? Expect: this week only; the layers are the long record. 3 minutes: Exit E93. Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X93]]. Look for: no, the prints and the guide show a warm, wet forest long ago; one week is a short record. Error A says yes, the log is cold so it always was.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Cards counted back; the wall stays up for day 94.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the wall model on the board; children copy one card onto paper. No weather tools: read the dated adult record. One projector: show the wall; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall: draw two boxes, long ago and now, and place paper cards. No second colour: use a pen. No thermometer: read a dated weather record, said to be supplied.

**Skip.** The wall turns and the helpers.

- **6 min. Revise.** Give back product F. The child does [[s05-P93-2]] alone.
- **10 min. The two boxes.** The child places three cards and does [[s05-P93-3]].
  **Say.** "Is that card evidence or an inference?" Expect: the trunk shows; a warm place is inferred.
- **8 min. The frame.** The child does [[s05-P93-4]] and [[s05-P93-5]].
  **Say.** "What can one week of weather not tell?" Expect: how a place was long ago.
- **5 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P93-1]].
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X93]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: place two cards, not three. Never cut the revision or the exit. Longer: write one card that would change the wall if found.

### Words and supports

Fossil, evidence, inference, short record, long record, solid line, dotted line. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___ (kind, size, place), so ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; a scribe may write for a child.

### Tomorrow

Day 94 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error A (it is cold in the log, so it always was): lay the one-week strip beside the rooted layers. Product S then comes back for revision, and pairs compare what a fossil record and a modern record each support. Day 93's s05-X93 is handed back then.
