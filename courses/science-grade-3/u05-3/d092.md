# Day 92: After a fire, does replanting help?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the post-fire cards and a trial table for each pair, and the exit ticket for each child. Draw the hillside on the board. Ask no one about a fire they know; if a child tells one, use the opener's care line.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. The drawing is plain and shows no harm to any animal. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, cards counted back.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 91's s05-P91-1, s05-P91-2 and the ticket s05-X91. Product S, s05-P91-3, is handed back on day 94 with its look-for checklist. Repair only the commoner letter of day 91's exit. If it is error E (whole-patch removal is best because its counts are largest), check the whole patch against the goal box and the limit box. If it is error D (the vine harmed every plant and animal the same way), say each living thing follows its own need. One child restates it; any child may pass. The other letter waits for day 97.

#### 10 min. Post-fire evidence

Say fire is part of many places. 3 minutes: show the hillside card and read it aloud: black trunks, green shoots in the ash, birds on a trunk, ten marked spots. 4 minutes: alone, [[s05-P92-1]], two things seen and one thing wondered. 3 minutes: share. Sharing is voluntary. **Ask.** Is food coming back on this hillside? Expect: some, green shoots show; the plots will tell how much.

#### 10 min. Comparison

Say the numbers are invented for practice. 3 minutes: read the goal card: at least 6 of 10 spots with seed plants, within 6 tokens. Read the three matched plots: left alone, 7 of 10 spots, 0 tokens; replanted, 9 of 10, 4 tokens; replanted and fenced, 10 of 10, 8 tokens. 5 minutes: 15 pairs mark each plot on the trial table, [[s05-P92-2]]. Error E calls the fenced plot best because 10 is the most: send the pair to the token box. 2 minutes: take answers. **Ask.** Which plots have merit? Expect: left alone and replanted; the fenced plot breaks the token limit. Say the plot left alone meets both for 0 tokens: replanting is not always needed.

#### 14 min. Argument

3 minutes: pairs plan with the frame on side 2. 11 minutes: each child writes alone, [[s05-P92-3]]: a claim, evidence and a reason. **Ask.** What does the record not show about replanting? Expect: it is one hillside and one season; it cannot show replanting always helps. Hear six children from the rotating six (review from kindergarten), 1 minute each, one at a time while the rest write; the record names who. Look for: counts and tokens quoted, and "always" never claimed.

#### 6 min. Exit E92

1 minute: read the card aloud twice. 4 minutes: alone, [[s05-X92]]. Ask [[s05-X92b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. 1 minute: collect. Look for: plots P and Q meet the count and the limit; plot R breaks the token limit; one set of plots cannot show reseeding always helps. Error E says plot R, because 9 is the most. Error D says every animal gets its food back the same way. Note the letter.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the hillside, the goal card and the trial table on the board and read every card aloud; children answer on notebook paper. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud and draws three boxes for the plots.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the rotating six.

- **8 min. The hillside.** Read the hillside card aloud. The child does [[s05-P92-1]].
  **Say.** "What do you see that could be food for animals?" Expect: green shoots in the ash.
- **10 min. The plots.** Read the goal card. The child does [[s05-P92-2]].
  **Say.** "Which plots meet the goal and the limit?" Expect: left alone and replanted; the fenced plot costs too many tokens.
- **12 min. Your claim.** The child does [[s05-P92-3]] alone.
  **Say.** "What does the record not show?" Expect: that replanting always helps.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X92]], answered alone. Then ask [[s05-X92b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark only the plot left alone and the replanted plot. Never cut the claim or the exit. Longer: write what a second hillside would need to show about replanting.

### Words and supports

Fire, replant, plot, shrub, merit, limit, token. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; a scribe may write for a child.

### Tomorrow

Day 93 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on the commoner letter of day 92's exit: error E (the biggest count wins) with the limit box beside it, or error D (every animal gets its food back the same way) with each one's own need; the other waits for day 97. Then product F comes back for revision, and the class joins its two models on one wall.
