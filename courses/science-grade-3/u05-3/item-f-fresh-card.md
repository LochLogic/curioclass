# Item F fresh card (day 95, the scored check)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Two outcrops on a dry plain. The layer is the same level at both. The layers are undisturbed.

## West outcrop

Complete freshwater shell fossils, 3 centimeters long. A guide says this shell form is extinct worldwide. Other shellfish are not.

## East outcrop

Land animal footprints, 14 centimeters long. Above the footprints is a rooted trunk fragment, 60 centimeters long.

## Your task

Compare two claims: continuous water, or a shoreline with nearby land. Annotate your model with kinds, sizes, places, order and limits of the evidence. Say how this long history differs from a week of weather.
