# Day 96: Check, a cold spell in a garden

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the fresh card and the student page for each child, and keep the score sheet, unit 5, in your folder. Put each child's day 95 pages in the folder; nothing goes back today.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback, no repair. The scored work for this check is the revised product S, s05-E7, s05-E8, s05-E9, s05-E10, s05-E11 and s05-E12, scored after class, outside lesson minutes; each child's revised product S was handed in on day 94. Day 95's pages stay held. Read the task aloud: make a merit claim, draw arrows and write one limit on the evidence. Read the fresh card aloud twice; it is invented for practice.

#### 32 min. Fresh diagnostic

Alone on paper, [[s05-P96-1]], [[s05-P96-2]], [[s05-P96-3]] and [[s05-P96-4]]. Give no help and no feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: plants stop flowering, moths leave, caterpillars die and beetles arrive, each its own way; arrows from the cold spell to each kind; the low cover meets both goals, the 10 token budget and the 50 centimeter limit; the tall shelter has bigger counts but stands 70 centimeters; the trials are models and do not show every animal benefits. Note error D (all organisms alike) and error E (the biggest count wins) for day 97. The fresh work is a diagnostic and is not scored.

#### 6 min. Independent checking

Children reread and tick each statement a record backs, [[s05-P96-5]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the fresh card on the board and read it aloud; children answer on notebook paper and draw the arrows by hand. One projector: show the card; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed card: the parent copies it onto paper. No scored page: skip the last part.

**Skip.** The scoring and the clean-up.

- **36 min. Assessment.** Read the fresh card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s05-P96-1]], [[s05-P96-2]], [[s05-P96-3]] and [[s05-P96-4]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "Which model meets every goal and limit?" Expect: the low cover.
- **4 min. Checking.** The child does [[s05-P96-5]].
- **4 min. Your revised product S.** Read [[s05-E7]], [[s05-E8]], [[s05-E9]], [[s05-E10]], [[s05-E11]] and [[s05-E12]]; they are scored from the revised product. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the claim and the limit. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Goal, limit, token, merit, claim, evidence. Frame: "I think ___ is better, because ___. The evidence cannot show ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply claims or limits.

### Tomorrow

Day 97 answers both checks. Day 95's assessment and day 96's pages and scores come back in the first 5 minutes, and each child joins the group for the component missed. Unit 6 opens on day 98 with the class weather log.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
