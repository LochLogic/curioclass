# Day 36: Fix one slip, then try new pictures

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the returned pages by child: day 34 and day 35, each with one written next step. Set four repair places, one for each group. Put a second-colour pencil out for each child. On the record sheet list which children take which letters.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[c1-P36-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 3 min. Return of feedback

Hand back day 34's assessment and day 35's assessment, each with one written next step. Children read their own, silently, and mark the one slip they will fix. Nothing is scored again. A child with no letter takes a fresh question card and writes a question that can be tested.

#### 14 min. Repair groups

Four groups, 3 minutes each with the teacher; a child goes to the group for the letter on their note, or to two groups. The rest work from the station cards in a second colour, [[c1-P36-2]]. Group 1, errors A and B, from c1-E34a: cross out every change but one and add tries until each way has three. Group 2, errors C and D, from c1-E34c: hold a book still between two hands, draw both arrows and say they cancel; trace a short arrow that starts on the book. Group 3, errors E and F, from c1-E34e: lay the six weight pictures in order and mark the order that repeats. Group 4, errors G, H and I, from c1-E35a to c1-E35c: touch the gap on board 1 beside claim A, turn which is best into one thing to change, and sort steel and brass beside a magnet. A group of up to eight gets about 20 seconds a child in a 3-minute turn; the spare 2 minutes cover moving.

#### 8 min. Exit

Alone, the exit ticket: [[c1-X36a]], [[c1-X36b]], [[c1-X36c]] and [[c1-X36d]]. Read each picture aloud twice, 2 minutes each. Note letters on the record sheet. Collect the tickets. The revised papers, [[c1-P36-1]] and [[c1-P36-2]], stay with the students in the folder.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Pencils and cards back to the folder. Wipe the tables. Wash hands.

#### 10 min. Readiness

The unit 3 readiness look: read [[s03-R1]] once aloud and give 4 minutes; read [[s03-R2]] once aloud and give 4 minutes; collect the papers in 2. Do not coach. It is not scored. Keep the papers.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the repair cards onto the board, one group at a time. No second colour: write the change in pencil and draw a box round it. A small class: the teacher takes each group in order and no card stays out.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the exit pictures onto paper. No second colour: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records, the groups and the readiness paper.

- **5 min. Your notes.** Read the next steps on your day 34 and day 35 pages. Pick one slip.
- **24 min. Fix one slip.** Do [[c1-P36-2]], then a second slip if you can.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: "I changed one thing, and the first answer is kept."
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[c1-X36a]], [[c1-X36b]], [[c1-X36c]] and [[c1-X36d]], the four exit pictures, answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fix one slip only. Never cut the new pictures. Longer: write a second question for the magnet and the washer and say what each would change.

### Words and supports

Force arrow, cancel, pattern, testable, need, criterion. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 37 starts unit 3. Today's readiness papers seat its pairs, and day 38 holds the ten-minute support.
