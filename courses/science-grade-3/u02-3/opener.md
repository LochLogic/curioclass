# Section 2.3 opener: one model, then fresh cases

## What the section does

Five days, 29 to 33. Day 29 builds the class's consensus model and the second pollination slot, and each child writes two records: an interaction record and a lid record. Day 30 returns a checklist on each record, and every child revises both records. The class also critiques a new statement and judges two proposals. Days 31 and 32 are the checks. They use fresh cases, and their records are scored. Day 31 asks questions about a comb-and-tissue arrangement and magnet evidence cards. Day 32 defines a problem for two toy cars. The revised day 29 records are marked beside the scores as repair evidence, not the score. Day 33 reteaches the two most common wrong ideas and closes the unit.

## How the days run

Each day is 45 minutes. Days 29, 30 and 33 start with the weather record. Days 31 and 32 give no feedback during the work. Day 31 has six groups of five at the table for four minutes each, while the others write their own questions. Every child may pass in public talk.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Both kinds of pull work across a gap.** A rubbed object pulls light bits. A magnet pulls steel. Neither needs touch. A pull gets weaker as the gap grows.
- **Only some metals are pulled.** Steel is. Brass and aluminium are not.
- **Turning a magnet flips magnet to magnet.** Unlike ends attract, like ends repel. A steel object is pulled by either end.
- **A testable question changes one thing, keeps the rest and watches one effect.**
- **A problem statement has a need, criteria and limits.** A changed user changes the criteria. The limits stay.
- **A ruler reading starts at zero on one edge and reads the other edge.**

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** a force requires contact. **B:** every metal responds the same way. **C:** turning a magnet makes it repel steel. **D:** changing two things finds the cause. **E:** a list of materials is the problem. **F:** a limit may be ignored if it works.

## Scoring

Fifteen points from the fresh check-day work. Items 1 to 3 (7 points, the last a ruler reading) are the day 31 question sheet. Items 4 to 6 (8 points) are the day 32 toy cars statement. Score 1 for each component met, 0 when not met, blank when not seen. Beside each score, mark the revised day 29 record shown or not yet for each component: repair evidence, never the score. Feedback is a six-tick checklist with one question stamp, about 1 minute a child. Day 33 returns the checklists; the scored papers come back on day 36.

## Supplies

Day 31: magnets, counted clips, strings, distance strips, combs, wool cloth, tissue bits and eye protection. Day 32: paper and the car cards only. Count magnets and clips out and back.
