# Cards, day 29

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Sort cards

Eight small cards for pairs to sort into the four columns of the chart.

1. A magnet pulls a hung clip across a 2 cm gap. (across a gap)
2. Two magnets, red end to blue end, pull together. (magnet to magnet)
3. Two magnets, red end to red end, push apart. (magnet to magnet)
4. A magnet pulls a steel ring on a thread. (magnet to steel)
5. Turn the magnet over near the steel ring: still a pull. (magnet to steel)
6. A rubbed straw lifts a few feather bits. (rubbed to paper)
7. An unrubbed straw lifts none. (rubbed to paper)
8. A foil square does not lean toward a magnet. (magnet to steel, not pulled)
