# Check cards, days 34 and 35

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 34. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no words except where a card says so. Print "invented for practice" in small letters beneath each card. Read each card aloud twice.

## Weight card (day 34)

Eight pictures in a row, each with a ruler standing beside it. A small weight hangs on a spring above a table. Turns 1 to 6 show the lower edge of the weight at 14, 10, 6, 10, 13 and 10 cm on the ruler. Turns 7 and 8 are blank boxes marked 7 and 8, with the same ruler. The record goes high, middle, low, middle, high, middle; the low and the high turns each come a little closer to the middle than the one before.

## Board 1: a comb and a foil ball (day 35)

A foil ball hangs on a thread. A plastic comb is rubbed on wool and held at a gap from the ball.

- Record 1: gap 2 cm, the ball leans 3 cm toward the comb. Nothing touches.
- Record 2: gap 6 cm, the ball leans 1 cm toward the comb. Nothing touches.
- Record 3: gap 10 cm, no lean seen.
- Record 4: a comb that was not rubbed, gap 2 cm, no lean.

Claims:

- A: A comb can only pull the ball when it touches it.
- B: A bigger gap gives a smaller lean.
- C: Rubbing the comb makes the pull.

## Board 2: two magnets and two washers (day 35)

A magnet hangs on a thread, red end toward you. A second magnet is held beside it. Two washers, steel and brass, are tried beside the hung magnet.

- Record 1: red end to red end, gap 3 cm, the hung magnet leans 2 cm away.
- Record 2: red end to blue end, gap 3 cm, the hung magnet leans 2 cm toward.
- Record 3: red end to blue end, gap 8 cm, no lean seen.
- Record 4: a steel washer hung beside the magnet, gap 3 cm, it leans 2 cm toward.
- Record 5: a brass washer hung beside the magnet, gap 3 cm, no lean.

Claims:

- D: Two red ends push. A red end and a blue end pull.
- E: Every metal washer leans toward a magnet.
- F: A bigger gap gives a weaker pull.
