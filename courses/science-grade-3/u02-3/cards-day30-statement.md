# Statement card, day 30

Labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawing, no brands, no names.

The school office needs a holder for the sign-in sheet. A card reads: "The office needs a holder that keeps a sign-in sheet flat on a steel shelf. Use magnets."

Pairs mark what can be checked and what cannot.

## Two proposals

Proposal 1: one magnet and two tape lengths. The sheet stays flat for a whole morning. It takes five making minutes. It costs 4 tokens.

Proposal 2: three magnets and one tape length. The sheet stays flat for a whole morning. It takes four making minutes. It costs 7 tokens.

The rewritten statement has a limit of 5 tokens, 6 making minutes and two magnets.
