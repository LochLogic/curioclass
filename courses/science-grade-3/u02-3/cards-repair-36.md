# Repair cards and exit cards, day 36

Four repair sets for day 36 and the exit cards. Plain line drawings, no brand names. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

## Repair sets

- **Set 1, letters A and B.** A plan card with two changes and one try of each way. Children cross out every change but one and add tries until each way has three.
- **Set 2, letters C and D.** A cork block between two hands, and a block with one long arrow along its slide. Children draw both arrows on the block, say they cancel, and trace a short arrow that starts on the block.
- **Set 3, letters E and F.** The six weight pictures from day 34 in a row. Children lay them in order, mark the order that repeats and read how the low turns change.
- **Set 4, letters G, H and I.** Board 1 records 1 and 2 beside claim A; the line "Which comb is the best?"; a steel washer and a brass washer beside a magnet. Children touch the gap, turn the question into one thing to change, and sort pulled and not pulled.

## Exit cards (answered alone, read aloud twice)

- **X36e.** First question: a drawer that slides easily in a desk, Jo's hand at the front and Kim's hand at the back, both pushing in. Second question: a bird feeder with a flap, two magnets and one tape length laid beside it.
- **P36-3, said aloud.** A seesaw drawn six times: up, level, down, level, up, level. Rulers show the two up turns at 40 and 36 cm.
- **P36-4, said aloud.** A magnet held beside a hanging steel washer.
