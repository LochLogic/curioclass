# Day 30: Can this statement judge a solution?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a statement card and the two proposals at each pair. Sort the day 29 records, each with its note, into piles. Put a second-color pencil out for each child. Write on the board: a magnet costs 2 tokens, a tape length costs 1.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. No magnets are out today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P30-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 29's exit tickets, s02-X29. Error A, 2 minutes: write "Neither pulls unless they touch" and say someone might write it. Trace a pull arrow across a drawn gap with a finger: day 19's clip leaned with nothing touching it. None seen at a far gap means the pull may be too small to see. Error B, 2 minutes: lay four records beside each other: a steel nail leans, a steel screw leans, copper wire and a foil square do not. Sort pulled and not pulled; not every metal is pulled.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 2 min. Recap

A need says who needs what so that. A criterion is a way to check it. A limit is the most we allow of materials, time and cost.

#### 8 min. Critique a new statement

Show the statement card. Pairs mark what can be checked and what cannot, 3 minutes, [[s02-P30-2]]. **Ask.** Can we judge a proposal with this statement? Why? Expect: not well; flat has no way to check, use magnets names a material, and there are no limits. Hear three pairs, 40 seconds each. Say the need is there, the office and the flat sheet, but nothing lets us check it.

#### 6 min. Rewrite and judge

Alone, rewrite it, [[s02-P30-3]]: the need, two criteria you can check, and the limits on the card: at most two magnets, six making minutes, five tokens. Then read the two proposals, [[s02-P30-4]]. Expect proposal 1: one magnet, 5 minutes, 4 tokens, within every limit; proposal 2 has three magnets and 7 tokens. A partner may scribe.

#### 15 min. Revise the day 29 records

Hand back the interaction record (s02-P29-4 to s02-P29-8) and the lid record (s02-P29-9 to s02-P29-12), each with its note. Alone, in a second color, change one thing the note names; keep the first answer and put initials beside it. Interaction record first, about 7 minutes, [[s02-P30-5]]; lid record, about 8, [[s02-P30-6]]. Nothing new is taught. Walk the room, and ask each child to read their note aloud to you, never to the class. The weather and sort pages stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X30]]; read all three cards aloud. Ask [[s02-X30b]] and [[s02-X30c]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Look for: (a) no, it lists things and has nothing to check; (b) time and cost; (c) no, it is over the time limit. Error E: "yes, it says what to use." Error F: "nothing else is needed" or "yes, it holds six." Note letters on the record sheet. Collect both revised records. Day 33 takes any letter that is common.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the statement and proposals onto the board. No second color: write the change in pencil beside the first and draw a box round it.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the statement card onto paper. No second color: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records and the sharing.

- **8 min. The statement.** Do [[s02-P30-2]].
  **Say.** "Can we check this? Why?" Expect: "No. It names a material and nothing to check."
- **8 min. Rewrite.** Do [[s02-P30-3]] and [[s02-P30-4]].
- **12 min. Revise.** Read your notes on the day 29 records. Do [[s02-P30-5]] and [[s02-P30-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X30]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X30b]] and [[s02-X30c]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise one record only. Never cut the rewrite, the second color or the exit. Longer: write a third criterion and say what it might pull against.

### Words and supports

Need, criterion, limit, token, proposal. Frames: "The limit is ___." "It can be checked by ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 31 is a check, the first of two. Children ask questions about records they have not seen, in groups of five at the table; nobody is given feedback while they work. You score the revised interaction record after class, outside lesson minutes, and the lid record before day 32. Any exit letter that is common waits for day 33.

Ours, all of it.
