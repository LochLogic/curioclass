# Day 35: A comb, a ball and two magnets

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the two boards. Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. Day 31's oral record stays on the record sheet unchanged and is not collected or scored again today. Day 34's pages are not handed back until day 36.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "This is the second part of the check, and day 34's was the first. I will not help. The boards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages and show where answers go. Practice one touch: "Touch the word claim."

#### 15 min. Board 1

Show board 1. Read the four records and the three claims aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c1-E35a]] (6 minutes) and [[c1-E35b]] (5 minutes). At 14 minutes say: "Turn the page."

#### 15 min. Board 2

Show board 2. Read the five records and the three claims aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c1-E35c]] (5 minutes) and [[c1-E35d]] (6 minutes).

#### 5 min. Own question

Alone, [[c1-E35e]] (4 minutes). Collect every page.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Boards and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks.

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the five items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: A, D, G, H or I. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each board on the board and hide it between parts; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Copy the records onto paper. Any two magnets and a paper clip may stand in for the boards. Use large magnets with an adult, keep fingers clear of pinches, and keep magnets, clips and washers from mouths, young children, phones and tablets.

**Skip.** The directions practice and the clean-up.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the boards are invented. Read each record aloud once. Your child answers [[c1-E35a]] to [[c1-E35e]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Did the comb have to touch the ball?" Expect: "No. The ball leaned across a gap."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Claim, record, pull, push, gap, lean, testable. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 36 hands day 34's assessment and day 35's assessment back, each with one written next step. It ends with a short readiness paper for unit 3.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
