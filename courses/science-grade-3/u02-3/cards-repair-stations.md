# Repair cards, day 33

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Station A and B

Two cards. A: a hung clip leans toward a magnet across a gap, with a hand-drawn ruler. B: a foil square and a steel clip beside a magnet; only the clip is pulled.

## Station C and D

C: two magnets, red to blue, then one turned; and a steel clip with the same turn. D: a plan that changes the gap and the turn together, crossed out to leave one.

## Stations E and F

E: a list that reads "two magnets and tape" beside a need written as who needs what so that. F: a printed limit of 4 tokens and a proposal that costs 6.

## New cases for day 33

A fresh question card: a small magnet through a plastic cup wall pulls a steel clip inside. A fresh statement case: the class needs a wall chart to stay on a steel board all day.
