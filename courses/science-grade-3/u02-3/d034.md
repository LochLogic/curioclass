# Day 34: A cork block and a bobbing weight

## Teacher page

### Before class

At the door, hand back day 33's exit ticket, s02-X33, and the revised papers, s02-P33-1 and s02-P33-2, without talk; none is used today. Set six places, each with a flat tray, the cork block and a ruler. Seat the rest so each child sees only their own page. Cover the walls. On the record sheet put the six groups of five in order and open the heard column.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes stay inside the tray, away from table edges, and the floor is clear. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 6 min. Launch

Say: "This is a check. I will not help. The block and the weight card are new, and invented for practice. You may pass." Show the six places and read the order of groups aloud. Hand out the pages and the weight cards, face down. Practice one touch: "Touch the cork block."

#### 24 min. Station turns

Six turns of 4 minutes, one group of five at the station each time. Each child has 40 seconds: an about-equal pair of pushes from opposite sides, then one stronger push, 10 seconds each; read the distance to the whole centimeter and jot it, 10 seconds; swap, 10 seconds. Five children take 200 seconds, so 40 seconds are left to come and go. Say nothing and tick the heard column. Every other child works alone: [[c1-E34a]] first, then the weight card, [[c1-E34d]] and [[c1-E34e]].

#### 7 min. Finish records

Everyone writes [[c1-E34b]] from their jotted readings and [[c1-E34c]], and finishes any weight answer. At 6 minutes say: "Pages in." Collect every page.

#### 2 min. Living care

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest sit with pencils down.

#### 6 min. Clean-up

Trays, cork blocks and rulers back in the box. Wipe the tables. Wash hands.

### Marking

After class, 30 minutes: mark the five items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap, A to F. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No tray: use a clipboard on a table. No cork block: a wooden block or a thick eraser, pushed gently. A small class: groups of up to five, the same 40 seconds each.

### At home

**Swap.** A cardboard tray and a wooden block work. Read the weight card yourself.

**Skip.** The station order, the heard column and the living care.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Your child plans [[c1-E34a]], pushes a block on a tray two ways and writes [[c1-E34b]] and [[c1-E34c]], then answers [[c1-E34d]] and [[c1-E34e]] from the weight card, alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Why did the block stay still when the pushes were equal?" Expect: "The two arrows are the same size and cancel."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Fair test, trial, force arrow, balanced, cancel, whole centimeter, pattern. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 35 is the second part of the check: two printed evidence boards, one on a comb and one on magnets. Nothing from today is handed back until day 36.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
