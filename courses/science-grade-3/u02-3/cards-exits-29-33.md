# Exit cards, days 29 to 33

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 29

Card 1: a rubbed pen lifts salt grains from 1 cm, and a magnet lifts a steel pin from 1 cm. Card 2: a brass key and a steel key beside a magnet. Pat says both are pulled because both are metal.

## Day 30

The art room needs a rack to hold six brushes upright all morning. Card 1: "Use two magnets and tape." Card 2: a statement with a materials limit only. Card 3: a proposal holds six brushes but takes ten making minutes. The limit is six.

## Day 33

A toy tram on a short track. A marked end of the track has a small block. The tram must stop at the mark without touching the end. Allowed: two magnets, one card, one tape length, five making minutes, three tokens. One token a supply. Card 2: Pat's proposal uses two magnets, the card and the tape.
