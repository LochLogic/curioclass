# Records for day 31 (printed only on day 31)

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Card 1: comb and tissue

A hard plastic comb is held over tissue bits. At 2 cm three bits lift and cling. The tissue is dry. The comb was rubbed on wool 15 times.

## Card 2: a magnet pair

Two flat magnets on a thread. At 4 cm the hung magnet leans 2 cm. Turned to red-to-red, it leans away 2 cm.

## Card 3: a steel spoon

A steel spoon hung on thread near a magnet. At 3 cm it leans toward the magnet. Turned the other end, it still leans toward the magnet.
