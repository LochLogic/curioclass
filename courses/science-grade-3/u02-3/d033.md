# Day 33: Fix one slip, then try a new problem

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the returned work into piles by child: the day 31 and day 32 checklists and day 30's exit ticket. The scored papers stay with you until day 36. Set the repair cards at six places, one for each of the errors A to F. Put a second-color pencil out for each child. On the record sheet, list which children take which letters.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. No magnets are out today.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P33-1]]. The last minute is hand washing.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest look.

#### 5 min. Return of feedback

Hand back day 30's exit tickets, s02-X30, and the checklists for day 31's assessment (s02-P31-1 to s02-P31-5) and day 32's assessment (s02-P32-1, s02-P32-2, s02-P32-3, s02-P32-4, s02-P32-5 and s02-P32-6), six ticks and a question stamp each. The scored papers come back on day 36. Children read their own checklists silently, 2 minutes, and mark the one slip they will fix. Give a child with no letter the fresh question card and let them write a question that can be tested. Today's work is not scored.

#### 24 min. Repair groups

Six station cards, one for each letter. A child goes to the card for each letter the record sheet lists for them. The teacher takes one letter at a time and the others work from the card, in a second color, [[s02-P33-2]]. Error A, 3 minutes: lay a ruler across the gap and trace the arrow along it; none seen means the pull may be too small to see. Error B, 3 minutes: sort four labeled records into pulled and not pulled; not every metal is pulled. Error C, 3 minutes: draw both arrow pairs, a magnet and steel, then two magnets; turning a magnet can make a push, turning steel does not. Error D, 3 minutes: cross out every change but one in a proposed test. Error E, 6 minutes: rewrite a materials list as who needs what so that. Error F, 6 minutes: check a proposal card line by line against the printed allowance. A group of up to six children gets about 30 seconds each in a 3-minute turn and 1 minute each in a 6-minute turn; keep the turns short and let the rest write.

#### 6 min. A new problem

Alone, [[s02-X33]]; read the tram picture aloud twice. Note letters on the record sheet. Collect the tickets and every revised paper.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Pencils and cards back to the folder. Wipe the tables. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the repair cards onto the board, one letter at a time. No second color: write the change in pencil and draw a box round it. A small class: the teacher takes each letter in order and no card stays out.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the tram picture onto paper. No second color: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records, the stations and the sharing.

- **5 min. Your checklists.** Read your checklists for the day 31 and day 32 checks. Pick one slip.
- **24 min. Fix one slip.** Do [[s02-P33-2]], then a second slip if you can.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: "I changed one thing, the gap or the need, and the first answer is kept."
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X33]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fix one slip only. Never cut the new problem. Longer: write a second criterion for the tram and say what it might pull against.

### Words and supports

Need, criterion, limit, token, pull, push, gap. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." "The limit is ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 34 is the first part of the course check: a fair test on a cork block and a bobbing weight record. Keep the revised papers for the record sheet; they are not used on day 34.

Ours, all of it.
