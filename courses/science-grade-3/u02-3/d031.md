# Day 31: Check, new records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set the table: comb, wool, tissue bits on a tray, a distance strip, two magnets, a clip on a string. Count the magnets and clips. Set the three record cards at each group's place and fit eye protection. Write the waiting task on the board: choose a card and write your question.

### Safety and set-up

Eye protection on every child all lesson. Magnets and clips counted out and back; fingers clear of pinches; magnets and clips from mouths, phones and tablets. Comb and cloth only; no circuits. The teacher alone handles the clip on its string. Clean-up is 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 6 min. Launch and transitions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Teach the stop signal. The revised day 29 interaction records (s02-E1, s02-E2 and s02-E3) are scored after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. Day 30's exit tickets, s02-X30, go back on day 33. Name the order of the six groups and explain the ten independent minutes.

#### 24 min. Item 1, six groups of five, 4 minutes each

One group at the table at a time. The teacher rubs the comb on wool 15 times and holds it over the tissue bits, then shows the magnets and clip. Each child asks one question and gives one reason, about 48 seconds a child. Listen for one change and a reason from a card or the table. Note A, C or D on the record sheet: touch needed, turning makes steel push, two changes. Give no feedback. The other groups write on the question sheet, [[s02-P31-1]] and [[s02-P31-2]]; start with the cards.

#### 10 min. Independent records

Alone, finish the question sheet: [[s02-P31-3]] and [[s02-P31-4]]. A partner may scribe or point. No feedback. Collect the sheets; they go forward to day 35 unchanged.

#### 5 min. Clean-up

Count the magnets and clips back. Collect combs and tissue bits. Eye protection wiped.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the three cards on the board. No magnets or combs: use the cards only, said aloud to be weaker than handling. One projector: show the cards; children write their own.

### At home

**Swap.** No comb: a plastic spoon. No magnets: use the cards only. No printer: copy the cards.

**Skip.** The group turns, the table and the scored record.

- **20 min. Assessment, the question.** Read the cards aloud. Do [[s02-P31-1]] and [[s02-P31-2]] alone.
  **Say.** "What would you change, keep and watch?" Expect: "One change, such as the gap; the rest kept; one thing watched."
- **12 min. Assessment, the cause.** Do [[s02-P31-3]] and [[s02-P31-4]] alone.
- **8 min. Your revised record.** Read [[s02-E1]], [[s02-E2]] and [[s02-E3]]; they are scored from the day 29 record. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who finishes writes a second question and says so. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Gap, pull, push, cause, test, evidence. Frames: "I change ___." "I watch ___." Read each card aloud; accept pointing, drawing or dictation; large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 32 is the second check, on paper only: own work, no help, no feedback. Day 31's question sheets go back on day 33 with a note on each part, and the two most common wrong ideas are reteaught then.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
