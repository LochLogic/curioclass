# Day 32: Check, a toy cars statement

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a toy cars card and a statement sheet at every place. Write the waiting task on the board: read the card and mark what you see. Have the record sheet ready for notes on errors B, E and F.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only today. No magnets, steel or cars in the room. Eye protection is not needed. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Launch

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Read the toy cars card aloud once. Say that this is the second check and that day 31 went the same way. Today's fresh work is scored after class, outside lesson minutes: s02-E4, s02-E5 and s02-E6. The revised day 29 lid records are marked beside it as repair evidence, not the score. Nothing goes back today. Day 31's assessment (s02-P31-1, s02-P31-2, s02-P31-3, s02-P31-4 and s02-P31-5) is scored after class; their checklists go back on day 33 with the day 32 checklist, and the scored papers on day 36.

#### 12 min. Item 2, why the ends, material and gap matter

Alone, do [[s02-P32-1]] and [[s02-P32-2]] on the statement sheet. Walk the room and note, never correct. Mark B on the record sheet for any child who says every metal pushes. A partner may scribe or point.

#### 20 min. Item 3, the statement

Alone, do [[s02-P32-3]], [[s02-P32-4]] and [[s02-P32-5]]. The limits are on the card: four magnets, cardboard and tape; ten making minutes; six tokens. Note E where the need is only a list of materials, and F where a limit is dropped. Give no feedback.

#### 5 min. Independent checking

Alone, read your statement once and do [[s02-P32-6]]. Add nothing to the earlier parts. A child who finishes sits quietly.

#### 3 min. Packing

Collect the statement sheets and the cards. After class, score them and fill checklist 4, about 1 minute a child. The checklists go back on day 33; the scored sheets on day 36.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the toy cars card on the board; children write their own sheet. One projector: show the card; children write on plain paper. A room with no desks: clipboards or books on knees.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No pencil: any writing tool.

**Skip.** The packing.

- **12 min. Assessment, the ends, material and gap.** Read the card aloud. Do [[s02-P32-1]] and [[s02-P32-2]] alone.
  **Say.** "What does red facing red do, and what does a steel washer do?" Expect: "Red to red pushes apart; steel is only pulled."
- **20 min. Assessment, the statement.** Do [[s02-P32-3]], [[s02-P32-4]] and [[s02-P32-5]] alone.
- **8 min. Check and your lid record.** Do [[s02-P32-6]]. Then read [[s02-E4]], [[s02-E5]] and [[s02-E6]]; they check today's statement. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who finishes writes a second criterion and says so. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Need, criterion, limit, token, gap, pull, push. Frames: "The children need ___ so that ___." "It is checked by ___." Read each part aloud; accept pointing, drawing or dictation; large print on request.

### Tomorrow

Day 33 is the respond day. The checklists for both checks go back, and day 30's exit tickets, s02-X30, go back too. The most common wrong ideas, A and B, C and D, and E and F, are reteaught in four short groups, then a new problem is tried alone.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
