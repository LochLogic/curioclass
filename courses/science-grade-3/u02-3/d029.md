# Day 29: One model for pulls across a gap

## Teacher page

### Before class

Rule two lines 3 cm apart on a card for each pair: the gap card. Set a ruler, a chart and the eight cards at each pair. Put the flowering pots and swabs out. Write the four columns on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Check allergies before any child touches a flower; no touching faces; wash hands after. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. Mealworms are never handled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s02-P29-1]]. Last minute: hand washing.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 28's exit tickets, s02-X28. Error E, 2 minutes: write "A magnet, a card and tape" as someone might. Rewrite it: a helper needs to lift caps without bending so that the room is clear. Error F, 2 minutes: set a proposal beside the printed allowance: one magnet, two tape lengths, seven minutes, four tokens. Proposal: one magnet, two tapes, nine minutes. Line by line, it is over the time.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date and note the pots and mealworm box.

#### 10 min. Pollination (set-up for unit 3), with the consensus chart

A set-up for unit 3, not a test. The rotating six work in pairs at the flowering pots, one swab per plant: brush each open flower, carry pollen to another plant, wash hands. An allergic child sorts instead. The other pairs sort the eight cards into the four columns, [[s02-P29-2]], and write a sentence a column, [[s02-P29-3]]. At 8 minutes the six rejoin. **Ask.** What is the same about a magnet and a rubbed straw? Expect: both pull across a gap with no touching. Hear three pairs, 40 seconds each; fill the board chart. The six copy it.

#### 10 min. Interaction record

Alone, on the interaction record. Draw the hung tack, the magnet and the gap, [[s02-P29-4]], with three arrows: the pull across the gap, gravity down, the thread's pull up along the thread. **Ask.** Where does the magnet's arrow start, and why? Expect: on the tack, because the tack is pulled. Write [[s02-P29-5]]. In pairs each child reads the gap card, 30 seconds a child, [[s02-P29-6]]. Write four questions, [[s02-P29-7]]: a rubbed straw and the gap, a magnet and the gap, turning, steel for a second magnet. Fill change, keep and watch for one, [[s02-P29-8]].

#### 10 min. Lid record

Alone, on the lid record: the need and two criteria, [[s02-P29-9]]. Say the allowance: two magnets, six making minutes, six tokens; a magnet costs 2, a tape length 1. Write the limits, [[s02-P29-10]], the materials and cost, [[s02-P29-11]], then the gap, facing ends and an arrow, [[s02-P29-12]]. Expect red facing blue. Last, the light-pull user, [[s02-P29-13]].

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone, [[s02-X29]]; read both cards aloud. Ask [[s02-X29b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) both pull across a gap; the pen needs rubbing, the magnet pulls steel; (b) no, only the steel key. Error A: "neither pulls unless they touch." Error B: "yes, all metals." Note letters. Collect both records. After class, fill checklists 1 and 2 for each child: six ticks and a question stamp, never the answer, about 30 minutes in all. Other pages stay in the folder.

#### 1 min. Question board

Group the questions into distance, materials, turning and uses; mark answered ones.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the columns, write the card lines on scraps, rule the gap card. No flowering plants: swab a drawn flower, said to be weaker. No ruler: draw one in whole centimeters.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: write the eight lines on scraps. No ruler: draw one in whole centimeters. No printer: draw the chart and records.

**Skip.** The weather, the pollination, the living records and the board.

- **8 min. Sort.** Do [[s02-P29-2]] and [[s02-P29-3]].
  **Say.** "What is the same about a magnet and a rubbed straw?" Expect: "Both pull across a gap with no touching."
- **8 min. The tack.** Do [[s02-P29-4]], [[s02-P29-5]] and [[s02-P29-6]].
- **8 min. Questions.** Do [[s02-P29-7]] and [[s02-P29-8]].
- **8 min. The lid.** Do [[s02-P29-9]], [[s02-P29-10]], [[s02-P29-11]], [[s02-P29-12]] and [[s02-P29-13]].
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s02-X29]], answered alone. Then ask [[s02-X29b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two questions, not four. Never cut the arrows, the need, the limits or the exit. Longer: add a third lid criterion; does it pull against another?

### Words and supports

Gap, pull, criterion, limit, token. Frames: "I ask ___ because ___." "The limit is ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 30 opens with the weather record, then 4 minutes of Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and hands back day 29's exit. Error A ("neither pulls unless they touch") traces an arrow along a ruler across the gap. Error B ("yes, all metals") sorts four records into pulled and not pulled. Both records come back with a checklist, to revise in a second color.

Ours, all of it.
