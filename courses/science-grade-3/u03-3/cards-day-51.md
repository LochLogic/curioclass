# Sorting cards, day 51

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Eight cards. Pairs sort each: from the parents, from the surroundings, or from both. Each sort names a record or a pattern.

- A: a trout has spots like its parents' spots. Trout from three different streams all have spots like their parents'.
- B: a fern has big leaves in shade and small leaves in sun.
- C: a chickadee learns where the feeder is.
- D: two grass kinds, tall and short. Both grow taller with more water.
- E: sparrow chicks have the brown streaks of both parents.
- F: a tomato plant given too little water has small leaves.
- G: a cat comes to the kitchen when it hears the can opener.
- H: two daisy kinds, one with white petals and one with pink. Both have fewer flowers in shade.
