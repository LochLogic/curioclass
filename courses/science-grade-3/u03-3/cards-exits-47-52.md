# Exit cards, days 47 to 52

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 47

Two photographs of one kind of rosebush. One is in a sunny yard with tall stems. One is beside a shed with short stems. Sam says the yard made it taller.

## Day 48

Pot X: 3 plants, 15, 16 and 15 centimetres tall. Pot Y: 9 plants, 9, 8, 10, 9, 9, 10, 8, 9 and 9 centimetres. Same kind, water, light and soil.

## Day 49

Pot A: 2 plants on a sunny sill. Pot B: 8 plants on a dim shelf. A's plants are taller.

## Day 50

(a) A parrot hatched from an egg. Its family says "hello" to it every morning. Now it says "hello". Lee says it was born knowing "hello". (b) A houseplant of a tall-growing kind sits behind a thick curtain with almost no daylight. It is short and pale.

## Day 51

(a) A calf has the white face of both its parents. (b) A geranium in a dim hall has long, pale stems. (c) Two pumpkin kinds grow in a garden. Big weighs more than small in every plot. Both weigh more in sun. (d) A hen runs to the barn at a rattle. Incubator chicks do not.

## Day 52 (exit ticket, on its own sheet)

Same-kind mint cuttings trace to parent M, which has toothed leaves. Every cutting keeps toothed leaves. Age, water, light, soil, container size and temperature are matched. Spaced cuttings reach 14 and 15 centimetres. Crowded cuttings reach 8 and 9.
