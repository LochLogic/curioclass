# Day 49: Do the records agree?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the crowding record card for each pair. Keep the day 48 graphs out for each group. Check allergies and look for mold; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil, living things and the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun; in unsafe weather the record is read indoors. An adult prechecks the weather, goes with the helpers and handles any ice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud. Children write [[s03-P49-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P49-2]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

The adult empties the rain jar and the tray. Wash hands in two waves at two sinks, about 7 children a sink, 12 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 48's exit tickets, s03-X48, and the day 48 written tasks, each with one short note. Error C, 2 minutes: draw a pot card on the board. Pots M and N: same seeds, soil, water and light. M has 2 plants, tall. N has 7 plants, short. Pairs cross out what was kept the same, leaving the one thing changed, and say what explains the heights. If many tickets showed error C, say aloud: only the crowding was changed.

#### 14 min. Compare the records

Say aloud that the card is a supplied record, invented for practice, not our pots. Read it aloud, 2 minutes. Pairs write [[s03-P49-3]], 3 minutes. Then they read the second record and write [[s03-P49-4]], 3 minutes. Then they set their day 48 graphs beside the card and write [[s03-P49-5]], 4 minutes. A class result that differs from the card, or a failed pot, is read as it is, and its origin is named. **Ask.** Do the pots show a difference, or can we not tell yet? Expect: whichever the class graphs show. A card that differs is no reason to change our readings. Pairs share one, 2 minutes.

#### 8 min. Revise the arrows

Review of unit 1: a cause and effect arrow says a change makes a difference. In the first color, write crowding, then height. In a second color, keep the arrow, change it or cross it out, by what the records show, [[s03-P49-6]], 5 minutes. Pairs show one arrow, 3 minutes. Watch for an arrow kept with no record named.

#### 5 min. Verdicts

Everyone writes [[s03-P49-7]], 2 minutes. Then hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes. Any child may pass. **Ask.** What did the records let us say? Expect: a verdict with what was seen, a difference, no difference or I cannot tell yet.

#### 6 min. Exit

Read the pots A and B card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X49]]. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: no; the light also differs, so we cannot tell. Error E: "yes, B has more plants, so crowding did it". Error C: "A's plants are taller because their parents were tall". Note letters.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the crowding card's table onto the board. No weather tools: read the dated weather record and write the last row. No class pots, or the plants failed: read the supplied records only, said aloud to be invented, and write a verdict on them.

### At home

**Swap.** No second color: use a pen. No class graphs: use your day 48 readings, or the supplied heights from day 48, said to be invented.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records and the clean-up.

- **10 min. A supplied record.** Do [[s03-P49-3]] and [[s03-P49-4]].
  **Say.** "Does each record show a difference?" Expect: "The first shows one by day 24. The second shows none: the heights match."
- **8 min. Your pots.** Do [[s03-P49-5]].
- **8 min. The arrow.** Do [[s03-P49-6]].
  **Say.** "Would you keep the arrow? What did you see?" Expect: "Keep it if the crowded plants were shorter."
- **4 min. Verdict.** Do [[s03-P49-7]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X49]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs do the first record and the class pots; the class hears the second. Never cut the no-difference record, the arrow or the exit. Longer: write what a record would need to show for crowding to make no difference.

### Words and supports

Supplied record, verdict, shows, cannot tell yet, crowded, spaced. Frames: "The record shows ___." "I cannot tell yet, because ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may read the table aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 50 takes two things from this exit in its 4-minute Return and repair. Error E, 2 minutes: name the second difference between the two pots. Error C, 2 minutes: cross out every difference but the one changed in a pot card. Error E reads: yes, B has more plants. Error C reads: A's plants are taller because their parents were tall. Then children read four short cases.
