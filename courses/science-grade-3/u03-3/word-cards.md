# Surroundings word cards (day 47)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Six word cards, each with a small line drawing.

- water: a watering can.
- light: a sunny window.
- space: two pots, one with many plants and one with few.
- soil: a hand holding soil.
- food: a bowl of seeds beside a bird.
- what an animal learns: a dog sitting beside a child's open hand.

The surroundings are the things around a plant or an animal.
