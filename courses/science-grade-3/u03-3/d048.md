# Day 48: Crowded pots and spaced pots

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set a ruler, a tray, a cup of water and safety glasses at each group, with its two radish pots and a pot record. Print a tally and graph sheet for each child. Label each pot C or S. Check allergies and look for mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. Measure without uprooting. The adult fills the cups and wipes spills at once, away from the lamp. Safety glasses stay on until the cups are emptied.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

The adult pours the same cup on each pot, group by group. Children write [[s03-P48-1]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

The adult empties the cups. Wipe the trays. Wash hands in two waves at two sinks, about 7 children a sink, 12 seconds each.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 47's exit tickets, s03-X47, and the written tasks. Error E, 2 minutes: show hydrangea pictures A and B; pairs name one condition the two pictures do not keep the same. If many tickets showed error E, say aloud: a picture shows a difference, not its cause.

#### 3 min. Recap

The fair test, as in unit 2: change one thing, keep the rest the same, watch one thing. Show the day 18 plan table on the board. Pairs write [[s03-P48-2]], 2 minutes. **Ask.** What did we change on day 18? Expect: the space between the seeds, about 1 cm and about 4 cm.

#### 12 min. Measure the pots

The ruler goes round each group of five. Each child reads two or three plants, about 40 seconds each, to the whole centimetre from the soil, never uprooting, and the group writes each reading, [[s03-P48-3]]. That is 8 minutes for 12 plants. Then check each pot's mark, C or S, 2 minutes. Wipe the rulers, 1 minute. A result is not presumed: a pot that failed, or shows no difference, is reported as it is.

#### 10 min. Graph and tally

Put every group's readings on the board, 2 minutes. Set three bands for both pots. Each group tallies both pots in the same bands, 3 minutes. Each child draws one graph, pot C or pot S, and a partner draws the other, 5 minutes, [[s03-P48-4]]. Same bands under both graphs, so the bars compare.

#### 7 min. What do the readings show?

Groups pin their graphs side by side, 1 minute. Pairs write [[s03-P48-5]], 3 minutes. **Ask.** What was the one thing changed? Expect: the space between the plants. **Ask.** Do the readings show why? Expect: no; they show these pots only. Watch for "the shorter plants were born smaller": error C. If many do, say aloud: the seeds came from one packet; only the space was changed.

#### 6 min. Exit

Read the pots X and Y card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X48]]. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: the crowded plants were shorter; the number of plants was the one change; crowding made a difference for these pots. Error C: "the shorter plants were born smaller". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the pot record onto the board. No class pots, or the plants failed: use a supplied record, said aloud to be invented. Crowded pot: 4, 5, 4, 3, 5 and 4 cm. Spaced pot: 7, 8, 7, 8, 6 and 7 cm. Label it supplied beside the class record.

### At home

**Swap.** No radish pots: use two small pots of any seedlings, or the supplied heights above, said to be invented. No squared paper: draw squares.

**Skip.** The living records, the clean-up and the groups.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Do [[s03-P48-2]].
  **Say.** "What was changed? What stayed the same?" Expect: "Only the space between the plants. The seeds, water, light and soil were the same."
- **12 min. Measure.** Do [[s03-P48-3]].
- **10 min. Graph.** Do [[s03-P48-4]].
- **5 min. What it shows.** Do [[s03-P48-5]].
  **Say.** "Do the readings show why?" Expect: "No. They show these pots only."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X48]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups graph one pot each and swap graphs. Never cut the one change, the same bands or the exit. Longer: write what a third pot, spaced farther, would need to keep the same.

### Words and supports

Crowded, spaced, changed, kept the same, watched, band. Frames: "We changed ___." "We kept ___ the same." "The readings show ___ and do not show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may read the ruler aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 49 takes error C from this exit in its 2-minute Return and repair: cross out every difference but the one changed in a pot card, then say what explains the heights. Error C reads: the shorter plants were born smaller. Then pairs set the class graphs beside a supplied dated record and decide what it supports.
