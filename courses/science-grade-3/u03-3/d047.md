# Day 47: What a picture shows

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the word cards for each pair and a tally and graph sheet for each child. Pin the hydrangea pictures face down. Keep the lettuce card in reach. Check allergies and look for mold; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P47-1]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 46's exit tickets, s03-X46. Error F, 2 minutes: on the lettuce card, circle the size that differs, [[s03-P47-2]]. The graph labels, 2 minutes: label one graph and match each bar to its tally, [[s03-P47-3]]. If many tickets showed error F, say aloud: young can be alike in one trait and different in another.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review of grade 2: a fair plant comparison changes one thing and keeps the rest the same. Read the bean record aloud. Children write [[s03-P47-4]], 2 minutes. Hear two of the rotating six, 30 seconds each. A miss gets two cards on the board: window and dark box, with pot, soil and water under both. No test is run again.

#### 10 min. Hook: the hydrangeas again

Turn the pictures over: same kind of bush, two gardens. Read back two of the class's day 37 ideas, 1 minute. Then read the day 47 captions aloud: a garden page says the soil is not the same and the flower color follows it. It says nothing about sun, water or age. We do not teach why. [[steps:s03-K47-1]] Children write [[s03-K47-1]], 3 minutes. **Ask.** What does the page tell us, and what does it leave out? Expect: soil differs and color follows it; sun, water and age are not said. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 8 min. What does a photograph show?

Teach: a picture shows what is there, not why. [[steps:s03-K47-2]] Pairs write [[s03-K47-2]], 3 minutes. Then pairs list two other things that could differ between the gardens, [[s03-P47-5]], 3 minutes. **Ask.** What else could differ between two gardens? Expect: sun, water, age, space. Say aloud: we believe the soil because the page reports it, not because the pictures show it. Watch for "the pictures prove it": error E. If many do, say aloud: a picture shows a difference, not its cause.

#### 5 min. Name the surroundings

Hold up the six word cards. [[steps:s03-K47-3]] Children write [[s03-K47-3]], 2 minutes. Pairs touch the card for each thing on their list, 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Question board

Read the board's questions aloud, 2 minutes, and write G, R or S beside each. Each child copies one S question, [[s03-P47-6]], 3 minutes.

#### 5 min. Pairs ask

Each pair writes one question about surroundings that a pot could answer, [[s03-P47-7]], 3 minutes. Post them unedited, 1 minute. Read three aloud, 1 minute.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the rosebush card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X47]]. Kneel by two of the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Look for: no; water, soil, age or space could differ; a test changes one thing. Error E: "yes, you can see the yard made it taller". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the six words on the board and describe the two bushes aloud. No class plants: skip the living records.

### At home

**Swap.** No pictures: draw one blue bush and one pink bush. No cards: write the six words on a page.

**Skip.** The living records, the board and the repair.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Do [[s03-P47-4]].
- **10 min. Two gardens.** Do [[s03-K47-1]] and [[s03-K47-2]].
  **Say.** "What can a picture show? What can it not show?" Expect: "It shows blue and pink flowers. It cannot show why."
- **8 min. Surroundings.** Do [[s03-K47-3]] and [[s03-P47-5]].
- **5 min. A pot question.** Do [[s03-P47-7]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X47]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: do [[s03-K47-2]] as a class and skip the board. Never cut the page against the picture, the word surroundings or the exit. Longer: draw a third garden and name three things that could differ from the other two.

### Words and supports

Surroundings, picture, shows, cause, kind. Word cards: water, light, space, soil, food, what an animal learns (word-cards.md). Frames: "The picture shows ___. It does not show ___." "Something else that could differ is ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 48 takes error E from this exit in its 2-minute Return and repair: name one condition the two photographs do not keep the same. Error E reads: yes, you can see the yard made it taller. Then groups measure and graph the class radish pots, and say what was changed.
