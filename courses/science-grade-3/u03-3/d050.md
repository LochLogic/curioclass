# Day 50: Four cases

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the four cases for each pair and a claim sheet for each child. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil, living things and the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun; in unsafe weather the record is read indoors. An adult handles any ice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud. Children write [[s03-P50-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P50-2]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 49's exit tickets, s03-X49, and the day 49 written tasks, each with one short note. Error E, 2 minutes: on the board, pot S has 3 plants on a sunny sill and is tall; pot T has 9 plants in a dark closet and is short. Pairs name the second difference. Error C, 2 minutes: pots G and H have the same seeds, soil, water and light; G has 3 plants and is taller, H has 9 and is shorter. Pairs cross out what was kept the same and say what explains the heights. If many tickets showed error E or C, say aloud: change one thing, and keep the rest the same.

#### 16 min. Four short cases

Read each card aloud, 1 minute. Pairs write the trait and the surroundings, 2 minutes. Hear two pairs, 1 minute. Case 1 is [[s03-K50-1]], case 2 [[s03-K50-2]], case 3 [[s03-K50-3]] and case 4 [[s03-K50-4]]. Teach: surroundings can shape a trait, and a learned response is not passed to young. [[steps:s03-K50-1]] [[steps:s03-K50-2]] [[steps:s03-K50-3]] [[steps:s03-K50-4]] Say aloud that case 3 is our retelling of a zoo page.

#### 8 min. A claim, evidence and reason

Review of grade 2, claim, evidence and reason: I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___. Do it together on case 1. [[steps:s03-K50-5]] Children write [[s03-K50-5]], 4 minutes. **Ask.** What does the so part say? Expect: only the water was changed, so the water explains the difference. Hear two of the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Your claim

Each child picks case 3 or case 4 and writes [[s03-P50-3]] on the claim sheet, 4 minutes. Say aloud: some traits depend on the parents and the surroundings together. Read two aloud, 1 minute.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the parrot and houseplant card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X50]]. Ask [[s03-X50b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: (a) no; it heard it and learned it, and a learned sound is not passed to young; (b) too little light is part of its surroundings. Error D: "it inherited saying hello". Error C: "that kind is only ever short". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the four cases from the board. No weather tools: read the dated weather record and write the last row. No claim sheet: write the three blanks on a page.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: read the cases from the page. No claim sheet: write the three blanks on a page.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records and the repair.

- **10 min. Two cases.** Do [[s03-K50-1]] and [[s03-K50-2]].
  **Say.** "What is the trait? What are the surroundings?" Expect: "Height; the water."
- **10 min. Foxes and a dog.** Do [[s03-K50-3]] and [[s03-K50-4]].
  **Say.** "Is sitting at a click passed to young?" Expect: "No. The dog learned it. The puppies do not sit."
- **10 min. A claim.** Do [[s03-K50-5]], then [[s03-P50-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X50]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X50b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs do cases 1 and 4, and the class hears the other two. Never cut the learned case, the claim or the exit. Longer: write a claim for case 2 and say what is not shown.

### Words and supports

Surroundings, trait, learned, evidence, reason. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." "The records do not show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may read each case aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 51 takes two things from this exit in its 4-minute Return and repair. Error D, 2 minutes: set one animal before and after the cue beside its young, who were never trained. Error C, 2 minutes: compare two light rows of one plant kind and name the surroundings. Error D reads: it inherited saying hello. Error C reads: that kind is only ever short. Then pairs sort eight cards: from the parents, from the surroundings, or from both.
