# Day 52: Explain what the records show

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print a claim, evidence and reason sheet for each child. Pin the hydrangea pictures with their captions and the class pot graphs from days 48 and 49. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P52-1]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 51's exit tickets, s03-X51. Error C, 2 minutes: on the board, two rows of one pea kind have the same seeds, soil and water; row 1 is spaced, 11 cm; row 2 is crowded, 6 cm. Pairs cross out what was kept the same and say what explains the heights. Error D, 2 minutes: a hen ignores a bell; after seeds with the bell, she comes at the bell; her chicks, never trained, ignore it. Pairs say what she learned and whether the chicks have it. If many tickets showed error C or D, say aloud: a trait can come from the surroundings, and a learned response is not passed to young.

#### 5 min. The frame

A recap, not a new frame: I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___. Show the kale explanation. Children mark the claim, the evidence and the reason, [[s03-P52-2]], 3 minutes. **Ask.** What does the reason say? Expect: only the water was changed, so the water explains the difference.

#### 12 min. Build one together

Pairs build the explanation of the class pots and the hydrangeas, [[s03-P52-3]], 8 minutes. The claim names a trait and a surrounding. The evidence is the class result as it came out: shorter in the crowded pot, no difference, or I cannot tell yet. The garden page is a sourced fact: the soil differs and the color follows it. The reason says why the pots can show a cause and the two pictures alone cannot. Hear three pairs, 45 seconds each, 2 minutes. Agree one class version on the chart, 2 minutes. **Ask.** What does the evidence let us say, and what does it not? Expect: a claim as strong as the records; no claim about every difference.

#### 8 min. Write your own

Each child writes one explanation of the class pots and the hydrangeas on the claim sheet, [[s03-P52-4]]. This is the section's written task. [[steps:s03-P52-4]] Walk and read three aloud. Dictation is welcome.

#### 4 min. Check with a partner

Partners read each other's and tick the claim, the evidence, the reason and what is not shown, [[s03-P52-5]]. Each partner reads for 1 minute. A missing part is written on the sheet, not argued.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

Read the mint card aloud twice. Alone, on its own sheet, [[s03-X52]]. Ask [[s03-X52b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: the matched spacing comparison supports an environmental effect; toothed leaves from the recorded parent show inherited likeness; spacing does not explain every difference. Error C: "spacing explains every difference in the plants". Other slips get a descriptive note. Note letters on the record sheet; day 53 takes the commonest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the claim frame on the board. No class pots, or the plants failed: use a supplied result, said aloud to be invented: crowded 5 cm, spaced 8 cm. Label it supplied.

### At home

**Swap.** No class pots: use your own pots, or the supplied result above, said to be invented. No claim sheet: write the three blanks on a page.

**Skip.** The living records, the repair and the partner check.

- **5 min. The frame.** Do [[s03-P52-2]].
  **Say.** "Which part is the reason?" Expect: "Only the water was changed, so the water explains it."
- **10 min. Build one.** Do [[s03-P52-3]].
  **Say.** "Why can the pots show a cause when two pictures cannot?" Expect: "The pots changed one thing and kept the rest the same."
- **12 min. Write your own.** Do [[s03-P52-4]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X52]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X52b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: build the explanation as a class and write only the claim and the evidence. Never cut the reason, what is not shown or the exit. Longer: write what a second change to the pots would tell us, and what would then have to stay the same.

### Words and supports

Claim, evidence, reason, not shown, surroundings, inherited. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." "The records do not show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 53 begins section 3.4 and takes this exit's commonest letters in 2 minutes. Error C, 2 minutes: compare the two spacing rows of one family and cross out every difference but spacing. Error C reads: spacing explains every difference. Then children revise the class life-cycle and trait models from the living records.
