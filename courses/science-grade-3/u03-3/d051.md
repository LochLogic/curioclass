# Day 51: Parents, surroundings or both

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the sorting cards for each pair. Keep the resemblance chart on the wall, with a second-color pen beside it. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P51-1]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 50's exit tickets, s03-X50, and the day 50 written tasks, each with one short note. Error D, 2 minutes: on the board, a dog does not sit at the click; after treats it does; its puppy, never trained, does not. Pairs say what the dog learned and whether the puppy has it. Error C, 2 minutes: two rows of one bean kind, row 1 in sun, 13 cm, and row 2 in shade, 7 cm. Pairs name the surroundings that differ. If many tickets showed error D or C, say aloud: a learned response is not passed to young, and the shade changed the height.

#### 3 min. Recap

A trait can come from the parents, from the surroundings, or from both. Children finish [[s03-P51-2]], 1 minute. **Ask.** Can a photograph alone tell us which? Expect: no; we need a record or a pattern.

#### 15 min. Sort the cards

Pairs sort cards A to D, [[s03-P51-3]], 7 minutes. Then cards E to H, [[s03-P51-4]], 8 minutes. Each sort names the record or pattern that shows it. Expect: A parents, B surroundings, C surroundings (learned), D both; E parents, F surroundings, G surroundings (learned); H petal color from the parents and flower number from the surroundings. Watch for "B is from the parents": error C. Watch for "G was born knowing it": error D. If many do, use the repair lines above.

#### 8 min. Check together

Pairs write [[s03-P51-5]], 3 minutes. Hear four pairs defend one sort, 45 seconds each, 3 minutes; the class asks which record supports it. Say aloud: photographs alone cannot assign a cause. **Ask.** Which cards needed both? Expect: D, and H for its two traits.

#### 5 min. Class chart

Each pair writes one line, [[s03-P51-6]], 3 minutes. Pairs add their line to the resemblance chart in a second color, under parents, surroundings or both, 2 minutes.

#### 8 min. Exit

Read the four cards aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X51]]. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: (a) parents; (b) surroundings; (c) both, with the two reasons written (the kinds differ; sun changes both); (d) surroundings, learned. Error C: "(b) is from the parents" or "(c) is only from the parents". Error D: "(d) the hen was born knowing the rattle". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: the card lines are on the student page. No chart: use a page with three headings.

### At home

**Swap.** No chart: use a page with three headings.

**Skip.** The living records, the repair and the chart.

- **5 min. Recap.** Do [[s03-P51-2]].
- **10 min. Cards A to D.** Do [[s03-P51-3]].
  **Say.** "Where did the trait come from? What shows it?" Expect: "The trout's spots match its parents, so parents. The bird learned it, so surroundings."
- **10 min. Cards E to H.** Do [[s03-P51-4]].
- **5 min. Check.** Do [[s03-P51-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X51]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort cards A to D together and E to H in pairs. Never cut the record named for each sort, the both cards or the exit. Longer: write a new card that needs both, and say why.

### Words and supports

From the parents (inherited), from the surroundings, both, learned, record, pattern. Frames: "This is from ___, because ___." "A photograph alone cannot show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may read each card aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 52 takes two things from this exit in its 4-minute Return and repair. Error C, 2 minutes: cross out every difference but the one changed, then say what explains the weights. Error D, 2 minutes: set one animal before and after the cue beside its young, who were never trained. Error C reads: (b) is from the parents. Error D reads: the hen was born knowing the rattle. Then children build and write an explanation.
