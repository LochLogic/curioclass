# Exit cards, days 98 to 102

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read each card aloud once.

## Day 98

Invented for practice. (a) Fenmoor had one cold, rainy day in July. Pia says all of Fenmoor's summer is cold. Is she right? Say why. (b) Fenmoor's winter log: cold, cold, snow, cold, mild, cold. What weather should Fenmoor expect in winter? Use the word usually.

## Day 99

Invented for practice. Ashby, six autumn days. Rain: yes, no, yes, no, yes, blank. Wind: west, west, north, west, east, blank. (a) How many rainy days are written? (b) The last row is blank. Was it a dry day? Why?

## Day 100

Invented for practice. (a) A town had rainy days in three months: May 9, June 3, July 6. Draw a picture graph or a bar graph. Write the key or scale first. (b) A bar graph counts 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10. A bar reaches the fourth line above 0. How many rainy days?

## Day 101

Invented for practice. Brightwater has ten autumn rows: 8 cool days and 2 mild days. Its ten winter rows: 9 cold days and 1 cool day. Compare the two seasons. What will tomorrow likely be in autumn? What could still happen?

## Day 102

Invented for practice. Ridgeway's winter record has 27 cold days and 3 warm days. (a) One winter day was warm. What is a Ridgeway winter day usually like? (b) In another town's 24-day record, 15 days were odd. Does the town change its mind about the usual? Say why.
