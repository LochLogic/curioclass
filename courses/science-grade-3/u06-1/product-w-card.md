# Product W card (day 101, revised on day 102)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read aloud once. Each child works alone on paper, with squared paper and a copy of the seasonal expectation model.

## Lake station records

Seasons are in this order: spring, summer, autumn, winter. Each season is a sample of 30 days.

- Average temperature, supplied: 18, 24, 30, 22 degrees Celsius.
- Days with rain or snow: 10, 4, 2, 8.
- Wind from most often: west, south, east, north.

## Your task

Put all three measures in a table. Graph the days with rain or snow. Count by 2 on the side. Label both sides. Compare two seasons using all three measures. Write a seasonal expectation that uses all three measures. Say how sure you are. Write what could still happen.
