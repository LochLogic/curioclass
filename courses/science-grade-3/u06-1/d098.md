# Day 98: Can we tell the season?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick four class-log pages from four months (cold, warm, windy, rainy) with headings covered, and a fifth face down. Sort 24 pages into four piles by month, six each; a station packet page fills any season the log has not reached. Draw the season board: four empty boxes. Highlight one wind row for each child who missed day 97's retrieval.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back the exit tickets, s05-X97 and s05-X97b. Children who missed the retrieval find the wind box in a highlighted row, 2 minutes. The rest tell a neighbour the wind on the newest page. Nobody is shown as a mistake.

#### 10 min. Hook

Stand the four covered pages in view. Pairs talk 3 minutes: what do you notice? Each child writes a guess for the face-down page, [[s06-P98-1]], 2 minutes. Reveal the four dates: they come from four seasons. Turn over the fifth page, read its date, and ask whose guess would change, 2 minutes. **Ask.** No two days are alike. Can we still tell the season? Expect: "Maybe. Pages from the same time of year look alike." Any child may pass.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Weather watch, from earlier years. Pairs find one earlier log entry and write its date, temperature, rain or snow, wind and sky, [[s06-P98-2]]. A miss matches the symbol cards during the weather record.

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P98-3]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 12 min. Worked example

4 minutes: lay one pile of six pages; read each date, temperature, rain or snow and wind. **Ask.** What stays the same about these days, though no two match? Expect: "Usually ___, because most pages say ___." Write three new words: season (spring, summer, autumn, winter; autumn is also called fall), usual, and expect, to look for it and never be sure. Fill that box with usually. Point to one page that differs: one day is not a season. 5 minutes: pairs take a pile you did not model and fill its row, [[s06-P98-4]]. 3 minutes: one pair from each pile reads its row, 40 seconds each. Say aloud which pages come from the packet.

#### 7 min. Question board

Write each notice as a question. Group them: what repeats (days 99 to 102), whether one year describes everywhere (103 to 107), how people get ready for the dangerous kind (108 to 112). Put up the town's weather warning from this year or last (the county record, if the class has not seen one) as one notice; sharing about it is a child's choice. Each child writes one question, [[s06-P98-5]].

#### 4 min. Exit E98

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X98]]. Look for: no, one day is not a season, or look at many winter days. Error B says yes, she saw it, or winter is the same every day. Note the letter on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No class log: use packet pages. No printer: draw the table on the board. One projector: show the pages in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No class log: the parent writes four dated weather notes, invented, from four months. No thermometer: write the sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The pairs, the helpers and the question board.

- **10 min. Four notes.** Read four notes, dates covered. The child does [[s06-P98-1]].
  **Say.** "No two days are alike. Can we still tell the season?" Expect: "Maybe. Notes from the same time of year look alike."
- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside. The child does [[s06-P98-3]].
- **15 min. One season.** Read six notes from one season. The child does [[s06-P98-4]].
  **Say.** "What stays the same about this season?" Expect: "Usually ___, because most notes say ___."
- **5 min. A question.** The child does [[s06-P98-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X98]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: pairs fill one row; never cut the exit or the word usually. Longer: write one thing in the pile that does not repeat, and why it does not break the pattern.

### Words and supports

Season, usual, expect, autumn (fall). Frame: "In ___ it is usually ___. One day can differ." Read cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 99 opens with Return and repair on error B from this exit, one day taken for the whole season: lay the odd day beside ten season rows. Then groups fill season tables.
