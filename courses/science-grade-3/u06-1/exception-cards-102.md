# Exception cards (day 102)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Pairs get six cards. Each card holds one summer record with one odd day. Ask: does the usual change? Add a "but" box to the model. Six pairs each give one exception.

## Card 1

Summer record, Oakfield: 28 warm days, 2 cool days. One cool day had rain.

## Card 2

Summer record, Harbor Reach: 25 dry days, 5 rainy days. One dry week was in the middle.

## Card 3

Summer record, Cedar Hill: 26 days with west wind, 4 days with north wind. One day had a strong north wind.

## Card 4

Summer record, Millbrook: 24 sunny days, 6 cloudy days. One cloudy day was cold.

## Card 5

Summer record, Rushwater: 27 days of 25 degrees Celsius or more, 3 days below 25. One day was only 19 degrees.

## Card 6

Summer record, Tallgrass: 29 days without rain, 1 day with a storm. One storm cut the day short.

## Rule for changing our minds

Many odd days, not one. How many would it take?
