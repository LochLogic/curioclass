# Key-reading cards (days 99 and 100)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Pairs read each key aloud. A pair that misses gets one card again on day 100.

## Card 1: a picture key

Key: one sun picture is 2 sunny days. The row shows 4 sun pictures and 1 half sun picture. Say how many sunny days the row shows. A half picture is 1 day.

## Card 2: a tick key

Key: one tick on the side is 2 days. The side counts by 2, from 0 up to 12. A bar stops at the top mark. Say how many days the bar shows.

## Card 3: say the key first

Before you read a graph, say its key. Finish the sentence. One ______ is ______ days.
