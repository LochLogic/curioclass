# Day 99: Season tables

## Teacher page

### Before class

Fill a dated rows sheet for spring and for summer, 30 rows each, from the station packet, leaving unwritten boxes blank. Mark 30 autumn and 30 winter rows on the wall record. Choose ten winter rows that are not all alike, one box blank; write them on the board. Copy each season's supplied average from the packet.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 98's pages, s06-P98-1, s06-P98-2, s06-P98-3, s06-P98-4 and s06-P98-5, and the exit tickets, s06-X98. Error B, 5 minutes: beside the ten winter rows put one page that differs from the rest. Count the rows that match and the one that differs, [[s06-P99-1]]. Say: a season is what most rows say; one odd day is one row. If many tickets showed error B, say aloud: one day is not a season.

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P99-2]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 5 min. Retrieval for day 100

Math review. Pairs read key cards 1 and 2 aloud and answer, [[s06-P99-3]]: one picture is 2 days; one tick is 2 days. Walk and listen. No scale is taught today. A pair that misses is noted; day 100 gives it card 3.

#### 24 min. Season tables

5 minutes: model one row from the ten winter rows. Count the days with rain or snow, marking each. Say each wind and mark the most frequent. Copy the supplied average; never work one out. A blank box stays blank: say "not recorded". 14 minutes: eight groups, two to a season. Autumn and winter read the wall record; spring and summer read their sheet. Each child counts 30 rows into the season table, [[s06-P99-4]]. Hear six children from the rotating six read their rain count and most frequent wind, 1 minute each, one at a time; the record names who. The rest agree one group table. 5 minutes: the two groups for a season compare tables, recheck any difference and tick the sheet.
**Ask.** A box is blank. Was it dry? Expect: "We do not know. Blank means not recorded." Watch for error B: blanks counted as dry days.

#### 4 min. Exit E99

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X99]]. Ask [[s06-X99b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) 3 rainy days; (b) we do not know, blank means not recorded. Error B says dry, because nothing was written. Error x counts the rows, 6, not the rainy days. Note the letters.

#### 2 min. Question board

Children say a notice from the tables; write it as a question. Any child may pass.

### Other rooms

No class record or packet: write 30 invented rows on the board, said to be invented. No printer: write the ten rows and key cards on the board. One projector: show the rows.

### At home

**Swap.** No class record: use a month of dated weather notes, or 30 rows the parent invents, said to be invented. No thermometer: write the sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The groups, the helpers and the question board.

- **5 min. Keys.** The child does [[s06-P99-3]].
  **Say.** "What does one sun stand for?" Expect: "2 sunny days; half a sun is 1."
- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside; never look at the sun. The child does [[s06-P99-2]].
- **20 min. A season row.** The child does [[s06-P99-4]] from 30 rows.
  **Say.** "This box is blank. Was it a dry day?" Expect: "We do not know. Blank means not recorded."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X99]], answered alone. Then ask [[s06-X99b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: groups count rain and wind only; never cut the blank boxes or the exit. Longer: write one question the table cannot answer.

### Words and supports

Table, row, blank, not recorded, rainy day, wind direction. Frame: "In ___ there were ___ days with rain or snow." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 100 opens with Return and repair. Error B on this exit, a blank taken as dry: a highlighted blank box said "not recorded". Error x, rows counted for rainy days, gets the same box beside the rain column. Pairs that missed the key cards read card 3. Then groups turn their rainy-day counts into graphs.
