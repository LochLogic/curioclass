A blank answer is an incomplete part, not a letter. Mark the rotating six heard each day beside the name. Days 98 to 114 and day 117 are not scored. The End columns hold the three scored records. After day 115, score the revised product W (End 1 to 6) and the revised product C (End 7 to 12). After day 116, score the revised product H (End 13 to 18). A missing component, or a record with no feedback and revision, is unscored, never zero. Put the date in any cell tried again later.
