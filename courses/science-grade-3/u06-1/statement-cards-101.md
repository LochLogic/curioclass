# Statement cards (day 101)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Pairs sort each card: the record shows it, or it goes beyond the record. Use a table of 10 spring rows for the town of Dunmere: 7 windy rows, 3 calm rows.

## The cards

1. Tuesday was windy in Dunmere.
2. Seven of the ten spring rows say windy.
3. Every spring day in Dunmere is windy.
4. Wednesday was calm. Wednesday was one row.
5. Spring in Dunmere is usually windy.
6. Next Monday will be windy.
7. Three of the ten spring rows say calm.
8. Dunmere never has a calm day in spring.

## The sort

Shows: cards ______. Goes beyond: cards ______.

One odd day is not a season.
