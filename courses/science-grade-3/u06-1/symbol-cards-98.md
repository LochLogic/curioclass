# Weather symbol cards (day 98)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Used only by children who missed the retrieval. Match each symbol to its label. Use them while the helper pair reads the weather.

## The symbols

1. A yellow circle with short lines around it. Label: sunny.
2. A gray cloud. Label: cloudy.
3. A cloud with slanted lines under it. Label: rain.
4. A cloud with small stars under it. Label: snow.
5. An arrow with a curly tail. Label: wind.
6. A thermometer with a bulb at the bottom. Label: temperature.

## The labels to match

Sunny. Cloudy. Rain. Snow. Wind. Temperature.

## The reading box

Date: ______ Temperature: ______ Rain or snow: ______ Wind from: ______ Sky: ______

A blank box means not recorded. It does not mean dry.
