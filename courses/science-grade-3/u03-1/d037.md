# Day 37: Alike, yet not the same

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check allergies the day before; if mold shows, follow the safety page. Hand back day 36's exit tickets. Use the papers from day 36's last ten minutes to seat children for talk. Print a plant sheet and a mealworm sheet for each child. Stand the class seedlings beside the seed-packet picture. Lay out a ruler, a tray, a cup and glasses for each group. Keep the hydrangea pictures face down.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. Mealworms, bran and the habitat are looked at through the lid, never handled, never released. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar; the thermometer is non-glass and no one looks at the sun. Wipe spills at once, away from sockets.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads the thermometer to the nearest mark, the rain jar, the direction card and the sky. Children write [[s03-P37-1]]. Below zero is said in words.

#### 2 min. Living records

The two observers date the living record; everyone writes [[s03-P37-2]].

#### 8 min. Hook: alike, yet not the same

Pairs look at their seedling beside the seed-packet picture and write [[s03-P37-3]]. Show the two bush pictures, invented for practice, with the caption "Same kind of bush. Two gardens. Look closely." Give no cause. **Ask.** How can plants of one kind look alike yet differ? Hear four voices from the rotating six; any child may pass. Each child writes an idea, [[s03-P37-4]]. Keep them unmarked.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review: pollen is not a seed (grade 2); flowers help make seeds and fruits keep them safe (grade 1). Pairs find sowing and pollination in the class's dated record, [[s03-P37-5]]. A miss gets a brown seed arrow and a yellow pollen arrow drawn apart.

#### 10 min. Look and measure

Six groups of five. The sealed habitat goes from group to group, 1 minute each, looked at through the lid, never opened. In its other 4 minutes a group measures its plant pair to the whole centimeter from the ruler's zero, without uprooting: two children hold and read the first plant, swap for the second, then all write [[s03-P37-6]]. If a pot or the habitat fails, an adult removes it and the dated photograph cards stand in, said aloud to be supplied and weaker than seeing.

#### 8 min. First life-cycle drawing

Worked example, 3 minutes: [[steps:s03-K37-1]] Children fill in the frame. Then each child draws a class plant's life and a mealworm's life on the sheets, [[s03-P37-7]]: boxes for stages, arrows that mean then, solid for a stage seen, dotted for one not seen. Nothing is marked wrong. Sheets stay in the folder; the first color is kept. Watch for an arrow from the last box back to the first: error A.

#### 3 min. Question board

Notices become questions, [[s03-P37-8]]. Post them unedited in three columns: growth, resemblance, surroundings.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Tip cups into the tray, wipe rulers and trays, and wash hands in two waves at two sinks.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the cards once. Alone, [[s03-X37]]. Six children from the rotating six say theirs to the teacher, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes in all; the other 24 write. Look for: egg, chick, hen; the arrow means then. Error A: "the hen turns back into the same egg". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the boxes on the board. No habitat: use the photograph cards; every box is dotted, and the class says so.

### At home

**Swap.** No class plant: use any plant and its seed packet. An adult picks a safe, non-toxic plant, checks allergies, and everyone washes hands after. No habitat: use a picture of a mealworm and call it pictured. No ruler: skip the measure.

**Skip.** The weather, the groups and the question board.

- **8 min. Look.** Put a plant beside its packet picture. Do [[s03-P37-3]].
  **Say.** "How is it like the picture? How is it different?" Expect: "Both have green leaves; this one is shorter."
- **7 min. The bush.** Show two pictures of one bush in two colors. Do [[s03-P37-4]].
- **15 min. Draw.** Do [[s03-K37-1]], then [[s03-P37-7]] for the plant.
  **Say.** "What does your arrow mean?" Expect: "Then, a later stage."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X37]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: draw the plant only. Never cut the then arrow, the solid and dotted boxes or the exit. Longer: add the day on every box.

### Words and supports

Life cycle, stage, then, seen, pictured, solid, dotted. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "We saw ___. We did not see ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may describe the habitat aloud; raised-outline cards are available.

### Tomorrow

Day 38 opens with the weather record, then 2 minutes of Return and repair. Error A, "the hen turns back into the same egg", sits beside the hen laying a new egg and the chick a new animal, redrawn in two colors. Day 38 then adds the flowers and seeds to the plant.
