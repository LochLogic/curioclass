# Day 40: Butterflies and frogs

## Teacher page

### Before class

Keep the beetle stage cards beside the class chart. Print the butterfly and frog cards and a cycle chart for each pair. Shuffle each set of eight cards. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The mealworm habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. Wipe spills at once, away from sockets.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Pairs measure their plant pair, [[s03-P40-1]]; the rest look at the habitat.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 39's exit tickets, s03-X39. Error A, 2 minutes: redraw one ant's life in two colors, the adult in one and the new ant in the other, with the arrow from the adult's eggs to the new ant. If many tickets on day 39's exit showed error A, say aloud: the adult lays eggs, and the egg is a new animal.

#### 6 min. Hook

Pairs sort eight shuffled cards into two animals, [[s03-P40-2]]. Give no names. **Ask.** How do you know which cards go together? Expect: same body shapes, same colors. Hear four voices from the rotating six; any child may pass.

#### 12 min. Teach two more cycles

Show the butterfly cards. [[steps:s03-K40-1]] Terms: egg, larva, pupa, adult, same as the beetle. **Ask.** Which stage of the butterfly is the caterpillar? Expect: the larva. Show the frog cards. [[steps:s03-K40-2]] The frog has egg, tadpole, froglet, adult, and no pupa. **Ask.** Do all animals have the same stages? Expect: no; the frog has no pupa. Cards are pictures; every box is dotted, said aloud.

#### 10 min. Compare on a chart

Pairs fill the chart for the butterfly and the frog, [[s03-P40-3]]. Each row is a stage; leave the die box empty, since no card shows it. Walk and read two charts aloud. Watch for an arrow from the adult back to the larva: error A.

#### 8 min. Revise your models

In a second color each child adds one idea from today to the plant or the mealworm drawing, [[s03-P40-4]]. The first color stays. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 2 min. Question board

Each child adds one question, [[s03-P40-5]]. Post them unedited.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the dragonfly cards twice. Alone, [[s03-X40]]. Ask [[s03-X40b]] and [[s03-X40c]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Look for: egg, nymph, adult laid in order; the arrow means then; the adult lays eggs. This ticket has no error letter; lay the cards in a line and count the stages. Note what you see on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw the stages on the board. No habitat: skip it.

### At home

**Swap.** No second color: use pencil in a box. No printer: draw the boxes.

**Skip.** The habitat, the pairs and the question board.

- **8 min. Sort.** Do [[s03-P40-2]] with eight pictures.
- **12 min. Two cycles.** Do [[s03-K40-1]] and [[s03-K40-2]].
  **Say.** "Which stage is the caterpillar?" Expect: "The larva; the frog has no pupa."
- **10 min. Chart.** Do [[s03-P40-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X40]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X40b]] and [[s03-X40c]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: chart the butterfly only. Never cut the frog's missing pupa, the dotted boxes or the exit. Longer: add the day for each box the cards allow.

### Words and supports

Butterfly, frog, caterpillar, tadpole, froglet, nymph, stage. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "The frog has no ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 41 opens with weather and living records, then 4 minutes of Return and repair. Error A from day 39's exit gets 2 minutes. The dragonfly cards get 2 minutes: lay them in a line and count the stages. Then the pattern chart, built from every animal and plant we have seen.
