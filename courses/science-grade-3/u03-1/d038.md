# Day 38: A flowering plant's whole life

## Teacher page

### Before class

Count the children who missed item s03-R1 or s03-R2 on day 36: over a third means the whole class takes the support, otherwise a small group does. Print tomato and radish cards for each pair. Fill a small cup for each pair. Check allergies and mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Safety glasses on for watering. An adult fills the cups and wipes spills at once, away from sockets. Wash hands after soil and living things. Mealworms are never handled or released. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s03-P38-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Pairs measure their plant pair and add the same cup of water, [[s03-P38-2]]; the rest look.

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 37's exit tickets, s03-X37. Error A, 2 minutes: redraw a chicken's life in two colours, the hen in one and her new chick in the other, with the arrow from the hen's egg to the chick. A child with no letter writes one thing the class plant did since day 37. If many tickets on day 37's exit showed error A, say aloud: the arrow means then, never back.

#### 10 min. Support: like parents

Review from grade 1: young share features with their parents but are not exact copies. The whole class, or a small group with you while the rest work alone: reread the s03-R1 picture, 2 minutes; match one shared feature and circle one difference on the radish card, [[s03-P38-3]], 4 minutes. **Ask.** How can the seedlings be alike and different? Expect: round leaves alike, heights different; young are not exact copies. Then [[s03-P38-4]], 2 minutes, and a fresh pair, [[s03-P38-5]], 2 minutes.

#### 5 min. Reading the arrows

**Ask.** What does an arrow between two boxes mean? Expect: then, a later stage. [[steps:s03-K38-1]] Read the class plant's own dated record aloud the same way: each day sits on its box, solid where the class saw it.

#### 12 min. Compare two records

Pairs lay the class plant's dated record beside the tomato cards. Teach: birth is the start of a new living thing, and in a flowering plant it starts in a seed. The adult reproduces by making seeds. **Ask.** Where does the arrow from the seeds go? Expect: to a new plant, never back to the dry one. [[steps:s03-K38-2]] Pairs add the pictured stages in dotted boxes, [[s03-P38-6]]. Watch for an arrow from the dry plant back to the seed: error A. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Revise your model

In a second colour, the plant drawing gains the seeds, a new plant labelled apart and the dry plant, [[s03-P38-7]]. The first colour stays.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Tip cups into the tray, wipe rulers and trays, wash hands in two waves at two sinks.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the corn cards aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X38]]. Look for: the ears holding kernels; kernels are seeds that can begin new plants; dotted, a photograph only. Error B: "picking the ears shows new plants were made". Error A: "the dry stalk starts again as the same plant". "Solid, because the photograph is clear" has no letter. Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No cards printed: draw four boxes on the board. No cups: measure only.

### At home

**Swap.** No class plant: use a picture sequence of any garden plant. No ruler: skip the measure.

**Skip.** The weather, the support group and the pairs.

- **8 min. Like parents.** Do [[s03-P38-3]].
  **Say.** "How are they alike? How are they different?" Expect: "Round leaves alike; heights differ."
- **15 min. Seeds start a new plant.** Do [[s03-K38-2]], then [[s03-P38-6]] with three pictures of a plant in order.
  **Say.** "Where does the arrow from the seeds go?" Expect: "To a new plant, not the dry one."
- **7 min. Revise.** Do [[s03-P38-7]] in a second colour.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X38]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the radish card for a ready child. Never cut the seed arrow, the dotted boxes or the exit. Longer: add the day on every dotted box.

### Words and supports

Seed, flower, fruit, birth, reproduce, new plant, dotted. Frames: "The arrow from the seeds goes to ___." "We saw ___. We did not see ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card; a drawing may be dictated.

### Tomorrow

Day 39 opens with living records, then 4 minutes of Return and repair. Error B, "picking the ears shows new plants were made", sits beside a seed beginning to grow, 2 minutes. Error A, "the dry stalk starts again as the same plant", is redrawn with the arrow from the seeds to a new plant, 2 minutes. Then day 39 adds the beetle.
