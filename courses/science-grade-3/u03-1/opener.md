# Section 3.1 opener: different lives, a shared pattern

## What the section does

Five days, 37 to 41. The big question is: "How do living things grow and change, and why do they look like their parents but not exactly?" Day 37 puts the class plants beside their packet picture, then shows two hydrangea pictures in two colours with the captions hidden. Children draw a first life cycle for a class plant and for a mealworm. Day 38 adds the plant's flowers and seeds. Day 39 adds the beetle stages from the sealed habitat. Day 40 adds the butterfly and the frog from cards. Day 41 builds the pattern that every life cycle shares. Each day ends with an exit. Day 41 ends with a ten-minute exit ticket on its own sheet. Day 38 holds a ten-minute support for the readiness look on how young are like their parents.

## How the days run

Each day is 45 minutes. Days 37, 38 and 41 start with the 5-minute weather record. Every day has 2 minutes of living records, and most have a short return and repair. The sealed habitat passes group to group, one minute each. Nobody handles the mealworms, the bran or the habitat. Every child may pass in public talk. The teacher hears the rotating six at each exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A life cycle is the pattern of a kind.** An adult makes new individuals. Later the adult dies. The same individual does not come back.
- **The four events stay the same.** Birth, growth, reproduction and death are in every cycle. Stage names, a pupa or none, and timing differ.
- **A new plant begins in a seed.** Germination is a growth stage, not a birth. Picking a crop is not reproduction.
- **Mealworm beetles, butterflies and ants have egg, larva, pupa and adult.** Frogs and dragonflies change without a pupa.
- **Dates describe these individuals.** They are not life spans for a whole kind.
- **Solid and dotted.** A solid box is a stage the class saw. A dotted box is one only pictured. The arrow means "then".
- **Flowering plants only.** No people and no human reproduction.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** the same individual lives again. Colour the adult and the new individual apart.
- **B:** picking a crop shows new plants. Show a seed that begins to grow.
- **F:** young must be exact copies. Match one feature and circle one difference.

Wrong answers are voiced as "someone might say". No child is the example of a mistake.

## Supplies for the section

Labelled class plants and pots, the sealed mealworm habitat, whole-centimetre rulers, a water tray, a thermometer, a rain jar, a direction card and the dated weather record. The picture sets are the hydrangea pictures, the drawing sheet, the tomato and radish cards, the beetle cards and table, the butterfly and frog cards, the cycle chart, the pattern chart, the exit cards and the moth and poppy sheet.

## A room with nothing

Use the cards and the dated class record only. Every box is dotted, and the class says so. Do not make up what the mealworms look like today.
