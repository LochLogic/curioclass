# Butterfly and frog cards (days 40 and 41)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Photograph cards. Pairs sort the cards that belong to one animal.

## Butterfly

Card A: a tiny egg on a leaf.
Card B: a caterpillar eating a leaf.
Card C: a chrysalis hanging from a twig.
Card D: an adult butterfly on a flower. A second picture shows her laying eggs.

## Frog

Card E: a clump of eggs in water.
Card F: a tadpole with a tail and no legs.
Card G: a froglet with small legs and a short tail.
Card H: an adult frog on a rock. A second picture shows eggs in the water beside her.

The chrysalis card has a pupa-like stage. The frog cards have none. Times on the cards describe these animals.
