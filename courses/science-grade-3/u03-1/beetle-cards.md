# Beetle stage cards (days 39 to 41)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Photograph cards of a mealworm beetle. The class habitat stays sealed.

## Card 1: egg

A tiny pale egg. This card is photograph only. Eggs are too small to see.

## Card 2: larva

The mealworm, long and tan with many small rings. The class may see this stage through the lid.

## Card 3: pupa

A pale, curved case with small bumps. It does not eat or move much.

## Card 4: adult beetle

A dark brown beetle with hard wing covers.

## Card 5: new beetle

The adult laid eggs. An arrow leaves the eggs and points to a box labeled "new beetle".

## Card 6: the adult dies

An adult beetle lying still. Adults die after weeks. This card is photograph only.

Times on any card describe these beetles, not every beetle.
