# Tomato and radish cards, day 38

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Tomato photograph cards (photograph only)

Card A: a tomato seedling with two leaves. Day 12.
Card B: a tomato plant with yellow flowers. Day 60.
Card C: a tomato plant with green fruit, then red fruit. One fruit is cut open to show seeds. Day 90.
Card D: a dry brown tomato plant. Day 130.

The class pairs lay the dated class plant record beside these cards. They add the pictured stages in dotted boxes.

## Radish card (for the support group)

Radish seedlings from one packet, day 6. Seedling A is 3 centimetres tall. Seedling B is 5 centimetres tall. Seedling C is 4 centimetres tall. All three have round leaves.

## Fresh pair (for the support group)

Two sage seedlings. Both have gray-green leaves with a soft fuzz. One is 4 centimetres tall. One is 6 centimetres tall.
