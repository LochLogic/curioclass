# Day 39: The beetle's life

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the beetle cards and the beetle table for each group. Draw a blank chart with the plant and the beetle side by side. Check allergies and look for mold; an adult removes any. Set the habitat where it can pass between six groups.

### Safety and set-up

Mealworms, bran and the habitat are looked at through the lid: no one opens it, handles an insect or releases one. Wash hands after soil and living things. Wipe water spills at once, away from sockets.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Pairs measure their plant pair over the tray, [[s03-P39-1]]; the rest look.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 38's exit tickets, s03-X38. Error B, 2 minutes: two cards, picked ears and a seed beginning to grow; children point to the one that shows a new plant. Error A, 2 minutes: redraw one cycle in two colors with the arrow from the adult's seeds to a new plant. If many tickets on day 38's exit showed error B or A, say aloud: a seed that begins to grow is the new plant. A solid box for a clear photograph has no letter: solid is for a stage the class saw.

#### 8 min. Observe the habitat

**Ask.** What do you see in the box, and what do you not see? Take it after the last group has looked; any child may pass. Six groups of five: the sealed habitat goes group to group, 1 minute each, 6 minutes, through the lid. The other groups lay the four beetle cards in order, [[s03-P39-2]]. Then 2 minutes: mark the beetle table, solid for a stage seen alive today and dotted for the rest, [[s03-P39-3]]. Write none seen where nothing was seen; none seen does not mean it is not there. If no pupa or adult shows, the cards cover it, said aloud to be supplied.

#### 10 min. Teach the animal cycle

Show the cards. [[steps:s03-K39-1]] Terms: egg, larva, pupa, adult, offspring. **Ask.** What does the adult do that starts the next beetle? Expect: it lays eggs. [[steps:s03-K39-2]] Pin each box on the board: solid if seen today.

#### 8 min. Revise your animal model

In a second color each child adds the missing stages to the day 37 mealworm drawing, dotted where unseen, [[s03-P39-4]]. Watch for an arrow from the adult back to the larva: error A. The first color stays. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 5 min. Beside the plant

The class chart puts the plant and the beetle side by side. Pairs write one thing the same, [[s03-P39-5]]. This is a first look; day 41 builds the pattern.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipe trays and rulers and wash hands in two waves at two sinks.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the ant cards aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X39]]. Ask [[s03-X39b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: the adult lays eggs; the egg card is the next ant's birth; the new ant is not the adult. Error A: "the adult ant turns into an egg". "The pupa is the birth" has no letter: birth is the start of the new ant, the egg. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 2 min. Question board

Each child adds one question, [[s03-P39-6]]. Post them unedited.

### Other rooms

No habitat, or no pupa showing: use the beetle cards; every box is dotted, and the class says so. No cards printed: draw four boxes on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No mealworms: use the four beetle cards and say they are pictures. No second color: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The habitat, the groups and the question board.

- **8 min. Order.** Put the four beetle cards in order. Do [[s03-P39-2]].
- **15 min. The cycle.** Do [[s03-K39-1]] and [[s03-K39-2]].
  **Say.** "What does the adult do that starts the next beetle?" Expect: "It lays eggs, and the new beetle is not the adult."
- **7 min. Revise.** Do [[s03-P39-4]] in a second color.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X39]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X39b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the beetle table in pairs, not as a class. Never cut the new beetle box, the dotted egg or the exit. Longer: add a day to each box the cards allow.

### Words and supports

Egg, larva, pupa, adult, offspring, mealworm, seen, pictured. Frames: "First ___, then ___." "We saw ___. We did not see ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may describe the habitat aloud; a photograph card is in hand.

### Tomorrow

Day 40 opens with living records, then 2 minutes of Return and repair. Error A, "the adult ant turns into an egg", is redrawn in two colors, the adult in one and the new animal in the other. Day 41 takes it again if it is still common. Then day 40 adds the butterfly and the frog.
