# Day 41: The same pattern

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the beetle stage cards and the butterfly and frog cards beside the chart, and a whole-centimetre ruler and water tray for each group. Print the pod pictures for each group and the pattern chart for the class. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Check allergies and look for mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun. Wipe spills at once, away from sockets.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look. Children write [[s03-P41-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Pairs measure their plant pair, [[s03-P41-2]]; the rest look.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back two sets. Day 39's exit tickets, s03-X39, 2 minutes: redraw one ant's life in two colours with the arrow from the adult's eggs to the new ant. If many tickets on day 39's exit showed error A, say aloud: the adult lays eggs, and the egg is a new animal. Day 40's exit tickets, s03-X40, 2 minutes: lay the dragonfly cards in a line and count the stages.

#### 3 min. Seed look

Groups open the pod pictures and find the seeds, [[s03-P41-3]]. Give no names. **Ask.** What is in the pod? Expect: seeds that can begin new plants.

#### 10 min. Build the pattern chart

Pin the plant, beetle, butterfly and frog cycles side by side. Each pair adds one row: what is the same in every cycle, [[s03-P41-4]]. Expect: every one starts with a birth, grows, reproduces and dies. Stages and timing differ; the pattern stays. **Ask.** What is the same, though the stages differ? Hear four voices from the rotating six; any child may pass. Watch for an arrow from the last box back to the first: error A.

#### 9 min. Revise your own models

Each child looks at their own plant and animal drawings and checks four things, [[s03-P41-5]]: boxes in order, arrows that mean then, solid for seen and dotted for not seen, and a new living thing apart from the adult. Fix one in a second colour. The first colour stays. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

Hand out the moth and poppy sheet. Alone, [[s03-X41]]. Look for: a moth and a poppy share a pattern; stages differ; the dead moth is not its own egg. Error A: "the dead moth turns into its own egg". Error B returns on day 42. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 2 min. Question board

Each child adds one question, [[s03-P41-6]]. Post them unedited.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four cycles on the board. No habitat: use the cards; every box is dotted, and the class says so.

### At home

**Swap.** No class plant: use any plant and its seed packet. No pods: use a picture of one.

**Skip.** The weather, the groups and the question board.

- **8 min. Pattern.** Draw a plant cycle and an animal cycle. Do [[s03-P41-4]].
  **Say.** "What is the same in both?" Expect: "Both start, grow, make young and die."
- **12 min. Revise.** Do [[s03-P41-5]] with your own drawings.
- **10 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X41]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: build the chart as a class, not in pairs. Never cut the pattern row, the dotted boxes or the exit. Longer: add the timing for each cycle.

### Words and supports

Pattern, stage, birth, reproduce, die, same, different. Frames: "All of them ___." "The stages are different, but ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the chart.

### Tomorrow

Day 42 takes the exit's letters. Error A, "the dead moth turns into its own egg", is redrawn in two colours. Error B returns there. Children also count the days between recorded stages.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
