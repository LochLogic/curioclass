# Day 83: What a find shows, and what we infer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort children into three: the readiness group, from day 81's readiness papers; the retrieval group, from day 82's misses; everyone else. Set the support group cards at the teacher's table. Give each pair its cards.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 82's s05-P82-1, s05-P82-2, s05-P82-3, s05-P82-4 and s05-P82-5, and the ticket s05-X82. If many made error A in day 82's exit (it is dry or treeless now, so it always was), read the pair aloud: "The Arctic has no trees now, so none ever grew there" beside "rooted trunks in the rock show a forest grew there". One child restates the right one; any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Support groups

Readiness group with the teacher, 7 minutes. Card 1 (bare soil beside a planted patch): name what is harmed, draw a border or a way around, say how it helps a plant. A missing harm link: match pictures to needs (card 2). A fix with no benefit: draw a before and after arrow (card 3). Then the fresh litter case, [[s05-P83-1]]. The retrieval group joins for the last 3 minutes and compares the trunk picture with the snowy picture from day 82. Everyone else works in pairs on the extra fossil card, [[s05-P83-2]]. If many missed the day 81 item, the whole class takes the 7 minutes.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P83-6]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 22 min. Interpreting finds

5 minutes: on the trunk picture, draw a solid line to what the find shows (a trunk with roots) and a dotted line to what we infer (a tree grew in soil on land), as in unit 3's boxes. **Ask.** Is that something you see or something you infer? Expect: the roots are seen; a forest is inferred.

6 minutes: 15 pairs sort the eight stump cards, [[s05-P83-3]]. The numbers are from park pages. A piece gives at least the size of the whole. Error F sounds like: it is big, so it was hot.

6 minutes: say the guide-card idea: living things needed then what they need now. That is stated, not proved. Pairs read guide card 1 and fill the guide row, [[s05-P83-4]].

5 minutes: each child takes the day 82 page and, in a second color, marks the model with solid and dotted lines and one change, [[s05-P83-5]]. Keep the first drawing.

#### 3 min. Exit E83

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X83]], 2 minutes. Look for: one thing seen (the print, its size or its place) and one inferred (a pond or slow stream near). Error A says there is no pond now, so never. Error F says the wing size shows heat.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the stump on the board and read the cards aloud; children sort on notebook paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The support groups, the pair sort and the helpers.

- **8 min. Stump.** The child does [[s05-P83-3]].
  **Say.** "Is that something you see, or something you infer?" Expect: the width is seen; the heat is not shown.
- **8 min. Guide.** The child does [[s05-P83-4]].
- **8 min. Mark.** The child does [[s05-P83-5]] on the day 82 page.
- **4 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P83-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X83]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four stump cards and skip the extra card. Never cut the marking or the exit. Longer: write one thing the stump cannot show, and the find needed.

### Words and supports

Show, infer, guide card, solid, dotted. Frame: "The find shows ___. I infer ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 84 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on the commoner of error F (it is big, so it was hot: the stump beside the guide card) and error A (it is dry now, so it never was). Then the same reading goes to a dry valley with whale bones and shells.
