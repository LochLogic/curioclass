# Support group cards (day 83)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Card 1: two corners of a schoolyard

Left: a worn patch of bare soil, hard and gray, where many feet cross. Nothing grows. Right: a planted patch inside a low border of stones. Green seedlings stand in rows.

Ask: What is harmed on the left? Draw a border or a way around for the left patch. Say how it helps a plant.

## Card 2: match the picture to the need

Three small pictures: a seedling bent flat, a seedling standing tall, a watering can. Three need words: room to stand, water, feet kept off. Match each picture to a need.

## Card 3: before and after arrow

Draw an arrow from a picture of the border to a picture of seedlings standing. Write one word on the arrow: keeps ______.

## Fresh case (the last two minutes)

A school garden has seedlings by a bench. Children drop litter on them. The litter covers the leaves. Draw one way to reduce the harm. Say how it helps a plant.
