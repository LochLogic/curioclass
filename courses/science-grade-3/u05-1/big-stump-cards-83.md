# Big Stump drawing and statement cards (day 83)

Each drawing says "schematic, not to scale" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Drawing

A very wide stump of stone-hard wood on a grassy slope. Its top is cut flat. A person stands beside it. The stump is much wider than the person.

## The facts, from park pages

The Big Stump is 12 feet across. It is 38 feet around. It is a stump of a redwood tree turned to stone. It is in the bed of an old lake.

## Eight statement cards

Each pair sorts the cards into three piles: shows, does not show, cannot tell yet.

1. The stump is 12 feet across.
2. The stump is 38 feet around.
3. The tree was at least 12 feet wide at its base.
4. This place was hot and tropical.
5. The tree was 100 feet tall.
6. Only one big tree grew here.
7. The tree was a small bush.
8. The tree grew a set number of years ago.

## The sort

Shows: cards 1, 2 and 3. Does not show: cards 4 and 7. Cannot tell yet: cards 5, 6 and 8.
