# Desert valley cards (day 84)

Each drawing says "schematic, not to scale" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Drawing

A dry valley of sand and bare rock. No plant grows. In the rock wall are rows of large bones. One has the long shape of a skull. Beside them is a layer with flat, round shell prints.

## Guide card

Whales and sea shells live in the sea today. The valley is a desert now.

## Two finds for the table of finds

Find 1: whale bones. The largest bone is 1 meter long. Place found: the valley. Invented for practice.

Find 2: shell prints. Each is 6 centimeters across. Place found: the valley. Invented for practice.
