# Time strip (day 85)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Pinned beside the board: one thin card. It holds one week of our weather log, seven small rows. Beside it stand the five thick layer cards in a tall stack.

The thin card is short. The thick cards each took very long. No numbers are printed.

Say together: A week of weather is a short record. Fossil layers are a long record.
