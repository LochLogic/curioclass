# Regional record: Florissant, Colorado (day 86)

Each drawing says "schematic, not to scale" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A class in another state may use its own state's fossil. The record below is Florissant, Colorado.

## Map

A schematic map of one valley with three dots: A, B and C. Dot A is a shale outcrop. Dot B is a layer with leaf prints. Dot C is the Big Stump.

## Find card A

Prints of small insects in shale. Each is 1 centimeter long. Invented for practice. Guide card: this shale is what is left of the bottom of an old lake.

## Find card B

Prints of leaves. Each is 8 centimeters long. Invented for practice. Guide card: these are leaves of plants that grew on land.

## Find card C

The Big Stump. It is 12 feet across, from a park page. Guide card: it is a redwood stump turned to stone, found near the old lake.

## Three rows

Fill the table of finds for A, B and C: kind, size, place found.

## Two claims

Each pair writes two claims and sorts them as shows or does not show. The three dots do not make the whole region one place.
