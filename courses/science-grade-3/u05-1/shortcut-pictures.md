# Shortcut pictures (day 81 readiness)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Picture 1: before

A school playground. A paved path runs to the gate. Beside the path is a small garden patch with rows of green seedlings. A girl and a boy with backpacks stand at the edge of the patch. They are about to cut across it to reach the gate.

## Picture 2: after

The same patch. A bare line of soil runs across it where they walked. The seedlings along the line are bent flat. The seedlings at the sides still stand tall. The paved path is unchanged.

## Your task

Look at both pictures. Draw one way to reduce the harm to the plants. Say how it helps a plant.
