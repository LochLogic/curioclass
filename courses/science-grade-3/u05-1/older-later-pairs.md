# Older and later pictures (day 82, retrieval)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Six pairs. Each pair shows the older view and the later view of one place. No numbers are printed.

## Pair 1: hillside

Older: a green hillside above a road. Later: the same hillside with a bare brown scar and mud piled on the road.

## Pair 2: canyon

Older: a shallow canyon with a thin stream. Later: the same canyon, much deeper, with steep walls.

## Pair 3: creek bank

Older: a creek bank with grass to the edge. Later: the same bank cut away, with roots hanging out.

## Pair 4: sea cliff

Older: a sea cliff with a small dent at its foot. Later: the same cliff with a large cave at its foot.

## Pair 5: path

Older: a dirt path down a slope. Later: the same path split by a deep gully after one big rain.

## Pair 6: mountain top

Older: a mountain with a sharp, rocky top. Later: the same mountain with a low, round top.

## The sort

Quick changes: pairs 1, 3 and 5. Very slow changes: pairs 2, 4 and 6.

## Fossil caption, read together if the grade 2 notes are missing

A shell print is in a rock. The shell is gone. Its shape stayed in the rock. A fossil tells us a living thing was there long ago.
