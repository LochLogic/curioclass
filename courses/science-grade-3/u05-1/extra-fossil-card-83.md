# Extra fossil card (day 83)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A print of a flat round leaf on a long stalk. The leaf is 15 centimeters across. It is in rock on a sandy hill. A guide card says plants with flat round leaves on long stalks float on calm water.

Fill a row in the table of finds: kind, size, place found. Write one thing the print shows. Write one thing you infer.
