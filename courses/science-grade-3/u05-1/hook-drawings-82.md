# Hook drawings (day 82)

Each drawing says "schematic, not to scale" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Show these with no captions. Say only: This is one place.

## Drawing 1: the trunks

A cut face of gray rock. Five tree trunks stand in it, close together. Each trunk has roots spreading out in the rock below. The trunks are broken off flat at the top.

## Drawing 2: the map

A map of the Arctic Ocean with land around it. One small dot marks an island far in the north. Nothing else is marked.

## Drawing 3: the landscape

A treeless, snowy plain under a gray sky. A few low rocks stick out of the snow. No plant is taller than a pebble.
