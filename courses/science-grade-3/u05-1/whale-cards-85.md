# Whale drawings and eight cards (day 85)

Each drawing says "schematic, not to scale" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Drawing 1: an early whale form

A long, narrow body with a flat tail and two flippers at the front. Two tiny back legs stick out near the tail.

## Drawing 2: a living whale

A large, smooth body with a wide tail and two front flippers. No back legs.

## What the guide says

The early whale form is extinct. None are alive anywhere. Whales are not extinct. Many live in the sea today.

## Eight cards

Each pair sorts them into two piles: extinct (none alive anywhere), or only missing here.

1. An early whale form with tiny back legs. The guide says none are alive anywhere.
2. Elephants. None live in our town. They live in other lands.
3. A spiky leaf kind found only as prints in rock. The guide says no plant like it lives anywhere.
4. Moths. None sit on our window this week. Moths live all over.
5. A track maker kind. The guide says no animal makes tracks like these anywhere today.
6. Bears. None live in this city park. They live in forests elsewhere.
7. A flower kind found only as prints in rock. The guide says none are alive anywhere.
8. Owls. None live in the school yard. Owls live in woods nearby.

## The sort

Extinct: cards 1, 3, 5 and 7. Only missing here: cards 2, 4, 6 and 8.
