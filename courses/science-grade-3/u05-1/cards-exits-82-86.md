# Exit cards, days 82 to 86

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 82

Invented for practice. A leaf print is in a rock on a dry hill. Sam says no plant lived here, because the hill is dry now. Is Sam right? Say why.

## Day 83

Invented for practice. A dragonfly wing print is in rock at the foot of a hill. It is 3 centimetres long. A guide card says dragonflies like this live near ponds and slow streams. Write one thing you see and one thing you infer.

## Day 84

Invented for practice. A desert quarry has prints of broad leaves 20 centimetres long, and bark, in one rock layer. A guide card says trees with leaves like these grow in rainy forests. What do the prints and the card support about this place long ago? What do they not show?

## Day 85

Invented for practice. (a) A landslide turned a rock block upside down. Now a seed-pod layer is on the bottom and a bone layer is on top. Is the seed-pod layer the older one?

(b) A card says a certain shell kind lives in other lakes. None are found in Lake Pine. Is that kind extinct?

## Day 86

Invented for practice. A map marks fossils of freshwater snails, 2 centimetres long, at a western site. It marks land-animal tracks, 9 centimetres long, at an eastern site. Both sites are dry ground today. A guide says these snails lived in fresh water. It says these tracks were made on land. What do the kinds, sizes and places support? What is still uncertain?
