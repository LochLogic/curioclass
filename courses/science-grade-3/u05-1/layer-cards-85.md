# Layer cards (day 85)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Five thick cards, one set for each pair. Each shows a band of rock with a few finds. The teacher says: These were laid down one on another, and no one has moved them. This is the order, oldest at the bottom.

## The five cards, oldest first

1. Clay with moss prints.
2. Sand with feather prints.
3. Gray ash with no fossils.
4. Red clay with prints of stems in rows.
5. Dark soil with grass roots.

## What pairs do

Lay the five cards in order. Say which is older and which is younger. Then flip the whole stack over. Say what you can no longer tell.

## Words

layer, older, younger, undisturbed. Deeper is older only when the layers are undisturbed.
