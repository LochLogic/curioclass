# New-find cards (day 85)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Two cards are face down. Pairs write what each find would change in the model, then turn the card over.

## Card X

A print of the same kind of leaf, found in the layer under the trunks.

## Card Y

A track of a small animal, found in the layer above the trunks.

## Write

This find would change ______ because ______.
