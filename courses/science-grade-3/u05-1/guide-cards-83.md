# Guide cards (day 83)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A guide card says what a living kind needs today. Living things needed then what they need now. So a guide card can tell us about a fossil kind.

## Guide card 1: tree with roots

Needs today: soil for its roots, enough water and enough warm days to grow. A rooted trunk in rock shows a tree grew there, in soil on land.

## Guide card 2: water plant with a flat round leaf

Needs today: calm water to float on. A print of its leaf shows water was there.

## Guide card 3: moss

Needs today: damp ground. A moss print shows damp ground was there.
