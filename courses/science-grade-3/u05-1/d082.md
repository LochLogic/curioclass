# Day 82: One place, long ago and now

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cover the hook drawings. Draw the table of finds on the board. Set find cards 2 and 3 and the older and later pictures at each pair's desk. Hand back day 81's exit tickets. Day 81's readiness papers seat the pairs.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands.

### The day

#### 8 min. Hook

Uncover the drawings one at a time, each marked schematic, not to scale: standing trunks with roots in rock, a map with one far-north dot, a treeless snowy plain. Say only: "This is one place." Pairs talk for 1 minute. The class shares for 2 minutes; any child may pass. Write the notices as questions on the board. Each child writes one idea, [[s05-P82-2]], in 3 minutes. If a child asks how old it is, say plainly: the fossils show these trees grew very long ago, when this land was warm. If a child's home sees it differently, say: "People bring different ideas. In science class we look at what the rocks and fossils show." Do not argue or ask about the family.

#### 4 min. Retrieval

2 minutes: pairs sort the older and later pictures into quick and very slow changes, [[s05-P82-1]]. Read each pair's words that say when: 1 "in one night's storm", 3 "in one flood", 5 "after one big rain"; 2, 4 and 6 "a little each year". 2 minutes: the rotating six each say one grade 2 slow-change note and its source, a picture or the words that say when, 20 seconds each. If notes are missing, read today's fossil caption together. A miss goes to day 83 "Support groups".

#### 12 min. Fossil evidence

3 minutes: say "fossil" (what is left of a living thing from long ago, kept in rock) and "kind, size, place found". Fill row 1 on the board from the trunk drawing. 5 minutes: 15 pairs fill rows 2 and 3 from find cards 2 and 3, [[s05-P82-3]]; the sizes are invented for practice. 4 minutes: six children each read one row aloud, 40 seconds each. **Ask.** Which finds are plants? Expect: all three.

#### 10 min. First past-environment model

Alone, in pencil, in the box on side 2, the past-environment model sheet: draw the place when the plants lived and label each part with its find, [[s05-P82-4]]. **Ask.** Which row of the table made you draw that? Expect: "I drew trunks because find 1 is a trunk with roots." Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Say the land was warm when the trees grew; never say it was always Arctic. Error A sounds like a drawing of snow only: point to find 1.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P82-5]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 3 min. Exit E82

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X82]], 2 minutes. Look for: water drawn with a fish and a shell, each labeled with its print, and a line that water was there long ago. Error A says it was always a dry hill. Note the letter.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Pages go in each child's folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the pictures and the table on the board; answer on notebook paper. No weather tools: read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult draws the three pictures. No thermometer: read a dated weather record, said to be supplied.

**Skip.** The sort of the six pictures, the pair talk and the helpers.

- **6 min. Three pictures.** Show the three drawings. The child does [[s05-P82-2]].
  **Say.** "How can all three be one place?" Expect: long ago a forest grew there; now it is snowy.
- **10 min. Finds.** The child does [[s05-P82-3]].
- **10 min. Draw.** The child does [[s05-P82-4]].
- **4 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P82-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X82]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill row 2 only. Never cut the drawing or the exit. Longer: write one question the table cannot answer yet.

### Words and supports

Fossil, kind, size, place found. Frame: "I drew ___ because of find ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 83 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error A (it is dry or treeless now, so it always was), with the fish and shell prints beside the dry hill. Retrieval misses join the support group. Then children separate what a find shows from what they infer.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
