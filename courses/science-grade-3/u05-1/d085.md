# Day 85: Extinct, and layers in order

## Teacher page

### Before class

Cut the layer cards apart, mix each set and leave off the numbers; keep your own list oldest first. Put two new-find cards face down on each pair's desk.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 84's s05-P84-1, s05-P84-2, s05-P84-3 and s05-P84-4, and the ticket s05-X84. If many made error A in day 84's exit (it is a desert, so a forest could never grow here), read the valley beside the whale bones: bones in the rock show sea water was here. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P85-7]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied. Then pin the one-week strip of the log beside the board.

#### 10 min. Extinct forms

Show an early whale form beside a living whale, marked schematic, not to scale. Read what the guide says: the early form is extinct, none alive anywhere; whales are not extinct. Say "extinct" means gone everywhere, and a guide must say so. 15 pairs sort the eight cards, [[s05-P85-1]], 6 minutes. Take answers for 2 minutes. **Ask.** How do we know a kind is extinct? Expect: a guide says none are alive anywhere. Error B sounds like: none here, so extinct. Point to the card that says they live in other lands.

#### 12 min. Undisturbed layers

4 minutes: stack your five cards oldest first and say "older" and "younger". Say the layers were laid down one on another and no one has moved them; deeper is older only then. 4 minutes: pairs lay their five cards in order, [[s05-P85-2]], then flip the stack and say what they can no longer tell, [[s05-P85-3]]. **Ask.** What would change if the layers were flipped? Expect: we could not say which is older. Error C says the bottom is always older. 4 minutes: pin the thin strip beside the tall stack. A week of weather is a short record; the layers are a long record; print no numbers. Children write [[s05-P85-4]].

#### 10 min. Model revision

7 minutes: each child takes the day 82 page and, in a second color, revises the Arctic model with the trunks in a lower layer, older and younger labeled, [[s05-P85-5]]. Keep the first drawing. A note box says new evidence can change an explanation. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. 3 minutes: Exit E85. Read both questions aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X85c]]. Ask [[s05-X85d]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: we cannot say, since the block was turned; and no, extinct means gone everywhere. Error C says yes, the bottom is always older. Error B says yes, none live in Lake Pine.

#### 3 min. Prediction

Pairs write what each face-down find would change in the model, [[s05-P85-6]], then turn the cards over and read them. 

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Layer cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the whales on the board; make five layer cards from paper; read the eight cards aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw five layers on five slips of paper. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The pair sort, the strip on the wall and the helpers.

- **8 min. Extinct.** The child does [[s05-P85-1]] on four of the cards.
  **Say.** "How do we know a kind is extinct?" Expect: a guide says none are alive anywhere.
- **10 min. Layers.** The child does [[s05-P85-2]] and [[s05-P85-3]].
- **8 min. Model.** The child does [[s05-P85-5]].
- **4 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P85-7]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X85c]], answered alone. Then ask [[s05-X85d]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort four cards and skip the strip. Never cut the layers, the flip or the exit. Longer: write one find that would make the model change.

### Words and supports

Extinct, layer, older, younger, undisturbed, short record, long record. Frame: "The ___ layer is older because ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 86 opens with Return and repair, 1 minute each: error C (the bottom is always older: the two stacks in hand) and error B (none here, so extinct: the guide statement). Then children read a regional fossil record and write product F.
