# Day 84: A dry valley with whale bones

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each pair the valley cards and a guide card. Write the four comparison rows on the board. Find the day 82 pages, with the Arctic model on side 2.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 83's s05-P83-1, s05-P83-2, s05-P83-3, s05-P83-4, s05-P83-5 and s05-P83-6, and the ticket s05-X83. If many made error F in day 83's exit (a size shows heat), put the stump beside the guide card and read both: a big stump shows a big tree grew; the card says what that tree needs. If many made error A (there is no pond now, so never), read the leaf print beside the dry hill instead. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 10 min. Desert evidence

Show the valley drawing, marked schematic, not to scale: a dry valley of sand and bare rock, with large bones and flat round shell prints in its wall. Read the guide card: whales and shells live in the sea today. State plainly that the valley is desert now. **Ask.** What does the card say these living things need? Expect: the sea. 15 pairs fill rows 1 and 2 of the table of finds from the two finds on the card, [[s05-P84-1]]. The sizes are invented for practice. Say aloud that a bone gives at least the size of the whale.

#### 10 min. Comparison

Pairs set the Arctic trunks beside the valley and fill the comparison, [[s05-P84-2]]: what was found, what the guide card says, the place then, the place now. Say that this is a very slow change over ages that no one watched, as in grade 2's slow changes. The steady-patterns idea carries it: living things needed then what they need now. Six pairs each read one row aloud, 40 seconds each, while the others check their own.

#### 14 min. Model revision

8 minutes: each child draws the valley when the whales lived and labels each part with its find, [[s05-P84-3]]. 3 minutes: on the day 82 page, in a second color, add one line to the Arctic model and circle a find that shows it, [[s05-P84-4]]. 3 minutes: hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. **Ask.** What does the place now not tell us? Expect: the place then. Error A sounds like a drawing of sand and rocks: point to the guide card. Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Exit E84

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X84]], 4 minutes. Ask [[s05-X84b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: a wet or rainy forest grew there long ago, and one thing the card does not show, such as heat or the whole tree. Error A says it is a desert, so a forest could never grow. Error F says big leaves prove it was boiling hot. Note letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the valley and the comparison on the board; children copy the rows onto notebook paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the repair.

- **8 min. Valley.** Read the guide card aloud. The child does [[s05-P84-1]].
  **Say.** "What do whales and these shells need today?" Expect: the sea, so the valley was once under water.
- **8 min. Compare.** The child does [[s05-P84-2]].
- **12 min. Draw.** The child does [[s05-P84-3]] and [[s05-P84-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X84]], answered alone. Then ask [[s05-X84b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill the comparison rows for the valley only. Never cut the drawing, the added line or the exit. Longer: write a question the valley cannot answer yet.

### Words and supports

Valley, guide card, then, now. Frame: "The place now is ___. The place then was ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 85 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error A (it is a desert, so a forest could never grow here: the valley beside the whale bones). Then children meet extinct forms and rock layers.
