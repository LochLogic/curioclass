# Product F card (day 86, revised on day 93)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud once. Each child works alone on a copy of the past-environment model sheet.

## A grassland site

The ground is grass today. The rock layers are undisturbed.

## Lower layer, at two mapped places

Complete fish fossils, 18 centimetres long. Leaf prints of water plants, 6 centimetres long. A guide says this fish form is extinct worldwide. Other fish are not. The guide says these finds go with lake water.

## Upper layer

Pieces of rooted tree trunks, 80 centimetres long. The guide says rooted trunks go with trees growing on land.

## Your task

Annotate your model. Explain what the kinds, sizes and places support. Say what changed. Say which layer is older. Say why a week of weather cannot tell us this.
