# Day 86: A regional record, and product F

## Teacher page

### Before class

Give each pair the regional record: the class state's documented fossil, or the Florissant, Colorado record. Print product F's model sheet for each child. Keep the product f card for the teacher's desk. Plan about 1 minute a child after class: a six-tick look-for checklist and one question stamp.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult alone empties the jar and handles any ice. Helpers wash their hands.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 85's s05-P85-1, s05-P85-2, s05-P85-3, s05-P85-4, s05-P85-5, s05-P85-6 and s05-P85-7, and the ticket s05-X85c. If many made error C in day 85's exit (the bottom is always older), 1 minute: lay the intact stack beside the flipped one. If many made error B (none here, so extinct), 1 minute: read the guide statement, extinct means none alive anywhere. One child restates each; any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s05-P86-4]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 18 min. Regional record

3 minutes: show the map and find card A, marked schematic, not to scale. 6 minutes: 15 pairs fill the table of finds for dots A, B and C, [[s05-P86-1]]; sizes printed "invented for practice", except the stump's, from a park page. 5 minutes: pairs write two claims and mark each shows or does not show, [[s05-P86-2]]. The three dots do not make the whole region one place. 4 minutes: six pairs each read one claim aloud, 40 seconds each. **Ask.** Which claim does the map not show? Expect: that the whole region was lake.

#### 12 min. Product F

Read the product f card once, invented for practice. Children work alone on the model sheet and [[s05-P86-3]]. Give no help beyond reading a line again. Collect every paper. After class, fill the look-for checklist: six ticks, one a look-for, each marked shown or not yet, and one question stamp; about 1 minute a child, never the answer. Papers come back on day 93 for revision.

#### 5 min. Exit E86

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X86]], 4 minutes. Ask [[s05-X86b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: freshwater snails at the west and land animals at the east, and one thing still uncertain, such as the whole region, a date or a climate. Error A says it is dry now, so it was always dry. Note the letter.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Helpers wash hands. Cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the map and the three finds on the board; children answer on notebook paper. Product F is read aloud and answered on notebook paper with a drawn model.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The pair claims and the helpers.

- **8 min. Finds.** The child does [[s05-P86-1]].
  **Say.** "Which claim do the finds not show?" Expect: that the whole region was lake.
- **6 min. Claims.** The child does [[s05-P86-2]].
- **12 min. Product F.** Read the card once. The child does [[s05-P86-3]] alone, with no help. Put it aside after.
- **4 min. Weather.** The child does [[s05-P86-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X86]], answered alone. Then ask [[s05-X86b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill rows A and B and write one claim. Never cut product F or the exit. Longer: write one find that would show the region was not all lake.

### Words and supports

Record, claim, shows, does not show, uncertain. Frame: "The finds show ___. I cannot tell ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 87 opens with Retrieval and repair on error A (it is dry now, so it was always dry: fossils of water plants show water was here). The product F checklist is filled after class. The unit then turns to what a change to a place does to the living things there.
