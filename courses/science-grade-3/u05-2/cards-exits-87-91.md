# Exit cards, days 87 to 91

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 87

Invented for practice. A farmer removes a hedge beside a small meadow. Wind now blows across the meadow. A tall wildflower that needs shelter stops flowering. A wind-blown grass spreads. Why did the wildflower and the grass have different results?

## Day 88

Invented for practice. A new canal cuts a field from a pond where toads breed. Goal: at least 6 of 10 marked model toads cross, within 4 tokens. Planks cost 2 tokens, and 2 of 10 cross. A tube costs 3 tokens, and 8 of 10 cross. A wide bridge costs 6 tokens, and 10 of 10 cross. Which has merit? Use the goal and the limit.

## Day 89

Invented for practice. A raised pipe stops frogs reaching a marsh. Goal: at least 6 of 10 marked model frogs reach it, within 5 tokens. Short ramp: 2 tokens, 3 of 10. Stepped ledges: 4 tokens, 8 of 10. Long slope: 7 tokens, 10 of 10. Which has merit? What does the record not show?

## Day 90

Invented for practice. A bare stretch of a small stream gets new trees. Two years on, its water is 4 degrees cooler. Cold-water fish counted there rise from 1 to 5. Warm-water snails fall from 8 to 3. A card says the fish need cool water and the snails need warm, sunny water. What changed first? Why did the fish and the snails change in different ways?

## Day 91

Invented for practice. A new vine covers seed plants that field mice eat. Goals: at least 7 seed plants and 5 feeding visits. Limits: within 5 tokens, and no clearing of the whole patch. Removing strips of vine costs 4 tokens. It restores 8 seed plants and 6 visits. Strips leave vine elsewhere. Removing the whole patch costs 6 tokens. It restores 10 seed plants and 8 visits. Which has merit? Use the goals and limits.
