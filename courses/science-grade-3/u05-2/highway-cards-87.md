# Highway cards (day 87)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A highway cuts a forest in two. Four organism cards. Pairs fill each card: need, condition now, result.

## Card 1: moss

Needs damp shade on the ground. Now the road edge is open and sunny. The ground is dry. Result: the moss dries out and dies near the road.

## Card 2: forest beetle

Needs leaf litter and dead wood under trees. Now the wood on each side still has both. Result: it stays and reproduces.

## Card 3: deer

Needs open paths to find food and water. Now the road is hard to cross. Result: it moves to the quieter side of the forest.

## Card 4: field beetle

Needs open, sunny ground. Now the road edge is open and sunny. Result: it arrives and reproduces along the edge.

## The sort

Four results: dies, stays, moves, arrives. The class chart has one place for each card.

## The problem card

The forest is halved. Say what merit means: how well a response meets its goals within its limits.
