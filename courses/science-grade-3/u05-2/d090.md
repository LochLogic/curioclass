# Day 90: Losing the shade

## Teacher page

### Before class

Before school the adult sets two matched plastic cups of the same tap water, each with a non-glass thermometer: one in direct sun at a window for two hours and one in a shaded corner. Print the reading and the record, one a pair, and a system revision sheet for each child.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. Thermometers are non-glass, the jar and cups plastic. The adult alone empties the jar and cups and wipes spills; helpers wash hands in the clean-up minute.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 89's s05-P89-1, s05-P89-2, s05-P89-3 and s05-P89-4, and the ticket s05-X89. If many made error E in day 89's exit (the long ramp, because 10 is the biggest), put the goal box and the limit box on the board and check the long ramp against both. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the outdoor thermometer and the rain jar, then the two cup thermometers, 30 seconds a reading, with the adult, while the class writes [[s05-P90-1]]. Say each reading to the nearest mark. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 6 min. Reading

You met this in kindergarten: sunlight warms water, shade keeps it off. Today we ask what that does to living things. Read "Shade on a small stream" aloud once. Pairs reread it. **Ask.** What changed in the stream? Expect: the shade is gone. **Ask.** What do the larvae and the small fish need? Expect: cool water. Say this is one change, and the card is invented for practice.

#### 12 min. Temperature evidence

3 minutes: the class cup readings, [[s05-P90-2]]. If both cups show the same mark, say the step is too coarse, and go on. 9 minutes: 15 pairs read the stream record, a shaded and a sunny stretch measured one afternoon, and trace shade, then temperature, then organisms, [[s05-P90-3]]. **Ask.** Which counts are lower where the shade is gone? Expect: both cold-water counts, the larvae, 3 to 12, and the fish, 1 to 7. A lower count may mean animals died or moved; the record does not say which.

#### 12 min. Model revision

4 minutes: each child takes a system revision sheet, tapes the unit 4 drawing in the large box and adds the stream beside it in pencil, with the cold-water larvae and the small fish each linked to cool water. 8 minutes: in a second colour, add the chain shade lost, warmer water, fewer larvae, and tag each living thing, [[s05-P90-4]]. The larvae are tagged dies or moves, the fish moves and the warm-water animals arrives. Error D looks like every animal tagged dies: ask the child to read one need beside the warmer water.

#### 5 min. Exit E90

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X90]], 4 minutes. Look for: the shade is named as the first change; the cooler water meets the fish need, not the snail need. Error D says the cooler water made every animal do better.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar and the cups. Helpers wash hands. Cards counted back.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the table on the board and read the cards aloud; children answer on notebook paper. No weather tools or cups: read the dated adult record and say the stream record is invented.

### At home

**Swap.** No second thermometer: read the stream record only and skip the cups. No second colour: use a pen.

**Skip.** The helpers and the pair talk.

- **8 min. Weather and cups.** The child does [[s05-P90-1]] and [[s05-P90-2]].
- **5 min. Read.** Read the shade card aloud.
  **Say.** "What changed in the stream?" Expect: the shade is gone.
- **10 min. Record.** The child does [[s05-P90-3]].
- **10 min. Draw.** The child does [[s05-P90-4]].
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X90]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: trace the larvae only. Never cut the drawing or the exit. Longer: add a fourth living thing that likes warm water and tag it.

### Words and supports

Shade, temperature, cool, warm, fewer, result. Frame: "The shade is lost, so the water is ___. So the ___ are ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 91 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error D (the cooler water made every animal do better): the new-trees pair read aloud, and each pair traces one need. Then the class judges a response to a spreading vine, and children write product S alone.
