# Dam and ladder cards (day 89)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## The paper river

A river runs left to right. A dam stands across it. Marked model fish wait at the foot of the dam. Upstream is gravel. The fish need that gravel to lay eggs. This is the research the ladder is built from.

## The ladder

A fish ladder is a staircase of small pools beside the dam. A fish rests in one pool, then jumps to the next. The class learns the mechanism only.

## The goal card

Goal: at least 5 of 10 marked fish get above the dam, within 8 tokens.

## Three trial cards

- Ladder A: 7 tokens, 6 of 10 pass.
- Ladder B: 5 tokens, 3 of 10 pass.
- Ladder C: 12 tokens, 9 of 10 pass.

Pairs mark each box. Ladder A meets both. Ladder B misses the count. Ladder C breaks the budget.

## Merit argument

Each child writes a claim, evidence and reason, with arrows. The record covers only the marked fish. The claim cannot cover all river life.
