# Day 88: Judging a wildlife crossing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the crossing case; cut the trial cards and change cards apart, one set a pair. Print a trial table for each pair. On the board write the model card: "Log ramp: 2 tokens, 3 of 10 cross. The road stays open."

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Clean-up is 3 minutes: cards counted back, papers collected.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 87's s05-P87-1, s05-P87-2 and s05-P87-3, and the ticket s05-X87. If many made error D in day 87's exit (the wind changed both, so both did the same), read "the highway changed the forest, so every animal did worse" beside "the moss dried, the deer moved, a field beetle arrived". Each pair traces one need aloud. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 4 min. Goals and limits

Say this is a design problem again, as in unit 2: a goal says what must happen, a limit says the most we allow. Read the goal card aloud: at least 7 of 10 marked model animals cross, within 8 tokens; the road stays open. Children fill in the goal card, [[s05-P88-2]]. Watch for "at most" written for "at least".

#### 8 min. Proposal

3 minutes: read the case aloud. The text marks each because and so link. Children circle them and write what the road does to the animals, [[s05-P88-1]]. 5 minutes: 15 pairs draw a crossing for the road, a tunnel, a rope bridge, a planted bridge or their own, and say which goal box it should meet, [[s05-P88-3]]. Say these are model animals, marked for a model trial; no real animal is tested.

#### 12 min. Test records

3 minutes: model one card. Read the log ramp. Say a matched model trial tries each crossing the same way and shows the model, not a real road. **Ask.** Which box on the goal card does the log ramp miss? Expect: the count; 3 of 10 is under 7. The tokens and the road are met. 9 minutes: pairs read the tunnel, rope bridge and planted bridge cards and mark each box met or missed, [[s05-P88-4]]. Walk round: error E picks the planted bridge because 10 of 10 is the most; send the pair back to the token box. **Ask.** Which crossing has merit? Expect: the tunnel; it meets the count and the limits.

#### 10 min. Improvement

6 minutes: give each pair the two change cards for the rope bridge: add cover, or add ropes. Pairs choose with the records and each child writes why, [[s05-P88-5]]. The partner asks: "What did you see that shows that?" 2 minutes: six pairs each say a choice, 20 seconds each. 2 minutes: show both cards again. Cover costs 1 more token, 4 in all, and gets 7 of 10. Ropes cost 5 in all and get 5 of 10. Any child may pass.

#### 6 min. Exit E88

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X88]], 5 minutes. Look for: the tube, which meets the count and the token limit; the planks miss the count; the wide bridge breaks the limit. Error E says the wide bridge, because 10 is the most. Error D says every animal will use the tube.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Cards counted back, papers collected.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the goal card and the trial table on the board; read each card aloud; children answer on notebook paper. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud. No second color needed today.

**Skip.** The pair drawing and the sharing.

- **6 min. Goal.** The child does [[s05-P88-1]] and [[s05-P88-2]].
- **10 min. Records.** Read the three cards. The child does [[s05-P88-4]].
  **Say.** "Which box does the rope bridge miss?" Expect: the count, 4 of 10 is under 7.
- **10 min. Improve.** The child does [[s05-P88-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X88]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark the tunnel and the rope bridge only. Never cut the improvement or the exit. Longer: write a third change card and say what record would show its merit.

### Words and supports

Goal, limit, token, merit, met, missed. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 89 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on the commonest letter of day 88's exit: error E (the biggest count wins) or error D (every animal will use the tube), with the two boxes on the cards. Then the class judges a fish ladder at a dam.
