# Stream record (day 90)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

A shaded stretch and a sunny stretch of one small stream, measured on one summer afternoon.

| | Shaded stretch | Sunny stretch |
|---|---|---|
| Water temperature | 14 degrees | 21 degrees |
| Cold-water larvae counted | 12 | 3 |
| Cold-water fish counted | 7 | 1 |

Pairs trace: shade, then temperature, then organisms. This is one change: shade lost.

Each child tags each organism: stays, moves, dies or arrives. The cold-water larvae die or move. The cold-water fish move. The warm-water animals arrive.
