# Day 91: A spreading vine, and product S

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the vine case, one a pair, and a trial table for each pair. Print the product S card and a system revision sheet for each child. Plan 3 minutes a child after class for written feedback.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, cards counted back.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 90's s05-P90-1, s05-P90-2, s05-P90-3 and s05-P90-4, and the ticket s05-X90. If many made error D in day 90's exit (the cooler water made every animal do better), read the new-trees pair: the fish rose and the snails fell. Each pair traces one need aloud. One child restates it; any child may pass.

#### 13 min. Spreading-plant case

3 minutes: read the vine case aloud: a spreading vine hides berry shrubs that fruit-eating birds feed on. Read the goal card: at least 4 of 8 shrubs show again and 5 bird visits; the limits are within 6 tokens and the soil stays whole. Say the numbers are invented for practice. 5 minutes: 15 pairs judge the two responses on the trial table, [[s05-P91-1]]. Walk round: error E calls digging out all roots best because 8 and 9 are the most; send the pair to the token box and the soil box. **Ask.** Which response has merit? Expect: cutting at the base; it meets both counts and both limits. 5 minutes: each child draws the plant-food link from the vine to the berry shrubs to the birds, [[s05-P91-2]]. **Ask.** What does the record not show? Expect: other animals, or whether the shrubs stay clear; it covers only these model counts.

#### 20 min. Product S

Each child works alone. Read the product S card once, invented for practice: a wet meadow after a drainage ditch opens. Children keep the card in front of them. They draw arrows tied to the four records on the system revision sheet, then write the claim and the limit, [[s05-P91-3]]. Give no help beyond reading a line again. Collect the page and the sheet. After class, write feedback: six look-fors, 1 point each, marked supported or not yet (listed in the key), and one question for the weakest; never write the answer. The papers go back on day 94 for revision.

#### 7 min. Exit E91

1 minute: read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. 5 minutes: alone, [[s05-X91]]. 1 minute: collect. Look for: strip removal, which meets both counts and both limits; whole-patch removal breaks a limit. Error E says whole-patch removal is best because its counts are largest. Error D says the vine harmed every plant and animal the same way.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, cards counted back, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the goal card and the trial table on the board and read the product S card aloud once; children answer on notebook paper with a drawn sheet. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the adult reads each card aloud. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The pair judging.

- **8 min. The vine.** Read the vine case. The child does [[s05-P91-1]].
  **Say.** "Which response has merit, and why?" Expect: cutting at the base; it meets both counts and both limits.
- **7 min. The link.** The child does [[s05-P91-2]].
- **15 min. Product S.** Read the card once. The child does [[s05-P91-3]] alone, with no help. Put it aside after.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X91]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark cutting at the base only. Never cut product S or the exit. Longer: write what record would show the vine is gone for good.

### Words and supports

Vine, shrub, visit, merit, limit, soil. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; a scribe may write for a child.

### Tomorrow

Day 92 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on the commonest letter of day 91's exit: error E (the biggest count wins) or error D (the vine harmed everything alike), with the two boxes on the cards. Product S feedback is written after class and goes back on day 94. Day 92 then asks what the evidence after a fire shows about replanting.
