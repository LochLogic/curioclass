# Day 87: What a highway changes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the highway cards, one set a pair, and fold each Result line under. Draw the class chart on the board.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, cards counted back.

### The day

#### 8 min. Retrieval and repair

Hand back day 86's s05-P86-1, s05-P86-2 and s05-P86-4 and the ticket s05-X86. Product F, s05-P86-3, is handed back on day 93 with its written feedback. 4 minutes: children take out the unit 4 drawing. Pairs pick one living thing on it, say a need and the condition that meets it, then say the latch goal and its limit, [[s05-P87-1]]. 2 minutes: if many made error A in day 86's exit (it is dry now, so it was always dry), put it beside "fossils of water plants show water was here" and read both. One child restates it; any child may pass. 2 minutes: a child who missed the retrieval gets a matched need picture or a goal-versus-limit card, drawn on the board with the teacher, while the rest read the four highway cards.

#### 22 min. Highway case

4 minutes: say this is one change: a highway cuts the forest in two. Draw the forest and an arrow from the highway to the moss, which needs damp shade. 8 minutes: 15 pairs fill the table from the four cards, [[s05-P87-2]]: the need, the condition now, then the result they decide from the two. A result is one of four: stays and reproduces, moves, dies, arrives. Say an animal can travel to where its needs are met, and moss cannot. Say plainly that some die; show no picture. 4 minutes: six children from the rotating six each place a pair's card under its result on the chart, 40 seconds each. 3 minutes: read the chart aloud and sort the four results. **Ask.** Did the highway do the same to every living thing? Expect: no, each one follows its own need. Error D says every one did worse: point to the field beetle, which arrives. 3 minutes: show the problem card, the forest halved. Say merit: how well a response meets its goals within its limits. **Ask.** Which condition changed, and who is affected? Expect: the road edge is open and sunny, and the moss, deer and field beetle. Any child may pass.

#### 9 min. System revision

Each child takes a system revision sheet. 3 minutes: in pencil, draw the forest in the big box as in unit 4: moss, forest beetle and deer, each with a line to what it needs; the field beetle waits at the edge. 6 minutes: in a second colour, add a what-changed box that names the highway, an arrow from it to each living thing, and a tag on each: stays, moves, dies or arrives, [[s05-P87-3]]. Keep the pencil drawing. Error D looks like four tags that all say dies.

#### 3 min. Exit E87

Read the card aloud twice, invented for practice. Alone, [[s05-X87]]. Look for: the wind meets the grass's need and not the wildflower's, so each follows its own need. Error D says the wind changed both, so both did the same.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, cards counted back, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the four cards and the chart on the board; children copy the table onto notebook paper and draw the forest on a plain page. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: the parent reads each card aloud. No second colour: use a pen.

**Skip.** The chart and the pair talk.

- **8 min. Remember.** The child does [[s05-P87-1]].
- **10 min. Four living things.** Read the four cards aloud. The child does [[s05-P87-2]].
  **Say.** "Did the highway do the same to each one?" Expect: no, each one follows its own need.
- **10 min. Draw.** The child does [[s05-P87-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s05-X87]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fill the table for the moss and the deer only, and tag two. Never cut the arrows or the exit. Longer: add a fifth card of your own and say its result.

### Words and supports

Change, condition, result, stays, moves, dies, arrives, merit. Frame: "The ___ needs ___, so it ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 88 opens with a 2-minute Return and repair on error D (the wind changed both, so both did the same): the highway pair read aloud, and each pair traces one need. Then the class judges a wildlife crossing against a goal and limits.
