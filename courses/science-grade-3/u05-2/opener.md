# Section 5.2 opener: judging a response

## What the section does

Five days, 87 to 91. The big question is: "When a place changes, what happens to the living things, and how do we judge a response?" Day 87 asks what a highway changes in a forest system and revises the unit 4 drawing with condition-to-organism arrows. Day 88 judges a wildlife crossing against a goal and limits. Day 89 judges a fish ladder in a dam case. Day 90 reads how losing stream shade changes temperature and the animals. Day 91 judges a response to a spreading vine, and children write product S alone. Each day ends with an exit. Days 89 and 90 hold the 5-minute weather record. Days 87, 88 and 91 hold no weather.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Each follows its own need.** When a place changes, some living things stay and reproduce, some move, some die and some arrive. One change at a time.
- **Merit.** How well a response meets its goals within its limits. Check the goal box and the limit box before reading any count. A count that breaks a limit has no merit.
- **A trial record is a model.** The cards are invented for practice. A result covers only the marked model animals, not every animal.
- **Research informs the design.** What the animals need is found out first. A fish ladder is a staircase of small pools. Mechanism only.
- **No graphic harm.** An animal that dies is told plainly, with no picture of it.
- **Scoring product S.** Six points, one for each complete component. Feedback is written after class. Revision is day 94.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** it is dry now, so it always was. Repaired on day 87.
- **D:** everything responds alike. Trace one need for each organism.
- **E:** the biggest count wins. Check the two boxes first.

Wrong answers are voiced as "someone might say". No child is the example of a mistake.

## Supplies for the section

The system model drawing from unit 4. The highway cards, the crossing case and trial table, the dam and ladder cards, the shade reading, the stream record, two cups for water, the vine case, the exit cards and product S card. Second-colour pencils. Weather equipment on days 89 and 90.

## A room with nothing

The teacher draws on the board. Cards are printed. Two cups of water, one in sun and one in shade, can be replaced by the dated record read aloud.
