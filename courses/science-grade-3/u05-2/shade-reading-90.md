# Shade on a small stream (day 90)

Read aloud by the teacher, then reread by pairs. No sentence is over 15 words.

A small stream runs through a wood. In summer, tall trees shade the water. The water stays cool.

Some insect larvae live in this stream. They need cool water. Some small fish live here too. They also need cool water.

One year, people cut the trees by one stretch of the stream. They wanted a field there. Now the sun shines on that water all day.

The water in that stretch grows warmer. The cool-water larvae become fewer. The small fish swim to a shaded stretch.

Other animals do well in warm, sunny water. They begin to move in.

One change made all of this. The shade is gone.
