# Product S card (day 91, revised on day 94, a repair mark)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud once. Each child works alone on a copy of the system revision sheet.

## A wet meadow

A drainage ditch opens beside a wet meadow. The water drains away.

## The records

- Wet-soil plants fail to make seeds.
- Frogs move to another pond.
- Water insects that were trapped die.
- Dry-ground beetles arrive and reproduce.

## The goal card

Restore wet plants and frog breeding. At least 7 wet plants and 5 frog egg clusters, within 6 tokens. The path must stay dry.

## Two model sites

- Ditch plug: 5 tokens, 8 of 10 wet plants, 6 egg clusters. The path floods.
- Side pool: 6 tokens, 7 of 10 wet plants, 5 egg clusters. The path stays dry.

## Your task

Make a merit claim that compares the two. Draw system arrows tied to the records. Write one limit on the evidence.
