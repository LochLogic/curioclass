# Crossing case (day 88)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## The goal card

Goal: at least 7 of 10 marked model animals cross, within 8 tokens. Limit: the road stays open.

## The case

A road runs between a wood and a stream. Animals from the wood go to the stream to drink. The road cuts the way. Cars slow animals down because the road stays open. So the animals cross too little. The text marks each because and so link.

## Proposal

Pairs draw a crossing for the road: a tunnel, a rope bridge, a planted bridge or their own. They say which goal it should meet.

## Trial cards, read by pairs

- Tunnel: 5 tokens, 7 of 10 cross. The road stays open.
- Rope bridge: 3 tokens, 4 of 10 cross. The road stays open.
- Planted bridge: 9 tokens, 10 of 10 cross. The road stays open.

Pairs mark each box as met or missed. The rope bridge meets the budget and misses the count. The planted bridge breaks the token limit. The tunnel meets both.

## Two change cards

- Add cover to the rope bridge: 1 more token, 7 of 10 cross.
- Add ropes to the rope bridge: 2 more tokens, 5 of 10 cross.

Cover meets both boxes. Ropes miss the count.
