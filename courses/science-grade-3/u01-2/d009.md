# Day 9: Record the swings

## Teacher page

### Before class

Tape a strip under each pendulum path: centre 0, a mark every centimetre each side. Hang each pendulum from a checked support with four washers counted out. Check the weather. Draw the class table on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone wears eye protection while a pendulum swings. Low arcs, below faces; strings off necks, washers out of mouths. An adult has secured every support. Nobody sits on a playground swing for this. Washers go out counted and come back counted. An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun; the thermometer is non-glass. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look; write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s01-P9-1]]. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 2 min. Measuring warm-up

Draw two strips above a ruler on the board. Children write [[s01-P9-2]]. A miss gets zero put at the strip's end and a second strip.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 8's pages and exit tickets (s01-P8-1, s01-P8-2 and s01-X8). Error A, 2 minutes: hold a washer on a string still; each child draws its two arrows. Error C, 2 minutes: redraw with arrows for strength only; draw the string separately.

#### 4 min. Set-up and safety

Fit eye protection. Teach the stop signal. Each recorder counts out four washers. Turns are counted, not timed.

#### 14 min. Record the swings

Six groups of five, one pendulum each. The releaser holds the washers still at the 10 cm mark on the left and lets go gently, no push. The reader counts ten turns aloud and says each side and mark as it passes; the releaser then stops the washers. Everyone writes the first six turns in [[s01-P9-3]]: turn 1 is the start, left, 10 cm; turn 2 is the centre, 0. Five runs, so each child reads once; in runs 2 to 5 each child checks every row and writes a different mark beside the first one, keeping both. A run is about a minute: five runs, 5 to 8 minutes of the 14. A mark too fast to read is order only; leave it blank. If a pendulum cannot hang, use an invented record, said aloud to be invented.

#### 9 min. Table and pattern

New: position table, turning point (where it stops for an instant and turns back), cycle (left turn, centre, right turn, centre). Put the invented table on the board. [[steps:s01-K9-1]]

**Ask.** What repeats? What changes? Expect: the order of the sides repeats; the far marks change a little.

Each child colours one cycle in their own table and writes [[s01-P9-4]]. Marks that stay the same so far are an honest answer: a drop of less than 1 cm may not show.

**Ask.** Is one run a pattern? Expect: no; the order came back in every run.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X9]]; read both cards aloud and wait. Look for: (a) toward the window; (b) no, the far turns have been getting smaller. Error E: "toward the door, it started there". Error F: "yes, it always goes the same". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Count washers in; wipe eye protection; collect the strips. Wash hands.

### Other rooms

No pendulum: hold a key on a string over a strip on a desk, low arc. No ruler marks: write the side only.

### At home

**Swap.** No washers: a key on a string, held by you at arm's length, low. No thermometer: look out the window.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the five runs.

- **5 min. The weather.** Do [[s01-P9-1]].
- **3 min. Ruler.** Do [[s01-P9-2]].
- **12 min. The swing.** Tape a strip with marks every centimetre under the key. Hold the key at 10 on the left; let go. Do [[s01-P9-3]].
  **Say.** "Which side comes next?" Expect: centre, then right.
- **8 min. The pattern.** Do [[s01-K9-1]] and [[s01-P9-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X9]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three runs, the first read in full and two checks. Never cut the table, the cycle or the exit. Longer: a group that finishes writes turns 7 to 10 in a second table.

### Words and supports

Position table, turning point, cycle, repeat, change. Frame: "The sides repeat. The marks ___." Home languages count. A partner may call the marks.

### Tomorrow

Day 10 opens with Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. If many wrote "toward the door, it started there" (error E), it lays turning-position cards in order and slides a finger along them. If many wrote "yes, it always goes the same" (error F), it compares successive far turns on the class table and says what changed. Then pairs predict from practice cards.
