# Day 6: Plan a test that changes one thing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the weather. Set six trays on the floor with a box, ball, ruler and team plan each. Put up the menu card and the two crate pictures. Mark day 5's pages with a tick or a short note.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges. An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun; the thermometer is non-glass. Wipes close the weather part.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look: thermometer to the nearest mark, rain jar, direction card, sky (never the sun). Write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s01-P6-1]]. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 6 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 5's pages and exit tickets (s01-P5-1, s01-P5-2, s01-P5-3, s01-X5); children read the note and file them. Error A, 2 minutes: each child holds a book still between two hands and draws both arrows. Error B, 2 minutes: groups predict, then push the tray box with two about equal pushes and read the ruler. Error C, 2 minutes: lay a long arrow beside a strip marked "moved 3 cm" and a short arrow beside "moved 9 cm"; the strip says how far, the arrow how strong.

#### 2 min. Set-up

Name the roles: pusher, second pusher, measurer, recorder, watcher. They rotate every try.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Say the fair-test words back: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Children write [[s01-P6-2]].

**Ask.** What did Mo change? Expect: the floor and the push, two things.

#### 7 min. The feature menu

Show the menu card: a push has three features, how many pushes, how strong, which way. How strong and which way are review from kindergarten; how many pushes is new. The crate pictures are invented. [[steps:s01-K6-1]]

**Ask.** Which feature changed? Expect: which way. Children write [[s01-K6-1]]. Then name the tray pairs: two about equal pushes from the front and back are balanced; two from the front are unbalanced.

#### 13 min. Group plans

Six groups fill in the team plan: does direction change what the box does? Change direction; keep the box, tray, line, two pushes about equal and where they touch; watch the end place in whole centimeters; three tries of each way. Groups choose their unbalanced pair: both from the front, or front and side. Each child writes [[s01-P6-3]], then arrows and a prediction, [[s01-P6-4]]; give feedback on arrows as you pass. Meet each group for 2 minutes, 12 in all.

**Ask.** Which feature are we changing, and what are we keeping? Expect: which way; the box, tray, line, two pushes, about equal, where they touch. Watch for error D: a plan that also changes the box or the number of fingers.

#### 4 min. Plan swap

Two groups swap plans. Each child reads the other plan and ticks what is there, [[s01-P6-5]]; each group says one question aloud to the other.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X6]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s01-X6b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: (a) same fingers, change only direction; (b) more tries of each way. Error D: "it is fine, the direction is different". Error H: "nothing, it has both ways". Note letters.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Stack trays, boxes and balls.

### Other rooms

No projector: draw the menu on the board. No trays: a book on a smooth desk, two pencils as pushers, a ruler. No thermometer: write sky and wind from the window, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a smooth table and a small book. Pencils can be the pushers. No thermometer: look out the window.

**Skip.** The groups, the plan swap and the repair.

- **5 min. The weather.** Look out the window. Do [[s01-P6-1]].
- **5 min. Fair or not?** Do [[s01-P6-2]].
  **Say.** "What did Mo change?" Expect: the floor and the push, two things.
- **8 min. Three features.** Read the menu on the page. Do [[s01-K6-1]].
- **12 min. Plan a test.** Do [[s01-P6-3]] and [[s01-P6-4]]. You are the second pusher.
  **Say.** "What are we changing, and what are we keeping?" Expect: which way; the rest the same.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X6]], answered alone. Then ask [[s01-X6b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the teacher reads one plan aloud instead of the swap. Never cut the menu, the prediction or the exit. Longer: a group that finishes plans a test of the number of pushes.

### Words and supports

Feature, number, strength, direction, front, back. Frame: "We change ___. We keep ___ the same." Home languages count.

### Tomorrow

Day 7 opens with Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. If many wrote "it is fine, the direction is different" (error D), it crosses out every change but one. If many wrote "nothing, it has both ways" (error H), it runs one condition again first. Then groups run the tests.
