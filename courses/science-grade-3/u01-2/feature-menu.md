# Feature menu card (day 6)

A card for each group. Plain list, large type.

**A push can change in three ways.**

1. How many pushes.
2. How strong.
3. Which way.

Pick one to change. Keep the other two the same.

## The invented crate

Said aloud to be invented. A crate sits on a smooth floor. A class wants to know: does the way we push change what the crate does? Drawing: two kids push the crate from the front and the back with about equal pushes. Next drawing: two kids push from the front, both the same way. Children say which feature changed and which two stayed.

## For the direction test

Balanced: two about equal pushes from the front and the back. Unbalanced: two pushes from the front, or one from the front and one from the side. The groups choose their unbalanced pair.
