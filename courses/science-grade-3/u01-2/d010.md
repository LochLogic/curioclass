# Day 10: Read the table and predict

## Teacher page

### Before class

Check the weather. Put each pair's six cards in an envelope. Draw the day 9 class table on the board and lay out the turning-position cards. Mark day 9's pages with a tick or a short note. Leave space for the class pattern list.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun; the thermometer is non-glass. Hand wipes close the weather part. Cards only today, so clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look; write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s01-P10-1]]. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 9's pages and exit tickets (s01-P9-1, s01-P9-2, s01-P9-3, s01-P9-4 and s01-X9). Error E, 2 minutes: lay the turning-position cards in order and slide a finger along them; ask what comes after the last one. Error F, 2 minutes: on the class table, compare each far turn with the one before and say what changed.

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Each child reads one row of their own table to a partner: turn, side, mark. The partner says which side comes next. Children write [[s01-P10-2]].

**Ask.** How did you know the next side? Expect: the sides take turns, so after a center comes the far side not seen last.

#### 12 min. Pairs with practice cards

Fifteen pairs, six cards each, every card said to be invented: a ceiling lamp, a hanging gate, a wind chime, a rope bridge, a hanging sign and a bell. The pair covers the last entry and says "I think ___, because I saw ___". Then they uncover it and name the earlier turns that supported them. Answers: lamp, center; gate, in; chime, center; bridge, a little less than 10; sign, center; bell, toward the fence. Each child writes two cards in [[s01-P10-3]]. Hear a rotating six, 40 seconds each: 4 of the 12 minutes. Every child may pass.

**Ask.** Will the bridge's next far turn be exactly 10? Why? Expect: no; the far turns have been getting a little smaller.

#### 6 min. Own table

Each child colors the repeating order on their day 9 table and writes the side of turn 7 with two earlier turns as evidence, [[s01-P10-4]].

#### 5 min. Exit, section exit ticket

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X10]]; read the card aloud and wait. A record may be read to a child. Look for: center, because every right turn was followed by center. Error E: "right turn, it stays where it is now". Error G: "left, I like left". A blank answer is an incomplete part, not a letter. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 5 min. Section close

Build the class pattern list: what repeats, the order of the turns; what changes, how far it swings. Update the question board: the order repeats in the swings we recorded; whether it holds for other swings stays open.

**Ask.** What did you see that makes you say so? Expect: earlier turns in the table. Every child may pass.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Collect the cards.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the six cards on scrap paper and cover the last entry. No thermometer: write sky and wind from the window, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No printed cards: draw two swing records on paper, lamp: left, center, right, center, left, center, right, and cover the last. No thermometer: look out the window.

**Skip.** The pairs, the class list and the repair.

- **5 min. The weather.** Do [[s01-P10-1]].
- **5 min. Read a row.** Read one row of the day 9 table aloud. Do [[s01-P10-2]].
- **12 min. Two cards.** Cover the last entry of each card. Do [[s01-P10-3]].
  **Say.** "What comes next? What did you see?" Expect: "I think center, because I saw right, then center."
- **8 min. Your table.** Do [[s01-P10-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X10]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: three cards a pair, one written. Never cut the evidence sentence, your own table or the exit. Longer: a pair writes a card for a swing it knows, eight turns, and swaps it.

### Words and supports

Table, turn, side, mark, predict, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home languages count. A partner may read the record.

### Tomorrow

Day 11, the first day of the next section, opens with Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. If many wrote "right turn, it stays where it is now" (error E), it lays the turning-position cards in order again. If many wrote "left, I like left" (error G), it highlights two earlier matching turns that support the answer. Then the class predicts a ball rolling in a bowl.
