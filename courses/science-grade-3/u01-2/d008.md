# Day 8: Two pulls on a swinging washer

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have an adult secure the support and hang the pendulum with four counted washers; check the knot and clear the path. Write a short note on each day 7 record, naming one thing to revise. Find day 1's swing drawings. Put the two question board questions up.

### Safety and set-up

Everyone wears eye protection while the pendulum swings. Low arcs, below faces; the string stays off necks and washers out of mouths. Nobody sits on a playground swing for this. Washers are counted out and counted in. Clean-up is 4 minutes: five captains wipe the 30 pairs of eye protection in 3.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 7's records with your note, and exit tickets (s01-P7-1, s01-P7-2, s01-P7-3 and s01-X7). Error B, 2 minutes: push the tray box with two about equal pushes again and read the ruler aloud. Error G, 2 minutes: pick one odd reading from the class table; the reading stays, and the group says what may explain it.

#### 8 min. Revise the record

Each child revises [[s01-P8-1]] on their day 7 record in a second color: the plan, the arrows or the sentences. The first numbers and initials stay.

#### 12 min. The pendulum, two pulls

Eye protection on. Hold the washers at the side and let go gently, then let them hang still. Draw each moment on the board with the class, [[s01-K8-1]]: gravity pulls the washer down; the string pulls it toward the support. Arrows start on the washer and show strength, not the string's length. [[steps:s01-K8-1]]

**Ask.** What pulls on the washer when it is held at the side? When it hangs still? Expect: gravity and the string; at rest they are the same length and cancel.

Then ask what could tell us where it goes next. Take answers; leave it open.

#### 8 min. Revise the swing model

Each child takes out day 1's swing drawing (s01-P1-2) and draws it again with the two arrows, [[s01-P8-2]]. Update the class model on the board beside it. Watch for error C: one arrow as long as the string.

#### 5 min. Question board

**Ask.** Can pushes act without movement? Expect: yes, when the pushes cancel; the box and the washer showed it. Mark it answered with the box evidence. "Will our explanation work in another test, and what repeats?" stays open.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X8]]; read the card aloud and wait. Look for: two arrows on the lantern, the same length, one down and one up. Error A: "nothing pulls it, it is still". Error C: an arrow as long as the rope. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Captains collect and wipe the eye protection. Count the washers in.

### Other rooms

No pendulum: hold a key on a string at arm's length, low arc. No projector: draw on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No washers: a key on a string, held by you, low and away from the child's mouth.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the question board.

- **8 min. Revise.** Read the note on the day 7 record. Do [[s01-P8-1]].
- **10 min. The swing.** Hold the key still, then to the side and let go. Do [[s01-K8-1]].
  **Say.** "What pulls on it when it hangs still?" Expect: gravity down, the string up, the same length.
- **10 min. Draw it.** Do [[s01-P8-2]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X8]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise one thing in the record, not three. Never cut the two arrows or the exit. Longer: draw the arrows for a swing at its far turn, then say whether they cancel.

### Words and supports

Gravity (a pull down), string pull, support, cancel, revise. Frame: "Gravity pulls ___. The string pulls ___." Home languages count. A partner may point to the arrows.

### Tomorrow

Day 9 opens with Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter. If many wrote "nothing pulls it, it is still" (error A), it holds a washer on a string still and draws its two arrows. If many drew an arrow as long as the string (error C), it redraws arrows for strength only and draws the string separately. Then groups record the swings.
