# Day 7: Run the group tests

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set six trays on the floor with a taped start line, box, soft ball, ruler and team plan each. Draw the results table on the board: a row a group, balanced and unbalanced readings. Mark day 6's pages with a tick or a short note.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges; nothing is thrown. Floors stay clear. Clean-up is 4 minutes with wipes; wash hands after.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Trays on the floor, walking space clear. Roles are the day 6 roles; they rotate every try. Remind the stop signal.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 6's pages and exit tickets (s01-P6-1, s01-P6-2, s01-P6-3, s01-P6-4, s01-P6-5 and s01-X6); children read the note and file them. Error D, 2 minutes: on the board, Pia pushes a ball gently on the tray, then a box hard on the carpet; cross out every change but one. Error H, 2 minutes: roll the soft ball with one gentle push, then again; say nothing until both are read.

#### 4 min. Own plan

Each child writes their own plan from the team plan, [[s01-P7-1]]: the one change, what is kept, how many tries. This starts the record revised on day 8; it gets a look-for checklist first.

#### 16 min. Run the group tests

Six groups of five run two conditions, three tries each: balanced, two about equal pushes from the front and back; and the unbalanced pair they chose. A try takes about 90 seconds (reset 15, push 10, read 20, every child writes 30): six tries are 9 minutes of the 16. Roles rotate so every child pushes at least once; the pusher's initials go in [[s01-P7-2]]. Every child records every try. Repeat an unclear try once and keep both. A box that moves 1 cm or more under balanced pushes is recorded as it happened; hand pushes are only about equal. If the unbalanced pair does not move the box, push harder and record both. If a kit fails, use an invented record (balanced 0, 1, 0; unbalanced 8, 6, 9), said aloud to be invented.

#### 7 min. Arrows and explanation

Each child draws the box with its arrows for both kinds of pushes and writes two sentences: what stayed or moved, and why, using balanced or unbalanced and a number from the table, [[s01-P7-3]]. Give feedback on arrows as you pass.

**Ask.** Why are the balanced arrows the same length? Expect: the pushes are about equal, so they cancel.

#### 5 min. Compare groups

Recorders write their group's readings and unbalanced pair on the board.

**Ask.** Did every group choose the same pair? Did they find the same pattern? Expect: pairs and numbers differ; balanced stayed near 0 and unbalanced moved. Different tests can give evidence for the same question.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X7]]; read the card aloud and wait. Ask [[s01-X7b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: it mostly stayed; the 3 cm may mean the hands were not equal; run it again and keep all three. Error B: "equal pushes make it move". Error G: "erase the 3". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Wipe and stack boxes and trays; collect the pages.

### Other rooms

No trays: a book on a smooth desk, two pencils as pushers, a ruler. One projector: show the board table there.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a smooth table and a small book. Pencils can be pushers; you push too.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the comparison.

- **5 min. Your plan.** Do [[s01-P7-1]].
- **15 min. Run the test.** Push the book from the front and the back, about equal, three times. Then you and your child both push from the front, three times. Fill in [[s01-P7-2]].
  **Say.** "Did it move? Why?" Expect: not with equal pushes; they cancel.
- **8 min. Arrows and sentences.** Do [[s01-P7-3]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X7]], answered alone. Then ask [[s01-X7b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two tries of each way, the third only if a result is odd. Never cut the plan, the readings, the arrows or the exit. Longer: a group that finishes tests one more unbalanced pair with a plan like its first.

### Words and supports

Balanced, unbalanced, cancel, try, reading. Frame: "The box ___ because the pushes were ___." Home languages count. A partner may move the box as directed.

### Tomorrow

Day 8 opens with Return and repair, 2 minutes a letter, and returns this record's look-for checklist. If many wrote "equal pushes make it move" (error B), it runs the same equal pushes again on the tray and reads the ruler. If many wrote "erase the 3" (error G), it keeps every reading and says what might explain the 3 cm. Then each child revises their record in a second color.
