# Exit cards, days 6 to 10

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 6

A plan on paper: a block pushed from the left with one finger, then pushed from the right with two fingers. The plan wants to know if direction matters. Each way is tried once.

## Day 7

A small results table for a group: about equal pushes from the left and the right on a lunch box, tries of 0 cm, 0 cm and 3 cm.

## Day 8

A lantern hangs still from a hook on a rope. The rope is drawn thin and long.

## Day 9

Two cards. A record of a hanging toy: toward the door, middle, toward the window, middle, toward the door, middle. A list of far turns of another swing: 15, 14, 14, 13 cm.

## Day 10

A rocking toy and its record: left turn, center, right turn, center, left turn, center, right turn, center, left turn, center, right turn.
