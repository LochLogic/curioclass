# Section 1.2 opener: group tests and the pendulum record

## What the section does

Five days, 6 to 10. The class asks: "Will our explanation work in another test, and what repeats?" On day 6 groups learn the three features of a push and plan a test that changes one of them. On day 7 each child carries out the test and records it. That record is first-teaching work. It gets written feedback, is revised on day 8, and is scored on day 15. Day 8 draws two arrows on the pendulum washer and revises the swing drawing. Day 9 records ten turns of the pendulum in a position table. Day 10 practises reading the table and ends with the section exit ticket.

## How the days run

Each day is 45 minutes. A hand-back from the day before opens most days. The weather record runs for five minutes on days 6, 9 and 10. Groups of five rotate roles each trial. Every child has an identified trial and fills in their own record. Every child may pass in public talk.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **A push has three features.** How many pushes, how strong, and which way. A fair test changes one and keeps the rest.
- **Different methods can give evidence for one question.** Groups choose different pairs of pushes and still see the same pattern.
- **Hand pushes are only about equal.** A box that moves 1 cm or more under about equal pushes is recorded as it happened.
- **A hanging weight has two pulls.** Gravity pulls it down and the string pulls it toward the support. At the bottom they are the same length and cancel.
- **A turn is a turning point.** A cycle is one left turn, centre, one right turn, centre. The order of turns repeats and the size of the swing shrinks a little.

Counts order the pendulum runs, not time. There is no period or frequency.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A, B, C:** repaired with the held box, the tray again and the arrow beside its own ruler record.
- **D, H:** cross out all changes but one, and run it again.
- **E:** lay turning-position cards in order and slide a finger along them.
- **F:** compare successive far turns on the class table.
- **G:** keep every reading and highlight two matching turns as evidence.

## Supplies for the section

Boxes, trays, soft balls and rulers for six groups. A secured pendulum for each group on day 9, with four washers, eye protection and a taped strip with a mark every centimetre. The menu card, team plan, record sheets, position tables and the practice and exit cards.

## A room with nothing

Use a book and two pencils for the push tests. Use a key on a string held by the teacher at a low arc for the pendulum. Draw the position table on scrap paper.
