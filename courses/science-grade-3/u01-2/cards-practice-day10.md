# Practice cards, day 10

Six cards of other swings, each labelled "invented for practice". Pairs cover the last entry, say "I think ______, because I saw ______", then uncover it. Plain line drawings, no brands.

1. A lamp hanging from a ceiling. Record: left, centre, right, centre, left, centre, right, ? Next: centre.
2. A gate hanging on one hinge, swung by a breeze. Record: out, middle, in, middle, out, middle, ? Next: in.
3. A wind chime. Record: right, centre, left, centre, right, centre, left, centre, right, ? Next: centre.
4. A rope bridge. Record of the end rail, in marks: 12, 11, 11, 10 cm. Next far turn: a little less than 10.
5. A hanging sign. Record: left, centre, right, centre, left, ? Next: centre.
6. A bell on a rope. Record: toward the wall, centre, toward the fence, centre, toward the wall, centre, ? Next: toward the fence.

Each answer page lists the two earlier turns that support the answer.
