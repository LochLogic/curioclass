# Section 4.4 opener: the tundra again, the checks and the response

## What the section does

Five days, 74 to 78. The big question is the unit's: "Why do some living things survive in a place while others do not?" Nothing new is taught. Day 74 joins the class models into one tundra consensus model: group arrows, part and job rows, and need and condition arrows with the schoolyard evidence. Children also revise item V in a second color and write the day 74 exit. Day 75 is the variation check: item V fresh, alone. Day 76 is the group check: six groups of five give spoken reasons, then each child writes. Day 77 is the habitat check: item H fresh, alone. Day 78 is the one response: each child works on the component missed, then writes a fresh explanation on the sand lizard card.

## How the days run

Each day is 45 minutes. Days 74, 77 and 78 hold the 5-minute weather record. There is no feedback and no reteaching on days 75 to 77. The scored work is the fresh check-day work: the item V, G and H fresh cards, 12 points across three items. The revised earlier items V, G and H are marked beside the score as repair evidence, not the score. Feedback is a look-for checklist, four ticks and one question stamp, about 1 minute a child. Day 78 hands back the checklists; the scored papers come back on day 81, in its Return of feedback. Every child may pass in public talk.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **The three fresh cards ask for the same reasoning in new cases.** Plants and birds, shore animals, a new pond.
- **Scoring.** Twelve points, one point for each complete component. A missed revision means no repair mark, not zero. A sparse answer gets component feedback, not a letter.
- **Day 76 oral record.** Each child's brief spoken reason is noted on the heard list. File it unchanged for day 80. Do not score it a second time.
- **The teacher judges components.** Children do not self-mark.
- **No genetics and no natural selection by name.** Differences can help. They do not promise success.

## The errors, in plain words

All seven letters can show on day 78. Each repair is one six-minute group on day 78. No repair is run on days 75 to 77.

## Supplies for the section

The three fresh cards, the day 74 exit card, the sand lizard card, the 12-point score sheet, copied models for annotation, second-color pencils and the weather equipment.

## A room with nothing

Read the records aloud as invented. The class uses paper only and needs no device.
