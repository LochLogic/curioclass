# Item H fresh card (day 77)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. This is the scored work. The revised day 71 item H is marked beside it as repair evidence.

## New shallow pond

A shallow pond is sunny and warm. It is flooded all year.

## Floating plant

A floating plant needs sunlight and floating roots. It grows many new leaves.

## Rooted plant

A rooted plant needs shallower water than this pond has. It stays alive but loses leaves.

## Land snail

A land snail needs dry resting places. This pond has none. The snail cannot remain.

## Your task

Which does well, which does poorly, which cannot remain? Use needs and conditions. Annotate a system model.
