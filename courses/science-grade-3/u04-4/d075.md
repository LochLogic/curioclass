# Day 75: Check, how differences within one kind can help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the class model from the wall, one for each child. Print the fresh card for each child. Write the three directions on the board. Clear desks.

### Safety and set-up

Paper work only; nothing is handled. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Today's fresh work is the scored work, s04-E1, s04-E2, s04-E3 and s04-E4, scored after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. The revised item V from day 74 is marked beside it as repair evidence, not the score. Day 74's pages, s04-P74-1, s04-P74-2 and s04-P74-3, and ticket, s04-X74, are held until day 78. Read the product aloud: explain each difference with the counts and mark the model. Hand out the card and read it aloud twice; it is invented for practice.

#### 32 min. Item V fresh

Alone on paper, [[s04-P75-1]], [[s04-P75-2]] and [[s04-P75-3]]. Give no help and no feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: the counts, seven and two, six and three, five and one; the wax and the call as the supplied functions; young kept as its own record; no promise of success. Note error A (birds and plants compared), error C (a promise) and error E (a pair shows young) on the unit 4 record sheet for day 78.

#### 5 min. Independent checking

Children reread and tick each statement a number backs, [[s04-P75-4]]. Then collect the pages and cards.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Desks cleared; papers and cards collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the card on the board and read it aloud; answer on notebook paper and draw the model by hand. One projector: show the card; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No class model: draw two boxes and arrows.

**Skip.** The record sheet and the clean-up.

- **38 min. Assessment.** Read the card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s04-P75-1]], [[s04-P75-2]] and [[s04-P75-3]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "Which count backs what you wrote?" Expect: a number from the record, such as seven against two.
- **4 min. Checking.** The child does [[s04-P75-4]].
- **3 min. What is scored.** Read [[s04-E1]], [[s04-E2]], [[s04-E3]] and [[s04-E4]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the counts. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Difference, kind, survive, mate, young, evidence, record. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply causes or arrows.

### Tomorrow

Day 76 is the group check: own work, no feedback. Each child gives a brief spoken reason on a shore record, then writes. Day 75's pages, s04-P75-1 to s04-P75-4, are scored after class and held; the checklist goes back on day 78 and the scored pages on day 81. Errors A, C and E are what day 78's groups take.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
