# Item G fresh card (day 76)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. This is diagnostic. The scored work is the revised day 62 item G.

## Shore record

A small group of shore animals looks for food. Some dig. One is a lookout. With a lookout, eight of ten food trips end safely. Without a lookout, three of ten do. After a warning call, the group retreats.

## Colony record

A much larger colony shares sheltered resting places. When cold wind rises, animals move into the shelter.

## Your task

How can these groups help? Say food, staying safe and cold wind. Compare their sizes and jobs. Use annotated models.
