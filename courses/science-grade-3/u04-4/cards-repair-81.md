# Repair cards and exit cards, day 81

Four repair sets for day 81 and the exit cards. Plain line drawings, no brand names. Print "invented for practice" beneath each card.

## Repair sets

- **Set 1, letters J and K.** The grasshopper and cosmos cycles from day 79 on two cards, with two colours. Children draw the adult in one colour and the new young in the other, move the arrow to the eggs, and mark one stage that differs between the two cards.
- **Set 2, letters L and M.** A fresh pair: two duck parents and three ducklings on a card, with one trait that matches and one that differs. Children circle one of each and say what keeps them one kind.
- **Set 3, letters N and O.** Two pots from one seed packet, one in light and one in shade, with stem heights; a before and after record of a trained response. Children say what the family gave, what the place gave, and which record the young did not have.
- **Set 4, letters P and Q.** A claim line with a blank and a card of two flocks, with their watchers and feeders. Children add the words "helps, but does not promise", and count who feeds more.

## Exit cards (answered alone, read aloud twice)

- **X81a.** A newt cycle in four pictures with a line of days, and Sam's arrow from the dead adult back to the young.
- **X81b.** Two parent tulips with red cup flowers and four seedlings with leaf rulers at 6, 9, 7 and 10 cm.
- **X81c.** Cress stems in light and in shade, with rulers; an otter pushing a button, and its pup beside it.
- **X81d.** A goose alone looking up, and a flock of geese with one looking up.
