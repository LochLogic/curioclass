# Day 74: Join the tundra into one model

## Teacher page

### Before class

Put the three wall models side by side and clear a space for the class model. Hand back each child's item V with its look-for checklist. Set out second-color pencils and blank cards.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic, and the adult empties it after the readings and handles any ice. Wash hands after the jar. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 73's pages, s04-P73-1, s04-P73-2 and s04-P73-3, and tickets, s04-X73. Say the commoner of error F (the jewelweed, the clover and the cricket all do equally well) and error G (the hollow is too wet for any kind). Fix it in one move: for error F, draw the missing link on the cricket card; for error G, point to the jewelweed whose needs are met. Nobody is the example.

#### 8 min. Item V revision

Alone, in a second color, from the item V checklist and its question stamp, the day 68 draft, [[s04-P74-1]]. Walk and read; answer for no child. Keep the pages: the revised item V gets a repair mark after day 75. Look for a change that answers the stamped question and leaves the first writing readable.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P74-2]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud as supplied.

#### 18 min. Consensus model

4 minutes: read the three wall models side by side, group arrows, part and job rows, need and condition arrows. Each child picks one link and writes it on a card, with its record, [[s04-P74-3]]. Good links: the musk oxen ring and wolves; a white coat on snow, where the color matches; a tree frog or a cactus whose needs the winter tundra does not meet.

10 minutes: two children place at once, one from each end of the model: 15 turns of 20 seconds, about 5 minutes. Each says, "My card shows ___, because the record says ___." A child may pass or point while a partner places. **Ask.** Which piece of the wall model explains the white hare on the snow? Expect: the part and job row, white matches snow so it is hard to see. **Ask.** Which piece explains the ring of musk oxen? Expect: a group arrow, the ring protects. The class then adds any missing link.

4 minutes: read the model back together, pointing to each card. This is the model kept on the wall for days 75 to 78.

#### 9 min. Exit E74

1 minute: read the card aloud twice. 7 minutes: alone, [[s04-X74]]. Ask [[s04-X74b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. 1 minute: collect. Look for: the call lets the group retreat; brown matches brown ground; no seeds, so a need is missing. Error D says white hides best because it is bright. Error G says no animal can live in a meadow without seeds. Note letters. Nothing goes back before day 78.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Cards and pencils collected; the class model stays up.

### Other rooms

No devices or wall: draw the three models on large sheets taped together. No weather tools: read the dated adult record. One projector: show the wall models; children still write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall: use a table. No cards: cut paper into four. No second color: use a pen.

**Skip.** The repair, the weather record and the class placing.

- **8 min. Revise.** The child changes one thing in item V with a second color, [[s04-P74-1]].
- **4 min. Weather.** Read today's temperature and sky from a window or a thermometer. The child does [[s04-P74-2]].
- **12 min. One model.** Draw the tundra with the child. The child makes one card, [[s04-P74-3]].
  **Say.** "Which part of our drawing explains the white hare on the snow?" Expect: its white coat matches the snow, so it is hard to see.
- **9 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X74]], answered alone. Then ask [[s04-X74b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one placing turn each, and the read-back. Never cut the exit. Longer: say which link in the model has the weakest evidence.

### Words and supports

Model, group, difference, condition, need, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers; a partner may read cards aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 75 is the first check: own work, no help, no feedback. The child reads a new plant record and a new marsh-bird record, explains how differences could help, and marks a copy of this model. Day 75's fresh work is scored after class, and the revised item V is marked beside it as repair evidence; the checklist goes back on day 78.
