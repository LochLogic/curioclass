# Sand lizard card (day 78)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Eight minutes, alone, unscored.

Invented for practice. Sand lizards of one kind run from a hunter over loose sand. Ten with long toe fringes: 9 escape. Ten with short fringes: 4 escape. How could long fringes help? Say one thing the card does not show.
