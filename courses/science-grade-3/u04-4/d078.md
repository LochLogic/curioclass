# Day 78: Respond to the three assessment records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have every paper back with its written feedback and score. Set four group spots and write four names on the board: within one kind, group claims, variation links, habitat evidence. Lay out the cards each group uses. Set the weather tools where the helpers can reach them.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult empties it and handles any ice. Wash hands. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return records

Hand back day 75's assessment, day 76's assessment and day 77's assessment with feedback: day 75's s04-P75-1, s04-P75-2, s04-P75-3 and s04-P75-4, 2 minutes; day 76's s04-P76-1 and s04-P76-2, 2 minutes; day 77's s04-P77-1, s04-P77-2, s04-P77-3 and s04-P77-4, 1 minute. Day 74's pages, s04-P74-1, s04-P74-2 and s04-P74-3, and ticket, s04-X74, go back too. Each child marks the one component to work on, [[s04-P78-1]]. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 24 min. Four six-minute groups

This is the one response. Each child goes to the group of the component marked; a group is about 7 or 8. The teacher takes the groups in turn, 6 minutes each. In each group pairs speak at once: each child says one revision to a partner, 45 seconds each way, 90 seconds a group. The other children revise alone in a second colour, [[s04-P78-6]], and each writes one question the record could answer.
- Within one kind, [[s04-P78-2]]. Error A, 6 minutes: sort the animal cards by kind, then compare two of one kind; a hare is never compared with a fox.
- Group claims, [[s04-P78-3]]. Error B, 6 minutes: put the day 62 lone animal card beside an animal card from a group and mark where each found food.
- Variation links, [[s04-P78-4]]. Error C, 2 minutes: find the exception in the record and read it aloud. Error D, 2 minutes: turn the cloth over and search again for one colour. Error E, 2 minutes: lay the pair card and the young card side by side and point to the one that shows young.
- Habitat evidence, [[s04-P78-5]]. Error F, 3 minutes: draw the missing link on the card. Error G, 3 minutes: point to a second kind on the same card whose needs are met.

A child with no gap works [[s04-P78-6]] on the clearest link. Any child may pass in public talk. **Ask.** What did your revised link change, and what in the record said so? Expect: "I changed ___, because the record says ___."

#### 8 min. Fresh explanation

1 minute: read the sand lizard card aloud twice; it is invented for practice. 6 minutes: alone, [[s04-X78]]. 1 minute: collect. Unscored. Look for: long fringes may help on loose sand, and one thing the card does not show, such as young or other places. Error A says lizards and snakes can be compared. Error C says long fringes mean every lizard escapes. Note letters; there is no later hand-back.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P78-7]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud as supplied.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Papers filed in each child's folder; cards put back.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the group tasks on the board; answer on notebook paper. No weather tools: read the dated adult record. No groups: the teacher takes each marked component with the whole class in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the parent takes the child's marked component alone. No second colour: use a pen.

**Skip.** The groups, the wall model and the clean-up.

- **5 min. Look back.** Read the feedback together. The child does [[s04-P78-1]].
- **15 min. Revise.** Work the marked component only: [[s04-P78-2]], [[s04-P78-3]], [[s04-P78-4]] or [[s04-P78-5]]. Then [[s04-P78-6]].
  **Say.** "What did you change, and what in the record says so?" Expect: "I changed ___, because the record says ___."
- **5 min. Weather.** The child does [[s04-P78-7]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X78]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one repair move in the marked group, then the fresh explanation. Never cut the exit. Longer: write one claim the records cannot support, and the record that would be needed.

### Words and supports

Revise, component, kind, record, evidence, difference. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Home-language answers count. Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 79 starts the course check: unfamiliar organism records on unit 3's life cycles and traits. Today's pages stay in each child's folder.
