# Exit card, day 74

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Nine minutes: 1 minute directions, 7 writing, 1 collecting. Synthesis evidence, not scored.

A cold meadow. A lookout calls as a hunter comes. The group runs into shelter. On brown ground, hunters find 9 of 10 white animals. They find 1 of 10 brown animals. A seed eater cannot stay: there are no seeds.

(1) How do the call and the brown colour each help?

(2) Why can the seed eater not stay?
