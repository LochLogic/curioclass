# Day 81: Fix one slip, then try new cards

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the returned pages by child: day 79 and day 80, each with one written next step. Set four repair places, one for each group. Put a second-color pencil out for each child. On the record sheet list which children take which letters.

### Safety and set-up

Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. Paper and card only.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return of feedback

Hand back day 79's assessment and day 80's assessment, each with one written next step. Children read their own, silently, and mark the one slip they will fix. Nothing is scored again. A child with no letter takes a fresh card and writes a question that can be tested.

#### 20 min. Repair groups

Four groups, 5 minutes each with the teacher; a child goes to the group for the letter on their note, or to two groups. The rest work from the station cards in a second color, [[c2-P81-1]]. Group 1, letters J and K, from c2-E79c: draw the adult in one color and the new young in another, move the arrow to the eggs, and mark one stage that differs between two cards. Group 2, letters L and M, from c2-E79d: circle one trait that matches and one that differs on a fresh pair, then say what keeps them one kind. Group 3, letters N and O, from c2-E80a: two pots from one packet, light and shade; say what the family gave and what the place gave. Group 4, letters P and Q, from c2-E80e: add the words "helps, but does not promise" to a claim and count who feeds more. A group of up to eight gets about 30 seconds a child in a 5-minute turn; the rest of the time covers moving.

#### 8 min. Exit

Alone, the exit ticket: [[c2-X81e]]. Ask [[c2-X81f]], [[c2-X81g]] and [[c2-X81h]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Read each card aloud twice, 2 minutes each. Note letters on the record sheet. Collect the tickets. Then ask aloud, 3 minutes, one child answering each and any child may pass: [[c2-P81-2]] and [[c2-P81-3]]; note the letters. The revised papers, [[c2-P81-1]], stay with the students in the folder.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Pencils and cards back to the folder. Wipe the tables. Wash hands.

#### 10 min. Readiness

The unit 5 readiness look: read [[s05-R1]] once aloud, give 2 minutes for directions and 6 minutes to draw and write, and collect the papers in 2. Do not coach. It is not scored. Keep the papers.

### Other rooms

No printer: copy the repair cards onto the board, one group at a time. No second color: write the change in pencil and draw a box round it. A small class: the teacher takes each group in order and no card stays out.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the exit cards onto paper. No second color: use pencil in a box.

**Skip.** The living records, the groups and the readiness paper.

- **5 min. Your notes.** Read the next steps on your day 79 and day 80 pages. Pick one slip.
- **24 min. Fix one slip.** Do [[c2-P81-1]], then a second slip if you can.
  **Say.** "What did you change, and why?" Expect: "I changed one thing, and the first answer is kept."
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[c2-X81e]], answered alone. Then ask [[c2-X81f]], [[c2-X81g]] and [[c2-X81h]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: fix one slip only. Never cut the new cards. Longer: write a second question about the flock and say what it would change.

### Words and supports

Life cycle, trait, learned, inherited, flock, claim, because. Frames: "I changed ___ because ___." "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home-language answers count.

### Tomorrow

Day 82 starts unit 5. Today's readiness papers seat its pairs, and day 83 holds the support group.
