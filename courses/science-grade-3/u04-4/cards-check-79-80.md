# Check cards, days 79 and 80

Held back. Not shown, handled or taught from before day 79. Plain black line drawings on white, thick lines, no shading, no brand names, no words except where a card says so. Print "invented for practice" in small letters beneath each card. Read each card aloud twice.

## Cosmos card (day 79)

A cosmos plant life in five pictures with day numbers. Day 0: a seed. Day 7: a seedling. Day 50: a flowering adult. Day 75: seed heads. Day 95: a dead plant with seeds on the ground.

## Grasshopper card (day 79)

Day 0: an egg in soil. Day 12: a young hopper with no wings. Day 55: a winged adult. Day 70: the adult lays eggs. Day 105: the adult is dead. Sam's drawing has an arrow from the dead adult back to the young hopper.

## Foxglove card (day 79)

Two parent plants with spotted bell flowers. Four seedlings, 20 days old, each with spotted bell flowers, with rulers: 8, 11, 9 and 14 cm tall.

## Wren card (day 79)

Two wren parents with speckled chests and curved tails. Five chicks at 10 days, each with a speckled chest and a curved tail. Their weights: 12, 14, 13, 15 and 13 g.

## Flamingo panel 1: colour, a feeding clap and legs (day 80)

Two zoos, A and B, with chicks from one flock of long-legged parents. The parents' legs are about 100 cm.

- Zoo A feeds brine shrimp. The chicks turn pink. Zoo B feeds pale grain. The chicks stay pale gray.
- A keeper claps before each feeding. In week 1 no chick walks to the clap. In week 4 the Zoo A chicks do. The Zoo B chicks were never clapped for and do not.
- Leg lengths at 10 weeks: Zoo A chicks 78, 80 and 79 cm. Zoo B chicks, fed less, 66, 64 and 67 cm.

## Flamingo panel 2: the flock (day 80)

- Bites in a minute: a bird alone 5; a flock of 8, 9 each; a flock of 200, 14 each.
- A fox comes. A lone bird sees it at 8 steps. A flock sees it at 40 steps.
- A dry spell shrinks the lagoon from 20 steps wide to 6. The whole flock walks together to the deep end. All drink there.
- A flock of 8 has 1 watcher and 7 feeders. A flock of 200 has 20 watchers and 180 feeders.
