# Day 79: Four lives and their young

## Teacher page

### Before class

At the door, hand back day 78's exit ticket, s04-X78, without talk; none is used today. Print the four cards, each marked "invented for practice". Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. On the record sheet open the heard column for any child who answers aloud.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Say: "This is the first part of a check. I will not help. The cards are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages and show where answers go. Practice one touch: "Touch the word egg."

#### 15 min. Cycle models

Show the cosmos card and the grasshopper card. Read each aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c2-E79a]] (6 minutes), [[c2-E79b]] (3 minutes) and [[c2-E79c]] (3 minutes). At 14 minutes say: "Turn the page."

#### 20 min. Trait data

Show the foxglove card and the wren card. Read each aloud twice (4 minutes). Children write [[c2-E79d]], [[c2-E79e]], [[c2-E79f]] and [[c2-E79g]], 4 minutes each. A child who answers aloud is heard by the teacher, who ticks the heard column and says nothing.

#### 2 min. Living care

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest sit with pencils down. Collect every page.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Cards and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks. Wash hands.

### Marking

After class, 40 minutes: mark the seven items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: J, K, L or M. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each card on the board and hide it between parts; answers go in boxes on plain paper. A small class: the same times.

### At home

**Swap.** Any two real plants or pets may stand in for the cards; read the numbers yourself.

**Skip.** The directions practice, the living care and the clean-up.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the cards are invented. Read each card aloud once. Your child answers [[c2-E79a]] to [[c2-E79g]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Do the young look just like their parents?" Expect: "Mostly, but one trait can differ, like height."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Life cycle, egg, seedling, adult, trait, likeness, differs, one kind. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 80 is the second part of the check: two flamingo panels, one on color and legs and one on the flock. Nothing from today is handed back until day 81.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
