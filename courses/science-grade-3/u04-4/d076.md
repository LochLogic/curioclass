# Day 76: Check, how a group can help

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set six group spots, five seats each, and the teacher's seat where each group speaks in turn. Print the fresh card for each child. Have the heard list ready: every child's name, one column.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and talk only; nothing is handled. Clean-up and filing is 6 minutes.

### The day

#### 6 min. Launch and transitions

Rules today: own work, no feedback. Today's fresh work is scored after class: s04-E5, s04-E6, s04-E7 and s04-E8. Nothing goes back today. The revised item G from day 63 gets a repair mark, not the score. Day 75's assessment pages, s04-P75-1, s04-P75-2, s04-P75-3 and s04-P75-4, are held until day 78. Read the card aloud twice, the shore record and the colony record; both are invented for practice. Call the first group to the teacher's seat; the others begin [[s04-P76-1]].

#### 24 min. Spoken reasons

Six groups of five, 4 minutes each. Each child gives one reason of about 45 seconds, five children 3 minutes 45 seconds, leaving 15 seconds to change groups. The five children take, by seat: food, staying safe, cold wind, the small group's jobs, and the colony's size against the small group. Each says, "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." The teacher notes a mark beside the child's name on the heard list: a record fact and a cause were both given, or not yet. Give no prompt and no feedback. Any child may pass and give the reason on paper.

The other five groups write evidence links, [[s04-P76-1]], with the card in front of them. Nobody talks across groups.

#### 9 min. Independent paper

Alone, each child completes the argument on the shore record, [[s04-P76-2]]. Give no help. Look for, on this fresh record (item G uses eight against four): eight of ten against three for food; the call and the retreat; the colony sharing shelter in cold wind; the small group with diggers and a lookout, the colony much larger. Note error B (every animal needs a group) and error C (a group always saves every one) for day 78.

#### 6 min. Clean-up and filing

Papers collected. The heard list is filed, unchanged, for day 80; no second observation or score. Chairs back.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the card on the board and read it aloud; answer on notebook paper. No room for six spots: three groups of ten speak in pairs. One projector: show the card.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No class: the parent is the listener.

**Skip.** The groups, the heard list and the filing.

- **34 min. Assessment.** Read the card aloud twice. The child writes [[s04-P76-1]] and then says one reason aloud to the parent. Then [[s04-P76-2]].
  **Say.** "What in the record shows the group helped?" Expect: eight of ten trips ended safely with a lookout, against three.
- **6 min. What is scored.** Read [[s04-E5]], [[s04-E6]], [[s04-E7]] and [[s04-E8]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the food link. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Group, lookout, job, food, safe, change, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply links.

### Tomorrow

Day 77 is the habitat check: own work, no feedback. The child reads a new pond record and argues who does well, who does poorly and who cannot remain. Day 76's pages, s04-P76-1 and s04-P76-2, are held until day 78. Errors B and C are what day 78's groups take.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
