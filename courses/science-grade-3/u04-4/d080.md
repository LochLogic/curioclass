# Day 80: A flamingo flock in two panels

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the two panels, each marked "invented for practice". Seat each child so they see only their own page. Cover the walls. Day 79's oral record stays on the record sheet unchanged and is not scored again today. Day 79's pages are not handed back until day 81.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and card only. Check allergies before any child touches a pot; wash hands after. Mealworms are never handled. Any child may pass.

### The day

#### 4 min. Directions

Say: "This is the second part of the check, and day 79's was the first. I will not help. The panels are new and invented for practice. You may pass." Hand out the pages. Practise one touch: "Touch the word claim."

#### 18 min. Panel 1

Show panel 1. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c2-E80a]], [[c2-E80b]] and [[c2-E80c]], 5 minutes each. At 17 minutes say: "Turn the page."

#### 18 min. Panel 2

Show panel 2. Read it aloud twice (3 minutes). Children write [[c2-E80d]], [[c2-E80e]], [[c2-E80f]] and [[c2-E80g]], about 4 minutes each. A child who answers aloud is heard by the teacher, who ticks the heard column and says nothing.

#### 2 min. Living care

The two observers date and note the pots and the mealworm box; the rest sit with pencils down. Collect every page.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Panels and pencils back to the folder. Wipe the desks. Wash hands.

### Marking

After class, 40 minutes: mark the seven items, each shown, shown with help or not yet seen; only shown counts a point. Name a letter only for its own gap: N, O, P or Q. Write one next step for one component on each page. Keep one record each.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw each panel on the board and hide it between parts; answers go in boxes on plain paper.

### At home

**Swap.** Any bird flock seen from a window or in a picture may stand in; read the numbers yourself.

**Skip.** The directions practice, the living care and the clean-up.

- **40 min. The assessment.** Say the panels are invented. Read each panel aloud once. Your child answers [[c2-E80a]] to [[c2-E80g]] alone. Give no hint.
- **5 min. Look together.**
  **Say.** "Why do flamingos feed together?" Expect: "Many birds stir the water and many eyes watch."

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; a check day is not cut. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Claim, data, because, learned, inherited, flock, watcher, feeder. Reading aloud, a scribe, speaking, drawing and pointing all count.

### Tomorrow

Day 81 hands day 79's assessment and day 80's assessment back, each with one written next step. It ends with a short readiness paper for unit 5.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
