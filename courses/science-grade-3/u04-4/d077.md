# Day 77: Check, who does well in a new pond

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the class model from the wall, and print the fresh card, one for each child. Set the thermometer, jar, direction card and dated record where the helpers can reach them.

### Safety and set-up

Weather: the adult prechecks conditions, the class stays indoors in a warning, and no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic; the adult empties it and handles any ice. Wash hands. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer to the nearest mark and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P77-1]]. Say below zero in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud as supplied.

#### 4 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Today's fresh work is scored after class: s04-E9, s04-E10, s04-E11 and s04-E12. Nothing goes back today. The revised item H from day 72 gets a repair mark, not the score. Day 76's assessment pages, s04-P76-1 and s04-P76-2, and the heard list stay held. Read the product aloud: judge three kinds, give a cause for each, and mark the model. Read the card aloud twice; it is invented.

#### 30 min. Item H fresh

Alone on paper, [[s04-P77-2]] and [[s04-P77-3]]. Give no help and no feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: the floating plant does well, since its needs, sunlight and floating roots, meet the sunny flooded pond; the rooted plant does poorly: the water is too deep, and it loses leaves but lives; the land snail cannot remain, since there are no dry resting places; an arrow for each need and condition link and a cross for the snail. Note error F (all three do equally well) and error G (a hard place has no living thing) for day 78.

#### 3 min. Independent checking

Children reread and tick each judgment a cause backs, [[s04-P77-4]]. 

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Adult empties the jar. Papers and cards collected and kept apart from the rest.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the card on the board and read it aloud; answer on notebook paper and draw the model by hand. No weather tools: read the dated adult record. One projector: show the card; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No class model: draw three boxes and arrows.

**Skip.** The weather record and the clean-up.

- **36 min. Assessment.** Read the card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s04-P77-2]] and [[s04-P77-3]]. Give no help.
  **Say.** "What does the snail need, and does the pond have it?" Expect: dry resting places, and the pond has none, so it cannot remain.
- **4 min. Checking.** The child does [[s04-P77-4]].
- **4 min. What is scored.** Read [[s04-E9]], [[s04-E10]], [[s04-E11]] and [[s04-E12]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the three judgments. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Need, condition, does well, does poorly, cannot remain, cause, evidence. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply causes or arrows.

### Tomorrow

Day 78 is the one response. Each child gets a look-for checklist for day 75's, day 76's and day 77's work and works on the component missed in a group by letter, then writes a fresh explanation alone. The scored pages, day 77's s04-P77-2, s04-P77-3, s04-P77-4 and s04-P77-1 among them, come back on day 81. Errors F and G are what day 78's habitat group takes.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
