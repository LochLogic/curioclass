# Product H card (day 112, revised on day 113)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read aloud once. Each child works alone on paper and a copy of the arrows sheet.

## A raised doorway

A model room sits on a table. Rainwater runs toward its door. A new raised doorway blocks the water. A ramp keeps the entrance step-free for a wheelchair.

## The goal and the limits

Goal: at most 2 counters wet. Limits: at most 11 tokens, and the entrance stays step-free through a ramp.

## The trials

With the doorway and the ramp: counters wet 1, 2 and 1. Without them: counters wet 6, 5 and 6. The doorway and the ramp cost 10 tokens.

## Your task

Draw the arrows. Make a merit claim. Say what the trials cannot show.
