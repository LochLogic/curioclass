# Day 112: One design, one claim

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out the student pages, product H cards and arrows sheets. Have a tally ready for the three designs: the flood wall, the fan-tested roof and the lightning system. Product H earns six points, one each: a claim; the counts; the cost against the limit; the step-free entrance; the harm made less; one thing not tested. Feedback is written after class, one note for each point.

### Safety and set-up

Paper and talk only; nothing is handled.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair, directions

Hand back day 111's exit tickets, s06-X111. The commonest letter, 2 minutes: error G, name one condition not tested for each record; or error E, one chain, hazard, design, harm. No child is named. Then read the product H directions once, 1 minute: you work alone on paper; you will use the card and your arrows sheet; I will not help.

#### 17 min. Design practice

Pairs choose one design studied, the wall, the roof tape or the lightning system, and tally the choices; if one is unchosen, ask a pair to take it. Every design is chosen by someone; no new design is built. If many made error G on day 111, open with one claim that names a condition not tested, 2 minutes. Pairs reread the records in their folders (3 minutes). Each child writes a merit claim, [[s06-P112-1]] (6 minutes): the goal, the limit, the counts, what harm is less, and one thing not tested.

Then pairs swap claims with another pair (5 minutes). The readers ask "what did you see that shows that?" and point to the record. Each author may pass. Children revise one part in a second color (3 minutes). No one is the example of a mistake.

**Ask.** What did you see that shows that? Expect: a count from the record, such as "2, 3 and 2 houses wet", or the strips and the trials.

#### 18 min. Product H

Alone, on paper. Read the directions and the card once: a model room, rain running toward its door, a raised doorway and a ramp that keeps the entrance step-free. Then do not help: point only to the card. Each child draws the arrows, [[s06-P112-2]]; checks the goal and both limits, [[s06-P112-3]]; makes a merit claim, [[s06-P112-4]]; and says what the trials cannot show, [[s06-P112-5]]. Do not supply the limits, the counts or the evidence limit. Do not score today: written feedback follows after class, and product H is revised on day 113 and scored after day 116.

#### 4 min. Exit E112

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X112]]. Ask [[s06-X112b]] and [[s06-X112c]] aloud, 3 to 4 children each. Look for: (a) the fastening has merit, the roof stays on within the 9-token limit; (b) less roof is lost; wind still happens, or stronger wind was not tested. Error E says the fastening stops wind. Error F says cost does not matter if it works. Error G says it will protect real roofs from any storm. Note the letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Product H and the arrows sheets are collected for scoring outside class. Everything else stays in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the product H card on the board; children answer on notebook paper. One projector: show the card once, then take it down.

### At home

**Swap.** No second pair: the adult reads the claim and asks the question. No printer: copy the card onto paper.

**Skip.** The swap between pairs and the tally.

- **15 min. Design practice.** The child picks one design from the folder and does [[s06-P112-1]].
  **Say.** "What did you see that shows that?" Expect: a count from the record.
- **15 min. Product H.** Read the card once, then wait. The child does [[s06-P112-2]] to [[s06-P112-5]] alone.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X112]], answered alone. Then ask [[s06-X112b]] and [[s06-X112c]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one design, no swap; never cut product H or the exit. Longer: the child writes how a second design could be added within the limit.

### Words and supports

Merit, goal, limit, claim, evidence, tested. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers; a partner may scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 113 opens with Return and repair on this exit, 2 minutes. Error E, 1 minute: one chain, hazard, design, harm. Error F, 1 minute: the limit box before any count. Error G is named in a word. Then each child gets product H back with its written notes and revises one part in a second color.
