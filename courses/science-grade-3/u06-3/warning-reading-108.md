# Warning reading (day 108)

Read aloud once, in our own words, from a public weather-service briefing for Omaha, Nebraska, dated December 17, 2025. It is a place far away. It is a warning, not an account of damage. No one is asked for a story from home.

## The reading

The date is December 17, 2025. The weather service for Omaha has news about wind.

On Wednesday afternoon the wind blows from the south. It gusts in strong puffs.

Then a cold front comes through. The wind turns and blows from the northwest. It gets much stronger on Thursday.

Strong gusts are likely on Thursday afternoon and evening. Some places in the east may see very strong gusts.

The service says strong wind can blow around things that are not tied down. It can break tree limbs. A few places may lose power. Tall vehicles may be hard to drive.

The service tells people to get ready. Tie down or bring in loose things from outside. Be careful on the road. Stay away from trees and anything that could fall.

The wind cannot be stopped. People can get ready so it hurts less. People get ready in different ways, and neighbors, schools and the weather service help.

Source: National Weather Service, Omaha, pages 1 to 3 of a briefing, retold by Curio Class.
