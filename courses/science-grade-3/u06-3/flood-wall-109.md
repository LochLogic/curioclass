# Flood wall evidence (day 109)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read aloud once. Paper only. No flood demonstration.

## The village

A model village sits on low ground beside a river. It has 10 houses.

## The goal and the limit

Goal: at most 3 houses wet. Limit: at most 12 tokens.

## The trials

Flood wall, 11 tokens. Three trials. Houses wet: 2, 3 and 2.

No wall, 0 tokens. Three trials. Houses wet: 8, 9 and 8.

## Your task

Use the trial table. Mark the goal box and the limit box for each design. Write one claim with the merit frame. Draw the arrows: the flood, the wall, less water at the houses.
