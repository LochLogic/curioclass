# Goal and limit cards (day 109)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Used for retrieval and, for children who missed, a small-group repair.

## Goal card and limit card, in pairs

- Goal card: what we want. Example: at least 5 of 8 paper boats stay dry.
- Limit card: what we may not pass. Example: at most 6 tokens to spend.

Sort these onto the right card: at least 3 plants kept; no more than 4 pieces of tape; at most 10 tokens; at least 6 of 9 stones stay put.

## A before-and-after diagram of a barrier

Before: a sandy beach, with waves reaching a row of houses. An arrow shows sand moving away.

After: the same beach with a low rock wall. The waves hit the wall. The sand stays. The houses are farther from the water.

Say what the wall changed. Say what the wall did not change. The waves still come.
