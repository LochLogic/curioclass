# Lightning protection evidence (day 111)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Paper only. No story and no storm watching. No explanation of how lightning forms.

## A labeled drawing

A building with a pointed metal rod on its roof, labeled "rod". A thick wire runs from the rod down the wall, labeled "wire". The wire ends in a metal bar pushed into the ground, labeled "ground".

## What each part does

A strike may land on the rod. The wire carries it down. The ground lets it spread out into the earth. The rod does not stop a strike. The three parts work together.

## The invented records

Ten model houses. Goal: at most 3 houses damaged. Limit: at most 9 tokens. Three trials each.

- Nothing: 0 tokens. Houses damaged: 9, 9 and 8.
- Rod alone: 3 tokens. Houses damaged: 6, 7 and 6.
- Complete system: 8 tokens. Houses damaged: 2, 2 and 1.

## Your task

Mark the goal box and the limit box for each design. Write a claim with the merit frame. Revise your arrows: the hazard, the design, less harm.
