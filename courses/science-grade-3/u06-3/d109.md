# Day 109: Judging a flood wall

## Teacher page

### Before class

Draw the trial table on the board with its goal and limit boxes. Print the goal and limit cards and the arrows sheets. Choose the helper pair and six children to hear.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. Thermometer non-glass, jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 108's exit tickets, s06-X108. Error E, 3 minutes: lay "yes, getting ready stops the wind" beside the chain, warning, get ready, less harm. The wind still blows. No child is named.

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P109-1]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record, said to be supplied.

#### 6 min. Retrieval and repair

Review: grade 2 barriers, goals and limits, merit. Pairs sort four cards, [[s06-P109-2]], then say what a low rock wall on a beach changes and does not change, [[s06-P109-3]] (4 minutes). Misses join a small group for 2 minutes: the before-and-after beach diagram, or matched goal and limit cards, which also serve children who missed day 97's retrieval.

**Ask.** What is a goal, and what is a limit? Expect: a goal is what we want; a limit is what we may not pass.

#### 10 min. Flood evidence

Paper only: no flood is made. Say the counts are invented. Read the village card once: a model village on low ground beside a river, 10 houses. Goal: at most 3 houses wet. Limit: at most 12 tokens. Fill the table for three trials each: flood wall, 11 tokens, houses wet 2, 3 and 2; no wall, 0 tokens, houses wet 8, 9 and 8.

Mark the no-wall row for the class: limit box first, 0 tokens is within 12, yes; goal box, 8, 9 and 8 are more than 3, no. Leave the wall's row for the pairs.

**Ask.** If the wall cost more than 12 tokens, which box would fail? Expect: the limit box, so it would not have merit, even if every house stayed dry.

#### 12 min. Judge the wall

Pairs mark both rows, [[s06-P109-4]] (3 minutes). Each child then writes a claim, [[s06-P109-5]] (6 minutes). While they write, hear six children from the rotating six (review), 1 minute each; the record names who. Expect: the wall has merit, 2, 3 and 2 wet is at most 3, 11 is within 12, fewer houses are wet; the record does not show a bigger flood or another village. If a claim says the wall stops the flood, ask what was counted: wet houses. In the last 3 minutes three children may read a claim aloud; each may pass.

#### 5 min. Arrows

Pairs fill the arrows sheet, [[s06-P109-6]]: the flood, the wall, less water at the houses. Say aloud: the flood still comes. If many made error E on day 108, open with one chain on the board, 2 minutes.

#### 4 min. Exit E109

Read the card once. Alone, [[s06-X109]]. Ask [[s06-X109b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: the ditch; the trench breaks the limit; one thing not shown. Error F picks the trench. Error E says the ditch stops the flood. Error G says every street with a ditch is safe. Note the letters. Student pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the table and boxes on paper. One projector: show the table; children copy the marks.

### At home

**Swap.** No thermometer: write the sky and wind from a window. No printer: copy the table onto paper.

**Skip.** The pairs, the helper pair, the small group and the heard six.

- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside; never look at the sun. The child does [[s06-P109-1]].
- **8 min. Goals and limits.** The child does [[s06-P109-2]] and [[s06-P109-3]].
  **Say.** "What is a goal? What is a limit?" Expect: a goal is what we want; a limit is what we may not pass.
- **10 min. The wall.** Read the village card aloud. The child does [[s06-P109-4]].
- **8 min. Claim and arrows.** The child does [[s06-P109-5]] and [[s06-P109-6]].
  **Say.** "Does the wall stop the flood?" Expect: no; fewer houses get wet.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X109]], answered alone. Then ask [[s06-X109b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: mark the wall's row and write the claim; never cut the limit box or the exit. Longer: one trial that would test a bigger flood.

### Words and supports

Goal, limit, token, trial, merit. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 110 opens with Return and repair on this exit. Error F, 2 minutes: the limit box before any count. Error E, 1 minute: one chain, hazard, design, harm. Then groups plan and test a taped roof in a low fan.
