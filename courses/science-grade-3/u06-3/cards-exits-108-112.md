# Exit cards, days 108 to 112

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read each card aloud once.

## Day 108

Invented for practice. A weather service card says strong wind is expected Tuesday afternoon in the river valley. Bring in loose things. Stay inside in the strongest gusts. Pip says: if we get ready, the wind will stay away. Is he right? Why?

## Day 109

Invented for practice. Goal: at least 8 of 10 paper cars stay dry. Limit: at most 9 tokens. The ditch costs 7 tokens. With it, 9, 8, 9 cars stay dry. Without it, 4, 3, 4. The trench costs 12 tokens. With it, 10, 10, 10 stay dry. Which design has merit? What do the trials not show?

## Day 110

Invented for practice. Goal: an awning stays on in at least 2 of 3 trials. Limit: at most 2 pieces of tape. Awning A: 1 piece, on in 1 of 3 trials. Awning B: 2 pieces, on in 3 of 3. Awning C: 4 pieces, on in 3 of 3. Which awning has merit? Say why.

## Day 111

Invented for practice. A model greenhouse in a hail box. Goal: at most 3 of 10 glass squares cracked. Limit: 9 tokens. Nothing: 8, 9 and 8 cracked. Net alone, 4 tokens: 5, 5 and 6. Net with frame and slope, 8 tokens: 2, 3 and 2. Solid roof, 12 tokens: 0, 0 and 1. Which design has merit? What do the records not show?

## Day 112

Invented for practice. A roof fastening costs 8 tokens. The limit is 9 tokens. In three wind trials the fastened roof stays on. The unfastened roof comes off. (a) Make a merit claim using the results and the cost. (b) What harm is less? What can the trials not show?
