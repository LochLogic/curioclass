# Day 108: A warning, and getting ready

## Teacher page

### Before class

Mark the warning's three pauses: date and place, hazard, what people are told. Print the three-box card for each pair and an arrows sheet for each child. Write on the board: How can a hazard cause less harm?

### Safety and set-up

Paper and talk only; nothing is handled.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 107's exit tickets, s06-X107. Error C, 3 minutes: lay the one day, or the 12 or the 22, beside the many-year strip; climate is the range over many years. Error D, 2 minutes: join one detail from each card, "warm all year" and "11 to 25 rainy days". No child is named.

#### 6 min. Retrieval and repair

Review, not new: kindergarten forecasts and grade 2 fast and slow change. Pairs answer [[s06-P108-1]] aloud, 4 minutes. Children who miss it join a 2-minute small group with the forecast pictures: the five-picture sequence, then the rapid and slow pair.

**Ask.** How does a forecast help people? Expect: they know what may come, so they get ready first.

#### 15 min. Warning reading

Say first: this is retold in our words from a public weather service briefing for Omaha, Nebraska, dated December 17, 2025. It is far away and says what is expected, not what happened. No one tells a story from home; sharing is a choice. If a child starts one or seems upset, use the opener's care line.

Read the warning once, in three pauses of about 1 minute each. After each pause pairs fill one box of the three-box card, [[s06-P108-2]], in 2 minutes: date and place; the hazard; what people are told to do. Spoken, drawn or pointed answers count. Expect: December 17, 2025, Omaha; strong wind gusts on Thursday; tie down or bring in loose things, take care on the road, stay away from trees.

**Ask.** What does the warning promise, and what does it not? Expect: strong gusts are likely and here is what to do; it does not say the wind will stop, or who exactly it will hurt ("some places", "a few").

#### 10 min. Hazard and harm

Say three board words: hazard, weather that can hurt (here, strong wind); harm, what a hazard does to people and things; warning, a weather service statement that a hazard is expected. Pairs name two harms from the reading, [[s06-P108-3]] (3 minutes). Expect: loose things blown around, broken tree limbs, a few places without power, tall vehicles hard to drive.

Then draw the kindergarten forecast chain (review) on the board, extended: warning, get ready, less harm. Children fill the arrows sheet, wind, what people do, less harm, and tick whether the wind still happens, [[s06-P108-4]] (4 minutes). Say aloud: weather cannot be stopped; harm can be lessened. Lessen means make smaller.

**Ask.** What can people not stop, and what can they lessen? Expect: not the wind; the harm.

#### 5 min. Question board

Point to the board question (review). Each pair writes one thing noticed in the reading on a slip, [[s06-P108-5]], and posts it. Read three slips aloud as they come. Student pages stay in folders.

#### 4 min. Exit E108

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X108]]. Look for: no, getting ready does not stop wind, it lessens the harm from flying things. Error E says yes, getting ready stops the wind. Note the letter.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw three boxes on paper. One projector: show the boxes and the chain; the warning is heard.

### At home

**Swap.** No partner: the adult is the pair. No printer: draw three boxes on paper.

**Skip.** The return and repair, the small group and the question board.

- **5 min. Forecasts.** The child does [[s06-P108-1]].
  **Say.** "How does a forecast help people?" Expect: they know what may come and get ready first.
- **12 min. The warning.** Read the warning aloud once, in three parts. The child fills the three boxes, [[s06-P108-2]].
- **10 min. Harm and chain.** The child does [[s06-P108-3]] and [[s06-P108-4]].
  **Say.** "Can people stop the wind? What can they do?" Expect: no; they can make the harm smaller.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X108]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one harm in [[s06-P108-3]]; never cut the three boxes, the chain or the exit. Longer: what a town could do before a warning.

### Words and supports

Hazard, harm, warning, lessen, forecast. Frame: "The hazard is ___. The harm is ___. People can ___, so the harm is less." Accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers; a partner may scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 109 opens with Return and repair on this exit. Error E, 3 minutes: the answer "yes, getting ready stops the wind" laid beside one chain drawn on the board, warning, get ready, less harm, with the wind still blowing. Then the class judges a flood wall from invented trials, against a goal and a limit.
