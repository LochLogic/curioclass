# Forecast and change pictures (day 108)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Used with children who missed the retrieval, in a small group.

## A forecast-to-action picture sequence

1. A radio on a table. A voice bubble says: strong storm tonight.
2. A family carries a chair and a flower pot from the yard into the house.
3. A person closes a window shutter and ties a gate.
4. Night. Heavy rain and wind. The family is inside. The things from the yard are safe.
5. Morning. A few leaves lie on the ground. The yard is tidy.

## A rapid-versus-slow pair

Picture A: a steep sandy bank. After one hard storm, a large piece has slid into the river. Label: rapid.

Picture B: a river bend. Over many years, the edge has worn back a little at a time. Label: slow.

Say which is rapid and which is slow. Say how a forecast could help with only one of them.
