# Day 110: A roof in a fan

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, test the fan and the house, inspect the cord, mark the fan distance and the observers' line, and cut the tape into strips. Print the roof records. Draw the pooled table on the board. Choose the helper pair.

### Safety and set-up

Per group: house, roof, tape, record. The adult alone places the house, plugs into a ground-fault outlet and switches the fan, on low, guarded, with water kept away. Everyone wears impact eye protection throughout, over glasses; observers stay behind the line, fingers and hair clear. Unplug before collecting anything. An adult prechecks the weather; no one looks at the sun; in a warning, stay indoors. The adult handles any ice. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P110-1]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record.

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 109's exit tickets, s06-X109. Error F, 2 minutes: the limit box before any count, on one goal card and one limit card. Error E, 1 minute: one chain, hazard, design, harm; the hazard still happens. No child is named.

#### 4 min. Plan

If many made error F, open with one goal card and one limit card, 2 minutes. Roof retention means the roof stays on for a 10-second run, yes or no. The tape limit is at most 4 strips. Goggles on. Groups write a tape plan, how many strips and where, and predict which roof stays on, [[s06-P110-2]].

#### 18 min. Fan station

Six groups of five visit the adult-run fan in turn, matched: low setting, marked distance, 10 seconds. Visit 1 is 2 minutes: one untaped run, one taped run to the group's plan, about 45 seconds each; the six visits take minutes 0 to 12. Each group then improves its roof within 4 strips. Visit 2 is 1 minute, minutes 12 to 18: one retest. Each child writes yes or no, [[s06-P110-3]]. Waiting groups plan and tally. Six groups times 3 minutes is 18.

If a roof comes off at once or never leaves, the adult changes the marked distance and repeats once; keep both. If the fan fails, use the invented record: 3 untaped roofs all come off, 1 of 3 taped roofs comes off. Say aloud that it is invented.

#### 7 min. Explanation

Each group gives its untaped and taped result; tally one class table on the board (3 minutes). Groups write one claim, [[s06-P110-4]] (4 minutes): did tape lessen roof loss in this model, and what does the model not show? Expect: taped roofs stayed on more often, so tape lessened roof loss here; not shown: real roofs, stronger wind.

#### 5 min. Record and exit E110

Copy the class table onto the record, [[s06-P110-5]] (2 minutes). Then read the card once. Alone, [[s06-X110]] (3 minutes). Look for: awning B; C breaks the tape limit or A misses the goal. Error F picks C for being strongest. Error G says B stays on in any storm. Error E says B stops the wind. Note the letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Unplug first, then collect parts (1 minute). Each child wipes and bags their goggles (1 minute). The adult bins scraps and empties the jar (1 minute). Student pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No fan or not enough eye protection: use the invented record, said to be invented, as the class table. No printer: copy the record onto paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No fan or eye protection: use the invented record, said to be invented. No printer: copy the table onto paper.

**Skip.** The class table, the groups and the line.

- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside; never look at the sun. The child does [[s06-P110-1]].
- **5 min. Plan.** The roof stays on for 10 seconds, yes or no. Use 4 strips or fewer. The child does [[s06-P110-2]].
- **15 min. Test.** The adult alone puts the house and roof in front of a room fan on low, 10 seconds a run. Both wear eye protection; the child stands back. The child does [[s06-P110-3]].
  **Say.** "Did tape help the roof stay on?" Expect: yes, if the taped roof stayed on and the untaped one did not.
- **5 min. Claim.** The child does [[s06-P110-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X110]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one untaped and one taped run, no retest; never cut the limit or the exit. Longer: one more test and its strips.

### Words and supports

Roof retention, trial, tape limit, merit, model. Frame: "I think ___ has merit because ___, so ___. The record does not show ___." Accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 111 opens with Return and repair on this exit, 5 minutes. Error E, 2 minutes: one chain, hazard, design, harm. Error F, 2 minutes: the limit box first, on one goal card and one limit card. Error G, 1 minute: name one condition not tested for each record. Then the class reads a lightning protection drawing.
