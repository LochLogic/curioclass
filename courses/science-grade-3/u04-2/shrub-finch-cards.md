# Shrub and finch cards (day 67)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud once. Each card is 50 words or fewer.

## Plum shrub card

Plum shrubs of one kind grow in a field. On some shrubs the thorns were clipped. Each clipped shrub lost 9 leaves to browsing. Each shrub with thorns left lost 3 leaves.

## Finch choice card

Female finches of one kind choose between males. Ten of 12 females chose the bright male.

## Hawk card

Invented for practice. In a bare field, hawks found bright males more often than dull males.

## Chick card

Chicks were seen at 9 of 12 nests.

## Statement cards

Sort each statement onto a shelf: survive, find a mate, or have young.

- The shrubs with thorns kept more leaves.
- Hawks found bright males more often.
- Ten of 12 females chose the bright male.
- Chicks were seen at 9 of 12 nests.
- The clipped shrubs lost more leaves.
- Eight of 12 females stayed near the bright male all morning (invented).
