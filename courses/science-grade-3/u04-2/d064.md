# Day 64: One kind, many looks

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the kind cards and the part job row sheet for each pair, and cut the six cards. Put two pictures of unit 3 class plants of one kind on the board, one tall and one short, or draw two. Write the board question large: Which differences matter? Do they help everywhere?

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Cards are paper. Clean-up is 3 minutes: cards into envelopes, row sheets collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 4 min. Retrieval

Alone, 2 minutes each: the two plants on the board, [[s04-P64-1]], then the burr seed, [[s04-P64-2]]. Walk and hear the rotating six, 20 seconds each. Key: the hooks fit the job.

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 63's pages and ticket, s04-X63. Run the commoner of B and C, 2 minutes. Error B (every animal needs a group): set the owl card beside the mice card and mark where each got food. Error C (the mice always get enough): the card says they carry seeds, not that they always get enough. Then the retrieval miss, 2 minutes: outline one feature on the board plant, or draw one hook-to-cloth arrow. Whole class if many; otherwise a small group while the rest compare the two plants. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 31 min. One kind, many looks

5 minutes: read the hare record aloud and say it is invented for practice. Model: "The thick coat holds in heat, so the hare stays warm." Because gives the job; so gives the result. Then children write [[s04-P64-3]]. Say that some thin-coated hares were strong too: a coat can help, and it does not promise.
**Ask.** Which hares were still strong more often? Expect: the thick-coated hares, 9 of 10 against 5 of 10.

8 minutes: pairs sort the six kind cards, [[s04-P64-4]]. The cards are Hare A and B, Musk ox A and B, Fox A and B.
**Ask.** Which pairs can be compared? Expect: the two hares, the two musk oxen, the two foxes. Error A sounds like: compare Hare A with Fox A. Say: compare within one kind.

10 minutes: model one row on the board: part, thick coat; job, holds in heat; who it helps, the hare. Pairs fill rows for Hare A and Fox A, [[s04-P64-5]], and say The ___ can help because ___, so ___. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. A tail that keeps a fox warm can help; it does not promise. Error C sounds like: thick coats keep every hare strong. Point to 5 of 10.

8 minutes: each child writes one question for the board, [[s04-P64-6]], 2 minutes. The rotating six read theirs and add them, 30 seconds each. Sort the questions under two headings: differences, places. Any child may pass.

#### 3 min. Exit E64

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X64]]. Look for: same kind of animal; big ears can give off heat. Error A says an elephant can be compared with a zebra. Error C says big ears always keep an elephant safe. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Cards into envelopes, row sheets collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the six cards and the hare record aloud, draw the cards on the board, and children answer on notebook paper. No plant pictures: draw two plants.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw two hares, two musk oxen and two foxes, small. No class plants: use two plants outdoors or drawn.

**Skip.** The class board.

- **5 min. Look back.** The child does [[s04-P64-1]] and [[s04-P64-2]].
- **8 min. Hares.** The parent reads the hare record aloud. The child does [[s04-P64-3]].
  **Say.** "Which hares were still strong more often?" Expect: the thick-coated ones, 9 of 10 against 5.
- **8 min. Sort.** [[s04-P64-4]], with the six drawings.
- **8 min. A row.** [[s04-P64-5]]
- **4 min. A question.** [[s04-P64-6]]
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X64]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: do the hare sentence, the sort and one row. Never cut the sort, the hare sentence or the exit. Longer: find two plants of one kind and write one way they are alike and one way they differ.

### Words and supports

Kind, difference, compare, part, job. Frame: "The ___ can help because ___, so ___." Home-language answers count. Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 65 opens with a 5-minute Return and repair. Error A from today's exit takes 2 minutes: an elephant compared with a zebra; sort by kind first, then compare. Error C reads: big ears always keep an elephant safe. Then the class searches for paper moths on a patterned cloth and tallies what is picked.
