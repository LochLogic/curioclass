# Day 67: Thorns and a bird

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the shrub and finch cards and the part job row sheet for each pair, and cut the six statement cards. Draw three shelves on the board: survive, find a mate, have young. Keep the plum shrub, finch, hawk and chick cards for reading aloud.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Cards are paper. Clean-up is 3 minutes: statement cards into envelopes, row sheets collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 66's pages, s04-P66-1, s04-P66-2, s04-P66-3, s04-P66-4, s04-P66-5, s04-P66-6 and s04-P66-7, and tickets, s04-X66. Error C (matching real bugs always live longer), 2 minutes: say what a paper trial cannot show. Paper is not alive. A second 2 minutes only if another letter shows. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 35 min. Thorns and a bird

8 minutes: read the plum shrub card aloud and say it is invented for practice. Clipped shrubs lost 9 leaves each; shrubs with thorns left lost 3. Pairs write [[s04-P67-1]].
**Ask.** What do the leaf counts show about thorns? Expect: thorns can protect leaves. Say: this does not show that thorn size is inherited. It shows protection.

10 minutes: read the finch choice card: 10 of 12 females chose the bright male. Say the counts are invented; a real study of house finches also found females preferring brighter males. Pairs write [[s04-P67-2]]. Then read the hawk card, invented: in a bare field hawks found bright males more often. Pairs write [[s04-P67-3]].
**Ask.** Is bright always better? Expect: no; in a bare field hawks found bright males more often. A difference can help in one way and cost in another.

8 minutes: pairs sort the six statement cards onto the three shelves, [[s04-P67-4]]. Cards: shrubs with thorns kept more leaves; the clipped shrubs lost more leaves; hawks found bright males more often (all survive); 10 of 12 females chose the bright male; females chose by looking (find a mate); chicks were seen at 9 of 12 nests (have young).
**Ask.** Which record shows young? Expect: the chick card, 9 of 12 nests. Error E sounds like: a pair formed, so chicks hatched. Lay the choice card beside the chick card: two records.

9 minutes: pairs fill a row for each case, [[s04-P67-5]], and write [[s04-P67-6]]. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass. Error C sounds like: thorns keep every shrub safe.

#### 3 min. Exit E67

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X67]]. Look for: it shows finding mates; it does not show young, which need their own record. Error E says pairs mean there are young. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Statement cards into envelopes, row sheets collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: read every card aloud, write the six statements on the board and children write shelf names beside them on notebook paper. One projector: show the cards; children write on paper.

### At home

**Swap.** No statement cards: write each statement on a paper strip. No shelves: use three piles.

**Skip.** The class sort and the clean-up.

- **8 min. Shrubs.** The parent reads the plum shrub card aloud. The child does [[s04-P67-1]].
- **8 min. Finches.** The parent reads the finch and hawk cards. The child does [[s04-P67-2]] and [[s04-P67-3]].
  **Say.** "Is bright always better?" Expect: no; hawks found bright males more often in a bare field.
- **7 min. Shelves.** [[s04-P67-4]]
  **Say.** "Which record shows young?" Expect: the chick card.
- **7 min. Rows.** The child does [[s04-P67-5]] and [[s04-P67-6]].
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X67]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read the shrub and finch cards, sort, and write the not-shown sentence. Never cut the sort, the not-shown sentence or the exit. Longer: write one more not-shown sentence about the hawk card.

### Words and supports

Thorns, protect, choose, mate, chick, shelf, record. Frames: "The ___ can help because ___, so ___." "This does not show ___." Home-language answers count. Read each card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 68 opens with a 3-minute Return and repair. Error E from today's exit takes 2 minutes: pairs mean there are young; lay the pair card beside the chick card and point to the one that shows young. Then children explain with evidence how a difference can help survive, find a mate and raise young, and write item V alone.
