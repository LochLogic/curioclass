# Day 68: Explain with the numbers

## Teacher page

### Before class

Hang the cause-and-effect model with its day 66 background box. Set out the hare record, the finch choice card and the chick card. Print one item V card sheet for each child. Plan 90 minutes after class for item V feedback, 3 minutes a child.

### Safety and set-up

No equipment and no weather record today. Pencils stay down while a card is read aloud. Clean-up is 3 minutes: papers collected, desks cleared.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 67's pages, s04-P67-1, s04-P67-2, s04-P67-3, s04-P67-4, s04-P67-5 and s04-P67-6, and tickets, s04-X67, 1 minute. Error E (a pair formed, so chicks hatched), 2 minutes: lay the choice card beside the chick card and point to the one that shows young.

#### 19 min. Explain

4 minutes: model an explanation on the day 64 hares. Part, thick coat; job, holds in heat; result, 9 of 10 strong against 5 of 10. Say: "The thick coat can help because it holds in heat, so the hare stays warm. Some thin-coated hares were strong too, so it does not promise."

8 minutes: children copy the model into the box and add arrows from the thorn and bird records, [[s04-P68-1]]: thorns to leaves kept; bright colour to being chosen; the chick record as its own box. Each arrow reads can help. Pairs check each arrow: does it promise?

7 minutes: pairs say a one-sentence explanation each, 45 seconds each, all pairs at once. Then each child writes it, [[s04-P68-2]], 4 minutes. Hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each. Any child may pass.

#### 12 min. Item V

Alone, with no help. Read the barley card and the meadow-bird card aloud once and say they are invented for practice. Children write [[s04-P68-3]] and mark their model copy. Four look-fors, 1 point each: root and survival, eight against three; plume and mates, seven against two; young, six against one, from the separate record; arrows that do not promise success. A support names the count. If a child stops, point to a card line; give no answer. Collect every paper.

After class, give written feedback, 3 minutes a child: mark each look-for supported or not yet and write one question for the weakest. Never write the answer. It is handed back on day 69, revised on day 74 and scored after day 75.

#### 8 min. Exit E68

1 minute: read the card aloud twice. 6 minutes: alone, [[s04-X68]]. 1 minute: collect. Look for: big thorns protect leaves; strong bills get food for the bird, for courting and for chicks; accepted seeds show a mate, and only the chick record shows young. Error E says pairing proves chicks hatched. Error C says big thorns guarantee a bush lives. Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: read the cards aloud and write the question on the board; children answer on notebook paper. No model on the wall: draw it on the board. One projector: show the model; children draw their copy.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall model: draw a cause box, an arrow marked so and an effect box. No second colour: use a pen.

**Skip.** The pair talk and the clean-up.

- **4 min. Hares.** The parent reads the hare record aloud.
  **Say.** "Why can the thick coat help?" Expect: it holds in heat, so the hare stays warm; some thin-coated hares were strong too.
- **8 min. The model.** The child does [[s04-P68-1]].
- **6 min. A sentence.** [[s04-P68-2]]
- **12 min. Item V.** The parent reads both cards aloud once. The child does [[s04-P68-3]] alone, with no help.
- **6 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X68]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: add the arrows, write item V and the exit. Never cut item V or the exit. Longer: write one question the barley record cannot answer.

### Words and supports

Evidence, count, survive, mate, young, arrow. Frames: "The ___ can help because ___, so ___." "This does not show ___." Home-language answers count. Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 69 opens section 4.3 with a 3-minute Return and repair: today's item V feedback, 1 minute, and the commoner of error E (pairing proves chicks hatched; lay the pair card beside the chick card) and error C (big thorns guarantee a bush lives; find the exception in the table), 2 minutes. Then the class matches what living things need to the conditions of a place. The revised item V stays in the folder for day 74.
