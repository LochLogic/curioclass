# Kind cards (day 64)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Six picture cards, two of each kind. Pairs sort them by kind, then say which pairs can be compared.

## The cards

- Hare A. A hare with a thick coat.
- Hare B. A hare with a thin coat.
- Musk ox A. A musk ox with horns set wide.
- Musk ox B. A musk ox with horns set close.
- Fox A. A fox with a bushy tail.
- Fox B. A fox with a thin tail.

## Hare record

Ten thick-coated and ten thin-coated hares of one kind live through a cold week. Nine thick-coated hares are still strong. Five thin-coated hares are still strong.
