# Fallback backgrounds (day 66)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Use these in place of the viewer. Predict first, then read the supplied trials.

## Background 1

A cloth with a yellow pattern. On it are ten yellow paper moths and ten green paper moths. They are laid out the same each time.

## Background 2

A cloth with a green pattern. On it are ten yellow paper moths and ten green paper moths. They are laid out the same each time.

## Supplied trials, six picks each

| Trial | Background 1: yellow picked | Background 1: green picked | Background 2: yellow picked | Background 2: green picked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 1 |
| 2 | 2 | 4 | 4 | 2 |
| 3 | 1 | 5 | 5 | 1 |

Say first: which colour will be picked less on each background?
