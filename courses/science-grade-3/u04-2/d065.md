# Day 65: Hide and search

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, an adult checks cloth allergies, clears tables and bags 20 moths for each group. Set two cloth squares and a tally sheet in each group's space. Write the class table large on the wall. Check conditions for the weather record.

### Safety and set-up

Paper moths only. Nobody plays prey, and nobody races: all search is seated. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar is plastic and no one looks at the sun. An adult prechecks the weather, goes with the two helpers, who alone touch the equipment, empties the jar and handles any ice. In a weather warning the class stays indoors. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair, rules and prediction

Hand back day 64's pages, s04-P64-1, s04-P64-2, s04-P64-3, s04-P64-4, s04-P64-5 and s04-P64-6, and tickets, s04-X64. Run the commoner of A and C, 2 minutes. Error A (an elephant with a zebra): sort by kind first, then compare. Error C (big ears always keep an elephant safe): in the hare record, 5 thin-coated hares were strong too. Rules, 1 minute: seated, ten of each color every turn, the same search each time. Each group writes its guess, [[s04-P65-1]], 2 minutes.

#### 30 min. Moth search

3 minutes: show one turn at the front. A turn is three steps of 30 seconds, called aloud as a pace cue. Hide: one child spreads all 10 yellow and 10 green moths flat while the group looks at the wall. Pick: the searcher picks the first six moths seen. Tally: the recorder marks yellow and green picks, then all 20 moths go back. Five turns, so each child searches once.

8 minutes: round 1 on the yellow cloth. Five turns of 90 seconds is 7 and a half minutes.

8 minutes: round 2 on the green cloth, the same turns. The cloth changed; nothing else did.

4 minutes: each recorder writes four totals on the class table, 40 seconds a group, while the others copy their totals into [[s04-P65-2]]. Each round adds to 30. A group that lost trials uses the supplied row, said aloud to be invented, in another color.

7 minutes: pairs read the table and write [[s04-P65-3]], 4 minutes. Then hear the rotating six, 30 seconds each.
**Ask.** What changed when the cloth turned over? Expect: the color picked less changed; on each cloth, the moths that matched it were picked less. Error D sounds like: bright colors hide best. Point to the cloth. If the table does not show this, say so: picks vary by chance; read the supplied row and say it is invented.

#### 3 min. Exit E65

Read the card aloud twice. Alone, [[s04-X65]]. Ask [[s04-X65b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: no, hiding depends on the cloth; test red bugs on a red cloth. Error D says bright colors hide best on any cloth. Note letters.

#### 5 min. Weather record

Two helpers from the rotating six read the thermometer and the rain jar, 30 seconds each, while the class writes [[s04-P65-4]]. Below zero is said in words. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record and say it is supplied.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Moths into bags, cloths folded, tables wiped, hands washed after the jar.

### Other rooms

No cloth: draw a yellow pattern and a green pattern on paper. No moths: cut small paper shapes. No weather tools: read the dated adult record aloud.

### At home

**Swap.** No precut moths: cut ten small paper shapes in each of two colors. No cloth: use a patterned towel, then turn it over or use another.

**Skip.** The class table and the weather record.

- **3 min. Guess.** The child does [[s04-P65-1]].
- **9 min. First cloth.** The adult spreads the moths flat. The child picks the first six seen, five turns, and the adult tallies and puts all 20 back.
- **9 min. Second cloth.** The same on the other cloth. The child does [[s04-P65-2]].
- **5 min. Read.** [[s04-P65-3]]
  **Say.** "What changed when the cloth changed?" Expect: the color picked less changed; the moths that matched were picked less.
- **4 min. Exit ticket.** [[s04-X65]], answered alone. Then ask [[s04-X65b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: skip the weather record and give its minutes to the turns. Never cut the second cloth, the totals or the exit. Longer: pick a third moth color and say which cloth would hide it.

### Words and supports

Moth, pick, tally, match, cloth. Frame: "The ___ moths were picked less on the ___ cloth." Home-language answers count. Moths with a texture as well as a color; a partner may tally. Read the directions aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 66 opens with a 3-minute Return and repair. Error D from today's exit takes 2 minutes: bright colors hide best on any cloth; turn the cloth over and search again for one color. Then the class uses the counts to say how a color could help a moth, and what a paper trial cannot show.
