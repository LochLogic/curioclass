# Exit cards, days 64 to 68

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 64

Invented for practice. One hot summer, ten elephants with big ears and ten with small ears. Weak from heat: 1 big-eared, 4 small-eared. (1) Why can we compare these two groups? (2) What job could big ears do?

## Day 65

Invented for practice. White paper bugs on a white cloth are hard to find. Mina says red bugs on the white cloth hide best, because red is bright. Is Mina right? What would you test?

## Day 66

Invented for practice. Paper bugs on a matching cloth: 4 of 20 were picked. On a different cloth: 11 of 20 were picked. What does this show? What does it not show?

## Day 67

Invented for practice. In one lizard kind, bright-throated males formed 11 of 15 pairs. No nests were checked. What does this show? Does it show young?

## Day 68

Invented for practice. Ten hawthorn bushes with big thorns each lose 2 leaves. Ten with small thorns lose 9 each. In one bird kind, strong bills open tough pods. Males offer seeds, and some are accepted. Later, chicks are seen eating seeds. (1) How can these differences help? (2) Do accepted seeds alone show chicks hatched?
