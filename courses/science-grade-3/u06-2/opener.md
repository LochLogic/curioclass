# Section 6.2 opener: does one year describe a climate?

## What the section does

Five days, 103 to 107. The big question is: "Does this year's log describe our town's climate?" Day 103 sets the class's one year beside a strip of the town's many years and teaches the words climate, region, range and year to year. Day 104 gathers climate information about the town and Manaus from two sources in a two-column chart. Day 105 compares Cairo and Utqiagvik, with whole-number month counts. Day 106 reads a place near the equator, a place near a pole and Omaha, then product C is done alone. Day 107 revises C, builds a wall world map of region cards and closes with the section exit.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Climate is the weather a place usually has over many years.** It includes the range, lowest to highest, and how much it changes from year to year.
- **One year is one year.** It sits inside the range. It is not the climate.
- **Two sources are better than one.** The atlas card says where and what kind. The station card says how much, with counts. Combine them.
- **Cold is told in months below freezing, never in numbers below zero.** No temperature below zero is graphed or compared.
- **Real records, our words.** The cards for Manaus, Omaha, Utqiagvik and Cairo come from public weather records. The numbers are rounded and the counts are ours. The cards say where the data came from.
- **No causes of climate.** Sun angle and air circulation are not taught here. If a child asks why, say it is a later grade's question.

## Scoring, outside class

Product C (day 106) gets a six-tick checklist and one question stamp, about 1 minute a child, and is revised on day 107. The six points come from day 115's fresh work; revised C is recorded beside them as a repair mark, not the score. Day 106 has no exit.

## The errors, in plain words

- **C:** this year is the whole climate. **D:** one source is enough.

## Supplies for the section

Climate strip sheets, printed maps (15), the two-column chart, atlas and station cards, region cards, a globe for the teacher if you have one, a second color. Weather equipment on days 105 and 106.

## A room with nothing

The printed map and the cards read aloud. The dated adult record for weather.
