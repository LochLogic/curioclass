# Manaus and town cards (day 104)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Teacher reads each card aloud once. Numbers come from public weather records, rounded, with counts made by us.

## Atlas card: Manaus

Manaus is a city in Brazil. It is just south of the equator. It stands beside a wide river in a green forest. Many people live in Manaus. The map is green.

## Station card: Manaus

Months below freezing: 0 of 12. Wet months with 20 millimeters or more of rain: 12 of 12. In January the many-year average is 307 millimeters. In January 2020 it was 442 millimeters. The driest month is August, with 56 millimeters. Data: Japan Meteorological Agency, edited by Curio Class.

## Atlas card: our town

The class fills this card from a map and an atlas page: where our town is, the kind of land around it, the color on the map.

## Station card: our town

The teacher prints this card from the town's station packet: months below freezing, wet months, a short many-year strip.
