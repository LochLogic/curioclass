# Exit cards, days 103 to 107

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read each card aloud once.

## Day 103

Invented for practice. Two towns counted rainy days in June for 30 years. Town A's counts run from 12 to 14. Town B's counts run from 3 to 20. Which town's rainy days change more from year to year? Say how you know. Spoken: this June, Town A had 19 rainy days. Is 19 usual for Town A? Say why.

## Day 104

Invented for practice. Atlas card: Region R is hot all year. Its map is brown, a dry place. Station card: rain falls on 1 to 4 days each month. Every year looks alike. Write one sentence about R's climate that uses both cards.

## Day 105

Invented for practice. Region V: the atlas says desert. The station says 0 months below freezing, 1 wet month. Region W: the atlas says mountain town. The station says 6 months below freezing, 5 wet months. It rained today in V. Which is colder? Which is drier? Name the card and numbers.

## Day 106

Invented for practice. Atlas card: Region M is a cold, high plateau. Station card: over 30 years, M has 7 months below freezing and 2 wet months. This year it had 3 wet months. Write one sentence about M's climate that uses both cards. Does this year change it? Why?

## Day 107

Invented for practice. Atlas card: Region T is warm all year. Station card: rainy September days over 30 years run from 11 to 25. One year had 12 and one had 22. (a) Combine both cards to describe T's climate. (b) One September day was dry. Does that change T's climate? Why?
