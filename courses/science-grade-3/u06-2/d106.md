# Day 106: Maps, texts and records together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Copy the product C card for each child and the three-place cards for each pair. Have the class's climate strip from day 103 ready for the repair. Choose the helper pair from the rotating six. Put second colours on the model table.

### Safety and set-up

An adult prechecks the weather; the class stays indoors in a warning; no one looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass, the jar plastic. Only the helper pair and the adult touch the jar; the adult empties it and handles ice. Helpers wipe hands inside the 5 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

The helper pair reads the thermometer to the nearest mark and the jar, 30 seconds each; the adult checks. Less than the first mark is "wet, too little to measure", never dry. Below zero is said in words. Children write [[s06-P106-1]]. If a reading fails, read the dated adult record aloud, said to be supplied.

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 105's exit tickets, s06-X105. Error C, 2 minutes: lay this year beside the many-year strip, one count among 30. Error D, 1 minute: link one detail from each card. The reading part opens with the same pair. Day 105's written tasks stay in folders. No child is named.

#### 8 min. Source reading

Read three cards aloud once: Manaus near the equator, Utqiagvik near a pole, Omaha in the middle. Pairs find each on the printed map, then write wet or dry and warm or cold for each, [[s06-P106-2]] (5 minutes). Then say which Omaha August is one year and which is many years, [[s06-P106-3]] (3 minutes). The pattern is the thing to use here, never a reason for it.

**Ask.** Which Omaha August describes the climate? Expect: the many years; one dry August is one year's weather.

#### 20 min. Product C

Alone, on paper, from the product C card. Read the directions and both cards once. Then do not help: point only to the card. Each child takes notes and names each source, [[s06-P106-4]]; describes P, [[s06-P106-5]], and Q, [[s06-P106-6]]; compares them, [[s06-P106-7]]; shows how the years change, [[s06-P106-8]]; and says how climate differs from one day's weather, [[s06-P106-9]]. Do not supply the link between the two cards. Collect the papers. Do not score today: written feedback follows after class, one note for each of the six points, and product C is revised on day 107 and scored after day 115.

#### 6 min. Model

The class adds a region column to the seasonal expectation model in a second colour: usual weather, range, year to year change, and the source. Each child may pass when the class talks. Say aloud: usual weather says what is likely, and a range from many years says how much it changes.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Papers collected for scoring outside class. The adult empties the jar.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the cards on the board; children answer on notebook paper. No globe: use the printed map. One projector: show the three cards in turn.

### At home

**Swap.** No thermometer: write the sky and wind from a window. No printer: copy the product C card onto paper.

**Skip.** The pairs, the helper pair and the model.

- **5 min. The weather.** Look outside. The child does [[s06-P106-1]].
- **5 min. Three cards.** Read the three cards; the child does [[s06-P106-2]] and [[s06-P106-3]].
  **Say.** "Which August is one year? Which is many years?" Expect: 12 millimeters, one year; 134, many years.
- **25 min. Product C.** The child does [[s06-P106-4]] to [[s06-P106-9]] alone; read the card once, then wait.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the child does only P in [[s06-P106-5]] and the years, [[s06-P106-8]]; never cut the sources or the one-day answer. Longer: the child writes a question a third source could answer.

### Words and supports

Pattern, wet, dry, warm, cold, many years, source. Frame: "The atlas says ___. The station says ___. So ___ has a ___ climate. Tomorrow can differ." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers; a partner may scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 107 returns product C with the written feedback. The commonest letter, error C or error D, is shown first for 2 minutes; then each child revises one part in a second colour. Then region cards go on the wall, and the class closes the section with an exit ticket.
