# Product C card (day 106, revised on day 107)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Read aloud once. Each child works alone on paper and a copy of the climate model.

## Atlas card

Region P is warm all year, with wetter and drier months. Region Q has cold winters and cool summers.

## Station card

A 30-year record of rainy August days. P runs from 12 to 24 days. Q runs from 2 to 10 days. Selected years: P had 13, 21 and 17. Q had 4, 9 and 6.

## Your task

Take notes from each card. Join them to describe P and Q. Compare the two. Show how the years change. Write how a climate is different from one day's weather. Name your source for each claim.
