# Day 104: Two sources, two columns

## Teacher page

### Before class

Make our town's atlas card from a map and an atlas page: where the town is, the land around it, the map color. Print its station card from the packet: months below freezing, wet months, a short many-year strip. Copy the cards and the chart for each pair. Choose today's six children to hear.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only today; no weather equipment. The globe stays with the teacher. Cards are collected in the last 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 103's exit tickets, s06-X103. Error C, one year taken as the climate: lay the class year beside the many-year strip, one count among 30. Day 103's written tasks stay in folders. No child is named.

#### 6 min. Map

On the globe and then the printed map, find our town and Manaus, [[s06-P104-1]]. Locations only; why places differ is a later grade's question, said plainly if asked. Pairs write while you walk.

#### 9 min. Source one

A source is a card or page that tells you something. Read the Manaus atlas card aloud once, then our town's. Pairs fill column one of the chart, [[s06-P104-2]]. Take the town's column from the class map and atlas page.

**Ask.** What does the map add that the atlas card does not? Expect: exactly where Manaus is, near the equator.

#### 9 min. Source two

Read the Manaus station card aloud once, then our town's. A wet month has 20 millimeters or more of rain. The many-year average is the usual amount over many years. Pairs fill column two, [[s06-P104-3]]. January 2020 was wetter than the average: one year, not the climate, so mark it as 2020. Our town's column comes from its packet card.

#### 10 min. Combine

Pairs write one sentence for each place with both columns: the atlas says, the station says, so. Manaus is [[s06-P104-4]]; our town is [[s06-P104-5]]. Hear six children, 1 minute each, the six you chose; the record names who. That is 6 of the 10 minutes. The rest keep writing and checking each other's use of both columns. Each child may pass; offer the sentence frame.

#### 4 min. Exit E104

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X104]]. Look for: hot all year and dry from the atlas card; rain on 1 to 4 days a month from the station card. Error D leaves one card out, either the dryness or the rainy days. Note the letters.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Cards and charts collected; maps back in the folder. Student pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: write each card on the board. No globe: use the printed map. One projector: show the chart and fill it as a class.

### At home

**Swap.** No packet: use Manaus only. No printer: draw two columns on paper.

**Skip.** The pairs, the heard six and our town.

- **5 min. The map.** The child does [[s06-P104-1]].
- **10 min. The atlas.** Read the Manaus atlas card; the child does [[s06-P104-2]].
- **10 min. The station.** Read the station card; the child does [[s06-P104-3]].
  **Say.** "What does the atlas say? What does the station say?" Expect: where it is and what it looks like; counts of months.
- **5 min. One sentence.** The child does [[s06-P104-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X104]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: Manaus only, no sentence for our town; never cut either column or the exit. Longer: write one question a third source could answer about Manaus.

### Words and supports

Source, atlas, station, wet month, average, column. Frame: "The atlas says ___. The station says ___. So ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 105 opens with the weather record, then Return and repair on this exit. Error D, one source left out: link one detail from each card. Then the class finds Cairo and Utqiagvik on the map, reads their cards and compares the two places.
