# Map cards (day 103)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Each pair gets a printed map and a key. An enlarged or tactile copy is ready for children who need one.

## The map

A simple world map. Land is white. Ocean is light blue. A dashed line runs across the middle: the equator. A dot marks the top of the map: the North Pole. A dot marks the bottom: the South Pole.

## The key

A white square: land. A blue square: ocean. A dashed line: the equator. A dot: a pole. A star: a marked region.

## The two marked regions

A star with the number 1 sits on land just above the equator. A star with the number 2 sits on land far up near the top, close to the North Pole.

## Your task

Find land and ocean. Find the equator. Find both poles. Find region 1 and region 2. Say which is nearer the equator and which is nearer a pole.
