# Day 103: One year or many years?

## Teacher page

### Before class

Pick one month. Count its rainy or snowy days in the class log and write the count on a card, face down. From the station packet, write the town's 30 counts for that month on the board, covered. Pick a month whose strip does not run from 4 to 14. With no packet, write this invented October strip, said aloud to be invented: 5, 3, 9, 4, 6, 2, 5, 8, 4, 6, 3, 5, 7, 4, 6, 5, 7, 3, 4, 6, 5, 8, 2, 5, 4, 6, 7, 3, 5, 6. This year was 7.

### Safety and set-up

Paper only today; no weather equipment. The globe stays with the teacher and is not passed round. Maps and sheets are collected in the last part.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 102's exit tickets, s06-X102. Error B, 2 minutes: lay the one warm day beside the winter record; most days were cold. A child who said the usual never changes, 3 minutes: count 25 odd days of 40; many odd days change our minds. Day 102's written tasks stay in folders. No child is named.

#### 6 min. Retrieval and repair

Pairs use the printed map and its key to find land, ocean, the equator and both poles, then region 1 and region 2, and write [[s06-P103-1]] (4 minutes). The map words are review from grade 2. Children who miss go to a small group with an enlarged or tactile map and a teacher model (2 minutes); the rest check each other. Show the town on the globe yourself.

**Ask.** Which region is nearer the equator? Expect: region 1; region 2 is nearer the North Pole.

#### 11 min. Hook

Show the card: this month our log has that many rainy or snowy days. Covered strip stays covered.

**Ask.** Does this year's log describe our climate? Pairs write [[s06-P103-2]] (3 minutes); any guess with a reason counts. Then uncover the strip and mark this year on it (4 minutes). It holds the town's same month over 30 years, one count a year.

**Ask.** What does the strip tell us that one year cannot? Expect: how many days are usual, the lowest and the highest, how much it changes (4 minutes).

#### 14 min. Worked example

Say it once, and write the words on the board (4 minutes). Climate is the range of weather a place usually has over many years, and how much it changes from year to year. A region is a large part of the world with its own usual weather. The range is from the lowest count to the highest. The strip counts rainy or snowy days only, so say the days, not the temperature.

Model the range on the board strip: point to the lowest count, then the highest. Pairs copy the 30 counts onto the sheet, one reading and one writing (3 minutes), then write [[s06-P103-3]] and mark this year (5 minutes). Last, [[s06-P103-4]] (2 minutes). If a pair says the range is 30, point to the counts.

#### 5 min. Model and question board

Add the strip beside the seasonal expectation model: our year, then many years from the lowest to the highest. Each child writes one question for the board, [[s06-P103-5]]. Each child may pass when the class talks.

#### 4 min. Exit E103

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X103]]. Look for: no; climate is the range over many years; one February is one year. Error C says yes, from 18 or from what was seen. Note the letters. Student pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the 30 boxes on the board. No globe: use the map. One projector: show the strip, then the model.

### At home

**Swap.** No class log: this year had 7. No packet: read 10 Octobers, invented: 5, 3, 9, 4, 6, 2, 5, 8, 4, 6.

**Skip.** The pairs, the board and the class model.

- **5 min. The map.** The child does [[s06-P103-1]] on any map.
- **10 min. The strip.** Read the counts aloud; the child writes them. The child does [[s06-P103-2]] before the range, then [[s06-P103-3]].
  **Say.** "What does one year not tell us?" Expect: the range over many years.
- **10 min. Words.** The child does [[s06-P103-4]] and [[s06-P103-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X103]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: copy ten counts, not 30; never cut the range, the sentences or the exit. Longer: find the year with the most days and ask if it changes the range.

### Words and supports

Climate, region, range, year to year, strip. Frame: "One year can tell us ___. It cannot tell us ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers.

### Tomorrow

Day 104 opens with Return and repair on this exit. Error C, one year taken as the climate: the class year laid beside the many-year strip. Then the class finds the town and Manaus on the map and gathers climate information from an atlas card and a station card, in two columns.
