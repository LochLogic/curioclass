# Day 107: The wall of regions

## Teacher page

### Before class

Write the written feedback on each product C, one note for each of the six points, after day 106. Fill the five place cards (our town, Manaus, Cairo, Utqiagvik, Omaha) with the ranges below. Put the wall map up. Choose six pairs from the rotating six to hear.

### The day

#### 6 min. Return and repair: product C revision

Hand back product C with its written feedback, 0 minutes. Show the commonest letter for 2 minutes: error C, one day taken as the climate, laid beside the many-year strip; or error D, one card left out, one detail from each. Each child then revises one part in a second colour, [[s06-P107-1]] (4 minutes). The revision is not scored today; product C is scored after day 115. No child is named.

#### 20 min. Synthesis

No new content: the wall is the practice.

**Wall (10 minutes).** Each child makes a region card, [[s06-P107-2]], from the cards of days 103 to 106, and places it on the wall map, about 20 seconds each. The ranges: our town, the strip from lowest to highest; Manaus, 56 millimeters in August to 307 in January; Cairo, 13 to 14 degrees in its two Januaries; Utqiagvik, below freezing to 6 degrees; Omaha, 23 to 25 degrees in summer. About six children take each place. Read the wall back aloud, twice, together.

**Pairs (6 minutes).** Six pairs describe one region in one sentence, 1 minute each; the record names who. The rest check the wall against their cards.

**Chart (4 minutes).** Pairs fill the three-row chart for one place, [[s06-P107-3]]. Today's weather is one day. A seasonal expectation is what a season usually brings. The climate range is the lowest to the highest over many years.

**Ask.** Which row can one day change? Expect: today's weather, not the climate range.

#### 12 min. Notebook

Each child writes in the notebook what changed in our model and which evidence changed it, [[s06-P107-4]]. The class adds one line to the seasonal expectation model in a second colour. Each child may pass when the class talks; offer the frame.

#### 4 min. Exit E107

Read the card once, invented for practice. Alone, [[s06-X107]]. Look for: warm all year and often rainy in September, from 11 to 25 days, from the two cards; no, one dry day is weather, and climate is the pattern over many years. Error C says yes, from one day or from 12 or 22. Error D leaves one card out. Note the letters.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Cards and charts collected. Student pages stay in folders.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the five places on the board; children draw the region card on paper. No globe: use the printed map. One projector: show the wall map and fill it as a class.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall map: draw one on paper. No cards: use the three-place cards.

**Skip.** The pairs, the heard six and the wall.

- **5 min. Revise.** Read the note on product C; the child does [[s06-P107-1]].
- **10 min. Region card.** The child does [[s06-P107-2]] for one place.
  **Say.** "What is the range? Is it one day or many years?" Expect: lowest to highest; many years.
- **8 min. Three rows.** The child does [[s06-P107-3]].
- **7 min. Notebook.** The child does [[s06-P107-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s06-X107]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: one region card, not five; never cut the revision, the range or the exit. Longer: write which place changes most year to year and how a card shows it.

### Words and supports

Region, range, weather, seasonal expectation, climate, source. Frame: "___ is ___ and ___. The range is from ___ to ___. One day is ___." Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, drawn or pointed answers; a partner may scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 108 opens with Return and repair on this exit. Error C, 3 minutes: one day laid beside the many-year strip. Error D, 2 minutes: one detail from each card, joined. Then the class reads a real forecast and a warning to ask how people get ready.
