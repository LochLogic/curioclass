# Three-place cards (day 106)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Teacher reads three cards aloud: a place near the equator, a place near a pole, and a middle place. Pairs say the wet, dry, warm and cold pattern of each.

## Near the equator: Manaus

Hot all year. Months below freezing: 0 of 12. Wet months: 12 of 12.

## Near a pole: Utqiagvik

Cool summers. Months below freezing: 8 of 12. Wet months: 3 of 12.

## In the middle: Omaha

Omaha is a city in Nebraska, in the United States. The summer months average 23, 25 and 24 degrees Celsius. Months below freezing: 3 of 12. Wet months with 20 millimeters or more: 11 of 12. In August the many-year average is 134 millimeters. In August 2020 it was 12 millimeters. Data: Japan Meteorological Agency, edited by Curio Class.

## Your task

For each place, say wet or dry, warm or cold. For Omaha, say which August is one year and which is many years.
