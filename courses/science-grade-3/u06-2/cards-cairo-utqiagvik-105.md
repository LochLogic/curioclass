# Cairo and Utqiagvik cards (day 105)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children. Teacher reads each card aloud once. Cold is told in months below freezing. Numbers are rounded.

## Atlas card: Cairo

Cairo is a big city in Egypt. It is beside a river. The land around it is desert. The map is tan.

## Station card: Cairo

Months below freezing: 0 of 12. Wet months with 20 millimeters or more of rain: 0 of 12. In a whole year about 25 millimeters of rain falls. Each of the two Januaries had 3 rainy days. The January average was 14 degrees in 2019 and 13 degrees in 2020. Data: World Meteorological Organization and a public daily record, edited by Curio Class.

## Atlas card: Utqiagvik

Utqiagvik is a town on the Arctic coast of northern Alaska, in the United States. It is near the top of the map. The sea beside it is ice for much of the year.

## Station card: Utqiagvik

Months below freezing: 8 of 12. Wet months with 20 millimeters or more of rain or melted snow: 3 of 12. The most in one month is 27 millimeters, in August. Summers are cool. No month is warmer than 6 degrees Celsius. Data: Japan Meteorological Agency, edited by Curio Class.
