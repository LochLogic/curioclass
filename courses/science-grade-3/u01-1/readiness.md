# Unit 1 readiness look

## Student

For the teacher. Day 1, the last ten minutes, after the hook. Use one minute for directions and nine for the three items. Read each item aloud once, without coaching. A child answers on paper by drawing or by writing one short sentence. Each page says "invented for practice". This look is not scored. Keep the papers, because day 2 uses them to choose the repair groups.
