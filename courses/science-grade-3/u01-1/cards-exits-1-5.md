# Exit cards, days 1 to 5

Each card is labelled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 1

A girl pushes a wagon along a path. Her hands touch the back of the wagon. Below it, two arrows, one longer than the other, labelled 1 and 2.

## Day 2

A small wind table: Monday north, Tuesday north, Wednesday south, Thursday north. Each entry has a small arrow picture.

## Day 3

Two small scenes side by side. Jo rolls a red toy truck gently on a carpet. Kim rolls a blue toy truck hard on a tile floor.

## Day 4

Two cards. A toy boat on a pond, with a child on each bank pulling a string. The two arrows are the same length. A girl holds a book still against a wall with her hand.

## Day 5

Four cards. 1: a ball at rest and one arrow pointing right. 2: a ball moving right and one arrow pointing left. 3: a ball moving right and one long arrow pointing toward the back of the tray. 4: a package at rest and two equal arrows from opposite sides. The arrows have different lengths on cards 1 to 3 so strength can be compared.
