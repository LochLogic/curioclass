# Exit cards, days 1 to 5

Each card is labeled "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names.

## Day 1

A girl pushes a wagon along a path. Her hands touch the back of the wagon. Below it, two arrows, one longer than the other, labeled 1 and 2.

## Day 2

A small wind table: Monday north, Tuesday north, Wednesday south, Thursday north. Each entry has a small arrow picture.

## Day 3

Two cards. Card 1: Mia flicks a toy car on a smooth floor, once gently and once hard. Card 2: Kim rolls a toy truck on a tile floor one time.

## Day 4

Two cards. A toy boat on a pond, with a child on each bank pulling a string. Sam drew two arrows of the same length, but the boat has moved toward the left bank. A girl holds a book still against a wall with her hand.

## Day 5

Four cards. 1: a wagon at rest and one arrow pointing left. 2: a wagon rolling left and one arrow pointing right. 3: a wagon rolling left and one long arrow pointing toward the top of the page. 4: a package at rest and two equal arrows from opposite sides. The arrows have different lengths on cards 1 to 3 so strength can be compared.
