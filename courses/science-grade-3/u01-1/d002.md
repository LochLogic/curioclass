# Day 2: Repair, and the weather record

## Teacher page

### Before class

Sort the readiness papers into three piles: pushes, sky patterns, weather. Write each child's slot on a table card. Set out six sets of tray, soft ball and whole-centimeter ruler; the small cardboard box waits for day 3. Cut apart the three sky rows. Whole centimeters on the ruler are review from grade 2.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges; floors stay clear. An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar. Nobody looks at the sun. The thermometer is non-glass.

### The day

#### 3 min. Return and repair

Return day 1's exit tickets (s01-X1). Error C, 2 minutes: lay arrow card A beside a strip marked "moved 8 cm" and card B beside one marked "moved 15 cm". Say: the arrow tells how strong the push is; the strip tells how far it went. Two records.

#### 1 min. Directions

Show the table cards: each child's slot (pushes, sky, weather) or "ready".

#### 30 min. Readiness repairs, up to three slots of 10 minutes

A slot that more than 10 of 30 children need is a whole-class slot. Otherwise meet that small group while ready children work at tables. All cards say invented for practice.

Slot 1, pushes. Groups of five push the ball in the tray gently, then harder, from one start line. Each child makes two pushes, about 20 seconds each: under 4 minutes a group. Read each distance in whole centimeters. Draw one ball and two arrows, [[s01-P2-1]]; the page stays in the folder. Watch for error C: arrows along the path.

**Ask.** Which push was stronger? How do you know? Expect: the harder one; its arrow is longer.

Slot 2, sky patterns, paper only. Put the three sky rows in date order. Point to the morning picture in every row. Ask where the next morning's sun goes. Expect: where the three mornings put it, from the records.

Slot 3, weather. Read the five-day record (sunny, sunny, cloudy, rainy, sunny): say each date, count the sunny days, and rehearse tomorrow's entry: write what you see.

Ready children, at tables: compare the pond record (sunny, sunny, cloudy, rainy, sunny) with the hill record (rainy, cloudy, rainy, rainy, sunny) and answer [[s01-P2-3]]. Freed slots go to this and more weather practice.

#### 5 min. Weather record, taught

New. Four looks, each with its tool; a helper from the rotating six reads each. Temperature: the thermometer, to the nearest mark (the step is usually 2 degrees; below zero, say it in words). Rain jar: water or none, a depth only if it reaches 1 cm. Wind: the direction card. Sky: a word, never the sun. Write one dated row; children copy it into [[s01-P2-2]], which stays in the folder. Point to the five-day record: sunny on three of five days.

**Ask.** What do we expect tomorrow, and how sure are we? Expect: sunny, because three of five days were sunny; not sure.

A liking is not a reason, and one day is not a pattern. Last minute: hand wipes, review from kindergarten.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X2]]; read the wind table aloud. Accept drawing, a sentence or pointing. Look for: north; the repeated days as the reason; that it is expected, not certain. Error H: "north, because Thursday was north". Error G: "south, because I like south". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipe and stack trays and balls; the adult empties the rain jar.

### Other rooms

No trays: slide a book on a smooth desk. No thermometer: write sky and wind from the window, and say so.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a book on a smooth table. No thermometer: write the sky and wind from a window.

**Skip.** The sky rows, the groups and the repair slots.

- **10 min. Two pushes.** Push a ball gently, then harder, from one line. Measure each in whole centimeters. Do [[s01-P2-1]].
  **Say.** "Which push was stronger? How do you know?" Expect: the harder one; the arrow is longer.
- **10 min. The weather.** Look out the window together. Do [[s01-P2-2]].
- **8 min. What next?** Do [[s01-P2-3]].
  **Say.** "What do you expect tomorrow, and why?" Expect: sunny, because it was sunny on three of five days; not sure.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X2]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: run only the slots the papers show; never cut the weather part or the exit. Longer: ready children predict from the hill record too.

### Words and supports

Predict: say what you expect, and why. Frame: "I expect ___, because I saw ___." Home languages count.

### Tomorrow

Day 3 opens with Return and repair. If many gave a liking (error G) or one day (error H) on the exit, it highlights the repeated days in the wind table and predicts again, then runs the same push twice before saying anything.
