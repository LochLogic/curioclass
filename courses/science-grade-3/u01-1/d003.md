# Day 3: Planning a fair test of two pushes

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the plan table for the board. Set out six sets of small cardboard box, flat tray, soft ball and whole-centimeter ruler. Write the question and the four plan headings on the board. Have the wind table from day 2 ready for the repair. Whole centimeters on the ruler and the question board are review from grade 2.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges. Pushers sit at opposite ends with a flat hand on the box and push no harder than they can repeat. Floors stay clear. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Day 2's exit (s01-X2). Error G, 2 minutes: highlight the repeated days in the wind table, then predict again. Error H, 2 minutes: roll the ball once and ask where the next roll will stop; roll again and compare both distances before saying anything.

#### 4 min. Warm-up

Fair test is review: keep the same, change one thing, watch one thing. Read [[s01-P3-1]] aloud, said to be invented. Pairs say what Rin changed and how to make it fair.

**Ask.** What did Rin change? Expect: the ball and the board, two things. Error D: "it is fair". Cross out every change but one.

#### 10 min. The class plan

Write the question: "Do equal pushes from opposite sides change how a box moves?" Build the plan from the plan table. Change: how strong one of two pushes is, about equal then one stronger. Keep: the box, tray, start line, where the pushes touch, and both pushes toward the box. Watch: where the box ends, to the whole centimeter; "stayed" means less than 1 cm. Repeat: three tries of each, a try being one go. Equal means about equal, judged by feel.

New words: balanced (about equal pushes from opposite sides), unbalanced (one push stronger), cancel (the pushes undo each other).

**Ask.** Why three tries? Expect: to see if it happens again.

Each child writes a prediction, [[s01-P3-2]]. Any prediction with a reason is welcome.

#### 14 min. Practice the pushes, not recorded

Groups of five; roles rotate, review from grade 2: pusher, second pusher, measurer, recorder, watcher. For 5 minutes each child pushes alone about three times, 20 seconds each, finding a gentle push they can repeat. Then the two pushers match their pushes by feel while the watcher draws the two arrows. Last, one practice try of each condition goes on the plan table's practice lines. Move between the six groups.

#### 7 min. Own plan

Each child fills in [[s01-P3-3]] from the board, then checks it: one change, the kept things, three tries. These pages stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X3]]; read the stem aloud twice. Ask [[s01-X3b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Look for: a plan that changes only how hard the flick is and keeps the car, floor and start line; roll again to see if it happens again. Error D: "change the car and the flick". Error H: "once is enough". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Board and clean-up

Write on the board: "Can pushes act without movement?" Leave it open. Wipe and stack boxes, trays and balls.

### Other rooms

No trays or boxes: a book on a smooth desk, two pencils as pushers, a ruler.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a smooth table. No box: a small book. Two pencils can be the pushers.

**Skip.** The groups and the class board.

- **5 min. Fair or not?** Do [[s01-P3-1]].
  **Say.** "What did Rin change?" Expect: the ball and the board, two things.
- **10 min. The plan.** Look at [[s01-P3-3]] together and fill it in.
  **Say.** "What do we change? What do we keep?" Expect: how strong one push is; the box, the line, the table.
- **10 min. Try it.** Each of you pushes the box with a flat hand from opposite sides, gently and the same. Then one pushes harder. Read how far it moved.
- **5 min. Prediction.** Do [[s01-P3-2]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X3]], answered alone. Then ask [[s01-X3b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: two solo pushes each. Never cut the plan, the warm-up fix or the exit. Longer: predict, with arrows, what a push from the side does to the resting box.

### Words and supports

Balanced: about equal pushes from opposite sides. Unbalanced: one push is stronger. Cancel: the pushes undo each other. Try: one go. Frame: "I think ___, because ___." Home languages count.

### Tomorrow

Day 4 opens with Return and repair. If many changed two things in their plan (error D) or accepted one go (error H) on the exit, it crosses out every change but one in a proposed test and runs the same condition again. Then groups run this plan, six tries each.
