# Day 1: What makes a swing start, stop and keep coming back?

## Teacher page

### Before class

The day before, inspect string, knots and support. Hang the pendulum with four metal washers counted on. With the whole-centimetre ruler, tape a side mark 20 cm from the hanging string, and a ring 2 m back. Fit 30 pairs of eye protection. Hang the question board.

### Safety and set-up

One pendulum, run by the teacher alone: only you secure, release and hold it. Swings stay low, below faces. Washers are counted on and off, away from mouths. Eye protection for the hook only. No child sits on a swing. An adult prechecks the weather and handles the rain jar; nobody looks at the sun.

### The day

#### 2 min. Eye protection and the stop signal

Check the fit. Teach the stop signal: a raised hand, and everyone freezes and looks.

#### 8 min. Hook

Ring 2 m back, eye protection on. Release the pendulum from the side mark for about ten swings. Hold it still at the bottom. Show the swing picture, or an empty swing in view. Ask only: "What makes a swing start, stop and keep coming back?" Give no explanation.

**Ask.** What did you see it do? Expect: back and forth; stopped when held; stayed still when let go.

#### 4 min. Notice and wonder

Eye protection off. Pairs share one notice. Volunteers share; any child may pass. Turn each notice into a question on the question board (review).

#### 5 min. The force arrow

New. Show cards A, B and C. A push or a pull is a force (review from kindergarten). Say the rules once: the arrow starts on the thing pushed, points the way the push goes, and is longer for a stronger push. It shows the push, never the path. Each child copies one card, [[s01-P1-1]].

**Ask.** Where does the arrow start? Expect: on the thing pushed. Which push is stronger, A or B? Expect: B, the longer arrow.

An arrow along the whole path is error C: "the arrow is the push, not the trip".

#### 6 min. First explanation

**Ask.** What do you think makes the washer start and stop? Expect: any idea tied to something seen. None is marked wrong.

Alone, 3 minutes: [[s01-P1-2]]. Pairs, 3 minutes: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Hear a rotating six, 30 seconds each, while pairs talk (review). It stays in the folder for days 8 and 13.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X1]]; read each part aloud. Accept drawing, a sentence or pointing. Look for: a short arrow starting on the wagon at her hands; a longer arrow is a stronger push; a question about a swing. Error C: an arrow along the whole path, or "the wagon went farther". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Captains collect and wipe the eye protection. Lower the pendulum; count four washers into the bag.

#### 5 min. Weather record, modelled

Show the thermometer, read to the nearest mark (below zero, in words); the rain jar, water or none; the direction card; the sky, never the sun. Write one row on the dated weather record. Children copy it on side 2. Last minute: hand wipes.

#### 10 min. Readiness check

One minute: "This is not marked. Draw or write one short sentence." Read [[s01-R1]], [[s01-R2]] and [[s01-R3]] once each, 3 minutes each, no coaching. Keep the readiness papers for day 2.

### Other rooms

No pendulum: a key on a string at a low arc. No thermometer: write sky and wind from the window, and say so. No projector or printer: draw the cards on the board.

### At home

**Swap.** No pendulum: a key on a string, held low by the adult. No printer: draw on paper.

**Skip.** The ring, the question board, the weather row and the readiness check.

- **8 min. Watch.** The adult swings the string low, lets it go, then holds it still. The child says what happened.
  **Say.** "What did you see it do?" Expect: back and forth; stopped when held; still when let go.
- **5 min. Questions.** The child says two questions about a swing. The adult writes them.
- **10 min. Arrows.** Do [[s01-P1-1]].
  **Say.** "Where does the arrow start?" Expect: on the thing pushed. "Which way does it point?" Expect: the way the push goes.
- **8 min. My drawing.** Do [[s01-P1-2]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X1]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: read the weather row without copying it. Never cut the rules, the drawing or the exit. Longer: draw how it stops.

### Words and supports

Force: a push or a pull. Arrow: it shows a force. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___." Home languages count.

### Tomorrow

Day 2 opens with Return and repair. If many drew an arrow along the whole path or wrote "the wagon went farther" (error C), it lays each arrow card beside its own ruler record: strength and distance are two things. Then up to three 10-minute repair slots; sort the readiness papers tonight.
