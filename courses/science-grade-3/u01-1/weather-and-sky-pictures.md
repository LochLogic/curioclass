# Weather record and sky pictures

## The class weather record (days 1, 2, 5, 6, 9, 10, 13, 14)

A wall table with these columns, one dated row each day: Date. Temperature (to the nearest mark; below zero in words). Rain or snow (water in the jar, or none; a depth only if it reaches 1 cm). Wind direction (from the direction card). Sky (words or a picture). A helper from the rotating six reads each look. The last minute is hand wipes. Nobody looks at the sun.

## Day 2 repair, slot 2: three sky rows (paper only)

Label: "invented for practice". Three dated rows, each with three small pictures: a morning sun low on the right, a noon sun high, an afternoon sun low on the left. Say aloud: "This time the morning sun is on the right; these rows face the other way." The rows are cut apart. Children put them in date order, point to the morning picture in every row, and say where the next morning sun goes.

## Day 2 repair, slot 3: a five-day weather record

Label: "invented for practice". Five dated pictures in a row: sunny, sunny, cloudy, rainy, sunny. Children say the date of each, count the sunny days, and rehearse tomorrow's entry. Tomorrow's entry is what is seen, not a guess.

## What the table is for

After five rows the table starts to show a pattern. We say what we expect next, and we say it is not sure.
