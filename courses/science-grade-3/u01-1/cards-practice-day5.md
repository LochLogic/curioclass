# Practice cards, day 5

Eight cards, each labeled "invented for practice". Pairs say what the object does, which arrow is stronger, and where gravity points. Plain line drawings, no brands.

1. A hockey puck at rest on flat ice, one short arrow behind it. It starts. Gravity points down.
2. A book sliding to the right on a desk, one arrow pointing left. It slows. Gravity points down.
3. A barrel rolling to the right, no arrow on it but gravity. It keeps rolling. Gravity points down.
4. A skateboard at rest, a long arrow to the right and a short arrow to the left. It starts to the right.
5. A shopping cart rolling right, a short arrow to the right. It speeds up a little.
6. A bar of soap sliding left in a dish, a long arrow to the right. It slows quickly.
7. A coin sliding to the left on a desk, one long arrow pointing left behind it. It speeds up.
8. A paper cup rolling forward, one arrow pointing to the left side. It turns to the left.

On the answer page, the stronger arrow is the longer arrow. Gravity is a pull down toward the floor on every card.
