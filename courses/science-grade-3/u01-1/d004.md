# Day 4: Running the box test

## Teacher page

### Before class

Set out six sets of small cardboard box, flat tray, soft ball and whole-centimetre ruler. Tape a start line on each tray. Put the plan table on the board. Have the invented record ready for a failed kit.

### Safety and set-up

Pushes are gentle, inside the tray, away from table edges; nothing is thrown. Trays sit on the floor with a clear walking space. Clean-up is 4 minutes.

### The day

#### 2 min. Set-up

Name the roles. Trays go on the floor, walking space clear.

#### 5 min. Return and repair

Day 3's exit (s01-X3). Error D, 2 minutes: Ana pushes a book on the desk, then a box on the floor; cross out every change but one. Error H, 2 minutes: roll the ball once, then again, and compare both before saying anything.

#### 3 min. Warm-up

Read [[s01-P4-1]] aloud (ruler zero). Then say balanced, unbalanced and cancel back with the plan on the board.

**Ask.** What does balanced mean? Expect: about equal pushes from opposite sides.

#### 14 min. Run the box test

Six groups run the day 3 plan: about equal pushes, three tries; one push stronger, three tries. A try is about a minute: reset 15 seconds, push 5, read the ruler from zero at the start line 15, record 20. Roles rotate each try: twelve pusher turns for five children, so each pushes at least twice. Every child fills in [[s01-P4-2]]; the pages stay in the folder. Repeat an unclear try once and keep both. A box that moves 1 cm or more under about equal pushes is recorded as it happened; hand pushes are never exactly equal. If one stronger shows no move, push harder and record both. If the kit fails, use the invented record (about equal 0, 0, 0 cm; one stronger 6, 8, 7 cm) and say it is invented.

#### 5 min. Arrows from the evidence

Draw one group's two conditions on the board. About equal pushes: two arrows of one length, each starting on the box. One stronger: one arrow longer; the box moves the way the stronger push goes. A box that stays shows the pushes were about equal, not exactly. Each child draws both and finishes the sentence, citing the centimetres, [[s01-P4-3]].

**Ask.** Why are the two arrows the same length when the box stayed? Expect: the pushes balanced; they cancel.

#### 6 min. Start, stop, turn

A demonstration; no new words but one. Roll the ball toward the far end of the tray. A push toward you slows it; a push from the left turns it. Draw an arrow for each. Then the resting box: gravity pulls it down and the tray pushes it up, two equal arrows. Gravity is only a pull down. Children do [[s01-P4-4]] and [[s01-P4-5]]; give feedback at tables.

#### 3 min. Exit

Alone on the exit ticket, [[s01-X4]]; read both cards aloud. Look for: (a) no, equal pulls cancel; (b) yes, her hand and the wall push, and cancel. Error B: "yes, two pulls make more go". Error A: "no, it is not moving". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 4 min. Clean-up

Wipe and stack boxes, trays and balls.

#### 3 min. Class share

**Ask.** Can pushes act without movement? What did your box show? Expect: yes, when the pushes cancel; our box stayed with about equal pushes. Mark the board question answered with the class's results, odd ones kept.

### Other rooms

No trays: a book on a smooth desk, two pencils as pushers, a ruler.

### At home

**Swap.** No tray: a smooth table. No box: a small book. Pencils can be the pushers.

**Skip.** The groups, the repair and the class share.

- **3 min. Ruler.** Do [[s01-P4-1]].
  **Say.** "How far did it move?" Expect: 7 cm, 9 take away 2.
- **15 min. Box test.** Push the box from opposite sides, about equal, three times. Then one push stronger, three times. Read each in whole centimetres. Fill in [[s01-P4-2]].
  **Say.** "Did it move? Why?" Expect: not with equal pushes; they cancel.
- **5 min. Arrows.** Do [[s01-P4-3]].
- **7 min. Start, stop, turn.** Roll a ball; push it from behind. Do [[s01-P4-4]] and [[s01-P4-5]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s01-X4]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: four tries, two of each. Never cut the arrows, the box result or the exit. Longer: predict, with arrows, what a push from the side does to the resting box, then test it.

### Words and supports

Gravity: a pull down. Balanced, unbalanced, cancel: review. Frame: "The pushes are ___ because ___." Home languages count.

### Tomorrow

Day 5 opens with Return and repair. If many wrote "two pulls make more go" (error B) or "no force, it is still" (error A) on the exit, it holds a box still between two hands and draws both arrows, and tests two pushers again, reading the ruler. Then the class practises arrows on fresh cards.
