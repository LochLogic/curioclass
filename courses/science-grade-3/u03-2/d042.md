# Day 42: Reading a packet card

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the cards for each pair and the exit cards for the class. Keep the day 41 models on the wall, and open the dated class record to the sowing and sprout dates. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Check allergies and look for mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun. Wipe spills at once.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud. Children write [[s03-P42-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers say one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P42-2]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 41's exit tickets, s03-X41. Error A, 2 minutes: redraw the moth model in two colours, with the arrow from the adult's eggs to the new moth. The days, 2 minutes: count the days between the recorded stages and correct the interval labels. If many showed error A, say aloud: the egg is a new moth.

#### 4 min. Readiness item 2 again

Day 36's item 2, s03-R2, asked again with new seedlings: read [[s03-P42-8]] aloud once. Each child writes one short sentence alone, 3 minutes; collect, 1 minute. Look for: the matching leaves named. Error F: "they are different kinds because their heights differ". Point to the matching leaves and ask for the sentence again.

#### 15 min. Seed-packet timing

Say aloud that the card is invented for practice and its days are dotted; the class record's seen days are solid. [[steps:s03-K42-1]] **Ask.** What comes after sprouts? Expect: more leaf growth; the card says leaves are ready on day 24. Pairs read the class record and do [[s03-P42-3]], 4 minutes. Our plants keep their own days. Then pairs do [[s03-P42-4]] and [[s03-P42-5]], 4 minutes. **Ask.** What does the card not show? Expect: seeds made by the plant, or a new plant. Hear three of the rotating six, 30 seconds each; any may pass. Watch for "next it makes seeds": the card does not show that.

#### 8 min. Where does the next generation come from?

Pin cards B and C beside the new plant box of the day 41 model. [[steps:s03-K42-2]] Pairs then sort all four cards, [[s03-P42-6]], 3 minutes. If the class pods are ripe, look at them for 1 minute; mark a stage solid only if seen. **Ask.** Where does the next plant start? Expect: in a seed that begins to grow. Watch for card A taken as a new plant: error B. If many do, say aloud: picked leaves are a harvest.

#### 4 min. Question board

Move the board's questions under three headings: growth, resemblance and surroundings, marked with the letters G, R and S. Each child marks one, [[s03-P42-7]]. Post them unedited. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the spinach card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X42]]. Kneel by two of the rotating six and hear their answers, 30 seconds each. Look for: no; picking is a harvest; a seed beginning to grow is needed. Error B: "picked leaves are new plants". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the packet card and the four cards on the board. No class record, or the plants failed: read a supplied record, said aloud to be invented: sown day 0, sprouts day 8, flowers day 33. Every box is then dotted.

### At home

**Swap.** No class record: use a seed packet or your own dates. No cards: draw four small pictures.

**Skip.** The weather, the groups and the question board.

- **5 min. Look again.** [[s03-P42-8]], answered alone.
- **10 min. Packet card.** Read [[s03-K42-1]] together. Then do [[s03-P42-4]].
  **Say.** "What comes after sprouts?" Expect: "More leaves grow. The card says leaves are ready on day 24."
- **10 min. New plants.** Do [[s03-K42-2]] with four pictures: picked leaves, a pod with seeds, a seed beginning to grow and a flower. Then [[s03-P42-6]].
  **Say.** "Which picture is a new plant?" Expect: "The seed beginning to grow."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X42]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: count the days as a class, not in pairs. Never cut what the card does not show, the sort or the exit. Longer: children draw the card's three stages as boxes and mark each dotted.

### Words and supports

Packet, sprout, harvest, next stage, record, new plant. Frames: "After sprouts comes ___." "The card does not show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 43 takes error B from this exit in its 2-minute Return and repair: sort picked leaves and a seed beginning to grow into harvest and new plant. Then it asks why seedlings from one packet are alike yet differ, and teaches four words: trait, inherited, likeness and variation.
