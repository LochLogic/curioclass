# Cards, day 42

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Packet-style card

A seed packet shape with a lettuce picture on it. Under the picture, our words, not packet text:

Sown day 0. Sprouts day 6. Leaves ready to pick day 24.

## Evidence cards

Card A: a hand picking leaves from a pot of leafy plants.
Card B: a dry seed pod split open, with seeds inside.
Card C: a small seed with a tiny root and two leaves, beginning to grow.
Card D: a plant in flower.

Pairs sort the cards. Only a seed that begins to grow shows a new plant growing.
