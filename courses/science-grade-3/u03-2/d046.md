# Day 46: Graph practice

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the lettuce and hamster cards for each pair and the tally and graph sheet for each child. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Keep the resemblance chart on the wall. Check allergies and look for mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things and after the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun, and in unsafe weather the record is read indoors. An adult handles any ice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud. Children write [[s03-P46-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change each, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P46-2]].

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 45's exit tickets, s03-X45. Error G, 2 minutes: show two pictures, one with a family record and one without; the class says which can be used as data. If many tickets showed error G, say aloud: looking alike does not show the parents.

#### 5 min. Warm-up

Show the rabbit card from day 45. Children write [[s03-P46-3]], alone. Hear two of the rotating six, 30 seconds each.

#### 15 min. Graph practice

No new content is taught. Pairs read the lettuce card and graph the heights, [[s03-P46-4]], 7 minutes. Then they read the hamster card and graph the coat colours, [[s03-P46-5]], 7 minutes. Each bar needs a name under it and a labelled side. Walk and read two answers aloud. Watch for "the next lettuce will be tall": the graph shows these plants only.

#### 6 min. Update the class chart

Each pair picks one graph and writes one sentence for the chart, [[s03-P46-6]], 3 minutes. Pairs add it in a second colour, 2 minutes. Read two aloud, 1 minute. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 10 min. Exit ticket

Read the squash and duck records aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X46]]. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: a bar for each size, a labelled count side, broad leaves alike, sizes different. Error F: "the ducklings must all have the same bill". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: write both cards on the board. No weather tools: read the dated weather record and write the last row.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw the six young for each card. No chart: use a page.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records and the chart.

- **5 min. Warm-up.** Do [[s03-P46-3]].
- **8 min. Lettuce.** Do [[s03-P46-4]].
  **Say.** "What is alike in all six? What is different?" Expect: "Frilly leaves are alike. The heights are different."
- **8 min. Hamsters.** Do [[s03-P46-5]].
- **4 min. Chart.** Do [[s03-P46-6]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X46]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: graph the lettuce card together and the hamster card in pairs. Never cut the labelled axis, the sentence for the chart or the exit. Longer: write a second sentence that the graph does not support, and say why.

### Words and supports

Trait, likeness, variation, tally, bar graph, count side, supported. Frames: "Alike in ___; different in ___." "The bars show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 47 takes two things from this exit in its Return and repair. Error F: circle the differing size in one documented family and write alike in ___; different in ___. The graph labels: on one graph, label the count side and the size names, and match each bar to its tally. Then children revisit hydrangea color and retrieve the grade 2 plant-needs evidence.
