# Day 43: Alike and different

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the goat cards for each pair and the resemblance chart for the class. On the board, write the class seedlings' recorded heights in a row, and pin the packet picture of the kind beside them. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers say one thing each that they see in the pots, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P43-1]].

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 42's exit tickets, s03-X42. Error B, 2 minutes: pairs sort two cards, picked leaves and a seed beginning to grow, into harvest and new plant. If many tickets showed error B, say aloud: a new plant starts in a seed that begins to grow.

#### 3 min. Retrieval

Review of grade 1: young share features with their parents but are not exact copies. Show the goat family card: Dot and Fern, and three kids, invented for practice, the parents named. Offspring, from day 39, are the young. Pairs write [[s03-P43-2]], 2 minutes. Hear two of the rotating six, 30 seconds each.

#### 7 min. Hook: same kind, not the same

Point to the seedling heights beside the packet picture. Children write [[s03-P43-3]], 3 minutes. **Ask.** Review from grade 1: what is alike? What is different? Why might that be? Expect: leaves like the picture; heights differ; ideas such as light, water or soil. Keep every idea on the board, uncorrected. Hear four of the rotating six, 30 seconds each; any child may pass.

#### 12 min. Teach the words

Pin the goat card. [[steps:s03-K43-1]] Write trait and inherited on the board. **Ask.** What is one trait of the kids? Expect: long ears. [[steps:s03-K43-2]] Write likeness and variation. **Ask.** Which trait differs among the kids? Expect: the patches. [[steps:s03-K43-3]] Say aloud: we never say which young gets which trait. A family record is evidence; a picture of a kind is not.

#### 8 min. Sort the traits

Pairs sort the goat trait cards, [[s03-P43-4]], 3 minutes. Then they sort the seedlings' leaf shape and height, [[s03-P43-5]], 3 minutes. Say: the picture names only the kind, so the leaf shape is like the kind. Watch for "patches are a likeness": error F. If many do, say aloud: kid 3 has no patches. **Ask.** Which is a variation for the seedlings? Expect: the heights.

#### 5 min. Revise the resemblance chart

In a second color, each pair writes [[s03-P43-6]]. The first color stays.

#### 3 min. Question board

Each child adds one question, [[s03-P43-7]]. Post them unedited. Sheets stay in the folder.

#### 3 min. Exit

Read the cat card aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X43]]. Show the fox picture and ask [[s03-X43b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Kneel by two of the rotating six and hear their answers, 30 seconds each. Look for: pointed ears as an inherited trait and the white paws as a variation; for the spoken one, a family record that names the parents. Error G: "that they look alike". Error F: "the kitten with white paws is not their kitten". Note letters on the record sheet.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw Dot, Fern and the three kids on the board. No class seedlings: read a supplied record, said aloud to be invented: heights 6, 8, 9 and 12 cm, with leaves like the picture.

### At home

**Swap.** No goat card: draw two parents and three kids. No chart: use a page.

**Skip.** The living records, the board and the chart.

- **8 min. The words.** Do [[s03-K43-1]] and [[s03-K43-2]] with the goat card.
  **Say.** "What is alike in all the kids? What is different?" Expect: "Long ears and a brown nose are alike. The patches are different."
- **9 min. Sort.** Do [[s03-P43-4]]. Then [[s03-K43-3]].
  **Say.** "Can a picture of a kind show who the parents are?" Expect: "No. A family record names them."
- **8 min. Alike and different.** Do [[s03-P43-2]], then [[s03-P43-5]] with any plant you have.
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X43]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X43b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: sort the goat traits as a class. Never cut the family record against the picture, the variation word or the exit. Longer: children find a trait on the goat card that is neither alike nor different, and say why.

### Words and supports

Trait, inherited, likeness, variation, offspring, record. Frames: "Alike in ___; different in ___." "A trait is ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 44 takes this exit's letters in 4 minutes. Error G, 2 minutes: two pictures, one with a family record and one without, and which can be used as data. Error F, 2 minutes: circle the differing feature in one documented family and write alike in ___; different in ___. Then children measure the class plant pairs and graph the heights.
