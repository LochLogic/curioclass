# Exit cards, days 42 to 46

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## Day 42

Card 1: radish. Sown day 0. Sprouts day 4. Roots ready to pull day 28. Our radishes are on day 15 and have leaves. Card 2 (spoken): Max says, "Picking spinach leaves means new plants were made."

## Day 43

Card 1: a family record names Mom cat and Dad cat. Their three kittens all have pointed ears like their parents. One kitten has white paws. Card 2 (spoken): a picture of two red foxes standing in a field beside a fox cub.

## Day 44

A record: pansy parents have ruffled petals. Their six offspring all have ruffled petals. Petal widths in whole centimeters: 4, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6.

## Day 45

A record: gerbil parents have long tails. Their four pups all have long tails. Two pups have sandy coats and two have gray coats. A gray gerbil in the next cage looks like the gray pups.

## Day 46 (exit ticket, on its own sheet)

Squash parents and all six offspring have broad leaves. Offspring leaf sizes: small 2, medium 3, large 1. Duck parents and all six ducklings have broad bills. Bill sizes: short 1, medium 2, long 3.
