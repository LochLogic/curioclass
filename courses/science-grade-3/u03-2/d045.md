# Day 45: Litters

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the litter and statement cards for each pair and the tally and graph sheet for each child. An adult prechecks the weather and empties the rain jar. Keep the resemblance chart on the wall. Check allergies and look for mould; an adult removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things and after the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. The thermometer is non-glass; no one looks at the sun, and in unsafe weather the record is read indoors. An adult handles any ice.

### The day

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud. Children write [[s03-P45-1]].

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one stage the class saw today, 20 seconds each. Everyone writes [[s03-P45-2]].

#### 2 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 44's exit tickets, s03-X44. Error F, 2 minutes: on the nasturtium card, circle the differing feature and write alike in ___; different in ___. If many tickets showed error F, say aloud: young need not match in every feature.

#### 3 min. Recap

Read the four words and the frame from the chart together. Pairs write [[s03-P45-3]].

#### 10 min. A puppy litter

A litter is the young born together. Read the puppy card aloud; the parents are named. Pairs tally the coat colours, name the likeness and the variation, and point to what shows each, [[s03-P45-4]]. Walk and read two answers aloud. Watch for "all the puppies are brown": error F.

#### 10 min. A rabbit litter

Same steps with the rabbit card. The ear lengths go on a bar graph, [[s03-P45-5]]. Count the squares for each bar. Watch for bars read as one rabbit's length.

#### 5 min. What the record shows

Pairs sort the six statement cards, [[s03-P45-6]]. **Ask.** What does the record show, and what does it not show? Expect: it shows what the cards count; it does not show which parent gave a colour or what the next litter will be. Watch for error G: "the brown colour came from the mother, because the puppy looks like her".

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the gerbil record aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X45]]. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: part (a) supported; part (b) goes beyond, since looking alike does not show a father. Error G: "(b) is supported because they look alike". Note letters on the record sheet.

#### 3 min. Share

Each child writes one claim and its observation, [[s03-P45-7]], 1 minute. Three pairs read theirs, 40 seconds each; any may pass. Sheets stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the litter records on the board. No weather tools: read the dated weather record and write the last row.

### At home

**Swap.** No cards: draw each litter from the words. No chart: use a page.

**Skip.** The weather, the living records and the share.

- **8 min. Puppy litter.** Do [[s03-P45-4]].
  **Say.** "What is alike in all the puppies? What is different?" Expect: "Coats and ears are alike. The colours are different."
- **8 min. Rabbit litter.** Do [[s03-P45-5]].
- **9 min. Shows or beyond.** Do [[s03-P45-6]].
  **Say.** "Does the card show which parent gave a colour?" Expect: "No. That goes beyond the record."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X45]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: do the puppy litter as a class and the rabbit litter in pairs. Never cut the sort, the labelled axis or the exit. Longer: write a second claim for each litter and its observation.

### Words and supports

Litter, offspring, tally, likeness, variation, record shows, goes beyond. Frames: "The record shows ___." "That goes beyond the record." "Alike in ___; different in ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may point to the card.

### Tomorrow

Day 46 takes error G from this exit in its 2-minute Return and repair: two pictures, one with a family record and one without, and which can be used as data. Then children graph two new cards, update the class chart and sit the section exit ticket, alone.
