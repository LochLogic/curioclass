# Day 44: Measuring and graphing

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the tally and graph sheet for each child and the nasturtium card for each pair. Set a ruler, a tray, a cup of water at each group. Pin the parent-plant photograph beside the resemblance chart. Check allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened. Measure without uprooting. The adult fills the cups and wipes spills at once.

### The day

#### 2 min. Living records

The adult pours the same cup on each plant of a pair, group by group. Children write [[s03-P44-1]].

#### 4 min. Return and repair

Hand back day 43's exit tickets, s03-X43. Error G, 2 minutes: show the goat card and the packet picture; the class says which one can be used as data. Error F, 2 minutes: on the goat card, circle the differing feature and write alike in ___; different in ___. If many tickets showed error G, say aloud: a family record names the parents.

#### 5 min. Graph words

Review of grade 2: a bar graph on squared paper. Here one square is one plant, and a bar's height says how many. Draw the tally chart and the bars on the board. [[steps:s03-K44-1]] Children write [[s03-P44-2]]. **Ask.** What does a bar's height say? Expect: how many plants.

#### 8 min. Measure the class pairs

In each group of five the ruler goes round, 1 minute a turn: two children read one plant each, two check the readings and one writes on the class list. That is 5 minutes. Read from the ruler's zero to the whole centimeter, never uprooting, [[s03-P44-3]]. Everyone writes the reading they read or checked. Carry the numbers to the board, 2 minutes; the adult checks the cups, 1 minute.

#### 8 min. Graph the class heights

Set three bands from the readings on the board. Pairs tally the 12 readings, [[s03-P44-4]], 3 minutes. Then they draw the bar graph, [[s03-P44-5]], 4 minutes. **Ask.** What does the graph show about seedlings of one kind? Expect: the heights differ. Say: the packet picture names the kind, not the parents, so this shows variation only. Watch for bars read as the parent's height.

#### 10 min. A family with a record

Show the parent-plant photograph and the card: nasturtium parents, named, and six offspring. Pairs tally and graph the leaf widths, [[s03-P44-6]], 6 minutes. Then [[s03-P44-7]], 2 minutes. **Ask.** What is alike? What is different? How do you know? Expect: round leaves alike; widths differ, 7 to 9 cm; the card says so. Watch for "all must be 7 cm wide": error F.

#### 3 min. Clean-up

The adult empties the cups. Wipe rulers and trays. Wash hands in two waves at two sinks, 7 children a sink, 12 seconds each.

#### 5 min. Exit

Read the pansy record aloud twice. Alone, [[s03-X44]]. Ask [[s03-X44b]] aloud, 3 to 4 children. Kneel by the rotating six, 30 seconds each, 3 minutes inside the exit. Look for: ruffled petals alike; widths different, 4 to 6; the record shows both. Error F: "all the offspring must be 5 centimeters wide". Note letters on the record sheet. Sheets stay in the folder.

### Other rooms

No printer: draw the tally chart on the board. No class plants, or the heights are too close to graph: use a supplied record, said aloud to be invented: 6, 7, 7, 8, 8, 8, 9, 9, 10, 10, 11 and 12 cm, in bands 6 to 8, 9 to 10 and 11 to 12.

### At home

**Swap.** No ruler: use a printed one. No squared paper: draw squares. No plants: use the bean heights in [[s03-K44-1]] and skip [[s03-P44-3]].

**Skip.** The groups, the class graph and the clean-up.

- **7 min. Graph words.** Do [[s03-K44-1]] and [[s03-P44-2]].
  **Say.** "What does the height of a bar say?" Expect: "How many plants."
- **8 min. Measure.** Measure three plants, [[s03-P44-3]], and tally them, [[s03-P44-4]].
- **10 min. A family.** Do [[s03-P44-6]] and [[s03-P44-7]] with the nasturtium card.
  **Say.** "What is alike? What is different?" Expect: "Round leaves are alike. The widths are different, 7 to 9."
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X44]], answered alone. Then ask [[s03-X44b]] aloud.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: tally the class readings as a class and graph only the nasturtium family. Never cut the family record, the labeled axis or the exit. Longer: graph a second trait of the family and say whether it shows likeness or variation.

### Words and supports

Tally, bar graph, band, whole centimeter, offspring, likeness, variation. Frames: "One mark is one ___." "Alike in ___; different in ___." "The bars show ___." Home-language answers count. A partner may read the ruler aloud.
