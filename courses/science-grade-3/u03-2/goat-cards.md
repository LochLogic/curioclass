# Goat family cards, day 43

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## The family record

The record names the parents. Dot and Fern are the parents. They both have long ears and a brown nose. Their three kids all have long ears and a brown nose.

Kid 1 has one brown patch. Kid 2 has two brown patches. Kid 3 has no patches.

## Trait cards

Long ears. Brown nose. Brown patches: none, one or two.

Pairs sort the trait cards into likeness and variation.

## Class seedlings

The same cards are made for the class seedlings: leaf shape and height. A packet picture names the kind only. It does not name parents.
