# Cards, day 45

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

## A puppy litter

Both parents have short smooth coats and floppy ears. Seven puppies all have short smooth coats and floppy ears. Coat colours: brown 4, black 2, tan 1.

## A rabbit litter

Both parents have upright ears. Five kits all have upright ears. Ear lengths in whole centimetres: 9, 10, 10, 11, 11.

## Statement cards

Sort each into "the record shows" or "goes beyond it".

- All seven puppies have short smooth coats.
- The brown colour came from the mother.
- The next litter will have brown puppies.
- All five kits have upright ears.
- The longest ears belong to the biggest kit.
- Ear lengths run from 9 to 11 centimetres.
