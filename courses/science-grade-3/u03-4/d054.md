# Day 54: Comparing, then revising the portfolio

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have the day 53 papers back with their look-for checklists and question stamps, no score. Keep the score sheet for yourself. An adult prechecks the weather and checks allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil, living things and the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released. The thermometer is non-glass; nobody looks at the sun; in unsafe weather read the record indoors. An adult prechecks the weather and goes with the helpers outdoors. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Evidence critique

Draw two photographs on the board: one lavender by a sunny wall with many flowers, one under a tree with few. A child says the wall made more. Pairs name one condition the photographs may not share, [[s03-P54-1]], 2 minutes. Hear three pairs, 30 seconds each; any may pass. **Ask.** Which observation would warrant the claim? Expect: a record where only the place changed and everything else matched. Watch for error E: "the wall made the lavender flower more".

#### 4 min. Unscored comparisons

Read the three practice cards, [[s03-P54-7]], aloud twice: cress, a crow, two pumpkin photographs, 1 minute. Pairs say what each card shows and which observation warrants it, 3 minutes. This talk is not collected. **Ask.** Which observation warrants each claim? Expect: the matched cress heights; the crow's chicks never shown; for the pumpkins, none, since the photographs differ in many ways.

#### 4 min. Exit

Read the three exit cards aloud twice: bean seedlings, a dog, two sunflower photographs, 1 minute. Alone, 3 minutes, [[s03-X54]]. Collect the tickets. Look for: a light effect with matched conditions, a dog that learned and puppies that did not, and a condition the sunflower photographs do not share. Error D: "the puppies inherited opening gates". Error E: "the fence made the sunflower taller". Day 58 takes the commonest.
#### 22 min. Revise the portfolio

Hand back the three records with their checklists and day 53's pages and exit tickets: s03-P53-1, s03-P53-2, s03-P53-3, s03-P53-4, s03-P53-5, s03-P53-6, s03-P53-7 and s03-X53. Alone, using the checklist and its question, each child revises in a second color and keeps the first answer. Record 1, [[s03-P54-2]], 7 minutes. Record 2, [[s03-P54-3]], 7 minutes. Record 3, [[s03-P54-4]], 8 minutes. For each change, mark the observation that warrants it; no change without one. Give no answers and no arrows. **Ask.** Which observation warrants each change? Expect: a matched height, a before and after, a dated stage, a shared feature or a count. Walk and read two aloud; a revision that follows a friend's page, not the record, is sent back to the record. Scoring happens after day 54, outside lesson time.

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud, 6 children at 30 seconds, 3 minutes inside the 5. Write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s03-P54-5]]. An adult empties the rain jar.

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change, 20 seconds each; others look. Everyone writes [[s03-P54-6]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Rain jar and desks wiped, papers collected into folders, hands washed in two waves at two sinks.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the three cards on the board; answer on notebook paper. No weather tools or plants: read the dated records and write the last row.

### At home

**Swap.** No photographs: draw two from the words. No second color: write the changes in capitals. No day 53 papers: write the explanations again from the cards.

**Skip.** The weather, the pairs and the clean-up.

- **5 min. Photographs.** Draw the two lavender photographs. Do [[s03-P54-1]].
  **Say.** "Why can't two photographs show the wall made it?" Expect: "They differ in many ways, like water and soil."
- **8 min. Record 1.** Read the checklist. Do [[s03-P54-2]].
- **8 min. Record 2.** Do [[s03-P54-3]].
- **8 min. Record 3.** Do [[s03-P54-4]].
- **5 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X54]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: revise record 1 and record 2 only, in 14 minutes. Never cut the marks on the observations or the exit. Longer: write one change you chose not to make, and why the record did not warrant it.

### Words and supports

Revise, warrants, observation, matched, learned. Frames: "I changed ___, because I saw ___." "The photographs do not keep ___ the same." Home-language answers count. Read each card aloud; dictation, enlarged cards and a partner reader are welcome.

### Tomorrow

Day 55 is the first check day: one new explanation about water, a learned response and a family pattern, alone, with no feedback. Day 55's fresh work is scored after class; revised record 1 is its repair mark. Errors C and D are what day 55 looks for. Day 54's exit tickets, s03-X54, wait for day 58, where error E is taken in a group.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
