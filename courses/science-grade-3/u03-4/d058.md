# Day 58: Respond to the three assessment records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Have every paper back with its written feedback. Set four spots for the groups and write the four names on the board. An adult checks allergies and looks for mould, and removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released. Wipe water spills at once, away from electrical equipment. Clean-up is 2 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Return records

Hand back day 55's assessment, day 56's assessment and day 57's assessment with feedback: day 55's s03-P55-1, s03-P55-2 and s03-P55-3, 2 minutes; day 56's s03-P56-1, s03-P56-2, s03-P56-3 and s03-P56-4, 2 minutes; day 57's s03-P57-1, s03-P57-2, s03-P57-3 and s03-P57-4, 1 minute. Day 54's pages, s03-P54-1 to s03-P54-6, and tickets, s03-X54, go back too. Each child marks the one component to work on, [[s03-P58-1]]. Nobody is the example of a mistake.

#### 24 min. Four six-minute groups

This is the one response. Each child goes to the group of the component they marked; a group is about 7 or 8. The teacher takes the groups in turn, 6 minutes each, and the other children revise alone or with a partner, [[s03-P58-6]]. In each group pairs speak at once: each child says one revision to a partner, 45 seconds each way, 90 seconds a group.
- Cycles, [[s03-P58-2]]. Error A, 3 minutes: redraw one cycle in two colours, the adult in one and the new individual in the other. Error B, 3 minutes: put the pod card and the seedling card beside the dated record and point to the one that shows a new plant.
- Inheritance and variation, [[s03-P58-3]]. Error F, 3 minutes: circle the differing feature in one family and write alike in ___; different in ___. Error G, 3 minutes: two pictures, one with a family record and one without; the class says which can be used as data.
- Environmental explanations, [[s03-P58-4]]. Error C, 3 minutes: compare the two watering rows of one family and cross out every difference but water. Error D, 3 minutes: set one animal's before and after card beside its young, never trained.
- Evidence limits, [[s03-P58-5]]. Error E, 6 minutes: name one condition two photographs do not keep the same, then say what record would let us tell. Hear three pairs, 30 seconds each.

A child with no gap works [[s03-P58-6]]. Any child may pass in public talk.

#### 8 min. Fresh transfer

Read the mustard and lacewing cards aloud twice, 1 minute. Alone, 6 minutes: [[s03-X58c]]. Unscored. Look for: both models, a pupa only in the lacewing, likeness, the leaf widths as variation, and a water effect. Then ask aloud, 1 minute, any child may answer: [[s03-P58-9]]; look for 26 cm. Collect the tickets.

#### 4 min. Model updating

Each child writes one change to the class model and the record that says so, [[s03-P58-7]], 1 minute. Then two waves of 15 come to the wall, 6 seconds each, and place their notes, 90 seconds a wave. **Ask.** What did your revised model change, and what in the record said so? Expect: "I added ___, because the record says ___." Any may pass.

#### 2 min. Living records

In each group of five, two pairs take the ruler in turn, 1 minute each: one child steadies the pot, the other reads each plant to the whole centimetre from zero, without uprooting. The fifth child writes the date. Everyone writes [[s03-P58-8]]; this is the final reading.

#### 2 min. Clean-up

Trays wiped, hands washed in two waves.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the group prompts on the board; answer on notebook paper. No plants: read the dated living record and write its last row.

### At home

**Swap.** No group: the parent takes the child's marked component alone. No ruler: use a strip of paper marked in centimetres.

**Skip.** The groups, the wall model and the clean-up.

- **5 min. Look back.** Read the feedback together. The child does [[s03-P58-1]].
- **15 min. Revise.** Work the marked component only: [[s03-P58-2]], [[s03-P58-3]], [[s03-P58-4]] or [[s03-P58-5]]. Then [[s03-P58-6]].
  **Say.** "What did you change, and what in the record says so?" Expect: "I changed ___, because the record says ___."
- **5 min. Update.** The child does [[s03-P58-7]].
- **8 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X58c]], answered alone.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: the transfer keeps its core: the models, one likeness and one variation. Never cut the groups' moves or the transfer. Longer: name a record that would test a prediction.

### Words and supports

Revise, component, warrants, offspring, matched, learned, evidence. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." "Alike in ___; different in ___." Home-language answers count. Read the cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe.

### Tomorrow

Day 59 starts unit 4: the class observes the tundra anchor, raises questions and draws a first habitat system model. Today's transfer ticket, s03-X58c, goes back on day 59.
