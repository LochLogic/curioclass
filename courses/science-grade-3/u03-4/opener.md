# Section 3.4 opener: the portfolio, the checks and the response

## What the section does

Six days, 53 to 58. No new content is taught: the unit's teaching ended on day 52. Day 53 joins the wall models into one class model, then each child works three new records alone, ten minutes each, and writes them on paper. Day 54 returns written feedback. Each child revises all three records and marks which observation warrants each change. Days 55, 56 and 57 are independent checks on fresh records, one a day. Fresh work is diagnostic. The scored work is the revised portfolio record, scored outside lesson minutes, 12 points in all. Day 58 is the one response, a fresh transfer, and the last living-record reading.

## How the days run

Each day is 45 minutes. Days 53 and 54 start with a short opening and hold the weather record and the living record. Days 55 to 57 give no feedback. Day 58 returns the written feedback, then four six-minute groups, an 8-minute transfer and a model update. Every child may pass in public talk.

## The ideas, at an adult's level

- **Life cycles.** Birth, growth, reproduction and death are in every model. A new individual starts in a seed or an egg. Dates describe these individuals.
- **Likeness and variation.** The data show a pattern in a group. They cannot predict one young.
- **Surroundings and learning.** A matched comparison can show a surroundings effect. A learned response is not passed to young. One feature can reflect both the family and the surroundings.
- **Photographs alone cannot show a cause.** Name a condition they do not keep the same.
- **Observed and supplied.** Stages actually seen are solid. Supplied cards are dotted.

## The errors, in plain words

- **A:** the dead adult starts again. Draw the arrow from the eggs or seeds to a new box.
- **B:** a pod shows reproduction. Show a seed that begins to grow.
- **C:** every difference is inherited. Compare the two watering rows.
- **D:** a learned response is inherited. Compare before and after.
- **E:** photographs prove a cause. Name a condition not kept the same.
- **F:** young must match in every feature. Circle the differing feature.
- **G:** looks show the parents. Add a family record.

## Supplies for the section

Paper models, a whole-centimeter ruler, a water tray and the class plants. The picture sets are the three portfolio record cards, the three day 55 to 57 cards, the day 54 and day 58 exit cards and the score sheet.

## A room with nothing

Paper models and the printed records only. Every reading is invented for practice and read aloud. No scale model or tool is built for this section.
