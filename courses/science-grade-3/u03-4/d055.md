# Day 55: Check, surroundings and inheritance together

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the item 1 card for each child. Write the three directions on the board. An adult checks allergies and looks for mould, and removes any.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil and living things. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released. Clean-up is 3 minutes.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Day 55's assessment is the revised day 53 record 1 (s03-E1, s03-E2 and s03-E3), scored after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. Day 54's revised pages, s03-P54-1, s03-P54-2, s03-P54-3, s03-P54-4, s03-P54-5 and s03-P54-6, are held with them. Today's fresh work is diagnostic. Read the product aloud: pick observations, link each condition to the changed feature, explain the combined case. Hand out the item 1 card and read it aloud twice; it is invented for practice.

#### 32 min. Item 1

Alone on paper, [[s03-P55-1]]. Give no help and no feedback; dictation is welcome. Walk and never prompt. Look for: the matched marigold heights used for water; the before and after for the ducklings; the family pattern and the water both named for height. Note error C, "all the height differences are inherited", and error D, "the ducklings inherited knowing the bell", on the record sheet for the day 58 groups.

#### 3 min. Checking

Children reread and mark their claim, evidence and reason, [[s03-P55-2]]. Then collect the pages and cards.

#### 2 min. Living records

Two observers name one change, 20 seconds each; others look. Everyone writes [[s03-P55-3]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Wipe the shelf where the pots stand, then wash hands in two waves at two sinks.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the card on the board and read it aloud; answer on notebook paper. No plants: read the dated living record and write one change from it.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No plants: use the dated living record notes.

**Skip.** The living records, the clean-up and the scoring.

- **35 min. Assessment.** Read the card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s03-P55-1]]. Give no help.
- **4 min. Checking.** The child does [[s03-P55-2]].
- **1 min. Your revised record.** Read [[s03-E1]], [[s03-E2]] and [[s03-E3]]; they are scored from the day 53 record. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child who runs out of time writes the claim and what was seen. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Surroundings, learned, family, evidence, reason. Frame: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn answers and a scribe. Do not supply causes.

### Tomorrow

Day 56 is the second check day, on life-cycle models: own work, no help, no feedback. Day 55's pages, s03-P55-1, s03-P55-2 and s03-P55-3, are held until day 58. Errors C and D are what day 58's groups take.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
