# Item 1 card (day 55)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. This is diagnostic. The scored work is the revised portfolio record 1.

## The records

Same-kind marigolds given enough water grow 12 and 13 centimetres. Matched plants given too little grow 6 and 7. Light, age, soil and spacing are fixed.

Two same-kind ducklings ignored a bell. After the bell sounded before every feeding, both ducklings come to it.

A short-marigold family and a tall-marigold family. Under matched enough water, parents and young share the family's shorter or taller pattern. Limited water lowers the young in both families.

## Your task

Explain the water effect, the learned response and the combined influences. Pick the observations. Give the reason each one matters.
