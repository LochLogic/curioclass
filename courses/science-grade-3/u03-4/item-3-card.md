# Item 3 card (day 57)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. This is scored work, done alone.

## Bean

Bean parents and all six offspring have broad leaves. Offspring leaf lengths in whole centimeters: 4, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6.

## Guinea pig

Guinea-pig parents have fur. Their four young have fur with differing patch patterns.

## Your task

Organize both records. Explain the likeness and the variation in each. Say which observation shows each. Say why these data do not tell us what a particular future young will be.
