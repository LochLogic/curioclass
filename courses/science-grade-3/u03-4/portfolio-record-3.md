# Portfolio record 3 (days 53 and 54)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Do not predict a future young.

## Sunflowers

Sunflower parents and six offspring share the same leaf form. Offspring leaf lengths in whole centimeters: 3, 3, 4, 4, 5, 5.

## Sheep

Two sheep parents and four lambs all have wool. The lambs' fleece shades differ.

## Your task

Organize both records. Explain the likeness and the variation in each kind. Say which observation shows each.
