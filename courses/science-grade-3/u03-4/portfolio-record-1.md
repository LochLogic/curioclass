# Portfolio record 1 (days 53 and 54)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Enlarge for those who need it. Do not give causes or arrows.

## The records

Basil seedlings from one seed family. They are equal in age, light, spacing, soil, container size and temperature. Only the water changed. With enough water they are 10 and 11 centimetres tall. With too little water they are 5 and 6.

Two chicks of one kind ignore a blue card. Then the chicks get fed after the card, again and again. After that they walk up to the card.

A short-basil family and a tall-basil family. Under matched enough water, the parents and young keep the family's shorter or taller pattern. With less water, the young are lower in both families.

## Your task

Write one explanation. Say what the water did, what the chicks learned, and what shaped the basil height.
