# Day 57: Check, parent and young data

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the item 3 card for each child. Write the directions on the board and set out straight edges for the bars.

### Safety and set-up

Nothing live is handled today. Papers and pencils only; keep walkways clear.

### The day

#### 5 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Today's fresh work is scored (s03-E7 and s03-E8), after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. The revised day 53 record 3 is recorded beside the score as repair evidence, a separate mark. Day 56's assessment pages, s03-P56-1, s03-P56-2, s03-P56-3 and s03-P56-4, are held; their checklists go back on day 58 and the scored pages on day 59. Read the product aloud: organize both records, explain the likeness and the variation in each, and say why the data do not tell one future young. Hand out the card and read it aloud twice.

#### 34 min. Item 3

Alone on paper. Children organize both records, a tally or a bar graph for the bean leaf lengths, [[s03-P57-1]], 14 minutes. They explain the likeness and the variation in each kind and name the observation that shows each, [[s03-P57-2]], 14 minutes. Then they say why the data do not tell what one future young will be, [[s03-P57-3]], 6 minutes. Give no help and no feedback; dictation and spoken answers are welcome. Look for: the broad leaves and the fur as likeness; the lengths 4, 5 and 6 and the patch patterns as variation; a group pattern, not a prediction of one young. Note error F, "different lengths mean they cannot be related", and error G, a look taken for a parent, on the record sheet for the day 58 groups.

#### 4 min. Independent checking

Children reread and tick each statement that uses a number or an observation, [[s03-P57-4]]. Then collect the pages and cards.

#### 2 min. Packing

Straight edges returned, desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the card on the board and read it aloud; answer on notebook paper. No straight edge: fold a paper strip.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the card by hand. No straight edge: fold a paper strip.

**Skip.** The packing and the scoring.

- **40 min. Assessment.** Read the card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s03-P57-1]], [[s03-P57-2]] and [[s03-P57-3]]. Give no help.
- **3 min. Checking.** The child does [[s03-P57-4]].
- **4 min. What is scored.** Read [[s03-E7]] and [[s03-E8]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child short of time writes one likeness and one variation for each kind. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Parents, young, likeness, variation, tally, bar graph, evidence. Frame: "Alike in ___; different in ___." Read the card aloud; accept spoken or pointed answers and a scribe. Do not supply the graph.

### Tomorrow

Day 58 is the one response. It returns the look-for checklists on the fresh work of days 55, 56 and 57, including s03-P57-1 to s03-P57-4; the scored papers come back on day 59. Four groups take errors A to G, then each child does one fresh transfer, alone.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
