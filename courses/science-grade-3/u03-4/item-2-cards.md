# Item 2 cards (day 56)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. This is scored work, done alone. Intervals are the differences between recorded days.

## Zinnia Z

Day 0: a seed containing a new plant. Day 5: seedling. Day 50: flowering adult. Day 70: dried flower head. Day 95: adult dead.

## Lady beetle L

Day 0: egg. Day 4: larva. Day 18: pupa. Day 25: adult. Day 35: adult lays new eggs. Day 90: adult dead.

## Your task

Draw a model for each, showing birth, growth, reproduction and death. Say what is the same and what differs in stages and timing. Predict what follows when a new zinnia seed begins growing in the recorded conditions.

## Second card (reveal after the first draft)

On day 70, the opened head of Z holds new seeds. One begins growing on day 75. It is a seedling on day 80. Revise your model to connect Z with the new individuals. Say what the record shows.
