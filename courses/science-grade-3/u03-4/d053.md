# Day 53: Putting it together, three new records

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the three portfolio records for each child and keep the score sheet for yourself. Pin the wall models: life cycles, trait charts, arrows, claim frame. An adult prechecks the weather and checks allergies; if mold shows, follow the safety page.

### Safety and set-up

Wash hands after soil, living things and the rain jar. The habitat is looked at through the lid and never opened; no mealworm is released. The thermometer is non-glass; nobody looks at the sun; in unsafe weather read the record indoors. Wipe spills at once, away from electrical equipment.

### The day

#### 5 min. Consensus models and repair

Hand back day 52's pages, s03-P52-1, s03-P52-2, s03-P52-3, s03-P52-4 and s03-P52-5, and its exit tickets, s03-X52. Error C, 2 minutes: on the mint ticket, pairs cross out every difference but spacing in the two rows of one family. If many tickets showed error C, say aloud: spacing explains that gap, not every difference. Then 3 minutes: point to the wall models; today they join in one class model, stages seen solid and supplied records dotted. **Ask.** Which of our class models does a new record change? Expect: the life cycle if it adds a stage, the chart if it adds a likeness or variation, the explanation if it adds a water effect. Each child writes [[s03-P53-1]], 1 minute.

#### 10 min. Record 1

Alone. Read portfolio record 1 aloud twice; each card says it is invented for practice. Children write one explanation, [[s03-P53-2]]. Give no causes; dictation is welcome. Watch for error C, "all the height differences are inherited", and error D, "the chicks inherited knowing the card".

#### 10 min. Record 2

Alone. Read the first card aloud twice, 1 minute. Children draw a model for each, compare and predict, [[s03-P53-3]], 6 minutes. Then reveal the second card and read it aloud. Children keep their first models and revise in a second color, [[s03-P53-4]], 3 minutes. Dates describe these two individuals, not a whole kind. Watch for error A, "the dead bee turns into its own egg", and error B, "the pods show the pea reproduced".

#### 10 min. Record 3

Alone. Read portfolio record 3 aloud, 1 minute. Children organize both records and explain likeness and variation in each, [[s03-P53-5]], 6 minutes. No prediction of a future young. Watch for error F, "different lengths mean they cannot be related", and error G, "the lamb looks like the ewe, so she is its parent". Last 3 minutes, the exit: read the look-fors aloud, 1 minute. Each child picks one record that misses a look-for and revises it in a second color, [[s03-X53]], 2 minutes. Written feedback on the rest comes on day 54.

#### 5 min. Weather record

A helper from the rotating six reads each look aloud, 6 children at 30 seconds, 3 minutes inside the 5. Write one dated row on the wall record; children copy it into [[s03-P53-6]]. An adult empties the rain jar.

#### 2 min. Living records

In each group of five, two pairs take the ruler in turn, 1 minute each: one child steadies the pot, the other reads each plant to the whole centimeter from zero, without uprooting. The fifth child writes the date. Everyone writes [[s03-P53-7]].

#### 3 min. Clean-up

Cups emptied, trays wiped, rulers dried, in two waves at two sinks; hands washed. Collect the records and tickets. After class, tick each look-for checklist and add one question stamp, about 1 minute a child, no score.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the records on the board; answer on notebook paper. No plants or weather tools: read the dated records and write the last row.

### At home

**Swap.** No wall models: use your child's unit notes and drawings. No ruler: use a strip of paper marked in centimeters. No plants: say the last reading from the notes.

**Skip.** The weather, the clean-up and the class model.

- **3 min. Look back.** Do [[s03-P53-1]] from the unit notes.
- **9 min. Record 1.** Read the card aloud. Do [[s03-P53-2]].
- **10 min. Record 2.** Read the first card, then the second. Do [[s03-P53-3]] and [[s03-P53-4]].
  **Say.** "Where does the new pea come from?" Expect: "From a seed in the pod; the adult does not start again."
- **8 min. Record 3.** Do [[s03-P53-5]].
- **2 min. Exit ticket.** [[s03-X53]], read the look-fors, then revise one record in a second color.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: write records 1 and 3 as three sentences each. Never cut the second card or the look-fors. Longer: write one prediction the records cannot support, and the record you would need.

### Words and supports

Life cycle, offspring, trait, surroundings, evidence, reason. Frames: "I think ___, because I saw ___, so ___." "Alike in ___; different in ___." Home-language answers count. Enlarged cards, tactile stage cards and dictation are welcome; a partner may read the ruler aloud.

### Tomorrow

Day 54 hands back the three records with their look-for checklists and day 53's exit tickets, s03-X53. Children read two unscored cards, then revise all three records, marking the observation that warrants each change.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
