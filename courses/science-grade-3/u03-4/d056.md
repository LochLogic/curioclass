# Day 56: Check, life-cycle models

## Teacher page

### Before class

Print the item 2 cards for each child and keep the second card face down. Write the directions on the board.

### Safety and set-up

Nothing live is handled today. Papers and pencils only; keep walkways clear.

### The day

#### 6 min. Directions

Rules today: own work, no help, no feedback. Today's fresh work is scored (s03-E4, s03-E5 and s03-E6), after class, outside lesson minutes; nothing goes back today. The revised day 53 record 2 is recorded beside the score as repair evidence, a separate mark. Day 55's assessment pages, s03-P55-1, s03-P55-2 and s03-P55-3, are held; their checklists go back on day 58 and the scored pages on day 59. Read the product aloud: a model for each, what is the same and what differs, a prediction, then a second card to revise on. Hand out the first card; the dates are differences between recorded days, not life spans for a whole kind.

#### 34 min. Item 2

Alone on paper. Read the first card aloud twice, 1 minute. Children draw a model for each, [[s03-P56-1]], 13 minutes, then compare and predict, [[s03-P56-2]], 8 minutes. Hand out the second card, read it aloud, 1 minute. Children keep their first models and revise in a second color, [[s03-P56-3]], 11 minutes. Give no help and no feedback; dictation and drawn or pointed models are welcome. Look for: the zinnia model with birth, growth and death and a dotted or blank reproduction box marked not in this record, the beetle model with all four stages; a seedling predicted after 5 days; an arrow from Z to the new seed, not back to Z. Note error A, "the same zinnia starts again after it dies", and error B, "the seeds in the head show it reproduced", on the record sheet for the day 58 groups.

#### 3 min. Checking

Children reread their models and tick each stage that matches a day in the record, [[s03-P56-4]].

#### 2 min. Packing

Collect the papers, both cards and the second-color pencils. Desks cleared.

### Other rooms

No printer: write the two cards on the board and read them aloud; draw on notebook paper. No second color: mark revisions with a box around each.

### At home

**Swap.** No printer: copy the first card by hand. No second color: draw boxes around the revisions.

**Skip.** The packing and the scoring.

- **36 min. Assessment.** Read the first card aloud twice. The child alone does [[s03-P56-1]] and [[s03-P56-2]]. Then read the second card; the child does [[s03-P56-3]]. Give no help.
- **3 min. Checking.** The child does [[s03-P56-4]].
- **1 min. What is scored.** Read [[s03-E4]], [[s03-E5]] and [[s03-E6]]; they are scored from today's work. Write nothing.

### Shorter or longer

Shorter: none; each part is the assessment. A child short of time draws both models and writes the prediction. Longer: none.

### Words and supports

Birth, growth, reproduce, die, stage, offspring, revise. Frame: "Next is ___ because the record says ___." Read both cards aloud; accept spoken, pointed or drawn models and a scribe. Do not supply arrows.

### Tomorrow

Day 57 is the third check day, on parent and young data: own work, no help, no feedback. Day 56's pages, s03-P56-1, s03-P56-2, s03-P56-3 and s03-P56-4, are held; their checklists go back on day 58 and the scored pages on day 59. Errors A and B are what day 58's groups take.

### Credit

Ours, all of it.
