# Portfolio record 2 (days 53 and 54)

Each card says "invented for practice" in small letters at the bottom. Plain line drawings, no brands, no names of real children.

Read aloud. Intervals are the differences between recorded days. They are not life spans for a whole kind.

## Pea P

Day 0: a seed containing a new plant. Day 7: seedling. Day 35: flowering adult. Day 45: closed pods. Day 70: adult dead.

## Honeybee queen Q

Day 0: egg. Day 3: larva. Day 8: pupa. Day 16: adult. Day 30: laying new eggs. Day 730: queen dead.

## Your task

Draw a model for each. Say what is the same and what differs. Predict what follows when a new pea seed begins growing in the recorded conditions.

## Second card (reveal after the first draft)

On day 49, the opened pod of P holds new seeds. One seed begins growing that day. It is a seedling on day 56. Revise your model to connect the adult with new individuals. Keep your first model. Revise in a second color.
